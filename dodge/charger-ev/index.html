Jeep Wagoneer S and Dodge Charger EV display may fail high

Reported Apr 23, 2026 · Campaign 26V262000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Wagoneer S and Dodge Charger electric vehicles. A software problem can make the display in front of the driver stop working, so the information it normally shows is not available. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet several federal safety standards, covering vehicle lamps, stability control, brakes, the tire pressure monitoring system and occupant crash protection.

Dodge Charger EV, Jeep Wagoneer S May Roll Away high

Reported Sep 4, 2025 · Campaign 25V574000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

On these Dodge Charger and Jeep Wagoneer S electric vehicles, an incorrectly installed spring may cause the vehicle to lose its 'Park' function when the gear selector is placed in Park, which can lead to the vehicle rolling away.

Dodge Charger EV may not sound pedestrian warning medium

Reported Jun 12, 2025 · Campaign 25V389000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

These Dodge Charger electric cars are supposed to make sound on the outside so people nearby can hear them coming. On the recalled vehicles, the amplifier that creates that sound may be missing its software. Without that software, the amplifier may not put out any exterior sound at all, including the alert meant to warn pedestrians. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule setting a minimum sound requirement for electric and hybrid vehicles.

Dodge Charger EV recall questions

How many recalls does the Dodge Charger EV have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2024–2025 Dodge Charger EV. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Jeep Wagoneer S and Dodge Charger EV display may fail" (NHTSA campaign 26V262000), reported Apr 23, 2026.

Which Dodge Charger EV model year has the most recalls?

The 2024 Dodge Charger EV has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2024 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 3. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Dodge Charger EV?

No. None of the 3 Dodge Charger EV recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Dodge Charger EV has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Dodge dealer, including for second owners.
