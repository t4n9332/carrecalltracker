Dodge Durango recalls

18 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Dodge Durango rear spoiler may come off in traffic medium

Reported 2026-08-13 · Campaign 26V528000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

The trim piece across the top of the rear window isn't lined up right with the roof, so opening and closing the rear hatch can damage it. A damaged spoiler can break off while you're driving and land in the road, where it becomes a hazard for the cars behind you. Dealers will inspect it and repair or replace it and realign the hatch, free. Note: if your Durango was fixed under an earlier recall, it needs this repair too.

Jeep Grand Cherokee, Dodge Durango brake module failure high

Reported 2024-11-07 · Campaign 24V838000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

A fault in the antilock brake computer can switch off both antilock braking and the stability control that keeps you from sliding in a turn. It can also make your brake lights come on when you are not braking, and it can let you start the vehicle and shift out of Park without your foot on the brake — so the vehicle could roll away. Dealers will replace a brake sensor and update the software, free. Vehicles already fixed under the earlier recall 22V-426 need this new repair too.

Jeep, Ram, Chrysler backup camera may show nothing medium

Reported 2024-06-13 · Campaign 24V436000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

The radio software in these vehicles can keep the backup camera picture from appearing at all. When you shift into reverse, the screen may not show what is behind you, so you cannot see a child, a person, or an object in the vehicle's path while backing up. That makes it much easier to hit something you never saw. The repair is a software update for the radio, done at the dealer or sent to the vehicle wirelessly.

Dodge Durango and Ram trucks lose stability control medium

Reported 2024-06-06 · Campaign 24V415000 · Model years: 2022

A software error in the computer that runs the anti-lock brakes can switch off the electronic stability control system. Stability control senses when the vehicle is starting to slide or spin in a turn and automatically brakes individual wheels to help you stay on course. With it shut off, you still have normal brakes and steering, but you lose that automatic help. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule that requires stability control to work.

Dodge Durango Pursuit shifter can slip out of gear high

Reported 2023-09-18 · Campaign 23V640000 · Model years: 2023

Some 2023 Dodge Durango Pursuit police models have the gear shifter mounted on the dashboard instead of the console. The rubber boot around that shifter can rub against it and push it out of gear while you are driving. If that happens, the engine stops sending power to the wheels and the vehicle coasts, even though the motor is still running. Chrysler's fix is a new shifter boot, installed at the dealer at no cost.

Dodge Durango rear spoiler can come loose medium

Reported 2023-02-23 · Campaign 23V115000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

The spoiler across the back roof of your Durango may not have been lined up correctly with the roof. Each time the rear hatch is opened and closed, that misalignment can damage the spoiler. A damaged spoiler can eventually break free and fall off while you are driving, landing in the road behind you where another driver could hit it. A dealer will inspect the spoiler and put on a new one if needed.

Dodge Charger, Durango shifter may slip out of drive high

Reported 2022-11-23 · Campaign 22V866000 · Model years: 2022

Certain 2022 Dodge Charger and Durango vehicles have a shifter assembly that was not heat-treated correctly, so the metal is not as strong as it should be. Because of that, the shifter may not fully catch in the "drive" position. It can slip out of drive on its own, and the vehicle would suddenly stop pulling while you are on the road. Dealers will replace the shifter assembly free of charge.

Jeep Grand Cherokee and Durango can roll out of Park critical

Reported 2022-06-09 · Campaign 22V426000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

A fault in the antilock brake computer can turn on your brake lights and let the vehicle start and shift out of Park even when your foot is not on the brake pedal. That means the vehicle can be moved out of Park by accident and roll away on its own, with no warning, possibly hitting someone nearby. Chrysler replaced this recall with a newer one, number 24V838, so even a vehicle already repaired needs the new fix.

Dodge Durango and Jeep Grand Cherokee can lose engine power high

Reported 2022-04-28 · Campaign 22V284000 · Model years: 2021

These SUVs have a valve that routes some exhaust back into the engine to cut pollution. On these vehicles the valve can stick open. When that happens the engine can suddenly stop making power, so the vehicle slows or quits pulling with no warning, which is dangerous if you are merging, passing, or climbing a hill. There is no way for you to see it coming. The dealer replaces the whole valve assembly at no cost to you.

Dodge Durango steering may bind or fail suddenly critical

Reported 2022-03-14 · Campaign 22V154000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

The steering rack is the gear assembly that turns your front wheels when you turn the wheel. A bar inside it may not have been hardened enough during manufacturing, so it can bind, or stick, inside the rack. When that happens the steering can feel very hard to turn, or you can lose steering control altogether, which can cause a crash. A dealer will replace the whole steering gear assembly at no charge to you.

Ram and Durango stability control warning light may not work medium

Reported 2022-03-10 · Campaign 22V140000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Certain Ram 2500 and 3500 trucks and Dodge Durango SUVs have a software problem in the anti-lock brake computer. Because of it, the dashboard warning light for the electronic stability control — the system that helps keep the vehicle from skidding or spinning — may not light up when that system has a problem. So you could be driving with the stability system not working and never be told about it. Dealers will update the software free of charge.

Dodge Durango rear spoiler may come loose medium

Reported 2021-10-28 · Campaign 21V842000 · Model years: 2021

On some 2021 Durango SUVs, the spoiler — the trim piece across the top of the rear window — may not line up properly with the roof. Because of that, opening and closing the rear liftgate can knock into the spoiler and damage it over time. A damaged spoiler can eventually work loose and fall off while you are driving, landing in the roadway where another vehicle could hit it or swerve to avoid it.

Dodge Durango side curtain airbag bolt loose or missing medium

Reported 2021-04-22 · Campaign 21V280000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On certain Dodge Durango SUVs, a bolt that holds the side curtain airbag in place may be loose or missing entirely. That airbag is the long one that drops down from the roof beside the windows. If the bolt is not holding properly, the airbag may not open the way it was designed to during a crash, so it may not protect people as well as it should. A dealer will tighten or replace the fastener free of charge.

Jeep, Ram, Chrysler backup camera image stays on screen low

Reported 2020-09-10 · Campaign 20V191000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

The software in your 8.4-inch or 12-inch radio screen has a bug. After you back up and shift out of reverse, the picture from the backup camera can stay on the screen instead of switching back to the normal display. That means you can be driving forward while a rear-facing picture is still showing, which can pull your eyes and attention off the road ahead. The fix is a software update for the radio screen.

Jeep Grand Cherokee and Dodge Durango can lose power suddenly critical

Reported 2020-03-26 · Campaign 20V183000 · Model years: 2019

The front differential — the gearbox that sends power to the front wheels — on these 2019 Jeep Grand Cherokee and Dodge Durango SUVs may contain gears that were not hardened enough. The teeth on those gears can wear away. If they wear off completely, the vehicle can suddenly lose power while you are driving. It can also lose the PARK function, meaning the vehicle may not stay put when you park it. Dealers replace the whole front differential.

Jeep, Ram, Dodge, Chrysler engine computer may cause stalling high

Reported 2018-08-09 · Campaign 18V524000 · Model years: 2018

The computer that runs the engine has a small part inside, a voltage regulator chip, that can fail. When it does, the engine can shut off while you are driving, or the vehicle may not start at all. A stall in traffic leaves you without engine power and can put you in the path of other vehicles. Dealers will replace the whole engine computer at no charge.

Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram cruise control may stick on high

Reported 2018-05-17 · Campaign 18V332000 · Model years: 2018

These vehicles have cruise control, which holds a set speed for you. A short circuit in the wiring can lock the cruise control on. If that happens, tapping the brake pedal or pressing the off button may not shut it off, and the vehicle can keep its speed or even speed up on its own. Chrysler tells owners to stop using cruise control until the free computer software update is done at a dealer.

Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, RAM may not stay in Park high

Reported 2018-05-01 · Campaign 18V280000 · Model years: 2018

The wrong part may have been installed inside the automatic transmission. It is a rod that locks the transmission when you move the shifter into Park. If the wrong rod is in there, the transmission may not actually lock, so the vehicle can roll on its own after you think you have parked it. That could hurt someone standing nearby or cause a crash. A dealer installs the correct rod for free.
