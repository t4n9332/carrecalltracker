Dodge Journey recalls

7 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Ram ProMaster and Dodge Journey backup camera may fail medium

Reported 2025-08-26 · Campaign 25V552000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

A tiny computer chip inside the backup camera can crack. When it does, the picture behind the vehicle may not show up properly on the screen as you reverse, so you could miss a person or object behind you. Chrysler will replace the camera at no cost, but the parts are not ready yet. The letter mailed September 30, 2025 is only a warning; a second letter with the actual fix is expected around July 2026.

Dodge Journey Side Reflector Is Too Dim low

Reported 2022-09-29 · Campaign 22V723000 · Model years: 2018

The headlight units on some 2017-2018 Dodge Journey SUVs contain a small amber reflector on the side. That reflector is supposed to bounce back the light from other cars' headlights so your vehicle shows up from the side, even when it is parked or its own lights are off. On these vehicles the reflector is too dim to do that job properly. The dealer will replace the whole headlight assembly on each side at no cost.

Jeep, Ram, Dodge, Chrysler engine computer may cause stalling high

Reported 2018-08-09 · Campaign 18V524000 · Model years: 2018

The computer that runs the engine has a small part inside, a voltage regulator chip, that can fail. When it does, the engine can shut off while you are driving, or the vehicle may not start at all. A stall in traffic leaves you without engine power and can put you in the path of other vehicles. Dealers will replace the whole engine computer at no charge.

Dodge and Jeep rear brake calipers may weaken braking high

Reported 2018-08-09 · Campaign 18V523000 · Model years: 2018

The pistons inside the rear brake calipers may not have enough protective coating. Without it, pockets of gas can form in the rear brake system, and that can make the rear brakes less effective than they should be. You may not notice anything day to day, but the vehicle could take longer to stop, which raises the chance of a crash. Dealers will bleed the brake system to flush the gas pockets out, free of charge. This recall started September 29, 2018.

Dodge Journey backup camera image may appear too slowly medium

Reported 2018-06-14 · Campaign 18V396000 · Model years: 2018

When you shift into reverse in these SUVs, the picture from the backup camera may take longer than two seconds to show up on the screen. Federal rules require the full view to appear within two seconds. That delay means you could start backing up before you can actually see what is behind the vehicle. The problem is in the rearview mirror unit that holds the display, so dealers will replace the entire mirror at no cost.

Dodge Journey and Ram backup camera image may vanish medium

Reported 2018-06-14 · Campaign 18V398000 · Model years: 2018

Chrysler is recalling certain 2018 Dodge Journey and Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 vehicles. The backup camera picture can disappear from the screen while you are reversing, which means you could be backing up with no view of what is behind you. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule for rear visibility. Dealers will replace the rearview mirror free of charge.

Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram cruise control may stick on high

Reported 2018-05-17 · Campaign 18V332000 · Model years: 2018

These vehicles have cruise control, which holds a set speed for you. A short circuit in the wiring can lock the cruise control on. If that happens, tapping the brake pedal or pressing the off button may not shut it off, and the vehicle can keep its speed or even speed up on its own. Chrysler tells owners to stop using cruise control until the free computer software update is done at a dealer.
