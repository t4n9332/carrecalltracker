Dodge Grand Caravan recalls

5 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Wheelchair Restraints in Various Vehicles May Not Lock high

Reported Dec 17, 2025 · Campaign 25V876000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

Certain vehicles equipped with QRT-Deluxe or QRT-Max wheelchair restraints may have retractors that fail to lock. If the retractor does not lock, a wheelchair inside the vehicle may not be properly secured.

Modified Grand Caravan fuel gasket may leak critical

Reported May 15, 2020 · Campaign 20V278000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On some Vantage-modified Grand Caravan vehicles, the fuel pump gasket may wear out over time and allow fuel to leak. If that fuel leak occurs near a source of ignition, it can increase the risk of a fire.

Dodge Grand Caravan rear seat strikers may fail medium

Reported Oct 24, 2019 · Campaign 19V759000 · Model years: 2019

Welds on the second-row seat strikers may fail in a front-impact crash, and welds on the third-row seat strikers may fail in a rear-impact crash. In addition, the second-row bench and bucket seats may not hold up to the loads they are required to withstand.

Dodge and Jeep Vehicles Have Rear Brake Problem high

Reported Aug 9, 2018 · Campaign 18V523000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On these Dodge and Jeep vehicles, the pistons inside the rear brake calipers may not have enough protective coating. Without enough coating, gas pockets can form inside the caliper pistons. These gas pockets can reduce how well the rear brakes perform, meaning the rear brakes may not work as effectively as they should.

Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram Vehicles May Stall high

Reported Aug 9, 2018 · Campaign 18V524000 · Model years: 2018

A voltage regulator chip on the circuit board inside the powertrain control module may fail. If this chip fails, the vehicle may stall while it is being driven, or it may fail to start at all. To correct this problem, Chrysler dealers will replace the entire powertrain control module with a new one so the vehicle does not experience this stalling or no-start condition again.

Dodge Grand Caravan recall questions

How many recalls does the Dodge Grand Caravan have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2018–2020 Dodge Grand Caravan. 4 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Wheelchair Restraints in Various Vehicles May Not Lock" (NHTSA campaign 25V876000), reported Dec 17, 2025.

Which Dodge Grand Caravan model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Dodge Grand Caravan has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2020 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Dodge Grand Caravan?

No. None of the 5 Dodge Grand Caravan recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Dodge Grand Caravan has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Dodge dealer, including for second owners.
