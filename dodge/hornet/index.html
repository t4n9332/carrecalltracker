Dodge Hornet, Alfa Romeo Tonale Seat Belts May Not Retract medium

Reported Aug 6, 2026 · Campaign 26V510000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

On these Dodge Hornet and Alfa Romeo Tonale vehicles, the rear seat belts next to the windows can become twisted. When that happens, the belt may not pull back in the way it should. A belt that does not retract properly may not hold an occupant in place the way it is meant to. Dealers will replace the retractors, the parts that wind the belt in and out, at no cost to the owner.

Dodge Hornet Rearview Camera Display Failure high

Reported Apr 15, 2025 · Campaign 25V246000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

In some Dodge Hornet vehicles, the display screen may not show the rearview camera image. Without that image, the driver has a reduced view of what's behind the vehicle while backing up. This reduced visibility increases the risk of a crash.

Dodge Hornet brake pedal may collapse while driving high

Reported Oct 9, 2024 · Campaign 24V752000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

On some 2024-2025 Dodge Hornet and Alfa Romeo Tonale vehicles, the brake pedal may collapse, causing a loss of brake function. Dealers will reinforce the brake pedal arm to fix the problem, free of charge.

Dodge Hornet Label Missing Tire and Rim Size medium

Reported Mar 19, 2024 · Campaign 24V209000 · Model years: 2024

The manufacturer label on affected vehicles is missing tire and rim size information. Without this information, a wheel of the wrong size could be installed on the vehicle.

Dodge Hornet tire label lists wrong weight limit medium

Reported Mar 5, 2024 · Campaign 24V175000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Chrysler is recalling certain Dodge Hornet vehicles because of wrong information printed on a label. The tire placard is the sticker that lists tire and loading information for the vehicle, and on these cars the maximum vehicle capacity weight shown on it is incorrect. That means the sticker does not meet the federal rule covering tire selection and rims. Dealers will place a corrected overlay label over the tire placard at no charge.

Dodge Hornet Pedestrian Alert Siren May Fail medium

Reported Oct 17, 2023 · Campaign 23V696000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Some Dodge Hornet vehicles may have a pedestrian warning siren that is missing or disconnected. This siren is meant to alert people outside the vehicle when it is in reverse. Because of this issue, the vehicle may fail to sound the alert while backing up.

Dodge Hornet Loose Cable Can Cause Fire critical

Reported Sep 7, 2023 · Campaign 23V623000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Certain Dodge Hornet and Alfa Romeo Tonale plug-in hybrid electric vehicles may have a 12-volt battery positive cable or a high voltage connector cable that was not tightened properly. A loose connection like this can overheat, and that overheating can result in a fire while the vehicle is parked or being driven.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Dodge Hornet recall questions

How many recalls does the Dodge Hornet have?

NHTSA lists 7 safety recalls covering the 2023–2025 Dodge Hornet. 3 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Dodge Hornet, Alfa Romeo Tonale Seat Belts May Not Retract" (NHTSA campaign 26V510000), reported Aug 6, 2026.

Which Dodge Hornet model year has the most recalls?

The 2024 Dodge Hornet has the most, with 7 recalls. The 2025 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 3. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Dodge Hornet?

Yes. 1 Dodge Hornet recall carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Dodge Hornet Loose Cable Can Cause Fire (23V623000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Dodge Hornet has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Dodge dealer, including for second owners.
