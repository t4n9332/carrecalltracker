A recall usually comes after owners complain. This list shows the model years with the most NHTSA complaints in a problem area where we found no recall for that vehicle.

Read it with care: complaints are unverified, best-selling models collect more of them, and a high count does not mean a defect exists or that a recall is coming. NHTSA labels complaint areas and recall areas differently, so we match them by broad category and may miss a related recall filed under another label.
