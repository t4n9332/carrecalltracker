Honda and Acura fuel pump can fail, stalling engine high

Reported 2023-12-18 · Campaign 23V858000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The fuel pump sits inside the gas tank and sends fuel to the engine. On these vehicles that pump can fail. When it does, the engine can stall while you are driving — losing power in traffic or on the highway raises the chance of a crash. A dealer will replace the entire fuel pump unit for free. This recall is an expansion of two earlier Honda fuel pump recalls covering additional vehicles.

Honda Clarity rear seat belts may not lock properly medium

Reported 2020-06-04 · Campaign 20V326000 · Model years: 2019

Certain 2019 Honda Clarity Fuel Cell, Electric, and Plug-in Hybrid cars have rear seat belts with a locking system that senses how fast the belt is pulled. On the affected cars, that locking part may not lock the way it should. If it does not lock, the belt may not hold a back-seat passenger in place during a crash. A dealer checks when the belt assemblies were made and replaces them if needed.
