Honda ADV160 Scooter Can Lose Power From Broken Gear high

Reported 2025-03-27 · Campaign 25V195000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

A small gear that drives the engine's oil pump can fail. When it does, the scooter can suddenly stop pulling while you are riding. Losing power in traffic, on a hill, or in the middle of an intersection can easily lead to a crash or an injury. Honda dealers will install a new oil pump driven gear at no cost. Letters went out to owners on August 6, 2025.
