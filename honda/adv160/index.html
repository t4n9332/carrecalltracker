Honda ADV160 Scooter Oil Pump Gear Failure medium

Reported Mar 27, 2025 · Campaign 25V195000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Honda is recalling certain 2024 and 2025 ADV160 scooters. The oil pump driven gear may fail. If this gear fails, the scooter can lose drive power.
