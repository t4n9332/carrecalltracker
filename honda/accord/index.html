Honda and Acura front passenger airbags may deploy unintentionally high

Reported May 21, 2026 · Campaign 26V332000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

Honda is recalling certain Honda and Acura vehicles, including the Accord, Civic, CR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Ridgeline, HR-V, Fit, Insight, Passport and the Acura TLX, RDX and MDX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short circuit. If that happens, the airbags can go off when they should not during a crash. Dealers will replace the seat weight sensors at no cost. This recall expands an earlier recall.

Honda and Acura passenger seat sensor may crack high

Reported Feb 1, 2024 · Campaign 24V064000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Honda is recalling certain 2020 through 2022 Honda and Acura vehicles, including Accord, Civic, CR-V, HR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Passport, Ridgeline, Fit, Insight and the Acura MDX, RDX and TLX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short out. When that happens, the system may not switch off the front passenger airbag when it is supposed to.

Honda and Acura Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported Dec 18, 2023 · Campaign 23V858000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

In a wide range of Honda and Acura vehicles, the fuel pump located inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails while you are driving, the engine can suddenly stall.

Acura Integra, RDX and Accord Brake Fluid Leak high

Reported Jun 15, 2023 · Campaign 23V430000 · Model years: 2022

On some Acura Integra, Acura RDX, and Honda Accord vehicles, a valve inside the brake system may leak brake fluid. This can cause the vehicle to move on its own when the brake hold feature is on, or make the brake pedal feel like it needs to be pushed further down than normal.

Honda, Acura Seat Belt Buckles May Not Latch high

Reported Mar 9, 2023 · Campaign 23V158000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Certain Honda and Acura vehicles, including the CR-V, Accord, Accord Hybrid, Odyssey, Insight, and RDX, may have a manufacturing problem with the front seat belts. The buckle's channel may interfere with the release button, which can prevent the seat belt buckle from latching properly. This means the seat belt may not click into place and hold securely the way it is supposed to for the driver or front passenger seat.

Honda Accord, CR-V, Ridgeline Center Belt May Not Lock high

Reported Nov 18, 2021 · Campaign 21V900000 · Model years: 2021

Honda is recalling certain Accord, Accord Hybrid, CR-V, CR-V Hybrid, Insight and Ridgeline vehicles. The automatic locking feature on the second-row center seat belt may turn off when it should not, and a child seat held by that belt can be left unsecured. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for occupant crash protection. Dealers will replace the second-row center seat belt assembly at no cost to the owner.

Honda, Acura fuel pump may fail, stall engine high

Reported Mar 25, 2021 · Campaign 21V215000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On many Honda and Acura models, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven.

Honda Accord, Insight software may disable key systems high

Reported Dec 10, 2020 · Campaign 20V771000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

This recall covers certain Honda Accord Sedan, Accord Hybrid, and Insight vehicles. A software error may cause communication problems between the body control module and other parts of the vehicle, which can lead to malfunctions in things like the windshield wipers and defroster, the rearview camera, exterior lights, the audible warning for a stopped vehicle, and the power windows.

Honda and Acura Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported May 28, 2020 · Campaign 20V314000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Certain Honda and Acura vehicles have a low-pressure fuel pump, inside the fuel tank, that may fail. If the pump fails while you are driving, the engine can stall, which raises the risk of a crash. Honda will replace the fuel pump assembly on affected vehicles, free of charge, as part of this recall.

Honda Accord and Insight backup camera display may fail medium

Reported Sep 13, 2018 · Campaign 18V629000 · Model years: 2018

On these Honda Accord and Insight cars, the back-up camera picture on the center screen may not work properly in certain situations. The screen is supposed to show the driver what is behind the car when it is put in reverse. Because the display may not do that, these vehicles do not meet the federal standard covering rearview equipment. The repair is a software update: dealers reprogram the display audio unit that runs the screen.

Honda Accord recall questions

How many recalls does the Honda Accord have?

NHTSA lists 10 safety recalls covering the 2018–2022 Honda Accord. 9 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Honda and Acura front passenger airbags may deploy unintentionally" (NHTSA campaign 26V332000), reported May 21, 2026.

Which Honda Accord model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Honda Accord has the most, with 6 recalls. The 2021 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 4. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Honda Accord?

No. None of the 10 Honda Accord recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Honda Accord has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Honda dealer, including for second owners.
