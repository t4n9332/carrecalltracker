Honda CMX300, CMX500 Handlebar Lock Screw May Loosen high

Reported Jul 30, 2026 · Campaign 26V500000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

Certain Honda CMX300 and CMX500 motorcycles, along with the Honda SCL500, have a screw that locks the handlebar in place. This lock screw can loosen over time and detach completely. If the screw comes loose, it may lodge between the motorcycle's frame and the steering stem, getting in the way of how the handlebars turn. This recall expands an earlier Honda recall that covered the same handlebar lock screw issue.

Honda CMX300/CMX500 Handlebar Lock Screw May Loosen high

Reported Mar 12, 2026 · Campaign 26V146000 · Model years: 2023

On certain Honda CMX300 and CMX500 motorcycles, the handlebar lock screw may gradually loosen and come loose from its mounting. If the screw detaches, it can become lodged between the frame and the steering stem. This can get in the way of normal steering, making the motorcycle harder to control and increasing the chance of a crash or injury for the rider.

Honda CMX500, CRF300L Rear Reflector Too Dim high

Reported May 24, 2021 · Campaign 21V382000 · Model years: 2021

Some Honda motorcycles, including the CMX500 and CRF300L, have a rear reflector lens that may not reflect light as brightly as it should. This lens is part of the rear lighting equipment, and its dimness means the motorcycle does not meet a federal lighting safety standard.
