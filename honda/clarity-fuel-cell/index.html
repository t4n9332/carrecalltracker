Honda Clarity Fuel Cell Water Pump May Fail high

Reported 2019-04-18 · Campaign 19V315000 · Model years: 2018

On certain Clarity Fuel Cell vehicles, internal parts may corrode and cause the water pump to fail. A failed water pump can cause the vehicle to stall.

Honda Clarity Fuel Cell: Earlier Software Fix Incomplete high

Reported 2018-12-20 · Campaign 18V921000 · Model years: 2018

Some 2017-2018 Honda Clarity Fuel Cell vehicles previously repaired under an earlier recall may not have been fully fixed. The fuel cell control unit's software can misread a small drop in cell voltage, which can cause the vehicle to partly or completely lose power. Dealers will update the fuel cell control unit software again.
