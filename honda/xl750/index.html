Honda CMX1100, CRF1100, XL750 Oil Leak Risk high

Reported May 29, 2025 · Campaign 25V358000 · Model years: 2024

Certain Honda CMX1100, CRF1100 and XL750 motorcycles have a bolt problem in the engine. The bolt that seals the crankcase may not have been made correctly, which can let engine oil leak out and land on the rear tire. Oil on the rear tire may cause a loss of control while riding, which increases the risk of a crash or injury.
