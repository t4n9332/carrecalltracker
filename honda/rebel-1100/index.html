Honda Rebel 1100 radiator cap may leak hot coolant high

Reported Dec 16, 2021 · Campaign 21V987000 · Model years: 2021

The radiator cap on some Honda Rebel 1100 motorcycles may not have been formed correctly during manufacturing. This can leave the cap without a tight enough seal. Because the seal may not be tight enough, hot coolant could leak out or spray from the radiator. This creates a risk of injury or of being involved in a crash while riding.
