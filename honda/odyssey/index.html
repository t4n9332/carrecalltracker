Honda Odyssey recalls

Honda Odyssey rearview camera may fail from water entry medium

Reported Jul 1, 2026 · Campaign 26V423000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

Honda is recalling certain Odyssey vehicles because water can get into the rearview camera. When that happens, the camera image may not show up on the screen when the vehicle is put in reverse. Because the image can fail to appear, these vehicles do not meet the federal rules for rear visibility. A dealer will replace the rearview camera at no cost to the owner. Honda mailed notification letters to owners and has a customer service line for questions.

Honda and Acura front passenger airbags may deploy unintentionally high

Reported May 21, 2026 · Campaign 26V332000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

Honda is recalling certain Honda and Acura vehicles, including the Accord, Civic, CR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Ridgeline, HR-V, Fit, Insight, Passport and the Acura TLX, RDX and MDX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short circuit. If that happens, the airbags can go off when they should not during a crash. Dealers will replace the seat weight sensors at no cost. This recall expands an earlier recall.

Honda Odyssey Side Air Bags May Deploy Unexpectedly high

Reported Apr 9, 2026 · Campaign 26V227000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

A software programming error in some Honda Odyssey minivans can cause the side air bags and side curtain air bags to deploy unexpectedly.

Honda, Chrysler and Toyota Vans Wheelchair Restraint Issue medium

Reported Dec 17, 2025 · Campaign 25V877000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

Some vehicles equipped with certain wheelchair restraint systems have retractors that may not lock properly. This means a wheelchair might not be securely held in place while the vehicle is being driven. Dealers will inspect the retractors and replace them if needed, free of charge.

Acura RDX and Odyssey Steering Corrosion Risk high

Reported Feb 15, 2024 · Campaign 24V114000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

On some Acura RDX and Honda Odyssey vehicles, the steering gear box may not have been tightened properly. This can let water get inside, causing corrosion and the gears to bind. Corroded, binding gears can make the steering harder to control.

Honda and Acura passenger seat sensor may crack high

Reported Feb 1, 2024 · Campaign 24V064000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Honda is recalling certain 2020 through 2022 Honda and Acura vehicles, including Accord, Civic, CR-V, HR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Passport, Ridgeline, Fit, Insight and the Acura MDX, RDX and TLX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short out. When that happens, the system may not switch off the front passenger airbag when it is supposed to.

Honda and Acura Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported Dec 18, 2023 · Campaign 23V858000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

In a wide range of Honda and Acura vehicles, the fuel pump located inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails while you are driving, the engine can suddenly stall.

Acura, Honda Engine Bearing May Wear and Seize critical

Reported Nov 13, 2023 · Campaign 23V751000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

These vehicles have an engine part called a connecting rod bearing, and a manufacturing error affects it. Because of this defect, the bearing can wear down over time and eventually seize up inside the engine. When that happens, the engine can become damaged. A damaged engine may not run the way it should, and it could even stop working suddenly while the vehicle is being driven.

Honda Odyssey, Pilot and Passport Rearview Camera Problem medium

Reported Jun 15, 2023 · Campaign 23V431000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

A faulty cable connector in these vehicles can cause the rearview camera image to not show up on your dashboard screen when you put the car in reverse. This means you may not be able to see what's behind your vehicle through the camera display. Dealers will fix this by replacing the cable and adding a cover to protect the connector, at no charge to you.

Honda Pilot, Ridgeline, Passport, Odyssey side mirror glass may fall off medium

Reported Mar 16, 2023 · Campaign 23V174000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Honda is recalling certain Pilot, Ridgeline, Passport and Odyssey vehicles. The heating pads sitting behind both side-view mirrors may not be bonded on properly. Because of that, the mirror glass can come loose and separate from the mirror. With the glass gone, these vehicles do not meet the federal rules for rear visibility. The recall covers the mirrors on both the left and the right side of the vehicle.

Honda, Acura Seat Belt Buckles May Not Latch high

Reported Mar 9, 2023 · Campaign 23V158000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

Certain Honda and Acura vehicles, including the CR-V, Accord, Accord Hybrid, Odyssey, Insight, and RDX, may have a manufacturing problem with the front seat belts. The buckle's channel may interfere with the release button, which can prevent the seat belt buckle from latching properly. This means the seat belt may not click into place and hold securely the way it is supposed to for the driver or front passenger seat.

Honda Odyssey Tires May Have Pinhole Leaks medium

Reported Sep 29, 2021 · Campaign 21V768000 · Model years: 2022

Some Odyssey minivans equipped with Bridgestone tires may have a tire with a small pinhole in it. This pinhole can cause the tire to slowly leak air, which can affect how well the tire performs.

Odyssey Second-Row Seats May Not Lock high

Reported Jun 7, 2021 · Campaign 21V432000 · Model years: 2022

On certain Odyssey vehicles, the second-row outboard seats may have a deformed locking mechanism, which can keep the seat rails from locking into place. A seat that is not locked can move unexpectedly, increasing the risk of injury.

Honda, Acura fuel pump may fail, stall engine high

Reported Mar 25, 2021 · Campaign 21V215000 · Model years: 2019

On many Honda and Acura models, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven.

Braun-Modified Honda Odyssey Fuel Tank May Leak critical

Reported Jan 14, 2021 · Campaign 21V010000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021

On some Honda Odyssey vans modified by Braun, the fuel tank can overfill and send extra fuel into the charcoal canister, which is part of the fuel system. If the canister collects more fuel than it can handle, that fuel may leak out of the vehicle. In other words, the fuel tank setup on these vans may not be containing fuel the way it's supposed to, allowing it to escape rather than staying inside the system.

Honda Pilot, Odyssey and Passport rearview cameras may lag medium

Reported Jul 28, 2020 · Campaign 20V440000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Honda is recalling certain Pilot, Odyssey and Passport vehicles because of a programming error in the central network software. The error can cause several faults that delay the rearview camera picture or keep it from appearing on the screen at all. Because the camera image may not show when it should, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard covering rear visibility. Honda has corrected software available for the vehicles that are covered.

Honda Odyssey, Passport, Pilot Instrument Panel Glitch medium

Reported Jul 28, 2020 · Campaign 20V439000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

Honda is recalling certain Odyssey, Passport and Pilot vehicles. A software problem in the instrument panel control module can cause the dashboard to stop showing important information, such as engine oil pressure, vehicle speed and what gear the car is in, until the vehicle is turned off and back on. The same problem can also prevent the rearview camera image from showing up on the screen.

Honda Odyssey Sliding Door Latch May Fail high

Reported Jul 28, 2020 · Campaign 20V437000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

Water may get into the outer door handle cables for the sliding doors. If this happens, the cables may freeze in cold temperatures, preventing the front and rear sliding door latches from securely latching.

Honda Odyssey Rearview Camera Display Problem high

Reported Jul 28, 2020 · Campaign 20V438000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Honda is recalling certain Odyssey vehicles. Water may get into the rearview camera mounting holes and into the camera lens, causing a distorted image or no image at all. The image may also fail to display if the camera is set to, or switched from, the top-down view in a bright environment.

Honda Odyssey Third-Row Outlet Wiring Can Short critical

Reported Feb 6, 2020 · Campaign 20V066000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

The wiring for the third-row seat's accessory power outlet can get pinched between the vehicle's frame and a rear trim panel. This pinching may damage the wires, which can cause an electrical short in that circuit, affecting only the third-row accessory outlet wiring.

Honda Odyssey Transmission Computer May Shift Into Park Unexpectedly medium

Reported Apr 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V299000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On certain Honda Odyssey vehicles, a loose battery connection or a weak battery can cause the transmission's control computer to unexpectedly restart. When this happens, the computer may shift the transmission into Park on its own, and doing so can damage the parking rod. If the parking rod is damaged, the vehicle may not stay in place after being parked and could roll, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.

Honda and Acura Models: Timing Belt May Fail high

Reported Apr 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V298000 · Model years: 2019

On certain Acura MDX, TLX, and Honda Odyssey, Pilot, and Ridgeline vehicles, the teeth on the timing belt may separate from the belt. If the timing belt teeth separate, the engine may stall while driving. Honda will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the vehicle and replace the timing belt if it's affected. If a new timing belt is installed, the engine will also be inspected and any damaged parts will be replaced, all free of charge.

Honda Odyssey Ramp Vans Have Wrong Brake Sensor Wiring medium

Reported Mar 20, 2019 · Campaign 19V213000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Braun Corporation converted some Honda Odyssey vans to add a wheelchair ramp. During this conversion, the wiring for the rear wheel speed sensors may have been connected to the wrong wheels. Because of this mix-up, the anti-lock braking system may not correctly recognize how fast each rear wheel is spinning, which can affect how the system responds while braking.

Honda Odyssey Power Sliding Doors May Not Latch high

Reported Nov 9, 2018 · Campaign 18V795000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The power sliding doors on the sides of these minivans are held shut by a latch that grips a catch on the door frame. On the vehicles being recalled, that latch can stick and may fail to grip the catch the way it should, so the door is not properly latched closed. Honda is replacing the rear latch assembly on both the left and right power sliding doors.

Acura and Honda Models Have Reduced Brake Performance high

Reported Nov 1, 2018 · Campaign 18V777000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On certain Acura MDX, Acura MDX Sport Hybrid, Honda HR-V, and Honda Odyssey vehicles, a coating inside the rear brake calipers may not have been applied correctly during manufacturing. This can allow small gas pockets to form inside the caliper pistons. Over time, these gas pockets can reduce how well the brakes are able to slow or stop the vehicle, lowering overall braking performance.

Honda Insight, Odyssey, Ridgeline Airbag Deployment Failure high

Reported Sep 27, 2018 · Campaign 18V664000 · Model years: 2019

The airbag control unit in these vehicles may have been built with a manufacturing error. Because of this, the airbags or seatbelt pretensioners might not deploy properly if the vehicle is in a crash.

Honda Odyssey Third-Row Seat Belt May Not Latch high

Reported Jun 23, 2017 · Campaign 17V397000 · Model years: 2018

Certain Honda Odyssey vehicles have a problem with the seat belt in the third-row center seat. The buckle tongue for that seat belt may not fit correctly with the latch, so the belt may not click securely into place. If the seat belt does not latch properly, it cannot hold the person sitting there safely in place. This affects the third-row center seating position only.

Honda Odyssey recall questions

How many recalls does the Honda Odyssey have?

NHTSA lists 27 safety recalls covering the 2018–2025 Honda Odyssey. 18 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Honda Odyssey rearview camera may fail from water entry" (NHTSA campaign 26V423000), reported Jul 1, 2026.

Which Honda Odyssey model year has the most recalls?

The 2019 Honda Odyssey has the most, with 20 recalls. The 2025 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Honda Odyssey?

No. None of the 27 Honda Odyssey recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Honda Odyssey has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Honda dealer, including for second owners.
