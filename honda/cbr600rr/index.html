Honda CBR600RR engine flaw risks rear wheel lockup critical

Reported Dec 18, 2025 · Campaign 25V891000 · Model years: 2025, 2026

Some 2024-2026 Honda CBR600RR motorcycles may have a cylinder head that was not manufactured the way it should have been. This manufacturing problem can lead to a bearing on one of the engine's connecting rods failing. If that bearing fails, it may cause the motorcycle's rear wheel to suddenly lock up, or it may cause engine oil to leak out and land on the rear tire.

Honda CBR600RR, CBR1000RR, GL1800 Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported Feb 15, 2024 · Campaign 24V113000 · Model years: 2019

Certain Honda GL1800 Gold Wing, CBR600RR, and CBR1000RR motorcycles have a fuel pump impeller that may have been molded incorrectly. This can cause the impeller to deform, leading to fuel pump failure. If the fuel pump fails, the engine can stall while the motorcycle is being ridden.
