Honda Goldwing, CBR fuel pumps can fail and stall high

Reported 2024-02-15 · Campaign 24V113000 · Model years: 2018

The fuel pump inside the gas tank has a spinning part that may have been molded wrong. Over time it can warp and jam, so the pump stops sending gas to the engine. If that happens while you're riding, the engine can quit without warning, which is dangerous on a motorcycle in traffic. A Honda dealer will check the fuel pump and put in a new one if needed, at no cost to you.
