Honda Gold Wing: engine bolt may break, locking rear wheel critical

Reported 2024-10-31 · Campaign 24V820000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021

Inside the engine of these Gold Wing motorcycles, the bolt that holds the main drive gear onto the crankshaft can snap. If it breaks, the engine can stall and the rear wheel can lock up. On a motorcycle, a rear wheel that suddenly stops turning can put you on the ground, and it can happen while you are riding. Honda mailed owner letters on December 30, 2024. A dealer will install a new primary drive gear bolt for free.

Honda Goldwing, CBR fuel pumps can fail and stall high

Reported 2024-02-15 · Campaign 24V113000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

The fuel pump inside the gas tank has a spinning part that may have been molded wrong. Over time it can warp and jam, so the pump stops sending gas to the engine. If that happens while you're riding, the engine can quit without warning, which is dangerous on a motorcycle in traffic. A Honda dealer will check the fuel pump and put in a new one if needed, at no cost to you.

Honda Gold Wing software error can stall engine medium

Reported 2022-06-23 · Campaign 22V449000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Certain 2020-2022 Honda GL1800 Gold Wing motorcycles with manual transmissions have an error in the software that runs the engine computer. The spark timing is off, which can make the engine lose power and can cause it to stall. A motorcycle that stalls without warning, especially in traffic or mid-turn, puts the rider at risk of a crash. Your Honda dealer will reload the engine computer with corrected software at no cost.
