Honda Passport recalls

16 recalls covering model years 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Honda, Acura Rear Subframe May Corrode And Fail high

Reported 2026-06-04 · Campaign 26V365000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

On these vehicles, the rear subframe can corrode at the points where the suspension is mounted. If this happens, rear suspension parts, such as the rear control arm, can fail. This affects vehicles sold in a number of states. Dealers will inspect the rear subframe and install a reinforcement kit, and repair or replace subframe components as needed, free of charge. A final remedy is expected later, and additional letters will follow once it's ready.

Honda and Acura front passenger airbags may deploy unintentionally high

Reported 2026-05-21 · Campaign 26V332000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Honda is recalling certain Honda and Acura vehicles, including the Accord, Civic, CR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Ridgeline, HR-V, Fit, Insight, Passport and the Acura TLX, RDX and MDX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short circuit. If that happens, the airbags can go off when they should not during a crash. Dealers will replace the seat weight sensors at no cost. This recall expands an earlier recall.

Honda Pilot and Passport fuel filler pipe may leak critical

Reported 2024-11-27 · Campaign 24V900000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

The tube that leads from the fuel filler opening to the gas tank, and the fuel filler pipe it connects to, may separate from each other on these Honda vehicles. When that happens, gasoline can leak out. Dealers will inspect the fuel filler neck tube and pipe and repair them as needed, at no charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 7, 2025.

Honda Passport, Ridgeline Steering Gearbox Can Loosen high

Reported 2024-03-07 · Campaign 24V184000 · Model years: 2023

Inside the steering gearbox is a ball joint housing that connects to the steering rack. On some of these vehicles, that housing can loosen and come apart from the rack. If the housing separates from the rack, you can lose the ability to steer the vehicle. Losing steering control while driving makes it much harder to keep the vehicle going where you want it to go, which raises the chance of a crash.

Honda and Acura passenger seat sensor may crack high

Reported 2024-02-01 · Campaign 24V064000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Honda is recalling certain 2020 through 2022 Honda and Acura vehicles, including Accord, Civic, CR-V, HR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Passport, Ridgeline, Fit, Insight and the Acura MDX, RDX and TLX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short out. When that happens, the system may not switch off the front passenger airbag when it is supposed to.

Honda and Acura Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported 2023-12-18 · Campaign 23V858000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

In a wide range of Honda and Acura vehicles, the fuel pump located inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails while you are driving, the engine can suddenly stall.

Honda, Acura Models: Brake Master Cylinder May Separate high

Reported 2023-06-29 · Campaign 23V458000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

A fastener that connects the brake booster to the brake master cylinder may not have been put together correctly when the vehicle was built. Over time, this can cause the master cylinder to come apart from the booster assembly.

Honda Odyssey, Pilot and Passport Rearview Camera Problem medium

Reported 2023-06-15 · Campaign 23V431000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

A faulty cable connector in these vehicles can cause the rearview camera image to not show up on your dashboard screen when you put the car in reverse. This means you may not be able to see what's behind your vehicle through the camera display. Dealers will fix this by replacing the cable and adding a cover to protect the connector, at no charge to you.

Honda Pilot, Ridgeline, Passport, Odyssey side mirror glass may fall off medium

Reported 2023-03-16 · Campaign 23V174000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Honda is recalling certain Pilot, Ridgeline, Passport and Odyssey vehicles. The heating pads sitting behind both side-view mirrors may not be bonded on properly. Because of that, the mirror glass can come loose and separate from the mirror. With the glass gone, these vehicles do not meet the federal rules for rear visibility. The recall covers the mirrors on both the left and the right side of the vehicle.

Honda Hood Latch May Open While Driving high

Reported 2021-11-29 · Campaign 21V932000 · Model years: 2019

On these Honda vehicles, the metal striker that helps keep the hood latch closed can become damaged and separate from the hood. If that happens, the hood can open while you are driving.

Honda, Acura fuel pump may fail, stall engine high

Reported 2021-03-25 · Campaign 21V215000 · Model years: 2019

On many Honda and Acura models, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven.

Honda Passport, Pilot Tires May Lose Air Suddenly high

Reported 2021-03-11 · Campaign 21V165000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On some Passport and Pilot SUVs equipped with certain Continental tires, one or more tires may have spent too long in the curing process during manufacturing. This can lead a tire's sidewall to develop a break, causing sudden air loss, or can cause the tread and belt to separate from the tire. Either problem can make the vehicle harder to control.

Honda Odyssey, Passport, Pilot Instrument Panel Glitch medium

Reported 2020-07-28 · Campaign 20V439000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Honda is recalling certain Odyssey, Passport and Pilot vehicles. A software problem in the instrument panel control module can cause the dashboard to stop showing important information, such as engine oil pressure, vehicle speed and what gear the car is in, until the vehicle is turned off and back on. The same problem can also prevent the rearview camera image from showing up on the screen.

Honda Pilot, Odyssey and Passport rearview cameras may lag medium

Reported 2020-07-28 · Campaign 20V440000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Honda is recalling certain Pilot, Odyssey and Passport vehicles because of a programming error in the central network software. The error can cause several faults that delay the rearview camera picture or keep it from appearing on the screen at all. Because the camera image may not show when it should, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard covering rear visibility. Honda has corrected software available for the vehicles that are covered.

Honda Passport, Pilot weight rating label may wipe off low

Reported 2020-02-06 · Campaign 20V067000 · Model years: 2020

On some 2020 Honda Passport and Pilot vehicles, the certification label may have been printed with ink that can be wiped away with a solvent. This can remove the information showing the vehicle's maximum axle and vehicle weight ratings. Without this label information, an owner may not know the vehicle's weight limits and could overload it. Dealers will replace the certification label, free of charge.

Honda Passport and Pilot Incomplete Frame Welding high

Reported 2019-10-31 · Campaign 19V784000 · Model years: 2019

On certain Pilot and Passport vehicles, the upper frame members on the left and right side at the front of the vehicle may not have been fully welded to the body. Because of this, the vehicle may not meet federal safety standards for protecting occupants in a crash and for limiting windshield intrusion. In a crash, incomplete welding may not protect occupants as well as intended, increasing the risk of injury.
