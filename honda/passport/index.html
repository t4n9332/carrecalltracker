Honda Passport recalls

16 recalls covering model years 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Honda, Acura SUVs: rear frame can rust through high

Reported 2026-06-04 · Campaign 26V365000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

These vehicles were sold in states that salt the roads in winter. The rear subframe, the metal frame that holds the rear suspension to the body, can rust badly where the suspension bolts onto it. If it rusts through, a rear suspension part such as a control arm can come loose. The back of the vehicle can then wander or slide and you could lose control. Honda's full repair is not ready yet and is expected in fall 2026.

Honda and Acura front passenger airbags may deploy unintentionally high

Reported 2026-05-21 · Campaign 26V332000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Honda is recalling certain Honda and Acura vehicles, including the Accord, Civic, CR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Ridgeline, HR-V, Fit, Insight, Passport and the Acura TLX, RDX and MDX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short circuit. If that happens, the airbags can go off when they should not during a crash. Dealers will replace the seat weight sensors at no cost. This recall expands an earlier recall.

Honda Pilot and Passport fuel pipe may leak gas critical

Reported 2024-11-27 · Campaign 24V900000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

The pipe you put gas into connects to a neck tube on the fuel tank. On these Passports and Pilots, those two pieces can come apart, and gasoline can leak out. Leaking fuel near anything hot or sparking can start a fire. Dealers will inspect the parts and repair them if needed, free of charge. Letters went to owners in January 2025, so many of these vehicles may already be fixed. If you smell gas, get it checked right away.

Honda Passport and Ridgeline may lose steering control critical

Reported 2024-03-07 · Campaign 24V184000 · Model years: 2023

A ball joint inside the steering gearbox can work loose and come apart from the steering rack. The steering rack is the part that turns your front wheels when you turn the wheel. If that joint separates, you can lose the ability to steer the vehicle. Honda dealers will inspect the steering gearbox and replace it if needed, at no charge. Owner letters went out in April 2024, so the inspection can be done now.

Honda and Acura passenger seat sensor may crack high

Reported 2024-02-01 · Campaign 24V064000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Honda is recalling certain 2020 through 2022 Honda and Acura vehicles, including Accord, Civic, CR-V, HR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Passport, Ridgeline, Fit, Insight and the Acura MDX, RDX and TLX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short out. When that happens, the system may not switch off the front passenger airbag when it is supposed to.

Honda and Acura fuel pump can fail, stalling engine high

Reported 2023-12-18 · Campaign 23V858000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

The fuel pump sits inside the gas tank and sends fuel to the engine. On these vehicles that pump can fail. When it does, the engine can stall while you are driving — losing power in traffic or on the highway raises the chance of a crash. A dealer will replace the entire fuel pump unit for free. This recall is an expansion of two earlier Honda fuel pump recalls covering additional vehicles.

Honda Civic, Pilot and Acura MDX Brake Failure Risk critical

Reported 2023-06-29 · Campaign 23V458000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

A fastener that connects the brake pedal booster to the brake master cylinder may have been put together wrong at the factory. If it fails, the master cylinder can separate from the booster and you could lose the ability to stop the vehicle. A dealer will inspect the brake booster assembly and repair it if needed at no cost. Honda mailed owner letters on August 7, 2023, so the repair is available now.

Honda Odyssey, Pilot and Passport rearview camera may fail medium

Reported 2023-06-15 · Campaign 23V431000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

When you shift into reverse, a camera is supposed to show what is behind you on the dashboard screen. In these vehicles, a connector on the cable that carries the camera picture can go bad, so the screen may show nothing at all. Without that view, it is easier to miss a child, a pet, or an object behind you while backing up. The dealer will replace the cable and add a cover that holds the connector straight.

Honda SUVs and vans: side mirror glass can fall off medium

Reported 2023-03-16 · Campaign 23V174000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

The glass in both outside mirrors has a small heating pad glued behind it to clear fog and frost. On these vehicles the glue may not hold properly, so the mirror glass can come loose and fall off, including while you are driving. Without a side mirror you cannot see traffic beside and behind you, which makes changing lanes and merging much riskier. The dealer will replace both the left and right outside mirrors for free.

Honda Pilot, Passport, Ridgeline hood can fly open high

Reported 2021-11-29 · Campaign 21V932000 · Model years: 2019

On certain Honda Passport, Pilot and Ridgeline vehicles, the metal catch on the hood, the piece the latch grabs onto, can become damaged and separate from the hood. If that happens, the hood is no longer held down and can fly up while you are driving. With the hood in the way you cannot see the road ahead, which can easily lead to a crash. Dealers will repair the catch or replace the entire hood if it is too damaged.

Honda and Acura fuel pump can fail and stall engine high

Reported 2021-03-25 · Campaign 21V215000 · Model years: 2019

The pump that pushes gasoline out of the fuel tank toward the engine sits inside the tank, and on these Honda and Acura vehicles it can stop working. When it fails, the engine can stall, meaning it shuts off while you are driving, and that can happen at any speed. Your dealer will replace the whole fuel pump assembly at no cost to you. This recall is a larger version of an earlier Honda recall, 20V-314.

Honda Pilot and Passport tires may fail suddenly critical

Reported 2021-03-11 · Campaign 21V165000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Some of these Passports and Pilots left the factory with Continental tires that were heated too long while they were being made. Tires cooked that way can crack in the sidewall or come apart inside. That can mean a tire suddenly goes flat, or the tread peels off the tire while you are driving, either of which can make the vehicle very hard to control. A dealer will look at all four tires and replace any bad ones at no cost.

Honda Pilot, Odyssey, Passport backup camera may not show medium

Reported 2020-07-28 · Campaign 20V440000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Software in the vehicle's main computer network was programmed incorrectly, so the backup camera picture can be slow to appear or may not appear at all when you shift into reverse. Without that view you can miss a child, a pedestrian, or an object directly behind the vehicle. Honda will mail instructions so you can download and install the update yourself for free, or a dealer will do it for you at no charge.

Honda Odyssey, Passport, Pilot dashboard and backup camera go blank medium

Reported 2020-07-28 · Campaign 20V439000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

A software error in the module that runs the dashboard can make the display go blank or leave out important information. You may lose the speedometer, the oil pressure warning, and the indicator showing which gear the car is in, and the backup camera picture may not appear. The information comes back the next time you start the car. A dealer reprograms the software to fix it.

Honda Passport and Pilot weight rating label may wipe off low

Reported 2020-02-06 · Campaign 20V067000 · Model years: 2020

The certification sticker on these vehicles was printed with ink that can wipe off if it comes into contact with a solvent or cleaner. That sticker lists how much weight the vehicle and each axle can safely carry. If those numbers rub away, you have no way to check the limits and could load the vehicle too heavily without knowing it. Dealers will put on a new label at no charge.

Honda Pilot and Passport may have missing frame welds high

Reported 2019-10-31 · Campaign 19V784000 · Model years: 2019

The upper frame members at the front of the vehicle may not have been fully welded to the body when the vehicle was built. Those welds help absorb and spread out the force of a front-end crash, so a vehicle missing them may not protect the people inside as well as it should. A dealer will inspect your vehicle for missing welds free of charge. If welds are missing, Honda will offer to buy the vehicle back or give you a similar replacement vehicle.
