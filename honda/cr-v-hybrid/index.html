Honda CR-V Hybrid recalls

Honda Hybrid Models: Tire Repair Kit Bottle Hazard high

Reported Jun 4, 2026 · Campaign 26V366000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

On some Accord Hybrid and CR-V Hybrid vehicles, using the tire repair kit can cause pressure to build inside the sealant bottle. That pressure can cause the bottle cap to detach while someone is using the kit.

Honda and Acura front passenger airbags may deploy unintentionally high

Reported May 21, 2026 · Campaign 26V332000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Honda is recalling certain Honda and Acura vehicles, including the Accord, Civic, CR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Ridgeline, HR-V, Fit, Insight, Passport and the Acura TLX, RDX and MDX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short circuit. If that happens, the airbags can go off when they should not during a crash. Dealers will replace the seat weight sensors at no cost. This recall expands an earlier recall.

Honda CR-V Hybrid Fuel Hose May Leak Fuel critical

Reported Jun 5, 2025 · Campaign 25V378000 · Model years: 2025

On certain 2025 Honda CR-V Hybrid vehicles, the fuel feed hose may not have been installed correctly. Because it wasn't installed the right way, fuel could leak out of the hose. As a result, these vehicles don't meet federal fuel system safety requirements. If fuel leaks and there's something nearby that could ignite it, this increases the risk of a fire.

Honda Accord and CR-V Hybrid Fuel Pump May Leak critical

Reported Oct 14, 2024 · Campaign 24V763000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

Certain 2023-2024 Honda Accord and Accord Hybrid, 2023-2025 CR-V Hybrid, and 2025 Civic and Civic Hybrid vehicles have a high-pressure fuel pump that may crack. A cracked pump can leak fuel. This means fuel that should stay inside the fuel system can leak out instead, rather than being delivered to the engine the way it normally would.

Honda CR-V Hybrid Battery May Spark or Lose Power critical

Reported Oct 4, 2024 · Campaign 24V745000 · Model years: 2023

On certain Honda CR-V Hybrid vehicles, the high-voltage battery modules may not have been manufactured correctly. This can cause a terminal or connector inside the battery to break and spark, which increases the risk of injury or fire. The same problem can also cause the vehicle to suddenly lose drive power while you are driving, which increases the risk of a crash.

Honda and Acura Steering Gearbox Can Cause Difficulty Steering medium

Reported Oct 3, 2024 · Campaign 24V744000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

Honda says the steering gearbox in these vehicles may not have been manufactured correctly. This defect can create too much internal friction inside the gearbox assembly. That extra friction can make the steering feel harder to turn, so drivers may have more difficulty steering the vehicle than they should. Honda is recalling the vehicles to fix this steering gearbox problem so the issue is corrected properly.

Honda and Acura passenger seat sensor may crack high

Reported Feb 1, 2024 · Campaign 24V064000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Honda is recalling certain 2020 through 2022 Honda and Acura vehicles, including Accord, Civic, CR-V, HR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Passport, Ridgeline, Fit, Insight and the Acura MDX, RDX and TLX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short out. When that happens, the system may not switch off the front passenger airbag when it is supposed to.

Honda and Acura Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported Dec 18, 2023 · Campaign 23V858000 · Model years: 2020

In a wide range of Honda and Acura vehicles, the fuel pump located inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails while you are driving, the engine can suddenly stall.

Honda CR-V Hybrid Battery Cable Fire Risk high

Reported Dec 13, 2023 · Campaign 23V844000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

On certain 2020-2022 CR-V Hybrid vehicles, a 12-volt battery cable that runs outside the vehicle's frame is missing a fuse on its power circuit. Without that fuse, the cable can short-circuit or overheat if the vehicle is involved in a crash. Honda is recalling these vehicles to correct this. The missing fuse means the power circuit for this cable does not have the protection it should have, which becomes a problem specifically if the vehicle experiences a crash.

Honda Accord and CR-V Hybrid Transmission May Fail high

Reported Aug 17, 2023 · Campaign 23V588000 · Model years: 2023

Honda is recalling certain CR-V Hybrid and Accord Hybrid vehicles because the electronic transmission may have been manufactured improperly. This can result in a loss of drive power while the vehicle is being driven.

Acura RDX, Honda CR-V Hybrid Driveshaft May Fail high

Reported Dec 2, 2021 · Campaign 21V939000 · Model years: 2022

Honda is recalling certain Acura RDX and CR-V Hybrid vehicles because a part in the front driveshaft, on the right or left side, may not have been heat-treated correctly during manufacturing. If that part fails, it can cause a sudden loss of drive power to the wheels, or let the vehicle roll away on its own if parked without the parking brake applied. Either situation can increase the risk of a crash or injury.

Honda Accord, CR-V, Ridgeline Center Belt May Not Lock high

Reported Nov 18, 2021 · Campaign 21V900000 · Model years: 2022

Honda is recalling certain Accord, Accord Hybrid, CR-V, CR-V Hybrid, Insight and Ridgeline vehicles. The automatic locking feature on the second-row center seat belt may turn off when it should not, and a child seat held by that belt can be left unsecured. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for occupant crash protection. Dealers will replace the second-row center seat belt assembly at no cost to the owner.

Honda Accord, CR-V Hybrid, Insight Power Loss Risk high

Reported Dec 17, 2020 · Campaign 20V798000 · Model years: 2020

Honda says the DC-DC converter in some CR-V Hybrid, Accord Hybrid, and Insight vehicles may shut down on its own. When the converter shuts down, it can stop the 12-volt battery from being recharged while the vehicle is being driven. If the 12-volt battery isn't recharging, the vehicle may end up losing the drive power it needs to keep moving down the road.

Honda CR-V Hybrid recall questions

How many recalls does the Honda CR-V Hybrid have?

NHTSA lists 13 safety recalls covering the 2020–2025 Honda CR-V Hybrid. 12 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Honda Hybrid Models: Tire Repair Kit Bottle Hazard" (NHTSA campaign 26V366000), reported Jun 4, 2026.

Which Honda CR-V Hybrid model year has the most recalls?

The 2020 Honda CR-V Hybrid has the most, with 5 recalls. The 2021 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 3. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Honda CR-V Hybrid?

No. None of the 13 Honda CR-V Hybrid recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Honda CR-V Hybrid has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Honda dealer, including for second owners.
