Honda and Acura front passenger airbags may deploy unintentionally high

Reported May 21, 2026 · Campaign 26V332000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

Honda is recalling certain Honda and Acura vehicles, including the Accord, Civic, CR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Ridgeline, HR-V, Fit, Insight, Passport and the Acura TLX, RDX and MDX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short circuit. If that happens, the airbags can go off when they should not during a crash. Dealers will replace the seat weight sensors at no cost. This recall expands an earlier recall.

Honda and Acura Steering Gearbox Can Cause Difficulty Steering medium

Reported Oct 3, 2024 · Campaign 24V744000 · Model years: 2022

Honda says the steering gearbox in these vehicles may not have been manufactured correctly. This defect can create too much internal friction inside the gearbox assembly. That extra friction can make the steering feel harder to turn, so drivers may have more difficulty steering the vehicle than they should. Honda is recalling the vehicles to fix this steering gearbox problem so the issue is corrected properly.

Honda and Acura passenger seat sensor may crack high

Reported Feb 1, 2024 · Campaign 24V064000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Honda is recalling certain 2020 through 2022 Honda and Acura vehicles, including Accord, Civic, CR-V, HR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Passport, Ridgeline, Fit, Insight and the Acura MDX, RDX and TLX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short out. When that happens, the system may not switch off the front passenger airbag when it is supposed to.

Honda and Acura Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported Dec 18, 2023 · Campaign 23V858000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021

In a wide range of Honda and Acura vehicles, the fuel pump located inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails while you are driving, the engine can suddenly stall.

Honda Civic Repaired Steering Rack May Damage Tire high

Reported Oct 19, 2023 · Campaign 23V704000 · Model years: 2022

Some Honda Civic vehicles that had their power steering rack replaced during an earlier repair may have had the new rack installed incorrectly. This can allow the tire to rub against the lower suspension or the tie rod end, which may damage the tire. A damaged tire can fail while driving, increasing the risk of a crash or injury. Dealers will inspect the steering rack and replace it if needed, free of charge.

Honda, Acura Models: Brake Master Cylinder May Separate high

Reported Jun 29, 2023 · Campaign 23V458000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

A fastener that connects the brake booster to the brake master cylinder may not have been put together correctly when the vehicle was built. Over time, this can cause the master cylinder to come apart from the booster assembly.

Honda, Acura fuel pump may fail, stall engine high

Reported Mar 25, 2021 · Campaign 21V215000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On many Honda and Acura models, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven.

Honda and Acura Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported May 28, 2020 · Campaign 20V314000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Certain Honda and Acura vehicles have a low-pressure fuel pump, inside the fuel tank, that may fail. If the pump fails while you are driving, the engine can stall, which raises the risk of a crash. Honda will replace the fuel pump assembly on affected vehicles, free of charge, as part of this recall.

Honda Civic Owners Guide Missing Required Information high

Reported Nov 16, 2018 · Campaign 18V817000 · Model years: 2018

Some Honda Civic Hatchback and Civic Type R vehicles may not have come with an owner's guide, or the guide that was included may be missing information it's required to have about installing child seats. Because this information may be missing or incomplete, an owner may not have the details needed to correctly use the child seat anchor points in the vehicle.

Honda Civic, CR-V Unintended Power Steering Input high

Reported Sep 27, 2018 · Campaign 18V663000 · Model years: 2018

Honda is recalling certain 2017 and 2018 Civic and CR-V vehicles. These vehicles have a magnet that controls the torque sensor signal for the electric power steering system. This magnet may not be properly secured and can become dislodged. If this happens during a full lock turn, the dislodged magnet may cause the steering assist to apply in the opposite direction.

Honda Civic Certification Label May Show Wrong Date low

Reported Jun 20, 2018 · Campaign 18V421000 · Model years: 2018

The manufacturing date area on some Civic certification labels may show random characters instead of the correct information. That label is used to check whether a vehicle is covered by a safety recall, so having the wrong information there can make it hard for an owner to tell if their vehicle is included in a recall.
