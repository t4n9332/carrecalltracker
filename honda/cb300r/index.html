Honda CB300R headlight may flicker or fail high

Reported Apr 3, 2025 · Campaign 25V210000 · Model years: 2019

On certain CB300R motorcycles, the headlight may flicker or shut off completely while you are riding. Losing headlight illumination makes it harder for you and others to see, which raises the risk of a crash or injury. Dealers will replace the headlight assembly on affected motorcycles at no cost to the owner.

Honda Motorcycle Transmission Part May Come Loose high

Reported Jun 4, 2019 · Campaign 19V412000 · Model years: 2019

On these motorcycles, a small retaining part on the transmission's main shaft may come loose. If it does, the gears inside the transmission can become misaligned. This misalignment can cause the transmission to shift out of neutral and into gear on its own when the engine is started, which could make the motorcycle move unexpectedly. It could also cause the transmission and rear wheel to seize while the motorcycle is being ridden.
