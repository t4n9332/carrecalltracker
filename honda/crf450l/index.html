Honda CRF450L horn can break loose and fall off medium

Reported 2019-10-03 · Campaign 19V703000 · Model years: 2019

On certain 2019 Honda CRF450L motorcycles, the bracket that holds the horn can break. If it does, the horn comes loose from the bike. A dangling horn can get in the way while you ride and make the motorcycle harder to handle, or it can drop off completely and land in the road, where it becomes a hazard for you and for anyone riding or driving behind you. Honda dealers will put in a new horn and a new horn mount at no cost.
