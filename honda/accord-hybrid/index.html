Honda Hybrid Models: Tire Repair Kit Bottle Hazard high

Reported Jun 4, 2026 · Campaign 26V366000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

On some Accord Hybrid and CR-V Hybrid vehicles, using the tire repair kit can cause pressure to build inside the sealant bottle. That pressure can cause the bottle cap to detach while someone is using the kit.

Honda and Acura front passenger airbags may deploy unintentionally high

Reported May 21, 2026 · Campaign 26V332000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

Honda is recalling certain Honda and Acura vehicles, including the Accord, Civic, CR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Ridgeline, HR-V, Fit, Insight, Passport and the Acura TLX, RDX and MDX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short circuit. If that happens, the airbags can go off when they should not during a crash. Dealers will replace the seat weight sensors at no cost. This recall expands an earlier recall.

Honda and Acura Driver Seats May Be Loose high

Reported Jan 29, 2026 · Campaign 26V054000 · Model years: 2023

Some Honda Accord, Accord Hybrid, HR-V, Pilot, and Acura Integra vehicles may have a driver's seat cushion frame that was not tightened properly. This can leave the seat loose. A loose driver's seat may not properly hold the driver in place during a crash, which increases the risk of injury.

Honda Accord Hybrid Loss of Drive Power high

Reported Nov 13, 2025 · Campaign 25V785000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

Some Honda Accord Hybrid vehicles have a software problem in a computer that helps control the hybrid system. Because of this software error, that computer's processor may reset itself while you are driving. When the reset happens, the vehicle can suddenly lose the power that drives it forward. Losing drive power unexpectedly while on the road can catch a driver off guard.

Honda and Acura Models Loose Driver's Seat Frame high

Reported Nov 14, 2024 · Campaign 24V859000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

In these vehicles, the frame underneath the driver's seat cushion may not have been tightened properly when the vehicle was built at the factory. Because the frame isn't properly tightened, the driver's seat can end up unsecured instead of being firmly fastened in place the way it should be. This means the seating system in these vehicles does not meet the federal safety standard that covers how seats must be secured.

Honda Accord and CR-V Hybrid Fuel Pump May Leak critical

Reported Oct 14, 2024 · Campaign 24V763000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Certain 2023-2024 Honda Accord and Accord Hybrid, 2023-2025 CR-V Hybrid, and 2025 Civic and Civic Hybrid vehicles have a high-pressure fuel pump that may crack. A cracked pump can leak fuel. This means fuel that should stay inside the fuel system can leak out instead, rather than being delivered to the engine the way it normally would.

Honda and Acura passenger seat sensor may crack high

Reported Feb 1, 2024 · Campaign 24V064000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Honda is recalling certain 2020 through 2022 Honda and Acura vehicles, including Accord, Civic, CR-V, HR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Passport, Ridgeline, Fit, Insight and the Acura MDX, RDX and TLX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short out. When that happens, the system may not switch off the front passenger airbag when it is supposed to.

Honda and Acura Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported Dec 18, 2023 · Campaign 23V858000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

In a wide range of Honda and Acura vehicles, the fuel pump located inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails while you are driving, the engine can suddenly stall.

Honda Accord and CR-V Hybrid Transmission May Fail high

Reported Aug 17, 2023 · Campaign 23V588000 · Model years: 2023

Honda is recalling certain CR-V Hybrid and Accord Hybrid vehicles because the electronic transmission may have been manufactured improperly. This can result in a loss of drive power while the vehicle is being driven.

Honda, Acura Seat Belt Buckles May Not Latch high

Reported Mar 9, 2023 · Campaign 23V158000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Certain Honda and Acura vehicles, including the CR-V, Accord, Accord Hybrid, Odyssey, Insight, and RDX, may have a manufacturing problem with the front seat belts. The buckle's channel may interfere with the release button, which can prevent the seat belt buckle from latching properly. This means the seat belt may not click into place and hold securely the way it is supposed to for the driver or front passenger seat.

Honda Accord, CR-V, Ridgeline Center Belt May Not Lock high

Reported Nov 18, 2021 · Campaign 21V900000 · Model years: 2021

Honda is recalling certain Accord, Accord Hybrid, CR-V, CR-V Hybrid, Insight and Ridgeline vehicles. The automatic locking feature on the second-row center seat belt may turn off when it should not, and a child seat held by that belt can be left unsecured. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for occupant crash protection. Dealers will replace the second-row center seat belt assembly at no cost to the owner.

Honda, Acura fuel pump may fail, stall engine high

Reported Mar 25, 2021 · Campaign 21V215000 · Model years: 2019

On many Honda and Acura models, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven.

Honda Accord, CR-V Hybrid, Insight Power Loss Risk high

Reported Dec 17, 2020 · Campaign 20V798000 · Model years: 2020

Honda says the DC-DC converter in some CR-V Hybrid, Accord Hybrid, and Insight vehicles may shut down on its own. When the converter shuts down, it can stop the 12-volt battery from being recharged while the vehicle is being driven. If the 12-volt battery isn't recharging, the vehicle may end up losing the drive power it needs to keep moving down the road.

Honda Accord, Insight software may disable key systems high

Reported Dec 10, 2020 · Campaign 20V771000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

This recall covers certain Honda Accord Sedan, Accord Hybrid, and Insight vehicles. A software error may cause communication problems between the body control module and other parts of the vehicle, which can lead to malfunctions in things like the windshield wipers and defroster, the rearview camera, exterior lights, the audible warning for a stopped vehicle, and the power windows.

Honda Accord Hybrid recall questions

How many recalls does the Honda Accord Hybrid have?

NHTSA lists 14 safety recalls covering the 2018–2025 Honda Accord Hybrid. 14 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Honda Hybrid Models: Tire Repair Kit Bottle Hazard" (NHTSA campaign 26V366000), reported Jun 4, 2026.

Which Honda Accord Hybrid model year has the most recalls?

The 2023 Honda Accord Hybrid has the most, with 6 recalls. The 2022 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Honda Accord Hybrid?

No. None of the 14 Honda Accord Hybrid recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Honda Accord Hybrid has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Honda dealer, including for second owners.
