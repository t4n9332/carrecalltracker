Honda CB1000SP motorcycle fuel hose may come loose critical

Reported 2026-03-26 · Campaign 26V194000 · Model years: 2025

On these motorcycles, the hose that carries gasoline from the fuel tank to the engine can come disconnected at the tank. If that happens, gas leaks out while you ride. The fuel can spill near the rear tire, which can make you lose control, and gasoline near anything hot or sparking can catch fire. A dealer will replace the fuel hose and secure a nearby wiring harness so the hose stays put.

Honda CB1000SP motorcycle engine may seize or leak oil high

Reported 2025-12-18 · Campaign 25V892000 · Model years: 2025

Some 2025 Honda CB1000SP motorcycles were built with pistons and piston rings that were not made correctly. Over time this can wear out an internal engine bearing. If that bearing fails, the engine can lock up and stop the rear wheel from turning, or engine oil can leak out onto the rear tire. Oil can also drip onto the hot exhaust. Honda has told owners about the danger, but the actual fix is not ready yet.

Honda CB1000SP Hornet shift lever bolt may fall out high

Reported 2025-10-23 · Campaign 25V723000 · Model years: 2025

On these Honda CB1000SP Hornet SP motorcycles, the bolt that the gearshift pedal pivots on can work its way loose and fall out. If that happens while you are riding, you cannot change gears at all — you would be stuck in whichever gear you were already in, with no way to shift up or down. Being unable to shift in traffic or on the highway can easily lead to a crash. A dealer will replace the pivot bolt.
