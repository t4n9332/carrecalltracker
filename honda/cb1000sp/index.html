Honda CB1000SP Fuel Hose May Disconnect and Leak critical

Reported Mar 26, 2026 · Campaign 26V194000 · Model years: 2025

On some Honda CB1000SP motorcycles, the hose that carries fuel to the engine may come loose from the fuel tank. This can cause fuel to leak out, including near the rear tire.

Honda CB1000SP Piston Defect Risks Rear Wheel Lockup critical

Reported Dec 18, 2025 · Campaign 25V892000 · Model years: 2025

Some CB1000SP motorcycles have pistons and piston rings that may not have been manufactured correctly. This can lead to rod bearing failure, which can cause the rear wheel to lock up while riding, or cause engine oil to leak onto the rear tire.

Honda CB1000SP Gearshift Pedal Bolt May Loosen high

Reported Oct 23, 2025 · Campaign 25V723000 · Model years: 2025

Certain Honda CB1000SP Hornet SP motorcycles may have a problem with the pivot bolt on the gearshift pedal. This bolt could loosen over time and even detach completely, which would leave the rider unable to shift gears while riding. This means the motorcycle doesn't meet a federal safety standard covering motorcycle controls and displays. Dealers will replace the pivot bolt to fix this, free of charge.
