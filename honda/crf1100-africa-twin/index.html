Honda Africa Twin DCT Software May Stall Engine high

Reported 2022-09-01 · Campaign 22V662000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

American Honda is recalling certain CRF1100 Africa Twin motorcycles equipped with a dual-clutch transmission. A software error in the engine's control computer can disconnect internal communication between systems and release the clutch on its own, without the rider doing anything. When that happens, the engine can stall, or the motorcycle can lose the power it needs to keep moving forward under its own power.
