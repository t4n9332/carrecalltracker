What are the Hyundai door and trunk latch recalls about?

3 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Latches/locks/linkages · model years 2018–2020 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-03-28

Two of these three Hyundai recalls concern the same trunk problem: a trunk latch that may become damaged and prevent the trunk from being opened from the inside, a failure to meet Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 401, "Internal Trunk Release." One covers certain 2017-2018 Sonata, 2016-2018 Sonata Hybrid, 2018-2020 Accent and 2016-2017 Azera vehicles; the other covers certain 2020 Sonata and Sonata Hybrid and 2019 Genesis G70 vehicles. Sonata appears in both. The third is different: on certain 2019 Veloster vehicles, the driver's door latch housing and locking cable can be damaged in certain side impact crashes, allowing the driver's side door to open.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2019 Veloster notice says the door opening could result in occupant ejection or increase the risk of injury in the event of a crash. Both trunk latch notices say a person inside the trunk compartment may become trapped, increasing their risk of injury. Beyond directing owners to dealers for the repair, the notices give no instruction to follow before the work is done.

What the free repair involves

For the 2019 Veloster, dealers replace the driver's side door latch assembly and lower channel. For the 2017-2018 Sonata, Sonata Hybrid, Accent and Azera recall, dealers replace the trunk latch base. For the 2020 Sonata, Sonata Hybrid and 2019 Genesis G70 recall, dealers inspect the trunk latch and replace the trunk latch base as necessary. All three notices say the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Which Hyundai models are in the trunk latch recalls?

One covers certain 2017-2018 Sonata, 2016-2018 Sonata Hybrid, 2018-2020 Accent and 2016-2017 Azera vehicles. The other covers certain 2020 Sonata and Sonata Hybrid and 2019 Genesis G70 vehicles. Both are recorded as failures to meet the internal trunk release standard.

Is the 2019 Veloster door latch a problem only in a crash?

The notice says the driver's door latch housing and locking cable can become damaged in certain side impact crashes, allowing the driver's side door to open, which could result in occupant ejection or increase injury risk in a crash. It describes no other condition.

Should I wait for a letter before booking the repair?

The notices give dates: the Veloster recall began June 17, 2019; letters for the Sonata, Accent and Azera trunk latch recall were mailed December 4, 2021, and for the 2020 Sonata and Genesis G70 recall May 26, 2022. Dealers perform each repair free of charge.
