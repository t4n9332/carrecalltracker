Why are Dodge airbag recalls covering Durango and Charger models?

3 NHTSA recalls · Dodge · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 14, 2024

Dodge's airbag recalls in this group are few but different in kind. The 2020-2021 Durango recall (Y16) covers a Side Air Bag Inflatable Curtain fastener that may be loose or missing, which the notice says may cause the curtain airbag to deploy incorrectly. The 2018-2021 Charger recall (19B), shared with the Chrysler 300, covers right and left side curtain inflators that may rupture due to a manufacturing defect, which the notice says can send sharp metal fragments at occupants. The third, on the 2019 Charger (V35), is a labeling compliance issue: the driver and passenger sun visors may lack the required airbag warning information under FMVSS 208. Side curtain hardware is the repeating theme, and the Charger appears in two of the three.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That includes the 2018-2021 Charger and Chrysler 300 side curtain inflator recall (19B), where the notice says a rupture may send sharp metal fragments striking occupants and result in injury or death, the 2020-2021 Durango fastener recall (Y16), and the 2019 Charger sun visor labeling recall (V35). No interim instruction appears in any of the three notices, which describe only the dealer repair for their own vehicles.

What the free repair involves

Dealers tighten or replace the loose or missing side curtain fastener on 2020-2021 Durango vehicles (Y16), replace both side curtain air bags on 2018-2021 Charger and Chrysler 300 vehicles (19B), and replace the driver and passenger sun visors on the 2019 Charger (V35). Each notice states the work is free of charge. No over-the-air update applies in this group.

Common questions

Is my Dodge Charger covered by the side curtain inflator recall?

Recall 19B covers certain 2018-2021 Dodge Charger and Chrysler 300 vehicles, where the right and left side curtain air bag inflators may rupture. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning February 20, 2025; a VIN check with a dealer or FCA at 1-800-853-1403 confirms coverage.

What is wrong with the Durango side curtain airbag?

On certain 2020-2021 Durango vehicles, the Side Air Bag Inflatable Curtain fastener may be loose or missing. The notice says this may cause the side curtain air bag to deploy incorrectly, increasing the risk of injury. Dealers tighten or replace the fastener, free of charge.

Why would a sun visor count as an airbag recall?

On certain 2019 Chargers, the driver and passenger sun visors may lack the required air bag warning information, so the vehicles do not comply with FMVSS 208. The notice says missing warning information increases the risk of injury, and dealers replace both visors free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
