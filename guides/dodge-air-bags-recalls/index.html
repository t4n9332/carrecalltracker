Why are Dodge Charger and Durango air bags being recalled?

3 NHTSA recalls · Dodge · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-03-14

Three Dodge air bag recalls cover different defects and vehicles. A 2020-2021 Dodge Durango recall covers a side curtain air bag fastener that may be loose or missing, which can cause the air bag to deploy incorrectly. A separate recall for 2018-2021 Dodge Charger and Chrysler 300 covers side curtain air bag inflators that may rupture due to a manufacturing defect, which the notice says can send sharp metal fragments at occupants. A third, unrelated 2019 Dodge Charger recall covers sun visors that may be missing the required air bag warning label. Charger appears in two of the three recalls, in different model years and for different reasons.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Side curtain air bag fastener may be loose or missing (Durango recall — no listed warning sign)
  • Air bag inflator may rupture and send metal fragments toward occupants (Charger/300 recall)
  • Sun visor missing required air bag warning label, noticeable by inspecting the visor (2019 Charger recall)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. The Durango fastener recall says a loose or missing fastener may cause the side curtain air bag to deploy incorrectly, increasing the risk of injury. The Charger/300 inflator recall says a rupture may send sharp metal fragments at occupants, resulting in injury or death. The sun visor recall says missing warning information increases the risk of injury. None of the three notices otherwise restrict driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers address each recall differently: tightening or replacing the loose air bag fastener on Durango, replacing both side curtain air bags on Charger and 300 with the rupture-prone inflators, and replacing the driver and passenger sun visors on the 2019 Charger. All repairs are free of charge, with owners notified by mail and a Chrysler/FCA customer service number provided.

Common questions

Is the Dodge Charger air bag inflator recall the most serious of these three?

The notice for the 2018-2021 Charger and Chrysler 300 inflator recall describes the risk as inflator rupture sending sharp metal fragments at occupants, which it says can result in injury or death — a more severe stated consequence than the other two recalls.

Does the Durango fastener recall mean the air bag won't deploy at all?

No, the notice says a loose or missing fastener may cause the side curtain air bag to deploy incorrectly, not that it will fail to deploy.

What does the 2019 Charger sun visor recall actually fix?

It replaces the driver and passenger sun visors because they may be missing the air bag warning label required by federal safety standard FMVSS 208 — it's a labeling issue, not a mechanical air bag defect.
