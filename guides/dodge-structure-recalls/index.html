Why does the Dodge Durango rear spoiler keep being recalled?

3 NHTSA recalls · Dodge · Structure · model years 2021–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-13

All three recalls in this group are the same Dodge Durango rear spoiler issue, restated across three notices. In each one, the rear spoiler may not be aligned properly with the roof, which can result in damage to the spoiler during liftgate use, and a damaged spoiler can detach and become a road hazard. Recall Y73, from 2021, covers certain 2021 Durango vehicles. Recall 20A, from 2023, covers certain 2021-2023 Durango vehicles, and its notice states that it is an expansion of recall number 21V-842. Recall 61D, from 2026, again covers certain 2021-2023 Durango vehicles, and its remedy adds aligning the liftgate. No other Dodge model appears in this group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Each says the same thing about the risk: a spoiler damaged during liftgate use can detach and become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash. The notices do not describe any condition that makes detachment more or less likely, and they give no interim instruction — nothing about removing the spoiler, avoiding the liftgate, or limiting speed. They cover only the inspection and repair a dealer performs.

What the free repair involves

Under recalls Y73 and 20A, dealers inspect and replace the rear spoiler as necessary. Under recall 61D, dealers inspect and replace or repair the spoiler and align the liftgate as necessary. All three notices state the work is free of charge. Owner notification letters for 61D are expected to be mailed beginning September 10, 2026.

Common questions

My Durango spoiler was replaced under an earlier recall — does 61D still apply?

Recall 61D covers certain 2021-2023 Dodge Durango vehicles, and its remedy includes aligning the liftgate, which the earlier notices do not mention. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed beginning September 10, 2026, and a dealer can confirm whether your VIN is included.

Which Durango model years do these spoiler recalls cover?

Recall Y73 names certain 2021 Durango vehicles. Recalls 20A and 61D each name certain 2021-2023 Durango vehicles. Falling inside those years does not by itself mean a vehicle is covered, since each recall applies only to certain vehicles, so check your VIN.

What do the notices say happens if the spoiler comes off?

They say a damaged rear spoiler can detach and become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash. They describe no warning sign beforehand, and they do not tell owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside.
