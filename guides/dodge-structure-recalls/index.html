Why Does the Dodge Durango Rear Spoiler Keep Getting Recalled?

3 NHTSA recalls · Dodge · Structure · model years 2021–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-13

All three recalls in this group describe the same defect being expanded over time: the rear spoiler on the Dodge Durango may not be properly aligned with the roof, so using the liftgate can damage it. The first recall, in 2021, covered only the 2021 Durango. A 2023 recall expanded coverage to add the 2022 model year. A further 2026 recall widened it again to include the 2023 model year. In every case the concern described is the same — a damaged spoiler that can detach and become a hazard for other vehicles on the road.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The rear spoiler appearing misaligned or not flush with the roofline
  • Visible damage or looseness on the spoiler after using the liftgate
  • The notices describe no other warning sign before a spoiler could detach

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Each notice describes a rear spoiler that may not be aligned properly with the roof and can be damaged by liftgate use, potentially detaching and becoming a road hazard. For the original 2021 recall, its 2023 expansion, and the 2026 expansion alike, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For all three recalls, dealers inspect the rear spoiler and its alignment with the roof, then replace or repair the spoiler as needed. The 2026 recall also has dealers correct the liftgate alignment. In every case, the inspection and any parts or repairs are provided at no cost to the owner.

Common questions

Why has the Dodge Durango spoiler recall been reissued multiple times?

Chrysler expanded the same spoiler-alignment defect twice: a 2023 recall added the 2022 model year, and a 2026 recall added the 2023 model year, to the original 2021 recall that covered only the 2021 Durango.

Is my 2023 Dodge Durango included in the spoiler recall?

Yes, under the 2026 recall (61D), which expands the spoiler-alignment issue to include 2021-2023 Durango models. Check your VIN with a dealer to confirm.

What actually happens to the spoiler during the repair?

Dealers inspect the rear spoiler's alignment with the roof and replace or repair it as needed; the 2026 recall also has dealers correct the liftgate alignment. All of this is done free of charge.
