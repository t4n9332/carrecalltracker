Which Volvo lighting recalls affect headlights, marker lights and turn signals?

6 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Exterior Lighting · model years 2025–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 6, 2026

Five of these six recalls are Volvo Trucks, and the new VNL (4) and VNR (4) cabs appear again and again. Center roof marker lights that fail to illuminate are recalled twice: once for certain 2026 New VN trucks and again for 2026-2027 VNL (4) and VNR (4) vehicles. Turn signals that may not function properly cover 2025-2027 VNL (4) and 2026-2027 VNR (4); automatic high beams that may not switch to low beam cover 2026 VNR (4) and 2024-2026 VNL (4); and 2025-2026 New VN trucks may lose trailer lights when towing. Four of these are FMVSS 108 noncompliances. The lone car recall is the 2025 EX90, whose headlight shutters may close over the high and low beams while driving.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recalls RVXX2509 and RVXX2608: the center roof marker lights may fail to illuminate.
  • Recall RVXX2507: when towing a trailer, the trailer tail lights and turn signals may fail to illuminate.
  • Recall RVXX2604: the turn signals may not function properly.
  • The November 2025 automatic high beam recall: the automatic high beams may not switch to low beam headlights as intended.
  • Recall R10298: the headlight shutters may close over the high and low beams while driving (2025 EX90).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The automatic high beam recall is the only one describing something done before the full repair: Volvo Trucks says that as an interim action it will disable the AHB feature through an over-the-air update, free of charge. For the two roof marker light recalls, the trailer lighting recall, the turn signal recall and the EX90 headlight shutter recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is free of charge. Dealers replace the center roof marker light in both marker light recalls and replace the trailer body input/output module for the trailer lighting recall. Software covers the rest: dealers update the turn signal indicator software, Volvo updates the EX90 low power controller software manually, and the high beam recall's interim over-the-air update disables the AHB feature.

Common questions

Why were the roof marker lights recalled twice?

The notices do not say the two are related. RVXX2509 covers certain 2026 New VN trucks and RVXX2608 covers 2026-2027 VNL (4) and VNR (4) vehicles; in both, dealers replace the center roof marker light free of charge.

Will the over-the-air update leave my automatic high beams working?

The notice says the over-the-air update is an interim action that disables the automatic high beam feature, free of charge. It does not describe what the final repair is or when it will be available. Owner letters were mailed June 1, 2026.

What does the EX90 recall actually do to the headlights?

Volvo's notice says a software error in the low power controller may cause the headlight shutters to close over the high and low beams while driving. The low power controller software will be updated manually, free of charge; owner letters were mailed March 12, 2025.
