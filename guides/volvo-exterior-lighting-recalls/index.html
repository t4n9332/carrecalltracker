Why are Volvo VNL and VNR trucks recalled for lights so often?

6 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Exterior Lighting · model years 2025–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-06

Five of these six Exterior Lighting recalls involve Volvo Trucks' VNL and VNR (4) models from 2024-2027, and each targets a different light: automatic high beams that don't dim for oncoming traffic, trailer tail lights and turn signals that fail when towing, center roof marker lights that don't illuminate (recalled twice, in November 2025 and again in August 2026), and turn signals that may not work. The sixth recall is unrelated to the trucks: the 2025 EX90, a car, was recalled because a software error can let the headlight shutters close over the headlights while driving.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Automatic high beams that don't switch to low beam for oncoming traffic (VNL/VNR)
  • Trailer tail lights or turn signals that don't come on when towing (VNL)
  • Center roof marker lights that don't illuminate (VNL/VNR, recalled twice)
  • Turn signals that don't work (VNL/VNR)
  • Headlight shutters that close over the high or low beams while driving (EX90)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. As an interim step, Volvo Trucks disabled the automatic high-beam feature with an over-the-air update on the affected VNL and VNR trucks until the permanent fix; the notices for the trailer-light, marker-light (both recalls), turn-signal, and EX90 headlight-shutter recalls also do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the failed hardware — the trailer body input/output module and the center roof marker lights (both recalls) — and reprogram the turn-signal indicator software on affected VNL/VNR trucks. The automatic high-beam issue is addressed with an over-the-air software update, and Volvo updates the EX90's low-power-controller software manually. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why has Volvo recalled VNL/VNR roof marker lights twice?

Two separate recalls, in November 2025 and August 2026, both cover center roof marker lights that can fail to illuminate on VNL and VNR trucks; each is a distinct notice with its own free dealer repair.

Does the EX90 headlight recall affect Volvo trucks too?

No. The EX90 headlight-shutter recall is a separate car recall caused by low-power-controller software; the truck lighting recalls involve different hardware such as marker lights, trailer lights, and turn signals.

Is the automatic high-beam recall fixed by a dealer visit?

As an interim step Volvo Trucks disables the automatic high-beam feature with an over-the-air update; the notice describes this as the interim action while the permanent repair is arranged.
