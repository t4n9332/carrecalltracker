Which BMW body and structure recalls affect pillars, spoilers and panels?

11 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Structure · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 4, 2024

Eleven BMW structure recalls split between crash protection and parts that can come off the vehicle. Two cover B-pillars: 2016-2019 X1 models whose pillar may not absorb enough impact, and 2025 X1 xDrive28i and M35i models with B-pillar reinforcement plates of insufficient strength. Two cover the X3 rear cargo rail attachment, one on 2018-2023 and a second on 2018-2022 models. Rear spoilers account for three, on the 2018 X3 and twice on the 2020 X6. The remainder are one-off: the 2024 X5 and X7 instrument panel, the 2024 XM tailgate panel, the 2020 330i and M340i interior trunk release, and the G310R and G310GS side stand.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The trunk may not open from the inside because the interior trunk release cable was improperly installed (2020 330i, 330i xDrive and M340i trunk release recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these eleven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That covers the B-pillar recalls on the 2016-2019 and 2025 X1, the instrument panel recall on the 2024 X5 and X7, the spoiler recalls on the 2018 X3 and 2020 X6, the XM tailgate panel, the X3 cargo rail recalls, the 330i and M340i trunk release, and the G310R and G310GS side stand. Each notice states the injury or crash risk and points owners to the dealer repair.

What the free repair involves

All of these repairs are free of charge. Dealers replace the B-pillar trim on the 2016-2019 X1, and inspect and replace the B-pillar on the 2025 X1. Elsewhere they replace the instrument panel, the tailgate panel as necessary, the cargo rail attachment bolts, and the spoilers after inspection. The trunk release cable is installed properly, and the motorcycle frame is inspected, then reinforced with a new side stand, or replaced.

Common questions

What does the X3 cargo rail recall actually fix?

The notices say the cargo rail attachment can be damaged in a rear crash and the rail can detach, increasing the risk of injury. Dealers replace the rear cargo rail attachment bolts free of charge on the affected 2018-2023 and 2018-2022 X3 models.

Are the two X1 B-pillar recalls the same problem?

They are separate recalls. The 2016-2019 X1 notice says the B-pillar may not absorb an adequate amount of impact if an occupant's head contacts it. The 2025 X1 notice says the B-pillar reinforcement plates may have insufficient structural strength. Each has its own repair.

Do the notices say a spoiler or panel will actually fall off?

They say it may. The 2018 X3 spoiler may not have been properly attached or may be missing screws, the 2020 X6 spoiler may detach or may not be appropriately bonded to its mounting plate, and the 2024 XM tailgate panel may detach from its mounting structure.
