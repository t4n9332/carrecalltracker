BMW Structure Recalls Cover Loose Panels and Crash Protection Problems

11 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Structure · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 4, 2024

BMW's structure notices repeatedly involve exterior pieces that may detach and interior structures that may provide inadequate protection in a crash. Rear spoiler recalls cover certain 2018 X3 and 2020 X6 vehicles; a separate notice concerns the tailgate panel on certain 2024 XM vehicles. X3 also appears in two cargo rail notices, covering certain 2018-2023 and 2018-2022 vehicles respectively. X1 B-pillar concerns differ by year: certain 2016-2019 vehicles have inadequate interior impact absorption, while certain 2025 vehicles have reinforcement plates with insufficient strength. Other notices concern instrument panels on certain 2024 X5 and X7 vehicles, trunk releases, and G310 motorcycle side stands and frames.

Warning signs to watch for

  • BMW 3 Series and M340i Trunk Release Problem, October 2019: the interior release may not open the trunk from inside.
  • BMW X6 Rear Spoiler May Detach While Driving, August 2020: the roof rear spoiler may become loose or detach while driving.
  • BMW G310 Motorcycles Side Stand Damage, June 2018: the motorcycle may unexpectedly fall over while stationary.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The supplied structure notices contain zero stop-driving instructions and zero park-outside instructions. The notices for G310 side stands, both X1 B-pillar problems, and the X5/X7 instrument panel do not tell owners to stop driving. The 2018 X3 spoiler, October 2019 trunk release, February and August 2020 X6 spoilers, and February 2024 XM tailgate panel notices also do not tell owners to stop driving. Neither the July 2024 nor October 2024 X3 cargo rail notice tells owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

The listed repairs are free of charge. The 2019 X1 recall replaces upper interior B-pillar trim; the 2024 X1 notice specifies B-pillar inspection and replacement if affected. The X5/X7 notice replaces the instrument panel. Both X3 cargo rail notices replace rear cargo rail attachment bolts. The XM notice specifies tailgate panel inspection and replacement as necessary.

Common questions

Do the two X3 cargo rail notices cover the same years?

The July 2024 notice covers certain 2018-2023 X3 vehicles, while the October 2024 notice covers certain 2018-2022 X3 vehicles. Both describe cargo rail attachment damage during a rear crash and specify free replacement of the rear cargo rail attachment bolts.

What is the difference between the X1 B-pillar problems?

The May 2019 notice concerns inadequate impact absorption on certain 2016-2019 X1 vehicles and replaces upper interior trim. The November 2024 notice concerns insufficient reinforcement-plate strength on certain 2025 X1 vehicles and specifies inspection and B-pillar replacement if affected.

How do dealers address the X6 rear spoiler recalls?

The February 2020 notice calls for inspection and rear spoiler replacement as necessary. The August 2020 notice also specifies inspection and replacement if necessary, and describes inadequate bonding to the mounting plate. Both notices provide the remedy free of charge.
