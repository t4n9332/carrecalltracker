Volvo Seat Recalls Involve Loose Attachments and Head Restraint Cushions

3 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Seats · model years 2020–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 9, 2026

Attachment strength is the recurring concern across these Volvo seat recalls, although each notice identifies a different component. RVXX2312 covers certain 2019-2024 VN and VHD trucks with passenger bench seats that may be inadequately attached to the floor. R10292 concerns improperly tightened bolts securing the second-row free-standing seat in certain 2025 EX90 vehicles. R10396 covers head restraint cushions that may detach during a crash in certain 2025-2026 EX30 and 2026 EX30CC vehicles. The truck recall spans the widest model-year range listed here, while the passenger-vehicle notices focus on newer EX90 and EX30 models. Each defect can increase injury risk during a crash.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seat recall notices includes a stop-driving instruction or a park-outside instruction. For RVXX2312, Volvo Truck Passenger Seat May Not Be Secured, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving. For R10292, Volvo EX90 Second-Row Seat Bolts May Be Loose, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving. For R10396, Volvo EX30 Head Restraint Cushion May Detach in Crash, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For RVXX2312, dealers replace the weld nut with two nuts and a washer free of charge. For R10292, dealers inspect and tighten the second-row seat bolts as necessary, free of charge. For R10396, dealers apply an adhesive to reinforce the head cushion attachment free of charge.

Common questions

Which Volvo truck seat is affected by RVXX2312?

RVXX2312 concerns the passenger-side bench seat in certain 2019-2024 VN and VHD vehicles. Its attachment points may fail, allowing the seat to shift during a crash. Dealers replace the weld nut with two nuts and a washer free of charge.

What will the dealer do about loose EX90 seat bolts?

For R10292, dealers inspect the bolts for the second-row free-standing seat in certain 2025 EX90 vehicles and tighten them as necessary, free of charge. The notice says an improperly tightened seat may not adequately restrain an occupant during a crash.

Does the EX30 head restraint recall require cushion replacement?

R10396 specifies applying an adhesive to reinforce the head cushion attachment. It covers certain 2025-2026 EX30 and 2026 EX30CC vehicles because the cushion may detach during a crash. The stated adhesive remedy is free of charge.
