Why are Kia vehicles with tow hitches being recalled for fire risk?

4 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Trailer Hitches · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-17

⚠ 3 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Three separate Kia recalls involve the same root cause: moisture or debris collecting on the tow hitch harness circuit board, which can cause an electrical short and a fire. This pattern repeats across the 2020-2022 Telluride, the 2018-2023 Sorento and Sportage (including Sorento Hybrid and Sorento PHEV), and the 2022-2023 Carnival, whenever the vehicle has a Genuine Kia tow hitch harness installed at the factory or added later at a dealership. A fourth, unrelated recall covers 2023-2025 Sportage models with a 4.2-inch instrument panel screen paired with a tow hitch harness accessory: a software error can make that screen fail, not a fire risk.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The three tow hitch fire recalls (Telluride, Sorento/Sportage, Carnival) describe no specific warning sign before a fire starts
  • On 2023-2025 Sportage models, the instrument panel screen may fail and stop showing the speedometer
  • A failed instrument panel screen can also stop showing warning lights

Is it safe to keep driving?

Three of these four recalls carry a park-outside instruction: the 2020-2022 Telluride, the 2018-2023 Sorento and Sportage recall, and the 2022-2023 Carnival all tell owners to park outside and away from structures until the tow hitch harness repair is done, because the described short circuit can start a fire while the vehicle is parked or being driven. The 2023-2025 Sportage instrument panel recall does not carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; that notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across the tow hitch fire recalls, dealers inspect the harness and, as needed, install a new fuse and wiring extension or replace the tow hitch harness module. For the 2023-2025 Sportage instrument panel recall, dealers replace the tow hitch harness control module. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Kia models are covered by the tow hitch fire recalls?

The Telluride (2020-2022), Sorento and Sorento Hybrid/PHEV (2018-2023), Sportage (2018-2022), and Carnival (2022-2023) are covered when equipped with a Genuine Kia tow hitch harness, which can short and start a fire from moisture or debris.

Is the 2023-2025 Sportage screen recall related to the fire risk?

No. That recall is a separate software error that can make the instrument panel screen fail, blocking the speedometer and warning lights. Dealers replace the tow hitch harness control module; it is not described as a fire risk.

Do I need to stop driving my Kia because of these recalls?

None of the four notices tell owners to stop driving. Three instruct parking outside and away from structures until the tow hitch harness repair is complete; the Sportage screen recall carries no such instruction.
