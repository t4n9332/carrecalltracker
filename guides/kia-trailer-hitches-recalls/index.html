Kia tow hitch harness recalls: fire risk and dashboard failures

4 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Trailer Hitches · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 17, 2025

⚠ 3 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

All four recalls here involve a Kia tow hitch harness, installed as original equipment or purchased as an accessory through a Kia dealership. Three are fire recalls with the same theme: debris and moisture on the tow hitch harness printed circuit board can cause an electrical short on 2020-2022 Telluride and on 2016-2022 Sorento, 2021-2022 Sorento Hybrid, 2022-2023 Sorento Plug-In Hybrid and 2017-2022 Sportage, while water accumulation on that board does the same on 2022-2023 Carnival. The fourth, SC359, is different: a software error can make the instrument panel screen fail on 2023-2025 Sportage Plug-In Hybrid and 2024-2025 Sportage vehicles with a 4.2-inch screen. Sportage and Sorento carry the widest year spans.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An instrument panel screen that fails, so the speedometer or warning lights are not displayed (Sportage recall SC359).
  • The notices for the three tow hitch harness fire recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Three of these four recalls carry a park-outside instruction. Owners of 2020-2022 Telluride vehicles are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete; the same instruction applies to the Sorento and Sportage tow hitch harness recall and to the 2022-2023 Carnival recall, where owners are told to park outside and away from structures until the remedy is completed. None of the three tells owners to stop driving. For SC359, the Sportage instrument panel screen recall, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers install a new fuse and wiring harness extension and inspect and replace the tow hitch harness assembly as necessary on Telluride; inspect and replace the fuse and/or harness module on Sorento and Sportage; verify the hitch and install a new fuse and wire extension kit on Carnival; and replace the tow hitch harness control module under SC359. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

I never tow anything. Does my Kia still fall under these recalls?

These recalls cover vehicles equipped with a Kia tow hitch harness, whether it came as original equipment or was purchased as an accessory through a Kia dealership. Towing is not the trigger; having the harness is. A dealer can confirm your VIN.

Where should I park until the tow hitch harness repair is done?

The Telluride, Sorento/Sportage and Carnival tow hitch harness notices all advise parking outside and away from structures until the recall repair or remedy is completed. None of them tells owners to stop driving, and they give no other interim instruction.

Why does a tow hitch recall affect my Sportage instrument panel screen?

SC359 covers 2023-2025 Sportage Plug-In Hybrid and 2024-2025 Sportage vehicles that have a 4.2-inch screen and a Genuine Kia tow hitch harness accessory. The notice attributes the screen failure to a software error, and dealers replace the tow hitch harness control module free of charge.
