Why Do Lincoln Vehicles Keep Rolling Away Or Losing Drive Power?

12 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Power Train · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-24

Lincoln's power train recalls in this group are dominated by two repeating problems: vehicles that can roll away despite showing 'Park,' and vehicles that lose drive power unexpectedly. Rollaway causes vary by recall: a missing roll pin on the 2018 Lincoln Navigator, loose park-gear bolts on the 2023 Lincoln Aviator, a fractured axle pinion on the 2023 Lincoln Navigator, a park pawl that can engage while moving on the 2018-2021 Lincoln Navigator and 2020-2021 Lincoln Aviator, and a missing park-release cover on the 2020 Lincoln Aviator. Drive-power loss recurs on the 2021-2022 Lincoln Corsair, recalled for a hybrid control module software defect in 2024 and again in 2025 after the first repair failed, and separately affects the 2025 Lincoln Aviator through loose transmission bolts and a mismachined valve body.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The vehicle rolling away after being parked, even though the shifter and display show 'Park'
  • A sudden loss of drive power or an unexpected shift into neutral while driving
  • Reverse gear failing to engage or unexpected forward movement in reverse or neutral
  • Loss of four-wheel drive or the front wheels locking up
  • A transmission fluid leak, which the notices say can increase fire risk near an ignition source

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the 12 power train recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe risks including vehicle rollaway, loss of drive power, unexpected shift to neutral, reverse gear failure, loss of four-wheel drive, and fluid leaks that can increase fire risk, but none instruct owners to stop driving. One recall, covering the transmission park system, was still awaiting a permanent remedy as of its July 2026 interim notice, though that notice also does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Depending on the recall, dealers inspect and replace parts such as the start/stop accumulator, driveshaft, roll pin, rear axle unit, transmission, axle pinion gear set, or transmission valve body, and/or update the powertrain or hybrid control module software. All repairs described across these 12 recalls are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why does the Lincoln Corsair keep getting recalled for shifting to neutral?

The 2021-2022 Corsair was recalled in 2024 for a hybrid control module software defect causing unexpected shifts to neutral, then recalled again in 2025 after Ford found the first repair had been performed incorrectly on some vehicles.

Can my Lincoln really roll away while it's in Park?

Several separate recalls in this group - covering different Navigator, Aviator, and Explorer model years - describe causes such as a missing roll pin, loose bolts, or a park pawl that can engage while moving, each of which can let the vehicle roll away.

Do any of these Lincoln power train recalls tell owners to stop driving?

No. None of the 12 recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, though the notices describe risks such as vehicle rollaway, loss of drive power, and loss of four-wheel drive.
