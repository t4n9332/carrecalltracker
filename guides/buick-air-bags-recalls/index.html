Buick air bag recalls: which models and what goes wrong

5 NHTSA recalls · Buick · AIR Bags · model years 2019–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 31, 2022

Five recalls cover Buick air bags, and three nameplates appear: Enclave, Encore and Envision. Roof rail air bags are the most repeated theme. The 2020 Enclave is included because the diffuser of the Roof-Rail Air Bag inflator may not have been properly crimped and could separate during deployment, and the 2021-2022 Enclave because the harness connector to the left or right roof rail air bag may have incompatible electrical terminals. Frontal deployment comes up twice: the 2019 Encore, whose Sensing Diagnostic Module may not have been turned off of manufacturing mode, and the 2021 Enclave, which may have received incorrect air bag calibration software during service by dealers. The 2021 Envision stands apart, involving the air bag warning light.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The air bag malfunction indicator light may illuminate inconsistently and may fail to warn the driver of a problem with the air bag — 2021 Buick Envision communications gateway module recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. The notices for the 2019 Encore, the 2020 Enclave, the 2021 Enclave, the 2021-2022 Enclave and the 2021 Envision each describe the risk in crash terms: air bags that may not deploy, roof rail air bags whose performance may be degraded, or a warning light that may not alert the driver. Beyond having the free remedy performed, the notices give owners no other instruction about driving.

What the free repair involves

Every repair here is free of charge. Dealers reprogram the Sensing Diagnostic Module on the 2019 Encore, replace the suspect roof-rail air bag modules on the 2020 Enclave, update the air bag calibration software on the 2021 Enclave, and replace the harness connector to the roof rail air bags on the 2021-2022 Enclave. The 2021 Envision gateway module software is updated by a dealer or over the air.

Common questions

Which Buick models are in these air bag recalls?

Three Buick nameplates appear across the five recalls: the 2019 Encore, the 2020 Enclave, the 2021 Enclave for incorrect calibration software, the 2021-2022 Enclave for the roof rail air bag harness connector, and the 2021 Envision for the air bag warning light.

Do the notices say anything about continuing to drive?

None of the five notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. They describe air bags that may not deploy or may not perform as intended in a crash and, on the 2021 Envision, a warning light that may fail to warn the driver.

What do dealers do for the roof rail air bag recalls?

For the 2020 Enclave, dealers replace the suspect roof-rail air bag modules. For the 2021-2022 Enclave, dealers replace the harness connector to the roof rail air bags. Both notices state that the work is done free of charge.
