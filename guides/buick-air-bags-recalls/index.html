Why have so many Buick models had air bag recalls?

5 NHTSA recalls · Buick · AIR Bags · model years 2019–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-03-31

Buick has been part of five General Motors air bag recalls, most shared with sibling Chevrolet, GMC, and Cadillac models built on the same platforms. The causes vary: a 2018 recall found the sensing diagnostic module left in 'manufacturing mode' on the Encore, blocking deployment; a 2020 recall covered a roof-rail air bag diffuser that could separate from its inflator on the Enclave and full-size SUVs and trucks; and two more recent recalls hit the Enclave and Chevrolet Traverse specifically — incorrect air bag calibration software installed during dealer service in 2021, and an incompatible wiring harness connector to the roof-rail air bags in 2022. A fifth recall covers an inconsistent air bag warning light on the Envision.

Warning signs to watch for

  • On the 2021 Buick Envision recall, the air bag malfunction indicator light may turn on and off inconsistently
  • The SDM programming, roof-rail diffuser, wrong calibration software, and wiring connector recalls describe no warning sign a driver would notice
  • Most of these defects are hidden manufacturing or software issues that a driver cannot detect through normal vehicle behavior

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five Buick-related air bag recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the 2018 sensing diagnostic module recall, the 2020 roof-rail diffuser recall, the 2021 wrong calibration software recall, the 2022 wiring connector recall, and the 2021 warning-light recall do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice instead describes how the defect could affect air bag deployment or the warning light in a crash, without directing owners to park the vehicle or avoid driving it until repaired.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, GM dealers reprogram the sensing diagnostic module, replace the roof-rail air bag module, update air bag calibration software, replace the wiring harness connector to the roof-rail air bags, or update the communications gateway module software — the last either at a dealer or via an over-the-air update. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Which Buick models are affected by these air bag recalls?

The Encore (2019), Enclave (2020-2022), and Envision (2021) are the Buick models named across these five recalls. Each recall also affects related Chevrolet, GMC, or Cadillac models built on shared platforms, such as the Traverse, Acadia, and Silverado.

Is the fix free for these Buick air bag recalls?

Yes. GM dealers perform every repair — reprogramming modules, updating software, or replacing parts like the roof-rail air bag or wiring connector — at no cost to the owner, according to each recall notice.

Do any of these recalls tell owners to stop driving?

No. None of the five Buick air bag recalls in this group include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe the defect and the free repair but do not direct owners to stop using the vehicle.
