GMC equipment recalls: block heater fire risk and wrong labels

4 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Equipment · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-02-26

GMC's equipment recalls divide into one fire risk and three information problems. The oldest covers certain 2017-2019 Sierra 2500 and 3500 trucks with a Duramax 6.6 liter diesel engine and an optional engine-block heater cord, where an electrical short-circuit may occur in the heater cable or in the terminals connecting it to the block heater. The other three concern what owners read: a 2023 GMC Acadia certification label whose tire size information may be illegible, a 2021 GMC Canyon Tire and Loading Information placard printed with 22.5 kg (51 lbs.) more load capacity than intended, and 2025 GMC Canyon and 2026 GMC Sierra 3500 vehicles whose radio may not have been set to download the electronic owner's manual.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, and each says owners are not told to stop driving. The notices state the risks in their own terms: an electrical short-circuit in the block heater cable on the 2017-2019 Sierra 2500 and 3500 recall can increase the risk of a fire; illegible tire size information on the 2023 Acadia may result in the wrong tires being installed; the 2021 Canyon placard may lead to unintentional overloading; and a missing owner's manual may leave owners unsure how to operate the vehicle.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the engine block heater and heater cord on the Sierra 2500 and 3500 recall; trucks whose heater was disabled are offered replacements under a separate customer satisfaction program, free of charge. Dealers replace the certification label on the 2023 Acadia and reset the radio on the owner's manual recall, both free. GM mails 2021 Canyon owners a new B-pillar label free, to apply or have a dealer install.

Common questions

Which Sierra trucks are covered by the block heater cord recall?

The notice covers certain 2017-2019 Sierra 2500 and 3500 vehicles equipped with a Duramax diesel 6.6 liter engine and an optional engine-block heater cord. An electrical short-circuit may occur in the cable or its terminals, increasing the risk of a fire.

Do I have to visit a dealer for the 2021 Canyon label recall?

GM mails owners a new B-pillar label, free of charge. The notice says owners may apply the label themselves or bring it to a dealer for installation. Owner notification letters were mailed on August 17, 2021.

Why is a missing owner's manual treated as a safety recall?

The notice says the radio may not have been set to the correct status to download the electronic owner's manual, and that without a manual to consult owners may not know how to safely use and operate the vehicle. Dealers reset the radio free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
