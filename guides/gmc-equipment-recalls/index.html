GMC equipment recalls: wrong labels, missing manuals, and a fire risk

4 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Equipment · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-02-26

These four GMC equipment recalls cover unrelated defects rather than one repeating cause. The earliest, from 2018-2019, affects 2500 and 3500 Duramax diesel Sierra trucks whose optional block heater cord can short-circuit. Two recalls involve incorrect labels: a 2023 GMC Acadia, alongside Cadillac XT5 and XT6, with an illegible certification label, and a 2021 GMC Canyon whose tire and loading placard overstates load capacity by 51 pounds. The newest, covering the 2025 GMC Canyon alongside Cadillac Optiq, Chevrolet Colorado, Equinox EV, and other 2025-2026 GM models, addresses radios that failed to download the required electronic owner's manual.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No warning sign is described for the engine block heater cord's short-circuit risk - it was found through GM's own investigation, not something owners would notice.
  • The certification label on the driver's side B-pillar may be hard to read, especially the tire size information.
  • The load capacity number on the Tire and Loading Information placard may read about 51 lbs higher than intended.
  • The vehicle's electronic owner's manual may never appear on the radio because it failed to download.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four notices carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. GM says the block heater cord's short-circuit risk can increase the risk of a fire, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving. The illegible tire-size label, the overstated load-capacity placard, and the missing electronic owner's manual are addressed with replacement labels and a manual download; none of those three notices tell owners to stop driving either, and none mention a park-outside precaution.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the engine block heater and heater cord on affected Duramax trucks, free of charge, with owners who disabled the heater later offered a free replacement. For the label recalls, dealers replace the certification label, or GM mails a corrected placard for the Canyon. For the manual recall, dealers reset the vehicle's radio so the electronic owner's manual downloads automatically, at no cost.

Common questions

Is the GMC block heater cord recall only for diesel trucks?

Yes. It covers 2018-2019 Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra 2500/3500 trucks built with the 6.6-liter Duramax diesel engine and the optional block heater cord, where a short-circuit in the cord or its terminals can increase fire risk.

What's wrong with the GMC Canyon load capacity label?

GM says the 2021 Canyon's Tire and Loading Information placard lists a load capacity about 51 lbs higher than intended, which could lead an owner to unintentionally overload the truck and increase crash risk.

Why would a GMC need a recall for a missing owner's manual?

GM found some 2025-2026 vehicles, including the Canyon, had radios that didn't trigger the automatic download of the electronic owner's manual, so dealers reset the radio to fix it, free of charge.
