Mercedes-Benz suspension recalls: Sprinter axles, springs and front carrier corrosion

4 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Suspension · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-07-09

Rear axle and spring hardware dominates this small group, and three of the four recalls are Sprinter vans. Two of them cover the same 2019 Sprinter VS30 and both are noncompliances with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 126 for electronic stability control: one for a chassis missing the additional stabilization required for its cargo capacity, the other for incorrect leaf spring shims fitted between the rear axle and the leaf springs. A newer recall covers 2023-2024 Sprinter vans whose rear axle spring U-bolts may have reduced strength from improper heat-treatment. The one passenger-car recall covers 2019-2020 A220, 2020 GLB250 and 2021 AMG GLB35 vehicles, where the integral carrier supporting the front axle may corrode.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the four notices in this group tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. That covers both 2019 Sprinter VS30 recalls, the missing chassis stabilization and the incorrect leaf spring shims, the front axle integral carrier corrosion recall for A220, GLB250 and AMG GLB35 vehicles, and the 2023-2024 Sprinter rear axle spring U-bolt recall. Each notice states the crash risk the defect creates and the free dealer repair, without giving owners any interim instruction about driving.

What the free repair involves

For the missing chassis stabilization, dealers replace the front axle stabilizer bar, replace the shock absorbers and install a rear axle stabilizer bar. For the shim recall they replace the leaf spring shims on both sides, renew the spring brackets, check the rubber buffers and adjust the headlamps. Elsewhere dealers inspect and replace the integral carrier as necessary, or replace the rear spring U-bolts. All free of charge.

Common questions

Which Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans are in these suspension recalls?

Two recalls cover certain 2019 Sprinter VS30 vans, one for missing chassis stabilization and one for incorrect leaf spring shims. A separate recall covers certain 2023-2024 Sprinter vans with rear axle spring U-bolts that may have reduced strength.

Is the GLB35 front axle carrier recall about corrosion?

The notice says the integral carrier that supports the front axle may corrode and fail, which can result in a loss of steering control. Dealers inspect and replace the integral carrier as necessary, free of charge.

Will I notice anything before these suspension parts fail?

The notices do not describe any warning sign. They state that handling may be affected, that the rear axle can shift out of position, or that the front axle carrier can fail, and they do not tell owners to stop driving.
