What suspension problems affect Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and GLB models?

4 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Suspension · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-07-09

Two 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter VS30 recalls addressed suspension components that failed to meet federal electronic stability control standards: one for a chassis missing extra stabilization for its cargo capacity, and one for incorrect rear leaf spring shims. Separately, a 2021 recall covering the AMG GLB35 (plus related A220 and GLB250 models) found the front axle's integral carrier could corrode and fail, and a 2024 recall for the 2023-2024 Sprinter 1500 found rear axle U-bolts with reduced strength from improper heat-treatment. All four recalls describe effects on handling, steering, or axle position.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No specific warning sign is described for the missing chassis stabilization or incorrect leaf spring shims — the notices say only that handling 'could be affected'
  • No warning sign is described before the front axle integral carrier corrodes and fails
  • No warning sign is described before the rear axle U-bolts lose strength and the axle shifts out of position

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four suspension recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. The two 2019 Sprinter VS30 recalls, covering the missing chassis stabilization and the incorrect rear leaf spring shims, the 2021 AMG GLB35 front axle carrier recall, and the 2024 Sprinter rear axle U-bolt recall all describe reduced vehicle control, loss of steering, or an axle shifting out of position as the risk, but none of the notices instructs owners to stop driving while awaiting repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers upgrade the chassis by replacing the front stabilizer bar and shocks and adding a rear stabilizer bar, or replace the rear leaf spring shims and brackets and check rubber buffers, depending on which 2019 Sprinter recall applies. They also inspect and replace the front axle integral carrier on the AMG GLB35, or replace the rear axle spring U-bolts on the 2024 Sprinter. All repairs are free.

Common questions

What's the difference between the two 2019 Sprinter suspension recalls?

One addresses a chassis missing required stabilization for its cargo capacity (stabilizer bars and shocks); the other addresses incorrect rear leaf spring shims. Both were issued the same day and both affect electronic stability control compliance.

Is the GLB35 axle recall related to the Sprinter recalls?

No — it's a separate defect involving corrosion of the front axle's integral carrier, affecting the AMG GLB35 along with related A220 and GLB250 models, and it is unrelated to the Sprinter van suspension recalls above.

What causes the 2024 Sprinter rear axle bolt recall?

Spring U-bolts on the rear axle were improperly heat-treated, reducing their strength and allowing the axle to shift out of position, increasing the risk of a crash.
