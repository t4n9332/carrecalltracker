Why Do So Many Nissan Models Have Fuel System Recalls?

10 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-02-12

Fuel leaks that raise fire risk are the most common thread here, showing up under several different causes: an unlocked fuel tube clip and a disengaged fuel pump lock ring on 2019 Altima, a thin fuel tank wall on 2020 Versa, an unsecured fuel hose and lock ring on 2021 Rogue, a fuel tank wall that's too thin on 2022 Rogue, and a fuel tank punctured during modifications on a separate 2022 Rogue recall. A second pattern is fuel pump failure that can stall the engine, covering 2020-2021 Armada and Infiniti QX80, 2021 Rogue, and 2018-2019 NV200 and City Express vehicles (the last from a damaged sensor harness blowing a fuse). A tenth recall, on 2024-2025 Rogue, involves throttle body gears breaking rather than fuel leaks or pump failure.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A fuel smell or visible fuel leak near the tank, hose, or pump area
  • Engine stalls or won't stay running (fuel hose disconnection, pump failure, or blown fuse recalls)
  • Sudden loss of drive power (2024-2025 Rogue throttle body recall)
  • The notices do not describe an advance warning sign for the thin fuel tank wall or puncture recalls

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the ten fuel-system recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the leak-related recalls — covering the Altima, Versa, and three separate Rogue recalls — say a fuel leak increases the risk of a fire. The pump- and fuse-related recalls covering the Armada, QX80, Rogue, and NV200/City Express say a stall increases crash risk, and the throttle body recall says lost drive power increases crash risk. None of the notices tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Depending on the cause, dealers reposition and lock fuel tube clips or pump lock rings, replace fuel tanks, hoses, or fuel pump modules, inspect and reroute or repair damaged wiring harnesses, and inspect or replace the throttle body assembly with an ECM software update. All of these repairs, including part replacements, are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Nissan models have had fuel leak recalls that raise fire risk?

Fuel leak recalls tied to fire risk cover 2019 Altima (fuel tube clip and pump lock ring), 2020 Versa (thin tank wall), and three separate 2021-2022 Rogue recalls (fuel hose, thin tank wall, and a punctured tank). Each notice says a leak increases fire risk.

Why do Nissan fuel pumps keep failing?

Different causes are behind each pump recall: an impeller that swells and binds on 2020-2021 Armada and Infiniti QX80, abnormal wear on 2021 Rogue, and a damaged sensor harness that blows the fuel pump fuse on 2018-2019 NV200 and City Express. Each can cause the engine to stall.

What's the 2024-2025 Rogue recall about if it's not a fuel leak?

This recall covers 3-cylinder 1.5L VC-Turbo engines where gears inside the electronic throttle body assembly may break, which Nissan says can cause a loss of drive power and increase crash risk. Dealers reprogram the ECM software and replace the throttle body assembly as needed, free of charge.
