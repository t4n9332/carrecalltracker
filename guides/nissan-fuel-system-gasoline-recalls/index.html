Which Nissan models have fuel system recalls for leaks and stalling?

10 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 12, 2026

Nissan's gasoline fuel-system recalls fall into a few repeating patterns. Fuel leaks are the largest: the 2019 Altima appears twice, once for a retainer clip connecting the low pressure fuel tube to the high pressure fuel pump that may not be locked into position, and once for a fuel pump lock ring that may not be fully engaged on front-wheel-drive 2.5-liter cars. Fuel tanks are the second pattern, covering insufficient wall thickness on the 2020 Versa and the 2022 Rogue, plus a separate 2022 Rogue tank punctured when vehicle modifications were performed. Fuel pumps are the third, with impeller swelling on 2020-2021 Armada and INFINITI QX80 V8s and abnormal internal wear on the 2021 Rogue.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine stalling: the 2019 Altima fuel tube clip recall says the engine can stall if the fuel tube fully disconnects.
  • Engine stalling: the 2021 Rogue fuel hose recall says a hose that disconnects completely can cause the engine to stall.
  • Engine stalling: the 2020-2021 Armada and INFINITI QX80 fuel pump recall and the 2021 Rogue fuel pump recall both say pump failure can cause an engine stall.
  • Engine stalling: the NV200 and Chevrolet City Express recall says a blown fuel pump fuse can cause the engine to stall.
  • Loss of drive power: the 2024-2025 Rogue recall says broken throttle body gears can cause a loss of drive power.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these ten notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The two 2019 Altima recalls, the 2020 Versa recall, both 2022 Rogue fuel tank recalls and the 2021 Rogue fuel hose recall describe a fire risk from leaking fuel. The Armada and QX80 pump recall, the 2021 Rogue pump recall, the NV200 and City Express fuse recall and the 2024-2025 Rogue throttle body recall describe an engine stall or a loss of drive power. None of these notices gives owners an interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Repairs differ by recall and every notice says the work is free of charge. Dealers route and lock the Altima fuel tube retainer clip; inspect the Altima fuel tank assembly and tighten the lock ring or replace the fuel pump; replace the Versa and 2022 Rogue fuel tanks; reposition and secure the 2021 Rogue fuel hose and lock ring; replace Armada, QX80 and Rogue fuel pumps; reroute the NV200 sensor harness; and reprogram the Rogue ECM software.

Common questions

Is my 2019 Altima covered by one fuel recall or two?

Two separate recalls cover 2019 Altimas: one for a retainer clip that may not be locked into position, and one for a fuel pump lock ring that may not be fully engaged on front-wheel-drive 2.5-liter cars. Check your VIN against both.

Why does the Rogue appear so many times in this group?

Five recalls here cover Rogues: a 2021 fuel hose and lock ring recall, a 2021 fuel pump recall, two separate 2022 fuel tank recalls, and a 2024-2025 throttle body recall on VC-Turbo engines. Each has its own cause, so check your VIN against each.

Do I have to pay for any of these fuel system repairs?

Each of these ten notices says the repair is free of charge, whether it is an inspection, a fuel tank replacement, a fuel pump replacement, a harness reroute or a software reprogram. Contact a Nissan or INFINITI dealer with your VIN to schedule the work.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
