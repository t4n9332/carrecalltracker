Why is Kia recalling brakes in the Stinger and Sportage?

4 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-06-07

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Kia's hydraulic brake recalls split into two patterns. Two involve a fire risk: the 2018-2021 Stinger and the 2018-2019 Cadenza together with the 2018-2021 Sportage were recalled because the Hydraulic Electronic Control Unit's electrical circuit can short-circuit and start a fire in the engine compartment. The other two involve reduced stopping power instead of fire: the 2020-2021 Rio's brake master cylinder can have sharp edges that damage an internal seal and reduce braking performance, and the 2023 Sportage's brake booster diaphragm can misalign and cause a vacuum leak that cuts power brake assist. The Sportage appears in both the fire-related and vacuum-leak recalls, for unrelated components in different model years.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No stated warning before the Stinger and Sportage/Cadenza HECU fire recalls occur
  • Longer brake pedal travel or an increased distance needed to stop (2020-2021 Rio)
  • A brake pedal that may require more effort to press if power assist is lost (2023 Sportage)

Is it safe to keep driving?

Two of the four recalls in this group carry a park-outside instruction because of fire risk: the 2018-2021 Stinger recall for an engine compartment fire near the brake hydraulic control unit, and the 2018-2019 Cadenza and 2018-2021 Sportage recall for a short circuit in the same brake module. Neither tells owners to stop driving. The Rio brake master cylinder recall and the 2023 Sportage brake booster recall do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers install a new fuse kit in the electrical junction box on the Stinger and Sportage/Cadenza fire recalls, adding a HECU software update on vehicles with electronic parking brakes; replace the brake master cylinder on the Rio; and inspect and replace the brake booster as needed on the 2023 Sportage. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why were the Kia Stinger and Sportage recalled for brake fires?

An electrical circuit in the brake Hydraulic Electronic Control Unit can short-circuit, which the notices say can cause a fire in the engine compartment. Kia tells owners to park outside and away from structures until repaired.

Is the Kia Rio brake recall a fire risk?

No. It involves a brake master cylinder with sharp edges that can damage an internal oil seal and reduce braking performance, not a fire — the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

Does the 2023 Sportage brake recall involve the same part as the fire recalls?

No. It's the brake booster diaphragm, which can misalign and cause a vacuum leak that reduces power brake assist — a separate component from the HECU involved in the fire recalls.
