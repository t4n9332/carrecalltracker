Why do Cadillac daytime running lights and turn signals stay on?

3 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Exterior Lighting · model years 2019–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 8, 2022

Two of these three recalls describe the same fault: daytime running lights that do not deactivate when the headlights are on, which GM lists as a failure to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108. One covers 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV, the other 2020-2023 CT4 and CT5 together with 2022-2023 Escalade and Escalade ESV, and both are addressed through body control module software. The full-size Escalade therefore appears in both, and the CT4 and CT5 sedans carry the widest year span. The remaining recall stands apart: on 2019 CT6 vehicles, rotating the steering wheel will not automatically cancel the turn signals.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Daytime running lights that do not turn off when the headlights are on (2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV recall).
  • Daytime running lights that stay on with the headlights on (2020–2023 CT4 and CT5, 2022–2023 Escalade and Escalade ESV recall).
  • Turn signals that keep flashing after the steering wheel is rotated (2019 CT6 turn-signal recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. The two daytime running light notices, covering 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV and the 2020-2023 CT4 and CT5 group, say only that glare from lights that do not deactivate reduces visibility and increases the risk of a crash. The 2019 CT6 notice says only that an erroneous active turn signal may mislead pedestrians and other drivers. None of the three gives owners any instruction to follow before the repair described in its own notice.

What the free repair involves

Both daytime running light recalls are handled with body control module software. On the 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV, dealers provide an over-the-air update; on the 2020-2023 CT4, CT5 and 2022-2023 Escalade group, the software is updated by a dealer or over the air. Both notices state this is free of charge. On the 2019 CT6, the notice says dealers will replace the turn-signal switch.

Common questions

Will my Escalade get the daytime running light fix over the air?

The 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV notice says dealers will provide an over-the-air body control module software update, free of charge. The 2022-2023 Escalade notice says the body control module software will be updated by a dealer or over the air, also free of charge.

Does the 2019 CT6 turn-signal repair cost anything?

The notice does not say. It states that GM has notified owners and that dealers will replace the turn-signal switch, with the recall beginning March 11, 2019, and lists Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 for questions.

Are the CT4 and CT5 affected by both lighting recalls?

The 2020-2023 CT4 and CT5 appear in only one of them, the December 2022 daytime running light recall. The 2021 recall lists Escalade, Escalade ESV, Tahoe, Suburban, Yukon and Yukon XL, and the turn-signal recall lists only the 2019 CT6.
