Why won't some Cadillac daytime running lights turn off?

3 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Exterior Lighting · model years 2019–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-12-08

These three Cadillac lighting recalls fall into two separate defects. Two of them, covering 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV (grouped with Chevrolet Tahoe/Suburban and GMC Yukon/Yukon XL) and a wider 2020-2023 group of CT4, CT5, Escalade, and Escalade ESV (grouped with Buick Envision, Chevrolet Silverado 1500/Suburban/Tahoe, and GMC Sierra 1500/Yukon/Yukon XL), involve daytime running lights that may not deactivate when the headlights are turned on. The third, a 2019 Cadillac CT6 recall, is unrelated: its turn signals may not cancel automatically after the steering wheel rotates back, so a signal can keep flashing after a turn.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Daytime running lights stay on even when the headlights are on (2021 Escalade/Escalade ESV group and 2020-2023 CT4/CT5/Escalade group)
  • Turn signal keeps flashing after a turn instead of cancelling automatically (2019 CT6)
  • No other warning signs are described in these notices

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the two daytime-running-light recalls, GM says lights that stay on can cause glare and reduce visibility for other drivers, but the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. For the CT6 turn-signal recall, GM says a signal that does not cancel may mislead pedestrians and other drivers about the vehicle's intentions; again, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving. Across all three recalls, owners are not instructed to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the daytime-running-light recalls, dealers update the body control module software, either in the shop or over the air. For the 2019 CT6 turn-signal recall, dealers replace the turn-signal switch itself. All of these repairs are provided to owners free of charge, with no cost for parts or labor.

Common questions

Why do the daytime running lights stay on?

GM found that in certain 2020-2023 Cadillac CT4, CT5, Escalade, and Escalade ESV models, and separately in 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV, the body control module software could fail to turn the daytime running lights off when headlights are on, causing glare for other drivers.

Is the CT6 turn-signal issue a safety recall?

Yes. GM recalled 2019 Cadillac CT6 vehicles because the turn signal does not automatically cancel when the steering wheel rotates back, which can mislead pedestrians and other drivers about the car's intentions. Dealers replace the turn-signal switch for free.

Does this recall affect just Cadillac vehicles?

No. The two daytime-running-light recalls also cover Buick Envision, Chevrolet Silverado 1500, Suburban, Tahoe, and GMC Sierra 1500, Yukon, and Yukon XL alongside the listed Cadillac models. The CT6 turn-signal recall applies only to Cadillac.
