Why were Ford tires recalled on F-150, Escape and Maverick?

3 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Tires · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 22, 2024

All three of these recalls are about the tires themselves rather than anything else on the vehicle. Ford recalled certain 2020 F-150 trucks equipped with 17-inch spare tires because the spare could have been damaged when it was installed on the rim. A second recall covers certain 2018 F-150, 2018-2020 F-250 and F-350, and 2019 Escape vehicles equipped with Continental tires, where one or more tires may have been cured for too long during tire production. The most recent covers certain 2023 Maverick and Bronco Sport vehicles whose tires may have been manufactured incorrectly. F-150 is the only nameplate that appears twice, and the group is entirely light-duty trucks and small SUVs.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Ford says the damage can cause the spare tire to rapidly lose air pressure or detach from the wheel (2020 F-150 17-inch spare tire recall).
  • Over-cured tires may develop a break in the sidewall, resulting in sudden air loss or belt edge separation that could lead to tread or belt loss (2018 F-150, 2018-2020 F-250 and F-350, and 2019 Escape Continental tire recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the three notices in this group tells owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside. For the 2020 F-150 spare tire recall, Ford says dealers will replace the spare tire. For the Continental tire recall covering 2018 F-150, 2018-2020 F-250 and F-350, and 2019 Escape vehicles, dealers will inspect the tires and replace them as necessary. The 2023 Maverick and Bronco Sport notice likewise has dealers inspect and replace the tires as necessary. Beyond that, the notices say nothing about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is tire work. Dealers replace the 17-inch spare tire on affected 2020 F-150 trucks. For the Continental tire recall and for the 2023 Maverick and Bronco Sport recall, dealers inspect the tires and replace them as necessary. No software update or over-the-air remedy is involved, and all of these repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Does the 2020 F-150 recall mean the tires I drive on are affected too?

Ford's notice covers 2020 F-150 trucks equipped with 17-inch spare tires and says the spare could have been damaged when it was installed on the rim. The remedy described is replacement of the spare tire, free of charge.

How do I know whether my Escape or F-Series has the recalled Continental tires?

Ford's notice covers certain 2018 F-150, 2018-2020 F-250 and F-350, and 2019 Escape vehicles equipped with Continental tires. Dealers inspect the tires and replace them as necessary, free of charge. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.

Will I have to pay for new tires?

All three notices state the work is free of charge: replacement of the spare tire on the 2020 F-150, and inspection with replacement as necessary for the Continental tire recall and the 2023 Maverick and Bronco Sport recall.
