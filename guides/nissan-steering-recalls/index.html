Why Do Nissan Vehicles Keep Getting Steering Recalls?

8 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Steering · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-12-21

Nissan's steering recalls cluster around three problems. Tie rods and suspension links have failed on several models: the 2020-2021 Altima for a loose tie rod ball joint fastener, the 2020 Murano for a transverse suspension link that can separate from its ball joint, and the Sentra for bent tie rods, first in 2020-2021 vehicles and then again in an expanded 2020-2022 recall. Separately, the 2021 Kicks and Versa lost power steering assist from an intermittent EPS torque sensor connection, recalled once in 2022 and again in 2023. Two one-off recalls round out the group: a loose or missing steering wheel bolt on the 2023 Ariya, and loosening steering rack bolts on the 2024 Altima.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering wheel feels loose when gently rocked forward and back (2023 Ariya recall)
  • An off-center steering wheel or vibration (2023 Sentra tie rod recall)
  • Increased steering effort, especially at low speeds, from a loss of power steering assist (2022 and 2023 Kicks and Versa recalls)
  • The Altima tie rod, Murano suspension link, and 2024 Altima steering rack bolt recalls describe no warning sign for owners to notice

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these eight steering recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside order. Two describe an interim step: the 2023 Ariya recall advises owners to contact their dealer for transport and immediate repair if the steering wheel feels loose when rocked, and the 2023 Sentra tie rod recall advises contacting the dealer for transport if the steering wheel is off-center or vibrating. For the remaining six recalls, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace or tighten the affected part depending on the recall: the steering wheel bolt, the tie rod fasteners or the tie rods themselves, the transverse suspension link, the EPS torque sensor or steering column, or the entire steering gear assembly, performing a wheel alignment where needed. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Which Nissan models had tie rod recalls?

The 2020-2021 Altima was recalled for a loose tie rod ball joint fastener, and the Sentra was recalled twice for bent tie rods, first in 2020-2021 vehicles and again in an expanded 2020-2022 recall.

Why do Nissan Kicks and Versa keep losing power steering assist?

An intermittent electrical connection in the electric power steering torque sensor can disable power assist on 2021 Kicks and Versa vehicles, making the wheel harder to turn at low speeds; Nissan recalled the same defect in both 2022 and 2023.

Is the Nissan Ariya steering wheel bolt recall serious?

Yes. An incorrectly tightened or missing steering wheel bolt can let the wheel detach from the column, causing loss of steering control; owners should contact their dealer immediately if the wheel feels loose when rocked.
