What keeps going wrong with Nissan steering, tie rods and columns?

8 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Steering · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-12-21

Eight Nissan steering recalls fall into three recurring patterns. Fasteners that may not be tightened or installed: the 2023 Ariya's steering wheel bolt, the 2020-2021 Altima's tie rod ball joint fastener, and the bolts connecting the electronic power steering unit to the steering rack on the 2024 Altima. Bent tie rods on the Sentra, covered first for 2020-2021 vehicles and again for 2020-2022 vehicles. And the electric power steering torque sensor on the 2021 Versa and Kicks, where an intermittent electrical connection can disable power steering assist, recalled in 2022 and again in 2023. The 2020 Murano stands apart, with a transverse link that may separate from its ball joint.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A steering wheel that feels loose when gently rocked forward and back by hand — named in the 2023 Ariya steering wheel bolt recall.
  • An off-center steering wheel or vibration — named in the 2023 Sentra tie rod recall covering 2020-2022 vehicles.
  • Increased steering effort, especially at low speeds, if power steering assist is lost — named in both 2021 Versa and Kicks torque sensor recalls.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the eight notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. Two give an instruction tied to something an owner notices: the 2023 Ariya notice advises owners to contact their dealer for transport and immediate repair if the steering wheel feels loose when gently rocked forward and back, and the 2023 Sentra tie rod notice advises owners experiencing an off-center steering wheel or vibration to contact their dealer for transport assistance. The remaining six notices say nothing about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

All repairs are free of charge. Dealers replace the Ariya's steering wheel bolt; tighten or install new tie rod ball joint fasteners on the Altima; inspect the Murano's transverse link lot codes, replacing them and performing an alignment if needed; inspect and replace bent Sentra tie rods; inspect the Versa and Kicks torque sensor lot code and replace the steering column if needed; and replace the 2024 Altima's steering gear assembly.

Common questions

Why were the 2021 Versa and Kicks recalled twice for power steering?

Both notices describe an intermittent electrical connection in the electric power steering torque sensor that can disable assist on 2021 vehicles. Under the 2022 recall dealers inspect the lot code and replace the column if necessary; the 2023 recall states the same remedy.

Is my 2022 Sentra included in the tie rod recall?

The 2021 recall covers 2020-2021 Sentra vehicles, and the 2023 recall covers 2020-2022 Sentra vehicles, so a 2022 Sentra falls under the later one. Owners can confirm with Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.

What does the Ariya recall say if the steering wheel feels loose?

The notice advises owners to contact their dealer for transport and immediate repair if the steering wheel feels loose when gently rocking it forward and back by hand. Dealers replace the steering wheel bolt free of charge. Nissan began notifying owners March 2, 2023.
