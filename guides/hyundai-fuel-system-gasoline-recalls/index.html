Hyundai fuel system recalls: fuel leaks, fire risk and power loss

5 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2019–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 18, 2025

Four of these five Hyundai recalls involve fuel leaking and the risk of fire. Two cover the same connection point: the fuel pipe between the high-pressure fuel pump and the fuel rail on 2.5-litre turbocharged engines, one covering certain 2021 Santa Fe vehicles and one covering certain 2021-2022 Santa Fe and Sonata vehicles. Two others involve the tank itself - certain 2022 Santa Fe Plug-In Hybrid vehicles with a tank that may have been improperly molded, and certain 2020-2023 Sonata vehicles where a damaged check valve can let the tank expand and contact hot exhaust components. The fifth is different: fuel pump failure on certain 2019-2022 Veloster N, 2019-2023 Genesis G70, and 2022-2023 Elantra N and Kona N vehicles, which can cause a loss of drive power.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Loss of drive power, stated in the fuel pump recall covering 2019-2022 Veloster N, 2019-2023 Genesis G70, and 2022-2023 Elantra N and Kona N.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving. The 2021 Santa Fe and the 2021-2022 Santa Fe and Sonata notices describe a fuel pipe that may leak, which increases the risk of fire. The 2022 Santa Fe Plug-In Hybrid notice describes a fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source. The 2020-2023 Sonata notice says a melted fuel tank can leak. The fuel pump notice says a loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash. None of the notices gives owners an instruction to follow before the repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers tightened the fuel pipe on the 2021 Santa Fe, and inspect and tighten or replace the pipe on 2021-2022 Santa Fe and Sonata. On the 2022 Santa Fe Plug-In Hybrid they inspect and replace the fuel tank as necessary. On 2020-2023 Sonata they replace the check valve, inspect the tank and update engine control unit software. The fuel pump recall covers software and pump replacement. All free of charge.

Common questions

Is my Santa Fe or Sonata affected by more than one fuel recall?

Two notices cover the fuel pipe on 2.5-litre turbocharged engines - one for certain 2021 Santa Fe vehicles, one for certain 2021-2022 Santa Fe and Sonata vehicles - and a separate notice covers fuel tanks on certain 2020-2023 Sonata vehicles. A VIN lookup shows which apply to your vehicle.

Does the 2021 Santa Fe fuel pipe recall still need a dealer visit?

That notice says dealers inspected and tightened the fuel pipe connection free of charge, that the affected vehicles have been repaired, and that no owner notification letters will be sent. Owners with questions can reach Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460; the recall number is 206.

What do the notices say about driving a car with a possible fuel leak?

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. They state the risk each defect creates - fire from a fuel or tank leak, and a loss of drive power in the fuel pump recall - and direct owners to dealers for the free repair.
