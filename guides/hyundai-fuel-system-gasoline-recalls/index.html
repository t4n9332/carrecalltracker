Why Do Some Hyundai Models Leak Fuel or Lose Power?

5 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2019–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-11-18

This group of five Hyundai recalls splits into two recurring problems. Three involve fuel leaks at or in the fuel tank: a fuel pipe connecting the high-pressure fuel pump to the fuel rail that was not tightened properly, first recalled on the 2021 Santa Fe and again on 2021-2022 Santa Fe and Sonata models with 2.5-liter turbo engines; a fuel tank on the 2022 Santa Fe Plug-In Hybrid that may have been improperly molded; and a damaged check valve on the 2020-2023 Sonata that lets the fuel tank expand and melt against hot exhaust parts. Separately, a fuel pump that can fail and cut drive power affects 2019-2023 Genesis G70, Veloster N, Elantra N, and Kona N models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Fuel odor or visible fuel leaking near the engine bay or fuel tank
  • Sudden loss of engine power or stalling while driving (fuel pump recall)
  • The fuel pipe and fuel tank recall notices describe no warning sign before a leak develops

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recall notices instruct owners to stop driving or park outside away from structures. That includes both fuel pipe recalls (2021 Santa Fe; 2021-2022 Santa Fe and Sonata), the 2022 Santa Fe Plug-In Hybrid fuel tank recall, the 2020-2023 Sonata fuel tank recall, and the fuel pump recall affecting Genesis G70, Veloster N, Elantra N, and Kona N models. For all five, the notices describe the defect and the free repair but do not tell owners to stop driving or keep the vehicle away from structures.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, dealers inspect and tighten or replace the fuel pipe connection, inspect and replace the fuel tank as needed, or inspect and replace the fuel pump assembly and update engine control module software. All repairs, inspections, and part replacements described in these five recalls are performed by dealers free of charge to the owner.

Common questions

Is it safe to keep driving a Hyundai with an open fuel system recall?

The notices for these fuel pipe, fuel tank, and fuel pump recalls do not instruct owners to stop driving or park away from structures. They describe the defect and the free dealer repair without giving a driving restriction.

Which Hyundai models are affected by the fuel pipe leak recalls?

The fuel pipe connection recall affects the 2021 Santa Fe, and a related recall covers 2021-2022 Santa Fe and Sonata models, all with 2.5-liter turbo engines where the pipe between the fuel pump and fuel rail wasn't tightened.

What causes the fuel pump power loss recall on Hyundai N models?

Hyundai says the fuel pump on certain 2019-2023 Genesis G70, Veloster N, Elantra N, and Kona N vehicles may fail, which can cause a loss of drive power. Dealers update engine control module software and replace the fuel pump as needed.
