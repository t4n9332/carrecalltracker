Why does my Audi's rearview camera fail when backing up?

6 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Back Over Prevention · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-19

These six Audi recalls all involve the rearview camera failing to display properly when the vehicle is shifted into reverse, violating the federal rear-visibility standard. Several are software glitches causing a blank or black screen (2021 models including A6, A7, Q5, Q7 and Q8), an image that fails to display at startup (2021-2022 A8, S8 and related models), or an image that doesn't display at all across a wide range of 2019-2026 Audi vehicles. The newest cases are the 2025 Q6 e-tron and SQ6 e-tron, where the image can be blurry or delayed after shifting into reverse, and the 2024-2025 Audi Q3, which shares a camera software error with the Volkswagen Tiguan.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A rearview camera screen that stays blank or black when shifting into reverse
  • A rearview image that is delayed, blurry, or doesn't appear at all when backing up
  • A rearview image that fails to display the first time the vehicle is put in reverse after startup

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six rearview camera recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. Each notice says only that a blank, delayed, blurry, or missing rearview camera image reduces the driver's view of what is behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. That applies whether the issue is the 2025 Q6 e-tron and SQ6 e-tron's blurry or delayed image, the shared Q3/Tiguan display error, or the broader software failures affecting Audi models from 2019 through 2026. For every recall in this group, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Every fix so far has been a software update, free of charge: dealers reprogram the infotainment unit or camera control unit so the rearview image displays correctly. The one exception is the 2025 Q6 e-tron/SQ6 e-tron blurry-image recall, where dealers also replace the physical rearview camera and inspect the surround-view cameras.

Common questions

Why do so many Audi models have rearview camera recalls?

Each recall traces to a different software error - at startup, during reverse, or in image processing - that keeps the rearview camera from meeting the federal rear-visibility standard, not one shared defect across all the recalls.

Does the Audi Q3 rearview camera recall affect Volkswagen vehicles too?

Yes. The 2025 recall for the 2024-2025 Audi Q3 also covers certain Volkswagen Tiguan models, since they share the same camera control unit software error.

Do I need a hardware repair or just a software update?

Most of these recalls are fixed with a free software update. Only the 2025 Q6 e-tron and SQ6 e-tron blurry-image recall also involves replacing the physical rearview camera.
