Audi rearview camera recalls: blank, blurry or delayed backup images

6 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Back Over Prevention · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-19

All six recalls here are rear visibility recalls under Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, and software is the repeated cause. The October 2021 recall covers the 2021 A8 and S8 plus many 2022 models, where a software error may keep the rear image from displaying as the vehicle begins to reverse. The October 2022 recall covers a long list of 2021 models, including A4, A5, A6, A7, Q5, Q7, Q8 and e-tron, where a blank or black screen may appear instead. A December 2025 software recall reaches 2019-2026 vehicles. The 2024-2025 Q3 shares a camera control unit recall with the Volkswagen Tiguan, and the 2025 Q6 e-tron appears twice.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A blank or black screen shown instead of the rearview image in reverse gear (October 2022 infotainment software recall).
  • The rear image not displaying when the vehicle begins to reverse (October 2021 A8 and S8 recall).
  • A blurry rearview image (April 2025 Q6 e-tron and SQ6 e-tron camera recall).
  • A rearview image delayed or deactivated after shifting into reverse (April 2025 Q6 e-tron camera control unit recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Each says much the same thing about risk: a rearview image that is blank, blurry, delayed, deactivated or absent reduces the driver's view of what is behind the vehicle and increases the risk of a crash, and the April 2025 Q6 e-tron camera control unit notice also names the risk of injury. These are rear visibility compliance recalls, and the notices place no interim limits on driving.

What the free repair involves

Five of these recalls are fixed with software: dealers update the infotainment unit parameters, the infotainment unit software, the camera control unit software, or the rearview camera control unit software. In the April 2025 Q6 e-tron and SQ6 e-tron blurry image recall, dealers replace the rearview camera and inspect and replace surround view cameras as necessary. Every notice says the work is free of charge.

Common questions

My backup camera usually works. Is my Audi still covered?

Coverage is set by vehicle identification number, not by how the screen behaves on a given day. The notices describe images that may fail to display at startup or when reversing, so check your VIN against the recall rather than the camera's behaviour.

Why is the 2025 Q6 e-tron in two rearview camera recalls?

They describe different faults. One April 2025 recall says the rearview camera may show a blurry image, and dealers replace the camera. The other says the image may be delayed or deactivated after shifting into reverse, and dealers update the camera control unit software.

Will I be charged for the camera repair?

The notices state the work is free of charge, whether that is a software update to the infotainment unit or camera control unit, or replacement of the rearview camera and, where necessary, the surround view cameras on the Q6 e-tron and SQ6 e-tron.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
