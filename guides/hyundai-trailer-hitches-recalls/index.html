Hyundai tow hitch wiring recalls: why owners are told to park outside

3 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Trailer Hitches · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-19

⚠ 3 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Three separate Hyundai recalls point to the same weak spot: the tow hitch wiring harness module. On the Palisade (2020-2022) and the Santa Fe family (2019-2023 gas models, 2021-2022 Santa Fe Hybrid, the 2022 Santa Fe Plug-In Hybrid, and the related 2022-2023 Santa Cruz), debris and moisture reach the harness module's circuit board and can short out, creating a fire risk. The Tucson recall (2022-2024) is a related but distinct problem: an optional Mobis tow hitch harness installed incorrectly lets water into the control module, which can knock out trailer lighting or cause a short. All three involve tow hitch wiring exposed to water, but only the Tucson notice describes a trailer-lighting failure alongside the fire risk.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Trailer lights that don't work, on Tucson models with the recalled hitch harness
  • No warning sign before a fire is described in the Palisade notice
  • No warning sign before a fire is described in the Santa Fe/Santa Cruz notice

Is it safe to keep driving?

All three recalls in this group carry a park-outside instruction: the Palisade recall, the Santa Fe/Santa Cruz recall, and the Tucson recall all tell owners to park outside and away from structures until the repair is done. None of the three notices tell owners to stop driving entirely. The Tucson notice additionally warns that nonworking trailer lights can raise the risk of a crash, on top of the fire risk described in all three recalls.

What the free repair involves

For the Palisade and Santa Fe/Santa Cruz recalls, dealers install a new fuse and wire extension kit on the tow hitch harness, free of charge. For the Tucson recall, dealers remove the trailer wiring harness and install a dust cap over the wiring receptacle, free of charge. No repair costs are passed to owners in any of the three recalls.

Common questions

Which Hyundai models are affected by the tow hitch wiring recalls?

The 2020-2022 Palisade, the 2019-2023 Santa Fe (including 2021-2022 Hybrid and 2022 Plug-In Hybrid), the 2022-2023 Santa Cruz, and the 2022-2024 Tucson are covered, but only vehicles equipped with the recalled tow hitch wiring harness are affected.

Do I have to park outside because of these recalls?

Yes. All three notices in this group instruct owners to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is completed, because moisture in the tow hitch wiring can create a fire risk.

Is the tow hitch wiring repair free?

Yes. Dealers install a new fuse and wire extension kit on the Palisade and Santa Fe/Santa Cruz recalls, or a wiring harness removal and dust cap on the Tucson recall, all at no cost to the owner.
