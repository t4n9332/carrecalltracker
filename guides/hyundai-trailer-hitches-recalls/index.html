Hyundai tow hitch wiring recalls: fire risk and park-outside warnings

3 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Trailer Hitches · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 19, 2025

⚠ 3 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

All three of these Hyundai recalls involve the tow hitch wiring harness and all three carry a park-outside instruction. The 2020-2022 Palisade recall covers vehicles with a tow hitch harness fitted as original equipment or bought as a dealer accessory, where debris and moisture on the harness module printed circuit board may cause an electrical short and a fire. The 2019-2023 Santa Fe, 2021-2023 Santa Fe HEV, 2022-2023 Santa Fe Plug-in HEV and Santa Cruz recall describes water accumulation on the tow hitch harness module printed circuit board. The 2022-2024 Tucson recall covers an optional Mobis harness that may have been installed incorrectly, letting water into the control module.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Trailer lighting that fails (2022-2024 Tucson tow hitch wiring harness recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

All three notices tell owners to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete: the 2020-2022 Palisade recall, the 2019-2023 Santa Fe, Santa Fe HEV, Santa Fe Plug-in HEV and Santa Cruz recall, and the 2022-2024 Tucson recall. None of the three tells owners to stop driving. The Palisade and Santa Fe notices state that a fire while parked or driving can increase the risk of injury; the Tucson notice states that nonfunctioning trailer lights can increase the risk of a crash and a short circuit can increase the risk of a fire.

What the free repair involves

For the 2020-2022 Palisade and for the Santa Fe, Santa Fe HEV, Santa Fe Plug-in HEV and Santa Cruz recall, dealers install a new fuse and wire extension kit as necessary. For the 2022-2024 Tucson, dealers remove the trailer wiring harness and install a dust cap over the wiring receptacle. Each notice states the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Does my Hyundai need a factory tow hitch to be included?

The Palisade and Santa Fe notices cover vehicles potentially equipped with a tow hitch harness installed as original equipment or purchased as an accessory through a Hyundai dealership. The Tucson notice covers vehicles equipped with an optional Mobis tow hitch wiring harness.

Where should I park my Palisade or Santa Fe before the repair?

Both notices advise owners to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. The Palisade notice describes debris and moisture on the tow hitch harness circuit board, and the Santa Fe notice describes water accumulation on it, either of which may cause an electrical short.

Will I still be able to tow after the Tucson recall repair?

The Tucson notice states that dealers will remove the trailer wiring harness and install a dust cap over the wiring receptacle, free of charge. It does not say anything further about towing or about replacing the harness afterwards.
