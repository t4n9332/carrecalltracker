Why Do Honda and Acura Seats Keep Coming Loose?

5 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Seats · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-01-29

Loose or detached seats are the dominant theme in these five recalls. Two separate 2024 and 2026 recalls address the same driver's seat cushion frame that was not tightened properly, spanning the 2023 Accord Hybrid, Civic Hatchback and Pilot, and the 2024 Acura Integra, Accord Hybrid, Civic Hatchback, HR-V and Pilot. A 2018 recall covers a different cause — improperly manufactured rivets that can let the front power seats detach from the floor in a crash on the Acura MDX, Pilot and Ridgeline. The 2022 Odyssey's second-row seats have a deformed rail locking mechanism, and the 2023 CR-V's front passenger seat back frame has a separate welding defect that can fail in a crash.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A driver's seat that feels loose or is not securely fastened (Accord Hybrid, Civic Hatchback, Pilot, Acura Integra, HR-V — two related recalls)
  • Second-row seat rails on the Odyssey that do not lock into place, allowing the seat to move unexpectedly
  • The notices do not describe a way to notice the power seat rivet issue (Acura MDX, Pilot, Ridgeline) or the CR-V's seat back weld issue before a crash

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five Honda and Acura seat recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe an increased risk of injury if a seat detaches from the floor, a seat back position fails, or a seat is not secured or locked during a crash, but none tell owners to stop driving in the meantime. This applies to the Acura MDX, Pilot, Ridgeline, Odyssey, Accord Hybrid, Civic Hatchback, Acura Integra, HR-V, and CR-V recalls.

What the free repair involves

Honda and Acura dealers replace the affected components: the driver seat frame or seat slide rail frames on the 2018 MDX, Pilot and Ridgeline; both second-row outboard seat frames on the Odyssey; the driver's seat cushion frame on the Accord Hybrid, Civic Hatchback, Pilot, Integra and HR-V models covered by the two loose-seat recalls; and the front passenger seat back frame on the CR-V. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why were there two separate recalls for a loose driver's seat?

Honda issued a November 2024 recall and a January 2026 recall for the same driver's seat cushion frame tightening issue, covering overlapping models including the Accord Hybrid, Acura Integra, HR-V and Pilot, because affected vehicles kept being identified.

Which models have the seat detachment risk from broken rivets?

The 2018 recall for improperly manufactured rivets affects the 2018 Acura MDX, Honda Pilot and Honda Ridgeline power seats, which could let the seat separate from the floor in a crash.

Is the CR-V seat recall the same as the driver's seat cushion frame recalls?

No. The 2023 CR-V recall involves a welding defect in the front passenger seat back frame, a different cause from the driver's seat cushion frame tightening issue in the other recalls.
