Why do Honda seat frames get recalled for coming loose?

5 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Seats · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 29, 2026

Seat frames are the theme. Driver's seat cushion frames that were not tightened properly appear twice: once for 2023-2024 Accord, Accord Hybrid, Civic Sedan, Civic Hatchback, Pilot and 2024 HR-V, Acura Integra and Integra Type S, and again for 2023 Accord and Accord Hybrid with 2024 HR-V, Pilot and Acura Integra. The rest are single models: rivets in the powered seats of 2018 Pilot, Ridgeline and Acura MDX, deformed second-row outboard seat rail locking mechanisms on the 2022 Odyssey, and an improperly welded front passenger seat back frame on the 2023 CR-V. Pilot, HR-V and Accord recur; most cite Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 207.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A second-row outboard seat that is not locked into place and can move unexpectedly — stated in the 2022 Odyssey seat rail recall.
  • A loose driver's seat — stated in the 2023 Accord, Accord Hybrid, 2024 HR-V, Pilot and Acura Integra seat cushion frame recall; the 2023-2024 recall describes the same frame as leaving the seat unsecured.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. That includes the 2018 Pilot, Ridgeline and Acura MDX powered seat rivet recall, the 2022 Odyssey second-row seat rail recall, the 2023 CR-V front passenger seat back frame recall, the 2023-2024 Accord, Civic, Pilot, HR-V and Acura Integra driver's seat cushion frame recall, and the 2023 Accord and 2024 HR-V, Pilot and Integra driver's seat cushion frame recall. Each describes an increased risk of injury, most of it in a crash.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is free of charge. On 2018 Acura MDX dealers replace the driver seat frame assembly, and on 2018 Pilot and Ridgeline the driver and front passenger seat slide rail frames. The 2022 Odyssey gets both second-row outboard seat frames replaced, the 2023 CR-V gets a new front passenger seat back frame assembly, and both driver's seat recalls replace the driver's seat cushion frame.

Common questions

My driver's seat feels loose. Which Honda recall covers that?

Two notices describe a driver's seat cushion frame that was not tightened properly. One lists 2023-2024 Accord, Accord Hybrid, Civic Sedan, Civic Hatchback, Pilot and 2024 HR-V and Acura Integra; the other lists 2023 Accord and 2024 HR-V, Pilot and Integra.

Will the dealer tighten my seat or replace the frame?

Both driver's seat notices say dealers replace the driver's seat cushion frame, free of charge. Letters for the 2023-2024 recall were mailed January 6, 2025, and for the 2023 Accord and 2024 HR-V, Pilot and Integra recall on March 10, 2026.

Is the 2022 Odyssey second-row seat problem the same issue?

That notice describes something different: deformed seat rail locking mechanisms on the second-row outboard seats, which may prevent the rails from locking into place. The notice says a seat that is not locked can move unexpectedly. Dealers replace both second-row outboard seat frames, free of charge.
