Chevrolet wheel recalls: loose lug nuts and damaged hub bolts

3 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Wheels · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-14

Only three recalls sit in this group, and two of them concern the same part: the front wheel hub bolts. On the 2023 Chevrolet Colorado, recalled in March 2024 alongside the GMC Canyon, the notice says the front wheel hub bolts may have been over-tightened and damaged, and that damaged bolts may loosen or break. On the 2025 Chevrolet Tahoe with 24-inch wheels, recalled in May 2026 with Cadillac Escalade and GMC Yukon models, incorrect bolts may have been installed on the left and right front wheel hubs, which can loosen or deform over time. The third is different: the 2019 Chevrolet 5500HD, recalled by Isuzu, whose rear outer wheel lug nuts may not have been properly tightened when installed onto the rear axles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside. The Isuzu recall of the 2019 Chevrolet 5500HD says lug nuts that were not properly tightened can loosen and cause the wheel to separate from the vehicle. The 2023 Colorado notice says damaged bolts may loosen or break, which can result in a loss of vehicle control. The 2026 recall of the 2025 Tahoe says a bolt that loosens or breaks during vehicle operation can result in the same loss of control.

What the free repair involves

For the 2019 Chevrolet 5500HD, dealers inspect the rear outer wheel lug nuts and tighten or replace them as necessary. For the 2023 Colorado and for the 2025 Tahoe with 24-inch wheels, dealers replace the left and right front wheel hub bolts. All three notices say the work is free of charge.

Common questions

My 2023 Colorado was recalled for wheel hub bolts. What does the dealer replace?

The notice says dealers replace the left and right front wheel hub bolts, free of charge, and that owner notification letters were mailed April 10, 2024. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving while they wait for an appointment.

Is the 2025 Tahoe bolt recall the same problem as the Colorado one?

Both involve front wheel hub bolts, but the notices give different causes. The Colorado's bolts may have been over-tightened and damaged; the Tahoe recall covers vehicles with 24-inch wheels on which incorrect bolts may have been installed that can loosen or deform over time.

Who handles the Chevrolet 5500HD lug nut recall?

That recall was filed by Isuzu. The notice says dealers inspect the rear outer wheel lug nuts and tighten or replace them as necessary, free of charge, and lists Isuzu customer service at 1-866-441-9638 and Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020.
