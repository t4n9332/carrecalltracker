GMC hood, door and grille parts that can come loose

3 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Structure · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 6, 2025

All three of these GMC recalls involve body hardware that can fracture and let a part come loose. Two of them affect things that open. On 2020 Sierra 2500 and 3500 trucks, hood latch striker wires on replacement hoods installed for Safety Recall 20V-142 may not have been heat treated properly and can fracture, and the hood may open unexpectedly while driving. On the 2024 GMC Terrain, the door strikers may fracture and result in a door opening unexpectedly. The third recall is narrower: chrome front grille deflector attachments on the 2022 Sierra 1500 may fracture and let the deflector detach. Heavy-duty Sierra pickups and the 2024 Terrain carry the GMC side of these recalls.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. For the 2020 Sierra 2500 and 3500 hood recall, the notice states only that a fractured striker wire may let the hood open unexpectedly while driving, increasing the risk of a crash. The 2024 Terrain door striker notice and the 2022 Sierra 1500 grille deflector notice likewise describe the injury or crash risk without telling owners to stop driving and without giving any step to take before the dealer repair.

What the free repair involves

All three repairs are free of charge. On the 2020 Sierra 2500 and 3500 hood recall, dealers replace the hood assembly, and for hoods sold over the counter dealers contact customers with instructions for hood replacement. On the 2024 Terrain, dealers replace all four door strikers and the attaching bolts. On the 2022 Sierra 1500, dealers reinforce or repair all attachment points on the chrome front grille deflector.

Common questions

Why is a replacement hood itself being recalled?

The notice says the hood latch striker wires on replacement hoods installed for Safety Recall 20V-142 may not have been heat treated properly and can fracture. Some covered trucks were not part of that earlier recall but received the parts during other service, typically collision repair.

Does the Terrain door striker recall cover every door?

The notice says dealers will replace all four door strikers and the attaching bolts, free of charge, on 2024 GMC Terrain, Chevrolet Blazer, Blazer EV and Equinox vehicles. It states the door strikers may fracture, resulting in the door opening unexpectedly.

Is the grille deflector recall only a cosmetic issue?

The notice treats it as a crash risk. It says the attachments holding the chrome front grille deflector on the 2022 Sierra 1500 may fracture, resulting in grille detachment, and that a detached deflector may become a road hazard. Dealers reinforce or repair the attachment points free.
