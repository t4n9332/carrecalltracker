Why do GMC truck hoods, doors and grille trim come loose?

3 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Structure · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-02-06

Three GMC recalls in this group involve fasteners or attachment points that fracture and let a body panel come loose. A 2020 recall covers replacement hoods installed on 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 2500/3500 and GMC Sierra 2500/3500 trucks under an earlier campaign (20V-142), or fitted during other service such as collision repair; the hood-latch striker wire on these replacement hoods may not have been heat-treated properly and can fracture, letting the hood open. A 2023 recall covers 2024 Chevrolet Blazer, Blazer EV, Equinox, and GMC Terrain vehicles, where the door strikers themselves may fracture and let a door open. The narrowest, from 2025, covers only 2022 GMC Sierra 1500 trucks with chrome front grille deflectors, whose attachment points may fracture and let the deflector detach.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A hood on a replacement part opening unexpectedly while driving (2020 Silverado 2500/3500 and Sierra 2500/3500)
  • A door opening unexpectedly while driving (2024 Blazer, Blazer EV, Equinox, and Terrain)
  • A chrome front grille deflector detaching from the truck (2022 Sierra 1500)
  • The notices describe no warning sign before any of these parts fracture

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2020 hood-latch striker recall on replacement hoods for 2020 Silverado 2500/3500 and Sierra 2500/3500, the 2023 door striker recall on 2024 Blazer, Blazer EV, Equinox and Terrain, and the 2025 grille deflector recall on 2022 Sierra 1500 trucks each describe a fracture risk that could let a hood, door, or grille deflector come loose while driving. For all three, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, dealers replace the whole hood assembly for the 2020 recall, contacting owners who bought hoods over the counter separately; replace all four door strikers and their attaching bolts for the 2023 recall; and reinforce or repair the attachment points on the chrome grille deflector for the 2025 recall. All of this work is done free of charge.

Common questions

Is the 2020 GMC hood recall the same as the earlier 20V-142 recall?

No, it's an expansion. This 2020 recall covers vehicles that were not part of the earlier campaign but received a replacement hood with the same defective striker wire during other service, such as collision repair, on 2020 Silverado and Sierra 2500/3500 trucks.

Which vehicles share the GMC Terrain door striker recall?

The same door striker defect was recalled on the 2024 Chevrolet Blazer, Blazer EV, and Equinox alongside the GMC Terrain. Dealers replace all four door strikers and their attaching bolts on each of these vehicles, free of charge.

Does the grille deflector recall affect all GMC Sierra 1500 trucks?

No, it only covers 2022 GMC Sierra 1500 trucks equipped with chrome front grille deflectors. Dealers reinforce or repair the attachment points on the affected trucks, free of charge.
