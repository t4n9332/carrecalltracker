Mercedes-Benz structure recalls: roof panels, Sprinter frames and loose bolts

9 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Structure · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 6, 2025

Two threads run through this group: detaching exterior panels on Mercedes-Benz cars and missing or loose fasteners on Sprinter vans. The front roof panel between the windshield and the panoramic sunroof appears in two recalls, one covering 2014-2020 C-Class, CLA, GLA, S-Class, GLC and GLE models and another covering a long list that includes the 2019 GLC350e. Tailgate side spoilers on 2020-2021 GLB250 and 2021 GLB35 AMG can also detach. On the van side, 2019-2021 Sprinter frame members may be missing reinforcement fasteners, the partition wall may be missing bolts on 2019-2023 Sprinters, 2020 Sprinter front door hinge screws may be loose, and a 2020 Sprinter propeller shaft connection may loosen. A 2023-2025 GLC C-pillar recall involves crash impact protection.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Greater force may be needed to open the doors after a severe frontal accident, in the 2019 Sprinter abutting wedge recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the nine notices in this group tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park the vehicle outside. That includes the Sprinter propeller shaft crossmember recall, which describes a fuel leak and fire risk if the shaft damages the fuel tank during a crash, the two front roof panel recalls, the Sprinter frame member and partition wall recalls, the door hinge and abutting wedge recalls, the GLB spoiler recall, and the GLC C-pillar recall. The notices set out the defect and the free repair only.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and replace the Sprinter crossmember as necessary, inspect and repair the frame members, secure the partition wall, check and tighten the front door hinge screws, and inspect the abutting wedge installation. The roof panel recalls replace the front panel, the GLB side spoilers are inspected and replaced if necessary, and the GLC roof lining and C-pillar trim are modified. Each notice states the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Why does my Mercedes-Benz have a roof panel recall?

Two recalls cover the front roof panel between the windshield and the panoramic sunroof. One describes adhesive bonding that might deteriorate; the other describes a panel not bonded correctly during a prior repair. Dealers install a new front panel in both.

Does the Sprinter frame recall affect the third-row seats?

The notice says longitudinal frame members may be missing reinforcement fasteners, which reduces stability and decreases the structural integrity of the third-row seats, and that the seat mountings can fail during a crash. Dealers inspect and repair the frame members.

Do any of these structure recalls tell owners to stop driving?

None of the nine notices give a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. They describe crash and injury risks and the free dealer repair. Owners can reach DVUSA at 1-877-762-8267 for the Sprinter recalls or MBUSA at 1-800-367-6372 for the cars.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
