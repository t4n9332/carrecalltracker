What structural defects has Mercedes-Benz recalled across its lineup?

9 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Structure · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-06-06

Structural recalls split between Sprinter vans and passenger models with panoramic sunroofs. Four separate 2019-2020 Sprinter recalls cover a driveshaft connection that can loosen at the crossmember, missing frame reinforcement fasteners that reduce stability and weaken third-row seat mounts, a partition wall that can detach in a crash, an incorrectly installed door wedge, and loose front door hinge screws. Separately, the panoramic sunroof's front roof panel — spanning models like the GLC350e, C-Class, GLA and S-Class from 2014-2020 — has needed adhesive-bonding repairs twice, in 2021 and again in 2023. The 2021 AMG GLB35 had detachable tailgate spoilers, and the 2025 GLC 300 has a roof pillar that may not absorb enough crash impact.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No owner-noticeable warning sign is described for the driveshaft connection, frame fasteners, partition wall, door wedge, or roof pillar defects — these were found through inspection or crash testing, not something a driver would feel
  • A panoramic sunroof's bonded front roof panel can visibly detach from the vehicle and become a hazard in the road
  • Loose front door hinge screws can change the gap between the door and the vehicle body
  • AMG GLB35 tailgate spoilers can loosen and detach, creating a hazard in the road

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these nine recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. The Sprinter driveshaft connection, frame fastener, partition wall, door wedge, and door hinge screw recalls, the two panoramic sunroof/roof panel recalls, the AMG GLB35 tailgate spoiler recall, and the GLC roof pillar recall all describe the defect and the resulting crash or injury risk, but none instructs owners to stop driving the vehicle or to park it outside while awaiting repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and, as necessary, replace or tighten the affected part: the driveshaft crossmember, frame reinforcement fasteners, partition wall bolts, door wedge installation, and door hinge screws on Sprinter vans; the bonded front roof panel on sunroof-equipped models; the tailgate spoilers on the AMG GLB35; and the roof lining and C-pillar trim on the GLC. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why do Mercedes-Benz panoramic sunroof panels keep detaching?

The adhesive bonding of the front roof panel between the windshield and sunroof can deteriorate over time; a second 2023 recall found that some prior repairs weren't bonded correctly either, so overlapping models were addressed twice.

Are the Sprinter van structural recalls all related?

No — each addresses a separate part found independently: the driveshaft crossmember, frame reinforcement fasteners, the partition wall, the door wedge, and the door hinges are five different defects, not one shared cause.

What does the 2025 GLC roof pillar recall mean for safety?

In certain rear-seat crash scenarios, the C-pillar may not absorb enough impact to meet federal occupant-protection standards, which can increase the risk of injury; dealers modify the roof lining and pillar trim for free.
