Why do Mazda's new CX-70 and CX-90 hybrids have electrical recalls?

5 NHTSA recalls · Mazda · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-09-02

Three of these five Mazda electrical recalls involve the new 2025 CX-70 and/or CX-90 hybrids, each with a different software fault: one had inverter software that could cut drive power in EV mode, one had a Dash Electrical Supply Unit that could disable the defroster, seat belt warning, 360-degree camera, or battery cooling at startup, and one had a fuel gauge that could read inaccurately. The other two recalls are older and unrelated: the 2018 CX-9 had a wiring harness fault that could disable the passenger air bag, turn signals, or the engine starting system, and the 2019 Mazda3 had incorrect programming that could turn off the passenger air bag deactivation light or seat belt warning lights even when they should be on.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A disabled passenger front air bag, inoperative turn signals, or a car that won't start
  • A passenger air bag deactivation light or seat belt warning light that stays off when it should be on
  • A sudden loss of drive power while in EV mode
  • A malfunctioning defroster, seat belt warning, 360-degree camera, or battery cooling at startup
  • An inaccurate fuel gauge reading

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. That covers the wiring harness recall on the 2018 CX-9, the warning-light recall on the 2019 Mazda3, the inverter software recall on the 2025 CX-70, the electrical unit software recall on the 2025 CX-70 and CX-90, and the fuel gauge recall on the 2025 CX-70 and CX-90. For each of them, the notices only say they do not tell owners to stop driving while the repair is scheduled.

What the free repair involves

Dealers repair each defect on its own terms: repairing the wiring harness on the CX-9, replacing the warning-light assembly on the Mazda3, and updating software — inverter, dash electrical unit, or body control module — on the CX-70 and CX-90 recalls. All of these repairs are done free of charge.

Common questions

Why do the new Mazda CX-70 and CX-90 have so many electrical recalls?

Three separate software faults have been found in these 2025 hybrids: one affecting the inverter and drive power, one affecting the dash electrical unit and features like the defroster, and one affecting the fuel gauge reading. Each is fixed with its own software update, free of charge.

Is it dangerous to keep driving a Mazda with an open electrical recall?

The notices for these recalls don't tell owners to stop driving. What each defect can do varies — from a wrong fuel gauge reading to a disabled air bag — so check your specific recall notice and schedule the free repair.

Are the CX-9 wiring recall and the CX-70/CX-90 recalls related?

No. The 2018 CX-9 recall involves a wiring harness that can disable the passenger air bag, turn signals, or starting system. The CX-70 and CX-90 recalls involve separate software faults in newer hybrid models built years later.
