Toyota Electrical Recalls Cover Fire Risks, Stalling and Blank Displays

18 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Electrical System · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 18, 2026

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Electrical short circuits recur in these Toyota notices, involving the 2016-2018 Prius engine harness, 2021-2022 RAV4 Prime converter, 2013-2018 RAV4 replacement battery fit, and 2023-2024 Prius and Prius Prime rear door switches. Supra starter recalls cover certain 2020-2022 vehicles for relay corrosion and certain 2021-2023 vehicles for internal component wear. RAV4 and RAV4 Prime recur across battery, converter, hybrid-control software, and instrument-panel notices. Display failures appear in certain 2022-2023 Tundra and Tundra Hybrid vehicles and a separate September 2025 notice spanning multiple Toyota models. Other notices address airbag deployment and aftermarket Highlander windshield wiring associated with possible engine stalling.

Warning signs to watch for

  • November 2018 aftermarket Highlander windshield recall: ECM damage may result in engine stalling.
  • February 2023 RAV4 Prime hybrid-control recall: the hybrid system may shut down and stall under the stated cold-weather EV-mode acceleration conditions.
  • February 2023 Tundra display recall: the instrument-panel LCD may go blank.
  • April 2024 Prius/Prius Prime door recall: an unlocked rear door may open unexpectedly.
  • September 2025 instrument-panel recall: vehicle speed, brake-system warnings, and tire-pressure warning lights may fail to display.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One supplied notice carries a park-outside instruction: the September 2025 starter relay recall covering certain 2020-2022 Toyota Supra vehicles tells owners to park outside and away from structures until the remedy is complete. None carries a stop-driving instruction; the remaining supplied notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Separately, the July 2023 RAV4 Prime converter recall advises owners not to charge when ambient temperature is below, or expected to drop below, 41°F (5°C). That charging instruction belongs specifically to the converter recall.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies include Prius harness inspection and replacement if damaged, with protective sleeve or tape; RAV4 Prime converter replacement; RAV4 battery clamp, tray, and terminal-cover replacement; and starter replacement for both Supra recalls. Software updates address specified airbag, hybrid-control, and display recalls. Other notices specify windshield/ECM work, instrument-panel inspection/replacement, or rear door switch replacement. The September 2025 display remedy distinguishes PHEVs.

Common questions

Which Toyota Supra electrical recall tells owners to park outside?

The September 2025 starter relay recall covering certain 2020-2022 Supra vehicles instructs owners to park outside and away from structures until the remedy is complete. The January 2026 starter recall covering certain 2021-2023 Supra vehicles does not give that instruction.

What charging restriction applies to the RAV4 Prime converter recall?

The July 2023 converter notice for certain 2021-2022 RAV4 Prime vehicles advises against charging when ambient temperature is below, or expected to fall below, 41°F (5°C). Dealers will replace the DC-DC converter free of charge.

How do the Toyota instrument-display recall repairs differ?

The February 2023 Tundra notice specifies a free combination-meter ECU software update. The September 2025 notice specifies free OTA instrument-panel software updates for non-PHEVs; for PHEVs, dealers inspect the assembly and either replace it or update its software, free.
