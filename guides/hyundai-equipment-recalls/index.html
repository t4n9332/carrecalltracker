Which Hyundai models were recalled over incorrect or missing labels?

4 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Equipment · model years 2020–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-09-13

All four of these Hyundai recalls involve information printed on a label rather than a mechanical part. Three concern tire and weight data: 2020 Sonata vehicles built between October 22, 2019 and February 13, 2020 carry a driver's door tire pressure label and owner's manual listing an incorrect tire size; certain 2020 Kona vehicles have a certification label showing an incorrect Gross Axle Weight Rating; and certain 2024 Tucson vehicles have a tire and loading information label that may incorrectly state spare tire information. The fourth covers certain 2025 Tucson vehicles whose glove box air bag warning label may be missing. Tucson appears twice, in the 2024 and 2025 model years. Each recall is filed as a failure to meet a Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving. For the 2020 Sonata, Hyundai says the incorrect tire size on the label may mislead an owner into installing the wrong tire size, potentially affecting handling. For the 2020 Kona, an incorrect Gross Axle Weight Rating could result in the vehicle being overloaded. For the 2024 Tucson, a label that states spare tire pressure when no spare is on board may cause confusion during an emergency tire replacement. For the 2025 Tucson, the notice concerns the risk an air bag poses to a child in the front seat.

What the free repair involves

For the 2020 Sonata, dealers install new label stickers over the tire pressure label and in the owner's manual. For the 2020 Kona, dealers install stickers showing the correct GAWR over the affected areas of the certification label. For the 2024 Tucson, dealers inspect and replace the tire and loading information label as necessary. For the 2025 Tucson, Hyundai mails owners the warning labels. All are free of charge.

Common questions

My Hyundai's label is wrong - does that mean the car itself is defective?

These four notices describe label and manual information, not a mechanical part. Each is recorded as a failure to meet a Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard, and the notices state the risks that incorrect or missing information can create for owners.

Do I have to visit a dealer for the 2025 Tucson air bag label?

That notice says Hyundai will mail owners the warning labels free of charge, with owner notification letters mailed October 4, 2024. The other three recalls in this group are handled by dealers, who install or replace the affected labels.

What does the notice say about my 2020 Sonata's wrong tire size label?

The notice for the 2020 Sonata says the door label and owner's manual state an incorrect tire size and that dealers install new label stickers over both. It does not tell owners to stop driving, and it gives no other interim instruction.
