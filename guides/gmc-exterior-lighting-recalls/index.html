GMC lighting recalls: headlights, brake lights and dim turn signals

6 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 10, 2024

Lighting is GMC's most repeated recall theme, and daytime running lights account for two of these six. One notice covers 2021 Yukon and Yukon XL; a later one covers 2022-2023 Sierra 1500, Yukon and Yukon XL along with several Cadillac, Buick and Chevrolet models. In both, the DRLs may not deactivate when the headlights are on. The remaining four are one-offs: 2024 Canyon headlights that may flicker while driving and while in park, 2024 Sierra EV front turn signals too similar in brightness to the parking lights, a 2022 Sierra 1500 accessory sport bar whose high-mounted brake light may not function, and 2017-2018 GMC G3500-based ambulances with Whelen brake lights that may come on when the pedal has not been pressed.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Daytime running lights that do not turn off when the headlights are on (2021 Yukon and Yukon XL recall, and the 2022-2023 Sierra 1500, Yukon and Yukon XL recall)
  • Headlights that flicker while driving and while the vehicle is in park (2024 GMC Canyon recall)
  • Front turn signals that are difficult to see because they are too similar in brightness to the parking lights (2024 GMC Sierra EV recall)
  • A high-mounted brake light on the accessory sport bar that does not function, or that blocks the truck's existing high-mounted brake light (2022 Sierra 1500 sport bar recall)
  • Brake lights that illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed (2017-2018 GMC G3500-based ambulance recall)

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; each states that owners are not told to stop driving. What they describe is how the fault affects other road users: glare from daytime running lights that do not deactivate, reduced visibility from flickering 2024 Canyon headlights, 2024 Sierra EV turn signals that may not clearly indicate the driver's intentions, and brake lights on the sport bar and ambulance recalls that can reduce visibility to other drivers or confuse them. The notices say nothing more about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

Both daytime running light recalls are a body control module software update — over the air on the 2021 Yukon recall, and by a dealer or over the air on the 2022-2023 recall. Dealers replace a headlight module on the 2024 Canyon and the Sierra EV headlamp LED driver module, and inspect the sport bar and provide correct wiring if needed. Whelen replaces the ambulance brake lights, free of charge.

Common questions

Why were daytime running lights recalled twice on GMC SUVs?

Two separate notices cover DRLs that may not deactivate when the headlights are on: one for 2021 Yukon and Yukon XL, another for 2022-2023 Sierra 1500, Yukon and Yukon XL plus other GM models. Both are addressed with body control module software.

My 2024 Canyon headlights flicker — is that this recall?

The notice describes headlights that may flicker while driving and while the vehicle is in park on certain 2024 Canyon vehicles. Dealers replace a module in the headlight, free of charge, and owner notification letters were mailed October 10, 2024.

Does the sport bar recall mean my accessory bar is removed?

The notice says dealers will inspect the installation of the accessory bar and provide the correct vehicle wiring, if necessary, free of charge. It does not describe removing the bar. Owner notification letters were mailed on January 27, 2023.
