GMC lighting recalls: daytime running lights, brake lights, and headlights

6 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-10-10

Across these six GMC-linked lighting recalls, the most frequent problem is daytime running lights failing to switch off when headlights are on: a 2021 recall covers GMC Yukon and Yukon XL, alongside Cadillac Escalade, Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Suburban and Tahoe, and a 2022 recall covers 2022-2023 Sierra 1500, Yukon and Yukon XL alongside Cadillac and Buick models; both were fixed with an over-the-air update. The rest are unrelated single-model issues: a 2022 Silverado and Sierra 1500 accessory sport bar can disable the brake light, a 2024 GMC Canyon's headlights may flicker, a 2024 GMC Sierra EV's turn signals may be too dim, and 2018 Ford and GMC G3500 ambulances may show false brake lights.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Daytime running lights that stay on even after the headlights have been turned on.
  • A high-mounted brake light on an accessory sport bar that doesn't work, or that blocks the truck's factory brake light.
  • Headlights that flicker while driving or while the vehicle is parked (GMC Canyon).
  • Front turn signals that are hard to see because they're similar in brightness to the parking lights (GMC Sierra EV).
  • Brake lights that illuminate even though the brake pedal has not been pressed (ambulance chassis).

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six notices carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. GM says daytime running lights that don't deactivate can cause glare and reduce visibility for other drivers, but the notices do not tell owners to stop driving; both daytime running light recalls were addressed with an over-the-air update. The sport bar brake light, ambulance brake light, Canyon headlight flicker, and Sierra EV turn signal notices likewise do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Fixes vary by recall: the two daytime running light recalls are corrected with an over-the-air body control module update. Dealers inspect and rewire the sport bar's brake light connection, replace the Whelen brake lights on affected ambulances, replace a module inside the GMC Canyon's headlight, and replace the headlamp LED driver module on the GMC Sierra EV. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Which GMC models are affected by the daytime running light recalls?

GMC Yukon and Yukon XL appear in both DRL recalls - a 2021 recall and a broader 2022 recall that also covers Sierra 1500 - alongside Cadillac, Buick and Chevrolet models. Both are fixed with a free over-the-air update.

Can I fix the GMC Canyon flickering headlight myself?

The recall notice does not describe a self-fix; GM says a dealer will replace a module inside the headlight, free of charge, after owner notification letters were mailed in October 2024.

Why are GMC Sierra EV turn signals recalled?

GM says the front turn signal and parking lights on the 2024 Sierra EV can be too similar in brightness, making the turn signal hard to see; dealers replace the headlamp LED driver module for free.
