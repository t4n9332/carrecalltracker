Why Do So Many Nissan And Infiniti Models Have Rearview Camera Recalls?

9 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-11-26

These nine recalls span seven years and multiple root causes, but they all end the same way: no rearview image when the vehicle is shifted into reverse. Early recalls (2018-2019 models, then a 2020 Leaf recall) involved camera settings or image glitches. Later recalls trace to infotainment software: a Sirius XM subscription setting rebooting the head unit on 2021-2022 Rogue, Frontier, and Pathfinder models, and separate reboot or startup software errors affecting 2024-2025 Rogue, Infiniti QX80, 2025 Armada, Murano, and Kicks. A third pattern is physical: damaged camera wiring harnesses distort or blank the image on 2019-2021 Altima, 2020-2021 Sentra, and 2018-2021 Leaf models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rearview camera screen stays blank when shifted into reverse
  • Camera image appears distorted or disappears entirely (harness-damage recalls on Altima, Sentra, and Leaf)
  • Infotainment system reboots or restarts on its own, then loses the rearview display
  • Backup camera setting silently stuck off, so no image shows even though the camera works

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these nine recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Across every affected model — including the 2018-2019 vehicles with the settings issue, the 2020 Leaf, the 2021-2022 Rogue, Frontier, and Pathfinder infotainment reboot recalls, the Altima, Sentra, and Leaf harness recalls, and the 2024-2025 Rogue, Kicks, Armada, Murano, and QX80 software recalls — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving, even though the loss of the rearview image is described as increasing the risk of a crash.

What the free repair involves

Depending on the recall, dealers update the back-up camera or infotainment software (sometimes delivered over the air instead of a dealer visit), or inspect and replace a damaged camera wiring harness, applying protective tape and rerouting it if undamaged. All of these repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Are all these Nissan backup camera recalls the same problem?

No. They involve at least three different causes across different models and years: camera settings glitches, infotainment software reboots, and damaged camera wiring harnesses. What they share is the same symptom — no rearview image when you shift into reverse.

Can the backup camera software update be done without a dealer visit?

Yes, for several of these recalls. The infotainment reboot recalls affecting Rogue, Frontier, Pathfinder, and Titan models, the 2024-2025 Rogue/QX80 software fix, and the 2025 Armada/Murano/QX80 recall can all be delivered as an over-the-air update instead of requiring a dealer visit.

Why did the Altima, Sentra, and Leaf need a wiring harness fix instead of a software update?

Those recalls involve physical damage to the rearview camera wiring harness, not a software bug. Dealers inspect the harness and replace it if damaged, or apply protective tape and reroute it if it is not, free of charge.
