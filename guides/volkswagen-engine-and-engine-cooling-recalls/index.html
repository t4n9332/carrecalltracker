Volkswagen engine and cooling recalls: rod bearings, radiator leaks, Taos stalls

5 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2022–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 23, 2023

These five Volkswagen recalls fall into three repeating problems. Engine connecting rod bearings that may become damaged and lead to engine failure appear twice: once for 2021-2023 Audi Q5 Sportback and Q5 with 2022-2023 Atlas FL and Atlas Cross Sport, and again for 2024 Atlas PA2 and Atlas Cross Sport PA. Engine control unit software that may cause a stall when coming to a stop appears twice as well, both times on 2022 Taos vehicles with all-wheel drive. The fifth covers 2022-2023 Golf R A8 and Golf GTI vehicles, where a radiator that may not have been attached correctly to the intercooler can damage a coolant hose. Atlas and Taos dominate the group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A stall when coming to a stop (both 2022 Taos all-wheel-drive engine control unit software recalls).
  • Engine failure and a stall, and an oil leak if the engine fails (the 2022-2023 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport and Audi Q5 connecting rod bearing recall, and the 2024 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport connecting rod bearing recall).
  • A leak from a damaged coolant hose, including large amounts of coolant spilling onto the roadway (2022-2023 Golf R A8 and Golf GTI radiator recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The two connecting rod bearing recalls, covering 2022-2023 and 2024 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport plus 2021-2023 Audi Q5 models, do not tell owners to stop driving. Neither 2022 Taos engine control unit software notice tells owners to stop driving. The 2022-2023 Golf R A8 and Golf GTI radiator notice does not tell owners to stop driving. Several describe an increased risk of a crash or fire, but none of them gives owners an interim instruction to follow before the repair.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is free of charge. For both connecting rod bearing recalls, dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace the affected engine. For the 2022-2023 Golf R A8 and Golf GTI radiator recall, dealers inspect the radiator and replace any damaged parts as necessary. For both 2022 Taos recalls, dealers update the engine control unit software. None of the notices describes an over-the-air update.

Common questions

Why are there two Taos stall recalls for the same model year?

Both notices cover 2022 Taos vehicles with all-wheel drive and describe engine control unit software that may cause a stall when coming to a stop. The notices do not say how the two relate. Dealers update the ECU software free of charge.

Will my Atlas get a whole new engine under the bearing recall?

The notices say dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace the affected engine, free of charge. That applies to 2022-2023 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport, 2024 Atlas PA2 and Atlas Cross Sport PA, and the listed Audi Q5 models.

What does the Golf R radiator recall say could happen?

The notice says a damaged coolant hose can leak, causing the engine to overheat and increasing fire risk, and that large amounts of coolant on the roadway can increase crash risk for other vehicles. Dealers inspect and replace damaged parts free.
