Why do Volkswagen engines keep failing or stalling?

5 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2022–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-08-23

Five Volkswagen engine recalls fall into three recurring problems. Connecting rod bearings can become damaged and cause engine failure, first affecting the 2021 Audi Q5 and 2022-2023 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport, then recurring a year later in the 2024 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport. Separately, the 2022 Taos with all-wheel drive was recalled twice, in 2021 and again in 2022, for engine control unit software that can stall the engine when coming to a stop. A fifth, unrelated recall covers 2022-2023 Golf R vehicles whose radiator may be attached incorrectly, damaging a coolant hose. Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, Taos and Golf R models are hit most.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Visible coolant leak or a puddle under the vehicle (Golf R radiator/hose issue)
  • Engine overheating from a damaged coolant hose (Golf R)
  • The engine stalling as the vehicle comes to a stop (Taos ECU software, both recalls)
  • Sudden engine failure or stall while driving (Audi Q5 and Atlas/Atlas Cross Sport bearing recalls)
  • The notices do not describe a warning light or noise occurring before these failures

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the Golf R coolant hose recall, the Audi Q5 and Atlas/Atlas Cross Sport connecting rod bearing recalls, and both Taos ECU software recalls, the notices describe the risk of continuing to drive - overheating, fire, stalling, or a crash - but do not instruct owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside.

What the free repair involves

For the Golf R, dealers inspect the radiator and replace damaged coolant parts as necessary. For the Audi Q5, Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport bearing recalls, dealers inspect the engine and replace it if necessary. For the Taos, dealers update the engine control unit software. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why was the Atlas engine bearing problem recalled twice?

The connecting rod bearing defect first affected the 2022-2023 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport, then a separate recall covered the 2024 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport for the same type of bearing damage, so both model-year groups need dealer inspection.

Does the Taos stalling recall affect every 2022 Taos?

No. Both Taos recalls specifically name 2022 Taos vehicles equipped with all-wheel drive; the notices do not describe this ECU software stalling issue in other Taos configurations.

Is the Golf R coolant leak related to the Atlas engine recalls?

No. The Golf R recall involves a radiator that may be attached incorrectly, damaging a coolant hose. The Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport and Audi Q5 recalls involve a separate defect in the engine's connecting rod bearings.
