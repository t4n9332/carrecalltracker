Why do Honda and Acura airbags deploy without warning or fail?

4 NHTSA recalls · Honda · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-21

Four Honda and Acura air bag recalls in this group involve air bags disabled or deploying when they should not. Two describe a front passenger seat weight sensor that may crack and short circuit: a 2024 recall covering 2020-2022 Accord, CR-V, Civic, Pilot, Odyssey, Acura MDX, RDX and TLX, among other models, and a broader 2026 recall extending the same defect back to 2016 and forward to 2026 models. A 2026 recall covers 2018-2022 Odyssey vehicles, where a software error can cause the side and side curtain air bags to deploy unexpectedly. Separately, a 2019 recall covers the CR-V, where burrs inside the steering wheel's metal core can damage the wiring harness, disabling the driver's air bag or causing it to deploy without warning.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An air bag deploying with no crash (2018-2022 Odyssey software error)
  • A driver's air bag disabled or deploying without warning due to steering wheel wiring damage (2019 CR-V)
  • The notices describe no warning sign for the cracked front passenger seat weight sensor before a crash occurs (2024 and 2026 recalls)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. That includes the 2019 CR-V steering wheel recall, the 2024 seat weight sensor recall, the 2026 Odyssey side air bag software recall, and the 2026 seat weight sensor recall. For the 2019 CR-V, the notice describes a disabled air bag or one that can deploy without warning; for the 2026 Odyssey, an air bag that can deploy with no crash; for the two seat sensor recalls, an air bag that may deploy unintentionally during a crash. None of the notices tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

All four repairs are free and require a dealer visit. For the two seat weight sensor recalls, dealers replace the sensors. For the 2026 Odyssey recall, dealers reprogram the SRS control unit software, or replace the unit if needed. For the 2019 CR-V, dealers install a protective cover on the steering wheel core and replace the clockspring and harnesses.

Common questions

Are the two Honda seat weight sensor recalls the same problem?

Yes. Both involve a front passenger seat weight sensor that can crack and short circuit, causing the air bag to deploy unintentionally during a crash. The 2024 recall and the broader 2026 recall cover different, overlapping ranges of Honda and Acura models.

Does the 2026 Odyssey air bag recall require a crash to happen?

No. The notice says a software programming error can cause the side and side curtain air bags to deploy unexpectedly, which the notice describes separately from the seat sensor defect that affects air bags during a crash.

What does the 2019 CR-V steering wheel recall actually affect?

Burrs inside the steering wheel's metal core can damage the wiring harnesses routed through it, which can disable the driver's air bag or cause it to deploy without warning. Dealers replace the clockspring and harnesses free of charge.
