Why do Honda air bags deploy unintentionally or fail?

4 NHTSA recalls · Honda · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 21, 2026

Two of these four Honda recalls share one part: the front passenger seat weight sensor, which the notices say may crack and short circuit. The 2024 recall covers 2020-2022 Pilot, Accord, Civic sedan, HR-V and Odyssey, 2020-2021 CR-V, CR-V Hybrid, Passport and Ridgeline, and several Acura models, and says the sensor can fail to suppress the air bag as intended. The 2026 recall spans far more model years, including 2016-2022 Accord, 2016-2022 Civic, 2017-2022 CR-V and Pilot and 2018-2026 Odyssey. Odyssey also appears alone: a software programming error can cause the side and side curtain air bags to deploy unexpectedly on 2018-2022 models. The 2019 CR-V steering wheel recall stands apart from the rest.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. The two seat weight sensor recalls say an air bag that deploys unintentionally during a crash can increase the risk of injury. The 2018-2022 Odyssey notice says air bags that deploy unexpectedly can increase the risk of injury. The 2019 CR-V notice says a disabled air bag would increase injury risk in a crash, and an uncommanded driver's air bag deployment increases the risk of injury and a crash.

What the free repair involves

All four repairs are free of charge. On both seat weight sensor recalls, dealers replace the seat weight sensors. On the 2018-2022 Odyssey recall, dealers reprogram the supplemental restraint system electronic control unit software or replace the control unit, as necessary. On the 2019 CR-V recall, dealers install a protective cover on the steering wheel core and replace the clockspring and the harnesses within.

Common questions

Can my Odyssey be covered by more than one of these recalls?

The notices list Odyssey in more than one. The side air bag software recall covers 2018-2022 Odyssey, the 2026 seat weight sensor recall covers 2018-2026 Odyssey, and the 2024 seat weight sensor recall lists 2020-2022 Odyssey. A VIN check shows which ones apply to your vehicle.

What exactly goes wrong with the passenger seat weight sensor?

Both notices say the front passenger seat weight sensor may crack and short circuit. The 2024 recall says this can fail to suppress the air bag as intended, and the 2026 recall says the air bags can deploy unintentionally during a crash.

Do these Honda and Acura air bag repairs cost anything?

Each of the four notices states the repair is free of charge: replacing the seat weight sensors, reprogramming or replacing the Odyssey restraint system control unit, and installing a protective cover with a new clockspring and harnesses on the 2019 CR-V.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
