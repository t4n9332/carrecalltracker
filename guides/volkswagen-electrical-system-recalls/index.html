Why does the Volkswagen ID.4 keep getting battery and electrical recalls?

18 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 30, 2026

⚠ 3 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Volkswagen's electrical recalls cluster heavily on the ID.4. High-voltage battery fire risk repeats: 2023-2024 ID.4s twice for battery cell modules with misaligned electrodes, 2023-2025 ID.4s for a battery that may overheat, and 2023-2026 ID.4s for one that may fail and lead to fire. Loss of drive power recurs: 2021 ID.4s for soldering inside the high-voltage battery and for battery management software, and 2023 ID.4s for coolant leaking into the high-voltage system, with rear-wheel-drive models recalled for a 12-volt charging cable contacting the steering column. Outside the ID.4, 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport relays and the 2025-2026 Jetta transmission ground wire carry fire risk, the 2019 Beetle is recalled over a missing brake warning display, and older Golf, GTI, Jetta and Beetle models over key removal and rollaway.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport relay recall: damaged relays could inadvertently activate the horn or engine starter, which can remain activated for an extended period.
  • 2019 Beetle and Beetle Convertible recall: the instrument cluster may not provide a visual warning when a brake system failure is detected.
  • Key removal recall covering GTI, Golf, Beetle, Golf SportWagen and Jetta models: the key may be removable from the ignition when the shift lever is not in Park.
  • 2023 ID.4 coolant leak recall: coolant in the high-voltage system may impact safety-critical systems and warning lights, causing a loss of vehicle control or unintended air bag deployment.
  • 2021 ID.4 flexible printed circuit recall: an unreliable battery connection may cause a stall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

No notice here tells owners to stop driving. Three carry park-outside instructions: the 2023 ID.4 12-volt battery charging cable recall, where owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the remedy is complete, and the two 2023-2024 ID.4 battery cell module recalls, where owners are advised to park outside immediately after charging, not to charge indoors overnight, not to use Level 3 DC chargers, and to limit charge to 80%. The 2023-2026 ID.4 battery failure notice advises charging to a maximum of 80% until the repair is complete. The remaining notices give no driving instruction.

What the free repair involves

Each notice that describes a completed remedy says it is free of charge. Dealers inspect and replace relays; repair or replace the 12-volt charging cable; replace battery cell modules; update high-voltage battery and pulse inverter software; replace the high-voltage battery as necessary; replace the electric motor if needed; update instrument cluster software; install an additional switch and circuit board; and repair the Jetta transmission ground wire. The 2023-2026 ID.4 remedy is under development.

Common questions

What do the notices say about charging my ID.4 before the battery recall repair?

The 2023-2024 battery cell module notices advise parking outside immediately after charging, not charging indoors overnight, not using Level 3 DC chargers, and limiting charge to 80%. The 2023-2026 battery failure notice advises charging only to 80% until the repair is complete.

How many separate ID.4 battery recalls are listed here?

The ID.4 high-voltage battery recalls here cover 2021 models for soldering inside the battery and for battery management software, 2023-2024 models twice for misaligned cell electrodes, 2023-2025 models for overheating, and 2023-2026 models for a battery failure leading to fire.

Does the Volkswagen key removal recall include my Jetta or Golf?

That notice covers 2015-2019 GTI, 2015-2016 and 2018-2019 Golf, 2012-2019 Beetle and Beetle Convertible, 2017-2019 Golf SportWagen, and 2011-2018 Jetta vehicles with automatic transmission, manual handbrake and keyless entry. Dealers install an additional switch and circuit board, free of charge.
