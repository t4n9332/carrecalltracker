Volkswagen Electrical Recalls Involve Battery Fires and Drive Power Loss

18 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Electrical System · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 30, 2026

⚠ 3 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

The ID.4 dominates the supplied Volkswagen electrical notices, particularly 2023–2024 vehicles with battery fire concerns. Specific notices identify misaligned battery module electrodes, while others separately describe battery overheating or failure. Other ID.4 defects include 2023 charging cable insulation wear, a 2021 battery connection problem, and 2021 control software that can interrupt drive power. Beyond the ID.4, damaged relays can activate the horn or starter in 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles, and 2025–2026 Jetta vehicles have a transmission ground wire concern. Older Golf, GTI, Beetle, Golf SportWagen, and Jetta vehicles appear in a key-removal recall; 2019 Beetles have a brake-warning display issue.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall 97FR: The horn or engine starter may activate inadvertently and remain activated.
  • Recall 37M2: The ignition key may be removable when the transmission shift lever is not in Park.
  • Recall 9302: An unreliable battery connection may cause a stall.
  • February 1, 2023 ID.4 recall: Battery management restart or inverter deactivation may cause loss of drive power.
  • Recall 90L8: The instrument cluster may fail to show a visual warning when a brake system failure is detected.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Three supplied notices give park-outside instructions; none orders stopping driving. The December 2022 ID.4 cable recall says park outside, away from structures, until repaired. The December 2025 and January 2026 ID.4 misaligned-electrode recalls each say park outside immediately after charging, avoid indoor overnight charging, avoid Level 3 DC charging, and limit charging to 80% until repaired. The September 2026 ID.4 battery-failure recall limits charging to 80% until repaired. The remaining supplied electrical notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Specified free dealer remedies include relay inspection and replacement as necessary under 97FR, cable inspection and repair or replacement under the December 2022 notice, battery module replacement under 9302, and battery software updates with battery replacement as necessary under 93EA. The September 2026 remedy remains under development; the supplied misaligned-electrode excerpts do not specify the final repair.

Common questions

Which ID.4 electrical recalls tell owners to park outside?

The December 2022 charging cable notice requires parking outside and away from structures until repaired. The December 2025 and January 2026 misaligned-electrode notices require parking outside immediately after charging and prohibit leaving vehicles charging indoors overnight until repaired.

Do the ID.4 battery notices give the same charging instructions?

The December 2025 and January 2026 misaligned-electrode notices specify an 80% limit and avoiding Level 3 DC chargers until repaired. The September 2026 battery-failure notice specifies an 80% limit until repaired; its supplied text does not add those other charging restrictions.

What is the repair for Volkswagen's key-removal electrical recall?

Recall 37M2 calls for dealers to install an additional switch and circuit board free of charge. It concerns specified automatic-transmission GTI, Golf, Beetle, Beetle Convertible, Golf SportWagen, and Jetta vehicles equipped with a manual handbrake and keyless entry.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
