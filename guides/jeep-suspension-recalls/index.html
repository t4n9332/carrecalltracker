Which Jeep suspension recalls involve control arms or coil springs?

7 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Suspension · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-09-01

Suspension recalls across Jeep models mostly involve control arms and, more recently, rear coil springs on the Grand Cherokee. The 2021 Cherokee has a front lower control arm that can fracture while driving; the 2018 Compass and 2020 Wrangler each have a separate control arm or bracket weld defect that can let the arm separate or detach. The Grand Cherokee's rear coil springs have been the subject of three recalls — in 2023, January 2026, and September 2026 — each addressing springs that were incorrectly installed and can detach while driving. A separate 2021-2023 Grand Cherokee recall covers an upper control arm ball joint that can let a wheel fall outward.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No warning sign is described before the 2021 Cherokee front lower control arm can fracture while driving
  • The three Grand Cherokee rear coil spring recalls (2023, and twice in 2026) describe no noticeable sign before a spring detaches while driving
  • The Compass control arm weld, Wrangler control arm, and Grand Cherokee ball joint recalls also describe no owner-noticeable warning sign

Is it safe to keep driving?

One of these seven recalls — the 2021 Jeep Cherokee front lower control arm recall — tells owners not to drive their vehicles until they are inspected and repaired if needed, because the arm can fracture without warning. The other six recalls, covering the 2018 Compass and 2020 Wrangler control arms, the three Grand Cherokee rear coil spring recalls, and the Grand Cherokee ball joint recall, do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the control arms, coil springs, and ball joints and replace them as necessary — including new front axles for the Wrangler recall and rear coil spring lower isolators for the newest Grand Cherokee recall. All of this work is done free of charge across all seven recalls.

Common questions

Why has the Grand Cherokee had multiple rear coil spring recalls?

The same incorrect installation issue was recalled three times — in 2023, January 2026, and September 2026 — as Chrysler issued interim notices before a final repair, replacing the rear coil spring lower isolators, became available.

Is it safe to drive a Jeep Cherokee with the control arm recall?

The notice tells owners not to drive their 2021 Cherokee until it has been inspected and repaired if needed, because the front lower control arm can fracture while driving without warning.

What Jeep models have control arm recalls besides the Cherokee?

The 2018 Jeep Compass has a front control arm welding defect, and the 2020 Jeep Wrangler has a control arm bracket that may not be properly welded, both of which can let the arm separate or detach.
