Which Jeep suspension recalls cover control arms and rear coil springs?

7 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Suspension · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-09-01

Two problems account for nearly all of Jeep's suspension recalls. Rear coil springs that may be incorrectly installed and detach while driving on the Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L have been recalled three times - in June 2023, January 2026 and September 2026 - each covering 2021-2023 Grand Cherokee L and 2022-2023 Grand Cherokee, with the newest notice adding the Grand Cherokee 4XE. Front lower control arms make up three more: incorrect welds on the 2018 Compass, a misplaced bracket and weld on the 2020 Wrangler, and arms that may fracture while driving on the 2021 Cherokee Latitude and Limited. A 2024 recall covers upper control arm ball joints on Grand Cherokee models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One recall carries a driving instruction: the 2021 Jeep Cherokee front lower control arm recall, where owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until they have been inspected and repaired if needed. The other six notices do not tell owners to stop driving. That covers the 2018 Compass and 2020 Wrangler control arm recalls, the 2024 Grand Cherokee ball joint recall, and all three Grand Cherokee rear coil spring recalls, which state only that a spring detaching while driving increases the risk of a crash.

What the free repair involves

Remedies vary. Dealers inspect the front lower control arms and replace them as necessary on the 2021 Cherokee and 2018 Compass; inspect the 2020 Wrangler front axles and replace as necessary; replace the upper control arm pinch bolts under the 2024 recall; inspect and repair the rear coil spring assemblies; and replace the rear coil spring lower isolators under the September 2026 notice. All work is free of charge.

Common questions

My Grand Cherokee L appears in three rear coil spring recalls. Which one applies to it?

The June 2023, January 2026 and September 2026 notices all list 2021-2023 Grand Cherokee L and 2022-2023 Grand Cherokee. The June 2023 notice says that recall is replaced by another NHTSA recall. A VIN lookup on NHTSA's site shows which remain open.

Is the rear coil spring repair available now?

The January 2026 notice says interim letters were mailed February 12, 2026, and additional letters will follow once the final remedy is available, anticipated in March 2026. The September 2026 notice says interim letters are expected September 29, 2026, with a further notice later.

What does the 2021 Cherokee control arm recall tell owners to do?

That notice says owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until they have been inspected and repaired if needed. Dealers inspect the front lower control arms and replace them as necessary, free of charge. The recall began November 6, 2020.
