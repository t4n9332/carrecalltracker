Why can some Chrysler Group vehicles roll away or lose power?

3 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · Power Train · model years 2018–2018 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2018-07-26

Three 2018 recalls from Chrysler (FCA US LLC) cover unrelated transmission and axle problems across different vehicles. The widest one covers 2018 Chrysler 300, Dodge Challenger, Charger and Durango, Jeep Grand Cherokee and Wrangler, and RAM 1500 models built with an incorrect transmission park lock rod that may not hold the vehicle in Park. Separately, a 2018 Chrysler Pacifica (non-hybrid) and 2019 Jeep Cherokee are recalled for a transmission weld that may fail and stop the vehicle from moving. A third recall covers 2018 Chrysler Pacifica vehicles whose front axle halfshafts may not be properly secured to the CV joint, which can also cause a loss of drive or unwanted movement while in Park.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Vehicle may not stay in the 'Park' position (park lock rod recall)
  • Vehicle stops transmitting power to the wheels while driving (transmission weld recall)
  • Vehicle loses drive or can move while in Park (axle shaft recall)
  • The notices describe no dashboard warning before these failures

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. The park lock rod recall says the transmission may not hold the vehicle in Park, increasing the risk of unintended movement and a crash. The transmission weld recall says the vehicle can stop transmitting power to the wheels while moving. The axle shaft recall says the vehicle can lose drive or move while in Park. In each case the notices describe the risk but do not instruct owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers handle each defect differently: for the park lock rod recall they install the correct rod; for the transmission weld recall they replace the entire transmission; for the axle shaft recall they replace the affected left or right halfshaft assembly. All three repairs are performed free of charge after Chrysler mails owner notification letters.

Common questions

Does the park lock rod recall mean my Chrysler could roll away when parked?

The notice says an incorrect rod may prevent the transmission from shifting into and holding 'Park,' which it says increases the risk of unintended vehicle movement and a crash — it doesn't say the vehicle will roll away on its own.

Is the Chrysler Pacifica transmission weld recall the same as the axle shaft recall?

No, they're separate 2018 recalls. The weld recall covers a transmission component that may stop the vehicle from moving; the axle recall covers halfshafts that may disengage from the CV joint, causing loss of drive or movement in Park.

Which vehicles are covered by the park lock rod recall?

The 2018 Chrysler 300, Dodge Challenger, Dodge Charger, Dodge Durango, Jeep Grand Cherokee, Jeep Wrangler, and RAM 1500 — all recalled together for a transmission that may have the incorrect park lock rod installed.
