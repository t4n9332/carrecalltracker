What are the Chrysler transmission and axle recalls about?

3 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · Power Train · model years 2018–2018 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2018-07-26

These three recalls share a theme of the vehicle not staying put or not moving as expected. Two of them concern the transmission. In 2018 Dodge Charger, Durango and Challenger, Jeep Grand Cherokee and Wrangler, Chrysler 300 and RAM 1500 vehicles, an incorrect transmission park lock rod may leave the transmission unable to shift into Park. In 2019 Jeep Cherokee and 2018 non-hybrid Chrysler Pacifica vehicles, a transmission component may not be properly welded, and the notice says the vehicle will stop moving if that weld fails. The third recall is specific to the 2018 Pacifica: front axle halfshafts that may not be properly secured to the constant-velocity joint. All three were announced in 2018.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The transmission may not shift into Park and keep the vehicle from moving (2018 park lock rod recall).
  • The vehicle stops moving, if the transmission weld fails (2019 Jeep Cherokee and 2018 Pacifica transmission recall).
  • A loss of drive, or the vehicle moving while in Park, if the axle shaft disengages from the CV joint (2018 Pacifica halfshaft recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The park lock rod notice says an incorrect rod may leave the transmission unable to shift into Park and hold the vehicle, increasing the risk of unintended movement. The transmission weld notice says the vehicle will stop moving if the weld fails. The halfshaft notice says a disengaged shaft means a loss of drive, or lets the vehicle move while in Park. Beyond those statements the notices give no interim instructions.

What the free repair involves

All three remedies are parts replacements done free of charge; there is no software update or over-the-air fix in this group. Dealers install the correct park lock rod on the 2018 Charger, Durango, Challenger, Grand Cherokee, Wrangler, 300 and RAM 1500 vehicles; replace the transmission on the 2019 Jeep Cherokee and 2018 Pacifica; and replace the left or right halfshaft assembly on the 2018 Pacifica.

Common questions

Is my 2018 Pacifica covered by more than one of these recalls?

Two of the three name the 2018 Pacifica: the front axle halfshaft recall, and the transmission weld recall, which covers 2018 non-hybrid Pacificas along with 2019 Jeep Cherokees. The park lock rod recall does not list the Pacifica.

Will the whole transmission be replaced?

Only in the weld recall covering 2019 Jeep Cherokee and 2018 non-hybrid Pacifica vehicles, where dealers replace the transmission free of charge. The park lock rod recall has dealers install the correct rod instead, and the halfshaft recall replaces the left or right halfshaft assembly.

What do these notices say about the vehicle moving while parked?

The park lock rod notice says the transmission may not shift into Park and keep the vehicle from moving, increasing the risk of unintended movement. The halfshaft notice says a disengaged shaft may allow the vehicle to move while in Park. Neither tells owners to stop driving.
