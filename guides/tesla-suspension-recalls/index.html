Tesla Suspension Recalls Involve Loose Fasteners and Fractured Knuckles

5 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Suspension · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 1, 2026

Loose front suspension connections recur across these Tesla notices, with Model Y appearing in four recalls and Model 3 in two. The November 2020 recall concerns upper control arm bolts on certain 2020 Model Y vehicles. The October 2021 and March 2023 notices concern lateral link fasteners on their specified Model 3 and Model Y vehicles; the September 2026 notice concerns lateral link fasteners on certain 2026 Model Y vehicles. A different November 2021 recall covers potentially fractured front and rear suspension knuckles on certain 2020-2022 Model Y vehicles. Across the notices, separation can affect steering, wheel alignment, stability, or vehicle control.

Warning signs to watch for

  • November 2020 Model Y control arm recall: wheels can lean inward or outward after upper control arm detachment.
  • October 2021 Model 3/Model Y lateral link recall: separation could shift wheel alignment and cause instability.
  • March 2023 Model 3 lateral link recall: separation could shift wheel alignment and cause instability.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the five supplied Tesla suspension notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. This applies to the November 2020 Model Y control arm bolt recall, October 2021 Model 3/Model Y lateral link recall, November 2021 Model Y knuckle recall, March 2023 Model 3 lateral link recall, and September 2026 Model Y lateral link recall. The notices describe crash risks involving reduced steering ability, instability, reduced control, or loss of vehicle control, depending on the specific suspension defect.

What the free repair involves

For the November 2020 recall, dealers inspect and tighten control arm bolts as necessary. Tesla Service tightens or replaces lateral link fasteners for the October 2021 and March 2023 recalls, and inspects and replaces knuckles as necessary for November 2021. September 2026 adds alignment checks and related damaged-part replacement as necessary. These remedies are free.

Common questions

Which Tesla models appear most often in these suspension recalls?

Model Y appears in four of the five notices, including recalls covering certain 2020 vehicles, 2020-2021 vehicles, 2020-2022 vehicles, and 2026 vehicles. Model 3 appears in the October 2021 recall for 2019-2021 vehicles and March 2023 recall for 2018-2019 vehicles.

Are the Model Y control arm and knuckle repairs the same?

The November 2020 control arm recall calls for inspecting and, as necessary, tightening the bolts. The November 2021 knuckle recall calls for inspecting and replacing the knuckles as necessary. Both notices state that their respective repairs are free.

Does the 2026 Model Y suspension recall include an alignment check?

The September 2026 notice says Tesla Service will tighten or replace the fasteners and check wheel alignment, free of charge. It also specifies replacing related parts that may have been damaged, as necessary. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.
