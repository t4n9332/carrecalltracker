Why do Tesla Model Y and Model 3 suspension fasteners keep loosening?

4 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Suspension · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 31, 2023

Two patterns repeat across these four Tesla suspension recalls. Three involve fasteners: the 2020 Model Y recall covers bolts connecting the front upper control arm and steering knuckle that may not have been properly tightened, while two later recalls cover front suspension lateral link fasteners that may loosen and allow the lateral link to separate from the sub-frame — one for 2019-2021 Model 3 and 2020-2021 Model Y, another for 2018-2019 Model 3. The fourth is different: front and rear suspension knuckles on 2020-2022 Model Y may fracture, causing suspension links to separate. Model Y is named in three of the four recalls, Model 3 in two. All four concern front suspension components, and one also covers the rear.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2020 Model Y control arm bolt recall says a detached upper control arm can cause the wheels to lean in or out.
  • Both lateral link recalls — 2019-2021 Model 3 with 2020-2021 Model Y, and 2018-2019 Model 3 — say a lateral link separation could shift the wheel alignment and cause instability.
  • The 2020-2022 Model Y knuckle recall says suspension links that separate can reduce vehicle stability and control.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2020 Model Y control arm bolt notice, the lateral link notice covering 2019-2021 Model 3 and 2020-2021 Model Y, the 2020-2022 Model Y knuckle notice and the 2018-2019 Model 3 lateral link notice each describe a crash risk without giving any interim driving instruction. The notices set out what Tesla Service will do and when owner letters were mailed; they say nothing further about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls Tesla Service handles the work free of charge. For the 2020 Model Y control arm recall, dealers inspect and, as necessary, tighten the bolts. For both lateral link recalls — 2019-2021 Model 3 and 2020-2021 Model Y, and 2018-2019 Model 3 — Tesla tightens or replaces the lateral link fasteners as necessary. For the 2020-2022 Model Y knuckle recall, Tesla inspects and replaces the knuckles as necessary.

Common questions

Which Teslas do these four suspension recalls cover?

One covers 2020 Model Y front upper control arm bolts. Two cover front suspension lateral link fasteners — 2019-2021 Model 3 with 2020-2021 Model Y, and 2018-2019 Model 3. The fourth covers front and rear suspension knuckles on 2020-2022 Model Y.

Will Tesla charge me for this suspension work?

Each of the four notices says the work is free of charge — tightening bolts, tightening or replacing lateral link fasteners, or inspecting and replacing knuckles as necessary. The notices direct owners to Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752.

Do the notices say anything about driving while I wait for the repair?

They do not. None of the four notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside, and none gives an interim instruction. They state the crash risk, the remedy Tesla Service will perform, and when owner letters were mailed.
