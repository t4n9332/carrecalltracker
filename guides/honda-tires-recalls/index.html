Why are Honda tires and tire repair kits under recall?

6 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Tires · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 8, 2026

Six Honda tire recalls split into three patterns. Two involve Continental tires that may have been cured beyond specification limits: the 2021 Pilot recall covers CrossContact LX Sport 245/50R20 tires with a specific DOT serial and mold number, while a second recall covers 2019-2020 Passport and Pilot vehicles whose tires may have been cured too long. Two more concern the tire repair kit on 2023-2026 CR-V Hybrid and Accord Hybrid models, along with CR-V Fuel Cell EV, where pressure may build in the sealant bottle and the cap may detach; the later recall covers vehicles previously remedied under recall 26V366. The remaining two are labeling and leak issues: a pinhole in a Bridgestone tire on the 2022 Odyssey, and a 2024 CR-V tire identification number missing its four-digit date code.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2021 Pilot Continental tire recall (U8Y) says over-cured tires may develop a break in the sidewall resulting in sudden air loss, or a belt edge separation leading to tread/belt loss.
  • The 2022 Odyssey Bridgestone recall (HBL) says an affected tire may contain a pinhole, causing a slow air leak.
  • The 2024 CR-V recall (BIS) says the tire identification number is missing its four-digit date code.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. For the tire repair kit recall on 2023-2026 CR-V Hybrid, Accord Hybrid and CR-V Fuel Cell EV announced in June 2026, Honda mailed interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk, with additional letters to follow once the final remedy is available. The 2024 CR-V notice states that an owner whose tire identification number information is missing may not recognize the vehicle is in a recall and continue to drive on recalled tires, increasing the risk of a crash.

What the free repair involves

On the two Continental recalls, dealers inspect all four tires and replace the affected ones. For the 2022 Odyssey, dealers inspect all four tires and replace a tire if a pinhole is found. For the 2024 CR-V, dealers inspect and replace tires as necessary. For the tire repair kit recalls, dealers replace the sealant bottle or nozzle, or inspect and replace the nozzle. All of this is free of charge.

Common questions

How do I know whether my Honda has the recalled tires?

The 2021 Pilot notice identifies Continental CrossContact LX Sport 245/50R20 102 H tires with DOT serial number A376 D3K9 2920 and mold numbers S-421920 and S-421921. For the other tire recalls, the notices say dealers inspect the tires, so a dealer check confirms it.

I already had the tire repair kit recall done — why another letter?

The October 2026 recall covers 2023-2026 CR-V Hybrid, Accord Hybrid and 2025 CR-V Fuel Cell EV vehicles previously remedied under NHTSA recall number 26V366. Its notice says dealers inspect and replace the tire repair kit nozzle, as necessary, free of charge.

Does a missing tire date code on my 2024 CR-V actually matter?

The notice says the tire identification number is missing its four-digit date code, so the vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 139. Dealers inspect and replace the tires as necessary, free of charge; letters were mailed August 27, 2024.
