Honda tire recalls: over-cured tires, pinholes and missing date codes

5 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Tires · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 4, 2026

Five Honda recalls concern tires, and the most repeated issue is tire curing. Continental tires cured beyond specification limits cover 2021 Pilot vehicles with specific DOT serial and mold numbers and 2019-2020 Passport and Pilot vehicles; those notices describe a sidewall break with sudden air loss, or belt edge separation leading to tread and belt loss. Two compliance problems follow: Bridgestone tires on the 2022 Odyssey that may contain a pinhole causing a slow air leak, and 2024 CR-V tires whose tire identification number is missing its four-digit date code. The newest recall is not about a tire at all — it covers the tire repair kit in 2023-2026 CR-V Hybrid, Accord Hybrid and 2025-2026 CR-V Fuel Cell EV.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2022 Odyssey Bridgestone recall describes a pinhole causing a slow air leak.
  • The Continental tire recalls on the 2021 Pilot and 2019-2020 Passport and Pilot describe sudden air loss from a sidewall break, or tread and belt loss from belt edge separation.
  • The 2024 CR-V recall describes a tire identification number that is missing its four-digit date code.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That covers both Continental tire recalls, the 2022 Odyssey pinhole recall, the 2024 CR-V date code recall and the tire repair kit recall. The date code notice states that an owner who cannot read the full tire identification number may not realize the tires are recalled and may keep driving on them. For the tire repair kit recall, the notice says interim letters were mailed July 27, 2026, with further letters once the final remedy is available.

What the free repair involves

For the Continental recalls dealers inspect all four tires and replace the affected ones — on the 2021 Pilot, those matching the listed DOT serial and mold numbers. For the Odyssey recall dealers inspect all four tires and replace any with a pinhole. For the 2024 CR-V dealers inspect and replace tires as necessary. For the repair kit recall dealers replace the nozzle or sealant bottle. All free of charge.

Common questions

How do I tell whether my Pilot has the recalled Continental tires?

The 2021 Pilot notice names Continental CrossContact LX Sport 245/50R20 102 H tires with DOT serial number A376 D3K9 2920 and mold numbers S-421920 and S-421921. Dealers inspect all four tires and replace the ones matching those numbers, free of charge.

My 2024 CR-V tires have no date code. What does that mean?

The notice says the tire identification number is missing its four-digit date code, so the vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 139. Dealers inspect and replace the tires as necessary, free. Owner letters were mailed August 27, 2024.

Can I use the tire repair kit in my CR-V Hybrid or Accord Hybrid?

The notice says pressure may build within the sealant bottle, causing the cap to detach and become a projectile that can strike the user or people nearby. It does not tell owners whether to use the kit. Dealers replace the nozzle or sealant bottle, free.
