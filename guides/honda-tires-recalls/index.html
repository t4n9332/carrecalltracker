Honda tire recalls: which Pilots, Odysseys and CR-Vs are affected?

5 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Tires · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-04

Honda's tire recalls split into two kinds. Three are tire defects: Continental tires cured too long during production on 2019-2020 Pilot and Passport, the same over-curing on 2021 Pilot with Continental CrossContact LX Sport 245/50R20 tires, and Bridgestone tires containing a pinhole that leaks air slowly on 2022 Odyssey. The Pilot shows up in both over-curing recalls, making it the most repeated model here. The other two are not about how tires wear at all: 2024 CR-V tires missing the four-digit date code from the tire identification number, a labeling violation of FMVSS 139, and 2023-2026 CR-V Hybrid, Accord Hybrid and CR-V Fuel Cell EV tire repair kits whose sealant bottle cap can detach under pressure.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A tire that loses air with no obvious puncture, or pressure that keeps dropping (2022 Odyssey pinhole)
  • Sudden air loss or tread separation at speed — the over-cured Continental tires can fail with no warning
  • Pressure building inside the sealant bottle while using the tire repair kit
  • The missing date code recall produces nothing you can feel; the four-digit code is simply absent from the tire marking

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The over-cured Continental tires on 2019-2021 Pilot and Passport are the serious ones, because a tire can lose air or shed tread without warning, so get the inspection done right away if it has not been done. The 2022 Odyssey pinhole leaks slowly, so watch your tire pressure until the inspection. The missing date code is a labeling problem, and the repair kit recall risks injuring whoever uses the kit rather than affecting how the vehicle drives.

What the free repair involves

For the tire recalls, dealers inspect all four tires and replace any that are affected — over-cured Continentals, a Bridgestone with a pinhole, or CR-V tires whose identification number lacks the date code. For the hybrid tire repair kit, dealers replace the nozzle or the sealant bottle, a remedy still at the interim stage with final letters to follow. Everything is free of charge.

Common questions

How do I know if my Pilot has the affected Continental tires?

The 2021 Pilot recall names Continental CrossContact LX Sport 245/50R20 102 H tires with DOT serial number A376 D3K9 2920 and mold numbers S-421920 and S-421921. Dealers check all four tires and replace only the matching ones, free.

Do I get four new tires or just one?

Dealers inspect all four tires in each of these recalls but replace only the ones actually affected — over-cured, pinholed, or missing the date code. How many tires you receive depends on what the inspection turns up.

What is wrong with the tire repair kit in my CR-V Hybrid?

When the kit is used, pressure may build inside the sealant bottle and cause the cap to detach. The notice describes this as an injury risk to the user, not a driving problem, and places no restriction on driving the vehicle.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
