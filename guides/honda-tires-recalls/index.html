Why does Honda keep recalling vehicles for tire problems?

5 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Tires · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-04

Honda's five tire recalls since 2020 fall into three groups. Two involve Continental tires that were cured too long during manufacturing, risking sidewall breaks or tread separation: the 2021 Pilot with CrossContact LX Sport tires, and the 2019-2020 Passport and Pilot. A third, on the 2022 Odyssey, involved Bridgestone tires with a manufacturing pinhole causing a slow air leak. A fourth, on the 2024 CR-V, was a labeling defect where the tire identification number was missing its date code, not a tire failure itself. The newest, in 2026, covers a tire repair kit's sealant bottle on Accord Hybrid and CR-V Hybrid models, not the tires themselves.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Sudden air loss or a break in the tire sidewall (2020 Pilot and 2021 Passport/Pilot Continental tire recalls)
  • A slow, pinhole tire leak (2021 Odyssey Bridgestone tire recall)
  • A tire ID number missing its date code, making it hard to tell if your tires are recalled (2024 CR-V)
  • A tire repair kit bottle cap that can detach and fly off under pressure (2026 Accord Hybrid/CR-V Hybrid)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls tell owners to stop driving or to park outside. The two Continental over-cured tire notices (2020 Pilot, 2021 Passport/Pilot) and the Bridgestone pinhole notice (2021 Odyssey) describe a risk of sudden air loss or tread separation but do not instruct owners to stop driving. The 2024 CR-V labeling recall and the 2026 tire-repair-kit recall likewise do not tell owners to stop driving. Owners are told to have dealers inspect and replace tires or kit parts as needed.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect all four tires and replace any found with the affected DOT codes or defects (2020, 2021 Continental recalls; 2021 Odyssey; 2024 CR-V labeling issue), free of charge. For the 2026 tire repair kit recall, dealers replace the kit's nozzle or sealant bottle; Honda says a final remedy part was still being finalized as of the interim notice.

Common questions

Are all of Honda's tire recalls about the same defect?

No. Two involve Continental tires cured too long in manufacturing, one involves a Bridgestone pinhole leak, one is a labeling issue on the CR-V, and the newest involves a tire repair kit accessory, not the tires themselves.

Is the 2024 CR-V tire recall about a tire defect?

Not directly. Honda says the tire identification number is missing its date code, which could keep an owner from recognizing that their tires are subject to a separate recall.

What's wrong with the 2026 tire repair kit recall?

Pressure can build inside the kit's sealant bottle and cause the cap to detach, which Honda says can strike the user or people nearby.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
