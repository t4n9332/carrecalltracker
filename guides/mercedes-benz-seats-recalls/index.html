Mercedes-Benz seat recalls: adjustment limits, switches and headrest mounting

5 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Seats · model years 2019–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 28, 2021

Seat position in relation to the front air bag drives most of this Mercedes-Benz group. One recall covers 2021 E350 and E450, 2019-2021 AMG E53 and 2020-2021 AMG E63 vehicles whose front passenger seat may be missing the mechanical seat adjustment range limit stop. A second covers 2021 E350, E450, E450 Wagon and E63S AMG, 2020 E63S AMG Wagon and 2020-2021 E53 AMG, where the passenger seat limiter may let the seat sit too close to the air bag. A faulty seat position switch affects 2021 C-Class, E-Class, GLC, CLS, AMG GT four-door and G-Class models. The remaining two are a front seat height adjuster weld on 2019 Sprinters and rear headrest mounting on 2021 C-Class and E-Class Cabriolets.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall for 2021 E350 and E450, 2019-2021 AMG E53 and 2020-2021 AMG E63: the front passenger seat may move outside the acceptable range in relation to the air bag because the mechanical seat adjustment range limit stop may be missing.
  • Recall for 2021 E350, E450, E63S AMG and 2020-2021 E53 AMG models: the passenger seat limiter may allow the seat to be positioned too close to the air bag.
  • Recall for 2021 C-Class and E-Class Cabriolets: the rear headrests may be incorrectly mounted to the seatbacks.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the five notices in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park the vehicle outside. That covers the 2019 Sprinter front seat weld recall, both passenger seat adjustment recalls, the seat position switch recall and the Cabriolet rear headrest recall. The notices describe risks that arise in a crash, including a seat cushion that may shift forward, air bags and seat belt force limiters that may not activate properly, and headrests that could move or detach, and direct owners to the dealer remedy.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the tilt adjuster for the front seats on the 2019 Sprinter recall, inspect for the passenger seat adjustment range limitation and install it if necessary, recalibrate the passenger seat limiter on the other E-Class recall, replace the seat position switches, and inspect the rear seatback guide bushings and replace them if necessary. All free of charge.

Common questions

What does the seat position switch recall mean for my air bags?

The notice says a faulty electrical contact within the seat position switch may prevent the driver and passenger air bags and seat belt force limiters from properly activating during a crash. Dealers replace the seat position switches, free of charge.

Two E-Class recalls mention the passenger seat. Is it the same repair?

The notices describe different remedies. One has dealers inspect for the mechanical seat adjustment range limitation and install it if necessary; the other has dealers recalibrate the passenger seat limiter. Both notices say the work is free of charge.

My Sprinter seat recall letter arrived before the repair was available.

The notice says DVUSA issued owners an interim notification on December 19, 2019, and that the recall began March 9, 2020. Dealers replace the tilt adjuster for the front seats on 2019 Sprinter vehicles, free of charge.
