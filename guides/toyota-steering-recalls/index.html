Toyota steering recalls: shafts, tie rods, power steering leaks and spiral cables

9 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Steering · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 29, 2025

Nine recalls sit under Toyota steering and they fall into four patterns. Lost steering assist covers the 2007-2021 Tundra and 2008-2022 Sequoia power steering gear assembly oil leak, a matching recall of JTEKT service parts for those models, and water entering the steering gear box cover on 2019-2020 RAV4 and 2020 RAV4 Hybrid. Shafts that may fracture cover Braun-modified 2017-2020 Sienna steering shaft extensions and the 2023-2024 Corolla and Corolla Hybrid intermediate shaft. Spiral cable welds that deactivate the driver's air bag hit 2023 Corolla, Corolla Cross, Highlander and Tacoma twice, the second covering vehicles Toyota says may have been incorrectly repaired under 23V-480. Loose fasteners cover the 2018-2019 4Runner, and a tie rod the 2020 Supra.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2019-2020 RAV4 and 2020 RAV4 Hybrid electric power steering recall says a loss of power steering assist could increase steering effort at low vehicle speeds.
  • The other eight notices in this group do not describe a warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the nine notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The wording stays on crash risk: a fractured steering shaft extension on Braun-modified Siennas, or an intermediate shaft that detaches on 2023-2024 Corolla, can cause a loss of steering control; a fractured tie rod may affect handling on the 2020 Supra; an oil leak may cause a sudden loss of power steering assist on Tundra and Sequoia. The spiral cable recalls describe a driver's air bag that will not deploy. The notices add no interim instructions.

What the free repair involves

Most remedies are part replacements, all free of charge: new steering shaft extensions on Braun-modified Siennas, a new intermediate steering shaft on 2023-2024 Corolla, new steering gear tie rods on the 2020 Supra, and a new power steering gear box on 2019-2020 RAV4. Tundra and Sequoia gear assemblies and the spiral cables are inspected and replaced, and the 4Runner's fasteners inspected and re-tightened.

Common questions

Why are there two spiral cable recalls for the same 2023 models?

The 2023 recall, 23TA08, covers an insufficiently welded connection inside the steering column spiral cable that deactivates the driver's air bag. The later recall, 25TA02, covers vehicles that may have been incorrectly repaired under NHTSA recall 23V-480. Both have dealers inspect and replace the assembly free.

Does the Tundra and Sequoia power steering recall cover replacement parts too?

There are two. One covers 2007-2021 Tundra and 2008-2022 Sequoia vehicles, numbered 21TB10 and 21TA10. A separate recall, 21TH01, covers JTEKT power steering gear assembly service parts 44250-0C160, 44250-0C131, 44250-0C170 and 11250-0C121. Dealers inspect and replace the assembly free of charge.

Is the Supra tie rod recall handled by Toyota or BMW?

The notice names BMW of North America as the recalling company, and the 2020 Toyota Supra is listed alongside several BMW models. Both BMW and Toyota notify owners, and dealers replace the steering gear tie rods free of charge. Toyota customer service: 1-888-270-9371.
