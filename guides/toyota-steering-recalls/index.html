Why Do Toyota Steering Systems Keep Losing Assist Or Control?

9 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Steering · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-01-29

Nine Toyota steering recalls fall into a few repeating patterns. Power steering assist can be lost through fluid leaks: two separate 2018-2022 Tundra and Sequoia recalls cover an incorrectly manufactured power steering gear assembly, and a 2019-2020 RAV4 and RAV4 Hybrid recall covers water entering the electric power steering gear box. Full loss of steering control from a fractured part appears in a Braun-converted 2018-2020 Sienna (steering shaft extension), a 2019-2020 Toyota Supra (steering tie rod, shared with several BMW models), and a 2023-2024 Corolla and Corolla Hybrid (intermediate steering shaft). A 2018-2019 4Runner recall covers loose steering and suspension bolts. Two recalls — 2023 and 2025 — cover a steering column spiral cable that can deactivate the driver's airbag on Corolla, Corolla Cross, Highlander, and Tacoma models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering suddenly becomes harder to turn, especially at low speed (Tundra/Sequoia power steering gear leaks; RAV4 electric power steering water damage)
  • No warning sign is described before a steering shaft or tie rod fractures (Sienna, Supra, Corolla intermediate shaft recalls)
  • No warning sign is described for loose steering and suspension bolts (4Runner)
  • No warning sign is described for the airbag-disabling steering column spiral cable defect (Corolla, Highlander, Tacoma)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these nine Toyota steering recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This includes the Sienna steering shaft extension recall, the 4Runner loose-bolt recall, the shared Toyota Supra/BMW tie rod recall, the RAV4 electric power steering recall, both Tundra and Sequoia power steering gear recalls, both Corolla and Highlander spiral cable airbag recalls, and the Corolla intermediate steering shaft recall. For all nine, the notices tell owners only to have the part inspected or replaced, not to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Depending on the recall, dealers replace the steering shaft extension, tighten loose steering and suspension bolts, replace the tie rod, replace the power steering gear box or gear assembly, inspect and replace the steering column spiral cable, or replace the intermediate steering shaft. Every one of these repairs is performed free of charge.

Common questions

Does the Toyota Supra tie rod recall also involve other brands?

Yes. The 2020 recall covers the tie rod on the Toyota Supra alongside several BMW models, since the two cars share steering-gear components, but each brand's dealers handle the repair separately.

Why did Toyota recall the same steering spiral cable twice?

The 2023 recall replaced faulty spiral cables that could disable the driver's airbag; the 2025 recall covers vehicles where that 2023 repair was done incorrectly and still needs to be fixed.

Are the two Tundra and Sequoia power steering recalls the same issue?

Both 2021 recalls cover an incorrectly manufactured power steering gear assembly that can leak oil; one covers vehicles built with the part, the other covers the same part sold as a service replacement.
