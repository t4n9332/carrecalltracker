Toyota Steering Recalls Cover Assist Loss, Shaft Fractures, and Airbags

9 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Steering · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 29, 2025

Two recurring themes are power-steering gear leaks and steering-column electrical connections that can deactivate the driver’s airbag. The November and December 2021 notices concern gear assemblies for 2007-2021 Tundra and 2008-2022 Sequoia vehicles; the December notice specifically covers service parts. The July 2023 and January 2025 spiral-cable notices name certain 2023 Corolla, Corolla Cross, Corolla Cross Hybrid, Highlander, Highlander Hybrid, and Tacoma vehicles. Separate mechanical concerns include shaft-extension fractures in Braun-modified 2017-2020 Sienna vehicles, loose steering and suspension fasteners in certain 2018-2019 4Runner vehicles, tie-rod damage in certain 2020 Supra vehicles, and intermediate-shaft fractures in certain 2023-2024 Corolla and Corolla Hybrid vehicles. RAV4 notices address water-related steering-assist loss.

Warning signs to watch for

  • June 2020 RAV4 steering-gear recall: loss of power steering assist could increase steering effort at low speeds.
  • November 2021 Tundra/Sequoia gear-assembly recall: an oil leak may cause sudden loss of power steering assist.
  • December 2021 Tundra/Sequoia service-part recall: an oil leak may cause sudden loss of power steering assist.
  • June 2020 Supra tie-rod recall: a damaged or fractured tie rod may affect handling and control.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the listed Toyota steering notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The July 2019 Braun Sienna, September 2019 4Runner, June 2020 Supra, and June 2020 RAV4 notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Neither the November 2021 Tundra/Sequoia gear-assembly notice nor the December 2021 service-part notice tells owners to stop driving. The July 2023 spiral-cable, November 2024 Corolla shaft, and January 2025 spiral-cable notices also do not tell owners to stop driving. These notices provide no stop-driving direction.

What the free repair involves

Free dealer remedies: Braun Sienna—replace extensions; 4Runner—inspect/re-tighten fasteners as necessary; Supra—replace tie rods; RAV4—replace steering gearbox; November 2021 Tundra/Sequoia—inspect and replace gear assembly as necessary; December 2021 service parts—inspect and replace gear assembly; July 2023 spiral cable—inspect/replace if necessary; Corolla shaft—replace intermediate shaft; January 2025 spiral cable—inspect/replace as necessary.

Common questions

Why do steering recalls include a driver’s airbag problem?

The July 2023 and January 2025 notices describe an insufficiently welded electrical connection inside the steering-column spiral cable assembly. Separation can deactivate the driver’s airbag. Each notice specifies inspection and replacement of the spiral cable assembly when necessary.

What distinguishes the two Tundra and Sequoia steering notices?

The November 2021 notice concerns certain 2007-2021 Tundra and 2008-2022 Sequoia vehicles. The December 2021 notice specifically concerns certain JTEKT steering-gear service parts for those model ranges. Both describe an oil leak that may cause sudden loss of steering assist.

Which RAV4 steering problem could make steering harder?

The June 2020 notice covers certain 2019-2020 RAV4 and 2020 RAV4 Hybrid vehicles. Water may enter through the steering gearbox cover, causing loss of electric power steering assist and increased steering effort at low speeds. Dealers replace the gearbox free of charge.
