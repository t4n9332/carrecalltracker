Why can a BMW windshield leak lead to an electrical fire?

3 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Visibility · model years 2019–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-02-17

This group covers three recalls tied to window, sunroof and windshield sealing. A 2022 recall for X5 and X7 involves a windshield that may not be properly bonded, allowing water to enter and potentially short electrical components, raising fire risk. Two related recalls involve power windows or the sunroof closing without the key present: a 2022 recall spans X5, X6, X7 and 4 Series Gran Coupe models across model years 2019-2023, and a 2023 recall spans Z4, M2, 8 Series and 2 Series models, each citing control unit or ECU software that allowed closing without the key fob or digital key detected inside.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Water entering the vehicle around the windshield (2022 X5 and X7 recall)
  • Power windows or the sunroof closing on their own after the key fob or digital key has been removed from the vehicle (2022 X5/X6/X7/4 Series recall and 2023 Z4/M2/8 Series/2 Series recall)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three BMW visibility recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. That covers the X5/X7 windshield bonding recall, the 2022 sunroof recall for X5, X6, X7 and 4 Series Gran Coupe, and the 2023 window and sunroof recall for Z4, M2, 8 Series and 2 Series. For all three, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the windshield seal and reinstall the windshield with a new seal where needed, or reprogram the roof function control unit or update the Electronic Control Unit software. The 2023 recall also deactivates the digital key's cellular and Wi-Fi function until the software update is applied. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Can a leaking BMW windshield really cause a fire?

According to the notice, an improperly bonded windshield could let water enter and contact electrical components, possibly causing a short circuit — the notice states this increases the risk of a fire.

Why would a BMW sunroof or window close without the key present?

The notices say the roof or window control unit software incorrectly allowed closing without detecting the key fob or digital key inside the vehicle, which does not meet federal safety standards for power window pinch protection.

Is my BMW affected by both the window and sunroof recalls?

It depends on the model: the 2022 recall covers X5, X6, X7 and 4 Series Gran Coupe, while the 2023 recall covers Z4, M2, 8 Series and 2 Series — check your VIN against each recall separately.
