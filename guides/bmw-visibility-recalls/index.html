BMW windshield, sunroof and window recalls: what owners should know

3 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Visibility · model years 2019–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 17, 2023

Three BMW visibility recalls cover glass and roof openings, and two involve the same kind of fault. On 2019-2022 X5, 2020-2022 X6, 2019-2023 X7, 2022 4 Series Gran Coupe and Alpina XB7 models, the roof function control unit may be programmed so the sunroof can close without the key fob inside. A second recall covers 2023 8 Series, 2023 2 Series Coupe and 2022-2023 Z4 models, where ECU software still allows the windows and sunroof to close after the digital key is removed. The third is different: on 2023 X5 and X7 models the windshield may not be properly bonded, letting water reach electrical components, which could short-circuit.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The sunroof can close without the key fob present inside the vehicle (roof function control unit recall on X5, X6, X7 and 4 Series Gran Coupe).
  • The power windows and sunroof can still be closed after the digital key is removed (8 Series, 2 Series Coupe and Z4 ECU software recall).
  • Water may enter the vehicle and contact electrical components (2023 X5 and X7 windshield bonding recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside — including the 2023 X5 and X7 windshield recall, where BMW says an improperly bonded windshield may let water contact electrical components and short-circuit, increasing the risk of a fire. The two roof and window recalls likewise carry no such instruction; their notices describe an increased risk of injury from closing without the key present. Each notice directs owners to the dealer remedy described below.

What the free repair involves

All three repairs are free of charge. For the windshield recall, dealers inspect the windshield seal and, as necessary, remove and reinstall the windshield with a new seal. For the sunroof recall, dealers reprogram the roof function control unit. For the windows and sunroof recall, dealers update the Electronic Control Unit software; the digital key function via cellular service and Wi-Fi is deactivated until the vehicle is updated.

Common questions

How can a windshield problem cause a fire in the X5 and X7?

The notice says the windshield may not have been properly bonded, which could allow water to enter the vehicle and contact electrical components, possibly resulting in a short-circuit. BMW states that an electrical short-circuit increases the risk of a fire. The repair is free of charge.

What exactly is wrong with the sunroof in these BMW recalls?

Both concern closing without the key present. On X5, X6, X7 and 4 Series models, the roof control unit may let the sunroof close with no key fob inside. On 8 Series, 2 Series and Z4 models, software lets windows and sunroof close after the digital key is removed.

Will I lose the digital key before the software update is done?

The 8 Series, 2 Series and Z4 notice says the digital key function via cellular service and Wi-Fi is deactivated until the vehicle is updated with new software. Dealers perform that update free of charge; the other two notices mention no such change.
