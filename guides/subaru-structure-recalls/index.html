Subaru body weld and rear hatch support bracket recalls

3 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Structure · model years 2019–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 19, 2025

Two of these Subaru structure recalls concern body welds on 2019 vehicles. The 2019 Ascent notice describes missing spot welds on or around the B-pillar, while the 2019 Legacy and Outback notice identifies improperly applied spot welds on the duct below the cowl panel. Both describe reduced body strength and increased injury risk in a crash. The remaining recall concerns certain 2026 Forester vehicles with an incorrect or incorrectly oriented rear hatch support bracket that can detach without prior warning. The listed recalls therefore concentrate on 2019 body strength issues, with a separate Forester hatch support problem affecting the 2026 model year.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2026 Forester rear hatch support bracket recall: the rear hatch may fall unexpectedly during use.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. The 2019 Ascent missing spot weld recall and the 2019 Legacy and Outback body weld recall describe increased injury risk in a crash from compromised body strength. The 2026 Forester rear hatch support bracket recall describes a hatch that can fall unexpectedly during use, increasing injury risk. That Forester notice also does not tell owners to stop driving. These notices provide no stop-driving instruction for the listed structural defects.

What the free repair involves

For the Ascent weld recall, dealers inspect for missing welds; an affected vehicle is replaced free of charge. For the Legacy and Outback weld recall, dealers inspect and repair or repurchase as necessary, free of charge. For the Forester hatch recall, dealers inspect the brackets and, if necessary, replace the stay and support bracket free of charge.

Common questions

Which Subaru models have body weld recalls in this group?

The body weld notices cover certain 2019 Ascent, Legacy and Outback vehicles. The Ascent issue concerns missing welds around the B-pillar. The Legacy and Outback issue concerns improperly applied welds on the duct below the cowl panel.

Will Subaru repair or replace a vehicle with missing welds?

The Ascent notice says dealers inspect for missing spot welds and replace the vehicle free of charge if welds are missing. The Legacy and Outback notice specifies inspection followed by repair or repurchase as necessary, also free of charge.

What does the Forester rear hatch bracket recall cover?

The notice covers certain 2026 Forester vehicles with an incorrect or incorrectly oriented rear hatch support bracket. It says the bracket can detach without prior warning, allowing the hatch to fall unexpectedly. Dealers inspect and replace specified parts if necessary.
