Why do Lincoln fuel system recalls involve leaks and engine stalls?

6 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 7, 2025

Fuel leaks that can reach an ignition source run through four of these six Lincoln recalls. The 2018 Navigator with the 3.5L GTDI V6 has high pressure fuel pump welds that can fracture, the 2020 Aviator has a vapor fuel line sleeve that can chafe through the plastic liquid fuel line, the 2022 Aviator has a fuel filler tube that may detach during a crash, and the 2023 Corsair has fuel injectors that may have been assembled without O-ring support discs. The other two involve stalling: the 2021 Corsair fuel delivery module may leak inside the fuel tank and cause low fuel pressure, and the low-pressure fuel pump may fail on the 2021-2023 Aviator and 2021-2022 Navigator.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An engine stall while driving — the 2021 Corsair fuel delivery module recall and the 2021-2023 Aviator / 2021-2022 Navigator low-pressure fuel pump recall both name it.
  • The notices for the 2018 Navigator, 2020 Aviator, 2022 Aviator and 2023 Corsair fuel leak recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2018 Navigator fuel pump weld recall, the 2020 Aviator fuel line chafing recall, the 2022 Aviator fuel filler tube recall, the 2023 Corsair fuel injector recall, the 2021 Corsair fuel delivery module recall and the 2021-2023 Aviator and 2021-2022 Navigator low-pressure fuel pump recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The 2020 Aviator notice says Ford issued owners an interim notification and that a second notice would be sent once parts were available.

What the free repair involves

All six repairs are free of charge. Dealers replace the high pressure fuel pump and components on the 2018 Navigator, install a longer protective sleeve on the 2020 Aviator fuel line, install a redundant locking clip on the 2022 Aviator, fit new injector O-rings and support discs on the 2023 Corsair, replace the 2021 Corsair fuel delivery module, and update powertrain control module software for the low-pressure fuel pump recall.

Common questions

Which Lincoln models are covered by these fuel system recalls?

The 2018 Navigator, 2020 and 2022 Aviator, 2023 Corsair, 2021 Corsair, plus the 2021-2023 Aviator and 2021-2022 Navigator in the low-pressure fuel pump recall. Each recall names its own model years, so check your VIN on NHTSA.gov.

Do the notices tell Lincoln owners to stop driving over a fuel leak?

None of the six notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Each states the risk — a fuel leak near an ignition source increasing fire risk, or an engine stall increasing crash risk — and directs owners to a dealer for the free repair.

What does the low-pressure fuel pump recall do for a Lincoln?

For Lincoln Aviator and Navigator vehicles in that recall, the notice says dealers update the powertrain control module software free of charge; low-pressure fuel pump replacement is described for Super Duty vehicles. The defect can cause an engine stall while driving.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
