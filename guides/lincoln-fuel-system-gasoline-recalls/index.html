Why Are Lincoln Vehicles Recalled For Fuel Leaks And Stalls?

6 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-07-07

Lincoln's fuel system recalls in this group split into two patterns: fuel leaks that raise fire risk, and fuel delivery problems that cause engine stalls. Four recalls involve leaks - fractured fuel pump welds on the 2018 Navigator, a chafed fuel line on the 2020 Explorer/Aviator, a fuel filler tube that can detach in a crash on the 2022 Explorer/Aviator, and fuel injectors missing O-ring support discs on the 2023 Lincoln Corsair, among other models. Two more recalls involve stalling: a leaking fuel delivery module on the 2021 Corsair, and a failing low-pressure fuel pump affecting the 2021-2023 Lincoln Aviator and 2021-2022 Lincoln Navigator, among other Ford models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A fuel odor or a visible fuel leak near the pump, fuel line, filler tube, or injectors
  • The engine stalling unexpectedly while driving (fuel delivery module and low-pressure fuel pump recalls)
  • The notices describe no other warning sign before a leak or stall occurs

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices say a fuel leak near an ignition source can increase fire risk (fuel pump weld, fuel line, filler tube, and injector recalls) or that an engine stall can increase crash risk (fuel delivery module and low-pressure fuel pump recalls), but none instruct owners to stop driving. Across all six recalls in this group, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the high-pressure fuel pump, install a longer protective sleeve on the fuel line, add a locking clip to the filler tube, install new fuel injector O-rings, replace the fuel delivery module, or replace the low-pressure fuel pump/update the powertrain control module software, depending on the recall. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why are so many Lincoln fuel system recalls tied to fire risk?

Four of the six recalls in this group - involving the fuel pump, fuel line, filler tube, and fuel injectors - describe a fuel leak that can occur near an ignition source, which the notices say increases fire risk.

Which Lincoln models had an engine stall recall?

The 2021 Lincoln Corsair was recalled for a leaking fuel delivery module, and the 2021-2023 Lincoln Aviator and 2021-2022 Lincoln Navigator were recalled, along with other Ford models, for a low-pressure fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine.

Is the fuel system recall repair free for Lincoln owners?

Yes. Every recall notice in this group states that dealers will perform the repair - whether it's a pump, fuel line, filler tube, or injector fix - free of charge to the owner.
