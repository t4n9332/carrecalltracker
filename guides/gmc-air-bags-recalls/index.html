Why do GMC trucks and SUVs keep getting air bag recalls?

11 NHTSA recalls · GMC · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 21, 2026

Roof-rail air bag inflators are the running thread here: four of these eleven recalls describe a left or right RRAB inflator end cap that may detach or an inflator sidewall that may rupture, and they land on the same trucks — the 2018 Sierra 1500 and the 2018-2019 Sierra 2500 and 3500. A fifth, from 2020, covers a roof-rail inflator diffuser that may not have been properly crimped, on the 2020 GMC Acadia and Sierra 1500, 2500 and 3500. The rest are sensing and diagnostic module problems on the 2018 Terrain, 2022 Acadia and 2024-2026 Canyon AT4X AEV, a passenger air bag module on the 2019 Sierra 1500, an occupant classification problem on the 2023 Terrain, and a warning light recall covering the 2021 Yukon and Yukon XL.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The air bag malfunction indicator light illuminates inconsistently and may fail to warn of an air bag problem (2021 Yukon and Yukon XL communications gateway module recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

No notice in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That includes the four roof-rail air bag inflator recalls on the 2018 Sierra 1500 and 2018-2019 Sierra 2500 and 3500, where the notices describe an end cap or inflator fragments being propelled into the vehicle, and the sensing and diagnostic module recalls on the 2018 Terrain, 2022 Acadia and 2024-2026 Canyon. None of the notices gives owners an interim instruction to follow before the repair.

What the free repair involves

Remedies split by defect, and each notice says the repair is free. Dealers replace the roof-rail air bag modules on the affected Sierra trucks in five of these recalls. Software is reprogrammed or updated for the 2018 Terrain, the Canyon and the 2021 Yukon, where the update can also come over the air. The 2019 Sierra 1500 air bag module and the 2023 Terrain passenger presence system are replaced.

Common questions

Why has the roof-rail air bag been recalled several times on my Sierra?

Four notices in this group describe a left or right roof-rail air bag inflator end cap that may detach or a sidewall that may rupture, on the 2018 Sierra 1500 and 2018-2019 Sierra 2500 and 3500. Each says dealers replace the modules free of charge.

What if my GMC's air bag warning light behaves oddly?

The 2021 Yukon and Yukon XL recall says the communications gateway module makes the air bag malfunction indicator light illuminate inconsistently, so it may fail to warn the driver of an air bag problem. The software is updated by a dealer or over the air.

My 2022 Acadia is listed. What does that recall say?

It says the sensing diagnostic module may have been left in manufacturing mode, so the air bags will not deploy. The notice says both affected vehicles were still in dealer inventory and are returned to the assembly plant for activation, so no owner letters were mailed.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
