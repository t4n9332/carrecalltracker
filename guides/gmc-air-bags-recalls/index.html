Why does GMC keep recalling air bags on Sierra and Terrain trucks?

11 NHTSA recalls · GMC · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-21

Eleven NHTSA recalls cover air bag problems across GMC models from 2018 through 2026. The most repeated issue is a roof-rail air bag inflator whose end cap may detach or whose sidewall may rupture on 2018-2019 Sierra 1500, 2500 and 3500 trucks (shared with Chevrolet Silverado); this same defect has triggered five separate recalls between 2020 and 2026. Other recalls involve the sensing diagnostic module (SDM) failing to power down correctly (2018 Terrain), being left inactive at the factory (2021 Acadia), or running incorrect software (2025 Canyon AT4X AEV). One-off recalls cover a damaged passenger air bag module (2019 Sierra), a child-seat-detection failure (2023 Terrain), and an inconsistent warning light (2021 Yukon).

Warning signs to watch for

  • Air bag warning light may illuminate inconsistently, the one warning sign the notices describe (2021 Yukon and related GM models)
  • For the 2023 Terrain child-seat-detection recall, the risk applies specifically when a small child or child restraint is in the front passenger seat
  • The remaining recalls (roof-rail inflator, SDM, passenger air bag module, Canyon software) describe no warning sign an owner could notice before a crash

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these eleven GMC air bag recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This includes the five roof-rail air bag inflator recalls on 2018-2019 Sierra trucks, the sensing diagnostic module recalls on the Terrain, Acadia and Canyon, the passenger air bag module recall on the 2019 Sierra, the child-seat-detection recall on the 2023 Terrain, and the inconsistent warning light recall on the 2021 Yukon. The notices describe increased injury risk in a crash from each defect but do not instruct owners to stop driving or park outside.

What the free repair involves

Repairs vary by recall: dealers replace the roof-rail air bag modules, replace or reprogram the sensing diagnostic module (by dealer or over-the-air update for the warning light issue), replace the passenger air bag module or passenger presence system, or activate an unactivated module at the factory. All repairs and software updates are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why do GMC Sierra trucks keep getting the same air bag recall?

Five separate recalls since 2020 cover the same roof-rail air bag inflator defect on 2018-2019 Sierra 1500, 2500 and 3500 trucks, where the inflator's end cap may detach or its sidewall may rupture. Dealers replace the modules free of charge each time.

What is a sensing diagnostic module recall?

The SDM detects a crash and triggers air bag deployment. GMC has recalled the Terrain, Acadia and Canyon for SDM software or activation problems that could prevent air bags from deploying or cause them to deploy incorrectly.

Can I fix a GMC air bag warning light recall myself?

No. The 2021 recall for an inconsistently illuminating warning light is fixed by a software update to the communications gateway module, applied by a dealer or delivered over-the-air, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
