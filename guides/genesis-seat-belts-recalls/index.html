Are Genesis seat belt pretensioner and anchor recalls dangerous to drive with?

5 NHTSA recalls · Genesis · Seat Belts · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-06

Nearly every Genesis seat belt recall involves the pretensioner — the device that yanks the belt tight in a crash — exploding when it deploys and throwing metal fragments into the cabin. It ran from a 2021 campaign covering the 2021 GV80 and 2022 GV70 alongside Hyundai Elantra and Venue, to a much larger 2023 action covering 2020-2023 GV80, 2021-2023 G80, 2022-2023 GV70 and the 2023 GV60, and a separate 2023 G90 recall that also included the rear belts. Two other defects differ: the 2023 GV60's front passenger retractor may not let the belt fasten or extend, and 2023-2026 G90 seat belt anchors may detach entirely.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The pretensioner defect gives no warning you can see or hear — nothing is wrong until a crash occurs
  • On a 2023 GV60, the front passenger belt will not fasten or will not extend properly
  • A driver or passenger belt anchor that looks or feels loose where it attaches to the vehicle
  • On the 2023 G90 the rear belt pretensioners are also covered, so have any belt that behaves oddly inspected

Is it safe to keep driving?

No Genesis seat belt notice tells owners to stop driving or park outside, and the 2023 pretensioner recall states plainly that the vehicle is safe to drive in the meantime — the danger appears only in a crash. The exception is the 2023 GV60 whose front passenger belt may not fasten or extend: until it is replaced, do not seat anyone in that front passenger seat if the belt is not working. Everyone else should keep belting up and book the free repair promptly.

What the free repair involves

All repairs are free. The 2021 pretensioner recall has dealers replace the front pretensioners outright. The 2023 GV80, G80, GV70, GV60 and G90 recalls instead secure the pretensioners with a cap, with GV60 owners sent an interim letter first. On the GV60, dealers inspect and replace the front passenger seat belt as needed, and on the G90 they inspect and reinforce or replace the seat belt anchors.

Common questions

Can my family keep using the seat belts before the recall repair?

Yes, keep wearing them. The notices describe a failure that only appears during a crash, and none tells owners to stop driving. The one exception is the GV60's front passenger belt, which may not fasten — leave that seat empty until it is replaced.

Why do some Genesis models get a cap and others new pretensioners?

The remedy differs by campaign. The 2021 recall covering the GV80 and GV70 replaces the front pretensioners; the 2023 recalls on GV80, G80, GV70, GV60 and G90 secure them with a cap instead. Your dealer performs whichever applies to your VIN, free.

Does the seat belt anchor recall cover the same cars as the pretensioner recalls?

No. The 2026 anchor recall covers 2023-2026 G90, along with Hyundai Ioniq 6 and Santa Fe, where the driver and passenger seat belt anchors may detach. The pretensioner recalls center on the GV80, G80, GV70, GV60 and G90.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
