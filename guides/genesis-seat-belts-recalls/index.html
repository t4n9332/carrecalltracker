Why do so many Genesis models have seat belt pretensioner recalls?

5 NHTSA recalls · Genesis · Seat Belts · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-06

Five NHTSA recalls cover seat belt problems across Genesis GV80, GV70, G80, G90 and GV60 models from 2021 through 2025. Three recalls involve front seat belt pretensioners that may explode in a crash: first affecting the 2021 GV80 and 2022 GV70 (shared with Hyundai Elantra and Venue), then expanding to G80, GV70, GV80 and the 2023 GV60, and separately to the 2023 G90's driver, passenger and rear pretensioners. A fourth recall covers the 2023 GV60's front passenger seat belt failing to fasten or extend properly. A fifth, newer recall covers seat belt anchors that may detach on the 2023-2025 G90, shared with Hyundai Ioniq 6 and Santa Fe.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices do not describe a warning sign before a crash for the three pretensioner-explosion recalls
  • GV60 front passenger seat belt may not fasten or extend properly
  • The anchor-detachment recall does not describe a warning sign owners could notice beforehand

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five Genesis seat belt recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The three pretensioner-explosion recalls (2021 GV80/GV70, the GV80/G80/GV70/GV60 expansion, and the 2023 G90) describe a risk of metal fragments striking occupants in a crash but do not tell owners to stop driving. The GV60 seat belt fastening recall and the G90 seat belt anchor recall likewise do not instruct owners to stop driving, though the notices say the affected seat belts may not properly restrain occupants in a crash.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the seat belt pretensioners or secure them with a cap, depending on the recall. For the GV60 fastening issue, dealers inspect and replace the front passenger seat belt as necessary. For the seat belt anchor recall, dealers inspect and reinforce or replace the anchors as necessary. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

What happens if a Genesis seat belt pretensioner explodes?

The notices say an exploding pretensioner can project metal fragments into the vehicle and strike occupants, increasing injury risk in a crash. Dealers replace the pretensioner or secure it with a cap, free of charge.

What's wrong with the GV60's seat belt?

The notice says the automatic locking retractor may prevent the front passenger seat belt from fastening or extending properly. Dealers will inspect and replace it as necessary, free of charge.

Which Genesis seat belt recall is newest?

A 2026 recall covers seat belt anchors that may detach on the 2023-2025 G90, shared with the Hyundai Ioniq 6 and Santa Fe. Dealers inspect and reinforce or replace the anchors free of charge.
