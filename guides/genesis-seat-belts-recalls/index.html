Which Genesis seat belt recalls cover pretensioners, retractors and anchors?

5 NHTSA recalls · Genesis · Seat Belts · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 6, 2026

Seat belt pretensioners that may explode upon deployment in a crash account for three of these five Genesis recalls, and the models overlap heavily: the 2021 GV80 with the 2022 GV70; then 2020-2023 GV80, 2021-2023 G80, 2022-2023 GV70 and the 2023 GV60; and separately the 2023 G90, the only notice here that names rear pretensioners as well as the front ones. The remaining two are different problems. A 2023 recall covers the 2023 GV60, where the automatic locking retractor may stop the front passenger belt fastening or extending properly. A 2026 recall covers 2023-2026 G90 vehicles whose driver and passenger seat belt anchors may detach.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The front passenger seat belt does not fasten or does not extend properly (2023 GV60 automatic locking retractor recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The three pretensioner recalls — the 2021 GV80 and 2022 GV70 recall, the 2020-2023 GV80, 2021-2023 G80, 2022-2023 GV70 and 2023 GV60 recall, and the 2023 G90 recall — describe a risk that arises in the event of a crash, when a pretensioner may explode upon deployment. The GV60 retractor and G90 anchor notices describe a belt that may not adequately restrain an occupant in a crash. No notice here gives an interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

All of these repairs are free of charge. For the 2021 GV80 and 2022 GV70 recall, dealers replace the seat belt pretensioners. For the pretensioner recalls covering GV80, G80, GV70, GV60 and the 2023 G90, dealers secure the pretensioners with a cap. The GV60 retractor recall inspects and replaces the front passenger belt as necessary, and the G90 anchor recall inspects and reinforces or replaces the anchors.

Common questions

What does it mean that a seat belt pretensioner may explode?

The notices say that in the event of a crash the front — and on the 2023 G90, also the rear — seat belt pretensioners may explode upon deployment, projecting metal fragments into the vehicle that can strike occupants and result in injury.

My GV60 was in the pretensioner recall. Was a remedy ready?

That notice says dealers secure the seat belt pretensioners with a cap, and that GV60 owners were sent interim letters about the safety risk on April 14, 2023, with a second notice to follow once the final remedy is available.

How do I know whether my G90 is in the anchor recall?

The 2026 recall covers 2023-2026 G90 vehicles whose driver and passenger seat belt anchors may detach. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning May 29, 2026, and Genesis customer service can be reached at 844-340-9741.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
