Why do Honda rearview cameras stop showing an image?

9 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 1, 2026

Nine Honda recalls in this group all involve a rearview camera image that may not display, and three causes repeat. Water entering the camera affects Odyssey: 2019-2020 vans under one recall and 2018-2020 vans under another. Broken tailgate camera wiring affects Ridgeline twice, covering 2017-2019 trucks and 2020-2024 trucks. Software and wiring faults in the display path cover the rest: a display audio power circuit error on 2018-2020 Fit and 2019-2022 HR-V, recalled twice; a MOST cable connector on 2018-2023 Odyssey, 2019-2022 Pilot and 2019-2023 Passport; instrument panel software on 2018-2020 Odyssey, 2019-2020 Passport and 2019-2021 Pilot; and display software on the 2018 Accord and 2019 Insight. Odyssey appears in four of the nine.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A distorted image, or no image at all (2019-2020 Odyssey camera water entry recall, W83).
  • No image when the camera is set to top-down view, or switched from that view in a bright environment (2019-2020 Odyssey recall W83).
  • The instrument panel may not show engine oil pressure, speedometer or gear selector position until the next ignition cycle (Odyssey, Passport and Pilot instrument panel software recall, T89).
  • No rearview image when the engine is started with a key (2018-2020 Fit and 2019-2022 HR-V recalls 6DW and TIQ/DIR).
  • No image when the vehicle is in reverse (2018-2020 Odyssey water entry recall, HOX).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these nine notices tells owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle away from structures, and none gives an interim instruction to follow while waiting for the repair. Each notice states the same kind of risk instead: a rearview camera image that is distorted or missing reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle and increases the risk of a crash. The Odyssey, Passport and Pilot instrument panel software recall adds that operating the vehicle without a functioning instrument panel or rearview camera display increases that risk.

What the free repair involves

The remedies differ by recall. Dealers replace the rearview camera on the two Odyssey water entry recalls, replace the tailgate camera wiring harness on both Ridgeline recalls, and replace the MOST cable harness and install a straightening cover on the Odyssey, Pilot and Passport recall. The Accord and Insight, Fit and HR-V, and instrument panel recalls are software updates at the dealer. Each notice says the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Which Honda models are in these rearview camera recalls?

Odyssey appears in four of the nine recalls, Ridgeline in two, Fit and HR-V in two, and Pilot and Passport in two. The 2018 Accord and 2019 Insight display software recall completes the group. Each recall lists its own model years.

Is the rearview camera repair free?

Yes. Every notice in this group says dealers will perform the remedy, whether camera replacement, harness replacement or a software update, free of charge. Honda notifies owners by mail for each recall, and owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 to confirm whether their vehicle is included.

What do the notices say about driving before the repair?

The notices do not tell owners to stop driving, and none gives an interim instruction. They state that a camera image that does not display reduces the driver's rear view and increases the risk of a crash. Honda's customer service line for these recalls is 1-888-234-2138.
