Why do Honda rearview cameras go blank or fail to display?

9 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-01

Nine Honda recalls in this group involve a rearview camera image that fails to display, several as repeat campaigns. Water entering the camera can distort or blank the image on 2019-2020 Odyssey vans, recalled again in 2026 for 2018-2020 Odyssey vans. A tailgate wire harness that fatigues and breaks affects 2018-2019 Ridgeline trucks in one recall and 2020-2024 Ridgeline trucks in another. A design error in the audio display power circuit keeps the camera from appearing on 2018-2020 Fit and 2019-2022 HR-V models, recalled in 2023 and again in 2024. Separate recalls cover a 2018 Accord and 2019 Insight display software issue, a 2018-2020 Odyssey, 2019-2020 Passport and 2019-2021 Pilot instrument panel software bug, and a MOST cable connector fault on 2018-2023 Odyssey, 2019-2022 Pilot and 2019-2023 Passport models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No image at all, or a distorted image, when shifting into reverse (Odyssey water-intrusion recalls)
  • The rearview camera dropping out after the tailgate has been opened and closed repeatedly (Ridgeline recalls)
  • The camera failing to appear when the engine is started with a key (Fit and HR-V recalls)
  • The instrument panel display going blank, including the speedometer, gear position and oil pressure, along with the camera (Odyssey, Passport and Pilot instrument panel recall)
  • The camera image not appearing due to a faulty cable connector (Odyssey, Pilot and Passport recall)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these nine recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. That covers both Odyssey water-intrusion recalls, both Ridgeline tailgate wiring recalls, both Fit and HR-V circuit design recalls, the Accord and Insight display recall, the Odyssey/Passport/Pilot instrument panel recall, and the Odyssey/Pilot/Passport MOST cable recall. Each notice describes a reduced view behind the vehicle from a rearview camera that fails to display, or in one case an instrument panel that also goes blank, but none of the notices tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, dealers replace the rearview camera on the water-intrusion Odyssey recalls, replace the tailgate wire harness on the Ridgeline recalls, update the display audio unit software on the Fit, HR-V, Accord and Insight recalls, reprogram the instrument panel control module software for the Odyssey/Passport/Pilot recall, and replace the MOST cable harness with a straightening cover for the other Odyssey/Pilot/Passport recall. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why has Honda recalled the Odyssey rearview camera twice for water?

The first recall, in 2020, covered 2019-2020 Odyssey vans where water could enter the camera mounting holes and distort or blank the image. A 2026 recall expands that same water-intrusion issue to 2018-2020 Odyssey vans.

Are the two Ridgeline tailgate wiring recalls the same defect?

Yes. Both involve the rearview camera tailgate wire harness fatiguing and breaking from repeated tailgate use, which can stop the camera image from displaying. One recall covers 2018-2019 Ridgeline trucks and the other covers 2020-2024 Ridgeline trucks.

Why did the Fit and HR-V camera recall happen twice?

The 2023 recall and the 2024 recall both describe the same design error in the audio display power circuit that can keep the camera from appearing when the engine is started with a key, covering 2018-2020 Fit and 2019-2022 HR-V models.
