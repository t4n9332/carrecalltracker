Ram electrical recalls: relay fires, blank clusters and dead lights

12 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Electrical System · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 9, 2026

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Ram's electrical recalls fall into three clusters. The biggest is fire risk on Cummins 6.7L turbo diesel trucks, where the solid state intake heater grid relay can short: one recall covers 2021 Ram 2500, 3500 and 5500, a second adds 2022 Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500, and a third reaches the 2023 Ram 3500. The second cluster is the instrument panel — a blank display on 2025 Ram 3500 and 5500 and 2026 Ram 3500 (B4C, 35D), warnings that may not illuminate on the 2019 Ram 1500 (V83), and an incorrect brake warning light on the 2026 Ram 3500 (79C). The rest are one-offs: a 48-volt auxiliary battery connection, ProMaster taillight wiring, cruise control, a powertrain control module, and ProMaster BEV drive power.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A blank instrument panel cluster display — 2025-2026 Ram recalls B4C and 35D.
  • An instrument cluster that does not properly illuminate driver warnings — 2019 Ram 1500 recall V83; an incorrect brake system warning light — 2026 Ram 3500 recall 79C.
  • The vehicle maintains its speed or accelerates despite attempts to deactivate cruise control — the 2018 cruise control recall.
  • A stall or a no-start condition — the 2018 powertrain control module recall U87.
  • An inoperative left taillight and left turn signal — 2021-2022 Ram ProMaster recall 21B.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Three notices carry a parking instruction, all on Cummins 6.7L turbo diesel trucks: the 2021 Ram 2500, 3500 and 5500 relay recall advises owners not to park these vehicles inside buildings; the 2021-2022 relay recall advises parking outside of buildings or structures and not near other vehicles until the vehicle has been remedied; and the 2021-2023 heater grid relay recall advises parking outside and away from structures until the repair is complete. The 2018 cruise control notice advises owners to stop using cruise control. None of the twelve notices tells owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Remedies vary. The relay recalls have dealers inspect and, if needed, replace the solid state intake heater grid relay; 2021-2023 trucks get a new relay. Others are software: instrument panel updates, ProMaster BEV control module updates, and an engine or powertrain control module flash for cruise control. Others are parts: powertrain control module, instrument panel cluster, ProMaster wiring harness, and securing the auxiliary battery fasteners. All free of charge.

Common questions

Why do the diesel relay recalls tell me to park outside?

Those notices say an electrical short in the intake heater grid relay can cause a vehicle or engine compartment fire with the ignition on or off. They advise parking outside of buildings and structures, and not near other vehicles, until the vehicle has been repaired.

My Ram's instrument cluster went blank — is there a recall for that?

Chrysler recalls B4C and 35D cover a software error that may leave the instrument panel display blank on 2025 Ram 3500 and 5500 and 2026 Ram 3500. Dealers update the software, and the December 2025 recall also replaces the cluster as necessary, free of charge.

Does the cruise control recall mean I should stop driving?

That notice does not tell owners to stop driving; it advises owners to stop using cruise control. It says the defect could prevent the cruise control from disengaging, and that dealers inspect the software and flash the engine or powertrain control module free of charge.
