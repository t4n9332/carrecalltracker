Why do Ram trucks keep having electrical fire and display recalls?

12 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Electrical System · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-09

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Ram's electrical recalls cluster around a few repeating problems. Diesel Cummins trucks, Ram 2500 through 5500 cab chassis, have had the same intake heater grid relay recalled three times between 2021 and 2023 for a short-circuit fire risk. Instrument displays are the other repeat issue: separate recalls hit 2019 Ram 1500 clusters that failed to illuminate warnings, 2025-2026 Ram 3500 and 5500 trucks with blank displays, and 2026 Ram 3500 trucks showing an incorrect brake warning light. Other recalls cover a Chrysler-group powertrain control module that could stall the Ram 1500, a cruise control circuit that could fail to disengage, a 2019 Ram 1500 auxiliary battery wiring issue, a Ram ProMaster wiring harness tied to taillights, and a 2024-2025 ProMaster electric drive module that could lose power.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No warning is described before the auxiliary battery wiring overheats (2019 Ram 1500)
  • Cruise control that won't disengage when switched off or braked
  • Engine stalling or a no-start condition
  • Instrument cluster or panel warning lights that don't illuminate, or a blank display
  • Left taillight or turn signal not working (ProMaster)

Is it safe to keep driving?

Two recalls in this group carry a park-outside instruction: the October 2021 and February 2023 notices covering 2021-2023 Ram 2500, 3500, 4500 and 5500 diesel trucks with the intake heater grid relay tell owners to park outside of buildings and away from other vehicles until repaired. None of the recalls in this group carry a stop-driving instruction. For the auxiliary battery, cruise control, stall, instrument display, and ProMaster wiring recalls, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across this group, dealers mainly replace parts: the intake heater grid relay, instrument panel clusters, engine control components, and wiring harnesses, or reinstall loose battery fasteners. Several recalls are software fixes — updates to the powertrain control module, body controller module, or instrument panel software. All repairs, inspections, and part replacements are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why does the same relay recall keep coming back for Ram diesel trucks?

Chrysler issued three separate recalls (2021, 2021, and 2023) for the same intake heater grid relay in Cummins 6.7L diesel Ram trucks after the fix did not fully resolve the short-circuit fire risk, expanding to more model years each time.

Is my Ram 1500 affected by the instrument cluster recalls?

Only specific years are affected: 2019 Ram 1500 clusters could fail to illuminate warning lights, while 2025-2026 Ram 3500 and 5500 trucks have separate recalls for blank displays or an incorrect brake warning light. Check your VIN with NHTSA to confirm.

Do I need to park my Ram diesel truck outside?

Only if it's covered by the October 2021 or February 2023 relay recalls for 2021-2023 Ram 2500, 3500, 4500 and 5500 diesel trucks — those notices tell owners to park outside and away from other vehicles until repaired.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
