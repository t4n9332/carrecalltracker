Why Does Hyundai Tell Nexo Owners to Park Outside?

3 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Fuel System, Other · model years 2019–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-10-11

⚠ 3 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

All three recalls in this group affect the hydrogen fuel cell system of the Hyundai Nexo and each ends with owners being told to park outside and away from structures. The first, on the 2019 Nexo, covers a hydrogen inlet receptacle filter that could detach and leak fuel after refueling. The second, on 2019-2021 Nexo models, covers hydrogen gas detection sensors that may fail to detect a leak in the delivery system. The third, on 2019-2023 Nexo models, covers a thermally activated pressure relief device that may break and leak hydrogen gas, which Hyundai says can result in a fire while the vehicle is parked.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A hydrogen fuel odor or leak noticed after refueling (2019 Nexo inlet receptacle recall)
  • The gas detection sensors may fail to warn of a leak, so a leak may go undetected (2019-2021 Nexo recall)
  • The 2019-2023 Nexo pressure relief device recall notice describes no warning sign before a leak or fire while parked

Is it safe to keep driving?

All three of these Nexo recalls tell owners to park outside and away from structures until the repair is complete: the 2019 Nexo inlet receptacle recall, the 2019-2021 Nexo gas detection sensor recall, and the 2019-2023 Nexo pressure relief device recall. None of the notices tell owners to stop driving. The park-outside instruction applies whether the vehicle is being driven or sitting parked, because Hyundai says an undetected hydrogen leak near an ignition source can lead to a fire.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the hydrogen fuel inlet receptacle on the 2019 Nexo recall, replace the hydrogen gas detection sensors on the 2019-2021 recall, and replace the thermally activated pressure relief device on the 2019-2023 recall. All three repairs are performed free of charge, and owners are told to keep parking outside until their vehicle is repaired.

Common questions

Why is Hyundai telling Nexo owners to park outside?

All three Nexo hydrogen recalls involve a potential hydrogen gas leak that could reach an ignition source. Hyundai tells owners to park outside and away from structures until dealers complete the free repair, to reduce fire risk while parked.

What's different between the three Nexo hydrogen recalls?

The 2019 Nexo recall covers a detachable inlet filter that can leak after refueling. The 2019-2021 recall covers sensors that may fail to detect a leak. The 2019-2023 recall covers a pressure relief device that may break and leak.

Is the Nexo the only Hyundai model with hydrogen fuel recalls?

Within this group, yes — all three recalls apply only to the Nexo, Hyundai's hydrogen fuel cell vehicle, spanning model years 2019 through 2023 depending on which specific recall notice is being described.
