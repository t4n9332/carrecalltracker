Hyundai Nexo hydrogen fuel recalls: why owners must park outside

3 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Fuel System, Other · model years 2019–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-10-11

⚠ 3 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

All three of these recalls involve the hydrogen fuel system on the Hyundai Nexo, and all three tell owners to park outside and away from structures until the repair is complete. Certain 2019 Nexo vehicles are covered because the inlet receptacle filter could detach after hydrogen refueling and cause a leak. Certain 2019-2021 Nexo vehicles are covered because the hydrogen gas detection sensors may fail to detect leaks in the hydrogen gas delivery system. Certain 2019-2024 Nexo vehicles are covered because the thermally activated pressure relief device may break and cause a hydrogen gas leak, which can result in a fire while parked. The 2019 model year appears in all three.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

All three notices tell owners to park outside and away from structures until the recall remedy is completed: the 2019 Nexo inlet receptacle filter recall, the 2019-2021 Nexo hydrogen gas detection sensor recall, and the 2019-2024 Nexo pressure relief device recall. None of the three tells owners to stop driving. The sensor notice says a hydrogen gas leak can increase the risk of fire while parked or driving, and the pressure relief device notice describes a fire while parked.

What the free repair involves

Each recall has its own part replacement. For the 2019 Nexo, dealers replace the hydrogen fuel inlet receptacle. For 2019-2021 Nexo vehicles, dealers replace the hydrogen gas detection sensors. For 2019-2024 Nexo vehicles, dealers replace the thermally activated pressure relief device. All three notices say the work is free of charge. The pressure relief device recall mailed interim notification letters, with final letters to follow.

Common questions

Why does Hyundai want my Nexo parked outside?

All three notices advise parking outside and away from structures until the recall remedy is completed. They describe hydrogen leaking from the inlet receptacle, leaks the detection sensors may miss, and a broken pressure relief device that can cause a fire while parked.

My Nexo is a 2019 - could all three recalls apply?

The 2019 model year appears in all three: the inlet receptacle recall covers certain 2019 vehicles, the detection sensor recall certain 2019-2021 vehicles, and the pressure relief device recall certain 2019-2024 vehicles. A VIN lookup shows which apply to your car.

Do the notices say anything about driving my Nexo before the repair?

None of the three notices tells owners to stop driving; each tells owners to park outside and away from structures until the repair is done. The sensor notice states that a hydrogen gas leak can increase the risk of fire while parked or driving.
