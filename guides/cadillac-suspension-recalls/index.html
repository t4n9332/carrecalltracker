Which Cadillac models have front and rear suspension recalls?

3 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Suspension · model years 2022–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 10, 2025

Three recalls cover Cadillac suspension hardware, and all three come down to bolts and fasteners. The 2022 recall covers 2022 XT5 and XT6 models, listed with the 2022 GMC Acadia, where the left-rear suspension toe link may have been tightened improperly and could end up loose or separated. The 2023 recall covers the 2023 Escalade and Escalade ESV, listed with Chevrolet Tahoe, Suburban and GMC Yukon models; their rear suspension outer control arm bolts may not have been properly heat-treated and can break. The 2025 recall covers 2023-2025 Lyriq vehicles, where front stabilizer bar bracket bolts may loosen and separate from the frame, and a detached stabilizer bar or bracket can damage high voltage cables or EV battery coolant lines.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The notice for the 2022 XT5 and XT6 toe link recall, the notice for the 2023 Escalade and Escalade ESV control arm bolt recall, and the notice for the 2023-2025 Lyriq stabilizer bar bolt recall each describe the risk involved — loss of vehicle control, a misaligned rear wheel, or a vehicle shutdown with loss of drive power — but none of them gives owners an interim instruction beyond having the dealer repair performed.

What the free repair involves

For the 2022 XT5 and XT6, dealers inspect the left-rear suspension and replace the fastener if it is loose or missing. For the 2023 Escalade and Escalade ESV, dealers replace both rear suspension outer control arm bolts. For 2023-2025 Lyriq vehicles, dealers inspect and tighten the front stabilizer bar bracket bolts as necessary. Each notice states the repair is free of charge.

Common questions

Is my Cadillac Lyriq included in a suspension recall?

The 2025 recall covers certain 2023-2025 Lyriq vehicles whose left or right front stabilizer bar bracket bolts may have been improperly tightened. GM mailed owner notification letters May 8, 2025. Check your VIN with a Cadillac dealer or NHTSA to confirm inclusion.

Do any of these recalls tell owners to stop driving?

None of the three notices includes a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. They state the risks — loss of vehicle control, rear wheel misalignment, or a vehicle shutdown with loss of drive power — and direct owners to the free dealer repair. They say nothing further about continuing to drive.

Can the Lyriq stabilizer bar problem affect the battery?

The notice states that a detached stabilizer bar or bracket can damage the vehicle's high voltage cables or EV battery coolant lines, and that damaged cables or coolant lines can result in a vehicle shutdown, causing a loss of drive power.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
