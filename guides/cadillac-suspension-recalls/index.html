Why Are Cadillac SUVs Recalled Over Suspension Bolt Failures?

3 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Suspension · model years 2022–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-04-10

Three separate GM recalls cover suspension hardware on Cadillac vehicles built between 2022 and 2025. The 2022 XT5 and XT6 share a rear suspension toe-link recall with the GMC Acadia, where a left-rear fastener may have been tightened improperly. The 2023 Escalade and Escalade ESV share a rear suspension outer control-arm bolt recall with the Chevrolet Suburban, Tahoe, and GMC Yukon models, where bolts that were not properly heat-treated can break. The 2023-2025 Lyriq has its own issue: front stabilizer bar bracket bolts that can loosen and separate, which on this electric SUV can also damage high-voltage cables or battery coolant lines.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices don't describe any noise, vibration, or other warning sign owners would notice before these suspension parts fail.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the XT5/XT6/Acadia toe-link recall, the Escalade/Suburban/Tahoe/Yukon control-arm bolt recall, and the Lyriq stabilizer bar recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. GM describes the risks as loss of vehicle control from a separated or loose toe link, rear-wheel misalignment from a broken control-arm bolt, and on the Lyriq, a possible vehicle shutdown and loss of drive power if the stabilizer bar damages high-voltage cables or coolant lines.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the affected suspension components free of charge. For the toe-link recall they replace the fastener if it's loose or missing; for the control-arm bolt recall they replace both rear bolts; for the Lyriq they inspect and tighten the stabilizer bar bracket bolts as needed. No software update or OTA fix is involved.

Common questions

Is my Cadillac Escalade included in a suspension recall?

Yes, if it's a 2023 Escalade or Escalade ESV. GM recalled these along with the Chevrolet Suburban, Tahoe, and GMC Yukon and Yukon XL because the rear suspension outer control-arm bolts were not properly heat-treated and can break.

What suspension problem affects the Cadillac XT5 and XT6?

On certain 2022 XT5 and XT6 models, built alongside the GMC Acadia, the left-rear suspension toe link may have been tightened improperly and could loosen or separate, which can affect vehicle control.

Does the Cadillac Lyriq suspension recall affect the battery?

It can. On 2023-2025 Lyriq models, loose front stabilizer bar bracket bolts can separate from the frame and damage nearby high-voltage cables or EV battery coolant lines, which could cause the vehicle to shut down.
