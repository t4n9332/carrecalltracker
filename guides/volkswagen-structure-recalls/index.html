Which Volkswagen body and door recalls affect ID.4 and Tiguan owners?

5 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Structure · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 4, 2024

This group splits between the ID.4 and the Tiguan. Two recalls cover ID.4 door handles that may allow water to enter the circuit board assembly, which can cause the doors to open unexpectedly: one covering 2021-2022 vehicles, where the notice describes this happening when the vehicle is subject to high lateral forces at speeds below 9 mph, and one covering 2021-2024 vehicles. A third ID.4 recall covers 2023-2024 vehicles whose interior sunshade for the panoramic glass roof may be insufficiently fire retardant under Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 302. The Tiguan entries are body parts: an incorrect roof reinforcement assembly installed on certain 2018 vehicles during repair of roof damage, and accessory rear hatch spoilers on 2022 Tiguan and 2018-2021 Tiguan long wheel base vehicles that may be insufficiently attached.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Doors opening unexpectedly while the vehicle is in motion — stated in the 2021-2022 ID.4 door handle recall, at speeds below 9 mph under high lateral forces.
  • Doors opening unexpectedly while driving — stated in the 2021-2024 ID.4 door handle recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the five notices in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. That covers the 2023-2024 ID.4 sunshade flammability recall, the 2018 Tiguan roof reinforcement recall, the accessory rear hatch spoiler recall on 2022 Tiguan and 2018-2021 Tiguan long wheel base vehicles, and both ID.4 door handle recalls, covering 2021-2022 and 2021-2024 vehicles. The notices state the risk each defect creates in a fire, in a crash or while driving, but they give owners no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

The work varies by defect. Dealers apply flame retardant to the sunshade fabric on affected ID.4 vehicles, replace the roof reinforcement on the 2018 Tiguan, and replace the spoiler on the hatch spoiler recall. On the 2021-2022 ID.4 door recall, dealers update the vehicle software and inspect the door handles as necessary; on the 2021-2024 door recall, dealers inspect and replace the door handles as necessary and update the software. All free of charge.

Common questions

My ID.4 is a 2021 — is it in both door handle recalls?

Both the 2021-2022 ID.4 door handle recall and the 2021-2024 ID.4 door handle recall list 2021 vehicles, but each covers only certain vehicles. A VIN check against each recall is the way to confirm which ones apply to your car.

What is wrong with the ID.4 panoramic roof sunshade?

The notice says the interior sunshade for the panoramic glass roof may be insufficiently fire retardant, failing Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 302. In a fire, fabric that is not fire retardant may further fuel a fire, increasing the risk of injury. Dealers apply flame retardant free of charge.

My Tiguan has the accessory rear spoiler — what is the concern?

The notice covers 2022 Tiguan and 2018-2021 Tiguan long wheel base vehicles where, due to incorrect installation instructions, the accessory rear hatch spoiler may be insufficiently attached and may separate from the vehicle, becoming a road hazard. Dealers replace the spoiler free of charge.
