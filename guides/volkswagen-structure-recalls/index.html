Volkswagen door, roof and spoiler recalls: what owners should know

5 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Structure · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-09-04

The recurring story here is the ID.4 door handle: two recalls, in 2023 and again in 2024, both tracing to handles that let water reach the circuit board assembly so doors can open unexpectedly. The first covered 2021-2022 cars and leaned on a software update; the follow-up widened coverage to 2021-2024 ID.4s and added handle replacement. The other ID.4 issue is a panoramic roof sunshade on 2023-2024 cars that is not sufficiently fire retardant under FMVSS 302. The Tiguan accounts for the remaining two: a 2018 car that received an incorrect roof reinforcement assembly during roof damage repair, and accessory rear hatch spoilers on 2018-2022 Tiguans left insufficiently attached by incorrect installation instructions.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A door opening on its own — the 2023 notice ties it to low speeds under high lateral load
  • A door that unlatches or pops open while the vehicle is moving
  • An accessory rear hatch spoiler that looks loose, lifted, or is not solidly attached
  • No visible warning for the 2018 Tiguan roof reinforcement or the ID.4 sunshade — both are identified by records or inspection

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls tells owners to stop driving, and none requires parking outside. The two ID.4 door handle recalls deserve the most urgency: the 2023 notice limits the behavior to low speeds under high lateral load, while the 2024 notice simply says doors may open unexpectedly, which could injure an occupant or lead to a crash. The 2018 Tiguan roof reinforcement may leave less protection in a crash or rollover, so schedule it rather than postponing. The Tiguan spoiler and the ID.4 sunshade allow normal driving until the free repair.

What the free repair involves

All work is free of charge. For the 2023 ID.4 recall, dealers update the vehicle software and inspect the door handles as necessary; the 2024 recall adds inspection and replacement of the handles alongside the software update. Tiguan owners get the roof reinforcement assembly or the accessory rear spoiler replaced. ID.4 sunshades have flame retardant applied. None of these is delivered over the air.

Common questions

My ID.4 was already fixed under the 2023 door handle recall — do I need the 2024 one too?

Possibly. The 2023 recall covered 2021-2022 ID.4s and centered on a software update; the 2024 recall covers 2021-2024 cars and adds handle inspection and replacement. Check your VIN with Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298.

Why is a sunshade a safety recall?

The panoramic roof sunshade in 2023-2024 ID.4s is not sufficiently fire retardant, so the vehicle fails Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 302. It matters only once a fire has started; dealers apply flame retardant to the fabric free of charge.

Does the Tiguan spoiler recall apply to every Tiguan?

No. It covers the 2022 Tiguan and 2018-2021 Tiguan long wheelbase models fitted with the accessory rear hatch spoiler, which incorrect installation instructions left insufficiently attached. Dealers replace the spoiler free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
