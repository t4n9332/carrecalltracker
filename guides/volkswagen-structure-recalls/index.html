Why do Volkswagen ID.4 doors open unexpectedly while driving?

5 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Structure · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-09-04

These five Volkswagen structure recalls are dominated by the ID.4's door handles, recalled twice for letting water reach the circuit board: once in 2023 for 2021-2022 ID.4 vehicles, and again in 2024 for 2021-2024 ID.4 vehicles, in both cases allowing doors to open unexpectedly under lateral force at low speed. A separate 2023-2024 ID.4 recall addressed a sunroof shade fabric that did not meet flammability standards. The Tiguan appears twice for unrelated body issues: a 2018 model may have received an incorrect roof reinforcement during a repair, and 2018-2022 Tiguans may have a rear hatch spoiler that is insufficiently attached.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Doors opening unexpectedly at speeds below 9 mph during hard cornering (2021-2024 ID.4)
  • A rear hatch spoiler that comes loose or detaches from the vehicle (2018-2022 Tiguan)
  • The notices describe no warning sign for the sunroof shade fabric or an incorrectly installed roof reinforcement

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the two ID.4 door-handle recalls — 2023, covering 2021-2022 ID.4 vehicles, and 2024, covering 2021-2024 ID.4 vehicles — the 2023-2024 ID.4 sunroof shade recall, the 2018 Tiguan roof reinforcement recall, and the 2018-2022 Tiguan rear spoiler recall all use the same language: they do not tell owners to stop driving or to park outside while waiting for the repair to be scheduled.

What the free repair involves

Dealers apply flame-retardant treatment to the ID.4 sunroof shade fabric, replace the incorrect roof reinforcement on the Tiguan, and replace the loose rear spoiler on affected Tiguans. For the door-handle recalls, dealers update the vehicle software and inspect or replace the door handles as needed. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why do Volkswagen ID.4 doors open by themselves?

The door handles can let water reach the circuit board assembly, which may cause a door to open unexpectedly when the vehicle experiences high lateral force at speeds below 9 mph. Two separate recalls, in 2023 and 2024, address this issue.

Is the Tiguan spoiler recall the same as the roof repair recall?

No. The spoiler recall covers 2018-2022 Tiguans with an insufficiently attached rear hatch spoiler due to incorrect installation instructions. The roof reinforcement recall separately covers 2018 Tiguans that received an incorrect part during a prior roof repair.

Do any Volkswagen structure recalls tell owners to stop driving?

No. None of these five recalls — the ID.4 door handle, sunroof shade, Tiguan spoiler, or Tiguan roof reinforcement recalls — tells owners to stop driving or to park outside while waiting for repair.
