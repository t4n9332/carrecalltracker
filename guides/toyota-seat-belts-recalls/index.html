Why Are Toyota Seat Belts Being Recalled Across Different Models?

6 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Seat Belts · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-17

These six recalls cover six unrelated seat-belt defects rather than one repeated cause. The 2020 Supra has a driver's seat belt guide loop mount that may be welded incorrectly. The C-HR and Corolla share a rear seat belt locking mechanism that may not engage during a multi-impact crash. The Sienna's rear seat belt webbing guide can tear the belt during pretensioner deployment. The Camry Hybrid's rear center seat belt webbing can be damaged. A 2021 recall covers a passenger seat belt retractor fault shared across several BMW, MINI, and the Supra. Most recently, the Highlander's third-row center seat belt anchor may be installed at an incorrect angle.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Seat belt webbing that appears frayed, torn, or damaged, particularly in rear seats (Camry Hybrid, Sienna)
  • A rear seat belt that does not lock normally during multiple impacts (C-HR, Corolla)
  • The Supra weld flaw, the BMW/MINI/Supra retractor fault, and the Highlander anchor-angle defect describe no warning sign an owner could notice before a crash

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six seat-belt recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving, not the Supra weld-mount recall, the C-HR and Corolla locking-mechanism recall, the Sienna webbing-guide recall, the Camry Hybrid webbing-damage recall, the shared BMW/MINI/Supra retractor recall, or the Highlander anchor-angle recall. Each notice says the defect could keep the belt from properly restraining an occupant in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the seat belt mount, retractor, or webbing guide and replace the seat belt assembly, retractor, or anchor bolt as needed; the Supra's vehicle is replaced if the weld mount is defective. Dealers reinstall the Highlander's third-row anchor with a new bolt. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

What happens if my Supra's seat belt mount weld is defective?

Toyota dealers inspect the driver's seat belt guide loop mount, and if it was improperly welded, the vehicle is replaced entirely, free of charge. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving in the meantime.

How do I know if my Sienna or Camry Hybrid seat belt webbing is damaged?

The notices don't describe a symptom owners would notice beforehand; the defect is found by dealer inspection. Dealers check the webbing guide and belt assembly and replace them if damaged, free of charge.

Is the Highlander third-row seat belt recall urgent?

The notice says an incorrectly angled anchor bolt can prevent the belt from properly restraining an occupant in a crash, but it does not tell owners to stop driving. Dealers reinstall the anchor with a new bolt, free of charge.
