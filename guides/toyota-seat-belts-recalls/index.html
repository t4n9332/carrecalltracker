Toyota seat belt recalls: which models and belts are affected

6 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Seat Belts · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 17, 2025

Rear seat belts dominate this group. Four of the six cover belts behind the front seats: rear assemblies with a dual-mode locking mechanism on the 2019-2020 C-HR and 2020 Corolla and Corolla Hybrid, second-row outboard assemblies with an incorrect webbing guide on the 2022 Sienna with seating for eight passengers, damaged second-row center webbing on the 2025 Camry Hybrid, and a third-row center roof anchor installed at the wrong angle on the 2024-2025 Highlander. The other two reach the Supra through BMW: an improperly welded driver's seat belt guide loop mount on the 2020 car, and passenger seat belt retractors on the 2021 car. Every notice frames the risk as injury in a crash.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2019-2020 C-HR and 2020 Corolla notice says the rear seat belt webbing sensor locking mechanism may not lock as intended.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Each describes the consequence in a crash instead: the 2020 Supra notice says a damaged guide loop mount may leave the seat belt not properly restraining the driver; the 2019-2020 C-HR and 2020 Corolla notice refers to a crash involving multiple impacts; the 2022 Sienna notice says the webbing may tear during pre-tensioner deployment; and the 2025 Camry Hybrid and 2024-2025 Highlander notices say a belt that does not restrain an occupant increases injury risk.

What the free repair involves

On the 2020 Supra, dealers inspect the seat belt guide loop mount and, if it is defective, the vehicle will be replaced. Elsewhere dealers inspect rear seat belt assembly production dates and replace assemblies as necessary, inspect and replace the Camry Hybrid webbing assembly, inspect and replace the 2021 Supra retractors, replace the Sienna second-row outboard assemblies, and reinstall the Highlander anchor with a new bolt. Free of charge.

Common questions

Which seats do these Toyota seat belt recalls involve?

Mostly rear seats. The C-HR and Corolla recall covers rear assemblies, the Sienna recall the second-row outboard belts, the Camry Hybrid recall the second-row center belt, and the Highlander recall the third-row center anchor. The Supra recalls cover the driver and passenger belts.

Can a dealer tell whether my belt is actually defective?

Several notices describe an inspection first: production dates on the C-HR and Corolla rear assemblies, the webbing on the 2025 Camry Hybrid, the retractors on the 2021 Supra, and the guide loop mount on the 2020 Supra, with replacement if necessary.

My Camry Hybrid recall also lists Lexus models — why?

The 2025 Camry Hybrid webbing recall covers Lexus NX250, NX350, NX350 Hybrid and 2024-2025 RX350, RX350 Hybrid and RX500 Hybrid vehicles as well, under Toyota recall numbers 25TA03 and 25LA03. The notice says the second-row center belt webbing may be damaged.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
