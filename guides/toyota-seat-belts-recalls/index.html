Toyota Seat Belt Recalls Involving Mounts, Retractors, and Rear Belts

6 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Seat Belts · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 17, 2025

Reduced occupant restraint during a crash is the recurring concern, but the affected belt components differ. The 2020 Supra notice concerns an improperly welded driver’s guide loop mount; the 2021 Supra notice concerns passenger belt retractor function. Rear-seat problems include a locking mechanism in certain 2019-2020 C-HR and 2020 Corolla and Corolla Hybrid vehicles, and potentially damaged center-belt webbing in the 2025 Camry Hybrid. Eight-passenger 2022 Sienna vehicles have an incorrect second-row outboard webbing guide that may tear the belt during pretensioner deployment. Certain 2024-2025 Highlander and Highlander Hybrid vehicles have a third-row center belt roof anchor positioned at an incorrect angle. Supra appears in two distinct notices.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the listed Toyota seat belt notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The September 2019 Supra mount notice and July 2021 Supra retractor notice do not tell owners to stop driving. Neither does the December 2019 C-HR and Corolla rear-belt notice. The November 2021 Sienna webbing-guide notice, February 2025 Camry Hybrid center-belt notice, and December 2025 Highlander anchor notice also do not tell owners to stop driving. These notices provide no stop-driving direction for their respective seat belt defects.

What the free repair involves

Repairs are free: dealers inspect the 2020 Supra mount and replace a defective vehicle; inspect and conditionally replace 2021 Supra retractors, C-HR/Corolla rear assemblies, and Camry Hybrid belt assemblies. They replace affected Sienna second-row outboard belt assemblies. For the Highlander recall, dealers reinstall the third-row center anchor using a new bolt.

Common questions

Does the Supra seat belt mount recall involve replacing the vehicle?

The September 2019 notice for certain 2020 Supra vehicles calls for inspecting the driver’s seat belt guide loop mount. If that mount is defective, the remedy is replacement of the vehicle, free of charge.

Which rear seat belt is affected in the Highlander recall?

The December 2025 notice covers the third-row center seat belt roof anchor in certain 2024-2025 Highlander and Highlander Hybrid vehicles. Its incorrect angle may prevent proper restraint. Dealers reinstall the anchor with a new bolt, free of charge.

How does the Sienna seat belt problem differ from the Camry Hybrid problem?

The 2022 eight-passenger Sienna notice concerns an incorrect second-row outboard guide that may tear webbing during pretensioner deployment. The 2025 Camry Hybrid notice concerns potentially damaged second-row center webbing. Sienna assemblies are replaced; Camry Hybrid assemblies are inspected and replaced if necessary.
