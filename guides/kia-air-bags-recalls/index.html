Why do Kia side curtain air bags keep getting recalled?

8 NHTSA recalls · Kia · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 21, 2026

Side curtain air bags are the thread running through this group: four of the eight recalls name them. SC257 covers 2023 Sportage, where they may have been twisted during installation; SC263 covers 2021-2023 K5, where they may have been installed incorrectly; SC159 covers 2018 Sorento, where a component may detach during deployment; and SC289 covers 2023 Soul and 2024 Seltos, where they may deploy unexpectedly. The second theme is child detection — SC207 on 2021 Telluride, K5 and Forte, and SC379 on 2025-2026 EV9, both involve the occupant detection system. SC262 covers second row side air bag wiring on 2023 Telluride, and SC226 covers air bag control units on 2017-2019 Sedona and Soul.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall SC289: the notice states that the side curtain air bags on 2023 Soul and 2024 Seltos may deploy unexpectedly. The other seven notices do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these eight notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park the vehicle outside. SC159, SC207, SC226, SC257, SC262, SC263, SC289 and SC379 each set out the defect and the injury risk in a crash, and then point owners to a dealer for the remedy. None of them names an interim step, a seat to avoid, or a temporary measure to use until the repair is done. What the notices do not say, this page will not say for them.

What the free repair involves

SC159 replaces both side curtain air bags, SC289 replaces both side curtain air bag modules as necessary, and SC207 replaces the OCS/ODS unit. SC257 and SC263 have dealers inspect and reinstall the side curtain air bags. SC262 replaces or secures the wire harnesses, SC379 replaces the ODS mat and bladder tube, and SC226 updates the ACU software or replaces the unit. All free of charge.

Common questions

I carry a child seat up front — which Kia recalls cover that?

SC207 covers certain 2021 Telluride, K5 and Forte, where the OCS/ODS unit may not detect a child restraint in the front passenger seat. SC379 covers certain 2025-2026 EV9, where the ODS may not deactivate the air bag for a child or child seat.

Will my air bag light come on if my Kia is affected?

The notices for these eight recalls do not mention an air bag warning light or any dashboard indicator. They describe what the defect does in a crash, not what an owner would see beforehand, so a VIN lookup is how you find out.

Why does the K5 appear in two different air bag recalls?

SC207 covers certain 2021 K5 for an OCS/ODS unit that may not detect a child restraint. SC263 covers certain 2021-2023 K5 for side curtain air bags that may have been installed incorrectly. They are separate defects with separate remedies.
