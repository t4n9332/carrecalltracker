Why are so many Kia models recalled for airbag deployment problems?

8 NHTSA recalls · Kia · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-21

Across these eight Kia recalls, air bags mostly fail in two directions: not deploying properly, or deploying when they shouldn't. Side curtain air bags were twisted (2023 Sportage), installed incorrectly (2021-2023 K5), or connected with a damaged wire harness (2023 Telluride), so they may not deploy in a crash. A circuit-damaging control unit cover affected 2018 Forte and 2018-2019 Sedona, Soul, and Soul EV air bags the same way. Separately, the Occupant Classification/Detection System failed to recognize a child restraint in 2021 Telluride, K5, and Forte models, and again in the 2025 EV9. The 2018 Sorento's curtain air bag could detach as a projectile, while the 2023 Soul and 2024 Seltos side curtain air bags could deploy unexpectedly.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices do not describe any warning sign an owner could notice before a crash.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these eight Kia air bag recalls include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2018 Sorento, 2021 Telluride/K5/Forte, 2018-2019 Forte/Sedona/Soul/Soul EV, 2023 Sportage, 2023 Telluride, 2021-2023 K5, 2023 Soul/2024 Seltos, and 2025 EV9 recalls, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The risk in each case is that an air bag may not work as intended in a future crash, not a defect that manifests while driving under normal conditions.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, Kia dealers inspect and, if needed, replace side curtain air bags, wire harnesses, or the air bag control unit; in one case they update or replace the Occupant Classification/Detection System unit, and in another they update or replace the air bag control unit's software. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Do I need to stop driving my Kia because of an air bag recall?

No. None of these Kia air bag recalls tell owners to stop driving. The notices describe air bags that may not deploy correctly, or may deploy unexpectedly, in a future crash, not a defect that shows up while driving under normal conditions.

Will I know if my Kia's air bags are affected before a crash?

The recall notices reviewed here do not describe any warning sign, such as a dashboard light or noise, that would tell an owner the air bags are affected. The defects are only found through dealer inspection or repair under the recall.

Is it free to get my Kia's air bags fixed under these recalls?

Yes. Every recall listed here has Kia dealers perform the inspection, repair, or replacement free of charge, whether that means replacing a side curtain air bag, a wire harness, or updating control unit software.
