Hyundai Suspension Recalls Cover Loose Fasteners and Fracturing Springs

5 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Suspension · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 20, 2026

Loose or improperly tightened suspension fasteners recur in four of these five Hyundai notices. Certain 2020 Elantras have lower control arm ball joint fasteners that may allow joint detachment. Certain 2025 Ioniq 5 vehicles appear in two notices: one concerns rear alignment adjustment bolts, while another covers rear suspension fasteners and also names certain 2026 Ioniq 9 vehicles. The October 2026 notice concerns front strut assembly fasteners in certain 2026 Tucson Hybrid and Elantra vehicles. Separately, certain 2018 Santa Fe Sport vehicles have front coil springs that may fracture under certain loads and puncture a tire. Control or stability loss is the recurring stated risk.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five suspension notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. That includes the September 20, 2017 Santa Fe Sport coil spring notice and the October 9, 2019 Elantra ball joint fastener notice. The September 12, 2025 Ioniq 5 adjustment bolt notice, May 19, 2026 Ioniq 5 and Ioniq 9 rear fastener notice, and October 20, 2026 Tucson Hybrid and Elantra front strut fastener notice likewise do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Santa Fe Sport dealers replace both front springs before sale; Elantra ball joint fasteners are inspected and tightened as necessary. The 2025 Ioniq 5 adjustment-bolt remedy includes bolt replacement, alignment and tires if necessary. The other rear-fastener and front-strut recalls specify inspection, fastener replacement and alignment as necessary. Each notice states its remedy is free of charge.

Common questions

Why does the 2025 Ioniq 5 appear in two suspension notices?

The September 2025 notice identifies improperly tightened rear suspension alignment adjustment bolts. The May 2026 notice identifies rear suspension fasteners that may loosen and detach, covering certain 2025 Ioniq 5 and 2026 Ioniq 9 vehicles. Each specifies its own remedy.

Does the Ioniq 5 adjustment bolt recall include tire replacement?

The September 12, 2025 notice says dealers will replace the adjustment bolts, align the wheels and replace tires if necessary, free of charge. That tire provision belongs to this recall for certain 2025 Ioniq 5 electric vehicles.

What does the Santa Fe Sport spring notice say about affected vehicles?

The September 20, 2017 notice says the affected 2018 Santa Fe Sport vehicles were unsold on dealer lots. It specifies replacement of both front coil springs, free of charge, before sale because a fractured spring could puncture a tire.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
