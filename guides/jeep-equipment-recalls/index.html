What Equipment Problems Have Led to Jeep Vehicle Recalls?

6 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Equipment · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-02-13

Six equipment recalls across different Jeep models share no single cause, but several involve missing or incorrect information rather than a broken part. Two 2018 Grand Cherokee SRT and Trackhawk recalls cover floor mats — one factory-installed, one an accessory Mopar mat — that can trap the accelerator pedal. A 2018 Wrangler recall involves a roof top tent's cover cracking and detaching. A 2017-2018 Cherokee recall and a 2022-2023 Grand Cherokee recall involve missing safety information: an incorrect tire and rim label, and a missing air bag warning label. A 2021-2022 Grand Cherokee recall covers a rearview camera display that can go blank from an overheating transistor.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The accelerator pedal not returning to the idle position after being pressed (floor mat recalls)
  • A roof top tent cover cracking, breaking, or detaching while driving (Wrangler)
  • The rearview camera image not displaying (2021-2022 Grand Cherokee L and Grand Cherokee)
  • The tire/rim label and air bag warning label issues are paperwork defects the notices describe with no physical symptom

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six equipment recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe risks from a trapped accelerator pedal, a detached roof top tent cover, an incorrect tire and rim combination, a missing air bag warning label, and a blank rearview camera image, but in each case the notice only states the defect and remedy without telling owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the affected floor mats or the rearview camera display module, free of charge, and provide corrected certification or air bag warning labels. The Wrangler roof top tent cover recall's remedy was still under development at the time of the notice. The rearview camera display has already been replaced on all affected vehicles.

Common questions

Are both Jeep Grand Cherokee floor mat recalls the same defect?

They cover the same clearance problem — insufficient space between the mat and accelerator pedal on 2018 Grand Cherokee SRT and Trackhawk models — but one recall is the factory floor mat and the other is a Mopar accessory mat sold separately.

What's wrong with the recalled Jeep Wrangler roof top tent?

On 2018 Wranglers sold with an installed roof top tent, the top cover may crack or break and detach from the vehicle while driving, creating a road hazard for the vehicle and others nearby.

Why was the Jeep Grand Cherokee rearview camera recalled?

On 2021-2022 Grand Cherokee L and 2022 Grand Cherokee vehicles, a transistor in the center stack display could overheat and prevent the rearview camera image from showing, reducing rear visibility for the driver.
