What do the Jeep equipment recalls cover, from floor mats to labels?

6 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Equipment · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 13, 2025

Six Jeep recalls land in the equipment category, and they are mostly about parts and labels rather than the vehicle's mechanical systems. The 2018 Grand Cherokee SRT and Trackhawk appears twice on the same day, once for the driver's floor mat fitted to the vehicle and once for two Mopar accessory floor mats sold separately, both for insufficient clearance that can stop the accelerator pedal returning to idle. Two recalls involve missing information: the certification label on 2017-2018 Cherokee omits tire and rim size, and the air bag warning label may be missing from the dashboard of 2022-2023 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L. The rest are a 2018 Wrangler rooftop tent cover that may crack or detach and a 2022 Grand Cherokee rearview camera display fault.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The accelerator pedal may not return to the idle position — the two 2018 Grand Cherokee SRT and Trackhawk floor mat recalls.
  • The rearview camera image may not display — the 2022 Grand Cherokee and 2021-2022 Grand Cherokee L center stack display recall.
  • The air bag warning label may be missing from the dashboard — the 2022-2023 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L recall.
  • Tire and rim size information may be missing from the certification label — the 2017-2018 Cherokee recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The two floor mat notices say a mat that keeps the accelerator pedal from returning to idle increases the risk of a crash; they say the mats will be replaced. The rooftop tent notice says the cover may detach while the vehicle is moving and create a road hazard, and that the remedy was still under development. The label and rearview camera notices give no driving instruction either.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the floor mats on both recalls, free of charge. For the 2017-2018 Cherokee, Chrysler provides a corrected certification label free of charge. FCA mails the air bag warning label information to owners free of charge, and dealers install labels on unsold vehicles. Dealers replaced the center stack upper display for the rearview camera recall free of charge. The rooftop tent cover remedy was still under development.

Common questions

Which floor mats are recalled on the 2018 Grand Cherokee?

Two notices, both for SRT and Trackhawk models: the driver's floor mat installed in the vehicle, and Mopar accessory mats with part numbers 6ML07TRMAA and 6GA92TRMAA. Each may have insufficient clearance to the accelerator pedal. Dealers replace the mats free of charge.

Has the Grand Cherokee rearview camera recall been fixed?

The notice for 2022 Grand Cherokee and 2021-2022 Grand Cherokee L says dealers replaced the center stack upper display free of charge and that all affected vehicles have been repaired. Owner notification letters were mailed February 27, 2025.

What happens with the Wrangler rooftop tent recall?

That recall was issued by American Fastbacks for 2018 Wrangler vehicles sold with a roof top tent. The notice says the top cover may crack or break and detach while moving, and that the remedy was still under development; these vehicles are also included in recall 19V-190.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
