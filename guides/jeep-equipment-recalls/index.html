Jeep Equipment Recalls Involving Floor Mats, Cameras and Missing Labels

6 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Equipment · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 13, 2025

Grand Cherokee equipment dominates this group, including two floor mat notices for 2018 SRT and Trackhawk vehicles. UB3 concerns affected vehicle floor mats, while UB5 identifies Mopar accessory mats with part numbers 6ML07TRMAA and 6GA92TRMAA; both describe insufficient accelerator pedal clearance. Other Grand Cherokee notices concern missing dashboard air bag warning labels on certain 2022-2023 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles, and rearview camera image failure on certain 2022 Grand Cherokee and 2021-2022 Grand Cherokee L vehicles. Separate equipment issues involve omitted tire and rim information on 2017-2018 Cherokee certification labels and a potentially detaching rooftop tent cover on certain 2018 Wranglers sold with that equipment.

Warning signs to watch for

  • UB3: the floor mat may prevent the accelerator pedal from returning to idle.
  • UB5: the accessory floor mat may inhibit the accelerator pedal from returning to idle.
  • April 23, 2019 Wrangler rooftop tent recall: the cover may crack or break and detach while moving.
  • 11C: the rearview camera image may not display.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The supplied equipment notices do not tell owners to stop driving. That applies to the October 29, 2018 Grand Cherokee floor mat recall, UB3, and the accessory floor mat recall, UB5; the April 23, 2019 Wrangler rooftop tent cover recall; the August 31, 2017 Cherokee certification label recall, T53; the May 11, 2023 Grand Cherokee air bag warning label recall; and the February 13, 2025 Grand Cherokee rearview camera recall, 11C. None of these notices gives a park-outside instruction.

What the free repair involves

UB3 and UB5 replace affected floor mats free of charge. T53 provides a corrected certification label free; the air bag label notice mails warning information free and provides free inspection and label installation as necessary for unsold vehicles. Under 11C, dealers replaced the DCSD free. The rooftop tent notice says its remedy remains under development.

Common questions

Which Grand Cherokee floor mats are identified in these recalls?

UB3 covers affected floor mats in certain 2018 Grand Cherokee SRT and Trackhawk vehicles. UB5 specifically identifies Mopar accessory floor mats numbered 6ML07TRMAA and 6GA92TRMAA for those vehicles. Both notices describe insufficient clearance around the accelerator pedal.

What repair does the Wrangler rooftop tent recall provide?

The supplied April 2019 notice says the remedy is still under development. It concerns certain 2018 Wranglers sold by American Fastbacks with an installed rooftop tent whose top cover may crack, break, and detach while the vehicle is moving.

What does the Grand Cherokee rearview camera recall say about repairs?

Recall 11C says dealers replaced the center stack upper display, or DCSD, free of charge and that all affected vehicles have been repaired. It covers certain 2022 Grand Cherokee and 2021-2022 Grand Cherokee L vehicles.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
