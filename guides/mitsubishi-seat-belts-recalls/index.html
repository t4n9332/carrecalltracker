Which Mitsubishi Outlander seat belt recalls affect your vehicle?

4 NHTSA recalls · Mitsubishi · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 13, 2022

Every recall in this Mitsubishi group involves the Outlander or Outlander PHEV, and all four concern seat belts in the front passenger or second-row seats. Two of them, from 2021 and 2022, cover the 2022 Outlander and describe the automatic locking retractor deactivating early so that a child restraint may not be secured; both cite Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 208. A 2020 recall covers 2019-2020 Outlander and 2019 Outlander PHEV vehicles whose right side second-row seat belt may have been assembled incorrectly. A second 2020 recall covers 2014-2019 Outlander and 2018-2019 Outlander PHEV vehicles, where the second-row center buckle can interfere with the right-side buckle when the seat backs are folded down.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2020 second-row center buckle recall states that the interference can cause the right-side seat belt buckle cover to come off, making the buckle latch inoperable.
  • The other three notices in this group do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four Mitsubishi seat belt notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. No notice in this group gives an interim instruction, such as leaving a seat or a belt unused while waiting for parts. The notices state the risk in crash terms only: a belt assembled incorrectly, a buckle that cannot latch, or a retractor that does not hold a child restraint may fail to restrain an occupant, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and, where needed, replace seat belt assemblies: the right side second-row belt in the 2020 assembly recall, and the right front plus both second-row belts in the 2021 and 2022 retractor recalls. For the second-row center buckle recall, dealers modify the buckle cover, install a new warning label on the seat support base and provide an owner's manual label insert. All of this work is free of charge.

Common questions

Does my 2022 Outlander have two separate seat belt recalls?

Two recalls here cover certain 2022 Outlander vehicles, one announced in 2021 and one in 2022, both describing an automatic locking retractor that deactivates early. Whether both apply to your vehicle depends on the VIN; Mitsubishi customer service is 1-888-648-7820.

What do the notices say about using a child seat in the second row?

The 2021 and 2022 notices say the automatic locking retractor may deactivate early, which can result in an unsecured child restraint system. They do not tell owners to stop driving or to stop using the seat; they say dealers will inspect and replace belts free of charge.

Is the Outlander PHEV included in these recalls?

In two of them. The 2020 assembly recall lists the 2019 Outlander PHEV, and the second-row center buckle recall lists 2018-2019 Outlander PHEV vehicles. The two automatic locking retractor recalls list the 2022 Outlander only, with no PHEV named.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
