Why Do Mitsubishi Outlander Recalls Keep Involving Seat Belts?

4 NHTSA recalls · Mitsubishi · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-05-13

Four separate seat belt defects recur across Outlander model years from 2018 through 2022, split between hardware assembly errors and retractors that deactivate too early. Two 2022 Outlander recalls involve the Automatic Locking Retractor releasing before the belt fully retracts in the front and second-row seats, which can leave a child restraint system unsecured under FMVSS 208. Two earlier recalls involve different hardware problems: a 2019-2020 Outlander and 2019 Outlander PHEV recall for an incorrectly assembled right-side second-row belt, and a 2018-2019 Outlander and Outlander PHEV recall where a folded-down second-row center buckle can dislodge the right-side buckle cover, making that buckle inoperable.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Second-row center seat belt buckle cover coming loose or detaching when the rear seats are folded down (2020-07-13 recall)
  • That buckle failing to latch once the cover has detached (2020-07-13 recall)
  • The notices describe no owner-noticeable warning sign for the incorrectly assembled belt (2020-05-18) or the early-deactivating retractors (2021-08-02, 2022-05-13) — these were found through inspection, not a symptom.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; for all four — the 2020-05-18, 2021-08-02, 2020-07-13, and 2022-05-13 recalls — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. They describe seat belts that may not properly restrain an occupant, or automatic locking retractors that release too early and can leave a child restraint system unsecured, but none of the notices instruct owners to stop using the vehicle or to park it away from structures while awaiting repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and replace the affected seat belt assembly, buckle, or buckle cover as necessary, and in one case add a warning label to the seat base and owner's manual. All repairs across these four recalls are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Outlander years have the incorrectly assembled seat belt recall?

2019-2020 Outlander and 2019 Outlander PHEV models. The right side second-row seat belt may have been assembled incorrectly and might not properly restrain an occupant in a crash. Dealers inspect and replace it free of charge.

Why can't I get a child seat to stay secure in my 2022 Outlander?

Two 2022 Outlander recalls involve automatic locking retractors in the front and second-row belts deactivating before the belt fully retracts, which can leave a child restraint system unsecured. Dealers inspect and replace the belts free.

What's wrong with the second-row center seat belt buckle on older Outlanders?

On 2018-2019 Outlander and Outlander PHEV models, folding down the rear seats can dislodge the right-side buckle cover, making that buckle unable to latch. Dealers modify the buckle cover and add warning labels free of charge.
