Why Do Mercedes-Benz Doors And Locks Keep Failing?

7 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Latches/locks/linkages · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-24

These seven recalls span two vehicle families. Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans have had three separate door-related recalls: 2018 Sprinter 2500/3500 right-side doors that could not be opened after being unlocked, a 2018-2019 hood catch that could let the hood open while driving, and a 2019-2020 sliding door with the wrong auxiliary lock for its electric mechanism. The G-Class has had rear door latch problems recalled twice, first in 2021 for G550 and G63 AMG models and again in 2024 for vehicles that received the earlier replacement latch. Separately, a 2026 GLA 250 recall covers a door cable, and a broader 2026 recall covers a corroding door lock switch across several compact models that can cause a rollaway.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Right-side doors on 2018 Sprinter vans that won't open from inside or outside after being unlocked electronically
  • The hood opening on its own while driving on 2018-2019 Sprinters
  • A rear door on G-Class models that looks closed but doesn't fully latch, or won't open from inside
  • On the 2026 door lock rollaway recall, the parking brake or Auto-Park feature not engaging automatically when a door is open
  • The notices describe no warning sign for the Sprinter sliding door or GLA 250 cable issues

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven recall notices tell owners to stop driving or park outside. For the Sprinter door, hood, and sliding door recalls, and the G-Class and GLA 250 door recalls, the notices only describe the defect and repair. The 2026 door lock recall that can cause a rollaway also does not instruct owners to stop driving; it says only that a vehicle could roll away if the parking brake fails to engage automatically.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the affected part free of charge in every recall here: door latches on the Sprinter and G-Class, the hood catch screw connection on Sprinter vans, the bowden cable on the GLA 250, and the driver's door lock on the 2026 rollaway recall. The Sprinter sliding door recall instead converts the door from electric to manual operation.

Common questions

Why did the G-Class rear door latch get recalled twice?

The first 2021 recall replaced the rear door latch on 2019-2021 G550 and G63 AMG models. A 2024 recall covers vehicles that received that replacement latch during service or the earlier recall, because the new latch components could also prevent the door from latching or opening properly.

Does my Sprinter need three separate repairs?

Only if it's affected by all three recalls, which cover different years and issues: 2018 right-side door latches, 2018-2019 hood catches, and 2019-2020 sliding doors with electric mechanisms. Check your VIN on NHTSA.gov, since not every Sprinter is covered by every recall.

What is the 2026 door lock rollaway recall about?

It covers a group of compact Mercedes-Benz models, including CLA, A-Class, GLA, and GLB, where a door lock micro-switch can corrode. That can stop the parking brake or Auto-Park feature from engaging automatically, which the notice says may let the vehicle roll away.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
