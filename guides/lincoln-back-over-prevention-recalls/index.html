Why does my Lincoln rearview camera image go blank?

19 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 19, 2026

Nearly every Lincoln recall in this area is the rearview camera, and the Aviator and Corsair carry most of them. The Aviator appears in the 2020-2021 image processing module recall (21C09), the 2020-2023 360-degree camera recalls (23V-022 and 23S23), a recall covering 2020-2023 Aviator with 2018-2021 Navigator, the 2020-2024 cell phone interference recall, and the 2025 recall shared with Explorer (24C34). The Corsair runs from 2020 through 2022 across several notices, including water entering the camera connectors (25S83). Older Lincolns appear too: 2017-2020 Continental for a degraded lens coating, and 2019 MKT and 2020 Nautilus for a blank or distorted image. The stated causes differ — video output failure, module software, a poor electrical connection, water, lens coating and interference.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A blank or distorted rearview camera image — stated in the 2019 MKT recall, the 2020 Nautilus and Corsair recall, the 2020-2022 Corsair water intrusion recall (25S83) and the 2020-2022 Corsair recall 25S05.
  • No rearview image when the vehicle is placed in reverse — stated in the recall covering 2018-2021 Navigator and 2020-2023 Aviator.
  • A foggy or cloudy camera image — stated in the 2017-2020 Continental 360-degree camera recall (22S51).
  • A flickering or distorted image — stated in the 2020-2024 Aviator accessory protocol image module recall.
  • Loss of the rearview camera image when vehicle speed reaches 10 MPH or greater — stated in the 2025 Aviator recall (24C34).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the notices listed here tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside; each describes reduced rearward visibility and an increased risk of a crash. One carries an interim instruction of its own: the 2020-2024 Aviator recall for cell phone interference with the accessory protocol image module advises owners not to use their cell phones while reversing until the remedy is completed. The 2019 MKT recall says a second notice will be sent once the final remedy is available. The others give no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Repairs split between software and hardware. Dealers update image processing module software on 21S44, 21C09 and 23V-022, and update accessory protocol interface module software on 24C34, which can also arrive over the air. Elsewhere dealers replace the rearview or 360-degree camera, replace the camera harness and camera on 25S83, and install shielding on the accessory protocol image module for the 2020-2024 Aviator. All free of charge.

Common questions

Why has my Lincoln had more than one camera recall?

Several recalls overlap on the same models with different stated causes: 360-degree camera video output failure, image processing module software, water entering the camera connectors on 2020-2022 Corsair, and cell phone interference on 2020-2024 Aviator. Each notice lists its own repair.

When will the 2019 Lincoln MKT camera fix be available?

That notice says interim letters about the safety risk were mailed January 22, 2025, and a second notice will be sent once the final remedy is available, anticipated March 2027. Dealers will replace the rearview camera as necessary, free of charge.

Does the recall replace my camera or just update software?

It depends on the recall. 21S44, 21C09 and 23V-022 are image processing module software updates. 23S23 replaces the 360-degree camera, 25S83 replaces the camera harness and camera, and 24C34 updates software over the air or at a dealer. All free of charge.
