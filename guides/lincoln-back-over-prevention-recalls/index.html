Why Does My Lincoln's Rearview Camera Keep Failing?

19 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-19

Across these Lincoln and paired Ford recalls, the rearview or 360-degree camera repeatedly fails to show a clear image on the Aviator, Corsair, Navigator, Continental, MKT, and Nautilus. The causes vary by recall: video output or image processing module failures, water entering the camera connectors, poor electrical connections, a degraded anti-reflective lens coating that fogs the picture, electromagnetic interference from a cell phone, and a software issue that drops the image once the vehicle reaches about 10 mph. The Aviator and Corsair appear most often, each named in several separate recalls between 2020 and 2025.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rearview or 360-degree camera screen shows a blank or black image
  • Camera picture looks distorted, fuzzy, or foggy
  • Backup camera image flickers when a cell phone is in the vehicle (Aviator)
  • Camera image disappears once the vehicle reaches about 10 mph in reverse (2025 Explorer/Aviator)
  • The notices describe no dashboard warning light for this problem

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The only interim step described is in the 2024 Lincoln Aviator recall for camera flicker caused by cell-phone interference: owners are advised not to use a cell phone while reversing until the accessory protocol image module shielding is installed. For every other recall in this group - including the camera failures on the Corsair, Navigator, Continental, MKT, and Explorer - the notices only describe the display problem and do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Depending on the recall, dealers replace the rearview or 360-degree camera, replace the camera wiring harness, or update the image processing module (IPMB) or accessory protocol interface module (APIM) software. One 2025 model-year fix can also be applied through an over-the-air update. All of these repairs are done at no cost to the owner.

Common questions

Which Lincoln models are covered by these rearview camera recalls?

The recalls in this group cover the Lincoln Aviator, Corsair, Navigator, Continental, MKT, and Nautilus, alongside related Ford models like the Explorer, Bronco, Escape, and F-Series trucks. The exact years and cause differ by recall, so check your VIN on NHTSA.gov.

Do I need to stop driving if my camera stops working?

No recall in this group tells owners to stop driving or park outside. Only the 2024 Lincoln Aviator flicker recall, fixed with APIM shielding, adds an interim step: avoid using a cell phone while backing up until the repair is done.

How do dealers actually fix a failed Lincoln backup camera?

Repairs vary by recall: dealers replace the camera itself, replace the wiring harness, or update the image-processing or accessory-protocol module software. One 2025 model-year recall allows the fix to be sent over-the-air instead of a dealer visit. All repairs are free.
