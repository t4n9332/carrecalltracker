Why do Kia Sorento, Stinger, Forte and Sportage have steering recalls?

5 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Steering · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-06-07

Kia's steering recalls in this group involve five different components across five model lines, with no single cause tying them together. The 2018 Sorento's cowl crossbar may be improperly welded, letting the steering column separate from the motor-driven power steering column. The 2020 Stinger's power steering assembly may have a misbuilt internal component that causes the steering to lock or become difficult to turn. A loose steering column bolt affects the 2021-2022 Forte and Rio, while a separate 2023 Forte recall covers a front left steering knuckle that may crack. The newest recall, for the 2024 Sportage, covers a power steering circuit board that may short circuit and cut power steering assist.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering that becomes difficult to turn or locks up while driving (2020 Stinger)
  • Steering that may require noticeably more effort if power steering assist is lost (2024 Sportage)
  • No stated warning signs for the Sorento steering column, Forte/Rio steering column bolt, or 2023 Forte steering knuckle recalls

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the five recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving for the Sorento steering column recall, the Stinger power steering lock-up recall, the Forte and Rio steering column bolt recall, the Forte steering knuckle recall, or the Sportage power steering short-circuit recall — even though each describes a possible loss of steering control that the notices say can increase the risk of a crash.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the cowl crossbar on the Sorento, replace the power steering assembly on the Stinger, tighten the steering column retention bolt on the Forte and Rio, replace the steering knuckle on the 2023 Forte as needed, and replace the power steering electric power pack on the Sportage. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Do any Kia steering recalls tell owners to stop driving?

No. None of these five recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, even though each describes a possible loss of steering control that can increase the risk of a crash.

What's different about the 2023 Forte steering recall versus the 2021-2022 Forte recall?

The 2021-2022 recall covers a loose steering column retention bolt shared with the Rio. The 2023 recall is a separate defect: a front left steering knuckle that may crack.

Is the Kia Stinger steering recall related to its brake fire recall?

No. The steering recall covers a misbuilt component in the power steering rack assembly on 2020 Stinger models, a different system from the earlier HECU brake fire recall.
