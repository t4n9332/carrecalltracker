Which Kia models have steering recalls, and what goes wrong?

5 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Steering · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 7, 2024

These five Kia steering recalls do not share one part; they share an outcome. Two involve the motor-driven power steering unit itself: the 2020 Stinger with the 2.0L Turbo-GDI engine, where a component in the rack-mounted MDPS assembly may have been manufactured incorrectly, and the 2024 Sportage, where the MDPS circuit board may short circuit. Two involve how the column is held: the 2018 Sorento, where the cowl crossbar that connects to the MDPS steering column may be improperly welded, and 2021-2022 Rio and Forte, where a steering column bolt may not be secured properly. The fifth covers the 2023 Forte, whose front left steering knuckle may crack. Forte appears twice.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2020 Stinger recall (SC193) says the steering may lock or become difficult while driving.
  • The 2024 Sportage recall (SC319) says a short circuit in the power steering circuit board can result in a loss of power steering assist.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That covers the 2018 Sorento cowl crossbar recall, the 2020 Stinger MDPS recall, the 2021-2022 Rio and Forte steering column bolt recall, the 2023 Forte steering knuckle recall and the 2024 Sportage power steering recall. Each notice states the risk instead: a loss of steering ability, a loss of steering control, or a loss of power steering assist, each increasing the risk of a crash. No notice gives owners an interim step.

What the free repair involves

All five repairs are free of charge. On the 2018 Sorento, dealers replace the cowl crossbar. On the 2020 Stinger, they replace the MDPS assembly. On 2021-2022 Rio and Forte vehicles, they tighten the lower steering column u-joint retention bolt. On the 2023 Forte, they inspect and replace the front left steering knuckle as necessary. On the 2024 Sportage, they replace the motor-driven power steering electric power pack.

Common questions

Is my Forte covered by both steering recalls?

They cover different model years. The steering column bolt recall covers certain 2021-2022 Rio and Forte vehicles, while the steering knuckle recall covers certain 2023 Forte vehicles. Check your VIN through NHTSA's lookup or with Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542.

Does the 2020 Stinger recall cover every Stinger?

The notice covers only certain 2020 Stinger vehicles equipped with a 2.0L Turbo-GDI engine. It says a component within the rack-mounted motor-driven power steering assembly may have been manufactured incorrectly, possibly resulting in the steering locking or becoming difficult while driving.

What do these notices say I might notice while driving?

Only two describe anything. The 2020 Stinger notice says the steering may lock or become difficult while driving, and the 2024 Sportage notice says the circuit board short can result in a loss of power steering assist. The other three describe crash risk only.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
