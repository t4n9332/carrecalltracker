Why Do So Many Honda Models Have Seat Belt Recalls?

7 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-02-05

These seven Honda seat belt recalls span different failure points rather than one repeated defect. Buckles or latches that fail to secure the belt appear twice: the 2018 Odyssey's third-row tongue, and a 2023 recall covering 2018-2020 Accord, Accord Hybrid, CR-V, Odyssey, Insight and Acura RDX buckle release buttons. Locking mechanisms that may not lock appear in the 2019 Clarity's rear belts and the 2021 Accord, CR-V, Ridgeline, Insight and CR-V Hybrid center belt retractor. A 2023-2024 HR-V recall involves a missing pretensioner rivet, a 2025 Pilot recall involves misrouted third-row webbing, and a 2019 Insight, Odyssey and Ridgeline recall involves an SRS control unit that may keep air bags or pretensioners from deploying.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A seat belt or buckle that will not latch or stay latched (Odyssey third row; Accord, Accord Hybrid, CR-V, Odyssey, Insight, Acura RDX buckles)
  • A rear or center seat belt that does not lock into place (Clarity Electric/Plug-in Hybrid; Accord, CR-V, Ridgeline, Insight, CR-V Hybrid center belt)
  • A third-row seat belt that is trapped behind a panel and does not function (Pilot)
  • The notices do not describe a way for owners to notice the SRS control unit issue (Insight, Odyssey, Ridgeline) or the missing pretensioner rivet (HR-V) before a crash

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven Honda seat belt recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe increased risk of injury in a crash if a seat belt fails to latch or lock, if the third-row belt cannot function, or if air bags and pretensioners do not deploy as intended, but none tell owners to stop driving in the meantime. This applies to the Odyssey, Insight, Ridgeline, Clarity, Accord, CR-V, Acura RDX, HR-V, and Pilot recalls alike.

What the free repair involves

Honda dealers address each recall with inspection and replacement: the Odyssey's third-row seat belt tongue, the SRS control unit on the Insight/Odyssey/Ridgeline, the Clarity's rear seat belt assemblies, the second-row center belt on Accord/CR-V/Ridgeline, buckle release buttons or assemblies on Accord/CR-V/Odyssey/Insight/Acura RDX, the HR-V's pretensioner assembly, and the Pilot's seat belt routing. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Do all these recalls involve the same seat belt defect?

No. The recalls cover different issues — buckles that won't latch, belts that won't lock, a missing pretensioner rivet, misrouted webbing, and an SRS control unit that may not deploy air bags or pretensioners — across different Honda and Acura models and years.

Is my Honda's seat belt recall about the third row specifically?

Only two are: the 2018 Odyssey's third-row tongue recall and the 2025 Pilot's third-row routing recall. The other five recalls involve front or second-row seat belt components on different models.

Will these repairs cost anything?

No. Honda dealers perform the seat belt inspection, repair, or part replacement free of charge for every recall in this group, whether it involves a buckle, retractor, pretensioner, or the SRS control unit.
