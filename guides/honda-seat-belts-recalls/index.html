Which Honda seat belt recalls affect your front, second or third row?

7 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-02-05

Seven Honda seat belt recalls cluster around the rows behind the driver. Center and third-row belts repeat most: the 2018 Odyssey third-row center tongue may not latch securely, the 2025 Pilot third-row belt can be trapped behind the passenger side panel, and the second-row center automatic locking retractor in 2021 Accord, Accord Hybrid, CR-V and Ridgeline plus 2022 Insight and CR-V Hybrid may deactivate and leave a child seat unsecured. Front belts appear twice: buckle release button interference on 2017-2020 CR-V, 2018-2019 Accord, 2018-2020 Odyssey, 2019 Insight and 2019-2020 Acura RDX, and a missing pretensioner rivet on 2023-2024 Accord and HR-V. Add a faulty SRS control unit on 2019 Insight, Odyssey and Ridgeline, and 2019 Clarity rear belts that may not lock.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A belt tongue that won't click into the buckle, or a buckle that releases too easily
  • Third-row belt you can't reach or pull out because it's routed behind a trim panel
  • Child seat that stays loose because the center belt won't switch into its locking mode
  • Rear belt webbing that doesn't lock when you tug it sharply
  • No visible sign at all for the missing pretensioner rivet and the SRS control unit fault — they only matter in a crash

Is it safe to keep driving?

No Honda seat belt recall here comes with a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, so the vehicle itself is fine to use. The limits are about specific seating positions. Don't install a child seat in the second-row center of the affected 2021-2022 models until the belt is replaced, and don't seat anyone in the 2018 Odyssey third-row center or the 2025 Pilot third-row position until the belt is fixed. The front-belt, Clarity and SRS recalls affect crash protection, so keep wearing your belt and book service quickly.

What the free repair involves

Every repair is free of charge. Dealers replace the second-row center seat belt assembly, the SRS control unit, and the front buckle release buttons or full buckle assemblies as necessary. Others begin with an inspection: the Odyssey third-row tongue, Clarity rear belt production dates, and Accord and HR-V pretensioner rivets. The Pilot's trapped belt is simply rerouted correctly. No software or over-the-air fixes apply here.

Common questions

Can I use a child seat in the middle of the second row?

Not in the recalled 2021 Accord, Accord Hybrid, CR-V and Ridgeline or 2022 Insight and CR-V Hybrid. The automatic locking retractor may deactivate improperly, leaving the restraint unsecured. Move the child seat to another position until the belt is replaced.

My belts seem to work fine — do I still need the recall done?

Yes. Several of these defects are invisible day to day. The missing pretensioner rivet on 2023-2024 Accord and HR-V models and the 2019 SRS control unit fault only show up in a crash, when belts or airbags may fail to work.

Which recall covers a front belt that won't stay latched?

The March 2023 recall for 2017-2020 CR-V, 2018-2019 Accord and Accord Hybrid, 2018-2020 Odyssey, 2019 Insight and 2019-2020 Acura RDX. The buckle channel can interfere with the release button; dealers replace the release buttons or buckle assemblies.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
