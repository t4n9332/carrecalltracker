Why are Honda seat belts recalled for failing to latch or lock?

7 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-02-05

Latching and locking failures run through this group. Buckles that will not latch cover 2017-2020 CR-V, 2018-2019 Accord and Accord Hybrid, 2018-2020 Odyssey, 2019 Insight and 2019-2020 Acura RDX, and separately the 2018 Odyssey third-row center belt with an incompatible tongue. Locking problems hit the 2019 Clarity Fuel Cell, Electric and Plug-in Hybrid rear belts and the second-row center belt in 2021 Accord, Accord Hybrid, CR-V, Ridgeline, 2022 Insight and CR-V Hybrid. Two more involve parts: pretensioners missing a rivet on 2023-2024 Accord and HR-V, and an SRS control unit on 2019 Insight, Odyssey and Ridgeline. Odyssey, Accord and CR-V appear most often.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A front seat belt buckle that will not latch — stated in the 2017-2020 CR-V, 2018-2019 Accord, 2018-2020 Odyssey, 2019 Insight and 2019-2020 Acura RDX recall.
  • A third-row center seat belt that does not latch securely — stated in the 2018 Odyssey seat belt tongue recall.
  • A third-row passenger seat belt trapped behind the passenger side panel — stated in the 2025 Pilot recall.
  • A rear seat belt that does not lock as intended — stated in the 2019 Clarity Fuel Cell, Clarity Electric and Clarity Plug-in Hybrid recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. That covers the 2018 Odyssey third-row belt tongue recall, the 2019 Insight, Odyssey and Ridgeline SRS control unit recall, the 2019 Clarity rear belt locking recall, the 2021 Accord, CR-V, Ridgeline and 2022 Insight and CR-V Hybrid second-row center belt recall, the front buckle recall covering 2017-2020 CR-V and related models, the 2023-2024 Accord and HR-V pretensioner recall and the 2025 Pilot third-row routing recall. Each describes an increased risk of injury in a crash.

What the free repair involves

All of this work is free. Dealers inspect the 2018 Odyssey third-row center belt for the correct tongue and replace the belt if needed; replace the 2019 SRS control unit; inspect Clarity rear belt assembly production dates and replace as necessary; replace the second-row center belt assembly; replace front buckle release buttons or buckle assemblies; inspect and replace HR-V and Accord pretensioners; and repair the 2025 Pilot belt routing.

Common questions

My Honda's seat belt buckle clicks but doesn't hold. Is it recalled?

The front buckle recall covers 2017-2020 CR-V, 2018-2019 Accord and Accord Hybrid, 2018-2020 Odyssey, 2019 Insight and 2019-2020 Acura RDX, and says a buckle channel can interfere with the release button. Check your VIN with Honda.

Can I still use the third-row seat in my 2025 Pilot?

The notice says the third-row passenger belt may not function because incorrect routing traps it behind the passenger side panel, and that a non-functioning belt increases injury risk in a crash. It does not give owners any interim instruction; dealers repair the routing free.

Does a child seat still lock in my 2021 Accord or CR-V?

That notice says the automatic locking retractor on the second-row center belt may deactivate improperly, which can leave a child restraint unsecured and increase injury risk in a crash. Dealers replace the second-row center seat belt assembly, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
