Why are Dodge rear seats and seat backs under recall?

3 NHTSA recalls · Dodge · Seats · model years 2019–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 1, 2024

Three Dodge recalls sit in this group, and all of them involve rear seating that may not hold as designed. Two are near-identical: the 2023 Dodge Charger (recall 37B) and the 2023 Dodge Challenger (recall 38B), where the left rear seat back may not lock in the upright position, a non-compliance with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 207, "Seating Systems." The third, recall V91, covers the 2019 Dodge Grand Caravan, where welds on the outboard rear seat strikers for the second-row bench and second-row bucket seats may fail in a front impact, the front outboard strikers for the third-row bench may fail in a rear impact, and the second-row bench and bucket seats may not withstand the required loads.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the three notices in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. For the 2019 Dodge Grand Caravan (V91), the notice says that if the seat strikers fail, the seat or seat belt may not adequately restrain the occupant, increasing the risk of injury. For the 2023 Charger (37B) and the 2023 Challenger (38B), the notices say a seat back that is not locked into position can increase the risk of injury in a crash. The notices give no interim instruction beyond that.

What the free repair involves

For the 2019 Grand Caravan, dealers weld in a striker reinforcement bracket on the second- and third-row outboard seat strikers. For the 2023 Charger and the 2023 Challenger, dealers inspect and adjust the seat back latch striker as necessary. All three notices state the work is done free of charge. FCA US customer service is 1-800-853-1403.

Common questions

How do I know whether my Charger or Challenger is included?

Both notices cover certain 2023 model year vehicles — recall 37B for the Charger and 38B for the Challenger — and both describe the left rear seat back not locking upright. Owner notification letters were mailed August 15, 2024. A dealer can check your VIN.

Can I still carry passengers in the rear seat?

The notices do not address carrying passengers and do not tell owners to stop driving. They state only that a seat back that is not locked into position, or a seat striker that fails, can increase the risk of injury to an occupant in a crash.

Does the Grand Caravan seat striker repair cost anything?

The notice for recall V91 states that dealers will weld in a striker reinforcement bracket on the second- and third-row outboard seat strikers free of charge. That recall began December 19, 2019. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
