Why do some Dodge rear seat backs fail to lock in place?

3 NHTSA recalls · Dodge · Seats · model years 2019–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-08-01

Three Dodge seat recalls involve rear seats that may not hold occupants securely in a crash. The 2019 Dodge Grand Caravan recall covers welds on the second-row and third-row outboard seat strikers that may fail during a front or rear impact, and notes the second-row bench and bucket seats may not withstand required loads. Separately, the 2023 Dodge Charger and 2023 Dodge Challenger each have their own 2024 recall for a left rear seat back that may not lock in the upright position, addressed with the same latch-striker adjustment. All three recalls cover different models and distinct seat-structure defects, even though the Charger and Challenger fixes are nearly identical.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2023 Dodge Charger: left rear seat back does not lock in the upright position
  • 2023 Dodge Challenger: left rear seat back does not lock in the upright position
  • 2019 Dodge Grand Caravan: no day-to-day warning sign is described; the defect is a seat striker weld weakness found in testing

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe seat strikers or seat backs that may not adequately restrain an occupant in a crash, but do not tell owners to stop driving. Owners should still schedule the free dealer repair listed for their specific model and recall.

What the free repair involves

Dealers weld in a striker reinforcement bracket on the Grand Caravan's second- and third-row outboard seat strikers, and inspect and adjust the seat back latch striker on the Charger and Challenger as necessary. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Are the Dodge Charger and Challenger seat recalls the same defect?

They're separate recalls for separate models, but both describe a left rear seat back that may not lock in the upright position, and both are fixed the same way: dealers inspect and adjust the seat back latch striker, free of charge.

What's wrong with the Dodge Grand Caravan seat recall?

The 2019 Grand Caravan recall covers welds on the second- and third-row outboard seat strikers that may fail in a front or rear impact, and notes the second-row seats may not withstand required crash loads.

Will I notice a problem before the Dodge seat recall is fixed?

For the Charger and Challenger, the left rear seat back may not lock upright, which could be noticeable. The Grand Caravan recall does not describe any warning sign owners would notice beforehand.
