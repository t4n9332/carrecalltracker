Why are Kia doors, trim and headliners under recall?

6 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Structure · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-07-28

Kia's structure recalls in this group split into two patterns. Power sliding doors that may not auto-reverse when blocked affect the 2018 Sedona and the 2022-2023 Carnival. Exterior trim that can loosen and detach, creating a road hazard for other vehicles, affects three later recalls: roof molding on the 2022-2024 Carnival and 2023-2024 Sportage, rear window trim on the 2023-2025 K5, and door belt molding on the 2023-2025 Telluride. A sixth recall, for the 2024 EV9, is unrelated to doors or trim — its headliner may not adequately absorb impact in a crash, failing a federal safety standard.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A power sliding door that does not reverse when its closing path is obstructed (2018 Sedona; 2022-2023 Carnival)
  • Roof molding, rear window trim, or door trim that loosens before it detaches (Carnival/Sportage roof molding; K5 rear window trim; Telluride door trim)
  • No stated warning signs for the EV9 headliner impact-protection recall

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the six recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving for the Sedona and Carnival power sliding door recalls, the Carnival and Sportage roof molding recall, the EV9 headliner recall, the K5 rear window trim recall, or the Telluride door trim recall — even though the notices say a closing door can strike an occupant and detached trim can become a road hazard for other vehicles, and the EV9's headliner may not properly protect occupants in a crash.

What the free repair involves

Dealers reprogram the power sliding door control module software on the Sedona and Carnival; inspect and replace or secure roof molding on the Carnival and Sportage; replace headliner impact-absorbing plates on the EV9; and inspect and replace rear window or door trim assemblies as needed on the K5 and Telluride. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Which Kia models have power sliding door recalls?

The 2018 Sedona and the 2022-2023 Carnival, both because the auto-reverse feature may not activate when something obstructs the door while it's closing.

Are the Kia trim recalls a fire or crash risk?

No. The roof molding, rear window trim, and door trim recalls describe a detached piece as a road hazard for other vehicles, not a fire or mechanical failure of the vehicle itself.

What is the Kia EV9 headliner recall about?

The 2024 EV9's headliner may not adequately absorb impact in a crash, failing a federal interior-impact safety standard. Dealers replace the headliner's impact-absorbing plates free of charge.
