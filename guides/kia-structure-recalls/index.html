Why do Kia trim pieces and sliding doors keep getting recalled?

6 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Structure · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-07-28

Six Kia recalls sit in this structural group, and they fall into three patterns. Two involve power sliding doors that may not auto-reverse when closing is obstructed: 2015-2018 Sedona under SC164 and all 2022 plus certain 2023 Carnival under SC266. Three involve exterior trim that can loosen and come off: roof molding on 2023-2024 Sportage and 2022-2024 Carnival under SC292, rear window trim on 2023-2025 K5 under SC346, and door belt molding on 2023-2025 Telluride under SC347. The sixth, SC317, covers 2024 EV9 headliners that may not adequately protect passengers from impact in a crash. The Carnival appears in three of the six; model years 2022 through 2025 dominate.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A power sliding door that does not auto-reverse when its closing is obstructed (Sedona recall SC164, Carnival recall SC266).
  • Roof molding that loosens and detaches (Sportage and Carnival recall SC292).
  • Left or right rear window trim that loosens and detaches (K5 recall SC346).
  • Door belt molding trim that delaminates and detaches from the vehicle (Telluride recall SC347).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six recalls carries a stop-driving instruction, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. For SC164 and SC266, the notices describe a power sliding door that may not auto-reverse and do not tell owners to stop driving. For SC292, SC346 and SC347, the notices describe trim that can detach and create a road hazard for other vehicles, and again do not tell owners to stop driving. SC317, the 2024 EV9 headliner recall, likewise gives owners no instruction to stop driving. Kia's remedy instructions are the only action the notices set out.

What the free repair involves

The two door recalls are software fixes: dealers reprogram the power sliding door module software under SC164 and update the door control module software under SC266. For trim, dealers inspect and replace or secure the roof molding (SC292), replace C-pillar trim assemblies as necessary (SC346), or replace belt molding trim assemblies as necessary (SC347). Under SC317 dealers replace the headliner's impact absorbing plates. All this work is free of charge.

Common questions

My Carnival is listed in more than one of these recalls. Why?

Carnival appears in two of these six: SC266, the power sliding door software recall covering all 2022 and certain 2023 Carnivals, and SC292, the roof molding recall covering 2022-2024 Carnivals. Each recall has its own separate repair at the dealer.

Will the trim recalls replace my molding or just refasten it?

It depends on the recall and the inspection. Under SC292 dealers inspect the roof molding and replace or secure it as necessary. Under SC346 and SC347 dealers inspect and replace the C-pillar or belt molding trim assemblies as necessary, free of charge.

Should I stop using the power sliding doors until the software update?

The notices for SC164 and SC266 do not give owners any interim instruction about using the doors. They state that the door may not auto-reverse when obstructed and that dealers will reprogram or update the door module software free of charge.
