Why are Audi turn signals and headlights subject to recalls?

3 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-05-22

Three Audi recalls in this group involve exterior lighting defects, but each has a different root cause. On 2020-2021 A5 and S5 models, the rear wiring harness lacks anti-abrasion protection near the spare wheel well, which matters only if a rear crash damages the wires. On the 2018 Q3, the body control module fails to alert drivers when a front turn signal stops working, a software gap tied to LED headlight equipment. On the 2024 Q4 e-tron, incorrect headlight control module software can keep parking lights from working correctly when headlights and turn signals are used together. No single cause repeats; each recall reflects a separate wiring, software, or manufacturing issue.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Front turn signal stops working with no dashboard indicator alerting you (2018 Q3)
  • Parking lights not turning on correctly when headlights are on and the turn signal is used (2024 Q4 e-tron)
  • No noticeable warning sign for the rear wiring defect — it only shows up after a rear-end crash (2020-2021 A5/S5)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the A5/S5 rear wiring recall, the 2018 Q3 turn-signal indicator recall, and the 2024 Q4 e-tron headlight software recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving or to park outside while awaiting repair. The notices describe reduced visibility to other drivers and, in a rear crash, wiring damage that could prevent doors from opening from outside, but none of the three notices direct owners to stop using the vehicle in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Dealers address each defect differently: applying scuff protection to the rear wiring harness on A5/S5 models, updating the body control module software on the Q3 to properly flag turn signal failures, and updating the headlight control module software with a new data set on the Q4 e-tron. All repairs are performed free of charge under the recalls.

Common questions

Do any of these Audi lighting recalls affect safety while driving?

The notices do not instruct owners to stop driving for any of the three recalls. They describe risks — reduced visibility from incorrect headlight software, no turn-signal-failure warning on the Q3, and a rear-crash wiring issue on the A5/S5 — without directing owners to stop driving in the meantime.

Is the Audi Q4 e-tron headlight issue fixed with a software update?

Yes. For the 2022-2024 Q4 e-tron and Q4 e-tron Sportback, dealers install a new headlight control module data set to correct parking lights that don't operate as intended when headlights are on and turn signals are used, free of charge.

What is wrong with the wiring on the Audi A5 and S5?

On 2020-2021 A5 and S5 vehicles, the rear light wiring harness near the spare wheel well may be missing anti-abrasion protection. If damaged in a rear crash, this could prevent doors from opening from the outside and disable the hazard lights.
