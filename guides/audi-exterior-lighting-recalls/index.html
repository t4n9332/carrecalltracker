Audi exterior lighting recalls: turn signals, headlight software and rear wiring

3 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 22, 2024

Audi's exterior lighting recalls cover three separate problems. In recall 97DK, certain 2016-2018 Q3 vehicles with LED headlights do not illuminate an indicator when a front turn signal is inoperable, a Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108 compliance issue. Recall 941L covers certain 2022-2024 Q4 E-Tron and Q4 E-Tron Sportback vehicles, where incorrect headlight control module software may keep the parking lights from operating as intended when the headlights are on and the turn signals are activated, also an FMVSS 108 issue. Recall 97EU is different: on certain 2020-2021 A5 Sportback and S5 Sportback and 2021 RS5 Sportback vehicles, the rear light wiring harness could be missing anti-abrasion protection in the area of the spare wheel well. Wiring and software, not bulbs, are the recurring theme here.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall 97DK (2016-2018 Q3 with LED headlights): a front turn signal may be inoperable without an indicator alerting the driver.
  • Recall 941L (2022-2024 Q4 E-Tron, Q4 E-Tron Sportback): the parking lights may not operate as intended when the headlights are on and the turn signals are activated.
  • Recall 97EU (A5, S5, RS5 Sportback): the notice says damaged rear wiring in a rear crash can cause a loss of hazard lights and keep the doors from being opened from the outside.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none carries a park-outside instruction. Audi states the risks in crash terms: under recall 97DK, a driver may turn or change lanes without a signal, increasing the risk of a crash; under recall 941L, reduced vehicle visibility increases the risk of a crash; under recall 97EU, wires damaged in a rear crash may disable the doors from being opened from the outside and cause a loss of hazard lights. The notices give owners no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

The repairs differ by recall, and all are free of charge. Under recall 97DK, dealers update the body control module. Under recall 941L, dealers update the headlight control module with a new data set. Under recall 97EU, dealers apply scuff protection. None of the three notices describes an over-the-air update; each remedy is carried out by a dealer.

Common questions

Is every Audi Q3 included in the turn signal recall?

Recall 97DK covers certain 2016-2018 Q3 vehicles equipped with LED headlights, not every Q3 from those years. Checking your Vehicle Identification Number on NHTSA.gov, or calling Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834, is how to confirm.

Can the Q4 E-Tron headlight software be fixed remotely?

The notice for recall 941L says dealers will update the headlight control module with a new data set, free of charge. It does not describe an over-the-air update, so the work is done at a dealer.

What do the rear light wires have to do with my doors?

Audi's notice for recall 97EU says that without the scuff protection, the wires may become damaged during a rear crash, disabling the doors from being opened from the outside and causing a loss of hazard lights.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
