Why might a Volvo's hydraulic brakes lose function?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2020–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-06-12

These three Volvo hydraulic brake recalls span trucks and hybrid or electric passenger cars, with no repeated cause. A 2021 VNR truck recall covers park brakes that could apply unexpectedly if a damaged air line fitting releases pressure. A 2020 recall covers S60, V60, S90, XC60, and XC90 hybrids with a loose pushrod screw connecting the brake pedal to the hydraulic brake unit. The newest recall covers 2021 XC90 PHEV, 2023 XC40 BEV, 2024 XC40, and 2025 EX40 vehicles that may lose brake function while coasting downhill in 'B' mode or 'One Pedal Drive.'

Warning signs to watch for

  • No warning sign is described before the park brakes apply unexpectedly (2021 VNR trucks)
  • No warning sign is described for the loose brake pedal pushrod in 2020 S60, V60, S90, XC60, and XC90 hybrids
  • Loss of brake function while coasting downhill using 'B' mode or 'One Pedal Drive' (2021 XC90 PHEV, 2023 XC40 BEV, 2024 XC40, 2025 EX40)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2021 VNR park brake recall, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving. For the 2020 hybrid brake pedal pushrod recall, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving. For the 2021 XC90 PHEV, 2023 XC40 BEV, 2024 XC40, and 2025 EX40 recall, the notice tells owners not to use 'B' Mode or 'One Pedal Drive' until the repair is completed.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the air brake line fitting engagement on the 2021 VNR trucks, tighten the brake pedal pushrod screw joint on the 2020 hybrids, and update the brake module software, either over-the-air or by a dealer, on the newest PHEV, BEV, and EX40 vehicles. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Volvo vehicles are covered by these hydraulic brake recalls?

The recalls cover 2021 VNR trucks, 2020 S60, V60, S90, XC60, and XC90 hybrids, and 2021 XC90 PHEV, 2023 XC40 BEV, 2024 XC40, and 2025 EX40 vehicles, each for a different hydraulic brake defect.

Can I still use One Pedal Drive or B Mode?

For the 2021 XC90 PHEV, 2023 XC40 BEV, 2024 XC40, and 2025 EX40 recall, the notice tells owners not to use 'B' Mode or 'One Pedal Drive' until the repair is completed.

What causes the park brakes to apply on their own?

On the 2021 VNR truck recall, a damaged fitting can release air line pressure on the park brake circuit, allowing the spring-loaded park brakes to apply unexpectedly, according to the notice.
