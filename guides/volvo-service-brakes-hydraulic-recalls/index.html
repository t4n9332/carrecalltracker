Volvo hydraulic brake recalls: which cars and trucks are affected?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2020–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-06-12

This group mixes one Volvo Trucks action with two Volvo Car actions. The truck recall covers 2021 VNL, VNR, VAH and VHD trucks, where collets for the brass fitting inserts in the pneumatic pass-through plate may have been damaged during leak testing, so the fitting may release the air brake line under pressure and allow the park brakes to apply. On the car side, R10289 covers 2020 S60, V60, S90I, XC60 and XC90 hybrids with a loose brake pedal pushrod screw joint, and the 2025 recall covers plug-in hybrid and electric models — among them 2020-2026 XC90 PHEV, 2022-2026 XC60 PHEV and 2025-2026 EX40 — that may lose brake function while coasting downhill.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Park brakes applying unexpectedly — 2021 VNL, VNR, VAH and VHD trucks (2020 pass-through plate recall).
  • A loss of brake function while coasting down a hill using "B" mode or One Pedal Drive — 2025 Volvo Car recall.
  • A loose brake pedal that can result in a loss of braking function — 2020 hybrid recall R10289.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2025 Volvo Car recall is the only one carrying an interim instruction: it advises owners not to use "B" Mode on plug-in hybrid vehicles, or One Pedal Drive on pure electric vehicles, until the repair has been completed. For the 2021 truck park brake recall and the 2020 hybrid pushrod recall R10289, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving and give no interim step of any kind.

What the free repair involves

Under R10289, dealers tighten the brake pedal pushrod screw joint, free of charge. Under the 2021 truck recall, dealers inspect the air brake line for proper engagement of the fitting in the pass-through plate, free of charge. Under the 2025 Volvo Car recall, the brake module software is updated over-the-air or by a dealer, free of charge.

Common questions

Can I still use One Pedal Drive on my EX40?

The 2025 notice advises owners not to use One Pedal Drive on pure electric vehicles, or "B" Mode on plug-in hybrids, until the repair has been completed. The brake module software update is delivered over-the-air or by a dealer, free of charge.

Does the 2025 brake function recall cover my XC60?

It lists 2022-2026 XC60 PHEV among the affected vehicles, along with 2020-2026 XC90 PHEV, 2023-2025 S60 PHEV, 2024-2025 V60 PHEV, 2023-2024 XC40 BEV and others. Volvo Car customer service at 1-800-458-1552 can confirm against your VIN.

What is the park brake problem on 2021 Volvo trucks?

The notice says damaged collets may let the brass fitting completely release the air brake line from the pass-through plate while under pressure. On the park brake circuit, the lost signal pressure can allow the park brakes to apply, risking loss of control.
