Infiniti QX60 Seat Recalls Cover Locking and Mounting Defects

3 NHTSA recalls · Infiniti · Seats · model years 2022–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 13, 2023

The Infiniti QX60 appears throughout these seat recalls, with two notices concerning 2023 models and another covering 2022 models. The 2022 QX60 notice concerns a potentially missing second-row seat track mounting bolt, which can allow track movement during a crash. One 2023 QX60 notice concerns a second-row adjustable seat that may extend beyond its intended rear stopper, become difficult to move, become stuck, or fail to lock correctly. The other 2023 notice concerns a driver's powered seat that may not be fully secured to its frame, allowing welds to detach from the rear inboard bracket. The repeated concern is seat security and occupant restraint during a crash.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2023 QX60 Second-Row Seat May Not Lock recall (PC953): the adjustable seat may extend past its intended rear stopper, be difficult to move, become stuck, or fail to lock correctly.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The notices for the 2022 QX60 missing seat track bolt recall, the 2023 QX60 second-row locking recall, and the 2023 QX60 driver's seat weld recall do not tell owners to stop driving. None includes a park-outside instruction. The second-row locking notice specifically describes a seat that may become difficult to move, stuck, or unable to lock correctly. The notices identify crash-related injury risks from seat track movement, a seat that does not lock securely, or an unsecured driver's seat.

What the free repair involves

For the 2022 QX60, dealers inspect the second-row seat track and install a mounting bolt as necessary. The 2023 second-row locking recall requires seat replacement. For the 2023 driver's seat weld recall, dealers inspect seat rail clearance and replace the driver's seat cushion frame if necessary. These remedies are free of charge.

Common questions

Why is my recalled 2023 QX60 second-row seat difficult to move?

Recall PC953 describes a second-row adjustable seat that may extend past its intended rear stopper point. The notice explicitly says it may be difficult to move, become stuck, or fail to lock correctly. Dealers replace the second-row seat free of charge.

Does the 2022 QX60 seat recall require a replacement seat?

The 2022 notice specifies inspection of the second-row seat track and installation of a new mounting bolt as necessary, free of charge. Its stated defect is a potentially missing mounting bolt, which may allow track movement during a crash.

What repair does the 2023 QX60 driver's seat weld recall specify?

Dealers inspect the driver's seat rail clearance and replace the driver's seat cushion frame if necessary, free of charge. This notice concerns a powered seat that may not be fully secured to its frame, allowing welds to detach.
