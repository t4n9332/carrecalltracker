BMW engine recalls cover oil leaks, shafts and brake assist

3 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Engine · model years 2019–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 2, 2021

This is a small group with three separate problems rather than one repeating fault. The 2020 S 1000 RR motorcycle recall involves oil cooler hoses that may not be attached properly to the oil pipes, possibly resulting in an oil leak. The 2019-2020 330i, 330i xDrive and 2019 Z4 recall involves needle roller bearings of the counterbalance shafts that may have been improperly installed, potentially letting the shaft loosen. The third, covering 2020 M340i and 540i variants, 2020-2021 X4 M40i and 745Le xDrive, 2019-2021 Z4 M40i and 2020-2021 Toyota Supra, is a software matter. The Z4 is the one model that appears in two of these three notices.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2020 S 1000 RR recall: the notice says leaking oil may drip in the path of the rear tire.
  • BMW and Toyota Supra recall: the notice says the engine management software may cause a loss of braking assist during engine start, with full mechanical braking, without assist, remaining available.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; they do not tell owners to stop driving. The notices state the consequences only: on the 2020 S 1000 RR, leaking oil may drip in the path of the rear tire, increasing the risk of a crash; on the 2019-2020 330i and 2019 Z4, a loosened counterbalance shaft could cause severe engine damage, increasing the risk of a crash; and on the software recall, a loss of braking assist can increase the risk of a crash.

What the free repair involves

The work differs by recall. On the 2020 S 1000 RR, dealers replace the oil cooler assembly, including the hoses. On the 2019-2020 330i, 330i xDrive and 2019 Z4, dealers replace the engine. On the BMW and Toyota Supra recall, dealers update the engine management software. Each of these three notices states the repair is free of charge.

Common questions

Do these three BMW engine recalls share a cause?

They are unrelated. One covers oil cooler hoses on 2020 S 1000 RR motorcycles, one covers counterbalance shaft needle roller bearings on 2019-2020 330i, 330i xDrive and 2019 Z4 vehicles, and one covers engine management software on BMW and Toyota Supra models.

Why is a Toyota Supra listed in a BMW engine recall?

The 2020-2021 Toyota Supra is included in BMW's recall of vehicles whose engine management software may cause a loss of braking assist during engine start. The notice says dealers update the engine management software free of charge.

How big is the repair for the counterbalance shaft recall?

That notice says dealers will replace the engine, free of charge, on affected 2019-2020 330i, 330i xDrive and 2019 Z4 vehicles. It states that if the counterbalance shaft loosens, severe engine damage could occur, increasing the risk of a crash.
