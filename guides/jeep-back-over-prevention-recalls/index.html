Why Does the Rearview Camera Fail on So Many Jeep Models?

9 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-12-19

Nine Jeep recalls in this group all trace back to the same federal requirement: a working rearview camera image, per Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 111. Software errors are the most common cause, hitting the Wrangler, Gladiator, and Ram 1500 (2022), Grand Cherokee and Wagoneer models (2023, 2024, 2024), and Compass, Durango, and Pacifica among many others (2024). Hardware problems show up too: a 2018-2024 Wrangler recall traces to a wiring harness short that can also disable the center brake light, and two 2024 Grand Cherokee recalls involve a loose coaxial cable and unfastened screen module connections. An older 2019-2020 recall covers a different symptom: the camera image lingering on screen after leaving reverse.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rearview camera image doesn't display when the vehicle is shifted into reverse
  • On some 2018-2024 Wranglers, the center brake light may also fail to illuminate
  • On some 2019-2020 model-year vehicles with 8.4-inch or 12-inch radio screens, the rearview image stays on screen after backing up instead of clearing

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these nine Jeep back-over-prevention recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; none of the notices tell owners to stop driving. That includes the radio and Central Vision Park Assist Module software errors, the wiring harness short affecting the Wrangler's camera and brake light, the loose coaxial cable and screen module connections, and the lingering camera-image software error. Each notice ties the defect to reduced visibility behind the vehicle, which the notices say increases the risk of a crash, but none of them directs owners to stop driving or park outside.

What the free repair involves

Dealers update the radio or camera module software, or repair the underlying hardware — replacing a wiring harness, securing a coaxial cable, or reconnecting a screen module — depending on the recall. Two of these recalls also offer the software fix as an over-the-air update. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why do so many Jeep models have rearview camera recalls?

Most trace to software errors — in the radio, the Central Vision Park Assist Module, or the camera module itself — that prevent the required rearview image from displaying. A few others involve a damaged wiring harness or a loose cable connection instead.

Can I fix a Jeep rearview camera recall without visiting a dealer?

Sometimes. Two recalls in this group, covering 2019-2023 model-year vehicles, let owners apply the software fix as a remote over-the-air update instead of a dealer visit. The rest require a dealer to perform the software update or hardware repair.

Does a failed backup camera mean my Jeep isn't safe to drive?

The recall notices don't tell owners to stop driving. They state that a rearview image that doesn't display reduces visibility behind the vehicle and increases crash risk, and direct owners to get the free dealer repair.
