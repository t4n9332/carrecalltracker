Jeep rearview camera recalls: blank screens and software fixes

9 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 19, 2024

Nine Chrysler (FCA US) recalls cover Jeep rear visibility, and most end the same way: the rearview camera image does not display. Six are software problems — radio software on 2020-2021 Wrangler and 2021 Gladiator, on 2022 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer, and on 2022-2023 Compass, Grand Cherokee and Wagoneer; Central Vision Park Assist module software on 2021-2023 Grand Cherokee L and 2022-2024 Wagoneer; and rearview camera module software on 2024 Wagoneer and Grand Cherokee. One of the six, covering 2019-2020 models, is the opposite: the image stays on screen after the vehicle leaves reverse. Two more are loose display connections on Grand Cherokee. The ninth covers 2018-2024 Wrangler, where a wiring harness short circuit can disable the camera image and the center brake light.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No image on the screen when the vehicle is shifted into reverse — the 2021-2023 Grand Cherokee L and 2022-2024 Wagoneer Central Vision Park Assist recall.
  • The rearview camera image stays on the screen after the vehicle is shifted out of reverse — the 2019-2020 radio display software recall.
  • A center brake light that does not illuminate, alongside a blank camera image — the 2018-2024 Jeep Wrangler wiring harness recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the nine notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. Each describes reduced rear visibility, or in the 2019-2020 recall a lingering image that can distract the driver, and each states an increased risk of a crash. The notices set out the dealer remedy and, for three of them, say all affected vehicles have already been repaired. Beyond that, no notice in this group gives owners an interim instruction, a temporary measure, or anything to do differently before the repair.

What the free repair involves

Most of these are software: dealers update the radio, Central Vision Park Assist module or rearview camera module software. In the June 2024 recall the update can also be delivered over the air, and the 2019-2020 radio display recall offered owners an optional over-the-air update. Elsewhere dealers secure the display screen module connections, secure the coaxial cable, or replace the Wrangler's harness. Every notice says free of charge.

Common questions

My Jeep's camera screen goes black in reverse — is there a recall?

Several notices describe exactly that. Which one applies depends on the model and year: radio software, Central Vision Park Assist module software, rearview camera module software, a loose screen module connection or coaxial cable, or the Wrangler wiring harness. Check your VIN with NHTSA.

Can any of these be fixed over the air instead of at a dealer?

Two notices mention it. The June 2024 radio software recall says dealers will update the software or it will be delivered over the air. The 2019-2020 lingering-image recall says owners could optionally update remotely, with the over-the-air update available from May 1, 2020.

Does the Wrangler recall affect anything besides the camera?

Yes. The 2018-2024 Wrangler notice says the wiring harness for the rearview camera and the center brake light may short circuit, which can disable both. It cites Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards 111 and 108. Dealers replaced the harness and, as necessary, the spare tire carrier assembly.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
