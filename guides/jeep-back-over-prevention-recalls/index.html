Why Is My Jeep Rearview Camera Image Not Displaying?

11 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 13, 2026

Missing rearview images dominate these Jeep recalls, with Grand Cherokee, Grand Cherokee L, Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer appearing repeatedly. The 2023 CVPAM recall covers 2022-2023 Grand Cherokee, 2021-2023 Grand Cherokee L and 2022-2024 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer. Separate 2024 notices identify display screen connections, a coaxial cable connection and rearview camera module software. Wrangler appears in the 2020-2021 radio software recall and the 2018-2024 camera and center brake light harness recall. The September 2020 notice describes a different problem: an image remaining after reverse. The 2026 notices include a front camera harness connection on Grand Wagoneer models and missing images across several Jeep models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • September 2020 lingering-image recall: the rearview image remains displayed after shifting out of reverse.
  • Z86: the rearview camera image may not display.
  • 56A: the rearview image may not display when the vehicle is placed in reverse.
  • September 2024 Wrangler harness recall: the rearview image and center brake light may stop working.
  • 78D: the rearview camera image may fail to display when the vehicle is placed in reverse.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these eleven notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. This includes Z86, 56A, 66B/79B, the September 2024 Wrangler camera and brake light harness recall, D5B, 47D, the September 2020 lingering-image recall, YA1, 44B, 40B and 78D. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving with the listed radio software, camera module software, CVPAM software, screen connection, coaxial cable connection or wiring harness defects, or with the lingering rearview image described in September 2020.

What the free repair involves

Repairs are free under these notices. Z86 updates radio software; 56A updates CVPAM software; D5B updates rearview camera module software. Recalls 44B and 40B secure screen module and coaxial cable connections, respectively. Recall 47D inspects and reconnects the harness as necessary. Recalls 66B/79B and 78D provide radio updates through a dealer or OTA.

Common questions

Which Jeep camera recalls offer an over-the-air update?

The September 2020 lingering-image notice offers an optional OTA radio display software update. Recall 66B/79B provides a dealer or OTA radio software update, and 78D also provides a dealer or OTA radio software update. These remedies are free.

Can a Wrangler recall affect both the camera and brake light?

The September 2024 notice covers certain 2018-2024 Wranglers whose camera and center brake light harness may short circuit. Dealers replaced the harness and/or spare tire carrier/harness assembly as necessary, free of charge. The notice says vehicles were repaired by January 15, 2024.

Do the Grand Cherokee connection recalls require software updates?

Recall 44B specifies securing display screen module connections on certain 2023 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles. Recall 40B specifies securing a coaxial cable connection on certain 2022-2023 Grand Cherokee and 2023-2024 Grand Cherokee L vehicles. Both remedies are free.
