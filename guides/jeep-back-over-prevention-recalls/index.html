Jeep Backup Camera Recalls Cover Missing and Lingering Images

11 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 13, 2026

Jeep's backup camera notices repeatedly describe a rearview image that fails to display, with Grand Cherokee, Grand Cherokee L, Wagoneer, and Grand Wagoneer appearing across several notices. Software defects affect different modules: the 2023 recall identifies Central Vision Park Assist software, while the December 2024 recall identifies rearview camera module software in certain 2024 SUVs. Connection defects also recur, including screen module and coaxial cable connections in separate April 2024 Grand Cherokee notices. The Wrangler harness recall covers certain 2018-2024 vehicles and also involves the center brake light. A different software defect in the September 2020 notice leaves the camera image displayed after shifting out of reverse.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Z86, 56A, 66B/79B, YA1, D5B, and 78D recalls: the rearview camera image may not display.
  • September 2020 lingering-image recall: the rearview camera image may remain displayed after shifting out of reverse.
  • September 2024 Wrangler camera and brake light recall: the rearview image may not display and the center brake light may not illuminate.
  • April 2024 Grand Cherokee recalls 44B and 40B: the rearview camera image may not display.
  • 2026 Grand Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer L recall 47D: the rearview camera image may not display.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The supplied Jeep backup camera notices do not tell owners to stop driving, and none includes a park-outside instruction. Most describe a missing rearview image that reduces rear visibility and increases crash risk. The September 2020 notice instead describes a lingering image that can distract the driver after shifting out of reverse. The September 2024 Wrangler notice also identifies a center brake light that may fail to indicate slowing to other drivers. These are the notices' stated hazards, without additional driving instructions.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies are recall-specific: Z86/YA1 radio software; 56A park-assist software; 66B/79B and 78D dealer/OTA radio software; D5B camera-module software; September 2020 dealer/optional OTA display software. Dealers secure screen connections for 44B and coaxial connections for 40B, inspect/reconnect wiring for 47D, and replace the specified harness and/or spare tire carrier/harness assembly as necessary for the Wrangler recall.

Common questions

Can a Jeep backup camera recall be repaired over the air?

The September 2020 lingering-image notice offers an optional OTA update. The June 2024 recall, numbered 66B and 79B, and August 2026 recall 78D specify dealer or OTA software updates. Those remedies are free; the other supplied notices specify dealer remedies.

Why does my recalled Jeep camera image stay on after reverse?

The September 2020 notice identifies a software error that can leave the rearview image displayed after shifting out of reverse. It covers specified Jeep models with 8.4-inch or 12-inch radio displays. The remedy is a free radio display software update.

Which Wrangler camera recall also affects the center brake light?

The September 2024 notice covers certain 2018-2024 Wranglers. A wiring harness short circuit can disable both functions. Dealers replaced the center brake light and camera harness and/or spare tire carrier/harness assembly as necessary, free of charge.
