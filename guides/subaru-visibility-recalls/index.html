Subaru visibility recalls involving glass, backup cameras and defrosters

5 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Visibility · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 28, 2026

Glass problems account for three of these five Subaru visibility recalls. Certain 2022 Impreza and Outback vehicles have insufficient windshield bonding associated with incorrect paint clearcoat. Certain 2026 Forester and Forester Hybrid vehicles have moonroof glass that may detach because of improper bonding, while certain 2019 Crosstrek vehicles have improperly tempered driver's side rear-door glass. The remaining notices address a blank backup camera display on certain 2018 Legacy, Outback and BRZ vehicles, and defroster or defogger failure during certain HVAC faults on 2023-2025 Solterra vehicles. Outback appears in two notices, covering separate camera and windshield problems in 2018 and 2022.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2018 Legacy, Outback and BRZ backup camera recall: the camera display may remain blank while backing up.
  • 2023-2025 Solterra defroster recall: the defroster and defogger may become inoperative during certain HVAC faults.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. The 2022 Impreza and Outback windshield notice describes glass detachment during a crash. The 2026 Forester moonroof notice describes detached glass becoming a road hazard, and the 2019 Crosstrek window notice describes injury risk from large glass pieces. The 2018 Legacy, Outback and BRZ camera notice concerns a missing rearview image, while the 2023-2025 Solterra HVAC notice concerns reduced visibility. Each notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Remedies are free of charge: Subaru offers exchange or repurchase for the Impreza/Outback windshield recall. Dealers update HVAC software and inspect/replace the compressor as necessary for Solterra; inspect/replace the moonroof glass assembly as necessary for Forester; reprogram audio display software for the Legacy/Outback/BRZ camera recall; and replace the driver's side rear-door glass for Crosstrek.

Common questions

Does the Subaru windshield recall provide a glass replacement?

The notice for certain 2022 Impreza and Outback vehicles specifies a vehicle exchange or repurchase, free of charge. It describes insufficient windshield bonding associated with incorrect paint clearcoat and says the windshield can detach during a crash.

Which Subaru backup camera recall describes a blank screen?

The notice for certain 2018 Legacy, Outback and BRZ vehicles describes an audio display software issue that can leave the camera display blank while backing up. Dealers reprogram the audio system display software free of charge.

What repair does the Solterra defroster recall require?

For the recall covering certain 2023-2025 Solterra vehicles, dealers update the HVAC control ECU software free of charge. They also inspect the electrical compressor and replace it if necessary, free of charge. The notice describes defroster and defogger failure during certain HVAC faults.
