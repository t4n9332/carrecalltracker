Why do these Subaru recalls affect windows, cameras and defrosters?

4 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Visibility · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-09-04

These four Subaru visibility recalls touch four unrelated systems rather than one repeated defect. On 2022 Impreza and Outback vehicles, an incorrect paint clearcoat left the windshield insufficiently bonded. On 2023-2025 Solterra vehicles (recalled together with Toyota bZ4X), an HVAC system fault can make the defroster and defogger stop working. On 2018 Legacy, Outback and BRZ vehicles, a software issue can leave the backup camera screen blank while backing up. On 2019 Crosstrek vehicles, the driver's side rear-door window glass may be improperly tempered and able to shatter into large pieces. Each recall addresses a distinct visibility-related part.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2022 Impreza/Outback: no warning sign is described before a windshield detaches in a crash
  • 2023-2025 Solterra (and Toyota bZ4X): the defroster and defogger may stop working during an HVAC system fault
  • 2018 Legacy, Outback and BRZ: the rearview camera screen stays blank when backing up
  • 2019 Crosstrek: no warning sign is described before the rear door window glass shatters

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four Subaru visibility recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. The 2022 Impreza and Outback windshield bonding recall, the 2023-2025 Solterra and Toyota bZ4X defroster recall, the 2018 Legacy, Outback and BRZ backup camera recall, and the 2019 Crosstrek rear window glass recall each describe a safety consequence — reduced crash protection, reduced visibility, a blank rearview image, or shattering glass — but none of the four notices instructs owners to stop driving before the repair is made.

What the free repair involves

Fixes differ by recall: Subaru offers a vehicle exchange or repurchase for the windshield bonding issue on the Impreza and Outback, dealers update HVAC control ECU software (and replace the compressor if needed) for the Solterra/bZ4X defroster recall, dealers reprogram the audio display software for the Legacy/Outback/BRZ camera recall, and dealers replace the rear door window glass on the Crosstrek. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Do all these Subaru visibility recalls share one cause?

No. They involve four separate issues: windshield bonding on the Impreza and Outback, defroster/defogger software on the Solterra, backup camera software on the Legacy, Outback and BRZ, and improperly tempered glass on the Crosstrek.

Why does the windshield recall result in a buyback instead of a repair?

The notice states that Subaru will offer affected 2022 Impreza and Outback owners a vehicle exchange or repurchase, free of charge, rather than describe an in-place repair of the insufficiently bonded windshield.

Is the 2018 backup camera recall a hardware problem?

No. The notice describes a software issue where the audio display system fails to initialize, leaving the camera screen blank. Dealers fix it by reprogramming the audio system display software, free of charge.
