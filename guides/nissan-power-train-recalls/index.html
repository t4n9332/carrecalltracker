Nissan rollaway and drive power recalls: which models are affected?

4 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Power Train · model years 2020–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 26, 2026

These four Nissan power train recalls follow two patterns. Two of them concern the transmission parking pawl, which the notices say may not engage when the vehicle is shifted into park, resulting in a vehicle rollaway. One covers certain 2020-2022 Frontier and Titan vehicles; the other covers certain 2020-2023 Titan, 2020-2022 Frontier and 2023 Z vehicles. The other two concern the Sentra front left-side driveshaft, which may not be fully seated in the continuously variable transmission assembly. The 2024 Sentra notice also cites a loose or missing retaining clip; the 2025 Sentra notice adds that a driveshaft that is not fully engaged can result in a rollaway if the parking brake is not applied.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Vehicle rollaway: both parking pawl recalls say the transmission parking pawl may not engage when the vehicle is shifted into park, which can result in a rollaway.
  • Transmission fluid leak and loss of drive power: the 2024 and 2025 Sentra recalls both say a driveshaft that is not fully seated in the CVT can cause a transmission fluid leak and loss of drive power.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Both parking pawl notices advise owners to apply the parking brake every time they park their vehicle. The 2024 Sentra notice says an unexpected loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash. The 2025 Sentra notice says the same and adds that a driveshaft that is not fully engaged can result in a rollaway if the parking brake is not applied. Neither Sentra notice gives an interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

All four notices say the work is free of charge. For the first parking pawl recall, dealers replace the transmission parking pawl pin. For the second, dealers reprogram the transmission control module on 2020-2022 Frontier vehicles, and the TCM and engine control module on 2020-2022 Titan vehicles. For both Sentra recalls, dealers inspect the front left driveshaft and, if necessary, replace the driveshaft and the CVT assembly.

Common questions

Which vehicles do the two parking pawl recalls cover?

One covers certain 2020-2022 Frontier and Titan vehicles. The other covers certain 2020-2023 Titan, 2020-2022 Frontier and 2023 Z vehicles. Both notices say the transmission parking pawl may not engage when the vehicle is shifted into park, which can result in a rollaway.

Why do the parking pawl notices mention the parking brake?

Both parking pawl notices advise owners to apply the parking brake every time they park their vehicle. The notices still direct owners to a dealer for the listed repair, either a parking pawl pin replacement or a control module reprogram, performed free of charge.

My Sentra lost drive power. Is that covered here?

The 2024 and 2025 Sentra notices say the front left-side driveshaft may not be fully seated in the CVT assembly, which can cause a transmission fluid leak and loss of drive power. Dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace the driveshaft and CVT assembly free of charge.
