Why Do Nissan Frontier, Titan and Sentra Lose Power or Roll Away?

4 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Power Train · model years 2020–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-26

This group covers two separate powertrain problems. In 2022, Nissan recalled 2020 Titan and 2022 Frontier trucks twice for the same transmission parking pawl defect: the pawl may not engage when the transmission is shifted into park, letting the vehicle roll away. Separately, Nissan recalled 2024 Sentra and 2025 Sentra models because the front left-side driveshaft may not be fully seated in the continuously variable transmission, which can cause a fluid leak and a sudden loss of drive power. The Sentra driveshaft problem showed up two years in a row, while the Frontier and Titan rollaway issue required two recalls in the same year to address.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Vehicle rolls away after being shifted into park (2020 Titan, 2022 Frontier)
  • Transmission fluid leak from the front left driveshaft area (2024-2025 Sentra)
  • Sudden loss of drive power while driving (2024-2025 Sentra)
  • The notices describe no warning sign before the parking pawl fails to engage

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recall notices carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The two 2022 recalls for the Frontier and Titan parking pawl tell owners to apply the parking brake every time they park, as an interim step until the repair is done. The 2024 and 2025 Sentra driveshaft recalls give no interim instruction; those notices say a driveshaft that isn't fully seated can cause a power loss or, if the parking brake isn't applied, a rollaway. None of the four notices tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the Frontier and Titan, dealers replace the transmission parking pawl pin, or reprogram the transmission control module (and engine control module, depending on model and year) to fix the rollaway defect. For the Sentra, dealers inspect the front left driveshaft and, if needed, replace the driveshaft and CVT assembly. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why does my Nissan Frontier or Titan need two separate rollaway recalls?

Nissan issued two 2022 recalls for the same transmission parking pawl defect, which may not engage in park and let the vehicle roll away. The second recall expanded the repair to include reprogramming the transmission control module.

What causes the Nissan Sentra driveshaft recall?

In both the 2024 and 2025 Sentra recalls, Nissan says the front left-side driveshaft may not be fully seated in the CVT, which can leak transmission fluid and cause a loss of drive power.

Do I need to do anything before my Nissan Frontier or Titan is repaired?

Nissan advises owners covered by the 2022 rollaway recalls to apply the parking brake every time they park the vehicle, until the dealer completes the parking pawl or software repair described in the notice.
