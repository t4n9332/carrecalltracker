Porsche Gasoline Fuel System Recalls Address Leaks and Fire Risks

4 NHTSA recalls · Porsche · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 23, 2025

Fuel leakage and fire risk recur across these four Porsche notices, but the affected parts differ. The February 2019 recall covers certain 2017–2019 718 Boxster and Cayman models and 2018–2019 GTS variants, with a crossmember that may detach during a crash and cause a fuel leak. The February 2020 Macan notice covers certain 2015–2018 vehicles and describes service cover contact that can crack the fuel pump flange. Cayenne appears in two notices: a May 2020 quick connector recall covering specified 2019–2020 variants, and a September 2025 high pressure fuel pump fastener recall covering certain 2024–2026 Cayenne and Panamera vehicles and 2025 Panamera E-Hybrids.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the four supplied Porsche gasoline fuel system notices instructs owners to stop driving or park outside. The February 2019 718 notice describes possible fuel leakage following crossmember detachment during a crash. The February 2020 Macan, May 2020 Cayenne Turbo, and September 2025 Cayenne/Panamera notices describe fuel leaks involving different fuel system components. Each identifies a fire risk when fuel leakage occurs near an ignition source. The supplied notices do not tell owners to stop driving or provide an owner workaround.

What the free repair involves

The 718 recall specifies additional crossmember rivets, free. The Cayenne Turbo recall specifies free fuel line and quick connector replacement. The September 2025 recall specifies free screw connection inspection and tightening, with pump and fuel line replacement as necessary. The Macan notice describes flange rework or replacement, protective film, and crack inspection; its supplied text does not state that repair is free.

Common questions

What causes the fuel leak risk in the Porsche 718 recall?

The February 2019 notice says a crossmember may detach from the luggage compartment bracket during a crash, possibly causing a fuel leak. It covers specified 718 Boxster and Cayman variants. Dealers secure the crossmember with additional rivets free of charge.

Do the two Cayenne fuel recalls require the same repair?

The May 2020 notice specifies free replacement of the fuel line and quick connector. The September 2025 notice specifies free inspection and tightening of high pressure fuel pump screw connections, with fuel pump and fuel line replacement as necessary.

Does the Macan fuel pump notice describe a smell or dashboard warning?

The supplied February 2020 Macan notice does not describe a fuel smell, dashboard warning, or another owner-visible warning sign. It describes possible flange cracking and leakage, and specifies flange work, protective film, and inspection with replacement if cracks are present.
