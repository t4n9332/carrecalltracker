Porsche fuel leak recalls cover Macan, Cayenne and Panamera fuel pumps

3 NHTSA recalls · Porsche · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-09-23

All three of these Porsche recalls end in the same consequence — a fuel leak near an ignition source — but they reach it different ways, and they are spread five years apart. The oldest, from February 2020, covers 2015-2018 Macan vehicles whose fuel pump service cover may contact the flow nozzle on the filter flange, causing cracks and a leak. A May 2020 recall covers 2019-2020 Cayenne Turbo and 2020 Cayenne Turbo Coupe, Turbo S Hybrid and Turbo S Hybrid Coupe models for a fuel line quick connector in the engine compartment that may leak. The newest, from September 2025, covers 2024-2026 Panamera and Cayenne and 2025 Panamera E-Hybrid vehicles whose high-pressure fuel pump fasteners may not be tightened properly.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle away from buildings. The 2015-2018 Macan fuel pump service cover recall, the 2019-2020 Cayenne Turbo fuel line quick connector recall and the 2024-2026 Panamera and Cayenne high-pressure fuel pump fastener recall all describe the fire risk and the dealer repair, and none of them sets out an interim instruction for owners to follow while they wait for the remedy.

What the free repair involves

For the 2015-2018 Macan, Porsche reworks or replaces the fuel pump flange and applies a protective film, inspecting it and replacing it if cracks are present. For the 2019-2020 Cayenne Turbo, dealers replace the fuel line and quick connector free of charge. For the 2024-2026 Panamera and Cayenne, dealers inspect and tighten the screw connections and replace the high-pressure fuel pump and fuel lines as necessary, free of charge.

Common questions

Which Porsche models are covered by these fuel leak recalls?

Three groups: 2015-2018 Macan, where the fuel pump service cover may contact the flow nozzle and cause cracks; 2019-2020 Cayenne Turbo and 2020 Turbo Coupe models with a leaking fuel line quick connector; and 2024-2026 Panamera and Cayenne with improperly tightened high-pressure fuel pump fasteners.

Is the repair free?

The 2019-2020 Cayenne Turbo notice and the 2024-2026 Panamera and Cayenne notice both state the repair is free of charge. The 2015-2018 Macan notice describes reworking or replacing the fuel pump flange and inspecting for cracks without stating a cost.

Do the notices say I should stop driving until the fuel leak is fixed?

None of the three notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside, and none gives an interim step to take before the repair. Each describes the fuel leak, the fire risk in the presence of an ignition source, and the dealer remedy.
