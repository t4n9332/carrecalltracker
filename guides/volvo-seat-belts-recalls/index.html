Why do some Volvo seat belts fail to secure or warn properly?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-11

These three Volvo seat belt recalls involve unrelated causes across different vehicle types. The 2018 S60 and V60 have a seat belt cable that may fatigue over time, so it can no longer secure the occupant in a crash. Separately, 2023 Volvo VAH trucks may have a passenger seat belt anchor that fails because the floor reinforcement underneath it is inadequate. The newest recall covers 2025-2026 EX30 and 2026 EX30CC electric vehicles, where the seat belt warning chime may not sound as intended. No single model or model year repeats across these three recalls; each reflects a distinct defect specific to that vehicle line.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Seat belt warning chime may not sound when a seat belt is unbuckled (2025-2026 EX30, 2026 EX30CC)
  • No warning sign is described for the seat belt cable fatigue in 2018 S60 and V60
  • No warning sign is described for the seat belt anchor failure in 2023 VAH trucks

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the seat belt cable fatigue in 2018 S60 and V60 vehicles, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving. For the seat belt anchor issue in 2023 VAH trucks, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving. For the seat belt warning chime issue in 2025-2026 EX30 and 2026 EX30CC vehicles, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the front seat belt anchor cable on affected S60 and V60 vehicles, install a new weld nut and washer on VAH truck seat belt anchors, and replace terminals at the audio module connector on EX30 and EX30CC vehicles to restore the seat belt warning chime. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Volvo models are affected by these seat belt recalls?

The recalls cover 2018 S60 and V60 cars, 2023 VAH trucks, and 2025-2026 EX30 and 2026 EX30CC electric vehicles. Each recall addresses a different seat belt defect specific to that model.

Will I notice if my seat belt cable has fatigued?

The notice for the 2018 S60 and V60 seat belt cable recall does not describe any warning sign, so the cable can weaken without the driver noticing anything before it fails in a crash.

What happens if the seat belt chime doesn't sound?

On affected 2025-2026 EX30 and 2026 EX30CC vehicles, the audible reminder may not alert an unbelted occupant, which the notice says can increase the risk of injury in a crash.
