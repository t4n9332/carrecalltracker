Which Volvo seat belt recalls affect cars and trucks?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-11

Volvo's seat belt recalls split between passenger cars and heavy trucks. The oldest, R10029, covers a wide span of cars — 2008-2016 V70 and XC70, 2007-2016 S80, 2011-2018 S60 and V60, 2016-2018 S60 Cross Country, 2014-2020 S60L, 2009-2015 S80L, 2009-2016 XC60 and 2015-2018 V60 Cross Country — where the flexible steel cable connecting the seat belt to the front outboard seating positions may fatigue over time. The other two are compliance recalls: 2019-2026 VAH trucks whose passenger seat belt anchor may fail due to inadequate floor reinforcement (FMVSS 210), and 2025-2026 EX30 and 2026 EX30CC vehicles whose seat belt warning system may not sound the audible chime (FMVSS 208).

Warning signs to watch for

  • The EX30 recall (R10365) says the seat belt warning system may not activate the audible warning chime as intended.
  • The other two notices do not describe a warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The cable fatigue recall R10029, the VAH truck anchor recall RVXX2510 and the EX30 chime recall R10365 each state that the notice does not tell owners to stop driving. Beyond that, the notices set out no interim step to take before the dealer work is done; they describe the increased risk of injury in a crash and point owners to Volvo customer service.

What the free repair involves

The remedy differs by recall. Under R10029, dealers replace the seat belt anchor cable for both front seats. Under RVXX2510, dealers install a new weld nut and washer on VAH trucks, free of charge. Under R10365, dealers replace the terminals at the audio module connector on EX30 and EX30CC vehicles, free of charge.

Common questions

Does the seat belt cable recall cover my S60 or V60?

R10029 lists 2011-2018 S60 and V60, 2016-2018 S60 Cross Country and 2015-2018 V60 Cross Country among the affected vehicles, alongside several other Volvo cars. Dealers replace the seat belt anchor cable for both front seats; Volvo customer service can check your VIN.

Why is a seat belt chime a safety recall?

The EX30 notice (R10365) says these vehicles fail to meet FMVSS 208 because the seat belt warning system may not activate the audible chime as intended. Without that reminder, an unbelted occupant may not be alerted, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.

Which seat is affected on the VAH truck recall?

RVXX2510 concerns the passenger seat belt anchor on certain 2019-2026 VAH trucks, which may fail due to inadequate floor reinforcement, failing FMVSS 210. Dealers install a new weld nut and washer free of charge; owner letters were mailed December 16, 2025.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
