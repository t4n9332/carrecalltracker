Ram truck wiper, mirror and defroster recalls: what owners should know

5 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Visibility · model years 2019–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 10, 2024

Visibility is where Ram's recall history repeats itself most clearly, and windshield wipers are the biggest single thread. Recall W25 covers 2019-2020 Ram 1500 and 1500 Classic trucks alongside the Jeep Compass, and recall Z08 covers 2019-2020 Ram 2500 and 3500 trucks plus 3500, 4500 and 5500 Cab Chassis models rated under 10,000 lbs; in both, the wiper arms may loosen. Mirrors are the second thread: recall W70 covers driver side mirror glass detaching on 2020 Ram 1500 and 2500 trucks, and recall 86B covers the heated trailer tow mirror glass on 2020-2023 Ram 1500 Classic. Recall V12 stands alone — 2019 Ram 1500 trucks with a 12-inch touchscreen and base HVAC have no functioning windshield defroster.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall V12 states that affected 2019 Ram 1500 vehicles do not have a functioning windshield defrosting and defogging system.
  • Recall W25 states that the wiper arm or arms may loosen, possibly causing the wipers to not operate properly on 2019-2020 Ram 1500, 1500 Classic and Jeep Compass vehicles.
  • Recall Z08 states that the wiper arms may loosen, possibly causing the wipers to function improperly on 2019-2020 Ram 2500, 3500 and Cab Chassis models.
  • Recall W70 states that the driver side mirror glass can detach from the backing plate and no longer provide a reflective surface on 2020 Ram 1500 and 2500 vehicles.
  • Recall 86B states that the mirror glass may detach from the driver's side heated trailer tow mirror on 2020-2023 Ram 1500 Classic vehicles.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2019 Ram 1500 defroster recall (V12), the 2019-2020 Ram 1500, 1500 Classic and Jeep Compass wiper recall (W25), the 2020 Ram 1500 and 2500 mirror glass recall (W70), the 2019-2020 Ram 2500, 3500 and Cab Chassis wiper recall (Z08) and the 2020-2023 Ram 1500 Classic tow mirror recall (86B), the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. What each does state is that the reduced visibility it describes increases the risk of a crash. No interim instruction is given in any of them.

What the free repair involves

The two wiper recalls are the simplest: dealers tighten the wiper nuts on the W25 and Z08 vehicles, free of charge. For recall W70, dealers replace the driver side mirror glass, and for recall 86B they replace the mirror glass, both free of charge. Recall V12 is different — the notice says dealers will repurchase the affected 2019 Ram 1500 vehicles.

Common questions

Why does the defroster recall end in a buyback rather than a repair?

The notice for recall V12 does not give a reason. It says 2019 Ram 1500 trucks with a 12-inch touchscreen and base-level HVAC have no functioning windshield defrosting and defogging system, and that dealers will repurchase the affected vehicles. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403.

My Ram 2500 was not in the first wiper recall. Why is it covered now?

The notice for recall Z08 states that it is an expansion of recall 20V-208. Z08 covers 2019-2020 Ram 2500 and 3500 trucks and 3500, 4500 and 5500 Cab Chassis models, where the wiper arms may loosen. Dealers tighten the wiper nuts free of charge.

Both mirror recalls sound the same. Are they the same repair?

They are separate recalls. Recall W70 covers the driver side mirror glass on 2020 Ram 1500 and 2500 trucks; recall 86B covers the driver's side heated trailer tow mirror on 2020-2023 Ram 1500 Classic. Each notice says dealers replace the mirror glass free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
