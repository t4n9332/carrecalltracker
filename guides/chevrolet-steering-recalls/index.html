Why are some Chevrolet trucks and cars experiencing steering failures?

3 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Steering · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-03-20

Chevrolet's steering-related recalls span three distinct problems. Heavy-duty 2024 Silverado 2500 and 3500 trucks have a steering gear shaft that can fracture and disconnect from the steering arm. Separately, 2021 model year full-size SUVs built on the same platform - Suburban, Tahoe, and related Cadillac and GMC siblings - can lose power steering assist because of a software glitch, correctable with an update. A third recall covers older 2016-2018 Cadillac CTS, 2017-2018 Cadillac ATS, Camaro, and Corvette models, where the electric power steering assist itself can fail. Across these recalls, the common thread is a loss or reduction of steering assistance rather than a single shared mechanical cause.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering may suddenly require much more effort, especially at low speeds (2021 SUVs and 2016-2018 Cadillac ATS, CTS, Camaro, and Corvette models)
  • A loss of power steering assist can occur at startup or while driving (2021 Suburban, Tahoe, and related SUVs)
  • The notice for the steering gear fracture recall (2024 Silverado and Sierra 2500/3500) describes no warning sign before failure

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three steering recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the 2024 Silverado and Sierra 2500/3500 steering gear fracture, the 2021 SUV power steering software issue, and the 2016-2018 Cadillac ATS, CTS, Camaro, and Corvette electric power steering failure do not tell owners to stop driving. Owners should follow the repair instructions dealers or GM provide for each specific vehicle.

What the free repair involves

Dealers address each steering recall differently: they inspect and replace the steering gear on affected Silverado and Sierra trucks, update the power steering module software - either at a dealer or automatically over-the-air - on the 2021 SUVs, and replace the power steering gear assembly on the affected Cadillac, Camaro, and Corvette models. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Chevrolet vehicles are covered by these steering recalls?

Three separate recalls affect the 2024 Silverado 2500 and 3500 heavy-duty trucks, the 2021 Suburban and Tahoe along with related Cadillac and GMC siblings, and 2016-2018 Cadillac CTS, Cadillac ATS, Camaro, and Corvette models.

Do the notices say to stop driving?

No. None of these three recalls include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices tell owners to schedule the free repair, but they do not direct owners to stop driving in the meantime.

How is the power steering software issue fixed?

For the 2021 SUVs, General Motors updates the power steering module software either during a dealer visit or automatically through an over-the-air update sent directly to the vehicle, at no cost to the owner.
