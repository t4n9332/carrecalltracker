What Chevrolet steering recalls mean for power steering and steering gear

3 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Steering · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 20, 2025

Chevrolet's steering recalls here are three different failures rather than one repeating pattern. The newest covers 2024 Silverado 2500 and 3500 heavy-duty trucks, where the steering gear shaft may fracture and disconnect from the steering arm. A 2021 recall covers full-size SUVs — Tahoe and Suburban alongside GMC Yukon, Yukon XL and Cadillac Escalade ESV — where a software issue may cause a loss of power steering assist at startup or while driving. The third covers cars: 2017-2018 Camaro and Corvette, with 2016-2018 Cadillac CTS and 2017-2018 ATS, where the electric power steering assist may fail. Two of the three describe a loss of power steering assist; the heavy-duty truck recall describes a total loss of steering control.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Increased steering effort, particularly at lower speeds — the 2021 Tahoe, Suburban, Yukon and Escalade power steering software recall.
  • Greater steering effort, especially at low speeds — the Camaro, Corvette, CTS and ATS electric power steering recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2024 Silverado 2500 and 3500 notice says a steering arm that disconnects may result in a total loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash. The 2021 Tahoe and Suburban notice and the Camaro and Corvette notice both say increased steering effort, particularly at lower speeds, increases the risk of a crash. Beyond describing those risks, the notices give owners no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

The remedies differ. For the 2024 Silverado 2500 and 3500, dealers inspect and replace the steering gear if necessary. For the 2021 Tahoe, Suburban and Escalade, the power steering module software is updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update. For the Camaro, Corvette, CTS and ATS, dealers replace the power steering gear assembly. Each notice says the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Does the 2021 SUV power steering recall require a dealer visit?

The notice says the power steering module software will be updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update, free of charge. Owner notification letters for the 2021 Tahoe, Suburban, Yukon, Yukon XL and Escalade ESV were mailed July 13, 2021.

Is my Camaro or Corvette part of the power steering recall?

That recall covers 2017-2018 Camaro and Corvette, along with 2016-2018 Cadillac CTS and 2017-2018 ATS. The notice says the electric power steering assist may fail. Dealers replace the power steering gear assembly free of charge; letters were mailed April 15, 2025.

What do the notices say about driving while I wait for the repair?

None of the three steering notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. They describe the risk — a total loss of steering control on the 2024 heavy-duty trucks, greater steering effort on the SUVs and cars — and give no other instruction.
