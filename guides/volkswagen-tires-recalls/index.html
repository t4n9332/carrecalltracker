Why are Volkswagen Atlas tires and tire pressure monitors being recalled?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Tires · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 31, 2022

Two of these three Volkswagen recalls involve Continental tires that the notices say were cured too long. The March 2021 recall covers 2018-2019 Atlas vehicles equipped with Continental tires; the November 2020 recall covers 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport and 2021 Atlas vehicles fitted with Continental Cross Contact LX Sport tires in size 255/50 R 20 105 T. Both notices describe a sidewall break with sudden air loss, or tread and belt separation. The third recall is a different problem: a tire pressure monitoring system that may not detect air loss in adequate time when all four tires deflate at once, covering a much wider 2019-2021 list including Jetta, Golf, GTI, Golf R, Tiguan, Atlas, Audi A3 and Audi Q3.

Warning signs to watch for

  • In the 2022 tire pressure monitoring recall, the notice says the system may not detect an air pressure loss in adequate time when deflating occurs among all four tires simultaneously.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2020 Continental Cross Contact LX Sport recall, the 2021 Atlas Continental tire recall and the 2022 tire pressure monitoring software recall each state that owners are not told to stop driving. The notices set out no interim step for owners to take before the dealer work is done. What they do state is the risk each defect creates, the remedy performed, and a Volkswagen customer service number for questions.

What the free repair involves

For the 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport and 2021 Atlas, dealers inspect the DOT date code and mold number on the tires to see if they are affected and replace them as necessary. For 2018-2019 Atlas vehicles, dealers inspect the tires and replace them as necessary. For the monitoring recall, dealers update the TPMS software. All three notices say this is free of charge.

Common questions

How do I find out whether my Atlas has the recalled Continental tires?

The 2020 notice says dealers inspect the DOT date code and mold number on the tires of 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport and 2021 Atlas vehicles to see if they are affected, replacing them as necessary, free of charge.

What is wrong with the tire pressure monitoring system?

The notice says the system may not detect an air pressure loss in adequate time when all four tires deflate simultaneously, so the vehicles fail Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 138. Dealers update the TPMS software free of charge.

Will Volkswagen pay for replacement tires?

Both tire notices say dealers inspect and replace the tires as necessary free of charge: the recall covering 2018-2019 Atlas vehicles, and the recall covering 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport and 2021 Atlas with Continental Cross Contact LX Sport tires.
