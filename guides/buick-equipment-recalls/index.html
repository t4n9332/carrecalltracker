Buick Equipment Recalls Address Emergency Jacks and Missing Owner Information

3 NHTSA recalls · Buick · Equipment · model years 2020–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 26, 2026

These Buick equipment notices concern an emergency jack and missing information owners use to operate their vehicles. Certain 2020–2022 Encore GX vehicles have a jack that can fracture if it is not positioned as directed in the owner's manual, allowing the vehicle to collapse. Certain 2024 Envision vehicles lack the tire and loading information label, creating a risk of improper tire inflation or overloading. The February 2026 electronic owner's manual notice names certain 2026 Enclave and Envision vehicles. Across the group, missing vehicle information appears in two notices, while the Encore GX recall concerns the physical lifting equipment supplied with the vehicle.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these Buick equipment notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The June 2021 emergency jack notice for certain 2020–2022 Encore GX vehicles does not tell owners to stop driving. The September 2024 notice about the missing tire size, pressure and loading information label on certain 2024 Envision vehicles does not tell owners to stop driving. The February 2026 electronic owner's manual notice naming certain 2026 Enclave and Envision vehicles also does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the June 2021 Encore GX recall, dealers replace the emergency jack. For the September 2024 Envision recall, dealers install the missing tire and loading information label. For the February 2026 electronic manual recall, dealers reset the radio to facilitate automatic download of the manual. Each notice specifies a free remedy.

Common questions

What does the Encore GX notice say causes the jack to fracture?

The June 2021 notice says the emergency jack can fracture while in use if it is not positioned as directed in the vehicle owner's manual. It covers certain 2020–2022 Encore GX vehicles and provides a free replacement jack.

What information is missing from the recalled Envision label?

The September 2024 recall concerns certain 2024 Envision vehicles missing the label that states tire size, tire pressure and loading information. The notice links that missing information to improper inflation or overloading. Dealers install the label free of charge.

How is the missing electronic owner's manual restored?

The February 2026 notice names certain 2026 Buick Enclave and Envision vehicles. Dealers reset the vehicle radio, which facilitates automatic download of the electronic owner's manual. The notice states that this remedy is free of charge.
