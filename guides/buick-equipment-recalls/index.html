Buick Equipment Recalls Cover Emergency Jacks and Missing Information

3 NHTSA recalls · Buick · Equipment · model years 2020–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 26, 2026

These Buick equipment recalls concern an emergency jack and missing owner information, with no Buick model repeated across the three notices. The June 2021 recall covers certain 2020-2022 Encore GX vehicles whose emergency jack can fracture if it is not positioned as directed in the owner's manual. The September 2024 recall concerns certain 2024 Envision vehicles missing the label specifying tire size, tire pressure, and loading information. The February 2026 notice identifies certain 2026 Enclave and Envision vehicles in its problem description and concerns an electronic owner's manual that may be unavailable. The recurring information issue involves access to operating guidance, while the Encore GX recall concerns the jack itself.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the three listed Buick equipment notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. The June 10, 2021 emergency-jack recall for certain Encore GX vehicles does not tell owners to stop driving. The September 25, 2024 missing tire-information-label recall for certain Envision vehicles also does not tell owners to stop driving. The February 26, 2026 electronic owner's manual recall likewise does not tell owners to stop driving. These are the driving instructions stated in the supplied notices.

What the free repair involves

For the June 2021 Encore GX recall, dealers replace the emergency jack free of charge. For the September 2024 Envision recall, dealers install the missing tire and loading information label free of charge. For the February 2026 electronic manual recall, dealers reset the radio to facilitate automatic download of the manual, free of charge.

Common questions

Which Buick Encore GX vehicles have the emergency-jack recall?

The June 10, 2021 notice covers certain 2020-2022 Buick Encore GX vehicles. It says the emergency jack can fracture if it is not positioned as directed in the owner's manual. Dealers replace the jack free of charge.

What information is missing from the recalled Envision label?

The September 25, 2024 notice concerns certain 2024 Buick Envision vehicles missing a label stating tire size, tire pressure, and loading information. It identifies risks from improper tire inflation or overloading. Dealers install the label free of charge.

How is the missing electronic owner's manual restored?

The February 26, 2026 notice says dealers reset the vehicle radio, which facilitates automatic download of the electronic owner's manual. That remedy is free of charge. The problem description names certain 2026 Buick Enclave and Envision vehicles.
