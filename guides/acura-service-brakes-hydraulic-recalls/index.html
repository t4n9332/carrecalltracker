Acura brake recalls: pedal, master cylinder and ABS problems explained

6 NHTSA recalls · Acura · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-11

These six recalls hit different parts of the brake system rather than repeating one defect. The MDX appears most often: 2017-2019 MDX and 2017-2018 MDX Sport Hybrid for rear brake caliper pistons with insufficient coating in November 2018, the 2020 MDX for a tie rod fastener that can let the brake master cylinder separate from the booster in June 2023, and 2023-2025 MDX alongside 2021-2025 TLX for a brake pedal pivot pin that was not secured properly in June 2025. The rest stand alone: 2023 Integra and RDX for a leaking VSA modulator ball valve, the 2024 ZDX for unexpected ABS activation, and 2016-2020 ILX for contaminated brake fluid.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Reduced braking performance - named in the November 2018 recall covering 2017-2019 Acura MDX and 2017-2018 MDX Sport Hybrid.
  • Unintended vehicle movement when the brake hold feature is engaged, or an unexpected increase in brake pedal travel - named in the June 2023 recall of 2023 Acura Integra and RDX.
  • Anti-lock brakes activating unexpectedly and releasing brake pressure - named in the August 2024 recall of the 2024 Acura ZDX All-Wheel Drive.
  • A brake pedal that shifts out of position - named in the June 2025 recall of 2021-2025 TLX and 2023-2025 MDX.
  • A longer distance required to stop - named in the December 2025 recall of 2016-2020 Acura ILX.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park outside. That includes the June 2023 recall of the 2020 Acura MDX, where the notice says brake master cylinder separation can cause a loss of brake function, and the August 2024 recall of the 2024 ZDX, where the notice says a release of brake pressure can cause a loss of braking ability. The remaining four notices describe reduced braking, longer stopping distances or pedal problems, but none gives owners any instruction about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is free. Dealers bleed the brakes in the November 2018 recall, replace the VSA modulator for 2023 Integra and RDX, inspect and repair the brake booster assembly for the 2020 MDX, inspect and replace the brake pedal assembly in the June 2025 recall, and replace the brake master cylinder for 2016-2020 ILX. The 2024 ZDX software update can come over the air or from a dealer.

Common questions

Is the 2024 Acura ZDX brake fix an over-the-air update?

The August 2024 notice says the electronic brake control module software will be updated through an over-the-air update or by a dealer, free of charge. It covers 2024 ZDX All-Wheel Drive vehicles, and owner letters were mailed September 26, 2024.

My MDX shows up on more than one brake recall list - is that possible?

Different MDX years sit on different lists: 2017-2019 MDX in the November 2018 caliper recall, the 2020 MDX in the June 2023 master cylinder recall, and 2023-2025 MDX in the June 2025 brake pedal recall. Check your VIN against each.

What does the Acura ILX brake fluid recall actually say?

The December 2025 recall of 2016-2020 Acura ILX says contaminated brake fluid may deform a seal in the brake master cylinder, causing an internal leak. Dealers replace the master cylinder free of charge, and owner letters were mailed May 4, 2026.
