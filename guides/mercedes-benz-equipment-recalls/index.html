Why are Mercedes-Benz Sprinter manuals and labels wrong or missing?

7 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Equipment · model years 2018–2019 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2021-04-05

Most Mercedes-Benz recalls in this group are paperwork and labeling problems on Sprinter vans rather than mechanical failures. Six of the seven recalls affect 2018-2019 Sprinter models: the owner's manual describes the Auto-P parking function incorrectly, cargo lashing rails may not be properly installed, the spare tire's maximum-speed label may show only kilometers per hour or be missing entirely, one van's VIN doesn't match its dashboard and certification labels, and a separate recall covers an owner's manual that was inaccurate or incomplete. The seventh recall is unrelated to Sprinter: a 2019 GLC 350e's passenger sun-visor airbag warning label may be printed in a foreign language.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Spare tire's speed label may be printed only in kilometers per hour, or missing altogether
  • Cargo tie-down (lashing) rails on the side walls may not be securely attached
  • VIN on the dashboard, certification label, and body-in-white may not match each other
  • Airbag warning label on the passenger sun visor may be in a foreign language
  • The notices describe no driving symptom for the Auto-P or owner's-manual defects — these are documentation errors, not something a driver would feel while driving

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the Auto-P manual error, DVUSA says relying on the manual's description could let the van roll away or move on its own. For the VIN mismatch, Mercedes-Benz says not receiving recall notices raises the risk of injury, a crash, or fire. For the remaining recalls — the tie-down rails, both spare-tire label issues, the incomplete owner's manual, and the GLC airbag label — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across this group, dealers correct or reprint the affected documents and labels: rewriting the Auto-P section of the manual, reattaching the cargo lashing rails, replacing or adding the spare-tire speed label in miles per hour, correcting the VIN on the dashboard and certification label, replacing the printed and digital owner's manual, and replacing the airbag warning label. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Do I need to bring my Sprinter in if only the manual is wrong?

Yes — DVUSA still schedules a free dealer visit to replace the printed manual and update the digital version, even though the defect is informational rather than mechanical, per the notice.

Why does one recall list a VIN mismatch on a single Sprinter?

DVUSA found one 2019 Sprinter's dashboard, certification label, and body-in-white VINs didn't match, which the notice says could keep the owner from learning about other open recalls.

Is the GLC airbag label recall related to the Sprinter recalls?

No — it's a separate 2019 GLC 350e issue in which the passenger sun-visor airbag warning label may be printed in a foreign language, unrelated to any Sprinter defect in this group.
