Mercedes-Benz recalls over wrong labels and manuals, mostly in Sprinter vans

7 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Equipment · model years 2018–2019 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 5, 2021

Six of these seven Mercedes-Benz equipment recalls are Sprinter vans, and most involve labels and printed information rather than mechanical parts. Two 2020 campaigns cover 2018-2019 Sprinters whose spare tire speed label was either not affixed at all or showed the maximum speed as 55 kilometers per hour rather than 34 mph. Two more cover 2019 Sprinter operator's manuals: one that may not correctly describe how the Auto-P automatic parking function works, and one delivered inaccurate or incomplete in both printed and digital form. A 2019 campaign covers 2019 Sprinter lashing rails that may not have been properly installed and could detach. One single 2019 Sprinter was recalled because its dashboard, certification label and body-in-white VINs do not match. The outlier is the 2019 GLC range, whose passenger side sun visor may carry air bag warning labels written in a foreign language.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none carries a park-outside instruction. The notices describe consequences instead: relying on the Auto-P description in the 2019 Sprinter manual may result in vehicle rollaway or movement; detached lashing rails may leave cargo unsecured; a missing or metric-only spare tire label may lead to operating the vehicle above the speed the spare tire can withstand; mismatched VINs may keep the owner from receiving open recall information; and a foreign-language air bag label may leave an occupant unaware of the risks to children sitting in the front seat.

What the free repair involves

Most remedies replace labels or printed information. Dealers provide the correct Auto-P description for the Operator's Manual, replace the printed operator's manual with the latest version and update the digital version's software, attach or replace the spare tire speed limitation label, correctly affix the lashing rails to the side walls, replace the VIN on the dash board and certification label, and inspect and replace GLC air bag warning labels as necessary. Each notice states this is free of charge.

Common questions

Is a wrong owner's manual really a safety recall?

It is treated as one here. Two campaigns cover 2019 Sprinters: one where the Operator's Manual may not contain correct information about the Auto-P function, and one where the printed and digital manuals may be inaccurate or incomplete. Dealers supply corrected versions free of charge.

What happens with the spare tire label on my Sprinter?

Two 2020 recalls cover 2018-2019 Sprinters. On some the spare tire speed label may not have been affixed to the spare tire; on others it showed 55 kilometers per hour instead of 34 mph. Dealers attach or replace the label with the correct speed in miles per hour, free of charge.

How do I know if my GLC has the foreign-language airbag label?

The 2019 recall covers the listed 2019 GLC and AMG GLC models whose passenger side sun visor may have incorrect air bag warning labels written in a foreign language. Dealers inspect the air bag warnings and replace them as necessary, free of charge. A VIN check confirms inclusion.
