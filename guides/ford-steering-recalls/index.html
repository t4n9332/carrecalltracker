Which Ford steering recalls involve loose bolts or lost steering control?

13 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 19, 2025

Loose or missing fasteners run through most of Ford's steering recalls: the steering wheel retaining bolt on 2014-2018 Fusion and MKZ, the steering wheel attachment bolt on 2022 F-53 and F-59, the intermediate shaft bolt on 2020-2022 F-59, and the steering column upper shaft bolts on 2023 Super Duty F-250, F-350 and F-450. Commercial vans form a second cluster: 2021-2022 E-350 and E-450 with dual rear wheels appear in two recalls over a power steering line connection that can lose fluid. The rest stand alone — a Bronco Raptor and Wildtrak steering gear, 2022-2023 Mustang torque sensor calibration in two recalls, 2025 F-650 and F-750 tie rods, a Super Duty clock spring, and an Icon stabilizer kit sold for F-250 and F-350 4x4 trucks.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2022 Bronco Raptor and 2023 Bronco Wildtrak (23S09): increased steering effort, or the steering wheel locking up.
  • 2022-2023 Mustang (24S44 and 25S11): the steering wheel turning side to side against the driver's intentions.
  • 2021-2022 E-350 and E-450 with dual rear wheels: a sudden loss of power steering fluid, resulting in a loss of power steering and power brake assist.
  • 2022 F-53 and F-59: the steering wheel attachment bolt may loosen and separate from the column, resulting in a loose or detached steering wheel.
  • Icon 05-UP FSD 2.5 Centerline Stabilizer Kits fitted to F-250 and F-350 4x4 trucks: the steering may lock up.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One recall here carries a stop-driving instruction: the 2022 Bronco Raptor and 2023 Bronco Wildtrak steering gear recall, 23S09, where owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until the remedy is performed. For every other recall in this group, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2024 E-350 and E-450 notice adds that these vehicles were previously recalled under 22V-089 and that owners need the new remedy completed even if the earlier repair was done.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the Bronco steering gear; fit a longer steering wheel bolt on Fusion and MKZ; tighten or replace the F-59 intermediate shaft bolt and the Super Duty column bolts; replace E-350 and E-450 power steering and hydroboost lines; replace F-650 and F-750 tie rod assemblies and the Super Duty clock spring; and update Mustang power steering control module software. Icon supplies a replacement shock assembly. All free of charge.

Common questions

Why do 2022-2023 Mustangs appear in two steering recalls?

The 2025 notice, 25S11, says it covers vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall 24V-493. Both describe a secondary steering torque sensor that may not be calibrated properly, and in both dealers update the power steering control module software free of charge.

My E-350 was already repaired under the 2022 recall — is anything left?

The 2024 notice says these vehicles were previously recalled under 22V-089 and that owners need the new remedy completed even if their vehicle was remedied under the prior recall. Dealers replace the power steering pressure line and the hydroboost jumper line, free of charge.

Does the Bronco steering gear recall cover every Bronco?

No — the notice covers certain 2023 Bronco Wildtrak and 2022 Bronco Raptor vehicles. It describes internal damage to the steering gear that may require increased steering effort or cause lock-up, and owners are advised not to drive until the gear is replaced.
