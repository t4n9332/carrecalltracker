Why does Ford keep recalling vehicles for steering bolts and sensors?

13 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-09-19

These Ford steering recalls fall into a few repeating patterns. Bolts and fasteners that aren't properly tightened turn up repeatedly — on the Fusion/MKZ steering wheel, the F-59 intermediate shaft, the F-53/F-59 steering wheel, and the Super Duty steering column. The 2021-2022 E-350/E-450 was recalled twice for the same power steering fluid loss defect after the first fix didn't fully resolve it. The 2022-2023 Mustang was also recalled twice for a steering wheel that turns on its own, the second time after an earlier repair failed. Only the 2022-2023 Bronco carries a stop-driving order, for a steering gear that can lock up.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering wheel turning side to side on its own, without driver input (Mustang)
  • Increased steering effort or the steering wheel locking up (Bronco)
  • Steering wheel feels loose or could detach (Fusion, MKZ, F-53/F-59)
  • Steering feels heavier, or brakes feel harder to press, from lost power assist (E-350/E-450)
  • Bolt and weld failures, like steering column bolts, the clock spring, or tie rods, are described with no noticeable warning before failure

Is it safe to keep driving?

One recall — the 2022-2023 Bronco (Wildtrak and Raptor) steering gear lock-up — tells owners not to drive until it's repaired. None of these recalls carry a park-outside instruction. For the other recalls in this group, including both Mustang steering-turn recalls, both E-350/E-450 power steering fluid-loss recalls, the Fusion/MKZ steering wheel bolt, the F-59 and F-53/F-59 bolt issues, the Super Duty column bolts and clock spring, the Icon stabilizer kit, and the F-650 tie rods, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the affected hardware free of charge: steering gears, steering wheel or column bolts, power steering pressure lines, tie rod assemblies, and the steering wheel clock spring. Two recalls are fixed with a software update to the power steering control module instead of a parts replacement. The Icon stabilizer kit recall replaces the shock assembly.

Common questions

Why was my Mustang recalled twice for the same steering problem?

Ford's first fix (recall 24V-493) for the steering wheel turning on its own didn't work for some vehicles, so a second recall in 2025 tells dealers to update the power steering control module software again on those vehicles.

Does a Ford steering recall always mean I should stop driving?

No. Only the 2022-2023 Bronco steering gear lock-up recall tells owners to stop driving until repaired. Every other steering recall in this group, including both Mustang and E-350/E-450 recalls, does not include a stop-driving instruction.

Are any of these suspension recalls for aftermarket parts?

Yes, two. Tenneco/DRiV recalled its MOOG ball joint sold for aftermarket installation on 2015-2018 Ford F-150 trucks, and MORryde recalled U-bolts on 2018 Ford E-450 bus chassis conversions — both aftermarket parts, not original Ford components.
