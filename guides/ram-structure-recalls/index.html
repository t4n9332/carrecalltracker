What structural and body recalls have hit Ram trucks?

4 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Structure · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-06-06

These four Ram structural recalls cover different parts of the truck bed and body rather than one repeating defect. Two involve bed steps: a 2019 Mopar accessory rear step kit for the 1500 that can let the rear control arms contact the fuel tank in a rear-end crash, and a separate 2019-2020 accessory bed step on 2500 and 3500 trucks that can fail unexpectedly under side loading. A 2019-2022 recall covers misaligned tailgate strikers on 1500, 2500 and 3500 pickups that can let the tailgate open while driving. Separately, 2021-2024 ProMaster vans are missing a required side door safety beam and carry an incorrect seating-capacity number on the tire placard.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Accessory bed step unexpectedly fails while stepping on it (2019-2020 2500/3500 step recall)
  • Fuel smell or leak near the rear of a 2019 Ram 1500 after a rear-end impact (bed step kit recall)
  • Tailgate does not latch securely or opens unexpectedly while driving (2019-2022 recall)
  • No owner-noticeable warning sign for the ProMaster missing door beam or tire placard error - it is found on inspection

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls tell owners to stop driving. That covers the 2019 Ram 1500 bed step kit linked to a possible fuel leak in a rear crash, the 2019-2020 2500/3500 accessory bed step that can fail under side loading, the 2019-2022 tailgate recall, and the 2021-2024 ProMaster missing door beam and tire placard recall. The notices do not give any interim driving instruction for any of the four campaigns in this group.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the bed step or its support brace, install a new door beam and overlay label on ProMaster vans, and adjust the tailgate striker alignment, free of charge. The 2019 Ram 1500 step-kit recall involves replacing the bed step itself. No repair in this group requires a software update.

Common questions

Is the bed step recall the same for all Ram trucks?

No. A 2019 Ram 1500 recall covers a Mopar rear step kit linked to a fuel tank risk in a rear crash. A separate 2019-2020 recall covers an accessory bed step on 2500 and 3500 trucks that can fail under side loading.

What is wrong with the tailgate on my Ram pickup?

A 2019-2022 recall found that misaligned tailgate strikers on 1500, 2500 and 3500 trucks can keep the tailgate from latching properly, letting it open while driving and potentially spill unsecured cargo.

What did NHTSA find wrong with the Ram ProMaster?

2021-2024 ProMaster vans were built missing a required side door safety beam, and their tire placard lists incorrect seating capacity information - both are compliance issues dealers fix free of charge.
