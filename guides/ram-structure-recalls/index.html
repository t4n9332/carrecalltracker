Ram body recalls: bed steps, tailgates and a missing door beam

4 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Structure · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 6, 2024

Two of these four recalls are about bed steps. One covers Mopar Rear Step Kits, part number 82215289AB, designed for 2019 Ram 1500 trucks, where a rear impact crash may cause the rear control arms to contact the fuel tank; the other covers the accessory bed step on 2019-2020 Ram 2500 and 3500 pickups, which may fail in certain side loading conditions. The widest by model spread is the tailgate recall on 2019-2022 Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 pickups, where misaligned tailgate strikers may keep the tailgate from latching properly. The odd one out is the 2021-2024 Ram ProMaster, missing a side door beam and carrying incorrect seating capacity information on the tire placard.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A tailgate that may not latch properly and could open while driving — the 2019-2022 Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 tailgate striker recall.
  • A bed step that may unexpectedly fail while in use under certain side loading conditions — the 2019-2020 Ram 2500 and 3500 accessory bed step recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. The Mopar Rear Step Kit notice for 2019 Ram 1500 says that in a rear impact crash the rear control arms may contact the fuel tank, and that a fuel leak increases the risk of fire. The ProMaster notice describes inadequate occupant protection in a crash and an improperly loaded vehicle. The tailgate notice describes loss of unsecured cargo creating a road hazard. Beyond those risks, the notices say nothing about continuing to use the vehicle.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the bed step on 2019 Ram 1500 trucks fitted with the Mopar Rear Step Kit, and replace the bed step support brace on 2019-2020 Ram 2500 and 3500 pickups. On the 2021-2024 ProMaster they install a door beam and apply an overlay label. For the tailgate recall they inspect the striker alignment to the box latch and adjust it if necessary. All free of charge.

Common questions

Is my Ram bed step covered by one recall or two?

Two separate recalls name bed steps. One covers Mopar Rear Step Kits sold for 2019 Ram 1500 trucks; the other covers the accessory bed step on 2019-2020 Ram 2500 and 3500 pickups. The remedies differ, so check your VIN against each one.

What does the missing door beam mean for my ProMaster?

The notice says 2021-2024 Ram ProMaster vans are missing a side door beam, which may not provide adequate occupant protection in a crash, and that the tire placard shows incorrect seating capacity. Dealers install a door beam and apply an overlay label, free of charge.

Will the dealer replace my tailgate under the striker recall?

The notice does not describe replacing the tailgate. It says dealers inspect the alignment of the tailgate striker to the box latch on 2019-2022 Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 pickups and adjust it if necessary, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
