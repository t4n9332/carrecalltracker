Why do Cadillac rearview cameras stop showing an image?

3 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Back Over Prevention · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 12, 2026

Three recalls cover Cadillac rearview cameras, and all three end in the same place: no usable image behind the vehicle. Two of them involve the optional Surround Vision feature. On the 2020-2021 XT5 and XT6, the rearview camera coaxial cable connectors may have been crimped improperly, causing the camera to fail or function intermittently. On the 2019-2020 XT4, the video processing module may lose communication with the rearview camera, causing a loss of the image. The third covers the 2024 Lyriq, where the rearview camera screen may turn gray with no camera image. The XT5 and XT6 recall also includes GMC Acadia vehicles of the same model years, and each notice cites reduced rear visibility.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The rearview camera fails or functions intermittently — 2020-2021 Cadillac XT5 and XT6 Surround Vision coaxial cable recall.
  • The rearview camera image does not display — 2019-2020 Cadillac XT4 video processing module recall.
  • The rearview camera screen turns gray with no camera image — 2024 Cadillac Lyriq recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. The 2020-2021 XT5 and XT6 notice, the 2019-2020 XT4 notice and the 2024 Lyriq notice each say the same thing about the risk: an inoperative rearview camera can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash. Past directing owners to the free remedy, the notices set out no other instruction about how or whether to drive the vehicle.

What the free repair involves

Each remedy is free of charge. Dealers replace the coaxial cables on the 2020-2021 XT5 and XT6. On the 2019-2020 XT4, dealers update the video processing module software or replace the module, as necessary. On the 2024 Lyriq, the vehicle cockpit unit software is updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update.

Common questions

Which Cadillacs have the rearview camera recalls?

The 2019-2020 XT4 and the 2020-2021 XT5 and XT6, both equipped with the optional Surround Vision feature, plus the 2024 Lyriq. The XT5 and XT6 recall also covers GMC Acadia vehicles from the same model years.

What does the repair involve for each one?

It depends on the recall: dealers replace the coaxial cables on the XT5, XT6 and Acadia; update or replace the video processing module on the XT4; and update the Lyriq cockpit unit software at a dealer or over the air. All free of charge.

Do the notices say anything about driving without the camera?

None of the three tells owners to stop driving. Each states that an inoperative rearview camera can reduce the driver's rear visibility and increase the risk of a crash, and each directs owners to the free remedy.
