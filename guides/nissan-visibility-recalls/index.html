Why do Nissan windows and windshields keep showing up in recalls?

4 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Visibility · model years 2020–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-11-21

Glass is the common thread across these four Nissan recalls, but it shows up in two different ways. Two involve glass coming loose: 2020 Altima, Maxima and Leaf back window glass that may not remain properly secured (PC752), and 2024 Murano left and right quarter glass that may detach, where the notice says the incorrect primer may have been used on the quarter glass (PD102). The other two are windshield air bubbles on the Sentra in back-to-back model years — the 2024 Sentra, where bubbles on the front lower driver's side can obscure the VIN (PMA38), and the 2025 Sentra, where visible bubbles can obscure the driver's visibility (PMA58). Sentra windshields dominate the most recent entries.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Air bubbles on the front lower driver's side of the windshield, where the VIN sits (2024 Sentra, PMA38).
  • Visible air bubbles in the windshield glass (2025 Sentra, PMA58).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2020 Altima, Maxima and Leaf notice (PC752) and the 2024 Murano notice (PD102) each say only that glass separating or detaching from the vehicle increases the risk of a crash. The 2024 Sentra notice (PMA38) says an owner who cannot see the VIN clearly may not have the recall remedy performed, which could increase the risk of injury, fire or a crash. The 2025 Sentra notice (PMA58) says reduced visibility while driving increases crash risk. No further interim instruction appears.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is glass work, free of charge. For the 2020 Altima, Maxima and Leaf (PC752), dealers replace the back window glass. For the 2024 Murano (PD102), dealers replace both side quarter glass panels. For the 2024 Sentra (PMA38) and the 2025 Sentra (PMA58), dealers inspect the windshield and replace it as necessary.

Common questions

My 2024 Sentra windshield has bubbles but I can see fine. Does the recall still apply?

PMA38 covers 2024 Sentras whose windshield may have air bubbles on the front lower driver's side that can obscure the VIN. The notice says dealers will inspect the windshield and replace it as necessary, free of charge.

Does the Murano recall cover the windshield as well?

PD102 covers the left and right quarter glass on 2024 Muranos, which may detach; the notice says the incorrect primer may have been used on that glass. Dealers replace both side quarter glass panels, free of charge.

What does the notice actually say about the 2020 Altima, Maxima and Leaf back window?

PC752 says the rear window glass may not remain properly secured to the vehicle, and that glass separating from the vehicle can increase the risk of a crash. Dealers will replace the back window glass, free of charge.
