Nissan Glass Recalls Cover Loose Windows and Windshield Air Bubbles

4 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Visibility · model years 2020–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 21, 2025

Nissan's visibility recalls show two repeated problems: glass that may detach and windshield bubbles that obscure something the owner needs to see. PC752 covers rear window glass that may not remain secured on certain 2020 Altima, Leaf and Maxima vehicles. PD102 concerns certain 2024 Murano vehicles whose quarter glass may detach because incorrect primer may have been used. Sentra appears in both windshield notices, with different consequences: PMA38 covers bubbles obscuring the VIN on certain 2024 vehicles, while PMA58 covers visible bubbles obscuring the driver's visibility on certain 2025 vehicles. These notices identify different glass locations and risks, so the Sentra windshield concerns should not be confused with the detachment defects.

Warning signs to watch for

  • PMA38, 2024 Sentra: air bubbles on the windshield's front lower driver's side may obscure the VIN.
  • PMA58, 2025 Sentra: visible windshield air bubbles may obscure the driver's visibility.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for PC752, covering rear window glass on the 2020 Altima, Leaf and Maxima; PD102, covering 2024 Murano quarter glass; PMA38, covering the 2024 Sentra windshield; and PMA58, covering the 2025 Sentra windshield, do not tell owners to stop driving. That absence is the extent of their guidance about continuing to drive; it does not establish that an affected vehicle is safe to drive while awaiting its specified glass remedy.

What the free repair involves

For PC752, dealers replace the back window glass. For PD102, dealers replace both side quarter glass panels. For PMA38 and PMA58, dealers inspect the windshield and replace it as necessary. Each of these notices explicitly states that its specified glass remedy is free of charge.

Common questions

What is different about the two Nissan Sentra windshield recalls?

PMA38 concerns certain 2024 Sentra windshields with bubbles that can obscure the VIN. PMA58 concerns certain 2025 Sentra windshields with visible bubbles that can obscure driving visibility. Both notices specify windshield inspection and replacement as necessary, free of charge.

Which Nissan windows may detach under these recalls?

PC752 covers rear window glass on certain 2020 Altima, Leaf and Maxima vehicles. PD102 covers the left and right quarter glass on certain 2024 Murano vehicles. Their remedies are back window replacement and replacement of both side quarter glass panels, respectively.

Do these Nissan notices require owners to stop driving?

The notices for PC752, PD102, PMA38 and PMA58 do not tell owners to stop driving or park outside. They describe glass detachment, an obscured VIN or reduced driving visibility and specify the corresponding dealer remedies.
