Why Do Nissan Windshields And Windows Keep Getting Recalled?

4 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Visibility · model years 2020–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-11-21

Nissan's visibility recalls split into a glass-detachment problem and a windshield-bubble problem. In 2020, the Altima, Leaf and Maxima were recalled because rear window glass may not stay properly secured, and in 2024 the Murano was recalled because incorrect primer let both quarter glass panels detach. Separately, the Sentra windshield has had visible air bubbles from manufacturing defects in two different model years: a 2024 recall because bubbles can obscure the Vehicle Identification Number, and a 2025 recall, covering the newer model year, because bubbles can obscure the driver's own view of the road.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Visible air bubbles in the windshield glass (2024 and 2025 Sentra recalls)
  • A Vehicle Identification Number that is hard to read through the windshield (2024 Sentra recall)
  • The 2020 Altima, Leaf and Maxima rear window recall and the 2024 Murano quarter glass recall describe no warning sign owners would notice

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four visibility recalls — the 2020 Altima, Leaf and Maxima rear window recall, the 2024 Murano quarter glass recall, and the 2024 and 2025 Sentra windshield air-bubble recalls — carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. All four notices describe the same posture: they do not tell owners to stop driving, and none set out an interim step to take before the dealer inspects or replaces the affected glass panel.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the affected glass free of charge: the rear window on the 2020 Altima, Leaf and Maxima recall, the left and right quarter glass panels on the 2024 Murano recall, and the windshield on both Sentra air-bubble recalls, which are inspected first and replaced as necessary.

Common questions

Why do 2024 and 2025 Nissan Sentra windshields have air bubbles?

Both model years had a windshield glass manufacturing defect that produces visible air bubbles. The 2024 recall was issued because the bubbles can obscure the VIN; the 2025 recall was issued because the bubbles can obscure the driver's view.

Which Nissan models had rear window or quarter glass detach recalls?

The 2020 Altima, Leaf and Maxima were recalled because the rear window glass may not stay properly secured, and the 2024 Murano was recalled because incorrect primer let both quarter glass panels detach from the vehicle.

Can I keep driving a Nissan with one of these visibility recalls?

The notices for all four visibility recalls do not tell owners to stop driving. Contact your Nissan dealer to schedule the free glass inspection or replacement described in your recall notice.
