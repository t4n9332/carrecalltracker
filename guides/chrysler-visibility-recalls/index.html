Chrysler windshield and wiper recalls: what's the visibility risk?

3 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · Visibility · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-03-28

Three Chrysler visibility recalls involve two different problems. Two of them cover windshields that may not have been properly bonded during manufacturing, allowing the windshield to detach in a crash — one affects 2020-2021 Dodge Challenger, Charger and Chrysler 300, the other affects 2024 Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager. Both cite the same regulation, Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 212 on windshield mounting. The third recall is unrelated: it covers 2022 Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager vehicles whose windshield wiper arm nuts may be improperly tightened, keeping the wipers from working properly. Pacifica and Voyager together appear in two of the three recalls, in different model years than the windshield-bonding Challenger/Charger/300 recall.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Windshield may detach from the vehicle in a crash (both windshield-bonding recalls)
  • Windshield wipers may not function properly due to loose arm nuts (wiper recall)
  • The notices describe no warning sign before a crash reveals the windshield bonding defect

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls tells owners to stop driving or park outside. The two windshield-bonding recalls say a windshield that isn't properly bonded can detach from the vehicle during a crash, increasing the risk of injury to occupants. The wiper arm recall says improperly tightened nuts can keep the wipers from working properly, which can reduce the driver's visibility in certain weather and increase the risk of a crash. None of the notices otherwise limit driving.

What the free repair involves

For both windshield recalls, dealers remove and replace the windshield or its urethane sealant so it is properly bonded, free of charge. For the wiper recall, dealers tighten the wiper arm nuts, also free of charge. Owners in all three cases are notified by mail with an FCA US LLC customer service number.

Common questions

Are the two Chrysler windshield recalls related?

They describe the same defect — a windshield not properly bonded so it can detach in a crash — but they're separate recalls for different vehicles: 2020-2021 Challenger, Charger and 300 in one, and 2024 Pacifica and Voyager in the other.

Could loose wiper nuts on my Pacifica cause the wipers to fall off?

The notice says the wiper arm nuts may be improperly tightened and can cause the wipers to not function properly; it does not describe the wipers detaching or falling off the vehicle.

Does a windshield bonding defect affect the wipers too?

No, the windshield-bonding recalls and the 2022 wiper arm nut recall are separate issues affecting different components, even though Pacifica and Voyager overlap between the wiper recall and one windshield recall.
