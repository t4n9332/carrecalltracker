Chrysler windshield and wiper recalls: what do they cover?

3 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · Visibility · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-03-28

Two of the three recalls here are windshield bonding problems, and both are Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 212 compliance issues. One covers 2020-2021 Dodge Challenger, Dodge Charger and Chrysler 300 vehicles, the other 2024 Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager vehicles; in each the windshield may not be properly bonded and can detach during a crash. The third recall is different in kind: on 2022 Pacifica and Voyager vehicles the windshield wiper arm nuts may be improperly tightened, which can cause the wipers to not function properly. Pacifica and Voyager therefore appear twice in this group, in separate model years, while the 300, Challenger and Charger appear only in the windshield bonding recall.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Wipers that do not function properly (2022 Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager wiper arm nut recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The two windshield notices describe the risk in a crash: a windshield that is not properly bonded may detach, increasing the risk of injury. The 2022 wiper notice says improperly functioning wipers can reduce the driver's visibility in certain weather conditions, increasing the risk of a crash. None of the three notices gives owners any interim instruction to follow before the dealer repair.

What the free repair involves

Each remedy is done by a dealer free of charge, and none is a software update or over-the-air fix. For 2020-2021 Challenger, Charger and 300 vehicles, dealers remove and replace the front windshield urethane sealant. For 2024 Pacifica and Voyager vehicles, dealers replace the windshield. For 2022 Pacifica and Voyager vehicles, dealers tighten the wiper arm nuts.

Common questions

Will my windshield be replaced or just resealed?

It depends on the recall. For 2020-2021 Challenger, Charger and 300 vehicles the notice says dealers remove and replace the front windshield urethane sealant. For 2024 Pacifica and Voyager vehicles the notice says dealers replace the windshield. Both are free of charge.

Does the wiper recall affect Pacificas of other model years?

The wiper arm nut recall names 2022 Chrysler Voyager and Pacifica vehicles only. The other Pacifica recall in this group covers the 2024 Pacifica and Voyager for windshield bonding. Neither notice extends to any other model year.

What does an FMVSS 212 failure mean for my car?

Both windshield recalls say the vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 212, 'Windshield Mounting,' because the windshield may not be properly bonded and could detach in a crash. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving; dealers perform the repair free of charge.
