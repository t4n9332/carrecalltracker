Rivian seat belt recalls: loose anchors, bolts and retractors explained

5 NHTSA recalls · Rivian · Seat Belts · model years 2022–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 15, 2026

Fastener tightness dominates this group. Three of the five recalls come down to seat belt bolts: the 2022 recall covers front anchors inadequately attached to the B-pillar on 2022 R1T and R1S, a 2025 recall covers D-ring bolts on the front seat belt anchorage assemblies of 2022-2025 R1T and R1S, and a 2026 recall covers improperly tightened second-row retractor bolts on 2022-2025 R1T and 2022-2026 R1S. The 2022 model year appears in every R1 recall listed here. The two others are different problems: a faulty automatic locking retractor sensor in the front passenger belt system of 2022 R1T and R1S, which Rivian reports as a Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 208 noncompliance, and a driver's seat belt pretensioner cable that may become damaged on 2022-2025 EDV vans.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Rivian describes each risk in crash terms only: an anchor, retractor or pretensioner cable that is not properly secured may not restrain the occupant as intended, increasing the risk of injury in a crash. The 2022 passenger seat belt sensor recall says the air bag may be suppressed or deploy improperly. The notices set no interim condition on driving; each gives a Rivian customer service number, 1-888-748-4261, for owners with questions.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls Rivian inspects the seat belt hardware and corrects what it finds: securing or tightening anchor and retractor bolts on the R1T and R1S recalls, inspecting and replacing front passenger seat belt system components on the 2022 sensor recall, and inspecting and replacing the driver's pretensioner assembly on the EDV, where Rivian also released an over-the-air software update. All of this work is free of charge.

Common questions

My R1T already had loose seat belt bolts fixed. Could another recall still apply?

The 2022 recall covered front anchors on 2022 R1T and R1S, the 2025 recall covers front D-ring bolts on 2022-2025 vehicles, and the 2026 recall covers second-row retractor bolts. They list different model years and different hardware, so a VIN check shows which apply to your vehicle.

Does the second-row seat belt recall include the R1S?

Yes. The 2026 recall lists 2022-2025 R1T and 2022-2026 R1S vehicles, and says the bolts securing the second-row driver and passenger side retractor assemblies may be improperly tightened. Rivian will inspect and tighten them as necessary, free of charge, with letters mailed March 2, 2026.

What does the over-the-air update on the EDV do?

The notice says Rivian released an over-the-air software update for the 2022-2025 EDV pretensioner cable recall, and that Rivian will also inspect and replace the driver's seat belt pretensioner assembly as necessary, free of charge. The notice does not describe what the update changes.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
