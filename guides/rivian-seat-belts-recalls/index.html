Why has Rivian recalled seat belts on R1T and R1S repeatedly?

5 NHTSA recalls · Rivian · Seat Belts · model years 2022–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-01-15

Rivian's seat belt recalls center on hardware that wasn't tightened correctly during assembly. Three separate recalls cover bolts holding seat belt anchors or retractors: 2022 R1T and R1S front anchor bolts at the B-pillar, a 2022-2025 repeat of loose front anchor D-ring bolts on the same models, and improperly tightened second-row retractor bolts on 2022-2025 R1T and R1S vehicles. A separate 2022 R1T and R1S recall involved a passenger seat belt sensor that could disable the air bag. The commercial EDV van has its own issue: a driver's seat belt pretensioner cable that can become damaged. R1T and R1S from the 2022 model year are named in the most recalls.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices describe no symptom or warning sign an owner could notice; these seat belt and sensor defects are typically found only through dealer inspection or in a crash.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five Rivian seat belt recalls include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the seat belt anchor and retractor bolt recalls (2022, 2025, and 2026) do not tell owners to stop driving. The same is true for the passenger seat belt sensor recall and the EDV pretensioner cable recall. In each case, Rivian directs owners to schedule a dealer inspection and repair rather than instructing them to stop using the vehicle.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, Rivian dealers inspect the seat belt anchor, retractor, and sensor assemblies and tighten, secure, or replace bolts and components as needed. For the EDV pretensioner cable, Rivian issued an over-the-air software update along with an inspection and replacement of the seat belt pretensioner assembly. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why has Rivian recalled the R1T and R1S seat belts so many times?

Rivian has issued separate recalls for different seat belt hardware problems: front anchor bolts (2022 and again 2022-2025 models), second-row retractor bolts (2022-2025 models), and a passenger sensor that could disable the air bag, plus a pretensioner cable issue on the EDV van.

Do I need to stop driving my Rivian because of these recalls?

No recall in this group carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices direct owners to schedule a free dealer inspection and repair of the seat belt hardware rather than telling them to stop using the vehicle.

Which Rivian models and years are covered?

The R1T and R1S recalls in this group span the 2022 through 2025 model years, covering seat belt anchor and retractor bolts. The seat belt pretensioner cable recall applies only to the 2022 EDV commercial van, Rivian's delivery vehicle.
