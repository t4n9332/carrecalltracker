Lincoln seat recalls: folding second rows, head restraints and loose seat frames

8 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Seats · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 21, 2026

Eight seat recalls, and the Aviator appears in five. Ford recalled 2020-2025 Aviators twice, in 2025 and 2026, because the easy-entry second-row outer seat switch may bind or stick; the 2025 notice describes seats unlatching, folding or sliding while driving, the 2026 notice unlatching, tipping or sliding. The 2020 Aviator also fell under the 2019 recliner recall covering rear outboard manual recliners missing a third pawl, and the 2025 Aviator was recalled in 2026 for an unoccupied driver's seat that may recline. Head restraints cover two more: the 2022 Aviator cushion and the 2020 Navigator second-row headrest weld. The 2018 Navigator may be missing second-row center J-channel brackets, and 16-way tilt seat wiring was recalled on the 2018 MKX and 2019-2020 Nautilus.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2025 and 2026 second-row seat recalls (2020-2025 Aviator) say the easy-entry switch may bind or stick and the seats may move unexpectedly.
  • The 2022 Aviator recall says the front power head restraint cushion may detach from the assembly when adjusted to the full forward position.
  • The 2026 driver's seat recall (2025 Aviator) says an unoccupied driver's seat may recline unintentionally in scenarios such as remote unlock or remote start.
  • The 2019 recliner recall (2020 Aviator) says the recliner mechanism may be missing the third pawl, resulting in a loose seat back.
  • The 2020 16-way seat wiring recall (2018 MKX, 2019-2020 Nautilus) says damaged wiring can result in inadvertent air bag deployment.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these eight notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Several sent interim letters about the safety risk before the remedy: the 2026 second-row switch recall, whose remedy comes in phases by model and model year; the 2018 Navigator center seat recall; and the 2022 Aviator head restraint recall, followed by notification letters in July 2022. The other notices state only the risk to occupants in a crash and give owners no instruction to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect seat structures and replace them as needed on the 2019 recliner and 2020 headrest weld recalls, replace the 2022 Aviator head restraints, replace the center seat frame if J-channel brackets are missing, tape the 16-way seat cushion frame edge and repair damaged wiring, replace the second-row switch bezel, and disable then replace the driver seat motor. Repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why is my Aviator's second-row seat covered by two recalls?

Two notices describe the easy-entry second-row outer seat switch binding or sticking: one in 2025 and one in 2026, both listing 2020-2025 Aviators. In each, dealers inspect and replace the bezel around the switch as necessary, free of charge. The 2026 remedy is phased by model year.

Does any Lincoln seat recall involve the airbag?

One. The 2020 recall on 2018 MKX and 2019-2020 Nautilus vehicles with 16-way electric tilt seats says the seat cushion frame edge may contact and damage the wire harness, which can result in inadvertent air bag deployment or non-deployment in a crash.

My 2025 Aviator's driver seat reclined on its own — is that known?

The 2026 recall on 2025 Aviators says an unoccupied driver's seat may recline unintentionally in scenarios such as remote unlock or remote start, which may trap a second-row occupant. Dealers disable the driver seat module feature until parts arrive, then replace the motor, free of charge.
