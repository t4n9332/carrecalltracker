Lincoln Seat Recalls Cover Unexpected Movement and Head Restraint Problems

9 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Seats · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 21, 2026

Lincoln Aviator appears in five notices, with recurring concerns about seat movement and occupant restraint. The June 2025 notice covers sticking easy-entry switches in 2020–2025 Aviators; the July 2026 notice lists 2020–2027 Aviators with switches that may bind or stick. Other Aviator notices concern a missing recliner pawl in 2020 models, detachable front power head restraint cushions in 2022 models, and unintended driver-seat reclining in 2025–2027 models. Navigator notices address missing second-row center-seat reinforcement brackets in 2018, a second-row headrest bracket weld in 2020, and driver-seat software in 2026. The wiring notice covers 2016–2018 MKX and 2019–2020 Nautilus vehicles with 16-way electric tilt seats.

Warning signs to watch for

  • August 2019 recliner recall (19C07): a loose seat back.
  • June 2025 second-row switch recall (25S67): seats unlatching, folding, or sliding unexpectedly while driving; July 2026 switch recall: seats unlatching, tipping, or sliding unexpectedly.
  • March 2026 Navigator recall (26S11): the driver seat reversing unexpectedly while driving at low speeds.
  • April 2022 Aviator head restraint recall: a front power head restraint cushion detaching when adjusted to the full forward position.
  • July 2026 driver-seat reclining recall: an unoccupied driver’s seat reclining unintentionally during scenarios such as remote unlock or remote start.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these nine notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving for the August 2019 recliner, July 2020 wiring, June 2025 second-row switch, March 2026 Navigator software, or July 2026 second-row switch recalls. The notices also do not tell owners to stop driving for the November 2018 center-seat bracket, June 2020 headrest weld, April 2022 head restraint cushion, or July 2026 driver-seat reclining recalls.

What the free repair involves

The notices specify free repairs: seat inspections and necessary structure or frame replacement; wiring protection and harness inspection, with repair or replacement as needed; switch bezel inspection and replacement as necessary; and front head restraint replacement. Navigator recall 26S11 receives dealer or OTA software updates. The July 2026 reclining recall disables the DSM feature until parts are available, then replaces the motor.

Common questions

Which Lincoln Aviator years have second-row easy-entry switch recalls?

The June 2025 notice lists certain 2020–2025 Aviators, while the July 2026 notice lists certain 2020–2027 Aviators. Both describe switches that may bind or stick and specify inspection and replacement of the switch bezel as necessary, free of charge.

Does the Navigator driver-seat recall require a dealer visit?

For certain 2026 Navigators, recall 26S11 specifies a seat control module software update through a dealer or an over-the-air update, free of charge. The notice describes obstacle detection software malfunctioning at low speeds and the seat reversing unexpectedly while driving.

What is the seat wiring repair for Lincoln MKX and Nautilus?

The July 2020 notice covers certain 2016–2018 MKX and 2019–2020 Nautilus vehicles with 16-way electric tilt seats. Dealers install protective flocking tape on the exposed seat cushion frame edge, inspect the harness, and repair or replace it as needed, free of charge.
