Toyota Air Bag Recalls Involve Passenger Sensors and Deployment Problems

10 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 20, 2024

Passenger detection and air bag deployment recur throughout these Toyota notices. The 2019 Camry notice concerns OCS calibration; the 2022 RAV4, RAV4 Hybrid, and RAV4 Prime notice concerns internal seat interference affecting occupant detection. A separate OCS short-circuit notice covers multiple Toyota and Lexus models, including certain 2020-2022 Camry and Camry Hybrid vehicles. Avalon and RAV4 appear in several notices, while the ECU notice reaches certain 2011-2019 Corollas. Other defects involve Sienna door welds, Land Cruiser seat-belt sensor wiring, Highlander seat trim, Avalon/RAV4 steering columns, Corolla Cross instrument-panel perforation, and Grand Highlander/Lexus TX curtain-air-bag deployment. The dominant concern is reduced crash protection.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Sienna door-weld recall K1G/K0G: affected air bags could deploy unintentionally.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The Sienna door-weld, Land Cruiser/LX 570 wiring, Camry calibration, Corolla/Matrix/Avalon ECU, Highlander seat-trim, and Avalon/RAV4 steering-column notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Neither do the Corolla Cross instrument-panel, Toyota/Lexus OCS short-circuit, Grand Highlander/Lexus TX curtain-air-bag, or 2022 RAV4 passenger-seat notices. Their stated concerns involve unintended deployment or reduced protection in a crash, but the notices do not direct owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

The notices specify free remedies tailored to each defect. Examples include Sienna passenger-door subassembly replacement, Camry OCS recalibration, ECU inspection and a noise filter as necessary for the Corolla/Matrix/Avalon recall, and steering-column replacement for the Avalon/RAV4 recall. Other notices specify seat-belt modification or replacement, seat-trim replacement as necessary, instrument-panel replacement as necessary, sensor replacement as necessary, curtain-air-bag replacement, or passenger-seat repair as necessary.

Common questions

Are the Toyota passenger-sensor recalls caused by the same problem?

The notices describe different defects: improper OCS calibration in certain 2019 Camrys, internal seat interference affecting occupant detection in certain 2022 RAV4 variants, and a possible OCS sensor short circuit across the models listed in the December 2023 sensor notice.

What does the Corolla Cross instrument-panel recall cover?

Recall 23TA16 covers certain 2022-2023 Corolla Cross vehicles previously inspected under recall 23V-384. Missing required instrument-panel perforation could prevent proper front-passenger air bag deployment. Dealers inspect and replace the instrument panel as necessary, free of charge.

What repair addresses Grand Highlander and Lexus TX curtain air bags?

For the listed 2024 Grand Highlander and Lexus TX variants, dealers replace the driver and passenger curtain shield air bag assemblies free of charge. The notice says these air bags may not unroll as intended and could partially deploy outside an open window.
