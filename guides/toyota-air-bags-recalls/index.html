Why do so many Toyota air bag recalls involve passenger sensors?

10 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 20, 2024

Six of these ten Toyota recalls concern the front passenger air bag or the passenger seat. The Occupant Classification System appears three times: improper calibration on 2019 Camry; interference between internal parts of the 2022 RAV4, RAV4 Hybrid and RAV4 Prime passenger seat; and a sensor short circuit on 2020-2021 Avalon, Corolla, Highlander, RAV4 and Lexus ES models plus 2020-2022 Camry. Seats and trim recur: 2020 Highlander seat trim covers, and the 2008-2019 Land Cruiser and Lexus LX 570 seat belt tension sensor harness. The rest stand alone: a 2019 Sienna door weld, an air bag ECU that may malfunction in certain crashes, steering column breakaway pins on 2020-2021 Avalon and 2020 RAV4, a 2022-2023 Corolla Cross instrument panel, and 2024 Grand Highlander and Lexus TX curtain air bags.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these ten notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. That includes the 2019 Sienna door recall, the Land Cruiser and LX 570 seat belt sensor recall, the 2019 Camry and 2022 RAV4 Occupant Classification System recalls, the Highlander seat trim recall, the Avalon and RAV4 steering column recall, the Corolla Cross instrument panel recall, the multi-model OCS sensor short circuit recall, the Grand Highlander and TX curtain air bag recall, and the air bag ECU recall. Each describes only an injury risk in a crash.

What the free repair involves

All ten remedies are free of charge. Two address the Occupant Classification System: recalibration on 2019 Camry, sensor inspection and replacement on the 2020-2022 Toyota and Lexus vehicles. The rest is parts work — a Sienna door sub assembly, Highlander seat trim covers, a steering column, an instrument panel, curtain air bag assemblies, a RAV4 passenger seat repair, a seat belt assembly — plus an air bag ECU inspection and noise filter.

Common questions

What is the Occupant Classification System in these recalls?

It is the front passenger seat sensor system these notices name. Three recalls involve it: 2019 Camry calibration, 2022 RAV4 seat interference, and a sensor short circuit across 2020-2021 Toyota and Lexus models plus 2020-2022 Camry. Dealers recalibrate or replace as each notice states.

Will I see a warning light if my air bag is affected?

The notices for these ten recalls do not describe any warning light, sound or other sign an owner would notice. They describe what may fail in a crash — air bags or seat belt pretensioners not deploying as intended — not what happens beforehand.

How do I find out if my Toyota or Lexus is included?

The notices give Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 (earlier notices list 1-888-270-9371) and Lexus at 1-800-255-3987, along with Toyota recall numbers such as 23TA16 and 24TB09. The OCS sensor notice says remedy letters continue in phases through January 2026.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
