Why do so many Toyota models have airbag deployment recalls?

10 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-06-20

Toyota's air bag recalls in this group repeat one theme: the front-passenger Occupant Classification System (OCS) sensor. A miscalibrated OCS blocked the 2019 Camry's passenger and knee air bags; seat interference misread occupants in 2022 RAV4, RAV4 Hybrid, and RAV4 Prime models; and a short-circuiting OCS sensor affected passenger air bags across many 2020-2022 models. Other recalls involve separate hardware: a wire harness break on 2018-2019 Land Cruiser and LX 570, an ECU fault on 2018-2019 Corolla and 2018 Avalon, seat trim blocking the 2020 Highlander's side air bag, damaged steering column pins on 2020-2021 Avalon and 2020 RAV4, a door weld defect on the 2019 Sienna, an instrument panel defect on the 2022-2023 Corolla Cross, and curtain air bags on the 2024 Grand Highlander and Lexus TX Hybrid.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices describe no symptom or warning sign an owner could notice; these defects are identified through dealer inspection or during a crash, not while driving.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these ten Toyota air bag recalls include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the Sienna door weld, Land Cruiser and LX 570 sensor, Camry OCS, Corolla/Avalon ECU, Highlander seat trim, Avalon/RAV4 steering column, Corolla Cross, the multi-model OCS sensor recall, Grand Highlander curtain air bag, and RAV4 OCS recalls do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice directs owners to a free dealer inspection or repair instead.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, Toyota dealers inspect and, as needed, replace or recalibrate the affected part: OCS sensors, seat belt wire harnesses, air bag ECUs, seat trim covers, steering columns, door assemblies, instrument panels, and curtain air bag assemblies. No recall in this group uses an over-the-air update. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why do so many different Toyota models have air bag recalls?

These ten recalls involve different causes: several trace to Occupant Classification System sensor problems that can block the front-passenger air bag, while others involve unrelated defects like a wire harness, an ECU fault, seat trim, or a steering column.

Will my air bag work if I don't get the recall repair?

The notices say the affected air bags may not deploy as intended, or could deploy unintentionally, which increases the risk of injury in a crash. None of the notices tell owners to stop driving, but they recommend scheduling the free repair.

Is there a warning sign before the air bag recall problem shows up?

No. None of these notices describe a warning sign an owner would notice; the defects are identified through dealer inspection or during a crash, not through dashboard warnings or other symptoms while driving.
