Why do Volkswagen suspension recalls keep involving springs, knuckles and loose bolts?

7 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Suspension · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 21, 2023

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Two themes run through these seven Volkswagen suspension recalls: rear coil springs that may prematurely fracture, and fasteners that were not properly tightened. The spring recalls cover 2018-2019 Atlas, 2015 Golf SportWagen and 2019 Jetta in one case, and 2015-2019 Golf, 2017-2019 Golf SportWagen, 2019 Jetta and 2018-2019 Tiguan in the other. The Tiguan appears repeatedly: a loose or improperly tightened lower ball joint nut on 2018 Tiguan and Tiguan long wheel base, a rear shock absorber lower loop that may separate on 2018 long wheel base vehicles, and rear suspension knuckles that may corrode, crack and break on 2021 Tiguan long wheel base, 2022 Tiguan and 2022 Taos. Loose bolts also hit 2022 Atlas FL and 2022-2023 Atlas Cross Sport trailing arms.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One recall in this group carries a stop-driving instruction: the lower ball joint nut recall on 2018 Tiguan and Tiguan long wheel base vehicles, where owners are told not to drive their vehicle until the recall repair has been performed. For the other six — the 2018 Tiguan long wheel base rear shock absorber, both rear coil spring recalls, the rear suspension knuckle recall on 2021-2022 Tiguan and 2022 Taos, the Atlas rear trailing arm bolts and the 2023 Golf R strut mount — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the nut on the lower ball joint and, as necessary, clean the thread in the ball joint; inspect the rear shock absorbers and replace them as necessary; replace the rear axle coil springs under both spring recalls; inspect and, if necessary, replace one or both rear suspension knuckles; replace the driver side rear trailing arm bolts; and inspect and replace the suspension strut mountings as necessary. Every notice states the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Which Volkswagens are covered by the rear coil spring recalls?

One notice lists 2018-2019 Atlas, 2015 Golf SportWagen and 2019 Jetta vehicles. The other lists 2015-2019 Golf, 2017-2019 Golf SportWagen, 2019 Jetta and 2018-2019 Tiguan vehicles. In both, dealers replace the rear axle coil springs free of charge.

Can I keep driving my 2018 Tiguan?

For the lower ball joint nut recall on 2018 Tiguan and Tiguan long wheel base vehicles, owners are told not to drive their vehicle until the recall repair has been performed. The separate 2018 Tiguan long wheel base shock absorber notice does not give that instruction.

What happens under the rear suspension knuckle recall?

The notice covers 2022 Tiguan, 2022 Taos and 2021 Tiguan long wheel base vehicles whose left or right rear suspension knuckles may corrode, crack and break. Dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace one or both knuckles free of charge. A second notice will be sent once remedy parts become available.
