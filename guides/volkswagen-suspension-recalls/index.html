Volkswagen suspension recalls involving loose fasteners and broken rear springs

7 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Suspension · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 21, 2023

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Volkswagen's suspension notices repeatedly address connections that can loosen and rear components that can fracture. The 2018 Tiguan appears in recalls involving lower ball joint nuts, rear shock absorber welds on LWB vehicles, and rear coil springs. Premature rear spring fracture also affects certain 2018–2019 Atlas, 2015 Golf SportWagen, and 2019 Jetta vehicles in the December 2018 notice. The March 2019 notice lists 2017–2019 Golf Sportwagen, 2015–2019 Golf, 2019 Jetta, and 2018–2019 Tiguan vehicles. Later notices cover rear suspension knuckles on certain Tiguan and Taos vehicles, trailing arm bolts on Atlas variants, and strut mountings on 2023 Golf R A8 and Golf GTI vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering, traction, or stability problems if the ball joint separates: 2018 VW Tiguan Loose Ball Joint Nut recall.
  • Sudden loss of vehicle control if the trailing arm fractures: 2022 VW Atlas Rear Trailing Arm Bolts May Loosen recall.
  • Steering instability from loose suspension connections: 2023 Volkswagen Golf R Suspension Strut Mount May Loosen recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The May 16, 2018 VW Tiguan Loose Ball Joint Nut recall tells owners not to drive until the recall repair has been performed. It is the only listed suspension recall carrying a stop-driving instruction; none carries a park-outside instruction. The notices for the rear shock absorber, both rear coil spring recalls, rear suspension knuckle, rear trailing arm bolt, and suspension strut mounting recalls do not tell owners to stop driving. Those notices describe crash risks involving vehicle control, stability, or steering.

What the free repair involves

Repairs are free of charge. Dealers replace ball joint nuts, cleaning threads as necessary; inspect rear shocks and replace as necessary; and replace rear axle springs under each spring recall. They inspect knuckles and strut mountings, replacing as necessary under their respective recalls. The trailing arm recall replaces driver-side rear trailing arm bolts.

Common questions

Which Volkswagen suspension recall tells owners to stop driving?

The May 16, 2018 Tiguan loose ball joint nut notice tells owners not to drive until the recall repair is performed. The other listed suspension notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

Which Volkswagen models have rear coil spring recalls?

The December 2018 notice lists 2018–2019 Atlas, 2015 Golf SportWagen, and 2019 Jetta. The March 2019 notice lists 2017–2019 Golf Sportwagen, 2015–2019 Golf, 2019 Jetta, and 2018–2019 Tiguan. Both specify rear axle coil spring replacement.

What does the Tiguan and Taos rear knuckle repair involve?

Recall 42L8 covers certain 2022 Tiguan and Taos vehicles and 2021 Tiguan LWB vehicles. Dealers inspect the rear suspension knuckles and, if necessary, replace one or both free of charge. The notice describes corrosion, cracking, and breakage.
