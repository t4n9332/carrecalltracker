Why do Volkswagen Tiguan and Atlas suspensions keep breaking?

7 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Suspension · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-06-21

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

These seven Volkswagen suspension recalls show two recurring failure points: loose fasteners and fractured components. A 2018 Tiguan recall for a loose front lower ball joint nut is the only one in this group with a stop-driving instruction; the same model year Tiguan was separately recalled for a rear shock absorber weld failure. Rear coil springs that may prematurely fracture were the subject of two separate 2018-2019 recalls, one covering Atlas and Jetta, the other covering Golf, Golf Sportwagen, Tiguan and Jetta. Later recalls cover 2021-2022 Tiguan and 2022 Taos rear suspension knuckles that may crack, 2022-2023 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport rear trailing arm bolts that may loosen, and a 2023 Golf R suspension strut mount that may loosen.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices describe no warning sign for a loose ball joint nut, cracked suspension knuckle, or loosening trailing-arm bolts
  • A rear coil spring that fractures and damages a tire while driving
  • A rear shock absorber loop separating from its mounting

Is it safe to keep driving?

One of these seven recalls carries a stop-driving instruction: the May 2018 recall of 2018 Tiguan and Tiguan long wheelbase vehicles for a loose or improperly tightened front lower ball joint nut, which tells owners not to drive until the repair is performed. The other six recalls — covering the rear shock absorber, both rear coil spring recalls, the rear suspension knuckle, the rear trailing arm bolts, and the Golf R strut mount — do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the ball joint nut and clean the joint threads, inspect and replace rear shock absorbers and rear coil springs as needed, inspect and replace rear suspension knuckles and trailing arm bolts, and inspect or replace the Golf R's suspension strut mountings. All of these repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Volkswagen suspension recall tells owners to stop driving?

Only the May 2018 recall of 2018 Tiguan vehicles for a loose front lower ball joint nut tells owners not to drive until the ball joint nut has been replaced. The other six suspension recalls do not carry that instruction.

Why were there two separate rear coil spring recalls?

The December 2018 recall covered 2018-2019 Atlas and 2019 Jetta vehicles, while the March 2019 recall covered 2018-2019 Golf, Golf Sportwagen and Tiguan and 2019 Jetta vehicles. Both addressed rear coil springs that may prematurely fracture.

Which models are affected by the rear suspension knuckle recall?

The March 2022 recall covers 2021 Tiguan long wheelbase, 2022 Tiguan, and 2022 Taos vehicles, whose left or right rear suspension knuckles may corrode, crack, and eventually break, according to the recall notice.
