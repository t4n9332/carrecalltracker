What do the Genesis fuel pump and fuel leak recalls cover?

6 NHTSA recalls · Genesis · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 10, 2026

Fuel pumps dominate this group of six Genesis recalls. Four involve the pump: a blocked jet nozzle on the 2021 G70 with the 2.0L GDI engine that can stall the engine, and three fuel pump failure recalls that can cause a loss of drive power — one for 2021-2022 G80 and GV80 with the 2022 GV70, one for 2022-2023 GV70, GV80, G80 and G90, and one for 2019-2023 G70. The other two are leaks at the fuel rail: the high pressure fuel tube on 2021 G80 and GV80 with the 2.5-litre turbo engine, and a 2026 recall covering 2021-2025 G80 and GV80, 2022-2026 GV70 and 2023-2025 G90.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The engine stalls because not enough fuel reaches it (2021 G70 2.0L GDI fuel pump jet nozzle recall).
  • A loss of drive power when the fuel pump fails (the 2021-2022 G80 and GV80, the 2022-2023 GV70, GV80, G80 and G90, and the 2019-2023 G70 fuel pump recalls).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That includes both leak recalls — the 2021 G80 and GV80 high pressure fuel tube recall and the 2026 fuel pipe recall covering 2021-2025 G80 and GV80, 2022-2026 GV70 and 2023-2025 G90, where the notices describe a fire risk — and all four fuel pump recalls, where the notices describe an engine stall or a loss of drive power. Beyond the repair itself, the notices give owners no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is free of charge. For the 2021 G70, dealers inspect and repair the fuel pump assembly jet nozzle. The fuel pump failure recalls have dealers replace the pump, or inspect and replace the pump assembly; the 2019-2023 G70 recall also updates engine control module software. The leak recalls replace the high pressure fuel tube, or inspect and tighten or replace the fuel pipe.

Common questions

Why does the Genesis fuel pump appear in more than one recall?

Four recalls in this group involve the fuel pump, covering different models and model years. The 2024 recall for 2022-2023 GV70, GV80, G80 and G90 says it is an expansion of recall number 23V-630.

What do the fuel leak recalls say about my G80 or GV80?

The 2021 recall covers 2021 G80 and GV80 with 2.5-litre turbo engines, where the high pressure fuel tube may not seal properly. The 2026 recall covers fuel leaking at the connection between the fuel pipe and fuel rail. Both notices cite a fire risk.

Do I pay for the fuel pump or fuel pipe repair?

No. Each notice in this group says the repair is performed free of charge, whether that is replacing the fuel pump assembly, repairing the jet nozzle, replacing the high pressure fuel tube, or tightening or replacing the fuel pipe.
