Why does Genesis keep recalling vehicles for fuel leaks and pump failures?

6 NHTSA recalls · Genesis · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-10

Six NHTSA recalls cover fuel system problems across Genesis G70, G80, G90, GV70 and GV80 models built between 2019 and 2025. Two recalls involve fuel leaking at the connection between a fuel pipe or tube and the fuel rail: one on 2021 G80 and GV80 with 2.5-litre turbo engines, the other spanning G80, G90, GV70 and GV80 built through 2025. The other four recalls involve the fuel pump itself: a manufacturing burr blocking the pump nozzle in the 2021 G70, and three separate pump-failure recalls affecting G70, G80, G90, GV70 and GV80 across 2021-2023 model years, one of which also covers related Hyundai N-line vehicles sharing the same engine.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Fuel odor or visible fuel leaking near the engine
  • Engine stalling caused by insufficient fuel reaching it
  • Sudden loss of drive power while driving
  • The notices do not describe a dashboard warning light for these fuel system issues

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six Genesis fuel-system recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the two fuel leak recalls (the 2021 G80/GV80 fuel tube recall and the later fuel pipe recall covering G80, G90, GV70 and GV80), the notices describe a fire risk from a fuel leak but do not tell owners to stop driving. For the four fuel pump recalls affecting G70, G80, G90, GV70 and GV80, which describe engine stalling or loss of drive power, the notices likewise do not instruct owners to stop driving or park outside.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, dealers inspect and replace or tighten the fuel tube or pipe connection, inspect and repair or replace the fuel pump jet nozzle or assembly, and in one case update engine control module software alongside a fuel pump inspection. All repairs, inspections, and software updates are performed free of charge to the owner.

Common questions

Is a Genesis fuel leak recall dangerous?

The notices say a fuel leak near an ignition source increases fire risk, but they do not instruct owners to stop driving. Dealers replace or tighten the fuel tube or pipe free of charge once notified.

Why does my Genesis lose power while driving?

Several recalls cover a fuel pump that can fail or become blocked, reducing fuel supply to the engine and causing a stall or loss of drive power. Dealers inspect and replace the pump free of charge.

Are Hyundai models affected by the same Genesis fuel pump recall?

Yes, one 2024 recall for fuel pump failure also covers related Hyundai Veloster N, Elantra N, and Kona N vehicles that share the same engine as the recalled Genesis G70.
