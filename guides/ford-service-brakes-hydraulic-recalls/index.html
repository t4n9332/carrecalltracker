Which Ford brake recalls involve leaking fluid or a failing pedal?

20 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 1, 2025

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

Ford's hydraulic brake recalls repeat a few shapes. Leaks dominate: front brake hoses on the 2018 Escape and Lincoln MKC and on 2013-2018 Fusion and MKZ, and a master cylinder that lets fluid into the brake booster on 3.5L EcoBoost 2016-2018 F-150 and 2016-2017 Expedition and Navigator, with a 2025 recall listing the same leak on 2017-2018 F-150, Expedition and Navigator. Pedal hardware is the other pattern, and the most serious: a loose pedal on the 2024 Mustang, a pedal bracket that may fracture on the 2020 Mustang, and a missing brake booster pushrod retaining spring on 2025 Super Duty trucks. Single entries cover 2019 Fiesta calipers, the 2023 Bronco Sport ABS module, 2022 F-53 and F-59 caliper bolts, and 2021 E-350 and E-450 wiring.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2024 Mustang (24S43): a constantly illuminated brake fluid level warning light, which the notice says can conceal a low brake fluid condition.
  • 2024 Mustang (the 2023 brake pedal recall): a loose brake pedal.
  • 2016-2018 F-150 and 2016-2017 Expedition and Navigator master cylinder recall: a change in brake pedal travel, increased pedal effort and reduced front brake function.
  • 2013-2018 Fusion and MKZ (23S12): increased brake pedal travel and a longer distance needed to stop.
  • 2023 Bronco Sport (23S01): an unexpected increase in brake pedal travel, or unexpected vehicle movement during an Auto Hold event.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two notices here tell owners not to drive. On the 2024 Mustang brake pedal recall, owners are advised not to drive their vehicle until the repair has been performed. On the 2025 F-250, F-350, F-450 and F-550 Super Duty brake booster pushrod recall, owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until they have been repaired. For the remaining recalls in this group, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving, and none instructs owners to park outside. The 2016-2018 F-150 master cylinder notice describes an interim letter sent to explain the safety defect.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace brake hoses on Escape, MKC, Fusion and MKZ; replace the master cylinder, and the booster if it leaks, on F-150, Expedition and Navigator; replace the 2024 Mustang pedal and bracket and the 2020 Mustang bracket; swap the Bronco Sport ABS module; flush fluid and fit new Fiesta calipers and hoses; tighten F-53 and F-59 caliper bolts; and update Mustang body control module software. All free of charge.

Common questions

My 2024 Mustang is under the brake pedal recall — can I drive it to the dealer?

The notice says owners are advised not to drive their vehicle until the repair has been performed, and it describes no exception or interim step. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 to arrange the repair.

Which of these recalls involve brake fluid leaking?

Several: incorrect front hoses that can chafe on the 2018 Escape and MKC, front hoses that may rupture on 2013-2018 Fusion and MKZ, master cylinders leaking into the booster on F-150, Expedition and Navigator, and an internal ABS module leak on the 2023 Bronco Sport.

What does the dealer do about the 2024 Mustang brake warning light?

The notice says the brake fluid level sensor may not be properly configured after a body control module service update, leaving the warning light constantly illuminated and able to conceal low brake fluid. Dealers update the body control module software, free of charge.
