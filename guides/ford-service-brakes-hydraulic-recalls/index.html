Why do Ford brake recalls keep involving fluid leaks and loose pedals?

20 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-01

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

Across these Ford brake recalls, two problems repeat most: brake fluid leaking out of the system, and pedal hardware that isn't secure. The F-150 was recalled twice — in 2022 and 2025 — for brake fluid leaking from the master cylinder into the brake booster, a defect the 2025 recall also covers on the Expedition and Navigator; separately, the Fusion, MKZ, Escape and MKC each had their own front brake hose leak recall. The Mustang appears three times for pedal problems: a loose 2024 pedal assembly, a fractured 2020 pedal bracket, and a 2024 brake fluid warning light that can stay lit and hide a real leak. Heavy-duty trucks, including Super Duty pickups and the F-53 and F-59 chassis, show caliper and bolt failures instead.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake pedal feels loose, spongy, or has more travel than normal
  • Visible brake fluid leak, or longer distance/more effort needed to stop
  • Brakes dragging or overheating after driving (Fiesta calipers)
  • Brake fluid warning light stays on but may not reflect the actual fluid level (Mustang)
  • Some recalls, like loose caliper bolts or wiring harness damage, describe no noticeable warning before failure

Is it safe to keep driving?

Two recalls tell owners not to drive until the vehicle is repaired: the 2024 Mustang loose brake pedal recall, and the 2025 Super Duty (F-450/F-550/F-350/F-250) recall for a missing brake booster pushrod spring that can cause sudden brake loss. None of these recalls carry a park-outside instruction. For the other recalls in this group — including the Escape, MKC, Fiesta, E-350/E-450, 2020 Mustang, F-150/Expedition/Navigator, Bronco Sport, Fusion, MKZ, and F-53/F-59 — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace or repair the affected part free of charge: brake pedal and bracket assemblies, brake master cylinders and boosters, front brake hoses, brake calipers, and caliper bolts. Some recalls involve inspecting and taping or splicing a wiring harness, or updating body control module software to fix the brake fluid warning light. No repair costs owners anything.

Common questions

Why does the Mustang have multiple brake-related recalls?

Ford issued separate Mustang recalls for a loose brake pedal assembly (2024), a fractured brake pedal bracket (2020), and a brake fluid warning light that stays on and can hide a real low-fluid condition (2024) — three distinct defects, not the same issue repeated.

What should I do if my Ford brake pedal feels loose or the brakes drag?

Contact a Ford dealer and reference the recall matching your model and year. If your vehicle is under the 2024 Mustang or 2025 Super Duty stop-driving recalls, do not drive it until the dealer completes the free repair.

Are these brake fluid leaks caused by the same part across different Ford models?

No. The F-150/Expedition/Navigator leaks come from the brake master cylinder, the Escape/MKC leak comes from incorrect front brake hoses, and the Fusion/MKZ leak also involves front brake hoses — each recall names a different failure point.
