Hyundai Power Seat Entrapment and Front Seat Recliner Recalls

3 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Seats · model years 2021–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 11, 2026

Two of these three Hyundai notices concern powered rear seats in 2026 vehicles. Certain Palisade and Palisade Hybrid Limited and Calligraphy models have second- and third-row seats that may fail to detect a person and continue moving after contact. Certain Ioniq 9 Limited, Calligraphy and Calligraphy Design models have a similar stated problem involving second-row powered seats. Both notices identify entrapment risks, especially for children; the Palisade notice includes the risk of death. The remaining recall concerns certain 2021 Elantras, whose improperly welded driver and front passenger seat back recliners could separate from the seat back frame during a crash.

Warning signs to watch for

  • March 17, 2026 Palisade and Palisade Hybrid recall: powered second- or third-row seats may continue moving after contacting a person.
  • September 11, 2026 Ioniq 9 recall: powered second-row seats may continue moving after contacting a person.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. The March 17, 2026 Palisade and Palisade Hybrid notice advises caution with second- and third-row power seat functions until repair. The September 11, 2026 Ioniq 9 notice gives that caution for second-row power seat functions. Both advise keeping children out of the rear seats and away from the rear seating area during power-folding operation. The April 28, 2021 Elantra notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the 2021 Elantra recall, dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace the seat back frame assembly free of charge. The Ioniq 9 excerpt specifies seat software inspection and an over-the-air update but truncates the remaining remedy. The Palisade remedy excerpt is also incomplete; neither powered-seat excerpt establishes whether its remedy is free.

Common questions

Which Hyundai powered seats are covered by these notices?

The Palisade and Palisade Hybrid notice covers second- and third-row power seats in certain 2026 Limited and Calligraphy vehicles. The Ioniq 9 notice covers second-row power seats in certain 2026 Limited, Calligraphy and Calligraphy Design vehicles.

What do the powered-seat notices say about children?

Both powered-seat notices advise that children should not be in the rear seats or near the rear seating area during power-folding operation. They also advise caution when operating the affected power seat functions until the repair is performed.

What repair does the Elantra seat recliner recall provide?

For certain 2021 Elantras, dealers inspect the seat back frame assembly and replace it if necessary, free of charge. The notice concerns improperly welded driver and front passenger seat back recliners that could separate from the frame during a crash.
