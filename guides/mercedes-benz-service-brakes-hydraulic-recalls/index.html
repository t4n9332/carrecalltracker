Why do Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Metris vans have brake defects?

5 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2020 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-02-08

All five hydraulic brake recalls in this group involve Mercedes-Benz vans, mostly the Sprinter. On 2020 Sprinter 3500 and 4500 vans, front and rear brake hoses damaged during production can leak fluid. On 2019-2020 Sprinter vans with electric parking brakes, rear brake calipers can leak fluid into the parking brake housing. Separately, other 2019-2020 Sprinter vans have a brake pedal retaining bolt that can loosen and leave the brake lights illuminated, and one individual 2020 Sprinter was built with the wrong-size front brake discs. A fifth recall covers 2016-2021 Metris vans, where a brake fluid reservoir may be incorrectly mounted and can detach, leaking brake fluid.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake lights may stay on even when the brake pedal is not pressed (Sprinter brake pedal recall).
  • The brake pedal may feel like it travels farther than normal before the brakes engage (Sprinter rear caliper recall).
  • Stopping distances may be longer than expected (2020 Sprinter wrong-size front brake discs).
  • Visible brake fluid leaks may appear near the hoses, calipers, or reservoir (Sprinter hose recall and Metris reservoir recall).

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five brake recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. The Sprinter brake hose recall, the rear brake caliper recall, the wrong-size front brake disc recall, the brake pedal bolt recall, and the Metris brake fluid reservoir recall each describe an increased risk of a crash from reduced braking performance, longer stopping distances, or brake lights that fail to signal properly, but none of the five notices instruct owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and replace parts as needed across these recalls: checking and replacing damaged brake hoses, replacing rear brake calipers, replacing the incorrect brake discs, adding a retaining ring to secure the brake pedal bolt, and repairing the brake fluid reservoir mounting on Metris vans. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why do the Sprinter brake lights stay on without pressing the pedal?

A retaining washer and bolt connecting the brake pedal to its bearing block can come loose and detach, which can cause the brake lights to stay illuminated even when the pedal is not pressed. Dealers add a retaining ring free of charge.

Is the Metris brake recall the same defect as the Sprinter recalls?

No. The Metris recall involves a brake fluid reservoir that may be incorrectly mounted and can detach and leak. The Sprinter recalls involve separate issues: damaged brake hoses, leaking rear calipers, wrong-size discs, and a loosening pedal bolt.

How many Sprinter vans got the wrong-size front brake discs?

According to the recall notice, one individual 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter was built with the incorrect-size front brake discs, which could extend stopping distance. A dealer will replace the discs free of charge.
