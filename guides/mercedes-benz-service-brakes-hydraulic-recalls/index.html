Why do Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and S-Class brakes keep getting recalled?

6 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 2, 2026

Nearly all of this group is the Sprinter van, recalled by Daimler Vans USA. Brake fluid leaks repeat: brake hoses on the front and rear axles of 2020 Sprinter 3500 and 4500 vans, and rear brake calipers leaking into the electric parking brake housing on 2019-2020 Sprinter 1500, 3500 and 4500 vans with electric parking brakes. The same 2019-2020 Sprinters also appear for a brake pedal retaining washer and bolt that may come loose. One recall covers a single 2020 Sprinter equipped with incorrect-size front brake discs. The 2016-2021 Metris adds a brake fluid reservoir that may detach. Separately, Mercedes-Benz USA recalled 2022-2025 S 500, S 580 and Maybach models for front brake hoses that may rupture.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake lights that stay illuminated even when the brake pedal is not pressed — stated in the 2019-2020 Sprinter brake pedal retaining washer and bolt recall.
  • Extended brake pedal travel — stated in the 2019-2020 Sprinter rear brake caliper recall.
  • Potential loss of directional stability when braking — stated in the 2020 Sprinter front and rear brake hose recall.
  • A longer distance required to stop — stated in the single-vehicle 2020 Sprinter incorrect-size brake disc recall and in the 2022-2025 S 580 and Maybach front brake hose recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the six notices carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction: the 2020 Sprinter brake hose recall, the 2019-2020 Sprinter rear caliper recall, the 2019-2020 Sprinter brake pedal recall, the single-vehicle 2020 Sprinter brake disc recall, the 2016-2021 Metris brake fluid reservoir recall and the 2022-2025 S 580 and Maybach front brake hose recall. Each describes a crash risk and the free dealer repair. For the brake pedal recall, the notice says DVUSA issued owners an interim notification on May 3, 2021. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers check the condition of the front and rear brake hoses on the 2020 Sprinter and replace them as needed, replace the rear brake calipers, replace the incorrect-size brake discs, inspect and repair the Metris brake fluid reservoir mounting as necessary, retrofit an additional retaining ring at the brake pedal bearing bracket, and replace the front brake hoses on the S-Class vehicles. All free of charge.

Common questions

Why does my 2019-2020 Sprinter show up in more than one brake recall?

The notices list the same Sprinter 1500, 3500 and 4500 model years for separate defects — rear brake calipers leaking into the parking brake housing and loose brake pedal hardware. Each has its own repair and DVUSA number; call 1-877-762-8267.

My Sprinter's brake lights stay on. What does the recall say?

The 2019-2020 Sprinter notice says the retaining washer and bolt connecting the brake pedal to the bearing block may come loose, which could leave the brake lights illuminated when the pedal is not pressed. Dealers retrofit a retaining ring free of charge.

Is the S 580 front brake hose recall a new one?

The notice says this recall expands previous NHTSA recall number 25V116 and covers listed 2022-2025 S 500, S 580, Maybach and AMG S 63 E Performance models. Dealers replace the front brake hoses free of charge; letters are expected November 27, 2026.
