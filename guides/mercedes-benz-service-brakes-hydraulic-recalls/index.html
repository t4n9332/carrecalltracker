Which Mercedes-Benz vans have brake fluid leak and braking recalls?

5 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2020 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 8, 2022

Five Mercedes-Benz recalls in this group cover hydraulic braking, and four of the five are Sprinter vans. Fluid leaks are the repeating theme: brake hoses on the front and rear axles of 2020 Sprinter 3500 and 4500 vans, rear brake calipers leaking into the electric parking brake housing on 2019-2020 Sprinter 1500, 4500 and 2020 3500 vans, and a brake fluid reservoir that may be incorrectly mounted on 2016-2021 Metris vans. The 2019-2020 Sprinter model years appear in two separate recalls, the caliper leak and a brake pedal retaining washer and bolt that may come loose. A single 2020 Sprinter VS30 was built with incorrect-size front brake discs.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Extended brake pedal travel, in the 2019-2020 Sprinter rear brake caliper recall, where air can enter the hydraulic brake system.
  • Brake lights that stay illuminated even when the pedal is not pressed, in the 2019-2020 Sprinter brake pedal retaining washer and bolt recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the five notices in this group tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. That applies to the brake hose recall for 2020 Sprinter 3500 and 4500 vans, the rear brake caliper recall for 2019-2020 Sprinter vans, the incorrect-size front brake disc recall for one 2020 Sprinter VS30, the Metris brake fluid reservoir recall, and the Sprinter brake pedal recall. The notices describe the risk each defect creates and the free repair, but they do not give owners any interim driving instruction.

What the free repair involves

Dealers check the front and rear brake hoses and replace them as needed, replace the rear brake calipers, replace the incorrect-size brake discs, inspect and repair the Metris brake fluid reservoir mounting as necessary, and retrofit an additional retaining ring at the bearing bracket of the brake pedal. No software updates or over-the-air fixes are involved. Each notice states the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Can my Sprinter be covered by more than one brake recall?

It can. The 2019-2020 Sprinter 1500, 4500 and 2020 3500 appear in both the rear brake caliper recall and the brake pedal retaining washer and bolt recall, which are separate repairs with separate DVUSA recall numbers.

Do these notices tell owners to stop driving until the brakes are fixed?

None of the five notices give a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. They state the defect, the crash risk it creates, and the free dealer repair, and direct owners to DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267 with the recall number.

Is the Metris brake recall the same as the Sprinter ones?

They are separate. The Metris recall covers a brake fluid reservoir that may be incorrectly mounted and can detach; dealers inspect and repair the mounting as necessary. The Sprinter recalls cover hoses, calipers, brake discs and the brake pedal connection.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
