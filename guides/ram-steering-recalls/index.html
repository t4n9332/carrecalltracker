What steering problems have led to Ram truck recalls?

7 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Steering · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-11-28

Heavy-duty 2500 and 3500 trucks have had two separate drag-link recalls that can let the drag link separate or fracture - one from a steering linkage jam nut that can loosen, the other from an aftermarket AEV suspension kit with a weakened steering knuckle joint. The 1500 has had two power-steering-assist recalls, from a loose battery ground fastener and a contaminated electric power steering gear, both of which can change how much effort it takes to steer. A 2019 recall covers steering wheel detachment on the Jeep Wrangler and Ram 1500 from a broken column stub shaft. The ProMaster City has a cracked steering rack recall, and a 2023-2024 Ram 3500/4500 recall covers a steering column module that can swap turn signals and high beams rather than affect steering.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering feels loose or the drag link visibly separates (2500/3500 drag-link recalls)
  • Steering suddenly needs much more effort, or power assist cuts out, especially during low-speed maneuvers (1500 recalls)
  • Steering wheel feels loose on the column or detaches (2019 Wrangler/1500 recall)
  • Steering fluid leak or a sudden increase in steering effort (ProMaster City rack recall)
  • Turn signal activates the high beams or vice versa, or the signal doesn't self-cancel (2023-2024 3500/4500 recall)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven steering recalls tell owners to stop driving. That includes both drag-link recalls on 2500 and 3500 trucks, the two 1500 power-steering-assist recalls, the 2019 steering wheel detachment recall on the Wrangler and 1500, the ProMaster City steering rack recall, and the 2023-2024 3500/4500 turn-signal and high-beam recall. The notices describe risks ranging from a possible loss of steering control to reduced visibility for other drivers, but none direct an owner to park the vehicle or avoid driving it.

What the free repair involves

Dealers repair or replace the affected part free of charge across all seven recalls: drag link or steering knuckle hardware, the battery ground fastener, the electric power steering gear, the steering column, the steering rack and pinion assembly, and the steering column control module. The 2023-2024 recall may also need a software update to the module. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Are the two Ram drag-link recalls the same problem?

No. One covers a factory steering linkage jam nut on 2500/3500 trucks that can loosen and let the drag link separate. The other covers an aftermarket AEV suspension kit whose steering knuckle installation can weaken the drag-link joint and cause it to fracture.

Why do two different Ram 1500 recalls both involve power steering?

They have different causes. One recall covers an improperly secured battery ground fastener; the other covers a contaminated electric power steering gear that can short circuit. Both can cause an intermittent loss of power steering assist.

Does the 2023-2024 steering-related recall affect how the truck steers?

No. That recall covers a steering column control module that can swap turn signal and high beam activation or fail to self-cancel the turn signal - it affects lighting, not the vehicle's steering control.
