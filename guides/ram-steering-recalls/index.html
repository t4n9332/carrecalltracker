Ram steering recalls: drag links, power steering loss, column faults

7 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Steering · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 28, 2023

Steering recalls on Ram cluster around two things. Heavy-duty trucks account for the linkage failures: Chrysler's recall of 2013-2018 Ram 3500, 2014-2018 Ram 3500 Chassis Cab and Ram 2500 for an outboard steering linkage jam nut that may loosen and let one end of the drag link separate, and Streetcar ORV's recall of AEV 3-inch Dualsport suspension kits sold for 2013-2018 Ram 2500 and 3500HD, where a weakened knuckle joint may fracture the drag link. The 2019 Ram 1500 alone appears three times — a stub shaft that may break and detach the steering wheel (shared with the 2019 Jeep Wrangler), a loose battery ground fastener, and a contaminated electric power steering gear. A 2018 ProMaster City rack casting defect and a 2023-2024 steering column control module fault round out the group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Inconsistent steering effort, especially during lower speed maneuvers, from an intermittent loss of power steering assist — the 2019 Ram 1500 electric power steering gear recall.
  • Varying steering effort, possibly causing oversteer or understeer — the 2019 Ram 1500 battery ground fastener recall.
  • Additional sudden steering effort after a hydraulic steering fluid leak — the 2018 Ram ProMaster City rack and pinion recall.
  • High beams activating when the turn signal is used, the turn signal activating when the high beams are used, or the turn signal not self-canceling — the 2023-2024 steering column control module recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. The two drag link recalls — Chrysler's on 2013-2018 Ram 3500, 2014-2018 3500 Chassis Cab and Ram 2500, and Streetcar ORV's on AEV suspension kits for 2013-2018 Ram 2500 and 3500HD — describe a loss of steering control. The 2019 Ram 1500 and Jeep Wrangler stub shaft notice describes the steering wheel detaching from the steering column. The notices state those risks and the repair dealers will make, and say nothing else about driving before the repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers check the steering linkage jam nut, replacing the drag link assembly or welding the nut to the adjuster sleeve; inspect the 2019 stub shaft column and replace as needed; secure the battery ground fastener; replace the EPS gear assembly; replace the ProMaster City rack-and-pinion; install a new steering knuckle on AEV kit trucks; and update the steering column control module software, replacing the module if necessary. Free of charge.

Common questions

Why does an aftermarket suspension kit appear in Ram steering recalls?

Streetcar ORV recalled AEV 3-inch Dualsport kits, part numbers N0436000 and N0436001, sold for installation on 2013-2018 Ram 2500 and 3500HD trucks. The notice says improper installation and machining of the original steering knuckle may weaken the drag link joint; dealers install a new knuckle free of charge.

I own a 2019 Ram 1500 — which steering recalls could apply to it?

Three notices name it: the improperly machined steering column stub shaft that may break, the driver-side battery ground fastener that may be improperly secured, and the contaminated electric power steering gear. Each has its own remedy, so check your VIN against all three.

My turn signal switches on the high beams — is that covered?

That matches the steering column control module recall on 2023 Ram 1500 Classic and 3500 Classic and 2023-2024 Ram 2500, 3500 and 4500/5500 Cab Chassis. The module may activate the high beams when the turn signal is used, or the reverse.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
