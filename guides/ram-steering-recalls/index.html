Why do Ram trucks and vans keep getting steering recalls?

8 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 29, 2026

Eight steering recalls cover Ram pickups and vans. The drag link appears twice: Chrysler recalls 2013-2018 Ram 3500 and 2014-2018 Ram 3500 Chassis Cab and Ram 2500 trucks for a loosening outboard steering linkage jam nut, while Streetcar recalls AEV 3-inch Dualsport suspension kits sold for 2013-2018 RAM 2500 and 3500HD. Power steering assist is the other theme: the 2019 Ram 1500 appears twice, once for a battery ground fastener and once for a contaminated electric power steering gear, and 2022-2026 Ram ProMaster vans and 2024-2026 Ram 3500 EVs are recalled for water entering the EPS wiring harness. The 2019 Ram 1500 appears in a steering column stub shaft recall with the Jeep Wrangler, and 2023-2024 Ram 2500, 3500 and 4500/5500 models in a steering column control module recall.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2019 Ram 1500 electric power steering gear recall: an intermittent loss of power steering assist may cause inconsistent steering effort, especially during lower-speed maneuvers.
  • 2019 Ram 1500 battery ground fastener recall: a loss of power steering could vary the steering effort required, possibly causing oversteer or understeer.
  • 2018 RAM ProMaster City recall: a hydraulic steering fluid leak may require additional sudden steering effort.
  • 2023-2024 Ram steering column control module recall: high beams may activate when the turn signal is used, the turn signal may activate when the high beams are used, and the turn signal may not self-cancel.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these eight notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The drag link, steering knuckle, steering column stub shaft, battery ground fastener, electric power steering gear, ProMaster City steering rack and steering column control module notices each state the defect, the risk and the dealer repair without any instruction about driving. For the Ram ProMaster power steering wiring harness recall, Chrysler says interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk are expected to be mailed November 17, 2026, and additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available.

What the free repair involves

Each repair is free. Dealers check the drag link jam nut and replace the drag link or weld the nut to the adjuster sleeve; install a new steering knuckle; inspect the steering column, replacing it as needed; secure the battery ground fastener; replace the EPS gear assembly; replace ProMaster harness and EPS gear parts as needed; replace the ProMaster City rack-and-pinion assembly; and update or replace the SCCM module.

Common questions

Is the Ram drag link recall the same as the AEV suspension kit recall?

They are separate notices. Chrysler's covers a loosening outboard steering linkage jam nut on 2013-2018 Ram 3500 and 2014-2018 Ram 3500 Chassis Cab and Ram 2500 trucks. Streetcar's covers AEV 3-inch Dualsport kits sold for 2013-2018 RAM 2500 and 3500HD trucks.

What do the notices say about driving my 2019 Ram 1500 before the power steering repair?

Neither the battery ground fastener notice nor the electric power steering gear notice tells owners to stop driving. Both describe a loss of power steering assist, the crash risk, and a free dealer repair, and list FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403.

Can my Ram ProMaster power steering recall be repaired now?

The notice says dealers will inspect and replace the wiring harness and electronic power steering gear, as necessary, free of charge. Interim letters are expected to be mailed November 17, 2026, with additional letters once the final remedy is available.
