Honda electrical recalls: wiring, software, and battery problems explained

12 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-23

Across these 12 Honda electrical recalls, software errors are the most frequent cause: the Accord and Insight (2018-2020) can lose communication between the body control module and wipers, defroster, camera, and lights; the Accord Hybrid, CR-V Hybrid, and Insight (2020-2021) can lose drive power from a DC-DC converter shutdown; and the 2023-2025 Accord Hybrid can lose power from an ICM software reset. Wiring harness damage repeats on motorcycles - the CRF250L, CRF450L, CRF1100, and CBR650R can each lose lights, horn, or instrument functions. The CR-V Hybrid also appears twice for separate battery issues: a 2023 manufacturing defect that can spark, and a 2020-2022 missing fuse that can let a battery cable short or overheat.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Windshield wipers, defroster, rearview camera, or exterior lights stop working (Accord, Insight)
  • Loss of drive power while driving (Accord Hybrid, CR-V Hybrid, and Insight hybrid models)
  • Horn stops working or headlight won't switch to high beam (CRF1100)
  • Turn signals, brake light, speedometer, and horn all fail together (CBR650R)
  • Rearview camera image is delayed or doesn't display (Pilot, Odyssey, Passport)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the 12 recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the third-row outlet wiring, CR-V Hybrid battery, motorcycle wiring harness, and software-related recalls, Honda's notices direct owners to have a dealer make the repair but do not instruct owners to stop driving in the meantime. The notices do not state that any of these vehicles are safe to drive; they simply do not include a stop-driving or park-outside warning for any of the 12 recalls.

What the free repair involves

Depending on the recall, Honda dealers inspect and repair or replace wiring harnesses and connectors, replace the CR-V Hybrid's battery module or 12-volt battery cable, replace horn or switch assemblies on the affected motorcycles, or reprogram the body control module, power converter, or ICM software - in one case downloadable by the owner. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Do I need to stop driving my Honda for these electrical recalls?

No. None of these 12 recall notices include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Honda asks owners to schedule a free dealer repair, but the notices do not tell owners to stop driving while waiting for the fix.

Why do so many Honda electrical recalls involve software?

Several recalls in this group trace back to software errors - in the body control module of the Accord and Insight, the power converter of hybrid models, and the ICM of the 2023-2025 Accord Hybrid - each causing lost communication or a loss of drive power.

Are Honda motorcycles affected by these electrical recalls?

Yes. The CRF250L, CRF450L, CRF1100, and CBR650R motorcycles are each covered by a separate wiring harness recall in this group, which can cause loss of the horn, lights, high-beam switch, turn signals, or instrument panel.
