Why are Cadillac driver's seats and child seat anchors recalled?

3 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Seats · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-03-27

Cadillac's seat recalls cover three unrelated issues. The newest and most serious involves a driver's seat cushion frame with an improper weld in the power tilt-adjustment mechanism on the 2022 XT4, alongside the 2022 Equinox, Blazer and 2021 Malibu. Earlier, 2016-2018 CT6 vehicles were recalled because excess structural adhesive on the inboard child seat anchor bars could block a child seat from attaching correctly. Most recently, GM recalled 2025 Lyriq vehicles not for a hardware defect but because the owner's manual inaccurately describes how the rear outboard head restraints work, which could lead an owner to adjust them incorrectly.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Driver's seat cushion frame may feel loose or move unexpectedly at the power tilt-adjustment area (2022 XT4)
  • A child seat's anchor bar connector may not attach correctly to the inboard anchorage points (2016-2018 CT6)
  • No physical warning sign for the Lyriq recall — it corrects incorrect head restraint instructions in the owner's manual, not a hardware defect

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. The notices do not describe a stop-driving or park-outside instruction for the 2022 XT4 driver's seat weld recall, the 2016-2018 CT6 child seat anchor recall, or the 2025 Lyriq owner's manual recall. For the Lyriq, the fix is a corrected manual insert rather than a vehicle repair, so there is no repair-related driving restriction described either.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the driver's seat and replace the cushion frame if the weld is faulty (XT4), inspect and clean the child seat anchor bars of excess adhesive (CT6), or simply mail owners a corrected owner's manual insert describing the head restraints (Lyriq). All of these are done free of charge.

Common questions

Is the Cadillac Lyriq head restraint recall a physical repair?

No. GM mails owners a corrected owner's manual insert with accurate head restraint illustrations and descriptions; there is no part replacement involved in this recall.

Can I still install a child seat in my recalled CT6?

The notice doesn't say installation is impossible, only that excess adhesive on the anchor bars can interfere with attaching a child seat; a dealer will remove the excess adhesive free of charge.

What's wrong with the driver's seat in the recalled XT4?

The seat cushion frame may have an improperly formed weld in the power tilt-adjustment mechanism, which the notice says may not adequately restrain the driver in a crash.
