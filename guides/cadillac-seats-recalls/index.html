Cadillac seat recalls cover welds, child seat anchors and head restraints

3 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Seats · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 27, 2025

Three recalls touch Cadillac seats, and they have little in common beyond the seat itself. The 2022 XT4 shares a recall with Chevrolet Malibu, Equinox and Blazer for a driver's seat cushion frame with an improper weld in the power tilt-adjustment mechanism. The 2016-2018 CT6 recall is a compliance issue: excess structural adhesive on the inboard child seat anchorage bars can interfere with installing a child seat, failing FMVSS 225. The 2025 Lyriq recall is a paperwork problem, the owner's manual inaccurately describes the rear outboard head restraints, failing FMVSS 202. Two of the three, the CT6 and Lyriq, are about how child seats and head restraints are secured or adjusted rather than a broken part.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The XT4 notice says an improperly welded seat frame may not adequately restrain the driver in a crash. The CT6 notice says a child seat that cannot be attached correctly to the anchorage bar increases injury risk. The Lyriq notice says incorrect head restraint information may lead to improperly adjusted head restraints. None of the three gives owners any instruction about driving before the repair.

What the free repair involves

The three remedies are different. For the 2022 XT4, dealers inspect the driver's seat and replace the cushion frame as necessary. For the 2016-2018 CT6, dealers inspect the inboard child seat anchorage bars, remove excess adhesive and apply anti-corrosion primer. For the 2025 Lyriq, GM mails an owner's manual insert with corrected illustrations and descriptions. All three notices state the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Is a recall about the owner's manual really a safety recall?

Yes. The 2025 Lyriq is recalled because the owner's manual inaccurately describes the rear outboard head restraints, so the vehicle fails FMVSS 202. The notice says incorrect information may lead to improperly adjusted head restraints and increase injury risk in a crash.

Can I still install a child seat in my CT6?

The notice does not tell owners to stop using the anchorage bars. It says excess structural adhesive on the inboard child seat anchorage bars can interfere with installing a child seat, and that a child seat not attached correctly increases injury risk. Dealers remove the adhesive free of charge.

Does the XT4 seat recall affect every 2022 XT4?

The notice covers certain 2022 Cadillac XT4 vehicles, along with 2021 Chevrolet Malibu and 2022 Equinox and Blazer, whose driver's seat cushion frame may have an improper weld. Dealers inspect and replace the frame as necessary, free. Owner letters were mailed June 23, 2022.
