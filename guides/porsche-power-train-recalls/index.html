Porsche powertrain recalls: loose bolts, fluid leaks and blank screens

4 NHTSA recalls · Porsche · Power Train · model years 2020–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-06-08

Porsche's four power-train recalls split between fasteners that left the factory under-tightened and two one-off defects. Two involve 911s: the front axle differential bracket on the 2020 911 Carrera 4S Coupe may not have been tightened correctly, and the bolts connecting the driveshaft to the wheel hub on the 2019 911 GT2 RS and 2020 911 Carrera S Coupe may be loose. The 2020 Cayenne has a different cause — an insufficient weld on a transmission oil pipe that can leak automatic transmission fluid onto the road. The fourth is pure software: 2020-2022 Taycans may start with the center screen, touch control panel and rearview camera image black. The 911 is the model that shows up most in this group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Automatic transmission fluid puddles or drips under a 2020 Cayenne
  • A fuel smell or signs of damage underneath if the 911's front differential works loose
  • The Taycan's center screen and touch control panel stay black right after the ignition is switched on
  • No rearview camera image when you select reverse in an affected Taycan
  • The two loose-fastener recalls describe no warning to the driver beforehand

Is it safe to keep driving?

No notice in this group tells owners to stop driving or park outside. The two 911 fastener recalls are the urgent ones: a loose front differential can damage surrounding components and cause a fuel leak and fire, and loose driveshaft bolts raise crash risk, so book those promptly. On the 2020 Cayenne, watch for fluid under the car — leaked transmission fluid can make the road slippery for you and others. The Taycan screen fault is the mildest; drive normally, but turn and look behind you when reversing instead of trusting the camera.

What the free repair involves

On the 2020 911 Carrera 4S, dealers remove the front axle differential to check the screw threads and inspect surrounding components for damage, with parts replaced depending on what they find. The driveshaft recall is a proper retightening of the connection to the wheel hub. Cayenne transmission oil pipes are inspected and replaced if necessary, and affected Taycans get new software installed at the dealer. Free of charge.

Common questions

Can I rely on my Taycan's backup camera until the software is updated?

No. The recall covers a software error that can leave the center screen, touch control panel and rearview camera image black right after startup. Until dealers install the new software, check over your shoulder and use your mirrors when reversing rather than the screen.

What if the 911 differential inspection finds damage?

Porsche's remedy is to remove the front axle differential, check the screw threads and inspect surrounding components for consequential damage. Depending on the inspection result, further parts work follows. Everything, including the inspection, is done free of charge at the dealer.

How do I tell transmission fluid from other leaks under my Cayenne?

The 2020 Cayenne recall involves an insufficient weld on a transmission oil pipe that can leak automatic transmission fluid. Any fresh puddle under the car is worth reporting to a dealer, since the notice warns the spilled fluid can make the road slippery.
