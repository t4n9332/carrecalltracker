What's going wrong in Porsche's power train recalls?

4 NHTSA recalls · Porsche · Power Train · model years 2020–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-06-08

These four Porsche power train recalls involve unrelated defects rather than one repeating cause. A 2020 911 Carrera S Coupe (also covering the 2019 911 GT2 RS) recall covers driveshaft-to-wheel-hub bolts that may not have been tightened properly. A separate 2020 911 Carrera 4S Coupe recall covers a front axle differential bracket that may not be tightened correctly and could damage nearby components. The 2020-2022 Taycan is covered by a recall for a software error that can make the center screen, touch panel, and rearview camera image go black or flicker after startup. A 2020 Cayenne recall covers a transmission oil pipe with an insufficient weld that may leak transmission fluid.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A blank or flickering center screen, touch panel, or rearview camera image right after starting the vehicle (2020-2022 Taycan)
  • Transmission fluid leaking from the vehicle, which the notice says can make the road surface slippery (2020 Cayenne)
  • The notices do not describe a noticeable warning sign for the loose driveshaft-to-wheel-hub bolts (2019 911 GT2 RS/2020 911 Carrera S) or the loose front differential bracket (2020 911 Carrera 4S Coupe).

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recall notices carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the driveshaft bolt and differential bracket recalls, the notices describe an increased risk of crash or component damage but do not tell owners to stop driving. The Taycan screen recall notes that a black or flickering display can block the driver's view of camera-assisted functions, and the Cayenne recall notes that leaking transmission fluid can make the road surface slippery — in both cases, the notices do not instruct owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers tighten or replace the driveshaft-to-wheel-hub bolts, and inspect and, if needed, reinstall or replace the front differential and its screw joints. For the Taycan, dealers install new software for the affected screen and camera systems. For the Cayenne, dealers inspect the transmission oil pipe and replace it if necessary. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Do I need to stop driving because of these Porsche power train recalls?

No. None of the four recall notices in this group tell owners to stop driving or park outside. Each notice describes a defect and directs owners to schedule a free repair at a Porsche dealer.

Why does my Taycan's screen go black after I start the car?

A 2020-2022 Taycan recall covers a software error that can make the center screen, touch panel, and rearview camera image go black or flicker right after the ignition is turned on; dealers install new software to fix it.

Are the loose bolt recalls related to each other?

No. The 2019-2020 911 driveshaft bolt recall and the 2020 911 Carrera 4S differential bracket recall are separate issues on different components, even though both involve fasteners that weren't tightened properly during assembly.
