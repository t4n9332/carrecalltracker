What are the Porsche 911, Taycan and Cayenne power train recalls?

4 NHTSA recalls · Porsche · Power Train · model years 2020–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 8, 2022

Two of the four recalls here are 911 fastener problems from 2019 and 2020: bolts connecting the driveshaft to the wheel hub on 2019 911 GT2 RS and 2020 911 Carrera S Coupe vehicles may not have been properly tightened, and the front axle differential bracket on 2020 911 Carrera 4S Coupe vehicles may not have been tightened correctly, which the notice says could damage surrounding components such as the fuel tank or drive shafts. The remaining two cover a software error that can leave the center screen and rearview camera image black or flickering on 2020-2022 Taycan vehicles, and an insufficient weld on a transmission oil pipe that may let 2020 Cayenne vehicles leak automatic transmission fluid.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2020-2022 Taycan recall states the center screen, including the rearview camera image, and the touch control panel may remain black directly after the ignition is activated, and that the Real-Top-View image could flicker.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside. The 2019-2020 911 driveshaft bolt recall, the 2020 911 Carrera 4S front axle differential bracket recall, the 2020-2022 Taycan center screen recall and the 2020 Cayenne transmission oil pipe recall each describe the defect, the risk and the dealer repair, and none of them gives owners an interim instruction to follow before the repair is carried out.

What the free repair involves

Dealers tighten the driveshaft connection to the wheel hub on the 911 recall, free of charge. For the front axle differential recall, the differential is removed so the screw threads can be checked and surrounding components inspected, then reinstalled or replaced using new screw joints. Taycan owners get new software installed, and Cayenne owners get the transmission oil pipe inspected and replaced if necessary, both free of charge.

Common questions

What does the Taycan black screen recall involve?

The notice states a software error could leave the center screen, including the rearview camera image, and the touch control panel black after the ignition is activated, and that the Real-Top-View image could flicker. Dealers install new software free of charge.

Why is a loose 911 differential bracket described as a fire risk?

The notice states that if the front axle differential bracket comes loose, the differential could damage surrounding components such as the fuel tank or drive shafts, and that damage to the fuel tank can result in fuel leakage, which near an ignition source may increase fire risk.

Which 911 models are in the two fastener recalls?

The driveshaft bolt recall covers 2019 911 GT2 RS and 2020 911 Carrera S Coupe vehicles. The front axle differential bracket recall covers 2020 911 Carrera 4S Coupe vehicles. The notices describe each as a tightening problem and do not tell owners to stop driving.
