Toyota stability control recalls: when VSC can switch itself off

3 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Electronic Stability Control (esc) · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 13, 2022

All three of these recalls cite Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 126, and each takes the stability control system out in a different way. Hybrids dominate: a brake booster pump that may fail on 2019 Prius, 2019 RAV4 Hybrid, 2019-2020 Prius Prime and 2020 Corolla Hybrid, deactivating Vehicle Stability Control and causing a loss of braking assist; and Skid Control ECU software on 2020-2022 Highlander Hybrid, 2021-2022 Mirai, RAV4 Prime, Sienna and Venza and 2022 RAV4 Hybrid, which may not turn VSC back on when the vehicle is restarted after the driver disabled it. The outlier is 2018 Tundra and Sequoia, where electrical interference within the power supply circuit may deactivate the system.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A loss of braking assist alongside deactivation of the Vehicle Stability Control system, in the brake booster pump recall covering 2019 Prius, RAV4 Hybrid, 2019-2020 Prius Prime and 2020 Corolla Hybrid.
  • Vehicle Stability Control not turning on when the vehicle is restarted after the driver has disabled it, in the Skid Control ECU software recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none carries a park-outside or other interim instruction. The brake booster pump notice says only that deactivation of the stability control system or a loss of braking assist increases the risk of a crash. The Skid Control ECU notice and the 2018 Tundra and Sequoia notice likewise describe an increased risk of a crash when the stability control system is deactivated, without giving owners any instruction about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

Two of the three are software. Dealers update the Skid Control ECU software on the 2020-2022 Highlander Hybrid, Mirai, RAV4 Prime, Sienna, Venza and RAV4 Hybrid group, and update the vehicle's software on 2018 Tundra and Sequoia. For the brake booster pump recall, dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace the brake booster pump. All of this work is free of charge.

Common questions

If I turn stability control back on myself, is the software problem handled?

The Skid Control ECU notice says the software may not turn Vehicle Stability Control on when the vehicle is restarted after the driver disabled it. It describes no owner action as the remedy — dealers update the Skid Control ECU software free of charge.

Is the 2018 Tundra recall the same problem as the hybrid ones?

The notices describe different causes. On 2018 Tundra and Sequoia, electrical interference within the power supply circuit may deactivate stability control. The hybrid recalls involve a brake booster pump that may fail and Skid Control ECU software. Each has its own remedy.

What exactly does the brake booster pump recall affect?

The notice says the brake booster pump may fail, causing deactivation of the Vehicle Stability Control system and a loss of braking assist, and that the vehicles do not comply with FMVSS 126. Dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace the pump free of charge.
