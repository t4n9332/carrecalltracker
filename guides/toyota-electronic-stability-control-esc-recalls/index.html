Why does Toyota stability control turn off in some hybrids?

3 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Electronic Stability Control (esc) · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-04-13

Toyota's stability-control recalls in this group center on the Vehicle Stability Control (VSC) system failing to stay active. A 2019 brake booster pump defect in the Prius, Prius Prime, RAV4 Hybrid and Corolla Hybrid can deactivate VSC and reduce brake assist. A 2020-2022 Skid Control ECU software error affects hybrid and electric models including the Highlander Hybrid, Mirai, RAV4 Prime, Sienna, Sienna Hybrid and Venza Hybrid, leaving VSC off after the driver disables it and the vehicle restarts. Separately, a 2018 electrical interference issue in the Tundra and Sequoia can deactivate stability control. All three recalls affect Toyota's FMVSS 126 compliance.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Reduced brake assist, requiring harder pedal pressure, in the 2019 brake booster pump recall
  • Stability control (VSC) not turning back on after being manually switched off and the vehicle restarted
  • The notices describe no specific driver-noticeable warning before stability control deactivates in the 2018 Tundra/Sequoia recall

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the three recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2019 brake booster pump recall covering the Prius, Prius Prime, RAV4 Hybrid and Corolla Hybrid, the 2020-2022 Skid Control ECU software recall covering the Highlander Hybrid, Mirai, RAV4 Prime, Sienna, Sienna Hybrid and Venza Hybrid, and the 2018 electrical interference recall covering the Tundra and Sequoia all describe a risk of losing stability control assistance, but none of the notices tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Toyota dealers address these recalls by inspecting and, if needed, replacing the brake booster pump, or updating the Skid Control ECU or vehicle software so stability control activates and stays on as intended. All three recalls describe these repairs and software updates as performed free of charge to the owner.

Common questions

What does it mean if Toyota's stability control is deactivated?

The notices describe stability control turning off or staying off due to a hardware or software fault, which the notices say increases the risk of a crash, particularly during driving conditions that would normally rely on the system.

Which Toyota and Lexus hybrid models are covered by these recalls?

Depending on the recall, affected vehicles include the Prius, Prius Prime, RAV4 Hybrid, Corolla Hybrid, Highlander Hybrid, Mirai, RAV4 Prime, Sienna, Sienna Hybrid, Venza Hybrid, Tundra and Sequoia — no single recall covers all of them.

Is there a cost to fix a stability control recall?

No. Each of the three notices states that Toyota dealers will inspect, repair, or update the relevant software free of charge, and owners are notified by mail once the remedy is available.
