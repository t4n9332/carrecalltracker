Why do Chevrolet trucks have brake fire and stopping-power recalls?

17 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-11-06

⚠ 3 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Two separate patterns run through this group. Three recalls - the 2021 Silverado 2500/3500 and Sierra 2500/3500, the 2019-2023 Chevrolet 4500HD-6500HD medium-duty trucks, and the 2019-2024 Silverado Medium Duty 4500HD-6500HD - involve water or brake fluid reaching an electrical component and creating a fire risk, and all three tell owners to park outside. The rest reduce braking performance or cause unexpected behavior: rear caliper coating or lubricant problems that drag or overheat brakes (2018-2019 Equinox, Impala, Cruze, Volt, Bolt EV, Terrain, Malibu, Lacrosse, Regal, XTS), EBCM software errors that cut brake assist or hide ESC/ABS warnings (Silverado, Sierra, Tahoe, Yukon, CT6), and caliper bolt or casting defects (Silverado, Sierra, Bolt EV).

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brakes that feel like they're dragging, or take longer to stop (2020 Equinox rear caliper lubricant issue; 2018 Silverado, Tahoe, Sierra, Yukon vacuum-assist issue)
  • The vehicle pulling to one side unexpectedly when braking (2015-2020 Silverado 1500, Sierra 1500, Suburban, Tahoe, Yukon)
  • No dashboard warning light for a stability-control or anti-lock brake problem, even when one exists (2019 Cadillac CT6, Silverado 1500, Sierra 1500)
  • The notices describe no warning sign at all for the three fire-risk brake recalls, which is why owners are told to park outside instead of watching for symptoms

Is it safe to keep driving?

Three recalls tell owners to park outside and away from other structures until repaired, because water or brake fluid reaching an electrical component increases the risk of fire: the 2021 Silverado 2500/3500 and Sierra 2500/3500 brake module recall, the 2019-2023 Chevrolet 4500HD, 5500HD, and 6500HD medium-duty truck recall, and the 2019-2024 Silverado Medium Duty 4500HD-6500HD recall. None of the recalls in this group carry a stop-driving instruction, and for every recall besides those three, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace or reprogram the affected part depending on the recall: brake pressure sensors, switches, or wire harnesses on the fire-risk trucks, rear brake calipers on the Equinox, caliper bolts on the Silverado and Sierra, a fractured caliper on the Bolt EV, or a reprogrammed Electronic Brake Control Module on the software-related recalls - all free of charge.

Common questions

Which Chevrolet brake recalls have a fire risk?

Three do: the 2021 Silverado 2500/3500 and Sierra 2500/3500 brake module recall, the 2019-2023 Chevrolet 4500HD-6500HD medium-duty truck recall, and the 2019-2024 Silverado Medium Duty 4500HD-6500HD recall. All three tell owners to park outside and away from structures until fixed.

Why didn't my dashboard warning light come on for a brake problem?

A 2019 recall found that a software error in the Electronic Brake Control Module on the Cadillac CT6, Chevrolet Silverado 1500, and GMC Sierra 1500 could disable stability control and anti-lock brakes without turning on the dashboard warning light.

Is the brake recall repair free?

Yes. GM says dealers will replace or reprogram the affected brake part - a sensor, caliper, or control module - at no cost to the owner, and owners are notified by mail when parts or software are ready.
