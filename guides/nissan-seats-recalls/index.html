Why do Nissan seats and headrests keep getting recalled?

6 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Seats · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 11, 2025

Six Nissan seat recalls cluster around welds and fasteners that hold seats and restraints in place. Welds recur most: third-row headrests on the 2022 Pathfinder, second-row seat back frames on the same 2022 Pathfinder, second-row upper tether wires on the 2021 Rogue, and driver's powered seat welds on the 2023 Rogue, Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60. Fasteners account for the rest — a missing second-row seat track mounting bolt on the 2022 Pathfinder and 2022 Infiniti QX60, and a driver's seat frame not fully secured to the seat track on the 2025 Sentra. Pathfinder appears in four of the six, and four notices cite a failure to comply with a Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park the vehicle outside. Each notice describes the risk as one that arises in a crash — a headrest, seat back, child seat anchor, seat track or seat frame that may not adequately restrain an occupant — and none of them gives an interim instruction to follow before the repair. The notices direct owners to a dealer for the recall repair and to Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 to confirm whether a vehicle is included.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy is free of charge. Dealers replace all three third-row headrests on the 2022 Pathfinder, inspect the second-row seat track and install a mounting bolt as needed on the 2022 Pathfinder and QX60, inspect and replace 2021 Rogue rear seatbacks and 2022 Pathfinder seat assemblies as needed, replace the 2023 driver's seat cushion frame if needed, and replace the 2025 Sentra's lower seat frame assembly as needed.

Common questions

Which Nissan and Infiniti models are covered by these seat recalls?

The 2022 Pathfinder (headrests, seat back welds, seat track bolt), the 2022 Infiniti QX60 (seat track bolt), the 2021 Rogue (child seat anchor), the 2023 Rogue, Pathfinder and QX60 (driver's seat welds), and the 2025 Sentra (driver's seat frame).

Is the 2021 Rogue child seat anchor recall the same problem as the seat weld recalls?

It is a separate recall. The notice says the 2021 Rogue's second-row upper tether wires may have been improperly welded to the seatback frame, preventing a child seat from being securely anchored, a failure to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 225.

My 2022 Pathfinder shows more than one seat recall — why?

Three separate notices cover 2022 Pathfinder seats: third-row headrests and second-row seat back welds, with letters mailed November 17, 2021, and a missing second-row seat track bolt, with letters mailed April 13, 2022. Each has its own repair.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
