Nissan Seat Recalls Involving Loose Frames, Headrests and Anchors

7 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Seats · model years 2021–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 13, 2026

Seat attachment and weld problems recur across these Nissan notices, with the 2022 Pathfinder appearing in three recalls: third-row headrests that may move or detach, a potentially missing second-row seat track bolt, and insufficient second-row seat back frame welds. Certain 2023 Pathfinder and Rogue vehicles have a separate driver's powered seat frame attachment problem. Rogue also appears in a 2021 recall concerning second-row upper tether wires for child seats. Certain 2025 Sentra vehicles have a driver's seat frame attachment defect, while a 2026 recall covers insufficiently welded front seat frame hook brackets on certain Kicks and Sentra vehicles. The stated risks concern occupant restraint.

Warning signs to watch for

  • PC844: third-row Pathfinder headrests may move or detach.
  • PMA64: front seat frames may become loose.
  • PMA45: the driver's seat frame may not be fully secured to the outer side of the seat track.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. That includes PC844 for Pathfinder headrests, PC867/PC868 for a second-row seat track bolt, the April 2023 driver's powered seat weld recall, PMA45 for the Sentra driver's seat frame, and PMA64 for Kicks and Sentra front seat frames. PC812, concerning Rogue child seat tether anchors, and PC845/PC846/PC847, concerning Pathfinder second-row seat back welds, also do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Repairs are free. PC844 replaces three third-row headrests; PC867/PC868 inspects the second-row seat track and installs a bolt as necessary. The April 2023 recall checks driver's seat rail clearance and replaces the cushion frame if necessary. PMA45 inspects and replaces the lower frame as necessary; PMA64 replaces front seat assemblies. PC812 and PC845/PC846/PC847 inspect and replace rear seatbacks and seat assemblies, respectively, as necessary.

Common questions

Which seating areas are involved in the 2022 Pathfinder recalls?

PC844 covers the third-row headrests and replaces all three. PC867/PC868 concerns a potentially missing second-row seat track mounting bolt. PC845/PC846/PC847 concerns insufficient second-row seat back frame welds and specifies inspection and seat assembly replacement as necessary.

What does the Rogue child seat anchor recall repair?

PC812 covers certain 2021 Rogue vehicles whose second-row upper tether wires may be improperly welded to the seatback frame, preventing secure child seat anchoring. Dealers inspect and replace rear seatbacks as necessary, free of charge under this notice.

How do the Sentra seat repairs differ by recall?

PMA45 covers certain 2025 Sentra vehicles and specifies inspecting and replacing the driver's side lower seat frame assembly as necessary. PMA64 covers certain 2026 Sentra and Kicks vehicles and specifies replacing front seat assemblies. Both repairs are free.
