Which Lincoln engine recalls warn about fire and parking outside?

6 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Engine · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-09-19

⚠ 3 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Fire is the theme here. Two notices cover the battery junction box on the Navigator — 2020, where a short circuit can cause an engine fire while driving or parked, and 2021, where an engine compartment fire may occur while driving or parked even with the ignition in the "Off" position — and both tell owners to park outside. The 2015-2019 MKC adds a third, for a battery monitor sensor that may short-circuit and overheat. The 2.5L HEV and PHEV engines in the 2021-2023 Corsair are recalled twice, in 2023 and again in 2025 for vehicles repaired incorrectly under the earlier recall, because engine failure may release oil and fuel vapor into the engine compartment. Only the 2020-2021 Aviator motor mount recall is not about fire.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Unexpected engine noises, a reduction in vehicle power, or smoke — recall 23V380 covering 2021-2023 Corsair with 2.5L HEV or PHEV engines tells owners to park and shut off the engine as quickly as possible if they notice these, as does the 2025 recall for vehicles repaired incorrectly under it.
  • Loss of power — named in the 2020-2021 Aviator motor mount fastener recall (20S72).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Three of these six recalls tell owners to park outside: the 2021 Navigator engine compartment fire recall, the 2020 Navigator battery junction box recall, and the 2015-2019 MKC battery monitor sensor recall. The first two advise parking outside and away from structures and other vehicles until the remedy is performed; the MKC notice advises parking outside and away from structures until the repair is complete. None of the six tells owners to stop driving. The two Corsair 2.5L engine recalls advise parking and shutting off the engine as quickly as possible if owners hear unexpected engine noises, notice reduced power, or see smoke.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and replace the battery junction box and remove a ground wire on the 2020 and 2021 Navigator recalls, add an in-line fuse to the battery monitor sensor power circuit on the MKC, replace the engine long-block on 2023 Corsair or update the powertrain control module on other vehicles, and replace the right hand motor mount fasteners on the Aviator. Several notices say free of charge.

Common questions

Which Lincoln engine recalls tell owners to park outside?

Three do: the 2021 Navigator engine compartment fire recall, the 2020 Navigator battery junction box recall, and the 2015-2019 MKC battery monitor sensor recall. Each advises parking outside and away from structures until the recall repair is performed.

What does the Corsair engine fire recall actually cover?

Recall 23V380 covers 2021-2023 Corsair with 2.5L HEV or PHEV engines, where engine failure may release engine oil and fuel vapor into the engine compartment near hot components. Dealers replace the engine long-block on 2023 Corsair; other vehicles get a powertrain control module update.

Why was my Corsair recalled again in 2025 for the same thing?

The 2025 notice says it covers 2021-2023 Corsair vehicles that were repaired incorrectly under recall 23V380, and describes the same release of engine oil and fuel vapor. Dealers update the powertrain control module software, free of charge, with letters mailed June 27, 2025.
