Why Do Some Lincoln Engines And Batteries Catch Fire?

6 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Engine · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-09-19

⚠ 3 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

This group is dominated by fire risks tied to the battery junction box on the Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator, recalled twice - in 2022 and again in 2025 - for a short circuit that can start an engine fire while parked or driving, even with the ignition off. A separate but related pattern is a hybrid engine fire risk on the Escape, Maverick, and Corsair's 2.5L HEV or PHEV engines, where a failed engine can release oil and fuel vapor onto hot components; this was recalled in 2023 and reissued in 2025 after the first fix proved incomplete. The 2018-2019 Lincoln MKC has its own battery monitor sensor fire recall, and the 2020-2021 Explorer and Aviator share a loose motor mount recall.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Unusual engine noise, reduced power, or smoke (Escape/Maverick/Corsair hybrid fire recalls)
  • Engine compartment fire while parked or driving, even with the ignition off (Expedition/Navigator)
  • Sudden loss of engine power (Explorer/Aviator motor mount recall)
  • The notices describe no specific warning sign for the Lincoln MKC battery sensor short circuit

Is it safe to keep driving?

Three recalls in this group tell owners to park outside and away from structures until repaired: the 2022 Ford Expedition/Lincoln Navigator battery junction box fire recall, the 2023 Lincoln MKC battery sensor fire recall, and the 2025 Expedition/Navigator battery fire recall. Two more (the 2023 Escape/Maverick/Corsair hybrid engine fire recall and its 2025 reissue) instead tell owners to park and shut off the engine quickly if they notice unusual noise, reduced power, or smoke. The remaining motor mount recall does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and replace the battery junction box and remove a ground wire (and upgrade the radiator fan on some models); replace the engine long-block or update the powertrain control module software; add an in-line fuse to the battery monitor sensor; or replace the loosened motor mount fasteners. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Which Lincoln recalls tell owners to park outside?

Three do: the 2022 Expedition/Navigator battery junction box fire recall, the 2023 Lincoln MKC battery sensor fire recall, and the 2025 Expedition/Navigator battery fire recall. All three describe a short circuit that can start a fire while parked or driving.

What should I do if my Escape-based Lincoln Corsair engine sounds off?

The 2023 and 2025 hybrid engine fire recalls tell owners to park and shut off the engine as quickly as possible if they hear unexpected engine noises, notice reduced power, or see smoke, since oil and fuel vapor could reach hot components.

What caused the Lincoln Navigator engine fires?

A short circuit in the battery junction box. It was recalled for 2021 Expedition/Navigator vehicles in 2022 and again for 2020 Expedition/Navigator vehicles in 2025. Both notices say it can start a fire while parked or driving, even with the ignition off.
