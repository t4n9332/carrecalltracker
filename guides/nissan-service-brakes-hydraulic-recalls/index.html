Why do Nissan brake recalls involve fluid leaks and calipers?

5 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 19, 2025

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Nissan's hydraulic brake recalls split into two problems. Two of the five cover ABS actuator pumps that may allow brake fluid to leak onto an internal circuit board, with the consequence described as an electrical short and an increased risk of fire. The 2018 recall covers 2015-2017 Murano, 2016-2017 Maxima, 2017-2018 Pathfinder and 2017 Infiniti QX60; the 2019 recall covers 2015-2018 Murano, 2016-2018 Maxima and 2017-2019 Pathfinder and QX60, and its notice says it expands the previous population. The other three involve calipers: the 2021 Rogue's rear calipers appear twice, missing an internal bushing that allows the O-ring seal to move and fluid to leak, and 2025 Murano, Pathfinder and QX60 front calipers may have reduced strength and break.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An ABS warning light that stays on for more than 10 seconds after the engine starts — stated in the 2018 and 2019 ABS actuator brake fluid recalls.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two of the five notices carry a park-outside instruction: the 2018 ABS actuator recall and the 2019 ABS actuator recall that expands it. Both tell owners that if the ABS warning light stays on for more than 10 seconds after the engine starts, they should park the vehicle outdoors, away from other vehicles or structures, and not drive it. The three caliper recalls — the two covering 2021 Rogue rear calipers and the 2025 front caliper recall — do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

All repairs are free of charge. Under the 2018 recall, dealers inspect the serial number on the ABS actuator and replace it as necessary; under the 2019 recall dealers replace the ABS actuator, and owners receive a second notice when remedy parts become available. For the 2021 Rogue, dealers inspect and replace rear calipers as necessary; the 2023 recall replaces both rear calipers. The 2025 recall inspects and replaces front caliper assemblies as necessary.

Common questions

Why was the 2021 Rogue recalled twice for rear brake calipers?

Both notices describe caliper assemblies missing an internal bushing, allowing the O-ring seal to move and brake fluid to leak. The 2021 recall has dealers inspect and replace as necessary; the 2023 recall has dealers replace the left and right rear calipers.

Does the ABS actuator recall mean my brakes will fail?

The notices describe brake fluid leaking onto an internal circuit board, which may result in an electrical short, increasing the risk of a fire. They do not describe reduced braking. Dealers inspect or replace the ABS actuator free of charge.

What do the notices say to do if my ABS warning light is on?

The 2018 and 2019 ABS actuator notices tell owners that if the light stays on more than 10 seconds after the engine starts, they should park the vehicle outdoors away from other vehicles or structures and not drive it. Contact Nissan at 1-800-867-7669.
