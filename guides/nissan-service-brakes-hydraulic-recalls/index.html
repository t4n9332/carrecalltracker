Which Nissan Models Have Recalls For Leaking Brakes?

5 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-03-19

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Nissan's brake recalls split into two separate problems. From 2015 through 2019, the Murano, Maxima, Pathfinder, and Infiniti QX60 were covered by two recalls (2018 and 2019) for ABS actuator pumps that let brake fluid leak onto a circuit board, raising a fire risk; the 2019 recall expanded and replaced the 2018 one. Separately, the 2021 Rogue was recalled twice, in 2021 and again in 2023, because rear brake calipers built without an internal bushing can leak fluid and reduce braking. Most recently, a 2025 recall covers the Murano, Pathfinder, and QX60 for front brake calipers that may break.

Warning signs to watch for

  • ABS warning light stays on for more than 10 seconds after the engine starts (2018 and 2019 Murano, Maxima, Pathfinder and QX60 recalls)
  • The 2021 and 2023 Rogue rear brake caliper recalls describe no warning sign owners would notice
  • The 2025 Murano, Pathfinder and QX60 front caliper recall also describes no warning sign owners would notice

Is it safe to keep driving?

Two recalls — the September 2018 and November 2019 ABS actuator recalls covering the Murano, Maxima, Pathfinder and QX60 — tell owners to park the vehicle outdoors, away from other vehicles or structures, and not drive it if the ABS warning light stays on for more than 10 seconds after starting. Neither recall issues a stop-driving order otherwise. The 2021 and 2023 Rogue rear brake caliper recalls and the 2025 Murano, Pathfinder and QX60 front caliper recall do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the ABS actuator and replace it if the serial number is affected, or replace the actuator outright once parts become available. For the Rogue recalls, dealers inspect and replace the rear brake calipers as necessary. For the 2025 recall, dealers inspect and replace the front brake calipers as necessary. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Which Nissan and Infiniti models were recalled for the ABS brake fluid fire risk?

The 2015-2018 Murano, 2016-2018 Maxima, and 2017-2019 Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60 were covered across the 2018 and 2019 recalls for ABS actuators that can leak brake fluid onto a circuit board and cause a fire.

Is the Nissan Rogue rear brake caliper recall still active?

Nissan recalled the 2021 Rogue in 2021 for rear calipers missing an internal bushing, then issued a second recall in 2023 covering the same 2021 Rogue vehicles for the identical defect; dealers replace the left and right rear calipers free of charge.

What should I do if my Nissan's ABS warning light won't turn off?

The 2018 and 2019 recalls tell owners to park the vehicle outdoors, away from other vehicles or structures, and not drive it if the ABS light stays on more than 10 seconds after starting, then contact Nissan for repair.
