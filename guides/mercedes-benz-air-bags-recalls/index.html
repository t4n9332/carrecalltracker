Why is Mercedes-Benz recalling vehicles for air bag problems?

5 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2021-11-05

Mercedes-Benz's air bag recalls mostly trace back to wiring, sensors, and software rather than defective air bags themselves. Sprinter vans show up twice: 2018-2019 models where the air bag systems may never have been activated after production, and 2019-2020 Sprinter vans with swivel seats where misrouted front-seat wiring harnesses can be jammed and damaged, affecting the side air bag inside the seat. AMG models also appear twice, with 2021 CLS53, E53, and G63 vehicles having side-impact crash sensor connectors that can come loose, and a 2021 GLB35 having its driver-side window air bag mounted incorrectly at the A-pillar. A separate 2018-2019 GLC350e recall involves only the passenger air bag status display, not the hardware itself.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Passenger air bag status display may show 'ON' incorrectly (2018-2019 GLC350e)
  • 2018-2019 Sprinter vans: notice describes no owner-noticeable warning that the air bag system was never activated
  • 2021 AMG CLS53/E53/G63: notice describes no warning before the side-impact sensor connector comes loose
  • 2021 GLB35: notice describes no warning that the window air bag was mounted incorrectly
  • 2019-2020 Sprinter swivel-seat vans: notice describes no warning before the seat wiring is damaged

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five air bag recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving for the 2018-2019 GLC350e passenger air bag display issue, the 2018-2019 Sprinter air bag activation recall, the 2021 AMG CLS53/E53/G63 side-impact sensor recall, the 2021 GLB35 window air bag mounting recall, or the 2019-2020 Sprinter swivel-seat wiring recall.

What the free repair involves

Repairs vary by recall: dealers update the audio head unit software on the GLC350e, inspect and correct air bag activation on Sprinter vans, inspect and replace side-impact crash sensors on AMG models, inspect and correct the window air bag mounting on the GLB35, and inspect or repair seat wiring with added clips on Sprinter swivel-seat vans. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Do I need to stop driving my Mercedes-Benz because of an air bag recall?

No recall in this group tells owners to stop driving. The notices instead ask owners to bring the vehicle in for inspection, software updates, or wiring repairs, which dealers perform free of charge.

Which Mercedes-Benz models are affected by air bag recalls?

Recalls in this group cover the 2018-2019 GLC350e, 2018-2020 Sprinter vans, 2021 AMG CLS53, E53, and G63, and the 2021 GLB35, each affected by a different wiring, sensor, or software issue.

Will fixing an air bag recall cost me anything?

No. Each notice states that Mercedes-Benz dealers will perform the inspection, software update, or repair free of charge to the owner.
