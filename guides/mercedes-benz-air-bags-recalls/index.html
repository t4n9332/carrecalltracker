Which Mercedes-Benz air bag recalls affect your car, SUV or Sprinter van?

5 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 5, 2021

These five Mercedes-Benz air bag recalls share little mechanically. Two involve Sprinter vans: 2018-2019 Sprinters whose air bag systems may not have been activated after production, and Sprinter vans on platform 970 with swivel seats, where front seat wiring can be jammed and damaged so a seat side air bag may deploy inadvertently or not at all. The passenger car recalls are about sensing and mounting: an incorrect passenger air bag status display on 2018 C-Class and 2018-2019 GLC-Class models with Audio 20 head units, side crash sensor connectors that may come loose on 2021 E-Class, CLS and AMG GT models, 2020 S450, S560 and G550, and 2020-2021 GLC300 and AMG G63, and a driver-side window air bag mounted incorrectly on the 2021 GLB 250 and AMG GLB 35.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall 2018120009 (2018 C-Class, 2018-2019 GLC-Class with Audio 20 head units): the passenger air bag status may be displayed as 'ON' when the air bag will not deploy.
  • The notices for the other four recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices carries a stop-driving instruction, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. That covers the passenger air bag display recall on the C-Class and GLC-Class, the Sprinter air bag activation recall, the side crash sensor recall, the GLB window air bag recall and the Sprinter swivel seat wiring recall. What the notices do say is what each defect can mean in a crash — an air bag that may not deploy, may deploy inadvertently, or may be delayed — and that owners should have the recall work performed by a dealer.

What the free repair involves

Remedies differ. Dealers update the audio head unit software for the passenger air bag display; inspect Sprinters and correct the air bag system activation as necessary; inspect and replace side crash sensors as necessary; inspect the GLB driver-side window air bag mounting and repair as necessary; and inspect the swivel seat harness routing, repairing it and adding clips or cable ties. Each notice states the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Why does my Sprinter van appear in two different air bag recalls?

The notices cover different problems. One says 2018-2019 Sprinter air bag systems may not have been activated after vehicle production. The other covers vans with swivel seats, where front seat wiring may be jammed and damaged in the seat frames.

How do I know if the passenger air bag display recall applies to my car?

That recall covers 2018 C-Class and 2018-2019 GLC-Class models equipped with Audio 20 head units and automatic child seat recognition. The notice says the passenger air bag status may not display correctly, and dealers update the audio head unit software free of charge.

Do any of these Mercedes-Benz air bag notices tell owners to stop driving?

None of the five does, and none tells owners to park outside. The notices describe what may happen in a crash — an air bag that does not deploy, deploys inadvertently, or activates late — and direct owners to a dealer for the free repair.
