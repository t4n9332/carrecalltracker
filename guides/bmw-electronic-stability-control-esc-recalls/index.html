What do BMW's integrated brake system recalls mean for owners?

3 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Electronic Stability Control (esc) · model years 2023–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 2, 2024

All three of these BMW recalls involve the same part: the integrated brake system. The February 2024 and October 2024 notices both say the integrated brake (IB) system may malfunction and result in a loss of power brake assist, or keep the Antilock Brake System and Dynamic Stability Control from functioning properly. The September 2024 notice is more specific: welds in the servomotor of the integrated brake control module may break, causing a loss of power brake assist and rear brake function. The vehicle lists overlap heavily — X1, X5, X6, X7, XM, 530i and i5 appear in all three, MINI models in the February and September recalls — across 2023 to 2025 model years.

Warning signs to watch for

  • All three notices describe a loss of power brake assist, which they say can extend the distance required to stop the vehicle.
  • The February 2024 and October 2024 notices say the ABS and Dynamic Stability Control systems may not function properly.
  • The September 2024 notice says rear brake function may be lost along with power brake assist.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, and none tells owners to stop driving. What they state is the consequence: a loss of power brake assist can extend the distance required to stop the vehicle, and malfunctioning ABS or DSC systems can cause a loss of vehicle control, either of which can increase the risk of a crash. The September 2024 notice adds that a loss of rear brake function can also extend stopping distance, and that losing ABS or DSC can cause a loss of vehicle handling and control.

What the free repair involves

These recalls are parts replacements, not software. In the February 2024 and September 2024 recalls, dealers replace the integrated brake system free of charge; in the October 2024 recall, dealers replace the integrated brake module free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed for the first two recalls, and interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk were expected for the October 2024 recall, with second letters once parts are available.

Common questions

Is my BMW in more than one integrated brake recall?

Overlap is heavy. X1, X5, X6, X7, XM, 530i and i5 appear in all three notices across 2023-2025 model years, and MINI models appear in the February and September 2024 recalls. A VIN lookup shows which notices apply to your vehicle.

What is the integrated brake system BMW keeps recalling?

The notices call it the integrated brake (IB) system, or the integrated brake control module. When it malfunctions, the notices say power brake assist can be lost and the Antilock Brake and Dynamic Stability Control systems may not function properly.

Does BMW fix this with a software update?

These three notices all describe hardware work: dealers replace the integrated brake system or the integrated brake module, free of charge. None of them describes a software update or an over-the-air remedy for this defect.
