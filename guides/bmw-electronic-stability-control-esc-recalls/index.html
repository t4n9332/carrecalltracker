Why is my BMW's brake assist and stability control being recalled?

3 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Electronic Stability Control (esc) · model years 2023–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-10-02

All three of these BMW recalls involve the same integrated brake (IB) system component: it can malfunction and cause a loss of power brake assist, or make the Antilock Brake System and Dynamic Stability Control stop working correctly. The recalls span 2023-2025 X1, X5, X6, X7, XM, 530i, 540i, i5, and i7 models, plus several MINI Cooper and Countryman variants and one 2024 Rolls-Royce Spectre. The September 2024 recall traces the fault to welds inside the brake's servomotor breaking, while the February and October 2024 recalls describe the same integrated brake malfunction without specifying the servomotor weld as the cause.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Longer stopping distance if power brake assist is lost
  • Loss of ABS or Dynamic Stability Control function
  • Loss of rear brake function as well, in the September 2024 recall
  • The notices do not describe a dashboard warning or other sign that would alert the driver beforehand

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three brake system recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. Each notice describes the same risk — that a malfunctioning integrated brake system can cause loss of power brake assist, loss of rear brake function (in the September 2024 recall), or failure of the ABS and Dynamic Stability Control systems, any of which can extend stopping distance or cause a loss of vehicle control. The notices do not instruct owners to stop driving while awaiting the repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the integrated brake system or module free of charge in all three recalls. Because the replacement part wasn't immediately available for some vehicles, BMW mailed interim notification letters warning owners of the risk first, then sent a second letter once parts and the remedy were ready.

Common questions

What is the integrated brake system these BMW recalls mention?

It's the component that provides power brake assist and works with the Antilock Brake System (ABS) and Dynamic Stability Control (DSC). In these recalls, its servomotor welds or internal function can fail, weakening braking or stability control.

Which BMW models are affected by the brake recalls?

The three recalls cover 2023-2025 X1, X5, X6, X7, XM, 530i, 540i, i5, and i7, along with several MINI Cooper/Countryman variants and one 2024 Rolls-Royce Spectre model.

Do I need to stop driving until my BMW is fixed?

No recall in this group instructs owners to stop driving. The notices warn that brake assist, rear brakes, or stability control could malfunction, but they only describe the risk — they don't tell owners to park the vehicle.
