BMW Stability Control Recalls Also Affect Brake Power Assist

3 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Electronic Stability Control (esc) · model years 2023–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 2, 2024

BMW’s listed electronic stability control recalls center on integrated braking equipment that can affect power brake assist, ABS, and Dynamic Stability Control together. The February and October 2024 notices describe integrated brake system malfunctions, while the September 2024 notice identifies servomotor welds that may break and also affect rear brake function. X1 and X7 recur throughout the listed vehicles, with 2023 and 2024 models appearing repeatedly. X5, XM, and 530i also recur, and some notices extend to 2025 vehicles. The February and September notices include MINI vehicles. Remedies differ in wording, with integrated brake system replacement specified in those notices and module replacement specified in October.

Warning signs to watch for

  • February 12, 2024 integrated brake recall — loss of power assist may extend stopping distance, and ABS or DSC malfunction may cause loss of control.
  • September 18, 2024 brake weld recall — loss of power assist or rear brake function may extend stopping distance, and loss of ABS or DSC may affect handling and control.
  • October 2, 2024 integrated brake recall — loss of power assist may extend stopping distance, and ABS or DSC malfunction may cause loss of control.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The February 12, 2024 BMW, MINI, and Rolls-Royce integrated brake system notice does not tell owners to stop driving. The September 18, 2024 notice concerning servomotor welds, brake assist, rear brake function, ABS, and DSC also does not tell owners to stop driving. The October 2, 2024 BMW integrated brake system notice likewise does not tell owners to stop driving. None of these supplied notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction.

What the free repair involves

For the February 12 and September 18, 2024 recalls, dealers replace the integrated brake system free of charge. For the October 2, 2024 recall, dealers replace the integrated brake module free of charge. The October notice says second owner letters will be mailed once parts are available.

Common questions

Can these BMW stability control defects also affect braking?

The notices describe possible loss of power brake assist alongside ABS or DSC malfunction. The September 18, 2024 notice additionally identifies possible loss of rear brake function. The stated consequences include extended stopping distance and loss of vehicle handling or control.

What causes the problem in the September BMW brake recall?

The September 18, 2024 notice identifies welds in the integrated brake control module’s servomotor that may break. It describes potential loss of power brake assist and rear brake function, with ABS and DSC also affected. Dealers replace the integrated brake system free of charge.

What repair does the October BMW integrated brake notice specify?

The October 2, 2024 notice specifies replacement of the integrated brake module free of charge. It says interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk were expected in November 2024, with second letters to follow once parts are available.
