Why do Jeep Grand Cherokee and Compass have brake recalls?

4 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2020 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-11-07

These four recalls involve separate hydraulic brake defects. The 2018-2019 Grand Cherokee (along with the Dodge Durango) was recalled in 2022, and again in 2024 with an expanded remedy, because a faulty antilock-brake system module can incorrectly light the brake lights, disable ABS and stability control, and let the vehicle start and shift out of Park without the brake pedal applied, risking a rollaway. A 2018 recall covers the 2018 Compass, 2019 Compass, and 2019 Cherokee (along with Dodge Grand Caravan and Journey) for rear brake caliper pistons with insufficient coating that can reduce rear brake performance. Separately, the 2020 Jeep Renegade was recalled because its right rear brake caliper could have cracked during casting, which can reduce braking performance.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake lights that turn on by themselves without the brake pedal applied (Grand Cherokee/Durango ABS module)
  • Vehicle starts and shifts out of Park without the brake pedal applied, and can roll away on its own (Grand Cherokee/Durango)
  • Reduced rear braking performance (2018 Compass, 2019 Compass, 2019 Cherokee caliper coating issue)
  • Reduced braking performance from a cracked right rear brake caliper (2020 Renegade)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four brake recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. That includes the 2022 Grand Cherokee/Durango ABS module recall and its 2024 replacement with the expanded remedy, the 2018 rear brake caliper piston coating recall covering the Compass and Cherokee, and the 2019 Renegade cracked rear brake caliper recall. The notices describe risks of vehicle rollaway, reduced braking performance, and reduced handling and control, but in each case the notice does not instruct owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers address each defect at no cost: updating the ABS module software (and replacing the pressure transducer in the 2024 remedy) on the Grand Cherokee and Durango, bleeding the brake system on affected Compass and Cherokee models, and inspecting and replacing the right rear brake caliper on the Renegade as necessary. All repairs described in these notices are free to the owner.

Common questions

Can my Jeep Grand Cherokee roll away on its own?

The 2022 and 2024 recalls say a faulty ABS module on 2018-2019 Grand Cherokee and Durango models can let the vehicle start and shift out of Park without the brake pedal applied, risking a rollaway.

Why was my Jeep Compass's brakes recalled?

A 2018 recall covers 2018-2019 Compass and 2019 Cherokee models (along with two Dodge models) because rear brake caliper pistons had insufficient coating, which can create gas pockets and reduce rear brake performance.

What caused the Jeep Renegade brake caliper recall?

NHTSA's 2019 recall says the right rear brake caliper on 2020 Renegade models could have been fractured during the casting process, which can fail and reduce braking performance.
