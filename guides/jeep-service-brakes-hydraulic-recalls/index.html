Which Jeep brake and ABS recalls affect Grand Cherokee and Compass?

4 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2020 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 7, 2024

Jeep's hydraulic brake recalls split into two patterns. The antilock brake module on 2018-2019 Grand Cherokee accounts for two of the four notices, in June 2022 and November 2024; both describe an ABS module malfunction that can illuminate the brake lights and let the vehicle start and shift out of Park without the brake pedal applied, and the 2024 notice adds disabled ABS and electronic stability control. Dodge Durango of the same two years is covered alongside it. The other pattern is rear brake hardware: 2018 Compass and 2019 Cherokee were recalled over rear caliper pistons with insufficient coating that can reduce rear braking, and 2020 Renegade over a right rear caliper fractured during casting.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The June 2022 and November 2024 Grand Cherokee ABS module recalls say the module may illuminate the brake lights.
  • Those same two recalls say the vehicle may start and shift out of Park without the brake pedal being applied.
  • The November 2024 Grand Cherokee ABS recall also says the ABS and electronic stability control may be disabled.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2022 and 2024 Grand Cherokee ABS module recalls, the rear caliper piston recall covering 2018 Compass and 2019 Cherokee, and the 2020 Renegade right rear caliper recall all state only the safety risk - rollaway, reduced braking, disabled ABS and stability control - without telling owners to stop driving. The notices say nothing about what to do with the vehicle between the owner letter and the dealer visit, so the only action they describe is having the recall work done.

What the free repair involves

The remedies differ by recall. Dealers bleed the brake system for the rear caliper piston recall; inspect and, as necessary, replace the right rear caliper on the Renegade; and replace the integrated pressure transducer and update the ABS module software as necessary under the November 2024 Grand Cherokee recall. Those three notices say the work is free of charge. The 2022 notice describes updated HCU/ABS module software.

Common questions

My 2018 Grand Cherokee was already repaired under the 2022 ABS recall. Is that the end of it?

The 2022 notice says that recall is replaced by NHTSA recall number 24V838, and that vehicles already repaired under it will need the new remedy completed. Under the later recall dealers replace the integrated pressure transducer and update the ABS module software as necessary.

Which Jeep models are in the rear brake caliper recalls?

The caliper piston recall lists the 2018 and 2019 Jeep Compass and the 2019 Jeep Cherokee, along with Dodge Grand Caravan and Journey models. The fractured caliper recall covers only the 2020 Jeep Renegade. A VIN lookup on NHTSA's site confirms your vehicle.

What do these notices say about driving in the meantime?

None of the four tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. They describe the risks - rollaway, reduced rear braking performance, a cracked caliper that can fail - and direct owners to have a dealer complete the recall repair.
