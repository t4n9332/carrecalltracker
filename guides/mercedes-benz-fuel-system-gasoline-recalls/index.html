Mercedes-Benz fuel system recalls: van fuel leaks and fuel pump shutdowns

5 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 21, 2023

These Mercedes-Benz gasoline fuel system recalls fall into two families. Three are leaks on vans: 2019-2020 Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinters whose fuel line clamps may loosen, 2019 Sprinter platform 907 (VS30) vans that may have an open port on the fuel delivery module, and 2017-2022 Metris vans whose upper and lower fuel hose connections to the engine may be insecure. The other two are fuel pump shutdowns on passenger models: a 2023 campaign covering 2021-2023 C-Class, E-Class, S-Class, CLS, SL, GT, GLC, GLE, GLS and G-Class vehicles, and a later campaign covering 2021-2024 GLE, GLS, C-Class, E-Class, S-Class, SL, GLC, CLS, AMG GT and G-Class vehicles. In both, the fuel pump may shut down, causing a loss of drive power.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A fuel leak (2019-2020 Sprinter fuel line clamp recall and 2017-2022 Metris fuel hose recall).
  • Fuel leaking onto the roadway (2019 Sprinter VS30 fuel delivery module recall).
  • A loss of drive power (both fuel pump shutdown recalls).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none carries a park-outside instruction, including the two leak recalls that mention fire. The notices state consequences only: loosened fuel line clamps on 2019-2020 Sprinters can result in a fuel leak that can increase the risk of a fire; on the Metris, a fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase fire risk; an open port on the 2019 Sprinter fuel pump module may result in fuel leaking onto the roadway; and both fuel pump recalls describe a loss of drive power.

What the free repair involves

Work varies by recall. For 2019-2020 Sprinters, dealers check the fuel hose clamp connections and tighten or replace components as needed. For the Metris, dealers inspect and repair the fuel hose connections and replace clamps and fuel hose as necessary. For 2019 Sprinter VS30 vans, dealers check for the additional nozzle on the fuel pump and install a closure as needed. Both fuel pump recalls replace the fuel pump. Every notice states this is free of charge.

Common questions

Which Mercedes-Benz models are covered by the fuel pump shutdown recalls?

The 2023 recall lists 2021-2023 C-Class, E-Class, S-Class, CLS, SL, GT, GLC, GLE, GLS and G-Class vehicles. The later one lists 2021-2024 GLE, GLS, C-Class, E-Class, S-Class, SL, GLC, CLS, AMG GT and G-Class. Both refer owners to MBUSA's recall report for specific vehicle details.

How serious is the Sprinter and Metris fuel leak?

The Sprinter clamp notice says a fuel leak can increase the risk of a fire, and the Metris notice says a fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase fire risk. Neither tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. Dealers repair free of charge.

What does a dealer do about the fuel pump shutdown?

Both fuel pump recalls say dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge. The notices describe the defect only as the fuel pump shutting down, resulting in a loss of drive power, and neither tells owners to stop driving while they wait for the repair.
