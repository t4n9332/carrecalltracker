Why are Mercedes-Benz fuel lines leaking and fuel pumps shutting down?

5 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-12-21

This group covers two separate fuel-system problems. In Sprinter vans (2019-2020, models 1500/3500/4500) and the 2018 Metris, fuel line or hose clamps and connections may loosen, which the notices say can cause a fuel leak and increase the risk of fire. A related Sprinter recall covers an open port on the fuel pump module on 2019 Sprinter VS30 vans that can leak fuel onto the roadway. Separately, on 2021 AMG models — C43, C63 S, G63, GLC43, GLC63, and E53 — the fuel pump may shut down and cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A fuel leak may be visible or smelled near the fuel lines, hoses, or pump
  • Loss of drive power while driving, if the fuel pump shuts down
  • The notices don't describe any warning sign before the hose clamps loosen or the fuel pump shuts down

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the Sprinter fuel line clamps, the Metris fuel hose connection, and the Sprinter fuel pump open port, the notices say a fuel leak increases the risk of fire or, in the fuel pump case, of a crash. For the AMG fuel pump shutdown recalls (C43, C63 S, G63, GLC43, GLC63, and separately E53), the notices say a loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash. None of the five notices tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers make free repairs specific to each defect: tightening or replacing fuel hose clamps and connections on the Sprinter and Metris, installing a closure on the Sprinter fuel pump's open port, and replacing the fuel pump entirely on the affected AMG models with the shutdown defect.

Common questions

Are the Sprinter fuel leak recall and the AMG fuel pump recall the same defect?

No. The Sprinter and Metris recalls involve loosened fuel line clamps or hose connections that can leak fuel and increase fire risk; the AMG recalls involve a fuel pump that can shut down and cause a loss of drive power.

Which AMG models are covered by the fuel pump shutdown recalls?

The notices list 2021 AMG C43, C63 S, G63, GLC43, and GLC63 in one recall, and 2021 AMG E53 in a separate recall, all for a fuel pump that may shut down.

Why is the Sprinter fuel pump recall separate from the fuel line clamp recall?

They're different defects: the fuel line clamp recall covers loose clamps that can leak fuel, while the fuel pump recall covers an open port on the fuel delivery module on 2019 Sprinter VS30 vans that can leak fuel onto the roadway.
