What Jeep electrical system recalls cover: battery fires and display failures

15 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 23, 2026

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Fifteen Jeep recalls sit here, and they split into two different problems. The largest is the 4xe plug-in hybrid high-voltage battery, which may fail internally and lead to a fire while parked or driving, across three recalls that between them reach Wrangler 4xe from 2020-2025 and Grand Cherokee 4xe from 2022-2026; a further recall covers an overheating 200-amp battery fuse on 2022-2023 Wrangler PHEVs. The rest are instrument panel and module faults: Compass cluster brightness and visibility appear three times across 2017-2018 and 2022-2023, the 2024 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L speedometer may read wrong, the 2019 Cherokee cluster may fail, and two 2018 recalls shared with other Chrysler, Dodge and Ram models cover cruise control that will not cancel and a powertrain control module that may stall.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Warning chimes, messages and gauges may not activate — 2019 Jeep Cherokee instrument cluster recall.
  • The speedometer may display the incorrect vehicle speed — 2024 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L recall.
  • Instrument cluster brightness cannot be adjusted or the center display may not be visible in daylight — the 2017-2018 and 2022-2023 Jeep Compass recalls and the 2022 Compass display recall.
  • The vehicle may maintain speed or accelerate despite attempts to deactivate cruise control — the 2018 cruise control recall.
  • A stall or a no-start condition — the 2018 powertrain control module recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Three recalls carry a parking and charging instruction: the 2021-2023 Wrangler PHEV battery recall, the 2020-2024 Wrangler 4xe and 2022-2024 Grand Cherokee 4xe battery recall, and the 2020-2025 Wrangler 4xe and 2022-2026 Grand Cherokee 4xe battery recall each tell owners to park outside and away from structures and not to recharge or charge the vehicle until the repair is complete. None of them tells owners to stop driving. For the remaining electrical recalls in this group, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The 2018 cruise control notice advises owners to stop using cruise control.

What the free repair involves

Remedies vary by recall. For the three high-voltage battery recalls, dealers update the battery software and replace the battery pack where needed. Other recalls replace the powertrain control module, the instrument cluster, or the high-voltage battery fuse; several are software updates to the body control module or instrument panel cluster. The 2022 Compass display recall replaces the windshield and headlamp switch. The notices state these repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Can I still charge my Jeep 4xe if it is under a battery recall?

The three high-voltage battery notices tell owners not to charge or recharge the vehicle, and to park outside and away from structures, until the repair is completed. They do not tell owners to stop driving.

Does the 2024 Grand Cherokee 4xe battery recall still need a repair?

That notice says the recall is replaced by NHTSA recall number 25V741, and that vehicles already repaired under it will need the new remedy completed. Owners are told to park outside, away from structures, and not to recharge until repaired.

My Jeep Compass cluster looks too dim or too bright — is that a recall?

Three Compass recalls involve cluster brightness: 2017-2018 backlighting that cannot be dimmed, 2022-2023 brightness that may not be adjustable, and a 2022 center cluster display that may not be visible in daylight. Dealers update software or fit new hardware free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
