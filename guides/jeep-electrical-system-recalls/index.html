Why Do Jeep Vehicles Have So Many Electrical System Recalls?

15 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-23

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

This group covers a wide range of electrical faults, but two patterns stand out. Three separate recalls address a high-voltage battery that can fail internally and cause a fire in Wrangler and Grand Cherokee plug-in hybrid models spanning 2020 through 2026, each carrying instructions to park outside and stop charging until repaired. Instrument panel and display problems form the other cluster, recurring three times in the Compass (2017-2018, 2022, and 2022-2023) and twice in the 2024 Grand Cherokee, from dimming failures to a mis-displayed speedometer. A separate 2018 recall wave, covering many Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, and Ram models, involved cruise control that couldn't be canceled and a powertrain control module chip that could cause stalling.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Dashboard or instrument panel display that's too dim, dark, or hard to see, especially in Compass models
  • Speedometer showing the wrong vehicle speed (2024 Grand Cherokee, Grand Cherokee L)
  • Cruise control that won't disengage when requested, or an engine that stalls or won't restart (2018 models)
  • No symptom is described for the high-voltage battery fire risk recalls — owners are told to park outside and stop charging regardless of how the vehicle is behaving

Is it safe to keep driving?

Two recalls in this group tell owners to park outside and away from structures, and not to charge their vehicles, until repaired: the 2021-2023 Wrangler plug-in hybrid battery fire recall, and the 2020-2025 Wrangler and 2022-2025 Grand Cherokee battery fire recall that replaced it. A related 2024 Grand Cherokee 4xe battery fire recall was itself superseded by that newer campaign. For the remaining recalls in this group — cruise control, stalling, instrument cluster, display, and speedometer issues — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Remedies vary widely: dealers update or replace the high-voltage battery on the plug-in hybrid models, flash the powertrain control module software, replace a failed instrument cluster, update instrument panel or radio software for dimming and display errors, and replace fuses or fasteners on the battery pack. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Jeep models have the high-voltage battery fire recalls?

The Wrangler plug-in hybrid (2020-2025) and the Grand Cherokee 4xe plug-in hybrid (2022-2025) are covered by battery fire recalls. Owners of these models are told to park outside, away from structures, and stop charging until the dealer completes the repair.

Why do so many Jeep Compass recalls involve the instrument panel?

Three separate Compass recalls in this group involve display brightness or visibility — a backlight that couldn't be dimmed, a display too dim to see, and a display that wasn't adjustable — each traced to a different software or wiring cause.

Is it safe to keep driving a Jeep with an open electrical recall?

The notices don't say that. For the battery fire recalls, owners are told to park outside and stop charging until repaired. For the rest, the notices don't tell owners to stop driving, but each describes an increased safety risk.
