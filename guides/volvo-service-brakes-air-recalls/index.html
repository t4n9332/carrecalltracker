What do Volvo truck air brake recalls cover?

5 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Service Brakes, AIR · model years 2021–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-23

All five recalls here are Volvo Trucks actions, and most are FMVSS 121 air brake compliance issues centred on the VNL line. Two concern timing: 2023-2025 New VN trucks whose brakes may take longer than expected to activate (RVXX2502), and 2025-2026 VNL (4) trucks whose service brake may take longer than expected to release (RVXX2602). Hardware accounts for the rest — a die cast pedal plate that may break on 2020-2021 VAH, VHD, VNL and VNR trucks (RVXX2004), air tank bracket fasteners that were excessively tightened and may have broken on 2022-2023 VNL trucks (RVXX2205), and rear brake modulator connectors that may short circuit on 2026-2027 VNR (4) and 2025-2027 VNL (4) trucks.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brakes that take longer than expected to activate — 2023-2025 New VN trucks (RVXX2502).
  • A service brake that takes longer than expected to release — 2025-2026 VNL (4) trucks (RVXX2602).
  • A dislodged brake pedal, which the notice says would be inoperable — 2020-2021 VAH, VHD, VNL and VNR trucks (RVXX2004).
  • Loss of the anti-lock brake system and electronic stability control — rear brake modulator recall on 2026-2027 VNR (4) and 2025-2027 VNL (4) trucks.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. RVXX2004, RVXX2205, RVXX2502, RVXX2602 and the rear brake modulator recall each state that the notice does not tell owners to stop driving. The notices give no interim step to follow before the dealer work is done. What they do state is the risk each defect carries — a dislodged pedal, a detached air tank becoming a road hazard, extended stopping distance, delayed brake release, or loss of ABS and ESC.

What the free repair involves

The work varies by recall and all five notices say it is free of charge. Dealers inspect and replace the pedal plates if required (RVXX2004), replace the air tank bracket fasteners (RVXX2205), install a new pneumatic circuit (RVXX2502), install an additional brake valve and fittings in the service brake pneumatic plumbing (RVXX2602), and inspect the rear brake modulator connectors, install a protective cover and replace modulators as necessary.

Common questions

My VNL's brakes seem slow to release — is that recalled?

RVXX2602 covers certain 2025-2026 VNL (4) trucks whose service brake may take longer than expected to release, an FMVSS 121 compliance issue. Dealers install an additional brake valve and fittings free of charge; owner letters were mailed July 29, 2026.

How do I know which air brake recall applies to my truck?

Model and model year decide it: 2020-2021 VAH, VHD, VNL and VNR for the pedal plate, 2022-2023 VNL for the air tank bracket fasteners, and 2023-2025 New VN for delayed brake activation. Volvo Trucks customer service at 800-528-6586 can check your VIN.

What does the rear brake modulator recall affect?

The notice says the rear brake modulators may have unprotected electrical connectors that can short circuit and lead to a loss of the anti-lock brake system and electronic stability control on certain 2026-2027 VNR (4) and 2025-2027 VNL (4) trucks.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
