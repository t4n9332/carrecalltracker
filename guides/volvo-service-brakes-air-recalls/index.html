Why do Volvo trucks keep having air brake defects?

5 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Service Brakes, AIR · model years 2021–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-23

These five Volvo truck recalls all involve the air brake system, mostly on VNL trucks, spanning 2020 through 2027 model years. A 2020-2021 VAH, VHD, VNL, and VNR recall covers a brake pedal plate that may break and dislodge. A 2022-2023 VNL recall covers air tank bracket fasteners that may break, letting the tank detach. A 2023-2025 VNL recall covers brakes that take longer than expected to activate. A 2025-2026 VNL recall covers brakes that take longer than expected to release. The newest, covering 2025-2027 VNL and VNR trucks, involves rear brake modulator connectors that can short circuit and disable ABS and stability control.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake pedal plate may break and the pedal could become dislodged (2020-2021 VAH, VHD, VNL, VNR)
  • Air tank may detach from the truck due to broken bracket fasteners (2022-2023 VNL)
  • Brakes may take longer than expected to activate (2023-2025 VNL)
  • Brakes may take longer than expected to release (2025-2026 VNL)
  • No warning is described for the rear brake modulator short circuit before ABS and stability control are lost (2025-2027 VNL, VNR)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2020-2021 brake pedal plate recall, the 2022-2023 air tank bracket fastener recall, the 2023-2025 delayed brake activation recall, the 2025-2026 delayed brake release recall, and the 2025-2027 rear brake modulator short circuit recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving, though each notice states the defect can increase the risk of a crash while the truck is in operation.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and replace pedal plates, replace air tank bracket fasteners, install a new pneumatic circuit, install an additional brake valve and fittings, and inspect or replace rear brake modulators with a protective cover added, depending on the recall. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Volvo trucks are covered by these air brake recalls?

The recalls cover 2020-2021 VAH, VHD, VNL, and VNR trucks; 2022-2023 VNL trucks; 2023-2025 VNL trucks; 2025-2026 VNL trucks; and 2025-2027 VNL and VNR trucks, each for a different air brake defect.

Could a brake defect leave my truck without brakes entirely?

The notices describe delayed brake activation, delayed brake release, a detached air tank, a dislodged brake pedal, or loss of ABS and stability control, not a total loss of braking; each increases the risk of a crash.

Do any of these air brake recalls require an immediate stop?

No. None of the five air brake recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, according to the notices, though each notice says the defect can increase the risk of a crash.
