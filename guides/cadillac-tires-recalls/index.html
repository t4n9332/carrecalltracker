Why were Cadillac tires and tire pressure sensors recalled?

4 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Tires · model years 2020–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 16, 2025

Continental tires run through three of these four recalls. The November 2020 recall covers the 2020 XT5 built with overcured Continental tires, listed alongside Buick, Chevrolet and GMC models. The February 2021 recall also covers over-cured tires, on a wider list that includes the 2020 Escalade, Escalade ESV, XT4 and XT5. In both, the notices describe a sidewall break causing sudden air loss, or a belt edge separation leading to tread and belt loss. The October 2025 recall moves to Cadillac's electric side: 2025-2026 Optiq vehicles on 21-inch Continental all-season tires that may lose tread. The outlier is the 2020 CT6 with dealer-installed accessory 20-inch wheels, whose tire pressure sensors use the wrong radio frequency.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2020 Cadillac CT6 tire pressure sensor recall states that the sensors cannot illuminate the low tire pressure warning light when tire pressure drops below the recommended level.
  • The November 2020 and February 2021 over-cured Continental tire recalls state that an affected tire may develop a break in the sidewall resulting in sudden air loss, or a belt edge separation leading to partial or full tread and belt loss.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The November 2020 and February 2021 over-cured Continental tire notices say a sidewall break or belt edge separation can cause a loss of vehicle control and increase the risk of a crash, and the October 2025 Optiq notice says tread detachment increases the risk of a crash, but none tells owners to stop driving. The 2020 CT6 notice says driving with low tire pressure and no warning can negatively affect handling; it likewise gives no stop-driving instruction.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is free of charge. Under the November 2020 recall, dealers inspect vehicles and replace tires with the DOT number and production mold number combinations Continental identified. Under the February 2021 recall, dealers inspect the tires and replace them as necessary. For the 2025-2026 Optiq, dealers inspect all four tires and replace any manufactured during DOT week 4024. For the CT6, dealers replace all four tire pressure sensors.

Common questions

How do I know whether my Cadillac has the recalled tires?

The notices identify affected tires by production details rather than appearance: the November 2020 recall by DOT number and production mold number combinations Continental identified, and the October 2025 Optiq recall by tires manufactured during DOT week 4024. A dealer inspection determines this.

Does the CT6 recall mean my low tire pressure light will not come on?

The notice states the accessory 20-inch wheel sensors use the incorrect wireless radio frequency and cannot illuminate the low pressure warning light when pressure drops below the recommended level, so the vehicles do not comply with FMVSS 138. Dealers replace all four sensors free of charge.

Do I get new tires, or only an inspection?

The notices describe inspection plus replacement where it applies: tires replaced as necessary under the February 2021 recall, replaced when they match Continental's identified numbers under the November 2020 recall, and replaced when manufactured during DOT week 4024 on the Optiq. All free of charge.
