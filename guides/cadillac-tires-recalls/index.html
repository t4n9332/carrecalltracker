Why Do Some Cadillac Models Keep Getting Tire Recalls?

4 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Tires · model years 2020–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-10-16

Four recalls cover tires and tire-pressure monitoring on Cadillac vehicles from 2020 to 2026. Two, filed in 2020 and 2021, involve the same root cause: Continental tires fitted to the 2020 Escalade, Escalade ESV, XT4, and XT5 that were overcured during manufacturing, which can cause a sidewall break or belt separation. A third, from 2025, covers 21-inch Continental tires on the 2025-2026 Optiq that may lose tread. The fourth is different: the 2020 CT6, when fitted with dealer-installed accessory wheels, has TPMS sensors on the wrong radio frequency, so the low-tire-pressure warning light won't turn on.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The over-cured tire recalls and the 2025 tread-detachment recall describe no warning sign before a sidewall break, air loss, or tread separation happens.
  • On the 2020 CT6 with accessory 20-inch wheels, the TPMS sensors use the wrong frequency and won't turn on the low-tire-pressure warning light, so low pressure can go unnoticed.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the two overcured-tire recalls, the 2025 Optiq/Equinox EV tread-detachment recall, and the CT6 TPMS recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. GM describes the risks as sudden air loss or belt/tread separation that can cause loss of vehicle control, and, for the CT6, driving on low tire pressure without any warning light, which can also affect handling.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the tires and replace any that match the affected DOT and mold-number combinations, free of charge, for the overcured-tire and tread-detachment recalls. For the CT6, dealers replace all four TPMS sensors with correctly frequenced units. No repair involves a software or over-the-air update.

Common questions

Which Cadillac models were recalled for overcured tires?

GM's 2020 and 2021 recalls covered Continental tires on the 2020 Escalade, Escalade ESV, XT4, and XT5, along with several other GM trucks and SUVs. The tires were cured too long during manufacturing, which can cause a sidewall break or tread separation.

What's wrong with tires on the new Cadillac Optiq?

GM recalled certain 2025-2026 Optiq and Chevrolet Equinox EV models equipped with 21-inch Continental all-season tires. One or more tires may experience partial or full tread detachment; dealers replace tires made during DOT week 4024.

Why doesn't my Cadillac CT6 warn me about low tire pressure?

On 2020 CT6 models fitted with dealer-installed 20-inch accessory wheels, the TPMS sensors transmit on the wrong radio frequency, so the low-pressure warning light can't turn on even if a tire is underinflated.
