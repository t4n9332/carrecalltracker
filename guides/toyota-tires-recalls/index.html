What Toyota tire recalls cover spare carriers, valve stems and load ratings?

5 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Tires · model years 2021–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 15, 2024

Toyota's tire-related recalls split into two patterns. Three involve wheels and tires added as accessories by regional distributors: Gulf States Toyota recalled 2023 RAV4s with 17-inch black alloy accessory wheels over valve stems that may have been improperly installed or damaged, and 2022-2024 Highlander L and LE models with the Blackout Package for tires with an insufficient load rating; Southeast Toyota Distributors recalled 2021-2024 Highlanders over 20-inch accessory tires whose load rating was insufficient for the vehicle's Gross Axle Weight Rating. The other two come from Toyota itself and hit full-size models: 2023 Tundra, Tundra Hybrid and Sequoia Hybrid for a spare tire carrier chain that may be insufficiently welded, and 2024 Tundra, Tundra Hybrid and Sequoia Hybrid for passenger-side tires with inner sidewall damage.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Air may leak from the tires, leaving them underinflated (2023 RAV4 accessory wheel valve stem recall).
  • A sudden loss of tire pressure in a passenger-side tire (2024 Tundra, Tundra Hybrid and Sequoia Hybrid inner sidewall damage recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park the vehicle outside. That applies to the 2023 Tundra, Tundra Hybrid and Sequoia Hybrid spare tire carrier recall, the 2023 RAV4 valve stem recall, both Highlander load rating recalls from Gulf States Toyota and Southeast Toyota Distributors, and the 2024 Tundra, Tundra Hybrid and Sequoia Hybrid tire recall. Each notice describes the risk — a detached spare tire, underinflated tires, overloaded tires, or a sudden loss of tire pressure — and says a dealer performs the repair free of charge.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the spare tire carrier assembly on the 2023 Tundra, Tundra Hybrid and Sequoia Hybrid. On the 2023 RAV4 they inspect valve stems and replace them and the load carrying capacity labels as needed. The two Highlander recalls and the 2024 Tundra, Tundra Hybrid and Sequoia Hybrid recall involve inspecting and replacing tires as necessary; the Highlander work also includes a new tire label or placard. Repairs are free.

Common questions

Does the spare tire recall apply to my 2023 Tundra?

It covers certain 2023 Tundra, Tundra Hybrid and Sequoia Hybrid vehicles whose spare tire securing chain may be insufficiently welded. Toyota's number for this recall is 23TB06/23TA06, and Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 can confirm whether a given vehicle is included.

Why were Highlanders recalled twice over tires?

Two distributors issued separate recalls. Gulf States Toyota covered 2022-2024 Highlander L and LE models with the Blackout Package and 20-inch black alloy wheels; Southeast Toyota Distributors covered 2021-2024 Highlanders with 20-inch accessory tires. Both notices say the tires have an insufficient load rating.

Do I have to pay for new tires?

The notices say the repairs are free of charge. In the Highlander recalls the distributor inspects and replaces tires as necessary at no cost, and in the 2024 Tundra, Tundra Hybrid and Sequoia Hybrid recall dealers inspect and replace tires free of charge.
