Why are Toyota Tundra, Sequoia and Highlander tires under recall?

5 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Tires · model years 2021–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-10-15

These five Toyota tire recalls fall into three groups. Tundra and Sequoia Hybrid models were recalled twice for different reasons: in 2023 for a spare tire chain that may not have been welded well enough to hold the spare under the vehicle, and in 2024 for front and rear passenger-side tires with inner sidewall damage that can cause sudden air loss. Highlander was recalled twice for accessory 20-inch wheel-and-tire packages rated below the vehicle's weight capacity, once by Gulf States Toyota for 2022-2024 models and once by Southeast Toyota Distributors for 2021-2024 models. A separate 2023 recall covers RAV4 valve stems damaged during accessory wheel installation.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Falling tire pressure or a soft-feeling tire (RAV4 valve stem leak)
  • Sudden loss of tire pressure while driving (2024 Tundra and Sequoia Hybrid)
  • No warning sign is described for the spare tire chain weld issue or the Highlander tire load-rating recalls

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the spare tire chain weld, the valve stem leaks, the two Highlander tire load-rating recalls, and the 2024 Tundra and Sequoia Hybrid sidewall damage, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Toyota, Gulf States Toyota, and Southeast Toyota Distributors each direct owners to bring their vehicle in for inspection and repair, but none of the five notices instruct owners to park outside or avoid driving before that repair is done.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the spare tire carrier assembly on affected Tundra and Sequoia Hybrid models, inspect and replace valve stems and labels on RAV4, inspect and replace tires and information labels on Highlander when the load rating is insufficient, and inspect and replace tires on the 2024 Tundra and Sequoia Hybrid with sidewall damage. All repairs are done free of charge.

Common questions

Is my Toyota's spare tire actually going to fall off?

Only certain 2023 Tundra, Tundra Hybrid, and Sequoia Hybrid vehicles are affected by the chain weld issue. The notice says an insufficiently welded chain could break and let the spare tire detach, so Toyota will replace the carrier assembly free of charge.

Why were Highlander tires recalled twice for basically the same problem?

Two different Toyota distributors, Gulf States Toyota and Southeast Toyota Distributors, each sold their own accessory 20-inch wheel-and-tire package with tires rated below the Highlander's weight capacity, covering overlapping but separate model years, so each issued its own recall.

Will fixing any of these tire recalls cost me anything?

No. Every remedy described in these five recall notices — replacing the spare tire carrier, valve stems, or tires themselves — is performed by dealers at no cost to the owner.
