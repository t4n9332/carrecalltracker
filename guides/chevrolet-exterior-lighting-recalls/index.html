Why do some Chevrolet SUVs and trucks have lighting recalls?

5 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-12-08

GM has issued two separate recalls for daytime running lights that fail to turn off when the headlights are on: one for the 2021 Chevrolet Tahoe, Suburban, GMC Yukon, Yukon XL, and Cadillac Escalade, and a broader one covering 2020-2023 Cadillac CT4, CT5, Buick Envision, and 2022-2023 Escalade, Silverado 1500, Suburban, Tahoe, Sierra 1500, Yukon, and Yukon XL. Separately, two ambulance recalls from different remounters, Excellance and North Central, cover 2018 Chevrolet G4500 chassis with Whelen brake lights that can illuminate without the brake pedal pressed. A fifth recall covers a 2022 Silverado 1500 and Sierra 1500 accessory sport bar whose brake light may not work.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Daytime running lights that stay on even when the headlights are turned on
  • Brake lights on Chevrolet G4500-based ambulances that light up without the brake pedal pressed
  • A high-mounted brake light that does not work on trucks with an accessory sport bar
  • The accessory sport bar blocking the truck's factory high-mounted brake light

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe glare from daytime running lights that fail to turn off on affected Cadillac, Buick, Chevrolet, and GMC models, confusion from ambulance brake lights that illuminate without the brake pedal being pressed on Chevrolet G4500-based ambulances, and reduced visibility from a non-functioning or blocked high-mounted brake light on Silverado and Sierra 1500 trucks with an accessory sport bar. In every case, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the two daytime running light recalls, GM updates the body control module software, which can be done over-the-air or by a dealer. Dealers replace the Whelen brake lights on the ambulance recalls, and inspect and correct the wiring for the sport bar brake light. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Can I fix the daytime running light recall myself?

No repair is needed from you directly, but GM delivers the fix as an over-the-air software update to the body control module, so many owners will not need a dealer visit at all.

Why are ambulances included in a Chevrolet lighting recall?

Two separate ambulance upfitters, Excellance and North Central, built emergency vehicles on Chevrolet G4500 chassis using the same Whelen brake lights, which can illuminate without the brake pedal being pressed.

Does the sport bar recall affect my truck's factory brake light?

Yes. GM says the accessory sport bar's own brake light may not work, and it can also block the truck's existing factory high-mounted brake light from being seen, reducing visibility to drivers behind.
