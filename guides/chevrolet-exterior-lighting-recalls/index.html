Chevrolet lighting recalls: daytime running lights and brake lights that misbehave

5 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 8, 2022

Two patterns dominate. The larger is daytime running lights that do not deactivate when the headlights are on, cited as a failure to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108: one recall covers 2021 Chevrolet Tahoe and Suburban alongside Cadillac Escalade and GMC Yukon, a second covers 2022-2023 Silverado 1500, Suburban and Tahoe together with Cadillac CT4, CT5 and Escalade and Buick Envision. The second pattern is brake lights. Two 2019 recalls of ambulances built on Chevrolet G4500 chassis with Whelen brake lights say the lights can illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed, and a 2022 recall says an accessory sport bar on 2022 Silverado 1500 trucks carries a high-mounted brake light that may not function.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Daytime running lights that do not deactivate when the headlights are on, causing glare — both GM daytime running light recalls.
  • Brake lights that illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed — the two 2019 ambulance recalls covering Chevrolet G4500 chassis with Whelen brake lights.
  • A high-mounted brake light on the accessory sport bar that does not function, or that blocks the truck's existing high-mounted brake light — 2022 Silverado 1500 and Sierra 1500 recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park outside. The two daytime running light notices say lights that do not deactivate may cause glare that reduces visibility. The sport bar notice says an inoperative high-mounted brake light reduces visibility to other drivers. Both ambulance notices say brake lights that illuminate incorrectly can confuse other drivers. Each notice frames the risk as an increased chance of a crash, and none gives an interim instruction to owners.

What the free repair involves

All of these repairs are free. For the daytime running lights, the body control module software is updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update. On the Silverado and Sierra sport bar recall, dealers inspect the installation of the accessory bar and provide the correct vehicle wiring if necessary. For the ambulances, the Whelen brake lights are replaced.

Common questions

How do I know if my Tahoe or Silverado has the daytime running light problem?

The notices describe daytime running lights that do not deactivate when the headlights are on. Covered vehicles include 2021 Chevrolet Tahoe and Suburban and 2022-2023 Silverado 1500, Suburban and Tahoe. Check your VIN with NHTSA to confirm which recall applies.

Does the sport bar recall mean the accessory bar has to come off?

The notice does not say that. It says dealers will inspect the installation of the accessory sport bar and provide the correct vehicle wiring, if necessary, free of charge, on 2022 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500 trucks.

Do the ambulance brake light recalls cover regular Chevrolet vans?

The two notices cover ambulances and emergency vehicles built or remounted on chassis including the Chevrolet G4500 and equipped with Whelen brake lights. They do not describe standard passenger vans. Owners are directed to Whelen or the ambulance builder for the free replacement.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
