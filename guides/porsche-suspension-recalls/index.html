Porsche suspension recalls: loose screws, control arms and air struts

13 NHTSA recalls · Porsche · Suspension · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 15, 2025

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

Fasteners dominate this Porsche group. Shock absorber screw connections on the 2021 Macan, rear axle upper control arm connections on 911 models and 2020 Taycans, and suspension nuts and bolts across a long 2021 list of Taycan, 911, Cayman and Boxster models were all recalled for connections that may be loose or not tightened correctly. A second thread is broken or cracked parts: front lower trailing arms not forged correctly on 2021 Panamera and Taycan models, a cracked front upper control arm on the 2024 Cayenne, a rear axle lock nut that may break on the 2021 Cayenne, and anti-roll bar links that can detach on 2017-2018 Panamera models. Air strut retaining rings appear in two recalls, covering the 2021 Taycan and the 2025 Panamera.

Warning signs to watch for

  • In the 2021-2022 Cayenne rear axle alignment recall (ANA1), the notice says rear axle misalignment may cause premature or uneven tire wear.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two of these recalls tell owners not to drive. In the 2021 Macan recall, owners are advised not to drive the vehicle until the remedy has been performed, because a failed screw connection may damage the braking system and cause a loss of control. In the 2024 Cayenne recall, owners are told not to drive until the driver's side upper control arm is replaced. For the rest — the Panamera, Taycan, 911, Cayman, Boxster and other Cayenne recalls here — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

These repairs are performed free of charge. Dealers rework and tighten screw connections on the Macan and 911, replace suspension nuts, bolts and screw connections on Taycan models and inspect surrounding parts for damage, replace anti-roll bar links, trailing arms, shock absorber forks, lock nuts and the Cayenne control arm, inspect and replace or reinstall air strut parts, and check rear axle alignment.

Common questions

Which Porsche suspension recalls say not to drive the car?

Two: the 2021 Macan shock absorber screw recall, where owners are advised not to drive until the remedy is performed, and the 2024 Cayenne recall, where owners are told not to drive until the control arm is replaced.

Why do so many of these involve loose screws or bolts?

The notices state only that particular connections may be loose or may not have been tightened properly — shock absorbers on the Macan, rear axle control arms on the 911 and Taycan, and suspension nuts and bolts on the 2021 models. They do not say more.

Will Porsche replace tires worn by the Cayenne alignment problem?

In the 2021-2022 Cayenne recall, dealers inspect the rear axle, adjust the alignment as necessary, and replace any prematurely or unevenly worn tires, free of charge. That applies to that recall only, not the other suspension recalls in this group.
