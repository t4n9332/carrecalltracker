Why Are Porsche Suspension Bolts And Control Arms Being Recalled?

13 NHTSA recalls · Porsche · Suspension · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-07-15

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

Most of these recalls involve suspension bolts, screws, or nuts that were not tightened correctly or that loosen over time: the 2021 Macan shock absorber screws, the 2019 Cayenne shock absorber forks, 2021 Taycan and related model suspension bolts, a 2020 911 Carrera rear screw connection, a 2021 Cayenne rear axle lock nut that can break from stress corrosion, and Taycan suspension strut retaining rings that can loosen in a 2021 Taycan recall and again in a 2026 Panamera recall. A smaller number involve parts that were manufactured incorrectly and can break, including a cracked 2024 Cayenne control arm, a 2018 Panamera anti-roll bar link, and a 2021 Panamera/Taycan front trailing arm that was not forged correctly. The 2021 Macan and 2024 Cayenne recalls are the most serious, both carrying a stop-driving instruction.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Uneven or premature tire wear (Cayenne rear axle alignment recall)
  • Unusual or unstable handling, or a change in how the vehicle steers (several suspension bolt and arm recalls)
  • A vehicle sitting lower on one corner from air loss in a suspension strut (Taycan/Panamera strut recalls)
  • No warning signs are described for most of these recalls before a bolt, nut, or arm fails

Is it safe to keep driving?

Two of these recalls tell owners not to drive until repaired: the 2021 Macan suspension screw connection recall and the 2024 Cayenne control arm crack recall. Neither of those two notices mentions parking outside. The rest of the recalls in this group, covering the Panamera, 911, Cayenne, and Taycan, do not tell owners to stop driving; each describes a suspension defect that Porsche says can affect handling, stability, or braking and increase the risk of a crash.

What the free repair involves

Across this group, dealers inspect and tighten or rework loose screw connections, replace shock absorbers, control arms, trailing arms, lock nuts, and suspension struts as needed, and in one case re-check rear axle alignment and replace worn tires. All of these repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Porsche models have a stop-driving suspension recall?

Two recalls in this group tell owners not to drive until the vehicle is repaired: the 2021 Macan, over a loose suspension screw connection, and the 2024 Cayenne, over a cracked front control arm. Other suspension recalls in this group do not carry that instruction.

Why do so many Taycan recalls involve suspension bolts?

The Taycan appears in multiple separate recalls for suspension bolts, control arm screws, and strut retaining rings that were not tightened correctly or that can loosen, according to the notices. Each recall covers a distinct part and was issued separately by Porsche.

What should I do if my Cayenne had a prior suspension recall?

A 2022 recall found that some 2021-2022 Cayenne vehicles did not get a rear axle alignment check after an earlier lock-nut recall repair. Dealers will inspect the alignment, adjust it if needed, and replace any tires that wore unevenly, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
