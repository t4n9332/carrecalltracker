Why do Porsche suspension recalls keep involving loose bolts and control arms?

13 NHTSA recalls · Porsche · Suspension · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-07-15

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

Nearly every Porsche suspension recall here traces to a fastener or a link that will not stay put. Screw connections left loose or improperly tightened hit the 2021 Macan's front and rear shock absorbers, 2021 Taycan, 911 and Cayman suspension nuts and bolts, the 2020 Taycan's rear axle upper control arms, and 2019-2020 911 Carrera rear axles. Parts that break come next: a cracked driver's side front upper control arm on the 2024 Cayenne, front lower trailing arms on 2021 Taycan and Panamera models, a stress-corroded rear trailing arm lock nut on the 2021 Cayenne, and strut retaining rings coming loose on 2021 Taycans and 2025 Panamera and Taycan models. Two 2022 recalls simply follow up on alignment and early tire wear.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Changed or unpredictable handling, wandering, or instability at speed from a loose or failing suspension connection.
  • Sudden loss of steering or wheel control, which the 2024 Cayenne notice says can happen without warning.
  • Air loss from the strut spring after a retaining ring comes loose and the strut dislodges (2021 Taycan, 2025 Panamera and Taycan).
  • Uneven or unusually early tire wear on the 2021-2022 Cayenne and the misaligned 2021 Taycan.
  • Several of these failures are described as arriving with no warning at all, so a VIN check matters more than waiting to feel something.

Is it safe to keep driving?

Two of these are do-not-drive recalls. Porsche tells 2021 Macan owners not to drive until the loose shock absorber screw connections are reworked, and 2024 Cayenne owners not to drive until the cracked driver's side front upper control arm is replaced, because steering can fail without warning. The rest carry no driving restriction, but several — the breakable front lower trailing arms, the loose rear-axle screw connections, the corroding lock nut and the strut retaining rings — can take away control suddenly, so schedule them immediately. The two alignment recalls are the least urgent; keep an eye on your tires.

What the free repair involves

Depending on the recall, dealers rework and tighten the screw connections, replace suspension nuts and bolts while inspecting surrounding parts for damage, replace shock absorber forks, front lower trailing arms, upper control arms or corroded lock nuts, inspect and reinstall or replace strut retaining rings, and on the two alignment recalls adjust the axle alignment and replace unevenly or prematurely worn tires. All of it is free of charge.

Common questions

Which Porsche suspension recalls say not to drive the car?

Two. The 2021 Macan recall for improperly tightened front and rear shock absorber screw connections tells owners not to drive until the repair is done, and the 2024 Cayenne cracked front upper control arm recall says the same because steering can fail.

Why are so many of these about bolts that were not tightened?

The notices repeatedly describe screw connections on shock absorbers and control arms that may not have been tightened properly, spanning the Macan, Taycan, Panamera, Cayenne, 911 and Cayman. Dealers rework and tighten them, or replace the nuts and bolts entirely.

My Cayenne already had recall 21V-271 done. Why did I get another letter?

Because the rear axle alignment may not have been inspected after those repairs. Under the follow-up recall on 2021-2022 Cayennes, dealers inspect the rear axle, adjust the alignment if needed, and replace any prematurely or unevenly worn tires free of charge.
