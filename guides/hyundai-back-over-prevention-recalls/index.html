Why does the rearview camera fail on some Hyundai models?

3 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Back Over Prevention · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-11-21

Three separate Hyundai recalls in this group all involve a rearview camera that fails to display, though the underlying causes differ. The Santa Fe is affected twice, in 2024-2025 models where the camera was installed improperly and damaged its own wiring harness, and separately in 2021-2022 Santa Fe and Santa Fe Hybrid models where a damaged printed circuit board keeps the image from displaying. A third 2024 Santa Fe recall involves the image being blocked, not disabled, by an on-screen trailer parking assist message. The same 2021-2022 circuit board issue also affects Elantra, Elantra Hybrid, and Elantra N models. No other Hyundai models appear in this group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rearview camera image does not display when in reverse
  • Rearview camera image is blocked by an on-screen trailer parking assist message
  • Notices describe no dashboard warning light for these camera failures

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the three recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2024-2025 Santa Fe wiring harness recall, the 2024 Santa Fe software recall, and the 2021-2022 Santa Fe and Elantra circuit board recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. All three note that a rearview image that fails to display, or is blocked, reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle and increases the risk of a crash, but none instruct owners to park outside or avoid driving until repaired.

What the free repair involves

Across these three recalls, Hyundai dealers replace the damaged camera wiring harness or the rearview camera itself, free of charge. For the 2024 Santa Fe trailer parking assist message issue, Hyundai instead pushes a free over-the-air software update rather than requiring a dealer visit. No cost to the owner is described for any of these repairs.

Common questions

Which Hyundai models are covered by these rearview camera recalls?

The recalls cover 2024-2025 Santa Fe and Santa Fe Hybrid, 2024 Santa Fe, and 2021-2022 Santa Fe, Santa Fe Hybrid, Elantra, Elantra Hybrid, and Elantra N models, each affected by a different camera defect.

Do I need to visit a dealer for the camera recall?

It depends on the recall. The 2024 Santa Fe trailer parking assist issue is fixed with a free over-the-air update, while the wiring harness and circuit board issues require a dealer to replace parts.

Is it dangerous to drive with the rearview camera not working?

The notices say a missing or blocked rearview image reduces what the driver can see behind the vehicle, increasing crash risk, but none of these recalls instruct owners to stop driving.
