Why does the rearview camera fail on recalled Hyundai Santa Fe models?

3 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Back Over Prevention · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-11-21

All three of these Hyundai back-over recalls involve the rearview camera image and Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 111, and Santa Fe is in every one. The 2024-2025 Santa Fe and Santa Fe Hybrid notice describes a camera that may not have been installed properly, damaging the wiring harness so the image does not display. The 2024 Santa Fe notice describes a trailer parking assist message obstructing the image instead. The broadest of the three reaches back to 2021-2022 Santa Fe, Santa Fe HEV, Elantra and Elantra HEV, plus the 2022 Elantra N and Santa Fe PHEV, where a damaged printed circuit board can stop the image displaying. Remedies split between hardware replacement and software.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The rearview camera image may not display (2024-2025 Santa Fe and Santa Fe Hybrid camera wiring harness recall).
  • A trailer parking assist message may obstruct the rearview camera image (2024 Santa Fe recall).
  • The rearview camera image may fail to display (2021-2022 Santa Fe and Elantra printed circuit board recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Each describes reduced rearward visibility and an increased risk of a crash, and each points owners to the dealer or software remedy. The 2024-2025 Santa Fe and Santa Fe Hybrid notice, the 2024 Santa Fe trailer message notice and the 2021-2022 Santa Fe and Elantra notice all stop at describing the risk and the fix; none of them sets out any interim instruction for owners in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Two of the three are hardware repairs at the dealer, free of charge: replacing the camera wiring harness on 2024-2025 Santa Fe and Santa Fe Hybrid vehicles, and replacing the rearview camera itself on the 2021-2022 Santa Fe and Elantra group. The 2024 Santa Fe trailer parking assist message is addressed by an over-the-air software update that Hyundai releases to the vehicle.

Common questions

My 2024 Santa Fe camera works — is it still part of a recall?

Two notices cover 2024 Santa Fe vehicles: one for a trailer parking assist message obstructing the image, fixed over the air, and one for a camera wiring harness fixed by the dealer free of charge. Check your VIN to see which applies.

Do I have to visit a dealer for the over-the-air update?

For the 2024 Santa Fe trailer parking assist recall, the notice says Hyundai will release an over-the-air software update, so the remedy reaches the vehicle itself. The other two rearview camera recalls are dealer repairs, replacing a wiring harness or the camera.

Is a blank rearview screen the only sign of these defects?

The notices describe the image failing to display on the 2024-2025 Santa Fe and 2021-2022 Santa Fe and Elantra recalls, and a trailer parking assist message covering the image on the 2024 Santa Fe. They describe no other sign.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
