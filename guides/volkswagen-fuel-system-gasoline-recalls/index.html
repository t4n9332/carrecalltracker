Volkswagen fuel leaks and stalling: which models are recalled?

10 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 22, 2026

Fuel leaks run through almost every recall here, most of them on the Atlas and Taos. The Atlas was recalled twice over fuel tanks that could leak (2018) and once for a suction jet welded in the wrong position that could also cause stalling (2019), then again for 2021-2022 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport injectors assembled without filters. The Taos appears three times: a 2022 underhood quick-connector that can detach, a 2022 fuel delivery pump that can break and stall the engine, and a 2025-2026 fuel pressure sensor that can dislodge in a crash. Golf, GTI, Golf Sportwagen, Jetta GLI and Audi A3 models share a suction jet pump seal problem, the 2016-2018 Jetta has loosening fuel rail bolts, and the 2018 Golf R its own quick-connector.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine stalling — recall 20BU says the 2019 Atlas may stall due to uneven fuel distribution.
  • A sudden engine stall — recall 20DV says the 2022 Taos fuel delivery pump may break and cause one.
  • A stall from a fuel leak — recall 20DB says the 2022 Taos leak may cause a stall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these ten notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park the vehicle outside. The notices state the consequences instead: a fuel leak near an ignition source increases the risk of fire in the Golf R, Atlas, Jetta, Taos, Golf, GTI, Golf Sportwagen and Audi A3 recalls, and a stall increases the risk of a crash in the 2019 Atlas and the 2022 Taos recalls. Beyond directing owners to the dealer, the notices give no interim instruction to follow before the repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace fuel tanks on the 2018 and 2019 Atlas, secure the Golf R fuel line connection, inspect and replace the Taos supply line and connector, replace the Taos fuel pump, the 2021-2022 Atlas and Cross Sport injectors, and the Golf, GTI, Sportwagen and A3 suction pump, and add a wiring harness extension on the 2025-2026 Taos, free of charge. The Jetta fuel rail bolt notice does not state a charge.

Common questions

Which Taos recall applies to my vehicle?

The 2022 Taos appears in two: recall 20DB for an underhood fuel supply quick-connector that may detach, and recall 20DV for a fuel delivery pump that may break. The 2025-2026 Taos is covered by recall 97TH for the fuel pressure sensor.

Why does the Atlas fuel tank appear twice?

Recall 20AQ covers 2018 Atlas fuel tanks that may have insufficient wall thickness. Recall 20AW covers Atlas vehicles whose fuel tank may leak due to a manufacturing error, and its notice says it is an expansion of recall 17V-766. Dealers replace the tank free of charge in both.

Do any of these notices tell owners to stop driving?

None of the ten notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. They state that a fuel leak near an ignition source increases the risk of fire, that a stall increases the risk of a crash, and that owners should have the recall work done.
