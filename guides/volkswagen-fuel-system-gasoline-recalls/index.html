Why do so many Volkswagen models have fuel leak recalls?

10 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-22

This group's ten fuel-system recalls fall into two patterns: fuel that can leak from a tank, hose, or connector, and parts that can cause the engine to stall. The 2018 Atlas was recalled twice for fuel tank defects — thin walls and a manufacturing error — and the 2019 Atlas had a mis-welded suction jet that could leak fuel and stall the engine. The Taos has three recalls: a 2022 connector that could detach, a 2022 fuel pump that could stall the engine, and a 2025-2026 pressure sensor that could dislodge in a crash. A separate tank seal leak affects the 2018-2020 Golf, GTI, Golf Sportwagen, Jetta, and Audi A3, while the 2018 Jetta and Golf R, and 2021-2022 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport, had fuel-line, hose, or injector leaks.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Smell of fuel or a visible fuel leak near the tank, engine bay, or fuel line
  • Sudden engine stalling while driving (2019 Atlas, 2022 Taos fuel pump)
  • Fuel odor from the evaporative emissions system or charcoal canister (2018-2020 Golf, GTI, Golf Sportwagen, Jetta, Audi A3 tank seal leak)
  • None of the notices describe a warning light for these fuel-system defects

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these ten fuel-system recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For all of them — the tank, hose, connector, seal, injector, and fuel pump issues affecting the Atlas, Taos, Golf, GTI, Golf Sportwagen, Jetta, Golf R, and Audi A3 — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving, even though several describe a fire risk from a fuel leak or a stall risk from a loss of engine power.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls dealers replace or reinforce the affected fuel tanks, replace fuel rail bolts, fuel hoses, fuel line connectors, fuel injectors, fuel pumps, or suction pump seals, and add a wiring harness extension for the 2025-2026 Taos sensor, all free of charge.

Common questions

Why has the Atlas had so many fuel tank recalls?

The 2018 Atlas was recalled twice for tank defects — thin tank walls and a separate manufacturing error — and the 2019 Atlas had a different issue, a mis-welded suction jet that could leak fuel and cause the engine to stall. These are three distinct problems, not one repeated defect.

Is it safe to keep driving with a fuel leak recall open?

The notices in this group do not instruct owners to stop driving, but they do warn that a fuel leak near an ignition source increases fire risk, and that some defects can cause the engine to stall while driving. Contact your dealer to schedule the free repair.

What does the Taos fuel pressure sensor recall fix?

Dealers install a fuel pressure sensor wiring harness extension on affected 2025-2026 Taos vehicles, free of charge. The notice says the sensor could dislodge or separate from the fuel tank in a rear-end crash, which can leak fuel and increase fire risk.
