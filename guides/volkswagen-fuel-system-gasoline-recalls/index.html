Volkswagen Gasoline Fuel System Recalls Cover Leaks and Engine Stalling

10 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 22, 2026

Fuel leakage is the recurring issue across these Volkswagen gasoline fuel system recalls, with Atlas and Taos appearing repeatedly. Certain 2018 Atlas vehicles have tank leak defects; certain 2019 Atlas vehicles have an incorrectly positioned suction jet and possible stalling. Certain 2021-2022 Atlas FL and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles have long injectors missing filters. Taos notices concern a detachable connector or pump failure on certain 2022 vehicles, and crash-related pressure sensor separation on certain 2025-2026 vehicles. Other notices address certain 2018 Golf R connectors, 2016-2018 Jetta fuel rail bolts, and suction jet pump seals across specified front-wheel-drive Golf-family, Jetta GLI, and Audi A3 vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Atlas fuel tank recall 20BU: the vehicle may stall due to uneven fuel distribution.
  • Taos fuel line connector recall 20DB: a fuel leak may cause an engine stall.
  • Taos fuel pump recall 20DV: pump failure may cause a sudden engine stall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The supplied notices do not tell owners to stop driving for any of the 10 recalls in this group, and none includes a park-outside instruction. That applies to the Golf R connector, Atlas tank and injector, Jetta fuel rail bolt, Taos connector, pump and pressure sensor, and Audi/VW suction pump seal notices. The notices identify fire risks from fuel leakage; the 2019 Atlas tank, 2022 Taos connector, and 2022 Taos pump notices also describe engine stalling and crash risk.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies include securing the Golf R connection under 20Bi; tank replacement under 20AQ, 20AW and 20BU; necessary line/connector replacement after inspection under 20DB; injector replacement under 24iD; pump replacement under 20DV; suction pump replacement in the 2024 notice; and a harness extension under 97TH. Recall 24Gi replaces bolts and, as necessary, the rail; its supplied remedy does not specify cost.

Common questions

Which Taos fuel recalls mention engine stalling?

For certain 2022 Taos vehicles, recall 20DB says a detached fuel supply connector can leak and cause stalling. Recall 20DV says the fuel delivery pump may break and cause a sudden engine stall. Each notice specifies a different remedy.

Why do several Atlas fuel recalls specify different repairs?

Recalls 20AQ and 20AW address leaking tanks on certain 2018 Atlas vehicles, while 20BU addresses the tank suction jet on certain 2019 Atlas vehicles. Those notices specify tank replacement. Recall 24iD instead replaces long injectors on certain 2021-2022 Atlas FL and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles.

What does the 2025-2026 Taos fuel sensor recall repair?

Recall 97TH provides a fuel pressure sensor wiring harness extension free of charge. The notice says the sensor may dislodge or separate from the tank in a rear-end crash, and that a separated sensor can allow leakage after a rollover crash.
