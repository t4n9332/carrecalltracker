What are the Nissan and Infiniti air bag recalls about?

4 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 13, 2026

These four Nissan air bag recalls do not share one cause. Two involve curtain air bags on single 2018 models: the Versa Note, where a component of the side curtain air bag inflator may detach during deployment, and the 370Z, where the driver's side curtain air bag may have been installed incorrectly. The largest is the 2024 recall of 2024 Titan, Frontier, Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60, 2023-2024 Sentra, Versa, Kicks and Infiniti QX50, and 2023 Rogue vehicles, whose driver's air bag inflator may absorb moisture and cause the air bag to deploy improperly. The newest covers 2025 Altima vehicles, where the front passenger air bag and front passenger knee air bag may have incorrect mounting fasteners.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four Nissan notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. They state the consequence in crash terms only: a detached inflator component that may become a projectile inside the cabin, a curtain air bag that may not deploy correctly, a driver's air bag that may deploy improperly, and front passenger air bags that may not deploy as intended. No notice gives an interim instruction, such as keeping anyone out of a particular seat before the repair.

What the free repair involves

The remedies differ by recall. Dealers replace the side curtain air bags on the Versa Note; inspect the 370Z curtain air bag and correct its installation as necessary; replace the front driver's air bag assembly in the 2024 recall; and replace the air bag mounting fasteners on the 2025 Altima. Each notice states the work is free of charge, and none is an over-the-air update.

Common questions

How do I know which of these air bag recalls applies to my Nissan?

Each covers different models and model years, so the VIN decides. The notices give Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 for three of them and 1-800-647-7261 for the 2018 Versa Note recall; Nissan's 2024 numbers are PD103, PD104 and PMA34.

My 2023 Rogue is on the list. What will the dealer do?

The 2024 notice includes the 2023 Rogue and says the driver's air bag inflator may absorb moisture and cause the air bag to deploy improperly. Dealers will replace the front driver's air bag assembly, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed April 10, 2024.

When will 2025 Altima owners hear about the passenger air bag recall?

That notice says owner notification letters are expected to be mailed September 25, 2026, and that dealers will replace the air bag mounting fasteners free of charge. Nissan's number for the recall is PD202, and customer service is 1-800-867-7669.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
