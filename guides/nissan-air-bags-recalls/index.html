Why Are Nissan And Infiniti Air Bags Failing To Deploy Correctly?

4 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-13

These four Nissan and Infiniti recalls involve four separate air bag defects, not one repeating problem. On 2018 Versa Note vehicles, a component inside the side curtain air bag inflator can detach during deployment. On 2018 370Z vehicles, the driver's curtain air bag may have been installed incorrectly at the factory, affecting deployment. The largest recall, covering 2023-2024 Kicks, Rogue, Sentra, Versa, and Infiniti QX50 and QX60 models, involves a driver air bag inflator that can absorb moisture and deploy improperly. Most recently, 2025 Altima vehicles were recalled because the front passenger and knee air bags may have incorrect mounting fasteners, preventing proper deployment.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices describe no way for an owner to notice these air bag defects before a crash — the problems only appear during air bag deployment itself

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices do not tell owners of the 2018 Versa Note, 2018 370Z, the 2023-2024 Kicks, Rogue, Sentra, Versa, and Infiniti QX50/QX60 models, or the 2025 Altima to stop driving. Each notice instead describes a risk that the air bag will not deploy, or will deploy improperly, in the event of a future crash, increasing the risk of injury at that time.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the side curtain air bags on Versa Note, correct the curtain air bag installation on the 370Z, replace the front driver's air bag assembly on the 2023-2024 models, and replace the passenger air bag mounting fasteners on the Altima. All of these repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Will I know if my air bag is affected before a crash?

No. None of these notices describe a warning sign you could notice while driving. The defects only affect how the air bag performs during deployment in an actual crash, which is why dealers replace or correct the parts as a precaution.

Which Nissan and Infiniti models have the moisture-related air bag inflator recall?

It covers 2023-2024 Kicks, 2023 Rogue and Sentra, 2023-2024 Versa, 2024 Pathfinder, and Infiniti QX50 and QX60 models, where the driver air bag inflator can absorb moisture and deploy improperly.

Is the Versa Note air bag recall the same defect as the 370Z recall?

No. The Versa Note recall involves a side curtain air bag inflator component that can detach during deployment, while the 370Z recall involves a driver curtain air bag that may have been installed incorrectly. They are separate defects.
