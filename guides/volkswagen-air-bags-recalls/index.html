What Volkswagen airbag recalls affect Atlas, Jetta, Passat and Beetle?

16 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-10

The Atlas dominates Volkswagen's air bag recalls. It appears for a twisted air conditioning drain tube that let water reach the air bag control module on 2018 models, an incorrectly manufactured crash sensor on 2018 models, corroded door wiring harness contacts delaying side air bag deployment across 2019-2023 Atlas and 2020-2023 Atlas Cross Sport models, a passenger occupant detection system fault deactivating the passenger air bag on 2018-2021 models, and a missing weld in a 2019 side air bag housing. A second pattern is wrong or badly folded air bags: the Tiguan Limited, 2020 Passat, 2021 ID.4 and 2019 Atlas/2020 Passat side bags. A 2024 recall covers 2017-2019 Beetle and Beetle Convertible plus certain Passat models whose driver side frontal air bag inflator may explode.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The air bags can deploy unintentionally (2018 Atlas air conditioning drain tube recall).
  • The air bags can deploy unintentionally (2020-2021 Jetta and Jetta GLI air bag control module recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these air bag notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park the vehicle outside. That includes the 2017-2019 Beetle inflator recall, the Atlas recalls for the drain tube, crash sensor, door wiring harness, occupant detection system and side air bag weld, the Tiguan Limited driver air bag recall, the 2018 Atlas and Tiguan passenger air bag recall, the Passat and ID.4 air bag folding recalls, the 2019 Atlas and 2020 Passat side air bag recall, and the Jetta control module recall. Each notice describes a risk that arises in a crash.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the driver's front air bag module on the Beetle, the steering wheel and driver air bag on the Tiguan Limited, the passenger air bag on the Atlas, Tiguan, Passat and ID.4, the crash sensor on the 2018 Atlas, the air bag control module on the Jetta, and the PODS sensor mat and harness on the Atlas. All free of charge.

Common questions

Why does the Atlas appear in so many air bag recalls?

Volkswagen filed separate Atlas notices for a twisted air conditioning drain tube, an incorrectly manufactured crash sensor, corroded door wiring contacts, a passenger occupant detection system wiring fault, and a missing weld at a side air bag housing. Each has its own model years and remedy.

What does the Beetle inflator recall say could happen?

The notice says the driver's side frontal air bag inflator may explode due to propellant degradation after long-term exposure to high absolute humidity, high temperatures and high temperature cycling, and that an explosion may send sharp metal fragments into occupants, causing serious injury or death.

Will my air bag warning light come on if my car is affected?

The notices listed here do not describe a warning light. They describe faults such as an incorrectly folded or incorrect air bag, a deactivated passenger air bag, delayed deployment, and, on two recalls, air bags that can deploy unintentionally.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
