GMC fuel system recalls: leaks, fires, and engines that stall

10 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-05

These ten GMC-linked fuel system recalls split into two repeating patterns. Several involve fuel leaks that raise fire risk: dual-tank overfilling on 2018 Silverado and Sierra 3500, a rollover valve on 2022 Cadillac XT5, XT6 and GMC Acadia, a fuel tank lock ring on 2024 Colorado and Canyon, a detaching fuel pump on 2018 Acadia, Canyon and Terrain, missing tank adhesive on 2021 Tahoe and Yukon, and loose fuel lines on 2021 XT5, XT6 and Acadia. Others involve fuel pumps that can cause an unexpected engine stall: a missing pressure regulator on 2020 Escalade, Sierra 1500 and Yukon models, a blocked nozzle on 2020 Acadia, a failing control module on 2021 Escalade, Suburban, Tahoe and Yukon, and inadequate fuel transfer on 2025-2026 Silverado and Sierra 3500.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The engine stalling unexpectedly while driving, described in several of the fuel pump recalls.
  • A fuel smell or visible fuel leak, which the notices link to an increased fire risk near an ignition source.
  • No driving symptom is described for the tank-overfill, rollover-valve, lock-ring, detaching-pump, missing-adhesive, or loose-fuel-line recalls - GM found these defects through inspection, not something an owner would notice while driving.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these ten notices carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the fuel-leak recalls, the dual-tank overfill, rollover valve, lock ring, detaching pump, missing tank adhesive, and loose fuel line recalls, GM says the resulting leak can increase fire risk, but does not tell owners to stop driving. For the stall recalls, the missing pressure regulator, blocked nozzle, failing control module, and inadequate fuel transfer recalls, GM says a stall increases crash risk, but likewise does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the affected part, a fuel pump, fuel tank, fuel supply lines, or the tank lock ring, depending on the recall, all free of charge. The newest recall, covering 2025-2026 Silverado and Sierra 3500, is fixed with an engine control module software update done by a dealer or over-the-air. No recall in this group charges the owner.

Common questions

Do any GMC fuel system recalls affect the same trucks twice?

Yes - GMC Sierra 3500 appears in both a 2018-2019 dual-tank overfill recall and a separate 2025-2026 recall where the fuel pump can't transfer enough fuel, causing a stall; the causes are unrelated.

Which GMC fuel recalls are about fire risk versus stalling?

The tank overfill, rollover valve, lock ring, detaching pump, missing adhesive, and loose fuel line recalls are all about leaks that raise fire risk. The pressure regulator, blocked nozzle, control module, and fuel transfer recalls are about engine stalls.

Is the GMC Acadia fuel pump recall fixed with a software update?

No. The 2018 Acadia fuel pump detachment and 2020 Acadia blocked-nozzle stall recalls are both fixed by dealers physically replacing the fuel pump, not a software update, free of charge.
