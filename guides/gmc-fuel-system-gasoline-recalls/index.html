GMC fuel system recalls: leaks, fuel pump failures and stalling

10 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 5, 2026

Ten gasoline fuel system recalls fall into three repeating patterns. Fuel pumps are the largest: a high pressure pump that may detach on 2015-2018 Canyon and 2017-2018 Acadia, a fuel pump missing a pressure regulator on 2020 Sierra 1500, Yukon and Yukon XL, a burr-blocked jet nozzle on the 2020 Acadia, and a fuel pump power control module on 2021 Yukon and Yukon XL. Tanks and lines form the second group: the 2021 Yukon tank missing adhesive, 2021 Acadia fuel supply lines, the 2022 Acadia rollover valve, and the 2024 Canyon fuel pump lock ring. Dual-tank 3500 trucks form the third, in 2016-2018 and again in 2026 Sierra 3500.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An engine stall without warning (2020 GMC Acadia fuel pump jet nozzle recall)
  • An unexpected engine stall from a fuel pump that fails or operates intermittently (2021 Yukon and Yukon XL fuel pump power control module recall)
  • An engine stall (2026 GMC Sierra 3500 rear fuel pump recall)

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these ten notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, and each says owners are not told to stop driving. Most describe a fuel leak that increases the risk of a fire in the presence of an ignition source: the 2016-2018 Sierra 3500 dual tank, 2015-2018 Canyon high pressure pump, 2020 Sierra 1500 pump, 2021 Yukon tank, 2021 Acadia fuel lines, 2022 Acadia rollover valve and 2024 Canyon lock ring recalls. The remaining notices describe an engine stall. They say nothing more about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls dealers replace fuel pumps and pump modules, fuel tanks and tank shells, and high pressure pipes; inspect and replace fuel supply lines on the 2021 Acadia; fully lock the fuel tank lock ring on the 2024 Canyon; and update the engine control module by dealer or over the air on the 2026 Sierra 3500. Every notice except the 2020 Acadia fuel pump one says free of charge.

Common questions

My Sierra 3500 has two fuel tanks — which recall applies?

Two dual-tank notices exist. One covers certain 2016-2018 Sierra 3500 trucks with gasoline engines, where the front tank fuel-level sensor may stick low. The 2026 notice covers 2025-2026 Sierra 3500 with RPOs L8T and N2N, where the rear pump may not transfer adequate fuel.

Is the fuel tank always replaced in these recalls?

It differs by recall. The 2021 Yukon notice says dealers replace the fuel tank. The 2022 Acadia notice says the fuel tank shell is replaced only if the rollover valve is dislodged or missing. The 2024 Canyon notice says the lock ring is inspected and locked.

Do the notices tell owners to stop driving until the fuel repair is done?

None of these ten notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. They state the risk — a fuel leak near an ignition source, or an engine stall — and direct owners to a dealer for the remedy. Beyond that they say nothing.
