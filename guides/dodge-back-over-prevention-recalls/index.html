Dodge Backup Camera and Pedestrian Warning Recalls Owners Should Check

9 NHTSA recalls · Dodge · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 13, 2026

Missing rearview images dominate these Dodge notices, while two recalls concern pedestrian warning sound. Journey appears in camera recalls covering certain 2018 and 2019-2020 vehicles; Durango appears in notices covering certain 2019-2020 and 2021-2022 vehicles. Charger issues differ: certain 2018-2020 Pursuit vehicles lose the camera display in stealth mode, certain 2024-2025 Charger vehicles may lack exterior sound, and certain 2026-2027 Charger vehicles have a camera-display recall. Hornet appears in a pedestrian siren recall for certain 2023-2024 plug-in hybrids and a display recall for certain 2023-2025 vehicles. A separate software recall affects certain 2019 Challenger and 2019-2020 Durango vehicles whose camera image can remain displayed after leaving reverse.

Warning signs to watch for

  • June 14, 2018 Journey recall: backup camera image lost while backing up.
  • August 27, 2020 Charger Pursuit recall: rearview image does not display in reverse when stealth mode is selected.
  • September 10, 2020 Challenger and Durango recall: rearview image remains displayed after shifting out of reverse.
  • October 17, 2023 Hornet recall: pedestrian alert siren fails to alert pedestrians in reverse.
  • June 12, 2025 Charger recall: missing exterior sound, including pedestrian warnings.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the nine listed Dodge back over prevention notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving for the Journey camera recalls, Charger Pursuit stealth-mode recall, Durango radio-software recall, Hornet display recall, or lingering camera-image recall. The Hornet pedestrian-siren notice and Charger amplifier-software notice also do not tell owners to stop driving. The August 2026 rearview-camera notice likewise does not tell affected Charger owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies: June 2018 replaces mirrors; August 2020 updates body control software; September 2020 and June 2024 update radio software, with OTA options. October 2023 inspects siren connections and secures or replaces parts as necessary. April 2025 updates software or replaces displays as necessary; June 2025 inspects/updates amplifier software; August 2025 replaces cameras; August 2026 updates radio software by dealer or OTA.

Common questions

Which Dodge camera recalls offer an over-the-air update?

The September 2020 lingering-image recall offers an optional OTA radio display software update. The June 2024 camera-image recall offers dealer or OTA radio software updates, as does the August 2026 notice. These notices describe their software remedies as free.

What does the Charger Pursuit stealth-mode recall change?

For certain 2018-2020 Charger Pursuit vehicles, the August 2020 notice says the rearview image will not display in reverse when stealth mode is selected. Dealers update body control module software to disable stealth mode in reverse, free of charge.

Are the Hornet siren and display remedies different?

The October 2023 siren notice specifies inspecting the rear pedestrian siren connection and securing or replacing the connector or siren as necessary. The April 2025 display notice specifies updating software or replacing the display as necessary. Both remedies are free.
