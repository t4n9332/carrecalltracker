Why do Dodge rearview cameras fail or show blank screens?

8 NHTSA recalls · Dodge · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-08-26

Eight Dodge recalls in this group center on backup visibility failing when drivers need it most. The Dodge Journey appears twice: in 2018 for a camera that can lose its image entirely, and again in 2019-2020 for a cracked microprocessor that keeps the rearview picture from displaying properly. Dodge Charger shows up in three separate recalls: a 2018-2020 defect where the camera won't display in "stealth mode," a 2019-2020 software bug that leaves the image stuck on screen after shifting out of reverse, and a 2024-2025 amplifier fault that can silence the Charger EV's pedestrian warning sound. Dodge Hornet has its own 2023-2025 display and pedestrian-siren recalls, separate from the camera issues above.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rearview camera screen stays blank or shows no image when backing up
  • Camera image doesn't appear when "stealth mode" is selected (2018-2020 Charger)
  • Rearview image stays on the display after shifting out of reverse (2019-2020 models)
  • No pedestrian alert sound when backing up (2023-2024 Hornet, 2024-2025 Charger EV)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these eight recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe camera and pedestrian-warning failures — a blank rearview screen, a stuck image, a stealth-mode display gap, or a missing back-up alert sound — but do not tell owners to stop driving. Owners should watch for the specific symptom named for their model and contact a dealer to schedule the free repair described in that recall's notice.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the rearview mirror, camera, or display module, update radio, body-control-module, or amplifier software, and inspect or secure the pedestrian alert siren connector, replacing it if needed. One recall (the 2021-2023 Chrysler/Jeep/Ram radio software fix) can also be applied over-the-air. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Which Dodge Journey years are affected by camera recalls?

Two separate recalls cover the Journey: 2018 models for a backup camera that can lose its image, and 2019-2020 models for a cracked microprocessor that keeps the rearview image from displaying properly. Each has its own dealer remedy.

Does the Dodge Charger camera recall affect all Chargers?

No. The 2018-2020 stealth-mode issue applies to Charger Pursuit models with stealth mode, while a separate 2018-2020 recall covers cameras that lose image entirely. Only vehicles built with the affected part are included.

Why does my Dodge Hornet have more than one camera-related recall?

The Hornet's recalls address different defects: a 2025 recall covers a display module that may not show the rearview image, and a separate 2023 recall covers a pedestrian alert siren that may be missing or disconnected.
