Why do Dodge backup camera and pedestrian alert recalls keep repeating?

8 NHTSA recalls · Dodge · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 26, 2025

Eight recalls here, and six are rear visibility failures under FMVSS 111. Software is the recurring cause: radio software on the 2021-2023 Pacifica and Grand Cherokee L, 2021-2022 Durango and 2022-2023 Ram ProMaster (66B/79B); radio display software that left the image on screen on 2019-2020 Durango and Ram pickups, the 2019 Challenger and several Jeep models; and body control module software behind the 2018-2020 Charger Pursuit stealth mode fault. Hardware accounts for the rest: a rearview mirror on the 2018 Journey and Ram pickups (U56), a display module on the 2023-2025 Hornet (13C/38C), and cracks in the microprocessor on 2019-2021 ProMaster and 2019-2020 Journey vehicles. The Hornet and Charger EV add two FMVSS 141 pedestrian sound recalls.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A loss of image display while backing up (2018 Journey and Ram 1500, 2500, 3500 recall, U56).
  • The rearview camera image remaining on screen after the vehicle is shifted out of reverse (2019-2020 Durango, Ram, Jeep and 2019 Challenger radio display recall).
  • No rearview image in reverse when stealth mode is selected (2018-2020 Charger Pursuit recall).
  • No sound to alert pedestrians when the vehicle is in reverse (2023-2024 Hornet pedestrian alert siren recall).
  • No exterior sound, including pedestrian warnings (2024-2025 Charger amplifier software recall, 44C).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

No notice in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That covers all eight, from the 2018 Journey and Ram pickup camera recall (U56) to the 2024-2025 Charger pedestrian sound recall (44C). For the 2019-2021 ProMaster and 2019-2020 Journey camera recall, the notice says interim letters describing the safety risk were mailed beginning September 30, 2025, with a second notice once the final remedy is available, anticipated July 2026. The other notices describe only the dealer repair for their own vehicles.

What the free repair involves

Remedies split between software and parts. Dealers update radio, radio display, body control module or amplifier software on the Pacifica, Grand Cherokee, Durango, Ram, Charger Pursuit and Charger recalls, with the 66B/79B radio update also delivered over the air. Others replace hardware: the rearview mirror (U56), the Hornet display as necessary, the ProMaster and Journey camera, or the Hornet siren connector. Each notice says free of charge.

Common questions

Which Dodge models appear more than once in this group?

The Hornet appears twice: the 2023-2025 display module recall (13C/38C) and the 2023-2024 pedestrian alert siren recall. The Journey also appears twice, in the 2018 backup camera recall (U56) and the 2019-2020 cracked microprocessor camera recall covering Journey and ProMaster.

Can the radio software recall be fixed without a dealer visit?

For the Pacifica, Grand Cherokee, Durango, Wagoneer and Ram ProMaster radio software recall (66B/79B), the notice says dealers will update the radio software or the software will be Over-The-Air, free of charge. The other notices in this group describe dealer work.

What does the Charger pedestrian sound recall involve?

On certain 2024-2025 Dodge Charger vehicles, the amplifier may be missing software, which can prevent exterior sound including pedestrian warnings, so they do not meet FMVSS 141. Dealers inspect and update the amplifier software as necessary, free of charge. Letters were mailed June 26, 2025.
