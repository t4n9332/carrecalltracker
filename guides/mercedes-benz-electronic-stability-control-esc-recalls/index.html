Mercedes-Benz stability control recalls mostly target Sprinter camper conversions

3 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Electronic Stability Control (esc) · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-04-17

Only three ESC recalls, and two of them involve Sprinter vans converted into campers and recreational vehicles — exactly the vehicles whose raised center of gravity makes stability control matter most. On 2012-2018 Sprinter 3500 camper and RV upfits the ESC unit may become disabled outright, traced to the rear wheel speed sensor. 2019 Sprinters built with the camper upfit option were delivered with electronic stability program software that was never updated for the taller, heavier body. The third recall is unrelated to vans: 2025 G 550, G 580 and AMG G 63 models may display an incorrect warning light during an actual ESP system failure, a federal safety-standard compliance problem rather than a loss of the system itself.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Stability control reported as disabled on 2012-2018 Sprinter 3500 camper conversions
  • A camper or RV that handles less predictably in a skid or on slippery surfaces
  • Anti-skid intervention that does not react properly in some situations on 2019 camper-upfit Sprinters
  • On 2025 G-Class, an incorrect warning light — so a real ESP failure may go unnoticed

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the three notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside, so you can keep using the vehicle. The trade-off is that the safety net may be missing when you need it. On Sprinter 3500 campers where ESC can be disabled, drive gently until the sensor is replaced. On the 2019 camper upfit, the anti-skid system may not react properly in some situations. On the 2025 G-Class, assume the dashboard may not tell you the truth about an ESP failure, and schedule the update.

What the free repair involves

Three different remedies. On 2012-2018 Sprinter 3500 camper and RV conversions, dealers replace the rear wheel speed sensor with a more robust sensor. On 2019 camper-upfit Sprinters, dealers update the stability program software for the higher center of gravity. On 2025 G 550, G 580 and AMG G 63 models, dealers update the ESP control unit software. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Does it matter that my Sprinter is a camper conversion?

It is the whole point of two recalls. They apply only to upfitted vehicles: 2012-2018 Sprinter 3500 campers and RVs, and 2019 Sprinters ordered with the camper upfit option, whose higher center of gravity the stability software was not calibrated for.

I've never seen a stability control warning light — can I skip the G-Class recall?

No. On 2025 G 550, G 580 and AMG G 63 models the warning light itself is the defect: the system may display an incorrect light during an actual ESP failure, so a missing or wrong warning is exactly what is being fixed.

How urgent is a stability control recall compared with a brake or airbag one?

None of these three carries a stop-driving order. Stability control matters most in a skid or emergency maneuver, so drive conservatively in slippery conditions and book the free repair — a sensor replacement or software update — rather than putting it off indefinitely.
