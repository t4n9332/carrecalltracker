Why is Mercedes-Benz recalling Sprinter and G-Class stability control?

3 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Electronic Stability Control (esc) · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-04-17

Mercedes-Benz's electronic stability control recalls in this group mostly affect Sprinter vans upfitted as campers or recreational vehicles, where the added weight and higher center of gravity strain the stability system. A 2012-2018 Sprinter 3500 camper recall covers an Electronic Stability Control unit that may become disabled. A 2019 Sprinter camper recall covers ESP software that was never updated to account for the camper upfit's higher center of gravity. Separately, a 2025 recall covers certain G 550, G 580, and AMG G 63 models, where the ESP system can display an incorrect warning light during a system failure rather than a stability defect itself.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Notice describes no specific warning sign before the ESC unit becomes disabled (2012-2018 Sprinter 3500 campers)
  • Notice describes no specific warning sign for the ESP software issue (2019 Sprinter campers)
  • Incorrect stability warning light displayed during an ESP system failure (2025 AMG G63)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three stability control recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving for the 2012-2018 Sprinter 3500 camper recall covering a disabled ESC unit, the 2019 Sprinter camper recall covering ESP software not updated for a higher center of gravity, or the 2025 G 550, G 580, and AMG G63 recall covering an incorrect stability warning light.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the rear-wheel speed sensor with a more robust part on the 2012-2018 Sprinter 3500 camper recall, update the ESP software on the 2019 Sprinter camper recall, and update the ESP control unit software on the 2025 G-Class recall. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why do Sprinter camper conversions have stability control recalls?

Both Sprinter recalls in this group involve vans upfitted into campers or RVs, where the added weight and higher center of gravity affect how the Electronic Stability Control system was designed to respond, according to the notices.

Does the G-Class stability control recall mean the system doesn't work?

No. The 2025 recall covers only the warning light: the ESP system may display an incorrect light during a failure, which could keep the driver from knowing about a real issue.

How does Mercedes-Benz fix these stability control issues?

Repairs vary by recall: dealers replace a wheel-speed sensor, update ESP software, or update the ESP control unit software, all free of charge, according to the notices.
