Why do Mercedes-Benz stability control recalls hit Sprinter campers and G-Class?

3 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Electronic Stability Control (esc) · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 17, 2025

Mercedes-Benz stability control recalls cluster in two very different places: Sprinter vans built out as campers, and the newest G-Class. Two of the three cover vans with a raised center of gravity. One 2021 campaign covers 2012-2018 Sprinter 3500 vehicles upfitted into campers or recreational vehicles, where the Electronic Stability Control unit may become disabled. A second 2021 campaign covers 2019 Sprinters with the camper upfit option, where the ESP software was not updated for a higher center of gravity. The third is newer and has nothing to do with vans: 2025 G 550, G 580 and AMG G 63 vehicles, where the ESP may display an incorrect warning light during an ESP system failure, failing FMVSS 126 and 101.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An incorrectly displayed warning light during an ESP system failure (2025 G 550, G 580 and AMG G 63 recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none carries a park-outside instruction. The notices state only what the defect affects: on the 2012-2018 Sprinter 3500 camper upfits, if ESC becomes disabled, driving stability may be impacted under certain driving conditions; on 2019 Sprinter camper upfits, the ESP may not respond as intended during certain driving scenarios; and on the 2025 G-Class, an incorrectly displayed warning light can fail to alert the driver to critical safety information. Beyond those statements, the notices say nothing about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

The remedies differ by recall. For 2012-2018 Sprinter 3500 camper and recreational vehicle upfits, dealers replace the rear-wheel speed sensor with a more robust sensor. For 2019 Sprinters with the camper upfit option, dealers update the software. For 2025 G 550, G 580 and AMG G 63 vehicles, dealers update the ESP control unit software. Each notice states the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Does my 2019 Sprinter camper need the stability control software update?

That recall covers 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vehicles equipped with the camper upfit option, where the ESP software was not updated for vehicles with a higher center of gravity. Dealers update the software free of charge, and a VIN check confirms whether your van is included.

Why does a stability control recall replace a wheel speed sensor?

That is the remedy the notice gives for 2012-2018 Sprinter 3500 camper and recreational vehicle upfits, where the Electronic Stability Control unit may become disabled. Dealers replace the rear-wheel speed sensor with a more robust sensor, free of charge. The notice does not say more.

Should I keep driving my 2025 G-Class while I wait for the update?

The notice does not tell owners to stop driving and gives no interim instruction. It says only that an incorrectly displayed warning light can fail to alert the driver to critical safety information. Dealers update the ESP control unit software free of charge.
