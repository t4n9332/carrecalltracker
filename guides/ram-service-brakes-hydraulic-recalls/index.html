Why do Ram trucks keep having brake and ABS recalls?

11 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-11-27

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Across these eleven Ram recalls, the same few systems keep resurfacing. The 1500 pickup has had its brake pedal separate from the pedal assembly twice, in 2019 and again in 2021 Classic SLT trucks. Cab Chassis 4500 and 5500 trucks have had rear brake line or hose problems three separate times - two involving aftermarket Liquid Spring and CLASS suspension kits that let the flexible line rub against suspension parts, and one involving an undersized factory hose. Several other recalls involve electronics rather than the hydraulic brakes themselves: a 2021 ProMaster City ABS module defect, a 2018 heavy-duty truck hydraulic control unit failure, and software problems in the 1500, 2500 and 3500 that can disable or hide stability-control warnings.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake pedal feels loose, moves too far, or separates when pressed (1500 pedal recalls)
  • Visible brake fluid leaks or dampness near hoses and lines (4500/5500 hose and line recalls)
  • Brakes dragging or overheating (2500/3500 caliper recall)
  • ABS, traction-control or stability-control warning lights on, or those systems not working (hydraulic control unit and software recalls)
  • ProMaster City notice: loss of braking or automatic braking functions can happen without prior warning

Is it safe to keep driving?

One of these eleven recalls tells owners to stop driving: the 2021 Ram 1500 brake-pedal-separation recall instructs owners not to drive until the vehicle is inspected and, if necessary, repaired. None of the other ten notices - covering the earlier 1500 pedal issue, the ProMaster City ABS module, the 4500/5500 hose and rear-brake-line recalls, the 2500/3500 caliper and stability-light recalls, and the heavy-duty hydraulic-control-unit recall - tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace or repair the affected part free of charge in every recall in this group: pedal assemblies, ABS modules and hydraulic control units, brake hoses and calipers, and rear brake line routing kits. Two recalls update ABS control module software instead of replacing hardware. All work, including inspection where the fix depends on found condition, is done at no cost to the owner.

Common questions

Why has my Ram 1500 had more than one brake pedal recall?

Ram issued separate brake-pedal-separation recalls for 2019 and 2021 model year 1500 trucks. The 2019 recall involved adjustable pedals moving too far and separating; the 2021 recall involved trucks built without a retaining clip on the master cylinder push rod.

Are the Ram 4500/5500 rear brake line recalls the same issue?

No. Two recalls cover aftermarket Liquid Spring and CLASS suspension kits where the rear brake line rubs against a suspension part; a separate recall covers a factory rear brake hose with too small an inside diameter that can rupture.

Does a stability-control or ABS warning light mean my brakes don't work?

Not necessarily. Two recalls involve software that can disable the electronic stability control system or keep its warning light from turning on when there is a problem, rather than a hydraulic brake failure itself.
