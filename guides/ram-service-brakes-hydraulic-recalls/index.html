Ram brake recalls: pedals, hoses, ABS modules and stability control

11 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 27, 2024

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Ram brake recalls fall into three clusters. The 4500 and 5500 cab chassis carry most of the plumbing faults: 2018 trucks whose brake hose ferrules lack anti-corrosion plating, 2019-2020 trucks whose rear brake hose inside diameter may be too small, and 2019-2020 trucks modified by Braun Northwest or Excellance, where the rear caliper flexible line may contact a suspension component. The Ram 1500 brings two pedal recalls: 2019 trucks with adjustable pedals, and 2020-2021 1500 Classic SLT missing the master cylinder push rod clip. Electronics make up the rest — ABS software on 2019 and 2021-2024 Ram 1500, the ESC warning light on 2019-2022 2500 and 3500 cab chassis, hydraulic control unit failure on 2017-2018 2500 and 3500, and ABS module damage on 2020-2021 ProMaster City.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brakes that drag and may overheat — the 2019 Ram 2500 and 3500 caliper seal recall.
  • An electronic stability control malfunction indicator light that does not illuminate during an ESC problem — the 2019-2022 Ram 2500 and 3500 cab chassis ABS software recall.
  • The 2020-2021 ProMaster City ABS module notice says the loss of braking performance or autonomous braking functions can occur without prior warning.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One of these eleven notices tells owners to stop driving: the recall on 2020-2021 Ram 1500 Classic SLT trucks built without the retaining clip that secures the master cylinder push rod advises owners not to drive their vehicles until they receive instructions on the inspection and repair. No notice in this group tells owners to park outside. For the other ten recalls the notices do not tell owners to stop driving; they state the risk the defect creates and the repair dealers will perform, and say nothing further about continuing to use the vehicle.

What the free repair involves

Dealers repair the 2019 Ram 1500 adjustable pedal assembly; install the push rod clip on 1500 Classic SLT; replace the ProMaster City ABS module; inspect 2018 4500/5500 brake hoses and replace them if needed; replace 2019-2020 rear brake hoses; install kits on Braun Northwest and Excellance trucks; replace 2019 2500/3500 front calipers and hoses; replace the HCU or its motor; and update ABS software. Free of charge.

Common questions

Is there a Ram brake recall where owners are told not to drive?

Yes — the recall on 2020-2021 Ram 1500 Classic SLT trucks built without the master cylinder push rod retaining clip. That notice advises owners not to drive their vehicles until they receive instructions on the inspection and repair. The other ten notices do not give that instruction.

Why does my Ram 4500 or 5500 show up in more than one brake recall?

Several separate recalls reach those trucks: 2018 cab chassis brake hose ferrules without anti-corrosion plating, 2019-2020 rear brake hoses with an inside diameter that may be too small, 2019-2020 units modified by Braun Northwest or Excellance, and 2017-2018 hydraulic control unit failures.

Can a software update fix a Ram stability control recall?

For two recalls the notices say dealers update the ABS control module software free of charge: 2019 and 2021-2024 Ram 1500, where the module may disable electronic stability control, and 2019-2022 Ram 2500 and 3500 cab chassis, where the ESC warning light may not illuminate.
