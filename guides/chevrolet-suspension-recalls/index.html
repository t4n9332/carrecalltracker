Why do Chevrolet Trax and other models have suspension control arm recalls?

5 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Suspension · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-06-28

Chevrolet's suspension recalls center on control arms and axle fasteners across several unrelated models. The Trax was recalled twice for the same underlying defect: an improperly welded joint in one or both front lower control arms that can partially separate, first covering 2017-2019 Trax and then extended to 2015-2018 model years. The 2019 Chevrolet 5500HD, part of a shared Isuzu-built recall, can have loosened rear axle U-bolt nuts that misalign the axle. The 2023 Cadillac Escalade, Chevrolet Suburban, Tahoe, and related GMC and Cadillac SUVs can have improperly heat-treated rear suspension outer control arm bolts that break. Separately, the 2024 Blazer EV has a manufacturing defect in its right front lower control arm that can cause it to fracture.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A front wheel that toes outward, affecting handling and steering (2015-2019 Trax, both recalls)
  • A stall from lost power to the rear wheels if the propeller shaft separates (2019 Chevrolet 5500HD)
  • Rear wheel misalignment if a suspension bolt breaks (2023 Escalade, Suburban, Tahoe, and related SUVs)
  • The notice for the 2024 Blazer EV front control arm fracture describes no driver-noticeable warning sign before failure

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five suspension recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This includes both Chevrolet Trax control arm weld recalls, the Chevrolet 5500HD rear axle bolt recall, the 2023 Cadillac Escalade, Chevrolet Suburban, Tahoe, and related SUV rear suspension bolt recall, and the 2024 Blazer EV front control arm recall. The notices tell owners to have dealers inspect and, if necessary, repair the affected parts, but none instruct owners to stop driving in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the affected control arms, axle bolts, or suspension bolts and replace them as necessary, free of charge, on the Trax, 5500HD, and 2023 SUV recalls. For the 2024 Blazer EV, dealers inspect and replace the right front lower control arm if needed. All repairs are performed at no cost to the owner.

Common questions

Why was the Chevrolet Trax recalled twice for the same suspension problem?

GM's first 2019 recall covered 2017-2019 Trax vehicles with improperly welded front lower control arm joints. A second 2019 recall extended the same fix to 2015-2018 Trax vehicles with the identical welding defect.

What's wrong with the 2019 Chevrolet 5500HD suspension recall?

Isuzu, which builds the 5500HD, found that rear axle U-bolt nuts may not have been properly tightened, letting the axle misalign. This can cause the propeller shaft to separate, cutting power to the rear wheels and causing a stall.

Are Cadillac and GMC SUVs affected by the same suspension issue as Chevrolet?

Yes. The 2023 rear suspension bolt recall covers the Cadillac Escalade and Escalade ESV, GMC Yukon and Yukon XL, and Chevrolet Suburban and Tahoe together, since they share the same rear suspension outer control arm bolts.
