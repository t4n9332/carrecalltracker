What Chevrolet suspension recalls say about control arms and axle bolts

5 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Suspension · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 28, 2024

Chevrolet's suspension recalls center on control arms. Two of them cover the Trax front lower control arms, where an improperly welded joint may break and partially separate the arm from the vehicle — one recall covering 2017-2019 Trax and a second covering 2015-2018 Trax. The 2024 Blazer EV has its own control arm recall, for a manufacturing defect in the right front lower arm that can cause it to fracture. The other two involve fasteners: rear suspension outer control arm bolts on the 2023 Tahoe and Suburban that may not have been heat-treated and can break, and rear axle U-bolt nuts on Chevrolet 4500XD, 4500HD, 5500HD and 5500XD medium-duty trucks that may loosen.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The attached front wheel toeing outward, affecting handling and steering — the Trax front lower control arm recalls.
  • A loss of power to the rear wheels and a stall — the Chevrolet 4500XD, 4500HD, 5500HD and 5500XD rear axle U-bolt recall.
  • A loss of vehicle control if the control arm fractures — the 2024 Blazer EV recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The Trax notices say a partial separation of the lower control arm could cause the attached front wheel to toe outward, affecting handling and steering. The Blazer EV notice says a control arm fracture can cause loss of vehicle control. The medium-duty truck notice says a misaligned axle may cause the propeller shaft to separate, leading to a stall. The 2023 Tahoe and Suburban notice describes rear wheel misalignment. No interim instructions are given.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the front lower control arms on the Trax recalls and replace them as necessary. On the 2024 Blazer EV, dealers inspect and replace the right front lower control arm as necessary. On the 2023 Tahoe and Suburban, dealers replace both rear suspension outer control arm bolts. On medium-duty trucks, dealers inspect and, if needed, replace and tighten the rear axle U-bolt nuts. Each notice says this is free.

Common questions

Why is the Trax in two control arm recalls?

Both describe an improperly welded joint in one or both front lower control arms that may break and partially separate the arm. One recall covers 2017-2019 Trax and began June 14, 2019; the second covers 2015-2018 Trax and began November 6, 2019.

Who handles the Chevrolet medium-duty rear axle recall?

Isuzu Technical Center of America issued it, covering 2017-2020 Chevrolet 4500XD, 4500HD and 5500HD and 2020 Chevrolet 5500XD trucks. Isuzu dealers inspect and, if needed, replace and tighten the rear axle U-bolt nuts, free of charge. Isuzu customer service is 1-866-441-9638.

What do these notices say about continuing to drive?

None of them tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. They describe what a failure could cause — a front wheel toeing outward on the Trax, a loss of vehicle control on the Blazer EV, a stall on the medium-duty trucks — and give no interim instruction.
