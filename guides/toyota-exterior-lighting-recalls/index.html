Why Do So Many Toyota Models Have Lighting Recalls?

10 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-06

These 10 recalls span headlight wiring, turn signals, and reverse lights across many Toyota models. The Tundra appears three times: headlight wiring that can overheat and increase fire risk, front turn signals wired too dim to notice, and reverse lights that fail after moisture enters the assembly. Water intrusion also causes a turn signal short circuit on the Venza Hybrid. The Supra, built with BMW, shares two headlight recalls with the Z4 for misalignment and control-unit failure. The RAV4 Prime and 4Runner each have a single headlight-aim or high-beam defect. The BRZ and GR86, sibling models, share a rear turn-signal recall, and the newest Camry Hybrid recall involves a cluster display failure that disables hazard and turn-signal lights.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Headlights or high beams that stop working, especially after wiring or control-unit failures
  • Turn signals or hazard lights that flash dim or stop flashing
  • Reverse lights that fail to illuminate, especially after exposure to moisture
  • Dashboard warnings, turn signals, and the seat belt reminder not displaying at startup (Camry Hybrid)
  • The headlight-wiring and headlight-aim recalls describe no warning sign before the defect occurs

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these 10 Toyota lighting recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving, not for the Tundra headlight-wiring recall that names a fire risk, the Venza and Tundra reverse-light recalls involving water, the Supra and Z4 headlight-alignment and control-unit recalls, the RAV4 Prime glare recall, the 4Runner high-beam recall, the BRZ and GR86 turn-signal recall, or the Camry Hybrid cluster-display recall.

What the free repair involves

Dealers repair the wiring harness or connectors, replace damaged bulbs and headlight, turn-signal, or reverse-light assemblies, replace headlight control units, correct or close headlight aiming caps, and update the instrument cluster display software on the Camry Hybrid. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Does the Tundra headlight wiring recall mean my truck could catch fire?

The notice says an overheated electrical connector increases fire risk, but it does not tell owners to stop driving. Dealers modify the wire harness and replace damaged bulbs, connectors, or the headlight assembly, free of charge.

Why do Toyota Supra and BMW Z4 share the same headlight recalls?

The Supra is built by BMW alongside the Z4 on a shared platform, so both models have had the same headlight-alignment and headlight-control-unit defects recalled together under BMW-issued notices.

Is the Camry Hybrid cluster display recall dangerous?

The notice says a failed display can disable hazard lights, turn signals, the seat belt warning, and the smart key reminder at startup, increasing crash risk, but it does not tell owners to stop driving.
