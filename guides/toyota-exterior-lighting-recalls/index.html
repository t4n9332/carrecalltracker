Why do Toyota headlights and turn signals keep getting recalled?

10 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 6, 2026

These ten Toyota recalls circle a few recurring trouble spots. The Tundra appears three times: overheating headlight circuits on 2018-2021 trucks, front turn signals that do not flash brightly enough on 2018-2020 LED-equipped trucks, and moisture in the reverse light assemblies on 2022-2025 Tundra and Tundra Hybrid. Headlight aim is a second theme, covering the 2020 Supra alongside BMW Z4 models and the 2021 RAV4 Prime, where horizontal adjustment can create glare. Rear turn signals are a third, on the 2021 Venza Hybrid and the 2022 GR86 shared with Subaru's BRZ. Two more stand alone: the 2022 4Runner, where the driver's side high beam may be inoperable after accessory fog light installation, and a 2025 Camry Hybrid instrument cluster that can deactivate the hazard lights and turn signals.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Dimming or failure of the rear turn signals and hazard lights — 2021 Venza Hybrid rear turn signal recall.
  • Front turn signals that do not flash brightly enough — 2018-2020 Tundra turn signal recall.
  • Loss of one or both headlights — 2020 Supra headlight control unit recall.
  • An inoperable driver's side high beam — 2022 4Runner recall.
  • Rear turn signals that intermittently become inoperative — 2022 GR86 and Subaru BRZ recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these ten notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. Each states a risk instead: the 2018-2021 Tundra headlight wiring recall describes an overheated connector that can increase the risk of a fire, and the remaining lighting recalls describe reduced visibility, increased glare, or the vehicle being harder for other drivers to see, increasing the risk of a crash. For the 2021 Venza Hybrid, the notice says interim owner letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed March 18, 2022, and that remedy parts were expected by June 30, 2022.

What the free repair involves

Remedies differ by recall. Dealers modify wire harnesses on the Tundra headlight and turn signal recalls, close the RAV4 Prime aiming caps, correct Supra headlamp adjustment plugs, install a 4Runner wiring harness, update the Camry Hybrid display software, and replace parts elsewhere: Supra headlight control units, Venza rear turn signal lights, GR86 rear lamp assemblies, and Tundra reverse light assemblies. Every notice says the work is free of charge.

Common questions

My Tundra is a 2019 — which lighting recalls could apply?

Two in this group cover 2019 Tundras: the headlight circuit recall for 2018-2021 trucks, where the connector may overheat, and the 2018-2020 LED headlight recall for front turn signals that may not flash brightly enough. The reverse light recall covers 2022-2025 trucks only.

Is my Supra covered by a Toyota recall or a BMW one?

Two lighting recalls here were filed by BMW and cover the 2020 Supra alongside BMW Z4 models: headlamps that can be adjusted horizontally, and headlight control units that may fail, causing loss of one or both headlights. Both notices list Toyota and BMW customer service numbers.

Does a lighting recall matter if my lights still seem to work?

The notices are written with "may" — the 2022-2025 Tundra notice says moisture may enter the reverse light assemblies and cause a light failure. Each notice states its own risk, such as increased crash risk from reduced visibility, and each says dealers will perform the remedy free of charge.
