Which Lincoln models are recalled for air bag defects?

5 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · AIR Bags · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 27, 2025

All five of these recalls involve air bag hardware — covers, fasteners, weld studs, sensors and the air bag itself. Three pair a Lincoln with a Ford SUV under one recall number: the 2020 Explorer and Aviator, whose side air bag and seat back module attachments may not have been properly tightened in the front seat backs; the 2021 Explorer and Aviator, whose side air bag attachment weld studs could detach during deployment; and the 2021 Navigator, listed with 2021 F-150 and Expedition and 2022 Super Duty, for front door side impact pressure sensor modules that may be damaged. The 2019 Nautilus without Adaptive Front Steering stands apart, with a driver frontal air bag cover that may detach. The newest covers 2025 Navigator passenger air bags.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2019 Nautilus air bag cover recall, the 2020 Explorer and Aviator seat back fastener recall, the 2021 Explorer and Aviator weld stud recall, the 2021 Navigator door sensor recall and the 2025 Navigator passenger air bag recall each describe the risk as injury in a crash, and each points owners to the dealer repair. Beyond scheduling that repair, the notices give owners no interim instructions.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the driver air bag module on the 2019 Nautilus, remove front seat back panels to verify the fasteners are tightened on the 2020 Explorer and Aviator, replace the side air bag assemblies on the 2021 Explorer and Aviator, replace the front door side impact sensors on the 2021 Navigator, and replace the passenger side air bags on the 2025 Navigator. All five notices say free of charge.

Common questions

Does my 2019 Lincoln Nautilus have the driver air bag recall?

Recall 18S38 covers certain 2019 Nautilus vehicles that are not equipped with Adaptive Front Steering. The notice says the plastic cover on the driver frontal air bag module may detach during air bag deployment, and dealers replace the driver air bag module free of charge.

Is the Aviator air bag recall the same as the Explorer one?

Two recalls list them together, but they are different. The 2020 recall covers 2020 Explorer and Aviator front seat back and side air bag fasteners. Recall 21C19 covers 2021 Explorer and Aviator side air bag attachment weld studs, with a different repair.

What happens with the 2025 Navigator passenger air bag recall?

Recall 25S51 says the passenger side air bag on certain 2025 Navigator and Expedition vehicles may have been manufactured incorrectly and can deploy with excessive force during a crash. Dealers replace the passenger side air bags free of charge, and letters were mailed August 8, 2025.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
