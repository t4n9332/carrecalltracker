Why has Lincoln recalled the Aviator and Navigator for airbags?

5 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · AIR Bags · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-06-27

Side air bag defects repeat across four of these five Lincoln recalls, but each involves a different cause. On 2020 Aviator vehicles, side air bag and seat back fasteners may not have been properly tightened. On 2021 Aviator vehicles, side air bag weld studs could detach during deployment. On 2021 Navigator vehicles, front-door side impact pressure sensor modules may have been damaged during supplier assembly. On 2025 Navigator vehicles, the passenger side air bag may have been manufactured incorrectly and deploy with excessive force. The fifth recall is different: on 2019 Nautilus vehicles without Adaptive Front Steering, the driver frontal air bag's plastic cover may detach during deployment.

Warning signs to watch for

  • None of these five notices describe a warning light or noticeable sign before a crash; the defects are identified through inspection or found only when an air bag deploys

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2019 Nautilus air bag cover recall, the 2020 and 2021 Aviator side air bag recalls, the 2021 Navigator sensor recall, and the 2025 Navigator excessive-force recall all state that the defect could increase the risk of injury if a crash occurs, and that dealers will complete a free repair. None of the five notices tells owners to stop driving in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace or repair the affected part for each recall: the Nautilus driver air bag module, the Aviator's fastener or side air bag assembly, the Navigator's side impact sensors, and the Navigator's passenger side air bag. All of these repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why was the Aviator recalled for air bags twice?

The 2020 Aviator recall involved side air bag and seat back fasteners that may not have been properly tightened. The 2021 Aviator recall was a separate defect: side air bag weld studs that could detach during deployment.

What's wrong with the 2025 Navigator air bag recall?

The passenger side air bag may have been manufactured incorrectly, which the notice says can cause it to deploy with excessive force during a crash. Dealers replace the passenger side air bags free of charge.

Is the Nautilus air bag recall related to the Aviator or Navigator recalls?

No. The 2019 Nautilus recall involves a separate defect from the Aviator and Navigator recalls: the driver frontal air bag's plastic cover may detach during deployment on vehicles without Adaptive Front Steering equipment, which increases injury risk in a crash.
