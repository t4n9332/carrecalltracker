Why does Ford keep recalling vehicles for fuel leaks and fires?

22 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-09

Nearly every recall in this group is rated critical and centers on a fuel leak that raises the risk of fire, though the specific cause varies by recall: cracked or leaking fuel injectors on the 2020-2023 Escape and 2021-2023 Bronco Sport, fractured fuel pump welds on 2018 Expedition, F-150, and Lincoln Navigator, a chafed fuel line on 2020 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator, tank welding or seam defects on 2020 Explorer, E-350/E-450/F-59 vans, and 2024 Maverick, and a detached filler tube on 2022 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator. One separate recall, on the 2012-2018 Focus, involves a canister purge valve fault that can cause the engine to stall while driving rather than a fuel leak.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine stalling while driving without warning and not restarting (2012-2018 Focus)
  • Visible fuel leak, fuel smell, or wet spots near the fuel tank or fuel lines
  • Malfunction Indicator Lamp (check engine light) illuminating, noted specifically for the 2024 Maverick fuel tank recall
  • Most of the other recalls describe the leak itself as the defect, without a separate driver-noticeable warning sign

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these recalls carry a blanket stop-driving instruction. For the 2024 Maverick fuel tank recall specifically, the notice tells owners to park the vehicle and shut off the engine immediately if a fuel leak is detected or the Malfunction Indicator Lamp illuminates. For the rest of the recalls in this group, including the fuel pump, fuel line, fuel injector, filler tube, and Focus stall recalls, the notices describe the fire or stall risk but do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace or inspect the affected part depending on the recall, fuel pumps, fuel lines, fuel tanks, filler tube clips, or fuel injectors, and for the Focus, reprogram the powertrain control module and replace the canister purge valve if needed. All repairs described in these notices are free of charge.

Common questions

Why are there so many different fuel leak recalls for Ford vehicles?

The notices describe different causes for each recall, fractured fuel pump welds, chafed fuel lines, cracked injectors, incomplete tank welds, and a detached filler tube, affecting different models and model years, rather than one single defect repeated across the lineup.

Is the Ford Focus stalling recall related to a fuel leak?

No. The Focus recall involves a canister purge valve that can cause excessive vacuum in the fuel vapor system, leading the engine to stall while driving without warning and without the ability to restart, a different defect from the fuel leak recalls in this group.

What should I do if I smell fuel or see a leak on my Maverick?

The 2024 Maverick fuel tank recall notice specifically advises owners to park the vehicle and shut off the engine immediately if a fuel leak is detected or the Malfunction Indicator Lamp illuminates. This instruction is not stated for the other recalls in this group.
