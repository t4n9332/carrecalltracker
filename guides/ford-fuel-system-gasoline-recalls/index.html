Why do Ford fuel systems keep leaking gasoline and causing recalls?

22 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-09

Fuel leaks drive nearly every recall in this group, and the Explorer turns up again and again: a leaking fuel pressure sensor on 2018 models with 2.3L and 3.5L GTDI engines, a chafed liquid fuel line on 2020 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator, an incompletely welded fuel tank inlet check valve on 2020 2.3L Explorers, a fuel filler tube that can detach on 2022 Explorer and Aviator, and an improperly tightened fuel line on 2022 2.3L models. Fuel tanks form a second pattern — drill damage on 2022 Mavericks, an open pinch joint on 2024 Mavericks, peeling paint and corrosion on 2022-2023 E-350 and E-450 and 2021-2022 F-59. Cracked 1.5L fuel injectors cover 2020-2023 Escape and 2021-2023 Bronco Sport.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2024 Maverick fuel tank recall names a detected fuel leak or an illuminated Malfunction Indicator Lamp.
  • The 2012-2018 Focus canister purge valve recall describes an engine stall while driving, without warning and without the ability to restart the vehicle.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these notices tell owners to stop driving. The 2024 Maverick fuel tank recall does add one instruction: owners are advised to park and shut off the engine immediately if a fuel leak is detected or the Malfunction Indicator Lamp illuminates. For the rest — the 2018 fuel pump welds on Expedition, F-150 and Navigator, the Explorer fuel line, sensor and tank recalls, the Escape and Bronco Sport injector recall, the E-350, E-450 and F-59 tank corrosion recall, the Transit generator fuel hose and the 2022 Maverick tank — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

All of these repairs are free of charge. Dealers replace the high pressure fuel pump on 2018 Expedition, F-150 and Navigator; the fuel line assembly on the 2018 Explorer; and the fuel tanks on the 2024 Maverick. The 2022 Maverick tank, the E-350, E-450 and F-59 tanks and the Transit fuel hose are inspected and replaced if necessary. Escape and Bronco Sport get engine control software and a drain tube.

Common questions

Do any of these Ford fuel system recalls tell me to stop driving?

The notices in this group do not tell owners to stop driving. The 2024 Maverick fuel tank recall does advise owners to park and shut off the engine immediately if a fuel leak is detected or the Malfunction Indicator Lamp illuminates.

Is the fuel tank or fuel line repair free?

The notices for these recalls say the work is free of charge, whether that is replacing the high pressure fuel pump, the fuel line assembly, the fuel tank, or inspecting a tank and replacing it only if necessary.

Why does the Explorer appear in so many Ford fuel recalls?

The Explorer notices name different causes: a leaking fuel pressure sensor on 2018 models, a chafed liquid fuel line on 2020 models, an incompletely welded tank inlet check valve on 2020 2.3L models, a detaching filler tube and a loose fuel line on 2022 models.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
