What keeps going wrong with Lincoln rear suspension parts?

6 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Suspension · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 23, 2023

All six of these Lincoln recalls sit at the rear suspension, and the same parts keep appearing: toe links, bolts and springs. The 2019 Navigator, shared with Expedition, has rear suspension toe link fasteners that may not be properly tightened to the frame. The 2013-2018 MKT with the Police Interceptor or SHO Performance Pack has rear toe links that may fracture. The 2020 Corsair may have insufficient clearance between the rear coil springs and the rear toe link brackets, resulting in a broken spring. The 2020 Aviator has rear lower control arm bolts that may not be heat-treated, the 2021 Aviator may have incorrect rear suspension module components, and the 2023 Nautilus has incorrectly manufactured rear shock absorbers.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Abnormal rear tire wear — in the 2019 Navigator toe link fastener recall, dealers inspect the rear tires for abnormal tire wear and replace them if necessary.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. The 2013-2018 MKT toe link notice says remedy parts were not available when owners were notified on March 3, 2020, and that a second notice would be mailed when remedy parts became available. The other five direct owners to a dealer for inspection or replacement. The notices say nothing else about whether to keep driving; Ford customer service is listed at 1-866-436-7332.

What the free repair involves

The work differs by recall. Dealers tighten the Navigator rear toe link fasteners and adjust rear toe alignment; replace the MKT rear toe links; trim the toe link bracket edge and install a new coil spring on the Corsair; replace both control arm bolts on the 2020 Aviator; replace incorrect suspension module parts on the 2021 Aviator; and replace Nautilus rear shocks and damaged parts. Free of charge.

Common questions

Which Lincolns are covered by the rear toe link recalls?

Two notices name toe links. One covers the 2019 Navigator, where rear toe link fasteners may not be properly tightened to the frame. The other covers 2013-2018 MKT vehicles with the Police Interceptor or SHO Performance Pack, where rear toe links may fracture.

Does the Nautilus shock recall involve the brakes?

Yes. The 2023 Nautilus notice says incorrectly manufactured rear shock absorbers can damage the rear brake hose, rear axle half shaft, wheel speed sensor and stabilizer bar end links, and that a damaged brake hose can extend stopping distance. Dealers inspect and replace these parts free.

How do I find out if my Lincoln is included?

Ford lists customer service at 1-866-436-7332 for these recalls, with recall numbers 21S11 for the 2020 Aviator control arm bolts and 21S47 for the 2021 Aviator suspension module. A VIN check shows which of the six notices apply to your vehicle.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
