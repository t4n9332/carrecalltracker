Why Is Ford Recalling Trucks Over Loose Wheels?

3 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Wheels · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-14

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Ford's wheel recalls in this group involve fasteners that were not properly installed or treated at the factory, rather than a defect in the wheel itself. The 2023 Bronco recall covers lug nuts on the driver's side that may have been improperly tightened, which carries a stop-driving instruction. The 2018 F53 stripped chassis recall covers front wheel hub studs that were not properly heat treated and could break. The 2025 F-53 and 2026 F-59 recall covers a front axle hub assembly that may be missing its spindle nut and cotter pin. In each case, the missing or defective hardware can allow a wheel to loosen or detach while driving.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A wheel that wobbles or feels loose while driving
  • Unusual vibration coming from a wheel or the steering
  • Vehicle handling that feels affected or harder to control
  • The notices describe no warning sign before a wheel stud breaks or hardware comes loose

Is it safe to keep driving?

One of these three recalls — the 2023 Bronco lug nut recall — tells owners not to drive their vehicle until the repair is completed, because of the risk of wheel detachment while driving. The 2018 F53 wheel stud recall and the 2025 F-53 and 2026 F-59 missing spindle nut recall do not tell owners to stop driving. None of the three recalls in this group carry a park-outside instruction, and the stop-driving instruction applies only to the 2023 Bronco recall.

What the free repair involves

Ford dealers address these wheel recalls by inspecting and, where needed, retightening the Bronco's lug nuts, replacing suspect front wheel hub studs on the 2018 F53, and replacing the front axle spindle nut and cotter pin on the 2025 F-53 and 2026 F-59. All repairs are performed free of charge, and none involve a software or over-the-air update.

Common questions

Why can't I drive my 2023 Ford Bronco right now?

If your 2023 Bronco is covered by the lug nut recall, Ford advises against driving it until a dealer inspects and, if necessary, retightens the driver's side lug nuts, free of charge, because loose lug nuts can allow a wheel to detach.

What is wrong with the wheel studs on the 2018 Ford F53?

The front wheel hub assembly on certain 2018 F53 stripped chassis vehicles may have studs that were not properly heat treated. If enough studs on one hub break, vehicle handling could be affected, increasing the risk of a crash.

What's missing on the recalled F-53 and F-59 models?

Certain 2025 F-53 and 2026 F-59 vehicles may be missing the front axle spindle nut and cotter pin, which could lead to wheel damage or a wheel coming off. Dealers inspect and replace the missing hardware free of charge.
