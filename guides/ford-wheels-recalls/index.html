Ford wheel recalls: lug nuts, wheel studs and hub nuts

3 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Wheels · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 14, 2026

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Three recalls, three different points where the wheel attaches to the vehicle. Ford recalled certain 2023 Bronco and Ranger vehicles because the lug nuts on the driver's side wheels may have been improperly tightened, and that is the one recall here carrying a stop-driving instruction. A 2018 recall covers certain F53 stripped chassis vehicles equipped with 22.5-inch front wheels, whose front wheel hub assembly may have wheel studs that were not properly heat treated. The newest covers certain 2025-2026 F-59 and F-53 vehicles where the front axle hub assembly may be missing the spindle nut and cotter pin. Two of the three cover F53 or F-59 stripped chassis rather than passenger vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One of the three notices carries a stop-driving instruction: for the 2023 Bronco and Ranger lug nut recall, Ford advises owners not to drive their vehicles until the remedy is completed. The 2018 F53 wheel stud notice and the 2025-2026 F-59 and F-53 spindle nut notice do not tell owners to stop driving, and none of the three tells owners to park outside. Beyond the Bronco and Ranger instruction, the notices say nothing further about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

Each remedy is an inspection with parts work as needed. Dealers inspect and, if necessary, retighten the lug nuts on 2023 Bronco and Ranger vehicles; replace any suspect front wheel hub studs on 2018 F53 stripped chassis; and inspect and replace the front axle spindle nut and cotter pin on 2025-2026 F-59 and F-53 vehicles. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Can I drive my 2023 Bronco while I wait for the lug nut recall repair?

Ford advises owners not to drive their vehicles until the remedy is completed. Dealers inspect and, if necessary, retighten the lug nuts free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 2, 2023, and Ford customer service is 1-866-436-7332.

Does the wheel stud recall cover every 2018 F53 chassis?

Ford's notice covers certain 2018 F53 stripped chassis vehicles equipped with 22.5-inch front wheels, where the front wheel hub assembly may have studs that were not properly heat treated. Dealers replace any suspect front wheel hub studs, free of charge.

What does a missing spindle nut mean on an F-53 or F-59?

Ford says the front axle hub assembly may be missing the spindle nut and cotter pin, and that a missing spindle nut may lead to brake system damage or wheel detachment and a loss of vehicle control. Dealers inspect and replace both parts free of charge.
