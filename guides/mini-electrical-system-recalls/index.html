Why are MINI hybrid and electric batteries being recalled for fire risk?

4 NHTSA recalls · MINI · Electrical System · model years 2020–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 15, 2024

Every recall in this MINI electrical group concerns the high voltage battery and the risk of fire. Two cover the plug-in hybrid Countryman SE ALL4 alongside BMW models: in one, loose welding beads may form a conductive path between hybrid battery cells; in the other, covering 2020-2021 Countryman, debris may have entered one or more of the hybrid battery cells during their production. The other two cover the all-electric MINI Hardtop 2 Door (Cooper SE), 2020-2024 — one because the high voltage battery housing may not have been sealed properly, allowing water to enter, the other because faults may occur in the high voltage battery or high voltage system, resulting in a short circuit. The remedies differ accordingly.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A warning message displayed in the vehicle — the Cooper SE high voltage battery and system fault recall tells owners that if this message appears, they should park outside and away from structures until the remedy is completed.
  • A vehicle stall — the Cooper SE battery housing recall states that a high voltage battery with water damage can cause a fire or a vehicle stall.
  • The two Countryman plug-in hybrid battery notices do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the four notices tells owners to stop driving. One carries a park-outside instruction: the Cooper SE high voltage battery and system fault recall advises that if a warning message is displayed in the vehicle, owners should park it outside and away from structures until the remedy has been completed. The hybrid battery debris recall instructs drivers not to charge their vehicles, not to drive in manual mode or sport mode, and not to use the shift paddles until the remedy is completed. The other two notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across the two plug-in hybrid recalls, dealers inspect the hybrid battery and, if necessary, replace battery modules. For the Cooper SE battery housing recall, dealers inspect and leak test the high voltage battery and repair it as necessary. For the Cooper SE high voltage system fault recall, dealers provide a software update. Every notice says this is free of charge.

Common questions

Can I charge my Countryman plug-in hybrid while I wait for the repair?

For the hybrid battery debris recall, the notice instructs drivers not to charge their vehicles until the remedy is completed, and also not to drive in manual mode or sport mode and not to use the shift paddles.

What should I do if my Cooper SE shows a battery warning message?

The high voltage battery and system fault recall advises owners that if a warning message is displayed in the vehicle, they should park it outside and away from structures until the remedy is completed. Dealers provide a software update, free of charge.

Why do some MINI battery recalls replace hardware and one only updates software?

The notices describe different defects. The two hybrid recalls have dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace battery modules; the water intrusion recall has dealers leak test and repair the high voltage battery; the system fault recall has dealers install a software update.
