MINI Cooper SE and Countryman: Why the Battery Fire Recalls?

4 NHTSA recalls · MINI · Electrical System · model years 2020–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-08-15

Four recalls address high-voltage battery defects across BMW-built MINI plug-in hybrid and electric models. Two 2020 recalls affect the 2020 MINI Countryman SE ALL4 (alongside several BMW plug-in hybrids): one from loose welding beads that can bridge battery cells and short-circuit, another from manufacturing debris inside battery cells that can also short-circuit. Two later recalls affect only the 2022-2023 MINI Cooper SE: a 2024 recall for a high-voltage battery housing that was not properly sealed, letting water enter and risking fire or a stall, and a second 2024 recall for faults in the high-voltage battery or system that can cause a short circuit.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A warning message displayed in the vehicle (2024-08-15 MINI Cooper SE high-voltage battery recall)
  • A vehicle stall linked to water damage in the high-voltage battery (2024-04-29 MINI Cooper SE recall)
  • The notices describe no owner-noticeable warning sign for the welding-bead or battery-debris defects (2020 recalls) — these were found through inspection, not a symptom.

Is it safe to keep driving?

One of these four recalls carries a park-outside instruction: for the 2024-08-15 MINI Cooper SE high-voltage battery fault recall, owners are told that if a warning message is displayed, they should park the vehicle outside and away from structures until the software update is completed. For the 2020-09-30 battery-debris recall, the notice adds an interim instruction to not charge the vehicle and to not drive in manual mode, sport mode, or use the shift paddles until the remedy is completed. The other two recalls do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the high-voltage battery modules and replace them if necessary (both 2020 recalls), inspect and leak-test the battery housing and repair it as needed (2024-04-29), or install a software update (2024-08-15). All repairs are free of charge across all four recalls.

Common questions

Which MINI models are covered by the battery welding-bead recall?

The 2020 MINI Countryman SE ALL4, along with several 2020-2021 BMW plug-in hybrid models. Loose welding beads inside the battery could bridge cells and short-circuit, raising fire risk. Dealers inspect and replace battery modules as needed, free.

What should I do if a warning message appears in my MINI Cooper SE?

On the 2024-08-15 recall for 2022-2023 Cooper SE models, owners are told to park outside and away from structures if a warning message displays, until the free software update is installed.

Why was my MINI Cooper SE's battery recalled for water damage?

On 2022-2023 Cooper SE models, the high-voltage battery housing may not have been sealed properly, letting water in and risking fire or a stall. Dealers inspect, leak-test, and repair the battery free of charge.
