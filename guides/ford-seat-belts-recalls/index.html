Ford seat belt recalls: buckles, retractors and pretensioners that fail

18 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 27, 2026

Assembly and attachment problems dominate. Buckles and anchors that are not properly secured cover the 2020 Explorer second-row center buckle, the 2021-2022 Mustang Mach-E and 2022 Maverick rear buckles, and the 2020-2021 Explorer and Aviator anchor bolts. Pretensioners and retractors form the second pattern: a badly crimped anchor on the 2019 Edge, a retractor pretensioner that may not function on the 2021 Edge, and pretensioners that may inadvertently deploy and lock the belt on 2018-2020 Expedition and Navigator. The Ranger appears twice, with an improperly assembled front passenger belt on 2019 SuperCab models and a misrouted belt on 2019-2021 Super Cab models. Child restraint issues cover the 2020 Expedition tension sensor and the 2021-2022 Transit and 2021 Transit Connect retractor.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2018-2020 Expedition and Navigator recall describes a seat belt that is locked and will not retract or extend.
  • The 2019-2021 Ranger recall has owners inspect their front seat belts and describes a front passenger outboard belt that may be misrouted.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One recall carries a conditional stop-driving instruction: for the 2019-2021 Ranger Super Cab, Ford provides owners with instructions to inspect their front seat belts, and owners who detect an issue with the driver's seat belt, are unsure of the results, or do not want to perform the inspection should not drive their vehicles and should contact a Ford dealership. For the other recalls in this group — the Explorer, Edge, Expedition, Navigator, Maverick, Transit, F-150 and van seat belt notices — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is free of charge. Most are replacements: the right front seat belt assembly on the 2019 Ranger, the driver's pretensioner assembly on the 2019 Edge, the second-row center seat assembly on the 2020 Explorer, the buckle on the 2020 Expedition, and the retractors on 2018-2020 Expedition and Navigator. The 2015-2018 F-150 recall removes B-pillar insulation and installs heat resistant tape.

Common questions

Does a seat belt recall mean I should stop driving my Ford?

Only the 2019-2021 Ranger Super Cab notice addresses this. It asks owners to inspect their front seat belts, and says owners who detect an issue with the driver's belt, are unsure, or skip the inspection should not drive and should contact a dealership.

Why was the Expedition recalled over a child in the front seat?

The 2020 Expedition notice says the seat belt tension sensor may classify a child occupant as an adult, which would enable the passenger air bag to deploy instead of disabling it in a crash. Dealers replace the seat belt buckle free of charge.

My belt won't pull out or retract — is that covered by a recall?

The 2018-2020 Expedition and Navigator notice describes a pretensioner that may inadvertently deploy and lock the seat belt so it will not retract or extend. Dealers replace the seat belt retractors as necessary, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
