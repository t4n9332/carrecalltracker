Why Do So Many Jeep Models Keep Getting Air Bag Recalls?

11 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-21

Eleven Jeep air bag recalls span 2017 through 2026, and one component keeps reappearing: the Occupant Restraint Controller, or ORC, which decides when air bags deploy. Incorrect or miscalibrated ORC software affected the 2019 Renegade twice, the 2021 Grand Cherokee L and 2022 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer, and the 2022-2026 Grand Cherokee. A second pattern is an unsecured air bag connector, found in the 2021-2022 Cherokee's side curtain air bag and separately in the 2021-2024 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L's side air bags. The rest are one-off causes: loose mounting nuts in a 2018 Compass, failed crash sensors in a 2019 Cherokee, an unwelded steering column module across several 2023-2024 Stellantis models, a seat belt buckle switch in the 2019-2023 Renegade, and missing bumper foam in a 2024 Grand Cherokee.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices describe no driver-noticeable symptom; several state the air bags can be disabled or deploy incorrectly without warning, found only through dealer inspection or software checks

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these 11 Jeep air bag recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; none of the notices tell owners to stop driving. That covers the loose mounting nuts, the ORC software and miscalibration issues, the failed crash sensors, the unwelded steering column module, the seat belt buckle switch, the unsecured side air bag connectors, and the missing bumper foam. Each notice says the affected air bags may fail to deploy, deploy incorrectly, or be disabled during a crash, which increases the risk of injury to occupants, but none directs owners to stop driving or park the vehicle outside.

What the free repair involves

Dealer remedies vary by cause: replacing the air bag module or ORC, updating ORC or steering-column software, replacing failed crash sensors, securing loose connectors, repairing the seat belt buckle switch connection, and installing missing bumper foam. Most repairs are software updates or part swaps completed at a dealership, and all are performed at no cost to the owner.

Common questions

What is the Occupant Restraint Controller these Jeep recalls mention?

It's the module that decides when to deploy air bags during a crash. Several recalls in this group, including two 2019 Jeep Renegade recalls and a 2021 Grand Cherokee L recall, involve incorrect or miscalibrated ORC software that can disable or mis-time air bag deployment.

Which Jeep models have had more than one air bag recall?

The Renegade, Cherokee, and Grand Cherokee each appear in multiple recalls in this group, covering different model years and different causes, from ORC software errors to unsecured side air bag connectors. Each recall addresses a distinct defect, not a repeat of the same problem.

Will I be notified if my Jeep is part of one of these recalls?

Yes. Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) mails owner notification letters once the remedy is ready, and dealers perform the repair free of charge. Owners can also contact FCA customer service directly using the recall number listed in their notice.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
