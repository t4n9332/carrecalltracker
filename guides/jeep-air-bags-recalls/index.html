Jeep air bag recalls: control module software and unsecured connectors

11 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-21

Eleven Chrysler (FCA US) recalls cover Jeep air bags, and two causes dominate. Four involve the occupant restraint controller: incorrect software on 2019 Renegade vehicles, a miscalibrated controller on 2019 Renegade Upland models, incorrect software that can disable air bags on the 2021 Grand Cherokee L and 2022 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer, and a software error causing delayed side air bag deployment on 2022-2026 Grand Cherokee and 2023-2025 Grand Cherokee L. Another four turn on hardware that was not properly secured — loose inflator mounting nuts on the 2018 Compass, an unsecured curtain air bag connector on 2021-2022 Cherokee, side air bag connectors on Grand Cherokee, and a buckle switch sensor connection on 2019-2023 Renegade. Grand Cherokee appears most often.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the eleven notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park the vehicle outside. Each describes an increased risk of injury in a crash: an air bag that does not deploy, that deploys incorrectly, that deploys late, or that has been disabled. Beyond the dealer remedy and the owner notification dates, the notices give owners nothing to do differently in the meantime. No notice in this group carries an interim instruction, a temporary measure, or a restriction on where or how the vehicle is used.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls dealers replace the passenger air bag module on the 2018 Compass, replace crash sensors on the 2019 Cherokee, replace or reprogram occupant restraint controllers, secure or repair air bag connectors, install bumper foam on the 2024 Grand Cherokee, and inspect and replace steering column control modules. Every notice states the work is free of charge except the 2019 Renegade Upland controller replacement, which does not mention cost.

Common questions

Which Jeep air bag recalls are fixed with a software update?

The 2019 Renegade occupant restraint controller software recall, the 2021 Grand Cherokee L and 2022 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer reprogramming recall, and the 2022-2026 Grand Cherokee and 2023-2025 Grand Cherokee L delayed side air bag recall are all handled by updating module software at a dealer.

Do any of these Jeep air bag recalls already have all vehicles repaired?

Two say so. The 2021-2022 Cherokee curtain air bag connector notice states all vehicles have been remedied, and the 2022-2024 Grand Cherokee and 2021-2024 Grand Cherokee L side air bag connector notice states all affected vehicles have been remedied. Check your VIN to confirm yours.

What does missing bumper foam have to do with the air bags?

The 2024 Grand Cherokee notice says the front bumper may be missing energy absorbing foam, which can cause the front air bags not to deploy as intended. Dealers inspect the vehicle and install the foam as necessary, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
