Jeep Air Bag Recalls Involve Software, Connectors, and Deployment

11 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 21, 2026

Jeep's air bag notices repeatedly concern deployment failures linked to restraint controller software, calibration, sensors, or connections. Grand Cherokee models recur prominently: a connector recall covers 2022-2024 Grand Cherokee and 2021-2024 Grand Cherokee L vehicles, while a software recall concerns delayed side air bag deployment in 2022-2026 Grand Cherokee and 2023-2025 Grand Cherokee L vehicles. The 2019 Renegade has separate software and Upland-trim controller calibration notices; 2019-2023 Renegade vehicles also appear in a buckle switch sensor connection recall. Other defects include loose passenger air bag mounting nuts in the 2018 Compass and potentially failing crash sensors in the 2019 Cherokee. These defects have distinct remedies.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The supplied Jeep air bag notices do not tell owners to stop driving, and none includes a park-outside instruction. Their stated hazards include disabled air bags, improper or delayed deployment, and loose mounting nuts that could become projectiles during deployment. The Y79 notice specifically says incorrect controller software may disable driver, passenger, and knee air bags without warning. That statement belongs to certain 2021 Grand Cherokee L and 2022 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer vehicles; it does not describe the other recalls.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies include T73 module replacement; V42, Y79, and 01D software work; V63 sensor replacement; 14B/33B steering column control module inspection/replacement; 82B connection repair; 71B and B3B connector securing; and 41B foam inspection/installation. V73 specifies ORC replacement but does not state it is free. Each remedy applies to its named recall.

Common questions

Which Grand Cherokee air bag recall concerns delayed deployment?

Recall 01D covers certain 2022-2026 Grand Cherokee and 2023-2025 Grand Cherokee L vehicles. An occupant restraint controller software error may delay side air bag deployment during a crash. Dealers update the controller software free of charge.

Why do the 2019 Renegade notices specify different controller repairs?

V42 concerns incorrect software that may disable passive safety features and specifies a free software update. V73 concerns an incorrectly calibrated controller in certain Upland-trim vehicles and specifies controller replacement. The supplied V73 notice does not state that replacement is free.

What does the 2024 Grand Cherokee missing-foam recall require?

Recall 41B concerns potentially missing energy absorbing foam in the front bumper of certain 2024 Grand Cherokee vehicles. The notice says this can affect front air bag deployment. Dealers inspect the vehicles and install the foam as necessary, free of charge.
