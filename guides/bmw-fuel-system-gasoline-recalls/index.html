BMW fuel system recalls: leaks, tank welds and fire risk

5 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-07-20

Fuel leaking is the thread through this BMW group: four of the five notices end in a leak and an increased risk of fire. Welds fail twice, and both times the dealer replaces the whole tank — the weld between the fuel tank halves on 2020-2021 Z4 and 2020-2021 Toyota Supra, and the inlet check valve weld on 2021 X3 and X4, which may allow the filling pipe to separate from the fuel tank. The X3 returns in 2022, where a cracked high-pressure fuel rail can leak within the engine compartment, alongside the X1 xDrive28i and 228i Gran Coupe. M models account for the rest: a 2020-2021 M2 and 2020 M4 injector issue, and a 2018 M5 fuel gauge fault.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A fuel gauge displaying an inaccurate and possibly larger amount of fuel than is actually available (2018 M5 recall).
  • A stall, which the 2018 M5 notice says an incorrect fuel reading may lead to.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside — including the four recalls that describe a fuel leak and an increased risk of fire. The 2021 X3 and X4 notice says owners were also contacted by telephone and that, to date, all repairs have been done. The 2018 M5 notice says an incorrect fuel reading may lead to the vehicle stalling. Beyond naming the risk and the free repair, the notices give owners no interim instructions.

What the free repair involves

Every notice says the work is free. Dealers replace the fuel tank on the Z4 and Supra and on the 2021 X3 and X4; replace the high-pressure fuel rail on the 2022 X1, X3 and 228i Gran Coupe; secure the in-tank fuel line on the M5; and on the M2 and M4 inspect the fuel injectors, replacing the injectors and fuel rail if a damping component is missing.

Common questions

Which BMW models get a new fuel tank under these recalls?

Two recalls replace the tank: 2020-2021 Z4 and 2020-2021 Toyota Supra, where the weld between the fuel tank halves may fail, and 2021 X3 sDrive30i, xDrive30i, M40i, X3M and X4 xDrive30i, where the inlet check valve weld may fail.

My 2021 X3 is on the list but I never received a letter. Why?

That notice says owners were contacted by telephone, that to date all repairs have been done, and that no further owner notification letters will be sent. Owners can still contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 to check their vehicle.

What does the M5 recall say about the fuel gauge?

It says the fuel level sensor may become stuck against an in-tank fuel line, causing the gauge to display an inaccurate and possibly larger amount of fuel than is available, and that an incorrect reading may lead to stalling. Dealers secure the line free of charge.
