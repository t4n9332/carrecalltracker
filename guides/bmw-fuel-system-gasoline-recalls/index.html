Why are several BMW fuel system recalls rated critical for fire risk?

5 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-07-20

Three of these five BMW fuel system recalls are rated critical because they involve fuel leaks that raise fire risk: a fuel tank weld that can fail on 2020-2021 Z4 and Toyota Supra models, a separate tank weld issue on 2021 X3 and X4 vehicles, and a high-pressure fuel rail that can crack on 2022 X1 and X3 vehicles. The other two recalls are rated high rather than critical: a 2018 M5 fuel gauge that can show more fuel than is actually in the tank, and 2020-2021 M2 Coupe and 2020 M4 Coupe fuel injectors installed without a damping component, which can damage the fuel line connection over time.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Fuel gauge showing a larger amount of fuel than is actually available, which the notice says may lead to stalling (2018 M5)
  • The Z4/Supra and X3/X4 fuel tank weld recalls describe no noticeable warning sign before a leak occurs
  • The X1/X3 fuel rail crack and M2/M4 fuel injector recalls also describe no noticeable warning sign before a leak occurs

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five fuel system recalls include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the Z4/Supra fuel tank weld, the X3/X4 fuel tank weld, the X1/X3 fuel rail crack, the M5 fuel gauge, and the M2/M4 fuel injector recalls do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice directs owners to have their dealer perform the free repair rather than instructing them to park outside or avoid driving in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the fuel tank on the Z4, Supra, X3 and X4 recalls, replace the high-pressure fuel rail on the X1 and X3, secure the in-tank fuel line on the M5, and inspect the M2/M4's fuel injectors, replacing the injectors and fuel rail if a damping component is missing. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which BMW fuel system recalls in this group carry the most serious risk?

Three are rated critical: the Z4/Supra tank weld, the X3/X4 tank weld, and the X1/X3 fuel rail crack, because each can leak fuel near a possible ignition source.

Does the M5 fuel gauge recall mean the car can run out of gas unexpectedly?

The notice says a fuel level sensor stuck against an in-tank fuel line can show more fuel than is actually available, which may lead to stalling.

Is the M4 fuel injector recall the same issue as the M2 Coupe?

Yes. The notice covers both 2020-2021 M2 Coupe and 2020 M4 Coupe vehicles for fuel injectors installed without a damping component.
