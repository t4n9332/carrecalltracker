Why does my Infiniti's backup camera go blank in reverse?

5 NHTSA recalls · Infiniti · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-11-26

Five separate recalls cover the same underlying problem: software errors that knock out the rearview camera image in Infiniti and shared Nissan vehicles. The 2025 QX80 recall involves an Around View Monitor that can freeze and go blank in reverse. A 2018-2019 recall (Q50, Q60, QX30, QX80, and many Nissan models) let owners turn the camera image off and have it stay off. A 2021 recall (Q50, Q60, QX80) involved a software error deactivating the control unit screen. Two more recent recalls, one covering the 2024-2025 Rogue and 2025 QX80, and another covering the 2025 QX80, Armada and Murano, involve similar blank-screen software errors and can be fixed over the air.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The rearview camera screen freezes or goes blank when shifting into reverse
  • The backup camera image is turned off in settings and stays off the next time you reverse
  • The screen deactivates entirely, so no rear-view image displays at all

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the five recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2025 QX80 recall, the 2018-2019 multi-model recall, the 2021 Q50/Q60/QX80 recall, the 2024-2025 Rogue/QX80 recall, and the 2025 QX80/Armada/Murano recall all leave owners free to keep driving; the notices only state that a missing or frozen rearview camera image reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.

What the free repair involves

Dealers reprogram or update the camera and infotainment software across all five recalls, free of charge. Two of the five recalls, covering the 2024-2025 Rogue/QX80 and the 2025 QX80/Armada/Murano, can also be fixed with an over-the-air software update instead of a dealer visit.

Common questions

Can I keep driving my Infiniti if the backup camera doesn't work?

The notices for these recalls do not instruct owners to stop driving. They state that a missing rearview camera image increases the risk of a crash while backing up, but do not require you to park the vehicle.

Do I need to bring my Infiniti to a dealer for the camera recall?

For most of these recalls, yes, a dealer updates the software. Two recalls, covering the 2024-2025 Rogue/QX80 and the 2025 QX80/Armada/Murano, can also be fixed with an over-the-air update, without a dealer visit.

Why does my Infiniti's rearview camera stay off after I turn it off once?

In the 2018-2019 recall, the camera settings could be adjusted so the rear-view image no longer appeared, and that setting was retained the next time the vehicle was shifted into reverse.
