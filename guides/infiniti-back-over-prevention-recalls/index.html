Infiniti rearview camera recalls: blank screens and failed rear visibility

5 NHTSA recalls · Infiniti · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 26, 2025

Software is behind almost all of these Infiniti backup camera recalls, and the QX80 appears in every one of the five. The 2025 QX80 recall covers vehicles with an Around View Monitor whose rearview camera may freeze and show a blank screen in reverse. A separate 2024-2025 Rogue and 2025 QX80 recall and a 2025 Murano, 2025-2026 Armada and QX80 recall also describe a blank rearview image, the latter from an infotainment error at startup. The 2021 Q50, Q60 and QX80 recall involves the audio/video control unit screen deactivating. The oldest, covering 2018-2019 Q50, Q60, QX30 and QX80 and 2019 QX50, QX60, Q70 and Q70L, concerns settings that hide the rear view image.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A rearview camera that freezes and displays a blank screen in reverse (2025 QX80 Around View Monitor recall).
  • No image in the back-up camera display (2018-2019 Nissan and Infiniti back-up camera settings recall).
  • The rear-view image not displaying (2021 Q50, Q60 and QX80 recall).
  • A blank screen in reverse (2024-2025 Rogue and 2025 QX80 recall).
  • The rearview camera image not displaying when shifted into reverse (2025 Murano, Armada and Infiniti QX80 recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. Each describes a rearview image that may not appear while the vehicle is in reverse, and each states that reduced rear visibility increases the risk of a crash. The 2025 QX80, 2021 Q50, Q60 and QX80, 2024-2025 Rogue and 2025 QX80, and 2025 Murano, Armada and QX80 notices all state that the vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, Rear Visibility. Beyond that, the notices say nothing about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

All five remedies are software. Dealers reprogram the Around View Monitor software on the 2025 QX80, update the back-up camera settings software on the 2018-2019 vehicles, and update the control unit software on the 2021 Q50, Q60 and QX80. The 2024-2025 Rogue and 2025 QX80 recall and the 2025 Murano, Armada and QX80 recall are done by an over-the-air update or by a dealer. All are free of charge.

Common questions

Can the Infiniti rearview camera recall be fixed over the air?

Two of the five say so. The 2024-2025 Rogue and 2025 QX80 recall and the 2025 Murano, 2025-2026 Armada and Infiniti QX80 recall state the infotainment software will be updated over the air or by a dealer, free of charge.

Why is my QX80 in more than one backup camera recall?

The notices describe separate software problems: an Around View Monitor error on the 2025 QX80, an audio/video control unit error on the 2021 Q50, Q60 and QX80, a blank screen on 2024-2025 Rogue and 2025 QX80, and an infotainment startup error.

What does the 2018-2019 back-up camera settings recall actually cover?

The notice states the back-up camera and display settings can be adjusted so the rear view image is no longer visible, and the system retains that setting the next time the vehicle is in reverse. Dealers update the settings software free of charge.
