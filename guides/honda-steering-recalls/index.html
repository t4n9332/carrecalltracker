Why does Honda keep recalling vehicles for steering gearbox problems?

8 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-30

Honda's steering recalls in this group center on the gearbox and rack assembly. A loose ball bearing can lock the steering gear on the 2023-2024 Pilot, water intrusion can corrode and bind the gearbox in the 2023-2024 Odyssey and 2024 Acura RDX, and a ball joint can detach inside the gearbox on the 2023 Passport and Ridgeline. A separate manufacturing defect causes excess friction and difficulty steering in the 2022-2025 Civic, Civic Hatchback, CR-V, CR-V Hybrid, HR-V and Acura Integra. Two more recalls affect the 2017-2018 Civic and CR-V, where a dislodged torque-sensor magnet reversed steering assist, and the 2022-2024 Civic, where a poorly assembled replacement steering rack can chafe a tire. Two motorcycle recalls cover a loosening handlebar lock screw on the CMX300 and CMX500.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering that suddenly assists in the opposite direction during a full-lock turn (2017-2018 Civic and CR-V)
  • Noticeably harder or stiffer steering effort (2022-2025 Civic, Civic Hatchback, CR-V, CR-V Hybrid, HR-V and Acura Integra)
  • A loose or detached handlebar lock screw on CMX300 and CMX500 motorcycles
  • Visible tire chafing or damage near the lower suspension or tie rod on a repaired 2022-2024 Civic steering rack
  • The notices describe no warning sign before a loss of steering control or lock-up on the Pilot, Odyssey, Acura RDX, Passport and Ridgeline

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these eight steering recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For each one, the notice tells owners only that the defect increases the risk of a crash or a loss of steering control - it does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside. Owners should still contact their dealer to schedule the free repair described in the notice for their specific vehicle, since the affected component and repair vary by model and recall.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, dealers inspect and replace the electric power steering rack or gearbox assembly (Pilot, Odyssey, Acura RDX, Passport, Ridgeline), replace the original steering gearbox (2018 Civic/CR-V), inspect and replace the steering rack for tire damage (2022 Civic), replace a worm gear spring and grease (Civic, CR-V, HR-V, Integra), or replace a handlebar lock screw with thread lock (CMX motorcycles). All repairs are free.

Common questions

Which Honda models have had steering gearbox recalls?

The Pilot, Odyssey, Passport, Ridgeline, Civic, Civic Hatchback, CR-V, CR-V Hybrid, HR-V, and Acura Integra and RDX have each had a separate steering gearbox or rack recall between 2018 and 2025, for causes ranging from loose bearings to corrosion and manufacturing friction.

Is the CMX300 or CMX500 handlebar lock screw recall related to the steering gearbox recalls?

No. The two CMX300 and CMX500 recalls involve a handlebar lock screw that can loosen and interfere with steering on the motorcycle; they are unrelated to the car and SUV steering gearbox or rack defects described in the other six recalls.

Does Honda tell owners to stop driving for any of these steering recalls?

No. None of the eight steering recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Each notice describes an increased crash risk from the specific defect but tells owners only to schedule the free dealer repair.
