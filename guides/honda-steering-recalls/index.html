Why do Honda steering gearboxes and racks keep getting recalled?

8 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 30, 2026

Eight steering recalls hit Honda and Acura, and the steering gearbox dominates. Gearbox and electric power steering rack problems cover a loose torque sensor magnet on 2017-2018 Civic and CR-V, a loose ball bearing on 2023-2024 Pilot, water intrusion and corrosion on 2023-2024 Odyssey and 2024 Acura RDX, an inner ball joint housing that can loosen on 2023 Passport and Ridgeline, and excessive internal friction on 2022-2025 Civic and Civic Hatchback and 2023-2025 CR-V, CR-V Hybrid, HR-V and Acura Integra. A separate recall covers 2022-2024 Civic 4-door and 5-door cars that received a replacement power steering rack as a service repair. Two more cover a handlebar lock screw on CMX300, CMX500, CL500 and SCL500 motorcycles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The gearbox friction recall on the 2022-2025 Civic and 2023-2025 CR-V, HR-V and Acura Integra describes difficulty steering the vehicle.
  • The 2017-2018 Civic and CR-V recall describes steering assist being applied in the opposite direction during a full lock turn.
  • The 2023-2024 Pilot recall describes a loss of steering control or steering lock-up.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these eight notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That covers the 2017-2018 Civic and CR-V torque sensor recall, the serviced Civic steering rack recall, the 2023-2024 Pilot gearbox recall, the Odyssey and Acura RDX corrosion recall, the 2023 Passport and Ridgeline ball joint housing recall, the gearbox friction recall, and both handlebar lock screw recalls. For the July 2026 handlebar screw recall the notice says interim letters about the safety risk were expected in September 2026, with further letters once the final remedy is available.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the steering gearbox assembly on the 2017-2018 Civic and CR-V, and replace the electric power steering rack on the Pilot, Odyssey and Acura RDX recalls. On the serviced Civic and the 2023 Passport and Ridgeline they inspect and replace as necessary. The friction recall replaces the worm gear spring and adds or redistributes grease; the motorcycle recalls replace the handlebar lock screw and apply thread lock. All free.

Common questions

My Civic already had its steering rack replaced. Is that a separate recall?

One recall covers certain 2022-2024 Civic 4-door and 5-door cars that received a replacement power steering rack as a service repair. The notice says the rack may have been incorrectly assembled, allowing the tire to chafe. Dealers inspect and replace the rack as necessary, free.

Does the steering corrosion recall cover my Odyssey?

It covers certain 2023-2024 Odyssey and 2024 Acura RDX vehicles where the steering gear box assembly may not have been tightened properly, letting water enter and cause corrosion and binding gears. Dealers replace the electric power steering rack free; letters were mailed April 4, 2024.

What should CMX300 and CMX500 riders expect?

Both handlebar lock screw notices say the screw may loosen and detach, and a detached screw may lodge between the frame and stem. Dealers replace the screw and apply thread lock, free. The July 2026 notice says further letters follow once the final remedy is available, anticipated Winter 2026.
