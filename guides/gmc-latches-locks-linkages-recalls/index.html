GMC Sierra hood and tailgate latch recalls: what can open while driving

3 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Latches/locks/linkages · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-11-26

All three GMC-linked latch recalls affect heavy-duty Sierra and Silverado trucks. A 2020 recall covers 2020 Silverado 2500, 3500 and Sierra 2500, 3500 whose hood-latch striker wire may not have been heat-treated properly and can fracture, letting the hood open while driving. Two later recalls cover the same trucks' power-unlatching tailgates: a 2024 recall spanning 2020-2024 Silverado 2500, 3500 and Sierra 2500, 3500, and a second 2024 recall limited to the 2024 model year, both caused by the tailgate's electronic release switch short-circuiting from water intrusion, which can unlatch the tailgate.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The hood opening unexpectedly while driving (2020 hood-latch recall).
  • The tailgate unlatching on its own, including while the truck is parked, on trucks with power-unlatching tailgates.
  • No advance warning sign is described for either defect - GM identified both through reports and inspection.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three notices carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For both tailgate recalls, GM's notices tell owners to check that the tailgate is closed and latched before driving, an interim step stated in those two notices specifically. The hood-latch recall carries no such interim instruction; GM says a fractured striker wire can let the hood open while driving, but does not tell owners to stop driving, and no recall in this group carries a park-outside instruction either.

What the free repair involves

For the hood-latch recall, dealers replace the entire hood assembly, free of charge. For both tailgate recalls, dealers replace the exterior touchpad switch assembly that controls the power-unlatching tailgate, also free of charge. No inspection-only fix is described for these three recalls.

Common questions

Is my GMC Sierra's tailgate recall the same as the hood recall?

No. The hood-latch recall involves a striker wire that can fracture and let the hood open, while the two tailgate recalls involve a water-damaged electronic switch that can unlatch the tailgate; they have separate causes and repairs.

Why were there two separate GMC Sierra tailgate recalls?

The first, in 2024, covers 2020-2024 Silverado and Sierra 2500/3500 trucks; a second 2024 recall covers only the 2024 model year with the same water-intrusion switch defect. Both are fixed by replacing the touchpad switch assembly.

What should I do about the GMC Sierra tailgate recall right now?

The notices say to check that the tailgate is closed and latched before driving. Dealers will replace the exterior touchpad switch assembly free of charge once parts and an appointment are available.
