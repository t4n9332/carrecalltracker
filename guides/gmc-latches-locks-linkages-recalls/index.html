Why do GMC Sierra hoods and tailgates unlatch on their own?

3 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Latches/locks/linkages · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 26, 2024

All three recalls in this group land on heavy-duty Sierra pickups, and they split between one hood problem and two tailgate problems. The 2020 recall covers 2020 Sierra 2500 and 3500 trucks whose hood-latch striker wires may not have been heat-treated properly, possibly causing them to fracture so the hood may open unexpectedly while driving. The other two involve the power-unlatching tailgate: one covering 2020-2024 Sierra 2500 and 3500 trucks, and a later one covering 2024 Sierra HD 2500 and 3500 trucks. In both, the tailgate's electronic gate-release switch may short circuit due to water intrusion, which can result in the tailgate inadvertently unlatching while the vehicle is in Park.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none asks owners to park outside. For the two tailgate recalls — the one covering 2020-2024 Sierra 2500 and 3500 trucks and the later one covering 2024 Sierra HD 2500 and 3500 trucks — the notices advise owners to check that the tailgate is closed and latched before driving. The 2020 hood-latch striker recall on 2020 Sierra 2500 and 3500 trucks carries no such instruction, and its notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

The remedies differ. For the hood-latch striker recall on 2020 Sierra 2500 and 3500 trucks, dealers will replace the hood assembly. For both tailgate recalls, dealers will replace the exterior touchpad switch assemblies. All three notices state the work is done free of charge, and none of the three is handled over the air.

Common questions

Does the tailgate recall apply to every Sierra HD?

Both tailgate recalls cover vehicles equipped with power-unlatching tailgates. One covers 2020-2024 Sierra 2500 and 3500; the later one covers 2024 Sierra HD 2500 and 3500. Check your VIN with GMC to confirm whether your truck is included.

What do the notices tell me to do while I wait for the tailgate repair?

For both tailgate recalls, owners are advised to check that the tailgate is closed and latched before driving. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Dealers will replace the exterior touchpad switch assemblies, free of charge.

Is the hood latch repaired or replaced?

For the 2020 Sierra 2500 and 3500 recall, the notice says dealers will replace the hood assembly, free of charge. The problem described is hood-latch striker wires that may not have been heat-treated properly, possibly causing them to fracture.
