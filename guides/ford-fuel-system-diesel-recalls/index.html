What's wrong with Ford Super Duty diesel fuel systems?

3 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Fuel System, Diesel · model years 2020–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 17, 2026

All three recalls here cover Ford Super Duty diesel trucks, and two of them cover the same high pressure fuel pump problem. The December 2024 recall covers 2020-2022 F-250, F-350, F-450, F-550 and F-600, and 2021-2022 F-650 and F-750 vehicles, where biodiesel deposits may form on the pump drivetrain roller components and lead to high pressure fuel pump failure. The March 2026 recall covers 2020-2022 F-250 through F-550, 2021-2022 F-600 and 2022 F-750 vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall 24V957, for the same deposit problem. The third, from January 2022, covers 2021 Super Duty F-250 through F-600 and Medium Duty F-650 and F-750 vehicles with 6.7L diesel engines, where the secondary fuel filter cap may leak.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The December 2024 and March 2026 recalls state that high pressure fuel pump failure can cause a loss of drive power.
  • The January 2022 recall states that a pinhole in the secondary fuel filter cap may allow fuel to leak from the top of the secondary fuel filter.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. The January 2022 fuel filter cap recall on 2021 Super Duty and Medium Duty 6.7L diesel vehicles does not tell owners to stop driving, even though it describes a fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increasing the risk of fire or injury. The December 2024 and March 2026 high pressure fuel pump recalls also do not tell owners to stop driving; they state that pump failure can cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.

What the free repair involves

For the January 2022 fuel filter cap recall, dealers replace the primary and secondary fuel filters. For the December 2024 fuel pump recall, dealers update the powertrain control module and transmission control module software. For the March 2026 recall, dealers update the powertrain control module software. All three notices say the work is free of charge, and none describe an over-the-air update.

Common questions

I already had the fuel pump recall done — why is my truck listed again?

The March 2026 notice states it covers 2020-2022 F-250 through F-550, 2021-2022 F-600 and 2022 F-750 vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall number 24V957. Dealers update the powertrain control module software free of charge under Ford's recall number 26S17.

Does using biodiesel cause this?

The December 2024 and March 2026 notices say biodiesel deposits may form on the pump drivetrain roller components, which could lead to failure of the high pressure fuel pump. The notices do not give owners fuel instructions; the remedy is a software update at a dealer.

Is the fuel filter cap recall the same problem as the fuel pump recalls?

The notices describe different problems. The January 2022 recall describes a void in the top-center of the underhood secondary fuel filter cap that may progress to a pinhole and allow fuel to leak. The fuel pump recalls describe biodiesel deposits on pump drivetrain roller components.
