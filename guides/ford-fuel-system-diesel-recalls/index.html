Why do Ford Super Duty diesel trucks keep having fuel pump recalls?

3 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Fuel System, Diesel · model years 2020–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-17

This group covers three Super Duty diesel recalls, and two of them describe the exact same defect. Both the December 2024 recall and the March 2026 recall involve biodiesel deposits forming on the high-pressure fuel pump's drivetrain roller components, which can cause the pump to fail and the truck to lose drive power; the 2026 recall covers trucks that were already repaired once under the 2024 recall (24V957) but need a further software update because the fix did not fully resolve the issue. The 2020-2022 model years and F-250 through F-750 range repeat across both. A separate, unrelated 2022 recall covers a fuel filter cap void on 2021 Super Duty and Medium Duty trucks with 6.7L diesel engines, which can develop a pinhole leak and leak fuel.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Loss of engine power or drive power while driving (fuel pump failure)
  • Visible fuel leak or fuel odor from the underhood fuel filter area
  • The notices do not describe a dashboard warning light or advance sign before the fuel pump or fuel filter fails

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The fuel filter cap recall, the December 2024 fuel pump recall, and the March 2026 fuel pump recall all describe the failure and its risk, a fuel leak near an ignition source for the filter cap recall, and loss of drive power for both fuel pump recalls, without telling owners to stop driving or park away from buildings while waiting for the repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the primary and secondary fuel filters for the fuel filter cap recall, free of charge. For the fuel pump defect, the December 2024 recall has dealers update the powertrain control module and transmission control module software; the March 2026 recall, covering trucks already repaired once, updates the powertrain control module software again. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why is there a second recall for the same Super Duty fuel pump problem?

The March 2026 recall covers trucks that were already repaired under the December 2024 recall (24V957) for biodiesel deposits causing fuel pump failure. The notice indicates the earlier software fix did not fully resolve the issue, so dealers update the powertrain control module software again.

Is the fuel filter cap recall related to the fuel pump recalls?

No. The fuel filter cap recall involves a void in the secondary fuel filter cap that can develop into a pinhole leak, a separate defect from the biodiesel deposits that cause the high-pressure fuel pump to fail in the other two recalls.

What happens if the high-pressure fuel pump fails while driving?

The notices for both fuel pump recalls state that failure of the high-pressure fuel pump can cause a loss of drive power, which increases the risk of a crash. Neither notice describes a stop-driving instruction.
