Why Do So Many Ram ProMaster Van Seats Fail To Lock?

4 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Seats · model years 2018–2019 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2019-04-29

Three of the four seat recalls in this Ram group describe the identical defect — a Freedman GO-ES foldaway seat back that may not stay locked in the upright position under load, such as in a crash or sudden stop — on 2018 Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster vans (one recall extends to 2019 ProMaster). Because these vans were built and modified by different upfitters, the same seat defect was recalled three separate times: once by Mobility Transportation Services, once by Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles, and once by New England Wheels. The fourth recall is unrelated: a 2019 Ram 1500 pickup recall in which the passenger seat track position sensor can come loose, preventing it from detecting the seat's full-forward position.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A foldaway seat back that may not lock into the upright position when raised for use (three 2018-2019 Ford Transit/Ram ProMaster recalls)
  • The seat back moving out of place specifically during a crash or sudden stop, per the notices (same three recalls)
  • No warning sign is described for the 2019 Ram 1500 passenger seat position sensor recall

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This includes the three separate recalls covering the same 2018-2019 Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster foldaway seat back defect, issued by three different vehicle modifiers, and the 2019 Ram 1500 passenger seat position sensor recall. The notices describe an increased risk of injury if a seat back moves during a crash, or if the airbag cannot adjust to the seat position, but none of them instruct owners to stop driving while the repair is scheduled.

What the free repair involves

For the three foldaway seat recalls, Freedman Seating Company dealers inspect and repair the seat lock mechanism, free of charge, regardless of which company sold or modified the van. For the Ram 1500 recall, dealers inspect the passenger seat track position sensor bracket and replace it if needed, also at no cost to the owner.

Common questions

Why were the same Ram ProMaster seat problems recalled three times?

The 2018-2019 Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster vans in this group were modified with Freedman GO-ES foldaway seats by different companies — Mobility Transportation Services, Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles, and New England Wheels — so each issued its own recall for the same seat lock defect.

What's wrong with the Ram 1500 passenger seat?

In the 2019 Ram 1500 recall, the passenger seat track position sensor can loosen and move out of position, so it may not detect that the seat is fully forward — which can affect how the airbag deploys in a crash.

Is the foldaway seat back repair free on my Ford Transit or Ram ProMaster?

Yes. Whichever company modified your Ford Transit or Ram ProMaster van, the recall directs owners to Freedman Seating dealers, who inspect and repair the foldaway seat lock mechanism at no charge to the owner.
