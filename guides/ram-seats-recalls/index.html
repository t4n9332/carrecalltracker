Ram seat recalls: foldaway seat locks and a loose sensor

4 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Seats · model years 2018–2019 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 29, 2019

Three of these four recalls come not from Ram but from vehicle modifiers — Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles, Mobility Transportation Services and New England Wheels — covering Ram ProMaster vans, alongside Ford Transit, fitted with Freedman GO-ES Foldaway Seats. In each, the seat back cushion rotated up from the stowed position may not remain locked under load, such as in a crash or sudden stop. The ProMaster years differ by modifier: 2016-2018 for Prime-Time, 2014-2018 for MobilityTrans, and 2014-2019 ProMaster 2500 and 3500 for New England Wheels. The fourth is Chrysler's own recall of 2019 Ram 1500 trucks, where the manually-adjusted passenger seat's track position sensor may come loose and fail to detect the full-forward position.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices instruct owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. Chrysler's 2019 Ram 1500 notice describes an increased risk of injury in a crash if the frontal air bag deployment cannot be adjusted to the seat position. The three foldaway seat recalls — from Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles, Mobility Transportation Services and New England Wheels — describe an increased risk of injury to the seat occupant if the seat back moves in a crash. Beyond those risks, the notices say nothing about use of the vehicle before the repair.

What the free repair involves

Chrysler dealers inspect the 2019 Ram 1500 passenger seat track position sensor bracket and replace it as necessary, free of charge. For the foldaway seats, Freedman dealers inspect and repair the seat for Prime-Time and MobilityTrans vehicles free of charge, and New England Wheels directs owners to Freedman for repairs free of charge. No software update is involved.

Common questions

My ProMaster was converted by an upfitter — who do I contact about the seats?

The company that recalled the conversion. Prime-Time and New England Wheels both list Freedman customer service at 1-800-443-4540; Mobility Transportation Services lists its own number, 1-734-453-6452. All three notices say the foldaway seat repair is free of charge.

Which seat does the 2019 Ram 1500 recall actually cover?

The notice names only the seat track position sensor on the manually-adjusted passenger seats of 2019 Ram 1500 trucks. It says that sensor may come loose and out of position, so it cannot detect when the seat is in the full-forward position.

Why is my Ram ProMaster in a recall from a company I've never heard of?

Those three recalls cover ProMaster vans modified to be equipped with Freedman GO-ES Foldaway Seats. The recalling company is the modifier — Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles, Mobility Transportation Services or New England Wheels — and the same notices also cover modified Ford Transit vehicles.
