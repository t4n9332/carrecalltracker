Why Are Porsche Brake Lines And Hoses Being Recalled?

4 NHTSA recalls · Porsche · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-06-19

These four Porsche recalls involve hydraulic brake components rather than one repeated defect. The 2018 Panamera Turbo S Hybrid group covers a front brake line that can corrode internally and reduce front braking performance. Two 2020-2021 models, the 911 Carrera and Taycan, share a brake booster pressure rod connection that can loosen and detach, causing the brake pedal to fail. The Taycan appears most often, also covered by a 2020-2022 recall for an instrument cluster that displays the brake-pad-wear warning in the wrong color, and a 2020-2024 recall for front brake hoses that can develop cracks and leak fluid.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake pad wear warning light displayed in the wrong color (Taycan, 2020-2022)
  • Visible brake fluid leaking near the front wheels (Taycan hose cracks, 2020-2024)
  • A brake pedal that feels soft, sinks, or fails to respond (911/Taycan booster rod, 2020-2021)
  • Longer stopping distances or reduced braking performance (Panamera brake line corrosion, 2018)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls tell Porsche owners to stop driving or park outside while waiting for repairs. That includes the 2018 Panamera brake line corrosion recall, the 2020-2021 911 Carrera and Taycan brake booster rod detachment recall, the 2020-2022 Taycan brake-wear warning color defect, and the 2020-2024 Taycan brake hose cracking recall. Each notice describes a mechanism that can reduce braking performance or cause pedal failure, but none of the four instructs owners not to drive the vehicle before it is repaired.

What the free repair involves

Dealers address each defect differently: replacing the corroded front brake line, repairing or replacing the brake booster on the affected 911 and Taycan models, updating instrument cluster software so the brake-wear warning shows the correct color, and replacing cracked front brake hoses on the Taycan. All of these repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Porsche models are covered by these brake recalls?

The recalls cover the 2018 Panamera Turbo S Hybrid variants, the 2020-2021 911 Carrera, and the Taycan across the 2020-2024 model years. Each recall applies only to the specific models and years named in its own notice, not to every Porsche.

Will I know if my Taycan's brake pads are worn?

On some 2020-2022 Taycans, the instrument cluster may display the brake-pad-wear warning in the wrong color, so the indicator alone may not reliably tell you the pads are worn. A dealer software update corrects the display, free of charge.

What happens if a brake hose or line fails on a Taycan or Panamera?

The notices say a leaking or corroded brake line or hose can reduce front braking performance and increase stopping distance, raising the risk of a crash. Dealers replace the affected line or hoses free of charge.
