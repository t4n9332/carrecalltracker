Why are Ram diesel fuel pumps and fuel lines recalled?

3 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Fuel System, Diesel · model years 2018–2020 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-06-09

These three recalls all involve diesel Ram trucks, and two of them are high pressure fuel pump failures. The 2019 recall covers 2019 Ram 2500 and 3500 trucks and 3500, 4500 and 5500 cab chassis with diesel engines, where the fuel lines may leak fuel into the engine compartment. The 2021 recall covers 2019-2020 Ram 2500 and 3500 pickups and 3500, 4500 and 5500 cab chassis with the Cummins 6.7L Turbo Diesel, where the high pressure fuel pump may fail. The 2022 recall reaches 2014-2019 Ram 1500 and 2014-2020 Jeep Grand Cherokee models with 3.0L diesel engines, where pump failure may introduce debris into the fuel system. The 2019 and 2020 heavy duties appear in two of the three.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A sudden engine stall without warning from high pressure fuel pump failure - the 2019-2020 Cummins 6.7L Turbo Diesel recall.
  • An unexpected loss of drive power from fuel starvation - the 2014-2019 Ram 1500 3.0L diesel recall.
  • Fuel leaking from the fuel lines into the engine compartment - the 2019 Ram 2500, 3500 and cab chassis recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three diesel notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2019 fuel line recall describes a fire risk from fuel leaking into the engine compartment, the 2021 Cummins 6.7L recall describes a sudden engine stall without warning, and the 2022 3.0L recall describes an unexpected loss of drive power. Each states the increased risk of fire or a crash and then the dealer remedy. The notices give no instruction about driving before that repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the 2019 fuel lines and replace them if necessary. In the 2021 Cummins 6.7L recall they replace the high pressure fuel pump, update the Powertrain Control Module software and inspect and replace additional fuel system components as necessary. In the 2022 3.0L recall they replace the pump and inspect and replace additional fuel system components if needed. All three notices say the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Which Ram diesels are covered by the high pressure fuel pump recalls?

Two notices name the pump. One covers 2019-2020 Ram 2500 and 3500 pickups and Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500 cab chassis with Cummins 6.7L Turbo Diesel engines. The other covers 2014-2019 Ram 1500 models with 3.0L diesel engines.

My Ram diesel stalled while driving. Is that in a recall?

The 2019-2020 Cummins 6.7L notice describes a high pressure fuel pump failure resulting in a sudden engine stall without warning, and says an engine stall increases the risk of a crash. Dealers replace the pump and update the Powertrain Control Module software, free of charge.

Do these notices tell owners to stop driving until the repair?

None of the three tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. They state the fire, stall and drive power consequences, the dealer remedy and the owner letters, and say nothing else about driving the truck before the repair.
