Why Do Ram Diesel Trucks Have Fuel System Problems?

3 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Fuel System, Diesel · model years 2018–2020 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-06-09

Ram's diesel fuel system recalls in this group cover two separate problems across different truck lines. The oldest recall affects 2019 Ram 2500, 3500, 4500, and 5500 diesel trucks, whose fuel lines could leak fuel into the engine compartment near a possible ignition source. A second recall covers 2019-2020 versions of those same Cummins-powered trucks, where a High Pressure Fuel Pump can fail and cause the engine to stall suddenly without warning. A third recall, shared with certain Jeep Grand Cherokee models, covers 2018-2019 Ram 1500 and 2018-2020 Jeep Grand Cherokee vehicles with 3.0L diesel engines, where a failing high pressure fuel pump can send debris into the fuel system and cause fuel starvation.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No warning sign is described for the fuel line leak recall (2019 Ram 2500-5500 diesels)
  • Sudden engine stall without warning while driving (2019-2020 Ram 2500-5500 diesels)
  • Unexpected loss of drive power (2018-2019 Ram 1500 and 2018-2020 Jeep Grand Cherokee diesels)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the fuel line leak recall on 2019 Ram 2500, 3500, 4500, and 5500 diesel trucks, the fuel pump stall recall on 2019-2020 versions of those same trucks, and the fuel pump recall on 2018-2019 Ram 1500 and 2018-2020 Jeep Grand Cherokee diesels, the notices describe the safety risk but do not instruct owners to stop driving while a repair is scheduled.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the fuel lines and replace them if needed for the 2019 Ram 2500-5500 leak recall. For the two High Pressure Fuel Pump recalls, dealers replace the HPFP, update the Powertrain Control Module software where required, and inspect and replace additional fuel system components as necessary. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Ram diesel trucks are affected by these fuel system recalls?

The recalls cover 2019 Ram 2500, 3500, 4500, and 5500 trucks for the fuel line leak, 2019-2020 versions of those same trucks for the fuel pump stall issue, and 2018-2019 Ram 1500 and 2018-2020 Jeep Grand Cherokee diesels for the fuel starvation recall.

Can a failing high pressure fuel pump cause my Ram diesel to stall?

Yes. Two recalls in this group involve a High Pressure Fuel Pump that can fail — one causing a sudden stall without warning in 2019-2020 Ram 2500, 3500, 4500, and 5500 trucks, and one causing fuel starvation in 2018-2019 Ram 1500 and 2018-2020 Jeep Grand Cherokee diesels.

Is the Ram diesel fuel system recall repair free?

Yes. Across all three recalls in this group, dealers inspect and repair or replace the fuel lines, fuel pump, and related fuel system components at no cost to the owner, as required under federal recall law.
