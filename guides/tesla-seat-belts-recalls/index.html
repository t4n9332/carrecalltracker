What are the Tesla seat belt recalls owners should know about?

5 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 28, 2024

Five Tesla recalls here, and four of them are about belts or anchors that may not be secured as designed. Two from 2021 cover fasteners: front shoulder belts at the b-pillar on 2018-2020 Model 3 and 2019-2021 Model Y, and second-row retractors on 2019-2021 Model Y. The 2022 recall covers 2017-2022 Model 3 vehicles whose second-row left seat belt buckle and second-row center seat belt anchor may have been incorrectly reassembled during vehicle service. The 2023 recall covers 2021-2023 Model S and Model X front-row belts that may not be connected properly to the pretensioner anchors and can detach. The fifth, from 2024, is different — a seat belt warning light and chime that may not activate across 2012-2024 Model S, 2015-2024 Model X, 2017-2023 Model 3 and 2020-2023 Model Y.

Warning signs to watch for

  • With an unbelted driver, the seat belt warning light and audible chime may not activate as intended (2024 recall SB-24-00-008).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The two 2021 fastener recalls, the 2022 Model 3 second-row reassembly recall, the 2023 Model S and Model X front-row pretensioner recall and the 2024 seat belt warning recall each describe the defect, the risk of injury in a crash, and the remedy, without giving owners any interim instruction about driving. What they do say is that the seat belt system may not perform as designed in a crash.

What the free repair involves

In the 2021 front seat recall Tesla Service inspects and repairs both fasteners; in the 2021 Model Y second-row recall it inspects and replaces both fasteners. The 2022 recall has Service inspect and reassemble the seat belt anchors, and the 2023 recall inspect and connect the front-row belts or replace the assemblies — those four free of charge. The 2024 chime recall is an over-the-air update.

Common questions

Can I tell whether my seat belt fastener or anchor is affected?

The notices do not describe anything for owners to look at; they say Tesla Service inspects the fasteners, anchors or belts and repairs, reassembles, connects or replaces them as necessary. Checking your VIN against each recall is how you find out.

Why is a Model 3 recalled over work done at a service center?

The 2022 recall covers 2017-2022 Model 3 vehicles where the second-row left seat belt buckle and second-row center seat belt anchor may have been incorrectly reassembled during vehicle service. Tesla Service inspects and reassembles the anchors as necessary, free of charge.

My belt chime works — does the 2024 recall still apply to my car?

The 2024 notice says that with an unbelted driver the seat belt warning light and audible chime may not activate as intended, making the vehicles noncompliant with FMVSS 208. It covers listed 2012-2024 Model S, 2015-2024 Model X, 2017-2023 Model 3 and 2020-2023 Model Y vehicles.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
