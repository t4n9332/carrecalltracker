Why do so many Cadillac SUVs have fuel system recalls?

6 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-06-23

Six separate Cadillac fuel-system recalls point to two recurring failure modes. Several involve fuel leaks that raise fire risk: a 2022 XT5/XT6 (with GMC Acadia) rollover valve that may not seal, a 2018 model-year fuel pump that can detach and damage the fuel line, a 2020 Escalade/Escalade ESV (with several Chevrolet and GMC models) fuel pump missing a pressure regulator, and 2021 XT5/XT6/Acadia fuel supply lines that may be improperly seated. Two others involve stalling rather than leaks: a 2020 Buick Enclave/XT5/XT6/Traverse/Acadia fuel pump nozzle blockage, and a 2021 Escalade/Escalade ESV/Suburban/Tahoe/Yukon/Yukon XL fuel pump control module that can interrupt fuel flow.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Sudden engine stall or loss of power while driving (fuel-pump nozzle blockage and fuel-pump control module recalls)
  • Fuel leak near the tank, fuel line, or fuel pump, which raises fire risk (rollover valve, fuel pump detachment, pressure regulator, and fuel-line recalls)
  • The notices do not describe any warning sign before these failures occur

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six fuel-system recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the recalls involving a rollover valve, a detaching fuel pump, a missing pressure regulator, or improperly seated fuel lines, GM says a resulting fuel leak increases the risk of a fire, but the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. For the recalls involving a blocked fuel pump nozzle or a failing fuel pump control module, GM says the engine can stall unexpectedly, again without an instruction to stop driving. Owners are not told to stop driving under any of these six recalls.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and, as needed, replace the fuel tank shell, fuel pump, fuel pump control module, or fuel supply lines depending on the recall. One recall covers replacement of the high-pressure fuel pump and pipe together. All of these repairs are performed free of charge to the owner.

Common questions

Are Cadillac fuel system recalls a fire risk?

Four of these six recalls say a fuel leak increases the risk of a fire: the 2022 rollover-valve recall, the 2018 fuel-pump-detachment recall, the 2020 pressure-regulator recall, and the 2021 fuel-line recall. None of the notices tell owners to stop driving.

Why would my Cadillac stall while driving?

Two recalls in this group describe stalling: a 2020 recall where a manufacturing burr can block the fuel pump nozzle, and a 2021 recall where the fuel pump control module can interrupt fuel flow. Both can cause an unexpected engine stall.

Is the repair the same for every fuel-system recall?

No. Each recall has its own fix: dealers replace the fuel tank shell, the fuel pump, the fuel pump control module, or the fuel supply lines depending on which recall applies to the vehicle. All repairs are free.
