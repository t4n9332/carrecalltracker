Cadillac fuel system recalls: leaks, pump failures and engine stalls

6 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 23, 2022

Fuel pumps are the thread running through this group. GM recalled 2018 Cadillac ATS vehicles for a high pressure fuel pump that may detach from its mounting flange and damage the fuel line; 2020 Escalade and Escalade ESV for a fuel pump missing its pressure regulator; 2020 XT5 and XT6 for a plastic burr blocking the fuel pump's jet nozzle; and 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV for a fuel pump power control module. The XT5 and XT6 crossovers appear three times, adding improperly seated fuel supply lines on 2021 models and a fuel tank rollover valve on 2022 all-wheel-drive models. Outcomes divide between leaks that raise fire risk and interrupted fuel flow that stalls the engine.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An engine stall without warning from insufficient fuel supply (2020 XT5 and XT6 fuel pump jet nozzle recall).
  • An unexpected engine stall (2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV fuel pump power control module recall).
  • A fuel leak (2021 XT5 and XT6 fuel supply line recall, and the 2018 ATS high pressure fuel pump recall, where a damaged fuel line can create one).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. That includes the two fire-risk recalls on 2018 ATS and 2020 Escalade and Escalade ESV vehicles, the 2021 XT5 and XT6 fuel supply line recall, the 2022 XT5 and XT6 rollover valve recall, and the two stall-related recalls on 2020 XT5 and XT6 and 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV vehicles. No notice in this group gives owners an interim instruction to follow before the dealer repair it describes.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the high pressure fuel pump and pipe on the 2018 ATS, the fuel pump on 2020 Escalade and Escalade ESV, and the fuel pump power control module on 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV. They inspect and replace 2021 XT5 and XT6 fuel supply lines as necessary, and inspect 2022 fuel tanks, replacing the shell if the rollover valve is dislodged or missing. Those notices say this is free.

Common questions

Which of these Cadillac fuel recalls involve a fire risk?

The 2018 ATS high pressure fuel pump recall, the 2020 Escalade and Escalade ESV pressure regulator recall, the 2021 XT5 and XT6 fuel supply line recall and the 2022 XT5 and XT6 rollover valve recall all describe fuel leaks that increase fire risk.

Is the 2020 XT5 and XT6 fuel pump repair free?

That notice does not say. It states that GM will notify owners and dealers will replace the fuel pump module, with the recall beginning November 16, 2020. Cadillac customer service is listed at 1-800-458-8006 for questions about that recall.

Does the rollover valve recall cover every 2022 XT5 and XT6?

The notice covers certain 2022 Cadillac XT5, XT6 and GMC Acadia all-wheel-drive vehicles, so it is limited by model year and drivetrain. GM says dealers will inspect the fuel tank and replace the shell if the rollover valve is dislodged or missing, free of charge.
