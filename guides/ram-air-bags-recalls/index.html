Ram air bag recalls: inflators, control modules and side curtains

12 NHTSA recalls · RAM · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 18, 2025

The Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 pickups dominate this group, and side curtain air bags are the recurring theme. Four separate recalls describe side curtain inflators that may rupture or whose end cap may detach, covering Ram 1500 Classic, Ram 2500 and Ram 3500 trucks from the 2015 through 2020 model years, with 2018 and 2019 model years appearing more than once. A second theme is the driver's air bag failing to deploy: the steering column control module is named in recalls covering 2023-2024 and 2021-2022 trucks, and a 2019-2024 Ram 1500 recall describes an improperly connected driver's air bag. Occupant restraint controller faults cover 2019-2020 Ram 1500s and 2025 heavy-duty trucks. ProMaster appears twice, in a door impact sensor recall and a side curtain recall.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these twelve notices tells owners to stop driving or to park the truck outside. Each one describes what could go wrong in a crash: side curtain inflators that may rupture and send metal fragments into the cabin, a driver's air bag or seat belt pretensioner that may not deploy, or air bags and pretensioners disabled by a corrupted occupant restraint controller. The 2025 Ram 2500 through 5500 recall adds that electronic stability control may be disabled. No notice in this group gives an interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

All of this dealer work is free of charge. Dealers replace one or both side curtain air bags where inflators may rupture, replace SABIC assemblies, inspect and replace the steering column control module, reprogram or replace the occupant restraint controller, update its software on the 2025 trucks, replace the ProMaster door impact sensors with the correct rivets, and secured the driver's air bag connectors on the 2019-2024 Ram 1500.

Common questions

My Ram is in more than one air bag recall — why?

Several recalls in this group cover overlapping model years of the Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 for different parts: side curtain inflators, SABIC assemblies, the steering column control module and the occupant restraint controller. A VIN check lists which apply to your truck.

Is there any way to tell if my air bag will deploy?

The notices do not describe any warning an owner would see or hear. They state the defect — a steering column control module that may not be welded properly, an air bag that may not be connected properly, corrupted controller memory — and direct owners to the free dealer repair.

What does the 2025 Ram heavy-duty software recall cover?

That recall describes a software error in the occupant restraint controller module of 2025 Ram 2500, 3500, 4500 and 5500 trucks that may keep the driver's air bag or seat belt pretensioner from deploying and disable electronic stability control. Dealers update the software free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
