Why Do So Many Ram Trucks Have Air Bag Recalls?

12 NHTSA recalls · RAM · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-18

Side curtain air bag inflators that can rupture from a manufacturing defect are the most repeated problem in this group, covering 2018-2020 Ram 1500, 2500, and 3500 trucks across four separate recalls issued between 2021 and 2025. A second recurring issue is a steering column control module that can prevent the driver's air bag from deploying, affecting 2021-2024 Ram trucks in two recalls. Other one-off causes include a corrupted occupant restraint controller on 2019-2020 Ram 1500, a 2025 software error that can also disable electronic stability control on 2025 heavy-duty Ram trucks, an improperly crimped side curtain diffuser on 2020 models, a door sensor rivet on the 2018 ProMaster, and a loose driver air bag connector on 2019-2021 Ram 1500.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A stability control system that shuts off or a related dashboard warning (2025 Ram 2500/3500/4500/5500 software recall)
  • No day-to-day warning signs are described for the side curtain air bag inflator or driver air bag deployment defects
  • These problems are only described as appearing during a crash, when an air bag or seat belt pretensioner fails to work as intended

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the 12 Ram air bag recalls in this group tell owners to stop driving or park outside while waiting for repairs. That includes the recalls where a driver's air bag, side air bag, or seat belt pretensioner might not work as intended in a crash, and the 2025 recall that can also disable electronic stability control on Ram 2500, 3500, 4500, and 5500 trucks. Each notice describes a defect that raises the risk of injury or a crash if the vehicle is in a collision, but none instructs owners not to drive beforehand.

What the free repair involves

Depending on the recall, dealers replace or reprogram the occupant restraint controller, replace side curtain air bag assemblies or inflators, replace the steering column control module, secure the driver air bag connector, or update software. All of these repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Ram models have side curtain air bag inflator recalls?

Several separate recalls between 2021 and 2025 cover 2018-2020 Ram 1500, 2500, and 3500 trucks whose side curtain air bag inflators can rupture due to a manufacturing defect. Each recall applies to specific model years and VIN ranges named in its own notice.

Can a Ram recall disable electronic stability control?

Yes. A 2025 recall for certain Ram 2500, 3500, 4500, and 5500 trucks involves an occupant restraint controller software error that can disable electronic stability control in addition to affecting the driver air bag and seat belt pretensioner. Dealers update the software free of charge.

Why do so many Ram recalls involve the driver's air bag not deploying?

Different recalls trace this to different causes: a steering column control module that isn't welded properly in 2021-2024 trucks, a loose driver air bag connector in 2019-2021 Ram 1500, and a corrupted control module memory in 2019-2020 Ram 1500. Dealers repair each cause free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
