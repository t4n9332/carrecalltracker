Why do GMC trucks keep having brake control and vacuum pump recalls?

11 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-09-19

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

These 11 recalls show two repeated brake weaknesses in GM's truck and SUV lineup: control-module software and hardware defects. Software errors have disabled ESC and ABS without a warning light on 2019 CT6, Silverado 1500, and Sierra 1500; disabled brake assist on 2019 Silverado 1500/Sierra 1500; and failed to show a low-fluid warning on 2023-2024 Escalade, Silverado 1500, Suburban, Tahoe, Sierra 1500, Yukon, and Yukon XL. A vacuum pump losing output over time reduced brake assist across two separate recalls covering 2018 Silverado 1500, Tahoe, Sierra 1500, Yukon, and Escalade. Hardware problems include unheated caliper bolts that can break on 2020 Silverado 1500/Sierra 1500, a rear-caliper coating flaw across many 2018-2019 GM cars, and a brake control module that can short from water intrusion and catch fire on 2021-2022 Silverado 2500/3500 and Sierra 2500/3500.

Warning signs to watch for

  • ESC, ABS, or brake-assist warning lights that stay off even though the system has been disabled (2019 CT6, Silverado 1500, Sierra 1500)
  • Increased brake pedal effort and a longer distance needed to stop the vehicle
  • The vehicle pulling to one side or braking unexpectedly on its own
  • No brake warning light illuminating even when brake fluid is low (2023-2024 recall)

Is it safe to keep driving?

One of these 11 recalls carries a park-outside instruction: the 2021-2022 Silverado 2500/3500 and Sierra 2500/3500 recall for a brake control module that can short from water intrusion, which tells owners to park outside and away from other structures until it is repaired. None of the 11 recalls tell owners to stop driving. For the remaining recalls, covering vacuum pump, caliper, sensor, and software defects, the notices do not instruct owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers reprogram the Electronic Brake Control Module for the software-related recalls, replace the brake pressure modulator valve or caliper bolts where hardware failed, bleed the brake system for the caliper coating issue, recalibrate the brake system after incorrect dealer setup, and update warning-light software over the air or in the shop. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why don't the ABS or brake warning lights turn on when something's wrong?

Several of these recalls involve an Electronic Brake Control Module software error that disables ESC, ABS, or brake assist without triggering the dashboard warning light that would normally alert the driver, so the system fails silently.

Which GMC brake recall requires parking outside?

Only the 2021-2022 Silverado 2500/3500 and Sierra 2500/3500 recall for a brake control module exposed to water intrusion tells owners to park outside and away from structures, because of a fire risk from a possible electrical short.

Is the vacuum pump issue the same repair as the ABS software recalls?

No. The vacuum pump recalls address lost mechanical brake assist through an Electronic Brake Control Module reprogram, while the ESC/ABS and low-fluid-warning recalls fix separate software errors in that same module for different symptoms.
