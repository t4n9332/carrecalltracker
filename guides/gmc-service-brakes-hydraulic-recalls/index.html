Which GMC brake recalls affect Sierra, Yukon and Terrain owners?

11 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 19, 2024

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Most of these eleven GMC brake recalls come back to one part: the electronic brake control module and its software. Four separate software errors hit Sierra 1500 and the full-size SUVs — disabled ESC and ABS on 2019 Sierra 1500, lost electronic brake assist on 2019 Sierra 1500, unintended braking that pulls 2014-2018 Sierra 1500 and 2015-2020 Yukon to one side, and a missing brake fluid warning light on 2023 Sierra 1500 and 2023-2024 Yukon and Yukon XL. Hardware defects are fewer: brake modulator bolts that may not be properly tightened on 2021-2022 Sierra 2500 and 3500, caliper bolts that were not heat treated on 2020 Sierra 1500, caliper pistons with insufficient coating on 2018-2019 Terrain, and brake line clearance on Savana Cutaway vans. Vacuum pump output loss covers 2018 Sierra 1500 and Yukon.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The vehicle brakes on one wheel on its own and pulls to one side — the driveline-protection recall covering 2014-2018 Sierra 1500 and 2015-2020 Yukon.
  • Higher brake pedal effort and a longer distance required to stop — the two vacuum pump recalls covering 2018 Sierra 1500 and Yukon.
  • No ESC or ABS malfunction warning light even though those systems are disabled — the 2019 Sierra 1500 software recall.
  • No warning light when brake fluid is lost — the 2023 Sierra 1500 and 2023-2024 Yukon and Yukon XL software recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One of these eleven recalls carries an interim instruction. In the 2021-2022 Sierra 2500 and 3500 brake pressure modulator valve recall, owners are advised to park outside and away from other structures until the recall repair is completed, because water in the valve may cause an electrical short and increase the risk of fire. None of the eleven notices tells owners to stop driving. For the other ten, the notices set out the crash risk each defect creates and do not tell owners to stop driving or give any step to take before the dealer visit.

What the free repair involves

Remedies differ by recall and all are free of charge. Dealers reprogram or recalibrate the electronic brake control module on the software and vacuum pump recalls, and the brake fluid warning light update is delivered over the air or by a dealer. Elsewhere dealers replace the brake pressure modulator valve assembly, replace the brake caliper bolts, bleed the brake system, or inspect and replace the brake line as necessary.

Common questions

Could my GMC be covered by more than one of these brake recalls?

It is possible. Sierra 1500 and Yukon appear in several of these recalls across different model years, and each recall covers its own list of vehicles and build conditions. Check your VIN with NHTSA's recall lookup or a GMC dealer to see every open recall.

What does GM say about the fire risk on Sierra 2500 and 3500 trucks?

That recall covers 2021-2022 Sierra 2500 and 3500 and says water in the brake pressure modulator valve may cause an electrical short and increase the risk of fire. Owners are advised to park outside and away from other structures, and dealers replace the assembly free of charge.

Will a dashboard light tell me my brakes are affected?

The notices do not say one will. Two of them describe the opposite: the 2019 Sierra 1500 software recall says the ESC and ABS malfunction lights will not illuminate, and the 2023-2024 recall says no warning light appears when brake fluid is lost.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
