What do Audi body and trim recalls cover on Q5 and Q7?

4 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Structure · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 2, 2021

These four Audi recalls are about body and interior parts rather than mechanical systems, and no two share a cause. On 2018-2019 SQ5 and 2018-2020 Q5 vehicles the rear wheel arch cover trim may loosen and detach. On 2018-2020 Q7 vehicles the C-pillar padding may not absorb enough impact in a crash. On 2021 E-Tron Quattro and E-Tron Sportback Quattro vehicles the right glove box hinge pin may not be secured, letting the glove box lid trim interfere with the knee air bag as it deploys. On 2017-2018 A4 Sedan and A4 allroad and 2018 A5 Sportback vehicles the aluminum trim around the door speakers can come loose and expose sharp edges.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. The notices describe risk in crash terms: Audi says detached rear wheel arch trim may become a road hazard, that the Q7 C-pillar padding may not absorb enough impact if an unbelted occupant's head strikes it, that the E-Tron glove box lid trim may interfere with knee air bag deployment, and that the loose A4 and A5 speaker trim leaves sharp edges that increase the risk of injury to occupants.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the four clips that secure the forward part of the rear wheel arch trim on the Q5 and SQ5, replace the deformation element on the Q7 C-pillars, inspect the glove box and secure the hinge pin as necessary on the E-Tron, and replace the aluminum speaker cover trim on the A4 and A5. All four notices say the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Is the Q5 wheel arch trim recall a safety issue?

Audi's notice says the rear wheel arch cover trim on 2018-2019 SQ5 and 2018-2020 Q5 vehicles may loosen and detach, and that detached trim may become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash. Dealers replace four clips free of charge.

Does the E-Tron glove box recall affect the air bag?

The notice says the right hinge pin for the glove box cover on 2021 E-Tron Quattro and E-Tron Sportback Quattro vehicles may not be properly secured, allowing the glove box lid trim to interfere with the knee air bag during deployment. Dealers inspect and secure the pin.

Do these recalls tell owners to stop driving?

None of the four notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. Each describes what the defect can do — a road hazard, reduced impact absorption, impaired knee air bag deployment, exposed sharp edges — and says dealers will make the repair free of charge.
