Why has Audi recalled trim and interior parts on several models?

4 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Structure · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2021-08-02

These four Audi recalls involve interior trim and structural padding rather than a single defect. The 2018-2020 Q5 and SQ5 were recalled because rear wheel arch trim can loosen and detach. The 2018-2020 Q7 was recalled separately because its C-pillar padding may not absorb enough impact in a crash. The 2018 A4 Allroad and A5 were recalled because aluminum door trim around the door speakers can come loose and expose sharp edges. The 2021 E-Tron was recalled because a loose glove box hinge pin can let the glove box lid trim interfere with the knee air bag during deployment. Each recall has a distinct cause tied to one model line.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rear wheel arch trim on the Q5/SQ5 loosening or coming off the vehicle
  • Aluminum door trim around the door speakers on the A4 Allroad/A5 coming loose and exposing sharp edges
  • A glove box lid on the E-Tron that sits loose due to an unsecured hinge pin
  • No warning sign is described for the Q7 C-pillar padding issue before a crash

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the Q5/SQ5 wheel arch trim, the Q7 C-pillar padding, the A4 Allroad/A5 door trim, and the E-Tron glove box hinge pin, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The notices instead describe increased risk if the part is not repaired — detached trim as a road hazard, sharp edges as an injury risk, inadequate padding as a crash-injury risk, and impaired knee air bag deployment as an injury risk.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the clips holding the rear wheel arch trim on the Q5/SQ5, replace the aluminum door speaker trim on the A4 Allroad/A5, replace the C-pillar deformation element on the Q7, and inspect and secure the glove box hinge pin on the E-Tron. All of these repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Is the Audi Q5 wheel arch trim recall dangerous?

Audi says the rear wheel arch trim on the 2018-2020 Q5 and SQ5 can loosen and detach, creating a road hazard that increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What is wrong with the Audi Q7 C-pillar recall?

The padding on the C-pillar of 2018-2020 Q7 models may not absorb enough impact in a crash, which Audi says could increase injury risk to an unbelted occupant whose head strikes the pillar.

Does the E-Tron glove box recall affect airbag safety?

Yes. An unsecured hinge pin can let the glove box lid trim interfere with the knee air bag during deployment on the 2021 E-Tron, which Audi says can increase the risk of injury in a crash.
