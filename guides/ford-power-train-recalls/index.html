Why do Ford transmissions, driveshafts and wheel hubs keep getting recalled?

56 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Power Train · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 24, 2026

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

Fluid leaks that can reach hot engine or exhaust parts run through this group: the 2016-2018 Focus with the 1.0L engine and 6-speed manual can fracture a clutch and leak transmission fluid; the start/stop accumulator endcap on 2019-2020 Edge, Transit Connect and Lincoln MKX and 2020 Escape and Lincoln Corsair may have missing or loose bolts; and 2021 F-650 and F-750 trucks with the 7.3L gas engine can have the transmission oil cooler line contact the air conditioning compressor pulley. Driveshafts are the other repeat: 2020 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator with the 10-speed automatic and all-wheel drive, and 2025-2026 F-250 and F-350 Super Duty. Super Duty trucks appear twice for front wheel hub extenders, in 2021 and 2022-2023.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Loss of gear indication on the instrument cluster display when the shift lever is moved quickly from Park to Drive — 2018 F-150 column shifter recall (17S35).
  • Momentary selection of an unintended gear, such as Reverse or Neutral, before Drive engages — same 2018 F-150 recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two notices here carry a stop-driving instruction, both on Super Duty front wheel hub extenders: the 2021 F-350, F-450, F-550 and F-600 recall tells owners not to drive until the torque of the wheel extender nuts can be verified, and the 2022-2023 F-350, F-450, F-550 and F-600 recall advises owners not to drive until the repair has been performed. For the other recalls listed here — clutch, accumulator, oil cooler line, driveshaft, Mustang clutch pressure line, Transit Connect software and F-150 shifter — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the start/stop accumulator on the Edge, Escape, Transit Connect, MKX and Corsair recall; inspect driveshaft labels or serial numbers and replace driveshafts as needed; inspect and tighten, or replace, Super Duty front wheel hub extender hardware; inspect the 2024 Mustang clutch pressure line; update Transit Connect PCM software; and update software with Focus clutch replacement as necessary. Each notice says the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Do any of these Ford power train recalls tell owners to stop driving?

Two do. The 2021 F-350, F-450, F-550 and F-600 recall instructs owners not to drive until the torque of the wheel extender nuts can be verified; the 2022-2023 Super Duty recall advises not driving until the repair has been performed.

What does Ford do about the transmission fluid leak recalls?

For the 2016-2018 Focus, dealers update software that detects and prevents prolonged clutch slip and replace the clutch as necessary. For the accumulator recall, dealers replace the start/stop accumulator. For 2021 F-650 and F-750, dealers inspect and correct or replace oil cooler lines. All three notices say free of charge.

Will I notice a warning sign before one of these parts fails?

Only the 2018 F-150 shifter notice describes something a driver would see: loss of gear indication on the instrument cluster display and momentary selection of an unintended gear when the lever is moved quickly from Park to Drive. The other notices listed name no warning sign.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
