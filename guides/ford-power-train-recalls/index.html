Why do so many Ford transmission and driveshaft recalls warn of fire?

55 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Power Train · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-24

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

This group has two repeat patterns. Fire risk from leaking fluid appears in several recalls: 2016-2018 Focus clutches that can fracture, 2019-2020 Edge, Transit Connect, Nautilus, Escape, and Corsair accumulator bolts, a 2021 F-650/F-750 transmission cooler line, a 2024 Mustang clutch pressure line, and 2019-2020 Transit Connect overheating software left from an earlier, incomplete repair. The Focus clutch fracture was recalled twice, in 2018 and again in 2026 after the first fix did not hold. The other pattern is wheel and driveshaft detachment on heavy-duty trucks: Super Duty front wheel hub extenders were recalled in both 2021 and 2022-2023, plus a 2020 Explorer/Aviator driveshaft, a 2023 E-450 rear axle bolt, and a 2026 F-350 driveshaft weld.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Transmission fluid leaking or a burning smell near the engine or exhaust
  • Sudden loss of drive power or an unexpected vehicle rollaway
  • A wheel or wheel extender that feels loose or separates from the truck
  • Delayed engine cooling that lets the engine run hot
  • The notices describe no advance warning before the shifter briefly selects the wrong gear (F-150)

Is it safe to keep driving?

Two recalls in this group carry a stop-driving instruction: the 2021 F-350, F-450, F-550, and F-600 recall and the 2022-2023 F-450, F-550, and F-600 recall, both for front wheel hub extenders that could detach, and both instruct owners not to drive until the extender nuts are inspected and tightened. For the remaining recalls in this group — including the Focus, Edge, Explorer, Mustang, Transit Connect, F-650/F-750, E-450, and F-150 recalls — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealer fixes vary by defect but are always free: replacing or updating clutches, accumulators, transmission cooler lines, and driveshafts; inspecting and tightening or replacing wheel hub extender nuts and axle bolts; reprogramming the powertrain control module for the shifter and overheating issues; and updating software that detects clutch slip. Some recalls combine a software update with a later parts replacement.

Common questions

Which Ford power train recalls tell owners to stop driving?

Two recalls do: the 2021 F-350/F-450/F-550/F-600 and the 2022-2023 F-450/F-550/F-600 recalls, both for front wheel hub extenders that could detach. Owners are told not to drive until the extender nuts are inspected and tightened.

Why do so many of these recalls mention fire risk?

Several separate defects — cracked clutches, loose accumulator bolts, a misrouted transmission cooler line, a melted clutch line, and overheating software — can each let transmission or brake fluid leak or heat build up near an ignition source.

Was my Focus clutch recall actually fixed the first time?

Not necessarily. Ford's 2026 recall covers 2015-2018 Focus and 2013-2016 Fusion vehicles that were previously repaired incorrectly under the earlier 18V169 and 18V845 recalls for the same clutch fracture and fire risk, so dealers are updating them again.
