Ford powertrain recalls: wheels, driveshafts, clutches and transmission fluid leaks

55 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Power Train · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 24, 2026

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

Three threads recur. Wheel separation runs through three recalls: front wheel hub extenders improperly attached on 2021 F-350, F-450, F-550 and F-600 trucks with dual rear wheels, insufficiently tightened extender fasteners on 2022-2023 Super Duty F-350 through F-600, and a loose rear axle rotor bolt on the 2023 E-450. Transmission fluid leaks come from a fractured clutch on 2016-2018 Focus with the 1.0L engine and 6-speed manual, a start/stop accumulator endcap with missing or loose bolts on 2019-2020 Edge, Transit Connect and Lincoln models, and a cooler line touching the A/C pulley on 2021 F-650 and F-750. Driveshafts can fracture on 2020 Explorer and Aviator and on 2025-2026 F-250 and F-350 Super Duty.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2018 F-150 shifter recall describes a loss of gear indication on the instrument cluster display when the shift lever is moved quickly from Park to Drive.
  • The 2025-2026 F-250 and F-350 driveshaft recall describes a sudden loss of drive power.
  • The 2020 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator driveshaft recall describes a vehicle rollaway when in Park or a loss of drive.
  • The 2019-2020 Transit Connect recall describes engine overheating.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two of these recalls carry a stop-driving instruction. For the 2021 F-350, F-450, F-550 and F-600 with front wheel hub extenders, owners are instructed not to drive their vehicles until the torque of the wheel extender nuts can be verified. For the 2022-2023 Super Duty F-350 through F-600, owners are advised not to drive their vehicle until the repair has been performed. For every other recall in this group — the clutch, driveshaft, transmission leak, rear axle, shifter and overheating recalls — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

All of this work is free of charge. The wheel recalls are inspections first: dealers check and tighten the 2021 hub extender nuts, and on 2022-2023 Super Duty replace the extender, nuts and hub assembly if the nuts are loose. Elsewhere dealers replace the start/stop accumulator, inspect driveshaft serial numbers and replace as needed, and update software on the Focus clutch, Transit Connect overheating and F-150 shifter recalls.

Common questions

Which Ford powertrain recalls tell owners not to drive?

Two do. The 2021 F-350 through F-600 hub extender recall tells owners not to drive until the torque of the wheel extender nuts is verified, and the 2022-2023 Super Duty recall tells owners not to drive until the repair has been performed.

Why has the Focus clutch been recalled more than once?

The 2016-2018 Focus notice covers a clutch that may fracture and leak transmission fluid. A later notice covers 2015-2018 Focus and 2013-2016 Fusion vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recalls 18V169 and 18V845, with the same fracture described.

Will I have to pay for a driveshaft or transmission repair?

The notices say these repairs are performed free of charge. That includes inspecting and replacing driveshafts on the 2020 Explorer and Aviator and the 2025-2026 F-250 and F-350, replacing the start/stop accumulator, and repairing the 2024 Mustang clutch pressure line.
