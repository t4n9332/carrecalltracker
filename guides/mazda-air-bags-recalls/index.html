Mazda Air Bag Recalls Involving Deployment Failures and Excessive Force

4 NHTSA recalls · Mazda · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 18, 2025

Mazda's air bag recalls repeatedly concern whether air bags deploy correctly during a crash, with module software central to three supplied notices. The broadest stated model-year span is the 2016-2023 MX-5 Miata, whose air bag control module software may cause excessive front air bag deployment force. Certain 2024-2025 Mazda3 and CX-30 vehicles have SAS units that may set an error code and deactivate air bags because of a low battery condition. A separate CX-50 and CX-50 Hybrid notice concerns incorrectly configured module software. The 2018 CX-5 notice differs: its side curtain air bags may not unfold properly during side-impact or rollover deployment.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the supplied Mazda air bag notices instructs owners to stop driving or park outside. The CX-5 notice describes curtain air bags that may not unfold properly; the MX-5 Miata notice describes excessive deployment force. The Mazda3 and CX-30 notice describes possible air bag deactivation, and the CX-50 notice describes improper deployment. For each recall, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving. These notices describe increased injury risk during a crash and do not establish that an affected vehicle is safe to drive.

What the free repair involves

Mazda specifies free repairs for these recalls. For the 2018 CX-5, dealers replace the curtain air bags. For the MX-5 Miata, dealers update SAS control module software. For affected Mazda3 and CX-30 vehicles, dealers reprogram the SAS unit with improved software or replace it. The CX-50 and CX-50 Hybrid remedy is a module software update.

Common questions

Will my Mazda need new air bags or a software update?

The remedy depends on the recall. The 2018 CX-5 requires curtain air bag replacement. The MX-5 Miata and CX-50 notices specify software updates. The Mazda3 and CX-30 notice specifies SAS reprogramming or unit replacement. Each notice says its repair is free.

Does the Mazda3 and CX-30 notice describe an air bag warning light?

The notice says a low battery condition may cause the SAS unit to set an error code and deactivate the air bags. It does not describe an owner-visible warning light. Its stated remedy is free SAS reprogramming with improved software or replacement.

Why does the CX-50 air bag recall say letters will not be mailed?

Recall 7625F says owner notifications will not be mailed because all affected vehicles are under Mazda dealer control. It describes incorrectly configured module software that may cause improper air bag deployment and specifies a free module software update.
