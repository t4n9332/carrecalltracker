What is wrong with the air bags in recalled Mazdas?

4 NHTSA recalls · Mazda · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 18, 2025

Air bag control module software is the common thread in three of these four Mazda recalls, and each fails in a different direction. On 2016-2023 MX-5 Miata, a software error in the air bag control module may cause the front air bags to deploy with excessive force, failing FMVSS 208. On 2024-2025 Mazda3 and CX-30, the Sophisticated Air Bag Sensor units may set an error code and deactivate the air bags due to a low battery condition. On 2025 CX-50 and CX-50 Hybrid, incorrectly configured module software may cause an air bag to deploy improperly. The oldest is hardware: 2018 CX-5 side curtain air bags may not properly unfold in a side impact or rollover, failing FMVSS 226.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four Mazda notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. All four describe the risk in a crash: air bags that may not unfold properly, may not deploy at all, or may deploy with excessive force. For the 2025 CX-50, Mazda says owner notifications will not be mailed because all vehicles are under Mazda dealer control. The notices say nothing further about whether to keep driving.

What the free repair involves

The remedies differ by recall. For the MX-5 Miata, dealers update the Sophisticated Air Bag Sensor control module software. For Mazda3 and CX-30, they reprogram the SAS unit with improved software or replace the unit. For the CX-50, they update the module software. For the 2018 CX-5, dealers replace the curtain air bags. Each of these repairs is free of charge.

Common questions

Do any of these Mazda recalls switch the air bags off?

The 2024-2025 Mazda3 and CX-30 notice says the Sophisticated Air Bag Sensor units may set an error code and deactivate the air bags due to a low battery condition, and that air bags which do not deploy in a crash increase the risk of injury.

My MX-5 is a 2016 model — is it included?

Mazda's notice covers certain 2016-2023 MX-5 Miata vehicles for the air bag control module software error, so 2016 cars can be included. Only specific VINs are affected. Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500 can confirm; the recall number is 6924I.

Why didn't I get a letter about the CX-50 recall?

Mazda's notice says owner notifications will not be mailed for the 2025 CX-50 recall because all affected vehicles are under Mazda dealer control. Dealers update the module software free of charge. Mazda's number for this recall is 7625F.
