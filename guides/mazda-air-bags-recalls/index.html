Why do so many recent Mazda airbag recalls involve software, not hardware?

4 NHTSA recalls · Mazda · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-06-18

Four Mazda air bag recalls appear here, and three of the four trace to software rather than a physical defect. The 2018 CX-5 had a hardware problem: its side curtain air bags could fail to unfold properly in a side impact or rollover. The 2018-2023 MX-5 had an air bag control module software error that could cause the front air bags to deploy with excessive force. The 2024 Mazda3 and the 2024-2025 CX-30 had a low battery condition that could make the air bag sensor set an error code and deactivate the air bags entirely. The 2025 CX-50 had incorrectly configured module software that could cause improper deployment. No single model or year repeats across more than one of these recalls.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Side curtain air bags that fail to fully unfold in a side impact or rollover crash
  • Front air bags that deploy with excessive force during a crash
  • Air bags deactivated by a low 12-volt-battery-triggered error code
  • Air bags that deploy improperly
  • None of these notices describe a symptom you'd notice before a crash happens

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. That covers the curtain air bag recall on the 2018 CX-5, the excessive-force deployment recall on the 2018-2023 MX-5, the deactivation recall on the 2024 Mazda3 and 2024-2025 CX-30, and the improper-deployment recall on the 2025 CX-50. For each of them, the notices only say they do not tell owners to stop driving in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Dealers address each defect differently: replacing the curtain air bags on the CX-5, updating air bag control module software on the MX-5 and CX-50, and reprogramming or replacing the air bag sensor unit on the Mazda3 and CX-30. All of these repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why did my Mazda's air bags get recalled for a software problem?

Three of these four recalls trace to software: an error in the air bag control module, a sensor unit that deactivates air bags after a low-battery error, and misconfigured module software. Dealers correct each with a software update, not a physical part swap.

Will my Mazda's air bags still work until I get the recall fixed?

The notices for these recalls don't tell owners to stop driving, but they also don't say the air bags will work correctly if needed. Contact your dealer to schedule the free repair for your specific recall as soon as possible.

Are the 2018 CX-5 and the newer Mazda3/CX-30 airbag recalls related?

No. The 2018 CX-5 recall involves side curtain air bags that may not unfold properly; the 2024-2025 Mazda3 and CX-30 recall involves a low-battery error code that deactivates the air bags. They are separate defects with separate repairs.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
