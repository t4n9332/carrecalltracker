Why do some Hyundai engines lose power or leak oil?

4 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2022–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-08-26

Four Hyundai recalls in this group share two recurring risks: loss of drive power and oil leaking near hot engine components. The 2024 Palisade recall involves engine valve springs that can break and even punch a hole in the engine block. The 2025 Santa Fe and Tucson recall involves connecting rod bolts that may loosen, damaging the engine and risking an oil leak. A separate 2022 Santa Fe and Santa Cruz recall involves a turbocharger oil supply pipe that can crack and leak. The 2024 Elantra, Kona, and Venue recall involves an EGR valve assembly that can short-circuit, causing a loss of drive power without an oil leak.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Sudden loss of drive power while driving
  • Oil leak in the engine compartment
  • Notices describe no dashboard warning before these failures

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the four recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the Palisade valve spring recall, the Santa Fe and Tucson connecting rod bolt recall, the Santa Fe and Santa Cruz turbo oil pipe recall, and the Elantra, Kona, and Venue EGR valve recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Several note that a loss of drive power increases crash risk, and that an oil leak near hot engine or exhaust parts increases fire risk, but none instruct owners to stop driving or park away from structures.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the affected part free of charge: the engine sub-assembly for the Palisade valve springs, the full engine for the Santa Fe and Tucson rod bolt issue, the turbocharger oil supply pipe for the Santa Fe and Santa Cruz recall, and the EGR valve assembly for the Elantra, Kona, and Venue recall.

Common questions

Which Hyundai models are covered by the engine recalls?

This group covers the 2024 Palisade, 2025 Santa Fe and Tucson, 2022 Santa Fe and Santa Cruz, and 2024 Elantra, Kona, and Venue, each tied to a different engine defect.

Can a broken valve spring or loose rod bolt cause a fire?

The notices say a hole in the engine block or an oil leak near hot engine components increases fire risk. This applies to the Palisade valve spring recall and the Santa Fe/Tucson rod bolt recall specifically.

Is the Santa Fe engine recall the same as the Santa Fe turbo recall?

No. The 2025 Santa Fe connecting rod bolt recall and the 2022 Santa Fe turbocharger oil pipe recall are different defects, covering different model years and different repairs.
