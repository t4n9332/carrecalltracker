Hyundai engine recalls: power loss and oil leak risks explained

4 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2022–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 26, 2025

Three of these four Hyundai engine recalls describe a loss of drive power, and three describe a fire risk. The 2024 Palisade notice describes engine valve springs breaking while driving, which can also create a hole in the engine block. The 2025-2026 Tucson and Santa Fe notice describes improperly tightened connecting rod bolts causing power loss, engine damage and a possible oil leak. The 2022 Santa Fe and Santa Cruz notice covers a turbocharger oil supply pipe that may crack and leak oil in the engine compartment. The 2024 Elantra, Venue and Kona notice describes an electrical short in the exhaust gas recirculation valve assembly. Each recall names a different model group and a narrow year range.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A loss of drive power (2024 Palisade valve spring recall, 2025-2026 Tucson and Santa Fe connecting rod recall, and 2024 Elantra, Venue and Kona EGR valve recall).
  • An oil leak in the engine compartment (2022 Santa Fe and Santa Cruz turbocharger oil supply pipe recall, and the 2025-2026 Tucson and Santa Fe connecting rod recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. The 2024 Palisade valve spring notice, the 2025-2026 Tucson and Santa Fe connecting rod notice, the 2022 Santa Fe and Santa Cruz turbocharger notice and the 2024 Elantra, Venue and Kona EGR notice each describe the risk — loss of drive power, fire, or both — and then describe the dealer repair. None of them sets out an interim instruction or a limit on driving while owners wait.

What the free repair involves

All four remedies are free of charge. Dealers replace the engine sub-assembly on the 2024 Palisade, replace the engine on 2025-2026 Tucson and Santa Fe vehicles, replace the turbocharger oil supply pipe on the 2022 Santa Fe and Santa Cruz, and replace the exhaust gas recirculation valve assembly on the 2024 Elantra, Venue and Kona. No software updates are involved.

Common questions

Will Hyundai replace my whole engine under these recalls?

Two notices say so: the 2024 Palisade recall replaces the engine sub-assembly, and the 2025-2026 Tucson and Santa Fe recall replaces the engine, both free of charge. The other two replace a turbocharger oil pipe or the EGR valve assembly.

What do these notices say about fire risk?

Three notices name it: a hole in the engine block on the 2024 Palisade, an oil leak near hot components on 2025-2026 Tucson and Santa Fe, and a cracked turbocharger oil pipe on the 2022 Santa Fe and Santa Cruz.

My 2024 Kona lost power — is that this recall?

The 2024 Elantra, Venue and Kona notice says an electrical short in the exhaust gas recirculation valve assembly can result in a loss of drive power, and dealers replace that assembly free of charge. Check your VIN and have the vehicle looked at.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
