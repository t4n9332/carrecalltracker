Audi seat belt recalls: buckles, retractors and child seat locking

6 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 24, 2026

Six recalls, and two patterns run through them. Child-seat anchoring appears twice: a 2021 recall says rear seat belt automatic locking retractors may deactivate early across a wide 2021 Audi range plus the Volkswagen ID.4, and recall 69D8 says the automatic locking retractor may fail to engage on certain 2025 Q6 E-Tron, Q6 Sportback E-Tron, SQ6 E-Tron and S6 Sportback E-Tron models. Front seat belt hardware on 2025 vehicles accounts for three more: a damaged retractor torsion bar under 69UM, front buckles missing a rivet on 2025 Q7, Q8, SQ8 and RS Q8 under 692K, and incorrectly manufactured front belts on 2025 Q5 and SQ5 under 69GT. An older recall covers pretensioners that may not activate on certain 2017 Q7 and A4 models and the 2018 Q5.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none carries a park-outside instruction. Each describes a risk that appears in a crash: pretensioners that may not activate properly, a retractor torsion bar that could fail, front buckles missing the rivet that secures them to the stalk, front belts that may not properly restrain occupants, and automatic locking retractors that may not hold a child restraint securely. Beyond the free repair, the notices give owners no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is carried out by a dealer, free of charge. Dealers replace the seat belt pretensioners under the 2016 recall; inspect and replace as necessary the middle-rear seat belt assemblies under the 2021 recall; replace one or both front seat belts under 69UM; replace both front seat belt buckles under 692K; inspect and replace the rear seat belt assemblies as necessary under 69D8; and replace the driver and front passenger belts under 69GT.

Common questions

Which of these recalls affect installing a child seat?

Two. The 2021 recall says rear seat belt automatic locking retractors may deactivate early, preventing a child restraint from securing properly, and recall 69D8 says the automatic locking retractor may fail to engage when a child seat is installed using the seat belt.

Are the 2025 Audi seat belt recalls all the same problem?

No. Recall 69UM covers a torsion bar in the front seat belt retractors, 692K covers front buckles missing a rivet, and 69GT covers driver and front passenger belts manufactured incorrectly. Each names its own vehicles and its own replacement remedy.

How do I check whether my Audi is included?

Audi mailed owner notification letters for each of these recalls and gives customer service at 1-800-253-2834. For the newer recalls, the notices say Vehicle Identification Numbers become searchable on NHTSA.gov, so a VIN check confirms your vehicle.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
