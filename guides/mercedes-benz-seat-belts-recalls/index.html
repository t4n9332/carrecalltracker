Mercedes-Benz seat belt recalls: buckles, anchors and child restraint locking

4 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Seat Belts · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 12, 2025

Child restraint locking is the thread running through two of these four Mercedes-Benz recalls. Rear seat belt automatic locking retractors may deactivate early on 2021 G550, AMG G63, E350, E450, AMG CLS53, AMG E53, AMG GT43, GT53 and GT63, 2021-2022 CLS450 and AMG E63 models, and the center rear belts on 2025-2026 GLE 350 4MATIC were installed without the child seat protection function. The other two sit at the front seats: front belt buckles on 2018-2019 E-Class and GLC models and 2019 CLS, G-Class and AMG GT four-door models may read a fastened belt as unfastened, and a driver-side belt cover on 2022-2025 C 300 and 2023-2024 AMG C 43 may leave the anchor loose.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Front seat belt buckle recall: although correctly fastened, the front seat belts may be inaccurately detected as being unfastened.
  • Recall for 2022-2025 C 300 and 2023-2024 AMG C 43: the seat belt cover may not be properly installed on the tensioner, which can cause the seat belt anchor to come loose.
  • Recall for 2025-2026 GLE 350 4MATIC: the center rear seat belts may fail to lock a child restraint properly.
  • Recall for 2021 G550, E-Class, CLS and AMG models: the rear seat belt automatic locking retractors may deactivate early, which can prevent a child restraint system from securing properly.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the four notices in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park the vehicle outside. That applies to the front seat belt buckle recall, the C 300 and AMG C 43 seat belt anchor recall, the GLE 350 4MATIC center rear belt recall and the 2021 rear seat belt automatic locking retractor recall. The notices describe risks that arise in a crash, including restraints and pretensioners that may not perform as intended, and direct owners to the dealer remedy.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the front seat belt buckles and replace them as necessary on the 2019 recall, inspect and repair the seat belt covers as necessary on the C 300 and AMG C 43 recall, replace the center rear seat belt assembly on the GLE 350 4MATIC recall, and inspect and replace the rear seat belt assemblies as necessary on the 2021 retractor recall. All free of charge.

Common questions

What do the notices say about using a child seat in the covered vehicles?

The notice says the center rear seat belts may have been installed without the child seat protection function and fail to lock a child restraint properly. It gives owners no interim instruction; dealers replace the center rear seat belt assembly free of charge.

Does the seat belt buckle recall affect my air bag system?

The notice says correctly fastened front belts may be inaccurately detected as unfastened, and that the electric PRE-SAFE function and the seat belt pretensioners may not deploy during a crash. Dealers inspect the front buckles and replace them as necessary, free of charge.

Which belt is covered by the C 300 anchor recall?

The notice names the driver-side seat belt, saying its cover may not be properly installed on the seat belt tensioner, which can cause the seat belt anchor to come loose. The recall covers 2022-2025 C 300 and 2023-2024 AMG C 43 vehicles.
