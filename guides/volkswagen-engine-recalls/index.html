Volkswagen engine recalls cover loose engine covers and Tiguan support bolts

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Engine · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-02-17

Volkswagen's three engine recalls are small in number but repeat one theme: the cover over the engine. On 2022 Golf GTI and Golf R vehicles, certain driving situations such as high acceleration maneuvers may loosen the engine design cover, which can then touch hot surfaces in the engine compartment and melt. On 2024-2025 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles, the engine cover may have been improperly installed after vehicle service, letting it come loose and contact hot engine surfaces the same way. Both notices describe a fire risk. The third recall is different: on 2018 Tiguan long wheel base vehicles the engine support bolts may not have been properly tightened, which the notice links to unexpected engine breakdown or loss of engine power.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Unexpected engine breakdown or loss of engine power, named in the 2018 Tiguan long wheel base engine support bolt recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

No recall in this group carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2022 Golf GTI and Golf R engine design cover notice does not tell owners to stop driving. The 2024-2025 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport engine cover notice does not tell owners to stop driving. The 2018 Tiguan long wheel base engine support bolt notice does not tell owners to stop driving either. Two of the three describe an increased risk of fire and the Tiguan notice describes an increased risk of a crash, but none of them gives owners an interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

All three remedies are free of charge. For the 2022 Golf GTI and Golf R recall, dealers remove the engine design cover, and Volkswagen says it will provide a free updated cover under a separate Service Action once parts become available. For the 2024-2025 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport recall, dealers remove the engine cover. For the 2018 Tiguan recall, dealers replace the engine support bolts.

Common questions

Which Volkswagen models are covered by the engine cover recalls?

The notices name 2022 Golf GTI and Golf R vehicles, where high acceleration maneuvers may loosen the engine design cover, and 2024-2025 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles, where the cover may have been improperly installed after vehicle service.

Will I get a replacement engine cover after the dealer removes mine?

For the 2022 Golf GTI and Golf R recall, the notice says Volkswagen will provide a free updated engine design cover under a separate Service Action once parts become available. The 2024-2025 Atlas notice only says dealers remove the cover.

What does the Tiguan engine support bolt recall involve?

It covers 2018 Tiguan long wheel base vehicles whose engine support bolts may not have been properly tightened. The notice says this may cause unexpected engine breakdown or loss of engine power, and dealers replace the bolts free of charge.
