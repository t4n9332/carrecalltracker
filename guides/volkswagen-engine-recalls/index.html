Why are some Volkswagen engine covers and mounting bolts coming loose?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Engine · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-02-17

These three Volkswagen engine recalls involve two separate problems. Two of them affect the engine design cover: on 2022 Golf R vehicles, high-acceleration driving maneuvers may loosen the cover, letting it contact hot engine surfaces and melt; on 2024-2025 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles, the cover may have been improperly reinstalled after service, letting it come loose and contact hot surfaces the same way. The third recall is different: on 2018 Tiguan long-wheelbase vehicles, the engine support bolts may not have been properly tightened, which can lead to an unexpected engine breakdown or loss of engine power. All three recalls raise separate risks - fire from the covers, a stall from the bolts.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The engine cover recalls describe no warning sign before the cover loosens or melts
  • Loosening is more likely after high-acceleration driving maneuvers (Golf R recall)
  • Unexpected engine breakdown or loss of engine power while driving (Tiguan bolts recall)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. The 2022 Golf R recall says a loosened engine cover that contacts hot engine surfaces can melt and increase the risk of fire. The 2024-2025 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport recall describes the same melting and fire risk from an improperly reinstalled cover. The 2018 Tiguan recall says loose engine support bolts can cause an unexpected engine breakdown or loss of engine power, increasing the risk of a crash. Beyond stating these risks, the notices give no other driving instructions.

What the free repair involves

For the Golf R recall, dealers remove the engine design cover free of charge, with a free updated cover to follow later. For the Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport recall, dealers remove the engine cover free of charge. For the Tiguan recall, dealers replace the engine support bolts free of charge.

Common questions

Is the Golf R engine cover recall the same as the Atlas one?

No. The 2022 Golf R recall involves a cover that can loosen during high-acceleration driving; the 2024-2025 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport recall involves a cover that may have been improperly reinstalled after service. Both can contact hot surfaces and melt, raising fire risk.

What happens if my Tiguan's engine support bolts are loose?

Volkswagen says improperly tightened engine support bolts on 2018 Tiguan long-wheelbase vehicles may cause an unexpected engine breakdown or loss of engine power while driving, which increases the risk of a crash.

Will Volkswagen give me a permanent fix for the engine cover recall?

For the 2022 Golf R recall, dealers first remove the engine design cover free of charge; Volkswagen said it would provide a free updated cover once parts became available, under a separate service action.
