Volkswagen engine cover and mounting bolt recalls: what owners should know

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Engine · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-02-17

All three Volkswagen engine-area recalls come down to parts that were not secured. Two of them involve the plastic engine design cover: on 2022 Golf R and Golf GTI models, hard acceleration can work the cover loose, and on 2024-2025 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport, a cover reinstalled incorrectly after service can shift onto hot engine surfaces. Both carry a fire risk, and in both cases Volkswagen's answer is to take the cover off rather than re-secure it. The third, on 2018 Tiguan long wheelbase models, is different: engine support bolts that may not have been properly tightened, which can let the engine lose power or quit. Atlas and Golf models account for the fire-related notices.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A rattle, buzz or knocking from the engine bay after hard acceleration (2022 Golf R/GTI)
  • A burning or melting-plastic smell, or smoke from under the hood
  • On a 2024-2025 Atlas, an engine cover sitting loose or out of place after recent service work
  • On the 2018 Tiguan, the engine losing power or stalling while you drive
  • Often no warning at all — nothing on the dashboard flags a loose cover

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside, so all three vehicles can be driven while you wait for a service appointment. That said, two of them are fire-related — the 2022 Golf R/GTI cover and the 2024-2025 Atlas cover — and fire recalls deserve the earliest appointment you can get. On the Golf R and GTI, the notice names high-acceleration driving as what loosens the cover, so go easy until it is removed. For the 2018 Tiguan, pull over safely if the engine loses power.

What the free repair involves

On both cover recalls, dealers simply remove the engine cover at no charge; for the Golf R and GTI, Volkswagen said an updated cover would be fitted later under a separate service action once parts became available. On the 2018 Tiguan, dealers replace the engine support bolts. All work is free, and none of it is done over the air.

Common questions

Why does Volkswagen just remove the engine cover instead of replacing it?

Removing it is the remedy the notices give, and it takes away the fire risk immediately. Golf R and GTI owners were told an updated engine design cover would follow later, free, under a separate service action once Volkswagen had parts available.

My Atlas was fine until a shop worked on it — does the recall still apply?

Yes. The 2024-2025 Atlas recall covers covers that were improperly installed after vehicle service, so the problem can appear on a car that was fine from new. Volkswagen mailed notification letters March 7, 2025, and the removal is free.

Is the 2018 Tiguan bolt recall the same problem as the cover recalls?

No. The Tiguan issue is engine support bolts that may not have been properly tightened, which can cause the engine to lose power or stall rather than catch fire. Dealers replace those bolts; that recall began March 26, 2018.
