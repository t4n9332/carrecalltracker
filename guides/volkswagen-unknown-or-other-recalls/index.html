Why Is Volkswagen Repurchasing Certain Atlas and Jetta Vehicles?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Unknown OR Other · model years 2018–2019 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2021-05-05

These three Volkswagen recalls share the same root cause: vehicles used internally for evaluation or testing were sold to customers without confirmation that they were built to every applicable Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard, and some were modified before sale. The affected models span many years and nameplates, including 2018 Atlas and Golf R, 2016-2019 Golf R GP and Passat, 2018 Tiguan, 2019 Jetta, and older Jetta, Golf, and Beetle models dating back to 2007. In each case Volkswagen is not describing a specific mechanical defect but a compliance gap, and the company's remedy is to repurchase the affected vehicles rather than repair them.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices do not describe any warning sign an owner could notice; the issue is that compliance with federal safety standards was not confirmed before sale

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Each notice says only that vehicles which do not meet all applicable Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards may carry an increased risk of crash, fire, or injury to occupants, without identifying a specific mechanical defect to watch for. Because the vehicles were sold without confirmed compliance rather than a known broken part, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving while a repurchase is arranged.

What the free repair involves

Unlike most recalls, the remedy here is not a repair. Volkswagen notifies owners and repurchases the affected vehicles, since they were originally used for internal testing or evaluation and sold without confirmed compliance to federal safety standards. This repurchase is provided to owners free of charge.

Common questions

Why is Volkswagen buying back my vehicle instead of repairing it?

These vehicles were originally used for internal testing or evaluation and may have been modified before sale, so Volkswagen could not confirm they met all federal safety standards, and repurchase was chosen as the remedy.

What specific defect caused this recall?

The notices do not identify one specific mechanical defect. They state only that the vehicles were sold without confirmation of compliance with various Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards.

Which Volkswagen models are covered by these repurchase recalls?

Models include the 2018 Atlas, 2018 Golf R and 2019 Golf R GP, 2018 Tiguan, 2019 Jetta, 2016-2018 Passat, and several older Jetta, Golf, and Beetle model years.
