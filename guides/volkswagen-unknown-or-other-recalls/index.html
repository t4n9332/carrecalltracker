Why is Volkswagen repurchasing certain internal-use Atlas, Jetta and Passat vehicles?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Unknown OR Other · model years 2018–2019 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 5, 2021

All three of these Volkswagen recalls cover internal-use or evaluation vehicles rather than one failing part. Recall 01F1 lists 2017 Touareg GP, Tiguan, Golf A6, E-Golf GP and CCF, 2016-2017 Passat GP, 2018-2019 Golf R GP and 2018 Atlas vehicles sold without confirmation that they were built to all applicable regulatory requirements and possibly modified before sale. Recall 01E9 repeats that description for 2018 Golf R and Atlas, 2017 Golf Alltrack and E-Golf, and 2016-2018 Passat. Recall 01D7 covers a long Jetta, Beetle, Golf, Passat and Tiguan list, from 2007-2009 Jetta Sedan through 2019 Jetta, modified during an internal evaluation period; that notice states it is an expansion of NHTSA recall 18V-329.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Recalls 01F1, 01E9 and 01D7 each state that vehicles which do not meet all regulatory requirements may have an increased risk of a crash, fire or injury, and each says Volkswagen will notify owners. The notices give no interim instruction to follow while the recall is open. Owners with questions are directed to Volkswagen customer service.

What the free repair involves

These notices do not describe a dealer repair at all. For recalls 01F1, 01E9 and 01D7, Volkswagen states that it will notify owners and repurchase the affected vehicles. No inspection, parts replacement, software update or over-the-air remedy is described, and the notices do not state any cost or charge to owners.

Common questions

What does it mean that Volkswagen will repurchase my vehicle?

The notices for recalls 01F1, 01E9 and 01D7 say only that Volkswagen will notify owners and repurchase the affected vehicles. They describe no dealer repair, and they give no terms, amounts or timing for the repurchase.

Why were these vehicles recalled when no specific part is named?

The notices say these internal-use vehicles were sold without confirmation that they were built to all applicable regulatory requirements, or were modified during an internal evaluation period, so they may fail to comply with various Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards.

Do the notices tell me to stop driving the vehicle?

None of the three notices includes a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. They state that vehicles not meeting all regulatory requirements may have an increased risk of a crash, fire or injury, and that Volkswagen will repurchase them.
