Why is Volkswagen buying back these cars instead of repairing them?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Unknown OR Other · model years 2018–2019 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2021-05-05

All three of these Volkswagen recalls are paperwork recalls, not mechanical ones. Each covers internal-use or company vehicles that Volkswagen sold without confirming they were built to every applicable regulatory requirement, and no specific broken part is named in any of the three notices. The same models keep reappearing: the 2018 Atlas and the 2018-2019 Golf R are listed in two of the three, alongside Passat, Tiguan, Jetta, Touareg, Golf Alltrack and e-Golf, clustered in the 2016-2019 model years. The remedy is identical every time and unusual for a recall: Volkswagen repurchases the vehicle rather than fixing it, under recall numbers 01D7, 01E9 and 01F1.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No warning signs at all — none of these three notices describes a fault you could see, hear or feel.
  • The first clue is usually the letter itself, which offers a repurchase instead of a repair appointment.
  • Paperwork or history showing the car was a Volkswagen internal-use or company vehicle before it was sold.
  • Volkswagen recall number 01D7, 01E9 or 01F1 appearing on your notice.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. What sets them apart is that there is no repair at all: Volkswagen is taking the vehicles back instead. The 2019 notice mentions a possible crash, fire or injury risk without naming a specific fault, and the 2021 notice says a vehicle that does not meet all the rules carries a higher risk of injury, crash or fire. Treat these as cars Volkswagen does not intend to keep on the road, and start the buyback promptly.

What the free repair involves

There is no dealer repair here — no parts, no software update, no over-the-air fix. In all three recalls Volkswagen notifies owners and repurchases the affected vehicle. Because this is the recall remedy, it costs the owner nothing. Owners work through Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298, referencing recall 01D7, 01E9 or 01F1.

Common questions

What does a buyback actually mean for me?

Volkswagen repurchases the vehicle instead of repairing it. There is no dealer fix for these three recalls, so the process runs through Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 rather than through a normal service appointment at a dealership.

Why is no repair available for my car?

The notices say these internal-use vehicles were sold without confirmation that they were built to all applicable regulatory requirements. No specific defective part is identified, so Volkswagen is taking the vehicles back entirely rather than replacing a component.

I bought mine used — am I still covered?

Recall remedies follow the vehicle, not the original buyer. Check your VIN against recalls 01D7, 01E9 and 01F1 with Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 to find out whether your car is one of the vehicles being repurchased.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
