Tesla recalls affecting automatic emergency braking, cameras and pedestrian warning sound

3 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Forward Collision Avoidance · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 27, 2024

These three Tesla recalls all involve the systems that watch the road ahead — or, in one case, the sound that warns people around the car. The 2022 camera recall covers 2020-2021 Model 3 and 2021 Model Y vehicles whose fisheye and narrow camera cable terminals were incorrectly installed in the harness connector, leaving the camera views swapped and impairing Autosteer and Automatic Emergency Braking. The 2021 recall is broader: 2017-2021 Model S, Model 3, Model X and 2020-2021 Model Y running software version 2021.36.5.2, where a communication error could cause false forward-collision warnings or unexpected automatic braking. The 2024 recall covers 2013 and 2018-2021 Model S, 2020-2021 Model X, 2018-2022 Model 3 and 2020-2022 Model Y with Pedestrian Warning System sounds muted by a factory reset.

Warning signs to watch for

  • False forward-collision warnings, or the automatic emergency brake activating unexpectedly and stopping the car suddenly (2021 recall SB-21-00-004).
  • Pedestrian Warning System sounds muted after a factory reset (2024 recall SB-24-00-006).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2022 camera recall, the 2021 forward-collision warning and automatic braking recall, and the 2024 Pedestrian Warning System recall all describe the defect and the remedy without giving any interim instruction about continuing to drive. What each notice does say is what the risk is: impaired Autosteer and Automatic Emergency Braking, an unexpected sudden stop, and pedestrians who may be unaware of an approaching vehicle. Check your VIN and book the remedy.

What the free repair involves

For the camera recall, Tesla Service corrects the swapped terminals and recalibrates the cameras. The 2021 forward-collision and automatic braking recall and the 2024 Pedestrian Warning System recall are both handled by an over-the-air software update — release 2023.44.30.14 in the 2024 case. All three notices state the remedy is free of charge.

Common questions

Does my Tesla need a service visit, or does the fix arrive on its own?

It depends which recall applies. The camera terminal recall is done at Tesla Service, which corrects the terminals and recalibrates the cameras. The 2021 braking recall and the 2024 pedestrian sound recall are over-the-air software updates, Tesla's notices say.

Which Teslas are covered by the camera wiring recall?

The notice lists 2020-2021 Model 3 and 2021 Model Y vehicles. It says the fisheye and narrow camera cable terminals were incorrectly installed in the cable harness connector, swapping the camera views and impairing Autosteer and Automatic Emergency Braking performance.

Do the notices tell owners to stop using Autosteer?

The notices do not give that instruction. The camera notice says swapped views can impair Autosteer and Automatic Emergency Braking performance, and the 2021 notice says a communication error may cause false forward-collision warnings or unexpected braking, but neither tells owners to stop driving.
