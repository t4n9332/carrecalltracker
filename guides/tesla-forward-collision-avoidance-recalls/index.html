Why does my Tesla's collision-avoidance system act unexpectedly?

3 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Forward Collision Avoidance · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-02-27

Three recalls address Tesla's forward collision-avoidance systems, each from a different cause. A 2022 recall affects 2021 Model 3, where fisheye and narrow camera cables were swapped during installation, which can prevent the cameras from detecting crossing objects or sharp curves and impair Autosteer and Automatic Emergency Braking. A 2021 recall covers 2018-2021 Model 3, 2020-2021 Model Y, and 2021 Model S running a specific software version, where a communication error could trigger a false forward-collision warning or an unexpected automatic brake activation. A 2024 recall covers 2018-2022 Model 3, 2020 Model Y, and 2021 Model S, where a factory reset could mute the Pedestrian Warning System sound without the driver knowing.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Camera views may be swapped, impairing detection of crossing objects and sharp curves for Autosteer and Automatic Emergency Braking
  • Vehicle may brake suddenly and unexpectedly from a false forward-collision warning
  • Pedestrian warning sound may be muted after a factory reset, with no alert to the driver

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recall notices carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe an increased crash risk from cameras that can't properly detect crossing objects or sharp curves, from the vehicle braking suddenly and unexpectedly due to a false collision warning, and from pedestrians being unaware of an approaching vehicle when the warning sound is muted. Across all three recalls in this group, the notice stops at describing that risk and does not instruct owners to stop driving or to park outside while awaiting the repair.

What the free repair involves

For the camera-wiring recall, Tesla Service corrects the swapped terminals and recalibrates the cameras, free of charge. The false-braking and muted pedestrian-warning-sound recalls are both fixed with a free over-the-air software update — no dealer visit needed for those two.

Common questions

Why did my Tesla suddenly brake for no reason?

One 2021 recall found that a software communication error on certain 2018-2021 Model 3, Model Y, and Model S vehicles could trigger a false forward-collision warning or unexpected automatic braking; Tesla fixed it with a free OTA update.

Does my Model 3 have a camera wiring problem?

Only certain 2021 Model 3 vehicles are covered by the recall for swapped fisheye and narrow camera cables, which can impair Autosteer and Automatic Emergency Braking; Tesla Service corrects the wiring for free.

Can a factory reset turn off my Tesla's pedestrian warning sound?

Yes — the 2024 recall notice says a factory reset muted the Pedestrian Warning System sound on certain 2018-2022 Model 3, Model Y, and Model S vehicles; Tesla fixed it with a free software update.
