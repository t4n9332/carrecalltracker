Tesla Forward Collision Avoidance Recalls Include Braking and Camera Problems

3 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Forward Collision Avoidance · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 27, 2024

Model 3 and Model Y appear throughout this Tesla group, but the notices describe distinct problems. Swapped camera connections affect certain 2020-2021 Model 3 and 2021 Model Y vehicles, impairing detection used by Autosteer and automatic emergency braking. A communication error affecting certain 2017-2021 Model S, Model 3, Model X, and 2020-2021 Model Y vehicles running software 2021.36.5.2 can trigger false collision warnings or unexpected braking. The pedestrian warning notice concerns muted sounds after a factory reset on certain 2013 and 2018-2021 Model S, 2020-2021 Model X, 2018-2022 Model 3, and 2020-2022 Model Y vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Software Bug Triggers Unexpected Automatic Braking (SB-21-00-004): false forward-collision warnings or unexpected automatic emergency braking may occur, potentially causing a sudden stop.
  • Pedestrian warning sound muted after a factory reset (SB-24-00-006): Pedestrian Warning System sounds are muted.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices includes a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The camera-terminal recall describes impaired Autosteer and automatic emergency braking performance. The unexpected-braking recall describes a crash risk from unexpected AEB activation causing a sudden stop. The pedestrian warning recall describes a crash risk because pedestrians may be unaware of an approaching vehicle when warning sounds are muted. For each of these recalls, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving; the supplied notices give no separate interim driving instruction.

What the free repair involves

Tesla Service corrects the swapped camera terminals and recalibrates the cameras free of charge under SB-22-17-007. The unexpected-braking recall, SB-21-00-004, receives a free OTA software update. The muted pedestrian warning recall, SB-24-00-006, also receives a free OTA update, specifically release 2023.44.30.14.

Common questions

Which Tesla recall describes unexpected automatic braking?

SB-21-00-004 concerns a communication error in software version 2021.36.5.2. The notice covers certain 2017-2021 Model S, Model 3, Model X, and 2020-2021 Model Y vehicles, and describes false forward-collision warnings or unexpected AEB activation.

Does the swapped-camera recall require a service repair?

The notice specifies that Tesla Service will correct the swapped cable terminals and recalibrate the cameras, free of charge. It covers certain 2020-2021 Model 3 and 2021 Model Y vehicles and describes impaired Autosteer and automatic emergency braking performance.

What fixes the pedestrian warning sound muted after a reset?

For SB-24-00-006, Tesla released OTA software update 2023.44.30.14 free of charge. The notice says a factory reset muted Pedestrian Warning System sounds, potentially leaving pedestrians unaware of an approaching vehicle. It does not instruct owners to stop driving.
