What are Ford's block heater, label and backup camera recalls about?

15 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Equipment · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 15, 2026

Ford's exterior lighting and equipment recalls fall into a few repeating groups. The largest group is engine block heater recalls: the heater may crack and develop a coolant leak, causing a short circuit when it is plugged in, across Explorer, Escape, Focus, Ranger, Bronco, Bronco Sport, Maverick, Fusion, Lincoln MKC and Corsair. A second cluster involves ASA Electronics Voyager backup camera monitors on ambulances, shuttle buses and upfitted trucks built on Ford chassis, which may revert to factory defaults and reverse the image. The third is labelling: an incorrect EcoSport brake fluid cap label, incorrect load carrying capacity on 2022-2023 Bronco and 2019-2023 Ranger labels, overstated payload values on certain 2020 F-350 diesel labels, and Safety Certification Labels missing tire size, rim type and pressure on 2022 Super Duty and 2023 E-Series vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The ASA Electronics Voyager backup camera recalls state the monitors may revert to factory default settings, which may cause the camera image to be reversed.
  • The 2018 EcoSport recall states the vehicles were shipped with the European brake reservoir pictogram cap label and European English owner's manual kits.
  • The block heater recalls state the engine block heater may crack and develop a coolant leak.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. The three engine block heater recalls do carry one interim instruction: owners are advised not to plug in their block heater until the vehicle is remedied. That instruction applies only to those recalls. For the backup camera monitor recalls, the EcoSport brake fluid cap label recall, the Bronco and Ranger load capacity label recall, the 2020 F-350 label recall and the Super Duty and E-Series Safety Certification Label recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the block heater recalls, dealers replace the block heater free of charge, with an alternative option to replace the heater element with a threaded plug and remove the electrical cord. For the camera recalls, a harness relay capacitor module is added or the display is replaced. The label recalls are fixed by mailing or installing replacement labels. All of this is free of charge.

Common questions

Can I still use my block heater while I wait for the repair?

The three block heater notices advise owners not to plug in the block heater until the vehicle is remedied. Dealers replace the heater free of charge, or replace the element with a threaded plug and remove the cord.

My truck's tire label is wrong — do I have to visit a dealer?

For the Bronco and Ranger load capacity recall, Ford mails a new TREAD label to install over the existing one, and owners may instead have a Ford or Lincoln dealer install it. Both options are free of charge.

Who do I contact about the backup camera monitor recalls?

Those recalls were filed by the companies that built the buses, ambulances and trucks on Ford chassis. The notices direct owners to ASA Electronics customer service at 1-800-384-4400, and in some cases to the body builder, such as SVO Group or Farber.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
