Ford Equipment Recalls Involving Block Heaters, Backup Cameras, and Labels

16 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Equipment · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 14, 2026

Block-heater fire risks and reversed backup-camera images recur in these Ford equipment notices. The October 2025 heater notice spans Explorer, Fusion, Ranger, Escape, Bronco, Bronco Sport, Maverick, and Lincoln models with specifically listed years. Two January 2026 notices separately cover Focus/Escape/MKC and Focus/Explorer heater leaks; April 2026 addresses overheating in 2025 Maverick and 2026 Bronco Sport vehicles. Camera notices repeatedly involve ASA Electronics Voyager monitors on converted commercial vehicles, including E-Series shuttle buses and F-550 specialty vehicles. Label problems form another pattern: 2018 EcoSport brake-fluid information, certain 2020 F-350 weight documentation, and load-capacity labels on specified Bronco, Ranger, and Super Duty F-250 vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • June 2019 SVO Group E-350/E-450 shuttle-bus recall: the backup-camera image may be reversed.
  • May 2019 LGE Coachworks recall: the backup-camera display may unexpectedly revert to default settings and show a reversed image.
  • May 2019 VCI ambulance recall: the backup-camera monitor may unexpectedly revert to default settings and show a reversed image.
  • June 2019 Farber F-550 recall: the backup-camera image may be reversed.
  • June 2019 Unicell recall: the backup-camera image may be reversed.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the supplied equipment notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. Four notices give block-heater restrictions: the October 2025 Ford/Lincoln notice, both January 2026 notices covering Escape/Focus and Explorer/Focus, and the April 2026 Bronco Sport/Maverick notice. Each advises owners not to plug in the block heater until the vehicle is remedied. The camera and labeling notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The heater restriction is an instruction about plugging in the heater for those four recalls.

What the free repair involves

The four heater recalls specify free block-heater replacement; April 2026 also specifies cord inspection and replacement if necessary. Farber's camera recall adds a harness relay capacitor module, while LGE's replaces displays, free of charge. Recall 18C04 replaces the EcoSport cap and manual kit free. The May 2023 load-label recall provides a free replacement label, with optional free dealer installation.

Common questions

Which Ford block-heater notices say not to plug it in?

The October 2025 Ford/Lincoln notice, both January 2026 heater notices, and the April 2026 Bronco Sport/Maverick notice advise against plugging in the block heater until repair. Their affected models and years differ, so each instruction belongs to its specific notice.

Will a reversed Voyager camera image get the same repair on each vehicle?

The supplied remedies differ by vehicle converter. SVO/ASA provides a harness relay capacitor module; Farber adds one; LGE replaces displays. VCI says it will remedy the displays, and Unicell says it will offer a remedy. Each describes free work.

Can Ford mail me a corrected load-capacity label?

The May 2023 notice for certain 2022-2023 Bronco, 2023 Super Duty F-250, and 2019-2023 Ranger vehicles says Ford will mail a free replacement TREAD label. It also offers free installation at a Ford or Lincoln dealer.
