Ram 1500 spare tire and tire pressure warning recalls explained

3 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Tires · model years 2019–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 9, 2021

All three of these Ram recalls involve the same truck: the Ram 1500. Two of them, covering 2019 trucks (recall V67) and 2021 trucks (recall Y62), share one pattern — the spare tire provided is not the size listed on the tire placard, so the inflation pressure shown on that label does not match the spare actually fitted. Both were filed as failures to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 110, Tire Selection and Rims. The third, recall VE1 on 2019 Ram 1500 trucks, is different: an incorrect tire pressure monitoring sensor may have been installed, so the low tire pressure warning indicator does not display, a failure to comply with FMVSS 138. No other Ram model appears in this group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall VE1 on 2019 Ram 1500 trucks states that the low tire pressure warning indicator may not display when tire pressure is low.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2019 Ram 1500 spare tire recall (V67), the 2021 Ram 1500 spare tire recall (Y62) and the 2019 Ram 1500 tire pressure sensor recall (VE1), the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice states only the risk it describes: in the two spare tire recalls, inflating the spare incorrectly increases the risk of a crash, and in the sensor recall the driver will be unaware if low tire pressure occurs, increasing the risk of a crash. The notices give no interim instructions.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the incorrect size spare tire with the correct size on 2019 Ram 1500 trucks under recall V67, replace the spare tire assembly on 2021 Ram 1500 trucks under recall Y62, and replace the TPMS sensor on 2019 Ram 1500 trucks under recall VE1. Each notice states the work is free of charge. Chrysler customer service is 1-800-853-1403.

Common questions

How would I know my Ram 1500 has the wrong-size spare tire?

The notices do not describe a check an owner can make. They state that the spare tire may differ in size and required inflation pressure from the tire placard label. Dealers replace the spare under recall V67 on 2019 trucks and recall Y62 on 2021 trucks, free of charge.

What does the TPMS sensor recall actually affect?

The notice for recall VE1 says an incorrect TPMS sensor could have been installed on certain 2019 Ram 1500 trucks, causing the sensor to not display the low tire pressure warning indicator, so the driver would be unaware of low pressure. Dealers replace the sensor free of charge.

My 2021 Ram 1500 spare has never been used. Does the recall still apply?

The notices do not make the remedy conditional on using the spare. Recall Y62 states that an incorrect-size spare may have been installed on certain 2021 Ram 1500 vehicles and that dealers will replace the spare tire assembly free of charge. FCA customer service is 1-800-853-1403.
