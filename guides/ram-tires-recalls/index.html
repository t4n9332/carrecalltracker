Why does my Ram 1500 have tire and spare tire recalls?

3 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Tires · model years 2019–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2021-09-09

All three tire recalls in this group affect only the Ram 1500, and none involve a tire actually failing. Two separate recalls - one in 2019 and one in 2021 - found that some 1500 trucks were built with a spare tire that does not match the size and inflation pressure printed on the tire placard, which could lead an owner to inflate the spare incorrectly. The third recall, also from 2019, found that some 1500 trucks had the wrong Tire Pressure Monitoring System sensor installed, so the low-tire-pressure warning light on the dashboard may not turn on when a tire is actually low.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Spare tire size or inflation pressure printed on it does not match the tire placard (2019 and 2021 recalls)
  • Low-tire-pressure warning light does not illuminate when a tire is low (2019 TPMS recall)
  • No warning sign of a tire actually failing - these recalls involve incorrect information and sensors, not tire defects

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls tell owners to stop driving. That includes both spare-tire-size recalls (2019 and 2021) and the 2019 recall for an incorrect TPMS sensor that may not display a low-tire-pressure warning. The notices describe the risk as coming from acting on wrong information - inflating a spare tire incorrectly, or not knowing a tire is low - rather than a part failing outright, and none direct an owner to stop driving or park outside.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the incorrect part free of charge: the wrong-size spare tire is swapped for the correct one in the 2019 and 2021 recalls, and the mismatched TPMS sensor is replaced in the 2019 recall. None of these fixes involve a software update; they are physical part replacements only.

Common questions

Are the two wrong-size spare tire recalls on my Ram 1500 the same issue?

They are two separate recalls, one from 2019 and one from 2021, but both involve the same kind of problem: a spare tire installed at the factory that does not match the size and pressure listed on the tire placard.

Why would my tire pressure warning light not work?

A 2019 recall found that some Ram 1500 trucks were built with an incorrect Tire Pressure Monitoring System sensor, which can prevent the low-tire-pressure warning light from displaying even when a tire is low.

Does a wrong-size spare tire recall mean my regular tires are defective?

No. These recalls involve only the spare tire's size and printed placard information, or the TPMS sensor. The notices do not describe any defect in the vehicle's four road tires themselves.
