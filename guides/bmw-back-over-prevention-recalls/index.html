Why does my BMW's backup camera show a blank or partial image?

5 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-01-25

Across five BMW back-over prevention recalls, the dominant problem is rearview camera software that fails to display a usable image in reverse. The 2019 recall covers a huge span of 2018-2020 models — M5, M550i, X3, X4, X5, X6, X7, Z4, 530e, 640i, M850i, plus the 2020 Toyota Supra — where the image can vanish entirely. The X3 shows up again in 2020 recalls, first because some units left the factory without camera software installed at all, then because the image was partly blocked. A 2021 recall covering many 2019-2021 X and 3 Series models describes missing or partial images. Separately, 2022-2023 i4 and iX electric vehicles can fail to emit their exterior pedestrian warning sound at startup, an unrelated defect.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rearview camera screen shows nothing when the transmission is put in reverse
  • Camera image is only partially visible or appears cut off
  • No rearview image at all on some 2020 X3 models built without the camera software
  • On 2022-2023 i4 and iX EVs, no exterior warning sound when starting up

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. The notices only describe the rearview camera or pedestrian warning defect and the software fix; they do not say the vehicle is unsafe to operate in the meantime. For the four camera recalls, the notices note that a missing, blank, or partially blocked backup image increases crash risk when reversing. For the 2023 i4/iX pedestrian sound recall, the notice says the missing warning sound raises injury risk to pedestrians, without instructing owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across all five recalls, BMW dealers update or program the rearview camera software or, for the i4/iX pedestrian sound issue, the external sound generator software — no parts are replaced. One 2020 X3 recall required dealers to program camera software missing from the factory. All repairs, including inspection and software updates, are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which BMW models are affected by the backup camera recalls?

The recalls cover a wide range of 2018-2021 BMW models, most often the X3, along with X4, X5, X6, X7, M5, M550i, Z4, and several sedan lines, plus the 2020 Toyota Supra in one recall.

Is the i4/iX pedestrian sound issue the same as the camera problem?

No. The 2023 recall for the i4 and iX involves the exterior sound generator that warns pedestrians at low speed, not the rearview camera; it's a separate software fault affecting only these electric models.

Do I need to stop driving until the recall is fixed?

None of these five notices instruct owners to stop driving or park outside. They describe the camera or sound defect and note the crash or injury risk, but the remedy is a free software update at a dealer.
