Why does the rearview camera image fail on BMW models?

5 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 25, 2023

Five BMW recalls sit here and four are the same story: the rearview camera. In the 2018-2020 recall — X3, X4, X5, X6, X7, Z4, 3, 5, 7 and 8 Series — the back-up camera display lacks an image. Certain 2020 X3 sDrive40i, xDrive40i and X3M40i show no image when the transmission is shifted to reverse. On 2020-2021 X3, X4, 530i, 540i and M5 models a small portion of the image may be slightly obscured, and on a broad 2019-2021 list the image may be missing or reduced. Two of those notices are recorded as failing Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 111. The fifth recall is different: 2022-2023 i4 eDrive40 and iX xDrive50 may not produce the external pedestrian warning sound.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2018-2020 camera recall: the back-up camera display lacks an image.
  • 2020 X3 recall: no rearview image is displayed when the transmission is shifted to reverse.
  • 2020-2021 X3, X4 and 5 Series recall: a small portion of the rearview image may be slightly obscured.
  • 2019-2021 camera recall: the rearview image may be missing or reduced.
  • 2022-2023 i4 and iX recall: the external pedestrian warning sound may not be generated during vehicle start-up.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The notices describe only the consequences: for the 2018-2020 camera recall, the lack of an image in the back-up camera display increases the risk of a crash; for the 2020 X3 recall, an inoperative display increases crash risk when reversing; for the 2020-2021 and 2019-2021 recalls, relying on only the camera image with a reduced or missing view increases the risk of a crash or injury; and for the i4 and iX, pedestrians may be unaware of an approaching vehicle.

What the free repair involves

All four camera recalls are handled at the software level: dealers update the back-up camera software (2018-2020 recall), program rearview camera software into the 2020 X3, and inspect the rearview camera software and update it if necessary (2020-2021 and 2019-2021 recalls). The i4 and iX get updated artificial sound generator software. The notices state the work is free of charge, except the 2020-2021 notice, which does not say.

Common questions

Which BMW models show up in more than one camera recall?

The X3 appears in all four camera recalls, and X4, X5, X7 and 5 Series cars appear in several of them. Model years overlap between 2018 and 2021, so one vehicle can fall under two notices. A VIN lookup shows which apply.

Do these recalls affect anything besides the camera image?

The camera notices describe only the rearview image — lacking, missing, reduced or slightly obscured. The fifth recall is separate: on 2022-2023 i4 eDrive40 and iX xDrive50, the artificial sound generator control unit may fail to make the external pedestrian warning sound.

What do the notices say about driving until the software work is done?

None of the five notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. They state that a missing or reduced rearview image increases the risk of a crash or injury when backing up, and that dealers carry out the software work.
