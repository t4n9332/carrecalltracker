Why can a Rivian R1S or R1T lose power or lights?

3 NHTSA recalls · Rivian · Electrical System · model years 2025–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-09-08

Three 2025 Rivian recalls involve electrical and software faults in the R1S and R1T. One is a hardware issue: an improperly grounded connection inside the High Voltage Distribution Box can cause a loss of drive power. The other two are software-only. Vehicles running a Highway Assist software version older than 2025.18.30 may fail to correctly identify a lead vehicle ahead. Separately, software version 2024.39.30 could prevent certain exterior lights from turning on when the Car Costume feature is activated. All three recalls apply to the 2025 model year, and the two software issues are corrected with an over-the-air update rather than a dealer visit.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Sudden loss of drive power while operating the vehicle
  • Highway Assist failing to recognize a vehicle ahead
  • Exterior lights that stay off when Car Costume mode is engaged
  • The notices describe no specific warning sign before the drive-power loss occurs

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices say a loss of drive power, a failure to detect a lead vehicle, or deactivated exterior lighting each increases the risk of a crash, but none tell owners to stop driving. This applies to the High Voltage Distribution Box grounding recall, the Highway Assist software recall, and the Car Costume exterior-lighting recall - all three affecting 2025 Rivian R1S and R1T vehicles.

What the free repair involves

For the grounding issue, Rivian inspects and repairs the High Voltage Distribution Box ground strap joint, or replaces the high-voltage battery pack if needed. The Highway Assist and exterior-lighting problems are both corrected with an over-the-air software update that installs without a dealer visit. All repairs and updates are free of charge.

Common questions

Why can a 2025 Rivian R1S or R1T suddenly lose drive power?

An improperly grounded connection inside the High Voltage Distribution Box can cause a loss of drive power. Rivian inspects and repairs the ground strap joint, or replaces the high-voltage battery pack as needed, free of charge.

Do I need a service appointment for the Highway Assist recall?

No. Rivian released an over-the-air software update for vehicles running a version older than 2025.18.30, so the fix for the Highway Assist lead-vehicle detection issue installs automatically at no cost.

What was the Car Costume lighting recall about?

Software version 2024.39.30 could keep certain exterior lights from turning on when the Car Costume feature was activated, reducing the vehicle's visibility to other drivers. Rivian fixed it with a free over-the-air update.
