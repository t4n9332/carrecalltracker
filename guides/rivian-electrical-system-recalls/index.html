Rivian R1S and R1T electrical recalls: drive power and software faults

3 NHTSA recalls · Rivian · Electrical System · model years 2025–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-09-08

Every recall in this group covers 2025 Rivian R1S and R1T vehicles, and two of the three are software problems addressed over the air. Recall FSAM-1744 applies to vehicles running software earlier than version 2025.18.30, where the Hands-Free Highway Assist software may fail to correctly identify a lead vehicle. Recall FSAM-1552 applies to software version 2024.39.30, which may prevent certain exterior lights from turning on when the Car Costume feature is activated. The outlier is FSAM-1723, a hardware fault: an improperly grounded connection inside the High Voltage Distribution Box may cause a loss of drive power. No earlier Rivian model years appear in this group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall FSAM-1723 states that a loss of drive power may occur on 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles.
  • Recall FSAM-1744 states that the Hands-Free Highway Assist software may fail to correctly identify a lead vehicle.
  • Recall FSAM-1552 states that certain exterior lights may not turn on when the Car Costume feature is activated.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the High Voltage Distribution Box recall (FSAM-1723), the Hands-Free Highway Assist recall (FSAM-1744) and the Car Costume exterior lighting recall (FSAM-1552), the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Each states only the risk involved: a loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash; failure to detect another vehicle increases the risk of a crash; and deactivated exterior lighting reduces the vehicle's visibility to other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash. No interim instructions are given.

What the free repair involves

For recall FSAM-1723, Rivian inspects and repairs the High Voltage Distribution Box ground strap joint or replaces the high voltage battery pack as necessary, free of charge. Recalls FSAM-1744 and FSAM-1552 are both handled by an over-the-air software update that Rivian has already released, free of charge. Owners may contact Rivian at 1-888-748-4261.

Common questions

Do I need a service appointment for the Highway Assist recall?

The notice for recall FSAM-1744 says Rivian has released an over-the-air software update, free of charge, for 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles running software earlier than version 2025.18.30. Owner notification letters were mailed October 17, 2025.

What does loss of drive power mean in the Rivian high voltage box recall?

The notice for recall FSAM-1723 says an improperly grounded connection inside the High Voltage Distribution Box may cause a loss of drive power, and that a loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash. It gives no further description.

Does the Car Costume lighting recall matter if I never use the feature?

The notice for recall FSAM-1552 ties the fault to software version 2024.39.30 and to the Car Costume feature being activated. It does not say the recall applies only to owners who use that feature; Rivian released a free over-the-air update.
