What do Rivian's electrical system recalls cover on R1S, R1T and R2?

4 NHTSA recalls · Rivian · Electrical System · model years 2025–2027 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 29, 2026

Rivian's electrical recalls cluster around two things: high-voltage power delivery and vehicle software. Two recalls involve the high-voltage system losing drive power — FSAM-1723 covers 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles with an improperly grounded connection inside the High Voltage Distribution Box, and FSAM-1888 covers 2027 R2 vehicles with a high voltage battery fastener that may have been improperly tightened. The other two are software. FSAM-1744 covers 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles running software prior to 2025.18.30, where Hands-Free Highway Assist may fail to correctly identify a lead vehicle. FSAM-1552 covers 2025 R1T and R1S vehicles on software version 2024.39.30, where certain exterior lights may not turn on with the Car Costume feature active. The 2025 R1S appears in three of the four.

Warning signs to watch for

  • FSAM-1552: certain exterior lights may not turn on when the Car Costume feature is activated.
  • FSAM-1723: a loss of drive power on 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles.
  • FSAM-1888: a loss of drive power on 2027 R2 vehicles.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For FSAM-1723 on 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles, FSAM-1888 on 2027 R2 vehicles, FSAM-1744 on 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles, and FSAM-1552 on 2025 R1T and R1S vehicles, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. They also do not say anything else about continuing to drive, and they do not give any interim instruction. Each notice states that the defect increases the risk of a crash and directs owners to the listed remedy.

What the free repair involves

For FSAM-1723, Rivian will inspect and repair the HVDB ground strap joint or replace the high voltage battery pack as necessary. For FSAM-1888, Rivian will inspect and repair the connection or replace damaged components as necessary. FSAM-1744 and FSAM-1552 are each addressed by an over-the-air software update Rivian has released. All four remedies are free of charge.

Common questions

Does my Rivian need a service visit, or can it update itself?

It depends on the recall. FSAM-1744 and FSAM-1552 are handled by over-the-air software updates. FSAM-1723 and FSAM-1888 involve inspection and repair or replacement of high voltage components, which the notices describe as work Rivian performs free of charge.

Is the R2 affected by the same problem as the R1S and R1T?

The notices describe two different causes. FSAM-1888 covers 2027 R2 vehicles, where a high voltage battery fastener may have been improperly tightened. FSAM-1723 covers 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles, where a connection inside the High Voltage Distribution Box may be improperly grounded. Both notices describe a loss of drive power.

How do I know if my software is one of the recalled versions?

FSAM-1744 applies to 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles operating a software version prior to 2025.18.30; FSAM-1552 applies to 2025 R1T and R1S vehicles on software version 2024.39.30. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261.
