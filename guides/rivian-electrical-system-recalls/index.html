Rivian Electrical Recalls Involve Drive Power, Highway Assist, and Lights

4 NHTSA recalls · Rivian · Electrical System · model years 2025–2027 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 29, 2026

Loss of drive power repeats in Rivian's electrical notices, but the affected hardware differs. FSAM-1723 covers certain 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles with an improperly grounded connection inside the High Voltage Distribution Box. FSAM-1888 covers certain 2027 R2 vehicles with a potentially improperly tightened high voltage battery fastener. The 2025 R1S and R1T appear most often, also featuring in two software notices. FSAM-1744 concerns Hands-Free Highway Assist failing to correctly identify a lead vehicle on software before 2025.18.30. FSAM-1552 concerns software version 2024.39.30 preventing certain exterior lights from turning on when the Car Costume feature is activated.

Warning signs to watch for

  • FSAM-1723: The vehicle may lose drive power.
  • FSAM-1744: Hands-Free Highway Assist may fail to correctly identify a lead vehicle.
  • FSAM-1888: The vehicle may lose drive power.
  • FSAM-1552: Certain exterior lights may not turn on when Car Costume is activated.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

FSAM-1723, covering the High Voltage Distribution Box ground connection, does not tell owners to stop driving. FSAM-1744, covering Hands-Free Highway Assist lead-vehicle detection, likewise does not tell owners to stop driving. FSAM-1888, concerning the R2 high voltage battery fastener, does not tell owners to stop driving. FSAM-1552, concerning exterior lighting when Car Costume is activated, also does not tell owners to stop driving. None of these notices includes a stop-driving or park-outside instruction.

What the free repair involves

Rivian provides these remedies free of charge. FSAM-1723 specifies inspecting and repairing the HVDB ground strap joint or replacing the high voltage battery pack as necessary. FSAM-1888 specifies inspecting and repairing the connection or replacing damaged components as necessary. FSAM-1744 and FSAM-1552 each provide an over-the-air software update.

Common questions

Which Rivian electrical recalls involve loss of drive power?

FSAM-1723 concerns certain 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles with an improperly grounded HVDB connection. FSAM-1888 concerns certain 2027 R2 vehicles with a potentially improperly tightened high voltage battery fastener. Both notices identify loss of drive power as a crash risk.

Can an over-the-air update address these electrical recalls?

Rivian released free over-the-air updates for FSAM-1744, concerning Hands-Free Highway Assist, and FSAM-1552, concerning Car Costume exterior lighting. The stated remedies for FSAM-1723 and FSAM-1888 involve hardware inspection and repair or replacement as necessary.

What software versions are named in the Rivian electrical notices?

FSAM-1744 covers certain 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles operating software before 2025.18.30. FSAM-1552 identifies version 2024.39.30 as potentially preventing certain exterior lights from turning on when Car Costume is activated. Each notice specifies a free OTA update.
