Why does power steering fail in these Cadillac models?

3 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Steering · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-03-20

Cadillac's three steering recalls all involve a loss of power steering assist, but from two different causes. A 2021 software issue could cut power steering assist at startup or while driving on the Escalade ESV, fixed by an over-the-air update. Separately, the electric power steering assist itself may fail on the Cadillac CTS: a 2019 recall covered 2016-2018 CTS vehicles, and a broader 2025 recall covered 2016-2018 CTS and 2017-2018 ATS vehicles for the same failure. In both CTS/ATS recalls, dealers replace the power steering gear assembly rather than reprogramming software.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering suddenly requires much more effort to turn, especially at low speeds (all three recalls)
  • Power steering assist loss may occur right at startup or while already driving (2021 Escalade ESV)
  • No other warning sign is described for the CTS/ATS electric power steering failures

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. The 2021 Escalade ESV software recall, the 2019 CTS recall, and the 2025 CTS/ATS recall notices all describe a loss of power steering assist and increased crash risk from higher steering effort, but none instructs owners to stop driving. The Escalade ESV recall is resolved through an over-the-air update or a dealer visit; the notices for the two CTS/ATS recalls do not mention an over-the-air option.

What the free repair involves

For the 2021 Escalade ESV, GM updates the power steering module software over-the-air or through a dealer, free of charge. For the CTS and CTS/ATS electric power steering recalls, dealers replace the power steering gear assembly, also free of charge.

Common questions

Which Cadillac steering recall was fixed with a software update instead of new parts?

The 2021 Escalade ESV recall was resolved by updating the power steering module software, either over-the-air or by a dealer, rather than replacing a physical part.

Is the CTS power steering issue a new problem or the same one twice?

It's the same electric power steering assist failure. A 2019 recall covered 2016-2018 CTS vehicles, and a 2025 recall expanded coverage to also include 2017-2018 ATS vehicles for the same defect.

What do dealers replace to fix the CTS power steering recall?

Dealers replace the power steering gear assembly free of charge, according to the recall remedy for both the 2019 and 2025 CTS/ATS electric power steering recalls.
