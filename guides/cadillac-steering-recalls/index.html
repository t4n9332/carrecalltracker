Cadillac power steering recalls: when does the assist stop working?

3 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Steering · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 20, 2025

All three Cadillac steering recalls come down to the same thing: losing power steering assist. Two of them describe the same defect, an electric power steering assist failure — one names 2016-2018 CTS and 2017 ATS, the other 2016-2018 CTS and 2017-2018 ATS, alongside Chevrolet Camaro and Corvette — and in both, dealers replace the power steering gear assembly. The third is different in cause: on the 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV, a software issue may cause a loss of power steering assist at startup or while driving, and that one is fixed with a software update. Every notice describes the same consequence: greater steering effort, especially at lower speeds.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Greater steering effort, especially at lower speeds — named in all three notices.
  • A loss of power steering assist at startup or while driving — 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV software recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV notice says increased steering effort, particularly at lower speeds, increases the risk of a crash. Both CTS and ATS notices say a loss of power steering assist requires greater steering effort, especially at low speeds, increasing crash risk. Beyond describing that risk, none of the three gives owners any instruction about driving before the repair is done.

What the free repair involves

For the 2016-2018 CTS and the ATS, dealers replace the power steering gear assembly. For the 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV, the power steering module software is updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update. All three notices state the work is free of charge.

Common questions

How would I know if my CTS has lost power steering assist?

The notices say a loss of power steering assist requires greater steering effort, especially at lower speeds. They do not describe a warning light or any other sign. Dealers replace the power steering gear assembly free of charge under both CTS recalls.

My 2021 Escalade got a software update — does that fix the steering?

The 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV notice says the power steering module software will be updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 13, 2021. It is the only one of the three that is fixed with software.

Is my 2018 ATS covered, or only the CTS?

The 2025 recall names 2016-2018 Cadillac CTS and 2017-2018 Cadillac ATS, with Chevrolet Camaro and Corvette. The earlier notice names 2016-2018 CTS and 2017 ATS. Both describe an electric power steering assist failure and a replacement of the power steering gear assembly.
