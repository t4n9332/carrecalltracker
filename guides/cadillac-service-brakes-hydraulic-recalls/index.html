Why do Cadillac models keep getting brake recalls?

6 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-09-12

Cadillac's six brake recalls span very different causes. The 2018-2019 XTS was recalled for rear brake caliper pistons with insufficient coating, which can reduce rear brake performance. The 2019 CT6 had a software error that could disable stability control and antilock brakes without illuminating a warning light. The 2018 Escalade could lose vacuum-assisted power brake output over time. More recently, 2023-2024 Lyriq all-wheel-drive vehicles can have the ABS unexpectedly release brake pressure, and 2023-2024 Escalade and Escalade ESV vehicles may not show a warning light when brake fluid is low. A 2022 XT6 recall involves only a missing brake fluid cap warning label.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Reduced rear braking performance or longer stopping distance (2018-2019 XTS)
  • No warning light appears on the instrument cluster even though stability control, antilock brakes, or brake fluid level are affected (2019 CT6; 2023-2024 Escalade and Escalade ESV)
  • Brake pedal may require noticeably more effort and a longer stopping distance over time (2018 Escalade)
  • Brake pressure may release unexpectedly, which could feel like reduced braking (2023-2024 Lyriq)
  • No owner-noticeable symptom described for the 2022 XT6 — its brake fluid cap is only missing a warning label

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. The notices for the XTS caliper coating, CT6 stability-control software, Escalade vacuum pump, Lyriq ABS pressure release, Escalade/Escalade ESV warning-light, and XT6 warning-label recalls all do not tell owners to stop driving. Two of the recalls — the Lyriq brake pressure recall and the Escalade/Escalade ESV warning-light recall — are fixed through an over-the-air software update as an alternative to a dealer visit, but neither notice includes a stop-driving or park-outside instruction.

What the free repair involves

Fixes vary by recall: dealers bleed the brake system (XTS), reprogram the brake control module (CT6, Escalade vacuum pump), or replace the brake fluid cap (XT6). The Lyriq and Escalade/Escalade ESV brake-software recalls are fixed with an over-the-air update or a dealer visit. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Which Cadillac brake recalls can be fixed without a dealer visit?

The 2023-2024 Lyriq brake-pressure recall and the 2023-2024 Escalade/Escalade ESV warning-light recall are both fixed through an over-the-air software update, or by a dealer if the owner prefers.

Will my brake warning light tell me if something is wrong?

Not necessarily. The 2019 CT6 and 2023-2024 Escalade/Escalade ESV recalls both involve the warning light failing to illuminate even when stability control, antilock brakes, or brake fluid level are affected.

Is the XT6 brake recall a real safety defect?

The notice describes a missing warning label on the brake fluid cap, which it says could let the fluid become contaminated by debris and affect braking performance. A dealer replaces the cap free of charge.
