What are the Cadillac brake recalls, from XTS calipers to Lyriq ABS?

6 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 12, 2024

Six brake recalls cover Cadillac, and the electronic brake control module is at the center of four of them. The 2019 CT6 has an EBCM software error that can disable stability control and ABS; the 2018 Escalade's mechanical vacuum pump output can decrease, reducing power brake assist; 2023-2024 all-wheel-drive Lyriq ABS may activate unexpectedly and release brake pressure; and on 2023-2024 Escalade and Escalade ESV the software may fail to show a warning light when brake fluid is lost. The other two are parts: 2018-2019 XTS and XTS Professional rear caliper pistons with insufficient coating, and a 2022 XT6 brake fluid reservoir cap missing its warning label.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Higher brake pedal effort and a longer distance needed to stop — 2018 Escalade brake assist recall.
  • The anti-lock brake system activating unexpectedly and releasing brake pressure — 2023-2024 Lyriq recall.
  • The notice says the ESC and ABS warning lights will not illuminate when the error occurs — 2019 CT6 recall.
  • The notice says a warning light may not be displayed when brake fluid is lost — 2023-2024 Escalade and Escalade ESV recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The notices describe the risk in each case: reduced rear brake performance on the XTS, stability control and ABS disabled without a warning light on the CT6, a release of brake pressure on the Lyriq, increased pedal effort on the 2018 Escalade, no low-fluid warning light on the 2023-2024 Escalade, and possible contamination of brake fluid on the XT6, and direct owners to have the recall work done. None gives an interim driving instruction.

What the free repair involves

Remedies differ by recall. Dealers bleed the brake system on the XTS, reprogram the EBCM on the 2019 CT6 and on the 2018 Escalade, and inspect and replace the brake fluid reservoir cap on the XT6. The Lyriq and the 2023-2024 Escalade software updates are delivered over the air or by a dealer. Every notice states the repair is free of charge.

Common questions

Will a warning light tell me if my Cadillac has one of these brake problems?

Two notices say the opposite. The 2019 CT6 notice says the ESC and ABS warning lights will not illuminate when that software error occurs, and the 2023-2024 Escalade notice says the software may fail to display a warning light when brake fluid is lost.

Can my Lyriq brake recall be fixed without a dealer visit?

The 2023-2024 Lyriq notice says the electronic brake control module software will be updated through an over-the-air update or by a dealer, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 3, 2024. The 2023-2024 Escalade brake light recall is handled the same way.

Why does a missing cap label count as a brake recall?

The 2022 XT6 brake fluid reservoir cap may be missing its warning label, so the vehicle fails FMVSS 135. The notice says brake fluid may be unintentionally contaminated by debris, which can affect brake performance. Dealers inspect the cap and replace it if necessary, free.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
