Why Do So Many Jeep Models Have Lighting Recalls?

10 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Exterior Lighting · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-02-02

Ten lighting recalls span nearly every current Jeep model. The Grand Cherokee accounts for four: two separate 2021 Grand Cherokee L high-beam defects (a switch logic error and an incomplete software update), a 2023-2024 slow high-beam activation issue, and a 2021-2022 tail light side marker that may not illuminate. Wrangler has two: 2022 side marker lights disabled by wiring, and 2023 models where the spare tire blocks the brake light. The 2022 Cherokee has a turn signal self-cancel defect, the 2021-2023 Renegade has incorrect reverse lights, the 2024-2025 Wagoneer S has a headlight aim defect, and a 2026 recall covers a trailer tow module affecting the Wagoneer S and shared with Ram heavy-duty trucks.

Warning signs to watch for

  • High beam headlights that won't activate, or take too long to activate, in auto mode (multiple Grand Cherokee recalls)
  • One or both headlights going completely inoperable (2021 Grand Cherokee L)
  • Tail light, side marker, or brake light segments that don't illuminate (Grand Cherokee, Wrangler)
  • A turn signal that doesn't self-cancel after a turn (2022 Cherokee)
  • Trailer lights and brakes that fail to work when towing (Wagoneer S)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these ten lighting recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe reduced visibility or non-functioning lights — including headlights, tail lights, side markers, turn signals, brake lights, and trailer lighting/brakes — but each one only states the defect and the free repair, without telling owners to stop driving while waiting for service.

What the free repair involves

Most fixes are software reprogramming: dealers reflash the headlight switch logic, the smart lighting module, or the high beam parameters. Physical parts are replaced where hardware is defective — tail light assemblies, side marker wiring harnesses, the spare tire carrier blocking a brake light, the steering column control module, reverse light assemblies, headlight aim covers, and the trailer tow module. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why have there been multiple Jeep Grand Cherokee headlight recalls?

The Grand Cherokee L has had separate 2021 recalls for a master lighting switch logic error and an incomplete software update, plus a 2023-2024 recall for slow high beam activation — three distinct defects with different causes and software fixes.

What's the trailer tow module recall on the Jeep Wagoneer S?

An improperly designed trailer tow module on 2024-2025 Wagoneer S vehicles (also affecting some Ram trucks) can cause trailer lights to fail to illuminate and trailer brakes to fail. Dealers replace the module free of charge.

Does the Jeep Wrangler have more than one lighting recall?

Yes. 2022 Wranglers have a side marker light wiring defect, and separately, 2023 Wranglers have a spare tire that can block the center high-mount brake light — two unrelated issues fixed with different parts.
