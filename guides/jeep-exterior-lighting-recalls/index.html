Which Jeep exterior lighting recalls affect headlights, markers and turn signals?

10 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Exterior Lighting · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-02-02

Ten Jeep recalls cover exterior lighting, and the Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L account for four of them. Two involve high beams that do not activate easily with the master switch in AUTO — 2021 Grand Cherokee L without automatic high beams, and 2023-2024 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L — while another 2021 Grand Cherokee L recall covers headlights made inoperable by an incomplete smart lighting module update. Marker lights repeat too: 2022 Wrangler side markers with an incompatible tail light harness, and damaged tail light side marker assemblies on 2022 Grand Cherokee and 2021-2022 Grand Cherokee L. The rest are 2023 Wrangler brake light obstruction, 2022 Cherokee turn signals, 2021-2023 Renegade reverse lights, Wagoneer S headlight aim, and a trailer tow module.

Warning signs to watch for

  • One or both headlights may become inoperable — the 2021 Grand Cherokee L smart lighting module recall.
  • High beams may not activate with the master lighting switch in AUTO — the 2021 Grand Cherokee L recall and the 2023-2024 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L recall.
  • Side marker lights may not illuminate — the 2022 Wrangler tail light harness recall and the 2022 Grand Cherokee and 2021-2022 Grand Cherokee L tail light recall.
  • Turn signals may not self-cancel — the 2022 Jeep Cherokee recall.
  • Trailer lights may fail to illuminate and trailer brakes may fail — the 2024-2026 Wagoneer S and 2026 Cherokee trailer tow module recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the ten notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Each describes reduced visibility or a signal other drivers cannot see: inoperable headlights and high beams on the Grand Cherokee L, unlit side markers on the Wrangler and Grand Cherokee, a center high-mount brake light blocked by the spare tire on the 2023 Wrangler, and, on the trailer tow module recall, trailer lights that may not illuminate and trailer brakes that may fail. Beyond the repair, the notices give owners no further instruction.

What the free repair involves

Remedies split between software and hardware. Dealers reprogram the 2021 Grand Cherokee L for high beams and for the smart lighting module, and reconfigure high beam parameters on 2023-2024 Grand Cherokee models. Elsewhere they replace tail light assemblies, the spare tire carrier, the steering column control module, bumper reverse light assemblies, and the trailer tow module, and move the plugs on Wagoneer S headlight aim adjusters. All free of charge.

Common questions

Why won't my Grand Cherokee L high beams come on in AUTO?

Two notices describe this. On 2021 Grand Cherokee L without automatic high beams, the high beams will not activate unless the switch is first moved to 'on.' On 2023-2024 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L, the driver may be unable to quickly activate them.

Does the 2023 Wrangler brake light recall mean a new spare tire carrier?

Yes — the notice says the spare tire may obstruct the view of the center high-mount brake light, and that dealers will replace the spare tire carrier assembly free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 28, 2023.

Which vehicles are in the trailer tow module recall?

The notice lists 2024-2026 Jeep Wagoneer S, 2026 Jeep Cherokee, and 2025-2026 Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 pickups plus 3500, 4500 and 5500 cab chassis. Trailer lights may fail to illuminate and trailer brakes may fail; dealers replace the module free of charge.
