Why do Jeep headlights and marker lights keep getting recalled?

10 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Exterior Lighting · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-02-02

Ten recalls, and nearly all are Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108 compliance problems rather than broken parts. The Grand Cherokee family carries four: 2021 Grand Cherokee L high beams that will not activate with the switch in AUTO, 2021 Grand Cherokee L headlights knocked out by an incomplete smart lighting module update, 2021-2022 tail light side marker assemblies that do not illuminate, and 2023-2024 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L high beams the driver cannot flash on quickly. Wrangler appears twice — 2022 side markers with an incompatible tail light wiring harness, 2023 spare tires blocking the center high-mount brake light. The rest scatter: 2022 Cherokee turn signals that will not self-cancel, 2021-2023 Renegade with the wrong bumper reverse light assembly, and Wagoneer S headlight aim and trailer tow module defects.

Warning signs to watch for

  • High beams will not come on unless you move the master lighting switch out of AUTO
  • One or both headlights stop working at night
  • Side marker or tail light markers stay dark, or reverse lights look weak
  • The turn signal keeps flashing after you complete a turn
  • Trailer lights and trailer brakes do not work when a trailer is hooked up

Is it safe to keep driving?

No recall in this group tells you to stop driving or park outside, and in daylight these vehicles behave normally. The trailer tow module recall is the one real restriction: the vehicle itself is fine, but avoid pulling a trailer until the module is replaced, since trailer lights and brakes may not work. For the headlight, high beam, marker and brake light recalls, drive with extra care after dark, leave more room in traffic, and cancel your own turn signal by hand on the 2022 Cherokee.

What the free repair involves

Several of these are software: dealers reprogram the vehicle for the AUTO-position high beams, reprogram the smart lighting module, and reconfigure high beam parameters on 2023-2024 Grand Cherokees. The rest are parts — tail light assemblies, the spare tire carrier, the steering column control module, the bumper reverse light assembly, and the trailer tow module. On Wagoneer S, dealers move the headlight aim plugs. All free.

Common questions

Nothing seems wrong with my lights — why is my Jeep recalled?

Most of these are compliance recalls under Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108. The lighting works, but not the way the standard requires — high beams too slow to activate, a marker lamp dark, or a spare tire hiding the center brake light.

Can I tow while waiting for the trailer tow module replacement?

Better not to. The improperly designed module means trailer lights and trailer brakes may fail. The notice does not restrict driving the Wagoneer S, Cherokee or Ram itself, so drive normally but leave the trailer parked until the module is replaced.

My high beams work if I turn the switch to ON — is that the defect?

That is exactly it on 2021 Grand Cherokee L without automatic high beams: with the master switch in AUTO, the brights will not come on until you first move it to ON. Dealers reprogram the vehicle free of charge.
