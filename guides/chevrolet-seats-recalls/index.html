Chevrolet seat recalls: weak welds and loose seat mounting bolts

7 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Seats · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-09-29

Two faults repeat across these seven recalls. Welds account for four: the driver's seat-back frame on the 2018 Sonic, head-restraint bracket welds in the rear-right seat of the 2019 Equinox, the driver's seat cushion frame power tilt-adjustment mechanism on the 2021 Malibu and 2022 Equinox and Blazer, and a passenger seat hook attachment on the 2022 Colorado with a manual passenger seat. Bolts account for the other three: front seat attachment bolts that may be loose or missing on the 2021 Tahoe and Suburban, rear attachment bolts that may not have been installed on the 2021 Trailblazer, and a stripped or loose bolt at the driver's seat height-adjust pivot on 2022-2023 Equinox. The Equinox appears in three of them.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. They describe what could happen in a crash: a seat-back that may fail in a rear impact on the 2018 Sonic, seats that may move if attachment bolts are loose or missing on the 2021 Tahoe, Suburban and Trailblazer, and welds or bolts that may not restrain an occupant on the Malibu, Equinox, Blazer and Colorado. The notices themselves give owners no instruction to stop driving or to leave a seat empty.

What the free repair involves

Every one of these repairs is free of charge. On the weld recalls, dealers replace the driver's seat-back on the Sonic and the rear-right seatback frame on the Equinox, and inspect the seat and replace the cushion frame if necessary on the Malibu, Equinox, Blazer, XT4 and Colorado. On the bolt recalls, dealers inspect both front seats and install attachment bolts as needed, or replace the seat cushion frame.

Common questions

How do I know if my Equinox is in one of these seat recalls?

Three cover the Equinox: rear-right head-restraint bracket welds on the 2019, a driver's seat cushion frame weld on the 2022, and a stripped or loose driver's seat bolt on 2022-2023. Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 can confirm by VIN.

Do the notices say anything I should check myself before the repair?

The notices do not describe anything for owners to check. They state the defect, the crash risk and the dealer repair, and none asks owners to inspect the seat, avoid a seating position, or stop driving before the free repair.

Will the dealer always replace the whole seat?

It depends on the recall. Some notices say a part is replaced outright, such as the Sonic driver's seat-back and the Equinox rear-right seatback frame. Others say dealers inspect first and replace the cushion frame or install attachment bolts only if necessary.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
