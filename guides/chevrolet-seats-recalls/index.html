Why do Chevrolet seats keep failing from loose bolts or bad welds?

7 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Seats · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-09-29

Seat recalls in this group split into two repeated problems: a seat frame with an improperly formed or missing weld - on the Sonic's driver seat-back, the Malibu, Equinox, Blazer, and XT4 driver's seat, the Colorado and Canyon front passenger seat, and the Equinox/Terrain rear-right seat - and a seat with loose or missing attachment bolts, on the Suburban, Tahoe, and Yukon front seats, the Equinox and Terrain driver's seat, and the Encore GX and Trailblazer front seats. Each recall affects one specific seat position in one set of models and model years, not the seat structure across the whole Chevrolet lineup.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices don't describe any noticeable sign of a weak seat weld or loose bolt before a crash - dealers find these through inspection, not owners through driving

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The concern in every one of them is what happens in a crash - a seat weld could fail or a loosely bolted seat could shift on impact, increasing the risk of injury to whoever is sitting there - not a problem GM says affects normal driving. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving while waiting for the repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the affected seat and replace the seat-back or seat cushion frame if the weld is defective, or inspect the seat attachment points and install or tighten the missing bolts - all free of charge. Which repair applies depends on whether the recall involves a weld or a bolt issue.

Common questions

Will I notice if my Chevrolet has a defective seat weld or bolt?

The recall notices don't describe any sign an owner could notice while driving. These are manufacturing defects that a dealer identifies by inspecting the seat frame and attachment bolts, not something that shows up in everyday use.

Which seat is affected in my Chevrolet's recall?

It depends on the recall - the driver's seat (Sonic, Malibu, Equinox, Blazer, XT4), the front passenger seat (Colorado, Canyon), the rear-right seat (2019 Equinox, Terrain), or both front seats (Suburban, Tahoe, Yukon, Encore GX, Trailblazer).

Is the seat recall repair free?

Yes. GM says dealers will inspect the seat and replace the frame, or install and tighten the attachment bolts, as needed, at no cost to the owner. Owners are notified by mail once the recall repair is ready.
