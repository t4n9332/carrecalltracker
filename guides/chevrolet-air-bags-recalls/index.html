Why Do So Many Chevrolet Models Have Air Bag Recalls?

19 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-21

A dozen GM recalls between 2018 and 2022 cover air bag defects on Chevrolet vehicles, with the Traverse and Silverado 1500 appearing most often. Traverse recalls span side curtain air bags that weren't properly attached (2018-2019), wrong air bag calibration software installed during service (2021), and an incompatible roof-rail air bag harness connector (2020-2022), each shared with the Buick Enclave. Silverado 1500 recalls include a passenger air bag module damaged during assembly (2019, shared with GMC Sierra), a driver air bag missing securing tabs (2021), and a sensing module left in manufacturing mode (2022). Other one-off issues hit the Corvette, Malibu, Camaro, Spark, Trax, and Bolt EV/EUV.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices don't describe a dashboard warning light or other sign owners could notice beforehand; these defects only affect how the air bags perform if a crash occurs.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these twelve recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Across the Traverse, Silverado, Corvette, Malibu, Camaro, Spark, Trax, Encore, and Bolt EV/EUV recalls, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. GM describes the shared risk as air bags that may not deploy, may deploy improperly, or may send plastic emblem pieces as projectiles in a crash, all of which increase the risk of injury to occupants if a crash happens.

What the free repair involves

Dealers reprogram sensing module or calibration software on affected vehicles, inspect and replace loose fasteners or damaged air bag modules, and replace side curtain air bag weld nuts or roof-rail harness connectors as needed. All repairs, including software reprogramming, module replacement, and hardware fixes, are done free of charge.

Common questions

Why do Chevrolet Traverse air bag recalls keep happening?

The Traverse has had separate air bag recalls for side curtain air bags that weren't properly attached, incorrect calibration software installed during dealer service, and a roof-rail air bag harness connector with incompatible terminals — three different causes, not one repeated defect.

Will my Chevrolet Silverado's air bags deploy in a crash?

It depends on the recall. Some 2019 and 2021 Silverado 1500s had a damaged or improperly secured passenger or driver air bag module, and some 2022 trucks left the factory with the sensing module still in manufacturing mode, which would prevent deployment until repaired.

What's the Chevrolet Camaro air bag emblem recall about?

On 2018-2021 Camaros, the plastic 'Camaro' emblem on the steering wheel air bag cover may separate when the driver air bag deploys, and pieces of it could act as projectiles, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
