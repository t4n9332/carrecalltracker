What Chevrolet air bag recalls exist and which models are affected?

19 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 21, 2026

Chevrolet's air bag recalls cluster around a few repeating themes. Traverse appears more than any other model: side curtain air bags that may not be properly attached on 2010-2019 models, incorrect air bag calibration software received during dealer service on 2021 models, and roof rail air bag harness connectors with incompatible terminals on 2020-2022 models. Silverado 1500 recurs too — a passenger frontal air bag module damaged during assembly on 2019 Crew Cabs, missing metal tabs on the 2021 driver air bag cover, and a sensing diagnostic module left in manufacturing mode on 2022 trucks, the same manufacturing-mode problem cited for 2019 Spark, Trax and Traverse. Others are model-specific: the Corvette ZR1 module fault, the Camaro emblem, the Malibu passenger presence system and Bolt EV/EUV fasteners.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the notices in this group carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. They describe what can go wrong in a crash — air bags that may not deploy on 2019 Spark, Trax and Traverse and 2022 Silverado 1500 vehicles with a sensing diagnostic module in manufacturing mode, roof rail air bags that may not deploy on 2020-2022 Traverse models, and degraded roof-rail performance on 2020 Blazer, Silverado and Traverse models — but the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. They direct owners to the dealer repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers reprogram the sensing diagnostic module on the 2019 Corvette ZR1, Spark, Trax and Traverse, and update air bag calibration software on 2021 Traverse models. Other recalls replace hardware: driver or passenger air bag modules, roof-rail modules, harness connectors, the Malibu passenger presence seat kit, or air bag fasteners; the 2010-2019 Traverse remedy inspects weld nuts. Those notices state the repair is free of charge.

Common questions

Which Chevrolet models appear in more than one air bag recall?

Traverse appears in three: side curtain attachment on 2010-2019 models, calibration software on 2021 models, and roof rail harness connectors on 2020-2022 models. Silverado 1500 appears in the 2019 passenger module, 2021 driver air bag tab, and 2022 sensing diagnostic module recalls.

Is there any way to tell if my air bags will deploy?

The notices do not describe anything an owner would see or hear. They describe conditions found in the vehicles themselves, such as a module left in manufacturing mode or an air bag that is not properly attached. A dealer or VIN check confirms whether your vehicle is included.

What is wrong with the Camaro steering wheel emblem?

The notice covers 2017-2021 Camaro vehicles and states the plastic Camaro emblem on the steering wheel air bag cover may separate during deployment of the frontal driver air bag, and plastic pieces acting as projectiles can increase injury risk. Dealers replace the driver air bag module free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
