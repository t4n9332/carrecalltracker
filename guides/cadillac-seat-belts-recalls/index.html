What do Cadillac's seat belt recalls say about buckles and anchors?

7 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 22, 2023

Across these seven Cadillac recalls the repeating theme is the hardware that holds a belt to the car. Buckle rivets appear twice: a missing rivet on the 2019 XTS front buckles, and an improperly formed third-row rivet on 2021-2022 Escalade and Escalade ESV. Fastening comes up again with incorrect seat belt bolts on the 2021 Escalade ESV and loose or missing rear retractor fasteners on 2019-2021 XT4. Third-row belts on the 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV may have been entrapped or misrouted behind the seat-folding mechanism. The 2023-2024 Lyriq has an improperly welded rear anchor bracket, and 2018-2019 CT6 and Escalade ESV rear retractors may not lock when the belt is fully pulled out. Escalade ESV appears in four of the seven.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. One notice does give an interim instruction: for the 2023-2024 Lyriq rear anchor bracket recall, owners are advised not to use the left-rear and center-rear seating positions until the recall repair is completed. The other six notices describe the injury risk in a crash and direct owners to have the free repair completed; they do not give owners any instruction about driving in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect in most of these recalls: XTS front buckle assemblies and Escalade third-row buckle rivets, replacing assemblies as needed; 2021 Escalade third-row belts, which are replaced if damaged and rerouted; XT4 rear retractors, which are tightened as necessary; and CT6 retractors, replaced if needed. Suspect bolts are replaced, and the Lyriq's rear seat cushion frame is replaced. Each notice states the repair is free of charge.

Common questions

My Cadillac is in a third-row seat belt recall — can my family still sit back there?

The 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV belt-routing notice and the 2021-2022 third-row buckle rivet notice do not tell owners to avoid the third row. They say a damaged belt may break in a crash and a deformed rivet may not properly restrain an occupant.

Can I use the back seat of my Lyriq before the recall repair?

For the 2023-2024 Lyriq rear anchor bracket recall, the notice advises owners not to use the left-rear and center-rear seating positions until the repair is completed. Dealers replace the rear seat cushion frame free of charge; owner notification letters were mailed January 17, 2024.

Will I be able to tell if my seat belt is affected?

None of these seven notices describes a warning light, sound or other sign an owner would notice. They describe missing or deformed rivets, incorrect bolts, loose or missing fasteners, an improper weld, misrouted belts and retractors that may not lock. Each recall is checked at a dealer.
