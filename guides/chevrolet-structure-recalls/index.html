Chevrolet body and structure recalls: hoods, doors, frames and seat belts

6 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Structure · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 21, 2023

Chevrolet's structure recalls cluster around parts that hold the body together, plus one repeated problem on the Bolt EV. Two separate recalls, in December 2022 and December 2023, cover 2017-2023 Bolt EV seat belt pretensioners whose exhaust may ignite carpet fibers near the B-pillar after a crash; the later one also includes vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall 22V-930. The rest are one-off manufacturing faults: a cracked right-hand frame rail on the 2020 Traverse and Buick Enclave, hood-latch striker wires on 2020 Silverado 2500 and 3500 replacement hoods, an improperly welded front impact bar on 2022-2023 Malibu, and door strikers that may fracture on the 2024 Blazer, Blazer EV and Equinox.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The hood may open unexpectedly while driving — the 2020 Silverado 2500 and 3500 hood latch striker wire recall.
  • A door may open while the vehicle is being driven — the 2024 Blazer, Blazer EV and Equinox door striker recall.
  • A fire after a crash in which the seat belt pretensioners deploy — the 2017-2023 Bolt EV pretensioner recalls.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The Bolt EV notices describe a fire only after a crash in which the seat belt pretensioners deploy. The 2020 Traverse notice says a crack in the frame rail may reduce crash performance. The Silverado 2500 and 3500 notice says the hood may open unexpectedly while driving, and the 2024 Blazer, Blazer EV and Equinox notice says a door opening while driving increases the risk of injury or a crash. No interim instruction is given.

What the free repair involves

For the Bolt EV recalls, dealers install metal foil near the pretensioner exhaust and a cover; the 2023 recall also inspects both front pretensioners. Dealers replace the hood assembly on Silverado 2500 and 3500 trucks, and all four door strikers on the Blazer and Equinox. The 2020 Traverse is inspected and replaced if needed. Those repairs are free. On the Malibu, an incomplete weld means GM repurchases the vehicle.

Common questions

Why is the Bolt EV in two seat belt recalls?

Both notices describe the same defect: after a crash with seat belt pretensioner deployment, the pretensioner exhaust may ignite carpet fibers near the B-pillar. The December 2023 recall also includes certain vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under NHTSA recall number 22V-930.

What happens if my Malibu has the incomplete weld?

The notice says dealers inspect the right-hand and left-hand side of the motor rail for an incomplete weld, and if the condition is found, the vehicle will be repurchased by GM. It covers 2022-2023 Malibu vehicles, with letters mailed January 20, 2023.

Do any of these structure recalls tell owners to stop driving?

None of these six notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. They state the risks — a hood or door opening unexpectedly while driving, reduced crash performance, front crash sensors not performing correctly, a fire after a crash — and give no further instruction.
