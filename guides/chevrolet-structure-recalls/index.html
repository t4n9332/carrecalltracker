Why are Chevrolet frames, hoods, and seat belts being recalled?

6 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Structure · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-12-21

Chevrolet's structural recalls cover several distinct issues rather than one repeating defect. The Bolt EV has been recalled twice, in 2022 and again in 2023, because seat belt pretensioners can ignite carpet fibers near the B-pillar after a crash, with the second recall targeting 2019-2023 Bolt EVs that were repaired incorrectly the first time. Separately, 2020 Buick Enclave and Chevrolet Traverse SUVs can have a cracked frame rail, and 2020 Silverado and Sierra 2500/3500 trucks can have replacement hoods with hood-latch striker wires that fracture. The 2022-2023 Malibu has a front frame welding defect affecting crash sensors, and 2024 Blazer, Blazer EV, Equinox, and GMC Terrain models can have door strikers that fracture.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A fire near the B-pillar after a crash involving seat belt pretensioner deployment (Bolt EV, both recalls)
  • A cracked frame rail in the engine compartment (2020 Buick Enclave, Chevrolet Traverse)
  • The hood opening unexpectedly while driving if a striker wire fractures (2020 Silverado and Sierra 2500/3500 with replacement hoods)
  • A door opening unexpectedly while driving if a striker fractures (2024 Blazer, Blazer EV, Equinox, GMC Terrain)
  • The notice for the Malibu front frame weld defect describes no driver-noticeable warning sign

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six structural recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This includes both Bolt EV seat belt pretensioner fire recalls, the Buick Enclave and Chevrolet Traverse frame rail crack, the Silverado and Sierra 2500/3500 hood latch recall, the Malibu front frame weld defect, and the Blazer, Blazer EV, Equinox, and GMC Terrain door striker recall. The notices direct owners to have dealers inspect and repair the vehicles but do not instruct owners to stop driving in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, dealers inspect the affected component - seat belt pretensioners, frame rails, hood latch striker wires, door strikers, or a frame weld - and repair or replace parts as needed, all free of charge. For the Malibu weld defect, GM will repurchase the vehicle if the incomplete weld is confirmed, rather than repairing it.

Common questions

Why has the Chevrolet Bolt EV been recalled twice for the same fire risk?

GM's first 2022 recall covered Bolt EVs whose seat belt pretensioners could ignite carpet after a crash. A second 2023 recall was issued because some 2019-2023 Bolt EVs were repaired incorrectly under that first recall and needed the fix redone.

What happens if my Malibu has the front frame welding defect?

Dealers inspect the front motor rail welds on 2022-2023 Malibu vehicles. If an incomplete weld is confirmed, GM will repurchase the vehicle rather than repair it, at no cost to the owner.

Do I need to stop driving my Chevrolet under one of these structural recalls?

No. None of these six recalls - covering the Bolt EV, Traverse, Enclave, Silverado, Sierra, Malibu, Blazer, Equinox, or Terrain - include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction in the notices.
