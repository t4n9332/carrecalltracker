Volkswagen Rearview Camera Recalls Cover Blank, Delayed, and Distorted Images

12 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Back Over Prevention · model years 2021–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 22, 2026

Missing or delayed rearview images recur throughout Volkswagen's notices, with the ID.4 appearing repeatedly across 2021–2023 model years. Its problems include displays that fail to boot or reset, delayed camera activation, and blank images when reverse or the park function is selected. Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport notices repeatedly cover camera problems, including image distortion for 2024–2025 vehicles and display disruption from a low-voltage electrical event for 2024–2026 vehicles. Golf R and Golf GTI also appear in camera recalls, while Tiguan notices identify camera control unit startup errors. Most listed remedies involve software, but specific notices require camera hardware replacement.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall 919A: Center and instrument panel displays may fail to boot or sporadically reset, removing speedometer information or the rearview image.
  • Recall 91BB/91BC: The rearview camera may show a black screen or the infotainment system may freeze.
  • November 2, 2022 recall: The rearview image may be blank or unresponsive when reverse or the park function is selected.
  • Recall 91SB: The rearview camera image may become distorted in reverse.
  • Recall 91US: The rearview image may be delayed or deactivated after shifting into reverse.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The supplied Volkswagen rearview camera notices do not tell owners to stop driving. None includes a park-outside instruction. That absence applies to the ID.4 display and camera notices, the Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport camera notices, and the listed Golf, Jetta, Taos, Tiguan, and Arteon camera notices. The notices do not provide a stop-driving direction for a delayed image, a blank or frozen display, image distortion, or loss of speedometer information. They also do not provide an interim driving workaround.

What the free repair involves

Dealers provide the listed remedies free of charge. Recall 919A updates vehicle software; 91DV and 91US update infotainment software; 91SB and 91NF update camera control unit software. The November 2022 recall replaces the rearview camera, while 91B3 replaces the peripheral camera control module. The notices describe dealer remedies rather than over-the-air updates.

Common questions

Why does my Volkswagen ID.4 appear in several camera recalls?

The notices describe different problems: 919A concerns displays that fail to boot or reset; 91EG concerns delayed or deactivated images; 93S7 identifies incorrect software in a replacement infotainment control unit. Recall 91B3 addresses a missing image with module replacement.

Which Atlas recalls involve distorted images versus missing images?

Recall 91SB covers distorted rearview images in certain 2024–2025 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles. Recall 91TV covers certain 2024–2026 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles whose low-voltage electrical event can affect displays and prevent the rearview image from appearing.

Does an earlier Tiguan camera repair cover the 2026 notice?

Recall 91NF specifically covers certain 2024 Tiguan vehicles previously repaired under recall 25V082. Its notice describes a camera control unit startup error and calls for a free camera control unit software update. It does not tell owners to stop driving.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
