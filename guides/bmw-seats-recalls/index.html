BMW Seat Recalls Involve Loose Bolts, Welds, and Head Restraints

9 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Seats · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 6, 2023

X5 and X7 models recur across BMW seating recalls, with several distinct attachment and head restraint defects. The 2019 X5 notice concerns driver seat or seat belt attachment bolts, while the 2023 X5 backrest notice concerns one backrest-to-frame bolt. Separate X5 head restraint notices cover incorrect second-row restraints in 2021 vehicles and missing guide-sleeve locking tabs in 2023 vehicles. X7 notices address 2019 business lounge seat attachment bolts and 2019-2022 third-row backrest springs. Weld defects recur separately in 2018-2019 X3 and 2019 X4 front seatbacks, selected 2019-2021 3 Series and 2020-2021 X3/2021 X4 seat frames, and 2020 M340i rear center headrests.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2019-07-31 BMW X3 and X4 Front Seatback Weld Failure — the front seatback may unexpectedly fold forward or rearward.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the supplied BMW seating notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. The 2019 X5 attachment bolt and X7 lounge seat notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Neither do the seat frame weld, X3/X4 front seatback weld, or M340i rear headrest notices. The 2021 X5/X7 head restraint, 2019-2022 X7 third-row spring, 2023 X5 driver backrest bolt, and 2023 X5 head restraint guide-sleeve notices also do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Free repairs follow each defect. Dealers check and torque 2019 X5 attachment bolts, replace 2019 X7 seat adapter bolts, and replace frames and backrests under the March 2023 weld recall. The X3/X4 seatback recall replaces front seats. Other specified free remedies address headrests, guide sleeves, X7 third-row springs, and the 2023 X5 backrest attachment bolt.

Common questions

What separates the two X5 seat bolt recalls?

The February 2019 notice concerns driver seat or seat belt attachment bolts in certain 2019 X5 vehicles, with inspection and torque correction as necessary. The December 2022 notice concerns a backrest-to-seat-frame bolt in certain 2023 X5 vehicles, with inspection and bolt installation if necessary.

Which X7 seating options are named in these recalls?

The February 2019 notice specifies certain 2019 X7 vehicles with the business lounge seat option and calls for seat adapter bolt replacement. The September 2022 notice covers certain 2019-2022 X7 vehicles with third-row seats and specifies spring inspection and correction.

Do the X5 head restraint recalls have the same remedy?

The March 2021 notice specifies inspection and replacement of incorrect second-row outer head restraints as necessary. The April 2023 notice for certain 2023 X5 vehicles specifies inspection and replacement of second-row outboard head restraint guide sleeves as necessary. Both remedies are free.
