Why do Nissan electrical recalls keep stalling engines and cutting power?

11 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 21, 2026

Nissan's electrical recalls cluster around four areas. Titan pickups appear twice for engine-bay wiring: the 2018-2019 Titan alternator harness, which may be damaged and short, and the 2020 Titan engine wiring harness, which may contain an exposed wire that can short or arc. Key and ignition hardware is another repeat: the 2018 Frontier, Versa and Versa Note mechanical key ignition can move out of the run position, and the jackknife key on 2018-2022 Rogue and Rogue Sport can collapse while driving. Nissan's EVs form the third group, with three separate 2023 Ariya recalls and Leaf quick-charge battery overheating on 2019-2021 cars. The 2025 Kicks adds two module and software faults.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine stall while driving (2018-2019 Titan alternator harness recall and 2020 Titan engine wiring recall)
  • The engine turning off when the ignition switch moves out of the run position to accessory (2018 Frontier, Versa and Versa Note ignition switch recall)
  • The key collapsing into a folded position while driving (2018-2022 Rogue and Rogue Sport jackknife key recall)
  • Unexpected loss of drive power (2023 Ariya traction motor and inverter software recalls)
  • The Shift-to-Park warning not activating when the vehicle is shut off in gear, and a partially or fully blank instrument screen (2025 Kicks recalls)

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these eleven notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park the vehicle outside. For the 2021 Leaf quick-charging battery recall, the notice advises owners not to use Level 3 quick charging until the remedy is completed. The other notices give no interim instruction of any kind. They state the risk instead: an engine stall, a loss of drive power, a rollaway, or a fire, and they direct owners to a dealer for the repair listed for that recall.

What the free repair involves

The remedies differ by recall. Dealers inspect the alternator harness and clip or replace it (2018-2019 Titan), inspect and if necessary replace the engine harness (2020 Titan), replace the front traction motor assembly or reprogram and update inverter software (2023 Ariya), update the Leaf battery software, insert a spacer into the jackknife key slot, reconfigure the body control module, and reprogram the Kicks combination meter software. All free of charge.

Common questions

Can I still use a DC fast charger on my Leaf?

The 2021 Leaf notice advises owners not to use Level 3 quick charging until the remedy is completed. The 2019-2020 Leaf notice does not give that instruction; it says dealers will update the battery software free of charge.

Why is my Ariya listed in more than one recall?

Three separate 2023 Ariya recalls appear here: damaged traction motor O-rings, inverter software that detects a short circuit, and inverter software that misdiagnoses an error. Each notice lists its own remedy, so a VIN check can return more than one.

Do the key recalls mean my Nissan can shut off while driving?

The notices say so. The Frontier, Versa and Versa Note notice says the ignition switch may move to accessory and turn off the engine. The Rogue notice says driver contact with a collapsed key could inadvertently shut off the vehicle.
