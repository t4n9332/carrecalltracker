Why Do So Many Nissan Models Keep Losing Power Or Stalling?

11 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-21

These eleven Nissan electrical recalls cluster into several repeating patterns rather than one cause. Titan pickups (2017-2019, then 2020 models) were recalled twice for wiring harness damage that can short and cause a stall or fire. The Ariya EV was recalled three times for loss of drive power — once for damaged motor O-rings, twice for inverter software shutting down the EV system. The Leaf was recalled twice, in different years, because its battery can overheat during Level 3 fast charging. Ignition or key defects caused unexpected engine shutoff in 2017-2018 Frontier, Versa, and Versa Note vehicles, and separately in 2014-2022 Rogue and Rogue Sport jackknife-key models. The 2025-2026 Kicks had two unrelated software recalls.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine stalls or the vehicle loses drive power unexpectedly while driving
  • Vehicle shuts off if a jackknife-style key folds while driving (Rogue, Rogue Sport)
  • Instrument cluster or center display screen going partially or fully blank (Kicks)
  • "Shift-to-Park" warning chime failing to sound when the vehicle isn't actually in Park (Kicks)
  • Battery overheating during Level 3 fast charging, per the notice (Leaf)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these eleven recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For one recall, covering 2021-2022 Leaf vehicles with a Level 3 quick-charge port, the notice specifically advises owners not to use Level 3 fast charging until the remedy is completed; a separate 2019-2020 Leaf battery recall does not include that advisory. For the Titan, Ariya, Rogue, Rogue Sport, Frontier, Versa, and Kicks recalls, the notices describe increased stall, power-loss, rollaway, or fire risk but do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Repairs vary by recall: dealers inspect or replace wiring harnesses, update battery or inverter software, replace the front traction motor assembly, replace ignition switches, insert a spacer into jackknife keys, reconfigure the body control module, and update instrument cluster software. Every one of these repairs is performed free of charge.

Common questions

Do I need to stop using fast charging on my Nissan Leaf?

Only if your Leaf is covered by the 2021-2022 recall for Level 3 quick-charge port vehicles — that notice specifically advises against Level 3 charging until repaired. The separate 2019-2020 Leaf battery recall does not include that advisory.

Why has the Nissan Ariya been recalled three times for power loss?

Each recall has a different cause: damaged O-rings in the front traction motor causing an oil leak, inverter software that shuts down the EV system after detecting a short circuit, and separate inverter software that misdiagnoses an error and cuts power.

What's the jackknife key problem on the Rogue?

On 2014-2020 Rogue and 2017-2022 Rogue Sport models with a jackknife-style key, the key can collapse into a folded position while driving, which can shut off the vehicle and, in a crash, may keep the air bags from deploying properly.
