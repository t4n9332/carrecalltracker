Why do so many Lincoln recalls involve windows and wipers?

13 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Visibility · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 31, 2026

These 13 Lincoln recalls fall into three repeating groups. Power windows that fail FMVSS 118 dominate: 2018 Navigator second row window control modules, the 2024 Nautilus and 2024-2025 Nautilus reversal system, two further Nautilus notices covering vehicles repaired incorrectly under earlier recalls, and the 2025 Aviator rear side windows. Wipers come next, with wiper arms that may break on 2020-2021 Navigator and wiper motors that may fail on 2020-2022 Aviator and Corsair. The rest are single issues: visible air bubbles in 2025-2026 Corsair and Aviator windshields, washer fluid contaminated with brake fluid in the 2020 Aviator, a wire harness contacting the A/C compressor pulley on 2020-2021 Aviators, and a moonroof wind deflector on 2021-2022 Navigator.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Visible air bubbles in the windshield glass — the 2025-2026 Corsair and Aviator windshield recall, 25C60.
  • Windows that do not automatically reverse after detecting an object — the Nautilus and Aviator power window recalls 24C24, 24C43, 25C10, 25C33 and 25C36.
  • Windshield wipers that stop working — the 2020-2021 Navigator wiper arm recall and the 2020-2022 Aviator and Corsair wiper motor recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. Two carry interim letters informing owners of the safety risk: the wiper arm recall, mailed May 24, 2022, and the 2020-2022 wiper motor recall, mailed April 2, 2026, with additional letters to follow once the final remedy is available. The remaining notices describe the risk and point owners to a dealer. Nothing in them says more about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

Many window recalls are handled with software: dealers update or recalibrate the driver and passenger door modules, or the body control module on the 2025 Aviator. 2018 Navigator window modules are inspected and replaced by build date code. Elsewhere dealers replace wiper arms or motors, replace windshields, flush the washer system, secure the wire harness, and repair the moonroof deflector. All free of charge.

Common questions

Why has my Nautilus been recalled for the windows more than once?

Four notices cover Nautilus windows: 24C24 and 24C43 on the automatic reversal system, plus 25C10 and 25C36, which cover vehicles that were repaired incorrectly under recalls 24V-597 and 24V953. Each is a separate free visit to the dealer.

What does the Lincoln windshield recall actually involve?

Ford says the windshield glass on certain 2025-2026 Corsair, Aviator and Explorer vehicles may have visible air bubbles that obscure the driver's visibility, failing FMVSS 205. Dealers inspect and replace the windshield as necessary, free of charge, under recall 25C60.

Is the wiper motor repair available yet?

The 2020-2022 Aviator and Corsair notice says interim letters were mailed April 2, 2026 and that additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available, anticipated in October 2026. Dealers then inspect and replace the front wiper motors free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
