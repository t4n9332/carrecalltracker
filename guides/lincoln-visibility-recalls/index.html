Why do so many Lincoln visibility recalls involve windows and wipers?

13 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Visibility · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-31

This group mixes window, wiper, washer, and windshield defects with no single cause. The clearest repeat is the Lincoln Nautilus: power windows that fail to reverse correctly on an obstruction led to four recalls (2024, 2024, 2025, and 2025), two of them issued because an earlier repair did not fix the defect. The 2025 Aviator had a similar window-reversal issue once. The 2018 Expedition and Navigator, separately, had second-row windows that could stop moving and fail to reverse. Wiper arms broke on 2020-2022 Super Duty and F-150 trucks and the Navigator, and a wiper motor failed on 2020-2022 Explorer, Escape, Aviator, and Corsair. The 2025 Explorer and Aviator had windshields with visible air bubbles, and the 2020-2021 Aviator had a wiring defect near the A/C compressor that raises fire risk.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A power window that closes with excessive force or fails to reverse on an obstruction
  • A second-row window that intermittently stops moving
  • A windshield wiper that stops working or has a visibly broken arm
  • Visible air bubbles inside the windshield glass
  • Washer fluid that doesn't clean the windshield effectively

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, so the notices do not tell owners to stop driving while waiting for the repair. Two of them — the wiper arm recall on the 2020-2022 Super Duty, F-150, Expedition, and Navigator, and the wiper motor recall on the 2020-2022 Explorer, Escape, Aviator, and Corsair — went out first as interim letters because remedy parts were not yet ready, but even those notices do not instruct owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace or repair the specific defective part for each recall: window control modules or software updates for the reversal issues, wiper arms or motors, windshield or washer system components, moonroof wind deflector clips, or the wiring harness and A/C belt on the Aviator fire recall. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why does my Lincoln Nautilus keep getting the same window recall?

The Nautilus has had four separate recalls for power windows that fail to reverse properly on an obstruction; two were issued because an earlier repair did not fully fix the problem. Check which recall number applies to your specific vehicle.

Is it safe to drive with a Lincoln wiper or window recall open?

None of the recalls in this group tell owners to stop driving, but that isn't a safety endorsement — it's simply what the notices say. Contact your dealer to schedule the free repair for your specific recall.

What do dealers actually fix for these Lincoln visibility recalls?

It depends on the recall: some replace wiper arms or motors, others update window control software, replace a windshield, or repair a wiring harness. Check your recall notice or VIN to see which repair applies, all done free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
