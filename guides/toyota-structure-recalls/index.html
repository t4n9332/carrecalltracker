Toyota recalls where a cover, grille or bumper may detach

4 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Structure · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 26, 2024

All four recalls in this group involve an exterior piece that may come off the vehicle and land on the road. Two concern accessories: an accessory Billet Grille Overlay on 2015-2018 4Runners, recalled by Southeast Toyota Distributors because the securing U-nut may fracture, and a Toyota genuine accessory tonneau cover on 2022-2023 Tundra and Tundra Hybrid whose attachment joints may come loose. The other two concern body parts — the front lower bumper cover on 2020-2023 Highlander and Highlander Hybrid, which may come loose or detach after minor impact, and the tow hitch cover on 2023-2024 Sequoia Hybrid. Highlander spans the most model years, and every notice frames the result as a road hazard.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2020-2023 Highlander recall says minor impact to the front lower bumper cover during normal operation may leave the cover loose or detached.
  • The 2022-2023 Tundra recall says the tonneau cover attachment joints may come loose.
  • The 4Runner and Sequoia Hybrid notices do not describe a warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Each frames the problem the same way: if the Billet Grille Overlay, the tonneau cover, the front bumper cover or the tow hitch cover detaches while driving, it can become a road hazard and increase the risk of a crash. The notices say nothing about removing the part, securing it, or where to drive in the meantime, and they give no interim instruction of any kind.

What the free repair involves

On the 4Runner, dealers replace the grille overlay's securing U-nuts with upgraded hardware; on the Highlander they repair or replace the upper and lower front bumper covers; on the Sequoia Hybrid they replace the tow hitch cover and modify the rear bumper. Those three notices say the work is free. For the Tundra, dealers install new hardware on the existing bracket and tonneau cover.

Common questions

My 4Runner grille was fitted by the dealer — is it covered?

The recall covers certain 2015-2018 4Runners with an accessory Billet Grille Overlay installed by Southeast Toyota Distributors, or provided by SET and installed by SET dealers. SET's number for the recall is SET17C and its customer service line is 1-866-405-4226.

Does the Tundra tonneau cover recall say the repair is free?

The notice describes owners being asked to return the vehicle so the dealer can install new hardware — bolts, spacers, washers, nuts, rubber washers and cotter pins — on the existing bracket and cover. It does not state that the work is free of charge, unlike the other three notices here.

Is my Highlander affected if the bumper has never been hit?

The notice covers certain 2020-2023 Highlander and Highlander Hybrid vehicles and says minor impact during normal operation may leave the front lower bumper cover loose or detached. It describes no owner check; dealers repair or replace the covers free of charge under 23TB12 and 23TA12.
