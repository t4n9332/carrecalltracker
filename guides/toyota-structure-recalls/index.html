Why Do Toyota Exterior Trim Pieces Keep Detaching While Driving?

4 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Structure · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-08-26

Four Toyota structure recalls all involve exterior accessory or trim pieces that can separate from the vehicle. A 2015-2018 4Runner recall covers an accessory grille overlay whose securing U-nut can fracture. A 2022-2023 Tundra and Tundra Hybrid recall covers a genuine-accessory tonneau cover whose attachment joints can loosen. A 2020-2023 Highlander and Highlander Hybrid recall covers a front bumper cover that can come loose after even a minor impact. A 2023-2024 Sequoia Hybrid recall covers a tow hitch cover that can come loose from the rear bumper. In every case the concern is the same: a detached piece becoming a hazard for other vehicles on the road.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Grille overlay accessory that comes loose or falls off (4Runner)
  • Tonneau cover that comes loose and separates from the bed (Tundra, Tundra Hybrid)
  • Front bumper cover that comes loose after even a minor impact (Highlander, Highlander Hybrid)
  • Tow hitch cover that comes loose from the rear bumper (Sequoia Hybrid)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four Toyota structure recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This covers the 4Runner grille overlay recall, the Tundra and Tundra Hybrid tonneau cover recall, the Highlander and Highlander Hybrid front bumper cover recall, and the Sequoia Hybrid tow hitch cover recall. For all four, the notices tell owners only to have the affected part inspected, repaired, or replaced, not to stop driving. Owners are told to schedule a dealer visit, not to alter how or where they park.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the U-nuts securing the 4Runner's grille overlay, install new hardware on the Tundra's tonneau cover, repair or replace the Highlander's front bumper cover, and replace the Sequoia Hybrid's tow hitch cover while modifying the rear bumper. All of these repairs are done free of charge.

Common questions

Are these Toyota recalls about the vehicle itself or an accessory?

Three of the four involve accessory or add-on parts: the 4Runner grille overlay, the Tundra tonneau cover, and the Sequoia Hybrid tow hitch cover. The Highlander recall covers the standard front bumper cover.

What is the actual danger if one of these parts detaches?

The notices say a detached grille overlay, tonneau cover, bumper cover, or tow hitch cover can become a hazard in the road for other vehicles, increasing the risk of a crash.

Do I need to stop driving until the part is fixed?

No. None of these four recalls tells owners to stop driving; the notices instruct owners to schedule a free dealer repair.
