Chevrolet Equipment Recalls Cover Heater Cords, Jacks, and Missing Manuals

6 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Equipment · model years 2018–2027 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 26, 2026

Owner's manual problems recur in this Chevrolet equipment group: certain 2024 Malibu vehicles may contain the wrong manual, certain 2026 Silverado 1500, 2500, and 3500 vehicles may lack one, and a broader notice addresses electronic manual availability across multiple Chevrolet models. Silverado also appears in a separate hardware recall covering certain 2017-2019 Silverado 2500 and 3500 trucks with the Duramax 6.6-liter diesel and optional engine-block heater cord. That notice concerns electrical shorts in heater cables or connecting terminals. Other equipment risks involve emergency jacks on certain 2021-2022 Trailblazers and improperly heat-treated suspension and steering lock nuts on certain 2021-2022 Chevrolet 4500HD, 5500HD, and 6500HD trucks.

Warning signs to watch for

  • June 10, 2021 Trailblazer emergency-jack recall: the jack can fracture during use if not positioned as directed in the owner's manual.
  • November 23, 2022 medium-duty truck lock-nut recall: broken nuts may lead to steering instability.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the listed Chevrolet equipment notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the heater-cord, emergency-jack, lock-nut, and owner's-manual recalls, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. They describe different risks: fire from an electrical short, vehicle collapse if a jack fractures, steering instability from broken lock nuts, and improper vehicle operation when manual information is wrong or unavailable. The absence of a stop-driving instruction does not provide a stated assurance about driving an affected vehicle before its recall remedy.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace Trailblazer jacks and medium-duty truck lock nuts as necessary, free of charge. Manual recalls provide the correct or missing manual, or reset the radio to facilitate automatic electronic-manual download, free of charge. The heater notice specifies heater and cord replacement, with free replacements offered under a separate customer satisfaction program for vehicles whose feature was disabled.

Common questions

Why is the Trailblazer emergency jack being replaced?

The June 10, 2021 notice covers certain 2021-2022 Chevrolet Trailblazer vehicles. It says the emergency jack can fracture during use if not positioned as directed in the owner's manual, allowing the vehicle to collapse. Dealers replace the jack free of charge.

How do dealers fix a missing electronic owner's manual?

The February 26, 2026 notice specifies resetting the vehicle radio to facilitate automatic download of the electronic owner's manual, free of charge. This differs from the Malibu wrong-manual and Silverado missing-manual notices, which specify providing an owner's manual.

Which Chevrolet trucks have the steering lock-nut recall?

The November 23, 2022 notice covers certain 2021-2022 Chevrolet 4500HD, 5500HD, and 6500HD vehicles. Improperly heat-treated hex flange lock nuts in suspension and steering joints may break, leading to steering instability. Dealers replace the nuts as necessary, free of charge.
