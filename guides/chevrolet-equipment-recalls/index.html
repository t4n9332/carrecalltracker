What equipment recalls have affected Chevrolet trucks and cars?

6 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Equipment · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-02-26

General Motors has issued several equipment recalls touching Chevrolet vehicles built alongside their GMC, Buick, and Cadillac siblings. The 2018-2019 Chevrolet Silverado 2500 and 3500 diesel trucks share a recall for an engine block heater cord that can short-circuit. The 2021-2022 Chevrolet Trailblazer and 2020-2022 Buick Encore GX share a recall for an emergency jack that can fracture during use. The 2021 Chevrolet 4500HD has hex flange lock nuts in its steering and suspension that may break. Separately, GM has issued paperwork recalls for missing or incorrect owner's manuals, covering the 2024 Chevrolet Malibu, the 2026 Chevrolet Silverado, and a wider batch of 2025-2026 GM vehicles missing an electronic manual download.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No owner's manual, or the wrong one, in the glove box
  • The electronic owner's manual missing from the vehicle's infotainment system
  • An emergency jack that cracks or breaks when used to lift the vehicle
  • Steering that feels loose or unstable, which the notice links to broken lock nuts
  • No warning signs are described for the block heater cord fire risk

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe several distinct risks: an electrical short in the diesel truck's block heater cord that can lead to a fire, an emergency jack that can fracture and let the vehicle collapse onto the person using it, hex flange lock nuts in the 4500HD's steering and suspension that may break, and owner's manuals that are missing, incorrect, or not downloaded electronically. In every case, the notice does not instruct owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the block heater and cord, replace the emergency jack, and replace the hex flange lock nuts as needed. For the paperwork recalls, dealers provide the correct printed owner's manual or reset the vehicle's radio so the electronic manual downloads automatically. All repairs, replacement parts, and manuals are provided free of charge.

Common questions

Why does a Chevrolet recall also list GMC or Buick models?

GM builds these vehicles on shared platforms and components, so a defect in a part like a block heater cord, jack, or lock nut can affect the Chevrolet, GMC, Buick, or Cadillac versions built with that same part.

Do I need to do anything for a missing owner's manual recall?

Yes. Contact your dealer or GM customer service to receive the correct printed manual, or in the case of the electronic manual recall, have the dealer reset your radio so it downloads automatically, at no cost.

Is the 4500HD steering recall linked to the jack or block heater recalls?

No. Each of these recalls addresses a separate part and a separate defect: the 4500HD's lock nuts, the Encore GX and Trailblazer's jack, and the diesel truck's block heater cord are unrelated components.
