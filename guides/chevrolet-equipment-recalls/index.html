Chevrolet equipment recalls: block heaters, jacks and missing owner's manuals

6 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Equipment · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-02-26

This group mixes hardware faults with paperwork. Three recalls concern documentation: 2024 Cadillac XT4 and Chevrolet Malibu vehicles that received the wrong owner's manual, 2026 Chevrolet Silverado 1500, 2500 and 3500 trucks that did not receive one in production, and a wide 2026 recall where the radio was not set to download the electronic manual, covering Chevrolet Colorado, Equinox EV, Silverado and many other GM models. Two of them are cited as failures to comply with the child restraint anchorage standard. The hardware recalls are narrower: an engine-block heater cord short-circuit on 2017-2019 Silverado 2500 and 3500 Duramax diesels, a jack that can fracture on 2020-2022 Buick Encore GX and 2021-2022 Chevrolet Trailblazer, and improperly heat-treated lock nuts on 2021-2022 Chevrolet 4500HD, 5500HD and 6500HD trucks.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Broken hex flange lock nuts may cause a loss of tension, leading to steering instability — 2021-2022 Chevrolet 4500HD, 5500HD and 6500HD recall.
  • A fractured jack can cause the vehicle to collapse while it is being used — 2020-2022 Buick Encore GX and 2021-2022 Chevrolet Trailblazer recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park outside. The block heater notice says an electrical short-circuit can increase the risk of a fire. The jack notice says a fractured jack can cause the vehicle to collapse, increasing the risk of injury. The lock nut notice says broken nuts can lead to steering instability and a crash risk. The three owner's manual notices say that without the manual, owners may not know how to safely use and operate the vehicle, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.

What the free repair involves

Every repair here is free. Dealers replace the engine block heaters and heater cords on the Duramax trucks, replace the emergency jack on Encore GX and Trailblazer, and replace the hex flange lock nuts as necessary on the 4500HD-series trucks. For the manual recalls, dealers provide the correct or missing owner's manual, or reset the radio so the electronic manual downloads.

Common questions

Why is a missing owner's manual treated as a safety recall?

Two of these notices say the vehicles fail Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 225, child restraint anchorage systems. All three say that without a manual to consult, owners may not know how to safely use and operate the vehicle, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.

Can I still use the jack in my Chevrolet Trailblazer?

The notice says the emergency jack can fracture while in use if it is not positioned as directed in the owner's manual, and that a fractured jack can cause the vehicle to collapse. Dealers replace the jack free of charge.

Which trucks are covered by the engine block heater recall?

The notice covers 2017-2019 Chevrolet Silverado 2500 and 3500 and GMC Sierra 2500 and 3500 trucks with the 6.6-liter Duramax diesel engine and the optional engine-block heater cord. Dealers replace the block heaters and heater cords free of charge.
