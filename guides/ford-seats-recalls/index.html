Why do so many Ford van and SUV seats have recalls?

21 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Seats · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-21

This group is dominated by one repeated defect: Freedman GO-ES foldaway seats installed in Ford Transit vans (and, in one case, E-350 and Ram Promaster vehicles) that don't stay locked in the upright position under load. Five separate companies — Quality Van, Waldoch, Royale/Cabot Coach, MobilityTrans, and National Van Builders — recalled 2015-2018 Transit-based conversions for this same seat cushion problem. Separately, Ford's own seat recalls involve moving parts: 2018-2020 F-150, Explorer, Expedition, Escape, and Aviator seats missing a recliner pawl; 2020-2025 Explorer and Aviator second-row seats that can unlatch, fold, or slide, a problem Ford expanded again in a 2026 recall; and a 2021-2023 Bronco front seat pivot bolt that can loosen.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A foldaway van seat back that doesn't stay locked upright under load (Transit conversions)
  • A second-row seat that unlatches, folds, or slides unexpectedly while driving (Explorer, Aviator)
  • A front seat that feels loose at the height-adjust pivot (Bronco)
  • A loose seat back on the driver or passenger seat (F-150, Explorer, Expedition, Escape, Aviator)
  • The notices describe no advance warning for the seat track and recliner defects — they mainly show up in a crash

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the seat recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. That includes the five Freedman foldaway-seat recalls affecting Transit-based conversions from Quality Van, Waldoch, Royale, MobilityTrans, and National Van Builders, the F-150, Explorer, Expedition, Escape, and Aviator recliner recall, the Ranger child-seat recall, the 2025 and 2026 Explorer and Aviator second-row seat recalls, the Bronco pivot bolt recall, and the two 2018 Expedition seat-track recalls. For all of these, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Fixes vary by defect: van upfitters inspect and repair the Freedman foldaway seat mechanism; Ford dealers inspect and replace recliner seat frames, second-row seat switch bezels, front seat pivot bolts, and center seat track brackets as needed; and Ranger dealers adjust child-seat anchor alignment and swap in removable head restraints. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why do so many Ford Transit van seats have the same recall?

Several different companies converted Transit vans with the same Freedman GO-ES foldaway seat, which can fail to stay locked upright under load such as a crash or sudden stop; each converter issued its own recall to inspect and repair the seat.

Can my Explorer or Aviator second-row seat move while I'm driving?

Ford recalled 2020-2025 Explorer and Aviator vehicles, later expanded to more model years in 2026, because the second-row seat switch can bind and cause the seat to unlatch, fold, or slide unexpectedly; dealers replace the switch bezel.

What's wrong with my Bronco's front seat?

On 2021-2023 Bronco vehicles, the front seat's height-adjust pivot bolt may be loose or dislodged, which could keep the seat from properly restraining an occupant in a crash; dealers inspect and replace the pivot links and bolts as needed.
