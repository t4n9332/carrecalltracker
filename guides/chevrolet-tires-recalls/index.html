Why do Chevrolet trucks and SUVs have so many tire recalls?

10 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Tires · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-13

Chevrolet's tire recalls fall into a few recurring patterns. Several 2019-2021 recalls involve overcured Continental tires that can develop sidewall breaks or belt-edge separation, affecting a wide range of GM trucks, SUVs, and crossovers including the Silverado 1500, Traverse, Acadia, and Blazer. Separately, Silverado and Sierra 1500 and 2500 trucks have had recalls (2021) where an incompatible spare tire can disable part of the ABS system. Heavy-duty Silverado and Sierra 2500 trucks also have tire pressure labeling problems, one in 2024-2025 models with underinflated rear tires and another affecting the Trailblazer's certification label in 2021. Newer recalls cover Hankook tread separation on the Equinox and Terrain, and Continental tread detachment on the 2025 Optiq and Equinox EV.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Sudden air loss or a belt-edge separation leading to tread loss from overcured tires (multiple 2019-2021 model recalls)
  • Reduced ABS control of the rear wheels when an incompatible spare tire is used (Silverado and Sierra 1500 and 2500 trucks)
  • Tread separating from the tire (2021-2022 Equinox and Terrain with Hankook tires)
  • Tire tread detachment (2025 Cadillac Optiq and Chevrolet Equinox EV with Continental tires)
  • Incorrect or missing tire pressure information on the label, so the TPMS may not warn of low pressure (Colorado, Trailblazer, and 2024-2025 Silverado/Sierra 2500)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these ten tire recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This includes the overcured Continental tire recalls, the Silverado and Sierra spare-tire ABS recalls, the Equinox and Terrain tread-separation recall, the Colorado incorrect-tire recall, the Optiq and Equinox EV tread-detachment recall, and the tire-pressure-label recalls on the Trailblazer and the 2024-2025 Silverado and Sierra 2500. The notices direct owners to have dealers inspect or replace tires and labels, but none instruct owners to stop driving in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, dealers mostly inspect tires and replace any that match the affected DOT or production codes, free of charge. Other fixes include providing a compatible spare tire and new label, updating software and re-learning the tire pressure monitor, or mailing corrected labels for owners to apply themselves. All repairs and replacement parts are free.

Common questions

Why have Chevrolet trucks had so many recalls for overcured tires?

Continental tires supplied to GM were cured too long during manufacturing, which can cause sidewall breaks or belt-edge separation. This defect showed up across several separate recalls between 2020 and 2021, covering different vehicle and tire combinations.

What is the spare tire recall on the Silverado and Sierra about?

On certain Silverado and Sierra 1500 and 2500 trucks, using the provided spare tire with specific accessory road tires can prevent the anti-lock braking system from fully controlling the rear wheels. Dealers provide a compatible spare tire and new label.

Do any of these Chevrolet tire recalls involve a stop-driving order?

No. None of the ten tire recalls in this group, from the overcured Continental tires to the 2025 Optiq and Equinox EV tread detachment, include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction in the notices.
