Why do so many Chevrolet recalls involve tires and tire pressure labels?

10 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Tires · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 13, 2026

Ten Chevrolet tire recalls fall into three patterns. The largest is tires cured too long: the February 2021 recall of 2019-2020 Silverado 1500 and Blazer plus the 2020 Tahoe and Traverse, the November 2020 recall of 2020-2021 Silverado 1500, and the June 2021 recall of 2019 Silverado 1500. Two more tire recalls name other suppliers — Hankook tires with too little curative agent on 2021-2022 Equinox, and 21-inch Continental tires that may lose tread on the 2025 Equinox EV. A second pattern is the 17-inch spare tire that keeps the anti-lock brakes from working properly on 2019-2021 Silverado 1500 and 2020-2021 Silverado 2500. A third is labels: the 2021 Trailblazer, 2024 Colorado and 2024-2025 Silverado 2500.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these ten notices tells owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside. That includes the over-cured Continental tire recalls covering 2019-2020 Silverado 1500 and Blazer, the Hankook tread-separation recall for 2021-2022 Equinox, the 2025 Equinox EV tread-detachment recall, both spare-tire ABS recalls, and the three labelling recalls covering the 2021 Trailblazer, 2024 Colorado and 2024-2025 Silverado 2500. Each notice describes the risk — loss of control, braking that may not work the way it should, or driving on underinflated tires — and points owners to the remedy.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect DOT numbers and replace affected tires on the over-cured Continental, Hankook Equinox, Equinox EV and Colorado recalls. The spare-tire recalls supply a compatible 17-inch spare and a new label. On the Silverado 2500 label recall dealers update engine control module software, apply a label overlay, set pressures and re-learn the TPMS. Trailblazer owners are mailed corrected labels. All free.

Common questions

My Silverado 1500 is on one of the over-cured tire lists. What will the dealer do?

The notices say dealers inspect the tires and replace them as necessary, free of charge; the November 2020 recall replaces tires with the DOT and production mold number combinations identified by Continental. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

Why were my spare tire and my anti-lock brakes recalled together?

Both spare-tire recalls say the 17-inch spare is not compatible with certain accessory road tires and wheels, which can keep the anti-lock braking system from working properly. Dealers provide a compatible spare and a new spare tire information label, free of charge.

Do all of these recalls mean my tires get replaced?

It depends on the recall. Several notices say dealers inspect DOT numbers and replace only affected tires. The 2021 Trailblazer and 2024-2025 Silverado 2500 recalls concern tire information labels, and their remedies centre on a corrected label rather than new tires.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
