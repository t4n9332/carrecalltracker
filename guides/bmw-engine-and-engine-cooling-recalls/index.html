Why are some BMW engines linked to stalling and fire risk?

4 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-08-13

These four recalls affecting BMW's engine and cooling systems point to separate problems rather than one shared cause. Loose exhaust fasteners on the 2023 X7 and XM can raise fire risk, and a bad water pump connector on the 2018 X4 and X5 can short and also raise fire risk. Separately, 2018 M240i, X1 and X2 models, along with several 2019 MINI Clubman, Cooper, Cooper S and Countryman vehicles, share a crankshaft sensor defect that can cause stalling. A single 2019 F850GS motorcycle was recalled because its engine connecting rods may not have been installed properly and could loosen and disconnect.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine stalling while driving (crankshaft sensor recall on affected M240i, X1, X2 and MINI models)
  • Loss of engine power from loose connecting rods (F850GS motorcycle recall)
  • The notices describe no noticeable warning sign for the X7/XM exhaust fastener or the X4/X5 water pump connector recalls

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the X7/XM exhaust fastener issue, the M240i/X1/X2/MINI crankshaft sensor stall risk, the F850GS connecting rod issue, and the X4/X5 water pump connector short do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice describes the defect and directs owners to have a dealer perform the repair rather than instructing them to park the vehicle or avoid driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers address each defect differently: replacing the gasket and tightening exhaust fasteners on the X7 and XM, replacing the crankshaft sensor on affected models, replacing the engine on the F850GS before sale, and inspecting or replacing the water pump and connector while adding a protective shield on the X4 and X5. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which BMW and MINI models are affected by the crankshaft sensor stalling recall?

The notice lists 2018 M240i, X1 and X2, plus 2019 MINI Clubman, Cooper, Cooper S and Countryman vehicles, all sharing the same crankshaft sensor firmware issue that can cause a stall.

Does the X7 exhaust fastener recall also cover the XM?

Yes. The notice covers both the 2023 X7 M60i and the 2023 XM, where exhaust-to-catalytic-converter fasteners may not have been tightened correctly.

Is the water pump connector recall limited to the 2018 X4 and X5?

As listed, this recall applies to 2018 X4 and X5 vehicles, where an improperly sealed electrical connector on the water pump can be exposed to water and short.
