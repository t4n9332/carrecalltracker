What steering problems has Hyundai recalled vehicles for?

3 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Steering · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-06-05

These three Hyundai steering recalls each involve a different failure. In 2018, the Santa Fe was recalled because a steering wheel assembly could break and separate from the steering column while driving. In 2022, the Accent was recalled because a loose steering column bolt could let the column detach from the steering rack, causing a loss of steering control. In 2024, the Tucson and Santa Cruz were recalled because a short circuit in the motor-driven power steering assembly's circuit board could cause a loss of power steering assist. None of these problems repeat across more than one recall in this group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering wheel separating from the steering column while driving (2018 Santa Fe)
  • Steering column loosening or detaching from the steering rack (2021-2022 Accent)
  • Loss of power steering assist (2024 Tucson, Santa Cruz)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three steering recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2018 Santa Fe recall says a broken steering wheel assembly could separate from the column while driving and cause a loss of vehicle control. The 2022 Accent recall says a loose column bolt could cause a loss of steering control. The 2024 Tucson and Santa Cruz recall says a loss of power steering assist can increase the risk of a crash. None of the notices tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers check the steering wheel assembly's production lot and replace it if needed, inspect and tighten the loose steering column bolt, or replace the motor-driven power steering assembly's electric power pack, depending on the recall. All three repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Does the Hyundai Accent steering column recall affect all Accents?

No. It covers certain 2021 and 2022 Accent vehicles where a bolt in the steering column may not have been secured properly, which can let the column detach from the steering rack.

What causes the power steering loss in the 2024 Tucson and Santa Cruz?

The circuit board inside the motor-driven power steering assembly may short circuit, which the notice says can result in a loss of power steering assist and increase the risk of a crash.

Is the 2018 Hyundai Santa Fe steering wheel recall still relevant to used-car buyers?

The recall covers 2018 Santa Fe vehicles whose steering wheel assembly may break and separate from the column while driving; the remedy replaces the assembly free of charge, based on a production lot check.
