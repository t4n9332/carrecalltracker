Hyundai steering recalls: steering wheel, column bolt and power steering assist

3 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Steering · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 5, 2024

These three Hyundai steering recalls are separate defects rather than one repeating pattern, and each covers a small, distinct group. The oldest, from February 2018, covers 2018 Santa Fe and Santa Fe Sport vehicles whose steering wheel assembly may break, possibly resulting in the steering wheel separating from the steering column while driving. The May 2022 recall covers 2021-2022 Accent vehicles, where a bolt in the steering column may not be secured properly, which can cause the column to detach from the steering rack and result in a loss of steering control. The newest, from June 2024, covers 2024 Tucson and Santa Cruz vehicles, where the circuit board in the motor-driven power steering assembly may short circuit, resulting in a loss of power steering assist.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The steering wheel separating from the steering column while driving (2018 Santa Fe and Santa Fe Sport recall).
  • A loss of steering control if the column detaches from the steering rack (2021-2022 Accent recall).
  • A loss of power steering assist (2024 Tucson and Santa Cruz recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none carries a park-outside instruction or any interim instruction. The 2018 Santa Fe and Santa Fe Sport notice states that a steering wheel separation while driving can cause a loss of vehicle control and increase the risk of a crash; the 2021-2022 Accent notice states that a loss of steering control can increase the risk of a crash; and the 2024 Tucson and Santa Cruz notice states that a loss of power steering assist can increase the risk of a crash.

What the free repair involves

For the 2018 Santa Fe and Santa Fe Sport, dealers check the production lot number of the steering wheel assembly and replace it as necessary. For 2021-2022 Accent vehicles, dealers inspect and tighten the lower steering column u-joint retention bolt as necessary. For 2024 Tucson and Santa Cruz vehicles, dealers replace the motor-driven power steering electric power pack. All three notices state the repair is free of charge.

Common questions

Can a Hyundai steering wheel actually come off?

The February 2018 recall states the steering wheel assembly on certain 2018 Santa Fe and Santa Fe Sport vehicles may break, possibly resulting in the steering wheel separating from the steering column while driving. Dealers check the production lot number and replace the assembly as necessary.

What happens if my Accent's steering column bolt is not secured?

The notice for 2021-2022 Accent vehicles states the steering column can detach from the steering rack, resulting in a loss of steering control. Dealers inspect and tighten the lower steering column u-joint retention bolt as necessary, free of charge.

Does the Tucson and Santa Cruz recall mean the steering fails completely?

The notice describes a short circuit in the motor-driven power steering assembly circuit board resulting in a loss of power steering assist, which it says can increase the risk of a crash. Dealers replace the electric power pack free of charge.
