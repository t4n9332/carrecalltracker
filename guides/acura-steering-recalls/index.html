Why is my Acura RDX or Integra hard to steer?

3 NHTSA recalls · Acura · Steering · model years 2023–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-09-05

These three Acura recalls involve two different steering defects. A 2024 recall covers the 2024 Acura RDX and 2023-2024 Honda Odyssey, where an improperly tightened steering gear box let water enter and corrode the gears, risking a loss of steering control. A separate 2025 recall affects only the 2025 Acura RDX, where miscalibrated power steering software can trip a failsafe mode and cut power steering assist, requiring more effort to turn the wheel. The third recall, from October 2024, covers the 2023-2025 Acura Integra along with many Honda Civic, CR-V and HR-V models, where a steering gearbox built with excessive internal friction can make the vehicle difficult to steer.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering that suddenly feels stiffer or requires more effort to turn (2023-24 Odyssey/2024 RDX corrosion recall)
  • Increased steering effort if the power steering assist drops into failsafe mode (2025 RDX)
  • Difficulty steering caused by excess friction in the gearbox (2023-2025 Integra and related Honda models)
  • The corrosion recall notice does not describe any dashboard warning light or noise before the steering problem occurs

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. The 2024 corrosion recall (2024 RDX/2023-2024 Odyssey) says corroded, binding steering gears can cause a loss of steering control. The 2025 software recall says a failsafe mode can cause loss of power steering assist, requiring greater effort to steer. The October 2024 gearbox-friction recall (2023-2025 Integra and related models) says difficulty steering can increase the risk of a crash. For all three, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers address each defect differently, and all repairs are free. For the corrosion recall, dealers replace the electric power steering rack. For the 2025 software issue, dealers reprogram the electric power steering assist software. For the gearbox-friction recall, dealers replace the worm gear spring and redistribute or add grease inside the steering gearbox.

Common questions

Does the Acura RDX steering recall affect the 2025 model?

Yes, but as a separate issue. The 2025 RDX recall involves power steering software that can trigger a failsafe mode, while the 2024 RDX corrosion recall involves a different steering gear box problem shared with certain Honda Odyssey models.

Is the Acura Integra steering recall the same as the RDX recalls?

No. The Integra recall covers a steering gearbox built with excess internal friction, shared with several Honda models, which is a different defect than either RDX steering recall.

Will fixing these Acura steering recalls cost anything?

No. Dealers perform the gear box replacement, software reprogramming, or gearbox spring and grease repair described in each recall free of charge.
