Acura steering recalls: gearbox friction, corrosion and power steering software

3 NHTSA recalls · Acura · Steering · model years 2023–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 5, 2025

Three recalls cover Acura steering, and each names a different failure. The October 2024 recall is the widest: a steering gearbox that may have been manufactured incorrectly can cause excessive internal friction on 2023-2025 Integra, 2024-2025 Integra Type S and a long list of Honda models including 2022-2025 Civic and 2023-2025 CR-V and HR-V. The February 2024 recall covers the 2024 RDX alongside 2023-2024 Odyssey, where a steering gear box assembly that was not tightened properly can let water in and cause corrosion and binding gears. The September 2025 recall is limited to the 2025 RDX, where electric power steering software may not be calibrated properly. RDX and Integra are the Acura models that appear.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Difficulty steering the vehicle (October 2024 steering gearbox recall).
  • Greater steering effort after a loss of power steering assist (September 2025 RDX power steering software recall).
  • A loss of steering control (February 2024 steering gear box corrosion recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The February 2024 corrosion recall says corroded and binding steering gears can result in a loss of steering control and increase the risk of a crash. The October 2024 gearbox recall says difficulty steering can increase the risk of a crash. The September 2025 RDX software recall says a loss of power steering assist can require greater steering effort, increasing the risk of a crash or injury. The notices give owners no other instructions before the repair.

What the free repair involves

The work differs by recall, and every notice says it is free of charge. For the February 2024 corrosion recall, dealers replace the electric power steering rack. For the October 2024 gearbox recall, dealers replace the worm gear spring and redistribute or add grease as necessary. For the September 2025 RDX recall, dealers reprogram the electric power steering software. None of the three is done over the air.

Common questions

Does the Acura RDX fall under more than one steering recall?

Two recalls here name the RDX. The February 2024 recall covers the 2024 RDX for a steering gear box that can corrode, and the September 2025 recall covers the 2025 RDX for electric power steering software calibration. The 2023-2025 Integra falls under the October 2024 gearbox recall.

Why are Honda models listed inside an Acura steering recall?

American Honda Motor Co. files these recalls covering both brands at once. The October 2024 gearbox recall lists Acura Integra together with Civic, Civic Hatchback, CR-V and HR-V models, and the February 2024 corrosion recall pairs the 2024 RDX with 2023-2024 Odyssey.

Do the notices tell me to stop driving until the steering repair is done?

None of the three notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. Each states a crash risk instead: corroded and binding gears, difficulty steering, or a loss of power steering assist requiring greater effort. Dealers perform each repair free of charge.
