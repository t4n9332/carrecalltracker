Acura Steering Recalls Cover Binding Gears and Lost Power Assist

3 NHTSA recalls · Acura · Steering · model years 2023–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 5, 2025

The Acura recalls involve two distinct steering concerns on the RDX and a separate gearbox friction problem on the Integra. Certain 2024 RDX vehicles have a steering gearbox assembly that may not have been tightened properly, allowing water entry, corrosion and binding gears. Certain 2025 RDX vehicles instead have improperly calibrated electric power steering software that can trigger failsafe mode and remove steering assistance. The gearbox friction recall covers certain 2023-2025 Integra and 2024-2025 Integra Type S vehicles. Its incorrectly manufactured gearbox assembly can create excessive internal friction and difficulty steering. RDX appears in two notices, with different defects and remedies.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2025 RDX power steering software recall GMS: loss of power steering assist can require greater steering effort.
  • October 2024 steering gearbox recall SJS/MJU/QJT/VJV: difficulty steering.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The notices for the 2024 RDX steering corrosion recall, the 2025 RDX EPS software recall GMS, and the Integra steering gearbox recall SJS/MJU/QJT/VJV do not tell owners to stop driving. None gives a park-outside instruction. The stated risks differ: corrosion and binding gears can cause loss of steering control, EPS assist loss can require greater steering effort, and excessive gearbox friction can cause difficulty steering. Each notice identifies an increased crash risk associated with its particular steering defect.

What the free repair involves

For the 2024 RDX corrosion recall, dealers replace the electric power steering rack. For 2025 RDX recall GMS, dealers reprogram the EPS software. For the Integra gearbox recall, dealers replace the worm gear spring and redistribute or add grease as necessary. Each notice states that its repair is free of charge.

Common questions

Do the RDX steering recalls require the same repair?

The remedies differ by recall. The 2024 RDX corrosion notice calls for replacement of the electric power steering rack. The 2025 RDX software notice, GMS, calls for dealers to reprogram the EPS software.

Which Integra model years are named in the gearbox recall?

The October 2024 notice names certain 2023-2025 Acura Integra and 2024-2025 Integra Type S vehicles. It describes excessive internal friction from an incorrectly manufactured gearbox assembly, with dealers replacing the worm gear spring and adjusting grease as necessary.

What steering changes do the RDX and Integra notices describe?

The 2025 RDX software notice describes greater steering effort after loss of power assistance. The Integra gearbox notice describes difficulty steering. The 2024 RDX corrosion notice identifies binding gears and possible loss of steering control.
