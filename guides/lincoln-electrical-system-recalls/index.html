Why Do Lincoln Hybrid Batteries And Wiring Pose Fire Risks?

17 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-09

The recurring issue in this group is high-voltage hybrid or plug-in-hybrid battery cells short-circuiting inside the Lincoln Corsair and Aviator, which repeats across five separate recalls issued between 2022 and 2026 and can cause both a sudden loss of drive power and a fire. A second repeating problem is trailer brake control software that can stop a towed trailer's brakes from working on the Navigator and Super Duty trucks, first recalled in 2022 and then reissued in 2025 after the earlier fix failed. Other one-off issues in this group include loose starter and alternator cables on the 2018 Lincoln MKX, an overheating block heater cord on the Corsair and Nautilus, and a software bug that can blank the Nautilus's displays.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Sudden loss of drive power while driving (hybrid battery recalls)
  • Trailer's brakes fail to engage when towing (trailer brake recalls)
  • Instrument cluster or center display suddenly reboots to a blank screen (Nautilus)
  • The notices describe no warning before a battery short circuit or fire

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Some do add charging-specific interim steps for the specific recall involved: the December 2024 Escape and Corsair battery recall tells owners not to charge the battery until the remedy is complete, and the November 2025 and February 2026 Escape and Corsair battery recalls tell owners to use only 'Auto EV' mode and limit the maximum charge until repaired. For every other recall in this group, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the battery recalls, dealers update the battery energy control module (BECM) and hybrid powertrain control module software, or replace the high-voltage battery pack. For the trailer brake recalls, dealers update the integrated trailer brake control module software. Other fixes include securing power cables, replacing the block heater element, and updating multimedia module software by dealer or over-the-air. All free.

Common questions

Which Lincoln models have the hybrid battery fire recalls?

The high-voltage battery fire recalls in this group cover the Lincoln Corsair and Aviator plug-in hybrids, model years ranging from 2020 through 2026 depending on the specific recall. Check your VIN on NHTSA.gov to see which recalls apply to your vehicle.

Should I stop charging my Lincoln Corsair plug-in hybrid?

The notices don't tell owners to stop driving, but three recalls add charging limits until repaired: the December 2024 recall says not to charge the battery, and the November 2025 and February 2026 recalls say to use only 'Auto EV' mode with a reduced maximum charge.

Why would towing a trailer with my Lincoln Navigator be risky?

Two recalls (2022 and a 2025 do-over of the same fix) found a software error in the integrated trailer brake control module that could prevent a towed trailer's electric or electric-over-hydraulic brakes from working. Dealers update that module's software for free.
