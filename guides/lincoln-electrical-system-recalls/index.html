Lincoln electrical recalls: hybrid batteries, fire risk and trailer brakes

17 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 9, 2026

High voltage batteries dominate this group. Lincoln Corsair plug-in hybrids appear repeatedly — 2021-2024, and again 2023-2026 — for a manufacturing defect in one or more battery cells that may cause an internal short circuit and battery failure, and the 2020-2022 Aviator with 3.0L PHEV engines carries the same described defect, followed by a 2020-2021 Aviator recall for vehicles repaired incorrectly under it. Earlier 2.5L hybrid recalls cover the 2022 Corsair and the 2021 Corsair with insufficient welds inside the battery. Away from batteries, the 2022 Navigator is recalled twice for trailer brake control software, the 2024 Nautilus for a multimedia module that can blank its displays, the 2023 and 2025 Corsair and 2024-2025 Nautilus for a block heater that may overheat, and the 2018 MKX and 2019 MKT for power supply cables.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Panoramic and center displays that reboot to a blank screen, taking the speedometer, warning lights and rearview camera image with them — stated in the 2024 Nautilus recall (25C21).
  • A sudden loss of drive power — stated in the 2022 Corsair 2.5L hybrid high voltage battery recall (21S48).
  • Loss of drive power from battery failure — stated in the Aviator and Corsair high voltage battery cell recalls.
  • A towed trailer with electric or electric-over-hydraulic brakes that may not brake — stated in the trailer brake control recalls 22S17 and 25S18.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Three carry interim instructions of their own. The 2021-2024 Corsair high voltage battery recall advises owners not to charge the battery until the remedy has been completed. The 2021-2024 Corsair plug-in hybrid recall and the 2023-2026 Corsair plug-in hybrid recall each advise using only "Auto EV" mode and say owners will be instructed by mail to limit the maximum charge of the battery until the remedy is completed. The rest give no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

These repairs are mostly software updates and battery replacement. Dealers update battery energy control module software, add hybrid powertrain control module software on the plug-in hybrid recalls, and replace the high voltage battery pack where a notice calls for it. Others inspect the starter and alternator power supply cables, replace the block heater element, or update trailer brake control or Nautilus multimedia module software. All free of charge.

Common questions

Which Lincolns are covered by the high voltage battery recalls?

The notices list 2020-2022 Aviator with 3.0L PHEV engines, 2021-2024 Corsair, 2023-2026 Corsair plug-in hybrids, 2022 Corsair with the 2.5L hybrid powertrain, and 2021 Corsair for insufficient welds inside the battery. Each recall names its own model years.

What do the Corsair battery notices tell owners to do meanwhile?

The 2020-2024 Escape and 2021-2024 Corsair recall advises owners not to charge the battery until the remedy has been completed. The later plug-in hybrid recalls advise using only "Auto EV" mode and say owners will be instructed by mail to limit maximum charge.

Why is a trailer brake problem in the electrical group?

Recalls 22S17 and 25S18 describe a software error under which a towed trailer with electric or electric-over-hydraulic brakes may not brake, on the 2022 Navigator and several Ford models. Dealers update the integrated trailer brake control module software, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
