Which Toyota wheel recalls tell owners to stop driving?

3 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Wheels · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 15, 2023

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

Three Toyota recalls concern the wheels themselves, and two of them are unusually urgent. In 2022 Toyota recalled 2023 Toyota BZ4X and Subaru Solterra vehicles because the wheel hub bolts may loosen and let a wheel detach after low-mileage use. In 2023 it recalled 2023 Camry and Camry Hybrid sedans because the lug nuts holding the wheels on may be loose, which can also result in wheel detachment. The oldest recall is different: certain 2018 Sienna minivans were built with a lot of 10-spoke alloy wheels from one production facility that may crack in the spokes from an impact while driving. Fastener problems on newer models are the recurring theme.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two of these three notices tell owners not to drive. For the 2023 BZ4X and Solterra hub bolt recall, owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until they are repaired. For the 2023 Camry and Camry Hybrid lug nut recall, owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until the repair has been performed. The 2018 Sienna alloy wheel notice does not tell owners to stop driving. None of the three notices tells owners to park outside.

What the free repair involves

For the BZ4X and Solterra, dealers replace the hub bolts and wheels. For the Camry and Camry Hybrid, dealers inspect and tighten the lug nuts and replace any damaged components. For the 2018 Sienna, dealers inspect the wheels and replace them if necessary depending on their production location. All three repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Can I drive my 2023 Camry with the lug nut recall?

The notice says owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until the repair has been performed. Dealers inspect and tighten the lug nuts and replace any damaged components free of charge. Toyota customer service can confirm whether a specific Camry is covered.

Why is a Subaru Solterra in a Toyota recall?

Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing filed the recall covering certain 2023 Subaru Solterra and Toyota BZ4X vehicles together. The notice says the hub bolts on the wheels may loosen, causing a wheel to detach after low-mileage use, and owners are told not to drive until repaired.

Will every 2018 Sienna get new wheels?

The notice says dealers inspect the wheels and replace them if necessary depending on their production location, free of charge. It covers vehicles built with a specific lot of 10-spoke alloy wheels from one production facility, not all 2018 Siennas.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
