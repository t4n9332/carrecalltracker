Why are Toyota Camry, BZ4X and Sienna wheels being recalled?

3 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Wheels · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-06-15

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

These three Toyota wheel recalls involve two different failure points. In 2022, Toyota recalled the new BZ4X (and its twin, the Subaru Solterra) because the hub bolts securing the wheels could loosen after low-mileage use, and in 2023 it recalled the Camry and Camry Hybrid because the lug nuts attaching the wheels could be loose from the factory — both are critical, do-not-drive recalls over wheels detaching. Separately, a 2018 recall covers Sienna minivans built with a specific lot of 10-spoke alloy wheels that were not properly cooled during manufacturing, which can cause the wheel to crack at the spokes after an impact while driving.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The BZ4X and Solterra hub bolt recall describes no driver-noticeable warning sign before a wheel could detach
  • The Camry loose lug nut recall also describes no driver-noticeable warning sign
  • The Sienna notice describes no warning sign either, saying a wheel could crack at the spokes after hitting something while driving

Is it safe to keep driving?

Two of these three recalls carry a stop-driving instruction: the 2022 BZ4X/Solterra hub bolt recall and the 2023 Camry/Camry Hybrid lug nut recall both tell owners not to drive their vehicle at all until it has been repaired. Neither carries a separate park-outside instruction. The third recall, covering 2018 Sienna wheels that may crack at the spokes after an impact, does not tell owners to stop driving — the notice only says dealers will inspect the wheels and replace them if necessary.

What the free repair involves

For the BZ4X and Solterra, dealers replace the hub bolts and the wheels. For the Camry and Camry Hybrid, dealers inspect and tighten the lug nuts and replace any damaged components. For the Sienna, dealers inspect the alloy wheels and replace them if needed based on where they were manufactured. All of this work is done free of charge.

Common questions

Why are BZ4X and Solterra owners told not to drive at all?

The notice says the hub bolts on the wheels can loosen after low-mileage use, letting a wheel detach from a moving vehicle. Because that risk is severe, Toyota told owners not to drive until a dealer replaces the hub bolts and wheels.

Is the Camry lug nut problem the same issue as the BZ4X recall?

No. They are separate recalls with separate causes — loose factory lug nuts on the Camry and Camry Hybrid versus loosening hub bolts on the BZ4X and Solterra — but both share the same do-not-drive instruction because either can let a wheel come off.

Should I be worried about my 2018 Sienna's wheels right now?

The notice does not tell Sienna owners to stop driving. It covers a specific lot of 10-spoke alloy wheels that may not have cooled properly during manufacturing, which the notice says can cause cracking at the spokes after an impact.
