Why Do Ram Trucks Keep Recalling Transmission And Shifter Problems?

13 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Power Train · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-05-08

Ram's powertrain recalls in this group cluster around two recurring problems. Transmission fluid leaking onto a possible ignition source appears twice on heavy-duty trucks: 2019-2020 Ram 2500 and 3500 with 68RFE transmissions, and again on 2020-2022 Ram 3500 and 2020-2021 Ram 2500. Shift and park-related malfunctions recur even more often — a 2018 shift interlock defect across the full Ram 1500-5500 lineup, a 2018 wrong park lock rod on Ram 1500, two separate ProMaster shifter cable separation recalls on 2018-2019 vans, a 2019-2021 ProMaster park pawl recall, and a 2025 Ram 3500 software defect that can show the wrong gear. The 2018-2019 Ram 1500 also had separate rear axle and driveshaft recalls, and a 2019 Ram 1500 4x4 had a transfer case defect.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Transmission fluid leaking from the dipstick tube (2019-2022 Ram 2500/3500 recalls)
  • Vehicle shifting out of Park or rolling away unexpectedly (2018 shift interlock, 2018 wrong park lock rod, and 2019-2021 ProMaster park pawl recalls)
  • Gear shift lever position not matching the actual transmission gear (ProMaster shifter cable recalls)
  • Sudden loss of power or the transfer case becoming stuck in neutral (2019 Ram 1500 4x4)
  • Instrument cluster showing the wrong gear selected (2025 Ram 2500/3500)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This includes both critical-severity transmission fluid leak recalls on Ram 2500 and 3500 trucks, the 2018 shift interlock and wrong park lock rod recalls, the two ProMaster shifter cable recalls, the ProMaster park pawl recall, the Ram 1500 rear axle, driveshaft, and transfer case recalls, the 2023 Ram 3500 transmission leak recall, and the 2025 Ram 3500 gear display software recall. The notices describe each defect and its risk but do not instruct owners to stop driving while a repair is scheduled.

What the free repair involves

Across this group, dealers replace or update the specific failed part — transmission valve bodies, dipsticks, vent assemblies, shift cables, park lock rods, driveshafts, axles, transfer cases, or control units — and in several recalls update software in the transmission or body control module. All of these repairs are performed free of charge to the owner.

Common questions

How many Ram powertrain recalls involve shifting out of Park unexpectedly?

Three separate recalls in this group involve Park-related rollaway risk: a 2018 shift interlock defect across the Ram 1500-5500 lineup, a 2018 wrong park lock rod on Ram 1500, and a 2019-2021 ProMaster park pawl recall that could let the vehicle roll away.

Why do Ram 2500 and 3500 trucks have transmission fire recalls?

Two recalls describe transmission fluid building up pressure and leaking from the dipstick tube on 2019-2020 and 2020-2022 Ram 2500 and 3500 trucks. If that fluid reaches an ignition source in the engine compartment, it increases the risk of a fire.

Are Ram ProMaster shifter cable recalls all the same problem?

Two separate 2018-2019 ProMaster recalls describe the transmission shifter cable separating from the transmission, which can make the gear shift lever not match the actual gear and lead to unintended vehicle movement. Repairs are free at Ram dealers.
