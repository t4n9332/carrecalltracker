Ram transmission, park and driveline recalls explained for owners

13 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Power Train · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-05-08

Park and gear engagement is the running theme. A Brake Transmission Shift Interlock pin can stick open on 2017-2018 Ram 1500 through 5500 column-shifter trucks, an incorrect park lock rod reached some 2018 Ram 1500s, shifter cables can separate on 2014-2019 ProMaster vans with 3.6L engines, the park pawl may not fully engage on 2019-2021 ProMasters, and a software error can display the wrong gear on 2025 Ram 2500 and 3500 trucks. The second theme is transmission fluid: leaks from the dipstick tube on 2019-2020 Ram 2500 and 3500 pickups and on 2020-2023 Ram 2500 and 2020-2022 Ram 3500, plus a leaking transmission control unit on 2023 Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500. Driveline items: 2018-2019 Ram 1500 axles and driveshafts, and a 2019 Ram 1500 4x4 transfer case.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The transmission can be shifted out of PARK into any gear without pushing the brake pedal or having the key in the ignition - the 2017-2018 Ram shift interlock recall.
  • The transmission may not shift into PARK - the 2018 park lock rod recall.
  • Shifts are not performed as the driver intends and the shift lever position does not match the actual gear - the Ram ProMaster shifter cable recalls.
  • The vehicle may become stuck in neutral, with a sudden loss of power or a loss of PARK function - the 2019 Ram 1500 4x4 transfer case recall.
  • An inaccurate gear selection display - the 2025 Ram 2500 and 3500 cluster software recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside or away from structures - including the two transmission fluid leak recalls that describe a fire risk and the recalls that describe rollaway or unintended vehicle movement. Each notice states the defect, the crash, rollaway or fire consequence, and the dealer remedy, without any interim instruction to limit or stop using the vehicle. What these notices say about driving before the repair ends there.

What the free repair involves

Dealers update software on several: Body Control Module for the shift interlock, powertrain control module for the park pawl and the 2019-2020 fluid leak, cluster software for the gear display. Others are parts work - valve body separator plate, dipstick and vent assembly, park lock rod, rear axle, rear driveshaft, transfer case, transmission shift cable, valve body and control unit. Where the notices state it, the repair is free of charge.

Common questions

Which of these Ram recalls involve rollaway or losing Park?

Several do: the shift interlock pin on 2017-2018 column-shifter trucks, the incorrect park lock rod on 2018 Ram 1500, the park pawl on 2019-2021 ProMaster, the 2023 transmission control unit leak, and the 2025 gear selection display error.

Do the transmission fluid leak recalls describe a fire risk?

Both do. The 2019-2020 Ram 2500 and 3500 notice says leaking fluid may contact the turbocharger or another ignition source in the engine compartment; the 2020-2023 Ram 2500 and 2020-2022 Ram 3500 notice says it may contact an ignition source.

What happens for the Ram ProMaster shifter cable recall?

The FCA notice for 2014-2019 ProMaster vans with 3.6L engines says dealers replace the transmission shift cable, free of charge. The Prime-Time paratransit notice instructs owners to take the vehicle to a Ram dealer for the necessary repairs, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
