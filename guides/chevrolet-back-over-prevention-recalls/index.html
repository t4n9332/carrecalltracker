Why are Chevrolet models being recalled over backup safety features?

3 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Back Over Prevention · model years 2023–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-20

Three Chevrolet-related back-over prevention recalls since December 2025 fall into two categories. The Equinox EV (2025-2026) was recalled for a pedestrian alert system that did not sufficiently change volume between being stopped and moving at low speed, a defect fixed with a software update. The other two recalls both involve rearview cameras displaying a distorted or blank image: one affecting the 2023-2025 Chevrolet Malibu, and a later one affecting the 2024-2026 Chevrolet Trailblazer and 2025 Chevrolet Trax, plus the related 2024-2025 Buick Envista and 2024-2026 Buick Encore GX. The camera defect is described the same way in both notices.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Pedestrian alert sound doesn't get noticeably louder when the Equinox EV starts moving from a stop
  • Rearview camera screen shows a distorted picture when backing up
  • Rearview camera screen goes completely blank when backing up

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls tell owners to stop driving or to park outside. The Equinox EV pedestrian alert notice, the Malibu rearview camera notice, and the Trailblazer/Envista/Encore GX/Trax rearview camera notice each describe an increased crash or injury risk but stop short of instructing owners to stop driving. Owners are told to have the body control module software updated (Equinox EV, by dealer or over the air) or to have the camera inspected and replaced if needed (Malibu, Trailblazer, Envista, Encore GX, Trax), free of charge.

What the free repair involves

For the Equinox EV, GM updates the body control module software, either by a dealer or automatically over the air. For the Malibu and the Trailblazer, Trax, Envista, and Encore GX, dealers inspect the rearview camera and replace it if it is found to be defective. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Are these camera and pedestrian alert issues part of the same defect?

No. The Equinox EV recall involves the pedestrian warning sound system, while the Malibu, Trailblazer, Trax, Envista, and Encore GX recalls involve a separate rearview camera display defect.

Can the Equinox EV recall be fixed without visiting a dealer?

Yes. GM says the software update can be applied over the air, though owners can also have a dealer perform it free of charge.

Does the camera recall affect Buick models too?

Yes, the 2026 recall also covers the Buick Envista and Encore GX because they were named alongside the affected Chevrolet Trailblazer and Trax for the same camera display defect.
