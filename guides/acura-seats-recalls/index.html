Why are Acura driver's seats being recalled for loose frames?

3 NHTSA recalls · Acura · Seats · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 29, 2026

All three of these recalls concern the driver's seat coming loose from where it should be anchored, but they split into two causes. The April 2018 recall covers 2018 Acura MDX vehicles whose driver powered seats may have been assembled with improperly manufactured rivets that can break in a crash, leaving the seat unsecured to the floor. The November 2024 and January 2026 recalls both cover 2024 Acura Integra vehicles - the 2024 recall also lists Integra Type S - where the driver's seat cushion frame may not have been tightened properly, resulting in an unsecured or loose seat. All three notices cite Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 207 on seating systems.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A loose driver's seat - the wording used in the January 2026 recall covering the 2024 Acura Integra; the November 2024 recall of the same model calls it an unsecured seat.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park outside. The April 2018 recall of the 2018 Acura MDX says that if the seat does not remain secured to the floor in a crash, the occupant faces an increased risk of injury. The November 2024 and January 2026 recalls covering the 2024 Acura Integra say a driver's seat that is unsecured or loose may not adequately restrain the driver during a crash. Beyond that, none of them gives owners any instruction about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

All three remedies are free of charge and all are dealer work, not software. In the April 2018 recall, dealers replace the driver seat frame assembly in Acura MDX vehicles. In both the November 2024 and January 2026 recalls, dealers replace the driver's seat cushion frame. None of the three notices mentions an inspection-only outcome or an over-the-air update.

Common questions

My 2024 Integra was already repaired once - why another seat recall?

The November 2024 and January 2026 recalls both list the 2024 Acura Integra and both describe a driver's seat cushion frame that may not have been tightened properly. The notices give separate Honda recall numbers, so check your VIN against each.

Which Acura models are in these seat recalls?

On the Acura side, the April 2018 recall lists 2018 MDX vehicles, the November 2024 recall lists 2024 Integra and Integra Type S, and the January 2026 recall lists 2024 Integra. Each also covers Honda models under the same notice.

Is the seat repair free?

Yes. All three notices say dealers will replace the affected seat frame free of charge - the driver seat frame assembly for the 2018 MDX, and the driver's seat cushion frame for the 2024 Integra recalls. Owners may contact Honda at 1-888-234-2138.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
