Why do Honda and Acura driver seats keep coming loose?

3 NHTSA recalls · Acura · Seats · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-01-29

Three Honda and Acura recalls over the past several years have targeted the same weak point: how the seat frame is fastened to the seat structure. In 2018, improperly manufactured rivets could let the front power seats in the 2018 Acura MDX, Honda Pilot, and Honda Ridgeline detach from the floor in a crash. In late 2024 and again in early 2026, Honda found that the driver's seat cushion frame was not tightened properly on 2023-2024 Accord Hybrid, Civic Hatchback, and Pilot models, plus the 2024 Acura Integra and Honda HR-V, with the 2026 notice repeating on several of the same 2023-2024 models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2018 rivet defect: no warning sign before a crash, since the seats only detach when a crash occurs
  • 2024 and 2026 recalls: the driver's seat cushion frame was not tightened, which may make the seat feel loose or wobble
  • No dashboard warning light or alert is described in any of the three notices

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2018 notice on the Acura MDX, Honda Pilot, and Ridgeline does not tell owners to stop driving despite the crash-related seat detachment risk. The 2024 and 2026 notices covering the Accord Hybrid, Civic Hatchback, Pilot, HR-V, and Integra likewise do not tell owners to stop driving over the loose driver's seat cushion frame. Owners are simply told to contact Honda to schedule the free repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the affected seat hardware free of charge. For the 2018 recall, that means a new driver seat frame assembly in the Acura MDX and new front seat slide rail frames in the Honda Pilot and Ridgeline. For the 2024 and 2026 recalls, dealers replace the driver's seat cushion frame on the affected Accord Hybrid, Civic Hatchback, Pilot, HR-V, and Integra models.

Common questions

Why do Honda and Acura keep recalling seats?

Two separate manufacturing problems are involved: 2018 models had rivets that could break in a crash, while 2023-2024 models had a driver's seat cushion frame that wasn't tightened properly, repeated in a 2026 recall on some of the same vehicles.

Is my Acura MDX affected by more than one seat recall?

Only the 2018 Acura MDX was named in the rivet recall. The 2024 Acura Integra was named in the loose cushion frame recalls, not the MDX.

How do I know if my vehicle is included?

Check your VIN against each specific recall notice; the three recalls list different model years and trims, so being named in one does not mean your vehicle is in another.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
