Is my Acura driver's seat loose? Seat recalls explained

3 NHTSA recalls · Acura · Seats · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-01-29

All three recalls here describe the same failure: an Acura driver's seat that is not properly secured to the floor. Two of them, filed in November 2024 and again in January 2026, cover the 2024 Integra and Integra Type S, where the driver's seat cushion frame may not have been tightened properly, leaving the seat loose. The 2026 filing repeats the 2024 Integra with Honda Accord, HR-V and Pilot models. The oldest, from April 2018, covers the 2018 MDX, where the driver's power seat was assembled with improperly manufactured rivets. Acura's exposure across this group is narrow: only the 2018 MDX and the 2024 Integra are named, with the remaining vehicles being Honda Accord, Civic, Pilot, Ridgeline and HR-V.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The driver's seat shifting, rocking or sliding slightly when you get in or brake
  • Play or looseness in the seat that you can feel by pushing on it
  • Movement or noise from the seat frame over bumps
  • The notices describe no warning light and no dashboard message for this defect

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls tells owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside. The danger is specific to a crash: a seat that is not properly secured may not hold the driver in place, which raises the chance of injury in a collision that the seat would otherwise have been designed for. Day-to-day driving is not restricted by the notices, but there is no benefit to waiting, and a seat that already feels loose to you is a reason to book the free repair sooner rather than later.

What the free repair involves

For the 2024 Integra and Integra Type S in both the 2024 and 2026 recalls, dealers replace the driver's seat cushion frame. For the 2018 MDX, dealers replace the driver seat frame assembly; the related Honda Pilot and Ridgeline vehicles get their driver and front passenger seat slide rail frames replaced instead. Every repair in this group is free of charge.

Common questions

Does this affect the passenger seat too?

For the Acura models, no. The notices name the driver's seat only: the driver seat frame assembly on the 2018 MDX and the driver's seat cushion frame on the 2024 Integra. Front passenger seats were included only for the 2018 Honda Pilot and Ridgeline.

My 2024 Integra was already repaired. Why did I get another letter?

The 2024 Integra appears in both the November 2024 recall and the January 2026 recall for an improperly tightened driver's seat cushion frame. They are separate campaigns, so give the dealer the new recall number and have your VIN checked against it.

How can I tell whether my seat frame is the loose one?

You may be able to feel play by pushing the seat side to side, but a frame can be under-tightened without being obvious. The only reliable check is a VIN lookup at an Acura dealer, which costs nothing.
