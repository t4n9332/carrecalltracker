Why are Volkswagen headlights and back-up lights under recall?

5 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 2, 2021

Every recall in this group is a Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108 lighting compliance issue, and four of the five concern headlights. Aim comes up repeatedly: the 2018-2020 Passat and 2018-2019 Atlas were recalled because the cap that blocks horizontal aim adjustment may not have been installed, the 2021 ID.4 had caps installed on the vertical aim instead of the horizontal, and the 2020 Atlas Cross Sport and 2021 Atlas had the driver's side headlight aimed too low. The 2019 Jetta with LED headlights had the passenger side unit incorrectly positioned. The one non-headlight recall is the 2018 Tiguan long wheel base, whose back-up lights may not be bright enough.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Dim back-up lights when reversing — recall 94J9 says the 2018 Tiguan's back-up lights may not be bright enough.
  • Reduced visibility when it is dark — recall 94L1 says the 2019 Jetta's incorrectly positioned passenger side LED headlight reduces the driver's visibility.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside. Each one describes the defect as reducing visibility — the driver's, or how well other drivers see the vehicle reversing — and states that this increases the risk of a crash. The Tiguan, Jetta, Passat, Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport and ID.4 notices all say the same thing on this point: they set out the risk and direct owners to the dealer repair, and none of them gives an interim instruction to follow before that repair is done.

What the free repair involves

All five repairs are free of charge. Dealers replace the inner taillight assemblies on the 2018 Tiguan and the passenger side headlight on the 2019 Jetta. On the Passat and Atlas they inspect and block the headlight horizontal adjustment; on the Atlas Cross Sport and 2021 Atlas they adjust the driver's side headlight; on the ID.4 they move the caps to the horizontal aim.

Common questions

My headlights look fine — does the recall still apply?

The notices treat these as failures to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108, not as something the driver must first notice. They tell owners the affected vehicles need the dealer repair, and that the repair is free of charge.

Which Volkswagen Atlas model years are in these lighting recalls?

Two recalls touch the Atlas. Recall 94L9 covers 2018-2020 Passat and 2018-2019 Atlas vehicles whose headlight horizontal aim blocking cap may be missing. Recall 94N7 covers the 2020 Atlas Cross Sport and 2021 Atlas, where the driver's side headlight may be aimed too low.

What is wrong with the 2018 Tiguan's back-up lights?

Recall 94J9 covers 2018 Tiguan long wheel base vehicles whose back-up lights in the taillight assemblies may not be bright enough to meet Standard 108. Dealers replace the affected inner taillight assemblies free of charge.
