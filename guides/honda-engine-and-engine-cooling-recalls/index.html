Honda engine recalls: rod bearings, oil leaks and stalling engines

11 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 18, 2025

Eleven recalls, and most cover motorcycles and scooters rather than cars. Rod bearing failure is the thread: a connecting rod bearing that can wear and seize covers 2015-2020 Acura TLX, 2016-2020 MDX, 2016 and 2018-2019 Pilot, 2017 and 2019 Ridgeline and 2018-2019 Odyssey, while rod bearing failure on the 2024-2026 CBR600RR and 2025 CB1000SP can lock the rear wheel. Oil leaking onto the rear tire repeats on the 2023 Rebel 300 and on the 2024 XL750, 2025 CMX1100 and CRF1100. Engine stalls from ECU software cover 2020-2022 GL1800 Gold Wing and 2020-2021 Africa Twin DCT models; a 2018-2021 GL1800 crankshaft bolt and a 2024-2025 ADV160 oil pump gear complete the group. A separate timing belt recall and the 2021 Rebel 1100 radiator cap fill out the list.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The engine may run improperly or stall while driving (Acura and Honda connecting rod bearing recall).
  • Reduced engine performance, which can result in an engine stall (2020-2022 GL1800 Gold Wing ECU software recall).
  • Oil leaking onto the exhaust pipe or rear tire after the press-fit plug falls out (2023 Rebel 300 crank case cover recall, KP2).
  • Hot coolant leaking or spraying from an insufficiently sealed radiator cap (2021 Rebel 1100 recall, KN1).
  • A loss of drive power (2024-2025 ADV160 oil pump driven gear recall, KR9).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these eleven notices tells owners to stop driving or riding, and none tells them to park outside; none gives an instruction to follow before the repair. The notices state risks instead. The CBR600RR and CB1000SP notices cite rear wheel lockup and oil leaking onto hot exhaust components; the GL1800 primary drive gear bolt notice cites an engine stall with rear wheel lockup; and the connecting rod bearing notice cites an engine that may run improperly or stall, increasing the risk of a fire, crash or injury.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the CBR600RR engine assembly, the Rebel 300 crank case cover, the Rebel 1100 radiator cap, the GL1800 primary drive gear bolt, the crankcase sealing bolt on the XL750, CMX1100 and CRF1100, and the ADV160 oil pump driven gear. The connecting rod bearing, CB1000SP and timing belt recalls are inspect and repair or replace. The Gold Wing and Africa Twin DCT recalls are ECU software updates. All free of charge.

Common questions

Which Honda and Acura vehicles have the connecting rod bearing recall?

The notice lists 2015-2020 Acura TLX, 2016-2020 Acura MDX, 2016 and 2018-2019 Pilot, 2017 and 2019 Ridgeline, and 2018-2019 Odyssey. Dealers inspect and repair, or replace the engine as necessary, free of charge.

My CBR600RR is recalled but the repair is not available yet — what do the notices say?

The notice says interim letters about the safety risk were mailed February 9, 2026, and a second letter will follow once the remedy is available, anticipated October 31, 2026. The listed remedy is engine assembly replacement, free of charge.

Do any of these recalls tell riders to stop riding?

None of the notices in this group carries a stop-riding or park-outside instruction. They describe risks such as rear wheel lockup, engine stalls and oil reaching hot exhaust parts, and they direct owners to dealers for the free remedy.
