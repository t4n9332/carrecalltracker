Why do these Honda and Acura engines stall or leak oil?

11 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-18

Engine bearing and manufacturing defects repeat across this group: connecting rod bearings can wear and seize in 2018-2020 Acura MDX/TLX and Honda Odyssey/Pilot/Ridgeline, cylinder head or piston defects can cause rod bearing failure and rear wheel lockup in the CBR600RR (2025-2026) and CB1000SP (2025), and a separate timing belt defect affects 2018-2019 Acura MDX/TLX and Honda Pilot/Odyssey/Ridgeline. Software errors can stall the engine on GL1800 Gold Wing (2020-2022, manual transmission) and CRF1100 Africa Twin (2020-2021, DCT) motorcycles. Oil leaks recur on motorcycles too: the CMX300's crank case cover, the Rebel 1100's radiator cap, and the shared crankcase sealing bolt on the CMX1100, CRF1100, and XL750.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine runs improperly or stalls (Acura MDX/TLX, Honda Odyssey/Pilot/Ridgeline bearing wear)
  • Rear wheel locks up or oil leaks onto the rear tire (CBR600RR, CB1000SP)
  • Engine stalls while riding (GL1800 Gold Wing, CRF1100 Africa Twin DCT, GL1800 primary drive gear bolt)
  • Coolant leaking or spraying from the radiator cap (Rebel 1100)
  • Loss of drive power (ADV160 oil pump gear failure)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the 11 recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This includes the CBR600RR and CB1000SP recalls, where remedy parts are not yet available and only interim notification letters have been mailed - the notices do not tell owners to stop driving or park outside while waiting. For the remaining recalls, covering bearing, timing belt, software, and oil-leak issues, the notices likewise do not instruct owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across this group, dealers inspect and repair or replace the engine, connecting rod bearings, or timing belt; replace the crank case cover, radiator cap, or crankcase sealing bolt to stop oil or coolant leaks; replace the primary drive gear bolt or oil pump gear; or reprogram the engine control unit software. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Is it safe to keep riding my CBR600RR or CB1000SP while I wait for parts?

The notices for both recalls do not tell owners to stop riding or park outside. Honda has mailed interim letters explaining the rod bearing risk while the replacement engine assembly or repair procedure is still being finalized.

Why do so many different Honda and Acura models share the same bearing recall?

The 2018-2020 Acura MDX, Acura TLX, Honda Odyssey, Pilot, and Ridgeline share an engine platform, and a manufacturing error in the connecting rod bearing affects all of them, which is why one recall covers models across both brands and several model years.

What's causing Honda motorcycles to stall unexpectedly?

It varies by model: software errors in the ignition or clutch control stall the GL1800 Gold Wing and CRF1100 Africa Twin DCT, while a broken primary drive gear bolt stalls the GL1800 Goldwing (2018-2021) and can also lock the rear wheel.
