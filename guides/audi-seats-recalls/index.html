Audi Seat Recalls Cover Frame Connections and Passenger Airbag Deactivation

3 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Seats · model years 2018–2020 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 10, 2021

Audi’s seat recalls combine structural restraint problems with a passenger airbag detection fault. Two notices concern seat structure: 72L4 covers one 2020 Q3 whose driver’s seat frame may be incorrectly bolted to its rail, while the August 2020 notice covers certain 2020 Q5 and SQ5 vehicles with potentially improper front seat frame and backrest-adjuster welds. Recall 74E3 spans more model years, covering 2017–2020 A4 Sedan and Allroad vehicles, specified 2018–2019 RS5 variants, and 2018–2020 A5, S4, and S5 variants. Its seat-heater connection fault can cause software to disable the passenger airbag. The common concern is reduced occupant protection during a crash.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for Q3 seat connection recall 72L4, passenger occupant detection recall 74E3, and the August 2020 front seat weld recall do not tell owners to stop driving. They describe injury risks from a failed seat connection, a disabled passenger airbag, or a backrest adjustment that breaks in a rear-end crash. Those risk descriptions and the absence of a stop-driving instruction do not establish that continued driving is safe.

What the free repair involves

The remedies are free of charge. For 72L4, dealers inspect the bolt connection and replace the seat structure as necessary. For 74E3, they replace the connecting cable and either the heating mat or entire seat cover. For the August 2020 weld recall, dealers inspect front seats and replace seatback frames as necessary.

Common questions

How can an Audi seat heater connection affect the passenger airbag?

Recall 74E3 describes a contact fault in the cable connecting the seat heater to the Passenger Occupant Detection System. The fault can cause software to misdiagnose a malfunction and disable the passenger airbag, increasing injury risk during a crash.

What does the Q3 driver seat recall require dealers to repair?

Recall 72L4 concerns one 2020 Audi Q3 whose driver’s seat frame may have an incorrect bolt connection to the seat rail. Dealers inspect that connection and replace the seat structure as necessary, free of charge.

Which Audi SUVs are listed in the front seat weld recall?

The August 2020 notice identifies certain 2020 Audi Q5 and SQ5 vehicles. Improper welds involving the front seat frame and backrest adjuster may allow the backrest adjustment to break in a rear-end crash. Dealers inspect and replace seatback frames as necessary, free.
