What seat defects have led to Audi recalls?

3 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Seats · model years 2018–2020 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2021-11-10

Three Audi seat recalls involve unrelated defects. On one 2020 Q3 vehicle, the driver's seat frame was incorrectly bolted to the seat rail, a bolt connection that could fail in a crash. On 2018-2020 A4, A5, S4, and S5 models, and 2018-2019 RS5, a faulty cable connecting the seat heater to the Passenger Occupant Detection System can cause the system to misdiagnose a fault and disable the passenger air bag. On 2020 Q5 and SQ5 (shared with 2020 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport, Jetta, and Tiguan, and 2021 Atlas), parts of the front seat frame and backrest adjuster may not have been welded properly, which in a rear-end crash could let the backrest break.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No warning described for the Q3 driver's seat bolt connection (2020, one vehicle)
  • No warning described for the passenger air bag disable caused by the seat heater cable fault (2018-2020 A4/A5/S4/S5, 2018-2019 RS5)
  • No warning described for the front seat frame weld defect (2020 Q5/SQ5 and shared Volkswagen models)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three seat recalls include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the Q3 seat bolt recall, the A4/A5/S4/S5/RS5 seat heater cable recall, and the Q5/SQ5 seat frame weld recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The notices describe increased risk of injury in a crash — from a failed seat bolt, a disabled passenger air bag, or a broken backrest in a rear-end crash — but none of the three direct owners to stop driving or park outside while awaiting repair.

What the free repair involves

Repairs differ by recall: dealers inspect the seat bolt connection and replace the seat structure if needed (Q3), replace the connecting cable and the seat heater mat or cover (A4/A5/S4/S5/RS5), and inspect the front seats and replace the seatback frames as necessary (Q5/SQ5 and related Volkswagen models). All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Are these three Audi seat recalls related to each other?

No. Each involves a different defect: an incorrectly bolted seat frame on one 2020 Q3, a seat heater cable fault that can disable the passenger air bag on 2018-2020 A4/A5/S4/S5 and 2018-2019 RS5, and a seat frame weld issue on 2020 Q5/SQ5.

Does the seat heater cable recall affect the driver's air bag?

No. The notice describes the faulty cable disabling the passenger air bag through the Passenger Occupant Detection System, not the driver's air bag, on affected 2018-2020 A4, A5, S4, and S5, and 2018-2019 RS5 vehicles.

How many Audi Q3 vehicles were affected by the seat bolt recall?

According to the recall notice, only one 2020 Audi Q3 vehicle was affected. The driver's seat frame may have been incorrectly bolted to the seat rail, and dealers inspect the connection and replace the seat structure if necessary, free of charge.
