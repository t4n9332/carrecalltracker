Audi seat recalls: seat frames, welds and a disabled air bag

3 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Seats · model years 2018–2020 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 10, 2021

Three recalls, three different seat problems, two of them structural. In an August 2020 recall, Volkswagen says parts of the front seat frame and backrest adjuster may not have been welded properly on 2020 Audi Q5 and SQ5 vehicles, alongside 2020 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport, Jetta, Jetta GLI and Tiguan and 2021 Atlas models. Recall 72L4 is far narrower: one 2020 Q3 whose driver's seat frame may have incorrectly been bolted to the seat rail. Recall 74E3 is electrical rather than structural and reaches the widest, covering certain 2017-2020 A4 Allroad and A4 Sedan, 2018-2020 A5 Cabriolet, A5 Coupe, A5 Sportback, S4 Sedan and S5 variants, and 2018-2019 RS5 Coupe and 2019 RS5 Sportback vehicles, where a seat heater cable may disable the passenger air bag.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none carries a park-outside instruction. The 2020 weld recall says that in a rear-end crash the backrest adjustment could break, possibly reducing the amount of occupant restraint. Recall 72L4 says the incorrect bolt connection could fail, increasing injury risk in a crash. Recall 74E3 says a disabled passenger air bag increases the risk of injury in a crash. Beyond arranging the free repair, the notices give owners no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

All three repairs are free of charge. Under the 2020 weld recall, dealers inspect the front seats and, as necessary, replace the seatback frames. Under recall 72L4, dealers inspect the bolt connection and, as necessary, replace the seat structure. Under recall 74E3, dealers replace the connecting cable and either the heating mat or the entire seat cover. None of the notices describes a software or over-the-air fix.

Common questions

Why is a seat heater cable treated as a safety recall?

Recall 74E3 says the cable connecting the seat heater to the Passenger Occupant Detection System may have a contact fault, causing the software to misdiagnose a malfunction and disable the passenger air bag, which increases the risk of injury in a crash.

How many vehicles does the Q3 seat bolt recall cover?

Audi's notice for recall 72L4 describes one 2020 Q3 vehicle, whose driver's seat frame may have incorrectly been bolted to the seat rail. Dealers inspect the bolt connection and replace the seat structure as necessary, free of charge.

Does the seat weld recall cover both Audi and Volkswagen?

The notice lists 2020 Audi Q5 and SQ5 together with 2020 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport, Jetta, Jetta GLI and Tiguan and 2021 Atlas vehicles. Audi owners are given customer service at 1-800-253-2834 and Volkswagen owners 1-888-241-2289.
