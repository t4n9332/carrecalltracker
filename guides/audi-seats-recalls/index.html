Audi seat recalls: passenger airbag shutoff, weak backrests, loose frames

3 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Seats · model years 2018–2020 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2021-11-10

These three Audi seat recalls are very different in scale. The largest covers 2017-2020 A4 Allroad and A4 Sedan, 2018-2020 A5 Cabriolet, Coupe and Sportback, S4, S5 and 2018-2019 RS5 models, where the cable linking the seat heater to the passenger occupant detection system can leave the front passenger airbag switched off. A smaller campaign covers 2020 Q5 and SQ5 models, sharing a VW Atlas, Jetta and Tiguan defect where the front seat frame and backrest adjuster may not have been welded properly, which matters in a rear-end crash. The third involves a single 2020 Q3 whose driver's seat frame may have been bolted to the seat rail incorrectly.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A passenger airbag 'off' indication with an adult sitting in the front passenger seat (A4, A5, S4, S5 and RS5 occupant detection cable).
  • Odd behavior from the front passenger seat heater, since the recalled cable runs between the seat heater and the occupant detection system.
  • A front backrest that feels loose or flexes more than it should (2020 Q5 and SQ5 welding recall).
  • No warning at all in the single 2020 Q3 — the notice describes a bolted connection that would only fail in a crash.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls tells owners to stop driving or park outside, and none affects how the car steers, stops or accelerates. Each matters only in a crash: a front passenger left without airbag protection, a backrest that could give way in a rear-end impact, or a driver's seat frame that could fail at its rail. That makes them prompt-appointment items rather than emergencies. Audi notes the single Q3's bolt inspection should be done right away, even though the vehicle may still be driven.

What the free repair involves

All three repairs are free. On the A4, A5, S4, S5 and RS5, dealers replace the cable connecting the seat heater to the passenger occupant detection system, plus either the heating mat or the entire seat cover. On the 2020 Q5 and SQ5, they inspect the front seats and replace seatback frames as necessary. On the one Q3, they inspect the bolt connection and replace the seat structure if needed.

Common questions

My passenger airbag light seems normal. Is my A4 still affected?

Possibly. The recall is defined by VIN and covers the cable between the seat heater and the passenger occupant detection system, so the fault need not be visible every time you drive. Confirm with Audi at 1-800-253-2834.

Only one Q3 was recalled. Should I be worried about mine?

That campaign covers a single 2020 Q3 whose driver's seat frame may have been bolted to the rail incorrectly. If your VIN is not listed, no action is needed; Audi's customer service line can confirm it.

Is it safe to carry a front passenger before the airbag repair?

The notice does not restrict driving, but a front passenger could be left without airbag protection in a crash, which is why Audi asks owners to have the cable and seat heater components replaced promptly, free of charge.
