Why do Ford doors and tailgates open unexpectedly?

6 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Latches/locks/linkages · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-05-09

Ford's latch and lock recalls in this group center on two recurring failure points: rear doors that can open even when the child safety lock is set to ON, and tailgates that release unexpectedly. The Bronco has been recalled three separate times for the same rear door child-lock problem, covering 2021-2022, 2023-2024, and 2024 model year four-door Broncos. Tailgates are the other repeat issue: 2018 F-150 trucks had latches that could unlatch while driving, and 2019 Super Duty F-250, F-350, and F-450 trucks had electric tailgate release switches that water could short-circuit. A separate 2018 Edge and Lincoln MKX recall involved door striker bolts that were shorter than intended, which could let doors open in a crash.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A rear door can be opened from inside even when the child safety lock is set to ON (Bronco)
  • A tailgate unlatches or opens on its own while driving (F-150, Super Duty)
  • A door may not stay closed in a crash due to a short striker bolt (Edge, MKX)
  • The notices describe no advance warning sign before these failures occur

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the six recalls in this group — covering the 2018 Edge and Lincoln MKX door striker bolts, the 2018 F-150 and 2019 Super Duty tailgate latches, and the three Bronco rear door child-lock recalls (2021-2022, 2023-2024, and 2024) — carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For all of these, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across this group, dealers replace the affected part: door striker bolts on the Edge and MKX, tailgate latches on the F-150, tailgate wiring and release switches on the Super Duty trucks, and child safety lock or door latch assemblies on the Bronco. Ford also inspects components first on some Bronco recalls before replacing them. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why does my Bronco's rear door open even with the child lock on?

Ford recalled 2021-2022, 2023-2024, and 2024 four-door Broncos because the passenger-side or left-side rear door could be opened from inside even when the child safety lock was set to ON; dealers inspect and replace the lock or door latch as needed.

Can my F-150 tailgate open by itself while I'm driving?

Yes. Ford recalled certain 2018 F-150 trucks with manual and power release tailgates because the latches could unlatch unexpectedly while driving, letting unrestrained cargo fall from the bed; dealers replace both tailgate latches free of charge.

What was wrong with the door bolts on my Edge or MKX?

Ford recalled 2018 Edge and Lincoln MKX vehicles because the door striker bolts were shorter than intended, which could let a door open in a crash; dealers replace the bolts free of charge.
