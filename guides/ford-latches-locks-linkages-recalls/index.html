Why do Ford doors, tailgates and child locks keep getting recalled?

6 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Latches/locks/linkages · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 9, 2025

Two patterns run through this group. Child safety locks on four-door Broncos account for three recalls: the passenger-side rear door on 2021-2022 models, and the left-side rear door on 2023-2024 models and again on 2024 models, all able to be opened from inside with the lock switched ON. Tailgates account for two: latches that may unlatch while driving on 2018 F-150 trucks with manual and power release tailgates, and water entering the wiring and short-circuiting the release switch on 2017-2019 Super Duty F-250, F-350 and F-450. The remaining recall covers 2018 Ford Edge and Lincoln MKX door striker bolts that are shorter than intended, which may let doors open in a crash.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2021-2022, 2023-2024 and 2024 Bronco recalls describe a rear door that can be opened from inside the vehicle while the child safety lock is in the "ON" position.
  • The 2018 F-150 recall describes tailgate latches that may unlatch unexpectedly while driving.
  • The 2017-2019 Super Duty recall describes a short circuit activating the switch and releasing the tailgate latches.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the six notices in this group tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. That applies to the 2018 Edge and Lincoln MKX door striker bolts, the 2018 F-150 tailgate latches, the 2017-2019 Super Duty tailgate release switch, and all three Bronco child safety lock recalls covering 2021-2022, 2023-2024 and 2024 models. The notices describe what can happen — doors opening in a crash, a tailgate opening and cargo falling out, a rear door opening with the child lock on — but give owners no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is free of charge. Dealers replace the door striker bolts on 2018 Edge and MKX, and both tailgate latches on the 2018 F-150. On 2017-2019 Super Duty trucks they modify the tailgate wiring harnesses with jumper pigtails and install a new tailgate handle release switch. For the Bronco recalls dealers inspect the child safety locks and replace the latch as necessary.

Common questions

Ford has recalled the Bronco child safety lock three times — what do the notices say?

The 2021-2022 Bronco notice covers the passenger-side rear door; the 2023-2024 and the 2024 notices cover the left-side rear door. Each says the door may be opened from inside with the child safety lock in the "ON" position.

Can I still haul cargo if my tailgate is under recall?

The 2018 F-150 and 2017-2019 Super Duty notices say an unexpected tailgate opening may allow unrestrained cargo to fall out of the truck bed and become a road hazard. Neither notice gives owners any instruction beyond having the recall repair performed.

What do I pay for a door latch or striker bolt repair?

The notices for all six recalls state the repair is free of charge, whether dealers replace door striker bolts, tailgate latches, a tailgate release switch and wiring, or inspect the child safety locks and replace a rear door latch.
