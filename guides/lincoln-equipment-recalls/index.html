Why Are Some Lincoln Vehicles Recalled Over Equipment Defects?

3 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Equipment · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-10-10

Lincoln's equipment recalls in this group are not mechanical wear issues but manufacturing and documentation gaps. One recall covers 2016-2019 Lincoln MKC and 2020-2022 Lincoln Corsair (alongside several Ford models) whose engine block heater can crack, leak coolant, and short circuit when plugged in, raising a fire risk. The other two recalls are paperwork defects: 2018-2023 Lincoln Navigator owner's manuals were missing instructions for adjusting or removing third-row head restraints, and 2018-2024 Lincoln Navigator, plus Aviator and Corsair, vehicles were missing the required airbag warning label on the dashboard. Both compliance issues involve federal safety standards on restraints, not a broken part.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Visible coolant leaking from the block heater near where its cord plugs in
  • No dashboard warning light or alert is described for any of these three recalls
  • The missing manual pages and missing airbag label are not something you would notice while driving

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the block heater recall, owners are advised not to plug in the block heater until it has been remedied, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving the vehicle itself. The head-restraint manual recall and the missing airbag label recall are compliance issues, and their notices likewise do not tell owners to stop driving. Across all three recalls in this group, the notices do not instruct owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Ford will replace the block heater or, as an alternative, install a blanking plug and remove the block heater cord, both free of charge. For the two documentation recalls, Ford will mail owners either an owner's manual addendum with head-restraint instructions or a replacement airbag warning label, also at no cost. No dealer visit is required for the manual or label fixes.

Common questions

Is the Lincoln block heater fire risk recall dangerous?

The notice says a cracked block heater can leak coolant and short circuit when plugged in, increasing fire risk. Owners are told not to plug in the block heater until it's repaired, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

Do I need to bring my Lincoln Navigator to a dealer for the head restraint recall?

No. Ford mails owners an addendum to the owner's manual with instructions for adjusting and removing the third-row head restraints; no dealer appointment or vehicle inspection is required for this recall.

What is the missing airbag label recall about on my Lincoln?

Certain Lincoln Navigator, Aviator, and Corsair vehicles are missing the required dashboard airbag warning label. Ford mails owners a replacement label free of charge; the notice does not describe any mechanical defect.
