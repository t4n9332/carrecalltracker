Lincoln block heater fire risk and missing safety label recalls

3 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Equipment · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-10-10

All three Lincoln equipment recalls come from Ford, and only one involves a part that can fail. The engine block heater recall covers 2016-2019 Lincoln MKC and 2020-2022 Corsair alongside their Ford cousins; the heater can crack, leak coolant and catch fire. The other two are documentation problems on big Lincoln SUVs: 2018-2023 Navigator owner's manuals lack instructions for adjusting or removing the center third-row head restraints, and 2018-2024 Navigator plus 2020-2025 Aviator and 2020-2024 Corsair vehicles were built without the dashboard air bag warning label, failing federal labeling rules. Navigator appears in both paperwork recalls, and cold-climate Lincolns with block heaters carry the only fire risk.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Coolant leaking or wet spots near the engine block heater
  • No air bag warning label on the dashboard
  • No head restraint adjustment or removal instructions in the owner's manual
  • The two paperwork recalls produce no driving symptom at all — you can only spot them by checking the dash and the manual

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls tells Lincoln owners to stop driving or park outside. For the block heater recall, Ford's only restriction is that you must not plug the block heater in until the replacement is done; the vehicle itself can be driven normally. The missing head restraint instructions and the missing dashboard air bag label are compliance problems, not broken parts, so those vehicles are safe to keep driving — though a badly positioned head restraint could add injury risk in a crash.

What the free repair involves

Only one of these recalls involves shop work: dealers replace the engine block heater, with an alternative option to replace the heater element instead. For the other two, Ford mails you the missing paperwork — an owner's manual addendum covering the center head restraints, and the dashboard air bag warning label. All three remedies are free of charge.

Common questions

Can I still plug in the block heater this winter?

No. Ford tells owners not to plug the block heater in until the vehicle is repaired, because a cracked heater can leak coolant and catch fire. Driving the vehicle itself is fine; only the heater is off limits.

Do I need a dealer visit for the missing label and manual pages?

No. Ford mails the dashboard air bag warning label and the owner's manual addendum for the center head restraints directly to owners, free of charge. If yours never arrived, call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.

Which Lincolns are covered by the block heater recall?

Ford lists 2016-2019 Lincoln MKC and 2020-2022 Lincoln Corsair alongside several Ford models. Only vehicles built with an engine block heater are affected, so check your VIN with Ford before assuming yours qualifies for the free replacement.
