Lincoln equipment recalls: block heater fire risk and missing safety labels

3 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Equipment · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 10, 2025

The three Lincoln recalls filed under equipment fall into two very different kinds. One is a fire risk: the engine block heater on the 2016-2019 MKC and the 2020-2022 Corsair may crack and develop a coolant leak, causing a short circuit when the heater is plugged in. The other two are labeling and documentation problems on the Navigator. On 2018-2023 Navigators with third-row seating, the owner's manual does not include instructions for adjusting or removing certain head restraints. On 2018-2024 Navigators, along with the 2020-2025 Aviator and 2020-2024 Corsair, the air bag warning label may be missing from the dashboard. Both label recalls are recorded as failures to comply with a Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. For the block heater recall covering the 2016-2019 MKC and 2020-2022 Corsair, the notice advises owners not to plug in their block heater until the vehicle is remedied. For the 2018-2023 Navigator owner's manual recall and the missing dashboard air bag label recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Both of those remedies are mailed to owners rather than performed at a dealer.

What the free repair involves

For the block heater recall, dealers replace the block heater free of charge, and owners also have an alternative option to replace the block heater element with a threaded blanking plug and remove the block heater electrical cord. For the two label recalls, Ford mails owners the head restraint owner's manual addendum and the dashboard air bag warning label, both free of charge.

Common questions

Can I use the engine block heater while I wait for the repair?

The notice advises owners not to plug in their block heater until the vehicle is remedied. It covers the 2016-2019 Lincoln MKC and 2020-2022 Corsair, where a cracked heater can leak coolant and short circuit.

Do I need to visit a dealer for the missing air bag label?

The notice says Ford will mail owners the warning labels, free of charge, for the 2020-2025 Aviator, 2020-2024 Corsair and 2018-2024 Navigator. It does not describe a dealer repair for this recall.

Why is a missing owner's manual page a safety recall?

The notice says the manual for 2018-2023 Navigators with third-row seating lacks instructions for adjusting or removing certain head restraints, so the vehicles do not comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 202, and restraints may be improperly adjusted.
