What are the Mercedes-Benz tire recalls, and which vans are affected?

3 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Tires · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-10-18

These three Mercedes-Benz tire recalls split between commercial vans and one SUV. Two come from Daimler Vans USA: 2019 Sprinter 1500 and 4500 vans fitted with winter tires that may not have been correctly installed, and Metris vans, listed for 2018 and described in the notice as 2016-2018, whose value for the maximum combined load weight capacity of the tires is incorrect, failing Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 110. The third covers 2025 GLB 250, GLB 250 4MATIC and GLB 35 4MATIC models, where the right-side front and rear tires may have sidewall damage. The repeating theme is tires mounted wrong, labelled with the wrong load value, or damaged, with Sprinter and Metris vans accounting for two of the three.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Reduced traction in wet or snowy conditions — stated in the 2019 Sprinter winter tire recall.
  • A sudden loss of tire pressure — stated in the 2025 GLB sidewall damage recall.
  • The Metris tire load label notice does not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2019 Sprinter winter-tire recall, the Metris tire load label recall and the 2025 GLB sidewall damage recall all state that the notice does not tell owners to stop driving. What they do state is the risk: reduced traction in wet or snowy conditions on the Sprinter, tire failure from an overloaded vehicle on the Metris, and a sudden loss of tire pressure on the GLB. None of the three gives owners an interim instruction to follow before the dealer work is done.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the running direction of the winter tires on the 2019 Sprinter and correct them as necessary, replacing tires with uneven tread wear. On the Metris, dealers install corrected labels on the B-pillar and fuel filler door and inspect and replace incorrect tires as necessary. On the 2025 GLB, dealers inspect and replace the tires as necessary. Each notice says the work is free of charge.

Common questions

My 2019 Sprinter has winter tires — what will the dealer actually check?

The notice says dealers inspect the running direction of the tires and correct them as necessary. If the tread wear is uneven, the tires are replaced. All services are performed free of charge, and the recall began May 15, 2020.

Why does an incorrect label matter on my Metris?

The notice says the value for the maximum combined load weight capacity of the tires is incorrect, so the van fails Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 110. An overloaded vehicle may cause tire failure, increasing the risk of a crash.

Does my 2025 GLB need new tires?

The notice says the right-side front and rear tires may have sidewall damage, which can result in a sudden loss of tire pressure. Dealers will inspect and replace the tires as necessary, free of charge. Letters were mailed November 22, 2024.
