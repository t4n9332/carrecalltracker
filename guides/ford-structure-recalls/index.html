Ford Structure Recalls Cover Detaching Trim, Loose Joints, and Protection

20 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Structure · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 20, 2026

Detached exterior parts and structural protection recur across these Ford notices. The May 2021 and July 2026 Explorer roof-rail notices both concern 2016-2019 vehicles. Other detachment notices cover 2017-2019 Flex B-pillar door trim and 2022-2026 Bronco fender flares. Super Duty appears in several distinct problems: pretensioner sparks near carpet in 2017-2019 SuperCrew trucks, loose underbody insulators contacting aluminum driveshafts in certain 2017-2022 F-250/F-350 trucks, and missing heat protection in 2021 F-650/F-750 trucks. Separate recalls address 2018 F-150 Raptor roof-brace rivets, 2022 Explorer/Aviator engine rails, modified Transit glass hatches, 2026 vehicle joints, and liftgate deck pins on one 2025 F-650 SD.

Warning signs to watch for

  • July 2020 F-650/F-750 heat-protection recall: cabin floor areas and seat attachments may reach elevated temperatures.
  • May 2021 Explorer roof-rail recall: roof-rail covers may detach.
  • October 2025 Flex door-trim recall: front B-pillar door trim may detach while driving.
  • June 2026 Bronco fender-flare recall: fender flares may detach.
  • July 2026 Explorer roof-rail recall: roof-rail covers may come loose and detach.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the supplied structure notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices concerning Super Duty pretensioner sparks, loose vehicle joints, Raptor roof rivets, Transit glass hatches, truck heat protection, Explorer roof covers, Super Duty insulators and driveshafts, Explorer/Aviator engine rails, Flex trim, Bronco fender flares, and F-650 liftgate pins do not tell owners to stop driving. The supplied notices describe crash, injury, fire, or road-hazard risks and specify remedies, but do not provide an instruction declaring continued driving safe.

What the free repair involves

Free repairs differ by recall. The May 2021 Explorer recall installs push-pins and replaces damaged clips or covers as necessary; July 2026 specifies inspection and necessary repairs or replacements. Recall 22S09 inspects and repairs the driveshaft as necessary and secures insulators. The Flex recall repairs or replaces trim as necessary. Recall 26S47 inspects and repairs or replaces fender flares as necessary.

Common questions

What do the two Explorer roof-rail notices say dealers will do?

The May 2021 notice specifies installing push-pins and replacing damaged rail clips and covers as necessary. The July 2026 notice specifies inspecting covers and repairing or replacing push-pins, clips, or covers as necessary. Both describe the work as free.

Why does the Super Duty structure recall include a driveshaft inspection?

The February 2022 notice says underbody heat and noise insulators may loosen, contact the aluminum driveshaft, and damage it. For affected gasoline-powered 2017-2022 F-250/F-350 vehicles, dealers inspect and repair the driveshaft as necessary and properly attach the insulators, free.

Does the Explorer and Aviator engine-rail recall offer a buyback?

The June 2022 notice for certain 2022 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator vehicles specifies a free engine-rail strength test and rail replacement as necessary. If replacement is required, owners may choose vehicle replacement or buyback; the supplied text does not describe those terms.
