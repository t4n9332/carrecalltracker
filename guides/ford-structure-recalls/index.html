Which Ford recalls cover trim, roofs and body parts detaching?

18 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Structure · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 20, 2026

Parts coming off the outside of the vehicle are the main thread here. Roof rail covers on 2016-2019 Explorers appear in recalls dated 2021 and 2026, B-pillar door trim can detach on 2017-2019 Flex, and fender flares can detach on 2022-2026 Bronco. A second thread is missing or loose underbody protection on trucks: heat shields not installed on 2021 F-650 and F-750 with the 7.3L gasoline engine, and heat and noise insulators loosening onto the aluminum driveshaft of 2017-2022 F-250 and F-350 gasoline trucks. The rest stand alone — seat belt pretensioner sparks on 2017-2019 Super Duty SuperCrew, missing roof brace rivets on the 2018 F-150 Raptor, improperly heat-treated engine rails on 2022 Explorer and Aviator, a Transit glass roof hatch, and Super Duty window software.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2017-2019 Flex: the driver and front passenger B-pillar door trim may detach while driving.
  • 2016-2019 Explorer: the roof rail covers may come loose and detach from the vehicle.
  • 2022-2026 Bronco: the fender flares may not be properly secured and may detach from the vehicle.
  • 2021 F-650 and F-750 with 7.3L gasoline engines: the interior cabin floor and seat attachments may reach elevated temperatures in certain driving conditions.
  • 2023 Super Duty F-250 through F-550 (23S37): a front door window may not automatically reverse when it meets an obstruction near the top edge.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside — including the 2017-2019 Super Duty SuperCrew recall over seat belt pretensioner sparks that could ignite carpeting or insulation in the B-pillar area. Several describe letters sent before a repair was ready: interim letters for the 2017-2019 Flex door trim were mailed December 17, 2025, with the remedy anticipated March 2027, and interim letters for the 2016-2019 Explorer roof rail covers were mailed August 28, 2026.

What the free repair involves

Remedies are specific to each recall: rivets for the F-150 Raptor roof braces, heat-resistant tape and modified insulation on Super Duty SuperCrew, missing heat shields fitted to F-650 and F-750, driveshaft repair and reattached insulators on F-250 and F-350, roof rail push-pins, clips and covers, Flex trim and Bronco flares repaired or replaced, Transit hatch lids replaced, and a Super Duty window software update — all free of charge.

Common questions

What happens if my 2022 Explorer or Aviator engine rails fail the test?

The notice says dealers perform an engine rail strength test and replace the rails as necessary, free of charge. If the engine rails require replacement, owners will have the option for a vehicle replacement or buyback.

Why are 2016-2019 Explorer roof rail covers listed in two recalls?

The 2021 notice describes retention pins loosening so the covers detach, with dealers installing push-pins and replacing damaged rail clips and covers. The 2026 notice describes covers coming loose and detaching, with inspection and repair or replacement of push-pins, clips or covers.

Is the Ford Flex door trim repair available yet?

The notice says interim letters explaining the safety risk were mailed December 17, 2025, and that additional letters will be sent once the remedy is available, anticipated March 2027. Dealers will then repair or replace the B-pillar trim, free of charge.
