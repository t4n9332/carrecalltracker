Ford Structure Recalls Cover Loose Trim, Roofs, and Truck Components

19 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Structure · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 20, 2026

Loose or detachable exterior parts recur in these Ford notices: 2016-2019 Explorer roof rail covers appear in two recalls, while 2017-2019 Flex B-pillar trim and 2022-2026 Bronco fender flares can detach. Super Duty trucks feature several distinct problems, including pretensioner sparks that may ignite carpeting or insulation in certain 2017-2019 SuperCrew Cab trucks and loose underbody insulators that can damage aluminum driveshafts on certain 2017-2022 F-250 and F-350 trucks. Other notices cover missing Raptor roof-brace rivets, Transit glass roof hatches, missing F-650/F-750 heat protection, Explorer engine rails, an F-650 liftgate, and tailgate cables. Explorer and Super Duty models recur most often among the supplied notices.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2020-07-17 F-650/F-750 missing heat shields recall: the cabin floor and seat attachments may reach elevated temperatures.
  • 2022-02-16 F-250/F-350 loose insulators recall: a fractured driveshaft can cause loss of drive power or unintended movement while parked.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the 12 supplied notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for Super Duty pretensioner fire risk, Raptor roof rivets, Transit roof hatches, F-650/F-750 heat shields, both Explorer roof rail cover recalls, F-250/F-350 underbody insulators, Explorer/Aviator engine rails, Flex door trim, Bronco fender flares, the F-650 liftgate, and tailgate cables do not tell owners to stop driving. That is the extent of their guidance about continuing to drive; these notices provide no statement establishing that an affected vehicle is safe to drive.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies vary by recall. Dealers install Raptor roof-brace rivets, replace Transit hatch lids, and install missing F-650/F-750 heat-protection components. For the 2022 underbody-insulator recall, dealers inspect and repair the driveshaft as necessary and properly attach the insulators. The 2026 tailgate recall replaces the left-side cable. Explorer engine rails receive a strength test and replacement as necessary.

Common questions

What repairs do the two Explorer roof rail cover notices describe?

The 2021 notice calls for installing push-pins and replacing damaged rail clips and covers as necessary. The 2026 notice calls for inspecting covers and repairing or replacing push-pins, rail clips, or covers as necessary. Both specify free repairs.

Which Super Duty trucks have the seat belt pretensioner fire recall?

The December 2019 notice covers certain 2017-2019 F-250, F-350, F-450, and F-550 SuperCrew Cab vehicles with carpet flooring. Dealers apply heat-resistant tape to the carpet and insulation and modify B-pillar insulation as necessary, free of charge.

What happens if my 2022 Explorer needs replacement engine rails?

The June 2022 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator notice specifies a free engine rail strength test and rail replacement as necessary. If the rails require replacement, owners have the option of a vehicle replacement or buyback under that notice.
