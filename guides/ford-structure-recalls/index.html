What structural and trim defects has Ford recalled vehicles for?

18 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Structure · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-20

These Ford structural recalls center on parts that can detach or fail without a crash. Roof rail covers on the 2018-2019 Explorer were recalled twice, in 2021 and again in 2026, for the same detachment risk. Trim and cladding also comes loose on the 2018-2019 Flex (door trim) and 2022-2024 Bronco (fender flares). Other recalls involve missing or insufficient hardware: rivets on the 2018 F-150 Raptor roof, heat shields on the 2021 F-650/F-750, and driveshaft insulators on the 2019-2022 F-250/F-350. A 2019 Super Duty recall addresses a fire risk from seat belt pretensioner sparks, and none of these recalls tell owners to stop driving.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Roof rail covers, door trim, or fender flares feel loose or detach while driving
  • Cabin floor or seat mounting areas feel unusually hot in certain driving conditions (F-650/F-750)
  • A power window fails to automatically reverse when it hits an obstruction (Super Duty)
  • Most other defects in this group, like the seat belt fire risk, driveshaft damage, or engine rail issues, are described with no noticeable warning before failure

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. That includes the Super Duty seat belt fire-risk recall, the Explorer roof rail cover recalls (2021 and 2026), the Flex door trim and Bronco fender flare recalls, the F-650/F-750 heat shield recall, the F-250/F-350 driveshaft recall, the Explorer/Aviator engine rail recall, the F-650 liftgate recall, and the Super Duty window recall. For all of these, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers repair or replace the affected part free of charge: installing missing rivets or heat shields, replacing roof rail covers, door trim, or fender flares, repairing the driveshaft and its insulators, replacing engine rails (with a buyback option in one case), and updating door module software to fix the window reversal issue.

Common questions

Why was the Ford Explorer's roof rail cover recalled twice?

The 2018-2019 Explorer was first recalled in 2021 for roof rail covers that could detach, then recalled again in 2026 for the same detachment risk involving push-pins, rail clips, and roof rail covers.

Is the Super Duty seat belt recall only a fire risk in a crash?

Yes. The notice says sparks from a deploying seat belt pretensioner could ignite carpet or insulation if the pretensioner fires during a crash — it does not describe a fire risk while driving normally.

Do any of these structural recalls tell me to stop driving?

No. None of the recalls in this Ford structure group include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, though several involve parts, like fender flares or roof rail covers, that can detach and become a road hazard.
