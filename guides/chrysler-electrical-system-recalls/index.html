Why do Chrysler electrical recalls keep pointing at the Pacifica?

9 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · Electrical System · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 4, 2026

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Almost every recall in this group traces back to the Pacifica, and most of them to the plug-in hybrid. The 12-volt isolator post appears twice: a high-resistance connection on 2017-2020 Pacifica PHEVs and an incorrectly installed isolator post on 2023 Pacifica PHEVs, both described as fire risks, while 2020-2022 Pacifica PHEVs were recalled over a battery pack that may cause a fire. A second thread is unexpected stalls and shutdowns: a powertrain control module chip across many 2017-2019 Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and RAM models, a loose battery ground on 2017-2019 Pacificas with stop/start, and a transmission wiring connector on 2017-2023 Pacifica PHEVs. Cruise control software, an instrument cluster that may not illuminate warnings, and a Manual Park Release plug on 2017-2018 Pacificas complete the group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Cruise control that does not disengage, with the vehicle maintaining its speed or accelerating (2018 cruise control recall).
  • A stall or a no-start condition (2018 powertrain control module recall).
  • An intermittent loss of power steering assist and/or a stall (2019 Pacifica battery ground recall).
  • An unexpected engine shutdown (2023 Pacifica plug-in hybrid transmission wiring connector recall).
  • Driver warnings in the instrument cluster that do not properly illuminate (2019 Chrysler 300, Challenger and Charger cluster recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two notices carry a park-outside instruction. For 2023 Pacifica plug-in hybrids with the incorrectly installed 12-volt isolator post, owners are told to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. For 2020-2022 Pacifica plug-in hybrids with the battery pack defect, owners are told not to charge the vehicle and to park outside and away from structures until it is repaired. The cruise control notice advises owners to stop using cruise control. None of the nine notices tells owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers increase torque and add a waterproof gasket to the 12-volt isolator post on 2017-2020 Pacifica PHEVs, correct the isolator on 2023 Pacificas, and update battery pack control module software, replacing the pack as necessary, on 2020-2022 models. Other recalls bring software flashes, a replacement powertrain control module, a new Manual Park Release plug, or a cleaned battery ground. Each notice that mentions cost says the work is free.

Common questions

Do all of these recalls involve the Pacifica Hybrid?

Four of the nine notices cover only Pacifica plug-in hybrids — the two 12-volt isolator post recalls, the battery pack recall and the transmission wiring connector recall. The rest cover non-hybrid Pacificas or other Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and RAM models.

Can I still charge my Pacifica plug-in hybrid?

The 2020-2022 Pacifica plug-in hybrid battery pack notice tells owners not to charge their vehicles, and to park outside and away from structures, until the vehicle is repaired. No other notice in this group gives a charging instruction.

What do the notices say about using cruise control?

The 2018 cruise control recall, covering Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and RAM models, addresses a defect that could prevent the system from disengaging; that notice advises owners to stop using cruise control. It does not tell owners to stop driving, and dealers flash the engine or powertrain control module.
