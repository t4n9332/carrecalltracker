Why Does the Chrysler Pacifica Have So Many Electrical Recalls?

9 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · Electrical System · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-04

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Eight of the nine electrical recalls in this group involve the Chrysler Pacifica, and three of those are separate fire-risk recalls on the Pacifica Hybrid's 12-volt isolator or battery pack, dated 2020, 2023 and 2026. Stalling is the other recurring theme, with three different causes across three recalls: a powertrain control module chip (2018-2019, multiple Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram models), a loose battery ground connection (2018-2019 Pacifica), and a transmission wiring short (2018-2023 Pacifica Hybrid). The remaining recalls are a 2018-2019 cruise-control-cancel defect spanning many Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram models, a 2018 Pacifica manual park release defect, and a 2019 instrument cluster warning-light failure on the Chrysler 300, Dodge Challenger and Charger.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine stalling or a no-start condition (2018 powertrain control module recall).
  • Intermittent loss of power steering assist and/or stalling (2019 Pacifica battery ground recall).
  • Unexpected engine shutdown while driving (2023 Pacifica Hybrid wiring recall).
  • Cruise control that does not disengage when requested (2018 cruise-control recall).
  • Instrument cluster warning lights that fail to illuminate properly (2019 Chrysler 300/Challenger/Charger recall).

Is it safe to keep driving?

Two recalls in this group tell owners to park outside and away from structures until repaired: a 2023 recall and a 2026 recall, both covering the Pacifica Hybrid's 12-volt battery or battery pack. The 2026 recall also tells owners not to charge the vehicle. The 2018 cruise-control recall separately advises owners to stop using cruise control. None of the other six recalls in this group, including the earlier 2020 isolator fire recall, tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Repairs vary widely across this group: dealers install or replace 12-volt isolator components and battery pack hardware and software for the fire-risk recalls, flash or update software for the cruise-control, wiring and instrument-cluster recalls, replace the manual park release plug, and clean or reconnect a loose battery ground connection. Every repair described in these nine recalls is free of charge.

Common questions

Do I need to park my Chrysler Pacifica Hybrid outside because of a fire recall?

Two recalls in this group do tell owners to park outside and away from structures until repaired: a 2023 recall and a 2026 recall, both covering the Pacifica Hybrid's 12-volt battery or battery pack. The 2026 recall also tells owners not to charge the vehicle.

Why does the Chrysler Pacifica have so many electrical recalls?

Eight of the nine recalls in this group involve the Pacifica, covering different causes: three separate battery/isolator fire risks, three separate stalling causes, a manual park release defect, and a cruise-control defect. Each recall addresses a distinct problem.

What should I do if my Chrysler cruise control won't turn off?

The 2018 cruise-control recall notice advises owners to stop using cruise control. It covers many 2018-2019 Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram models with a possible short circuit that could prevent the system from disengaging.
