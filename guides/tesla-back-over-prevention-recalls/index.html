Why do Tesla rearview cameras go blank or show a delayed image?

5 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 5, 2026

All five of these Tesla recalls involve the same thing an owner relies on when backing up: the rearview camera image. Three describe a delayed image — SB-22-00-004 for 2018-2019 Model S and Model X and 2017-2020 Model 3 with Autopilot Computer 2.5, SB-24-00-016 for the 2024 Cybertruck, and SB-26-00-001 for 2017 and 2021-2023 Model 3, 2020-2023 Model Y, 2021-2023 Model S, and Model X running software version 2026.8.6. The January 2025 recall covers 2024-2025 Model 3 and Model S and 2023-2025 Model X and Model Y, where a computer circuit board may short and the image is lost. SB-22-00-009 covers 2021-2022 Model S and Model X and 2022 Model 3 and Model Y, where the infotainment CPU may lag or restart.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The rearview image may not immediately display when the vehicle begins to reverse (recall SB-22-00-004).
  • The rearview camera displays no image at all (the January 2025 circuit board recall).
  • The rearview camera image may be delayed when the vehicle is placed in reverse (recall SB-26-00-001).
  • The rearview camera image may be delayed after shifting into reverse on the Cybertruck (recall SB-24-00-016).
  • The center screen may not display the rearview camera image, gear selection, windshield visibility control settings or warning lights (recall SB-22-00-009).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. Each describes the same consequence in the notices' terms: a rearview image that is delayed or missing reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. SB-22-00-009 adds that a lagging or restarting CPU may also prevent the center screen from showing gear selection, windshield visibility control settings and warning lights. The notices give owners no interim instruction beyond having the recall remedy applied.

What the free repair involves

Four of these recalls are remedied with an over-the-air software update, free of charge: SB-22-00-004, SB-22-00-009 (which improves CPU temperature management), SB-24-00-016 and SB-26-00-001. The January 2025 recall was also addressed by an over-the-air update, and Tesla will additionally identify vehicles that experienced a circuit board failure, or stress that may lead to one, and replace the affected computers free of charge.

Common questions

Do I need a service center visit for these camera recalls?

For SB-22-00-004, SB-22-00-009, SB-24-00-016 and SB-26-00-001 the notices say Tesla performs or has released an over-the-air software update, free of charge. The January 2025 recall also involves replacing computers in vehicles Tesla identifies as having a circuit board failure or stress that may lead to one.

Is the Cybertruck camera recall the same as the Model 3 one?

Each is its own recall. SB-24-00-016 covers certain 2024 Cybertruck vehicles whose rearview image may be delayed after shifting into reverse. SB-26-00-001 covers certain Model 3, Model Y, Model S and Model X vehicles operating software version 2026.8.6 with a delayed rearview image.

My screen restarts while fast charging — which recall is that?

SB-22-00-009 covers certain 2021-2022 Model S and Model X and 2022 Model 3 and Model Y vehicles operating certain firmware releases, where the infotainment CPU may overheat during the preparation or process of fast-charging, causing it to lag or restart. Tesla's remedy is an over-the-air software update, free of charge.
