BMW Power Train Recalls Cover Transmission Failures and Reverse Gear Overheating

13 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Power Train · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 7, 2026

BMW motorcycle recalls repeatedly involve reverse gear overheating, transmission damage, and loss of drive power. The September 2024 and October 2026 R 18 notices cover certain 2021–2024 motorcycles; the April 2026 notice covers certain 2017–2025 K 1600 GT, GTL, and B motorcycles and 2018–2025 Grand America vehicles. Each describes moisture entering a reverse gear control unit through a deteriorating seal. Other motorcycle notices address clutch cover oil leakage, a loose final drive connection, gearbox shaft damage, and cardan shaft failure. Passenger vehicle notices cover transmission wiring damage in M5/M8 vehicles, a starter bolt in 2020 X5/X7 vehicles, and transmission leakage in certain 2022 and 2023 BMW models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • December 2019 M5/M8 transmission wiring recall: the transmission can shift to neutral, resulting in loss of propulsion.
  • August 2020 X5/X7 starter bolt recall: the engine may stall.
  • January 2023 motorcycle gearbox shaft recall: gearbox shaft damage may result in a blocked rear wheel.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the supplied BMW notices instructs owners to stop driving or park outside. The R 18 and K 1600 reverse gear notices describe fire risks from overheating control units. Other notices describe crash risks involving rear wheel locking, reduced motorcycle stability, engine stalling, rollaway, or loss of propulsion; the clutch cover notice also identifies a fire risk. These notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The September 2024 R 18 notice specifies disconnecting the reverse gear control unit as an interim remedy.

What the free repair involves

BMW specifies free, recall-specific repairs: reverse gear control unit replacement; clutch cover bolt, transmission component, pivot pin, or cardan shaft replacement; and an engine control software update for the January 2023 gearbox recall. M5/M8 harness repairs and the specified transmission mechatronics repairs are also free. The X5/X7 notice specifies starter inspection, bolt removal, and replacement of damaged parts as necessary.

Common questions

Which BMW motorcycles have reverse gear overheating recalls?

The September 2024 notice covers certain 2021–2024 R18 motorcycles. The October 2026 notice specifies certain 2021–2024 R 18 motorcycles with optional reverse gear. The April 2026 notice covers certain 2017–2025 K 1600 GT, GTL, B and 2018–2025 Grand America vehicles.

Does the R1250 gearbox recall require replacing the gearbox?

The January 2023 notice specifies a free engine control unit software update for certain 2019–2023 R1250GS, R1250GS Adventure, and R1250RTP motorcycles. It describes possible gearbox input shaft damage or breakage and a blocked rear wheel, but does not specify gearbox replacement.

What repairs do the BMW transmission leak recalls specify?

The February 2022 notice specifies free replacement of the transmission mechatronics unit in certain 2022 330i, 530i, X3 xDrive30i, and X4 xDrive30i vehicles. The December 2023 notice specifies free replacement of the mechatronics module and automatic transmission fluid, as necessary.
