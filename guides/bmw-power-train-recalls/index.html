What BMW power train recalls cover transmissions, driveshafts and motorcycles?

12 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Power Train · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 12, 2026

BMW's power train recalls in this group split between motorcycles and cars. On two wheels, the pattern is drivetrain hardware: clutch cover bolts on 2022 S1000RR and M1000RR, a final drive pivot pin on 2020 R1250GS and R1250RT, a gearbox input shaft on 2019-2023 R1250GS and R1250GS Adventure, and a cardan shaft on the 2026 R 1300 RT. A deteriorating reverse gear control unit seal repeats twice, on the R18 and on K 1600 GT, GTL and B models. On the car side, transmission mechatronics and control units leaking oil hit 2022 and 2023 X3 and X4 models, with 330i and 530i sedans also named.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An oil leak at the clutch cover, named in the clutch cover bolt recall covering 2022 S1000RR and M1000RR and 2022-2023 S1000XR and S1000R motorcycles.
  • The transmission shifting to neutral with a loss of propulsion, named in the 2019-2020 M5 and 2020 M8 transmission wiring harness recall.
  • An engine stall, named in the 2020 X5 and X7 starter motor bolt recall.
  • The vehicle rolling away, named in the 2022 330i, 530i, X3 and X4 mechatronics recall and in the driveshaft-to-differential recall covering 840i, 540i, M550i xDrive and 750e xDrive models.
  • The rear wheel locking up or becoming blocked, named in the K 1600 transmission parts recall and in the R1250GS and R1250GS Adventure gearbox input shaft recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the twelve notices in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That includes the recalls describing a loss of propulsion on the 2019-2020 M5 and 2020 M8, a rollaway on the 2022 330i, 530i, X3 and X4, and a fire risk from the reverse gear control unit on the R18 and the K 1600. For the R18 recall only, the notice says the reverse gear control unit will be disconnected from the motorcycle's electrical system as an interim remedy, free of charge.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace parts on most: clutch cover bolts, a final drive pivot pin, a cardan shaft, transmission mechatronics units, reverse gear control units and transmission wiring harnesses. Others are inspections, such as the starter motor on the 2020 X5 and X7 and the driveshaft-to-differential connection on the 840i, 540i, M550i xDrive and 750e xDrive. The 2019-2023 R1250GS recall is an engine control unit software update. All of it is free of charge.

Common questions

Does the reverse gear control unit recall affect both the R18 and the K 1600?

These are two separate recalls. One covers 2021-2024 R18 motorcycles, the other 2017-2025 K 1600 GT, GTL, B and 2018-2025 Grand America. Both notices describe a seal that may deteriorate and let moisture into the unit, which can overheat.

Do any of these BMW power train notices tell owners to stop riding or driving?

None of the twelve notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. Each describes the risk, crash, fire, loss of drive power or rollaway, and points owners to a dealer. Only the R18 notice describes an interim step, disconnecting the reverse gear control unit.

Why is a Toyota GR Supra listed in a BMW recall?

BMW of North America issued the December 2023 recall, and it lists the 2023 Toyota GR Supra alongside BMW 3, 4, 5 and 8 Series, X3, X4, X5, X6 and Z4 models. The notice says transmission oil leaking into the control unit can seize the transmission gears.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
