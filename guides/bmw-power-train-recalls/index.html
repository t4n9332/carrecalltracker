Why do BMW transmissions, driveshafts and motorcycle gearboxes keep failing?

12 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Power Train · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-12

Across these twelve BMW recalls, motorcycles dominate. Transmission internals repeat: K 1600 touring bikes (2017-2025) with under-hardened shift forks and 6th-gear parts, and R 1250 GS, GS Adventure and RT bikes whose gearbox input shaft can break and block the rear wheel. The reverse gear control unit seal appears twice — on 2021-2024 R 18 models and again on K 1600 GT, GTL, B and Grand America — letting moisture in and overheating the unit. Final-drive hardware is the other thread: the R 1250 pivot pin, the R 1300 RT cardan shaft, and on cars the driveshaft-to-rear-differential joint on 540i, M550i, 840i and 750e. Transmission mechatronics units cover 2022-2023 3, 5, X3, X4, X5, X6 and the GR Supra.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rear wheel locking or grabbing on a K 1600 or R 1250 motorcycle
  • Oil leaking from around the clutch cover on an S 1000 RR
  • Burning smell, smoke or unusual heat near the reverse gear unit on K 1600 or R 18 bikes
  • Jerking, hesitation, sudden loss of drive power, or a transmission warning light
  • A parked vehicle rolling away; several of these faults give little or no warning first

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these BMW notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. Two are effectively park-it cases anyway: the 2020 R 1250 GS and RT pivot pin bikes were still at dealers, and the single 2026 R 1300 RT cardan shaft bike has already been repaired. For the rest — K 1600 gearbox and reverse gear unit, R 18, S 1000 RR clutch cover, mechatronics units, driveshaft joints — riding or driving until the repair is accepted, but go promptly and stop if the vehicle jerks, hesitates, smells of burning, or shows a transmission warning.

What the free repair involves

All of this work is free. Dealers replace shift forks, the selector sleeve and 6th-gear wheel; the 10 clutch cover bolts; the reverse gear control unit (disconnected first as an interim step on R 18s); the pivot pin or cardan shaft; and transmission mechatronics units with fluid. Elsewhere they inspect the driveshaft-to-differential joint and apply adhesive or replace parts, remove a stray starter bolt, or update engine control software.

Common questions

My K 1600 is covered by two different recalls — is that right?

Yes. One covers transmission parts that were not hardened properly, with shift forks, the selector sleeve and the 6th-gear wheel replaced. A separate, later recall covers the reverse gear control unit seal and overheating. Both repairs are free and independent of each other.

I got a letter about a fire risk but no repair was offered. Why?

For the K 1600 reverse gear control unit, BMW mailed an interim letter informing owners of the safety risk before parts were ready. A second letter goes out once the remedy is available. Until then, watch for burning smells, smoke or heat around the bike.

Why is a Toyota GR Supra listed in a BMW recall?

BMW of North America filed the 2023 recall covering the GR Supra alongside its own 3, 4, 5, X3, X4, X5 and X6 models, because they share the transmission mechatronics module. Dealers replace the module and automatic transmission fluid as necessary, free of charge.
