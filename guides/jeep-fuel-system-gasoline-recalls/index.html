Which Jeep Models Have Had Fuel Leak or Stall Recalls?

5 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-10-13

Five fuel system recalls affect different Jeep models and engines, mostly raising fire or stalling risks. Two are rated critical: a 2018 Grand Cherokee Trackhawk fuel line that may separate on the 6.2L supercharged engine, and a 2020 Wrangler fuel supply line connector that may crack on the 2.0L engine, both raising fire risk. Three high-severity recalls involve stalling: a 2018 Cherokee fuel tube that may leak on the 2.4L engine, a 2017-2018 Renegade fuel pump manufacturing error that can lose pressure under high power demand, altitude, or heat, and a shared 2020-2022 Wrangler, Gladiator, and Ram 1500 diesel high-pressure fuel pump that can fail.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A fuel smell or visible fuel leak from a separated or cracked fuel line (Grand Cherokee Trackhawk, Wrangler, Cherokee)
  • Engine stalling caused by a loss of fuel pressure (Renegade, and the shared Wrangler/Gladiator/RAM diesel fuel pump recall)
  • The notices do not describe an advance warning sign before the fuel line separates or cracks

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, despite two being rated critical. The Grand Cherokee Trackhawk fuel line separation and the Wrangler fuel line crack notices describe an increased fire risk, and the Cherokee fuel tube, Renegade fuel pump, and shared diesel high-pressure fuel pump recalls describe stalling risks, but each notice only states the defect and free repair without telling owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and replace the affected fuel line, fuel tube, or fuel pump, free of charge. The diesel high-pressure fuel pump recall also has dealers inspect and replace additional fuel system components as needed. All five recalls are dealer repairs at no cost to the owner.

Common questions

Which Jeep fuel system recalls are rated critical?

The 2018 Grand Cherokee Trackhawk fuel line separation recall and the 2020 Wrangler fuel supply line crack recall are both rated critical because they raise the risk of a fire in the engine compartment.

What caused the Jeep Renegade fuel pump recall?

On 2017-2018 Renegade 4x2 flex fuel/E85 vehicles with the 2.4L engine, a manufacturing error in the fuel pump assembly can cause a loss of fuel pressure under high power demand, high altitude, or high heat.

Is the Wrangler diesel fuel pump recall the same as the Renegade recall?

No. The Renegade recall involves a gasoline fuel pump manufacturing defect on 2017-2018 models, while the Wrangler/Gladiator/Ram 1500 recall involves a separate high-pressure fuel pump on 2020-2022 diesel engines that can fail and stall the engine.
