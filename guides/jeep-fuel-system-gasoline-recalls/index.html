Jeep fuel line and fuel pump recalls: what owners should know

5 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 13, 2022

Five Jeep fuel-system recalls fall into two repeating patterns. The first is fuel escaping into the engine compartment: a separating fuel line on 2018 Grand Cherokee Trackhawk models with the 6.2L supercharged engine, a leaking fuel tube on 2018 Cherokees with the 2.4L engine, and a cracked fuel supply line connector on 2020 Wranglers with the 2.0L engine. The second is fuel delivery failing and stalling the engine: a fuel pump assembly error on 2017-2018 Renegade flex-fuel models with the 2.4L engine, and a high-pressure fuel pump failure on 2020-2022 Wrangler and Ram 1500 and 2021-2022 Gladiator models with 3.0L diesel engines. Wrangler appears in both patterns, and each recall is limited to one specific engine.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The Grand Cherokee Trackhawk fuel line recall says a separated fuel line may cause the engine to stall.
  • The 2017-2018 Renegade fuel pump recall says a loss of fuel pressure can cause a stall.
  • The 3.0L diesel high-pressure fuel pump recall says the pump may fail, causing an engine stall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside or away from buildings. The Trackhawk, Renegade and 3.0L diesel pump notices say the defect can stall the engine and raise crash risk; the Trackhawk, Cherokee and Wrangler notices say leaking fuel raises fire risk. Beyond describing those risks, the notices give owners no interim instruction to follow before the repair. They direct owners to a dealer and list Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and, if needed, replace the fuel line on the Trackhawk recall, the fuel tube on the 2018 Cherokee recall, and the fuel supply line on the 2020 Wrangler recall. The Renegade recall replaces the fuel pump. The diesel recall replaces the high-pressure fuel pump and inspects and replaces additional fuel system components as necessary. Each notice says the work is free of charge.

Common questions

How do I find out if my Jeep is included in one of these fuel recalls?

Each recall is limited to a specific engine, so a VIN check is the only reliable answer. Run your VIN on NHTSA's recall lookup or give it to a Jeep dealer, who can match it against Chrysler's recall numbers U02, U39, Y10, UB2 and Z96.

Are all Jeep Wranglers covered by these recalls?

Two recalls name the Wrangler: 2020 models with the 2.0L engine for the cracked fuel supply line connector, and 2020-2022 models with the 3.0L diesel engine for the high-pressure fuel pump. Other Wranglers fall outside these two fuel recalls.

What do the notices say about the cost of these repairs?

All five notices state that the dealer work is free of charge, whether that is inspecting and replacing a fuel line or tube or replacing a fuel pump. Recall start and owner letter dates range from February 2018 through October 2023.
