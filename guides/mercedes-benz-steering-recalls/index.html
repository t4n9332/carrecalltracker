Why do Mercedes-Benz vehicles keep getting steering recalls?

8 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-08-20

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Steering recalls for Mercedes-Benz cluster heavily around the Sprinter van: a 2019 Sprinter VS30 steering shaft could disconnect from the steering gear, 2019 Sprinter 1500/4500 models had bearing screws that could loosen and a missing steering column cover, and 2018-2020 Sprinter vans across several model years had power steering hoses that could leak fluid in two separate recalls. The 2018 Metris had an electric power steering sensor that could deactivate assist. Two AMG models, the 2021 CLS53 and E53, share a software defect disabling hands-off-wheel detection, and the 2025 EQE and GLC share a steering coupling bolt that can loosen. Sprinter accounts for five of the eight recalls.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Sudden loss of steering control with no advance warning (2019 Sprinter steering shaft recall)
  • Power steering fluid visibly leaking from hoses onto the ground
  • Steering suddenly feels harder to turn or power assist cuts out
  • No physical warning sign for the AMG hands-off-detection software or the EQE/GLC coupling-bolt defect — these are found through inspection, not something a driver would feel

Is it safe to keep driving?

One of these eight recalls — the 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter steering shaft disconnection recall — tells owners to stop driving the vehicle immediately, because the steering shaft can separate from the steering gear without warning. For the other seven recalls, covering the Metris power steering sensor, Sprinter bearing screws, missing column covers, leaking power steering hoses, the AMG hands-off detection software, and the EQE/GLC steering coupling bolt, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, dealers tighten or replace loose bolts and screws, replace the electric power steering unit or steering column cover, correct or clamp leaking power steering hose connections, and update the hand-detection software for AMG models with DISTRONIC. Every repair, whether a hardware replacement or a software update, is performed free of charge to the owner.

Common questions

Why do so many Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans have steering recalls?

Sprinter vans have been recalled multiple times for separate steering issues — a disconnecting steering shaft, loose bearing screws, a missing column cover, and leaking power steering hoses — each involving different parts and different model years, not one repeated defect.

Is my Mercedes-Benz Metris affected by a steering recall?

Yes, if it's a 2018-2019 Metris; an internal sensor in the electric power steering unit may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can deactivate power steering assist. Dealers replace the steering unit for free.

Does the AMG hands-off detection recall affect regular steering?

No. It affects the DISTRONIC Active Distance Assist software's ability to detect when a driver's hands leave the wheel, which can disable safety warnings — it doesn't change how the steering itself operates.
