Mercedes-Benz steering recalls: Sprinter bolts, hoses and lost power assist

8 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 20, 2025

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Steering fasteners that were not properly tightened drive this group. The 2019 Sprinter VS30 steering shaft may disconnect from the steering gear, 2019 Sprinter 1500 and 4500 vans may have loose steering spindle intermediate bearing screws, and 2023-2026 EQE and GLC vehicles may have an improperly tightened steering coupling bolt. Leaking hydraulic power steering return line hoses appear twice, once for 2015-2020 Sprinter vans and again for 2019-2020 Sprinter vans. The Sprinter also accounts for a missing steering column casing cover on 2019 models. Outside the Sprinter line, 2018-2019 Metris vans can lose electric power steering assist through an improperly manufactured sensor, and 2021-2022 cars with a heated leather steering wheel have a hand detection control unit software error.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Difficulty steering the vehicle, in the 2019 Sprinter steering spindle intermediate bearing screw recall.
  • Deactivation of electric power steering assist, in the 2018-2019 Metris electric power steering unit recall.
  • A sudden loss of power steering assist, in the 2019-2020 Sprinter power steering return line hose recall.
  • Hydraulic fluid leaking onto the roadway, in the 2015-2020 Sprinter power steering return line hose recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One notice in this group carries a stop-driving instruction: the 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter VS30 recall for a steering shaft that may disconnect from the steering gear tells owners to stop driving the vehicle immediately. None of these notices tell owners to park outside. For the other seven recalls — the Metris power steering unit, the Sprinter bearing screws, the missing steering column cover, the two power steering hose recalls, the hands-off detection software, and the EQE and GLC steering coupling bolt — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and tighten the steering shaft bolts on the 2019 Sprinter VS30, replace the Metris steering unit, replace and tighten the Sprinter steering spindle bearing screws, replace the steering column with one that has a cover, check the power steering return line connections, install a screw-type clamp, update the hand detection control unit software, and tighten the steering coupling bolts. Every notice states the repair is free.

Common questions

Which Mercedes-Benz steering recall tells owners to stop driving?

The 2019 Sprinter VS30 recall for a steering shaft that may disconnect from the steering gear. That notice advises owners to stop driving the vehicle immediately, and dealers inspect the bolts connecting the shaft to the gear, free of charge.

Are there two Mercedes-Benz Sprinter power steering hose recalls?

Two separate recalls cover power steering return line hoses that may detach and leak. One covers 2015-2020 Sprinter vans and has dealers check the return line connections; the other covers 2019-2020 vans and adds a screw-type clamp.

What does the heated steering wheel recall actually affect?

The notice describes a software error in the hand detection control unit that can stop hands-off detection from working with Active Distance Assist DISTRONIC, including grab-the-wheel warnings and the Active Emergency Stop Assist call to first responders.
