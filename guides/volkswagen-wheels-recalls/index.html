Which Volkswagen Wheel and Bearing Recalls Involve a Stop-Drive Warning?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Wheels · model years 2019–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2021-10-15

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Three Volkswagen recalls in this group involve the wheels or the hardware that attaches them. On 2019 Golf and Jetta, the front wheel bearings may have been improperly machined, putting excessive stress on the wheel, bolts, or bearings; this is the only recall in the group with a stop-driving instruction. On 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport, the lug bolt holes on the wheels may be machined incorrectly, which can prevent a wheel bolt from properly attaching to the wheel bearing hub. On 2021 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, and Passat, the front wheel bearing bolts may have been incorrectly tightened and can loosen.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering, traction, or other stability problems (2019 Golf, Jetta wheel bearing recall)
  • A wheel separating from the vehicle (2021 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport lug bolt recall)
  • Loss of vehicle control from loosened wheel bearing bolts (2021 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, Passat)

Is it safe to keep driving?

One of these three recalls carries a stop-driving instruction: the December 2019 recall for 2019 Volkswagen Golf and Jetta front wheel bearings tells owners not to drive their vehicles until the repair is made, because excessive stress can cause steering, traction, or stability problems, or breakage at the front wheels. The other two recalls, covering wheel lug bolt holes and wheel bearing bolt tightness on 2021 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, and Passat vehicles, do not carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; those notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the wheel bearing recall, dealers replace the wheel bearing assemblies, wheels, and wheel bolts. For the lug bolt hole recall, dealers inspect all four wheels and replace wheels, lug bolts, and wheel bearings as necessary. For the bearing bolt recall, dealers inspect and replace the bolts as necessary. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Which Volkswagen wheel recall tells owners not to drive?

The recall for 2019 Golf and Jetta front wheel bearings tells owners not to drive until dealers replace the wheel bearing assemblies, wheels, and wheel bolts, because of excessive stress that can affect steering and stability.

Can a wheel actually fall off my Atlas?

The 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport recall notice says incorrectly machined lug bolt holes may prevent a wheel bolt from sufficiently attaching to the wheel bearing hub, which could allow a wheel to separate from the vehicle.

What's different about the Atlas and Passat bolt recall?

That separate 2021 recall involves front wheel bearing bolts that may have been incorrectly tightened and could loosen over time, which is a different cause from the wheel machining issues in the other two recalls.
