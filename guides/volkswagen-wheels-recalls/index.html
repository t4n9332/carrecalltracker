Volkswagen Wheel Recalls Involve Bearings, Loose Bolts and Wheel Separation

4 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Wheels · model years 2019–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 4, 2025

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

Wheel attachment problems recur across these Volkswagen notices, with Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport appearing in three of the four recalls. Certain 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles have incorrectly machined wheel lug-bolt holes, while another 2021 recall concerns incorrectly tightened front wheel bearing bolts on Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, and Passat vehicles. The December 2025 recall covers incorrect wheel bolts on certain 2026 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, and ID.4 vehicles. Separately, certain 2019 Jetta GLI, Golf Alltrack, and Golf GTI vehicles have improperly machined front wheel bearings. The notices describe wheel separation, loss of control, or front-wheel stress and stability risks.

Warning signs to watch for

  • December 2019 Jetta/Golf front wheel bearing recall: excessive stress can cause steering, traction, or other stability problems.
  • October 2021 Atlas/Atlas Cross Sport/Passat bearing bolt recall: bolts may loosen and cause loss of control.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two supplied Volkswagen wheel notices tell owners not to drive until repairs are completed: the December 2019 front wheel bearing recall for certain Jetta GLI, Golf Alltrack, and Golf GTI vehicles, and the December 2025 incorrect wheel bolt recall for certain 2026 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, and ID.4 vehicles. Neither gives a park-outside instruction. The March 2021 Atlas/Atlas Cross Sport wheel recall and October 2021 Atlas/Atlas Cross Sport/Passat wheel bearing bolt recall do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Each notice specifies free repairs. December 2019 requires replacing wheel bearing assemblies, wheels, and wheel bolts. December 2025 requires inspecting and replacing wheel bolts as necessary. March 2021 requires inspecting all four wheels and replacing wheels, lug bolts, and wheel bearings as necessary. October 2021 requires checking bearing-bolt tightness and replacing bolts as necessary.

Common questions

Which Volkswagen wheel recalls require owners to stop driving?

The December 2019 Jetta GLI, Golf Alltrack, and Golf GTI bearing recall and December 2025 recall for certain 2026 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, and ID.4 vehicles instruct owners not to drive until repaired. The other two supplied notices do not.

Why are there two wheel recalls for the 2021 Atlas?

The March 2021 notice concerns incorrectly machined lug-bolt holes that may prevent sufficient wheel attachment. The October 2021 notice concerns incorrectly tightened front wheel bearing bolts. Both cover certain 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles; the October notice also includes Passat.

Does the Jetta and Golf bearing recall replace just the bearings?

The December 2019 notice specifies replacing the wheel bearing assemblies, wheels, and wheel bolts free of charge. It covers certain 2019 Jetta GLI, Golf Alltrack, and Golf GTI vehicles and tells affected owners not to drive until repairs are made.
