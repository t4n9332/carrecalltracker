Why do Volkswagen wheel bearings and wheel bolts keep getting recalled?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Wheels · model years 2019–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 15, 2021

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

All three of these Volkswagen recalls concern front wheel hardware, and two of them land on the 2021 Atlas family. The December 2019 recall covers 2019 Jetta GLI, Golf Alltrack and Golf GTI vehicles whose front wheel bearings may have been improperly machined, potentially causing excessive stress on the wheel, wheel bolts or wheel bearings. The March 2021 recall covers 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles where the lug bolt holes on the wheels may have been machined incorrectly, preventing the wheel bolt from sufficiently attaching to the wheel bearing hub. The October 2021 recall covers 2021 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport and Passat vehicles whose front wheel bearing bolts may have been incorrectly tightened.

Warning signs to watch for

  • In the December 2019 Jetta GLI, Golf Alltrack and Golf GTI recall, the notice says the excessive stress can cause steering, traction or other stability issues, or breakage relating to the front wheels.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One of these three recalls carries a stop-driving instruction: the December 2019 recall (40P1) of 2019 Jetta GLI, Golf Alltrack and Golf GTI vehicles, where owners are told not to drive their vehicles until the repairs have been made. For the March 2021 wheel recall covering 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport, and the October 2021 wheel bearing bolt recall covering 2021 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport and Passat, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. None of the three tells owners to park outside.

What the free repair involves

On the 2019 Jetta GLI, Golf Alltrack and Golf GTI, dealers replace the wheel bearing assemblies, wheels and wheel bolts. On 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport, dealers inspect all four wheels and, as necessary, replace the wheels, lug bolts and wheel bearings. On 2021 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport and Passat, dealers inspect the tightness of the wheel bearing bolts and replace the bolts as necessary. All free of charge.

Common questions

Which Volkswagen wheel recall tells owners not to drive?

The December 2019 recall (40P1) covering 2019 Jetta GLI, Golf Alltrack and Golf GTI vehicles states that owners are told not to drive their vehicles until the repairs have been made. Dealers replace the bearing assemblies, wheels and bolts free of charge.

Why is the 2021 Atlas named in two separate wheel recalls?

The notices describe different defects. One says the lug bolt holes on the wheels may have been machined incorrectly; the other says the front wheel bearing bolts may have been incorrectly tightened and may loosen, causing a loss of control.

What do the notices say could happen if the wheels are not repaired?

The March 2021 notice says an insufficiently attached wheel may separate from the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. The October 2021 notice says the bolts may loosen and cause a loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash.
