Why do Subaru electrical faults cause stalling and fire risk?

6 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Electrical System · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-08-04

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

These six Subaru recalls span 2018-2024 model years and cluster around two problems: components that lose power unexpectedly and one that raises a fire risk. The 2019-2022 Ascent had a heater ground bolt that could melt, prompting a park-outside warning. Two separate recalls, covering 2018-2019 Impreza and Crosstrek, describe an Engine Control Module that keeps powering the ignition coil after shutoff, risking a short circuit and stall. A 2024 Crosstrek and Impreza recall covers an instrument panel harness rubbing against the steering beam bracket, also causing power loss. Separately, 2018 Legacy and Outback vehicles got a software fix for an inaccurate fuel gauge, and 2021 Crosstrek Hybrid vehicles had a corroding 12-Volt charging harness.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Smoke or a burning smell from the dash or driver's footwell area
  • Engine stalling suddenly while driving
  • Low fuel warning light not illuminating, or the miles-to-empty display showing a positive number on an empty tank
  • Sudden loss of drive power

Is it safe to keep driving?

Of these six recalls, one — the Subaru Ascent cabin heater fire risk — instructs owners to park away from structures and not leave the engine running unattended, due to a melting ground terminal that increases fire risk; owners who notice or smell smoke from the dash or footwell area should stop driving immediately. None of the six carries a stop-driving order. For the remaining five recalls — covering ignition coil short circuits, wiring harness damage, fuel gauge errors, and 12-Volt battery charging faults — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls dealers inspect and replace parts free of charge: updating ECM software and replacing ignition coils (and the exhaust pipe if needed) for the stalling recalls, adding a clamp or replacing the instrument panel harness, reprogramming the combination meter software for the fuel gauge, and replacing the 12-Volt converter bracket and harness. For the Ascent heater recall, the notice describes interim parking instructions rather than a repair.

Common questions

Why is my Subaru Ascent being told to park away from buildings?

The 2019-2022 Ascent recall found the heater's ground bolt may not be fastened properly, which can melt the ground terminal. Subaru is telling owners to park away from structures and not leave the engine running unattended until it's repaired.

Why did my Impreza or Crosstrek stall unexpectedly?

Two recalls found the Engine Control Module can keep powering the ignition coil after the engine shuts off, which can cause a short circuit and a blown fuse, leading to a stall while driving.

What does the Legacy/Outback fuel gauge recall affect?

A software error in certain 2018 Legacy and Outback vehicles can keep the low fuel warning light from illuminating and show a positive miles-to-empty reading on an empty tank, risking an unexpected stall.
