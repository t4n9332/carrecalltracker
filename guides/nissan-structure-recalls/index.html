Why Do Nissan Z And Sentra Have Structural Safety Recalls?

4 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Structure · model years 2021–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-10-24

Two of these four recalls hit the same vehicles for different causes: the 2023-2024 Nissan Z was recalled in October 2023 because fasteners in the front bumper reinforcement clip can loosen and stop the pop-up engine hood from activating in a pedestrian crash, then recalled again in October 2024 because reversed sensor wiring in the front bumper harness can cause the same failure. The other two recalls are one-offs: the 2021 Rogue Sport for liftgate hinges that can separate from the vehicle in a rear-end crash, and the 2022 Sentra for a missing or improper cowl seal that lets water leak in and corrode electrical wiring.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Wipers or exterior lights failing, air bags not working, or the vehicle not shifting into gear without pressing the brake pedal — signs of the electrical corrosion described in the 2022 Sentra recall
  • The 2021 Rogue Sport liftgate hinge recall describes no warning sign owners would notice
  • Both Nissan Z pop-up hood recalls (2023 and 2024) describe no warning sign owners would notice

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four structural recalls — the 2021 Rogue Sport liftgate hinge recall, the 2022 Sentra cowl seal recall, the 2023 Nissan Z pop-up hood fastener recall, and the 2024 Nissan Z pop-up hood wiring recall — carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For each of the four, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, and none describe an interim step to take before the dealer repair is completed.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the liftgate hinges, inspect and reseal the cowl area (replacing the wiring harness if corrosion is found), tighten the loosened hood-fastener clip, or replace the front bumper harness, depending on the recall. All four repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why does the Nissan Z have two separate pop-up hood recalls?

The first, in October 2023, was caused by loosening fasteners in the front bumper clip; the second, in October 2024, was caused by reversed sensor wiring in the bumper harness. Both can stop the hood from activating in a pedestrian crash.

What does the 2022 Nissan Sentra cowl seal recall affect?

A missing or improper seal in the driver's side cowl can let water leak in and corrode electrical components, which could disable the wipers, exterior lights, air bags, or the ability to shift into gear without the brake.

Is the 2021 Nissan Rogue Sport liftgate hinge recall dangerous?

The liftgate hinges can separate from the vehicle in a rear-end crash, so the liftgate no longer meets federal door-retention standards, which can increase the risk of injury in that kind of crash.
