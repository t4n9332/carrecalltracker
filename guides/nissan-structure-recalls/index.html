Nissan body structure recalls: liftgates, cowl leaks and pop-up hoods

4 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Structure · model years 2021–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 24, 2024

These four Nissan recalls cover body structure, and two of them are the same car. The 2023-2024 Nissan Z appears twice for its pop-up engine hood, which is meant to raise in a frontal pedestrian crash: once because fasteners in the left-side front bumper reinforcement clip may loosen, and again because the right-side pedestrian detection sensor connectors may be reversed in the front bumper harness. The 2021 Rogue Sport's liftgate hinges can separate from the vehicle during a rear-end crash, a failure to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 206. The 2022 Sentra is the outlier: a missing or improper driver's side cowl seal that can let water leak in and corrode electrical components.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Loss of wiper motor function or exterior lighting — named in the 2022 Sentra cowl seal recall.
  • Inoperative air bags, or the ability to shift into gear without applying the brake pedal — also named in the 2022 Sentra cowl seal recall.
  • The Rogue Sport liftgate hinge recall and both Nissan Z pop-up hood recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park the vehicle outside. The 2021 Rogue Sport liftgate hinge recall and both 2023-2024 Nissan Z pop-up hood recalls describe risks that arise in a crash, and give owners no instruction to follow before the repair. The 2022 Sentra cowl seal notice describes possible electrical failures and a short circuit that increases the risk of a fire, and it also does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

All four repairs are free. Dealers replace the left and right liftgate hinges on the 2021 Rogue Sport, tighten the bumper reinforcement clip fasteners on the Nissan Z, and replace the front bumper harness on the Z for the sensor connector recall. For the 2022 Sentra, dealers inspect and, if necessary, reseal the driver's side cowl, replacing the main harness and engine room harness if moisture or corrosion is found.

Common questions

Why is my Nissan Z recalled twice for the pop-up hood?

The two notices give different causes. One says fasteners in the left-side front bumper reinforcement clip may loosen; the other says the right-side pedestrian detection sensor connectors may be reversed in the front bumper harness. Letters were mailed December 7, 2023 and December 11, 2024.

What do the notices say the pop-up engine hood does?

The notices describe a hood that activates and raises its rear end during a frontal pedestrian crash. Both recalls concern conditions that can prevent it from activating as intended, which the notices say increases the risk of injury.

Does the 2022 Sentra cowl recall mean my car will have electrical problems?

The notice says a missing or improper driver's side cowl seal can let water leak in and corrode electrical components, which could result in failures of various electrical systems. Dealers inspect and reseal the cowl, replacing harnesses if moisture or corrosion is found, free of charge.
