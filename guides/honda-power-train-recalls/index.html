What do the Honda power train recalls cover on cars and motorcycles?

6 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Power Train · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 23, 2025

This group splits between motorcycle shift controls and car drivetrains. On motorcycles, fasteners loosen: the 2024 CB650R and CBR650R gear shift arm and the 2025 CB1000SP Hornet SP gearshift pedal pivot bolt, both leaving the rider unable to shift, plus a transmission main shaft circlip on 2019 CB300R, 2018 CBR300R, 2018 CRF250L and 2018-2019 CMX300. On the car side, hybrids appear twice: an improperly manufactured e-CVT on 2023 CR-V Hybrid and Accord Hybrid, and an improperly heat-treated front driveshaft part on 2021 Acura RDX and 2022 CR-V Hybrid. The 2018-2019 Odyssey recall is different again — battery terminal or battery condition rebooting the transmission control unit.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The inability to shift gears while riding — stated in the 2024 CB650R and CBR650R gear shift arm recall and the 2025 CB1000SP Hornet SP pivot bolt recall.
  • A sudden loss of drive power — stated in the 2021 Acura RDX and 2022 CR-V Hybrid driveshaft recall; the 2023 CR-V Hybrid and Accord Hybrid recall states a loss of drive power.
  • The transmission shifting to the Park position after the transmission control unit reboots — stated in the 2018-2019 Odyssey recall.
  • Unexpected motorcycle movement during engine start, or the transmission and rear wheel seizing while the motorcycle is in motion — stated in the CB300R, CBR300R, CRF250L and CMX300 circlip recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. That includes the 2019 CB300R, 2018 CBR300R, CRF250L and 2018-2019 CMX300 circlip recall, the 2021 Acura RDX and 2022 CR-V Hybrid driveshaft recall, the 2023 CR-V Hybrid and Accord Hybrid e-CVT recall, the 2024 CB650R and CBR650R gear shift arm recall, the 2025 CB1000SP pivot bolt recall and the 2018-2019 Odyssey transmission control unit recall. The driveshaft notice adds that the vehicle can roll away when parked if the parking brake is not applied.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is free. Dealers replace the transmission main shaft on the CB300R, CBR300R, CRF250L and CMX300; the front driveshaft on the 2021 Acura RDX and 2022 CR-V Hybrid; the e-CVT on the 2023 CR-V Hybrid and Accord Hybrid; the gear shift arm on the 2024 CB650R and CBR650R; and the CB1000SP pivot bolt. On the Odyssey they update TCU software, secure battery terminals and check the parking rod.

Common questions

My Honda motorcycle won't shift gears. Is that one of these recalls?

Two notices describe the inability to shift gears: the 2024 CB650R and CBR650R gear shift arm fasteners, and the 2025 CB1000SP Hornet SP gearshift pedal pivot bolt. Check your VIN with Honda; dealers replace the arm or the bolt free of charge.

Is the 2023 CR-V Hybrid transmission repaired or replaced?

The 2023 CR-V Hybrid and Accord Hybrid notice says the electronic transmission may have been improperly manufactured and that dealers replace the electronic continuously variable transmission, free of charge. Owner notification letters for that recall were mailed October 11, 2023.

What does the Odyssey transmission control unit recall actually fix?

The 2018-2019 Odyssey notice says dealers make sure the battery terminal connections are secure, update the TCU software and check parking rod effectiveness, free of charge. The notice describes a TCU reboot that may shift the transmission to Park and damage the parking rod.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
