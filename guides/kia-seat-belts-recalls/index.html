Which Kia models have seat belt recalls, and what's wrong?

7 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Seat Belts · model years 2019–2027 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 2, 2026

Kia's seat belt recalls split into a few repeating themes. Buckles and anchors that cannot be used or latched correctly show up in the 2019 Sedona with the 8-passenger seat arrangement (SC177) and again in the 2027 Telluride third-row center and 2026 K4 rear center buckle recall (SC364). Retractors are the other pattern: the 2024 EV9 front passenger retractor cover (SC312) and the 2027 Telluride and Telluride Hybrid driver belt that may lock and not extend (SC372). Seat belt pretensioners that may explode upon deployment cover the 2022 Sorento Hybrid and Sorento Plug-in Hybrid (SC231) and the 2021-2022 Sorento Hybrid plus 2022 Plug-in Hybrid (SC241). The 2020 Telluride appears separately for belts that may not tightly secure a child restraint.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A second-row middle seat belt buckle on the wrong side of the seat, preventing proper use of the 3-point belt — 2019 Sedona (SC177).
  • A third-row center or rear center seat belt anchor buckle that may not latch properly — 2027 Telluride and 2026 K4 (SC364).
  • A driver seat belt strap that does not extend because the emergency locking retractor may lock — 2027 Telluride and Telluride Hybrid (SC372).
  • A front passenger seat belt retractor that may not operate as intended — 2024 EV9 (SC312).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. Kia gives no interim instruction in any of them. What the notices say is limited to the repair and the notification: SC177 began April 8, 2019, SC231 letters were mailed May 3, 2022, SC241 August 17, 2022, SC312 June 5, 2024, SC364 May 5, 2026, and SC372 letters are expected July 31, 2026. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542.

What the free repair involves

The work varies by recall: SC177 inspects the Sedona belt installation and reinstalls the buckle on the correct side; SC231 replaces the Sorento Hybrid pretensioners, while SC241 secures the pretensioners with a cap; SC312 replaces the EV9 front passenger belt assembly; SC364 inspects and replaces the anchor buckle assembly; SC372 replaces the driver belt assembly; the 2020 Telluride recall replaces incorrect belt assemblies. Each notice says free of charge.

Common questions

My Sorento Hybrid is listed for a pretensioner recall — which one applies?

SC231 covers 2022 Sorento Hybrid and Sorento Plug-in Hybrid; SC241 covers 2021-2022 Sorento Hybrid and 2022 Plug-in Hybrid. The SC241 notice says it expands and replaces NHTSA recall 22V-127. Check your VIN or call Kia at 1-800-333-4542.

Can I still use the seat belt in the affected seat?

The notices give owners no instruction about using or avoiding the affected seat, and they do not tell owners to stop driving. They state only that the belt may not restrain an occupant properly in a crash, and that dealers will repair it free of charge.

My Telluride is a 2027 — which seat belt recall is mine?

Two notices list 2027 Tellurides: SC364 for the third-row center seat belt anchor buckle that may not latch, and SC372 for the driver seat belt that may not extend on the Telluride and Telluride Hybrid. Both notices say VINs become searchable on NHTSA's site.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
