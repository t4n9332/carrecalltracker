Why do some Kia seat belts fail to work properly?

5 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Seat Belts · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-05-14

Kia's seat belt recalls involve several distinct defects rather than one shared cause. The 2021-2022 Sorento Hybrid had front seat belt pretensioners that could explode in a crash; Kia issued a repair in March 2022 covering the 2022 model, then an expanded recall in June 2022 that added the 2021 model year and used a different fix. The 2019 Sedona had its second-row middle seat belt buckle installed on the wrong side, preventing proper 3-point use. The 2024 EV9 has a front passenger seat belt retractor cover that may not be secured, affecting the belt's locking function. The 2020 Telluride's seat belts may not tightly secure a child restraint system.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A seat belt buckle installed on the wrong side, preventing correct 3-point belt use (2019 Sedona)
  • The notices do not describe a warning sign for a seat belt pretensioner that could explode in a crash (Sorento Hybrid recalls)
  • A front passenger seat belt that does not lock as expected (2024 EV9)
  • A child restraint system that is not held tightly by the seat belt (2020 Telluride)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five seat belt recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the Sorento Hybrid pretensioner recalls, the Sedona buckle recall, the EV9 retractor recall, and the Telluride child-seat recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice instead describes an increased risk of injury in a crash — from an exploding pretensioner, a seat belt that can't be used properly, a malfunctioning retractor, or a child restraint that isn't held tightly — rather than a driving restriction.

What the free repair involves

Dealers make free repairs specific to each defect: reinstalling the Sedona's seat belt buckle on the correct side, replacing or capping the Sorento Hybrid's seat belt pretensioners depending on which recall applies, replacing the EV9's front passenger seat belt assembly, and replacing seat belt assemblies on affected Telluride seating positions.

Common questions

Why did Kia recall the Sorento Hybrid for seat belts twice?

Kia first recalled 2022 Sorento Hybrid vehicles in March 2022 for pretensioners that could explode in a crash. In June 2022, it expanded the recall to include 2021 models and switched to a different repair, replacing the earlier one.

What's wrong with the Kia Sedona's seat belt recall?

On some 2019 Sedona vehicles with an 8-passenger layout, the second-row middle seat belt buckle was installed on the wrong side, which prevents the 3-point belt from being used correctly and fails a federal safety standard.

Do Kia seat belt recalls affect child seat safety?

Yes. The 2020 Telluride was recalled because its seat belts in several rows may not tightly secure a child restraint system, which could increase injury risk to a child in a crash. This is separate from the pretensioner recalls.
