Kia seat belt recalls: buckles, pretensioners and child seat problems

7 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Seat Belts · model years 2019–2027 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 2, 2026

Five Kia seat belt recalls split into two patterns. The repeat is the front seat belt pretensioner on the Sorento Hybrid and Sorento Plug-in Hybrid: a March 2022 recall covering 2022 models and a June 2022 recall covering 2021-2022 Sorento Hybrid and 2022 Sorento Plug-in Hybrid, both saying a front pretensioner may explode upon deployment in a crash. The other three each cover one model: the 2019 Sedona with the 8-passenger seat arrangement, where the second-row middle buckle may sit on the wrong side; the 2020 Telluride, whose belts may not tightly secure a child restraint system; and the 2024 EV9, whose front passenger retractor cover may not be secured. Sorento Hybrid owners are named twice.

Warning signs to watch for

  • In the 2019 Sedona recall (SC177), the second-row middle seat belt buckle may be on the wrong side of the seat, preventing proper use of the 3-point seat belt.
  • In the 2024 EV9 recall (SC312), the front passenger seat belt's automatic locking retractor and emergency locking retractor may not operate as intended.
  • In the 2020 Telluride recall, the driver's seat belt may carry a label that is not U.S. specification.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That includes both Sorento Hybrid and Sorento Plug-in Hybrid pretensioner recalls, the 2019 Sedona buckle recall, the 2020 Telluride child restraint recall and the 2024 EV9 retractor recall. What the notices state instead is the risk each defect carries in a crash: injury from an exploding pretensioner, or from a belt that cannot be properly used or cannot properly restrain. The notices do not give owners any interim step beyond having the recall work done.

What the free repair involves

Remedies differ. On the 2019 Sedona, dealers inspect the installation and reinstall the buckle on the correct side if needed. On the March 2022 Sorento Hybrid recall they replace the pretensioners; on the June 2022 recall they secure the pretensioners with a cap. On the 2020 Telluride they inspect and replace incorrect belt assemblies, and on the 2024 EV9 they replace the front passenger belt assembly. Each is free of charge.

Common questions

My Sorento Hybrid was repaired under the first pretensioner recall. Is it covered again?

The June 2022 notice states that it expands and replaces the March 2022 recall, 22V-127. To see whether your vehicle still has open recall work, check your VIN through NHTSA's lookup or with Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542.

Does the 2020 Telluride recall mean my child's car seat is not secure?

The notice says the front passenger, second, and/or third row seat belt assemblies may not tightly secure a child restraint system in the event of a crash, and that the vehicles fail FMVSS 208. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

Which Sedona vehicles are covered by the middle seat belt buckle recall?

The notice covers certain 2019 Sedona vehicles equipped with an 8-passenger seat arrangement. It says the second-row middle seat belt buckle may have been installed on the wrong side of the seat, preventing proper use of the 3-point belt. Kia's number is SC177.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
