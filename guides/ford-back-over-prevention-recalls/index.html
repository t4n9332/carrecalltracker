Why Do So Many Ford Models Have Rearview Camera Recalls?

34 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-19

This group covers repeated rearview-camera and backup-alarm defects across many Ford and Lincoln models from 2018 through 2026. The Explorer, Lincoln Aviator, and Lincoln Corsair were recalled in both 2021 and 2023 for the same 360-degree camera video-output failure, the second recall simply adding more model years. The Bronco's camera-image-stuck-on-screen defect was recalled in 2022 and again in 2023 after the first fix didn't resolve it on 8-inch-screen vehicles. Other causes include poor electrical connections (a broad 2020 recall, and a separate 2019 F-150/Super Duty recall), a software error on the Edge, a fogging lens on the Lincoln Continental and Super Duty, an overheating control module on the Bronco and Edge, and a detaching backup alarm connector on the E-450 van.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rearview or 360-degree camera image that is blank, distorted, or foggy/cloudy
  • Camera image freezing or staying on screen after you've shifted out of reverse (certain Bronco models)
  • Backup camera not appearing if the display was turned off before shifting into reverse (Explorer Police Interceptor)
  • No audible backup alarm when reversing (2026 E-450)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe reduced rear visibility — a blank, distorted, or foggy camera image, one that sticks on screen and can distract the driver, or in one case a backup alarm that fails to sound — as increasing the risk of a crash, but in every one of these notices Ford does not instruct owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside.

What the free repair involves

Fixes vary by recall: dealers replace the rearview camera or its module, update control-module software, or replace a wiring connector, depending on the cause. A few recalls in this group — covering the Edge and certain Bronco models — can be completed with an over-the-air software update instead of a dealer visit. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why was my Ford Bronco recalled twice for the rearview camera?

The first 2022 recall fixed a camera image that stayed on screen after backing up, but on vehicles with 8-inch SYNC screens the fix didn't fully work, so Ford issued a second 2023 recall for the same problem on those vehicles.

Can the Ford camera recall be fixed without visiting a dealer?

Sometimes. The Edge software-error recall and some Bronco camera recalls can be completed with an over-the-air update; others, like electrical-connection or lens-fogging problems, require a dealer to replace the camera or module.

Does the backup camera recall affect commercial Ford vans?

Yes, one recall in this group covers the 2026 Ford E-450, where a loose backup alarm connector can prevent the alarm from sounding while reversing, separate from the camera-image recalls affecting other models.
