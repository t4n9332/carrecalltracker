Why do Volvo steering recalls keep involving loose fasteners?

6 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Steering · model years 2020–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 9, 2026

Loose fasteners dominate Volvo's steering recalls, and nearly all are Volvo Trucks actions. Steering gear fasteners that may not be properly tightened appear twice — on 2020-2021 VNL and VNR trucks (RVXX2001) and again on 2023-2024 VN, VHD and VAH trucks (RVXX2307). A king pin nut that may not have been installed or properly tightened covers 2021 VNL, VHD and VNR trucks, and RVXX2607 covers 2026 VAH, VN and VHD plus 2026-2027 VNL (4) and VNR (4) trucks with insufficiently tightened steering arms. Two outliers round it out: 2025-2026 New VN trucks whose dynamic steering and oversteer guidance feature may malfunction (RVXX2506), and 2019-2022 V60CC, XC60 and XC90 cars where a screw may fall into the steering gearbox housing (R10213).

Warning signs to watch for

  • The steering wheel locking up if a screw is in the gearbox housing — 2019-2022 V60CC, XC60 and XC90 (R10213).
  • A loss of steering control if the dynamic steering and oversteer guidance feature malfunctions — 2025-2026 New VN trucks (RVXX2506).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. RVXX2001, the 2021 king pin nut recall, R10213, RVXX2307, RVXX2506 and RVXX2607 each state that the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, and none of them gives an interim step to follow before the dealer work. What the notices do describe is the risk in each case — loose fasteners or a loose steering arm leading to a loss of vehicle control, a screw locking the steering wheel, or a malfunctioning oversteer guidance feature.

What the free repair involves

All six notices say the work is free of charge. Dealers tighten the steering gear fasteners to specification (RVXX2001 and RVXX2307), inspect the king pin nut for installation and correct tightening, replace the steering gear (R10213), inspect and tighten the fasteners between the steering arm and steering knuckle as necessary (RVXX2607), and disable the oversteer guidance feature over-the-air or at a dealer (RVXX2506).

Common questions

Which Volvo cars are in the steering gear screw recall?

R10213 covers certain 2019-2022 V60CC, XC60 and XC90 vehicles. The notice says double screws may have been assembled on top of each other, which can cause a screw to fall into the gearbox housing and lock the steering wheel. Dealers replace the steering gear free of charge.

Why do steering gear fasteners appear in two recalls?

Two separate Volvo Trucks recalls list steering gear fasteners that may not be properly tightened: RVXX2001 for certain 2020-2021 VNL and VNR trucks, and RVXX2307 for certain 2023-2024 VN, VHD and VAH trucks. Under both, dealers tighten the fasteners free of charge.

What happens with the oversteer guidance recall?

RVXX2506 covers certain 2025-2026 Volvo New VN trucks where the dynamic steering and oversteer guidance feature may malfunction and cause a loss of steering control. The feature is disabled through an over-the-air update or by a dealer, free of charge.
