Why do Volvo vehicles keep having steering fastener problems?

6 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Steering · model years 2020–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-09

These six Volvo steering recalls mostly involve loose or improperly tightened fasteners on trucks. Steering gear fasteners were not properly tightened on 2020-2021 VNL and VNR trucks and again on 2023-2024 VN, VHD, and VAH trucks. A 2021 VNL, VHD, and VNR recall covers a loose or missing king pin nut, and a 2026-2027 VNL and VNR recall covers steering arms that were not tightened enough on the steering knuckle. Separately, 2019-2022 V60CC, XC60, and XC90 cars can have a stray screw inside the steering gearbox that locks the steering wheel, and 2025-2026 VNL trucks can have a dynamic steering assist malfunction.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering wheel may suddenly lock up due to a screw inside the gearbox (2019-2022 V60CC, XC60, XC90)
  • No warning sign is described for loose steering gear fasteners in 2020-2021 VNL/VNR trucks and 2023-2024 VN/VHD/VAH trucks
  • No warning sign is described for the loose or missing king pin nut in 2021 VNL, VHD, and VNR trucks
  • Loss of steering control from a dynamic steering assist malfunction (2025-2026 VNL trucks)
  • No warning sign is described for the insufficiently tightened steering arm fasteners (2026-2027 VNL/VNR trucks)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2020-2021 VNL/VNR and 2023-2024 VN/VHD/VAH steering gear fastener recalls, the 2021 king pin nut recall, the 2019-2022 V60CC/XC60/XC90 steering-gear-screw recall, the 2025-2026 dynamic steering assist recall, and the 2026-2027 steering arm recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving, even though several of these recalls are rated a critical risk of losing steering control while the vehicle is being operated.

What the free repair involves

Dealers tighten or replace the steering gear fasteners, inspect and tighten the king pin nut, replace the steering gear on the affected passenger cars, disable the oversteer guidance feature through an over-the-air or dealer update, and inspect and tighten the steering arm fasteners, depending on the recall. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Volvo models have these steering recalls?

The recalls cover VNL, VNR, VHD, VAH, and VN trucks from 2020 through 2027, plus 2019-2022 V60CC, XC60, and XC90 passenger vehicles, each for a different steering defect.

Can a Volvo's steering wheel really lock up while driving?

On 2019-2022 V60CC, XC60, and XC90 vehicles, a stray screw inside the steering gearbox can cause the steering wheel to lock up, according to the notice for that specific recall.

Is the newer VNL steering recall a fastener problem too?

No. The 2025-2026 VNL recall involves a dynamic steering assist and oversteer guidance malfunction, fixed with a software update, unlike the other recalls in this group, which involve loose fasteners.
