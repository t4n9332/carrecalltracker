Dodge power train recalls: park failures, shifters and rollaway risk

7 NHTSA recalls · Dodge · Power Train · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 4, 2025

Losing PARK or losing drive is the thread through these seven. Three involve the shifter or park function: an incorrect transmission park lock rod on 2018 Charger, Durango, Challenger and Ram 1500 models (U43), a shifter assembly that was not properly heat-treated on the 2022 Charger and Durango (ZB3), and a shifter boot interference on the 2023 Durango Pursuit (94A). The 2024-2025 Charger EV and Jeep Wagoneer S add a rollaway recall from an incorrectly installed spring (63C). Durango appears in four of the seven. The rest are drivetrain hardware — a front differential with insufficiently hardened pinion gears on the 2019 Durango and Grand Cherokee (W22), and a front driveshaft on 2015-2018 Charger pursuit police vehicles (U37) — plus a Mopar performance module cruise control recall.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The transmission may not shift into 'PARK' and keep the vehicle from moving (2018 park lock rod recall, U43).
  • Loss of power while driving and loss of the transmission PARK function when stationary (2019 Durango and Grand Cherokee front differential recall, W22).
  • The shifter slipping out of the 'drive' position, resulting in a sudden loss of drive power (2022 Charger and Durango recall, ZB3).
  • Coming out of gear while driving, resulting in a sudden loss of drive power (2023 Durango Pursuit shifter boot recall, 94A).
  • Cruise control that cannot be shut off by the brake pedal or manually, with the vehicle maintaining its speed (Mopar Stage 1 Kit and Demon Crate PCM recall, U82/U83).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That includes the 2019 Durango and Grand Cherokee front differential recall (W22), which describes a sudden loss of power while driving, the 2022 Charger and Durango shifter recall (ZB3), the 2023 Durango Pursuit shifter boot recall (94A), and the 2024-2025 Charger EV and Wagoneer S rollaway recall (63C). No interim instruction appears in any of them; each notice describes only the dealer repair for its own vehicles.

What the free repair involves

Parts replacement dominates: dealers install the correct park lock rod (U43), or replace the front driveshaft (U37), the front differential (W22), the shifter assembly (ZB3), the instrument panel mounted shifter boot (94A), or the electric drive module (63C). For the Mopar Stage 1 Kit and Demon Crate powertrain control modules, dealers either perform a software update or replace the PCM. Each notice says free of charge.

Common questions

Why does the Durango appear in several power train recalls?

Four of the seven list Durango: the 2018 park lock rod recall (U43), the 2019 front differential recall (W22), the 2022 shifter assembly recall (ZB3), and the 2023 Durango Pursuit shifter boot recall (94A). Each covers a different part and different model years.

What does the Charger EV park function recall say?

Recall 63C covers certain 2024-2025 Dodge Charger and Jeep Wagoneer S electric vehicles, where an incorrectly installed spring may cause loss of the park function with the gear selector in park, which can result in a rollaway. Dealers replace the electric drive module, free of charge.

Does the driveshaft recall apply to regular Chargers?

Recall U37 covers certain 2015-2018 Dodge Charger pursuit police vehicles equipped with all-wheel drive and V8 engines, where the front driveshaft universal joint may seize or fracture and the shaft may detach. Dealers replace the front driveshaft, free of charge.
