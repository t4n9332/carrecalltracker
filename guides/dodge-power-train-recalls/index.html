Why do Dodge transmissions, shifters, and driveshafts fail unexpectedly?

7 NHTSA recalls · Dodge · Power Train · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-09-04

Seven Dodge powertrain recalls cluster around losing control of gear selection or drive power. Three involve shifters or park function: a 2018 incorrect park lock rod that can keep vehicles from staying in PARK, a 2022 Charger/Durango shifter that wasn't properly heat-treated and may not engage drive, and a 2023 Durango Pursuit shifter that can come out of gear from boot interference. A 2025 Charger EV/Wagoneer S recall covers an incorrectly installed spring that can cause a rollaway from PARK. Separately, a 2015-2018 Charger Pursuit recall covers a driveshaft that can detach, a 2018 Challenger recall covers cruise control that may not disengage, and a 2019 Durango/Grand Cherokee recall covers front differential gears that can wear down and cause a loss of power.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Vehicle does not stay in PARK, or moves unexpectedly after being placed in PARK (2018 park lock rod, 2025 Charger EV/Wagoneer S rollaway)
  • Shifter does not engage drive or slips out of drive while underway (2022 Charger/Durango, 2023 Durango Pursuit)
  • Sudden loss of power while driving (2019 Durango/Grand Cherokee differential)
  • Cruise control that won't shut off with the brake pedal or manual switch (2018 Challenger with Mopar performance PCM)
  • No warning sign is described for the 2015-2018 Charger Pursuit driveshaft-detachment recall

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe risks including unintended vehicle movement, loss of drive power, a detached driveshaft, and cruise control that won't disengage, but do not instruct owners to stop driving. Owners of the affected shifter, park-lock, differential, and driveshaft vehicles should schedule the free dealer repair described in their own recall notice.

What the free repair involves

Dealers install the correct park lock rod, replace the front driveshaft, update or replace the powertrain control module, replace the front differential, or replace the shifter assembly or its boot, depending on the recall. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Can my Dodge roll away even when it's in PARK?

Yes, in two separate recalls: 2018 vehicles with an incorrect park lock rod, and 2024-2025 Dodge Charger EV/Jeep Wagoneer S vehicles with an incorrectly installed spring that can cause the PARK function to fail and the vehicle to roll away.

Are the Dodge Charger and Durango shifter recalls the same issue?

No. The 2022 Charger/Durango recall involves a shifter assembly that wasn't properly heat-treated, while the 2023 Durango Pursuit recall involves boot interference causing the shifter to come out of gear. Each has a separate remedy.

Does the Dodge Charger driveshaft recall apply to all Chargers?

No. It applies only to 2015-2018 Dodge Charger Pursuit police vehicles equipped with all-wheel drive and V8 engines, where the front driveshaft universal joint may seize or fracture and detach.
