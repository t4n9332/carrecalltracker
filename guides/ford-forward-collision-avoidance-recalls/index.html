Why are Ford driver assist cameras and radars recalled for misalignment?

4 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Forward Collision Avoidance · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 14, 2024

All four of these Ford recalls come down to the same thing: a forward-looking sensor pointed the wrong way. Two cover the front camera on the Mustang — 2020 Mustang vehicles with a forward-looking camera, and 2022 Mustang vehicles with an Image Processing Module A or forward-facing camera — where features including Pre-Collision Assist, Adaptive Cruise Control, Lane Keeping System, Driver Alert and Auto High Beam Control may not work as intended. The other two cover the cruise control radar module, misaligned during production on 2021 Ranger and Bronco vehicles and on 2023-2024 Escape two-wheel drive vehicles equipped with adaptive cruise control. Mustang appears in two of the four; Ranger, Bronco and Escape each appear once.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2023-2024 Escape recall states adaptive cruise control may misclassify an overhead object as an object in the vehicle's path, causing the vehicle to unexpectedly slow and/or stop.
  • The 2021 Ranger and Bronco recall states the automatic emergency braking system may react slowly, or not react at all, and the vehicle may not maintain a safe distance when adaptive cruise control is active.
  • The 2020 and 2022 Mustang notices do not describe a warning sign; they state the camera-dependent features may not function as intended.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. The 2020 Mustang, 2022 Mustang, 2021 Ranger and Bronco, and 2023-2024 Escape notices give no interim instruction for owners to follow before the repair. What they do describe is what may go wrong with the systems: camera-dependent features that may not function as intended on the Mustangs, and radar-dependent systems, including automatic emergency braking and adaptive cruise control, that may not respond as intended on the Ranger, Bronco and Escape.

What the free repair involves

On the 2020 Mustang recall, dealers perform the Lane Departure Warning System camera alignment procedure. On the 2022 Mustang recall, dealers realign the front-facing camera. On the 2021 Ranger and Bronco recall, and on the 2023-2024 Escape recall, dealers adjust the angle of the cruise control radar module. Each notice says the work is free of charge; no software update or over-the-air fix is described.

Common questions

Will my dashboard tell me the camera or radar is misaligned?

The notices do not describe a warning light. The 2020 Mustang notice says the driver may be unaware that Pre-Collision Assist is not operating effectively. The 2023-2024 Escape notice says the vehicle may unexpectedly slow and/or stop.

Should I turn off adaptive cruise control until the recall is done?

The notices do not tell owners to turn any system off, and they do not tell owners to stop driving. They describe what may not work: adaptive cruise control, automatic emergency braking and, on the Mustangs, Pre-Collision Assist and Lane Keeping System.

Does the Escape radar recall cover all-wheel drive models?

The notice covers certain 2023-2024 Escape two-wheel drive vehicles equipped with adaptive cruise control. Owners can check whether a specific vehicle is included by its VIN, or contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 and reference Ford's recall number 24S39.
