Why do Ford's forward collision cameras and radar misalign?

4 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Forward Collision Avoidance · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-06-14

Two Mustang recalls, one for 2020 and one for 2022 model-year vehicles, both involve a forward-facing camera that was misaligned during production, which can disable or degrade Pre-Collision Assist, Adaptive Cruise Control, the Lane Keeping System, Driver Alert, and Auto High Beam Control. Separately, two recalls involve a cruise control radar module misaligned during production: one covering 2021 Bronco vehicles, where a misaligned radar can make adaptive cruise control fail to maintain distance and make automatic emergency braking react slowly or not at all, and one covering 2023-2024 Escape two-wheel-drive vehicles, where the radar can misclassify an overhead object as being in the vehicle's path, causing unexpected slowing or stopping.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Adaptive cruise control not maintaining a safe following distance (Bronco)
  • Vehicle unexpectedly slowing or stopping for objects that aren't actually in its path (Escape)
  • Pre-Collision Assist, Lane Keeping System, Driver Alert, or Auto High Beam not functioning as expected (Mustang)
  • The notices describe no dashboard warning light or driver-noticeable sign of the misalignment itself

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For both Mustang camera misalignment recalls, the Bronco radar misalignment recall, and the Escape radar misalignment recall, the notices describe how the misalignment can affect Pre-Collision Assist, Adaptive Cruise Control, Lane Keeping, and automatic emergency braking, but do not instruct owners to stop driving or avoid using the vehicle while a repair is scheduled, even though these systems are meant to help prevent a crash.

What the free repair involves

Dealers perform a camera alignment or realignment procedure for both Mustang recalls, and adjust the angle of the cruise control radar module for the Bronco and Escape recalls. None of these repairs involve replacing the camera or radar hardware itself, and all are done free of charge.

Common questions

What's the difference between the two Mustang camera recalls?

One covers 2020 Mustangs and the other covers 2022 Mustangs; both involve a forward-facing camera misaligned during production that can affect Pre-Collision Assist, Adaptive Cruise Control, Lane Keeping, Driver Alert, and Auto High Beam Control. Dealers realign the camera for each.

Does the Escape radar recall affect all-wheel-drive models?

The notice specifically covers 2023-2024 Escape two-wheel-drive vehicles equipped with adaptive cruise control, where the radar module was misaligned during production. The notice does not describe an all-wheel-drive Escape being included in this recall.

What can happen if the Bronco's radar misalignment isn't fixed?

The notice says a misaligned radar module may prevent the vehicle from maintaining a safe following distance when adaptive cruise control is active, and automatic emergency braking may react slowly or not react at all during a crash-imminent situation.
