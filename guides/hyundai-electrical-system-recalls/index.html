Which Hyundai electrical recalls involve battery fire and power loss?

23 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 2, 2026

⚠ 4 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Hyundai's electrical recalls cluster around high voltage batteries and control software. Kona Electric and Ioniq Electric appear for lithium-ion battery short circuits on 2019-2020 models, while Ioniq 5 appears three times: improperly tightened bus bars on 2025 and on 2025-2026 vehicles with the 2026 Ioniq 9, and misaligned battery cell electrodes on 2023-2024 cars. Software is the other pattern — the Integrated Central Control Unit on the 2022 Tucson, motor control unit software on 2021-2023 Elantra HEV, EPCU and ground wiring on 2017-2019 Ioniq Electric, and hybrid power control unit overheating on 2024-2026 Elantra Hybrid. The ICCU recall reaches across 2022-2024 Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6 and Genesis electrified models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Reduced acceleration and power in fail-safe mode, and acceleration after the gas pedal is released (2017-2019 Ioniq Electric ground wiring recall).
  • Inoperative headlights and/or taillights (2022 Tucson control unit software recall).
  • Unintended acceleration after the brake pedal is released (2021-2023 Elantra HEV motor control unit recall).
  • A loss of drive power when the unit stops charging the 12-volt battery (2022-2024 Ioniq 5, 2023-2024 Ioniq 6 and Genesis ICCU recall).
  • Unintended vehicle movement when the parking assist system malfunctions (2020 Nexo and Sonata recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Four of these notices tell owners to park outside and away from structures: the 2019-2020 Kona Electric battery recall, the 2019-2020 Kona Electric and 2020 Ioniq Electric recall (until the interim action is complete), the 2025 Ioniq 5 bus bar recall, and the 2023-2024 Ioniq 5 battery cell recall, which also tells owners to limit charging to a maximum of 80% until the repair is complete. None of these notices tells owners to stop driving, and the remaining notices give no parking or driving instruction.

What the free repair involves

The remedies named here are free of charge. Dealers update Battery Management System software and inspect the battery, replacing it if necessary (2019-2020 Kona Electric); inspect and repair the high voltage battery system (2025 Ioniq 5); tighten bus bar retention bolts and replace the battery system assembly as necessary (2025-2026 Ioniq 5 and 2026 Ioniq 9); and replace the battery system assembly (2023-2024 Ioniq 5). Other recalls are software updates.

Common questions

Why does my Ioniq 5 appear on more than one battery recall?

Separate notices cover separate defects: improperly tightened bus bars on 2025 vehicles, the same on 2025-2026 vehicles alongside the 2026 Ioniq 9, and misaligned battery cell electrodes on 2023-2024 cars. Your VIN determines which of them applies to your vehicle.

Can I still charge my recalled Hyundai EV at home?

The 2023-2024 Ioniq 5 notice tells owners to limit charging to a maximum of 80% and to park outside, away from structures, until the repair is complete. The other notices in this group do not set out charging limits.

What does the loss of drive power recall actually say about my Ioniq?

The ICCU notice covering 2022-2024 Ioniq 5 and 2023-2024 Ioniq 6 and Genesis electrified models says the unit may become damaged and stop charging the 12-volt battery, which can result in a loss of drive power, increasing crash risk.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
