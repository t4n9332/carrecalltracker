Why do so many Hyundai electrical recalls involve fire risk?

23 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-02

⚠ 4 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

This group spans a wide range of electrical defects, but two patterns stand out. Several recalls involve high-voltage EV battery systems that can short-circuit and catch fire, hitting the Kona Electric, Ioniq Electric, and Ioniq 5 across multiple model years. Others involve unintended acceleration after the driver releases the pedal, affecting the Ioniq Electric and Elantra Hybrid. The remaining recalls cover a mix of issues: a Tucson software fault that can disable headlights or taillights, a Nexo and Sonata remote parking assist that may not stop the vehicle, a Kona 12-Volt battery cable that can chafe and short, and an Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6, and Genesis charging unit that can cause a loss of drive power.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Loss of drive power while driving
  • Vehicle accelerating after the gas or brake pedal is released
  • Headlights or taillights that stop working
  • Remote parking assist that fails to stop the vehicle's movement
  • Notices describe no driver-noticeable warning before a battery short-circuit or fire

Is it safe to keep driving?

Four recalls in this group tell owners to park their vehicle outside and away from structures until repairs are complete: the 2019-2020 Kona Electric battery recall, the 2019-2020 Kona Electric and 2020 Ioniq Electric battery recall, the 2025 Ioniq 5 battery wiring recall, and the 2023-2024 Ioniq 5 battery cell recall, which also tells owners to limit charging to a maximum of 80% until repaired. None of the recalls in this group carry a stop-driving instruction. For the remaining recalls, covering software, wiring, and charging unit issues, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Fixes vary by defect: dealers update battery management, hybrid power control unit, or charging control unit software; inspect and replace battery packs, wiring, or 12-Volt battery cable sheathing as needed; and reprogram parking-assist or motor control software. One recall also offers an interim battery update through the vehicle's infotainment system. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Which Hyundai EVs are covered by the battery fire recalls?

The Kona Electric (2019-2020), Ioniq Electric (2020), and Ioniq 5 (2023-2025) are each covered by separate battery short-circuit or fire-risk recalls in this group, with different causes and repair timelines.

What does 'park outside and away from structures' mean for my Hyundai?

It's an interim safety instruction some of these recalls give owners to follow until the dealer repair is complete, reducing the chance a battery fire spreads to a building. Four recalls in this group include it.

Are the Ioniq 5 recalls in this group all the same issue?

No. The 2025 Ioniq 5 wiring recall, the 2025-2026 Ioniq 5/9 bolt recall, and the 2023-2024 Ioniq 5 battery cell recall are three separate defects with different causes and different instructions.
