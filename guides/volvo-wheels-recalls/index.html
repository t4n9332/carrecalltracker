Volvo Trucks wheel and hubcap recalls: what owners should know

3 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Wheels · model years 2021–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 28, 2026

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

All three recalls in this group come from Volvo Trucks North America and involve heavy-duty tractors rather than passenger cars. The wheel end is the common thread, but each recall names a different part. The 2026 recall covers 2026 VAH, 2026-2027 VN, VHD and VNL (4), and 2027 VNR (4) trucks whose wheel lug nuts may loosen and allow a wheel to detach. A 2021 recall covers 2022 VNL trucks equipped with Alcoa Mirror Polish wheels, part number ULT 391, size 22.5 x 8.25, which may not support the load and can bend or break. A second 2021 recall covers 2021-2022 VAH, VDH, VNL and VNR trucks where the hubcap compression spring can cause the hubcap to separate. VNL appears in all three.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One of the three recalls carries a stop-driving instruction: the wheel lug nut recall on 2026 VAH, 2026-2027 VN, VHD and VNL (4), and 2027 VNR (4) trucks, where owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until the repair has been performed. For the Alcoa Mirror Polish wheel recall on 2022 VNL trucks and the hubcap compression spring recall on 2021-2022 VAH, VDH, VNL and VNR trucks, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. None of the three notices tells owners to park outside.

What the free repair involves

For the lug nut recall, dealers will inspect the wheel and hub mating surfaces, remove any masking material, and replace any worn components if necessary, free of charge. For the Alcoa Mirror Polish wheel recall, dealers will replace the wheels, free of charge. For the hubcap recall, dealers will remove the hubcap, free of charge. No over-the-air update is involved.

Common questions

Which of these Volvo wheel recalls says not to drive the truck?

The wheel lug nut recall on 2026 VAH, 2026-2027 VN, VHD and VNL (4), and 2027 VNR (4) trucks advises owners not to drive until the repair is performed. The other two notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

How do I find out which wheel recall applies to my Volvo truck?

Check your VIN against NHTSA's recall lookup, or call Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586. The notices identify trucks by model and model year, and the Alcoa recall also by wheel part number ULT 391 and size 22.5 x 8.25.

What does the hubcap recall repair involve?

The notice says dealers will remove the hubcap, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 12, 2021. It applies to 2021-2022 VAH, VDH, VNL and VNR trucks where the hubcap compression spring can cause the hubcap to separate.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
