Why are Volvo trucks being recalled for wheel and hubcap problems?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Wheels · model years 2021–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-28

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

These three recalls hit Volvo's VNL line repeatedly through three different wheel-related failure paths. On 2026 VNL trucks, lug nuts may loosen and let a wheel detach. On 2022 VNL trucks fitted with Alcoa Mirror Polish Wheels (part number ULT 391, size 22.5 x 8.25), the wheels may not support the load and can bend or break. On 2021 VNR and 2022 VNL trucks, a hubcap compression spring can let the hubcap separate. Each recall describes a distinct cause - loosened fasteners, wheel load failure, or a spring defect - but the VNL model appears across all three, and every recall centers on wheel or wheel-attachment hardware rather than brakes, engines, or electronics.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Loose lug nuts on the wheels of a 2026 VNL, which the notice says can allow the wheel to detach
  • A wheel that appears bent or cracked, as described in the Alcoa wheel recall for 2022 VNL trucks
  • A hubcap that is loose or has separated from the wheel, as described in the 2021 VNR/2022 VNL hubcap recall
  • The notices do not describe any warning sign that occurs before a wheel or hubcap fails

Is it safe to keep driving?

One of these three recalls - the 2026 VNL wheel lug nut recall - instructs owners not to drive their vehicles until the repair has been performed, since a detached wheel can cause loss of control or create a road hazard. The other two recalls, covering Alcoa wheels that may bend or break on 2022 VNL trucks and hubcaps that can separate on 2021 VNR and 2022 VNL trucks, do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the 2026 VNL recall, dealers inspect the wheel and hub mating surfaces, remove masking material, and replace worn components. For the Alcoa wheel recall, dealers replace the affected wheels on 2022 VNL trucks. For the hubcap recall, dealers remove the affected hubcap on 2021 VNR and 2022 VNL trucks. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Which Volvo trucks are covered by these wheel recalls?

Three separate recalls: 2026 VNL trucks for lug nuts that may loosen, 2022 VNL trucks with Alcoa Mirror Polish Wheels (part ULT 391) that may bend or break, and 2021 VNR plus 2022 VNL trucks for a hubcap that can separate.

Do I need to stop driving my Volvo truck?

Only the 2026 VNL lug nut recall tells owners not to drive until the repair is done. The Alcoa wheel recall and the hubcap recall do not instruct owners to stop driving, according to their notices.

Will I get a letter if my truck is affected?

Owners in the 2026 VNL lug nut recall and the VNR/VNL hubcap recall received mailed notification letters. For the Alcoa wheel recall, the manufacturer says affected vehicles were already contained, so no owners will be notified.
