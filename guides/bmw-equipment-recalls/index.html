BMW equipment recalls: from a charging cable fire risk to tire labels

8 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Equipment · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-05-10

This BMW equipment group is dominated by incorrect labeling: the 2024-2025 X5, the 2021 X7 and the 2022 X4 were each recalled for tire placards or labels showing the wrong tire size or pressure, and R 1250 GS, R 1250 RS and R 1250 RT motorcycles were recalled because their tire pressure labels could fall off. A 2019 X5 was separately recalled for a missing or incorrect airbag warning label on its sun visor. The most serious recall here covers the 2018 X5 and 2019 530e, whose TurboCord portable charger capacitors may fail. Motorcycle recalls also cover darkened accessory windscreens and luggage cases that can block the rear reflectors.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A darker-than-normal accessory windscreen that can limit the rider's view (recalled motorcycle windscreens)
  • Rear reflectors on the motorcycle blocked from view by an installed aluminum luggage case or its mounting bracket
  • A missing or incorrect airbag warning label on the driver or passenger sun visor (2019 X5)
  • A tire placard or label showing tire size and pressure information that doesn't match the tires installed (X5, X7, X4, and the affected motorcycles)
  • The notice describes no noticeable warning sign before the TurboCord charging cable's capacitors fail

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these eight recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the TurboCord charging cable fire and shock risk, the motorcycle windscreen visibility issue, the tire placard and label recalls on the X5, X7, X4 and affected motorcycles, the luggage case reflector recall, and the sun visor airbag label recall do not tell owners to stop driving. Each directs owners to schedule the free dealer repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealer remedies vary by defect: inspecting and replacing the charging cable, replacing the windscreen, replacing tire information placards or labels on the X5, X7, X4 and affected motorcycles, adding reflectors or brackets to luggage cases, and inspecting or replacing sun visors with correct airbag labels. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why are so many different BMW models recalled just for tire labels?

These are four separate recalls, not one: the 2024-2025 X5, 2021 X7 and 2022 X4 each got incorrect tire placards, and R 1250 GS/RS/RT motorcycles got tire pressure labels that could detach.

Is the TurboCord charging cable recall a fire risk?

Yes. The notice says capacitors within the TurboCord portable charger may fail, which can result in a shock hazard or a fire, affecting the 2018 X5 and 2019 530e among other plug-in models.

Do the motorcycle recalls in this group affect visibility?

Yes. The windscreen recall can limit the rider's view, and the luggage case recall can block the rear reflectors from being seen by other drivers.
