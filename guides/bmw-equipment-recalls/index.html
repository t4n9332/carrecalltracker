BMW equipment recalls: wrong labels, chargers and motorcycle accessories

8 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Equipment · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 10, 2024

Labels dominate this BMW group: five of the eight notices concern information that is wrong or missing rather than a broken part. Four involve tires — the 2024-2025 X5 xDrive40i placard, the 2021 X7 xDrive40i and M50i labels, the 2022 X4 xDrive30i label, and tire pressure labels that may not stay affixed to 2021 R 1250 RT, R, RS, GS and GS Adventure motorcycles — and each cites a Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard. A fifth covers incorrect or missing sun visor air bag labels on 2019 X5 xDrive40i and xDrive50i. Motorcycle accessories account for two more: windscreens sold too dark, and optional aluminum luggage cases that may block rear reflex reflectors. One recall covers TurboCord portable chargers.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A tire pressure label that has not remained affixed to the motorcycle frame (2021 R 1250 label recall).
  • An incorrect or missing air bag warning label on the driver or passenger sun visor (2019 X5 recall).
  • A windscreen darker than it should be, which the notice says can inhibit the rider's visibility (accessory windscreen recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these eight notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The tire placard and label recalls, the sun visor air bag label recall, the motorcycle windscreen and luggage case recalls and the TurboCord portable charger recall each describe the risk and the free repair without any instruction to stop driving or to change how the vehicle is used while waiting. Beyond arranging the repair, the notices say nothing about what owners should do in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Most of the work is replacing a label or placard, and every notice says it is free. Dealers replace the tire information placard or label on the X5, X7, X4 and R 1250 motorcycles, inspect the 2019 X5 sun visor and replace it as necessary, replace the accessory windscreen, install reflectors or a reflector bracket for the luggage cases, and inspect the charging cable, replacing it if necessary.

Common questions

Why are so many of these BMW recalls just about labels?

Five of the eight are. Four cover tire size and pressure information on the 2024-2025 X5, 2021 X7, 2022 X4 and 2021 R 1250 motorcycles; the fifth covers sun visor air bag labels on the 2019 X5. Each notice cites a federal safety standard.

What does the TurboCord portable charger recall involve?

The notice says capacitors within the TurboCord Portable Chargers may fail, possibly resulting in a shock hazard or a fire, and that shock or fire can increase the risk of injury or death. Dealers inspect the charging cable and replace it if necessary, free of charge.

Do these recalls cover accessories I bought separately for my motorcycle?

Two do. One covers accessory windscreens sold by model number for installation on various motorcycles; the other covers optional aluminum luggage cases, and on R1200GS models the case mounts, which may partially block rear reflex reflectors. Both remedies are free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
