Why Do Some Nissan Hoods and Doors Open While Driving?

3 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Latches/locks/linkages · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-01-21

Three separate Nissan recalls in this group involve latches or hardware that failed to hold a hood or door closed. The 2018 Altima's secondary hood latch can corrode over time, so if the primary latch releases, the hood may not stay secured. The 2022 Pathfinder has a similar hood latch problem, but caused by insufficient welds on the secondary latch retainer bracket rather than corrosion. Most recently, 2025 Altima and Sentra models were recalled because door strikers may have been improperly welded and can break, letting a door open while the vehicle is moving. Across all three recalls, the underlying issue is a fastening or welding defect in a component meant to keep a hood or door securely shut.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A hood that pops open or feels loose while driving
  • Difficulty getting the secondary hood latch to fully engage (2018 Altima)
  • A door that opens unexpectedly while the vehicle is moving (2025 Altima/Sentra)
  • The 2022 Pathfinder hood latch bracket defect gives no described warning sign before failure

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the three recall notices in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2018 Altima hood latch recall, the 2022 Pathfinder hood latch bracket recall, and the 2025 Altima and Sentra door striker recall each say only that dealers will perform a repair; the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Nissan does note that a hood popping open or a door opening while the vehicle is moving increases the risk of a crash or injury, but no notice directs owners to stop driving in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Across these three recalls, dealers inspect and repair the affected latch or striker hardware at no cost to the owner. For the 2018 Altima, dealers install a stronger release spring, a warning label, and an owners manual addendum. For the 2022 Pathfinder, dealers inspect and replace the hood latch bracket. For the 2025 Altima and Sentra, dealers replace the door strikers. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why is my 2018 Nissan Altima's hood latch being recalled?

Nissan says the secondary hood latch on certain 2018 Altima models can corrode over time. If the primary latch is inadvertently released and the corroded secondary latch doesn't engage, the hood could open while driving.

Which Nissan models are affected by the door striker recall?

The recall covers 2025 Altima and Sentra models. Nissan says the door strikers may have been improperly welded and can break, allowing a door to open while the vehicle is being driven.

What causes the 2022 Nissan Pathfinder hood latch recall?

Nissan says insufficient welds may allow the secondary hood latch retainer bracket to detach from the front bumper assembly, which could let the hood open if the primary latch isn't engaged.
