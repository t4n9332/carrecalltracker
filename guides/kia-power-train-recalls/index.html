Which Kia power train recalls cause lost drive power or rollaway?

8 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Power Train · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 16, 2024

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Kia's power train recalls split into three recurring themes. Driveshafts and axles account for three of the eight: a left front axle driveshaft that may not have been heat-treated on 2019 Forte, an improperly heat-treated drive shaft on 2023-2024 Niro EV and 2023 EV6 (SC295), and loose differential gear bolts on 2023 Niro EV and 2024 EV9 (SC308). Vehicle movement in Park is the second theme, covering 2015-2019 Soul EV (SC287) and 2020-2024 Telluride. Fire risk is the third: an Idle Stop & Go oil pump controller on 2023 Soul and Sportage and 2023-2024 Seltos, and a hydraulic clutch actuator on 2017-2022 Niro and 2018-2022 Niro Plug-in Hybrid (SC276). A 2022 recall covers drive power loss on 2021-2022 Sorento and 2021-2023 K5.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A loss of drive power — the 2019 Forte axle recall, SC295 (Niro EV, EV6), SC308 (Niro EV, EV9), and the 2021-2022 Sorento and 2021-2023 K5 recall.
  • Unintended vehicle movement with the gear shift lever in Park and the parking brake not applied — the 2015-2019 Soul EV recall (SC287).
  • Roll away while in PARK — the 2020-2024 Telluride recall.
  • The differential may lock up — SC308 (2023 Niro EV, 2024 EV9).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One of these eight notices carries a park-outside instruction: the Idle Stop & Go oil pump recall covering 2023 Soul and Sportage and 2023-2024 Seltos, where owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. No notice in this group tells owners to stop driving. The 2020-2024 Telluride notice advises owners of those vehicles to manually engage the emergency parking brake before exiting until the remedy is performed. The remaining notices give owners no interim instruction beyond having the recall work done.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace drive shafts (SC295), the gear drive unit (SC308), and the 2019 Forte left front axle driveshaft when its heat treatment code is missing. The oil pump controller and SC276's hydraulic clutch actuator are inspected and replaced as necessary, with a new fuse installed. Software updates cover the Soul EV parking mechanism (SC287), the Telluride parking brake, and the Sorento and K5 transmission control unit. All free of charge.

Common questions

Which of these Kia recalls tells owners to park outside?

The Idle Stop & Go oil pump recall — covering 2023 Soul and Sportage and 2023-2024 Seltos — advises owners to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. Kia says damaged components in the controller can make the pump overheat.

My Telluride is in this group — what does its notice ask me to do?

The 2020-2024 Telluride notice advises owners to manually engage the emergency parking brake before exiting until the remedy is performed. Dealers update the electronic parking brake software and replace damaged intermediate shafts as necessary, free of charge. The notice ties this to shafts that may not be fully engaged.

How many of these Kia power train recalls involve electric models?

Three. SC287 covers 2015-2019 Soul EV, SC295 covers 2023-2024 Niro EV and 2023 EV6, and SC308 covers 2023 Niro EV and 2024 EV9. The Niro Plug-in Hybrid appears separately in SC276, alongside 2017-2022 Niro.
