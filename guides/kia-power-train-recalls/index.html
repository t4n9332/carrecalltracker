Why do some Kia models lose power or roll away?

8 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Power Train · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-04-16

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Kia's power train recalls cluster around drive shafts and drivetrain electronics failing in different ways. Several models can lose drive power from broken or improperly built components: the 2019 Forte's untreated axle driveshaft, the 2023 EV6 and 2023-2024 Niro EV's improperly heat-treated drive shaft, and loose differential gear bolts in the 2023 Niro EV and 2024 EV9. The 2021-2022 Sorento and 2021-2023 K5 can lose all drive power when a transmission oil pump malfunction disables the fail-safe mode. Separately, fire risk appears in the 2023-2024 Seltos, 2023 Soul, and 2023 Sportage from an overheating oil pump, and in the 2021-2022 Niro from a leaking hydraulic clutch actuator. The 2020-2024 Telluride and 2018-2019 Soul EV both involve unwanted vehicle movement while parked.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A sudden loss of drive power while driving (Forte, K5, Sorento, EV6, Niro EV, and EV9 recalls)
  • The vehicle rolling away or moving unexpectedly after being shifted into Park (Telluride and Soul EV recalls)
  • A fire in the engine compartment (Niro recall) or an overheating oil pump assembly (Seltos, Soul, and Sportage recall)
  • The notices do not describe a dashboard warning light or other advance sign for any of these defects

Is it safe to keep driving?

Of these eight power train recalls, one — covering the 2023-2024 Seltos, 2023 Soul, and 2023 Sportage — tells owners to park outside and away from structures until the repair is done, because of fire risk. For the 2020-2024 Telluride, Kia tells owners to manually engage the parking brake before exiting the vehicle until the recall remedy is performed. None of the other six recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; their notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Repairs vary by defect: dealers replace driveshafts, transmissions, gear drive units, or hydraulic clutch actuators as needed; update transmission, engine, or parking brake software; or replace an oil pump controller. All inspections, part replacements, and software updates across these eight recalls are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Kia power train recall tells owners to park outside?

The recall covering the 2023-2024 Seltos, 2023 Soul, and 2023 Sportage tells owners to park outside and away from structures until repaired, because a damaged Idle Stop & Go oil pump controller can overheat and increase the risk of a vehicle fire.

Why does my Kia Telluride need the parking brake engaged?

Kia recalled 2020-2024 Telluride vehicles because the intermediate shaft and right front driveshaft may not fully engage, letting the vehicle roll away while in Park. Owners are told to manually engage the parking brake before exiting until it's repaired.

Do any Kia power train recalls tell owners to stop driving?

No recall in this group tells owners to stop driving entirely. One recall (Seltos, Soul, Sportage) instructs parking outside due to fire risk, and the Telluride recall adds a parking brake step, but driving itself is not prohibited by these notices.
