Why do Honda's Equipment recalls mostly involve labels and manuals?

7 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Equipment · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-07-27

Most of the seven recalls in this Honda equipment group involve incorrect or missing paperwork rather than a mechanical defect: the 2017-2018 Civic Hatchback's owner's guide was missing required child-seat anchorage information, the 2019 CR-V's owner's guide misstated how the passenger airbag indicator works, the 2020 and 2022 CR-V's door label listed the wrong weight and tire ratings, the 2018 Civic's certification label could show a garbled date, and the 2020 Passport and Pilot's weight rating label could wipe off. The two mechanical exceptions are the Ridgeline's accessory tonneau cover, which can flip and separate a rear panel if not properly secured, and the Clarity Fuel Cell's water pump, which can corrode and fail.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No mechanical warning sign for the label and owner's guide recalls - the defect is incorrect or missing printed information, not something you'd feel or hear while driving
  • Tonneau cover flips or buckles and a rear panel separates from the Ridgeline bed cover
  • Vehicle stalls while driving (Clarity Fuel Cell water pump failure)
  • Certification or door label text is missing, garbled, or can be wiped away (affected Civic and CR-V labels)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. That includes the Clarity Fuel Cell water pump recall, where a failure can cause the vehicle to stall, and the Ridgeline tonneau cover recall, where a separated panel can become a road hazard - the notices direct owners to get the free repair or replacement part but do not tell owners to stop driving. The label and owner's guide recalls, which do not describe a mechanical failure, also carry no stop-driving instruction.

What the free repair involves

For the label and owner's guide recalls, dealers replace the certification label, owner's information kit, or provide a corrected sticker. For the mechanical recalls, dealers replace the water pump on the Clarity Fuel Cell, or add tethers and warning labels to the Ridgeline's tonneau cover to stop the panel from separating. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why would Honda recall an owner's manual instead of a part?

Federal safety standards require owner's guides to contain accurate safety information, such as child-seat anchorage locations or how the passenger airbag indicator works. When a Honda guide has missing or wrong information, that counts as a safety recall even without a mechanical defect.

Is the Ridgeline tonneau cover recall about the truck or an accessory?

It covers the Honda Genuine Accessory Tonneau Cover sold for 2017-2020 Ridgeline trucks, not the truck itself. Dealers install tethers and warning labels to keep the rear panel from separating if the cover isn't properly secured.

Could a mislabeled certification sticker actually cause a problem?

Yes - the notices say it can. On the 2020 and 2022 CR-V, a wrong tire size on the door label could lead an owner to use an incorrectly sized tire, which the notice says increases the risk of premature wear or a blowout.
