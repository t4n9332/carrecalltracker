Why is Honda recalling door labels and owner's guides?

7 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Equipment · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-07-27

Honda's equipment recalls are mostly paperwork and labels rather than parts. Three cover the certification label on the driver's door: random characters in the manufacturing date area on the 2018 Civic, ink that wipes off on the 2020 Passport and Pilot, and wrong weight ratings and tire size on 2017, 2020 and 2022 CR-V vehicles. Two more cover printed information: an owners guide on 2017-2018 Civic Hatchback and Type R, and a wrong airbag indicator description in the 2019 CR-V owner's guide. Only two involve hardware: a water pump that can corrode on 2017-2018 Clarity Fuel Cell vehicles, and a Honda accessory tonneau cover sold for 2017-2020 Ridgeline trucks.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The vehicle may stall if the water pump fails (2017-2018 Clarity Fuel Cell water pump recall, L4N).
  • A cover not properly secured open or closed may flip and buckle in wind, deforming the hinges between the center and rear panel (Ridgeline accessory tonneau cover recall).
  • The manufacturing date area of the certification label may show random characters (2018 Civic label recall, U23).
  • The certification label's weight rating information can be wiped away with a solvent (2020 Passport and Pilot label recall, B6T).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park outside; none gives an interim instruction. The notices state their own risks: the Clarity Fuel Cell notice says a water pump failure can cause the vehicle to stall, increasing crash risk; the Ridgeline tonneau cover notice says a separated panel may become a road hazard; the CR-V label notice says an incorrectly sized tire may wear early or blow out; and the Passport and Pilot label notice says the vehicle may be overloaded, increasing crash risk.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the Clarity Fuel Cell water pump and install a new certification label on the 2018 Civic, the 2017, 2020 and 2022 CR-V, and the 2020 Passport and Pilot. For the Civic Hatchback and Type R, dealers replace the owners information kit; the 2019 CR-V gets a correction sticker for the owner's guide; and tonneau covers get tethers, warning labels and an updated manual. All free of charge.

Common questions

Why would a wrong door label matter?

The CR-V label notice says incorrect weight rating and tire size information can lead to an incorrectly sized tire, which may wear prematurely or blow out. The 2020 Passport and Pilot notice says a missing rating can leave the vehicle overloaded, increasing crash risk.

I have a Honda tonneau cover on my Ridgeline — what is the recall?

It covers Honda Genuine Accessory Tonneau Cover part number 08Z07-T6Z-100F sold for 2017-2020 Ridgeline trucks. Dealers install tethers to prevent panel separation, apply warning labels and provide an updated accessory user's information manual, free of charge.

Is there anything to do for an owner's guide recall?

The notices say Honda will replace the owners information kit for the 2017-2018 Civic Hatchback and Type R, and provide a sticker with correct airbag indicator information for the 2019 CR-V owner's guide, both free of charge. Owners may contact Honda at 1-888-234-2138.
