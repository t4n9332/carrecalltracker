Tesla electrical system recalls affecting cameras, software and drive power

14 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Electrical System · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 10, 2025

Tesla's listed electrical recalls repeatedly involve software-controlled functions across Model 3, Model S, Model X and Model Y. Two Boombox notices describe external sounds obscuring pedestrian warnings; other notices cover rolling stops, a missing seat belt chime, reduced defrosting and insufficient Autosteer controls. Model 3 appears most often, particularly the 2017-2022 vehicles included in several software notices. Hardware defects include the 2017-2020 Model 3 rearview camera cable and the 2023 Model 3 and Model Y battery disconnect. Later notices cover the 2024 Cybertruck inverter, 2026 Model Y horn and reverse lights, and battery contactors on 2025 Model 3 and 2026 Model Y vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Model 3 rearview camera cable recall, SB-21-17-008: the rearview camera image may not display.
  • Seat belt chime recall, SB-22-00-002: the chime may not sound at startup when the driver is unbuckled.
  • 2024 Cybertruck drive inverter recall, SB-24-40-009: drive power to the wheels may be lost.
  • 2026 Model Y horn recall, SB-25-17-009: the horn may not sound.
  • Battery contactor recall, SB-25-16-005: affected 2025 Model 3 and 2026 Model Y vehicles may lose drive power.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the supplied Tesla electrical system notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. This includes both Boombox notices and the notices covering the rearview camera cable, battery disconnect, Autosteer controls, Cybertruck inverter, Model Y horn, Model Y reverse lights, battery contactors, rolling stops, seat belt chime and defrosting performance. The inverter and contactor notices describe possible loss of drive power; the battery disconnect notice describes electrical shock risk after a crash or fault detection. Those notices also do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies include OTA updates for both Boombox recalls, Autosteer, rolling stops, seat belt chime and defrosting. Camera service inspects and installs a guide protector/new harness as necessary. Other recalls replace their specified parts: battery disconnect; Cybertruck inverter; Model Y steering wheel for the horn; Model Y reverse lights/reverse facia harness; and battery contactors for affected Model 3/Model Y vehicles.

Common questions

Which Tesla electrical recalls use an over-the-air update?

The supplied notices specify free OTA updates for both Boombox recalls, Autosteer controls, rolling-stop functionality, the startup seat belt chime and reduced defrosting performance. The other supplied notices specify physical inspection, installation or replacement remedies through Tesla Service.

What do the two Tesla Boombox remedies disable?

The February 2022 notice disables Boombox in Drive, Neutral and Reverse through a free OTA update. The April 2022 notice specifies those modes, including Summon and Smart Summon. Both notices describe Boombox sounds potentially obscuring pedestrian warning sounds.

Which Tesla electrical recalls describe loss of drive power?

The 2024 Cybertruck notice describes an inverter fault and specifies free inverter replacement. The battery contactor notice covers certain 2025 Model 3 and 2026 Model Y vehicles and specifies free contactor replacement. Neither notice tells owners to stop driving.
