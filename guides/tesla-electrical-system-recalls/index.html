What do Tesla's electrical system recalls cover, from cameras to software?

11 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Electrical System · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-11-05

Software dominates this group: eight of the eleven Tesla recalls are remedied with an over-the-air software update rather than a part. Displays and alerts repeat — SB-22-00-002 for a seat belt chime that may not sound, SB-22-00-008 for 2018-2022 Model 3 Performance speedometers in Track Mode, and SB-24-00-003 for Brake, Park and ABS warning lights shown in an incorrect font size. Two Boombox recalls, from February and April 2022, cover external sounds that may obscure the Pedestrian Warning System. Hardware appears too: SB-21-17-008 covers all 2017-2020 Model 3 rearview camera harnesses, SB-23-16-005 the pyrotechnic battery disconnect on 2023 Model 3 and Model Y, and SB-24-40-009 the 2024 Cybertruck drive inverter.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The rearview camera image does not display because the cable harness may be damaged by the trunk lid (recall SB-21-17-008).
  • The audible chime may not activate when the vehicle starts and the driver has not buckled their seat belt (recall SB-22-00-002).
  • Decreased windshield defrosting performance (recall SB-22-18-002).
  • A loss of drive power to the wheels (recall SB-24-40-009, 2024 Cybertruck).
  • Brake, Park and Antilock Brake System warning lights displayed in an incorrect, smaller font size (recall SB-24-00-003).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these eleven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. They describe risks in their own terms: loss of drive power to the wheels (SB-24-40-009), a battery disconnect that may not isolate the high voltage battery after a crash or fault detection (SB-23-16-005), a reduced rear view (SB-21-17-008), reduced visibility from decreased defrosting (SB-22-18-002), and driver misuse of Autosteer (SB-23-00-008). Beyond the remedies listed, the notices give owners no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Most are over-the-air software updates, free of charge: Boombox is disabled in Drive, Neutral and Reverse, the rolling stop function is disabled, and updates address the seat belt chime, defrosting, speedometer display, warning light font and Autosteer controls. Three require service work, also free: a guide protector and new cable harness as necessary (SB-21-17-008), a new pyrotechnic battery disconnect (SB-23-16-005), and a new drive inverter (SB-24-40-009).

Common questions

Will my Tesla fix itself, or do I need an appointment?

The notices for the Boombox, rolling stop, seat belt chime, defrosting, speedometer, warning light font and Autosteer recalls say Tesla performs or releases an over-the-air software update, free of charge. The camera harness, battery disconnect and drive inverter recalls are handled by Tesla service, also free of charge.

Why are there two Boombox recalls listed for my Tesla?

The notices do not say. The February 2022 notice covers 2020-2022 Model S, Model X, Model Y and 2017-2022 Model 3; the April 2022 notice covers 2020-2022 Model Y, Model X, Model S and 2017-2022 Model 3, and its update also disables Boombox during Summon and Smart Summon.

Can the Cybertruck drive inverter fault cut power to the wheels?

SB-24-40-009 covers certain 2024 Cybertruck vehicles and states that a fault in the drive inverter may cause a loss of drive power to the wheels. Tesla service replaces the drive inverter, free of charge. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.
