Tesla Electrical System Recalls: Camera, Warning Sound, and Power Problems

14 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Electrical System · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 10, 2025

Tesla’s electrical-system notices span software controls, warning functions, camera wiring, and propulsion hardware. Model 3 appears repeatedly, including 2017-2020 vehicles with rearview-camera harness damage from trunk-lid movement and several software recalls covering 2017-2022 vehicles. Two 2022 Boombox notices describe external audio obscuring pedestrian warning sounds; separate notices address rolling stops, missing seat belt chimes, reduced defrosting, and Autosteer misuse controls. Hardware problems include defective battery disconnects in certain 2023 Model 3/Y vehicles and drive-inverter faults in certain 2024 Cybertrucks. Certain 2026 Model Y vehicles appear in separate horn, reverse-light wiring, and battery-contactor notices; the contactor notice also covers certain 2025 Model 3 vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • December 21, 2021 Model 3 camera-cable recall: the rearview camera image may not display.
  • November 5, 2024 Cybertruck drive-inverter recall: drive power to the wheels may be lost.
  • July 28, 2025 Model Y horn recall: the horn may not sound.
  • October 2, 2025 Model Y reverse-light recall: reverse lights may fail to illuminate in reverse.
  • October 10, 2025 battery-contactor recall: drive power may be lost.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The supplied notices do not tell owners to stop driving for the camera harness, either Boombox notice, battery disconnect, Autosteer controls, Cybertruck inverter, Model Y horn, reverse-light wiring, battery contactors, rolling-stop software, seat belt chime, or defrosting software recalls. None of the supplied notices carries a park-outside instruction. These statements describe the instructions in the supplied notices; they do not establish that an affected Tesla is safe to drive or that a missing warning, camera image, or electrical function is acceptable.

What the free repair involves

Free OTA remedies address Boombox, rolling stops, seat belt chimes, defrosting, and Autosteer. Free hardware remedies include camera-harness inspection and a guide protector/new harness as necessary; battery-disconnect replacement; Cybertruck inverter replacement; Model Y steering-wheel replacement for the horn recall; reverse-light and reverse facia harness replacement for the reverse-light recall; and contactor replacement for the battery-contactor recall.

Common questions

Which Tesla electrical recalls describe loss of drive power?

The November 2024 notice identifies a drive-inverter fault in certain 2024 Cybertrucks. The October 2025 notice identifies failing battery pack contactors in certain 2025 Model 3 and 2026 Model Y vehicles. Their remedies replace the respective components free of charge.

What do the two Boombox recall remedies change?

The February 2022 remedy disables Boombox in Drive, Neutral, and Reverse. The April 2022 remedy specifies those modes, including Summon and Smart Summon. Both notices describe free OTA updates addressing sounds that may obscure the Pedestrian Warning System.

Why does the Model Y horn recall replace the steering wheel?

The July 2025 notice says an improperly secured horn ground-wire ring terminal may allow the circuit to open, preventing the horn from sounding in certain 2026 Model Y vehicles. The specified remedy is free steering-wheel replacement.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
