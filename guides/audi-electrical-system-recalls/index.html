Audi Electrical System Recalls Involving Batteries, Charging, and Power Loss

16 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 21, 2026

Battery overheating and short-circuit fire risks recur across Audi’s e-tron and plug-in hybrid notices. The e-tron Quattro appears for 2019–2022 battery-module overheating, while the E-Tron GT and RS E-Tron GT appear for 2022–2024 battery-module short circuits. Q5 battery notices cover 2022–2023 PHEVs and, separately, 2021–2024 Q5 vehicles; both also identify 2022 A7 plug-in hybrids. The 2024–2025 Q4 E-Tron and Q4 Sportback E-Tron have a battery-failure fire notice. Other recurring electrical concerns involve moisture: the 2019 e-tron charging-socket seal, 2019–2020 A6 starter generators, and Q5/SQ5 gateway modules. Separate notices address portable charging cables, door crash sensors, and inaccurate fuel readings.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2019 e-tron charging-socket seal recall (93E8): warning lights together with the message "Electrical system: fault."
  • 2021 Q5/SQ5 gateway control module recall (90S9): suddenly reduced engine power.
  • 2022 fuel level sender recall (20DN): an inaccurate fuel reading on the instrument cluster and possible engine stalling from lack of fuel.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One notice gives a conditional stop-driving and park-outside instruction: the June 2019 e-tron seal recall says to stop immediately and park outdoors away from structures if warning lights accompany “Electrical system: fault.” The remaining notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The March 2024 E-Tron GT and October 2026 Q4 E-Tron recalls limit charging to 80% until repair. The December 2023 portable-cable recall says to avoid the 220V/240V cable and use the 110V home cable or public charging stations. The November 2024 and February 2025 battery notices describe contacting owners as necessary with instructions not to charge.

What the free repair involves

Specified free dealer remedies include a new charging-socket seal and affected components for 93E8, a starter generator for 27H2, and crash-sensor inspection and replacement as necessary for 69BM. Recall 90S9 adds a gateway cover and seam sealing as necessary; 20DN replaces the tank sensor. Selected battery notices specify free diagnostic software or module replacement. The October 2026 Q4 remedy remains under development.

Common questions

Which Audi electrical recall tells me to stop when a dashboard warning appears?

The June 2019 e-tron charging-socket seal recall, 93E8, names warning lights together with “Electrical system: fault.” Its notice instructs owners encountering that combination to stop driving immediately and park outdoors away from structures.

Do the E-Tron GT and Q4 E-Tron battery notices give the same charging limit?

The March 2024 E-Tron GT and RS E-Tron GT notice and the October 2026 Q4 E-Tron and Q4 Sportback E-Tron notice both specify a maximum 80% charge until repair. Their remedies differ: battery-data analysis and module replacement as necessary versus a remedy under development.

What does Audi replace for the inaccurate fuel gauge recall?

Recall 20DN specifies installing a new sensor in the tank, free of charge. The notice says the fuel level sender may stick, producing an inaccurate instrument-cluster reading that can lead to running out of fuel and an engine stall.
