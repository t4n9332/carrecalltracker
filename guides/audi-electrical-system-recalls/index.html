Audi Electrical System Recalls Include Battery Fires and Power Loss

16 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 21, 2026

Battery overheating and short circuits dominate these Audi electrical notices, especially among e-tron and plug-in hybrid models. Certain 2019 e-tron vehicles have a charging-socket seal defect allowing moisture into the high-voltage system. Other notices address overheating modules in certain 2019-2022 e-tron Quattro and 2020-2022 e-tron Sportback Quattro vehicles, and battery-module short circuits in certain 2022-2024 e-tron GT and RS e-tron GT vehicles. Q5 battery notices cover differing populations, including certain 2021-2024 Q5 vehicles in the February 2025 notice. Separate concerns include Q5/SQ5 gateway water exposure, A6 starter-generator moisture, sticking fuel senders, detachable crash-sensor connectors and portable charging cable overheating.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2019 e-tron moisture recall 93E8: warning lights together with the message "Electrical system: fault."
  • Q5/SQ5 gateway recall 90S9: suddenly reduced engine power.
  • Fuel sender recall 20DN: an inaccurate fuel level reading on the instrument cluster and possible engine stall from lack of fuel.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One supplied notice carries conditional stop-driving and park-outside instructions: 2019 e-tron recall 93E8 says that if warning lights appear with "Electrical system: fault," immediately stop driving and park outdoors away from structures. The other supplied notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The March 2024 e-tron GT and October 2026 Q4 notices limit charging to 80% until repair. The December 2023 cable notice prohibits the 220V/240V portable cable, specifying the 110V home cable or public charging stations.

What the free repair involves

Recall 93E8 installs a seal and replaces affected components as necessary; 27H2 replaces the starter generator. The December 2023 e-tron battery notice installs monitoring software and replaces affected modules where necessary. Recall 90S9 adds a protective cover and seals an underbody seam as necessary; 20DN installs a tank sensor. These specified remedies are free.

Common questions

Which Audi electrical notice tells owners to stop driving?

The 2019 e-tron moisture notice, 93E8, gives a conditional instruction: if warning lights appear with "Electrical system: fault," immediately stop driving and park outdoors away from structures. The other supplied electrical notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

Which battery recalls specify an 80% charging limit?

The March 2024 notice for certain 2022-2024 e-tron GT and RS e-tron GT vehicles specifies an 80% maximum until repair. The October 2026 notice gives the same limit for certain 2024-2025 Q4 e-tron and Q4 Sportback e-tron vehicles.

What does the portable charging cable notice tell owners to use?

The December 2023 notice advises owners to stop using the 220V/240V compact or portable charging cable and use only the 110V home charging cable or public charging stations. Dealers supply a replacement cable incorporating a temperature sensor, free of charge.
