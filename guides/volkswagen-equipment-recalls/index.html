Why is Volkswagen recalling vehicles over incorrect labels?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Equipment · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-02-16

These three Volkswagen recalls all involve incorrect or missing labels rather than a mechanical defect. The 2019 Tiguan LWB has a tire information label with inaccurate tire dimension and inflation pressure specifications. Two 2018 Golf GTI vehicles have a certification label printed with a VIN that does not match the vehicle's actual VIN. The 2018-2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport equipped with accessory side steps are missing a reduced Gross Vehicle Weight Rating label that should reflect the added weight of the side steps. Each recall corrects federally required labeling rather than repairing a vehicle part.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No driving symptom - these are labeling defects, not mechanical failures
  • Tiguan: the tire placard lists tire size and pressure numbers that don't match the required specifications
  • Golf GTI: the VIN printed on the certification label doesn't match the vehicle's actual VIN
  • Atlas with accessory side steps: the reduced weight-rating label is missing from the vehicle

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the Tiguan tire label recall, the Golf GTI VIN label recall, and the Atlas reduced GVWR label recall, the notices describe risks such as improper tire inflation, difficulty confirming recall coverage, or vehicle overloading, but do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers install a corrected tire information label on affected Tiguan vehicles, a corrected certification label with the accurate VIN on the two affected Golf GTI vehicles, and a reduced GVWR label on affected Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles fitted with accessory side steps. All three repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why is an incorrect label treated as a safety recall?

Federal safety standards require accurate tire, certification, and weight-rating labels. Incorrect specifications on the Tiguan, a mismatched VIN on the Golf GTI, or a missing weight label on the Atlas can each fail those federal requirements.

Does the Atlas label recall affect every Atlas?

No. It only applies to 2018-2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport vehicles equipped with accessory side steps, which need a reduced GVWR label reflecting the steps' added weight.

How many Golf GTI vehicles are affected by the VIN label recall?

The notice covers only two 2018 Golf GTI vehicles, whose certification label VIN does not match the vehicle's actual VIN. Owners should contact Volkswagen to confirm if their vehicle is one of them.
