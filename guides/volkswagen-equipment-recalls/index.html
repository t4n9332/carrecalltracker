Why does Volkswagen recall Tiguan and Atlas information labels?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Equipment · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 16, 2022

All three Volkswagen equipment recalls are about labels rather than mechanical parts, and each one involves different vehicles. The 2019 Tiguan LWB recall covers a tire information label printed with inaccurate tire dimension and inflation pressure specifications, which the notice ties to Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 110. The 2018-2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport recall covers vehicles fitted with accessory side steps, where the reduced Gross Vehicle Weight Rating label reflecting the extra weight was not installed. The third is much narrower: two 2018 Golf GTI vehicles whose Federal Certification label may show a VIN that does not match the vehicle. Tiguan, Atlas and Golf GTI each appear once, and label wording is the recurring theme.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2019 Tiguan LWB tire information label notice does not tell owners to stop driving. The 2018-2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport reduced GVWR label notice does not tell owners to stop driving. The two-vehicle 2018 Golf GTI certification label notice does not tell owners to stop driving. The notices describe risks tied to improper tire inflation, wrong tire size, overloading and not knowing whether a vehicle is under recall, but they give owners no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Each remedy is a label replacement and free of charge. For the 2019 Tiguan LWB recall, dealers install a corrected tire information label. For the 2018-2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport recall, dealers install the reduced GVWR label. For the two 2018 Golf GTI vehicles, dealers install a correct certification label. No inspection, part replacement or software update is described in these notices.

Common questions

Does a wrong label really need a recall visit?

The notices treat each as a safety standard or certification non-compliance and say dealers install the corrected label free of charge. They do not tell owners the label can be ignored, and they do not describe any alternative to the dealer visit.

Which tire pressure should I use on my 2019 Tiguan now?

The notice says the tire information label on 2019 Tiguan LWB vehicles carries inaccurate tire dimension and inflation pressure specifications, and that dealers install a corrected label free of charge. Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 can answer questions about your vehicle.

Why does my Atlas need a new weight rating label?

The recall covers 2018-2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport vehicles equipped with accessory side steps, where the reduced Gross Vehicle Weight Rating label was not installed. The notice says the vehicle could be overloaded, leading to a loss of control.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
