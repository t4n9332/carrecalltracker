Why is Mercedes-Benz recalling vehicles for rearview camera failures?

6 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-06-05

Rearview camera failures make up most of Mercedes-Benz's back-over prevention recalls, and the same MBUX/Connect multimedia software issue recurs across several model lines. 2019-2020 Sprinter vans with the Connect 5 system can show a delayed image or have it covered by an eCall pop-up. 2021 AMG models (A35, CLA35, CLA45, CLS53, E53, GLB35, GLC43, GLC63) and a separate 2018-2021 AMG group (C43, C63 S, CLA35, CLA45, GLB35) can have a black screen or software error blocking the image. 2015-2018 Metris vans have a hardware issue instead: a defective memory chip that freezes the display. A 2025 recall covers a different system — an incorrect pedestrian warning sound while reversing on EQS SUV and S 580 E models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rearview camera image not displaying within 2 seconds of backing up, or covered by an eCall pop-up (2019-2020 Sprinter vans)
  • Rearview camera display going black or rebooting (2021 AMG A35/CLA/CLS53/E53/GLB35/GLC43/GLC63 and 2019-2021 Sprinter)
  • Rearview camera image freezing instead of updating (2015-2018 Metris)
  • Rearview camera not displaying at all due to a software error (2018-2021 AMG C43/C63 S/CLA/GLB35 and 2019-2021 Sprinter)
  • Vehicle making the wrong warning sound while reversing (2025 EQS SUV, S 580 E)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six back-over prevention recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving for the 2019-2020 Sprinter Connect 5 camera delay, the 2021 AMG camera black-screen recall, the 2018 Metris camera freeze, the 2021 AMG rearview software error recall, the 2019-2021 Sprinter rearview software error recall, or the 2025 EQS SUV/S 580 E pedestrian warning sound recall.

What the free repair involves

Across this group, dealers update multimedia or camera software — on some AMG and Sprinter models this can be done over the air instead of a dealer visit — to restore the rearview image or correct the eCall pop-up conflict. The Metris recall instead requires dealers to replace the physical camera. The 2025 pedestrian sound recall is fixed with an AVAS software update. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Can I still back up safely with a rearview camera recall?

The notices don't tell owners to stop driving, but they do state the rearview image may be delayed, blocked, frozen, or missing entirely, so drivers should rely on mirrors and caution when reversing until the recall repair is done.

Is the Mercedes-Benz rearview camera recall fixed with a software update or a new part?

Most recalls in this group are fixed with a software update, sometimes performed over the air. The 2015-2018 Metris recall is different — it requires dealers to replace the physical camera due to a defective memory chip.

Why did Mercedes-Benz recall vehicles for the wrong reversing sound?

On certain 2025 EQS SUV and S 580 E models, a software error in the Acoustic Vehicle Alerting System can make the vehicle emit an incorrect pedestrian warning sound while reversing, which dealers fix with a free software update.
