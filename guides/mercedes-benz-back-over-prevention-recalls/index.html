Mercedes-Benz backup camera recalls: blank, frozen and delayed rear views

6 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 5, 2025

Five of these six recalls are about the rearview camera image failing to show what is behind the vehicle, and software is the common cause. MBUX software that leaves the display black or reboots after startup covers 2019-2021 A-Class, CLA, E-Class, GLA/GLB, GLC, GLE/GLS, CLS and AMG GT 4-door models; a separate software error stops the camera displaying on 2018-2021 models including the C-Class, and on 2019-2021 Sprinter vans. The Sprinter also appears for a Connect 5 image that may not display within two seconds or may be covered by an eCall pop-up, and 2015-2018 Metris vans for a camera memory chip that freezes the image. The sixth is different: AVAS software producing the wrong pedestrian sound while reversing on 2022-2025 S-Class, EQE and EQS models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall 2021050012 (MBUX): the display may remain black or reboot after startup, so the rearview camera image does not display properly.
  • Recall VS2RU54KAM (2015-2018 Metris): the rearview camera display image may freeze.
  • Recall VS3RUECON (Sprinter with Connect 5): the rearview image may not display within two seconds of the start of backing, or may be covered by the eCall pop-up window.
  • Recalls 2022060007 and VS3KOMRUE: the rearview camera may not display the rearview image.
  • Recall 2025070005 (S-Class, EQE, EQS): the vehicle may make an incorrect external pedestrian warning sound while reversing.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices carries a stop-driving instruction, and none tells owners to park outside. That applies to the Connect 5 Sprinter recall, the MBUX recall, the Metris camera recall, the two rearview camera software recalls and the AVAS sound recall. What the notices state is the effect each fault has: a delayed, obscured, blank or frozen image reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, and an incorrect sound may fail to alert pedestrians that the vehicle is reversing. Each increases the risk of a crash, in the notices' words.

What the free repair involves

Five of the six are software fixes. Dealers update the Connect 5 software, the rearview camera software on the cars and on the Sprinter vans, and the AVAS software on the S-Class, EQE and EQS. For the MBUX recall the multimedia software is updated by a dealer or over the air. For the Metris, dealers replace the rearview camera. All six notices state the work is free of charge.

Common questions

My Mercedes is listed in more than one backup camera recall — what now?

Different notices cover different faults: MBUX software leaving the display black, a separate rearview camera software error, and on vans a Connect 5 display delay. Each recall has its own remedy and its own owner notification letter from Mercedes-Benz or Daimler Vans.

Can the backup camera recall be fixed over the air?

Only the MBUX recall mentions that option: its notice says the multimedia system software will be updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update. The other camera and AVAS notices say a dealer performs the software update or replaces the camera.

What does the AVAS recall on the EQS and S-Class actually change?

The notice says Acoustic Vehicle Alerting System software may produce an incorrect external pedestrian warning sound while reversing, and that the vehicles do not comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 141. Dealers update the AVAS software free of charge.
