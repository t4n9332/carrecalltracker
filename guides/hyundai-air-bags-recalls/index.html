Why do Hyundai Santa Fe and Palisade keep having airbag recalls?

10 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 21, 2026

Side curtain air bags are the thread running through Hyundai's air bag recalls. Mounting bolts may damage the curtains on the 2019 Santa Fe and 2020 Palisade, a curtain component could detach on the 2018 Santa Fe Sport, and curtains may have been installed improperly on 2025 Tucson, 2025-2026 Santa Cruz, Santa Fe and Santa Fe Hybrid vehicles. Palisade appears again for third-row curtain deployment on 2020-2025 models. The Occupant Detection System is the other repeat theme, covering the 2021 Santa Fe and 2024 Palisade with dark premium suede seats. Santa Fe and Palisade carry the most entries; Elantra shows up for an air bag cover emblem and for B-pillar impact sensors.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Air bags may deploy unintentionally (2024 Santa Fe and Santa Fe HEV wiring harness recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these ten air bag recalls carries a stop-driving instruction, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. For the 2020-2025 Palisade third-row curtain recall, Hyundai's notice says interim letters telling owners of the safety risk were mailed beginning March 13, 2026, with final remedy letters mailed in phases beginning June 2026. For the other nine, the notices describe the crash-injury risk and the dealer repair, and do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is free of charge. Dealers replace the side curtain air bags (2018 Santa Fe Sport), install protective covers over the mounting bolts (2019 Santa Fe, 2020 Palisade), reinstall the curtains (2025 Tucson, Santa Cruz, Santa Fe), replace the ODS module (2021 Santa Fe) or update ODS software (2024 Palisade), inspect and repair or replace wiring (2024 Santa Fe), and inspect and replace impact sensors (2025 Elantra N).

Common questions

My Santa Fe shows up on more than one air bag recall. Why?

Different Santa Fe model years are covered by different notices: mounting bolts on the 2019, the Occupant Detection System on the 2021, wiring harness chafing on the 2024, and improper curtain installation on 2025 models. Each needs its own repair.

Does the Palisade third-row recall mean I should keep people out of the back row?

The notice does not say that. It says third-row side curtain air bags may deploy improperly, that interim letters about the safety risk were mailed, and that dealers will install a protective film over the rear window and remove padding as necessary, free of charge.

Will I be able to tell if my air bag has one of these defects?

Apart from the 2024 Santa Fe recall, where the notice says air bags may deploy unintentionally, these notices describe no warning light, noise or other sign an owner would notice. Checking your VIN and having the dealer perform the free recall repair is what they point owners to.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
