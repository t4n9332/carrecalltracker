Why does Hyundai keep recalling Santa Fe and Palisade for airbag defects?

10 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-21

Side curtain air bags are the recurring issue in this group. Mounting bolts have interfered with deployment on the 2018 Santa Fe Sport, 2019 Santa Fe, and 2020 Palisade, and a separate recall covers third-row curtain air bags on the 2020-2025 Palisade. A 2025-2026 recall covers curtain air bags installed improperly on the Santa Cruz, Santa Fe, Santa Fe Hybrid and Tucson. Separately, the Occupant Detection System failed to deactivate the passenger air bag for a child seat on the 2021 Santa Fe and failed to detect an occupant on the 2024 Palisade. A chafed wiring harness, a misassembled sensor, and a welding defect round out the group, affecting the 2024 Santa Fe, 2025-2026 Elantra N/Tucson/Elantra, and 2022-2023 Elantra.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A loose or detached emblem on the driver's front air bag cover (2022-2023 Elantra, Elantra N)
  • The notices describe no warning sign for the side curtain air bag mounting and installation defects (Santa Fe Sport, Santa Fe, Palisade, Santa Cruz, Tucson)
  • The notices describe no warning sign for the Occupant Detection System, wiring harness, or impact sensor defects (Santa Fe, Palisade, Elantra N, Tucson, Elantra)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these ten air bag recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Each notice says only that the specific defect increases the risk of injury in a crash - air bags that may not inflate or deploy properly, may deploy for the wrong occupant, or may be delayed - but none tell owners to stop driving. Owners should contact their dealer to schedule the free repair described in the notice for their vehicle and model year.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace or reinstall the side curtain air bags (Santa Fe Sport, Santa Fe, Palisade, Santa Cruz, Tucson), install a protective bolt cover (Santa Fe 2019, Palisade 2020), replace the ODS module or update its software (Santa Fe 2021, Palisade 2024), repair or replace the wiring harness (Santa Fe 2024), replace impact sensors (Elantra N, Tucson, Elantra), or replace the air bag (Elantra 2022-2023). All repairs are free.

Common questions

Which Hyundai model has had the most air bag recalls?

The Santa Fe is named in five separate air bag recalls in this group, from a 2018 Santa Fe Sport side curtain bolt issue to a 2024-2025 wiring harness and installation defect. The causes differ across each recall.

Do any of these Hyundai air bag recalls tell owners to stop driving?

No. None of the ten recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Each notice describes how the specific defect could keep an air bag from working correctly in a crash, but instructs owners only to get the free dealer repair.

What is wrong with the side curtain air bags on the Palisade?

Two separate defects are involved: mounting bolts that can damage the side curtain air bags during deployment on the 2020 Palisade, and improper third-row side curtain air bag deployment on the 2020-2025 Palisade, addressed with a protective film and padding changes.
