Why do Lincoln recalls cover engine rails, subframe bolts and trim?

4 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Structure · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 1, 2025

Two threads run through these four Lincoln structure recalls: fasteners that hold major parts in place, and exterior trim that can come off. The Aviator is in three of them. On the 2022 Aviator, shared with Explorer, the engine rails may have been improperly heat-treated, failing FMVSS 208 and 301. On the 2025 Aviator, also shared with Explorer, a front subframe mounting bolt may have been tightened improperly. Across 2020-2025 Aviators, rear door window bars and C-pillar trim may have insufficient adhesive bonding and detach. The fourth covers 2016-2019 MKT, where driver and front passenger B-pillar door trim may detach while driving.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. For the 2016-2019 MKT B-pillar trim recall, Ford mailed interim letters on December 23, 2025 notifying owners of the safety risk, and says additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available. The other three notices describe the risk and direct owners to a dealer for the repair. Beyond that, the notices say nothing about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

Dealers perform an engine rail strength test on the 2022 Aviator and replace the rails as necessary; if the rails require replacement, owners have the option of a vehicle replacement or buyback. On the 2025 Aviator, dealers inspect and tighten the subframe bolts. Aviator C-pillar appliques and window division bars are repaired or replaced, and MKT B-pillar trim is repaired. All free of charge.

Common questions

Is the Lincoln MKT B-pillar trim repair available yet?

Ford mailed interim letters on December 23, 2025 notifying MKT owners of the safety risk. The notice says additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available, anticipated in July 2026, when dealers will repair the B-pillar trim free of charge.

Can my Aviator be bought back under the engine rail recall?

The 2022 engine rail notice says dealers test rail strength and replace the rails as necessary. If the engine rails require replacement, owners have the option of a vehicle replacement or buyback. That option belongs to that recall only, not the others here.

Does the trim recall cover every Lincoln Aviator?

No. The notice covers certain 2020-2025 Aviator vehicles whose rear door window bars and C-pillar trim may have insufficient adhesive bonding. Owners can check whether their VIN is included through Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, under recall 25S66.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
