Why do Lincoln Aviator and MKT have structural recalls?

4 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Structure · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-01

Structural recalls for Lincoln vehicles cover four unrelated issues on two models. The 2022 Aviator has a recall for improperly heat-treated engine rails, which can reduce crash protection and allow a rail to detach and contact the road, with Ford offering a vehicle replacement or buyback if replacement is needed. A 2025 Aviator recall covers a front subframe bolt that may not have been tightened properly, and a separate 2025 Aviator recall covers rear door window bars and C-pillar trim (2020-2025 model years) that can detach due to insufficient adhesive. The 2018-2019 Lincoln MKT has its own recall for B-pillar door trim that can detach while driving.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rear door window bar or C-pillar trim feels loose or detaches (Aviator)
  • B-pillar door trim detaches while driving (MKT)
  • The engine rail and subframe bolt recalls describe no warning sign owners can notice beforehand

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The engine rail notice says reduced crash protection increases the risk of injury, and a detached rail contacting the road increases the risk of a crash. The subframe bolt notice says a loose bolt can reduce crash performance. The trim recalls say detached trim can become a road hazard and increase the risk of a crash. None of the notices tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers test engine rail strength and replace rails as necessary, with a replacement vehicle or buyback option if needed; inspect and tighten loose subframe bolts; repair or replace C-pillar appliques and window division bars on the Aviator; and repair B-pillar trim on the MKT. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

What is the Lincoln Aviator engine rail recall about?

Certain 2022 Aviator vehicles have engine rails that may have been improperly heat-treated, reducing crash protection and risking a rail detaching and contacting the road. Dealers test rail strength and replace them as necessary, with a replacement or buyback option if needed.

Why is trim falling off my Lincoln Aviator or MKT?

Two separate recalls found insufficient trim adhesion: 2020-2025 Aviator rear window bars and C-pillar trim, and 2018-2019 MKT B-pillar door trim. Both can detach while driving, creating a road hazard, and dealers repair or replace the trim free of charge.

Does the subframe bolt recall mean my Aviator is unsafe to drive?

The notice does not tell owners to stop driving. It says a mounting bolt for the front subframe on some 2025 Explorer and Aviator vehicles may have been tightened improperly, which dealers inspect and tighten as necessary, free of charge.
