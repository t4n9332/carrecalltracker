Why do Ram diesel engines keep causing fire and stall recalls?

7 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-10-23

Ram's engine and cooling recalls center on Cummins diesel engines and reappearing fire and stall risks. Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500 cab chassis trucks with the 6.7L High Output engine were recalled twice, in 2020 and 2021, because inadequate warmup protection or a low-oil warning software gap could damage the engine block and cause an oil leak near hot components. A 2022 recall covered a misaligned engine control module capacitor in 2022 3500-5500 diesels that could short-circuit and cut drive power. The 3.0L diesel Ram 1500 was twice recalled, in 2020 and 2023, for a crankshaft sensor that can delaminate and stall the engine. The EGR cooler on 2018-2019 Ram 1500 diesels could also crack and cause a fire, and 2018-2025 ProMaster vans can overheat in the radiator fan circuit.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No warning is stated before the 3.0L EGR cooler cracks (2018-2019 Ram 1500 diesel)
  • Engine stalling in 3.0L diesel Ram 1500 trucks
  • Loss of drive power in 2022 diesel trucks with the ECM short-circuit
  • Low oil level not triggering a warning (2019-2020 Cummins High Output trucks)
  • Overheating radiator fan electrical circuit in ProMaster vans

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the EGR cooler fire risk on 2018-2019 Ram 1500 diesels, the engine block damage risk on 2019-2020 Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500 trucks, the low-oil warning software gap on those same trucks, the crankshaft sensor stall risk on 3.0L diesel Ram 1500s, the 2022 engine control module short-circuit, and the ProMaster radiator fan overheating risk, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers mostly update software: engine calibration flashes to improve warmup protection and oil-level warnings, and powertrain control module updates for the stall-prone crankshaft sensor. Hardware fixes include replacing the EGR cooler and intake manifold, the engine control module, and the ProMaster's cooling fan module and fuse. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why do Cummins diesel Ram trucks keep having engine fire recalls?

Two related recalls (2020 and 2021) found that the 6.7L High Output engine in 2019-2020 Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500 trucks lacked adequate warmup protection and a reliable low-oil warning, either of which could damage the engine block and raise fire risk.

Is the crankshaft sensor stall recall the same for Ram 1500 diesels?

Both the 2020 and 2023 recalls describe the same 3.0L diesel crankshaft position sensor tone wheel delaminating and causing an engine stall; the 2023 notice expanded coverage to additional Ram 1500 and Jeep Grand Cherokee model years.

What causes ProMaster vans to overheat under this group?

A 2025 recall covers 2018-2025 Ram ProMaster vans whose radiator cooling fan electrical circuit may overheat, increasing fire risk. Dealers replace the cooling fan module and fuse and add warning labels, free of charge.
