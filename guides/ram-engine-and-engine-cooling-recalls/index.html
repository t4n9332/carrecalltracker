Which Ram engine recalls cover diesel stalls and fire risk?

7 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 23, 2025

Seven engine recalls for Ram, and diesel runs through nearly all of them. Two cover the same crankshaft position sensor tone wheel, which may delaminate on 3.0L diesel Ram 1500 trucks — the 2018 model year in one recall, 2018-2019 in the other — with an engine stall as the stated result. Two more cover 2019-2020 Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500 cab chassis with the Cummins 6.7L High Output engine: warmup protection that can lead to connecting rod failure and a punctured block, then calibration software that may not warn about low oil volume. Fire is the consequence named in four, including the EcoDiesel EGR cooler on 2018-2019 Ram 1500 and the ProMaster radiator fan circuit.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An engine stall — the 3.0L diesel crankshaft position sensor tone wheel recalls covering 2018 Ram 1500 and 2018-2019 Ram 1500 with Jeep Grand Cherokee.
  • A loss of drive power — the 2022 Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500 engine control module recall Z87.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The notices state their own risks: combustion in the intake manifold can increase the risk of a fire on the EcoDiesel EGR cooler recall, a damaged engine block may leak oil onto hot engine or exhaust components on the Cummins cab chassis recalls, and an overheating radiator fan circuit increases the risk of a fire on the ProMaster. The 2018-2019 Ram 1500 and Jeep Grand Cherokee stall recall says interim letters notified owners of the safety risk.

What the free repair involves

Dealers update software on four: powertrain control module software for the tone wheel stalls, engine calibration software for warmup protection and for the low oil warning. The EcoDiesel recall replaces the EGR cooler and inspects, and if necessary replaces, the intake manifold; the 2022 Cummins recall replaces the engine control module. Six notices say free of charge; the ProMaster fan notice describes replacing the cooling fan module and fuse.

Common questions

Which Ram engines do these recalls actually involve?

Mostly diesels: the 3.0L EcoDiesel Ram 1500 for the EGR cooler and the tone wheel stalls, the Cummins 6.7L High Output in 2019-2020 Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500 cab chassis, and 2022 Cummins 6.7L turbo diesel cab chassis for the engine control module.

Do any of these fire-risk notices tell owners to stop driving?

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. They describe the risk — combustion in the intake manifold, an oil leak onto hot engine or exhaust components, or an overheating radiator fan circuit — and direct owners to dealers for the repair.

My ProMaster is a gasoline van — why is it in an engine recall?

The 2018-2025 Ram ProMaster recall concerns the electrical circuit for the radiator fan, which the notice says may overheat and increase the risk of a fire. The notice describes dealers replacing the cooling fan module and fuse, plus a manual addendum and a label.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
