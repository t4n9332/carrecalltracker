Why are Subaru brake lights, turn signals, and headlights failing?

4 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-08-31

These four Subaru exterior lighting recalls, from 2018 through 2023 models, each involve a different lamp or switch failing to work as intended. A 2023 Impreza recall covers a misadjusted brake light switch that can illuminate the brake lights without pedal pressure and let the vehicle shift out of Park without braking. A 2018-2021 WRX recall covers a corroding backup light switch that can knock out both the backup lights and the rearview camera in reverse. A 2018-2019 Impreza recall covers reflectors that don't reflect enough light. A 2022 BRZ and Toyota GR86 recall covers rear turn signals that intermittently stop working.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake lights illuminating without the pedal being pressed, or the vehicle shifting out of Park without braking
  • Backup lights not illuminating and the rearview camera not displaying in reverse
  • Reduced visibility from reflectors that don't reflect enough light
  • Rear turn signals intermittently not working

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls tells owners to stop driving or park away from structures. The notices describe only increased crash risk: brake lights illuminating without the pedal pressed or the vehicle shifting out of Park unexpectedly on 2023 Impreza, inoperative backup lights and camera on 2018-2021 WRX, inadequate reflector illumination on 2018-2019 Impreza, and intermittently inoperative rear turn signals on 2022 BRZ and GR86. For all four, owners are not instructed to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and adjust the brake light switch gap on affected Impreza models, inspect and replace the corroding backup light switch on WRX models, replace the front headlamp assemblies on Impreza reflector recalls, and replace both rear combination lamp assemblies on BRZ and GR86 models. All of these repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why do my 2023 Impreza's brake lights turn on by themselves?

The recall found the brake light switch may be misadjusted, letting the brake lights illuminate without the pedal being pressed, and in some cases letting the transmission shift out of Park without pressing the brake.

Why don't my WRX's backup lights or camera work?

The recall found the backup light switch on certain 2018-2021 WRX vehicles can corrode, which can stop the backup lights from illuminating and the rearview camera image from displaying in reverse.

Why are my BRZ's rear turn signals not working?

The recall found the rear turn signals on certain 2022 BRZ and Toyota GR86 vehicles may intermittently become inoperative, which can fail to alert other drivers that the vehicle is turning.
