Subaru lighting recalls: brake lights, backup lights, turn signals and reflectors

4 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 31, 2023

Every recall here involves a lamp that stops telling other drivers, or the driver, what the vehicle is doing. Two are switch problems: on 2023 Impreza vehicles the brake light switch may be improperly adjusted, lighting the brake lights with no pedal input and also letting the vehicle start and shift out of Park without the brake pedal pressed; on 2015-2021 WRX vehicles the backup light switch may corrode and become inoperative. Two are lamp problems: 2017-2019 Impreza left and right side reflex reflectors and the left side low beam reflector may inadequately reflect light, and 2022 BRZ and Toyota GR86 rear turn signals may intermittently become inoperative. Impreza is the only model named in two of the four.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake lights illuminating without the brake pedal being applied, and the vehicle starting or shifting out of Park without the brake pedal pressed (2023 Impreza brake light switch recall).
  • Backup lights not illuminating and the rearview camera image not displaying when the vehicle is in reverse (2015-2021 WRX backup light switch recall).
  • Rear turn signals that intermittently become inoperative (2022 BRZ and Toyota GR86 recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Each states the consequence in terms of visibility and signalling: on the 2023 Impreza, brake lights that fail to alert other motorists of actual brake application and a vehicle that can shift out of Park and roll away; on the WRX, inoperative backup lights and rearview camera; on the 2017-2019 Impreza, reflectors that reduce the driver's ability to see; on the BRZ and GR86, turn signals that may not notify other drivers.

What the free repair involves

The fixes range from an adjustment to a new lamp assembly. On the 2023 Impreza, dealers inspect the gap between the brake light switch and stopper and adjust the switch as necessary. On the WRX, they inspect and replace the backup light switch. On the 2017-2019 Impreza, they replace the front headlamp assemblies, and on the BRZ and GR86 both rear combination lamp assemblies. All free of charge.

Common questions

My brake lights come on when I am not braking — is that the Impreza recall?

The 2023 Impreza notice describes exactly that: a brake light switch that may be improperly adjusted and illuminate the brake lights without the pedal being applied. Dealers inspect the gap between the switch and stopper and adjust the switch as necessary, free of charge.

Why is a Toyota GR86 listed in a Subaru recall?

Subaru's 2023 recall lists both 2022 BRZ and 2022 Toyota GR86 vehicles for rear turn signals that may intermittently become inoperative. Both rear combination lamp assemblies are replaced free of charge. GR86 owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331.

Does the WRX recall cover my rearview camera too?

The notice says a corroded backup light switch can leave the backup lights unlit and the rearview camera image not displaying in reverse. The remedy it describes is inspection and replacement of the backup light switch as necessary, free of charge, on 2015-2021 WRX vehicles.
