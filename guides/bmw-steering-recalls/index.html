Which BMW steering recalls cover tie rods, racks and columns?

10 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-12

Ten BMW steering recalls repeat a few faults. Tie rods lead: 2018-2019 X1, 2019 X2 and MINI Cooper variants were recalled for tie rods that could wear and break, and the 2019 Z4, 2019-2020 330i xDrive and M340i, 2020 X3M, X4M and Toyota Supra for tie rods that could fracture. Steering rack pinion teeth that break under load cover the 2020 X5, X7, X3 M40i and X4 M40i. The rest are connections not tightened or attached correctly: the column bearing plate on 2019-2020 X5 and 2020 X6M, the column shaft universal joint on 2021 X5, X6 and X7, the steering gear on 2024 2 Series, 330i, X3 and X4, and the steering spindle on 2024-2026 X3. A 2025-2026 X3 software recall and an M 1000 damper recall follow.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Unexpected steering wheel rotation while the vehicle is stationary (2025-2026 X3 steering software recall).
  • An unexpected increase in steering effort (2024-2025 M 1000 XR and M 1000 R steering damper recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these ten notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. That applies to the tie rod recalls on the X1, X2, MINI, Z4, 330i, M340i, X3M, X4M and Supra, the steering rack recalls on the 2020 X5, X7, X3 M40i and X4 M40i, the column, gear and spindle recalls on the X5, X6, X7, 2 Series, 330i, X3 and X4, the M 1000 damper recall and the X3 steering software recall. The notices describe the risk each defect creates and direct owners to the dealer remedy.

What the free repair involves

Remedies differ by recall and are free of charge. Dealers inspect and replace tie rods and ball joints as necessary, or replace the steering gear tie rods; replace the steering rack or steering gear; inspect and reattach or replace the column bearing plate; replace or retighten the universal joint bolt; replace the steering damper; inspect the spindle connection. The X3 steering software is updated over-the-air or by a dealer.

Common questions

Does BMW tell owners to stop driving for any of these steering recalls?

None of the ten notices in this group tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. Each says what the defect can cause — a loss of steering control or affected handling — and that dealers will carry out the repair free of charge.

Why does the X3 keep appearing in BMW steering recalls?

The X3 appears in five of the ten: the 2020 X3 M40i steering rack, the 2020 X3M tie rod, the 2024 steering gear, the 2024-2026 steering spindle, and the 2025-2026 steering software recall. Each names its own models and repair.

Is the Toyota Supra really part of a BMW steering recall?

Yes. The tie rod recall filed in 2020 covers the Toyota Supra alongside the 2019 Z4 sDrive30i, 2019-2020 330i xDrive and M340i, and 2020 X3M and X4M. BMW and Toyota notify their own owners, and dealers replace the tie rods free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
