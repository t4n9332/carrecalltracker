Why do so many BMW models have separate steering recalls?

10 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-12

Across these ten recalls, BMW steering defects cluster around a few repeating causes: tie rods that wear or fracture (X1, X2, MINI Cooper in 2019; Z4, M340i, X3, X4 and the shared Toyota Supra in 2020), steering rack pinion teeth breaking under load (X5, X7 and separately X3, X4, all 2020), and steering column or spindle hardware coming loose (X5 and X6M in 2020, X5/X6/X7 in 2021, and X3 in 2024-2026). A 2024 recall covers a loosening steering gear on X3 and X4, a 2025 X3 recall covers unexpected steering wheel rotation while parked, and one 2025 motorcycle recall covers a steering damper defect. X3 and X5 appear most often across these separate defects.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Steering effort suddenly getting harder while riding (2024-2025 M 1000 XR/R motorcycle recall)
  • The steering wheel rotating on its own while the vehicle is parked and stationary (2025-2026 X3 recall)
  • The other steering recalls in this group describe no warning sign an owner would notice before a tie rod, steering rack, column or gear component fails

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these ten BMW steering recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. That includes the tie rod recalls (X1/X2/MINI and Z4/M340i/X3/X4/Supra), the steering rack pinion recalls (X5/X7 and X3/X4), the steering column and spindle recalls (X5/X6M, X5/X6/X7, and X3), the steering gear recall (X3/X4), the 2025 X3 steering wheel movement recall, and the motorcycle steering damper recall. For all of them, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Depending on the recall, dealers inspect and replace tie rods and ball joints, replace the steering rack or steering gear, inspect and tighten or replace steering column and spindle hardware, or replace a motorcycle's steering damper. The 2025 X3 fix is a steering system software update delivered over-the-air or by a dealer. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why has BMW issued so many separate steering recalls?

Each recall traces to a different cause — tie rod wear, steering rack pinion teeth, loose column hardware, or a loosening steering gear — affecting different model lines, not one shared defect repeated across the lineup.

Is my BMW X3 affected by more than one steering recall?

The X3 appears in five separate steering recalls listed here (2020 rack pinion, 2020 tie rod, 2024 steering gear, 2025 wheel movement, and 2024-2026 spindle), each with a distinct cause. Check your VIN against each one.

Does the 2025 BMW X3 steering wheel recall need a dealer visit?

The notice says the fix is a steering system software update delivered either over-the-air or by a dealer, free of charge, so a visit may not be required depending on how the update reaches your vehicle.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
