Why do so many Volvo electrical systems trigger recalls?

9 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Electrical System · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-25

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Volvo's electrical-system recalls split between its trucks and its cars. On the truck side, Volvo Trucks has recalled 2018-2020 VAH, VHD, VNL and VNR models for a blown fuse that stops the transmission from shifting, 2020-2025 VN, VAH, VHD and VNRE trucks for a Bendix ECU that can misread signals to ABS and stability control, and 2025-2026 VNL trucks for an instrument panel that can go blank. On the car side, the 2025 EX30 has been recalled twice for a high-voltage battery that can short-circuit and overheat, while older V60, V90, XC40, XC60 and XC90 models were recalled for a blown fuel-pump fuse, a GPS module that can't reach emergency services, or a turn signal that won't light.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Truck transmission suddenly won't shift gears (VNL, blown fuse)
  • Engine stalls due to a blown fuel-pump fuse (V60 Cross Country, V90 Cross Country)
  • ABS, stability control, traction control, or collision-mitigation warnings or reduced function (VN/VAH/VHD/VNRE trucks)
  • Instrument panel goes blank, hiding the speedometer and warning lights (VNL trucks)
  • Left rear turn signal doesn't light up (XC40); the EX30 battery, VNR Electric coolant, and GPS-module recalls describe no owner-noticeable warning sign

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these nine recalls carries a stop-driving order. One recall, the January 2026 EX30 high-voltage battery fire-risk recall, tells owners to park outside and away from structures and limit charging to 70% until the repair is complete. For the other eight recalls — covering the VNR Electric coolant leak, the second EX30 battery recall, the VNL transmission fuse, the V60CC/V90CC fuel pump, the VN/VAH/VHD/VNRE ECU, the VNL instrument panel, the V60/V90CC GPS module, and the XC40 turn signal — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across this group dealers inspect and replace high-voltage battery modules or repair the coolant line (VNR Electric, EX30 x2), replace blown fuses (VNL transmission, V60CC/V90CC fuel pump), reprogram control-unit software (VN/VAH/VHD/VNRE ECU, V60/V90CC GPS module), replace the instrument panel cluster (VNL), and update software over-the-air or at a dealer (XC40 turn signal). All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why are two different Volvo EX30 recalls about the same battery fire risk?

NHTSA treats them as separate recalls issued months apart, both covering a high-voltage battery that can short-circuit and overheat on 2025 EX30 vehicles; the first also adds a park-outside instruction the second does not repeat.

Does the Volvo VNL transmission fuse recall affect my car?

No. That recall is from Volvo Trucks, not Volvo Car, and applies to 2018-2020 VAH, VHD, VNL, and VNR trucks whose transmission-circuit fuse can blow and prevent shifting.

Will a dealer or an over-the-air update fix my Volvo?

It depends on the recall. The XC40 turn signal is fixed by a dealer or an OTA update; the other recalls in this group require a dealer visit for hardware replacement or a manual software update.
