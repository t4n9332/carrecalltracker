Why do Volvo electrical recalls keep hitting batteries, fuses and modules?

9 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Electrical System · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 25, 2026

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Volvo's electrical recalls split between Volvo Trucks and Volvo Car, and high-voltage batteries dominate the newest ones. Two separate 2025 EX30 recalls cover a high-voltage battery that may short circuit and overheat, and 2020-2024 VNR Electric trucks were recalled for a coolant line in the battery pack that may not be fully seated. Fuses appear twice: 2018-2020 VAH, VHD, VNL and VNR transmission circuits, and the low-pressure fuel pump on 2019-2020 V90, XC60, S60, V60, V60CC, S90, V90CC, S90L and XC90 cars. Software and modules make up the rest — the Bendix EC80 ECU on 2020-2025 VN, VAH, VHD and VNRE trucks, the VCM on 2017-2019 cars, the CEM on 2024 XC40, and 2025-2026 VNL (4) instrument panels.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall R10256: the left rear turn signal indicator does not function (2024 XC40).
  • Recall RVXX2603: the instrument panel may fail while driving and not show the speedometer or warning lights (2025-2026 VNL (4)).
  • Recall R10100: failure of the low-pressure fuel pump can cause a stall.
  • Recall RVXX1903: if the fuse blows, the transmission may not shift.
  • Recall RVXX2409: automatic traction control, ABS, electronic stability control, active cruise control and the collision mitigation system may have diminished or lost functionality.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these nine notices tells owners to stop driving. The January 2026 EX30 recall tells owners to park outside and away from structures and to limit charging to a maximum of 70% until the recall repair is complete; the February 2026 EX30 recall also tells owners to limit charging to 70% until the repair is complete. For the VNR Electric coolant line, the transmission fuse, the Bendix EC80 ECU, the VNL (4) instrument panel, the low-pressure fuel pump, the VCM software and the XC40 turn signal, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Remedies differ by recall and all are free. Volvo Trucks and battery maker Akasol inspect and replace VNR Electric batteries as necessary; dealers inspect and replace EX30 battery modules as necessary, replace the transmission fuse and the fuel pump fuse, replace VNL (4) instrument panel clusters, reprogram the Bendix EC80 ECU and correct the VCM software. The XC40 CEM software is updated by a dealer or over the air.

Common questions

I own a 2025 EX30 — can I charge and park it as usual?

The recall notices tell EX30 owners to limit charging to a maximum of 70% until the recall repair is complete, and the January 2026 notice also tells owners to park outside and away from structures. Dealers inspect and replace the battery modules as necessary, free of charge.

Is this group all trucks, or do Volvo cars appear too?

Both. Volvo Car recalls cover 2025 EX30 batteries, the 2024 XC40 left rear turn signal, VCM software on 2017-2019 models including XC90, S90, V60 and XC40, and the low-pressure fuel pump fuse on 2019-2020 models. The rest are Volvo Trucks VN, VNL, VNR, VAH and VHD vehicles.

Can the XC40 turn signal recall be fixed without a dealer visit?

The notice says the CEM software will be updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update, free of charge. The other eight recalls in this group name dealer work — inspections, part replacement or reprogramming — with no over-the-air option stated.
