Volvo Electrical System Recalls Cover Battery Fires and Equipment Failures

9 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Electrical System · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 25, 2026

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Volvo's electrical recalls span passenger vehicles and commercial trucks, with battery short circuits, fuse failures, and software faults recurring. Certain 2025 EX30 vehicles appear in two notices about battery short circuits and overheating. Certain 2020-2024 VNR Electric trucks face a different battery risk involving an unsecured coolant line. Fuse problems affect transmission shifting in certain 2018-2020 VAH, VHD, VNL, and VNR trucks and fuel-pump operation in several 2019-2020 passenger models. Other notices address truck safety-system controls, 2025-2026 VNL (4) instrument panels, emergency-location transmission in certain 2017-2019 passenger vehicles, and the left rear turn signal on certain 2024 XC40 vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • July 2019 transmission fuse recall, RVXX1903: the transmission may not shift.
  • June 2021 fuel-pump fuse recall, R10100: fuel-pump failure can cause an engine stall.
  • October 2024 truck ECU recall, RVXX2409: dependent safety systems may have diminished or lost functionality.
  • March 2026 VNL instrument-panel recall, RVXX2603: the display may fail while driving and not show the speedometer or warning lights.
  • January 2024 XC40 recall, R10256: the left rear turn signal may not function.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The January 2026 EX30 battery recall is the only supplied electrical notice carrying a park-outside instruction: park outside and away from structures, and limit charging to 70% until repair. The February 2026 EX30 notice separately limits charging to 70% until repair but gives no park-outside instruction. None of the supplied electrical notices tells owners to stop driving. The remaining notices likewise do not tell owners to stop driving, including those addressing VNR Electric batteries, fuses, truck controls, instrument panels, emergency-location software, and XC40 turn signals.

What the free repair involves

The listed remedies are free. VNR Electric batteries receive inspection and replacement as necessary; both EX30 notices specify battery-module inspection and replacement as necessary. RVXX1903 specifies fuse inspection and replacement if necessary; R10100 specifies fuse replacement. Dealers reprogram the truck ECU, replace VNL instrument clusters, and correct connectivity software. XC40 CEM software receives a dealer or over-the-air update.

Common questions

Do both EX30 battery notices require parking outside?

The January 2026 notice instructs owners to park outside and away from structures and limit charging to 70% until repair. The February 2026 notice specifies the 70% charging limit until repair but does not state a park-outside instruction.

Can these Volvo electrical defects affect shifting or stall the engine?

The July 2019 truck notice says a transmission-circuit fuse may blow and prevent shifting. The June 2021 passenger-vehicle notice says the low-pressure fuel pump can blow a fuse and fail, potentially causing a stall. These are separate specified defects.

Which Volvo electrical recall offers an over-the-air repair?

The January 2024 recall for certain 2024 XC40 vehicles offers a Central Electronic Module software update through a dealer or over the air, free of charge. It addresses a diagnostic software error affecting the left rear turn signal.
