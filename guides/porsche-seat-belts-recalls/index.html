Porsche Seat Belt Recalls Involving Child Seats and Anchors

6 NHTSA recalls · Porsche · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 20, 2024

Child restraint security and seat belt attachment problems recur across Porsche's seat belt recalls. AMB6 covers certain 2021 911, Panamera and Taycan variants whose automatic locking retractors may deactivate early; ARA2 describes the same retractor problem in certain 2023 911 variants. A separate recall affects certain 2020-2021 Taycans with the optional rear center seat because buckle wiring can obstruct right rear child seat anchors. Anchor screw tightening is another repeated issue: certain 2022 911 GT3 vehicles and specified Macan and Panamera variants have separate notices. The Macan coverage includes 2016-2020 Macan S vehicles. Certain 2019 Cayennes instead have a passenger-side rear buckle that may break under load.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The supplied notices contain neither a stop-driving instruction nor a park-outside instruction. The AMB6 and ARA2 automatic locking retractor notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Neither does the notice about child seat anchor access in certain 2020-2021 Taycans. The 2022 911 GT3 anchor screw notice, APA9 rear outboard anchor screw notice and AJ09 Cayenne buckle notice also do not tell owners to stop driving. These notices provide no separate interim driving instruction to repeat here.

What the free repair involves

Repairs are free. AMB6 specifies inspection and seat belt assembly replacement as necessary; ARA2 specifies inspection and rear three-point belt replacement as necessary. APA9 replaces and tightens rear anchor screws. The GT3 repair replaces and tightens screws, replacing the seat frame if threads are damaged. AJ09 replaces the buckle. The Taycan wiring recall reroutes and secures the harness, replacing a damaged buckle as necessary.

Common questions

Which Porsche seat belt recalls involve securing a child seat?

AMB6 concerns early retractor deactivation in certain 2021 911, Panamera and Taycan variants; ARA2 concerns certain 2023 911 variants. The February 2022 Taycan notice instead describes buckle wiring obstructing access to right rear lower child seat anchors.

What is the difference between the GT3 and rear anchor screw repairs?

The 2022 911 GT3 notice specifies replacing and tightening the screws, plus replacing the seat frame if screw threads are damaged. APA9 specifies replacing and tightening the lower rear outboard seat belt anchor screws. Both repairs are free.

Does the Cayenne buckle recall involve the automatic locking retractor?

AJ09 describes a passenger-side rear seat belt buckle that may break under load in certain 2019 Cayennes. Its remedy is free buckle replacement. Early automatic locking retractor deactivation is the separate problem described in AMB6 and ARA2.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
