Which Toyota brake recalls affect Camry, Tacoma, RAV4 and Sienna?

8 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-02-06

Eight recalls sit under Toyota hydraulic brakes, and the brake booster vacuum pump comes up twice: an improperly machined oil galley in the pump rotor on 2018 Camry and Highlander, and a vane cap that may break inside the pump on 2018-2019 Camry. Machining also explains the 2018-2019 Tacoma brake master cylinder seal damage. Hoses and fasteners make up much of the rest, with rear brake hoses damaged by mud and dirt buildup on 2024-2025 Tacoma 4-wheel drive, and loose front caliper and hub bearing bolts on the 2024 RAV4. Two recalls cover Sienna mobility conversions, filed by Braun and by Vantage, and the 2022 Tundra recall concerns the electronic parking brake.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2022 Tundra recall says the Skid Control ECU can enter a failsafe mode that prevents the electronic parking brake from being engaged.
  • The other seven notices in this group do not describe a warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these eight notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The notices describe consequences instead: a sudden loss of brake assist on 2018 Camry and Highlander and on 2018-2019 Camry, a longer stopping distance on 2018-2019 Tacoma, reduced rear braking power on the Braun-converted Sienna, a brake fluid leak on 2024-2025 Tacoma, and a vehicle rollaway on the 2022 Tundra. No notice in this group sets out anything for owners to do before the dealer repair.

What the free repair involves

The work varies. Dealers replace the brake booster vacuum pump on 2018 Camry and Highlander, and repair or replace the vacuum pump on 2018-2019 Camry. The 2018-2019 Tacoma gets a new master cylinder and the 2024-2025 Tacoma new rear brake hoses. The 2024 RAV4 is inspected and its bolts tightened, and the 2022 Tundra's Skid Control ECU is reprogrammed. All free of charge.

Common questions

My Sienna is a wheelchair van — is that a separate recall?

Yes. Braun's recall covers Toyota Sienna mobility van conversions with incorrect brake line tube nut fittings, and Vantage's covers AMS Genesis wheelchair vans built on the Sienna with rear brake hoses that rub the trailing arm. Braun: 1-800-488-0359. Vantage: 1-800-488-9082.

Is the Tundra parking brake recall fixed with software?

The notice says dealers reprogram the Skid Control ECU free of charge. It does not describe an over-the-air update, so the vehicle goes to a dealer. Toyota's numbers for that recall are 22TA11 and 22LA04, and its customer service line is 1-800-331-4331.

Does Toyota tell owners to stop driving in any of these brake recalls?

None of the eight notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Each states only the defect, the crash risk and the dealer remedy. The notices also do not describe any interim step for owners while they wait for the repair appointment.
