Toyota Brake Recalls Cover Assist Loss, Leaks, and Parking Brakes

8 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 6, 2025

Camry, Tacoma, and converted Sienna vans each appear in two notices. Brake-assist loss affects certain 2018 Camry and Highlander and 2017 Sienna and Tacoma vehicles through a vacuum-pump rotor oil-galley machining problem; a separate 2018-2019 Camry notice identifies a vacuum-pump vane cap that may break. Tacoma concerns differ between master-cylinder seal damage in certain 2018-2019 vehicles and rear-hose damage from mud and dirt buildup in certain 2024-2025 four-wheel-drive vehicles. Sienna conversion notices address incorrect brake-line fittings in certain 2014-2019 Braun conversions and short rear hoses in certain 2012-2018 AMS Genesis wheelchair vans. Other notices concern 2022 Tundra electronic parking-brake engagement and loose brake and hub bolts in certain 2024 RAV4 vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • April 2018 brake-assist recall: sudden loss of brake assist.
  • December 2018 Tacoma master-cylinder recall: reduced braking performance can lengthen stopping distance.
  • November 2021 Camry vacuum-pump recall: sudden loss of braking assist.
  • September 2022 Tundra parking-brake recall: the electronic parking brake may not engage.
  • February 2025 Tacoma rear-hose recall: a brake fluid leak can reduce braking ability.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the listed Toyota brake notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The April 2018 brake-assist, December 2018 Tacoma master-cylinder, and November 2021 Camry vacuum-pump notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The June 2019 Braun Sienna fitting and July 2019 Vantage wheelchair-van hose notices also do not tell owners to stop driving. Neither do the September 2022 Tundra parking-brake, December 2024 RAV4 bolt, or February 2025 Tacoma rear-hose notices. These notices provide no stop-driving direction.

What the free repair involves

Free dealer remedies: April 2018—replace booster vacuum pump; December 2018—replace Tacoma master cylinder; June 2019—install Braun adaptor; July 2019—install clearance-providing Vantage rear hoses; November 2021—repair or replace Camry vacuum pump as necessary; September 2022—reprogram Tundra Skid Control ECU; December 2024—inspect/tighten RAV4 bolts as necessary, replace damaged components; February 2025—replace both Tacoma rear hoses.

Common questions

Are both Tacoma brake recalls about leaking rear hoses?

The December 2018 notice concerns master-cylinder machining that may damage a seal in certain 2018-2019 Tacoma vehicles. The February 2025 notice concerns rear-hose damage from mud and dirt buildup in certain 2024-2025 four-wheel-drive Tacoma vehicles.

Do the Sienna brake notices cover mobility conversions?

The June 2019 notice covers certain 2014-2019 Braun Sienna mobility conversions with incorrect brake-line fittings. The July 2019 notice covers certain 2012-2018 AMS Genesis wheelchair vans built on Sienna vehicles, whose short rear brake hoses may rub the trailing arm.

What does the Tundra parking-brake recall fix?

The 2022 Tundra notice describes a Skid Control ECU that may falsely detect an electronic parking-brake actuator overcurrent condition. Its failsafe mode can prevent parking-brake engagement. Dealers reprogram the Skid Control ECU free of charge.
