Why Do So Many Toyota Models Have Brake System Recalls?

8 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-02-06

Eight Toyota brake recalls span two different failure paths. Several involve a loss of brake power-assist: 2018 Camry and Highlander and, separately, 2018-2019 Camry, both tied to brake booster or vacuum pump defects. Tacoma has two separate hydraulic recalls of its own — a 2018-2019 master cylinder seal problem and, years later, 2024-2025 rear brake hoses damaged by mud and dirt. Two conversion-van recalls affect the Sienna: Braun-converted 2018-2019 vans with incorrect brake-line fittings, and a Vantage-built wheelchair van with rear brake hoses that rub the axle. A 2022 Tundra recall covers an electronic parking brake that can fail to engage, and a 2024 RAV4 recall covers loose front brake caliper and wheel bolts.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake pedal suddenly needs more effort due to lost power assist (Camry, Highlander vacuum-pump/booster recalls)
  • Longer distance needed to stop (Tacoma master cylinder recall)
  • Parking brake that will not engage, letting the vehicle roll away while parked (Tundra)
  • Visible brake fluid leak (RAV4 loose caliper/hub bolts; Tacoma mud-damaged rear hoses)
  • No warning sign described for the two Sienna mobility-van brake hose recalls (Braun, Vantage)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these eight Toyota brake recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This covers the 2018 Camry and Highlander vacuum-pump recall, the 2018-2019 Tacoma master cylinder recall, the 2018-2019 Braun Sienna brake-line recall, the 2018 Vantage Sienna wheelchair-van hose recall, the 2018-2019 Camry vacuum pump recall, the 2022 Tundra parking brake recall, the 2024 RAV4 caliper bolt recall, and the 2024-2025 Tacoma rear brake hose recall. For all eight, the notices tell owners only to schedule a repair, not to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Repairs vary by recall: dealers replace the brake booster vacuum pump or its vane cap, replace the master cylinder, install an adaptor or replace brake hoses on the Sienna conversion vans, reprogram the Tundra's parking-brake control module, tighten or replace RAV4 caliper and hub bolts, and replace Tacoma's rear brake hoses. All of these repairs are done free of charge.

Common questions

Why has the Tacoma had more than one brake recall?

The Tacoma has two separate hydraulic brake recalls: a 2018-2019 master cylinder seal defect and a 2024-2025 recall for rear brake hoses that mud and dirt can damage. They are unrelated issues.

Do the Sienna mobility van brake recalls affect my Sienna Hybrid?

No. Both Sienna brake hose recalls apply only to Braun and Vantage wheelchair-accessible conversion vans, not to standard Sienna or Sienna Hybrid vehicles.

Could my Tundra roll away because of its parking brake recall?

The notice for the 2022 Tundra recall says the parking brake may enter a failsafe mode and fail to engage in park, which can let the vehicle roll away.
