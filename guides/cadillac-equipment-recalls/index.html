Cadillac recalls: blank displays, wrong manuals, and unreadable tire labels

4 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Equipment · model years 2023–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-02-26

These four Cadillac recalls involve information owners rely on rather than a single mechanical part. The 2023-2024 Lyriq's driver display can go blank while driving, cutting off the speedometer, warning lights, and rearview camera. The 2023 XT5 and XT6 (grouped with the GMC Acadia) received an illegible tire-size certification label on the B-pillar. The 2024 XT4 (grouped with the Chevrolet Malibu) may have shipped with the wrong printed owner's manual in the glove box. Most recently, 2025 Optiq and 2026 Escalade IQ and IQL models (grouped with several Chevrolet and GMC vehicles) may be missing the electronic owner's manual because the radio failed to download it.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Driver video display going blank while driving (2023-2024 Lyriq)
  • Illegible tire-size text on the driver's side B-pillar label (2023 XT5, XT6, Acadia)
  • Wrong owner's manual found in the glove box (2024 XT4, Malibu)
  • Electronic owner's manual missing from the vehicle's infotainment/radio system (2025-2026 models)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four notices include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the Lyriq display recall, GM says a blank display can hide the speedometer, warning lights, and rearview camera image, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving. The tire-label, owner's-manual, and electronic-manual recalls are documentation issues; their notices likewise do not instruct owners to stop driving or park outside. Across all four recalls in this group, owners are not told to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers update the video display control module software, either in the shop or over the air, for the Lyriq. For the other three recalls, dealers replace the certification label, provide the correct printed owner's manual, or reset the vehicle's radio so the electronic manual downloads automatically. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Is the Lyriq display recall dangerous?

GM says a blank driver display can hide the speedometer, warning lights, and rearview camera image, which increases crash risk. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving; GM updates the display software by dealer visit or over-the-air, free of charge.

Why were Cadillac and GMC recalled over a tire label?

Federal rules require a legible certification label listing correct tire sizes. GM found the label on 2023 XT5, XT6, and GMC Acadia B-pillars could be unreadable, which could lead to the wrong tires being installed. Dealers replace the label free of charge.

Do I need to do anything if my manual is missing?

Yes. For the 2024 XT4 and Malibu, dealers hand owners the correct printed manual. For 2025-2026 models missing the electronic manual, a dealer resets the radio so the manual downloads automatically. Both fixes are free.
