Cadillac equipment recalls: blank displays, wrong labels and manuals

4 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Equipment · model years 2023–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 26, 2026

Four recalls, and none of them is about a mechanical part — each concerns information the driver relies on. Two involve the owner's manual: 2024 Cadillac XT4 vehicles may have the wrong manual in the glove box, and 2025 Cadillac Optiq, 2026 Escalade IQ and Escalade IQL are among the GM vehicles whose radio was not set to the correct status to download the electronic manual. A third covers 2023 Cadillac XT5 and XT6, where tire size information on the driver's side B-pillar certification label may be illegible. The fourth is the only screen problem: on 2023 and 2024 Lyriq vehicles the driver video display may go blank while driving.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A driver video display that goes blank while driving (2023–2024 Lyriq display recall).
  • The wrong owner's manual in the glove box (2024 XT4 owner's manual recall).
  • Tire size information on the driver's side B-pillar label that cannot be read (2023 XT5 and XT6 certification label recall).
  • No electronic owner's manual available to consult in the vehicle (2025 Optiq, 2026 Escalade IQ and Escalade IQL recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices carries a stop-driving instruction, and none tells owners to park outside. The Lyriq display notice, the XT5 and XT6 certification label notice, the XT4 owner's manual notice and the Optiq, Escalade IQ and Escalade IQL electronic manual notice each describe an increased risk of a crash or injury, and each states that owners are not told to stop driving. None of the four gives any interim instruction to follow before the dealer work described in the notice is carried out.

What the free repair involves

Each notice states its repair is free of charge. On the Lyriq, video display control module software is updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update. Dealers replace the vehicle certification label on the 2023 XT5 and XT6, provide the correct owner's manual for the 2024 XT4, and reset the radio so the electronic owner's manual downloads on the Optiq, Escalade IQ and Escalade IQL.

Common questions

Does the Lyriq display recall need a dealer visit?

The notice says the video display control module software will be updated by a dealer, or through an over-the-air update, free of charge. It does not say which vehicles get which method; Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223 is listed for this recall.

Why is the wrong owner's manual a safety recall?

GM states the affected 2024 XT4 vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 225. The notice says a wrong manual may contain inaccurate information about vehicle functions, which could lead to improper operation and increase the risk of a crash or injury.

Which Cadillacs have the illegible tire size label?

The notice covers certain 2023 Cadillac XT5 and XT6 vehicles, along with the 2023 GMC Acadia. Tire size information on the driver's side B-pillar certification label may be illegible, and GM says dealers will replace the label free of charge.
