Why are Kia Telluride and Niro seats under recall?

5 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Seats · model years 2020–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-02

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Kia's seat recalls cluster around the Telluride, which has been recalled twice for front power seat motors that can overheat and start a fire in 2020-2024 model year trucks — first over a stuck seat slide knob, then again in 2026 after some earlier repairs were done improperly. A separate 2025 Telluride recall covers a different problem: seat back frames that may not restrain occupants in a crash. The 2023-2025 Niro and Niro EV recall involves floor wiring under the front passenger seat that can disable air bags or trigger one unexpectedly. The 2024-2025 EV9 recall is unrelated to fire or wiring — some vehicles left the factory with second- and third-row seats missing their mounting bolts.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A power seat slide knob on the 2020-2024 Telluride that sticks or won't move freely
  • No stated warning signs before the Niro air bag wiring problem occurs
  • No stated warning signs for the 2025 Telluride seat frame recall
  • No stated warning signs for the EV9 missing seat bolt recall

Is it safe to keep driving?

Two of these recalls — the 2024 and 2026 Telluride front power seat fire recalls — tell owners to park outside and away from structures until the repair is complete, because of the fire risk; neither tells owners to stop driving. For the other three recalls in this group — the Niro seat wiring recall, the 2025 Telluride seat frame recall, and the EV9 missing seat bolt recall — the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers install a bracket and replace the seat slide knob, or install an electronic fuse assembly, on the two Telluride fire recalls; reroute or replace floor wiring and add wiring covers on the Niro; replace seat back frames on the 2025 Telluride; and inspect and install seat mounting bolts as needed on the EV9. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Is the 2026 Kia Telluride seat fire recall the same as the 2024 one?

No, they're separate. The 2024 recall addressed a stuck seat slide knob overheating the seat motor; the 2026 recall covers the same fire risk from an improper 24V407 repair and adds an electronic fuse assembly.

Does the Kia Niro seat recall affect the air bags?

Yes. Damaged floor wiring beneath the front passenger seat on 2023-2025 Niro, Niro EV, and Niro PHEV vehicles can prevent air bags and seat belts from deploying properly or cause an unintended deployment.

What Kia EV9 model years have the missing seat bolt recall?

2024 and 2025 EV9 vehicles, where second- and third-row seat mounting bolts may be missing. Dealers inspect and install bolts as necessary, free of charge.
