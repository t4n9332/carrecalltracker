Why are Kia seats being recalled for fire and restraint risks?

6 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Seats · model years 2020–2027 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 2, 2026

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

The Telluride dominates this group. Two recalls, from June 2024 and July 2026, both cover 2020-2024 Telluride vehicles and both describe the front power seat motor overheating and causing a fire while parked or driving; the 2024 notice names a stuck power seat slide knob, the 2026 notice names a stuck knob or an improper 24V407 repair. A third Telluride recall covers 2025 vehicles with power front seats, whose driver and passenger seat back frame assemblies may fail to restrain occupants in a crash. The other two are electrified models: 2023-2025 Niro EV, Plug-in Hybrid and Hybrid for damaged floor wiring beneath the front passenger seat, and 2024-2025 EV9 for missing second- and third-row seat mounting bolts.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Both 2020-2024 Telluride fire recalls name a stuck power seat slide knob as what makes the front power seat motor overheat.
  • The 2023-2025 Niro recall names an unintended side curtain air bag deployment as a possible result of the damaged floor wiring.
  • The 2024-2025 EV9 recall says the second- and third-row seat mounting bolts may be missing.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two recalls here carry an interim instruction. The June 2024 recall for 2020-2024 Telluride vehicles advises owners to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete, and the July 2026 recall for 2020-2024 Telluride vehicles gives the same instruction. Neither one tells owners to stop driving. For the 2025 Telluride seat back frame recall, the 2023-2025 Niro floor wiring recall and the 2024-2025 EV9 mounting bolt recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

All of this work is free of charge. June 2024 Telluride recall: dealers install a bracket for the power seat switch back covers and replace the seat slide knobs. July 2026 Telluride recall: dealers install an electronic fuse assembly. 2025 Telluride: inspect and replace the seat back frame assemblies. Niro: inspect, replace and reroute the floor wiring and install wiring covers. EV9: inspect and install seat mounting bolts.

Common questions

My Telluride was already repaired under recall 24V407. Is it affected again?

The July 2026 notice covers certain 2020-2024 Telluride vehicles and names a stuck power seat slide knob or an improper 24V407 repair as causing the front power seat motor to overheat. It advises parking outside and away from structures until that repair is complete.

Do I really have to park my Telluride outside?

Both the June 2024 and the July 2026 notices for 2020-2024 Telluride vehicles advise owners to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete, because the front power seat motor may overheat and result in a fire.

What does the Niro recall mean for my air bags?

The notice says damaged floor wiring beneath the front passenger seat can prevent the front air bags and seat belts from deploying properly, or cause an unintended side curtain air bag deployment. Dealers inspect, replace and reroute the wiring free of charge.
