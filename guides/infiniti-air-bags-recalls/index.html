Infiniti air bag recalls: what the QX30, QX50 and QX60 notices say

3 NHTSA recalls · Infiniti · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 27, 2024

These three Infiniti air bag recalls each describe a different failure. The 2017-2018 QX30 recall covers a driver's air bag that may unexpectedly deploy due to insufficient grounding of the steering components if an electrostatic discharge occurs and the air bag clockspring is broken. The 2019 QX50 recall covers the passenger-side knee air bag trim cover, which in a crash in very cold temperatures may separate from the module instead of splitting at the tear seam. The newest recall is much wider, covering 2023-2024 Infiniti QX50, 2024 QX60 and several Nissan models whose driver's air bag inflator may absorb moisture and deploy improperly.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. The 2017-2018 QX30 notice states that if the driver air bag inadvertently deploys it can increase the risk of injury and a crash. The 2019 QX50 notice states that if the passenger-side knee air bag cover detaches during deployment it can increase the risk of injury. The inflator moisture notice states that an air bag deploying improperly may not protect the driver as intended in a crash. Beyond that, the notices say nothing about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

For the 2017-2018 QX30, dealers add sufficient grounding to the steering components. For the 2019 QX50, retailers replace the passenger-side knee air bag module. For the inflator moisture recall covering 2023-2024 QX50, 2024 QX60 and the Nissan models listed, dealers replace the front driver's air bag assembly. Each notice states the repair is free of charge.

Common questions

Why does an Infiniti air bag recall list Nissan models too?

The driver's air bag inflator recall covers certain 2024 Titan, Frontier, Infiniti QX60 and Pathfinder, 2023-2024 Sentra, Versa, Kicks and Infiniti QX50, and 2023 Rogue vehicles. Nissan North America issued it, and dealers replace the front driver's air bag assembly free of charge.

Is the QX50 knee air bag recall about the driver or the passenger?

The 2019 QX50 notice describes the passenger-side knee air bag module. It states that in a crash in very cold temperatures the trim cover may separate from the module instead of splitting at the tear seam as designed.

What do the notices say about when these repairs started?

The QX30 recall began March 26, 2018, and the QX50 knee air bag recall began October 23, 2018. For the inflator moisture recall, owner notification letters were mailed April 10, 2024. Owners can contact Infiniti customer service to check a specific vehicle.
