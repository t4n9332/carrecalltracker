Infiniti airbag recalls: what QX30, QX50 and QX60 owners should know

3 NHTSA recalls · Infiniti · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-02-27

Three separate Infiniti airbag recalls, each with a different cause. The 2017-2018 QX30 recall involves the driver air bag itself: insufficient grounding in the steering components means an electrostatic discharge can make the air bag deploy unexpectedly if the clockspring is broken. A 2023-2024 recall, shared with several Nissan models, covers the QX50, QX60 and Pathfinder: a manufacturing issue lets the driver air bag inflator absorb moisture, so it may deploy improperly. The 2019 QX50 recall is different again, a passenger-side knee air bag whose trim cover may separate unexpectedly in a crash that happens in very cold temperatures, instead of splitting along its designed tear seam.

Warning signs to watch for

  • None of the three notices describe any warning sign an owner could notice before an air bag deploys unexpectedly or fails to work properly

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the three Infiniti air bag recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2017-2018 QX30 unexpected-deployment recall, the 2023-2024 QX50/QX60 moisture recall, and the 2019 QX50 knee air bag recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice states only that the defect increases the risk of injury, either from an unexpected deployment or from an air bag that may not perform as intended in a crash.

What the free repair involves

Dealers add grounding to the steering components on the 2017-2018 QX30 recall, replace the front driver air bag assembly on the 2023-2024 recall, and replace the passenger-side knee air bag module on the 2019 QX50 recall. All repairs are performed by dealers free of charge.

Common questions

Why would my Infiniti's air bag deploy on its own?

In the 2017-2018 QX30 recall, insufficient grounding in the steering components can let an electrostatic discharge trigger the driver air bag if the clockspring is broken, causing it to deploy without a crash.

What's wrong with the 2023-2024 QX50 and QX60 air bags?

A manufacturing issue allows the driver air bag inflator to absorb moisture. If that happens, the air bag may not deploy properly in a crash, which can reduce how well it protects the driver.

Is there a fee to fix these Infiniti air bag recalls?

No. Dealers perform the grounding fix, the air bag assembly replacement, or the knee air bag module replacement free of charge for owners of vehicles covered by these three recalls.
