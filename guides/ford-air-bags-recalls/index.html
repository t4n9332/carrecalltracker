Why have Ford side curtain and frontal airbags been recalled?

13 NHTSA recalls · Ford · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 27, 2025

Thirteen Ford airbag recalls sit here, and side airbags dominate. Side curtain or side impact bags account for most of them: the 2018 Escape (17S44), 2020 Escape and Transit diffusers, 2020 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator seat back fasteners, 2021 Explorer and Aviator weld studs (21C19), 2021 Expedition, Lincoln Navigator and 2022 Super Duty F-250 door sensors (21C26), 2021 Mustang curtain inflators, 2022 Maverick curtain modules, and 2021-2022 Bronco side impact sensors. Explorer, Aviator, Bronco and Mustang each appear twice, and the 2021 model year recurs most. The remainder are frontal and knee bags: missing booster propellant on the 2020 EcoSport and 2019-2020 Transit Connect, a 2021 Bronco passenger bag vent, a 2022 Super Duty driver bag cover, and 2021-2022 Mustang knee bag welds.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the thirteen notices in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. What they describe is crash behaviour: bags that may not deploy or inflate properly, a component or weld stud that may detach during deployment, a cover that may break apart and project fragments, and, on the 2021-2022 Bronco, a sensor that may cause bags to deploy unintentionally or not deploy as intended. For the 2020 Escape and Transit, the notice says an interim letter about the safety risk was mailed August 3, 2020, and the recall began January 11, 2021.

What the free repair involves

Most of these recalls are parts replacement: dealers replace the side curtain or side air bag modules, the front door side impact sensors, the frontal air bag module on the affected side, the driver-side air bag module, or the front passenger knee air bag. Two are inspections: the 2020 Explorer and Aviator fastener check, and the 2021-2022 Bronco sensor attachment inspect-and-repair. Every notice states the work is free of charge.

Common questions

My Ford shows more than one airbag recall — is that normal here?

Several nameplates appear twice in this group with different defects: the 2020 and 2021 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator, the 2021 and 2021-2022 Bronco, and the 2021 and 2021-2022 Mustang. Each recall has its own remedy, so check your VIN against every open recall.

Would I notice anything before an airbag failed to work?

None of these notices describes a warning light, noise, smell or other sign an owner would notice. They describe only what may happen during a crash or during deployment. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving, and each states the repair is free of charge.

Which Ford model years come up most often in these recalls?

The 2021 model year appears most: Explorer, Lincoln Aviator, Expedition, Lincoln Navigator, Bronco and Mustang all have 2021 vehicles in this group. Other years covered include the 2018 and 2020 Escape, 2019-2020 Transit Connect, 2020 EcoSport, 2022 Maverick and 2022 Super Duty.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
