Ford Air Bag Recalls Cover Deployment Problems and Loose Sensors

14 NHTSA recalls · Ford · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 31, 2026

Ford's listed air bag notices repeatedly concern improper deployment, with side and curtain air bags appearing frequently. Mustang recurs in a curtain-inflation recall for certain 2021 vehicles and a passenger knee air bag weld recall for certain 2021-2022 vehicles. Bronco appears in a passenger air bag vent recall for certain 2021 vehicles and a side impact sensor attachment recall for certain 2021-2022 vehicles. Escape has separate curtain air bag recalls for certain 2018 and 2020 vehicles; Explorer has separate attachment recalls for certain 2020 and 2021 vehicles. Certain 2022 Super Duty trucks appear in sensor and driver air bag cover recalls. Other notices cover EcoSport, Transit, Transit Connect, Expedition, F-150, and Maverick.

Warning signs to watch for

  • December 14, 2022 Bronco side impact sensor recall: air bags may unintentionally deploy.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the supplied Ford air bag notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. The notices covering Escape curtain air bags, Explorer side air bag attachments, and EcoSport and Transit Connect frontal air bags do not tell owners to stop driving. Neither do the Bronco passenger air bag and sensor notices, Mustang curtain and knee air bag notices, Super Duty cover notice, front-door sensor notice, Maverick curtain air bag notice, or Escape and Transit curtain-module notice.

What the free repair involves

The listed remedies are free. Escape, Transit, EcoSport, Transit Connect, Explorer weld-stud, Bronco passenger, Super Duty cover, and Mustang recalls replace the specified air bags or modules. Explorer's 2020 recall verifies fastener tightness. The October 2021 sensor recall replaces front-door sensors; Bronco's December 2022 recall inspects and repairs sensor attachments as necessary. Maverick receives redesigned curtain modules.

Common questions

What are the two Mustang air bag problems?

The December 2021 notice concerns incorrect compressed gas amounts in curtain inflators on certain 2021 Mustangs. The February 2022 notice concerns an insufficient passenger knee air bag weld on certain 2021-2022 Mustangs. Each specifies replacing the affected air bag free of charge.

What does the Bronco side impact sensor notice say?

The December 14, 2022 notice covers certain 2021-2022 Broncos whose side impact sensors may not be properly secured. It says air bags may unintentionally deploy or fail to deploy as intended. Dealers inspect attachments and repair as necessary, free of charge.

Do both Explorer air bag recalls have the same remedy?

The August 2020 Explorer notice specifies removing VIN-specific front seat back panels and verifying fastener tightness. The August 2021 Explorer notice concerns attachment weld studs and specifies replacing side air bag assemblies. Both notices describe the remedies as free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
