Which Subaru Suspension Recalls Tell Owners to Stop Driving?

4 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Suspension · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 19, 2026

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

Subaru’s suspension notices repeatedly concern lower control arm connections, with separate rear stabilizer bracket bolt issues. Certain 2021 Imprezas have a left-front lower control arm weld that may fail, allowing separation from the crossmember. Certain 2023 Ascents have an improperly tightened front ball joint castle nut that can lead to retention pin failure and ball joint separation. The 2026 Crosstrek and Forester notice concerns an improperly fastened castle nut on the left-front lower control arm ball joint. Crosstrek and Forester also appear in the April 2021 rear stabilizer bolt notice, covering 2018-2019 Crosstrek, 2019 Crosstrek Hybrid, and 2019 Forester vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • August 2021 Impreza control arm weld recall: tire contact with the wheel well and loss of control.
  • October 2026 Crosstrek/Forester suspension joint recall: loss of vehicle control.
  • WRJ-23 Ascent ball joint recall: loss of vehicle control while the vehicle is moving.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two recalls explicitly tell owners not to drive until the repair is completed: the August 30, 2021 Impreza control arm weld recall and the October 19, 2026 Crosstrek and Forester left-front suspension joint recall. The April 15, 2021 rear stabilizer bracket bolt notice, WRD-21, does not tell owners to stop driving. The June 1, 2023 Ascent front control arm ball joint notice, WRJ-23, also does not tell owners to stop driving. None carries a park-outside instruction.

What the free repair involves

The 2021 Impreza remedy is free control arm inspection and replacement as necessary. The October 2026 Crosstrek/Forester remedy is free inspection and replacement of the left-front lower control arm ball joint assembly as necessary. WRD-21 provides free bolt retorquing and replacement of missing bolts. WRJ-23 provides free lower control arm inspection and replacement if necessary.

Common questions

Which Subaru suspension recalls have a stop-driving instruction?

The August 2021 recall for certain 2021 Imprezas and the October 2026 recall for certain 2026 Crosstrek and Forester vehicles advise owners not to drive until repairs are complete. The other two listed suspension notices do not give that instruction.

Are the Ascent and Crosstrek/Forester ball joint repairs identical?

WRJ-23 specifies inspection and, if necessary, replacement of the lower control arm on certain 2023 Ascents. The October 2026 Crosstrek/Forester notice specifies inspection and replacement of the left-front lower control arm ball joint assembly as necessary. Both remedies are free.

What does the rear stabilizer bolt recall cover?

WRD-21 covers certain 2018-2019 Crosstrek, 2019 Crosstrek Hybrid, and 2019 Forester vehicles. Rear stabilizer bracket bolts may loosen and detach, allowing the bracket to shift. Dealers retorque the bolts and replace missing bolts free of charge.
