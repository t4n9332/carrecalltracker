Why is my Subaru's suspension recalled for loose bolts and welds?

3 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Suspension · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-06-01

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

These three Subaru suspension recalls involve different front and rear suspension hardware failing to stay properly secured. The 2021 Impreza recall covers a left-front lower control arm weld that may fail and let the control arm separate from the crossmember. The 2018-2019 Crosstrek and 2019 Forester recall covers rear stabilizer bracket bolts that may loosen and detach. The 2023 Ascent recall covers a front lower control arm ball joint castle nut that may be improperly tightened, letting the ball joint separate from the control arm. Across all three, a fastener or weld failure allows a suspension component to move or separate while driving.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2021 Impreza: tire rubbing against the wheel well if the left-front control arm weld fails
  • 2018-2019 Crosstrek and 2019 Forester: the notice describes no warning sign before the rear stabilizer bolts loosen
  • 2023 Ascent: the notice describes no warning sign before the ball joint retention pin fails

Is it safe to keep driving?

Of these three suspension recalls, one — the 2021 Impreza control arm weld recall — instructs owners not to drive the vehicle until the repair is complete, because a separated control arm may let the tire contact the wheel well and cause a loss of control. Neither the 2018-2019 Crosstrek/2019 Forester stabilizer bolt recall nor the 2023 Ascent ball joint recall tells owners to stop driving. None of these three notices include a park-outside instruction.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace the affected part for each recall: the front control arm on the 2021 Impreza, missing stabilizer bracket bolts on the 2018-2019 Crosstrek and 2019 Forester, and the front lower control arm on the 2023 Ascent. All repairs are performed at no cost to the owner.

Common questions

Are these three Subaru suspension recalls connected?

No. Each covers a different part on different models and years — a control arm weld on the 2021 Impreza, stabilizer bolts on the 2018-2019 Crosstrek and 2019 Forester, and a ball joint nut on the 2023 Ascent — so a repair under one recall does not address the others.

Can I keep driving my Impreza, Crosstrek, Forester, or Ascent under these recalls?

The 2021 Impreza notice tells owners not to drive until the control arm is repaired. The 2018-2019 Crosstrek/2019 Forester bolt recall and the 2023 Ascent ball joint recall do not tell owners to stop driving.

How do I find out if my Subaru is included?

Contact Subaru customer service or a Subaru dealer with your vehicle's VIN to confirm whether it is included in the Impreza control arm, Crosstrek/Forester bolt, or Ascent ball joint recall.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
