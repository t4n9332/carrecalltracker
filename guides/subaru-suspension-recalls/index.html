Which Subaru suspension recalls tell owners to stop driving?

4 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Suspension · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 19, 2026

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

Front lower control arm problems recur in three of these four Subaru suspension recalls. Certain 2021 Impreza vehicles have a left-front control arm weld that may fail. Certain 2023 Ascent vehicles have an improperly tightened front ball joint castle nut that can lead to retention pin failure and ball joint separation. Certain 2026 Crosstrek and Forester vehicles have an improperly fastened left-front ball joint castle nut. Crosstrek and Forester appear in two notices each: the other notice concerns rear stabilizer bracket bolts on 2018-2019 Crosstrek, 2019 Crosstrek Hybrid and 2019 Forester vehicles. The Impreza and 2026 Crosstrek/Forester notices carry stop-driving instructions.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2021 Impreza control arm weld recall: a separated control arm may cause tire contact with the wheel well and loss of control.
  • 2026 Crosstrek and Forester front suspension joint recall: joint separation can cause loss of vehicle control.
  • 2023 Ascent front control arm ball joint recall: ball joint separation while moving can cause loss of vehicle control.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two notices explicitly tell owners not to drive until repairs are completed: the 2021 Impreza control arm weld recall and the 2026 Crosstrek and Forester front suspension joint recall. The Impreza notice describes control arm separation and possible tire contact with the wheel well; the Crosstrek and Forester notice describes ball joint separation and loss of control. The 2018-2019 Crosstrek, 2019 Crosstrek Hybrid and 2019 Forester loose suspension bolt notice and the 2023 Ascent ball joint notice do not tell owners to stop driving. None gives a park-outside instruction.

What the free repair involves

Repairs are free of charge. For the Impreza, dealers inspect and replace the control arm as necessary. For 2026 Crosstrek and Forester vehicles, they inspect and replace the left-front ball joint assembly as necessary. The rear stabilizer recall requires retorquing and replacing missing bolts. For the Ascent, dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace the lower control arm.

Common questions

Which Subaru suspension notices say not to drive?

The 2021 Impreza control arm weld notice and the 2026 Crosstrek and Forester front suspension joint notice advise owners not to drive until the repair is completed. The other two suspension notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

Are the Crosstrek and Forester suspension problems the same across years?

The notices describe different parts and defects. The earlier recall concerns rear stabilizer bracket bolts on specified 2018-2019 vehicles. The 2026 recall concerns the left-front lower control arm ball joint castle nut and carries a stop-driving instruction.

What will dealers replace for the Ascent ball joint recall?

For certain 2023 Ascent vehicles, dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace the lower control arm free of charge. The notice describes an improperly tightened ball joint castle nut that can result in retention pin failure and ball joint separation.
