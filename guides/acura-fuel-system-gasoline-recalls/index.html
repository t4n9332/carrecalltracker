What do Acura's fuel system recalls say about stalling and leaks?

6 NHTSA recalls · Acura · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 23, 2025

Fuel pump failure is the thread running through this group. Three recalls - May 2020, March 2021 and December 2023 - cover a low-pressure fuel pump inside the tank that may fail and stall the engine. The December 2023 one is the broadest, listing 2013-2023 Acura ILX, MDX, MDX Hybrid, RDX, RLX and TLX plus 2015-2020 NSX. The other three recalls are separate problems: 2021-2023 TLX vehicles whose fuel filler neck and tank may have been improperly welded, allowing a fuel leak; 2021-2025 TLX Type-S and 2022-2025 MDX Type-S with an FI-ECU software error that may cause a stall or loss of power; and 2014-2019 ILX vehicles whose fuel gauge may show an inaccurate level.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An engine that stalls while driving - named in the fuel pump recalls of May 2020, March 2021 and December 2023.
  • An engine stall or loss of power - named in the January 2025 recall of Acura MDX Type-S and TLX Type-S.
  • A fuel leak - named in the December 2024 recall of 2021-2023 Acura TLX vehicles.
  • A fuel gauge showing more gas than the vehicle actually has, so it may run out of gas and stall - named in the January 2019 recall of 2014-2019 ILX vehicles.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park the vehicle outside - including the December 2024 recall of 2021-2023 TLX vehicles, where the notice says a fuel leak near an ignition source increases the risk of fire. The three fuel pump recalls, the January 2025 FI-ECU software recall and the January 2019 ILX fuel gauge recall all describe an engine stall and the crash risk that follows, but none of them gives owners any instruction about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is free of charge. The three fuel pump recalls have dealers replace the fuel pump assembly, or the fuel pump module in the December 2023 recall. The December 2024 TLX recall has dealers replace the fuel tank assembly. The January 2019 ILX recall has dealers inspect and, as necessary, replace the fuel tank. The January 2025 Type-S recall has dealers reprogram the FI-ECU software.

Common questions

My Acura was recalled for the fuel pump more than once - why?

The December 2023 recall states it is an expansion of NHTSA recalls 21V-215 and 20V-314, and the March 2021 recall states it is an expansion of 20V-314. Check your VIN against each recall rather than assuming one covers the others.

Does the fuel tank weld recall apply to every Acura TLX?

The December 2024 notice lists 2021-2023 TLX vehicles only, and says the fuel filler neck and fuel tank may have been improperly welded. Dealers replace the fuel tank assembly free of charge; Honda's customer service line is 1-888-234-2138.

What do the notices say to do while I wait for a fuel pump appointment?

The notices for the three fuel pump recalls say dealers will replace the pump free of charge and give mailing dates for owner letters. They do not tell owners to stop driving, and they do not set out any interim step to take beforehand.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
