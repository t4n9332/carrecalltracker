Why does my Acura keep getting fuel pump and tank recalls?

6 NHTSA recalls · Acura · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-01-23

Six Acura fuel-system recalls cluster into three patterns. Three separate campaigns (2020, 2021, and 2023) each expand the last to cover a failing low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank that can stall the engine, eventually reaching Acura ILX, MDX, MDX Hybrid, RDX, RLX and TLX across many model years. A December 2024 recall is different: welding defects in 2021-2023 TLX fuel tanks that can leak fuel near an ignition source, a fire risk rated critical. A January 2025 recall covers a software error in TLX Type S, MDX Type S and Pilot that can stall the engine or cut power, unrelated to the fuel pump hardware. A 2019 recall covers ILX fuel gauges misreading from a deformed tank.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine stalling or losing power while driving (fuel pump recalls and the TLX Type S/MDX Type S/Pilot software recall)
  • Fuel smell or a visible leak near the fuel tank (2021-2023 TLX weld recall)
  • Fuel gauge showing more fuel than the tank actually holds (ILX recall)
  • Running out of gas unexpectedly even though the gauge shows fuel remaining (ILX recall)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the six recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, including the critical-severity TLX fuel tank weld recall. For the three fuel pump recalls, the TLX weld recall, the Type S/Pilot software recall, and the ILX fuel gauge recall, the notices describe the risk of stalling or fire but do not instruct owners to stop driving or to park away from structures while awaiting repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the fuel pump assembly or module in the three fuel-pump recalls, replace the fuel tank assembly in the TLX weld recall, reprogram the fuel-injection control unit software in the Type S/Pilot recall, and inspect and, if needed, replace the fuel tank in the ILX gauge recall. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why did my Acura get more than one fuel pump recall?

Honda issued three separate campaigns, in 2020, 2021 and 2023, each one expanding on the last as more vehicles and model years were found to have the same low-pressure fuel pump failure. Check your VIN under each recall number to see which applies.

Is the TLX fuel tank recall a fire risk?

Yes. The December 2024 recall says an improperly welded fuel filler neck and tank can leak fuel, and a leak near an ignition source increases fire risk. It is rated critical severity and affects 2021-2023 TLX.

If my fuel gauge reads full, could my ILX still be low on gas?

The 2019 ILX recall says a deformed fuel tank can cause the fuel level float to stick, making the gauge show more fuel than is actually in the tank, which can lead to unexpectedly running out of gas.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
