BMW Electrical Recalls Involve Battery Fires and Starter Connection Problems

28 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 23, 2026

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory. 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Battery short circuits, overheating connections, and starter-related fire risks recur in BMW’s supplied electrical notices. The 2020 plug-in hybrid notices repeatedly include X3 xDrive30e, alongside certain 330e and X5 xDrive45e vehicles, but identify separate welding-bead and battery-cell debris problems. Electric i4 and iX models recur in the July 2022 battery short-circuit and July 2025 battery-module notices. Starter-generator connections feature in the December 2020 notice and two 2025 notices involving X3 vehicles. The August 2024 notice addresses starter overheating after repeated starting attempts following starter failure. Other listed cases include the 2019 X5 body controller and 2024 R 1300 GS starter relay.

Warning signs to watch for

  • August 1, 2024 starter motor recall — the notice describes starter motor failure.
  • February 21, 2025 X3 battery cable recall — the engine may stall.
  • April 1, 2025 starter connection recall — a loose connection may cause an engine stall.
  • July 16, 2025 battery module recall — high-voltage system shutdown may cause loss of drive power.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One supplied notice carries both stop-driving and park-outside instructions. The July 27, 2022 i4/iX battery recall tells owners not to drive or charge affected vehicles and to park outside, away from structures, until the remedy is complete. The September 30, 2020 hybrid battery debris notice instructs drivers not to charge or drive in manual or sport mode until the remedy is completed. Its shift-paddle instruction is truncated in the supplied text. The remaining supplied electrical notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

The July 2022 recall specifies free high-voltage battery replacement, while July 2025 specifies free replacement of specific cell modules. The 2020 hybrid notices specify free inspection and necessary module replacement. February 2025 specifies free connection inspection and tightening or necessary component replacement. August 2024 specifies a free software update. July 2024 R 1300 GS repairs include free relay replacement and software updating.

Common questions

Which BMW electrical recall tells owners to stop driving and park outside?

The July 27, 2022 notice covers certain 2022-2023 iX xDrive50 and iX M60, plus 2022 i4 eDrive40 and i4 M50 vehicles. Owners are instructed not to drive or charge them and to park outside, away from structures, until the remedy is complete.

What is different about the two 2025 BMW X3 connection notices?

The February notice identifies an improperly tightened battery cable connection and specifies inspection and tightening or necessary replacement. The April notice says the starter-generator connection may loosen and specifies inspection and necessary starter-generator and positive battery cable replacement. Both remedies are free.

What does BMW say causes starter overheating in the August 2024 recall?

The August 1, 2024 notice says repeated starting attempts after starter motor failure can cause electrical overload and overheating. It identifies a risk of nearby combustible material igniting in the engine compartment. Dealers update the vehicle software free of charge.
