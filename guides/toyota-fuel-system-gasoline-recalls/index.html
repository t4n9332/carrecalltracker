Why do Toyota fuel pumps and fuel lines keep getting recalled?

10 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 22, 2025

Five of these ten recalls involve a failing fuel pump, and each of those notices says pump failure can stall the engine: the two broad Toyota and Lexus fuel pump campaigns covering Camry, Corolla, Highlander, Sienna, Tacoma, Tundra, 4Runner and many Lexus models, the 2018-2019 Toyota 86 shared with Subaru, the 2019-2020 Yaris with a cracking impeller, and a 2018 Camry recall announced in January 2025. Four more are fuel leak and fire recalls: 2018 Camry V6 delivery pipes, 2019 Camry Hybrid and Avalon Hybrid filler pipes, the 2020-2021 Supra fuel tank weld shared with BMW Z4, and 2022-2023 Tundra fuel tubes chafing against brake lines. A 2020 Highlander ECU programming error stands apart.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An engine that stalls while driving — the 2018-2019 Toyota and Lexus fuel pump recalls.
  • Engine stall — 2019-2020 Yaris, where the notice says an impeller may crack and deform, causing the pump to fail.
  • Engine stall while driving — 2018-2019 Toyota 86 and Subaru low-pressure fuel pump recall.
  • A stall while using the stop and restart feature — 2020 Highlander ECU programming recall.
  • Engine stall — 2018 Camry low-pressure fuel pump recall (25TA01).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these ten notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The four leak recalls — 2018 Camry V6, 2019 Camry Hybrid and Avalon Hybrid, 2020-2021 Supra fuel tank, and 2022-2023 Tundra fuel tube — say leaking fuel in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of fire. The fuel pump and Highlander ECU notices say a stall while driving increases the risk of a crash. Beyond stating those risks, the notices give owners no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Repairs depend on the recall. Dealers replace the fuel pump or pump assembly on the five pump recalls, inspect the 2018 Camry V6 fuel pipes for correct installation, inspect and if necessary replace the 2019 Camry Hybrid and Avalon Hybrid filler pipe, replace the Supra fuel tank, add protective materials and a clamp to the Tundra fuel tube, and reprogram the 2020 Highlander ECU. All free of charge.

Common questions

I have a 2018 Camry — is it in more than one fuel recall?

The 2018 Camry appears in four of these recalls: the V6 fuel delivery pipe recall, both broad Toyota and Lexus fuel pump campaigns, and the low-pressure fuel pump recall announced in January 2025. Each carries its own Toyota recall number, so a VIN check shows which ones apply.

What do the fuel pump recalls say can happen?

The pump notices say the fuel pump may fail — several specify the low-pressure pump inside the fuel tank — and that failure can cause the engine to stall while driving, increasing the risk of a crash. The 2019-2020 Yaris notice adds that an impeller may crack and deform.

Do any of these Toyota fuel recalls involve a fire risk?

Four do. The 2018 Camry V6, 2019 Camry Hybrid and Avalon Hybrid, 2020-2021 Supra fuel tank, and 2022-2023 Tundra fuel tube notices each describe fuel that may leak, and say leaking fuel in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire. None tells owners to park outside.
