Toyota Fuel System Recalls Cover Pump Failures and Fuel Leaks

11 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 14, 2026

Fuel pump failures and fuel leaks recur across these 11 Toyota recall notices. The 2018 Camry appears repeatedly: in the V6 fuel delivery pipe recall and several fuel pump recalls. The broad January and November 2020 pump notices cover numerous Toyota models, with 2018–2019 vehicles recurring prominently; the November notice also includes several 2020 models. Separate pump recalls cover 2018–2019 Toyota 86 and 2019–2020 Yaris vehicles. Leak concerns involve improperly connected pipes on 2018 Camry V6 and 2019 Camry Hybrid/Avalon Hybrid vehicles, tank weld failure on 2020–2021 Supra vehicles, fuel tube chafing on 2022–2023 Tundra/Tundra Hybrid vehicles, and a disconnecting Land Cruiser Hybrid fuel connection. The 2020 Highlander programming recall concerns fuel supply during stop and restart.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine stalling while driving: the January 13, 2020 and November 4, 2020 Toyota/Lexus fuel pump recalls.
  • Engine stalling while driving: the July 29, 2021 Subaru/Toyota 86 fuel pump recall.
  • Engine stalling: the January 22, 2025 Camry fuel pump recall and September 27, 2021 Yaris fuel pump recall.
  • Vehicle stalling: the March 19, 2020 Highlander fuel programming recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these 11 notices instructs owners to stop driving or park outside. The five leak-related notices—Camry V6, Camry/Avalon Hybrid, Supra, Tundra/Tundra Hybrid, and Land Cruiser Hybrid—describe fire risks but do not tell owners to stop driving. The January and November 2020 Toyota/Lexus pump notices, Toyota 86 pump notice, 2025 Camry pump notice, Yaris pump notice, and Highlander programming notice describe stalling risks but likewise do not tell owners to stop driving. The notices provide no stop-driving or park-outside instruction for this group.

What the free repair involves

The notices specify free remedies: fuel pump replacement for the January 2020 recall; an improved pump assembly for November 2020; and pump or assembly replacement for Toyota 86, 2025 Camry, and Yaris. Other remedies include Camry pipe inspection/correction, hybrid filler-pipe inspection/replacement, Supra tank replacement, Tundra protective materials and clamp installation, Land Cruiser connection inspection with specified clamp/tube work, and Highlander ECU reprogramming.

Common questions

Why does the 2018 Toyota Camry appear in several fuel recalls?

The notices identify different problems involving certain 2018 Camry vehicles. The February 2018 notice concerns fuel delivery pipe connections on V6 models. The January and November 2020 notices and January 2025 notice include fuel pump failure concerns.

What repair does the Tundra fuel tube recall specify?

The August 10, 2023 notice covers certain 2022–2023 Tundra and Tundra Hybrid vehicles whose plastic fuel tube may chafe against metallic brake lines. Dealers install protective materials and a clamp on the fuel tube, free of charge.

Does the Highlander fuel programming recall require a replacement pump?

The March 19, 2020 notice specifies free ECU reprogramming for certain 2020 Highlanders with the 3.5-liter V6 gasoline engine. It describes incorrect fuel supply while using stop and restart. The separate November 2020 pump notice specifies an improved fuel pump assembly.
