Why Do Toyota Fuel Systems Keep Leaking or Stalling Engines?

10 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-01-22

These 10 recalls fall into two groups. Four involve fuel leaks that raise fire risk: improperly connected fuel pipes or hoses on the 2018 Camry V6 and 2019 Camry/Avalon Hybrid, a failed fuel-tank weld shared by the Supra and BMW Z4, and a chafing fuel tube on the Tundra and Tundra Hybrid. The other six involve fuel pumps or fuel delivery that can stall the engine, including two very large recalls spanning dozens of Toyota and Lexus models from 2020, a shared Subaru/Toyota 86 fuel-pump recall, a 2025 Camry low-pressure fuel-pump recall, a Highlander ECU programming error affecting the stop-start feature, and a Yaris fuel-pump impeller defect.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A fuel odor or visible fuel leak near the engine, especially in the four leak-related recalls
  • The engine stalling or failing to restart, particularly after using the stop-and-restart feature (Highlander)
  • The engine stalling while driving at high speed or under load
  • The fuel-leak recalls describe no warning sign before a hose, pipe, or tank weld fails

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these 10 fuel-system recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving, not the four fire-risk fuel-leak recalls on the Camry, Avalon Hybrid, Supra, Z4, and Tundra, and not the six fuel-pump or fuel-delivery recalls covering Toyota, Lexus, Subaru, Toyota 86, Yaris, Camry, and Highlander models that can cause the engine to stall while driving. Several of these recalls, including the fuel-pump campaigns, note that a stall increases the risk of a crash.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and correctly reconnect or replace fuel pipes, hoses, and the fuel tank; install a protective sleeve and clamp on the Tundra's fuel tube; replace the fuel pump assembly on the affected pump recalls; and reprogram the engine control unit on the Highlander. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Do I need to stop driving if my Toyota has an open fuel-leak recall?

None of these leak recalls include a stop-driving or park-outside order. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving, but they do say a leak near an ignition source raises fire risk, so scheduling the free repair promptly matters.

Why were so many different Toyota and Lexus models recalled for fuel pumps in 2020?

Toyota used the same low-pressure fuel pump design across many Toyota and Lexus models built in overlapping years, so when the pump's impeller was found to crack and stall the engine, the recall had to cover all of them.

Is the Tundra fuel tube recall related to the Tundra headlight recalls?

No. The fuel tube recall involves chafing against brake lines that can leak fuel and raise fire risk, a separate issue from the Tundra's wiring and lighting recalls covered under a different recall group.
