Why do so many Toyota trucks and SUVs get label recalls?

16 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Equipment · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-09-02

Nearly every recall in this Toyota equipment group involves an incorrect or illegible load-carrying capacity label, not a mechanical defect. Tundra appears most often — its bed liner and spray-in liner accessories, floor mats, and general production labels have each triggered separate recalls between 2018 and 2026. The 4Runner has three separate label recalls tied to TRD wheels, 20-inch wheels, and label fading. Broader multi-model recalls in 2017-2018 and 2019 covered labels across the 4Runner, Highlander, RAV4, Camry, Corolla, Sienna, Yaris and Lexus GX460. Aside from labels, a single unrelated 2018 recall covers a missing air bag and child-seating warning label on the Prius C sold in Puerto Rico.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A load capacity label showing an incorrect weight value, or overstating or understating capacity
  • A load capacity label that is faded, damaged, or illegible over time
  • A missing air bag and child-seating warning label (Prius C, Puerto Rico)
  • Most label recalls describe no other mechanical warning sign — the label itself is the defect

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the recalls in this Toyota equipment group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Every recall here — covering label errors on the Tundra, 4Runner, RAV4, Highlander, Camry, Corolla, Sienna, Yaris, Lexus GX460, and the missing air bag and child-seating warning label on the Prius C — describes a risk tied to overloading or restraint guidance rather than a mechanical failure, and none of the notices tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across this group, Toyota and its regional distributors correct the problem by mailing or having dealers install a replacement label with accurate load-capacity or weight information, or in one case a missing air bag and child-seating label. No parts are repaired or replaced — only the labels — and every recall states the correction is provided free of charge.

Common questions

Why do Toyota label recalls happen so often?

Load carrying capacity labels must meet exact federal accuracy requirements under FMVSS 110. Errors can occur from double-counted accessory weight, incorrect calculations, or labels that fade or become illegible, each triggering a separate recall even without a mechanical defect.

Do I need to bring my Toyota to a dealer for a label recall?

It depends on the recall — some send a replacement label directly by mail, while others have the dealer install it. The notices in this group specify which method applies to each affected vehicle.

Does an incorrect label mean my Toyota is already overloaded?

No. The notices say an incorrect or illegible label could lead an owner to overload the vehicle without realizing it, increasing crash risk — not that the vehicle is currently overloaded.
