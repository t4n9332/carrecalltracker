Toyota load capacity label recalls: what the wrong sticker means

16 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Equipment · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 2, 2026

Nearly every recall here concerns a label rather than a mechanical part. The repeat offender is the load carrying capacity modification label: values that are wrong after an accessory such as a spray-in or spray-on bed liner, factory floor mats or a wheel package was added, and text that is illegible or fades. These are Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 110 compliance recalls. Tundra and 4Runner come up most often — 4Runner from 2017 through 2023, Tundra from 2017 into 2025 — and the regional distributors Southeast Toyota and Gulf States Toyota issued several alongside Toyota itself. One recall stands apart: 2018 Prius C sold in Puerto Rico, where the front passenger air bag and child seating position label may not have been attached.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A load capacity label whose text has become illegible or faded, in the Gulf States Toyota recalls covering 2019 models and 2022 4Runner, Tacoma, Highlander and Highlander Hybrid.
  • A missing front passenger air bag and child occupant seating position label, in the 2018 Prius C recall for vehicles sold in Puerto Rico.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the notices in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. What they describe is loading: an incorrect, illegible or missing load carrying capacity label may lead an owner to overload the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. In the 2018-2019 Tundra floor mat label recall, Toyota describes only that the label falls outside the margin the standard allows, so the vehicles are not compliant. The 2018 Prius C notice says occupants may not follow proper restraint usage guidelines without the label.

What the free repair involves

Most of these are corrected with a new label. For the 2026 RAV4 Hybrid and Tundra recall and the 2023 Tundra bed liner recall, dealers replace the load carrying capacity modification label. Southeast Toyota and Gulf States Toyota mail replacement labels to owners, with detailed replacement instructions in the 2022 GST recall. Toyota mails the removable Prius C labels. All of it is free of charge.

Common questions

Is a wrong load capacity label really worth a dealer visit?

The notices say an incorrect, illegible or missing label can lead to overloading the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash, and that the vehicles do not comply with FMVSS 110. The remedy is a corrected label, free of charge, mailed to owners in several of these recalls.

Why would adding a bed liner or floor mats trigger a recall?

Several notices cover accessory-related labels: 2019 Tundra with a spray-in bed liner, 2023 Tundra and Tundra Hybrid with a spray-on bed liner, and 2018-2019 Tundra where accessory all-weather floor mats were counted twice. Each recall provides a corrected label free of charge.

My vehicle came through a regional distributor — who do I call?

Southeast Toyota Distributors recalls list SET customer service, given as 1-866-405-4226 in some notices and 954-429-2000 in another. Gulf States Toyota recalls list GST at 1-800-444-1074. Toyota's own label recalls list Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 or 1-888-270-9371.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
