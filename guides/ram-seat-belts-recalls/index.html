Why Do Ram ProMaster Vans Keep Getting Seat Belt Recalls?

6 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Seat Belts · model years 2019–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-30

Seat belt recalls in this Ram group split between commercial vans and the 1500 pickup. Four recalls affect the Ram ProMaster: two 2019 ProMaster models (shared with 2020 Ford Transit vans built by outside upfitters) had Freedman foldaway seat belt retractors set incorrectly and unable to properly secure occupants; a 2021 ProMaster and ProMaster City recall involved child seat retractors deactivating too early; and a 2022-2023 ProMaster recall involved a driver seat belt warning light and chime that might not activate. Separately, the Ram 1500 and 2500 pickups had two unrelated seat belt recalls — front seat belt retractors that may not properly lock in 2019 Ram 2500 and 2019-2020 Ram 1500 models, and rear seat belt buckle anchors improperly installed in 2019-2021 Ram 1500 models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No warning sign is described for the ProMaster foldaway seat belt retractor recalls
  • A child seat that does not stay secured because the seat belt retractor deactivates early (2021 ProMaster/ProMaster City)
  • A seat belt warning light or chime that fails to activate for the driver (2022-2023 ProMaster)
  • Front seat belts that may not properly hold an occupant during a crash (2019 Ram 2500 and 2019-2020 Ram 1500)
  • Rear seat belt anchors improperly installed (2019-2021 Ram 1500)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This includes the two Ford Transit/Ram ProMaster foldaway seat retractor recalls, the Ram 1500 and 2500 front seat belt retractor recall, the Ram 1500 rear seat belt anchor recall, the ProMaster/ProMaster City child seat retractor recall, and the ProMaster driver seat belt warning recall. The notices describe how an unsecured or improperly restrained occupant faces a higher risk of injury in a crash, but they do not instruct owners to stop driving while a repair is arranged.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, dealers replace the affected seat belt retractors — including the foldaway seat retractors, the front seat belt retractors, and the ProMaster child-seat retractors — inspect and attach the Ram 1500 rear seat belt anchor to the body structure, and replace the ProMaster driver seat belt buckle as needed. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why do Ram ProMaster vans have so many seat belt recalls?

Four separate defects are involved: two recalls cover Freedman foldaway seat retractors set incorrectly on 2019 ProMaster vans, one covers 2021 ProMaster/ProMaster City child seat retractors deactivating early, and one covers a 2022-2023 ProMaster driver seat belt warning chime that may not activate.

What's wrong with the Ram 1500 seat belts?

Two unrelated issues: 2019 Ram 2500 and 2019-2020 Ram 1500 front seat belts may not properly lock and slow an occupant's forward movement in a crash, and 2019-2021 Ram 1500 rear seat belt buckle anchors may have been improperly installed.

Are these seat belt recall repairs free?

Yes. In every recall in this group, the manufacturer notifies owners and dealers replace or repair the affected seat belt retractor, buckle, or anchor at no cost, as required under federal recall rules.
