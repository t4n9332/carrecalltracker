Which Ram seat belt recalls affect your truck or van?

6 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Seat Belts · model years 2019–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-30

Two problems dominate. Retractors account for most of the group: block-out zones set incorrectly on Freedman foldaway seats in converted 2019 Ram ProMaster and 2019-2020 Ford Transit vans, in recalls from National Van Builders and TransitWorks; front retractors that may not properly slow an occupant's forward movement after locking on 2019 Ram 2500, 2019-2020 Ram 1500, 2019 Jeep Wrangler and 2020 Jeep Gladiator; and automatic locking retractors deactivating early on 2021 Ram ProMaster and ProMaster City, preventing a child restraint from securing. The other two cover second-row center and left-side buckle anchors improperly installed on 2019-2026 Ram 1500, and a driver seat belt warning light and chime that may not activate on 2022-2023 Ram ProMaster. The ProMaster line appears more than any other Ram model here.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The driver seat belt warning light and audible chime may not activate as intended - the 2022-2023 Ram ProMaster recall.
  • A child restraint system may not secure properly because the automatic locking retractor deactivates early - the 2021 Ram ProMaster and ProMaster City recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices carries a stop-driving instruction, and none tells owners to park outside. The two Freedman retractor conversion recalls, the front retractor recall, the 2021 child restraint retractor recall, the 2019-2026 Ram 1500 buckle anchor recall and the 2022-2023 ProMaster warning system recall each describe an increased risk of injury in a crash, then move to the dealer remedy. The notices say nothing more about driving the vehicle before that repair is carried out.

What the free repair involves

Work is inspection and replacement. Dealers replace the retractors in the National Van Builders and TransitWorks conversion recalls, and the front seat belt retractors in the 2019-2020 Ram 1500 and 2019 Ram 2500 recall. For the 2021 ProMaster they inspect and replace retractors as necessary, for 2022-2023 ProMaster the driver seat belt buckle, and for Ram 1500 they inspect and attach the buckle anchor. All free of charge.

Common questions

Which Ram models have the seat belt retractor recalls?

The front retractor recall covers 2019 Ram 2500 and 2019-2020 Ram 1500, alongside two Jeep models. The child restraint retractor recall covers 2021 Ram ProMaster and ProMaster City. Converted 2019 ProMaster vans with Freedman foldaway seats have their own retractor recall.

What is being checked on my Ram 1500 second-row seat belt?

The 2019-2026 Ram 1500 notice says the second-row center and left-side seat belt buckle anchors may have been improperly installed. Dealers inspect and attach the seat belt buckle anchor to the body structure as necessary, free of charge.

Do these notices tell owners to stop using the affected seats?

None of the six tells owners to stop driving or to stop using a seat. They state the increased risk of injury in a crash, the safety standard involved, and the free dealer inspection or replacement, and say nothing further.
