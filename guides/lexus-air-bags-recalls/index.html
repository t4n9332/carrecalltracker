Which Lexus models have air bag recalls, and what's wrong?

4 NHTSA recalls · Lexus · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 20, 2024

Occupant detection is the thread running through this Lexus group. Two recalls involve the Occupant Classification System. A short circuit in the OCS sensor can prevent the front passenger air bag from deploying on 2020-2021 Lexus ES350, RX350 and RX450H and on 2020-2022 ES300H. Interference between internal parts of the front passenger seat can cause the OCS sensor to detect the occupant incorrectly on 2018-2021 LS500 and 2018-2020 LS500 Hybrid with the Executive Package, recall 24LA03. Separately, a broken seat belt tension sensor wire harness can deactivate three passenger air bags on 2008-2019 LX 570, and on 2024 TX350, TX500 Hybrid and TX550 Hybrid+ the curtain air bags may not unroll as intended.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four Lexus air bag recalls tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The LX 570 seat belt tension sensor recall, the OCS sensor short circuit recall, the LS500 Executive Package recall 24LA03 and the TX curtain air bag recall 24LA05 each describe a risk of injury in a crash without giving owners any driving instruction. For the OCS sensor recall, Toyota mailed interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk in February 2024 ahead of final remedy letters.

What the free repair involves

Repairs differ by recall. For the LX 570, dealers modify the front passenger seat belt assembly or replace it with an improved one as necessary. For the OCS sensor short circuit, dealers inspect and replace the sensor as necessary. Under 24LA03, dealers inspect and repair the front passenger seat as necessary. Under 24LA05, dealers replace the driver and passenger curtain shield air bag assemblies. All are free of charge.

Common questions

How do I know if my Lexus passenger air bag is affected?

It depends which recall lists your model. The OCS sensor recall covers 2020-2021 ES350, RX350 and RX450H and 2020-2022 ES300H; 24LA03 covers 2018-2021 LS500 and 2018-2020 LS500 Hybrid with the Executive Package. A dealer can check your VIN.

Do the notices say what an owner would see or feel beforehand?

These four notices describe the defect and the crash risk only. None of them states a warning light, sound or other sign an owner would notice, so checking your VIN with a Lexus dealer is how to find out whether your vehicle is covered.

My TX is new. Why are the curtain air bags being replaced?

The 2024 TX350, TX500 Hybrid and TX550 Hybrid+ recall states the driver and passenger side curtain air bags may not unroll as intended and could partially deploy outside an open window. Dealers replace both curtain shield air bag assemblies free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
