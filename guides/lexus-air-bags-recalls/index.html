Lexus air bag recalls involving passenger sensors and curtain deployment

4 NHTSA recalls · Lexus · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 20, 2024

Front-passenger protection is the recurring concern in these Lexus air bag notices. Certain 2008-2019 LX 570 vehicles have a seat belt tension sensor wire harness that may break and deactivate three passenger air bags. Another notice identifies possible Occupant Classification System sensor shorts, covering 2020-2021 ES350, RX350 and RX450H, 2021 ES250 and 2020-2022 ES300H vehicles. Certain Executive Package LS500 vehicles from 2018-2021 and LS500 Hybrid vehicles from 2018-2020 have a separate occupant-detection problem involving interference between seat parts. The remaining notice concerns curtain air bags on certain 2024 TX350, TX500 Hybrid and TX550 Hybrid+ vehicles. Passenger detection repeats, while TX curtain deployment presents a distinct protection problem.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The LX 570 seat belt sensor-harness notice, the December 2023 OCS sensor notice, the LS Executive Package seat notice and the 2024 TX curtain-air-bag notice do not tell owners to stop driving. None carries a park-outside instruction. Their stated risks concern deactivated passenger air bags, passenger air bags that may not deploy, incorrect occupant detection or improper curtain-air-bag deployment during a crash. The notices describe those risks and dealer remedies without providing a stop-driving instruction for these recalls.

What the free repair involves

LX 570 dealers modify the passenger seat belt assembly or replace it with an improved one as necessary. The December 2023 recall requires sensor inspection and replacement as necessary. LS 24LA03 requires passenger-seat inspection and repair as necessary. TX dealers replace both curtain shield air bag assemblies. Each notice states its remedy is free of charge.

Common questions

Which air bags can the LX 570 harness problem deactivate?

The notice for certain 2008-2019 Lexus LX 570 vehicles says a broken seat belt tension sensor wire harness may deactivate the front passenger air bag, knee air bag and passenger seat-mounted side air bag. Dealers modify or replace the passenger seat belt assembly as necessary.

Which Lexus LS vehicles have the Executive Package seat recall?

Recall 24LA03 covers certain 2018-2021 LS500 and 2018-2020 LS500 Hybrid vehicles equipped with the Executive Package. Interference between internal passenger-seat parts can cause incorrect occupant detection. Dealers inspect and repair the front passenger seat as necessary, free of charge.

What happens to the curtain air bags in the Lexus TX recall?

The notice for certain 2024 TX350, TX500 Hybrid and TX550 Hybrid+ vehicles says curtain air bags may not unroll as intended and could partially deploy outside an open window. Dealers replace the driver and passenger curtain shield air bag assemblies free of charge.
