Why has Lexus recalled several models over airbag defects?

4 NHTSA recalls · Lexus · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-06-20

Three of these four recalls share a pattern: a front passenger airbag that can fail to deploy because a sensor or wiring problem stops it from working correctly. On the 2018-2019 Lexus LX570, a seat belt tension sensor wire harness can break and deactivate the front passenger, knee and side airbags. On the 2020-2021 Lexus ES, a short circuit in the Occupant Classification System sensor can block the front passenger airbag. On the 2018, 2019 and 2021 Lexus LS, built with the Executive Package, the same sensor can misread the seat and again block proper deployment. The fourth recall is different: on the 2024 Lexus TX Hybrid, the side curtain airbags may not unroll correctly and could deploy outside an open window.

Warning signs to watch for

  • None of these notices describe a warning light or other noticeable sign before a crash; the defects are found through inspection, not through anything an owner would notice while driving

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2018-2019 LX570 seat belt sensor recall, the 2020-2021 ES sensor recall, the 2018, 2019 and 2021 LS sensor recall, and the 2024 TX Hybrid curtain airbag recall all describe only a free dealer repair. Each notice states that a failed or improperly deployed airbag increases the risk of injury in a crash, but none of the four notices tells owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace or repair the affected part for each recall: the LX570's front passenger seat belt assembly, the ES's Occupant Classification System sensor, the LS's front passenger seat, and the TX Hybrid's driver and passenger curtain airbag assemblies. All of these repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Lexus models had front passenger airbags that might not deploy?

The 2018-2019 LX570 (broken seat belt sensor wire), the 2020-2021 ES (short circuit in the Occupant Classification System sensor), and the 2018, 2019 and 2021 LS with the Executive Package (a seat assembly that can make the same sensor misread the occupant).

Is the TX Hybrid curtain airbag recall the same defect?

No. It is a separate issue: the driver and passenger curtain airbags on the 2024 TX Hybrid and related 2024 Grand Highlander models may not unroll correctly and could deploy outside an open window.

Will I be told if my airbag isn't working before a crash?

The notices do not describe a warning light or noticeable sign for any of these recalls. The defects are identified through the recall inspection or repair process, not through anything drivers would notice.
