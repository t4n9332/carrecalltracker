Chrysler Wheelchair Restraint Recalls Address Retractors That May Not Lock

3 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · Equipment Adaptive/mobility · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 11, 2026

These mobility equipment notices repeat one problem: wheelchair restraint retractors may not lock, leaving the wheelchair improperly secured and able to move during transit. The affected Chrysler vehicles depend on the company issuing the recall. Rollx's December 2025 notice lists Pacifica, Voyager, and Town and Country among multiple makes and directs readers to its recall report for specific model years. Vantage Mobility's December 2025 notice identifies certain 2021-2025 Pacificas equipped with QRT-Deluxe or QRT-Max restraints. Braun's May 2026 notice covers certain 2023-2026 Pacificas. Pacifica is therefore the recurring Chrysler model, with restraint inspection and replacement appearing across the three remedies.

Warning signs to watch for

  • December 2025 Rollx recall: restraint retractors may not lock, leaving a wheelchair improperly secured.
  • December 2025 Vantage Mobility recall: restraint retractors may not lock, leaving a wheelchair improperly secured.
  • May 2026 Braun recall: restraint retractors may not lock, leaving a wheelchair improperly secured.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The December 2025 Rollx and Vantage Mobility notices and the May 2026 Braun notice do not tell owners to stop driving. None of these three notices gives a park-outside instruction. Each describes the same injury risk: a wheelchair that is not properly secured can move while the vehicle is in transit. The supplied notices prescribe inspection and replacement work, but they do not provide an assurance about continued driving or a separate interim instruction for transporting a wheelchair.

What the free repair involves

The remedies are free. Rollx will work with Q'Straint to inspect and replace retractors as necessary. Vantage Mobility's notice says dealers will inspect and replace the retractors. Braun will work with Q'Straint to inspect and replace retractors as necessary. The repair concerns the wheelchair restraint retractors identified in each notice.

Common questions

Which Pacifica model years appear in these wheelchair restraint recalls?

Vantage Mobility identifies certain 2021-2025 Pacificas with QRT-Deluxe or QRT-Max restraints. Braun identifies certain 2023-2026 Pacificas. Rollx lists Pacifica among its affected models but directs owners to its recall report for specific model year information.

Who handles the wheelchair restraint repair on a converted Chrysler?

The remedy depends on the recall issuer. Rollx and Braun say they will work with Q'Straint to inspect and replace retractors as necessary. Vantage Mobility says dealers will inspect and replace retractors. Each notice states that its remedy is free.

What restraint problem should wheelchair van owners know about?

Each supplied notice says the retractors may not lock, preventing proper wheelchair securement. The stated consequence is wheelchair movement during transit and an increased risk of injury. The notices do not describe a warning light, alarm, or noise.
