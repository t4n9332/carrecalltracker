Why are Chrysler Pacifica wheelchair van restraints being recalled?

3 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · Equipment Adaptive/mobility · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-11

All three recalls in this group are about the same part: QRT-Deluxe and QRT-Max wheelchair restraints whose retractors may not lock, leaving the wheelchair unsecured. They come from three different mobility converters rather than from Chrysler. Rollx Vans covers Chrysler Pacifica, Voyager and Town and Country along with Dodge Grand Caravan, Ram Promaster and vehicles from several other makes. Vantage Mobility covers 2021-2025 Chrysler Pacifica alongside Honda Odyssey and Toyota Sienna. Braun covers 2023-2026 Chrysler Pacifica. The Pacifica is the Chrysler model that appears in all three, with Voyager and Town and Country appearing in the Rollx recall. Each notice gives the converter's own customer service number.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. All three describe the same consequence: an unsecured wheelchair can move during transit, increasing the risk of injury. Beyond that, the Rollx, Vantage Mobility and Braun notices say nothing about whether or how to keep using the vehicle before the retractors are inspected, and they give no interim instruction of any kind. They direct owners to the recalling company for the inspection and replacement.

What the free repair involves

The work is the same in all three recalls and is free of charge. Rollx Vans and Braun will each work with Q'Straint to inspect and replace the retractors as necessary. In the Vantage Mobility recall, dealers will inspect and replace the retractors. There is no software update or over-the-air remedy here; owners contact Rollx, Vantage Mobility or Braun directly.

Common questions

My Pacifica was converted by a different company — is it included?

These three recalls name vehicles converted by Rollx Vans, Vantage Mobility and Braun with QRT-Deluxe and QRT-Max restraints. If your converter is not one of those three, your van is not covered by these three notices. Each notice lists the vehicles its own recall covers.

Who pays for the retractor replacement?

All three notices say the inspection and replacement are free of charge. Rollx and Braun are working with Q'Straint, and Vantage Mobility has its dealers do the work. Owner notification letters were mailed on dates each notice gives.

Do the notices say the wheelchair can still be secured before the repair?

The notices state only that the retractors may not lock, preventing the wheelchair from being properly secured, and that an unsecured wheelchair can move during transit. They do not tell owners to stop driving, and they give no instructions for use before the retractors are inspected.
