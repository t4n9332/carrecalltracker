Why might a wheelchair restraint fail to lock in your Chrysler?

3 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · Equipment Adaptive/mobility · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-11

Three separate 2025-2026 recalls affect Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager minivans that were converted for wheelchair access, along with related conversions on Dodge Grand Caravan and RAM Promaster vans in one of them. Each recall targets QRT-Deluxe or QRT-Max wheelchair restraint retractors installed by a different converter — Rollx, Vantage Mobility, and Braun — but describes the same underlying defect: the retractor mechanism may not lock, so a wheelchair cannot be properly secured. Pacifica is named in all three recalls, spanning 2018 through 2025 model years across the group, while Voyager appears in two of them. Two of the three recalls note the converter is working with retractor supplier Q'Straint on the remedy.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Wheelchair restraint retractor does not lock or click into place
  • Wheelchair can shift or move during transit even when the restraint is used
  • The notices describe no dashboard warning — the defect is in the restraint hardware itself, not something the vehicle signals

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls instructs owners to stop driving or to park outside. The notices say only that an unsecured wheelchair can move during transit, which they describe as increasing the risk of injury to the occupant using it. Owners are not told the vehicle itself is unsafe to operate — the concern is limited to whether the restraint holds the wheelchair in place. Follow the notice for your specific recall and converter (Rollx, Vantage Mobility, or Braun) to schedule the retractor inspection and replacement.

What the free repair involves

In all three recalls, the converter arranges for inspection and, if needed, replacement of the wheelchair restraint retractors — Rollx and Braun both say they work with retractor maker Q'Straint on parts, while Vantage Mobility has dealers handle it directly. The repair is free of charge in every case; owners are notified by mail and given a customer service number.

Common questions

Is my Chrysler Pacifica part of more than one of these recalls?

Only if it has a wheelchair conversion. Pacifica appears in all three recalls, but each covers a different converter's retractors (Rollx, Vantage Mobility, or Braun), so check your specific notice to see which one applies.

Do I need to stop using the wheelchair restraint until it's fixed?

The notices don't tell owners to stop driving or stop using the vehicle, but they do describe the retractor as possibly failing to lock, so an owner should follow the notice's inspection and replacement instructions.

Is this a defect in the Chrysler vehicle itself?

No — the recalls are issued by companies that convert these vans for wheelchair access (Rollx, Vantage Mobility, Braun) and cover the QRT-Deluxe/QRT-Max retractor hardware they installed, not the vehicle as built by Chrysler.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
