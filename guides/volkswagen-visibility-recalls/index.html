Volkswagen sunroof and AC drain recalls owners should understand

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Visibility · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 27, 2022

Volkswagen's visibility recalls cover two sunroof issues and an air conditioning drain problem with an airbag consequence. Certain 2018 Tiguan vehicles have a panoramic sunroof ambient light LED module that may short circuit, increasing fire risk. Certain 2022 Golf R A8 and Golf GTI vehicles have an overly sensitive sunroof touch switch that can be inadvertently pressed, causing unintended automatic closure. The Atlas issue affects certain 2021 Atlas and 2020–2021 Atlas Cross Sport vehicles: a blocked AC drain tube can leak water near the airbag control module. The sunroof notices concern different components, while the Atlas notice links water damage to inadvertent airbag deployment.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Water leaking inside near the airbag control module: 2021 VW Atlas AC Drain Tube May Leak Near Airbag Module recall.
  • Unintended automatic sunroof closure after inadvertent switch activation: 2022 VW Golf R Sunroof May Close Unintentionally recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The July 2018 Tiguan sunroof light recall, November 2021 Atlas AC drain tube recall, and July 2022 Golf R sunroof switch recall do not tell owners to stop driving. None includes a park-outside instruction. The Tiguan notice describes a fire risk from an LED module short circuit. The Atlas notice describes possible inadvertent airbag deployment after water damage, and the Golf R notice describes injury risk from unintended sunroof closure. These notices do not give owners a continuing-driving assurance.

What the free repair involves

Repairs are free of charge. For the Tiguan recall, dealers disconnect the panoramic sunroof LED module's power supply until a future service action can safely reactivate it. For Atlas recall 87H4, dealers repair the drain tube and replace the airbag control module as necessary. For recall 60F6, dealers replace the roof module switch.

Common questions

What happens to the Tiguan panoramic sunroof lighting?

The July 2018 notice specifies disconnecting power to the panoramic sunroof LED module, free of charge. It says this remains the remedy until a future service action can safely reactivate the feature; it does not specify a reactivation date.

Why does an Atlas AC drain recall involve the airbag module?

Recall 87H4 says a blocked AC drain tube can leak water near the airbag control module. Water damage may cause inadvertent airbag deployment. Dealers repair the drain tube and replace the airbag control module as necessary, free of charge.

What causes unintended sunroof closure in the Golf R recall?

Recall 60F6 describes a touch switch that may be too sensitive, allowing inadvertent activation and unintended automatic sunroof closure. It covers certain 2022 Golf R A8 and Golf GTI vehicles. Dealers replace the roof module switch free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
