Why Do Some Volkswagen Models Have Sunroof and Fire Recalls?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Visibility · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-07-27

Three unrelated defects sit in this group. The 2018 Tiguan has a panoramic sunroof ambient light bar whose LED module can short circuit, raising a fire risk. Separately, 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport and 2021 Atlas vehicles have an air conditioning drain tube that can become blocked, letting water leak inside the vehicle near the air bag control module and potentially trigger an inadvertent air bag deployment. A third recall covers 2022 Golf R and Golf GTI vehicles whose sunroof touch switch is too sensitive and can be pressed inadvertently, causing the sunroof to close on its own. These are three distinct causes affecting three different models and years.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Sunroof ambient light bar shorting out, which can raise fire risk (2018 Tiguan)
  • Water leaking inside the vehicle near the air bag control module (2020-2021 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport)
  • Sunroof closing on its own when the touch switch is inadvertently pressed (2022 Golf R)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the Tiguan sunroof LED recall, dealers disconnect power to the light module until a future repair can safely reactivate it, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving in the meantime. For the Atlas drain tube recall and the Golf R sunroof switch recall, the notices likewise do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the Tiguan, dealers disconnect the power supply to the panoramic sunroof LED module until a future service action can reactivate it safely. For the Atlas, dealers repair the AC drain tube and replace the air bag control module as necessary. For the Golf R, dealers replace the roof module switch. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Is the Volkswagen Tiguan sunroof recall a fire risk?

Yes. The 2018 Tiguan's panoramic sunroof LED light bar module may short circuit, which the recall notice says can increase the risk of a fire.

How does an AC drain tube affect my Atlas's airbag?

If the drain tube is blocked, water can leak inside near the air bag control module, potentially damaging it and causing an inadvertent air bag deployment, according to the notice.

Why would a Golf R sunroof close by itself?

The 2022 Golf R's sunroof touch switch may be too sensitive and get pressed inadvertently, causing the sunroof to close unintentionally, per the recall notice.
