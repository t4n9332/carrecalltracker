What are the Volkswagen sunroof and interior water leak recalls about?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Visibility · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 27, 2022

These three Volkswagen recalls sit together because each involves the roof or the cabin, but the defects are unrelated. The 2018 recall covers 2018 Tiguan vehicles whose LED module for the panoramic sunroof ambient light bar may short circuit, which the notice says can increase the risk of a fire. The 2021 recall covers 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport vehicles where the air conditioning system drain tube could be blocked, letting water leak inside the vehicle near the air bag control module. The 2022 recall covers 2022 Golf R A8 and Golf GTI vehicles with a sunroof touch switch that may be too sensitive, failing Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 118.

Warning signs to watch for

  • In the 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport recall, the notice describes water leaking inside the vehicle near the air bag control module from a blocked air conditioning drain tube.
  • In the 2022 Golf R and Golf GTI recall, the notice describes the sunroof switch being inadvertently pressed, resulting in unintended auto-closure of the sunroof.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside: not the 2018 Tiguan sunroof LED recall, not the 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport drain tube recall, and not the 2022 Golf R and Golf GTI sunroof switch recall. Each notice states the risk it creates, whether fire, inadvertent air bag deployment, or injury from unintended auto-closure of the sunroof, and each states the dealer remedy, but none sets out an interim instruction for owners in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

For the 2018 Tiguan, dealers disconnect the power supply for the panoramic sunroof LED module until a future service action can safely reactivate the feature. For the 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport, dealers repair the drain tube and replace the air bag control module as necessary. For the 2022 Golf R and Golf GTI, dealers replace the roof module switch. All free of charge.

Common questions

Will my Tiguan sunroof light still work after the recall visit?

The notice says dealers disconnect the power supply for the panoramic sunroof LED module, free of charge, until a future service action can safely reactivate the feature. It does not say when that later service action takes place.

Can a water leak in my Atlas really affect the air bag?

The notice says a blocked air conditioning drain tube could cause water to leak inside near the air bag control module, and that such a leak may damage the module, causing an inadvertent air bag deployment and increasing the risk of injury or a crash.

What is wrong with the Golf R sunroof switch?

The notice says the touch switch for the sunroof may be too sensitive, allowing it to be inadvertently pressed, which can result in unintended auto-closure of the sunroof and an increased risk of injury. Dealers replace the roof module switch free of charge.
