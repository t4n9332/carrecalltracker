Acura Rearview Camera Recalls for Blank or Distorted Screens

3 NHTSA recalls · Acura · Back Over Prevention · model years 2024–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 14, 2026

Loss of the rearview camera image is the recurring problem in these Acura notices. The 2024 ZDX appears in two recalls: the February 2026 notice describes software errors affecting both the instrument panel and rearview camera, while the May 2026 notice describes a distorted or blank rearview image. The 2025 MDX appears in the January 2025 recall because its center console touchscreen may go blank and prevent the camera image from displaying as intended. Although the missing camera view is common to these notices, their remedies differ: radio control module software for the February ZDX recall, a camera replacement for the May ZDX recall, and a display unit replacement for the MDX.

Warning signs to watch for

  • February 26, 2026 ZDX recall: the instrument panel may fail, and the rearview camera may show a blank screen in reverse.
  • May 14, 2026 ZDX recall: the rearview camera screen may display a distorted or blank image.
  • January 23, 2025 MDX recall: the center console touchscreen may go blank, hiding the rearview camera image.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The February 26, 2026 Acura ZDX Instrument Panel and Camera Software Failure notice does not tell owners to stop driving. The May 14, 2026 Acura ZDX Rearview Camera Display Failure notice also does not tell owners to stop driving. The January 23, 2025 Acura MDX Touchscreen May Go Blank, Hiding Camera notice likewise does not tell owners to stop driving. These statements describe the instructions in the supplied notices.

What the free repair involves

For the February 2026 ZDX recall, dealers update the vehicle radio control module software. For the May 2026 ZDX recall, dealers replace the rearview camera. For the January 2025 MDX recall, dealers replace the center information display unit. Each notice states that its remedy is free of charge.

Common questions

Why does the 2024 Acura ZDX have two camera recall notices?

The February 2026 notice describes software errors affecting the instrument panel and rearview camera. The May 2026 notice describes a distorted or blank rearview camera image. Their stated remedies are a radio control module software update and camera replacement, respectively.

Does the Acura MDX camera recall require a new camera?

The January 2025 notice for certain 2025 MDX vehicles specifies replacement of the center information display unit, free of charge. It describes the center console touchscreen going blank and preventing the rearview camera image from displaying as intended.

What does the May 2026 ZDX notice say about repair availability?

The notice specifies a free rearview camera replacement. It says interim safety-risk letters were mailed July 7, 2026, and additional letters would be sent once the remedy became available, anticipated in late 2026. It does not confirm availability for an individual vehicle.
