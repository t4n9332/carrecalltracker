Why do Acura ZDX and MDX rearview cameras go blank?

3 NHTSA recalls · Acura · Back Over Prevention · model years 2024–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-14

Three Acura recalls in this group all involve displays or the rearview camera, not backup sensors or cross-traffic warning systems. The 2024 ZDX has two separate camera-related recalls: a February 2026 recall for a software error that can blank the instrument panel and also blank the rearview camera image while in reverse, and a May 2026 recall for a rearview camera screen that shows a distorted or blank image, whose camera-replacement fix was still pending at the time of the interim notice. Separately, a 2025 MDX recall involves the center touchscreen going blank, which also hides the rearview camera image. No model years outside 2024 ZDX and 2025 MDX appear in this group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Instrument panel display going blank (2024 ZDX)
  • Rearview camera image blank or not displaying when backing up
  • Rearview camera screen showing a distorted image
  • Center touchscreen going blank, hiding the rearview camera (2025 MDX)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the three recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the ZDX instrument panel and camera software failure recall, the ZDX rearview camera display failure recall, and the MDX touchscreen recall, the notices describe the defect and its safety risk but do not instruct owners to stop driving, avoid highway speeds, or park away from structures while a repair is scheduled.

What the free repair involves

Dealers update software for the ZDX instrument panel and camera issue, replace the rearview camera itself for the second ZDX recall once the part becomes available, and replace the center information display unit for the MDX touchscreen recall. All repairs are done at no cost to the owner, per the notices.

Common questions

Are the two Acura ZDX camera recalls the same issue?

No. The February 2026 recall involves a software error affecting the instrument panel and rearview camera, fixed with a software update. The May 2026 recall is a separate hardware issue where the camera itself shows a distorted or blank image and needs replacement.

Is a fix available yet for the ZDX rearview camera recall?

As of the interim notice mailed July 2026, the replacement camera part was not yet available. Honda said additional letters with remedy details would follow once the fix was ready, anticipated in late 2026.

Does the MDX touchscreen recall affect anything besides the camera?

The notice describes the center console touchscreen going blank, which in turn keeps the rearview camera image from displaying. Dealers replace the center information display unit to fix it.
