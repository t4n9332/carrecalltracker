Why does the rearview camera on your Acura go blank?

3 NHTSA recalls · Acura · Back Over Prevention · model years 2024–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 14, 2026

All three of these Acura recalls involve a screen going dark rather than a mechanical failure, and two of them land on the same vehicle. The 2024 Acura ZDX appears twice: a February 2026 recall covering a software error that can blank the instrument panel display and leave the rearview camera showing a blank screen in reverse, and a May 2026 recall covering a rearview camera screen that may show a distorted or blank image. The third, from January 2025, covers the 2025 Acura MDX, where the center console touchscreen may go blank so the rearview camera image does not display. Honda cites Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 111 on rear visibility in the ZDX software recall and the MDX recall.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A blank instrument panel display - named in the February 2026 recall of the 2024 Acura ZDX.
  • A blank rearview camera screen when the vehicle is in reverse - also from that February 2026 ZDX recall.
  • A distorted or blank rearview camera image - named in the May 2026 recall of the 2024 Acura ZDX.
  • A center console touchscreen that goes blank, leaving no rearview camera image - named in the January 2025 recall of the 2025 Acura MDX.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park outside. For the May 2026 recall of the 2024 Acura ZDX, the notice says interim letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed July 7, 2026, and that additional letters will follow once the remedy is available, anticipated in late 2026. The February 2026 ZDX recall and the January 2025 MDX recall describe the risk of a crash or injury from lost display or camera images, but neither notice gives owners any instruction about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

The repairs differ by recall and each is free of charge. For the February 2026 recall of the 2024 Acura ZDX, dealers update the vehicle radio control module software. For the May 2026 ZDX recall, dealers replace the rearview camera. For the January 2025 recall of the 2025 Acura MDX, dealers replace the center information display unit. None of these notices mentions an over-the-air update.

Common questions

Is my 2024 Acura ZDX covered by both camera recalls?

Both the February 2026 and May 2026 recalls list the 2024 Acura ZDX, and the two remedies are different. Honda's customer service line is 1-888-234-2138 for both, so check your VIN against each recall rather than assuming one covers the other.

Why is the May 2026 ZDX camera repair not available yet?

That notice says interim letters explaining the safety risk were mailed July 7, 2026, and that additional letters will be sent once the remedy - replacing the rearview camera - is available, which Honda anticipates in late 2026.

Is the 2025 Acura MDX fix a software update?

For the 2025 MDX recall, the notice says dealers will replace the center information display unit free of charge, not reprogram it. The software update in this group belongs to the February 2026 recall of the 2024 ZDX.
