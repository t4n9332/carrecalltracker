Why Do Mitsubishi Outlander Recalls Keep Citing the Backup Camera?

4 NHTSA recalls · Mitsubishi · Back Over Prevention · model years 2022–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-25

All four recalls in this group share the same root cause: a software error in the Outlander's Alliance In-Vehicle Infotainment (A-IVI) system that stops the backup camera image from displaying in reverse. The defect keeps resurfacing across model years — first on 2022-2023 Outlander and Outlander PHEV vehicles, then again on 2022-2024 Outlander and 2023-2025 Outlander PHEV vehicles after the earlier fix proved incomplete, and most recently on 2025-2026 Outlander vehicles. Each later recall explicitly replaces or expands an earlier NHTSA recall number, and all four cite noncompliance with FMVSS 111, "Rear Visibility."

Warning signs to watch for

  • Backup camera image not appearing on the display when shifted into reverse
  • Backup camera image freezing instead of updating (2025-06-02 recall)
  • Backup camera image vanishing after previously displaying (2025-06-02 recall)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls — the 2023-06-06, 2022-07-08, 2025-06-02, or 2026-08-25 Outlander backup camera recalls — carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For all four, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Each simply describes a rearview image that may fail to display, freeze, or not appear when the vehicle is shifted into reverse, which fails to meet the federal rear-visibility standard, but no notice restricts continued driving or requires parking away from structures.

What the free repair involves

Across all four recalls, dealers update the A-IVI infotainment software, free of charge, to restore the backup camera image. No hardware is replaced and no over-the-air update is used — each fix requires a dealer visit. Owners receive notification letters advising them to schedule the free software update.

Common questions

Why does my Outlander need another camera recall after a previous fix?

Mitsubishi's software fixes for the A-IVI infotainment system have not fully resolved the rearview camera display problem; the 2025 recall replaced and expanded the 2023 recall, and some previously repaired vehicles needed the newer update too.

Is it illegal to drive with a broken backup camera under these recalls?

The notices cite noncompliance with FMVSS 111, the federal rear-visibility standard, but they do not instruct owners to stop driving. Dealers fix the underlying A-IVI software defect free of charge.

Does the backup camera recall affect the Outlander PHEV too?

Yes. The 2023-06-06 and 2025-06-02 recalls both list the Outlander PHEV (2023 and 2023/2025 model years) alongside the standard Outlander, tied to the same A-IVI software error.
