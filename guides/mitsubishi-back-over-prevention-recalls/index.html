Why does the Mitsubishi Outlander backup camera keep getting recalled?

4 NHTSA recalls · Mitsubishi · Back Over Prevention · model years 2022–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 25, 2026

All four recalls here are the same failure on the same vehicle line: the rearview camera image on the Mitsubishi Outlander. In each case the vehicles are said to fail Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 111, Rear Visibility. The 2022 Outlander recall names a rearview camera software error; the others name a software problem in the Alliance In-Vehicle Infotainment (A-IVI) system, covering 2022-2023 Outlander and 2023 Outlander PHEV, then 2022-2024 Outlander and 2023-2025 Outlander PHEV, and finally 2025-2026 Outlander and 2026 Outlander PHEV. The notices state that the two earliest recalls were replaced by 25V369, and that the 2025 recall replaces and expands 23V345.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The rearview image does not appear on the display — stated in the 2022 Outlander recall and in the 2022-2023 Outlander and 2023 Outlander PHEV recall.
  • The rearview camera image may freeze, or not appear — stated in the 2022-2024 Outlander and 2023-2025 Outlander PHEV recall.
  • The A-IVI system may fail to display the rearview camera image when the vehicle is placed in reverse — stated in the 2025-2026 Outlander and 2026 Outlander PHEV recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the four Outlander camera notices tells owners to stop driving, and none carries a park-outside instruction. That applies to the 2022 Outlander recall, the 2022-2023 Outlander and 2023 Outlander PHEV recall, the 2022-2024 Outlander and 2023-2025 Outlander PHEV recall, and the 2025-2026 Outlander and 2026 Outlander PHEV recall. What the notices do say is that a rearview image that does not display properly reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. None of them gives owners an interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Every remedy here is a software update done at the dealer, free of charge. For the 2022 Outlander recall, dealers update the rearview camera software. For the other three, dealers update the A-IVI software. The notices do not describe these as over-the-air updates; they say dealers perform the update.

Common questions

My Outlander already had this software updated — is it covered again?

The notices say the 2025 recall replaces and expands 23V345, and that the 2022 and 2023 recalls were replaced by 25V369. They do not confirm any individual vehicle's status, so check your VIN with Mitsubishi or NHTSA and read the owner letter.

Which Outlander model years are involved?

The four notices name the 2022 Outlander; 2022-2023 Outlander with 2023 Outlander PHEV; 2022-2024 Outlander with 2023-2025 Outlander PHEV; and 2025-2026 Outlander with 2026 Outlander PHEV. Three of the four include Outlander PHEV models alongside the gasoline Outlander.

Will I be charged for the camera fix?

Each notice says dealers will update the software free of charge. Owner letters were mailed August 1, 2022 and June 20, 2023; for the two newer recalls, notifications were expected June 30, 2025 and September 24, 2026.
