Mitsubishi Outlander Backup Camera Recalls Address Missing or Frozen Images

4 NHTSA recalls · Mitsubishi · Back Over Prevention · model years 2022–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 25, 2026

Missing rearview camera images are the recurring problem in these Mitsubishi Outlander notices, with software identified in each. The July 2022 notice covers certain 2022 Outlanders, and the June 2023 notice covers certain 2022-2023 Outlanders and 2023 Outlander PHEVs. The June 2025 notice covers certain 2022-2024 Outlanders and 2023-2025 Outlander PHEVs, adding an image that may freeze as well as fail to appear. The August 2026 notice covers certain 2025-2026 Outlanders and 2026 Outlander PHEVs whose A-IVI system may fail to display the image in reverse. The 2022-2023 Outlander model years appear across the most notices listed here.

Warning signs to watch for

  • July 2022 recall SR-22-003: the rearview camera may not display an image.
  • June 2023 recall SR-23-001: the backup camera image may not appear on the display.
  • June 2025 recall SR-25-001: the rearview camera image may freeze or fail to appear.
  • August 2026 recall SR-26-002: the rearview camera image may not display when the vehicle is placed in reverse.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The July 2022, June 2023, June 2025, and August 2026 Outlander camera notices do not tell owners to stop driving. None gives a park-outside instruction. Each identifies reduced rear visibility and an increased crash risk when the rearview image does not display properly; the June 2023 notice also identifies injury risk. The notices describe dealer software remedies, but they do not state that an affected Outlander or Outlander PHEV is safe to drive while awaiting that remedy.

What the free repair involves

The July 2022 notice specifies a dealer update to the rearview camera software. The June 2023, June 2025, and August 2026 notices each specify a dealer update to the A-IVI software. Each listed notice states that its software remedy is free of charge.

Common questions

Which Outlander camera recall includes a frozen rearview image?

The June 2025 notice, SR-25-001, says the rearview image may freeze or fail to appear because of an A-IVI software error. It covers certain 2022-2024 Outlanders and 2023-2025 Outlander PHEVs. Dealers update the A-IVI software free of charge.

What do these notices say about earlier camera recalls?

The July 2022 and June 2023 notices state that they were replaced by NHTSA recall 25V369. The June 2025 notice states that it replaces and expands recall 23V345. Its listed remedy is a free A-IVI software update.

What does the camera recall for 2025-2026 Outlanders cover?

The August 2026 notice, SR-26-002, covers certain 2025-2026 Outlanders and 2026 Outlander PHEVs. It says improper A-IVI software programming may prevent the rearview image from displaying when reverse is selected. Dealers update the A-IVI software free of charge.
