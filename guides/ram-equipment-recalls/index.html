Why Are Ram Vehicles Recalled For Fire Extinguishers And Equipment?

4 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Equipment · model years 2018–2018 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2019-11-15

These four recalls cover unrelated equipment on Ram vehicles rather than a single repeated defect. A 2017 recall affects Kidde fire extinguishers on 2018 Chrysler Pacifica and Ram 2500-5500 vehicles that can clog or lose a nozzle. A 2019 recall covers 2015-2018 Ram ProMaster vans with 3.0L diesel or 3.6L gas engines and air conditioning, where the engine cooling fan can seize and overheat. Another 2019 recall covers 2018 Ram ProMaster vans, shared with other manufacturers' vans, where the back-up camera monitor can revert to factory settings and show a reversed image. A 2018 recall covers 2018 Ram ProMaster City vans with a tire placard listing the wrong occupant capacity.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A fire extinguisher that feels clogged, needs excessive force to activate, or has a nozzle that has detached (2018 Pacifica and Ram 2500-5500)
  • The notices describe no specific warning sign before the ProMaster cooling fan seizes and overheats
  • A back-up camera image that appears reversed, showing the wrong direction to turn (2018 ProMaster)
  • A tire placard on a 2018 ProMaster City that lists more occupants than the vehicle is rated for

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recall notices tell Ram owners to stop driving or park outside. The fire extinguisher, cooling fan, back-up camera, and tire placard recalls each describe only the defect and the free repair, without any instruction to stop driving. The cooling fan recall, which can lead to an overheated fan motor, still does not carry a stop-driving or park-outside warning in the notice, even though FCA sent an interim letter before the remedy was available.

What the free repair involves

Repairs vary by recall: Kidde replaces the fire extinguisher, dealers replace the ProMaster's engine cooling fan module, Unicell and ASA Electronics work with owners to fix the reversed camera display, and dealers install a corrected tire placard on the ProMaster City. All of these repairs or replacements are provided free of charge.

Common questions

Is my Ram's fire extinguisher actually dangerous?

The recall says affected Kidde extinguishers on 2018 Pacifica and Ram 2500-5500 vehicles may clog and fail to discharge, or the nozzle may detach with enough force to cause injury. Contact Kidde for a free replacement extinguisher rather than waiting for a problem to occur.

Why would a cooling fan recall increase fire risk?

In the 2015-2018 ProMaster recall, the engine cooling fan can seize, which the notice says can cause the fan motor itself to overheat. An overheated motor increases fire risk, so FCA sent an interim warning letter before the replacement fan module was ready.

Is the reversed back-up camera recall specific to Ram?

No. It covers the same ASA Electronics Voyager monitor used across several 2017-2019 vans, including the 2018 Ram ProMaster and 2019 Ford Econoline, where the monitor can revert to factory settings and show a mirror-reversed camera image.
