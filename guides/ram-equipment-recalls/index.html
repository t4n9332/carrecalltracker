Ram equipment recalls: fire extinguishers, cooling fans and tire placards

4 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Equipment · model years 2018–2018 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 15, 2019

Only four recalls sit in Ram's equipment group, and three of them involve the ProMaster or ProMaster City rather than the pickups. Two carry a fire or injury risk: Kidde plastic-handle and push-button 'Pindicator' fire extinguishers fitted to 2018 Ram 2500, 3500, 4500 and 5500 vehicles and the 2018 Chrysler Pacifica may clog or shed their nozzle, and the engine cooling fan on 2015-2018 Ram ProMaster vans with 3.0L diesel or 3.6L gasoline engines and air conditioning may seize and overheat the fan motor. The other two are compliance items: an ASA Electronics Voyager monitor on 2018 ProMaster-based vehicles that may revert to factory defaults and show a reversed camera image, and a tire placard on the 2018 ProMaster City that may list the wrong occupant count.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A fire extinguisher that will not discharge as expected or requires excessive force to activate, and a nozzle that may detach from the valve assembly — the Kidde extinguisher recall.
  • A reversed backup camera image on the ASA Electronics Voyager monitor — the Unicell recall covering 2018 Ram ProMaster-based vehicles.
  • A tire placard showing an incorrect total number of occupants allowed — the 2018 Ram ProMaster City recall U17.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The extinguisher notice says an extinguisher that does not function properly can increase the risk of injury in a fire, and that on certain models the nozzle may detach with enough force to cause injury. The ProMaster cooling fan notice says interim letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed December 10, 2019, with a second notice when the remedy became available. The notices give no other instruction about driving.

What the free repair involves

The extinguisher recall tells owners to contact Kidde for a replacement fire extinguisher, free of charge. On the ProMaster van, dealers replace the engine cooling fan module, free of charge. For the reversed camera image, Unicell works with ASA Electronics to notify owners and offer a remedy, free of charge. On the ProMaster City, dealers install a corrected tire placard, free of charge.

Common questions

How do I get the recalled fire extinguisher replaced?

Chrysler's notice instructs owners to contact Kidde for a replacement fire extinguisher, free of charge. The affected units are Kidde plastic-handle or push-button 'Pindicator' extinguishers fitted to 2018 Ram 2500, 3500, 4500 and 5500 vehicles and the 2018 Chrysler Pacifica.

What is wrong with the 2018 ProMaster City tire placard?

The placard may indicate an incorrect total number of occupants allowed. The notice says an incorrect seating capacity may lead the operator to overload the vehicle, which can cause tire failure and increase the risk of a crash. Dealers install a corrected placard free of charge.

Should I stop driving my ProMaster because of the cooling fan recall?

That notice does not tell owners to stop driving. It says the engine cooling fan may seize, which can cause the cooling fan motor to overheat and increase the risk of a fire, and that dealers replace the engine cooling fan module free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
