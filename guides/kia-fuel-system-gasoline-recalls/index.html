Why do some Kia fuel systems leak or lose power?

7 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-10

Kia's gasoline fuel system recalls fall into two repeating patterns. Fuel can leak at a pipe connection or from a damaged component in several models: the 2022 Carnival (and again in a 2022-2025 Carnival recall) at the pipe-to-fuel-rail connection, the 2021 Sorento and 2021-2022 K5 at the fuel pump-to-rail joint, the 2021-2024 K5 from a melted fuel tank, and the 2018 Sportage from pipe damage left over from an earlier engine-replacement repair. Separately, the Stinger has had two fuel pump defects — a manufacturing burr in the 2020-2021 models and a sticking valve plunger in the 2018-2021 models — that starve the engine of fuel and cause a loss of drive power rather than a leak.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A fuel odor or a visible fuel leak near the fuel lines, tank, or engine (Carnival, K5, Sorento, and Sportage recalls)
  • A sudden loss of engine power or reduced acceleration while driving (Stinger fuel pump recalls)
  • The notices do not describe a dashboard warning light or other advance sign for any of these defects

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the fuel leak recalls affecting the Carnival, K5, Sorento, and Sportage, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The same is true for the two Stinger recalls involving fuel pump defects that can cause a loss of drive power. The notices describe an increased risk of fire (leak recalls) or crash (Stinger power-loss recalls), but do not direct owners to stop driving or park away from structures in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the affected component and replace it when necessary: the fuel rail tube, the fuel pipe connection, the check valve and fuel tank, or the fuel pump jet nozzle housing. The 2023 Stinger recall also includes an engine control unit software update. All inspections and part replacements are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Kia models are affected by fuel leak recalls?

Kia's fuel leak recalls cover the 2022 Carnival (and a separate 2022-2025 Carnival recall), the 2021 Sorento and 2021-2022 K5, the 2021-2024 K5 for a melted fuel tank, and the 2018 Sportage for damaged fuel pipe connections.

Why do some Kia Stinger recalls cause a loss of power instead of a leak?

Two separate Stinger recalls (2020-2021 and 2018-2021) involve fuel pump defects — a manufacturing burr and a sticking valve plunger — that restrict fuel reaching the engine, causing a loss of drive power rather than a fuel leak.

Do I need to stop driving because of a Kia fuel system recall?

None of these seven fuel system recalls include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction from Kia. The notices describe fire or crash risks but do not direct owners to halt use of the vehicle before the free repair.
