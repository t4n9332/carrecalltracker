What Kia fuel system recalls mean for leaks and lost power

7 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 10, 2026

Two patterns dominate Kia's gasoline fuel system recalls: leaks around fuel rail and pipe connections, and high pressure fuel pump problems on the Stinger. Leaks account for four recalls — plastic fuel rail tubes on 2022 Carnival (SC211), the pump-to-rail pipe connection on 2021 Sorento and 2021-2022 K5 with 2.5L turbocharged engines (SC214), the fuel pipe-to-rail connection on 2022-2026 Carnival (SC368), and the high pressure fuel pipe on 2011-2018 Sportage, 2011-2017 Optima and 2012-2017 Sorento that previously had an engine replaced (SC172). A 2025 recall covers 2021-2024 K5 vehicles whose fuel tank can melt after a check valve fault. The Stinger appears twice, 2020-2021 (SC198) and 2018-2021 (SC281), both ending in lost drive power.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Fuel may leak at the pipe connection between the high pressure fuel pump and fuel rail — the 2021 Sorento and 2021-2022 K5 recall (SC214).
  • Fuel may leak at the fuel pipe and fuel rail connection — the 2022-2026 Carnival recall (SC368).
  • A melted fuel tank can leak — the 2021-2024 K5 recall.
  • A loss of drive power — the 2018-2021 Stinger fuel pump recall (SC281) and the 2020-2021 Stinger jet nozzle recall (SC198).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices carries a stop-driving instruction, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. That includes the four leak recalls — SC211, SC214, SC368 and SC172 — and the 2021-2024 K5 fuel tank recall, all of which Kia describes in terms of an increased risk of fire, and the two Stinger fuel pump recalls, SC198 and SC281, which Kia describes as a loss of drive power increasing the risk of a crash. The notices give owners no interim instruction beyond having the recall repair performed.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and tighten or replace the fuel pipe (SC214, SC368), inspect and replace the fuel rail (SC211), inspect and replace the fuel pipe (SC172), replace the check valve and replace the fuel tank if damaged (2021-2024 K5), and replace the jet nozzle housing assembly if necessary (SC198). SC281 adds an engine control unit software update plus fuel pump replacement as needed. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Which Kia models appear more than once in these fuel recalls?

The K5 appears in SC214 (2021-2022 with 2.5L turbocharged engines) and in the 2021-2024 fuel tank recall. The Carnival appears in SC211 (2022) and SC368 (2022-2026). The Stinger appears in SC198 (2020-2021) and SC281 (2018-2021).

Do any of these notices tell owners to stop driving because of the fire risk?

None of the fuel leak or fuel tank notices includes a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. They state that a fuel leak, or a melted fuel tank that leaks, increases the risk of fire, and they direct owners to have the free repair performed by a dealer.

My Sportage already had its engine replaced — why is it in a fuel recall?

SC172 covers 2011-2018 Sportage, 2011-2017 Optima and 2012-2017 Sorento vehicles that previously received an engine replacement under recall 17V-224, warranty, or the Knock Sensor Detection System campaign. The notice says the high pressure fuel pipe may have been damaged, misaligned or improperly torqued during that procedure.
