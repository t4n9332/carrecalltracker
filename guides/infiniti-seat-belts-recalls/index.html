Infiniti Seat Belt Recalls Involve Loose Anchors and Locking Problems

3 NHTSA recalls · Infiniti · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 5, 2024

These Infiniti seat belt recalls concern three different restraint components, with no single model recurring in the supplied notices. The 2018 QX30 has a right-hand lower anchorage bolt that may have been incorrectly installed, allowing the anchorage to detach in a crash. The 2020 Q60 has rear seat belt assemblies whose webbing sensor locking mechanism may not lock as intended, affecting restraint during a crash involving multiple impacts. The 2024 QX60 has a left-side front lap belt pretensioner that may be unsecured because of a missing rivet. The recurring concern is inadequate occupant restraint during a crash, rather than one shared defect across these models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The notices for the 2018 QX30 anchorage recall, 2020 Q60 rear seat belt locking recall, and 2024 QX60 lap belt pretensioner recall do not tell owners to stop driving. None includes a park-outside instruction. Their stated risks concern crash protection: an anchorage may detach, a rear belt may not properly restrain someone during multiple impacts, or an unsecured pretensioner may not properly restrain an occupant. The notices do not describe an owner-observable warning sign for any of these defects.

What the free repair involves

For the QX30, dealers weld in a new retaining plate and secure the anchorage with a new anchor bolt. For the Q60, dealers inspect rear belt assembly production dates and replace assemblies as necessary. For the QX60, dealers inspect and replace left-side front lap belt pretensioner assemblies as necessary. These remedies are free of charge.

Common questions

Which seat belt is affected in the 2024 Infiniti QX60?

The notice identifies the left-side front seat belt assembly. A missing rivet may leave its lap belt pretensioner improperly secured. Dealers inspect and replace the left-side front lap seat belt pretensioner assemblies as necessary, free of charge.

What is wrong with the 2020 Infiniti Q60 rear seat belts?

The recall concerns rear seat belt assemblies with a dual-mode locking mechanism. Their webbing sensor locking mechanism may not lock as intended, potentially reducing restraint during a crash involving multiple impacts. Dealers inspect production dates and replace assemblies as necessary.

How is the 2018 Infiniti QX30 seat belt anchorage repaired?

The specified repair adds a welded retaining plate and a new anchor bolt to secure the seat belt anchorage, free of charge. The notice states that all vehicles covered by this recall were repaired by July 17, 2018.
