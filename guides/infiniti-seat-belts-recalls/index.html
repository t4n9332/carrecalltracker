Infiniti seat belt recalls: anchorage, locking and pretensioner problems

3 NHTSA recalls · Infiniti · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 5, 2024

Three Nissan North America recalls cover Infiniti seat belts, and two of them come down to a fastener that was not properly secured. On the 2018 Infiniti QX30, the bolt holding the right-hand lower seat belt anchorage was incorrectly installed, so the anchorage can detach in a crash. On the 2024 Infiniti QX60, shared with the 2024 Nissan Pathfinder, a missing rivet leaves the left-side front lap belt pretensioner improperly secured. The third, on the 2020 Infiniti Q60, is different: rear seat belt assemblies with a dual-mode locking mechanism may not lock as intended. The QX30 and QX60 recalls are also noncompliance recalls, citing Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards 208, 209 and 210.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. The 2018 Infiniti QX30 anchorage notice, the 2020 Infiniti Q60 rear seat belt locking notice and the 2024 Infiniti QX60 lap belt pretensioner notice each describe an increased risk of injury in a crash, and each points owners to a dealer repair rather than to any interim instruction. No notice in this group sets out anything for owners to do differently in the meantime, and none offers a temporary measure.

What the free repair involves

On the 2018 QX30, dealers welded in a new retaining plate and secured the seat belt anchorage with a new anchor bolt. On the 2020 Q60, dealers inspect rear seat belt assembly production dates and replace assemblies as necessary. On the 2024 QX60, dealers inspect and replace the left-side front lap belt pretensioner assembly as necessary. All three notices say the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Does my 2018 Infiniti QX30 still need the seat belt anchorage repair?

The notice says all affected 2018 QX30 vehicles were repaired on July 17, 2018. If you are unsure whether yours was among them, check your VIN with Infiniti customer service at 1-800-662-6200 or on NHTSA's recall lookup.

Which seat belt is affected on the 2024 Infiniti QX60?

The notice names the left-side front seat belt assembly, where the lap belt pretensioner may be improperly secured because a rivet is missing. Dealers inspect and replace that pretensioner assembly as necessary, free of charge. The same recall covers the 2024 Nissan Pathfinder.

Will every 2020 Infiniti Q60 rear seat belt be replaced?

The notice says dealers inspect the rear seat belt assembly production dates and replace the assemblies as necessary, free of charge, so replacement depends on what the inspection finds. The recall began May 4, 2020; Nissan customer service is 1-800-867-7669.
