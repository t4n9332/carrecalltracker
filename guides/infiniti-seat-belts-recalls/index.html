Why are some Infiniti seat belts being recalled?

3 NHTSA recalls · Infiniti · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-03-05

Three separate Infiniti seat belt recalls, each affecting a different part of the restraint system. The 2018 QX30 recall involves a bolt that may have been incorrectly installed, letting the right-hand lower seat belt anchorage detach in a crash. The 2020 Q60 recall covers rear seat belts with a dual-mode locking mechanism whose webbing sensor may not lock as intended during a crash involving multiple impacts. The 2024 QX60 recall, shared with the Nissan Pathfinder, involves the left-side front lap belt pretensioner, which may be improperly secured because of a missing rivet. In each case the concern is that the seat belt may not properly restrain the occupant.

Warning signs to watch for

  • None of the three notices describe a warning sign an owner could notice before a crash; the anchorage, locking mechanism, or pretensioner defect is not something a driver would detect during normal use

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the three Infiniti seat belt recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2018 QX30 anchorage recall, the 2020 Q60 rear seat belt recall, and the 2024 QX60 pretensioner recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice states only that the defect can increase the risk of injury if a crash occurs, because the seat belt may not properly restrain the occupant in that event.

What the free repair involves

Dealers weld in a new retaining plate and anchor bolt for the 2018 QX30 recall, inspect and replace rear seat belt assemblies as necessary for the 2020 Q60 recall, and inspect and replace the left-side front lap belt pretensioner as necessary for the 2024 QX60 recall. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Will my Infiniti's seat belt recall repair cost anything?

No. Dealers perform the anchorage weld repair, the rear seat belt assembly replacement, or the pretensioner inspection and replacement free of charge, depending on which of the three recalls applies to your vehicle.

How would I know if my seat belt has this defect?

You likely wouldn't. None of the three notices describe a symptom you could notice during normal driving; the anchorage bolt, locking mechanism, or pretensioner issue only becomes apparent in the event of a crash.

Which Infiniti models are covered by these seat belt recalls?

The 2018 QX30, the 2020 Q60, and the 2024 QX60 (shared with the Nissan Pathfinder) are each covered by a separate recall addressing a different seat belt component.
