Ford Electrical System Recalls Involve Battery Fires and Cable Problems

40 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Electrical System · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 6, 2026

The supplied Ford notices repeatedly address high voltage battery failures and electrical connections that can create fire risks. Escape and Lincoln Corsair plug-in hybrids recur in the December 2024, November 2025, and February 2026 notices, with coverage extending through certain 2026 vehicles in the last notice. Separate battery recalls cover 2019–2020 Fusion PHEVs and 2020–2022 Explorer/Aviator PHEVs. F-150 notices focus on a battery terminal connection in 2019–2020 trucks and a starter cable attachment nut in certain 2020 trucks. Other notices address Explorer harness contact, Edge/Flex and Lincoln power cables, block heater overheating, Mustang gear position display failures, and Taurus ignition key removal outside Park.

Warning signs to watch for

  • November 2019 F-150 battery cable fastener recall: instrument panel displays and braking or steering assist may be affected, and the engine could stall.
  • December 2017 Mustang cluster recall: the PRNDL display may not illuminate and the "Key in Ignition" chime may not sound under the conditions described.
  • March 2018 Taurus recall: the ignition key may be removable when the transmission is not in Park.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the supplied notices instructs owners to stop driving or park outside. The June 2023 Fusion PHEV notice advises against charging until the remedy is completed. The December 2024 Escape/Corsair notice separately advises against charging until its remedy is completed. The November 2025 and February 2026 Escape/Corsair notices advise using only "Auto EV" mode and say mailed instructions will limit maximum battery charge until the remedy is completed. The remaining supplied notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies vary by notice: cable inspections and corrections, harness restraint, starter nut replacement, cluster software updates, and Taurus shifter replacement. Battery notices specify different combinations of diagnostics, battery replacement, and control module updates. The May 2025 block heater notice specifies element replacement and cord inspection, with replacement if necessary, and also describes an alternative blanking plug option.

Common questions

Do the Escape and Corsair battery recalls give the same charging instructions?

The December 2024 notice advises against charging until its remedy is completed. The November 2025 and February 2026 notices instead specify using only "Auto EV" mode and say owners will receive mailed instructions limiting maximum battery charge until the remedy is completed.

What does the Fusion plug-in hybrid battery recall offer?

The June 2023 notice advises owners not to charge until the remedy is completed. It specifies battery replacement and a BECM software update for free, or vehicle repurchase by Ford. The supplied excerpt does not provide complete terms for its additional repair option.

Which F-150 electrical connections are covered by these notices?

The November 2019 notice covers a potentially loose positive battery terminal connection in certain 2019–2020 F-150s, with inspection and joint correction as necessary. The August 2020 notice covers an incorrect starter motor cable attachment nut in certain 2020 F-150s, requiring free nut replacement.
