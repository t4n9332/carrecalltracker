Which Nissan engines are recalled for bearing failure and stalling?

3 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 12, 2026

Two of these three recalls concern the same family of engines. Nissan's variable compression turbo engines appear first in a recall covering 2021-2024 Rogue, 2019-2020 Altima, 2019-2022 Infiniti QX50 and 2022 QX55 with 1.5L three-cylinder or 2.0L four-cylinder VC-Turbo engines, where the engine bearings may have manufacturing defects that lead to engine failure. A second recall covers 2023-2025 Rogue with the 1.5L VC-Turbo, where increased temperatures can break down the engine oil and cause the bearings to seize. Rogue is the model that carries through both. The remaining recall is unrelated: on 2018-2019 Rogue Hybrid, contact with the ECM bracket may damage the engine harness and blow a fuse.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine failure (2019-2024 VC-Turbo engine bearing recall and 2023-2025 Rogue 1.5L VC-Turbo recall)
  • Loss of drive power (2023-2025 Rogue 1.5L VC-Turbo recall)
  • Engine stall (2018-2019 Rogue Hybrid engine harness recall)

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park the vehicle outside. The two VC-Turbo bearing notices state that engine failure increases the risk of a crash, and that a bearing failure may cause a breach in the engine block allowing hot oil to be discharged, increasing the risk of an engine fire; neither gives an interim instruction. The Rogue Hybrid notice states that a blown fuse may cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash, and it likewise does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For both VC-Turbo bearing recalls, dealers reprogram the engine control module software. Under the 2023-2025 Rogue recall they also inspect for diagnostic trouble codes and test drive the vehicle, may inspect the oil pan for metal debris, and replace the engine if necessary. For the 2018-2019 Rogue Hybrid, dealers install a harness protector cover. All of this is free of charge.

Common questions

Does the recall replace my engine?

Only under one recall, and only if needed. The 2023-2025 Rogue notice says dealers reprogram the ECM, inspect for diagnostic trouble codes and test drive the vehicle, may inspect the oil pan for metal debris, and replace the engine if necessary, free of charge.

Which engines are actually covered?

The bearing recalls cover 1.5L three-cylinder and 2.0L four-cylinder VC-Turbo engines in the 2021-2024 Rogue, 2019-2020 Altima, 2019-2022 QX50 and 2022 QX55, and 1.5L VC-Turbo engines in the 2023-2025 Rogue. The Rogue Hybrid recall involves engine harness damage.

My Rogue appears more than once. Is that possible?

Yes. Rogue appears in all three notices, covering different model years and different problems: 2021-2024 and 2023-2025 for VC-Turbo bearings, and 2018-2019 Rogue Hybrid for harness damage. A VIN check shows which of them apply to your vehicle.
