Why Are Nissan Engines Failing Across Multiple Rogue and Altima Models?

3 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-02-12

Two of the three recalls in this group trace back to the same variable compression turbo (VC-Turbo) engine used in the Rogue, Altima, and Infiniti QX50/QX55. A 2025 recall covers bearing manufacturing defects in 2019-2022 Altima, 2021-2024 Rogue, 2019-2022 QX50, and 2022 QX55 vehicles, while a 2026 recall covers 2023-2025 Rogue engines where high heat breaks down the oil and seizes the bearings. Either failure mode can stop the engine and, if a bearing breaches the block, let hot oil escape and raise fire risk. The third recall is unrelated: 2018-2019 Rogue Hybrid vehicles where the engine harness can be damaged by contact with the ECM bracket, blowing a fuse and stalling the engine.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine failure or loss of power (VC-Turbo bearing recalls)
  • Oil leaking from the engine, which can indicate a bearing breach
  • Engine stalls unexpectedly (2018-2019 Rogue Hybrid wiring recall)
  • The notices do not describe a specific warning sign before bearing failure occurs

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the three recalls in this group — the 2025 and 2026 VC-Turbo bearing recalls or the 2021 Rogue Hybrid wiring recall — carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the bearing recalls warn that engine failure increases the risk of a crash and that a bearing breach can release hot oil and raise fire risk, while the wiring recall warns that a blown fuse can stall the engine and increase crash risk. In each case, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving the vehicle.

What the free repair involves

Dealers address the VC-Turbo bearing recalls by reprogramming the engine control module software, and for the 2026 recall also inspecting for trouble codes, test-driving the vehicle, checking the oil pan for metal debris, and replacing the engine if needed. For the Rogue Hybrid recall, dealers install a harness protector cover. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Nissan and Infiniti models are affected by the engine bearing recalls?

The 2025 recall covers 2019-2022 Altima, 2021-2024 Rogue, 2019-2022 Infiniti QX50, and 2022 QX55 vehicles with VC-Turbo engines. The 2026 recall covers 2023-2025 Rogue vehicles with the 1.5L VC-Turbo engine. Both involve bearings that can lead to engine failure.

Is it safe to keep driving with an open engine bearing recall?

The notices do not tell owners to stop driving for any of these recalls. They warn that engine failure raises crash risk, and for the bearing recalls, that a breach can leak hot oil and increase fire risk. Repairs are free through Nissan dealers.

What causes the Nissan Rogue Hybrid engine to stall?

Nissan says contact with the Electronic Control Module bracket can damage the engine wiring harness on 2018-2019 Rogue Hybrid vehicles, which may blow a fuse. A blown fuse can cause the engine to stall while driving, and dealers install a harness protector cover free of charge.
