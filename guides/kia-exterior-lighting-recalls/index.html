Why do Kia headlights and parking lights keep getting recalled?

3 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Exterior Lighting · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 27, 2025

Kia's exterior lighting recalls are three separate problems on three different cars, not one repeating defect. The oldest, from 2018, covers 2019 Forte models with LED headlights whose low beams were not aimed correctly, cited as a failure to meet Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108. The 2024 recall covers 2020 Telluride vehicles whose high beam headlights may fail. The newest, from 2025, covers 2025 K5 vehicles where a software error in the Power-net Domain Controller may make the parking lights flicker, also cited under FMVSS 108. Model years run from 2019 through 2025, and no model appears twice. Two of the three involve headlights themselves; one involves parking lights.

Warning signs to watch for

  • High beam headlights may fail — the 2020 Telluride recall (SC299).
  • Parking lights may flicker — the 2025 K5 recall (SC341).
  • Low beams not aimed correctly — the 2019 Forte LED headlight recall (SC171).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices carries a stop-driving instruction, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2020 Telluride high beam recall, the 2019 Forte headlight aiming recall and the 2025 K5 parking light recall each describe the risk as reduced visibility increasing the risk of a crash, and each directs owners to have the recall work performed. Beyond that, the notices give owners no interim instruction about when or how to drive the vehicle, and they say nothing further about driving conditions.

What the free repair involves

For the 2020 Telluride, dealers inspect and repair or replace the high beam headlights as necessary. For the 2019 Forte, dealers inspect the headlight aiming and replace the headlights if necessary. For the 2025 K5, the Power-net Domain Controller software is updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update. All of this work is free of charge.

Common questions

My 2020 Telluride's high beams work now — do I still need the recall done?

The notice covers certain 2020 Tellurides because the high beam headlights may fail, and it does not tell owners to judge by whether the lights currently work. Dealers inspect and repair or replace the high beam headlights as necessary, free of charge.

Can the 2025 K5 parking light recall be fixed without a dealer visit?

Kia's notice says the Power-net Domain Controller software will be updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 24, 2025. Owners can contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542 and reference SC341.

Do any of these Kia lighting recalls tell owners to park outside?

None of the three notices includes a park-outside instruction, and none tells owners to stop driving. Each describes reduced visibility increasing the risk of a crash and directs owners to have the free recall repair performed by a Kia dealer.
