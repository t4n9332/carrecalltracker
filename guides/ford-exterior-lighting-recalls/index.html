Why do Ford trucks keep getting brake light and headlight recalls?

26 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-09

This group mixes Ford's own truck recalls with several separate recalls from ambulance and fire-truck upfitters. The most repeated pattern is aftermarket Whelen brake lights wired incorrectly on 2018 model-year emergency vehicles built on Ford E-350, E-450, F-450, and F-550 chassis, causing brake lights to illuminate when the pedal is not pressed; six separate recalls describe this same wiring issue. Ford's own lighting recalls cover different causes: daytime running lights that fail to dim on the 2018-2020 F-150 and 2018-2019 F-250/F-350/F-450 Super Duty, flickering parking lights on the 2022-2023 Bronco Sport, a burnt LED driver diode causing headlight failure on 2025 Mustang and Lincoln Nautilus, a faulty stop light switch on 2025 F-53, and an intermittent tail light connector on 2019 Ranger.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Daytime running lights staying on instead of dimming when switching to headlights (F-150, Super Duty)
  • Parking lights flickering when the headlights are turned on (Bronco Sport)
  • Brake lights illuminating when the brake pedal has not been pressed (ambulance and fire-truck conversions)
  • Low-beam, high-beam, daytime running, or turn signal lights failing on one side (Mustang, Nautilus)
  • Tail lights working intermittently or not at all (Ranger)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The F-150 and Super Duty daytime running light recalls, the Bronco Sport parking light recall, the Mustang and Nautilus headlight recall, the F-53 brake light switch recall, the Ranger tail light connector recall, and the six ambulance and fire-truck brake light recalls all describe the defect and its risk without directing owners to stop driving or park outside.

What the free repair involves

Dealers reprogram the body control module for the daytime running light recalls, replace the LED driver module or parking light components for the Bronco Sport and Mustang/Nautilus recalls, replace the stop light switch on the F-53, replace or inspect the tail light connector on the Ranger, and replace the Whelen brake lights on the ambulance and fire-truck conversions. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why do so many ambulances and fire trucks built on Ford chassis have brake light recalls?

Six separate recalls, from Watts Manufacturing, Excellance, FES, Blanchat, Taylor Made, and Unruh Fire, involve Whelen brake lights wired so they can illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed, affecting 2018 emergency vehicles built on Ford E-350, E-450, F-450, and F-550 chassis.

Is the F-150 daytime running light recall the same as the Super Duty one?

No. Both involve daytime running lights that fail to dim, but the F-150 recall covers 2018-2020 trucks with high-series LED headlamps, while the Super Duty recall covers 2018-2019 F-250, F-350, and F-450 trucks; both are fixed with a body control module software update.

What causes the 2025 Mustang and Lincoln Nautilus headlight failures?

The notice says a burnt diode in the LED driver module can cause low-beam, high-beam, daytime running, front position, and turn signal lights to fail in the affected headlight, and on the Nautilus can also affect rear tail, turn signal, and position lights.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
