Why do Ford exterior lighting recalls keep covering brake lights and headlights?

26 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-09

Ford's exterior lighting recalls fall into a few repeating groups. The largest covers Whelen brake lights on ambulances, fire trucks and first responder vehicles built on Ford F-550, E-350 and E-450 chassis, where the brake lights may illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed; Watts Manufacturing, Excellance, Fire Equipment Sales & Service, Blanchat, Taylor Made and Unruh Fire each filed one. A second group is daytime running lights that do not dim, on 2018-2020 F-150 and 2018-2019 F-250, F-350 and F-450 Super Duty trucks. A third involves LED driver modules: flickering parking lights on 2022-2023 Bronco Sport and 2022 F-150, and headlight failure on 2025 Mustang, Mustang Mach-E and Lincoln Nautilus.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake lights may illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed — the Whelen brake light recalls on ambulances, fire trucks and first responder vehicles.
  • Daytime running lights may remain activated instead of dimming when the headlight switch is moved on — the 2018-2020 F-150 and 2018-2019 F-250, F-350 and F-450 Super Duty recalls.
  • Parking lights may flicker when the headlights are activated — the 2022-2023 Bronco Sport and 2022 F-150 recall.
  • Rear lights may function intermittently or not at all, losing stop, turn, rear position and reverse lamp functions — the 2019 Ranger recall.
  • Low-beam and high-beam headlights, daytime running lights, front position lights and front turn signals may fail in the affected headlight — the 2025 Mustang, Mustang Mach-E and Lincoln Nautilus recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park outside. That includes the 2025 Mustang, Mustang Mach-E and Lincoln Nautilus headlight recall, the 2025 F-53 and F-59 brake light recall, the 2022-2023 Bronco Sport and 2022 F-150 parking light recall, the 2018-2020 F-150 and 2018-2019 Super Duty daytime running light recalls, the 2019 Ranger rear light recall, and the Whelen brake light recalls on ambulances, fire trucks and first responder vehicles. None of the notices give owners an interim instruction to follow while waiting.

What the free repair involves

Dealers reprogram the body control module on the F-150 and Super Duty daytime running light recalls, replace the LED driver module on the Bronco Sport and F-150 parking light recall, inspect and replace LED driver modules on the Mustang and Nautilus recall, replace the stop light switch on the F-53 and F-59 recall, and replace Ranger rear light connectors if needed. Whelen replaces the brake lights. All free of charge.

Common questions

My brake lights come on when I'm not braking. Is that a recall?

Six notices cover Whelen brake lights on ambulances, fire trucks and first responder vehicles built on Ford chassis, which may illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed. Whelen or the body builder replaces the brake lights free of charge.

Do any of these lighting recalls need a software fix instead of a part?

Yes. The 2018-2020 F-150 and the 2018-2019 F-250, F-350 and F-450 Super Duty daytime running light recalls are fixed by reprogramming the body control module at a dealer. The other lighting recalls listed here replace a part.

Does the F-53 brake light recall affect anything besides the lights?

The notice says the brake lights may illuminate unexpectedly or fail to illuminate when the brake pedal is applied, and that the vehicle may be shifted out of park without the brake pedal applied, increasing the risk of a rollaway and crash.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
