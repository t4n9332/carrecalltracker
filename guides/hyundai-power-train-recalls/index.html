Hyundai power train recalls: oil pump fires and lost drive power

6 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Power Train · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 6, 2025

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Six Hyundai power train recalls fall into two clusters. Two involve the electric oil pump controller: the July 2023 recall covers 2023-2024 Palisade and Tucson, 2023 Sonata, Elantra and Kona, where damaged components can cause the controller to overheat; the May 2025 recall covers the 2025 Palisade, where an insufficient seal lets moisture reach the printed circuit board. Both carry a park-outside instruction. The rest are loss-of-drive-power cases: a transmission oil pump fail-safe fault on 2021-2022 Santa Fe, Sonata and Veloster N with 2022 Santa Cruz, Elantra N and Kona N; a rear inner driveshaft on 2023 IONIQ 5, IONIQ 6 and Genesis GV60; differential gear bolts on the 2024 IONIQ 6; and a transmission control unit software error that can let a 2024 Santa Fe roll away in PARK.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A complete loss of drive power (October 2022 fail-safe drive mode recall covering 2021-2022 Santa Fe, Sonata and Veloster N, and 2022 Santa Cruz, Elantra N and Kona N).
  • A loss of drive power if the rear inner driveshaft breaks (2023 IONIQ 5, IONIQ 6 and Genesis GV60 recall).
  • A loss of drive power or the differential locking up (2024 IONIQ 6 gear drive unit recall).
  • The vehicle rolling away when it is in PARK (2024 Santa Fe transmission control unit software recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two of these six recalls carry a park-outside instruction. In the July 2023 electric oil pump controller recall covering 2023-2024 Palisade and Tucson and 2023 Sonata, Elantra and Kona, owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the repair is complete; the May 2025 Palisade oil pump controller recall gives the same instruction. For the 2024 Santa Fe transmission control unit recall, owners are advised to use the emergency parking brake after parking. No notice in this group tells owners to stop driving, and the other notices give no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and replace the electric oil pump controller as necessary in the 2023 recall, and replace it in the 2025 Palisade recall. They replace the rear inner driveshaft on the 2023 IONIQ 5, IONIQ 6 and GV60, and the gear drive unit on the 2024 IONIQ 6. The 2022 recall adds transmission inspection and control unit software; the 2024 Santa Fe gets an over-the-air update. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why does Hyundai tell me to park my Palisade outside?

The July 2023 and May 2025 oil pump controller recalls both advise parking outside and away from structures until the repair is complete, because the controller can overheat or short and result in a fire. Contact a dealer to confirm whether your VIN is included.

Does the driveshaft recall cover the Genesis GV60 as well?

Yes. The February 2024 recall covers 2023 IONIQ 5, IONIQ 6 and Genesis GV60 vehicles, where the rear inner driveshaft may break under load and result in a loss of drive power. Dealers replace the rear inner driveshaft free of charge.

My 2024 Santa Fe is in the rollaway recall. What does the notice say to do?

The notice says a transmission control unit software error may result in a roll away when the vehicle is in PARK, and advises using the emergency parking brake after parking. Hyundai will release an over-the-air update, and dealers will inspect and replace the dual clutch transmission as necessary.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
