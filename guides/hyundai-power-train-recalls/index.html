Why Do Some Hyundai Powertrains Overheat or Lose Power?

6 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Power Train · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-05-06

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Six Hyundai powertrain recalls in this group fall into three patterns. Two involve an electric oil pump controller that can overheat and start a fire: one covers 2023-2024 Palisade, Tucson, Sonata, Elantra, and Kona models, the other covers the 2025 Palisade with a separate seal defect. Three involve a loss of drive power: a 2021-2022 recall covering Santa Fe, Sonata, Veloster N, Santa Cruz, Elantra N, and Kona N tied to a transmission fail-safe mode; a 2023 Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6, and Genesis GV60 recall over a rear driveshaft that can break; and a 2024 Ioniq 6 recall over differential gear bolts. A sixth recall covers a 2024 Santa Fe software defect that can let the vehicle roll away in PARK.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Sudden loss of drive power while driving (transmission fail-safe, driveshaft, and differential bolt recalls)
  • The vehicle rolling away after being shifted into PARK (2024 Santa Fe recall)
  • The oil pump controller recalls describe no warning sign before the controller overheats or catches fire

Is it safe to keep driving?

Two of these six recalls tell owners to park outside and away from structures until repaired: the 2023-2024 Palisade, Tucson, Sonata, Elantra, and Kona oil pump recall, and the 2025 Palisade oil pump recall. The 2024 Santa Fe rollaway recall tells owners to use the emergency parking brake after parking, as an interim step. None of the six notices tell owners to stop driving; for the transmission, driveshaft, and differential bolt recalls, the notices give no driving restriction at all.

What the free repair involves

Repairs vary by recall: dealers replace the electric oil pump controller, inspect and replace the transmission with a software update, replace the rear driveshaft, replace the differential gear drive unit, or install an over-the-air software update plus inspect and replace the transmission (2024 Santa Fe). All repairs across these six recalls are free of charge.

Common questions

Which Hyundai oil pump recalls carry a park-outside warning?

Two do: the 2023-2024 recall covering Palisade, Tucson, Sonata, Elantra, and Kona, and the 2025 Palisade recall. Both involve an electric oil pump controller that can overheat, and both tell owners to park outside and away from structures until repaired.

What does the 2024 Hyundai Santa Fe rollaway recall involve?

Hyundai says a software error in the transmission control unit can let a 2024 Santa Fe roll away while in PARK. Owners are told to use the emergency parking brake; Hyundai will send an over-the-air update and inspect the transmission.

Why do so many Hyundai models have loss-of-drive-power recalls?

Three separate defects cause it in this group: an impaired transmission fail-safe mode (2021-2022 models), a driveshaft that can break (2023 Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6, GV60), and loose differential gear bolts (2024 Ioniq 6). Each is a distinct mechanical or software cause.
