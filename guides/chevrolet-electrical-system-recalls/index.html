Chevrolet Electrical Recalls Include Bolt Battery Fires and Power Loss

14 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 12, 2024

⚠ 5 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Bolt battery fire concerns recur most often in these Chevrolet electrical notices. The 2020 and July 2021 notices address 2017-2019 Bolt EV vehicles within their stated coverage; the August 2021 notice covers certain 2020-2022 Bolt EV and 2022 Bolt EUV vehicles. June and October 2024 notices cover those later model years with diagnostic-software installation problems. Other defects are distinct: 2019-2020 Silverado 1500 cable connections, optional rear-HVAC wiring on 2003-2023 Express Cutaway incomplete vehicles, 2018 Malibu injector-control software, and medium-duty truck ABS indicator programming. Additional notices address the 2020 Corvette front trunk lid, 2024 Trax instrument display, and rear-drive-unit wiring on 2024 Blazer EV and Silverado EV vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • December 12, 2019 Silverado battery-cable recall: the vehicle may stall.
  • September 6, 2019 Malibu ECM recall: the engine may not start or may stall.
  • August 18, 2020 Corvette hood recall: visual and audible warnings accompany an unlatched front trunk lid.
  • November 6, 2023 Trax instrument-panel recall: the display may intermittently go blank at startup or while driving.
  • May 9, 2024 Blazer EV/Silverado EV rear-drive-unit recall: loss of drive power.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Five listed Bolt recalls carry park-outside instructions: November 13, 2020; July 23, 2021; August 20, 2021; June 27, 2024; and October 31, 2024. The 2020 notice specifies limiting charging to 90% or parking outside until its software update. July 2021 specifies parking outside after charging and avoiding indoor overnight charging; August 2021 specifies parking outside after charging. Both 2024 notices specify parking outside and away from structures after charging until repair. None carries a stop-driving instruction. For the remaining listed recalls, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Explicitly free remedies include sealing Express rear-HVAC circuits, reprogramming the Malibu ECM, updating Corvette body-control software, updating Trax VCU software by dealer or OTA, and replacing affected Blazer EV/Silverado EV rear drive units. The Silverado cable notice describes inspection, cleaning, reattachment, and replacement of damaged components; its supplied remedy excerpt does not establish cost.

Common questions

Which Bolt electrical recalls tell owners to park outside?

The listed notices dated November 13, 2020; July 23, 2021; August 20, 2021; June 27, 2024; and October 31, 2024 include park-outside instructions. Their charging conditions and parking wording differ, so each instruction belongs to its specific recall.

Does the 2024 Trax blank instrument-panel recall have an OTA remedy?

The November 6, 2023 notice covers certain 2024 Chevrolet Trax vehicles. VCU software may make the instrument display intermittently go blank at startup or while driving. The notice specifies a free software update through a dealer or over the air.

What does the Corvette hood software repair change?

For certain 2020 Corvette vehicles, the August 18, 2020 notice specifies a free body control module software update limiting speed to 26 mph when the hood is not fully closed and latched. It also specifies modifying key-fob hood-release operation.
