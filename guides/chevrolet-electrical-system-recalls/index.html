What Chevrolet electrical recalls mean for Bolt, Silverado and Corvette owners

14 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 12, 2024

⚠ 5 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

The dominant thread here is the Chevrolet Bolt's high-voltage battery: five of these recalls cover 2017-2019 Bolt EV and 2020-2022 Bolt EV and Bolt EUV vehicles. Three say the battery could catch fire when charged to full or nearly full capacity; two cover cars previously repaired under recall 21V-650, where the advanced diagnostic software may have been installed incorrectly or failed to install. The rest are scattered GM software and wiring faults: an engine control module error on 2018 Malibu 1.5L turbo cars, a blank instrument panel on the 2024 Chevrolet Trax, a 2020 Corvette that can be driven with the front trunk lid unlatched, battery cable contamination on 2019-2020 Silverado 1500 trucks, and rear drive unit wiring on 2024 Blazer EV and Silverado EV.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Visual and audible warnings are given, yet the notice says the vehicle can still be driven with the front trunk lid unlatched — 2020 Corvette recall.
  • The instrument panel display goes blank at vehicle startup or while driving — 2024 Chevrolet Trax and Buick Encore GX and Envista recall.
  • The engine will not start, and may stall while driving — 2018 Malibu 1.5L turbo engine control module recall.
  • The ABS malfunction indicator light may not illuminate when the ABS system is malfunctioning — 2019-2020 Chevrolet 4500HD, 5500HD and 6500HD recall.
  • The vehicle may stall — 2019-2020 Silverado 1500 and Sierra 1500 battery positive cable recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

No notice here tells owners to stop driving. Five Bolt recalls carry a park-outside instruction. The 2020 recall of 2017-2019 Bolt EV tells owners to limit charging to 90% or park outside until the software update is complete. The 2021 recall of those cars, the 2021 recall of 2020-2022 Bolt EV and 2022 Bolt EUV, and the two 2024 recalls of those same vehicles tell owners to limit the charge to 90%, charge more often, avoid dropping below 70 miles of range, park outside after charging and not charge indoors. The rest do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

All these repairs are free. For the 2020 Bolt EV recall, dealers reprogram the hybrid propulsion control module as an interim repair. Others are software: the Malibu engine control module, body control module updates on the Corvette and medium-duty trucks, and Trax virtual cockpit software a dealer or an over-the-air update can install. Silverado dealers clean and reattach the battery cable connection; 2024 EV dealers replace the rear drive unit.

Common questions

Is my Bolt still under a battery recall if it was already repaired?

The two 2024 notices cover 2020-2022 Bolt EV and 2022 Bolt EUV vehicles previously repaired under recall 21V-650, saying the advanced diagnostic software may have been installed incorrectly or failed to install. Check your VIN on NHTSA's site to see what remains open.

Does the Chevrolet Trax blank instrument panel recall need a dealer visit?

The notice says the Virtual Cockpit Unit Module software will be updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update, free of charge, on 2024 Chevrolet Trax and Buick Encore GX and Envista vehicles.

What do dealers do for the Silverado 1500 battery cable recall?

Dealers inspect for glue or other contamination where the battery positive cable attaches to the alternator, then clean the connection and reattach the cable. If arcing has damaged the cable, nut or alternator, the damaged part is replaced.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
