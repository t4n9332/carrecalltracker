Why do Subaru driveshafts and transmissions keep failing?

8 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Power Train · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-11-09

These eight Subaru powertrain recalls, covering 2019-2024 models, repeat two defects and add several others. Driveshaft center support bolts that can loosen and disconnect the front driveshaft appear in both a 2019 Ascent recall and a 2023 recall covering Ascent, Impreza, Legacy, and Outback. A transmission programming error that lets the clutch engage before the drive chain is fully clamped, risking a broken chain, appears in a 2019-2020 Ascent/Legacy/Outback recall and again in a 2020-2021 Legacy/Outback recall. Separately, 2019 Ascent vehicles had a misreading transmission sensor, 2023-2024 Forester, WRX, Crosstrek, and Impreza vehicles had front driveshafts that can crack, 2021 Impreza and Outback vehicles had a loosening shifter cable nut, and 2021-2023 vehicles across several models had an inhibitor switch that can disable reverse lights and the camera.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Loss of drive power while driving
  • A driveshaft disconnecting while driving, or the vehicle rolling away when parked without the parking brake set
  • Gear selector or shifter not functioning properly
  • No reverse lights or rearview camera image when backing up

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these eight recalls instructs owners to stop driving or park away from structures. The notices describe increased crash risk from a driveshaft disconnecting while driving, a driveshaft-related vehicle rollaway when parked without the parking brake set, loss of drive power from a misreading transmission sensor or a slipping drive chain, a gear selector that may not function properly, and inoperative reverse lights and camera view — but for all eight recalls, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the driveshaft assembly or center support brace and bolts for the two driveshaft recalls, reprogram the transmission control unit and inspect or replace the drive chain and transmission for the two chain-slip recalls, reprogram the transmission control unit for the sensor recall, tighten or replace the shifter cable nut, replace the front driveshafts for the cracking recall, and replace the inhibitor switch — all free of charge.

Common questions

Why is my Subaru's driveshaft at risk of disconnecting?

Two recalls — a 2019 Ascent recall and a 2023 recall covering Ascent, Impreza, Legacy, and Outback — found the driveshaft's center support bolts can loosen, letting the front end of the driveshaft disconnect while driving.

Why does my Subaru's transmission lose power?

Two recalls found a transmission control unit programming error that lets the clutch engage before the drive chain is fully clamped, which can let the chain slip and break, causing a loss of drive power.

Why don't my reverse lights or camera work when backing up?

The recall found an inhibitor switch weld on certain 2021-2023 Crosstrek, Legacy, Outback, and Forester vehicles can let water in and fail, which can prevent the reverse lights and rearview camera from working.
