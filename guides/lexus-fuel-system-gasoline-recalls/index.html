Lexus fuel pump recalls: why engines stall and what dealers replace

4 NHTSA recalls · Lexus · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2020 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 8, 2026

Three of these four Lexus recalls are the same defect: a low-pressure fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine. The January 2020 recall and the November 2020 recall between them sweep up a long Lexus list - ES350, GS, GX460, IS, LC500, LS500, LX570, NX300, RC 300, RC 350, RX350 and RX350L, with Lexus model years running from 2013 to 2020. The April 2026 recall returns to the same part on 2015-2022 RC, 2016-2020 GS and 2022 IS. The outlier is 21LA01: on certain 2019 and 2021 RX350 vehicles the transport hook may not have been removed before sale, and in a rear-end crash it can damage the fuel filler pipe.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An engine that stalls while driving if the fuel pump fails (the January 2020 and November 2020 fuel pump recalls and the April 2026 RC, GS and IS recall).
  • The 2019 and 2021 RX350 transport hook notice, 21LA01, does not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The January 2020, November 2020 and April 2026 fuel pump notices all say a fuel pump failure can stall the engine while driving and increase the risk of a crash, but none of them tells owners to stop driving. The 2019 and 2021 RX350 transport hook notice, 21LA01, describes a fire risk in a rear-end crash and likewise does not tell owners to stop driving. Contact a Lexus dealer to have the recall work scheduled.

What the free repair involves

For the fuel pump recalls, dealers replace the fuel pump: the January 2020 recall replaces the fuel pump, the November 2020 recall replaces the fuel pump assembly with an improved one, and the April 2026 recall replaces the fuel pump assembly. Under 21LA01, dealers inspect and, as necessary, remove the rear transport hook. All of this work is free of charge.

Common questions

Are parts available for the 2026 Lexus RC, GS and IS recall?

That notice says interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk were mailed June 5, 2026, and that additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available. The remedy itself is replacement of the fuel pump assembly, free of charge.

My RX350 was repaired in 2020. Could it be listed again?

Possibly. The RX350 and RX350L appear in both the January 2020 and November 2020 fuel pump recalls, and 2019 and 2021 RX350 vehicles also appear in 21LA01, the transport hook recall. Each recall is tracked separately by VIN at a Lexus dealer.

What does a transport hook have to do with fuel system safety?

21LA01 states the transport hook may not have been removed prior to sale, so the affected 2019 and 2021 RX350 vehicles do not comply with FMVSS 301. In a rear-end crash the hook can damage the fuel filler pipe, increasing fire risk.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
