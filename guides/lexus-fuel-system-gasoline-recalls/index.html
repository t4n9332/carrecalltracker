Why do these Lexus recalls involve fuel pump failure?

4 NHTSA recalls · Lexus · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2020 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-08

Three of these four recalls involve the same failure: a fuel pump that can fail and cause the engine to stall while driving. This affected 2018-2019 Lexus ES350, GX460, LS500, LX570, NX300, RC300, RC350, RX350 and RX350L models across two recalls issued in January and November 2020, and it recurred in a 2026 recall covering 2018-2019 Lexus GS and 2018-2020 Lexus RC models with the same low-pressure in-tank fuel pump. The fourth recall is unrelated: on the 2019 Lexus RX350, a transport hook used during shipping may not have been removed, and in a rear-end crash it can damage the fuel filler pipe and increase the risk of a fire.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Fuel pump failure can cause the engine to stall or lose power while driving (2020 and 2026 recalls)
  • The 2019 RX350 transport hook recall describes no warning sign — the risk applies only if the vehicle is struck from behind

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The two 2020 fuel pump recalls and the 2026 Lexus GS/RC fuel pump recall state that a failed pump can cause the engine to stall while driving, increasing the risk of a crash; the 2019 RX350 transport hook recall states that a rear-end crash could damage the fuel filler pipe and increase the risk of a fire. Even so, none of the four notices tells owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the fuel pump recalls, dealers replace the fuel pump (or fuel pump assembly) with an improved part, free of charge. For the 2019 RX350 recall, dealers inspect the vehicle and remove the leftover rear transport hook if present, also free of charge.

Common questions

Which Lexus models are affected by the fuel pump stall recalls?

The 2018-2019 ES350, GX460, LS500, LX570, NX300, RC300, RC350, RX350 and RX350L were recalled in 2020, and the 2018-2019 GS and 2018-2020 RC were recalled again in 2026 for the same type of fuel pump failure.

What's the risk with the 2019 RX350 transport hook recall?

A leftover shipping hook may not have been removed from some 2019 RX350 vehicles. In a rear-end crash, it can damage the fuel filler pipe, which the notice says increases the risk of a fire.

Do these recalls tell owners to stop driving?

No. None of the four recalls in this group carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; each notice describes the risk and states that dealers will complete a free repair when parts are available.
