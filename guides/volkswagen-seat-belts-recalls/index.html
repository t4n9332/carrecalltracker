Which Volkswagen seat belt recalls affect your Atlas, Tiguan or ID.4?

5 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 5, 2021

Child restraints and rear seating come up again and again in these five recalls. Two 2021 recalls cover automatic locking retractors: the ID.4 may have rear outboard belt assemblies without an ALR function, and a second recall covering the ID.4 alongside many 2021 Audi models involves rear retractors that may deactivate early, either of which can stop a child restraint from securing properly. The 2018 Atlas was recalled because its owner's manual omitted the child restraint size limit for the second row center position, and wider bases can damage the buckles. The Tiguan appears twice — 2018 long wheel base front belt webbing that may tear in a crash, and 2020 long wheel base third row anchor bolts that may not have been properly torqued.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A second row buckle that releases unexpectedly — the 2018 Atlas notice says a damaged center or adjacent outboard buckle can release unexpectedly.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving. One carries an interim instruction: the 2020 Tiguan long wheel base third row anchor bolt notice advises that no one should occupy the third row seat because of the safety risk, and says Volkswagen will repurchase the vehicles. The other four — the 2018 Atlas buckles, the 2018 Tiguan front belt webbing, and the two 2021 rear belt child restraint recalls — describe an increased risk of injury in a crash and give owners no instruction beyond having the recall work done.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the 2018 Atlas center and left outboard buckles and replace them if necessary, and Volkswagen supplies an owner's manual supplement on child restraints. They inspect and replace as needed the 2018 Tiguan front belts, the ID.4 rear outboard belt assemblies and the middle-rear assemblies in the Audi and ID.4 recall — all free of charge. For the 2020 Tiguan, Volkswagen will repurchase the vehicles.

Common questions

Will a child seat secure properly in my 2021 ID.4?

Two notices cover the ID.4. One says rear outboard belt assemblies without an automatic locking retractor may have been installed; the other says rear retractors may deactivate early. Both say this can prevent a child restraint from securing properly, and dealers inspect and replace as necessary, free.

What does the 2020 Tiguan third row recall involve?

Recall 69BA covers 2020 Tiguan long wheel base vehicles whose third row seat belt anchor bolts may not have been properly torqued. The notice advises that no one should occupy the third row seat, and says Volkswagen will repurchase the vehicles.

Why was an owner's manual the reason for the Atlas recall?

The 2018 Atlas manuals did not tell owners the child restraint size limit for the second row center position, and a child seat base wider than 12.6 inches can damage the buckles. Dealers inspect the center and left outboard buckles and replace them if necessary, free of charge.
