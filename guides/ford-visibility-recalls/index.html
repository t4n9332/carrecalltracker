Why Do So Many Ford Windshields and Glass Panels Come Loose?

17 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Visibility · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-30

Ford's visibility recalls center on windshields and glass panels that were not properly bonded during manufacturing: 2020-2021 Super Duty trucks, the 2020 Transit Connect, the 2022 Bronco, and 2025 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator windshields, plus moonroof glass on the 2021 Bronco Sport, rear glass on 2023-2024 Bronco, and a roof panel on 2018-2019 Transit Connect vans. Separately, 2018 Expedition and Navigator power windows could fail to reverse on an obstruction, 2020 Explorer Police Interceptor and Aviator washer fluid was contaminated with brake fluid, and 2022 wiper arms could break on several F-150, Expedition, Super Duty, and Navigator models. Two 2019 Ranger recalls involved an HVAC blower motor electrical short.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A windshield or glass panel that looks loose or not fully sealed to the body
  • A power window that fails to reverse after catching an object such as a finger
  • Cloudy or streaky windshield after using the washer fluid
  • Windshield wipers that stop moving or a wiper arm that appears broken
  • The HVAC blower motor recalls describe no warning sign before an electrical short

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these twelve Ford visibility-related recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, including the windshield and glass panel bonding recalls, the power window issue, the contaminated washer fluid recall, the wiper arm failure, and the two Ranger HVAC blower motor recalls. The notices describe risks such as reduced visibility, glass detaching as a road hazard, and increased fire risk from an electrical short, but in each case the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, Ford dealers remove and reinstall or replace windshields and glass panels, replace the HVAC blower motor or window control modules as needed, flush and replace washer system components, and replace wiper arms, all free of charge. No recall in this group is completed with a software or over-the-air update.

Common questions

Which Ford windshields have been recalled for detaching?

Windshield bonding recalls cover 2020-2021 Super Duty trucks, the 2020 Transit Connect, the 2022 Bronco, and 2025 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator models. Each recall addresses a windshield that was not properly bonded or, for the 2025 models, glass with visible air bubbles.

Do I need to stop driving my Ford because of these recalls?

No. None of these visibility-related Ford recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. Dealers inspect and repair or replace the affected windshield, glass panel, or component once the owner schedules a free appointment.

What is the Ranger HVAC blower motor recall about?

Two separate recalls cover 2019 Ford Ranger trucks whose HVAC blower motor may develop an electrical short due to improper clearance, which increases the risk of a fire. Dealers inspect and replace the blower motor free of charge.
