Ford windshield, glass and wiper recalls: what owners should know

17 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Visibility · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 30, 2026

Glass bonding runs through most of this group. Ford recalled 2021 F-150 and 2020-2021 Super Duty F-250 through F-550 trucks, 2016 and 2020 Transit Connect vans, and 2022 Bronco and Ranger vehicles because the windshield may not have been properly bonded. Bond problems also affect 2021 Bronco Sport moonroof glass, 2017-2019 Transit Connect panoramic roof panels, and 2023-2024 Bronco rear quarter glass. The rest affect visibility or cabin systems: wiper arms that may break on 2020-2021 F-150, Expedition and Navigator and 2020-2022 Super Duty trucks, washer fluid contaminated with brake fluid on 2020 Explorer Police Interceptor Utility and Aviator, air bubbles in 2025-2026 Explorer, Aviator and Corsair windshields, second row power window modules on 2018 Expedition and Navigator, and two 2019 Ranger HVAC blower motor recalls.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Ford says the 2025-2026 Lincoln Corsair, Lincoln Aviator and Explorer windshield glass may have visible air bubbles that can obscure the driver's visibility.
  • The windshield wiper arms may break, causing the wipers to fail (2020-2021 F-150, Expedition and Navigator and 2020-2022 Super Duty F-250 through F-550 wiper arm recall).
  • The second row window control modules may intermittently stop the movement of the power window (2018 Expedition and Lincoln Navigator power window recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these notices tells owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside, including the two 2019 Ranger HVAC blower motor recalls that describe a fire risk. The windshield, moonroof, rear quarter glass and panoramic roof notices state their crash or injury risk and then direct owners to the dealer repair. For the wiper arm recall, Ford mailed interim owner notification letters informing owners of the safety risk before remedy parts were available. Beyond that, the notices say nothing about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

Dealers remove and reinstall the windshield on the Super Duty, Transit Connect and Bronco and Ranger recalls, and inspect and replace the 2025-2026 windshields with air bubbles. They reinstall or replace Bronco rear quarter glass, replace Bronco Sport moonroof glass, and remove, clean and reinstall the Transit Connect panoramic roof. Other remedies replace both front wiper arms, flush the washer system, and replace blower motors or second row window modules. All free of charge.

Common questions

Is a windshield bonding recall really a safety problem?

Ford's notices say a windshield that was not properly bonded may detach during a crash, increasing the risk of injury, and that the vehicles fail Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 212, "Windshield Mounting." Dealers remove and reinstall the windshield free of charge.

My 2019 Ranger blower motor was already replaced — why is there another recall?

Ford recalled certain 2019 Rangers previously repaired under recall 19V-726 because the service replacement HVAC blower motor may have improper clearance between an electrical terminal and the conductive base plate, which may result in a resistive electrical short. Dealers inspect and replace it free of charge.

Does the contaminated washer fluid recall apply to a regular Explorer?

Ford's notice covers 2020 Explorer Police Interceptor Utility and 2020 Lincoln Aviator vehicles containing windshield washer fluid contaminated with brake fluid. Dealers flush the washer system and replace the washer bottle neck, washer pump assembly, fluid level sensor and wiper blades, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
