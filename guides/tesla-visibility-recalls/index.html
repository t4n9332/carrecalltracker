Tesla visibility recalls: wipers, power windows and Model S mirrors

3 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Visibility · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 19, 2024

These three Tesla recalls cover different things an owner sees through or around the vehicle, not one repeating defect. One covers 2024 Cybertruck vehicles, where excessive electrical current can cause the front windshield wiper motor controller to fail. The widest covers the window automatic reversal system, which may not react correctly after detecting an obstruction, on 2017-2022 Model 3, 2020-2022 Model Y and 2021-2022 Model S and Model X — a failure to comply with FMVSS 118. The third covers 2021 Model S exterior side mirrors, part numbers 1622232-00-B and 1622233-00-B: the driver side glass is convex and the passenger side lacks the etched "Objects in Mirror Are Closer Than They Appear" warning, failing FMVSS 111. Model S is the only model named in two of the three.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2024 Cybertruck recall says the front windshield wiper motor controller can fail.
  • The power window recall says a closing window may pinch a driver or passenger with excessive force before retracting.
  • The 2021 Model S mirror recall says the driver side exterior mirrors are convex and the passenger side mirrors do not include the etched warning.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2024 Cybertruck wiper motor notice, the window automatic reversal notice covering Model 3, Model Y, Model S and Model X, and the 2021 Model S mirror notice each state the risk — reduced visibility, a pinching window, a misjudged distance — and then give only the remedy and the owner-letter date. Nothing in them addresses whether to limit driving while waiting for the repair.

What the free repair involves

Each of the three remedies is free of charge. For the 2024 Cybertruck, Tesla service replaces the front windshield wiper motor. For the window automatic reversal system, Tesla performs an over-the-air software update rather than a parts repair. For the 2021 Model S, Tesla Service replaces the exterior side rearview mirrors.

Common questions

Does the power window recall require a service visit?

The notice says Tesla performs an over-the-air software update of the automatic window reversal system, free of charge. It does not describe a dealer visit for that recall. The Cybertruck wiper motor and Model S mirror recalls are parts replacements by Tesla service.

Which Tesla models are in the power window recall?

The notice lists 2017-2022 Model 3, 2020-2022 Model Y, and 2021-2022 Model S and Model X. It describes a window automatic reversal system that may not react correctly after detecting an obstruction, failing FMVSS 118 on power-operated window systems.

Why are the 2021 Model S mirrors being replaced?

The notice says the driver side exterior mirrors are convex and the passenger side mirrors lack the etched "Objects in Mirror Are Closer Than They Appear" warning, so they do not meet FMVSS 111. Tesla Service replaces the mirrors free of charge.
