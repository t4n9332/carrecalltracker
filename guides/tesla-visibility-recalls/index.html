Why do some Teslas have wiper, window, and mirror recalls?

3 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Visibility · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-06-19

Tesla's visibility recalls in this group cover three unrelated problems. The 2024 Cybertruck's windshield wiper motor controller can fail from excessive electrical current, cutting off the wipers. Separately, Model 3 vehicles from 2018-2021, the 2020 Model Y, the 2021 Model S, and the 2022 Model 3 share a power window issue: the automatic reversal system may not react correctly to an obstruction. The third recall is narrower, affecting only 2021 Model S vehicles fitted with side mirrors that lack the required "Objects in Mirror Are Closer Than They Appear" etching. No single model year appears in more than one of these three recalls.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Windshield wipers may stop working while driving (2024 Cybertruck).
  • A closing power window may pinch before reversing, instead of stopping right away (2018-2022 Model 3, 2020 Model Y, 2021 Model S).
  • Missing "Objects in Mirror Are Closer Than They Appear" etching on the passenger-side mirror, with convex glass on the driver's side (2021 Model S).

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three Tesla visibility recalls include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The Cybertruck wiper motor notice does not tell owners to stop driving. The power window reversal recall, which Tesla addressed with an over-the-air update, likewise does not instruct owners to stop driving. The Model S mirror etching recall also contains no stop-driving instruction. In each case, the notice describes the safety risk and the free repair without directing owners to park the vehicle or stop using it.

What the free repair involves

Tesla's fixes vary by recall: service centers replace the wiper motor on affected Cybertrucks, and replace the incorrect side mirrors on the 2021 Model S. The power window issue was fixed with an over-the-air software update to the automatic reversal system, requiring no service visit. All repairs are provided free of charge.

Common questions

Does the Tesla power window recall need a dealer visit?

No. Tesla fixed the automatic window reversal system with an over-the-air software update sent directly to affected 2018-2022 Model 3, 2020 Model Y, and 2021 Model S vehicles, so no service appointment was required for this recall.

What's wrong with the Cybertruck's wipers?

Excessive electrical current can cause the front windshield wiper motor controller to fail on 2024 Cybertruck vehicles, which can knock out the wipers. Tesla service replaces the wiper motor free of charge.

Why were 2021 Model S mirrors recalled?

Some 2021 Model S side mirrors used the wrong parts: the driver's side had convex, curved glass, and the passenger side lacked the required "Objects in Mirror Are Closer Than They Appear" etching. Tesla service replaces the mirrors free of charge.
