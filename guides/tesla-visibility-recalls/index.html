Tesla visibility recalls: mirrors, wipers and windows — what owners should know

3 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Visibility · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-06-19

Tesla's visibility recalls are three separate one-off defects rather than one repeating fault. The oldest involves 2021 Model S cars built with the wrong exterior mirror glass: a convex driver's mirror and a passenger mirror missing the 'Objects in Mirror Are Closer Than They Appear' etching. The newest hits the 2024 Cybertruck, where excessive electrical current can kill the front windshield wiper motor controller. The largest by volume is the window automatic reversal system, covering 2017-2022 Model 3, 2020-2022 Model Y and 2021-2022 Model S and Model X — a federal safety standard noncompliance fixed remotely. Model 3 and Model S appear most often, and only the window recall is handled over the air; the mirror and wiper repairs need a Tesla Service visit.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Driver's side mirror glass looks curved, making objects behind you appear smaller and farther away than they are
  • Passenger mirror is missing the 'Objects in Mirror Are Closer Than They Appear' etching
  • Cybertruck windshield wipers slow, stop, or stop responding — most dangerous in heavy rain or snow
  • A power window keeps closing on a hand or object instead of reversing when it meets an obstruction
  • The mirror and window problems produce no warning light; you have to look for them

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the three recalls tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. With the Model S mirrors you can drive normally, but judge distances behind you carefully until the glass is swapped. The Cybertruck wiper is the one to treat with urgency: the truck is fine in clear weather, but a motor that quits in heavy rain or snow can leave you unable to see, so get it replaced promptly. The window recall does not affect driving at all; just keep children clear of closing windows until the update lands.

What the free repair involves

Repairs differ by recall. Tesla Service replaces the incorrect exterior rearview mirrors on affected Model S cars, and replaces the front windshield wiper motor on the Cybertruck. The window automatic reversal problem is corrected by an over-the-air software update that installs itself, with no appointment needed. All three repairs are free of charge, and owners can reach Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752.

Common questions

Will the window recall fix arrive automatically?

Yes. Tesla corrects the automatic window reversal system with a free over-the-air software update, so there is no service appointment. Keep the car connected to Wi-Fi so the update can download and install. The mirror and wiper recalls do require a service visit.

How do I tell if my Model S has the wrong mirrors?

Check whether the passenger mirror carries the 'Objects in Mirror Are Closer Than They Appear' etching and whether the driver's glass is convex. The recall names mirror part numbers 1622232-00-B and 1622233-00-B; Tesla Service can confirm your car by VIN.

Can I keep driving my Cybertruck before the wiper is replaced?

Tesla does not tell owners to stop driving. The hazard is losing your view when the wiper quits, so avoid relying on it in heavy rain or snow and schedule the free wiper motor replacement promptly through Tesla Service.
