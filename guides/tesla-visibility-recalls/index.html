Tesla Visibility Recalls Cover Wipers, Washer Spray, Windows and Mirrors

4 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Visibility · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 30, 2025

These Tesla notices cover four different visibility-related components rather than one repeating defect. Certain 2024 Cybertrucks have a windshield wiper motor controller that can fail from excessive electrical current. Certain 2026 Model Y vehicles have a hose connector that may block washer nozzles. The broadest model-year coverage belongs to the automatic window reversal recall: certain 2017-2022 Model 3, 2020-2022 Model Y, and 2021-2022 Model S and Model X vehicles. A separate recall concerns certain 2021 Model S exterior mirrors with incorrect glass or missing warning etching. Model Y and Model S each appear in two notices, while the window recall spans the most years.

Warning signs to watch for

  • June 2024 Cybertruck wiper recall: windshield wiper failure.
  • May 2025 Model Y washer recall: washer fluid may not reach the windshield.
  • September 2022 power window recall: a closing window may pinch an occupant with excessive force before retracting.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the four supplied Tesla visibility notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. That includes the June 2024 Cybertruck wiper motor recall, May 2025 Model Y washer connector recall, September 2022 automatic window reversal recall, and November 2022 Model S mirror recall. The wiper and washer notices describe reduced visibility, the window notice describes an injury risk from excessive closing force, and the mirror notice describes a risk of misjudging the distance of other vehicles.

What the free repair involves

Tesla replaces the wiper motor for the Cybertruck recall and inspects and replaces the wiper arm elbow connector as necessary for the Model Y washer recall. The window reversal recall receives an over-the-air software update. Tesla Service replaces the mirrors for the Model S mirror recall. Each notice specifies a free remedy.

Common questions

Which Tesla visibility recall can be repaired over the air?

The September 2022 power window recall specifies a free over-the-air update to the automatic window reversal system. The Cybertruck wiper motor, Model Y washer connector, and Model S mirror notices instead specify replacement or inspection and replacement of their respective components.

What is wrong with the recalled Model S mirrors?

The November 2022 notice concerns certain 2021 Model S exterior mirrors. It says the driver-side mirrors have convex glass and the passenger-side mirrors lack the required distance-warning etching. Tesla Service will replace the mirrors free of charge.

Do the Cybertruck and Model Y windshield recalls require the same repair?

The June 2024 Cybertruck notice specifies replacing the windshield wiper motor. The May 2025 notice for certain 2026 Model Y vehicles specifies inspecting and replacing the wiper arm elbow connector as necessary. Both remedies are free of charge.
