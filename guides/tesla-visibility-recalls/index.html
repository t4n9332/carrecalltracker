Tesla Visibility Recalls Cover Wipers, Washer Spray, Windows and Mirrors

4 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Visibility · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 30, 2025

Windshield clearing is the recurring concern in the Cybertruck and Model Y notices, but their defects differ. Certain 2024 Cybertrucks have a wiper motor controller that can fail because of excessive electrical current. Certain 2026 Model Y vehicles have a hose connector that may block washer nozzles. The window-reversal notice spans certain 2017-2022 Model 3, 2020-2022 Model Y, and 2021-2022 Model S and Model X vehicles, making it the broadest model-and-year coverage listed here. A separate notice concerns certain 2021 Model S exterior mirrors with incorrect driver-side glass and missing passenger-side warning etching. These problems involve windshield visibility, window pinching, and judging other vehicles' distance.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Cybertruck wiper recall SB-24-17-006: windshield wiper failure can reduce visibility.
  • Model Y washer recall SB-25-20-001: washer fluid may not reach the windshield.
  • Window-reversal recall SB-22-00-013: a closing window may exert excessive force before retracting.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these visibility notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The Cybertruck windshield-wiper motor notice does not tell owners to stop driving, nor does the Model Y washer-nozzle notice. The automatic window-reversal notice and the Model S exterior-mirror notice also do not tell owners to stop driving. The notices identify reduced windshield visibility, injury from excessive window-closing force, and misjudgment of other vehicles' distance as the respective risks; they provide no stop-driving instruction for those conditions.

What the free repair involves

Tesla replaces the Cybertruck wiper motor free of charge. For the Model Y washer recall, service inspects and replaces the wiper arm elbow connector as necessary, free of charge. The window-reversal recall receives a free over-the-air software update. The Model S mirror recall receives replacement mirrors, free of charge.

Common questions

Which Tesla visibility recall has an over-the-air remedy?

The automatic window-reversal recall, SB-22-00-013, specifies a free over-the-air software update. Its notice covers certain 2017-2022 Model 3, 2020-2022 Model Y, and 2021-2022 Model S and Model X vehicles with a reversal system that may react incorrectly to an obstruction.

Do the Cybertruck wiper and Model Y washer recalls replace the same part?

The Cybertruck notice specifies replacing the wiper motor because excessive electrical current can cause its controller to fail. The Model Y notice specifies inspecting and replacing the wiper arm elbow connector as necessary because a hose connector may block washer nozzles.

What is wrong with the recalled Model S mirrors?

The notice concerns certain 2021 Model S exterior mirrors. The driver-side mirrors have convex glass, while the passenger-side mirrors lack the required distance-warning etching. Tesla Service replaces the mirrors free of charge under recall SB-22-12-002.
