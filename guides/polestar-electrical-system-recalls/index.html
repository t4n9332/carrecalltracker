What keeps going wrong with Polestar electrical systems and drive power?

3 NHTSA recalls · Polestar · Electrical System · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 7, 2025

These three Polestar recalls each end in the loss of something the driver relies on, but the parts involved differ. The oldest, R10079, covers 2021-2022 Polestar 2 vehicles whose Battery Energy Control Module microprocessor may reset and cause the high voltage system to disconnect. The other two are both 2025 Polestar 3. RP1043 covers 2025 Polestar 3 hybrid electric vehicles in which fluctuations in the 12-volt system may have damaged the High to Low Voltage Converter Module. RP1063 covers 2025 Polestar 3 vehicles where water may damage the electrical connectors between the engine bay and front bumper cable harnesses. Two of the three name a loss of drive power; the newest names a loss of power steering assist.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A loss of drive power if the high voltage system disconnects (2021-2022 Polestar 2, R10079).
  • A sudden loss in drive power (2025 Polestar 3, RP1043).
  • Electrical malfunctions such as a loss of power steering assist (2025 Polestar 3, RP1063).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. R10079 says a disconnected high voltage system can cause a loss of drive power on 2021-2022 Polestar 2 vehicles, increasing the risk of a crash. RP1043 says a damaged High to Low Voltage Converter Module causes a sudden loss in drive power on 2025 Polestar 3 hybrid electric vehicles. RP1063 says damaged harnesses can cause electrical malfunctions such as a loss of power steering assist. The notices add no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

The remedies differ by recall and all are free of charge. For R10079 on 2021-2022 Polestar 2 vehicles, the software is updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update. For RP1043 on 2025 Polestar 3 hybrid electric vehicles, dealers replace the High to Low Voltage Converter Module. For RP1063, Polestar replaces the bumper harness and the inline front bumper/engine bay connector on the engine bay harness.

Common questions

What does Polestar do about the Polestar 3 drive power recall?

Under RP1043, dealers replace the High to Low Voltage Converter Module free of charge on 2025 Polestar 3 hybrid electric vehicles. The notice says fluctuations in the 12-volt system may have damaged the module, causing a sudden loss in drive power.

Can the water damage recall be handled with a software update?

RP1063 is a parts repair. The notice says Polestar will replace the bumper harness and the inline front bumper/engine bay connector on the engine bay harness, free of charge, on 2025 Polestar 3 vehicles where water may damage those connectors.

Does my 2021 or 2022 Polestar 2 need a trip to the dealer?

For R10079, covering 2021-2022 Polestar 2 vehicles, the notice says the software will be updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update, free of charge. It addresses a Battery Energy Control Module microprocessor that may reset.
