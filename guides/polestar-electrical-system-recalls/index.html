Why do some Polestar 2 and 3 models suddenly lose drive power?

3 NHTSA recalls · Polestar · Electrical System · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-07-07

These three Polestar electrical-system recalls involve three different causes. In 2021-2022 Polestar 2 vehicles, the Battery Energy Control Module's microprocessor could reset and disconnect the high-voltage system, cutting drive power. In 2025 Polestar 3 hybrids, fluctuations in the 12-volt system could damage the High to Low Voltage Converter Module, causing a sudden loss of drive power. A separate 2025 Polestar 3 recall involves water reaching the electrical connectors between the engine bay and front bumper wiring harnesses, which can damage the harness and cause electrical malfunctions, including a loss of power steering assist. Each cause and each fix is specific to its own recall.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Loss of drive power from the high-voltage system disconnecting (2021-2022 Polestar 2)
  • Sudden loss of drive power (2025 Polestar 3, damaged HLCM)
  • Loss of power steering assist or other electrical malfunctions (2025 Polestar 3, water-damaged wiring)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2021-2022 Polestar 2 BECM recall, the 2025 Polestar 3 HLCM recall, and the 2025 Polestar 3 wiring-harness recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice describes only the mechanical cause — a resetting control module, a damaged converter module, or water-damaged wiring — and the resulting loss of drive power or power steering assist, without any instruction to park the vehicle outside or stop using it.

What the free repair involves

For the 2021-2022 Polestar 2, dealers or an over-the-air update reprogram the BECM software. For the 2025 Polestar 3, dealers replace the damaged High to Low Voltage Converter Module for the drive-power recall, and separately replace the bumper wiring harness and its connector for the water-damage recall. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Are the two Polestar 3 power-loss recalls the same issue?

No. One recall covers a damaged High to Low Voltage Converter Module from 12-volt fluctuations, causing sudden power loss. The other is a separate recall about water damage to wiring harnesses near the engine bay, which can affect power steering.

Is the 2021 Polestar 2 battery recall related to a fire risk?

No. This recall describes the Battery Energy Control Module's microprocessor resetting and disconnecting the high-voltage system, which the notice says can cause a loss of drive power and increase crash risk — it does not mention fire.

How will I know if the fix has been applied to my Polestar?

Polestar mailed owner notification letters for each recall — April 2021 for the Polestar 2 BECM recall, and January and July 2025 for the two Polestar 3 recalls. Contact Polestar customer service at 1-800-806-2504 to confirm your vehicle's repair status.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
