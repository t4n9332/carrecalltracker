Why were Volkswagen front seats recalled for bolts and welds?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Seats · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2021-02-01

All three of these Volkswagen recalls trace back to how the seats were put together, and none of them changes how the car drives. Two involve welding: 2018 e-Golf and Golf R rear seat frames whose head restraint guide sleeves may be welded incorrectly, shared with the Audi A3 Sedan and RS3, and 2020 front seat frames and backrest adjusters that may not have been welded properly on the Atlas Cross Sport, Jetta, Jetta GLI, Tiguan, Audi Q5 and SQ5, plus the 2021 Atlas. The third is simpler: bolts missing from the front seat tracks of 2021 Jetta and Tiguan long wheelbase models. The Jetta and Tiguan names recur most across the group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A front seat that rocks or shifts on its track (2021 Jetta and Tiguan)
  • A front backrest that feels loose or moves more than expected when pushed against
  • A rear head restraint that wobbles in its guide sleeves on an e-Golf or Golf R
  • Often nothing at all — these defects show up in a crash rather than in normal driving

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving or park the vehicle outside. Each defect matters in a crash rather than on the road: a loose front seat can increase injury, an improperly welded backrest matters in a rear-end impact, and badly welded guide sleeves mean worse neck and head protection for rear passengers. Volkswagen's guidance across all three is to keep driving normally and book the free inspection promptly. If a seat visibly shifts or a backrest feels loose, have the dealer look sooner.

What the free repair involves

Each one starts as an inspection. Dealers check the front seat tracks on 2021 Jetta and Tiguan models and install the missing bolts, inspect the guide sleeve welds on e-Golf and Golf R rear seat frames and replace the frame if needed, and inspect the 2020 front seats, replacing the seatback frames where welds are bad. No software or over-the-air fix is involved, and all work is free.

Common questions

Is my 2021 Jetta or Tiguan safe with missing seat bolts?

The notice does not tell owners to stop driving. The risk is that a loose front seat increases injury in a crash, so drive normally but schedule the dealer inspection, which installs the missing front seat track bolts free of charge.

Does the seat weld recall also cover Audi models?

Yes. The 2020 seat frame and backrest adjuster recall includes the Audi Q5 and SQ5 alongside the VW models, and the head restraint guide sleeve recall covers the Audi A3 Sedan and RS3 together with the e-Golf and Golf R.

Can I still carry passengers in the back seat?

Nothing in these notices restricts seating. The rear head restraint recall on the 2018 e-Golf and Golf R means rear passengers could suffer worse head and neck injuries in a crash, which is a reason to book the inspection promptly.
