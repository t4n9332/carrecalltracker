Volkswagen seat frame and head restraint recalls: which models?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Seats · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 1, 2021

All three of these recalls come down to how the seats were put together, and the fault is a weld or a bolt rather than the upholstery or the electrics. The 2018 e-Golf and Golf R, along with Audi A3 Sedan and RS3 models, were recalled because the rear head restraint guide sleeves may be incorrectly welded to the seat frame, which can leave the restraints less stable in a crash. The largest recall covers 2020 Atlas Cross Sport, Jetta, Jetta GLI and Tiguan and Audi Q5 and SQ5 plus the 2021 Atlas, where the front seat frame and backrest adjuster may not have been welded properly. The 2021 Jetta and Tiguan long wheelbase recall involves bolts missing from the front seat tracks.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A front seat that can move front and back without restriction — recall 72L6 says this happens on the 2021 Jetta and Tiguan if seat track bolts are missing.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park outside or to leave a seat unoccupied. Each notice states the risk in crash terms: reduced stability of the rear head restraints in the e-Golf and Golf R, a backrest adjustment that could break in a rear-end crash in the 2020 Atlas Cross Sport, Jetta, Tiguan, Q5 and SQ5 and the 2021 Atlas, and a front seat that can move without restriction in the 2021 Jetta and Tiguan. Beyond directing owners to the dealer, the notices give no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

All three repairs are free of charge. On the 2018 e-Golf and Golf R, dealers inspect the head restraint guide sleeves for incorrect welds and replace the rear seat frame if necessary. On the 2020 and 2021 vehicles they inspect the front seats and replace the seatback frames as necessary. On the 2021 Jetta and Tiguan they inspect the front seat tracks and install the bolts if they are missing.

Common questions

Is my 2020 Jetta covered by the front seat weld recall?

That recall covers 2020 Atlas Cross Sport, Jetta, Jetta GLI and Tiguan and Audi Q5 and SQ5, plus the 2021 Atlas, where the front seat frame and backrest adjuster may not have been welded properly. Dealers inspect the front seats and replace seatback frames as necessary, free.

Are Audi models part of these seat recalls?

Yes. Audi A3 Sedan and RS3 vehicles are in the head restraint guide sleeve recall, and Audi Q5 and SQ5 are in the 2020 front seat weld recall. Those notices give Audi owners a separate customer service number to call.

How would I know if my seat track bolts are missing?

The 2021 Jetta and Tiguan notice says the seat can move front and back without restriction if bolts are missing from the front seat tracks. It describes no other sign, and tells owners dealers will inspect the tracks and install the bolts as necessary.
