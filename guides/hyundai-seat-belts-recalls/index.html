Hyundai seat belt recalls: exploding pretensioners and buckles that fail to latch

12 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Seat Belts · model years 2020–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 14, 2026

Twelve Hyundai seat belt recalls split into two waves. Seven, filed between October 2021 and October 2022, cover front seat belt pretensioners that may explode upon deployment in a crash, running through the Accent, Elantra and Elantra HEV, Venue, Santa Fe Hybrid and Santa Fe Plug-In Hybrid, and Genesis GV80, GV70 and G80 Electric, mostly in 2020-2022 model years; several notices state they expand earlier recalls. The later recalls involve the belts themselves: rear seat belt assemblies on the 2024 Kona, buckles that may fail to latch across 2020-2025 Palisade, unsecured front retractors on the 2025 IONIQ 5, anchors that may detach on IONIQ 6, Genesis G90 and Santa Fe, and the Kona rear center seat buckle.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A seat belt buckle that fails to latch in the driver, passenger or second-row window seats (2020-2025 Palisade buckle recall, whose notice advises pulling on the belt to confirm it is fully secured).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these twelve notices tells owners to stop driving, and none carries a park-outside instruction. Two give interim advice for their own recall: the 2020-2025 Palisade buckle recall advises passengers to insert the belt firmly into the buckle with a quick and direct motion and pull on the belt to confirm it is fully secured until the remedy is performed, and the Kona rear center seat belt recall advises passengers not to use the rear center seat until the remedy is performed. The other notices give no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Remedies vary by recall. In the earlier pretensioner recalls dealers replace the seat belt pretensioners; in the later ones they secure the pretensioners with a cap. Elsewhere dealers inspect and replace the rear seat belt retractor as necessary on the 2024 Kona, replace buckle assemblies on Palisade and Kona, inspect and secure the 2025 IONIQ 5 retractors, and inspect and reinforce or replace the seat belt anchors. All free of charge.

Common questions

Which Hyundai models are in the exploding pretensioner recalls?

Across seven recalls filed from October 2021 to October 2022, Hyundai listed Accent, Elantra and Elantra HEV, Venue, Santa Fe Hybrid and Santa Fe Plug-In Hybrid, and Genesis GV80, GV70 and G80 Electric vehicles. Check your VIN with a dealer for the exact recall.

My Palisade seat belt sometimes will not latch. Is that the recall?

The 2020-2025 Palisade recall states the driver, passenger and second-row window seat belt buckles may fail to latch. Until the remedy, the notice advises inserting the belt firmly and pulling on it to confirm it is fully secured. Dealers replace the buckle assemblies free of charge.

Can passengers still use the Kona's rear center seat?

The recall notice for 2024-2025 Kona Electric and 2024-2026 Kona advises passengers not to use the rear center seat until the remedy is performed, because that buckle may fail to properly restrain an occupant during a crash. Dealers replace the buckle assembly free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
