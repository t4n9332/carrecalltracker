Hyundai Seat Belt Recalls Cover Pretensioners, Buckles, Anchors and Retractors

13 NHTSA recalls · Hyundai · Seat Belts · model years 2020–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 14, 2026

Exploding front seat belt pretensioners dominate this Hyundai group, appearing in seven notices dated October 2021 through October 2022. Accent coverage includes 2020-2022 vehicles, while Elantra and Elantra HEV coverage includes 2021-2022 vehicles. Venue pretensioner coverage includes 2020-2022 vehicles; another notice names 2021-2022 Santa Fe Hybrid and 2022-2023 Santa Fe Plug-In Hybrid vehicles. Later notices address different restraint problems: buckles that may fail to latch in 2020-2025 Palisades, unsecured retractors in certain 2025 Ioniq 5 vehicles, and a misleading belt display in certain 2026 Palisade models. Additional notices cover detachable anchors and rear center buckle restraint failures in specified Hyundai models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • September 12, 2025 Palisade buckle recall: driver, passenger and second-row window-seat buckles may fail to latch.
  • March 20, 2026 Palisade and Palisade Hybrid recall: the instrument panel may permanently display the third-row driver-side seat as belted.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these 13 notices instructs owners to stop driving or park outside. The September 12, 2025 Palisade buckle notice advises passengers to insert the belt firmly with a quick, direct motion and pull on it to confirm it is secured until repair. The July 14, 2026 Kona and Kona Electric buckle notice advises passengers not to use the rear center seat until repair. These seating and buckling instructions belong to those specific recalls; the remaining notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Pretensioner remedies specify replacement in four notices and securing with caps in three. Other recalls specify buckle replacement, retractor inspection and securing, or anchor inspection with reinforcement or replacement as necessary. The 2026 Palisade display recall specifies a wiring harness extension and seat belt assembly replacement as necessary. These notices state their remedies are free of charge.

Common questions

Which Hyundai models have the recurring pretensioner explosion problem?

The pretensioner notices include certain 2020-2022 Accents, 2021-2022 Elantras and Elantra HEVs, and 2020-2022 Venues. The October 2022 notice also names certain 2021-2022 Santa Fe Hybrids and 2022-2023 Santa Fe Plug-In Hybrids. Each notice specifies its own affected vehicles.

What do the Palisade seat belt notices tell passengers to do?

The September 2025 notice for certain 2020-2025 Palisades advises firm belt insertion with a quick, direct motion and a pull to confirm security until repair. The March 2026 notice concerns a misleading third-row belt display in certain 2026 Limited and Calligraphy vehicles.

Does the Kona buckle recall restrict use of a seat?

The July 14, 2026 notice advises passengers not to use the rear center seat until the remedy is performed. It covers certain 2024-2025 Kona Electric and 2024-2026 Kona vehicles. Dealers will replace the seat belt buckle assembly free of charge.
