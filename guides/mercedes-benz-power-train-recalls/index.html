Mercedes-Benz power train recalls: axles, driveshafts and rollaway risks

5 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Power Train · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 3, 2026

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

These five Mercedes-Benz recalls cover parts that deliver drive or hold the vehicle still. Two involve welds: an improperly welded camber strut bracket on the rear axle carrier of 2025 E 350, E 350 4MATIC, E 450 4MATIC and E 450 4MATIC station wagons, and an improperly welded spur gear in the front axle electric drivetrain of 2022 EQB 300 and EQB 350. Two involve drive shafts: securing bolts that may loosen at the transmission flange on 2017-2018 Metris vans, and a universal joint that may break on 2018 E 400 4MATIC, 2018-2020 Maybach S 560 4MATIC and S 450 4MATIC, and 2019-2020 E 450 4MATIC models. The fifth is a parking pawl guide bushing on 2019-2022 Sprinters with a manual parking brake and 7-speed automatic transmission.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One recall in this group carries a stop-driving instruction: the rear axle carrier weld recall covering 2025 E 350, E 350 4MATIC, E 450 4MATIC and E 450 4MATIC station wagons, where owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until the remedy has been performed. No notice tells owners to park outside. For the Metris driveshaft bolts, the Sprinter parking pawl, the EQB spur gear and the E-Class and S-Class drive shaft universal joint, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. That last recall's interim letters notified owners of the safety risk on May 22, 2026.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the rear axle carrier on the E-Class weld recall and the front axle electric drivetrain on the EQB recall. On the Metris, they replace the transmission flange and securing bolts and clean the shaft seal. The Sprinter parking pawl remedy is a software update to control units, and for the drive shaft universal joint dealers inspect and replace the drive shaft as necessary. All free of charge.

Common questions

What should I do if my 2025 E 350 is covered by the rear axle recall?

The notice advises owners not to drive their vehicles until the remedy has been performed. Dealers will replace the rear axle carrier free of charge, and owner notification letters were mailed January 22, 2025. Owners may contact MBUSA at 1-800-367-6372.

Why is a parking pawl problem fixed with a software update?

The notice says dealers will update the software of the electronic stability, transmission control, direct select module and electronic ignition switch control units, free of charge, for 2019-2022 Sprinters with a manual parking brake and 7-speed automatic transmission.

My E 450 4MATIC is in the drive shaft recall but no repair date was given.

The notice says interim letters about the safety risk were mailed May 22, 2026, and that additional letters will be sent once the remedy is available. Dealers will inspect and replace the drive shaft as necessary, free of charge.
