Why are Mercedes-Benz axles and driveshafts failing across five recalls?

5 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Power Train · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-03

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Across these five power train recalls, Mercedes-Benz repeatedly flags welding and assembly defects in axles and driveshafts rather than one shared design flaw. The 2025 E 350 and E 350 4MATIC have a rear axle carrier with an improperly welded camber strut bracket, the most serious case, carrying a stop-driving order. The 2022 EQB 300 and EQB 350 share a similar theme with a poorly welded front axle drivetrain gear. Separately, 2018 Metris vans have driveshaft bolts that can loosen, 2019-2020 Sprinter vans have a parking pawl bushing that can fatigue, and 2018-2020 E-Class and S-Class 4MATIC vehicles have a drive shaft universal joint that may break.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices do not describe warning signs to watch for before failure occurs.
  • Sprinter and EQB vehicles could roll away on their own even with the parking brake set.
  • E-Class, S-Class, and Metris vehicles could suddenly lose the ability to drive forward.
  • The E 350 rear axle defect could cause a sudden loss of handling and control while driving.

Is it safe to keep driving?

Of these five recalls, only the 2024 recall for the 2025 E 350 and E 350 4MATIC rear axle carrier tells owners not to drive the vehicle until the repair is completed. None of the recalls instruct owners to park outside. For the Metris driveshaft bolts, Sprinter parking pawl, EQB front axle gear, and E-Class/S-Class drive shaft joint recalls, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving; they describe increased crash risk from loss of control, rollaway, or loss of drive power.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the affected part in most of these recalls: the rear axle carrier, the front axle electric drivetrain, and the drive shaft as needed. For the Metris, dealers replace the transmission flange and securing bolts. For the Sprinter parking pawl, dealers update software in several control units instead of replacing hardware. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

How many Mercedes-Benz power train recalls are in this group, and which is most serious?

There are five recalls covering axle and driveshaft defects. The most serious is the 2025 E 350 and E 350 4MATIC rear axle carrier recall, which tells owners not to drive until the repair is completed.

Can I keep driving my Sprinter with the parking pawl recall?

The notice does not tell owners to stop driving. It says the parking pawl guide bushing may fatigue and fail to engage, which can let the vehicle roll away, so dealers update software free of charge.

What's wrong with the EQB 300 and 350 front axle?

The welded spur gear inside the front axle electric drivetrain may not have been welded properly. If it fails, the vehicle could roll away on its own, so dealers replace the front axle drivetrain free of charge.
