BMW Seat Belt Recalls Cover Retractors, Sensors, and Missing Warnings

8 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 17, 2025

BMW seat belt recalls repeatedly involve retractors and buckle sensing, alongside attachment and warning-system defects. Certain 2018 4 Series convertibles and M4 convertibles have incompatible front retractor locking mechanism housings. A July 2021 notice covers passenger retractor problems across numerous BMW models, including the X1, Z4, and 3 Series. Certain 2025-2026 X1 and X2 vehicles appear in the September 2025 front retractor notice. Sensor issues differ between notices: the March 2020 recall describes belted occupants detected as unbelted, while the May 2024 SUV recall describes passengers incorrectly detected as belted. The 2023 X1 also has a separate second-row belt pinching issue.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2024-05-15 BMW SUVs May Have Seat Belt Sensor Defect — the seat belt warning light may not illuminate for an unbelted passenger.
  • 2023-08-16 BMW seat belt warning chime may not sound — the audible reminder to fasten the driver's seat belt may fail.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the supplied BMW seat belt notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. The 2018 convertible locking mechanism, 2023 X1 pinched belt, 2024 SUV sensor, and 2025 retractor notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The March 2020 buckle sensor notice and April 2021 attachment bolt notice also carry no stop-driving instruction. Neither the July 2021 passenger retractor notice nor the August 2023 audible seat belt warning notice tells owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies vary by notice. Dealers inspect and conditionally replace front belt assemblies, buckles, lower belts, or retractors under the applicable recalls. The September 2025 recall specifies replacing both front retractors free of charge. The 2023 X1 remedy is free trim-panel inspection and adjustment as necessary. The warning-chime recall provides a free receiver audio module software update.

Common questions

Why might the BMW seat belt warning chime fail?

The August 2023 notice identifies receiver audio module software that may fail to generate the driver's audible seat belt warning properly. It covers specified 3 Series, 4 Series, and 228i xDrive Gran Coupe vehicles. Dealers update that software free of charge.

How do the two seat belt sensor recalls differ?

The March 2020 notice describes buckle sensors detecting a belted occupant as unbelted, potentially affecting airbags and pretensioners. The May 2024 SUV notice describes passengers incorrectly detected as belted, potentially suppressing the warning light and affecting supplemental restraint deployment.

What is the repair for pinched second-row belts in the X1?

The April 2023 notice covers certain 2023 X1 xDrive28i and X1 M35i vehicles. It says an interior trim panel may pinch the second-row outboard seat belts. Dealers inspect and adjust the interior trim panel as necessary, free of charge.
