Why Does BMW Keep Recalling Seat Belts On X-Series SUVs?

8 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-09-17

BMW's seat belt recalls in this group cluster around two repeating problems. Several campaigns involve sensors or retractors that don't work as intended — buckle sensors that misread a belted occupant as unbelted in 2020 and 2024, and retractor defects in 2021 and 2025 — which can affect how airbags and pretensioners deploy in a crash. Other recalls stem from assembly mistakes: an improperly tightened seat belt bolt in 2021, a seat belt pinched by a trim panel in 2023, and an incompatible locking mechanism on the 2018 M4. A software bug also silenced the seat belt warning chime in 2023. The M340i appears in three separate recalls, and X-series SUVs recur across most of the others.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A seat belt warning light that may not turn on when a seat belt is unbuckled (2024 recall)
  • A seat belt warning chime that may not sound when a seat belt is unfastened (2023 recall)
  • No other warning sign is described — the buckle sensor, retractor, bolt, and locking mechanism defects in the rest of this group are not something an owner would notice before a crash

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these eight recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving, whether they describe a seat belt that may not lock or restrain properly in a crash, a seat belt warning light or chime that may fail to alert the driver, or a retractor that may not work as intended. Owners are directed to have the dealer perform the free repair or inspection described in the applicable notice.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and, when necessary, replace the affected part: seat belt buckles, retractors, locking mechanisms, or trim panels pinching a belt; tighten or replace an under-torqued mounting bolt; or update the receiver audio module software to restore the warning chime. All of these inspections, part replacements, and software updates are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why do so many BMW recalls involve seat belt sensors?

Several separate campaigns, in 2020 and 2024, found that the front seat belt buckle sensors could misread a belted occupant as unbelted, which can affect how the airbags and pretensioners deploy in a crash. Each recall covers different model years and trims.

Does the seat belt warning chime recall affect the airbags?

No. The 2023 chime recall involves only the receiver audio module software failing to sound an audible seat belt reminder for the driver; it does not describe any effect on airbag deployment.

Are MINI and Toyota Supra models included in any BMW seat belt recall?

Yes. The 2021 seat belt retractor recall and the 2025 torsion bar recall both include MINI models, and the 2021 retractor recall also includes the Toyota Supra, since BMW issued that recall covering all three brands together.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
