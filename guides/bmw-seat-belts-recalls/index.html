Which BMW seat belt recalls cover retractors, buckles and warning chimes?

8 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 17, 2025

Seat belt recalls for BMW cluster around two themes. The first is hardware that may not restrain an occupant: emergency locking retractors on 2018 430i, 440i and M4 convertibles, a seat belt bolt not tightened to specification on 2021 330i, M340i and M3 sedans, retractors across a wide 2021 range shared with MINI and the Toyota Supra, and damaged retractor torsion bars on 2025-2026 X1 and X2 models and several MINI models. The second theme is detection and warning: buckle sensors on 2020 X3, X4 and 330i models, a belt detection fault on the 2024 XM and 2025 X5, X6 and X7, and audio module software on 330i and M340i models that may not sound the chime.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The seat belt warning light may not illuminate even when an occupant is unbelted, stated in the 2024 XM and 2025 X5, X6 and X7 seat belt detection recall.
  • The audible seat belt warning may not sound to prompt the driver to buckle up, stated in the receiver audio module software recall covering 2019-2022 330i and M340i models.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the eight seat belt notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That applies to the retractor, buckle and seat belt bolt recalls, and to the two that BMW describes as failing to meet Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 208: the belt detection fault on the 2024 XM and 2025 X5, X6 and X7, and the missing audible chime on 330i, M340i, 430i, M440i and 228i xDrive Gran Coupe models. The notices set out the injury risk in a crash and point owners to a dealer.

What the free repair involves

Most of these recalls start with an inspection. Dealers inspect and, where needed, replace front seat belt assemblies, buckles, retractors or the lower front belts. On the 2025-2026 X1 and X2 recall both front retractors are replaced outright, the 2023 X1 recall adjusts an interior trim panel, and the chime recall is a receiver audio module software update. Every notice says the work is free of charge.

Common questions

My BMW seat belt warning light never comes on. Is that covered by a recall?

Two notices describe warning failures. The 2024 XM and 2025 X5, X6 and X7 recall says the system may read passengers as belted, so the light does not illuminate. A separate recall says the audible chime may not sound on certain 330i and M340i models.

Do these BMW seat belt recalls cover rear seats too?

Mostly front seats. The 2023 X1 recall is the exception: it covers the second-row outboard seat belts, which the notice says may have been pinched by an interior trim panel. The other notices name front seat belt assemblies, buckles, retractors and lower belts.

Why do MINI and Toyota Supra models appear in a BMW seat belt recall?

BMW of North America filed both notices. The July 2021 retractor recall lists 2021 MINI and Toyota Supra models with BMW models, and the 2025 retractor torsion bar recall lists MINI Cooper, MINI Cooper Convertible and MINI Countryman alongside the X1 and X2.
