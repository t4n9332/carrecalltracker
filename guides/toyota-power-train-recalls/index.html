Toyota Axle, Driveshaft, and Transmission Recalls: What's the Risk?

7 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Power Train · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-10-01

These seven recalls center on axles, driveshafts, and transmissions across Toyota trucks and the Supra. Loose or improperly secured axle fasteners appear three times: the C-HR's rear axle hub bearing bolts, the Tundra's rear axle flange nuts, and the Tacoma's rear axle shaft, each of which can let a wheel or axle shaft separate. A related 2025 Tacoma recall covers a front driveshaft joint that can deform and break. The Tundra and Sequoia Hybrid share a transmission-control software recall for unexpected movement in neutral, while the Supra, built with BMW, shares a transmission control unit oil-leak recall with several BMW models. A separate 2018 recall covers a torque converter defect in Corolla hatchbacks with CVT transmissions.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The vehicle moving on its own after being shifted into neutral without the brake applied (Tundra, Sequoia Hybrid)
  • A stall while driving at high load or high speed (Corolla CVT)
  • Harder steering if the front driveshaft breaks (Tacoma)
  • A sudden loss of drive power (Supra, shared BMW transmission recall)
  • The axle-bolt and axle-shaft recalls (C-HR, Tundra, Tacoma rear axle) describe no warning sign before the fasteners loosen or the axle separates

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven power-train recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving, not the C-HR, Tundra, or Tacoma rear-axle recalls, the Tacoma front-driveshaft recall, the Supra's shared BMW transmission control unit recall, the Tundra and Sequoia Hybrid unexpected-movement-in-neutral recall, or the Corolla torque-converter recall. Several of these notices say the underlying defect can cause a stall, unexpected movement, or a loss of vehicle stability, increasing the risk of a crash.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the rear axle assembly and tighten or replace axle bolts, nuts, or shafts; inspect and replace the front driveshaft assemblies; replace the transmission mechatronics module and fluid; update the transmission control ECU software; and replace the CVT and torque converter on affected Corolla models. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why do so many Tundra and Tacoma recalls involve the rear axle?

Separate recalls found the C-HR's, Tundra's, and Tacoma's rear axle fasteners could loosen or the axle shaft could separate from the housing, each raising the risk of a crash. Dealers tighten or replace the affected parts, free of charge.

Is the Supra transmission recall the same defect as the BMW recall?

Yes. The Supra is built by BMW and shares the same transmission control unit, so BMW issued one recall in 2023 covering both the Supra and several BMW models for a transmission-fluid leak that can seize the gears.

Can my Tundra roll away in neutral because of this recall?

The 2024 Tundra and Sequoia Hybrid recall says unexpected vehicle movement may occur when the vehicle is in neutral and the brakes are not applied. Dealers update the transmission control ECU software free of charge.
