What do Toyota's axle, driveshaft and transmission recalls cover?

7 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Power Train · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-10-01

Rear axle hardware is the thread running through this group. Three recalls describe fasteners: rear axle hub bearing bolts on the 2019 C-HR, axle shaft flange nuts on the 2022 Tundra and Tundra Hybrid, and axle retaining fasteners on the 2022-2023 Tacoma, with the two truck notices warning of axle shaft separation. Tundra appears again in a 2022-2024 Tundra, Tundra Hybrid and 2023-2024 Sequoia Hybrid recall over unexpected movement in neutral. The remaining three are drivetrain failures on quite different vehicles: a 2025 Tacoma front driveshaft CV joint, a 2023 Supra transmission control unit shared with several BMW models, and a 2019 Corolla hatchback CVT torque converter.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Unexpected vehicle movement when the vehicle is in neutral and the brakes are not applied — 2022-2024 Tundra and 2023-2024 Sequoia Hybrid recall.
  • A vehicle stall, especially when driven under high load — 2019 Corolla hatchback torque converter recall.
  • A sudden loss of drive power — 2023 Supra transmission control unit recall.
  • Impaired steering, and on full-time 4WD models a rollaway while parked without the parking brake applied — 2025 Tacoma driveshaft recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The notices state consequences instead. The 2022 Tundra and 2022-2023 Tacoma notices say axle shaft separation can cause a loss of vehicle stability and brake performance. The 2019 C-HR notice says loosening bolts can damage rear brake components or cause a rear wheel to detach. The 2025 Tacoma notice says a broken driveshaft can impair steering, and that full-time 4WD vehicles may roll away while parked without the parking brake applied.

What the free repair involves

Remedies vary. Dealers inspect the C-HR rear axle hub bearing bolts and replace the affected assemblies if one is loose or detached, inspect the Tundra and Tacoma rear axles and tighten the nuts, replace damaged components, inspect and replace the 2025 Tacoma front driveshafts as necessary, replace the Supra mechatronics module and fluid, update the transmission control ECU software, and replace the Corolla CVT and torque converter. Free of charge.

Common questions

Why does the Tundra show up in several power train recalls?

Two of these recalls cover the 2022 Tundra and Tundra Hybrid: axle shaft flange nuts that can loosen and let the axle shaft separate, and a 2022-2024 recall covering unexpected vehicle movement in neutral, which also includes the 2023-2024 Sequoia Hybrid.

Is the Supra transmission recall a Toyota or BMW campaign?

BMW of North America filed it, covering the 2023 Toyota GR Supra alongside BMW models including the X3, X4, X5 and X6. The notice says transmission oil leaking into the control unit can lead to gear seizure, and lists BMW customer service for owners.

What does the 2025 Tacoma driveshaft recall say about parking?

The notice says the front driveshaft CV joint may deform and break, and that vehicles with full-time 4WD may experience a rollaway while parked without the parking brake applied. Dealers inspect and replace both front driveshaft assemblies as necessary, free of charge.
