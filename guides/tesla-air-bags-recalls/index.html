Which Tesla air bag recalls affect Model 3, Model S and X?

6 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · AIR Bags · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 19, 2025

Six Tesla air bag recalls cluster around a few repeated problems: driver air bags that tear during deployment and side curtain air bags that may not deploy as designed. Tearing is the theme for the Model S and Model X — SB-21-20-007 covers 2021 Model X and Model S, and SB-24-20-001 covers 2021-2025 Model S and Model X. On the curtain side, SB-21-20-006 covers 2020-2021 and 2023 Model 3 vehicles whose curtain air bag was improperly secured to the roof rail and could be twisted, while SB-22-20-001 covers 2021-2022 Model S vehicles with incorrectly assembled curtain inflators. SB-24-13-001 involves a single 2024 Model 3 missing a side air bag deflector, and SB-25-20-001 covers 2021-2024 Model S and 2021-2025 Model X air bags fitted with the wrong horn pad.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The horn does not sound, because an air bag with the incorrect horn pad may have been installed (recall SB-25-20-001).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. The notices describe the risk in crash terms: a twisted curtain air bag (SB-21-20-006), a torn driver's air bag (SB-21-20-007 and SB-24-20-001), an incorrectly assembled curtain inflator (SB-22-20-001) or a missing deflector (SB-24-13-001) may not protect occupants properly in a crash. SB-25-20-001 describes a horn that does not sound, which the notice says may prevent the driver from alerting other drivers. Beyond that, the notices give owners no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Remedies are split between inspection and replacement, all free of charge. For SB-21-20-006 Tesla inspects and realigns the left and right side curtain air bags as necessary. SB-21-20-007 replaces the driver's air bag; SB-22-20-001 replaces the side curtain air bags; SB-24-20-001 replaces the air bag assembly; SB-24-13-001 replaced the side air bag. SB-25-20-001 inspects and installs an air bag with the correct horn pad as necessary.

Common questions

My Model S horn does not work — is that one of these recalls?

Recall SB-25-20-001 covers 2021-2024 Model S and 2021-2025 Model X vehicles on which an air bag with the incorrect horn pad may have been installed, preventing the horn from sounding. Tesla inspects and installs an air bag with the correct horn pad as necessary, free of charge.

Can I tell whether my air bag is affected just by looking at it?

The notices do not describe anything an owner would see or feel, apart from SB-25-20-001, where the horn does not sound when the wrong horn pad was installed. Affected vehicles are identified by VIN, so owners can contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752 to check a specific car.

How many cars does the 2024 Model 3 deflector recall cover?

The notice for SB-24-13-001 states that Tesla is recalling one 2024 Model 3 vehicle, whose driver seat side air bag module was assembled without a deflector that directs gas flow during deployment. The notice says that vehicle was repaired April 12, 2024, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
