Why does Tesla keep recalling airbags in Model S and Model 3?

6 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · AIR Bags · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-03-19

These six Tesla airbag recalls span Model 3 and Model S vehicles from 2020 to 2025, and no two share the same defect. On 2020-2021 and 2023 Model 3 vehicles, side curtain airbags could be twisted from improper roof-rail attachment. On 2021 Model S vehicles, the driver's airbag could tear during deployment — a defect recalled twice, once in 2021 and again in 2024. Also on 2021 Model S vehicles, side curtain airbag inflators could be assembled incorrectly, and a separate 2021 Model S recall found an airbag with the wrong horn pad could block the horn. One individual 2024 Model 3 was missing a deflector inside its side airbag module.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2021 Model S (horn pad recall): the horn does not sound when pressed
  • 2020-2021, 2023 Model 3 side curtain airbag recall: no warning sign is described
  • 2021 Model S driver airbag tear recalls (2021 and 2024): no warning sign is described
  • 2021 Model S side curtain inflator recall and the 2024 Model 3 deflector recall: no warning sign is described

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six Tesla airbag recalls instruct owners to stop driving or park outside. Each notice describes a way an airbag could fail to protect an occupant properly in a crash — a twisted or incorrectly assembled side curtain airbag, a driver airbag that could tear during deployment, a side airbag missing an internal deflector, or an airbag with the wrong horn pad that could silence the horn — but none of the six notices tells owners to stop driving before the vehicle is repaired.

What the free repair involves

Tesla Service Centers inspect and repair or replace the affected airbag component for each recall — realigning or replacing side curtain airbags, replacing driver airbags, or installing an airbag with the correct horn pad — free of charge. The affected 2024 Model 3 with the missing deflector was already repaired before its notice was issued.

Common questions

Why has Tesla recalled the Model S driver airbag twice?

The 2021 and 2024 recalls both describe the driver's airbag possibly tearing during deployment on 2021 Model S vehicles. The notices do not state whether the later recall stems from a repeat of the same defect or a new batch of parts.

Does the horn recall mean my airbag won't deploy?

No. That 2021 Model S recall involves an airbag assembled with the wrong horn pad, which can prevent the horn from sounding when pressed. The notice does not describe any effect on airbag deployment itself.

How many vehicles are affected by the 2024 Model 3 deflector recall?

The notice describes the recall as covering one 2024 Model 3 vehicle, whose side airbag module was assembled without a deflector that directs gas flow during deployment. It was repaired before the notice was issued.
