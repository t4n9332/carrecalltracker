Why do so many Jeep models have transmission and clutch recalls?

17 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Power Train · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-30

These 12 recalls cover three recurring problems. Wrangler and Gladiator manual-transmission models were recalled three times — 2020, 2021, and 2023 — because the clutch pressure plate can overheat and fracture; the 2021 fix added software limiting engine torque near overheating, and 2023 replaced the clutch assembly. The Cherokee shows up for separate transmission faults: a 2019 model-year welding defect, a 2018 model-year halfshaft bearing that can break, and a 2019 model-year calibration issue that can stall the 2.4L engine; a 2021 Cherokee recall covers a leaking transmission oil cooler hose. The 2019 Grand Cherokee and Durango share a worn front differential recall, and the 2020 Gladiator was recalled for an ungreased rear driveshaft. A 2018 park lock rod recall also touched the Wrangler and Grand Cherokee.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Burning smell, smoke, or odor from an overheating clutch (Wrangler/Gladiator manual transmissions)
  • Sudden engine stall or loss of drive power while driving
  • Vehicle moves or rolls even when shifted into Park
  • A transmission fluid leak or smell near the transmission oil cooler hose (Cherokee)
  • Several recalls in this group — the transmission weld, halfshaft bearing, park lock rod, and differential issues — describe no driver-noticeable warning sign

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. That includes the clutch-overheating recalls (2020, 2021, 2023) on manual-transmission Wrangler and Gladiator models, and the separate Cherokee transmission weld, halfshaft bearing, calibration-stall, and oil cooler hose recalls, plus the Grand Cherokee/Durango differential recall, the Gladiator driveshaft and transmission-park recalls, the 2018 park lock rod recall, and the 2024 software stall recall. Each notice describes a safety risk — fire, loss of drive power, or unintended movement — but none tells owners to stop driving or park outside.

What the free repair involves

Dealers address each cause directly and at no cost to the owner: replacing or updating the clutch assembly (including a torque-limiting software update), rerouting wiring harnesses, replacing transmissions, halfshafts, differentials, driveshafts, and park lock rods, and reflashing or updating transmission and powertrain control module software. All repairs described in these notices are free of charge.

Common questions

Is it safe to keep driving a Jeep with a clutch overheating recall?

The notices for the 2020, 2021, and 2023 clutch recalls describe a risk of fire and crash from an overheated clutch, but they do not instruct owners to stop driving. Dealers replace the clutch assembly and update software at no cost.

Why has the Jeep Wrangler clutch been recalled three times?

FCA issued clutch pressure plate recalls in 2020, 2021, and 2023 for overlapping Wrangler and Gladiator model years. Each expanded the covered vehicles or changed the remedy, ending with a full clutch assembly replacement and software update in 2023.

What transmission problems has the Jeep Cherokee had?

The Cherokee has been recalled for several distinct transmission faults: a 2019 model-year weld issue, a 2018 model-year halfshaft bearing that could break, a 2019 model-year calibration issue that could stall the engine, and in 2021 a transmission oil cooler hose that could leak fluid.
