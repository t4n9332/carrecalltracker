Why do Audi brake recalls keep hitting the Q5 and E-Tron?

8 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 15, 2026

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Audi's hydraulic brake recalls cluster in two places. The Q5 and SQ5 appear twice — 2018 cars share a brake caliper piston coating problem with several Volkswagen models, and 2018-2019 cars were recalled for a brake master cylinder casting that may be too short. The E-Tron accounts for four: an improperly welded rotor and shaft inside the integrated brake system on 2020-2021 cars, brake booster corrosion on 2021 cars, and two recalls covering a brake pedal fastener that can let the pedal detach from the booster, one listing 2019 E-Tron and one listing 2020-2021 E-Tron Sportback. The rest stand alone: the 2020 Q3 brake pedal plate, and an incorrectly labeled brake fluid cap on 2019-2021 A6 and A7 and 2021 S7 models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Audi's 2021 E-Tron brake booster corrosion recall (47R7) says the ABS, electronic stability control, brake assist and the speedometer display may stop functioning.
  • The same 47R7 notice says a loss of brake assist, ABS and ESC will increase the effort and distance required to stop.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One notice here tells owners to stop driving: the June 2018 recall of 2018 Q5 and SQ5 vehicles for brake caliper pistons with insufficient coating, where owners are advised not to drive until the brake system has been checked. The other seven notices — the Q5 and SQ5 master cylinder, the Q3 brake pedal plate, the two E-Tron brake pedal fastener recalls, the E-Tron rotor and shaft and brake booster corrosion recalls, and the brake fluid cap recall — do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls dealers bleed the rear brake calipers, inspect the master cylinder and replace it as necessary, replace the brake booster on two E-Tron recalls, inspect and repair the booster or tighten the pushrod screw joint on the pedal fastener recalls, replace the Q3 brake pedal, and inspect or replace the brake fluid cap. Seven of the eight notices say the work is free.

Common questions

Which Audi models appear most in these brake recalls?

The Q5 and SQ5 appear in the 2018 caliper piston recall and the 2018-2019 master cylinder recall. The E-Tron and E-Tron Sportback appear in four notices, covering rotor and shaft separation, brake booster corrosion and two brake pedal fastener recalls.

Do any of these recalls tell owners to stop driving?

Yes, one. The 2018 brake caliper piston recall covering 2018 Q5 and SQ5 vehicles says owners are advised not to drive until the brake system has been checked. The other seven notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What is wrong with the brake fluid cap on the A6 and A7?

The 2023 notice covering 2019-2021 A6 and A7 and 2021 S7, S6 Sedan, RS7 and RS6 Avant models says an incorrectly labeled brake fluid cap may lead to the wrong brake fluid being used, which can reduce braking ability. Dealers inspect the cap and replace it if necessary, free.
