Toyota Backup Camera Problems: Blank Screens, Freezing, and Quiet Warnings

7 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Back Over Prevention · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 31, 2026

Missing rearview images dominate Toyota's back-over prevention recalls. Tundra and Tundra Hybrid appear repeatedly: the October 2025 notice covers 2022-2025 models, while the January 2026 notice covers certain 2024-2025 models with a Panoramic View Monitor. The October 2025 notice also includes 2023-2025 Sequoia Hybrid vehicles. Other camera problems include a damaged connector in 2019 RAV4 and RAV4 Hybrid vehicles, delayed images in certain 2023 Mirai vehicles, and freezing or blank screens in the October 2025 multi-model PVM recall. The 2020 Supra appears in the September 2019 camera recall. A different reversing concern affects 2023-2025 Corolla Cross Hybrid vehicles: insufficient pedestrian warning sound.

Warning signs to watch for

  • August 2019 RAV4 recall K0N: the back-up camera may not activate in reverse.
  • February 2024 Mirai recall 24TA01: the rearview image may not display immediately after shifting into reverse.
  • October 2025 PVM recall 25TB13: the rearview camera may freeze or display a blank screen in reverse.
  • January 2026 Tundra recall 26TB02/26TA02: the rearview camera image may not display in reverse.
  • March 2026 Corolla Cross Hybrid recall 26TB08/26TA08: pedestrian warning sounds may be insufficient when reversing.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The supplied notices contain zero stop-driving instructions and zero park-outside instructions. The September 2019 Supra camera notice and August 2019 RAV4 camera notice do not tell owners to stop driving. Neither do the February 2024 Mirai, October 1, 2025 Tundra and Sequoia Hybrid, October 30, 2025 multi-model PVM, or January 2026 Tundra notices. The March 2026 Corolla Cross Hybrid pedestrian warning sound notice also does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies differ by recall. The 2019 Supra gets back-up camera software; RAV4 K0N receives inspection and an audio display replacement if necessary. Mirai 24TA01 and Tundra 26TB02/26TA02 receive parking-assist ECU updates. The October 1, 2025 recall updates multimedia display software; October 30 updates parking-assist software. Corolla Cross Hybrid 26TB08/26TA08 updates pedestrian warning sound software.

Common questions

Which Toyota Tundra backup camera recalls are listed?

The October 1, 2025 notice covers certain 2022-2025 Tundra and Tundra Hybrid vehicles and specifies a multimedia display software update. The January 23, 2026 notice covers certain 2024-2025 versions equipped with PVM and specifies a parking-assist ECU software update.

Why might my Toyota rearview camera freeze or show a blank screen?

The October 30, 2025 notice describes a software error that may freeze the rearview camera or produce a blank screen in reverse. It covers certain Toyota, Lexus, and Subaru Solterra vehicles equipped with PVM; the notice references a complete model list.

What is the Corolla Cross Hybrid reversing sound recall?

The March 2026 notice covers certain 2023-2025 Corolla Cross Hybrid vehicles that may produce insufficient pedestrian warning sound in reverse. Pedestrians may not be alerted to the reversing vehicle. Dealers update the pedestrian warning sound software free of charge.
