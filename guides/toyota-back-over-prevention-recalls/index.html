Why do so many Toyota rearview cameras fail to display?

7 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Back Over Prevention · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-31

Most of these Toyota and Lexus recalls share one problem: a software error that keeps the backup camera image from displaying in reverse. It shows up repeatedly on vehicles with a Panoramic View Monitor system: the 2023 Mirai saw a delayed image, a separate 2025 recall covering 2022-2025 Tundra and 2023-2025 Sequoia Hybrid lost the image entirely, a 2026 recall repeated that on 2024-2025 Tundra, and a broad 2022-2026 Toyota, Lexus, and Subaru Solterra recall saw the camera freeze or go blank. Two recalls differ: a 2019 RAV4 connector fault, and a 2020 Toyota Supra recall issued jointly with BMW. The 2023-2024 Corolla Cross Hybrid recall instead covers a pedestrian warning sound that's too quiet.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rearview camera image doesn't appear, is delayed, or disappears when the vehicle is shifted into reverse (several Toyota/Lexus recalls).
  • Rearview camera freezes or shows a blank screen in reverse (2025 multi-model Panoramic View Monitor recall).
  • Back-up camera fails to turn on at all when reversing (2019 RAV4, damaged connector).
  • Backup warning sound for pedestrians is too quiet when reversing (2023-2024 Corolla Cross Hybrid).

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven Toyota back-over prevention recalls include a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This covers the 2020 Supra camera recall shared with BMW, the 2019 RAV4 connector recall, the Mirai and other Panoramic View Monitor display recalls from 2024 through 2026, the two Tundra rearview recalls, the broad 2025 freeze/blank-screen recall, and the Corolla Cross Hybrid warning-sound recall. Each notice directs owners to a free dealer or software repair rather than telling them to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, Toyota (and BMW, for the shared Supra recall) dealers update the parking-assist, multimedia, back-up camera, or pedestrian warning sound software, free of charge. The 2019 RAV4 recall instead has dealers replace the audio display unit if needed. None of these updates are delivered over-the-air; each requires a dealer visit.

Common questions

Why do so many Toyota and Lexus rearview cameras have recalls?

Most of these recalls trace to a software error that keeps the rearview camera image from displaying, freezing, or going blank when the vehicle is in reverse. They affect different models across several years, including repeat issues on the Tundra.

Is the Corolla Cross Hybrid recall about the camera too?

No. The 2023-2024 Corolla Cross Hybrid recall in this group is about an insufficient pedestrian warning sound when the vehicle is in reverse, not the rearview camera. Dealers update the warning sound software free of charge.

Why is a Toyota Supra recall linked to BMW?

The 2020 Toyota Supra shares its backup camera software with related BMW models, so both automakers issued a joint recall when the camera image could vanish in reverse. Dealers update the back-up camera software free of charge.
