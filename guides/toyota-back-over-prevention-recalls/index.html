Toyota rearview camera recalls: blank screens and a quiet reverse alert

7 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Back Over Prevention · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 31, 2026

Six of these seven recalls are about the rearview image itself, and five of those are fixed with software. Toyota's Panoramic View Monitor system is the common thread: it appears in the 2023 Mirai and Lexus recall, the 2022-2026 Toyota, Lexus and Subaru Solterra recall where the camera may freeze or go blank, and the 2024-2025 Tundra recall. Tundra and Tundra Hybrid are named twice, once with Sequoia Hybrid for 2022-2025. The outliers are a 2019 RAV4 recall where a damaged electrical connector may keep the camera from activating in reverse, a BMW recall that includes the 2020 Supra, and a 2023-2025 Corolla Cross Hybrid recall about reverse pedestrian warning sounds rather than the camera.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2022-2026 Panoramic View Monitor recall says the rearview camera may freeze or display a blank screen when the vehicle is in reverse.
  • The 2023 Mirai and Lexus recall says the rearview camera image may not immediately display within a couple of seconds after the shifter is placed into reverse.
  • The 2019 RAV4 recall says the back-up camera may not activate when the vehicle is in reverse.
  • The 2023-2025 Corolla Cross Hybrid recall says the vehicles may fail to make sufficient pedestrian warning sounds when in reverse.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That covers the 2019 RAV4 connector recall, the 2023 Mirai and Lexus software recall, the 2022-2025 Tundra and Sequoia Hybrid recall, the 2022-2026 Panoramic View Monitor recall, the 2024-2025 Tundra recall, the Corolla Cross Hybrid pedestrian sound recall, and the BMW recall covering the 2020 Supra. Each states a crash or injury risk and then moves straight to the dealer remedy and the owner-letter date.

What the free repair involves

All this work is free of charge. Most remedies are software updates: parking assist ECU software (2023 Mirai and Lexus, 2024-2025 Tundra), multimedia display software (2022-2025 Tundra and Sequoia Hybrid), parking assist software (Panoramic View Monitor recall), and pedestrian warning sound software (Corolla Cross Hybrid). For the 2019 RAV4, dealers inspect the camera and replace the audio display unit if necessary; BMW dealers update the Supra's back-up camera software.

Common questions

Is my Tundra covered by more than one camera recall?

Two recalls name Tundra and Tundra Hybrid: one covers 2022-2025 Tundra, Tundra Hybrid and 2023-2025 Sequoia Hybrid with a multimedia display software update, the other covers 2024-2025 Tundra and Tundra Hybrid with a Panoramic View Monitor and a parking assist ECU software update.

Why is a Toyota Supra listed under a BMW recall?

The notice is BMW's, covering many BMW models and the 2020 Toyota Supra, where the back-up camera display may lack an image. BMW dealers update the back-up camera software free of charge, and the notice gives Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371 for Supra owners.

The Corolla Cross Hybrid recall is not about the camera — what is it?

That notice says 2023-2025 Corolla Cross Hybrid vehicles may fail to make sufficient pedestrian warning sounds in reverse, failing FMVSS 141 on minimum sound requirements for hybrid and electric vehicles. Dealers update the pedestrian warning sound software free of charge.
