Why has Volkswagen recalled the Atlas for steering knuckle failures twice?

5 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-08-13

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

These five Volkswagen steering recalls center on the Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport, which were recalled twice for steering knuckle problems with different causes. In November 2020, Volkswagen recalled 2020 Atlas Cross Sport and 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles because steering knuckles may have been damaged during production; this is the only recall in the group with a stop-driving instruction. In January 2021, the same 2020-2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport population was recalled again because the front steering knuckle may fracture near the strut mounting, a separate defect. Other recalls cover a 2018 Golf and Golf Sportwagen ignition key that can be removed without shifting to Park, a 2021 ID.4 steering gear bushing, and a 2025 Tiguan rear tie rod bolt.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Key can be removed from the ignition while the shifter is not in Park (2018 Golf, Golf Sportwagen)
  • The notices describe no driver-noticeable warning before a steering knuckle is damaged or fractures
  • The notices describe no warning sign for a loose tie rod bolt or faulty steering gear bushing

Is it safe to keep driving?

One of these five recalls carries a stop-driving instruction: the November 2020 recall of 2020 Atlas Cross Sport and 2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles for steering knuckles possibly damaged during production, which tells owners not to drive until the vehicle is inspected and repaired. The other four recalls — the January 2021 Atlas/Atlas Cross Sport knuckle fracture recall, the 2018 Golf/Golf Sportwagen key recall, the 2021 ID.4 steering gear recall, and the 2025 Tiguan tie rod recall — do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and, when necessary, replace the affected steering knuckles, install an additional switch and circuit board to fix the key-removal issue, replace the steering gear on the ID.4, and replace the rear tie rod bolt on the Tiguan. All of these repairs are performed free of charge, and none involve a software or over-the-air update.

Common questions

Do I need to stop driving my Atlas because of a steering knuckle recall?

Only the November 2020 recall of 2020-2021 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles for knuckles damaged during production tells owners not to drive until inspected. The January 2021 Atlas knuckle-fracture recall does not tell owners to stop driving.

Why was the Atlas recalled for steering knuckles twice?

The two recalls cover different defects: the November 2020 recall addresses knuckles damaged during production, while the January 2021 recall addresses a separate risk of the front steering knuckle fracturing near the strut mounting.

What did Volkswagen do about the Golf key removal issue?

For 2018 Golf and Golf Sportwagen vehicles where the key could be removed without shifting to Park, dealers installed an additional switch and circuit board, free of charge, to correct the fault.
