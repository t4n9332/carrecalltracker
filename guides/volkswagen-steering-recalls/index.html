Volkswagen steering recalls: which Atlas, Tiguan and ID.4 are affected?

6 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 11, 2026

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Steering knuckles on the Atlas family dominate this list: 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport appear twice, in 40Q6 for knuckles damaged during production — the one notice here with a do-not-drive instruction — and in 40Q7 for a front knuckle that may fracture in the area of the strut mounting. Bolts are the other pattern: a loose tie rod bolt on the 2025 Tiguan rear axle (42E7), and a steering rack-to-subframe bolt that may corrode and break on the 2018 Tiguan and 2018-2019 Atlas (48LG/48VT). Two stand alone: one 2021 ID.4 with a missing or incorrectly assembled steering gear drive bushing, and the Golf and Golf Sportwagen, where silicate on the shift lever micro switch contacts may let the key be removed while the lever is not in 'Park'.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Being able to remove the key from the ignition while the shift lever is not in 'Park' — the Golf and Golf Sportwagen rollaway recall (37L5).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One notice carries a stop-driving instruction: 40Q6, covering 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport, where Volkswagen advises owners not to drive the vehicle until it has been inspected and repaired. No notice here tells owners to park outside. The other five — 40Q7 for the Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport front knuckle, 48Q7 for the single 2021 ID.4, 42E7 for the 2025 Tiguan tie rod bolt, 48LG/48VT for the 2018 Tiguan and 2018-2019 Atlas rack bolt, and 37L5 for the Golf and Golf Sportwagen — do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Most of this work is replacement. For 40Q6 and 40Q7 dealers inspect the Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport steering knuckles and replace one or both as necessary. 48Q7 replaces the ID.4 steering gear, 42E7 replaces the Tiguan rear tie rod bolt, and 48LG/48VT replaces the right-side steering rack mounting bolt. For 37L5, dealers install an additional switch and circuit board. Every notice says free of charge.

Common questions

Volkswagen told me not to drive my Atlas — which recall is that?

That is 40Q6, covering 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport with steering knuckles damaged during production. The notice advises owners not to drive until the vehicle has been inspected and repaired; dealers inspect and replace the steering knuckle as necessary, free of charge.

My Atlas Cross Sport already had a knuckle recall — what is 40Q7?

40Q7 is a separate notice for 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport, describing a front steering knuckle that may fracture in the area of the strut mounting. Dealers inspect and replace one or both knuckles free of charge. Call Volkswagen at 1-800-893-5298.

Only one ID.4 is listed — how do I know whether it is mine?

48Q7 covers one 2021 ID.4 whose steering gear may have an incorrectly assembled or missing drive bushing. Volkswagen mailed owner notification letters November 5, 2021, and a dealer replaces the steering gear free of charge. Owners may contact Volkswagen at 1-800-893-5298.
