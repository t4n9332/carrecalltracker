Why do Volkswagen Atlas steering knuckles keep cracking or breaking?

5 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-08-13

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Volkswagen's steering recalls cluster around one part on one family: the front steering knuckle on the 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport, covered by two separate campaigns — knuckles damaged during production, carrying a do-not-drive warning, and knuckles that can fracture at the strut mounting. The others are isolated build errors rather than a shared pattern: a single 2021 ID.4 with an incorrectly assembled or missing steering gear drive bushing, a loose rear-axle tie rod bolt on the 2025 Tiguan, and Golf and Golf Sportwagen cars — the notice covers certain 2015-2018 models — where silicate buildup on the shift lever micro switch lets the key come out with the lever not in Park.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Sudden loss of steering or vehicle control; several notices say it can happen with little or no warning
  • On the Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport, no reliable outward sign — damaged or cracked knuckles are found by inspection
  • On the 2025 Tiguan, a loose rear tie rod bolt may first show up as a loss of control rather than an early warning
  • On the Golf and Golf Sportwagen, the key can be pulled from the ignition while the shift lever is not in Park, so the car can roll away

Is it safe to keep driving?

One recall is a stop-driving order: the November 2020 campaign for 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport steering knuckles damaged in production — Volkswagen says do not drive until it has been inspected and repaired if needed. The other four do not tell owners to park the vehicle. None requires parking outside. Still, the ID.4 steering gear, the 2025 Tiguan rear tie rod bolt and the second Atlas knuckle recall all describe a loss of control with little warning, so treat them as next-stop repairs rather than something scheduled weeks out.

What the free repair involves

Repairs are free of charge. On both Atlas campaigns dealers inspect the front steering knuckles and replace one or both as necessary. The ID.4 gets a new steering gear, and the 2025 Tiguan gets its rear-axle tie rod bolt replaced. For the Golf and Golf Sportwagen, dealers install an additional switch and circuit board. No over-the-air update applies to any of these.

Common questions

How do I know if my Atlas falls under the do-not-drive recall?

Both Atlas knuckle recalls list the 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport, so your VIN may fall under one, both, or neither. Check your VIN or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 before driving.

Why was the Atlas recalled twice for the same steering knuckle?

They are different defects. The November 2020 recall covers knuckles damaged during production; the January 2021 recall covers knuckles that can fracture in the strut mounting area. Both are handled by inspecting and replacing one or both knuckles.

Is the Golf key recall really a steering problem?

Not steering itself — it is a rollaway hazard. Silicate buildup on the shift lever micro switch lets the key be removed when the lever is not in Park. Always confirm Park and set the parking brake before leaving the car.
