What steering problems has Volkswagen recalled across Atlas, Tiguan and ID.4?

5 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 13, 2025

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Steering knuckles on the Atlas family drive this group. Two recalls cover 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport: one for knuckles that may have been damaged during production, the other for a front steering knuckle that may fracture in the area of the strut mounting. The remaining three are single-issue recalls on different models: 2015-2018 Golf and Golf SportWagen, where a build of silicate on the shift lever micro switch contacts may let the key be removed while the shift lever is not in Park; one 2021 ID.4 with an incorrectly assembled or missing steering gear drive bushing; and 2025 Tiguan with a loose tie rod bolt on the rear axle assembly. Loose or broken hardware is the common thread.

Warning signs to watch for

  • On the 2015-2018 Golf and Golf SportWagen recall, the key can be removed from the ignition while the shift lever is not in "Park."

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One recall here carries a stop-driving instruction: the steering knuckle recall on 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport, where Volkswagen is telling owners not to drive the vehicle until it has been inspected and repaired. The other four notices — the front steering knuckle fracture recall on 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport, the 2015-2018 Golf and Golf SportWagen key removal recall, the single 2021 ID.4 steering gear, and the 2025 Tiguan tie rod bolt — do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Under both Atlas recalls, dealers inspect and, as necessary, replace one or both steering knuckles. For the Golf and Golf SportWagen recall, dealers install an additional switch and circuit board. The single 2021 ID.4 has its steering gear replaced, and affected 2025 Tiguan vehicles have the tie rod bolt replaced. Every notice in this group states the repair is free of charge.

Common questions

Why is my Atlas listed in two steering knuckle recalls?

The notices list separate defects. One covers 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport vehicles where one or both steering knuckles may have been damaged during production. The other covers a front steering knuckle that may fracture in the area of the strut mounting.

What is the concern with the Golf ignition key recall?

The notice says a build of silicate on the shift lever micro switch contacts may allow the key to be removed while the shift lever is not in Park, increasing the risk of an unintended rollaway that may result in personal injury or a crash.

My Tiguan is a 2025 — what does the recall replace?

The notice covers certain 2025 Tiguan vehicles where a tie rod bolt on the rear axle assembly may be loose, which can lead to a loss of vehicle control. Dealers replace the affected tie rod bolt free of charge; letters were mailed September 5, 2025.
