Why do so many Audi airbag recalls involve the passenger seat?

14 NHTSA recalls · Audi · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 3, 2024

Passenger-side protection is the thread through these Audi recalls. Four involve the Passenger Occupant Detection System: sensor mat stress or wear on 2012-2018 A6, A7, S6, S7 and RS7 seats, an improper electrical contact on 2015-2019 A3 models and the 2016-2018 A3 e-tron, cable oxidation on 2017-2019 A4, A5, S4, S5 and RS5 models, and a PODS malfunction on 2015-2020 A3, S3 and 2017-2020 RS3. The instrument panel is the second pattern: 2019 Q3 and 2020 A6, A6 Allroad and A7 airbags may not deploy through the tear seam, a 2019 Q5 and SQ5 panel carrier may break, and a 2019-2021 A6 and A7 score line may be wrong. Curtain airbags cover the 2018 A5 Coupe and 2019 Q7.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the notices listed here tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. What each describes is what happens in a crash: a passenger airbag that is deactivated or disabled, a head curtain or side curtain airbag that may not unfold or inflate properly, an airbag that may not deploy through the instrument panel tear seam, or, on 2020-2021 Q7 vehicles, a second stage that may deploy too quickly when an unbelted child occupies the front passenger seat. The notices set out no interim instructions before the repair.

What the free repair involves

Remedies vary by recall and each notice says the work is free of charge. Dealers install a PODS system repair kit, replace the PODS sensor mat and update its control module, replace the connector and reroute the cable, or install cable ties on the harness. Others modify the head curtain airbags, replace side curtain airbags, replace instrument panels, inspect the airbag score line and repair it as necessary, inspect the airbag mounting screw, or update the airbag control module.

Common questions

Can I tell whether my passenger airbag has been switched off?

The notices do not describe any light, message or other sign an owner would notice. They state only that a PODS malfunction, oxidation on a connecting cable, or an improper electrical contact can deactivate or disable the passenger airbag, increasing injury risk in a crash.

Which Audi models come up most often in these airbag recalls?

The A6 and A7 families and the A3, S3 and RS3 recur, mostly through Passenger Occupant Detection System recalls. Q5, SQ5, Q3 and Q7 appear in instrument panel and curtain airbag recalls, and 2018 A4 Allroad, A5, S4 and S5 models appear in others.

What does a dealer do if the airbag may not deploy through the tear seam?

For the 2019 Q3 recall, dealers inspect the instrument panel and replace it if necessary. For the 2020 A6, A6 Allroad and A7 recall, dealers replace the instrument panels. Both notices state that the work is done free of charge.
