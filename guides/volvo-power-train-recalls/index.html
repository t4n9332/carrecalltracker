Why do Volvo VNL trucks have transmission and air tank recalls?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Power Train · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2021-10-06

These three Power Train recalls all originate with Volvo Trucks and center on the VNL. In 2018-2019 VNL trucks with an automated manual transmission, an internal clutch-assembly component can fail and cause unintended vehicle movement. In 2022 VNL trucks, electronic control unit software can shift the transmission into neutral unexpectedly. In 2019-2020 VAH, VHD, VNL, and VNR trucks, over-tightened t-bolt clasps can deform the transmission's auxiliary air tank strap, letting the air tank drop and become a road hazard.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Unintended vehicle movement from a failed clutch-assembly component (2018-2019 VNL)
  • Transmission unexpectedly shifts into neutral (2022 VNL)
  • Air tank drops from an unsecured strap onto the road (2019-2020 VAH/VHD/VNL/VNR)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the 2018-2019 VNL clutch recall, the 2022 VNL neutral-shift recall, and the 2019-2020 VAH/VHD/VNL/VNR air tank strap recall each describe the defect and the free dealer repair without telling owners to stop driving or to park the truck away from structures while waiting for service.

What the free repair involves

Dealers update the Transmission Control Module software for the clutch-assembly recall, reprogram the electronic control module to stop the unexpected shift to neutral, and inspect and replace the air tank strap as needed for the t-bolt recall. All three repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

What does 'unintended vehicle movement' mean in the Volvo VNL clutch recall?

The notice says an internal clutch-assembly component in the automated manual transmission can fail, which may allow the 2018-2019 VNL to move unexpectedly; the remedy is a free Transmission Control Module software update.

Can my 2022 VNL shift itself into neutral while I'm driving?

The notice states that electronic control unit software may allow the transmission to shift into neutral unexpectedly; dealers will reprogram the electronic control module free of charge to fix it.

What happens if the air tank strap fails on my VNL?

The notice covering 2019-2020 VAH, VHD, VNL, and VNR trucks says over-tightened t-bolt clasps can deform the strap until the tank disconnects and drops to the ground, creating a road hazard; dealers inspect and replace the strap free of charge.
