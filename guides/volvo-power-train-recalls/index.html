What Volvo truck power train recalls affect clutch and transmission?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Power Train · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 6, 2021

All three power train recalls in this group are Volvo Trucks, and the VNL appears in every one. Two involve the transmission: certain 2018-2019 VNL vehicles with an automated manual transmission, where an internal component in the clutch assembly may fail and possibly result in unintended vehicle movement, and certain 2022 VNL vehicles, where electronic control unit software may allow the transmission to shift into neutral unexpectedly. The third is hardware: on certain 2019-2020 VAH, VHD, VNL and VNR vehicles, over-tightened t-bolt clasps on the transmission auxiliary air tank straps may deform the strap, and the t-bolt may disconnect and let the tank drop to the ground.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall RVXX1901: an internal component in the clutch assembly may fail, possibly resulting in unintended vehicle movement (2018-2019 VNL).
  • Recall RVXX2104: the transmission may shift into neutral unexpectedly (2022 VNL).
  • Recall RVXX1904: the t-bolt may disconnect from the strap, allowing the air tank to drop to the ground.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. RVXX1901 says unintended vehicle movement can increase the risk of a crash, RVXX2104 says an unexpected shift into neutral can increase the risk of a crash, and RVXX1904 says a dropped air tank can become a road hazard and increase the risk of a crash. Beyond those risks, the notices give owners no instruction to follow before the dealer repair.

What the free repair involves

Two of the three are software: for RVXX1901 dealers update the Transmission Control Module software, and for RVXX2104 dealers reprogram the electronic control module. For RVXX1904 dealers inspect and replace the air tank strap if necessary. All three notices say the work is free of charge, and owner notification has already gone out for each.

Common questions

Does my 2018 VNL need the clutch recall?

RVXX1901 covers certain 2018-2019 VNL vehicles equipped with an automated manual transmission. Volvo says dealers update the Transmission Control Module software free of charge. A VIN check with Volvo customer service or NHTSA shows whether a specific truck is included.

Are the clutch and neutral-shift recalls the same problem?

The notices describe different vehicles and different causes: RVXX1901 covers 2018-2019 VNL trucks with an internal clutch assembly component that may fail, while RVXX2104 covers 2022 VNL trucks whose electronic control unit software may allow a shift into neutral.

What is the air tank strap recall about?

RVXX1904 covers certain 2019-2020 VAH, VHD, VNL and VNR vehicles whose transmission auxiliary air tank strap t-bolt clasps may have been over-tightened. The notice says dealers will inspect and replace the air tank strap if necessary, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
