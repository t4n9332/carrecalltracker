Why do multiple Cadillac models have air bag deployment recalls?

4 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · AIR Bags · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-05-02

Cadillac has four air bag recalls in this group, each with a different cause. A 2020 recall found the roof-rail air bag diffuser could separate from its inflator on the XT5 and XT6, shared with several Chevrolet, GMC, and Buick models. A 2021 recall covers the CT4 and CT5 (2020-2022), where roof rail side-curtain air bags may not have been installed correctly. A 2024 recall is specific to the XT5, where the driver's front air bag cushion may tear during deployment. A fourth 2021 recall covers an inconsistent air bag warning light on the CT4, CT5, Escalade, and Escalade ESV, shared with several other GM models.

Warning signs to watch for

  • On the 2021 warning-light recall (CT4, CT5, Escalade, Escalade ESV), the air bag malfunction indicator light may turn on and off inconsistently
  • The 2020 roof-rail diffuser, 2021 CT4/CT5 installation, and 2024 XT5 cushion-tear recalls describe no warning sign a driver would notice
  • These air bag defects are generally hidden issues that would only become apparent in a crash

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four Cadillac air bag recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the 2020 roof-rail diffuser recall, the 2021 CT4 and CT5 installation recall, the 2024 XT5 air bag cushion recall, and the 2021 warning-light recall do not tell owners to stop driving. Each describes how the defect could reduce air bag protection or fail to warn the driver in a crash, but none direct owners to park the vehicle outside or stop using it before the repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the roof-rail air bag module, inspect and reinstall the roof rail side-curtain air bags on the CT4 and CT5, replace the driver's front air bag module on the XT5, or update the communications gateway module software (by dealer or over-the-air) for the warning-light recall. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Which Cadillac models are covered by these air bag recalls?

The XT5 (2020, 2024), XT6 (2020), CT4 and CT5 (2020-2022), and Escalade and Escalade ESV (2021) are named across these four recalls, several of which also affect related Chevrolet, GMC, or Buick models.

What exactly can go wrong with the CT4 and CT5 air bags?

GM says the roof rail side-curtain air bags on 2020-2022 CT4 and CT5 vehicles may not have been installed correctly, which could result in improper air bag deployment during a crash. Dealers inspect and reinstall the air bags as necessary, free of charge.

Is the 2024 XT5 air bag recall a new issue?

Yes, it's separate from the earlier roof-rail diffuser recall. GM says the driver's front air bag cushion on 2024 XT5 vehicles may tear during deployment, so it does not deploy as intended. Dealers replace the air bag module for free.
