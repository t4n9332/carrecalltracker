Cadillac air bag recalls: which models and what fails

4 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · AIR Bags · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 2, 2024

Four recalls cover Cadillac air bags, and roof rail hardware is the recurring thread. The 2020 XT5 and XT6 are included because the diffuser of the Roof-Rail Air Bag inflator may not have been properly crimped to the inflator and could separate during deployment. The 2020-2022 CT4 and CT5 are recalled because the roof rail side-curtain air bags may not have been installed correctly. Frontal air bags appear once, on the 2024 XT5, whose driver's front air bag cushion may tear during deployment. The fourth recall is different in kind: on the 2021 CT4, CT5, Escalade and Escalade ESV, the communications gateway module causes the air bag warning light to illuminate inconsistently. CT4, CT5 and XT5 each appear twice.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The air bag malfunction indicator light may illuminate inconsistently and may fail to warn the driver of a problem with the air bag — 2021 Cadillac CT4, CT5, Escalade and Escalade ESV communications gateway module recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. The 2020 XT5 and XT6 notice describes degraded roof-rail air bag performance in a crash; the 2020-2022 CT4 and CT5 notice describes an improper deployment; the 2024 XT5 notice describes a driver's air bag that may not deploy as intended; and the 2021 warning light notice describes a light that may fail to warn the driver. Each points owners to the free remedy and gives no further driving instruction.

What the free repair involves

All of this work is free of charge. Dealers replace the suspect roof-rail air bag modules on the 2020 XT5 and XT6, inspect both left and right-side roof rail air bags and reinstall them as necessary on the 2020-2022 CT4 and CT5, and replace the driver's front air bag module on the 2024 XT5. The 2021 gateway module software is updated by a dealer or over the air.

Common questions

Which Cadillac models are covered by these air bag recalls?

The 2020 XT5 and XT6, the 2020-2022 CT4 and CT5, the 2024 XT5, and the 2021 CT4, CT5, Escalade and Escalade ESV, which are included for the air bag warning light rather than the air bag hardware itself.

What happens to the 2024 XT5 driver's air bag?

That notice says the driver's front air bag cushion may tear during deployment, resulting in the air bag not deploying as intended, which increases the risk of injury in a crash. Dealers replace the driver's front air bag module free of charge.

Do the notices tell Cadillac owners to stop driving?

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. Each frames the risk around a crash — an air bag that may not deploy or perform properly, or a warning light that may fail to warn the driver.
