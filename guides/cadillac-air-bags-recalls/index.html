Cadillac Air Bag Recalls Cover Deployment and Warning Light Problems

4 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · AIR Bags · model years 2020–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 2, 2024

Roof-rail air bag performance recurs across these Cadillac notices, alongside a front air bag cushion defect and warning-light software. Certain 2020 XT5 and XT6 vehicles have an inflator diffuser that could separate during deployment. Certain 2020–2022 CT4 and CT5 vehicles have roof-rail side-curtain air bags that may be installed incorrectly. CT4 and CT5 also appear in the warning-light recall for certain 2021 vehicles, together with Escalade and Escalade ESV. That notice concerns communications gateway software and inconsistent indicator illumination. XT5 appears again in the separate 2024 recall, where the driver's front air bag cushion may tear during deployment. CT4 and CT5 span the broadest model-year range here.

Warning signs to watch for

  • June 10, 2021 warning-light recall — the air bag malfunction indicator light may illuminate inconsistently on certain 2021 CT4, CT5, Escalade and Escalade ESV vehicles.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these Cadillac air bag notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The July 2020 roof-rail inflator notice for certain XT5 and XT6 vehicles does not tell owners to stop driving. The August 2021 CT4 and CT5 side-curtain air bag installation notice also does not tell owners to stop driving. Neither the May 2024 XT5 driver's front air bag notice nor the June 2021 warning-light notice for certain CT4, CT5, Escalade and Escalade ESV vehicles tells owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace suspect roof-rail modules for the July 2020 recall. For the August 2021 recall, they inspect both roof-rail air bags and reinstall them as necessary. The May 2024 XT5 remedy replaces the driver's front air bag module. The June 2021 warning-light remedy updates gateway software through a dealer or OTA. Each remedy is free of charge.

Common questions

What air bag problems affect the Cadillac XT5?

The July 2020 recall covers certain 2020 XT5 vehicles with a roof-rail inflator diffuser that could separate during deployment. The May 2024 recall covers certain 2024 XT5 vehicles whose driver's front air bag cushion may tear during deployment.

What do dealers check on recalled CT4 and CT5 side-curtain air bags?

For the August 2021 recall covering certain 2020–2022 CT4 and CT5 vehicles, dealers inspect both left and right roof-rail air bags. They reinstall the air bags as necessary. The notice specifies that this work is free of charge.

Which Cadillac air bag recall offers an over-the-air update?

The June 2021 warning-light recall covers certain 2021 CT4, CT5, Escalade and Escalade ESV vehicles. Its communications gateway software update can be performed by a dealer or delivered over the air, free of charge.
