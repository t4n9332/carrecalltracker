Nissan seat belt recalls: which models and which belts are affected?

6 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Seat Belts · model years 2021–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 5, 2024

Fasteners and pretensioners are the two threads running through Nissan's seat belt recalls, and the Rogue family carries most of them. The 2023 Rogue appears twice: once for rear seat belt anchor fasteners that may not have been tightened properly and can loosen over time, and once for driver and front passenger pretensioners that may have been incorrectly manufactured. Certain 2021-2022 Rogue vehicles have rear seat belts that may not retract properly, and the 2021 Rogue Sport has an automatic locking retractor that may deactivate early. The 2022 Frontier recall involves left-rear seat belt bracket mounting bolts, and the 2024 Pathfinder and INFINITI QX60 recall involves a missing rivet in the left-side front lap belt pretensioner.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Seat belts that are hard to use: the 2021-2022 Rogue recall says the left-hand and right-hand rear seat belts may not retract properly, which may make them difficult to operate.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2023 Rogue anchor bolt notice says a loose anchor fastener may not properly restrain an occupant in a crash. The 2023 Rogue and the Pathfinder and QX60 pretensioner notices say improperly restrained occupants have an increased risk of injury during a crash. The Rogue Sport, Frontier and 2021-2022 Rogue notices each describe an increased risk of injury in a crash as well. None of the six gives owners an interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Every notice here says the work is free of charge. Dealers inspect and tighten the 2023 Rogue rear anchor bolts and add labels showing bolt size and torque; replace the 2023 Rogue front seat belt assemblies as necessary; replace the Pathfinder and QX60 left-side front lap belt pretensioner as necessary; replace Rogue Sport belts; replace the Frontier left-rear bolts; and inspect and replace the 2021-2022 Rogue rear belts.

Common questions

Which seat belts does each Rogue recall actually cover?

The 2023 Rogue recalls cover rear seat belt anchor attachment fasteners and the driver and front passenger pretensioners. The 2021-2022 Rogue recall covers the left-hand and right-hand rear seat belts, and the 2021 Rogue Sport recall covers the front passenger belt and both outer rear belts.

Can I tell by looking whether my belt is affected?

Only the 2021-2022 Rogue notice describes something an owner would encounter, rear belts that may not retract properly. The other notices describe fasteners, a missing rivet and pretensioner manufacturing, and they do not say owners would notice anything. A VIN check confirms coverage.

Does any of these recalls affect a child seat?

The 2021 Rogue Sport notice says the seat belt automatic locking retractor may deactivate early, reducing the protection offered to an installed child seat. Dealers replace the front passenger seat belt and, as necessary, both outer rear seat belts, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
