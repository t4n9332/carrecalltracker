Why Are So Many Nissan Seat Belts Being Recalled?

6 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Seat Belts · model years 2021–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-03-05

Nissan's Rogue shows up in three of these six seat belt recalls: loose rear seat belt anchor bolts on the 2023 Rogue, incorrectly manufactured front seat belt pretensioners on the 2023 Rogue, and rear seat belts that may not retract properly on 2021-2022 Rogue models. The 2022 Frontier has a similar loose rear seat belt bolt problem. The 2024 Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60 share a seat belt pretensioner recall caused by a missing rivet, and the 2021 Rogue Sport has a seat belt automatic locking retractor that may deactivate early, which affects child seat installation. Across the group, the recurring theme is hardware - bolts, rivets, and pretensioner components - that isn't secured or manufactured correctly.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rear seat belt that feels loose, is hard to pull, or won't retract (2021-2022 Rogue)
  • A seat belt that doesn't lock a child seat in place (2021 Rogue Sport)
  • The loose anchor/bolt recalls (2023 Rogue, 2022 Frontier) and the pretensioner recalls (2023 Rogue, 2024 Pathfinder/QX60) describe no warning sign owners can notice before a crash

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the six seat belt recall notices in this group instruct owners to stop driving or park outside. Each says only that a seat belt, anchor, bracket, or pretensioner that isn't properly secured may not restrain an occupant in a crash, which the notices say increases the risk of injury. That applies to the 2023 Rogue anchor bolt and pretensioner recalls, the 2021-2022 Rogue retraction recall, the 2022 Frontier bolt recall, the 2021 Rogue Sport retractor recall, and the 2024 Pathfinder/QX60 pretensioner recall. None of the notices tell owners to stop driving in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Depending on the recall, dealers tighten and torque the seat belt anchor bolts, replace the front seat belt or pretensioner assembly, replace the rear seat belts, or replace the left-rear seat belt bolts and repair damaged threads. Some dealers also add warning labels showing the correct bolt torque. All of these seat belt repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why has the Nissan Rogue had so many seat belt recalls?

The Rogue has three separate seat belt recalls: loose rear anchor bolts (2023), incorrectly manufactured front pretensioners (2023), and rear seat belts that may not retract (2021-2022). Each recall addresses a different seat belt component.

What's wrong with the seat belts on the 2024 Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60?

Nissan says the left-side front lap belt pretensioner may be missing a rivet, so it isn't properly secured. This can prevent the seat belt from restraining the occupant correctly in a crash.

Is the 2021 Nissan Rogue Sport seat belt recall about child seats?

Yes. Nissan says the seat belt's automatic locking retractor may deactivate early on certain 2021 Rogue Sport models, reducing the protection offered to a child seat installed using that belt.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
