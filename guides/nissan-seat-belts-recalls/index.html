Nissan Seat Belt Recalls Cover Anchors, Pretensioners, and Retraction Problems

6 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Seat Belts · model years 2021–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Mar 5, 2024

Rogue appears most often in these Nissan seat belt notices. Certain 2023 Rogues have separate recalls for rear anchor fasteners that can loosen and front pretensioners that may not properly restrain occupants. Certain 2021-2022 Rogues have rear belts that may not retract properly. The distinct 2021 Rogue Sport notice concerns automatic locking retractors that may deactivate early, reducing protection for an installed child seat. Attachment problems also affect the left-rear seat belt bracket bolts in certain 2022 Frontiers. Certain 2024 Pathfinder and INFINITI QX60 vehicles have a left-front lap belt pretensioner that may be unsecured because of a missing rivet.

Warning signs to watch for

  • PC915, 2021-2022 Rogue rear seat belt recall: the left and right rear belts may not retract properly and may be difficult to operate.
  • RT049/RT050, 2021 Rogue Sport recall: the seat belt automatic locking retractor may deactivate early.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the supplied seat belt notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for 2023 Rogue rear anchor bolts and front pretensioners do not tell owners to stop driving. Neither does the notice for the 2024 Pathfinder and INFINITI QX60 left-front lap belt pretensioner. The 2021 Rogue Sport automatic locking retractor, 2022 Frontier left-rear bracket bolt, and 2021-2022 Rogue rear belt retraction notices also do not tell owners to stop driving. These statements describe the notices' instructions.

What the free repair involves

For the 2023 Rogue anchor recall, dealers inspect and tighten bolts and add warning labels; PD105 replaces front belt assemblies as necessary. Pathfinder/QX60 dealers inspect and replace left-front lap pretensioners as necessary. Rogue Sport receives specified belt replacements. Frontier receives replacement left-rear bolts and thread repairs as necessary. PC915 inspects and replaces rear belts as necessary. Repairs are free.

Common questions

Are the two 2023 Rogue seat belt recalls about the same parts?

The May 2023 notice concerns rear seat belt anchor fasteners that may loosen. PD105 concerns driver and front passenger seat belt pretensioners that may not restrain occupants properly. The remedies address rear anchor bolts and front seat belt assemblies, respectively.

What rear seat belt behavior does the 2021-2022 Rogue notice describe?

PC915 says the left and right rear seat belts may not retract properly. The notice explains that this may make them difficult to operate, preventing their use. Dealers inspect and replace the rear seat belts as necessary, free of charge.

How does the 2021 Rogue Sport recall affect child seats?

The RT049/RT050 notice says the automatic locking retractor may deactivate early, reducing protection for an installed child seat. Dealers replace the front passenger seat belt and, as necessary, both outer rear seat belts. The remedy is free of charge.
