Nissan brake light, headlight and turn signal recalls: what's affected?

5 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 13, 2022

These Nissan lighting recalls split into three repeating problems. Brake light switches account for two: the 2019 Altima, where the brake switch bracket may be in the incorrect location and the switch may remain on, and the 2018-2019 Sentra, where the switch may become contaminated so the brake lights do not illuminate. Headlight aim accounts for two more: the 2019 Titan with LED headlights that may carry incorrect aiming marks, and the 2020 Sentra whose right-hand headlight aim may be misaligned. The fifth covers 2020 Titan, Titan XD and 2020-2021 Versa, where a front turn signal bulb can crack and fail to illuminate. Sentra and Titan each appear twice.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake lights remaining illuminated when the brakes are not being pressed (2019 Altima brake switch bracket recall)
  • The transmission shift lever being able to move out of the Park position (2019 Altima brake switch bracket recall)
  • Brake lights that do not illuminate (2018-2019 Sentra brake light switch recall)
  • A front turn signal that does not light up (2020 Titan, Titan XD and 2020-2021 Versa bulb recall)
  • Insufficient illumination of the road while driving at night (2019 Titan LED headlight aiming recall)

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park the vehicle outside. Each describes a visibility or signalling risk instead: on the 2019 Altima, brake lights that stay on may confuse other drivers and the shift lever may be moved out of Park; Sentra brake lights may not illuminate; inoperable front turn signals cannot alert oncoming drivers or pedestrians; and misaimed headlights may leave the road insufficiently lit at night or reduce the driver's visibility. No notice gives an interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Each recall has its own repair. Dealers inspect and install a new brake pedal as needed (2019 Altima), replace the brake light switch and install a protective grommet (2018-2019 Sentra), inspect and replace turn signal bulbs as necessary (2020 Titan, Titan XD and 2020-2021 Versa), inspect and replace the headlight assemblies (2019 Titan), and replace the right-hand headlight (2020 Sentra). All free of charge.

Common questions

Can I tell by looking whether my car is affected?

Some notices name things you could see: brake lights staying on, brake lights not illuminating, or a front turn signal that does not light. The headlight recalls are not described that way. A VIN check confirms which recalls apply to your vehicle.

Why does an aiming mark problem mean a new headlight?

The 2019 Titan notice says incorrect aiming marks may cause the headlights to be aimed incorrectly during servicing, and that dealers will inspect and, as necessary, replace the headlight assemblies free of charge. The 2020 Sentra notice says dealers replace the right-hand headlight.

My Altima's shift lever moves out of Park easily. Is that this recall?

The 2019 Altima notice describes a brake switch that may remain in the ON position, with brake lights illuminated when the brakes are not pressed and the shift lever able to be moved out of Park. Dealers inspect and install a new brake pedal as needed.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
