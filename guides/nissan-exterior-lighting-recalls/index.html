Why Do Nissan Brake Lights and Headlights Keep Failing?

5 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2021 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-04-13

Lighting problems repeat in two areas across this group. Brake light switches misbehave in two recalls: a bracket misalignment on 2019 Altima can leave the brake lights on and even let the shifter move out of Park without the brake pressed, while contamination in the switch on 2018-2019 Sentra can prevent the brake lights from illuminating at all. Headlight aim is the other repeat issue: 2019 Titan LED headlights carry incorrect aiming marks, and 2020 Sentra vehicles have a misaligned right headlight, both of which can leave the road under-lit at night. A fifth recall covers 2020-2021 Titan, Titan XD, and Versa vehicles whose front turn signal bulbs can crack and fail to light.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake lights stay illuminated even when the brake pedal isn't pressed (2019 Altima)
  • Brake lights fail to illuminate at all (2018-2019 Sentra)
  • Front turn signal doesn't light up (2020-2021 Titan, Titan XD, Versa)
  • Headlights aimed too low or off-target, reducing how well the road ahead is lit at night (2019 Titan, 2020 Sentra)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the five recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices say a malfunctioning brake light switch, misaimed headlights, or a non-working turn signal can each increase the risk of a crash — the Altima notice adds that the shift lever could move out of Park without the brake pressed. In every case, though, the notice stops short of telling owners to stop driving the vehicle.

What the free repair involves

Repairs vary by cause: dealers install a new brake pedal for the Altima switch issue, replace the brake light switch and add a protective grommet for the Sentra, inspect and replace headlight assemblies or a single headlight for the Titan and Sentra aiming recalls, and inspect and replace cracked turn signal bulbs for the Titan, Titan XD, and Versa. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Which Nissan models have had brake light recalls?

2019 Altima vehicles were recalled for a misaligned brake switch bracket that can leave the brake lights on and let the shift lever move out of Park without the brake pressed. 2018-2019 Sentra vehicles were recalled separately for a brake light switch that can become contaminated and stop working.

Why were Nissan headlights recalled for aiming problems?

2019 Titan LED headlights had incorrect aiming marks that could cause the headlights to be aimed wrong during servicing, and 2020 Sentra vehicles had a misaligned right headlight from the factory. Both problems can leave the road poorly lit at night, the notices say.

What's wrong with the turn signal bulbs on the Titan and Versa?

On 2020-2021 Titan, Titan XD, and Versa vehicles, the front right and/or left turn signal bulb can crack and fail to illuminate, which prevents the turn signal from warning other drivers. Dealers inspect and replace the bulbs as necessary, free of charge.
