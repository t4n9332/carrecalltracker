Jeep steering recalls cover the Wrangler, Cherokee and Grand Cherokee.

6 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-04

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Jeep's steering recalls span multiple models and defect types rather than one recurring cause. Two recalls hit the Wrangler's steering column and intermediate shaft directly: a 2019 stub-shaft machining defect (shared with the Ram 1500) that can let the steering wheel detach, and a 2018 weld defect that can let the shaft split. The Cherokee has a separate steering rack gear that may break, and the Grand Cherokee has two distinct issues — an intermediate shaft that can disconnect from its u-joint, and a steering knuckle that can let a wheel fall outward. Most recently, a 2026 recall covers Wrangler and Gladiator power steering pump wiring that can overheat and start a fire.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A steering wheel that feels off-center or less responsive can signal the 2018 Wrangler intermediate shaft weld defect
  • The 2026 Wrangler and Gladiator power steering pump recall describes no warning sign — Chrysler says the fire risk exists even when the vehicle is parked and off
  • The remaining steering recalls (steering wheel detachment, rack gear, steering shaft disconnect, steering knuckle) describe no warning sign owners could notice beforehand

Is it safe to keep driving?

Across these six steering recalls, only the 2026 Wrangler and Gladiator power steering pump wiring recall tells owners to park outside and away from structures until repaired, because the wiring can overheat and start a fire even when the vehicle is off. The 2019 steering wheel detachment recall (Wrangler and Ram 1500), the 2018 Wrangler weld defect, the 2019-2020 Cherokee steering rack recall, the Grand Cherokee steering shaft disconnect recall, and the 2024 Grand Cherokee steering knuckle recall do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the affected steering components — the steering column, intermediate shaft, rack gear, steering knuckle, and power steering pump wiring — and replace or repair them as necessary. For the 2018 Wrangler weld defect, dealers repair the weld directly. All of this repair work, across all six recalls, is done free of charge.

Common questions

Is it safe to keep driving a Jeep while waiting for a steering recall repair?

The notices do not say. Only the 2026 Wrangler and Gladiator power steering pump recall tells owners to park outside and away from structures until the vehicle is repaired; the other five recalls in this group give no stop-driving instruction at all.

Which Jeep models are covered by these steering recalls?

Recalls cover the 2018-2019 Wrangler, the 2019 Wrangler and Ram 1500 together, 2021-2025 Wrangler and Gladiator, the 2019-2020 Cherokee, and the 2021 and 2023-2024 Grand Cherokee — each tied to a different steering part.

What part of the steering system fails most often in these recalls?

No single part repeats across all six recalls. Two involve the Wrangler's steering column or shaft, one involves the Cherokee's steering rack, two involve the Grand Cherokee's shaft or knuckle, and one involves the Wrangler/Gladiator power steering pump wiring.
