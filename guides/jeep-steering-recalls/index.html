Jeep Steering Recalls Include Shaft Failures and Pump Fire Risks

6 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 4, 2026

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Wrangler and Grand Cherokee recur most often in these steering notices. The 2018 Wrangler has an intermediate-shaft weld concern, while the 2019 Wrangler has a steering-column stub shaft that may break and allow steering-wheel detachment. Another Wrangler and Gladiator notice describes power-steering pump wiring that may overheat and cause fire, including with the ignition off. Grand Cherokee concerns involve intermediate-shaft disconnection on certain 2022-2023 Grand Cherokee and 2021-2023 Grand Cherokee L vehicles, plus steering-knuckle separation on certain 2024 Grand Cherokee vehicles. The 2019-2020 Cherokee has a separate steering-rack gear breakage concern. Loss of steering control repeats across several mechanical defects.

Warning signs to watch for

  • May 2018 Wrangler steering-shaft recall, U48 — a split weld may cause the steering wheel to lose center positioning and reduce steering responsiveness.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The June 2026 Wrangler and Gladiator steering-pump fire recall tells owners to park outside and away from structures until repaired. It does not tell owners to stop driving. This is the only listed steering recall with a park-outside instruction. The March 2019 steering-column, May 2018 intermediate-shaft weld, August 2019 Cherokee steering-rack, May 2023 Grand Cherokee intermediate-shaft, and February 2024 Grand Cherokee steering-knuckle notices do not tell owners to stop driving. None of the listed steering notices carries a stop-driving instruction.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies differ by recall. V29 covers steering-column inspection and replacement as necessary. U48 covers intermediate-shaft inspection and replacement as necessary. V99 replaces the steering gear. Recall 58A inspects and replaces the intermediate shaft and assembly parts if necessary. Recall 07B replaces the steering knuckle. The June 2026 recall inspects and replaces wiring and the electric hydraulic power steering pump as necessary.

Common questions

Which Jeep steering recall tells owners to park outside?

The June 4, 2026 Wrangler and Gladiator steering-pump notice advises parking outside and away from structures until repair. It describes wiring that may overheat and cause a vehicle fire even when the vehicle is parked with the ignition off.

What is the difference between the two Wrangler shaft defects?

The May 2018 notice concerns an intermediate-shaft weld that may split on certain 2018 Wranglers. The March 2019 notice concerns a steering-column stub shaft that may break on certain 2019 Wranglers, potentially allowing the steering wheel to detach.

Which Grand Cherokee steering parts do dealers repair?

Under 58A, dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace the intermediate shaft and assembly parts on the vehicles covered by that notice. Under 07B, dealers replace the steering knuckle on certain 2024 Grand Cherokee vehicles. Both remedies are free.
