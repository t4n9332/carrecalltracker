Why do Jeep steering recalls keep hitting Wrangler and Grand Cherokee?

6 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Steering · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 4, 2026

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Wrangler and Grand Cherokee dominate Jeep's steering recalls. The Wrangler appears three times: a 2018 intermediate steering shaft with an improperly welded seam, a 2019 steering column stub shaft that may break and let the steering wheel detach (shared with the 2019 Ram 1500), and a 2026 recall of 2021-2025 Wrangler, Wrangler 4XE and Gladiator over power steering pump wiring that may overheat and cause a fire even when the vehicle is parked with the ignition off. Grand Cherokee accounts for two: an intermediate shaft that may disconnect from the u-joint on 2021-2023 Grand Cherokee L and 2022-2023 Grand Cherokee, and a steering knuckle that may separate on the 2024. The 2019-2020 Cherokee steering rack gear recall completes the set.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2018 Wrangler intermediate steering shaft recall says a split weld seam may cause the steering wheel to lose center positioning and a loss of steering responsiveness.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One recall carries an instruction for owners before repair: the 2026 recall of 2021-2025 Jeep Wrangler, Wrangler 4XE and Gladiator tells owners to park outside and away from structures until the vehicle is repaired, because the power steering pump wiring can cause a fire even with the ignition off. None of these notices tells owners to stop driving. The 2018 Wrangler shaft, 2019 Wrangler and Ram stub shaft, 2019-2020 Cherokee rack gear, Grand Cherokee intermediate shaft and 2024 Grand Cherokee knuckle recalls state the crash risk only.

What the free repair involves

All six remedies are free of charge. Dealers inspect the steering column and replace it as necessary on the 2019 Wrangler; inspect and replace the 2018 Wrangler intermediate steering shaft as necessary; replace the Cherokee steering gear; inspect and if necessary replace the Grand Cherokee intermediate shaft and assembly parts; replace the 2024 Grand Cherokee steering knuckle; and inspect and replace the power steering pump wiring and pump as necessary.

Common questions

My Wrangler is covered by the power steering pump recall. What does the notice tell me to do?

It advises owners to park outside and away from structures until the vehicle is repaired, because the wiring may overheat and cause a fire even when parked with the ignition off. Dealers inspect and replace the wiring and pump as necessary, free of charge.

Do any of these recalls cover the Gladiator?

Only the power steering pump wiring recall, which lists 2021-2025 Jeep Gladiator alongside Wrangler and Wrangler 4XE. The other five steering recalls cover Wrangler, Cherokee and Grand Cherokee models. Checking your VIN on NHTSA's lookup is the way to confirm which apply.

Would I notice anything before a steering part failed?

Only one notice describes anything: the 2018 Wrangler intermediate shaft recall says a split weld seam may cause the steering wheel to lose center positioning and a loss of steering responsiveness. The other five notices describe no sign an owner would notice.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
