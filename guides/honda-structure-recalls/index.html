Honda structure recalls cover subframes, welds, hood latches and trim

6 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Structure · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 4, 2026

Six Honda recalls involve body and frame structure, and the rear subframe is the recurring theme. Rear subframe bolts that may loosen affect 2019-2020 CR-V, while rear subframe corrosion at the suspension mounting points affects 2016-2022 Pilot, 2017-2023 Ridgeline, 2019-2023 Passport and 2014-2020 Acura MDX sold in 22 states and the District of Columbia. The truck and SUV line shows up again in incomplete front frame welds on 2019-2020 Pilot and 2019 Passport, and a hood latch striker that can separate on 2016-2019 Pilot, 2017-2020 Ridgeline and 2019 Passport. The remaining two cover a center pillar trim clip on 2019 Pilot and Ridgeline and a Genuine Accessory centerstand kit for 2016-2018 Africa Twin motorcycles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The hood latch recall on the 2016-2019 Pilot, 2017-2020 Ridgeline and 2019 Passport describes the hood opening while driving and obstructing the driver's view.
  • The 2019-2020 CR-V subframe bolt recall describes reduced vehicle handling and says a separated rear subframe can suddenly disable the vehicle.
  • The rear subframe corrosion recall describes a loss of vehicle handling and control if rear suspension components such as the rear control arm fail.
  • The Africa Twin centerstand kit recall describes the centerstand detaching, and a motorcycle parked on it falling over.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The centerstand kit recall is the one with its own instruction: Honda notified 2016-2018 Africa Twin owners that if they purchased the affected kit they should not use it and should take the motorcycle to a dealer. For the rear subframe corrosion recall, the notice says interim letters about the safety risk were mailed July 27, 2026, with further letters once the final remedy is available. The other notices give no interim instructions.

What the free repair involves

Dealers install a bracket on the right center pillar for the trim clip recall, retorque or replace CR-V rear subframe bolts, repair the hood latch striker or replace the hood, replace the centerstand circlip and washer, and install a rear subframe reinforcement kit with repairs as needed. That work is free. The weld recall is a free inspection; vehicles missing welds get a repurchase or replacement offer.

Common questions

Does the rear subframe corrosion recall apply everywhere in the country?

The notice limits it to certain Pilot, Ridgeline, Passport and Acura MDX vehicles sold in 22 listed states and the District of Columbia, where the rear subframe may corrode at the suspension mounting points. Dealers inspect and install a rear subframe reinforcement kit, free.

What happens if my Pilot or Passport is missing frame welds?

The notice says dealers inspect for missing welds free of charge on certain 2019-2020 Pilot and 2019 Passport vehicles. If a vehicle is identified as missing welds, the dealer will offer to repurchase it or provide a similar replacement vehicle. Notification began November 6, 2019.

Is the hood latch recall really about the hood opening while driving?

The notice says the hood latch striker may become damaged and separate from the hood, which can result in the hood opening while driving and obstructing the driver's view. Dealers repair the striker or replace the hood, free. Letters were mailed January 18, 2022.
