Why do Honda Pilot, Ridgeline and CR-V have frame and subframe recalls?

6 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Structure · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-04

This group covers frame, subframe and body-hardware defects across several Honda models. Two recalls affect the rear subframe: loose bolts can let it separate on the 2019-2020 CR-V, and corrosion can cause suspension components to fail on the 2016-2022 Pilot, 2017-2023 Ridgeline, 2019-2023 Passport and 2014-2020 Acura MDX. The 2019-2020 Pilot and 2019 Passport were built with incomplete front frame welding. Separately, a damaged hood latch striker can let the hood open while driving on the 2016-2020 Pilot, Ridgeline and Passport. The 2019 Pilot and Ridgeline have an air bag trim clip defect that can interfere with curtain air bag deployment, and a 2016-2018 Africa Twin centerstand accessory kit can detach from the motorcycle.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Visible corrosion or rust on the rear subframe near the suspension mounting points (Pilot, Ridgeline, Passport, Acura MDX)
  • A hood that pops open or does not stay securely latched while driving (Pilot, Ridgeline, Passport)
  • Reduced handling or a rear subframe that feels loose before it separates (2019-2020 CR-V)
  • A centerstand that detaches from a parked or moving Africa Twin motorcycle
  • The notices describe no warning sign for the incomplete frame welding or the air bag trim clip defect

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six structure and frame recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Each notice describes an increased risk of injury or crash - from a separated subframe, an open hood, incomplete frame welding, an air bag trim clip that can interfere with deployment, or a detached centerstand - but none tell owners to stop driving. Owners should contact their dealer for the free inspection or repair described for their specific recall.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and, as needed, retorque or replace rear subframe bolts (CR-V) or install a subframe reinforcement kit (Pilot, Ridgeline, Passport, Acura MDX), inspect frames for missing welds and repurchase or replace affected vehicles (Pilot, Passport), repair or replace the hood latch striker or hood (Pilot, Ridgeline, Passport), install a trim-clip bracket (Pilot, Ridgeline), or replace a centerstand circlip and washer (Africa Twin). All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why do Honda Pilot and Ridgeline have so many frame-related recalls?

In this group, the Pilot and Ridgeline are each named in multiple separate recalls: incomplete frame welding (2019-2020 Pilot), a hood latch striker defect (2016-2020 Pilot and Ridgeline), and rear subframe corrosion (2016-2022 Pilot, 2017-2023 Ridgeline). Each has a different manufacturing cause.

Does a corroded rear subframe mean my Honda or Acura MDX is unsafe to drive?

The notice for this recall does not tell owners to stop driving. It says corrosion at the suspension mounting points can cause a rear suspension component to fail, increasing crash risk, and it applies to vehicles sold in specific northern and eastern states.

What is the Africa Twin centerstand recall about?

It covers a Honda Genuine Accessory centerstand kit sold for 2016-2018 Africa Twin motorcycles. A circlip in the kit can break, letting the centerstand detach while riding or while the motorcycle is parked on it.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
