Honda Structure Recalls Cover Hood Latches, Subframes and Crash Protection

6 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Structure · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 4, 2026

Pilot, Passport and Ridgeline recur across Honda's structure notices. The hood-latch recall covers certain 2016-2019 Pilots, 2019 Passports and 2017-2020 Ridgelines whose latch striker can separate from the hood. The June 2026 corrosion notice covers certain 2016-2022 Pilots, 2017-2023 Ridgelines, 2019-2023 Passports and 2014-2020 Acura MDXs sold in the listed jurisdictions. Other defects affect crash protection, including incomplete frame welding in certain 2019-2020 Pilots and 2019 Passports, and a trim clip that can interfere with curtain air bag deployment in certain 2019 Pilots and Ridgelines. Separate notices address rear subframe bolts on 2019-2020 CR-Vs and accessory centerstand kits sold for possible installation on 2016-2018 Africa Twin motorcycles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • December 2019 CR-V rear subframe recall — reduced handling and possible sudden vehicle disablement if the subframe separates.
  • November 2021 hood latch recall — the hood may open while driving and obstruct the driver's view.
  • June 2026 rear subframe corrosion recall — suspension component failure can cause loss of handling and control.
  • August 2018 Africa Twin centerstand recall — the motorcycle can fall over if the centerstand detaches while supporting it.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these structure notices instructs owners to stop driving or park outside. The August 2018 Africa Twin accessory centerstand notice does specifically tell owners who purchased the affected kit not to use it and to take the motorcycle to a dealer. That instruction concerns the affected centerstand kit. The CR-V subframe, hood latch, frame welding, pillar trim and rear subframe corrosion notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Their stated risks include loss of handling, obstructed visibility and reduced occupant protection in a crash.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies include centerstand circlip and washer replacement, a pillar trim bracket, CR-V subframe bolt inspection and retorquing or replacement, and hood striker repair or hood replacement. The corrosion recall specifies inspection, reinforcement and component repair or replacement as necessary. Missing-weld inspections are free, with repurchase or a similar replacement vehicle offered if welds are missing.

Common questions

What happens if my Pilot or Passport is missing frame welds?

The October 2019 notice covers certain 2019-2020 Pilots and 2019 Passports. Dealers inspect for missing welds free of charge. If welds are missing, the notice says the dealer will offer repurchase or a similar replacement vehicle.

Which vehicles are included in the rear subframe corrosion recall?

The June 2026 notice covers certain 2016-2022 Pilots, 2017-2023 Ridgelines, 2019-2023 Passports and 2014-2020 Acura MDXs sold in the jurisdictions it lists. The defect concerns corrosion at rear suspension mounting points that can cause suspension component failure.

What does Honda tell Africa Twin owners about the recalled centerstand?

The August 2018 notice concerns accessory centerstand kit 08M70-MJP-G50, sold for possible installation on 2016-2018 Africa Twins. Owners who purchased the affected kit are told not to use it and to take the motorcycle to a dealer.
