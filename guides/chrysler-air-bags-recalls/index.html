Why Are So Many Chrysler Air Bag Recalls For Pacifica and Voyager?

7 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-06-02

Seven Chrysler-brand air bag recalls make up this group, and five of them involve the Pacifica minivan (plus its Voyager twin). Four separate recalls between 2024 and 2026 affect the side curtain air bags on Pacifica and Voyager: one traces to a loose connector, and three others involve insufficient pressure retention from improperly sealed seams on 2022-2026 models. A 2026 recall covers a front seat wiring fault on 2023-2025 Pacifica and Voyager that can disable air bag deployment. Separately, a broader 2024 recall covers a steering column control module weld defect across 2023-2024 Jeep, Ram, and Chrysler Pacifica/Voyager models, and another 2024 recall covers ruptured side curtain inflators on 2018-2021 Chrysler 300 and Dodge Charger sedans.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice before a crash.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the seven recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the steering column control module recall, the Chrysler 300/Dodge Charger side air bag inflator rupture recall, the four Pacifica/Voyager side curtain air bag recalls, and the seat wiring recall do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice states only that an air bag may fail to deploy, or, in the inflator rupture recall, that a rupture may send metal fragments at occupants, increasing the risk of injury or death in a crash.

What the free repair involves

Repairs vary by recall: dealers inspect and replace the steering column control module as necessary, replace both side curtain air bags on the 300 and Charger, secure a loose connector or replace the side curtain air bags on Pacifica and Voyager, and replace a spring inside the front seat harness connector. All of these repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Do these Chrysler air bag recalls mean I have to stop driving?

No. None of the seven recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe how the air bags may fail to deploy or, in one case, rupture, but they do not tell owners to stop using the vehicle.

Why has the Pacifica had so many side air bag recalls?

Four separate recalls between 2024 and 2026 affect the Pacifica and Voyager side curtain air bags: one from a loose connector, and three others from improperly sealed seams that reduce pressure retention on 2022-2026 models.

What's different about the Chrysler 300 and Charger air bag recall?

That 2024 recall involves the side curtain air bag inflators on 2018-2021 models rupturing due to a manufacturing defect, which the notice says can send sharp metal fragments at occupants, causing injury or death.
