Why do Chrysler Pacifica side curtain air bags keep getting recalled?

7 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 2, 2026

Six of these seven recalls involve side curtain air bags, and the Chrysler Pacifica appears in most of them. Three notices — May 2025, September 2025 and March 2026 — describe the same defect on Pacifica and Voyager minivans: right and left side curtain air bags with insufficient pressure retention from improperly sealed seams, reaching Pacifica model years 2022 through 2025. A fourth, from October 2024, covers 2020-2023 Pacifica plus 2020-2021 and 2023 Voyager with an improperly secured side curtain connector. The 2018-2021 Chrysler 300 stands apart, with side curtain inflators that may rupture. Two others reach other air bags: a steering column control module weld on 2023-2024 Pacifica, and a seat harness spring on Braun-modified Pacifica and Voyager minivans.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside. That includes the 2018-2021 Chrysler 300 recall, whose notice says an inflator rupture may result in sharp metal fragments striking occupants, resulting in injury or death, and the three seam-sealing recalls on Pacifica and Voyager. The October 2024 connector recall states that all vehicles have been remedied. The remaining notices describe what could happen in a crash — an air bag that does not deploy, or one that does not hold pressure.

What the free repair involves

Remedies differ by recall. Dealers replace the side curtain air bags in the Chrysler 300 inflator recall and in the three seam-sealing Pacifica and Voyager recalls. In the October 2024 recall they secure the connector. For the steering column control module recall they inspect and replace the module as necessary, and in the Braun recall dealers replace the spring. Every notice says free of charge.

Common questions

My Pacifica has been recalled more than once for side curtain air bags. Why?

The notices list separate defects: an improperly secured connector for the side curtain air bags in the October 2024 recall, and improperly sealed seams leaving insufficient pressure retention in the May 2025, September 2025 and March 2026 recalls. Each carries its own recall numbers.

What does "insufficient pressure retention" mean on my minivan?

The notices say the right and left side curtain air bags may not hold pressure because their seams were improperly sealed, which increases the risk of ejection and injury during a crash. Dealers replace the side curtain air bags, as necessary, free of charge.

How serious is the Chrysler 300 inflator recall?

The notice says the right and left side curtain air bag inflators may rupture, and that an inflator rupture may result in sharp metal fragments striking occupants, resulting in injury or death. It does not tell owners to stop driving. Dealers replace both side curtain air bags free of charge.
