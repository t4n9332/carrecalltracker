Why do Chevrolet trucks keep having tailgate and latch recalls?

6 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Latches/locks/linkages · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-11-26

GM has issued repeated recalls for components that can unlatch or open while driving. Heavy-duty 2020 Silverado and Sierra 2500/3500 trucks have a hood-latch striker wire that can fracture, letting the hood open unexpectedly. The same HD trucks, across 2020-2024 model years, are covered by two separate power tailgate recalls in which the electronic release switch can short-circuit from water intrusion and unlatch the tailgate. The 2019-2020 Chevrolet Bolt EV has a rear door-handle cable that can be damaged, opening the door or disabling the handle. The 2025 Blazer, Blazer EV, and Equinox EV have door strikers that were not properly heat-treated. A 2020 Corvette's front trunk release button can stop working, risking entrapment.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A hood opening unexpectedly while driving (Silverado/Sierra 2500/3500 hood-latch recall)
  • A tailgate unlatching or opening while driving, linked to water intrusion in the release switch
  • A rear door opening while driving, or the inside door handle not working (Bolt EV)
  • A door opening unexpectedly due to a broken door striker (Blazer, Blazer EV, Equinox EV)
  • The front trunk release button not working after the vehicle has been off for ten minutes (Corvette)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the two Silverado/Sierra HD power-tailgate recalls, owners are advised to check that the tailgate is closed and latched before driving. The other notices describe a hood, rear door, side door, or front trunk that can open or fail to release, but do not give a similar interim instruction. In every case, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the hood assembly, the rear door-handle cables, the tailgate touchpad switch assemblies, and the door strikers and attachment bolts, depending on the recall. For the Corvette, dealers update the Body Control Module software, which can also be done over-the-air. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why did GM recall the Silverado and Sierra tailgate twice?

The first recall covered 2020-2024 model year HD trucks, and a second recall in late 2024 covered additional 2024 model year trucks with the same water-intrusion defect in the tailgate's release switch.

Can the Corvette trunk release recall be fixed without a dealer visit?

Yes, GM allows this recall to be completed through an over-the-air software update to the Body Control Module, in addition to the option of a dealer visit, so many owners will not need to schedule an appointment.

Is the hood latch recall related to the tailgate recalls?

No. The hood-latch recall involves a striker wire that can fracture, while the tailgate recalls involve a separate electronic release switch that can short-circuit from water intrusion; they are different parts and different defects.
