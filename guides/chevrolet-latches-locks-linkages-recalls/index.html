Why do Chevrolet doors, hoods and tailgates open on their own?

6 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Latches/locks/linkages · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 26, 2024

These six Chevrolet recalls all involve something that is supposed to stay shut. Tailgates dominate: two recalls cover power-unlatching tailgates on Silverado 2500 and 3500 HD trucks, one for 2020-2024 and one for 2024, both describing an electronic gate-release switch that may short circuit due to water intrusion and unlatch the tailgate while the truck is in Park. Improper heat treatment shows up twice more, on hood-latch striker wires in 2020 Silverado 2500 and 3500 and on side door strikers in 2025 Blazer, Blazer EV and Equinox EV. The 2019-2020 Bolt EV has a rear door-handle cable that may be damaged by the window, and the 2020 Corvette's interior front trunk release may stop working. GMC Sierra HD trucks are named alongside the Silverados.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2019-2020 Bolt EV door-handle cable recall: the notice says the rear inside door handle may not work.
  • 2020 Corvette front trunk recall: the notice says the release button inside the front trunk compartment may not function after the vehicle has been shut off for ten minutes.
  • Silverado 2500 and 3500 tailgate recalls: the notices say the tailgate may inadvertently unlatch while the vehicle is in Park.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The two tailgate recalls covering 2020-2024 and 2024 Silverado 2500 and 3500 HD trucks do carry one instruction: owners are advised to check that the tailgate is closed and latched before driving. That instruction belongs to those tailgate recalls only. For the hood-latch, Bolt EV door-handle cable, 2025 door striker and Corvette front trunk release recalls, the notices simply do not tell owners to stop driving; they describe the risk and the repair.

What the free repair involves

All of these repairs are free of charge. Dealers replace the hood assembly on 2020 Silverado HD trucks, the rear inside door-handle cables on the Bolt EV, the exterior touchpad switch assemblies on both tailgate recalls, and all four side door strikers and attachment bolts on the 2025 Blazer, Blazer EV and Equinox EV. The 2020 Corvette gets a Body Control Module software update, also available over the air.

Common questions

My Silverado's tailgate has never opened by itself. Do I still need the recall done?

The notices say the electronic gate-release switch may short circuit due to water intrusion and unlatch the tailgate while the truck is in Park. They advise owners to check that the tailgate is closed and latched before driving, and dealers replace the touchpad switch assembly free.

Can the Corvette front trunk recall be handled without a dealer visit?

For that recall, yes. The notice says dealers will update the Body Control Module software so the release switch always works, free of charge, and that owners may also allow the vehicle to be remedied through an over-the-air software update.

My 2025 Blazer EV is brand new. Is it in the door striker recall?

The door striker recall covers certain 2025 Blazer, Blazer EV and Equinox EV vehicles, and the notice says all affected vehicles were still in dealership inventory, so no owner letters were expected. Owners may call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020.
