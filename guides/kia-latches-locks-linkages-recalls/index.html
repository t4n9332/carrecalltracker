Kia trunk latch and sliding door recalls owners should know about

3 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Latches/locks/linkages · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 24, 2023

Two of Kia's three latch recalls are the same compliance problem: a trunk latch that stops the trunk being opened from the inside, a failure to meet Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 401. SC216 covers 2016-2018 Forte 4-door and Forte Koup and 2018-2019 Rio 4-door vehicles with a damaged trunk latch; SC278 covers 2016-2017 Rio, 2016-2018 Optima and 2017-2018 Optima Hybrid and Optima Plug-in Hybrid vehicles with a trunk latch base that may crack. The Rio shows up in both, in different model years. The third recall has nothing to do with trunks: on 2022 Carnival vehicles, the passenger-side power sliding door's remote controller assembly may malfunction so the door does not latch securely.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The passenger-side power sliding door may fail to latch and may open while the vehicle is moving — the 2022 Carnival recall.
  • The trunk cannot be opened from inside the trunk compartment — SC216 (Forte, Rio) and SC278 (Rio, Optima).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices carries a stop-driving instruction, and none tells owners to park outside. For the 2022 Carnival, Kia says a power sliding door that fails to latch may open while moving, increasing the risk of injury, but the notice stops there. For SC216 and SC278, what Kia describes is a person inside the trunk compartment becoming trapped, not anything about the vehicle in motion. In all three, the notices direct owners to have the recall repair performed and give no other interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Each of these recalls is a parts replacement. For the 2022 Carnival, dealers replace the drum and holder inside the passenger-side power sliding door release actuator's remote controller assembly. For SC216, dealers replace the trunk latch assembly. For SC278, dealers replace the trunk latch base subassembly. All three repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Is my Rio covered by one trunk latch recall or both?

The Rio model years differ between the two. SC216 covers 2018-2019 Rio 4-door vehicles with a damaged trunk latch; SC278 covers 2016-2017 Rio vehicles with a trunk latch base that may crack. Kia can confirm coverage by VIN at 1-800-333-4542.

What does the 2022 Carnival sliding door recall actually replace?

The notice says dealers replace the drum and holder inside the passenger-side power sliding door release actuator's remote controller assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 16, 2021. The defect described is a remote controller assembly that may malfunction so the door does not latch securely.

Do the trunk latch notices say anything about using the trunk?

The notices describe the failure — a trunk latch that prevents the trunk being opened from the inside — and the risk that a person inside the trunk compartment becomes trapped. They do not tell owners to stop driving, and they give no interim instruction beyond having the free repair done.
