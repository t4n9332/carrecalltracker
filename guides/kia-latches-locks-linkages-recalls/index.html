Why do some Kia trunks and doors fail to latch?

3 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Latches/locks/linkages · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-08-24

Kia's latch and lock recalls involve two unrelated defects. On the 2022 Carnival, the remote controller for the passenger-side power sliding door can malfunction, so the door may not latch securely and could open while the vehicle is moving. On other models, the trunk latch itself is defective: the 2018 Forte and 2018-2019 Rio have trunk latches that can become damaged, and the 2018 Optima and Optima Hybrid have trunk latch bases that can crack — both preventing the trunk from being opened from inside, which fails a federal safety standard for internal trunk releases. There is no shared cause between the sliding door and trunk latch defects.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A power sliding door on the 2022 Carnival that does not latch securely or opens on its own while moving
  • A trunk that cannot be opened from inside on the affected Forte, Rio, Optima, and Optima Hybrid models
  • The notices do not describe any warning sign before these failures occur

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2022 Carnival's power sliding door defect, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, though it says a door that fails to latch may open while the vehicle is moving, increasing the risk of injury. For the Forte, Rio, Optima, and Optima Hybrid trunk latch recalls, the notices likewise do not tell owners to stop driving, though they warn that a person trapped inside the trunk compartment faces an increased risk of injury.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the defective part free of charge. For the Carnival, that means replacing the drum and holder inside the power sliding door's remote controller assembly. For the Forte, Rio, Optima, and Optima Hybrid, dealers replace the trunk latch assembly or trunk latch base subassembly so the trunk can be opened from inside again.

Common questions

Why did my Kia Carnival's sliding door open while driving?

Kia recalled 2022 Carnival vehicles because the remote controller assembly for the passenger-side power sliding door can malfunction, preventing the door from latching securely, which may let it open while the vehicle is moving.

Why can't I open my Kia's trunk from inside?

The 2018 Forte, 2018-2019 Rio, and 2018 Optima and Optima Hybrid were recalled because their trunk latches can become damaged or crack, preventing the internal trunk release from working as required by federal safety standards.

Does Kia tell owners to stop driving because of these latch recalls?

No. None of the three latch recalls direct owners to stop driving or park outside. The notices describe an increased risk of injury if a door opens while moving or if someone becomes trapped in the trunk.
