Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and GLE Recalls: Wiring, Glass and Mirrors

3 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Visibility · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-05-30

These three Mercedes-Benz recalls cover unrelated defects on different vehicles rather than one repeating problem. Two involve 2018-2020 Sprinter vans: an inadequate HVAC blower motor circuit that can let the wiring harness overheat and raise fire risk, and rear side windows fitted with single-pane glass instead of laminated safety glass, which can let rear occupants be ejected in a side impact or rollover. The third involves 2025 GLE and GLS SUVs, where the windshield-mounted multi-purpose camera and rearview mirror may not be securely attached and can detach, reducing driver visibility and disabling assistance features like Active Lane Keeping Assist and Active Brake Assist.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rearview mirror or windshield-mounted camera detaching (2025 GLE/GLS)
  • Loss of driver-assistance features such as Active Lane Keeping Assist or Active Brake Assist if the camera detaches (2025 GLE/GLS)
  • The notices describe no owner-noticeable warning sign for the Sprinter blower wiring or rear window glass issues — these were identified through inspection, not a symptom.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; for all three, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. For the 2019-2020 Sprinter blower wiring recall specifically, the notice adds an interim instruction: operate the HVAC blower only in manual mode, and avoid the maximum blower setting or the lowest/highest temperature settings, whenever the engine is running, until the wiring harness and fuse are replaced.

What the free repair involves

Dealers relocate and replace the blower fuse and wiring harness on affected Sprinter vans, free of charge. On the window recall, dealers replace the single-pane rear side windows with laminated safety glass. On the GLE/GLS mirror recall, dealers replace the windshield and reinstall the multi-purpose camera and rearview mirror. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Which Sprinter years are covered by the blower wiring fire recall?

2019-2020 Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter VS30 vans. An inadequate HVAC blower motor circuit can let the wiring harness overheat, raising fire risk. Dealers relocate and replace the fuse and harness free of charge.

Why were Sprinter rear windows recalled?

2018-2019 Sprinter 1500-4500 vans were fitted with single-pane glass instead of laminated safety glass in the rear side windows, which can let rear occupants be ejected in a side impact or rollover. Dealers replace the glass free.

What happens if my GLE or GLS rearview mirror detaches?

On affected 2025 GLE and GLS models, the windshield-mounted camera and mirror may be insufficiently attached and detach, reducing visibility and disabling features like Active Brake Assist. Dealers replace the windshield and reinstall both parts free.
