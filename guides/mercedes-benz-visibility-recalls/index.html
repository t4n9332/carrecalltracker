Mercedes-Benz visibility recalls: Sprinter glass, blower wiring and GLE mirrors

3 NHTSA recalls · Mercedes-Benz · Visibility · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 30, 2025

Mercedes-Benz's visibility recalls in this group are mostly Sprinter vans from Daimler Vans USA. On 2018-2019 Sprinter 1500, 2500, 3500 and 4500 vans, the rear side windows may be single-pane safety glass instead of laminated safety glass. On 2019-2020 Sprinter VS30 vans, the HVAC blower motor circuit may be inadequate, which can allow the wiring harness to overheat. The third recall is different in kind and in vehicle: 2024 GLE 350 4MATIC and a range of 2025 GLE, GLS, Maybach GLS and AMG models, where the multi-purpose camera and the rearview mirror may be insufficiently attached to the windshield and may detach. So: two van recalls about glass and wiring, one SUV recall about what is attached to the windshield.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2024-2025 GLE and GLS recall states that a detached camera can cause a loss of certain driving assistance systems, such as Active Lane Keeping Assist and Active Brake Assist, and that a detached mirror can reduce visibility for the driver.
  • The Sprinter VS30 blower wiring notice and the Sprinter rear side window notice do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none carries a park-outside instruction. The 2019-2020 Sprinter VS30 blower recall is the one with an interim instruction: owners are advised to operate the HVAC front blower in manual, not automatic, mode whenever the engine is on, and not to use the maximum blower or the lowest or highest temperature settings until the remedy is performed. The 2018-2019 Sprinter rear side window notice and the GLE and GLS mirror notice do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the Sprinter VS30 blower recall, dealers relocate and replace the fuse and the wiring harness. For the 2018-2019 Sprinter, dealers replace the single-pane safety glass with laminated safety glass. For the GLE and GLS models, dealers replace the windshield and reinstall the multi-purpose camera and rearview mirror. Each notice says the work is done free of charge.

Common questions

Can I still run the heater and air conditioning in my Sprinter VS30?

That notice advises owners to operate the HVAC front blower in manual, not automatic, mode whenever the engine is on, and not to use the maximum blower or the lowest or highest temperature settings until the remedy is performed.

Why are my Sprinter's rear side windows being replaced?

The notice says the rear side windows may be single-pane safety glass instead of laminated safety glass, and that in a side impact crash or rollover they may not adequately protect rear occupants from being ejected. Dealers replace the glass free of charge.

Does the GLE mirror recall really mean a whole new windshield?

For that recall the notice says dealers will replace the windshield and reinstall the multi-purpose camera and rearview mirror, free of charge, because the camera and mirror may be insufficiently attached to the windshield and may detach.
