Why Are Jeep Wrangler Frames And Wagoneer Trim Recalled?

9 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Structure · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-09-10

These nine recalls split into two groups. Three affect the 2018-2022 Jeep Wrangler's frame: a 2020-2022 stud that can puncture the fuel tank in a crash, 2018-only rear underbody rail corrosion that can loosen the left rear seat mount, and 2018-2019 front track bar brackets with bad welds that can separate from the frame. The other six affect 2022-2026 Jeep Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer models, where trim and structural pieces have repeatedly failed to stay attached: B-pillar trim that can block a side curtain airbag, an incorrect instrument panel cross beam in the Wagoneer S, and door trim, window trim, liftgate hinge covers, and roof rack crossbars that can detach.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rust or corrosion on the rear underbody rail, or a loose left rear seat, on 2018 Wranglers
  • A loose or reduced feeling in the steering on 2018-2019 Wranglers
  • Door trim, window trim, a liftgate hinge cover, or roof rack crossbars that feel loose or detach on Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer models
  • The notices describe no warning sign for the frame stud, wrong cross beam, or B-pillar trim recalls

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these nine recall notices tell Jeep owners to stop driving or to park outside. For the Wrangler frame stud, rear frame rail corrosion, and track bar bracket recalls, the notices say only that dealers will inspect and repair as needed. For the Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer trim recalls - including the B-pillar trim, cross beam, door trim, window trim, hinge cover, and roof rack crossbar issues - the notices likewise do not instruct owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, dealers inspect the frame stud, rear frame rail, and track bar brackets on Wranglers and repair or coat them as needed, and they inspect, reseat, or replace trim, the instrument panel cross beam, hinge covers, and roof rack crossbar bolts on Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer models. All of this work is done free of charge.

Common questions

Does the Wrangler frame stud recall mean my fuel tank is leaking?

No. The recall covers 2020-2022 Wranglers where a frame stud could puncture the fuel tank only in a crash. The notice does not say tanks are currently leaking; dealers inspect the stud and remove it or apply paint if needed, free of charge.

Why do so many Wagoneer recalls involve trim falling off?

Chrysler has issued separate recalls for Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer door trim, window trim, and a liftgate hinge cover that were not properly secured during assembly. Each notice says detached trim can become a road hazard for other vehicles, and dealers replace the pieces free of charge.

Is my 2018 Wrangler's frame rusting through?

The 2018-only recall covers the rear underbody ladder rail, which may corrode enough to loosen the left rear seat mount and reduce floor pan strength. Dealers coat the rail with rust protection free of charge; the notice does not describe a specific warning sign to look for.
