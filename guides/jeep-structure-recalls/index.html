What Jeep structure recalls cover Wrangler frames and Wagoneer trim parts?

9 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Structure · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-09-10

This group splits cleanly by model. The Wrangler recalls are frame and body structure: a 2018 rear underbody ladder rail that may corrode and loosen the left rear seat mount, improperly welded front track bar brackets on 2018-2019 models, and a frame stud on 2020-2022 Wranglers that may puncture the fuel tank in a crash. Everything else involves the Wagoneer family: B-pillar trim on 2022-2023 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer that may interfere with side curtain air bag deployment, an incorrect instrument panel cross car beam on the 2025 Wagoneer S, and four recalls over parts that may detach - door trim on 2022-2025 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer, quarter window trim on 2022-2024 models, a Wagoneer S liftgate hinge cover, and roof rack crossbar pivot bolts.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these nine notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park outside. That includes the 2020-2022 Wrangler frame stud recall, where the notice describes a fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source, and the two federal compliance recalls - the Wagoneer B-pillar trim and the Wagoneer S cross car beam - where the stated risk is reduced occupant protection in a crash. The four recalls over detaching parts describe a road hazard for other vehicles. The notices direct owners to the dealer repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and, if necessary, remove the Wrangler frame stud and apply paint; coat the 2018 Wrangler ladder rail with three-part rust protection; repair the track bar bracket welds; reseat or replace the B-pillar trim; and replace the cross beam, door trim, quarter window glass assembly and roof rack pivot bolts. The liftgate hinge cover is repaired or replaced. Every notice but the ladder rail recall states the work is free.

Common questions

Is the roof rack crossbar repair available yet?

The notice says interim letters telling owners about the safety risk are expected to be mailed October 8, 2026, and that a second notice will be sent once the final remedy is available. Dealers will replace the roof rack crossbar pivot bolts, free of charge.

Why are so many of these Wagoneer recalls about trim?

Four separate notices cover parts that may detach: door trim on 2022-2025 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer, quarter window trim on 2022-2024 models, a Wagoneer S liftgate hinge cover, and roof rack crossbars. Each says a detached part creates a road hazard for other vehicles.

Does the B-pillar trim recall mean my side airbag will not work?

The notice says the upper B-pillar interior trim may not be fully seated and could interfere with side curtain air bag deployment, which can reduce occupant protection in a crash. Dealers inspect and reseat or replace the trim pieces as necessary, free of charge.
