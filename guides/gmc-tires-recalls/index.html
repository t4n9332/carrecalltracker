GMC tire recalls: over-cured tires, wrong spares and bad labels

7 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Tires · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-13

Five of these seven GMC recalls are about the tires themselves, and one cause repeats: tires cured for too long during production. Three separate over-curing recalls, two of them naming Continental tires, reach 2019-2020 GMC Acadia and Sierra 1500, 2020 Yukon and Yukon XL, and 2020-2021 Sierra 1500, with a sidewall break causing sudden air loss or a belt edge separation as the described result. A fourth covers 2021-2022 GMC Terrain built with certain Hankook tires made with an insufficient amount of curative agent. The rest are equipment mismatches on pickups: a 17-inch spare that is not compatible with accessory road wheels on 2019-2021 Sierra 1500 and 2020-2021 Sierra 2500, and an incorrect rear tire pressure label on 2024-2025 Sierra 2500.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The tire pressure monitoring system may not display a low tire pressure warning — the 2024-2025 Sierra 2500 tire information label recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. The three over-curing recalls covering 2019-2020 Acadia and Sierra 1500, 2020 Yukon and Yukon XL and 2020-2021 Sierra 1500 state that a sidewall break causing sudden air loss, or a belt edge separation leading to tread and belt loss, can cause a loss of vehicle control. The Terrain Hankook recall, the two spare tire recalls and the 2024-2025 Sierra 2500 label recall each describe the crash risk only, without telling owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

All repairs here are free of charge. On the over-curing recalls dealers inspect the tires and replace them as necessary, and on the Terrain recall they inspect the tire DOT numbers and replace affected tires. For the spare tire recalls dealers supply a compatible spare and a new spare tire information label. On the 2024-2025 Sierra 2500 recall dealers update the engine control module software, apply a label overlay and inflate the tires.

Common questions

How do I find out whether my GMC has the recalled tires?

The notices leave the identifying to dealers. On the over-curing recalls dealers inspect the tires, in one case replacing those with DOT number and production mold number combinations identified by Continental, and on the Terrain recall they inspect tire DOT numbers before replacing affected tires.

Why would a spare tire be a safety recall?

On 2020-2021 Sierra 2500 with a 17-inch spare and 20-inch or 18-inch accessory road tires, the notice says operating that spare can prevent the anti-lock braking system from engaging. A separate recall says the same spare is not compatible with accessory wheels on 2019-2021 Sierra 1500.

What is wrong with the tire pressure label on the 2024-2025 Sierra 2500?

That notice covers trucks with the enhanced towing option and the 20-inch accessory tire and wheel package. It says the tire information label may state the incorrect rear tire pressure, resulting in underinflated tires and preventing the monitoring system from displaying a low pressure warning.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
