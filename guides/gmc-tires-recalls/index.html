Why do GMC trucks keep getting tire and spare tire recalls?

7 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Tires · model years 2019–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-13

Seven GMC-related tire recalls fall into three groups. Three separate recalls, issued in 2020 and 2021, cover Continental tires cured too long during production on 2019-2021 GMC Sierra 1500 and Acadia, and 2020 GMC Yukon and Yukon XL, along with related Chevrolet, Buick and Cadillac models; overcuring can cause a sidewall break or belt edge separation. A 2021 recall covers Hankook tires with insufficient curative agent on 2021-2022 Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain, which can cause tread separation. Two more recalls, from 2021, cover a 17-inch spare tire that is incompatible with 18- or 20-inch accessory wheels on Sierra 1500 and Sierra 2500 trucks, which can prevent the anti-lock brake system from working correctly. The newest, from 2026, covers a wrong tire pressure label on 2024-2025 Sierra 2500 trucks with enhanced towing.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A sudden loss of tire air pressure, or a break in the tire sidewall (overcured Continental tires on 2019-2021 Sierra 1500 and Acadia, and 2020 Yukon and Yukon XL)
  • Tread separating from a tire (Hankook tires on 2021-2022 Equinox and Terrain)
  • Reduced anti-lock brake control of the rear wheels when driving on the 17-inch spare tire with 18- or 20-inch accessory wheels (Sierra 1500 and Sierra 2500)
  • Rear tires that read as properly inflated on the label but are actually underinflated, with no low-pressure warning from the TPMS (2024-2025 Sierra 2500)
  • The notices describe no advance warning sign before an overcured tire fails

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. That covers the three overcured-tire recalls, the Hankook tread separation recall on Equinox and Terrain, the two spare-tire recalls affecting the anti-lock brake system, and the tire pressure label recall on Sierra 2500 trucks. For each of these, the notices describe the risk from the defect but do not tell owners to stop driving, avoid highway speeds, or park the vehicle away from structures.

What the free repair involves

Across these recalls, dealers inspect tire DOT and mold numbers and replace any tires that match the affected codes for the overcured Continental and Hankook recalls, supply a compatible spare tire and wheel plus a new spare tire information label for the ABS recalls, and update engine control module software, apply a label overlay, and set correct pressures for the 2026 label recall. All of this is free.

Common questions

Are the three GMC overcured tire recalls all the same problem?

Yes, all three involve Continental tires cured too long during production, which can cause a sidewall break or belt edge separation. They were issued separately in 2020 and 2021 to cover different DOT and production mold number combinations.

Can I use my 17-inch spare tire with accessory wheels?

The notices say operating the 17-inch spare with 18- or 20-inch accessory road tires can prevent the anti-lock brake system from engaging correctly. Dealers provide a compatible spare tire, wheel, and updated label free of charge.

How do I know if my Sierra 2500's tire pressure label is wrong?

This recall covers 2024-2025 Sierra 2500 trucks with the enhanced towing option and 20-inch accessory tire/wheel package. Dealers correct the label, reprogram software, and reinflate tires as part of the free repair.
