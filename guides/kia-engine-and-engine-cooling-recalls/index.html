What keeps going wrong with Kia engines in these recalls?

6 NHTSA recalls · Kia · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 25, 2025

Two patterns dominate these six Kia engine recalls: turbocharger oil lines that leak, and internal engine parts that fail. SC233 covers 2022 Sorento, where the oil supply pipe to the turbocharger may crack, and SC300 covers 2019-2020 K900 and 2018-2023 Stinger with the 3.3-liter turbo GDI engine, where the left turbocharger oil feed pipe and hose may deteriorate and leak. On the internal side, SC296 covers 2024 Telluride valve springs, SC349 covers 2025 K4 and Sorento connecting rod bolts, SC210 covers 2017-2018 Forte with the 2.0L Nu MPI engine and its oil pump, and the 2021-2023 Seltos and Soul recall covers piston oil rings. Fire and loss of drive power are the risks named.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall SC210: the notice says a damaged engine may stall on 2017-2018 Forte with the 2.0L Nu MPI engine.
  • Recall SC296: the notice says the valve springs may break while driving on 2024 Telluride, which can result in a loss of drive power.
  • Recall SC349: the notice says improperly tightened connecting rod bolts on 2025 K4 and Sorento can cause a loss of drive power.
  • The 2021-2023 Seltos and Soul piston oil ring recall: the notice says engine damage can cause a vehicle stall or fire.
  • Recalls SC233 and SC300: the notices describe an oil leak in the engine compartment.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park the vehicle outside. SC233, SC296, SC300, SC349, SC210 and the 2021-2023 Seltos and Soul piston oil ring recall each describe the defect and the fire, stall or crash risk it creates, then direct owners to a dealer. None sets a mileage limit, a driving restriction or a temporary measure to follow until the remedy is done. Nothing further is stated in the recall documents, and nothing further is added here.

What the free repair involves

Replacement is the rule here. SC233 replaces the turbocharger oil supply pipe and SC300 replaces the left turbocharger oil feed pipe and hose assembly. SC296 replaces the engine sub-assembly, SC349 replaces the sub engine assembly, and SC210 replaces the oil pan. The Seltos and Soul recall has dealers inspect and replace the engine as necessary and install piston-ring noise sensing system software. All free of charge.

Common questions

Does the Stinger turbo oil leak recall cover every Stinger?

SC300 covers certain 2018-2023 Stinger and 2019-2020 K900 vehicles equipped with the 3.3-liter turbo gasoline direct injection engine, where the left turbocharger oil feed pipe and hose assembly may deteriorate and leak oil. Other engines are outside that recall.

What is the piston-ring noise sensing software Kia installs on my Seltos?

The notice for the 2021-2023 Seltos and Soul recall says dealers will inspect and replace the engine as necessary and also install piston-ring noise sensing system software, free of charge. The notice does not describe how that software works.

My 2025 Sorento is in a recall — is it the engine bolts?

SC349 covers certain 2025 K4 and Sorento vehicles for connecting rod bolts that may have been improperly tightened. Kia has other 2025 Sorento recalls outside this engine group, so run your VIN to see every open recall on your vehicle.
