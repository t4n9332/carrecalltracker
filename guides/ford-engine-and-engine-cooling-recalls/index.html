Ford Engine Recalls Cover Oil Leaks, Stalling, and Cooling Problems

14 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 25, 2026

Oil leaks and engine failures dominate these Ford engine notices. Escape appears repeatedly: the 2021 Hybrid crankshaft recall concerns engine damage; the 2020-2022 Escape and 2021-2022 Bronco Sport oil-separator recall concerns a cracked housing; and the July 2022 hybrid notice covers fluid release following engine failure. Separate December 2024 and March 2025 cup-plug notices include 2024-2025 F-150 trucks, with Expedition coverage differing between notices. Explorer appears in turbocharger oil-line, intake-valve, connecting-rod, and EGR-valve recalls. Other recurring concerns include lost oil pressure in 2016-2018 Focus and 2018-2022 EcoSport vehicles, delayed cooling protection in 2019-2020 Transit Connect vans, and fuel leaks from certain 2018 Mustang aftermarket supercharger kits.

Warning signs to watch for

  • July 2022 Escape/Maverick/Corsair engine-fire recall: unexpected engine noises, reduced vehicle power, or smoke.
  • August 2023 Explorer turbo oil-line recall: an oil-pressure warning light or smoke from the engine compartment.
  • March 2026 EGR-valve recall: unexpected loss of drive power.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The supplied engine notices contain no park-outside instruction or unconditional stop-driving instruction. The July 2022 Escape/Maverick/Corsair engine-fire notice advises parking and shutting off the engine as quickly as possible for unexpected engine noises, reduced power, or smoke. The August 2023 Explorer turbo oil-line notice advises parking and shutting off the engine as soon as possible for an oil-pressure warning light or engine-compartment smoke. The remaining supplied engine notices do not tell owners to stop driving. These two conditional shutdown instructions belong to their respective recalls.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies depend on the defect. Recall 20S60 updates Transit Connect PCM software; 22S21 inspects the oil separator and replaces it and seals as necessary. The August 2023 Explorer recall replaces the turbo oil supply line assembly. Recalls 24S70 and 25S30 inspect cup-plug alignment and replace the plug if necessary. Recall 26S10 replaces EGR valves.

Common questions

Which Ford engine recalls tell me to shut the engine off?

The July 2022 hybrid engine-fire notice gives shutdown instructions for unexpected noises, reduced power, or smoke. The August 2023 Explorer turbo oil-line notice gives shutdown instructions for an oil-pressure warning light or smoke from the engine compartment.

What is the oil-pump recall repair for Focus and EcoSport?

The December 2023 notice covers certain 2016-2018 Focus and 2018-2022 EcoSport vehicles. Dealers replace the oil-pump tensioner assembly and drive belt free of charge. The notice also authorizes engine replacement as an interim repair for specified belt-related engine failures.

Does the intake-valve recall require replacing my Ford engine?

Recall 24S55 covers specified 2021-2022 Bronco, F-150, Edge, Explorer, Lincoln Nautilus, and Lincoln Aviator vehicles with 2.7L or 3.0L Nano EcoBoost engines. Dealers perform an engine cycle test and replace the engine as necessary, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
