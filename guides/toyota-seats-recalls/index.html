Toyota Seat Recalls Address Loose Bolts, Welds, and Seatback Locking

6 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Seats · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 20, 2026

Sienna Hybrid appears in three of these six Toyota seat recalls, covering third-row seatback bolts on certain 2021-2025 vehicles, second-row rail welds on certain 2025 vehicles, and second-row seatbacks on certain 2022-2026 seven-passenger vehicles. Weld problems also appear separately in the 2025 RAV4 front seat brackets and 2023-2024 Camry and Camry Hybrid rear head restraint brackets. Certain 2021-2025 Highlander and Highlander Hybrid vehicles have second-row seatbacks that may fail to lock during adjustment. Across these notices, the recurring concern is reduced occupant restraint or protection during a crash. Sienna Hybrid has the most separate notices, spanning both second-row and third-row seating components.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2026-03-05 Highlander and Highlander Hybrid recall: second-row seatbacks may fail to lock into position during seatback adjustment.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the six supplied Toyota notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices covering Sienna Hybrid third-row bolts, Sienna Hybrid second-row rail welds, Highlander and Highlander Hybrid second-row seatback locking, RAV4 front seat bracket welds, Sienna Hybrid second-row seatback security, and Camry and Camry Hybrid head restraint bracket welds do not tell owners to stop driving. Their lack of a stop-driving instruction does not establish that an affected vehicle is safe to drive.

What the free repair involves

Free dealer remedies include tightening Sienna third-row bolts, replacing 2025 Sienna second-row rails, replacing Highlander recliner return springs, inspecting and repairing RAV4 bracket welds as necessary, and replacing the Camry 60% rear seat-back frame sub-assembly. For the October 2026 Sienna recall, dealers update SRS ECU software; vehicles with the MaaS package receive two replacement second-row seatbacks.

Common questions

Which seating rows are covered by the Sienna Hybrid recalls?

The February 2025 notice covers third-row seatback bolts on certain 2021-2025 vehicles. The October 2025 notice covers second-row rails on certain 2025 vehicles. The October 2026 notice covers second-row seatbacks on certain 2022-2026 seven-passenger vehicles.

What does Toyota replace for the Highlander seatback locking problem?

For certain 2021-2025 Highlander and Highlander Hybrid vehicles, dealers replace the return springs in the seatback recliner assemblies, free of charge. The notice describes second-row seatbacks that may fail to lock into position during seatback adjustment.

Does the October 2026 Sienna recall require software or replacement seatbacks?

The notice specifies a dealer update to the supplemental restraint system electronic control unit software. For vehicles equipped with the Mobility as a Service package, dealers replace the two second-row seatbacks. Repairs are performed free of charge.
