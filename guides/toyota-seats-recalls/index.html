Which Toyota seats are under recall for loose bolts and welds?

5 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Seats · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 22, 2026

Across these five Toyota recalls the recurring theme is how the seat structure is held together. Welding appears three times: the second-row seat rails on the 2025 Sienna Hybrid, the front seat brackets for the seat rails on the 2025 RAV4, and the second-row center and right-side head restraint brackets on the 2023-2024 Camry and Camry Hybrid. Bolts are the issue on 2021-2025 Sienna Hybrid third-row seatbacks, which Toyota says do not meet FMVSS 202, 207 and 225. The 2021-2025 Highlander and Highlander Hybrid recall is different again: second-row seat backs may fail to lock into position during adjustment. Sienna Hybrid appears in two of the five, and second-row seats are involved in three.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2021-2025 Highlander and Highlander Hybrid recall says the second-row seat backs may fail to lock into position during seat back adjustment.
  • The other four notices in this group do not describe a warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. Each describes the risk in crash terms: a seatback, seat rail, seat bracket or head restraint bracket that may not restrain an occupant as intended during a crash, increasing the risk of injury. Beyond that, the notices say nothing about using the vehicle before the repair is done. They give no interim instruction about which seats to use or who may sit in them.

What the free repair involves

Dealers tighten the third-row seat assembly bolts on the Sienna Hybrid and replace the second-row seat rails on the 2025 Sienna Hybrid. On the Highlander they replace the seat back recliner return springs; on the 2025 RAV4 they inspect and repair the seat bracket welds as necessary; on the Camry they replace the 60% rear seat-back frame sub-assembly. Each notice says the work is free of charge.

Common questions

How do I know which of these seat recalls applies to my Toyota?

The notices list Toyota's own recall numbers: 25TA05 for the Sienna third-row bolts, 25TB12 and 25TA12 for the Sienna seat rails, 26TB06 and 26TA06 for the Highlander, 26TB09 and 26TA09 for the RAV4, and 24TB04 and 24TA04 for the Camry. Toyota's customer service line is 1-800-331-4331.

Do these recalls cover hybrid versions as well?

It depends on the recall. Both Sienna recalls name Sienna Hybrid only. The Camry recall names 2023-2024 Camry and Camry Hybrid, and the Highlander recall names 2021-2025 Highlander and Highlander Hybrid. The RAV4 recall names 2025 RAV4 vehicles.

Should I leave the affected seats empty until the repair?

The notices do not tell owners to stop driving and give no instruction about leaving a seat unused or limiting passengers. They set out only the defect, the crash risk it describes, and the free repair a Toyota dealer performs.
