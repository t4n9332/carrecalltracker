Why Do Toyota Seat Bolts And Brackets Keep Failing Recalls?

5 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Seats · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-22

Five Toyota seat recalls share one theme: components meant to hold seats and head restraints in place were not properly tightened or welded during assembly. Two involve the Sienna Hybrid — third-row seatback bolts (2021-2025) and second-row seat rail welds (2025 only). A 2021-2025 Highlander and Highlander Hybrid recall covers second-row seat backs that may fail to lock when adjusted. A 2025 RAV4 recall covers improperly welded front seat brackets, and a 2023-2024 Camry and Camry Hybrid recall covers poorly welded rear head restraint brackets. In every case the concern is the same: a seat or head restraint that will not properly restrain an occupant in a crash.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Highlander second-row seat back that fails to lock into position when you adjust it
  • No warning sign described for the Sienna third-row seatback bolt recall
  • No warning sign described for the Sienna second-row seat rail weld recall
  • No warning sign described for the RAV4 front seat bracket weld recall
  • No warning sign described for the Camry rear head restraint bracket weld recall

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five Toyota seat recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the Sienna Hybrid third-row seatback bolts, Sienna Hybrid second-row seat rails, Highlander and Highlander Hybrid second-row seat backs, RAV4 front seat brackets, and Camry and Camry Hybrid head restraint brackets, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice states only that the repair will be made free of charge.

What the free repair involves

Across these five recalls, Toyota dealers tighten the third-row seatback bolts, replace the second-row seat rails, replace the return springs in the second-row recliner assembly, inspect and repair the front seat bracket welds, and replace the rear seat-back frame sub-assembly. Toyota performs every one of these repairs at no cost to the owner.

Common questions

Do I need to stop driving because of these Toyota seat recalls?

No. None of these five recalls tells owners to stop driving; the notices only instruct owners to bring the vehicle to a dealer to have the bolts, welds, or seat parts repaired for free.

Is my Toyota Sienna Hybrid affected by more than one seat recall?

Yes. The Sienna Hybrid has two separate seat recalls: 2021-2025 models for loose third-row seatback bolts, and 2025 models for improperly welded second-row seat rails.

What will the dealer actually do to fix my seat?

It depends on the recall: dealers tighten bolts, replace seat rails or return springs, or inspect and repair welded brackets, then replace parts as needed - always free of charge.
