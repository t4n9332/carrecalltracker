Jeep seat belt recalls: buckles, retractors and pretensioner failures

5 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 13, 2024

These Jeep seat belt recalls split between hardware in the front seats and buckles in the rows behind. Two cover front seat belt retractors: 2019 Wrangler, 2019 Ram 2500, 2019-2020 Ram 1500 and 2020 Gladiator vehicles whose retractors may not properly slow an occupant after locking, and 2021 Wrangler, 2022 Gladiator and 2022 Ram 1500 vehicles with an improperly welded micro gas generator that can cause pretensioner failure. The other three involve buckles: a mounting strap that may fracture on 2011-2018 right-hand-drive Wranglers, third-row belts that may not be accessible or fastenable on 2022-2024 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer, and an improperly tightened second-row buckle bolt on the 2023 Grand Cherokee. Wrangler and Gladiator recur across the group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2022-2024 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer recall says the third-row seat belts may not allow occupants to properly access or fasten the seat belt to the buckle.
  • The 2011-2018 right-hand-drive Wrangler recall says a separated or severed buckle strap will result in an inoperable seat belt.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Each describes a risk that arises in the event of a crash: a belt that cannot lock, restrain or be fastened may not protect the occupant. For the 2011-2018 right-hand-drive Wrangler recall, the notice says interim letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed November 19, 2019, with a second letter when the recall began March 26, 2020. The notices give owners no other instruction beyond scheduling the repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the driver's seat belt buckle on the right-hand-drive Wrangler recall and replace the front seat belt retractors on both retractor recalls. On the Wagoneer recall they inspect and replace the third-row seat belt buckle as necessary, and on the 2023 Grand Cherokee they inspect and tighten the second-row buckle bolt as necessary. Each notice says the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Which Jeeps are in the front seat belt retractor recalls?

The 2020 recall names 2019 Wrangler, 2019 Ram 2500, 2019-2020 Ram 1500 and 2020 Gladiator vehicles whose retractors may not properly slow an occupant after locking. The 2022 recall names 2021 Wrangler, 2022 Gladiator and 2022 Ram 1500 vehicles with pretensioner failure.

Does the right-hand-drive Wrangler recall apply to my regular Wrangler?

That notice covers 2011-2018 Jeep Wrangler right-hand-drive vehicles, where the driver's seat belt buckle mounting strap may fracture and separate from the seat frame. Checking your VIN with a dealer or NHTSA's lookup is the way to confirm whether yours is listed.

What if a third-row belt in my Wagoneer will not fasten?

The notice for 2022-2024 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer says third-row belts may not allow occupants to properly access or fasten the belt, and that dealers will inspect and replace the third-row buckle as necessary, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed November 16, 2023.
