Why do so many Jeep seat belts fail to lock or fasten?

5 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-06-13

These five recalls involve different seat belt hardware failures across separate Jeep models. A 2019 recall covers a driver's seat belt buckle mounting strap that can fracture on right-hand-drive Wranglers. A 2020 recall covers front seat belt retractors on the 2019 Wrangler and 2020 Gladiator (shared with certain Ram trucks) that may not properly slow an occupant in a crash. A 2022 recall covers a seat belt pretensioner that can fail due to an improperly welded micro gas generator on the 2021 Wrangler and 2022 Gladiator. Separately, Jeep's Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer (2022-2024) were recalled because third-row seat belts may not let occupants fasten them, and the 2023 Grand Cherokee was recalled for a loose second-row seat belt buckle bolt.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A separated or fractured driver's seat belt buckle mounting strap (2018 Wrangler, right-hand drive)
  • A front seat belt that does not properly slow an occupant, noticeable only in a crash (Wrangler/Gladiator)
  • A third-row seat belt that cannot be accessed or fastened to its buckle (Wagoneer/Grand Wagoneer)
  • A loose second-row seat belt buckle bolt (2023 Grand Cherokee)
  • The notice describes no driver-noticeable warning sign for the pretensioner weld defect (2021 Wrangler/2022 Gladiator)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five seat belt recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. That covers the driver's buckle strap recall, the front seat belt retractor recall, the seat belt pretensioner weld recall, the third-row buckle recall on Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer, and the second-row buckle bolt recall on the Grand Cherokee. Each notice states that the affected seat belt may not properly restrain or protect an occupant in a crash, increasing the risk of injury, but none of the notices tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace or repair the affected seat belt hardware at no cost: the driver's seat belt buckle, front seat belt retractors, seat belt pretensioners, third-row seat belt buckles, and the second-row buckle mounting bolt, inspecting and replacing parts as necessary. All repairs described in these notices are free to the owner.

Common questions

Is it dangerous to drive a Jeep with a seat belt recall?

The notices describe an increased risk of injury in a crash if the affected seat belt buckle, retractor, or pretensioner fails, but none instruct owners to stop driving. Dealers repair the seat belt hardware free of charge.

Why can't my Jeep Wagoneer's third-row seat belt fasten?

FCA recalled 2022-2024 Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer models because the third-row seat belts may not let occupants properly access or fasten the belt to the buckle, which fails federal seat belt standards.

What is wrong with the Jeep Wrangler seat belt pretensioner recall?

The 2021 Wrangler and 2022 Gladiator were recalled because the front seat belt retractor's micro gas generator may have been improperly welded, which can cause the pretensioner to fail during a crash.
