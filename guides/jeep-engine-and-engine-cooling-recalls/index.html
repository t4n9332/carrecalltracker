Which Jeep engine recalls cause stalling, fires, or lost power?

6 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-12-12

Six recalls, and most trace back to two things: exhaust gas recirculation hardware and engines that quit running. The 3.0L EcoDiesel Grand Cherokee appears twice — 2014-2019 models whose EGR cooler can crack and let vaporized coolant combust inside the intake manifold, and 2014-2020 models (alongside Ram 1500) whose crankshaft position sensor tone wheel can delaminate and stall the engine. The 3.6L V6 in 2021-2022 Grand Cherokee, Grand Cherokee L and Dodge Durango can lose drive power when the EGR valve sticks open. Four-cylinder Cherokees form the other cluster: a cracked oil pump housing on 2018 Cherokee and Compass 2.4L engines, and missing valve stem keepers plus a reversed camshaft cap on 2019 Cherokee 2.0L engines. A 2024 Grand Cherokee radiator crossmember bolt recall rounds it out.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine stalls or loses drive power with no warning while you are driving
  • Smoke, a burning smell, or fire from the engine compartment on 3.0L EcoDiesel models
  • Rough running or engine damage if a valve drops into a cylinder on the 2.0L Cherokee
  • Loss of oil pressure or oil pump failure on 2.4L Cherokee and Compass engines
  • The 2024 Grand Cherokee loose crossmember bolts give no warning at all — the danger only shows up in a crash

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these six recalls tells you to stop driving or park outside. The diesel EGR cooler notice says only to get it repaired as soon as parts are available, and the tone wheel recall expects you to drive to the dealer for the software update. Still, four of them — oil pump, valve keepers, EGR valve, tone wheel — mean power can disappear in traffic, so book the appointment rather than waiting. If your EcoDiesel ever smokes or smells of burning under the hood, stop and have it towed.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the EGR cooler and inspect or replace the intake manifold, replace the 3.6L EGR valve assembly, inspect and replace the oil pump, inspect and repair or replace the 2.0L valve train parts, secure the radiator crossmember bolts, and update powertrain control module software for the diesel tone wheel. Every repair is free. The EGR cooler recall opened before parts were available.

Common questions

My Grand Cherokee is an EcoDiesel — am I in both diesel recalls?

Possibly. The EGR cooler fire recall covers 2014-2019 3.0L EcoDiesel Grand Cherokees; the crankshaft tone wheel stall recall covers 2014-2020 Grand Cherokees and 2014-2019 Ram 1500 diesels. Run your VIN to see which apply to your truck.

Why is a radiator bolt recall filed under engine cooling?

The radiator crossmember bolts sit in the cooling structure, but the hazard is elsewhere: on 2024 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L, unsecured bolts can cause improper airbag deployment and a fuel leak in a crash. Dealers inspect and secure them.

Can a software update really stop the diesel from stalling?

That is the remedy Chrysler filed. Dealers reprogram the powertrain control module so the engine keeps propulsion when the tone wheel delaminates. Interim letters warning owners of the risk went out August 3, 2023, and the update is free.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
