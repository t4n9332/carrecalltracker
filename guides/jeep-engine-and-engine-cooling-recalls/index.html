What Jeep engine and cooling recalls say about stalling and fire risk

6 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 12, 2024

Six Jeep recalls cover engine and engine cooling, and stalling or a loss of drive power runs through four of them. Exhaust gas recirculation appears twice: 2014-2019 Grand Cherokee 3.0L EcoDiesel models can crack an EGR cooler, letting vaporized coolant combust in the intake manifold, while 2021 Durango and Grand Cherokee WK, 2022 Grand Cherokee and 2021-2022 Grand Cherokee L 3.6L models can have an EGR valve stick open and lose drive power. The Cherokee appears twice more: a cracked oil pump housing on 2018 Cherokee and Compass 2.4L engines, and missing valve stem keepers or a reversed camshaft cap on 2019 Cherokee 2.0L engines. A 3.0L diesel crankshaft position sensor tone wheel on 2014-2020 Grand Cherokee models, and loose radiator crossmember bolts on 2024 Grand Cherokee models, complete the group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The engine may stall — the 2018 Cherokee and Compass 2.4L oil pump recall, the 2019 Cherokee 2.0L engine recall, and the 3.0L diesel crankshaft position sensor tone wheel recall.
  • An unexpected loss of drive power — the 2021 Durango and Grand Cherokee 3.6L EGR valve recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the six notices in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside or away from structures. The 2014-2019 Grand Cherokee EcoDiesel notice describes coolant combusting in the intake manifold and an increased risk of fire, and the 2024 Grand Cherokee crossmember bolt notice describes a fuel leak and improper air bag deployment in a crash, but neither gives owners an interim instruction. The stall and loss of drive power notices likewise say only that the risk of a crash increases.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the EGR cooler and inspect the intake manifold on the EcoDiesel Grand Cherokee recall, replace the EGR valve assembly on the 3.6L recall, inspect and replace the oil pump on 2018 Cherokee and Compass, repair or replace affected engine components on the 2019 Cherokee 2.0L, update powertrain control module software on the 3.0L diesel tone wheel recall, and secure the 2024 crossmember bolts. All are free of charge.

Common questions

Which Jeep engines are covered by the EGR recalls?

Two: 2014-2019 Grand Cherokee with the 3.0L EcoDiesel, where the EGR cooler may crack, and 2021 Durango and Grand Cherokee WK, 2022 Grand Cherokee and 2021-2022 Grand Cherokee L with 3.6L engines, where the EGR valve may stick open.

What does the diesel tone wheel recall do to my Grand Cherokee?

The notice covers 2014-2020 Grand Cherokee and 2014-2019 Ram 1500 with 3.0L diesel engines. A delaminating crankshaft position sensor tone wheel can cost the engine its fuel injector and camshaft timing synchronization, possibly causing a stall. Dealers update powertrain control module software free of charge.

Is there anything I should do while I wait for the EcoDiesel EGR repair?

The notice does not give owners an interim instruction and does not tell them to stop driving. It says dealers will replace the EGR cooler and inspect and, as necessary, replace the intake manifold, free of charge, and that parts were not available.
