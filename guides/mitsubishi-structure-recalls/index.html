What Mitsubishi Outlander liftgate and structure recalls mean for owners

3 NHTSA recalls · Mitsubishi · Structure · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Apr 20, 2026

Two of the three recalls here cover the same part: the liftgate gas spring cylinders on 2018-2022 Outlander PHEV and 2014-2020 Outlander vehicles, which may corrode and lose pressure. The 2025 recall is limited to vehicles sold or currently registered in 27 listed states and the District of Columbia; the 2026 recall of the same models carries no such list. The third recall is far smaller: it covers one 2022 Outlander whose center pillar may have been welded incorrectly, which Mitsubishi says leaves the vehicle out of compliance with the standards for side impact protection, seat belt assembly anchorages and roof crush resistance. The Outlander name covers the whole group, and the PHEV model years listed run later than the gasoline Outlander years.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Both liftgate recalls state that the gas spring cylinders may corrode and lose pressure.
  • Both liftgate recalls state that a gas spring cylinder may rupture or the liftgate may fall unexpectedly.
  • The 2021 center pillar recall does not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the three notices in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. The two liftgate gas spring recalls describe the injury risk from a cylinder rupture or a liftgate falling unexpectedly, but give no instruction about using the liftgate while waiting for parts. For the single 2022 Outlander with the center pillar concern, the notice says only that the reduced strength of the vehicle body may increase the risk of injury in a crash.

What the free repair involves

For both liftgate recalls, Mitsubishi will replace the left and right liftgate gas springs, free of charge. The center pillar recall is different: Mitsubishi says it will repurchase and replace the affected vehicle, free of charge, and describes no repair to the pillar. No software update or over-the-air remedy is described anywhere in this group.

Common questions

Why are there two liftgate gas spring recalls for the same Outlanders?

The notices describe the same defect, cylinders that corrode and lose pressure, but different populations. The 2025 notice lists vehicles sold or currently registered in 27 states and the District of Columbia; the 2026 notice lists the same model years without that restriction.

What does repurchase and replace mean for the center pillar recall?

That notice covers one 2022 Outlander and says Mitsubishi will repurchase and replace the affected vehicle, free of charge. It describes no dealer repair to the center pillar. Owners may contact Mitsubishi customer service at 1-888-648-7820 about recall SR-21-009.

Is my Outlander PHEV covered by the liftgate recalls?

Both liftgate notices list 2018-2022 Outlander PHEV along with 2014-2020 Outlander vehicles. Whether your specific vehicle is included depends on the VIN, and for the 2025 recall also on where the vehicle was sold or is currently registered.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
