Why Is Mitsubishi Recalling The Outlander For Liftgate And Structural Problems?

3 NHTSA recalls · Mitsubishi · Structure · model years 2018–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-04-20

Two of these three Mitsubishi recalls involve the same defect: on 2018-2020 Outlander and 2018-2022 Outlander PHEV models, the liftgate's gas spring cylinders can corrode, lose pressure, and either rupture or let the liftgate fall unexpectedly. Mitsubishi first recalled this defect in 2025 for vehicles registered in specific states, then expanded the recall in 2026 to cover the same models more broadly. The third recall is unrelated and affected only a single 2022 Outlander, whose center pillar was welded incorrectly because of a robot programming error, weakening the vehicle's side-impact protection, seat belt anchorages, and roof crush resistance. The liftgate spring problem is this group's clear recurring pattern; the welding defect was an isolated production mistake.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Liftgate that falls unexpectedly instead of staying propped open
  • A liftgate gas spring cylinder that ruptures when the liftgate is opened
  • Visible corrosion on the liftgate's gas spring cylinders
  • No stated warning sign for the center pillar welding defect on the affected 2022 Outlander

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the two liftgate recalls on 2018-2020 Outlander and 2018-2022 Outlander PHEV models, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving, even though a ruptured gas spring or a falling liftgate can increase the risk of injury. For the 2022 Outlander center pillar recall, the notice also does not tell the owner to stop driving, despite the reduced crash protection described in the notice.

What the free repair involves

For the liftgate recalls, Mitsubishi dealers replace the left and right liftgate gas springs. For the center pillar recall, Mitsubishi will repurchase and replace the single affected 2022 Outlander rather than repair it. All of these remedies are performed free of charge to the owner.

Common questions

Do I need to do anything right now for the Outlander liftgate recall?

The notices for both liftgate recalls do not instruct owners to stop driving. Once you receive your notification letter, contact a Mitsubishi dealer to schedule the free replacement of the left and right liftgate gas springs.

Is my Outlander affected by the center pillar recall?

This recall covers only one 2022 Outlander vehicle, identified by its specific VIN, whose center pillar was welded incorrectly due to a robot programming error. Mitsubishi will repurchase and replace that vehicle free of charge.

What's actually wrong with the Outlander liftgate?

The gas spring cylinders that hold the liftgate open can corrode over time and lose pressure, which can cause a cylinder to rupture or let the liftgate fall unexpectedly. Mitsubishi replaces both springs free of charge.
