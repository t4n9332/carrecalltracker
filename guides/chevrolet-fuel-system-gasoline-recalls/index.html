Why do so many Chevrolet models have gasoline fuel system recalls?

13 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-05

Chevrolet's gasoline-engine recalls fall into two recurring patterns. Several involve fuel leaks that raise a fire risk: a 2016-2018 Cruze fuel tank vent sensor, a 2016-2018 Silverado/Sierra 3500 dual tank that can overfill, a 2019-2020 Equinox tank seam, a 2024 Colorado/Canyon fuel pump lock ring, a 2023-2024 Corvette fuel-filler spillage issue, a 2018 Colorado/Equinox/Malibu lineup with a detaching high-pressure fuel pump, a 2020 full-size truck and SUV over-pressurized fuel pump, and a 2021 Tahoe/Yukon tank missing adhesive. Others involve fuel pumps that can cause the engine to stall: a 2020 Enclave/Acadia/XT5/XT6/Traverse fuel pump nozzle blockage, a 2021 Escalade/Suburban/Tahoe/Yukon pump control module, a 2021-2022 Equinox fuel pump, and a 2018 City Express and 2018-2019 Nissan NV200 wiring short.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine stalling or losing power unexpectedly while driving, described in several fuel-pump recalls
  • Fuel visibly leaking near the tank, fuel lines, or fuel pump
  • Fuel spilling into the fuel-filler pocket area during refueling, described in the Corvette recall
  • The notices describe no driver-noticeable warning for most of the fuel-tank leak recalls before a leak is found

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe fuel leaks that increase fire risk in the presence of an ignition source across the Cruze, Silverado/Sierra 3500, Equinox, Colorado/Canyon, Corvette, and several SUV and truck models, and separately describe fuel pump problems that can cause an unexpected engine stall in the Enclave, Acadia, XT5, XT6, Traverse, Escalade, Suburban, Tahoe, Yukon, Equinox, and City Express/NV200. In every case, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace or reseal fuel tanks, install lock rings or shields, and replace fuel pump modules, pumps, or pipes depending on the recall. For the City Express and NV200, dealers inspect and reroute a sensor harness or replace the fuel pump assembly. None of these fixes are delivered over-the-air; all repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why does Chevrolet have both fuel leak and fuel pump stall recalls?

These are separate defects with separate causes: leak recalls involve tank seams, lock rings, or pump mounting, while stall recalls involve a fuel pump module, nozzle, or wiring that interrupts fuel flow to the engine.

Is the Equinox fuel recall about a leak or a stall?

It depends on the model year: the 2019-2020 Equinox recall covers a leaking fuel tank seam, while the 2021-2022 Equinox recall covers a separate fuel pump issue that can cause the engine to stall.

Does the Chevrolet City Express recall also affect the Nissan NV200?

Yes. Nissan issued this recall jointly because the City Express is a rebadged NV200, and both share the same fuel tank temperature sensor harness that can short-circuit the fuel pump fuse.
