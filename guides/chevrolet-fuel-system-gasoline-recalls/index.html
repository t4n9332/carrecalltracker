Chevrolet fuel system recalls: leaks, failing fuel pumps and stalling

13 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-05

Two problems repeat. The first is fuel leaking from the tank or its fittings: a 2016-2018 Cruze LS that can leak after a rear impact and rollover, an improperly sealed tank seam on 2019-2020 Equinox all-wheel drive, missing adhesive between tank layers on 2021 Tahoe, a fuel pump lock ring not fully locked on 2024 Colorado, dual tanks that can overfill on 2016-2018 Silverado 3500, and refueling spill into the filler pocket on 2023-2026 Corvette. The second is fuel pumps. Pump defects cover 2016-2018 Colorado and Malibu, 2020 Silverado 1500, Tahoe and Camaro, 2020 Traverse, 2021 Suburban and Tahoe, and 2021-2022 Equinox, described as a detached pump, a missing pressure regulator, a blocked nozzle or a failing control module.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An engine stall without warning — 2020 Chevrolet Traverse, Buick Enclave, Cadillac XT5 and XT6 and GMC Acadia fuel pump nozzle recall.
  • An unexpected engine stall — 2021 Chevrolet Suburban and Tahoe, Cadillac Escalade and GMC Yukon fuel pump power control module recall.
  • An engine stall — 2021-2022 Chevrolet Equinox and 2022 GMC Terrain fuel pump module recall.
  • An engine stall from a blown fuel pump fuse — 2015-2018 Chevrolet City Express and Nissan NV200 recall.
  • Excess fuel spilling into the fuel-filler pocket during refueling — 2023-2026 Chevrolet Corvette recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

No notice in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park outside. Several describe a fuel leak that, in the presence of an ignition source, increases the risk of a fire: the Cruze, Equinox, Tahoe, Colorado, Silverado 3500 and Corvette notices. Others describe an engine stall that increases the risk of a crash: the 2020 Traverse, 2021 Suburban and Tahoe, 2021-2022 Equinox and City Express notices. None of these notices gives owners an interim instruction to follow while waiting for the repair.

What the free repair involves

Every one of these repairs is free. The tank-related recalls are hardware work: dealers replace the fuel tank on Equinox and on Tahoe, install a lock-ring on the Cruze, fully lock the ring on Colorado, install a shield on the Corvette, and replace the rear-tank fuel-pump module on Silverado 3500. The pump recalls mean replacing the pump, the pump module or its control module.

Common questions

My Chevrolet stalls — could it be one of these fuel pump recalls?

Four notices here describe engine stalls: the 2020 Traverse blocked pump nozzle, the 2021 Suburban and Tahoe pump control module, the 2021-2022 Equinox pump module, and the City Express fuel pump fuse. Check your VIN with NHTSA to see which apply.

Would I smell fuel if my vehicle had one of the tank leak recalls?

The notices do not describe a smell or any other warning sign. The Cruze and Colorado leaks are described as occurring in a crash, while the Equinox, Tahoe and Silverado 3500 notices describe leaks from the tank itself.

What does the Corvette fuel recall actually change on the car?

The notice says excess fuel may spill into the fuel-filler pocket during refueling on 2023-2026 Corvettes equipped with a left-side radiator and fan combination. Dealers install a shield to divert spilled fuel, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
