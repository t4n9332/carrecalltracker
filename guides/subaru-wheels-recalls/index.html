Why Subaru wheels and hub bolts have been recalled repeatedly

3 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Wheels · model years 2023–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 23, 2025

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

Every recall in this group comes back to how a Subaru wheel is attached. Two cover the 2023 Solterra: hub bolts that may loosen and let a wheel detach, one filed by Toyota alongside the bZ4X and one filed by Subaru for improperly tightened hub bolts. Both tell owners not to drive until the repair is done. The third is different in kind — certain 2025 Foresters with 18-inch aluminum alloy wheels may have wheels made with an incorrectly specified lug seat surface, which can leave the wheel improperly attached and cause a loss of vehicle stability. The Solterra is by far the most affected model here.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two of the three notices carry a stop-driving instruction, and both cover the 2023 Solterra. The Toyota-filed recall for the 2023 Solterra and bZ4X advises owners not to drive their vehicles until they are repaired; that notice also says letters about the safety risk were mailed June 27, 2022, with a second letter to follow once the remedy is available. The Subaru-filed 2023 Solterra hub bolt recall likewise advises owners not to drive until the repair is complete. The 2025 Forester notice does not tell owners to stop driving. None carries a park-outside instruction.

What the free repair involves

Remedies differ. Under the Toyota-filed recall, dealers replace the hub bolts and wheels on the 2023 Solterra and bZ4X. Under the Subaru-filed 2023 Solterra recall, dealers inspect the hub bolts and tighten them if necessary. For the 2025 Forester, dealers inspect the 18-inch aluminum alloy wheels and replace them as necessary. All three notices say the work is free of charge.

Common questions

Can I drive my 2023 Solterra while waiting for the hub bolt repair?

Both Solterra notices advise owners not to drive the vehicle until the repair is completed. One has dealers replace the hub bolts and wheels; the other has dealers inspect the hub bolts and tighten them if necessary, free of charge.

Why are there two separate Solterra wheel recalls?

One was filed by Toyota covering the 2023 Solterra and bZ4X, for hub bolts that may loosen and cause a wheel to detach after low-mileage use. The other was filed by Subaru for 2023 Solterras with improperly tightened hub bolts. The notices do not explain how they relate.

Which 2025 Foresters are affected by the wheel recall?

The notice covers certain 2025 Forester vehicles equipped with 18-inch aluminum alloy wheels that may have an incorrectly specified lug seat surface. Dealers inspect and replace the wheels as necessary, free of charge; a VIN lookup confirms whether a vehicle is included.
