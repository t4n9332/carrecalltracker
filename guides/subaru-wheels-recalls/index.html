Why are Subaru Solterra and Forester wheels being recalled?

3 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Wheels · model years 2023–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-01-23

⚠ 2 of these recalls carry a do-not-drive advisory.

These three wheel recalls affect two different Subaru models for two different reasons. The 2023 Solterra was recalled twice for hub bolts that can loosen: once together with the related Toyota bZ4X, for hub bolts that loosen after low-mileage use, and again in a separate recall for hub bolts that were improperly tightened at the factory. Both describe a wheel detaching from the vehicle. Separately, 2025 Forester vehicles built with 18-inch aluminum alloy wheels were recalled because the wheels may have an incorrectly specified lug seat surface, which can leave a wheel improperly attached and reduce vehicle stability.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2023 Solterra (and Toyota bZ4X): the notice describes no warning sign before a wheel detaches after low-mileage use
  • 2023 Solterra (second recall): the notice describes no warning sign before hub bolts loosen and a wheel detaches
  • 2025 Forester: the notice describes no warning sign before a loss of vehicle stability

Is it safe to keep driving?

Two of these three wheel recalls tell owners not to drive their vehicle until it is repaired: the recall covering 2023 Solterra and Toyota bZ4X vehicles with loosening hub bolts, and the separate recall covering 2023 Solterra vehicles with improperly tightened hub bolts, both citing the risk of a wheel detaching and a loss of vehicle control. The 2025 Forester alloy wheel recall does not tell owners to stop driving; its notice does not include a park-outside instruction either.

What the free repair involves

For the two Solterra/bZ4X hub bolt recalls, dealers replace the hub bolts and wheels, or inspect and tighten the hub bolts as needed, free of charge. For the 2025 Forester recall, dealers inspect the 18-inch alloy wheels and replace them if necessary, also free of charge.

Common questions

Why was the Solterra recalled for wheels twice?

The two recalls address different problems: one, shared with the Toyota bZ4X, covers hub bolts that can loosen after low-mileage use; the other, specific to the Solterra, covers hub bolts that were improperly tightened during assembly.

Can I drive my Solterra while waiting for the wheel recall repair?

No. Both Solterra hub bolt recall notices tell owners not to drive the vehicle until it has been repaired, because a wheel could detach and cause a loss of control.

Is the 2025 Forester wheel recall as serious as the Solterra ones?

The Forester notice does not tell owners to stop driving, unlike the two Solterra hub bolt recalls. It describes a risk of improperly attached wheels reducing vehicle stability, addressed by dealer inspection and replacement of the wheels.
