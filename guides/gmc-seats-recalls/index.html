What's wrong with GMC seat frames and mounting bolts?

4 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Seats · model years 2019–2022 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 29, 2022

Four recalls, each about a seat that may not hold as intended in a crash. Two involve fasteners: 2021 Yukon and Yukon XL front seats whose attachment bolts may not have been properly reinstalled after a repair that involved removing one or both front seats, and 2022 Terrain driver's seats with a stripped or loose bolt at the seat frame height-adjust pivot. Two involve welds: 2022 Canyon vehicles with a manual passenger seat, where a hook attachment may not have been properly welded and could separate from the inboard track of the seat frame, and 2019 Terrain vehicles whose rear-right seat may have insufficient welds on the head-restraint bracket.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving, and none asks owners to park outside. Each frames the risk around a crash: a front seat with loose or missing attachment bolts that may move, a passenger seat frame that may not properly restrain the seat occupant, a driver's seat with a missing or loose bolt that may not properly restrain the occupant, and rear-right welds that may increase the risk of injury for someone seated there. The notices give owners no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the seat attachments for both front seats and install attachment bolts as needed on the 2021 Yukon and Yukon XL, inspect the front passenger seat and replace the cushion frame if necessary on the 2022 Canyon, and inspect the driver's seat and replace the seat cushion frame if necessary on the 2022 Terrain. On the 2019 Terrain, dealers replace the rear-right seatback frame. All free of charge.

Common questions

Will the dealer replace my seat or just tighten it?

It depends on the recall. On the 2021 Yukon and Yukon XL, dealers inspect the attachments for both front seats and install attachment bolts as needed. On the 2019 Terrain, dealers replace the rear-right seatback frame. Both are free of charge.

Which seat is affected in each Terrain recall?

Two different positions. The 2019 Terrain recall covers the rear-right seat, where the head-restraint bracket may have insufficient welds. The 2022 Terrain recall covers the driver's seat, which may have a stripped or loose bolt at the seat frame height-adjust pivot.

Does the Canyon recall cover both front seats?

The notice covers 2022 Canyon vehicles equipped with a manual passenger seat, and names the front passenger seat's hook attachment, which may not have been properly welded. Dealers inspect the front passenger seat and replace the cushion frame, if necessary, free of charge.
