GMC Seat Recalls Cover Loose Bolts and Insufficient Welds

5 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Seats · model years 2019–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 21, 2026

These five GMC seat recalls center on attachment bolts and welds that can compromise occupant restraint in a crash. Terrain appears twice: the 2019 recall concerns insufficient welds on the rear-right seat’s head-restraint bracket, while the 2022 recall concerns a stripped or loose driver’s seat height-adjust pivot bolt. The 2021 Yukon and Yukon XL recall involves front-seat attachment bolts that may not have been properly reinstalled. The 2022 Canyon recall covers an improperly welded hook attachment on a manual front passenger seat. The 2026 Acadia recall concerns insufficient welds where a front-seat recliner pivot mechanism attaches to a seat-frame bracket.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the 2021 Yukon and Yukon XL front-seat bolt recall, 2022 Canyon passenger-seat weld recall, 2022 Terrain driver-seat bolt recall, 2026 Acadia front-seat weld recall, and 2019 Terrain rear-right seat weld recall do not tell owners to stop driving. Each describes an increased injury risk in a crash involving the affected seat or seating position. The notices provide no additional driving instructions for these seat defects.

What the free repair involves

Repairs are free of charge. For 2021 Yukon and Yukon XL, dealers inspect both front-seat attachments and install bolts as needed. For 2022 Canyon and 2022 Terrain, dealers inspect and replace the affected cushion frame if necessary. Dealers replace the affected front seat back frames for 2026 Acadia and the rear-right seatback frame for 2019 Terrain.

Common questions

Which GMC Terrain seats are covered by these recalls?

The 2019 Terrain recall concerns insufficient welds on the rear-right seat’s head-restraint bracket. The 2022 Terrain recall concerns a stripped or loose bolt at the driver’s seat frame height-adjust pivot. The notices identify different seating positions and repairs.

Does the GMC Canyon recall cover power passenger seats?

The May 26, 2022 notice identifies certain 2022 Canyon vehicles equipped with a manual passenger seat. It describes an improperly welded hook attachment. Dealers inspect the front passenger seat and replace the cushion frame if necessary, free of charge.

What repair does the 2026 GMC Acadia seat recall require?

The October 21, 2026 notice says dealers will replace the driver or front passenger seat back frames, free of charge. The defect involves insufficient welds at the recliner pivot mechanism’s attachment to a seat-frame bracket. Owner letters are expected December 7, 2026.
