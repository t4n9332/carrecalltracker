Why do Toyota Tundra and RAV4 engines lose power or stall?

6 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-20

This group is dominated by engine stalling risks. Three separate Tundra recalls (2024, 2025 and 2026) describe the same V35A engine problem: manufacturing debris contaminating the main bearings, which can fail and cause a stall. A 2019-2020 RAV4 and 2020 Camry recall covers porous engine castings that can crack and leak coolant, risking overheating, stalling and engine damage. Separately, oversized pistons caused stalling in the 2018 Camry, and a 2022 Prius and Prius Prime software error can shut down the hybrid system and cause a loss of drive power.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine stalling while driving
  • Loss of drive power without further detail from the notices
  • Engine overheating or coolant loss (2019-2020 RAV4, 2020 Camry recall)
  • The notices describe no driver-noticeable warning sign before the Tundra main bearing failures

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the six recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This includes the three separate Tundra main bearing recalls dated 2024, 2025 and 2026, the 2019-2020 RAV4 and 2020 Camry coolant leak recall, the 2018 Camry piston recall, and the 2022 Prius and Prius Prime software recall. Each notice describes a risk of stalling or losing drive power, but none instruct owners to stop driving or park outside.

What the free repair involves

Depending on the recall, Toyota dealers replace the engine or engine block, inspect and replace affected pistons, update the Engine Control Unit software, or inspect the number-one main bearing using diagnostic software and drive data before replacing the engine if needed. All of these repairs are performed free of charge to the owner.

Common questions

Why have there been three separate Tundra engine recalls?

Toyota issued three recalls (2024, 2025 and 2026) covering different model-year ranges of Tundra trucks with the V35A engine, each describing the same cause: manufacturing debris contaminating the main bearings, which can fail and stall the engine.

Does the RAV4/Camry coolant leak recall affect the same engines as the Tundra recalls?

No. The 2019-2020 RAV4 and 2020 Camry coolant recall involves porosity in 2.5L four-cylinder engine castings, a different defect from the V35A main bearing issue described in the Tundra recalls.

Is the repair free for these engine recalls?

Yes. Every recall in this group states that Toyota dealers will inspect, repair, or replace the engine free of charge, with owners notified by mail when the remedy is ready.
