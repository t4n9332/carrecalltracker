Toyota Engine Recalls: Stalling, Coolant Leaks, and Drive Power Loss

7 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 7, 2026

Tundra bearing failures are the most repeated engine issue in these notices. The May 2024 recall covers certain 2022-2023 Tundra vehicles, the November 2025 recall covers certain 2022-2024 Tundra vehicles, and the May 2026 recall covers certain 2024 Tundra vehicles. Each identifies debris that may cause V35A engine main-bearing failure, stalling, and loss of drive power. Other failures have distinct causes: oversized pistons in certain 2018 Camry engines, casting porosity and coolant leaks in the February 2020 notice, ECU software shutdown in certain 2022 Prius and Prius Prime vehicles, and motor generator ECU errors in certain 2026 RAV4 Plug-In Hybrid vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • February 2020 engine-casting recall 20TA04: coolant may leak internally or externally, and the engine may overheat.
  • March 2018 Camry piston recall: the engine may stall.
  • September 2021 Prius recall 21TB07/21TA07: hybrid-system shutdown may cause loss of drive power.
  • May 2024, November 2025, and May 2026 Tundra bearing recalls: the engine may stall and lose drive power.
  • October 2026 RAV4 Plug-In Hybrid recall 26TA22: the hybrid system may reduce power or shut down at higher speeds.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The supplied engine and engine-cooling notices contain zero stop-driving instructions and zero park-outside instructions. The February 2020 coolant-leak, March 2018 Camry piston, and September 2021 Prius software notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Neither do the May 2024 Tundra engine-debris notice, November 2025 Tundra bearing notice, or May 2026 Tundra bearing notice. The October 2026 notice for the RAV4 Plug-In Hybrid motor generator ECU also does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies vary: Camry's March 2018 notice specifies piston date-code inspection and qualifying engine-assembly replacement; February 2020 specifies inspection and engine replacement as necessary. May 2024 Tundra receives an engine assembly. November 2025 and May 2026 Tundra notices specify #1 main-bearing inspection using software and drive data, with engine replacement if necessary. September 2021 Prius and October 2026 RAV4 Plug-In Hybrid receive ECU updates.

Common questions

Do the Tundra bearing recalls specify the same repair?

The May 2024 notice specifies engine-assembly replacement. The November 2025 and May 2026 notices specify inspection of the #1 main bearing using vehicle inspection software and drive data, followed by engine replacement if necessary. These remedies are free of charge.

Which Toyota vehicles are covered by the coolant-leak engine notice?

The February 2020 notice covers certain 2020 Avalon Hybrid, Camry, and Camry Hybrid vehicles and 2019-2020 RAV4 and RAV4 Hybrid vehicles with 2.5-liter four-cylinder engines. Casting porosity may cause cracks and coolant leaks. Dealers inspect and replace the engine as necessary.

What does the 2026 RAV4 Plug-In Hybrid power-loss recall address?

The October 2026 notice describes a motor generator ECU error that may reduce power or shut down the plug-in-hybrid system at higher speeds. It covers certain 2026 RAV4 Plug-In Hybrid vehicles. Dealers update the ECU software free of charge.
