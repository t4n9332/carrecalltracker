Toyota engine recalls: Tundra bearings, Camry pistons, RAV4 coolant leaks

6 NHTSA recalls · Toyota · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 20, 2026

Tundra with the V35A engine accounts for half of this group. Three recalls — covering 2022-2023 Tundra, 2022-2024 Tundra and 2024 Tundra — each describe debris from the manufacturing process contaminating the engine and causing main bearings to fail, which can result in an engine stall and loss of drive power. The remaining three affect different models. Porosity in the castings of 2.5L four-cylinder engines may cause cracks that leak coolant on 2019-2020 RAV4 and RAV4 Hybrid and 2020 Camry and Camry Hybrid. The 2018 Camry with the 2.5L engine may have pistons larger than specified. And on 2022 Prius and Prius Prime, Engine Control Unit software may falsely detect an error and shut down the hybrid system.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An engine stall and loss of drive power, in the three Tundra V35A main bearing recalls covering 2022-2023, 2022-2024 and 2024 Tundra.
  • An engine that overheats and stalls, in the 2.5L coolant leak recall covering 2019-2020 RAV4 and RAV4 Hybrid and 2020 Camry and Camry Hybrid.
  • An engine stall, in the 2018 Camry piston recall.
  • A loss of drive power, in the 2022 Prius and Prius Prime ECU software recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The three Tundra V35A main bearing notices, the 2018 Camry piston notice, the 2022 Prius software notice and the 2.5L coolant leak notice describe the risk in similar terms — an engine stall, overheating or a loss of drive power increasing the risk of a crash — without any instruction about continuing to drive. The 2024 Tundra notice says interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk were mailed, with additional letters to follow.

What the free repair involves

Remedies vary. For 2022-2023 Tundra, dealers replace the engine assembly; for 2022-2024 and 2024 Tundra, they inspect the #1 main bearing using vehicle inspection software and drive data and replace the engine if necessary. The 2.5L coolant recall inspects and replaces the engine as necessary, the 2018 Camry replaces the engine if affected pistons are found, and 2022 Prius gets an ECU software update. All free of charge.

Common questions

I own a Tundra — which of the bearing recalls applies to mine?

The notices cover 2022-2023 Tundra, 2022-2024 Tundra and 2024 Tundra built with the V35A engine, and the model years overlap. Check your VIN through NHTSA's recall lookup or with Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 to see which applies.

Does the bearing recall mean my engine gets replaced?

It depends on the recall. The 2022-2023 Tundra notice says dealers replace the engine assembly. The 2022-2024 and 2024 Tundra notices say dealers inspect the #1 main bearing using inspection software and drive data, then replace the engine if necessary. All of this work is free of charge.

What does the 2.5L coolant leak recall actually involve?

The notice says porosity in the engine castings may result in cracks that allow coolant to leak internally and/or externally on 2019-2020 RAV4 and RAV4 Hybrid and 2020 Camry and Camry Hybrid. Dealers inspect and replace the engine, including the engine block, as necessary, free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
