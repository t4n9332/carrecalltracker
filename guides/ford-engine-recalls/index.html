Ford Engine Recalls Cover Fire Risks and Loss of Power

13 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Engine · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 20, 2026

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Fire risk repeats across Ford's engine notices, but the defects differ. The December 2018 and April 2019 truck recalls concern block heater cables on certain 2015-2019 F-150 and 2017-2019 Super Duty trucks. Engine failure that can release oil and fuel vapor appears repeatedly for 2020-2023 Escape and 2022-2023 Maverick hybrids, alongside Lincoln Corsair vehicles. Separate notices address engine compartment fires in 2021 Expedition and Navigator vehicles and battery junction box shorts in 2020 models. Other problems include damaged wiring on 2021-2026 Bronco and Bronco Raptor vehicles, cylinder head ball plug failures on 2023-2025 Escape and 2025 Bronco Sport vehicles, and loose motor mount fasteners on 2020-2021 Explorer and Aviator vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • May 2023 Escape/Maverick/Corsair recall: unexpected engine noises, reduced vehicle power, or smoke.
  • August 2024 Explorer Police Interceptor recall: unexpected engine noises, unexpected power reduction, or engine compartment smoke.
  • May 2025 Escape/Maverick/Corsair recall: unexpected engine noises, reduced vehicle power, or smoke.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two notices require parking outside, away from structures and other vehicles until repaired: the May 2022 Expedition/Navigator engine fire recall and September 2025 Expedition/Navigator battery fire recall. None of the supplied notices carries a blanket stop-driving instruction. The May 2023 and May 2025 Escape/Maverick/Corsair notices instruct owners to park and shut off quickly for unexpected engine noises, reduced power, or smoke. The August 2024 Explorer Police Interceptor notice gives that instruction for unexpected engine noises, unexpected power reduction, or engine compartment smoke. The remaining notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies explicitly include disabling and later replacing block heater cables under the December 2018 notice, replacing motor mount fasteners under the December 2020 Explorer/Aviator notice, and updating powertrain software under the May 2025 hybrid notice. The July 2026 Bronco notice specifies free wiring sheathing installation; the June 2025 Escape notice specifies cylinder head replacement as necessary, free of charge.

Common questions

Why do Escape and Maverick hybrids appear in multiple engine fire notices?

The May 2023 and May 2025 notices describe engine failure releasing oil and fuel vapor near ignition sources. The May 2025 notice specifically covers vehicles repaired incorrectly under recall 23V380 and specifies a free powertrain control module software update.

Which Expedition engine recalls tell owners to park outside?

The May 2022 notice for certain 2021 Expedition and Navigator vehicles and the September 2025 notice for certain 2020 vehicles give this instruction. Both tell owners to park outside, away from structures and other vehicles, until the remedy is performed.

Does the Bronco wiring recall require an engine replacement?

The July 2026 notice describes engine compartment wiring harness damage that may cause a short circuit. Its stated remedy is installing sheathing over the wiring, free of charge. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.
