Ford engine recalls: fire risk from oil, wiring and block heaters

13 NHTSA recalls · Ford · Engine · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-20

⚠ 2 carry a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Fire runs through most of Ford's engine recalls. The largest cluster describes an engine failure releasing engine oil and fuel vapor near hot engine or exhaust components: 2020-2023 Escape, 2021-2023 Lincoln Corsair and 2022-2023 Maverick with 2.5L HEV or PHEV engines, and 2020-2022 Explorer with the Police Interceptor Utility Package. Two notices name the battery junction box on 2021 and on 2020 Expedition and Lincoln Navigator, and two name the engine block heater cable on 2015-2019 F-150 and 2017-2019 Super Duty trucks. Damaged engine compartment wiring covers 2021-2026 Bronco. The rest stand alone: 2018 F-150 cylinder heads machined without lubrication supply holes, loose motor mount fasteners on 2020-2021 Explorer and Aviator, failing cylinder head ball plugs on 2023-2025 Escape, and a hot engine cover on 2021 E-350 and E-450.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Unexpected engine noises, a reduction in vehicle power, or smoke — the 2020-2023 Escape, 2021-2023 Corsair and 2022-2023 Maverick engine fire recall (23V380) and its 2025 counterpart.
  • The same three signs in the 2020-2022 Explorer Police Interceptor Utility engine fire recall.
  • An engine cover in the passenger compartment that becomes excessively hot — the 2021 E-350 and E-450 recall.
  • An engine stall — the 2018 F-150 3.5L cylinder head recall.
  • A loss of power — the 2020-2021 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator motor mount fastener recall (20S72).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

Two notices carry a park-outside instruction: the 2021 Expedition and Lincoln Navigator recall and the 2020 Expedition and Lincoln Navigator recall each advise owners to park outside and away from structures and other vehicles until the remedy is performed. Three notices — the 2020-2023 Escape, Corsair and Maverick fire recall, its 2025 counterpart for vehicles repaired incorrectly under 23V380, and the Explorer Police Interceptor recall — advise owners to park and shut off the engine if they hear unexpected engine noises, notice a reduction in power, or see smoke. No notice here tells owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

All of these remedies are free. Dealers replace the battery junction box as necessary and remove a ground wire on the two Expedition and Navigator recalls; update powertrain control module software, or replace the engine long block on 2023 Escape and Corsair, for the hybrid fire recalls; disable and later replace the block heater cable on the truck recalls; sheathe Bronco wiring; and replace cylinder heads or motor mount fasteners.

Common questions

My Escape or Maverick was already repaired for the engine fire recall. Why another letter?

The 2025 notice says it covers 2020-2023 Escape, 2021-2023 Corsair and 2022-2023 Maverick vehicles with 2.5L HEV or PHEV engines that were repaired incorrectly under recall 23V380. Its remedy is a free powertrain control module software update at a dealer.

Why does Ford tell Expedition and Navigator owners to park outside?

Both notices describe an engine compartment fire that can occur while driving or parked, even with the ignition in the Off position. They advise parking outside and away from structures and other vehicles until the remedy — battery junction box inspection and replacement as necessary — is performed.

Can I use the engine block heater on my recalled F-150 or Super Duty?

The notices say dealers will disable the block heater from being used, and that once replacement cables are available a second notice will be mailed and dealers will replace the engine block heater cord free of charge. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving.
