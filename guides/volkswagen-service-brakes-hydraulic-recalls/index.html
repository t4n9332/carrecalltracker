Volkswagen Hydraulic Brake Recalls Involving Leaks and Reduced Braking

7 NHTSA recalls · Volkswagen · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 27, 2024

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Volkswagen’s hydraulic brake notices repeatedly address caliper hardware and brake fluid problems. Atlas appears most often: certain 2018 vehicles have caliper coating or bracket bolt concerns, 2023 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles have leaking brake line connections, and 2024 versions have a master cylinder retainer that can block a fluid port. Other notices cover loose front caliper bolts on one 2020 Jetta and a loose brake pipe nut on certain 2022 Tiguan vehicles. The September 2020 notice concerns replacement Beck/Arnley master cylinders sold for 2004-2018 Jetta and 2012-2019 Beetle vehicles. Across these notices, the stated consequences include reduced braking, longer stopping distances and loss of brake function.

Warning signs to watch for

  • June 2018 brake caliper coating recall: reduced braking performance.
  • November 2021 Tiguan brake pipe nut recall (19Q4): an extended stopping distance associated with a brake fluid leak.
  • September 2022 Atlas brake line recall (47T2): an increased stopping distance associated with a brake fluid leak.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One listed notice carries a stop-driving instruction: the June 2018 brake caliper coating recall advises owners not to drive until the brake system has been checked. The August 2018 caliper bracket bolt, June 2020 Jetta caliper bolt, September 2020 Beck/Arnley master cylinder, November 2021 Tiguan brake pipe nut, September 2022 Atlas brake line and September 2024 Atlas master cylinder notices do not tell owners to stop driving. None of the listed notices gives a park-outside instruction. The June 2018 restriction specifically requires a brake system check.

What the free repair involves

The June 2018 notice specifies bleeding Volkswagen’s front and rear calipers but does not state a charge. Other notices specify free remedies: bracket bolt inspection and correction in August 2018; Jetta caliper bolt replacement in June 2020; Beck/Arnley cylinder exchange in September 2020; Tiguan nut inspection and tightening in November 2021; Atlas line replacement in September 2022; and Atlas cylinder replacement in September 2024.

Common questions

Which Volkswagen brake recall tells owners not to drive?

The June 2018 brake caliper coating notice advises owners not to drive until the brake system has been checked. It covers certain 2018 Atlas, Passat, Beetle, Beetle Convertible, Golf, Golf SportWagen and GTI vehicles, alongside the Audi vehicles named in that notice.

Does the Beck/Arnley recall cover the original master cylinder in my Jetta?

The September 2020 notice identifies Beck/Arnley master cylinders, part number 072-9831, sold as aftermarket or replacement parts for 2004-2018 Jetta and 2012-2019 Beetle vehicles. It specifies exchanging affected cylinders for part number 072-9990, free of charge.

Are the 2023 and 2024 Atlas brake problems the same?

The September 2022 notice concerns leaking threaded brake line connections in certain 2023 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles. The September 2024 notice concerns an incorrect master cylinder secondary piston spring retainer in certain 2024 versions. Their remedies replace different components.
