Cadillac Electrical Recalls Cover Battery Defects and Missing Display Information

7 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Electrical System · model years 2023–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 30, 2026

Lyriq appears most often in this group, with affected model years spanning 2023 through 2025 across separate notices. Battery connections in certain 2023 Lyriq vehicles can cause loss of drive power; insufficiently insulated rear-drive-unit wires can cause power loss in certain 2024 Lyriq vehicles. Missing or loose battery mounting bolts affect certain 2025 Lyriq and 2026 Vistiq vehicles. A separate internal battery component defect affects certain 2026 Escalade IQ and IQL vehicles. Other recalls concern a blank instrument panel or missing pedestrian sounds on certain 2023 Lyriq vehicles, and missing dashboard light reminders on certain 2023–2025 XT5 and 2024–2025 XT6 vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • May 2023 Lyriq battery-pack recall and May 2024 rear-drive-unit recall: loss of drive power.
  • September 2022 Lyriq instrument-panel recall: the instrument panel may go blank.
  • October 2023 Lyriq pedestrian-warning recall: external pedestrian warning sounds may be absent.
  • June 2024 XT5/XT6 reminder-light recall: the instrument panel may not illuminate when hazards, high beams, or turn signals are activated.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the seven electrical-system notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The battery-bolt, internal battery component, battery-pack connection, rear-drive-unit, blank instrument-panel, pedestrian-warning-sound, and dashboard-reminder notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The notices describe fire risk for the battery mounting and internal component defects, crash risk from loss of drive power, and crash risks associated with missing displays or warnings. They do not provide a separate continuing-driving instruction for those conditions.

What the free repair involves

Remedies are free of charge: July 2025 battery bolts are inspected and replaced or re-tightened as necessary; July 2026 batteries are replaced; May 2023 battery packs are inspected and replaced if necessary; May 2024 rear drive units are replaced. September 2022 display software is updated, October 2023 BCMs are reprogrammed, and June 2024 instrument-panel software is updated.

Common questions

Which Lyriq recalls involve losing drive power?

The May 2023 notice covers certain 2023 Lyriq vehicles with battery-pack connections that may be out of position or incorrectly welded. The May 2024 notice covers certain 2024 Lyriq vehicles with insufficiently insulated wires in rear drive unit motors.

Do the battery-bolt and internal-component recalls use the same repair?

The July 2025 notice calls for inspecting battery mounting bolts and replacing or re-tightening them as necessary. The July 2026 Escalade IQ and IQL notice calls for replacing the high-voltage battery because an internal module component may be improperly secured.

Which Cadillac electrical recalls involve missing dashboard information?

The September 2022 notice concerns a potentially blank instrument panel in certain 2023 Lyriq vehicles. The June 2024 notice concerns missing instrument-panel illumination for activated hazards, high beams, or turn signals in certain 2023–2025 XT5 and 2024–2025 XT6 vehicles.
