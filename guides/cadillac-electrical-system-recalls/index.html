What electrical problems keep showing up in Cadillac recalls?

7 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Electrical System · model years 2023–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-30

Cadillac's electrical system recalls in this group split between the Lyriq's high-voltage battery and software, and unrelated electrical faults on gas-powered SUVs. Two 2025 and 2026 recalls cover loose or improperly secured high-voltage battery components on the Lyriq and Escalade IQ/IQL that raise fire risk in a crash. Two more, from 2023 and 2024, cover battery pack or rear-drive-unit wiring problems on the Lyriq and other GM EVs that can cause a sudden loss of drive power. Three additional Lyriq-specific software recalls cover a blank instrument panel, missing pedestrian warning sounds, and on the XT5 and XT6, dashboard lights that fail to show when hazards, high beams, or turn signals are on.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Sudden loss of drive power while driving (2023 Lyriq battery pack and 2024 multi-model EV rear-drive-unit recalls)
  • Instrument panel screen going blank, losing speed and warning-light information (2022 Lyriq recall)
  • Pedestrian warning sounds not playing outside the vehicle (2023 Lyriq recall)
  • Dashboard indicator lights failing to illuminate for hazards, high beams, or turn signals (2024 XT5/XT6 recall)
  • The two battery-bolt/fire-risk recalls (2025 Lyriq, 2026 Escalade IQ/IQL and Hummer EV) describe no warning sign a driver would notice before a crash

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven Cadillac electrical system recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This includes the 2025 and 2026 high-voltage battery recalls that raise fire risk in a crash, the 2023 and 2024 recalls describing a loss of drive power, and the three software recalls covering a blank instrument panel, missing pedestrian warning sounds, and dashboard indicator lights that fail to illuminate. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and tighten or replace battery bolts, replace the high-voltage battery pack or rear drive unit, and update software for the instrument panel, pedestrian warning sounds, and dashboard indicator lights — the last two by dealer software update. All repairs across these seven recalls are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Cadillac electrical recalls involve a fire risk?

Two recalls: the 2025 Lyriq recall for loose or missing high-voltage battery bolts, and the 2026 recall for an improperly secured internal battery component on the Escalade IQ and IQL and GMC Hummer EV. Both say a damaged battery in a crash increases fire risk.

Why would a Cadillac Lyriq suddenly lose drive power?

Two separate recalls describe this: a 2023 recall citing high-voltage battery pack connections that are out of position or incorrectly welded, and a 2024 recall citing insufficiently insulated wires in the rear drive unit that can contact each other.

Are the XT5 and XT6 dashboard light problems dangerous?

GM says the instrument panel may not illuminate when hazard lights, high beams, or turn signals are on, so a driver could leave them on unintentionally, which can confuse other drivers and raise crash risk. Dealers update the software for free.
