Cadillac electrical recalls: battery, drive power and display problems

7 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Electrical System · model years 2023–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 30, 2026

Seven recalls sit under Cadillac's electrical system, and they split into high-voltage hardware and software. Four involve the battery or drive power: bolts attaching the high voltage battery to the floor may be missing or loose on the 2025 Lyriq; an improperly secured internal module component in the high voltage battery on the 2026 Escalade IQ and IQL; battery pack connections out of position or incorrectly welded on the 2023 Lyriq; and insufficiently insulated rear drive unit wires on the 2024 Lyriq. The Lyriq appears in five of the seven. The rest are software: a blank instrument panel on the 2023 Lyriq, missing pedestrian warning sounds on the 2023 Lyriq, and instrument panel illumination on the 2023-2025 XT5 and 2024-2025 XT6.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The instrument panel may go blank and not show critical information such as vehicle speed and warning lights — 2023 Cadillac Lyriq video display control module recall.
  • The vehicle may fail to make its pedestrian warning sounds — 2023 Cadillac Lyriq body control module recall.
  • The instrument panel may not illuminate when the hazard warning lights, high beams or turn signals are activated — 2023-2025 Cadillac XT5 and 2024-2025 XT6 recall.
  • A loss of drive power — 2023 Cadillac Lyriq high-voltage battery pack recall and the 2024 Cadillac Lyriq rear drive unit recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside — including the two fire-risk recalls, the 2025 Lyriq battery bolts and the 2026 Escalade IQ and IQL battery module component. The notices state their risks in their own terms: a battery that may be damaged and increase fire risk, a loss of drive power, a blank instrument panel, pedestrian warning sounds that are not made, or lights left activated unintentionally. Each directs owners to the free remedy and says nothing further about driving.

What the free repair involves

All of this work is free of charge. Dealers inspect the high-voltage battery bolts and re-tighten or replace them as necessary on the 2025 Lyriq, replace the high voltage battery on the 2026 Escalade IQ and IQL, inspect and if necessary replace the battery pack on the 2023 Lyriq, and replace the rear drive unit on the 2024 Lyriq. The remaining three are software updates or module reprogramming.

Common questions

Which Cadillac models are affected by these electrical recalls?

The Lyriq appears most, in five of the seven recalls, across the 2023, 2024 and 2025 model years. The others are the 2026 Escalade IQ and IQL, the 2023-2025 XT5 and the 2024-2025 XT6 for instrument panel illumination.

Do the fire-risk battery notices tell owners to park outside?

Neither fire-risk notice — the 2025 Lyriq battery bolts or the 2026 Escalade IQ and IQL battery module component — tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. Both describe a fire risk tied to a damaged high voltage battery and direct owners to the free repair.

What do dealers do about the high-voltage battery recalls?

For the 2025 Lyriq, dealers inspect the high-voltage battery bolts and replace or re-tighten them as necessary. For the 2026 Escalade IQ and IQL, dealers replace the high voltage battery. Both notices say the work is free of charge.
