Why do some Porsche airbags fail to deploy — or deploy unexpectedly?

3 NHTSA recalls · Porsche · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-05-05

These three Porsche air bag recalls involve unrelated causes across different models. In 2018 Macan and Macan S vehicles, the front passenger seat occupancy sensor mat may degrade and fail to detect a passenger, so the air bag won't deploy in a crash. In 2022-2023 Taycan vehicles, an error in the occupant classification system may deactivate the front passenger air bag for the same reason — non-deployment. Separately, in certain 2021-2024 911 models equipped with optional full bucket seats, the seat-mounted air bags may deploy unnecessarily during a crash — the opposite problem, an air bag firing when it shouldn't.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No dashboard or driver warning is described for the Macan sensor mat degradation before a crash
  • No dashboard or driver warning is described for the Taycan classification-system error before a crash
  • No warning sign is described for the 911 unnecessary air bag deployment before it happens

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2018 Macan sensor mat recall, the 2022-2023 Taycan classification-system recall, and the 2021-2024 911 bucket-seat recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice explains only what could go wrong with the air bag during a crash — non-deployment in the Macan and Taycan, unnecessary deployment in the 911 — without directing owners to park outside or avoid driving in the meantime.

What the free repair involves

Dealers address each cause differently: for the Macan, they replace the front passenger seat's sensor mat and seat cushion; for the Taycan, they replace the front passenger seat cushion; for the 911, they reprogram the air bag control unit. All three repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Is this the same airbag problem across Macan, Taycan, and 911?

No. The Macan and Taycan recalls involve the front passenger air bag failing to deploy due to a sensor or classification error. The 911 recall involves seat-mounted air bags, in cars with optional bucket seats, deploying when they shouldn't.

Which 911 models are covered by the unnecessary-deployment recall?

The recall covers certain 2021-2024 911 Turbo S, 911 Turbo, 911 GT3, 911 GT3 Touring, 911 GT3 RS, 911 Carrera GTS, 911 Carrera 4 GTS, and 911 Dakar vehicles equipped with optional full bucket seats.

How do I know if my Macan or Taycan needs this fix?

Check your VIN against the recall on NHTSA.gov, or contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. The Macan recall number is AMA2; the Taycan front-passenger air bag recall number is ASA2.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
