Porsche airbag recalls center on passenger detection and seat-mounted bags

3 NHTSA recalls · Porsche · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 5, 2025

Two of these three Porsche recalls turn on whether the car correctly detects a front seat passenger, and both are remedied in the seat itself. AMA2 covers 2017-2018 Macan, Macan Turbo Kits, Macan GTS, 2015-2018 Macan S and Macan Turbo vehicles, where the front passenger seat occupancy sensor mat may degrade and fail to detect a passenger. ASA2 covers 2022-2023 Taycans, where an occupant classification system error may deactivate the front passenger air bag. The third, APB3, runs the other direction: across 911 models from 2021 to 2024 equipped with optional full bucket seats, the seat-mounted air bags may deploy unnecessarily during a crash. The Macan and Taycan entries both concern an air bag that may not deploy.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. For the Macan recall (AMA2) the notice says only that the air bag will not deploy during a crash if a front passenger is undetected, increasing the risk of injury. For the Taycan recall (ASA2) it says an air bag that fails to deploy during a crash increases the risk of injury. For the 911 recall (APB3) it says air bags that deploy unnecessarily can increase the risk of injury. The notices give no other interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

Each remedy is free of charge and specific to its own recall. For AMA2 on the listed Macan models, dealers replace the sensor mat and seat cushion. For ASA2 on 2022-2023 Taycans, dealers replace the front passenger seat cushion. For APB3 on the listed 911 models with optional full bucket seats, dealers reprogram the air bag control unit.

Common questions

Can I tell whether my Macan's occupancy sensor mat has failed?

The AMA2 notice states only that the front passenger seat occupancy sensor mat may degrade and fail to detect a passenger. It does not describe any warning sign for an owner. Dealers replace the sensor mat and seat cushion, free of charge.

Does the 911 recall apply to every 911 from those years?

APB3 applies to the listed 911 models equipped with optional full bucket seats: 2021-2023 911 Turbo S, 2022 911 Turbo, 2022-2023 911 GT3 Touring, GT3 and Carrera 4 GTS, 2022-2024 Carrera GTS, 2023 GT3 RS and Dakar, and 2024 Carrera T.

What do dealers do for the Taycan front passenger airbag recall?

Under ASA2, dealers replace the front passenger seat cushion free of charge on 2022-2023 Taycans. The notice says an occupant classification system error may deactivate the front passenger air bag, and that an air bag failing to deploy increases the risk of injury.
