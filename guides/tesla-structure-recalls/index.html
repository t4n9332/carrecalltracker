Tesla recalls for Cybertruck body parts and missing occupant protection

3 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Structure · model years 2024–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-07

These three Tesla recalls have little in common beyond the body of the vehicle. Two involve Cybertruck exterior parts: the 2025 recall covers 2024-2025 Cybertruck vehicles whose light bar can delaminate and detach, and the 2024 recall covers 2024 Cybertruck vehicles whose trunk bed trim sail applique could have been improperly adhered and can loosen. In both, the notice's concern is a part coming off and becoming a road hazard for following motorists. The third, from 2026, is a compliance recall: certain 2026 Model Y and 2024 Model 3 vehicles originally built for a foreign market do not have knee air bags, may not be equipped with certain interior safety countermeasures, and may not have been tested for bumper impact resistance procedures.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2025 Cybertruck light bar recall and the 2024 Cybertruck trim applique recall both describe a part that can detach and become a road hazard for following motorists, and the 2026 noncompliance recall describes reduced occupant protection in a crash, but none of the three gives owners any interim instruction about continuing to drive. Each notice sets out only the defect, the risk and the remedy.

What the free repair involves

For the light bar recall, Tesla service replaces the cant rail assembly. For the trim applique recall, it applies adhesion promoter and pressure sensitive tape, or replaces a missing applique as necessary. The 2026 noncompliance recall is not a repair: Tesla will repurchase noncompliant vehicles currently registered in the United States. All three notices say this is free of charge.

Common questions

What does Tesla do about the Cybertruck light bar?

The 2025 notice covers 2024-2025 Cybertruck vehicles whose light bar can delaminate and detach, becoming a road hazard. Tesla service replaces the cant rail assembly, free of charge; owner notification letters were mailed September 19, 2025.

Why would Tesla buy back a Model 3 instead of repairing it?

The 2026 notice says certain 2026 Model Y and 2024 Model 3 vehicles were originally built for a foreign market and do not meet FMVSS 208, 201 and Part 581. The remedy it states is that Tesla will repurchase noncompliant vehicles currently registered in the United States, free of charge.

Should I check the trunk bed trim myself before the repair?

The notice does not tell owners to inspect anything. It says the trunk bed trim sail applique on certain 2024 Cybertruck vehicles could have been improperly adhered and can loosen, and that Tesla service applies adhesion promoter and tape or replaces a missing applique, free of charge.
