Why is Tesla recalling Cybertruck trim and Model 3 safety gear?

3 NHTSA recalls · Tesla · Structure · model years 2024–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-07

These three Tesla recalls cover unrelated structural issues rather than one repeating defect. The most serious covers 2024 Model 3 and 2026 Model Y vehicles built for a foreign market that lack knee air bags, may be missing interior safety countermeasures, and were not tested to U.S. bumper impact standards. The other two recalls affect the Cybertruck's exterior trim: a 2025 recall for the light bar delaminating and detaching from the vehicle, and a 2024 recall for the trunk bed trim sail applique coming loose because it was improperly adhered. Both trim issues create a road hazard if the piece separates while driving.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No owner-noticeable symptom for the Model 3/Model Y safety-standard recall — the vehicles were built to a foreign market's specifications
  • Light bar may delaminate or detach from the Cybertruck (2025 recall)
  • Trunk bed trim sail applique may loosen or come off the Cybertruck (2024 recall)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The Model 3/Model Y safety-standard recall, in which Tesla will repurchase affected noncompliant vehicles, does not instruct owners to stop driving. The Cybertruck light bar recall describes a road hazard risk if the bar detaches, without a stop-driving instruction. The Cybertruck trim applique recall likewise describes a road hazard for following motorists without telling owners to stop driving. Across this group, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the Model 3/Model Y recall, Tesla repurchases the noncompliant vehicles rather than repairing them. For the Cybertruck light bar recall, Tesla Service replaces the cant rail assembly. For the trim applique recall, Tesla Service applies adhesion promoter and tape or replaces the missing applique. All remedies are provided free of charge.

Common questions

Why is Tesla repurchasing some Model 3 and Model Y vehicles instead of repairing them?

Those vehicles were originally built for a foreign market and lack U.S.-required knee air bags, certain interior safety features, and bumper testing, so Tesla is buying back noncompliant vehicles registered in the United States.

Is the Cybertruck light bar recall dangerous to other drivers?

The notice says the light bar can delaminate and detach from the vehicle, which can become a road hazard and increase the risk of a crash.

What causes the Cybertruck trim piece to come loose?

The notice says the trunk bed trim sail applique could have been improperly adhered during assembly, allowing it to loosen and potentially detach while driving.
