Which BMW recall told owners to stop driving over suspension bolts?

8 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Suspension · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-12-05

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Suspension bolt and control arm defects repeat across several BMW model lines: bolts not properly tightened or hardened led to separate recalls for X5/X6 front suspension bolts (2017), X5/X6 front lower control arms (2018), X6 rear control arms (2018), and an X5/X6/X7 front suspension weld (2020). Wheel bearing cracking appears twice — a critical 2019 recall for X3/X4 front axle swivel bearings, and a 2024 recall for the X3 front left wheel bearing. BMW motorcycles are affected too, with rear suspension strut recalls for K1600 models (2022) and front strut recalls for K1600 and R1250RT (2024).

Warning signs to watch for

  • None of these suspension recall notices describe a warning sign an owner would notice before a bolt, control arm, weld, strut or bearing fails

Is it safe to keep driving?

One of these eight recalls carries a stop-driving instruction: the 2019 recall for 2020 BMW X3 sDrive30i, X3 xDrive30i, X3 M40i, X4 xDrive30i and X4 M40i, where BMW told owners not to drive their vehicles until the front axle swivel bearings are replaced. None of the other seven suspension recalls — covering X5/X6 bolts, control arms, the X5/X6/X7 weld, the K1600 and R1250RT motorcycle struts, or the 2024 X3 wheel bearing — carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; for those, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace or properly tighten suspension bolts, replace front or rear control arms, replace the front axle support where the weld may fail, replace front or rear swivel and wheel bearings, or replace motorcycle suspension struts. All of these repairs are free.

Common questions

Which BMW suspension recall told owners to stop driving?

The 2019 recall for 2020 X3 sDrive30i, X3 xDrive30i, X3 M40i, X4 xDrive30i and X4 M40i vehicles, over front axle swivel bearings that may not have been properly heat treated and could break.

Why do so many BMW X5 and X6 suspension recalls involve bolts?

Three separate recalls (2017 front suspension bolts, 2018 front lower control arm, 2018 rear control arm) each cite bolts that weren't properly tightened or hardened — distinct incidents, not one repeated fix.

Are BMW motorcycles affected by suspension recalls too?

Yes. K1600 B, GT and GTL models have a 2022 rear suspension strut recall, and K1600 B and R1250RT models have a separate 2024 front suspension strut recall.
