Which BMW suspension recalls cover control arms, bearings and struts?

8 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Suspension · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 5, 2024

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Eight BMW suspension recalls turn on parts that can break or come loose. Control arms and their bolts repeat: pivot bolts that may not have been properly hardened on 2018 X5 and X6 lower-left control arms and on 2019 X6 rear upper control arms, plus untightened front suspension bolts on the 2018 X5 and X6. Front axle swivel bearings appear twice — improperly heat treated on 2020 X3 and X4 models, and cracking on the 2025 X3. A weld between the front axle support and control arm bearing bracket covers 2020 X5, X6, X7 and 2021 X5. Two motorcycle recalls involve K 1600 and R 1250 RT struts.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One recall here carries a stop-driving instruction: for the 2020 X3 sDrive30i, X3 xDrive30i, X3 M40i, X4 xDrive30i and X4 M40i front axle swivel bearings, BMW notified owners and instructed them not to drive their vehicles until the repair is done. The other seven notices — the control arm, suspension bolt, front axle support weld, 2025 X3 swivel bearing and the K 1600 and R 1250 RT strut recalls — do not tell owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside.

What the free repair involves

These repairs are free of charge. Dealers replace the front axle swivel bearings on the 2020 X3 and X4, and the front left swivel bearing on the 2025 X3. They inspect and tighten the wishbone suspension bolts, inspect and replace the lower-left control arms if necessary, replace both rear upper control arms, replace the front axle support, replace the rear link strut, and replace front spring struts after inspection.

Common questions

Which BMW suspension recall tells owners not to drive?

The 2020 X3 and X4 front axle swivel bearing recall. The notice says BMW notified owners and instructed them not to drive their vehicles; the bearings may not have been properly heat treated and can break. Dealers replace them free of charge.

Are the two swivel bearing recalls on the X3 the same thing?

They are different recalls. The 2020 recall covers X3 sDrive30i, xDrive30i and M40i plus X4 xDrive30i and M40i, with bearings that may not have been properly heat treated. The 2025 recall covers the X3 30, where the front left swivel bearing may crack.

Are BMW motorcycles included in these suspension recalls?

Two of the eight are motorcycle recalls. The 2019-2020 K 1600 GT, GTL and B were recalled for a rear link strut with insufficient strength, and the 2024 R 1250 RT, K 1600 GT, GTL and B for a front spring strut proportional valve that can loosen.
