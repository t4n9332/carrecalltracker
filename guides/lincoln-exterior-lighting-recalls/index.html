Why do Lincoln exterior lighting recalls keep hitting headlights and rear lights?

6 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Exterior Lighting · model years 2022–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 1, 2025

Six Lincoln recalls here concern lights that can stop working, and the rear of the vehicle comes up again and again. Water and moisture drive two of them: the 2024-2025 Corsair, where water may leak into the rear combination lights and cost reverse, tail, brake or rear turn signal function, and the 2025 Navigator, where cracks in the rear lightbar lens or weld track let moisture in. The 2022 Navigator appears in two recalls over headlight control module software affecting side marker and daytime running lights, the second covering vehicles previously repaired under the first. The 2025 Nautilus is recalled for LED driver modules with a burnt diode, and the 2025 Aviator for trailer tail lights that may not illuminate.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Low-beam and high-beam headlights, daytime running lights, front position lights or front turn signals failing in the affected headlight — the 2025 Nautilus LED driver module recall, which says rear tail light, rear turn signal and rear position light may also fail on Nautilus.
  • Side marker and daytime running lights failing — both 2022 Navigator headlight control module software recalls.
  • Loss of reverse light, tail light, brake light or rear turn signal function — the 2024-2025 Corsair rear combination light recall.
  • Reverse lights that are inoperative or flickering — the 2025 Navigator rear lightbar recall.
  • Trailer tail lights that fail to illuminate when towing — the 2025 Aviator body control module recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, and none tells owners to stop driving. That applies to the 2025 Nautilus LED driver module recall, both 2022 Navigator headlight software recalls, the 2025 Aviator trailer light recall, the 2024-2025 Corsair rear combination light recall and the 2025 Navigator rear lightbar recall. Each notice describes the lighting failure and the crash risk it creates, and each directs owners to a dealer for the repair, but none gives an interim instruction about driving.

What the free repair involves

All of these repairs are free of charge. Dealers inspect and replace the LED driver modules on the 2025 Nautilus and the rear combination light assemblies on the 2024-2025 Corsair, as necessary. The 2022 Navigator recalls are headlight control module software updates, with headlight recalibration in the earlier one. Dealers replace the 2025 Navigator rear lightbar, and replace or functionally check body control module C on the 2025 Aviator.

Common questions

My 2022 Navigator already had a headlight software recall. Why another one?

The 2025 recall covers 2022 Navigators previously repaired under recall 22V455 (22C14). The notice says the headlight control module software may cause the side marker and daytime running lights to fail, and dealers will update the software free of charge.

Which Lincoln models have rear lighting recalls?

The 2024-2025 Corsair, where water may leak into the rear combination lights, and the 2025 Navigator, where cracks in the rear lightbar can let moisture in. On the 2025 Nautilus, the LED driver module recall can also affect rear tail, turn and position lights.

Does the 2025 Aviator recall affect the vehicle's own lights?

The notice describes trailer tail lights that may fail to illuminate when towing, due to an improperly manufactured body control module. It does not describe a failure of the Aviator's own exterior lights, and it cites Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108.
