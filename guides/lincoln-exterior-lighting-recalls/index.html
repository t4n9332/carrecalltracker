Why Do Lincoln Vehicles Keep Losing Exterior Lights?

6 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Exterior Lighting · model years 2022–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-01

Lincoln's exterior lighting recalls in this group center on repeated headlight and taillight failures across several models. The 2022 Lincoln Navigator had a headlight control module software defect that caused side marker and daytime running lights to fail; Ford recalled it again in 2025 after the same defect resurfaced on vehicles previously repaired under the first recall. Separately, water intrusion caused the 2024-2025 Lincoln Corsair's rear combination lights to lose reverse, brake, tail, or turn-signal function, and a cracked rear lightbar can cause the 2025 Navigator's reverse lights to flicker or fail. The 2025 Lincoln Nautilus shares a burnt-diode LED driver module defect with the Ford Mustang, and the 2025 Lincoln Aviator's trailer lights can fail to illuminate when towing.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Low-beam, high-beam, daytime running, front position, or front turn signal lights not working (Nautilus)
  • Side marker lights or daytime running lights not working (2022 Navigator)
  • Trailer tail lights failing to illuminate while towing (Explorer, Aviator)
  • Reverse, brake, tail, or turn signal lights not working, especially after water exposure (Corsair)
  • Reverse lights flickering or not working (2025 Navigator)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the six recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe reduced visibility to the driver or other road users as the risk in each case, but do not tell owners to stop driving for the Nautilus/Mustang headlight recall, either Navigator headlight software recall, the Explorer/Aviator trailer light recall, the Corsair rear light recall, or the 2025 Navigator reverse light recall. Across all six recalls, the notices only describe the loss of exterior lighting function and do not instruct owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and replace the LED driver module (Nautilus), update the headlight control module software (Navigator, 2022 defect), inspect and replace the body control module (Explorer/Aviator trailer lights), replace the rear combination light assemblies (Corsair), or replace the rear lightbar (2025 Navigator). All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why did the Lincoln Navigator headlight recall happen twice?

Ford recalled the 2022 Navigator in 2022 for a headlight control module software defect, then recalled the same vehicles again in 2025 because the side marker and daytime running light failure returned on vehicles already repaired under the first recall.

Can I still drive my Lincoln Corsair with the rear light recall?

The notice does not tell owners to stop driving. It says water can leak into the rear combination lights and cause a loss of reverse, brake, tail, or turn signal function, which can reduce visibility to other drivers.

What's causing the Lincoln Aviator trailer light problem?

An improperly manufactured body control module can prevent the trailer's tail lights from illuminating while towing. Dealers replace or inspect the module depending on vehicle mileage, free of charge.
