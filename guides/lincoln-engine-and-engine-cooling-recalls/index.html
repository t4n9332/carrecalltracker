Why Do Lincoln Engines Leak Oil Or Lose Power?

5 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-03

The clearest pattern here is an oil leak from a misaligned engine cup plug on the 3.5L GTDI engine in the Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator, recalled in back-to-back notices in late 2024 and again in early 2025. A separate hybrid engine fire risk - oil and fuel vapor collecting near hot components - affects the Escape, Maverick, and Corsair's 2.5L HEV or PHEV engines in a 2022 recall. Two more recalls describe a loss of drive power from valve failure: broken intake valves on 2021-2022 Bronco, Explorer, Aviator, Edge, and Nautilus vehicles with 2.7L or 3.0L EcoBoost engines (2024), and a failing EGR valve on 2025 Explorer, Mustang, and Nautilus vehicles (2026).

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rapid oil leak that can cause the engine to stall (Expedition/Navigator cup plug recalls)
  • Unusual engine noise, reduced power, or smoke (2022 Escape/Maverick/Corsair hybrid fire recall)
  • Unexpected loss of drive power (2024 intake valve and 2026 EGR valve recalls)
  • The notices describe no dashboard warning for the oil leak recalls

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2022 Escape/Maverick/Corsair hybrid engine fire recall does tell owners to park and shut off the engine as quickly as possible if they hear unexpected engine noises, notice reduced power, or see smoke, but that instruction applies only to that recall. For the two oil-leak recalls and the two valve-failure recalls, the notices describe the defect and its consequence but do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and, if needed, replace the misaligned engine cup plug; modify the under-engine shield and active grille shutter on the hybrid fire recall; perform an engine cycle test and replace the engine if intake valves are affected; or replace the EGR valve. All of these repairs are done free of charge.

Common questions

Why is my Lincoln Navigator leaking oil?

Two recalls (late 2024 and early 2025) point to a misaligned engine cup plug on the 3.5L GTDI engine, which can cause a rapid oil leak. That can stall the engine and, if the oil reaches hot parts, raise fire risk.

What should I do if my hybrid Corsair or Escape smells like fuel?

The 2022 recall for 2.5L HEV/PHEV Escape, Maverick, and Corsair models tells owners to park and shut off the engine as quickly as possible if they hear unusual noises, notice reduced power, or see smoke.

What's the EGR valve recall about?

A 2026 recall for 2025 Explorer, Mustang, and Lincoln Nautilus vehicles: the EGR valve may fail and cause an unexpected loss of drive power. Dealers replace the EGR valves free of charge.
