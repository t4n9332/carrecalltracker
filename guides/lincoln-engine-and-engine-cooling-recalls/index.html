Why do Lincoln engine recalls point to oil leaks and fires?

5 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-03

These five Lincoln engine recalls split between leaks that can feed a fire and engines that lose power. The 2021-2022 Corsair with the 2.5L HEV or PHEV engine is recalled because an engine failure can release engine oil and fuel vapor into the engine compartment near hot components. Two separate recalls name the 2024 Navigator with the 3.5L GTDI engine for a misaligned engine cup plug that can cause a rapid oil leak, an engine stall, or a fire. The 2021-2022 Aviator and Nautilus with 2.7L or 3.0L Nano EcoBoost engines can break an intake valve while driving, and the 2025 Nautilus is recalled for an EGR valve that may fail.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Unexpected engine noises, a reduction in vehicle power, or smoke — the 2021-2022 Corsair 2.5L HEV/PHEV engine fire recall names these.
  • An engine stall — the 2024 Navigator engine cup plug recalls say a rapid oil leak can result in an engine stall.
  • A loss of drive power — named by the 2021-2022 Aviator and Nautilus intake valve recall and the 2025 Nautilus EGR valve recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. For the 2021-2022 Corsair 2.5L HEV or PHEV recall, the notice advises owners to park and shut off the engine as quickly as possible if they hear unexpected engine noises, notice a reduction in vehicle power, or see smoke. For the two 2024 Navigator cup plug recalls, the 2021-2022 Aviator and Nautilus intake valve recall, and the 2025 Nautilus EGR valve recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Repairs differ by recall and all are free of charge. For the 2021-2022 Corsair, dealers modify the under engine shield and active grille shutter. For the 2024 Navigator, dealers inspect the engine cup plug alignment and replace the plug if necessary. For the 2021-2022 Aviator and Nautilus, dealers perform an engine cycle test and replace the engine as necessary. For the 2025 Nautilus, dealers replace the EGR valves.

Common questions

Which Lincoln models are in these engine recalls?

The 2021-2022 Corsair for the 2.5L HEV or PHEV fire risk, the 2024 Navigator for the engine cup plug oil leak, the 2021-2022 Aviator and Nautilus for intake valve failure, and the 2025 Nautilus for EGR valve failure.

Do any of these recalls tell Lincoln owners to stop driving?

None of the five notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 2021-2022 Corsair notice does advise owners to park and shut off the engine as quickly as possible if they hear unexpected engine noises, notice a reduction in power, or see smoke.

Will my Lincoln get a new engine under the intake valve recall?

For the 2021-2022 Aviator and Nautilus with 2.7L or 3.0L Nano EcoBoost engines, the notice says dealers perform an engine cycle test and replace the engine as necessary, free of charge. It does not say every vehicle receives a replacement.
