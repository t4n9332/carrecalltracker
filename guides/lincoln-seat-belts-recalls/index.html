Why do Lincoln seat belts keep getting recalled?

7 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-27

Three of these seven Lincoln recalls describe the same defect: the front seat belt pretensioner in the Navigator (shared with the Ford Expedition) inadvertently deploys or locks, so the driver or front passenger belt will not retract or extend. Ford covered 2018-2020 Navigators in 2024 and again in 2025, then widened it to 2018-2022 in 2026. A second pattern is fastener torque: seat belt buckle, retractor and anchor bolts on 2020-2022 Aviators (and Explorers) were improperly secured, first at several seating positions, then again at the second-row outer positions. The remaining two involve 2020-2023 Aviator and Corsair models with the Revel audio system, where the unbuckled-driver chime sounds too briefly; the 2025 recall re-repairs cars fixed incorrectly the first time.

Warning signs to watch for

  • A driver or front passenger belt that will not extend or will not retract, as if locked.
  • A belt buckle, retractor or anchor that feels loose at any seating position, especially second-row outer belts in an Aviator.
  • The seat belt warning chime stops after only a moment when the driver is unbuckled (Aviator and Corsair with the Revel audio system).
  • For the anchor bolt recalls, there may be no warning at all; improperly secured bolts are found by inspection.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside, so you can keep driving while you wait. Treat them differently, though. If a front belt in a Navigator will not extend or retract, Ford's 2026 notice says to avoid using that seat until the retractor is replaced, and a loose anchor bolt in an Aviator leaves that belt unable to protect a rider in a crash, so book those repairs promptly. The Aviator and Corsair chime recalls change nothing about the belts themselves; just buckle up every trip.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and replace the front seat belt retractors on Navigator and Expedition, adding a missing HVAC drain tube elbow in the earliest campaign. On Aviator they inspect the buckle, retractor and anchor bolts and repair or replace them as needed. For Aviator and Corsair, the audio control module software is reprogrammed. All repairs are free; some Navigator owners received interim letters while parts were arranged.

Common questions

My Navigator was already repaired, so why another seat belt letter?

Ford issued the front pretensioner recall three separate times, in 2024, 2025 and 2026, with the last one widening coverage to 2018-2022 vehicles. The 2025 Aviator and Corsair chime recall similarly re-repairs cars that were fixed incorrectly under the 2022 campaign.

Can I still use a seat whose belt has locked up?

Ford's 2026 notice advises avoiding a front seat whose belt will not extend or retract until the retractor is replaced. A belt stuck in that state cannot restrain you properly in a crash, so use another seating position until the repair is done.

How would I notice loose seat belt bolts in my Aviator?

You may not. The notices describe improperly secured buckle, retractor and anchor bolts rather than anything you would see or feel, which is why dealers inspect every seating position on 2020-2021 cars and the second-row outer belts on later ones.
