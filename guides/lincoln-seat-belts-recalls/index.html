Why do Lincoln Navigator and Aviator seat belts keep locking or loosening?

7 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-27

Seat belt recalls for Lincoln models cluster around three distinct problems. Three recalls target the Expedition-based Navigator (2018-2022 model years), where a driver or front-passenger pretensioner can lock the belt so it won't retract or extend. Two recalls cover the Explorer-based Aviator (2020-2025 model years), where seat belt buckle or second-row anchor bolts were improperly secured or can loosen over time. Two more recalls affect the Aviator and Corsair (2020-2023 model years) equipped with a Revel Audio System, where the seat belt warning chime sounds for less than the required four seconds when the driver is unbuckled, including one recall issued after an earlier repair failed to fix it.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Seat belt won't retract or extend after buckling
  • Seat belt buckle or anchor bolt feels loose
  • Warning chime sounds for less than 4 seconds when unbuckled

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the pretensioner-lock recalls (Expedition and Navigator), the notices say only that a locked belt may not properly restrain an occupant in a crash. For the loose anchor bolt recalls (Explorer and Aviator) and the short-chime recalls (Aviator and Corsair), the notices likewise do not tell owners to stop driving, describing only the increased risk of injury during a crash or from an inadequate seat belt warning.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and replace seat belt retractors on the Expedition and Navigator, and inspect seat belt buckle and anchor bolts on the Explorer and Aviator, replacing or repairing components found to be loose. On the Aviator and Corsair, dealers update the audio control module software so the seat belt chime sounds long enough. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Which Lincoln models are affected by the seat belt pretensioner recall?

The pretensioner-lock recalls cover 2018-2022 Lincoln Navigator and Ford Expedition vehicles, where the driver or front-passenger seat belt pretensioner may inadvertently lock, preventing the belt from retracting or extending, according to the notices.

What's wrong with the seat belts in the Lincoln Aviator?

Two recalls cover 2020-2025 Aviator vehicles: one for improperly secured seat belt buckle or second-row anchor bolts, and a separate one for a seat belt warning chime that sounds for less than four seconds.

Do I need to stop driving because of these seat belt recalls?

No. None of the seven seat belt recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices describe increased risk of injury in a crash but do not instruct owners to stop driving the vehicle.
