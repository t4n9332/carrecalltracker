Lincoln seat belt recalls: locking belts, loose bolts, short chimes

7 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 27, 2026

Seven seat belt recalls split into three repeating problems. The Navigator carries the first: recalls in 2024, 2025 and 2026 all say the driver and/or front passenger pretensioner may lock the belt so it cannot retract or extend, covering 2018-2020 Navigators twice and 2018-2022 Navigators in the 2026 notice. The Aviator carries the loose bolts — a 2025 recall on 2020-2021 Aviators for buckle and second-row center anchor bolts, and a 2026 recall on 2020-2025 Aviators for second-row outer anchor bolts. Aviators and Corsairs with the Revel Audio System were recalled twice, in 2022 and 2025, for a seat belt warning chime that sounds less than four seconds.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2024, 2025 and 2026 Navigator pretensioner recalls say the driver and/or front passenger belt may lock and will not retract or extend.
  • The 2022 and 2025 Revel Audio System recalls (Aviator, Corsair) say the warning chime sounds for less than four seconds when the vehicle is started with the driver's belt unbuckled.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

No notice in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. For the 2026 Navigator pretensioner recall, Ford mailed interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk, with additional letters to follow once the remedy is available, anticipated in August 2026. The other six notices describe only the risk — a belt that does not retract or extend, a loose belt or anchor, or a chime that does not properly alert the driver — and do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the seat belt retractors on the Navigator pretensioner recalls, and under the 2024 recall also install a missing HVAC drain tube elbow. On the Aviator bolt recalls dealers inspect buckle, retractor and anchor bolts and replace or repair affected seat components. The chime recalls update the audio control module software. Every notice that names a cost says the repair is free of charge.

Common questions

My Navigator's belt has locked and will not pull out — is that recalled?

Three recalls describe that. The 2024 and 2025 notices cover 2018-2020 Navigators and say the driver and/or front passenger pretensioner may lock the belt; the 2026 notice covers 2018-2022 Navigators. Dealers replace the retractors as necessary. Check your VIN with Ford or NHTSA.

Why was my Lincoln recalled twice for the seat belt chime?

The 2022 recall covers 2020-2023 Aviators and 2020-2022 Corsairs with the Revel Audio System, whose chime may sound under four seconds. The 2025 recall covers 2020-2023 Aviators and Corsairs that its notice says were repaired incorrectly under the earlier recall. Both update software free.

What do the notices say about using the belts before the repair?

The notices do not tell owners to stop driving and do not give any instruction about using the belts before the repair. They state the risk: a belt that will not retract or extend, a loose anchor, or a short chime may not restrain an occupant properly in a crash.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
