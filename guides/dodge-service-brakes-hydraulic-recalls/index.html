Dodge Brake Recalls Cover Rollaway, Pedal Collapse, and Warning Failures

5 NHTSA recalls · Dodge · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 7, 2024

Durango ABS module problems recur in this group. The June 2022 and November 2024 notices cover certain 2018-2019 Durangos and describe brake light illumination and the ability to start and shift out of Park without pressing the brake pedal. The November notice also identifies disabled ABS and electronic stability control. A separate notice covers an ESC warning indicator failure in 2021-2022 Durangos. Other models have different brake defects: certain 2018-2019 Grand Caravans and 2018 Journeys may have reduced rear braking from insufficiently coated caliper pistons, while certain 2024-2025 Hornets may experience brake pedal collapse and loss of brake function.

Warning signs to watch for

  • June 2022 Durango ABS recall: Brake lights may illuminate, and the vehicle may start and shift out of Park without brake pedal application.
  • August 2018 rear brake recall, U86: Rear braking performance may be reduced.
  • October 2024 Hornet recall, B7B: The brake pedal may collapse, causing loss of brake function.
  • November 2024 Durango ABS recall, 94B: Brake lights may illuminate incorrectly, and the vehicle may start and shift out of Park without brake pedal application.
  • March 2022 Durango recall, Z20: The ESC malfunction indicator may fail to illuminate during an electronic stability control problem.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five brake recall notices tells owners to stop driving, and none includes a park-outside instruction. The June 2022 Durango rollaway notice and November 2024 Durango ABS module notice do not tell owners to stop driving. The August 2018 rear brake notice for Grand Caravan and Journey, October 2024 Hornet brake pedal notice, and March 2022 Durango ESC warning indicator notice likewise do not tell owners to stop driving. Those are the driving instructions provided in the supplied notices.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies include bleeding brakes for U86, reinforcing the brake pedal arm for B7B, replacing the integrated pressure transducer and updating ABS software as necessary for 94B, and updating ABS software for Z20. The June 2022 notice specifies updated HCU/ABS software and says the new remedy is required after its replacement by 24V838; its excerpt gives no cost statement.

Common questions

Does a repair under the June 2022 Durango recall finish the required work?

The June 2022 notice says that recall is replaced by NHTSA recall 24V838. It explicitly states that vehicles already repaired under the June 2022 recall need the new remedy completed. The listed November 2024 notice specifies pressure transducer replacement and ABS software work.

What repair does the Dodge Hornet brake pedal recall require?

The October 2024 notice covers certain 2024-2025 Dodge Hornets whose brake pedal may collapse and cause loss of brake function. Dealers reinforce the brake pedal arm free of charge. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

Which Durango brake recall concerns a missing stability warning light?

The March 2022 recall, Z20, covers certain 2021-2022 Dodge Durangos. ABS control module software may prevent the ESC malfunction indicator from illuminating during certain conditions. Dealers update the ABS control module software free of charge.
