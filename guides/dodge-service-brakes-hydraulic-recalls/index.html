Why Dodge brake, ABS and stability-control systems have been recalled

5 NHTSA recalls · Dodge · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Nov 7, 2024

The recurring thread in this group is the antilock-brake module rather than the friction brakes. Two recalls cover the same 2018-2019 Dodge Durango and Jeep Grand Cherokee: an ABS module malfunction that may illuminate the brake lights and allow the vehicle to start and shift out of Park without the brake pedal applied. The later notice, 94B, adds disabled ABS and electronic stability control and states that it replaces the earlier recall. Recall Z20 is also ABS software, covering 2019-2022 Ram 2500 and 3500 cab chassis and 2021-2022 Dodge Durango. Two hardware recalls stand apart: rear brake caliper piston coating on the 2018-2019 Grand Caravan, 2018 Journey and Jeep models, and a collapsing brake pedal on the 2024-2025 Dodge Hornet.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake lights may illuminate when they should not — recall 94B and the earlier ABS module recall it replaces, on 2018-2019 Dodge Durango and Jeep Grand Cherokee.
  • The vehicle may start and shift out of Park without the brake pedal being applied — the same two 2018-2019 Durango and Grand Cherokee ABS module recalls.
  • The electronic stability control malfunction indicator light may fail to illuminate during an ESC problem — recall Z20, 2019-2022 Ram 2500 and 3500 cab chassis and 2021-2022 Dodge Durango.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the five notices in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2022 ABS notice for the 2018-2019 Durango and Grand Cherokee is the strongest wording here — it describes vehicle rollaway, a crash without prior warning, and injury to people outside the vehicle — yet it still carries no stop-driving instruction. The Hornet notice (B7B) describes a loss of brake function and the Grand Caravan and Journey notice (U86) a reduction of braking performance; neither tells owners to stop driving. No notice gives any other interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

The remedies differ by recall. For the 2018-2019 Durango and Grand Cherokee under 94B, dealers replace the integrated pressure transducer and update the ABS module software as necessary. Recall Z20 is an ABS control module software update. For the 2018-2019 Grand Caravan, 2018 Journey and the Jeep models under U86, dealers bleed the brake system. For the 2024-2025 Hornet under B7B, dealers reinforce the brake pedal arm. Each notice states the work is free of charge.

Common questions

My Durango was already repaired under the earlier ABS recall — is that the end of it?

The 2022 notice says that recall is replaced by NHTSA recall number 24V838, and that vehicles already repaired under it will need the new remedy completed. Under 94B, dealers replace the integrated pressure transducer and update the ABS module software as necessary, free of charge.

What exactly does the Dodge Hornet brake pedal recall describe?

Recall B7B covers certain 2024-2025 Dodge Hornet and Alfa Romeo Tonale vehicles, and states that the brake pedal may collapse and cause a loss of brake function. Dealers reinforce the brake pedal arm free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 17, 2024.

Under recall Z20, is my stability control itself faulty?

The notice states only that the ABS control module software may prevent the ESC malfunction indicator light from illuminating during certain conditions, a non-compliance with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 126. It describes no other effect. Dealers update the ABS control module software free of charge.
