Why do GMC trucks and SUVs keep getting seat belt recalls?

9 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 16, 2025

Fasteners dominate this group. Buckle bolts that were not tightened properly cover 2023-2024 Hummer EV, 2023 Canyon and 2024-2025 Sierra EV; incorrect bolts cover 2021 Canyon, Sierra 1500, 2500 and 3500, Yukon and Yukon XL; and front-row center brackets that may not be secured to the seat frame cover 2019-2021 Sierra 1500, 2020-2021 Sierra 2500 and 3500 and 2021 Yukon XL. Third-row belts on Yukon and Yukon XL appear twice: 2021 models for belts entrapped or misrouted behind the seat-folding mechanism, and 2021-2022 models for an improperly formed buckle rivet. Two older recalls differ — incorrect retractor torsion bars on 2017-2018 Sierra 2500 and 3500, and pretensioner gas that may ignite carpet on 2019-2020 Sierra 1500 and 2020 Sierra 2500 and 3500.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these nine notices tells owners to stop driving, and none asks owners to park outside. That includes the pretensioner recall on 2019-2020 Sierra 1500 and 2020 Sierra 2500 and 3500, whose notice says a vehicle fire could result if materials ignite inside the vehicle. The other eight describe belts, brackets, bolts, rivets or retractors that may not restrain an occupant as intended in a crash. Beyond having the free repair performed, the notices give owners no interim instruction of any kind.

What the free repair involves

The work differs by recall. Dealers tighten buckle attachment bolts, replace suspect bolts, inspect front-row center bracket attachments and reassemble them correctly as necessary, inspect third-row belts and replace or reroute them, inspect and replace third-row buckle assemblies, replace both front retractor assemblies, or close off the opening in the pretensioner bracket. Every notice states the repair is free of charge.

Common questions

What does the seat belt fire recall actually describe?

That notice, covering 2019-2020 Sierra 1500 and 2020 Sierra 2500 and 3500 with carpet floor covering, says that when the front seat belt pretensioners deploy, hot gas may vent through an opening in the pretensioner bracket, possibly igniting the carpet.

Why haven't I received a letter about the Sierra EV seat belt recall?

The 2024-2025 Sierra EV notice states that all affected vehicles are still in dealer inventory, so no owner letters will be mailed. GM will tighten the bolt that attaches the center seat belt assembly to the rear seat structure, free of charge.

Which GMC models are in the third-row seat belt recalls?

Both cover Yukon and Yukon XL with third-row seating. One covers 2021 models whose third-row outboard belts may have been entrapped or misrouted behind the seat-folding mechanism; the other covers 2021-2022 models with an improperly formed buckle rivet.
