Why does BMW have so many exterior lighting recalls?

10 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-09-29

BMW's exterior lighting recalls in this group cover many different lights rather than one repeated defect. The X7 appears three times: dim rear reflectors in 2020, a front parking light that can go dark in 2023, and blue-glowing headlights in 2020-2021 X5, X6 and X7 models. The Z4, shared with the Toyota Supra, was recalled twice for headlights: horizontal misalignment in 2019-2020 models and control units that can cause failure in 2020 models. Other recalls are one-off: flashing brake lights on 2020 motorcycles, wrong-colored turn signals on the 2018 X3, detaching taillights on the 2018-2019 X1, a detaching third brake light on the 2018 M6 Gran Coupe, and an unlit license plate on the 2022 X4.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake light flashing instead of staying steady during hard braking (2020 R1250 GS, R1250 R and S1000RR motorcycles)
  • Turn signals on the outboard and inboard sides flashing different colors at once (2018 X3)
  • A taillight that feels loose or has detached from the vehicle (2018-2019 X1)
  • A third brake light that becomes loose or detaches (2018 M6 Gran Coupe)
  • Headlights glowing blue instead of white (2020-2021 X5, X6 and X7)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these ten lighting recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the flashing brake lights, misaligned or failing Z4/Supra headlights, wrong-colored X3 turn signals, detaching X1 taillights, dim X7 reflectors, the detaching M6 Gran Coupe third brake light, the X7 parking light and blue-glow headlight issues, and the unlit X4 license plate all direct owners to schedule a free repair rather than telling them to stop driving or park outside.

What the free repair involves

Dealer fixes vary by defect: reprogramming the brake-light and parking-light control units, inspecting or replacing headlamp adjustment plugs and control units, inspecting or replacing turn signal and taillight assemblies, replacing rear reflectors and headlamps, resecuring or replacing the M6 Gran Coupe's third brake light, and modifying the X4's license plate bracket. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why has the X7 had three separate lighting recalls?

Each addresses a different light: dim rear reflectors in 2020, a front parking light that can go dark in 2023, and headlights that can glow blue in 2020-2021 models — not one repeated problem.

Do the Z4 lighting recalls also affect the Toyota Supra?

Yes. Both the headlight misalignment recall and the headlight control unit failure recall list the Toyota Supra alongside the Z4, since the two models share components.

Is a flashing brake light on my BMW motorcycle dangerous?

The notice says a flashing rather than steady brake light during emergency braking can confuse other drivers, increasing crash risk; it affects 2020 R1250 GS, R1250 R and S1000RR models.
