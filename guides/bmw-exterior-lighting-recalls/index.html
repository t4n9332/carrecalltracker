BMW exterior lighting recalls: headlights, reflectors and turn signals

10 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-09-29

One federal lighting standard drives most of this group: seven of the ten notices say the vehicles fail Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108. The X7 appears three times — inadequate rear reflex reflectors on 2020 xDrive40i and xDrive50i, blue light from 2020-2021 headlamps shared with the X5 and X6, and a 2023 X7 where only one of the two front parking lights may remain illuminated. The Z4 appears twice, both times alongside the Toyota Supra: headlamps that can be adjusted horizontally, and headlight control units that may fail. Two more involve lights working loose — 2018-2019 X1 taillight housings and the 2014-2019 M6 Gran Coupe third brake light. Motorcycles contribute one brake light recall.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rear turn signals illuminating red on the outboard side panel while the inboard signals illuminate amber (2018 X3 recall).
  • Loss of one or both headlights (2020 Z4, 330i and Toyota Supra headlight control unit recall).
  • Only one of the two front parking lights remaining illuminated (2023 X7 recall).
  • A brake light that flashes instead of remaining steady during emergency braking (2020 BMW motorcycle recall).
  • Blue light from the headlamps (2020-2021 X5, X6 and X7 recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these ten notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That includes the two recalls where a light can come off the vehicle — the 2018-2019 X1 taillights and the 2014-2019 M6 Gran Coupe third brake light — and the 2020 Z4, 330i and Supra headlight control units that may fail, causing a loss of one or both headlights. Each notice states the crash risk and the free repair, and none gives owners an interim instruction or tells them to limit how the vehicle is used.

What the free repair involves

Repairs vary and every notice says they are free. Dealers replace the 2020 X7 rear reflectors and the Z4, 330i and Supra headlight control units; secure X1 taillights and the M6 third brake light; inspect and, if needed, replace X3 turn signals and X5, X6 and X7 headlamps; reprogram the motorcycle emergency stop signal and the 2023 X7 parking light module; and modify the X4 license plate bracket.

Common questions

Why do these BMW lighting recalls keep citing a federal standard?

Seven of the ten notices say the vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment." It covers the reflectors, turn signal colors, parking lights, license plate lighting, headlamp adjustment and blue headlamp light described here.

Why is the Toyota Supra listed in BMW lighting recalls?

Two notices list the 2020 Toyota Supra with the Z4: headlamps able to be adjusted horizontally, and headlight control units that may fail. Toyota owners are directed to Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371; BMW owners to BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

Which recalls involve a light that can come off the vehicle?

Two. On 2018-2019 X1 models, contaminated hardware may crack the taillight housing attachment brackets, letting the lights loosen or detach. On 2014-2019 M6 Gran Coupe, the third brake light may become loose and detach. Both repairs are free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
