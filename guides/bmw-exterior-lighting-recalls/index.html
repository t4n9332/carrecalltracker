Why do BMW lights keep failing, detaching, or shining wrong?

10 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2022-09-29

Ten recalls, and most are Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108 compliance problems rather than outright failures. The X7 appears three times: 2020 rear reflex reflectors that reflect too little light, headlamps allowing blue light on 2020-2021 X5, X6 and X7, and a 2023 X7 front parking light that can leave only one side illuminated. The Z4 and its Toyota Supra twin appear twice — headlight control units that fail on 2020 cars, and 2019-2020 headlamps that can be adjusted horizontally. Parts working loose is the other theme, with 2018-2019 X1 taillight brackets cracking from contaminated hardware and 2014-2019 M6 Gran Coupe third brake lights detaching. Three more cover X3 turn signal color, X4 license plate lighting, and motorcycle emergency brake light flashing.

Warning signs to watch for

  • One or both headlights suddenly go dark on a 2020 Z4, 330i or Toyota Supra.
  • A taillight housing that sits loose, rattles, or has detached from an X1.
  • The third brake light on an M6 Gran Coupe loose, hanging, or missing.
  • Rear turn signals lighting red on the outboard side while the inboard side lights amber.
  • Only one of two front parking lights lit on a 2023 X7, or a motorcycle brake light that flashes instead of staying steady during emergency braking.

Is it safe to keep driving?

No recall in this group carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, and BMW says owners can keep driving while scheduling the free repair. The headlight control unit recall on 2020 Z4, 330i and Toyota Supra is the one to treat as urgent: if a headlight has already failed, the notice advises avoiding night driving and poor visibility until it is repaired. The rest — reflectors, blue-tinted headlamps, license plate lights, misaimed headlamps, turn signal color, the parking light — mainly raise risk after dark and can wait for a normal service visit.

What the free repair involves

Everything is free of charge. Dealers replace failed headlight control units, rear reflex reflectors and out-of-spec headlamps; secure loose X1 taillights and refasten or replace the M6 third brake light; correct the headlamp vertical and horizontal adjustment plugs; modify the X4 rear license plate bracket; and reprogram control units for the X7 front parking lights and the motorcycle emergency stop signal. Rear turn signals are inspected and replaced if needed.

Common questions

Why is my Toyota Supra included in a BMW recall?

The Z4 and Supra share hardware, so BMW files the recall and both makes are covered. Two recalls here list them together — the headlight control unit failure and the headlamps that can be adjusted horizontally. Supra owners can call Toyota at 1-888-270-9371.

My X1 taillight feels loose — can I keep driving it?

BMW does not tell X1 owners to stop driving. Contaminated hardware can crack the taillight attachment brackets and let the light loosen or detach, which matters most at night, so book the free repair promptly and be cautious after dark.

Are any of these lighting fixes done over the air?

No. Two involve reprogramming — the 2023 X7 front parking light control unit and the motorcycle emergency stop signal function — but the notices have dealers perform that work. Every repair in this group requires a dealer visit and is free.
