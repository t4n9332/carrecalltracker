Audi Fuel System Recalls Cover Leaks, Stalling, and Filler Spills

5 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 24, 2025

Fuel leakage repeats across Audi’s gasoline fuel system notices, but the components differ. Recall 24DP concerns leaking fuel rails on 2016–2018 Q7, A6, and A7 vehicles and 2015–2018 A8 vehicles. The February 2024 notice covers front-wheel-drive 2015–2020 A3 Sedan and 2015–2019 A3 Cabriolet vehicles, where a suction jet pump seal problem can send fuel into the EVAP system. Recall 23HD concerns fuel pump fasteners on one 2025 SQ7. Separately, 20DR addresses pump failure and stalling in 2019–2021 Q8 and 2020–2021 Q7 vehicles. The September 2022 notice concerns filler-neck spills on 2021–2022 Q3 vehicles. Q7 appears in both fuel-rail and pump-failure notices.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 20DR: Fuel pump failure can cause an engine stall.
  • September 2022 Q3 recall: Fuel may spill from the filler neck when the fuel cap is opened.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for fuel rail recall 24DP, the February 2024 A3 suction pump recall, SQ7 recall 23HD, Q7/Q8 pump recall 20DR, and the September 2022 Q3 vent-pipe recall do not tell owners to stop driving. The notices identify fire risks from fuel leaks, crash risk from an engine stall, or injury risk from fuel spilling when the cap opens. They do not establish that continued driving is safe.

What the free repair involves

Free remedies differ: 24DP replaces both fuel rails; the February 2024 recall replaces the suction pump; 23HD inspects and tightens screw connections, replacing the high pressure pump and fuel lines as necessary; 20DR replaces the fuel pump. The Q3 remedy inspects the internal vent pipe and replaces the charcoal canister and fuel tank if necessary.

Common questions

Which Audi fuel pump recall can cause an engine stall?

Recall 20DR covers certain 2019–2021 Audi Q8 and 2020–2021 Audi Q7 vehicles. A component inside the fuel pump may break, causing pump failure and an engine stall. Dealers replace the fuel pump free of charge.

What causes the A3 fuel leak described in the 2024 notice?

The February 2024 notice identifies a suction jet pump seal problem in certain front-wheel-drive 2015–2020 A3 Sedan and 2015–2019 A3 Cabriolet vehicles. Fuel may enter the EVAP system and leak from the charcoal canister. Dealers replace the suction pump free.

Why might fuel spill when an affected Q3 fuel cap opens?

The September 2022 recall says a damaged vent pipe inside the fuel tank may allow this spill on certain 2021–2022 Q3 vehicles. Dealers inspect the pipe and replace the charcoal canister and fuel tank if necessary, free of charge.
