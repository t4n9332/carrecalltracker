Audi fuel system recalls: what leaks and stalls do they cover?

5 NHTSA recalls · Audi · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Sep 24, 2025

Fuel leaks dominate this group. Recall 24DP covers certain 2016-2018 Q7, A6 and A7 and 2015-2018 A8 vehicles whose left and right fuel rails may leak fuel. A February 2024 recall covers certain front-wheel drive 2015-2020 A3 Sedan and 2015-2019 A3 Cabriolet vehicles, alongside several Volkswagen Golf, Golf GTI, Golf Sportwagen and Jetta GLI variants, where a suction jet pump seal inside the fuel tank may let fuel flow into the evaporative emissions system and leak out of the charcoal canister. Recall 23HD covers one 2025 SQ7 with high pressure fuel pump fasteners that may not be tightened properly. Two others are not leak-driven: recall 20DR covers 2019-2021 Q8 and 2020-2021 Q7 vehicles where a component inside the fuel pump may break, and a separate recall covers 2021-2022 Q3 vehicles with a damaged fuel tank vent pipe.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall 20DR (2019-2021 Q8, 2020-2021 Q7): the notice says fuel pump failure can cause an engine stall.
  • Q3 vent pipe recall (2021-2022 Q3): the notice says fuel may spill out of the filler neck when the fuel cap is opened.
  • The other notices in this group describe fuel leaking but do not state a warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none carries a park-outside instruction. Recall 24DP, recall 23HD and the A3 suction jet pump recall each describe a fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source as increasing the risk of a fire. Recall 20DR describes fuel pump failure causing an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash. The Q3 notice says a damaged vent pipe may allow fuel to spill out of the filler neck when the fuel cap is opened, increasing the risk of an injury. The notices give owners no interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

All remedies are free of charge. Under 24DP dealers replace the left and right fuel rails; under the A3 recall, the suction pump; under 20DR, the fuel pump. Under 23HD dealers inspect and tighten the screw connections and replace the high pressure fuel pump and fuel lines as necessary. For the Q3, dealers inspect the internal vent pipe and replace the charcoal canister and the fuel tank if necessary.

Common questions

Does the Audi A3 fuel tank recall also cover Volkswagen models?

Yes. That recall lists certain front-wheel drive 2015-2020 A3 Sedan and 2015-2019 A3 Cabriolet vehicles together with certain Golf, Golf GTI, Golf Sportwagen and Jetta GLI models. Audi owners and Volkswagen owners received separate final notification letters in 2024.

Will Audi pay to replace my Q7 or Q8 fuel pump?

The notice for recall 20DR says dealers will replace the fuel pump free of charge on certain 2019-2021 Q8 and 2020-2021 Q7 vehicles. Owner notification letters were mailed December 22, 2022; Audi customer service is 1-800-253-2834.

What does the Q3 recall say about opening the fuel cap?

The notice for certain 2021-2022 Q3 vehicles says a damaged vent pipe may allow fuel to spill out of the filler neck when the fuel cap is opened, increasing injury risk. It does not tell owners to stop driving.
