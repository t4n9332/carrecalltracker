GMC electrical recalls: stalling, lost drive power and battery fires

4 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Electrical System · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 30, 2026

Four GMC electrical recalls split across two eras. The oldest covers 2019-2020 Sierra 1500 trucks, where battery positive cable rings may have been manufactured with excessive glue, potentially causing an intermittent connection between the cable and the alternator, a stall, and electrical arcing. Two more center on GM's electric lineup: 2024 GMC Hummer EV SUV and Sierra EV rear drive units whose motor wires may be insufficiently insulated and contact each other, and the 2022 GMC Hummer EV Pickup high voltage battery with an improperly secured internal module component. Separately, 2023 GMC Canyon trucks have a front camera module that may falsely detect an obstacle and engage automatic emergency braking. Sierra 1500, Sierra EV, Hummer EV and Canyon are the GMC names involved.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The vehicle stalls (2019-2020 Sierra 1500 battery positive cable recall)
  • A loss of drive power (2024 GMC Hummer EV SUV and Sierra EV rear drive unit recall)
  • Automatic emergency braking engages unexpectedly and the truck abruptly stops or slows down (2023 GMC Canyon front camera module recall)

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; each states that owners are not told to stop driving. What they do describe is the risk itself: a stall and electrical arcing on the 2019-2020 Sierra 1500 cable recall, a loss of drive power on the 2024 Hummer EV SUV and Sierra EV recall, a fire risk from a loose component inside the high voltage battery on the 2022 Hummer EV Pickup recall, and unexpected automatic braking on the 2023 Canyon recall. The notices say nothing further about continuing to drive.

What the free repair involves

For the 2019-2020 Sierra 1500, dealers inspect the B+ cable connection at the alternator, clean and reattach it, and replace the cable, nut or alternator if arcing damaged them. Dealers replace the high voltage battery on the Hummer EV Pickup recall, replace the rear drive unit on the 2024 EV recall, and update front camera module software on the 2023 Canyon. Those three notices say free of charge.

Common questions

What does the Sierra 1500 battery cable recall actually involve?

It covers certain 2019-2020 Sierra 1500 trucks whose battery positive cable rings may have excessive glue. Dealers inspect for glue or contamination at the alternator attachment, clean the connection and reattach the cable, and replace the cable, nut or alternator if arcing damaged them.

Is the Hummer EV high voltage battery repaired or replaced?

The notice says dealers will replace the high voltage battery, free of charge, on certain 2022 GMC Hummer EV Pickup and 2026 Cadillac Escalade IQ and IQL vehicles with an improperly secured internal module component. Owner notification letters were mailed August 24, 2026.

Can the 2023 GMC Canyon braking recall be done over the air?

The notice says dealers will update the front camera module software, free of charge, and it does not describe an over-the-air update. Owner notification letters were mailed April 5, 2024, and GM's number for this recall is A232424660.
