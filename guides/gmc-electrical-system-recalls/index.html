Why does GMC keep recalling electrical and battery systems?

4 NHTSA recalls · GMC · Electrical System · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-30

Four NHTSA recalls cover electrical system problems across GMC models from 2019 through 2026. Two involve fire risk: excess glue on the battery positive cable causing an intermittent connection and possible arcing on 2019-2020 Sierra 1500 trucks, and a loose internal component in the high voltage battery on the 2022 Hummer EV Pickup (shared with the 2026 Cadillac Escalade IQ/IQL). A third recall covers insufficiently insulated wiring in the rear drive unit that can cause a loss of drive power on the 2024 Hummer EV and Sierra EV (shared with Cadillac Lyriq and Chevrolet Blazer EV/Silverado EV). A fourth, lower-severity recall covers the 2023 Canyon's front camera falsely detecting obstacles and triggering unexpected automatic braking.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Vehicle may stall unexpectedly (2019-2020 Sierra 1500 battery cable recall)
  • Sudden loss of drive power (2024 Hummer EV/Sierra EV rear drive unit recall)
  • Truck may brake or slow down unexpectedly without driver input (2023 Canyon camera recall)
  • The notices describe no driver-noticeable warning sign for the high voltage battery recall before a fire risk develops

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four GMC electrical system recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The battery cable recall (2019-2020 Sierra 1500) describes stall and fire risk from arcing, and the high voltage battery recall (2022 Hummer EV Pickup) describes fire risk from a loose internal component, but neither tells owners to stop driving. The rear drive unit recall describes a loss of drive power, and the front camera recall describes unexpected automatic braking; both notices likewise do not instruct owners to stop driving or park outside.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect the battery cable connection and clean or replace damaged parts, replace the high voltage battery, replace the rear drive unit, or update the front camera module software, depending on the recall. All inspections, part replacements, and software updates are performed free of charge to the owner.

Common questions

Can a GMC battery cable recall cause a fire?

The notice says excess glue on the battery cable connection can cause an intermittent connection and electrical arcing, which can generate enough heat to increase fire risk. Dealers inspect and clean or replace the connection free of charge.

Why would my GMC EV lose power while driving?

A 2024 recall covers insufficiently insulated wiring in the rear drive unit of the Hummer EV and Sierra EV, which can allow wires to contact each other and cause a loss of drive power. Dealers replace the rear drive unit free of charge.

Why does my GMC Canyon brake on its own?

The 2023 Canyon's front camera module can falsely detect an obstacle and trigger automatic emergency braking unexpectedly. Dealers update the front camera module software free of charge to fix the false detection.
