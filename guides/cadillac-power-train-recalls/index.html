Cadillac transmission recalls: wheel lock-up, half-shafts and lost drive power

7 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Power Train · model years 2019–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-02-13

Transmissions dominate this group, and wheel lock-up is the repeating outcome. Three recalls describe it: 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV with diesel engines and 2022 Escalade and Escalade ESV with the 10-speed transmission and Electronic Transmission Range Select, both for a transmission control valve, and 2019-2020 CT6 with 2020-2021 CT4 and CT5 for internal transmission damage. All three are answered with transmission control module software. The XT crossovers carry the second pattern, half-shafts leaving the transmission: 2023 XT5 for a right-front half-shaft connection and 2022-2023 XT5 with 2023 XT6 for an incorrect sun gear. Two more cover 2019-2020 XT4 and 2020 XT6 accumulator bolts and 2021 Escalade rear driveshafts.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rear wheels that lock up while driving (2021 diesel Escalade and Escalade ESV recall, and the 2022 Escalade and Escalade ESV 10-speed recall).
  • Wheels that lock up while driving (2019-2020 CT6, 2020-2021 CT4 and CT5 10-speed transmission recall).
  • A loss of drive power, or the vehicle rolling away when placed in park (2023 XT5 half-shaft recall and the 2022-2023 XT5 and 2023 XT6 sun gear recall).
  • A sudden loss of drive power (2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV rear driveshaft recall).
  • A transmission oil leak that may progress to a loss of propulsion (2019-2020 XT4 and 2020 XT6 start/stop accumulator recall).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seven notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. That includes the three wheel lock-up recalls on 2021 diesel Escalade and Escalade ESV, 2022 Escalade and Escalade ESV, and the CT4, CT5 and CT6 sedans; the two half-shaft recalls on 2023 XT5 and XT6; the 2021 Escalade driveshaft recall; and the XT4 and XT6 accumulator recall, whose notice describes both crash and fire risk. None of them gives owners an interim instruction to follow before the repair.

What the free repair involves

Three recalls are software: dealers install transmission control module software on the 2021 diesel and 2022 Escalade groups, and monitoring software on the CT4, CT5, CT6 and Camaro group. The rest are parts — rear driveshaft assemblies on 2021 Escalade, right-front half-shafts on 2023 XT5, sun gears on the XT5 and XT6 group, and inspection of the start/stop accumulator with replacement if bolts are missing. All state free of charge.

Common questions

Which Cadillacs are in the wheel lock-up recalls?

Three notices describe lock-up: 2021 Escalade and Escalade ESV with diesel engines, 2022 Escalade and Escalade ESV with the 10-speed transmission and Electronic Transmission Range Select, and 2019-2020 CT6 with 2020-2021 CT4 and CT5 fitted with a 10-speed transmission.

What does the diesel recall's special coverage program cover?

The diesel notice says GM will provide a special coverage program to cover repair of transmissions that the remedy software identifies as containing a defective control valve, on top of the free transmission control module software installation.

Why do two XT5 recalls both mention half-shafts?

They name different causes. The 2023 XT5 and GMC Acadia notice says right-front half-shafts may be improperly connected to the transmission; the 2022-2023 XT5, 2023 XT6, Blazer, Traverse and Acadia notice says an incorrect sun gear can let the driver-side half-shaft disengage.
