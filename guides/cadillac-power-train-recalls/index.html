Cadillac Power Train Recalls Involve Wheel Lockup and Lost Propulsion

8 NHTSA recalls · Cadillac · Power Train · model years 2019–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 7, 2026

⚠ 1 of these recalls carries a do-not-drive advisory.

Cadillac's listed power train recalls repeatedly involve wheel lockup, lost propulsion, and half-shaft separation. Escalade and Escalade ESV recur most: separate notices cover 2021 driveshaft failure, a transmission control valve problem in 2021 diesel vehicles, another control valve problem in certain 2022 vehicles, and missing transfer case components in certain 2015-2020 and 2026 vehicles. XT5 appears in two half-shaft notices covering certain 2023 and 2022-2023 vehicles, respectively. Other notices concern missing accumulator endcap bolts in 2019-2020 XT4 and 2020 XT6 vehicles, and internal transmission damage in certain 2019-2020 CT6 and 2020-2021 CT4 and CT5 vehicles. The causes and remedies differ by recall.

Warning signs to watch for

  • October 29, 2020 transmission accumulator recall: transmission oil leakage and loss of propulsion.
  • January 27, 2022 driveshaft recall: sudden loss of drive power.
  • February 2 and March 16, 2023 half-shaft recalls: loss of drive power or rollaway when placed in park.
  • October 24, 2024, March 6, 2025, and February 13, 2026 transmission recalls: wheel lockup.
  • May 7, 2026 transfer case recall: front and/or rear wheels locking up without warning.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One of the eight listed recalls carries a stop-driving instruction: the May 7, 2026 transfer case recall tells affected owners not to drive until the remedy is completed. None carries a park-outside instruction. The other seven notices do not tell owners to stop driving: October 29, 2020; January 27, 2022; February 2, 2023; March 16, 2023; October 24, 2024; March 6, 2025; and February 13, 2026. The stop-driving instruction belongs specifically to the May 2026 transfer case recall.

What the free repair involves

Remedies are free: the 2020 recall inspects accumulators and replaces those missing bolts; January 2022 replaces affected rear driveshafts; February 2023 replaces right-front half-shafts; March 2023 replaces sun gears. October 2024, March 2025, and February 2026 install transmission control module software. May 2026 inspects and, if necessary, replaces transfer case assemblies.

Common questions

Which Cadillac power train recall says to stop driving?

The May 7, 2026 transfer case notice tells affected owners not to drive until the remedy is completed. Its Cadillac coverage includes certain 2015-2020 and 2026 Escalade and Escalade ESV vehicles. Dealers inspect and replace the transfer case assembly if necessary.

Why do two XT5 notices mention half-shaft separation?

The February 2023 notice concerns improperly connected right-front half-shafts in certain 2023 XT5 vehicles. The March 2023 notice concerns an incorrect transmission sun gear that can let the driver-side half-shaft disengage in certain 2022-2023 XT5 vehicles.

Which Cadillac transmission recalls use a software remedy?

The October 2024 notice specifies new transmission control module software for affected 2021 diesel Escalade models. March 2025 specifies monitoring software for affected CT4, CT5, and CT6 vehicles. February 2026 specifies new TCM software for affected 2022 Escalade models.
