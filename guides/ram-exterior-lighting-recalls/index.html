Why Do Ram Trucks Have Recurring Brake And Signal Light Problems?

5 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-02-02

Three of these five recalls involve emergency vehicles built on Ram chassis with Whelen brake lights that can illuminate even when the brake pedal is not pressed: a 2019 recall covering various ambulance chassis including a 2018 Ram 4500, and two more 2019 recalls each covering a single 2018 Ram 5500 (one a Minuteman vehicle, one a fire truck). Separately, a 2021 recall covers 2019-2021 Ram ProMaster City vans whose turn signal flash rate does not change to warn the driver when a bulb fails. A 2026 recall covers a trailer tow module shared with the Jeep Wagoneer S that can prevent 2025-2026 Ram 3500 and 5500 trailer lights and trailer brakes from working.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake lights on an ambulance, Minuteman vehicle, or fire truck built on a Ram chassis that turn on without the brake pedal being pressed
  • Turn signals on a 2019-2021 ProMaster City that keep flashing at the normal rate instead of speeding up when a bulb has failed
  • Trailer lights that don't illuminate or trailer brakes that don't work when towing with a 2025-2026 Ram 3500 or 5500

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recall notices tell owners to stop driving or park outside. The ambulance, Minuteman, and fire truck brake light recalls, the ProMaster City turn signal recall, and the Jeep and Ram trailer tow module recall each describe the defect and the repair without instructing owners to stop driving. For the trailer tow module recall, the notice does say trailer brakes may fail, but it does not tell owners to stop driving or towing.

What the free repair involves

The brake light recalls have owners contact Whelen or the vehicle builder for free replacement brake lights. Dealers update the Body Controller Module software on ProMaster City vans to fix the turn signal warning, and dealers replace the trailer tow module on affected Jeep Wagoneer S and Ram trucks. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why do three separate recalls involve the same Whelen brake lights?

Whelen brake lights were installed by different vehicle builders - an ambulance maker, and two builders of Ram 5500-based trucks - and each builder issued its own recall once NHTSA identified the wiring defect that can make the lights illuminate without the brake pedal being pressed.

Will my ProMaster City turn signal recall need a part replaced?

No. The 2019-2021 ProMaster City recall is fixed with a free software update to the Body Controller Module, which changes the flash rate to warn the driver when a turn signal bulb fails, rather than replacing any bulbs or wiring.

What does the trailer tow module recall affect if I'm not towing?

The notice describes the defect only in terms of towing: trailer brake lights, turn signals, and trailer brakes may fail on affected 2025-2026 Ram trucks and Jeep Wagoneer S models. It does not describe any effect on the vehicle when no trailer is attached.
