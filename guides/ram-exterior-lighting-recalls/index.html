Ram exterior lighting recalls: turn signals, brake lights and trailer lamps

5 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Exterior Lighting · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Feb 2, 2026

Three of these five recalls are the same brake light problem on 2018 Ram heavy chassis: Excellance ambulances on a Ram 4500 chassis, a Blanchat B-27 Minuteman on a Ram 5500 chassis, and an Unruh Fire truck on a 2018 Dodge 5500 chassis, all fitted with Whelen brake lights that may illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed. The other two come from FCA. The 2019-2021 Ram ProMaster City recall covers turn signal lights that fail without the flash rate changing to warn the driver, a Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 108 compliance issue. The newest covers a trailer tow module on 2025-2026 Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 pickups and 3500, 4500 and 5500 cab chassis.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake lights that illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed - the Excellance ambulance, Blanchat Minuteman and Unruh Fire brake light recalls.
  • A turn signal flash rate that does not change when one or both turn signal lights fail - the 2019-2021 Ram ProMaster City recall.
  • Trailer lights that fail to illuminate and trailer brakes that fail - the 2024-2026 trailer tow module recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The ProMaster City turn signal notice, the trailer tow module notice and the three Whelen brake light notices from Excellance, Blanchat and Unruh Fire each describe an increased risk of a crash and then move straight to the repair. They set out the defect, the consequence and the remedy, and say nothing further about driving the vehicle before that repair is done.

What the free repair involves

Remedies differ by recall. For the ProMaster City, dealers update the Body Controller Module software. For the trailer tow module recall, dealers replace the module. In the Excellance recall owners are instructed to contact Whelen to have the brake lights replaced; Blanchat will replace the brake lights, and Unruh Fire will provide replacement brake lights. Each notice says the work is free of charge.

Common questions

My ProMaster City turn signal does not flash faster when a bulb fails. Is that the recall?

That is what the 2019-2021 Ram ProMaster City notice describes: if one or both turn signal lights fail, the flashing rate may not change to warn the driver. Dealers update the Body Controller Module software, free of charge.

Are the 2018 Ram 4500 and 5500 brake light recalls handled at a Ram dealer?

Those three notices point owners to the body builder or the lamp supplier instead. Excellance instructs owners to contact Whelen to have the brake lights replaced, Blanchat will replace the brake lights, and Unruh Fire will provide replacement brake lights, each free of charge.

Which Ram models are in the trailer tow module recall?

That notice lists 2025-2026 Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 Pickup and Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500 Cab Chassis, plus 2024-2026 Jeep Wagoneer S and 2026 Jeep Cherokee. Dealers replace the trailer tow module, free of charge.
