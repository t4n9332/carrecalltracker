What BMW brake recalls cover calipers, hoses and the integrated brake system?

11 NHTSA recalls · BMW · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 1, 2026

Brake recalls at BMW divide between motorcycles and cars. On motorcycles and scooters, calipers dominate: corroded pistons on the C 400 X, G 310 GS and G 310 R, a front caliper that leaks when parked on 2019-2020 R 1250 GS, GS Adventure and RT models, and a cracking front brake hose on C600 and C650 scooters. Two March 2020 recalls cover brake fluid reservoir caps that do not state the required fluid. On cars, the X3 and X5 recur: rear caliper coating on 2018-2019 X3 and 2019 X4, a loose right front disc on the 2021 X5, and rear brake hoses and front discs on the 2025 X3. The integrated brake system is recalled twice.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake fluid leaking from the front brake calipers when the motorcycle is parked, named in the front caliper recall covering 2019-2020 R 1250 GS, GS Adventure and RT and 2020 S 1000 RR and S 1000 XR.
  • A front brake hose that cracks and leaks, named in the C600 Sport, C650 Sport and C650 GT scooter recall.
  • A brake caliper piston sticking or dragging in the caliper bore, named in the C 400 X, G 310 GS and G 310 R recall.
  • A longer distance needed to stop the vehicle from a loss of power brake assist, named in the integrated brake system recall covering 2023-2025 BMW and MINI models.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the eleven brake notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. That includes the integrated brake system recalls, the 2019-2021 X5, X7, X6, 7 Series and 8 Series weld recall and the 2023-2025 BMW and MINI recall describing a loss of power brake assist, and the 2025 X3 rear brake hose recall. The 2020 front brake caliper leak recall on R 1250 and S 1000 motorcycles says BMW sent owners an interim notification before the remedy was available.

What the free repair involves

The work differs by recall. Dealers bleed the rear brake circuit on 2018-2019 X3 and X4 models, replace brake hoses on the C650 scooters and the 2025 X3, replace corroded calipers on the G 310 and C 400 X, swap brake fluid reservoir caps, replace front discs and pads on the 2025 X3, and inspect or replace the integrated brake system. Every notice says the repair is free of charge.

Common questions

What is the BMW integrated brake system recall about?

Two notices name it. The 2021 recall covers 2019-2021 X5, X7 and 2020-2021 X6, 7 Series and 8 Series models, where a rotor and shaft may separate during hard braking, with a loss of ABS. The 2026 recall covers 2023-2025 BMW and MINI models losing power brake assist.

Why would a brake fluid reservoir cap be recalled?

Two March 2020 notices say the cap may not clearly state the required brake fluid type and the filling process, which fails Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 122. One covers R 1250 and RnineT motorcycles, the other accessory reservoir covers sold for them. Dealers replace the cap.

Is the 2025 BMW X3 covered by more than one brake recall?

Two notices name it. A March 2025 recall covers 2025 X3 30 xDrive and X3 M50 xDrive for rear brake hoses that may leak, taking rear braking, ABS and DSC with them. An April 2025 recall covers front brake discs damaged during transport.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
