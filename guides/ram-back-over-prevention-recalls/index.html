Why Does My Ram Truck's Backup Camera Keep Getting Recalled?

12 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-08-26

Software problems that prevent the rearview camera image from displaying are the dominant issue in this group, repeating across radio software errors (Ram 3500, ProMaster, and other Ram models in recalls from 2022 through 2023), trailer reverse steering control module errors on the 2021 Ram 1500, a security gateway software error on 2022-2025 ProMaster vans, and cracked microprocessors on 2019-2021 ProMaster and Dodge Journey vehicles. Two recalls describe different display problems: a 2020 recall where the camera image lingers on screen after leaving reverse on several 2019-2020 Ram and Jeep models, and a 2018 recall where the image is lost entirely on 2018 Dodge Journey and Ram 1500, 2500, and 3500 trucks. A 2022 ProMaster recall involves the wrong camera hardware being installed rather than a software defect.

Warning signs to watch for

  • No rearview camera image displaying when the vehicle is in reverse
  • The rearview camera image staying on the screen after shifting out of reverse (2020 recall)
  • A rearview camera that loses its image while backing up (2018 recall)
  • A ProMaster built with the wrong type of camera that isn't functional at all (2022 recall)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the 12 Ram back-over-prevention recalls in this group tell owners to stop driving or park outside while waiting for repairs. That includes the recalls where the rearview camera image fails to display at all, the 2020 recall where the image lingers on screen after the vehicle leaves reverse, and the 2022 ProMaster recall involving the wrong camera hardware. Each notice says the reduced rear visibility or driver distraction increases the risk of a crash, but none instructs owners not to drive the vehicle beforehand.

What the free repair involves

Across this group, dealers mostly update software: reprogramming the radio, security gateway module, or trailer reverse steering control module so the camera image displays correctly. Two recalls also offered an over-the-air update instead of a dealer visit. Other recalls replaced the rearview camera hardware, the rearview mirror, or supplied the correct digital camera. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why does my Ram truck's backup camera screen stay black?

Several Ram recalls describe a software error, in the radio, security gateway module, or trailer reverse steering control module, that can prevent the rearview camera image from displaying when the truck is in reverse. Dealers update or reprogram the affected software free of charge.

Can I fix a Ram backup camera recall without visiting the dealer?

For two recalls in this group, a 2020 recall covering several Ram and Jeep models and a 2024 recall covering Ram, Jeep, and Chrysler vehicles, owners could update the software over-the-air instead of visiting a dealer. Other recalls in this group require a dealer visit.

Why did my ProMaster get a camera recall for the wrong part?

A 2022 recall found some Ram ProMaster chassis cab vehicles were built with an analog rearview camera that is the wrong type and cannot be installed. Dealers supplied the correct digital rearview camera free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
