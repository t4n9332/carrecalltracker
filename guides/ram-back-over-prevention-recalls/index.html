Why does my Ram's backup camera image fail to display?

12 NHTSA recalls · RAM · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 26, 2025

Twelve back-over prevention recalls cover Ram trucks and vans, and most come back to the same thing: the rearview image not appearing. Radio software is the most repeated cause, in recalls Z86 (2020-2021 Ram 1500), Z47 (2022 Ram 3500), 97A (2022-2023 Ram 3500) and 66B/79B (2022 Ram 3500 and 2022-2023 ProMaster). The 2021 Ram 1500 appears three times over its trailer reverse steering control module, in recalls Y30, Y80 and 14A. ProMaster has its own set: the wrong analog camera type in 2022 (ZC5), security gateway software in 2022-2025 (40C), and a cracked camera microprocessor in 2019-2021. The oldest, U56, covers 2018 Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 and replaces the mirror.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rearview image does not display — 2021 Ram 1500 trailer reverse steering control module recalls Y30, Y80 and 14A.
  • No rearview image — radio software recalls Z86 (2020-2021 Ram 1500), Z47 (2022 Ram 3500), 97A (2022-2023 Ram 3500) and 66B/79B.
  • Rearview image stays on the screen after the vehicle is shifted out of reverse — 2019-2020 Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 radio display recall.
  • Rearview image does not display properly — 2019-2021 Ram ProMaster camera microprocessor recall; and recall 40C on 2022-2025 ProMaster.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the twelve notices in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Each states its own risk: Chrysler says a rearview image that does not display reduces the driver's rear view and increases the risk of a crash, while the 2019-2020 radio display recall says a lingering rearview image can distract the driver. The 2019-2021 ProMaster and 2019-2020 Dodge Journey camera recall says interim letters notified owners of the safety risk and a second notice will follow when the final remedy is available. The notices give no other driving instruction.

What the free repair involves

Most are software fixes: dealers reprogram radio software (Z86, Z47, 97A, 66B/79B), trailer reverse steering module software (Y30, Y80, 14A) or security gateway module software (40C). Recall 66B/79B may also arrive over the air, and the 2019-2020 display recall offered an optional over-the-air update. Others are hardware: a replacement rearview mirror (U56), a replacement camera on 2019-2021 ProMaster, or a digital camera (ZC5). Nearly every notice says free of charge.

Common questions

My Ram's backup camera screen stays black — which recall covers that?

Several do, and the cause differs by truck: radio software on 2020-2021 Ram 1500 and 2022-2023 Ram 3500, the trailer reverse steering control module on 2021 Ram 1500, and security gateway software on 2022-2025 ProMaster. A VIN check identifies which applies.

Do the notices say I should stop driving until the camera is repaired?

The notices in this group do not tell owners to stop driving. They say a rearview image that does not display reduces the driver's rear view and increases the risk of a crash, and nearly all of them say the dealer repair is free of charge.

Can this recall be handled over the air instead of at a dealer?

Two notices mention it. Recall 66B/79B (2022 Ram 3500, 2022-2023 ProMaster) says dealers will update the radio software or the software will be over-the-air, and the 2019-2020 display software recall offered an optional over-the-air update. The rest are dealer repairs.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
