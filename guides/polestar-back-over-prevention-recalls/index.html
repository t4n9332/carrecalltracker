Why does the Polestar rearview camera sometimes fail to display?

3 NHTSA recalls · Polestar · Back Over Prevention · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-09-15

All three of these Polestar recalls come back to the same condition: the rearview camera image may not display when the vehicle is placed in reverse. Two of them cover the same span of Polestar 2 model years, 2021 through 2025 — RP1056, which states these vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility," and RP1069. The third, RP1057, covers the 2025 Polestar 3 and also cites FMVSS 111. Every recall in this group is addressed with software rather than hardware. The Polestar 2 is the most affected model, appearing in two of the three, while the Polestar 3 appears once. Owner letters for the group run from June 2025 to June 2026.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The rearview camera image may not display when the vehicle is placed in reverse (2021-2025 Polestar 2, RP1056 and RP1069; 2025 Polestar 3, RP1057).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. RP1056, covering 2021-2025 Polestar 2 vehicles, says a rearview camera image that does not display reduces the driver's view of what is behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. RP1069 says the same for 2021-2025 Polestar 2 vehicles, and RP1057 says the same for the 2025 Polestar 3. Beyond describing that risk, the notices give owners no interim instruction while they wait for the software update.

What the free repair involves

All three recalls are fixed with software, free of charge. For the 2021-2025 Polestar 2 recalls RP1056 and RP1069, dealers update the vehicle software. For the 2025 Polestar 3 recall RP1057, the software is updated either by a dealer or through an over-the-air update, also free of charge. Owners do not pay for any of these remedies.

Common questions

Will the camera fix come over the air, or do I need a dealer visit?

For the 2025 Polestar 3 recall RP1057, the notice says the software will be updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update, free of charge. The two Polestar 2 recalls, RP1056 and RP1069, say dealers will update the vehicle software.

My Polestar 2 camera works today. Is my car still included?

The notices say the rearview camera image may not display when the vehicle is placed in reverse; they do not say how often. If your VIN is covered by RP1056 or RP1069, the free software update still applies to it.

What safety standard do these Polestar camera recalls cite?

RP1056 and RP1057 both state the vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility." The RP1069 notice describes the same condition, a rearview camera image that may not display in reverse, without citing that standard.
