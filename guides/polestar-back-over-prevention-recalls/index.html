Why doesn't the Polestar backup camera show anything in reverse?

3 NHTSA recalls · Polestar · Back Over Prevention · model years 2021–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-09-15

Two separate Polestar 2 recalls, filed in 2025 for model years 2021 through 2025, describe the same problem: the rearview camera image may not display when the vehicle is shifted into reverse. A third recall covers the 2025 Polestar 3 for an identical failure — the camera image may not display in reverse — but is fixed differently, through a dealer or over-the-air update rather than a dealer-only update. All three recalls exist because these vehicles fail to meet the federal rear-visibility standard that requires a working backup camera image. No mechanical cause is given in these notices; the fix in every case is a software update.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rearview camera image fails to display in reverse (Polestar 2)
  • Back-up camera picture may not appear (Polestar 2, second recall)
  • Rearview camera image fails to display in reverse (Polestar 3)
  • No other warning sign is described in these notices

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the 2021-2025 Polestar 2 rearview-camera recall, the 2021-2025 Polestar 2 back-up-camera recall, and the 2025 Polestar 3 rearview-camera recall, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice only explains that the camera image may not display when reversing and that the vehicles do not meet the federal rear-visibility standard; none direct owners to park the vehicle outside or avoid driving it while waiting for the software update.

What the free repair involves

Across these three recalls, Polestar's fix is a software update: two Polestar 2 recalls are corrected with a dealer software update, and the Polestar 3 recall is corrected with a software update performed either by a dealer or automatically over-the-air. No parts are replaced. All three repairs are done at no cost to the owner.

Common questions

Is my Polestar 2 backup camera issue covered under one of these recalls?

If your 2021-2025 Polestar 2's rearview camera image doesn't display in reverse, it's likely covered by RP1069 or RP1056. Vehicles previously repaired under an earlier related recall (24V-477) may need a further check under RP1056.

Does the Polestar 3 camera recall require a dealer visit?

Not necessarily. Polestar says the fix for the 2025 Polestar 3 rearview-camera recall (RP1057) can be applied by a dealer or delivered automatically as an over-the-air software update, unlike the Polestar 2 recalls, which require a dealer visit.

Will I be charged for the camera software update?

No. Polestar's notices for all three of these recalls state the software update is performed free of charge, whether it's done at a dealer or, for the Polestar 3, through an over-the-air update.
