Subaru engine recalls: PCV valves, coolant leaks and stalling risks

4 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 26, 2023

The recurring item here is the aluminum Positive Crankcase Ventilation valve. Two recalls describe it coming apart and letting the separated components enter the engine, resulting in a loss of power while driving: one covers 2017-2018 Impreza and 2018 Crosstrek vehicles equipped with that valve, the other covers 2019 Crosstrek, Forester and Ascent. The remaining two are separate problems. On 2022 Outback vehicles the engine wiring harness may be damaged, leaving a loose electrical connection to the ECU and allowing an engine stall. On 2024 Forester vehicles the engine water pipe may have loose attachment bolts, letting the pipe lose its seal and leak coolant onto a hot exhaust pipe — the only fire consequence in this group. Loss of power or a stall is the stated consequence in three of the four.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. Three describe an unexpected loss of power or an engine stall while driving — the two PCV valve recalls and the 2022 Outback wiring harness recall — and say that increases the risk of a crash. The 2024 Forester notice says leaking coolant can contact a hot exhaust pipe, increasing the risk of a fire, and adds that those vehicles had not been delivered to dealers or sold to owners, so no owner notification letters will be sent.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace the PCV valve on both PCV recalls; if it has separated and the pieces cannot be found, the engine is replaced on the 2017-2018 Impreza and 2018 Crosstrek recall and the short block engine on the 2019 recall. The 2022 Outback gets a new engine wiring harness and ECU. That work is free of charge. On the 2024 Forester, distributor facilities tighten the water pipe bolts.

Common questions

How do I know whether my Impreza has the aluminum PCV valve?

The 2019 notice covers 2017-2018 Impreza and 2018 Crosstrek vehicles equipped with an aluminum PCV valve. It does not describe a way for an owner to tell; a VIN check on NHTSA's site, and the dealer inspection the recall calls for, identify affected vehicles.

What happens if the separated PCV valve pieces are never found?

Both PCV notices cover that. On the 2017-2018 Impreza and 2018 Crosstrek recall the engine is replaced free of charge; on the 2019 Crosstrek, Forester and Ascent recall the short block engine is replaced free of charge.

Do I need to book an appointment for the 2024 Forester coolant recall?

The notice says those vehicles had not been delivered to dealers or sold to owners, so no owner notification letters will be sent, and that Subaru's independent distributor processing facilities will tighten the water pipe attachment bolts. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614.
