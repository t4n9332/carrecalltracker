Why do Subaru engines lose power or leak coolant?

4 NHTSA recalls · Subaru · Engine And Engine Cooling · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2023-10-26

These four Subaru engine and cooling recalls cover 2017-2024 models and split into three separate defects. Two recalls describe the same aluminum PCV valve coming apart and entering the engine: one for 2017-2018 Impreza and 2018 Crosstrek, the other for 2019 Ascent, Crosstrek, and Forester, both risking a loss of engine power while driving. A third recall covers 2024 Forester vehicles with loose water pipe bolts that can leak coolant onto the hot exhaust, raising a fire risk. A fourth, unrelated recall covers 2022 Outback vehicles with a damaged engine wiring harness that can loosen its connection to the ECU and stall the engine.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Sudden loss of engine power while driving
  • Coolant leaking from the engine water pipe
  • Engine stalling while driving
  • The notices describe no dashboard warning before these defects occur

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls instructs owners to stop driving or park away from structures. The notices state only that the defects increase the risk of a crash or, for the Forester coolant leak, a fire: loss of engine power from the two PCV valve recalls, coolant leaking near the hot exhaust on the 2024 Forester, and a stall from a loose ECU connection on the 2022 Outback. For all four, owners are not told to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers inspect and, if necessary, replace the PCV valve — replacing the whole engine if separated pieces can't be located — for the two power-loss recalls. For the Forester coolant recall, technicians tighten the water pipe attachment bolts. For the Outback recall, dealers replace the damaged engine wiring harness and ECU. All repairs are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why did my Subaru lose power while driving?

Two recalls found the aluminum PCV valve can come apart and its pieces enter the engine — one covering 2017-2018 Impreza and 2018 Crosstrek, the other 2019 Ascent, Crosstrek, and Forester — both risking a loss of engine power.

Why is my 2024 Forester leaking coolant?

The recall found the engine water pipe may have loose attachment bolts, letting the pipe lose its seal and leak coolant, which can contact the hot exhaust pipe and raise a fire risk.

Why did my 2022 Outback stall?

The recall found the engine wiring harness on certain 2022 Outback vehicles may be damaged, causing a loose electrical connection to the engine control unit, which can result in the engine stalling while driving.
