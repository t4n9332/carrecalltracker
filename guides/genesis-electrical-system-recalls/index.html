Why Do So Many Genesis Models Have Electrical Recalls?

8 NHTSA recalls · Genesis · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-01-16

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Genesis's electrical recalls fall into three repeating patterns. Instrument panel display failures, caused by software errors, appear three times: G90 and GV60 (2023-2025), G80 and GV80 (2025), and an earlier G80, GV80, and GV70 recall (2023-2025) - each can blank the speedometer or warning lights. Charging unit (ICCU) failures affecting the GV60 recur across two related 2024 recalls, which can cut 12-volt charging and cause a loss of drive power. Standalone issues include a 2018-2019 G70, G80, and G90 starter that can short and cause an engine fire; a 2024 GV70 transmission harness short from water intrusion that can shift the vehicle from Park to Neutral; and a 2023-2025 G90 radar that can trigger sudden unintended braking.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Instrument panel display that goes blank, hiding the speedometer or warning lights
  • Loss of drive power from a failed charging unit
  • Vehicle unexpectedly shifting from Park to Neutral on its own
  • Sudden, unintended braking while driving
  • The starter fire recall describes no warning sign before an engine compartment fire

Is it safe to keep driving?

Of these eight recalls, one — covering the 2018 G80, 2018 G90, and 2019 G70, G80, and G90 — tells owners to park outside and away from structures until repaired, due to fire risk. The GV70 Park-to-Neutral recall advises owners to turn off the ignition and use the parking brake after parking. The G90 radar recall advises against using Highway Drive Assist, and the G80/GV80 display recall advises disabling HD radio, until each repair is done. The remaining recalls in this group do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Repairs vary by recall: dealers install a relay kit for the starter, inspect and replace the ICCU and its fuse plus update software for charging failures, repair or replace the transmission control harness for the GV70, update the instrument display software (sometimes over-the-air) for the display recalls, and replace the front bumper beam for the radar recall. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why is Genesis recalling the G70, G80, and G90 for a fire risk?

Water can enter the starter solenoid on certain 2018 G80, 2018 G90, and 2019 G70, G80, and G90 vehicles and cause an electrical short, which can lead to an engine compartment fire. Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the repair is done.

Why does my Genesis instrument panel display go blank?

Several Genesis recalls (G90 and GV60, G80 and GV80, and G80/GV80/GV70) cite a software error that can cause the instrument panel display to fail, hiding the speedometer or warning lights. Dealers or an over-the-air update fix the software free of charge.

What is wrong with the Genesis GV60 charging unit?

The Integrated Charging Control Unit (ICCU) on certain GV60 and related Hyundai Ioniq 5 and 6 vehicles may become damaged and stop charging the 12-volt battery, which can cause a loss of drive power. Dealers inspect and replace the ICCU and fuse, free of charge.
