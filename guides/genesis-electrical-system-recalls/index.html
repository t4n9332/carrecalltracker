Why does Genesis have electrical recalls for fires, charging and displays?

8 NHTSA recalls · Genesis · Electrical System · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 16, 2026

⚠ 1 carries a park-outside (fire risk) advisory.

Three problems repeat across these eight Genesis electrical recalls. Instrument panel display software is the most common: three recalls cover 2023-2025 G90 and GV60; 2023-2024 G80 and GV80 with the 2025 GV70; and 2025-2026 G80 and GV80 with 2026 GV60, GV70 and G80 "Electrified" models. Two more cover the Integrated Charging Control Unit on the Genesis GV60, GV70 "Electrified" and G80 "Electrified", where damage stops the 12-volt battery charging and can cause a loss of drive power. Water intrusion drives two others: the starter solenoid on 2017-2019 G80 and G90 and the 2019 G70, and the 2024 GV70 transmission control harness. The eighth is a Highway Drive Assist radar recall on 2023-2026 G90 vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The instrument panel display fails and does not show information such as the speedometer or warning lights (the G90 and GV60 display recall, and the G80, GV80 and GV70 display recalls).
  • Sudden, unintended braking while driving (2023-2026 G90 Highway Drive Assist radar recall).
  • The transmission shifts unexpectedly from Park to Neutral and the vehicle can roll away (2024 GV70 transmission control harness recall).
  • A loss of drive power after the charging unit stops charging the 12-volt battery (GV60, GV70 "Electrified" and G80 "Electrified" ICCU recalls).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

One recall carries a parking instruction: the starter solenoid fire recall on 2017-2019 G80 and G90 and the 2019 G70 tells owners to park outside and away from structures until the repair is complete. No notice in this group tells owners to stop driving. Three notices add an instruction of their own — the 2024 GV70 harness recall advises using the emergency parking brake and turning the ignition off after parking, the G90 radar recall advises not using Highway Drive Assist until the remedy, and the 2025-2026 G80 and GV80 display recall advises disabling HD radio until then.

What the free repair involves

Remedies differ by recall and all are free. For the starter fire recall dealers install a remedy relay kit in the engine junction box. For the display recalls dealers update software, over the air or at the dealer. ICCU recalls inspect and replace the charging unit and its fuse and update its software. The GV70 harness is repaired or replaced, and the G90 radar recall replaces the front bumper beam.

Common questions

My Genesis instrument display went blank. Which recall covers that?

Three recalls in this group cover an instrument panel display that may fail because of a software error: one for 2023-2025 G90 and GV60, one for 2023-2024 G80 and GV80 with the 2025 GV70, and one for 2025-2026 G80 and GV80.

Do I have to park my Genesis outside?

The starter solenoid fire recall tells owners of 2017-2019 G80 and G90 and the 2019 G70 to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. The other notices in this group give no parking instruction.

My GV60 already had the ICCU recall done. Is that the end of it?

The March 2024 ICCU notice says that recall is replaced by NHTSA recall number 24V-868, and that vehicles already repaired under it need the new remedy completed. Dealers inspect and replace the ICCU and its fuse as necessary and update the ICCU software.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
