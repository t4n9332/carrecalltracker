Why do Porsche Taycan batteries and Panamera electrics keep getting recalled?

13 NHTSA recalls · Porsche · Electrical System · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 1, 2024

Most of Porsche's electrical recalls centre on the Taycan's high-voltage battery: five recalls between December 2023 and October 2024 cover short circuits within the battery modules on 2020-2024 Taycan, Taycan 4S, Turbo, Turbo S, GTS and Cross Turismo variants. A 2023 recall covers 2023 Taycan models whose high-voltage battery may have insufficient sealant, allowing liquid to accumulate. Charging hardware appears once: the 220V/240V compact charging cable on Cayenne E-Hybrid, Panamera 4S E-Hybrid and Taycan models may overheat at the 100% charge setting. Older recalls include a climate-control coolant pump short on 2017-2021 Panamera, incorrect soldering in 2018 Panamera control units, a 2018 Macan control unit that may fail, and two recalls where the instrument cluster on 2017-2018 Panamera and 2019-2020 Cayenne may not warn of worn brake pads.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2019 Panamera and Cayenne brake-wear recalls state the instrument cluster may not provide a visual warning when the brake pads are worn out.
  • The 2018 Macan control unit recall states the failure may prevent driver display alerts.
  • The 2018 Panamera soldering recall describes intermittent failure of various systems, possibly affecting driveability.
  • The charging cable recall states the outlet or charging cable may overheat when the 220V/240V plug is used at the 100% charge setting.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these notices tells owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside. Two of the March 2024 Taycan battery recalls and the October 2024 Taycan battery recall advise owners to charge only to a maximum of 80 percent until the repair is completed, and a second October 2024 recall says Porsche will contact owners as necessary with that same charging advice. The charging cable notice advises owners not to use the 220V/240V compact cable and to use the 110V home cable or public charging stations. The other notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the Panamera external coolant pump and inspect the plug-in connection; test the Taycan battery for leakage and replace it as necessary; replace high-voltage battery modules; supply a new 220V/240V charging cable with a temperature sensor; replace Macan and Panamera control units; and update or reprogram the instrument cluster software, each free of charge. One 2024 Taycan recall names advanced diagnostic software as its final remedy.

Common questions

Can I keep charging my Taycan the way I normally do?

Several of the battery short-circuit notices advise charging only to a maximum of 80 percent until the repair is completed. The charging cable recall advises owners not to use the 220V/240V compact cable and to use the 110V cable or public charging stations.

What does Porsche do about the Taycan high-voltage battery?

The notices describe dealers testing the battery for leakage, inspecting it, analyzing battery data and replacing affected modules or the battery itself, free of charge. One October 2024 recall states the final remedy is advanced diagnostic software installed by dealers.

Will my Panamera warn me when the brake pads are worn?

The two brake-wear recalls state the instrument cluster may not provide a visual warning when the brake pads are worn out. Dealers update or reprogram the instrument cluster free of charge. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving.
