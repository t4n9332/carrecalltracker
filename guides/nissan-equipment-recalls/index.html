Nissan label and manual recalls: weight ratings, wheels and defroster instructions

4 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Equipment · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jul 15, 2026

Three of these four recalls come down to a label that states the wrong information. 2018 Titan trucks did not receive a Load Carrying Capacity modification label after installed accessories reduced their load carrying capacity. 2025 Kicks certification labels may have been printed with incorrect Gross Vehicle Weight Rating and Gross Axle Weight Rating values, and SR models with the optional 19-inch wheel package may show an incorrect wheel size. 2020-2025 Armada and 2018-2025 Infiniti QX80 vehicles with 22-inch wheels carry an incorrect Gross Axle Weight Rating. The outlier is the 2018-2021 LEAF, where the Owner's Manual defroster instructions are incorrect and may result in reduced defroster performance.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Reduced defroster performance under specific conditions, which can limit visibility out of the windshield (2018-2021 LEAF Owner's Manual defroster recall)

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tell owners to stop driving, and none tell owners to park the vehicle outside. The three label recalls state that an overloaded vehicle can increase the risk of a crash, and the 2025 Kicks notice adds that incorrect wheel and tire information may lead to tires that are inflated improperly, which can also increase the risk of a crash. The LEAF notice states that reduced defroster performance can limit visibility out of the windshield. None of the four notices give owners an interim instruction.

What the free repair involves

These remedies are paperwork rather than parts. For the 2018 Titan, Nissan provides a new modification label with installation instructions. For the 2025 Kicks and for the Armada and QX80, dealers install new labels and replacement labels are also mailed to owners. For the LEAF, Nissan mails an addendum with updated instructions on how to operate the defroster. All free of charge.

Common questions

Is a wrong label really a safety recall?

These are noncompliance recalls under federal certification and tire selection standards. The notices say an incorrect or missing capacity label can allow the vehicle to be overloaded, which can increase the risk of a crash, so corrected labels are provided free of charge.

Do I need a dealer visit for the LEAF defroster recall?

The notice says Nissan will mail an addendum with updated instructions on how to operate the defroster, free of charge. It does not describe a dealer repair for this recall, and it does not tell owners to stop driving.

How do I find the correct rating for my Armada or QX80?

The notice says the Gross Axle Weight Rating on the certification label is incorrect on vehicles equipped with 22-inch wheels, and that dealers will install new labels, with replacement labels also mailed to owners. The notice does not state the corrected figures.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
