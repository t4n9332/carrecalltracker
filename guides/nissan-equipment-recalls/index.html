Why Are Nissan Vehicles Recalled Over Labels and Manuals?

4 NHTSA recalls · Nissan · Equipment · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-07-15

Three of the four recalls in this group involve incorrect labels rather than mechanical defects. The 2025 Kicks and the 2026 Armada and Infiniti QX80 recalls both involve certification labels with the wrong Gross Vehicle or Axle Weight Rating, which can let owners overload the vehicle; the Kicks recall also affects some SR trim wheel-size labels. The 2018 Titan recall covers trucks missing a required load-capacity label after accessories were installed. The fourth recall is different: 2018-2021 LEAF owner's manuals give incorrect defroster operating instructions, which can reduce defroster performance under some conditions and does not comply with federal windshield defrosting standards.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Reduced defroster performance in certain conditions (LEAF manual recall)
  • No dashboard warning — these are labeling and documentation errors, not mechanical faults
  • Incorrect weight rating or wheel-size information printed on the vehicle's certification label

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the label recalls covering the Titan, Kicks, and Armada/QX80, the notices say only that an overloaded vehicle, or tires inflated based on incorrect wheel information, increases the risk of a crash. For the LEAF recall, the notice says reduced defroster performance can limit visibility through the windshield and increase crash risk. In each case, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

For the Titan, Kicks, and Armada/QX80 recalls, dealers install correct certification or wheel-size labels, and in some cases Nissan mails replacement labels directly to owners. For the LEAF recall, Nissan mails an addendum with corrected defroster operating instructions. No parts are replaced in these repairs, and all corrections are free of charge.

Common questions

Do I need to bring my Nissan in for a mechanical repair for these recalls?

No. Three of the four recalls in this group — covering the Titan, Kicks, and Armada/QX80 — involve replacing incorrect labels, not repairing parts. The LEAF recall involves mailing owners corrected defroster instructions. All corrections are free.

Why does an incorrect weight rating label matter?

The label tells owners how much weight the vehicle can safely carry. If the Gross Vehicle or Axle Weight Rating printed on the label is wrong, as in the Kicks and Armada/QX80 recalls, an owner could unknowingly overload the vehicle, which the notices say increases crash risk.

What's wrong with the Nissan LEAF's defroster instructions?

Nissan says the Owner's Manual for 2018-2021 LEAF vehicles gives incorrect defroster operating instructions, which can result in reduced defroster performance under specific conditions. Nissan is mailing owners an addendum with the correct instructions rather than repairing any part.
