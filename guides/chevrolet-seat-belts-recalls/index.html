Chevrolet Seat Belt Recalls Cover Loose Attachments and Damaged Belts

15 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 16, 2025

The supplied Chevrolet notices repeatedly identify seat belt attachment problems: unsecured brackets, incorrect bolts, loose fasteners, and improperly formed buckle rivets. Silverado trucks, Tahoe, and Suburban recur most often, with several notices involving 2019-2021 Silverado models and 2021 Tahoe and Suburban vehicles. The 2024 Silverado EV appears in separate notices for front buckle bolts, a second-row center belt bezel, and a rear center belt attachment bolt. Other defects include crash-locking problems in the 2020 Corvette, improperly secured rear retractors in 2016-2021 Malibu vehicles, and carpet ignition when pretensioners deploy in specified 2019-2020 Silverado trucks. These defects involve different seating positions and remedies.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2021-03-25 Chevrolet Tahoe Center Seat Belt Latch Is Blocked: the second-row center latch may be inaccessible beneath the trim bezel, leaving the passenger unable to buckle.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The supplied notices contain no stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The February 8, 2024 Silverado EV center seat belt recall specifically tells owners not to allow passengers in the second-row center seat until remedied. That seating restriction belongs to that recall. For the other listed recalls, including the Silverado pretensioner fire risk, Corvette retractor, Tahoe latch, Malibu retractor, and Silverado EV attachment-bolt notices, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Repairs are free under the supplied notices. For the pretensioner fire recall, dealers close the bracket opening; for the Corvette recall, they replace the driver's retractor. The Tahoe latch recall requires repositioning the latch plate. The Silverado EV bezel recall requires bezel replacement, while its front buckle-bolt recall requires tightening the attachment bolts.

Common questions

Which Silverado EV seat belt recall restricts passenger seating?

The February 8, 2024 recall for certain 2024 Silverado EV vehicles tells owners to keep passengers out of the second-row center seat until remedied. An incorrect bezel may damage or tear that seat belt during a crash.

What seat belt problems affect Tahoe and Suburban third rows?

The March 2021 notice covers third-row outboard belts that may be entrapped or misrouted in certain 2021 Tahoe and Suburban vehicles. The August 2022 notice covers improperly formed buckle rivets in certain 2021-2022 models with third-row seats.

Why is a Silverado seat belt recall described as a fire risk?

The November 2019 notice describes hot gas from deploying front pretensioners potentially igniting carpet through a bracket opening. It covers specified carpet-equipped Silverado trucks. Dealers close that opening free of charge; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.
