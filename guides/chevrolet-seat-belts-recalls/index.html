Chevrolet seat belt recalls: loose bolts, buckles and retractor problems

15 NHTSA recalls · Chevrolet · Seat Belts · model years 2018–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jan 16, 2025

Fasteners are the recurring story. Loose, missing or wrong hardware shows up in five of these recalls: front-row center bracket attachments on 2019-2021 Silverado 1500 and 2021 Tahoe and Suburban, incorrect bolts on the 2020 Bolt EV and a long list of 2021 models, loose rear retractor fasteners on 2016-2021 Malibu, untightened front buckle bolts on the 2024 Silverado EV, and an untightened rear center bolt on the same truck. Full-size SUVs account for the third-row problems, with damaged or misrouted belts and improperly formed buckle rivets on 2021 and 2021-2022 Tahoe and Suburban. Two recalls involve the belts themselves: pretensioners that may ignite carpet in 2019-2020 Silverado 1500, and a Corvette retractor that may not lock.

Warning signs to watch for

  • 2021 Tahoe second-row center recall: the notice says the seat belt latch may be trapped beneath the trim bezel and inaccessible, leaving the second-row center passenger unable to wear a seat belt.
  • The other notices in this group do not describe a warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these seat belt notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. One carries a seating instruction: the 2024 Silverado EV second-row center bezel recall says not to allow passengers to sit in the second-row center seat until the vehicle is remedied. That instruction applies to that recall only. For the rest, including the pretensioner carpet fire recall on 2019-2020 Silverado 1500 and the retractor, bracket, bolt, rivet and third-row belt recalls, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

The work is mostly inspect-and-correct hardware, free of charge. Dealers tighten or replace bolts and fasteners, reassemble front-row center bracket attachments, inspect third-row belts and replace damaged ones, inspect and replace third-row buckle assemblies as necessary, replace the Corvette driver retractor and both front retractors on the Silverado HD trucks, reposition the Tahoe center latch plate, and close off the opening in the pretensioner bracket.

Common questions

Can a dealer tell whether my truck actually has the wrong seat belt bolts?

The incorrect bolt recall covers the 2020 Bolt EV and many 2021 Chevrolet models, and the notice says dealers will replace the suspect bolts free of charge. Owners can confirm coverage through Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020.

What does the Silverado EV center seat belt notice tell owners to do in the meantime?

It says not to allow passengers to sit in the second-row center seat until the vehicle is remedied, because an incorrect bezel may damage or tear the seat belt in a crash. Dealers replace the bezel free of charge.

Why is a seat belt recall listed as a fire risk?

In the 2019-2020 Silverado 1500 recall, the notice says hot gas may vent through an opening in the front pretensioner bracket when the pretensioner deploys, possibly igniting the carpet. Dealers close off that opening free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
