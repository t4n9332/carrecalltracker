Why do Lincoln brake lines and hoses keep leaking fluid?

7 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-12-01

Brake recalls for Lincoln vehicles center on fluid leaks that can extend stopping distance. The MKC, MKZ, and MKX each have a recall for a front or rear brake hose that can rupture or chafe and leak fluid (2018, 2013-2018, and 2016-2018 model years). The Navigator and Expedition have four separate brake recalls: a 2017-2018 master cylinder that can leak into the brake booster, a 2022-2024 brake line that can be damaged by contact with the air cleaner outlet pipe, and two 2025 recalls for an Electronic Brake Booster module that can malfunction and cause a loss of power brake assist.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Brake fluid leak visible near the front or rear wheels
  • Increased brake pedal travel
  • Reduced brake function or longer stopping distance
  • Sudden loss of power brake assist while driving or using a driver-assist feature

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these seven recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, despite involving brake fluid leaks and loss of power brake assist. The notices for the hose and master-cylinder recalls say a leak can lengthen stopping distance and increase the risk of a crash. The two Electronic Brake Booster recalls say an unexpected loss of power brake assist can extend stopping distance, and that a loss of assist while a driver-assist feature is active can mean the vehicle doesn't brake as expected. None instruct owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace or inspect brake hoses on the MKC, MKZ, and MKX; replace the master cylinder and brake booster as needed on early Navigator and Expedition models; and replace the brake line or air cleaner outlet pipe on 2022-2024 models. For the Electronic Brake Booster module, one 2025 recall updates software over-the-air or at a dealer, while a second replaces the module. All repairs are free.

Common questions

Why does my Lincoln Navigator keep getting recalled for brakes?

The Navigator and Expedition have had four separate brake recalls: a leaking master cylinder, a brake line that can be damaged by the air cleaner outlet pipe, and two recalls for an Electronic Brake Booster module that can cause a loss of power brake assist.

What does a brake fluid leak mean for stopping distance?

The notices say a brake fluid leak, or in the MKZ's case increased brake pedal travel, can lengthen the distance needed to stop the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. None of these recalls tell owners to stop driving.

Will the Electronic Brake Booster recall be fixed with a software update?

One 2025 recall updates the EBB module software over-the-air or at a dealer, free of charge. A separate 2025 recall for the same malfunction instead has dealers replace the electronic brake booster module itself.
