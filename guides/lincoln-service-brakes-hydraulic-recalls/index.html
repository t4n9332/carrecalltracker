Lincoln brake recalls: leaking hoses and lost power brake assist

7 NHTSA recalls · Lincoln · Service Brakes, Hydraulic · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 1, 2025

Fluid leaks dominate this group. Four recalls involve hoses or lines: the 2018 MKC's incorrect front brake hoses that may chafe, the 2018 MKZ's front hoses that may rupture, the 2018 MKX's rear jounce hose that may rupture, and 2022-2024 Navigator front brake lines that may contact the engine air cleaner outlet pipe. The 2018 Navigator has its own leak path — the brake master cylinder may let fluid leak from the front wheel circuit into the brake booster. The 2025 Navigator accounts for the two newest recalls, both for an Electronic Brake Booster module that may malfunction and cause a loss of power brake assist, including while driver assistance features are engaged.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The 2018 MKZ front brake hose recall says a brake fluid leak will increase brake pedal travel and extend the distance needed to stop.
  • The two 2025 Navigator Electronic Brake Booster recalls say the module may malfunction while driving, resulting in a loss of power brake assist, and that with ADAS features engaged the vehicle may not brake as expected.
  • The 2018 Navigator master cylinder recall says a brake fluid leak can cause reduced brake function and extend the distance required to stop.
  • The 2018 MKC and 2018 MKX recalls say a brake fluid leak may lengthen the distance needed to stop; the 2022-2024 Navigator recall says a damaged front brake line may result in a leak and reduced brake function.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

No notice in this group tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. For the 2018 MKX rear jounce hose recall, Ford mailed interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk, with additional letters once the remedy is available, anticipated August 2026. The other six notices set out the consequence — longer stopping distances, reduced or lost brake function, or a loss of power brake assist — and none of them tells owners to stop driving or offers any interim measure.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the MKZ front brake hoses, replace incorrect MKC front hoses, and inspect, replace or adjust the MKX rear jounce hoses. The 2018 Navigator gets a master cylinder, plus the booster if it leaks; the 2022-2024 Navigator a brake line or air cleaner outlet pipe. For the 2025 Navigator, one recall updates EBB software over-the-air or at a dealer, the other replaces the booster. All free of charge.

Common questions

What does the Electronic Brake Booster recall mean for my 2025 Navigator?

Two 2025 notices cover it. Both say the EBB module may malfunction while driving or while an ADAS feature is used, causing a loss of power brake assist. One is fixed by a software update, over-the-air or at a dealer; the other by replacing the booster.

Are the older Lincoln brake hose recalls the same problem?

They are separate. The 2018 MKC has incorrect front hoses that may chafe; the 2018 MKZ has front hoses that may rupture; the 2018 MKX has a rear jounce hose that may rupture. Each notice ties a fluid leak to a longer stopping distance.

The MKX jounce hose remedy is not ready — what do the notices say?

The notice says interim letters went out about the safety risk and that the remedy is anticipated August 2026, with additional letters to follow. It does not tell owners to stop driving and gives no other interim instruction. Dealers will then inspect, replace or adjust the hoses free of charge.
