Which Jeep visibility recalls cover defrosters, wipers and rearview cameras?

4 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Visibility · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Jun 26, 2025

Four Jeep recalls here all end in the same place: the driver cannot see. Two of them are defroster failures on the newest SUVs — Chrysler's 04B covers 2021-2024 Wrangler and 2022-2024 Grand Cherokee, where a hybrid control processor software error can leave the defrosting and defogging system inoperative, while 49C covers 2023 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L, where the blower motor may fail and knock out the same system. The Grand Cherokee L appears again in Y67, a 2021 radio software error that may keep the rearview image from displaying. The outlier is W25, where the windshield wiper arm or arms on 2019-2020 Compass may loosen. Grand Cherokee and software faults dominate the group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall 04B: the notice says the defrosting and defogging system may be inoperative on 2021-2024 Wrangler and 2022-2024 Grand Cherokee.
  • Recall 49C: the notice says a failed blower motor can leave the defrosting and defogging system inoperative on 2023 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L.
  • Recall Y67: the notice says the rearview image may not display on the 2021 Grand Cherokee L.
  • Recall W25: the notice says the wipers may not operate properly on 2019-2020 Compass.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these four notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells them to park the vehicle outside. That is all the notices say on the subject — W25, Y67, 49C and 04B each describe the defect and the crash risk it creates, then leave the instruction at contacting a dealer for the repair. NHTSA's entries carry no interim step, no restriction on when or where to drive, and no temporary measure to use until the remedy is done. Anything beyond that is not in the recall documents.

What the free repair involves

The remedies split three ways. For W25 dealers tighten the wiper nuts. For 49C dealers inspect the blower motor and replace it as necessary. For Y67 FCA US updates the radio software, and for 04B dealers update the hybrid control processor software. Each notice states the work is done free of charge. None of the four is described as an over-the-air update.

Common questions

My Grand Cherokee defroster stopped working — which recall is that?

Two cover it. 49C applies to 2023 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L, where the blower motor may fail. 04B applies to 2022-2024 Grand Cherokee and 2021-2024 Wrangler, where a hybrid control processor software error is named. Your VIN decides which.

Does the rearview camera recall affect every 2021 Grand Cherokee?

Y67 is written for certain 2021 Grand Cherokee L vehicles, not the whole model year, and names a radio software error that may prevent the rearview image from displaying. NHTSA recalls cover specific VIN ranges, so check yours against the recall.

My Compass wipers seem fine. Is W25 still worth doing?

W25 covers certain 2019-2020 Compass where the wiper arm or arms may loosen. The notice describes the condition as something that may happen, not something already visible, and the remedy is dealers tightening the wiper nuts free of charge.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
