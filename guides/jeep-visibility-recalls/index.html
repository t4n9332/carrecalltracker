Jeep Visibility Recalls Involving Windshield Wipers, Defrosters and Cameras

5 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Visibility · model years 2019–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 13, 2026

These Jeep visibility recalls repeatedly involve windshield clearing, with separate defects affecting wipers and defrosting systems. Certain 2019-2020 Compass vehicles have wiper arms that may loosen, while certain 2026 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles have a body control module software issue that may cause wiper failure. Defrosting and defogging problems affect certain 2021-2024 Wrangler and 2022-2024 Grand Cherokee vehicles through HCP software, and certain 2023 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles through blower motor failure. A separate radio software recall concerns missing rearview images on certain 2021 Grand Cherokee L vehicles. Grand Cherokee models appear most frequently in this group.

Warning signs to watch for

  • W25: windshield wipers may not operate properly.
  • 49C: windshield defrosting and defogging may become inoperative.
  • 77D: windshield wipers may fail.
  • Y67: the rearview camera image may not display.
  • 04B: windshield defrosting and defogging may become inoperative.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. W25 does not tell owners to stop driving for the Compass wiper arm problem. Recall 49C does not give that instruction for the Grand Cherokee blower motor problem, nor does 77D for Grand Cherokee wiper failure. Y67 does not tell owners to stop driving for the Grand Cherokee L camera problem. Recall 04B likewise does not tell owners to stop driving for the Wrangler and Grand Cherokee defrosting problem.

What the free repair involves

Repairs are free. W25 tightens wiper nuts; 49C inspects and replaces the blower motor as necessary. Recall 77D updates body control module software, Y67 updates radio software, and 04B updates HCP software. The notices specify these remedies for their respective defects.

Common questions

Which Jeep recalls involve a defroster that stops working?

Recall 04B concerns HCP software on certain 2021-2024 Wrangler and 2022-2024 Grand Cherokee vehicles. Recall 49C concerns blower motor failure on certain 2023 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles. Both notices describe inoperative defrosting and defogging systems.

Are the Compass and Grand Cherokee wiper repairs the same?

W25 specifies tightening wiper nuts on certain 2019-2020 Compass vehicles. Recall 77D specifies updating body control module software on certain 2026 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles. Each notice states that its repair is free of charge.

What repair does the Grand Cherokee L camera notice specify?

Y67 covers certain 2021 Grand Cherokee L vehicles. Its notice identifies a radio software error that may prevent the rearview image from displaying. FCA specifies a free radio software update; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.
