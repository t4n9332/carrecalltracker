Jeep Visibility Recalls Affect Windshield Wipers, Defrosters, and Rearview Cameras

5 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Visibility · model years 2019–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 13, 2026

Windshield wiper and defrosting failures repeat across these Jeep visibility recalls, with Grand Cherokee models appearing most often. The 2019-2020 Compass recall concerns wiper arms that may loosen. For 2026 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles, a body control module software issue may instead cause wiper failure. Defrosting problems have separate causes: blower motor failure in certain 2023 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles, and hybrid control processor software in certain 2021-2024 Wranglers and 2022-2024 Grand Cherokees. A separate radio software error affects the rearview camera image in certain 2021 Grand Cherokee L vehicles. These defects can reduce the driver's view ahead or behind.

Warning signs to watch for

  • W25: Windshield wipers may not operate properly.
  • 49C: Windshield defrosting and defogging may not work.
  • 77D: Windshield wipers may fail.
  • Y67: The rearview camera image may not display.
  • 04B: Windshield defrosting and defogging may not work.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the five listed Jeep visibility notices tells owners to stop driving or park outside. W25 describes reduced visibility from wipers that may not operate properly; 77D describes windshield wiper failure. The 49C and 04B notices describe inoperative defrosting and defogging systems, while Y67 describes a missing rearview camera image. Each notice identifies an increased crash risk associated with reduced visibility. The notices do not tell owners to stop driving while awaiting their respective repairs.

What the free repair involves

The listed remedies are free of charge. Dealers tighten wiper nuts for W25, inspect and replace the blower motor as necessary for 49C, and update body control module software for 77D. Y67 provides a radio software update. For 04B, dealers update the hybrid control processor software.

Common questions

Why do Grand Cherokee defroster recalls have different repairs?

Recall 49C concerns blower motor failure in certain 2023 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles, requiring inspection and replacement as necessary. Recall 04B concerns hybrid control processor software in certain 2022-2024 Grand Cherokees and specifies a software update.

Which Jeep wiper recalls involve loose hardware or software?

W25 concerns wiper arms that may loosen on certain 2019-2020 Compass vehicles; dealers tighten the wiper nuts. Recall 77D concerns body control module software on certain 2026 Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles; dealers update that software.

Which Grand Cherokee has the rearview camera software recall?

The Y67 notice identifies certain 2021 Grand Cherokee L vehicles. A radio software error may prevent the rearview image from displaying, reducing the driver's rear view. The remedy is a radio software update provided free of charge.
