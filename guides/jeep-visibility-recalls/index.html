Which Jeep recalls affect wipers, defrosters, or the rearview camera?

4 NHTSA recalls · Jeep · Visibility · model years 2019–2024 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-06-26

Visibility recalls in this group involve three different systems rather than one repeated defect. Windshield wipers on the 2019-2020 Compass and Ram 1500 can loosen and stop clearing the windshield properly. Two separate defroster issues affect Jeep SUVs: a failing blower motor on the 2023 Grand Cherokee, and a software error in the 2021-2024 Wrangler and 2022-2024 Grand Cherokee that can disable the windshield defroster and defogger. A fourth recall covers a 2021 Grand Cherokee L radio software error that can prevent the rearview camera image from displaying. None of these four recalls involve a mechanical steering or structural defect — each is tied to visibility equipment specifically.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Wipers that don't clear the windshield properly in certain weather (2019-2020 Compass and Ram 1500)
  • A defroster or defogger that doesn't clear the windshield (2023 Grand Cherokee blower motor; 2021-2024 Wrangler and 2022-2024 Grand Cherokee software)
  • A rearview camera image that doesn't display (2021 Grand Cherokee L)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these four visibility recalls tell owners to stop driving or park outside. That includes the 2019-2020 Compass and Ram 1500 wiper recall, the 2023 Grand Cherokee blower motor recall, the 2021 Grand Cherokee L rearview camera recall, and the 2021-2024 Wrangler and Grand Cherokee defroster software recall. The notices say reduced visibility increases crash risk, but none instruct owners to stop driving or park the vehicle outside while awaiting repair.

What the free repair involves

Dealers tighten the wiper arm nuts, replace the blower motor as necessary, or update software — the radio software for the rearview camera and the hybrid control processor software for the defroster. All of this work is done free of charge across all four recalls.

Common questions

Can a windshield wiper recall really affect visibility?

Yes — Chrysler says the wiper arm or arms on affected 2019-2020 Compass and Ram 1500 vehicles may loosen, which can keep the wipers from clearing the windshield properly in certain weather.

Is a broken defroster covered by these Jeep recalls?

Two separate recalls cover it: a failing blower motor on the 2023 Grand Cherokee, and a software error on the 2021-2024 Wrangler and 2022-2024 Grand Cherokee that can disable the windshield defroster and defogger.

Why would a rearview camera recall be a safety issue?

On affected 2021 Grand Cherokee L vehicles, a radio software error can prevent the rearview camera image from displaying, reducing the driver's view behind the vehicle and increasing crash risk.
