What do Volvo truck equipment and weight label recalls cover?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Equipment · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-11-20

All three equipment recalls here are Volvo Trucks vehicles, and two of them are paperwork rather than hardware. The 2020 recall covers 2018-2021 VNL and VNR buses whose Federal Certification Label states a rear drive axle Gross Axle Weight Rating higher than the wheel hubs are rated for. The 2023 recall covers 2024 VN and VHD trucks equipped with size 295/75 or 385/65 Continental front tires, where the GAWR on the certification label is incorrect; both fail 49 CFR Part 567. The newest, from November 2025, is physical: load lock clamps on the back of the cab of 2024-2026 VNL (4) trucks may loosen, leaving the load lock bars unsecured.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall RVXX2512: the load lock clamps on the back of the cab may loosen and cause the load lock bars to become unsecured.
  • Recalls RVXX2002 and RVXX2314: the Gross Axle Weight Rating shown on the certification label is incorrect; the notices describe no other sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these three notices tells owners to stop driving, and none tells owners to park outside. The 2020 label recall (RVXX2002) says that if the rear axles are overloaded the wheel hubs may fail prematurely over time, and the 2023 label recall (RVXX2314) says an overloaded vehicle can increase the risk of a crash, but neither notice gives owners an instruction to follow before the repair. The load lock clamp recall (RVXX2512) says an unsecured load lock bar can create a road hazard; it does not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

Two of the three remedies are label work: for RVXX2002 Volvo sends corrected replacement labels, and for RVXX2314 dealers remove and replace the label. For RVXX2512 dealers replace the load lock clamps. All three notices say the remedy is free of charge, and owner notification has gone out for each.

Common questions

Is a wrong weight label really a safety recall?

Both label recalls are noncompliances with 49 CFR Part 567. RVXX2002 says an overloaded rear axle can make wheel hubs fail prematurely, and RVXX2314 says an overloaded vehicle can increase the risk of a crash. Corrected labels are supplied free of charge.

Which trucks have the load lock clamp problem?

The November 2025 recall covers certain 2024-2026 VNL (4) vehicles. The notice says the load lock clamps on the back of the cab may loosen and leave the bars unsecured, and that dealers will replace the clamps free of charge. Owner letters were mailed December 18, 2025.

What does the corrected label change on my truck?

The notices say only that the Gross Axle Weight Rating on the certification label is incorrect and that the label will be replaced. RVXX2002 concerns the rear drive axles on 2018-2021 VNL and VNR buses; RVXX2314 concerns the front axle on 2024 VN and VHD trucks.
