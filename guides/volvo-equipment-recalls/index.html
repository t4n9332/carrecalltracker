Why do Volvo trucks have cargo and weight-label recalls?

3 NHTSA recalls · Volvo · Equipment · model years 2018–2026 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2025-11-20

All three Equipment recalls in this group involve Volvo Trucks, not Volvo cars. Two are certification-label errors: 2018-2021 VNL and VNR trucks were recalled because the Gross Axle Weight Rating on the rear axle exceeds the wheel hubs' rating, and 2024 VN and VHD trucks with certain 295/75 or 385/65 Continental front tires carry a GAWR that lets them be overloaded. The third recall covers 2024-2026 VNL trucks whose load lock clamps on the back of the cab can loosen, letting load lock bars come unsecured. This group's pattern is cargo-securement hardware and mislabeled weight ratings, not mechanical failure.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Load lock bars that become loose or unsecured in the cargo area (2024-2026 VNL)
  • No physical warning sign for the incorrect rear-axle weight-rating labels — the certification label itself is wrong (2018-2021 VNL/VNR)
  • No physical warning sign for the 2024 VN/VHD front-axle weight-label error either; it only matters if the truck is loaded to the incorrect rating

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these three recalls — the 2024-2026 VNL load lock clamp recall, the 2018-2021 VNL/VNR rear-axle weight-rating label recall, or the 2024 VN/VHD front-axle weight-rating label recall — carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices simply describe the defect and the free repair; they do not instruct owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle away from structures while waiting for the fix.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the load lock clamps on affected VNL trucks, and for the two weight-label recalls dealers remove and replace the incorrect Federal Certification Label with a corrected one, including mailed replacement labels for the 2018-2021 VNL/VNR recall. All repairs, including label replacement, are done free of charge.

Common questions

Do I need to unload my truck for the Volvo weight-label recalls?

The notices only describe replacing the incorrect certification label; they don't instruct owners to unload the truck. They do warn that operating with the wrong rating can let axles be overloaded over time.

Will Volvo replace all the load lock bars on my VNL?

The notice says dealers will replace the load lock clamps that let the bars come loose, not the bars themselves, free of charge, on affected 2024-2026 VNL trucks.

Are these Volvo Equipment recalls about mechanical failure?

No. Two are incorrect certification labels overstating what the axles can carry, and one is a cargo clamp that can loosen — none involves an engine, brake, or steering defect.
