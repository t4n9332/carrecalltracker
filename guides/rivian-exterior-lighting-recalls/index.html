Rivian R1S and R1T lighting recalls: headlights, turn signals, reverse lights

5 NHTSA recalls · Rivian · Exterior Lighting · model years 2022–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 6, 2026

Front turn signals are the repeat problem here. A June 2025 recall covers 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles on which one or both front turn signals may fail to illuminate and flash, and recall FSAM-1833, filed in August 2026, describes the same failure across 2025-2026 R1S and R1T vehicles; its notice states it is an expansion of an earlier recall. The other three are separate lighting problems: reverse light beams that may not be visible from certain angles on 2022-2023 R1S (FSAM-1216), improperly aimed headlights on 2023-2024 EDV, R1S and R1T (FSAM-1445), and low beams that may fail to illuminate when starting in cold weather on 2025 R1S and R1T (FSAM-1612).

Warning signs to watch for

  • Recall FSAM-1833 states that one or both front turn signals may fail to illuminate and flash on 2025-2026 R1S and R1T vehicles.
  • The June 2025 front turn signal recall states that one or both front turn signals may fail to illuminate and flash on 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles.
  • Recall FSAM-1216 states that the reverse light beam may not be visible from certain angles on 2022-2023 R1S vehicles.
  • Recall FSAM-1445 states that improperly aimed headlights can result in insufficient illumination of the road on 2023-2024 EDV, R1S and R1T vehicles.
  • Recall FSAM-1612 states that the headlight low beams may fail to illuminate when starting the vehicle in cold weather on 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For recall FSAM-1833, the June 2025 front turn signal recall, recall FSAM-1216, recall FSAM-1445 and recall FSAM-1612, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. Each states only the risk it describes: turn signals that do not function properly increase the risk of a crash, reduced visibility of the reverse lights increases the risk of a crash, and headlights that are misaimed or fail to illuminate reduce visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.

What the free repair involves

For the two front turn signal recalls — FSAM-1833 and the June 2025 recall — Rivian inspects and replaces the front turn signals as necessary, free of charge. Recall FSAM-1216 repairs or replaces the reverse lights, FSAM-1445 inspects and adjusts headlight alignment as necessary, and FSAM-1612 replaces the headlight control modules. Each notice states the work is free of charge.

Common questions

I already had a turn signal recall done. Why is there another one?

The notice for recall FSAM-1833 states that it is an expansion of an earlier recall and covers 2025-2026 R1S and R1T vehicles with the same front turn signal failure. Rivian inspects and replaces the front turn signals as necessary, free of charge.

My turn signal recall letter said the remedy was not ready yet. What happens now?

The June 2025 front turn signal notice says owner notification letters were mailed September 22, 2025, and that owners will receive a second notice once the remedy becomes available. Rivian customer service is 1-888-748-4261.

Do the headlight recalls cover older R1S and R1T models?

They cover different years. Recall FSAM-1445 covers improperly aimed headlights on 2023-2024 EDV, R1S and R1T vehicles, while recall FSAM-1612 covers low beams failing to illuminate when starting in cold weather on 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
