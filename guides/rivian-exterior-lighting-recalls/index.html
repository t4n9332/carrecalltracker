Why do Rivian R1S and R1T headlights and turn signals fail?

5 NHTSA recalls · Rivian · Exterior Lighting · model years 2022–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-08-06

Exterior lighting problems recur across several Rivian recalls. Front turn signals that fail to illuminate and flash first affected 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles in one recall, then were expanded to also cover 2026 model-year vehicles. Headlight problems appear twice separately: low-beam headlights that may not turn on when starting a 2025 R1S in cold weather, and headlights that may be improperly aimed on 2023-2024 R1S and R1T vehicles. An earlier recall covered reverse lights on 2022-2023 R1S vehicles that may not be visible from certain angles. All five recalls were remedied by inspection and, where necessary, part replacement rather than a software update.

Warning signs to watch for

  • One or both front turn signals fail to illuminate and flash
  • Reverse lights that are hard to see from certain angles
  • Headlights that seem improperly aimed, reducing road illumination
  • Low-beam headlights that fail to turn on when starting in cold weather

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these five recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices say malfunctioning turn signals, hard-to-see reverse lights, misaimed headlights, or headlights that fail to turn on in cold weather each increase the risk of a crash, but none tell owners to stop driving. This applies to both front-turn-signal recalls (2025 R1S/R1T, later expanded to 2026 models), the 2022-2023 reverse-light recall, the 2023-2024 headlight-aim recall, and the 2025 cold-weather headlight recall.

What the free repair involves

Rivian inspects and, as necessary, replaces the front turn signals, repairs or replaces the reverse lights, adjusts headlight aim, or replaces the headlight control modules for the cold-weather low-beam failure. All inspections, replacements, and adjustments across these five recalls are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why did Rivian recall the front turn signals twice?

One or both front turn signals could fail to illuminate and flash on 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles. A later recall expanded this issue to also cover 2026 model-year vehicles. Rivian inspects and replaces the turn signals as needed, free of charge.

Why are some Rivian R1S headlights not turning on?

On certain 2025 R1S vehicles, the low-beam headlights may fail to illuminate when starting the vehicle in cold weather. Rivian replaces the headlight control modules as the free remedy for this recall.

What's wrong with the reverse lights on some Rivian R1S SUVs?

On 2022-2023 R1S vehicles, the reverse light beam may not be visible from certain angles, reducing visibility during reversing. Rivian repairs or replaces the reverse lights free of charge to correct it.
