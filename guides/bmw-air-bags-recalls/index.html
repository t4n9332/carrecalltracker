Why do BMW air bag recalls keep hitting the X models?

14 NHTSA recalls · BMW · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 5, 2024

Almost every recall here ends the same way: an air bag that may not deploy as intended. The X family dominates. Instrument panel manufacturing affected 2019 X3 and X4 vehicles and a single 2019 X5. Knee air bags come up twice — improperly folded modules on 2019-2020 2 Series and 4 Series models including the M4, and the front passenger knee air bag on the 2023 XM and 2024 X5, X6 and X7. Head and curtain air bags cover the 2019 X7, the 2025 X3 and the 2025 X4. Two more are electronic: a rollover sensor in the air bag control unit on 2020 X1 and X2 and MINI models, and an occupant classification system that can deactivate the passenger air bag on 2018-2019 X3 and 2019 X4 vehicles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these BMW air bag notices tells owners to stop driving or to park outside. They describe the risk in a crash: an air bag that does not deploy, deploys late or is damaged during deployment increases the risk of injury. Three of the recalls — the 2021 430i and M440i convertible software update and the two 2025 X3 recalls — cover vehicles the notices say had not been sold, so no owner letters were mailed for them.

What the free repair involves

Dealers replace the instrument panel, knee air bag modules, the driver's or front passenger air bag, the air bag control unit, or the seat foam holding the occupant classification system. Elsewhere they inspect: the X7 head air bags are reinstalled as needed, the 2025 X3 curtain air bag and X4 roof frame repaired if needed. One recall is a software update. The notices say the work is free.

Common questions

Which BMW models show up most in these air bag recalls?

The X series. X3 appears in four notices (2018-2019 occupant classification, 2019 instrument panel, and two 2025 recalls), and X1, X2, X4, X5, X6 and X7 each appear at least once, along with the iX, XM and some MINI models.

Do any of these air bag recalls require a software update?

One does. The 2021 430i and M440i 4 Series Convertible recall, where the head-thorax air bag may not deploy as intended in certain side-impact crashes, is fixed with a dealer software update at no charge. The other repairs replace or inspect parts.

Will I get a letter about my BMW air bag recall?

BMW mailed owner notification letters for most of these recalls, with dates from 2019 through January 2025. For the 2021 convertible recall and the two 2025 X3 recalls the notices say the vehicles had not been sold, so no letters were mailed.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
