BMW Air Bag Recalls Cover Deployment Failures and Passenger Protection

14 NHTSA recalls · BMW · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Dec 5, 2024

BMW’s listed air bag recalls repeatedly concern protection that may fail during a crash, with X3 and X4 appearing across several notices. The 2019 X3 and X4 instrument-panel recall concerns passenger frontal deployment, while a separate notice covers occupant classification in certain 2018-2019 X3 and 2019 X4 vehicles. For 2025 X3 vehicles, separate notices address damaged passenger air bag fabric and a right-side curtain air bag gas generator. Other patterns include knee air bag deployment problems in the 2019-2020 model range and certain 2023 XM and 2024 SUVs. The 2022-2023 iX and X1 notice concerns incorrectly manufactured driver air bags.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The notices for these recalls do not describe any warning sign an owner would notice.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The supplied notices do not tell owners to stop driving. This applies to the April 2019 X3/X4 instrument-panel, May 2019 knee air bag and X7 alignment, May 2020 rollover-sensor, March 2021 convertible, and July 2021 occupant-classification and X5 instrument-panel recalls. The February 2023 driver air bag, September 2023 passenger knee air bag, October 2024 X3 passenger air bag, November 2024 X3 curtain air bag, and December 2024 X4 roof-frame notices likewise do not tell owners to stop driving.

What the free repair involves

The X3/X4 and X5 instrument-panel recalls specify free panel replacement. Other notices specify free replacement of knee air bags, driver or passenger air bags, the rollover-related control unit, or occupant-classification seat foam. The X7 alignment, X3 curtain, and X4 roof-frame recalls specify free inspection and applicable reinstallation, replacement, or repair. The 2021 convertible recall specifies a software update at no charge.

Common questions

Why does the BMW X3 appear in several air bag recalls?

The notices identify different X3 problems. Certain 2019 vehicles have an instrument-panel issue, certain 2018-2019 vehicles have an occupant-classification issue, and separate 2025 notices concern passenger air bag fabric and the right-side head curtain air bag gas generator.

Which BMW air bag recall uses a software update?

The March 15, 2021 notice for certain 2021 430i and M440i 4 Series Convertibles specifies a dealer software update at no charge. It concerns head-thorax air bag deployment in certain side-impact crashes and says affected vehicles remained in dealer inventory.

Why does the BMW X4 roof-frame recall involve an air bag?

The December 5, 2024 notice covers certain 2025 X4 xDrive 30i, X4 M40i, and X4 M vehicles. A crack near the head air bag can damage it during deployment. Dealers inspect and, if necessary, repair the inner-left roof frame free of charge.
