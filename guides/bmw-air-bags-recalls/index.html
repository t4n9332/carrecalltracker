Why does BMW keep recalling the X3 for different air bag defects?

14 NHTSA recalls · BMW · AIR Bags · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2024-12-05

BMW's X3 appears in four separate air bag recalls with unrelated causes: a 2019 instrument panel manufacturing defect affecting passenger air bag deployment, a 2018-2019 occupant classification system that could misclassify the seat and deactivate the passenger air bag, a 2025 recall for passenger air bag fabric damaged during transport, and a 2025 recall for an incorrectly assembled gas generator in the side head curtain air bag. Other X-series models were recalled individually: the X7 for misaligned head air bags, X1/X2 and MINI models for a rollover sensor issue, the X5 for an instrument panel defect, the iX and X1 for a driver air bag manufacturing defect, and the X5/X6/X7/XM for a passenger knee air bag issue, each with its own distinct cause.

Warning signs to watch for

  • None of these recalls describe a warning light, noise, or other sign owners would notice before a crash
  • The defects were found through manufacturing and assembly inspections, not from any driving symptom
  • Owners cannot tell whether their vehicle is affected without checking their VIN against the recall

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these 14 BMW air bag recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. For the X3, X4, X7, X1, X2, X5, X6, iX, XM, and MINI recalls listed here, the notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The notices describe the risk in terms of what happens if a crash occurs before the repair — an air bag failing to deploy, deploying improperly, or not activating at all, along with related systems like seat belt pretensioners not activating — which BMW says increases the risk of injury in a crash.

What the free repair involves

Repairs vary by recall: dealers replace the instrument panel assembly, knee air bag modules, head air bags, air bag control unit, seat foam and occupant classification system, driver's or passenger's air bag, or the roof frame near the head air bag, and in one case update software. Some recalled vehicles were still in dealer inventory and were repaired before sale. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Why has BMW recalled the X3 for air bags four times?

Each X3 air bag recall has a different cause: a 2019 instrument panel defect, a 2018-2019 occupant classification system that could deactivate the passenger air bag, a 2025 damaged air bag fabric issue, and a separate 2025 curtain air bag assembly defect.

Does the BMW rollover air bag recall affect the X3?

No. That 2020 recall covers the X1, X2, and several MINI Cooper models, not the X3, and involves a rollover sensor issue that could prevent head air bags and seat belt pretensioners from activating in a rollover crash.

Do any BMW air bag recalls tell owners to stop driving?

No. None of the 14 air bag recalls in this group instruct owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside, according to the notices.
