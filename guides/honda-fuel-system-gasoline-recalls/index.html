Why do Honda fuel system recalls warn about fire or stalling?

14 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-26

This is Honda's largest recall group, split between fuel leaks and fuel pump failures. Leak-related recalls involve a cracked or failed weld on the 2019 CR-V's fuel tank, an overfilling Braun-modified Odyssey (2018-2021) tank, a cracking high-pressure fuel pump on the Accord and CR-V Hybrid (2023-2025), a separating fuel filler pipe on the Pilot and Passport (2023-2025), and hose problems on the 2025 CR-V Hybrid and CB1000SP - all raising fire risk. Separately, a low-pressure fuel pump defect that can stall the engine has been recalled and expanded three times, eventually covering Honda and Acura models from 2013-2023, alongside a related impeller defect on the CBR600RR, CBR1000RR, and GL1800 motorcycles.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Fuel smell or visible fuel leak near the tank, pump, filler pipe, or hose
  • Engine stalls while driving (fuel pump failure recalls)
  • Loss of power or stall when the Wheelie Control feature is active (CRF1100)
  • Fuel leaking onto the rear tire (CB1000SP)

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of these 14 recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, even though many describe a fire risk from a fuel leak or a stall risk from fuel pump failure. Honda's notices for the CR-V, Braun-modified Odyssey, Accord and CR-V Hybrid, Pilot and Passport, CR-V Hybrid, and CB1000SP leak recalls direct owners to a free dealer repair without telling them to stop driving. The fuel pump stall recalls, across numerous Honda and Acura models and Honda motorcycles, are handled the same way.

What the free repair involves

For the leak recalls, dealers inspect and replace the fuel tank, fuel pump, filler pipe, or fuel hose as needed. For the stall recalls, dealers replace the fuel pump module or impeller, or reprogram the fuel injection control software on the CRF1100. All repairs, including replacement parts, are performed free of charge.

Common questions

Why has Honda recalled fuel pumps so many times?

The low-pressure fuel pump defect started as one recall and was expanded twice as more affected vehicles were identified, eventually covering Honda and Acura models from 2013-2023. Each expansion is a separate NHTSA recall, which is why the same defect appears multiple times.

Is the Braun-modified Odyssey fuel tank recall from Honda?

No - it was issued by Braun Corporation, which modifies Odysseys for wheelchair accessibility, not Honda. Braun will notify owners and have dealers inspect for a fuel leak, installing a new fuel tank assembly and Honda charcoal canister if needed, free of charge.

What's different about the CB1000SP fuel hose recall?

Unlike the other fuel leak recalls in this group, the CB1000SP's notice says a leak near the rear tire can also directly increase the risk of a crash or injury, in addition to the fire risk near an ignition source that the other leak recalls describe.
