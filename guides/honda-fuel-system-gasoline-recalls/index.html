Honda Fuel System Recalls Address Leaks, Pump Failures and Stalling

15 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Oct 1, 2026

Honda’s gasoline fuel-system notices cluster around fuel leaks and pump failures that can stall the engine. Accord, Civic and CR-V recur across the automobile pump notices, with 2018–2020 vehicles appearing repeatedly in the supplied listings. Newer leak notices cover high-pressure pumps on certain 2023–2024 Accord and Accord Hybrid, 2023–2025 CR-V Hybrid, and 2025 Civic and Civic Hybrid vehicles. Separate notices identify filler-pipe separation on 2023–2024 Passport and 2023–2025 Pilot vehicles and a fuel-feed-hose issue on the 2025 CR-V Hybrid. Motorcycle notices include fuel-pump failure, a disconnecting CB1000SP fuel hose, and CRF1100 fuel-injection software that can cause stalling or power loss when Wheelie Control activates.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Engine stalling: May 2020 Honda and Acura fuel-pump recall.
  • Engine stalling while driving: March 2021 Honda and Acura low-pressure fuel-pump recall.
  • Engine stalling while driving: December 2023 Honda and Acura in-tank fuel-pump recall.
  • Engine stalling: February 2024 GL1800, CBR600RR and CBR1000RR fuel-pump recall.
  • Engine stalling or loss of power when Wheelie Control activates: November 2024 CRF1100 FI-ECU software recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the supplied notices carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The CR-V tank-weld, Braun-modified Odyssey, high-pressure-pump, Passport/Pilot filler-pipe, CR-V Hybrid hose, CB1000SP hose, and Ridgeline feed-port notices do not tell owners to stop driving. The May 2020, March 2021 and December 2023 automobile fuel-pump notices likewise do not tell owners to stop driving. Neither do the February 2024 motorcycle fuel-pump or November 2024 CRF1100 software notices. The supplied notices do not direct owners to park outside.

What the free repair involves

The specified remedies are free. Dealers replace the 2019 CR-V fuel tank; inspect and replace high-pressure pumps as necessary under the October 2024 notice; and install a new hose and connector cover for the June 2025 CR-V Hybrid recall. The December 2023 pump recall specifies module replacement. The November 2024 CRF1100 recall specifies FI-ECU software reprogramming.

Common questions

Are the CR-V Hybrid fuel-pump and fuel-hose recalls the same problem?

The October 2024 notice identifies a high-pressure fuel pump that may crack and leak, including certain 2023–2025 CR-V Hybrids. The June 2025 notice identifies an improperly installed fuel feed hose on certain 2025 CR-V Hybrids. Their specified remedies differ.

What does the Braun-modified Odyssey fuel recall require?

The January 2021 notice concerns certain 2018–2021 Braun-modified Odysseys whose fuel tank assembly may overfill into the charcoal canister. Dealers inspect the vehicle. If evidence of leakage is present, they install a new tank assembly and Honda charcoal canister free.

When does the CRF1100 fuel-injection software notice describe power loss?

The November 2024 notice describes possible engine stalling or loss of power when Wheelie Control activates on certain 2022 and 2024–2025 CRF1100 Africa Twin motorcycles. Dealers reprogram the FI-ECU software free of charge. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.
