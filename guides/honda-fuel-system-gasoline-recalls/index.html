What are the Honda fuel system recalls for leaks and stalling?

14 NHTSA recalls · Honda · Fuel System, Gasoline · model years 2018–2025 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-03-26

Two problems dominate this group. Fuel pump failure inside the tank is the biggest: it starts with 2018-2019 Accord, Civic Hatchback, HR-V, 2019 Fit and 2019-2020 Insight, expands in 2021 to 2019-2020 Acura MDX, RDX, TLX and 2018-2019 CR-V, then a 2023 recall covering 2013-2023 Accord, Civic, CR-V, HR-V, Ridgeline and Odyssey. The rest are fuel leaks from different parts: the 2019 CR-V tank weld, the 2017-2019 Ridgeline pump feed port, the 2023-2025 Pilot and 2023-2024 Passport filler pipe, high-pressure pumps on 2023-2024 Accord and 2023-2025 CR-V Hybrid, and a 2025 CR-V Hybrid feed hose. Motorcycles are included: GL1800, CBR600RR, CBR1000RR and CB1000SP. CR-V and Accord recur most.

Warning signs to watch for

  • An engine stall while driving — stated in the low-pressure fuel pump recalls covering 2018-2019 Accord, Civic Hatchback, HR-V, Fit and Insight, their 2021 expansion, and the 2013-2023 fuel pump module recall.
  • An engine stall while riding — stated in the 2018-2024 GL1800, CBR600RR and 2018-2019 CBR1000RR fuel pump recall.
  • An engine stall or loss of power when the Wheelie Control feature is activated — stated in the CRF1100 Africa Twin FI-ECU software recall.
  • Fuel leaking from the vehicle — stated in the 2019 CR-V fuel tank weld recall, the 2025 CR-V Hybrid fuel feed hose recall and the 2023-2025 Pilot and 2023-2024 Passport filler pipe recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of the notices in this group carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the fuel pump recalls on 2018-2019 Accord, Civic Hatchback, HR-V, Fit and Insight, their 2021 expansion, the 2013-2023 fuel pump module recall, the GL1800, CBR600RR and CBR1000RR recall and the CRF1100 recall do not tell owners to stop driving; neither do the fuel leak notices for the 2019 CR-V, 2017-2019 Ridgeline, Braun-modified 2018-2021 Odyssey, 2023-2024 Passport and 2023-2025 Pilot, 2023-2024 Accord and 2023-2025 CR-V Hybrid, 2025 CR-V Hybrid or 2025 CB1000SP. Only your dealer can complete the recall repair.

What the free repair involves

The remedies differ by recall, and each notice says the work is free. Dealers replace the fuel pump assembly or fuel pump module on the low-pressure pump recalls; replace the 2019 CR-V fuel tank; inspect and replace high-pressure pumps as necessary; inspect and repair the Pilot and Passport filler neck tube and pipe; install a new fuel feed hose on the 2025 CR-V Hybrid; and reprogram the CRF1100 FI-ECU software.

Common questions

Is my Honda covered by the big fuel pump recall?

Three notices cover the in-tank fuel pump. The 2020 recall lists 2018-2019 Accord, Civic Hatchback, Civic Type R, HR-V, 2019 Fit and 2019-2020 Insight. The 2021 expansion and the 2023 recall covering 2013-2023 models add many more. Check your VIN with Honda.

Are Honda motorcycles part of these fuel system recalls?

Yes. One recall covers fuel pump modules on 2018-2024 GL1800 Goldwing, CBR600RR and 2018-2019 CBR1000RR. Another reprograms FI-ECU software on CRF1100 Africa Twin models, and a third replaces the fuel hose on the 2025 CB1000SP.

Do the notices tell me to park my Honda outside?

None of the notices in this group gives a park-outside instruction, and none tells owners to stop driving. Each one states the defect, what it can cause, and that the dealer repair is free of charge.
