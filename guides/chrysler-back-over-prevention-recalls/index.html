Why won't the rearview camera display on Chrysler Pacifica models?

6 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2027 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 13, 2026

All six of these Chrysler recalls involve the rearview camera system and a failure to meet Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 111. Two patterns repeat. In the 2018-2019 Pacifica recall and the broad 2019-2020 recall covering Pacifica, Durango, Challenger, Grand Cherokee, Wrangler, Renegade, Cherokee, Gladiator and Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 trucks, a software error leaves the rearview image on the screen after the driver shifts out of reverse. In the four newer recalls the image does not appear at all: radio software on 2021-2023 models, a manufacturing error on 2023 Pacifica and Voyager, module contamination or programming on 2019 and 2023 Pacifica, and a display failure on 2027 Pacifica. The Pacifica appears in five of the six.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The rearview image stays on the radio screen after the vehicle is shifted out of reverse (2018-2019 Pacifica recall VE2).
  • The rearview camera image remains displayed after the vehicle is shifted out of reverse (2019-2020 Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram radio display recall).
  • The rearview camera image may not display (2023 Pacifica and Voyager recall 34C; 2019 and 2023 Pacifica recall 46D).
  • The rearview camera image may fail to display when the vehicle is placed in reverse (2027 Pacifica recall 78D).

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these six notices carries a stop-driving instruction, and none tells owners to park the vehicle outside. Each notice states the same kind of consequence — a rearview image that does not display, or one that lingers after the driver shifts out of reverse, reduces or distracts from the driver's view of what is behind the vehicle and increases the risk of a crash — and then says only that owners are not told to stop driving. No notice in this group offers an interim step to take before the dealer repair.

What the free repair involves

Remedies differ by recall. For the 2018-2019 Pacifica and the 2019-2020 recall, dealers update the radio or radio display software; the 2019-2020 recall also offered an over-the-air update. Recall 66B/79B updates radio software at a dealer or over the air, as does recall 78D. Recall 34C inspects and replaces the rearview camera as necessary. Recall 46D updates or replaces the central vision processing module. All free of charge.

Common questions

My 2023 Pacifica shows up in more than one of these recalls — why?

Two recalls cover the 2023 Pacifica: recall 34C, for a rearview image that may not display, and recall 46D, covering contamination in the central vision processing module or improper programming. Check your VIN against both.

Can the fix be done over the air instead of at a dealer?

Some of these recalls say so. The notices for recall 66B/79B and recall 78D say the radio software will be updated by a dealer or through an over-the-air update. The 2019-2020 recall says owners could optionally update remotely. The other notices describe dealer work only.

Does this affect anything besides the backup camera?

The notices describe the rearview camera image only — it either fails to display or stays on screen after the driver shifts out of reverse — and cite Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard 111. They do not describe any other system as affected.

A recall applies only to specific vehicles. Check your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
