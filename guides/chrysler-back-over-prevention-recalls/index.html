Chrysler Rearview Camera Recalls Cover Missing and Lingering Images

6 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2027 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest Aug 13, 2026

Chrysler Pacifica appears throughout these rearview camera recalls, but the display problems differ. The December 2019 notice covers certain 2018-2019 Pacificas with specified entertainment and camera equipment; its software error leaves the image displayed after reverse. The September 2020 notice describes the same lingering-image behavior for certain 2019-2020 Pacificas among other vehicles. Missing images recur in June 2024 radio software, June 2025 camera, May 2026 central vision processing module, and August 2026 radio software notices. Voyager appears in the June 2024 and June 2025 notices and the August 2026 notice. Pacifica model years mentioned across the supplied notices extend from 2018 through 2027.

Warning signs to watch for

  • December 2019 recall VE2 and the September 2020 recall: the rearview image remains displayed after shifting out of reverse.
  • June 2024 recall 66B/79B: the rearview camera image may not display.
  • June 2025 recall 34C: the rearview camera image may not display.
  • May 2026 recall 46D: the rearview camera image may not display.
  • August 2026 recall 78D: the rearview camera image may fail to display when reverse is selected.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

The six listed camera notices do not tell owners to stop driving, and none gives a park-outside instruction. The December 2019 and September 2020 notices describe distraction from a rearview image that remains displayed after leaving reverse. The June 2024, June 2025, May 2026, and August 2026 notices describe reduced rear visibility when the image does not display. These notices identify crash risks and prescribed repairs; they do not provide an assurance about continuing to drive an affected vehicle.

What the free repair involves

Repairs are free. VE2 and the September 2020 recall update radio software; the latter offers optional OTA updating. Recalls 66B/79B and 78D provide dealer or OTA software updates. Recall 34C inspects and replaces the camera as necessary. Recall 46D updates central vision processing module software or replaces the module as necessary.

Common questions

Can a Chrysler camera recall cover an image that stays on?

The December 2019 VE2 notice and September 2020 notice both describe rearview images remaining displayed after shifting out of reverse. Their stated risk is driver distraction. Both prescribe free radio software updates, with optional OTA updating specified in the September notice.

Which Chrysler rearview camera recalls offer an OTA update?

The June 2024 66B/79B notice and August 2026 78D notice specify dealer or OTA software updates. The September 2020 notice also offers an optional remote update. Those remedies are free; the other supplied camera notices specify dealer work.

Does a missing Pacifica camera image always require camera replacement?

The prescribed repair depends on the notice. Recall 34C calls for camera inspection and replacement as necessary. Recall 46D calls for central vision processing module software updating or module replacement as necessary. Recalls 66B/79B and 78D specify radio software updates.
