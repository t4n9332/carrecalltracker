Why Do Chrysler Rearview Cameras Keep Failing or Freezing?

5 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest 2026-05-21

All five recalls in this group involve the Chrysler Pacifica, and most also cover other Jeep, Ram, or Dodge models. Two different problems repeat: a rearview camera image that fails to display at all, caused by radio software (2021-2023 models), a manufacturing defect (2023 Pacifica/Voyager), or contamination and improper programming in the central vision processing module (2019 and 2023 Pacifica); and a rearview image that stays on screen after the vehicle is shifted out of reverse, caused by a software error (2018-2019 Pacifica, and again in 2019-2020 across the wider Jeep, Ram, Dodge and Chrysler lineup).

Warning signs to watch for

  • Rearview camera image does not appear on the screen when the vehicle is in reverse (several recalls).
  • Rearview camera image stays on the screen after shifting out of reverse, instead of clearing.

Is it safe to keep driving?

None of the five rearview camera recalls in this group carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The notices for the radio software recall, the two lingering-image recalls, and the two camera-not-displaying Pacifica/Voyager recalls do not tell owners to stop driving. Each notice instead warns that a missing or lingering rearview image reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle or can distract the driver, increasing the risk of a crash, without telling owners to stop using the vehicle.

What the free repair involves

Repairs across this group are mostly software fixes: dealers update the radio or central vision processing module software, with two of the five recalls also offering the update over the air. Where hardware is at fault, dealers inspect and replace the rearview camera or processing module as necessary. All repairs are free of charge.

Common questions

Do I need to stop driving if my Chrysler's backup camera fails?

No. None of the five recalls in this group tell owners to stop driving or park outside. The notices say a missing or lingering camera image reduces visibility or distracts the driver, increasing crash risk, but they do not order owners to stop using the vehicle.

Can I fix a Chrysler rearview camera recall without visiting a dealer?

Sometimes. Two of the five recalls in this group can be corrected with an over-the-air software update; the rest require a dealer to update or replace the camera or processing module, at no cost to the owner.

Why does my Pacifica's backup camera image stay on the screen after I shift out of reverse?

Two recalls, covering 2018-2019 and 2019-2020 model years, describe a software error that leaves the rearview image displayed after the vehicle leaves reverse, which the notices say can distract the driver.
