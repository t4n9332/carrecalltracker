Chrysler Pacifica rearview camera recalls: image missing or stuck on screen

5 NHTSA recalls · Chrysler · Back Over Prevention · model years 2018–2023 (we track 2018 and newer) · newest May 21, 2026

All five recalls in this group are about the Pacifica's rearview camera, and they split into two patterns. In the older pair — December 2019 for 2018-2019 Pacifica with the 8.4-inch radio, 360-degree camera and seat back video screens, and September 2020 for 2019-2020 Pacifica with 8.4-inch or 12-inch displays — a software error leaves the camera image on the screen after the driver shifts out of reverse. The newer three are the reverse problem: no image at all. Radio software is named in the June 2024 recall of 2021-2023 Pacifica and 2022 Voyager, a manufacturing error in the June 2025 recall of 2023 Pacifica and Voyager, and module contamination or improper programming in the May 2026 recall of 2019 and 2023 Pacifica.

Warning signs to watch for

  • The rearview camera image remains displayed after the vehicle is shifted out of reverse — the December 2019 recall of 2018-2019 Pacifica and the September 2020 recall of 2019-2020 Pacifica.
  • The rearview camera image does not display — the June 2024 radio software recall, the June 2025 recall of 2023 Pacifica and Voyager, and the May 2026 central vision processing module recall.

Can you keep driving? What the notices say

None of these five notices tells owners to stop driving or to park the vehicle outside. The two older recalls, from December 2019 and September 2020, say a lingering rearview image can distract the driver. The June 2024, June 2025 and May 2026 recalls say an image that does not display reduces the driver's view of what is behind the vehicle. Every one of them describes that as increasing the risk of a crash, and none gives owners an interim instruction beyond having the remedy performed.

What the free repair involves

Dealers update the radio software in the December 2019 and September 2020 recalls; owners in the 2020 recall may instead choose an over-the-air update. The June 2024 recall is a dealer radio software update or an over-the-air update. In the June 2025 recall dealers inspect and replace the rearview camera as necessary, and in May 2026 they update or replace the central vision processing module. All free of charge.

Common questions

Can any of these camera recalls be fixed without a dealer visit?

Two of them mention it. The September 2020 notice says owners can optionally update the software over the air, and the June 2024 notice says the radio software update may be done by a dealer or over the air. The other three describe dealer work.

My 2023 Pacifica shows no camera image. Which recall covers it?

Two notices list the 2023 Pacifica: the June 2025 recall, where a manufacturing error may keep the image from displaying and dealers inspect and replace the camera, and the May 2026 recall, covering contamination or improper programming in the central vision processing module.

Why is a backup camera problem a safety recall at all?

The notices say these vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, covering rear visibility. A missing image reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, and a lingering image can distract the driver; both are described as increasing the risk of a crash.
