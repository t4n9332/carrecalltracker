Privacy policy

Car Recall Tracker does not require an account and does not collect personal information directly. We never receive or store VINs. If you type a VIN into the finder on our home page, your browser sends it directly to NHTSA's public VIN decoder (vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov) to identify the year, make and model; it is not sent to our server, added to any URL on this site, or saved. NHTSA's own privacy policy applies to that request.

If you use "Save to My Garage", the list of models you saved is kept in your own browser's local storage on that device. It is never sent to us, and you can remove it at any time with the Remove links on the home page or by clearing your browser's site data.

This site may display advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. See how Google uses data.

Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) may be kept by the hosting provider for security purposes.
