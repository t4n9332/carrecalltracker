Volkswagen Golf R recalls

Volkswagen Atlas and Golf backup camera may fail medium

Reported Jun 27, 2024 · Campaign 24V480000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

On these Volkswagen vehicles, the rearview camera image may be delayed or may not turn on at all after the driver shifts the vehicle into reverse gear. Because the camera may not show the view behind the vehicle right away, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for rear visibility. Dealers will update the infotainment system software at no charge to the owner. Owner notification letters were mailed in mid-July 2024.

Volkswagen Golf R Suspension Strut Mount May Loosen high

Reported Jun 21, 2023 · Campaign 23V437000 · Model years: 2023

The mount that holds the front suspension strut in place may be deformed, or bent out of shape. When that happens, the bolted connections holding the strut to the suspension can come loose.

Volkswagen Golf R and ID.4 rearview camera may go blank medium

Reported Nov 2, 2022 · Campaign 22V823000 · Model years: 2022

Volkswagen is recalling certain Golf R and ID.4 vehicles, along with some Audi and Golf GTI models. The rearview camera image may be blank or may not respond when the vehicle is shifted into reverse or when the park function is turned on by hand. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal rules for rear visibility. A dealer will replace the rearview camera at no cost to the owner. Volkswagen and Audi each have a customer service line for questions.

Volkswagen and Audi Tire Pressure Monitor Software Issue medium

Reported Oct 31, 2022 · Campaign 22V815000 · Model years: 2019

Your vehicle's tire pressure monitoring system is supposed to warn you when tire pressure gets too low. In these vehicles, if all four tires lose air pressure at the same time, the system may not detect this quickly enough to warn you. This means you might not get a timely alert if every tire loses pressure at once. Dealers will fix the problem by updating the tire pressure monitoring software at no cost.

Volkswagen Golf R Radiator May Leak Coolant critical

Reported Sep 21, 2022 · Campaign 22V707000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

In some Volkswagen Golf R vehicles, the radiator may not have been attached to the intercooler correctly. This can damage a coolant hose, causing it to leak coolant from the cooling system.

VW Golf R Sunroof May Close Unintentionally high

Reported Jul 27, 2022 · Campaign 22V542000 · Model years: 2022

The touch switch that controls the sunroof in these Volkswagen vehicles may be too sensitive and could be pressed without anyone meaning to. If that happens, the sunroof may close on its own unexpectedly.

Volkswagen Golf R Engine Cover May Loosen and Melt critical

Reported Mar 16, 2022 · Campaign 22V163000 · Model years: 2022

During certain driving situations, such as hard acceleration, the engine design cover may come loose. A loose cover can contact hot surfaces inside the engine compartment and melt.

Volkswagen Atlas, Golf R May Miss Safety Standards high

Reported May 5, 2021 · Campaign 21V321000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

These vehicles were used internally by Volkswagen for its own purposes before being sold to customers, and they may have been modified at some point before that sale. Volkswagen did not confirm that these vehicles were built to meet all of the regulatory requirements that apply to them before they were sold. Because of this, the vehicles may not meet all of the federal motor vehicle safety standards that they are required to meet.

VW Atlas, Golf R, Passat May Not Meet Safety Rules medium

Reported Sep 16, 2020 · Campaign 20V561000 · Model years: 2018

These are Volkswagen vehicles that were originally used internally by the company. Before being sold, there was no confirmation that they had been built to meet all of the required federal vehicle safety rules, and some of them may have been modified before they were sold. Because of this, these vehicles may not actually meet every safety requirement they are supposed to meet.

Volkswagen Golf R Fuel Hose May Detach critical

Reported Dec 5, 2018 · Campaign 18V864000 · Model years: 2018

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2018 Golf R vehicles because the fuel hose quick connector in the engine bay may detach from the fuel supply line, resulting in a fuel leak in the engine compartment. Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will secure the fuel line connection as necessary to fix the issue. This repair will be done free of charge for all affected vehicles.

e-Golf, Golf R rear head restraints may weaken high

Reported Nov 14, 2018 · Campaign 18V803000 · Model years: 2018

On some e-Golf and Golf R vehicles, the guide sleeves for the rear seat head restraints may not have been welded to the seat frame correctly. If a crash happens, these head restraints may not stay stable, which increases the risk of injury.

Volkswagen Golf R recall questions

How many recalls does the Volkswagen Golf R have?

NHTSA lists 11 safety recalls covering the 2018–2024 Volkswagen Golf R. 7 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Volkswagen Atlas and Golf backup camera may fail" (NHTSA campaign 24V480000), reported Jun 27, 2024.

Which Volkswagen Golf R model year has the most recalls?

The 2022 Volkswagen Golf R has the most, with 5 recalls. The 2024 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Volkswagen Golf R?

No. None of the 11 Volkswagen Golf R recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Volkswagen Golf R has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Volkswagen dealer, including for second owners.
