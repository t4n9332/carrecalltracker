Volkswagen Beetle Driver Airbag Inflator May Explode critical

Reported Oct 30, 2024 · Campaign 24V834000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

In certain Volkswagen Beetle and Beetle Convertible vehicles, the airbag in front of the driver can explode instead of inflating normally. This happens because the propellant inside the inflator breaks down over time, after long-term exposure to high humidity, high temperatures, and repeated temperature swings. If the inflator explodes, it can throw sharp pieces of metal at the driver or other people in the car.

VW Jetta and Beetle Brake Master Cylinder Leak high

Reported Sep 23, 2020 · Campaign 20E064000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Tenneco is recalling aftermarket Beck/Arnley brake master cylinders that were sold as replacement parts for certain Volkswagen Jetta and Beetle vehicles. The crimp that holds the fluid seal in place on the master cylinder may not be tight enough. Because of this, brake fluid can leak out, or the master cylinder itself can come apart. A leak like this can reduce how well the brakes work.

Volkswagen Jetta and Golf key removable when not in Park high

Reported Aug 22, 2019 · Campaign 19V615000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Volkswagen is recalling certain Beetle, Beetle Convertible, Golf, Golf SportWagen, GTI and Jetta vehicles that have an automatic transmission, a manual handbrake and keyless entry. On these cars, a build-up of silicate on the contacts of the small switch at the shift lever can let the key be pulled out of the ignition even when the shift lever is not in Park. Dealers will install an extra switch and circuit board at no cost.

VW Beetle Instrument Cluster May Not Warn of Brake Trouble high

Reported Feb 13, 2019 · Campaign 19V094000 · Model years: 2019

Some Volkswagen Beetle and Beetle Convertible cars have a dashboard warning light system that may fail to alert the driver when there's a problem with the brakes. Normally, if something goes wrong with the brakes, a light on the instrument cluster should turn on to let the driver know. In these vehicles, that warning may not appear, so the driver could be unaware that the brakes have an issue. Because of this, the vehicles don't meet federal safety rules for brake warning systems.

VW and Audi Brake Calipers May Reduce Braking critical

Reported Jun 1, 2018 · Campaign 18V369000 · Model years: 2018

Certain 2018 Volkswagen Atlas, Passat, Beetle, Beetle Convertible, Golf, Golf SportWagen, GTI, and Audi Q5 and SQ5 vehicles may have brake caliper pistons without enough protective coating. This lack of coating can reduce how well the brakes work.

Volkswagen Beetle recall questions

How many recalls does the Volkswagen Beetle have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2018–2019 Volkswagen Beetle. 5 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Volkswagen Beetle Driver Airbag Inflator May Explode" (NHTSA campaign 24V834000), reported Oct 30, 2024.

Which Volkswagen Beetle model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Volkswagen Beetle has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2018 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 4. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Volkswagen Beetle?

Yes. 1 Volkswagen Beetle recall carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: VW and Audi Brake Calipers May Reduce Braking (18V369000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Volkswagen Beetle has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Volkswagen dealer, including for second owners.
