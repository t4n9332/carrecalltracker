Volkswagen Jetta recalls

22 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Volkswagen Jetta and Taos Instrument Panel Display Failure high

Reported 2026-03-25 · Campaign 26V185000 · Model years: 2025

The instrument panel in these vehicles may fail to show warning lights or gauges. This means the driver may not be able to see important safety information, such as the speedometer or warning lights, while driving. Not being able to see this information increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will update the instrument panel software and replace the instrument panel if needed, free of charge.

Volkswagen Jetta Ground Wire May Cause Fire Risk critical

Reported 2026-03-11 · Campaign 26V138000 · Model years: 2025

On certain 2025-2026 Volkswagen Jetta vehicles, the ground wire for the transmission may not have been properly connected when the vehicle was built, leaving an open electrical circuit.

Audi, VW Fuel Tank Seal May Cause Leaks critical

Reported 2024-02-14 · Campaign 24V110000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Volkswagen is recalling certain front-wheel-drive Audi A3, Volkswagen Golf, Golf Sportwagen, GTI, and Jetta vehicles. A seal on a suction jet pump inside the fuel tank may fail, allowing fuel to flow into the evaporative emissions system and possibly leak out of the charcoal canister. A fuel leak near a source of ignition can increase the risk of a fire. Dealers will replace the suction pump. This recall replaces an earlier recall, so vehicles already repaired under it will need the new repair.

Volkswagen Jetta may not chime for key left in ignition low

Reported 2023-09-06 · Campaign 23V619000 · Model years: 2019

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2019 Jetta vehicles. The instrument cluster may not sound the audible warning that tells the driver the key is still in the ignition when the door is open. Because that warning may not sound, these vehicles do not meet the federal theft protection rule. Dealers will update the instrument cluster free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed November 3, 2023. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 90W7.

VW Golf, GTI, Jetta Ignition Switch Can Fail high

Reported 2023-08-30 · Campaign 23V604000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On certain Volkswagen Golf, Golf Alltrack, Golf Sportwagen, Golf GTI, and Jetta vehicles with a conventional ignition switch, the switch itself may fail, and this is more likely in high-temperature conditions. If the switch fails, the vehicle's electrical system can shut down and the engine can stall while you are driving, cutting power in the middle of a drive.

Volkswagen and Audi Tire Pressure Monitor Software Issue medium

Reported 2022-10-31 · Campaign 22V815000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Your vehicle's tire pressure monitoring system is supposed to warn you when tire pressure gets too low. In these vehicles, if all four tires lose air pressure at the same time, the system may not detect this quickly enough to warn you. This means you might not get a timely alert if every tire loses pressure at once. Dealers will fix the problem by updating the tire pressure monitoring software at no cost.

Volkswagen Rearview Camera May Fail To Display medium

Reported 2022-07-20 · Campaign 22V514000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Certain Volkswagen vehicles have a manufacturing problem with a memory module in the infotainment system. Because of this, the rearview camera image may fail to display. This means the vehicle does not meet a federal safety standard for rear visibility.

Volkswagen Jetta Air Bag Module Installed Incorrectly high

Reported 2021-06-30 · Campaign 21V492000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Some Jetta and Jetta GLI vehicles have an air bag control module that was welded into the wrong position during assembly. Because of this, the air bags may not deploy correctly if the vehicle is in a crash. It's also possible for the air bags to go off on their own, without a crash happening.

Volkswagen Jetta and Tiguan Front Seat Bolts Missing medium

Reported 2021-02-01 · Campaign 21V038000 · Model years: 2021

On certain Tiguan Long Wheelbase and Jetta vehicles, bolts that hold the front seat track in place may be missing. Without these bolts, the seat can slide forward and backward without being restrained. If a crash happens while the bolts are missing, the seat's freedom to move increases the risk of injury to the person sitting in it during the crash.

Volkswagen Rearview Camera May Fail or Freeze low

Reported 2020-11-18 · Campaign 20V716000 · Model years: 2021

In several Volkswagen models, including the Atlas Cross Sport, Golf, Jetta, and Tiguan, the rearview camera can stop working properly when the vehicle is started. When this happens, the screen may go black or the infotainment system may freeze instead of showing the view behind the vehicle. Dealers will update the infotainment system software to fix the problem, free of charge.

Volkswagen Jetta fuel rail bolts may leak fuel critical

Reported 2020-10-21 · Campaign 20V648000 · Model years: 2018

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2016-2018 Jetta vehicles. The bolts holding the fuel rail in place may loosen over time, which can allow fuel to leak.

VW Jetta and Beetle Brake Master Cylinder Leak high

Reported 2020-09-23 · Campaign 20E064000 · Model years: 2018

Tenneco is recalling aftermarket Beck/Arnley brake master cylinders that were sold as replacement parts for certain Volkswagen Jetta and Beetle vehicles. The crimp that holds the fluid seal in place on the master cylinder may not be tight enough. Because of this, brake fluid can leak out, or the master cylinder itself can come apart. A leak like this can reduce how well the brakes work.

Audi, VW Front Seat Frame Weld Defect medium

Reported 2020-08-26 · Campaign 20V505000 · Model years: 2020

Volkswagen is recalling certain Audi Q5 and SQ5 SUVs, and Volkswagen Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, Jetta, Jetta GLI, and Tiguan vehicles. Parts of the front seat frame and the backrest adjuster may not have been welded together correctly during manufacturing, meaning the weld holding these seat parts together may not be as strong as it's supposed to be.

Volkswagen Jetta Front Brake Caliper Bolts May Loosen high

Reported 2020-06-17 · Campaign 20V359000 · Model years: 2020

The bolts that hold the front brake calipers in place may come loose.

Volkswagen Jetta and Golf Front Wheel Bearings critical

Reported 2019-12-11 · Campaign 19V879000 · Model years: 2019

Certain Volkswagen Jetta GLI, Golf Alltrack, and Golf GTI vehicles may have front wheel bearings that were not machined correctly. This kind of improper machining can put too much stress on the wheel, the wheel bolts, or the wheel bearings.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired.

Volkswagen Jetta, Tiguan Vehicles May Not Comply medium

Reported 2019-09-25 · Campaign 19V679000 · Model years: 2019

Some modifications were made to these vehicles while they were in an internal evaluation period. Because of those modifications, the vehicles may not meet all of the regulatory requirements that vehicles like these are required to meet.

Volkswagen Jetta and Golf key removable when not in Park high

Reported 2019-08-22 · Campaign 19V615000 · Model years: 2018

Volkswagen is recalling certain Beetle, Beetle Convertible, Golf, Golf SportWagen, GTI and Jetta vehicles that have an automatic transmission, a manual handbrake and keyless entry. On these cars, a build-up of silicate on the contacts of the small switch at the shift lever can let the key be pulled out of the ignition even when the shift lever is not in Park. Dealers will install an extra switch and circuit board at no cost.

Volkswagen Models May Have Rear Coil Spring Fracture high

Reported 2019-03-06 · Campaign 19V188000 · Model years: 2019

On certain Volkswagen Golf, Golf Sportwagen, Jetta, and Tiguan vehicles, a spring in the rear suspension may crack or break earlier than it should. If a spring breaks while the vehicle is being driven, it can damage a nearby rear tire. A damaged tire in this situation can cause the driver to lose control of the vehicle, raising the risk of a crash.

Volkswagen Jetta May Have Wrong Airbag Installed medium

Reported 2019-02-20 · Campaign 19V110000 · Model years: 2019

On certain Jetta vehicles, an incorrect driver frontal airbag may have been installed during a previous service or repair visit. Dealers will replace the driver frontal airbag with the correct part, free of charge.

Volkswagen Atlas, Jetta Rear Coil Spring May Fracture high

Reported 2018-12-19 · Campaign 18V904000 · Model years: 2019

Volkswagen is recalling certain Atlas and Jetta vehicles because the rear coil springs may fracture prematurely. If a spring breaks while the vehicle is being driven, it can damage a rear tire, and a damaged tire can cause the driver to lose control of the vehicle.

Volkswagen Atlas, Tiguan, Jetta missing key-in-ignition warning chime low

Reported 2018-11-26 · Campaign 18V824000 · Model years: 2019

These Volkswagens do not have keyless entry, so they rely on the instrument cluster to sound an audible warning when the driver opens the door with the key still in the ignition. On some vehicles, that warning tone may not sound. Without it, the driver may not realize the key was left in the ignition. Volkswagen dealers will reprogram the instrument cluster at no charge. The recall began December 13, 2018.

Jetta LED Headlight Positioned Incorrectly medium

Reported 2018-09-26 · Campaign 18V671000 · Model years: 2019

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2019 Jetta vehicles equipped with LED headlights because the passenger side headlight may be positioned incorrectly. This can reduce how well the driver can see when it's dark. Dealers will replace the passenger side headlight to fix the problem.
