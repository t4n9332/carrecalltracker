Jeep Wagoneer Roof Rack Crossbars May Come Loose medium

Reported Sep 10, 2026 · Campaign 26V584000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Wagoneer, Wagoneer L, Grand Wagoneer, and Grand Wagoneer L vehicles because the pivot bolts holding the roof rack crossbars may not have been tightened correctly. This could let the crossbars come loose and detach from the roof while the vehicle is being driven, rather than staying securely attached as intended.
