Jeep Wagoneer Roof Rack Crossbars May Come Loose medium

Reported Sep 10, 2026 · Campaign 26V584000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2026

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Wagoneer, Wagoneer L, Grand Wagoneer, and Grand Wagoneer L vehicles because the pivot bolts holding the roof rack crossbars may not have been tightened correctly. This could let the crossbars come loose and detach from the roof while the vehicle is being driven, rather than staying securely attached as intended.

Jeep tire pressure warning system may miss low tires medium

Reported Sep 1, 2026 · Campaign 26V559000 · Model years: 2026

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Grand Cherokee, Grand Cherokee L, Grand Cherokee 4xe, Wagoneer, Wagoneer L, Grand Wagoneer and Cherokee vehicles. A software error means the tire pressure monitoring system may not detect a tire that is low, or may not turn on the tire pressure warning light the way it should. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal standard for tire pressure monitoring systems. Dealers will update the software free of charge.

Chrysler Pacifica Rearview Camera May Not Display medium

Reported Aug 13, 2026 · Campaign 26V531000 · Model years: 2026

On some Chrysler Pacifica vehicles, the rearview camera image may fail to show up on the screen when the vehicle is shifted into reverse. Without that camera view, drivers have less visibility behind the vehicle while backing up. Because the camera image may not display, these vehicles do not meet a federal rear-visibility safety standard that requires a working rearview image when backing up.

Jeep Grand Wagoneer Software May Disable Brake Assistance high

Reported Jun 30, 2026 · Campaign 26V414000 · Model years: 2026

Jeep is recalling certain Grand Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer L vehicles because software in the brake system may cause electronic stability control and electronic brake assistance to stop working. The vehicles do not meet the federal safety requirements listed in the notice for these systems. Dealers will update the brake system software free of charge.

Jeep Grand Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer L Camera Image Failure high

Reported May 21, 2026 · Campaign 26V326000 · Model years: 2026

Certain Jeep Grand Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer L vehicles may have an improperly connected bundle of front camera wires. This connection problem can prevent the rearview camera image from appearing, reducing the driver's view behind the vehicle. The affected vehicles do not meet federal rear visibility requirements. Dealers will inspect the wiring connection and reconnect it if needed, free of charge.

Which Jeep Grand Wagoneer L model year has the most recalls?

The 2026 Jeep Grand Wagoneer L has the most, with 5 recalls. The 2023 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Jeep Grand Wagoneer L?

No. None of the 5 Jeep Grand Wagoneer L recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Jeep Grand Wagoneer L has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Jeep dealer, including for second owners.
