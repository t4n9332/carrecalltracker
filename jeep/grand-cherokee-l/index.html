Jeep tire pressure warning system may miss low tires medium

Reported Sep 1, 2026 · Campaign 26V559000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Grand Cherokee, Grand Cherokee L, Grand Cherokee 4xe, Wagoneer, Wagoneer L, Grand Wagoneer and Cherokee vehicles. A software error means the tire pressure monitoring system may not detect a tire that is low, or may not turn on the tire pressure warning light the way it should. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal standard for tire pressure monitoring systems. Dealers will update the software free of charge.

Jeep Grand Cherokee Rear Coil Spring May Detach high

Reported Sep 1, 2026 · Campaign 26V562000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

On these Jeep Grand Cherokee models, the rear coil springs may have been installed incorrectly. Because of this, a spring can come loose and separate from the vehicle while you are driving. This recall replaces two earlier recalls for the same problem, and vehicles already repaired under those earlier recalls will still need this new fix.

Jeep Grand Cherokee and Wagoneer gear display may not appear high

Reported Jun 30, 2026 · Campaign 26V413000 · Model years: 2024

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Grand Cherokee, Grand Cherokee L, Wagoneer and Wagoneer L vehicles. A software error can keep the display that shows which gear the transmission is in from appearing at all. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard that covers the shift lever sequence, the starter interlock and the transmission braking effect. Dealers will update the software in the shifter module.

Jeep Grand Cherokee Side Air Bags May Deploy Late high

Reported May 21, 2026 · Campaign 26V328000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

A software error in the computer that controls the air bags may cause the side air bags to deploy late during a crash, instead of deploying when needed. This affects certain Jeep Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles. Dealers will fix the problem by updating the control module's software, free of charge to the owner.

Jeep Grand Cherokee Rear Coil Springs Can Detach high

Reported Jan 29, 2026 · Campaign 26V051000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

On certain Jeep Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles, the rear coil springs may not have been installed the right way. Because of this, a spring could work loose and detach from the vehicle while you are driving it. Jeep already mailed owners a letter warning about this risk, and will send a second letter once the final repair is ready, expected in March 2026.

Jeep Grand Cherokee Head Restraints May Not Lock high

Reported Jul 17, 2025 · Campaign 25V472000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Chrysler says the head restraints on the second-row seats in some Jeep Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles may not lock into the upright position. Because these head restraints can fail to lock, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety requirement covering head restraints. Owners are being notified so the head restraints can be checked and fixed if needed.

Jeep Grand Cherokee blower motor may fail, disabling defroster high

Reported Jun 26, 2025 · Campaign 25V430000 · Model years: 2023

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles. The blower motor, the fan that pushes air through the vents, can fail. If it fails, the system that clears fog and frost from the windshield stops working. Federal safety rules set requirements for windshield defrosting and defogging, and these vehicles do not meet them. A dealer will inspect the blower motor and replace it if needed, at no cost.

Jeep Grand Cherokee rearview camera may go blank medium

Reported Feb 13, 2025 · Campaign 25V083000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

This recall affects certain Jeep Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles. A part inside the center touchscreen display may overheat. If that happens, the rearview camera image may not display on the screen.

Jeep SUVs: Rearview Camera Image May Not Show high

Reported Dec 19, 2024 · Campaign 24V944000 · Model years: 2024

Chrysler (FCA) is recalling certain 2024 Jeep Wagoneer, Grand Wagoneer, Grand Cherokee, and Grand Cherokee L vehicles. Incorrect software in the rearview camera module may cause the rearview camera image to not display when the driver puts the vehicle in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet federal rear visibility requirements.

Jeep Grand Cherokee Radiator Crossmember Bolts May Be Loose critical

Reported Dec 12, 2024 · Campaign 24V926000 · Model years: 2024

On certain 2024 Jeep Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles, the bolts that secure the radiator crossmember may not be properly secured. If the crossmember is not secure, it can lead to improper air bag deployment and a fuel leak during a crash.

Jeep Grand Cherokee Side Air Bag Connector May Fail high

Reported Nov 27, 2024 · Campaign 24V897000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

A connector for the front seat side air bags may not have been properly secured, which could prevent the air bags from deploying. This affects certain Jeep Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles. Dealers have already secured the connector on all affected vehicles, free of charge.

Jeep Grand Cherokee Speedometer Software Display Error high

Reported Nov 19, 2024 · Campaign 24V872000 · Model years: 2024

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles. These vehicles have a software error in the instrument panel cluster. Because of this error, the speedometer may show the wrong vehicle speed instead of the actual speed the vehicle is traveling. This means the vehicle does not meet a federal safety standard that covers how vehicle displays and controls must work.

Jeep Grand Cherokee L recall questions

How many recalls does the Jeep Grand Cherokee L have?

NHTSA lists 12 safety recalls covering the 2021–2025 Jeep Grand Cherokee L. 10 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Jeep tire pressure warning system may miss low tires" (NHTSA campaign 26V559000), reported Sep 1, 2026.

Which Jeep Grand Cherokee L model year has the most recalls?

The 2024 Jeep Grand Cherokee L has the most, with 8 recalls. The 2025 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Jeep Grand Cherokee L?

No. None of the 12 Jeep Grand Cherokee L recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Jeep Grand Cherokee L has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Jeep dealer, including for second owners.
