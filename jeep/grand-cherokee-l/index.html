Jeep Grand Cherokee L recalls

12 recalls covering model years 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Jeep Grand Cherokee rear springs can fall off critical

Reported 2026-09-01 · Campaign 26V562000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

The rear coil springs are the big metal springs that hold up the back of your Jeep and soak up bumps. On these vehicles they may have been installed incorrectly at the factory, and a spring can work loose and come off the vehicle while you are driving. Chrysler is replacing the rubber pads the springs sit on. The final fix is not ready yet, so owners are getting a warning letter first and a second letter later.

Jeep Grand Cherokee and Wagoneer Tire Warning Light May Fail low

Reported 2026-09-01 · Campaign 26V559000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

A software error means the tire pressure system may not spot a low tire, and the low-pressure warning light on your dash may not come on when it should. You could drive on an underinflated tire without knowing, which makes the vehicle harder to control and raises the chance of a crash. The fix is a free software update at the dealer. Owner letters are expected to start going out September 29, 2026; until then, check your tire pressures yourself with a gauge.

Jeep Grand Cherokee and Wagoneer gear display may vanish medium

Reported 2026-06-30 · Campaign 26V413000 · Model years: 2024

Certain Jeep Grand Cherokee, Grand Cherokee L, Wagoneer and Wagoneer L vehicles have a software error that can stop the gear display from showing. That is the readout that tells you whether the vehicle is in park, reverse, neutral or drive. Without it, you cannot confirm the vehicle is actually in park before you step out, and it may move when you do not expect it or roll away on its own. Dealers reprogram the shifter software for free.

Jeep Grand Cherokee side airbags may deploy too late high

Reported 2026-05-21 · Campaign 26V328000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

A software mistake in the computer that controls the airbags and seat belts can delay the side airbags during a crash. The airbags still go off, but not as quickly as they should, so they may not be fully in place when you need them. Because of this, these Jeeps do not meet the federal side-impact protection rule. The fix is a free software update at the dealer. Your VIN becomes searchable on NHTSA's website on May 28, 2026.

Jeep Grand Cherokee rear coil springs may fall off critical

Reported 2026-01-29 · Campaign 26V051000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

The rear coil springs are big metal springs that hold up the back of the vehicle. On these Jeeps, they may have been installed incorrectly and can come loose and fall off while you are driving. Chrysler says that raises the risk of a crash. Dealers will inspect and repair the rear spring assembly free of charge. Interim letters went out in February 2026, and the final repair was expected in March 2026, with a second letter to follow.

Jeep Grand Cherokee second-row headrests may not lock medium

Reported 2025-07-17 · Campaign 25V472000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

The headrests on the second-row seats of these Jeep Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L models may not lock in the upright position. A headrest that will not stay up cannot support the head of the person sitting there, so passengers in that row have less protection in a crash. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for head restraints. Dealers will inspect both second-row headrests and replace them if needed, free of charge.

Jeep Grand Cherokee blower motor can kill defroster medium

Reported 2025-06-26 · Campaign 25V430000 · Model years: 2023

The blower motor — the fan that pushes air through the vents — can fail. If it quits, the defroster and defogger stop working, so you cannot clear fog or frost from the windshield. That leaves you looking through a clouded or iced-over window, and poor visibility makes a crash more likely. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal standard for windshield defrosting and defogging. A dealer will inspect the blower motor and replace it if needed, free of charge.

Jeep Grand Cherokee backup camera screen may go blank low

Reported 2025-02-13 · Campaign 25V083000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

A small electrical part inside the big center touchscreen can get too hot, and when it does the backup camera picture doesn't come up on the screen. Backing up without that view makes it harder to see people or objects behind you, and federal rules require the image. Chrysler says dealers already replaced the display in every affected Jeep, so if yours was covered the work has been done.

Jeep Wagoneer and Grand Cherokee backup camera may not show medium

Reported 2024-12-19 · Campaign 24V944000 · Model years: 2024

Certain 2024 Jeep Wagoneer, Grand Wagoneer, Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L models have incorrect software in the rearview camera. Because of it, the camera picture may not appear on the screen when you shift into reverse. Without that view, you cannot see what is directly behind the vehicle while backing up. This also means these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility rule. Dealers will reprogram the camera module with corrected software at no charge to you.

Jeep Grand Cherokee loose radiator bolts risk airbag, fuel problems critical

Reported 2024-12-12 · Campaign 24V926000 · Model years: 2024

Bolts that hold the metal support bar for the radiator at the front of these SUVs may not be tightened properly. Because of that, in a crash the airbags may not deploy the way they should, and fuel can leak. Airbags that go off incorrectly can hurt the people inside, and leaking gasoline can catch fire. The vehicles fail two federal safety standards, one for crash protection and one for fuel system integrity. Dealers will check the bolts and tighten them free of charge.

Jeep Grand Cherokee side airbags may not deploy high

Reported 2024-11-27 · Campaign 24V897000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

A connector for the side airbags built into the front seats may not have been fastened properly at the factory. If that connection is loose, the side airbags might not inflate during a side-impact crash, leaving you without that protection. The airbags look normal and there is no warning you would notice while driving. The fix is simple — the dealer secures the connector at no cost. Chrysler says every affected vehicle has already been repaired.

Jeep Grand Cherokee speedometer may show wrong speed medium

Reported 2024-11-19 · Campaign 24V872000 · Model years: 2024

The screen behind the steering wheel runs on software, and an error in that software can make the speedometer show a speed that isn't your actual one. You could be traveling faster or slower than the number you see. Nothing is physically broken and the rest of the vehicle works normally, but the display fails the federal rule requiring accurate speed readings. A dealer checks the cluster and loads corrected software.
