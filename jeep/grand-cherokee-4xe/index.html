Jeep Grand Cherokee and Wagoneer Tire Warning Light May Fail low

Reported 2026-09-01 · Campaign 26V559000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

A software error means the tire pressure system may not spot a low tire, and the low-pressure warning light on your dash may not come on when it should. You could drive on an underinflated tire without knowing, which makes the vehicle harder to control and raises the chance of a crash. The fix is a free software update at the dealer. Owner letters are expected to start going out September 29, 2026; until then, check your tire pressures yourself with a gauge.

Jeep Grand Cherokee 4xe engine may fail or catch fire critical

Reported 2025-11-06 · Campaign 25V766000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Leftover debris inside the engine can damage it and make the engine quit. If that happens, the vehicle can lose power while you are driving, and in some cases a fire can start under the hood. Dealers will load a software update now, and will replace the engine block or the whole engine if it is needed. Chrysler has started mailing owner letters, and more letters will go out when the full fix is ready.

Jeep Wrangler and Grand Cherokee 4xe batteries can catch fire critical

Reported 2024-09-27 · Campaign 24V720000 · Model years: 2024

The large battery that powers these plug-in hybrid Jeeps can fail inside and catch fire, while you are driving or even while the vehicle sits parked. Chrysler tells owners to park outside, away from buildings and other cars, and to stop plugging the vehicle in to charge until it is fixed. Dealers update the battery software and replace the battery pack if needed. A newer recall has since replaced this one, so repaired vehicles need the new fix too.
