Jeep Compass recalls

11 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2023. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Chrysler, Jeep, Ram Rearview Camera May Not Display high

Reported 2024-06-13 · Campaign 24V436000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

A software issue in the radio can prevent the rearview camera image from displaying on the screen. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the requirements of the federal safety standard for rear visibility, FMVSS 111. To fix the problem, dealers will update the radio software, or the software update will be delivered over-the-air. This repair is free of charge for owners of the affected vehicles.

Jeep Compass Dashboard Display May Be Too Dim high

Reported 2023-08-14 · Campaign 23V576000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

On certain Jeep Compass vehicles, the brightness of the instrument panel display cannot be adjusted because of incorrect software in the body control module.

Jeep Compass Instrument Display May Be Invisible medium

Reported 2023-02-09 · Campaign 23V058000 · Model years: 2022

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Compass vehicles because the instrument panel's center cluster display may not be lit brightly enough. As a result, the display can be hard or impossible to see while driving during the day. Because drivers may not be able to see this display in daylight, these vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard covering vehicle controls and displays.

Jeep Compass Front Head Restraints Improperly Welded medium

Reported 2022-04-14 · Campaign 22V248000 · Model years: 2022

Some 2022 Jeep Compass vehicles have front seat head restraints that were not welded correctly. Because of this welding problem, the vehicles don't meet the federal safety standard that covers head restraints.

Ram 1500 and Jeep Compass windshield wipers may loosen high

Reported 2020-04-09 · Campaign 20V208000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Chrysler is recalling certain Ram 1500, Ram 1500 Classic and Jeep Compass vehicles. The arm or arms that hold the windshield wipers may come loose. If that happens, the wipers may not work properly, and the driver may not be able to see as well in some weather. Dealers will tighten the nuts that hold the wiper arms in place, free of charge.

Dodge and Jeep Vehicles Have Rear Brake Problem high

Reported 2018-08-09 · Campaign 18V523000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On these Dodge and Jeep vehicles, the pistons inside the rear brake calipers may not have enough protective coating. Without enough coating, gas pockets can form inside the caliper pistons. These gas pockets can reduce how well the rear brakes perform, meaning the rear brakes may not work as effectively as they should.

Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram Vehicles May Stall high

Reported 2018-08-09 · Campaign 18V524000 · Model years: 2018

A voltage regulator chip on the circuit board inside the powertrain control module may fail. If this chip fails, the vehicle may stall while it is being driven, or it may fail to start at all. To correct this problem, Chrysler dealers will replace the entire powertrain control module with a new one so the vehicle does not experience this stalling or no-start condition again.

Jeep Compass Front Control Arm Welding Defect high

Reported 2018-05-01 · Campaign 18V279000 · Model years: 2018

Certain 2018 Jeep Compass vehicles may have front lower control arms that were incorrectly welded. This welding problem can cause the control arm to separate from the vehicle.

Jeep Compass instrument panel light can't be dimmed medium

Reported 2017-11-21 · Campaign 17V740000 · Model years: 2018

Chrysler (FCA US) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Jeep Compass vehicles. The brightness of the backlighting on the instrument panel cluster cannot be adjusted, so it cannot be dimmed.

Jeep Compass passenger air bag has loose mounting nuts high

Reported 2017-11-21 · Campaign 17V732000 · Model years: 2018

Chrysler (FCA US) is recalling certain 2018 Jeep Compass vehicles. The passenger side air bag assembly may have inflator mounting nuts that are loose inside the module.

Jeep Cherokee, Compass Oil Pump May Fail high

Reported 2017-10-24 · Campaign 17V670000 · Model years: 2018

Some Jeep Cherokee and Compass vehicles equipped with 2.4-liter engines may have a cracked oil pump housing. A crack in this housing can cause the oil pump to fail. If the oil pump fails, the engine will stall while the vehicle is being driven.
