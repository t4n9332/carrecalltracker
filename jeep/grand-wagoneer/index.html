Jeep Grand Wagoneer recalls

13 recalls covering model years 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Jeep Wagoneer Roof Rack Crossbars May Come Loose medium

Reported 2026-09-10 · Campaign 26V584000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Wagoneer, Wagoneer L, Grand Wagoneer, and Grand Wagoneer L vehicles because the pivot bolts holding the roof rack crossbars may not have been tightened correctly. This could let the crossbars come loose and detach from the roof while the vehicle is being driven, rather than staying securely attached as intended.

Jeep tire pressure warning system may miss low tires medium

Reported 2026-09-01 · Campaign 26V559000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Grand Cherokee, Grand Cherokee L, Grand Cherokee 4xe, Wagoneer, Wagoneer L, Grand Wagoneer and Cherokee vehicles. A software error means the tire pressure monitoring system may not detect a tire that is low, or may not turn on the tire pressure warning light the way it should. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal standard for tire pressure monitoring systems. Dealers will update the software free of charge.

Jeep Wagoneer Window Trim Piece Can Detach medium

Reported 2025-09-25 · Campaign 25V642000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer SUVs. A trim piece next to the driver's and passenger's front windows, called the quarter trim, may not have been properly secured when the vehicle was built. Because it was not attached the correct way, this trim piece can come loose and detach from the vehicle.

Jeep Wagoneer door trim may come off the vehicle medium

Reported 2025-09-11 · Campaign 25V593000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Wagoneer and Jeep Grand Wagoneer vehicles. The trim on the driver and passenger doors may not have been installed properly when the vehicles were built, and that can allow the trim to come off the vehicle. Dealers will inspect the door trim and replace it if needed, at no cost to the owner. Owner notification letters about this recall were mailed on September 25, 2025.

Jeep SUVs: Rearview Camera Image May Not Show high

Reported 2024-12-19 · Campaign 24V944000 · Model years: 2024

Chrysler (FCA) is recalling certain 2024 Jeep Wagoneer, Grand Wagoneer, Grand Cherokee, and Grand Cherokee L vehicles. Incorrect software in the rearview camera module may cause the rearview camera image to not display when the driver puts the vehicle in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet federal rear visibility requirements.

Chrysler, Jeep, Ram Rearview Camera May Not Display high

Reported 2024-06-13 · Campaign 24V436000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

A software issue in the radio can prevent the rearview camera image from displaying on the screen. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the requirements of the federal safety standard for rear visibility, FMVSS 111. To fix the problem, dealers will update the radio software, or the software update will be delivered over-the-air. This repair is free of charge for owners of the affected vehicles.

Jeep, Ram, Chrysler Air Bag May Not Deploy high

Reported 2024-03-14 · Campaign 24V199000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Certain Jeep, Ram, and Chrysler vehicles may have a steering column control module that wasn't welded correctly. Because of this welding problem, the driver's air bag may not deploy during a crash the way it is supposed to. This affects a range of trucks and SUVs, including the Wrangler, Grand Cherokee, Gladiator, Wagoneer, Grand Wagoneer, Pacifica, and Voyager, meaning the driver may not get the protection the air bag is meant to provide.

Jeep Wagoneer Third-Row Seat Belt Won't Fasten medium

Reported 2023-10-26 · Campaign 23V716000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer SUVs. On some of these vehicles, people sitting in the third-row seats may not be able to properly reach or fasten their seat belt into the buckle. This means the seat belt may not go on correctly, even when someone is trying to buckle up.

Jeep Grand Cherokee, Wagoneer Rearview Camera May Fail high

Reported 2023-08-14 · Campaign 23V577000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

In certain Jeep Grand Cherokee, Grand Wagoneer, and Wagoneer vehicles, a software problem in the park assist module can prevent the rearview camera image from showing up on the screen when the vehicle is put into reverse. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet federal rear visibility requirements. When the camera image does not appear, the driver has less view of what is behind the vehicle while backing up.

Jeep Wagoneer pillar trim may block side curtain airbag high

Reported 2023-08-03 · Campaign 23V545000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Some Jeep Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer vehicles have interior trim at the top of the pillar between the front and rear doors that may not be fully seated. Loose trim can get in the way of the side curtain airbag when it inflates. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard meant to keep people from being thrown out of the vehicle in a crash. Dealers will reseat or replace the trim free of charge.

Jeep Wagoneer Rearview Camera May Not Display medium

Reported 2021-12-21 · Campaign 21V00A000 · Model years: 2022

In some Jeep Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer vehicles, a radio software problem may prevent the rearview camera image from appearing on the screen when you shift into reverse.

Jeep Wagoneer second-row seat may not lock upright medium

Reported 2021-11-23 · Campaign 21V919000 · Model years: 2022

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer SUVs. The pull strap used to recline the second-row center seat may catch on the seat trim, which can keep the seat back from locking in the upright position. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for seating systems. Dealers will inspect the strap and replace it if needed.

Jeep Grand Cherokee, Wagoneer Air Bags May Disable high

Reported 2021-11-10 · Campaign 21V873000 · Model years: 2022

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Grand Cherokee L, Wagoneer, and Grand Wagoneer vehicles. The computer that controls the air bags may have incorrect software installed. Because of this software problem, the driver's air bag, the front passenger's air bag, and the knee air bags could all become disabled without warning, meaning they would not be available to help protect occupants.
