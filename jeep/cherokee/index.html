Jeep Cherokee recalls

21 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Jeep Cherokee Power Transfer Unit May Fail high

Reported May 7, 2026 · Campaign 26V290000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

Some Jeep Cherokee vehicles have a power transfer unit that may fail. If it fails, the vehicle could lose the power that drives the wheels, or the vehicle could roll away on its own.

Jeep Cherokee Drivetrain Failure Can Cause Rollaway high

Reported Apr 29, 2025 · Campaign 25V011000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The part that sends engine power to the wheels can become damaged and disconnect the transmission and differential. This can cause the vehicle to lose drive power, or lose the ability to stay in park. Dealers will update the drivetrain control module and the power transfer unit's software, and replace the unit if needed, free of charge.

Jeep Cherokee Side Airbag Connector Not Secured high

Reported Jul 8, 2024 · Campaign 24V516000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

In certain Jeep Cherokee vehicles, the connector for the right-side curtain airbag may not be properly secured. This could keep the airbag from deploying the way it's supposed to.

Jeep Cherokee Turn Signals May Not Self-Cancel medium

Reported Mar 28, 2024 · Campaign 24V239000 · Model years: 2022

Certain 2022 Jeep Cherokee vehicles have a problem with their turn signals. The self-canceling feature, which is supposed to automatically turn the blinker off after you finish a turn, may not work correctly and could stay on. Because of this, the vehicle does not meet a federal safety standard covering lights, reflectors, and other signaling equipment.

Jeep Cherokee Transmission Hose Leak May Cause Fire critical

Reported May 4, 2021 · Campaign 21V310000 · Model years: 2021

Certain 2021 Jeep Cherokee vehicles may have a transmission oil cooler hose that was not cured correctly during manufacturing. This can allow transmission fluid to leak from the hose. Dealers will inspect the hose and replace it as needed to fix the problem.

Jeep Cherokee Front Lower Control Arm May Fracture critical

Reported Oct 27, 2020 · Campaign 20V657000 · Model years: 2021

On some Jeep Cherokee Latitude and Jeep Cherokee Limited vehicles, one or both of the front lower control arms may fracture while the vehicle is being driven.

Jeep, Ram and Dodge backup camera image stays on screen medium

Reported Sep 10, 2020 · Campaign 20V191000 · Model years: 2020

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep, Ram, Dodge and Chrysler vehicles with 8.4-inch or 12-inch radio screens. A software error can leave the backup camera picture on the screen after the driver has shifted out of reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule covering rear visibility. Dealers will update the radio display software. Owners can also choose to have the software updated remotely, over the air, instead of visiting a dealer.

Jeep Cherokee Steering Rack Gear May Break high

Reported Aug 29, 2019 · Campaign 19V637000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

The gear inside the steering rack could break, which may cause a loss of steering control. Dealers will replace the steering gear to fix this, free of charge.

Jeep Cherokee Crash Sensors May Cause Airbag Failure high

Reported Jun 13, 2019 · Campaign 19V449000 · Model years: 2019

Some Jeep Cherokee vehicles have a problem with the sensors that detect crashes for the airbag system. These sensors may fail, making it harder for the system to properly sense when a crash is happening. If that happens in an actual crash, the airbags may not deploy the way they are supposed to. Airbags that do not deploy correctly may not protect occupants as intended during a collision.

Jeep Cherokee Passenger Seat Mounting Bolts Loose high

Reported Mar 28, 2019 · Campaign 19V248000 · Model years: 2019

Certain 2019 Jeep Cherokee vehicles may have loose mounting bolts on the front passenger seat. Because the bolts are not properly tightened, the seat is not as securely attached to the vehicle floor as it should be.

Jeep Cherokee: Instrument Cluster May Fail high

Reported Dec 20, 2018 · Campaign 18V917000 · Model years: 2019

Some 2019 Jeep Cherokee vehicles have an instrument cluster that may fail. If it does, the warning chimes, messages, and gauges that give the driver information about the vehicle will not activate. Dealers will inspect the instrument cluster and replace it as necessary.

Jeep Cherokee Transmission Calibration May Cause Stalling high

Reported Oct 19, 2018 · Campaign 18V739000 · Model years: 2019

The transmission in these Jeep Cherokee vehicles may have been calibrated incorrectly. This improper calibration can cause the vehicle's engine to stall while the vehicle is being driven. A stalled engine increases the risk of a crash. To correct the problem, Chrysler dealers will reprogram the transmission control module with a new, corrected calibration, free of charge to the owner.

Dodge and Jeep Vehicles Have Rear Brake Problem high

Reported Aug 9, 2018 · Campaign 18V523000 · Model years: 2019

On these Dodge and Jeep vehicles, the pistons inside the rear brake calipers may not have enough protective coating. Without enough coating, gas pockets can form inside the caliper pistons. These gas pockets can reduce how well the rear brakes perform, meaning the rear brakes may not work as effectively as they should.

Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram Vehicles May Stall high

Reported Aug 9, 2018 · Campaign 18V524000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

A voltage regulator chip on the circuit board inside the powertrain control module may fail. If this chip fails, the vehicle may stall while it is being driven, or it may fail to start at all. To correct this problem, Chrysler dealers will replace the entire powertrain control module with a new one so the vehicle does not experience this stalling or no-start condition again.

Jeep Cherokee Halfshaft Bearing Failure May Cause Drive Loss high

Reported Jul 26, 2018 · Campaign 18V494000 · Model years: 2018

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Cherokee all-wheel-drive vehicles because the bearing cage in the right front halfshaft may not have been properly heat treated. This could cause the bearing cage to break and the halfshaft to fail. If the halfshaft cannot transmit power, the vehicle could lose the ability to drive, or it could move even when placed in Park.

Jeep Cherokee and Chrysler Pacifica Transmission Weld Risk high

Reported Jul 26, 2018 · Campaign 18V492000 · Model years: 2019

Chrysler is recalling certain 2019 Jeep Cherokee vehicles and 2018 Chrysler Pacifica non-hybrid vehicles because a component inside the transmission may not have been welded properly. If this weld fails, the transmission may not be able to send engine power to the wheels. That means the vehicle could stop moving while it is being driven, which raises the risk of a crash.

Jeep Cherokee 2.0L Engine Component Failure Risk high

Reported May 25, 2018 · Campaign 18V344000 · Model years: 2019

Some 2.0L engines in these Cherokees may be missing valve stem keepers, which can let a valve drop into the engine cylinder and cause engine damage. These engines may also have a reversed camshaft cap, which can damage the camshaft bearing and cause the camshaft to fail.

Dodge, Jeep, Ram and Chrysler cruise control may not cancel high

Reported May 17, 2018 · Campaign 18V332000 · Model years: 2018

Chrysler is recalling certain Dodge, Jeep, Ram and Chrysler vehicles from several model years because the cruise control may not shut off. If a wire inside the vehicle shorts out while cruise control is in use, the driver may not be able to cancel it by pressing the brake pedal or by switching the system off. The vehicle could keep going at its set speed or even speed up. Dealers will inspect and reprogram the engine or powertrain computer.

Jeep Cherokee Fuel Tube May Leak Fuel high

Reported May 1, 2018 · Campaign 18V282000 · Model years: 2018

Certain 2018 Jeep Cherokee vehicles with a 2.4L engine may have been built with a fuel tube that can leak fuel into the engine compartment.

Jeep Cherokee, Compass Oil Pump May Fail high

Reported Oct 24, 2017 · Campaign 17V670000 · Model years: 2018

Some Jeep Cherokee and Compass vehicles equipped with 2.4-liter engines may have a cracked oil pump housing. A crack in this housing can cause the oil pump to fail. If the oil pump fails, the engine will stall while the vehicle is being driven.

Jeep Cherokee Label Omits Correct Tire and Rim Size medium

Reported Aug 31, 2017 · Campaign 17V543000 · Model years: 2018

Jeep put a label on these vehicles that's supposed to list the correct tire and rim size, but that information was left off during manufacturing. Without it, someone could end up installing the wrong size tire and rim combination on the vehicle, which doesn't meet federal tire and rim safety labeling rules.

Jeep Cherokee recall questions

How many recalls does the Jeep Cherokee have?

NHTSA lists 21 safety recalls covering the 2018–2023 Jeep Cherokee. 18 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Jeep Cherokee Power Transfer Unit May Fail" (NHTSA campaign 26V290000), reported May 7, 2026.

Which Jeep Cherokee model year has the most recalls?

The 2019 Jeep Cherokee has the most, with 11 recalls. The 2023 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Jeep Cherokee?

No. None of the 21 Jeep Cherokee recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Jeep Cherokee has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Jeep dealer, including for second owners.
