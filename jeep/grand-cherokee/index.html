Jeep Grand Cherokee recalls

49 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Jeep Grand Cherokee rear springs can fall off critical

Reported 2026-09-01 · Campaign 26V562000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

The rear coil springs are the big metal springs that hold up the back of your Jeep and soak up bumps. On these vehicles they may have been installed incorrectly at the factory, and a spring can work loose and come off the vehicle while you are driving. Chrysler is replacing the rubber pads the springs sit on. The final fix is not ready yet, so owners are getting a warning letter first and a second letter later.

Jeep Grand Cherokee and Wagoneer Tire Warning Light May Fail low

Reported 2026-09-01 · Campaign 26V559000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

A software error means the tire pressure system may not spot a low tire, and the low-pressure warning light on your dash may not come on when it should. You could drive on an underinflated tire without knowing, which makes the vehicle harder to control and raises the chance of a crash. The fix is a free software update at the dealer. Owner letters are expected to start going out September 29, 2026; until then, check your tire pressures yourself with a gauge.

Jeep Grand Cherokee and Wagoneer gear display may vanish medium

Reported 2026-06-30 · Campaign 26V413000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

Certain Jeep Grand Cherokee, Grand Cherokee L, Wagoneer and Wagoneer L vehicles have a software error that can stop the gear display from showing. That is the readout that tells you whether the vehicle is in park, reverse, neutral or drive. Without it, you cannot confirm the vehicle is actually in park before you step out, and it may move when you do not expect it or roll away on its own. Dealers reprogram the shifter software for free.

Jeep Grand Cherokee side airbags may deploy too late high

Reported 2026-05-21 · Campaign 26V328000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

A software mistake in the computer that controls the airbags and seat belts can delay the side airbags during a crash. The airbags still go off, but not as quickly as they should, so they may not be fully in place when you need them. Because of this, these Jeeps do not meet the federal side-impact protection rule. The fix is a free software update at the dealer. Your VIN becomes searchable on NHTSA's website on May 28, 2026.

Reported 2026-01-29 · Campaign 26V051000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Jeep Wrangler and Grand Cherokee 4xe Battery Fire Risk critical

Reported 2025-10-30 · Campaign 25V741000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

The large high-voltage battery in these plug-in hybrid Jeeps can fail inside and catch fire. It can happen while you are driving or while the vehicle is parked. Vehicles already repaired under recalls 24V720 or 23V787 still need this new repair.
