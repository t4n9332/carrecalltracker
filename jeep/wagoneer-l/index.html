Jeep Grand Cherokee and Wagoneer Tire Warning Light May Fail low

Reported 2026-09-01 · Campaign 26V559000 · Model years: 2025

A software error means the tire pressure system may not spot a low tire, and the low-pressure warning light on your dash may not come on when it should. You could drive on an underinflated tire without knowing, which makes the vehicle harder to control and raises the chance of a crash. The fix is a free software update at the dealer. Owner letters are expected to start going out September 29, 2026; until then, check your tire pressures yourself with a gauge.

Jeep Grand Cherokee and Wagoneer gear display may vanish medium

Reported 2026-06-30 · Campaign 26V413000 · Model years: 2024

Certain Jeep Grand Cherokee, Grand Cherokee L, Wagoneer and Wagoneer L vehicles have a software error that can stop the gear display from showing. That is the readout that tells you whether the vehicle is in park, reverse, neutral or drive. Without it, you cannot confirm the vehicle is actually in park before you step out, and it may move when you do not expect it or roll away on its own. Dealers reprogram the shifter software for free.
