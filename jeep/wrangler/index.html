Jeep Wrangler recalls

36 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator Power Steering Wiring Fire Risk critical

Reported 2026-06-04 · Campaign 26V363000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

The wiring that feeds the electric power steering pump can overheat. That overheating can start a fire in the vehicle, and it can happen even when the Jeep is parked with the ignition switched off — so shutting it down does not make it safe. A dealer will inspect the wiring and the power steering pump and replace either part if needed, at no cost. Owner notification letters are expected to go out July 9, 2026.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Jeep Wrangler and Grand Cherokee 4xe Battery Fire Risk critical

Reported 2025-10-30 · Campaign 25V741000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

The large high-voltage battery in these plug-in hybrid Jeeps can fail inside and catch fire. It can happen while you are driving or while the vehicle is parked. Until it is fixed, Jeep says park outside away from buildings and do not plug the vehicle in to charge. A dealer will update the battery software and replace the battery if needed, free of charge. Vehicles already repaired under recalls 24V720 or 23V787 still need this new repair.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Jeep Wrangler wiring may disable camera and brake light medium

Reported 2024-09-12 · Campaign 24V676000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

The wiring for the rearview camera and the center brake light — the one mounted high on the back — can short out. When that happens, the camera picture will not show when you back up, and the center brake light will not light up when you slow down, so drivers behind you may not see that you are braking. Dealers replaced the wiring, and the spare tire carrier assembly if needed, free of charge. Chrysler says all affected vehicles were repaired by January 15, 2024.

Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator Dashboard Cluster Can Short Out medium

Reported 2024-09-05 · Campaign 24V652000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

The instrument cluster, the panel behind the steering wheel with your speedometer, gauges and warning lights, can short out inside and stop working. If it goes dark while you are driving, you will not see how fast you are going or any warning lights telling you something is wrong, which can lead to a crash. Because the problem is in the hardware, dealers replace the whole cluster, free of charge. Owner letters went out between October 2024 and January 2025, so the repair is available now.

Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator engine may stall unexpectedly high

Reported 2024-08-01 · Campaign 24V572000 · Model years: 2021

Certain 2021 Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator vehicles have a software problem in the computer that controls the engine and transmission. The software can cause the engine to stall, which means you suddenly lose power to the wheels while driving. These vehicles were already fixed once under an earlier recall, but that fix was not enough — they need the new repair too. A dealer will load updated software into the powertrain control module at no cost.

Jeep, Ram and Chrysler vehicles: driver airbag may not deploy high

Reported 2024-03-14 · Campaign 24V199000 · Model years: 2023

A part in the steering column may not have been welded correctly at the factory. That part carries the electrical signal to the driver's airbag in the steering wheel. If the weld is bad, the airbag may not inflate during a crash, so the driver would not get the protection it is supposed to give. A dealer will check the part and replace it if needed at no cost to you.

Jeep Wrangler and Grand Cherokee defroster may stop working medium

Reported 2024-02-15 · Campaign 24V111000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

A software mistake in one of the vehicle's computers can stop the windshield defroster and defogger from working. That is the system that clears fog and ice off the inside and outside of your windshield. If it quits on a cold or damp day, you may not be able to see clearly out the front. The vehicles also do not meet the federal safety rule for defrosters. Dealers will update the software free.

Jeep Wrangler plug-in hybrid battery can catch fire critical

Reported 2023-11-22 · Campaign 23V787000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

Your Wrangler is a plug-in hybrid, so it carries a large battery under the vehicle. On these vehicles that battery can break down inside itself and start a fire — either while you are driving or while the car sits parked and switched off. Chrysler says to stop plugging it in to charge, and to park it outdoors, away from your house, garage, carport or anything else that could burn, until a dealer fixes it. The dealer updates the battery software and replaces the battery pack if needed.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator backup camera may fail medium

Reported 2023-09-18 · Campaign 23V641000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

On these manual-transmission Wranglers and Gladiators, shifting into reverse may not bring up the backup camera picture and may not turn on the reverse lights. That means you can be backing up with no camera view on the screen and no lights to warn people behind you that you are moving. The vehicles do not meet the federal rules for rear visibility and lighting. Dealers will replace the gear position sensor free of charge. Letters were mailed November 16, 2023.

Jeep Wrangler spare tire blocks center brake light medium

Reported 2023-08-10 · Campaign 23V565000 · Model years: 2023

On these Wranglers, the spare tire mounted on the back can block the view of the brake light high in the center of the rear window. When you press the brake pedal, drivers behind you may not see that light come on, so they may not realize you are slowing or stopping. The vehicle does not meet the federal rule for vehicle lights. A dealer will replace the carrier that holds the spare tire, free of charge.

Jeep Wrangler Plug-In Hybrid Battery Fuse Can Overheat high

Reported 2023-04-27 · Campaign 23V303000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

The big battery pack that powers this plug-in hybrid was built with the wrong fasteners holding a large 200-amp fuse in place. That can make the fuse run too hot. Too much heat can make the fuse fail, and the vehicle can lose drive power while you are on the road. A dealer will inspect the fuse and replace it if needed, and also check the battery pack itself and replace it if it is damaged — all free.

Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator diesel fuel pump may fail high

Reported 2023-04-13 · Campaign 23V263000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

These vehicles have a 3.0-liter diesel engine with a high-pressure fuel pump that can break. If the pump fails, the engine can stall, meaning it can quit while you are driving and leave you without power in traffic. Dealers will replace the fuel pump, along with other fuel system parts if they are needed, free of charge. Chrysler mailed owner notification letters on October 17, 2023.

Jeep Wrangler frame stud can puncture fuel tank critical

Reported 2023-03-23 · Campaign 23V191000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

On certain 2020-2022 Jeep Wrangler vehicles, a metal stud on the frame sits close to the fuel tank. In a crash, that stud can punch a hole in the tank and let gasoline leak out. If leaking fuel reaches a hot surface or a spark, it can catch fire. Dealers will look at the frame and, if the stud is there, remove it and paint the area, all at no charge.

Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator manual clutch can overheat, catch fire critical

Reported 2023-02-23 · Campaign 23V116000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

These Jeep Wranglers and Gladiators have a manual transmission, and the clutch — the part that connects the engine to the gears when you press the left pedal — can get too hot and crack apart. Overheated clutch parts can start a fire. Broken pieces can also damage nearby parts, drop debris onto the road, or leave the vehicle unable to move under its own power. The dealer replaces the whole clutch assembly and updates the software, free.

Jeep Wrangler 4xe hybrids can shut off while driving high

Reported 2022-11-23 · Campaign 22V865000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

Your Jeep Wrangler 4xe is a plug-in hybrid. The computers that run the engine, transmission and hybrid system can stop talking to each other. When that happens, the vehicle shuts the engine down completely instead of letting you keep moving at reduced power. If it happens while you are driving, you lose the power that moves the vehicle. The repair is new software for three control computers so the vehicle drops into a limited limp-home mode instead of shutting off.

Jeep and Ram diesel fuel pump can fail high

Reported 2022-10-13 · Campaign 22V767000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

These trucks and SUVs have a 3.0-liter diesel engine with a high-pressure fuel pump that pushes fuel to the engine. That pump can fail. When it does, the engine stalls, which can leave you without power while you're driving — in traffic or at highway speed. Dealers will replace the pump free of charge and will also check the rest of the fuel system, replacing any other parts that need it. Owner letters were mailed in October 2023.

Jeep Wrangler plug-in hybrid battery fuse may overheat high

Reported 2022-10-13 · Campaign 22V768000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

These plug-in hybrid Wranglers have a large high-voltage battery pack. Inside the pack, a 200-amp fuse was fastened with the wrong hardware, and that can make the fuse build up too much heat. If the fuse fails from the heat, the vehicle can lose the power that drives the wheels. Dealers will check the fuse and replace it if needed, check the battery pack for damage and replace that if needed, all at no cost.

Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator front seat belts may fail high

Reported 2022-10-13 · Campaign 22V766000 · Model years: 2021

The front seat belts in these vehicles have a small part inside the belt reel that yanks the belt tight the instant a crash begins. On some vehicles that part was welded incorrectly at the factory, so the tightener may not work. The belt would still latch and hold you, but it would not pull snug the way it is designed to, so you could move farther forward in a crash. Dealers will replace the front belt reels.

Jeep Wrangler, Gladiator and Ram 1500 backup camera blanks medium

Reported 2022-08-25 · Campaign 22V638000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

The radio software in these trucks and SUVs can glitch so the backup camera picture never appears on the screen when you shift into reverse. You may see a blank screen instead of the view behind you. That does not meet the federal rule requiring rear visibility. A dealer fixes it by loading updated radio software — no parts needed. Chrysler mailed owner letters in October 2022, and the update costs nothing.

Jeep Wrangler side marker lights may not work medium

Reported 2022-05-12 · Campaign 22V326000 · Model years: 2022

The side marker lights - the small lights on the sides of your Jeep near the front and rear corners - may not light up. The wrong wiring harness was used in the tail light assembly, so the lights never get power. Federal rules require these lights to work. Without them, other drivers have a harder time seeing your Jeep from the side, especially at night or in bad weather. Dealers replace the tail light assemblies free of charge.

Jeep Wrangler fuel line may leak and burn critical

Reported 2021-08-26 · Campaign 21V665000 · Model years: 2020

A connector on the fuel supply line can crack and let gasoline leak out into the engine compartment. Fuel spilling near a hot engine can catch fire. This affects 2020 Wranglers built with the 2.0-liter engine. Dealers will inspect the fuel line and replace it if needed, at no cost. Chrysler mailed owner letters on September 10, 2021, and the recall number is Y10.

Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator manual clutch can overheat critical

Reported 2021-01-28 · Campaign 21V028000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021

Certain Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator models with a manual transmission can overheat the clutch. The pressure plate, the part that squeezes the clutch together when you let the pedal out, may get hot enough to crack and break apart. Broken pieces can damage nearby parts, drop debris onto the road, or leave the vehicle unable to move under its own power. The overheated clutch parts can also start a fire. Dealers add software that limits engine power when the clutch gets too hot.

Jeep and Ram front seat belts may not restrain high

Reported 2020-09-24 · Campaign 20V580000 · Model years: 2019

The front seat belts in these trucks and SUVs have a part called a retractor, which locks the belt in a crash and then controls how far you move forward. On the recalled vehicles, the retractor may lock but then fail to slow your forward movement properly. That means the belt would not hold you back as well as it should, and the people in the front seats could be hurt more seriously in a crash. Dealers will replace the front seat belt retractors.

Jeep, Ram, Chrysler backup camera image stays on screen low

Reported 2020-09-10 · Campaign 20V191000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

The software in your 8.4-inch or 12-inch radio screen has a bug. After you back up and shift out of reverse, the picture from the backup camera can stay on the screen instead of switching back to the normal display. That means you can be driving forward while a rear-facing picture is still showing, which can pull your eyes and attention off the road ahead. The fix is a software update for the radio screen.

Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator clutch can overheat and catch fire critical

Reported 2020-03-03 · Campaign 20V124000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

These Wrangler and Gladiator models with a manual transmission can overheat the clutch pressure plate, the part that clamps the clutch together when you shift gears. If it gets hot enough it can crack and break apart. That heat can start a fire. Broken pieces can also damage nearby parts, drop debris onto the road behind you, or leave the vehicle unable to move under its own power. A dealer reroutes a wire harness, inspects the clutch, and replaces parts as needed.

Jeep Wrangler front control arm may separate from axle critical

Reported 2020-01-24 · Campaign 20V042000 · Model years: 2020

On some 2020 Jeep Wranglers, a bracket on the left side of the front axle and the weld holding it may be in the wrong position. That bracket anchors the lower control arm, a key part that locates the front wheel. If the bad weld lets go, the control arm can come apart from the axle. Losing that connection while driving can cause a crash. Dealers will inspect the front axle and replace it if needed.

Jeep Wrangler driver seat belt buckle can break high

Reported 2019-09-26 · Campaign 19V680000 · Model years: 2018

This covers 2011-2018 Jeep Wranglers built with the steering wheel on the right side. The metal strap that holds the driver's seat belt buckle to the seat frame can crack and break away from the seat. If that happens, the driver's seat belt won't latch or hold you at all, so it can't do its job in a crash. Chrysler dealers will replace the driver's seat belt buckle for free. Repairs have been available since March 2020.

Jeep Wrangler roof tent cover may break off high

Reported 2019-04-23 · Campaign 19V320000 · Model years: 2018

Some 2018 Jeep Wranglers were sold with a roof top tent mounted on top. The hard top cover of that tent can crack or break apart, and a broken cover may come off the vehicle while you are driving. A cover that flies off turns into an obstacle in the road for you and for the drivers behind you, which could lead to a crash. A repair has not been finalized yet, so owners will be told once one is ready.

Jeep Wrangler and Ram 1500 steering wheel may detach critical

Reported 2019-03-14 · Campaign 19V201000 · Model years: 2019

A short shaft connects your steering wheel to the rest of the steering system. On these vehicles that shaft was machined incorrectly and can break. If it breaks, the steering wheel can come off in your hands and you would have no way to steer, with no warning beforehand. A dealer will inspect the steering column and replace it if needed, at no cost. This one is worth getting checked right away rather than waiting.

Jeep Wrangler right rear door may open unexpectedly high

Reported 2018-11-08 · Campaign 18V786000 · Model years: 2018

On some four-door Wranglers, the latch for the right rear passenger door may have been installed in the wrong spot. When it is out of position, the door may not close and lock into place the way it should. That means the door could pop open on its own, including while the vehicle is moving. Anyone sitting in that rear seat could be hurt if the door opens unexpectedly.

Jeep Wrangler track bar bracket welds may fail critical

Reported 2018-09-28 · Campaign 18V675000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The front track bar is a steel link that keeps the front axle centered under the truck. On these Wranglers, the brackets holding that bar to the frame may have been welded poorly. A bad weld can let the bracket break away from the frame, and when that happens the steering can suddenly stop working properly. Dealers will check the welds and fix the brackets if needed.

Jeep, Ram, Dodge, Chrysler engine computer may cause stalling high

Reported 2018-08-09 · Campaign 18V524000 · Model years: 2018

The computer that runs the engine has a small part inside, a voltage regulator chip, that can fail. When it does, the engine can shut off while you are driving, or the vehicle may not start at all. A stall in traffic leaves you without engine power and can put you in the path of other vehicles. Dealers will replace the whole engine computer at no charge.

Jeep Wrangler steering shaft weld may split apart critical

Reported 2018-05-25 · Campaign 18V343000 · Model years: 2018

On some 2018 Wranglers, a shaft in the steering column may have been welded badly at the factory. The bad weld can split open where the grooves are cut into the shaft. If that happens, the steering wheel can lose its center position and the front wheels may stop responding the way you expect when you turn. Dealers will look at the shaft and replace it if the weld is bad.

Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram cruise control may stick on high

Reported 2018-05-17 · Campaign 18V332000 · Model years: 2018

These vehicles have cruise control, which holds a set speed for you. A short circuit in the wiring can lock the cruise control on. If that happens, tapping the brake pedal or pressing the off button may not shut it off, and the vehicle can keep its speed or even speed up on its own. Chrysler tells owners to stop using cruise control until the free computer software update is done at a dealer.

Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, RAM may not stay in Park high

Reported 2018-05-01 · Campaign 18V280000 · Model years: 2018

The wrong part may have been installed inside the automatic transmission. It is a rod that locks the transmission when you move the shifter into Park. If the wrong rod is in there, the transmission may not actually lock, so the vehicle can roll on its own after you think you have parked it. That could hurt someone standing nearby or cause a crash. A dealer installs the correct rod for free.

Jeep Wrangler rear frame rail rust can loosen seat medium

Reported 2018-04-12 · Campaign 18V231000 · Model years: 2018

The metal rail that runs under the back of your Jeep can rust. If it rusts badly, the floor under the rear seat gets weaker and the left rear seat mount can work loose. A loose seat is not an obvious problem day to day, but in a crash the back seat could break free from the floor. Dealers check the rail and coat it inside and out with rust protection so the corrosion stops.
