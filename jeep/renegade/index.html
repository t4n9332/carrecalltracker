Jeep Renegade recalls

8 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Jeep Renegade may have wrong reverse light assembly medium

Reported 2024-12-10 · Campaign 24V918000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

The wrong reverse light assembly may have been installed in the rear bumper at the factory, so these vehicles do not meet the federal rules for lamps and reflectors. The result is that the backup lights may not light up the area behind the vehicle as well as they should. They also may not be noticeable enough to warn people walking behind you, or other drivers, that your Renegade is about to back up.

Jeep Renegade front airbag may not deploy in crash high

Reported 2024-07-03 · Campaign 24V510000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

A small switch in the front seat belt buckle may not be plugged in correctly. That switch tells the vehicle whether someone is buckled in, and the airbag system uses that information to decide how to fire. Because of the bad connection, the front seat airbag may not go off the way it should in a crash. Nothing warns you that the connection is wrong. A dealer will repair the connection at no cost.

Jeep, Ram, Chrysler backup camera image stays on screen low

Reported 2020-09-10 · Campaign 20V191000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

The software in your 8.4-inch or 12-inch radio screen has a bug. After you back up and shift out of reverse, the picture from the backup camera can stay on the screen instead of switching back to the normal display. That means you can be driving forward while a rear-facing picture is still showing, which can pull your eyes and attention off the road ahead. The fix is a software update for the radio screen.

Jeep Renegade and Fiat 500X rear brake caliper may crack high

Reported 2019-12-19 · Campaign 19V909000 · Model years: 2020

Some 2020 Jeep Renegade and Fiat 500X vehicles may have a cracked brake part at the right rear wheel. The caliper is the clamp that squeezes the brake pads against the wheel to slow the car down, and on these vehicles it may have been damaged when it was made. A cracked caliper can fail, and if it does, the vehicle takes longer to stop. Dealers will inspect the right rear caliper and replace it if there is a problem.

Jeep Renegade airbag computer set up incorrectly high

Reported 2019-06-11 · Campaign 19V438000 · Model years: 2019

Certain Jeep Renegade vehicles with the Upland trim package have a computer that decides when the airbags should fire in a crash. On these vehicles that computer may have been programmed with the wrong settings. Because of that, during a head-on crash the airbags may not go off the way they should. Airbags that deploy incorrectly do not protect people the way they were designed to, so someone in the vehicle could be hurt more badly.

Jeep Renegade software may switch off crash safety features high

Reported 2019-04-10 · Campaign 19V287000 · Model years: 2019

The computer that controls the airbags and other crash protection has the wrong software loaded. Because of that, it may switch off some of the safety features meant to protect people in a crash, so they may not work when they are needed. The same problem affects certain Fiat 500X models. Dealers will load the correct software into that control computer, and the work is free of charge.

Jeep Renegade fuel pump flaw can stall engine high

Reported 2018-10-17 · Campaign 18V731000 · Model years: 2018

A manufacturing mistake in the fuel pump can let fuel pressure drop when the engine is working hard — for example, climbing at high altitude or driving in very hot weather. If that happens the engine can stall, leaving you without power. This covers certain 2017-2018 Renegade 4X2 flex-fuel (E85) models with the 2.4-liter engine. Dealers replace the fuel pump free of charge. The recall began April 26, 2019.

Jeep, Ram, Dodge, Chrysler engine computer may cause stalling high

Reported 2018-08-09 · Campaign 18V524000 · Model years: 2018

The computer that runs the engine has a small part inside, a voltage regulator chip, that can fail. When it does, the engine can shut off while you are driving, or the vehicle may not start at all. A stall in traffic leaves you without engine power and can put you in the path of other vehicles. Dealers will replace the whole engine computer at no charge.
