Jeep Wagoneer S recalls

6 recalls covering model years 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Jeep Wagoneer S and Dodge Charger EV display may fail high

Reported 2026-04-23 · Campaign 26V262000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Wagoneer S and Dodge Charger electric vehicles. A software problem can make the display in front of the driver stop working, so the information it normally shows is not available. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet several federal safety standards, covering vehicle lamps, stability control, brakes, the tire pressure monitoring system and occupant crash protection.

Jeep Wagoneer S Liftgate Hinge Cover May Detach medium

Reported 2026-03-12 · Campaign 26V141000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

The liftgate is the hatch at the back of the vehicle, and the cover over its hinge is meant to clip into place. On the vehicles being recalled, that hinge cover may not have been clipped in properly and can come off the vehicle. Dealers will inspect the cover and will repair or replace it if it is not secured the way it should be.

Jeep And Ram Trailer Tow Module Failure high

Reported 2026-02-02 · Campaign 26V059000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

On these Jeep Wagoneer S, Ram, and Jeep Cherokee vehicles, the trailer tow module was not designed correctly. Because of this, the lights on a trailer you're towing may fail to come on, and the trailer's brakes may not work.

Dodge Charger EV, Jeep Wagoneer S May Roll Away high

Reported 2025-09-04 · Campaign 25V574000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

On these Dodge Charger and Jeep Wagoneer S electric vehicles, an incorrectly installed spring may cause the vehicle to lose its 'Park' function when the gear selector is placed in Park, which can lead to the vehicle rolling away.

Jeep Wagoneer S Recalled for Wrong Cross Beam high

Reported 2025-09-04 · Campaign 25V575000 · Model years: 2025

These Jeep Wagoneer S vehicles have the wrong support beam installed behind the dashboard. This beam is supposed to help protect people inside the vehicle if it's ever in a crash, but because the wrong one was used, it doesn't meet federal crash-protection standards. That means it may not do its job properly to protect occupants if a collision happens.

Jeep Wagoneer S Headlights May Not Aim Correctly medium

Reported 2025-03-20 · Campaign 25V174000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Chrysler says the headlights on certain Jeep Wagoneer S electric vehicles cannot be properly adjusted. The part used to adjust the headlights up and down is not accessible, and the part covering the side-to-side adjustment is not covered the way it should be. Because of this, the headlights may not be aimed correctly.
