Lincoln Aviator recalls

49 recalls covering model years 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Ford Explorer and Lincoln Aviator driver's seat reclines unexpectedly medium

Reported 2026-07-21 · Campaign 26V470000 · Model years: 2025

On these SUVs, the driver's seat can tip backward on its own when nobody is sitting in it — for example, after you unlock or start the vehicle remotely. If someone is riding or sitting in the second row behind that seat, the seat back can move into them and pin them against their own seat. Until replacement parts are ready, the dealer will switch off the seat feature that causes it, then replace the seat motor later.

More Ford Explorer and Lincoln Aviator second-row seats may move medium

Reported 2026-07-14 · Campaign 26V451000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

This is a wider version of an earlier recall and covers more model years. The button for the second-row outside easy-entry seats can stick or jam, so the seat may unlatch, tip, or slide when you do not expect it. A seat that moves on its own may not hold a passenger properly in a crash. Dealers will check the switch and replace the plastic ring around it if needed, free.

Ford Expedition, Explorer and Lincoln SUVs May Roll Away high

Reported 2026-06-24 · Campaign 26V402000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

The part inside the automatic transmission that locks the wheels when you shift into Park can catch while the vehicle is still moving. That can damage the parking system, so the vehicle may not stay put after you park it and could roll on its own. Ford will update the transmission computer software and inspect and replace any damaged parts. The fix is not ready yet; Ford expects it around April 2027 and will mail a second letter then.

Ford Explorer and Lincoln SUVs lose rearview camera medium

Reported 2026-03-19 · Campaign 26V165000 · Model years: 2025

A computer module that handles the camera images can restart itself over and over without warning. When it does, you lose the backup camera picture on your screen, along with driver-assist features: the pre-collision system, lane-keeping help, and blind-spot monitoring. Those features may not be working when you expect them, which makes it harder to spot a hazard around you. The fix is a free software update, done either at the dealer or sent to the vehicle over the air.

Reported 2026-03-17 · Campaign 26V159000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

The bolts that hold the bottom end of the second-row outside seat belts to the vehicle may not have been fastened properly. A seat belt held by a loose bolt may not keep a passenger in place during a crash, so the belt may not protect them the way it should. Dealers will inspect those lower anchor bolts on both outer second-row seats and repair or replace them as needed, at no charge.

Ford Escape, Explorer, Lincoln backup camera image may flip medium

Reported 2026-03-03 · Campaign 26V123000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

When you shift into reverse, the picture from the backup camera can show up flipped or upside down on the center screen. A reversed image makes it hard to tell what is actually behind you and which way to steer, so you could back into a person or an object.
