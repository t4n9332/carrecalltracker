Ford Escape and Lincoln Corsair plug-in hybrid battery fire risk critical

Reported 2026-02-17 · Campaign 26V091000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

A defect in one or more cells inside the large high-voltage battery can cause a short circuit and make the battery fail. If that happens while you are driving, the car can lose power to the wheels, and there is also a risk of fire. Ford's fix is a software update for the two computers that manage the battery and the hybrid system. Until it is done, Ford says to drive only in "Auto EV" mode and to limit how much you charge the battery.

Ford Escape, Lincoln Corsair plug-in hybrid battery may fail critical

Reported 2025-11-14 · Campaign 25V789000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

A flaw in how the high-voltage battery cells were made can cause a short circuit inside the battery pack. The battery can fail while you're driving, and the car can suddenly lose power. In some cases the failure can start a fire. Dealers will reprogram two computers that manage the battery and hybrid system. Until that's done, Ford says to drive only in "Auto EV" mode and to limit how much you charge the battery. Cars already fixed under the earlier recall still need this new repair.
