Lincoln MKT B-Pillar Door Trim Can Detach medium

Reported Dec 1, 2025 · Campaign 25V827000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On affected Lincoln MKT vehicles, the trim panel covering the pillar next to the driver's and front passenger's doors can come loose and detach while you are driving. Because this happens while the vehicle is in motion, a piece of trim landing on the road can become a hazard for you and other drivers nearby. Ford has already mailed an interim letter about this safety risk, with a full repair notice to follow once the fix is ready.

Ford and Lincoln rearview cameras may show blank image medium

Reported Oct 13, 2025 · Campaign 25V695000 · Model years: 2019

Ford is recalling a range of Ford and Lincoln vehicles, including the Fiesta, Flex, Explorer, Escape, C-Max, Fusion, Taurus, Mustang and the Lincoln MKT and MKZ. On these vehicles, the camera that shows what is behind you when you shift into reverse may show a picture that is distorted, that cuts in and out, or that is blank. Ford says dealers will check the camera and replace it if needed.

Ford, Lincoln Rearview Camera May Show Blank Image high

Reported Dec 20, 2024 · Campaign 24V951000 · Model years: 2019

Some Ford Flex, Ford Fiesta, and Lincoln MKT vehicles have a rearview camera that may sometimes show a blank or distorted picture instead of a clear view behind the vehicle. Ford has already sent owners an interim letter about this issue. Dealers will replace the rearview camera, and a second letter will be sent to owners once the final fix is available. This work will not cost owners anything.

Ford Flex, Taurus, Lincoln MKT: Rear Toe Link May Fracture high

Reported Feb 10, 2020 · Campaign 20V072000 · Model years: 2018

Ford is recalling certain Flex and Taurus vehicles, along with Lincoln MKT vehicles, that are equipped with the Police Interceptor or SHO Performance Pack. On these vehicles, a rear suspension part called a toe link may crack or break apart because of stress placed on the rear suspension. If this happens, the toe link could fail while the vehicle is being driven on the road.

Ford and Lincoln Power Cable Fire Risk critical

Reported Aug 20, 2018 · Campaign 18V548000 · Model years: 2019

The cables that carry power to the starter and the alternator may not have been properly secured when the vehicle was built. If these cables come loose, they can create an electrical arc, which is a spark jumping between two points.

Lincoln MKT recall questions

How many recalls does the Lincoln MKT have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2018–2019 Lincoln MKT. 3 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Lincoln MKT B-Pillar Door Trim Can Detach" (NHTSA campaign 25V827000), reported Dec 1, 2025.

Which Lincoln MKT model year has the most recalls?

The 2019 Lincoln MKT has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2018 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Lincoln MKT?

No. None of the 5 Lincoln MKT recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Lincoln MKT has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Lincoln dealer, including for second owners.
