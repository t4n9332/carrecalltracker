Ford and Lincoln Rearview Camera Image May Invert medium

Reported Mar 3, 2026 · Campaign 26V123000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

The image on the center display may flip or turn upside down, showing an incorrect rearview image when the vehicle is put into reverse. Because the rearview image is not displayed correctly, these vehicles do not meet federal safety standards for rear visibility. To fix the problem, Ford dealers will update the software in the accessory protocol interface module at no charge to the owner.

Ford, Lincoln Wiper Motor May Fail To Work medium

Reported Mar 3, 2026 · Campaign 26V117000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Ford is recalling certain Explorer, Escape, Lincoln Aviator, and Lincoln Corsair vehicles because the windshield wiper motor may fail. If the motor fails, the windshield wipers may stop working properly, which can make it harder for the driver to see the road. Dealers will inspect the front wiper motors and replace them if necessary, at no cost to the owner. A final repair is expected to be available in October 2026.

Lincoln Corsair Rear Lights May Fail From Water medium

Reported Oct 10, 2025 · Campaign 25V688000 · Model years: 2024

Water may leak into the left and right rear combination light assemblies on some Corsair SUVs. This can cause the reverse light, tail light, brake light, or rear turn signal to stop working.

Reported Oct 10, 2025 · Campaign 25V685000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

In certain Ford and Lincoln vehicles, the engine block heater can crack and develop a coolant leak. If the block heater is plugged in when this happens, it can short circuit.

Ford, Lincoln Fuel Injectors May Leak Fuel critical

Reported Aug 23, 2024 · Campaign 24V634000 · Model years: 2023

Ford is recalling these vehicles because the fuel injectors may have been assembled without O-ring support discs. Without these discs in place, the fuel injectors can develop a fuel leak. This affects certain Lincoln Corsair, Escape, Mustang, and Explorer vehicles built with this fuel injector assembly problem.

Lincoln Corsair recall questions

How many recalls does the Lincoln Corsair have?

NHTSA lists 30 safety recalls covering the 2020–2024 Lincoln Corsair. 17 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Ford and Lincoln Rearview Camera Image May Invert" (NHTSA campaign 26V123000), reported Mar 3, 2026.

Which Lincoln Corsair model year has the most recalls?

The 2021 Lincoln Corsair has the most, with 22 recalls. The 2024 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 4. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Lincoln Corsair?

No. None of the 30 Lincoln Corsair recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Lincoln Corsair has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Lincoln dealer, including for second owners.
