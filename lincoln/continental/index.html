Ford and Lincoln rearview cameras may show blank image medium

Reported Jun 27, 2025 · Campaign 25V442000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Ford is recalling certain Ford and Lincoln vehicles, including the Explorer, Escape, F-150, Expedition, Ranger, Mustang, Bronco Sport, Maverick, Transit and the Lincoln Aviator, Corsair, Navigator and Nautilus. A software problem can make the rearview camera show a blank picture when the vehicle is backing up. The picture can also stay on the screen after the driver has finished backing up. Dealers will update the rearview camera software at no cost. Letters were mailed to owners in September 2025.

Ford Super Duty and Lincoln Continental rearview camera fogs medium

Reported Aug 26, 2022 · Campaign 22V644000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

Ford is recalling certain Ford Super Duty F-250, F-350 and F-450 trucks and Lincoln Continental cars that have the 360-degree camera system. The anti-reflective coating on the camera lens can break down, and when that happens the rearview camera picture can look foggy or cloudy instead of clear. Ford says dealers will replace the rearview camera at no cost to the owner.

Lincoln Continental Doors May Open While Driving high

Reported Feb 11, 2019 · Campaign 19V077000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Ford is recalling certain Lincoln Continental vehicles because the motors in the door latches may not work properly. When a latch motor fails, the door latch may not fully engage, meaning the door is not securely closed even though it looks shut.

Lincoln Continental recall questions

How many recalls does the Lincoln Continental have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2018–2020 Lincoln Continental. 1 of them is rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Ford and Lincoln rearview cameras may show blank image" (NHTSA campaign 25V442000), reported Jun 27, 2025.

Which Lincoln Continental model year has the most recalls?

The 2019 Lincoln Continental has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2018 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Lincoln Continental?

No. None of the 3 Lincoln Continental recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Lincoln Continental has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Lincoln dealer, including for second owners.
