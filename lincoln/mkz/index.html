Ford and Lincoln rearview camera image may go blank medium

Reported 2025-06-27 · Campaign 25V442000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

A software error affects the backup camera. The screen may show a blank image when you shift into reverse, so you cannot see what is behind the vehicle. Or the camera picture may stay on the screen after you have shifted out of reverse, which can distract you while driving forward. Either situation raises the risk of a crash. A dealer will update the rearview camera software free of charge.

Ford Fusion and Lincoln MKZ front brake hoses can leak high

Reported 2023-03-10 · Campaign 23V162000 · Model years: 2018

The front brake hoses on certain Ford Fusion and Lincoln MKZ cars can split and leak brake fluid. As fluid escapes, the brake pedal goes farther down toward the floor and the car needs a longer distance to stop. A pedal that feels soft or sinks lower than usual is the warning sign to watch for. Ford dealers will replace both front brake hoses. Ford mailed letters to owners in June 2023 and the work is free.

Ford Fusion and Lincoln MKZ steering wheels may detach critical

Reported 2018-03-12 · Campaign 18V167000 · Model years: 2018

On these Fusion and MKZ cars, the large bolt that holds the steering wheel onto the steering column can work itself loose over time. If it backs out far enough, the steering wheel can come off in your hands while you are driving, leaving you with no way to steer the car. Ford dealers replace the original bolt with a longer one that holds the wheel securely. The repair costs you nothing, and Ford has been notifying owners by mail.
