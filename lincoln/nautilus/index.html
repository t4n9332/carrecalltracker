Lincoln Nautilus recalls

23 recalls covering model years 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Lincoln Nautilus lane-centering may miss hands-off-wheel driver warnings medium

Reported 2026-06-09 · Campaign 26V377000 · Model years: 2019

Your 2019 Nautilus has a system that helps keep the car centered in its lane. It is supposed to notice when you take your hands off the steering wheel for too long and warn you to put them back. On these vehicles, an earlier repair was not done correctly, so that check may not work properly. The car may fail to warn you. A dealer will reload the correct steering software.

Ford and Lincoln dashboard display can go blank medium

Reported 2026-06-09 · Campaign 26V372000 · Model years: 2019

When you start the vehicle, the main display behind the steering wheel — the one showing your speed and warning lights — may come up completely blank. These vehicles had an earlier recall repair for this, but it was not done correctly, so the problem can still happen. With a blank display you cannot see how fast you are going or whether any safety warning lights have come on. A dealer will reload the correct software into that display unit at no cost.

Ford Explorer and Lincoln SUVs lose rearview camera medium

Reported 2026-03-19 · Campaign 26V165000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

A computer module that handles the camera images can restart itself over and over without warning. When it does, you lose the backup camera picture on your screen, along with driver-assist features: the pre-collision system, lane-keeping help, and blind-spot monitoring. Those features may not be working when you expect them, which makes it harder to spot a hazard around you. The fix is a free software update, done either at the dealer or sent to the vehicle over the air.

Ford Explorer, Mustang, Lincoln Nautilus can lose driving power high

Reported 2026-03-03 · Campaign 26V122000 · Model years: 2025

A valve that routes some exhaust back into the engine can fail. When it does, the vehicle can suddenly lose the power that drives the wheels while you are on the road. That can leave you slowing down or coasting in traffic with other cars around you, with little warning. Dealers will replace the valve at no charge. Notification letters went out and you can look your vehicle up by VIN now.

Ford Mustang and Lincoln Nautilus headlights can fail high

Reported 2025-08-08 · Campaign 25V519000 · Model years: 2025

The small driver modules that run the LED headlights can contain a burnt diode. When that happens, the affected headlight can lose its low beam, high beam, daytime running light, front position light and front turn signal. On the Nautilus, the tail light, rear turn signal and rear position light on that side can go out as well. That means the vehicles do not meet the federal rule for lamps. Dealers inspect and replace the modules free.

Lincoln Nautilus power windows may not reverse when blocked medium

Reported 2025-08-08 · Campaign 25V518000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

These Nautilus SUVs were brought in for an earlier window recall, 24V953, and were repaired incorrectly. Power windows are supposed to sense something in the way — a hand, an arm, a child's fingers — and go back down. On these vehicles the window may not react correctly and can keep pressing with too much force before it retracts, so the vehicles do not meet the federal rule for power windows. A dealer reloads the door module software free.

Ford and Lincoln rearview camera image may go blank medium

Reported 2025-06-27 · Campaign 25V442000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

A software error affects the backup camera. The screen may show a blank image when you shift into reverse, so you cannot see what is behind the vehicle. Or the camera picture may stay on the screen after you have shifted out of reverse, which can distract you while driving forward. Either situation raises the risk of a crash. A dealer will update the rearview camera software free of charge.

Lincoln Nautilus screens can go blank while driving high

Reported 2025-05-23 · Campaign 25V337000 · Model years: 2024

A software problem in the multimedia system can make the big center screen and the display in front of the driver restart on their own and go blank. When that happens, you lose the speedometer and your warning lights, and the backup camera picture won't show when you shift into reverse. Ford dealers can update the software, or the fix can be sent to the vehicle as an over-the-air update, at no charge.

Ford and Lincoln engine block heaters can overheat critical

Reported 2025-05-23 · Campaign 25V343000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

These vehicles have an engine block heater, a device that warms the engine in cold weather while the car is plugged into an outlet. On the recalled vehicles, that heater can get too hot while the car sits plugged in, and the heat can start a fire. Ford will replace the heating element and check the cord, replacing it if needed. You can instead ask to have the heater element taken out, the opening plugged, and the cord removed.

Ford and Lincoln rearview cameras may freeze or blank medium

Reported 2025-05-09 · Campaign 25V315000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

A software error in the module that runs the screen can make the backup camera image lag, freeze, or not appear at all when you put the vehicle in reverse. You could back into something or someone you cannot see. The fix is a software update to that module, done free at a dealer or, on some vehicles, sent to the vehicle over the air. Ford is doing this in phases, so timing depends on your model and year.

Lincoln Nautilus power windows may not stop closing medium

Reported 2025-04-11 · Campaign 25V237000 · Model years: 2024

Certain 2024 Lincoln Nautilus and 2024 Ford Ranger vehicles were repaired incorrectly under an earlier recall, so they still have the original problem. When you close the windows using the one-touch global closing feature, a window may not automatically stop and go back down after it senses something in the way. These vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for power window systems. A dealer will reinstall updated software for the driver's door and passenger door control units.

Lincoln Nautilus: power windows may not reverse on obstruction medium

Reported 2024-12-20 · Campaign 24V953000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Power windows are supposed to stop and go back down if they bump into something while closing, such as a hand, an arm, or a child's fingers. On these vehicles that safety feature may not react correctly, and the window does not drop back down far enough afterward. A closing window could squeeze a person hard before it lets go. The dealer will reprogram the driver's and passenger's door control units at no charge.

Ford and Lincoln EcoBoost engines can break, losing power high

Reported 2024-08-23 · Campaign 24V635000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Some 2021-2022 Ford and Lincoln models with the 2.7L or 3.0L EcoBoost engine can have an intake valve break while you are driving. Those valves let air into the engine, and if one snaps, the engine can fail and stop making power. That means you could suddenly lose the ability to accelerate or hold speed in traffic. Dealers run a test on the engine and, if it fails the test, replace the whole engine at no charge.

Lincoln Nautilus windows may pinch before reversing when closing medium

Reported 2024-08-09 · Campaign 24V597000 · Model years: 2024

On certain 2024 Nautilus SUVs (and 2024 Rangers), the one-touch feature that closes all the windows at once may not work the way it should. Normally a window that bumps into something should stop and go back down. These windows may keep pressing before they reverse, so a hand, arm or a child's finger can get pinched hard. That does not meet the federal rule for power windows. A dealer will update the door software.

Lincoln Nautilus rear shocks may damage brake hoses high

Reported 2023-06-23 · Campaign 23V439000 · Model years: 2023

The rear shock absorbers on these SUVs may have been built incorrectly. A badly made shock can damage nearby parts, including the rubber hose that carries brake fluid to a rear wheel, the axle shaft, a wheel speed sensor, and the suspension links. If that brake hose gets damaged, your vehicle can take longer than normal to stop. Ford dealers will inspect these parts and replace the shocks, brake hoses, sensor, or axle shafts as needed at no cost.

Ford Edge and Lincoln Nautilus child seat anchor weak high

Reported 2023-03-24 · Campaign 23V198000 · Model years: 2023

The strap on the back of a child safety seat hooks onto an anchor built into the seat back. On the left-side second-row seat in these vehicles, the welds holding that anchor may be too weak. In a crash, the anchor could give way and the child seat would not hold the child as securely as it should. The dealer replaces that entire seat back frame at no charge.

Ford and Lincoln SUVs may lose rear axle lubricant high

Reported 2021-01-15 · Campaign 21V011000 · Model years: 2020

The rear axle unit on these vehicles may not have enough oil inside it. Running low on that lubricant can make the rear drive unit lock up. If it locks up while you are driving, the vehicle can lose power to the wheels and become hard to control. Dealers will check the oil level, top it up if it is low, and replace the whole unit if it is too low. All free of charge.

Ford and Lincoln backup camera image may go blank medium

Reported 2020-09-23 · Campaign 20V575000 · Model years: 2020

A poor electrical connection to the backup camera can make the screen go blank or show a fuzzy, distorted picture when you shift into reverse. It may happen on and off rather than all the time. If you cannot see what is behind you, it is easier to back into a person or an object. Federal rules require a working rear camera on these vehicles. Dealers will replace the camera at no charge.

Ford and Lincoln transmission leak creates fire risk critical

Reported 2020-09-10 · Campaign 20V550000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Bolts on a part of the transmission tied to the automatic start/stop feature may be loose or missing entirely. That lets transmission fluid leak out. As the fluid drains away the transmission can stop working, and if leaking fluid reaches a hot enough source it can start a fire. Ford is replacing that part on affected vehicles. A dealer will fit a new start/stop accumulator free of charge, and this repair is not one to put off.

Lincoln MKX and Nautilus power seats can damage airbag wiring high

Reported 2020-07-17 · Campaign 20V414000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

These vehicles have 16-way power seats that tilt. The edge of the seat cushion frame can rub against a bundle of wires underneath when someone uses the tilt function, and over time that can damage the wires. Those wires are connected to the airbag, so a damaged wire can make the airbag go off on its own while you are driving, or keep it from going off in a crash. Dealers will add protective tape and fix any damaged wiring.

Ford and Lincoln gauge display may go blank medium

Reported 2019-02-11 · Campaign 19V076000 · Model years: 2019

When you start the vehicle, the instrument display behind the steering wheel may come up completely blank instead of showing your gauges, which means it does not meet the federal rule for controls and displays. With a blank screen you cannot see how fast you are going, how much fuel is left, the engine temperature, or any warning lights from the safety systems. Driving without that information makes it easier to speed or to miss a problem the vehicle is trying to tell you about.

Lincoln Nautilus lane assist may miss hands-off driver medium

Reported 2019-01-14 · Campaign 19V031000 · Model years: 2019

This vehicle has a system that helps keep the car centered in its lane. It is supposed to notice when you take your hands off the wheel for too long and then stop helping. On these cars it may not notice, so it can keep steering for you while you are not paying attention to the road. Ford's fix is new software for the power steering computer, loaded by the dealer at no cost to you.

Lincoln Nautilus driver airbag cover may fly off high

Reported 2018-11-14 · Campaign 18V806000 · Model years: 2019

On these Lincoln Nautilus SUVs, the plastic cover over the driver's airbag — the panel in the middle of the steering wheel — may come loose when the airbag inflates in a crash. Instead of folding open the way it should, the cover can separate and hit the driver, causing injury on top of whatever the crash itself does. Only vehicles built without adaptive front steering are included. The dealer replaces the whole driver airbag unit.
