GMC Sierra 1500 recalls

30 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra roof airbag recall expanded high

Reported 2026-05-21 · Campaign 26V325000 · Model years: 2018

This covers more trucks with the same roof airbag problem as an earlier recall. The airbags mounted in the roof rails above the side windows have a gas canister whose end cap can detach, or whose sidewall can split open. If that happens, the compressed gas escapes and can launch the cap or metal fragments into the cab, where they can injure the people inside. Dealers will replace both roof rail airbag modules at no cost to you.

Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra roof airbag can rupture high

Reported 2026-03-19 · Campaign 26V166000 · Model years: 2018

Your truck has airbags built into the roof rails above the side windows. On these trucks, the end cap on the airbag's gas canister can come off, or the wall of the canister can split open. Either way, the compressed gas inside escapes and can throw the cap or pieces of metal into the cabin where people are sitting. That can seriously hurt someone. GM's fix is to replace the roof airbag units on both the left and right sides.

Silverado and Sierra: rear wheels can suddenly lock up critical

Reported 2026-02-12 · Campaign 26V083000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

These trucks were repaired under an earlier transmission recall, but that repair may not have been done correctly. A valve inside the automatic transmission can fail, and when it does the rear wheels can lock up while you are driving. Locked rear wheels can make the truck slide or spin and you could crash. The dealer will inspect your truck and install corrected transmission software at no charge. Owner letters went out on March 6, 2026.

Rollx Wheelchair Vans: Restraints May Not Lock Wheelchair high

Reported 2025-12-17 · Campaign 25V876000 · Model years: 2025

These are vans converted by Rollx for wheelchair use. The straps that hold a wheelchair use retractors, which work like a seat belt: they pull in slack and then lock so the chair cannot roll. On these vans, the QRT-Deluxe and QRT-Max retractors may fail to lock. If they do not lock, the wheelchair and the person in it can shift or slide while the van is moving, which raises the risk of injury. Rollx and the restraint maker will check the retractors and replace them if needed.

Chevrolet Silverado, GMC Sierra roof airbag may burst apart high

Reported 2025-06-26 · Campaign 25V432000 · Model years: 2018

The roof-rail airbags run along the ceiling above the doors. The metal canister that fills these airbags with gas can come apart: an end cap can detach, or the side of the canister can split open. If that happens, pressurized gas escapes and can throw the end cap or pieces of metal into the cabin toward the people inside. Dealers replace both the left and right roof airbag units at no cost to you.

Chevrolet, GMC and Cadillac 6.2L V8 engines may fail high

Reported 2025-04-24 · Campaign 25V274000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

These full-size trucks and SUVs have the 6.2-liter V8 gas engine. Some were built with faulty connecting rods or crankshafts — heavy internal parts that keep the engine turning. Those parts can break, damaging the engine badly enough that it quits. Dealers will inspect your engine and repair or replace it if needed. If it passes inspection, they will switch it to a thicker oil, put on a new oil filter and oil fill cap, and update your owner's manual. All free.

GMC Sierra 1500 chrome grille piece may fall off low

Reported 2025-02-06 · Campaign 25V060000 · Model years: 2022

Certain 2022 GMC Sierra 1500 trucks have a chrome deflector on the front grille. The clips and attachment points that hold this trim piece on can break, which lets the grille deflector come loose and fall off the truck. If that happens while driving, the loose piece becomes debris in the road that other drivers could hit or swerve to avoid. A dealer will reinforce or repair all of the attachment points so the piece stays put. Owner letters went out March 17, 2025.

Chevy, GMC and Cadillac diesels: rear wheels can lock critical

Reported 2024-10-24 · Campaign 24V797000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

A part inside the automatic transmission, called the control valve, can fail on these diesel-powered pickups and large SUVs. If it breaks, the rear wheels can suddenly lock up while you are driving, which can make the vehicle skid or spin. Your dealer will load new transmission computer software free of charge. That software also checks for a bad control valve, and GM has a special coverage program to pay for repairing transmissions found to have one.

Silverado and Sierra crew cabs: roof airbag can burst high

Reported 2024-10-10 · Campaign 24V756000 · Model years: 2018

The airbags that drop down from the roof along the side windows are inflated by a metal canister of compressed gas. On these trucks, the end cap can detach from that canister, or the canister wall can split open. If that happens, gas escapes suddenly and can throw the cap or other pieces into the cabin, injuring people inside or causing the driver to crash. The dealer will replace both the left and right roof airbag units at no cost to you.

Chevy, GMC and Cadillac trucks miss brake fluid warning high

Reported 2024-09-12 · Campaign 24V674000 · Model years: 2023

Your truck or SUV has a computer that watches the brake system. Because of a software problem, that computer may not turn on the dashboard warning light when brake fluid gets low. You could keep driving without knowing anything is wrong, and low fluid can make the brakes work less well. The fix is new software, sent to your vehicle over the air or installed by a dealer.

Buick, Cadillac, Chevy, GMC: Daytime Running Lights Stay On medium

Reported 2022-12-08 · Campaign 22V903000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

On these Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet and GMC vehicles, the daytime running lights can stay lit when you turn the headlights on. They are supposed to switch off at that point. Because they stay bright, they can create glare for other drivers and make it harder for people to see. This does not meet the federal rules for vehicle lamps. A dealer can update the software that controls the lights, or the fix can be sent to the vehicle wirelessly.

Silverado and Sierra sport bar blocks brake light medium

Reported 2022-06-30 · Campaign 22V463000 · Model years: 2022

Some of these trucks were fitted with an accessory sport bar. The brake light mounted on that bar may not work, and the bar can also block the truck's own high center brake light. Either way, drivers behind you may not get a clear signal when you brake, so the truck does not meet the federal lighting standard. A dealer will check how the bar was installed and correct the wiring if needed.

Chevy and GMC trucks have mismatched spare tire medium

Reported 2021-12-16 · Campaign 21V985000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

These Sierra 1500 and Silverado 1500 trucks were sold with add-on accessory wheels and tires, but the 17-inch spare tire that came with the truck does not match those accessory wheels. If you put that spare on, the difference can keep the anti-lock brakes from working the way they should in some situations, which affects how the truck stops and steers in a hard or slippery braking situation.

Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra tires may fail suddenly high

Reported 2021-06-03 · Campaign 21V423000 · Model years: 2019

Some tires on these trucks were left curing too long when they were made. Tires that were over-cured can break in the sidewall and lose all their air at once, or the tread can start separating and peel off the tire. Either problem can happen while you are driving and make the truck very hard to control. A dealer will inspect your tires and replace any that are affected.

GM SUVs and trucks may have defective Continental tires high

Reported 2021-02-25 · Campaign 21V115000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Some Continental tires on these vehicles were heated too long when they were made. A tire like that can crack in the sidewall and lose air all at once, or the tread can start peeling away from the tire. Either one can make the vehicle hard to control while you are driving. The dealer will look at your tires and replace any bad ones for free.

GM cars and trucks may have wrong seat belt bolts high

Reported 2020-12-23 · Campaign 20V811000 · Model years: 2021

Seat belts are bolted to the vehicle body and to the seats. On these vehicles, the wrong bolts may have been used at one or more of those spots, so a belt may not be attached securely. If a belt pulls loose in a crash, it may not hold the person wearing it, which can mean more serious injuries. Dealers will find and replace any wrong bolts at no charge.

Chevy and GMC trucks: center seat belt bracket unsecured high

Reported 2020-12-17 · Campaign 20V792000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

The front-row center seat belt has a bracket that bolts it to the seat frame. On these trucks and SUVs, that bracket may not have been fastened properly at the factory. If it comes loose, the belt may not hold a person in place during a crash, so someone sitting in the middle front seat could be hurt worse than they should be. Dealers will check both sides and put it together correctly for free.

GM trucks and SUVs may have faulty Continental tires high

Reported 2020-11-05 · Campaign 20V684000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

These Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet and GMC vehicles may have left the factory with Continental tires that were heated too long while being made. A tire made this way can crack in the sidewall and lose air suddenly, or the tread can start peeling away and come partly or completely off the tire. Either problem can happen while you are driving. Dealers will inspect your tires and replace any that match the specific tire codes Continental identified.

Chevy Silverado and GMC Sierra driveshaft may separate high

Reported 2020-10-22 · Campaign 20V650000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

One of the joints on the driveshaft may not have been welded correctly. The driveshaft is the spinning bar that carries power from the transmission to the wheels. A bad weld can let it come apart while the truck is moving, and the truck will suddenly stop pulling even though the engine keeps running. This covers certain 2019-2020 Silverado 1500 and Sierra 1500 trucks and 2020 Silverado 2500 trucks. Dealers will replace the driveshaft at no cost.

GM trucks and SUVs may lose brake power assist medium

Reported 2020-10-01 · Campaign 20V603000 · Model years: 2018

These trucks and SUVs use a pump to help you push the brake pedal down. Over time that pump can get weaker, so the pedal takes more foot pressure and the vehicle needs more room to come to a stop. The brakes still work, but they can feel hard and stopping takes longer than you expect. Dealers reprogram the computer that manages the brake system so it handles the weaker assist better. This repair has been available since November 2020.

GM Trucks and SUVs May Have Weak Roof Airbags medium

Reported 2020-07-30 · Campaign 20V446000 · Model years: 2020

The roof-rail airbags run along the edge of the ceiling above the side windows and are meant to protect your head in a side crash or rollover. On these vehicles, a piece that directs gas into the airbag may not have been crimped onto the inflator tightly enough and can come off when the airbag deploys. If that happens, the airbag may not protect you as well in a crash. Dealers replace the affected airbag units at no cost.

Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra Brake Caliper Bolts Can Break high

Reported 2020-02-27 · Campaign 20V116000 · Model years: 2020

The bolts that hold the brake calipers — the parts that squeeze the brake pads against the wheels — may have skipped a hardening step at the factory. A weak bolt can snap when you press the brake pedal hard. If that happens, the truck may not stop as well as it should, and a broken bolt can also get caught in the spinning wheel. Dealers replace the front and rear caliper bolts at no cost.

Chevy Silverado, GMC Sierra lose electronic brake assist high

Reported 2020-01-30 · Campaign 20V055000 · Model years: 2019

These trucks received a software update for the computer that manages the brakes, and that update contains an error. Because of it, the truck's electronic brake assist can be turned off. That system is part of the braking and stability controls, so with it disabled the truck no longer meets two federal safety standards. Losing brake assist makes a crash more likely. A dealer reloads corrected software into the module at no cost.

Chevy Silverado and GMC Sierra battery cable may stall truck critical

Reported 2019-12-12 · Campaign 19V888000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Some 2019-2020 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500 trucks were built with too much glue on the ring at the end of the main positive battery cable. That glue can keep the cable from making solid contact with the alternator, the part that charges the battery while you drive. The connection can come and go, which can shut the engine off while you're driving. It can also spark, and the heat from that sparking can start a fire.

Silverado, Sierra and CT6 can lose anti-lock brakes quietly high

Reported 2019-12-12 · Campaign 19V889000 · Model years: 2019

A software error in the brake control computer can shut off your anti-lock brakes — the system that keeps wheels from locking up in a hard stop — and stability control, which helps keep the vehicle from sliding in a turn. Normally a dash warning light would tell you. With this fault, the lights stay off, so you could be driving without either system and never know. A dealer reprograms the computer at no cost.

GM trucks and SUVs fuel pump leak fire risk critical

Reported 2019-11-21 · Campaign 19V837000 · Model years: 2020

Some of these vehicles left the factory with a fuel pump that is missing its pressure regulator — the part that keeps fuel pressure in check. Without it, pressure can build too high, crack the fuel pump, and let gasoline leak out. Leaking fuel near anything hot or sparking can start a fire. Your dealer will replace the fuel pump at no cost to you. This recall began back in December 2019, so a used vehicle you bought may still need it.

Chevy Silverado and GMC Sierra Seat Belt Fire Risk high

Reported 2019-11-14 · Campaign 19V814000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Certain Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra pickups with carpet floor covering have a problem with the front seat belt tensioners — the devices that yank the belt tight in a crash using a small charge of gas. Hot gas can escape through an opening in the tensioner's metal bracket and set the carpet on fire. That could start a fire inside the truck. Dealers will close off the opening in the bracket free of charge.

GM trucks and SUVs may suddenly pull to one side high

Reported 2019-10-24 · Campaign 19V761000 · Model years: 2018

On these 5.3-liter four-wheel-drive trucks and SUVs, a failed wheel speed sensor can fool the brake computer into braking one wheel by itself. It happens between about 41 and 60 mph in four-wheel or automatic mode, and it makes the vehicle pull toward one side with no warning to the driver. The repair is a free software update to the brake control computer at the dealer. Owners were notified starting in December 2019.

Chevrolet and GMC trucks, SUVs may lose brake assist high

Reported 2019-09-06 · Campaign 19V645000 · Model years: 2018

The pump that provides the suction your power brakes rely on can get weaker as the miles add up. As it fades, the brake pedal gets harder to push and the vehicle takes longer to stop. The brakes still work, but you have to press much harder. Dealers will reprogram the brake control computer at no charge. Repairs began October 8, 2019, under GM recall number N192268490.

Chevy Silverado and GMC Sierra passenger airbag may not inflate high

Reported 2018-12-06 · Campaign 18V868000 · Model years: 2019

The passenger-side front airbag may have been damaged while the truck was being built. Because of that damage, the airbag may not inflate the way it should if you are in a crash. Nothing looks or feels different from the driver's seat, so there is no warning sign to watch for. Your dealer will put in a new passenger airbag at no charge. This recall started in December 2018, so the fix is available now.
