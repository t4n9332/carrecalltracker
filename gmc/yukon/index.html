Cadillac, Chevrolet, GMC SUVs Have Wrong Wheel Bolts high

Reported May 14, 2026 · Campaign 26V304000 · Model years: 2025

Some 2025 and 2026 Cadillac Escalade, Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Tahoe, GMC Yukon, and Yukon XL vehicles with 24-inch wheels may have the wrong bolts installed on the left and right front wheel hubs. Over time, these incorrect bolts can loosen or become deformed. This affects how securely the front wheels are held onto the vehicle, since the bolts holding them in place are not the correct type for these hubs.

Chevrolet Tahoe, GMC Yukon Wheels Can Lock Up critical

Reported May 7, 2026 · Campaign 26V289000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

A part is missing from the transfer case, which is the part of your vehicle that sends power to the front and rear wheels. Because this part is missing, the front wheels, the rear wheels, or both can suddenly lock up while you are driving. This can happen with no warning to you as the driver, at any time you are on the road.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired.

Cadillac, Chevrolet, GMC SUVs Rear Wheel Lockup high

Reported Feb 13, 2026 · Campaign 26V085000 · Model years: 2022

On these Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC SUVs equipped with a 10-speed automatic transmission, the transmission control valve may fail. If the valve fails, it can cause the rear wheels to lock up while the vehicle is being driven.

Wheelchair Restraints in Various Vehicles May Not Lock high

Reported Dec 17, 2025 · Campaign 25V876000 · Model years: 2025

Certain vehicles equipped with QRT-Deluxe or QRT-Max wheelchair restraints may have retractors that fail to lock. If the retractor does not lock, a wheelchair inside the vehicle may not be properly secured.

Cadillac, Chevy, GMC SUVs Have Wrong Fuel Tank critical

Reported Sep 18, 2025 · Campaign 25V619000 · Model years: 2022, 2024

Certain Cadillac Escalade, Chevrolet Tahoe, and GMC Yukon vehicles with a 6-cylinder diesel engine may have had their original fuel tank replaced with the wrong one. If that happened, fuel could leak out of the tank if the vehicle is involved in a rollover crash.

GM Trucks and SUVs Engine May Fail high

Reported Apr 24, 2025 · Campaign 25V274000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Certain Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC trucks and SUVs with a 6.2L V8 gas engine may have manufacturing defects in the connecting rod and/or crankshaft. These defects can damage the engine and cause it to fail.

Diesel GM trucks, SUVs risk rear wheel lock-up high

Reported Oct 24, 2024 · Campaign 24V797000 · Model years: 2021

On certain diesel Chevrolet, GMC and Cadillac trucks and SUVs, the transmission control valve may fail. If it fails, the rear wheels can lock up while the vehicle is being driven.

GM SUVs, Trucks Brake Warning Light May Fail high

Reported Sep 12, 2024 · Campaign 24V674000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Certain Chevrolet, GMC, and Cadillac SUVs and trucks may have a software problem in the brake control module. This software is supposed to turn on a warning light when the brake fluid level gets too low, but it may fail to do so. Because of this, a driver might not realize the brake fluid is low, since the vehicle isn't giving them the warning it's designed to show.

Cadillac, Chevrolet and GMC SUVs Rear Suspension Bolts May Break high

Reported Sep 20, 2023 · Campaign 23V642000 · Model years: 2023

The bolts that attach the rear outer control arm to the suspension may not have been heat-treated correctly. This can cause the bolts to break.

GM Vehicles Daytime Running Lights Stay On medium

Reported Dec 8, 2022 · Campaign 22V903000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

In certain Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC vehicles, the daytime running lights may not turn off when the headlights are switched on. This can create glare and reduce visibility for other drivers.

GM SUVs: Daytime Running Lights May Not Turn Off high

Reported Nov 3, 2022 · Campaign 22V827000 · Model years: 2021

On some Cadillac Escalade and Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Tahoe and Suburban, and GMC Yukon and Yukon XL vehicles, the daytime running lights may not turn off when the headlights are on. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety lighting standard.

GM SUVs Third-Row Seat Belt Buckle Rivet Flaw high

Reported Aug 11, 2022 · Campaign 22V617000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

General Motors is recalling certain Cadillac Escalade, Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Suburban, Tahoe, GMC Yukon, and Yukon XL vehicles with third-row seats. The rivet that holds the buckle to its mounting bracket in the left or right third-row seat belt buckle may have been improperly formed. A seat belt with a deformed rivet may not properly restrain a passenger. Dealers will inspect the rivet on both third-row seat belt buckles and replace the buckle assemblies if needed, at no cost.

Cadillac, Chevrolet, GMC SUV Driveshaft May Fail high

Reported Jan 27, 2022 · Campaign 22V036000 · Model years: 2021

General Motors is recalling certain GMC Yukon, Yukon XL, Cadillac Escalade, Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Suburban, and Chevrolet Tahoe SUVs. The rear driveshaft assembly may have internal components that were not heat-treated properly during manufacturing. This improper heat treatment can cause the driveshaft to fail.

Escalade, Tahoe, Yukon Fuel Pump May Stall Engine high

Reported Sep 23, 2021 · Campaign 21V739000 · Model years: 2021

On certain Cadillac Escalade, Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Suburban, Tahoe, GMC Yukon, and Yukon XL SUVs, a fuel pump control module problem can make the fuel pump fail or work on and off. This can interrupt the flow of fuel to the engine and cause it to stall unexpectedly while driving. Dealers will replace the fuel pump power control module at no cost to you.

Chevy Tahoe, GMC Yukon Brake Calibration Error high

Reported Jul 29, 2021 · Campaign 21V579000 · Model years: 2021

Some dealers may have set up the brake system's control module incorrectly on these vehicles, which can affect stability control, traction control, anti-lock brakes, and braking while towing a trailer. Dealers will recalibrate the brake system control module to correct this, free of charge.

GM SUVs: Software Issue May Cut Power Steering high

Reported Jun 17, 2021 · Campaign 21V455000 · Model years: 2021

A software problem in these vehicles can cause the power steering assist to stop working, either when starting the vehicle or while driving. This makes the steering wheel harder to turn, especially at low speeds. GM will fix this with a software update, done either by a dealer or automatically over-the-air, at no cost to owners.

GM vehicles' airbag warning light works inconsistently medium

Reported Jun 10, 2021 · Campaign 21V421000 · Model years: 2021

General Motors is recalling certain Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet and GMC vehicles. The module that passes messages between vehicle systems does not correctly handle a loss of communication with the airbag sensing module. As a result, the airbag warning light on the dashboard turns on inconsistently. A dealer can update that module's software, or the update can be sent to the vehicle over the air, free of charge. GM mailed owner notification letters on July 1, 2021.

Escalade, Tahoe, Suburban, Yukon Third-Row Seat Belts May Break high

Reported Mar 18, 2021 · Campaign 21V190000 · Model years: 2021

General Motors is recalling certain full-size SUVs with third-row seating, including the Cadillac Escalade and Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Tahoe and Suburban, and GMC Yukon and Yukon XL. The seat belts for the outer third-row seats may have been caught or routed the wrong way behind the seat-folding mechanism, which can damage the belts. Dealers will inspect these seat belts, replace any that are damaged, and reroute them if needed, at no cost to the owner.

GM Vehicles Recalled for Over-Cured Tire Failure Risk critical

Reported Feb 25, 2021 · Campaign 21V115000 · Model years: 2020

The tires on these vehicles were left in the curing process for too long during manufacturing. Tires that are cured too long can develop a break in the sidewall, which can cause sudden air loss, or the tire's belt edge can separate, causing the tread or belt to come apart. Either problem can cause the driver to lose control of the vehicle.

Chevrolet Tahoe, GMC Yukon fuel tank leak risk high

Reported Feb 11, 2021 · Campaign 21V064000 · Model years: 2021

Some of these SUVs have fuel tanks where the glue between two layers of the tank shell may be missing. This can let fuel leak out of the tank. If that fuel leak happens near something that could ignite it, it raises the chance of a fire.

GM Vehicles Seat Belt May Use Wrong Bolts high

Reported Dec 23, 2020 · Campaign 20V811000 · Model years: 2021

Incorrect bolts may have been used at one or more places where the seat belts attach to these vehicles. If the wrong bolt was used at any of these points, the seat belt may not be properly attached to the vehicle. GM dealers will inspect the fastening locations and replace any bolts that are found to be incorrect, so the seat belts are properly secured to the vehicle as they should be.

Suburban, Tahoe, Yukon Front Seat Bolt Issue high

Reported Nov 25, 2020 · Campaign 20V735000 · Model years: 2021

On some Suburban, Tahoe, Yukon, and Yukon XL vehicles, a repair during assembly involved removing one or both front seats. The bolts that attach the seat afterward may not have been properly reinstalled. If a seat has loose or missing bolts, it could move out of position during a crash. Dealers will inspect the front seat attachments and install new bolts as needed, free of charge.

GM Trucks And SUVs Lose Brake Power Assist medium

Reported Oct 1, 2020 · Campaign 20V603000 · Model years: 2018

On these Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC trucks and SUVs with a 5.3L or 6.2L V8 engine, the mechanical vacuum pump that helps power the brakes can lose output over time. As the pump weakens, the vehicle gets less power assist for braking, so it takes more pedal effort and a longer distance to stop.

GM Trucks and SUVs: Fuel Pump Over-Pressurization Fire Risk high

Reported Nov 21, 2019 · Campaign 19V837000 · Model years: 2020

The fuel pump in these GM trucks and SUVs may be missing a part called a pressure regulator. Without it, the fuel system can build up too much pressure. This extra pressure can crack the fuel pump, allowing fuel to leak out. If that leaked fuel comes near something that can ignite it, it raises the chance of a fire starting in the vehicle.

Chevrolet, GMC trucks and SUVs may brake and pull unexpectedly high

Reported Oct 24, 2019 · Campaign 19V761000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Suburban, Tahoe and Silverado 1500 and GMC Yukon and Sierra 1500 vehicles built with a 5.3-liter engine, a 3.08-ratio rear axle and four-wheel drive. If a wheel-speed sensor fails while the vehicle is traveling between 41 and 60 mph in four-wheel or automatic mode, the software in the electronic brake control module may turn on the driveline-protection system when it should not.

GM Trucks and SUVs Brake Assist May Weaken high

Reported Sep 6, 2019 · Campaign 19V645000 · Model years: 2018

In some Silverado, Tahoe, Sierra and Yukon trucks and SUVs, the vacuum pump that helps power the brakes may lose vacuum over time. As the vacuum drops, braking takes more effort and the vehicle needs a longer distance to stop.

GMC Yukon recall questions

How many recalls does the GMC Yukon have?

NHTSA lists 26 safety recalls covering the 2018–2025 GMC Yukon. 23 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Cadillac, Chevrolet, GMC SUVs Have Wrong Wheel Bolts" (NHTSA campaign 26V304000), reported May 14, 2026.

Which GMC Yukon model year has the most recalls?

The 2021 GMC Yukon has the most, with 13 recalls. The 2019 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the GMC Yukon?

Yes. 1 GMC Yukon recall carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Chevrolet Tahoe, GMC Yukon Wheels Can Lock Up (26V289000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my GMC Yukon has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised GMC dealer, including for second owners.
