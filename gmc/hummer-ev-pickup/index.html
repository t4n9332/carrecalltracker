Cadillac Escalade IQ and GMC Hummer EV battery fire risk critical

Reported 2026-07-30 · Campaign 26V494000 · Model years: 2022

A part inside the big battery pack that powers these electric vehicles may not have been fastened down properly. If it comes loose, it can shift around and damage the battery, and a damaged battery can catch fire. You cannot see or hear this problem from the driver's seat, so there is no warning sign to watch for. Your dealer will replace the entire high-voltage battery pack at no cost to you.
