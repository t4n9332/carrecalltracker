Chevy Silverado EV, GMC Sierra EV Seat Belt Bolt high

Reported Jan 16, 2025 · Campaign 25V015000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

On some of these electric pickup trucks, the bolt that holds the rear center seat belt to the seat structure may not have been tightened enough during assembly. Because of this, the bolt securing that seat belt to the seat frame could be looser than it is supposed to be, instead of being properly fastened in place. That means the seat belt itself may not stay as firmly attached as it should.

GMC Sierra EV Front Turn Signals Too Dim low

Reported Oct 10, 2024 · Campaign 24V755000 · Model years: 2024

On some Sierra EV trucks, the front turn signal lights and the parking lights are too close in brightness. This makes it hard for other drivers to tell when your turn signal is actually flashing.

GM Electric Vehicles May Lose Drive Power high

Reported May 9, 2024 · Campaign 24V320000 · Model years: 2024

In some 2024 Cadillac Lyriq, Chevrolet Blazer EV, Chevrolet Silverado EV, GMC Hummer EV, and GMC Sierra EV vehicles, the electric motors in the rear drive unit may have wires that aren't insulated well enough. If those wires touch each other, the vehicle can lose the power that drives it.

GMC Sierra EV recall questions

How many recalls does the GMC Sierra EV have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2024–2025 GMC Sierra EV. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Chevy Silverado EV, GMC Sierra EV Seat Belt Bolt" (NHTSA campaign 25V015000), reported Jan 16, 2025.

Which GMC Sierra EV model year has the most recalls?

The 2024 GMC Sierra EV has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2025 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the GMC Sierra EV?

No. None of the 3 GMC Sierra EV recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my GMC Sierra EV has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised GMC dealer, including for second owners.
