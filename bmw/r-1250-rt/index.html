BMW Motorcycle Front Strut Failure Risk high

Reported Feb 29, 2024 · Campaign 24V165000 · Model years: 2024

BMW is recalling certain R 1250RT, K 1600GT, K 1600GTL, and K 1600B motorcycles because the front spring strut may contain an incorrectly manufactured proportional valve. This valve can loosen, and when it does, the strut itself can fail. If the strut fails, the motorcycle can become unstable while it is being ridden, affecting how well the rider can keep the motorcycle steady and balanced.

BMW Motorcycle Gearbox Shaft May Break high

Reported Jan 18, 2023 · Campaign 23V011000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

In certain engine and riding conditions, a shaft inside the motorcycle's gearbox can become damaged or break. If this happens, the rear wheel could become blocked, meaning it stops turning freely while the motorcycle is moving.

BMW motorcycle tire pressure labels may fall off medium

Reported Aug 20, 2020 · Campaign 20V495000 · Model years: 2021

Certain BMW R 1250 motorcycles have a tire pressure label that may not stay attached to the motorcycle frame. If the label comes off, riders may not have the correct tire pressure information readily available when they need it.

BMW Motorcycle Front Brake Fluid Leak high

Reported Aug 13, 2020 · Campaign 20V476000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On these BMW motorcycles, the front brake calipers can leak brake fluid while the bike is parked. Over time, this leak can lower the amount of fluid in the brake fluid reservoir. If the fluid level gets too low, it can affect how well the front brakes work.

BMW R 1250 Motorcycles Have Unclear Brake Fluid Cap high

Reported Mar 19, 2020 · Campaign 20V166000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Certain BMW R 1250 GS, GS Adventure, R, and RT motorcycles have a brake fluid reservoir cap that may not clearly show the type of brake fluid needed or how to fill the reservoir correctly. Without that information, an owner or mechanic could use the wrong fluid or fill it incorrectly during maintenance.

BMW Aftermarket Brake Fluid Cap Missing Fluid Info high

Reported Mar 19, 2020 · Campaign 20E013000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

This recall covers certain aftermarket brake fluid reservoir covers sold for BMW R 1250 and BMW RnineT motorcycles. The covers may not clearly show the type of brake fluid required or explain how to fill the reservoir correctly. Without that information, an owner or mechanic could use the wrong fluid or fill the reservoir incorrectly during maintenance.

BMW R1250GS/RT Final Drive Pivot Pin May Loosen high

Reported Mar 19, 2020 · Campaign 20V165000 · Model years: 2020

BMW is recalling certain R1250GS and R1250RT motorcycles. These motorcycles have a pin that connects the final drive to the swing arm. On affected bikes, this pivot pin may be damaged, and if it is, the connection between the final drive and the swing arm can become loose. This recall was issued because that pin needs to be replaced before it causes a problem with the connection.

BMW R 1250 RT recall questions

How many recalls does the BMW R 1250 RT have?

NHTSA lists 7 safety recalls covering the 2019–2024 BMW R 1250 RT. 6 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "BMW Motorcycle Front Strut Failure Risk" (NHTSA campaign 24V165000), reported Feb 29, 2024.

Which BMW R 1250 RT model year has the most recalls?

The 2020 BMW R 1250 RT has the most, with 5 recalls. The 2022 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the BMW R 1250 RT?

No. None of the 7 BMW R 1250 RT recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my BMW R 1250 RT has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised BMW dealer, including for second owners.
