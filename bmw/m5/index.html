BMW X3, X4 and M5 rearview camera image partly blocked medium

Reported Sep 30, 2020 · Campaign 20V598000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

BMW is recalling certain X3, X4, 5 Series and M5 vehicles, including the X3 sDrive 30i and xDrive 30i, X3M 40i, X3 xDrive 30e, X4 xDrive 30i, X4M 40i, 530i, 540i, 540i xDrive, M550i xDrive, M5 and 550e models. A small part of the picture shown by the rearview camera may be slightly blocked. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard covering rear visibility.

BMW M5 And M8 Transmission Wiring May Short high

Reported Dec 11, 2019 · Campaign 19V883000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On these BMW M5 and M8 models, the wiring harness that connects to the transmission can become damaged over time. When the wiring is damaged, it can cause an electrical short circuit inside the system. That short circuit can make the transmission shift into neutral by itself, without the driver doing anything, which means the vehicle suddenly loses the power it needs to keep moving forward.

BMW, Toyota Supra backup camera image can vanish high

Reported Sep 27, 2019 · Campaign 19V684000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

On certain BMW, Rolls-Royce and Toyota Supra models, the backup camera and display settings can be adjusted so the rear-view image no longer shows on the screen. Once set this way, the system keeps that setting the next time the vehicle is put in reverse, so no camera image appears.

BMW Fuel Pump May Crack and Leak Fuel critical

Reported Jul 3, 2019 · Campaign 19V513000 · Model years: 2019

On these BMW vehicles, the high-pressure fuel pump may not have been properly attached to the engine. Over time, this can cause the fuel pump flange to crack. A cracked flange can allow fuel to leak from the fuel system.

BMW M5 Fuel Gauge May Show Incorrect Level high

Reported Jul 17, 2018 · Campaign 18V473000 · Model years: 2018

The fuel level sensor inside the fuel tank may get stuck against a fuel line. When this happens, the fuel gauge can show an inaccurate reading, possibly showing more fuel than is actually in the tank.

BMW M5 Software Fault May Stop the Fuel Pump high

Reported May 16, 2018 · Campaign 18V323000 · Model years: 2018

In certain driving conditions, a problem with the engine control unit's software may cause the fuel pump to stop working.
