BMW, Mini Integrated Brake System May Malfunction high

Reported Jul 1, 2026 · Campaign 26V422000 · Model years: 2024

The integrated brake system in these vehicles may malfunction. This can cause a loss of power brake assist, which affects how the brakes work. It can also cause the Antilock Brake System and the Dynamic Stability Control system to stop functioning properly. To fix this, the integrated brake system will be inspected and, if necessary, replaced, free of charge for owners.

BMW, Toyota Supra: Engine Starter May Catch Fire critical

Reported Jan 30, 2026 · Campaign 26V056000 · Model years: 2024

On affected vehicles, the engine starter may overheat and catch fire due to wear on an internal component.

BMW Brake System May Lose Power Assist high

Reported Oct 2, 2024 · Campaign 24V739000 · Model years: 2024

Certain BMW models with an integrated brake system may have the system malfunction. This can cause a loss of power assist for the brakes, meaning it takes more pedal effort and distance to stop the vehicle. The malfunction can also stop the Antilock Brake System and Dynamic Stability Control from working properly, which can affect the driver's ability to keep control of the vehicle.

BMW and MINI Brake System Weld May Fail high

Reported Sep 18, 2024 · Campaign 24V697000 · Model years: 2024

The welds inside the servomotor of the integrated brake control module may break. If this happens, the vehicle can lose power assist for the brakes and lose function in the rear brakes. The antilock brake system and the dynamic stability control system may also stop working properly. To fix the problem, BMW dealers will replace the entire integrated brake system with a new one.

BMW, Mini Brake System May Lose Power Assist high

Reported Feb 12, 2024 · Campaign 24V104000 · Model years: 2024

The integrated brake system in these vehicles may malfunction. This can cause a loss of power brake assist, which affects how the brakes work. It can also cause the Antilock Brake System and the Dynamic Stability Control system to stop functioning properly. To fix this, dealers will replace the integrated brake system, and this repair is free of charge for owners.

BMW 530i and i5 steering wheel detection may fail medium

Reported Oct 18, 2023 · Campaign 23V700000 · Model years: 2024

BMW is recalling certain 530i and i5 models, along with other 7 Series and i7 models. The ground wire for the steering wheel may not have been attached correctly. Because of that, the system that senses whether the driver's hands are on the steering wheel may not work. Dealers will check the ground connection and reattach it if needed. Owner notification letters have been mailed. The repair is free.
