BMW motorcycles and scooter: brake calipers can corrode high

Reported 2019-08-14 · Campaign 19V593000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On these BMW motorcycles and the C 400 X scooter, the pistons inside the front and rear brake calipers can rust. A rusty piston can stick or drag instead of moving freely, which changes how well the brakes work. Your dealer will replace both the front and rear brake calipers at no charge. The repair has been available since March 2020, so if your bike has never had it done, it is worth calling BMW to check.

BMW G310R and G310GS side stand may damage frame medium

Reported 2018-06-15 · Campaign 18V408000 · Model years: 2018

BMW is recalling certain 2017-2018 G310R and 2018 G310GS motorcycles. Using and leaning on the side stand over and over can damage the stand itself and the part of the frame it attaches to. If that happens, the motorcycle can tip over on its own while it is parked and standing still, which can injure you or someone standing nearby. Dealers will inspect the frame and install a reinforcement plate and a new side stand, or replace the frame.
