BMW K 1600 Motorcycles Face Transmission Gear Risk high

Reported Oct 1, 2019 · Campaign 19V692000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Some BMW K 1600 GT, GTL, and B motorcycles have transmission parts that may not have been made to the correct hardness level. This could affect how the transmission works, sometimes causing two gears to engage at the same time or causing damage to transmission parts.
