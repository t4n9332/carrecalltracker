BMW, Mini Integrated Brake System May Malfunction high

Reported Jul 1, 2026 · Campaign 26V422000 · Model years: 2025

The integrated brake system in these vehicles may malfunction. This can cause a loss of power brake assist, which affects how the brakes work. It can also cause the Antilock Brake System and the Dynamic Stability Control system to stop functioning properly. To fix this, the integrated brake system will be inspected and, if necessary, replaced, free of charge for owners.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the BMW I7?

No. None of the 2 BMW I7 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my BMW I7 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised BMW dealer, including for second owners.
