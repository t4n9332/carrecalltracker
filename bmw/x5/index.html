BMW X5 recalls

42 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

BMW, Mini Integrated Brake System May Malfunction high

Reported 2026-07-01 · Campaign 26V422000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

The integrated brake system in these vehicles may malfunction. This can cause a loss of power brake assist, which affects how the brakes work. It can also cause the Antilock Brake System and the Dynamic Stability Control system to stop functioning properly. To fix this, the integrated brake system will be inspected and, if necessary, replaced, free of charge for owners.

BMW X5, X6, X7 Starter May Overheat critical

Reported 2025-09-25 · Campaign 25V644000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

After repeated starting attempts, the starter motor in these vehicles may overheat because of an electrical overload. An overheated starter sitting in the engine compartment can get hot enough to ignite nearby material that catches fire easily.

BMW Brake System May Lose Power Assist high

Reported 2024-10-02 · Campaign 24V739000 · Model years: 2024

Certain BMW models with an integrated brake system may have the system malfunction. This can cause a loss of power assist for the brakes, meaning it takes more pedal effort and distance to stop the vehicle. The malfunction can also stop the Antilock Brake System and Dynamic Stability Control from working properly, which can affect the driver's ability to keep control of the vehicle.

BMW and MINI Brake System Weld May Fail high

Reported 2024-09-18 · Campaign 24V697000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

The welds inside the servomotor of the integrated brake control module may break. If this happens, the vehicle can lose power assist for the brakes and lose function in the rear brakes. The antilock brake system and the dynamic stability control system may also stop working properly. To fix the problem, BMW dealers will replace the entire integrated brake system with a new one.

BMW X4, X5 Water Pump Connector May Short high

Reported 2024-08-13 · Campaign 24V608000 · Model years: 2018

An electrical connector on the engine's water pump was not properly sealed, so it can be exposed to water. If water reaches it, the connector can short circuit.

BMW Starter Motor Overheating May Cause Engine Fire critical

Reported 2024-08-01 · Campaign 24V576000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

If the starter motor fails, repeated attempts to start the vehicle can cause the starter motor to overheat from an electrical overload. This affects certain BMW M340i, X5, X6, and X7 vehicles, among other models. Dealers will update the vehicle software, free of charge.

BMW SUVs May Have Seat Belt Sensor Defect high

Reported 2024-05-15 · Campaign 24V345000 · Model years: 2025

Certain BMW X5, X6, X7, XM, and Alpina XB7 vehicles have a seat belt system that may wrongly detect a passenger as buckled in when they are not. This can keep the seat belt warning light from turning on, and it may also keep the airbag system from deploying the way it is supposed to in a crash. Dealers will inspect the front seat lower seat belts and replace them if necessary.

BMW X5 Tire Placard Lists Wrong Information high

Reported 2024-05-10 · Campaign 24V327000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

The tire information placard on these vehicles may show the wrong tire size and tire pressure. Because the numbers printed on it are incorrect, the placard does not give accurate information about which tires should be used or how much air should be in them. This means the information a driver would see on that label may not match what the vehicle actually needs.

BMW X5, X7 Instrument Panel Airbag Deployment Issue high

Reported 2024-02-23 · Campaign 24V138000 · Model years: 2024

On some 2024 X5 and X7 models, the instrument panel may not have been built correctly. Because of this, the panel could come apart when the airbag deploys, or the airbag itself may not deploy properly. Either problem could mean pieces of the panel strike someone inside the vehicle, or that an occupant isn't restrained as intended by the airbag during a crash.

BMW, Mini Brake System May Lose Power Assist high

Reported 2024-02-12 · Campaign 24V104000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

The integrated brake system in these vehicles may malfunction. This can cause a loss of power brake assist, which affects how the brakes work. It can also cause the Antilock Brake System and the Dynamic Stability Control system to stop functioning properly. To fix this, dealers will replace the integrated brake system, and this repair is free of charge for owners.

BMW, Toyota Supra Transmission Control Units May Leak Oil high

Reported 2023-12-06 · Campaign 23V821000 · Model years: 2023

Some BMW X3, X4, X5, X6 and Toyota GR Supra vehicles have a transmission control unit with a cover that was not welded correctly. Because of this manufacturing issue, automatic transmission oil can leak into the control unit. If oil gets inside, it can cause the transmission gears to seize up, which suddenly takes away the vehicle's drive power.

BMW X5, X6, X7 knee airbag may not deploy properly high

Reported 2023-09-07 · Campaign 23V622000 · Model years: 2024

BMW is recalling certain BMW X5, X6, X7 and XM models, along with the Alpina XB7. On these vehicles, the knee airbag for the front passenger may not deploy the way it is meant to. This airbag is one of the airbags meant to protect the person sitting in the front passenger seat. Dealers will replace the front passenger knee airbag free of charge, and owner letters have already been mailed.

BMW X5, X6, X7, XM knee airbag may not deploy medium

Reported 2023-07-10 · Campaign 23V471000 · Model years: 2024

BMW is recalling certain X5, X6, X7, Alpina XB7, and XM vehicles. The knee airbag on the driver's side, the passenger's side, or both may not have been built correctly. Because of that, the knee airbag may not open the way it is supposed to. Dealers will replace the affected knee airbag or airbags with new ones, at no cost to the owner. BMW has mailed notification letters to owners about this recall.

BMW X5 Rear Head Restraints May Not Lock medium

Reported 2023-04-06 · Campaign 23V253000 · Model years: 2023

Some BMW X5 vehicles have second-row outboard seat head restraints that may have been manufactured without a locking tab in the guide sleeves. Because that tab is missing, the head restraint may not stay properly locked into position. A head restraint that isn't locked in place may not properly restrain a passenger's head during a crash.

BMW X5 Driver Seat Bolt May Be Loose medium

Reported 2022-12-19 · Campaign 22V942000 · Model years: 2023

On certain X5 vehicles, one of the four bolts holding the driver's seat backrest to the seat frame may not have been installed correctly. This means the seat backrest could be loose. Dealers will inspect the driver's seat backrest and install a bolt if needed, free of charge.

BMW X5 and X7 windshield leak can cause fire critical

Reported 2022-11-02 · Campaign 22V820000 · Model years: 2023

BMW is recalling certain X5 and X7 models. The windshield may not have been bonded to the vehicle properly. A poor bond can let water get into the vehicle and reach electrical parts, which can cause a short circuit. Dealers will inspect the windshield seal and, if necessary, take the windshield out and reinstall it with a new seal, free of charge. BMW mailed owner notification letters on December 30, 2022.

BMW X5, X6 and X7 sunroof may close unexpectedly medium

Reported 2022-04-20 · Campaign 22V267000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

BMW is recalling certain X5, X6, X7 and 4 Series Gran Coupe vehicles, including Alpina XB7 models. The control unit that operates the roof may have been programmed incorrectly, which allows the sunroof to close even when the key fob is not inside the vehicle. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for power-operated window systems. Dealers will reprogram the roof control unit at no cost, and BMW has mailed notification letters to owners.

BMW X5 High-Pressure Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported 2021-07-29 · Campaign 21V586000 · Model years: 2018

On some BMW diesel models, including the X5 xDrive35d, the high-pressure fuel pump that delivers fuel to the engine may fail.

BMW X5 passenger airbag may not deploy properly high

Reported 2021-07-08 · Campaign 21V520000 · Model years: 2019

BMW is recalling one 2019 X5 xDrive 40i. The instrument panel, the dashboard section of the interior, may not have been built correctly on that vehicle. Because of the way it was made, the passenger side airbag could be prevented from opening properly if the vehicle is in a crash. Only that single vehicle is covered by this recall. A dealer will replace the instrument panel free of charge, and owner notification letters were mailed on July 26, 2021.

BMW X5 and X7 may have incorrect second-row head restraints medium

Reported 2021-03-10 · Campaign 21V156000 · Model years: 2021

On these BMW X5 and X7 vehicles, the head restraints, meaning the padded supports at the top of the seat backs, on the two outer seats in the second row may be the wrong type for the vehicle. A dealer will look at those head restraints and, if they are not the correct ones, replace them. BMW is writing to owners about the problem.

BMW backup camera may show partial or blank image medium

Reported 2021-02-19 · Campaign 21V096000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

BMW is recalling certain 2019-2021 cars and SUVs across many models because of a problem with the backup camera. When the driver shifts into reverse, a small part of the picture behind the vehicle may be hidden, or the screen may not light up at all. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for rear visibility. Dealers will check the backup camera software and update it if needed.

BMW X5 Front Brake Disc May Come Loose high

Reported 2021-02-18 · Campaign 21V089000 · Model years: 2021

Some X5 SUVs may have had the right front brake disc assembled with the wrong fasteners. Over time, this could cause the brake disc to become loose. A loose brake disc can affect how the vehicle handles and increases the risk of a crash.

BMW Models: Integrated Brake System May Lose ABS high

Reported 2021-02-10 · Campaign 21V062000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

The rotor and shaft inside the vehicle's integrated brake system may not have been welded together correctly. During hard braking, they could come apart from each other.

BMW X5, X6 and X7 Loose Steering Column Risk high

Reported 2021-01-29 · Campaign 21V031000 · Model years: 2021

Certain BMW X5, X6 and X7 vehicles may have a steering column problem. The connection joining the steering column shaft was not tightened to the correct specification when the vehicle was built, and it can come loose over time. A loose connection in the steering column can affect how the steering responds, making the vehicle harder to handle and control while driving.

BMW 48-Volt Battery Connection May Cause Fire critical

Reported 2020-12-09 · Campaign 20V761000 · Model years: 2021

On certain BMW models, the connection between the 48-volt battery and the starter-generator may not have been put together correctly. This can lead to increased electrical resistance at that connection point in the vehicle's electrical system.

BMW X5, X6, X7 Front Suspension Weld May Fail high

Reported 2020-11-04 · Campaign 20V678000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On these BMW X5, X6, and X7 vehicles, the weld between the front axle support and the right front control arm bearing bracket may separate.

BMW, MINI Hybrid Battery Debris Fire Risk critical

Reported 2020-09-30 · Campaign 20V601000 · Model years: 2021

Certain plug-in hybrid BMW and MINI models, along with the BMW i8, may have debris that got into one or more cells of the high-voltage hybrid battery during manufacturing. This debris can cause a short-circuit inside the battery. A short-circuit like this raises the chance of a fire starting in the vehicle, or of someone being injured.

BMW X5, X6 and X7 headlights may glow blue low

Reported 2020-09-01 · Campaign 20V528000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

BMW is recalling certain X5, X6 and X7 models, including several performance and plug-in versions, because the headlights may give off a blue light. Federal rules set which colors a vehicle's lamps are allowed to show, and headlights that let blue light through do not meet that standard. BMW is having dealers look at the headlamps on the vehicles covered and fit new ones where an inspection shows they are needed.

BMW and Mini Plug-In Hybrid Battery Fire Risk critical

Reported 2020-08-18 · Campaign 20V490000 · Model years: 2021

In certain plug-in hybrid BMW and MINI models, loose metal welding beads inside the hybrid battery could form a conductive path between battery cells that are not supposed to be connected to each other. This stray connection could cause the battery to short-circuit. Dealers will inspect the battery modules and replace them if necessary, and the repair is free of charge for the owner.

BMW X5 and X6 Tires May Lose Pressure Fast high

Reported 2020-08-14 · Campaign 20V483000 · Model years: 2021

On certain BMW X5 and X6 vehicles equipped with specific Pirelli tires, the inner layer of the tire may be damaged. This can cause the tire to rapidly lose air pressure while the vehicle is moving.

BMW X5, X7 Starter Bolt May Cause Stall high

Reported 2020-08-07 · Campaign 20V465000 · Model years: 2020

Some of these SUVs left the factory with an extra bolt in the starter motor that should not be there. That extra bolt could come loose over time and fall into the transmission housing. If the bolt ends up in the transmission housing, it can cause the engine to stall while you are driving. An engine that suddenly stalls while underway increases the chance of a crash.

BMW X5, X6M Loose Steering Column Bearing Plate high

Reported 2020-07-28 · Campaign 20V434000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

BMW is recalling certain X5 and X6M vehicles. The steering column shaft bearing plate may not have been properly attached to the vehicle, which can allow it to loosen over time.

BMW, Toyota Supra backup camera image can vanish high

Reported 2019-09-27 · Campaign 19V684000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On certain BMW, Rolls-Royce and Toyota Supra models, the backup camera and display settings can be adjusted so the rear-view image no longer shows on the screen. Once set this way, the system keeps that setting the next time the vehicle is put in reverse, so no camera image appears.

BMW X5 and X7 Steering Rack Can Break high

Reported 2019-08-28 · Campaign 19V631000 · Model years: 2020

BMW is recalling certain X5 and X7 SUVs because the steering rack's pinion teeth may break under load while driving. If the pinion teeth break, the driver can lose the ability to steer the vehicle. This loss of steering control can happen while the vehicle is in use, affecting the driver's ability to guide the car in the direction intended.

BMW X5, X7 Left Side Wheel Bolts Loose high

Reported 2019-07-23 · Campaign 19V541000 · Model years: 2019

On some X5 and X7 vehicles, the wheel bolts on the left side of the vehicle may not have been tightened enough when the vehicle was built. Because these bolts were not tightened sufficiently, they may gradually loosen further over time as the vehicle is driven.

BMW Fuel Pump May Crack and Leak Fuel critical

Reported 2019-07-03 · Campaign 19V513000 · Model years: 2019

On these BMW vehicles, the high-pressure fuel pump may not have been properly attached to the engine. Over time, this can cause the fuel pump flange to crack. A cracked flange can allow fuel to leak from the fuel system.

BMW X5 Driver Seat Bolts May Be Loose high

Reported 2019-02-13 · Campaign 19V096000 · Model years: 2019

On some 2019 BMW X5 xDrive40i and xDrive50i vehicles, the bolts that attach the driver's seat and seat belt to the vehicle may not have been tightened properly. Because these bolts hold the seat and seat belt in place, a loose bolt could leave the seat or seat belt less securely attached than it should be. Dealers will inspect these attachment bolts and tighten them to the correct specification if needed, free of charge.

BMW X5 Sun Visor Airbag Warning Label Missing low

Reported 2018-12-19 · Campaign 18V918000 · Model years: 2019

BMW is recalling certain X5 vehicles because the driver and/or passenger sun visor may have an incorrect airbag warning label, or the label may be missing altogether. Because of this, the vehicle doesn't meet federal safety labeling requirements for occupant crash protection. Dealers will inspect the sun visors and replace them if needed to correct the problem.

BMW X5 Body Control Unit May Overheat critical

Reported 2018-10-17 · Campaign 18V732000 · Model years: 2019

Some X5 SUVs have an electrical control unit that manages a range of body functions. Inside this unit, small electrical parts called capacitors can short circuit and overheat. When that happens, other components located near the unit may also start to overheat. This buildup of heat can increase the chance that a fire begins somewhere in the vehicle. BMW says the affected vehicles remain on dealer lots and have not yet been sold to owners.

BMW 530e and X5 Charging Cable Fire Risk critical

Reported 2018-09-20 · Campaign 18V652000 · Model years: 2018

BMW says a part inside the portable TurboCord charging cable that comes with certain plug-in hybrid and electric models can fail. If that happens, it could cause an electrical shock or start a fire. This applies to the charging cord itself, not the car's main electrical system. BMW will have dealers look at the charging cable and swap it out for a new one if it's affected, at no cost to the owner.

BMW X5, X6 Front Lower Control Arm Failure high

Reported 2018-06-28 · Campaign 18V439000 · Model years: 2018

BMW is recalling certain X5 and X6 vehicles. The pivot bolt on the front lower-left control arm may not have been properly hardened. This can cause the control arm to break.

BMW X5 and X6 Front Suspension Bolts May Loosen high

Reported 2017-11-17 · Campaign 17V727000 · Model years: 2018

On some BMW X5 and X6 vehicles, the bolts that secure the front suspension may not have been tightened properly. Loose bolts can affect how the suspension performs.
