BMW X2 recalls

7 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

BMW, Mini Integrated Brake System May Malfunction high

Reported 2026-07-01 · Campaign 26V422000 · Model years: 2025

The integrated brake system in these vehicles may malfunction. This can cause a loss of power brake assist, which affects how the brakes work. It can also cause the Antilock Brake System and the Dynamic Stability Control system to stop functioning properly. To fix this, the integrated brake system will be inspected and, if necessary, replaced, free of charge for owners.

BMW X1, X2, Mini Countryman Seat Belt Retractor Damage high

Reported 2025-09-17 · Campaign 25V616000 · Model years: 2025

In certain BMW X1, X2, and Mini Countryman S ALL4 vehicles, the torsion bar inside both front seat belt retractors may have been damaged during manufacturing.

BMW and MINI Brake System Weld May Fail high

Reported 2024-09-18 · Campaign 24V697000 · Model years: 2024

The welds inside the servomotor of the integrated brake control module may break. If this happens, the vehicle can lose power assist for the brakes and lose function in the rear brakes. The antilock brake system and the dynamic stability control system may also stop working properly. To fix the problem, BMW dealers will replace the entire integrated brake system with a new one.

BMW, Mini Brake System May Lose Power Assist high

Reported 2024-02-12 · Campaign 24V104000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

The integrated brake system in these vehicles may malfunction. This can cause a loss of power brake assist, which affects how the brakes work. It can also cause the Antilock Brake System and the Dynamic Stability Control system to stop functioning properly. To fix this, dealers will replace the integrated brake system, and this repair is free of charge for owners.

BMW X1/X2 and MINI airbags may fail in rollover high

Reported 2020-05-20 · Campaign 20V283000 · Model years: 2020

BMW is recalling certain X1, X2, and MINI models because of a problem with the sensor that detects a rollover, located inside the airbag control unit. If the vehicle is in a rollover crash, this problem could mean the head airbags, seat belt pretensioners, and other safety systems don't activate the way they're supposed to.

BMW X1, X2, MINI Cooper Steering Tie Rod May Break high

Reported 2019-08-14 · Campaign 19V601000 · Model years: 2019

Some BMW X1, X2, and MINI Cooper vehicles may have a steering tie rod that wasn't assembled correctly. Over time, this can cause the tie rod end to wear down more than it should, and it could eventually break.

BMW, MINI: Crankshaft Sensor May Cause Stalling high

Reported 2018-07-12 · Campaign 18V465000 · Model years: 2018

On certain BMW and MINI vehicles, the crankshaft sensor may be installed with incorrect firmware. This incorrect firmware can prevent the sensor from properly reading signals from the crankshaft reluctor ring. As a result, the vehicle may stall while it is being driven. BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the crankshaft sensor, free of charge, to correct the problem.
