BMW I4 recalls

8 recalls covering model years 2022. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

BMW Electric Models May Lose Drive Power high

Reported 2025-07-23 · Campaign 25V395000 · Model years: 2022

Certain BMW i4, i5, i7, and iX electric vehicles have a software issue in the electric drive motor. This software may shut down the vehicle's high-voltage system, which causes a sudden loss of drive power while you're driving. BMW will fix this with a software update, either sent over-the-air or done by a dealer, at no cost to you. Owner notification letters have been mailed to affected drivers.

BMW i4 and iX: Battery Module Failure Risk critical

Reported 2025-07-16 · Campaign 25V470000 · Model years: 2022

Some high-voltage battery cell modules were not put together correctly, which can put stress on the module frame. Over time, this may cause a battery module to fail. BMW has already mailed letters warning owners about this risk, and will send another notice once dealers can begin replacing the affected battery modules for free.

BMW i4, iX High-Voltage Battery Charging Unit Fault high

Reported 2023-06-28 · Campaign 23V449000 · Model years: 2022

The high voltage battery's charging unit may not have been manufactured correctly, leaving components inside it improperly connected or assembled.

BMW Software Glitch May Leave Doors Unlocked high

Reported 2023-02-23 · Campaign 23V118000 · Model years: 2022

BMW is recalling certain vehicles because, during a service visit, a replacement electronic control unit may have been installed with incorrect programming. This can cause the automatic door locking function to stop working as intended, meaning the doors may not lock themselves automatically while the vehicle is being driven, even though they are supposed to.

BMW i4 and iX may not make pedestrian warning sound medium

Reported 2023-01-25 · Campaign 23V026000 · Model years: 2022

BMW is recalling certain 2022 and 2023 i4 eDrive40 and iX xDrive50 electric vehicles. These cars have a system that makes an artificial sound so people outside can hear the vehicle. When the vehicle starts up, the control unit for that sound system may have a fault and fail to make the outside warning sound. Because of that, these vehicles do not meet the federal rule setting a minimum sound level for hybrid and electric vehicles. Dealers will update the sound system software.

BMW i4 and iX battery software may cut power high

Reported 2022-12-20 · Campaign 22V944000 · Model years: 2022

Certain BMW i4 and iX electric vehicles have high voltage battery control software that may cause an interruption in electrical power. This means the flow of electricity that powers the vehicle could unexpectedly stop or be disrupted while driving, which can cause the vehicle to suddenly lose drive power.

BMW i4 and iX batteries may short circuit, causing fire critical

Reported 2022-07-27 · Campaign 22V541000 · Model years: 2022

BMW is recalling certain iX xDrive50, iX M60, i4 eDrive40 and i4 M50 electric vehicles. The high-voltage battery that powers the vehicle may be damaged inside, and that damage can cause an electrical short circuit in the battery. Dealers will replace the high-voltage battery at no cost to owners. BMW mailed an early letter telling owners about the safety risk, and will send another letter once replacement parts are available. Owners will also be contacted by phone.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired. park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

BMW i4, iX Screen May Miss Warning Lights high

Reported 2022-07-07 · Campaign 22V483000 · Model years: 2022

In certain BMW i4 and iX vehicles, when the vehicle is switched into Valet Parking Mode, the central display screen may fail to show important safety information, such as warning messages and warning lights. This means that while the vehicle is in that mode, a warning that would normally appear on the screen may not be shown to the driver, so the driver would not see it.
