BMW Starter Motor Overheating May Cause Engine Fire critical

Reported Aug 1, 2024 · Campaign 24V576000 · Model years: 2020

If the starter motor fails, repeated attempts to start the vehicle can cause the starter motor to overheat from an electrical overload. This affects certain BMW M340i, X5, X6, and X7 vehicles, among other models. Dealers will update the vehicle software, free of charge.

BMW seat belt warning chime may not sound medium

Reported Aug 16, 2023 · Campaign 23V584000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

BMW is recalling certain 3 Series, 4 Series and 2 Series Gran Coupe cars, including the M340i and M440i. The software in the car's audio receiver control unit may not properly sound the audible warning that reminds the driver to buckle their seat belt. Because that warning does not work as it should, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard covering occupant crash protection.

BMW M340i, X3, X4 Seat Frames Improperly Welded high

Reported Mar 29, 2023 · Campaign 23V211000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On certain BMW 330i, M340i xDrive, X3, X3M, X4, and X4M vehicles, the frame of a seat may not have been welded to the seat rails properly. Because the weld may not be secure, the seat frame might not hold and support the occupant the way it's supposed to if the vehicle is in a crash, which raises the chance of injury.

BMW and Toyota Supra May Lose Brake Assist medium

Reported Aug 2, 2021 · Campaign 21V598000 · Model years: 2020

During engine start-up, a problem with the engine's control software may cause a loss of brake assist. Full mechanical braking, without the assist, would still be available. Losing brake assist increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will update the engine control software on affected vehicles, free of charge.

BMW, MINI and Toyota Supra passenger seat belt retractor fault medium

Reported Jul 21, 2021 · Campaign 21V554000 · Model years: 2021

BMW is recalling certain BMW, MINI, Rolls Royce and Toyota Supra vehicles. The passenger seat belt has a locking feature that can switch off too early, before the belt webbing has pulled all the way back in. Because of this, these seat belt assemblies do not meet the federal safety standard for occupant crash protection. Dealers will inspect the seat belt retractors and the seat belts themselves, and replace the retractors if that is needed.

BMW 330e, M3, M340i Seat Belt Bolt Issue medium

Reported Apr 28, 2021 · Campaign 21V298000 · Model years: 2021

The bolt that secures the seat belt assembly to the frame was not tightened to the correct specification.

BMW backup camera may show partial or blank image medium

Reported Feb 19, 2021 · Campaign 21V096000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

BMW is recalling certain 2019-2021 cars and SUVs across many models because of a problem with the backup camera. When the driver shifts into reverse, a small part of the picture behind the vehicle may be hidden, or the screen may not light up at all. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for rear visibility. Dealers will check the backup camera software and update it if needed.

BMW 48-Volt Battery Connection May Cause Fire critical

Reported Dec 9, 2020 · Campaign 20V761000 · Model years: 2021

On certain BMW models, the connection between the 48-volt battery and the starter-generator may not have been put together correctly. This can lead to increased electrical resistance at that connection point in the vehicle's electrical system.

BMW and Toyota Sports Cars: Steering Tie Rod May Fracture high

Reported Jun 15, 2020 · Campaign 20V355000 · Model years: 2020

Certain 2019 Z4 sDrive30i, 2019-2020 330i xDrive, M340i and M340i xDrive, 2020 X3M and X4M, and Toyota Supra vehicles may have a steering gear tie rod that becomes damaged. This can happen in certain driving conditions, such as high temperatures and rough road surfaces. This damage can develop gradually before resulting in an actual fracture of the tie rod.

BMW seat belt buckle sensors may misread belted occupants medium

Reported Mar 19, 2020 · Campaign 20V164000 · Model years: 2020

BMW is recalling certain 2020 X3, X4, 3 Series, 8 Series and M models. Sensors inside the front seat belt buckles can wrongly read a buckled-up driver or front passenger as not wearing the belt. Because of that, the airbags and the seat belt tighteners may not go off the way they should if the car is in a crash. Dealers will check the front seat belt buckles and replace them if needed.

BMW 3 Series and M340i Trunk Release Problem medium

Reported Oct 23, 2019 · Campaign 19V755000 · Model years: 2020

Certain BMW 330i, 330i xDrive and M340i cars may have the interior trunk release cable installed incorrectly. Because of this, someone inside the trunk may not be able to open it from the inside using the release handle. This does not meet a federal safety standard that requires cars to have a working interior trunk release. Dealers will inspect the cable and install it correctly if needed.

BMW, Toyota Supra backup camera image can vanish high

Reported Sep 27, 2019 · Campaign 19V684000 · Model years: 2020

On certain BMW, Rolls-Royce and Toyota Supra models, the backup camera and display settings can be adjusted so the rear-view image no longer shows on the screen. Once set this way, the system keeps that setting the next time the vehicle is put in reverse, so no camera image appears.

BMW M340i Rear Headrest May Fold Down medium

Reported Aug 14, 2019 · Campaign 19V600000 · Model years: 2020

Some BMW M340i vehicles have a rear center headrest that was not welded correctly. Because of this, the headrest may fold down if the vehicle is struck from behind in a crash. This is the center headrest in the back seat, not the two outer ones. The issue comes from an improper welding process used when the vehicle was built, and it affects only vehicles covered by this recall.

BMW M340I recall questions

How many recalls does the BMW M340I have?

NHTSA lists 13 safety recalls covering the 2020–2022 BMW M340I. 5 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "BMW Starter Motor Overheating May Cause Engine Fire" (NHTSA campaign 24V576000), reported Aug 1, 2024.

Which BMW M340I model year has the most recalls?

The 2020 BMW M340I has the most, with 10 recalls. The 2022 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the BMW M340I?

No. None of the 13 BMW M340I recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my BMW M340I has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised BMW dealer, including for second owners.
