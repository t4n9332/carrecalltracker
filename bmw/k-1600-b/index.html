BMW K 1600 Motorcycles Reverse Gear Overheating Risk critical

Reported Apr 2, 2026 · Campaign 26V214000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

On these motorcycles, a seal on the reverse gear control unit can deteriorate over time and let moisture get inside the unit. If moisture gets in, the control unit can overheat. Dealers will replace the reverse gear control unit at no charge, and a follow-up letter will be sent once the final remedy is ready.

BMW Motorcycle Front Strut Failure Risk high

Reported Feb 29, 2024 · Campaign 24V165000 · Model years: 2024

BMW is recalling certain R 1250RT, K 1600GT, K 1600GTL, and K 1600B motorcycles because the front spring strut may contain an incorrectly manufactured proportional valve. This valve can loosen, and when it does, the strut itself can fail. If the strut fails, the motorcycle can become unstable while it is being ridden, affecting how well the rider can keep the motorcycle steady and balanced.

BMW K 1600 Rear Suspension Strut Defect high

Reported Jan 21, 2022 · Campaign 22V023000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

The link strut that connects the rear suspension to the frame on your BMW K1600 motorcycle may not be strong enough and can become damaged over time.
