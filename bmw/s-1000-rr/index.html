BMW S 1000 RR recalls

5 recalls covering model years 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

BMW S 1000 RR Ignition Lock May Fail high

Reported 2026-07-23 · Campaign 26V478000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

On some BMW S 1000 RR motorcycles, vibration can cause a part inside the ignition lock to fail. This can knock out the instrument display, rear lighting, and other systems like ABS, or cause the engine to stall while you're riding. BMW is still developing a repair. In the meantime, the company is mailing letters to let owners know about this safety risk.

BMW R 1300 GS, S 1000 RR eCall Disabled medium

Reported 2025-07-25 · Campaign 25V480000 · Model years: 2025

These motorcycles can come equipped with an optional emergency call system, a feature designed to automatically reach out for help if you're in a crash. A software programming error may have caused this system to be turned off. When that happens, the emergency call feature will not work the way it's meant to when it's needed most, since it has already been deactivated by the error.

BMW S 1000 RR Motorcycle Hand Brake Lever Defect high

Reported 2023-02-24 · Campaign 23V123000 · Model years: 2023

On some BMW S 1000 RR motorcycles, the hand brake lever may not work the way it should. This can reduce how well the brakes perform, which means the motorcycle could take longer to stop than expected. Dealers will replace a small part in the brake lever, called the fulcrum pin, to fix the problem. This repair will be done free of charge.

BMW Motorcycle Clutch Cover Bolts May Loosen critical

Reported 2022-10-07 · Campaign 22V752000 · Model years: 2022

BMW is recalling certain S1000RR, M1000RR, S1000XR, and S1000R motorcycles. The bolts that hold the clutch cover in place were not lubricated correctly when the bikes were assembled. Because of this, the bolts can work loose over time. When the clutch cover bolts loosen, the cover itself can become loose as well, and this can allow engine oil to leak out from that part of the motorcycle.

BMW Motorcycle Front Brake Fluid Leak high

Reported 2020-08-13 · Campaign 20V476000 · Model years: 2020

On these BMW motorcycles, the front brake calipers can leak brake fluid while the bike is parked. Over time, this leak can lower the amount of fluid in the brake fluid reservoir. If the fluid level gets too low, it can affect how well the front brakes work.
