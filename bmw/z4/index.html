BMW Z4 recalls

11 recalls covering model years 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

BMW, Toyota Supra: Engine Starter May Catch Fire critical

Reported 2026-01-30 · Campaign 26V056000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

On affected vehicles, the engine starter may overheat and catch fire due to wear on an internal component.

BMW and Toyota Supra Starter Relay Fire Risk critical

Reported 2025-09-23 · Campaign 25V636000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

Certain BMW X3, X4, Z4, and Toyota Supra vehicles have an engine starter relay that can corrode over time. Once corroded, the relay may overheat and short circuit. This electrical problem can increase the risk of a fire, whether the vehicle is being driven or is already parked.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

BMW windows and sunroof may close without key present medium

Reported 2023-02-17 · Campaign 23V099000 · Model years: 2022

BMW is recalling certain 2023 8 Series and 2 Series models and 2022-2023 Z4 sDrive30i and Z4 M40i vehicles. After the digital key is taken out of the vehicle, the software may still let the power windows and sunroof be closed. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for power-operated window systems. Dealers will update the software free of charge, and the digital key function through cellular service and Wi-Fi will be turned off until the update is done.

BMW and Toyota Supra May Lose Brake Assist medium

Reported 2021-08-02 · Campaign 21V598000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

During engine start-up, a problem with the engine's control software may cause a loss of brake assist. Full mechanical braking, without the assist, would still be available. Losing brake assist increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will update the engine control software on affected vehicles, free of charge.

BMW, MINI and Toyota Supra passenger seat belt retractor fault medium

Reported 2021-07-21 · Campaign 21V554000 · Model years: 2021

BMW is recalling certain BMW, MINI, Rolls Royce and Toyota Supra vehicles. The passenger seat belt has a locking feature that can switch off too early, before the belt webbing has pulled all the way back in. Because of this, these seat belt assemblies do not meet the federal safety standard for occupant crash protection. Dealers will inspect the seat belt retractors and the seat belts themselves, and replace the retractors if that is needed.

BMW Z4, Toyota Supra Fuel Tank May Leak critical

Reported 2020-11-12 · Campaign 20V700000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On some BMW Z4 and Toyota Supra vehicles, the weld joining the two halves of the fuel tank may not have been done well enough, which can cause the weld to fail over time. If the weld fails, fuel could leak from the tank. If that leaking fuel comes into contact with something that can ignite it, it increases the risk of a fire.

BMW Z4 and Toyota Supra Headlights May Be Misaligned high

Reported 2020-08-27 · Campaign 20V516000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On certain BMW Z4 and Toyota Supra vehicles, the headlamps can be adjusted horizontally, which does not meet federal lighting requirements.

BMW and Toyota Sports Cars: Steering Tie Rod May Fracture high

Reported 2020-06-15 · Campaign 20V355000 · Model years: 2019

Certain 2019 Z4 sDrive30i, 2019-2020 330i xDrive, M340i and M340i xDrive, 2020 X3M and X4M, and Toyota Supra vehicles may have a steering gear tie rod that becomes damaged. This can happen in certain driving conditions, such as high temperatures and rough road surfaces. This damage can develop gradually before resulting in an actual fracture of the tie rod.

BMW Z4 and Toyota Supra Headlights May Fail medium

Reported 2019-11-27 · Campaign 19V850000 · Model years: 2020

The control units that operate the headlights on these vehicles may fail over time. When a control unit fails, it can cause the vehicle to lose the use of one headlight, or in some cases both headlights at once. Losing headlights reduces the driver's visibility, making it harder to see the road while driving.

BMW Z4 Engine Counterbalance Shaft May Loosen high

Reported 2019-10-15 · Campaign 19V732000 · Model years: 2019

The needle roller bearings that support the engine's counterbalance shaft may not have been installed correctly during manufacturing. This can allow the counterbalance shaft to loosen over time while the vehicle is being driven.

BMW, Toyota Supra backup camera image can vanish high

Reported 2019-09-27 · Campaign 19V684000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On certain BMW, Rolls-Royce and Toyota Supra models, the backup camera and display settings can be adjusted so the rear-view image no longer shows on the screen. Once set this way, the system keeps that setting the next time the vehicle is put in reverse, so no camera image appears.
