BMW C400X Scooter Throttle May Get Stuck medium

Reported Feb 13, 2020 · Campaign 20V083000 · Model years: 2019

Some BMW C400X scooters can let water collect inside the throttle cable. In cold weather, that water can freeze, which can cause the throttle to get stuck in the open position.

BMW Motorcycles and Scooters: Brake Caliper Corrosion Risk high

Reported Aug 14, 2019 · Campaign 19V593000 · Model years: 2019

Certain 2019 BMW C 400 X scooters, 2018 to 2020 G 310 GS, and 2017 to 2020 G 310 R motorcycles may have front and rear brake caliper pistons that corrode. This corrosion can cause a piston to stick or drag inside the caliper bore, instead of moving smoothly as intended. When this happens, the affected brake may not engage or release the way it normally would.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the BMW C 400 X?

No. None of the 2 BMW C 400 X recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my BMW C 400 X has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised BMW dealer, including for second owners.
