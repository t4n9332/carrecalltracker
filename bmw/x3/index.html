BMW X3 recalls

40 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

BMW X3 Steering Spindle May Be Loose high

Reported 2026-08-12 · Campaign 26V524000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

On certain BMW X3 vehicles, the steering spindle—the part that connects the steering wheel to the steering column—may not have been properly attached. This is a problem with the physical connection inside the steering system, between the steering wheel and the column beneath it. If this connection is not secure, the way the vehicle steers could be affected.

BMW, Toyota Supra: Engine Starter May Catch Fire critical

Reported 2026-01-30 · Campaign 26V056000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

On affected vehicles, the engine starter may overheat and catch fire due to wear on an internal component.

BMW X3 Steering Wheel May Move Unexpectedly high

Reported 2025-12-11 · Campaign 25V857000 · Model years: 2025

Certain 2025 and 2026 BMW X3 vehicles may experience unexpected movement of the steering wheel while the vehicle is stationary, meaning it isn't being driven at that moment. The steering wheel can rotate on its own, without the driver turning it, even though no one is actively steering the vehicle.

BMW and Toyota Supra Starter Relay Fire Risk critical

Reported 2025-09-23 · Campaign 25V636000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Certain BMW X3, X4, Z4, and Toyota Supra vehicles have an engine starter relay that can corrode over time. Once corroded, the relay may overheat and short circuit. This electrical problem can increase the risk of a fire, whether the vehicle is being driven or is already parked.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

BMW X3 Front Brake Discs Damaged in Transport high

Reported 2025-04-16 · Campaign 25V247000 · Model years: 2025

The front brake discs on affected vehicles may have been damaged during transport, before they were ever installed on the vehicle.

BMW 430i, X3 Starter Connection May Loosen critical

Reported 2025-04-01 · Campaign 25V202000 · Model years: 2025

The electrical connection between the starter-generator and the battery in your vehicle may come loose. If this happens while you're driving, your engine could stall. The wiring involved in this connection may also overheat. Dealers will inspect the starter-generator and battery cable and replace them if necessary, free of charge. BMW has already mailed an interim letter about this, and a second letter will follow once the full repair is ready.

BMW X3 Rear Brake Hoses May Leak, Cut Braking high

Reported 2025-03-12 · Campaign 25V154000 · Model years: 2025

On some BMW X3 vehicles, the rear brake hoses may develop a leak. If that happens, the vehicle can lose rear brake function, and it can also lose the Antilock Brake System and Dynamic Stability Control, systems that help keep the vehicle stable.

BMW X3 Battery Cable May Overheat critical

Reported 2025-02-21 · Campaign 25V109000 · Model years: 2025

On some 2025 BMW X3 30 xDrive vehicles, the connection between the positive battery cable and the starter-generator may not have been tightened properly, which can cause it to overheat.

BMW X3 rear door locks may open too easily high

Reported 2025-02-20 · Campaign 25V106000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

Certain BMW X3 vehicles have door locking modules on the second-row side doors that may not work correctly. Normally, unlocking and opening a rear door takes separate steps. But with this problem, those doors could be unlocked and opened at the same time with just one motion, meaning a single accidental movement could open the door instead of just unlocking it.

BMW X3 Front Left Wheel Bearing May Crack high

Reported 2024-12-05 · Campaign 24V909000 · Model years: 2025

The swivel bearing at the front left wheel may crack. If it does, it can cause the driver to lose handling and control of the vehicle.

BMW X3 Side Curtain Airbag May Not Deploy Right high

Reported 2024-11-12 · Campaign 24V856000 · Model years: 2025

On some 2025 BMW X3 30 xDrive vehicles, the gas generator inside the right-side head curtain airbag may have been put together incorrectly during assembly. This could cause the airbag to deploy late or not work the way it's supposed to in a crash. Dealers will inspect the airbag and replace it if needed, free of charge. None of these vehicles have been sold yet.

BMW X3 Passenger Air Bag May Not Inflate high

Reported 2024-10-17 · Campaign 24V772000 · Model years: 2025

Some new X3 SUVs may have a front passenger air bag with damaged fabric. The damage is believed to have happened while the air bag was being shipped between suppliers before it was installed in the vehicle. If the fabric is damaged, the air bag may not inflate the way it's supposed to in a crash. That means it may not protect the front passenger as intended, raising the risk of injury if a crash occurs. None of the affected vehicles have been sold yet.

BMW X3 rear cargo rail can detach in crash medium

Reported 2024-10-15 · Campaign 24V764000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

BMW is recalling certain X3 models. The bracket that holds the cargo rail in the rear of the vehicle can be damaged in a rear-end crash, and the rail can come loose and separate from the vehicle. Dealers will replace the bolts that hold the rear cargo rail in place, free of charge. Vehicles covered by this recall had work done for this same problem under an earlier recall, and they still need the new repair.

BMW X3 Interior Cargo Rail May Come Loose medium

Reported 2024-07-17 · Campaign 24V534000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

BMW is recalling certain X3 vehicles, including the sDrive30i, xDrive30i, M40i and X3 M. The attachment that holds the interior cargo rail in place can be damaged if the vehicle is struck from behind, and the cargo rail can then come loose inside the vehicle. Dealers will replace the bolts that fasten the rear cargo rail at no cost. Owners have been sent notification letters about this repair.

BMW, Toyota Supra Transmission Control Units May Leak Oil high

Reported 2023-12-06 · Campaign 23V821000 · Model years: 2023

Some BMW X3, X4, X5, X6 and Toyota GR Supra vehicles have a transmission control unit with a cover that was not welded correctly. Because of this manufacturing issue, automatic transmission oil can leak into the control unit. If oil gets inside, it can cause the transmission gears to seize up, which suddenly takes away the vehicle's drive power.

BMW X3, X4 Steering Gear May Loosen high

Reported 2023-11-16 · Campaign 23V770000 · Model years: 2024

BMW is recalling certain 2024 vehicles, including 2 Series Coupe, 3 Series, X3, and X4 models. The steering gear on these vehicles may not have been tightened properly, which can allow it to become loose. If the steering gear loosens, it can cause the driver to lose control of the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.

BMW M340i, X3, X4 Seat Frames Improperly Welded high

Reported 2023-03-29 · Campaign 23V211000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On certain BMW 330i, M340i xDrive, X3, X3M, X4, and X4M vehicles, the frame of a seat may not have been welded to the seat rails properly. Because the weld may not be secure, the seat frame might not hold and support the occupant the way it's supposed to if the vehicle is in a crash, which raises the chance of injury.

BMW X1, X3 Fuel Rail May Crack, Leak Fuel critical

Reported 2022-07-20 · Campaign 22V513000 · Model years: 2022

BMW says the high-pressure fuel rail on certain X1 and X3 vehicles may develop a crack. If the fuel rail cracks, fuel can leak out inside the engine compartment.

BMW X3, X4 Transmission Leak May Cause Rollaway high

Reported 2022-02-09 · Campaign 22V070000 · Model years: 2022

Some BMW X3 and X4 vehicles have a transmission mechatronics unit that may not have been assembled correctly, which could cause an internal leak.

BMW and Toyota Supra May Lose Brake Assist medium

Reported 2021-08-02 · Campaign 21V598000 · Model years: 2020

During engine start-up, a problem with the engine's control software may cause a loss of brake assist. Full mechanical braking, without the assist, would still be available. Losing brake assist increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will update the engine control software on affected vehicles, free of charge.

BMW X3 And X4 Passenger Air Bag Shutoff high

Reported 2021-07-08 · Campaign 21V521000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On certain 2018-2019 BMW X3 and 2019 X4 models, the system that senses whether the front passenger seat is occupied may have been put together incorrectly. This can make the system think the seat is empty even when someone is sitting in it, which turns off the front passenger air bag.

BMW X3, X4 Fuel Tank Weld May Fail critical

Reported 2021-03-24 · Campaign 21V199000 · Model years: 2021

Some BMW X3 and X4 vehicles have a fuel tank inlet check valve where the weld holding it in place may fail. If this weld fails, the pipe that fills the fuel tank can separate from the tank itself. Once separated, fuel from the tank may leak out of the vehicle.

BMW backup camera may show partial or blank image medium

Reported 2021-02-19 · Campaign 21V096000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

BMW is recalling certain 2019-2021 cars and SUVs across many models because of a problem with the backup camera. When the driver shifts into reverse, a small part of the picture behind the vehicle may be hidden, or the screen may not light up at all. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for rear visibility. Dealers will check the backup camera software and update it if needed.

BMW, MINI Hybrid Battery Debris Fire Risk critical

Reported 2020-09-30 · Campaign 20V601000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Certain plug-in hybrid BMW and MINI models, along with the BMW i8, may have debris that got into one or more cells of the high-voltage hybrid battery during manufacturing. This debris can cause a short-circuit inside the battery. A short-circuit like this raises the chance of a fire starting in the vehicle, or of someone being injured.

BMW X3, X4 and M5 rearview camera image partly blocked medium

Reported 2020-09-30 · Campaign 20V598000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

BMW is recalling certain X3, X4, 5 Series and M5 vehicles, including the X3 sDrive 30i and xDrive 30i, X3M 40i, X3 xDrive 30e, X4 xDrive 30i, X4M 40i, 530i, 540i, 540i xDrive, M550i xDrive, M5 and 550e models. A small part of the picture shown by the rearview camera may be slightly blocked. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard covering rear visibility.

BMW and Mini Plug-In Hybrid Battery Fire Risk critical

Reported 2020-08-18 · Campaign 20V490000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

In certain plug-in hybrid BMW and MINI models, loose metal welding beads inside the hybrid battery could form a conductive path between battery cells that are not supposed to be connected to each other. This stray connection could cause the battery to short-circuit. Dealers will inspect the battery modules and replace them if necessary, and the repair is free of charge for the owner.

BMW X3 Rearview Camera Image Missing medium

Reported 2020-07-29 · Campaign 20V443000 · Model years: 2020

Some 2020 BMW X3 sDrive40i, X3 xDrive40i, and X3 M40i vehicles left the factory without the rearview camera software installed. Because of this, when the driver shifts into reverse, no rearview camera image appears on the screen.

BMW and Toyota Sports Cars: Steering Tie Rod May Fracture high

Reported 2020-06-15 · Campaign 20V355000 · Model years: 2020

Certain 2019 Z4 sDrive30i, 2019-2020 330i xDrive, M340i and M340i xDrive, 2020 X3M and X4M, and Toyota Supra vehicles may have a steering gear tie rod that becomes damaged. This can happen in certain driving conditions, such as high temperatures and rough road surfaces. This damage can develop gradually before resulting in an actual fracture of the tie rod.

BMW seat belt buckle sensors may misread belted occupants medium

Reported 2020-03-19 · Campaign 20V164000 · Model years: 2020

BMW is recalling certain 2020 X3, X4, 3 Series, 8 Series and M models. Sensors inside the front seat belt buckles can wrongly read a buckled-up driver or front passenger as not wearing the belt. Because of that, the airbags and the seat belt tighteners may not go off the way they should if the car is in a crash. Dealers will check the front seat belt buckles and replace them if needed.

BMW X3 and X4: Airbag Panel Piece Risk high

Reported 2020-03-18 · Campaign 20V152000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

The casing around the instrument panel on these vehicles may not have been fastened tightly enough to the passenger air bag. If the air bag has to inflate during a crash, it could damage the instrument panel and cause small pieces to break off and strike the driver or passengers. Dealers will replace the instrument panel casing for free.

BMW X3, X4 Steering Rack Pinion May Break high

Reported 2019-10-16 · Campaign 19V738000 · Model years: 2020

BMW is recalling certain 2020 X3 M40i and X4 M40i vehicles. Inside the steering system, there is a rack and pinion, and the teeth on the pinion gear may break while the vehicle is under load. If the pinion teeth break, the driver could lose control of the vehicle's steering, meaning the steering wheel would no longer properly turn the wheels.

BMW, Toyota Supra backup camera image can vanish high

Reported 2019-09-27 · Campaign 19V684000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

On certain BMW, Rolls-Royce and Toyota Supra models, the backup camera and display settings can be adjusted so the rear-view image no longer shows on the screen. Once set this way, the system keeps that setting the next time the vehicle is put in reverse, so no camera image appears.

BMW X3 And X4 Front Bearing Failure critical

Reported 2019-09-25 · Campaign 19V678000 · Model years: 2020

Some BMW X3 and X4 SUVs have front axle swivel bearings that may not have been properly heat treated during manufacturing. This can leave the bearings weaker than they should be, and they could break.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired.

BMW X3 and X4 Front Seatback Weld Failure medium

Reported 2019-07-31 · Campaign 19V563000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On certain X3 and X4 vehicles, the frame inside the front seatback, on the driver's or passenger's side, may not have been welded correctly. This can allow the seatback to suddenly fold forward or backward instead of staying upright. If this happens during a crash, the seat may not hold the occupant in place the way it should, which increases the risk of injury.

BMW X3, X4 Passenger Air Bag May Not Deploy high

Reported 2019-04-10 · Campaign 19V291000 · Model years: 2019

The instrument panel on these vehicles may have been manufactured incorrectly. Because of how the panel was put together, it may affect whether the front passenger air bag deploys the way it is supposed to. This means the air bag positioned in front of the passenger seat may not function correctly when it needs to inflate, affecting the passenger sitting in that seat.

BMW X3 and X4 Rear Brake Caliper Coating high

Reported 2018-07-06 · Campaign 18V453000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The pistons inside the rear brake calipers on these vehicles may not have enough protective coating applied to them. Without sufficient coating, the rear brake calipers may not work as well as intended, which can reduce how well the rear brakes perform when the vehicle is stopping.

BMW X3 Rear Turn Signals Show Wrong Colors medium

Reported 2018-06-21 · Campaign 18V431000 · Model years: 2018

On some vehicles, the outer rear turn signal glows red while the inner rear turn signal glows amber at the same time. Because the two lights flash different colors together, the turn signals don't work the way they're supposed to.

BMW X3 Lane Keep Assist Hands-Off Detection Flaw high

Reported 2018-06-06 · Campaign 18V374000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Some BMW X3 models with the Active Lane Keeping Assistant may have a steering wheel that isn't compatible with this system. Because of this mismatch, the system may fail to detect when the driver's hands are off the steering wheel.

BMW X3 Rear Spoiler May Detach While Driving medium

Reported 2018-03-07 · Campaign 18V154000 · Model years: 2018

BMW is recalling certain 2018 X3 xDrive30i and X3 M40i vehicles because the rear spoiler may not have been properly attached during assembly, or the spoiler may be missing screws. Either problem can cause the spoiler to come loose and detach from the vehicle while it is being driven, leaving it in the roadway where it becomes a hazard for other vehicles.

BMW Instrument Panel Display May Not Light Up medium

Reported 2017-11-15 · Campaign 17V719000 · Model years: 2018

BMW is recalling certain 230i, 230i xDrive, M240i, M240i xDrive, M2, and X3 xDrive 30i vehicles. A software error in the instrument panel may prevent the display from lighting up properly. This can happen at startup or while driving, and can affect warning and control symbols shown on the display. Dealers will update the instrument cluster display software to fix the problem, free of charge to owners.
