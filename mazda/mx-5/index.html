Mazda MX-5 stability control warning light may not illuminate medium

Reported May 22, 2025 · Campaign 25V336000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Mazda is recalling certain MX-5 models. Because of a software error, the dashboard light for the traction control and stability control systems may not come on when it should. As a result, these cars do not meet the federal safety rule for electronic stability control systems. Dealers will replace the stability control unit.

Mazda MX-5 Miata Air Bags May Deploy Too Forcefully high

Reported Sep 18, 2024 · Campaign 24V695000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

Mazda is recalling certain MX-5 Miata vehicles. A software error in the control unit that manages the air bags can cause the front air bags to inflate with too much force in a crash. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for occupant crash protection. Dealers will update the air bag sensor control software at no cost to the owner. Mazda has already mailed notification letters to owners.

Mazda Fuel Pump May Fail and Stall Engine high

Reported Nov 12, 2021 · Campaign 21V875000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The impeller inside the low-pressure fuel pump may crack and become deformed, which can cause the fuel pump to fail. If the pump fails while you are driving, the engine may stall, and stalling increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will replace the fuel pump in affected vehicles, free of charge.

Mazda MX-5 Transmission May Downshift Unexpectedly high

Reported Feb 1, 2019 · Campaign 19V072000 · Model years: 2018

In some Mazda MX-5 Miata vehicles with automatic transmissions, the computer that controls the transmission was programmed incorrectly. Under certain conditions, this can cause the vehicle to suddenly downshift on its own and abruptly slow down, without the driver choosing to do so.

Mazda MX-5 recall questions

How many recalls does the Mazda MX-5 have?

NHTSA lists 4 safety recalls covering the 2018–2025 Mazda MX-5. 3 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Mazda MX-5 stability control warning light may not illuminate" (NHTSA campaign 25V336000), reported May 22, 2025.

Which Mazda MX-5 model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Mazda MX-5 has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2020 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Mazda MX-5?

No. None of the 4 Mazda MX-5 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Mazda MX-5 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Mazda dealer, including for second owners.
