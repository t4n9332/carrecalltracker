Mazda CX-9 recalls

4 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Mazda Fuel Pump May Fail and Stall Engine high

Reported 2021-11-12 · Campaign 21V875000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The impeller inside the low-pressure fuel pump may crack and become deformed, which can cause the fuel pump to fail. If the pump fails while you are driving, the engine may stall, and stalling increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will replace the fuel pump in affected vehicles, free of charge.

Mazda CX-9 second-row seat may slide forward in crash high

Reported 2021-09-27 · Campaign 21V750000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Mazda is recalling certain CX-9 vehicles that have the 60/40 split bench second-row seats. The left-side second-row seat may unexpectedly slide forward during a crash. Federal safety rules cover how seat belt anchorages must hold, and these vehicles do not meet that standard. A dealer will replace the internal parts inside the affected second-row seats at no cost to the owner.

Mazda CX-9 Tires May Lose Tread While Driving high

Reported 2019-10-28 · Campaign 19V770000 · Model years: 2019

Some CX-9 SUVs were built with specific Sumitomo Falken tires that may have been manufactured using an incorrect rubber compound. Because of this, the tread on the tire can separate and detach from the tire itself, rather than remaining attached.

Mazda CX-9 Wiring Fault Disables Airbag, Signals high

Reported 2019-05-30 · Campaign 19V403000 · Model years: 2018

Mazda says the wiring harness on some CX-9 SUVs can disrupt electrical connections. This may stop the passenger-side front airbag from working, cause the turn signals to fail, or prevent the engine from starting. These are separate possible effects of the same wiring problem, not something you would notice unless one of them happens on your vehicle.
