Mazda Fuel Pump May Fail and Stall Engine high

Reported Nov 12, 2021 · Campaign 21V875000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The impeller inside the low-pressure fuel pump may crack and become deformed, which can cause the fuel pump to fail. If the pump fails while you are driving, the engine may stall, and stalling increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will replace the fuel pump in affected vehicles, free of charge.

Mazda3, Mazda6 and CX-5 engines may stall unexpectedly high

Reported Jun 27, 2019 · Campaign 19V497000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

A software error in the computer that controls the engine may cause the engine to stall while the vehicle is being driven. Mazda dealers will reprogram that computer with corrected software at no charge. The recall began August 20, 2019, and owners can contact Mazda customer service to schedule the free repair.

Mazda CX-5 Side Curtain Airbags May Fail high

Reported Jun 25, 2018 · Campaign 18V426000 · Model years: 2018

In some Mazda CX-5 vehicles, the side curtain airbags may not fully unfold the way they are supposed to if the vehicle is in a side-impact crash or a rollover. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard meant to help keep passengers from being thrown from the vehicle during a crash. If the airbags don't work correctly, they may not protect occupants as intended.

Mazda CX-5 recall questions

How many recalls does the Mazda CX-5 have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2018–2019 Mazda CX-5. 3 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Mazda Fuel Pump May Fail and Stall Engine" (NHTSA campaign 21V875000), reported Nov 12, 2021.

Which Mazda CX-5 model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Mazda CX-5 has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2019 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Mazda CX-5?

No. None of the 3 Mazda CX-5 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Mazda CX-5 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Mazda dealer, including for second owners.
