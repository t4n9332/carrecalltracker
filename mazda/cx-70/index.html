Mazda CX-70, CX-90 Fuel Gauge May Read Wrong medium

Reported Sep 2, 2025 · Campaign 25V568000 · Model years: 2025

On affected vehicles, the fuel gauge in the instrument cluster may show an inaccurate reading of how much fuel is in the tank. Because the gauge isn't accurate, you could unexpectedly run out of fuel and the vehicle could stall while you are driving.

Mazda CX-70 Control Module Software May Cut Power high

Reported Oct 31, 2024 · Campaign 24V815000 · Model years: 2025

On these vehicles, a software issue in the powertrain control module and engine control module may cause the malfunction indicator light to turn on, and in some cases the vehicle may lose drive power.

Mazda CX-70 Hybrid Inverter Software May Cut Power high

Reported Oct 31, 2024 · Campaign 24V817000 · Model years: 2025

On these plug-in hybrid vehicles, improper inverter software may cause the malfunction indicator light to come on, and the vehicle may lose drive power while running in electric vehicle mode.

Mazda CX-70 and CX-90 Electrical Unit Software Issue medium

Reported Oct 31, 2024 · Campaign 24V814000 · Model years: 2025

In some Mazda CX-90 and CX-70 vehicles equipped with a Dash Electrical Supply Unit, several features may not work correctly during startup. This can affect the defroster, the seat belt warning, the 360-degree view monitor, and, on plug-in hybrid models, the system that cools the high-voltage battery.

Mazda CX-70 recall questions

How many recalls does the Mazda CX-70 have?

NHTSA lists 4 safety recalls covering the 2025 Mazda CX-70. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Mazda CX-70, CX-90 Fuel Gauge May Read Wrong" (NHTSA campaign 25V568000), reported Sep 2, 2025.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Mazda CX-70?

No. None of the 4 Mazda CX-70 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Mazda CX-70 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Mazda dealer, including for second owners.
