Mazda CX-70 recalls

4 recalls covering model years 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Mazda CX-90 and CX-70 fuel gauge may read wrong medium

Reported 2025-09-02 · Campaign 25V568000 · Model years: 2025

The fuel gauge on the dashboard of these mild hybrid Mazda SUVs can show an incorrect amount of fuel. You may believe you still have gas left when the tank is nearly empty. If that happens, the vehicle can run out of fuel and stall unexpectedly, which is dangerous in traffic or on the highway. A dealer will reprogram the computer that controls the gauge so it shows the correct reading, at no charge to you.

Mazda CX-90 and CX-70 can lose engine power high

Reported 2024-10-31 · Campaign 24V815000 · Model years: 2025

Mazda is recalling certain 2024 CX-90 and 2025 CX-70 SUVs. The software in the computers that run the engine and the transmission may have a fault. When that happens, warning lights come on the dashboard and the vehicle can lose drive power, meaning it may stop pulling while you are driving. Losing power in traffic makes a crash more likely. Dealers will load corrected software into both computers at no cost.

Mazda plug-in hybrids can lose power in electric mode high

Reported 2024-10-31 · Campaign 24V817000 · Model years: 2025

Mazda is recalling certain 2024 CX-90 and 2025 CX-70 plug-in hybrid SUVs. The software in the inverter — the part that sends power from the battery to the electric motor — is not correct. Because of this, warning lights may come on and the vehicle can lose drive power while it is running in electric mode. Losing power while driving makes a crash more likely. Dealers will install improved inverter software for free.

Mazda CX-70 and CX-90 dash unit software problem medium

Reported 2024-10-31 · Campaign 24V814000 · Model years: 2025

An electrical control unit behind the dashboard may not start up properly when you turn the vehicle on. When that happens, several things can quit at once: the windshield defroster, the seat belt warning, the 360-degree camera view, and, on plug-in hybrid models, the cooling system for the high-voltage battery. Losing the defroster in bad weather is the most likely everyday problem. A dealer will load improved software into that unit free of charge.
