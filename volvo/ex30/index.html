Volvo EX30 seat belt chime may not sound medium

Reported 2026-03-11 · Campaign 26V136000 · Model years: 2025

The seat belt warning system in these vehicles may fail to play the audible chime that is supposed to alert an unbelted occupant. This means the vehicle doesn't fully meet a federal safety standard for occupant protection.

Volvo EX30 Battery May Overheat and Catch Fire critical

Reported 2026-02-20 · Campaign 26V103000 · Model years: 2025

On certain EX30 vehicles, the high voltage battery may short circuit and overheat. Until the repair is complete, owners are advised to limit charging to a maximum of 70%. Dealers will inspect the battery and replace battery modules as necessary, free of charge.

Volvo EX30 High-Voltage Battery Fire Risk critical

Reported 2026-01-02 · Campaign 26V001000 · Model years: 2025

Some Volvo EX30 vehicles have a high-voltage battery that may experience a short circuit. When this happens, the battery can overheat.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).
