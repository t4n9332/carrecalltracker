Volvo EX30 recalls

3 recalls covering model years 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Volvo EX30 seat belt warning chime may not sound low

Reported 2026-03-11 · Campaign 26V136000 · Model years: 2025

The chime that reminds you to buckle up may not sound. A bad electrical connection at the audio module keeps the warning beep from playing, so a driver or passenger riding unbelted may never get the reminder. The seat belts themselves still work normally; only the alert is affected. Because of this, the vehicle does not meet the federal safety rule for seat belt warnings. A dealer will replace the connector parts at no charge.

Volvo EX30 high voltage battery can overheat and catch fire critical

Reported 2026-02-20 · Campaign 26V103000 · Model years: 2025

The large battery that powers some 2025 Volvo EX30 vehicles can short out inside and get too hot, which can lead to a fire. Until your vehicle is repaired, Volvo says you should charge the battery no higher than 70 percent. A dealer will inspect the battery and replace any battery sections that need it, free of charge. Owner letters went out in August 2026, and this recall replaces an earlier one.

Volvo EX30 high-voltage battery can overheat and catch fire critical

Reported 2026-01-02 · Campaign 26V001000 · Model years: 2025

The large battery that powers your EX30 can short out inside and get too hot, which Volvo says raises the risk of a fire. Until the vehicle is fixed, Volvo wants owners to park outside and away from buildings, and to charge the battery no higher than 70 percent. A dealer will inspect the battery and replace modules if needed, free of charge. Volvo says this recall has been replaced by a newer one, number 26V103.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).
