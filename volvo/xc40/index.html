Volvo rearview camera image may not appear in reverse medium

Reported Dec 23, 2025 · Campaign 25V908000 · Model years: 2024

Volvo is recalling a range of recent Volvo cars and SUVs. When the driver shifts into reverse, the picture from the rearview camera may not come up on the screen. Because of that, these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety rule. A dealer can install updated software, or the update can be sent to the car over the air. This recall replaces an earlier Volvo recall, and cars already repaired under that earlier recall need the new repair too.

Volvo EX40, XC40, XC90 May Lose Brake Function high

Reported Jun 12, 2025 · Campaign 25V392000 · Model years: 2024

On some Volvo EX40, XC40, XC40 BEV, and XC90 PHEV vehicles, using "B" mode or One Pedal Drive while coasting down a hill in certain driving situations can cause the vehicle to lose brake function. Losing the ability to brake properly in this way can make it harder to slow down or stop the vehicle when needed, which increases the risk of a crash on the road.

Volvo rearview camera image may not appear in reverse medium

Reported May 8, 2025 · Campaign 25V282000 · Model years: 2024

On these Volvo vehicles, the backup camera picture may not show up on the screen when you shift into reverse. The camera view is supposed to appear every time the vehicle is put in reverse, and because it may not, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for rear visibility. Volvo will fix this with a software update, either at a dealer or sent to the vehicle over the air.

Volvo XC40 left rear turn signal may not work medium

Reported Jan 10, 2024 · Campaign 24V010000 · Model years: 2024

Volvo is recalling certain 2024 XC40 vehicles. A fault in the software that runs the car's central electronics module can stop the left rear turn signal from lighting up. The signal light on that corner of the car may simply not come on when the driver signals a turn. Volvo says the software can be updated by a dealer, or sent to the car as an over the air update, free of charge.

Volvo XC40 recall questions

How many recalls does the Volvo XC40 have?

NHTSA lists 4 safety recalls covering the 2024 Volvo XC40. 1 of them is rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Volvo rearview camera image may not appear in reverse" (NHTSA campaign 25V908000), reported Dec 23, 2025.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Volvo XC40?

No. None of the 4 Volvo XC40 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Volvo XC40 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Volvo dealer, including for second owners.
