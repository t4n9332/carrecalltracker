Volvo Truck Windshield Wiper Motor May Fail medium

Reported Jul 24, 2023 · Campaign 23V511000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

On these trucks, the gears inside the windshield wiper motor may wear out sooner than they should, which can eventually cause the wiper motor to fail. When the wipers stop working, the driver has reduced visibility through the windshield. To correct this, dealers will replace the entire windshield wiper motor assembly on the affected trucks, at no charge to the owner.

Volvo VNL Trucks: Side Airbag May Not Deploy high

Reported Jun 4, 2023 · Campaign 23V403000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Volvo Trucks is recalling certain VNL and VHD trucks that are equipped with RollTek driver seats. On these trucks, a circuit needed for the side airbag is missing. Without this circuit in place, the side airbag will not deploy the way it is supposed to if the truck is involved in a crash. This means the airbag will not provide protection when needed.

Volvo VNL Air Tank Bracket Fasteners May Break high

Reported Dec 6, 2022 · Campaign 22V896000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

The bolts that hold the air tank bracket in place on these trucks were tightened too much, and some may have broken. If a fastener breaks, the air tank can come loose from the truck. A tank that detaches while the truck is on the road becomes a hazard for other vehicles nearby, and it raises the chance of a crash.

Volvo VNL Trucks May Shift Into Neutral high

Reported Oct 6, 2021 · Campaign 21V781000 · Model years: 2022

Some 2022 Volvo VNL trucks have electronic control unit software that may allow the transmission to shift into neutral unexpectedly while the truck is being driven. When this happens, the truck loses drive power in that moment even though the driver did not select neutral.

Volvo VNL, VNR Trucks Hubcap Can Separate medium

Reported Jun 2, 2021 · Campaign 21V417000 · Model years: 2022

On affected trucks, the spring that holds the hubcap in place can fail, causing the hubcap to separate from the wheel while the truck is being driven. A hubcap that comes off on the road can become a hazard for the truck and for other vehicles nearby.

Volvo VNL Alcoa Wheels May Bend or Break high

Reported Feb 19, 2021 · Campaign 21V098000 · Model years: 2022

Some 2022 VNL trucks equipped with specific Alcoa Mirror Polish wheels may have wheels that can't support the load under certain circumstances. This can cause the wheels to bend or break while in use.

Volvo VNL Truck Flywheel Bolts May Be Loose high

Reported Feb 11, 2021 · Campaign 21V065000 · Model years: 2022

On certain 2022 Volvo VNL trucks with Cummins X15 diesel engines, the bolts that attach the flywheel to the crankshaft may not have been tightened to the correct torque. This could allow the flywheel to come loose from the crankshaft.

Volvo VNL and VNR vehicles have wrong weight labels medium

Reported Jul 1, 2020 · Campaign 20V384000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Volvo is recalling certain VNL and VNR vehicles because of a mistake on the label that certifies the vehicle. The label lists a maximum weight the rear drive axles can carry that is higher than what the wheel hubs are actually rated to handle. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet federal certification requirements. Volvo will send owners corrected replacement labels at no cost. Volvo has a customer service line for owners who have questions about this recall.

Volvo trucks air tank strap bolts may fail medium

Reported Jul 2, 2019 · Campaign 19V510000 · Model years: 2019

Volvo Trucks is recalling certain VAH, VHD, VNL, and VNR trucks because the t-bolt clasps on the transmission auxiliary air tank straps may have been over-tightened. Over-tightening can deform the strap at the point where the t-bolt is anchored. If the strap is deformed, the t-bolt may disconnect from it, and the air tank could then drop to the ground.

Volvo VNL trucks may lose the ability to shift gears high

Reported Jul 2, 2019 · Campaign 19V509000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Volvo Trucks is recalling certain VAH, VHD, VNL, and VNR trucks. A fuse in the transmission circuit may blow. If it does, the transmission will not shift. Dealers will inspect the fuse and replace it if necessary, at no cost to the owner. Volvo Trucks is notifying owners, and the recall began August 7, 2019. Owners can call Volvo Trucks customer service and refer to the company's number for this recall, RVXX1903.

Volvo VNL Sleeper Bunk Window May Fall Off high

Reported Mar 18, 2019 · Campaign 19V208000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Volvo is recalling certain VNL and VNX trucks built with 740 and 760 sleeper cabs because the window in the sleeper bunk area may come loose and detach from the vehicle. The window is meant to stay securely in place, but on affected trucks it may separate and fall off instead. This recall covers trucks equipped with this sleeper window configuration.

Volvo VNL Clutch Part May Fail, Causing Movement high

Reported Jan 14, 2019 · Campaign 19V032000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Certain Volvo VNL trucks that are built with an automated manual transmission have an internal part inside the clutch assembly that may fail. If this part fails, it could cause the truck to move on its own without the driver meaning it to. This kind of unintended vehicle movement creates a safety concern for the driver and for anyone else nearby on the road.

Volvo VNL and VHD Truck Tires May Lose Air high

Reported Nov 15, 2018 · Campaign 18V811000 · Model years: 2019

Certain 2019 Volvo VNL and VHD trucks have specific Bridgestone tires — model M854, M860A, or M864 in size 425/65R22.5 — where the steel cords inside the tire's sidewall may become exposed. When this happens, the tire can lose air rapidly and unexpectedly while the truck is being driven. Because of this, the tires do not meet a federal safety standard for truck tires.

Volvo VNL Trucks Fuel Line May Burst critical

Reported Sep 25, 2018 · Campaign 18V668000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On certain drives, such as going down a long, steep hill, a screen inside the fuel pump's cooling circuit can become blocked or restricted. If that screen is restricted, the fuel line may burst. This is a problem with the fuel system on these trucks that only shows up in certain driving conditions, like extended downhill grades.

Volvo VNL And VNR Trucks Fifth Wheel Defect high

Reported Aug 16, 2018 · Campaign 18V542000 · Model years: 2019

Some Volvo trucks have a fifth wheel coupling, the part that connects the trailer to the truck, built with a sliding bracket that may be too narrow. Because the bracket doesn't fit correctly, it's possible for the trailer to separate from the truck while the vehicle is being driven, since the bracket isn't wide enough to keep the connection secure the way it needs to be.
