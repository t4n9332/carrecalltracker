Volvo VNR recalls

9 recalls covering model years 2021, 2023. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Volvo trucks' windshield wiper motor can quit working medium

Reported 2023-07-24 · Campaign 23V511000 · Model years: 2023

The gears inside the windshield wiper motor can wear down sooner than they should. Once they do, the motor stops turning and the wipers stop moving. In rain, snow, or road spray you would be left looking through a blurred or covered windshield with no way to clear it, and that can happen suddenly while you are driving. The dealer swaps in a whole new wiper motor assembly at no charge to you.

Volvo VNR Electric battery coolant leak can cause fire critical

Reported 2023-04-19 · Campaign 23V280000 · Model years: 2021, 2023

On certain 2020-2024 Volvo VNR Electric trucks, a coolant line inside the high-voltage battery pack may not be pushed all the way in and locked. If it is loose, coolant can leak out of the pack. That leak can cause an electrical short inside the battery, and a short in a large battery pack can lead to a fire. Volvo Trucks is working with the battery maker, Akasol, to inspect the batteries and replace them if needed.

Volvo VNR Electric trucks brake too hard on their own high

Reported 2023-04-07 · Campaign 23V255000 · Model years: 2021, 2023

Some Volvo VNR Electric trucks built for model years 2020 through 2023 have adaptive cruise control, the feature that slows the truck automatically to keep space from traffic ahead. When the truck is running light, that system can grab the brakes far harder than it should. The sudden hard braking can make the rear wheels skid or the brakes lock up, and the driver can lose control. Dealers will remove the radar parts and turn the feature off.

Volvo VNL and VNR trucks can lose a hubcap medium

Reported 2021-06-02 · Campaign 21V417000 · Model years: 2021

A spring inside the hubcap presses against it in a way that can let the hubcap work loose and fly off the wheel while the truck is moving. A hubcap that comes off becomes a loose object in the road, which other drivers can hit or swerve to avoid. The repair is simple: the dealer takes the hubcap off and does not put it back, at no cost to you. The recall covers certain 2021-2022 Volvo VAH, VDH, VNL and VNR trucks.

Volvo trucks may apply parking brakes without warning critical

Reported 2020-12-15 · Campaign 20V782000 · Model years: 2021

The air lines that work the truck's brakes plug into a pass-through plate using small brass fittings. On these trucks, the gripping jaws inside some fittings may have been damaged during factory leak testing. A damaged fitting can let go of the pressurized air line entirely. If that happens on the parking brake line, the air pressure that holds the parking brakes off is lost, and the parking brakes clamp down on their own, possibly while you are driving.

Volvo VNR and other trucks: brake pedal may break off high

Reported 2020-09-02 · Campaign 20V536000 · Model years: 2021

The metal plate that holds the brake pedal in place is made of cast metal, and it can crack at one of the points where it bolts on. If that happens, the brake pedal can come loose from its mounting. A pedal that has come loose will not work, so pressing it would not slow or stop the truck. Volvo says these trucks do not meet the federal safety rule for air brake systems.

Volvo trucks may have loose or missing front axle nut critical

Reported 2020-09-02 · Campaign 20V535000 · Model years: 2021

When the front axle was put together, a large nut that holds the steering knuckle onto the axle may have been left off or not tightened enough. The steering knuckle is the part the front wheel turns on. If that nut is missing or loose, the knuckle can come apart from the axle. If that happens while the truck is moving, the driver could suddenly lose control, and a crash could follow.

Volvo VNL and VNR weight label overstates axle rating low

Reported 2020-07-01 · Campaign 20V384000 · Model years: 2021

The certification sticker on these vehicles lists how much weight the rear drive axles are allowed to carry, and the number printed there is higher than what the wheel hubs can actually handle. If someone loads the vehicle up to the weight shown on that label, the rear wheel hubs can wear out and fail earlier than they should, which could lead to a crash. Volvo is mailing a corrected sticker; nothing on the vehicle itself needs to be repaired.

Volvo VNR truck steering bolts may be loose critical

Reported 2020-04-29 · Campaign 20V241000 · Model years: 2021

The bolts that hold the steering gear in place may not have been tightened enough at the factory. The steering gear is the part that turns your steering wheel movement into the wheels turning. If those bolts work loose, the driver can lose control of the truck. A Volvo Trucks dealer will tighten the steering gear bolts to the correct specification, free of charge. The recall started May 20, 2020.
