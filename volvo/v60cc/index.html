Volvo Steering Gear Screw May Lock Steering Wheel critical

Reported Jan 5, 2023 · Campaign 23V001000 · Model years: 2020

On certain Volvo V60CC, XC60, and XC90 vehicles, the steering gear may have been assembled with two screws stacked on top of each other instead of one in the correct spot. One of these screws could come loose and fall inside the steering gearbox housing. If a screw ends up inside the gearbox, it can cause the steering wheel to lock up while the vehicle is being driven.

Volvo V60CC, V90CC Fuel Pump Can Fail high

Reported Jun 2, 2021 · Campaign 21V414000 · Model years: 2020

On affected vehicles, a fuse for the low-pressure fuel pump can blow, causing the pump to stop working. If the fuel pump fails while you are driving, the engine can stall.

Volvo V60, V90 Cross Country Braking System Issue high

Reported Mar 12, 2020 · Campaign 20V144000 · Model years: 2020

Some Volvo V60 Cross Country and V90 Cross Country vehicles have a software and hardware incompatibility in the Automatic Emergency Braking (AEB) system. Because of this incompatibility, the AEB system may not detect obstacles and engage the brakes as it is designed to do.

Volvo V60CC recall questions

How many recalls does the Volvo V60CC have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2020 Volvo V60CC. 3 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Volvo Steering Gear Screw May Lock Steering Wheel" (NHTSA campaign 23V001000), reported Jan 5, 2023.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Volvo V60CC?

No. None of the 3 Volvo V60CC recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Volvo V60CC has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Volvo dealer, including for second owners.
