Volvo XC60 hybrid brake pedal connection may be loose high

Reported 2024-10-23 · Campaign 24V788000 · Model years: 2020

The brake pedal is joined to the braking system by a rod held in place with a screwed connection. On these Volvo hybrids, that connection may be loose. If it works its way apart, pressing the pedal may not slow the car the way it should, and you could lose your brakes while driving. A dealer simply tightens the connection. If the pedal ever feels loose, soft or different from normal, stop driving and call for help right away.
