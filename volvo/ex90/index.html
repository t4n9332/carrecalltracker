Volvo EX90 Power Tailgate May Suddenly Drop high

Reported Sep 30, 2025 · Campaign 25V654000 · Model years: 2025

On certain 2025 Volvo EX90 vehicles, a nut that holds part of the power tailgate mechanism in place — called a spindle nut — may come loose and detach from the tailgate's drive units. If this happens, the power tailgate could suddenly drop instead of staying open or closing normally. A tailgate that drops unexpectedly could hit someone standing near it, which increases the risk of injury.

Volvo EX90 Headlight Shutters May Close While Driving medium

Reported Feb 7, 2025 · Campaign 25V071000 · Model years: 2025

Volvo is recalling certain EX90 vehicles because of a software error in the low power controller. This error can cause the headlight shutters to close over both the high and low beams while the vehicle is being driven. When the shutters close, the headlights stop putting out light, which can leave the driver with little or no forward lighting on the road.

Volvo EX90 Second-Row Seat Bolts May Be Loose high

Reported Nov 19, 2024 · Campaign 24V869000 · Model years: 2025

Volvo is recalling certain EX90 vehicles because the bolts for the second-row free-standing seat may not be tightened properly. A seat that isn't securely bolted down may not properly hold the occupant in place during a crash, which could increase the risk of injury. Dealers will inspect the seat bolts and tighten them as needed to correct the problem.

Volvo EX90 recall questions

How many recalls does the Volvo EX90 have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2025 Volvo EX90. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Volvo EX90 Power Tailgate May Suddenly Drop" (NHTSA campaign 25V654000), reported Sep 30, 2025.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Volvo EX90?

No. None of the 3 Volvo EX90 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Volvo EX90 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Volvo dealer, including for second owners.
