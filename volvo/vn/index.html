Volvo VN and VNL Trucks Idler Gear Detaches high

Reported Apr 17, 2025 · Campaign 25V257000 · Model years: 2024

Volvo Trucks North America is recalling certain Volvo VN and VNL trucks because the fasteners on the idler gear bracket may not have been tightened properly. This can allow the idler gear to come loose from the engine.

Volvo trucks' electronic control unit may misread signals high

Reported Oct 23, 2024 · Campaign 24V790000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2024

These trucks use a Bendix electronic control unit that can be affected by electrical noise and a low signal in the power line carrier. This can cause the unit to process commands incorrectly or to stop working. This affects certain Volvo VN, VAH, VHD, and VNRE trucks. Volvo Trucks is recalling these vehicles so dealers can reprogram the control unit's software.

Volvo VN Trucks Risk Unintended Acceleration high

Reported Oct 3, 2024 · Campaign 24V741000 · Model years: 2024

On certain 2022-2024 Volvo VN trucks, if communication on the vehicle's internal computer network is interrupted while the truck is in limp mode, the accelerator pedal signal can be read incorrectly. This can cause the truck to accelerate on its own.

Volvo VN and VHD trucks carry wrong weight label medium

Reported Dec 5, 2023 · Campaign 23V816000 · Model years: 2024

Volvo Trucks is recalling certain VN and VHD trucks that have size 295/75 or 385/65 Continental tires on the front axle. The certification label on these trucks lists the wrong gross axle weight rating, the weight the axle is rated to carry. Because the listed rating is wrong, the truck can be loaded beyond what it should carry, and the truck does not meet the federal certification rule. Dealers will remove the label and put on a correct one, free of charge.

Volvo VN Truck Accelerator Sensor Disconnection Risk high

Reported Nov 22, 2023 · Campaign 23V793000 · Model years: 2024

Volvo is recalling certain VN trucks because the engine's throttle system may not return to idle fast enough if the sensor that reads the accelerator pedal position becomes disconnected. In plain terms, if that sensor comes loose, the engine could keep pushing power even after the driver lets off the gas pedal, instead of slowing back down right away as it's supposed to.

Volvo Truck Passenger Seat May Not Be Secured high

Reported Nov 15, 2023 · Campaign 23V769000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2024

Volvo Trucks is recalling certain VN and VHD trucks because the passenger side bench seat may not be securely attached to the truck's floor. The attachment points holding the seat down may not be strong enough to keep it in place. To fix this, dealers will replace the weld nut with two nuts and a washer, strengthening how the seat is held to the floor. This repair will be done free of charge.

Volvo VN, VAH Trucks Steering Fasteners May Loosen high

Reported Jun 23, 2023 · Campaign 23V441000 · Model years: 2024

On certain Volvo VN and VAH trucks, the fasteners that hold the steering gear in place may not have been tightened properly at the factory. Because these steering gear fasteners may be loose, they could work themselves looser over time as the truck is used.
