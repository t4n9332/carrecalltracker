Volvo VN recalls

7 recalls covering model years 2020, 2021, 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Volvo VN trucks: loose engine gear bolts can stall engine high

Reported 2025-04-17 · Campaign 25V257000 · Model years: 2024

Inside the engine of these Volvo trucks, a gear called the idler gear is held in place by bolts on a bracket. Those bolts may not have been tightened properly. If they loosen, the gear can come away from the engine and tear up parts inside it. The engine can then stall, leaving you without power in traffic. Dealers will inspect the truck and replace the turbo compound unit if needed, at no cost to you.

Volvo VN and VAH trucks lose electronic safety systems high

Reported 2024-10-23 · Campaign 24V790000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2024

These trucks have a control unit that runs several driver-assist and braking systems. Electrical interference and a weak signal can make that unit misread commands or quit working. When it does, anti-lock brakes, traction control, stability control, adaptive cruise control, and the collision warning system can work poorly or stop working at all. A dealer fixes this by loading new software into the control unit, which does not require replacing parts.

Volvo VN trucks can speed up on their own high

Reported 2024-10-03 · Campaign 24V741000 · Model years: 2024

On certain 2022-2024 Volvo VN trucks, the computers that talk to each other over the truck's internal network can lose communication, which puts the truck into a reduced-power "limp" mode. When that happens, the truck can read the gas pedal wrong and speed up when the driver is not asking it to. That failure also means these trucks do not meet the federal safety rule for accelerator controls. Dealers will load corrected software into the control unit at no cost.

Volvo VN and VHD trucks have wrong weight label medium

Reported 2023-12-05 · Campaign 23V816000 · Model years: 2024

Certain 2024 Volvo VN and VHD trucks with size 295/75 or 385/65 Continental tires on the front axle have a sticker that lists the wrong maximum weight the front axle can carry. Because the number on the certification label is too high, the truck can be loaded past what it should safely handle. Nothing mechanical is broken — the information is simply wrong. A dealer will peel off the incorrect label and put on a correct one, free of charge.

Volvo VN trucks may not return to idle high

Reported 2023-11-22 · Campaign 23V793000 · Model years: 2024

On some 2023-2024 Volvo VN trucks, the sensor that tells the engine where the gas pedal is can come unplugged. If that happens, the engine is supposed to drop back to idle within two seconds, but it may not. The engine could keep pulling while you are trying to slow down, which makes the truck hard to control. This breaks the federal rule for accelerator controls. Volvo will put in a new throttle pedal and update the software.

Volvo VN and VHD trucks: passenger bench seat may loosen medium

Reported 2023-11-15 · Campaign 23V769000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2024

The bench seat on the passenger side may not be properly attached to the floor. The welded nut holding it down was not made strong enough, and that same area anchors the seat belt. In a crash the attachment points can fail, letting the whole bench seat and the person in it shift out of place. Dealers will fix it by taking out the weld nut and putting in two nuts and a washer instead.

Volvo heavy trucks may have loose steering gear bolts high

Reported 2023-06-23 · Campaign 23V441000 · Model years: 2024

The bolts that hold the steering gear in place may not have been tightened properly at the factory. The steering gear is the part that turns your steering wheel motion into the wheels actually turning. If those bolts are loose, you can lose control of the truck while driving. The dealer will tighten the steering gear bolts to the correct specification. The check and the repair cost you nothing. Volvo is mailing letters to owners of the affected trucks.
