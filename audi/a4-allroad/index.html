Audi A4 Allroad recalls

5 recalls covering model years 2018, 2020, 2021, 2023. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Audi front passenger airbag may switch itself off high

Reported 2024-04-03 · Campaign 24V251000 · Model years: 2023

An electrical connection inside the front passenger seat can work loose. That connection belongs to the system that senses whether someone is sitting in the seat, and if it comes loose the front passenger airbag is switched off. In a crash, that airbag would not inflate to protect the person riding up front, so injuries could be worse. The dealer will replace the seat's occupant detection control unit free of charge.

Audi rear axle alignment may cause uneven tire wear medium

Reported 2022-01-26 · Campaign 22V034000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

These Audis were part of an earlier recall (21V-295) that involved work on the rear axle. Afterward, the shop was supposed to check that the rear wheels were still lined up correctly, and on some cars that check was skipped. If the rear axle is out of alignment, the tires can wear out early or wear unevenly, which increases the chance of a crash. Dealers will inspect the rear axle, adjust the alignment if needed, and replace any badly worn tires at no charge.

Audi A4, A5, S4, S5 passenger airbag may shut off high

Reported 2019-07-24 · Campaign 19V547000 · Model years: 2018

These Audis have a sensor system in the front passenger seat that tells the car whether someone is sitting there. The cable connected to that sensor can corrode. When it does, the car's software can wrongly decide something is broken and switch off the front passenger airbag. If a crash happens while the airbag is switched off, the person in that seat has less protection and can be hurt more badly. Dealers will add cable ties to the wiring so the cable is protected.

Audi A5 and A4 Allroad passenger airbag screw loose medium

Reported 2018-11-27 · Campaign 18V836000 · Model years: 2018

On these Audis, a screw holding the front passenger airbag module in place may not have been tightened enough at the factory. If the screw is loose, the airbag may not deploy the way it is supposed to in a crash, so a front passenger might not get the protection the airbag is designed to give. Dealers will inspect that mounting screw and tighten it or replace it as needed, free of charge to the owner.

Audi A4, A5 door speaker trim leaves sharp edges low

Reported 2017-10-25 · Campaign 17V675000 · Model years: 2018

The aluminum trim around the door speakers on some 2017-2018 Audi A4, A4 allroad, and A5 Sportback models can come loose. Once a piece lifts away from the door panel, it leaves a sharp metal edge where people put their hands, reach for the door, or brush past while getting in and out. That edge can cut someone. Nothing about how the car drives, steers, or stops is affected. Dealers replace the trim free.
