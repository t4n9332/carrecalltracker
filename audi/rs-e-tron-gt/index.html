Audi rearview camera may fail to show image medium

Reported Dec 19, 2025 · Campaign 25V900000 · Model years: 2024

Audi is recalling certain 2019 through 2026 vehicles, including A6, A7, Q7, Q8 and e-tron models. A software error can keep the backup camera picture from showing on the screen the way it is supposed to. Because the rear camera view may not appear, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule covering what a driver must be able to see behind the vehicle.

Audi E-Tron GT Battery Short Circuit Fire Risk critical

Reported Sep 30, 2024 · Campaign 24V726000 · Model years: 2024

Audi says the high-voltage battery in some E-Tron GT and RS E-Tron GT vehicles may develop a short circuit inside a battery module. Dealers will install new diagnostic software as the fix. Until then, some vehicles will have their battery data monitored online at no cost; if a problem is found, the owner will be told to charge only to 80% capacity until the module is replaced. Vehicles without online monitoring will get a dealer inspection instead.

Audi E-Tron GT Front Brake Hose Can Leak high

Reported Jun 20, 2024 · Campaign 24V465000 · Model years: 2024

On these cars, the brake hoses that run to the front wheels can develop tears. A tear can let brake fluid leak out, which can cause that brake circuit to stop working properly. When this happens, the brake pedal may need to be pushed down farther than normal, and the brakes may not slow the car as well as they should, making it take longer to come to a stop.

Audi E-Tron GT High-Voltage Battery Fire Risk critical

Reported Mar 22, 2024 · Campaign 24V228000 · Model years: 2024

In these electric Audis, the high-voltage battery pack may develop a short circuit inside its battery modules.

Audi portable charging cables can overheat and cause fire critical

Reported Dec 13, 2023 · Campaign 23V842000 · Model years: 2024

Audi is recalling certain electric and other models over the portable charging system supplied with the vehicle. When the industrial 220-volt/240-volt plug is used at the 100% charge setting, some home outlets cannot handle the electrical current the charging system needs. The outlet or the charging cable can then overheat. Dealers will supply a new 220-volt/240-volt portable charging cable with a temperature sensor built in, free of charge.

Audi RS E-TRON GT recall questions

How many recalls does the Audi RS E-TRON GT have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2024 Audi RS E-TRON GT. 4 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Audi rearview camera may fail to show image" (NHTSA campaign 25V900000), reported Dec 19, 2025.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Audi RS E-TRON GT?

No. None of the 5 Audi RS E-TRON GT recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Audi RS E-TRON GT has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Audi dealer, including for second owners.
