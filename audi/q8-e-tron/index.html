Audi Q8 E-Tron Brake Line Connection May Leak high

Reported Jun 10, 2024 · Campaign 24V428000 · Model years: 2024

Audi is recalling certain Q8 E-Tron Quattro and Q8 Sportback E-Tron Quattro vehicles because a brake line connection may have been tightened too much. The connection may fail, allowing brake fluid to leak and reducing braking ability. The brake pedal may travel farther, or the vehicle may need more distance to stop. Dealers will inspect the brake line and replace it if needed, free of charge.
