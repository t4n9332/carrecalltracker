Audi e-tron brake pedal can detach from brake booster critical

Reported 2026-04-15 · Campaign 26V240000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Audi is recalling certain 2019-2023 e-tron Quattro, 2020-2023 e-tron Sportback, and 2024 Q8 e-tron, Q8 Sportback e-tron, SQ8 e-tron and SQ8 Sportback e-tron vehicles. The fastener that joins the brake pedal to the brake booster may have been put together incorrectly at the factory. If it works loose, the brake pedal can come apart from the booster and you would lose braking. Dealers will inspect and tighten that pushrod screw joint. This expands an earlier recall, 24V621.

Audi rearview camera image may fail to display medium

Reported 2025-12-19 · Campaign 25V900000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

A software problem can keep the backup camera picture from showing up on the dashboard screen when you shift into reverse. Without that image, you cannot see what is directly behind the car — a person or an object — so you are more likely to hit something while backing up. Audi says the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility rule. The dealer will update the software for free.
