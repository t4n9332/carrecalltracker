Audi rearview camera may fail to show image medium

Reported Dec 19, 2025 · Campaign 25V900000 · Model years: 2019

Audi is recalling certain 2019 through 2026 vehicles, including A6, A7, Q7, Q8 and e-tron models. A software error can keep the backup camera picture from showing on the screen the way it is supposed to. Because the rear camera view may not appear, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule covering what a driver must be able to see behind the vehicle.

Audi S8 Engine Control Module Moisture Risk high

Reported Feb 24, 2021 · Campaign 21V111000 · Model years: 2019

Some Audi vehicles are missing a small sealing pin in the connector for the engine's control computer. Without that pin, moisture can get into the connector, and moisture inside the connector can cause the control computer to malfunction. If the control computer malfunctions, the engine may stall while you are driving. Dealers will check the connector for the missing pin and for corrosion, and fix it if needed.

Audi A8 Front Passenger Seat Track Rivet May Fail high

Reported Jun 5, 2019 · Campaign 19V415000 · Model years: 2019

Audi is recalling certain A8 vehicles equipped with rear relaxation seats because a rivet in the front passenger seat track may fail. If that happens, a bracket in the seat track may separate. The notice identifies the front passenger seat track as the affected part. Audi will notify owners, and dealers will replace the passenger seat tracks at no charge.

Audi A8 recall questions

How many recalls does the Audi A8 have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2019 Audi A8. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Audi rearview camera may fail to show image" (NHTSA campaign 25V900000), reported Dec 19, 2025.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Audi A8?

No. None of the 3 Audi A8 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Audi A8 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Audi dealer, including for second owners.
