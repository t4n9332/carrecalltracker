Audi A6 recalls

16 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2023. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Audi A6, A7, Q8 Lane Departure Warning May Deactivate medium

Reported 2026-03-04 · Campaign 26V125000 · Model years: 2019

These Audi vehicles were already repaired earlier under a different recall. Because of a software error, the lane departure warning system, the feature that alerts the driver if the car drifts out of its lane, may be switched off. The driver may not realize the system is off and may count on warnings that will not come. A dealer will update the software to correct it.

Audi rearview camera may fail to show image medium

Reported 2025-12-19 · Campaign 25V900000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2023

Audi is recalling certain 2019 through 2026 vehicles, including A6, A7, Q7, Q8 and e-tron models. A software error can keep the backup camera picture from showing on the screen the way it is supposed to. Because the rear camera view may not appear, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule covering what a driver must be able to see behind the vehicle.

Audi Models: Instrument Panel Display May Fail medium

Reported 2025-03-31 · Campaign 25V201000 · Model years: 2021

Due to a software error, the instrument panel display on affected vehicles may fail, meaning it can stop showing information to the driver.

Audi A6, A7, S7 Wrong Brake Fluid Cap Risk high

Reported 2023-08-25 · Campaign 23V601000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Some of these Audi vehicles may have a brake fluid reservoir cap that was installed with the wrong label. Because the cap's label does not match what should be used, it is possible for the wrong type of brake fluid to end up in the vehicle. Using the incorrect brake fluid can reduce how well the brakes work, making the vehicle less able to slow down or come to a stop when needed.

Audi Liquid Spill Can Cut Engine Power high

Reported 2022-11-21 · Campaign 22V861000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

If liquid is spilled in the back seat, it can leak into a part called the gateway control module and cause it to shut down. When this control module shuts down, it can suddenly reduce how much power the engine is producing while the vehicle is being driven.

Audi backup camera screen may go blank in reverse medium

Reported 2022-10-05 · Campaign 22V742000 · Model years: 2021

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2021 and 2022 Audi cars and SUVs because of a software problem with the backup camera display. When the vehicle is put into reverse, the screen may go blank or black instead of showing the view behind the vehicle. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for rear visibility. Dealers will update the software in the infotainment unit, the system that runs the center screen.

Audi A6, A7, S6, S7 Fuel Level Sensor Problem high

Reported 2022-03-14 · Campaign 22V155000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

In some Audi A6, A7, S6, and S7 vehicles, the fuel level sensor in the tank may stick. This can cause the fuel gauge on the instrument cluster to show an inaccurate reading, so it may not reflect how much fuel is actually in the tank. Because the gauge can be wrong, a driver may run out of fuel without realizing it, which can cause the engine to stall while driving.

Audi rear axle alignment may not have been checked medium

Reported 2022-01-26 · Campaign 22V034000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2019 through 2021 Audi cars and SUVs, including A4, A5, A6, A7, A8, Q5, Q7, Q8 and S and RS versions. These vehicles had repairs done under an earlier recall, and the rear axle alignment may not have been checked afterward as it should have been. Rear axle alignment that is off can wear the tires out early or wear them unevenly.

Audi and VW rear belts may not secure child seats high

Reported 2021-08-05 · Campaign 21V606000 · Model years: 2021

Volkswagen and Audi are recalling certain 2021 and 2022 cars and SUVs because of a problem with the rear seat belts. The part of the belt that locks it in place for a child seat may switch off too early, so the child seat may not be held securely. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule on protecting people in a crash. Dealers will check the middle rear seat belts and replace them if needed.

Audi rear axle lock nut may break high

Reported 2021-04-26 · Campaign 21V295000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On certain Audi models, the lock nut on a rear axle trailing arm may break because of corrosion. If this happens, the rear axle can become misaligned, affecting how the vehicle handles.

Audi passenger airbag may not open properly in crash high

Reported 2021-03-10 · Campaign 21V159000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Audi is recalling certain A6, A6 Allroad, A7, S6, S7, RS6 Avant and RS7 vehicles. The passenger airbag has a line in it that is meant to split open when the airbag goes off, and that line may not have been made correctly. Dealers will inspect the passenger airbag and repair it if needed, at no cost. Audi is contacting owners about the recall, and a customer service number is available for owners who have questions.

Audi A6, Q7 Door Sensor Connector May Detach high

Reported 2020-12-02 · Campaign 20V749000 · Model years: 2021

Volkswagen Group of America is recalling certain Audi A6 Allroad and Q7 vehicles because the electrical connector to the door crash sensor can come loose in either front door. If that connector detaches, it can delay how quickly the airbags, side curtain airbags, and seat-belt pretensioners activate during a side-impact crash. That delay can raise the risk of injury in a side-impact collision.

Audi A6 and A7 passenger airbag may deploy improperly high

Reported 2020-05-13 · Campaign 20V274000 · Model years: 2020

In these Audi vehicles, the passenger front airbag may not come through the instrument panel the way it is designed to. The dashboard has a seam that is meant to split open so the airbag can push through and inflate in a crash. On the recalled cars, that may not happen properly. The notice also says small plastic pieces could end up inside the passenger compartment. Dealers are replacing the entire instrument panel to fix it.

Audi A6 hybrid models: starter generator fire risk critical

Reported 2020-03-19 · Campaign 20V153000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Some 2019-2020 Audi A6 vehicles equipped with mild-hybrid technology and 2.0 TFSI engines may allow moisture to get into the starter generator. If moisture gets inside, it can cause an electrical short circuit in that component.

Audi A6, A7, Q7: Fuel Rails May Leak critical

Reported 2019-01-16 · Campaign 19V035000 · Model years: 2018

On some of these Audi vehicles, the fuel rails on the left and right side of the engine may leak. These are the pipes that deliver fuel from the fuel system to the engine's fuel injectors. A leak in either rail lets fuel escape from where it should stay contained, in the area around the engine, instead of staying inside the closed fuel system as designed.

Audi Seat Sensor Fault May Stop Airbag high

Reported 2018-06-04 · Campaign 18V370000 · Model years: 2018

On these vehicles, the front passenger seat has a body-sensing mat built into it. Over time, this mat can become stressed or worn. When that happens, it can cause the control module that detects whether someone is sitting in the passenger seat to malfunction. If this control module malfunctions, the front passenger airbag may not deploy properly if the vehicle is in a crash.
