Audi E-Tron rear seat belts may not lock child seats high

Reported 2025-12-19 · Campaign 25V899000 · Model years: 2025

On certain 2025 Audi Q6 E-Tron, SQ6 E-Tron and related Sportback E-Tron models, the rear seat belt has a locking feature that is supposed to click on and hold a child seat tightly in place. That feature may not engage, so a child seat installed with the seat belt could move around instead of staying firmly secured. Dealers will inspect the right and left rear seat belt assemblies and replace them if needed, at no cost to you.

Audi Q6 and SQ6 e-tron rearview camera shows blurry image medium

Reported 2025-04-09 · Campaign 25V225000 · Model years: 2025

The camera that shows what is behind you when you back up may display a fuzzy, unclear picture. A blurry image makes it harder to see people, pets or objects behind the car while reversing. Because the picture is not clear enough, these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility rule. A dealer will fit a new rearview camera, and will also check the surround view cameras and replace them if needed.

Audi Q6 e-tron backup camera may not display medium

Reported 2025-04-09 · Campaign 25V226000 · Model years: 2025

On these Audi Q6 and SQ6 electric SUVs, the backup camera picture may come up late or not turn on at all after you shift into reverse. Federal rules require the image to show when you back up, and these vehicles do not meet that rule. Without it, you see less of what is behind the SUV, so there is a greater chance of backing into someone or something. A dealer will update the camera's software for free.
