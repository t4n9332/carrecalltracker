Audi E-Tron Models: Rear Seat Belt May Not Lock high

Reported Dec 19, 2025 · Campaign 25V899000 · Model years: 2025

Audi is recalling certain 2025 SQ6 E-Tron, SQ6 Sportback E-Tron, Q6 Sportback E-Tron, A6, A5, A6 Sportback E-Tron, Q6 E-Tron, S5, and S6 Sportback E-Tron vehicles. The automatic locking retractor on the rear seat belts may fail to engage when a child seat is installed using the seat belt. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet federal occupant protection and seat belt standards.
