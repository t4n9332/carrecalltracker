Audi E-Tron rear seat belts may not lock child seats high

Reported 2025-12-19 · Campaign 25V899000 · Model years: 2025

On certain 2025 Audi Q6 E-Tron, SQ6 E-Tron and related Sportback E-Tron models, the rear seat belt has a locking feature that is supposed to click on and hold a child seat tightly in place. That feature may not engage, so a child seat installed with the seat belt could move around instead of staying firmly secured. Dealers will inspect the right and left rear seat belt assemblies and replace them if needed, at no cost to you.
