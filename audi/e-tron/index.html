Audi E-TRON recalls

Audi rearview camera may fail to show image medium

Reported Dec 19, 2025 · Campaign 25V900000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Audi is recalling certain 2019 through 2026 vehicles, including A6, A7, Q7, Q8 and e-tron models. A software error can keep the backup camera picture from showing on the screen the way it is supposed to. Because the rear camera view may not appear, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule covering what a driver must be able to see behind the vehicle.

Audi E-Tron Brake Pedal May Detach critical

Reported Aug 19, 2024 · Campaign 24V621000 · Model years: 2019

A bolt that connects the brake pedal to the brake booster may not have been properly installed during manufacturing. If this fastener fails, the brake pedal can come apart from the brake booster. When that happens, the vehicle can lose its ability to brake normally, which increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will inspect the brake booster and repair it as needed, free of charge.

Audi E-Tron High-Voltage Battery May Overheat critical

Reported Dec 20, 2023 · Campaign 23V867000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Some Audi E-Tron vehicles have a high-voltage battery that may overheat. Dealers will install onboard software that keeps an eye on how well the battery modules are performing. If a module is found to have a problem, dealers will replace it. This applies to the E-Tron Sportback Quattro and E-Tron Quattro models covered by this recall, and the work will not cost owners anything. Audi has already begun mailing notification letters to owners about this issue.

Audi portable charging cables can overheat and cause fire critical

Reported Dec 13, 2023 · Campaign 23V842000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Audi is recalling certain electric and other models over the portable charging system supplied with the vehicle. When the industrial 220-volt/240-volt plug is used at the 100% charge setting, some home outlets cannot handle the electrical current the charging system needs. The outlet or the charging cable can then overheat. Dealers will supply a new 220-volt/240-volt portable charging cable with a temperature sensor built in, free of charge.

Audi Rearview Camera Display May Stop Working medium

Reported Oct 26, 2022 · Campaign 22V806000 · Model years: 2021

On a range of Audi models, the infotainment system's main unit can become damaged internally when the vehicle is shut off. When this happens, the rearview camera image will not display the next time the vehicle is started, and the vehicle no longer meets the federal rule requiring a working rear visibility display.

Audi backup camera screen may go blank in reverse medium

Reported Oct 5, 2022 · Campaign 22V742000 · Model years: 2021

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2021 and 2022 Audi cars and SUVs because of a software problem with the backup camera display. When the vehicle is put into reverse, the screen may go blank or black instead of showing the view behind the vehicle. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for rear visibility. Dealers will update the software in the infotainment unit, the system that runs the center screen.

Audi E-Tron Brake Booster Corrosion Issue high

Reported Sep 13, 2021 · Campaign 21V707000 · Model years: 2021

Certain 2021 Audi E-Tron Quattro and E-Tron Sportback Quattro vehicles may develop corrosion inside the brake booster. Over time, this corrosion can cause several systems to stop working, including the Anti-Lock Brake System (ABS), Electronic Stability Control (ESC), the speedometer display, and brake assist. This means multiple safety systems tied to braking could be affected at the same time in these vehicles.

Audi and VW rear belts may not secure child seats high

Reported Aug 5, 2021 · Campaign 21V606000 · Model years: 2021

Volkswagen and Audi are recalling certain 2021 and 2022 cars and SUVs because of a problem with the rear seat belts. The part of the belt that locks it in place for a child seat may switch off too early, so the child seat may not be held securely. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule on protecting people in a crash. Dealers will check the middle rear seat belts and replace them if needed.

Audi E-Tron glove box may block knee airbag high

Reported Aug 2, 2021 · Campaign 21V595000 · Model years: 2021

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2021 Audi E-Tron Quattro and E-Tron Sportback Quattro vehicles. The right hinge pin that holds the glove box cover may not be properly secured. If it is loose, the trim on the glove box lid can get in the way of the knee airbag as that airbag inflates, so the airbag may not open the way it is designed to. Dealers will look at the glove box and secure the hinge pin if needed.

Audi E-Tron Brake Rotor and Shaft Separation Risk high

Reported Jul 26, 2021 · Campaign 21V569000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2021 E-Tron Quattro and 2020-2021 Audi E-Tron Sportback Quattro vehicles because a rotor and shaft inside the integrated brake system may have been welded improperly and could separate. If this separation happens, it can reduce the vehicle's braking assistance and cause a loss of the Antilock Brake System and Electronic Stability Control, which raises the risk of a crash.

Audi e-tron battery may let moisture in, fire risk critical

Reported Jun 10, 2019 · Campaign 19V434000 · Model years: 2019

Volkswagen's Audi brand is recalling certain 2019 Audi e-tron vehicles. A faulty seal on the charging socket can let moisture get into the high voltage battery system. Audi says that if warning lights appear along with the message "Electrical system: fault," owners should immediately stop driving, park outdoors away from buildings, and not charge the vehicle. Dealers will install a new seal and, if needed, replace affected parts of the system at no cost to the owner.

Audi E-TRON recall questions

How many recalls does the Audi E-TRON have?

NHTSA lists 11 safety recalls covering the 2019–2021 Audi E-TRON. 8 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Audi rearview camera may fail to show image" (NHTSA campaign 25V900000), reported Dec 19, 2025.

Which Audi E-TRON model year has the most recalls?

The 2021 Audi E-TRON has the most, with 9 recalls. The 2020 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 4. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Audi E-TRON?

No. None of the 11 Audi E-TRON recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Audi E-TRON has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Audi dealer, including for second owners.
