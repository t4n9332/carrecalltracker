Audi Q3 recalls

10 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Volkswagen Tiguan and Audi Q3 rearview camera may not display medium

Reported 2025-02-12 · Campaign 25V082000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Volkswagen is recalling certain Tiguan and Arteon vehicles and certain Audi Q3 vehicles. The control unit that runs the backup camera can hit an error when the vehicle is started, and when that happens the rearview camera image does not show up the way it should. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal government's rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will install updated software for the camera control unit free of charge.

Audi SUVs and A4 Allroad Risk Passenger Airbag Shutoff high

Reported 2024-04-03 · Campaign 24V251000 · Model years: 2024

An electrical connection in the front passenger seat for the occupant detection system may loosen. If it loosens, the system can deactivate the front passenger air bag, meaning the air bag would not be active for that seat.

Volkswagen and Audi Tire Pressure Monitor Software Issue medium

Reported 2022-10-31 · Campaign 22V815000 · Model years: 2019

Your vehicle's tire pressure monitoring system is supposed to warn you when tire pressure gets too low. In these vehicles, if all four tires lose air pressure at the same time, the system may not detect this quickly enough to warn you. This means you might not get a timely alert if every tire loses pressure at once. Dealers will fix the problem by updating the tire pressure monitoring software at no cost.

Audi Q3 fuel may spill from filler neck medium

Reported 2022-09-08 · Campaign 22V673000 · Model years: 2021

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2021 and 2022 Audi Q3 vehicles. A vent pipe inside the fuel tank may have been damaged while the tank was being assembled. If that pipe is damaged, gasoline can spill out of the filler neck when someone opens the fuel cap. Dealers will inspect the vent pipe inside the tank and, if it is damaged, replace the charcoal canister and the fuel tank, all free of charge. Notification letters were mailed November 1, 2022.

Audi and VW rear belts may not secure child seats high

Reported 2021-08-05 · Campaign 21V606000 · Model years: 2021

Volkswagen and Audi are recalling certain 2021 and 2022 cars and SUVs because of a problem with the rear seat belts. The part of the belt that locks it in place for a child seat may switch off too early, so the child seat may not be held securely. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule on protecting people in a crash. Dealers will check the middle rear seat belts and replace them if needed.

Audi Q3 Steering Rack Can Lock Unexpectedly high

Reported 2021-01-27 · Campaign 21V027000 · Model years: 2019

A part in the steering system, called a circlip, was not installed correctly at the factory because of an issue with another part, the belt pulley. This means the circlip may not properly hold the steering rack in place. If it comes loose, it can catch on nearby parts and cause the steering wheel to lock up while you are driving, making the vehicle hard or impossible to steer.

Audi Q3 Brake Pedal Plate May Bend Or Detach high

Reported 2020-12-16 · Campaign 20V786000 · Model years: 2020

Some Audi Q3 vehicles have a brake pedal plate, the part the driver presses to brake, that can bend or come loose when pressed hard during sudden stopping. If the plate bends or detaches, there is less surface area for the driver's foot to press against, making it harder to apply the brakes fully and consistently, which can affect how well the vehicle responds when the driver needs to stop.

Audi Q3 Driver Seat Bolt May Fail high

Reported 2020-10-19 · Campaign 20V644000 · Model years: 2020

The driver's seat frame in this vehicle may not have been bolted to the seat rail correctly. If this bolt connection fails, the seat may not stay securely attached. This increases the risk of injury to the driver if the vehicle is in a crash. To fix this, dealers will inspect the bolt connection and, if necessary, replace the seat structure, free of charge.

Audi Q3 Passenger Airbag May Not Deploy Properly high

Reported 2020-05-13 · Campaign 20V273000 · Model years: 2019

On some 2019 Audi Q3 vehicles, the front passenger airbag may not properly deploy through the tear seam in the instrument panel in front of the passenger seat.

Audi Q3 May Not Warn of Turn Signal Failure medium

Reported 2019-05-23 · Campaign 19V385000 · Model years: 2018

Some Audi Q3 vehicles with LED headlights may have a front turn signal that stops working without alerting the driver. Normally a dashboard indicator would let the driver know a turn signal isn't working, but on these vehicles that warning may not appear. Because the driver isn't told the signal has failed, they may turn or change lanes without a working signal, which increases the risk of a crash.
