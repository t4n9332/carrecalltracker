Audi Q4 e-tron and VW ID.4 may lose power high

Reported 2025-02-28 · Campaign 25V125000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

These electric vehicles have a part called the on-board charger that keeps the small 12-volt battery topped up. That battery runs the car's basic electronics. If the charger fails, the 12-volt battery stops being charged, and the vehicle can lose drive power — meaning it may stop pulling and coast to a stop, possibly in traffic. The dealer will replace the on-board charger free of charge.

Audi Q4 e-tron wrong headlight software affects parking lights medium

Reported 2024-05-22 · Campaign 24V361000 · Model years: 2024

Some 2022-2024 Q4 e-tron and Q4 e-tron Sportback vehicles were built with the wrong software in the module that controls the headlights. Because of that mistake, the parking lights may not work the way they should when the headlights are on and you switch on a turn signal. That makes your car harder for other drivers to see in those moments. These vehicles do not meet the federal rule for lights. A dealer reloads the correct software.

Audi electric models: portable charging cable can overheat critical

Reported 2023-12-13 · Campaign 23V842000 · Model years: 2024

These Audis came with a portable charging cable that plugs into a 220/240-volt outlet, the kind used for a large appliance. At the 100% charge setting, the cable can draw more electricity than some home outlets can handle, and the outlet or the cable can get dangerously hot. Overheating like this can start a fire. Audi says to stop using that cable for now and charge with the 110-volt cable or at a public charging station until you receive a free replacement.
