Mercedes-Benz Door Lock Defect May Cause Rollaway high

Reported 2026-07-24 · Campaign 26V481000 · Model years: 2021

Certain Mercedes-Benz vehicles have a micro-switch in the driver's door lock that can corrode. When this happens, the switch may fail to detect that the door is open, which can prevent the electronic parking brake's Auto-Park feature from engaging on its own. Without the parking brake engaging automatically, the vehicle may roll away on its own.

Mercedes-Benz vehicles may lose automatic emergency call system medium

Reported 2022-05-20 · Campaign 22V365000 · Model years: 2021

Mercedes-Benz is recalling a wide range of its cars and SUVs, including many AMG models. A software error on the SIM card inside the vehicle's communication module can cause the car to lose its mobile network connection. When that happens, the emergency call system is disabled. That is the system an occupant uses to reach an emergency services call center. The software will be updated either over the air or by a dealer, free of charge.

Mercedes-Benz AMG Models Rearview Camera Software Failure medium

Reported 2021-05-14 · Campaign 21V354000 · Model years: 2021

A software problem in these vehicles' MBUX multimedia system can cause the display screen to stay black, or to reboot after the vehicle starts up. Because the rearview camera image appears on this same display, the software problem can also stop the rearview camera picture from showing properly when the driver is backing up. This means the vehicle's rear visibility system may not work the way it is supposed to.

Mercedes A35 Missing Child Seat Anchor Bar medium

Reported 2021-01-29 · Campaign 21V034000 · Model years: 2021

Some 2021 Mercedes-Benz A220, A35 AMG, and CLA250 vehicles may be missing the right-side anchor bar from the ISOFIX child seat bracket on the right-rear seat. Without that bar in place, a child seat that uses the ISOFIX system cannot be properly attached and secured to the right-rear seat.
