Mercedes-Benz Door Lock Defect May Cause Rollaway high

Reported Jul 24, 2026 · Campaign 26V481000 · Model years: 2025

Certain Mercedes-Benz vehicles have a micro-switch in the driver's door lock that can corrode. When this happens, the switch may fail to detect that the door is open, which can prevent the electronic parking brake's Auto-Park feature from engaging on its own. Without the parking brake engaging automatically, the vehicle may roll away on its own.

Mercedes-Benz GLA 250 door may not open inside high

Reported Mar 6, 2026 · Campaign 26V134000 · Model years: 2026

Mercedes-Benz is recalling certain GLA 250 vehicles. A cable that works the door latch may have been installed incorrectly. When that happens, the door cannot be opened from inside the vehicle. Dealers will inspect the cable assembly and repair it if needed, at no charge to the owner. Owner letters were mailed May 1, 2026, and the vehicle identification numbers covered by this recall can be searched on NHTSA.gov beginning March 13, 2026.
