Mercedes-Benz Instrument Panel Display May Go Blank high

Reported May 1, 2026 · Campaign 26V281000 · Model years: 2025

The system that controls the infotainment screen may reset on its own while the vehicle is being driven. When this happens, the instrument panel display can go blank, meaning the driver can no longer see it.

Mercedes EQE and GLC steering coupling bolt may loosen high

Reported Aug 20, 2025 · Campaign 25V533000 · Model years: 2025

Mercedes-Benz is recalling certain EQE and GLC models because the bolt that holds the steering coupling together may not have been properly tightened. This can allow the steering coupling to loosen and come apart from the steering rack.
