Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 1500 recalls

40 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Mercedes Sprinter Parking Brake Bracket Welds May Fail high

Reported 2025-02-28 · Campaign 25V135000 · Model years: 2024

Daimler Vans is recalling certain 2024 Sprinter vans. The welds on the parking brake cable bracket may fail, which can cause the parking brake to disengage on its own.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Instrument Cluster Too Bright high

Reported 2024-12-13 · Campaign 24V930000 · Model years: 2019

On certain Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans, the brightness of the instrument cluster display is not adjustable the way it should be. In some lighting conditions, this can cause the display to appear too bright to the driver. A display that is too bright can make it difficult for the driver to see important safety information shown on the instrument cluster while driving.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Rear Axle Bolts May Fail high

Reported 2024-07-09 · Campaign 24V517000 · Model years: 2024

Daimler Vans USA is recalling certain Sprinter vehicles because the bolts that attach the rear axle springs may not be as strong as they should be, due to a manufacturing problem with how they were heat-treated. Because of this, the rear axle could shift out of its proper position. An axle that has moved out of position can affect how the vehicle steers and handles on the road.

Mercedes Sprinter Partition Wall May Detach high

Reported 2023-12-21 · Campaign 23V876000 · Model years: 2019

Daimler Vans is recalling certain 2019-2023 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and 2019-2021 Freightliner Sprinter vans because the partition wall inside the vehicle may be missing bolts. Without enough bolts holding it firmly in place, the partition wall could come loose and detach if the vehicle is in a crash. Dealers will inspect the partition wall and secure it with the necessary bolts, at no cost to owners.

Mercedes Sprinter Frame May Be Missing Fasteners high

Reported 2023-12-21 · Campaign 23V875000 · Model years: 2019

Daimler Vans is recalling certain 2019-2021 Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans because the longitudinal frame members that run the length of the vehicle may be missing reinforcement fasteners. Without these fasteners in place, the frame is less stable and the mounting for the third-row seats is less secure. Dealers will inspect the frame members and install any missing fasteners as needed, at no cost to owners.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Instrument Cluster May Go Blank medium

Reported 2023-03-24 · Campaign 23V204000 · Model years: 2019

On these vehicles, the instrument cluster may fail to show any information when the vehicle is started. This means the panel can go completely blank, so the driver cannot see important details like the vehicle's speed, the fuel gauge, warning messages, or warning lights. To fix this, dealers will update the software in the instrument cluster on affected vehicles, at no charge to the owner.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Parking Pawl May Fail To Engage high

Reported 2022-10-21 · Campaign 22V796000 · Model years: 2019

Daimler Vans is recalling certain 2019-2022 Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans with a manual parking brake and 7-speed automatic transmission. A part called the guide bushing inside the parking pawl can fatigue over time. If it does, the parking pawl may not engage as it should, which means the vehicle may not stay in place while parked. Dealers will update several control unit software programs to fix this at no charge.

Mercedes Sprinter Vans: Emergency Call System May Fail high

Reported 2022-05-20 · Campaign 22V360000 · Model years: 2019

Daimler Vans USA is recalling certain 2019-2021 Freightliner Sprinter, Mercedes-Benz Metris, and Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vehicles. An error in the communication module's SIM card software can cause the mobile network connection to fail. When this happens, it disables the emergency call, or eCall, system that is meant to connect occupants to emergency services.

Sprinter Vans Rearview Camera May Not Display medium

Reported 2022-04-27 · Campaign 22V281000 · Model years: 2019

Some Freightliner Sprinter and Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans have a software problem that can keep the rearview camera from showing its image on the display. When this happens, the driver has a reduced view of what's behind the vehicle. This reduced rear visibility increases the risk of a crash while driving.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter may move after being shifted into Park high

Reported 2021-12-15 · Campaign 21V972000 · Model years: 2019

Daimler Vans USA is recalling certain Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Freightliner Sprinter vans. A software error in the system that locks the van in Park can let the van move after the driver has put the gear selector in Park without also setting the parking brake. Dealers will install updated software in the control unit for the electronic stability program, at no cost to owners. Letters telling owners about this recall have already been mailed.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter seat wiring may disable side airbag high

Reported 2021-06-02 · Campaign 21V410000 · Model years: 2019

Some Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans built with swivel front seats may have the seat wiring routed the wrong way. The wiring can get jammed in the swivel seat frame and be damaged. If that wiring is damaged, the side airbag built into the seat may go off when it should not, or fail to go off as designed. Dealers will move the mounting clip to the correct position at no cost.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Multimedia Screen and Camera Failure high

Reported 2021-06-02 · Campaign 21V411000 · Model years: 2019

Daimler Vans is recalling certain Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vehicles because the multimedia system's software can cause the display screen to go black or to restart itself after the vehicle is started. When this happens, the rearview camera image may not show up properly on the screen. Because of this, the vehicle no longer meets the federal safety standard for rear visibility.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Steering Hoses May Leak Fluid high

Reported 2021-04-06 · Campaign 21V241000 · Model years: 2019

Some Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans have a hydraulic power steering return line hose that may come loose and detach. If this happens, hydraulic fluid can leak out of the steering system. Losing this fluid can cause a sudden loss of power steering assist.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Instrument Display Too Bright low

Reported 2021-04-05 · Campaign 21V235000 · Model years: 2019

On some Sprinter vans, the multifunctional display in the instrument cluster cannot be adjusted and may appear too bright in certain lighting conditions. Because the brightness can't be turned down, the display can make it harder for the driver to see clearly while driving.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter sliding door may open too far in crash medium

Reported 2021-03-10 · Campaign 21V153000 · Model years: 2019

Daimler Vans USA is recalling certain Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans that were ordered with the optional electric sliding door. The extra lock on that sliding door may be the wrong type for an electric door. Because of this, these vans do not meet a federal safety rule covering how well doors keep people inside during a crash. Dealers will change the sliding door so it works by hand instead of electrically, at no cost to owners.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter brake pedal part may loosen, lights stay on medium

Reported 2021-03-10 · Campaign 21V152000 · Model years: 2019

Daimler Vans USA is recalling certain Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Freightliner Sprinter vans. The washer and bolt that hold the brake pedal to its mounting block can work loose and come off. If that happens, the brake lights can stay lit even when the driver is not pressing the brake pedal. Dealers will add an extra retaining ring at the pedal's mounting bracket to hold everything in place, at no cost to owners.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter rearview camera image delayed or blocked medium

Reported 2021-02-17 · Campaign 21V077000 · Model years: 2019

Daimler Vans USA is recalling certain Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Freightliner Sprinter vans that have the "Connect 5" multimedia system and a rearview camera. The camera image may not appear on the screen within two seconds after the driver starts backing up. The image can also be covered or hidden by the emergency call pop-up window if that system turns on while the van is in reverse. These vans therefore do not meet the federal rear visibility standard.

Mercedes-Benz vans may send wrong location in emergency call medium

Reported 2021-02-05 · Campaign 21V055000 · Model years: 2019

Daimler Vans USA is recalling certain Mercedes-Benz Metris and Sprinter vans. Because of the way the software in the communication module was designed, the automatic emergency call system may not send the correct location of the vehicle if the vehicle is in a crash. Dealers will update the software in the communication module for the automatic emergency call system, and the recall began in May 2021.

Mercedes Sprinter Rear Brake Calipers May Leak Fluid high

Reported 2020-12-21 · Campaign 20V802000 · Model years: 2019

Daimler Vans is recalling certain 2019-2020 Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans equipped with electric parking brakes. The rear brake calipers may leak brake fluid into the parking brake housing, which can let air get into the hydraulic brake system. This can make the brake pedal travel farther than normal before the brakes engage. Dealers will replace the rear brake calipers at no cost to owners.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter owner's manual may be inaccurate or incomplete low

Reported 2020-12-08 · Campaign 20V756000 · Model years: 2019

These Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans may have been delivered with an owner's manual, both the printed book and the digital version in the vehicle, that is inaccurate, incomplete, or both. The manual is what tells you how the van's features work and gives other safety information, so some of what it says about operating the vehicle may be wrong or missing. A dealer will hand over an updated printed manual and update the software for the digital one.

Mercedes-Benz, Freightliner Sprinter Power Steering Hoses May Leak high

Reported 2020-10-28 · Campaign 20V663000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The hydraulic power steering return line hoses may detach at their connection points under certain operating conditions, causing hydraulic fluid to leak. This affects certain Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter van models. Dealers will check the power steering return line connections and correct them as needed, free of charge.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Fuel Line Clamps May Loosen critical

Reported 2020-09-29 · Campaign 20V595000 · Model years: 2019

Certain 2019 and 2020 Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans have clamps that hold the fuel lines in place. Over time, these clamps may loosen. When a clamp loosens, fuel can leak out of the line instead of staying contained. A fuel leak means fuel is escaping where it should not be, which raises the chance of a fire starting. This recall covers both the Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner versions of the Sprinter.

Sprinter Owner's Manual Wrong About Auto-Parking high

Reported 2020-03-26 · Campaign 20V180000 · Model years: 2019

On these Sprinter vans, the Operator's Manual may give incorrect information about how the automatic parking function, called Auto-P, actually works. Dealers will provide owners with the correct description of how Auto-P operates for the manual.

Sprinter Van Brake Hose Can Wear Through high

Reported 2020-03-25 · Campaign 20V181000 · Model years: 2019

On these Sprinter vans, part of the fender liner near the front wheel can rub against a brake hose. Over time, that rubbing can wear through the hose and let brake fluid leak out. Brake fluid is what lets the brakes work properly, so losing it can make the vehicle take longer to stop than it normally would. Dealers will inspect the brake hoses, replace them if needed, and modify the fender liner so the rubbing does not happen again.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Winter Tires May Be Installed Wrong high

Reported 2020-03-18 · Campaign 20V157000 · Model years: 2019

Daimler Vans USA is recalling certain 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Freightliner Sprinter vans that came equipped with winter tires. The tires may not have been installed the correct way, which could reduce how well they grip the road in wet or snowy conditions. Because the tires may be mounted incorrectly, they might not perform the way they are supposed to when the weather turns wet or snowy.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Missing Spare Tire Speed Label low

Reported 2020-03-18 · Campaign 20V156000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Some Sprinter vans left the factory without a label on the spare tire that lists its maximum speed. Without this label, a driver may not know how fast the spare tire is rated to go and could end up driving faster than it can safely handle. Driving the spare tire too fast increases the chance that the tire could fail while the vehicle is being driven. Dealers will attach the correct speed label to the spare wheel.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans have wrong spare tire speed label medium

Reported 2020-03-18 · Campaign 20V155000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Mercedes-Benz is recalling certain Sprinter vans. The label for the spare tire lists the top speed only in kilometers per hour, showing 55, instead of listing it as 34 miles per hour. A driver reading that label could take the number as miles per hour and drive faster on the spare tire than the tire is built to handle. Dealers will put on a corrected label that shows the top speed in miles per hour, free of charge.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Steering Bearing Screws May Loosen high

Reported 2020-02-25 · Campaign 20V109000 · Model years: 2019

The screws that hold the steering shaft's bearing in place may not have been tightened properly. This could let the screws loosen and detach. If the screws detach, the driver may find it more difficult to steer the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will replace the steering bearing screws and tighten them correctly, and this repair will be done at no cost to the owner.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Missing Steering Column Cover high

Reported 2020-02-25 · Campaign 20V110000 · Model years: 2019

The cover for the steering column casing may not have been installed on these vans, leaving the steering column area exposed. If small parts, such as nuts or screws, get into this exposed area, they could affect how the steering works. This raises the risk of a crash. Dealers will replace the steering column with one that includes the cover, free of charge.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Exhaust Flange Fire Risk critical

Reported 2020-02-12 · Campaign 20V079000 · Model years: 2019

On some Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans, the flange connection between the exhaust gas turbocharger and the exit to the diesel particulate filter may not be attached properly. This can allow an exhaust gas leak in the engine compartment.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter EGR Pipe Fire Risk critical

Reported 2020-02-12 · Campaign 20V078000 · Model years: 2019

On some Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans, the screws connecting the exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) pipe may not have been tightened correctly. If these connecting screws are not properly tight, hot exhaust gases may leak out of the EGR pipe.

Sprinter cargo tie-down rails may detach medium

Reported 2019-12-19 · Campaign 19V908000 · Model years: 2019

Certain Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans have lashing rails on the side walls that are used to secure cargo. These rails may not have been installed correctly, which could cause them to come loose and detach from the wall. If the rails detach while the van is loaded, cargo being carried inside could become unsecured while the vehicle is being driven.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Vans May Leak Diesel Fuel high

Reported 2019-11-07 · Campaign 19V798000 · Model years: 2019

On some Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans with diesel fuel systems, a locking fitting that connects the fuel pump to the fuel tank may not have been closed and locked properly when the vehicle was built. Because of this, fuel could leak out between the pump and the tank. In addition, when filling up at a gas station, the pump may not shut off automatically once the tank is full, which could cause diesel fuel to spill over the top.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Front Seat Weld Can Break high

Reported 2019-11-06 · Campaign 19V800000 · Model years: 2019

The front seats have a weld on the part that lets you adjust the seat height. On some vans, that weld may not have been made strong enough. If the vehicle is in a crash, the weak weld could break and let the seat cushion shift forward. When a seat cushion shifts out of place during a crash, it raises the chance that the person sitting in it will be injured.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Door Wedge Installed Incorrectly medium

Reported 2019-10-08 · Campaign 19V716000 · Model years: 2019

Some Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans have a part called the abutting wedge, attached to the door frame to make it easier to open the door after a severe frontal crash, that may have been installed incorrectly.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Vans May Have Inactive Airbags high

Reported 2019-10-02 · Campaign 19V695000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On some Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans, the airbag system may never have been turned on after the vehicle was built. Because the system was not activated, the airbags may not work as intended. This means the vehicle does not meet the federal safety standard for occupant crash protection.

Mercedes Sprinter and Metris Vans Skipped Final Inspection high

Reported 2019-10-02 · Campaign 19V693000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Some Sprinter and Metris vans left the reassembly plant without a final quality inspection of safety-related parts and bolt connections. Because that inspection was skipped, some parts or bolts may not have been properly checked before the vehicle was delivered, creating unknown safety risks.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Hood May Open While Driving medium

Reported 2019-09-18 · Campaign 19V665000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On some 2018 and 2019 Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans, the screw holding the upper hood catch in place may not have been tightened correctly. Because of this, factors like driving speed, wind, and road conditions could cause the hood to pop open while the van is being driven. Dealers will check the screw connection and tighten it as needed, free of charge.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Taillights May Dim or Turn Off medium

Reported 2019-08-12 · Campaign 19V591000 · Model years: 2019

On certain Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans, quickly turning the manual light switch from one position to another can cause the taillights to become much dimmer or shut off completely. Reduced taillight visibility makes it harder for other drivers to see the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Rear Side Windows Are Wrong Glass high

Reported 2019-07-02 · Campaign 19V507000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Some Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans were built with the wrong type of glass in their rear side windows. The windows are made of a single layer of safety glass instead of the laminated safety glass they are supposed to have. In a side-impact crash or a rollover, this type of glass may not hold together and protect rear-seat occupants from being ejected from the vehicle as well as laminated glass would.
