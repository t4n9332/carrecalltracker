Mercedes-Benz GLE, GLS Rearview Mirror May Detach medium

Reported May 30, 2025 · Campaign 25V325000 · Model years: 2025

Certain Mercedes-Benz GLE and GLS SUVs have a multi-purpose camera and a rearview mirror that are mounted on the windshield. These parts may not be attached firmly enough, and either one could come loose over time. If the mirror detaches, the driver is left with less visibility. If the camera detaches, features such as Active Lane Keeping Assist and Active Brake Assist may stop working.
