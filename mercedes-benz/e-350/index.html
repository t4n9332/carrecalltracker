Mercedes-Benz dashboard display can go blank while driving high

Reported 2026-05-01 · Campaign 26V281000 · Model years: 2025

In these Mercedes-Benz cars and SUVs, the infotainment computer can restart by itself while you are driving. When it does, the digital dashboard in front of the driver goes completely dark. You lose your speedometer and any warning lights, so you cannot tell how fast you are going or whether something else on the car needs attention. The dealer loads updated infotainment software at no cost to fix it.

Mercedes-Benz E-Class rear axle bracket may break loose critical

Reported 2024-12-18 · Campaign 24V941000 · Model years: 2025

A bracket on the rear suspension may have been welded on badly at the factory, and it can break away from the rear axle carrier. If that happens, the back of the car can suddenly stop handling normally and you could lose control. Mercedes says do not drive the car until it is fixed. The dealer will replace the whole rear axle carrier at no charge. Letters went out to owners in January 2025.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired.
