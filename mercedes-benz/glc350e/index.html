Mercedes-Benz Front Roof Panel May Detach medium

Reported Dec 15, 2023 · Campaign 23V854000 · Model years: 2019

On certain 2014 through 2020 Mercedes-Benz C-Class, CLA, GLA, S-Class, GLC, and GLE vehicles, the front roof panel, located between the windshield and the panoramic sunroof, may not have been properly bonded to the vehicle during an earlier repair. Because it was not attached correctly, this front panel can come loose and detach from the vehicle. Mercedes-Benz USA says owners should refer to its recall report for details on which specific vehicles are affected.

Mercedes-Benz GLC350E Sunroof Panel May Detach high

Reported Mar 19, 2021 · Campaign 21V197000 · Model years: 2019

On some vehicles with a panoramic sunroof, the front roof panel between the windshield and the sunroof may not have been bonded correctly during an earlier repair. The adhesive bond can weaken over time, and the panel could come loose and detach from the vehicle while driving, becoming a hazard on the road.

Mercedes GLC Airbag Warning Label in Wrong Language low

Reported Dec 20, 2019 · Campaign 19V914000 · Model years: 2019

On some Mercedes-Benz GLC models, the airbag warning label on the passenger sun visor may be incorrect, printed in a foreign language instead of the proper wording for these vehicles.

Mercedes-Benz seat belts may be read as unbuckled high

Reported Nov 1, 2019 · Campaign 19V788000 · Model years: 2019

On these Mercedes-Benz vehicles, a front seat belt can be fastened correctly but still be read by the car as unbuckled. When that happens, the car shuts off an electronic safety feature called PRE-SAFE and also shuts off the part of the belt that pulls it snug against the body in a crash. Dealers will inspect the front seat belt buckles and replace them if needed. The recall covers a range of Mercedes-Benz cars, wagons and SUVs from 2018 through 2019.

Mercedes-Benz Passenger Air Bag Status May Display Incorrectly high

Reported Nov 27, 2018 · Campaign 18V838000 · Model years: 2019

In certain Mercedes-Benz vehicles with the Audio 20 head unit and automatic child seat recognition, the passenger air bag status may not display correctly on the dashboard. If the display incorrectly shows the passenger air bag as 'on' when it is actually off, a front seat passenger may not realize the air bag will not inflate in a crash. Not knowing this increases their risk of injury if a crash happens.

Mercedes-Benz Glc350e recall questions

How many recalls does the Mercedes-Benz Glc350e have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2019 Mercedes-Benz Glc350e. 3 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Mercedes-Benz Front Roof Panel May Detach" (NHTSA campaign 23V854000), reported Dec 15, 2023.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Mercedes-Benz Glc350e?

No. None of the 5 Mercedes-Benz Glc350e recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Mercedes-Benz Glc350e has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Mercedes-Benz dealer, including for second owners.
