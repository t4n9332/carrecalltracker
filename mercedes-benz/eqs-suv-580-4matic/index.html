Mercedes-Benz EVs play wrong backup warning sound for pedestrians medium

Reported 2025-06-05 · Campaign 25V366000 · Model years: 2025

Electric and hybrid vehicles run so quietly that they play an artificial sound outside the car to warn people nearby. A software problem makes these vehicles play an incorrect warning sound while backing up. Someone walking behind the vehicle may not realize it is reversing toward them. The vehicles do not meet the federal rule for minimum sound. Dealers will update the software at no charge — a short visit with no parts needed.

Mercedes-Benz EQ electric models may suddenly lose driving power critical

Reported 2024-05-28 · Campaign 24V372000 · Model years: 2025

These electric Mercedes models, including the EQS SUV 580 4MATIC, run on a large high-voltage battery controlled by software. That software can shut the battery down while you are driving. If it does, the car suddenly loses power to the wheels, which can leave you slowing down in traffic with no warning and lead to a crash. Dealers will install updated battery software; nothing needs to be taken apart or replaced.
