Porsche 911 rear seat belt may not hold child seat securely medium

Reported 2024-02-20 · Campaign 24V123000 · Model years: 2023

Porsche is recalling certain 911 vehicles. The rear seat belts have a locking feature that is meant to hold a child seat firmly in place. On these vehicles that lock can switch off too early, so the child seat may not end up properly secured. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for occupant crash protection. Dealers will look at the rear three-point belts and replace them where needed.
