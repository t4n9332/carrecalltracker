Porsche Cayenne, Panamera and Taycan Rearview Camera Failure medium

Reported Dec 19, 2025 · Campaign 25V896000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

In these vehicles, the rearview camera image may not show up on the display screen when the vehicle is shifted into reverse. Dealers will fix this by updating the driver assistance software, free of charge.

Porsche Cayenne and Panamera fuel pump bolts may leak fuel critical

Reported Sep 23, 2025 · Campaign 25V634000 · Model years: 2024, 2026

The fasteners holding the high pressure fuel pump in place on these vehicles may not be tightened properly, which can allow fuel to leak. This affects certain Porsche Panamera, Panamera E-Hybrid, and Cayenne vehicles. Porsche is recalling these vehicles so dealers can inspect and tighten the connections, and replace the fuel pump and fuel lines if needed.

Porsche Cayenne Control Arm Crack Risk critical

Reported Jul 16, 2024 · Campaign 24V533000 · Model years: 2024

The upper control arm on the driver's side front axle may have been installed with cracks. A cracked control arm can break, which can cause a sudden loss of steering and wheel control.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired.

Porsche Cayenne Rear Hazard and Parking Lights May Fail high

Reported Jul 27, 2023 · Campaign 23V527000 · Model years: 2024

After a Cayenne is turned off, the rear hazard warning lights and parking lights may fail to come on if they are turned on within 10 seconds after the vehicle's gateway control unit shuts off. Because of this, the vehicle does not meet the federal lighting safety standard for lamps and reflective devices. In this situation, other drivers may not be able to see the hazard or parking lights when they are needed.

Porsche Cayenne Rear Axle Alignment Missed low

Reported Jan 28, 2022 · Campaign 22V040000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

After an earlier recall repair was completed on these vehicles, the rear axle alignment may not have been inspected as it should have been. Skipping that inspection step means the rear axle could be misaligned. A misaligned rear axle can cause the tires to wear unevenly or wear out faster than normal.

Porsche Cayenne Steering Column May Detach high

Reported Jun 30, 2021 · Campaign 21V493000 · Model years: 2021

Porsche says the screw connecting the steering column to the steering box on some Cayenne SUVs may fail. If it does, the steering column can become detached from the steering box, affecting the driver's ability to steer the vehicle.

Porsche Cayenne Passenger Airbag May Not Deploy high

Reported May 5, 2021 · Campaign 21V318000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On certain Porsche Cayenne, Cayenne Coupe, Cayenne S Coupe, Cayenne GTS, Cayenne GTS Coupe, and Cayenne E-Hybrid Coupe vehicles, a heating element that should have been installed in the front passenger seat back was left out. This part affects the system that senses whether a passenger is sitting in the seat, which can throw off its readings. Because of this, the front passenger airbag may fail to deploy.

Porsche Cayenne Rear Axle Lock Nut May Break high

Reported Apr 21, 2021 · Campaign 21V271000 · Model years: 2021

On certain 2021 Porsche Cayenne models, the lock nut on the rear axle's trailing arm may break due to corrosion.

Porsche Cayenne Transmission Pipe May Leak Fluid medium

Reported May 13, 2020 · Campaign 20V271000 · Model years: 2020

On some 2020 Porsche Cayenne vehicles, a weld on a pipe that carries automatic transmission fluid was not made strong enough. This weak weld can allow transmission fluid to leak out of the vehicle.

Porsche Cayenne, Panamera Brake Wear Warning Light Fails high

Reported Oct 16, 2019 · Campaign 19V735000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

In certain Porsche Cayenne and Panamera vehicles, the instrument cluster may fail to show a warning when the brake pads are worn out. Without that alert, the driver may not know the brake pads need replacing, and the vehicle may not brake the way the driver expects.

Porsche Cayenne Rearview Camera Display May Be Delayed medium

Reported Feb 21, 2019 · Campaign 19V111000 · Model years: 2019

On some Porsche Cayenne and Cayenne S vehicles, a software failure may cause the rearview camera image to respond with a delay instead of showing right away.

Porsche Panamera and Cayenne may not warn of worn brake pads high

Reported Feb 20, 2019 · Campaign 19V115000 · Model years: 2019

Porsche is recalling certain Panamera and Cayenne vehicles. The instrument cluster, the display behind the steering wheel, may fail to show a visual warning when the brake pads have worn out. Without that warning, the driver is not told that the pads need attention. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for light vehicle brake systems. Dealers will correct it by updating the instrument cluster software.

Porsche Cayenne Shock Absorbers May Loosen high

Reported Feb 20, 2019 · Campaign 19V112000 · Model years: 2019

One Cayenne Turbo vehicle has shock absorber forks that may allow the shock absorbers to become loose. Dealers will replace the shock absorber forks, free of charge.

Porsche Cayenne and Macan ski bag strap may tear medium

Reported Nov 28, 2018 · Campaign 18V844000 · Model years: 2018

Porsche is recalling certain Cayenne and Macan models that were built with the optional ski bag. The strap that fastens the ski bag may have been sewn with the wrong thread. The seams on the strap can then tear, leaving the ski bag unsecured in a crash. Dealers will replace the ski bags at no charge. Porsche began this recall on January 10, 2019, and is notifying owners.

Porsche Cayenne rear seat belt buckle may break medium

Reported Oct 24, 2018 · Campaign 18V751000 · Model years: 2019

The rear seat belt buckle on the passenger side may break if it comes under heavy load, such as in a crash. Because of this, the seat belt does not meet federal safety standards for seat belt assemblies.
