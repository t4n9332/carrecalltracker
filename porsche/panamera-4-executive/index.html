Porsche Panamera Coolant Pump May Spark Electrical Fire critical

Reported 2023-01-27 · Campaign 23V033000 · Model years: 2018

Certain Porsche Panamera models may have a problem with the external coolant pump used for the climate control system. Moisture can get inside this pump, and when it does, it can cause an electrical short circuit. This kind of short circuit happens when the pump is exposed to humidity it isn't designed to handle, affecting the electrical connection inside it.

Porsche Panamera Control Unit Solder Defect high

Reported 2019-03-06 · Campaign 19V180000 · Model years: 2018

A control unit in these vehicles was soldered incorrectly. This soldering problem can cause various systems in the vehicle to fail intermittently, meaning they may stop working and then start again, which can affect how well the vehicle drives.

Porsche Panamera and Cayenne may not warn of worn brake pads high

Reported 2019-02-20 · Campaign 19V115000 · Model years: 2018

Porsche is recalling certain Panamera and Cayenne vehicles. The instrument cluster, the display behind the steering wheel, may fail to show a visual warning when the brake pads have worn out. Without that warning, the driver is not told that the pads need attention. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for light vehicle brake systems. Dealers will correct it by updating the instrument cluster software.
