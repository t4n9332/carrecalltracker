Porsche Macan recalls

7 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Porsche Rear Seat Belt Anchors May Not Be Tight high

Reported 2023-06-26 · Campaign 23V443000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

The screw that attaches the lower seat belt anchor to the seat frame on the outboard rear seats may not have been tightened properly. This affects certain Porsche Macan, Macan S, Panamera 4, and Panamera 4 E-Hybrid vehicles, among other Porsche models. Dealers will replace and tighten the lower rear outboard seat belt anchor screws, free of charge.

Porsche Macan Missing Headlight Adjustment Screw Caps medium

Reported 2022-08-30 · Campaign 22V656000 · Model years: 2020

Some Porsche Macan and Macan S vehicles are missing the caps that cover the screws used to adjust the low-beam headlights. Without these caps in place, the headlights on these vehicles could end up improperly adjusted.

Porsche Macan Suspension Screws May Loosen critical

Reported 2021-03-31 · Campaign 21V224000 · Model years: 2021

On some Macan models, the screws holding the shock absorbers on the front and rear axle may not have been tightened properly. A failed screw connection can damage the braking system and make the vehicle unstable to drive.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired.

Porsche Macan front passenger seat sensor may fail high

Reported 2021-03-03 · Campaign 21V131000 · Model years: 2018

Porsche is recalling certain Macan vehicles because of a problem with the front passenger seat. A sensor mat inside that seat is supposed to tell the car when someone is sitting in the front passenger position. That mat can break down and fail to detect the passenger. Dealers will replace the sensor mat and the seat cushion free of charge, and Porsche has already mailed letters to owners.

Porsche Macan Fuel Pump Cover May Crack, Leak Fuel critical

Reported 2020-02-12 · Campaign 20V077000 · Model years: 2018

On some Porsche Macan vehicles, a cover on the fuel pump can rub against a nozzle on the fuel pump's filter flange. This contact may wear cracks into the flange over time, letting fuel leak out. A fuel leak on its own is one thing, but if it happens near something that could spark or ignite it, the leaking fuel raises the chance of a fire starting in the vehicle.

Porsche Cayenne and Macan ski bag strap may tear medium

Reported 2018-11-28 · Campaign 18V844000 · Model years: 2018

Porsche is recalling certain Cayenne and Macan models that were built with the optional ski bag. The strap that fastens the ski bag may have been sewn with the wrong thread. The seams on the strap can then tear, leaving the ski bag unsecured in a crash. Dealers will replace the ski bags at no charge. Porsche began this recall on January 10, 2019, and is notifying owners.

Porsche Macan Control Unit May Fail high

Reported 2018-05-16 · Campaign 18V318000 · Model years: 2018

Porsche is recalling certain 2018 Macan S and Macan vehicles. These vehicles have a control unit that may fail. If it fails, it can prevent important safety features from working properly, such as alerts shown on the driver's display. It may also affect how well the vehicle can be driven, and it could affect certain systems after a crash has occurred.
