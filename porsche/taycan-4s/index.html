Porsche Taycan Battery Short Circuit Fire Risk critical

Reported Mar 20, 2024 · Campaign 24V217000 · Model years: 2020

The high-voltage battery in these vehicles may develop a short circuit inside its battery modules. A short circuit like this increases the risk of the battery catching fire.

Porsche Taycan high-voltage battery may short circuit critical

Reported Mar 20, 2024 · Campaign 24V215000 · Model years: 2020

Porsche is recalling certain Taycan models because the high-voltage battery may develop a short circuit within its battery modules.

Porsche portable charging cable and outlet can overheat critical

Reported Dec 13, 2023 · Campaign 23V841000 · Model years: 2020

Porsche is recalling a number of electric and plug-in hybrid models, including the Taycan, Cayenne E-Hybrid and Panamera 4S E-Hybrid. When the compact charging system is used with its industrial 220-volt or 240-volt plug at the 100 percent charge setting, some home outlets cannot handle the electrical current the system needs. The outlet or the charging cable can then overheat. Dealers will supply a new 220-volt/240-volt portable charging cable with a built-in temperature sensor, free of charge.

Porsche Taycan passenger seat belt warning does not work medium

Reported Mar 17, 2023 · Campaign 23V176000 · Model years: 2020

Porsche is recalling certain Taycan vehicles. When someone is sitting in the passenger seat without the seat belt fastened, the seat belt warning light and the reminder chime do not come on the way they are supposed to. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal government's safety standard covering occupant crash protection. Dealers will update the software in the instrument cluster, the display area behind the steering wheel, free of charge.

Porsche Taycan 4S recall questions

How many recalls does the Porsche Taycan 4S have?

NHTSA lists 4 safety recalls covering the 2020 Porsche Taycan 4S. 3 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Porsche Taycan Battery Short Circuit Fire Risk" (NHTSA campaign 24V217000), reported Mar 20, 2024.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Porsche Taycan 4S?

No. None of the 4 Porsche Taycan 4S recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Porsche Taycan 4S has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Porsche dealer, including for second owners.
