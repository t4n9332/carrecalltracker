Porsche Panamera Control Unit Solder Defect high

Reported Mar 6, 2019 · Campaign 19V180000 · Model years: 2018

A control unit in these vehicles was soldered incorrectly. This soldering problem can cause various systems in the vehicle to fail intermittently, meaning they may stop working and then start again, which can affect how well the vehicle drives.

Porsche Panamera Rear Suspension Link Can Detach high

Reported Jun 13, 2018 · Campaign 18V388000 · Model years: 2018

Certain Porsche Panamera models have connecting links that attach the rear anti-roll bar to the suspension. These links can come loose and detach. When that happens, nearby suspension parts can be damaged, and the way the car handles on the road can change unexpectedly. This issue affects several Panamera variants, including the 4S, Turbo, and Turbo S E-Hybrid versions covered by this recall.
