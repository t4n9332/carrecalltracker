Porsche Cayenne, Panamera and Taycan Rearview Camera Failure medium

Reported Dec 19, 2025 · Campaign 25V896000 · Model years: 2019, 2021

In these vehicles, the rearview camera image may not show up on the display screen when the vehicle is shifted into reverse. Dealers will fix this by updating the driver assistance software, free of charge.

Porsche portable charging cable and outlet can overheat critical

Reported Dec 13, 2023 · Campaign 23V841000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Porsche is recalling a number of electric and plug-in hybrid models, including the Taycan, Cayenne E-Hybrid and Panamera 4S E-Hybrid. When the compact charging system is used with its industrial 220-volt or 240-volt plug at the 100 percent charge setting, some home outlets cannot handle the electrical current the system needs. The outlet or the charging cable can then overheat. Dealers will supply a new 220-volt/240-volt portable charging cable with a built-in temperature sensor, free of charge.

Porsche Cayenne Passenger Airbag May Not Deploy high

Reported May 5, 2021 · Campaign 21V318000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On certain Porsche Cayenne, Cayenne Coupe, Cayenne S Coupe, Cayenne GTS, Cayenne GTS Coupe, and Cayenne E-Hybrid Coupe vehicles, a heating element that should have been installed in the front passenger seat back was left out. This part affects the system that senses whether a passenger is sitting in the seat, which can throw off its readings. Because of this, the front passenger airbag may fail to deploy.

Porsche Cayenne E-Hybrid recall questions

How many recalls does the Porsche Cayenne E-Hybrid have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2019–2021 Porsche Cayenne E-Hybrid. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Porsche Cayenne, Panamera and Taycan Rearview Camera Failure" (NHTSA campaign 25V896000), reported Dec 19, 2025.

Which Porsche Cayenne E-Hybrid model year has the most recalls?

The 2021 Porsche Cayenne E-Hybrid has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2019 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Porsche Cayenne E-Hybrid?

No. None of the 3 Porsche Cayenne E-Hybrid recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Porsche Cayenne E-Hybrid has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Porsche dealer, including for second owners.
