Porsche Taycan recalls

25 recalls covering model years 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Porsche Cayenne, Panamera, Taycan: backup camera may go blank medium

Reported 2025-12-19 · Campaign 25V896000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

When you shift into reverse, the backup camera picture may not appear on the screen. That leaves you backing up using only your mirrors and your own look over your shoulder, which makes it easier to miss a child, a pet or an object behind you. Federal rules require that image to show. Porsche dealers will load updated driver assistance software at no charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on February 13, 2026.

Porsche Taycan passenger airbag may switch itself off high

Reported 2025-05-05 · Campaign 25V221000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

The front passenger seat has a system that senses who is sitting there and decides whether the passenger airbag should be switched on. On these cars that system can make an error and turn the front passenger airbag off. If that happens, the airbag will not inflate in a crash, so a person riding in the front passenger seat has less protection. Dealers will replace the front passenger seat cushion at no cost.

Porsche Taycan battery short circuit can cause fire critical

Reported 2024-10-01 · Campaign 24V732000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

The large battery that powers your Taycan can short-circuit inside one of its sections, and a short in a battery this big can start a fire. Porsche is watching data that cars send in online and will contact owners whose battery looks suspect, telling them to charge only to 80 percent until it is replaced. Dealers install new monitoring software as the permanent fix. If your car does not send data, the dealer will test it and replace the battery section if needed.

Porsche Taycan high-voltage battery may short circuit and catch fire critical

Reported 2024-10-01 · Campaign 24V731000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

A part inside the large battery that powers certain 2020-2023 Taycan models can short circuit. A short circuit can start a fire. Until the repair is done, Porsche says to charge the car no higher than 80 percent. A dealer will replace the affected battery modules at no cost. This recall covers more cars than three earlier ones, so even a Taycan already fixed before needs this new repair.

Porsche Taycan software makes parking lights fail with turn signals low

Reported 2024-09-05 · Campaign 24V654000 · Model years: 2024

The wrong software was loaded into the module that controls the headlights. Because of it, the parking lights may not work the way they should when you switch on a turn signal, so other drivers may have a harder time seeing your car. That does not meet the federal rule for lights on vehicles. A dealer can fix it by loading the correct software into the headlight control unit; no parts are needed, and the update is free.

Porsche Taycan front brake hoses can crack and leak high

Reported 2024-06-19 · Campaign 24V455000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

The brake hoses at the front wheels on these Taycans can develop cracks and let brake fluid leak out. Brake fluid is what carries the force from your foot to the brakes at the wheels, so losing it weakens how well the car stops. The car may need a longer distance to come to a halt than you expect, especially in a sudden stop. Dealers replace the front brake hoses at no charge.

Porsche Taycan battery can short circuit and catch fire critical

Reported 2024-03-20 · Campaign 24V215000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

The large high-voltage battery that powers these electric Taycans can short circuit inside one of its modules. A short circuit can start a fire. Until the repair is finished, Porsche tells owners to charge the car no higher than 80 percent of its capacity. Dealers replace the affected battery modules at no cost to you. Owner notification letters were mailed in October 2024, so many owners have already been contacted about this repair.

Porsche Taycan battery short circuit creates fire risk critical

Reported 2024-03-20 · Campaign 24V217000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

The large battery that powers your electric Porsche is built from modules, and one of those modules can short out inside. A short circuit in a battery like this can start a fire. Until the car is repaired, Porsche is telling owners to charge only up to 80 percent of full. At the dealer, technicians will read the battery's data and replace any modules that turn out to be bad. The check and any replacement parts cost you nothing.

Porsche Taycan and Cayenne portable charging cable can overheat critical

Reported 2023-12-13 · Campaign 23V841000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Some of these electric and plug-in hybrid Porsches came with a portable 220/240-volt charging cable. If you plug it into a home outlet that cannot handle that much electricity and charge to 100%, the outlet or the cable can get very hot. That heat can start a fire. Porsche says to stop using that cable for now and charge with the 110-volt home cable or a public charging station instead.

Porsche Taycan battery may short circuit and catch fire critical

Reported 2023-12-13 · Campaign 23V840000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

Porsche is recalling certain 2021-2023 Taycan models, including the 4S, Turbo, Turbo S, GTS and Cross Turismo versions. The large battery pack that powers these electric cars can develop a short circuit inside one of its modules, an electrical fault where current takes the wrong path. That can create enough heat to start a fire. Your dealer will check the battery and replace any bad modules at no cost to you.

Porsche Taycan battery sealing flaw creates fire risk critical

Reported 2023-09-01 · Campaign 23V615000 · Model years: 2023

Some 2023 Porsche Taycans were built without enough sealant around the large high-voltage battery pack. Without a proper seal, liquid can work its way in and collect inside the battery. Liquid where it does not belong can let electricity jump across where it should not, and that can start a fire. There is no reliable way for you to spot this from outside the car. A dealer will test the battery for leakage and replace it if needed.

Porsche Taycan shows wrong worn brake pad warning medium

Reported 2023-05-16 · Campaign 23V347000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

When the brake pads on these Taycans wear down, the car does put a warning on the instrument cluster, but it uses the wrong symbol in the wrong color. A driver can easily misread it and keep driving on worn-out pads, and worn pads mean the car does not stop as well as it should. Porsche dealers will update the instrument cluster software so the brake pad warning shows up correctly and is easy to understand.

Porsche Taycan seat belt warning chime does not work low

Reported 2023-03-17 · Campaign 23V176000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

When someone sits in a seat without buckling up, the car is supposed to show a warning light and sound a chime. On these cars that reminder does not come on for an unbelted passenger the way it should, which does not meet the federal safety rule for occupant protection. That means a passenger may not be reminded to buckle, and an unbelted person is far more likely to be hurt in a crash. Dealers update the instrument cluster software for free.

Porsche Taycan front strut ring can come loose high

Reported 2022-12-13 · Campaign 22V921000 · Model years: 2021

A ring that holds the top of a front suspension strut in place can work loose. If it does, the strut can come out of position and lose the air that supports the car's weight on that corner. That side of the car can drop, and the car can become hard to steer and control. Your Porsche dealer will look at the strut and put in a new one if it is needed, at no cost to you.

Porsche Taycan center screen and backup camera can go black medium

Reported 2022-06-08 · Campaign 22V404000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

A software problem can leave the big center screen and the touch panel below it completely black right after you start the car. Because the backup camera picture shows on that screen, you may see nothing behind you when you shift into reverse. The screen can also show a flickering image from the surround-view camera. Porsche says this means the vehicles do not meet the federal rear-visibility rule. Dealers fix it with new software.

Porsche Taycan seat wiring damage can disable airbags high

Reported 2022-04-26 · Campaign 22V278000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Moving the driver or front passenger seat forward and back can damage the wiring bundle attached to the seat on these Porsche Taycans. That wiring is part of the airbag system. If it is damaged, airbags may be switched off without you knowing, so they might not inflate in a crash. A dealer inspects the wiring, wraps it with fabric tape so it stops rubbing, and repairs any wires that are already damaged.

Porsche Taycan wiring blocks child seat anchors medium

Reported 2022-02-22 · Campaign 22V100000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Certain 2020-2021 Porsche Taycan cars that were ordered with the optional rear center seat have a wiring bundle for the center seat belt buckle that sits in the way. It can block access to the lower child seat anchor points for the right rear seat, so a child seat cannot be attached properly. Because of this, the car does not meet the federal safety rule for child seat anchors.

Porsche Taycan suspension misalignment wears tires early medium

Reported 2022-02-02 · Campaign 22V052000 · Model years: 2021

One 2021 Taycan left the factory with its front and rear suspension out of alignment. When the wheels don't point exactly where they should, the tires scrub against the road and wear out faster than normal, often unevenly across the tread. Porsche dealers will check both axles, reset the alignment if it is off, and replace any tire that has already worn prematurely or unevenly. All of the inspection and parts work is free.

Porsche Taycan rear suspension control arm bolts may loosen high

Reported 2021-11-19 · Campaign 21V911000 · Model years: 2020

On some 2020 Taycans, the bolted connections holding the upper control arms at the rear axle may be loose. Control arms are the parts that hold each rear wheel in the right position. If a loose connection comes apart, the car can suddenly become unstable and hard to control. The notice does not say the bolts can simply be retightened; dealers will replace the screw connections at the rear axle at no charge to you.

Porsche Taycan hazard lights may flash when not needed low

Reported 2021-10-13 · Campaign 21V794000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

These cars can turn on the hazard flashers automatically when the vehicle senses a possible collision ahead. On these vehicles, the flashers may switch on in ordinary driving situations when there is no emergency. Because federal rules cover how warning lamps behave, these cars do not meet that standard. Drivers around you may not understand why your flashers are on and may react in an unexpected way. Dealers fix this with a free software update.

Porsche 911 Carrera and Taycan brake pedal may fail critical

Reported 2021-09-08 · Campaign 21V697000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

A rod inside the brake booster — the part that links your brake pedal to the rest of the braking system — may be loose and could come apart. If that happens, the brake pedal fails, so pressing it will not slow or stop the car. This affects certain 2020-2021 911 Carrera and Taycan models. Dealers will inspect the brake booster and repair or replace it, free of charge. Owner letters went out in late October 2021.

Porsche seat belts may not hold child seats securely medium

Reported 2021-08-04 · Campaign 21V608000 · Model years: 2021

Your seat belts have a locking feature that clamps the belt tight so a child seat stays firmly in place. On these cars, that lock can switch off too early, letting the belt loosen so the child seat is no longer held securely. Because of this, the vehicle does not meet the federal safety rule covering how people are protected in a crash. A dealer will inspect the seat belts and replace any that are faulty, free of charge.

Porsche Taycan software may shut down power while driving high

Reported 2021-06-29 · Campaign 21V486000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

The software that watches over the Taycan's electric drive system can mistakenly think something has gone wrong. When that happens, it shuts the drive system down and the car loses power while you are driving. Nothing is actually broken — the software is reading a fault that is not there — but you can still be left without power on the road, which can lead to a crash. A dealer will reprogram the power electronics and the control unit to correct it.

Porsche Taycan and Panamera front suspension arm can break critical

Reported 2021-05-17 · Campaign 21V362000 · Model years: 2021

Each front wheel is held in place by suspension arms. On these cars one of those arms, the lower trailing arm, was not made correctly at the factory and can break. If an arm breaks while you are driving, that wheel is no longer held properly and you can lose control of the car. Dealers replace the front lower trailing arms at no charge to you.

Porsche Taycan and 911 suspension bolts may loosen high

Reported 2021-03-10 · Campaign 21V157000 · Model years: 2021

Some nuts and bolts holding suspension parts together may not have been tightened properly when the car was built, so they can work loose over time. The suspension is what keeps the wheels attached and pointed the right way. If a loose part comes off, the car can suddenly become unstable and hard to control. Dealers will replace the affected nuts and bolts, check nearby parts for damage, and replace anything damaged, free.
