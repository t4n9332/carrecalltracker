Porsche Panamera Coolant Pump May Spark Electrical Fire critical

Reported 2023-01-27 · Campaign 23V033000 · Model years: 2021

Certain Porsche Panamera models may have a problem with the external coolant pump used for the climate control system. Moisture can get inside this pump, and when it does, it can cause an electrical short circuit. This kind of short circuit happens when the pump is exposed to humidity it isn't designed to handle, affecting the electrical connection inside it.

Porsche seat belts may not lock child car seats properly high

Reported 2021-08-04 · Campaign 21V608000 · Model years: 2021

On some Porsche cars, including the Panamera, Taycan and 911, the seat belts have a locking feature used to hold a child seat in place. That lock can switch off too soon, so the belt may not hold the child restraint securely. Because of this, the cars do not meet a federal safety standard covering occupant protection in a crash. Dealers will check the seat belt assemblies and replace them if needed at no cost.

Porsche Panamera and Taycan: Front Suspension Arm Defect high

Reported 2021-05-17 · Campaign 21V362000 · Model years: 2021

The front lower trailing arms on these vehicles were not manufactured correctly and may break. Dealers will replace the front lower trailing arms with correctly made parts, free of charge.
