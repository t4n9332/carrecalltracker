Porsche Cayenne Engine Cylinder Cracks Can Stall Car high

Reported May 12, 2021 · Campaign 21V341000 · Model years: 2021

Some Cayenne GTS vehicles have an engine problem where cracks can develop on the surface inside the engine cylinder. Over time, these cracks may cause damage to the engine, which could make the engine stall while you are driving, or cause the engine to develop an oil leak. Porsche dealers will replace the entire engine completely, free of charge, to fix the issue with these vehicles.

Porsche Cayenne Passenger Airbag May Not Deploy high

Reported May 5, 2021 · Campaign 21V318000 · Model years: 2021

On certain Porsche Cayenne, Cayenne Coupe, Cayenne S Coupe, Cayenne GTS, Cayenne GTS Coupe, and Cayenne E-Hybrid Coupe vehicles, a heating element that should have been installed in the front passenger seat back was left out. This part affects the system that senses whether a passenger is sitting in the seat, which can throw off its readings. Because of this, the front passenger airbag may fail to deploy.

Porsche Cayenne GTS recall questions

How many recalls does the Porsche Cayenne GTS have?

NHTSA lists 2 safety recalls covering the 2021 Porsche Cayenne GTS. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Porsche Cayenne Engine Cylinder Cracks Can Stall Car" (NHTSA campaign 21V341000), reported May 12, 2021.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Porsche Cayenne GTS?

No. None of the 2 Porsche Cayenne GTS recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Porsche Cayenne GTS has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Porsche dealer, including for second owners.
