Where is my VIN? Is it private?Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. The NHTSA recall number is printed on your recall letter (for example 24V-744). Your VIN goes straight from your browser to NHTSA's public decoder to identify the year, make and model; we never receive or store it. We then show the recalls for that model year. To see which are still unrepaired on your exact car, use the official NHTSA VIN lookup.
