Ford F-650 and F-750 fluid leak can start engine fire

NHTSA campaign 20V691000 · Reported 2020-11-10 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford F-650
  • 2021 Ford F-750

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2021 F-650 and F-750 trucks with the 7.3-liter gas engine, a line that carries transmission fluid to the cooler can rub against the spinning pulley on the air conditioning compressor. That contact can wear the line and let transmission fluid leak out. If the leaking fluid drips onto hot engine parts, it can catch fire under the hood. Dealers will check that the lines are routed correctly, straighten them out, and replace any that are damaged.

How serious is it?

A fluid leak that can start an engine fire is serious; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside, but this should be inspected as soon as possible.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your truck is included. 2. Call your Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 20S66) to schedule the inspection. 3. Have the cooler lines checked, corrected, and replaced if damaged — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:COOLING UNIT AND LINES
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 F-650 and F-750 vehicles equipped with 7.3L gas engines. The transmission oil cooler line may contact the air conditioning compressor pulley, possibly causing a transmission fluid leak.
Safety risk
Leaking transmission fluid may contact hot engine components, increasing the risk of an engine compartment fire.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will inspect transmission oil cooler lines for proper orientation and damage, correcting the lines and replacing any that are damaged, free of charge. The recall began December 21, 2020. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 20S66.
