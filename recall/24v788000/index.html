Volvo Hybrid Models Brake Pedal Connection May Loosen

NHTSA campaign 24V788000 · Reported 2024-10-23 · Volvo Car USA, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A loose brake pedal connection can cause a loss of braking function, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Volvo S60, V60, S90, XC60 and XC90 hybrid vehicles may have a loose connection where the brake pedal links to the hydraulic brake unit, at a part called the pushrod screw joint. If this connection loosens, pressing the brake pedal may not properly engage the brakes. Dealers will inspect and tighten this connection as needed to fix the problem, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Volvo dealer, or Volvo Car customer service at 1-800-458-1552, to schedule a repair.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:PEDALS AND LINKAGES
Summary
Volvo Car USA, LLC (Volvo Car) is recalling certain 2020 S60, V60, S90I, XC60 and XC90 hybrid vehicles. The pushrod screw joint, connecting to the brake pedal to the hydraulic brake unit, may be loose.
Safety risk
A loose brake pedal can result in a loss of braking function, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will tighten the brake pedal pushrod screw joint, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 17, 2024. Owners may contact Volvo Car customer service at 1-800-458-1552. Volvo's number for this recall is R10289.
