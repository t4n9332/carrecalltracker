Mitsubishi Outlander fuel pump can fail and stall engine

NHTSA campaign 22V027000 · Reported 2022-01-24 · Mitsubishi Motors North America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Mitsubishi Outlander

What's wrong, in plain English

The fuel pump sits in the gas tank and pushes gasoline to the engine. Inside these pumps, small electrical parts and wires were joined together the wrong way during manufacturing. That bad connection can make the pump quit working. If the pump stops, the engine stops getting fuel, and the car can shut off while you are driving, possibly in traffic or at highway speed. Dealers will install a new fuel pump at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious: an engine that shuts off in traffic can leave you without power and lead to a crash, and while the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, you should get the free repair as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Outlander is included.
2. Call your Mitsubishi dealer, or Mitsubishi customer service at 1-888-648-7820 (recall SR-22-001), and book an appointment.
3. Have the dealer replace the fuel pump — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Mitsubishi Motors North America, Inc. (MMNA) is recalling certain 2022 Outlander vehicles. The commutator and wires inside the fuel pump may be fused incorrectly, potentially causing the fuel pump to fail.
Safety risk
Fuel pump failure can cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 18, 2022. Owners may contact MMNA customer service at 1-888-648-7820. MMNA's number for this recall is SR-22-001.
