Audi rearview camera image may fail to display

NHTSA campaign 25V900000 · Reported 2025-12-19 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderate — a missing backup image cuts your view behind the car and raises crash risk while reversing; the vehicle is safe to keep driving, so just be extra careful backing up until it is fixed.

What's wrong, in plain English

A software problem can keep the backup camera picture from showing up on the dashboard screen when you shift into reverse. Without that image, you cannot see what is directly behind the car — a person or an object — so you are more likely to hit something while backing up. Audi says the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility rule. The dealer will update the software for free.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls (VINs became searchable December 23, 2025).
2. Contact your Audi dealer, or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834 (recall number 90TV).
3. Have the software updated at no charge.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2019-2026 vehicles. Please see the recall report for a complete list of models. A software error may prevent the rearview camera image from displaying as intended. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview image that does not display reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 14, 2026. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 90TV. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on December 23, 2025.
