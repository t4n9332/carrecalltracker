Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator Second-Row Seat May Shift

NHTSA campaign 18V805000 · Reported 2018-11-14 · Ford Motor Company · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Ford Expedition
  • 2018 Lincoln Navigator

What's wrong, in plain English

The middle seat in the second row may be missing one or both of the reinforcement brackets that belong in its seat track. Without them, the seat could shift or move during a crash instead of staying put. A seat that moves cannot hold the person in it properly, so that passenger could be hurt more badly. Ford will check the seat and, if brackets are missing, replace the center seat frame. A second letter follows once the remedy is ready.

How serious is it?

It will not cause a crash, but a passenger in that middle seat could be less protected in one, and since the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, you may keep driving while you wait for the repair.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Expedition or Navigator is covered.
2. Contact your dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall number 18S37); an interim letter went out December 21, 2018, with a second letter to follow when the remedy is ready.
3. Have the second row center seat inspected and the frame replaced if brackets are missing — it is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:MID/REAR ASSEMBLY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2018 Lincoln Navigator, and Ford Expedition vehicles. The second row center seat track assemblies may be missing one or both of the J-Channel reinforcement brackets, possibly allowing the seat to move in the event of a crash.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, a seat that moves may not properly restrain the seat occupant, increasing their risk of injury.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the second row center seat for any missing J-channel reinforcement brackets, replacing the center seat frame assembly as necessary, free of charge. An interim owner notification was sent on December 21, 2018. A second letter will be mailed once the remedy is available. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 18S37
