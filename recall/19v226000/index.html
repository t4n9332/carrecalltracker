Ford F-550 First Responder Brake Lights Activate Wrongly

NHTSA campaign 19V226000 · Reported 2019-03-22 · Watts Manufacturing, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Brake lights turning on unexpectedly can confuse other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain first responder vehicles built on Ford F-550 chassis with Whelen brake lights may have wiring that turns the brake lights on even when the brake pedal hasn't been pressed. Because of this, the lights don't meet federal lighting safety requirements.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Watts Manufacturing customer service at 1-785-404-6003, or a Whelen dealer.
3. The brake lights will be replaced for free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
Watts Manufacturing, LLC (Watts Manufacturing) is recalling certain 2019 First responder vehicles built on Ford F-550 chassis and equipped with Whelen brake lights. Depending on the specific wiring of the vehicle, these brake lights may illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
If the brake lights incorrectly illuminate, it can confuse other drivers, possibly increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Watts Manufacturing will notify owners and Whelen dealers will replace the brake lights, free of charge. The recall began March 19, 2019. Owners may contact Watts Manufacturing customer service at 1-785-404-6003.
