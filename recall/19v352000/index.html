BMW M4 knee airbags may be folded incorrectly

NHTSA campaign 19V352000 · Reported 2019-05-09 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 BMW M4

What's wrong, in plain English

These BMWs have knee airbags — cushions low in the dash that protect the driver's and front passenger's legs in a crash. On the recalled cars, those airbags may have been folded and put together incorrectly at the factory. If that happened, the airbag may not open the way it should during a crash, so it may not protect you properly and you could be injured. A dealer will replace both knee airbag units free of charge.

How serious is it?

This only affects you in a crash, where the knee airbags may not deploy as intended and injury is more likely; the notice does not restrict driving, so the car is safe to drive until the repair.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your BMW is on the list.
2. Contact your BMW dealer to schedule the airbag replacement, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. Have both knee airbag modules replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:KNEE BOLSTER
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2019-2020 230i, 230i xDrive, M240i, M240i xDrive, M2 Competition Coupe, 430i, 430i xDrive, 440i, 440i xDrive, M4, and 2019 330i xDrive Sportswagon vehicles. The driver and passenger knee air bag assemblies may have been improperly folded and assembled.
Safety risk
An improperly folded and assembled air bag may not deploy as intended, increasing risk of injury.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the knee air bag modules, free of charge. The recall began July 8, 2019. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
