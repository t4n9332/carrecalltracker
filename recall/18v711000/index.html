Toyota Tundra weight capacity label lists wrong number

NHTSA campaign 18V711000 · Reported 2018-10-11 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Toyota Tundra
  • 2019 Toyota Tundra

What's wrong, in plain English

There is a sticker in these 2018-2019 Tundras that tells you how much extra weight you can carry after accessories were added. Because the all-weather floor mats were accidentally counted twice, the sticker claims 10 pounds more capacity than the truck actually has. Nothing on the truck itself is broken or worn out; only the printed number is wrong, and that makes the label fall short of federal labeling rules. Toyota is mailing every owner a corrected sticker.

How serious is it?

This is a minor paperwork problem with a 10-pound difference, and the truck is safe to keep driving normally.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is on the list.
2. Watch for the corrected label Toyota is mailing to you, and stick it over the old one.
3. If it does not arrive, call Toyota at 1-888-270-9371 (recall J0Z); there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Toyota Tundra vehicles. The accessory all-weather floormats may have been counted twice when creating the load carrying capacity modification label, resulting in the capacity modification label incorrectly overstating the additional weight by 10 pounds. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
Since the incorrect label is not within 1% of the additional weight, the vehicles are not compliant with the FMVSS.
Remedy
All vehicle owners will be sent a corrected label. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. Toyota's number for this recall is J0Z.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
