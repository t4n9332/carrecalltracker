Dodge Journey Side Reflector Is Too Dim

NHTSA campaign 22V723000 · Reported 2022-09-29 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Dodge Journey

What's wrong, in plain English

The headlight units on some 2017-2018 Dodge Journey SUVs contain a small amber reflector on the side. That reflector is supposed to bounce back the light from other cars' headlights so your vehicle shows up from the side, even when it is parked or its own lights are off. On these vehicles the reflector is too dim to do that job properly. The dealer will replace the whole headlight assembly on each side at no cost.

How serious is it?

Low urgency: the vehicle is safe to keep driving according to the notice, but a dim reflector makes you harder for other drivers to see from the side, which slightly raises crash risk.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Journey is affected.
2. Call your Dodge dealer or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403 and mention recall Z99.
3. Have the headlamp assemblies replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Dodge Journey vehicles. The amber side reflex reflector assembled into the headlamp may not reflect brightly enough. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
A dim reflector may reduce the visibility of the vehicle to other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the headlamp assemblies, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 16, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is Z99.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
