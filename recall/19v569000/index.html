Honda CR-V Fuel Tank Weld Can Leak, Cause Fire

NHTSA campaign 19V569000 · Reported Aug 1, 2019 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak like this increases the risk of a fire if it reaches an ignition source. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In these vehicles, the weld joining the fuel tank to a vapor return line may not have been made well enough. Over time, this weak weld can fail. If it fails, fuel can leak out of the tank. A fuel leak that reaches a spark or flame can catch fire. To fix this, Honda will replace the entire fuel tank with a new one, at no cost to the owner.

What you should do

1. Check your vehicle's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm it's included in this recall.
2. Contact a Honda dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138, to schedule the repair.
3. The fuel tank will be replaced free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V569000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2019 CR-V vehicles. The joint weld between the fuel tank and the vapor return line may have been insufficiently welded, causing the weld to fail.
Safety risk
The failed weld may allow fuel to leak, increasing the risk of a fire in the presence of an ignition source.
Remedy
Honda will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel tank, free of charge. The recall began September 16, 2019. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is A5I.
