Jeep Cherokee 2.0L Engine Component Failure Risk

NHTSA campaign 18V344000 · Reported 2018-05-25 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

Cylinder damage or camshaft failure can cause the engine to stall, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2.0L engines in these Cherokees may be missing valve stem keepers, which can let a valve drop into the engine cylinder and cause engine damage. These engines may also have a reversed camshaft cap, which can damage the camshaft bearing and cause the camshaft to fail.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (reference recall U52), to schedule an inspection.
3. The engine component inspection and repair or replacement, if needed, is free.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Jeep Cherokee vehicles equipped with 2.0L engines. The engines in these vehicles may be missing valve stem keepers, which can allow the valve to drop into the engine cylinder causing engine damage. These engines may also have a reversed camshaft cap that can damage the camshaft bearing causing camshaft failure.
Safety risk
Cylinder damage or camshaft failure can cause the engine to stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and repair or replace the affected engine components, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began August 9, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U52.
