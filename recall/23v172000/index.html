Cadillac, Chevy, GMC SUVs Transmission Half-Shaft Failure

NHTSA campaign 23V172000 · Reported Mar 16, 2023 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

If the half-shaft disengages, the vehicle can lose drive power, or it can roll away on its own when placed in park, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Cadillac XT5, XT6, Chevrolet Blazer, Traverse and GMC Acadia SUVs may have been built with an incorrect transmission part called a sun gear. This can cause the half-shaft on the driver's side to come loose from the transmission.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Cadillac dealer at 1-800-458-8006, a Chevrolet dealer at 1-800-222-1020, or a GMC dealer at 1-800-462-8782 to schedule the repair.
3. Dealers will replace the sun gears, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V172000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023 Cadillac XT6, GMC Acadia, Chevrolet Blazer, Traverse, and 2022-2023 Cadillac XT5 vehicles. The transmission may have been built with an incorrect sun gear, which can result in the driver-side half-shaft disengaging from the transmission.
Safety risk
A half-shaft disengaging from the transmission can result in a loss of drive power or vehicle rollaway when the vehicle is placed in park. Either of these scenarios can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the sun gears, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on July 27, 2023. The remedy is expected to become available August 31, 2023. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006, Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020, or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GMC's number for this recall is N222389310.
