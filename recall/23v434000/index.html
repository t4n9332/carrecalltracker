Tesla Model 3 Battery Disconnect May Fail

NHTSA campaign 23V434000 · Reported 2023-06-19 · Tesla, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Failure to isolate the high-voltage battery can raise the risk of electrical shock and injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

A part designed to disconnect the vehicle's high-voltage battery may be defective. If this part fails, the battery may not be properly isolated after a crash or after the vehicle's system detects a fault. This can increase the risk of electrical shock and injury to people near or in the vehicle. To address this, Tesla will replace the battery disconnect part free of charge.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
Contact Tesla Service, or call 1-877-798-3752, to schedule the repair.
The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2023 Model 3 and Model Y vehicles. The pyrotechnic battery disconnect may be defective.
Safety risk
A defective battery disconnect may not isolate the vehicle's high voltage battery after a crash or fault detection, increasing the risk of electrical shock and injury.
Remedy
Tesla Service will replace the pyrotechnic battery disconnect, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 18, 2023. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-23-16-005.
