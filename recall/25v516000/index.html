Ford Mustang Instrument Cluster May Not Light Up

NHTSA campaign 25V516000 · Reported 2025-08-08 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

A cluster that doesn't show warning lights or vehicle speed increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling one 2024 Mustang that was repaired incorrectly under an earlier recall. The instrument panel cluster, which shows things like warning lights and vehicle speed, may not illuminate at startup or while driving. Because this display may not show critical information, the vehicle does not meet federal safety standards covering controls and displays, and transmission shift and braking indicators.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Mustang is included.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, to schedule the software update.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling one 2024 Mustang vehicle that was repaired incorrectly under recall 24V802. The Instrument Panel Cluster (IPC) may not illuminate at startup or while driving. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 101, "Control and Displays" and 102, "Transmission shift lever sequence, starter interlock, and transmission braking effect."
Safety risk
An instrument panel display that does not show critical information, such as warning lights or vehicle speed, increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
A dealer will update the instrument panel cluster software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on September 12, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25C35.
