Ford Expedition, Lincoln Navigator Airbag Force Defect

NHTSA campaign 25V439000 · Reported 2025-06-27 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

An air bag that deploys with excessive force can increase the risk of injury during a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain 2025 Lincoln Navigator and Ford Expedition vehicles. The passenger side air bag installed on these vehicles may have been manufactured incorrectly. Because of this manufacturing error, the air bag can deploy with excessive force if it goes off during a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 to schedule a free dealer repair.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2025 Navigator and Expedition vehicles. The passenger side air bag may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can cause the air bag to deploy with excessive force during a crash.
Safety risk
An air bag that deploys with excessive force can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the passenger side air bags, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 8, 2025. Owners may contact Ford Customer Service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S51.
