Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator Passenger Airbag Too Forceful

NHTSA campaign 25V439000 · Reported 2025-06-27 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Ford Expedition
  • 2025 Lincoln Navigator

What's wrong, in plain English

The airbag in front of the passenger seat may have been built incorrectly at the factory. Instead of cushioning the person in a crash, it can inflate with too much force. That extra force can injure whoever is sitting in that seat during a crash. Dealers will remove the passenger side airbag and install a new one at no charge. Owner letters were mailed on August 8, 2025.

How serious is it?

Serious — if you are in a crash, the passenger airbag itself could hurt the person it is supposed to protect; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is on the list.
2. Schedule the free airbag replacement with your Ford or Lincoln dealer, or call 1-866-436-7332 (recall 25S51).
3. Until it is replaced, consider seating passengers in the back seat where possible.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2025 Navigator and Expedition vehicles. The passenger side air bag may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can cause the air bag to deploy with excessive force during a crash.
Safety risk
An air bag that deploys with excessive force can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the passenger side air bags, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 8, 2025. Owners may contact Ford Customer Service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S51.
