Porsche Macan front passenger seat sensor may fail

NHTSA campaign 21V131000 · Reported 2021-03-03 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

If the car does not detect a front passenger, the airbag will not deploy in a crash, which increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Porsche is recalling certain Macan vehicles because of a problem with the front passenger seat. A sensor mat inside that seat is supposed to tell the car when someone is sitting in the front passenger position. That mat can break down and fail to detect the passenger. Dealers will replace the sensor mat and the seat cushion free of charge, and Porsche has already mailed letters to owners.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Porsche dealer, or Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243 (recall number AMA2), to book an appointment.
3. Have the dealer replace the sensor mat and seat cushion — the repair is free.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2018 Porsche Macan
  • 2018 Porsche Macan S

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SENSOR:OCCUPANT CLASSIFICATION:FRONT PASSENGER
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Macan, Macan Turbo Kits, Macan GTS, 2015-2018 Macan S, and Macan Turbo vehicles. The front passenger seat occupancy sensor mat may degrade and fail to detect a passenger in the front seat.
Safety risk
The air bag will not deploy during a crash if a front passenger is undetected, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Porsche will notify owners, and dealers will replace the sensor mat and seat cushion, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 25, 2021. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AMA2.
