Volvo VNL Trucks: Delayed Air Brake Response

NHTSA campaign 25V102000 · Reported 2025-02-18 · Volvo Trucks North America

high How serious is it? About this label

A brake delay like this can increase the distance needed to stop the truck, which raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Volvo is recalling certain New VN trucks because the air brakes may take longer than expected to start working once the driver presses the brake pedal. This means there could be a delay before the brakes fully engage when the driver tries to slow down or stop. Because of this delay in brake activation, the affected trucks do not meet a federal safety standard covering air brake systems on trucks like these.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is affected.
2. Contact a Volvo Trucks dealer, or call Volvo customer service at 800-528-6586, to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, AIR:CONTROLS:PROPORTIONING RELAY VALVE
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2023-2025 New VN trucks. The brakes may take longer than expected to activate. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 121, "Air Brake Systems."
Safety risk
Delayed brake activation can extend the distance required to stop, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install a new pneumatic circuit, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 11, 2025. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2502.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
