Mercedes-Benz Sprinter may lose power steering from leaking hose

NHTSA campaign 21V241000 · Reported 2021-04-06 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter (VS30)
  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 1500
  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500
  • 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 3500
  • 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500

What's wrong, in plain English

A hose that carries fluid back from the power steering system may come loose and let the fluid leak out. Without that fluid, the steering can suddenly become very heavy while you are driving. The van still steers, but it takes far more muscle than you expect, and a sudden change like that can make you lose control. A dealer will install a screw-type clamp to hold the hose in place, at no cost.

How serious is it?

This one is serious because the steering can go heavy without warning, so get the van in quickly and stop driving it if you see fluid leaking or the steering suddenly stiffens.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your van is included. 2. Call Daimler Vans USA at 1-877-762-8267 (recall NV3LETULPX) or your dealer for an appointment. 3. Have the clamp installed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:HYDRAULIC POWER ASSIST:HOSE, PIPING, AND CONNECTIONS
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Mercedes Benz Sprinter and Freightliner Sprinter vehicles. The hydraulic power steering return line hoses may detach and cause hydraulic fluid to leak.
Safety risk
A hydraulic fluid leak may cause a sudden loss of power steering assist, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and dealers will install a screw-type clamp, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 28, 2021. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is NV3LETULPX.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
