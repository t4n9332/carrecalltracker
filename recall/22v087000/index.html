Ford Super Duty driveshaft may break while driving

NHTSA campaign 22V087000 · Reported 2022-02-16 · Ford Motor Company · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Ford F-250 SD
  • 2019 Ford F-350 SD
  • 2020 Ford F-350 SD
  • 2021 Ford F-250 SD
  • 2021 Ford F-350 SD
  • 2022 Ford F-250 SD

What's wrong, in plain English

On these gasoline F-250 and F-350 Super Duty trucks with aluminum driveshafts, the heat and noise insulation panels under the truck can come loose. A loose panel can rub against the spinning driveshaft, damage it, and cause it to snap. If that happens, the truck can lose power, and a broken driveshaft dragging on the road can make the truck hard to control. A parked truck could also roll away if the parking brake is not set.

How serious is it?

Serious — a broken driveshaft can cause loss of power or loss of control and raise the risk of a crash, so get it inspected promptly and always set the parking brake when parked.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included. 2. Call your Ford dealer and mention recall 22S09 (Ford customer service: 1-866-436-7332). 3. Have the dealer inspect and repair the driveshaft and reattach the insulators — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:FRAME AND MEMBERS:UNDERBODY SHIELDS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2017-2022 F-250 and F-350 Super Duty vehicles equipped with gasoline engines and aluminum driveshafts. Underbody heat and noise insulators may loosen and contact the aluminum driveshaft, which could damage the driveshaft and cause it to fracture.
Safety risk
A fractured driveshaft can cause a loss of drive power, or a loss of vehicle control if the driveshaft contacts the ground. Additionally, unintended movement could occur while parked if the parking brake is not engaged. Any of these scenarios can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and repair the driveshaft as necessary, and properly attach the underbody insulators, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on April 7, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S09.
