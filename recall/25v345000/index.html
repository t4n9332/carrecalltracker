What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Ford Escape, Lincoln Corsair, and Ford Maverick hybrid and plug-in hybrid vehicles were repaired incorrectly under an earlier recall. If the engine fails, engine oil and fuel vapor may be released into the engine compartment and build up near hot engine or exhaust parts, which are possible sources of ignition. This may result in a fire in the engine compartment.

What you should do

1. If you notice unexpected engine noises, a reduction in engine power, or smoke, park the vehicle and shut off the engine as soon as possible.
2. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
3. Contact a Ford dealer to schedule the free powertrain control module software update; you can also call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V345000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2023 Escape, 2021-2023 Corsair, and 2022-2023 Maverick vehicles equipped with 2.5L HEV or PHEV engines that were repaired incorrectly under recall 23V380. In the event of an engine failure, engine oil and fuel vapor may be released into the engine compartment and accumulate near ignition sources such as hot engine or exhaust components, possibly resulting in an engine compartment fire.
Safety risk
An engine compartment fire increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park and shut off the engine as quickly as possible if they hear unexpected engine noises, notice a reduction in vehicle power, or see smoke. Dealers will update the powertrain control module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 27, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S54.
