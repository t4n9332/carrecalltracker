Jeep Grand Cherokee and Ram 1500 Diesel Fuel Pump Failure

NHTSA campaign 22V406000 · Reported 2022-06-09 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious: an unexpected loss of engine power increases the risk of a crash, and although the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle, the repair should not be put off.

What's wrong, in plain English

These diesel trucks and SUVs have a pump that pushes fuel to the engine at very high pressure. If that pump fails, debris from inside it can get into the fuel system and leave the engine starved of fuel. When that happens, the vehicle can suddenly lose power while you are driving. Your dealer will replace the pump, and also check the rest of the fuel system and replace any other parts that need it, at no charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle falls under campaign 22V406000.
2. Contact your Jeep or Ram dealer for an appointment, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall number Z46).
3. Let the dealer replace the fuel pump and inspect the fuel system. There is no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2014-2020 Jeep Grand Cherokee, and 2014-2019 Ram 1500 vehicles equipped with 3.0L diesel engines. A high pressure fuel pump (HPFP) failure may introduce internal debris into the fuel system, potentially resulting in fuel starvation.
Safety risk
Fuel starvation may result in an unexpected loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the HPFP, and inspect and replace, if necessary, additional fuel system components, free of charge. Interim notification letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed July 21, 2022. Owner notification letters for were mailed for Jeep Grand Cherokee owners starting January 11, 2024. Owner notification letters for Ram 1500 owners were mailed March 7, 2024. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLS's number for this recall is Z46.
