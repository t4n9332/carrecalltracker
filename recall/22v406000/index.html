What's wrong, in plain English

On some Jeep Grand Cherokee and RAM 1500 vehicles with 3.0L diesel engines, the high-pressure fuel pump can fail and send debris into the fuel system. This can starve the engine of fuel.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
Contact your dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to schedule the repair.
The repair, including replacement of the fuel pump and any needed fuel system parts, is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V406000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2014-2020 Jeep Grand Cherokee, and 2014-2019 Ram 1500 vehicles equipped with 3.0L diesel engines. A high pressure fuel pump (HPFP) failure may introduce internal debris into the fuel system, potentially resulting in fuel starvation.
Safety risk
Fuel starvation may result in an unexpected loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the HPFP, and inspect and replace, if necessary, additional fuel system components, free of charge. Interim notification letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed July 21, 2022. Owner notification letters for were mailed for Jeep Grand Cherokee owners starting January 11, 2024. Owner notification letters for Ram 1500 owners were mailed March 7, 2024. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLS's number for this recall is Z46.
