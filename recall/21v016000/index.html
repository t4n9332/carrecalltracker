Ram 4500, 5500 rear brake hose may wear through

NHTSA campaign 21V016000 · Reported 2021-01-20 · Custom Truck And Body Works · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 RAM 4500
  • 2020 RAM 5500

What's wrong, in plain English

These trucks were fitted with a suspension system by an outside company, and the flexible rubber hose feeding a rear brake may have been routed where it rubs against a suspension part. Over time that rubbing wears the hose thin and brake fluid can leak out. If that happens, the rear brakes can work weakly or stop working altogether, so the truck takes much longer to stop. The shop repairs the suspension and the worn brake line.

How serious is it?

Losing part or all of your rear brakes is serious and could cause a crash, so get this inspected right away even though the notice does not order owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is affected.
2. Call Custom Truck And Body Works customer service at 1-706-553-9178 (recall 20E-031) and arrange the inspection with a dealer.
3. Have the suspension and rear brake line repaired — there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR
Summary
Custom Truck And Body Works is recalling certain 2020 Dodge 4500 and 5500 vehicles. In the affected suspension systems, the rear brake caliper flexible line may have been routed so that it contacts a suspension component, possibly causing excessive wear and a loss of rear brakes.
Safety risk
A worn rear brake caliper flexible line may cause brake fluid leakage, which may result in a partial or complete loss of the rear brakes, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Custom Truck And Body Works will notify owners, and dealers will repair the affected suspension systems, and rear brake caliper flexible line, free of charge. Owners may contact Custom Truck And Body Works customer service at 1-706-553-9178. Custom Truck And Body Works number for this recall is 20E-031.
