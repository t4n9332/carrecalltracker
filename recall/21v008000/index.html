RAM 4500 and 5500 rear brake hose can wear through

NHTSA campaign 21V008000 · Reported 2021-01-13 · Braun Northwest · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 RAM 4500
  • 2019 RAM 5500
  • 2020 RAM 4500
  • 2020 RAM 5500

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2019-2020 RAM 4500 and 5500 trucks built by Braun Northwest with Liquid Spring rear suspension have a flexible rear brake hose routed where it rubs against a suspension part. Over time the rubbing wears the hose through, and brake fluid can leak out. If that happens, the rear brakes may work poorly or stop working at all, leaving you with much less stopping power than you expect.

How serious is it?

Serious: losing the rear brakes raises your chance of a crash, so have it fixed as soon as you can, although the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is covered.
2. Contact Braun Northwest at 1-360-748-0195 or your dealer to book the repair.
3. The dealer installs a repair kit, re-routes the hose, and replaces any worn brake lines free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:POWER ASSIST:VACUUM:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Braun Northwest (Braun Northwest) is recalling certain 2019-2020 RAM 4500/5500 vehicles equipped with Liquid Spring rear suspension systems. In the affected suspension systems, the rear brake caliper flexible line may have been routed so that it contacts a suspension component, possibly causing excessive wear and a loss of rear brakes.
Safety risk
A worn rear brake line may leak fluid, resulting in a partial or complete loss of the rear brakes, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Braun Northwest will notify owners, and dealers will install a repair kit to each rear caliper and modify the rear brake caliper flexible line mounting. Any worn rear brake caliper flexible lines will be replaced as needed, free of charge. The recall began June 8, 2020. Owners may contact Braun Northwest customer service at 1-360-748-0195.
