Jeep Wrangler spare tire blocks center brake light

NHTSA campaign 23V565000 · Reported 2023-08-10 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Jeep Wrangler

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Wranglers, the spare tire mounted on the back can block the view of the brake light high in the center of the rear window. When you press the brake pedal, drivers behind you may not see that light come on, so they may not realize you are slowing or stopping. The vehicle does not meet the federal rule for vehicle lights. A dealer will replace the carrier that holds the spare tire, free of charge.

How serious is it?

A hidden brake light raises the chance of being rear-ended, and while the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, you should get the free fix promptly and leave extra room in traffic.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Wrangler is covered. 2. Call your Jeep dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and ask for recall 84A. 3. Have the spare tire carrier replaced; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2023 Jeep Wrangler vehicles. The spare tire may obstruct the view of the center high-mount brake light. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
A brake light that is obstructed may not be visible to other drivers to indicate the intention to slow or stop, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the spare tire carrier assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 28, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 84A.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
