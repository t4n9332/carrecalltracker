Jeep Wrangler Spare Tire Blocks Brake Light

NHTSA campaign 23V565000 · Reported Aug 10, 2023 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

A brake light that other drivers can't see may not signal that the vehicle is slowing or stopping, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Wrangler vehicles, the spare tire mounted on the back of the vehicle may block the view of the center high-mount brake light. Because that light is partly or fully hidden behind the spare tire, drivers behind the vehicle may not be able to see it clearly when the brakes are applied, since the spare tire sits directly in the line of sight to that light.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a dealer (FCA US, LLC customer service: 1-800-853-1403) to have the spare tire carrier assembly replaced.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V565000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2023 Jeep Wrangler vehicles. The spare tire may obstruct the view of the center high-mount brake light. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
A brake light that is obstructed may not be visible to other drivers to indicate the intention to slow or stop, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the spare tire carrier assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 28, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 84A.
