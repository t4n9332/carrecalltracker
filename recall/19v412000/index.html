What's wrong, in plain English

On these motorcycles, a small retaining part on the transmission's main shaft may come loose. If it does, the gears inside the transmission can become misaligned. This misalignment can cause the transmission to shift out of neutral and into gear on its own when the engine is started, which could make the motorcycle move unexpectedly. It could also cause the transmission and rear wheel to seize while the motorcycle is being ridden.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your motorcycle is affected. 2. Contact a Honda dealer to have the transmission main shaft replaced; Honda customer service is 1-866-784-1870. 3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V412000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:MANUAL TRANSMISSION
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2019 CB300R, 2018 CBR300R, 2018 CRF250L, 2018 CRF250L Rally, and 2018-2019 CMX300 motorcycles. The circlip, on the transmission's main shaft, may detach allowing for gear misalignment.
Safety risk
A misaligned gear can shift the transmission from neutral into gear during engine start, potentially resulting in unexpected motorcycle movement or seize the transmission and rear wheel while the motorcycle is in motion. Both conditions increase the risk of crash or injury.
Remedy
Honda will notify owners, and dealers will replace the transmission main shaft, free of charge. The recall began July 8, 2019. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KK3.
