Honda Fit and HR-V rearview camera may not appear

NHTSA campaign 24V384000 · Reported May 31, 2024 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

medium How serious is it? About this label

A rearview camera display that does not work gives the driver less view behind the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Honda is recalling certain Fit and HR-V vehicles that were already part of an earlier recall. Because of a design problem in the power circuit for the audio display, the rearview camera picture may not come up on the screen when the engine is started with a key. These vehicles therefore do not meet the federal rear visibility standard. Vehicles that were repaired under the earlier recall still need this new repair done.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered.
2. Contact a Honda dealer to have the display audio unit software updated, or call Honda service at 1-888-234-2138. Book the appointment even if your vehicle was already repaired under the earlier recall.
3. The software update is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V384000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:DISPLAY FUNCTION
Summary
Honda (America Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2018-2020 Fit and 2019-2022 HR-V vehicles that were previously recalled under NHTSA recall number 23V-046. The rearview camera image may not display when the engine is started with a key, due to a design error in the audio display power circuit. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
An inoperative rearview camera display can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the display audio unit software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 8, 2024. Owners may contact Honda service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's numbers for this recall are TIQ and DIR. Vehicles previously repaired under recall 23V-046 will need to have the new remedy completed.
