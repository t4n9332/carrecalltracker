Audi A3, S3, RS3 Passenger Airbag May Deactivate

NHTSA campaign 21V198000 · Reported 2021-03-22 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A deactivated passenger airbag increases the risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

These Audi vehicles have a system that senses whether the front passenger seat is occupied and turns the passenger airbag on or off accordingly. This system may malfunction and shut off the passenger airbag even when someone is sitting in that seat. If the airbag is switched off while a passenger is there, it will not be available to help protect that person.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact an Audi dealer, or call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834, to schedule the repair.
3. The sensor mat replacement and control module update will be done free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SENSOR:OCCUPANT CLASSIFICATION:FRONT PASSENGER
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2015-2020 Audi S3 Sedan, A3 Sedan, 2016-2018 A3 Etron, 2017-2020 RS3 Sedan, and 2015-2019 A3 Cabriolet vehicles. The passenger occupant detection system (PODS) may malfunction and switch off the passenger air bag even when the seat is occupied.
Safety risk
A deactivated passenger air bag increases the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the PODS sensor mat and update the PODS control module, free of charge. Owners letters were mailed December 23, 2021. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 69BY.
