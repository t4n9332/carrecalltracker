Volkswagen ID.4 Battery Modules Can Catch Fire

NHTSA campaign 25V836000 · Reported Dec 3, 2025 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

Owners are told to park outside immediately after charging, not leave the vehicle charging indoors overnight, avoid Level 3 DC fast chargers, and limit charging to 80% until the repair is complete. A vehicle fire while parked or driving can increase the risk of injury.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Volkswagen ID.4 vehicles have high voltage battery cell modules that may contain misaligned electrodes inside them. This kind of misalignment inside the battery modules can result in a battery fire.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 to schedule the free repair.
3. Until repaired, park outside right after charging, don't charge indoors overnight, avoid Level 3 DC fast chargers, and limit charging to 80%.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V836000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2023-2024 ID.4 vehicles. The high voltage (HV) battery cell modules may contain misaligned electrodes that can result in a battery fire.
Safety risk
A vehicle fire while parked or driving can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside immediately after charging and not leave their vehicles charging indoors overnight until the recall repair is complete. Also, owners are advised not to use Level 3 DC chargers and to limit the maximum charge of the battery to 80% until the remedy has been completed. Dealers will replace the high voltage battery cell modules as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 11, 2026. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 93EV. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on December 5, 2025.
