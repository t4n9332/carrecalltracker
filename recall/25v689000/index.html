Ford Mustang Low Brake Fluid Light May Fail

NHTSA campaign 25V689000 · Reported Oct 10, 2025 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

Driving with low brake fluid that goes unnoticed can reduce how well the brakes work, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Mustang vehicles that were repaired incorrectly under an earlier recall. A sensor that is supposed to turn on a dashboard warning light when brake fluid runs low may fail to activate. Without that warning light, a driver could continue driving without realizing the brake fluid has dropped to a low level.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm if your Mustang is affected.
2. Contact a dealer, or call Ford Customer Service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. The body control module software update is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V689000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:BODY CONTROL MODULE/BCM
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Mustang vehicles repaired incorrectly under recall number 23V727. The brake fluid level sensor may not activate the visual warning indicator when the brake fluid is low. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 135, "Light Vehicle Brake Systems."
Safety risk
Without a low brake fluid warning light, the vehicle may be driven with low brake fluid, reducing braking performance and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the body control module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 11, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25C55. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning November 17, 2025.
