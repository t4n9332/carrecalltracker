Tesla Model S side curtain airbags may deploy improperly

NHTSA campaign 22V104000 · Reported 2022-02-23 · Tesla, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Tesla Model S

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2021 and 2022 Model S cars were built with side curtain airbags whose inflators, the part that fills the bag with gas, may have been put together incorrectly. These airbags drop down from above the side windows to cushion heads in a side impact. If the inflator is wrong, the bag can open late or not open the way it should, so it may not protect you as designed. Tesla will replace the airbags.

How serious is it?

The car drives normally, but in a side crash the airbag may not protect you properly, so get the free replacement done as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Model S is affected.
2. Schedule service with a Tesla Service Center for the free side curtain airbag replacement; letters were mailed April 28, 2022.
3. Call Tesla at 1-877-798-3752 and reference recall SB-22-20-001 if you need help booking.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN:INFLATOR
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Model S vehicles. The right or left side curtain air bag inflators may have been assembled incorrectly.
Safety risk
An incorrectly assembled inflator can cause a delayed or improper deployment of the side curtain air bag, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Tesla Service Centers will replace the side curtain air bags, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 28, 2022. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-22-20-001.
