Tesla Model S Side Airbags May Not Deploy Properly

NHTSA campaign 22V104000 · Reported 2022-02-23 · Tesla, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A side airbag that deploys late or does not deploy properly can increase the risk of injury during a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2021-2022 Tesla Model S vehicles may have a right or left side curtain airbag with an inflator that was not assembled correctly during manufacturing. Because the inflator was not assembled as intended, the airbag may deploy later than expected, or it may not deploy properly, when needed during a crash. This means the airbag may not respond correctly at the moment it is needed most.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Tesla Service or Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752 to schedule the free repair.
3. Tesla Service Centers will replace the side curtain airbags at no cost.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN:INFLATOR
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Model S vehicles. The right or left side curtain air bag inflators may have been assembled incorrectly.
Safety risk
An incorrectly assembled inflator can cause a delayed or improper deployment of the side curtain air bag, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Tesla Service Centers will replace the side curtain air bags, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 28, 2022. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-22-20-001.
