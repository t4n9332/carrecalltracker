Nissan Leaf Battery May Overheat While Fast Charging

NHTSA campaign 24V700000 · Reported 2024-09-20 · Nissan North America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

A battery that overheats while fast charging increases the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Leaf models with fast-charging capability, the main battery can overheat while using a Level 3 quick charger. Nissan is fixing this with a software update to the battery, done by the dealer. Owners are being notified in phases, so letters may go out at different times.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 or your dealer to schedule the software update.
3. The update is free.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2019 Nissan Leaf
  • 2020 Nissan Leaf

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2019-2020 LEAF vehicles equipped with a Level 3 quick charging port. The lithium-ion battery may overheat during Level 3 charging.
Safety risk
A quick charging battery that overheats increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will update the battery software, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed June 3, 2026. This is a phased recall. Owners may contact Nissan Customer Service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's numbers for this recall are R24B2, P4A38, and P5A22.
