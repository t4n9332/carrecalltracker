Ford Transit Van Foldaway Seat May Unlock

NHTSA campaign 19V252000 · Reported Mar 28, 2019 · Waldoch Crafts, Inc

high How serious is it? About this label

If the seat back moves during a crash, the person sitting in it faces an increased risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

This recall covers Ford Transit shuttle vans that have been fitted with folding seats. When one of these seats is folded up from its stored position into the upright, usable position, the seat cushion might not lock securely into place the way it's supposed to. If the vehicle is in a crash or has to stop suddenly, the seat cushion could move because it wasn't locked properly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact Waldoch customer service at 1-651-464-3215 to schedule a dealer inspection. 3. The seat will be inspected and repaired free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V252000. Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Waldoch Crafts, Inc. (Waldoch) is recalling certain 2018 Ford Transit shuttle vans modified to be equipped with Freedman GO-ES Foldaway Seats, part number 43705. When the back seat cushion is rotated from the stowed position to the upright position, the seat cushion may not remain locked into place when under load, such as in a crash or a sudden stop.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, if the seat back moves, the seat occupant has an increased risk of injury.
Remedy
Waldoch will notify owners and dealers will inspect and repair the foldaway seat, free of charge. The recall began September 25, 2020. Owners may contact Waldoch customer service at 1-651-464-3215.
