Honda motorcycles may leak engine oil onto rear tire

NHTSA campaign 25V358000 · Reported 2025-05-29 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Honda XL750
  • 2025 Honda Cmx1100
  • 2025 Honda Crf1100

What's wrong, in plain English

A sealing bolt on the engine case may have been made incorrectly. If it does not seal properly, engine oil can seep out and drip onto the rear tire. Oil on a tire makes it slippery and can cause you to lose control of the motorcycle, especially while braking, cornering, or riding on wet pavement. Dealers will replace the crankcase sealing bolt at no charge, and owner letters went out in July 2025.

How serious is it?

Oil on the rear tire can cause a crash or injury, so get the bolt replaced soon and stop riding if you see oil leaking or dripping near the rear wheel.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your motorcycle is affected.
2. Contact a Honda motorcycle dealer or Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870 and reference recall KS2.
3. Have the crankcase sealing bolt replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:OIL/LUBRICATION
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2024 XL750, 2025 CMX1100, and CRF1100 motorcycles. The crankcase sealing bolt may have been improperly manufactured, which can cause the engine to leak oil onto the rear tire.
Safety risk
Oil on the rear tire may cause a loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the crankcase sealing bolt, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 15, 2025. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KS2.
