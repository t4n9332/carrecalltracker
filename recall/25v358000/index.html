Honda CMX1100, CRF1100, XL750 Oil Leak Risk

NHTSA campaign 25V358000 · Reported 2025-05-29 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

Oil leaking onto the rear tire may cause a loss of control while riding, increasing the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Honda CMX1100, CRF1100 and XL750 motorcycles have a bolt problem in the engine. The bolt that seals the crankcase may not have been made correctly, which can let engine oil leak out and land on the rear tire. Oil on the rear tire may cause a loss of control while riding, which increases the risk of a crash or injury.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your motorcycle is included in this recall.
2. Contact your Honda dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870, to schedule a repair.
3. The crankcase sealing bolt will be replaced free of charge.

Affected vehicles (3)

  • 2024 Honda XL750
  • 2025 Honda Cmx1100
  • 2025 Honda Crf1100

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:OIL/LUBRICATION
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2024 XL750, 2025 CMX1100, and CRF1100 motorcycles. The crankcase sealing bolt may have been improperly manufactured, which can cause the engine to leak oil onto the rear tire.
Safety risk
Oil on the rear tire may cause a loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the crankcase sealing bolt, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 15, 2025. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KS2.
