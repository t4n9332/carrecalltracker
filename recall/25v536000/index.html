Chevrolet Corvette Fuel Leak Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 25V536000 · Reported Aug 21, 2025 · General Motors, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source, such as a hot engine or exhaust component, can increase the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Chevrolet Corvette vehicles with a left-side radiator and fan combination may allow extra fuel to spill into the fuel-filler pocket area during refueling. That spilled fuel could then leak out and land on a nearby ignition source, such as a hot engine or exhaust part.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer, or call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020.
3. The repair (installing a shield to divert spilled fuel) is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V536000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023–2026 Chevrolet Corvette vehicles equipped with a left-side radiator/fan combination. Excess fuel may spill into the vehicle fuel-filler pocket during refueling and leak onto an ignition source.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source, such as hot engine or exhaust components, can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will install a shield to divert spilled fuel, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 14, 2025. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N252514930.
