Chevrolet Corvette can leak fuel while refueling, risking fire

NHTSA campaign 25V536000 · Reported 2025-08-21 · General Motors, LLC · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Chevrolet Corvette
  • 2024 Chevrolet Corvette

What's wrong, in plain English

When you fill up, extra gas can splash and collect in the pocket around the fuel filler door instead of going into the tank. On these cars, that spilled fuel can run out and reach hot engine or exhaust parts, which can start a fire. It affects Corvettes built with a particular left-side radiator and fan setup. The fix is a shield the dealer installs to steer any spilled gas away from the hot parts.

How serious is it?

This is serious because spilled fuel reaching hot engine or exhaust parts can cause a fire; GM does not tell owners to stop driving or to park outside, but fill the tank slowly and avoid topping off until the shield is installed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Corvette is included in recall 25V536000 (GM's number N252514930).
2. Contact your Chevrolet dealer, or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020, to schedule the repair.
3. Have the dealer install the fuel-diverting shield — it is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023–2026 Chevrolet Corvette vehicles equipped with a left-side radiator/fan combination. Excess fuel may spill into the vehicle fuel-filler pocket during refueling and leak onto an ignition source.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source, such as hot engine or exhaust components, can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will install a shield to divert spilled fuel, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 14, 2025. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N252514930.
