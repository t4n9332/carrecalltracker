Rivian R1T and R1S accelerator pedal misreads release

NHTSA campaign 23V883000 · Reported 2023-12-21 · Rivian Automotive, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Rivian R1S
  • 2022 Rivian R1T

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2022 Rivian R1T and R1S trucks and SUVs, the accelerator pedal may not sense when you have taken your foot off it. Because of that, the vehicle may not switch on "auto-hold" or shift into "park" the way it should. The result is that the vehicle can move when you expect it to stay still, which could catch you or someone nearby by surprise.

How serious is it?

Serious — unintended vehicle movement can cause a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is covered. 2. Make sure your vehicle has installed the free over-the-air software update Rivian released. 3. Contact Rivian at 1-888-748-4261 (recall FSAM 1342) to arrange the free accelerator pedal replacement.

Official notice

Component
VEHICLE SPEED CONTROL:ACCELERATOR PEDAL
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022 R1T and R1S vehicles. The accelerator pedal may not detect when the driver releases the pedal, failing to activate "auto-hold" or "park" as intended.
Safety risk
Failure to activate "auto-hold" or "park" can result in unintended vehicle movement, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian has released an over-the-air (OTA) software update. Rivian will also replace the accelerator pedals, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 18, 2024. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM 1342. (over-the-air software update)

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
