Rivian R1T and R1S Accelerator Pedal Glitch

NHTSA campaign 23V883000 · Reported 2023-12-21 · Rivian Automotive, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

If auto-hold or park doesn't activate as expected, the vehicle could move unintentionally, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Rivian R1T and R1S vehicles, the accelerator pedal may not detect when you release it. This can prevent 'auto-hold' or 'park' from activating the way it's supposed to.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261; Rivian will send a software update and replace the accelerator pedal.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2022 Rivian R1S
  • 2022 Rivian R1T

Official notice

Component
VEHICLE SPEED CONTROL:ACCELERATOR PEDAL
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022 R1T and R1S vehicles. The accelerator pedal may not detect when the driver releases the pedal, failing to activate "auto-hold" or "park" as intended.
Safety risk
Failure to activate "auto-hold" or "park" can result in unintended vehicle movement, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian has released an over-the-air (OTA) software update. Rivian will also replace the accelerator pedals, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 18, 2024. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM 1342. (over-the-air software update)
