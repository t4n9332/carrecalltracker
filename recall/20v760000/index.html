Audi RS5 Coupe has wrong battery terminal cover fitted

NHTSA campaign 20V760000 · Reported 2020-12-09 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Audi RS5
  • 2019 Audi RS5

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Audi RS5 Coupes were built with the wrong cover over the positive battery terminal. The cover matters in a rear-end crash: with the wrong one, the car's 12-volt electrical supply can be knocked out. If that happens, the doors may not open from the outside and the hazard warning flashers may not come on, which makes it harder for anyone to reach you or see you after a crash. Dealers will install the correct cover.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious: the car drives normally and the problem only matters if you are hit from behind, but that is exactly when you would want the doors and flashers to work, so get it done.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your RS5 is on the list. 2. Contact an Audi dealer or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834 (recall 27i6) to schedule the swap. 3. Have the positive terminal cover replaced — it is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Audi RS5 Coupe vehicles. An incorrect positive battery terminal cover was installed.
Safety risk
In the event of a rear crash, the incorrect cover can cause the 12-volt power supply to be compromised, preventing the vehicle doors from being opened from the outside, or the hazard warning lights from functioning, increasing risk of injury.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will replace the positive terminal cover, free of charge. The recall began January 19, 2021. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 27i6.
