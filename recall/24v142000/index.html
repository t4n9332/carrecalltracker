Ford Bronco Rear Glass Panels May Detach

NHTSA campaign 24V142000 · Reported Feb 23, 2024 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

A glass panel coming loose while driving is a hazard to others on the road and raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Bronco and Bronco Heritage models, the rear quarter glass panels may not have been installed correctly onto the hard top. Because of this, the panels can come loose and detach from the vehicle while it is being driven. A glass panel that comes off creates a hazard on the road for other drivers and increases the chance of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. 3. The inspection, reinstallation, or panel replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V142000. Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:GLASS, SIDE/REAR
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Bronco and Bronco Heritage vehicles. The rear quarter glass panels may have been improperly installed to the vehicle hard top, which can allow them to detach.
Safety risk
Detached rear glass panels may create a road hazard and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and reinstall the rear glass panels on Bronco vehicles. On Bronco Heritage vehicles, dealers will replace the rear left-hand removable quarter glass panel. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Bronco owner notification letters were mailed June 6, 2024. Bronco Heritage owner letters were mailed June 12, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S10.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
