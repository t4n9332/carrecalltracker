Tesla Model 3 sun visor missing airbag warning label

NHTSA campaign 19V719000 · Reported 2019-10-10 · Tesla, Inc.

low How serious is it? About this label

This is a labeling problem rather than a mechanical failure, and the vehicle is safe to keep driving, though the missing warning still increases injury risk by leaving out required safety information.

What's wrong, in plain English

The sun visor on the driver's side is missing the printed airbag warning information that federal rules require. That label carries safety information about the airbags for anyone using the vehicle. Nothing is mechanically broken and the notice does not say the airbags themselves fail, but without the required warning people may not get safety information they are supposed to have. Tesla service centers will inspect the visor and replace it if needed, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Model 3 is covered. 2. Contact Tesla service or Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752 and reference recall SB-19-15-003. 3. Have the sun visor inspected and replaced at no charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Tesla Motors, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2019 Tesla Model 3 vehicles. The driver-side sun visor may not have the required air bag warning information. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
Failure to provide the air bag warning information increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Tesla will notify owners, and Tesla Service Centers will inspect the visor and replace it, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began December 6, 2019. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-19-15-003.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
