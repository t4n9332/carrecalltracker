Mercedes GLB35 Front Axle Carrier May Corrode

NHTSA campaign 21V990000 · Reported 2021-12-17 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing steering control increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some AMG GLB35, A220, and GLB250 vehicles have a part called the integral carrier that supports the front axle. Over time, this part may corrode and eventually fail. If the carrier fails, the driver could lose steering control, which increases the risk of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer, or call MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. 3. Inspection and replacement of the carrier, if needed, is free.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC. (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2021 AMG GLB35, 2019-2020 A220, and 2020 GLB250 vehicles. The integral carrier that supports the front axle may corrode, causing it to fail.
Safety risk
Front axle carrier failure may result in a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the integral carrier, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 29, 2022. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2022070009.
