Ford E-350/E-450 Shuttle Bus Backup Camera May Reverse

NHTSA campaign 19V466000 · Reported 2019-06-18 · SVO Group Inc

high How serious is it? About this label

If the camera image is reversed, the driver may turn the wrong way trying to avoid something behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

SVO Group is recalling certain Ford E-350 and E-450 shuttle buses equipped with ASA Electronics Voyager monitors that display the back-up camera image. These monitors may revert back to their factory default settings, and when that happens, the image shown on the screen may appear reversed instead of showing accurately what is behind the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact your dealer, or call ASA Electronics customer service at 1-800-384-4400, to arrange the fix.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2018 Ford E-350
  • 2018 Ford E-450

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT
Summary
SVO Group Inc. (SVO Group) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Ford E-350 and E-450-based shuttle buses equipped with ASA Electronics Voyager monitors used to display the back-up camera image. The affected monitors may revert back to the factory default settings which may cause the camera image to be reversed.
Safety risk
The driver may inadvertently turn the wrong direction to avoid an object behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
SVO Group will work with ASA to notify owners, and ASA will provide a harness relay capacitor module to eliminate the image reversal, free of charge. The recall began May 9, 2019. Owners may contact ASA Electronics customer service at 1-800-384-4400.
