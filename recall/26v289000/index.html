Chevy Tahoe and GMC Yukon wheels can lock up

NHTSA campaign 26V289000 · Reported 2026-05-07 · General Motors, LLC · critical severity

⚠ NHTSA advises not driving this vehicle until it is repaired. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Chevrolet Tahoe
  • 2019 GMC Yukon
  • 2020 GMC Yukon

What's wrong, in plain English

These four-wheel-drive and all-wheel-drive trucks and SUVs may be missing a part inside the transfer case, the gearbox that sends power to the front and rear wheels. Because of that, the front wheels, the rear wheels, or both can suddenly lock up with no warning at all. GM says not to drive the vehicle until the repair is done. Locked wheels can easily cause a crash. A dealer will inspect the transfer case and replace it if needed, free.

How serious is it?

This is the most serious kind of recall — wheels can lock without warning and cause a crash, and GM tells owners not to drive the vehicle at all until it has been repaired.

What you should do

1. Stop driving the vehicle now — GM says do not drive it until the remedy is complete.
2. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
3. Call your dealer or GM (Chevrolet 1-800-222-1020, GMC 1-800-462-8782, Cadillac 1-800-333-4223; recall N262557620) to arrange the free inspection and repair.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:TRANSFER CASE (4-WHEEL DRIVE)
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2026 Cadillac Escalade and Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Silverado 1500, Suburban, and Tahoe, and GMC Sierra 1500, Yukon, and Yukon XL vehicles with four-wheel (4WD) or all-wheel drive (AWD), and certain 2015-2020 Suburban, Escalade, Escalade ESV, Yukon, Yukon XL, and Tahoe vehicles. A component missing from the drivetrain transfer case may cause the front and/or rear wheels to lock up without warning to the driver.
Safety risk
Front or rear wheel lockup increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to drive their vehicle until the remedy is completed. Dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace the transfer case assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 18, 2026. Owners may contact GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782, Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223. GM's number for this recall is N262557620. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on May 7, 2026.
