Cadillac XT5, XT6 and GMC Acadia fuel leak risk

NHTSA campaign 21V422000 · Reported 2021-06-03 · General Motors, LLC · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Cadillac XT5
  • 2021 Cadillac XT6
  • 2021 GMC Acadia

What's wrong, in plain English

The lines that carry gasoline to the engine may not have been connected and seated properly when the vehicle was built. A line that is not seated can work loose or come apart, letting gasoline leak out. Fuel leaking near a hot engine can catch fire. If you ever smell gasoline or see a puddle under the vehicle, stop driving it and call for help. Dealers will inspect the lines and replace them if needed.

How serious is it?

This is the most serious kind of defect because a fuel leak can start a fire; the notice does not order you to park the vehicle, but get it inspected immediately and stop driving if you smell fuel.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls right away. 2. Call Cadillac at 1-800-458-8006 or GMC at 1-800-462-8782 (GM recall N212335590), or go straight to your dealer; letters were mailed July 1, 2021. 3. The inspection and any fuel line replacement are free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
General Motors, LLC. (GM) is recalling certain 2021 Cadillac XT5, XT6, and GMC Acadia vehicles. The fuel supply lines may be improperly attached and seated.
Safety risk
Improperly attached and seated fuel supply lines may loosen or separate, causing a fuel leak and increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace fuel supply lines as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on July 1, 2021. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 and GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N212335590.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
