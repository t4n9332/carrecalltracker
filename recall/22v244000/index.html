Nissan Sentra right headlight may be aimed wrong

NHTSA campaign 22V244000 · Reported 2022-04-13 · Nissan North America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Nissan Sentra

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2020 Nissan Sentra cars with LED headlights may have the right-hand headlight pointed in the wrong direction. A headlight aimed incorrectly doesn't light up the road the way it should, so the driver may not see as much at night. The dealer will replace the right-hand headlight free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 10, 2022. Nissan's number for this recall is R20B4.

How serious is it?

A moderate concern — poorer visibility raises crash risk, especially at night — but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving the car.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your Sentra is included. 2. Contact your Nissan dealer or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 (recall R20B4) to schedule the headlight replacement. 3. Have the headlight replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020 Sentra vehicles, equipped with LED headlights. The right-hand headlight aim may be misaligned. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamp, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
An improperly aimed headlight may reduce the driver's visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the right-hand headlight, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 10, 2022. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is R20B4.
