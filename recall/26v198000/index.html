Mercedes-Benz G580 EV Wheel Bolts May Fail

NHTSA campaign 26V198000 · Reported Mar 27, 2026 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A wheel that loosens or comes off while driving raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

During manufacturing, the wrong bolts may have been used to attach the wheels on these vehicles. Bolts that are not the correct type may not hold the wheel as securely as they should. If this happens, a wheel could become loose or detach from the vehicle while you are driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included. 2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer, or call MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. 3. The wheel bolt replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V198000. Official notice

Component
WHEELS:LUGS/NUTS/BOLTS/STUDS
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2025 G580 vehicles. The incorrect wheel bolts may have been installed during vehicle manufacturing.
Safety risk
Incorrect wheel bolts can allow a wheel to loosen or detach from the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the wheel bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 24, 2026. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2026040008. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on April 2, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
