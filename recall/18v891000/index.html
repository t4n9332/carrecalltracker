Hyundai Elantra GT sunroof may close on obstacles

NHTSA campaign 18V891000 · Reported 2018-12-14 · Hyundai Motor America · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Hyundai Elantra GT

What's wrong, in plain English

The motor for the large glass panoramic sunroof on these Hyundai Elantra GT models may have the wrong software loaded. A sunroof is supposed to sense when something is in the way as it closes and then stop and pull back. With the wrong programming, it may not notice an obstacle and can keep closing on a hand, an arm, or a child's head. The dealer will inspect the sunroof and replace it if needed.

How serious is it?

The car is safe to drive, but the sunroof can pinch or injure someone, so be careful closing it — especially with children aboard — until it is repaired.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is covered.
2. Contact a Hyundai dealer, or Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460, and mention recall 179.
3. Have the panoramic sunroof inspected and, if necessary, replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2018 Hyundai Elantra GT vehicles. The panoramic sunroof motor may be incorrectly programmed, preventing the sunroof from detecting an obstacle while closing.
Safety risk
If the sunroof does not detect an obstacle while closing and then retract, there could be an increased risk of injury.
Remedy
Hyundai will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the panoramic sunroof, replacing it as necessary, free of charge. The recall began February 5, 2019. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 179.
