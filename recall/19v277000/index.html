Audi Q5 and SQ5 brake master cylinder may fail

NHTSA campaign 19V277000 · Reported 2019-04-08 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Audi Q5
  • 2018 Audi SQ5
  • 2019 Audi Q5
  • 2019 Audi SQ5

What's wrong, in plain English

The part that pushes brake fluid to your wheels when you press the brake pedal was made too short at the factory. Because of that, pressing the pedal may not build enough pressure, and your brakes can stop working suddenly while you are driving. The electric parking brake, including its emergency stopping function, is not affected and will still work. Dealers will check the part and replace it if needed.

How serious is it?

This is serious because the brakes can quit with no warning, and while Audi has not told owners to stop driving, you should get the inspection done as soon as a dealer can take the car.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included in campaign 19V277000.
2. Call your Audi dealer or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834 (recall 47O9) and ask for the earliest appointment.
3. Have the master cylinder inspected and replaced if needed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:MASTER CYLINDER
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Audi Q5 and SQ5 vehicles. The brake master cylinder casting may be too short.
Safety risk
A short casting can cause a sudden loss of brakes, increasing the risk of crash. The electronic parking brake and its emergency brake function are not affected and will still function.
Remedy
Audi has notified owners, and dealers will inspect the brake master cylinder, replacing it as necessary, free of charge. The recall began May 3, 2019. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 47O9.
