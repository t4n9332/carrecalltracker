Chevy Bolt EV Brake Caliper May Fracture

NHTSA campaign 20V808000 · Reported Dec 22, 2020 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

If a brake caliper fractures and brake fluid is lost, the vehicle may experience reduced brake performance, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

GM says some Chevrolet Bolt EV vehicles may have a front left brake caliper that was cast improperly. This caliper can fracture during a high-pressure braking event. GM is recalling the affected vehicles so dealers can inspect the casting lot number on the front left brake caliper and replace it if necessary.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer, or call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V808000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:DISC:CALIPER
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2020 Chevrolet Bolt EV vehicles. These vehicles may have an improperly cast front left brake caliper that can fracture during a high-pressure braking event.
Safety risk
If a brake caliper fractures and brake fluid is lost, the vehicle may experience reduced brake performance, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the casting lot number on the front left brake caliper and replace it if necessary, free of charge. The recall began January 29, 2021. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N202321400.
