Chevrolet Bolt EV front brake caliper can crack

NHTSA campaign 20V808000 · Reported 2020-12-22 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A caliper that breaks during hard braking can cut your braking power with little warning and lead to a crash, so treat it seriously; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving before the free inspection.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2020 Bolt EVs have a front left brake caliper — the part that presses the brake pads to slow the wheel — that was poorly made. If you brake hard, it can crack. A cracked caliper can let brake fluid escape, and losing fluid means the brakes may not stop the car as well as they should. A dealer will check the lot number stamped on that caliper and put in a new one if yours is affected.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Bolt EV is included.
2. Call your Chevrolet dealer or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 and mention recall N202321400.
3. Have the front left caliper inspected, and replaced if needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:DISC:CALIPER
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2020 Chevrolet Bolt EV vehicles. These vehicles may have an improperly cast front left brake caliper that can fracture during a high-pressure braking event.
Safety risk
If a brake caliper fractures and brake fluid is lost, the vehicle may experience reduced brake performance, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the casting lot number on the front left brake caliper and replace it if necessary, free of charge. The recall began January 29, 2021. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N202321400.
