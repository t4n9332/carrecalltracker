Ford Super Duty Trucks Front Wheels Can Detach

NHTSA campaign 23V758000 · Reported 2023-11-09 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity

⚠ NHTSA advises not driving this vehicle until it is repaired. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Ford F-450 SD
  • 2022 Ford F-550 SD
  • 2022 Ford F-600 SD
  • 2023 Ford F-450 SD
  • 2023 Ford F-550 SD
  • 2023 Ford F-600 SD

What's wrong, in plain English

The nuts that hold the front wheel hub extender — the spacer the front wheel bolts onto — may not have been tightened enough. If they work loose, the wheel and the extender can come off the truck while you are driving. That can make you lose control, and a loose wheel in the road is dangerous for other drivers too. Ford says do not drive these trucks until a dealer has checked them.

How serious is it?

Very serious — a front wheel can come off and cause a crash, and Ford tells owners not to drive the truck until the repair has been performed.

What you should do

1. Stop driving the truck and confirm your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls under campaign 23V758000.
2. Call your Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 23S59) and arrange to get the truck in without driving it.
3. The dealer will check the hub extender nuts and replace the extender, nuts, and hub if they are loose — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AXLE HUBS
Summary
Ford Motor Company is recalling certain 2022-2023 Ford Super Duty F-350, F-450, F-550, and F-600 vehicles. The fasteners for the front wheel hub extender may be insufficiently tightened, which can result in the wheel and wheel extender detaching from the vehicle.
Safety risk
Wheel detachment can result in a loss of vehicle control and create a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to drive their vehicle until the repair has been performed. Dealers will inspect the front wheel hub extender retaining nuts. If the retaining nuts are loose, dealers will replace the wheel hub extender, retaining nuts, and hub assembly. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 7, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at -866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23S59.
