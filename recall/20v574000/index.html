Ford Expedition Seat Belt Sensor Misreads Child Passengers

NHTSA campaign 20V574000 · Reported Sep 23, 2020 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

If a child is misclassified as an adult, the passenger air bag could deploy instead of being turned off in a crash, increasing the risk of injury to that child. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Ford Expedition vehicles have a problem with the seat belt tension sensor. This sensor is meant to tell whether the person in the seat is a child or an adult. In some cases, it may incorrectly identify a child as an adult.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Ford dealer; Ford's customer service number is 1-866-436-7332.
3. The seat belt buckle replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V574000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020 Expedition vehicles. The seat belt tension sensor may incorrectly classify a child occupant as an adult.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, the incorrect classification would enable the passenger air bag to deploy instead of disabling it, increasing the risk of injury to the seat occupant.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will replace the seat belt buckle, free of charge. The recall began October 16, 2020. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 20S54.
