Toyota 4Runner has wrong weight limit sticker

NHTSA campaign 22V480000 · Reported 2022-07-06 · Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Toyota 4runner
  • 2021 Toyota 4runner
  • 2022 Toyota 4runner

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 4Runners fitted with 20-inch Black Gunner Wheels left the distributor with the wrong label showing how much weight the vehicle can safely carry. That sticker tells you the maximum combined weight of people and cargo you should put in the truck. Because the number printed on it is incorrect, you could load up the 4Runner past what it is actually rated to handle without realizing it. The distributor is mailing corrected labels to owners at no cost.

How serious is it?

This is a paperwork and labeling problem rather than a broken part, and the vehicle is safe to keep driving, but overloading it because of the wrong number could increase your chance of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your 4Runner is affected. 2. Watch for the replacement label in the mail and apply it, or call Southeast Toyota customer service at 954-429-2000 (recall SET22A) if it does not arrive. 3. The new label is free — until it arrives, avoid loading the vehicle heavily.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC (SET) is recalling certain 2020-2022 4Runner vehicles equipped with 20-inch Black Gunner Wheels. The load carrying capacity modification labels are incorrect. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
An incorrect label may lead to unintentionally overloading the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
SET will mail replacement labels to owners, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed September 4, 2022. Owners may contact SET's customer service at 954-429-2000. SET's number for this recall is SET22A.
