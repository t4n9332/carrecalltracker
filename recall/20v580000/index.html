Jeep, Ram Front Seat Belts May Not Lock

NHTSA campaign 20V580000 · Reported 2020-09-24 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

If the front seat belts fail to properly lock, there is an increased risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In the event of a crash, the front seat belt retractors on these vehicles may not properly slow an occupant's forward movement after the belt locks. This means the seat belt may not restrain the occupant as effectively as intended during a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Chrysler dealer to have the front seat belt retractors replaced; call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The repair is free.

Affected vehicles (5)

  • 2019 Jeep Wrangler
  • 2019 RAM 1500
  • 2019 RAM 2500
  • 2020 Jeep Gladiator
  • 2020 RAM 1500

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Jeep Wrangler and Ram 2500, 2019-2020 Ram 1500 and 2020 Jeep Gladiator vehicles. In the event of a crash, a front seat belt retractor may not properly slow the occupant's forward movement after locking.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, if the front seat belts fail to lock, there is an increased risk of injury.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the front seat belt retractors, free of charge. The recall began November 12, 2020. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is W67.
