Subaru Ascent may be missing spot welds near B-pillar

NHTSA campaign 18V508000 · Reported 2018-08-02 · Subaru of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Subaru Ascent

What's wrong, in plain English

Some of these SUVs left the factory missing spot welds on or around the B-pillar — the post between the front and back doors on each side. Those welds are part of what holds the body together and gives it strength. If welds are missing, the vehicle may not hold up as well in a crash, so people inside could be hurt more badly. Dealers will check; if welds are missing, Subaru replaces the whole vehicle.

How serious is it?

This is a serious build defect because it weakens the body in a crash, though Subaru issued no park-it warning, so you may keep driving while you get the vehicle inspected promptly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Ascent is on the list.
2. Contact your Subaru dealer, or Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614 (recall WTU-80), to arrange the inspection.
3. If welds are missing, Subaru replaces your vehicle free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:ROOF AND PILLARS
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2019 Subaru Ascent vehicles. These vehicles may be missing spot welds on, or around, the B-Pillar, compromising vehicle strength.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, the missing spot welds may compromise the vehicle's strength, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Subaru will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the vehicle to check for missing spot welds. If the welds are missing, the vehicle will be replaced, free of charge. The recall began July 31, 2018. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WTU-80.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
