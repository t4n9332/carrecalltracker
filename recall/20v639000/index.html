Buick, Cadillac, Chevy, GMC SUVs: fuel pump can stall engine

NHTSA campaign 20V639000 · Reported 2020-10-15 · General Motors LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

This is serious because the engine can shut off suddenly in traffic, which raises the risk of a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get the free repair done right away.

What's wrong, in plain English

The fuel pump inside the gas tank pushes fuel to the engine. On these vehicles, a small piece of leftover plastic from the factory can be stuck in the pump's tiny nozzle. That bit can block the opening so the engine does not get enough fuel. If that happens, the engine can quit while you are driving, with no warning first. Dealers replace the whole fuel pump unit.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Call your brand's customer service (GMC 1-888-988-7267, Buick 1-866-608-8080, Chevrolet 1-800-222-1020, Cadillac 1-800-458-8006) or your dealer and mention recall N202314760. 3. Have the dealer replace the fuel pump module; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2020 Buick Enclave, Cadillac XT5, Cadillac XT6, Chevrolet Traverse, and GMC Acadia vehicles. The fuel pump's jet nozzle may have a plastic burr remaining from the manufacturing process, possibly causing a blocked nozzle and an insufficient fuel supply to the engine.
Safety risk
Insufficient fuel supply can result in an engine stall without warning, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel pump module. The recall began November 16, 2020. Owners may contact GMC customer service at 1-888-988-7267, Buick Customer service at 1-866-608-8080, Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N202314760.
