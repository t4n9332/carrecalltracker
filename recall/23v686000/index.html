Ford Explorer backup camera can show blank blue screen

NHTSA campaign 23V686000 · Reported 2023-10-13 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Ford Explorer

What's wrong, in plain English

When you shift into reverse, the backup camera may show a blank blue screen instead of the view behind your Explorer. The cause is a wiring mistake made during assembly — a loose connection in the bundle of wires that feeds the camera. If you cannot see what is behind you when backing up, it is easier to hit a person, a pet, or another vehicle. A dealer will check the camera wiring and repair any loose connections for free.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the vehicle is safe to drive, but until it is repaired, turn and look behind you and use your mirrors instead of relying on the camera.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Explorer is covered. 2. Contact your Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 23C31) to schedule the wiring inspection. 3. Get the repair — it is free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:DISPLAY FUNCTION
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023 Explorer vehicles equipped with a standard rearview camera. Due to a manufacturing error in the wire harness, the rearview camera may display a blue image on the SYNC screen when the vehicle is placed in reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview camera that does not properly display an image can reduce the driver's rear view, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the rearview camera wire harness and repair any loose splices, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 2, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23C31.
