Nissan Murano and Infiniti Steering Parts May Crack

NHTSA campaign 21V402000 · Reported 2021-05-27 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A steering knuckle or axle that deforms after an impact can cause loss of steering control or a wheel to separate, both of which increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Nissan Murano and Infiniti Q50, QX50, and QX60 vehicles have a front steering knuckle or rear axle housing that may not be strong enough because of a manufacturing heat-treatment problem. If the part is struck hard, such as when a vehicle hits a curb, it could deform. That could cause the driver to lose steering control, or a wheel could separate from the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact your Nissan dealer (1-800-867-7669) or Infiniti dealer (1-800-662-6200) to schedule an inspection. 3. The repair is free if needed.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR:AXLE:SPINDLE
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020 Nissan Murano, INFINITI QX60, 2021 Nissan GT-R, INFINITI QX50, and Q50 vehicles. The front steering knuckle or rear axle housing may have insufficient strength due to improper heat-treatment.
Safety risk
The steering knuckle or rear axle could deform upon impact, such as from hitting a curb, resulting in a loss of steering control, or possible wheel separation. Both of these conditions can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the front steering knuckle and rear axle housing, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 26, 2021. Nissan owners may contact customer service at 1-800-867-7669. INFINITI owners may contact customer service at 1-800-662-6200.
