Honda CR-V Front Passenger Seat Frame Weld Issue

NHTSA campaign 23V092000 · Reported 2023-02-16 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

medium How serious is it? About this label

A seat back that fails to hold its position in a crash may not properly protect the passenger, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The front passenger seat back frame may not have been welded properly. If the vehicle is in a crash, the seat back could fail to hold its position instead of staying firm and supporting the passenger the way it's supposed to.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Honda dealer to schedule the repair, or call Honda at 1-888-234-2138.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:FRONT ASSEMBLY:RECLINER
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023 CR-V vehicles. The front passenger seat back frame may not have been welded properly, which can cause the seat back position to fail in the event of a crash. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 207, "Seating Systems" and 201, "Occupant Protection in Interior Impact."
Safety risk
A seat back position that fails may not adequately restrain the occupant during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front passenger seat back frame assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 27, 2023. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is 6PK.
