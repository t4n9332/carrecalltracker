Tesla Cybertruck wheel studs can crack and break off

NHTSA campaign 26V255000 · Reported 2026-04-22 · Tesla, Inc. · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Tesla Cybertruck

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2024-2026 Tesla Cybertrucks with 18-inch steel wheels can develop cracks around the holes in the brake rotor where the wheel studs sit. A stud can then break away from the hub. The studs are what hold your wheel on, so if one or more separate you could lose control of the truck. Tesla Service will replace the front and rear brake rotors, hubs, and lug nuts at no charge.

How serious is it?

Losing wheel studs can mean a sudden loss of control and a crash; the notice does not tell owners to park the truck, but this should be repaired as soon as possible.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Cybertruck is covered.
2. Book a service appointment through the Tesla app, or call Tesla at 1-877-798-3752 (recall SB-26-33-003).
3. Have the rotors, hubs, and lug nuts replaced free of charge, and report any clunking or vibration from a wheel right away.

Official notice

Component
WHEELS:LUGS/NUTS/BOLTS/STUDS
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2024-2026 Cybertruck vehicles equipped with 18-inch steel wheels. The brake rotor stud holes may crack and allow the stud to separate from the wheel hub.
Safety risk
Wheel stud separation can cause a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla Service will replace the front and rear brake rotors, hubs, and lug nuts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 20, 2026. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-26-33-003.
