Hyundai Santa Fe Sport Side Airbag Defect

NHTSA campaign 17V801000 · Reported 2017-12-13 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

If the airbag doesn't inflate properly, or the loose part enters the cabin, occupants could be injured in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Hyundai Santa Fe Sport vehicles have side curtain airbags with a part that may come loose when the airbag deploys in a crash. If this part detaches, the curtain airbag may not inflate the way it's supposed to. It's also possible for the loose part to end up inside the vehicle where passengers are sitting.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact a Hyundai dealer or call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-671-3059. 3. Replacement of the side curtain airbags is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2018 Santa Fe Sport vehicles. The side curtain air bags on these vehicles have a component that may detach during deployment of the inflatable curtain air bag.
Safety risk
If the component detaches during deployment, the curtain air bag may not inflate properly during a crash, increasing the risk of injury. Additionally, if the detached component were to enter the vehicle occupant compartment, there could be an increased risk of occupant injury.
Remedy
Hyundai will notify owners, and dealers will replace the driver and passenger side curtain air bags, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin February 9, 2018. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-671-3059. Hyundai's number for this recall is 171.
