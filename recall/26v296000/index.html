Chrysler Pacifica Wheelchair Vans Recalled for Loose Restraints

NHTSA campaign 26V296000 · Reported May 11, 2026 · Braun Corporation

medium How serious is it? About this label

An unsecured wheelchair can move while the vehicle is in transit, which the notice says increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Braun Corporation is recalling certain Chrysler Pacifica vehicles equipped with wheelchair securement systems because the retractors that hold a wheelchair in place may not lock as they should. If the retractors fail to lock, the wheelchair may not be properly secured while the vehicle is being driven. Braun will work with Q'Straint to inspect the retractors and replace them as necessary, free of charge.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
Contact Braun customer service at 1-800-488-0359 or Q'Straint customer service at 1-800-987-9987 to schedule an inspection.
The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V296000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT ADAPTIVE/MOBILITY:WHEELCHAIR RESTRAINTS/SECUREMENT:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Braun Corporation (Braun) is recalling certain 2023-2026 Chrysler Pacifica vehicles. The retractors may not lock, preventing the wheelchair from being properly secured.
Safety risk
An unsecured wheelchair can move during transit, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Braun will work with Q'Straint to inspect and replace the retractors as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 16, 2026. Owners may contact Braun's customer service at 1-800-488-0359 or Q'Straint's customer service at 1-800-987-9987.
