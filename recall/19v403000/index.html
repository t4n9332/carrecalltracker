Mazda CX-9 Wiring Fault Disables Airbag, Signals

NHTSA campaign 19V403000 · Reported May 30, 2019 · Mazda North American Operations

high How serious is it? About this label

If the passenger airbag fails, it will not protect that occupant in a crash. If the turn signals do not work, other drivers may not see you signal, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Mazda says the wiring harness on some CX-9 SUVs can disrupt electrical connections. This may stop the passenger-side front airbag from working, cause the turn signals to fail, or prevent the engine from starting. These are separate possible effects of the same wiring problem, not something you would notice unless one of them happens on your vehicle.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
Contact a Mazda dealer, or call Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500, Option 4, to schedule the repair.
The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V403000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING
Summary
Mazda North American Operations (Mazda) is recalling certain 2018 CX-9 vehicles. The wiring harness may disrupt electrical communications and disable the passenger frontal air bag, turn signals, and/or engine starting system.
Safety risk
The passenger frontal air bag may not deploy, increasing the risk of injury. Inoperable turn signals prevent the driver from signaling to other drivers, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Mazda will notify owners, and dealers will repair the wiring harnesses, free of charge. The recall began July 26, 2019. Owners may contact Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500, Option 4. Mazda's number for this recall is 3419E.
