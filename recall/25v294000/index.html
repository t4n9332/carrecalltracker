Audi Q5 Cylinder Head Cover Screws Can Loosen

NHTSA campaign 25V294000 · Reported 2025-05-07 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

An oil leak can increase the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The screws that hold the cylinder head cover in place on these SUVs may not have been tightened correctly at the factory. Over time, those screws can work themselves loose. Once they loosen, engine oil can start to leak out from under the cover. An oil leak like this raises the chance that a fire could start.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact an Audi dealer, or call 1-800-253-2834.
3. The repair (inspecting and replacing the screws if needed) is free.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2023 Audi Q5
  • 2024 Audi Q5

Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Q5 Quattro and Q5 Sportback Quattro vehicles. The cylinder head cover screws may have been improperly tightened.
Safety risk
The screws can loosen, allowing oil to leak and increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the screws as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 5, 2025. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 15ZK.
