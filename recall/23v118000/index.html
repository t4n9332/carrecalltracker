BMW Software Glitch May Leave Doors Unlocked

NHTSA campaign 23V118000 · Reported Feb 23, 2023 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Doors that don't lock automatically while driving may open in a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling certain vehicles because, during a service visit, a replacement electronic control unit may have been installed with incorrect programming. This can cause the automatic door locking function to stop working as intended, meaning the doors may not lock themselves automatically while the vehicle is being driven, even though they are supposed to.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact a BMW dealer or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 to schedule the repair. 3. The ECU software update is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V118000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:BODY CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2023 330i, 330i xDrive, M340i, M340i xDrive, M3, 330e, 330e xDrive, 430i Convertible, 430i xDrive, M440i, 2022-2023 430i, 430i xDrive, M440i xDrive, i4 eDrive40, and i4 M50 vehicles. During a service visit, a replacement electronic control unit (ECU) may have been installed with incorrect programming, which can cause the automatic door locking (ADL) function to not lock the doors as intended.
Safety risk
Doors that do not lock as intended while the vehicle is in motion may open during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will update the ECU software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 10, 2023. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
