Mazda3 and CX-30 Air Bags May Not Deploy

NHTSA campaign 25V357000 · Reported May 29, 2025 · Mazda North American Operations

high How serious is it? About this label

Air bags that fail to deploy in a crash increase the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Mazda3 and CX-30 vehicles have an air bag problem. The air bag sensor unit may show an error and turn off the air bags if its battery charge gets too low. When the air bags are turned off this way, they will not deploy if the vehicle is in a crash, which increases the risk of injury to people in the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact your Mazda dealer to schedule the free repair.
3. Dealers will reprogram or replace the air bag sensor unit at no cost.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V357000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS: AIR BAG/RESTRAINT CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE
Summary
Mazda North American Operations is recalling certain 2024-2025 Mazda3 and CX30 vehicles. The Sophisticated Air Bag Sensor (SAS) units may set an error code and deactivate the air bags, due to a low battery condition.
Safety risk
Air bags that do not deploy in the event of a crash increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the SAS unit with improved software or replace the unit, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 24, 2025. Mazda's number for this recall is 7525E.
