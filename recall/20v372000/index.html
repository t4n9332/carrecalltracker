VW Golf GTI Certification Label Has Wrong VIN

NHTSA campaign 20V372000 · Reported 2020-06-24 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the VIN doesn't match, owners may not be able to tell whether their vehicle is included in an important safety recall, which could raise the risk of a crash, fire, or injury down the line. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Volkswagen is recalling two Golf GTI vehicles because the vehicle identification number printed on the federal certification label may not match the actual VIN stamped on the vehicle itself. This mismatch means the number owners see on the label may not be the true VIN that identifies their specific car.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. 3. Installation of a correct certification label is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling two 2018 Golf GTI vehicles. The VIN printed on the Federal Certification label may not match the actual VIN on the vehicle. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of 49 CFR Part 567, "Certification."
Safety risk
If the stamped vehicle identification number (VIN) does not match the VIN on the Vehicle Certification Label, owners may be unable to tell if their vehicle is under an important safety recall, possibly exposing them to an increased risk of a crash, fire or injury.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will install a correct certification label, free of charge. This recall began July 9, 2020. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 01E7.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
