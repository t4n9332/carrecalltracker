Volkswagen Golf GTI Has Wrong VIN on Label

NHTSA campaign 20V372000 · Reported 2020-06-24 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Volkswagen Golf

What's wrong, in plain English

Only two 2018 Golf GTIs are involved. The sticker that certifies the car meets federal safety rules shows a vehicle identification number that does not match the number actually stamped on the car. Nothing mechanical is wrong. The problem is that the wrong number can stop you from finding out whether your car is included in a future safety recall, which could leave a real defect unrepaired. The dealer puts on a corrected label for free.

How serious is it?

There is no mechanical danger here and the car is safe to keep driving; the concern is that a mismatched number could keep you from learning about a future recall.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls, using the number stamped on the vehicle. 2. Contact your Volkswagen dealer or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 and mention recall 01E7. 3. Have the corrected certification label installed — it is free.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling two 2018 Golf GTI vehicles. The VIN printed on the Federal Certification label may not match the actual VIN on the vehicle. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of 49 CFR Part 567, "Certification."
Safety risk
If the stamped vehicle identification number (VIN) does not match the VIN on the Vehicle Certification Label, owners may be unable to tell if their vehicle is under an important safety recall, possibly exposing them to an increased risk of a crash, fire or injury.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will install a correct certification label, free of charge. This recall began July 9, 2020. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 01E7.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
