Kia Stinger fuel pump may not supply enough fuel

NHTSA campaign 20V560000 · Reported Sep 16, 2020 · Kia Motors America

high How serious is it? About this label

Too little fuel reaching the engine can cause a loss of drive power, which Kia says increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Kia is recalling certain 2020-2021 Stinger vehicles. A small piece of leftover plastic from the manufacturing process may be left in the jet nozzle of the fuel pump. That can block the nozzle, so the engine does not get enough fuel. Dealers will inspect the fuel pump and replace the jet nozzle housing assembly if needed, free of charge. The recall began October 29, 2020.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Kia dealer, or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542 (recall number SC198), to book an appointment.
3. Have the dealer inspect the fuel pump and replace the nozzle housing if needed — the repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V560000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Stinger vehicles. The fuel pump's jet nozzle may have a plastic burr remaining from the manufacturing process, possibly causing a blocked nozzle and an insufficient fuel supply to the engine.
Safety risk
Insufficient fuel supply to the engine can result in a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Kia will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the fuel pump and replace the jet nozzle housing assembly, if necessary, free of charge. The recall began October 29, 2020. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC198.
