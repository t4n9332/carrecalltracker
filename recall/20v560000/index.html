Kia Stinger fuel pump flaw can cut engine power

NHTSA campaign 20V560000 · Reported 2020-09-16 · Kia Motors America · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Kia Stinger
  • 2021 Kia Stinger

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2020-2021 Kia Stinger cars have a small piece of leftover plastic in the fuel pump's nozzle from when it was built. That bit of plastic can block the nozzle, so the engine doesn't get enough fuel. When that happens, the car can lose power while you're driving. A Kia dealer will check the fuel pump and replace the nozzle housing if needed, free of charge. Kia began notifying owners on October 29, 2020.

How serious is it?

Losing engine power in traffic raises your chance of a crash; the notice does not say to park the car, so you may keep driving while you arrange the free inspection.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Stinger is covered. 2. Contact a Kia dealer about recall SC198, or call Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. 3. Let the dealer inspect the fuel pump and replace the part if needed — at no charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Stinger vehicles. The fuel pump's jet nozzle may have a plastic burr remaining from the manufacturing process, possibly causing a blocked nozzle and an insufficient fuel supply to the engine.
Safety risk
Insufficient fuel supply to the engine can result in a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Kia will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the fuel pump and replace the jet nozzle housing assembly, if necessary, free of charge. The recall began October 29, 2020. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC198.
