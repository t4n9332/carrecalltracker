Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator front seat belts may fail

NHTSA campaign 22V766000 · Reported 2022-10-13 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Jeep Wrangler
  • 2022 Jeep Gladiator

What's wrong, in plain English

The front seat belts in these vehicles have a small part inside the belt reel that yanks the belt tight the instant a crash begins. On some vehicles that part was welded incorrectly at the factory, so the tightener may not work. The belt would still latch and hold you, but it would not pull snug the way it is designed to, so you could move farther forward in a crash. Dealers will replace the front belt reels.

How serious is it?

Serious in a crash because you may not be held as tightly as designed, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside, so the vehicle can be driven while you arrange the repair.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered by recall Z95.
2. Call your Chrysler, Jeep or Ram dealer for an appointment, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. Have the front seat belt retractors replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:PRETENSIONER
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Ram 1500, Jeep Gladiator, and 2021 Jeep Wrangler vehicles. The front seat belt retractors may have an improperly welded micro gas generator, which can result in seat belt pretensioner failure.
Safety risk
A seat belt with pretensioner failure may not properly restrain an occupant during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front seat belt retractors. free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 8, 2022. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is Z95.
