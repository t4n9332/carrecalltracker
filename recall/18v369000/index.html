Volkswagen and Audi brake calipers can reduce braking power

NHTSA campaign 18V369000 · Reported 2018-06-01 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · critical severity

⚠ NHTSA advises not driving this vehicle until it is repaired. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Audi Q5
  • 2018 Audi SQ5
  • 2018 Volkswagen Atlas
  • 2018 Volkswagen Beetle
  • 2018 Volkswagen GTI
  • 2018 Volkswagen Golf
  • 2018 Volkswagen Golf Sportwagen
  • 2018 Volkswagen Passat

What's wrong, in plain English

The brake calipers — the parts that squeeze the brake pads against the wheels — were built with pistons that did not get enough protective coating. Over time that can make the brakes work less well than they should, so the car may not slow down or stop the way you expect. Volkswagen and Audi say owners should not drive these vehicles until the brake system has been checked. Dealers bleed the brake lines to fix it.

How serious is it?

Very serious — weaker brakes can lead to a crash, and the notice tells owners not to drive the vehicle until the brake system has been checked.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Do not drive it until the brakes are checked — call your Volkswagen or Audi dealer and arrange service or a tow (Volkswagen 1-800-893-5298, Audi 1-800-253-2834).
3. Have the dealer bleed the brake calipers; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:DISC:CALIPER
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2018 Volkswagen Atlas, Passat, Beetle, Beetle Convertible, Golf, Golf SportWagen and GTI and Audi Q5 and SQ5 vehicles. The brake caliper pistons on these vehicles may have insufficient coating, potentially reducing the brake performance.
Safety risk
A reduction of braking performance can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Volkswagen and Audi will notify their owners. Audi dealers will bleed the rear brakes. Volkswagen dealers will bleed the front and rear brake calipers. Note: Owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until the brake system has been checked. The recall began June 29, 2018. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 and Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Volkswagen's numbers for this recall are 47N8, 47N9, and 47N6.
