Porsche seat belts may not hold child seats securely

NHTSA campaign 21V608000 · Reported 2021-08-04 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Porsche Panamera
  • 2021 Porsche Panamera GTS
  • 2021 Porsche Panamera Turbo S
  • 2021 Porsche Taycan

What's wrong, in plain English

Your seat belts have a locking feature that clamps the belt tight so a child seat stays firmly in place. On these cars, that lock can switch off too early, letting the belt loosen so the child seat is no longer held securely. Because of this, the vehicle does not meet the federal safety rule covering how people are protected in a crash. A dealer will inspect the seat belts and replace any that are faulty, free of charge.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the car is safe to drive, but a child seat installed with an affected belt may not protect a child properly in a crash.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your Porsche is affected.
2. Call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243 or your dealer and ask about recall AMB6 to have the belts inspected and replaced free.
3. Until it is repaired, double-check that any child seat is held tightly, and ask your dealer how best to secure it.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER:RETRACTOR
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2021 911 Carrera Coupe, 911 Carrera S Coupe, 911 Carrera 4 Coupe, 911 Carrera 4S Coupe, 911 Turbo Coupe, 911 Turbo S Coupe, Panamera, Panamera 4 E-Hybrid, Panamera 4S, Panamera GTS, Panamera Turbo S, Taycan, Taycan 4S, Taycan Turbo, and Taycan Turbo S vehicles. The seat belt automatic locking retractors may deactivate early, which can prevent the child restraint system from securing properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An unsecured child restraint system can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the seat belt assemblies, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 1, 2021. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AMB6.
