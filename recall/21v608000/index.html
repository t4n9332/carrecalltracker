Porsche seat belts may not lock child car seats properly

NHTSA campaign 21V608000 · Reported 2021-08-04 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

The notice says a child restraint that is not properly secured increases the risk of injury during a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Porsche cars, including the Panamera, Taycan and 911, the seat belts have a locking feature used to hold a child seat in place. That lock can switch off too soon, so the belt may not hold the child restraint securely. Because of this, the cars do not meet a federal safety standard covering occupant protection in a crash. Dealers will check the seat belt assemblies and replace them if needed at no cost.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is covered.
2. Contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243 (recall number AMB6) or your dealer to arrange the inspection.
3. The inspection and any seat belt replacement are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER:RETRACTOR
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2021 911 Carrera Coupe, 911 Carrera S Coupe, 911 Carrera 4 Coupe, 911 Carrera 4S Coupe, 911 Turbo Coupe, 911 Turbo S Coupe, Panamera, Panamera 4 E-Hybrid, Panamera 4S, Panamera GTS, Panamera Turbo S, Taycan, Taycan 4S, Taycan Turbo, and Taycan Turbo S vehicles. The seat belt automatic locking retractors may deactivate early, which can prevent the child restraint system from securing properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An unsecured child restraint system can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the seat belt assemblies, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 1, 2021. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AMB6.
