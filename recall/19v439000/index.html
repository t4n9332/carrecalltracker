Ford Transit glass roof hatch may shatter

NHTSA campaign 19V439000 · Reported 2019-06-11 · LA West Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Ford Transit

What's wrong, in plain English

These Ford Transit vans were modified by LA West to add a glass roof hatch. Heat, cold, and the shaking of normal driving can put stress on that tempered glass. Over time the stress can make the glass hatch suddenly break apart. If that happens while people are in the van, pieces of glass can fall down onto passengers below and hurt them. The hatch lid itself is the part that needs to be replaced.

How serious is it?

Falling glass can injure people riding in the van, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so schedule the free repair promptly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is covered by recall 19V439000.
2. Contact LA West customer service at 1-704-498-4940 to arrange service at a dealer.
3. Have the glass hatch lid replaced, which is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:EMERGENCY:ESCAPE/EGRESS/EXIT
Summary
LA West Inc. (LA West) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Ford Transit vehicles modified to be equipped with glass roof hatches. Temperature and vibration stresses may cause the tempered glass roof hatch to shatter.
Safety risk
If the tempered glass roof hatch shatters, glass fragments may fall onto passengers, increasing the risk of an injury.
Remedy
LA West will notify owners, and dealers will replace the hatch lids, free of charge. The recall began July 11, 2019. Owners may contact LA West customer service at 1-704-498-4940.
