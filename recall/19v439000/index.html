Ford Transit Glass Roof Hatch May Shatter

NHTSA campaign 19V439000 · Reported 2019-06-11 · LA West Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

If the glass shatters, fragments may fall onto passengers, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

LA West Inc. is recalling certain 2018 and 2019 Ford Transit vehicles that were modified to include glass roof hatches. Stress caused by temperature changes and vibration may cause the tempered glass used in the roof hatch to shatter. This problem affects the hatch itself, which was added to the vehicle as part of an aftermarket conversion rather than being original factory equipment.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact LA West customer service at 1-704-498-4940.
3. The hatch lid replacement is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:EMERGENCY:ESCAPE/EGRESS/EXIT
Summary
LA West Inc. (LA West) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Ford Transit vehicles modified to be equipped with glass roof hatches. Temperature and vibration stresses may cause the tempered glass roof hatch to shatter.
Safety risk
If the tempered glass roof hatch shatters, glass fragments may fall onto passengers, increasing the risk of an injury.
Remedy
LA West will notify owners, and dealers will replace the hatch lids, free of charge. The recall began July 11, 2019. Owners may contact LA West customer service at 1-704-498-4940.
