Cadillac XT5, XT6 and GMC Acadia windshield problem

NHTSA campaign 23V681000 · Reported Oct 12, 2023 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A windshield that isn't properly attached could detach in a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

This recall affects certain GMC Acadia and Cadillac XT5 and XT6 vehicles. Some windshields may have been installed without the correct sealant needed to bond the glass to the vehicle's body. Without that seal, the windshield may not be properly attached, and it could come loose from the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Cadillac dealer at 1-800-458-8006 or a GMC dealer at 1-800-462-8782.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V681000. Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023 GMC Acadia and 2024 Cadilac XT5 and XT6 vehicles. The windshield may have been assembled without the proper urethane sealant required to bond the windshield to the vehicle body.
Safety risk
A windshield that is not properly bonded may detach during a crash and increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the windshield and apply primer and urethane sealant. If the windshield is damaged, the dealer will replace it. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 14, 2023. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N232419690.
