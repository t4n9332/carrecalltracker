Ford Maverick Instrument Cluster Lights May Fail

NHTSA campaign 25V123000 · Reported Feb 28, 2025 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

Missing critical warning lights or gauge information increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

These vehicles were repaired once already under an earlier recall, but that repair was not performed correctly. Because of this, the instrument panel cluster may fail to properly illuminate warning lights or gauges, such as the speedometer. When critical safety information on the instrument panel does not light up correctly, the driver may be missing information that is important while driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. Dealers will update the instrument panel cluster software free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V123000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Maverick Hybrid vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall number 24V-140. The instrument panel cluster may not properly illuminate warning lights or gauges. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 101, "Control and Displays," and 102, "Transmission shift position sequence, starter interlock, and transmission braking effect" as well as FMVSS numbers 105, 108, and 208.
Safety risk
An instrument panel that does not illuminate critical safety information, such as the speedometer or warning lights, can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the instrument panel cluster software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 9, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25C06.
