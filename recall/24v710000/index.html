Buick Envision Missing Tire Information Label

NHTSA campaign 24V710000 · Reported Sep 25, 2024 · General Motors, LLC

low How serious is it? About this label

Missing tire and loading information may lead to driving with improperly inflated tires or an overloaded vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Buick Envision vehicles because they are missing a label that lists the correct tire size, correct tire pressure, and vehicle loading information. Because this label is missing, that tire and loading information is not available to the owner where it is supposed to be. As a result, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard that covers tire and rim selection information for vehicles.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Envision is included in this recall.
2. Contact a Buick dealer, or call Buick Customer Service at 800-521-7300, to have the label installed.
3. The label installation is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V710000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024 Buick Envision vehicles. The vehicles are missing the label that states the tire size, pressure, and the loading information. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
Missing tire and load rating information may result in driving with improperly inflated tires or an overloaded vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install the label, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 1, 2024. Owners may contact Buick Customer Service at 800-521-7300.
