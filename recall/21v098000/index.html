Volvo VNL trucks may have wheels that bend or break

NHTSA campaign 21V098000 · Reported 2021-02-19 · Volvo Trucks North America

high How serious is it? About this label

Bent or broken wheels raise the risk of a crash, which is serious, but Volvo says the affected trucks were held by the manufacturer, so almost no one should be driving one.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2022 Volvo VNL trucks were built with Alcoa Mirror Polish wheels, part number ULT 391, in the 22.5 x 8.25 size. In some situations these wheels may not be strong enough to carry the weight they are holding, and they can bend or break while the truck is being used. Volvo says these trucks were caught before reaching customers, so no owner letters are being mailed. Dealers will replace the wheels.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your truck is on the list.
2. Contact Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586 and reference recall 21E-007 if your truck is included.
3. Have a Volvo Trucks dealer replace the wheels — the repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
WHEELS
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2022 VNL vehicles equipped with Alcoa Mirror Polish Wheels, part number ULT 391, and wheel size 22.5 x 8.25. Under certain circumstances, these wheels may not be able to support the load, which can cause the wheels to bend or break while in use.
Safety risk
Bent or broken wheels can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The affected vehicles have been contained by the manufacturer, and therefore no owners will be notified. Dealers will replace the wheels, free of charge. The recall began January 29, 2021. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks number for this recall is 21E-007.
