Ford Mustang Steering Wheel May Turn On Its Own

NHTSA campaign 24V493000 · Reported 2024-06-28 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Ford Mustang

What's wrong, in plain English

Your Mustang has a backup sensor that tells the power steering system how hard you are turning the wheel. On these cars it may have been set up wrong, and when that happens the steering wheel can move side to side by itself even though you are holding it steady. That makes the car hard to control. The repair is a software update to the power steering computer, done at the dealer.

How serious is it?

Serious — the steering can move without you doing anything and cause a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but do not put this repair off.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Mustang is covered. 2. Contact a Ford dealer and book the power steering software update, Ford recall number 24S44. 3. The update is free; call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 if the dealer cannot help.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:ELECTRIC POWER ASSIST SYSTEM
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Ford Mustang vehicles. The secondary steering torque sensor may not be calibrated properly, which can cause the steering wheel to turn side to side against the driver's intentions.
Safety risk
A steering wheel that turns unintentionally can cause a loss of vehicle control and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the power steering control module (PSCM) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on October 11, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S44.
