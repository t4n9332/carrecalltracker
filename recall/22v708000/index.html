Cadillac XT5, XT6, GMC Acadia tire label unreadable

NHTSA campaign 22V708000 · Reported 2022-09-22 · General Motors, LLC · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Cadillac XT5
  • 2023 Cadillac XT6
  • 2023 GMC Acadia

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2023 Cadillac XT5, XT6 and GMC Acadia vehicles, the sticker on the post behind the driver's door may be hard to read. That label lists the tire size your vehicle is built for, and federal rules require it to be legible. If a tire shop cannot read it, the wrong size tires could be put on the vehicle, and the wrong tires can make it harder to handle and raise crash risk.

How serious is it?

This is a paperwork and labeling problem rather than a broken part, so the vehicle is safe to keep driving — just make sure you buy the correct tire size until the new label is installed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is covered by recall N222381690.
2. Contact your dealer, or call Cadillac at 1-800-458-8006 or GMC at 1-800-462-8782, to have the certification label replaced free of charge.
3. Until then, confirm your tire size from the owner's manual before buying new tires.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023 Cadillac XT5, XT6, and GMC Acadia vehicles. The certification label located on the driver's side B-pillar contains tire size information that may be illegible. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
Illegible tire size information may result in the wrong tires being installed, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the vehicle certification label, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 12, 2022. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N222381690.
