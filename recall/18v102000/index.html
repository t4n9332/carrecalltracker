Volkswagen Tiguan has wrong driver front airbag installed

NHTSA campaign 18V102000 · Reported 2018-02-09 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Volkswagen says that in a crash the incorrect airbag may not protect the driver as intended, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2017 and 2018 Tiguan Limited vehicles. The front driver airbag installed in these vehicles may not be the type intended for vehicles sold in the United States. Because the airbag may be the wrong one, these vehicles do not meet the federal standard for occupant crash protection. Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will replace the steering wheel and the front driver airbag unit free of charge. This recall began on May 30, 2018.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer to have the steering wheel and driver airbag replaced, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (the recall number is 69V2).
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Tiguan Limited vehicles. The front driver air bag installed on these vehicles may not be intended for use in vehicles sold in the United States. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, the incorrect air bags may not protect the driver as intended, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will replace the steering wheel and front driver air bag module, free of charge. The recall began May 30, 2018. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 69V2.
