Buick Enclave, Chevy Traverse Frame Rail Crack

NHTSA campaign 19V643000 · Reported Sep 6, 2019 · General Motors LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A cracked frame rail can reduce how well the vehicle performs in a crash, which increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Buick Enclave and Chevrolet Traverse vehicles may have a crack in the frame rail on the right side of the engine compartment.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer at 1-800-630-2438 or a Buick dealer at 1-800-521-7300.
3. Dealers will inspect the frame rail and replace the vehicle if necessary, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V643000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:FRAME AND MEMBERS
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2020 Buick Enclave and Chevrolet Traverse vehicles. The right-hand frame rail in the engine compartment may be cracked.
Safety risk
A crack in the frame rail may reduce crash performance, increasing the risk of an injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and, as necessary, replace the vehicle, free of charge. The recall began October 4, 2019. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-630-2438 or Buick customer service at 1-800-521-7300. GM's number for this recall is N192268310.
