Chevy Silverado and GMC Sierra passenger airbag may not inflate

NHTSA campaign 18V868000 · Reported 2018-12-06 · General Motors LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

The truck is safe to drive normally, but anyone riding in the front passenger seat may not be properly protected in a crash, so this should be repaired soon.

What's wrong, in plain English

The passenger-side front airbag may have been damaged while the truck was being built. Because of that damage, the airbag may not inflate the way it should if you are in a crash. Nothing looks or feels different from the driver's seat, so there is no warning sign to watch for. Your dealer will put in a new passenger airbag at no charge. This recall started in December 2018, so the fix is available now.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is affected.
2. Call your dealer, or Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC at 1-800-462-8782, and give them recall number N182198820.
3. Book the airbag module replacement — there is no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL:PASSENGER SIDE:INFLATOR MODULE
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 Crew Cab and GMC Sierra 1500 Crew Cab vehicles. The passenger-side frontal air bag module may have been damaged during assembly, possibly resulting in the air bag not inflating properly in the event of a crash.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, an air bag that does not inflate properly can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace the passenger side air bag module, free of charge. The recall began on December 18, 2018. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 and GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N182198820.
