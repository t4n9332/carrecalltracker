What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2017-2023 Chevrolet Bolt EV vehicles have a problem with the front seat belt pretensioners. After a crash in which the pretensioners deploy, the exhaust they release can ignite carpet fibers near the B-pillar, which can start a fire. This recall also includes vehicles that were already repaired for this same issue under an earlier recall, because that repair was done incorrectly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls, even if your vehicle was already repaired for this issue before.
2. Contact your dealer to schedule the free repair (GM EV Concierge: 1-833-382-4389, or GM customer service: 1-800-222-1020).
3. The repair is free, including for vehicles that need the new fix performed again.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V845000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:ROOF AND PILLARS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2017-2023 Chevrolet Bolt EV vehicles. This recall includes certain vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under NHTSA recall number 22V-930. After a crash with seat belt pretensioner deployment, the pretensioner exhaust may ignite carpet fibers near the B-pillar, causing a fire.
Safety risk
A fire increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect both front seat belt pretensioners and, if necessary, install metal foil at the carpet near the pretensioner exhaust. Certain vehicles will also need a pretensioner cover installed. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 24, 2024. These vehicles were previously recalled for this same issue under recall number 22V-930 and will need to have the new remedy performed. Owners may contact GM EV Concierge at 1-833-EVCHEVY (1-833-382-4389) (TTY 711 / 1-800-833-2438) or Customer Service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N232421970.
