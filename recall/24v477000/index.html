Polestar 2 Rearview Camera May Not Display

NHTSA campaign 24V477000 · Reported 2024-06-26 · Polestar Automotive USA, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

A rearview camera that doesn't display gives the driver less visibility, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Polestar 2 vehicles, a software problem in the infotainment head unit may prevent the rearview camera image from appearing on the screen. Without that camera image on the screen, the driver has less visibility behind the vehicle when backing up, which can make it harder to see what's behind the car. Polestar has released an over-the-air software update that fixes the issue. The update installs automatically and there is no cost to the owner.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Polestar customer service at 1-800-806-2504 if the over-the-air update hasn't installed automatically.
3. The update is free.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:ELECTRICAL:INFOTAINMENT:SOFTWARE
Summary
Polestar Automotive USA, Inc. (Polestar) is recalling certain 2021-2024 Polestar 2 vehicles. The Infotainment Head Unit (IHU) software may prevent the rearview camera image from displaying. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview camera image that does not display decreases the driver's visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Polestar has released an over-the-air IHU software update, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 9, 2024. Owners may contact Polestar customer service at 1-800-806-2504. Polestar's number for this recall is RP1016. (over-the-air software update)
