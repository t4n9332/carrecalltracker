Toyota Sienna Hybrid Second-Row Seat Rails Flawed

NHTSA campaign 25V668000 · Reported Oct 7, 2025 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

If the second-row seat is not properly secured because of the weld problem, it may not protect the occupant as well as it should in a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Toyota is recalling certain Sienna Hybrid vehicles because the rails that hold the second-row seats in place may have been welded incorrectly. A seat rail that is not welded properly may not hold the seat securely in a crash. If the seat cannot properly restrain the person sitting in it during a crash, that person has a higher chance of being injured.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer to schedule the free repair; you can also call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331.
3. The seat rail replacement will be done at no cost to you.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V668000. Official notice

Component
SEATS:MID/REAR ASSEMBLY
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2025 Sienna Hybrid vehicles. The second-row seat rails may be welded improperly.
Safety risk
Improperly welded seat rails can prevent the seat from adequately restraining an occupant during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the second-row seat rails, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed May 15, 2026. This is a phased recall. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 25TB12 and 25TA12.
