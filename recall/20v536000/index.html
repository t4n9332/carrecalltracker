Volvo VNR Brake Pedal Plate May Break

NHTSA campaign 20V536000 · Reported Sep 2, 2020 · Volvo Trucks North America

high How serious is it? About this label

A brake pedal that comes loose would not work, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Volvo Trucks is recalling certain VNR vehicles because the die-cast pedal plate on the brake pedal may break at one of the points where it attaches to the truck. If this happens, the brake pedal could come loose and no longer work properly. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet federal safety standards that apply to air brake systems on trucks.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Volvo Trucks dealer, or call Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-458-1552, to schedule an inspection.
3. Volvo Trucks will inspect and replace the pedal plate if needed, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V536000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, AIR
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2020-2021 VAH, VHD, VNL and VNR vehicles. The die cast pedal plate may break at one of its attachment points, possibly resulting in the brake pedal dislodging. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 121, "Air Brake Systems."
Safety risk
A dislodged brake pedal would be inoperable, increasing a risk of a crash.
Remedy
Volvo Trucks will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and replace the pedal plates if required, free of charge. The recall began October 7, 2020. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-458-1552. Volvo Truck's number for this recall is RVXX2004.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
