Toyota Highlander sold with tires rated too weak

NHTSA campaign 24V452000 · Reported 2024-06-18 · Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Toyota Highlander
  • 2022 Toyota Highlander
  • 2023 Toyota Highlander
  • 2024 Toyota Highlander

What's wrong, in plain English

These Highlanders were fitted with 20-inch add-on tires that are not rated to carry as much weight as the vehicle puts on them. Tires that are not strong enough for the load can be overloaded and fail while you are driving. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule on tire selection. Southeast Toyota will inspect the tires and the tire information sticker on the vehicle and replace them if needed, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed August 16, 2024.

How serious is it?

Serious, because a tire that fails at speed can cause a crash; there is no instruction to stop driving, but have the tires checked soon.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Highlander is covered. 2. Contact your dealer or Southeast Toyota customer service at 1-866-405-4226 about recall SET24A. 3. Have the tires and the information placard inspected and replaced if needed — free.

Official notice

Component
TIRES
Summary
Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC (SET) is recalling certain 2021-2024 Highlander vehicles. The 20-inch accessory tires with an insufficient load rating for the vehicle's Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR) were installed. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
Tires with an insufficient load rating can be overloaded and fail, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
SET will inspect and replace the tires and information placard, as necessary free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 16, 2024. Owners may contact SET's customer service at 1-866-405-4226. SET's number for this recall is SET24A.
