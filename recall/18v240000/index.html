What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2016 to 2018 Nissan Titan and Titan XD trucks, accessories were installed that lowered how much weight the vehicle can safely carry. However, a label showing this reduced load carrying capacity was not installed on the vehicle. Without that label, an owner may not know the vehicle's true weight limit and could end up loading it more than it can safely handle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
3. Nissan will provide a corrected label and installation instructions, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V240000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Nissan Titan, 2016 and 2018 Nissan Titan XD vehicles. Accessories installed on these vehicles reduced the load carrying capacity, however, a Load Carrying Capacity modification label was not installed, possibly allowing the vehicle to be overloaded. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
An overloaded vehicle can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners and provide a new modification label, with installation instructions, free of charge. The recall began on June 4, 2018. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
