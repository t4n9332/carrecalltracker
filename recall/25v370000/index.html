What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Rivian R1T and R1S vehicles may have front seat belts that were not properly bolted in place. The D-ring bolts that anchor one or both front seat belts to the vehicle may not have been installed correctly. If a seat belt is not properly secured, it may not hold an occupant in place the way it's supposed to during a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261 to schedule an inspection.
3. The seat belt anchorage will be secured or repaired free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V370000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:ANCHORAGE
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022-2025 R1T and R1S vehicles. The D-ring bolts for one or both front seat belt anchorage assemblies may not have been properly installed.
Safety risk
An improperly secured seat belt may not restrain an occupant as intended, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will inspect and properly secure the seat belt anchorage assemblies as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 8, 2025. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1681.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
