Rivian R1T and R1S front seat belt anchors may be loose

NHTSA campaign 25V370000 · Reported 2025-06-03 · Rivian Automotive, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Rivian R1S
  • 2022 Rivian R1T
  • 2023 Rivian R1S
  • 2023 Rivian R1T
  • 2024 Rivian R1S
  • 2024 Rivian R1T
  • 2025 Rivian R1S
  • 2025 Rivian R1T

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Rivian trucks and SUVs, the bolts holding the front seat belt anchor — the metal loop the belt passes through near your shoulder — may not have been tightened correctly at the factory. This can affect one or both front seats. If an anchor is not secured properly, the seat belt may not hold you in place the way it should in a crash. Rivian will inspect the anchors on both front seats and tighten or secure them as needed.

How serious is it?

A seat belt that is not anchored properly may not protect you in a crash, so have it checked soon; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261 and mention recall FSAM-1681. 3. Schedule the free inspection and have the anchors secured.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:ANCHORAGE
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022-2025 R1T and R1S vehicles. The D-ring bolts for one or both front seat belt anchorage assemblies may not have been properly installed.
Safety risk
An improperly secured seat belt may not restrain an occupant as intended, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will inspect and properly secure the seat belt anchorage assemblies as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 8, 2025. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1681.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
