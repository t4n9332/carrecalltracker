Chevrolet Silverado Driver Air Bag May Not Deploy

NHTSA campaign 21V495000 · Reported 2021-07-01 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

An air bag that fails to deploy properly increases the risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2021 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 vehicles may have a front driver's side air bag module missing one or more metal tabs. These tabs secure the plastic air bag cover to its housing, so without them the air bag may not deploy properly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer to have the driver's air bag inspected and, if necessary, replaced; you can also call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
General Motors, LLC. (GM) is recalling certain 2021 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 vehicles. The front driver's side air bag module may be missing one or more metal tabs that secure the plastic air bag cover to the housing.
Safety risk
The missing tabs may prevent the air bag from deploying properly, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the driver frontal air bag, and replace it if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 16, 2021. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N212336560.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
