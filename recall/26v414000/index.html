What's wrong, in plain English

Jeep is recalling certain Grand Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer L vehicles because software in the brake system may cause electronic stability control and electronic brake assistance to stop working. The vehicles do not meet the federal safety requirements listed in the notice for these systems. Dealers will update the brake system software free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact a Jeep dealer to arrange the free brake system software update; Chrysler customer service is available at 800-853-1403.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V414000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRONIC STABILITY CONTROL (ESC)
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2026 Jeep Grand Wagoneer and Jeep Grand Wagoneer L vehicles. The brake system control module software may cause a loss of electronic stability control and a loss of electronic brake assist. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 126, "Electronic Stability Control Systems" and 135, "Light Vehicle Brake Systems."
Safety risk
A loss of electronic stability control and/or electronic brake assist increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the brake system control module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed July 30, 2026. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 50D. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning July 9, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
