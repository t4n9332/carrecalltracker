Chevrolet Equinox fuel pump problem can stall engine

NHTSA campaign 23V013000 · Reported 2023-01-19 · General Motors, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Chevrolet Equinox
  • 2022 Chevrolet Equinox

What's wrong, in plain English

The fuel pump unit may not send enough gasoline to the engine at all times. When that happens the engine can stall, meaning it shuts off while you are driving. A stall can happen with no warning, including in traffic, and you lose engine power. The dealer replaces the fuel pump unit at no charge. This recall covers certain Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain models built for those years.

How serious is it?

Serious — an engine that quits in traffic raises the chance of a crash, so have it fixed soon; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Call Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC at 1-800-462-8782, or contact your dealer, and mention recall N222372310.
3. The dealer replaces the fuel pump module, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP:CONTROL/DRIVE MODULE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Chevrolet Equinox and 2022 GMC Terrain vehicles. The fuel pump module may not consistently provide sufficient fuel to the engine, resulting in an engine stall.
Safety risk
An engine stall increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 5, 2023. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N222372310.
