LGE Coachworks Ford vehicles: back-up camera image may reverse

NHTSA campaign 19V398000 · Reported 2019-05-29 · LGE Coachworks Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderate — a flipped camera image can lead you to back into something, so until it's fixed, check your mirrors and surroundings rather than trusting the screen; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

These are commercial-type vehicles built on Ford and Freightliner chassis by LGE Coachworks, fitted with a Voyager back-up camera screen. The screen can reset itself to the factory settings unexpectedly, and when it does, the picture shows up reversed — what is on your left appears on the right. Backing up, you could steer the wrong way to miss something behind you and hit it instead. The dealer replaces the display free.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls for campaign 19V398000.
2. Call ASA customer service at 1-800-384-4400 or LGE Coachworks at 1-800-209-3985 to arrange the repair.
3. Have the dealer replace the back-up camera display, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT
Summary
LGE Coachworks, Inc. (LGE Coachworks) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Ford E-450, F-550, and Freightliner M2-based vehicles equipped with ASA Electronics Voyager back-up camera displays. The displays may unexpectedly revert back to the factory default settings which may cause the camera image to be reversed.
Safety risk
The driver may inadvertently turn the wrong direction to avoid an object behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
ASA will notify LGE Coachworks owners, and dealers will replace the displays, free of charge. The recall began May 9, 2019. Owners may contact ASA customer service at 1-800-384-4400 or LGE Coachworks at 1-800-209-3985.
