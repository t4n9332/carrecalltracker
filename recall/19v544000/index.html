Toyota and Lexus hybrids can lose braking assist

NHTSA campaign 19V544000 · Reported 2019-07-24 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Toyota Prius
  • 2019 Toyota Prius Prime
  • 2019 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid
  • 2020 Toyota Corolla Hybrid
  • 2020 Toyota Prius Prime

What's wrong, in plain English

A pump that helps power the brakes can fail on these vehicles. If it does, the system that keeps the car from sliding or spinning in a turn stops working, and the brakes lose their power assist. The brake pedal will feel much harder to push and the car will take longer to stop. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for stability control. A dealer will check the brake booster pump and replace it if needed.

How serious is it?

Losing brake assist and stability control makes a crash more likely, so treat this as urgent; the notice does not tell owners to park the car, but get it inspected quickly and watch for brake warning lights or a hard pedal.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected. 2. Call your Toyota dealer at 1-888-270-9371 or Lexus at 1-800-255-3987 (recall K1L/K0L, or K2F/KLF for Lexus). 3. The inspection and any pump replacement are free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRONIC STABILITY CONTROL (ESC)
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2019 Lexus LS500, LC500, ES300h, UX250h, LS500h, Toyota Prius, RAV4 HV, 2019-2020 Toyota Prius Prime, and 2020 Corolla HV vehicles. The brake booster pump may fail causing a deactivation of the Vehicle Stability Control system and a loss of braking assist. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 126, "Electronic Stability Control Systems."
Safety risk
Deactivation of the vehicle stability control system or a loss of braking assist increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace the brake booster pump, free of charge. The recall began September 20, 2019. Toyota owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371 and Lexus owners may contact Lexus customer service at 1-800-255-3987. Toyota's numbers for this recall are K1L/K0L. Lexus' numbers for this recall are K2F/KLF.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
