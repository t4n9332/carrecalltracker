BMW X3 And X4 Passenger Air Bag Shutoff

NHTSA campaign 21V521000 · Reported Jul 8, 2021 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A deactivated front passenger air bag will not deploy in a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2018-2019 BMW X3 and 2019 X4 models, the system that senses whether the front passenger seat is occupied may have been put together incorrectly. This can make the system think the seat is empty even when someone is sitting in it, which turns off the front passenger air bag.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a BMW dealer, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. The repair (seat foam replacement) is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V521000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2018-2019 X3 sDrive30i, X3 xDrive30i, X3 M40i and 2019 X4 xDrive30i, and X4 M40i vehicles. The front passenger seat occupant classification system may have been improperly assembled, which could result in the misclassification of the seat as unoccupied, and deactivate the front air bag.
Safety risk
A deactivated front air bag will not deploy in the event of a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the seat foam, which includes the occupant classification system, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on September 3, 2021. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
