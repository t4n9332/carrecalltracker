Mercedes-Benz Instrument Panel Display May Go Blank

NHTSA campaign 26V281000 · Reported May 1, 2026 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

An instrument panel that fails to show safety information such as the speedometer and warning lights increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The system that controls the infotainment screen may reset on its own while the vehicle is being driven. When this happens, the instrument panel display can go blank, meaning the driver can no longer see it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact a dealer, or call Mercedes-Benz USA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. 3. The infotainment control unit software will be updated, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V281000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2024-2026 AMG GT, C-Class, E-Class, SL, CLE, and GLC vehicles. The infotainment control unit may reset while the vehicle is in motion and cause the instrument panel display to go blank.
Safety risk
An instrument panel display that fails to show critical safety information, such as the speedometer and warning lights, increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the infotainment control unit software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 29, 2026. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2026050004. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning May 8, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
