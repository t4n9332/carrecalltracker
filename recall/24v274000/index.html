Toyota Prius Rear Doors May Open Unexpectedly

NHTSA campaign 24V274000 · Reported Apr 17, 2024 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

A door that opens unexpectedly while the vehicle is moving or during a crash can raise the chance of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Toyota Prius and Prius Prime vehicles may let water get into the rear door switch, causing a short circuit. This can make an unlocked rear door open unexpectedly. Dealers will replace both the right and left rear door opener switches.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331.
3. The switch replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V274000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:SWITCHES/KNOBS/BUTTONS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Prius and Prius Prime vehicles. Water may enter the rear door switch and cause a short circuit, allowing an unlocked rear door to open unexpectedly.
Safety risk
A door that unexpectedly opens while the vehicle is moving or during a crash increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace both right and left rear door opener switches, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed August 9, 2024. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 24TB06 and 24TA06.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
