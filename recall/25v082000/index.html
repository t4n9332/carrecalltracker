Volkswagen Tiguan and Audi Q3 rearview camera may not display

NHTSA campaign 25V082000 · Reported 2025-02-12 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

A rearview camera that does not display an image reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Volkswagen is recalling certain Tiguan and Arteon vehicles and certain Audi Q3 vehicles. The control unit that runs the backup camera can hit an error when the vehicle is started, and when that happens the rearview camera image does not show up the way it should. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal government's rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will install updated software for the camera control unit free of charge.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included. 2. Contact your dealer for the software update, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. 3. The update is free; Volkswagen owners will get a second notice once remedy parts are available.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Tiguan, 2023 Arteon, and 2024-2025 Audi Q3 vehicles. An error may occur in the camera control unit at vehicle startup, resulting in the rearview camera image not displaying as intended. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview camera that does not display an image can reduce the driver's rear view, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the camera control unit software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed to Audi owners April 3, 2024, and Volkswagen owners November 18, 2025.Volkswagen owners will receive a second notice once remedy parts become available. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Volkswagen's numbers for this recall are (Volkswagen) 91NY and (Audi) 90AV.
