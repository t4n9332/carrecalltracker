RAM 5500 Minuteman Brake Lights Turn On Wrongly

NHTSA campaign 19V255000 · Reported Mar 28, 2019 · Blanchat Mfg., Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Brake lights turning on when they shouldn't can confuse other drivers and possibly increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

This recall covers a vehicle built on a RAM 5500 truck chassis and fitted with Whelen brake lights that were already recalled once before. Depending on how the brake lights are wired on this vehicle, they may turn on even though the driver has not pressed the brake pedal.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact Blanchat customer service at 1-620-896-7145. 3. The brake lights will be replaced free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V255000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
Blanchat Mfg., Inc. (Blanchat) is recalling one 2018 B-27 Minuteman vehicle built on a RAM 5500 chassis and equipped with Whelen brake lights recalled under 18E-063. Depending on the specific wiring of the vehicle, these brake lights may illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed.
Safety risk
If the brake lights incorrectly illuminate, it can confuse other drivers, possibly increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Blanchat has notified the owner, and will replace the brake lights, free of charge. The recall began March 27, 2019. Owners may contact Blanchat customer service at 1-620-896-7145.
