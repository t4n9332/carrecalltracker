Blanchat Minuteman on RAM 5500 has faulty brake lights

NHTSA campaign 19V255000 · Reported 2019-03-28 · Blanchat Mfg., Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderate: it is safe to keep driving, but the false brake signal can confuse drivers behind you and raise the chance of a rear-end crash until the lights are replaced.

What's wrong, in plain English

Blanchat built a single 2018 B-27 Minuteman vehicle on a RAM 5500 chassis using Whelen brake lights that were recalled under an earlier campaign. Depending on how this vehicle is wired, the brake lights may light up when the brake pedal has not been pressed. Other drivers then think the vehicle is braking when it is not, which makes it hard for them to judge what it will do next. Blanchat has notified the owner and will replace the lights free.

What you should do

1. Enter the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm the recall and its status. 2. Contact Blanchat customer service at 1-620-896-7145 to schedule the brake light replacement. 3. The replacement is free of charge to the owner.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
Blanchat Mfg., Inc. (Blanchat) is recalling one 2018 B-27 Minuteman vehicle built on a RAM 5500 chassis and equipped with Whelen brake lights recalled under 18E-063. Depending on the specific wiring of the vehicle, these brake lights may illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed.
Safety risk
If the brake lights incorrectly illuminate, it can confuse other drivers, possibly increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Blanchat has notified the owner, and will replace the brake lights, free of charge. The recall began March 27, 2019. Owners may contact Blanchat customer service at 1-620-896-7145.
