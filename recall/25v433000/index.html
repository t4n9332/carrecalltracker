Chevrolet Blazer EV parking brake wiring can fail

NHTSA campaign 25V433000 · Reported 2025-06-25 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious, since the parking brake can come on unexpectedly while you drive or fail to hold the parked vehicle; the notice does not order owners to park it, but get the inspection done soon and use the parking gear on hills.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2024-2025 Chevrolet Blazer EVs, the wiring that runs to the rear parking brake can get rubbed through or corroded. Damaged wiring can make the parking brake grab on its own while you are driving, or it can leave the parking brake unable to hold at all, so the vehicle could roll away after you park it. Dealers will check the wiring, reroute it and add protective tape, and replace it if it is already damaged, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your Blazer EV is affected.
2. Contact your Chevrolet dealer, or Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020 (recall N252503010), to schedule the inspection.
3. Have the wiring inspected and repaired or replaced — it is free.

Official notice

Component
PARKING BRAKE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Chevrolet Blazer EV vehicles. The rear parking brake wiring harness may become damaged or corroded, resulting in unintended activation of the parking brake or loss of the parking brake function.
Safety risk
Unintended activation of the parking brake while driving increases the risk of a crash. Loss of the parking brake function while parked can result in a rollaway, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the harness and, if necessary, reroute the harness and install anti-abrasive tape. Any harness that has existing damage will be replaced. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on October 1, 2025. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N252503010.
