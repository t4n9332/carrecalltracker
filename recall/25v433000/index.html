Chevrolet Blazer EV Parking Brake Wiring Problem

NHTSA campaign 25V433000 · Reported Jun 25, 2025 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Unintended parking brake activation while driving increases the risk of a crash, and losing parking brake function while parked increases the risk of the vehicle rolling away and causing a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The wiring that controls the rear parking brake on these vehicles can become damaged or corroded over time. If this happens, the parking brake may switch on by itself while you are driving, even though you didn't touch it. The same wiring problem can also cause the parking brake to stop working altogether once the vehicle is parked, so it may not hold the vehicle in place. Either way, the parking brake isn't functioning the way it's supposed to.

What you should do

1. Check your vehicle's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm it's affected.
2. Contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or your dealer to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V433000. Official notice

Component
PARKING BRAKE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Chevrolet Blazer EV vehicles. The rear parking brake wiring harness may become damaged or corroded, resulting in unintended activation of the parking brake or loss of the parking brake function.
Safety risk
Unintended activation of the parking brake while driving increases the risk of a crash. Loss of the parking brake function while parked can result in a rollaway, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the harness and, if necessary, reroute the harness and install anti-abrasive tape. Any harness that has existing damage will be replaced. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on October 1, 2025. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N252503010.
