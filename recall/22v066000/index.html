Mercedes-Benz Metris brake fluid tank can come loose

NHTSA campaign 22V066000 · Reported 2022-02-08 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious — losing brake fluid means losing braking power and can lead to a crash, so get the van inspected promptly even though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2016-2021 Mercedes-Benz Metris vans, the tank that holds the brake fluid may not have been mounted correctly. If the mounting is wrong, the tank can work loose and detach from where it belongs. Brake fluid can then leak out, and without enough fluid the brakes may not work the way they should when you press the pedal. Dealers will inspect how the tank is mounted and repair it if needed.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your van is affected. 2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer or Daimler Vans USA at 1-877-762-8267 about recall VS2BE42BRE (22V066000). 3. Have the reservoir mounting inspected and repaired — the work is free; letters were mailed May 2, 2022.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FLUID
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2016-2021 Mercedes-Benz Metris vehicles. The brake fluid reservoir may be incorrectly mounted, which can allow it to detach.
Safety risk
A detached brake fluid reservoir can leak brake fluid, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and repair the brake fluid reservoir mounting, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 2, 2022. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS2BE42BRE.
