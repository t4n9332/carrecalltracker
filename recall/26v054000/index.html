Honda and Acura models: driver's seat may be loose

NHTSA campaign 26V054000 · Reported 2026-01-29 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Honda Accord Hybrid
  • 2024 Acura Integra
  • 2024 Honda HR-V
  • 2024 Honda Pilot

What's wrong, in plain English

The fasteners holding the driver's seat cushion frame may not have been tightened enough at the factory, so the driver's seat can be loose. A seat that is not solidly attached may not hold the driver in place in a crash, which means a higher chance of injury. The vehicles also do not meet the federal safety rule for seating systems. Dealers will replace the driver's seat cushion frame at no charge.

How serious is it?

A loose driver's seat may not protect you properly in a crash, so get it fixed soon; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Search your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls under campaign 26V054000 — VINs for this recall became searchable February 5, 2026.
2. Contact your Honda or Acura dealer, or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (recall numbers ONC, KND, BNE and ZNF).
3. Have the dealer replace the driver's seat cushion frame, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023 Honda Accord, Accord Hybrid, and 2024 HR-V, Pilot, and Acura Integra vehicles. The driver's seat cushion frame may not have been tightened properly, which can result in a loose seat. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 207, "Seating Systems."
Safety risk
A loose driver's seat may not adequately restrain the driver during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver's seat cushion frame, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 10, 2026. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's numbers for this recall are ONC, KND, BNE and ZNF. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning February 5, 2026. This recall expands previous NHTSA recall number 24V859.
