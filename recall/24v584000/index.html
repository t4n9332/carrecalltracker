Genesis GV60, GV80 Backup Camera May Go Dark

NHTSA campaign 24V584000 · Reported 2024-08-07 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing the rearview camera image while backing up reduces what the driver can see behind the vehicle, raising the chance of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In certain Genesis GV60 and GV80 vehicles, the rearview camera image may shut off while the vehicle is being driven in reverse. This means that the picture the camera normally shows on the display, which helps the driver see what is behind the vehicle, may disappear while the driver is backing up, leaving the driver without that view of what is behind the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a dealer, or call Genesis customer service at 1-844-340-9741.
3. The rearview camera software update will be done free of charge.

Affected vehicles (3)

  • 2024 Genesis GV60
  • 2025 Genesis GV60
  • 2025 Genesis GV80

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain Genesis 2024-2025 GV60 hybrid and 2025 GV80 vehicles. The rearview camera display may shut off when the vehicle is driven in reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview camera image that does not display decreases the driver's visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the rearview camera software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 11, 2024. Owners may contact Genesis Customer service at 1-844-340-9741. Hyundai's number for this recall is 024G.
