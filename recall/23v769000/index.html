Volvo VN and VHD trucks: passenger bench seat may loosen

NHTSA campaign 23V769000 · Reported 2023-11-15 · Volvo Trucks North America · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Volvo VN
  • 2021 Volvo VN
  • 2024 Volvo VN

What's wrong, in plain English

The bench seat on the passenger side may not be properly attached to the floor. The welded nut holding it down was not made strong enough, and that same area anchors the seat belt. In a crash the attachment points can fail, letting the whole bench seat and the person in it shift out of place. Dealers will fix it by taking out the weld nut and putting in two nuts and a washer instead.

How serious is it?

Serious in a crash — the seat and its belt may not hold someone in place, though the notice does not tell owners to park the truck.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is covered.
2. Contact a Volvo Trucks dealer, or customer service at 1-800-528-6586, and mention recall RVXX2312.
3. Schedule the repair — it is done free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2019-2024 VN and VHD vehicles. The passenger side bench seat may be inadequately attached to the floor. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 207, "Seating Systems" and 210, "Seatbelt Assembly Anchorage."
Safety risk
The attachment points may fail, allowing the bench seat to shift and increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the weld nut with two nuts and a washer, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed February 29, 2024. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks number for this recall is RVXX2312.
