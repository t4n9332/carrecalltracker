NHTSA campaign 20V144000 · Reported Mar 12, 2020 · Volvo Car USA LLC

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Volvo V60 Cross Country and V90 Cross Country vehicles have a software and hardware incompatibility in the Automatic Emergency Braking (AEB) system. Because of this incompatibility, the AEB system may not detect obstacles and engage the brakes as it is designed to do.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact Volvo customer service at 1-800-458-1552; dealers will update the Active Safety Domain Master (ASDM) software.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V144000. Official notice

Component
FORWARD COLLISION AVOIDANCE: AUTOMATIC EMERGENCY BRAKING
Summary
Volvo Cars USA LLC (Volvo Cars) is recalling certain 2019-2020 S60, V60, V60 Cross Country, S90L, V90, V90 Cross Country, XC40, XC60, and XC90 vehicles. Due to a software/hardware incompatibility, the Automatic Emergency Brake (AEB) system may not detect obstacles and engage as intended.
Safety risk
If the automatic emergency braking system does not engage as intended, it can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Volvo will notify owners, and dealers will update the Active Safety Domain Master (ASDM) software, free of charge. The recall began April 9, 2020. Owners may contact Volvo customer service at 1-800-458-1552. Volvo's number for this recall is R29998.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
