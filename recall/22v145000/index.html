Ford F-59 Recalled for Steering Bolt Issue

NHTSA campaign 22V145000 · Reported Mar 11, 2022 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

Separation of the steering shaft from the gear box can cause a loss of steering control, which the notice says increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain F-59 vehicles because the bolt that secures the intermediate steering shaft to the steering gear box may not be properly tightened. If this bolt is not secure, the intermediate steering shaft could separate from the steering gear box. This separation can affect the driver's ability to steer the vehicle. Dealers will inspect the bolt and replace it if necessary, free of charge.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
Contact a Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, to schedule an inspection.
The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V145000. Official notice

Component
STEERING:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2022 F-59 vehicles. The bolt that secures the intermediate steering shaft to the steering gear box may be improperly tightened, which could result in separation of the intermediate steering shaft from the steering gear box.
Safety risk
Separation of the intermediate steering shaft from the steering gear box can result in the loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the intermediate shaft bolt, replacing it if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 1, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S13.
