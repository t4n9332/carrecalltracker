Chrysler Pacifica loose battery connection may cause stalling

NHTSA campaign 19V348000 · Reported 2019-05-09 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Chrysler Pacifica
  • 2019 Chrysler Pacifica

What's wrong, in plain English

On Pacificas with the system that shuts the engine off at stoplights, the battery's ground connection may be loose. A loose connection can cut electrical power on and off, so you may suddenly lose power steering assist, meaning the wheel becomes much harder to turn, or the engine may stall while you are driving. A dealer will inspect the connection, clean the contact surfaces if needed, and put it back together properly and tightly.

How serious is it?

Losing steering assist or stalling in traffic can cause a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get this checked promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is included. 2. Call your Chrysler dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall V53), to schedule the inspection. 3. Have the repair done free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Pacifica vehicles equipped with engine stop/start systems. A loose battery ground connection may result in an intermittent loss of power steering assist and/or a stall.
Safety risk
A loss of power steering assist or a stall can increase the risk of crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the battery ground surfaces, clean the contact surfaces as needed, and properly reassemble the ground connection, free of charge. The recall began May 24, 2019. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US' number for this recall is V53.
