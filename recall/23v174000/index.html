Honda SUVs and vans: side mirror glass can fall off

NHTSA campaign 23V174000 · Reported 2023-03-16 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · medium severity what's this?

  • 2022 Honda Passport

What's wrong, in plain English

The glass in both outside mirrors has a small heating pad glued behind it to clear fog and frost. On these vehicles the glue may not hold properly, so the mirror glass can come loose and fall off, including while you are driving. Without a side mirror you cannot see traffic beside and behind you, which makes changing lanes and merging much riskier. The dealer will replace both the left and right outside mirrors for free.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the vehicle is safe to keep driving, but losing a mirror hurts your view of traffic and raises your chance of a crash.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (recall FE5) or contact your dealer for an appointment.
3. Have both side mirrors replaced — the repair costs you nothing.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:REARVIEW MIRRORS/DEVICES:EXTERIOR
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Pilot and Ridgeline, and 2020-2022 Passport and Odyssey vehicles. The heating pads behind both side-view mirrors may not be bonded properly, allowing the mirror glass to detach. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
Detached mirror glass can reduce driver visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace both left and right side-view mirrors, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed January 17, 2024. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is FE5.
