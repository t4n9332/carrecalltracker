Porsche Macan Fuel Pump Cover May Crack, Leak Fuel

NHTSA campaign 20V077000 · Reported Feb 12, 2020 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

Fuel leaking near a source of ignition increases the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Porsche Macan vehicles, a cover on the fuel pump can rub against a nozzle on the fuel pump's filter flange. This contact may wear cracks into the flange over time, letting fuel leak out. A fuel leak on its own is one thing, but if it happens near something that could spark or ignite it, the leaking fuel raises the chance of a fire starting in the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your vehicle's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Porsche dealer, or call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V077000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2015-2018 Macan vehicles. The fuel pump service cover may contact the flow nozzle on the filter flange of the fuel pump, potentially causing cracks and a fuel leak.
Safety risk
Fuel leakage in the presence of an ignition source may increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Porsche will rework or replace and apply a protective film to the fuel pump flange. in addition, the fuel pump flange will be inspected and replaced if cracks are present. The recall began April 10, 2020. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ALA2.
