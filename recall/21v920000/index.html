Toyota Tundra, Sequoia Power Steering Gear May Leak

NHTSA campaign 21V920000 · Reported 2021-11-23 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

A sudden loss of power steering assist increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The power steering gear in these Toyota Tundra and Sequoia vehicles may have been manufactured incorrectly. This manufacturing problem can cause the steering gear to develop an oil leak. If enough oil leaks out, the power assist that normally makes the steering wheel easier to turn can suddenly stop working, which makes the vehicle much harder to steer while you are driving it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer, or call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331, to have the steering gear inspected.
3. Any necessary repair will be done free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:HYDRAULIC POWER ASSIST SYSTEM
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2008-2022 Sequoia and 2007-2021 Tundra vehicles. The power steering gear assembly may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can result in an oil leak.
Safety risk
An oil leak may cause a sudden loss of power steering assist, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the power steering gear assembly as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on January 21, 2022. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 21TB10 and 21TA10.
