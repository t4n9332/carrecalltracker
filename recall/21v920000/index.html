Toyota Tundra and Sequoia may lose power steering assist

NHTSA campaign 21V920000 · Reported 2021-11-23 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Toyota Sequoia
  • 2018 Toyota Tundra
  • 2019 Toyota Sequoia
  • 2019 Toyota Tundra
  • 2020 Toyota Tundra
  • 2021 Toyota Sequoia
  • 2021 Toyota Tundra
  • 2022 Toyota Sequoia

What's wrong, in plain English

The power steering gear — the part that makes the wheel easy to turn — may have been built incorrectly at the factory, so its oil can leak out. If enough oil escapes, the steering can suddenly get much harder to turn while you are driving, which can catch you off guard in a turn or at speed. A dealer will inspect the part and replace it for free if needed. Owner letters were mailed in January 2022.

How serious is it?

Serious, because losing steering assist without warning makes the truck much harder to control; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get it inspected soon and watch for stiff steering or oil leaks.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck falls under campaign 21V920000.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer, or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 (recall 21TB10/21TA10), to arrange the inspection.
3. Let the dealer replace the steering gear if it fails inspection — inspection and replacement cost you nothing.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:HYDRAULIC POWER ASSIST SYSTEM
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2008-2022 Sequoia and 2007-2021 Tundra vehicles. The power steering gear assembly may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can result in an oil leak.
Safety risk
An oil leak may cause a sudden loss of power steering assist, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the power steering gear assembly as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on January 21, 2022. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 21TB10 and 21TA10.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
