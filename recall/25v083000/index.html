Jeep Grand Cherokee backup camera screen may go blank

NHTSA campaign 25V083000 · Reported 2025-02-13 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Jeep Grand Cherokee L
  • 2022 Jeep Grand Cherokee
  • 2022 Jeep Grand Cherokee L

What's wrong, in plain English

A small electrical part inside the big center touchscreen can get too hot, and when it does the backup camera picture doesn't come up on the screen. Backing up without that view makes it harder to see people or objects behind you, and federal rules require the image. Chrysler says dealers already replaced the display in every affected Jeep, so if yours was covered the work has been done.

How serious is it?

Low at this point — a missing backup image does increase the chance of backing into something, but the notice states all affected vehicles have already been repaired, and there is no warning against driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your Jeep was on the list.
2. If it was, call a Chrysler dealer or customer service at 800-853-1403 (recall 11C) to confirm the display was replaced.
3. Any remaining work under this recall is free.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:ELECTRICAL:INFOTAINMENT:VIDEO (TOUCH)SCREEN/MONITOR/UNIT
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Jeep Grand Cherokee and 2021-2022 Jeep Grand Cherokee L vehicles. The center stack upper display (DCSD) contains a transistor that may overheat and prevent the rearview camera image from displaying. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview image that does not display reduces the driver's visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers replaced the DCSD, free of charge. All affected vehicles have been repaired. Owner notification letters were mailed February 27, 2025. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 11C.
