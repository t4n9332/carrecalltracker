Toyota Camry pump failure can cut brake power assist

NHTSA campaign 21V890000 · Reported 2021-11-17 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Toyota Camry
  • 2019 Toyota Camry

What's wrong, in plain English

These Camrys use a pump that makes the brake pedal easier to push. A small part inside the pump can break, and the pump can then stop working. When that happens you suddenly lose the power help behind the pedal, so braking takes much more effort from your foot and the car needs more room to stop. That can lead to a crash. A dealer will repair or replace the pump for free. Owner letters were mailed in May 2022.

How serious is it?

Serious, because the loss of braking help comes on suddenly and can cause a crash; the notice does not tell owners to park the car, but do not put this repair off.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 21V890000 covers your Camry. 2. Contact your Toyota dealer for an appointment, or call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. 3. The pump repair or replacement is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:POWER ASSIST:VACUUM
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Camry vehicles. The vane cap inside the vacuum pump may break, causing the pump to fail, which could result in a sudden loss of braking assist.
Safety risk
A sudden loss of braking assist can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will repair or replace the vacuum pump, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 16, 2022. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331.
