Toyota Camry Brake Vacuum Pump May Fail

NHTSA campaign 21V890000 · Reported Nov 17, 2021 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Camry vehicles have a part inside the brake vacuum pump that may break. If it breaks, the pump can fail, which may cause a sudden loss of power brake assist.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact your Toyota dealer or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331.
3. Repair or replacement of the vacuum pump is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V890000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:POWER ASSIST:VACUUM
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Camry vehicles. The vane cap inside the vacuum pump may break, causing the pump to fail, which could result in a sudden loss of braking assist.
Safety risk
A sudden loss of braking assist can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will repair or replace the vacuum pump, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 16, 2022. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331.
