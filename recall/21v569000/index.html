Audi E-Tron Brake Rotor and Shaft Separation Risk

NHTSA campaign 21V569000 · Reported Jul 26, 2021 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing braking assistance along with ABS and stability control raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2021 E-Tron Quattro and 2020-2021 Audi E-Tron Sportback Quattro vehicles because a rotor and shaft inside the integrated brake system may have been welded improperly and could separate. If this separation happens, it can reduce the vehicle's braking assistance and cause a loss of the Antilock Brake System and Electronic Stability Control, which raises the risk of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834 or your dealer to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V569000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2021 E-Tron Quattro and 2020-2021 Audi E-Tron Sportback Quattro vehicles. An improperly welded rotor and shaft may separate within the integrated brake system.
Safety risk
Separation of the rotor and shaft may cause a reduction in braking assistance and a loss of the Antilock Brake System (ABS) and Electronic Stability Control (ESC), increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the brake booster, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 14, 2021. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 47R1.
