Polestar 2 May Lose Braking In One Pedal Drive

NHTSA campaign 25V409000 · Reported 2025-07-02 · Polestar Automotive USA, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing brake function increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On affected vehicles, in certain driving situations — such as coasting down a hill while using the "One Pedal Drive" feature — the vehicle's brakes may stop working.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Until your vehicle receives the software update, avoid using "One Pedal Drive."
3. Polestar will install a free over-the-air software update; contact Polestar customer service with questions (1-800-806-2504).

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2021 Polestar Polestar 2
  • 2024 Polestar Polestar 2

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:POWER ASSIST:ELECTRIC:CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE
Summary
Polestar Automotive USA, Inc. (Polestar) is recalling certain 2021 and 2024 Polestar 2 vehicles. In certain driving scenarios while coasting down a hill and using "One Pedal Drive," the vehicle may lose brake function.
Safety risk
A loss of brake function increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to use "One Pedal Drive" until the repair has been completed. Polestar has released an over-the-air (OTA) software update, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 18, 2025. Owners may contact Polestar's customer service at 1-800-806-2504. Polestar's number for this recall is RP1061.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
