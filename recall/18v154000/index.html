BMW X3 rear spoiler may detach while driving

NHTSA campaign 18V154000 · Reported 2018-03-07 · BMW of North America, LLC · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 BMW X3

What's wrong, in plain English

On these BMW X3s, the rear spoiler — the trim piece across the back of the vehicle — may not have been attached correctly at the factory, or may be missing screws altogether. Because of that, it can come loose and fly off while you are driving. A part that size landing on the road becomes a hazard for the drivers behind you, who may swerve or hit it. The dealer inspects how your spoiler is attached and secures it properly if needed.

How serious is it?

A detached spoiler mainly endangers other drivers behind you and can cause a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so the vehicle can be used until the inspection.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your X3 is included. 2. Contact your BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, to schedule the spoiler inspection. 3. Have the spoiler inspected and re-secured as needed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2018 BMW X3 xDrive30i and X3 M40i vehicles. The rear spoiler may not have been properly attached during assembly, or may be missing screws, possibly causing the spoiler to detach while driving.
Safety risk
If the spoiler detaches while driving, it can become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the spoiler, correcting its attachment as necessary, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin May 1, 2018. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
