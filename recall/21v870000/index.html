Ford Explorer, Lincoln Aviator Have Wrong Suspension Parts

NHTSA campaign 21V870000 · Reported Nov 10, 2021 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Explorer and Lincoln Aviator vehicles. Because of a labeling error, some vehicles may have received the wrong components in the rear suspension module. Having incorrect parts installed there can change how the vehicle handles, its ride height, and how well it brakes, compared to how the vehicle was designed to perform.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a dealer, or call Ford Customer Service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. The inspection and replacement of any incorrect parts is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V870000. Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 Explorer and Lincoln Aviator vehicles. Incorrect rear suspension module components may have been installed due to a labeling error.
Safety risk
A rear suspension module with incorrect components may affect vehicle handling, ride height, and braking characteristics, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the rear suspension module, and replace any incorrect parts, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 3, 2021. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21S47.
