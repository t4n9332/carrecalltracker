Audi Q5 and SQ5 front seat belts made wrong

NHTSA campaign 26V266000 · Reported 2026-04-24 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Audi Q5
  • 2025 Audi SQ5

What's wrong, in plain English

The driver and front passenger seat belts in some of these vehicles were built incorrectly at the factory. Because of that, a belt may not hold the person in the seat the way it is supposed to during a crash. There is no way to tell from the outside whether your belts are affected, so the dealer needs to check them. A dealer will inspect both front belts and replace them if needed, free of charge.

How serious is it?

A front seat belt that does not restrain properly leaves you and your passenger more exposed to injury in a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get the belts checked soon.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is part of recall 26V266000 (Audi 69GT).
2. Contact your Audi dealer, or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834, for an appointment.
3. Have the driver and front passenger seat belts inspected and replaced if necessary at no cost.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:PRETENSIONER
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2025 Q5, Q5 Sportback, SQ5, and SQ5 Sportback vehicles. The driver and front passenger seat belts may have been manufactured incorrectly, and fail to properly restrain occupants during a crash.
Safety risk
Improperly restrained occupants have an increased risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver and front passenger seat belts as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 29, 2026. Owners may contact Audi's customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 69GT.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
