Ford Explorer and Lincoln Aviator Trailer Lights May Fail

NHTSA campaign 25V543000 · Reported Aug 22, 2025 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

Trailer tail lights that don't work make it harder for other drivers to see the trailer, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Ford Explorer and Lincoln Aviator vehicles have a body control module that was not manufactured correctly. When you tow a trailer, this problem can cause the trailer's tail lights to fail to turn on the way they should. Because these lights are not working properly, the vehicle does not meet federal safety standards for lights and reflective equipment. This mainly affects visibility for the trailer being towed behind the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. The repair - replacing or checking the body control module - is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V543000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TAIL LIGHTS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2025 Explorer and Aviator vehicles. When towing a trailer, the tail lights on the trailer may fail to illuminate due to an improperly manufactured body control module. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, " Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Trailer tail lights that do not illuminate as intended can reduce visibility to other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the body control module C on vehicles with less than 9,000 miles. For vehicles over 9,000 miles, dealers will perform a functional check on the body control module C and replace it as necessary. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Aviator 2025 owner notification letters were mailed October 13, 2025. Explorer 2025 owner letters were mailed November 25, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25C42.
