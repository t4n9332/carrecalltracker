Toyota Camry Engine Pistons May Cause Stalling

NHTSA campaign 18V200000 · Reported Mar 28, 2018 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

An engine that stalls while driving can increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2018 Toyota Camry cars with a 2.5-liter four-cylinder engine may have pistons inside the engine that are bigger than they're supposed to be. This can cause the engine to stall unexpectedly while driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact a Toyota dealer or Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. 3. If your engine has an affected piston, the engine assembly will be replaced free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V200000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018 Toyota Camry vehicles equipped with a 2.5L, four-cylinder engine. The pistons on these vehicles may be larger than specified, possibly causing the engine to stall.
Safety risk
An engine stall can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the production date codes of the pistons and, if an engine contains a piston from the affected production period, the engine assembly will be replaced, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin May 25, 2018. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. Toyota's number for this recall is J1M/J0M.
