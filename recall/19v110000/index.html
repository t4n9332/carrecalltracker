Volkswagen Jetta may have wrong driver airbag installed

NHTSA campaign 19V110000 · Reported 2019-02-20 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious, since the driver's airbag may not protect you properly in a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but schedule the fix quickly.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2019 Volkswagen Jettas had the wrong driver's airbag put in during a service or repair visit at the shop. The airbag in the steering wheel is not the correct part for the car, so in a crash bad enough to set it off, it may not work the way it is supposed to. Only a check of your VIN will tell you whether your car is one of them. A dealer will put in the correct airbag at no charge.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Jetta is affected. 2. Contact your Volkswagen dealer or customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall 69Y7). 3. Have the driver's frontal airbag replaced with the correct part — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019 Jetta vehicles. An incorrect driver frontal airbag may have been installed during a service/repair visit.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash necessitating air bag deployment, the wrong air bag may not work correctly, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will replace the driver frontal air bag with the correct part, free of charge. The recall began April 19, 2019. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 69Y7.
