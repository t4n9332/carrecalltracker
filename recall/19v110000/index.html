Volkswagen Jetta May Have Wrong Airbag Installed

NHTSA campaign 19V110000 · Reported Feb 20, 2019 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the airbag needs to deploy in a crash, the wrong airbag may not work correctly, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Jetta vehicles, an incorrect driver frontal airbag may have been installed during a previous service or repair visit. Dealers will replace the driver frontal airbag with the correct part, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298.
3. The airbag replacement is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V110000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019 Jetta vehicles. An incorrect driver frontal airbag may have been installed during a service/repair visit.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash necessitating air bag deployment, the wrong air bag may not work correctly, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will replace the driver frontal air bag with the correct part, free of charge. The recall began April 19, 2019. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 69Y7.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
