Audi Q3: brake pedal plate can bend or fall off

NHTSA campaign 20V786000 · Reported 2020-12-16 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Audi Q3

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2020 Audi Q3 vehicles, the flat plate on the face of the brake pedal — the part your foot actually presses — can bend or come off when you push hard on the brakes during a sudden stop. If that happens, there is less surface for your foot to press against, which can make it harder to brake the way you expect. Audi dealers will check the weld holding the plate to the pedal and replace the whole brake pedal if needed.

How serious is it?

This is serious because it affects braking in an emergency stop, and while the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, you should get the pedal inspected promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your car is affected. 2. Call your Audi dealer, or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834 (recall 46i7), to schedule the brake pedal inspection. 3. Have the pedal inspected and replaced if necessary — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:PEDALS AND LINKAGES
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2020 Audi Q3 vehicles. The brake pedal plate can bend or detach under the pressure applied during sudden stopping.
Safety risk
A bent or detached brake pedal plate decreases the contact area for the driver's foot and increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the weld between the brake pedal and the brake pedal plate and, as necessary, replace the entire brake pedal, free of charge. The recall began January 12, 2021. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 46i7.
