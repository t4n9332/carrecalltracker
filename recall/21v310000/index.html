Jeep Cherokee transmission hose can leak and cause fire

NHTSA campaign 21V310000 · Reported 2021-05-04 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Jeep Cherokee

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2021 Jeep Cherokees, the hose that carries transmission fluid to the cooler was not made correctly at the factory. The rubber was not cured the right way, so the hose can leak. A transmission fluid leak raises the risk of a fire. A dealer will inspect the hose and replace it at no charge. Because fire is involved, this one should not wait; get it looked at as soon as you can.

How serious is it?

Potentially very serious: the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside, but a leak that can lead to a fire means you should get the inspection done right away.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Jeep is affected. 2. Call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 or your dealer and reference recall Y18. 3. Have the hose inspected and replaced free of charge as soon as an appointment is available.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:COOLER/RADIATOR:HOSES AND FITTINGS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2021 Jeep Cherokee vehicles. The transmission oil cooler hose may have been incorrectly cured, allowing transmission fluid to leak.
Safety risk
A transmission fluid leak increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the hose, free of charge. Owners notification letters were mailed May 18, 2021. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is Y18.
