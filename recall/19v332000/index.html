Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster foldaway seats may unlock

NHTSA campaign 19V332000 · Reported 2019-03-01 · Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles

high How serious is it? About this label

A seat back that moves in a crash raises the risk of injury to whoever is sitting there, but the notice does not say to stop driving the van.

What's wrong, in plain English

These Ram ProMaster and Ford Transit vans were modified to add Freedman GO-ES Foldaway Seats. When you flip the seat back up from its folded, stowed position, it may not lock firmly in place. Under a hard load — a sudden stop or a crash — the seat back can move instead of holding. Anyone sitting in that seat is less protected if it shifts. Freedman dealers have already inspected and repaired the foldaway seats at no cost to owners.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your van is covered and whether the repair was done. 2. Contact Freedman customer service at 1-800-443-4540 or Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles at 1-574-293-9191. 3. Have the foldaway seats inspected and repaired — it is free.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles (Prime-Time) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Ram ProMaster and Ford Transit vehicles modified to be equipped with Freedman GO-ES Foldaway Seats. When the seat back cushion is rotated from the stowed position to the upright position, the seat cushion may not remain locked into place when under load, such as in a crash or a sudden stop.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, if the seat back moves, the seat occupant has an increased risk of injury.
Remedy
Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles has notified owners, and Freedman dealers have inspected and repaired the foldaway seats, free of charge. The recall began November 16, 2018. Owners may contact Freedman customer service at 1-800-443-4540 or Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles at 1-574-293-9191.
