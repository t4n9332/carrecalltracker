Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster Foldaway Seat Lock Failure

NHTSA campaign 19V332000 · Reported Mar 1, 2019 · Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles

medium How serious is it? About this label

A seat back that comes unlocked during a crash or sudden stop increases the risk of injury to the occupant. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Ram ProMaster and Ford Transit vehicles fitted with Freedman GO-ES foldaway seats, the seat back cushion may not lock securely in place when it is raised from its folded position to the upright position. If the vehicle is in a crash or stops suddenly, the seat back may not stay locked under that load. A seat back that moves increases the risk of injury to the person sitting in it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Freedman dealer at 1-800-443-4540, or Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles at 1-574-293-9191, to schedule service.
3. The foldaway seats will be inspected and repaired at no cost to you.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V332000. Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles (Prime-Time) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Ram ProMaster and Ford Transit vehicles modified to be equipped with Freedman GO-ES Foldaway Seats. When the seat back cushion is rotated from the stowed position to the upright position, the seat cushion may not remain locked into place when under load, such as in a crash or a sudden stop.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, if the seat back moves, the seat occupant has an increased risk of injury.
Remedy
Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles has notified owners, and Freedman dealers have inspected and repaired the foldaway seats, free of charge. The recall began November 16, 2018. Owners may contact Freedman customer service at 1-800-443-4540 or Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles at 1-574-293-9191.
