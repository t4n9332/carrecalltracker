Hyundai Palisade tow hitch wiring can start a fire

NHTSA campaign 22V633000 · Reported 2022-08-18 · Hyundai Motor America · critical severity

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Hyundai Palisade
  • 2021 Hyundai Palisade
  • 2022 Hyundai Palisade

What's wrong, in plain English

This covers Palisades that came with tow hitch wiring from the factory, or had a kit added at a Hyundai dealer. Dirt and water can collect on the small circuit board inside that wiring, which lets the electricity short out. A short like that can start a fire, either while you are driving or while the vehicle is parked and shut off. The dealer installs a new fuse and a wire extension kit if needed, at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious — a fire can break out while parked or driving and could injure someone, so Hyundai says to park outside and away from buildings until the free fix is done, but does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Palisade is affected.
2. Park outside and away from your house, garage, or other structures until the repair is complete.
3. Contact your Hyundai dealer or call 1-855-371-9460 (recall 235) to schedule the free repair.

Official notice

Component
TRAILER HITCHES
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling 2020-2022 Palisade vehicles potentially equipped with a tow hitch harness installed as original equipment, or purchased as an accessory through a Hyundai dealership. Debris and moisture accumulation on the tow hitch harness module printed circuit board (PCB) may cause an electrical short, which can result in a fire.
Safety risk
A fire while parked or driving can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. Dealers will install a new fuse and wire extension kit, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 18, 2023. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 235.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
