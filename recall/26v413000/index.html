Jeep Grand Cherokee and Wagoneer gear display may not appear

NHTSA campaign 26V413000 · Reported 2026-06-30 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

Without the gear display, the vehicle may move when it is not meant to or roll away, which raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Grand Cherokee, Grand Cherokee L, Wagoneer and Wagoneer L vehicles. A software error can keep the display that shows which gear the transmission is in from appearing at all. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard that covers the shift lever sequence, the starter interlock and the transmission braking effect. Dealers will update the software in the shifter module.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Jeep dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403 (recall number 30D), to have the shifter module software updated.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:GEAR POSITION INDICATION (PRNDL)
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Jeep Grand Cherokee, 2023 Jeep Wagoneer, 2024 Jeep Grand Cherokee L, and Jeep Wagoneer L vehicles. A software error may cause the transmission gear selection indicator not to display. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 102, "Transmission shift lever sequence, starter interlock, and transmission braking effect."
Safety risk
A missing gear selection indicator may lead to unintended vehicle movement or a vehicle rollaway, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the shifter module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 20, 2026. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 30D. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning July 9, 2026.
