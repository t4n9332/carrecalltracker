Tesla Software Bug Reduces Windshield Defrosting Power

NHTSA campaign 22V050000 · Reported 2022-02-02 · Tesla, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Reduced windshield defrosting can limit the driver's visibility, which raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Tesla is recalling certain Model 3, Model S, and Model Y vehicles because of a software error. The error can cause a valve inside the heat pump to open when it shouldn't, which traps refrigerant inside the evaporator. When this happens, the vehicle's ability to defrost or defog the windshield is reduced. Vehicles with the problem no longer meet the federal safety standard for windshield defrosting and defogging systems.

What you should do

1) Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2) Tesla will send the fix as an over-the-air software update; you can also contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. 3) The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Model 3, Model S, Model X, and 2020-2022 Model Y vehicles. A software error may cause a valve in the heat pump to open unintentionally and trap the refrigerant inside the evaporator, resulting in decreased defrosting performance. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 103, "Windshield Defrosting and Defogging Systems."
Safety risk
Decreased defrosting performance may reduce the driver's visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla will perform an over-the-air (OTA) software update, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 1, 2022. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-22-18-002. (over-the-air software update)
