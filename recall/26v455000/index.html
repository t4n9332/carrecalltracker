Nissan Armada and Infiniti QX80: wrong weight rating label

NHTSA campaign 26V455000 · Reported 2026-07-15 · Nissan North America, Inc.

low How serious is it? About this label

This is a paperwork and labeling problem rather than a broken part, and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but going by the wrong weight limit could overload the vehicle and lead to a crash.

What's wrong, in plain English

The sticker on the door frame lists how much weight each axle can safely carry. On these vehicles with 22-inch wheels, that number is printed too high. If you load up to what the label says, you could actually be carrying more than the tires and wheels are built to handle. Nissan will install a corrected label free of charge and is also mailing replacement labels to owners. Nothing mechanical is wrong with the vehicle itself.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is covered.
2. Watch for the replacement label Nissan is mailing, or ask your dealer about recall R26B1 / P6A13-4 to have a new label installed free.
3. Until the label is corrected, avoid loading the vehicle to its posted limit; questions go to Nissan at 1-800-867-7669.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020-2026 Nissan Armada, 2011-2013 Infiniti QX56, and 2014-2027 QX80 vehicles equipped with 22" wheels. The Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR) listed on the certification label is incorrect, which can allow the vehicle to be overloaded. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
An overloaded vehicle can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install new labels, free of charge. Additionally, Nissan will mail replacement labels to owners. Interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk were mailed September 2, 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available, anticipated Fall 2026. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's numbers for this recall are R26B1 and P6A13/4. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on July 16, 2026.
