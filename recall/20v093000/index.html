Audi Q7 side curtain airbags may not inflate properly

NHTSA campaign 20V093000 · Reported 2020-02-19 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Audi Q7

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2019 Audi Q7 SUVs have a problem with the side curtain airbags — the airbags that drop down from above the side windows to protect people's heads in a crash. In a crash, these airbags may not fill with air the way they are supposed to. That means the protection you expect in a side impact or rollover may not be there. Audi dealers will replace the side curtain airbags on affected vehicles.

How serious is it?

This is serious because an airbag that does not inflate correctly leaves you with less protection and a higher risk of injury in a crash, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your 2019 Q7 is affected.
2. Contact your Audi dealer to schedule the repair, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 and reference recall 69AJ.
3. Have the dealer replace the side curtain airbags — the repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019 Audi Q7 vehicles. In the event of a crash, the side curtain air bags may not properly inflate.
Safety risk
An air bag that does not properly inflate in the event of a crash can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will replace the side curtain air bags, free of charge. The recall began March 19, 2020. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 69AJ.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
