Audi Q7 Side Curtain Airbags May Not Inflate

NHTSA campaign 20V093000 · Reported Feb 19, 2020 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

An airbag that doesn't properly inflate in a crash can increase the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2019 Audi Q7 vehicles, the side curtain airbags may not inflate properly if the vehicle is in a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact an Audi/Volkswagen dealer, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall number 69AJ).
3. The airbag replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V093000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019 Audi Q7 vehicles. In the event of a crash, the side curtain air bags may not properly inflate.
Safety risk
An air bag that does not properly inflate in the event of a crash can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will replace the side curtain air bags, free of charge. The recall began March 19, 2020. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 69AJ.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
