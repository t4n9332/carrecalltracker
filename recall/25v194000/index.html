BMW M 1000 XR and R steering may stiffen suddenly

NHTSA campaign 25V194000 · Reported 2025-03-27 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 BMW M 1000 XR

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW found that some 2024-2025 M 1000 XR and M 1000 R motorcycles may have a loose pin inside the steering damper — the part that helps keep the handlebars steady. If that pin shifts, the steering can suddenly take much more effort to turn than the rider expects. On a motorcycle, an unexpected change like that can mean losing control of the bike. Dealers replace the steering damper at no charge.

How serious is it?

An unexpected jump in steering effort can cause a loss of control, so this is a serious defect; the notice does not tell riders to stop riding, but get it repaired right away.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm whether your motorcycle is included.
2. Contact your BMW Motorrad dealer to schedule the steering damper replacement under campaign 25V194000.
3. The replacement is free. BMW customer service: 1-800-525-7417.

Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2024-2025 M 1000 XR, and M 1000 R motorcycles. The steering damper may have a loose valve pin within the housing, which can cause an unexpected increase in steering effort.
Safety risk
An unexpected increase in steering effort can result in a loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the steering damper, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 5, 2025. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
