Ford Explorer Police and Lincoln Aviator have contaminated washer fluid

NHTSA campaign 21V014000 · Reported 2021-01-15 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Ford Explorer Police Intercept
  • 2020 Lincoln Aviator

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain 2020 Explorer Police Interceptor Utility and 2020 Lincoln Aviator vehicles. They left the factory with brake fluid mixed into the windshield washer fluid. That mixture does not clean glass the way washer fluid should, so spraying it can leave the windshield smeared and hard to see through. It can also damage paint and parts on the outside of the vehicle. Dealers will flush the system and replace several washer parts and the wiper blades.

How serious is it?

You can keep driving, but smeared glass in rain, snow or glare makes it harder to see the road and raises your chance of a crash, so avoid using the washers until the fix is done.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your vehicle is included.
2. Contact your Ford or Lincoln dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and mention recall 21C01.
3. Have the dealer flush the washer system and replace the bottle neck, pump, fluid level sensor and wiper blades, plus any affected paint or exterior parts — all free.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD WIPER/WASHER
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020 Ford Explorer Police Interceptor Utility and 2020 Lincoln Aviator vehicles. The affected vehicles contain windshield washer fluid that was contaminated with brake fluid. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 104, "Windshield Wiping and Washing Systems."
Safety risk
A washer system contaminated with brake fluid can reduce the driver's ability to clear windshield, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will flush out the washer system, replacing the washer bottle neck, washer pump assembly, fluid level sensor and the front and rear wiper blades. Dealers will also inspect and repair the vehicle's paint and exterior components if affected, free of charge. The recall began March 2, 2021. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21C01.
