Genesis G80, GV70 and GV80 fuel pump may fail

NHTSA campaign 23V630000 · Reported 2023-09-11 · Hyundai Motor America · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Genesis G80
  • 2021 Genesis GV80
  • 2022 Genesis G80
  • 2022 Genesis GV70
  • 2022 Genesis GV80

What's wrong, in plain English

The fuel pump on these Genesis models can fail. The pump is what sends gasoline from the tank to the engine, so if it stops working the vehicle can lose drive power and you may not be able to keep up with traffic or get out of the way. A Genesis dealer will replace the fuel pump for free. Hyundai mailed owner letters in November 2023, so parts and the repair are available now. Call the dealer even if you never got a letter.

How serious is it?

Losing engine power in traffic can lead to a crash, so this is a serious one; the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but schedule the free repair soon.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle falls under recall 016G. 2. Book an appointment with a Genesis dealer for the free fuel pump replacement. 3. Call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 if you have questions or trouble scheduling.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Genesis G80, GV80, and 2022 Genesis GV70 vehicles. The fuel pump may fail, which can result in a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 8, 2023. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 016G.
