Genesis Fuel Pump Failure May Cut Power

NHTSA campaign 23V630000 · Reported Sep 11, 2023 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Genesis G80, GV70, and GV80 vehicles, the fuel pump may fail. If it does, the vehicle can lose drive power while being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a dealer, or call Hyundai/Genesis customer service at 1-855-371-9460.
3. Fuel pump replacement is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V630000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Genesis G80, GV80, and 2022 Genesis GV70 vehicles. The fuel pump may fail, which can result in a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 8, 2023. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 016G.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
