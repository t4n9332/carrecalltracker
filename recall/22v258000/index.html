Hyundai Nexo leak sensors may miss hydrogen leaks

NHTSA campaign 22V258000 · Reported 2022-04-14 · Hyundai Motor America · critical severity

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Hyundai Nexo
  • 2020 Hyundai Nexo
  • 2021 Hyundai Nexo

What's wrong, in plain English

The Nexo runs on hydrogen gas instead of gasoline. It has sensors whose whole job is to notice if that gas starts leaking out of the system that carries it to the engine. On these vehicles, those sensors may not catch a leak. If hydrogen escapes without being detected, it raises the chance of a fire, either while you are driving or while the vehicle is parked. The dealer replaces the leak sensors with new ones at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious — an undetected leak can lead to a fire while parked or driving, so Hyundai says to park outside and away from buildings until it is fixed, though it does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your Nexo is included.
2. Park outside and away from your house, garage, or other structures until the sensors are replaced.
3. Contact your Hyundai dealer or call 1-855-371-9460 (recall 225) to book the free repair.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, OTHER:DELIVERY
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2019-2021 Nexo vehicles. The hydrogen gas detection sensors may fail to detect leaks in the hydrogen gas delivery system.
Safety risk
A hydrogen gas leak can increase the risk of a fire while parked or driving.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park their vehicles outside and away from structures until the recall remedy is completed. Dealers will replace the hydrogen gas detection sensors, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 12, 2022. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 225.
