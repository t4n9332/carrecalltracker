Hyundai Nexo Hydrogen Leak Detection May Fail

NHTSA campaign 22V258000 · Reported 2022-04-14 · Hyundai Motor America

critical How serious is it? About this label

Hyundai is telling owners to park outside and away from buildings until the repair is complete, because an undetected gas leak can lead to a fire.

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

What's wrong, in plain English

The sensors that are supposed to detect hydrogen gas leaks in the fuel system may fail to do their job. If a leak isn't detected, it can increase the risk of a fire, whether the car is parked or being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Park outside and away from structures until repaired, and contact a Hyundai dealer (1-855-371-9460) for the fix. 3. The repair is free.

Affected vehicles (3)

  • 2020 Hyundai Nexo
  • 2021 Hyundai Nexo

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, OTHER:DELIVERY
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2019-2021 Nexo vehicles. The hydrogen gas detection sensors may fail to detect leaks in the hydrogen gas delivery system.
Safety risk
A hydrogen gas leak can increase the risk of a fire while parked or driving.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park their vehicles outside and away from structures until the recall remedy is completed. Dealers will replace the hydrogen gas detection sensors, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 12, 2022. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 225.
