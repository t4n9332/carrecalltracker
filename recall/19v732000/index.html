BMW Z4 engine balance shaft may come loose

NHTSA campaign 19V732000 · Reported 2019-10-15 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 BMW Z4

What's wrong, in plain English

Inside your engine there are balance shafts that keep it running smoothly. On some of these cars, the small roller bearings that hold those shafts were not installed correctly at the factory. Over time a shaft can work loose. If that happens, the engine can be badly damaged, which could leave you without power while you are driving and lead to a crash. BMW dealers will replace the whole engine at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Serious — a damaged engine could fail while you are driving and cause a crash, and although the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, you should get this scheduled right away.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included in this recall.
2. Call your BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, to book the engine replacement.
3. Have the work done at no charge — parts and labor are free under the recall.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2019-2020 330i, 330i xDrive, and 2019 Z4 vehicles. The needle roller bearings of the counterbalance shafts may have been improperly installed, potentially causing the counterbalance shaft to loosen.
Safety risk
If the counterbalance shaft loosens, severe engine damage could occur, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the engine, free of charge. The recall began December 13, 2019. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
