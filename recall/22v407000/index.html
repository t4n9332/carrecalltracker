Ram 3500 backup camera may not show an image

NHTSA campaign 22V407000 · Reported Jun 9, 2022 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

A rear camera that does not show a picture cuts down what the driver can see behind the truck, which the notice says increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain 2022 Ram 1500 and 2500 pickups and 3500 cab chassis trucks. The software that runs the radio may keep the rear view camera picture from showing up on the screen. Because the rear view image does not appear, these trucks do not meet the federal rule for rear visibility. Dealers will reload the radio software at no cost to the owner.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is included.
2. Contact a Ram dealer to schedule the software update, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall number Z47).
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V407000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:DISPLAY FUNCTION
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Ram 1500, 2500 pickup trucks, and 3500 Cab Chassis vehicles with GVWR less than 10,000 lbs. The radio software may prevent the rearview image from displaying. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview camera that does not display an image reduces the driver's rear view, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the radio software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 5, 2022. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLS's number for this recall is Z47.
