Ram pickup backup camera image may not appear

NHTSA campaign 22V407000 · Reported 2022-06-09 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 RAM 3500

What's wrong, in plain English

A software glitch in the radio can stop the backup camera picture from showing on the screen when you shift into reverse. This affects certain 2022 Ram 1500 and 2500 pickups and 3500 cab chassis trucks. Federal rules require these trucks to display that view, so they do not meet the standard. Without the picture you lose the rear view the camera is supposed to give you while backing up. The dealer reloads the radio software, which takes care of it.

How serious is it?

The truck is safe to drive and nothing else is affected, but backing up without the camera view increases your chance of hitting something or someone behind you, so back up slowly and use your mirrors and a look over your shoulder until it is fixed.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is part of campaign 22V407000.
2. Contact your Ram dealer, or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and reference recall Z47 to schedule the software update.
3. The reprogramming is free and usually quick; until then, check behind the truck before reversing.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:DISPLAY FUNCTION
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Ram 1500, 2500 pickup trucks, and 3500 Cab Chassis vehicles with GVWR less than 10,000 lbs. The radio software may prevent the rearview image from displaying. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview camera that does not display an image reduces the driver's rear view, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the radio software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 5, 2022. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLS's number for this recall is Z47.
