Mercedes G-Class shows wrong stability system warning light

NHTSA campaign 25V254000 · Reported 2025-04-17 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderately serious — you might not be warned that a safety system has failed, which raises crash risk, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2025 Mercedes-Benz G 550, G 580 and AMG G 63 SUVs have a software problem in the system that helps keep the vehicle from skidding or sliding. If that system stops working, the dashboard may show the wrong warning light, so you would not know the system has failed. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet federal rules for stability control and for dashboard displays. A dealer will reload the correct software into the control unit for free.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your vehicle is included. 2. Call your Mercedes-Benz dealer or MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372 (recall 2025050002) for an appointment. 3. Have the software updated — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRONIC STABILITY CONTROL (ESC):CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2025 G 550, G 580, and AMG G 63 vehicles. The vehicle's electronic stability program (ESP) may display an incorrect warning light during an ESP system failure. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 126, "Electronic Stability Control Systems," and 101, "Control and Displays."
Safety risk
An incorrectly displayed warning light can fail to alert the driver to critical safety information, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the ESP control unit software, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed June 17, 2025. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2025050002.
