Cadillac Lyriq battery connections may cut drive power

NHTSA campaign 23V367000 · Reported 2023-05-25 · General Motors, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Cadillac Lyriq

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2023 Cadillac Lyriq and 2022 GMC Hummer EV models have connections inside the big high-voltage battery pack that may be out of position or welded wrong. A bad connection there can cut the power that drives the wheels, so the vehicle can lose the power to move while you are driving. Dealers will open up and inspect the battery pack and replace the whole pack if they find a problem. GM mailed owners a separate letter explaining the safety risk.

How serious is it?

Serious: GM did not tell owners to stop driving, but suddenly losing drive power in traffic can lead to a crash, and GM thought the risk important enough to send owners an extra warning letter, so schedule the inspection soon.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered by recall N232404441.
2. Call your Cadillac dealer or GM Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 (Hummer EV owners: 1-833-486-6373).
3. The dealer inspects the high-voltage battery pack and replaces it if necessary, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2022 GMC Hummer EV and 2023 Cadillac Lyriq vehicles. Certain connections within the high-voltage battery pack may be out of position or incorrectly welded, which can cause a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace the high-voltage battery pack, free of charge. Letters explaining the safety risk were sent to owners January 5, 2024. Owner notification letters were mailed June 6, 2023. Owners may contact GM's Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 and the EV Concierge number: 1-833-HUMMER-EV (1-833-486-6373). GM's number for this recall is N232404441.
