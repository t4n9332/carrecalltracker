Tesla Model 3 and Model Y Headlights Too Bright

NHTSA campaign 26V507000 · Reported 2026-08-05 · Tesla, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

The notice says overly bright headlights can reduce visibility for oncoming drivers, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Tesla Model 3 and Model Y vehicles, the low beam headlights may shine brighter than they are allowed to be. Headlights that are too bright can reduce how well oncoming drivers are able to see the road, other vehicles, and their surroundings. This reduced visibility for drivers coming the other way raises the chance of a crash happening.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Tesla, or call Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752, for updates on the repair.
3. The repair will be free once it is available.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2017-2023 Model 3 and 2020-2023 Model Y vehicles. The headlight low beams may be too bright and exceed the maximum light output. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Headlight low beams that are too bright can reduce visibility for oncoming drivers, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The remedy is currently under development. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed October 3, 2026. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-24-17-003.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
