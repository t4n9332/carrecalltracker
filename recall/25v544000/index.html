Ford Edge and Lincoln MKX rear brake hose may leak

NHTSA campaign 25V544000 · Reported 2025-08-22 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious, since a longer stopping distance raises the chance of a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but the full repair is not available until about August 2026.

What's wrong, in plain English

A rear brake hose on these Ford Edge and Lincoln MKX vehicles can split and leak brake fluid. That hose is the flexible line carrying fluid to the rear brakes, and it flexes as the suspension moves. If fluid leaks out, the vehicle needs a longer distance to come to a stop. Ford mailed a first warning letter in September 2025, but the parts for the fix are not expected until around August 2026. Dealers will inspect, replace, or adjust the hoses free.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Contact your Ford or Lincoln dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 25S87), and ask about an inspection now and the remedy when parts arrive. 3. Call Ford right away if you notice any braking problem; all recall work is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Lincoln MKX and 2015-2018 Edge vehicles. The rear brake jounce hose may rupture and leak brake fluid.
Safety risk
A brake fluid leak can extend the distance required to stop the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the brake jounce hoses and adjust brake jounce hoses with no damage present, free of charge. Interim owner letters notifying owners of the safety risk were mailed September 8, 2025. Additional letters will be sent once the remedy is available, anticipated August 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S87.
