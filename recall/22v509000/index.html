NHTSA campaign 22V509000 · Reported Jul 19, 2022 · Kia Motors America

What's wrong, in plain English

Kia is recalling certain Sorento and Telluride vehicles. These vehicles may have been built with the wrong spare tire. If that spare is put on the vehicle, it can interfere with the anti-lock brakes and the traction control system, so the vehicles do not meet the federal rule for tire and rim selection. Dealers will look at the spare tire and replace it if needed. Owner letters have been mailed, and the replacement is free.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Kia dealer to have the spare tire inspected, or call Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542.
3. The inspection and replacement are free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V509000. Official notice

Component
TIRES:TEMPORARY/EMERGENCY SPARE TIRE
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) is recalling certain 2022 Sorento and Telluride vehicles. The vehicles may be equipped with an incorrect spare tire which, when installed, can impair the anti-lock braking system (ABS) and the traction control system (TCS). As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
Impaired anti-lock braking or traction control systems can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the spare tire, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 27, 2022. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC244.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
