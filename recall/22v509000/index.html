Kia Sorento and Telluride may have wrong spare tire

NHTSA campaign 22V509000 · Reported 2022-07-19 · Kia Motors America · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Kia Sorento
  • 2022 Kia Telluride

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2022 Kia Sorento and Telluride vehicles left the factory with the wrong spare tire. If you put that spare on the vehicle, it can interfere with the anti-lock brakes and the traction control — the systems that keep your wheels from locking up when you brake hard or spinning when you accelerate. With those systems not working properly, the vehicle is harder to control. Dealers will check the spare tire and replace it if it is the wrong one.

How serious is it?

The vehicle is fine to drive on its regular tires; the danger comes only if you have to put the spare on, so get the spare checked before you need it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether recall 22V509000 applies to your vehicle.
2. Contact your Kia dealer, or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542, and reference recall SC244.
3. Have the spare tire inspected and replaced if necessary — it is free.

Official notice

Component
TIRES:TEMPORARY/EMERGENCY SPARE TIRE
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) is recalling certain 2022 Sorento and Telluride vehicles. The vehicles may be equipped with an incorrect spare tire which, when installed, can impair the anti-lock braking system (ABS) and the traction control system (TCS). As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
Impaired anti-lock braking or traction control systems can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the spare tire, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 27, 2022. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC244.
