What's wrong, in plain English

Some Ford F-150 trucks with manual and power release tailgates have latches that may unlatch on their own while the truck is being driven. If the tailgate opens unexpectedly, anything sitting in the truck bed that isn't secured could fall out onto the road, creating a hazard for the truck and for other vehicles nearby, which raises the chance of a collision.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is affected.
2. Contact a Ford dealer to schedule a tailgate latch replacement; Ford customer service can be reached at 1-866-436-7332.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V983000. Official notice

Component
LATCHES/LOCKS/LINKAGES:TAILGATE:LATCH
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2018 F-150 vehicles with manual and power release tailgates. The tailgate latches may unlatch unexpectedly while driving.
Safety risk
Unexpected tailgate opening may allow unrestrained cargo to fall out of the truck bed and become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the left-hand and right-hand tailgate latches, free of charge. An interim notification letter was mailed January 31, 2022. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 31, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21S53.
