Genesis G80 and GV80 fuel line may leak gasoline

NHTSA campaign 21V208000 · Reported 2021-03-24 · Hyundai Motor America · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Genesis G80
  • 2021 Genesis GV80

What's wrong, in plain English

On these cars with the 2.5-liter turbo engine, the metal tube that carries gasoline from the pump to the engine may not seal tightly where it connects. Fuel can seep out of that joint. If the leaking gas reaches a spark or hot surface, it can catch fire. The dealer fix is a new high-pressure fuel tube, and it costs you nothing. Hyundai mailed letters to owners of affected Genesis vehicles in May 2021.

How serious is it?

Leaking fuel near an ignition source can start a fire, which is serious; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but have the repair done as soon as possible.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included in recall 21V208000 (Hyundai 008G).
2. Contact your Genesis dealer, or Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460, to schedule service.
3. Have the high-pressure fuel tube replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2021 Genesis G80 and GV80 vehicles equipped with 2.5-litre turbo engines. The fuel tube connecting the high pressure fuel pump to the fuel rail may not seal properly, which could allow fuel to leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Hyundai will notify owners, and dealers will replace the high pressure fuel tube, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 24, 2021. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 008G.
