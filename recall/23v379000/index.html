Ford Bronco, Ranger Tire Label Shows Wrong Capacity

NHTSA campaign 23V379000 · Reported 2023-05-26 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

Loading a vehicle based on an incorrect capacity number can result in an overloaded vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Bronco and Ranger vehicles, the tire and loading information label lists the wrong load-carrying capacity. Because the number is incorrect, an owner following the label could end up putting too much weight in the vehicle. Ford will mail a corrected label for owners to put over the old one, or a dealer can install it, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, or wait for the corrected label to arrive by mail.
3. The new label is free.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Bronco, 2023 Super Duty F-250, and 2019-2023 Ranger vehicles. The Tire and Loading Information (TREAD) label states the incorrect load carrying capacity, which can result in an overloaded vehicle. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
An overloaded vehicle increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford will mail a new TREAD label to install over the existing label, free of charge. Owners will also have the option to take their vehicle to a Ford or Lincoln dealer to have the new label installed, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 21, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23C18.
