Toyota Sienna Wheelchair Van Brake Hose Problem

NHTSA campaign 19V553000 · Reported 2019-07-25 · Vantage Mobility International, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A worn or failed brake hose can lead to a loss of vehicle control and reduced braking ability, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain wheelchair-accessible vans built on the Toyota Sienna and sold as AMS Genesis models may have rubber brake hoses at the rear wheels that are too short. Because the hoses are too short, they can rub against a part of the rear axle called the trailing arm. Over time, this rubbing can wear against the material of the brake hose itself.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact Vantage customer service at 1-800-488-9082. 3. Dealers will install a set of rear brake hoses with proper clearance, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Vantage Mobility International, LLC (Vantage) is recalling certain 2012-2018 AMS Genesis wheelchair vans, built on a Toyota Sienna vehicles. The rubber brake hoses that attach to the rear wheel assembly may be too short, causing them to rub against the rear axle trailing arm.
Safety risk
The contact may cause failure of the rear brake hoses, resulting in a loss of vehicle control and reduced braking ability, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Vantage will notify owners, and dealers will install a set of rear brake hoses that will provide the proper clearance, free of charge. The recall began December 16, 2019. Owners may contact Vantage customer service at 1-800-488-9082.
