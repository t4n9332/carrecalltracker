Kia Telluride front seat motor fire risk from stuck knob

NHTSA campaign 24V407000 · Reported 2024-06-05 · Kia America, Inc. · critical severity what's this?

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Kia Telluride
  • 2023 Kia Telluride
  • 2024 Kia Telluride

What's wrong, in plain English

The motor that moves your front power seat can overheat if the seat's slide knob gets stuck. That can start a fire while the vehicle is parked or while you are driving. Kia dealers will add a bracket behind the seat switch covers and replace the slide knobs at no cost. Letters went out in July 2024. Kia has since issued a newer recall that replaces this one, so ask your dealer which repair your vehicle now needs.

How serious is it?

Very serious: an overheating seat motor can cause a fire whether parked or driving, and Kia advises parking outside until repaired, but does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see which seat recall still applies to your vehicle. 2. Park outside and away from buildings until the work is complete. 3. Contact your Kia dealer or Kia at 1-800-333-4542 (recall SC316) for the free repair.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:FRONT ASSEMBLY:POWER ADJUST
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2020-2024 Telluride vehicles. The front power seat motor may overheat due to a stuck power seat slide knob, which can result in a fire while parked or driving.
Safety risk
A fire increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. Dealers will install a bracket for the power seat switch back covers and replace the seat slide knobs, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 30, 2024. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC316.
