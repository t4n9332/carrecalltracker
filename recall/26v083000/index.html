Silverado and Sierra: rear wheels can suddenly lock up

NHTSA campaign 26V083000 · Reported 2026-02-12 · General Motors, LLC · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 1500
  • 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 2500
  • 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 3500
  • 2020 GMC Sierra 1500
  • 2020 GMC Sierra 2500
  • 2020 GMC Sierra 3500
  • 2021 Chevrolet Silverado 1500
  • 2021 GMC Sierra 1500
  • 2022 Chevrolet Silverado 1500
  • 2022 GMC Sierra 1500

What's wrong, in plain English

These trucks were repaired under an earlier transmission recall, but that repair may not have been done correctly. A valve inside the automatic transmission can fail, and when it does the rear wheels can lock up while you are driving. Locked rear wheels can make the truck slide or spin and you could crash. The dealer will inspect your truck and install corrected transmission software at no charge. Owner letters went out on March 6, 2026.

How serious is it?

Very serious — the rear wheels can lock with no warning while you are driving; the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but get it in as soon as the dealer can take it.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls (these VINs became searchable February 12, 2026).
2. Call Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC at 1-800-462-8782 (GM recall N252516560) and ask for the earliest appointment.
3. Have the dealer inspect the truck and update the transmission software — it is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE
Summary
General Motors, LLC is recalling certain 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 2500, 3500, GMC Sierra 2500, 3500, and 2020-2022 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500 vehicles that may have been repaired incorrectly under recall 24V797. The transmission control valve may fail and cause the rear wheels to lock up.
Safety risk
Rear wheel lock up increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the vehicles and update the transmission control software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 6, 2026. Owners may contact GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N252516560. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on February 12, 2026.
