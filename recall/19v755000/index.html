What's wrong, in plain English

Certain BMW 330i, 330i xDrive and M340i cars may have the interior trunk release cable installed incorrectly. Because of this, someone inside the trunk may not be able to open it from the inside using the release handle. This does not meet a federal safety standard that requires cars to have a working interior trunk release. Dealers will inspect the cable and install it correctly if needed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included.
2. Contact a BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, to schedule an inspection.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V755000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:TRUNK LID
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020 330i, 330i xDrive and M340i vehicles. The interior trunk release cable may have been improperly installed, preventing the opening of the trunk from the inside. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 401, "Internal Trunk Release."
Safety risk
If a person were inside the trunk compartment, they may not be able to get out, increasing their risk of injury.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the interior trunk release cable and properly install the cable, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began December 13, 2019. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
