BMW M340i trunk release may not open from inside

NHTSA campaign 19V755000 · Reported 2019-10-23 · BMW of North America, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 BMW M340I

What's wrong, in plain English

Every new car sold in the U.S. must have a release inside the trunk so that anyone shut in there can push the lid open from the inside. On some of these BMW 3 Series cars, including the M340i, that release cable was not installed correctly at the factory, so pulling it may not open the trunk. Someone closed inside — most likely a child playing — might not be able to get out. A dealer will check the cable and install it properly.

How serious is it?

The car itself drives normally with no added crash risk, but a person trapped in the trunk could be seriously hurt, so it is worth fixing promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is affected. 2. Call your BMW dealer, or BMW at 1-800-525-7417, to book the inspection. 3. Have the trunk release cable installed correctly — there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:TRUNK LID
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020 330i, 330i xDrive and M340i vehicles. The interior trunk release cable may have been improperly installed, preventing the opening of the trunk from the inside. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 401, "Internal Trunk Release."
Safety risk
If a person were inside the trunk compartment, they may not be able to get out, increasing their risk of injury.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the interior trunk release cable and properly install the cable, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began December 13, 2019. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
