What's wrong, in plain English

On some BMW S 1000 RR motorcycles, the hand brake lever may not work the way it should. This can reduce how well the brakes perform, which means the motorcycle could take longer to stop than expected. Dealers will replace a small part in the brake lever, called the fulcrum pin, to fix the problem. This repair will be done free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your motorcycle is included.
2. Contact a BMW dealer to schedule the repair (BMW customer service: 1-800-525-7417).
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V123000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2023 S 1000 RR motorcycles. The motorcycle hand brake lever may not function correctly, reducing brake performance.
Safety risk
Reduced brake performance can extend the distance required to stop the motorcycle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the hand brake lever fulcrum pin, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 25, 2023. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
