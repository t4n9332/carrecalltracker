BMW S 1000 RR hand brake lever reduces stopping power

NHTSA campaign 23V123000 · Reported 2023-02-24 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 BMW S 1000 RR

What's wrong, in plain English

The hand brake lever is the one you squeeze to slow the bike. On these motorcycles, that lever may not work properly, so you do not get the braking power you expect. The bike can take longer to stop than it should, which matters most in a sudden stop or in heavy traffic. The problem is traced to a small pin the lever pivots on; dealers will replace that pin free of charge.

How serious is it?

Serious: a longer stopping distance on a motorcycle can cause a crash, so have the lever fixed promptly, though the notice does not tell riders to stop riding.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your 2023 S 1000 RR is covered.
2. Contact a BMW Motorrad dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, to schedule the work.
3. Have the hand brake lever fulcrum pin replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2023 S 1000 RR motorcycles. The motorcycle hand brake lever may not function correctly, reducing brake performance.
Safety risk
Reduced brake performance can extend the distance required to stop the motorcycle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the hand brake lever fulcrum pin, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 25, 2023. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
