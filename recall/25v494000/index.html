Kia Telluride door belt molding trim may detach

NHTSA campaign 25V494000 · Reported 2025-07-28 · Kia America, Inc. · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Kia Telluride
  • 2024 Kia Telluride
  • 2025 Kia Telluride

What's wrong, in plain English

The belt molding is the trim strip that runs along the bottom edge of each door window. On some of these SUVs the layers of that strip can separate, and the trim can peel away and come off the vehicle while you are driving. Trim lying in the road, or coming loose at highway speed, can be a hazard for the cars behind you. Dealers will inspect the belt moldings and replace any that need it, free.

How serious is it?

Low for you: the SUV is safe to keep driving and occupants are not at risk, but a detached piece of trim can become a road hazard and cause a crash for other drivers.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is part of recall 25V494000 (Kia's number is SC347).
2. Call your Kia dealer, or Kia customer service at 800-333-4542, to book the inspection.
3. Have the dealer inspect and replace the belt molding trim as needed; there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:EXTERIOR TRIM
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2023-2025 Telluride vehicles. The door belt molding trim can delaminate and detach from the vehicle.
Safety risk
A detached trim piece can become a road hazard for other vehicles, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the belt molding trim assemblies as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 19, 2025. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC347.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
