Volvo EX90 power tailgate can suddenly drop down

NHTSA campaign 25V654000 · Reported 2025-09-30 · Volvo Car USA, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Volvo EX90

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2025 EX90s, a nut inside the motors that raise and lower the power tailgate can come loose and fall off. If that happens, the tailgate can drop suddenly instead of staying up, and it could come down on someone standing behind the car. Volvo tells owners not to use the power tailgate feature until the repair is done. A dealer will replace both tailgate motors free. Owner letters were mailed November 24, 2025.

How serious is it?

The car is safe to drive, but the falling tailgate can injure whoever is standing under it, so follow Volvo's advice and stop using the power tailgate until it is fixed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your EX90 is included. 2. Stop using the power tailgate function, and contact your Volvo dealer or Volvo customer service at 1-800-458-1552 (recall R10342). 3. Have both tailgate drive units replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:HATCHBACK/LIFTGATE:SUPPORT DEVICE/STRUT
Summary
Volvo Car USA, LLC (Volvo) is recalling certain 2025 EX90 vehicles. A spindle nut may detach from the Power Operated Tailgate (POT) drive units, causing the tailgate to suddenly drop.
Safety risk
A tailgate that suddenly drops can hit a person, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to use the POT function until the repair is completed. Dealers will replace both POT drive units. Owner letters were mailed November 24, 2025. Owners may contact Volvo's customer service at 1-800-458-1552. Volvo's number for this recall is R10342. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning October 7, 2025.
