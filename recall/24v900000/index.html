Honda Pilot and Passport fuel pipe may leak gas

NHTSA campaign 24V900000 · Reported 2024-11-27 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Honda Passport
  • 2023 Honda Pilot
  • 2024 Honda Passport
  • 2024 Honda Pilot
  • 2025 Honda Pilot

What's wrong, in plain English

The pipe you put gas into connects to a neck tube on the fuel tank. On these Passports and Pilots, those two pieces can come apart, and gasoline can leak out. Leaking fuel near anything hot or sparking can start a fire. Dealers will inspect the parts and repair them if needed, free of charge. Letters went to owners in January 2025, so many of these vehicles may already be fixed. If you smell gas, get it checked right away.

How serious is it?

This is serious because a fuel leak can lead to a fire; Honda has not told owners to park the vehicle or stop driving, but you should get the inspection done as soon as a dealer can take you in.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Pilot or Passport is covered.
2. Call your Honda dealer and ask for recall OKM, or call Honda at 1-888-234-2138.
3. Have the inspection and any needed repair done — there is no charge to you.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY:FILLER PIPE AND CAP
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Passport and 2023-2025 Pilot vehicles. The fuel filler neck tube and fuel filler pipe may separate, allowing fuel to leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and repair the fuel filler neck tube and pipe as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 7, 2025. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is OKM.
