Tesla Model S driver airbag may tear during deployment

NHTSA campaign 24V967000 · Reported 2024-12-24 · Tesla, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A torn airbag may not properly protect the driver in a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The driver's airbag could tear as it deploys. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet federal safety standards for occupant crash protection and windshield mounting.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Tesla service center, or call Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL:DRIVER SIDE:CUSHION
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2021-2025 Model S and Model X vehicles. The driver's air bag could tear during deployment. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) numbers 208, "Occupant Crash Protection" and 212, "Windshield Mounting."
Safety risk
A torn air bag may not adequately protect an occupant in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Tesla service will replace the air bag assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 21, 2025. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-24-20-001.
