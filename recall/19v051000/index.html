Ram 1500 Windshield Defroster Doesn't Work

NHTSA campaign 19V051000 · Reported 2019-01-24 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

Without a working defroster, the driver's visibility can be reduced, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain 2019 Ram 1500 trucks equipped with a 12-inch touchscreen and a base-level heating and air conditioning system. In this combination, the windshield defrosting and defogging system doesn't work.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Chrysler dealer, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The dealer will repurchase the affected vehicle at no cost to the owner.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:DEFROSTER/DEFOGGER/HVAC SYSTEM
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 RAM 1500 vehicles equipped with a 12" touchscreen display and a base-level heating, ventilation and air conditioning ("HVAC") system. Vehicles with this combination do not have a functioning windshield defrosting and defogging system. As a result, these vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 103, "Windshield defrosting and defogging systems."
Safety risk
The lack of a functioning windshield defrosting and defogging system may decrease the driver's visibility, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will repurchase the affected vehicles. The recall began March 7, 2019. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V12.
