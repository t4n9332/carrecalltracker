Audi Q7 C-Pillar Padding Doesn't Absorb Impact

NHTSA campaign 20V658000 · Reported Oct 27, 2020 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

In a crash, an unbelted occupant's head could hit the C-pillar padding, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The padding on the vehicle's C-pillar may not absorb enough impact in the event of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact an Audi dealer at 1-800-253-2834 to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V658000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:ROOF AND PILLARS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2018-2020 Audi Q7 vehicles. The padding on the vehicle's C-pillar may not absorb enough impact in the event of a crash.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, an unbelted occupant's head may impact the vehicle's C-pillar padding, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will replace the deformation element on the C-pillars, free of charge. The recall began November 17, 2020. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 70H8.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
