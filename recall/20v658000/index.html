Audi Q7 roof pillar padding may not absorb impact

NHTSA campaign 20V658000 · Reported 2020-10-27 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Audi Q7
  • 2019 Audi Q7
  • 2020 Audi Q7

What's wrong, in plain English

The Audi Q7 has padded trim on the roof pillar behind the rear side window. That padding is meant to soften a blow if someone's head hits it in a crash. On these vehicles the padding does not absorb as much impact as it should. If a person riding in the car is not wearing a seat belt and their head strikes that pillar during a crash, the injury could be worse than it otherwise would be.

How serious is it?

This is a moderate risk that only comes into play in a crash and mainly affects people who are not belted in; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so keep driving and make sure everyone buckles up.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether recall 70H8 applies to your Q7.
2. Contact your Audi dealer for an appointment, or call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834.
3. The dealer will replace the padding parts in both roof pillars, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:ROOF AND PILLARS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2018-2020 Audi Q7 vehicles. The padding on the vehicle's C-pillar may not absorb enough impact in the event of a crash.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, an unbelted occupant's head may impact the vehicle's C-pillar padding, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will replace the deformation element on the C-pillars, free of charge. The recall began November 17, 2020. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 70H8.
