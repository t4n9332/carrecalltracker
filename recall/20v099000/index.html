Ford Trucks May Have Rear Axle, Driveshaft Failure

NHTSA campaign 20V099000 · Reported Feb 21, 2020 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

If the driveshaft separates, the vehicle can lose drive, increasing the risk of a crash. If the parking brake is not applied, the vehicle could move unexpectedly, increasing the risk of injury or a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Ford F-550, E-350, and E-450 trucks may have rear axle differential fluid that is below the proper level. This can lead to the rear axle assembly failing and the driveshaft separating from the vehicle, which can cause a loss of drive. Dealers will inspect the differential fluid level and adjust it, or replace the rear axle if it is needed. This repair will not cost owners anything, and Ford has already notified owners about this issue.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V099000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:DRIVELINE:DIFFERENTIAL UNIT
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020 F-550 and 2021 E-350 and E-450 vehicles. The rear axle differential fluid may be below the minimum level, possibly resulting in a failure of the rear axle assembly and driveshaft separation.
Safety risk
If the driveshaft separates, there may be a loss of drive, increasing the risk of crash. Additionally, if the parking brake is not applied, unintended vehicle movement can occur and increase the risk of injury or crash.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the rear axle differential fluid level and adjust it, or replace the rear axle as necessary, free of charge. The recall began March 19, 2020. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 20S09.
