Sprinter vans may roll away after shifting into Park

NHTSA campaign 22V796000 · Reported 2022-10-21 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 1500
  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500
  • 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 3500
  • 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2019-2022 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Freightliner Sprinter vans with a manual parking brake and the 7-speed automatic transmission have a part that can wear out. A small guide bushing on the parking pawl, the piece inside the transmission that locks the wheels when you shift into Park, can fatigue. When that happens, Park may not actually hold the van, and it can roll away on its own after you park it, which increases the risk of a crash.

How serious is it?

Serious: the van is not listed as park-it or park-outside, but a vehicle that rolls away on its own can cause a crash, so always set the parking brake firmly and get the free fix promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Sprinter is included.
2. Contact your Sprinter dealer, or Daimler Vans USA customer service at 1-877-762-8267.
3. Have the dealer install the updated control unit software, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:INTERNAL:PARK PAWL
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2019-2022 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Freightliner Sprinter vehicles equipped with a manual parking brake and 7-speed automatic transmission. The guide bushing on the parking pawl may fatigue, which can cause the parking pawl not to engage and result in vehicle rollaway.
Safety risk
Vehicle rollaway increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the software of the electronic stability, transmission control, direct select module, and electronic ignition switch control units, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 10, 2023. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's numbers for this recall are 22V2790611, 22V2790612, and 22V2790613.
