GM trucks and SUVs fuel pump leak fire risk

NHTSA campaign 19V837000 · Reported 2019-11-21 · General Motors LLC · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Cadillac Escalade
  • 2020 Cadillac Escalade ESV
  • 2020 Chevrolet Camaro
  • 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 1500
  • 2020 Chevrolet Tahoe
  • 2020 GMC Sierra 1500
  • 2020 GMC Yukon
  • 2020 GMC Yukon XL

What's wrong, in plain English

Some of these vehicles left the factory with a fuel pump that is missing its pressure regulator — the part that keeps fuel pressure in check. Without it, pressure can build too high, crack the fuel pump, and let gasoline leak out. Leaking fuel near anything hot or sparking can start a fire. Your dealer will replace the fuel pump at no cost to you. This recall began back in December 2019, so a used vehicle you bought may still need it.

How serious is it?

Very serious — a fuel leak can lead to a fire; GM does not instruct owners to park the vehicle, but get it repaired right away and have it checked immediately if you smell gasoline.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether the repair was ever done on your vehicle. 2. Contact your dealer now — Cadillac 1-800-333-4223, Chevrolet 1-800-222-1020, or GMC 1-888-988-7267 (recall N192283991). 3. The new fuel pump and labor are free, even for second or third owners.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2020 Cadillac Escalade, Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Camaro, Silverado 1500, Suburban, Tahoe, GMC Sierra 1500, Yukon, and Yukon XL vehicles. The fuel pump may be missing a pressure regulator, allowing for over-pressurization of the fuel system.
Safety risk
Over-pressurization can crack the fuel pump and cause leaks, increasing the risk of a fire in the presence of an ignition source.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge. The recall began December 18, 2019. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223, Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020, or GMC customer service at 1-888-988-7267. GM's number for this recall is N192283991.
