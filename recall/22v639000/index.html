Jeep Gladiator Transmission May Fail To Park

NHTSA campaign 22V639000 · Reported 2022-08-25 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A vehicle that rolls away unexpectedly after being placed in park increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2022 Jeep Gladiator vehicles have a transmission parking gear that may not properly engage with the vehicle's parking mechanism. This means the transmission may fail to fully hold the vehicle in the 'park' position after the driver has shifted into park. If this happens, the vehicle could lose the park position and roll away on its own.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact your Jeep dealer, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403. 3. Dealers will replace the transmission, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:INTERNAL:PARK PAWL
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Jeep Gladiator vehicles. The transmission parking wheel may not properly engage with the parking mechanism.
Safety risk
A malfunctioning parking mechanism may result in the loss of the 'park' position, including vehicle rollaway, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the transmission, free of charge. Entire US population are unsold units and will be repaired before they can be sold. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA, LLC's number for this recall is Z84.
