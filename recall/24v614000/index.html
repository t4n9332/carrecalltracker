Acura ZDX Anti-Lock Brakes May Release Pressure

NHTSA campaign 24V614000 · Reported Aug 15, 2024 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

A release of brake pressure can cause a loss of braking ability, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In certain all-wheel-drive ZDX vehicles, the anti-lock brake system may activate on its own and release pressure in the vehicle's regular brake system. This can happen unexpectedly while the vehicle is being driven, reducing the vehicle's ability to brake normally.

What you should do

1. Check if your VIN is affected at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact an Acura dealer at 1-800-382-2238.
3. The repair — a software update, done over-the-air or by a dealer — is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V614000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:ANTILOCK/TRACTION CONTROL/ELECTRONIC LIMITED SLIP:CONTROL UNIT/MODULE
Summary
Acura (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2024 Acura ZDX All-Wheel Drive vehicles. The anti-lock brake system (ABS) may activate unexpectedly and release brake pressure in the vehicle's service brake system.
Safety risk
A release of brake pressure can cause a loss of braking ability, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The electronic brake control module software will be updated through an over-the-air (OTA) update or by a dealer, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 26, 2024. Owners may contact Acura customer service at 1-800-382-2238. Acura's number for this recall is UJE. (over-the-air software update)
