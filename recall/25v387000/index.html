Rivian R1S and R1T front turn signals may fail

NHTSA campaign 25V387000 · Reported 2025-06-11 · Rivian Automotive, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

The notice does not restrict driving, but failed turn signals raise the crash risk, so check that both front signals flash before you drive and use hand signals if one is out.

What's wrong, in plain English

One or both of the front turn signals may not light up or blink. Drivers around you would have no way of knowing you plan to turn or change lanes, which can lead to a crash. Rivian will check the front turn signals and replace them if needed, at no cost. The parts are not ready yet, so owners get a first letter now and a second letter when the repair can be scheduled.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your R1S or R1T is included. 2. Contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261 with recall number FSAM-1693. 3. Watch for the second notice saying the remedy is ready, then schedule the free inspection and replacement.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TURN SIGNAL
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles. One or both front turn signals may fail to illuminate and flash. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Turn signals that do not function properly, failing to indicate the driver's intention to change direction, increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will inspect and replace the front turn signals as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 22, 2025. Owners will receive a second notice once the remedy becomes available. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1693.
