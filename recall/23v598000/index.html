What's wrong, in plain English

On these Ford and Lincoln vehicles, the backup camera may show nothing on the screen when you shift into reverse. Some models also have a 360-degree camera view that can go blank. Without the picture, you cannot see what is behind you on the screen, so you could back into a person, another car, or an object. Dealers will replace the backup camera and update the software and wiring if needed, all free of charge.

How serious is it?

Moderate — a blank camera reduces what you can see behind you and raises the risk of a crash, but the vehicle is otherwise safe to drive if you back up slowly and check your mirrors and over your shoulder.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered by recall 23V598000 (Ford's number 23S48).
2. Call your Ford or Lincoln dealer to schedule the free camera replacement; ask about parts availability, since Ford parts were expected in the second quarter of 2024.
3. Until it is fixed, turn and look behind you and use your mirrors every time you reverse.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2023 Lincoln Aviator, 2018-2021 Lincoln Navigator, 2022-2023 Ford Transit, and 2021 Ford Bronco vehicles. The rearview camera, or 360-degree view camera if equipped, may not display a rearview image when the vehicle is placed in reverse.
Safety risk
Loss of the rearview camera image can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the rearview camera, and update the software and wiring as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 20, 2023 to Lincoln owners. Ford vehicle owners notification letters were mailed March 4, 2024. Ford remedy parts are anticipated 2nd Quarter 2024. Ford's number for this recall is 23S48.
