Porsche Cayenne and Panamera fuel pump bolts may leak fuel

NHTSA campaign 25V634000 · Reported 2025-09-23 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak near a source of ignition increases the risk of a fire.

What's wrong, in plain English

The fasteners holding the high pressure fuel pump in place on these vehicles may not be tightened properly, which can allow fuel to leak. This affects certain Porsche Panamera, Panamera E-Hybrid, and Cayenne vehicles. Porsche is recalling these vehicles so dealers can inspect and tighten the connections, and replace the fuel pump and fuel lines if needed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Porsche dealer, or call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243, to schedule the inspection and repair.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2024-2026 Panamera, Cayenne, and 2025 Panamera E-Hybrid vehicles. The fasteners securing the high pressure fuel pump may not be tightened properly, resulting in a fuel leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and tighten the screw connections and replace the high pressure fuel pump and fuel lines as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 19, 2025. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ASA9. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning October 10, 2025.
