Sprinter Van Brake Hose Can Wear Through

NHTSA campaign 20V181000 · Reported 2020-03-25 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing brake fluid can make the vehicle take longer to stop, which raises the chance of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Sprinter vans, part of the fender liner near the front wheel can rub against a brake hose. Over time, that rubbing can wear through the hose and let brake fluid leak out. Brake fluid is what lets the brakes work properly, so losing it can make the vehicle take longer to stop than it normally would. Dealers will inspect the brake hoses, replace them if needed, and modify the fender liner so the rubbing does not happen again.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
Contact a Mercedes-Benz or Freightliner Sprinter dealer, or call 1-877-762-8267, to schedule the inspection and repair.
The inspection, any hose replacement, and the fender liner modification are all free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, AIR:SUPPLY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2019 Freightliner Sprinter and Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vehicles. The rear part of the fender liner on the front axle may contact and chafe the brake hose, possibly resulting in a loss of brake fluid.
Safety risk
Loss of brake fluid can extend the distance needed to stop the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and Mercedes-Benz or Freightliner Sprinter dealers will inspect and replace the brake hoses, as necessary. Additionally, the fender liners will be modified. All services will be performed free of charge. The recall began May 8, 2020. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3BRADVER.
