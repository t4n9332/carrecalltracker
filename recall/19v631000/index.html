BMW X5 and X7 steering gear teeth may break

NHTSA campaign 19V631000 · Reported 2019-08-28 · BMW of North America, LLC · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 BMW X5
  • 2020 BMW X7

What's wrong, in plain English

The steering rack is the part that turns your front wheels when you turn the wheel. Inside it, a small gear meshes with a toothed bar. On these vehicles those gear teeth can break when they are under load, for example while you are turning. If they break, the steering wheel stops turning the front wheels and you lose steering control. Dealers replace the entire steering rack at no charge to you, and the recall started in August 2019.

How serious is it?

Very serious — losing steering control while driving is one of the most dangerous failures a vehicle can have, so get this done right away even though the notice does not order owners to park the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your vehicle is affected and still unrepaired.
2. Call a BMW dealer immediately to schedule the free steering rack replacement.
3. Contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 if a dealer cannot see you soon.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:RACK AND PINION:RACK
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020 X5 sDrive40i, X5 xDrive40i, X5 M50i, X7 xDrive40i and X7 M50i vehicles. The steering rack pinion teeth may break under load, resulting in a loss of steering control.
Safety risk
A loss of steering control increases risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the steering rack, free of charge. The recall began August 21, 2019. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
