Porsche 911 Carrera loose front differential fire risk

NHTSA campaign 20V381000 · Reported 2020-07-01 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak and fire are possible if the differential comes loose, so this is serious; Porsche has not told owners to park the car, but schedule the free repair promptly.

What's wrong, in plain English

The bracket that holds the front differential, the gearbox that sends power to the front wheels, may not have been tightened correctly. If it works loose, the differential can shift around and bang into nearby parts, including the fuel tank and the drive shafts. A damaged fuel tank can leak gasoline, and leaking fuel near anything hot or sparking can start a fire. Your dealer will remove the unit, check the bolt threads, look for damage, and reinstall or replace it.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is covered by recall ALA8.
Book the inspection with a Porsche dealer, or call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243.
Let the dealer inspect and re-torque or replace the front differential mounting — there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:DRIVELINE:DIFFERENTIAL UNIT
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2020 Porsche 911 Carrera 4S Coupe vehicles. The front axle differential bracket may not have been tightened correctly. If it comes loose, the differential could potentially cause damage to surrounding components.
Safety risk
Components such as the fuel tank or drive shafts may become damaged from the loose differential. Damage to the fuel tank can result in fuel leakage, which in the presence of an ignition source may increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Porsche will notify owners, and dealers will remove the front axle differential to check the screw threads, and to inspect surrounding components for any consequential damage. Depending on the inspection result, the front axle differential will be reinstalled or replaced using new screw joints torqued to the required specification. All work will be performed free of charge. The recall began August 20, 2020. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ALA8.
