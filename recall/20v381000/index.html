What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2020 Porsche 911 Carrera 4S Coupe vehicles have a front axle differential bracket that may not have been tightened correctly. If it comes loose, the differential could damage nearby components such as the fuel tank or drive shafts. Damage to the fuel tank can cause fuel to leak, which in the presence of an ignition source may increase the risk of a fire.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Porsche dealer to have the front axle differential inspected and, depending on the result, reinstalled or replaced; you can also call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V381000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:DRIVELINE:DIFFERENTIAL UNIT
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2020 Porsche 911 Carrera 4S Coupe vehicles. The front axle differential bracket may not have been tightened correctly. If it comes loose, the differential could potentially cause damage to surrounding components.
Safety risk
Components such as the fuel tank or drive shafts may become damaged from the loose differential. Damage to the fuel tank can result in fuel leakage, which in the presence of an ignition source may increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Porsche will notify owners, and dealers will remove the front axle differential to check the screw threads, and to inspect surrounding components for any consequential damage. Depending on the inspection result, the front axle differential will be reinstalled or replaced using new screw joints torqued to the required specification. All work will be performed free of charge. The recall began August 20, 2020. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ALA8.
