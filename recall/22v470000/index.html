Mitsubishi Outlander backup camera image fails to appear

NHTSA campaign 22V470000 · Reported 2022-07-08 · Mitsubishi Motors North America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Mitsubishi Outlander

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2022 Mitsubishi Outlanders have a software error that can stop the backup camera picture from showing on the dashboard screen when you shift into reverse. With no picture, you cannot see children, pets, or objects directly behind the vehicle. Federal rules require a working rear view, so these vehicles do not meet the standard. A dealer will reload the camera software free of charge. Mitsubishi has since replaced this recall with a newer one.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the vehicle is safe to drive, but without the camera you are more likely to hit something while backing up, so use your mirrors and turn to look behind you until it is repaired.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — it will show whether your Outlander is affected and which recall now applies, since 22V470000 was replaced by recall 25V369.
2. Contact your Mitsubishi dealer, or Mitsubishi customer service, and mention recall SR-22-003 to schedule the camera software update.
3. Have the software updated — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:DISPLAY FUNCTION
Summary
Mitsubishi Motors North America, Inc. (MMNA) is recalling certain 2022 Outlander vehicles. The rearview camera may not display the rearview image due to a software error. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
An inoperable rearview camera display reduces the driver's rear view, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the rearview camera software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 1, 2022. Owners may contact MMNA customer service at 1-88-648-7820. MMNA's number for this recall is SR-22-003. This recall was replaced by NHTSA recall number 25V369.
