BMW S 1000 RR motorcycles may leak oil onto tire

NHTSA campaign 19V697000 · Reported 2019-10-02 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 BMW S1000 RR

What's wrong, in plain English

On these motorcycles, the hoses that carry oil to the oil cooler may not have been attached to the oil pipes correctly at the factory. A loose hose can let engine oil escape while you ride. The leaking oil can drip down in the path of the rear tire, and oil on a tire or on the road makes the bike slide very easily. A rider could lose control and crash.

How serious is it?

Oil reaching the rear tire can cause a sudden loss of grip, so while the notice does not say to park the bike, this should be repaired before you ride much more.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your motorcycle is part of recall 19V697000.
2. Contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 or your BMW motorcycle dealer to schedule the repair.
3. The dealer will replace the oil cooler assembly and its hoses, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020 S 1000 RR motorcycles. The oil cooler hoses may not be attached properly to the oil pipes, possibly resulting in an oil leak.
Safety risk
Leaking oil may drip in the path of the rear tire, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the oil cooler assembly, including the hoses, free of charge. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
