Jeep Compass Dashboard Display May Be Too Dim

NHTSA campaign 23V576000 · Reported 2023-08-14 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A display that isn't bright enough may not clearly show important safety information, like the speedometer or warning lights, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Jeep Compass vehicles, the brightness of the instrument panel display cannot be adjusted because of incorrect software in the body control module.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a dealer to have the body control module software updated; customer service can be reached at 1-800-853-1403.
3. This repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Jeep Compass vehicles. The brightness level of the instrument panel cluster (IPC) may not be adjustable due to incorrect software in the body control module (BCM). As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 101, "Control and Displays."
Safety risk
An IPC display that is not bright enough may not clearly show critical safety information, such as the speedometer or warning lights, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the body control module (BCM) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 29, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 88A.
