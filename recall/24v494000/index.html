Ford F-650 Tie Rods May Break and Fail

NHTSA campaign 24V494000 · Reported 2024-06-28 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

A tie rod failure can cause a loss of steering control and increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain F-Super Duty F-650 and F-750 trucks may have front axle tie rod assemblies with ball studs that were not heat-treated correctly. Because of this incorrect heat treatment, the ball studs can break. If a ball stud breaks, it can cause the tie rod assembly itself to fail. This tie rod problem involves the front axle steering linkage on these trucks.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Ford dealer to schedule an inspection; you can also call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. Dealers will inspect and replace the tie rod assembly as needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:LINKAGES:TIE ROD ASSEMBLY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2025 F-Super Duty F-650 and F-750 vehicles. The front axle tie rod assemblies may contain ball studs that have been incorrectly heat-treated, which can cause them to break, and possibly result in tie rod failure.
Safety risk
Tie rod failure can cause a loss of vehicle steering control and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the front axle tie rod assembly as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 14, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S42.
