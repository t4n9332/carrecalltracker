Ford E-350 and E-450 engine cover may get hot

NHTSA campaign 25V793000 · Reported 2025-11-17 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

Ford says direct contact with certain areas of the engine cover inside the passenger compartment may burn or injure the driver or front seat passenger. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain 2021 E-350 and E-450 vehicles that received an incomplete software update under an earlier recall, 20V795. On these vehicles, the engine cover may become very hot. Dealers will update the engine software that manages heat during high idle, free of charge. Ford mailed owner notification letters on January 7, 2026, and says the vehicle identification numbers in this recall can be looked up on NHTSA.gov beginning December 22, 2025.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall number 25SC7), to book an appointment.
3. Have the dealer update the powertrain calibration — the repair is free.

  • 2021 Ford E-450

Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 E-350 and E-450 vehicles that received an incomplete software update under recall 20V795. The engine cover may become excessively hot.
Safety risk
Direct contact with certain areas of engine cover surface in the passenger compartment may increase the risk of injury or burns to the skin of the driver or front seat passenger.
Remedy
Dealers will update the powertrain calibration for heat management under elevated idle conditions, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 7, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25SC7. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning December 22, 2025.
