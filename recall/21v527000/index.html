Mercedes-Benz AMG side-impact airbag sensors may loosen

NHTSA campaign 21V527000 · Reported 2021-07-12 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Air bags and seatbelts that don't work as intended can increase the risk of injury in a side-impact crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

This recall affects certain Mercedes-Benz AMG CLS53, AMG E53, and AMG G63 vehicles. The connectors for the side-impact crash sensors may come loose. If that happens, it could delay the airbags and seatbelts from activating the way they're supposed to during a side crash, which means they may not respond as quickly as needed when the vehicle is hit from the side.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer, or call MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SENSOR:SIDE IMPACT
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2021 CLS450, AMG E53, AMG CLS53, E350, E450, AMG GT43, AMG GT53, 2020 S450, S560, G550, AMG GLC63, 2020-2021 GLC300, and AMG G63 vehicles. The side crash sensor connectors may come loose, which could delay the activation of air bags and seatbelts during a side impact crash.
Safety risk
Air bags and seatbelts that don't function as intended can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace as necessary, the side impact crash sensors, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on September 3, 2021. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2021070032/33.
