BMW X5 Tire Placard Lists Wrong Information

NHTSA campaign 24V327000 · Reported 2024-05-10 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Incorrect tire information can lead to the wrong size tire being installed or tires being inflated to the wrong pressure, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The tire information placard on these vehicles may show the wrong tire size and tire pressure. Because the numbers printed on it are incorrect, the placard does not give accurate information about which tires should be used or how much air should be in them. This means the information a driver would see on that label may not match what the vehicle actually needs.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
Contact a BMW dealer, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
The tire information placard will be replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2024-2025 X5 xDrive40i vehicles. The tire information placard may indicate an incorrect tire size and tire pressure. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
Incorrect tire information can allow an incorrectly sized tire to be installed or result in improperly inflated tires, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the tire information placard, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 9, 2024. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
