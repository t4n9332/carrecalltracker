Ram ProMaster seat belts may not hold child seats

NHTSA campaign 21V699000 · Reported 2021-09-09 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 RAM Promaster
  • 2021 RAM Promaster City

What's wrong, in plain English

Seat belts have a locking mode you use to hold a child safety seat tightly in place. In these vans, that locking feature may switch off too early, so the belt does not stay locked and the child seat may not be held securely. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule on protecting people in a crash. A dealer will inspect the seat belt retractors, the parts that wind the belt up, and replace them if needed.

How serious is it?

A child seat that is not held tightly can let a child be badly hurt in a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get this fixed before relying on a child seat.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is included. 2. Contact your Ram dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall Y61), to schedule the inspection. 3. Have any needed retractors replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2021 Ram ProMaster City and Ram ProMaster vehicles. The seat belt automatic locking retractors may deactivate early, which can prevent the child restraint system from securing properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An unsecured child restraint system can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the seat belt retractors as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 28, 2021. Owners may contact FCA US LLC customer service at 1-800-853- 1403. FCA US LLC's number for this recall is Y61.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
