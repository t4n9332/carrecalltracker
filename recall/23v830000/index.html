Kia Soul and Seltos Side Airbags May Deploy Unexpectedly

NHTSA campaign 23V830000 · Reported 2023-12-22 · Kia America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

An airbag that deploys unexpectedly can increase the risk of injury or a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Kia Soul and Kia Seltos vehicles, a manufacturing error can cause the side curtain airbags to deploy unexpectedly. This means an airbag could inflate at a time when it isn't meant to, rather than only when needed to protect occupants in a crash. This issue can involve the side curtain airbag module on either side of the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm it's included.
2. Contact a Kia dealer, or call Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542.
3. Dealers will inspect and replace both side curtain airbag modules as necessary, free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2023 Kia Soul
  • 2024 Kia Seltos

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2023 Soul and 2024 Seltos vehicles. Due to a manufacturing error, the side curtain air bags may deploy unexpectedly.
Safety risk
An air bag that deploys unexpectedly can increase the risk of injury or crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace both side curtain air bag modules as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 31, 2024. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC289.
