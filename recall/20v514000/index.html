BMW X7 tire information label shows wrong size and pressure

NHTSA campaign 20V514000 · Reported 2020-08-27 · BMW of North America, LLC · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 BMW X7

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain BMW X7 xDrive40i and M50i models, the sticker that lists your tire size and the correct cold tire pressure does not match the tires actually fitted to the vehicle. That fails the federal rule on tire and rim selection. If you or a tire shop follow the label, you could end up with the wrong size tire or the wrong inflation pressure, which can affect how the vehicle drives. Dealers replace the label free of charge.

How serious is it?

Low risk in itself, but acting on wrong tire information could lead to a crash, so get the corrected label before buying tires or setting pressures; the vehicle is safe to drive.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your X7 is affected.
2. Contact your BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, to get the corrected label installed free.
3. Until then, do not rely on the current sticker when replacing tires or checking pressures — ask the dealer for the right figures.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain X7 xDrive40i and M50i vehicles. The tire information labels may contain tire size and cold tire inflation pressure information that does not match the specific tires that were installed on these vehicles. As such, these vehicles fail to conform to Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
The vehicle operator may be confused by the tire information on the label and this could lead to the installation of an inappropriate size tire and/or inflation pressure, potentially increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the tire information label, free of charge. The recall began October 26, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
