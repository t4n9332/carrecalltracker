Porsche Cayenne rear suspension lock nut can break

NHTSA campaign 21V271000 · Reported 2021-04-21 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Porsche Cayenne

What's wrong, in plain English

The lock nut that holds part of the rear suspension in place on certain 2021 Cayenne models, including S, Turbo, GTS and Coupe versions, can corrode under stress and break. If it breaks, the rear wheels can fall out of alignment while you are driving, and the driver can lose control of the vehicle. Porsche dealers will replace the lock nuts. Owners began getting letters in June 2021, and the work is free.

How serious is it?

A broken nut can cause a loss of control and a crash, so this should be fixed promptly; Porsche has not told owners to stop driving the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Cayenne is affected.
2. Book the repair with a Porsche dealer, or call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243 and mention recall AMA9.
3. Have the lock nuts replaced at no charge.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR:AXLE:NON-POWERED AXLE ASSEMBLY
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2021 Cayenne, Cayenne S, Cayenne Turbo, Cayenne GTS, Cayenne Coupe, Cayenne S Coupe, Cayenne Turbo Coupe, and Cayenne GTS Coupe vehicles. The lock nut on the trailing arm of the rear axle may break due to stress corrosion.
Safety risk
A broken lock nut may misalign the rear axle, causing a loss of control and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Porsche will notify owners, and dealers will replace the lock nuts, free of charge. The recall began on June 18, 2021. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AMA9.
