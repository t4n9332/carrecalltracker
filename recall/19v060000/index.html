Acura MDX, TLX Fuel Pump May Cause Engine Stall

NHTSA campaign 19V060000 · Reported Jan 29, 2019 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

An engine that stalls while driving raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Acura MDX and TLX vehicles have a problem with the fuel pump. Tiny particles in the fuel can stick to parts inside the pump over time, causing it to lose performance. When the fuel pump isn't working the way it should, it can make the engine stall while the vehicle is being driven. This kind of engine stall increases the risk of a crash, since it can happen while you are out on the road driving normally.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included in this recall.
2. Contact your Acura dealer, or call Acura customer service at 1-888-234-2138, to schedule a repair appointment.
3. The repair, including the software update and fuel pump replacement if needed, is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V060000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, OTHER:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Acura MDX, 2015-2019 Acura TLX and 2015-2017 Honda Accord vehicles, equipped with a 3.5L V6 engine. Particulates in fuel may adhere to the internal components of the fuel pump, reducing its performance.
Safety risk
Reduced fuel pump performance can result in an engine stall, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Honda will notify owners, and dealers will update the fuel injection engine control unit (FI-ECU) software and, if necessary, replace the fuel pump, free of charge. The recall began March 25, 2019. Owners may contact customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Acura's recall number for this recall is P3W. Honda's recall number for this recall is N3X.
