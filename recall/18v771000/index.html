Kia Forte LED Headlights Aimed Incorrectly

NHTSA campaign 18V771000 · Reported 2018-10-31 · Kia Motors America

medium How serious is it? About this label

Reduced visibility from misaimed headlights increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some of these Fortes with LED headlights may have left the factory with the low beam aimed incorrectly. When headlights aren't aimed properly, drivers have reduced visibility, which raises the risk of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Kia dealer or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542 to have your headlights inspected.
3. Any needed repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) is recalling certain 2019 Kia Forte vehicles equipped with LED headlights. The headlights may not have been manufactured with the correct low beam aiming. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
If the headlights are not properly aimed, the driver may have reduced visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Kia will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the headlight aiming and replace the headlights, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began November 30, 2018. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC171.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
