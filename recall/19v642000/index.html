Chevrolet Malibu software error may shut off fuel injectors

NHTSA campaign 19V642000 · Reported 2019-09-06 · General Motors LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

With the fuel injectors switched off, the engine would not start and may stall while driving, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain 2018 Chevrolet Malibu vehicles with 1.5L turbo engines. An error in the engine control module software, the computer that manages the engine, may switch off the fuel injectors, the parts that deliver fuel to the engine. GM will notify owners, and dealers will reprogram the software free of charge. The recall began October 16, 2019. GM's number for this recall is N192221960.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included. 2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer to have the engine software reprogrammed, or call GM customer service at 1-800-630-2438. 3. The reprogramming is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2018 Chevrolet Malibu vehicles equipped with 1.5L turbo engines. An error in the Engine Control Module (ECM) software may result in the fuel injectors being disabled.
Safety risk
Disabled fuel injectors would prevent the engine from starting and may cause a stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will reprogram the ECM software, free of charge. The recall began October 16, 2019. Owners may contact GM customer service at 1-800-630-2438. GM's number for this recall is N192221960.
