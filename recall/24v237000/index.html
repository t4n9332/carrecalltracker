Chevrolet Colorado, GMC Canyon Front Wheel Bolt Damage

NHTSA campaign 24V237000 · Reported Mar 28, 2024 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Damaged bolts that loosen or break can cause a loss of vehicle control, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Colorado and GMC Canyon trucks. The front wheel hub bolts may have been over-tightened and damaged when they were installed. Over time, this damage could cause the bolts to loosen or come apart.

What you should do

1. Check your vehicle's recall status by VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782 to schedule a repair.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V237000. Official notice

Component
WHEELS:LUGS/NUTS/BOLTS/STUDS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023 Chevrolet Colorado and GMC Canyon vehicles. The front wheel hub bolts may have been over-tightened and damaged during installation.
Safety risk
Damaged bolts may loosen or break, which can result in a loss of vehicle control and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the left and right front wheel hub bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 10, 2024. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N232431480.
