Chevrolet Colorado and GMC Canyon front wheel bolts may break

NHTSA campaign 24V237000 · Reported 2024-03-28 · General Motors, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Chevrolet Colorado
  • 2023 GMC Canyon

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts that hold each front wheel hub in place may have been tightened too hard during assembly, which can damage them. Damaged bolts can work loose or break while you are driving. If that happens, the wheel hub is no longer held tight and you can lose control of the truck. Clunking, knocking, or a wobble from the front end are warning signs. The dealer will replace the front hub bolts on both sides at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious — broken bolts can cause you to lose control and crash; there is no order to stop driving, but have it fixed quickly and stop driving if you feel a wobble or hear knocking up front.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is affected.
2. Contact your Chevrolet dealer at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC at 1-800-462-8782 and mention recall N232431480.
3. Have both front wheel hub bolts replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
WHEELS:LUGS/NUTS/BOLTS/STUDS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023 Chevrolet Colorado and GMC Canyon vehicles. The front wheel hub bolts may have been over-tightened and damaged during installation.
Safety risk
Damaged bolts may loosen or break, which can result in a loss of vehicle control and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the left and right front wheel hub bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 10, 2024. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N232431480.
