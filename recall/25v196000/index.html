Ford Transit Connect engine computer can let engine overheat

NHTSA campaign 25V196000 · Reported 2025-03-28 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Ford Transit Connect
  • 2020 Ford Transit Connect

What's wrong, in plain English

These Transit Connect vans were fixed once before under an earlier recall, but that repair was done incorrectly. The van's main engine computer has the wrong temperature settings, so it waits too long before it starts cooling the engine down. That means the engine can get much hotter than it should, and an engine that overheats can catch fire. Your dealer will reload the correct software into the computer. It takes one visit and costs you nothing.

How serious is it?

Serious — an overheating engine can lead to a fire, and while Ford does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside, this should be fixed as soon as you can get an appointment.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is included and whether it has already been repaired correctly.
2. Call any Ford dealer and ask for recall 25S32; the software update is free.
3. If you have questions or trouble scheduling, contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:CONTROL MODULE (TCM/PCM/TECM)
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Transit Connect vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall number 20V-636. The powertrain control module (PCM) software has fail-safe temperature threshold values higher than intended for the engine. This may delay engine cooling and result in engine overheating.
Safety risk
An engine that overheats increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will update the powertrain control module (PCM) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 21, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S32.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
