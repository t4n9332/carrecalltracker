Genesis Fuel Pipe Leak May Increase Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 26V229000 · Reported 2026-04-10 · Hyundai Motor America

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak increases the risk of a fire, which is a serious hazard. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Fuel may leak from the connection point where the fuel pipe meets the fuel rail in these Genesis vehicles. A fuel leak in this location increases the risk of a fire. Genesis dealers will fix the problem by inspecting the connection and tightening it as needed, or by replacing the fuel pipe entirely if that turns out to be necessary for the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Genesis dealer, or call Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Genesis) is recalling certain Genesis 2022-2026 GV70, 2023-2025 G90, 2021-2025 G80, and GV80 vehicles. Fuel may leak at the pipe connection between the fuel pipe and fuel rail.
Safety risk
A fuel leak increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and tighten, or replace the fuel pipe as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning July 20, 2026. Owners may contact Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741. Genesis' number for this recall is 033G. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning April 11, 2026.
