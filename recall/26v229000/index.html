What's wrong, in plain English

Fuel can leak where the fuel pipe connects to the fuel rail, the part that feeds gasoline to the engine. A loose or faulty connection can let gasoline escape in the engine bay, where it can reach hot parts. You might smell gas or see drips under the front of the car. A dealer will check the connection and tighten it, or replace the pipe if needed, at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious, because leaking gasoline can start a fire; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside, but get it inspected right away and stop driving if you smell fuel.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included (VINs have been searchable since April 11, 2026).
2. Call your Genesis dealer or Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741 and give them recall number 033G to book an appointment.
3. Have the dealer inspect and tighten or replace the fuel pipe; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Genesis) is recalling certain Genesis 2022-2026 GV70, 2023-2025 G90, 2021-2025 G80, and GV80 vehicles. Fuel may leak at the pipe connection between the fuel pipe and fuel rail.
Safety risk
A fuel leak increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and tighten, or replace the fuel pipe as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning July 20, 2026. Owners may contact Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741. Genesis' number for this recall is 033G. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning April 11, 2026.
