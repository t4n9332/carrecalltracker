Chevrolet Medium Duty Silverado Brake Sensor Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 25V390000 · Reported 2025-06-12 · General Motors, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

Owners are told to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. An electrical short that overheats the circuit can increase the risk of a fire while driving or parked.

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Chevrolet Silverado 4500HD, 5500HD, and 6500HD medium duty trucks have a brake pressure sensor assembly that may leak brake fluid into the nearby brake pressure switch. This leak can cause an electrical short circuit, which can overheat the circuit and increase the risk of a fire, whether the truck is being driven or is parked.

What you should do

1. Park outside and away from structures until the repair is complete.
2. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
3. Contact a Chevrolet dealer, or call GM customer assistance at 1-866-467-9700, for the free repair.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:BRAKE FLUID LOW WARNING:SWITCH/SENSOR/FLOAT
Summary
General Motors (GM) is recalling certain 2019-2024 Chevrolet Silverado Medium Duty 4500HD, 5500HD, and 6500HD vehicles. The brake pressure sensor assembly may leak brake fluid into the brake pressure switch and cause a short circuit.
Safety risk
An electrical short in the brake pressure switch can overheat the circuit and increase the risk of a fire while driving or parked.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. Dealers will replace the brake pressure switch wire harness, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 11, 2025. Owners may contact GM customer assistance number at 1-866-467-9700. GM's number for this recall is N242482680. Vehicles in this recall that were previously repaired under 23V266 will need to have the new remedy completed.
