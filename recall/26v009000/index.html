Rivian R1T and R1S Seat Belt Bolts Loose

NHTSA campaign 26V009000 · Reported 2026-01-15 · Rivian Automotive, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious for anyone riding in the second row: a seat belt whose retractor is not properly secured may not hold the person in that seat during a crash, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving the vehicle.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Rivian R1T pickups and R1S SUVs, the bolts that hold the second-row seat belt retractors — the spools the belts wind into on the driver and passenger sides — may not have been tightened enough. If a bolt is loose, that seat belt may not be held firmly in place. Rivian will inspect the bolts and tighten any that need it, free of charge, so the belts work the way they should.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is part of recall 26V009000.
2. Contact Rivian service to arrange the free inspection and bolt tightening, or call Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261 (recall number FSAM-1795).
3. Have the work completed at no cost and keep the confirmation.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022-2025 R1T and 2022-2026 R1S vehicles. The bolts that secure the second-row driver and passenger side seat belt retractor assemblies may be improperly tightened.
Safety risk
An improperly secured seat belt retractor may not adequately restrain the seat occupant, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will inspect and tighten the bolts as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 2, 2026. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1795.
