Rivian R1T and R1S Loose Second-Row Seat Belts

NHTSA campaign 26V009000 · Reported 2026-01-15 · Rivian Automotive, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

An improperly secured seat belt retractor may not properly restrain the person sitting there, which the notice says increases the risk of injury during a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In these Rivian vehicles, the bolts that secure the second-row seat belt retractor assemblies, on both the driver's side and the passenger's side, may not have been tightened properly. Because these bolts may be improperly tightened, the seat belt retractor assemblies in the second row may not be as securely attached as they are meant to be, on either side of the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Rivian dealer, or call Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261.
3. The inspection and any needed bolt tightening is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022-2025 R1T and 2022-2026 R1S vehicles. The bolts that secure the second-row driver and passenger side seat belt retractor assemblies may be improperly tightened.
Safety risk
An improperly secured seat belt retractor may not adequately restrain the seat occupant, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will inspect and tighten the bolts as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 2, 2026. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1795.
