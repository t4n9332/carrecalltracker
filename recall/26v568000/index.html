Ford F-650 liftgate platform can fall from broken pins

NHTSA campaign 26V568000 · Reported 2026-08-20 · Marathon Industries Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Ford F-650 SD

What's wrong, in plain English

Marathon Industries is recalling a single 2025 Ford F-650 SD work truck. The pins that hold the liftgate platform in place can break. If they do, the platform can drop without warning, and anyone standing near it or loading cargo could be hurt. Marathon will work with the equipment supplier to check the platform bushings and hydraulic parts and install new deck pins, free of charge.

How serious is it?

The danger here is injury from a platform that falls unexpectedly rather than a driving hazard, and no order to stop driving was given, but nobody should stand under or work near the liftgate until it is repaired.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is the one covered.
2. Contact Marathon Industries customer service at 1-661-286-1520 to arrange the inspection and new deck pins.
3. Watch for the owner notification letter expected in September 2026 — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:HATCHBACK/LIFTGATE:HINGE AND ATTACHMENTS
Summary
Marathon Industries Inc. (Marathon Industries) is recalling one 2025 Ford F-650 SD vehicle. The deck pins may break, resulting in the liftgate platform falling.
Safety risk
A liftgate that falls unexpectedly increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Marathon Industries will work with the supplier, Hiab, to inspect the platform bushings and hydraulic systems and replace the deck pins, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed September 2026. Owner may contact Marathon Industries' customer service at 1-661-286-1520.
