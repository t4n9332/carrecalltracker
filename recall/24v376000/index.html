Tesla Seat Belt Warning Chime May Not Activate

NHTSA campaign 24V376000 · Reported May 28, 2024 · Tesla, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

A seat belt warning system that doesn't alert the driver increases the risk of injury if a crash happens. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Tesla is recalling certain Model S, Model X, Model 3 and Model Y vehicles. If the driver is not wearing a seat belt, the seat belt warning light and audible chime may not turn on the way they are supposed to. This means the vehicle may fail to alert an unbelted driver that their seat belt is not fastened.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Tesla customer service (1-877-798-3752) with questions.
3. Tesla will send the fix as a free over-the-air software update.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V376000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:WARNING LIGHT/DEVICES
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2012-2024 Model S, 2015-2024 Model X, 2017-2023 Model 3, and 2020-2023 Model Y vehicles. In the event of an unbelted driver, the seat belt warning light and audible chime may not activate as intended. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
A seat belt warning system that fails to alert occupants of an unbelted seat belt can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Tesla will release an over-the-air (OTA) software update. Owner notification letters were mailed July 26, 2024. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-24-00-008. (over-the-air software update)
