Mercedes-Benz Metris fuel hoses can leak and catch fire

NHTSA campaign 22V134000 · Reported 2022-03-08 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

Very serious — a fuel leak can lead to a fire, so if you smell gasoline stop driving and have the van towed; otherwise get it to a dealer right away.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2017-2022 Mercedes-Benz Metris vans, the upper and lower fuel hoses may not be attached securely where they connect to the engine. A loose connection can let gasoline leak out. If that leaking fuel reaches something hot or a spark, it can start a fire. Dealers will inspect the fuel hose connections and repair them, replacing the clamps and the fuel hose itself if that is needed. Owner letters were mailed May 5, 2022.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is affected. 2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer or Daimler Vans USA at 1-877-762-8267 about recall VS2KRAUCH (22V134000). 3. Have the fuel hose connections inspected and repaired — the work is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2017-2022 Mercedes-Benz Metris vehicles. The connection of the upper and lower fuel hoses to the engine may be insecure, which can result in a fuel leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and repair the fuel hose connections, and replace the clamps and fuel hose, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 5, 2022. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS2KRAUCH.
