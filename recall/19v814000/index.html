Chevy Silverado and GMC Sierra Seat Belt Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 19V814000 · Reported 2019-11-14 · General Motors LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 1500
  • 2019 GMC Sierra 1500
  • 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 1500
  • 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 2500
  • 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 3500
  • 2020 GMC Sierra 1500
  • 2020 GMC Sierra 2500
  • 2020 GMC Sierra 3500

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra pickups with carpet floor covering have a problem with the front seat belt tensioners — the devices that yank the belt tight in a crash using a small charge of gas. Hot gas can escape through an opening in the tensioner's metal bracket and set the carpet on fire. That could start a fire inside the truck. Dealers will close off the opening in the bracket free of charge.

How serious is it?

Serious: the notice says a fire could start inside the vehicle if the carpet ignites, so while there is no instruction to park the truck or keep it outside, you should get the free fix promptly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is covered by recall 19V814000.
2. Schedule the repair with a Chevrolet or GMC dealer, or call Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC at 1-888-988-7267 (recall number N192270600).
3. Have the bracket opening sealed at no cost and keep the repair receipt.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:PRETENSIONER
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500, and 2020 Silverado 2500, Silverado 3500, Sierra 2500, and Sierra 3500 vehicles equipped with carpet floor covering. When the front seat belt pretensioners deploy, hot gas may vent through an opening in the pretensioner bracket, possibly igniting the carpet.
Safety risk
A vehicle fire could result if materials ignite inside the vehicle.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will close off the opening in the pretensioner bracket, free of charge. The recall began December 13, 2019. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-888-988-7267. GM's number for this recall is N192270600.
