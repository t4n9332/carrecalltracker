Silverado and Sierra Seat Belt Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 19V814000 · Reported 2019-11-14 · General Motors LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

The notice warns that a fire could result inside the vehicle if materials ignite. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

These trucks have carpet floor covering and front seat belt pretensioners. When a seat belt pretensioner deploys, it releases hot gas. In these vehicles, that hot gas may escape through an opening in the pretensioner's mounting bracket instead of staying contained. If that hot gas reaches the carpet inside the vehicle, it could ignite the carpet material and start a fire inside the cabin.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer at 1-800-222-1020, or a GMC dealer at 1-888-988-7267.
3. The repair (closing off the opening in the pretensioner bracket) is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:PRETENSIONER
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500, and 2020 Silverado 2500, Silverado 3500, Sierra 2500, and Sierra 3500 vehicles equipped with carpet floor covering. When the front seat belt pretensioners deploy, hot gas may vent through an opening in the pretensioner bracket, possibly igniting the carpet.
Safety risk
A vehicle fire could result if materials ignite inside the vehicle.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will close off the opening in the pretensioner bracket, free of charge. The recall began December 13, 2019. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-888-988-7267. GM's number for this recall is N192270600.
