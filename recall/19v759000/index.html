Dodge Grand Caravan rear seat anchors may fail in crash

NHTSA campaign 19V759000 · Reported 2019-10-24 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

Chrysler did not tell owners to stop driving, but a rear seat that comes loose in a crash puts passengers — often kids — at higher risk of injury, so get it repaired soon.

What's wrong, in plain English

The metal latches that anchor the rear seats to the floor are held on by welds that may not be strong enough. The welds on the outer second-row seats can fail in a front-end crash, and the ones on the third-row bench can fail in a rear-end crash. The second-row bench and bucket seats also may not hold up to the required loads, so a seat or its belt may not hold someone in place.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your 2019 Grand Caravan is included.
2. Call a Dodge dealer or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 and ask for recall V91.
3. The dealer will weld reinforcement brackets onto the second- and third-row seat strikers, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Dodge Grand Caravan vehicles. The welds on the outboard rear seat strikers for the second row bench and second row bucket seats may fail during a front impact crash. The front outboard seat strikers for the third row bench seats may fail in the event of a rear impact crash. In addition, the second row bench and second row bucket seats may not withstand the required loads.
Safety risk
If the seat strikers fail, the seat or seat belt may not adequately restrain the occupant, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will weld in a striker reinforcement bracket on the second and third row outboard seat strikers, free of charge. The recall began December 19, 2019. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's number for this recall is V91.
