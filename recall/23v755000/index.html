Subaru Crosstrek, Forester, Legacy, Outback reverse lights may fail

NHTSA campaign 23V755000 · Reported 2023-11-09 · Subaru of America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Subaru Crosstrek
  • 2021 Subaru Legacy
  • 2021 Subaru Outback
  • 2022 Subaru Forester
  • 2022 Subaru Legacy
  • 2022 Subaru Outback
  • 2023 Subaru Legacy
  • 2023 Subaru Outback

What's wrong, in plain English

A weld on a switch that tells the car which gear you are in was not done well enough, so water can seep inside and break it. When that switch fails, your reversing lights may not come on and the backup camera screen may go blank. That makes it easy to miss someone or something behind you while backing up. A dealer will replace the switch free of charge.

How serious is it?

The car is safe to keep driving, but with no reverse lights or camera you are much more likely to hit something while backing up, so be extra careful and schedule the fix.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is part of campaign 23V755000.
2. Contact your Subaru dealer for an appointment, or call Subaru customer service at 1-800-782-2783 (recall WRQ-23).
3. Have the inhibitor switch replaced — parts and labor are free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:PARK/NEUTRAL START INTERLOCK SWITCH
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. is recalling certain model year 2021 Crosstrek, 2022 Forester, 2021-2023 Legacy, and Outback vehicles. An insufficient weld may allow water to enter the inhibitor switch, causing it to fail.
Safety risk
An inoperative inhibitor switch may prevent the reverse lights from illuminating and the rearview camera image from displaying, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the inhibitor switch, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 8, 2024. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-800-782-2783. Subaru's number for this recall is WRQ-23.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
