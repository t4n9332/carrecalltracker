Genesis GV70 May Shift From Park to Neutral

NHTSA campaign 24V205000 · Reported Mar 15, 2024 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

An unexpected shift from Park to Neutral can let the vehicle roll away on its own, increasing the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Genesis GV70 vehicles may allow water to leak into the wiring harness for the transmission control system. If that happens, it can cause a short circuit inside the harness. This short circuit may cause the transmission to unexpectedly shift out of Park and into Neutral by itself, even though the driver has not touched the gear selector or done anything to cause the shift.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Until it is repaired, turn off the ignition and set the emergency parking brake every time you park. 3. Contact a dealer to schedule the free repair, or call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V205000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2024 Genesis GV70 vehicles. Water may leak into the transmission control harness, which can cause a short circuit and unexpectedly shift the transmission from Park to Neutral.
Safety risk
An unexpected shift from Park to Neutral can cause a vehicle roll away and increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to turn off the ignition and use the emergency parking brake after parking their vehicles. Dealers will inspect the transmission control harness and repair the connector, or replace the harness and internal wiring, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 9, 2024. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 020G.
