Ford Fusion and Lincoln MKZ front brake hoses can leak

NHTSA campaign 23V162000 · Reported 2023-03-10 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Ford Fusion
  • 2018 Lincoln MKZ

What's wrong, in plain English

The front brake hoses on certain Ford Fusion and Lincoln MKZ cars can split and leak brake fluid. As fluid escapes, the brake pedal goes farther down toward the floor and the car needs a longer distance to stop. A pedal that feels soft or sinks lower than usual is the warning sign to watch for. Ford dealers will replace both front brake hoses. Ford mailed letters to owners in June 2023 and the work is free.

How serious is it?

Serious, because longer stopping distances can lead to a crash; you can generally keep driving, but if the pedal feels soft or sinks, stop driving and have the car towed in.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is covered. 2. Contact your Ford or Lincoln dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 23S12), to schedule the repair. 3. Have both front brake hoses replaced at no cost.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2013-2018 Fusion and Lincoln MKZ vehicles. The front brake hoses may rupture and leak brake fluid.
Safety risk
A brake fluid leak will increase brake pedal travel and extend the distance needed to stop the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front brake hoses, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 27, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23S12.
