Jeep Cherokee turn signals may not shut off automatically

NHTSA campaign 24V239000 · Reported 2024-03-28 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) (Stellantis) · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Jeep Cherokee

What's wrong, in plain English

After you finish a turn, your blinker is supposed to click off by itself. On some 2022 Jeep Cherokees, it may not. That means the signal can keep flashing while you are driving straight, or act in ways other drivers do not expect. When your blinker does not match what you are actually doing, drivers around you cannot tell which way you plan to go, and that can cause a crash. Dealers will check the steering column control module and replace it if needed.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the vehicle is safe to drive, but until it is fixed, watch your blinker and turn it off by hand after every turn.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Cherokee is covered.
2. Contact your Jeep dealer or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall 24B) to schedule the inspection.
3. Get the control module checked or replaced at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TURN SIGNAL
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Jeep Cherokee vehicles.  The turn signal self-canceling feature may not function properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Turn signals that do not function properly may fail to indicate the driver's intention to change vehicle direction, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the steering column control module as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 8, 2024. Owners may contact FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's number for this recall is 24B.
