Ford Transit, Ram ProMaster Vans Have Seat Belt Issue

NHTSA campaign 20V222000 · Reported Apr 20, 2020 · TransitWorks

medium How serious is it? About this label

A seat belt that does not properly secure an occupant increases the risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

TransitWorks says some vans built on Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster chassis, equipped with certain foldaway seats, may have seat belt retractors that were not set correctly. Because of this, the seat belt in an affected seat may not properly hold the occupant in place.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a dealer to schedule the free retractor replacement; dealers can reach Freedman Seating Company for parts at 1-800-443-4540.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V222000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:RETRACTOR
Summary
TransitWorks is recalling certain Driverge vehicles based on 2019-2020 Ford Transit vans equipped with Freedman Seating Company (FSC) PT Foldaway and GO-ES 3-point Foldaway seats. The seat belt retractor block out zone may have not been set correctly, potentially resulting in a seat belt that may not properly secure the occupant. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 209, "Seat Belt Assemblies."
Safety risk
An unsecured occupant has an increased risk of injury in the event of a crash.
Remedy
TransitWorks will notify owners, and dealers will replace the retractors, free of charge. The recall began May 13, 2020. Dealers can contact Freedman Seating Company for parts and reimbursement information at 1-800-443-4540.
