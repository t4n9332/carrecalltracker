Cadillac XT6 brake fluid cap missing its warning label

NHTSA campaign 22V392000 · Reported 2022-06-02 · General Motors, LLC · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Cadillac XT6

What's wrong, in plain English

Under the hood, the cap on the brake fluid tank is missing its printed warning label. That label is what warns against letting dirt or other debris get into the fluid when the cap is opened. Without it, the brake fluid could be contaminated without anyone realizing. Dirty brake fluid can affect how well the brakes work. Federal brake rules require the label, so these cars don't meet the standard as built.

How serious is it?

This is a low-level labeling problem with no park-it warning, so the vehicle is safe to keep driving — just avoid opening the brake fluid cap yourself until the dealer replaces it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your XT6 is covered.
2. Contact your Cadillac dealer, or Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 (recall N222369130), to have the cap inspected.
3. The dealer replaces the cap if needed, at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FLUID
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2022 Cadillac XT6 vehicles. The brake fluid reservoir cap may be missing the warning label. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 135, "Light Vehicle Brake Systems."
Safety risk
Without a warning label, the brake fluid may be unintentionally contaminated by debris, which can affect brake performance and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the brake fluid reservoir cap and replace it, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 30, 2022. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N222369130.
