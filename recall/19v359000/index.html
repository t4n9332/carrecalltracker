Dodge 5500 fire truck brake lights turn on wrongly

NHTSA campaign 19V359000 · Reported 2019-05-13 · Unruh Fire, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 RAM 5500

What's wrong, in plain English

This recall covers one 2018 Dodge 5500-based fire truck built by Unruh Fire with Whelen brake lights. Depending on how that truck was wired, the brake lights may light up even when the driver has not pressed the brake pedal. Other drivers may believe the truck is braking when it is not. Unruh Fire has notified the owner and will provide replacement brake lights at no charge. The recall began May 20, 2019.

How serious is it?

A moderate risk, since false brake lights can confuse drivers behind the truck and raise the chance of a crash, but the notice does not tell the owner to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm the truck is covered. 2. Contact Unruh Fire customer service at 1-800-856-7080 about the replacement brake lights. 3. Get the lights replaced — they are provided free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
Unruh Fire, Inc. (Unruh Fire) is recalling one 2018 Dodge 5500-based fire truck equipped with Whelen brake lights. Depending on the specific wiring of the vehicle, these brake lights may illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed.
Safety risk
If the brake lights incorrectly illuminate, it can confuse other drivers, possibly increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Unruh Fire has notified the owner and will provide replacement brake lights, free of charge. The recall began May 20, 2019. Owners may contact Unruh Fire customer service at 1-800-856-7080.
