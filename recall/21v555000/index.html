Nissan Rogue Sport Seat Belt May Release Early

NHTSA campaign 21V555000 · Reported Jul 21, 2021 · Nissan North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

A seat belt that doesn't lock a child seat properly can increase the risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The front passenger seat belt's automatic locking feature, which is used to hold a child seat tightly in place, may switch off earlier than it should. When that happens, the seat belt doesn't hold a child seat as securely as it's supposed to.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Nissan dealer to have the front passenger seat belt, and both rear outer seat belts if needed, replaced (customer service: 1-800-867-7669).
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V555000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:RETRACTOR
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2021 Rogue Sport vehicles. The seat belt automatic locking retractor (ALR) may deactivate early, reducing the protection offered to an installed child seat. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
A seat belt retractor that does not function as intended can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front passenger seat belt and as necessary both outer rear seat belts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on September 14, 2021. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is RT049 & RT050.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
