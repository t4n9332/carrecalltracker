Kia EV6 and EV9 battery cells can catch fire

NHTSA campaign 26V431000 · Reported 2026-07-02 · Kia America, Inc. · critical severity

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Kia EV6
  • 2023 Kia EV6
  • 2024 Kia EV6
  • 2024 Kia EV9

What's wrong, in plain English

Some of the cells inside the big battery that powers your EV were built with parts slightly out of line. A cell like that can overheat and start a fire, whether the vehicle is parked or driving. Until the repair is done, Kia says to park outside, away from buildings, and to charge the battery no higher than 80 percent. Dealers will replace the entire high-voltage battery pack at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Very serious: a battery fire can happen parked or on the road, so park outside and keep the charge under 80 percent, though the notice does not tell you to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your EV6 or EV9 is included. 2. Park outside away from structures and set your charge limit to 80 percent. 3. Contact your Kia dealer or Kia at 1-800-333-4542 (recall SC375) to arrange the free battery replacement.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2022-2024 EV6 and 2024 EV9 vehicles. The high voltage battery cells may contain misaligned electrodes, which can lead to a fire while parked or driving.
Safety risk
A fire increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures and limit their charge to a maximum of 80% until the recall repair is complete. Dealers will replace the high voltage battery system assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 7, 2026. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC375. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning July 17, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
