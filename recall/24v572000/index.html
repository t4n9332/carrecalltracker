What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain 2021 Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator vehicles that were already repaired once under an earlier recall. The software that controls the powertrain may cause the engine to stall, which can take away the vehicle's ability to keep driving under its own power. This recall provides an updated software fix for vehicles that still need it, including ones that received the previous repair.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact FCA US (Chrysler) customer service at 1-800-853-1403 or your dealer to schedule the software update.
3. If your vehicle was already repaired under recall 23V-116, it will need this new update too; the repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V572000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:MANUAL TRANSMISSION:CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2021 Jeep Wrangler and Jeep Gladiator vehicles that were previously remedied under NHTSA recall number 23V-116. The software for the powertrain control module may cause an engine stall, which can result in a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the powertrain control module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 15, 2024. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US LLC's number for this recall is 92B. Vehicles previously repaired under recall 23V-116 will need to have the new remedy completed.
