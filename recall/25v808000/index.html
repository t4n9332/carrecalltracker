What's wrong, in plain English

Hyundai is recalling certain Santa Fe and Santa Fe Hybrid vehicles. The rearview camera may not have been installed correctly. A camera fitted the wrong way can damage the wiring that connects it, and the camera image may then fail to appear on the screen. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for rear visibility. Dealers will replace the camera wiring harness.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Hyundai dealer to schedule the repair, or call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460.
3. The replacement wiring harness is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V808000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:DISPLAY FUNCTION
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Santa Fe and Santa Fe Hybrid vehicles. The rearview camera may not have been installed properly, which can damage the wiring harness and cause the rearview camera image not to display. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview image that does not display reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the camera wiring harness, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning January 15, 2026. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 288. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on November 22, 2025.
