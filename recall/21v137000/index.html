Kia Sportage and Cadenza brake control unit fire risk

NHTSA campaign 21V137000 · Reported 2021-03-04 · Kia Motors America · critical severity

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Kia Cadenza
  • 2018 Kia Sportage
  • 2019 Kia Cadenza
  • 2019 Kia Sportage
  • 2020 Kia Sportage
  • 2021 Kia Sportage

What's wrong, in plain English

The control unit for the anti-lock brakes has an electrical circuit that can short out. If it does, it can start a fire in the engine compartment. Kia says park outside and away from buildings as a precaution until the repair is done. Dealers replace certain fuses in the electrical box under the hood, and cars with an electronic parking brake also get updated software for the brake control unit. All of the work is free.

How serious is it?

Serious — an engine compartment fire can cause injury, so as a precaution park outdoors away from buildings until the free repair is complete.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Park outside and away from structures as a precaution.
3. Contact your Kia dealer or Kia at 1-800-333-4542 (recall SC206) for the free fuse replacement and, if applicable, the software update.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:ANTILOCK/TRACTION CONTROL/ELECTRONIC LIMITED SLIP:CONTROL UNIT/MODULE
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) is recalling certain 2017-2021 Sportage and Cadenza vehicles. The electrical circuit in the Hydraulic Electronic Control Unit (HECU) may short-circuit, which can cause a fire in the engine compartment.
Safety risk
An engine compartment fire can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures as a precaution until the recall repair is complete. Kia will notify owners, and dealers will replace certain fuses in the electrical junction box. Vehicles equipped with an electronic parking brake (EPB) will also receive a HECU software update. Repairs will be performed free of charge. The recall began on April 30, 2021. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC206.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
