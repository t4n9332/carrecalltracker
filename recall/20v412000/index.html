Nissan Leaf Backup Camera Image May Disappear

NHTSA campaign 20V412000 · Reported Jul 17, 2020 · Nissan North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

The lack of an image in the backup camera display increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2020 Nissan LEAF electric vehicles, the vehicle's backup camera system is designed to show the driver an image of what is behind the car while backing up. On affected vehicles, that camera image can disappear from the display while the vehicle is in reverse. This means that, under certain circumstances, the driver may temporarily lose the rearview image they would normally rely on while backing up.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
3. Dealers will update the backup camera software, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V412000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020 LEAF electric vehicles. Under certain circumstances, the images for the back-up camera can disappear while in reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
The lack of an image in the back-up camera display increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will update the back-up camera software, free of charge. This recall began August 31, 2020. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
