Nissan LEAF back-up camera image can disappear

NHTSA campaign 20V412000 · Reported 2020-07-17 · Nissan North America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Nissan Leaf

What's wrong, in plain English

When you shift into reverse, the picture from the back-up camera can vanish from the dashboard screen under certain conditions. That leaves you backing up with no camera view of what is behind you, which federal safety rules require the car to provide. Nothing is physically broken — the problem is in the camera's software, and a Nissan dealer reloads corrected software at no cost. The recall began August 31, 2020.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the car drives normally, so it is safe to keep driving, but back up slowly and check over your shoulder and in your mirrors until the software is updated.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your LEAF is included.
2. Call your Nissan dealer, or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669, and book the camera software update.
3. Have the update done — the repair is completely free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020 LEAF electric vehicles. Under certain circumstances, the images for the back-up camera can disappear while in reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
The lack of an image in the back-up camera display increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will update the back-up camera software, free of charge. This recall began August 31, 2020. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
