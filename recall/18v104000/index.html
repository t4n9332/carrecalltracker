Ford Escape and Lincoln MKC brake fluid may leak

NHTSA campaign 18V104000 · Reported 2018-02-12 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Ford Escape
  • 2018 Lincoln MKC

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2018 Escape and MKC vehicles left the factory with the wrong front brake hoses. Because they are not the right ones, they can rub against nearby parts until they wear through and start leaking brake fluid. Losing brake fluid means your brakes get weaker and the vehicle takes longer to stop than you expect. Dealers will look at the hoses and put in correct ones wherever the wrong parts were used.

How serious is it?

Weakened brakes are a real crash risk, so get this checked quickly and stop driving if the pedal feels soft or goes low.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is covered.
2. Contact your dealer or Ford at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 18S04) and ask for a prompt appointment.
3. Have the brake hoses inspected and replaced if needed; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2018 Ford Escape and Lincoln MKC vehicles. These vehicles may be equipped with incorrect front brake hoses that may chafe against other components, possibly resulting in a brake fluid leak.
Safety risk
A brake fluid leak may lengthen the distance needed to stop the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the brake hoses, replacing any that are incorrect, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin March 2018. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 18S04.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
