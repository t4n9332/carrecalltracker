NHTSA campaign 19V086000 · Reported Feb 7, 2019 · General Motors LLC

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Cadillac CTS, Cadillac ATS, Chevrolet Camaro, and Chevrolet Corvette vehicles because the electric power steering assist system may fail. Power steering assist normally makes it easier to turn the steering wheel. If the assist fails, steering the car will take noticeably more effort than usual, and this increase in effort is greater at lower speeds.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is included.
2. Contact your GM dealer to schedule the repair.
3. The repair, replacing the power steering gear assembly, is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V086000. Official notice

Component
STEERING:ELECTRIC POWER ASSIST SYSTEM
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Cadillac CTS, and 2017 Cadillac ATS, Chevrolet Camaro, and Chevrolet Corvette vehicles. The electric power steering (EPS) assist system may fail.
Safety risk
Loss of power steering assist would require a higher steering effort, especially at lower speeds, which may increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace the power steering gear assembly, free of charge. The recall began June 13, 2019. GM' number for this recall is N182182760.
