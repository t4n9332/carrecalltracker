What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Cadillac LYRIQ, CELESTIQ and VISTIQ vehicles and Chevrolet Blazer EVs because of a software defect in the unit that controls the brakes. Under certain driving conditions, this defect may cause a loss of braking ability. The recall repair is a free software update, which can be delivered wirelessly or performed by a dealer for affected vehicles.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a dealer about the recall. For help, call Cadillac at 1-800-333-4223 or Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020.
3. Have the brake control software updated wirelessly or by a dealer. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V717000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:ANTILOCK/TRACTION CONTROL/ELECTRONIC LIMITED SLIP:CONTROL UNIT/MODULE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023–2027 Cadillac LYRIQ, 2025–2026 model year Cadillac CELESTIQ, 2024–2027 Chevrolet Blazer EV, and 2026-2027 Cadillac VISTIQ vehicles. The brake system control module (BSCM) contains a software defect that may cause a loss of braking during certain driving conditions.
Safety risk
A loss of braking ability increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The BSCM software will be updated through an over-the-air (OTA) update or by a dealer, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed December 14, 2026. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N262570900. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on October 29, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
