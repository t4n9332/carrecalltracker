Subaru Crosstrek Rear Window Glass May Shatter

NHTSA campaign 19V404000 · Reported May 30, 2019 · Subaru of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Glass that shatters into large pieces raises the risk of injury to anyone in the vehicle. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2019 Subaru Crosstrek vehicles, the window glass in the driver's side rear door may not have been properly tempered during manufacturing. This means the vehicle does not meet the federal safety standard covering glazing materials, the glass used in vehicle windows. If this glass isn't properly tempered, it could shatter into large pieces, which increases the risk of injury to anyone in the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is part of this recall.
2. Contact a Subaru dealer, or call Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614 (recall number WUG-92).
3. The dealer will replace the driver's side rear-door window glass at no cost.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V404000. Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:GLASS, SIDE/REAR
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2019 Crosstrek vehicles. The driver's side rear-door window glass may be improperly tempered. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 205, "Glazing Materials."
Safety risk
Improperly tempered glass can shatter into large pieces, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Subaru will notify owners, and dealers will replace the driver's side rear-door window glass, free of charge. The recall began June 11, 2019. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WUG-92.
