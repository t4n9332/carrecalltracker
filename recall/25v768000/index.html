What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Silverado 4500HD, 5500HD, and 6500HD trucks. These vehicles already had a brake wiring fix done under an earlier recall, but the wire harness used in that repair was installed without protective tape. Without this tape, the wires can become damaged. Because of this, GM says these trucks need to have that earlier repair redone, with the tape properly added before the wiring harness is put back in place.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is included.
2. Contact your GM dealer, or call GM customer assistance at 1-866-467-9700.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V768000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:POWER ASSIST
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Chevrolet Silverado Medium Duty 4500HD, 5500HD, and 6500HD vehicles. These vehicles were remedied under NHTSA Recall 25V390 with a brake jumper wire harness that was assembled without protective tape. A wiring harness that is missing protective tape can allow the wires to become damaged.
Safety risk
Damaged brake jumper wires can result in power brake assist failure, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will remove the installed wire harness, apply protective tape, and reinstall the wire harness, free of charge. Owner notification letters are planned to be mailed December 22, 2025. Owners may contact GM customer assistance number at 1-866-467-9700. GM's number for this recall is N252531990. Vehicles in this recall were previously repaired under 25V390 and will need to have the new remedy completed. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning November 6, 2025.
