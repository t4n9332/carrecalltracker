Ford Super Duty tailgate can open on its own

NHTSA campaign 19V864000 · Reported 2019-12-04 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Ford F-250 SD
  • 2019 Ford F-350 SD
  • 2019 Ford F-450 SD

What's wrong, in plain English

These trucks have a button in the tailgate handle that releases the tailgate electrically. Water can get into the wiring behind it and cause a short circuit, which acts like someone pressing that button. The tailgate can then drop open by itself, including while you are driving. Anything loose in the bed can slide out onto the road behind you and become a hazard for the drivers following you.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious — the truck is safe to drive, but avoid hauling loose cargo in the bed until the wiring and handle switch are replaced.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is covered. 2. Schedule the free repair at a Ford dealer — they add wiring pigtails and install a new tailgate handle switch. 3. Call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 and reference recall 19S48 with any questions.

Official notice

Component
LATCHES/LOCKS/LINKAGES:TAILGATE:LATCH
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Super Duty F-250, F-350, and F-450 trucks equipped with an electric tailgate latch release switch mounted in the tailgate handle. Water may enter the electrical wiring system and cause a short circuit, activating the switch and releasing the tailgate latches.
Safety risk
Unintended tailgate opening may allow unrestrained cargo to fall out of the truck bed and become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will modify the tailgate/frame wiring harnesses by adding jumper pigtails to isolate the tailgate release control circuits and will install a new tailgate handle release switch, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin January 20, 2020. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 19S48.
