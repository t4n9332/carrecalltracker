NHTSA campaign 25V356000 · Reported May 29, 2025 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

What's wrong, in plain English

The driver's video display may suddenly go blank while you're driving. That screen shows things like the speedometer, warning lights, and the rearview camera image, so losing it means losing that information while on the road. Dealers will update the display's software, or it can be updated automatically over the air, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Cadillac dealer or customer service at 1-800-333-4223.
3. The software update is free and may be done at the dealer or over the air.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V356000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:ELECTRICAL:INFOTAINMENT:VIDEO (TOUCH)SCREEN/MONITOR/UNIT
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023 – 2024 model year Cadillac Lyriq vehicles. The driver video display may go blank while driving.
Safety risk
A blank video display can result in a loss of critical safety information, such as the speedometer, warning lights, and rearview camera image, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The video display control module software will be updated by a dealer, or through an over-the-air (OTA) update, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed July 14, 2025. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223. GM's number for this recall is N252500680.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
