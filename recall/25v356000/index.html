Cadillac Lyriq driver display may go blank while driving

NHTSA campaign 25V356000 · Reported 2025-05-29 · General Motors, LLC · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Cadillac Lyriq
  • 2024 Cadillac Lyriq

What's wrong, in plain English

The screen in front of the driver can suddenly go dark while you are driving. When it does, you lose the information it shows — your speed, the warning lights that tell you something is wrong with the car, and the backup camera picture when you shift into reverse. Driving without that information makes a crash more likely. GM will fix it with new software for the display's control module, installed either at a dealer or sent to your car over the air.

How serious is it?

Losing your speedometer, warning lights, and backup camera view increases crash risk; the notice does not tell you to stop driving, but get the update as soon as it is available.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Lyriq is included.
2. Watch for an over-the-air update on your car's screen, or contact your Cadillac dealer.
3. If you need help, call Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223 (recall N252500680) — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:ELECTRICAL:INFOTAINMENT:VIDEO (TOUCH)SCREEN/MONITOR/UNIT
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023 – 2024 model year Cadillac Lyriq vehicles. The driver video display may go blank while driving.
Safety risk
A blank video display can result in a loss of critical safety information, such as the speedometer, warning lights, and rearview camera image, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The video display control module software will be updated by a dealer, or through an over-the-air (OTA) update, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed July 14, 2025. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223. GM's number for this recall is N252500680.
