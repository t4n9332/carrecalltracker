What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2024 Kia EV9 vehicles, a software error can affect the remote smart parking assist feature, which lets the driver move the vehicle remotely without being inside it. Because of this error, the feature may miscalculate how much distance the vehicle needs in order to come to a stop. This means the vehicle may not stop exactly where the software expects it to while being operated remotely.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542.
3. Dealers will update the integrated electronic brake software, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V693000. Official notice

Component

Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2024 EV9 vehicles. A software error may cause the remote smart parking assist feature, which allows the operator to remotely move the vehicle, to miscalculate stopping distances.
Safety risk
A remote smart parking assist feature that miscalculates stopping distances may increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the integrated electronic brake software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 4, 2024. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC324.
