Ram 1500 auxiliary battery connection can overheat, risking fire

NHTSA campaign 19V142000 · Reported 2019-02-28 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

critical How serious is it? About this label

This is serious because a fire can start inside the cabin even with the ignition off, so get the free repair done right away, and in the meantime park away from your home or garage if you can.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2019 Ram 1500 trucks have an extra 48-volt battery mounted behind the rear seat. Because of a mistake during an assembly repair, the wire connection on that battery may not be tight enough, and a loose connection can get very hot. The heat can start a fire inside the cab, and it can happen even when the truck is turned off. Dealers will properly tighten and secure the battery fasteners at no cost.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included. 2. Call your Ram dealer now and ask for recall V17, or contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. 3. Have the dealer secure the battery fasteners — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Ram 1500 vehicles equipped with a 48v auxiliary battery system located behind the rear seat (sales code: XBZ). Due to an incorrect assembly repair process, the auxiliary battery wire terminal connection may overheat.
Safety risk
If the auxiliary battery system overheats, there is an increased risk of a fire within the cabin even if the ignition is off.
Remedy
FCA US has notified owners, and dealers will properly secure the auxiliary battery fasteners, free of charge. The recall began March 15, 2019. Owners may contact Chrysler's customer service at 1-800-853-1403. The Chrysler number for this recall is V17.
