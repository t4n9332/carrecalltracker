Kia Stinger brake control unit can cause engine fire

NHTSA campaign 20V518000 · Reported 2020-08-27 · Kia Motors America · critical severity

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Kia Stinger
  • 2019 Kia Stinger
  • 2020 Kia Stinger
  • 2021 Kia Stinger

What's wrong, in plain English

A fire can start under the hood, in the area around the control box for the anti-lock brakes. It can happen while you are driving. Kia first announced this in 2020 and later added more Stingers to the list. The fix is a new fuse kit installed in the electrical box under the hood, which is free. Until that work is done, Kia says to park outside and away from buildings, so a fire cannot spread to your house or garage.

How serious is it?

An under-hood fire while driving could injure you or lead to a crash; the notice does not tell you to stop driving, but you should park outside and away from structures until the fuse kit is installed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Stinger is covered.
2. Park outside and away from buildings until the repair is finished.
3. Call a Kia dealer, or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542 (recall SC196), to schedule the free fuse kit installation.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:ANTILOCK/TRACTION CONTROL/ELECTRONIC LIMITED SLIP:CONTROL UNIT/MODULE
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) recalled certain 2019 Stinger vehicles equipped with 3.3L T-GDI engines on August 27, 2020. On December 30, 2020, Kia expanded the recall population and added certain 2018-2021 Stinger vehicles. While driving, an engine compartment fire may occur in the area where the Anti-lock Brake Hydraulic Electronic Control Unit (HECU) is located.
Safety risk
An engine compartment fire can increase the risk of injury or crash.
Remedy
Kia will notify owners, and dealers will install a new fuse kit in the electrical junction box, free of charge. Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures as a precaution until the recall repair is complete. The recall began November 30, 2020. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC196.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
