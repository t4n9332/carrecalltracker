NHTSA campaign 18V118000 · Reported Feb 17, 2018 · Hyundai Motor America

What's wrong, in plain English

The steering wheel assembly on some Hyundai Santa Fe and Santa Fe Sport vehicles may break. If it breaks, the steering wheel could separate from the steering column while you are driving. Losing the steering wheel while driving would mean losing control of the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash. This is a serious mechanical failure that affects your ability to steer safely.

What you should do

1) Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2) Contact a Hyundai dealer, or call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. 3) The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V118000. Official notice

Component
STEERING: STEERING WHEEL/HANDLE BAR
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2018 Hyundai Santa Fe and Santa Fe Sport vehicles. The steering wheel assembly may break, possibly resulting in the steering wheel separating from the steering column while driving.
Safety risk
A steering wheel separation while driving can cause a loss of vehicle control and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Hyundai will notify owners, and dealers will check the production lot number of the steering wheel assembly, replacing it as necessary, free of charge. The recall began on March 26, 2018. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 173.
