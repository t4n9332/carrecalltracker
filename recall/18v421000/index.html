Honda Civic Certification Label May Show Wrong Date

NHTSA campaign 18V421000 · Reported Jun 20, 2018 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

low How serious is it? About this label

Not being able to verify whether your vehicle is part of a recall can increase the risk of injury or crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The manufacturing date area on some Civic certification labels may show random characters instead of the correct information. That label is used to check whether a vehicle is covered by a safety recall, so having the wrong information there can make it hard for an owner to tell if their vehicle is included in a recall.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Honda dealer to have the certification label replaced; call 1-888-234-2138.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V421000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2018 Honda Civic vehicles. The manufacturing date area of the certification labels may have random characters which can affect the owner's ability to determine if a safety recall includes their vehicle.
Safety risk
If the owner is not able to verify if their vehicle is involved in safety recall, it can increase the risk of injury or crash.
Remedy
Honda will notify owners, and dealers will replace the certification label, free of charge. The recall began August 7, 2018. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is U23.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
