Chevrolet Traverse, Buick Enclave airbags may not deploy properly

NHTSA campaign 21V472000 · Reported 2021-06-24 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious: the vehicle drives normally and you may keep driving it, but if the front airbags do not deploy as designed in a crash, injuries can be worse — so have the software updated promptly.

What's wrong, in plain English

While these SUVs were in for service at a dealer, some of them had the wrong software loaded into the system that controls the front airbags. That software decides how the airbags fire in a front-end crash. With the wrong version in place, the front airbags may not go off the way they are supposed to in a crash. There is no warning light or symptom; a dealer loads the correct software.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is part of recall N212340220.
2. Call your Chevrolet dealer (1-800-222-1020) or Buick dealer (1-800-521-7300) for an appointment.
3. Have the airbag software updated — the update is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SENSOR:FRONTAL IMPACT
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2021 Chevrolet Traverse and Buick Enclave vehicles. During service by dealers, certain vehicles may have received incorrect air bag calibration software that may affect frontal airbag deployment.
Safety risk
Air bags that do not deploy as intended can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will update the air bag calibration software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on July 29, 2021. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 and Buick customer service at 1-800-521-7300. GM's number for this recall is N212340220.
