Ford Super Duty trucks may lose steering control

NHTSA campaign 25V626000 · Reported 2025-09-19 · Ford Motor Company

critical How serious is it? About this label

Losing steering control is extremely dangerous and can happen without warning, so have this checked right away, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain F-250, F-350 and F-450 Super Duty trucks, the upper shaft in the steering column can come apart from the column itself. If that happens, turning the steering wheel may no longer steer the truck. Dealers will inspect the steering column and repair or replace the upper shaft if it needs it, at no cost to you. Ford mailed owner notification letters on February 20, 2026, and this repair should not be put off.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — these VINs became searchable September 23, 2025. 2. Call your Ford dealer and schedule the steering column inspection using Ford recall number 25S94. 3. Have the free repair completed; Ford customer service is at 1-866-436-7332.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:COLUMN
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2021 F-250, F-350, and F-450 vehicles. The steering column upper shaft may detach from the steering column and result in a loss of steering control.
Safety risk
A loss of steering control increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the steering column, and repair or replace the upper shaft as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 20, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S94. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning September 23, 2025.
