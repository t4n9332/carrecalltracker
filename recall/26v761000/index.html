Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022-2027 R1S, R1T, and 2027 R2 vehicles. The tire placard and/or certification label may not correctly list the wheel, tire, and spare tire configuration of the vehicle. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
The tires may be improperly inflated and/or the wrong size replacement tire may be installed after referencing the incorrect information, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will replace the tire placard and/or certification labels, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed January 18, 2027. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1900. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov on January 18, 2027.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
