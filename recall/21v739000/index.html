Chevrolet Tahoe, Suburban, Escalade, Yukon fuel pump can stall engine

NHTSA campaign 21V739000 · Reported 2021-09-23 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious — an unexpected engine stall increases the risk of a crash, so have it done soon, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

A control module that runs the fuel pump can fail or work only part of the time. When it does, fuel stops flowing steadily to the engine and the engine can quit without warning while you are driving. Losing the engine in traffic makes a crash more likely. Dealers will replace the fuel pump control module at no charge. Owner letters went out back in October 2021, so if you bought the vehicle used, the repair may still be waiting.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this repair (GM number N212332040) is still open on your vehicle.
2. Contact your dealer — Chevrolet 1-800-222-1020, Cadillac 1-800-458-8006, or GMC 1-800-462-8782 — and schedule the work.
3. The replacement fuel pump power control module is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP:CONTROL/DRIVE MODULE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2021 Cadillac Escalade, Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Suburban, Tahoe, GMC Yukon, and Yukon XL vehicles. The fuel pump power control module may cause the fuel pump to fail or operate intermittently, which can interrupt fuel flow to the engine, and cause an unexpected engine stall.
Safety risk
An engine stall increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump power control module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on October 14, 2021. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020; Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006; or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N212332040.
