Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, RAM may not stay in Park

NHTSA campaign 18V280000 · Reported 2018-05-01 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Chrysler 300
  • 2018 Dodge Challenger
  • 2018 Dodge Charger
  • 2018 Dodge Durango
  • 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee
  • 2018 Jeep Wrangler
  • 2018 RAM 1500

What's wrong, in plain English

The wrong part may have been installed inside the automatic transmission. It is a rod that locks the transmission when you move the shifter into Park. If the wrong rod is in there, the transmission may not actually lock, so the vehicle can roll on its own after you think you have parked it. That could hurt someone standing nearby or cause a crash. A dealer installs the correct rod for free.

How serious is it?

This is serious because the vehicle can move when you expect it to be parked; the notice does not say to stop driving, but get the free repair done promptly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact your dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall U43), to schedule the work. 3. Have the correct park lock rod installed — there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018 Dodge Charger, Durango and Challenger, Jeep Grand Cherokee and Wrangler, Chrysler 300, and RAM 1500 vehicles. An incorrect transmission park lock rod may have been installed in the transmission.
Safety risk
If the incorrect park lock rod is installed, the transmission may not shift into 'PARK' and keep the vehicle from moving, increasing the risk of unintended vehicle movement and the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will install the correct park lock rod, free of charge. The recall began June 28, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U43.
