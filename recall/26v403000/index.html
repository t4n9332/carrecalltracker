Ford Bronco fender flares can fall off while driving

NHTSA campaign 26V403000 · Reported 2026-06-24 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Ford Bronco
  • 2023 Ford Bronco
  • 2024 Ford Bronco

What's wrong, in plain English

The fender flares are the plastic trim pieces that arch over each wheel. On these Broncos they may not be fastened properly and can come off while you are driving. A flare that falls onto the road becomes an obstacle for the vehicles behind you, and swerving to avoid it can cause a crash. A dealer will look at the flares and either re-secure them or put on new ones at no charge.

How serious is it?

This is a moderate risk that mostly endangers other drivers behind you rather than the people in your Bronco, and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Bronco is on the list.
2. Contact your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and ask about recall 26S47.
3. Have the dealer inspect and repair or replace the fender flares — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022-2026 Bronco vehicles. The fender flares may not be properly secured and detach from the vehicle.
Safety risk
A detached fender flare can become a road hazard and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and repair, or replace the fender flares as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 1, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S47. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning August 7, 2026.
