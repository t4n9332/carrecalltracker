Mercedes-Benz GLA 250 door may not open from inside

NHTSA campaign 26V134000 · Reported 2026-03-06 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2026 Mercedes-Benz GLA 250

What's wrong, in plain English

A cable inside the door, called a bowden cable, may have been installed incorrectly. When it is wrong, the door cannot be opened using the inside handle. That means someone in the car could be stuck and unable to get out on their own, which Mercedes-Benz says can increase the risk of injury. A dealer will inspect the cable assembly and repair it if needed, at no cost. Owner notification letters were mailed May 1, 2026.

How serious is it?

It does not affect how the car drives, so it is safe to keep driving, but a trapped occupant is a real hazard in an emergency — test your inside door handles and get the repair done soon.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls (VINs are searchable as of March 13, 2026). 2. Contact your Mercedes-Benz dealer, or MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. 3. Have the door cable inspected and repaired — free of charge.
