Jeep Cherokee front passenger seat bolts may be loose

NHTSA campaign 19V248000 · Reported 2019-03-28 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderate: the vehicle is safe to drive, but a front passenger faces a greater chance of injury in a crash until the bolts are tightened.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2019 Jeep Cherokees, the bolts that hold the front passenger seat to the floor may not be tightened all the way. Day to day you probably would not notice anything, but in a crash a seat that is not bolted down firmly can shift, and the person sitting in it is more likely to be hurt. The fix is quick: Chrysler has already sent letters, and a dealer simply tightens the mounting bolts, at no charge.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Cherokee is included. 2. Call your Jeep dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall V31), to book the bolt tightening. 3. The work is free — consider keeping the front passenger seat empty until it is done.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Jeep Cherokee vehicles. The front passenger seat mounting bolts may be loose.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, the seat may move, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Chrysler has notified owners, and dealers will tighten the mounting bolts, free of charge. The recall began April 11, 2019. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V31.
