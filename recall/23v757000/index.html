What's wrong, in plain English

These Toyota Tundra trucks carry a load carrying capacity modification label that lists the wrong capacity. Because the number on the label is incorrect, the truck can end up loaded with more weight than it should carry. Federal rules on tire selection and rims require this label to be correct, so these trucks do not meet that standard. The distributor will mail owners a new, corrected label at no cost.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a dealer or call Southeast Toyota Distributors customer service at 1-866-405-4226 about receiving the corrected label in the mail.
3. The new label is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V757000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC (SET) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Toyota Tundra vehicles. The load carrying capacity modification label states the incorrect load carrying capacity, which can result in an overloaded vehicle. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
An overloaded vehicle increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
SET will mail a new modified label, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 18, 2023. Owners may contact SET's customer service at 1-866-405-4226. SET's number for this recall is SET23B.
