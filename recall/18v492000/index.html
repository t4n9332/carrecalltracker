Chrysler Pacifica and Jeep Cherokee transmission may stop working

NHTSA campaign 18V492000 · Reported 2018-07-26 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Chrysler Pacifica
  • 2019 Jeep Cherokee

What's wrong, in plain English

A part inside the automatic transmission may not have been welded properly. The transmission is what sends engine power to the wheels. If that weld breaks, the connection is lost and the vehicle simply stops moving — the engine may still be running, but the wheels get no power. Losing drive without warning, especially in traffic or at speed, raises the risk of a crash. Chrysler dealers will replace the entire transmission at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Serious — the vehicle can lose all power to the wheels suddenly and with no warning; there is no stop-driving order, but get it to a dealer promptly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact your Chrysler or Jeep dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and reference recall U85. 3. Have the transmission replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Jeep Cherokee vehicles and 2018 Chrysler Pacifica non-hybrid vehicles. A component in the transmission may not have been welded properly, possibly causing the transmission to not transmit engine power to the wheels.
Safety risk
If the transmission weld fails, the vehicle will stop moving, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the transmission, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin September 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U85.
