Nissan Titan engine wiring can short and spark

NHTSA campaign 20V759000 · Reported 2020-12-08 · Nissan North America, Inc. · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Nissan Titan

What's wrong, in plain English

The bundle of electrical wires that runs across the engine may have a spot where the covering is missing, leaving bare wire exposed. If that bare wire touches another wire, it can short out or spark. That can affect how the engine, transmission, or fuel pump work, and the truck could stall while you are driving. A short or spark can also start a fire under the hood. A dealer will check the wiring and replace it if needed.

How serious is it?

Serious: the notice warns of both a sudden stall that could cause a crash and a risk of fire, but Nissan has not told owners to stop driving or park outside, so you can keep driving while you get this fixed promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included.
2. Call your Nissan dealer to schedule the engine harness inspection, or contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-647-7261.
3. Have the inspection and any harness replacement done — it is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FRONT UNDERHOOD
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020 Titan and Titan XD pickup trucks. The engine wiring harness could contain an exposed wire that can cause an electrical short or arcing if it contacts another wire.
Safety risk
An electrical short can impair the performance of the engine, transmission, or fuel pump, increasing the risk of an engine stall and a crash. An electrical short or arcing increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the engine harness, replacing it if necessary, free of charge. The recall began January 27, 2021. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-647-7261

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
