Sprinter Owner's Manual Wrong About Auto-Parking

NHTSA campaign 20V180000 · Reported Mar 26, 2020 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

If a driver relies on the Auto-P description as written in the manual, the vehicle could roll away or move on its own, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Sprinter vans, the Operator's Manual may give incorrect information about how the automatic parking function, called Auto-P, actually works. Dealers will provide owners with the correct description of how Auto-P operates for the manual.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
Contact a Mercedes-Benz or Freightliner Sprinter dealer, or call 1-877-762-8267, for the corrected Auto-P information.
This service is provided free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V180000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:OWNERS/SERVICE/OTHER MANUAL
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2019 Freightliner Sprinter and Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vehicles. The Operator's Manual may not contain the correct information about how the automatic parking function ("Auto-P") operates.
Safety risk
If the driver relies on the Auto-P function as described in the Operator's Manual, vehicle rollaway or movement may occur, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and Mercedes-Benz or Freightliner Sprinter dealers will provide the correct description of the Auto-P function for the Operator's Manual, free of charge. The recall began May 18, 2020. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3BAUTOP.
