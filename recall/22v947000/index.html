Ram ProMaster Rearview Camera Is Wrong Type

NHTSA campaign 22V947000 · Reported Dec 22, 2022 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

A rearview camera that does not work as intended can reduce the driver's view of what's behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash while backing up. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Ram ProMaster Chassis Cab and incomplete vehicles were built with an analog rearview camera, which is the wrong type for this vehicle and cannot be properly installed. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet federal safety rules that require a working rearview camera. Without the correct camera, the driver may not get a proper rearview image when backing up, which can reduce how well they can see behind the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact your Ram dealer or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 to schedule the free repair.
3. Dealers will install a digital rearview camera at no cost.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V947000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Ram Promaster Chassis Cab and incomplete vehicles equipped with an analog rearview camera. The analog rearview camera is the incorrect type of camera and cannot be installed. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
An inoperative rearview camera can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will supply a digital rearview camera, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 7, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is ZC5
