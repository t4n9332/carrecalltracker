Rivian R1S and R1T driver-assist may miss vehicle ahead

NHTSA campaign 25V585000 · Reported 2025-09-08 · Rivian Automotive, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Rivian R1S
  • 2025 Rivian R1T

What's wrong, in plain English

Your Rivian's Hands-Free Highway Assist is a driver-assist feature that helps keep pace with traffic on the highway. On these R1S and R1T vehicles running software older than version 2025.18.30, that system may not correctly spot the vehicle ahead of you. If it misses the car in front, it may not react the way it should. Rivian fixes this with a free over-the-air software update that downloads to the vehicle itself, so no shop visit is needed.

How serious is it?

Rivian does not tell owners to stop driving, but a system that fails to see the car ahead raises your chance of a crash, so install the update right away and stay fully attentive whenever the feature is on.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Park where your Rivian has a strong Wi-Fi or cellular connection and accept the over-the-air update to version 2025.18.30 or later — it is free.
3. If the update will not install, call Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261 and mention recall FSAM-1744.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:ADAS:AUTONOMOUS/SELF DRIVING:SOFTWARE
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2025 R1S and R1T vehicles operating a software version prior to 2025.18.30. The Hands-Free Highway Assist (HWA) software may fail to correctly identify a lead vehicle.
Safety risk
Failure to detect another vehicle increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian has released an over-the-air (OTA) software update, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 17, 2025. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1744.
