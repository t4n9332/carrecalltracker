Mercedes-Benz Metris and Sprinter emergency call sends wrong location

NHTSA campaign 21V055000 · Reported 2021-02-05 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Mercedes-Benz Metris
  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 1500
  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500
  • 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 3500
  • 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500

What's wrong, in plain English

These vans have an automatic emergency call system that dials for help after a crash and tells responders where you are. A software problem in the van's communication unit can send the wrong location. If you crash and cannot call for help yourself, ambulances and police could be sent to the wrong place, delaying the help you need. Everything else about the van works normally. A dealer will load corrected software at no cost.

How serious is it?

The van is safe to drive, but if you are hurt in a crash and cannot call for yourself, help could be delayed by being sent to the wrong address.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is covered. 2. Call Daimler Vans USA at 1-877-762-8267 (recall VSXHERMPOS) or your dealer to schedule the software update. 3. Get the update installed — it is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2016-2020 Mercedes-Benz Metris and 2019-2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vehicles. The software design of the communication module may fail to communicate the correct vehicle location for the emergency call system (eCall) in the event of a crash.
Safety risk
Failure of the eCall system may result in emergency responders being dispatched to the wrong location, increasing the risk of injury following a crash.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and dealers will update the software communication module for the automatic emergency call system, free of charge. The recall began on May 5, 2021. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VSXHERMPOS.
