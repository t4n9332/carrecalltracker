Hyundai Elantra Hybrid power unit can overheat, fire risk

NHTSA campaign 26V308000 · Reported 2026-05-15 · Hyundai Motor America · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Hyundai Elantra Hybrid

What's wrong, in plain English

The hybrid power control unit, the box that manages electricity between the battery and the motor, can overheat on some 2024 through 2026 Hyundai Elantra Hybrids. Overheating raises the risk of a fire. The fix is a free software update at the dealer that keeps the unit from getting too hot. Hyundai mailed letters to owners on July 3, 2026, and affected vehicle numbers have been searchable on the government recall site since May 16, 2026.

How serious is it?

Very serious, because any fire risk should be treated urgently; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside, but get the update as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is affected. 2. Call your Hyundai dealer, or Hyundai at 1-855-371-9460, and ask for recall 301 right away. 3. Have the free software update installed; do not pay anything for this repair.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:HYBRID MANAGEMENT:POWER CONTROL UNIT/MODULE (HPCU)
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2024-2026 Elantra Hybrid vehicles. The hybrid power control unit (HPCU) assembly may overheat.
Safety risk
Overheating in the HPCU increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will update the HPCU software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 3, 2026. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 301. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on May 16, 2026.
