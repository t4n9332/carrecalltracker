Toyota Tacoma rear brake hoses may leak fluid

NHTSA campaign 25V058000 · Reported 2025-02-06 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

The notice says reduced braking ability increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2024-2025 Toyota Tacoma 4-wheel drive vehicles, mud and dirt can build up inside the rear wheels and damage the rear brake hoses. This damage can cause a brake fluid leak. A brake fluid leak can reduce the vehicle's braking ability, meaning it could take longer to stop. Dealers will replace both rear brake hoses, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer at 1-800-331-4331 to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Tacoma 4-wheel drive vehicles. The rear brake hoses can be damaged if mud and dirt builds up inside the rear wheels. This can result in a brake fluid leak.
Safety risk
A brake fluid leak can reduce braking ability and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace both rear brake hoses, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed August 1, 2025. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 25TB04 and 25TA04.
