Kia Telluride Spare Tire May Impair Braking

NHTSA campaign 25V745000 · Reported Oct 31, 2025 · Kia America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the incorrect spare tire is installed and these systems don't work properly, it increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

These vehicles come with an incorrect spare tire. If that spare tire is installed, it can impair the anti-lock braking system and the traction control system.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Kia dealer or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542 to have the spare wheel and tire assembly replaced.
3. The replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V745000. Official notice

Component
TIRES:TEMPORARY/EMERGENCY SPARE TIRE
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) is recalling certain 2025 Telluride and 2026 Sorento vehicles. The vehicles are equipped with an incorrect spare tire which, when installed, can impair the anti-lock braking system (ABS) and the traction control system (TCS). As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
Anti-lock braking or traction control systems that do not function properly can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the spare wheel and tire assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 3, 2025. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC355. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning November 13, 2025.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
