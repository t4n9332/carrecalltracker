Toyota RAV4 Electric Power Steering Water Damage

NHTSA campaign 20V373000 · Reported 2020-06-24 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing power steering assist can increase how much effort it takes to steer, especially at low speeds, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Toyota is recalling certain RAV4 and RAV4 Hybrid vehicles equipped with electric power steering (EPS). Water may enter through the cover on the steering gear box. If water gets inside, it can cause the vehicle to lose its electric power steering assist, meaning the steering no longer gets that extra powered help.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
Contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371 (recall number 20TB11/20TA11).
Dealers will replace the power steering gear box, free of charge.

Affected vehicles (3)

  • 2020 Toyota RAV4
  • 2020 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid

Official notice

Component
STEERING:ELECTRIC POWER ASSIST SYSTEM
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2019-2020 RAV4 and 2020 RAV4 Hybrid vehicles equipped with electric power steering (EPS). Water may enter through the steering gear box cover and cause a loss of electric power steering assist.
Safety risk
A loss of power steering assist could increase steering effort at low vehicle speeds, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners, and dealers will replace the power steering gear box, free of charge. The recall began August 17, 2020. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. Toyota's number for this recall is 20TB11 / 20TA11.
