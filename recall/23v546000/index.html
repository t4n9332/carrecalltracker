Ford Bronco may roll away when parked

NHTSA campaign 23V546000 · Reported 2023-08-03 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Ford Bronco

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2023 Broncos, the transmission's park system may have been damaged during assembly. The parking pawl is the part inside the transmission that locks the wheels when you shift into Park. If it does not fully engage, the vehicle can roll away on its own after you park it. Ford dealers will replace the entire transmission at no cost to you. Owner notification letters for this recall were mailed on August 11, 2023.

How serious is it?

A vehicle that rolls away after being parked can cause a crash, so have this repaired as soon as possible.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Bronco is affected. 2. Schedule the transmission replacement with a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall number 23S44). 3. The new transmission is installed free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:INTERNAL:PARK PAWL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023 Bronco vehicles. The transmission park system may have been damaged during assembly, which can prevent the parking pawl from fully engaging and result in a vehicle rollaway.
Safety risk
A vehicle rollaway can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the transmission, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 11, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23S44.
