Chrysler Pacifica plug-in hybrid battery part can cause fire

NHTSA campaign 23V753000 · Reported 2023-11-09 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · critical severity

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Chrysler Pacifica

What's wrong, in plain English

These 2023 Pacifica plug-in hybrids have a part on the 12-volt battery, called an isolator post, that may have been installed wrong. That can cause an electrical short, and the short can start a fire, either while you are driving or while the van is sitting parked. Chrysler says to park outside and away from buildings until it is repaired. A dealer will refit the part correctly at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious fire risk — the notice does not tell you to stop driving, but a fire can start even while parked, so park outside and away from structures until it is fixed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is covered.
2. Park outside, away from your house, garage, or other buildings, until the repair is done.
3. Contact your Chrysler dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall B0A), for the free repair.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2023 Chrysler Pacifica plug-in hybrid electric vehicles. An electrical short may occur due to an incorrectly installed 12-volt battery isolator post, resulting in a fire while parked or driving.
Safety risk
A fire while parked or driving can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. Dealers will correct the isolator to fit the anti-rotation stud, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 28, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is B0A.
