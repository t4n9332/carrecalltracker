Ram 2500 and 3500 dashboard shows wrong gear

NHTSA campaign 25V297000 · Reported 2025-05-08 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 RAM 3500

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Ram 2500 and 3500 trucks with the small 3.5-inch dashboard display and a column shifter have a software error. The display can show the wrong gear, so it might read park or neutral when the truck is actually in drive or reverse. A driver who trusts that reading could step out or release the brake and have the truck move unexpectedly. Federal rules cover what these displays must show, so the trucks do not meet the standard. Dealers reload the software free.

How serious is it?

Fairly serious — the truck is drivable, but it can move when you do not expect it, so always set the parking brake and make sure the truck has fully stopped before shifting or getting out.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is part of recall 25V297000 (Chrysler's number 36C).
2. Call your Ram dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403, to schedule the dashboard software update.
3. Get the update installed — the software fix costs you nothing and usually takes only a short visit.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:GEAR POSITION INDICATION (PRNDL)
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain Ram 2500 and Ram 3500 vehicles equipped with a 3.5" cluster and column shifter. A software error may cause the gear selection display to be inaccurate. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 101, "Control and Displays."
Safety risk
An inaccurate gear selection display may mislead the driver as to what gear is actually selected, leading to unexpected vehicle movement and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the cluster software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 22, 2025. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 36C.
