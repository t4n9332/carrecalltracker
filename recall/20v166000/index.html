BMW R 1250 Motorcycles Have Unclear Brake Fluid Cap

NHTSA campaign 20V166000 · Reported 2020-03-19 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Incorrect brake fluid or improper reservoir filling can affect how well the brakes work, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain BMW R 1250 GS, GS Adventure, R, and RT motorcycles have a brake fluid reservoir cap that may not clearly show the type of brake fluid needed or how to fill the reservoir correctly. Without that information, an owner or mechanic could use the wrong fluid or fill it incorrectly during maintenance.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your motorcycle is included.
2. Contact a BMW dealer to have the brake fluid reservoir cap replaced; BMW customer service is at 1-800-525-7417.
3. The replacement cap is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FLUID
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2018-2020 RnineT Scrambler and RnineT, 2020 R1250R and R1250RS, 2019-2020 Rnine T Pure, R1250GS,R1250GS Adventure and R1250RT and 2018 RnineT Racer motorcycles. The brake fluid reservoir cap may not clearly state the required type of brake fluid and the process for filling the reservoir, possibly leading to an improper maintenance procedure. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 122, "Motorcycle Brake Systems."
Safety risk
Improper brake system maintenance could affect braking performance and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the brake fluid reservoir cap, free of charge. The recall began December 23, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
