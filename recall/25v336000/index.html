Mazda MX-5 stability control warning light may stay off

NHTSA campaign 25V336000 · Reported 2025-05-22 · Mazda North American Operations · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Mazda MX-5
  • 2025 Mazda MX-5

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Mazda MX-5 sports cars, a software error can keep the traction control and stability control warning light from lighting up. Those systems help keep the car from sliding or spinning on slippery roads or in fast turns. If one of them develops a fault, the dashboard light is supposed to warn you, but it may stay dark instead. The car does not meet a federal safety rule because of this. A dealer will replace the control unit for free.

How serious is it?

Low risk and safe to keep driving; the concern is that you might not be warned if the anti-skid systems stop working, which could increase crash risk.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is covered. 2. Contact your Mazda dealer, or Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500 Option 6 (recall 7425E), for an appointment. 3. Have the unit replaced — there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
TRACTION CONTROL SYSTEM
Summary
Mazda North American Operations (Mazda) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Mazda MX-5 vehicles. The Traction Control System (TCS) and Dynamic Stability Control (DSC) indicator light may fail to illuminate as intended due to a software error. As such, these vehicles fail to conform to Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 126, "Electronic Stability Control Systems."
Safety risk
An indicator light that does not illuminate may fail to warn the driver of a TCS or DSC system issue, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the DSC unit, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 18, 2025. Owners may contact Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500 Option 6. Mazda's number for this recall is 7425E.
