Porsche 911 driveshaft bolts may be loose

NHTSA campaign 19V445000 · Reported 2019-06-12 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Porsche 911 Carrera

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2019 911 GT2 RS and 2020 911 Carrera S Coupe cars, the bolts that hold the driveshaft to the wheel hub may not have been tightened enough at the factory. The driveshaft is the part that spins the wheel and makes the car move. If those bolts are loose, the driveshaft can come apart from the wheel hub while you are driving. Porsche dealers will tighten the connection properly at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious — a driveshaft coming loose from the wheel raises the risk of a crash, so get the bolts checked as soon as you can; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included.
2. Call your Porsche dealer or Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243 (recall AKB2) and book an appointment.
3. Have the dealer tighten the driveshaft-to-hub connection — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AXLE HUBS
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2019 911 GT2 RS and 2020 911 Carrera S Coupe vehicles. The bolts connecting the driveshaft to the wheel hub may not have been properly tightened.
Safety risk
If the driveshaft disconnects from the wheel hub, it can increase the risk of crash.
Remedy
Porsche will notify owners, and dealers will properly tighten the driveshaft connection to the wheel hub, free of charge. The recall began August 9, 2019. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AKB2.
