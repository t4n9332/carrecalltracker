Porsche 911 Driveshaft Bolts May Be Loose

NHTSA campaign 19V445000 · Reported Jun 12, 2019 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A disconnected driveshaft can increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some 911 Carrera vehicles, the bolts that connect the driveshaft to the wheel hub may not have been properly tightened. This means the driveshaft could become disconnected from the wheel hub. Porsche dealers will properly tighten this connection between the driveshaft and the wheel hub, and the repair is provided free of charge to owners.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is affected.
2. Contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243 to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V445000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AXLE HUBS
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2019 911 GT2 RS and 2020 911 Carrera S Coupe vehicles. The bolts connecting the driveshaft to the wheel hub may not have been properly tightened.
Safety risk
If the driveshaft disconnects from the wheel hub, it can increase the risk of crash.
Remedy
Porsche will notify owners, and dealers will properly tighten the driveshaft connection to the wheel hub, free of charge. The recall began August 9, 2019. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AKB2.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
