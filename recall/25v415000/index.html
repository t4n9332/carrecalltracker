Porsche Panamera Suspension Strut May Loosen

NHTSA campaign 25V415000 · Reported Jul 15, 2025 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A dislodged strut can cause a loss of vehicle handling and control, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On the front suspension, a retaining ring that holds the top of the strut in place may come loose over time. If the ring lets go, the strut can become dislodged from its mount, and the strut spring can lose air pressure, affecting how the front suspension holds together.

What you should do

1. Check if your VIN is affected at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Porsche dealer at 1-800-767-7243.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V415000. Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:MACPHERSON STRUT
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2025 Panamera E-hybrid, Taycan, and Panamera vehicles. A retaining ring on top of the suspension strut may come loose, resulting in a dislodged strut and air loss from the strut spring.
Safety risk
An air suspension strut failure may cause a loss of vehicle handling and control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the retaining ring and reinstall it as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 15, 2025. Owners may contact Porsche's customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ASA6.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
