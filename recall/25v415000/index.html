Porsche Panamera and Taycan front suspension can come apart

NHTSA campaign 25V415000 · Reported 2025-07-15 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2026 Porsche Panamera

What's wrong, in plain English

A retaining ring at the top of the front suspension strut can work loose on some 2025 Porsche Panamera, Panamera E-Hybrid and Taycan models. If it does, the strut can pull out of place and the air suspension can lose its air on that corner of the car. That can make the car hard to steer and control. Dealers will inspect the ring and put it back on properly if needed. Owner letters were mailed August 15, 2025.

How serious is it?

Serious, because losing suspension can mean losing control of the car while driving, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving in the meantime.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your car is included. 2. Schedule the free inspection with your Porsche dealer, or call 1-800-767-7243 and reference recall ASA6. 3. Get it looked at promptly, and tell the dealer if you notice unusual handling, noise, or the car sitting low on one side.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:MACPHERSON STRUT
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2025 Panamera E-hybrid, Taycan, and Panamera vehicles. A retaining ring on top of the suspension strut may come loose, resulting in a dislodged strut and air loss from the strut spring.
Safety risk
An air suspension strut failure may cause a loss of vehicle handling and control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the retaining ring and reinstall it as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 15, 2025. Owners may contact Porsche's customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ASA6.
