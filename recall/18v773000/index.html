Subaru Legacy and Outback Fuel Gauge Software Error

NHTSA campaign 18V773000 · Reported Nov 1, 2018 · Subaru of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

An inaccurate fuel display may cause a driver to unexpectedly run out of fuel and the vehicle to stall, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Subaru is recalling certain 2018 Legacy and Outback vehicles because of a software error affecting the fuel display. The low fuel warning light may not turn on when it should, and the miles-to-empty display can show a positive number even when the tank is actually empty. Because the display isn't accurate, a driver may not realize how little fuel actually remains in the tank while driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614 or your dealer to schedule the software update.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V773000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2018 Subaru Legacy and Outback vehicles. Due to a software error, the low fuel warning light may not illuminate at the intended remaining fuel level and the miles-to-empty display may incorrectly indicate a positive number despite the tank being empty.
Safety risk
The inaccurate fuel display may cause a driver to unexpectedly run out of fuel and the vehicle to stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Subaru will notify owners, and dealers will reprogram the combination meter software, free of charge. The recall began February 20, 2019. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WTW-82.
