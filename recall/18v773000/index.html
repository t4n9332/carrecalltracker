Subaru Legacy and Outback fuel gauge software reads wrong

NHTSA campaign 18V773000 · Reported 2018-11-01 · Subaru of America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Subaru Legacy
  • 2018 Subaru Outback

What's wrong, in plain English

A software mistake in your Subaru's dashboard gauges means the fuel readings can mislead you. The low fuel warning light may not come on when it should, and the display showing how many miles you have left may still show fuel remaining when the tank is actually empty. Because of that, you could run out of gas with no warning and the engine could quit while you are driving. A dealer reloads corrected software into the gauge.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious — the car itself is not failing, so you can keep driving it, but do not trust the fuel gauge or the miles-to-empty number until the software is updated, because an unexpected stall in traffic can cause a crash.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included. 2. Call any Subaru dealer or Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614 (recall WTW-82) and book the software reprogramming, which is free. 3. Until it is fixed, fill up early and go by your odometer and past fill-ups rather than the gauge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2018 Subaru Legacy and Outback vehicles. Due to a software error, the low fuel warning light may not illuminate at the intended remaining fuel level and the miles-to-empty display may incorrectly indicate a positive number despite the tank being empty.
Safety risk
The inaccurate fuel display may cause a driver to unexpectedly run out of fuel and the vehicle to stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Subaru will notify owners, and dealers will reprogram the combination meter software, free of charge. The recall began February 20, 2019. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WTW-82.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
