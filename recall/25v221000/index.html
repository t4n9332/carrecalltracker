Porsche Taycan passenger airbag may switch itself off

NHTSA campaign 25V221000 · Reported 2025-05-05 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Porsche Taycan
  • 2023 Porsche Taycan

What's wrong, in plain English

The front passenger seat has a system that senses who is sitting there and decides whether the passenger airbag should be switched on. On these cars that system can make an error and turn the front passenger airbag off. If that happens, the airbag will not inflate in a crash, so a person riding in the front passenger seat has less protection. Dealers will replace the front passenger seat cushion at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious: the car is safe to keep driving, but a front passenger could be injured more badly in a crash because the airbag may not go off, so get it repaired promptly.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Taycan is part of campaign 25V221000 (Porsche recall ASA2).
2. Call your Porsche dealer or Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243 to make an appointment.
3. Have the front passenger seat cushion replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SENSOR:OCCUPANT CLASSIFICATION:FRONT PASSENGER
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Taycan vehicles. An occupant classification system error may deactivate the front passenger air bag.
Safety risk
An air bag that fails to deploy during a crash increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front passenger seat cushion, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 24, 2025. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ASA2.
