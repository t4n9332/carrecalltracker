Toyota Tacoma Child Seat Anchors May Fail

NHTSA campaign 22V520000 · Reported Jul 21, 2022 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

The notice says a child seat that isn't properly anchored could move during a sudden stop or crash, increasing the risk of injury to the child. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Toyota Tacoma trucks have upper child seat anchors where one or more of the welds may not be strong enough. These anchors are used to secure a child seat to the vehicle. If the welds are insufficient, the child seat anchor may not hold as it should, which could let the child seat shift or move during a sudden stop or a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer, or call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331.
3. The repair (inspecting and rewelding the anchors as necessary) is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V520000. Official notice

Component
CHILD SEAT:VEHICLE LATCH ANCHOR
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Tacoma vehicles. One or more of the individual welds in the upper child seat anchors may be insufficient. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 225, "Child Restraint Anchorage System."
Safety risk
An insufficient weld may prevent the child seat from being anchored properly, allowing the seat to move during a sudden stop or crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the upper child seat anchors and reweld the child seat anchors, as necessary. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed September 02, 2022. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 22TB09/ 22TA09.
