Kia Sorento airbag warning labels can fall off visors

NHTSA campaign 23V579000 · Reported 2023-08-15 · Kia America, Inc. · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Kia Sorento

What's wrong, in plain English

The airbag warning labels on the driver's and front passenger's sun visors can peel off on some 2023 Kia Sorentos. Those labels warn people about the dangers of an airbag going off, such as sitting too close to it. Without the labels, that warning is missing, and the vehicle does not meet a federal safety rule. Nothing on the car is broken. A dealer will check the visors and replace them if needed, at no cost.

How serious is it?

Low, since the airbags themselves work normally and the car is safe to drive, but the missing warning matters for passengers unfamiliar with airbag risks.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Sorento is covered. 2. Contact your Kia dealer, or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542, and mention recall SC277. 3. Have the sun visors inspected and replaced if needed, free of charge, at your next service visit.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2023 Sorento vehicles. The air bag warning labels on the driver and front passenger sun visors may detach. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
A detached air bag warning label will not warn occupants about the risks of air bag deployment, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the driver and front passenger sun visors, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 10, 2023. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC277.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
