Dodge Challenger dashboard warning lights too dim to see

NHTSA campaign 21V664000 · Reported 2021-08-26 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Dodge Challenger

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2021 Dodge Challengers, the warning and status lights in the dashboard cluster don't light up brightly enough. That means the small symbols that tell you whether things like your airbag or brake system are working properly can be hard or impossible to see, day or night. The dashboard software was built with the wrong version, so the brightness is off. A dealer can reload the correct software into the cluster, and it costs you nothing.

How serious is it?

This is on the milder end and the notice does not tell you to stop driving, but you could miss a warning about a real safety problem, so get it done soon.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Challenger is covered.
2. Call a Dodge dealer or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403 and ask for recall Y58.
3. Have the dealer reprogram the instrument cluster — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2021 Dodge Challenger vehicles. The affected vehicles do not illuminate their safety system status indicators sufficiently in both daylight and nighttime driving conditions. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 101, "Control and Displays."
Safety risk
Without visible safety system indicators, a driver may not receive information regarding vehicle safety, increasing the risk of injury or a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will re-program instrument clusters with the correct software version, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on October 5, 2021. Owners may contact FCA US LLC customer service at 1-800-853-140. FCA US LLC's number for this recall is Y58.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
