Mopar Floor Mats for Jeep Grand Cherokee Block Pedal

NHTSA campaign 18E097000 · Reported 2018-10-29 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee

What's wrong, in plain English

These are Mopar accessory floor mats, part numbers 6ML07TRMAA and 6GA92TRMAA, sold separately for 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee SRT and Trackhawk models. The mat does not leave enough space around the gas pedal, so it can hold the pedal down and stop it from coming back to its normal resting position after you take your foot off. The vehicle could keep accelerating when you want it to slow. Your dealer will replace an affected mat at no charge.

How serious is it?

A gas pedal held down by a mat is a serious hazard that can happen suddenly; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but the mat should be dealt with promptly.

What you should do

1. Check the part number on the underside of your mat, and look up your vehicle's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact your Jeep dealer or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 and reference recall UB5.
3. Have the affected mat replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:ACCESSORY:FLOOR MAT
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain Mopar Floor Mats, part numbers 6ML07TRMAA and 6GA92TRMAA, sold as accessory equipment for 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee Street and Racing Technology ("SRT") and Trackhawk vehicles. These floor mats may have insufficient clearance between the mat and the accelerator pedal, possibly inhibiting the pedal from returning to an idle position.
Safety risk
If the floor mat prevents the accelerator pedal from returning to idle, there would be an increased risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace any of the affected floor mats, free of charge. The recall began November 15, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is UB5.
