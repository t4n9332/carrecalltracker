Audi Q5 and SQ5 wheel arch trim can detach

NHTSA campaign 19V416000 · Reported 2019-06-05 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Audi Q5
  • 2018 Audi SQ5
  • 2019 Audi Q5
  • 2019 Audi SQ5

What's wrong, in plain English

The plastic trim that covers the wheel arch — the curved edge of the body above each wheel — may work loose and fall off while you drive. A piece coming off on the road can become an obstacle for the cars behind you. Dealers will fasten the trim down with an extra screw and nut at no cost. The repair started July 26, 2019, and Volkswagen calls it 66K6.

How serious is it?

This is a low-level safety concern that mainly puts other drivers at risk if the trim lands on the road, and the notice does not restrict driving your Audi in the meantime.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if recall 66K6 applies to your vehicle.
2. Schedule a visit with an Audi dealer to have the wheel arch trim secured — the work is free.
3. For help finding a dealer or checking status, call Volkswagen customer care at 1-800-253-2834.

Official notice

Component
WHEELS:HUB
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Audi Q5 and SQ5 vehicles. The wheel arch cover trim may loosen and detach.
Safety risk
The detached trim may become a road hazard, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will secure the wheel arch trim with an additional screw and nut, free of charge. The recall began July 26, 2019. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer at 1-800-253-2834. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 66K6.
