Buick Regal Front Seat Frame Weld Defect

NHTSA campaign 19V762000 · Reported 2019-10-24 · General Motors LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A seat with a bad weld may not protect you as well in a crash, which raises the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Buick Regal vehicles have a front seat frame with a lower crossbar that may not have been welded correctly, on either the driver's or passenger's seat. Seats with this kind of weld problem may not hold together as well as they should, which can affect how much protection the seat provides in a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Buick dealer, or call Buick customer service at 1-800-521-7300.
3. If the weld is faulty, the seat back frame will be replaced free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2019 Buick Regal
  • 2020 Buick Regal

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Buick Regal vehicles. The driver or passenger front seat frames may have a lower crossbar that was improperly welded.
Safety risk
Seats with improper welds may not provide adequate protection in the event of a crash, increasing the risk of an injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the crossbar welds on the front seats. If the crossbar is improperly welded, the seat back frame will be replaced, free of charge. Interim notices informing owners of the safety risk were mailed November 26, 2019. Owners received a second notice and the recall began February 20, 2020. Owners may contact Buick customer service at 1-800-521-7300. GM's number for this recall is N192278300.
