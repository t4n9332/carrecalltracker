Volvo S60 and V60 front seat belts may fail

NHTSA campaign 20V378000 · Reported 2020-06-30 · Volvo Car USA LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Volvo S60
  • 2018 Volvo V60

What's wrong, in plain English

A flexible steel cable anchors each front seat belt to the seat. Over years of use that cable can weaken and fail. If it does, the belt will not hold you in place in a crash, even though it may look and feel normal when you buckle up. Anyone in a front seat is at greater risk of injury. Volvo dealers replace the anchor cable on both front seats at no charge.

How serious is it?

Serious, since a front seat belt may not protect you in a crash; Volvo does not tell owners to stop driving, so keep wearing your belt and book the free repair soon.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Volvo is covered.
2. Contact a Volvo dealer or Volvo customer service at 1-800-458-1552 and mention recall R10029.
3. Have the dealer replace both front seat belt anchor cables — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT
Summary
Volvo Cars of N.A., LLC (Volvo) is recalling certain 2008-2016 V70 and XC70, 2007-2016 S80, 2011-2018 S60 and V60, 2016-2018 S60 Cross Country, 2014-2020 S60L, 2009-2015 S80L, 2009-2016 XC60 and 2015-2018 V60 Cross Country vehicles. The flexible steel cable that connects the seat belt to the front outboard seating positions may fatigue over time, causing the seat belt to not secure the occupant in the event of a crash.
Safety risk
An unsecured occupant has an increased risk of injury in the event of a crash.
Remedy
Volvo will notify owners, and dealers will replace the seat belt anchor cable for both front seats. The recall began December 10, 2020. Owners may contact Volvo customer service at 1-800-458-1552. Volvo's number for this recall is R10029.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
