Volvo S60 and V60 Seat Belt Cable May Fatigue

NHTSA campaign 20V378000 · Reported 2020-06-30 · Volvo Car USA LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

An occupant not properly secured by the seat belt has an increased risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Volvo S60 and V60 vehicles use a flexible steel cable that connects the seat belt to the front outboard seats. Over time, this cable can weaken and fatigue. If the cable fails, the seat belt will not be able to hold the occupant securely in place if the vehicle is in a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Volvo dealer to schedule the repair; Volvo customer service is at 1-800-458-1552.
3. Dealers will replace the seat belt anchor cable on both front seats, free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2018 Volvo S60
  • 2018 Volvo V60

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT
Summary
Volvo Cars of N.A., LLC (Volvo) is recalling certain 2008-2016 V70 and XC70, 2007-2016 S80, 2011-2018 S60 and V60, 2016-2018 S60 Cross Country, 2014-2020 S60L, 2009-2015 S80L, 2009-2016 XC60 and 2015-2018 V60 Cross Country vehicles. The flexible steel cable that connects the seat belt to the front outboard seating positions may fatigue over time, causing the seat belt to not secure the occupant in the event of a crash.
Safety risk
An unsecured occupant has an increased risk of injury in the event of a crash.
Remedy
Volvo will notify owners, and dealers will replace the seat belt anchor cable for both front seats. The recall began December 10, 2020. Owners may contact Volvo customer service at 1-800-458-1552. Volvo's number for this recall is R10029.
