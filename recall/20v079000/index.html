Mercedes Sprinter turbocharger joint leak creates fire risk

NHTSA campaign 20V079000 · Reported 2020-02-12 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 1500
  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2019 Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans, the joint where the turbocharger connects to the exhaust filter may not be fastened properly. A loose joint lets hot exhaust gas escape into the engine compartment, and that leaking gas can heat up or ignite parts around it and start a fire. Dealers will replace the seals at that joint and tighten the connection correctly so the exhaust stays inside the system.

How serious is it?

A fire under the hood is a serious danger, so get this repaired as soon as possible and stop driving if you smell exhaust or see smoke.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is included.
2. Contact Daimler Vans USA at 1-877-762-8267 (recall VS3FLABIND) or your Sprinter dealer to book the work.
3. Have the seals replaced and the connection tightened; there is no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:EXHAUST SYSTEM
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2019 Mercedes Benz Sprinter and Freightliner Sprinter vehicles. The flange connection between the exhaust gas turbocharger and the exit to the diesel particulate filter may not be attached properly, potentially causing an exhaust gas leak.
Safety risk
An exhaust gas leak in the engine compartment increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and Mercedes-Benz or Freightliner Sprinter dealers will replace the flange seals and properly tighten the connection, free of charge. The recall began April 10, 2020. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3FLABIND.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
