BMW X3, X4 Passenger Air Bag May Not Deploy

NHTSA campaign 19V291000 · Reported Apr 10, 2019 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

If the front passenger air bag doesn't deploy properly in a crash, the passenger has a higher risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The instrument panel on these vehicles may have been manufactured incorrectly. Because of how the panel was put together, it may affect whether the front passenger air bag deploys the way it is supposed to. This means the air bag positioned in front of the passenger seat may not function correctly when it needs to inflate, affecting the passenger sitting in that seat.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
Contact a BMW dealer, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
The instrument panel replacement is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V291000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL:PASSENGER SIDE:INFLATOR MODULE
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2019 X3 sDrive30i, X3 xDrive30i, X3 M40i, and X4 M40i vehicles. The instrument panel assembly may have been improperly manufactured, possibly affecting the deployment of the passenger's frontal air bag.
Safety risk
If the front passenger air bag does not deploy properly in the event of a crash, the occupant has an increased risk of injury.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the instrument panel assembly, free of charge. The recall began June 3, 2019. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
