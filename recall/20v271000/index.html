Porsche Cayenne transmission fluid leak creates slippery road

NHTSA campaign 20V271000 · Reported 2020-05-13 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Porsche Cayenne

What's wrong, in plain English

The pipe that carries automatic transmission fluid on these Cayennes may have been welded poorly at one joint. That faulty weld can allow transmission fluid to leak from the vehicle. Fluid dripping onto the pavement as you drive can leave a slick spot on the road, which can cause a vehicle to lose traction. Porsche dealers will inspect the transmission oil pipe and install a replacement if they find a problem.

How serious is it?

Serious, because spilled fluid can make the road slippery and lead to a crash; the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but have it repaired soon and watch for puddles under the car.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Cayenne is covered. 2. Call your Porsche dealer or Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243 (recall ALA5) for an appointment. 3. Let the dealer inspect and replace the pipe — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:COOLING UNIT AND LINES
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2020 Cayenne vehicles. An insufficient weld on a transmission oil pipe may cause the vehicle to leak automatic transmission fluid (ATF).
Safety risk
If transmission fluid leaks while driving, the road surface may be made slippery, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Porsche will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the transmission oil pipe and replace it if necessary, free of charge. The recall began September 8, 2020. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ALA5.
