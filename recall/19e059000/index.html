Ram 1500 Mopar rear step kit can cause fuel leak

NHTSA campaign 19E059000 · Reported 2019-08-27 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 RAM 1500

What's wrong, in plain English

This recall covers a Mopar rear step kit, part number 82215289AB, sold for 2019 Ram 1500 trucks. If your truck is hit from behind, the step kit can let the rear control arms — suspension parts under the back of the truck — press against the fuel tank. That contact can tear or puncture the tank and let gasoline leak out, which can start a fire. Dealers will replace the bed step, free of charge.

How serious is it?

This is a fire risk after a rear-end crash rather than an everyday one, and the notice gives no park-it or park-outside instruction, but it should be replaced as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls and see whether this step kit is on your truck.
2. Call your Ram dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall V93), to arrange replacement.
3. Have the dealer install the new bed step — there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain Mopar Rear Step Kits, part number 82215289AB, designed for model year 2019 Ram 1500 trucks. In the event of a rear impact crash, the rear step kit may cause the rear control arms to contact the fuel tank.
Safety risk
Contact with the fuel tank may cause a fuel leak, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the bed step, free of charge. The recall began September 26, 2019. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V93.
