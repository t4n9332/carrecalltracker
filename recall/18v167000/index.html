Ford Fusion and Lincoln MKZ steering wheels may detach

NHTSA campaign 18V167000 · Reported 2018-03-12 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Ford Fusion
  • 2018 Lincoln MKZ

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Fusion and MKZ cars, the large bolt that holds the steering wheel onto the steering column can work itself loose over time. If it backs out far enough, the steering wheel can come off in your hands while you are driving, leaving you with no way to steer the car. Ford dealers replace the original bolt with a longer one that holds the wheel securely. The repair costs you nothing, and Ford has been notifying owners by mail.

How serious is it?

This is about as serious as a recall gets — a steering wheel can come off with no warning, so get it checked before you drive it much further.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is covered.
2. Contact your Ford or Lincoln dealer right away, or call Ford at 1-866-436-7332, and give them recall number 18S08.
3. Have the longer steering wheel bolt installed — it is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STEERING: STEERING WHEEL/HANDLE BAR
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2014-2018 Lincoln MKZ and Ford Fusion vehicles. The steering wheel retaining bolt may loosen allowing the steering wheel to detach while driving.
Safety risk
A steering wheel detachment can cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will replace the steering wheel bolt with a longer bolt, free of charge. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 18S08.
