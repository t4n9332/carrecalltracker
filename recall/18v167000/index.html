Ford Fusion, Lincoln MKZ Steering Wheel Detachment

NHTSA campaign 18V167000 · Reported Mar 12, 2018 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

A steering wheel detachment can cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Ford Fusion and Lincoln MKZ vehicles have a bolt that holds the steering wheel in place. This bolt may loosen over time, which can allow the steering wheel to come loose and detach from the steering column while the vehicle is being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 to schedule a dealer visit; the dealer will replace the steering wheel bolt with a longer bolt.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V167000. Official notice

Component
STEERING: STEERING WHEEL/HANDLE BAR
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2014-2018 Lincoln MKZ and Ford Fusion vehicles. The steering wheel retaining bolt may loosen allowing the steering wheel to detach while driving.
Safety risk
A steering wheel detachment can cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will replace the steering wheel bolt with a longer bolt, free of charge. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 18S08.
