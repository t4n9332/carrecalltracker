Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain driver seat bolt loose

NHTSA campaign 22V724000 · Reported 2022-09-29 · General Motors, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Chevrolet Equinox
  • 2022 GMC Terrain
  • 2023 Chevrolet Equinox

What's wrong, in plain English

A bolt in the driver's seat frame, at the pivot point where the seat height adjusts, may be stripped or loose. With that bolt missing or not holding, the seat may not hold the driver in place properly during a crash. You might also notice the seat feels wobbly or shifts when you move. Dealers will inspect the driver's seat and replace the seat cushion frame if it is damaged, at no cost.

How serious is it?

A seat that does not hold you in a crash raises the risk of injury; the vehicle can be driven, but have the seat inspected soon, especially if it feels loose.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered.
2. Contact your dealer, or Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC at 1-800-462-8782, referencing recall N222377890.
3. Have the driver's seat inspected and repaired — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Chevrolet Equinox and 2022 GMC Terrain vehicles. The driver's seat may have a stripped or loose bolt at the seat frame height-adjust pivot.
Safety risk
A seat with a missing or loose bolt may not properly restrain the occupant in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the driver's seat and replace the seat cushion frame, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on November 4, 2022. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GMC's number for this recall is N222377890.
