Chevrolet Equinox, GMC Terrain Loose Driver Seat Bolt

NHTSA campaign 22V724000 · Reported 2022-09-29 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A seat with a stripped or loose bolt may not properly restrain the driver during a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain vehicles may have a problem with the driver's seat. A bolt that holds the seat frame in place, allowing it to adjust up and down, may be stripped or loose. If this bolt isn't secure, the seat may not properly hold the driver in place during a crash, which increases the risk of injury.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact your Chevrolet dealer at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC dealer at 1-800-462-8782 to schedule an inspection.
3. Any needed seat repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Chevrolet Equinox and 2022 GMC Terrain vehicles. The driver's seat may have a stripped or loose bolt at the seat frame height-adjust pivot.
Safety risk
A seat with a missing or loose bolt may not properly restrain the occupant in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the driver's seat and replace the seat cushion frame, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on November 4, 2022. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GMC's number for this recall is N222377890.
