Nissan Murano, Pathfinder, Infiniti QX60: front calipers may break

NHTSA campaign 25V173000 · Reported 2025-03-19 · Nissan North America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Infiniti QX60
  • 2025 Nissan Murano
  • 2025 Nissan Pathfinder

What's wrong, in plain English

One or both front brake calipers — the parts that squeeze the brake pads against the wheel to slow the car — may not be as strong as they should be, and could break. If one breaks, you can lose braking at the front wheels, which do most of the stopping. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal brake safety standard. Dealers will inspect both front calipers and replace them where needed.

How serious is it?

Serious — a broken caliper means losing front braking and a real risk of a crash; the notice does not order you to park the car, but get it inspected right away.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your 2025 Murano, Pathfinder or QX60 is affected.
2. Call your dealer, or Nissan customer service at 800-647-7261 (Infiniti: 1-800-662-6200), recall PD137/PD138, and ask for the earliest appointment.
3. Have the front calipers inspected and replaced as needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:DISC:CALIPER
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2025 Murano, Pathfinder, and INFINITI QX60 vehicles. One or both front brake caliper assemblies may have reduced strength and could break. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 135, "Light Vehicle Brake Systems."
Safety risk
A broken front brake caliper can cause a loss of front brake function, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the front brake caliper assemblies as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 9, 2025. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 800-647-7261 or INFINITI's customer service at 1-800-662-6200. Nissan's numbers for this recall are PD137 and PD138.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
