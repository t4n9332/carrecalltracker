Jeep Renegade fuel pump flaw can stall engine

NHTSA campaign 18V731000 · Reported 2018-10-17 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Jeep Renegade

What's wrong, in plain English

A manufacturing mistake in the fuel pump can let fuel pressure drop when the engine is working hard — for example, climbing at high altitude or driving in very hot weather. If that happens the engine can stall, leaving you without power. This covers certain 2017-2018 Renegade 4X2 flex-fuel (E85) models with the 2.4-liter engine. Dealers replace the fuel pump free of charge. The recall began April 26, 2019.

How serious is it?

Serious — a stall can happen while you are driving and leave you without engine power in traffic, so get this fixed promptly rather than putting it off.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Renegade is affected.
2. Contact your Jeep dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and mention recall UB2.
3. Schedule the fuel pump replacement — it costs you nothing.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Jeep Renegade 4X2 flex fuel/E85 vehicles equipped with a 2.4L engine. In certain operating conditions such as high power demand in conjunction with high altitude and/or high ambient temperatures, a manufacturing error in the fuel pump assembly may result in a loss of fuel pressure.
Safety risk
A loss of fuel pressure can cause a stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge. The recall began April 26, 2019. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is UB2.
