Jeep Renegade Fuel Pump May Lose Pressure

NHTSA campaign 18V731000 · Reported Oct 17, 2018 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A loss of fuel pressure can cause the engine to stall, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain flex fuel/E85 Renegade models with the 2.4L engine, a manufacturing error in the fuel pump can cause a loss of fuel pressure. This is more likely to happen under conditions like high power demand combined with high altitude or high temperatures.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Chrysler/Jeep dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V731000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Jeep Renegade 4X2 flex fuel/E85 vehicles equipped with a 2.4L engine. In certain operating conditions such as high power demand in conjunction with high altitude and/or high ambient temperatures, a manufacturing error in the fuel pump assembly may result in a loss of fuel pressure.
Safety risk
A loss of fuel pressure can cause a stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge. The recall began April 26, 2019. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is UB2.
