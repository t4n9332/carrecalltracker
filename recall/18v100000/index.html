RAM Trucks' Shift Interlock May Allow Rollaway

NHTSA campaign 18V100000 · Reported 2018-02-08 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

Being able to shift out of Park without the brake pedal or a key increases the risk of the truck rolling away on its own, which could cause injury or a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some RAM pickups and cab chassis trucks with a column-mounted gear shifter, holding the brake pedal down for a long time while the truck is running and in Park can cause a small part called the brake-transmission shift interlock pin to get stuck open. When that happens, the truck can be shifted out of Park into another gear without anyone pressing the brake pedal or having the key in the ignition.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is included.
Contact your RAM dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to schedule the repair.
The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2017-2018 RAM 1500, 2500, 3500, 3500 Cab Chassis, 4500 Cab Chassis, and 5500 Cab Chassis vehicles, all equipped with a column shifter. Pushing the brake pedal for prolonged periods when the vehicle is running and in PARK may cause the Brake Transmission Shift Interlock (BTSI) pin to stick in the open position. With the pin in the open position, the transmission can be shifted out of PARK into any gear without pushing the brake pedal or having the key in the ignition.
Safety risk
Being able to shift the transmission without pushing the brake pedal and/or without a key in the ignition can increase the risk of an unintended vehicle rollaway that may result in personal injury or a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners and dealers will update the Body Control Module software. Dealers will also test the BTSI operation, replacing the solenoid as necessary. The recall began February 26, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U11.
