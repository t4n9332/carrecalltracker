Kia Telluride seat belts may not secure child seats

NHTSA campaign 19V594000 · Reported 2019-08-13 · Kia Motors America · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Kia Telluride

What's wrong, in plain English

Some seat belts in these Tellurides — in the front passenger seat and the second or third row — may not hold a child car seat tightly enough in a crash. That means the vehicle does not meet the federal rule for protecting people in a crash. A separate issue is that the driver's seat belt may carry a label that is not the U.S. version. Dealers check the belts and replace any wrong ones, including that driver's belt.

How serious is it?

Moderate: the belts work normally for adults and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but a child seat may not stay tight in a crash, so get it fixed before relying on those positions for a child seat.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm campaign 19V594000 (Kia's number SC181) is open for your Telluride.
2. Contact your Kia dealer, or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542, to schedule the seat belt inspection.
3. Have any incorrect belts, plus the driver's belt with the wrong label, replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) is recalling certain 2020 Telluride vehicles. The seat belt assemblies in the front passenger, second, and/or third row seats may not tightly secure a child restraint system in the event of a crash. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
If a child seat is not properly secured, there is an increased risk of injury to the occupant in a crash.
Remedy
Kia will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the vehicles and replace any incorrect seat belt assemblies. The repair will also include replacing the driver's seat belt which has a non-U.S. specified label, with a U.S. specification seat belt. The repairs will be performed free of charge. The recall began August 30, 2019. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC181.
