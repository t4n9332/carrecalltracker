Some Sprinter and Metris vans skipped final safety check

NHTSA campaign 19V222000 · Reported 2019-03-20 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Loose or unsecured safety-related parts increase the risk of a crash and injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Metris vans left the reassembly plant without a final quality inspection of safety-related parts and bolted connections. Because this inspection was not done, there could be parts or bolts on these vans that are loose or not properly secured, and this may not have been caught before the vehicle was delivered to its owner.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your van is included in this recall.
2. Contact Daimler Vans USA customer service at 1-877-762-8267, or your dealer, to schedule the inspection.
3. The dealer will complete the final quality inspection at no cost to you.

Affected vehicles (3)

  • 2018 Mercedes-Benz Metris
  • 2018 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 2500
  • 2018 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 3500

Official notice

Component
UNKNOWN OR OTHER
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Metris vehicles. The affected vehicles left the reassembly plant without the final quality inspection of safety-relevant parts or bolt connections, resulting in possible unknown safety risks.
Safety risk
Failure to identify loose safety related parts can increase the risk of crash and injury.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and dealers will complete the final quality inspection, free of charge. The recall began May 17, 2019. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VSX566ZP25.
