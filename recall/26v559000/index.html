Jeep tire pressure warning system may miss low tires

NHTSA campaign 26V559000 · Reported Sep 1, 2026 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the warning light does not alert the driver to low tire pressure, the risk of a crash increases. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Grand Cherokee, Grand Cherokee L, Grand Cherokee 4xe, Wagoneer, Wagoneer L, Grand Wagoneer and Cherokee vehicles. A software error means the tire pressure monitoring system may not detect a tire that is low, or may not turn on the tire pressure warning light the way it should. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal standard for tire pressure monitoring systems. Dealers will update the software free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Jeep dealer to have the software updated, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (the recall number is 54D).
3. The software update is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V559000. Official notice

Component
TIRES:PRESSURE MONITORING AND REGULATING SYSTEMS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Jeep Wagoneer, 2024-2026 Jeep Grand Cherokee, Jeep Grand Cherokee L, Jeep Grand Cherokee 4XE, Jeep Grand Wagoneer, 2025 Jeep Wagoneer L, 2025-2026 Jeep Grand Wagoneer L, 2026 Jeep Cherokee, and Jeep Grand Wagoneer L PHEV vehicles. Due to a software error, the tire pressure monitoring system may fail to detect low tire pressure and/or fail to illuminate the tire pressure monitoring system (TPMS) warning light as intended. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 138, "Tire Pressure Monitoring System."
Safety risk
A TPMS warning light that fails to notify the driver of low tire pressure increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the radio frequency hub software, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed beginning September 29, 2026. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 54D. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov on September 9, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
