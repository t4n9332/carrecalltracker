Tahoe, Suburban, Yukon and Escalade third-row seat belt flaw

NHTSA campaign 22V617000 · Reported 2022-08-11 · General Motors, LLC · high severity

What's wrong, in plain English

This affects SUVs equipped with third-row seats. The small metal rivet that holds the seat belt buckle to its bracket may have been made wrong on the left or right third-row belt. If the rivet is not formed properly, the buckle may not hold a passenger in place during a crash, so someone in the back row could be hurt. A dealer will inspect both third-row buckles and replace them if needed, free of charge.

How serious is it?

Serious for anyone riding in the third row; the notice does not say to stop driving, but get the buckles checked before carrying passengers back there.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your SUV is included (GM's recall number is N222372380).
2. Call Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020, GMC at 1-800-462-8782, or Cadillac at 1-800-458-8006, or contact your dealer, to schedule the inspection.
3. Have both third-row seat belt buckles inspected and replaced if necessary. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (M) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Cadillac Escalade, Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Suburban, Tahoe, GMC Yukon, and Yukon XL vehicles equipped with third-row seats. The rivet that retains the buckle to the mounting bracket in the left or right side third-row seat belt buckle assembly may have been improperly formed.
Safety risk
A seat belt with a deformed rivet may not properly restrain an occupant, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the rivet head formation on both the left and right side third-row seat belt buckle assemblies and replace seat belt buckle assemblies, as necessary, free of charge. Interim owner notification letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed September 28, 2022. Owner notification letters were mailed on November 1, 2022. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020; Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006; and GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N222372380.
