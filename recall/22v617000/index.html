GM SUVs Third-Row Seat Belt Buckle Rivet Flaw

NHTSA campaign 22V617000 · Reported 2022-08-11 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A seat belt buckle with a deformed rivet may not properly restrain an occupant in a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Cadillac Escalade, Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Suburban, Tahoe, GMC Yukon, and Yukon XL vehicles with third-row seats. The rivet that holds the buckle to its mounting bracket in the left or right third-row seat belt buckle may have been improperly formed. A seat belt with a deformed rivet may not properly restrain a passenger. Dealers will inspect the rivet on both third-row seat belt buckles and replace the buckle assemblies if needed, at no cost.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact your dealer — Chevrolet: 1-800-222-1020, Cadillac: 1-800-458-8006, GMC: 1-800-462-8782.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (M) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Cadillac Escalade, Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Suburban, Tahoe, GMC Yukon, and Yukon XL vehicles equipped with third-row seats. The rivet that retains the buckle to the mounting bracket in the left or right side third-row seat belt buckle assembly may have been improperly formed.
Safety risk
A seat belt with a deformed rivet may not properly restrain an occupant, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the rivet head formation on both the left and right side third-row seat belt buckle assemblies and replace seat belt buckle assemblies, as necessary, free of charge. Interim owner notification letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed September 28, 2022. Owner notification letters were mailed on November 1, 2022. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020; Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006; and GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N222372380.
