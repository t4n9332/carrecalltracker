What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Bolt EV and Bolt EUV vehicles that were previously repaired under an earlier recall for the vehicle's battery. On these vehicles, the advanced diagnostic software meant to watch for defective battery modules may not have been installed correctly. Because of this, the software may fail to detect a defective battery module, which could possibly result in a battery fire.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if it's included in this recall.
2. Interim steps: limit charging to 90%, charge more frequently, avoid depleting below 70 miles of range, and park outside (not indoors overnight) after charging.
3. Contact the Bolt EV Concierge Team at 1-833-382-4389 for a dealer to reinstall the software; the repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V481000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2020-2022 Chevrolet Bolt EV and 2022 Chevrolet Bolt EUV vehicles that were previously remedied under recall number 21V-650. The advanced diagnostic software may have been incorrectly installed and fail to detect defective battery modules, possibly resulting in a battery fire.
Safety risk
A battery fire increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to take the following interim steps: Set the Target Charge Level feature in their vehicle to limit the charge level to 90%, charge their vehicle more frequently, avoid depleting the battery to 70 miles range remaining, park outside after charging, and do not charge the vehicle indoors overnight. Dealers will reinstall the advanced diagnostic software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on August 1, 2024. Owners may contact the Bolt EV Concierge Team at 1-833-382-4389. GM's numbers for this recall are N242443000 and N242443002.
