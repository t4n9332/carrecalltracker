Chevy Bolt battery software fix may not have installed

NHTSA campaign 24V481000 · Reported 2024-06-27 · General Motors, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

A battery fire is serious, but GM does not tell you to stop driving — you can keep driving as long as you follow the charging limits and park outside after every charge.

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

What's wrong, in plain English

Your Bolt was already fixed under an earlier battery recall, when a dealer added software that watches the battery and spots a bad battery module. On these cars that software may not have installed properly, so a defective module could go undetected and the battery could catch fire. Until a dealer reloads the software, GM says to charge only to 90%, charge more often, don't drop below 70 miles of range, park outside after charging, and never charge indoors overnight.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Bolt is included.
2. Follow the interim steps now: 90% charge limit, charge often, stay above 70 miles of range, park outside after charging, no indoor overnight charging.
3. Book the free software reinstall with a Chevrolet dealer, or call the Bolt EV Concierge Team at 1-833-382-4389.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2020-2022 Chevrolet Bolt EV and 2022 Chevrolet Bolt EUV vehicles that were previously remedied under recall number 21V-650. The advanced diagnostic software may have been incorrectly installed and fail to detect defective battery modules, possibly resulting in a battery fire.
Safety risk
A battery fire increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to take the following interim steps: Set the Target Charge Level feature in their vehicle to limit the charge level to 90%, charge their vehicle more frequently, avoid depleting the battery to 70 miles range remaining, park outside after charging, and do not charge the vehicle indoors overnight. Dealers will reinstall the advanced diagnostic software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on August 1, 2024. Owners may contact the Bolt EV Concierge Team at 1-833-382-4389. GM's numbers for this recall are N242443000 and N242443002.
