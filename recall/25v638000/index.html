Audi S6 Sportback E-Tron front seat belts may fail

NHTSA campaign 25V638000 · Reported 2025-09-24 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Audi S6 Sportback E-TRON

What's wrong, in plain English

Inside each front seat belt is a spring-loaded bar that helps the belt hold you during a crash. On these cars, that bar may have been damaged while the belts were being built. Nothing looks or feels different day to day, but in a crash a damaged bar can break, and the belt may not hold the person in the seat properly. The same problem covers certain Volkswagen GTI, Golf R and other Audi models.

How serious is it?

Audi has not told owners to stop driving, but a front seat belt that does not restrain properly means a higher chance of injury in a crash, so schedule the fix promptly.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is on the list.
2. Call Audi at 1-800-253-2834 (recall 69UM) or Volkswagen at 1-800-893-5298 (recall 694T) to book an appointment.
3. The dealer will replace one or both front seat belts free of charge; owner letters were mailed October 30–31, 2025.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:RETRACTOR
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2025 GTI, Golf R, Audi A5, A6 Sportback E-Tron, S5, S6 Sportback E-Tron, 2026 Q4 Sportback E-Tron, and Q4 E-Tron vehicles. The torsion bar in one or both front seat belt retractors may have been damaged during production.
Safety risk
In a crash, a damaged torsion bar in a retractor could fail. If this happens, the seat belt may not properly restrain an occupant during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace one or both front seat belts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 30, 2025, and October 31, 2025. Owners may contact Audi at 1-800-253-2834 or Volkswagen at 1-800-893-5298. Audi's number for this recall is 69UM; Volkswagen’s number for this recall is 694T. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning September 26, 2025.
