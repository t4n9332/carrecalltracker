Chevrolet, GMC, Buick, Cadillac fuel pump leak fire risk

NHTSA campaign 18V358000 · Reported 2018-05-31 · General Motors LLC · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Buick Lacrosse
  • 2018 Cadillac ATS
  • 2018 Chevrolet Colorado
  • 2018 Chevrolet Equinox
  • 2018 Chevrolet Malibu
  • 2018 GMC Acadia
  • 2018 GMC Canyon
  • 2018 GMC Terrain

What's wrong, in plain English

The high pressure fuel pump on these vehicles can come loose from the bracket that holds it in place. Once loose, the pump can damage the high pressure fuel line next to it. A damaged line can leak gasoline into the engine compartment, where hot parts can set it on fire. A dealer will replace both the fuel pump and the fuel pipe at no charge to you.

How serious is it?

Serious — a leaking high pressure fuel line can start an engine fire, and although GM does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside, you should schedule the free repair quickly and stop driving if you smell gasoline.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm campaign 18V358000 covers your vehicle.
2. Make an appointment with your dealer to have the fuel pump and fuel pipe replaced free of charge.
3. For questions call Buick 1-800-521-7300, Cadillac 1-800-458-8006, Chevrolet 1-800-222-1020, or GMC 1-800-462-8782; recall 18188.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2015-2018 GMC Canyon, 2016-2017 Buick Envision, 2016-2018 Chevrolet Colorado and Malibu, 2017-2018 GMC Acadia, 2018 Buick LaCrosse, Cadillac ATS, Chevrolet Equinox, and GMC Terrain vehicles. The high pressure fuel pump may detach from its mounting flange, possibly resulting in the pump damaging the high pressure fuel line.
Safety risk
A damaged fuel line can create a fuel leak, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace the high pressure fuel pump, and high pressure fuel pipe, free of charge. The recall began July 2, 2018. Owners may contact Buick customer service at 1-800-521-7300, Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006, Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020, or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is 18188.
