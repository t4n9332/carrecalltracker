Infiniti QX80 backup camera screen can go blank

NHTSA campaign 24V747000 · Reported 2024-10-03 · Nissan North America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Infiniti QX80

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2025 Infiniti QX80 SUVs with the Around View Monitor camera system have a software bug. When you shift into reverse, the backup camera picture can freeze or the screen can go blank instead of showing what is behind you. Without that picture you cannot see people, pets, or objects behind the vehicle as well as you should. Because of this, the SUVs do not meet the federal rear-visibility rule. Dealers will reload the camera system software at no cost.

How serious is it?

Losing the rear camera view while backing up increases the chance of a crash, and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so you can keep using the vehicle while you arrange the free fix.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your QX80 is included. 2. Contact your Infiniti dealer, or Infiniti customer service at 1-800-662-6200 (recalls R24B5 and R24C2), to book service. 3. Have the camera software reprogrammed free of charge.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2025 INFINITI QX80 vehicles equipped with an Around View Monitor (AVM). A software error may cause the rearview camera to freeze and display a blank screen when the vehicle is in reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
The loss of the rearview camera image while in reverse can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the AVM software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 2, 2024. Owners may contact INFINITI's customer service at 1-800-662-6200. Nissan's numbers for this recall are R24B5 and R24C2.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
