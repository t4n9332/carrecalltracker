What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2025 INFINITI QX80 SUVs have a software error in the Around View Monitor system. When the vehicle is shifted into reverse, the rearview camera image can freeze and go blank instead of showing what is behind the vehicle. This means the driver may lose the rearview camera image while backing up, so they cannot rely on it to see what is behind the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact an INFINITI dealer, or call INFINITI customer service at 1-800-662-6200, to schedule the software update.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V747000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2025 INFINITI QX80 vehicles equipped with an Around View Monitor (AVM). A software error may cause the rearview camera to freeze and display a blank screen when the vehicle is in reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
The loss of the rearview camera image while in reverse can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the AVM software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 2, 2024. Owners may contact INFINITI's customer service at 1-800-662-6200. Nissan's numbers for this recall are R24B5 and R24C2.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
