Nissan Pathfinder second-row seat backs may have weak welds

NHTSA campaign 21V743000 · Reported 2021-09-23 · Nissan North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Serious for second-row passengers in a crash, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or to keep anyone out of those seats.

What's wrong, in plain English

The metal frames inside the second-row seat backs may not have been welded together well enough. If those welds come apart, the seat back can give way instead of staying firm. In a crash, the seat is a big part of what holds a passenger in place, so a seat back that separates may not hold someone properly and that person could be hurt more badly. A dealer will inspect the seats and replace any that are affected.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Pathfinder is covered. 2. Contact your Nissan dealer, or Nissan at 1-800-867-7669, and mention recall PC845, PC846 or PC847. 3. Have the seats inspected and replaced if needed — there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2022 Pathfinder vehicles. The second-row seat back frames may have insufficient welds that could separate.
Safety risk
Seat backs with separated welds may fail to adequately restrain occupants during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replaced the seat assemblies as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 17, 2021. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's numbers for this recall are PC845, PC846, and PC847.
