Nissan Pathfinder Seat Back Welds Could Separate

NHTSA campaign 21V743000 · Reported 2021-09-23 · Nissan North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the seat back weld fails, it may not properly hold you in place during a crash, raising the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Nissan Pathfinder SUVs have a problem with the second-row seat backs. The metal frame inside each seat back may not have been welded together securely enough, and the welds could come apart over time. If that happens, the seat back may not hold up the way it's supposed to. Nissan is recalling these vehicles so dealers can inspect the seat frames and replace the seat assembly if the welds are found to be insufficient.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm it's included.
2. Contact a Nissan dealer or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 to schedule an inspection.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2022 Pathfinder vehicles. The second-row seat back frames may have insufficient welds that could separate.
Safety risk
Seat backs with separated welds may fail to adequately restrain occupants during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replaced the seat assemblies as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 17, 2021. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's numbers for this recall are PC845, PC846, and PC847.
