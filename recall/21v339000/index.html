Audi SQ7, SQ8 and RS Engine Cylinders May Crack

NHTSA campaign 21V339000 · Reported 2021-05-12 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Audi RS Q8
  • 2021 Audi SQ7
  • 2021 Audi SQ8

What's wrong, in plain English

Cracks can form on the inner walls of the engine cylinders in these high-performance Audis. Those cracks can damage the engine, make it stall while you are driving, or cause an oil leak. Stalling in traffic raises the chance of a crash, and an oil leak raises the chance of a fire. There is no quick patch for this problem. The dealer replaces the entire engine at no cost to you, so plan on leaving the car for a while.

How serious is it?

Serious: the engine can stall while you are driving or leak oil and catch fire, so arrange the repair promptly, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is part of the recall.
2. Contact your Audi dealer about recall 10G9 to schedule the engine replacement, or call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834.
3. The new engine and the work to install it are free of charge; ask the dealer about a loaner while the car is in the shop.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2021 Audi SQ7, RSQ8, RS7, RS6 AVANT, and SQ8 vehicles. Cracks may develop on the surface of the engine cylinder bore.
Safety risk
Cracks may cause engine damage and a stall or an oil leak, increasing the risk of a crash or fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the engine, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 9, 2021. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 10G9.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
