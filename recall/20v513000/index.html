Ram 1500 floormat can trap the accelerator pedal

NHTSA campaign 20V513000 · Reported 2020-08-27 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 RAM 1500
  • 2020 RAM 1500

What's wrong, in plain English

These Ram 1500 trucks have pedals that move forward and back to fit the driver. In some pedal positions, the driver's floormat can get in the way of the gas pedal and stop it from springing back to idle when you lift your foot. That means the truck may keep pulling when you expect it to slow down. Dealers will modify the driver's floormat so it can no longer interfere with the pedal, at no charge.

How serious is it?

A gas pedal that sticks instead of returning to idle can keep the truck accelerating when you want to slow down and raises the risk of a crash, so get this fixed promptly, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your truck is covered. 2. Contact your Ram dealer or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall W63) to book the free floormat modification. 3. In the meantime, make sure the driver's floormat is properly positioned and not stacked on top of another mat.

Official notice

Component
VEHICLE SPEED CONTROL:ACCELERATOR PEDAL
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Ram 1500 vehicles equipped with adjustable pedals. In certain accelerator pedal positions, the floormats may prevent the pedal from returning to the normal resting (idle) position.
Safety risk
A pedal that does not return as expected may affect the vehicle's deceleration, thereby increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will modify the driver's floormat to remove the potential for pedal interference, free of charge. The recall began September 10, 2020. Owners may contact FCA US LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is W63.
