Audi Q3 Driver Seat Bolt May Fail

NHTSA campaign 20V644000 · Reported Oct 19, 2020 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A failed seat bolt could increase the risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The driver's seat frame in this vehicle may not have been bolted to the seat rail correctly. If this bolt connection fails, the seat may not stay securely attached. This increases the risk of injury to the driver if the vehicle is in a crash. To fix this, dealers will inspect the bolt connection and, if necessary, replace the seat structure, free of charge.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
Contact an Audi dealer, or call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834, to schedule the repair.
The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V644000. Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling one 2020 Audi Q3 vehicle. The driver's seat frame may have incorrectly been bolted to the seat rail.
Safety risk
The incorrect bolt connection could fail, increasing the risk of injury in the event of a crash.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the bolt connection and, as necessary, replace the seat structure, free of charge. The recall began November 10, 2020. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 72L4.
