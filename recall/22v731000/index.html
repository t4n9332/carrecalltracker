Ford F53 RV chassis rear sway bar brackets can break

NHTSA campaign 22V731000 · Reported 2022-09-30 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Ford F53
  • 2021 Ford F53

What's wrong, in plain English

These are the bare chassis that motorhomes are built on. The brackets holding the rear sway bar — the part that keeps the vehicle steady in turns — can break and fall off while you're driving. A bracket lying in the road is a hazard for you and for anyone behind you. A dealer will check how tight the bolts are on both rear brackets and replace the bolts and nuts if they need it.

How serious is it?

Ford has not issued a do-not-drive warning, so you may keep driving, but a part breaking off at highway speed can cause a crash for you or the vehicle behind you, so schedule this repair soon.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected. 2. Call a Ford dealer and reference recall 22S62, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. 3. Have both rear brackets inspected and the bolts replaced as needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2021 F-53 recreational stripped chassis vehicles. The rear stabilizer bar attachment brackets may break and separate from the vehicle.
Safety risk
Brackets that separate from the vehicle can create a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the bolt tightness for both rear stabilizer attachment brackets and replace the weld nuts and bolts, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 8, 2023. Remedy parts are expected to become ready February 28, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S62.
