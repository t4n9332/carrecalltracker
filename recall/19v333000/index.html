Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster foldaway seats may unlatch

NHTSA campaign 19V333000 · Reported 2019-04-29 · New England Wheels · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Ford Transit
  • 2018 RAM Promaster
  • 2019 RAM Promaster

What's wrong, in plain English

These vans were modified with Freedman Go-ES Foldaway seats, which fold up out of the way and then swing back down for passengers. When the seat cushion is swung back down into the upright seating position, it may not lock securely. Under a heavy load — such as a crash or a hard, sudden stop — the seat back can shift instead of holding firm. Freedman will repair the foldaway seats at no cost.

How serious is it?

A seat that moves in a crash means a passenger can be hurt worse; the van can still be driven, but the affected seats should be repaired before carrying passengers in them.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is included.
2. Call Freedman customer service at 1-800-443-4540, or New England Wheels at 1-800-886-9247, to arrange the seat repair.
3. Have the foldaway seats fixed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
New England Wheels (New England) is recalling certain 2014-2018 Ford Transit and 2014-2019 Ram Promaster 2500 and 3500 vehicles modified to be equipped with Freedman Go-ES Foldaway seats. When the back seat cushion is rotated from the stowed position to the upright position, the seat cushion may not remain locked into place when under load, such as in a crash or a sudden stop.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, if the seat back moves, the seat occupant has an increased risk of injury.
Remedy
New England has notified owners, directing them to contact Freedman for repairs to the foldaway seats, free of charge. The recall began May 27, 2019. Owners may contact Freedman customer service at 1-800-443-4540 or New England at 1-800-886-9247.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
