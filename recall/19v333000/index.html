Modified Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster seats may not lock

NHTSA campaign 19V333000 · Reported Apr 29, 2019 · New England Wheels

medium How serious is it? About this label

In a crash, a seat back that moves increases the risk of injury to the person sitting in it. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

New England Wheels is recalling certain Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster 2500 and 3500 vans that were modified with Freedman Go-ES Foldaway seats. When the back seat cushion is turned up from its stowed position into the upright position, it may not stay locked in place when weight pushes on it, such as in a crash or a sudden stop. Owners have been notified and told to contact Freedman, which will repair the foldaway seats at no charge.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your van is covered. 2. Contact Freedman customer service at 1-800-443-4540 to arrange the seat repair; you can also reach New England Wheels at 1-800-886-9247. 3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V333000. Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
New England Wheels (New England) is recalling certain 2014-2018 Ford Transit and 2014-2019 Ram Promaster 2500 and 3500 vehicles modified to be equipped with Freedman Go-ES Foldaway seats. When the back seat cushion is rotated from the stowed position to the upright position, the seat cushion may not remain locked into place when under load, such as in a crash or a sudden stop.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, if the seat back moves, the seat occupant has an increased risk of injury.
Remedy
New England has notified owners, directing them to contact Freedman for repairs to the foldaway seats, free of charge. The recall began May 27, 2019. Owners may contact Freedman customer service at 1-800-443-4540 or New England at 1-800-886-9247.
