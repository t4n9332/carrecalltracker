Honda CR-V tires missing required date code marking

NHTSA campaign 24V508000 · Reported 2024-07-02 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Honda CR-V

What's wrong, in plain English

The code stamped on the tire sidewall that identifies each tire is missing its four-digit date section. That date is how tire makers and owners tell which tires are covered by a safety recall. Without it, you might never learn your tires were recalled and could keep driving on them. This recall is about that missing marking, which federal tire rules require, and Honda will replace the tires if needed.

How serious is it?

The risk here is indirect — the tires are not reported to be failing — so there is no park-it warning and the vehicle is safe to keep driving while you arrange the inspection.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your CR-V is included.
2. Call your Honda dealer, or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (recall BIS), to schedule a tire inspection.
3. The dealer inspects and replaces the tires as necessary, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
TIRES:MARKINGS
Summary
Honda (America Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2024 CR-V vehicles. The tire identification number (TIN) is missing the four-digit date code. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 139, "New Pneumatic Radial Tires for Light Vehicles."
Safety risk
An owner whose TIN information is missing may not recognize that their vehicle is involved in a safety recall and continue to drive on recalled tires, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the tires as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 27, 2024. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is BIS.
