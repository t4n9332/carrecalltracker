Honda CR-V tires missing required date code marking

NHTSA campaign 24V508000 · Reported 2024-07-02 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Honda says an owner who cannot read the full tire number may not realize the tires are under recall and may keep driving on them, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Honda is recalling certain 2024 CR-V vehicles because the identification number stamped on the tires is missing its four-digit date code. That code is part of the marking required by a federal safety standard for tires on light vehicles, so the tires as built do not meet that standard. Without the full number, an owner may not be able to tell whether their tires are covered by a safety recall. Dealers will inspect the tires and replace them if needed.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm whether your CR-V is included.
2. Contact a Honda dealer, or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (Honda recall number BIS), to arrange the inspection.
3. The inspection and any tire replacement are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
TIRES:MARKINGS
Summary
Honda (America Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2024 CR-V vehicles. The tire identification number (TIN) is missing the four-digit date code. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 139, "New Pneumatic Radial Tires for Light Vehicles."
Safety risk
An owner whose TIN information is missing may not recognize that their vehicle is involved in a safety recall and continue to drive on recalled tires, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the tires as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 27, 2024. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is BIS.
