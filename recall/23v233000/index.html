Rivian R1S reverse lights hard to see from angles

NHTSA campaign 23V233000 · Reported 2023-03-31 · Rivian Automotive, LLC

low How serious is it? About this label

Reduced visibility of the reverse lights increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The white lights that come on when you shift into reverse can't be seen from some angles on these SUVs. That means a person walking behind you, or a driver in the next lane over, may not get the usual warning that you're about to back up. The lights don't meet the federal rule for how visible they have to be. Rivian will repair or replace the reverse lights at no cost to you.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your R1S is affected. 2. Contact Rivian about recall FSAM-1216 at 1-855-748-4265. 3. Have the reverse lights repaired or replaced — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BACK UP LIGHTS
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022-2023 R1S vehicles. The reverse light beam may not be visible from certain angles. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Reduced visibility of the reverse lights increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will repair or replace the reverse lights, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 12, 2023. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-855-748-4265. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1216.
