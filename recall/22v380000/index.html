Honda CR-V Fuel Gauge May Read Inaccurately

NHTSA campaign 22V380000 · Reported May 26, 2022 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

medium How serious is it? About this label

An inaccurate fuel gauge can cause the vehicle to unexpectedly run out of fuel and stall, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Honda CR-V vehicles, a clip that holds a part inside the fuel tank wasn't secured properly and may come loose. This can cause the fuel gauge to show an inaccurate reading of how much fuel is left in the tank.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138, to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V380000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2020 CR-V vehicles. The clip for the absorber inside the fuel tank was not secured properly and may detach, which can cause an inaccurate fuel gauge reading.
Safety risk
An inaccurate fuel gauge may result in the vehicle unexpectedly running out of fuel and stalling, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel tank, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 5, 2022. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is RCB.
