Nissan 370Z Driver Curtain Air Bag May Not Deploy

NHTSA campaign 18V153000 · Reported 2018-03-06 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

An incorrectly installed curtain air bag can prevent it from deploying correctly, increasing the risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The curtain air bag on the driver's side may have been installed incorrectly at the factory. This can affect how the air bag deploys in a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Nissan dealer; you can also call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2018 Nissan 370Z vehicles. The driver's side curtain air bag may have been installed incorrectly during production, possibly affecting its deployment. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 214, "Side Impact Protection," and 226, "Ejection Mitigation."
Safety risk
The incorrect curtain air bag installation can prevent the air bag from deploying correctly in the event of a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the curtain air bag, correcting its installation as necessary, free of charge. The recall began May 1, 2018. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
