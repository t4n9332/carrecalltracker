Dodge Charger, Challenger, Chrysler 300 tire pressure sensors fail

NHTSA campaign 22V808000 · Reported 2022-10-27 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

This is a moderate risk because a blown tire can cause a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so keep driving but check your tire pressures yourself with a gauge until the sensors are fixed.

What's wrong, in plain English

These Dodge Challenger, Dodge Charger, and Chrysler 300 cars have small sensors in the wheels that watch your tire pressure. The batteries inside those sensors can die early, so a sensor stops working. Then the car cannot warn you when a tire is actually low. Worse, if the warning light is already lit because of a dead sensor battery, you may ignore it and miss a real low tire. Driving on a low tire can cause it to fail.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is part of recall ZB5.
2. Call your Chrysler or Dodge dealer, or FCA US at 1-800-853-1403, to set up an inspection.
3. The dealer will inspect and replace the tire pressure sensors as needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
TIRES:PRESSURE MONITORING AND REGULATING SYSTEMS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Dodge Challenger, Dodge Charger, and Chrysler 300 vehicles. The Tire Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS) sensor battery may fail prematurely and cause the sensor to become inoperative. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 138, "Tire Pressure Monitoring Systems."
Safety risk
Inoperative TPMS sensors will not alert the driver to low tire pressure. In addition, a TPMS warning light that is already illuminated from a failed battery sensor will mask an actual tire pressure failure issue. Either of these scenarios can result in tire failure and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the TPMS sensors, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 10, 2022. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is ZB5. This recall is an expansion of previous recall number 22V-504.
