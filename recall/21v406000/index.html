Mercedes-Benz convertible rear headrests may be mounted incorrectly

NHTSA campaign 21V406000 · Reported May 28, 2021 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Headrests that are not mounted correctly could move or come off the seat during a crash, which increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Mercedes-Benz is recalling certain 2021 C300, C43 AMG, C63 AMG, C63 AMG S, E450 and E53 AMG Cabriolet convertibles. The headrests on the rear seats may not be attached to the seatbacks the right way. Dealers will inspect the guide bushings in the rear seatbacks and replace them if they are needed. Owner notification letters were mailed in July 2021, and the work is done at no charge to the owner.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer to have the rear seatbacks inspected. MBUSA customer service is 1-800-367-6372.
3. The inspection and any replacement parts are free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V406000. Official notice

Component
SEATS:MID/REAR ASSEMBLY:HEAD RESTRAINT
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2021 C300 Cabriolet, C43 AMG Cabriolet, C63 AMG Cabriolet, C63 AMG S Cabriolet, E450 Cabriolet, and E53 AMG Cabriolet vehicles. The rear headrests may be incorrectly mounted to the seatbacks.
Safety risk
Incorrectly mounted headrests could move or detach from the seat during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the rear seatback guide bushings and replace them if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 16, 2021. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2021060010.
