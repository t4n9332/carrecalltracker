Mercedes-Benz Cabriolet rear headrests may be mounted incorrectly

NHTSA campaign 21V406000 · Reported 2021-05-28 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Incorrectly mounted headrests could move or detach from the seat during a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The rear headrests in these convertibles may not have been attached to the seatbacks correctly at the factory. The guide bushings that hold a headrest in place can be wrong or poorly assembled, so a headrest could shift out of position or come off the seat during a crash, which is exactly when a back-seat passenger needs it most. A dealer will inspect the rear seatback guide bushings and replace them if necessary, at no cost to you.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is covered by recall 21V406000 (Mercedes' number is 2021060010).
2. Contact your Mercedes-Benz dealer, or MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372, to schedule the inspection.
3. Have the dealer inspect and replace the parts as needed; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:MID/REAR ASSEMBLY:HEAD RESTRAINT
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2021 C300 Cabriolet, C43 AMG Cabriolet, C63 AMG Cabriolet, C63 AMG S Cabriolet, E450 Cabriolet, and E53 AMG Cabriolet vehicles. The rear headrests may be incorrectly mounted to the seatbacks.
Safety risk
Incorrectly mounted headrests could move or detach from the seat during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the rear seatback guide bushings and replace them if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 16, 2021. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2021060010.
