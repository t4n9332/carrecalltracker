Gulf States Toyota vehicles have wrong weight capacity labels

NHTSA campaign 24V548000 · Reported 2024-07-23 · Gulf States Toyota, Inc.

low How serious is it? About this label

Minor for everyday driving and safe to keep driving, but an overloaded vehicle handles poorly and can crash, so be careful with heavy loads until the new label arrives.

What's wrong, in plain English

These Toyotas were sold with accessories added through Gulf States Toyota. When accessories are added, a sticker is supposed to be placed on the vehicle showing how much less weight it can now carry in people and cargo. On these vehicles that sticker shows the wrong numbers. If you go by it, you could load the vehicle heavier than it is rated for. Gulf States Toyota is mailing corrected labels to owners at no charge; nothing on the vehicle needs repair.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Watch your mail for the corrected label, or call Gulf States Toyota at 1-800-444-1074 (recall 24R2) if you did not get one.
3. Put the new label on over the old one — it costs nothing, and no dealer visit is needed.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Gulf States Toyota, Inc. (GST) is recalling certain Toyota 2023 GR Supra, 2024 4 Runner, Corolla, Grand Highlander, Grand Highlander Hybrid, Land Cruiser Hybrid, Tacoma, Tacoma Hybrid, 2023-2024 BZ4X, Corolla Cross Hybrid, GR Corolla, GR86, Highlander, Highlander Hybrid, Prius, Prius Prime, Sequoia Hybrid, Tundra, Tundra Hybrid, Venza Hybrid, 2023-2025 Crown, and 2025 Camry Hybrid vehicles equipped with GST accessories. The load carrying capacity modification label may display inaccurate added weight values. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
A vehicle with an incorrect maximum capacity weight value may be overloaded, which can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
GST will mail new labels to owners, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 16, 2024. Owners may contact GST customer service at 1-800-444-1074. GST's number for this recall is 24R2.
