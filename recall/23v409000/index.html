BMW iX cruise control can turn itself back on

NHTSA campaign 23V409000 · Reported 2023-06-07 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 BMW IX
  • 2023 BMW IX
  • 2024 BMW IX

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling certain 2022-2024 iX electric SUVs because of a problem with the cruise control. If you turn the steering wheel at low speed, the cruise control can switch itself back on even though you did not ask it to. When that happens, the car can speed up suddenly on its own. The fix is a free software update at the dealer, and owner letters were mailed August 2, 2023.

How serious is it?

A car that speeds up on its own without warning can cause a crash, and while BMW has not told owners to stop driving, you should get the free software update as soon as you can and be careful using cruise control at low speeds until then.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your iX is included.
2. Call your BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, and book the software update.
3. Have the update done - the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
VEHICLE SPEED CONTROL:CRUISE CONTROL
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2022-2024 iX xDrive50 and iX M60 vehicles. The driver may unintentionally reactivate the cruise control system while turning the steering wheel at low speeds, resulting in a sudden increase of vehicle speed.
Safety risk
A sudden increase in vehicle speed can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the vehicle software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 2, 2023. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
