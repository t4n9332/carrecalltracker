BMW iX Cruise Control May Suddenly Speed Up

NHTSA campaign 23V409000 · Reported Jun 7, 2023 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

What's wrong, in plain English

In certain BMW iX vehicles, turning the steering wheel at low speeds may accidentally reactivate the cruise control system, even though the driver did not intend to turn it back on. If this happens, the vehicle's speed can suddenly increase without the driver asking for it. This unexpected jump in speed can occur while the driver is maneuvering the vehicle at low speed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a dealer, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. The software update will be done free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V409000. Official notice

Component
VEHICLE SPEED CONTROL:CRUISE CONTROL
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2022-2024 iX xDrive50 and iX M60 vehicles. The driver may unintentionally reactivate the cruise control system while turning the steering wheel at low speeds, resulting in a sudden increase of vehicle speed.
Safety risk
A sudden increase in vehicle speed can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the vehicle software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 2, 2023. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
