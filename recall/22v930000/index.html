Chevrolet Bolt EV seat belt part can cause fire

NHTSA campaign 22V930000 · Reported 2022-12-15 · General Motors, LLC · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Chevrolet Bolt EV
  • 2020 Chevrolet Bolt EV
  • 2021 Chevrolet Bolt EV
  • 2022 Chevrolet Bolt EV
  • 2023 Chevrolet Bolt EV

What's wrong, in plain English

Seat belts have a device that yanks the belt tight in a crash. It works with a small charge, and the hot gas it lets out can escape near the floor by the door pillar. On these cars that hot exhaust can set the carpet fibers on fire after a crash. Dealers add a metal foil shield to the carpet in that spot, and a cover over the device if needed. The work is free.

How serious is it?

A fire after a crash can cause injury, so this is serious, though the problem only happens once a crash sets the belt tightener off; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Bolt EV is included. 2. Call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or your dealer and give recall number N222383790. 3. Have the foil and cover installed at the dealer; there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:ROOF AND PILLARS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2017-2023 Chevrolet Bolt EV vehicles. After a crash with seat belt pretensioner deployment, the pretensioner exhaust may ignite carpet fibers near the B-pillar, causing a fire.
Safety risk
A vehicle fire can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will install metal foil at the carpet near the pretensioner exhaust, and install a pretensioner cover as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed between January 23, 2023 and April 25, 2023. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N222383790.
