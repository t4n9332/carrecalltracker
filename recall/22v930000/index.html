Chevrolet Bolt EV Seat Belt Fire Risk After Crash

NHTSA campaign 22V930000 · Reported 2022-12-15 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A vehicle fire after a crash can increase the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

After a crash where the seat belt pretensioner goes off, hot gas released by the pretensioner may set fire to carpet fibers near the pillar next to the front seat. Dealers will install a piece of metal foil over the carpet near the pretensioner, and add a cover over the pretensioner if needed, at no cost to the owner.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Bolt EV is included. 2. Contact your dealer, or call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. 3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:ROOF AND PILLARS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2017-2023 Chevrolet Bolt EV vehicles. After a crash with seat belt pretensioner deployment, the pretensioner exhaust may ignite carpet fibers near the B-pillar, causing a fire.
Safety risk
A vehicle fire can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will install metal foil at the carpet near the pretensioner exhaust, and install a pretensioner cover as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed between January 23, 2023 and April 25, 2023. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N222383790.
