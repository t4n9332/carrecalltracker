Kia K5 rear window trim may loosen and detach

NHTSA campaign 25V493000 · Reported 2025-07-28 · Kia America, Inc. · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Kia K5
  • 2024 Kia K5
  • 2025 Kia K5

What's wrong, in plain English

The trim pieces along the outside of the rear windows, on the pillars behind the back doors, may not stay attached. On some cars the left and right pieces can work loose and fall off the vehicle while you are driving. A piece of trim in the roadway can strike or startle the drivers behind you. A dealer will inspect the trim assemblies on both sides of the car and replace them if necessary, at no cost.

How serious is it?

Low for you: the car is safe to drive and there is no danger inside it, but trim that falls off can become a road hazard and cause a crash for other drivers.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is covered by recall 25V493000 (Kia's number is SC346).
2. Contact your Kia dealer, or Kia customer service at 800-333-4542, to schedule the inspection.
3. Have the dealer inspect and replace the trim as needed; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:ROOF AND PILLARS
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2023-2025 K5 vehicles. The left and right rear window trim pieces may loosen and detach.
Safety risk
Detached trim pieces can create a road hazard for other vehicles, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the C-pillar trim assemblies, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 19, 2025. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC346.
