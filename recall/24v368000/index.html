Lincoln Aviator backup camera image can flicker badly

NHTSA campaign 24V368000 · Reported 2024-05-24 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Lincoln Aviator
  • 2021 Lincoln Aviator
  • 2022 Lincoln Aviator
  • 2023 Lincoln Aviator
  • 2024 Lincoln Aviator

What's wrong, in plain English

A module behind the Aviator's screen can pick up interference from a cell phone inside the vehicle. When that happens, the picture from the backup camera can flicker or look distorted while you are in reverse. That makes it harder to see people, pets, or objects behind you. Ford says owners should not use a cell phone while backing up until the fix is done. A dealer will add shielding to the module so phone signals no longer disturb the picture.

How serious is it?

A distorted rear camera view raises the chance of backing into something or someone; the vehicle is safe to keep driving, but do not use your phone while reversing and look over your shoulder until it is repaired.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Aviator is covered. 2. Keep phones out of use while reversing, and check your mirrors and surroundings. 3. Contact your Lincoln dealer or Ford at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 24S35) for the free shielding installation.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:DISPLAY FUNCTION
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2024 Lincoln Aviator vehicles. The accessory protocol image module (APIM) can be affected by electromagnetic interference with a cell phone in the vehicle, causing the rearview camera image to flicker or become distorted.
Safety risk
A flickering or distorted rearview camera image can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to use their cell phones while reversing until the remedy is completed. Dealers will install shielding on the accessory protocol image module (APIM), free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on October 23, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S35.
