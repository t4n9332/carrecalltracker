Subaru BRZ and Toyota GR86 rear turn signals fail

NHTSA campaign 23V609000 · Reported 2023-08-31 · Subaru of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Rear turn signals that do not work may fail to tell other drivers the vehicle is turning, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Subaru is recalling certain BRZ vehicles and Toyota GR86 vehicles because the rear turn signals may stop working from time to time. The problem may come and go rather than staying constant. Dealers will replace both rear combination lamp assemblies, the units that hold the rear lights, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed January 8, 2024. Subaru owners and Toyota owners each have their own customer service line for this recall.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Subaru owners can contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614; Toyota owners can contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331, or go through a dealer.
3. The replacement is free.

  • 2022 Toyota GR86

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TURN SIGNAL
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2022 BRZ and Toyota GR86 vehicles. The rear turn signals may intermittently become inoperative.
Safety risk
Inoperative rear turn signals may not notify other drivers of a turning vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace both rear combination lamp assemblies, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 8, 2024. Subaru owners may contact Subaru's customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Toyota owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Subaru's number for this recall is WRM-23.
