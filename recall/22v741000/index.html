BMW iX Electric Drive Gear Weld May Break

NHTSA campaign 22V741000 · Reported Oct 5, 2022 · BMW of North America, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

If the weld seam breaks, the vehicle could roll away on its own when the parking brake isn't applied, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling a 2023 iX M60 hybrid electric vehicle because the transmission final drive gear inside the electric drive unit may have an improperly manufactured weld seam. A dealer will replace the entire electric drive unit to fix the problem, free of charge. Because of the safety risk, BMW sent an interim owner notification letter ahead of the standard notification letter.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a BMW dealer, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. The electric drive unit replacement will be done free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V741000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling one 2023 iX M60 hybrid electric vehicle. The transmission final drive gear in the electric drive unit may have an improperly manufactured weld seam.
Safety risk
The gear weld seam could break, resulting in a vehicle rollaway if the parking brake is not applied, and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
A dealer will replace the electric drive unit, free of charge. Interim owner notification letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed on November 30, 2022. The owner notification letter is expected to be mailed November 28, 2022. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
