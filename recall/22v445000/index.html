Toyota Tundra rear axle shafts can come loose and separate

NHTSA campaign 22V445000 · Reported 2022-06-23 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Toyota Tundra
  • 2022 Toyota Tundra Hybrid

What's wrong, in plain English

The nuts holding the rear axle shafts in place can work loose over time. If they loosen enough, one or both axle shafts can pull away from the axle housing. When that happens, the truck can become unstable and the brakes may not work as well as they should. The dealer will check the rear axle, tighten the nuts to the correct setting, and replace any damaged axle parts if they find them. All of this is done at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Losing stability and braking while driving can cause a crash, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving the truck.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is covered. 2. Contact your Toyota dealer or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 (recall 22TA05) to schedule the inspection. 3. Have the axle nuts tightened and any damaged parts replaced — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AXLE ASSEMBLY:AXLE SHAFT
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2022 Tundra and Tundra Hybrid vehicles. The flange nuts on the axle shaft sub-assemblies can loosen over time and potentially cause one or both axle shaft sub-assemblies to separate from the axle housing.
Safety risk
Axle shaft separation can cause a loss of vehicle stability and brake performance, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the rear axle assembly, tighten the axle flange nuts, and replace any damaged axle-related components if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed July 15, 2022. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 22TA05.
