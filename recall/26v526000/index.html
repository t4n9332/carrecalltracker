What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2025 Nissan Altima vehicles may have had the front passenger air bag and the front passenger knee air bag installed with incorrect mounting fasteners. Because of this, these air bags may not deploy as intended if the vehicle is in a crash. This affects the front passenger seating position in affected vehicles, where both the main air bag and the knee air bag could be affected.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact your dealer or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 to schedule the repair.
3. The air bag mounting fastener replacement is done free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V526000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2025 Altima vehicles. The front passenger air bag and front passenger knee air bag may have been installed with incorrect mounting fasteners, which can cause the air bags not to deploy as intended.
Safety risk
Air bags that do not deploy as intended increase the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the air bag mounting fasteners, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed September 25, 2026. Owners may contact Nissan’s customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PD202. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning August 17, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
