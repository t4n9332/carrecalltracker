Mercedes-Benz C-Class driver seat belt anchor may loosen

NHTSA campaign 25V253000 · Reported 2025-05-02 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Mercedes-Benz C 300

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Mercedes C 300 and AMG C 43 vehicles, a cover on the driver's side seat belt may not have been installed correctly on the belt tensioner. If that cover is wrong, the seat belt anchor — the point where the belt is fastened to the car — can work loose. A loose anchor means the belt may not hold you properly in a crash. Dealers will inspect the seat belt covers and repair them if needed, free of charge.

How serious is it?

A seat belt that doesn't restrain you properly raises your risk of injury in a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but have it inspected soon.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered. 2. Contact your Mercedes-Benz dealer or MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372 (recall 2025040012). 3. Have the driver's seat belt cover inspected and repaired — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2022-2025 C 300, and 2023-2024 AMG C 43 vehicles. The driver-side seat belt cover may not be properly installed on the seat belt tensioner, which can cause the seat belt anchor to come loose.
Safety risk
A loose seat belt may not properly restrain an occupant during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and repair the seat belt covers, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 16, 2025. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2025040012.
