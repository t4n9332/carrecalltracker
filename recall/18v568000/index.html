Ford F-150 Seat Belt Pretensioner May Cause Fire

NHTSA campaign 18V568000 · Reported Aug 31, 2018 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

If the pretensioner deploys and ignites nearby materials, it could start a vehicle fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On these trucks, if the front seat belt pretensioner deploys during a crash, it produces sparks that could ignite nearby materials such as carpeting or insulation near the B-pillar. If those materials catch fire, a vehicle fire could result.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V568000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:PRETENSIONER
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2015-2018 Ford F-150 Regular Cab and SuperCrew Cab vehicles. If a front seat belt pretensioner deploys as the result of a crash, the sparks may ignite materials such as carpeting or insulation within the B-pillar area.
Safety risk
A vehicle fire could result if materials ignite inside the vehicle.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will remove the B-pillar insulation material and wiring harness tape, and install heat resistant tape, free of charge. The recall began September 28, 2018. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 18S27.
