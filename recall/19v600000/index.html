BMW M340i rear center headrest may fold in crash

NHTSA campaign 19V600000 · Reported 2019-08-14 · BMW of North America, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 BMW M340I

What's wrong, in plain English

Only two of these cars are affected by this recall. The headrest in the middle of the back seat was not welded together properly when it was built. Because of that bad weld, it can fold forward if the car is hit from behind. A headrest is meant to stay upright and support a passenger's head and neck in a rear-end crash, so someone riding in the middle of the back seat could be hurt worse. Dealers will replace it.

How serious is it?

Moderate and very limited in scope: the notice says a folding headrest increases the risk of injury in a rear-end crash, and there is no instruction to stop driving, so the car is safe to drive.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — only two cars are involved in recall 19V600000, so this is the only way to know if yours is one of them.
2. If it is, call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 or your BMW dealer to have the rear center headrest replaced free of charge.
3. Until it is replaced, avoid seating a passenger in the middle of the rear seat.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling one 2020 M340i and one M340i xDrive vehicle. Due to improper welding, the rear center headrest may fold in the event of a rear impact crash.
Safety risk
If the headrest folds down in a crash, there would be an increased risk of injury.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the rear center headrest, free of charge. The recall began August 28, 2019. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
