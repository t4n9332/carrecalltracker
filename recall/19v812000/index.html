Ram 1500 can lose power steering assist suddenly

NHTSA campaign 19V812000 · Reported 2019-11-14 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 RAM 1500

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2019 Ram 1500 trucks left the factory with a contaminated power steering gear. The contamination can cause an electrical short, and when that happens the power assist that makes the wheel easy to turn can cut out for a moment and then come back. The steering still works, but it can suddenly feel very heavy, especially when parking or turning at low speed. The dealer replaces the steering gear.

How serious is it?

Serious — steering that goes heavy without warning can cause a crash, and while the notice does not tell owners to park the truck, this should be fixed promptly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is affected.
2. Call a Ram dealer, or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and ask about recall VB8.
3. Book the free replacement of the electric power steering gear assembly.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:ELECTRIC POWER ASSIST SYSTEM
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Ram 1500 trucks. These vehicles may have been built with a contaminated electric power steering (EPS) gear that may short circuit, potentially resulting in an intermittent loss of power steering assist.
Safety risk
An intermittent loss of power steering assist may cause an inconsistent steering effort, especially during lower speed maneuvers, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the EPS gear assembly, free of charge. The recall began December 12, 2019. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's number for this recall is VB8.
