Toyota RAV4, Tundra Hybrid Weight Label Error

NHTSA campaign 26V571000 · Reported Sep 2, 2026 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

A vehicle loaded beyond its actual weight capacity because of an incorrect label has an increased risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The label on your vehicle that shows the load carrying capacity modification may display an inaccurate added weight value. This means the number telling you how much extra weight your vehicle can carry may not be correct. Dealers will replace the load carrying capacity modification label with a corrected one, free of charge, once new labels are ready to be mailed to owners.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 or your dealer.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V571000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2024 Toyota Tacoma Hybrid, 2025 Tundra Hybrid, Tundra, 2026 RAV4 Hybrid, 4Runner i-Force Max Hybrid, and 4Runner vehicles. The load carrying capacity modification label may display inaccurate added weight values. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
A vehicle with an incorrect maximum capacity weight value may be overloaded, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the load carrying capacity modification label, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed October 18, 2026. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 26TA18.
