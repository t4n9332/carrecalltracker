Ford Escape, Lincoln Corsair Hybrid Battery May Fail

NHTSA campaign 22V149000 · Reported 2022-03-11 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

A sudden loss of drive power while driving increases the risk of a crash, as stated in the notice. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Corsair and Escape vehicles equipped with the 2.5L hybrid powertrain because the high voltage battery may fail. If the battery fails, the vehicle can experience a sudden loss of drive power while it is being driven, meaning the vehicle stops moving forward under its own power. Dealers will replace the high voltage battery, free of charge, so the vehicle's power system works as intended.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
Contact a Ford or Lincoln dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, to schedule the battery replacement.
The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022 Corsair and 2021-2022 Escape vehicles equipped with a 2.5L Hybrid powertrain. The high voltage battery may fail, resulting in a sudden loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A sudden loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the high voltage battery, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 28, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21S48.
