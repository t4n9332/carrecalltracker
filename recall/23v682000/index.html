Cadillac Lyriq Recalled for Missing Pedestrian Warning Sounds

NHTSA campaign 23V682000 · Reported 2023-10-12 · General Motors, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Without these warning sounds, pedestrians may be unaware of an approaching vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The body control module on some vehicles may have been updated with incorrectly calibrated software, causing it to fail to make the sounds meant to warn pedestrians that the vehicle is approaching.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 to schedule a repair.
3. The body control module will be reprogrammed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:BODY CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023 Cadillac LYRIQ vehicles. The body control module (BCM) may have been updated with incorrectly calibrated software and fail to make the pedestrian warning sounds.
Safety risk
Without external warning sounds, pedestrians may be unaware of an approaching vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the BCM, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 14, 2023. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N232422960.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
