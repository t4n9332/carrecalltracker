RAM Trucks Transmission Leak Can Cut Power

NHTSA campaign 24V413000 · Reported Jun 6, 2024 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A loss of drive power or the ability to hold the truck in park increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2023 Ram 1500, 2500, and 3500 trucks have a transmission control unit with a weld that may not be strong enough. This can allow transmission fluid to leak onto the electrical components inside the unit. If that happens, the truck can lose the ability to drive under power, or it may not properly hold in the park position.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact an FCA US dealer, or call customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The valve body and control unit replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V413000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2023 Ram 1500, 2500, and 3500 vehicles. An insufficient weld in the transmission control unit can allow transmission fluid to leak on to electrical components, resulting in a loss of drive power or loss of park function.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power or a loss of park function increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the valve body and control unit, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expecting to be mailed July 26, 2024. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 63B.
