Jeep Wagoneer S built with wrong dashboard support beam

NHTSA campaign 25V575000 · Reported 2025-09-04 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Jeep Wagoneer S

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2025 Jeep Wagoneer S vehicles were built with the wrong support beam running across the inside of the dashboard. That beam is part of the structure that helps hold things in place and protect people up front in a crash. Because the wrong part was used, the vehicle may not protect the driver and front passenger as well as it should in a collision. Dealers will replace the beam with the correct one at no charge.

How serious is it?

It is a real crash-protection shortfall rather than something you would notice day to day, and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so the vehicle can be driven while you schedule the free repair.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is part of recall 25V575000 (Chrysler's number 71C). 2. Contact your Jeep dealer or Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403 to book the work. 3. Have the cross beam replaced — the repair costs you nothing.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2025 Jeep Wagoneer S vehicles. The vehicles are equipped with an incorrect cross car beam in the instrument panel. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An incorrect cross beam may not adequately protect occupants in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the cross beam, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 16, 2025. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 71C. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning September 11, 2025.
