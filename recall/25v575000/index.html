What's wrong, in plain English

These Jeep Wagoneer S vehicles have the wrong support beam installed behind the dashboard. This beam is supposed to help protect people inside the vehicle if it's ever in a crash, but because the wrong one was used, it doesn't meet federal crash-protection standards. That means it may not do its job properly to protect occupants if a collision happens.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Chrysler/Jeep dealer or call Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V575000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2025 Jeep Wagoneer S vehicles. The vehicles are equipped with an incorrect cross car beam in the instrument panel. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An incorrect cross beam may not adequately protect occupants in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the cross beam, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 16, 2025. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 71C. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning September 11, 2025.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
