Chevrolet Cruze May Leak Fuel After Crash

NHTSA campaign 18V304000 · Reported May 10, 2018 · General Motors LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

A gasoline leak in the presence of an ignition source raises the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Chevrolet Cruze LS vehicles have a gasoline engine and come equipped with a tire inflator kit instead of a spare tire. These vehicles may develop a gasoline leak if they are involved in a rear-impact crash or a rollover. A gasoline leak that occurs in the presence of a source of ignition increases the risk of a fire starting.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer, or call GM customer service at 1-800-222-1020.
3. The lock-ring installation is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V304000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Chevrolet Cruze LS vehicles, equipped with a gasoline engine and a tire inflator kit instead of a spare tire. The vehicles may leak gasoline following a rear-impact crash and a rollover. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 301, "Fuel System Integrity."
Safety risk
A fuel leak, in the presence of an ignition source, increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will install a lock-ring on the fuel tank that will shield the fuel tank vapor pressure sensor from damage in a rear-impact crash, free of charge. The recall began June 7, 2018. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is 18159.
