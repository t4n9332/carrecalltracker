Chevrolet Cruze LS may leak gas after rear crash

NHTSA campaign 18V304000 · Reported 2018-05-10 · General Motors LLC · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Chevrolet Cruze

What's wrong, in plain English

On these cars, which came with a tire inflator kit instead of a spare tire, the fuel tank can leak gasoline after a hard hit from behind followed by a rollover. A pressure sensor on the fuel tank can be damaged in that kind of crash and let fuel escape. Spilled gasoline near anything hot or sparking can catch fire. Dealers will install a lock-ring that shields the sensor from damage in a rear-end crash, free of charge.

How serious is it?

The danger here is fire after a crash, which is about as serious as it gets, though the leak only happens in a rear-impact and rollover and GM does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 18159 covers your Cruze. 2. Book the repair with a Chevrolet dealer — they install a protective lock-ring on the fuel tank. 3. Call Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020 for help; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Chevrolet Cruze LS vehicles, equipped with a gasoline engine and a tire inflator kit instead of a spare tire. The vehicles may leak gasoline following a rear-impact crash and a rollover. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 301, "Fuel System Integrity."
Safety risk
A fuel leak, in the presence of an ignition source, increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will install a lock-ring on the fuel tank that will shield the fuel tank vapor pressure sensor from damage in a rear-impact crash, free of charge. The recall began June 7, 2018. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is 18159.
