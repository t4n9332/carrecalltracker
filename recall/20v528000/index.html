BMW X5, X6 and X7 headlights may glow blue

NHTSA campaign 20V528000 · Reported 2020-09-01 · BMW of North America, LLC

low How serious is it? About this label

Drivers coming the other way may be confused by the blue light of an approaching vehicle, which could increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling certain X5, X6 and X7 models, including several performance and plug-in versions, because the headlights may give off a blue light. Federal rules set which colors a vehicle's lamps are allowed to show, and headlights that let blue light through do not meet that standard. BMW is having dealers look at the headlamps on the vehicles covered and fit new ones where an inspection shows they are needed.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a BMW dealer to have the headlamps inspected and replaced if needed, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. The inspection and any replacement are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020-2021 X5 sDrive40i, xDrive40i, xDrive50i, X5 M50i, X5M, X6 sDrive40i, xDrive40i, X6 M50i, X6M, X7 M50i and 2021 X5 xDrive45e vehicles. The headlamps may allow a blue light to illuminate. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Oncoming drivers may be confused by the blue light of approaching vehicles, which could potentially increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the headlamps and, if necessary, replace them, free of charge. The recall began October 30, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
