BMW X5, X6, X7 headlights may shine blue light

NHTSA campaign 20V528000 · Reported 2020-09-01 · BMW of North America, LLC · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 BMW X5
  • 2020 BMW X6
  • 2020 BMW X7
  • 2021 BMW X5
  • 2021 BMW X6
  • 2021 BMW X7

What's wrong, in plain English

The headlights on these SUVs can give off a blue tint instead of the white light required by federal rules. Blue-tinted headlights can confuse drivers coming the other way, who may misjudge what they are seeing on the road at night. The issue is the color of the light, not whether the headlights turn on. A dealer will inspect the headlights and replace them if necessary, free of charge. BMW started this recall on October 30, 2020.

How serious is it?

A relatively minor problem that could confuse oncoming drivers at night; the vehicle is safe to keep driving while you wait for the inspection.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your SUV is affected.
2. Make an appointment with your BMW dealer for a headlight inspection, or call BMW at 1-800-525-7417.
3. If the headlights need replacing, that is done at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020-2021 X5 sDrive40i, xDrive40i, xDrive50i, X5 M50i, X5M, X6 sDrive40i, xDrive40i, X6 M50i, X6M, X7 M50i and 2021 X5 xDrive45e vehicles. The headlamps may allow a blue light to illuminate. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Oncoming drivers may be confused by the blue light of approaching vehicles, which could potentially increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the headlamps and, if necessary, replace them, free of charge. The recall began October 30, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
