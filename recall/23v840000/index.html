Porsche Taycan battery may short circuit and catch fire

NHTSA campaign 23V840000 · Reported 2023-12-13 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

This is serious because a battery short circuit can lead to a fire, and Porsche has not told owners to stop driving or to park outside, so get the inspection done as soon as your dealer can take the car.

What's wrong, in plain English

Porsche is recalling certain 2021-2023 Taycan models, including the 4S, Turbo, Turbo S, GTS and Cross Turismo versions. The large battery pack that powers these electric cars can develop a short circuit inside one of its modules, an electrical fault where current takes the wrong path. That can create enough heat to start a fire. Your dealer will check the battery and replace any bad modules at no cost to you.

What you should do

1. Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included in recall APB5.
2. Call your Porsche dealer, or Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243, and schedule the battery inspection.
3. Have the battery inspected and any faulty modules replaced; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Porsche Taycan, 2021-2022 Taycan 4S, Taycan Turbo S, Taycan Turbo, Taycan 4 Cross Turismo, Taycan 4S Cross Turismo, Taycan Turbo Cross Turismo, 2022 Taycan GTS, and Taycan GTS Sport Turismo vehicles. The high-voltage battery may experience a short circuit within the battery modules.
Safety risk
A short circuit in the battery increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and repair the high-voltage battery by replacing modules as necessary, free of charge Owner notification letters were mailed on June 14, 2024. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is APB5.
