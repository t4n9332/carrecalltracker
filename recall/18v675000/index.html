Jeep Wrangler Front Track Bar Bracket Weld Defect

NHTSA campaign 18V675000 · Reported 2018-09-28 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

An unexpected reduction in steering ability increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Wrangler vehicles because the front track bar brackets may have been improperly welded. A poorly welded bracket can separate from the vehicle's frame while the vehicle is being driven. If this happens, the driver may experience an unexpected reduction in the vehicle's steering ability, making the vehicle harder to steer and control on the road.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Jeep dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall number UA5).
3. The bracket weld inspection and repair are free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2018 Jeep Wrangler
  • 2019 Jeep Wrangler

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:FRAME AND MEMBERS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Jeep Wrangler vehicles. The front track bar brackets may be improperly welded, which can cause the bracket to separate from the frame, reducing steering capability.
Safety risk
An unexpected reduction in steering ability, can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and repair the track bar bracket welds, as necessary, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin November 17, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is UA5.
