Nissan Kicks sticker lists wrong weight and wheel info

NHTSA campaign 25V188000 · Reported 2025-04-18 · Nissan North America, Inc. · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Nissan Kicks

What's wrong, in plain English

The certification sticker on the car shows the wrong maximum weight the vehicle and its axles are allowed to carry. Going by those numbers, you could load the car more heavily than it is built to handle. On some SR models with the optional 19-inch wheel package, the same label also lists the wrong wheel size, which can lead to putting the wrong air pressure in the tires. A dealer puts on corrected labels.

How serious is it?

An overloaded car or badly inflated tires raise the chance of a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Kicks is covered. 2. Watch for the replacement labels Nissan is mailing, or contact your dealer to have new ones installed — questions go to Nissan at 800-647-7261. 3. Get the new labels applied at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2025 Kicks vehicles. The certification label may have been printed with the incorrect Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR) and Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR) values, which can result in the vehicle being overloaded. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of 49 CFR Part 567, "Certification." Additionally, certain 2025 Nissan Kicks SR trim level vehicles equipped with an optional 19-inch wheel package may have a certification label that states an incorrect wheel size. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
An overloaded vehicle can increase the risk of a crash. Vehicles with incorrect wheel and tire information may lead to tires that are inflated improperly, which can also increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install new labels, free of charge. Additionally, dealers will mail replacement labels to owners. Owner notification letters were mailed May 27, 2025. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 800-647-7261. Nissan's numbers for this recall are PD136 and PMA47.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
