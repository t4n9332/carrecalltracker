Cadillac XTS Front Seat Belt Buckle Rivet Missing

NHTSA campaign 18V899000 · Reported 2018-12-19 · General Motors LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

If the buckle separates in a crash, the seat belt won't properly restrain the occupant, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Cadillac XTS vehicles because the front seat belt buckles may be missing a rivet that secures the buckle head to the cable mounting strap. Without that rivet, the buckle could separate from the strap in a crash, so the seat belt would not properly hold the occupant in place. Dealers will inspect the front seat buckle assemblies and replace them if the rivet is missing.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Cadillac dealer, or call Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006, to schedule an inspection.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019 Cadillac XTS vehicles. The front seat belt buckles may be missing a rivet that secures the buckle head to the cable mounting strap.
Safety risk
If the securing rivet is missing, the buckle may separate from the cable mounting strap in the event of a crash, preventing the occupant from being properly restrained, thereby increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the front seat buckle assemblies, replacing them if the rivets are missing, free of charge. The recall began February 7, 2019. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N182204040.
