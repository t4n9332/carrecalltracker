Dodge Hornet may lack warning sound when backing up

NHTSA campaign 23V696000 · Reported 2023-10-17 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Dodge Hornet
  • 2024 Dodge Hornet

What's wrong, in plain English

Hybrid and electric vehicles run so quietly that federal rules require a small speaker to make a sound when the vehicle is in reverse, warning people nearby. On these vehicles that speaker may be missing altogether, or its plug may have come loose, so nothing sounds when you back up. Someone walking behind you may not realize the vehicle is moving and could be struck. A dealer will check the connection and secure or replace the plug or speaker.

How serious is it?

The danger here is to people around your vehicle rather than to you, and the notice puts no limit on driving — but back up with extra care until it is fixed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 23V696000 (Chrysler's number B5A). 2. Schedule the inspection with your dealer, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403. 3. The inspection and any new connector or siren are free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:WARNINGS:EXTERNAL/PEDESTRIAN ALERT
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Dodge Hornet and Alfa Romeo Tonale Plug-In Hybrid vehicles. The pedestrian alert siren may be missing or disconnected and fail to alert pedestrians when the vehicle is in reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 141, "Minimum Sound Requirements for Hybrid and Electric Vehicles."
Safety risk
Failure to alert pedestrians when backing up increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the rear pedestrian siren connection and, if necessary, secure and/or replace the connector or siren, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 14, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is B5A.
