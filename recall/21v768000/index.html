Honda Odyssey Tires May Have Pinholes That Leak Air

NHTSA campaign 21V768000 · Reported 2021-09-29 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Honda Odyssey

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2022 Honda Odyssey vans left the factory with Bridgestone tires that may have a tiny pinhole in the sidewall. The hole is too small to see, but it lets air seep out slowly, so a tire can go soft over days or weeks without any obvious warning. An underinflated tire does not grip or handle the way it should, which raises the chance of a crash. A dealer will check all four tires and replace any with a hole.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious: the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but a slowly leaking tire increases crash risk, so keep an eye on your tire pressure and get the inspection done soon.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is included.
2. Call your Honda dealer, or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (recall number HBL), and book the tire inspection.
3. Have all four tires checked; any tire with a pinhole is replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
TIRES:SIDEWALL
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2022 Odyssey vehicles equipped with Bridgestone tires. The affected vehicles may have a tire that contains a pinhole, causing a slow air leak. As such, these tires fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 139, "New Pneumatic Radial Tires for Light Vehicles."
Safety risk
A slow air leak can affect tire performance, and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect all four tires and replace the tire if a pinhole is found, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 24, 2021. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is HBL.
