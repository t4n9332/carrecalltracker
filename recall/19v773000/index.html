Ford Expedition and Navigator Rear Suspension Fasteners

NHTSA campaign 19V773000 · Reported 2019-10-28 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

A sudden change in vehicle handling increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Expedition and Lincoln Navigator SUVs because the fasteners that hold the rear suspension toe link to the vehicle's frame may not have been properly tightened. If a toe link comes loose or separates from the frame, it can cause a sudden change in how the vehicle handles. This can happen while the vehicle is being driven, making the vehicle's behavior harder to predict and control on the road.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Ford or Lincoln dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall number 19S36).
3. Dealers will inspect and tighten the fasteners, adjust rear alignment, and check the rear tires, replacing them if needed, free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2019 Ford Expedition
  • 2019 Lincoln Navigator

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2019 Expedition and Lincoln Navigator vehicles. The rear suspension toe link fasteners may not have been properly tightened to the frame, possibly allowing separation from the frame.
Safety risk
A disconnected toe link can cause a sudden change to vehicle handling, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford has notified owners, and dealers will inspect and tighten the rear toe link fasteners, check and adjust the rear toe alignment, and inspect the rear tires for abnormal tire wear, replacing them if necessary. Repairs will be performed free of charge. The recall began November 14, 2019. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 19S36.
