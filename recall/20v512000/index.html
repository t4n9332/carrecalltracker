Dodge Charger Pursuit backup camera blanks in stealth mode

NHTSA campaign 20V512000 · Reported 2020-08-27 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

This is a moderate risk that raises the chance of backing into someone or something; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so you can keep driving, but back up slowly and turn around and look until the software fix is done.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Dodge Charger Pursuit police cars have a setting called stealth mode, which turns off the lights and screens so the car is less noticeable at night. The problem is that when stealth mode is on, the backup camera picture does not appear on the screen when you shift into reverse. That means you back up with no camera view, and you cannot see a child, a person, or an object behind the car.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included in recall W62.
2. Call a Chrysler dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and book an appointment.
3. Have the dealer update the software so the camera always shows in reverse — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018-2020 Dodge Charger Pursuit vehicles equipped with a "stealth mode." When stealth mode is selected, the backup camera rearview image will not display when the vehicle is in reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rearview Mirrors."
Safety risk
The lack of a back-up camera display increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will update body control module software to disable stealth mode when the vehicle is shifted into "Reverse", free of charge. The recall began October 22, 2020. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is W62.
