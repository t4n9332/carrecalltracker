BMW X1 door pillars may be too weak

NHTSA campaign 24V829000 · Reported 2024-11-04 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 BMW X1

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2025 X1 models, the reinforcement plates inside the body posts between the front and rear doors may not be as strong as they should be. Those posts, called B-pillars, are a key part of the structure around the passenger area. If the plates are weak, that part of the body may not hold up in a crash the way it is designed to, leaving people inside less protected. Dealers will check both sides and replace the pillar if needed.

How serious is it?

Weaker body structure means less protection for passengers in a crash, which is serious, but there is no warning sign in normal driving and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving while they wait for the inspection.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your X1 is included in recall 24V829000. 2. Contact your BMW dealer or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 to schedule the B-pillar inspection. 3. If your vehicle is affected, have the pillar replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:ROOF AND PILLARS
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2025 X1 xDrive28i and X1 M35i vehicles. The left and right-side B-Pillar reinforcement plates may have been manufactured with insufficient structural strength.
Safety risk
A B-Pillar structure with reduced strength may not adequately protect passengers in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the left-side and right-side B-Pillar and, if affected, replace the B-Pillar, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 7, 2025. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
