Volkswagen Atlas Engine Cover May Cause Fire

NHTSA campaign 25V100000 · Reported 2025-02-17 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

A loose engine cover touching hot engine parts can melt and raise the chance of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Volkswagen Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles may have an engine cover that was not put back on correctly after a service visit. This can let the cover come loose and touch hot parts of the engine. Dealers will remove the engine cover to fix the problem.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Volkswagen customer service at 800-893-5298.
3. Removal of the engine cover is free.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles. The engine cover may have been improperly installed after vehicle service, allowing it to come loose and contact hot engine surfaces.
Safety risk
A loose engine cover that contacts hot surfaces in the engine compartment can melt, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will remove the engine cover, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 7, 2025. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 10X5.
