Ford Super Duty diesel fuel filter cap can leak

NHTSA campaign 22V013000 · Reported 2022-01-14 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford F-250 SD
  • 2021 Ford F-350 SD
  • 2021 Ford F-450 SD
  • 2021 Ford F-550 SD
  • 2021 Ford F-600 SD
  • 2021 Ford F-650
  • 2021 Ford F-750

What's wrong, in plain English

These trucks have a 6.7-liter diesel engine. The cap on the secondary fuel filter under the hood may have a tiny void at its top center. Over time that weak spot can turn into a pinhole, letting diesel fuel leak out of the top of the filter. Fuel leaking near anything hot or sparking can start a fire. Dealers will replace both the primary and secondary fuel filters at no charge.

How serious is it?

Very serious — a fuel leak near an ignition source can cause a fire or injury; Ford has not issued a park-it or park-outside warning, but treat any fuel smell as urgent and get this repaired immediately.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included. 2. Contact your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and reference recall 22S01. 3. Have both the primary and secondary fuel filters replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 Super Duty F-250, F-350, F-450, F-550, F-600, and Medium Duty F-650 and F-750 vehicles equipped with 6.7L diesel engines. A small void may be present in the top-center of the vehicle's underhood secondary fuel filter cap, which may progress to a pinhole over time, allowing fuel to leak from the top of the secondary fuel filter.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the primary and secondary fuel filters, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 28, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S01.
