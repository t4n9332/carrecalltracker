Ford Super Duty Fuel Filter Cap Can Leak

NHTSA campaign 22V013000 · Reported Jan 14, 2022 · Ford Motor Company

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak near a source of ignition can increase the risk of a fire or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Ford Super Duty and Medium Duty trucks with 6.7-liter diesel engines, a small void in the underhood secondary fuel filter cap may develop into a pinhole over time. This can allow fuel to leak from the top of the secondary fuel filter.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is included. 2. Contact a Ford dealer to have the primary and secondary fuel filters replaced. You can also call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. 3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V013000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 Super Duty F-250, F-350, F-450, F-550, F-600, and Medium Duty F-650 and F-750 vehicles equipped with 6.7L diesel engines. A small void may be present in the top-center of the vehicle's underhood secondary fuel filter cap, which may progress to a pinhole over time, allowing fuel to leak from the top of the secondary fuel filter.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the primary and secondary fuel filters, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 28, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S01.
