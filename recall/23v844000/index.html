Honda CR-V Hybrid battery cable can overheat in a crash

NHTSA campaign 23V844000 · Reported 2023-12-13 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Honda CR-V Hybrid
  • 2021 Honda CR-V Hybrid
  • 2022 Honda CR-V Hybrid

What's wrong, in plain English

The cable running from the 12-volt battery along the outside of the frame is missing a fuse. A fuse is the small part that cuts the power if something goes wrong. Without it, a crash that damages the cable can let it short out or get very hot, and that can start a fire. The notice describes this happening in a crash, not during ordinary driving. A dealer will replace the cable with a corrected one.

How serious is it?

Honda has not told owners to park the vehicle or keep it outside, but because the result can be a fire or injury during a crash, treat this repair as a priority.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your CR-V Hybrid is covered.
2. Call your Honda dealer or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (recall FGB) to set an appointment.
3. Have the dealer replace the 12-volt battery cable — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY:CABLES
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2020-2022 CR-V Hybrid vehicles. The 12-Volt battery cable routed outside of the body frame is missing a fuse on the power circuit, which can allow the battery cable to short circuit or overheat during a crash.
Safety risk
An overheated battery cable or short circuit can increase the risk of a fire or injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the battery cable, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 29, 2024. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is FGB.
