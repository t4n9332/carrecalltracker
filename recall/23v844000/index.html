Honda CR-V Hybrid Battery Cable Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 23V844000 · Reported 2023-12-13 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

If this cable shorts out or overheats during a crash, it can increase the risk of fire or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2020-2022 CR-V Hybrid vehicles, a 12-volt battery cable that runs outside the vehicle's frame is missing a fuse on its power circuit. Without that fuse, the cable can short-circuit or overheat if the vehicle is involved in a crash. Honda is recalling these vehicles to correct this. The missing fuse means the power circuit for this cable does not have the protection it should have, which becomes a problem specifically if the vehicle experiences a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Honda dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138, to schedule the repair.
3. The battery cable replacement is free of charge.

Affected vehicles (3)

  • 2020 Honda CR-V Hybrid
  • 2021 Honda CR-V Hybrid
  • 2022 Honda CR-V Hybrid

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY:CABLES
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2020-2022 CR-V Hybrid vehicles. The 12-Volt battery cable routed outside of the body frame is missing a fuse on the power circuit, which can allow the battery cable to short circuit or overheat during a crash.
Safety risk
An overheated battery cable or short circuit can increase the risk of a fire or injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the battery cable, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 29, 2024. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is FGB.
