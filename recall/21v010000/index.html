Braun-modified Honda Odyssey may leak fuel and burn

NHTSA campaign 21V010000 · Reported 2021-01-14 · Braun Corporation · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Honda Odyssey
  • 2019 Honda Odyssey
  • 2020 Honda Odyssey
  • 2021 Honda Odyssey

What's wrong, in plain English

Braun is recalling certain 2018-2021 Honda Odyssey vans that Braun modified. The replacement fuel tank Braun installed can overfill, pushing gasoline into the charcoal canister, a small container that is meant to catch fuel vapor rather than liquid fuel. If enough gasoline collects in it, fuel can leak out of the van. Leaking gasoline near anything hot or sparking can start a fire. Dealers will inspect the van and replace parts if needed, free.

How serious is it?

This is serious because a fuel leak can lead to a fire, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or to park the van outside.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is covered.
2. Call Braun customer service at 1-800-488-0359 or your dealer to arrange the inspection.
3. If a fuel leak is found, a new fuel tank and charcoal canister are installed at no charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE
Summary
Braun Corporation (Braun) is recalling certain 2018-2021 Braun-modified Honda Odyssey vehicles. The fuel tank assembly installed by Braun may be susceptible to the fuel tank overfilling into the charcoal canister. If the charcoal canister fills with a sufficient amount of fuel, fuel may leak out.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source may increase the risk of fire.
Remedy
Braun will notify owners, and dealers will inspect vehicles. If evidence of a fuel leak is present, a new fuel tank assembly and a new Honda charcoal canister will be installed, free of charge. The recall began February 22, 2021. Owners may contact Braun customer service at 1-800-488-0359.
