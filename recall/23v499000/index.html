Ford Transit van generator fuel hose can leak gasoline

NHTSA campaign 23V499000 · Reported 2023-07-19 · QUALITY VANS

critical How serious is it? About this label

This is a fire risk, so treat it seriously: the notice does not tell owners to park the van, but if you smell gasoline, stop using it and have it inspected right away.

What's wrong, in plain English

These vans were built with an Onan brand generator installed. The fuel hose that runs between the inline fuel filter and the pump module on that generator may not be properly secured, so gasoline can leak out. Leaking gas near anything hot or sparking can start a fire. The company will inspect the hose and clamps and replace them if needed, at no cost to you. Owner letters were mailed August 4, 2023.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is affected.
2. Call Quality Vans customer service at 1-480-464-7007 to arrange the inspection.
3. Have the fuel hose and clamps inspected and replaced as needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Quality Vans (Quality Vans) is recalling certain 2020-2023 Ford Transit vans equipped with Onan QG 2800i EVAP generators, part number 2.8HGLAA-8303A. The fuel hose between the inline fuel filter and the Injection Pump Module (IPM) may not be properly secured, which can cause a gas leak.
Safety risk
A gasoline leak in the presence of an ignition source may increase the risk of fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the fuel hose and clamps as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 4, 2023. Owners may contact Quality Vans customer service at 1-480-464-7007.
