Chrysler 300, Charger, Challenger parking brake may not hold

NHTSA campaign 24V112000 · Reported 2024-02-15 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Chrysler 300
  • 2023 Dodge Challenger
  • 2023 Dodge Charger

What's wrong, in plain English

The parking brake on these cars may not have been adjusted correctly when they were built. That means the brake may not hold the car the way it should, and the car can roll away after you park it. Because of this, the vehicles also do not meet the federal safety rule for light vehicle brakes. A dealer will adjust the parking brake properly, and the work is free. Owner letters went out in late February 2024.

How serious is it?

This is serious because a car that rolls away can crash or injure someone, and although the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, you should not rely on the parking brake until it is adjusted.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is covered by recall 24V112000 (Chrysler number 01B).
2. Make a dealer appointment to have the parking brake adjusted, free of charge.
3. Until then, park on level ground and leave the car in park; call Chrysler at 1-800-853-1403 with questions.

Official notice

Component
PARKING BRAKE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2023 Chrysler 300, Dodge Charger, and Dodge Challenger vehicles. The parking brake may be improperly adjusted, which can result in a vehicle roll away. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 135, "Light Vehicle Brake Systems."
Safety risk
A vehicle roll away increases the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will properly adjust the parking brake, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 29, 2024. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 01B.
