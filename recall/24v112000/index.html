Chrysler 300 and Dodge Parking Brake Roll-Away Risk

NHTSA campaign 24V112000 · Reported Feb 15, 2024 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A vehicle that rolls away on its own increases the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The parking brake on some Chrysler 300, Dodge Charger, and Dodge Challenger vehicles may not be adjusted correctly. If the parking brake isn't holding properly, the vehicle could roll away on its own. This roll-away risk increases the chance of a crash or injury.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a dealer (1-800-853-1403) to schedule the repair.
3. The parking brake adjustment will be done free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V112000. Official notice

Component
PARKING BRAKE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2023 Chrysler 300, Dodge Charger, and Dodge Challenger vehicles. The parking brake may be improperly adjusted, which can result in a vehicle roll away. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 135, "Light Vehicle Brake Systems."
Safety risk
A vehicle roll away increases the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will properly adjust the parking brake, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 29, 2024. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 01B.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
