Silverado and Sierra HD hoods may fly open while driving

NHTSA campaign 20V324000 · Reported 2020-06-04 · General Motors LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 2500
  • 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 3500
  • 2020 GMC Sierra 2500
  • 2020 GMC Sierra 3500

What's wrong, in plain English

These trucks received a replacement hood, either under the earlier recall 20V-142 or during other service such as collision repair. The wire loop on the hood that the latch grabs onto may not have been hardened properly, so it can crack and break. If it breaks, the hood can swing open while you are driving and block your view of the road. A dealer will replace the whole hood at no charge.

How serious is it?

Serious — a hood opening at highway speed can block your vision and cause a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get this done as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is covered.
2. Contact your Chevrolet dealer (1-800-222-1020) or GMC dealer (1-888-988-7267) and mention recall N202306490 to schedule the free hood replacement.
3. If you bought a replacement hood over the counter, ask the dealer for the replacement instructions.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:HOOD
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 2500 and 3500 and GMC Sierra 2500 and 3500 vehicles previously recalled under 20V-142. Some of the vehicles in 20V-324 were not part of Recall 20V-142 but received defect parts during another service (typically during collision repair). The hood-latch striker wires on replacement hoods installed for Safety Recall 20V-142 may not have been heat-treated properly, possibly causing them to fracture.
Safety risk
If a striker wire fractures, the hood may open unexpectedly while driving, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace the hood assembly, free of charge. For hoods sold over the counter, dealers will contact customers with instructions for hood replacement. The recall began July 7, 2020. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-888-988-7267. GM's number for this recall is N202306490.
