BMW X5 and X7 dashboard may break during airbag deployment

NHTSA campaign 24V138000 · Reported 2024-02-23 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 BMW X5
  • 2024 BMW X7

What's wrong, in plain English

The dashboard in your X5 or X7 may not have been built correctly. That matters because the front passenger airbag comes out through the dash. In a crash, the airbag may not inflate the way it is supposed to and may not hold the person in the seat properly. The dashboard itself can also break apart, sending pieces flying at the people inside. Your dealer will replace the entire dashboard at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

There is no warning sign day to day and the notice does not say to stop driving, but if you are in a crash before the repair, people in the front seats could be seriously hurt — book this quickly.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
Contact your BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, to schedule the work.
Have the dashboard replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:INTERIOR PANELS:DASHBOARD
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2024 X5 sDrive40i, X5 xDrive40i and X7 xDrive40i vehicles. The instrument panel may not have been manufactured properly, which can result in improper air bag deployment or the panel separating during air bag deployment.
Safety risk
Instrument panel separation during air bag deployment can result in pieces of the panel striking occupants. Improper air bag deployment may not restrain an occupant as intended during a crash. Either of these scenarios can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the instrument panel, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 16, 2024. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
