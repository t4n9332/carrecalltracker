Mercedes Sprinter campers have wrong stability control software

NHTSA campaign 21V360000 · Reported 2021-05-17 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderately serious — the anti-skid system may not react properly in some situations, raising crash risk, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans built with the camper package have the wrong software in the system that helps keep the van from skidding or tipping during a swerve. Camper vans sit taller and carry weight higher up, and the software was never updated to account for that. Because of this, the system may not step in the way it should in certain driving situations. A dealer will load the correct software at no cost to you.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Sprinter is covered. 2. Contact your dealer or DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267 (recall VS3HOLAPLI) to book the update. 3. Have the software updated — it is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRONIC STABILITY CONTROL (ESC):CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vehicles equipped with the camper upfit option. The Electronic Stability Control (ESP) software was not updated for vehicles with a higher center of gravity.
Safety risk
The ESP may not respond as intended during certain driving scenarios, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on July 2, 2021. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3HOLAPLI.
