What's wrong, in plain English

This recall covers certain aftermarket brake fluid reservoir covers sold for BMW R 1250 and BMW RnineT motorcycles. The covers may not clearly show the type of brake fluid required or explain how to fill the reservoir correctly. Without that information, an owner or mechanic could use the wrong fluid or fill the reservoir incorrectly during maintenance.

What you should do

1. Check whether your accessory brake fluid reservoir cover is part of this recall at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a BMW dealer to have the cover replaced; BMW customer service is at 1-800-525-7417.
3. The replacement cover is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20E013000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FLUID
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain Motorcycle Accessory Brake Fluid Reservoir Covers, part numbers 8405-752, 8405-753, 8405-754 and 9829547 designed to fit certain 2019-2020 BMW R 1250 motorcycles, and part numbers 8404-098, 8405-100, 8405-102, and 1542056 designed to fit certain 2018-2020 BMW Rnine T motorcycles. The brake fluid reservoir cap may not clearly state the required type of brake fluid and the process for filling the reservoir, possibly leading to an improper maintenance procedure. As such, these caps fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 122, "Motorcycle Brake Systems."
Safety risk
Improper brake system maintenance could affect braking performance and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the brake fluid reservoir cap, free of charge. The recall began December 14, 2020. Parts will be available in mid-September 2020 for the recall repairs. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
