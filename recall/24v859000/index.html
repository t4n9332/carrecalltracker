Honda and Acura Driver's Seat May Be Loose

NHTSA campaign 24V859000 · Reported 2024-11-14 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Honda Accord, Accord Hybrid, Civic Sedan, Civic Hatchback, Pilot and HR-V vehicles and Acura Integra models, the driver's seat cushion frame may not have been tightened properly when the car was built. That can leave the driver's seat not firmly secured to the floor. These vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for seating systems. Dealers will replace the driver's seat cushion frame at no cost to the owner.

How serious is it?

Serious: a driver's seat that is not properly secured may not hold the driver in place during a crash, which increases the chance of injury; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but the free repair should not be put off.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included in recall 24V859000.
2. Contact your Honda or Acura dealer to schedule replacement of the driver's seat cushion frame, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. Get the repair done — it is free — and keep a record showing it was completed.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Honda Accord, Accord Hybrid, Civic Sedan, Civic Hatchback, Pilot, and 2024 HR-V, Acura Integra and Acura Integra Type S vehicles. The driver's seat cushion frame may not have been tightened properly, which can result in an unsecured seat. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 207, "Seating Systems."
Safety risk
An unsecured driver's seat may not adequately restrain the driver during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver's seat cushion frame, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 6, 2025. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
