Nissan Rogue Fuel Pump May Fail and Stall Engine

NHTSA campaign 21V957000 · Reported 2021-12-09 · Nissan North America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Nissan Rogue

What's wrong, in plain English

The fuel pump sends gasoline from the tank to the engine. Inside the pumps on certain 2021 Rogues, parts can wear abnormally, which makes the pump overheat and fail. If that happens, the engine stalls and the vehicle loses power while you are driving — possibly in traffic or at highway speed. A stall like that increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will replace the entire fuel pump assembly, so the worn parts are gone for good.

How serious is it?

Serious — the engine can quit without warning while you are moving; the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but have the pump replaced promptly.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls under recall 21V957000 (Nissan's R21B8).
2. Call your Nissan dealer, or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669, to schedule the fuel pump replacement.
3. The new fuel pump assembly and the labor are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2021 Rogue vehicles. Abnormal wear inside the fuel pump may cause it to overheat and fail.
Safety risk
Fuel pump failure may cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 27, 2022. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is R21B8.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
