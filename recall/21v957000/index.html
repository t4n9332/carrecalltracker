Nissan Rogue Fuel Pump May Overheat and Fail

NHTSA campaign 21V957000 · Reported Dec 9, 2021 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

An engine that stalls while driving can lead to a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Nissan Rogue vehicles have a fuel pump that wears unevenly on the inside. This abnormal wear can cause the pump to overheat and eventually fail while the vehicle is being driven. A fuel pump failure can cause the engine to stall, and a stalled engine while driving increases the chance of a crash for you and for others on the road.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Nissan dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
3. The fuel pump replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V957000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2021 Rogue vehicles. Abnormal wear inside the fuel pump may cause it to overheat and fail.
Safety risk
Fuel pump failure may cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 27, 2022. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is R21B8.
