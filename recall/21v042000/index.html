Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 3500 campers may lose stability control

NHTSA campaign 21V042000 · Reported Feb 3, 2021 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

What's wrong, in plain English

Daimler Vans USA is recalling certain 2012 through 2018 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 3500 and Freightliner Sprinter 3500 vans that were built out as campers or recreational vehicles. On these vehicles, the electronic stability control unit, the system that helps keep the van steady, may shut off. Dealers will replace the rear wheel speed sensor with a sturdier one. The company sent owners an early notice about this recall before the repair was available.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm whether your van is included.
2. Contact a dealer, or Daimler Vans USA customer service at 1-877-762-8267 (recall number NC3DREZAHA), to schedule the sensor replacement.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V042000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRONIC STABILITY CONTROL (ESC):CONTROL MODULE
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2012-2018 Mercedes Benz Sprinter 3500 and Freightliner Sprinter 3500 vehicles upfitted into campers or recreational vehicles. The Electronic Stability Control (ESC) unit may become disabled on these vehicles.
Safety risk
If ESC becomes disabled, vehicle driving stability may be impacted under certain driving conditions, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and dealers will replace the rear-wheel speed sensor with a more robust sensor, free of charge. DVUSA issued owners an interim notification on March 10, 2021. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is NC3DREZAHA.
