What's wrong, in plain English

The motor that powers the front seat may overheat if the seat slide knob gets stuck, or if an earlier repair for this same problem was done incorrectly. This overheating can lead to a fire while the vehicle is parked or being driven. This recall replaces an earlier one for the same issue, so even vehicles that already had that repair done will need this new fix completed as well.

What you should do

1. Park outside and away from structures until the repair is complete.
2. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
3. Contact a Kia dealer, or call Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542.
4. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V430000. Official notice

Component
SEATS:FRONT ASSEMBLY:POWER ADJUST
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2020-2024 Telluride vehicles. The front power seat motor may overheat due to a stuck power seat slide knob or an improper recall 24V407 repair, which can result in a fire while parked or driving.
Safety risk
A fire increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. Dealers will install an electronic fuse assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 13, 2026. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC374. This recall replaces NHTSA recall number 24V407. Vehicles already repaired under the previous recall will need to have the new remedy completed. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning July 17, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
