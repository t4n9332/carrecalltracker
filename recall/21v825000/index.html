Audi S8 backup camera may not show image

NHTSA campaign 21V825000 · Reported 2021-10-20 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Audi S8

What's wrong, in plain English

A software glitch in certain Audi models, including the S8, can keep the backup camera picture from showing up on the screen when you first shift into reverse. Federal rules require that image to appear, so these vehicles do not meet the standard. Without the picture, you cannot see what is directly behind you, which makes backing into a person or object more likely. Dealers will reprogram the infotainment screen settings free of charge.

How serious is it?

This reduces your view when backing up and raises the chance of a crash, but the car is otherwise fine to drive — just look behind you and use your mirrors until the software is updated.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Audi is covered by recall 91CR.
2. Schedule the software update with your Audi dealer, or call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834.
3. Have the infotainment update installed — it is free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2021 Audi A8 and S8, 2022 Audi A5 Sportback, A4 Sedan, E-Tron GT, Q3, Q5, RS6 Avant, A3 Sedan, S5 Sportback, Q5 Sportback, Q7, Q8, RSQ8, SQ7, SQ8, and S3 Sedan vehicles. A software error may prevent the rear-view image from initially displaying when the vehicle begins to reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rear-view camera that does not display an image reduces the driver's rear view, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the infotainment unit parameters, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 9, 2021. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 91CR.
