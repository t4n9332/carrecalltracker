What's wrong, in plain English

Some Ford Fusion Energi hybrid vehicles may not fully protect people from coming into direct or indirect contact with the vehicle's high-voltage electrical components during normal driving. This means the parts of the vehicle that carry high voltage may not be shielded well enough, so there is a chance someone could accidentally touch or come near a high-voltage source while using the vehicle normally.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included. 2. Contact a dealer to schedule the repair, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. 3. Dealers will install a protective cover over the high-voltage fuses, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V099000. Official notice

Component
HYBRID PROPULSION SYSTEM
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2019 Fusion Energi Hybrid models. The affected vehicles may not adequately protect against direct and indirect contact of high voltage sources during normal operation. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 305, "Electric-Powered Vehicles: Electrolyte spillage and electrical shock protection."
Safety risk
Potential contact with high voltage sources increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will install a Bus Electrical Center (BEC) cover over the high voltage fuses, free of charge. The recall began April 2, 2019. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 19C05.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
