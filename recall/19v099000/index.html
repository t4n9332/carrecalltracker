Ford Fusion Energi high-voltage fuses lack proper shielding

NHTSA campaign 19V099000 · Reported 2019-02-15 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Ford Fusion Energi

What's wrong, in plain English

These Ford Fusion Energi plug-in hybrids do not have enough shielding around some high-voltage parts — specifically the fuses in the electrical center. Federal rules require that people cannot touch those live parts during normal use, and these cars fall short. Anyone reaching into that area could get a serious electric shock. The dealer will install a cover over the high-voltage fuses, free of charge, which brings the car back in line with the rules.

How serious is it?

There is a shock risk if someone reaches into the electrical area, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving the car.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Fusion Energi is included.
2. Call a Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and reference recall 19C05.
3. Have the cover installed over the high-voltage fuses at no cost.

Official notice

Component
HYBRID PROPULSION SYSTEM
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2019 Fusion Energi Hybrid models. The affected vehicles may not adequately protect against direct and indirect contact of high voltage sources during normal operation. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 305, "Electric-Powered Vehicles: Electrolyte spillage and electrical shock protection."
Safety risk
Potential contact with high voltage sources increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will install a Bus Electrical Center (BEC) cover over the high voltage fuses, free of charge. The recall began April 2, 2019. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 19C05.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
