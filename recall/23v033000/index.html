Porsche Panamera coolant pump can short out and catch fire

NHTSA campaign 23V033000 · Reported 2023-01-27 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · critical severity what's this?

What's wrong, in plain English

Moisture can get inside the pump that circulates coolant for the climate control system. Water and electricity do not mix: the moisture can cause an electrical short, and a short in that area can start a fire in the car. Porsche sent an early warning letter before the repair was ready. Dealers will replace the pump, and check the plug connection and replace it too if needed, free of charge.

How serious is it?

Very serious, because this is a fire risk; the notice does not tell owners to park the car or stop driving, but get the repair done as soon as your dealer can do it.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included (Porsche's recall number is APA1).
2. Call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243 or your dealer to schedule the pump replacement.
3. Have the coolant pump replaced. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2017-2021 Panamera, Panamera 4, Panamera GTS and Panamera Turbo vehicles. Please see the recall report for a complete list of specific model names and model years. Humidity may enter the external coolant pump for the climate control system and cause an electrical short circuit.
Safety risk
An electrical short circuit increases the risk of a vehicle fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the external coolant pump and inspect and replace the plug-in connection, if necessary, free of charge. Interim owner notification letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed March 6, 2023. Owners will receive a second notice once remedy becomes available, anticipated July 2023. Owners may contact Porsche's customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is APA1.
