BMW i4 and iX batteries may short circuit, causing fire

NHTSA campaign 22V541000 · Reported 2022-07-27 · BMW of North America, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

A short circuit in the high-voltage battery increases the risk of a fire. Owners are told not to drive or charge these vehicles, and to park them outside and away from buildings until the repair is finished.

⚠ NHTSA advises not driving this vehicle until it is repaired. Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling certain iX xDrive50, iX M60, i4 eDrive40 and i4 M50 electric vehicles. The high-voltage battery that powers the vehicle may be damaged inside, and that damage can cause an electrical short circuit in the battery. Dealers will replace the high-voltage battery at no cost to owners. BMW mailed an early letter telling owners about the safety risk, and will send another letter once replacement parts are available. Owners will also be contacted by phone.

What you should do

1. Do not drive or charge the vehicle, and park it outside and away from buildings until the repair is complete.
2. Check whether your vehicle is included by entering your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
3. Contact your dealer or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417; the battery replacement is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2022-2023 iX xDrive50, iX M60, 2022 i4 eDrive40, and i4 M50 vehicles. The high voltage battery may have internal damage, resulting in an electrical short-circuit.
Safety risk
A short-circuit in a high voltage battery increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to drive or charge their vehicles, and to park outside and away from structures until the remedy is complete. Dealers will replace the high voltage battery, free of charge. Interim owner notification letters, informing owners of the safety risk, were mailed September 23, 2022. A second letter will be mailed when remedy parts become available. Owner notification letters were mailed December 23, 2022. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417. Owners will also be contacted by phone.
