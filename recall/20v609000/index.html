Tesla Model Y trailer brake lights may not light up

NHTSA campaign 20V609000 · Reported 2020-09-30 · Tesla, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Tesla Model Y

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2020 Model Y vehicles built with the tow package had a software bug: when you are pulling a trailer and press the brake pedal, the trailer's brake lights may not come on. Drivers behind you would have no warning that you are slowing down. Tesla fixed this with a free software update sent to cars over the air starting September 23, 2020, so most affected vehicles already received the fix automatically.

How serious is it?

This only affects you when towing a trailer, and the car itself is safe to drive, but it is worth confirming your car actually received the update before you hitch anything up.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Model Y is on the list. 2. Make sure your car is connected to Wi-Fi and its software is up to date so the free update installs. 3. If you are unsure it applied, contact Tesla at 1-877-798-3752 (recall SB-20-00-002).

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2020 Model Y vehicles equipped with a global rear lamp and tow package configuration. A software error prevents the illumination of trailer brake lights. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Trailer brake lights that fail to illuminate during braking increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla will notify owners, and has released the firmware update over-the-air (OTA) to vehicles as of September 23, 2020. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-20-00-002. (over-the-air software update)
