Jeep Grand Cherokee Trackhawk fuel line can separate, leak

NHTSA campaign 18V048000 · Reported 2018-01-11 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee

What's wrong, in plain English

On these supercharged Grand Cherokee Trackhawk models, a fuel line can come apart. If it does, the engine may stall while you are driving, which can leave you stopped in traffic without warning. A separated fuel line can also spray gasoline in the engine bay, which can start an engine compartment fire and injure someone. Your dealer will look at the fuel line and replace it if needed, at no charge to you.

How serious is it?

Very serious — this involves both stalling and a possible engine fire, so stop driving the vehicle any more than necessary and get it inspected immediately.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Trackhawk is covered. 2. Call your Jeep dealer for the soonest appointment, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and mention recall U02. 3. The dealer will inspect and, if needed, replace the fuel line free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee Trackhawk vehicles equipped with a 6.2L Supercharged Engine. The fuel line may separate.
Safety risk
If the fuel line separates, the engine may stall, increasing the risk of a crash. In addition, a fuel line separation increases the risk of an engine compartment fire and injury.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and, as necessary, replace the fuel line, free of charge. The recall began February 23, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U02.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
