VW Golf, GTI, Jetta Ignition Switch Can Fail

NHTSA campaign 23V604000 · Reported Aug 30, 2023 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Ignition switch failure can cause the electrical system to shut down and the engine to stall while driving, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Volkswagen Golf, Golf Alltrack, Golf Sportwagen, Golf GTI, and Jetta vehicles with a conventional ignition switch, the switch itself may fail, and this is more likely in high-temperature conditions. If the switch fails, the vehicle's electrical system can shut down and the engine can stall while you are driving, cutting power in the middle of a drive.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 to schedule the repair.
3. The repair (replacing the ignition switch) is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V604000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:IGNITION:SWITCH
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019 Golf, Golf Alltrack, Golf Sportwagen, 2019-2020 Golf GTI, and Jetta vehicles equipped with a conventional ignition switch. The ignition switch may fail, especially in high temperature environments.
Safety risk
Ignition switch failure can cause the electrical system to shut down and the engine to stall while driving, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the ignition switch, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 1, 2024. Owners may contact Volkswagen's customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 28H7.
