Ford Explorer front brake caliper bolts may loosen

NHTSA campaign 25V371000 · Reported 2025-06-03 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Ford Explorer

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2025 Explorers, the bolts that hold the front brake caliper in place may not have been tightened properly. The caliper is the part that squeezes the brake pads to slow the wheel. If those bolts loosen or fall out, you can lose front braking on that wheel, and the wheel itself may be damaged. Ford dealers will check the bolts and tighten them, or replace the caliper and pads if bolts are missing.

How serious is it?

Losing front brake function is serious and raises the risk of a crash, so get this checked right away.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Explorer is covered. 2. Contact a Ford dealer to have the caliper bolts inspected, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall number 25S60). 3. The inspection and any parts needed are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:DISC:CALIPER
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2025 Explorer vehicles. The bolts securing the front brake caliper may not be tightened properly, which can result in a loss of front brake function and possible wheel damage.
Safety risk
Loss of brake function and wheel damage can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and tighten the bolts as necessary. If bolts are missing, the brake caliper and pads will be replaced. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 19, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S60.
