Ford Mustang brake pedal may be loose and fail

NHTSA campaign 23V800000 · Reported 2023-11-28 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity

⚠ NHTSA advises not driving this vehicle until it is repaired. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Ford Mustang

What's wrong, in plain English

The brake pedal assembly in these cars may have been built wrong, which can leave the brake pedal feeling loose. A loose brake pedal can mean the brakes stop working when you press it. Ford is telling owners not to drive these cars until they are repaired. A dealer will replace the brake pedal and its mounting bracket with correctly built parts, and you will not be charged anything for the work.

How serious is it?

Very serious — you could lose braking entirely, so do not drive the car until the pedal is replaced.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included. 2. Do not drive it; contact your Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 23S61) to arrange the repair. 3. Have the dealer replace the brake pedal and bracket assembly — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:PEDALS AND LINKAGES
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2024 Mustang vehicles. The brake pedal assembly may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can result in a loose brake pedal.
Safety risk
A loose brake pedal can result in a loss of braking function, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to drive their vehicle until the repair has been performed. Dealers will replace the brake pedal and bracket assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 21, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23S61.
