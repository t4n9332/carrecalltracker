Chrysler Pacifica wrong radio can blank backup camera

NHTSA campaign 25V471000 · Reported 2025-07-17 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Chrysler Pacifica
  • 2025 Chrysler Pacifica

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Pacifica minivans were built with the wrong radio, and that radio can keep the backup camera picture from appearing on the screen. Federal safety rules require a working rearview image, so these vans did not meet the standard. Without the camera view you cannot see everything behind you while reversing. Chrysler says dealers have already replaced the radio in every affected van at no charge, and owner letters were mailed in August 2025.

How serious is it?

It broke a federal safety rule because no backup image raises the chance of a crash while reversing, but the notice places no limits on driving and says all affected vehicles have already been fixed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your van was part of recall 25V471000 (Chrysler's number 57C) and whether the work is recorded as done. 2. If it still shows as open, contact your Chrysler dealer or customer service at 800-853-1403. 3. The radio replacement costs you nothing.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:APPLIANCE:TV/RADIO/SPEAKERS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Chrysler Pacifica vehicles. The vehicles were built with an incorrect radio, which can cause the rearview camera image not to display. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview image that does not display reduces the driver's view of what is behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers replaced the radio, free of charge. All affected vehicles have been repaired. Owner notification letters were mailed August 7, 2025. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 57C.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
