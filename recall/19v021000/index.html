Ram 2500 and 3500 Steering Drag Link Separation

NHTSA campaign 19V021000 · Reported Jan 25, 2019 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

critical How serious is it? About this label

If the drag link separates, you could lose steering control, which raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Ram 2500 and 3500 trucks have a steering part called the drag link, which is held in place by a jam nut. That nut may loosen, which can let one end of the drag link separate from the rest of the steering.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Ram dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. Dealers will check the jam nut and repair or replace the steering parts as needed, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V021000. Official notice

Component
STEERING:LINKAGES:DRAG:LINK:CONNECTION
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2013-2018 Ram 3500 and 2014-2018 Ram 3500 Chassis Cab and Ram 2500 vehicles. The outboard steering linkage jam nut may loosen, allowing one end of the drag link to separate.
Safety risk
A drag link separation can cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will check the steering linkage jam nut tightness, replacing the drag link assembly or welding the nut to the adjuster sleeve, free of charge. The recall began March 22, 2019. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V06.
