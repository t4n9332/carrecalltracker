Porsche 911 Carrera hazard flashers may not work

NHTSA campaign 19V878000 · Reported 2019-12-11 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Porsche 911 Carrera

What's wrong, in plain English

A software error in these 2020 911 Carrera models means the hazard warning lights may not come on when you press the button. Those are the four-way flashers you use to warn other drivers when you are stopped on the road, broken down, or slowing suddenly. If they do not come on, drivers behind you may not realize your car is a hazard. A dealer will reprogram the control unit so the flashers work normally again.

How serious is it?

Moderate — without working flashers you cannot warn other drivers in an emergency, which raises crash risk, but Porsche does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is affected.
2. Call your Porsche dealer or Porsche at 1-800-767-7243 (recall AKC1) to schedule the software update.
3. Get the reprogramming done — it is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HAZARD FLASHING WARNING LIGHTS
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2020 911 Carrera S Coupe, Cabrio, Coupe, 4 Coupe, 4S Coupe, 4 Cabrio, 4S Cabrio, and S Cabrio vehicles. Due to a software error, the hazard warning lights may not function when activated by an operator. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
If the hazard warning lights cannot be activated, there is an increased risk of a crash in a hazard situation.
Remedy
Porsche will notify owners, and dealers will reprogram the control unit, free of charge. The recall began February 7, 2020. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AKC1.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
