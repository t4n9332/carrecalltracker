Porsche 911 Carrera Hazard Lights May Fail

NHTSA campaign 19V878000 · Reported 2019-12-11 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the hazard lights can't be turned on during a hazard situation, it increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2020 Porsche 911 Carrera models, across several coupe and cabriolet versions, have a software error that can prevent the hazard warning lights from turning on when the driver tries to activate them. Instead of flashing to alert other drivers, the hazard lights may simply fail to come on in a situation where the driver needs them.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact your Porsche dealer, or Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243.
3. The software reprogramming repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HAZARD FLASHING WARNING LIGHTS
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2020 911 Carrera S Coupe, Cabrio, Coupe, 4 Coupe, 4S Coupe, 4 Cabrio, 4S Cabrio, and S Cabrio vehicles. Due to a software error, the hazard warning lights may not function when activated by an operator. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
If the hazard warning lights cannot be activated, there is an increased risk of a crash in a hazard situation.
Remedy
Porsche will notify owners, and dealers will reprogram the control unit, free of charge. The recall began February 7, 2020. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AKC1.
