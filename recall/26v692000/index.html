What's wrong, in plain English

The fasteners securing the front suspension strut assemblies in certain Hyundai Elantra vehicles may not have been tightened properly. This can cause the driver to lose steering control. Dealers will inspect the fasteners and replace them as necessary. They will also perform a wheel alignment as needed. The inspection, any necessary fastener replacement, and any necessary alignment are free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Hyundai dealer to arrange the repair. Hyundai customer service: 1-855-371-9460.
3. Have the front suspension fasteners inspected and replaced, with a wheel alignment as necessary, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V692000. Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:MACPHERSON STRUT
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2026 Tucson Hybrid and Elantra vehicles. The front strut assembly fasteners may not be tightened properly, which can result in a loss of vehicle steering control.
Safety risk
A loss of vehicle steering control increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the front strut assembly fasteners and perform a wheel alignment, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed December 19, 2026. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 309. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov on October 23, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
