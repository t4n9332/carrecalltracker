Nissan Sentra windshields may have vision-blocking air bubbles

NHTSA campaign 25V807000 · Reported 2025-11-21 · Nissan North America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Nissan Sentra

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2025 Sentra windshields left the factory with air bubbles trapped in the glass. You may see them as small blisters or distortions in your line of sight. Because of this, the glass does not meet the federal safety rule for car windows. Bubbles in the wrong spot can hide another car, a cyclist, or a person crossing the road. A Nissan dealer will check your windshield and replace it if needed, free.

How serious is it?

This is a moderate concern rather than an emergency — the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but anything that blocks your view raises the chance of a crash, so get it looked at soon.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls (Sentra VINs became searchable November 21, 2025).
2. Contact your Nissan dealer, or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669, and mention recall PMA58.
3. Have the windshield inspected and replaced if needed — there is no charge to you.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2025 Sentra vehicles. The windshield glass may have visible air bubbles, which can obscure the driver's visibility. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 205, "Glazing Materials."
Safety risk
Reduced visibility while driving can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the windshield as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 21, 2026. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PMA58. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning November 21, 2025.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
