Ford Econoline and Ram ProMaster backup camera image reversed

NHTSA campaign 19V428000 · Reported 2019-06-11 · Unicell Body Company Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 RAM Promaster
  • 2019 Ford Econoline

What's wrong, in plain English

These work vans and trucks have an add-on backup camera screen that can reset itself to factory settings. When that happens, the picture flips left to right, like a mirror image. Something that appears on the right side of the screen is really on your left. Backing up, you could steer the wrong way to avoid a person or object behind you. The company that installed the screens will notify owners and provide a free fix.

How serious is it?

The notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but a reversed image can make you turn the wrong way while reversing and cause a crash, so rely on your mirrors and a spotter until it is repaired.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected. 2. Call ASA Electronics at 1-800-384-4400 or Unicell at 1-716-853-8628 to arrange the remedy. 3. Have the monitor corrected; there is no charge to you.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT
Summary
Unicell Body Company Inc. (Unicell) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Chevrolet Express and GMC Savanna, 2018-2019 Hino 155, Ford Econoline and Transit and Isuzu NPR, and 2018 Ram ProMaster-based vehicles equipped with ASA Electronics Voyager monitors used to display the back-up camera image. The affected monitors may revert back to the factory default settings which may cause the camera image to be reversed.
Safety risk
The driver may inadvertently turn the wrong direction to avoid an object behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Unicell will work with ASA Electronics to notify owners and offer a remedy, free of charge. The recall began May 9, 2019. Owners may contact ASA Electronics customer service 1-800-384-4400 or Unicell at 1-716-853-8628.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
