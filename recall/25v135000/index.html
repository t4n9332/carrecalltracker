Mercedes-Benz Sprinter parking brake may release, van rolls away

NHTSA campaign 25V135000 · Reported 2025-02-28 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 1500

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2024 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans, the welds holding the bracket for the parking brake cable can break. If that happens, the parking brake can let go on its own after you have set it. A van parked on any kind of slope could then roll away by itself and hit a person, another vehicle, or property. Dealers will inspect the parking brake cable bracket and repair it if needed, at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Serious — the van still drives and stops normally, but it may not stay put when parked, so until it is repaired park on level ground, leave it in gear or in Park, and use wheel chocks.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Sprinter is included. 2. Contact your Sprinter dealer about recall VS3HALHAN, or call Daimler Vans USA at 877-762-8267. 3. Have the parking brake cable bracket inspected and repaired free of charge.

Official notice

Component
PARKING BRAKE
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2024 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vehicles. The parking brake cable bracket welds may fail and disengage the parking brake. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 105, "Hydraulic and Electric Brake Systems."
Safety risk
Parking brake failure can result in a vehicle rollaway, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and repair the parking brake cable bracket as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 30, 2025. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3HALHAN.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
