Ford Heavy-Duty Trucks: Front Wheel Extender Can Detach

NHTSA campaign 21V390000 · Reported 2021-05-25 · Ford Motor Company

critical How serious is it? About this label

This is serious: Ford says owners should not drive their trucks until the wheel extender nuts are checked, because a detached wheel increases the risk of a crash.

⚠ NHTSA advises not driving this vehicle until it is repaired. See all such recalls.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Super Duty trucks with dual rear wheels and front wheel hub extenders installed on the front wheels. On these trucks, the wheel hub extenders may not be properly attached to the front rotor hub assemblies. Because of this, a wheel along with its hub extender could become loose and separate from the truck while it is being driven, which could lead to a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is included.
2. Do not drive the truck; contact a Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 to arrange inspection.
3. The dealer will inspect and tighten the wheel hub extender nuts free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AXLE HUBS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 F-350, F-450, F-550, and F-600 vehicles equipped with dual rear wheels and front wheel hub extenders. The front wheel hub extenders may be improperly attached to the front rotor hub assemblies, which could result in separation of the wheels and wheel extenders from the vehicle.
Safety risk
A detached wheel and wheel extender can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are instructed not to drive their vehicles until the torque of the wheel extender nuts can be verified. Dealers will inspect and tighten the front wheel hub extender nuts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 18, 2021. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21S26.
