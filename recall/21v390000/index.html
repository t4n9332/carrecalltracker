Ford Super Duty trucks may lose a front wheel

NHTSA campaign 21V390000 · Reported 2021-05-25 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity

⚠ NHTSA advises not driving this vehicle until it is repaired. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford F-350 SD
  • 2021 Ford F-450 SD
  • 2021 Ford F-550 SD
  • 2021 Ford F-600 SD

What's wrong, in plain English

These trucks have dual rear wheels and extenders on the front wheel hubs. The extenders may not have been bolted on correctly, so a front wheel and its extender could come off while you are driving. Ford is telling owners not to drive these trucks until a dealer checks how tight the extender nuts are. The dealer will inspect and tighten them at no charge. Letters went out to owners in June 2021.

How serious is it?

Extremely serious: a wheel can separate from the truck and cause a crash, and Ford instructs owners not to drive until the nuts have been checked.

What you should do

1. Stop driving the truck and check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm it is affected. 2. Call your Ford dealer or Ford at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 21S26) and ask how to get the truck in without driving it. 3. Have the dealer inspect and tighten the front wheel hub extender nuts, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AXLE HUBS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 F-350, F-450, F-550, and F-600 vehicles equipped with dual rear wheels and front wheel hub extenders. The front wheel hub extenders may be improperly attached to the front rotor hub assemblies, which could result in separation of the wheels and wheel extenders from the vehicle.
Safety risk
A detached wheel and wheel extender can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are instructed not to drive their vehicles until the torque of the wheel extender nuts can be verified. Dealers will inspect and tighten the front wheel hub extender nuts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 18, 2021. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21S26.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
