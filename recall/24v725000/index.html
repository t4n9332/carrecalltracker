Ford F-53 and F-59 chassis gear indicator may fail

NHTSA campaign 24V725000 · Reported 2024-09-27 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Ford F-53

What's wrong, in plain English

On these 2024 Ford F-53 motorhome and F-59 commercial chassis, the display on the dashboard that shows which gear you are in may not light up correctly. Without it, you cannot easily tell whether the vehicle is in park, reverse, neutral, or drive. That falls short of a federal safety rule. A dealer fixes it by reloading the software in the instrument cluster, the unit behind the gauges.

How serious is it?

Not being able to confirm the gear you are in raises the chance of a crash, and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so you may keep driving while you arrange the free software update.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 24C32) or your Ford dealer to book the software update. 3. Have the dealer reprogram the instrument cluster — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2024 F-53 Recreational Stripped Chassis and 2024 F-59 Commercial Stripped Chassis vehicles. The instrument panel cluster may not properly display the transmission gear position indicator. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 102, "Transmission Shift Position Sequence, Starter Interlock, and Transmission Braking Effect."
Safety risk
A transmission gear position indicator that fails to illuminate can make it difficult to identify the correct transmission shift position, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the instrument panel cluster (IPC) module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on February 12, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24C32.
