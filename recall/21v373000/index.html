Nissan Armada and INFINITI QX80 fuel pumps can fail

NHTSA campaign 21V373000 · Reported 2021-05-20 · Nissan North America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Infiniti QX80
  • 2020 Nissan Armada
  • 2021 Infiniti QX80
  • 2021 Nissan Armada

What's wrong, in plain English

A small spinning part inside the fuel pump can swell up and jam, which makes the pump quit. When the pump stops, gasoline stops reaching the engine and the engine can shut off while you are driving. Losing the engine in traffic makes a crash more likely. This affects Armada and QX80 models with the V8 engine. Dealers will put in a whole new fuel pump unit at no charge to you.

How serious is it?

Serious, because the engine can stall while you are moving; the notice does not say to park the vehicle, so you may keep driving, but schedule the free repair promptly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is covered.
2. Nissan owners call 1-800-867-7669 (recall R21A2); INFINITI owners call 1-800-662-6200 (recall R21A3), or contact your dealer.
3. Have the fuel pump module replaced at the dealer — it is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP:CONTROL/DRIVE MODULE
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Nissan Armada and 2020-2021 INFINITI QX80 vehicles equipped with V8 engines. The impeller can swell and bind within the fuel pump module, causing it to fail.
Safety risk
Fuel pump failure can cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 4, 2022. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is R21A2. Owners may contact INFINITI customer service at 1-800-662-6200. INFINITI's number for this recall is R21A3.
