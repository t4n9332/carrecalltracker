Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator lose reverse camera and lights

NHTSA campaign 23V641000 · Reported Sep 18, 2023 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Without the camera image the driver has less view of what is behind the vehicle, and without reverse lights other people may not realize the vehicle is backing up, which raises the risk of injury or a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator vehicles built with a manual transmission. When the vehicle is shifted into reverse, the rearview camera may not show the picture behind the vehicle and the reverse lights may not come on. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standards covering rear visibility and vehicle lamps.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Jeep is included. 2. Contact a dealer to have the gear position sensor replaced, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall number 92A). 3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V641000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:MANUAL TRANSMISSION
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US), LLC is recalling certain 2022-2023 Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator vehicles equipped with a manual transmission. The rearview camera may fail to display the rearview image and the reverse lights may not illuminate when the vehicle is placed in reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 111, "Rear Visibility" and 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
A disabled rearview image can decrease the driver's visibility of the rear view, and disabled reverse lights may not alert others that the vehicle is reversing, increasing the risk of injury or crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the gear position sensor, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 16, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 92A.
