Mazda3 wheel lug nuts may loosen and wheel detach

NHTSA campaign 19V425000 · Reported 2019-06-07 · Mazda North American Operations

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing a wheel while driving can easily cause a crash, so this is serious; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get it checked right away.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2019 Mazda3 cars, the lug nuts that hold the wheels onto the vehicle may not be tight enough and can work themselves loose over time. This can happen on any of the four wheels. If the nuts loosen far enough, a wheel can come off the car while you are driving. You might notice a clicking, rattling, or vibration from a wheel before it happens, but you should not count on a warning.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is on the list. 2. Contact your Mazda dealer or Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500, Option 4, about recall 3519F. 3. Have the dealer tighten all four wheels' lug nuts to the correct specification, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
WHEELS:LUGS/NUTS/BOLTS/STUDS
Summary
Mazda North American Operations (Mazda) is recalling certain 2019 Mazda3 vehicles. The lug nuts on all four wheels may loosen, potentially resulting in a wheel detaching from the vehicle.
Safety risk
A wheel separating from the vehicle increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Mazda will notify owners, and dealers will tighten the front and rear wheel lug nuts to the correct specification, free of charge. The recall began July 9, 2019. Owners may contact Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500, Option 4. Mazda's number for this recall is 3519F.
