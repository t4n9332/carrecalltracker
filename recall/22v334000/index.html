Ford Mustang Front Camera Is Misaligned

NHTSA campaign 22V334000 · Reported May 13, 2022 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

A camera that isn't working properly can disable features like Pre-Collision Assist, Adaptive Cruise Control, Lane Keeping System, Driver Alert, and Auto High Beam Control, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2022 Ford Mustangs have a forward-facing camera, used for driver-assist features, that is misaligned with the vehicle. Because of this misalignment, the camera does not work the way it's supposed to.

What you should do

Check your vehicle's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm it's included.
Contact a Ford dealer to schedule the repair, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
Dealers will realign the camera, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V334000. Official notice

Component
FORWARD COLLISION AVOIDANCE: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022 Mustang vehicles equipped with an Image Processing Module A (IPMA) or forward-facing camera. The camera is misaligned to the vehicle, resulting in the camera not functioning as intended.
Safety risk
A front-facing camera that is not functioning properly may restrict or disable certain features including Pre-Collision Assist, Adaptive Cruise Control, Lane Keeping System, Driver Alert, and Auto High Beam Control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will realign the front-facing camera, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 27, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S34.
