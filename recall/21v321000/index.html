Volkswagen Atlas and Golf R sold without safety verification

NHTSA campaign 21V321000 · Reported 2021-05-05 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Volkswagen says a vehicle that does not meet all the rules carries a higher risk of injury, crash, or fire, and the company is taking the vehicles back entirely rather than repairing them; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

These were Volkswagen's own internal-use vehicles that were later sold to the public. Volkswagen never confirmed they were built to all the federal safety rules that apply, and they may have been changed or modified before they were sold. Because of that, Volkswagen cannot say these vehicles meet the standards every new vehicle has to meet. Rather than fixing anything, Volkswagen is buying the vehicles back from their owners.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 and ask about recall 01F1; notification letters went out May 20, 2021.
3. Arrange the buyback through Volkswagen or your dealer — there is no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
UNKNOWN OR OTHER
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2017 Touareg GP, Tiguan, Golf A6, E-Golf GP, CCF, 2016-2017 Passat GP, 2018-2019 Golf R GP, and 2018 Atlas vehicles. These internal-use vehicles were sold without confirmation that they were built to all applicable regulatory requirements and may have been modified prior to sale. As such, these vehicles may fail to comply with the requirements of various Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards.
Safety risk
Vehicles that do not meet all regulatory requirements may have an increased risk of injury, crash, or fire.
Remedy
Volkswagen will repurchase the vehicles. Owner notification letters were mailed May 20, 2021. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 01F1.
