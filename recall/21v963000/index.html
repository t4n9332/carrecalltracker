Toyota 4Runner driver side high beam may not work

NHTSA campaign 21V963000 · Reported 2021-12-13 · Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Toyota 4runner

What's wrong, in plain English

On these 4Runners, accessory fog lights were installed, and the wiring can leave the driver's side high beam unable to come on. Federal rules require both high beams to work. With only one working, you get less light on dark roads and it is harder to see far ahead or spot hazards early. A dealer will install an updated wiring harness so the high beam works again, at no charge to you.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the vehicle is safe to drive, but be extra careful on unlit roads at night until the wiring is fixed.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Test your high beams at night to see whether both come on. 3. Contact your Toyota dealer or Southeast Toyota customer service at 1-866-405-4226 (recall SET21A) for the free wiring repair.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC (SET) is recalling certain 2022 Toyota 4Runner vehicles. The driver's side high beam may be inoperable after the installation of accessory fog lights. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
An inoperable driver's side high beam may reduce visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install an updated wiring harness, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 11, 2022. Owners may contact SET customer service at 1-866-405-4226. SET's number for this recall is SET21A.
