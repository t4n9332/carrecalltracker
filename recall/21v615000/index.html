Volkswagen Taos engine software may cause engine stall

NHTSA campaign 21V615000 · Reported Aug 6, 2021 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

What's wrong, in plain English

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2022 Taos vehicles equipped with all-wheel drive. The software in the engine control unit, the computer that manages the engine, may cause the engine to stall as the vehicle comes to a stop. Dealers will update that software free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 5, 2021. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 24HH, and owners with questions can reach the company's customer service line.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included. 2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer to have the engine software updated, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. 3. The software update is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V615000.
