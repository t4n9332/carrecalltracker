Tesla brake warning lights display in too-small font

NHTSA campaign 24V051000 · Reported 2024-01-30 · Tesla, Inc.

low How serious is it? About this label

Minor — the brakes themselves are not affected and the car is safe to drive; the concern is only that you might miss a warning light because it is hard to read.

What's wrong, in plain English

The Brake, Park, and Antilock Braking System warning lights on the dashboard display are printed in a font that is too small. The lights still work, but the small size can make these important warnings hard to read while you are driving, and it does not meet federal safety rules. Tesla has been sending a free over-the-air software update to the car to correct the size, so the fix can install without a trip to the shop.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Tesla is included in recall 24V051000 (Tesla's number SB-24-00-003).
2. Connect your car to Wi-Fi and install any pending software update — the fix is delivered over the air at no cost.
3. If the update does not appear, contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2012-2023 Model S, 2016-2024 Model X, 2017-2023 Model 3, 2019-2024 Model Y, and 2024 Cybertruck vehicles. An incorrect font size is displayed on the instrument panel for the Brake, Park, and Antilock Brake System (ABS) warning lights. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 105, "Hydraulic and Electric Brake Systems" and 135, "Light Vehicle Brake Systems."
Safety risk
Warning lights with a smaller font size can make critical safety information on the instrument panel difficult to read, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla began releasing an over-the-air (OTA) software update, free of charge. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-24-00-003. (over-the-air software update)
