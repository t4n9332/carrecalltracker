Ram ProMaster Paratransit Shifter Cable Separation

NHTSA campaign 20V582000 · Reported Oct 15, 2020 · Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles

high How serious is it? About this label

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Ram ProMaster Paratransit vehicles with a 3.6L engine, the transmission shifter cable can separate and disconnect from the transmission. When this happens, the vehicle may not shift the way the driver intends, and the gear shown on the shift lever may not match the gear the transmission is actually in. Because of this mismatch, the driver may not be aware of what gear the vehicle is actually in.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Take your vehicle to a Ram dealer for the needed repair; you can also contact Prime-Time customer service at 1-574-293-9191 (recall W00).
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V582000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:LEVER AND LINKAGE:COLUMN SHIFT
Summary
Prime-Time Specialty Vehicles (Prime-Time) is recalling certain 2014-2019 Paratransit vehicles equipped with 3.6L engines. The transmission shifter cable may separate and disconnect from the transmission, causing the vehicle to not perform shifts intended by the driver and the gear shift lever position not matching the actual transmission gear.
Safety risk
The driver may be unaware of the actual gear position and unintended vehicle movement can occur, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Prime-Time will notify owners instructing them to take their vehicles to Ram dealers for the necessary repairs, free of charge. Owners may contact Prime-Time customer service at 1-574-293-9191. Prime-Time's number for this recall is W00.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
