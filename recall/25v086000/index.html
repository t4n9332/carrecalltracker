Toyota Sienna Third-Row Seat Bolts Loose

NHTSA campaign 25V086000 · Reported Feb 13, 2025 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Toyota Sienna Hybrid vehicles, the bolts holding the third-row seatback assemblies in place may not have been tightened properly. Because of this, these seats do not meet federal safety standards covering head restraints, seating systems, and child restraint anchors.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer, or call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331.
3. Tightening of the seat assembly bolts is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V086000. Official notice

Component
SEATS:MID/REAR ASSEMBLY:RECLINER
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2021-2025 Sienna Hybrid vehicles. The bolts for the seatbacks on the third-row seat assemblies may not be tightened properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) numbers 202, "Head Restraints," 207, "Seating Systems," and 225, "Child Restraint Anchorage Systems."
Safety risk
An improperly tightened seatback may not adequately restrain the occupant during a crash, increasing their risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will tighten the seat assembly bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 1, 2025. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 25TA05.
