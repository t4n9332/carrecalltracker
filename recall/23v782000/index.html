Honda HR-V Seat Belt Pretensioner Missing Rivet

NHTSA campaign 23V782000 · Reported Nov 21, 2023 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

A seat belt pretensioner that isn't properly assembled may not restrain you correctly in a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Honda HR-V vehicles, the front seat belt pretensioner may be missing a rivet that holds the connector and wire plate in place. Without that rivet, the pretensioner assembly is not held together the way it should be.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Honda dealer, or call 1-888-234-2138, to have the seat belts inspected.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V782000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Accord and HR-V vehicles. The front seat belt pretensioners may be missing the rivet that secures the quick connector and wire plate. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 208, "Occupant Crash Protection," 209, "Seat Belt Assembles," and 210, "Seat Belt Assembly Anchorages."
Safety risk
A seat belt pretensioner missing a rivet will not properly restrain the occupant, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the seat belt pretensioner assemblies as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 8, 2024. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's numbers for this recall are MG7 and NG5.
