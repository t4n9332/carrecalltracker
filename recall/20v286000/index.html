Toyota RAV4: Front Suspension Arm May Crack

NHTSA campaign 20V286000 · Reported May 20, 2020 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

If a suspension control arm breaks, it can result in a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Toyota is recalling certain 2019-2020 RAV4 and RAV4 Hybrid vehicles. The front lower suspension arms may develop cracks. If a crack progresses far enough, the suspension arm may separate from the front wheel assembly. This affects the parts that connect the front wheels to the rest of the vehicle's suspension.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371 to schedule an inspection.
3. Dealers will replace both front lower suspension arms, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V286000. Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:CONTROL ARM:LOWER ARM
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2019-2020 RAV4 and RAV4 Hybrid vehicles. The front lower suspension arms may have cracks, which may result in the suspension arm separating from the front wheel assembly.
Safety risk
If a suspension control arm breaks, it can result in a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners, and dealers will replace both front lower suspension arms, free of charge. This recall began August 10, 2020. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. Toyota's interim numbers for this recall are 20TB08 / 20TA08.
