Audi Models: Instrument Panel Display May Fail

NHTSA campaign 25V201000 · Reported Mar 31, 2025 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the instrument panel display fails, the driver may not be able to see important safety information, such as the speedometer or warning lights, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Due to a software error, the instrument panel display on affected vehicles may fail, meaning it can stop showing information to the driver.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact an Audi dealer, or call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V201000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2021 SQ8, A6 SEDAN, A7, A8, Q7, Q8, RS6 Avant, RS7, RSQ8, S6 SEDAN, S7, S8, A6 Allroad, and SQ7 vehicles. Due to a software error, the instrument panel display may fail.
Safety risk
An instrument panel display that fails to show critical safety information, such as the speedometer or warning lights, increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the instrument panel module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 24, 2025. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 90VC.
