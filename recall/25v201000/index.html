What's wrong, in plain English

A software error can make the main dashboard display go blank. That screen is where your speedometer, fuel gauge and warning lights live, so if it quits you will not know how fast you are going or whether the car is trying to warn you about something else. A dealer reloads the instrument panel software at no charge, and no parts are needed. Audi mailed owner letters on April 24, 2025, so the fix is available now.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious, because driving without a speedometer or warning lights raises the chance of a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, and the repair is a quick free software update.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Book a service appointment with your Audi dealer for recall 90VC; the software update is free.
3. If you have questions or never got a letter, call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2021 SQ8, A6 SEDAN, A7, A8, Q7, Q8, RS6 Avant, RS7, RSQ8, S6 SEDAN, S7, S8, A6 Allroad, and SQ7 vehicles. Due to a software error, the instrument panel display may fail.
Safety risk
An instrument panel display that fails to show critical safety information, such as the speedometer or warning lights, increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the instrument panel module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 24, 2025. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 90VC.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
