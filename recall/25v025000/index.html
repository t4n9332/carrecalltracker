Ford Bronco rear shocks can corrode and shed parts

NHTSA campaign 25V025000 · Reported 2025-01-17 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford Bronco
  • 2022 Ford Bronco
  • 2023 Ford Bronco
  • 2024 Ford Bronco

What's wrong, in plain English

The rear shock absorbers can rust and fail. Each one has a separate fluid canister attached to the outside, and that canister can break away and fall off the vehicle entirely. A heavy part left on the road is a hazard for you and for anyone driving behind you. Ford dealers will look at the rear shocks and replace them where needed, with no charge for parts or labor.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious — Ford has not issued a do-not-drive warning and the Bronco is safe to keep driving, but a part coming off at speed can cause a crash, so get it checked without delay.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Bronco is included.
2. Contact your Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 25S01) to schedule an inspection.
3. Replacement of the rear shock absorbers is free if they need it.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR:SHOCK ABSORBER
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2024 Bronco vehicles. The rear shock absorbers may corrode and fail, resulting in detachment of the external reservoir.
Safety risk
An external reservoir that detaches from a vehicle can create a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the rear shock absorbers, as necessary free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 15, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S01.
