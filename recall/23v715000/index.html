Porsche 911 Seat-Mounted Air Bags May Deploy Unnecessarily

NHTSA campaign 23V715000 · Reported 2023-10-25 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Air bags going off when they shouldn't can increase the risk of injury to the person in that seat. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Porsche is recalling certain 911 models that have the optional full bucket seats installed. These seats include air bags built into them. In some cases, those seat air bags may deploy unnecessarily during a crash, meaning they can go off even when they are not needed. This problem only affects vehicles equipped with the special bucket seat option, not vehicles with standard seats.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact your Porsche dealer, or call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2021-2023 911 Turbo S, 2022 911 Turbo, 2022-2023 911 GT3 Touring, 911 GT3, 911 Carrera 4 GTS, 2022-2024 911 Carrera GTS, 2023 911 GT3 RS, 911 Dakar, and 2024 911 Carrera T vehicles equipped with optional full bucket seats. The seat-mounted air bags may deploy unnecessarily during a crash.
Safety risk
Air bags that deploy unnecessarily can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the air bag control unit, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 11, 2023. Owners may contact Porsche's customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is APB3.
