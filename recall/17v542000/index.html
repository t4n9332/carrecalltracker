Chrysler Pacifica Second-Row Seat Belt May Unlatch

NHTSA campaign 17V542000 · Reported Aug 31, 2017 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A seat belt that unlatches on its own increases the risk of injury if a crash occurs. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler says some Pacifica minivans with 8-passenger seating can have an issue with the second-row center seat belt buckle. In certain seating and vehicle situations, it can cause the left outboard seat belt in that row to become unlatched.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
Contact a Chrysler dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to schedule the repair.
The repair, installing a shorter second-row seat belt buckle, is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 17V542000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Chrysler Pacifica vehicles equipped with the 8-passenger seating option. In certain passenger seating and vehicle situations, the second-row center seating position seat belt buckle could cause the left outboard seat belt to become unlatched.
Safety risk
Inadvertent unlatching of the left outboard seat belt can increase the risk of injury in the event of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will install a shorter second-row seat belt buckle, free of charge. The recall began October 26, 2017. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is T54.
