One Tesla Model 3 has an incomplete side airbag

NHTSA campaign 24V284000 · Reported 2024-04-19 · Tesla, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Tesla Model 3

What's wrong, in plain English

One 2024 Tesla Model 3 was built with a small part missing inside the side airbag in the driver's seat. That piece is meant to aim the gas that inflates the bag, so the airbag fills the right way at the right moment during a crash. Without it, the airbag can inflate improperly and may not protect the driver the way it should. Only a single vehicle is affected, and Tesla already repaired it in April 2024.

How serious is it?

This affects only one car and the repair is already done, so for nearly every owner there is nothing to worry about and no reason to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your car is the one vehicle in campaign 24V284000.
2. If it shows as open, contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752 and reference SB-24-13-001.
3. Tesla replaces the side airbag free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:THORAX
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling one 2024 Model 3 vehicle. The driver seat side air bag module was assembled without a deflector that directs gas flow during deployment.
Safety risk
Without a deflector, the air bag may deploy improperly, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Tesla service replaced the side air bag, free of charge. The affected vehicle was repaired April 12, 2024. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-24-13-001.
