Ford Mustang Passenger Knee Air Bag Weld Issue

NHTSA campaign 22V083000 · Reported 2022-02-16 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

An air bag that doesn't deploy the right way can increase the risk of injury during a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

A weld on the front passenger knee air bag in these vehicles may not be strong enough. Because of this weak weld, the knee air bag may not deploy correctly if it is needed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Ford dealer to schedule replacement of the front passenger knee air bag; Ford customer service is 1-866-436-7332.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2021 Ford Mustang
  • 2022 Ford Mustang

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:KNEE BOLSTER
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Mustang vehicles. An insufficient weld on the front passenger knee air bag may result in an improper air bag deployment.
Safety risk
An improper air bag deployment can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front passenger knee air bag, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on March 8, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S07.
