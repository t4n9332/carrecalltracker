Ford Mustang passenger knee airbag may deploy improperly

NHTSA campaign 22V083000 · Reported 2022-02-16 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford Mustang
  • 2022 Ford Mustang

What's wrong, in plain English

One of the welds on the knee airbag for the front passenger was not done well enough. That airbag sits low in the dash and is meant to cushion the passenger's legs in a crash. Because of the weak weld, it may not open the way it should. If it opens the wrong way during a crash, it can cause injury instead of preventing it. Dealers will replace the front passenger knee airbag at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Serious if you are in a crash with someone in the front passenger seat, but the notice places no restriction on driving the car in the meantime.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Mustang is covered. 2. Call a Ford dealer at 1-866-436-7332 and mention recall 22S07. 3. Have the front passenger knee airbag replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:KNEE BOLSTER
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Mustang vehicles. An insufficient weld on the front passenger knee air bag may result in an improper air bag deployment.
Safety risk
An improper air bag deployment can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front passenger knee air bag, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on March 8, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S07.
