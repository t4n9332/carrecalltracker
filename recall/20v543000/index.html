Hyundai Tucson brake control unit can corrode and cause fire

NHTSA campaign 20V543000 · Reported 2020-09-04 · Hyundai Motor America · critical severity

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Hyundai Tucson
  • 2019 Hyundai Tucson
  • 2020 Hyundai Tucson
  • 2021 Hyundai Tucson

What's wrong, in plain English

The control box for the anti-lock brakes sits in the engine bay and can rust inside. That can cause an electrical short and start a fire under the hood. Dealers will replace a fuse in that control box at no charge, and on 2019-2021 models they will also update the stability control software. Hyundai first announced this recall in September 2020 and added more Tucsons, going back to 2016, that December.

How serious is it?

Very serious: an engine compartment fire can injure people or lead to a crash, so park outside and away from buildings until it is repaired, though the notice does not tell you to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Tucson is covered. 2. Park outside and away from structures as a precaution. 3. Call your Hyundai dealer or Hyundai at 1-855-371-9460 (recall 195) for the free fuse replacement and, if applicable, the software update.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:ANTILOCK/TRACTION CONTROL/ELECTRONIC LIMITED SLIP:CONTROL UNIT/MODULE
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) recalled certain 2019-2021 Tucson vehicles on September 4, 2020. On December 30, 2020, Hyundai expanded the recall population, and added certain 2016-2018 Tucson vehicles. The Anti-lock Brake Hydraulic Electronic Control Unit (HECU) could corrode internally and cause an electrical short, possibly resulting in an engine compartment fire.
Safety risk
An engine compartment fire can increase the risk of injury or crash.
Remedy
Hyundai will notify owners, and dealers will replace the HECU fuse. For 2019-2021 model years, the Electronic Stability Control (ESC) software will also be updated. Recall repairs will be performed free of charge. Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures as a precaution until the recall repair is complete. The recall began November 2, 2020. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 195.
