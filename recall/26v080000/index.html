Nissan Rogue Turbo Engines May Fail Or Catch Fire

NHTSA campaign 26V080000 · Reported Feb 12, 2026 · Nissan North America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

Engine failure can cause the vehicle to suddenly lose drive power, which the notice says raises the risk of a crash. A bearing failure can also let hot oil escape, which the notice says increases the risk of an engine fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In these Nissan Rogue vehicles with the 1.5L turbo engine, high engine temperatures can break down the engine oil. When the oil breaks down, the engine bearings can seize up and become damaged, which can lead to the engine failing. If a bearing fails badly enough, it can also cause a breach in the engine block, allowing hot oil to leak out of the engine.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Rogue is included.
2. Contact a Nissan dealer for the software update and inspection (Nissan customer service: 800-647-7261).
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V080000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:HARD PARTS INTERNAL/MECHANICAL
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2023-2025 Rogue vehicles equipped with 3-cylinder 1.5L variable compression turbo (VC-Turbo) engines. Increased temperatures can break down the engine oil, which may cause the engine bearings to seize and become damaged, leading to engine failure.
Safety risk
Engine failure can cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash. A bearing failure may cause a breach in the engine block, allowing hot oil to be discharged, increasing the risk of an engine fire.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the engine control module (ECM) software, inspect for diagnostic trouble codes, and test drive the vehicle, free of charge. The dealer may also inspect the oil pan for metal debris, and replace the engine if necessary. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 8, 2026. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 800-647-7261. Nissan's numbers for this recall are R25E2 and R25E3. This recall expands previous NHTSA recall number 25V437. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning February 27, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
