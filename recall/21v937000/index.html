Hyundai Accent brake part may weaken stopping power

NHTSA campaign 21V937000 · Reported 2021-12-02 · Hyundai Motor America · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Hyundai Accent

What's wrong, in plain English

The brake master cylinder is the part that pushes brake fluid to the brakes when you press the pedal. On these cars it may have been made with sharp edges inside that can damage the rubber seal. A damaged seal lets pressure escape, so the brake pedal may travel farther toward the floor than normal and the car may need a longer distance to come to a stop than it should.

How serious is it?

Longer stopping distances raise the chance of a crash; the notice does not tell owners to park the car, but if the pedal feels soft or sinks, get to a dealer immediately.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Accent is part of recall 21V937000.
2. Call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 (recall number 213) or your Hyundai dealer to schedule service.
3. The dealer will replace the brake master cylinder, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:MASTER CYLINDER
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2021 Accent vehicles. The brake master cylinder may have been manufactured with sharp edges that can damage the inner oil seal, resulting in reduced braking performance.
Safety risk
Reduced braking performance can cause longer brake pedal travel or extend the distance required to stop the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the brake master cylinder, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 10, 2022. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 213.
