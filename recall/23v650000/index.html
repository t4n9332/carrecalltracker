Hyundai Kona rear seat belts may not work properly

NHTSA campaign 23V650000 · Reported 2023-09-22 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

A rear seat belt that does not restrain a passenger properly can mean serious injury in a crash; Hyundai does not tell owners to stop driving, but get it checked soon, especially if you carry back seat passengers.

What's wrong, in plain English

The seat belts for the two outer rear seats in some 2024 Kona vehicles may not work correctly. The problem is in the retractor, the spool inside the belt that pulls it snug and locks it in a crash. If a belt does not lock or hold as it should, a passenger in the back may not be held safely during a crash. Dealers will check both belts and replace the retractor if needed.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Kona is affected. 2. Contact your Hyundai dealer, or Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460, and reference recall 250. 3. Have the dealer inspect and, if needed, replace the rear seat belt retractors at no cost.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER:RETRACTOR
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2024 Kona vehicles. The seat belt assemblies in the right and left rear seats may not function properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 208, "Occupation Crash Protection" and 209, "Seat Belt Assemblies."
Safety risk
A seat belt that does not function properly may not adequately restrain an occupant in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the seat belt retractor as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 21, 2023. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 250.
