Subaru Ascent Front Tie Rod May Separate

NHTSA campaign 21V954000 · Reported 2021-12-09 · Subaru of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A tie rod that separates from its housing causes a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Subaru is recalling certain Ascent vehicles because of an incorrect tapered hole in the tie rod housing. This can cause the front tie rod ends to not fit properly, and the tie rods and housings can become deformed during assembly or service if too much torque is applied. As a result, the tie rods may separate from the housing.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Subaru dealer to have the front tie rod ends and housings replaced; you can also call Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614.
3. The repair is free.

  • 2020 Subaru Ascent

Official notice

Component
STEERING:LINKAGES:TIE ROD ASSEMBLY
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2020 Ascent vehicles. Due to an incorrect tapered hole in the housing, the front tie rod ends may not fit properly. The improper fit can result in the tie rods and housings being deformed during assembly and service replacement if excessive torque is applied.
Safety risk
The tie rods may separate from the housing, causing a loss of steering control and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the left and right front tie rod ends and housings, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 7, 2022. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRL-21.
