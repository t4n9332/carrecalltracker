Subaru Ascent Tie Rods May Separate From Steering

NHTSA campaign 21V954000 · Reported 2021-12-09 · Subaru of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Subaru Ascent

What's wrong, in plain English

Tie rods are the steering parts that push and pull the front wheels when you turn the wheel. They seat into a tapered hole in the steering housing, and on some 2020 Ascents that hole was made incorrectly, so the tie rod ends do not fit properly. Over-tightening them during assembly or a later repair can deform the parts. A deformed tie rod can separate from the housing, and if it does you lose steering control.

How serious is it?

Serious — losing a tie rod means losing steering, which can cause a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but this should go to the top of your to-do list.

What you should do

1. Verify your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls under recall 21V954000 (Subaru's WRL-21).
2. Call your Subaru dealer, or Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614, to schedule the repair.
3. The dealer replaces both front tie rod ends and their housings at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:LINKAGES:TIE ROD ASSEMBLY
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2020 Ascent vehicles. Due to an incorrect tapered hole in the housing, the front tie rod ends may not fit properly. The improper fit can result in the tie rods and housings being deformed during assembly and service replacement if excessive torque is applied.
Safety risk
The tie rods may separate from the housing, causing a loss of steering control and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the left and right front tie rod ends and housings, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 7, 2022. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRL-21.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
