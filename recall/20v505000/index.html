Volkswagen and Audi front seat backrests may be weak

NHTSA campaign 20V505000 · Reported 2020-08-26 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderate: it only matters in a rear-end crash, where an occupant could be hurt worse, and the notice does not tell you to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Welds holding part of the front seat frame and the backrest adjuster together may not have been done correctly at the factory. During normal driving you would not notice anything different about the seat. But if another vehicle hits you from behind, the bad weld could break and the seat back could give way, so the seat would not hold the person in it as well as it should. Dealers inspect the front seats and replace the seat back frames if necessary.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle falls under recall 20V505000.
2. Contact your dealer, or Volkswagen customer service at 1-888-241-2289 (Audi owners: 1-800-893-5298), to book the seat inspection.
3. The inspection and any seat frame replacement are free.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen ) is recalling certain 2020 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport, Jetta, Jetta GLI and Tiguan and Audi Q5 and SQ5 and 2021 Volkswagen Atlas vehicles. Parts of the front seat frame and backrest adjuster may not have been welded properly.
Safety risk
In the event of a rear-end crash, the backrest adjustment could break, possibly reducing the amount of occupant restraint, thus increasing risk of injury.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the front seats, and, as necessary, replace the seatback frames, free of charge. The recall began September 11, 2020. Volkswagen owners may contact their customer service at 1-888-241-2289. Audi owners may contact their customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's numbers for this recall are 72L1, 72K8, and 72K9.
