Jeep Wrangler frame stud can puncture fuel tank

NHTSA campaign 23V191000 · Reported 2023-03-23 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Jeep Wrangler
  • 2021 Jeep Wrangler
  • 2022 Jeep Wrangler

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2020-2022 Jeep Wrangler vehicles, a metal stud on the frame sits close to the fuel tank. In a crash, that stud can punch a hole in the tank and let gasoline leak out. If leaking fuel reaches a hot surface or a spark, it can catch fire. Dealers will look at the frame and, if the stud is there, remove it and paint the area, all at no charge.

How serious is it?

The danger only shows up in a crash, but the result then is a fuel leak and possible fire; Jeep does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside, so drive normally and get the inspection done.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Wrangler is included in recall 28A.
2. Contact your Jeep dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to schedule the frame inspection.
3. Have the stud removed if needed — the inspection and repair are free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:FRAME AND MEMBERS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2020-2022 Jeep Wrangler vehicles. A stud on the frame assembly may puncture the fuel tank in a crash, which can result in a fuel leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and, if necessary, remove the frame stud and apply paint, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 6, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 28A.
