Ford Bronco backup camera image stays on screen too long

NHTSA campaign 23V165000 · Reported 2023-03-10 · Ford Motor Company · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford Bronco
  • 2022 Ford Bronco

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Ford Broncos with the 8-inch SYNC screen keep showing the backup camera picture even after you shift out of reverse. These vehicles were already repaired once under an earlier recall, and the problem came back. A camera view that lingers on the screen while you are driving forward can pull your eyes off the road. The fix is a software update for the SYNC system, done at a dealer or sent to your Bronco over the air.

How serious is it?

The least serious of the bunch — it is a distraction rather than a mechanical failure, and the vehicle is safe to keep driving while you wait for the update.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Bronco is included. 2. Watch for the over-the-air software update on your screen, or contact your dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 23C05). 3. Have the SYNC software updated — either way, it is free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Bronco vehicles equipped with 8-inch SYNC infotainment screens that were previously remedied under recall 22V-825. The rearview camera image may still be displayed after a backing event has ended. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview image that continues to be displayed after the vehicle is shifted out of reverse may distract the driver, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The SYNC module software will be updated by a dealer, or through an over-the-air (OTA) update, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on March 28, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23C05. (over-the-air software update)
