Volvo VNL Clutch Part May Fail, Causing Movement

NHTSA campaign 19V032000 · Reported 2019-01-14 · Volvo Trucks North America

high How serious is it? About this label

Unintended movement of the truck increases the chance of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Volvo VNL trucks that are built with an automated manual transmission have an internal part inside the clutch assembly that may fail. If this part fails, it could cause the truck to move on its own without the driver meaning it to. This kind of unintended vehicle movement creates a safety concern for the driver and for anyone else nearby on the road.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your VNL is included.
2. Contact a Volvo dealer, or call Volvo customer service at 1-800-528-6586, to have the Transmission Control Module software updated.
3. This repair is free.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2018 Volvo VNL
  • 2019 Volvo VNL

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:CLUTCH ASSEMBLY
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Volvo VNL vehicles equipped with an automated manual transmission. An internal component in the clutch assembly may fail, possibly resulting in unintended vehicle movement.
Safety risk
Unintended vehicle movement can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Volvo has notified owners and dealers will update the Transmission Control Module software, free of charge. The recall began February 1, 2019. Owners can contact Volvo customer service at 1-800-528-6586. Volvo's number for this recall is RVXX1901.
