Kia Stinger wiring may be damaged, risking engine fire

NHTSA campaign 18V754000 · Reported 2018-10-25 · Kia Motors America · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Kia Stinger

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2018 Stingers, a bundle of wiring that runs through the engine bay and into the cabin can rub against a sharp metal edge left on the left fender panel. Over time that edge can cut through the covering and into the wires themselves. Damaged wires can short out, and a short circuit under the hood can start a fire. A dealer inspects the wiring, replaces it if it is damaged, and adds a protective cover either way.

How serious is it?

This is the most serious kind of recall because it can lead to a fire, and the notice does not restrict driving, but you should get the inspection done as soon as possible rather than waiting.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Stinger is included.
2. Call a Kia dealer right away, or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542 (recall SC170), and ask for the earliest appointment.
3. Have the dealer inspect the wiring harness and install the cover or replacement; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FRONT UNDERHOOD
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) is recalling certain 2018 Kia Stinger vehicles. The front wiring harness located in the engine and passenger compartment may get damaged from contacting a burr on the left fender body panel.
Safety risk
The damaged wiring harness may short circuit, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Kia will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the wiring harness for damage. If no damage is found, a cover will be installed. If damage is found, the wiring harness will be replaced and a cover will be installed, free of charge. The recall began December 20, 2018. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall SC170.
