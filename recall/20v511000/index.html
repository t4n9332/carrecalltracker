Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500 engine damage can cause fire

NHTSA campaign 20V511000 · Reported Aug 27, 2020 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

critical How serious is it? About this label

A damaged engine block can leak oil onto hot engine or exhaust parts, which raises the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500 cab chassis trucks built with a Cummins 6.7L High Output engine. While the engine is warming up to its normal operating temperature, it may not have enough warmup protection, and the oil film that should be on the connecting rod bearings inside the engine can be missing. That can damage the engine and cause a connecting rod to fail, which could punch a hole in the engine block.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included.
2. Contact a Ram dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall number W57), to book the engine software update.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V511000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:OIL/LUBRICATION
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500 cab chassis built with a Cummins 6.7L High Output engine. Inadequate warmup protection can cause a lack of oil film on the engine connecting rod bearings while the engine is reaching operating temperature. This can result in engine damage and connecting rod failure, which could potentially puncture the engine block.
Safety risk
A damaged engine block may leak oil, which can contact hot engine or exhaust components, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will flash the engine calibration software to enhance the engine warm up protection, free of charge. The recall began September 10, 2020. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is W57.
