Volkswagen Atlas Fuel Tank Defect Risks Leaks, Stalling

NHTSA campaign 19V614000 · Reported 2019-08-21 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak near a source of ignition increases the risk of a fire, and an engine that stalls while driving increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

A part inside the fuel tank called the suction jet may have been welded in the wrong spot. This can allow fuel to leak from the tank. Uneven fuel distribution caused by the problem can also make the engine stall while driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer to have the fuel tank replaced (customer service: 1-800-893-5298).
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019 Atlas vehicles. The suction jet in the fuel tank may have been welded in the incorrect position allowing fuel to leak. Additionally, the vehicle may stall due to uneven fuel distribution.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire. An engine stall can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel tank, free of charge. The recall began August 30, 2019. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 20BU.
