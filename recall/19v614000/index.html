Volkswagen Atlas fuel tank may leak gasoline

NHTSA campaign 19V614000 · Reported 2019-08-21 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

This is very serious — a fuel leak can start a fire and the engine can stall while you drive, so get it fixed right away, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Volkswagen Atlas vehicles, a part inside the fuel tank may have been welded in the wrong spot at the factory. Because it sits incorrectly, gasoline can leak out. The same problem can also spread fuel unevenly inside the tank, which can make the engine shut off while you're driving. Leaking gasoline near anything hot or sparking can catch fire, and an engine that quits unexpectedly can leave you stranded in traffic. Dealers will replace the entire fuel tank.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall number 20BU) or your dealer to schedule the repair as soon as possible. 3. Have the dealer replace the fuel tank — it costs you nothing.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019 Atlas vehicles. The suction jet in the fuel tank may have been welded in the incorrect position allowing fuel to leak. Additionally, the vehicle may stall due to uneven fuel distribution.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire. An engine stall can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel tank, free of charge. The recall began August 30, 2019. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 20BU.
