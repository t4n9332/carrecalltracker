Subaru Legacy, Outback Passenger Air Bag May Not Deploy

NHTSA campaign 24V227000 · Reported 2024-03-22 · Subaru of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

An air bag that fails to deploy in a crash increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

A capacitor inside the sensor that detects whether the front passenger seat is occupied may crack and short circuit. If this happens, the front passenger air bag may not deploy in a crash.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
Contact a Subaru dealer at 1-844-373-6614 to schedule a repair.
The front passenger seat's occupant detection sensor will be replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SENSOR:OCCUPANT CLASSIFICATION:FRONT PASSENGER
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2020-2022 Outback and Legacy vehicles. A capacitor in the sensors for the Occupant Detection System (ODS) may crack and short circuit, which can prevent the front passenger air bag from deploying in a crash.
Safety risk
An air bag that does not deploy during a crash can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the ODS sensors on the front passenger seat, free of charge. Owner notifications were mailed February 3, 2025. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRA-24.
