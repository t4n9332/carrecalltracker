Chevrolet Silverado EV and Hummer EV Seat Belt Bolts Loose

NHTSA campaign 23V786000 · Reported Nov 22, 2023 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A front seat belt that isn't properly attached may not restrain an occupant as intended, increasing the risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Chevrolet Silverado EV and GMC Hummer EV vehicles, the bolts that attach the left or right front seat belt buckle may not have been tightened properly. Because of this, that seat belt buckle may not stay attached and hold a passenger securely the way it's designed to. This means the front seat belt on the affected side might not restrain the driver or front passenger as firmly as intended.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm it's included.
2. Contact a Chevrolet or GMC dealer, or call 1-833-382-4389 (EVCHEVY) or 1-833-486-6373 (HUMMER-EV).
3. The repair - tightening the seat belt buckle bolts - is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V786000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:ANCHORAGE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023-2024 GMC Hummer EV pick up, 2024 GMC Hummer EV SUV, and 2024 Chevrolet Silverado EV vehicles. The left or right front seat belt buckle attachment bolts may not be tightened properly.
Safety risk
An improperly tightened seat belt buckle may not restrain an occupant as intended, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will tighten the left and right front seat belt buckle attachment bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 21, 2023. Owners may contact GMC customer service at 1-833-EVCHEVY (1-833-382-4389) (TTY 711 / 1-800-833-2438) /1-833-HUMMER-EV (1-833-486-6373) (TTY 711 / 1-800-833-2438). GM's number for this recall is N232419280.
