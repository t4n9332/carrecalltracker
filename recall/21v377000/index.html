Ford Edge Driver's Seat Belt May Not Tighten

NHTSA campaign 21V377000 · Reported 2021-05-24 · Ford Motor Company · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford Edge

What's wrong, in plain English

The driver's seat belt in these Ford Edge vehicles has a part that is supposed to pull the belt tight the instant a crash starts, holding you firmly against the seat. On the affected vehicles that part may not work correctly when a crash sets it off. If the belt does not tighten the way it should, the driver can be thrown forward more than intended and hurt worse. Dealers replace the driver's seat belt retractor with a new one, free of charge.

How serious is it?

This is serious because the driver may not be properly restrained in a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so keep wearing your seat belt and get the repair done soon.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Edge is affected. 2. Contact your Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 and reference recall 21C13. 3. Schedule the seat belt replacement — there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:RETRACTOR
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 Edge vehicles. The driver's seat belt retractor pretensioner assembly may not function correctly when activated during a crash. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
A seatbelt assembly that does not function as intended can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver's seat belt retractor pretensioner assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 15, 2021. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21C13.
