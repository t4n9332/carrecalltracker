What's wrong, in plain English

A sensor that measures brake pressure may leak brake fluid into a nearby switch. That leak can cause an electrical short, which can overheat and raise the risk of a fire, whether the truck is being driven or parked.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Park outside and away from structures until repaired, and contact a Chevrolet dealer (1-800-222-1020) for the fix. 3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V266000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC
Summary
General Motors. LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019-2023 Chevrolet 4500HD, 5500HD, and 6500HD Medium-Duty trucks. The brake pressure sensor assembly may leak brake fluid into the brake pressure switch and cause an electrical short-circuit.
Safety risk
An electrical short in the brake pressure switch can overheat the circuit and increase the risk of a fire while driving or parked.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. Dealers will replace the master cylinder pressure sensor and inspect the wire harness, replacing it, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 11, 2023. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N222375270.
