Nissan Rogue fuel hose may leak and cause fire

NHTSA campaign 21V068000 · Reported 2021-02-11 · Nissan North America, Inc. · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Nissan Rogue

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Rogues, the hose that carries gasoline may not be clamped in the right spot, and the ring that locks the fuel tank in place may not be seated all the way. A loose hose can leak fuel, and leaking gasoline can catch fire. The hose can also come off completely, which would make the engine stall while you are driving. A dealer will reposition and secure the hose and the lock ring.

How serious is it?

Very serious — the notice describes both a fire risk and a stalling risk, so have it inspected right away rather than waiting.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Rogue is included. 2. Contact your Nissan dealer promptly, or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669, and mention recall PC791. 3. The inspection and any repair to the fuel hose and lock ring are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2021 Nissan Rogue vehicles. The fuel hose may not be secured in the proper position and the fuel tank lock ring may not be fully seated.
Safety risk
An improperly secured fuel hose may leak, increasing the risk of a fire. The fuel hose may also disconnect completely, causing the engine to stall and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and, as necessary, reposition and secure the fuel hose and lock ring, free of charge. The recall began March 16, 2021. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PC791.
