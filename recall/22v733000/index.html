Mercedes-Benz AMG E53 trunk battery may come loose in crash

NHTSA campaign 22V733000 · Reported 2022-09-30 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

The car is safe to keep driving day to day, but if you are in a crash these safety features may not work, which can make injuries worse, so get the battery secured.

What's wrong, in plain English

The 12-volt battery in the trunk may not be bolted down tightly. In a crash it could move and break its electrical connections. If that happens, several things that help you afterward may stop working: the automatic emergency call that dials for help, the hazard flashers, the power seat controls, and the doors that unlock by themselves. The car drives normally otherwise. A dealer will secure the battery properly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included. 2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer about recall 2022100009, or call MBUSA at 1-800-367-6372. 3. Have the 12-volt battery secured — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2019-2022 CLS450 Coupe and AMG E53, 2019-2021 AMG CLS53 Coupe, and 2021-2022 E450 vehicles. The 12-Volt battery in the trunk may not be properly secured, which, in the event of a crash, can cause electrical connection failure, disabling various safety features including, emergency call (eCall) system, electric seat adjustment, hazard warning lights, and automatic door locks.
Safety risk
The inability to operate safety features (eCall system, hazard lights, electric seats, or automatic door locks) can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will secure the 12V battery, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 20, 2023. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2022100009.
