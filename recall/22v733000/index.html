Mercedes-Benz trunk battery may come loose in a crash

NHTSA campaign 22V733000 · Reported Sep 30, 2022 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Mercedes-Benz says being unable to use these safety features can increase the risk of injury during a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The 12-volt battery in the trunk of these cars may not be held down properly. In a crash, that can cause the electrical connection to fail and shut off several safety features, including the emergency call system, the hazard warning lights, the power seat adjustment and the automatic door locks. Mercedes-Benz is recalling certain 2019-2022 CLS450 Coupe and AMG E53, 2019-2021 AMG CLS53 Coupe, and 2021-2022 E450 cars. Dealers will secure the battery at no charge.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is covered. 2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer to have the battery secured, or call MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. 3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V733000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2019-2022 CLS450 Coupe and AMG E53, 2019-2021 AMG CLS53 Coupe, and 2021-2022 E450 vehicles. The 12-Volt battery in the trunk may not be properly secured, which, in the event of a crash, can cause electrical connection failure, disabling various safety features including, emergency call (eCall) system, electric seat adjustment, hazard warning lights, and automatic door locks.
Safety risk
The inability to operate safety features (eCall system, hazard lights, electric seats, or automatic door locks) can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will secure the 12V battery, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 20, 2023. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2022100009.
