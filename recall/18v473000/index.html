BMW M5 Fuel Gauge May Show Incorrect Level

NHTSA campaign 18V473000 · Reported Jul 17, 2018 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

An incorrect fuel reading may lead to the vehicle stalling, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The fuel level sensor inside the fuel tank may get stuck against a fuel line. When this happens, the fuel gauge can show an inaccurate reading, possibly showing more fuel than is actually in the tank.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a BMW dealer, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V473000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2018 BMW M5 vehicles. The fuel level sensor in the fuel tank may become stuck against an in-tank fuel line, causing the fuel gauge to display an inaccurate and possibly larger amount of fuel than is actually available.
Safety risk
An incorrect fuel reading may lead to the vehicle stalling and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will secure the in-tank fuel line to keep the fuel line from contacting the fuel level sensor, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin September 2018. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
