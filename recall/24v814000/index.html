Mazda CX-70 and CX-90 Electrical Unit Software Issue

NHTSA campaign 24V814000 · Reported Oct 31, 2024 · Mazda North American Operations

medium How serious is it? About this label

A dash electrical unit that is not functioning properly increases the risk of a crash, according to the notice. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Mazda CX-90 and CX-70 vehicles equipped with a Dash Electrical Supply Unit, several features may not work correctly during startup. This can affect the defroster, the seat belt warning, the 360-degree view monitor, and, on plug-in hybrid models, the system that cools the high-voltage battery.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Mazda dealer to have the Dash ESU reprogrammed with updated software; call Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500, option 6.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V814000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
Mazda North American Operations (Mazda) is recalling certain 2024-2025 CX-90 and 2025 CX-70 vehicles equipped with a Dash Electrical Supply Unit (ESU). During vehicle startup, the defroster, seat belt warning, 360° view monitor functions, and the PHEV high-voltage battery cooling system may not function properly.
Safety risk
An ESU that does not function properly, increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the Dash ESU with improved software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 18, 2024. Owners may contact Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500 Option 6. Mazda's number for this recall is 7124J.
