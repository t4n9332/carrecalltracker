BMW R 1300 GS starter relay can overheat

NHTSA campaign 24V557000 · Reported 2024-07-26 · BMW of North America, LLC · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 BMW R 1300 GS

What's wrong, in plain English

The relay that switches on the starter motor has a housing that may have been made incorrectly. A faulty housing can damage the relay, and a damaged relay can get very hot. An overheating electrical part on the motorcycle creates a fire risk. Dealers will fit a new starter relay and load updated software, both at no cost to you. BMW mailed letters to owners on September 23, 2024.

How serious is it?

This is a fire risk, which is very serious, so get the free repair right away even though the notice does not tell riders to stop riding the motorcycle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your R 1300 GS is affected.
2. Contact a BMW Motorrad dealer immediately for an appointment, or call BMW at 1-800-525-7417.
3. Have the starter relay replaced and the software updated; both are free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:STARTER ASSEMBLY:RELAY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2024 R 1300 GS motorcycles. The housing for the starter motor relay may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can result in a damaged relay that can overheat.
Safety risk
An overheated electrical relay increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the starter relay and update the software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 23, 2024. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
