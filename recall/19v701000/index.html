Subaru Forester Passenger Airbag May Deactivate

NHTSA campaign 19V701000 · Reported Oct 3, 2019 · Subaru of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

If the airbag deactivates while the seat is occupied, that passenger faces an increased risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2015-2018 Forester vehicles equipped with heated seats, an electrical connection for the front passenger seat's occupant detection system may loosen. If this happens, the front passenger airbag may turn off even though the seat is occupied.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Subaru dealer (1-844-373-6614) to schedule an inspection.
3. Dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace the sensor mat harness free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V701000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SENSOR:OCCUPANT CLASSIFICATION
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2015-2018 Forester vehicles equipped with heated seats. An electrical connection in the front passenger seat for the Occupant Detection System (ODS) may loosen.
Safety risk
If the connection loosens, the front passenger airbag may deactivate even though the seat is occupied, increasing the risk of injury to the front passenger in the event of a crash.
Remedy
Subaru will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and, as necessary, replace the ODS sensor mat harness, free of charge. The recall began February 11, 2020. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WUM-98.
