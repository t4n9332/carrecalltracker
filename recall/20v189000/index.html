Subaru Crosstrek rear seat belts may not lock

NHTSA campaign 20V189000 · Reported 2020-03-31 · Subaru of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Subaru Crosstrek

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2019 Crosstrek vehicles have rear seat belts with a locking mechanism that senses the belt webbing pulling out. On these cars, that lock may not grab the way it should. In a crash where the car is hit more than once, the belt may not hold the person in place properly, so a rear-seat passenger could be injured more badly. Subaru dealers will replace the rear seat belt assemblies entirely.

How serious is it?

Serious for anyone riding in back during a multiple-impact crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so you can keep using the car while you arrange the free replacement.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your Crosstrek is included. 2. Contact a Subaru dealer, or Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614 (recall WRC-20), to book the work. 3. Have the rear seat belts replaced at no cost.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2019 Crosstrek vehicles with rear seat belt assemblies with a dual-mode locking mechanism. The seat belt webbing sensor locking mechanism may not lock as intended.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash involving multiple impacts, the seat belt may not properly restrain the occupant, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Subaru will notify owners, and dealers will replace rear seat belt assemblies, free of charge. The began April 15, 2020. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRC-20.
