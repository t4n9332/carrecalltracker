Audi Q7 and Q8 Side Airbag Mounting

NHTSA campaign 23V868000 · Reported Dec 20, 2023 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

An improperly mounted air bag may not restrain the driver as intended, increasing the risk of injury during a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The side air bag built into the driver's seat may not have been properly mounted to the seat's frame during assembly. Because it isn't attached the way it should be, the air bag may not work as intended if it needs to deploy.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact an Audi dealer, or call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834.
3. Dealers will inspect and reinstall the air bag as needed, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V868000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2023-2024 SQ8, RSQ8, Q8, Q7, and 2024 SQ7 vehicles. The driver's seat side air bag may have been improperly mounted to the seatback frame. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 214, "Side Impact Protection."
Safety risk
An improperly mounted air bag may not restrain an occupant as intended, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and reinstall the air bag as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 17, 2024. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 69GA.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
