Acura RDX and Integra brake unit can leak fluid

NHTSA campaign 23V430000 · Reported 2023-06-15 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Honda Accord
  • 2023 Acura Integra
  • 2023 Acura RDX

What's wrong, in plain English

These cars have a part that helps control braking and stability. A small valve inside it can leak brake fluid. When that happens, the car may creep or roll on its own while the automatic brake hold feature is holding it still, or the brake pedal may sink farther down than normal before the brakes bite. Either one makes a crash or injury more likely. Dealers will replace the whole unit at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious — unexpected vehicle movement or a longer-travelling brake pedal raises the risk of a crash or injury; there is no instruction to stop driving, but have it fixed promptly.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is affected.
2. Contact a Honda or Acura dealer, or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (recall numbers OEJ, XEK, AEY, XEX, OEW).
3. Have the vehicle stability assist modulator replaced at no charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:ANTILOCK/TRACTION CONTROL/ELECTRONIC LIMITED SLIP:CONTROL UNIT/MODULE
Summary
Honda (America Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023 Civic, Acura RDX, Acura Integra, and 2022 Honda Accord vehicles. A ball valve in the vehicle stability assist (VSA) modulator may leak brake fluid, which can result in unintended vehicle movement when the brake hold feature is engaged or an unexpected increase in brake pedal travel.
Safety risk
Unintended vehicle movement or an unexpected increase in brake pedal travel can increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the VSA modulator, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 24, 2023. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's numbers for this recall are OEJ, XEK, AEY, XEX and OEW.
