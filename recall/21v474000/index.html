Nissan Rogue Child Seat Anchors May Be Weak

NHTSA campaign 21V474000 · Reported 2021-06-24 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

This is serious if you carry a child in a car seat that uses the top strap, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so get the inspection done as soon as you can.

What's wrong, in plain English

The back seat of these Rogues has metal loops behind the seatback that a child safety seat's top strap hooks onto. On the affected vehicles those loops may have been welded to the seat frame poorly. A weak weld can give way, so a child seat anchored there may not stay put during hard braking or a crash, letting it move and the child get hurt. Dealers will check the rear seatbacks and replace any that are bad, at no cost.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Rogue is included. 2. Call your Nissan dealer or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 and reference recall PC812. 3. Have the rear seatbacks inspected and replaced if needed — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:MID/REAR ASSEMBLY
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2021 Rogue vehicles. The second-row upper tether wires may have been improperly welded to the seatback frame, preventing a child seat from being securely anchored. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 225, "Child Restraint Anchorage Systems."
Safety risk
An insecurely anchored child seat can move during a sudden stop or crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the rear seatbacks as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 30, 2021. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PC812.
