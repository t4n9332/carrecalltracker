Mercedes-Benz GLB side spoilers may detach from tailgate

NHTSA campaign 21V231000 · Reported 2021-04-01 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

The vehicle is safe to drive, but a piece coming off at highway speed could cause a crash or injury for someone behind you, so have it inspected soon.

What's wrong, in plain English

The small side spoilers on the tailgate of these Mercedes-Benz GLB models may not have been attached correctly at the factory. A spoiler that works loose can fall off while you are driving and land in the road, where it becomes a hazard for the cars behind you. A dealer will inspect both spoilers and replace them if needed, free of charge. If you notice a loose or missing trim piece at the back, mention it.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your GLB is covered by recall 2021050009.
2. Book an inspection with your Mercedes-Benz dealer, or call MBUSA at 1-800-367-6372.
3. The inspection and any spoiler replacement are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:TAILGATE
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2021 GLB35 AMG, and 2020-2021 GLB250 vehicles. The side spoilers on the tailgate might not have been installed correctly, allowing them to detach.
Safety risk
Detached spoilers can create a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
MBUSA will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace the side spoilers, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed July 2, 2021. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2021050009.
