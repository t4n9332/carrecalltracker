Mercedes-Benz GLB tailgate spoilers may come off

NHTSA campaign 21V231000 · Reported 2021-04-01 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

A spoiler that detaches can create a hazard in the road, increasing the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Mercedes-Benz is recalling certain GLB35 AMG and GLB250 vehicles because the side spoilers on the tailgate may not have been installed correctly. Spoilers that are not properly attached can come off the vehicle. Dealers will inspect the side spoilers and replace them if necessary, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed July 2, 2021. Owners can reach Mercedes-Benz USA customer service at 1-800-367-6372 with questions about this recall.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer, or MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372, to have the spoilers inspected.
3. The inspection and any replacement are free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:TAILGATE
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2021 GLB35 AMG, and 2020-2021 GLB250 vehicles. The side spoilers on the tailgate might not have been installed correctly, allowing them to detach.
Safety risk
Detached spoilers can create a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
MBUSA will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace the side spoilers, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed July 2, 2021. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2021050009.
