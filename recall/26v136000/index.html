Volvo EX30 seat belt warning chime may not sound

NHTSA campaign 26V136000 · Reported 2026-03-11 · Volvo Car USA, LLC · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Volvo EX30
  • 2026 Volvo EX30CC

What's wrong, in plain English

The chime that reminds you to buckle up may not sound. A bad electrical connection at the audio module keeps the warning beep from playing, so a driver or passenger riding unbelted may never get the reminder. The seat belts themselves still work normally; only the alert is affected. Because of this, the vehicle does not meet the federal safety rule for seat belt warnings. A dealer will replace the connector parts at no charge.

How serious is it?

This is a low-level problem and the notice does not restrict driving, but an unbelted person who never hears a reminder faces a higher chance of injury in a crash, so buckle up every trip.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls (these vehicles became searchable March 13, 2026). 2. Call Volvo customer service at 1-800-458-1552 or your dealer, referencing recall R10365. 3. Book the connector terminal replacement; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:WARNING LIGHT/DEVICES
Summary
Volvo Car USA, LLC (Volvo Car) is recalling certain 2025-2026 EX30 and 2026 EX30CC vehicles. The seat belt warning system may not activate the audible warning chime as intended. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An audible warning chime that does not properly alert of an unbelted seat belt can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the terminals at the audio module connector, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 26, 2026. Owners may contact Volvo Car’s customer service at 1-800-458-1552. Volvo Car’s number for this recall is R10365. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on March 13, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
