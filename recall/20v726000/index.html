Lincoln Aviator steering part may crack while driving

NHTSA campaign 20V726000 · Reported 2020-11-20 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

Very serious, because steering can be reduced suddenly and with no warning; the notice does not say to park the vehicle, but this should be handled right away.

What's wrong, in plain English

The steering knuckle is the part that connects each front wheel to the steering and suspension. On some Aviators it may have been made incorrectly, and a bad one can crack. If that happens, you lose some of your ability to steer the vehicle, which could cause a crash. There is no warning light for this. Ford dealers will inspect the front knuckle and put in a new one if there is a problem, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Aviator is affected.
2. Call a Lincoln or Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and give them recall number 20S69.
3. Have the front knuckle inspected and replaced if necessary — there is no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 Lincoln Aviator vehicles. The steering knuckles may have been manufactured improperly.
Safety risk
The improperly manufactured knuckle may fracture, reducing steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the front knuckle, and replace it if necessary, free of charge. The recall began December 16, 2020. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 20S69.
