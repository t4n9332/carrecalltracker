Hyundai Ioniq Electric May Accelerate Unexpectedly

NHTSA campaign 21V944000 · Reported Dec 7, 2021 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

A vehicle that speeds up on its own after the driver releases the gas pedal is harder to control and can increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Hyundai is recalling certain Ioniq Electric vehicles because an unstable electrical ground can cause the car to enter a fail-safe mode with less power and slower acceleration. In certain conditions while the vehicle is in this fail-safe mode, it may speed up on its own even after the driver has let go of the gas pedal.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Hyundai dealer to schedule the free repair; you can also call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460.
3. Dealers will update software and repair wiring at no cost to you.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V944000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:CHARGING:MODULE:ONBOARD (OBCM)
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Ioniq electric vehicles. An unstable electrical ground may cause the vehicle to enter a fail-safe mode with reduced acceleration and power. In certain conditions while in fail-safe mode, the vehicle may accelerate after the gas pedal is released.
Safety risk
Unexpected acceleration following gas pedal release can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the EPCU (Electric Power Control Unit) software and repair the ground wiring, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 9, 2022. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 215.
