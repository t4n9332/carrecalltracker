GMC Sierra EV front turn signals hard to see

NHTSA campaign 24V755000 · Reported 2024-10-10 · General Motors, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 GMC Sierra EV

What's wrong, in plain English

On these trucks, the front turn signal lights are too close in brightness to the parking lights beside them, so when you signal a turn the blinking light does not stand out. Other drivers, riders and people crossing the street may not notice that you plan to turn. Because of that, the truck does not meet the federal rules for lights and reflectors. Dealers will replace the module that runs the headlamp LEDs, free of charge.

How serious is it?

Turn signals that others cannot clearly see raise the risk of a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving; get the fix done and use extra caution when turning.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Sierra EV is included. 2. Call your GMC dealer or GMC customer service at 888-988-7267 and reference recall N242474560. 3. Have the headlamp LED driver module replaced, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:LIGHTING CONTROL MODULE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024 GMC Sierra EV vehicles. The front turn signal lights and parking lights may be too similar in brightness, making it difficult to see the front turn signals when they are activated. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Front turn signals that are difficult to see may not clearly indicate the driver's intentions to other road users, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the headlamp multipurpose LED driver (MLD) module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 4, 2024. Owners may contact GMC customer service at 888-988-7267. GM's number for this recall is N242474560.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
