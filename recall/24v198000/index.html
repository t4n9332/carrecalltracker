Chrysler 300 and Dodge Charger Side Airbag Rupture

NHTSA campaign 24V198000 · Reported Mar 14, 2024 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) (Stellantis)

high How serious is it? About this label

This is a serious hazard: a ruptured airbag inflator can send sharp metal fragments at people in the vehicle, which the notice says can cause injury or death. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The right and left side curtain airbags in these vehicles may rupture. This happens because of a defect in the way the airbag inflator was manufactured. If the inflator ruptures, it can send sharp pieces of metal flying inside the vehicle's cabin. Those metal fragments could strike the driver or any passengers riding inside, and this could result in injury or even death to the people in the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Chrysler or Dodge dealer, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The repair (replacing both side curtain airbags) is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V198000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN:INFLATOR
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) (Stellantis) is recalling certain 2018-2021 Dodge Charger and Chrysler 300 vehicles. The right and left side curtain air bag inflators may rupture due to a manufacturing defect.
Safety risk
An inflator rupture may result in sharp metal fragments striking occupants, resulting in injury or death.
Remedy
Dealers will replace both side curtain air bags, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning February 20, 2025. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 19B.
