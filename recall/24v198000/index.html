Dodge Charger and Chrysler 300 Curtain Airbags May Rupture

NHTSA campaign 24V198000 · Reported 2024-03-14 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) (Stellantis)

critical How serious is it? About this label

Very serious: the notice says a burst inflator can send sharp metal fragments into occupants and cause injury or death; there is no order to stop driving, but this repair should be scheduled right away.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Dodge Charger and Chrysler 300 cars, the side curtain airbags that run above the side windows were built with a defect. The metal canister that fills the bag with gas can burst instead of inflating the bag the way it should. If that happens, sharp pieces of metal can fly into the passenger area and strike the people inside. Dealers will replace both the right and left side curtain airbags at no cost to you.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is part of recall 24V198000.
2. Contact your Dodge or Chrysler dealer to book the airbag replacement, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall number 19B).
3. Have both side curtain airbags replaced — the repair is free — and ask for written confirmation when it is done.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN:INFLATOR
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) (Stellantis) is recalling certain 2018-2021 Dodge Charger and Chrysler 300 vehicles. The right and left side curtain air bag inflators may rupture due to a manufacturing defect.
Safety risk
An inflator rupture may result in sharp metal fragments striking occupants, resulting in injury or death.
Remedy
Dealers will replace both side curtain air bags, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning February 20, 2025. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 19B.
