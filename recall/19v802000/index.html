Hyundai Palisade side curtain airbags may not inflate properly

NHTSA campaign 19V802000 · Reported 2019-11-07 · Hyundai Motor America · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Hyundai Palisade

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts that hold the side curtain airbags in place can damage the airbag as it inflates. These are the airbags that drop down along the side windows to protect people's heads in a side impact. If one is torn or damaged while deploying, it may not fill the way it should and may not protect you in a crash. Dealers will install a protective cover over the mounting bolts, free of charge, so the bolts cannot damage the airbag.

How serious is it?

Serious in a side crash because an airbag may not work as intended, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Palisade is covered by recall 186.
2. Contact your Hyundai dealer or call 1-855-371-9460 to schedule the work.
3. Have the protective covers installed on the airbag mounting bolts — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2020 Palisade vehicles. The mounting bolts used to secure the side curtain air bags may damage the side curtain air bags during deployment.
Safety risk
Damaged side curtain air bags may not inflate properly in the event of a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Hyundai will notify owners, and dealers will install a protective cover on the mounting bolts, free of charge. The recall began December 20, 2019. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 186.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
