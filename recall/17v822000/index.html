Audi A5: Side Curtain Airbags May Not Deploy Properly

NHTSA campaign 17V822000 · Reported Dec 20, 2017 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

If the head curtain airbag does not deploy properly in a crash, vehicle occupants have an increased risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2018 Audi A5 Coupe vehicles were built with head curtain airbags that may not unfold properly when needed. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard covering occupant ejection protection in a crash. Dealers will modify the head curtain airbags so they deploy properly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact an Audi dealer, or call Audi customer service at 11-800-253-2834.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 17V822000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2018 Audi A5 Coupe vehicles. The affected vehicles were built with head curtain air bag assemblies that may not unfold properly. As such, these vehicle fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 226, "Ejection Mitigation."
Safety risk
In the event of a crash necessitating deployment of the head curtain air bag, if the air bag does not deploy properly, the vehicle occupants have an increased risk of injury.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will modify the head curtain air bags so that they deploy properly, free of charge. The recall began on January 18, 2018. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 11-800-253-2834. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 69T7.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
