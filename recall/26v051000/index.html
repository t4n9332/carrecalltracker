Jeep Grand Cherokee rear coil springs may fall off

NHTSA campaign 26V051000 · Reported 2026-01-29 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Jeep Grand Cherokee L
  • 2022 Jeep Grand Cherokee
  • 2022 Jeep Grand Cherokee L
  • 2023 Jeep Grand Cherokee
  • 2023 Jeep Grand Cherokee L

What's wrong, in plain English

The rear coil springs are big metal springs that hold up the back of the vehicle. On these Jeeps, they may have been installed incorrectly and can come loose and fall off while you are driving. Chrysler says that raises the risk of a crash. Dealers will inspect and repair the rear spring assembly free of charge. Interim letters went out in February 2026, and the final repair was expected in March 2026, with a second letter to follow.

How serious is it?

A suspension spring coming off while you are moving can cause a crash with no warning, so treat this as urgent; the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but call your dealer right away rather than waiting for the second letter.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — these VINs became searchable on January 30, 2026.
2. Call FCA at 1-800-853-1403 about recall 20D, and mention it if your Jeep was already repaired under recall 23V413 — those vehicles still need the new remedy.
3. Have the inspection and repair done at the dealer at no cost.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR:SPRINGS:COIL SPRINGS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Jeep Grand Cherokee L and 2022-2023 Jeep Grand Cherokee vehicles. The rear coil springs may be incorrectly installed and detach from the vehicle while driving.
Safety risk
Rear coil springs that detach from the vehicle while driving increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and repair the rear coil spring assembly, as necessary, free of charge. Interim notification letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, were mailed February 12, 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available, anticipated in March 2026. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 20D. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on January 30, 2026. This recall replaces NHTSA recall number 23V413. Vehicles already repaired under the previous recall will need to have the new remedy completed.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
