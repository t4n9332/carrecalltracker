Honda CB500X and CBR500R motorcycles may leak brake fluid

NHTSA campaign 21V690000 · Reported 2021-09-02 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Honda CB500X

What's wrong, in plain English

These motorcycles have an anti-lock braking part that had too much grease packed into it. Over time that extra grease can pick up dirt and grit, and the debris can get caught in a small valve inside. When the valve is held open by debris, brake fluid can leak out. Losing brake fluid means the brakes may not work as well as they should, and stopping the bike can take longer than you expect.

How serious is it?

Weakened brakes on a motorcycle are a serious crash risk; the notice does not tell riders to park the bike, but have it inspected before riding much further.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your bike is covered by recall 21V690000.
2. Call Honda Powersports customer service at 1-866-784-1870 (recall number KM6) or contact your Honda dealer for an appointment.
3. The dealer will check the ABS part and replace it if needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2019-2020 CB500X and CBR500R ABS motorcycles. Excessive grease in the ABS modulator may collect debris, which can get stuck in the check valve and cause a brake fluid leak.
Safety risk
A brake fluid leak can reduce braking performance and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the lot number on the ABS modulator, and replace the modulator as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 12, 2021. Owners may contact Honda Powersports customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KM6.
