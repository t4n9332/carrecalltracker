Honda CR-V door label lists wrong weight and tire info

NHTSA campaign 23V524000 · Reported 2023-07-27 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Honda CR-V
  • 2022 Honda CR-V

What's wrong, in plain English

The sticker on the driver's door frame of these CR-Vs shows the wrong numbers. It lists an incorrect maximum loaded weight for the vehicle and each axle, and the wrong tire size. Owners and tire shops use that sticker when buying replacement tires and when loading up the car, so the bad information could lead to the wrong size tire being put on. Honda will put a new, corrected label on the door at no cost.

How serious is it?

The label itself does not break anything, but a wrongly sized tire fitted because of it can wear out early or blow out and cause a crash, so the car is generally safe to drive while you wait for the new label.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your CR-V is included. 2. Call your Honda dealer, or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (recall CF7), and schedule the label replacement free of charge. 3. Until the new label is on, don't rely on the door sticker for tire size or weight limits — ask the dealer for the correct figures before buying tires or loading heavy cargo.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2017, 2020, and 2022 CR-V vehicles. The certification label on the driver's side door states incorrect Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR), Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR), and tire size information. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
An incorrectly sized tire may cause premature tread wear, or a tire blow out, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install a new corrected label, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 18, 2023. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is CF7.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
