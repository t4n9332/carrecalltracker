What's wrong, in plain English

Subaru is recalling certain Crosstrek Hybrid, Forester, Forester Hybrid and Ascent vehicles. The certification label on these vehicles shows an incorrect Gross Axle Weight Rating, the maximum weight an axle is rated to carry. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard covering tire and rim selection. Subaru will mail owners a new label free of charge, and owners who prefer can have a dealer install the new label instead, also free.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is covered. 2. Watch for the replacement label in the mail, or ask a Subaru dealer to install it; you can also call Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614 (recall number WRH-26). 3. The new label, whether mailed or dealer-installed, is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V436000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2026 Crosstrek Hybrid, 2025-2026 Forester Hybrid, Forester, and 2019-2026 Ascent vehicles. These vehicles were manufactured with an incorrect Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR) on the certification label. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) 110, "Tire selection and rims."
Safety risk
An incorrect GAWR label may lead to an overloaded vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Subaru will mail a new certification label to owners, free of charge. Owners may also choose to have a dealer install the new label, free of charge. Letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed August 27, 2026. Additional letters will be mailed once the remedy is available. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRH-26.
