Cadillac, Chevy, GMC SUVs Have Wrong Fuel Tank

NHTSA campaign 25V619000 · Reported 2025-09-18 · General Motors, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak near a spark or other ignition source increases the risk of a vehicle fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Cadillac Escalade, Chevrolet Tahoe, and GMC Yukon vehicles with a 6-cylinder diesel engine may have had their original fuel tank replaced with the wrong one. If that happened, fuel could leak out of the tank if the vehicle is involved in a rollover crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm whether your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a dealer to have the fuel tank inspected and, if needed, replaced; Cadillac at 1-800-333-4223, Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020, or GMC at 1-800-462-8782.
3. The inspection and any replacement are free.

Affected vehicles (6)

  • 2021 Cadillac Escalade
  • 2021 Chevrolet Tahoe
  • 2022 GMC Yukon
  • 2023 Chevrolet Tahoe
  • 2024 Chevrolet Tahoe
  • 2024 GMC Yukon

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2021 Cadillac Escalade, 2021, 2023-2024 Chevrolet Tahoe, and 2022 and 2024 GMC Yukon vehicles equipped with a 6-cylinder diesel engine. The original fuel tank may have been replaced with an incorrect fuel tank, which can allow fuel to leak in a rollover crash.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a vehicle fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect for the correct fuel tank and replace it, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 5, 2025. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223, Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020, or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N252512770. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on September 18, 2025.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
