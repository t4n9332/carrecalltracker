NHTSA campaign 26V633000 · Reported Oct 1, 2026 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

What's wrong, in plain English

Honda is recalling certain VT750 motorcycles because the fuel pump may malfunction due to a manufacturing defect. A fuel pump that fails can cause the engine to suddenly stall while the motorcycle is being ridden. Dealers will replace the fuel pump free of charge to correct the problem.

What you should do

1. Check if your VIN is included at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Honda dealer, or Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870, to schedule a repair.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V633000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023-2025 VT1300CX and 2024-2025 VT750 motorcycles. The fuel pump may malfunction due to a manufacturing defect.
Safety risk
Fuel pump failure can cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge. Owner notification letters will be mailed beginning November 27, 2026. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KU9. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning October 8, 2026.
