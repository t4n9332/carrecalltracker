Volkswagen Tiguan Rearview Camera May Not Display

NHTSA campaign 26V321000 · Reported 2026-05-20 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

A rearview camera that doesn't display an image reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Volkswagen Tiguan vehicles that were previously repaired under an earlier recall have a new problem. An error can occur in the camera control unit when the vehicle starts up, which can cause the rearview camera image not to display as it should. This means the picture drivers rely on when backing up may not show up on the screen.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact your Volkswagen dealer, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298.
3. The software update is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2024 Tiguan vehicles previously repaired under recall 25V082. An error may occur in the camera control unit at vehicle startup, resulting in the rearview camera image not displaying as intended. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview camera that does not display an image can reduce the driver's rear view, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the camera control unit software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 4, 2026. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 91NF. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning May 27, 2026.
