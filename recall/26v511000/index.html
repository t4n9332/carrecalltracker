Toyota Camry Hybrid dash display failure disables hazard lights

NHTSA campaign 26V511000 · Reported 2026-08-06 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious: the car still drives, but with no turn signals or hazard lights other drivers cannot tell what you are doing, which raises the risk of a crash.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2025-2026 Toyota Camry Hybrids, the instrument cluster — the display screen behind the steering wheel — can fail as you start the car. When it fails, the hazard lights, turn signals, seat belt warning, and smart key reminder all stop working. That means other drivers cannot see when you are signaling a turn or warning them of trouble. The vehicles do not meet several federal safety rules. Dealers will update the display software for free.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Camry Hybrid is included. 2. Watch for your notification letter, expected September 21, 2026, or call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 (recalls 26TB16 and 26TA16). 3. Schedule the free software update at your dealer, and use hand signals or avoid driving if the display goes dark.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HAZARD FLASHING WARNING LIGHTS:SWITCH
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2025-2026 Camry Hybrid vehicles. The instrument cluster display may fail during vehicle startup, deactivating the hazard lights, turn signals, seat belt warning system, and smart key reminder. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) numbers 101, "Control and Displays," 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices and Associated Equipment," 114, "Theft Protection and Rollaway Prevention," and 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An instrument cluster that fails to display critical safety information increases the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will update the display software, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed September 21, 2026. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 26TB16 and 26TA16.
