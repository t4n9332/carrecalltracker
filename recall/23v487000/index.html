Mazda3 and CX-3 Rearview Camera May Malfunction

NHTSA campaign 23V487000 · Reported Jul 14, 2023 · Mazda North American Operations

high How serious is it? About this label

A rearview camera that doesn't work properly reduces how well you can see behind the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2014 to 2018 Mazda3 and 2016 to 2021 CX-3 vehicles may have a rearview camera that does not display properly. Instead of showing a clear picture, the camera image can flicker or appear distorted. This means the picture you see when using the rearview camera may not be reliable or clear, which can make it harder to see what the camera is supposed to show you behind the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact your dealer to schedule an inspection and repair (Mazda customer service: 1-800-222-5500, option 6).
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V487000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Mazda North American Operations (Mazda) is recalling certain 2014-2018 Mazda3 and 2016-2021 CX-3 vehicles. The rearview camera image may not display properly by showing a flickering or distorted image.
Safety risk
A rearview camera that does not display properly can reduce the driver's rear view, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the rearview camera, as necessary. In addition, dealers will install a wiring harness fastening seal. All repairs will be performed free of charge. Interim notification letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, were mailed September 13, 2023. Final owner letters for affected models and model years in the states of CT, MA, MD, NJ, NY, PA and VA were mailed August 14, 2024. The remaining owner notification letters will be mailed at a future date, anticipated on or before May 1, 2025. Owners may contact Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500, Option 6. Mazda's number for this recall is 6023G.
