Rivian R1T and R1S rear suspension link may come apart

NHTSA campaign 26V003000 · Reported 2026-01-05 · Rivian Automotive, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A rear suspension part separating while driving can cause a sudden loss of control and a crash, so treat this as urgent, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

This recall covers Rivian trucks and SUVs that had service work done before March 10, 2025. During that work, a rear suspension part called the toe link may have been put back together incorrectly. The toe link helps keep the rear wheel pointed straight. If its joint comes apart while you are driving, the wheel can move out of position and you could lose control. Rivian will replace the rear toe link bolts at no cost to you.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is on the list. 2. Call Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261 and reference recall FSAM-1794. 3. Book the free repair to have the rear toe link bolts replaced.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022-2025 R1S and R1T vehicles that previously had service performed. The toe link may have been reassembled incorrectly during service procedures performed before March 10, 2025.
Safety risk
The toe link joint may separate, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will replace the rear toe link bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 17, 2026. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1794. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning February 24, 2026.
