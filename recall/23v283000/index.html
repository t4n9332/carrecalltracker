Ford Bronco and Ranger wheels may have loose lug nuts

NHTSA campaign 23V283000 · Reported 2023-04-20 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity

⚠ NHTSA advises not driving this vehicle until it is repaired. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Ford Bronco

What's wrong, in plain English

The nuts that hold the wheels onto the driver's side of your truck may not have been tightened properly at the factory. If they are loose, a wheel can come off while you are driving. Ford is telling owners not to drive these vehicles until they are checked. A dealer will look at the lug nuts and tighten them if needed, at no charge to you.

How serious is it?

Very serious — a wheel could come off while driving, so do not drive the vehicle until a dealer has inspected it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is affected. 2. Do not drive it; contact your Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 23S17) to set up the inspection. 3. Have the dealer inspect and retighten the lug nuts — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
WHEELS:LUGS/NUTS/BOLTS/STUDS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023 Bronco and Ranger vehicles. The lug nuts on the driver's side wheels may have been improperly tightened.
Safety risk
Loose lug nuts may result in wheel detachment while driving, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until the remedy is completed. Dealers will inspect, and if necessary, retighten the lug nuts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 2, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23S17.
