Lincoln Aviator Plug-In Hybrid Can Lose Drive Power

NHTSA campaign 22V187000 · Reported 2022-03-25 · Ford Motor Company · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Lincoln Aviator Phev

What's wrong, in plain English

These Aviators have a plug-in hybrid system that charges from an outlet. Inside the charging port, a metal connector in the wiring can work loose. If that happens, the vehicle can lose its drive power, meaning it stops pulling and you cannot keep up with traffic. Losing power in a traffic lane raises the risk of being hit. Dealers will check a date code on the charge port wiring and replace the wiring if it is affected, free.

How serious is it?

Serious, because power can be lost while you are driving; Ford has not told owners to stop driving, but this one is worth booking right away.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls against campaign 22V187000.
2. Contact a Lincoln or Ford dealer to have the charge port harness inspected, or call 1-866-436-7332 (Ford's recall number is 22S19).
3. If the harness needs replacing, the parts and labor are free.

Official notice

Component
HYBRID PROPULSION SYSTEM
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022 Lincoln Aviator vehicles equipped with the 3.0L plug-in hybrid powertrain. The inlet charge port harness bus bar may become unseated, leading to a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the inlet charge port harness date code label, and replace the harness, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 2, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S19.
