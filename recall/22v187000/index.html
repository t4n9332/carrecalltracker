Lincoln Aviator Plug-In Hybrid Power Loss Risk

NHTSA campaign 22V187000 · Reported Mar 25, 2022 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

What's wrong, in plain English

On these plug-in hybrid Aviators, a connector bus bar inside the charge port wiring harness may become unseated. If that happens, the vehicle can lose drive power while being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (reference recall 22S19), to schedule an inspection.
3. The harness inspection and replacement, if needed, is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V187000. Official notice

Component
HYBRID PROPULSION SYSTEM
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022 Lincoln Aviator vehicles equipped with the 3.0L plug-in hybrid powertrain. The inlet charge port harness bus bar may become unseated, leading to a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the inlet charge port harness date code label, and replace the harness, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 2, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S19.
