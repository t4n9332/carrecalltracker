Dodge Hornet, Alfa Romeo Tonale Seat Belts May Not Retract

NHTSA campaign 26V510000 · Reported Aug 6, 2026 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

A seat belt that does not restrain an occupant as intended increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Dodge Hornet and Alfa Romeo Tonale vehicles, the rear seat belts next to the windows can become twisted. When that happens, the belt may not pull back in the way it should. A belt that does not retract properly may not hold an occupant in place the way it is meant to. Dealers will replace the retractors, the parts that wind the belt in and out, at no cost to the owner.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included. 2. Contact your dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403, to schedule the replacement. 3. The new retractors are free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V510000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:RETRACTOR
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2023-2025 Dodge Hornet and 2023-2026 Alfa Romeo Tonale vehicles. The rear outboard seat belts may become twisted and fail to retract properly.
Safety risk
A seat belt that does not retract may fail to restrain properly an occupant as intended, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the retractors, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed beginning September 24, 2026. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 84D. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on August 15, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
