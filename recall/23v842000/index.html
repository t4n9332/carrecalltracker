Audi electric models: portable charging cable can overheat

NHTSA campaign 23V842000 · Reported 2023-12-13 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

Serious fire risk — the car itself is fine to drive, but do not use the 220/240-volt portable charging cable until the dealer gives you the new one.

What's wrong, in plain English

These Audis came with a portable charging cable that plugs into a 220/240-volt outlet, the kind used for a large appliance. At the 100% charge setting, the cable can draw more electricity than some home outlets can handle, and the outlet or the cable can get dangerously hot. Overheating like this can start a fire. Audi says to stop using that cable for now and charge with the 110-volt cable or at a public charging station until you receive a free replacement.

What you should do

1. Stop using the 220/240-volt portable cable; use the 110-volt home cable or public chargers instead.
2. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
3. Call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834 (recalls 93U6 and 93U8) and have your dealer supply the free replacement cable.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:CHARGING:CABLE/CORD:ONBOARD
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2022-2024 RS e-tron GT, e-tron GT, Q4 e-tron Sportback, Q4 e-tron, 2020-2021 Audi A8, 2019-2024 e-tron Quattro, 2020-2024 e-tron Sportback Quattro, Q5, and 2021-2022 A7 vehicles. When using the industrial 220V/240V plug at the 100% charge setting, some home power outlets may be incapable of handling the electrical current required to utilize the compact charging system, causing the outlet or charging cable to overheat.
Safety risk
An overheated outlet or charging cable can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to use the 220V/240V compact/portable charging cable and only use the 110V home charging cable or public charging stations. Dealers will supply a new 220V/240V compact/portable charging cable with an incorporated temperature sensor, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on March 28, 2024. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's numbers for this recall are 93U6 and 93U8.
