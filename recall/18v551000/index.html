Nissan Frontier, Versa Key Ignition May Shut Off Engine

NHTSA campaign 18V551000 · Reported 2018-08-22 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

If the vehicle stalls this way, it can increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Nissan is recalling certain Frontier, Versa, and Versa Note vehicles with a mechanical key ignition system. Over time, the weight hanging on the key ring, combined with road conditions or another jarring event, may cause the ignition switch to move out of the run position and into the accessory position. When that happens, the engine turns off while the vehicle is being driven, since the ignition switch is no longer in the position needed to keep the engine running.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Nissan dealer to have the ignition switch inspected and, if needed, replaced; Nissan customer service is 1-800-867-7669.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Affected vehicles (3)

  • 2018 Nissan Frontier
  • 2018 Nissan Versa
  • 2018 Nissan Versa Note

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:IGNITION
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2017 Nissan NV200, Sentra, NV3500 Van, NV3500 Bus, NV200 Taxi, NV1500, NV2500 Van, Chevrolet City Express, 2017-2018 Frontier, Versa Note, and Versa Sedan vehicles that have a mechanical (physical) key ignition system. In these models, over time, the weight on the key ring and road conditions or some other jarring event may cause the ignition switch to move out of the run position to the accessory position, turning off the engine.
Safety risk
If the vehicle stalls, it can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the production information on the ignition switch, and replace it as necessary, free of charge. The recall began October 1, 2018. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
