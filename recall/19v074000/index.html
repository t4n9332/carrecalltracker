BMW X1 taillights can loosen or fall off

NHTSA campaign 19V074000 · Reported 2019-01-30 · BMW of North America, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 BMW X1
  • 2019 BMW X1

What's wrong, in plain English

The metal hardware used to mount the rear taillight housings on these BMWs was contaminated when it was made. Because of that, the brackets holding a taillight can crack, letting the light come loose or even fall off the car while you drive. Drivers behind you would lose the rear lighting the law requires, and a taillight lying in the road can become a hazard for other cars.

How serious is it?

Serious but not urgent: BMW does not tell owners to stop driving, so you can keep driving while you schedule the free repair, though a missing taillight makes a crash more likely, especially at night.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included.
2. Call a BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, to book the repair.
3. Have the dealer secure the taillights — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TAIL LIGHTS
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2018-2019 BMW X1 sDrive28i and X1 xDrive28i vehicles. Contaminated hardware may cause the attachment brackets of the taillight housings to crack, causing the lights to loosen or possibly detach from the vehicle.
Safety risk
If a taillight detaches from the vehicle, it would no longer provide the required lighting to following traffic, or it may become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will properly secure the taillights, free of charge. The recall began March 28, 2019. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
