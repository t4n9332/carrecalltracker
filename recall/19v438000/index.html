What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain 2019 Jeep Renegade vehicles with the Upland trim package. The vehicle's air bag control module may have been calibrated incorrectly. Because of this incorrect calibration, the module may cause the air bags to deploy the wrong way if the vehicle is in a frontal crash, instead of deploying the way they're designed to in that situation.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Chrysler dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The air bag control module replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V438000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS: AIR BAG/RESTRAINT CONTROL MODULE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Jeep Renegade vehicles equipped with the Upland trim package. The Occupant Restraint Controller (ORC) may have been incorrectly calibrated.
Safety risk
The miscalibrated ORC may incorrectly deploy the air bags during a frontal crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the ORC. The recall began September 24, 2019. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V73.
