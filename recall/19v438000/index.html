Jeep Renegade airbag computer set up incorrectly

NHTSA campaign 19V438000 · Reported 2019-06-11 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Jeep Renegade

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Jeep Renegade vehicles with the Upland trim package have a computer that decides when the airbags should fire in a crash. On these vehicles that computer may have been programmed with the wrong settings. Because of that, during a head-on crash the airbags may not go off the way they should. Airbags that deploy incorrectly do not protect people the way they were designed to, so someone in the vehicle could be hurt more badly.

How serious is it?

This affects how well the airbags protect you in a front-end crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so book the free repair soon.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if recall 19V438000 (Chrysler number V73) applies to you.
2. Call a Jeep dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to set an appointment.
3. Have the dealer replace the airbag control computer, which is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS: AIR BAG/RESTRAINT CONTROL MODULE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Jeep Renegade vehicles equipped with the Upland trim package. The Occupant Restraint Controller (ORC) may have been incorrectly calibrated.
Safety risk
The miscalibrated ORC may incorrectly deploy the air bags during a frontal crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the ORC. The recall began September 24, 2019. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V73.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
