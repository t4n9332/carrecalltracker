Acura TLX fuel tank weld may leak fuel

NHTSA campaign 24V950000 · Reported Dec 19, 2024 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak near a source of ignition increases the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Acura TLX cars have a fuel filler neck and fuel tank that may not have been welded together properly during manufacturing. This improper welding can allow fuel to leak out of the tank. A fuel leak is a serious problem on its own, and it becomes especially dangerous if it happens near a possible source of ignition.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is included in this recall.
2. Contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138, or your Acura dealer, to schedule the repair.
3. The dealer will replace the fuel tank assembly at no cost to you.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V950000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Acura (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2021-2023 TLX vehicles. The fuel filler neck and fuel tank may have been improperly welded, which can result in a fuel leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel tank assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 3, 2025. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Acura's number for this recall is JKX.
