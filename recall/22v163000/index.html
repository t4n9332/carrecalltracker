Volkswagen Golf R Engine Cover May Loosen and Melt

NHTSA campaign 22V163000 · Reported Mar 16, 2022 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

A melting engine cover increases the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

During certain driving situations, such as hard acceleration, the engine design cover may come loose. A loose cover can contact hot surfaces inside the engine compartment and melt.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer to have the engine cover removed, or call Volkswagen at 1-800-893-5298.
3. The repair is free; a replacement cover will be provided later at no charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V163000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2022 Golf GTI and Golf R vehicles. Certain driving situations, such as high acceleration driving maneuvers, may loosen the engine design cover.
Safety risk
A loose engine design cover may contact hot surfaces in the engine compartment and melt, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will remove the engine design cover, free of charge. Once parts become available, Volkswagen will provide a free updated engine design cover under a separate Service Action. Owner notification letters were mailed by March 29, 2022. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 10H5.
