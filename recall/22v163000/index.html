Volkswagen Golf R engine cover can loosen, risking fire

NHTSA campaign 22V163000 · Reported 2022-03-16 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Volkswagen Golf R

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2022 Golf GTI and Golf R cars, the decorative plastic cover that sits on top of the engine can work loose, especially during hard acceleration. If it shifts, it can touch hot parts under the hood and melt. Melting plastic against hot engine parts raises the chance of a fire. Volkswagen's fix is for a dealer to take the cover off entirely. A redesigned cover will be offered later at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious, because the notice describes a fire risk; Volkswagen has not told owners to stop driving or park outside, but you should get the cover removed as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included (Volkswagen's recall number is 10H5). 2. Call your Volkswagen dealer or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 and book an appointment to have the engine cover removed. 3. Have the work done free of charge, and ask the dealer to contact you when the updated cover is available under the separate service action.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2022 Golf GTI and Golf R vehicles. Certain driving situations, such as high acceleration driving maneuvers, may loosen the engine design cover.
Safety risk
A loose engine design cover may contact hot surfaces in the engine compartment and melt, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will remove the engine design cover, free of charge. Once parts become available, Volkswagen will provide a free updated engine design cover under a separate Service Action. Owner notification letters were mailed by March 29, 2022. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 10H5.
