Volvo V60CC and V90CC fuel pump fuse can blow

NHTSA campaign 21V414000 · Reported 2021-06-02 · Volvo Car USA, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Volvo V60CC
  • 2020 Volvo V90CC

What's wrong, in plain English

The fuse protecting the low-pressure pump that feeds fuel from the tank toward the engine can blow, and when it does the pump stops working. With no fuel being delivered, the engine can shut off while you are driving, which can leave you without power in traffic or on the highway. The recall covers a range of 2019-2020 Volvo cars and wagons, not just yours. The fix is quick: a dealer installs a different fuse, free of charge.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious — the car does not have to be parked, but an engine that shuts off unexpectedly at speed can cause a crash, so have the fuse changed soon.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your Volvo is covered. 2. Call a Volvo dealer or Volvo Car customer service at 1-888-458-1552 (recall R10100) to schedule the free fuse replacement. 3. If your car has already stalled or lost power, mention that when you book the appointment.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FUSES AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS
Summary
Volvo Car USA, LLC (Volvo Car) is recalling certain 2019-2020 V90, XC60, S60, V60, V60CC, S90, V90CC, S90L, and XC90 vehicles. The low-pressure fuel pump can blow a fuse and fail.
Safety risk
Failure of the low-pressure fuel pump can cause a stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuse, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 2, 2021. Owners may contact Volvo Car customer service at 1-888-458-1552. Volvo Car's number for this recall is R10100.
