What's wrong, in plain English

Mitsubishi is recalling certain 2022 Outlander vehicles because the front and second-row passenger seat belt automatic locking retractors may deactivate too early. When this happens, the retractor may not hold a child restraint system securely in place the way it is designed to. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet federal requirements covering occupant crash protection and child seat security.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Mitsubishi dealer, or call Mitsubishi customer service at 1-888-648-7820.
3. The seat belt inspection and replacement, if needed, is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V596000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:RETRACTOR
Summary
Mitsubishi Motors North America, Inc. (MMNA) is recalling certain 2022 Outlander vehicles. The front and second-row passenger seat belt automatic locking retractors may deactivate early, which can prevent the child restraint system from securing properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An unsecured child restraint system can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect, and replace as necessary, the right front seat belt assembly, and the right and left second-row seat belt assemblies, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed September 28, 2021. Owners may contact MMNA customer service at 1-888-648-7820. MMNA's number for this recall is SR-21-007.
