Acura TLX Tires May Be Damaged

NHTSA campaign 22V950000 · Reported Dec 22, 2022 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

Damaged tires can lead to a sudden loss of air pressure and loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Honda says the tires on some Acura TLX vehicles may have been cut or torn during the sorting process. This tire damage can affect how the tires hold up on the road. Honda is recalling the affected vehicles so dealers can inspect the tires and replace them if necessary.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact an Acura dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V950000. Official notice

Component
TIRES:BEAD
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2022 Acura TLX vehicles. The tires may have sustained cuts and tears during the sorting process, which can result in tire damage.
Safety risk
Damaged tires can lead to a sudden loss of air pressure and loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the tires as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 30, 2023. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is GCW.
