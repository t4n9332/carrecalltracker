What's wrong, in plain English

On some Toyota Tacoma vehicles, a metal flange on the front and rear shock absorbers may corrode. If it corrodes enough to fail, the external oil reservoir attached to the shock absorber can detach from the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer at 1-800-331-4331 for an inspection.
3. The shock absorbers will be replaced, if needed, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V514000. Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR:SHOCK ABSORBER
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2024 Tacoma Hybrid and 2024-2025 Tacoma vehicles. The metal flange on the front and rear shock absorbers may corrode and fail, resulting in detachment of the external oil reservoir.
Safety risk
An oil reservoir that detaches can create a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the front and rear shock absorber assemblies, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed September 21, 2026. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 26TB14 and 26TA14.
