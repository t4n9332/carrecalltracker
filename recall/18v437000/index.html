Cadillac CT6 Child Seat Anchor Bars May Be Blocked

NHTSA campaign 18V437000 · Reported Jun 28, 2018 · General Motors LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

If a child seat cannot be attached correctly to the anchor bar, there is an increased risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2016-2018 Cadillac CT6 vehicles were built with extra adhesive on the rear inboard child seat anchor bars. This buildup can get in the way when someone tries to attach a child safety seat to those bars, making correct installation difficult. If the child seat cannot be attached the right way to the anchor bar, it may not hold the seat securely.

What you should do

1. Check your CT6's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Cadillac dealer, or call Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006.
3. The inspection and repair are free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V437000. Official notice

Component
SEATS:MID/REAR ASSEMBLY
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Cadillac CT6 vehicles. The inboard child seat anchorage bars may have had excess structural adhesive applied during assembly, which can interfere with the installation of a child seat. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 225, "Child Restraint Anchorage Systems."
Safety risk
If the child seat cannot be attached correctly to the anchorage bar, there is an increased the risk of injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will inspect, remove any excess adhesive, and apply anti-corrosion primer to the inboard child seat anchorage bars, free of charge. The recall began August 3, 2018. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is 18228.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
