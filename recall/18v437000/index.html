Cadillac CT6 child seat anchors blocked by extra glue

NHTSA campaign 18V437000 · Reported 2018-06-28 · General Motors LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Cadillac CT6

What's wrong, in plain English

The metal bars in the back seat that a child seat clips onto got too much glue during assembly on some 2016-2018 Cadillac CT6s. The extra glue is on the inner anchor bars and can get in the way, so a child seat may not clip on correctly. A child seat that is not attached properly may not protect a child the way it should in a crash. Dealers will scrape off the extra glue and add rust protection.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the car itself is fine to drive, but if you carry a child seat on those inner anchors, make sure it clicks on tightly or secure it with the seat belt until the repair is done.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your CT6 is included.
2. Contact your Cadillac dealer or Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 (recall 18228).
3. Have the anchor bars cleaned and primed free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:MID/REAR ASSEMBLY
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Cadillac CT6 vehicles. The inboard child seat anchorage bars may have had excess structural adhesive applied during assembly, which can interfere with the installation of a child seat. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 225, "Child Restraint Anchorage Systems."
Safety risk
If the child seat cannot be attached correctly to the anchorage bar, there is an increased the risk of injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will inspect, remove any excess adhesive, and apply anti-corrosion primer to the inboard child seat anchorage bars, free of charge. The recall began August 3, 2018. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is 18228.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
