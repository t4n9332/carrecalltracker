Ram ProMaster left taillight and turn signal quit

NHTSA campaign 24V197000 · Reported 2024-03-14 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) (Stellantis)

medium How serious is it? About this label

This raises the chance of being hit by another driver rather than losing control yourself, and the van is safe to keep driving while you arrange the fix — though it is worth having someone check your rear lights now.

What's wrong, in plain English

A wiring connection in these vans can be poor, and when it is, the left taillight and the left turn signal stop working. Other drivers then cannot see that you are turning left, and at night or in bad weather your van is harder to see from behind. Both make a crash more likely, especially in traffic. A dealer will inspect the wiring harness and replace it if needed, free. You may not notice a rear light is out from the driver's seat.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is covered by recall 21B.
2. Contact your Ram dealer for an appointment, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. Have the wiring harness inspected and replaced if needed, at no cost.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) (Stellantis) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Ram ProMaster vehicles. The wiring harness may have a poor connection, which can cause the left taillight and left turn signal to become inoperative. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
An inoperative left turn signal may not notify other drivers of a turning vehicle, and an inoperative left taillight can reduce the vehicle's visibility to other drivers. Both scenarios can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the harness, as necessary free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 20, 2025. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 21B.
