Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Van Seat Airbags May Fail

NHTSA campaign 20V322000 · Reported 2020-06-03 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the side airbag deploys unexpectedly or fails to deploy in a crash, occupants face a greater risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In vans with swivel seats, the wiring for the front seats may have been installed incorrectly. This wiring can get pinched and damaged inside the seat's swivel frame. If the wiring is damaged, the side airbag built into the seat may go off when it isn't supposed to, or it may fail to go off during a crash when it is needed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is affected.
2. Contact an authorized Mercedes-Benz or Freightliner Sprinter dealer, or Daimler Vans USA customer service at 1-877-762-8267.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2019 Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Spinter vans (platform 970 (VS30)), equipped with swivel seats. The wiring harnesses for the front seats may have been routed incorrectly and could become jammed and damaged in the swivel seat frames.
Safety risk
If the wiring harness is damaged, the side air bag within the seat may deploy inadvertently or not deploy as designed in the event of a crash, increasing the risk of injury to vehicle occupants.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and an authorized Mercedes-Benz or Freightliner Sprinter dealer will inspect the wire harness routing for both front seats and will repair the wiring and add clips or cable ties as needed, free of charge. The recall began August 24, 2020. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3DREHSIK.
