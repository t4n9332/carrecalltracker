Volvo VNR truck steering bolts may be loose

NHTSA campaign 20V241000 · Reported 2020-04-29 · Volvo Trucks North America · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Volvo VNR

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts that hold the steering gear in place may not have been tightened enough at the factory. The steering gear is the part that turns your steering wheel movement into the wheels turning. If those bolts work loose, the driver can lose control of the truck. A Volvo Trucks dealer will tighten the steering gear bolts to the correct specification, free of charge. The recall started May 20, 2020.

How serious is it?

Loss of steering control can cause a serious crash, so although the notice does not tell owners to park the truck, this should be checked before further use if possible.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is covered.
2. Contact your Volvo Trucks dealer or customer service at 800-528-6586 and reference recall RVXX2001.
3. Have the steering gear fasteners tightened — the work is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2020-2021 VNL and VNR trucks. The steering gear fasteners may have not been properly tightened.
Safety risk
The loose fasteners may result in a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Volvo Trucks will notify owners, and dealers will tighten the steering gear fasteners to specification, free of charge. The recall began May 20, 2020. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks customer service at 800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2001.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
