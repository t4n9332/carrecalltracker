Lincoln Corsair backup camera image can go blank or distorted

NHTSA campaign 25V510000 · Reported 2025-08-08 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Lincoln Corsair
  • 2021 Lincoln Corsair
  • 2022 Lincoln Corsair

What's wrong, in plain English

Water can get into the electrical connectors for the camera that shows what is behind you when you back up. Once water gets in, the camera picture may not come up on the dashboard screen at all, or it may look distorted. Without that picture you can miss a child, a pet, or an object behind the vehicle while reversing. Dealers replace the camera and the wiring harness that plugs into it, at no charge.

How serious is it?

The risk is moderate and limited to backing up — Ford has not told owners to stop driving, but check your mirrors and look over your shoulder every time you reverse until it is repaired.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Corsair is included.
2. Contact a Lincoln or Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and mention recall 25S83.
3. Have the camera and harness replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2022 Lincoln Corsair vehicles. Water may enter into the rearview camera connectors, which can cause the rearview camera image not to appear on the display or display a distorted image.
Safety risk
A rearview camera that displays a blank or distorted image can reduce the drivers view of what is behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the rearview camera harness and rearview camera, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 1, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S83.
