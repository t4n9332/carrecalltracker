Audi e-tron battery can take on moisture and catch fire

NHTSA campaign 19V434000 · Reported 2019-06-10 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

This is a fire risk: you may keep driving unless warning lights and the "Electrical system: fault" message appear, and at that point you must stop immediately, park outside away from structures, skip charging, and have the car towed.

What's wrong, in plain English

A faulty seal on the charging socket can let moisture get into the big high-voltage battery that powers the car. Water inside that system can cause a short circuit, and that can start a fire. If warning lights come on along with the message "Electrical system: fault," stop driving right away, park outdoors away from buildings, do not plug the car in, and call Audi Roadside Assistance at 1-800-411-9988 for a tow. Dealers replace the seal and any damaged parts free.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your e-tron is covered. 2. Contact an Audi dealer or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834 (recall 93E8) to book the free seal replacement and inspection. 3. If the fault warning ever appears, stop, park outdoors away from buildings, do not charge, and call Audi Roadside Assistance at 1-800-411-9988 for a tow.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2019 Audi e-tron vehicles. Moisture can enter the high-voltage battery system through a faulty seal on the charging socket.
Safety risk
Moisture in the high-voltage system can cause a short-circuit, increasing the risk of fire. If warning lights appear along with the message "Electrical system: fault," immediately stop driving the vehicle, park outdoors away from structures, do not charge the vehicle, and contact Audi Roadside Assistance at 1-800-411-9988 to have the vehicle towed.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will install a new seal and, as necessary, replace the affected system components, free of charge. The recall began September 6, 2019. Owners may contact Audi's customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 93E8.
