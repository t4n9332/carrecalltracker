Audi e-tron battery may let moisture in, fire risk

NHTSA campaign 19V434000 · Reported 2019-06-10 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

Moisture inside the high voltage system can cause a short circuit, which the notice says increases the risk of fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Volkswagen's Audi brand is recalling certain 2019 Audi e-tron vehicles. A faulty seal on the charging socket can let moisture get into the high voltage battery system. Audi says that if warning lights appear along with the message "Electrical system: fault," owners should immediately stop driving, park outdoors away from buildings, and not charge the vehicle. Dealers will install a new seal and, if needed, replace affected parts of the system at no cost to the owner.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. If warning lights appear along with the message "Electrical system: fault," immediately stop driving, park outdoors away from structures, do not charge the vehicle, and call Audi Roadside Assistance at 1-800-411-9988 to have it towed.
3. Contact an Audi dealer for the repair, or call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834 (recall number 93E8); the repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2019 Audi e-tron vehicles. Moisture can enter the high-voltage battery system through a faulty seal on the charging socket.
Safety risk
Moisture in the high-voltage system can cause a short-circuit, increasing the risk of fire. If warning lights appear along with the message "Electrical system: fault," immediately stop driving the vehicle, park outdoors away from structures, do not charge the vehicle, and contact Audi Roadside Assistance at 1-800-411-9988 to have the vehicle towed.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will install a new seal and, as necessary, replace the affected system components, free of charge. The recall began September 6, 2019. Owners may contact Audi's customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 93E8.
