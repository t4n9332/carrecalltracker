What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Volkswagen ID.4 vehicles have an interior sunshade over the panoramic glass roof that is made of fabric that isn't as fire-resistant as federal safety rules require. Because of this, the vehicle doesn't meet the flammability standard for interior materials. If a fire were to start inside the vehicle, this fabric could help the fire spread further instead of resisting it the way it's supposed to.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298.
3. Dealers will apply flame retardant to the sunshade fabric, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V750000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2023-2024 ID.4 vehicles. The interior sunshade for the panoramic glass roof may be insufficiently fire retardant. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 302, "Flammability of Interior Materials."
Safety risk
In a fire, fabric that is not fire retardant may further fuel a fire, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will apply flame retardant to the sunshade fabric, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 15, 2023. Owners may contact Volkswagen's customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 60G3.
