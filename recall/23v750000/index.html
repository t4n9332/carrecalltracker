Volkswagen ID.4 roof sunshade fabric burns too easily

NHTSA campaign 23V750000 · Reported 2023-12-07 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Volkswagen ID.4
  • 2024 Volkswagen ID.4

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2023 and 2024 Volkswagen ID.4 vehicles have a pull-across sunshade under the large glass roof made from fabric that does not resist flame as well as federal safety rules require. The shade will not start a fire on its own. But if a fire began inside the vehicle for some other reason, this fabric could burn more readily and help the fire spread, leaving people less time to get out. Dealers will treat the fabric with flame retardant.

How serious is it?

This is a lower-level safety concern that only matters if a fire has already started, and Volkswagen has not told owners to stop driving or park outside, so normal use is fine until the free treatment is done.

What you should do

1. Confirm your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact your Volkswagen dealer or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 and reference recall 60G3. 3. Schedule the appointment — applying the flame retardant is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2023-2024 ID.4 vehicles. The interior sunshade for the panoramic glass roof may be insufficiently fire retardant. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 302, "Flammability of Interior Materials."
Safety risk
In a fire, fabric that is not fire retardant may further fuel a fire, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will apply flame retardant to the sunshade fabric, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 15, 2023. Owners may contact Volkswagen's customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 60G3.
