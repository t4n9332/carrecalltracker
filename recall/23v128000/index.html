Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator front axle gear may break

NHTSA campaign 23V128000 · Reported 2023-02-24 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Ford Expedition
  • 2023 Lincoln Navigator

What's wrong, in plain English

A gear inside the front axle may have skipped a hardening step at the factory, which leaves it weaker than it should be. That gear can snap. If it does, you lose four-wheel drive, and the front wheels may lock up, which could make you lose control. A dealer will replace the front axle gear set, or the whole axle if the part has already broken. The same recall also covers certain 2023 F-150 trucks.

How serious is it?

Front wheels locking up while you are moving can cause a crash, so this is serious; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but schedule the free repair promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle falls under recall 23V128000 (Ford 23S07).
2. Call your Ford or Lincoln dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, for an appointment.
3. Have the front axle pinion gear set — or the full axle, if it is already broken — replaced at no cost.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AXLE ASSEMBLY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023 F-150, Expedition, and Lincoln Navigator vehicles. The front axle pinion may not have been heat-treated, which can cause it to fracture.
Safety risk
A fractured pinion can result in a loss of 4-wheel drive and may cause the front wheels to lock up, increasng the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front axle pinion gear set. If there is a broken front axle pinion, the entire axle assembly will be replaced. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 21, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23S07.
