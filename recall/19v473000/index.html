What's wrong, in plain English

Volkswagen is recalling certain Audi Q5 and SQ5 vehicles. In a crash where the passenger frontal air bag deploys, the instrument panel carrier inside the dashboard may break because it was not welded correctly. If that carrier breaks, it can damage the passenger frontal air bag itself, so the air bag may not work the way it is supposed to when it is needed most.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact an Audi dealer or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-253-2834 to schedule the repair.
3. The instrument panel and passenger air bag will be replaced free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V473000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL:PASSENGER SIDE:INFLATOR MODULE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019 Audi Q5 and SQ5 vehicles. In the event of a crash deploying the passenger's frontal air bag, the instrument panel carrier may break due to incorrect welding. If the carrier breaks, there may be damage to the passenger frontal air bag.
Safety risk
A damaged frontal air bag may not provide adequate protection, increasing the risk of an injury.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will replace the instrument panel and the passenger frontal air bag, free of charge. The recall began August 16, 2019. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 90N1.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
