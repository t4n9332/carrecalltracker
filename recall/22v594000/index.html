Ford Maverick side-curtain airbags may not deploy correctly

NHTSA campaign 22V594000 · Reported 2022-08-08 · Ford Motor Company · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Ford Maverick

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2022 Ford Mavericks have side-curtain airbags that may not open properly. These are the airbags that drop down along the side windows to cushion your head and help keep people from being thrown out of the vehicle in a side crash or rollover. If they do not work as designed, people inside can be hurt more seriously. Dealers will replace both the left and right side-curtain airbag units with a redesigned part, free of charge.

How serious is it?

Serious — the truck drives normally, but protection in a side crash or rollover may fall short of what it should be, so arrange the free replacement as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Maverick is covered by recall 22V594000 (Ford's number 22C20).
2. Call your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, to schedule replacement of both side-curtain airbags.
3. Have the new airbag units installed — parts and labor are free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022 Maverick vehicles. The side-curtain air bags may not deploy properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 226, "Ejection Mitigation."
Safety risk
Improper deployment of an air bag increases the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the left and right side-curtain air bag modules with a new design, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 30, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22C20.
