Honda Pilot and Passport tires may fail suddenly

NHTSA campaign 21V165000 · Reported 2021-03-11 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Honda Passport
  • 2019 Honda Pilot
  • 2020 Honda Passport
  • 2020 Honda Pilot

What's wrong, in plain English

Some of these Passports and Pilots left the factory with Continental tires that were heated too long while they were being made. Tires cooked that way can crack in the sidewall or come apart inside. That can mean a tire suddenly goes flat, or the tread peels off the tire while you are driving, either of which can make the vehicle very hard to control. A dealer will look at all four tires and replace any bad ones at no cost.

How serious is it?

Very serious, because a tire can lose air or shed its tread with no warning and cause a crash; the notice does not order owners to park the vehicle, but this should be checked right away if it has not been done already.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is covered and whether the work was already completed — this recall began April 22, 2021. 2. Call a Honda dealer or American Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 and mention recall UA2. 3. Have all four tires inspected and any affected tires replaced, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
TIRES
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Passport and Pilot vehicles equipped with Continental tires. On the affected vehicles, it is possible that one or more tires were cured for too long during tire production.
Safety risk
Overcured tires may develop a break in the sidewall, resulting in sudden air loss or belt edge separation, which could lead to tread/belt loss. Either condition can cause a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Honda will notify owners, and dealers will inspect all four tires and replace them as necessary, free of charge. The recall began April 22, 2021. Owners may contact American Honda Motor Co. customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is UA2.
