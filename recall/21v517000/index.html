Chevrolet Bolt EV and EUV Airbag Bolt Loose

NHTSA campaign 21V517000 · Reported 2021-07-08 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

An airbag that isn't properly secured may not deploy correctly in a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Chevrolet Bolt EV and Bolt EUV vehicles, one of the two bolts that hold the driver's front airbag to the steering wheel may be missing, or it may not have been tightened enough.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020.
3. Dealers will inspect and replace the bolts, or the airbag if needed, free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2022 Chevrolet Bolt EUV
  • 2022 Chevrolet Bolt EV

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
General Motors, LLC. (GM) is recalling certain 2022 Chevrolet Bolt EV and Bolt EUV vehicles. One of the two fasteners that secure the driver's frontal air bag to the steering wheel may be missing or improperly tightened.
Safety risk
An improperly installed air bag may not deploy properly in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace as necessary, the driver-side air bag fasteners. If the fasteners cannot be tightened properly, the air bag assembly will be replaced. All repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on July 29, 2021. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N212340860.
