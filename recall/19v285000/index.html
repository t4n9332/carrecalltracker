Chevrolet G4500 ambulance brake lights turn on by themselves

NHTSA campaign 19V285000 · Reported 2019-04-10 · North Central Emergency Vehicles · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Chevrolet G4500

What's wrong, in plain English

These ambulances were built on Ford and Chevrolet van chassis and fitted with Whelen brake lights. Depending on how the vehicle was wired, the brake lights can come on when nobody is pressing the brake pedal. Drivers behind may think the ambulance is slowing or stopping when it is not, and they cannot tell a real stop from a false one. The company will have dealers replace the brake lights at no charge.

How serious is it?

This is a nuisance-level fault rather than a dangerous one and the vehicle remains safe to drive, but it can confuse traffic behind you, so schedule the fix.

What you should do

1. Check the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm the vehicle is covered. 2. Contact North Central Emergency Vehicles at 1-320-395-2529 to arrange the work. 3. The dealer replaces the brake lights free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
North Central Emergency Vehicles (North Central) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Ambulances remounted on Ford E-350 and E-450 and Chevrolet G4500 chassis equipped with Whelen brake lights. Depending on the specific wiring of the vehicle, these brake lights may illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed.
Safety risk
If the brake lights incorrectly illuminate, it can confuse other drivers, possibly increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
North Central will notify owners and dealers will replace the brake lights, free of charge. The recall began May 20, 2019. Owners may contact North Central Emergency Vehicles customer service at 1-320-395-2529.
