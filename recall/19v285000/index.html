Chevrolet G4500 ambulance brake lights may come on wrongly

NHTSA campaign 19V285000 · Reported Apr 10, 2019 · North Central Emergency Vehicles

medium How serious is it? About this label

Brake lights that light up when the vehicle is not braking can confuse other drivers, which the notice says may increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

North Central Emergency Vehicles is recalling certain 2016 through 2018 ambulances remounted on Ford E-350, Ford E-450 and Chevrolet G4500 chassis that have Whelen brake lights. Depending on how a particular vehicle is wired, the brake lights can come on even when the driver has not pressed the brake pedal. Dealers will replace the brake lights at no cost to the owner.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact your dealer to have the brake lights replaced, or call North Central Emergency Vehicles customer service at 1-320-395-2529.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V285000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
North Central Emergency Vehicles (North Central) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Ambulances remounted on Ford E-350 and E-450 and Chevrolet G4500 chassis equipped with Whelen brake lights. Depending on the specific wiring of the vehicle, these brake lights may illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed.
Safety risk
If the brake lights incorrectly illuminate, it can confuse other drivers, possibly increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
North Central will notify owners and dealers will replace the brake lights, free of charge. The recall began May 20, 2019. Owners may contact North Central Emergency Vehicles customer service at 1-320-395-2529.
