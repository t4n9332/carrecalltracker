Hyundai Santa Fe backup camera view can be blocked

NHTSA campaign 24V246000 · Reported 2024-04-02 · Hyundai Motor America · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Hyundai Santa FE

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2024 Santa Fe vehicles, a message about trailer parking assist can pop up over the backup camera picture and block part of your view of what's behind you. Because the camera image gets covered, the vehicle doesn't meet the federal rear-visibility rule. Hyundai is fixing this with a software update sent to the vehicle over the air, so most owners won't need to visit a dealer at all. There's no charge either way.

How serious is it?

A blocked camera view makes it harder to see behind you and increases the risk of backing into someone or something, but the notice doesn't tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Santa Fe is covered. 2. Accept the free over-the-air software update when your vehicle offers it. 3. If you have questions or don't receive the update, call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 (recall 258).

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2024 Santa Fe vehicles. The rearview camera image may be obstructed by a trailer parking assist message. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
An obstructed rearview camera image reduces rearward visibility and increases the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Hyundai will release an over-the-air (OTA) software update. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning May 31, 2024. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 258. (over-the-air software update)
