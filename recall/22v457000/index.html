Nissan Frontier and Titan may roll away after parking

NHTSA campaign 22V457000 · Reported Jun 24, 2022 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A vehicle that rolls away increases the risk of a crash. Nissan tells owners to apply the parking brake every time they park. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Nissan is recalling certain Frontier and Titan vehicles. The part inside the automatic transmission that holds the vehicle in place when you shift into park may not lock in properly. If it does not engage, the vehicle can roll away on its own. Nissan tells owners to apply the parking brake every time they park. A dealer will replace the pin for that transmission part, and there is no charge for the work.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Apply the parking brake every time you park, as Nissan instructs.
3. Call a Nissan dealer or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 to book the repair, which is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V457000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020-2022 Frontier and Titan vehicles. The transmission parking pawl may not engage when the vehicle is shifted into park, which can result in a vehicle rollaway.
Safety risk
A vehicle rollaway increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised to apply the parking brake every time they park their vehicle. Dealers will replace the transmission parking pawl pin, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 2022. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is R22A2 R22A3.
