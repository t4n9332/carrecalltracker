Volvo Trucks Park Brakes May Apply Unexpectedly

NHTSA campaign 20V782000 · Reported Dec 15, 2020 · Volvo Trucks North America

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2021 Volvo VNL, VNR, VAH, and VHD trucks, small internal jaws called collets, used with a brass fitting on the truck's pneumatic pass-through plate, may have been damaged during leak testing. Because of this damage, the brass fitting can come apart from the pass-through plate while the air brake system is under pressure. If this happens in the park brake air line, the pressure that keeps the spring-loaded park brakes released can be lost, causing the park brakes to apply on their own.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is included.
2. Contact a Volvo Trucks dealer, or call Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586.
3. The inspection and any needed repair are free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V782000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling 2021 VNL, VNR, VAH, and VHD vehicles. The collets (internal jaws) for the brass fitting inserts used in the pneumatic pass-through plate may have been damaged during leak testing. As a result, the brass fitting may completely release the air brake line from the pass-through plate while under pressure. If this occurs on the park brake circuit, signal pressure that holds off the mechanical spring in the park brake chambers will be lost allowing the park brakes to apply.
Safety risk
If the park brakes apply unexpectedly, the driver may lose control of the vehicle, increasing the risk of a vehicle crash.
Remedy
Volvo Trucks will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the air brake line for proper engagement of the fitting in the pass-through plate, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin January 31, 2021. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2006.
