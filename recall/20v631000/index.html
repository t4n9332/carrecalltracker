Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Vans: Brake Hoses May Leak Fluid

NHTSA campaign 20V631000 · Reported 2020-10-14 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 3500
  • 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500

What's wrong, in plain English

The rubber brake hoses that carry brake fluid to the front and rear wheels may have been damaged on the outside while the van was being built. Over time a damaged hose can start to leak. Losing brake fluid weakens your brakes and can make the van pull to one side or feel unstable when you stop, which could cause a crash. A dealer will look at all the hoses and replace any that are damaged.

How serious is it?

Anything affecting the brakes is serious, and while the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, you should get the inspection done promptly and have the van checked right away if the brake pedal feels soft or the brake warning light comes on.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Sprinter is affected.
2. Call your dealer or DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267 (recall number VS3MONTLAU) to book the inspection — the recall began November 24, 2020.
3. Have the front and rear brake hoses inspected and replaced as needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vehicles. The outer area of the brake hoses on the front and rear axles may have been damaged during production.
Safety risk
Over time, the hose could begin to leak brake fluid, affecting the braking performance of the vehicle, including the potential loss of directional stability when braking, thereby increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and dealers will check the condition of the brake hoses on the front and rear axles and replace them as needed, free of charge. The recall began November 24, 2020. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3MONTLAU.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
