Porsche Taycan and 911 suspension bolts may loosen

NHTSA campaign 21V157000 · Reported 2021-03-10 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Porsche Taycan

What's wrong, in plain English

Some nuts and bolts holding suspension parts together may not have been tightened properly when the car was built, so they can work loose over time. The suspension is what keeps the wheels attached and pointed the right way. If a loose part comes off, the car can suddenly become unstable and hard to control. Dealers will replace the affected nuts and bolts, check nearby parts for damage, and replace anything damaged, free.

How serious is it?

This is a serious one because a part coming loose can cause a sudden loss of control while you are driving; Porsche does not tell owners to stop driving, but this should be handled right away.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car falls under recall AMA3. 2. Schedule the inspection and repair with a Porsche dealer as soon as possible, or call 1-800-767-7243. 3. All parts and labor are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2021 Porsche Taycan Turbo, Taycan Turbo S, Taycan, Taycan 4S, 911 Turbo S Coupe, Cayman, Cayman T, Cayman S, Cayman GT4, 911 Carrera Coupe, 911 Carrera S Coupe, 911 Carrera 4S Coupe, 911 Carrera 4S Targa, 911 Carrera Cabriolet, 911 Carrera S Cabriolet, 911 Carrera 4S Targa Heritage Design Edition, 911 Carrera 4S Cabriolet, 911 Turbo Coupe, 911 Turbo Cabriolet, 911 Turbo S Cabriolet, 718 Spyder, and Boxster vehicles. Certain suspension components may not have been tightened correctly and can loosen.
Safety risk
Loose suspension components may detach, causing a sudden loss of vehicle stability and control, and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the affected suspension nuts and bolts. Dealers will also inspect surrounding components for damage, and replace any damaged parts as necessary. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 7, 2021. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AMA3.
