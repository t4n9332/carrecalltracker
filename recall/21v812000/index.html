Volkswagen Atlas and Passat front wheel bolts may be loose

NHTSA campaign 21V812000 · Reported 2021-10-15 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Volkswagen Atlas
  • 2021 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport
  • 2021 Volkswagen Passat

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts that hold the front wheel bearings may not have been tightened correctly when these vehicles were built. If those bolts work loose, the front wheel can move out of position and you could lose control of the vehicle while driving. A dealer will check how tight the bolts are on the front wheels and install new bolts wherever they find a problem. The inspection and any parts needed are done at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Serious, since loose bolts can lead to a loss of control and a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected. 2. Call your Volkswagen dealer, or VW customer service at 1-800-893-5298, and ask about recall 40R7. 3. Have the wheel bearing bolts inspected and replaced as needed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
WHEELS:LUGS/NUTS/BOLTS/STUDS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2021 Atlas, Atlas Cross Sport, and Passat vehicles. The front wheel bearing bolts may have been incorrectly tightened.
Safety risk
The bolts may loosen and cause a loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the tightness of the wheel bearing bolts and replace the bolts, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 2, 2021. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 40R7.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
