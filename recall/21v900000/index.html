Honda Vehicles: Center Seat Belt May Not Lock Child Seats

NHTSA campaign 21V900000 · Reported 2021-11-18 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious if you use that middle seat for a child seat; the vehicle is safe to drive, but move the child seat to another seating position until the belt is replaced.

What's wrong, in plain English

The middle seat belt in the second row has a feature that locks the belt tight so a child's car seat stays put. On these vehicles that locking feature can switch off when it should not, leaving a child seat loose instead of held firmly in place. A loose child seat can move in a crash and injure the child. A dealer will replace the whole second-row center seat belt assembly at no cost.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Call your Honda dealer or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 to book the repair.
3. Have the second-row center seat belt assembly replaced — it is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER:RETRACTOR
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2021 Accord Sedan, Accord Hybrid, CR-V, Ridgeline, 2022 Insight and CR-V Hybrid vehicles. The automatic locking retractor on the second-row center seat belt assembly may deactivate improperly, which can result in an unsecured child restraint system. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An unsecured child restraint system can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the second-row center seat belt assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 14, 2022. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
