Toyota and Lexus vehicles carry wrong weight capacity label

NHTSA campaign 26V179000 · Reported 2026-03-24 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

low How serious is it? About this label

A vehicle with an incorrect maximum capacity weight value may be overloaded, which can increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On many recent Toyota and Lexus models, the sticker that tells you how much added weight the vehicle can carry shows incorrect numbers. That label is what owners use to judge how many passengers plus how much luggage, gear or cargo is safe to load. Because the figures are wrong, you could load the vehicle heavier than it is built to handle without realizing it. The dealer replaces the label free.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact your Toyota or Lexus dealer, or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331, and ask for the free replacement label.
3. Until the new label is on, avoid loading the vehicle heavily with passengers and cargo at the same time.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Toyota Tundra, Tacoma Hybrid, Tacoma, RAV4 Hybrid, Land Cruiser Hybrid, Lexus GX550, 2024-2026 Tundra Hybrid, 2025 Sequoia Hybrid, Crown Signia, Grand Highlander, Lexus TX500 Hybrid, Lexus NX350 Hybrid, 2025-2026 Grand Highlander Hybrid, and Lexus TX350 vehicles. The load carrying capacity modification label may display inaccurate added weight values. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
A vehicle with an incorrect maximum capacity weight value may be overloaded, which can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the load carrying capacity modification label, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed May 26, 2026. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 26LB04, 26LA04, 26TB07, and 26TA07.
