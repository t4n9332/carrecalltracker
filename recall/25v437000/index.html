Nissan, Infiniti Engine Bearings May Fail

NHTSA campaign 25V437000 · Reported Jun 27, 2025 · Nissan North America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

Engine failure increases the risk of a crash, and hot oil leaking from the engine increases the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain engines in these vehicles use bearings that may have a manufacturing defect. Over time, this defect can lead to the engine failing. In some cases, a failed bearing can cause a break in the engine block, which allows hot oil inside the engine to leak out while the vehicle is being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact a dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 800-647-7261 or Infiniti customer service at 1-800-662-6200. 3. The engine control module software will be reprogrammed, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V437000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:HARD PARTS INTERNAL/MECHANICAL
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2021-2024 Rogue, 2019-2020 Altima, 2019-2022 Infiniti QX50, and 2022 Infiniti QX55 vehicles equipped with 3-cylinder 1.5L or 4-cylinder 2.0L variable compression turbo (VC-Turbo) engines. The engine bearings may have manufacturing defects that can lead to engine failure.
Safety risk
Engine failure increases the risk of a crash. A bearing failure may cause a breach in the engine block, allowing hot oil to be discharged, increasing the risk of an engine fire.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the engine control module (ECM) software, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed April 8, 2026. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 800-647-7261 or Infiniti's customer service at 1-800-662-6200. Nissan's numbers for this recall are R25A8/A9 and R25B1/B2.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
