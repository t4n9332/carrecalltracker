Cadillac LYRIQ owner's manual misdescribes rear head restraints

NHTSA campaign 25V193000 · Reported 2025-03-27 · General Motors, LLC · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Cadillac Lyriq

What's wrong, in plain English

GM found that the owner's manual for some 2025 Cadillac LYRIQ vehicles describes the rear outside head restraints — the padded headrests above the back seats — incorrectly. Following the wrong pictures and instructions, someone could set those headrests to the wrong position. A headrest that is not adjusted properly does not support a passenger's head the way it should, so someone could be injured more seriously in a crash.

How serious is it?

Nothing on the vehicle is broken and it is safe to keep driving; the concern is only that bad manual information could lead to poorly adjusted rear headrests.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your LYRIQ is covered.
2. Watch your mail for the free corrected owner's manual insert, or ask a Cadillac dealer for it, referencing recall N252492340.
3. Use the corrected instructions to adjust the rear head restraints. Cadillac customer service: 1-800-458-8006.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:MID/REAR ASSEMBLY:HEAD RESTRAINT
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2025 Cadillac LYRIQ vehicles. The owner's manual inaccurately describes the rear outboard head restraints. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 202, "Head Restraints."
Safety risk
Incorrect head restraint information may lead to improperly adjusted head restraints, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
GM will mail owner's manual inserts with the correct illustrations and descriptions for rear outboard head restraints, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 30, 2025. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N252492340.
