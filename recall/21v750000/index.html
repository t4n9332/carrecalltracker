NHTSA campaign 21V750000 · Reported Sep 27, 2021 · Mazda North American Operations

What's wrong, in plain English

Mazda is recalling certain CX-9 vehicles that have the 60/40 split bench second-row seats. The left-side second-row seat may unexpectedly slide forward during a crash. Federal safety rules cover how seat belt anchorages must hold, and these vehicles do not meet that standard. A dealer will replace the internal parts inside the affected second-row seats at no cost to the owner.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Mazda dealer, or Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500, Option 4, to schedule the seat repair.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V750000. Official notice

Component
SEATS:MID/REAR ASSEMBLY
Summary
Mazda North American Operations (Mazda) is recalling certain 2020-2021 CX-9 vehicles equipped with 60/40 split bench second-row seats. The left-side second-row seat may unexpectedly slide forward during a crash. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 210, "Seat Belt Assembly Anchorages."
Safety risk
A seat that slides forward during a crash can transfer excessive force onto a belted occupant, increasing their risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the internal parts of the affected second-row seats, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 4, 2021. Owners may contact Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500, Option 4. Mazda's number for this recall is 5221I.
