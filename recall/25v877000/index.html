What's wrong, in plain English

These minivans were converted by Vantage Mobility so a wheelchair can ride inside. The wheelchair is held down by belt-style restraints with retractors that are supposed to lock, much like a seat belt locks. On these vans, the retractors may fail to lock, so the wheelchair is not really tied down. If it is not secured, the chair and the person in it can roll or tip while the van is moving, braking, or turning.

How serious is it?

Serious: an unsecured wheelchair can move during the ride and injure the passenger, so have the restraints checked before carrying someone in a wheelchair.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is part of this recall.
2. Contact your mobility dealer or Vantage Mobility customer service at 1-800-488-9082 to arrange an inspection.
3. Have the retractors inspected and replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT ADAPTIVE/MOBILITY:WHEELCHAIR RESTRAINTS/SECUREMENT:LATCH/ANCHOR:
Summary
Vantage Mobility, LLC (VM) is recalling certain 2021-2026 Honda Odyssey, 2021-2025 Chrysler Pacifica, and Toyota Sienna vehicles equipped with QRT-Deluxe and QRT-Max wheelchair restraints. The retractors may not lock, preventing the wheelchair from being properly secured.
Safety risk
An unsecured wheelchair can move during transit, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the retractors, free of charge. Owner notification letters was mailed January 27, 2026. Owners may contact Vantage Mobility customer service at 1-800-488-9082.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
