Honda, Chrysler and Toyota Vans Wheelchair Restraint Issue

NHTSA campaign 25V877000 · Reported 2025-12-17 · Vantage Mobility

medium How serious is it? About this label

An unsecured wheelchair could move while the vehicle is being driven, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some vehicles equipped with certain wheelchair restraint systems have retractors that may not lock properly. This means a wheelchair might not be securely held in place while the vehicle is being driven. Dealers will inspect the retractors and replace them if needed, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Vantage Mobility customer service at 1-800-488-9082 to schedule an inspection.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT ADAPTIVE/MOBILITY:WHEELCHAIR RESTRAINTS/SECUREMENT:LATCH/ANCHOR:
Summary
Vantage Mobility, LLC (VM) is recalling certain 2021-2026 Honda Odyssey, 2021-2025 Chrysler Pacifica, and Toyota Sienna vehicles equipped with QRT-Deluxe and QRT-Max wheelchair restraints. The retractors may not lock, preventing the wheelchair from being properly secured.
Safety risk
An unsecured wheelchair can move during transit, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the retractors, free of charge. Owner notification letters was mailed January 27, 2026. Owners may contact Vantage Mobility customer service at 1-800-488-9082.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
