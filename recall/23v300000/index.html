What's wrong, in plain English

Some all-wheel drive Genesis GV60 vehicles may have rear driveshafts that can fracture. If a driveshaft fractures, the vehicle can lose drive power.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a dealer, or call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460, to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V300000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:DRIVELINE:DRIVESHAFT
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2023 Genesis GV60 vehicles equipped with an all-wheel drive powertrain system.  The rear driveshafts may fracture, which can result in a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the left and right rear driveshafts, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed June 23, 2023.  Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460.  Hyundai's number for this recall is 014G.
