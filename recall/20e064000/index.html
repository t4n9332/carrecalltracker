NHTSA campaign 20E064000 · Reported Sep 23, 2020 · Tenneco Automotive

What's wrong, in plain English

Tenneco is recalling aftermarket Beck/Arnley brake master cylinders that were sold as replacement parts for certain Volkswagen Jetta and Beetle vehicles. The crimp that holds the fluid seal in place on the master cylinder may not be tight enough. Because of this, brake fluid can leak out, or the master cylinder itself can come apart. A leak like this can reduce how well the brakes work.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if this affects your vehicle.
Contact Tenneco customer service at 1-800-625-9319, or the distributor/shop that sold the part.
The master cylinder will be exchanged for a new one, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20E064000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:MASTER CYLINDER
Summary
Tenneco Automotive (Tenneco) is recalling certain Beck/Arnley Master Cylinders, part number 072-9831, sold as aftermarket or replacement parts for 2004-2018 Volkswagen Jetta, and 2012-2019 Volkswagen Beetle vehicles. The fluid seal crimp on the master cylinder may be insufficient, causing brake fluid to leak or the cylinder to come apart.
Safety risk
If there is a brake fluid leak, it can reduce braking ability, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Beck/Arnley will notify the distributors that sold the master cylinders and will work with the distributors to notify the end users. Affected master cylinders will be exchanged for a new master cylinder (part number 072-9990), free of charge. The recall began October 30, 2020. Owners may contact Tenneco customer service at 1-800-625-9319.
