Tesla Model S Side Mirrors Missing Warning Etching

NHTSA campaign 22E092000 · Reported 2022-11-01 · Tesla, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Without the correct mirrors, a driver may misjudge how close another vehicle is, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The outside rearview mirrors on your Model S don't meet federal visibility standards. The mirror on the driver's side is curved, or convex, glass instead of flat glass. The mirror on the passenger's side is missing the etched warning that reads 'Objects in Mirror Are Closer Than They Appear.' Because these mirrors are the wrong type, the vehicle doesn't meet the federal rear visibility safety standard.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Contact Tesla Service to schedule the mirror replacement, or call Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. 3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:REARVIEW MIRRORS/DEVICES:EXTERIOR
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2021 Model S exterior side rearview mirrors, part numbers: 1622232-00-B and 1622233-00-B. The driver side exterior rearview mirrors are convex (curved glass) and the passenger side mirrors do not include the "Objects in Mirror Are Closer Than They Appear" etched warning. As such, these mirrors fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
When using the incorrect mirrors, the driver may misjudge the distance of other vehicles, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla Service will replace the mirrors, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 31, 2022. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-22-12-002.
