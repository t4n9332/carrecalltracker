Toyota Yaris fuel pump can fail and stall engine

NHTSA campaign 21V617000 · Reported 2021-09-27 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Toyota Yaris
  • 2020 Toyota Yaris

What's wrong, in plain English

A small spinning part inside the low-pressure fuel pump in these Yaris models can crack and change shape. If that happens, the pump may stop delivering fuel to the engine properly and fail. When the pump fails, the engine can stall — it simply quits running, which may happen while you are driving. Dealers will replace the entire low-pressure fuel pump assembly at no cost to the owner.

How serious is it?

An engine that stalls unexpectedly, especially in traffic or at speed, increases the risk of a crash, so schedule the repair soon; the notice does not instruct owners to stop driving the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Yaris is covered. 2. Call your Toyota dealer or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 (recall 21TB05 or 21TA05) to book the free fuel pump replacement. 3. If the engine ever sputters, runs roughly, or stalls, get the car off the road safely and have it towed to the dealer.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Yaris, Yaris Hatchback, and Yaris Sedan vehicles. An impeller within the low-pressure fuel pump may crack and deform, potentially causing the fuel pump to fail.
Safety risk
A fuel pump failure may cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the low-pressure fuel pump assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 30, 2021. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 21TB05 and 21TA05.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
