Infiniti Q60 rear seat belts may not lock

NHTSA campaign 20V145000 · Reported 2020-03-13 · Nissan North America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Infiniti Q60

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2020 Infiniti Q60 cars have rear seat belts with a dual-mode locking mechanism. The sensor that is supposed to lock the belt webbing may not lock the way it should. If that happens, the rear belt may not hold a passenger firmly in place during a crash. Dealers will check the production dates on the rear seat belt assemblies and replace them if needed, at no charge. The recall began May 4, 2020.

How serious is it?

This is a real injury risk in a crash with more than one impact, since the belt may not restrain the person properly, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Q60 is covered. 2. Contact your Infiniti dealer, or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669, to book the rear seat belt inspection. 3. Let the dealer replace the belts if needed — inspection and replacement are free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020 Infiniti Q60 vehicles equipped with rear seat belt assemblies with a dual-mode locking mechanism. The seat belt webbing sensor locking mechanism may not lock as intended.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash involving multiple impacts, the seat belt may not properly restrain the occupant, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the rear seat belt assembly production dates, and replace the assemblies, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began May 4, 2020. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
