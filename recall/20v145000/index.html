Infiniti Q60 Rear Seat Belts May Not Lock

NHTSA campaign 20V145000 · Reported Mar 13, 2020 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

If the seat belt doesn't lock properly in a crash with multiple impacts, it may not properly restrain the occupant, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Infiniti Q60 vehicles have rear seat belts built with a locking feature meant to help keep the belt snug during a crash that involves more than one impact. In some vehicles, this locking feature may not engage the way it is supposed to, so the seat belt may not tighten and hold the passenger as intended.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included in this recall.
2. Contact a Nissan/Infiniti dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
3. The seat belt assembly inspection and replacement, if needed, is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V145000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020 Infiniti Q60 vehicles equipped with rear seat belt assemblies with a dual-mode locking mechanism. The seat belt webbing sensor locking mechanism may not lock as intended.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash involving multiple impacts, the seat belt may not properly restrain the occupant, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the rear seat belt assembly production dates, and replace the assemblies, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began May 4, 2020. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
