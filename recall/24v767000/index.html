Toyota Tundra and Sequoia tires may lose air suddenly

NHTSA campaign 24V767000 · Reported 2024-10-15 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Toyota Sequoia Hybrid
  • 2024 Toyota Tundra
  • 2024 Toyota Tundra Hybrid

What's wrong, in plain English

The front and rear tires on the passenger side may have damage on the inner sidewall, which is the part of the tire facing under the vehicle where it is hard to see. Damage in that spot can let a tire lose its air all at once rather than going down slowly. A sudden flat while you are moving can pull the vehicle to one side and make it hard to control. A dealer will inspect the tires and replace any that are damaged.

How serious is it?

Very serious, because a tire can go flat suddenly and without warning and cause a loss of control; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get it inspected right away.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck or SUV is included. 2. Contact your Toyota dealer, or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331, and reference recall 24TA12. 3. Have the tires inspected and any damaged ones replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
TIRES:SIDEWALL
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2024 Sequoia Hybrid, Tundra and Tundra Hybrid vehicles. The passenger-side front and rear tires may have inner sidewall damage, which can cause a sudden loss of tire pressure.
Safety risk
A sudden loss of tire pressure can result in a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the tires as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 5, 2024. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 24TA12.
