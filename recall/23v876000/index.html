Mercedes-Benz Sprinter partition wall may be missing bolts

NHTSA campaign 23V876000 · Reported 2023-12-21 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

This matters mainly in a crash rather than in everyday driving, and the notice does not tell owners to stop using the van, so it is reasonable to keep driving while you arrange the free inspection soon.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2019-2023 Mercedes-Benz and 2019-2021 Freightliner Sprinter vans, bolts are missing from the partition wall — the divider between the front seats and the cargo area behind them. Without all of its bolts, the wall is not held firmly in place and could break loose in a crash and injure people up front. A dealer will look at the partition and install whatever bolts are missing, at no charge to the owner.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall VS3TREWAN applies to your van.
2. Call your Sprinter dealer or Daimler Vans USA at 1-877-762-8267 and set up the inspection.
3. Have the partition wall checked and bolted down — the work costs you nothing.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2019-2023 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and 2019-2021 Freightliner Sprinter vehicles. The partition wall may be missing bolts, allowing the wall to detach during a crash.
Safety risk
A detached partition wall can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and secure the partition wall as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed February 16, 2024. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3TREWAN.
