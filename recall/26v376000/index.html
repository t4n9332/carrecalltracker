Ford Focus/Fusion Clutch Failure May Cause Fire

NHTSA campaign 26V376000 · Reported 2026-06-09 · Ford Motor Company

critical How serious is it? About this label

A transmission fluid leak near hot engine or exhaust parts can start a fire, which is a serious safety risk. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Focus and Fusion vehicles because two earlier repairs for this problem did not fully fix it. The clutch inside the transmission may crack or break, which can damage the transmission and cause a fluid leak. If that leaked fluid reaches a hot part of the engine or exhaust, it could ignite. Ford has sent owners an interim letter about this fire risk while it finishes developing the full repair for this problem.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. The repair, a software update and (for Focus vehicles) a clutch inspection and replacement if needed, is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:MANUAL TRANSMISSION
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2015-2018 Focus and 2013-2016 Fusion vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall numbers 18V169 and 18V845. The clutch may fracture, resulting in damage to the transmission assembly and possibly a transmission fluid leak.
Safety risk
A transmission fluid leak in the presence of an ignition source such as hot engine or exhaust components can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will update the software, free of charge. Additionally, dealers will inspect Focus vehicles and replace the clutch assembly as necessary, free of charge. Interim letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, were mailed August 5, 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the remedy is available. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S43. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov on July 6,2026.
