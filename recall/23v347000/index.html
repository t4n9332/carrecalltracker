Porsche Taycan shows wrong worn brake pad warning

NHTSA campaign 23V347000 · Reported 2023-05-16 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Porsche Taycan
  • 2021 Porsche Taycan
  • 2022 Porsche Taycan

What's wrong, in plain English

When the brake pads on these Taycans wear down, the car does put a warning on the instrument cluster, but it uses the wrong symbol in the wrong color. A driver can easily misread it and keep driving on worn-out pads, and worn pads mean the car does not stop as well as it should. Porsche dealers will update the instrument cluster software so the brake pad warning shows up correctly and is easy to understand.

How serious is it?

Moderate — a misread warning can leave you driving on worn brake pads with reduced stopping power, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is affected.
2. Call a Porsche dealer, or Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243, and mention recall APA5.
3. Get the free software update, and have your brake pads checked while the car is in.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2020-2022 Taycan vehicles. An incorrect warning indicator with an incorrect color is displayed in the instrument cluster when notifying owners of worn brake pads. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 135, "Light Vehicle Brake Systems," and 101, "Control and Displays."
Safety risk
An incorrect warning indicator may be misinterpreted and result in driving on worn brake pads with reduced brake performance, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the software for the instrument cluster, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed July 14, 2023. Owners may contact Porsche's customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is APA5.
