Mercedes-Benz emergency call system may report wrong crash location

NHTSA campaign 21V058000 · Reported 2021-02-05 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz AMG GLA45
  • 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG C43
  • 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG CLA35
  • 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG CLA45
  • 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG E53
  • 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG GLB35
  • 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG GLC43
  • 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG GLC63

What's wrong, in plain English

These Mercedes-Benz vehicles have a built-in system that automatically calls for help after a crash. Because of a software flaw in the car's communication unit, that call may send the wrong location. Emergency crews could then be sent to the wrong place, delaying help for you or your passengers. The car itself drives normally; only the emergency call feature is affected. The fix is updated software, installed by a dealer or sent to your car over the air.

How serious is it?

The car still drives and brakes normally, so it is safe to keep driving, but help could be delayed after a crash, which raises the risk of injury.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your vehicle is included. 2. Contact Mercedes-Benz customer service at 1-877-762-8267 or your dealer about recall 2021020025. 3. Accept the over-the-air update or have the dealer install it free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2016 - 2021 CLA-Class, GLA-Class, GLE-Class, GLS-Class, SLC-Class, A-Class, GT-Class, C-Class, E-Class, S-Class, CLS-Class, SL-Class, B-Class, GLB-Class, GLC-Class, and G-Class vehicles. Please refer to the Part 573 report for specific vehicle model details. The software design of the communication module may fail to communicate the correct vehicle location for the emergency call system (eCall) in the event of a crash.
Safety risk
Failure of the eCall system may result in emergency responders being dispatched to the wrong location, increasing the risk of injury following a crash.
Remedy
MBUSA will notify owners, and the communication module software will be updated by a dealer, or through an over-the-air (OTA) update, free of charge. The recall began March 12, 2021. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2021020025. (over-the-air software update)
