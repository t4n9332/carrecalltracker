Mercedes-Benz emergency call system may send wrong location

NHTSA campaign 21V058000 · Reported 2021-02-05 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the emergency call system fails this way, rescuers may be sent to the wrong place after a crash, which increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Mercedes-Benz is recalling a wide range of cars and SUVs, including C-Class, E-Class, GLA-Class, GLC-Class and AMG models. The way the software in the car's communication module was designed means the automatic emergency call system may not report the correct vehicle location if the car is in a crash. A dealer will update that software, or the update can be delivered over the air, at no cost to the owner.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is covered. 2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer, or call MBUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267 (recall number 2021020025). 3. The software update is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2016 - 2021 CLA-Class, GLA-Class, GLE-Class, GLS-Class, SLC-Class, A-Class, GT-Class, C-Class, E-Class, S-Class, CLS-Class, SL-Class, B-Class, GLB-Class, GLC-Class, and G-Class vehicles. Please refer to the Part 573 report for specific vehicle model details. The software design of the communication module may fail to communicate the correct vehicle location for the emergency call system (eCall) in the event of a crash.
Safety risk
Failure of the eCall system may result in emergency responders being dispatched to the wrong location, increasing the risk of injury following a crash.
Remedy
MBUSA will notify owners, and the communication module software will be updated by a dealer, or through an over-the-air (OTA) update, free of charge. The recall began March 12, 2021. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2021020025. (over-the-air software update)
