Ram 1500 brake pedal can separate from its assembly

NHTSA campaign 19V067000 · Reported 2019-02-05 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

critical How serious is it? About this label

Losing the brakes is about as serious as a defect gets; the notice does not order owners to park the truck, but treat this as urgent and get it repaired before further driving if you can.

What's wrong, in plain English

This affects 2019 Ram 1500 trucks with adjustable pedals, the kind you can slide closer to you or farther away. On these trucks the brake pedal can move too far and come apart from the assembly it attaches to. If the pedal separates, pushing on it does nothing, and you would have no normal way to slow down or stop the truck. Dealers will repair the adjustable pedal assembly so the pedal stays put.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is part of recall 19V067000 (Chrysler's number V07).
2. Call a Ram dealer or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 and tell them it is the brake pedal recall so they schedule you quickly.
3. Let the dealer repair the adjustable pedal assembly at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:PEDALS AND LINKAGES
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Ram 1500 vehicles equipped with adjustable pedals. The affected vehicles have a brake pedal that may move too far and separate from pedal assembly.
Safety risk
If the brake pedal separates from the pedal assembly, the driver may be unable to brake the vehicle, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will repair the adjustable pedal assembly, free of charge. The recall began on February 25, 2019. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V07.
