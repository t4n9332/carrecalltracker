Hyundai Elantra front seat backs may be poorly welded

NHTSA campaign 21V302000 · Reported 2021-04-28 · Hyundai Motor America · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Hyundai Elantra

What's wrong, in plain English

The part that lets your front seat back tilt and lock in place was not welded correctly on some 2021 Elantras. Both the driver's seat and the front passenger seat can be affected. In a crash, a bad weld could let the seat back pull away from its frame, so the seat would not hold the person in it the way it is supposed to. Dealers will inspect the seats and replace the seat back frame if there is a problem.

How serious is it?

Serious in a crash, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving — keep driving normally but get the inspection done soon.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Elantra is affected.
2. Call your Hyundai dealer or Hyundai customer service at 1-888-371-9460 (recall 204) for an appointment.
3. Have the seats inspected and the frame replaced if needed — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:FRONT ASSEMBLY:RECLINER
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2021 Elantra vehicles. The driver and front passenger seat back recliners may have been improperly welded.
Safety risk
An improperly welded seat back recliner could separate from the seat back frame during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and if necessary, replace the seat back frame assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 21, 2021. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-888-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 204.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
