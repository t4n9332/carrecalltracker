Ford Bronco 5-Door: hard-to-reach front seat belt latches

NHTSA campaign 23V358000 · Reported 2023-05-18 · Ford Motor Company · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford Bronco
  • 2022 Ford Bronco
  • 2023 Ford Bronco

What's wrong, in plain English

On five-door Bronco models, the metal tab you click into the front seat belt buckle can be hard to reach when it is tucked into its stowed position. Because of that, these vehicles do not meet the federal crash-protection rule for seat belts. Ford's fix is a small sliding clip added to the driver and front passenger belts that stops the tab from sliding out of easy reach, so you can grab the belt normally.

How serious is it?

The belts themselves still work and the car is safe to drive, but anyone who skips buckling up because the latch is awkward to grab faces a higher risk of injury in a crash.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Bronco is included.
2. Call your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 23C16), and book the repair — parts have been available since October 2023.
3. Have the dealer install the sliding clip latch stops; the work is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Bronco vehicles built with the 5-Door body style. The first-row seat belt latch plates may be difficult to access from their stowed position. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
Difficulty accessing the seat belt latches may result in occupants not wearing their seat belts, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will add a sliding clip latch stop to the driver and front passenger seat belts, free of charge. Interim owner notification letters notifying owners of the safety risk were mailed July 14, 2023. Owner letters informing owners that parts are available were mailed on October 2, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23C16.
