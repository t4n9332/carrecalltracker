Cadillac and Camaro Transmission May Lock Wheels

NHTSA campaign 25V148000 · Reported 2025-03-06 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Wheel lock-up while driving increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The 10-speed transmission in these vehicles may become damaged internally, and that damage can cause the wheels to lock up while driving. A wheel locking up while driving increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will install new monitoring software in the transmission's computer to address the problem, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Cadillac dealer (1-800-333-4223) or Chevrolet dealer (1-800-222-1020) to schedule the software update.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Cadillac CT6, 2020-2021 CT4, CT5, and 2020-2022 Chevrolet Camaro vehicles equipped with a 10-speed transmission. The transmission may become damaged internally and cause the wheels to lock-up.
Safety risk
Wheel lock-up while driving increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install transmission control module monitoring software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 7, 2025. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N242480630.
