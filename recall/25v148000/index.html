Cadillac and Camaro transmission may lock the wheels

NHTSA campaign 25V148000 · Reported 2025-03-06 · General Motors, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Cadillac CT6
  • 2020 Cadillac CT4
  • 2020 Cadillac CT5
  • 2020 Cadillac CT6
  • 2020 Chevrolet Camaro
  • 2021 Cadillac CT4
  • 2021 Cadillac CT5
  • 2021 Chevrolet Camaro
  • 2022 Chevrolet Camaro

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Cadillac CT4, CT5, CT6 and Chevrolet Camaro models with the 10-speed automatic transmission can suffer internal damage inside the transmission. If that happens, the wheels can suddenly lock up, meaning they stop turning while you are driving and you lose control of the car. GM dealers will install new monitoring software in the transmission's computer so the problem can be caught, and the work costs you nothing.

How serious is it?

Wheels locking up while driving is serious and can happen without warning, though the notice does not instruct owners to stop driving the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is affected. 2. Contact your dealer, or Cadillac at 1-800-333-4223 or Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020, and mention recall N242480630. 3. Schedule the free software installation.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Cadillac CT6, 2020-2021 CT4, CT5, and 2020-2022 Chevrolet Camaro vehicles equipped with a 10-speed transmission. The transmission may become damaged internally and cause the wheels to lock-up.
Safety risk
Wheel lock-up while driving increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install transmission control module monitoring software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 7, 2025. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N242480630.
