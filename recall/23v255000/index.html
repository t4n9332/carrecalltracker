Volvo VNR Electric Adaptive Cruise Can Brake Hard

NHTSA campaign 23V255000 · Reported Apr 7, 2023 · Volvo Trucks North America

high How serious is it? About this label

Overly aggressive automatic braking can cause skidding or a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

These electric trucks have adaptive cruise control that can apply the brakes on its own. Under light loads, that automatic braking can be much more aggressive than it should be. Braking that hard and suddenly can cause the rear wheels to skid, the brakes to lock up, or the driver to lose control of the truck. Losing control while braking raises the chance of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is included.
2. Contact a Volvo Trucks dealer, or call 1-800-528-6586.
3. The repair (securing wiring, removing the radar equipment, and reprogramming the brake system to disable adaptive cruise control) is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V255000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT ADAPTIVE/MOBILITY:VEHICLE CONTROLS:BRAKING
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2020-2023 VNR Electric vehicles equipped with Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC). When the adaptive cruise control braking activates under light load conditions, it can trigger an aggressive braking response.
Safety risk
Overly aggressive braking can result in the rear axle skidding, the brakes locking up, and/or a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will secure wiring and remove radar antennas and brackets from the vehicle. The anti-lock braking system will be reprogrammed to disable adaptive cruise control. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed June 2, 2023. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 1-800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2303.
