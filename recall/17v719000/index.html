BMW Instrument Panel Display May Not Light Up

NHTSA campaign 17V719000 · Reported 2017-11-15 · BMW of North America, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the display or its warning and control symbols don't light up, drivers may not get information they need, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling certain 230i, 230i xDrive, M240i, M240i xDrive, M2, and X3 xDrive 30i vehicles. A software error in the instrument panel may prevent the display from lighting up properly. This can happen at startup or while driving, and can affect warning and control symbols shown on the display. Dealers will update the instrument cluster display software to fix the problem, free of charge to owners.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a BMW dealer to schedule the software update (1-800-525-7417).
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2018 230i, 230i xDrive, M240i, M240i xDrive, M2, and X3 xDrive 30i SAV vehicles. These vehicles may have an error in the software for the instrument panel, causing the display to not properly illuminate. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 101, "Control and Displays."
Safety risk
If the display does not illuminate during start up or does not illuminate various control and warning symbols while driving, it can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will update the instrument cluster display software, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin January 8, 2018. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
