Volkswagen Passat passenger airbag may have been folded incorrectly

NHTSA campaign 20V533000 · Reported 2020-09-02 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Volkswagen Passat

What's wrong, in plain English

The airbag in front of the passenger seat may have been folded the wrong way when it was built. A folded airbag has to unpack in a specific way so it inflates at the right speed. If it was folded incorrectly, it can come out with more force than it should. Someone sitting in that seat during a crash could be hurt by the airbag itself. Federal crash-protection rules cover how these airbags must perform.

How serious is it?

This is serious because the airbag meant to protect a passenger could injure them in a crash, but Volkswagen issued no park-it warning, so you can keep driving while you arrange the free replacement.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Passat is included.
2. Contact your Volkswagen dealer, or Volkswagen customer service at 1-888-241-2289 (recall 69AY), to book the repair.
3. The dealer replaces the passenger front airbag free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:PASSENGER SIDE FRONTAL:CUSHION
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2020 Passat vehicles. The passenger frontal air bag may have been folded incorrectly affecting its deployment force. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An incorrectly folded air bag can deploy with too much force, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will replace the passenger frontal air bag, free of charge. The recall began October 14, 2020. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-888-241-2289. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 69AY.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
