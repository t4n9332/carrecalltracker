Ford Explorer and Lincoln Aviator engine rails may be weak

NHTSA campaign 22V454000 · Reported 2022-06-24 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Ford Explorer
  • 2022 Lincoln Aviator

What's wrong, in plain English

The engine rails are heavy steel beams in the front of the vehicle that hold the engine and help absorb a front-end crash. On these vehicles the steel may not have been hardened correctly, so the rails can be weaker than they should be. That means less protection for people inside during a crash, and a rail could break loose and drag on the road while you are driving. Dealers test the rails and replace weak ones.

How serious is it?

Very serious: there is no park-it order, but a rail can come loose and hit the road while you are driving and the vehicle may protect you less in a crash, so get it checked right away.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included in recall 22C13.
2. Call your Ford or Lincoln dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and book the engine rail strength test as soon as possible.
3. If the rails need replacing, the repair is free — and Ford also offers you the choice of a replacement vehicle or a buyback.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:FRAME AND MEMBERS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022 Lincoln Aviator and Ford Explorer vehicles. The engine rails may have been improperly heat-treated. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 208, "Occupant Crash Protection" and 301, "Fuel System Integrity."
Safety risk
Improperly heat-treated engine rails may reduce the vehicle's structural protection in a crash, increasing the risk of injury. In addition, an engine rail may detach and contact the road while driving, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will perform an engine rail strength test and replace the rails as necessary, free of charge. If the engine rails require replacement, owners will have the option for a vehicle replacement or buyback. Owner notification letters were mailed August 22, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22C13.
