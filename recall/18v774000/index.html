Buick, Chevrolet Vehicles' Air Bags May Not Deploy

NHTSA campaign 18V774000 · Reported 2018-11-01 · General Motors LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

If the air bags do not deploy in the event of a crash, it can increase the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2019 Buick Encore, Chevrolet Spark, Traverse, and Trax vehicles, the computer module that controls the air bags may have been left switched to a factory 'manufacturing mode' instead of being switched over to normal driving mode before the vehicle was sold. While the module is in that mode, the air bags will not deploy if the vehicle is in a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Buick customer service at 1-800-521-7300 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 to schedule the reprogramming.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019 Buick Encore, Chevrolet Spark, Traverse, and Trax vehicles. The Sensing Diagnostic Module (SDM) on these vehicle may not have been turned off of "manufacturing mode," preventing the air bags from deploying in the event of a crash.
Safety risk
If the air bags do not deploy in the event of a crash, it can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will reprogram the SDM module to the correct mode, free of charge. The recall began November 6, 2018. Owners may contact Buick customer service at 1-800-521-7300 and Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is 18365.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
