Audi S8 Engine Control Module Moisture Risk

NHTSA campaign 21V111000 · Reported 2021-02-24 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

An engine that stalls while driving increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Audi vehicles are missing a small sealing pin in the connector for the engine's control computer. Without that pin, moisture can get into the connector, and moisture inside the connector can cause the control computer to malfunction. If the control computer malfunctions, the engine may stall while you are driving. Dealers will check the connector for the missing pin and for corrosion, and fix it if needed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact an Audi dealer to schedule the repair (Audi customer service: 1-800-253-2834).
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECU/ECM)
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2019-2021 Audi A8 and 2020-2021 Audi S8 vehicles. A missing sealing pin may allow moisture to enter the engine control module connector, possibly causing a control module malfunction.
Safety risk
An engine control module malfunction may cause the engine to stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the engine control module connector for a missing sealing pin, and install it, if necessary. The connector will also be inspected for corrosion, and replaced as necessary. Repairs will be performed free of charge. The recall began March 12, 20121. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 97EV.
