NHTSA campaign 19V495000 · Reported Jun 26, 2019 · Nissan North America, Inc.

What's wrong, in plain English

Nissan is recalling certain 2017-2019 gasoline, light duty, Titan vehicles because the alternator harness may have been damaged when the engine was installed. This damage could lead to an electrical short in the harness. An electrical short like this may cause the engine to stall while driving, which raises the risk of a crash, and it also increases the chance of a fire.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 or your dealer to schedule an inspection.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V495000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2017-2019 gasoline, light duty, Titan vehicles. The alternator harness may have been damaged during the engine installation process, possibly resulting in an electrical short.
Safety risk
An electrical short may cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash. In addition, it increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the alternator harness for the proper routing and any damage. The harness will be clipped into the correct position or replaced as necessary, free of charge. The recall began August 8, 2019. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
