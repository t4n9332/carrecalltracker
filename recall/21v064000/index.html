Chevrolet Tahoe and GMC Yukon fuel tanks may leak

NHTSA campaign 21V064000 · Reported 2021-02-11 · General Motors, LLC · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Chevrolet Tahoe
  • 2021 GMC Yukon

What's wrong, in plain English

The fuel tank on these vehicles is built from two layers bonded together, and on some trucks the glue between those layers was left out. Without it, the tank can leak gasoline. Leaking fuel near anything hot or sparking can catch fire. There is no fix you can do yourself: the dealer replaces the whole fuel tank at no cost to you. Watch for a strong gasoline smell or puddles under the vehicle.

How serious is it?

This is serious because leaking gasoline near an ignition source raises the risk of a fire, so get the tank replaced right away rather than putting the repair off.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Call your Chevrolet dealer at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC at 1-800-462-8782 and give them GM recall number N212327720. 3. Have the fuel tank replaced; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2021 Chevrolet Tahoe and GMC Yukon vehicles. The fuel tank assemblies may be missing adhesive between two layers of the fuel tank shell which may result in a fuel leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel tank, free of charge. The recall began March 6, 2021. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020, and GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N212327720.
