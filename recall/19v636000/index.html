Jeep Gladiator Rear Driveshaft May Lack Grease

NHTSA campaign 19V636000 · Reported Aug 29, 2019 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

If the driveshaft overheats and breaks, the truck can lose the power that drives the wheels, and a separated driveshaft left in the road can be a hazard to other drivers — both of which increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Gladiators, the rear driveshafts may have been built without grease in the monoblock joint that connects sections of the driveshaft. Without that grease, the joint can overheat and seize up, which may cause the driveshaft to crack or break. If the driveshaft fully separates from the truck, it can also become a hazard on the road for other drivers.

What you should do

1. Check the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is affected.
2. Contact a Jeep dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to schedule the repair.
3. The repair — replacing the driveshaft — is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V636000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:DRIVELINE:DRIVESHAFT
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2020 Jeep Gladiator vehicles. The rear driveshafts may have been incorrectly assembled without grease in the monoblock joint.
Safety risk
A joint without grease may overheat and seize, possibly causing the driveshaft to fracture, resulting in a sudden loss of drive power. If the driveshaft completely separates from the vehicle it may become a road hazard. Either of these scenarios can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
FCA US will notify owners, and dealers will replace the driveshaft, free of charge. The recall began October 10, 2019. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US number for this recall is V95.
