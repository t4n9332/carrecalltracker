Toyota and Lexus SUV curtain airbags may deploy wrong

NHTSA campaign 24V461000 · Reported 2024-06-20 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

An air bag that does not deploy correctly can reduce protection for occupants in a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Toyota Grand Highlander and Lexus TX vehicles have driver and passenger side curtain air bags that may not unroll the way they are supposed to. Instead of deploying properly to protect occupants, the air bag could partially deploy outside of an open window. This means that in a crash, the air bag may not be positioned correctly to help protect the people inside the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included in this recall.
2. Contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331, or your Toyota or Lexus dealer, to schedule the repair.
3. The dealer will replace the curtain air bag assemblies at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2024 Toyota Grand Highlander, Grand Highlander Hybrid, Lexus TX350, TX500 Hybrid, and TX550 Hybrid+ vehicles. The driver and passenger side curtain air bags may not unroll as intended and could partially deploy outside of an open window. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 226, "Ejection Mitigation."
Safety risk
An improper deployment of the side curtain air bag can reduce occupant protection during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver and passenger curtain shield air bag assemblies, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed November 4, 2024. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 24TB09/24TA09 and Lexus' numbers are 24LB05/24LA05.
