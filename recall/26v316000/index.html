Hyundai SUVs: Automatic Braking May Trigger Unexpectedly

NHTSA campaign 26V316000 · Reported May 19, 2026 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

Unexpected braking increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Hyundai is recalling certain 2025-2026 Santa Cruz, Tucson, Tucson Hybrid, and Tucson Plug-In Hybrid vehicles. Software in the front cameras may cause the forward collision avoidance system to turn on too early. When this happens, the system unexpectedly applies the brakes on its own, even when it isn't needed. This is a software problem in the camera that watches for collisions.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 to schedule the software update.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V316000. Official notice

Component
FORWARD COLLISION AVOIDANCE: AUTOMATIC EMERGENCY BRAKING
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2025-2026 Santa Cruz, Tucson, Tucson Hybrid, and Tucson Plug-In Hybrid Electric (PHEV) vehicles. Software in the front cameras may cause the forward collision avoidance system to activate prematurely and unexpectedly apply the brakes.
Safety risk
Unexpected braking increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the front camera software, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed July 17, 2026. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 302. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on May 20, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
