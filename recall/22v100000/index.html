Porsche Taycan Rear Seat Belt Blocks Child Seat

NHTSA campaign 22V100000 · Reported Feb 22, 2022 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

An improperly secured child seat increases the risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Taycan vehicles with the optional rear center seat have a seat belt buckle wiring harness that can block access to the lower anchor points for the right rear seat. This can make it hard to properly attach a child seat in that seat.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
Contact a Porsche dealer or call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243.
The repair, which reroutes and secures the wiring and replaces the buckle if needed, is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V100000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS: REAR/OTHER:BUCKLE ASSEMBLY
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Taycan vehicles equipped with an optional rear center seat. The center seat belt buckle wiring harness may obstruct access to the right rear seat lower child seat anchors, preventing a child seat from being properly secured. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 225, "Child Restraint Anchorage Systems."
Safety risk
An improperly secured child seat increases the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reroute and secure the center rear seat belt buckle wiring harness and, as necessary, replace a damaged buckle, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 7, 2022. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ANA4.
