Porsche Taycan wiring blocks child seat anchors

NHTSA campaign 22V100000 · Reported 2022-02-22 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Porsche Taycan
  • 2021 Porsche Taycan

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2020-2021 Porsche Taycan cars that were ordered with the optional rear center seat have a wiring bundle for the center seat belt buckle that sits in the way. It can block access to the lower child seat anchor points for the right rear seat, so a child seat cannot be attached properly. Because of this, the car does not meet the federal safety rule for child seat anchors.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious — a child seat that is not properly secured raises the risk of injury in a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is covered. 2. Contact your Porsche dealer or Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243 (recall ANA4). 3. The dealer will reroute and secure the wiring, and replace a damaged buckle if needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS: REAR/OTHER:BUCKLE ASSEMBLY
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Taycan vehicles equipped with an optional rear center seat. The center seat belt buckle wiring harness may obstruct access to the right rear seat lower child seat anchors, preventing a child seat from being properly secured. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 225, "Child Restraint Anchorage Systems."
Safety risk
An improperly secured child seat increases the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reroute and secure the center rear seat belt buckle wiring harness and, as necessary, replace a damaged buckle, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 7, 2022. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ANA4.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
