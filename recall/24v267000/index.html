Ford Bronco Sport, Maverick Battery Detection Problem

NHTSA campaign 24V267000 · Reported 2024-04-12 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

An undetected low battery charge can cause a loss of electrical accessories like hazard lights, or a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Ford vehicles, the body and powertrain control modules may fail to notice when the 12-volt battery's charge gets low. If a low battery charge isn't detected, the vehicle could lose electrical accessories such as the hazard lights, or it could lose the power needed to keep driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Ford dealer or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 to schedule the repair.
3. Dealers will recalibrate the body control module and powertrain control module free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:BODY CONTROL MODULE/BCM
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2024 Bronco Sport and 2022-2023 Maverick vehicles. The body and power train control modules may fail to detect a change in the 12-volt battery state of charge.
Safety risk
An undetected low battery charge can result in a loss of electrical accessories such as hazard lights, or cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will recalibrate the body control module and power train control module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 4, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S24.
