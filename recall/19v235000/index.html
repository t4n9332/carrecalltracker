Nissan Altima Brake Lights Stay On, Rollaway Risk

NHTSA campaign 19V235000 · Reported 2019-03-25 · Nissan North America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Nissan Altima

What's wrong, in plain English

A small part behind the brake pedal may sit in the wrong spot, so the car thinks the brake is being pressed all the time. Two things can go wrong. Your brake lights can stay lit even when you are not braking, which confuses drivers behind you. And the shift lever can be moved out of Park without your foot on the brake, so the car could roll away on its own. Both raise the risk of a crash.

How serious is it?

Serious — a car that can be shifted out of Park without the brake can roll unexpectedly — but Nissan has not told owners to stop driving, so get it in soon rather than leaving it.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your 2019 Altima is part of campaign 19V235000.
2. Call your Nissan dealer to book the inspection, or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
3. The dealer will inspect and install a new brake pedal if needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS:SWITCH
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2019 Nissan Altima vehicles. The brake switch bracket may be in the incorrect location, which may cause the brake switch to remain on.
Safety risk
If the brake switch remains in the ON position, the brake lights will remain illuminated when the brakes are not being pressed, which may confuse other drivers. Additionally, the transmission shift lever may be moved out of the Park position without the brake pedal being pressed, allowing the vehicle to roll unexpectedly. Either condition increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and install a new brake pedal as needed, free of charge. The recall began May 15, 2019. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
