Nissan Altima Brake Light Switch Misalignment Issue

NHTSA campaign 19V235000 · Reported 2019-03-25 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Either problem raises the risk of a crash, including an unexpected vehicle rollaway. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The bracket holding the brake light switch may be installed in the wrong spot, causing the brake switch to stay turned on. This can make the brake lights stay lit even when the driver isn't pressing the brake pedal, which may confuse other drivers. It can also let the transmission shift lever move out of Park without the brake pedal being pressed, allowing the vehicle to roll away unexpectedly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Nissan dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669, to schedule the repair.
3. The brake pedal replacement is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS:SWITCH
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2019 Nissan Altima vehicles. The brake switch bracket may be in the incorrect location, which may cause the brake switch to remain on.
Safety risk
If the brake switch remains in the ON position, the brake lights will remain illuminated when the brakes are not being pressed, which may confuse other drivers. Additionally, the transmission shift lever may be moved out of the Park position without the brake pedal being pressed, allowing the vehicle to roll unexpectedly. Either condition increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and install a new brake pedal as needed, free of charge. The recall began May 15, 2019. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
