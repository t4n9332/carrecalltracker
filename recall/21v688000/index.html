Toyota Tundra headlight wiring can overheat and cause fire

NHTSA campaign 21V688000 · Reported 2021-09-02 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Toyota Tundra
  • 2019 Toyota Tundra
  • 2020 Toyota Tundra
  • 2021 Toyota Tundra

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Tundra pickups, the headlight circuits can send power to the high beams and the low beams at the same time. They were never meant to run together, and the extra electrical load can make the connector behind the headlight get very hot. An overheated connector can start a fire. At the dealer, the engine wiring harness is modified, and any bulb, bulb connector, or headlight assembly already damaged by the heat is replaced. All of this is free.

How serious is it?

This is a fire risk, so it is serious; Toyota does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside, but do not put off the free repair.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included.
2. Call a Toyota dealer or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 (recall 21TB06 / 21TA06) and make an appointment.
3. Have the wiring modified and any burned parts replaced at no cost.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018-2021 Tundra vehicles. The headlight electrical circuits may power the high and low beams simultaneously, which could overheat the electrical connector.
Safety risk
An overheated electrical connector can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will modify the engine wire harness assembly and replace any damaged bulb, bulb connector, and/or headlight assembly as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 25, 2022. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 21TB06 and 21TA06.
