Mercedes-Benz Passenger Air Bag Status May Display Incorrectly

NHTSA campaign 18V838000 · Reported Nov 27, 2018 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC.

high How serious is it? About this label

A front seat passenger who believes an air bag will deploy when it will not faces a greater risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In certain Mercedes-Benz vehicles with the Audio 20 head unit and automatic child seat recognition, the passenger air bag status may not display correctly on the dashboard. If the display incorrectly shows the passenger air bag as 'on' when it is actually off, a front seat passenger may not realize the air bag will not inflate in a crash. Not knowing this increases their risk of injury if a crash happens.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact Mercedes-Benz USA customer service at 1-800-367-6372 to schedule a dealer visit. 3. Dealers will update the audio head unit software for free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V838000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SENSOR:OCCUPANT CLASSIFICATION
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2018 C300 Cabriolet, C300 Coupe, C300 4Matic Cabriolet, C300, C300 4Matic Coupe, C300 4Matic, C43 AMG Cabriolet, C43 AMG Coupe, C63 S AMG Cabriolet, C350e Plug-In Hybrid, C63 AMG Cabriolet, C63 AMG Coupe, C63 S AMG Coupe, C63 AMG, C63 S AMG, 2018-2019 GLC300 4Matic Coupe, GLC300, GLC63 AMG Coupe, GLC300 4Matic, GLC350e Plug-In Hybrid, GLC43 AMG Coupe, GLC43 AMG, GLC63 S AMG Coupe, and GLC63 AMG vehicles equipped with Audio 20 head units and automatic child seat recognition. The status of the passenger side air bag may not display correctly.
Safety risk
If the passenger air bag is incorrectly displayed as 'ON' when it is not, a front seat passenger may be unaware that the air bag will not deploy in the event of a crash, increasing their risk of injury.
Remedy
MBUSA will notify owners, and dealers will update the audio head unit software, free of charge. The recall began January 3, 2019. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2018120009.
