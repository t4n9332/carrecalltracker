Nissan Altima: Fuel Tube Clip May Be Unlocked

NHTSA campaign 18V922000 · Reported Dec 20, 2018 · Nissan North America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak increases the risk of a fire, and the engine stalling increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2019 Nissan Altima vehicles, the clip connecting the low pressure fuel tube to the high pressure fuel pump may not have locked into position. If the clip stays unlocked, the fuel tube may shift and leak fuel. If the tube fully disconnects, the engine can stall. Dealers will route the fuel hose into the holding clip and lock it into place as necessary.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Nissan dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V922000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc (Nissan) is recalling certain 2019 Altima vehicles. The retainer clip connecting the low pressure fuel tube to the high pressure fuel pump may not have been locked into position.
Safety risk
If the retainer clip is unlocked, the fuel tube may shift and cause a fuel leak, increasing the risk of a fire. If the fuel tube fully disconnects, the engine can stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Nissan has notified owners, and dealers will route the fuel hose into the holding clip and lock the retainer clip into place, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began February 11, 2019. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
