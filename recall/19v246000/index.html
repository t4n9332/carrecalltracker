What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain 2019 Dodge Charger vehicles. The driver and passenger sun visors may be missing the required airbag warning information, which means the vehicles don't meet a federal safety standard for occupant protection.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
Contact an FCA/Chrysler dealer, or call customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
The replacement sun visors are free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V246000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Dodge Charger vehicles. The driver and passenger sun visors may lack the required air bag warning information. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
Failure to provide the air bag warning information increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Chrysler has notified owners, and dealers will replace the driver and passenger sun visors, free of charge. The recall began April 17, 2019. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V35.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
