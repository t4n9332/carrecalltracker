GM Full-Size SUVs: Third-Row Seat Belts May Break

NHTSA campaign 21V190000 · Reported 2021-03-18 · General Motors, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Cadillac Escalade
  • 2021 Cadillac Escalade ESV
  • 2021 Chevrolet Suburban
  • 2021 Chevrolet Tahoe
  • 2021 GMC Yukon
  • 2021 GMC Yukon XL

What's wrong, in plain English

On these three-row SUVs, the outer seat belts in the back row may have been caught or run the wrong way behind the mechanism that folds the seat. Pinched or misrouted like that, the belt can get damaged. A damaged belt can break in a crash, so it may not protect the person sitting there. A dealer will look at both third-row outer belts, reroute them if needed, and replace any that are damaged.

How serious is it?

Serious for anyone riding in the third row, since the belt could fail in a crash; the SUV is otherwise safe to drive, and you can avoid using those seats until it is fixed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your SUV is covered.
2. Contact your dealer or GM customer service — Chevrolet 1-800-222-1020, GMC 1-800-462-8782, Cadillac 1-800-458-8006 — and mention recall N202313000.
3. Have the third-row belts inspected, rerouted, or replaced at no charge.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2021 Cadillac Escalade, Escalade ESV, GMC Yukon, Yukon XL, Chevrolet Suburban, and Tahoe vehicles with third-row seating. The third-row outboard seat belts may have been entrapped or misrouted behind the outboard seat-folding mechanism, which could result in damaged seat belts.
Safety risk
A damaged seat belt can break during a crash, increasing the risk of injury to the occupant.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the third-row outboard seat belts, replacing any damaged seat belts, and rerouting them if necessary, free of charge. The recall began on April 21, 2021. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020, GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782, and Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N202313000.
