Chevrolet Camaro steering wheel emblem may break loose in crash

NHTSA campaign 21V211000 · Reported 2021-03-25 · General Motors, LLC · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Chevrolet Camaro
  • 2019 Chevrolet Camaro
  • 2020 Chevrolet Camaro
  • 2021 Chevrolet Camaro

What's wrong, in plain English

The plastic Camaro badge sits on the cover of the driver's airbag, in the middle of the steering wheel. If the airbag goes off in a crash, that badge can break away from the cover instead of staying put. Pieces of plastic can then be thrown toward the driver at the same moment the airbag is inflating, which can cause injuries on top of those from the crash itself. The dealer replaces the whole driver airbag unit, free.

How serious is it?

A real injury risk if you are ever in a crash, but nothing goes wrong during normal driving and the notice does not tell you to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Camaro is affected.
2. Call a Chevrolet dealer, or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020, and reference recall N212327220.
3. Schedule replacement of the driver's airbag module — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2017-2021 Chevrolet Camaro vehicles. The plastic "Camaro" emblem, located on the steering wheel air bag cover, may separate during deployment of the frontal driver air bag.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, plastic pieces of emblem may act as projectiles from deployment of the driver air bag can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace the frontal driver side air bag module, free of charge. The recall began April 14, 2021. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N212327220.
