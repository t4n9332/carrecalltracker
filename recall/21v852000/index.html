VW ID.4 Steering Gear May Have Faulty Bushing

NHTSA campaign 21V852000 · Reported Nov 3, 2021 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

An incorrectly assembled or missing drive bushing may cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

One 2021 Volkswagen ID.4 has a steering gear that may have been built with an incorrectly assembled or missing drive bushing, a part inside the steering gear. A problem with this bushing can affect how the steering gear performs. A dealer will replace the entire steering gear to correct the problem.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298, to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V852000. Official notice

Component
STEERING:GEAR BOX (OTHER THAN RACK AND PINION)
Summary
Volkswagen Group of American, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling one 2021 ID.4 vehicle. The steering gear may have an incorrectly assembled or missing drive bushing.
Safety risk
An incorrectly assembled or missing drive bushing may cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
A dealer will replace the steering gear, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 5, 2021. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 48Q7.
