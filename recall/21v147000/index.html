Ram ProMaster City can lose braking without any warning

NHTSA campaign 21V147000 · Reported 2021-03-09 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 RAM Promaster City
  • 2021 RAM Promaster City

What's wrong, in plain English

These 2020-2021 Ram ProMaster City vans may have a damaged anti-lock brake control box. That box manages the brakes so the wheels do not lock up, and it also runs the automatic emergency braking. If it fails, the van can lose braking power or lose automatic braking, and it can happen suddenly, with no warning beforehand. That makes a crash much more likely. A dealer will replace the whole unit at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

This is one of the more serious kinds of recall: braking can be reduced with no warning at all, so treat it as urgent and get the free replacement before doing much more driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is affected.
2. Contact your Ram dealer immediately to have the ABS module replaced free of charge.
3. If you need help or a loaner, call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:ANTILOCK/TRACTION CONTROL/ELECTRONIC LIMITED SLIP:CONTROL UNIT/MODULE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Ram ProMaster City vehicles. The Anti-Lock Brake System (ABS) module may have internal damage, which could result in a loss of braking performance or autonomous braking functions.
Safety risk
The loss of braking performance or autonomous braking functions can increase the risk of a vehicle crash without prior warning.
Remedy
FCA US, LLC will notify owners, and dealers will replace the ABS module, free of charge. The recall began on March 30, 2021. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
