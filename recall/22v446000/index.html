Cadillac XT5, XT6, GMC Acadia fuel tank valve leaks

NHTSA campaign 22V446000 · Reported 2022-06-23 · General Motors, LLC · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Cadillac XT5
  • 2022 Cadillac XT6
  • 2022 GMC Acadia

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2022 Cadillac XT5, XT6 and GMC Acadia all-wheel-drive vehicles, a valve on the fuel tank may not seal properly. That valve is meant to keep gasoline from escaping if the vehicle ever rolls over in a crash. If it is knocked out of place or missing, fuel can leak during a rollover, and spilled gasoline can catch fire. Dealers will inspect the tank and replace the tank shell if the valve is bad.

How serious is it?

The problem only matters in a rollover crash, where it could lead to a fuel leak and fire; GM does not tell owners to stop driving, so keep driving but schedule the inspection.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is in recall N222366070.
2. Contact your dealer, or call Cadillac at 1-800-458-8006 or GMC at 1-800-462-8782, for the fuel tank inspection.
3. Let the dealer replace the fuel tank shell if needed — inspection and repair are free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2022 Cadillac XT5, XT6, and GMC Acadia All-Wheel Drive (AWD) vehicles. The fuel tank rollover valve may not provide a proper seal in the event of a rollover crash.
Safety risk
A fuel tank rollover valve that does not properly seal can result in a fuel leak during a rollover crash, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the fuel tank and replace the fuel tank shell if the rollover valve is found to be dislodged or missing, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 27, 2022. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 and GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N222366070.
