Kia K5 and Sorento fuel leak can cause fire

NHTSA campaign 21V519000 · Reported 2021-07-08 · Kia Motors America · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Kia K5
  • 2021 Kia Sorento
  • 2022 Kia K5

What's wrong, in plain English

These cars and SUVs have a 2.5-liter turbocharged engine. Gasoline can leak from the spot where the high-pressure fuel pump connects to the fuel rail, the pipe that carries fuel to the engine. Fuel escaping into a hot engine bay can start a fire. A dealer will look at that connection and either tighten it or put in a new fuel pipe, at no cost to you. Kia mailed notices to owners in July 2021.

How serious is it?

A leaking fuel line can lead to a fire, so this is one to take seriously — Kia has not told owners to park the vehicle or stop driving, but get it inspected as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your K5 or Sorento is affected.
2. Call a Kia dealer, or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542, and reference recall SC214.
3. Get the fuel pipe inspected, tightened, or replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Kia Motor America (Kia) is recalling certain 2021 Sorento and 2021-2022 K5 vehicles equipped with 2.5L turbocharged engines. Fuel may leak at the pipe connection between the high-pressure fuel pump and fuel rail.
Safety risk
A fuel leak increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and tighten, or replace the fuel pipe as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 21, 2021. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC214.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
