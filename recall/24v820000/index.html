NHTSA campaign 24V820000 · Reported Oct 31, 2024 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2018 to 2021 Honda GL1800 Goldwing motorcycles have a bolt that secures the primary drive gear to the crankshaft. Over time, this bolt can break. If it breaks, the engine may stall while you are riding, and the rear wheel can lock up. An engine stall and a locked rear wheel happening together is a significant mechanical problem while the motorcycle is in motion.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your motorcycle is included.
2. Contact Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870 to schedule the bolt replacement.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V820000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:HARD PARTS INTERNAL/MECHANICAL
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2018-2021 GL1800 Goldwing motorcycles. The primary drive gear bolt on the crankshaft may break, which can result in an engine stall and lock up the rear wheel.
Safety risk
An engine stall and rear wheel lockup can increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the primary drive gear bolt, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 30, 2024. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KQ9.
