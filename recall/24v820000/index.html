Honda Gold Wing: engine bolt may break, locking rear wheel

NHTSA campaign 24V820000 · Reported 2024-10-31 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Honda GL1800
  • 2019 Honda GL1800
  • 2020 Honda GL1800
  • 2021 Honda GL1800

What's wrong, in plain English

Inside the engine of these Gold Wing motorcycles, the bolt that holds the main drive gear onto the crankshaft can snap. If it breaks, the engine can stall and the rear wheel can lock up. On a motorcycle, a rear wheel that suddenly stops turning can put you on the ground, and it can happen while you are riding. Honda mailed owner letters on December 30, 2024. A dealer will install a new primary drive gear bolt for free.

How serious is it?

Very serious, because the engine can quit and the rear wheel can lock while you are riding; the notice does not tell owners to stop riding, but this repair should not wait.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Gold Wing is included.
2. Contact a Honda motorcycle dealer or Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870 and give them recall number KQ9.
3. Schedule the free bolt replacement as soon as you can.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:HARD PARTS INTERNAL/MECHANICAL
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2018-2021 GL1800 Goldwing motorcycles. The primary drive gear bolt on the crankshaft may break, which can result in an engine stall and lock up the rear wheel.
Safety risk
An engine stall and rear wheel lockup can increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the primary drive gear bolt, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 30, 2024. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KQ9.
