BMW X7 rear reflectors do not reflect enough light

NHTSA campaign 20V052000 · Reported 2020-01-30 · BMW of North America, LLC · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 BMW X7

What's wrong, in plain English

The two reflectors on the back of some 2020 BMW X7 SUVs do not bounce back enough light. Those reflectors are what make the rear of your vehicle show up when another car's headlights shine on it, which matters most at night or when you are stopped or moving slowly. Because they are too dim, other drivers may not spot your vehicle as early as they should. Dealers will replace both rear reflectors at no charge.

How serious is it?

This is a lower-level problem that slightly increases the chance another driver hits you from behind in the dark; the vehicle is safe to keep driving, and you can have the reflectors replaced at a normal service visit.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your X7 is included.
2. Contact your BMW dealer or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 to book the replacement.
3. Have both rear reflectors replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020 X7 xDrive40i and X7 xDrive50i vehicles. The rear reflex reflectors may not reflect an adequate amount of light. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
An inadequate amount of reflected light will decrease the vehicle's visibility to other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace both rear reflex reflectors, free of charge. The recall began March 30, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
