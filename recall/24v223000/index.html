Ford Maverick and Bronco Sport tires built incorrectly

NHTSA campaign 24V223000 · Reported 2024-03-22 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Ford Bronco Sport
  • 2023 Ford Maverick

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Ford Maverick and Bronco Sport vehicles left the factory with tires that were not made correctly. A tire built wrong can behave unpredictably on the road, and the driver may lose the ability to steer and control the vehicle. The notice does not describe any warning signs you would feel or see beforehand. A dealer will inspect the tires and replace any that are faulty, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed in April 2024.

How serious is it?

Potentially serious, because a bad tire can cause a sudden loss of control; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get the tires checked promptly.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 24S20), for an inspection. 3. Have any faulty tires replaced — the inspection and replacement are free.

Official notice

Component
TIRES
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023 Maverick and Bronco Sport vehicles. The tires may have been manufactured incorrectly, which may result in a loss of vehicle handling and control.
Safety risk
A loss of vehicle handling and control increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the tires, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 5, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S20.
