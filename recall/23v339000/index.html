NHTSA campaign 23V339000 · Reported May 11, 2023 · General Motors, LLC

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain vehicles. The four lower child seat anchor bars in the back seat may have too much powder coating on them, and the extra finish could keep a child seat from being attached to the bars. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for child restraint anchorage systems. Dealers will inspect the bars and, if needed, remove and replace the finish, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is covered. 2. Until the repair is done, install child seats using the vehicle's rear seat belts, as the notice advises. 3. Contact a dealer, or call Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC at 1-800-462-8782 (recall number N232403240); the inspection and refinishing are free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V339000. Official notice

Component
CHILD SEAT:VEHICLE LATCH ANCHOR
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2020-2023 Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain vehicles. The four rear-seat lower child seat LATCH anchorage bars may have excessive powder coating, which could prevent a child seat from being installed with the anchorage bars. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 225, "Child Restraint Anchorage Systems."
Safety risk
The inability to install a child seat with the LATCH anchorage bars could result in an unsecured child seat, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised to install child seats using the vehicle's rear seat belts until the remedy is complete. Dealers will inspect the latch anchorage bars, and remove and replace the finish, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 2, 2023. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N232403240.
