Rivian R1T, R1S, EDV Loose Steering Fastener

NHTSA campaign 22V744000 · Reported Oct 6, 2022 · Rivian Automotive, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A separated steering knuckle fastener can cause a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The fastener that connects the front upper control arm to the steering knuckle may not have been tightened properly when the vehicle was built. If this fastener is loose, the connection between the control arm and the steering knuckle could come apart while the vehicle is being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Rivian dealer for a free inspection and tightening of the fastener if needed; call Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V744000. Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:CONTROL ARM:UPPER ARM
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022 R1T, R1S, and EDV vehicles. The fastener connecting the front upper control arm and steering knuckle may have been improperly tightened.
Safety risk
A loose steering knuckle fastener could separate, causing a loss of vehicle control and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and, as necessary, tighten the steering knuckle fastener, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 11, 2022. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM 997.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
