Rivian trucks and SUVs may have loose steering bolt

NHTSA campaign 22V744000 · Reported 2022-10-06 · Rivian Automotive, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

Losing steering control while driving is very serious and can happen without warning, so get this inspected right away even though the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle.

What's wrong, in plain English

A bolt that joins the front suspension arm to the part the wheel steers on may not have been tightened enough when the vehicle was built. If that bolt works its way loose and comes apart, the connection holding the front wheel in place can separate. You could suddenly lose the ability to steer the vehicle. Rivian dealers and service technicians will inspect the bolt and tighten it if needed, and the check costs you nothing.

What you should do

1. Verify your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Rivian is covered. 2. Contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261 (recall FSAM 997) to arrange the inspection as soon as possible. 3. The inspection and any tightening are free of charge.

Affected vehicles (3)

  • 2022 Rivian EDV
  • 2022 Rivian R1S
  • 2022 Rivian R1T

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:CONTROL ARM:UPPER ARM
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022 R1T, R1S, and EDV vehicles. The fastener connecting the front upper control arm and steering knuckle may have been improperly tightened.
Safety risk
A loose steering knuckle fastener could separate, causing a loss of vehicle control and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and, as necessary, tighten the steering knuckle fastener, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 11, 2022. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM 997.
