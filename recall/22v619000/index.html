Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager wiper arm nuts may loosen

NHTSA campaign 22V619000 · Reported 2022-08-11 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Chrysler Pacifica
  • 2022 Chrysler Voyager

What's wrong, in plain English

The nuts that hold the windshield wiper arms in place may not have been tightened enough at the factory. Loose nuts can let the wiper arms slip so the wipers do not sweep the glass properly, or stop moving at all. In rain or snow that leaves you with a poor view of the road. The repair is quick: a dealer tightens the wiper arm nuts at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

The notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but wipers that quit in bad weather cut your visibility and raise the crash risk, so get this done before the next storm.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your van is included. 2. Call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 or your dealer and mention recall Z80. 3. Have the wiper arm nuts tightened; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD WIPER/WASHER:LINKAGES
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Voyager and Pacifica vehicles. The windshield wiper arm nuts may be improperly tightened, which can cause the wipers to not function properly.
Safety risk
Improperly functioning wipers can reduce the driver's visibility in certain weather conditions, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will tighten the wiper arm nuts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 30, 2022. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US's number for this recall is Z80.
