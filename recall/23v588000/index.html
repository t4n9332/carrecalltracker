Honda Accord and CR-V Hybrid Transmission May Fail

NHTSA campaign 23V588000 · Reported 2023-08-17 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

A loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Honda is recalling certain CR-V Hybrid and Accord Hybrid vehicles because the electronic transmission may have been manufactured improperly. This can result in a loss of drive power while the vehicle is being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Honda dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. The repair is free.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2023 Honda Accord Hybrid
  • 2023 Honda CR-V Hybrid

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:INTERNAL:CVT
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023 CR-V Hybrid and Accord Hybrid vehicles. The electronic transmission may have been improperly manufactured, which can result in a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the electronic continuously variable transmission (e-CVT), free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 11, 2023. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's numbers for this recall are NFG and HFF.
