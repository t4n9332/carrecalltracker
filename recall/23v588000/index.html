Honda CR-V, Accord Hybrid transmission may lose power

NHTSA campaign 23V588000 · Reported 2023-08-17 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Honda Accord Hybrid
  • 2023 Honda CR-V Hybrid

What's wrong, in plain English

The transmission in these hybrids may not have been built correctly. If it fails, the vehicle can lose drive power — you press the accelerator and the car will not pull. Losing power like that in traffic can lead to a crash. Honda's fix is not a patch or an adjustment: the dealer installs a completely new transmission at no cost to you. Owner letters were mailed in October 2023, so the repair is available now.

How serious is it?

Serious, because losing drive power can cause a crash; the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but this one should be scheduled right away.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 23V588000 applies (Honda's numbers are NFG and HFF). 2. Call your Honda dealer for the earliest appointment, or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. 3. The new transmission is installed free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:INTERNAL:CVT
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023 CR-V Hybrid and Accord Hybrid vehicles. The electronic transmission may have been improperly manufactured, which can result in a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the electronic continuously variable transmission (e-CVT), free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 11, 2023. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's numbers for this recall are NFG and HFF.
