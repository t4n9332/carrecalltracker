Dodge Hornet sticker lists wrong maximum load weight

NHTSA campaign 24V175000 · Reported 2024-03-05 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) (Stellantis)

low How serious is it? About this label

This is a labeling problem rather than a broken part, and it is safe to keep driving as long as you do not overload the vehicle.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2023-2024 Dodge Hornet and Alfa Romeo Tonale vehicles have the wrong number printed on the tire information sticker, the label in the driver's door opening that tells you how much weight the vehicle can carry. Because the number is wrong, an owner could load in more people and cargo than the vehicle is built to handle. An overloaded vehicle handles and stops poorly. A dealer puts a corrected label over the old one.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is on the list.
2. Contact your dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and reference recall 28B.
3. Have the corrected overlay label applied — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Dodge Hornet and Alfa Romeo Tonale vehicles. The maximum vehicle capacity weight value on the tire placard label is incorrect. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
A vehicle with an incorrect maximum capacity weight value may be overloaded, which can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will add an overlay label to the tire placard information, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 28, 2024. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 28B.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
