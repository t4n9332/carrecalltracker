Volkswagen Jetta front brake caliper bolts may loosen

NHTSA campaign 20V359000 · Reported 2020-06-17 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Volkswagen Jetta

What's wrong, in plain English

This recall covers a single 2020 Volkswagen Jetta. On that car, the bolts holding the front brake calipers — the parts that squeeze the brake pads against the wheels to slow you down — may come loose. Loose bolts can hurt how well the brakes work, meaning the car may not stop the way it should. Volkswagen will contact the owner, and a dealer will replace all four front brake caliper bolts at no charge.

How serious is it?

Serious, because weakened braking can cause a crash, but the notice does not tell the owner to stop driving before the repair.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Jetta is the affected vehicle. 2. Contact your Volkswagen dealer or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall 47Q2). 3. Have all four front brake caliper bolts replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:DISC:CALIPER
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling one 2020 Jetta vehicle. The front brake caliper bolts may come loose.
Safety risk
If a brake caliper bolt comes loose, it may affect the vehicle's brake performance, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify the owner, and a dealer will replace all four front brake caliper bolts, free of charge. The recall began July 1, 2020. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 47Q2.
