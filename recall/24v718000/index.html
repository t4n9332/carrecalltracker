Tesla Cybertruck backup camera image appears too slowly

NHTSA campaign 24V718000 · Reported 2024-09-26 · Tesla, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Tesla Cybertruck

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2024 Tesla Cybertrucks, the backup camera picture takes too long to appear on the screen after you shift into reverse. For those first moments you're backing up, you can't see what's behind the truck on the display, which is exactly when you need it — a child, a pet, or a low obstacle could be back there. Tesla has already fixed this with a free software update, so the repair arrives over the internet rather than at a dealer.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the truck is safe to drive and the notice sets no restrictions, but back up slowly and check your mirrors and surroundings until the update is installed.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Cybertruck is covered.
2. Make sure your truck is connected to Wi-Fi and install any pending software update — it's free.
3. If the camera still lags, call Tesla at 1-877-798-3752 and reference SB-24-00-016.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2024 Cybertruck vehicles. The rearview camera image may be delayed after shifting into reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A delayed rearview image reduces the driver's view of what is behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla released an over-the-air (OTA) software update, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 25, 2024. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-24-00-016. (over-the-air software update)
