What's wrong, in plain English

The metal arms that hold the windshield wipers can break. If an arm breaks, that wiper stops sweeping the glass. In heavy rain or snow you could suddenly be left with little or no clear view through the windshield while you are driving. A dealer will inspect the wiper arms and replace them if needed, free of charge. Ford mailed owner letters in August 2026, and you can look up your VIN online now.

How serious is it?

Moderate to serious: the vehicle drives normally, but losing a wiper in bad weather can leave you unable to see, so have it checked soon.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included (Ford's recall number is 26S24).
2. Call your dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 to book the inspection.
3. Have the wiper arms inspected and replaced if necessary. It is free.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD WIPER/WASHER:LINKAGES
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Lincoln Navigator, Expedition, and 2022-2023 F-250 SD, F-350 SD, F-450 SD, F-550 SD, and F-600 SD vehicles. The windshield wiper arms may break, causing the wipers to fail.
Safety risk
Windshield wiper failure can reduce visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the wiper arms, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 5, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S24.Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on April 1, 2026.
