Porsche 911 Carrera and Taycan brake pedal may fail

NHTSA campaign 21V697000 · Reported 2021-09-08 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Porsche 911 Carrera
  • 2020 Porsche Taycan
  • 2021 Porsche 911 Carrera
  • 2021 Porsche Taycan

What's wrong, in plain English

A rod inside the brake booster — the part that links your brake pedal to the rest of the braking system — may be loose and could come apart. If that happens, the brake pedal fails, so pressing it will not slow or stop the car. This affects certain 2020-2021 911 Carrera and Taycan models. Dealers will inspect the brake booster and repair or replace it, free of charge. Owner letters went out in late October 2021.

How serious is it?

Complete brake pedal failure is about as serious as a defect gets, and although Porsche has not issued a do-not-drive order, this should be your next appointment.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm campaign 21V697000 applies to your car.
2. Call your Porsche dealer or Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243 and reference recall AMB9.
3. Have the brake booster repaired or replaced at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2020-2021 911 Carrera and Taycan vehicles. The pressure input rod connection on the brake booster may be loose and could detach.
Safety risk
A detached pressure input rod will cause the brake pedal to fail, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will repair or replace the brake booster as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 28, 2021. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AMB9.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
