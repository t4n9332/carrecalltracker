Porsche 911 and Taycan Brake Booster Rod Detachment

NHTSA campaign 21V697000 · Reported 2021-09-08 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A detached rod can cause the brake pedal to fail completely, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Porsche is recalling certain 911 Carrera and Taycan vehicles because a rod connection in the brake booster may be loose. The rod could come loose and detach completely. If it detaches, the brake pedal can fail, and a failed brake pedal raises the risk of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Porsche dealer (1-800-767-7243) to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Affected vehicles (4)

  • 2020 Porsche 911 Carrera
  • 2020 Porsche Taycan
  • 2021 Porsche 911 Carrera
  • 2021 Porsche Taycan

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2020-2021 911 Carrera and Taycan vehicles. The pressure input rod connection on the brake booster may be loose and could detach.
Safety risk
A detached pressure input rod will cause the brake pedal to fail, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will repair or replace the brake booster as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 28, 2021. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AMB9.
