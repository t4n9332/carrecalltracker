BMW X5, X6 Front Lower Control Arm Failure

NHTSA campaign 18V439000 · Reported 2018-06-28 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

If the control arm breaks, it can affect vehicle handling and control, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling certain X5 and X6 vehicles. The pivot bolt on the front lower-left control arm may not have been properly hardened. This can cause the control arm to break.

What you should do

1. Check your vehicle's recall status by VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 to schedule an inspection.
3. The inspection and any needed repair are free.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:CONTROL ARM:LOWER ARM
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2018 BMW X5 xDrive35i, X5 xDrive50i, X5 xDrive35d, and X6 sDrive35i vehicles. The pivot bolt on the front lower-left control arm may not have been properly hardened, which may cause the control arm to break.
Safety risk
If the lower control arm breaks, vehicle handling and control can be affected, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the lower-left control arms and replace if necessary, free of charge. The recall began July 11, 2018. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
