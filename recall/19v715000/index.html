Mercedes-Benz Metris Battery Cover Missing, Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 19V715000 · Reported 2019-10-08 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

An electrical short from an exposed battery terminal increases the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Daimler Vans USA is recalling certain 2016-2019 Mercedes-Benz Metris vans because the backup battery compartment under the front passenger seat may not have its own cover. This can leave the battery terminals exposed when the seat is moved forward. If a conductive object, such as metal, falls into the opening and touches the terminals, it can cause an electrical short.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Metris is affected.
2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer, or call Daimler Vans USA customer service at 1-877-762-8267, to have the battery compartment cover installed.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2016-2019 Mercedes-Benz Metris vehicles. The integrated backup battery compartment located underneath the front passenger's seat may not have its own cover, potentially exposing the battery when the seat is moved forward.
Safety risk
If conductive material, such as metal, unintentionally drops into the opening and contacts the terminals on the battery, an electrical short can occur, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and Mercedes-Benz dealers will install covers over the battery compartment area, free of charge. DVUSA issued owners an interim notification on December 2, 2019. The recall began March 9, 2020. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS2SITZAB.
