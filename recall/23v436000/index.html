Volkswagen ID.4 backup camera image may not appear

NHTSA campaign 23V436000 · Reported 2023-06-21 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Volkswagen ID.4

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Volkswagen ID.4 vehicles that had their infotainment screen unit replaced during a repair got the wrong software loaded onto the new unit. Because of that, the backup camera picture may be slow to show up or may not come on at all when you shift into reverse. Federal rules require the rear camera view to appear properly when backing up. Without it, you can't see what's directly behind the vehicle. Dealers will load the correct software onto the unit.

How serious is it?

This is a moderate concern — a missing or delayed backup camera view makes it harder to see behind you and raises crash risk, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall number 93S7) or your dealer for an appointment. 3. Have the dealer update the software — the fix is free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:DISPLAY FUNCTION
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2022 ID.4 vehicles. The replacement infotainment central control unit (CCU) installed in the vehicle during a repair contains incorrect software that can cause the rearview camera image to be delayed or deactivated when reversing. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview camera with a delayed or deactivated image may reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the software, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 18, 2023. Owners may contact Volkswagen's customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 93S7.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
