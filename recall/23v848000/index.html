Ford Maverick Turn Signal Failure Won't Warn Driver

NHTSA campaign 23V848000 · Reported Dec 15, 2023 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

A turn signal that fails without warning the driver means other drivers may not know the vehicle is turning, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Maverick trucks. If one of the rear turn signals stops working, the vehicle is supposed to change the turn signal's flash rate to alert the driver that something is wrong, but on these vehicles it will not do that. This means the truck does not meet a federal safety standard for lights and signaling equipment. Dealers will update the body control module software to fix the issue, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Ford dealer to schedule the software update (1-866-436-7332).
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V848000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TURN SIGNAL:BULBS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Maverick vehicles. In the event that one of the rear turn signals stops working, the vehicle will not adjust the turn signal flash rate frequency to warn the driver that a turn signal is not working correctly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
A nonfunctioning exterior turn signal will not notify other drivers that the vehicle is turning, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the body control module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 16, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23C41.
