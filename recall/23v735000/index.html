Honda Pilot Steering Gear Box May Lock Up

NHTSA campaign 23V735000 · Reported Nov 2, 2023 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

What's wrong, in plain English

Honda is recalling certain Pilot vehicles because a manufacturing error may leave a loose ball bearing inside the steering gear box assembly. This loose bearing can cause a driver to lose steering control, or cause the steering to lock up completely while the vehicle is being driven. Either of these problems can increase the risk of a crash while on the road.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Honda dealer or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. The steering rack replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V735000. Official notice

Component
STEERING:RACK AND PINION:RACK
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Pilot vehicles. Due to a manufacturing error, the gear box assembly may contain a loose ball bearing, which can result in a loss of steering control or steering lock-up.
Safety risk
A loss of steering control or steering lock-up can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the electric power steering rack, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 18, 2023. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is FFX.
