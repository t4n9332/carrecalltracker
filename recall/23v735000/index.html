Honda Pilot steering may lock up or fail

NHTSA campaign 23V735000 · Reported 2023-11-02 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Honda Pilot
  • 2024 Honda Pilot

What's wrong, in plain English

Because of a mistake during manufacturing, the steering gear box on some Pilots can contain a loose ball bearing. That loose part can cause you to lose steering control, or it can make the steering lock up so the wheel will not turn. This can happen while you are driving, without warning. A dealer will replace the entire electric power steering rack with a new one, at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Losing steering or having it lock up while driving is extremely dangerous and can happen with no warning; the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but this repair should be treated as urgent.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Pilot is included in recall 23V735000.
2. Call your Honda dealer right away, or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (recall FFX), to book the repair.
3. Have the dealer replace the electric power steering rack — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:RACK AND PINION:RACK
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Pilot vehicles. Due to a manufacturing error, the gear box assembly may contain a loose ball bearing, which can result in a loss of steering control or steering lock-up.
Safety risk
A loss of steering control or steering lock-up can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the electric power steering rack, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 18, 2023. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is FFX.
