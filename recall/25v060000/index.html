GMC Sierra 1500 chrome grille piece may fall off

NHTSA campaign 25V060000 · Reported 2025-02-06 · General Motors, LLC · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 GMC Sierra 1500

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2022 GMC Sierra 1500 trucks have a chrome deflector on the front grille. The clips and attachment points that hold this trim piece on can break, which lets the grille deflector come loose and fall off the truck. If that happens while driving, the loose piece becomes debris in the road that other drivers could hit or swerve to avoid. A dealer will reinforce or repair all of the attachment points so the piece stays put. Owner letters went out March 17, 2025.

How serious is it?

The truck remains safe to drive, but a piece that falls off can become a road hazard for you and others, so get it fixed at your next convenient opportunity.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your truck is covered. 2. Schedule the repair with your GMC dealer, or call GM customer service for GMC at 1-800-462-8782 (recall N242490120). 3. Have the grille deflector attachment points reinforced or repaired — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:BUMPERS:ACTIVE SHUTTERS/GRILL
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2022 GMC Sierra 1500 vehicles equipped with chrome front grille deflectors. The attachments that hold the front grille deflectors in place may fracture, resulting in grille detachment.
Safety risk
A detached front grille deflector may become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reinforce or repair all attachment points on the chrome front grille deflector, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 17, 2025. Owners may call GM customer service for GMC at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N242490120.
