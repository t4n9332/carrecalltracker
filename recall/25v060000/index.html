GMC Sierra 1500 chrome grille deflector may detach

NHTSA campaign 25V060000 · Reported 2025-02-06 · General Motors, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

A grille deflector that comes off can become a hazard in the road, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain GMC Sierra 1500 pickups that were built with a chrome front grille deflector. The attachments that hold the deflector in place can break. If they do, the grille deflector can come off the vehicle. Dealers will reinforce or repair all of the attachment points on the chrome front grille deflector at no cost to the owner. Owner notification letters for this recall were mailed on March 17, 2025.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is covered.
2. Contact a GMC dealer, or GM customer service for GMC at 1-800-462-8782, to book the repair (GM's recall number is N242490120).
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:BUMPERS:ACTIVE SHUTTERS/GRILL
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2022 GMC Sierra 1500 vehicles equipped with chrome front grille deflectors. The attachments that hold the front grille deflectors in place may fracture, resulting in grille detachment.
Safety risk
A detached front grille deflector may become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reinforce or repair all attachment points on the chrome front grille deflector, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 17, 2025. Owners may call GM customer service for GMC at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N242490120.
