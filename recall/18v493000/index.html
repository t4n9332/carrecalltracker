Chrysler Pacifica Axle Shaft May Disengage From Joint

NHTSA campaign 18V493000 · Reported 2018-07-26 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A loss of drive or unexpected vehicle movement while in Park can increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain Pacifica vehicles because the front axle halfshafts may have been assembled incorrectly, which can prevent the shaft from being properly secured to the CV joint. If the shaft becomes disengaged, the vehicle could lose the ability to drive, or it could move even when placed in Park.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Chrysler dealer to schedule the repair (Chrysler customer service: 1-800-853-1403).
3. The halfshaft assembly replacement is provided at no cost.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AXLE ASSEMBLY:AXLE SHAFT
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018 Chrysler Pacifica vehicles. The front axle halfshafts may have been incorrectly assembled, preventing the shaft from being properly secured to the constant-velocity (CV) joint.
Safety risk
If the axle shaft disengages from the CV joint, the vehicle will have a loss of drive or allow the vehicle to move while in the "Park" position. Either condition may increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the left or right halfshaft assembly, free of charge. The recall began September 18, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U80.
