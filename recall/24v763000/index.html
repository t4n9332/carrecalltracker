Honda Accord, CR-V and Civic fuel pump can leak

NHTSA campaign 24V763000 · Reported 2024-10-14 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Honda Accord Hybrid
  • 2023 Honda CR-V Hybrid
  • 2024 Honda Accord Hybrid
  • 2024 Honda CR-V Hybrid
  • 2025 Honda CR-V Hybrid

What's wrong, in plain English

The high-pressure fuel pump on these cars can crack and let gasoline leak out. Leaking fuel near anything hot or sparking can start a fire. A dealer will inspect the pump and replace it if it needs it, at no cost to you. Honda mailed owner letters on February 20, 2025, so if you have not had this checked, book it now. If you ever smell gasoline or see fuel dripping under the car, call your dealer right away.

How serious is it?

Very serious: a fuel leak can lead to a fire, and while the notice does not tell owners to park the car, you should get it inspected immediately and stop driving it if you smell or see leaking fuel.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Accord, Accord Hybrid, CR-V Hybrid, Civic or Civic Hybrid is covered.
2. Contact your Honda dealer, or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138, and reference recall PJW.
3. Have the high-pressure fuel pump inspected and replaced if needed — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Honda Accord, Accord Hybrid, 2023-2025 Honda CR-V Hybrid, and 2025 Honda Civic and Civic Hybrid vehicles. The high-pressure fuel pump may crack and leak fuel.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the high-pressure fuel pump as necessary, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed February 20, 2025. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is PJW.
