Hyundai Accent Seat Belt Pretensioners May Explode

NHTSA campaign 22V069000 · Reported Feb 9, 2022 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

An exploding seat belt pretensioner can send metal fragments into the vehicle, striking occupants and causing injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Hyundai Accent vehicles have front seat belt pretensioners, on the driver and/or passenger side, that may explode when they deploy in a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Hyundai dealer to have the seat belt pretensioners replaced; call 1-855-371-9460 with questions.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V069000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Accent vehicles. In the event of a crash, the front driver-side and/or passenger-side seat belt pretensioner(s) may explode upon deployment.
Safety risk
An exploding seat belt pretensioner can project metal fragments into the vehicle, striking vehicle occupants and resulting in injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the seat belt pretensioners, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 24, 2022. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 219. This recall is an expansion of NHTSA recall number 21V-796. This recall is replaced by NHTSA recall number 22V-354. Vehicles already repaired under this recall will need to have the new remedy completed.
