What's wrong, in plain English

These trucks have a diesel engine with a high-pressure pump that pushes fuel to the engine. Deposits left behind by biodiesel fuel can build up on moving parts inside that pump. Over time the buildup wears the parts out and the pump can quit working. If the pump fails, the engine stops making power, so the truck can slow down or stop while you are driving. Dealers will reprogram the truck's computers to ease the strain on the pump.

How serious is it?

Serious: a pump failure means a sudden loss of engine power while driving, which could leave you stranded in traffic, so get the update done soon.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included.
2. Call your Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 and reference recall 24S78.
3. Have the free engine and transmission computer software update installed.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2022 F-Super Duty F250, F350, F450, F550, F600, and 2021-2022 F-650, F750 vehicles. Biodiesel deposits may form on the pump drivetrain roller components, which could lead to failure of the high pressure fuel pump.
Safety risk
High pressure fuel pump failure can cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the powertrain control module (PCM) and Transmission Control Module (TCM) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 23, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S78.
