Toyota Corolla Steering Shaft May Crack And Detach

NHTSA campaign 24V878000 · Reported Nov 21, 2024 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Toyota Corolla and Corolla Hybrid vehicles may have a steering intermediate shaft that develops cracks during the manufacturing process. Over time, the shaft may fracture and possibly detach from the steering column, so it no longer connects properly. Dealers will replace the intermediate steering shaft to fix this, at no cost to owners. Toyota has already mailed notification letters to owners about this issue.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer, or call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V878000. Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Corolla and Corolla Hybrid vehicles. Due to cracks developed during the manufacturing process, the steering intermediate shaft assembly may fracture and possibly result in the shaft detaching from the steering column.
Safety risk
A steering shaft that detaches can result in a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the intermediate steering shaft, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 17, 2025. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 24TB13/24TA13.
