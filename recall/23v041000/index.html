Toyota RAV4 Prime Can Stall While Accelerating

NHTSA campaign 23V041000 · Reported Feb 2, 2023 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

An engine stall while driving increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In certain cold weather driving conditions, while driving in EV Mode, the computer that controls the hybrid system in your RAV4 Prime may shut the hybrid system down if you press the accelerator quickly to speed up further. When the hybrid system shuts down like this, it results in the engine stalling.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your RAV4 Prime is included. 2. Contact a Toyota dealer to schedule the free software update, or call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. 3. The software update is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V041000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:HYBRID MANAGEMENT:POWER CONTROL UNIT/MODULE (HPCU)
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2021 RAV4 Prime Plug-In Hybrid vehicles. During certain cold weather driving conditions, while driving in EV Mode, the hybrid vehicle control engine control unit (HEV ECU) software may cause the hybrid system to shut down if the accelerator is rapidly pressed to further accelerate the vehicle, resulting in a stall.
Safety risk
An engine stall increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the HEV ECU software, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed February 27, 2023. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 23TA01.
