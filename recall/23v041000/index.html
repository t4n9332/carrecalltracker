Toyota RAV4 Prime can stall in cold weather

NHTSA campaign 23V041000 · Reported 2023-02-02 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

A stall can happen while you're driving and increases the risk of a crash, so get the software updated as soon as you can — the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle.

What's wrong, in plain English

In certain 2021 RAV4 Prime plug-in hybrids, the software that manages the hybrid system can misbehave in cold weather while you're driving on electric power. If you press the accelerator down quickly to speed up, the hybrid system may shut itself down and the vehicle stalls. Losing power like that in traffic is dangerous. Toyota dealers will install updated software for the hybrid control computer, at no cost to the owner.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your RAV4 Prime is covered. 2. Contact a Toyota dealer or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 (recall 23TA01). 3. Have the hybrid control software updated — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:HYBRID MANAGEMENT:POWER CONTROL UNIT/MODULE (HPCU)
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2021 RAV4 Prime Plug-In Hybrid vehicles. During certain cold weather driving conditions, while driving in EV Mode, the hybrid vehicle control engine control unit (HEV ECU) software may cause the hybrid system to shut down if the accelerator is rapidly pressed to further accelerate the vehicle, resulting in a stall.
Safety risk
An engine stall increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the HEV ECU software, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed February 27, 2023. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 23TA01.
