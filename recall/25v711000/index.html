Mercedes AMG G63 front radar failure disables emergency braking

NHTSA campaign 25V711000 · Reported 2025-10-17 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderate — the vehicle remains drivable, but you lose safety systems that would normally help avoid a crash, so do not count on them until the sensor is replaced.

What's wrong, in plain English

A circuit board inside the short-range radar sensor at the front of the vehicle can fail. If it does, the vehicle loses automatic emergency braking along with active brake assist, lane keeping assist, and Distronic cruise control. Your own steering and brake pedal still work normally, but those systems will not step in to help you. Dealers replace the sensor free of charge. Owner letters were mailed November 26, 2025.

What you should do

1. Search your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact your Mercedes-Benz dealer, or MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372, and reference recall 2025110002.
3. Have the radar sensor replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FORWARD COLLISION AVOIDANCE: SENSING SYSTEM: RADAR
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2025 AMG G63 and G 580 vehicles. The front short-range radar sensor printed circuit board may fail and result in a loss of automatic emergency braking and other driver assistance features, including the active brake assist, lane keeping assist, and Distronic cruise control.
Safety risk
An automatic emergency braking system that does not function as intended can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the short-range sensor, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 26, 2025. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2025110002. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning October 24, 2025.
