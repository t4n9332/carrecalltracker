NHTSA campaign 25V711000 · Reported Oct 17, 2025 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

What's wrong, in plain English

Some AMG G63 and G580 SUVs have a front radar sensor with a circuit board that can fail. This sensor helps power several driver assistance features, including automatic emergency braking, active brake assist, lane keeping assist, and Distronic cruise control. If the sensor fails, these features may stop working. Without automatic emergency braking working as intended, the vehicle may not brake automatically to help avoid a crash, increasing the risk of a collision.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer, or call MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. 3. Replacement of the sensor is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V711000. Official notice

Component
FORWARD COLLISION AVOIDANCE: SENSING SYSTEM: RADAR
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2025 AMG G63 and G 580 vehicles. The front short-range radar sensor printed circuit board may fail and result in a loss of automatic emergency braking and other driver assistance features, including the active brake assist, lane keeping assist, and Distronic cruise control.
Safety risk
An automatic emergency braking system that does not function as intended can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the short-range sensor, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 26, 2025. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2025110002. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning October 24, 2025.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
