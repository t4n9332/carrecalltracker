Kia Sportage and Carnival Roof Trim May Fall Off

NHTSA campaign 24V025000 · Reported 2024-01-18 · Kia America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Kia Carnival
  • 2023 Kia Carnival
  • 2023 Kia Sportage
  • 2024 Kia Carnival
  • 2024 Kia Sportage

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2023-2024 Kia Sportage and 2022-2024 Kia Carnival vehicles, the strip of trim along the roof may work loose and come off. If it detaches while you are driving, it can land in the road and become an obstacle for the cars behind you. A dealer will look at the roof trim and either fasten it properly or replace it. Owner letters went out in March 2024, so the repair is available now.

How serious is it?

Moderate: the loose trim mainly endangers other drivers if it flies off, and the notice does not tell you to stop driving, so you can keep using the vehicle while you schedule the free inspection.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Sportage or Carnival is covered. 2. Call your Kia dealer or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542 and mention recall SC292. 3. Bring the vehicle in for the inspection and any repair — there is no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:ROOF AND PILLARS
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Sportage and 2022-2024 Carnival vehicles. The roof molding may loosen and detach.
Safety risk
A detached roof molding can create a road hazard for other vehicles, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the roof molding and replace or secure it as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 15, 2024. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC292.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
