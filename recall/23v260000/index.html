BMW X1 rear seat belts may be pinched by trim

NHTSA campaign 23V260000 · Reported 2023-04-12 · BMW of North America, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 BMW X1

What's wrong, in plain English

During assembly, the seat belts for the two outer seats in the second row may have been pinched by a piece of interior trim. A pinched belt can be squeezed or caught where you cannot see it, so it may not tighten and hold a passenger the way it should in a crash. Everything can look and feel normal from the back seat. Dealers will check both belts and adjust the trim panel if it is trapping one.

How serious is it?

The belt may not protect a back-seat passenger properly in a crash, which is a real injury risk, but the notice does not restrict driving in the meantime.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your X1 is affected.
2. Call your BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, to schedule the inspection.
3. Have the belts checked and the trim adjusted - the work is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2023 X1 xDrive28i, X1 M35i vehicles. On the second-row outboard seats, the seat belts may have been pinched by an interior trim panel during vehicle assembly.
Safety risk
A pinched seat belt may not function properly, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and adjust the interior trim panel, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 9, 2023. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
