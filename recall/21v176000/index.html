BMW 4 Series Convertible side airbag may not deploy

NHTSA campaign 21V176000 · Reported 2021-03-15 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious, because a side airbag that deploys incorrectly raises your chance of injury in a crash, but the notice puts no restriction on driving the car.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2021 BMW 430i and M440i 4 Series Convertibles, the airbag that protects your head and chest in a side crash may not inflate the way it should. The problem is in the car's software, not the airbag itself. BMW says every affected car is still sitting on dealer lots and none have been sold, so no letters are going out to owners. A dealer reprograms the software to fix it.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is included. 2. Call your BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, and ask for recall 21V176000. 3. Have the software update done, which is free; if you bought the car recently, confirm the dealer already did it.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:THORAX
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2021 430i and M440i 4 Series Convertible vehicles. In certain side-impact crashes, the head-thorax air bag may not deploy as intended.
Safety risk
An air bag that deploys incorrectly can increase the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the software, at no charge. All affected vehicles are still in dealer inventory, and therefore no owner letters will be mailed. The recall began March 9, 2021. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
