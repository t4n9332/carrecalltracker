Chevrolet and GMC HD trucks have faulty front belts

NHTSA campaign 17V567000 · Reported 2017-09-14 · General Motors LLC · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Chevrolet Silverado 2500
  • 2018 Chevrolet Silverado 3500
  • 2018 GMC Sierra 2500
  • 2018 GMC Sierra 3500

What's wrong, in plain English

The front seat belts in these trucks were built with the wrong part inside the reel that lets the belt pull out and pull back in. Because of that, the front belts may not behave the way they are designed to during a crash, so you or your front passenger could be injured more seriously than you should be. The trucks also do not meet the federal safety standard for seat belts. A dealer will replace both front seat belt reels for free.

How serious is it?

This raises your risk of injury if you are in a crash, but the notice does not say to stop driving the truck.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Silverado or Sierra 2500/3500 is included. 2. Contact your Chevrolet dealer at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC at 1-888-988-7267 and mention recall 17368, 17375, or 17376. 3. Schedule the free replacement of both front seat belt retractors.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:RETRACTOR
Summary
General Motors (GM) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Chevrolet Silverado 2500 and 3500 and GMC Sierra 2500 and 3500 trucks. The front seat belt retractor assemblies were built with the incorrect torsion bar. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 209, "Seat Belt Assemblies."
Safety risk
The seat belt retractors may not perform as intended during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace both front seat belt retractor assemblies, free of charge. The recall began March 24, 2021. Owners may contact GMC customer service at 1-888-988-7267 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's numbers for this recall are 17368, 17375, and 17376.
