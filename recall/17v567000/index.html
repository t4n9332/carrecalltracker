Chevrolet and GMC Trucks Have Faulty Seat Belt Retractors

NHTSA campaign 17V567000 · Reported 2017-09-14 · General Motors LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A seat belt that does not work properly in a crash can raise the chance of getting hurt. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Chevrolet Silverado 2500/3500 and GMC Sierra 2500/3500 trucks have front seat belts built with the wrong internal part. Because of this, the front seat belt retractors on both sides may not work the way they should if the truck is in a crash. These trucks do not meet the federal safety standard that applies to seat belts. Dealers will replace both front seat belt retractor assemblies.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included.
2. Contact GMC customer service at 1-888-988-7267 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020.
3. The repair, replacing both front seat belt retractors, is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:RETRACTOR
Summary
General Motors (GM) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Chevrolet Silverado 2500 and 3500 and GMC Sierra 2500 and 3500 trucks. The front seat belt retractor assemblies were built with the incorrect torsion bar. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 209, "Seat Belt Assemblies."
Safety risk
The seat belt retractors may not perform as intended during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace both front seat belt retractor assemblies, free of charge. The recall began March 24, 2021. Owners may contact GMC customer service at 1-888-988-7267 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's numbers for this recall are 17368, 17375, and 17376.
