Jeep Wrangler front control arm may separate from axle

NHTSA campaign 20V042000 · Reported 2020-01-24 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

critical How serious is it? About this label

A control arm separating from the axle is a serious crash risk that can happen without warning, and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so have it inspected as soon as possible.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2020 Jeep Wranglers, a bracket on the left side of the front axle and the weld holding it may be in the wrong position. That bracket anchors the lower control arm, a key part that locates the front wheel. If the bad weld lets go, the control arm can come apart from the axle. Losing that connection while driving can cause a crash. Dealers will inspect the front axle and replace it if needed.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Wrangler is included. 2. Contact your Jeep dealer or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 and reference recall W01. 3. Have the front axle inspected and replaced if necessary, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:CONTROL ARM:LOWER ARM
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2020 Jeep Wrangler vehicles. The left side lower control arm bracket and weld may not be correctly positioned.
Safety risk
The improper weld may allow the lower control arm to separate from the axle which can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the front axles, replacing them as necessary, free of charge. The recall began February 27, 2020. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is W01.
