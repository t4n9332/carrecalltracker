Jeep Wrangler Control Arm May Detach

NHTSA campaign 20V042000 · Reported Jan 24, 2020 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

The improper weld could allow the lower control arm to separate from the axle, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain 2020 Jeep Wrangler vehicles because the left side lower control arm bracket and its weld may not be positioned correctly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Chrysler dealer, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The dealer will inspect the front axles and replace them if necessary, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V042000. Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:CONTROL ARM:LOWER ARM
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2020 Jeep Wrangler vehicles. The left side lower control arm bracket and weld may not be correctly positioned.
Safety risk
The improper weld may allow the lower control arm to separate from the axle which can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the front axles, replacing them as necessary, free of charge. The recall began February 27, 2020. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is W01.
