Ford Bronco Sport, Escape: Fuel Injector Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 25V467000 · Reported Jul 11, 2025 · Ford Motor Company

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak near a hot engine or exhaust component increases the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Ford Bronco Sport and Escape vehicles with 1.5-liter engines, a fuel injector may crack and leak fuel inside the engine compartment. If this fuel leak occurs near a hot engine or exhaust part, it could ignite. Ford will update the engine control software as an interim fix, and will later replace the high-pressure fuel rail assembly and fuel injectors to fully correct the problem.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. As an interim repair, dealers will update the engine control software for free; the full repair (replacing the fuel rail assembly and injectors) will also be free once available.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V467000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2024 Bronco Sport and 2020-2022 Escape vehicles equipped with 1.5L engines. A fuel injector may crack and leak fuel inside the engine compartment.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source, such as hot engine or exhaust components, can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will update the engine control software. In addition, dealers will replace the high-pressure fuel rail assembly and fuel injectors, free of charge. As an interim repair, dealers will update the engine control software, free of charge. Interim owner notification letters notifying owners of the safety risk were mailed August 29, 2025. Second letters will be sent once the final remedy is available, anticipated in July 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S76. This recall expands and replaces previous recall numbers 22V-859, 24V187, and 25V165. Vehicles previously remedied under 22V-859, 24V187, and 25V165 will need to have the new final remedy performed once it is available.
