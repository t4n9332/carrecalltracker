INFINITI QX50 knee airbag cover may detach in crash

NHTSA campaign 18V593000 · Reported Sep 6, 2018 · Nissan North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the knee airbag cover comes off while the airbag is going off, it can increase the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Nissan is recalling certain 2019 INFINITI QX50 vehicles. The passenger-side knee airbag has a trim cover that is meant to split along a built-in tear seam when the airbag goes off. In a crash that happens in very cold temperatures, that cover may instead separate from the knee airbag unit unexpectedly. INFINITI will notify owners, and retailers will replace the passenger-side knee airbag unit free of charge. The recall began October 23, 2018.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included. 2. Contact an INFINITI retailer to schedule the replacement, or call INFINITI customer service at 1-800-662-6200. 3. The replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V593000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:KNEE BOLSTER
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2019 INFINITI QX50 vehicles. In the event of a crash in very cold temperatures, the trim cover may separate unexpectedly from the passenger-side knee air bag module instead of splitting at the trim cover tear seam as designed.
Safety risk
If the passenger-side knee air bag cover detaches during deployment, it can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
INFINITI will notify owners, and retailers will replace the passenger-side knee air bag module, free of charge. The recall began October 23, 2018. Owners may contact INFINITI customer service at 1-800-662-6200.
