Infiniti QX50 knee airbag cover may detach in cold

NHTSA campaign 18V593000 · Reported 2018-09-06 · Nissan North America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Infiniti QX50

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2019 INFINITI QX50 vehicles have a problem with the passenger-side knee airbag, the small airbag low in the dash that protects a front passenger's legs. In a crash during very cold weather, the trim cover over that airbag can break away completely instead of splitting along the seam built into it. A loose cover flying free during deployment can hurt the passenger it was meant to protect. Retailers will replace the passenger-side knee airbag at no charge.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious: the vehicle is safe to drive day to day, but in a cold-weather crash the airbag cover could cause injury to the front passenger.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your QX50 is covered. 2. Contact your INFINITI retailer or INFINITI customer service at 1-800-662-6200 to book the repair. 3. Have the passenger-side knee airbag replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:KNEE BOLSTER
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2019 INFINITI QX50 vehicles. In the event of a crash in very cold temperatures, the trim cover may separate unexpectedly from the passenger-side knee air bag module instead of splitting at the trim cover tear seam as designed.
Safety risk
If the passenger-side knee air bag cover detaches during deployment, it can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
INFINITI will notify owners, and retailers will replace the passenger-side knee air bag module, free of charge. The recall began October 23, 2018. Owners may contact INFINITI customer service at 1-800-662-6200.
