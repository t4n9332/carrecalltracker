Jeep Cherokee and Compass oil pump may fail, stalling engine

NHTSA campaign 17V670000 · Reported 2017-10-24 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Jeep Cherokee
  • 2018 Jeep Compass

What's wrong, in plain English

The housing around the oil pump may be cracked. The oil pump is what pushes oil through the engine, and if it quits, the engine loses oil pressure and can shut off while you are driving. A stall can happen at any speed and you lose power without much warning, which is dangerous in traffic. A dealer checks the oil pump and puts in a new one if yours has the problem.

How serious is it?

Serious: the notice does not tell you to stop driving, but an engine that stalls in traffic can lead to a crash, so have the oil pump inspected as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is part of recall T65.
2. Call your Jeep dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to book the oil pump inspection.
3. Have the pump inspected and replaced if necessary — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018 Jeep Cherokee and Compass vehicles equipped with 2.4L engines. The engines may have a cracked oil pump housing that can result in oil pump failure.
Safety risk
If the oil pump fails, the engine will stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the oil pump, replacing it as necessary, free of charge. The recall began December 5, 2017. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is T65.
