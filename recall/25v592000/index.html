Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 side airbags can burst apart

NHTSA campaign 25V592000 · Reported 2025-09-11 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 RAM 1500
  • 2018 RAM 2500
  • 2018 RAM 3500

What's wrong, in plain English

The curtain airbags that drop down over the side windows have a part inside that inflates them with gas. Because of a manufacturing mistake, that part can break apart when the airbag goes off. Instead of just cushioning you, it can send sharp metal pieces flying into the cab and hurt the people inside. Dealers will replace one or both side curtain airbags as needed, at no cost. Owner letters were mailed in September 2025.

How serious is it?

This is serious: in a crash the airbag could wound the people it is supposed to protect. The notice does not tell you to park the truck, but treat the repair as urgent.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is affected.
2. Call your Ram dealer or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403 and reference recall 83C, 91C or 92C.
3. Have the side curtain airbags replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN:INFLATOR
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2018 Ram 1500, Ram 2500, and Ram 3500 vehicles. The right and left side curtain air bag inflators may rupture due to a manufacturing defect.
Safety risk
An inflator rupture may result in sharp metal fragments striking occupants, resulting in injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace one or both side curtain air bags, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 25, 2025. Owners may contact FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's numbers for this recall are 83C, 91C, and 92C. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning September 18, 2025.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
