BMW F850GS motorcycle may have loose connecting rods

NHTSA campaign 18V830000 · Reported 2018-11-26 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 BMW F850GS

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW found that one 2019 F850GS motorcycle may have been built with engine connecting rods that were not installed correctly. These rods are internal engine parts that push the pistons up and down. If they are loose, they can work their way free over time, wreck the engine, and cause the bike to suddenly lose power while you are riding. This motorcycle was never sold, so the dealer is fixing it first.

How serious is it?

A sudden loss of engine power on a motorcycle is serious, but no rider is at risk here because the bike was never delivered and the dealer replaces the engine before sale.

What you should do

1. Look up your bike's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm whether it is one of the affected motorcycles.
2. If it is, contact your BMW Motorrad dealer and ask about recall campaign 18V830000 before riding it.
3. Have the engine replaced — the repair is completely free. BMW customer service: 1-800-525-7417.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling one 2019 F850GS motorcycle. The engine connecting rods may not have been installed properly, possibly causing them to become loose and disconnect over time.
Safety risk
Loose engine connecting rods can damage the engine, causing a loss of engine power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The motorcycle has not yet been sold, and the dealer will replace the engine, free of charge, prior to sale. The recall began December 7, 2018. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
