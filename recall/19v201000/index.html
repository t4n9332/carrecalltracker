Jeep Wrangler and Ram 1500 steering wheel may detach

NHTSA campaign 19V201000 · Reported 2019-03-14 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Jeep Wrangler
  • 2019 RAM 1500

What's wrong, in plain English

A short shaft connects your steering wheel to the rest of the steering system. On these vehicles that shaft was machined incorrectly and can break. If it breaks, the steering wheel can come off in your hands and you would have no way to steer, with no warning beforehand. A dealer will inspect the steering column and replace it if needed, at no cost. This one is worth getting checked right away rather than waiting.

How serious is it?

Losing the steering wheel while driving would almost certainly cause a crash and there is no warning first, so even though the notice does not order the vehicle parked, drive it only as far as the dealer.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered by recall V29.
2. Call your dealer or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 and ask for the soonest appointment.
3. Have the steering column inspected and replaced if necessary, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Jeep Wrangler and 2019 Ram 1500 vehicles. These vehicles have improperly machined steering column stub shafts that may break, possibly causing the steering wheel to detach from the steering column.
Safety risk
If the steering wheel detaches from the steering column, the driver may lose control of the vehicle, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the steering column, replacing it, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began April 5, 2019. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V29.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
