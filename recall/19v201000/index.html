Jeep Wrangler, Ram 1500 Steering Wheel Detachment Risk

NHTSA campaign 19V201000 · Reported Mar 14, 2019 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

critical How serious is it? About this label

If the steering wheel detaches, the driver can lose control of the vehicle, which greatly increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Jeep Wrangler and Ram 1500 vehicles have a part in the steering column, called the stub shaft, that was not machined correctly during the manufacturing process. Because of this, the part may break. If it breaks, the steering wheel could come loose and separate completely from the steering column, leaving the driver without the ability to steer the vehicle at all.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a dealer, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to have the steering column inspected.
3. Dealers will replace the steering column if needed, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V201000. Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Jeep Wrangler and 2019 Ram 1500 vehicles. These vehicles have improperly machined steering column stub shafts that may break, possibly causing the steering wheel to detach from the steering column.
Safety risk
If the steering wheel detaches from the steering column, the driver may lose control of the vehicle, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the steering column, replacing it, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began April 5, 2019. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V29.
