Rivian R1T and R1S front turn signals may fail

NHTSA campaign 26V508000 · Reported 2026-08-06 · Rivian Automotive, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Rivian R1S
  • 2025 Rivian R1T

What's wrong, in plain English

One or both front turn signals on these trucks and SUVs may not light up and flash when you signal. Drivers ahead of you and at intersections then get no warning that you are about to turn or change lanes. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule covering lamps and signals. You may not notice the problem from the driver's seat, since the front lamps are hard to see while you are driving.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the vehicle drives normally and is not unsafe to use, but a signal other drivers cannot see raises your risk of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — Rivian says VINs for this recall will be searchable there on August 31, 2026.
2. Contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261 (recall FSAM-1833); owner letters are expected to be mailed September 29, 2026.
3. Rivian inspects and replaces the front turn signals as necessary, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TURN SIGNAL
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2025-2026 R1S and R1T vehicles. One or both front turn signals may fail to illuminate and flash. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Turn signals that do not function properly increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will inspect and replace the front turn signals as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed September 29, 2026. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1833. This is an expansion of Recall 25V387. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov on August 31, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
