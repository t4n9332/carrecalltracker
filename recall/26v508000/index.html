Rivian R1S and R1T Front Turn Signals May Fail

NHTSA campaign 26V508000 · Reported 2026-08-06 · Rivian Automotive, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Turn signals that do not work properly increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Rivian R1S and R1T vehicles, one or both of the front turn signals may fail to light up and flash. Because the front turn signals may not work, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for lights and reflectors. Rivian will have the front turn signals inspected and replaced if needed, at no cost. This recall expands an earlier Rivian recall, number 25V387.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is covered.
2. Contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261, or a Rivian service center, to arrange the inspection.
3. The inspection and any replacement parts are free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TURN SIGNAL
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2025-2026 R1S and R1T vehicles. One or both front turn signals may fail to illuminate and flash. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Turn signals that do not function properly increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will inspect and replace the front turn signals as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed September 29, 2026. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1833. This is an expansion of Recall 25V387. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov on August 31, 2026.
