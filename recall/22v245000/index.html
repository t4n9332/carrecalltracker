Volkswagen Atlas Fuel Injector Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 22V245000 · Reported 2022-04-13 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak like this increases the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Volkswagen Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles, the long fuel injectors may have been assembled without the filters they are supposed to have inside them. Fuel injectors that are missing these filters can develop a fuel leak. A leaking fuel injector can allow fuel to escape from the fuel system, and Volkswagen says this increases the risk of a fire.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 to schedule a repair.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:INJECTORS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Atlas FL and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles. The long fuel injectors may have been assembled without filters.
Safety risk
Long fuel injectors with missing filters may leak fuel, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the long fuel injectors, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 20, 2022. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 24iD.
