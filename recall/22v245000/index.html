Volkswagen Atlas fuel injectors missing filters can leak fuel

NHTSA campaign 22V245000 · Reported 2022-04-13 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

Serious: a fuel leak can lead to a fire, so schedule the free repair promptly, though Volkswagen has not told owners to stop driving or park outdoors.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2021-2022 Volkswagen Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport SUVs left the factory with long fuel injectors that are missing their filters. The injectors are the parts that spray gasoline into the engine. Without the filter, an injector can leak fuel. Leaking gasoline near a hot engine can catch fire. Volkswagen mailed letters to owners in July 2022, and dealers replace the injectors.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your SUV is affected.
2. Call Volkswagen at 1-800-893-5298 or your dealer and mention recall 24iD.
3. Have the dealer replace the long fuel injectors — there is no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:INJECTORS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Atlas FL and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles. The long fuel injectors may have been assembled without filters.
Safety risk
Long fuel injectors with missing filters may leak fuel, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the long fuel injectors, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 20, 2022. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 24iD.
