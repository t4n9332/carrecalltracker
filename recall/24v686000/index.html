Rivian R1S steering stalk missing cruise control markings

NHTSA campaign 24V686000 · Reported 2024-09-13 · Rivian Automotive, LLC · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Rivian R1S

What's wrong, in plain English

The control stalk on the steering column — the one you use to turn cruise control on and off — may be missing its printed markings. Without those symbols, a driver may not know how to switch cruise control on or how to shut it off. The cruise control itself is not broken; only the labeling is missing. Rivian will inspect the control unit on the steering column and replace it if the markings are missing, at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Low — the vehicle is safe to drive, but not knowing how to switch cruise control off could lead to a crash, so make sure every driver knows the controls until it is fixed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4264 (recall FSAM-1532) to arrange the inspection. 3. Have the control unit replaced if needed — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
VEHICLE SPEED CONTROL:CRUISE CONTROL
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC. (Rivian) is recalling certain 2025 R1S vehicles. The steering column control module (SCCM) may be missing cruise control markings. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 101, "Control and Displays."
Safety risk
The driver may not know how to activate or deactivate the cruise control without the proper markings, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will inspect and replace the SCCMs as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 18, 2024. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4264. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1532.
