Rivian R1S Missing Cruise Control Markings

NHTSA campaign 24V686000 · Reported Sep 13, 2024 · Rivian Automotive, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Not knowing how to turn cruise control on or off could increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Rivian R1S vehicles, the steering column controls for cruise control may be missing the labels that show how to turn the system on and off. Federal safety rules require these markings so drivers know how to use cruise control correctly. Without them, a driver might not understand how to activate or deactivate cruise control while driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact a Rivian dealer or call Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4264. 3. The inspection and, if needed, replacement of the control module is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V686000. Official notice

Component
VEHICLE SPEED CONTROL:CRUISE CONTROL
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC. (Rivian) is recalling certain 2025 R1S vehicles. The steering column control module (SCCM) may be missing cruise control markings. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 101, "Control and Displays."
Safety risk
The driver may not know how to activate or deactivate the cruise control without the proper markings, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will inspect and replace the SCCMs as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 18, 2024. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4264. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1532.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
