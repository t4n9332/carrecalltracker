Ford Explorer and Lincoln SUVs lose rearview camera image

NHTSA campaign 25V159000 · Reported Mar 14, 2025 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

Losing the rearview camera image reduces how well the driver can see behind the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Explorer, Lincoln Aviator and Lincoln Corsair vehicles that were already repaired once under an earlier recall, because that earlier repair was not done correctly. The video output can stop working, so the rearview camera picture may not appear on the screen. Dealers will update the software in the module that processes the camera image, free of charge. Ford mailed owner notification letters on April 25, 2025.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included. 2. Contact a Ford or Lincoln dealer to schedule the software update, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. 3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V159000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2023 Explorer, Lincoln Aviator, and 2020-2022 Lincoln Corsair vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall number 23V-342. The video output may fail, preventing the rearview camera image from displaying.
Safety risk
Loss of the rearview camera image can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the Image Processing Module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 25, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S27.
