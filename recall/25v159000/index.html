What's wrong, in plain English

This is a follow-up to an earlier backup camera recall. Some of these Explorers, Aviators and Corsairs were repaired incorrectly the first time, so the problem can still happen: the video signal fails and the rearview camera picture does not show up on the screen when you shift into reverse. With no image, you cannot see people or objects behind you. Dealers will reload the software in the camera's image processing module.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the vehicle is safe to drive, but until the software is corrected you may lose the rear camera view when backing up, which raises the risk of a backing crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this second repair applies to your vehicle.
2. Call your Ford or Lincoln dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and ask for recall 25S27.
3. Have the free software update installed; owner letters were mailed April 25, 2025.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2023 Explorer, Lincoln Aviator, and 2020-2022 Lincoln Corsair vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall number 23V-342. The video output may fail, preventing the rearview camera image from displaying.
Safety risk
Loss of the rearview camera image can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the Image Processing Module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 25, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S27.
