Tesla Model Y missing its weight certification label

NHTSA campaign 26V315000 · Reported 2026-05-19 · Tesla, Inc. · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Tesla Model Y

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2025-2026 Tesla Model Y vehicles left the factory without the certification label installed. That is the sticker, normally found in the driver's door opening, that lists how much weight your vehicle can safely carry, counting passengers and cargo together. Without it, there is no simple way to know that limit, so you could load the car heavier than it was built to handle. An overloaded car is harder to steer and stop, which raises the chance of a crash.

How serious is it?

Low — the car drives normally and the notice places no restriction on it; just avoid heavy loads, roof cargo, or towing until the label is in place.

What you should do

1. Look for the label in your driver's door opening and check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls for campaign 26V315000.
2. Schedule service through the Tesla app or call Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752, referencing SB-26-19-002.
3. Tesla inspects the car and installs the certification label free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2025-2026 Model Y vehicles. The certification label may not have been installed. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of 49 C.F.R. Part 567, "Certification."
Safety risk
Missing weight information provided by the label may lead to unintentionally overloading the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla Service will inspect and install a certification label as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 17, 2026. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-26-19-002.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
