Toyota Floor Mat Label May Be Wrong

NHTSA campaign 19V503000 · Reported 2019-06-28 · Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC

low How serious is it? About this label

An incorrect load-carrying label could lead to unintentionally overloading the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Toyota vehicles with factory-installed floor mats have a load-carrying capacity label that may show incorrect information, so these vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard for tire and rim selection. An incorrect label could lead someone to unintentionally overload the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash. The distributor will provide a corrected label to place over the wrong one, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer, or call Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371, to get the corrected label.
3. The replacement label is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC (SET) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Toyota Camry, Corolla, Rav4, Sienna, and Yaris iA vehicles equipped with factory-installed floor mats. The load carrying capacity modification label may be incorrect. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
An incorrect label may lead to unintentionally overloading the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
SET will notify owners and provide a corrected label for placement over the inaccurate label, free of charge. The recall began August 23, 2019. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. SET's number for this recall is SET19A.
