Ram 1500 driver airbag may not be connected

NHTSA campaign 25V298000 · Reported 2025-05-08 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

An airbag that does not deploy in a crash means more risk of injury to the driver, though there is no instruction to stop driving and Chrysler says every affected truck has already been fixed.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain 2019-2024 Ram 1500 pickups. The driver's side airbag may not have been connected properly, so it might not inflate the way it is supposed to in a crash. Dealers fixed this by securing the airbag connectors at no charge, and Chrysler reports that all affected trucks have already been repaired. Owner notification letters were mailed May 27, 2025. It is still worth checking your own truck to confirm the work was done.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall still shows as open for your truck.
2. If it does, contact your Ram dealer or Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403 and reference recall 08C.
3. Have the airbag connectors secured - the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2019-2024 Ram 1500 vehicles. The driver's side air bag may not be connected properly, which can cause the air bag not to deploy as intended. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
A driver's side air bag that does not deploy as intended increases the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers secured the connectors for the air bag, free of charge. All vehicles have been remedied. Owner notification letters were mailed May 27, 2025. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 08C.
