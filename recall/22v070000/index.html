BMW X3 and X4 transmission fault can cause rollaway

NHTSA campaign 22V070000 · Reported 2022-02-09 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 BMW X3
  • 2022 BMW X4

What's wrong, in plain English

These 2022 BMWs, including the X3 xDrive30i and X4 xDrive30i, may have a control unit inside the automatic transmission that was not put together correctly. Fluid can leak inside the transmission, and that leak can let the vehicle roll away on its own after you park it. A rolling vehicle can hit a person or another car. Dealers replaced the faulty unit free of charge, and BMW says every vehicle in this recall has already been repaired.

How serious is it?

A vehicle that rolls away on its own can seriously hurt someone, but BMW reports the entire recall population has already been fixed, so most owners have nothing left to do beyond confirming their VIN is clear.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm the repair shows as completed on your vehicle.
2. If it still shows open, contact a BMW dealer to have the transmission mechatronics unit replaced free of charge.
3. For questions, call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2022 330i, 530i, X3 xDrive30i, and X4 xDrive30i vehicles. The mechatronics unit inside the transmission may not have been assembled properly, which could result in an internal leak.
Safety risk
An internal transmission leak can result in a vehicle rollaway, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the transmission mechatronics unit, free of charge. All of the vehicle population for this recall have been repaired. Owner notification letters were not mailed. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
