Nissan Rogue Hybrid Wiring Damage Can Blow Fuse

NHTSA campaign 21V839000 · Reported 2021-10-26 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A blown fuse may cause the engine to stall while driving, which raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Rogue Hybrid vehicles, a bracket for the engine's Electronic Control Module can come into contact with the engine wiring harness nearby. Nissan says this contact can damage the harness and cause a fuse to blow.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Nissan dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669, to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2018 Nissan Rogue
  • 2019 Nissan Rogue

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECU/ECM)
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Rogue Hybrid vehicles. Contact with the Electronic Control Module (ECM) bracket may damage the engine harness, causing a blown fuse.
Safety risk
A blown fuse may cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install a harness protector cover, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 16, 2021. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is R21B6.
