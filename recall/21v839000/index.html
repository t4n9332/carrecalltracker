Nissan Rogue Hybrid wiring damage can stall engine

NHTSA campaign 21V839000 · Reported 2021-10-26 · Nissan North America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Nissan Rogue
  • 2019 Nissan Rogue

What's wrong, in plain English

In these hybrids, the bundle of electrical wires for the engine can rub against the bracket that holds the engine's computer. That contact can wear through and damage the wiring, which blows a fuse. When the fuse blows, the engine can shut off while you are driving, which means losing power and speed in traffic. The repair adds a protective cover over the wiring so the bracket can no longer damage it, and it costs you nothing.

How serious is it?

Serious — an engine that stalls in traffic increases the risk of a crash, and although the notice does not restrict driving, the repair should not wait.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm campaign 21V839000 (Nissan recall R21B6).
2. Schedule the repair with a Nissan dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
3. The dealer installs a harness protector cover, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECU/ECM)
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Rogue Hybrid vehicles. Contact with the Electronic Control Module (ECM) bracket may damage the engine harness, causing a blown fuse.
Safety risk
A blown fuse may cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install a harness protector cover, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 16, 2021. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is R21B6.
