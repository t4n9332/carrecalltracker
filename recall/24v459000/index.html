Cadillac XT5 and XT6 dashboard reminder lights may not work

NHTSA campaign 24V459000 · Reported Jun 20, 2024 · General Motors, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Without the lit reminder, a driver could leave the hazard lights, high beams, or turn signals on without meaning to, and lights left on unintentionally can confuse other drivers and raise the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Cadillac XT5 and XT6 vehicles. On these cars, the instrument panel may not light up when the hazard warning lights, the high beams, or the turn signals are switched on, so the usual reminder on the dash is missing. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet two federal safety rules, one covering controls and displays and one covering lamps and reflectors. Dealers will update the instrument panel software.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included. 2. Contact a Cadillac dealer to arrange the software update, or call Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. 3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V459000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023-2025 Cadillac XT5 and 2024-2025 Cadillac XT6 vehicles. The instrument panel may not illuminate when the hazard warning lights, high beams, or turn signals are activated. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 101, "Controls and Displays" and 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Assoc. Equipment."
Safety risk
Without the illuminated reminder, the driver could fail to turn off the lights when no longer needed. Hazard warning lights, high beams, or turn signals that remain activated unintentionally can confuse other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the instrument panel software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 13, 2024. Owners may contact Cadillac's customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N242447940.
