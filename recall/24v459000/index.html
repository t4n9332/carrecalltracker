Cadillac XT5 and XT6 dashboard lights may not illuminate

NHTSA campaign 24V459000 · Reported 2024-06-20 · General Motors, LLC · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Cadillac XT5
  • 2024 Cadillac XT5
  • 2024 Cadillac XT6
  • 2025 Cadillac XT5
  • 2025 Cadillac XT6

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Cadillacs, the indicators on the instrument panel may not light up when you switch on the hazard lights, the high beams or a turn signal. Those small reminders are how you notice the lights are still running, so you could leave a turn signal or your high beams on without realizing it, which confuses other drivers around you. These vehicles do not meet two federal rules. Dealers update the instrument panel software at no charge.

How serious is it?

This is a low-level hazard and the vehicle is safe to keep driving, though other drivers can be confused by lights you left on, so get the free software update at your next convenient visit.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered.
2. Contact your Cadillac dealer, or Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006, and mention recall N242447940.
3. Have the instrument panel software updated — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023-2025 Cadillac XT5 and 2024-2025 Cadillac XT6 vehicles. The instrument panel may not illuminate when the hazard warning lights, high beams, or turn signals are activated. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 101, "Controls and Displays" and 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Assoc. Equipment."
Safety risk
Without the illuminated reminder, the driver could fail to turn off the lights when no longer needed. Hazard warning lights, high beams, or turn signals that remain activated unintentionally can confuse other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the instrument panel software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 13, 2024. Owners may contact Cadillac's customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N242447940.
