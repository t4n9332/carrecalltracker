Infiniti QX60 headlights aim too low at speed

NHTSA campaign 23V814000 · Reported 2023-12-04 · Nissan North America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Infiniti QX60
  • 2023 Infiniti QX60
  • 2024 Infiniti QX60

What's wrong, in plain English

These SUVs have headlights that aim themselves automatically as you drive. At certain higher speeds, the system can tilt the beams too far downward. That is the opposite of what you want at speed: the light lands close to the front of the vehicle instead of reaching far down the road, so you see less of what is coming. The dealer fixes it by changing settings in one of the vehicle's control modules.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious — the SUV is safe to drive, but be extra cautious driving at night at highway speeds until the fix is done.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Make a free appointment with your Infiniti dealer to have the module settings reconfigured. 3. Call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 and reference recall PC994 if you need help.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:LIGHTING CONTROL MODULE
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Infiniti QX60 vehicles. When driving at certain high speeds, the Adaptive Front-Light System (AFS) may incorrectly adjust the headlights downward.
Safety risk
Incorrectly adjusted headlights can reduce visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reconfigure the Intelligent Power Distribution Module (IPDM) settings, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 26, 2024. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PC994.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
