Toyota Highlander second-row seat backs may not lock

NHTSA campaign 26V128000 · Reported 2026-03-05 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Toyota Highlander
  • 2021 Toyota Highlander Hybrid
  • 2022 Toyota Highlander
  • 2022 Toyota Highlander Hybrid
  • 2023 Toyota Highlander
  • 2023 Toyota Highlander Hybrid
  • 2024 Toyota Highlander
  • 2025 Toyota Highlander

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Highlanders, the second-row seat backs may fail to lock in place after someone adjusts them. That means a seat back that looks set may not actually be latched. In a crash, an unlocked seat back may not hold the person sitting there the way it should, which could lead to injury. Dealers will replace the springs inside the seat back recliner so the seat locks properly. Toyota is handling this recall in stages, so your repair may be scheduled later.

How serious is it?

An unlocked seat back may not protect a second-row passenger in a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but be careful with who rides back there until it is fixed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Highlander is included. 2. Call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 or your dealer about recall 26TB06 or 26TA06. 3. Schedule the free seat repair when parts are available for your vehicle.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:MID/REAR ASSEMBLY:RECLINER
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2021-2025 Highlander and Highlander Hybrid vehicles. The second-row seat backs may fail to lock into position during seat back adjustment.
Safety risk
A seat back that fails to lock may not properly restrain an occupant during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the return springs in the seat back recliner assemblies, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed September 9, 2026. This is a phased recall. Owners may contact Toyota’s customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 26TB06 and 26TA06.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
