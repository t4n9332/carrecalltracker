Chevrolet Traverse side curtain airbags may be improperly attached

NHTSA campaign 21V246000 · Reported 2021-04-08 · General Motors, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Chevrolet Traverse
  • 2019 Chevrolet Traverse

What's wrong, in plain English

The side curtain airbags are the long bags that drop down over the windows in a side crash or rollover. On these vehicles they may not be fastened to the body correctly, because the weld nuts holding them can be out of line. A bag that is not held firmly may not work the way it is supposed to. A dealer inspects the mounting and realigns or replaces those nuts. Owner letters went out in May 2021.

How serious is it?

It matters in a crash rather than day to day, and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so you can keep driving while you schedule the free inspection.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Call your Chevrolet dealer, or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020, and ask for recall N202321200.
3. Have the inspection and any repair done — it is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2010-2015, 2017 Buick Enclave, 2010-2019 Chevrolet Traverse, and 2011-2016 GMC Acadia vehicles. The side curtain air bags may not be properly attached.
Safety risk
Unsecured side curtain air bags may not perform as intended, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and, if necessary, realign or replace the weld nuts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 24, 2021. Owners may contact Buick customer service at 1-800-521-7300, Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020, and GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N202321200.
