BMW, Mini Brake System May Lose Power Assist

NHTSA campaign 24V104000 · Reported 2024-02-12 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A loss of power brake assist can make it take longer to stop the vehicle, and malfunctioning ABS or stability control can lead to a loss of vehicle control, both of which can increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The integrated brake system in these vehicles may malfunction. This can cause a loss of power brake assist, which affects how the brakes work. It can also cause the Antilock Brake System and the Dynamic Stability Control system to stop functioning properly. To fix this, dealers will replace the integrated brake system, and this repair is free of charge for owners.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a BMW dealer (1-800-525-7417) or Rolls-Royce dealer (1-877-877-3735).
3. The repair (replacing the integrated brake system) is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRONIC STABILITY CONTROL (ESC)
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC. (BMW) is recalling certain 2023-2025 BMW, MINI, and Rolls-Royce vehicles. Please see the recall report for a complete list of models and model years. The integrated brake (IB) system may malfunction and result in a loss of power brake assist or cause the Antilock Brake (ABS) and Dynamic Stability Control (DSC) systems to not function properly.
Safety risk
A loss of power brake assist can extend the distance required to stop the vehicle. Additionally, malfunctioning ABS and/or DSC systems can cause a loss of vehicle control. Either of these scenarios can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the integrated brake system, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed between June and December 2024. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 or Rolls Royce customer service at 1-877-877-3735.
