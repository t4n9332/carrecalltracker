Ram ProMaster driver seat belt warning may not work

NHTSA campaign 24V129000 · Reported Feb 22, 2024 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the seat belt reminder does not work properly, the seat belt may not be used correctly, which raises the chance of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain Ram ProMaster vans. The warning light and chime that remind the driver to buckle up may not turn on the way they are supposed to. Because of this, these vans do not meet the federal safety rule covering occupant crash protection. Dealers will look at the driver's seat belt buckle and replace it if needed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your van is included. 2. Contact a Chrysler or Ram dealer to schedule the inspection, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall number 02B). 3. The inspection and any buckle replacement are free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V129000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:BUCKLE ASSEMBLY
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Ram Promaster vehicles. The driver seat belt warning light and audible chime may not activate as intended. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
A malfunctioning seat belt warning system can result in improper seat belt use, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the driver seat belt buckle as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 9, 2024. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 02B.
