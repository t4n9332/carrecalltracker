Ram 1500 through 5500 driver airbag may not deploy

NHTSA campaign 25V765000 · Reported 2025-11-06 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 RAM 1500
  • 2021 RAM 2500
  • 2021 RAM 3500
  • 2021 RAM 5500
  • 2022 RAM 3500
  • 2022 RAM 4500
  • 2022 RAM 5500

What's wrong, in plain English

A control part inside the steering column may keep the driver's airbag from working the way it should. If you are in a crash, the airbag in the steering wheel may not inflate, leaving you without that protection. These trucks do not meet the federal rule for crash protection because of it. Nothing about the way the truck drives will feel different, so you cannot tell on your own whether yours is affected.

How serious is it?

Serious if you are in a crash, since the driver may be left without airbag protection; the notice does not restrict driving, so you can drive while waiting for the free repair.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — Ram VINs for campaign 25V765000 became searchable November 13, 2025.
2. Call your Ram dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403 (recall 14C), to book an appointment.
3. Have the steering column control module replaced at no cost.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Ram 1500, Ram 2500, Ram 3500, Ram 4500, and Ram 5500 vehicles. The steering column control module may cause the driver's side air bag not to deploy as intended. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
A driver's side air bag that does not deploy as intended increases the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the steering column control module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning December 11, 2025. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 14C. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning November 13, 2025.
