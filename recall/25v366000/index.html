Mercedes-Benz models make wrong pedestrian warning sound in reverse

NHTSA campaign 25V366000 · Reported Jun 5, 2025 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

A vehicle making the wrong sound may fail to warn pedestrians that it is reversing, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Mercedes-Benz is recalling certain electric and plug-in models, including the EQS SUV and S-Class. These vehicles have a system that makes an artificial sound outside the car so that people nearby can hear it coming. A software problem can make the car play the wrong sound while it is backing up, which means the vehicles do not meet the federal rule on minimum sound for hybrid and electric vehicles. Dealers will update the software free of charge.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer, or MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372 (recall number 2025070005), to book an appointment.
3. Have the dealer update the sound system software — the repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V366000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:WARNINGS:EXTERNAL/PEDESTRIAN ALERT
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2022-2025 S-Class, EQE SUV, EQE, EQS SUV and EQS vehicles. Please see the recall report for a complete list of models. Software in the Acoustic Vehicle Alerting System (AVAS) may cause an incorrect external pedestrian warning sound while reversing. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 141, "Minimum Sound Requirements for Hybrid Electric Vehicles."
Safety risk
A vehicle that makes an incorrect sound may fail to alert pedestrians of a reversing vehicle, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the AVAS software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 1, 2025. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2025070005.
