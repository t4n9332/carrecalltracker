Toyota Camry and Lexus fuel pumps may stall engine

NHTSA campaign 25V028000 · Reported 2025-01-22 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Toyota Camry

What's wrong, in plain English

The low-pressure fuel pump sits inside the gas tank and sends gasoline to the engine. On certain 2018 Camry, 2019 Lexus NX300 and 2019 RX350L vehicles, that pump can fail. When it does, the engine can stall, which may happen while you are moving — in an intersection, merging, or on the highway. Toyota and Lexus dealers will replace the whole fuel pump assembly for free. Owner letters for this recall were mailed in March 2025.

How serious is it?

An engine that stalls in traffic can lead to a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so keep driving is acceptable, but book the free repair soon.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is covered.
2. Call your Toyota or Lexus dealer, or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331, and mention recall 25TA01 or 25LA01.
3. Have the fuel pump assembly replaced at no charge, and report any stalling or rough running to the dealer.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018 Camry, 2019 Lexus NX300, and 2019 RX350L vehicles. The low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail.
Safety risk
Fuel pump failure can cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 12, 2025. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 25TA01 and 25LA01.
