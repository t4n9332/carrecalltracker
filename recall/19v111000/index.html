Porsche Cayenne backup camera image is slow

NHTSA campaign 19V111000 · Reported 2019-02-21 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

A rearview camera display that does not function as designed can reduce the driver's view of what is behind the car, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2019 Porsche Cayenne and Cayenne S models have a software problem that makes the backup camera picture slow to appear or slow to react on the dashboard screen. When you shift into reverse, you may not see what is behind the vehicle right away. That means you could start backing up without a clear view of a child, a pet, or an object behind you. A dealer will load updated software to fix how quickly the camera image shows up.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Cayenne is affected. 2. Contact your Porsche dealer or Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243, recall AKA0. 3. Have the free software update installed.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:REARVIEW MIRRORS/DEVICES:INTERIOR
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc (Porsche) is recalling certain 2019 Cayenne and Cayenne S vehicles. A software failure may cause the rearview camera image display to be delayed in responding. As such, these vehicles fail to comply to Federal Motor Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 111, "Rearview Mirrors."
Safety risk
A rearview camera display that does not function as designed can reduce the driver's view of what is behind the car, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Porsche will notify owners, and dealers will update the related software, free of charge. The recall began April 19, 2019. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AKA0.
