Volkswagen Jetta and Tiguan front seats missing bolts

NHTSA campaign 21V038000 · Reported 2021-02-01 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Volkswagen Jetta
  • 2021 Volkswagen Tiguan

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Volkswagen Tiguan Long Wheelbase and Jetta cars left the factory with bolts missing from the tracks that hold the front seats to the floor. Those tracks are what let the seat slide forward and back and then lock in place. Without the bolts, a front seat can slide freely instead of staying put. In a crash, a seat that moves does not hold the person in it the way it should. A dealer will look at the seat tracks and put in any missing bolts.

How serious is it?

This is serious in a crash because a loose seat can increase injury, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included. 2. Call a Volkswagen dealer or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall 72L6) to book an inspection. 3. Have the dealer inspect the seat tracks and install the bolts — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2021 Volkswagen Tiguan Long Wheelbase and Jetta vehicles. Bolts may be missing from the front seat tracks.
Safety risk
The seat can move front and back without restriction if bolts are missing, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the front seat tracks and, as necessary, install the bolts, free of charge. The recall began February 18, 2021. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 72L6.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
