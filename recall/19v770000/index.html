Mazda CX-9 Tires May Lose Tread While Driving

NHTSA campaign 19V770000 · Reported Oct 28, 2019 · Mazda North American Operations

high How serious is it? About this label

A tire that loses its tread can make the vehicle less stable and increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some CX-9 SUVs were built with specific Sumitomo Falken tires that may have been manufactured using an incorrect rubber compound. Because of this, the tread on the tire can separate and detach from the tire itself, rather than remaining attached.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Mazda dealer (1-800-222-5500, Option 4) to have all four tires inspected.
3. Tires will be replaced as necessary, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V770000. Official notice

Component
TIRES:TREAD/BELT
Summary
Mazda North American Operations (Mazda) is recalling certain 2019 CX-9 vehicles equipped with Sumitomo Falken ZIEX CT50 A/S tires, size P255/50R20 104V, having date code 1619. An incorrect rubber compound may have been used in the tire's manufacturing, allowing the tread to separate and detach.
Safety risk
A detached tire tread can decrease vehicle stability and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Mazda will notify owners, and dealers will inspect all four tire serial numbers, replacing the tires as necessary, free of charge. The recall began December 4, 2019. Owners may contact Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500, Option 4. Mazda's number for this recall is 4019J.
