What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Lincoln Navigator vehicles because the driver seat’s obstacle detection software may malfunction at low speeds. This can cause the seat to reverse unexpectedly while someone is driving. The unexpected movement may affect the driver’s ability to control the vehicle. The recall repair updates the seat control software, either at a dealer or through a wireless update.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Lincoln dealer about the recall; Ford customer service is available at 1-866-436-7332.
3. Get the free seat control software update through a dealer or a wireless update.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V120000. Official notice

Component
SEATS:FRONT ASSEMBLY:POWER ADJUST
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2026 Lincoln Navigator vehicles. The obstacle detection software for the driver seat may malfunction at low speeds, resulting in the seat reversing unexpectedly while driving.
Safety risk
Unexpected movement of the driver’s seat may affect the driver’s ability to control the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The seat control module software will be updated by a dealer, or through an over-the-air (OTA) update, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 13, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S11. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on March 5, 2026.
