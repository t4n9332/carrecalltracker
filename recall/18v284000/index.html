Volkswagen Atlas fuel tank may leak gasoline

NHTSA campaign 18V284000 · Reported 2018-05-02 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Volkswagen Atlas

What's wrong, in plain English

Because of a mistake during manufacturing, the fuel tank on certain Volkswagen Atlas vehicles may leak gasoline. The tank doesn't meet the federal standard for fuel system integrity. Leaking fuel near anything hot or sparking can catch fire. Volkswagen dealers will replace the fuel tank entirely, free of charge. This recall is an expansion of an earlier one, so some vehicles not covered before are included now. Owners were notified starting in June 2018.

How serious is it?

A fuel leak near an ignition source can cause a fire, which is serious; the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but this should be repaired right away.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Atlas is included. 2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer or VW customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall 20AW) to schedule the work. 3. Have the fuel tank replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain Volkswagen Atlas vehicles. The fuel tank on these vehicles may leak fuel due to a manufacturing error. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 301, "Fuel System Integrity."
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel tank, free of charge. The recall began June 22, 2018. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 20AW. Note: This recall is an expansion of recall 17V-766.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
