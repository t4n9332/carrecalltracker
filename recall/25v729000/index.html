Ford Expedition, F-Series moonroof wind deflector can fly off

NHTSA campaign 25V729000 · Reported 2025-10-24 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford Expedition
  • 2021 Lincoln Navigator
  • 2022 Ford Expedition
  • 2022 Ford F-250 SD
  • 2022 Ford F-450 SD
  • 2022 Lincoln Navigator

What's wrong, in plain English

The clips that hold the wind deflector to the moonroof frame may not be attached well enough. The deflector is the small screen at the front edge of the moonroof opening that cuts down wind noise. If the clips let go, the deflector can come off the vehicle and land on the road, where it becomes a hazard for you and for drivers behind you. Dealers will inspect it and repair or reattach it as needed, free of charge.

How serious is it?

Moderate — a detached deflector becomes road debris that could cause a crash for you or someone behind you; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — these VINs became searchable October 30, 2025 (Ford's recall number is 25SB7).
2. Schedule an inspection with a Ford or Lincoln dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. The inspection and any needed repair to the moonroof wind deflector are free.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:SUN/MOON ROOF ASSEMBLY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Expedition, Lincoln Navigator, 2021-2023 F-150, 2022 F-250 SD, F-350 SD, and F-450 SD vehicles. The moonroof wind deflector clips may be inadequately attached to the moonroof frame, which can result in the wind deflector detaching from the vehicle.
Safety risk
A detached wind deflector can become a road hazard and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and repair the moonroof wind deflector as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 10, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25SB7. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning October 30, 2025.
