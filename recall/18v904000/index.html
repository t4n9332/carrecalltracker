Volkswagen Atlas and Jetta rear coil springs can break

NHTSA campaign 18V904000 · Reported 2018-12-19 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Volkswagen Atlas
  • 2019 Volkswagen Atlas
  • 2019 Volkswagen Jetta

What's wrong, in plain English

The coil springs in the rear suspension — the large metal coils that hold the car up and soak up bumps — can crack and break earlier than they should. If a spring breaks while you are driving, a piece of it can cut into a rear tire. A tire that goes flat at speed can make the car hard to control. Dealers will replace the rear axle coil springs on affected cars at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious — a broken spring can damage a tire and cause you to lose control, so if your car has not had this repair yet, get it scheduled promptly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — this recall dates back to January 2019, so first confirm whether the work was already done.
2. Contact your Volkswagen dealer, or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall 42J2/42J3).
3. Have the rear coil springs replaced free of charge if the repair is still outstanding.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR:SPRINGS:COIL SPRINGS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Volkswagen Atlas, 2015 Volkswagen Golf SportWagen, and 2019 Volkswagen Jetta vehicles. The rear coil springs may prematurely fracture.
Safety risk
If a coil spring fractures while driving, it may damage a rear tire causing a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Volkswagen has notified owners, and dealers will replace the rear axle coil springs, free of charge. The recall began January 31, 2019. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 42J2/42J3.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
