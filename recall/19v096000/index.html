BMW X5 driver seat and belt bolts possibly loose

NHTSA campaign 19V096000 · Reported 2019-02-13 · BMW of North America, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 BMW X5

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2019 BMW X5 xDrive40i and xDrive50i models, the bolts holding down the driver's seat and/or the driver's seat belt anchor may not have been tightened enough at the factory. Those bolts are what keep the seat and the belt firmly attached to the floor during a crash. If they are loose, the seat or belt may not hold the driver the way it is designed to in an accident. A dealer checks each bolt and tightens it correctly.

How serious is it?

You may not notice anything wrong day to day, but loose bolts raise the risk of injury in a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so it is safe to drive while you arrange the inspection.

What you should do

1. Confirm your vehicle is included by entering your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact your BMW dealer or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 to schedule the inspection.
3. Have the dealer torque the seat and seat belt bolts to specification — the work is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2019 X5 xDrive40i and xDrive50i vehicles. The attachment bolts for the driver's seat and/or seat belt may not have been properly tightened.
Safety risk
The improperly tightened bolts can increase the risk of injury in the event of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the attachment bolts for the driver's seat and seat belt, and torque them to the correct specifications, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began April 9, 2019. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
