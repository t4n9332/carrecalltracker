Ram ProMaster side airbag sensor may crack in doors

NHTSA campaign 17V712000 · Reported 2017-11-14 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 RAM Promaster

What's wrong, in plain English

The wrong rivet was used to attach the crash sensor inside each front door of these Ram ProMaster vans. Because of that, the sensor's housing can crack. That sensor is what tells the side airbags to inflate, so if it is broken the side airbags may not go off properly in a side-impact crash, leaving people less protected. The dealer will replace both front door sensors and attach them with the correct rivets.

How serious is it?

Serious — a side airbag that does not deploy correctly can mean worse injuries in a side crash, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving the van.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your van is affected.
2. Contact a Ram dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and mention recall T64.
3. Schedule the free replacement of both front door impact sensors.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018 Ram ProMaster vehicles. An incorrect rivet may have been used to attach the front door impact sensor which can cause the sensor housing to fracture.
Safety risk
A fractured sensor could affect the deployment of the standard supplemental side airbags during certain side impact crashes, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace both front door impact pressure sensors, securing them with the correct rivets, free of charge. The recall began January 11 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is T64.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
