Ford Expedition, Lincoln Navigator Engine Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 22V346000 · Reported 2022-05-17 · Ford Motor Company

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fire in the engine can put you at risk of injury whether the vehicle is parked or being driven. Owners are told to park their vehicle outside and away from structures and other vehicles until the repair is done.

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Expedition and Navigator vehicles can develop a fire in the engine compartment. This can happen while the vehicle is being driven or while it is parked, even when the engine has been switched off. Ford will fix the issue by inspecting and, if necessary, replacing a part in the electrical system connected to the battery, all free of charge to the owner.

What you should do

1. Park your vehicle outside and away from structures and other vehicles until the repair is completed.
2. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
3. Contact your Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, to schedule the free repair.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 Expedition and Lincoln Navigator vehicles. An engine compartment fire may occur while driving or parked, even with the ignition in the "Off" position.
Safety risk
A vehicle fire increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park their vehicles outside and away from structures and other vehicles, until the remedy is performed. Dealers will inspect and replace the battery junction box, as necessary, and remove a ground wire. On models equipped with standard-duty radiator fan motors, dealers will also install an auxiliary electrical box with a wire jumper. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Interim owner notification letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed May 25, 2022. Owners will receive a second letter once the remedy is available. Currently the remedy is available for vehicles equipped with heavy-duty radiator fan motors, and those letters were mailed July 21, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S36.
