Lincoln Nautilus windows may not reverse when blocked

NHTSA campaign 24V597000 · Reported Aug 9, 2024 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

A closing window may press with too much force and pinch the driver or a passenger before it pulls back, which the notice says increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain 2024 Lincoln Nautilus and 2024 Ranger vehicles. When the windows are closed using the feature that shuts them all at once, a window may fail to stop and go back down after it senses something in the way. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal rule for power operated window systems. Dealers will update the software in the driver and passenger door modules at no charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Lincoln dealer to have the door module software updated, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall number 24C24).
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V597000. Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:POWER WINDOW DEVICES AND CONTROLS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2024 Ranger and 2024 Lincoln Nautilus vehicles. When closing the windows with the global closing feature, the windows may not automatically reverse after detecting an object. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 118, "Power-Operated Window Systems."
Safety risk
A closing window may exert excessive force by pinching a driver or passenger before retracting, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will update the driver door module (DDM) and the passenger door module (PDM) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 13, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24C24.
