Lincoln Nautilus windows may pinch before reversing when closing

NHTSA campaign 24V597000 · Reported 2024-08-09 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Lincoln Nautilus

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2024 Nautilus SUVs (and 2024 Rangers), the one-touch feature that closes all the windows at once may not work the way it should. Normally a window that bumps into something should stop and go back down. These windows may keep pressing before they reverse, so a hand, arm or a child's finger can get pinched hard. That does not meet the federal rule for power windows. A dealer will update the door software.

How serious is it?

Nobody is in danger unless someone has a hand or arm in the window opening, so the vehicle is safe to keep driving — just be careful using the all-windows close with children or pets aboard until the update is installed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact your Lincoln or Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and reference recall number 24C24.
3. Have the driver and passenger door module software updated free of charge — letters were mailed September 13, 2024.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:POWER WINDOW DEVICES AND CONTROLS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2024 Ranger and 2024 Lincoln Nautilus vehicles. When closing the windows with the global closing feature, the windows may not automatically reverse after detecting an object. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 118, "Power-Operated Window Systems."
Safety risk
A closing window may exert excessive force by pinching a driver or passenger before retracting, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will update the driver door module (DDM) and the passenger door module (PDM) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 13, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24C24.
