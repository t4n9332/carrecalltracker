Porsche manual lacks clear child seat installation instructions

NHTSA campaign 19V875000 · Reported Dec 11, 2019 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

low How serious is it? About this label

A child seat that is not installed properly increases the risk of injury to the person in it in the event of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The owner's manual in these Porsche cars does not give enough detail about how to properly install a child restraint system, meaning a child safety seat. Federal rules covering child seat anchorages require clear instructions, and because the manual falls short, these cars do not meet that standard. Porsche is contacting owners, and dealers will provide a supplement to the owner's manual containing proper child seat installation instructions.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Porsche dealer to get the owner's manual supplement, or call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243.
3. The supplement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V875000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:OWNERS/SERVICE/OTHER MANUAL
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2014 Cayman and Cayman S, 2014-2016 911 GT3, 2016 911 GT3 RS and 911 R, 2018 911 GT3 and 911 GT2 RS and 2019 911 GT3 RS, 911 GT3, and 911 GT2 RS vehicles. The Child Restraint System (CRS) instructions within the Owner's Manual may not provide enough specificity for proper installation. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 225, "Child Restraint Anchorage Systems."
Safety risk
Improper CRS installation increases the risk of an injury to the occupant in the event of a crash.
Remedy
Porsche will notify owners, and dealers will provide a an Owner's Manual supplement with proper CRS installation instructions, free of charge. The recall began February 7, 2020. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AKC0.
