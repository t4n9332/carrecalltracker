Ford Explorer backup camera image goes blank or scrambled

NHTSA campaign 24V188000 · Reported 2024-03-08 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Ford Explorer

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2020 Ford Explorers have a poor electrical connection at the rearview camera. Because of it, the camera picture on your dashboard screen can go blank or turn distorted while you are backing up, and the problem may come and go without warning. Federal safety rules require a working backup camera view, so these vehicles do not meet the standard. A Ford dealer will install a new camera at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the vehicle is safe to drive, but a blank or garbled screen hides what is behind you and raises the chance of a backing crash, so use your mirrors and look over your shoulder until it is repaired.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Explorer is included in recall 24V188000 (Ford calls it 20C19).
2. Call your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and book an appointment to have the rearview camera replaced.
3. Have the work done — the new camera and labor are free, even if you bought the vehicle used.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020 Explorer vehicles. Due to a poor electrical connection, the rearview camera may intermittently display a blank or distorted image. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview camera that displays a blank or distorted image can reduce the drivers view of what is behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the rearview camera, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 4, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 20C19. This recall is an expansion of NHTSA recall numbers 20V-575 and 22V-252.
