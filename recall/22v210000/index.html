What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Chevrolet Traverse and Buick Enclave vehicles, the electrical connector for the wiring harness leading to the roof rail (side curtain) air bag may have terminals that don't fit together correctly. If the connection isn't right, the roof rail air bag on that side of the vehicle may not deploy the way it's supposed to in a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Buick customer service at 1-800-521-7300 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 to schedule the repair.
3. The repair (replacing the harness connector) is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V210000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2020-2022 Chevrolet Traverse and 2021-2022 Buick Enclave vehicles. The harness connector to the left or right side roof rail air bag may have incompatible electrical terminals, which could prevent the air bags from deploying in a crash.
Safety risk
Roof rail air bags that do not deploy as intended increase the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the harness connector to the roof rail air bags, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on April 29, 2022. Owners may contact Buick customer service at 1-800-521-7300 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N212352140.
