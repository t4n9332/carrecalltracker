Mitsubishi Outlander Center Pillar May Be Weak

NHTSA campaign 21V821000 · Reported 2021-10-18 · Mitsubishi Motors North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

In a crash, the weakened vehicle structure could increase the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On one 2022 Outlander, a software error in a welding robot may have caused the vehicle's center pillar to be welded incorrectly. Because of this, the vehicle does not meet federal safety standards for side-impact protection, seat belt anchoring, and roof strength. If the vehicle is in a crash, this weaker body structure could increase the risk of injury.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Mitsubishi customer service at 1-888-648-7820.
3. The vehicle will be repurchased or replaced, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:ROOF AND PILLARS
Summary
Mitsubishi Motors North America, Inc. (MMNA) is recalling one 2022 Outlander vehicle. A software programming error with a welding robot may have caused the vehicle's center pillar to be welded incorrectly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) numbers 214, "Side Impact Protection" 210, "Seat Belt Assembly Anchorages" and 216, "Roof Crush Resistance."
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, the reduced strength of the vehicle body may increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
MMNA will repurchase and replace the affected vehicle, free of charge. Owners may contact MMNA customer service at 1-888-648-7820. MMNA's number for this recall is SR-21-009.
