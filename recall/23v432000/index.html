Toyota Camry lug nuts may loosen and wheel detach

NHTSA campaign 23V432000 · Reported 2023-06-15 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · critical severity

⚠ NHTSA advises not driving this vehicle until it is repaired. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Toyota Camry
  • 2023 Toyota Camry Hybrid

What's wrong, in plain English

The nuts that hold the wheels on these Camry and Camry Hybrid cars may not be tight enough. A loose wheel can work its way off while you are driving, which can make you lose control of the car. Toyota says not to drive these vehicles at all until they are fixed. A dealer will check and tighten every lug nut, and replace any parts that were damaged, at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Extremely serious — a wheel can come off with no warning, so Toyota says do not drive the vehicle until the repair is done.

What you should do

1. Stop driving the car and check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm it is affected.
2. Call a Toyota dealer or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 (recall 23TA05) and explain you were told not to drive it — ask about towing.
3. Have the dealer tighten the lug nuts and replace any damaged parts, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
WHEELS:LUGS/NUTS/BOLTS/STUDS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2023 Camry and Camry Hybrid vehicles. The lug nuts that attach the wheels to the vehicle may be loose, which can result in wheel detachment.
Safety risk
A wheel that detaches from a moving vehicle can cause a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until the repair has been performed. Dealers will inspect and tighten the lug nuts, and replace any damaged components, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed on or before August 14, 2023. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 23TA05.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
