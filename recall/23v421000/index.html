Nissan Sentra water leak can corrode electrical parts

NHTSA campaign 23V421000 · Reported 2023-06-12 · Nissan North America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Nissan Sentra

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2022 Sentra cars, the seal near the base of the windshield on the driver's side may be missing or installed wrong. Water can get inside the car and rust the electrical wiring. That damage can knock out important systems — the windshield wipers, outside lights, or the airbags may stop working, and you might be able to shift out of Park without pressing the brake. Damaged wiring can also short out and start a fire.

How serious is it?

Serious — failed airbags, lights, wipers, or an unexpected shift out of Park can lead to a crash, and a short circuit could cause a fire; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Sentra is covered.
2. Call your Nissan dealer or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 to schedule the inspection.
3. Have the dealer reseal the cowl and replace any corroded wiring — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2022 Sentra vehicles. The driver's side cowl area may have a missing or improper seal, which can allow water to leak inside the vehicle and corrode electrical components.
Safety risk
Corrosion of the electrical components could result in failure of various electrical systems, such as loss of wiper motor function or exterior lighting, inoperative air bags, or the ability to shift into gear without applying the brake pedal. Any of these scenarios can increase the risk of a crash. In addition, an electrical short-circuit may occur, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and, if necessary, reseal the driver's side cowl. If moisture or corrosion is found, the main harness and engine room harness will be replaced. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed July 26, 2023. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 1-800-867-7669.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
