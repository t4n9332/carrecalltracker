Jeep Wrangler right rear door may open unexpectedly

NHTSA campaign 18V786000 · Reported 2018-11-08 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A rear door that can swing open on its own is a real injury risk for passengers; the notice does not say to stop driving, but avoid seating anyone there until it is repaired.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some four-door Wranglers, the latch for the right rear passenger door may have been installed in the wrong spot. When it is out of position, the door may not close and lock into place the way it should. That means the door could pop open on its own, including while the vehicle is moving. Anyone sitting in that rear seat could be hurt if the door opens unexpectedly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Wrangler is covered. 2. Call your Jeep dealer or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall UB6) for an appointment. 3. The dealer repairs the right rear door latch reinforcement free of charge.

Official notice

Component
LATCHES/LOCKS/LINKAGES:DOORS:LATCH
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018 Jeep Wrangler four-door vehicles. The right rear passenger door latch may not be in the correct location, possibly preventing the door from latching properly.
Safety risk
If the right rear passenger door opens unexpectedly, it can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will repair the right rear door latch reinforcement, free of charge. The recall begin December 6, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is UB6.
