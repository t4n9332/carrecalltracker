Honda CR-V Hybrid fuel hose can leak gasoline

NHTSA campaign 25V378000 · Reported 2025-06-05 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak creates a real fire risk, so get this repaired right away, and if you smell gasoline or see fuel under the car, stop driving it and call the dealer.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2025 Honda CR-V Hybrids, the hose that carries gasoline from the tank toward the engine may not have been installed correctly at the factory, which lets fuel leak out. Leaking gasoline only needs a spark or a hot surface to catch fire. You might notice a strong gas smell, or wet spots and drips under the vehicle. Dealers will install a new fuel hose along with a cover for the connector so it seals properly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 25V378000 applies (Honda's number TLS).
2. Call your Honda dealer or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 and ask for the fuel hose recall repair as soon as possible.
3. Have the new fuel feed hose and connector cover installed free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2025 Honda CR-V Hybrid vehicles. The fuel feed hose may have been improperly installed, allowing fuel to leak. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 301, "Fuel System Integrity."
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will install a new fuel feed hose and connector cover, free of charge. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is TLS.
