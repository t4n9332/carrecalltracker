Subaru Impreza headlight reflectors do not reflect enough light

NHTSA campaign 22V624000 · Reported 2022-08-12 · Subaru of America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Subaru Impreza
  • 2019 Subaru Impreza

What's wrong, in plain English

The reflectors built into the front headlight units — the side reflectors on both sides, plus the reflector that shapes the low beam on the left side — may not reflect light as well as they should. That makes it harder for you to see the road ahead and harder for other drivers to notice your car. Because of this, the car does not meet federal lighting rules. Dealers replace the front headlamp assemblies free of charge.

How serious is it?

Weaker lighting raises your chance of a nighttime crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so the car is safe to drive while you schedule the free repair.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is covered. 2. Call your Subaru dealer or Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614 (recall WRG-22). 3. Have the front headlamp assemblies replaced at no charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Impreza vehicles. The left and right side reflex reflector and left side low beam reflector may inadequately reflect light. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Reflectors that do not provide adequate illumination reduce the driver's ability to see and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front headlamp assemblies, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 15, 2023. Owners may contact Subaru's customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRG-22.
