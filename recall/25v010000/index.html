Ram Trucks Side Airbag Inflator Can Rupture

NHTSA campaign 25V010000 · Reported 2025-01-16 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

If an inflator ruptures, sharp metal fragments could strike occupants and cause injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Ram trucks, the right and left side curtain air bag inflators may rupture due to a manufacturing defect.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included. 2. Contact a dealer to have one or both side curtain air bags replaced, as necessary. You can also call FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403. 3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN:INFLATOR
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2016-2019 Ram 3500, Ram 1500, 2016-2020 Ram 2500, and 2016 Ram 3500 Cab Chassis vehicles. The right and left side curtain air bag inflators may rupture due to a manufacturing defect.
Safety risk
An inflator rupture may result in sharp metal fragments striking occupants, resulting in injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace one or both side curtain air bags, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 13, 2025. Owners may contact FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's numbers for this recall are 05C, 09C, and 10C.
