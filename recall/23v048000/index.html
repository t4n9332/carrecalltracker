Nissan LEAF manual gives wrong defroster operating instructions

NHTSA campaign 23V048000 · Reported Feb 2, 2023 · Nissan North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

A defroster that performs less well than it should can limit how much the driver can see through the windshield, which raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Nissan is recalling certain LEAF vehicles. The instructions in the Owner's Manual for operating the defroster are incorrect, and following them can leave the defroster performing less well than it should under certain conditions. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule covering windshield defrosting and defogging. Nissan will mail owners an addendum with corrected instructions for using the defroster, at no cost.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included. 2. Watch for the mailed addendum with the corrected defroster instructions, or contact a Nissan dealer or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. 3. The corrected instructions are free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V048000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:OWNERS/SERVICE/OTHER MANUAL
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2018-2023 LEAF vehicles. The Owner's Manual instructions for defroster operation are incorrect, and may result in reduced defroster performance under specific conditions. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 103, "Windshield Defrosting and Defogging Systems."
Safety risk
Reduced defroster performance can limit visibility out of the windshield, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Nissan will mail an addendum with updated instructions on how to operate the defroster, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed April 1, 2023. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is R22C5.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
