Lincoln Aviator rear trim pieces may come loose

NHTSA campaign 25V407000 · Reported Jun 13, 2025 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

Trim that detaches from the vehicle can become a hazard in the road and increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Lincoln Aviator SUVs. The bars that divide the rear door windows and the trim on the pillar behind the rear doors may not have enough adhesive holding them in place. Without enough bonding, those pieces can come away from the vehicle. Dealers will repair or replace the pillar appliques and the window division bars on the vehicles that are covered by this recall.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Lincoln or Ford dealer to have the C-pillar trim and window division bars repaired or replaced, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V407000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2025 Aviator vehicles. The rear door window bars and C-pillar trim may have insufficient adhesive bonding, which can allow the trim to detach from the vehicle.
Safety risk
Detached trim may cause a road hazard and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will repair or replace C-pillar appliques and window division bars, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 29, 2025. Owners may contact Ford Customer Service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S66.
