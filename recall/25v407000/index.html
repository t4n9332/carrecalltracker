Lincoln Aviator rear window trim can fall off

NHTSA campaign 25V407000 · Reported 2025-06-13 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Lincoln Aviator
  • 2021 Lincoln Aviator
  • 2022 Lincoln Aviator
  • 2023 Lincoln Aviator
  • 2024 Lincoln Aviator
  • 2025 Lincoln Aviator

What's wrong, in plain English

The glue holding some trim pieces on the outside of the Aviator may not be strong enough. The strips around the rear door windows and the covers on the pillars behind the rear doors can come unstuck and fall off while you are driving. A piece flying off on the road can hit or surprise other drivers behind you. Dealers will repair or replace those trim pieces at no cost.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious: the vehicle is safe to keep driving, but loose trim can become a road hazard for others and should be repaired when you can.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 25V407000 applies to your Aviator. 2. Contact your Lincoln dealer, or Ford Customer Service at 1-866-436-7332, and reference recall 25S66. 3. Schedule the free repair or replacement of the trim pieces.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2025 Aviator vehicles. The rear door window bars and C-pillar trim may have insufficient adhesive bonding, which can allow the trim to detach from the vehicle.
Safety risk
Detached trim may cause a road hazard and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will repair or replace C-pillar appliques and window division bars, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 29, 2025. Owners may contact Ford Customer Service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S66.
