NHTSA campaign 21V980000 · Reported Dec 16, 2021 · Ford Motor Company

What's wrong, in plain English

The side curtain airbags in some Mustangs may have been made with the wrong amount of compressed gas inside them. Because of this manufacturing problem, the airbags may not inflate the way they are supposed to when a crash happens. An airbag that doesn't inflate correctly can't work as intended, and this could affect how much protection it gives you in a crash.

What you should do

1) Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2) Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. 3) The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V980000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 Mustang vehicles. The side curtain air bag inflators may have been manufactured with the incorrect amount of compressed gas, which can result in improper air bag inflation. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 226, "Ejection Mitigation," and 214, "Side Impact Protection."
Safety risk
An air bag that does not inflate properly can increase the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the side curtain air bags, free of charge. An interim owner notification letter was mailed on February 3, 2022. Owner notification letters were mailed on April 22, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21C37.
