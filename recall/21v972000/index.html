Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vans can roll away after shifting to Park

NHTSA campaign 21V972000 · Reported 2021-12-15 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious but not a stop-driving recall — Mercedes has not told owners to park the van, and it is safe to keep driving as long as you use the parking brake every time you park, but a van that rolls away can cause a crash.

What's wrong, in plain English

A software glitch in the van's park lock means putting the shifter in 'P' may not actually hold the van still. If you don't also set the parking brake, the van can move on its own after you shift into Park and step away. The repair is a free software update to the control unit that manages the stability system. Until that is done, always set the parking brake when you park.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is included.
2. Call a Mercedes-Benz or Freightliner Sprinter dealer, or Daimler Vans USA at 1-877-762-8267 (recall VS3PA27), and book the software update.
3. The update is free; always apply the parking brake until it is installed.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Freightliner Sprinter vehicles. A software error in the park lock system may allow the vehicle to move after the gear selector has been placed in the Park 'P' position without the parking brake applied.
Safety risk
Unintentional vehicle movement can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the electronic stability program control unit software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 11, 2022. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3PA27 ERI/ERO.
