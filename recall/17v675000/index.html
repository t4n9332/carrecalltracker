What's wrong, in plain English

Volkswagen is recalling certain Audi A4 sedan, A4 allroad and A5 Sportback cars. The aluminum trim on the front and rear doors can come loose from around the door speakers. Once the trim is loose, it leaves sharp edges exposed on the inside of the doors. Audi will notify owners, and a dealer will replace the aluminum trim that covers the door speakers at no cost to the owner.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is included.
2. Contact an Audi dealer, or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834, to have the speaker trim replaced.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 17V675000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:DOOR:HINGE AND ATTACHMENTS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Audi A4 Sedan and A4 allroad, and 2018 A5 Sportback vehicles. The aluminum trim on the front and rear doors can come loose from around the door speakers, leaving sharp edges exposed.
Safety risk
The sharp edges of the speaker trim increase the risk of injury to vehicle occupants.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will replace the speaker cover aluminum trim, free of charge. The recall began March 13, 2018. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 70G6.
