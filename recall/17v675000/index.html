Audi A4, A5 door speaker trim leaves sharp edges

NHTSA campaign 17V675000 · Reported 2017-10-25 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Audi A4 Allroad
  • 2018 Audi A5

What's wrong, in plain English

The aluminum trim around the door speakers on some 2017-2018 Audi A4, A4 allroad, and A5 Sportback models can come loose. Once a piece lifts away from the door panel, it leaves a sharp metal edge where people put their hands, reach for the door, or brush past while getting in and out. That edge can cut someone. Nothing about how the car drives, steers, or stops is affected. Dealers replace the trim free.

How serious is it?

This is a cut-and-scrape hazard rather than a driving hazard, so the car is safe to keep driving, though passengers and kids should avoid the loose trim until it is replaced.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Audi is included. 2. Call your Audi dealer to have the speaker trim replaced. 3. The replacement is free; Audi customer service is 1-800-253-2834, recall number 70G6.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:DOOR:HINGE AND ATTACHMENTS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Audi A4 Sedan and A4 allroad, and 2018 A5 Sportback vehicles. The aluminum trim on the front and rear doors can come loose from around the door speakers, leaving sharp edges exposed.
Safety risk
The sharp edges of the speaker trim increase the risk of injury to vehicle occupants.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will replace the speaker cover aluminum trim, free of charge. The recall began March 13, 2018. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 70G6.
