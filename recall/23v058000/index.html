Jeep Compass dashboard display may be too dim

NHTSA campaign 23V058000 · Reported 2023-02-09 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Jeep Compass

What's wrong, in plain English

The display in the middle of the dashboard may not be lit brightly enough, so it can be hard or impossible to read in daylight. That screen shows things you need while driving, such as your fuel level and warning lights, so you could miss an important alert. To fix it, the dealer replaces the windshield and the headlight switch, adds a light sensor and a small wiring harness so the screen lights up correctly. All free.

How serious is it?

Moderate: not being able to see warning lights or your fuel gauge could contribute to a crash, but the notice places no restriction on driving, so keep driving and schedule the repair.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your 2022 Compass is affected.
2. Book the repair with a Jeep dealer, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 and reference recall 15A.
3. Allow extra shop time — the fix includes a new windshield — and expect no charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Jeep Compass vehicles. The instrument panel center cluster display may be insufficiently illuminated and may not appear visible during daytime driving conditions. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 101, "Control and Displays."
Safety risk
An instrument panel display that is not visible to the driver may result in the inability to see safety-critical information, such as the fuel gauge and warning lights, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the windshield and headlamp switch, add a light sensor module, and add a wiring jumper harness to allow the instrument panel center cluster display backlighting to function correctly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 30, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 15A.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
