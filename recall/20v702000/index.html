Volkswagen Atlas Continental Tire Failure Risk

NHTSA campaign 20V702000 · Reported 2020-11-13 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A tire that suddenly loses air or separates while driving increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Volkswagen Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles have Continental tires that may have been cured for too long during manufacturing. Tires that were overcured like this can develop a break in the sidewall, causing the tire to suddenly lose air. They can also have the tread separate from the rest of the tire. Either of these problems happening while driving increases the risk of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298, to have your tires inspected.
3. Any needed tire replacement is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
TIRES:SIDEWALL
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport and 2021 Atlas vehicles equipped with Continental Cross Contact LX Sport tires, size 255/50 R 20 105 T. The tires may have been over cured.
Safety risk
Over cured tires can result in a sidewall break causing a sudden loss of air pressure or the tread separating from the tire. Both conditions increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will inspect DOT date code and mold number on the vehicle's tires to see if they are affected, replacing them as necessary, free of charge. The recall began November 30, 2020. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 44Q5.
