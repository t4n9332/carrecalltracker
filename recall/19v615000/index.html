Volkswagen key can come out when not in Park

NHTSA campaign 19V615000 · Reported 2019-08-22 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Volkswagen Beetle
  • 2018 Volkswagen GTI
  • 2018 Volkswagen Golf
  • 2018 Volkswagen Golf Sportwagen
  • 2018 Volkswagen Jetta
  • 2019 Volkswagen Beetle
  • 2019 Volkswagen GTI
  • 2019 Volkswagen Golf
  • 2019 Volkswagen Golf Sportwagen

What's wrong, in plain English

On these cars a small switch by the gear lever can get a mineral build-up on its contacts. When that happens, you can pull the key out of the ignition even though the lever is not in "Park." If the handbrake is not set, the car can roll away on its own after you get out, and someone could be hurt or the car could crash. Dealers will add an extra switch and circuit board free of charge.

How serious is it?

Serious — the car can roll away unexpectedly after you park; the vehicle is safe to drive, but always shift fully into Park and set the handbrake until it is fixed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included. 2. Call your Volkswagen dealer or customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall 37M2) to schedule the free repair. 3. Until then, make sure the lever is in "Park" and the handbrake is set before leaving the car.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:IGNITION:ANTI-THEFT:CONTROL MODULE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2015-2019 GTI, 2015-2016 and 2018-2019 Golf, 2012-2019 Beetle and Beetle Convertible, 2017-2019 Golf SportWagen and 2011-2018 Jetta vehicles equipped with an automatic transmission, manual handbrake and keyless entry. A build-up of silicate on the shift lever micro switch contacts may enable the key to be removed from the ignition when the transmission shift lever is not in "Park."
Safety risk
Removing the key while the shift lever is in a position other than "Park" increases the risk of an unintended vehicle rollaway that may result in personal injury or a crash.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will install an additional switch and circuit board, free of charge. The recall began November 19, 2019. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 37M2.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
