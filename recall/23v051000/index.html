BMW S 1000 XR Lift-Off Assistant May Deactivate

NHTSA campaign 23V051000 · Reported 2023-02-03 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Riding without knowing the front wheel lift-off assistant has been turned off can lead to unexpected handling and control, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling certain S 1000R and S 1000XR motorcycles because a dealership software update may have accidentally turned off the 'front wheel lift-off assistant' feature within the Dynamic Pro riding mode, without telling the rider it had been turned off. A rider who doesn't know this safety feature is deactivated may experience unexpected handling and control of the motorcycle, increasing the risk of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a BMW dealer or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. Check and adjust the Dynamic Pro riding mode settings as instructed; dealers will also inspect the motorcycle for free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2022-2023 S 1000R and 2020-2023 S 1000XR motorcycles. During a software update performed at a dealership, the "Dynamic Pro" riding mode feature "front wheel lift-off assistant" may have been inadvertently deactivated without alerting the rider.
Safety risk
Riding a motorcycle without knowing that the "front wheel lift-off assistant" has been deactivated can result in unexpected handling and control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will instruct owners to check the configuration settings in the "Dynamic Pro" riding mode and adjust the settings as needed. Dealers will also inspect the motorcycles, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 4, 2023. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
