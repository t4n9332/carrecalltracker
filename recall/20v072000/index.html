Ford and Lincoln rear suspension links can break

NHTSA campaign 20V072000 · Reported 2020-02-10 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Ford Flex
  • 2018 Ford Taurus
  • 2018 Lincoln MKT

What's wrong, in plain English

These vehicles have a part in the rear suspension called a toe link, which helps hold the back wheels pointed straight. Stress on the rear suspension can crack one of these links and it can break apart. If that happens while you are driving, the car's steering and handling change suddenly and without warning, and it becomes very hard to control. Only Police Interceptor and SHO Performance Pack vehicles are affected. Dealers replace the rear toe links at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

A part that breaks suddenly and takes away control of the car is among the most serious kinds of defect, so treat this as urgent and get it repaired before further driving if you can.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Flex, Taurus, or MKT is covered by campaign 20V072000 (Ford's number 20S04).
2. Call your Ford dealer at 1-866-436-7332 or Lincoln at 1-800-521-4140 and ask for the toe link replacement right away.
3. Have both rear toe links replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2013-2018 Lincoln MKT and Ford Flex and Taurus vehicles with the Police Interceptor or SHO Performance Pack. The rear suspension toe links may fracture due to stress on the rear suspension.
Safety risk
A fractured rear toe link will cause a sudden change in vehicle handling and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will replace the rear suspension toe links, free of charge. Remedy parts are not currently available. Owners were notified about the recall on March 3, 2020. A second notice will be mailed when remedy parts become available, which is currently expected to be the end of September 2020. The recall began October 28, 2020. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 or Lincoln customer service at 1-800-521-4140. Ford's number for this recall is 20S04.
