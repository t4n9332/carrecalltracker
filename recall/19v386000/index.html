BMW X7 Head Air Bags May Be Misaligned

NHTSA campaign 19V386000 · Reported 2019-05-23 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

If the head air bags are impaired during deployment, this increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some BMW X7 vehicles may have driver and passenger head air bags that were not properly aligned with the trim when they were installed. Because of this misalignment, the head air bags may not work correctly if they need to deploy. If the air bags are impaired during deployment, this increases the risk of injury to the driver or passenger.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a BMW dealer or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 to schedule an inspection.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2019 X7 xDrive40i and X7 xDrive50i vehicles. The driver and passenger head air bags may not have been properly aligned with the trim during installation.
Safety risk
The head air bags may be impaired during deployment, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and, as necessary, reinstall the head air bags, free of charge. The recall began July 18, 2019. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
