BMW XM tailgate panel may detach from vehicle

NHTSA campaign 24V139000 · Reported 2024-02-23 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 BMW XM

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2024 BMW XM vehicles, the panel on the tailgate, the rear hatch you lift to reach the cargo area, was not glued on properly during manufacturing. The adhesive may not hold, and the panel can come away from the vehicle. If it drops off while you are driving, it becomes debris in the road that drivers behind you could hit or swerve to avoid. A dealer will inspect the panel and replace it if needed.

How serious is it?

A panel coming off at speed can cause a crash for you or the drivers behind you; the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but get it inspected quickly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your XM is affected.
2. Contact your BMW dealer for the free tailgate panel inspection and replacement if needed; letters were mailed April 17, 2024.
3. Report any loose edges, rattles, or gaps around the tailgate panel when you call. BMW customer service: 1-800-525-7417.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2024 XM vehicles. The tailgate panel may detach from the mounting structure due to improper adhesive bonding that occurred during manufacturing.
Safety risk
A panel that detaches from a vehicle can become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the tailgate panel as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 17, 2024. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
