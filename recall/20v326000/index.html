Honda Clarity Rear Seat Belts May Not Lock

NHTSA campaign 20V326000 · Reported 2020-06-04 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

If the seat belt does not lock properly in a crash, it may not properly restrain the occupant, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Honda is recalling certain Clarity Electric and Clarity Plug-in Hybrid vehicles. These vehicles have rear seat belts built with a dual-mode locking mechanism. This mechanism relies on a sensor in the seat belt webbing to trigger the lock. On the affected vehicles, that sensor may not engage the lock the way it is supposed to, so the locking mechanism may not work as intended.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Honda dealer to have the rear seat belt assembly inspected and replaced if necessary; you can also call Honda at 1-888-234-2138.
3. The repair is free.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2019 Honda Clarity Electric
  • 2019 Honda Clarity Plug-In Hybrid

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2019 Clarity Fuel Cell, Clarity Electric, and Clarity Plug-in Hybrid vehicles with rear seat belt assemblies with a dual-mode locking mechanism. The seat belt webbing sensor locking mechanism may not lock as intended.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, the seat belt may not properly restrain the occupant, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Honda will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the rear seat belt assembly production dates, and replace the assemblies, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began July 24, 2020. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is Z7L.
