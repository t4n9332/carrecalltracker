Hyundai Palisade Engine Valve Springs May Break

NHTSA campaign 24V106000 · Reported Feb 13, 2024 · Hyundai Motor America

critical How serious is it? About this label

Losing engine power increases the risk of a crash, and a hole in the engine block increases the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Hyundai Palisade vehicles have engine valve springs that may break while the vehicle is being driven. A broken valve spring can cause the engine to lose power. It is also possible for a broken spring to create a hole in the engine block.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included in this recall.
2. Contact a Hyundai dealer, or call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460.
3. The engine sub-assembly replacement is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V106000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:HARD PARTS INTERNAL/MECHANICAL
Summary
Hyundia Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2024 Palisade vehicles. The engine valve springs may break while driving, which can result in a loss of drive power and possibly create a hole in the engine block.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash. A hole in the engine block increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the engine sub-assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed by December 6, 2024. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 255.
