Porsche Taycan Software Fault Can Cut Power

NHTSA campaign 21V486000 · Reported 2021-06-29 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A sudden loss of motive power increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

A software problem in the monitoring system can wrongly detect a fault and shut down the powertrain, causing the car to suddenly lose the power that drives it. Dealers will reprogram the power electronics and the engine control unit to fix this, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Porsche dealer or customer service at 1-800-767-7243.
3. The software reprogramming is free.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2020 Porsche Taycan
  • 2021 Porsche Taycan

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECU/ECM):SOFTWARE
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Taycan Turbo, Taycan Turbo S, Taycan 4S, 2021 Taycan, Taycan Cross Turismo, Taycan Turbo S Cross Turismo, Taycan 4S Cross Turismo, and Taycan Turbo Cross Turismo vehicles. The monitoring software can incorrectly detect a fault and shutdown the power train, causing a loss of motive power.
Safety risk
A loss of motive power increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the power electronics and the engine control unit, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 30, 2021. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AMB5.
