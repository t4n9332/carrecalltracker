Genesis G90 Radar May Cause Sudden Unintended Braking

NHTSA campaign 25V833000 · Reported 2025-12-02 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

Braking that happens suddenly and without the driver intending it increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The Highway Drive Assist system on these Genesis G90 vehicles uses a radar sensor at the corner of the vehicle to sense other cars. On some vehicles, that radar can mistakenly detect a vehicle that is not there. When it does, the car may brake suddenly and on its own while you are driving, without the driver intending it. Genesis will replace the front bumper beam to correct the problem.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included. 2. Until the repair is done, do not use the Highway Drive Assist feature. 3. Contact your Genesis dealer or Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741 to schedule the repair, which is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:ADAS
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2023-2026 Genesis G90 vehicles. The Highway Drive Assist (HDA) corner radar may falsely detect another vehicle, which can result in sudden, unintended braking while driving.
Safety risk
Sudden, unintended braking increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to use the Highway Drive Assist feature until the remedy has been performed. Dealers will replace the front bumper beam, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 26, 2026. Owners may contact Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741. Hyundai's number for this recall is 030G. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on December 3, 2025.
