Genesis G90 driver-assist radar causes sudden unexpected braking

NHTSA campaign 25V833000 · Reported 2025-12-02 · Hyundai Motor America · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Genesis G90
  • 2024 Genesis G90
  • 2025 Genesis G90

What's wrong, in plain English

The radar sensor in the front corner of the car, used by the Highway Drive Assist system, can mistakenly think another vehicle is there. When that happens the car can brake hard on its own even though nothing is in the way. Being slowed suddenly at highway speed can surprise you and the drivers behind you. Genesis says to leave Highway Drive Assist switched off until the repair is done; the fix replaces the front bumper beam.

How serious is it?

Serious — sudden braking you did not ask for can cause a crash, so you may keep driving but should not use Highway Drive Assist until the repair is done.

What you should do

1. Turn off Highway Drive Assist now and leave it off. 2. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — VINs became searchable December 3, 2025. 3. Call Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741 (recall 030G) or your dealer for the free front bumper beam replacement; letters were mailed January 26, 2026.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:ADAS
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2023-2026 Genesis G90 vehicles. The Highway Drive Assist (HDA) corner radar may falsely detect another vehicle, which can result in sudden, unintended braking while driving.
Safety risk
Sudden, unintended braking increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to use the Highway Drive Assist feature until the remedy has been performed. Dealers will replace the front bumper beam, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 26, 2026. Owners may contact Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741. Hyundai's number for this recall is 030G. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on December 3, 2025.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
