Chevrolet Malibu front frame weld may affect crash sensors

NHTSA campaign 22V923000 · Reported 2022-12-14 · General Motors, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Chevrolet Malibu
  • 2023 Chevrolet Malibu

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain 2022-2023 Chevrolet Malibu cars. The front impact bar, a structural piece of the car's frame, may not have been welded properly to the frame rail behind it. The car's front crash sensors depend on that structure, so a bad weld could keep them from reading a collision correctly. GM is not repairing these cars: if a dealer finds the incomplete weld, GM will buy the vehicle back from you instead.

How serious is it?

There is no instruction to stop driving, but crash sensors that misread an impact can leave you less protected in a collision, and the fact that GM repurchases affected cars rather than fixing them shows this is treated seriously.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Malibu is affected.
2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 to schedule the inspection, which costs you nothing.
3. If the bad weld is found, work with GM on the vehicle repurchase they are offering.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:FRAME AND MEMBERS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Chevrolet Malibu vehicles. The front impact bar, a structural portion of the vehicle frame, may not be properly welded to the front frame rail.
Safety risk
An improperly welded front impact bar may result in the front crash sensors not performing correctly, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the right-hand and left-hand side of the vehicle's motor rail for an incomplete weld. If the condition is found, the vehicle will be repurchased by GM. Owner notification letters were mailed January 20, 2023. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020.
