Ford Expedition and Navigator Side Air Bags May Fail

NHTSA campaign 21V805000 · Reported 2021-10-15 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

An air bag that doesn't deploy properly in a crash increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Expedition, Super Duty F-250, and Lincoln Navigator vehicles. The front-door side impact sensor modules may have been damaged when they were put together at the supplier, which can prevent the side air bags from deploying properly in a crash. Dealers will replace the driver and passenger front door side impact sensors to fix the problem, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Ford dealer to schedule the repair (1-866-436-7332).
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SENSOR:SIDE IMPACT
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022 Super Duty F-250, F-350, 2021 F-150, Expedition, and Lincoln Navigator vehicles. The front-door side impact pressure sensor modules may have been damaged during supplier assembly, which can cause the side impact air bags to not deploy properly in a crash. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 214, "Side Impact Protection."
Safety risk
An air bag that does not deploy properly during a crash increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver and passenger front door side impact sensors, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 3, 2021. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21C26.
