Chrysler Pacifica park release plug can be pulled out

NHTSA campaign 18V395000 · Reported 2018-06-14 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Chrysler Pacifica

What's wrong, in plain English

On these minivans, the manual park release plug — the small cover you can pull to shift the transmission out of park by hand — can be taken off without any tool. Federal safety rules say it should not come off that easily. If someone pulls it out and the release gets used by accident, the van can slip out of park and roll away on its own, possibly hitting someone standing nearby. Dealers will install a new plug free of charge.

How serious is it?

Serious but not urgent: the notice says the van could roll away and hurt a bystander or cause a crash, and it does not tell owners to stop driving, so you can keep driving while you wait for the free fix.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your minivan is included in recall 18V395000.
2. Call your Chrysler dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall U50), and book an appointment to have the park release plug replaced.
3. Have the work done — the new plug and the labor are free; you should not be charged anything.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:IGNITION:ANTI-THEFT:CONTROL MODULE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Chrysler Pacifica vehicles. The Manual Park Release (MPR) plug may be removed without a tool. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 114, "Theft Protection."
Safety risk
If the MPR is engaged unintentionally, the vehicle could roll away striking and injuring a bystander or cause a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the MPR plug, free of charge. The recall began July 9, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U50.
