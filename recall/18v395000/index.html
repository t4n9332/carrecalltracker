Chrysler Pacifica Manual Park Release Defect

NHTSA campaign 18V395000 · Reported Jun 14, 2018 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

If the Manual Park Release is triggered by accident, the vehicle could roll away and strike or injure a bystander, or cause a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2017-2018 Chrysler Pacifica vehicles, the Manual Park Release plug can be pulled out without using a tool. Because of this, the vehicles don't meet federal theft protection requirements.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Chrysler dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The Manual Park Release plug replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V395000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:IGNITION:ANTI-THEFT:CONTROL MODULE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Chrysler Pacifica vehicles. The Manual Park Release (MPR) plug may be removed without a tool. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 114, "Theft Protection."
Safety risk
If the MPR is engaged unintentionally, the vehicle could roll away striking and injuring a bystander or cause a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the MPR plug, free of charge. The recall began July 9, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U50.
