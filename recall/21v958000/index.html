Nissan Frontier left-rear seat belt bolts may loosen

NHTSA campaign 21V958000 · Reported 2021-12-09 · Nissan North America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Nissan Frontier

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts that hold the left-rear seat belt bracket to the body may not have been tightened enough at the factory. That bracket is the anchor point the belt pulls against. If it is loose, the bracket can break under the heavy load of a crash, and the belt would no longer hold the passenger in place. Nissan dealers will replace those bolts and fix any damaged threads in the seat frame at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious but only in a crash: the truck drives normally and the notice does not tell you to stop driving it, though anyone riding in the left-rear seat may not be properly restrained if you are hit, so get it fixed promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included in recall PC856. 2. Call any Nissan dealer for an appointment, or reach Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. 3. Have the dealer replace the bolts — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2022 Frontier vehicles. The seat belt bracket mounting bolts on the left-rear seat may have been incorrectly tightened. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 207, "Seating Systems," 209, "Seatbelt Assemblies," and 210, "Seat Belt Assembly Anchorages."
Safety risk
During a crash, a loose seat belt bracket may break, causing a loss of seat belt restraint and increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the bolts on the left-rear seat and repair any damaged threads as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were January 27, 2022. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PC856.
