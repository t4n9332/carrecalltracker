Ford Escape side airbag part may fly loose

NHTSA campaign 17V803000 · Reported 2017-12-18 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Ford Escape

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2018 Ford Escapes have a problem with the side curtain airbags, the ones that drop down along the windows to protect your head in a crash. A part inside the airbag can come loose when the airbag inflates. If that happens, the loose piece can be thrown into the cabin where you and your passengers are sitting. Dealers will replace the driver and passenger side curtain airbags at no cost.

How serious is it?

If the airbag goes off in a crash, the loose part could hit someone and cause injury, but the notice does not say to stop driving the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Escape is affected. 2. Contact a Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 to book the repair. 3. Have the side curtain airbags replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2018 Ford Escape vehicles. The side curtain air bags on these vehicles have a component that may detach during deployment of the inflatable curtain air bag.
Safety risk
If the component detaches during deployment, it may enter the passenger compartment and be a projectile within the vehicle cabin, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will replace the driver and passenger side curtain air bags, as necessary, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin January 2018. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 17S44.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
