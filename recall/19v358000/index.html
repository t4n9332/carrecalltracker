Ford F-550 fire trucks show false brake lights

NHTSA campaign 19V358000 · Reported 2019-05-13 · Unruh Fire, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Ford F-550

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2017-2018 fire trucks built on the Ford F-550 by Unruh Fire have Whelen brake lights. Depending on how the truck was wired, the brake lights may come on even though the driver has not pressed the brake pedal. Drivers behind the truck may think it is slowing down when it isn't. Unruh Fire has notified owners and will replace the brake lights free of charge. The recall began May 20, 2019.

How serious is it?

A moderate risk, because brake lights that come on for no reason can confuse drivers behind you and lead to a crash, but the notice does not say to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check the VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm the truck is included. 2. Contact Unruh Fire customer service at 1-800-856-7080 to arrange the brake light replacement. 3. Have the lights replaced — there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
Unruh Fire, Inc. (Unruh Fire) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Ford F-550-based fire trucks equipped with Whelen brake lights. Depending on the specific wiring of the vehicle, these brake lights may illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed.
Safety risk
If the brake lights incorrectly illuminate, it can confuse other drivers, possibly increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Unruh Fire has notified owners and will replace the brake lights, free of charge. The recall began in May 20, 2019. Owners may contact Unruh Fire customer service at 1-800-856-7080.
