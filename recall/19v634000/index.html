MINI Cooper hardtops may be missing rear crash padding

NHTSA campaign 19V634000 · Reported 2019-08-28 · BMW of North America, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 MINI Cooper
  • 2020 MINI Cooper S

What's wrong, in plain English

These MINI Hardtops may have left the factory missing a crash pad behind the rear side trim panels. That pad is a block of padding built into the body that soaks up some of the force if another vehicle hits the side of your car. Without it, anyone sitting in the back seat is more likely to be hurt in a side-impact crash. The car drives normally and nothing will feel wrong. MINI dealers will install the missing pads for free.

How serious is it?

Moderate — it does not make a crash more likely and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but rear passengers are less protected in a side crash until the pads are installed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your MINI is on the list.
2. Book the free crash pad installation at a MINI dealer.
3. Call MINI customer service at 1-866-825-1525 if you have questions or did not receive a letter.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020 MINI Hardtop 2-Door Cooper, Cooper S, and JCW vehicles. The rear side trim panels may have been assembled without a crash pad. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 214, "Side Impact Protection."
Safety risk
Without a crash pad, rear seat passengers would have an increased risk of injury in the event of a side impact crash.
Remedy
MINI will notify owners, and MINI dealers will install crash pads, free of charge. The recall began October 11, 2019. Owners may contact MINI customer service at 1-866-825-1525.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
