Ford Mustang steering wheel may turn by itself

NHTSA campaign 25V096000 · Reported 2025-02-14 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Ford Mustang

What's wrong, in plain English

These Mustangs were already in the shop for an earlier steering recall, and that repair was not done correctly. A backup sensor that reads how hard you are turning the wheel may not be set right. Because of that, the steering wheel can move side to side on its own, even when you are holding it steady and not trying to turn. The dealer will load corrected power steering software at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Very serious: the wheel can turn against your intentions with no warning, which can cause a loss of control and a crash, so get this done immediately.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Mustang is included. 2. Call your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 866-436-7332, and ask for recall 25S11. 3. Have the power steering software updated — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:ELECTRIC POWER ASSIST SYSTEM
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Ford Mustang vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall number 24V-493. The secondary steering torque sensor may not be calibrated properly, which can cause the steering wheel to turn side to side against the driver's intentions.
Safety risk
A steering wheel that turns unintentionally can cause a loss of vehicle control and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the power steering control module (PSCM) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 25, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S11.
