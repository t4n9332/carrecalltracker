Nissan Ariya Software Error May Cut Power

NHTSA campaign 24V560000 · Reported 2024-07-25 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

An unexpected loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Nissan Ariya electric vehicles have software that helps control the electric drive system, and this software may sometimes incorrectly detect a problem that isn't actually there. When that happens, the software can shut down the vehicle's electric drive system. This shutdown causes the vehicle to lose the power it needs to keep moving while it is being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact a Nissan dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. 3. Dealers will update the vehicle's software if necessary, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:INVERTER
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2023 Ariya vehicles. The inverter software may misdiagnose an error, which can shut down the EV system, resulting in a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
An unexpected loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the vehicle's inverter software version and update the software, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 12, 2024. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is R24A8.
