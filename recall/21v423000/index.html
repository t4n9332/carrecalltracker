Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra tires may fail suddenly

NHTSA campaign 21V423000 · Reported 2021-06-03 · General Motors, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 1500
  • 2019 GMC Sierra 1500

What's wrong, in plain English

Some tires on these trucks were left curing too long when they were made. Tires that were over-cured can break in the sidewall and lose all their air at once, or the tread can start separating and peel off the tire. Either problem can happen while you are driving and make the truck very hard to control. A dealer will inspect your tires and replace any that are affected.

How serious is it?

Serious — a sudden blowout or tread loss can cause a crash, and while the notice does not tell you to park the truck, you should get the tires checked right away.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck has the affected tires.
2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer (1-800-222-1020) or GMC dealer (1-800-462-8782) and reference recall N212336230.
3. Have the dealer inspect and replace the tires as needed — inspection and any replacement are free.

Official notice

Component
TIRES
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500 vehicles. The affected tires were cured for too long during production.
Safety risk
Over cured tires may develop a break in the sidewall resulting in sudden air loss or a belt edge separation which could lead to a tread/belt loss. Either condition can cause a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers inspect and replace the tires as necessary, free of charge, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 25, 2021. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 and GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N212336230.
