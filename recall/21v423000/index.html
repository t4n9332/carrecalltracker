Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra Tire Failure Risk

NHTSA campaign 21V423000 · Reported Jun 3, 2021 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Sudden air loss or tread separation can cause a driver to lose control of the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some tires on these trucks were left in the curing process too long during manufacturing. Tires that were cured too long can develop a break in the sidewall, causing sudden air loss, or the edge of the tire's belt can separate, causing the tread to come apart from the tire. Either problem can cause the driver to lose control of the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included.
2. Contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782 to have your tires inspected.
3. Replacement tires are free if needed.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V423000. Official notice

Component
TIRES
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500 vehicles. The affected tires were cured for too long during production.
Safety risk
Over cured tires may develop a break in the sidewall resulting in sudden air loss or a belt edge separation which could lead to a tread/belt loss. Either condition can cause a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers inspect and replace the tires as necessary, free of charge, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 25, 2021. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 and GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N212336230.
