Dodge Charger Pursuit Driveshaft May Detach

NHTSA campaign 18V281000 · Reported 2018-05-01 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A detached driveshaft may become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2015-2018 Dodge Charger pursuit police vehicles equipped with all-wheel drive and a V8 engine have a front driveshaft with a universal joint that may seize or fracture. If that happens, the front driveshaft can detach from the vehicle while it is being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Chrysler dealer to have the front driveshaft replaced; you can also call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:DRIVELINE:DRIVESHAFT
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2015-2018 Dodge Charger pursuit police vehicles equipped with all-wheel drive and V8 engines. The front driveshaft universal joint may seize or fracture causing the shaft to detach from the vehicle.
Safety risk
If the front driveshaft detaches from the vehicle it may become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the front driveshaft, free of charge. The recall began June 27, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U37.
