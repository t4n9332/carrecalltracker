NHTSA campaign 23V704000 · Reported Oct 19, 2023 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Honda Civic vehicles that had their power steering rack replaced during an earlier repair may have had the new rack installed incorrectly. This can allow the tire to rub against the lower suspension or the tie rod end, which may damage the tire. A damaged tire can fail while driving, increasing the risk of a crash or injury. Dealers will inspect the steering rack and replace it if needed, free of charge.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
Contact a Honda dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138, to schedule the repair.
The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V704000. Official notice

Component
STEERING:RACK AND PINION
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Civic 4-door and Civic 5-door vehicles that received a replacement power steering rack as part of a service repair. The steering rack may have been incorrectly assembled, which can allow the tire to chafe against the lower suspension or tie rod end, possibly resulting in tire damage.
Safety risk
A damaged tire can fail and increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the electric power steering rack, as necessary, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed January 16, 2024. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is YFW.
