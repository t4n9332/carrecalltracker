Honda Civic replacement steering rack may damage a tire

NHTSA campaign 23V704000 · Reported 2023-10-19 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

A tire damaged this way can fail while you drive, which could cause a crash or injury, so even though the notice does not tell owners to park the car, have it inspected soon.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2022-2024 Civic 4-door and 5-door cars got a new power steering rack during a service visit, and that part may have been put together wrong. When it is, a front tire can rub against the lower suspension or the tie rod end. That constant rubbing wears into the tire and can damage it. Only cars that actually received a replacement rack are affected. A Honda dealer will inspect the electric power steering rack and replace it if needed, free.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Civic falls under recall 23V704000.
2. Contact a Honda dealer about recall YFW, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. Have the steering rack inspected and replaced if necessary — there is no charge to you.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:RACK AND PINION
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Civic 4-door and Civic 5-door vehicles that received a replacement power steering rack as part of a service repair. The steering rack may have been incorrectly assembled, which can allow the tire to chafe against the lower suspension or tie rod end, possibly resulting in tire damage.
Safety risk
A damaged tire can fail and increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the electric power steering rack, as necessary, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed January 16, 2024. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is YFW.
