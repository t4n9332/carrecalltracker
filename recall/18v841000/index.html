Porsche Panamera hybrid front brake lines can corrode

NHTSA campaign 18V841000 · Reported 2018-11-28 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Weaker front braking raises the chance of a crash, and while the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, you should drive gently and get the free repair promptly.

What's wrong, in plain English

The metal brake lines that run to the front brakes on these Panamera hybrids can rust over time. Rust inside the line can weaken it and cut down how well the front brakes work, so the car may take longer to stop than you expect. Porsche dealers will replace the front brake line, and any brake parts the corrosion has damaged, at no cost to you. Repairs under this recall started June 26, 2019.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Panamera is included.
2. Call your Porsche dealer, or Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243, and ask for recall AJ11.
3. Have the brake line replaced — the repair is free, and go sooner if braking feels soft or different.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2018 Porsche Panamera Turbo S Hybrid Executive, Panamera 4 Hybrid, Panamera Turbo S Hybrid, Panamera 4 Hybrid Executive, Panamera 4 Hybrid Sport Turismo, and Panamera Turbo S Hybrid Sport Turismo vehicles. The brake lines installed on the front axle may corrode over time. Corrosion inside the line may affect the front braking performance.
Safety risk
Braking ability that is reduced due to a corroded brake line can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Porsche will notify owners, and dealers will replace the brake line, and any damaged brake components, free of charge. The recall began June 26, 2019. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AJ11.
