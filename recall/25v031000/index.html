Acura and Honda Pilot software glitch can stall engine

NHTSA campaign 25V031000 · Reported 2025-01-23 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Acura TLX Type S
  • 2022 Acura MDX Type S
  • 2022 Acura TLX Type S
  • 2023 Acura MDX Type S
  • 2023 Acura TLX Type S
  • 2023 Honda Pilot
  • 2024 Acura MDX Type S
  • 2024 Honda Pilot
  • 2025 Honda Pilot

What's wrong, in plain English

The computer that controls how fuel is fed to the engine has a software bug on these Acura and Honda models. Because of it, the engine can stall or suddenly lose power while you are driving. Losing power in traffic or on the highway raises the risk of a crash. The dealer will reload corrected software into that computer at no charge; owner letters were mailed in March 2025.

How serious is it?

Serious — a stall or loss of power while driving can lead to a crash or injury; no park-it warning was issued, so you can keep driving, but schedule the software update promptly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Honda or Acura dealer, or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (recall numbers EL1 and AL0).
3. Have the fuel injection computer reprogrammed — the update is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2022-2025 Acura MDX Type-S, 2023-2025 Honda Pilot, and 2021-2025 Acura TLX Type-S vehicles. A software error in the fuel injection electronic control unit (FI-ECU) may cause an engine stall or a loss of power.
Safety risk
An engine stall or loss of power can increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the FI-ECU software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 17, 2025. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's numbers for this recall are EL1 and AL0.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
