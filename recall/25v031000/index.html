What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Acura MDX Type-S, Acura TLX Type-S, and Honda Pilot vehicles have a software error in the fuel injection electronic control unit, sometimes called the FI-ECU. Because of this software error, the engine may stall or lose power while the vehicle is being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a dealer to schedule the repair; you can also call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. The FI-ECU software update is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V031000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2022-2025 Acura MDX Type-S, 2023-2025 Honda Pilot, and 2021-2025 Acura TLX Type-S vehicles. A software error in the fuel injection electronic control unit (FI-ECU) may cause an engine stall or a loss of power.
Safety risk
An engine stall or loss of power can increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the FI-ECU software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 17, 2025. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's numbers for this recall are EL1 and AL0.
