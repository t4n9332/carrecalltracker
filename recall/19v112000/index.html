Porsche Cayenne Shock Absorbers May Loosen

NHTSA campaign 19V112000 · Reported 2019-02-20 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Loose shock absorbers may affect the vehicle's steering, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

One Cayenne Turbo vehicle has shock absorber forks that may allow the shock absorbers to become loose. Dealers will replace the shock absorber forks, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Porsche dealer, or call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243.
3. The shock absorber fork replacement is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:SHOCK ABSORBER
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling one 2019 Cayenne Turbo vehicle. The affected vehicle has shock absorber forks that may allow the shock absorbers to loosen.
Safety risk
Loose shock absorbers may impair vehicle steering, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Porsche will notify the owner, and a dealers will replace the shock absorber forks, free of charge. The recall began April 19, 2019. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AKA2.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
