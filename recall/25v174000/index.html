Jeep Wagoneer S Headlights May Not Aim Correctly

NHTSA campaign 25V174000 · Reported Mar 20, 2025 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Headlights that are not aimed correctly can reduce how well the driver sees the road or create glare for other drivers, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler says the headlights on certain Jeep Wagoneer S electric vehicles cannot be properly adjusted. The part used to adjust the headlights up and down is not accessible, and the part covering the side-to-side adjustment is not covered the way it should be. Because of this, the headlights may not be aimed correctly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Jeep dealer to schedule the free repair, or call Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V174000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Jeep Wagoneer S battery electric (BEV) vehicles. The headlight vertical aim adjustment is not accessible, and the horizontal aim adjustment is not covered as intended. As a result, the headlights may not be able to be adjusted properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Incorrectly adjusted headlights can reduce driver visibility or create a glare for oncoming traffic, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will remove the plugs from the vertical aim and install plugs on the horizontal aim, as necessary free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 3, 2025. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 23C.
