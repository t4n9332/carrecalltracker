Jeep Wagoneer S headlights cannot be aimed correctly

NHTSA campaign 25V174000 · Reported 2025-03-20 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderate: poorly aimed headlights can cut your night vision or glare at oncoming traffic and raise the chance of a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The headlights on these electric Jeep Wagoneer S SUVs cannot be aimed correctly. The opening used to adjust how high or low the beams point is blocked by a plug, and the side-to-side adjustment is left uncovered when it should be plugged. That means a shop cannot set the beams where they belong. Headlights pointed too low leave less of the road lit, and beams aimed too high shine into other drivers' eyes. A dealer moves the plugs so the aim can be set.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Call a Jeep dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403 (recall number 23C), and book the fix. 3. Have the plugs moved so your headlights can be aimed; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Jeep Wagoneer S battery electric (BEV) vehicles. The headlight vertical aim adjustment is not accessible, and the horizontal aim adjustment is not covered as intended. As a result, the headlights may not be able to be adjusted properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Incorrectly adjusted headlights can reduce driver visibility or create a glare for oncoming traffic, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will remove the plugs from the vertical aim and install plugs on the horizontal aim, as necessary free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 3, 2025. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 23C.
