Ford Escape and Focus Block Heater Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 26V011000 · Reported 2026-01-15 · Ford Motor Company

critical How serious is it? About this label

An electrical short circuit from the leaking block heater can increase the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The engine block heater in some Ford Escape and Focus vehicles can crack over time and leak coolant. If coolant leaks while the heater is plugged in, it can cause an electrical short circuit inside the heater. This short circuit raises the chance that a fire could start in the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Until the repair is done, do not plug in the block heater.
3. Contact a Ford dealer (1-866-436-7332) for the free repair.

Affected vehicles (3)

  • 2018 Ford Escape
  • 2018 Ford Focus
  • 2019 Ford Escape

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:ELECTRICAL:ENGINE BLOCK HEATER
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2013-2018 Focus, 2013-2019 Escape, and 2015-2016 MKC vehicles equipped with a 2.0L engine. The engine block heater may crack and develop a coolant leak, causing it to short circuit when the block heater is plugged in.
Safety risk
An electrical short circuit can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to plug in their block heater until the vehicle is remedied. Dealers will replace the block heater, free of charge. Owners will also have a alternative option to replace engine block heater element with a threaded plug, and remove the block heater electrical cord. Interim letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, were mailed March 6, 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S01. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on January 16, 2026.
