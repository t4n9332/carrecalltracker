BMW X5 and X7 windshield leak may cause fire

NHTSA campaign 22V820000 · Reported 2022-11-02 · BMW of North America, LLC · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 BMW X5
  • 2023 BMW X7

What's wrong, in plain English

The windshield on these BMW SUVs may not have been bonded to the body properly when the vehicle was built. A poor seal can let rainwater leak inside the vehicle, where it can reach electrical parts. Water on those parts can cause a short circuit, and a short circuit can start a fire. A dealer will inspect the windshield seal and, if needed, remove the windshield and reinstall it with a new seal, at no cost.

How serious is it?

A fire risk is serious, but the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle or stop driving, so you may keep driving while you arrange the free inspection.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered by recall 22V820000.
2. Contact your BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, to schedule the windshield inspection.
3. Have the seal inspected and the windshield resealed if necessary — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2023 X5 sDrive40i, xDrive40i, xDrive45e, and X7 xDrive40i vehicles. The windshield may not have been properly bonded to the vehicle which could allow water to enter the vehicle and contact electrical components, possibly resulting in a short-circuit.
Safety risk
An electrical short-circuit increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the windshield seal and as necessary, remove and reinstall the windshield with a new seal, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 30, 2022. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
