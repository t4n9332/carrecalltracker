Honda CRF1100 Africa Twin can stall with Wheelie Control

NHTSA campaign 24V882000 · Reported 2024-11-21 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Honda Crf1100
  • 2022 Honda Crf1100
  • 2025 Honda Crf1100

What's wrong, in plain English

These Africa Twin motorcycles have a software mistake in the computer that controls the fuel injection. When the Wheelie Control feature is switched on, that error can make the engine stall or suddenly lose power. Losing power without warning on a motorcycle can make it hard to stay in control or keep up with traffic. The repair is a software update for the fuel injection computer, which your Honda dealer loads free of charge.

How serious is it?

A sudden stall or power loss on a motorcycle can lead to a crash or injury; Honda has not told riders to stop riding, but the problem is tied to Wheelie Control, so schedule the update promptly.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your bike is covered. 2. Contact your Honda motorcycle dealer or Honda at 1-866-784-1870 (recall KR1) to book the reprogramming. 3. The software update costs you nothing.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2022 and 2024-2025 CRF1100 Africa Twin motorcycles. A software error in the fuel injection electronic control unit (FI-ECU) may cause an engine stall or a loss of power when the Wheelie Control feature is activated.
Safety risk
An engine stall or loss of power can increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the FI-ECU software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 15, 2025. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KR1.
