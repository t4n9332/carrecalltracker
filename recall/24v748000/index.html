Nissan Rogue and INFINITI QX80 Rearview Camera May Go Blank

NHTSA campaign 24V748000 · Reported Oct 3, 2024 · Nissan North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Losing the camera image while in reverse reduces how well the driver can see behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

A software error can leave the rearview camera screen blank when the vehicle is shifted into reverse. Instead of showing the view behind the vehicle, the display shows nothing. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard that requires that image to appear. The fix is a software update for the screen system, which can either be sent to the vehicle over the air or installed by a dealer.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included. 2. Contact your dealer, or Nissan customer service at 800-647-7261 or INFINITI customer service at 1-800-662-6200, to arrange the software update. 3. The update is free of charge, whether it arrives over the air or is done by the dealer.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V748000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Rogue and 2025 INFINITI QX80 vehicles. A software error may cause the rearview camera to display a blank screen when the vehicle is in reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
The loss of the rearview camera image while in reverse can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The in-vehicle infotainment software will be updated by an over-the-air (OTA) software update or by a dealer, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 2, 2024. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 800-647-7261 or INFINITI's customer service at 1-800-662-6200. Nissan's numbers for this recall are R24B3 and R24B4. (over-the-air software update)
