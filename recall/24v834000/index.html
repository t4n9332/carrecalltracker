Volkswagen Beetle Driver Airbag Inflator May Explode

NHTSA campaign 24V834000 · Reported Oct 30, 2024 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

An exploding inflator can send sharp metal fragments into the driver or other occupants, which the notice says can cause serious injury or death. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In certain Volkswagen Beetle and Beetle Convertible vehicles, the airbag in front of the driver can explode instead of inflating normally. This happens because the propellant inside the inflator breaks down over time, after long-term exposure to high humidity, high temperatures, and repeated temperature swings. If the inflator explodes, it can throw sharp pieces of metal at the driver or other people in the car.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer to schedule replacement of the driver's side airbag module; call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V834000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL:DRIVER SIDE:INFLATOR MODULE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Beetle, Beetle Convertible, 2012-2014 Passat, 2017 Passat Wagon, and 2006-2007 Passat Sedan vehicles. The driver's side frontal air bag inflator may explode due to propellant degradation occurring after long-term exposure to high absolute humidity, high temperatures, and high temperature cycling.
Safety risk
An inflator explosion may result in sharp metal fragments striking the driver or other occupants resulting in serious injury or death.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver's side front air bag module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 4, 2024. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's numbers for this recall are 69EG, 69GQ, and 69E6.
