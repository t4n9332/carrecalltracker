Hyundai Sonata Fuel Tank Damage Risk of Fire

NHTSA campaign 25V796000 · Reported Nov 18, 2025 · Hyundai Motor America

critical How serious is it? About this label

Hyundai says a melted fuel tank can leak, increasing the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Hyundai says a damaged check valve in some Sonata vehicles can let air get into the fuel tank. That extra air can make the tank expand, and the expanded tank can touch hot parts of the exhaust system. Contact with the hot exhaust can melt the fuel tank, so Hyundai is having dealers check the check valve, fuel tank, and engine control software.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Hyundai dealer or call 1-855-371-9460 to schedule the inspection and repair.
3. The check valve, fuel tank (if needed), and software update are all free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V796000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2020-2023 Sonata vehicles. A damaged check valve can allow air to enter the fuel tank, causing it to expand and contact hot exhaust components, melting the tank.
Safety risk
A melted fuel tank can leak, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the check valve. The fuel tank assembly will be inspected for damage and replaced if necessary. Additionally, dealers will inspect and update the engine control unit software. All repairs will be performed as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning March 13, 2026. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 286. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on November 19, 2025.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
