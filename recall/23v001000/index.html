Volvo steering gear screw can lock the steering wheel

NHTSA campaign 23V001000 · Reported 2023-01-05 · Volvo Car USA, LLC · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Volvo V60CC

What's wrong, in plain English

When the steering gear was put together at the factory, two screws may have been stacked on top of each other. If that happened, one screw can come loose and drop down inside the steering gear housing. A loose screw in there can jam the parts that turn your front wheels, and the steering wheel can lock up without warning while you are driving. Volvo dealers will replace the entire steering gear unit on affected vehicles.

How serious is it?

This is serious because the steering can seize with no warning; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but you should get this fixed as soon as a dealer can take the car.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included in recall 23V001000 (Volvo's number R10213).
2. Call your Volvo dealer or Volvo customer service at 1-800-458-1552 and ask for the earliest appointment for the steering gear replacement.
3. Have the repair done — the new steering gear and the labor are free, and report any steering stiffness or noise to the dealer right away.

Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
Volvo Car USA, LLC (Volvo Car) is recalling certain 2019-2022 V60CC, XC60, and XC90 vehicles. During steering gear assembly, double screws may have been assembled on top of each other, which can cause a screw to fall into the gearbox housing.
Safety risk
A screw inside the gearbox housing can cause the steering wheel to lock up and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the steering gear, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed March 1, 2023. Owners may contact Volvo Car's customer service at 1-800-458-1552. Volvo Car's number for this recall is R10213.
