BMW X3 front left wheel bearing may crack

NHTSA campaign 24V909000 · Reported 2024-12-05 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

This is a serious safety problem because losing control of the car can cause a crash, so have it repaired as soon as possible even though the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2025 BMW X3 30 vehicles, the swivel bearing at the front left wheel — a part that lets the wheel turn and steer — may crack. If it cracks, you can lose the ability to handle and control the vehicle while driving. This is a problem with the part itself, not something you can prevent by driving carefully. A dealer will replace the front left swivel bearing with a new one at no cost. Only a very small number of vehicles are involved; one owner was contacted on December 12, 2024.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to find out if your X3 is included. 2. Call your BMW dealer to schedule the repair, or reach BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417. 3. Have the front left swivel bearing replaced — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:WHEEL BEARING
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2025 X3 30 vehicles. The front wheel left-side swivel bearing may crack, causing a loss of vehicle handling and control.
Safety risk
A loss of vehicle handling and control increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front left-side swivel bearing, free of charge. One owner was contacted December 12, 2024. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
