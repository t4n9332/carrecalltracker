Hyundai Tucson tire label wrongly lists a spare tire

NHTSA campaign 23V556000 · Reported 2023-08-04 · Hyundai Motor America · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Hyundai Tucson

What's wrong, in plain English

The tire and loading information sticker on some 2024 Tucsons may list a pressure for a spare tire when the vehicle does not actually come with one. Nothing on the vehicle itself is broken — the label simply gives information that does not match the car. The concern is confusion: if you get a flat tire and check the sticker while sorting things out on the roadside, it could send you looking for a spare that is not there. Dealers will replace the label.

How serious is it?

This is a low-level labeling problem with no mechanical defect, and the vehicle is safe to keep driving; the only risk is confusion during an emergency tire change, which the notice says could contribute to a crash or injury.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Tucson falls under recall 23V556000 (Hyundai's number 247). 2. Contact your Hyundai dealer or Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 to have the label checked. 3. Get the corrected label installed — it is free.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2024 Tucson vehicles. The tire and loading information label may incorrectly indicate information about a spare tire. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
A label that incorrectly states spare tire pressure when there is no spare tire on-board may cause confusion during an emergency tire replacement, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the tire and loading information label, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 5, 2023. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 247.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
