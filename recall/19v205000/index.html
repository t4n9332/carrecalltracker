Ford Transit and E-350 van fold-away seat backs unlock

NHTSA campaign 19V205000 · Reported 2019-03-18 · Quality Van Sales, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Ford E-350
  • 2018 Ford Transit

What's wrong, in plain English

These 2015-2018 Ford Transit and E-350 vans were modified and fitted with Freedman fold-away seats. The seat back may not stay locked in position when weight pushes against it, such as in a crash or a sudden stop. The seats do not meet the federal safety standard for seating systems. If the seat back gives way, the person sitting in it can be hurt. Quality Van Sales will have the fold-away seats inspected and repaired free of charge.

How serious is it?

Serious for anyone riding in those seats — the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but a seat back that moves in a crash increases the risk of injury.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is part of this recall.
2. Contact Quality Van Sales customer service at 1-800-408-8550 to arrange the inspection; the recall began March 21, 2019.
3. Have the fold-away seat inspected and repaired — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Quality Van Sales, Inc. (Quality Van) is recalling certain 2015-2018 Ford Transit and E-350 vehicles modified to be equipped with Freedman GO-ES Foldaway Seat back cushions. The seat cushion may not remain locked into place when under load, such as in a crash or a sudden stop. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 207, "Seating Systems."
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, if the seat back moves, the seat occupant has an increased risk of injury.
Remedy
Quality Van will notify owners and dealers will inspect and repair the foldaway seat, free of charge. The recall began March 21, 2019. Owners may contact Quality Van customer service at 1-800-408-8550.
