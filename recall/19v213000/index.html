Honda Odyssey wheelchair conversions have crossed brake sensors

NHTSA campaign 19V213000 · Reported 2019-03-20 · Braun Corporation · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Honda Odyssey
  • 2019 Honda Odyssey

What's wrong, in plain English

These Odysseys were modified by another company to add a wheelchair ramp built into the floor. During that work, the wiring for the two rear wheel speed sensors may have been connected to the wrong wheels. The anti-lock brake system relies on those readings to keep the wheels from locking up during hard or slippery stops, so with them swapped the system may not work properly. A dealer inspects the wiring and corrects it if needed.

How serious is it?

If the anti-lock brakes don't work right, the wheels can lock and you can lose steering control during a hard stop, raising crash risk; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is covered.
2. Contact Braun Corporation customer service at 1-800-488-0359 and arrange a dealer inspection.
3. Have the rear wheel speed sensor wiring checked and corrected — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, AIR:ANTILOCK:WHEEL SPEED SENSOR
Summary
Braun Corporation (Braun) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Honda Odyessy vehicles modified to be equipped with an in-floor wheelchair ramp conversion. The rear wheel speed sensor harnesses may have been routed to the incorrect rear wheels, resulting in the possibility that the anti-lock brake system will not recognize each rear wheel speed properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 135, "Light Vehicle Brake System" and 404, "Electronic Stability Control Systems."
Safety risk
If the rear wheel speeds cannot be correctly monitored, the anti-lock brake system may not engage properly to prevent the wheels from locking up, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Braun has notified owners, and dealers will inspect and correct the rear wheel speed sensor harnesses, if necessary, free of charge. The recall began on April 4, 2019. Owners may contact Braun customer service at 1-800-488-0359.
