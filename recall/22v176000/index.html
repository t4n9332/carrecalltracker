Volkswagen Tiguan, Taos rear suspension part can break

NHTSA campaign 22V176000 · Reported 2022-03-23 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Volkswagen Tiguan
  • 2022 Volkswagen Taos
  • 2022 Volkswagen Tiguan

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2022 Volkswagen Tiguan and Taos vehicles, and 2021 long-wheelbase Tiguans, a metal part that holds a rear wheel to the suspension can rust, crack and finally break. If it breaks while you are driving, the vehicle can suddenly become unstable and hard to control, which can cause a crash. Dealers will inspect the left and right rear parts and replace one or both if needed. Volkswagen is sending a second letter when repair parts are available.

How serious is it?

Serious, because the part can fail suddenly at speed; Volkswagen does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but have it inspected promptly and report any new noise, vibration or handling change.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is in recall 42L8.
2. Call your VW dealer, or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298, to schedule the rear suspension inspection.
3. Watch for the second notice about parts availability; the inspection and any replacement are free.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2022 Tiguan, Taos, and 2021 Tiguan long wheel base (LWB) vehicles. A second notice will be sent once remedy parts become available. The left or right rear suspension knuckles may corrode, crack, and break.
Safety risk
A broken rear suspension knuckle can cause a loss of stability, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace one or both rear suspension knuckles, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 13, 2022. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 42L8.
