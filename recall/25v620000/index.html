Volvo VN trucks may have doors that won't open inside

NHTSA campaign 25V620000 · Reported 2025-09-18 · Volvo Trucks North America · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2026 Volvo VNL (4)

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2026 Volvo VN trucks left the factory with the cable inside the interior door handle hooked up wrong. When that happens, pulling the inside handle will not open the door. Anyone in the cab could be stuck, which matters most after a crash or any time someone needs to get out quickly. The dealer replaces the door latch cable and the small clip that holds it, free of charge. Owner letters went out November 11, 2025.

How serious is it?

A door that traps people inside is a real injury risk in an emergency, though the notice does not say to park the truck, so it can stay in service until the free repair is done.

What you should do

1. Check your truck's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if it falls under recall RVXX2508.
2. Contact a Volvo Trucks dealer or customer service at 800-528-6586 to schedule the cable and clip replacement.
3. Until it's fixed, test both doors from the inside so you know which one opens, and get the free repair done promptly.

Official notice

Component
LATCHES/LOCKS/LINKAGES:DOORS:LATCH
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2026 New VN trucks. The door latch cable on the interior door handle may be incorrectly installed, preventing the door from opening.
Safety risk
A door that cannot be opened from the inside may trap occupants, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the door latch cable and pivot clip, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 11, 2025. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2508. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning September 25, 2025.
