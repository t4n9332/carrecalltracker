Nissan Kicks may roll away without shift-to-park warning

NHTSA campaign 24V969000 · Reported 2024-12-23 · Nissan North America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Nissan Kicks

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2025 Nissan Kicks, the reminder that is supposed to tell you the car is not in Park may not come on. If you shut the engine off while the car is still in Drive or another gear, you may get no warning at all. You could walk away thinking the car is parked, and it can roll on its own and hit something or someone. The dealer reprograms the body control computer so the warning works again.

How serious is it?

A car that rolls away can cause a crash or injury, and while the notice does not tell you to stop driving, always make sure the car is fully in Park until the fix is done.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Kicks is affected. 2. Contact your Nissan dealer or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 and reference recall PMA42. 3. Have the computer reprogrammed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:BODY CONTROL MODULE/BCM
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2025 Kicks vehicles. The "Shift-to-Park" warning may not activate as intended when the vehicle is shut off and still in a gear other than "Park." As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 114, "Theft Protection and Rollaway Prevention."
Safety risk
When the vehicle is not in "Park" and the ignition is shut off, the vehicle could move unexpectedly, increasing the risk of a vehicle rollaway and crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reconfigure the body control module (BCM) to activate the "Shift-to-Park" warning, free of charge. Some owner notification letters were mailed January 29, 2025. The remaining owner notification letters will be mailed at a future date, anticipated, May 7, 2025. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PMA42.
