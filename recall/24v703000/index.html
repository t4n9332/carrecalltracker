Chevrolet Colorado and GMC Canyon loose seat belt bolts

NHTSA campaign 24V703000 · Reported 2024-09-19 · General Motors, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Chevrolet Colorado
  • 2023 GMC Canyon
  • 2024 Chevrolet Colorado

What's wrong, in plain English

A bolt that holds a front seat belt buckle onto the seat may not have been tightened enough at the factory. It could be the left seat, the right seat, or both. A buckle that is not held down tightly may not hold a person in place properly during a crash, which can mean worse injuries. The repair is quick and simple: dealers tighten the buckle bolts on both front seats to the correct setting.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious — the vehicle is safe to drive day to day, but the front belts may not restrain you as well as they should in a crash, so book it soon.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Colorado or Canyon is included.
2. Contact your dealer, or call Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC at 1-800-462-8782 (recall N242453720).
3. Have both front seat belt buckle bolts tightened — it is free and only takes a short visit.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Chevrolet Colorado and 2023 GMC Canyon vehicles. The left or right front seat may have a seat belt buckle attachment bolt that was not tightened properly.
Safety risk
A loose seat belt may not properly restrain an occupant in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will tighten the left and right front seat belt buckle attachment bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 30, 2024. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020, or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N242453720.
