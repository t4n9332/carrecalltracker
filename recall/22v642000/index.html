Ram Diesel Trucks' Engine Computer May Short-Circuit

NHTSA campaign 22V642000 · Reported 2022-08-26 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A loss of communication in the engine control module can cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Ram 3500, 4500, and 5500 trucks with the Cummins 6.7L turbo diesel engine, the engine control module may contain a misaligned capacitor. That capacitor can short-circuit, causing the engine control module to lose communication internally. Dealers will replace the engine control module to fix the problem.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
Contact a dealer, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to schedule the replacement.
The engine control module replacement is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECU/ECM)
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Ram 3500, 3500 Cab Chassis, Ram 2500, and 4500 and 5500 Cab Chassis vehicles equipped with Cummins 6.7L turbo diesel engines. The engine control module (ECM) may have been built with a misaligned capacitor that can short-circuit, resulting in an internal loss of communication.
Safety risk
An internal loss of communication can result in a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the ECM, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 20, 2022. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is Z87.
