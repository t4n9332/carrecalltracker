Honda Ridgeline rearview camera image may not display

NHTSA campaign 24V321000 · Reported May 9, 2024 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

A rearview camera that doesn't display an image gives the driver a reduced view behind the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The wire harness that connects the rearview camera to the vehicle's tailgate may fatigue over time and eventually break. If the harness breaks, the rearview camera image may stop displaying on the vehicle's screen. Without a working rearview camera, the driver has a reduced view of the area behind the vehicle. A reduced rear view increases the risk of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Honda dealer (1-888-234-2138) to schedule the wire harness replacement.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V321000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Honda (America Honda Motor Co) is recalling certain 2020-2024 Ridgeline vehicles. The rearview camera (RVC) tailgate wire harness may fatigue and break, which can prevent the rearview camera image from displaying.
Safety risk
A rearview camera that does not display an image can reduce the driver's rear view, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the RVC tailgate wire harness, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed November 8, 2024. Owners may contact Honda service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is YI7.
