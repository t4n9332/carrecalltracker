Toyota Sienna second-row seat belts may tear

NHTSA campaign 21V889000 · Reported 2021-11-17 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Toyota Redundant Sienna
  • 2022 Toyota Sienna Hybrid

What's wrong, in plain English

Your 2022 Sienna was built with seating for eight, and the second-row seat belts on the outside seats may have the wrong webbing guide — a small part the belt strap slides through. In a crash, the belt tightens fast, and the wrong guide can damage the strap and tear it. A torn belt cannot hold you the way it is supposed to. Dealers will replace both outboard second-row seat belt assemblies at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious but not urgent day to day — the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, though anyone riding in an outboard second-row seat faces a higher chance of injury in a crash until the belts are replaced.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Sienna is included (Toyota's recall numbers are 21TB08 and 21TA08).
2. Call your Toyota dealer for an appointment, or reach Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331; notification letters went out January 10, 2022.
3. Have the seat belts replaced — the repair is free, and until then consider seating passengers in other positions.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER:ANCHORAGE
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2022 Sienna vehicles equipped with seating for eight passengers. An incorrect seat belt webbing guide may have been installed on the outboard second-row seat belt assemblies.
Safety risk
During seat belt pre-tensioner deployment, the incorrect webbing guide may damage the seat belt webbing, causing it to tear and increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the outboard second-row seat belt assemblies, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 10, 2022. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 21TB08 and 21TA08.
