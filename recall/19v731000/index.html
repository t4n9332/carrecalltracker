Cadillac CT6 accessory wheels have wrong tire pressure sensors

NHTSA campaign 19V731000 · Reported 2019-10-15 · General Motors LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderate — the car is safe to keep driving, but you have no automatic low-pressure warning, so check your tire pressure yourself with a gauge until it is fixed.

What's wrong, in plain English

If you bought your CT6 with the dealer-installed 20-inch accessory wheels, the sensors that watch your tire pressure are sending on the wrong radio channel. The car cannot hear them, so the low-tire-pressure warning light on your dash will never come on, no matter how flat a tire gets. You could drive on a badly underinflated tire without knowing it, which makes the car harder to handle. Your dealer will swap in all four correct sensors for free.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your CT6 is covered.
2. Contact your Cadillac dealer, or GM customer service at 1-586-596-1733 (recall N192279200), to have all four sensors replaced.
3. The replacement is free — do not pay for the sensors or the labor.

Official notice

Component
TIRES:PRESSURE MONITORING AND REGULATING SYSTEMS
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2020 Cadillac CT6 vehicles equipped with dealer-installed accessory 20" x 8.50" wheels (RPO 5JO or SHH). The tire pressure monitoring system (TPMS) sensors use the incorrect wireless radio frequency and cannot illuminate the low pressure warning light when the tire pressure drops below the recommended level. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 138, "Tire Pressure Monitoring Systems."
Safety risk
Drivers may drive the vehicle with low tire pressure without a low tire pressure warning, negatively impacting the vehicle handling and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace all four TPMS sensors with the correct sensors, free of charge. The recall began December 3, 2019. Owners may contact GM customer service at 1-586-596-1733. GM's number for this recall is N192279200.
