Ram 1500 Diesel Trucks Risk Engine Stall

NHTSA campaign 20V475000 · Reported 2020-08-13 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

An engine stall while driving increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2014-2018 Ram 1500 trucks with the 3.0L diesel engine have a sensor wheel on the engine's crankshaft that can delaminate, or come apart. This sensor helps the engine keep the fuel injectors and camshaft timing working together. If the wheel breaks down, the engine can lose that timing information, and the truck's engine may stall while you are driving it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is included.
2. Contact your dealer or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-2002 to schedule the repair.
3. The software update is done free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:DIESEL
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2014-2018 Ram 1500 and 2014-2016 Jeep Grand Cherokee vehicles equipped with 3.0L diesel engines. The crankshaft position sensor tone wheel may delaminate causing the engine to lose its ability to synchronize the fuel injector pulses and cam shaft timing, possibly resulting in an engine stall.
Safety risk
An engine stall can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
FCA US will notify owners, and dealers will update the powertrain control module software to maintain vehicle propulsion by reading the camshaft position signal in the event that the crankshaft position signal is lost, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 13, 2021. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-2002. FCA US's number for this recall is W58.
