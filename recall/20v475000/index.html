Ram 1500 diesel sensor ring may cause engine stall

NHTSA campaign 20V475000 · Reported 2020-08-13 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 RAM 1500

What's wrong, in plain English

These trucks have a 3.0-liter diesel engine. A toothed ring on the crankshaft, which the engine uses to keep track of its own timing, can come apart in layers. If that happens, the engine loses the signal it needs to time the fuel injectors, and it can stall. The fix is a software update: the engine computer is reprogrammed to use a second timing signal from the camshaft so the engine keeps running if the first signal is lost.

How serious is it?

Serious, since an engine that stalls while you are driving can lead to a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so you can keep using the truck, but get the free update soon.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm campaign 20V475000 (FCA's number W58) applies to your Ram 1500.
2. Contact your Ram dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-2002, to book the engine computer software update.
3. Have the update installed — it is free, and it usually takes only a short shop visit.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:DIESEL
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2014-2018 Ram 1500 and 2014-2016 Jeep Grand Cherokee vehicles equipped with 3.0L diesel engines. The crankshaft position sensor tone wheel may delaminate causing the engine to lose its ability to synchronize the fuel injector pulses and cam shaft timing, possibly resulting in an engine stall.
Safety risk
An engine stall can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
FCA US will notify owners, and dealers will update the powertrain control module software to maintain vehicle propulsion by reading the camshaft position signal in the event that the crankshaft position signal is lost, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 13, 2021. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-2002. FCA US's number for this recall is W58.
