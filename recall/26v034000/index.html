Hyundai Palisade Third-Row Airbag May Deploy Wrong

NHTSA campaign 26V034000 · Reported 2026-01-23 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

Improper airbag deployment increases the risk of injury during a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Hyundai Palisade SUVs have side curtain airbags for the third-row seats that may not deploy correctly in a crash. These airbags are meant to help protect third-row passengers, but in some vehicles they may not work the way they're supposed to. Hyundai has already begun mailing letters to owners about this safety risk. Dealers will install a protective film over the rear window and remove certain padding where needed, at no cost to you.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Hyundai dealer, or call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460, to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2020-2025 Palisade vehicles. The side curtain air bags for third-row occupants may deploy improperly in a crash. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 226, "Ejection Mitigation."
Safety risk
Improper side curtain air bag deployment increases the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install a protective film over the rear window and remove padding, as necessary free of charge. Interim notification letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, were mailed beginning March 13, 2026. Final remedy owner letters will be mailed in phases, beginning in June 2026 and expected to continue through December 2026. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 292. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on January 24, 2026.
