Ram 1500 eTorque software glitch can stall engine

NHTSA campaign 23V265000 · Reported 2023-04-13 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 RAM 1500

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2021 Ram 1500 trucks with the 5.7L eTorque engine have a software problem in the computer that controls the engine and transmission. The software can create the wrong mix of fuel and air in the engine, which can make the engine shut off while you're driving. The dealer will reload updated software at no cost. Owner notification letters were mailed April 27, 2023. Chrysler's number for this recall is 37A.

How serious is it?

Serious, since an engine that stalls in traffic increases the risk of a crash, though the notice does not tell owners to park the truck.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Ram 1500 is covered. 2. Call your dealer or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 and reference recall 37A to book the software update. 3. Bring the truck in — the update is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:CONTROL MODULE (TCM/PCM/TECM)
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2021 Ram 1500 vehicles equipped with 5.7L eTorque engines. The powertrain control module software may cause an incorrect fuel mixture condition in the engine, and result in an engine stall.
Safety risk
An engine stall can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the Powertrain Control Module calibration software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 27, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 37A.
