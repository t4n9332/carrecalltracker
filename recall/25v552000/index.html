Ram ProMaster, Dodge Journey backup camera may fail

NHTSA campaign 25V552000 · Reported 2025-08-26 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

The notice says a rearview image that does not display properly reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Cracks can develop in a microprocessor used by the rearview camera on these vehicles. When that happens, the camera's image may not display properly on the screen, so these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for rear visibility. Dealers will replace the rearview camera at no charge, though the replacement parts are not yet available. Interim letters warning owners of the issue were mailed starting September 30, 2025, and a second letter announcing the final remedy is expected around July 2026.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403 (recall 59C); a follow-up letter with the final remedy is expected around July 2026.
3. The camera replacement will be free of charge.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2019-2021 Ram ProMaster and 2019-2020 Dodge Journey vehicles. Cracks in the microprocessor may cause the vehicle's rearview camera image not to display properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 111, "Rear Visibility.
Safety risk
A rearview image that does not display properly reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the rearview camera, free of charge. Interim notification letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, were mailed beginning September 30, 2025. A second notice will be sent once the final remedy is available, anticipated July 2026. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 59C.
