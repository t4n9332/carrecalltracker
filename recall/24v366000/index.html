Chevrolet Colorado may have wrong tires and faulty pressure warnings

NHTSA campaign 24V366000 · Reported 2024-05-23 · General Motors, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Serious but not urgent: the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, though wrong tires and a pressure warning you can't trust raise the chance of a crash, so check your own tire pressure with a gauge until it's fixed.

What's wrong, in plain English

Your truck may have left the factory with tires that don't match the tire information label on the door. Because the tires are wrong for that label, you or a tire shop could end up putting on the wrong size later. The tire pressure monitoring system may also read those tires incorrectly, so the low-pressure warning light might not come on when a tire is actually going flat. Chevrolet dealers will check the tires and replace them if needed at no cost.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included.
2. Call your Chevrolet dealer, or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 (recall N242444030), and schedule the inspection.
3. Have the tires inspected and replaced if needed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
TIRES
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024 Chevrolet Colorado vehicles. Incorrect tires may have been installed that do not match the tire label, and the tire pressure monitoring system (TPMS) may provide inaccurate information for the installed tires. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 110, "Tire Selection and Rims" and 138, "Tire Pressure Monitoring Systems."
Safety risk
A tire label that does not accurately reflect the installed tires may result in the installation of incorrectly sized tires. In addition, the TPMS may not warn the driver of a drop in tire pressure. Both of these scenarios can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the tires as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 19, 2024. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N242444030.
