Chevrolet Colorado May Have Incorrect Tires Installed

NHTSA campaign 24V366000 · Reported May 23, 2024 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Incorrectly sized tires and a tire pressure system that may not warn the driver of low pressure both increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Chevrolet Colorado vehicles may have tires installed that do not match the tire label on the vehicle. Because of this mismatch, the tire pressure monitoring system may give inaccurate information for the tires that are actually installed. This means the wrong size tires could be on the vehicle, and the system may not warn the driver if tire pressure drops. Both of these problems increase the risk of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 (recall number N242444030) to have the tires inspected.
3. Any needed tire replacement is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V366000. Official notice

Component
TIRES
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024 Chevrolet Colorado vehicles. Incorrect tires may have been installed that do not match the tire label, and the tire pressure monitoring system (TPMS) may provide inaccurate information for the installed tires. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 110, "Tire Selection and Rims" and 138, "Tire Pressure Monitoring Systems."
Safety risk
A tire label that does not accurately reflect the installed tires may result in the installation of incorrectly sized tires. In addition, the TPMS may not warn the driver of a drop in tire pressure. Both of these scenarios can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the tires as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 19, 2024. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N242444030.
