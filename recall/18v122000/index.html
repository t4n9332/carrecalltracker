Toyota Tundra, Sequoia Stability Control May Deactivate

NHTSA campaign 18V122000 · Reported Feb 20, 2018 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

medium How serious is it? About this label

Losing stability control can make the vehicle harder to control in certain driving conditions, which raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Toyota says electrical interference in a power supply circuit inside your vehicle can cause the electronic stability control system to be deactivated. This means the system could turn off without warning. As a result, the vehicle does not meet the federal safety standard that applies to electronic stability control systems.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer to schedule the free software update, or call Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V122000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRONIC STABILITY CONTROL (ESC)
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018 Toyota Tundra and Sequoia vehicles. Electrical interference within the power supply circuit may cause the vehicle's electronic stability control system to be deactivated. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 126, "Electronic Stability Control Systems."
Safety risk
Deactivation of the vehicle stability control system can increase the risk of a crash in certain conditions.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners, and dealers will update the vehicle's software, free of charge. The recall began March 14, 2018. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. Toyota's number for this recall is J0H. Note: This recall has been superseded by 18V-685. The software remedy for 18V-685 includes the updates to the software for this recall.
