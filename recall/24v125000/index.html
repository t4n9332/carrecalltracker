Toyota Tundra, Sequoia Can Move Unexpectedly in Neutral

NHTSA campaign 24V125000 · Reported 2024-02-21 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

Unexpected vehicle movement increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In these Toyota trucks and SUVs, the vehicle may move on its own after being shifted into neutral, if the brakes are not being applied. This means the vehicle could start moving without the driver pressing the gas pedal, simply because it is in neutral and the brakes aren't holding it in place. This unexpected movement can happen even though the driver did not intend for the vehicle to move.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer, or call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331, to schedule the software update.
3. The transmission control ECU software update will be done free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Tundra, Tundra Hybrid, Lexus LX600, and 2023-2024 Sequoia Hybrid vehicles. Unexpected vehicle movement may occur when the vehicle is in neutral and the brakes are not applied.
Safety risk
Unexpected vehicle movement can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the transmission control ECU software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 8, 2024. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 24TA02 and 24LA02.
