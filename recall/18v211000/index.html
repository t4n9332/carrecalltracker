Toyota Camry and Highlander May Lose Brake Assist

NHTSA campaign 18V211000 · Reported 2018-04-03 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Toyota Camry
  • 2018 Toyota Highlander

What's wrong, in plain English

A part inside the pump that boosts your braking was machined incorrectly at the factory. That pump is what makes the brake pedal easy to press. If it fails, the power assist can disappear suddenly while you are driving. The brakes themselves still work, but the pedal becomes much harder to push and stopping takes far more effort and distance. The failure can happen without warning. Dealers replace the brake booster vacuum pump at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Very serious — the loss of braking help comes on suddenly and can cause a crash, so treat this as an urgent repair even though the notice does not say to park the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered. 2. Call your Toyota or Lexus dealer now and book the free brake booster vacuum pump replacement (recall J0K/JLD). 3. Toyota: 1-888-270-9371. Lexus: 1-800-255-3987. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:POWER ASSIST:VACUUM
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018 Toyota Camry and Highlander vehicles, 2017 Toyota Sienna and Tacoma vehicles and 2017 Lexus RX350 vehicles. During the manufacturing process, the oil galley in the rotor for the brake booster vacuum pump assembly may have been improperly machined possibly resulting in a sudden loss of brake assist.
Safety risk
A sudden loss of braking assist can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners, and dealers will replace the brake booster vacuum pump, free of charge. The recall began on May 9, 2018. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371, or Lexus customer service at 1-800-255-3987. Toyota's numbers for this recall are J0K/JLD.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
