Toyota Tacoma front driveshaft joint may break

NHTSA campaign 25V656000 · Reported 2025-10-01 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Toyota Tacoma

What's wrong, in plain English

On these 2025 Toyota Tacomas, a joint in the front driveshaft can deform and then break. If it breaks, steering can become difficult while you are driving. On full-time four-wheel-drive versions, a broken driveshaft can also let the truck roll away while parked if the parking brake is not set. A dealer will inspect both front driveshaft assemblies and replace them if needed, at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

This one is serious — losing steering control or having the truck roll away can cause a crash, so have it inspected as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Always set the parking brake when you park until the truck is inspected. 2. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Tacoma is included. 3. Contact your Toyota dealer, or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 (recall 25TB11), for the free inspection and repair.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:DRIVELINE:DRIVESHAFT
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2025 Tacoma and Tacoma Hybrid vehicles. The front driveshaft constant-velocity (CV) joint may deform and break.
Safety risk
A broken driveshaft can impair steering. Vehicles with full-time 4WD may experience a vehicle rollaway while parked without the parking brake applied. Both scenarios increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace both front driveshaft assemblies as necessary, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed August 11, 2026. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 25TB11 and 25TA11.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
