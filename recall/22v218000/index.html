Hyundai Elantra and Accent: seat belt part can explode

NHTSA campaign 22V218000 · Reported 2022-04-01 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious, because the injury happens during a crash rather than out of the blue; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but front seat belt safety is affected.

What's wrong, in plain English

These cars have a device in the front seat belts that pulls the belt tight during a crash. On the recalled cars, that device can blow apart instead of working normally, sending metal pieces flying inside the car and injuring the driver or front passenger. Hyundai mailed a warning letter on May 31, 2022, before replacement parts were ready. Important: this recall has been replaced by recall 22V-354, and cars already repaired under this one need the newer repair too.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls and check for both recall 22V218000 and its replacement, 22V-354.
2. Contact your Hyundai dealer or Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460, referring to recall 223.
3. Have the front seat belt pretensioners replaced free of charge, and ask for the newer 22V-354 repair even if your car was already fixed once.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Elantra and 2020 Accent vehicles. In the event of a crash, the front driver and passenger-side seat belt pretensioners may explode upon deployment.
Safety risk
Exploding seat belt pretensioners can project metal fragments into the vehicle, strike vehicle occupants, and result in injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the seat belt pretensioners, free of charge. Interim notification letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, were mailed on May 31, 2022. A second notice will be sent once remedy parts become available. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 223. This recall is replaced by NHTSA recall number 22V-354. Vehicles already repaired under this recall will need to have the new remedy completed.
