Jeep and Ram diesel fuel pump can fail

NHTSA campaign 22V767000 · Reported 2022-10-13 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious — a stalled engine increases the risk of a crash; Chrysler has not told owners to stop driving, but get the free repair scheduled soon.

What's wrong, in plain English

These trucks and SUVs have a 3.0-liter diesel engine with a high-pressure fuel pump that pushes fuel to the engine. That pump can fail. When it does, the engine stalls, which can leave you without power while you're driving — in traffic or at highway speed. Dealers will replace the pump free of charge and will also check the rest of the fuel system, replacing any other parts that need it. Owner letters were mailed in October 2023.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Wrangler, Gladiator, or Ram 1500 is covered. 2. Call your Jeep or Ram dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and reference recall Z96. 3. Have the high-pressure fuel pump and any affected fuel parts replaced at no cost.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2020-2022 Jeep Wrangler, Ram 1500, and 2021-2022 Jeep Gladiator vehicles equipped with 3.0L diesel engines. The high pressure fuel pump (HPFP) may fail, causing an engine stall.
Safety risk
An engine stall can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the HPFP and inspect and replace additional fuel system components, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 17, 2023. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is Z96.
