GMC Canyon headlights may flicker while driving

NHTSA campaign 24V673000 · Reported 2024-09-12 · General Motors, LLC · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 GMC Canyon

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2024 GMC Canyon trucks have headlights that flicker, both while you're driving and while the truck is parked. A part inside the headlight assembly is at fault. Flickering headlights make it harder to see the road ahead at night or in bad weather, and they don't meet the federal rules for vehicle lighting. Your dealer will replace the faulty module inside the headlight so the lights stay steady and bright again.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious — poor visibility raises the chance of a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so you can keep driving while you arrange the free repair.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included. 2. Call your GMC dealer, or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782 (GM recall number N242468880), to book an appointment. 3. Have the headlight module replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024 GMC Canyon vehicles. The headlights may flicker while driving and while the vehicle is in park. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Headlights flickering can reduce visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace a module in the headlight, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 10, 2024. Owners can contact GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N242468880.
