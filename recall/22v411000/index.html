Ford Bronco rear child safety lock may not work

NHTSA campaign 22V411000 · Reported 2022-06-10 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford Bronco
  • 2022 Ford Bronco

What's wrong, in plain English

On four-door Broncos, the rear passenger-side door may still open from the inside even when the child safety lock is switched on. That means a child sitting in that seat could open the door when you expect it to be locked. A dealer will inspect that door's child safety lock and latch and replace them if needed, at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

This raises the risk of injury to a child riding in that seat, and Ford has not told owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Bronco is covered. 2. Contact a Ford dealer for the inspection, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 and mention recall 22S44. 3. Have the child safety lock and latch inspected and replaced if needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
LATCHES/LOCKS/LINKAGES:DOORS:LOCK
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain four-door 2021-2022 Bronco vehicles. The passenger-side rear door may be opened from inside of the vehicle when the child safety lock is in the "ON" position.
Safety risk
A malfunctioning child safety lock increases the risk of injury to a child occupant.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the passenger-side child safety locks door child safety lock and latch, and replace them, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 28, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S44.
