Hyundai IONIQ 5 front seat belt retractors may be loose

NHTSA campaign 25V797000 · Reported 2025-11-18 · Hyundai Motor America

medium How serious is it? About this label

The belt still works normally day to day and the notice places no limits on driving, but it may fail you in a crash, so schedule the free inspection soon.

What's wrong, in plain English

The retractor is the spool hidden in the pillar beside your seat that lets the seat belt pull out and winds it back in. On some 2025 Hyundai IONIQ 5 vehicles, the driver and front passenger retractors may not be fastened down securely. A retractor that is loose may not lock and hold you in place during a crash, so the belt would not protect you the way it is designed to. Hyundai dealers check and secure them free.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your IONIQ 5 is covered by recall 287.
2. Book the free inspection with a Hyundai dealer, or call Hyundai at 1-855-371-9460.
3. Keep wearing your seat belt as usual, and have the dealer tighten the retractors at no charge.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:RETRACTOR
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2025 IONIQ 5 vehicles. The seat belt retractors for the driver and front passenger seats may not be securely fastened.
Safety risk
An improperly secured seat belt retractor may not function properly in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and secure the seat belt retractors, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 30, 2025. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 287. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on November 19, 2025.
