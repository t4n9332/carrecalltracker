Ford E-350, E-450 and F-59 fuel tanks may rust

NHTSA campaign 22V925000 · Reported 2022-12-14 · Ford Motor Company

critical How serious is it? About this label

Very serious because a fuel leak can lead to a fire; Ford does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but have the tank inspected promptly and stop driving if you smell or see fuel.

What's wrong, in plain English

On these vans and truck chassis, the paint along a welded seam on the gas tank can peel off. Once bare metal is exposed, it can rust through, and gasoline can start leaking from the tank. If that leaking fuel reaches anything hot or a spark, it can start a fire. Dealers will inspect the fuel tank and replace it if needed, at no charge to you.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 22V925000 applies. 2. Call your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and reference recall 22S76. 3. Book the free fuel tank inspection, and replacement if the tank is affected.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022-2023 E-350, E-450, and 2021-2022 F-59 vehicles. The paint along the weld seam of the fuel tank may peel and lead to corrosion of the exposed metal, which can result in a fuel leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the fuel tank and replace, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on April 14, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S76.
