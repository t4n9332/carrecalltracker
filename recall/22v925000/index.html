Ford E-350, E-450, F-59 Fuel Tank Leak Risk

NHTSA campaign 22V925000 · Reported Dec 14, 2022 · Ford Motor Company

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak near a source of ignition increases the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Ford E-350, E-450 and F-59 vehicles have a fuel tank with paint along the weld seam that can peel away. Once the paint peels, the exposed metal underneath can corrode, and that corrosion can lead to a fuel leak.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Ford dealer or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 to schedule an inspection.
3. Dealers will inspect the fuel tank and replace it if necessary, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V925000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022-2023 E-350, E-450, and 2021-2022 F-59 vehicles. The paint along the weld seam of the fuel tank may peel and lead to corrosion of the exposed metal, which can result in a fuel leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the fuel tank and replace, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on April 14, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S76.
