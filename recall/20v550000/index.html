Ford, Lincoln transmission bolts may leak, risk fire

NHTSA campaign 20V550000 · Reported 2020-09-10 · Ford Motor Company

critical How serious is it? About this label

Missing or loose bolts could cause a transmission fluid leak, which may lead to a loss of transmission function. If the leak happens near a source of ignition, it may also increase the risk of fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

This recall covers certain Ford Edge, Ford Escape, Ford Transit Connect, Lincoln Corsair, and Lincoln Nautilus vehicles. The bolts on the start/stop accumulator end cap may be missing or loose.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 to schedule the repair.
3. The repair (replacing the start/stop accumulator) is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Edge, Transit Connect and Lincoln MKX and 2020 Escape and Lincoln Corsair vehicles. The start/stop accumulator endcap may have missing or loose bolts.
Safety risk
Missing or loose bolts on the start stop accumulator endcap could result in a transmission fluid leak and may progress to a loss of transmission function. A transmission fluid leak in the presence of an ignition source may increase the risk of fire.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will replace the start/stop accumulator, free of charge. The recall began September 23, 2020. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 20S49.
