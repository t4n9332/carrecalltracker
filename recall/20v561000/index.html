Volkswagen internal-use cars sold without safety standard confirmation

NHTSA campaign 20V561000 · Reported 2020-09-16 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Volkswagen Atlas
  • 2018 Volkswagen Golf R
  • 2018 Volkswagen Passat

What's wrong, in plain English

A small number of Volkswagens that the company had been using internally were sold to customers without confirming they were built to all federal safety rules, and they may have been changed in some way before being sold. Because nobody verified them, Volkswagen cannot say they meet the safety standards every new car must meet. Rather than repair them, Volkswagen is buying these vehicles back from the people who own them.

How serious is it?

The notice says a vehicle that does not meet all safety requirements may carry a higher risk of a crash or injury to the people inside, and it does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is one of these.
2. Call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 and mention recall 01E9.
3. Arrange the buyback — Volkswagen will repurchase the vehicle at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
UNKNOWN OR OTHER
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2018 Volkswagen Golf R and Atlas, 2017 Golf Alltrack and E-Golf and 2016-2018 Passat vehicles. These internal use vehicles were sold without confirmation that they were built to all applicable regulatory requirements and may have been modified prior to sale. As such, these vehicles may fail to comply with the requirements of various Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards.
Safety risk
Vehicles that do not meet all regulatory requirements may have an increased risk of a crash or injury to vehicle occupants.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners and will repurchase the vehicles. The recall began October 6, 2020. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 01E9.
