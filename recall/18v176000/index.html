Toyota Prius C missing passenger airbag warning label

NHTSA campaign 18V176000 · Reported 2018-03-15 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Toyota Prius C

What's wrong, in plain English

A sticker is missing. On these Toyota Prius C cars sold in Puerto Rico, the factory did not attach the label that warns about the front passenger airbag and tells you where children should ride. Nothing on the car is broken — but without that label, someone may put a child in the front seat where the airbag could hurt them in a crash. Toyota will mail replacement stick-on labels to owners at no cost.

How serious is it?

Low as a mechanical matter — the car is safe to drive — but the missing warning matters if children ride with you, so follow the standard rule of keeping kids in the back seat.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Prius C is on the list.
2. Contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371 about recall J0L if the labels have not arrived in the mail.
3. Apply the free replacement labels when you receive them.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018 Toyota Prius C vehicles sold in Puerto Rico. The front passenger air bag and recommended child occupant seating positions label may not have been attached during assembly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
If the label is missing, occupants may not follow the proper restraint usage guidelines, increasing the risk of injury in the event of a crash.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners and will mail the removable labels to all known owners of the subject vehicles, free of charge. The recall began on April 11, 2018. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. Toyota's number for this recall is J0L.
