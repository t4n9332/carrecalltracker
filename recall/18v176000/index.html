Toyota Prius C Missing Airbag Warning Label

NHTSA campaign 18V176000 · Reported 2018-03-15 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

low How serious is it? About this label

Without the correct label, occupants may not follow proper restraint guidelines, which increases the risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Toyota Prius C vehicles sold in Puerto Rico may be missing a label that identifies the front passenger air bag and shows the recommended seating positions for children. Without this label attached to the vehicle, occupants may not have the information they need to know how to properly seat and restrain children, or to follow the correct guidance for that passenger seat.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Contact a dealer if you have questions, or call Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. 3. Toyota will mail the replacement label to owners, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018 Toyota Prius C vehicles sold in Puerto Rico. The front passenger air bag and recommended child occupant seating positions label may not have been attached during assembly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
If the label is missing, occupants may not follow the proper restraint usage guidelines, increasing the risk of injury in the event of a crash.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners and will mail the removable labels to all known owners of the subject vehicles, free of charge. The recall began on April 11, 2018. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. Toyota's number for this recall is J0L.
