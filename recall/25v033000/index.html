Subaru Forester alloy wheels may be improperly attached

NHTSA campaign 25V033000 · Reported 2025-01-23 · Subaru of America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Subaru Forester

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2025 Foresters left the factory with 18-inch aluminum alloy wheels that were made wrong in the spot where the lug nuts press against the wheel. Because that surface is not shaped correctly, the lug nuts may not clamp the wheel tightly to the car. A wheel that is not properly attached can make the vehicle unstable and hard to control. Subaru dealers will inspect the wheels and replace any bad ones at no charge.

How serious is it?

This is serious, because a wheel that is not holding tight can cause a sudden loss of control; the notice does not say to park the car, but do not put this off.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Forester is on the list.
2. Call your Subaru dealer now and ask for the wheel inspection under recall WRB-25; inspection and any replacement wheels are free.
3. Report any vibration, wobble, or clicking from a wheel when you book the appointment. Subaru customer service: 1-844-373-6614.

Official notice

Component
WHEELS
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2025 Forester vehicles equipped with 18" aluminum alloy wheels. The wheels may have been manufactured with an incorrectly specified lug seat surface, which can result in the wheel being improperly attached and cause a loss of vehicle stability.
Safety risk
A loss of vehicle stability increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the wheels, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 28, 2025. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRB-25.
