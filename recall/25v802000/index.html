Volvo VNL Trucks May Lose Secured Cargo Bars

NHTSA campaign 25V802000 · Reported 2025-11-20 · Volvo Trucks North America

high How serious is it? About this label

An unsecured load lock bar can fall or shift, creating a road hazard and increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain VNL trucks, the clamps that hold the load lock bars in place on the back of the cab may loosen over time. If a clamp loosens, the load lock bar it holds can come unsecured. Dealers will replace the load lock clamps.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Volvo Trucks customer service at 800-528-6586 (reference RVXX2512) to schedule service.
3. The clamp replacement is free.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:MECHANICAL
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2024-2026 VNL (4) vehicles. The load lock clamps, located on the back of the cab, may loosen and cause the load lock bars to become unsecured.
Safety risk
A loose or unsecured load lock bar can result in a loss of cargo, creating a road hazard and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the load lock clamps, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 18, 2025. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2512. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning November 27, 2025.
