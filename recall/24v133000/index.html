Chevrolet Colorado GMC Canyon Camera May Trigger Sudden Braking

NHTSA campaign 24V133000 · Reported 2024-02-22 · General Motors, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Unexpected automatic braking can cause the truck to abruptly stop or slow down, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Colorado and Canyon trucks have a front camera system that may mistakenly detect an obstacle that isn't there. This can cause the automatic emergency braking system to engage unexpectedly.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is affected.
Contact a Chevrolet dealer at 1-800-222-1020 or a GMC dealer at 1-800-462-8782.
The camera software update is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:ADAS:DRIVER MONITORING:CAMERA/SENSOR
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023 Chevrolet Colorado and GMC Canyon vehicles. The front camera module (FCM) system may falsely detect an obstacle, causing the automatic emergency braking (AEB) to engage unexpectedly.
Safety risk
Unexpected automatic emergency braking can cause the vehicle to abruptly stop or slow down, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the front camera module (FCM) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 5, 2024. Owners may contact GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is A232424660.
