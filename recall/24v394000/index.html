Ford Mustang clutch line may melt, leak fluid

NHTSA campaign 24V394000 · Reported 2024-06-03 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Ford Mustang

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2024 Mustangs, the clutch pressure line may not be held in place properly and can touch hot exhaust parts. The heat can melt the line, which leads to a brake fluid leak. If that happens, the car can lose drive power while you are driving, and leaking brake fluid can catch fire if it meets a source of ignition. Ford dealers will inspect the clutch pressure line and repair it if needed, at no charge.

How serious is it?

This can cause both a loss of drive power and a fire, so have the car inspected as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Mustang is affected. 2. Call your Ford dealer for an inspection appointment, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall number 24S36). 3. The inspection and any repair are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:CLUTCH ASSEMBLY:LINKAGE:HYDRAULIC HOSE/FITTINGS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2024 Mustang vehicles. The clutch pressure line may be improperly secured and contact hot exhaust components, which can result in melting of the clutch pressure line and cause a brake fluid leak.
Safety risk
A melted clutch pressure line can result in a loss of drive power, increases the risk of a crash. A brake fluid leak increases the risk of fire in the presence of an ignition source.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the clutch pressure line and repair it as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 14, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S36.
