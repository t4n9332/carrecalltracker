Ford Maverick, Escape and Lincoln Corsair may lose power

NHTSA campaign 24V330000 · Reported 2024-05-10 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing drive power in moving traffic raises the risk of a crash, so while the notice does not tell you to stop driving, book the update right away.

What's wrong, in plain English

The software in the hybrid system on these vehicles can slip the transmission into neutral on its own. When that happens the engine keeps running but no power reaches the wheels, so the vehicle coasts and slows down even though you are pressing the gas. That can leave you stranded in traffic with little warning. The fix is a software update that a dealer loads onto the vehicle at no charge; it does not require new parts.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered by campaign 24V330000.
2. Call your Ford or Lincoln dealer to schedule the software update, or reach Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 24S33).
3. Get the update installed — it is free and usually a same-day visit.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:CONTROL MODULE (TCM/PCM/TECM)
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Maverick, 2020-2022 Escape, and 2021-2022 Lincoln Corsair vehicles. The Hybrid Powertrain Control Module (HPCM) software may cause the vehicle to shift into neutral unexpectedly, causing a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the HPCM software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 22, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S33.
