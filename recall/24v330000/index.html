Ford Escape, Maverick may shift to neutral unexpectedly

NHTSA campaign 24V330000 · Reported 2024-05-10 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

This recall covers certain Ford Maverick, Ford Escape, and Lincoln Corsair vehicles. A software problem in the system that manages the hybrid powertrain may cause the vehicle to shift into neutral unexpectedly, resulting in a loss of drive power.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 to schedule the software update.
3. The update is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:CONTROL MODULE (TCM/PCM/TECM)
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Maverick, 2020-2022 Escape, and 2021-2022 Lincoln Corsair vehicles. The Hybrid Powertrain Control Module (HPCM) software may cause the vehicle to shift into neutral unexpectedly, causing a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the HPCM software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 22, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S33.
