Nissan Versa Fuel Tank May Leak, Cause Fire

NHTSA campaign 20V112000 · Reported 2020-02-26 · Nissan North America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

If a small hole is present and the tank is filled more than half full, a fuel leak may occur, which increases the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Nissan Versa vehicles, a manufacturing issue may cause the fuel tank's wall to be thinner than intended in certain areas. Because of this, a small hole could form in the seam area of the tank. This affects the fuel tank located underneath the vehicle, where fuel is stored, and relates specifically to how the tank was built rather than any other part of the fuel system.

What you should do

1. Check your vehicle's recall status by VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Until the tank is replaced, avoid filling the fuel tank more than half full. 3. Contact a Nissan dealer to have the fuel tank replaced; call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. 4. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020 Versa vehicles. Due to a manufacturing issue, the fuel tank wall thickness may be insufficient, potentially causing a small hole in the tank seam area.
Safety risk
If a small hole is present, a fuel leak may occur if the operator fills the tank over half full, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel tank, free of charge. The recall began April 6, 2020. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
