Nissan Versa gas tank may develop a leak

NHTSA campaign 20V112000 · Reported 2020-02-26 · Nissan North America, Inc. · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Nissan Versa

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2020 Nissan Versa cars, the wall of the gas tank may be too thin along the seam where the tank is joined together, and a small hole can form there. If a hole is present, fuel can leak out once you fill the tank more than about halfway. Leaking gasoline near hot engine and exhaust parts can catch fire. The fix is a complete replacement of the fuel tank by a Nissan dealer.

How serious is it?

A fuel leak means a fire risk, so this is serious; the notice does not tell owners to park the car, but get it in right away, and the leak described only occurs when the tank is filled over half full.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Versa is covered.
2. Call your Nissan dealer, or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669, and ask for the fuel tank recall as soon as possible.
3. The dealer replaces the fuel tank free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020 Versa vehicles. Due to a manufacturing issue, the fuel tank wall thickness may be insufficient, potentially causing a small hole in the tank seam area.
Safety risk
If a small hole is present, a fuel leak may occur if the operator fills the tank over half full, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel tank, free of charge. The recall began April 6, 2020. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
