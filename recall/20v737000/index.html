Audi A5, S5 Rear Wiring Lacks Scuff Protection

NHTSA campaign 20V737000 · Reported 2020-11-25 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

If the wiring is damaged in a rear crash, the doors may not open from the outside and the hazard lights may not work, raising the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some A5 and S5 Sportback vehicles, the wiring for the rear lights may be missing a protective covering near the spare tire area. Without that covering, the wires could be damaged in a rear-end crash. If that happens, the doors might not open from the outside, and the hazard lights could stop working. Dealers will add the missing protection at no cost.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834 to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TAIL LIGHTS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2021 Audi RS5 Sportback, 2020-2021 Audi A5 Sportback, and S5 Sportback vehicles. The wiring harness for the rear lights could be missing the anti-abrasion protection in the area of the spare wheel well.
Safety risk
Without the scuff protection, the wires may become damaged during a rear crash, disabling the doors from being opened from the outside, causing a loss of hazard lights, and increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will apply scuff protection, free of charge. The recall began January 21, 2021. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 97EU.
