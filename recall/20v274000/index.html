Audi A6 and A7 passenger airbag may not open properly

NHTSA campaign 20V274000 · Reported 2020-05-13 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Audi A6
  • 2020 Audi A7

What's wrong, in plain English

The front passenger airbag is packed inside the dashboard and is meant to burst out through a hidden seam when it inflates. On these vehicles, that seam may not tear open the way it should. In a crash, the airbag may not open and fill correctly, so it may not protect the passenger as designed. Small pieces of plastic from the dashboard could also be thrown into the cabin. Dealers replace the whole dashboard panel at no cost.

How serious is it?

This is serious because the airbag may not protect a front-seat passenger in a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so the car can still be driven while you wait for the repair.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included. 2. Call an Audi dealer to book the free instrument panel replacement, or reach Audi customer service at 1-800-822-2834 (recall 70H6). 3. Keep the appointment even if everything seems normal, since there is no way to see the problem from inside the car.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL:PASSENGER SIDE:INFLATOR MODULE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2020 Audi A6, A6 Allroad and A7 vehicles. The passenger air bag may not properly deploy through the instrument panel tear seam.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, the passenger air bag may not properly deploy and inflate, and small plastic parts may enter the cabin, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will replace the instrument panels, free of charge. The recall began June 24, 2020. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-822-2834. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 70H6.
