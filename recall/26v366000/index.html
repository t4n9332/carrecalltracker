Honda CR-V and Accord Hybrid tire kit cap flies off

NHTSA campaign 26V366000 · Reported 2026-06-04 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Honda Accord Hybrid
  • 2023 Honda CR-V Hybrid
  • 2024 Honda Accord Hybrid
  • 2024 Honda CR-V Hybrid
  • 2025 Honda Accord Hybrid
  • 2025 Honda CR-V Hybrid

What's wrong, in plain English

These hybrids come with a tire repair kit instead of a spare tire. Inside the kit is a bottle of sealant you squeeze into a flat tire. Pressure can build up inside that bottle while you use it, and the cap can pop off. A cap flying off under pressure can hit you or anyone standing nearby and cause an injury. Honda has already mailed a warning letter, and the parts needed for the final repair are expected in fall 2026.

How serious is it?

The danger is an injury to whoever is using the tire repair kit, not a problem with how the car drives, and the notice places no restriction on driving the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is covered. 2. Call your Honda dealer or Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870 about recall DOV and ask when the final parts are available. 3. Have the nozzle or sealant bottle replaced free of charge once the remedy is ready.

Official notice

Component
TIRES:TEMPORARY/EMERGENCY SPARE TIRE
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023-2026 CR-V Hybrid, Accord Hybrid, and 2025-2026 CR-V Fuel Cell EV vehicles. When using the tire repair kit, pressure may build within the sealant bottle, causing the bottle cap to detach.
Safety risk
A detached bottle cap can become a projectile and strike the user or others nearby, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the tire repair kit nozzle or sealant bottle, free of charge. Interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk were mailed July 27, 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available, anticipated in Fall 2026. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is DOV. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning June 11, 2026.
