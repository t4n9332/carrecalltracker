Acura RDX Power Steering Software May Fail

NHTSA campaign 25V582000 · Reported 2025-09-05 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing power steering assist takes more effort to steer and can increase the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Honda is recalling certain Acura RDX vehicles because the software that controls the electric power steering assist may not be calibrated properly. If this happens, the steering system can enter a failsafe mode, resulting in a loss of power steering assist while driving. Dealers will reprogram the electric power steering software so it is calibrated correctly, and this repair will be done free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact an Acura dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. The software reprogramming will be done free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:ELECTRIC POWER ASSIST SYSTEM
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2025 Acura RDX vehicles. The software for the electric power steering (EPS) assist may not be calibrated properly, which can cause the EPS system to enter a failsafe mode, resulting in a loss of power steering assist.
Safety risk
A loss of power steering assist can require greater steering effort, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the EPS software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 20, 2025. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is GMS. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning September 11, 2025.
