Audi Q4 e-tron wrong headlight software affects parking lights

NHTSA campaign 24V361000 · Reported 2024-05-22 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Audi Q4 E-TRON

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2022-2024 Q4 e-tron and Q4 e-tron Sportback vehicles were built with the wrong software in the module that controls the headlights. Because of that mistake, the parking lights may not work the way they should when the headlights are on and you switch on a turn signal. That makes your car harder for other drivers to see in those moments. These vehicles do not meet the federal rule for lights. A dealer reloads the correct software.

How serious is it?

This is a moderate problem — reduced visibility to other drivers can increase crash risk, especially at night — but there is no warning to stop driving, so the car is safe to use until the software update.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Q4 e-tron is affected. 2. Call your Audi dealer, or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834, and give them recall number 941L. 3. Have the dealer load the new headlight control module software, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:LIGHTING CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Q4 E-TRON and Q4 E-TRON Sportback vehicles. The incorrect headlight control module software may have been installed. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Incorrect headlight control module software may cause the parking lights not to operate as intended when the headlights are turned on and the turn signals are activated, which can reduce vehicle visibility and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the headlight control module with a new data set, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 6, 2024. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 941L.
