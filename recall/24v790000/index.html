Volvo trucks' electronic control unit may misread signals

NHTSA campaign 24V790000 · Reported 2024-10-23 · Volvo Trucks North America

high How serious is it? About this label

When this control unit malfunctions, safety systems that depend on it, including automatic traction control, ABS, electronic stability control, active cruise control, and the collision mitigation system, may work less well or stop working, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

These trucks use a Bendix electronic control unit that can be affected by electrical noise and a low signal in the power line carrier. This can cause the unit to process commands incorrectly or to stop working. This affects certain Volvo VN, VAH, VHD, and VNRE trucks. Volvo Trucks is recalling these vehicles so dealers can reprogram the control unit's software.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Volvo Trucks dealer, or call Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586, to schedule the repair.
3. The software reprogramming is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2020-2025 VN, VAH, VHD and VNRE trucks, equipped with Bendix EC80 Advanced Electronic Control Units (ECU). Electrical noise and low signal to the power line carrier may cause the ECU to incorrectly process commands or stop working.
Safety risk
Safety systems that depend on the ECU (Automatic Traction Control, ABS, Electronic Stability Control, Active Cruise Control, and Collison Mitigation System) may have diminished or lost functionality, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the ECU software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 9, 2024. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586. Volvo Truck's number for this recall is RVXX2409.
