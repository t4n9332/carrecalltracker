Mercedes Sprinter swivel seat wiring can disable side airbag

NHTSA campaign 21V410000 · Reported 2021-06-02 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 1500
  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500
  • 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 3500
  • 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Sprinter vans built with swivel front seats may have the seat wiring routed the wrong way. When the seat turns, the wires can get pinched or jammed in the seat frame and damaged. Those wires run to the side airbag built into the seat. If they are damaged, that airbag may go off when it should not, or fail to go off in a crash. A dealer moves the wiring clip to the correct spot at no charge.

How serious is it?

Serious but not a park-it recall: the van is fine to drive, though a side airbag that fires unexpectedly or fails in a crash can injure people, so schedule the free fix soon.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is included.
2. Call a Mercedes-Benz/Freightliner Sprinter dealer, or Daimler Vans USA at 1-877-762-8267 (recall VS3DREHSEL), for an appointment.
3. Have the wiring clip repositioned — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling 2019-2021 Mercedes Benz Sprinter and Freightliner Sprinter vehicles equipped with swivel seats. The wiring harnesses for the front seats may have been routed incorrectly and could become jammed and damaged in the swivel seat frames.
Safety risk
Damage to the wiring harness can prevent the side air bag within the seat to deploy inadvertently or not deploy as designed, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will attach the mounting clip to the correct position, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on August 11, 2021. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3DREHSEL.
