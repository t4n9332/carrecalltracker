Chrysler Pacifica, Voyager: Side Air Bag Seal Issue

NHTSA campaign 25V302000 · Reported May 8, 2025 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

An air bag that doesn't hold pressure properly increases the risk of ejection and injury during a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Pacifica and Voyager minivans, the side curtain air bags may not hold pressure properly because of seams that weren't sealed correctly. These air bags are designed to stay inflated and help protect occupants during a crash, but this sealing problem means they may lose pressure and not work as intended. Dealers will replace the affected side curtain air bags to fix the issue.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Chrysler dealer, or call customer service at 800-853-1403.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V302000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022-2025 Pacifica and Voyager vehicles. The right and left side curtain air bags may have insufficient pressure retention from improperly sealed seams. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 226, "Ejection Mitigation."
Safety risk
An air bag with insufficient pressure retention increases the risk of ejection and injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the side curtain air bags, as necessary free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning October 14, 2025. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's numbers for this recall are 06C, 54C, 55C. This recall is replaced by NHTSA recall number 26V189. Vehicles already repaired under this recall will need to have the new remedy completed.
