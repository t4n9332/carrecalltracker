Volkswagen Tiguan rear tie rod bolt may be loose

NHTSA campaign 25V526000 · Reported 2025-08-13 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

Very serious, because a loose bolt here can mean losing control of the vehicle with little warning; the notice does not order owners to park it, but this should be your next stop rather than something you schedule weeks out.

What's wrong, in plain English

On the rear axle of these new Tiguans, a bolt that holds a steering link — called a tie rod — may be loose. A tie rod is a link that helps hold a wheel in its proper position. If that bolt is loose, you can lose control of the vehicle while driving. The repair is quick: the dealer replaces the affected bolt. Volkswagen mailed owner letters in September 2025, and there is no charge for the work.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm campaign 25V526000 (Volkswagen's number 42E7) applies to your Tiguan.
2. Call your Volkswagen dealer, or Volkswagen customer service at 800-893-5298, and ask for the earliest appointment.
3. Have the rear tie rod bolt replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:LINKAGES:TIE ROD ASSEMBLY
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2025 Tiguan vehicles. A tie rod bolt on the rear axle assembly may be loose, which can lead to a loss of vehicle control.
Safety risk
A loss of vehicle control increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the affected tie rod bolt, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 5, 2025. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 42E7.
