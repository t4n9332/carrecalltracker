Honda CR-V Owner's Guide Has Wrong Airbag Info

NHTSA campaign 19V694000 · Reported 2019-09-26 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

medium How serious is it? About this label

If a front passenger does not correctly understand how the airbag works, the notice says they may be at increased risk of personal injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Honda is recalling certain 2019 CR-V vehicles because the owner's guide incorrectly describes when the Passenger Airbag Off indicator should turn on. If the front passenger does not correctly understand how the airbag functions, they may be at an increased risk of personal injury in the event of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact your Honda dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. Honda will provide a corrected sticker for the owner's guide, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:OWNERS/SERVICE/OTHER MANUAL
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2019 CR-V vehicles. The owner's guide incorrectly describes when the "Passenger Airbag Off" indicator should illuminate. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
If the front passenger does not correctly understand how the airbag functions, they may be at an increased risk of personal injury in the event of a crash.
Remedy
Honda will notify owners and provide a sticker with the correct information to be added to the owner's guide, free of charge. The recall began November 14, 2019. Owner's may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is X6G.
