Cadillac Lyriq Battery Bolts May Be Loose

NHTSA campaign 25V483000 · Reported Jul 24, 2025 · General Motors, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

If the high-voltage battery isn't properly secured, it could be damaged in a crash, increasing the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Cadillac Lyriq and Vistiq vehicles may have loose or missing bolts holding the high-voltage battery to the floor of the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Cadillac dealer or Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223.
3. The inspection and repair are free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V483000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
General Motors (GM) is recalling certain 2026 Cadillac VISTIQ vehicles and 2025 Cadillac LYRIQ vehicles. The bolts attaching the high voltage battery to the interior floor may be missing or loose.
Safety risk
An improperly secured battery may become damaged in a crash, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the high-voltage battery bolts and replace or re-tighten the bolts, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are scheduled to be mailed September 8, 2025. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223. GM's number for this recall is N252511300.
