BMW iX and X1 Driver Air Bag May Not Deploy

NHTSA campaign 23V079000 · Reported 2023-02-14 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

An air bag that doesn't deploy correctly in a crash increases the risk of injury to the driver. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some BMW iX and X1 vehicles have a front driver's air bag that may have been built incorrectly. Because of this manufacturing problem, the air bag might not inflate the way it's designed to if the vehicle is in a crash. This means that in a collision, the driver may not get the protection the air bag is meant to provide, since it may not work correctly when it's needed most.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a BMW dealer or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. The air bag replacement is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2022-2023 iX xDrive40, iX xDrive50, iX M60 and 2022-2023 X1 xDrive28i vehicles. The front driver's air bag may have been manufactured incorrectly and may not deploy as intended during a crash.
Safety risk
A driver's air bag that does not deploy as intended during a crash increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver's air bag, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 14, 2023. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
