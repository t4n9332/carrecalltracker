Audi Q5, VW Atlas Engine Bearings May Fail

NHTSA campaign 22V753000 · Reported Oct 7, 2022 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

An engine that fails or stalls while driving increases the risk of a crash, and if the failure causes an oil leak, it can also increase the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Audi Q5 and Volkswagen Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles may have damaged connecting rod bearings inside the engine. Damaged bearings can cause the engine to fail while driving. If the engine failure also causes an oil leak, that leak can raise the chance of a fire.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834, or your dealer, to schedule an inspection.
3. Dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace the engine, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V753000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:HARD PARTS INTERNAL/MECHANICAL
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Audi Q5 Sportback, Q5, 2022-2023 Volkswagen Atlas FL, and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles. The engine connecting rod bearings may become damaged, which can result in engine failure.
Safety risk
A damaged engine can result in engine failure and a stall, increasing the risk of a crash. If the engine failure causes an oil leak, it can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace an affected engine, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed December 6, 2022. Owners may contact Volkswagen's customer service at 1-800-893-5298 and Audi's customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Volkswagen's numbers for this recall are 13i4 (VW) and 13i5 (Audi).
