BMW X4 tire label shows wrong size and pressure

NHTSA campaign 21V646000 · Reported 2021-08-18 · BMW of North America, LLC · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 BMW X4

What's wrong, in plain English

The tire information label on certain X4 xDrive30i vehicles lists the wrong tire size and the wrong air pressure. If you or a tire shop go by that sticker, the car could end up with tires that are the wrong size for it, or tires inflated to the wrong pressure. A dealer swaps in a corrected tire label free of charge, which puts the right numbers back on the car.

How serious is it?

The wrong tires or wrong tire pressure make a crash more likely, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your X4 is affected. 2. Contact your BMW dealer to have the label replaced, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417. 3. Have the new label installed at no cost.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain X4 xDrive30i vehicles. The tire label may contain the wrong tire size and pressure information. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
Incorrect tire size and pressure information could result in the wrong tires being installed, or improperly inflated tires, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the tire information label, free of charge. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
