Toyota Tacoma Brake Master Cylinder Seal Problem

NHTSA campaign 18V888000 · Reported Dec 13, 2018 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

Needing a longer distance to stop raises the chance of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Toyota Tacoma trucks, the brake master cylinder was not machined properly during manufacturing. This improper machining can damage one of the seals inside the master cylinder. A damaged seal can reduce how well the brakes work, which means the truck may need a longer distance to come to a stop. This reduced braking performance can make it harder to stop the vehicle as quickly as normal.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Tacoma is included.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer, or call Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371, to schedule the repair.
3. The brake master cylinder replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V888000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:MASTER CYLINDER
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Toyota Tacoma vehicles. Improper machining of the brake master cylinder may result in internal damage of one of the seals, affecting brake performance.
Safety risk
Reduced brake performance can lengthen the distance needed to stop the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners, and dealers will replace the brake master cylinder, free of charge. The recall began January 28, 2019. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. Toyota's numbers for this recall are J16/J06.
