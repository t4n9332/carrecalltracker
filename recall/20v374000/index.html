Ram 1500 and ProMaster side curtain airbags may not inflate

NHTSA campaign 20V374000 · Reported 2020-06-25 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious, because the airbag may not protect you in a crash, though the vehicle drives normally and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2020 Ram ProMaster vans and Ram 1500 pickups have a side curtain airbag — the one that drops down along the windows to protect your head — that was put together wrong. A part inside that channels the gas into the bag was not crimped properly, so it can come loose when the airbag goes off. If that happens in a crash, the airbag may not fill the way it should and may not protect you. Dealers will replace the airbags free.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered.
2. Call your Ram dealer, or FCA US at 1-800-853-1403 (recall W52), for an appointment.
3. Have the side curtain airbag assemblies replaced at no charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2020 Ram Promaster vans and 1500 pickups. The affected vehicles may have an improperly crimped diffuser in the side curtain airbag which may result in the diffuser detaching from the inflator in the event of an air bag deployment.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, if the diffuser detaches from the inflator during air bag deployment, the air bag may not inflate properly, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
FCA US will notify owners, and dealers will replace the side curtain air bag assemblies, free of charge. The recall began July 16, 2020. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US's number for this recall is W52.
