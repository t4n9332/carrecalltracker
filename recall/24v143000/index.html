Ford F-650 parking brake may release without warning

NHTSA campaign 24V143000 · Reported 2024-02-23 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

A heavy truck rolling away with nobody at the wheel can cause a serious crash or injury; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but treat this as urgent.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2023 and 2024 Ford F-650 and F-750 trucks have a faulty parking brake. Normally you must press a button on the lever before the brake will let go. On these trucks the lever can release without that button being pressed, which means the parking brake can come off on its own. If that happens while the truck is parked, it can roll away and strike whatever or whoever is in its path.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included.
2. Call your Ford dealer and schedule the free replacement of the parking brake handle assembly under recall 24S09; letters were mailed June 17, 2024.
3. Until it is repaired, park on level ground and chock the wheels. Ford customer service: 1-866-436-7332.

Official notice

Component
PARKING BRAKE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023-2024 F-650 and F-750 vehicles. The parking brake may malfunction and allow the lever to be released without pressing the release button.
Safety risk
A parking brake that is released unexpectedly may result in a vehicle roll away, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the parking brake handle assembly. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 17, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S09.
