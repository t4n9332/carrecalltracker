Lincoln Aviator Power Windows May Not Reverse

NHTSA campaign 25V484000 · Reported Jul 25, 2025 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

A window that fails to reverse properly can pinch a passenger before it retracts, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2025 Lincoln Aviator vehicles have rear side windows that may not work correctly with the automatic closing feature. When one of these windows is closing and encounters an object in its path, it may exert too much force before automatically reversing direction. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety requirement for how power windows are supposed to behave when they meet resistance while closing.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact a dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. 3. Dealers will update the vehicle's software, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V484000. Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:POWER WINDOW DEVICES AND CONTROLS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2025 Lincoln Aviator vehicles. When closing the windows with the global closing feature, the side rear windows may exert excessive force when encountering an object, before they automatically reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 118, "Power-Operated Window Systems."
Safety risk
A closing window that does not reverse direction as expected can pinch a passenger before retracting, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will update the Body Control Module (BCM) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 7, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25C33.
