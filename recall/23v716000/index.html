Jeep Wagoneer third-row seat belts may not fasten

NHTSA campaign 23V716000 · Reported 2023-10-26 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Jeep Grand Wagoneer
  • 2022 Jeep Wagoneer
  • 2023 Jeep Grand Wagoneer
  • 2023 Jeep Wagoneer
  • 2024 Jeep Grand Wagoneer
  • 2024 Jeep Wagoneer

What's wrong, in plain English

In the third row, the seat belt may be hard to reach or may not latch properly into its buckle. That means someone riding back there could end up with no belt on at all, either because they cannot get to it or because it will not click in. A passenger who is not belted is much more likely to be badly hurt in a crash. Dealers inspect the third-row buckles and replace them where needed, at no cost.

How serious is it?

Moderate: the vehicle drives normally, but anyone in the third row may be unprotected in a crash, so avoid seating people there until it is fixed.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 23V716000 (FCA's number A7A).
2. Contact your Jeep dealer, or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to schedule the buckle inspection.
3. The inspection and any buckle replacement are free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS: REAR/OTHER:BUCKLE ASSEMBLY
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Jeep Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer vehicles. The third-row seat belts may not allow occupants to properly access or fasten the seat belt to the buckle. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 208, "Occupant Crash Protection" and 209, "Seat Belt Assemblies."
Safety risk
An unfastened seat belt may increase the risk of injury to occupants during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the third-row seat belt buckle, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 16, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is A7A.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
