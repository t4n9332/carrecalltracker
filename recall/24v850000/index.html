Ford Escape Passenger Air Bag Cover May Break

NHTSA campaign 24V850000 · Reported 2024-11-08 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

During a crash, a broken air bag cover could send fragments into the vehicle, or the air bag may not deploy properly, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain 2024-2025 Escape vehicles because the passenger side instrument panel cover may have been manufactured incorrectly. This can prevent the air bag underneath from deploying as intended. During deployment, the air bag cover may break apart and send fragments into the vehicle, or the air bag itself may deploy improperly. As a result, these vehicles do not meet federal occupant crash protection standards.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Escape is affected.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, to schedule service.
3. Dealers will replace the passenger side instrument panel cover, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Escape vehicles. The passenger side instrument panel cover may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can cause the air bag not to deploy as intended. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
During air bag deployment, the air bag cover may break and project fragments into the vehicle, or the air bag may deploy improperly, increasing the risk of occupant injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the passenger side instrument panel cover, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on February 18, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24C38.
