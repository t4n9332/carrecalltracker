Lincoln MKC Battery Sensor Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 23V378000 · Reported May 26, 2023 · Ford Motor Company

critical How serious is it? About this label

Owners are told to park outside and away from structures until the repair is complete. A fire in the engine compartment increases the risk of injury.

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Lincoln MKC vehicles have a battery monitor sensor that can short-circuit and overheat. This can start a fire in the engine compartment, and it can happen either while the vehicle is parked or while it's being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Park outside and away from structures until the repair is complete.
3. Contact a Ford dealer or call 1-866-436-7332 to schedule the free repair.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V378000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2015-2019 Lincoln MKC vehicles. The battery monitor sensor may short-circuit and overheat, causing a fire in the engine compartment while parked or driving.
Safety risk
An engine compartment fire while parked or driving can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. Dealers will add an in-line fuse to the battery monitor sensor power circuit, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 8, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23S28.
