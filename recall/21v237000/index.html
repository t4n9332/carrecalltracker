Mercedes-Benz Sprinter VIN does not match its labels

NHTSA campaign 21V237000 · Reported 2021-04-05 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the owner does not get information about open safety recall campaigns, the notice says that increases the risk of injury, a crash, or fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Daimler Vans USA is recalling one Mercedes-Benz Sprinter van. The vehicle identification number shown on the dashboard, on the certification label, and on the vehicle body are not the same. Because of that mismatch, the van does not meet the federal certification rule. That number is how a vehicle is identified, so the owner may not receive information about open safety recalls. A dealer will replace the number on the dashboard and on the certification label, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is covered.
2. Contact a dealer, or DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267, to arrange the label replacement (the recall number is VS3VINCHA).
3. The work is free.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling one 2019 Mercedes Benz Sprinter vehicle. The VIN number listed on the vehicle's dash board, certification label, and body-in-white do not match. As such, this vehicle fails to comply with the requirements of 49 CFR Part 567, "Certification."
Safety risk
The consumer may not receive information about open safety-recall campaigns, increasing the risk of injury, a crash, or fire.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify the owner, and a dealer will replace the VIN listed on the vehicle's dash board and certification label, free of charge. The recall began May 11, 2021. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3VINCHA.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
