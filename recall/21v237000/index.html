Mercedes Sprinter van has mismatched identification numbers

NHTSA campaign 21V237000 · Reported 2021-04-05 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter (VS30)

What's wrong, in plain English

Just one 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter van is affected. The vehicle identification number, the code that identifies your exact van, does not match in all the places it appears: the dashboard, the certification label, and the van's body. Because those numbers disagree, the owner might not be notified about future safety recalls on this van, so a real problem could go unrepaired. A dealer will correct the number on the dashboard and on the certification label.

How serious is it?

Nothing on the van is mechanically wrong and it is safe to keep driving; the concern is only that recall notices might not reach you, so get the labels corrected at your convenience.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your van is the one covered by recall 21V237000 (recall number VS3VINCHA).
2. Call Daimler Vans USA customer service at 1-877-762-8267 or your Sprinter dealer to arrange the label correction.
3. Have the dealer replace the dashboard and certification label numbers, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling one 2019 Mercedes Benz Sprinter vehicle. The VIN number listed on the vehicle's dash board, certification label, and body-in-white do not match. As such, this vehicle fails to comply with the requirements of 49 CFR Part 567, "Certification."
Safety risk
The consumer may not receive information about open safety-recall campaigns, increasing the risk of injury, a crash, or fire.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify the owner, and a dealer will replace the VIN listed on the vehicle's dash board and certification label, free of charge. The recall began May 11, 2021. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3VINCHA.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
