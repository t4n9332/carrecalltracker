Subaru Crosstrek and Forester rear suspension bolts can fall out

NHTSA campaign 21V263000 · Reported 2021-04-15 · Subaru of America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Subaru Crosstrek
  • 2019 Subaru Crosstrek
  • 2019 Subaru Forester

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts that hold the rear stabilizer bar brackets in place can work loose and fall out. The stabilizer bar is part of the rear suspension, and it helps keep the car steady in turns. If the bolts go missing, the bracket can shift out of position and the car may not handle the way you expect. Subaru dealers will check the bolts, tighten them to the correct setting, and install any that are missing.

How serious is it?

Serious enough to get fixed promptly because a shifting bracket raises the chance of a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Call your Subaru dealer, or Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614, and ask for recall WRD-21. 3. Have the bolts retorqued and replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Subaru Crosstrek, 2019 Crosstrek Hybrid, and 2019 Forester vehicles. The rear stabilizer bracket bolts may loosen and detach.
Safety risk
Missing bolts may allow the rear stabilizer bracket to shift, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Subaru will notify owners, and dealers will retorque and replace any missing bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 17, 2021. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRD-21.
