BMW Diesel High-Pressure Fuel Pump Can Fail, Stalling Engine

NHTSA campaign 21V586000 · Reported 2021-07-29 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 BMW X5

What's wrong, in plain English

These BMW diesels have a high-pressure fuel pump that can fail. If it quits, the engine can shut off while you are driving — you could lose power in traffic or on the highway. The recall covers a range of diesel models, including the X5 xDrive35d, 328d and 535d. Dealers will replace the high-pressure fuel pump at no cost. Owner letters were mailed January 14, 2022.

How serious is it?

An engine that stalls while you are moving raises the risk of a crash, so get it done soon; the notice does not tell owners to park the car.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your diesel BMW is included.
2. Contact a BMW dealer or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 to book the work.
3. Have the high-pressure fuel pump replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2014-2018 328d, 328d xDrive, X5 xDrive35d, 2014-2016 535d, 535d xDrive, 2015 740Ld xDrive, and 2015-2017 X3 xDrive28d vehicles. The high-pressure fuel pump may fail.
Safety risk
Fuel pump failure can cause an engine stall while driving, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the high-pressure fuel pump, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on January 14, 2022. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
