Jeep Compass Front Head Restraints Improperly Welded

NHTSA campaign 22V248000 · Reported Apr 14, 2022 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Improperly welded head restraints can raise the risk of injury to people in the front seats during a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2022 Jeep Compass vehicles have front seat head restraints that were not welded correctly. Because of this welding problem, the vehicles don't meet the federal safety standard that covers head restraints.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to have both front head restraints replaced.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V248000. Official notice

Component
SEATS:FRONT ASSEMBLY:HEAD RESTRAINT
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Jeep Compass vehicles. The front seat head restraints were not welded properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 202, "Head Restraints."
Safety risk
Improperly welded head restraints can result in an increased risk of injury to seat occupants during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace both front seat head restraints, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 3, 2022. Owners may contact FCA US LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is Z36.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
