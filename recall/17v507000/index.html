BMW M4 front seat belts may not lock

NHTSA campaign 17V507000 · Reported 2017-08-16 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 BMW M4

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2018 BMW 430i, 440i and M4 convertibles may have front seat belts built with the wrong locking parts inside the retractor, the mechanism that is supposed to grab and hold the belt when the car stops hard. Because the parts do not match, the belt may not lock and may fail to hold the person in the seat during a crash, raising the risk of injury. A dealer will check both front belts and replace them if needed.

How serious is it?

Serious, because a front seat belt may not hold you in a crash; there is no instruction to stop driving, but get the belts inspected soon and keep wearing them in the meantime.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is affected. 2. Contact your BMW dealer or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 to schedule the front seat belt inspection. 3. The inspection and any replacement belts are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2018 430i, 430i xDrive, 440i, 440i xDrive, and M4 convertible vehicles. The Emergency Locking Retractors (ELR) within the front seat belt assemblies may have been produced with incompatible vehicle-sensitive locking mechanism housings.
Safety risk
If the vehicle has an incompatible vehicle-sensitive locking mechanism, the seat belt may not restrain the seat occupant in the event of a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and, if necessary replace, the front seat belt assemblies, free of charge. The recall began October 13, 2017. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
