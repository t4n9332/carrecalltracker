NHTSA campaign 22V473000 · Reported Jul 1, 2022 · Subaru of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

A loose connection like this can lead to the engine stalling while driving, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In these vehicles, the wiring that runs to the engine's control computer may have been damaged. This damage can create a loose electrical connection where the wiring plugs into the computer. A loose connection like this can cause the engine to stall while you are driving. Subaru will fix this by replacing the damaged wiring harness along with the engine control computer, so this loose connection can no longer cause the engine to stall.

What you should do

1. Check your vehicle's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if it's affected.
2. Contact a Subaru dealer, or call Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614, to schedule the repair.
3. The wiring harness and engine control unit will be replaced free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V473000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECU/ECM)
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2022 Outback vehicles. The engine wiring harness may have been damaged, resulting in a loose electrical connection to the engine control unit (ECU).
Safety risk
A loose electrical connection to the ECU may cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the engine wiring harness and ECU, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 30, 2022. Owners may contact Subaru's customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRC-22.
