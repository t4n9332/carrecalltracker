Subaru Outback wiring fault may cause engine stall

NHTSA campaign 22V473000 · Reported 2022-07-01 · Subaru of America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Subaru Outback

What's wrong, in plain English

The wiring bundle that runs to the engine may have been damaged during building, leaving a loose connection at the engine's computer. That computer manages how the engine runs. A loose connection can make the engine cut out and stall, which could happen while you are driving and leave you without power. Subaru will replace both the engine wiring harness and the engine computer at no cost to you. Owner letters were expected to go out August 30, 2022.

How serious is it?

An engine that stalls in traffic can lead to a crash, so this is serious; Subaru has not told owners to stop driving, but do not put the repair off.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall WRC-22 applies to your Outback.
Call a Subaru dealer for an appointment, or reach Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614.
Have the wiring harness and engine computer replaced — both are free.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECU/ECM)
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2022 Outback vehicles. The engine wiring harness may have been damaged, resulting in a loose electrical connection to the engine control unit (ECU).
Safety risk
A loose electrical connection to the ECU may cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the engine wiring harness and ECU, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 30, 2022. Owners may contact Subaru's customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRC-22.
