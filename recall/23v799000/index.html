Ram truck light controls may swap signals and high beams

NHTSA campaign 23V799000 · Reported 2023-11-28 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Turn signals or high beams that do not work as intended can confuse other road users or reduce their visibility, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain Ram trucks. The control module in the steering column can mix up the light controls: using the turn signal may switch on the high beams, and using the high beams may switch on a turn signal. The turn signal may also fail to shut itself off after a turn. Because of this, these trucks do not meet the federal safety standard for lamps and reflective equipment. Dealers will update the module's software and replace the module if needed.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your truck is covered. 2. Contact a dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and mention recall B2A or D5A. 3. The software update, inspection and any replacement are free.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:STEERING CONTROL MODULE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2023 Ram 1500 Classic, Ram 3500 Classic, 3500 Cab Chassis, 2023-2024 Ram 2500, 4500/5500 Cab Chassis, and Ram 3500 vehicles. The steering column control module may cause the high beams to activate when the turn signal is used or activate the turn signal when the high beams are used. In addition, the turn signal self-canceling feature may not function properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Turn signals and/or high beams that do not function as intended may cause confusion or reduced visibility to other road users, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the steering column control module (SCCM) software, and inspect and replace the module, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 19, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's numbers for this recall are B2A and D5A.
