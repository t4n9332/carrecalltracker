Ford Transit rear seat cushion may not lock upright

NHTSA campaign 19V229000 · Reported 2019-03-22 · TransitWorks · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Ford Transit
  • 2018 Ford Transit Connect

What's wrong, in plain English

These vans were fitted by an outside company with Freedman GO-ES fold-away seats. When you flip the rear seat cushion down from its stored position and someone sits on it, the cushion may not lock securely in place. In a crash or a sudden stop, the person's weight can make the seat move instead of holding firm. The seat maker supplies a free repair kit, and owners were told to contact Freedman Seating to get it.

How serious is it?

Serious in a crash — the van can still be driven, but anyone in a seat that shifts during a hard stop or collision faces a higher risk of injury.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your van is covered. 2. Contact Freedman Seating at 1-800-443-4540, or TransitWorks at 1-855-337-9543, to request the repair kit. 3. Have the seat repaired — there is no charge to you.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
TransitWorks is recalling certain 2015-2018 Ford Transit, 2018 Ford Transit Connect, and 2016 Dodge Ram ProMaster vehicles modified to be equipped with Freedman Seating GO-ES seats. When the back seat cushion is rotated from the stowed position to the upright position, the seat cushion may not remain locked into place when under load, such as in a crash or a sudden stop.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, if the seat back moves, the seat occupant has an increased risk of injury.
Remedy
TransitWorks has notified owners and instructed them to contact Freedman seats for a repair kit. The recall began December 19, 2019. Owners may contact Freedman Seating customer service at 1-800-443-4540 or TransitWorks customer service at 1-855-337-9543.
