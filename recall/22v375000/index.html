What's wrong, in plain English

GM is recalling certain 2022 GMC Canyon and Chevrolet Colorado vehicles that have a manual passenger seat. A hook attachment on the front passenger seat may not have been welded properly, which can allow it to separate from the inboard track of the seat frame. If this weld fails, the seat may not properly hold the passenger in place, and that can increase the chance of injury if the vehicle is in a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020, or your dealer, to schedule an inspection.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V375000. Official notice

Component
SEATS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2022 GMC Canyon and Chevrolet Colorado vehicles equipped with a manual passenger seat. The front passenger seat may have a hook attachment that was not properly welded, allowing it to separate from the inboard track of the seat frame.
Safety risk
An improperly welded seat frame may not properly restrain the seat occupant, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the front passenger seat and replace the cushion frame, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 6, 2022. Owners may contact GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N222368320.
