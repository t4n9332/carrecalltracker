Hyundai Palisade windshield wipers may stop working entirely

NHTSA campaign 22V627000 · Reported 2022-08-15 · Hyundai Motor America

medium How serious is it? About this label

Losing your wipers in bad weather makes a crash more likely, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so it is reasonable to keep driving while you schedule the free repair.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2020-2021 Hyundai Palisade vehicles, the motor that drives the windshield wipers can quit working. When that happens, the wipers stop moving. In rain, snow, or road spray, you would have no way to clear the windshield, and you could lose much of your view of the road. The fix is a new driver-side wiper arm, which the dealer installs for you.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Palisade is included in campaign 22V627000 (Hyundai calls it 233).
2. Contact your Hyundai dealer, or Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460, to make an appointment.
3. The dealer will replace the driver-side windshield wiper arm at no charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2020 Hyundai Palisade
  • 2021 Hyundai Palisade

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD WIPER/WASHER:MOTOR
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Palisade vehicles. The windshield wiper motor may become inoperative, causing the wipers to fail.
Safety risk
Windshield wiper failure can reduce visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver-side windshield wiper arm, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 13, 2022. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 233.
