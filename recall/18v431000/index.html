BMW X3 rear turn signals flash wrong color

NHTSA campaign 18V431000 · Reported 2018-06-21 · BMW of North America, LLC · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 BMW X3

What's wrong, in plain English

On some BMW X3 models, the rear turn signals don't all light up the same color. When you signal, the outer light on the side panel glows red while the inner light glows amber at the same time. Turn signals are supposed to be amber so other drivers can tell instantly that you're turning. A signal showing two different colors at once doesn't meet the federal rules for vehicle lighting. Dealers will look at the rear turn signal lights and swap out any that are wrong.

How serious is it?

This is a low-level safety concern — the mismatched colors may confuse drivers behind you and raise crash risk, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered. 2. Call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 or your local BMW dealer to book an appointment. 3. Have the dealer inspect and replace the rear turn signal lights — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TURN SIGNAL
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2018 BMW X3 xDrive30i and X3 M40i vehicles. The rear turn signals on the outboard side panel will illuminate red, while the inboard turn signals simultaneously illuminate amber. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Turn signals that flash multiple colors may confuse other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the rear turn signal lights, and replace if necessary, free of charge. The recall began August 20, 2018. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
