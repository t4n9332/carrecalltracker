What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Wagoneer S and Dodge Charger electric vehicles. A software problem can make the display in front of the driver stop working, so the information it normally shows is not available. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet several federal safety standards, covering vehicle lamps, stability control, brakes, the tire pressure monitoring system and occupant crash protection.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included — VINs for this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov starting April 30, 2026. 2. Contact a dealer to have the display software updated, or call FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall number 39D). 3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V262000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Jeep Wagoneer S and Dodge Charger electric vehicles. A software error may cause the instrument panel display to fail. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment," 126, "Electronic Stability Control Systems for Light Vehicles," 135, "Light Vehicle Brake Systems," 138, "Tire Pressure Monitoring System," and/or 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An instrument panel display that fails to show critical safety information, such as gear selection and warning lights, increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the instrument panel software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 14, 2026. Owners may contact FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's number for this recall is 39D. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning April 30, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
