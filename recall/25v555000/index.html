Polestar 3 front bumper piece may fall off

NHTSA campaign 25V555000 · Reported 2025-08-26 · Polestar Automotive USA, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious enough to handle promptly, since a part coming off at speed can cause a crash for you or someone behind you, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2025 Polestar 3 SUVs left the factory with bolts missing from the front bumper's hood wing piece. Without those bolts holding it in place, that piece of bodywork can work loose and fall off the car while you're driving. A bumper part lying in a traffic lane is a hazard for you and for drivers behind you. Polestar dealers will check for missing screws and replace the bumper and screws if any are gone, at no cost.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Polestar 3 is included.
2. Contact your Polestar dealer or customer service at 1-800-806-2504 and reference recall RP1064.
3. Have the bumper inspected and, if screws are missing, replaced — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:BUMPERS
Summary
Polestar Automotive USA, Inc. (Polestar) is recalling certain 2025 Polestar 3 vehicles. The hood wing front bumper may be missing bolts, allowing the bumper to detach.
Safety risk
A detached bumper can become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Polestar will inspect the hood wing bumper for missing screws. If any screws are missing, the bumper and screws will be replaced. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 30, 2025. Owners may contact Polestar's customer service at 1-800-806-2504. Polestar's number for this recall is RP1064.
