Toyota Highlander Front Bumper Cover May Detach

NHTSA campaign 23V720000 · Reported 2023-10-26 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

medium How serious is it? About this label

A bumper cover that comes loose or falls off can become a hazard on the road, increasing the chance of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Toyota is recalling certain Highlander and Highlander Hybrid SUVs. During normal driving, a minor impact to the front lower bumper cover may cause the cover to come loose or fall off the vehicle. This means something as small as a light bump could leave the front bumper cover hanging or detached from the car while it's on the road.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer (1-800-331-4331) to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:BUMPERS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2020-2023 Highlander & Highlander Hybrid vehicles. During normal vehicle operation, minor impact to the front lower bumper cover may result in the cover coming loose or detaching.
Safety risk
A detached front bumper cover can become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will repair or replace the upper and lower front bumper covers as necessary, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed April 22, 2024. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 23TB12/23TA12.
