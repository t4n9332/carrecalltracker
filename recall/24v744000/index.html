What's wrong, in plain English

The steering gearbox — the part that turns your steering wheel motion into the wheels turning — may have been built wrong on these Honda and Acura models. Parts inside can rub too hard against each other, which can make the vehicle difficult to steer. Difficulty steering raises the chance of a crash. The dealer will replace a spring inside the gear and add or move grease around as needed, all free of charge.

How serious is it?

Serious — trouble steering increases the risk of a crash; owners were not told to stop driving, but get the free repair soon, and see a dealer immediately if the steering feels wrong.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your vehicle is covered.
2. Contact your Honda or Acura dealer, or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (recall numbers SJS, MJU, QJT, VJV).
3. Have the steering gear spring replaced and grease serviced at no cost.

Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023-2025 Acura Integra, Civic Type R, CR-V Hybrid, CR-V, HR-V, 2022-2025 Civic, Civic Hatchback, 2024-2025 Acura Integra Type S, 2025 CR-V Fuel Cell EV, Civic Hybrid, and Civic Hatchback Hybrid vehicles. The steering gearbox assembly may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can cause excessive internal friction and lead to difficulty steering the vehicle.
Safety risk
Difficulty steering can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the worm gear spring and redistribute or add grease as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 18, 2024. Owners may contact Honda Customer Service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's numbers for this recall are SJS, MJU, QJT and VJV.
