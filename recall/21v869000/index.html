Ford Escape hybrid battery can fail, cutting drive power

NHTSA campaign 21V869000 · Reported 2021-11-10 · Ford Motor Company · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford Escape

What's wrong, in plain English

This recall covers a single 2021 Escape hybrid. The large battery pack that helps drive the car can fail. If it does, the car can suddenly lose power to the wheels while you are driving, with no time to prepare. You could be left coasting in traffic or on a highway. Ford is replacing the battery pack at no cost to you. Because only one vehicle is involved, most owners will not be affected.

How serious is it?

Serious — a sudden loss of drive power raises the risk of a crash, and while the notice does not tell you to park the vehicle, the repair should be done right away.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Escape is the vehicle involved. 2. If it is, contact your Ford dealer immediately, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and mention recall 21S48. 3. The dealer will replace the high voltage battery free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling one 2021 Escape vehicle equipped with a 2.5L hybrid powertrain. The high voltage battery may fail, resulting in a sudden loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A sudden loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
A dealer will replace the high voltage battery, free of charge. The owner notification letters were mailed December 1, 2021. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21S48.
