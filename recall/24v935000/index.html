Tesla Cybertruck, Model 3, Model Y: tire warning light fault

NHTSA campaign 24V935000 · Reported 2024-12-17 · Tesla, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Tesla Model 3
  • 2019 Tesla Model 3
  • 2020 Tesla Model 3
  • 2020 Tesla Model Y
  • 2021 Tesla Model 3
  • 2022 Tesla Model 3
  • 2023 Tesla Model 3
  • 2024 Tesla Cybertruck
  • 2024 Tesla Model 3
  • 2025 Tesla Model Y

What's wrong, in plain English

Your vehicle watches tire pressure and turns on a dashboard light when a tire is low. On these vehicles, that light may not stay on the next time you start the car, even though the tire is still low. You could keep driving on a soft tire without knowing it, and a poorly inflated tire handles badly and is more likely to fail. Tesla has already released a free software update that is sent to the car over the internet.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the vehicle is safe to keep driving, but you may not be warned about a low tire, which raises your risk of a crash.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Make sure your car has installed the over-the-air software update; check your tire pressures by hand in the meantime.
3. If the update has not arrived, call Tesla at 1-877-798-3752 (recall SB-24-00-018) — the fix is free.

Official notice

Component
TIRES:PRESSURE MONITORING AND REGULATING SYSTEMS
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2024 Cybertruck, 2017-2025 Model 3, and 2020-2025 Model Y vehicles. The tire pressure monitoring system (TPMS) warning light may not remain illuminated between drive cycles, failing to warn the driver of low tire pressure. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 138, "Tire Pressure Monitoring Systems."
Safety risk
Driving with improperly inflated tires increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla released an over-the-air (OTA) software update, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 15, 2025. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-24-00-018. (over-the-air software update)

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
