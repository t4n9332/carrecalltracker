Volvo heavy trucks may lose transmission shifting from blown fuse

NHTSA campaign 19V509000 · Reported 2019-07-02 · Volvo Trucks North America

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing the ability to shift in a loaded heavy truck can cause a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so you may keep driving while you arrange the free inspection.

What's wrong, in plain English

These Volvo heavy trucks have a fuse that protects the wiring for the transmission. On some of them, that fuse can blow. When it does, the transmission stops shifting gears, so the truck can be stuck and will not change gears the way the driver expects. That can leave you unable to speed up or slow down normally in traffic. A dealer will check the fuse and install a new one if needed, at no cost.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included in recall 19V509000.
2. Call Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586 (recall RVXX1903) or your Volvo dealer to schedule the inspection.
3. Have the dealer inspect and replace the fuse — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FUSES AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2018-2020 VAH, VHD, VNL, and VNR vehicles. The fuse in the transmission circuit may blow, preventing the transmission from shifting.
Safety risk
If the fuse blows the transmission may not shift, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Volvo Trucks will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and replace the fuse, if necessary, free of charge. The recall began August 7, 2019. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586. Volvo Truck's number for this recall is RVXX1903.
