Ford Escape and Bronco Sport fuel injector may leak

NHTSA campaign 25V165000 · Reported 2025-03-14 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Ford Escape
  • 2021 Ford Bronco Sport
  • 2021 Ford Escape
  • 2022 Ford Bronco Sport
  • 2022 Ford Escape
  • 2023 Ford Bronco Sport

What's wrong, in plain English

These vehicles had an earlier repair done incorrectly. A fuel injector, the part that sprays gasoline into the engine, can crack and let fuel leak out under the hood. Engine and exhaust parts nearby get very hot, so leaking gasoline there can catch fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside, but a fuel smell should be taken seriously. Dealers will update the engine software free.

How serious is it?

Very serious because of the fire risk; Ford does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but get it in right away and stop driving if you smell gasoline.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls for recall 25V165000, including if you already had recall 22V-859 or 24V-187 work done. 2. Contact your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and mention recall 25S21. 3. Get the free engine software update as soon as an appointment is available.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2022 Escape and 2021-2023 Bronco Sport vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall numbers 22V-859 or 24V-187. A fuel injector may crack and leak fuel inside the engine compartment.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source such as hot engine or exhaust components, can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will update the powertrain control module (PCM) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 22, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S21.
