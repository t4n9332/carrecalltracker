Nissan Ariya steering wheel bolt may be loose or missing

NHTSA campaign 23V131000 · Reported 2023-02-28 · Nissan North America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

This is very serious — a steering wheel that detaches means a sudden total loss of steering, and Nissan says that if the wheel feels loose you should not drive the car but have it towed to a dealer for immediate repair.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2023 Nissan Ariya vehicles, the bolt that holds the steering wheel onto the steering column may have been tightened incorrectly or never installed. If that bolt is loose or missing, the steering wheel can come off the column while you are driving, and you would have no way to steer. Nissan says to gently rock the steering wheel forward and back with your hands to check whether it feels loose. Dealers replace the bolt free.

What you should do

1. Gently rock the steering wheel forward and back; if it feels loose, stop driving and arrange for the dealer to transport the car. 2. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls and call Nissan at 1-800-867-7669 about recall PC956. 3. Have the dealer replace the steering wheel bolt — it is free.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2023 Ariya vehicles. The steering wheel bolt may have been tightened incorrectly or not installed.
Safety risk
A steering wheel with a loose or missing bolt can detach from the steering column, causing a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised to contact their dealer for transport and immediate repair if the steering wheel feels loose when gently rocking the wheel forward and back with their hands. Dealers will replace the steering wheel bolt, free of charge. Nissan began notifying owners on March 2, 2023 via phone and will follow up with owner notification letters beginning on March 30, 2023. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PC956.
