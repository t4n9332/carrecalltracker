GM vehicles may have badly welded driver seat frame

NHTSA campaign 22V359000 · Reported 2022-05-19 · General Motors, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

The seat may not hold you properly in a crash, raising your chance of injury, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving the vehicle.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Chevrolet Malibu, Equinox and Blazer models and the Cadillac XT4 may have a bad weld in the driver's seat cushion frame, in the part that tilts the seat up and down. Because the weld is not done properly, the frame may not hold the driver firmly in place during a crash. Dealers will inspect the driver's seat and replace the cushion frame if it needs it, at no charge to you. Notification letters were mailed to owners on June 23, 2022.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected. 2. Call your Chevrolet dealer (1-800-222-1020) or Cadillac dealer (1-800-458-8006) and give them GM recall number N212356050. 3. Have the seat inspected and the frame replaced if needed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2021 Chevrolet Malibu, 2022 Chevrolet Equinox, Blazer and Cadillac XT4 vehicles. The driver's seat cushion frame may have an improper weld in the power tilt-adjustment mechanism.
Safety risk
An improperly welded seat frame may not adequately restrain the driver in a crash, increasing their risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the driver's seat and replace the cushion frame, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 23, 2022. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N212356050.
