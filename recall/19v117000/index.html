Cadillac CT6 turn signals do not shut off

NHTSA campaign 19V117000 · Reported 2019-02-21 · General Motors LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Cadillac CT6

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2019 Cadillac CT6 cars, the turn signal does not switch itself off after you finish a turn. Normally, turning the steering wheel back cancels the blinker automatically. With this fault, the signal keeps flashing until you reach over and turn it off yourself, and it is easy to forget. Other drivers and people crossing the street may then think you are about to turn when you are not. A dealer will replace the turn signal switch.

How serious is it?

This is a moderate concern rather than an emergency, and the car is safe to drive as long as you check your blinker and turn it off manually after every turn.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included. 2. Contact your Cadillac dealer or Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006, recall N182207090. 3. Have the turn signal switch replaced at no charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TURN SIGNAL:SWITCH
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019 Cadillac CT6 vehicles. Rotation of the steering wheel will not automatically cancel the turn-signals from flashing. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
If the turn-signals do not automatically deactivate, the driver may fail to turn them off manually. An erroneous active turn-signal may mislead pedestrians and other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
GM has notified owners, and dealers will replace the turn-signal switch. The recall began March 11, 2019. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. The Cadillac number for this recall is N182207090.
