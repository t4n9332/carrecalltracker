Jeep Wagoneer S liftgate hinge cover may fall off

NHTSA campaign 26V141000 · Reported 2026-03-12 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Jeep Wagoneer S
  • 2025 Jeep Wagoneer S

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Jeep Wagoneer S vehicles, the cover over the rear liftgate hinge may not have been clipped fully into place when the vehicle was built. If it works free, it can come off the vehicle while you are driving. The main danger is to people behind you — a loose piece of trim in the road can cause another driver to crash. Your dealer will inspect the cover and refit or replace it as needed, free of charge.

How serious is it?

Low for you personally, since it does not affect how the vehicle drives, but the loose piece is a hazard to other drivers, so it is fine to keep driving while you book the repair.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls (VINs are searchable as of March 19, 2026).
2. Call your Jeep dealer or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 and cite recall 26D.
3. Bring the vehicle in for the free inspection and repair or replacement.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:HATCHBACK/LIFTGATE:HINGE AND ATTACHMENTS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2024-2026 Jeep Wagoneer S vehicles. The liftgate hinge cover may not be properly clipped into position and may detach from the vehicle.
Safety risk
A detached hinge cover can become a road hazard for other vehicles, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealer will inspect, repair, or replace the hinge cover as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 1, 2026. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 26D. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov on March 19, 2026.
