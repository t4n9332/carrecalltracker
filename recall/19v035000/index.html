Audi Q7, A6 and A7 fuel rails can leak gasoline

NHTSA campaign 19V035000 · Reported 2019-01-16 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

Serious — a fuel leak near an ignition source can cause a fire; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get this fixed right away.

What's wrong, in plain English

The fuel rails are the metal tubes that carry gasoline under pressure to the engine. On these Audis, the left and right fuel rails can leak. Gasoline escaping near anything hot or sparking can start a fire. Audi has already notified owners, and dealers will replace both the left and the right fuel rail at no cost to you. This recall began in February 2019, so the free repair has been available for some time.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is covered and the repair was never done.
2. Call an Audi dealer, or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834, and reference recall 24DP.
3. Book the fuel rail replacement as soon as you can — it is free of charge.

Affected vehicles (3)

  • 2018 Audi A6
  • 2018 Audi A7
  • 2018 Audi Q7

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Q7, A6 and A7 vehicles, and 2015-2018 Audi A8 vehicles. The left and right fuel rails may leak fuel.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase risk of a fire.
Remedy
Audi has notified owners, and dealers will replace the left and right fuel rail, free of charge. The recall began February 28, 2019. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 24DP.
