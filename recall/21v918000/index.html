Ram 1500 backup camera image may not appear

NHTSA campaign 21V918000 · Reported 2021-11-23 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 RAM 1500

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Ram 1500 pickups, a control module tied to the trailer reverse steering feature can keep the backup camera picture from showing on the screen. With no image, you cannot see what is directly behind the truck when you back up, and it is easier to hit something — or someone — you never saw. A dealer updates the software on that module. Note that this recall has since been replaced by a newer one, 23V-059.

How serious is it?

The truck drives normally and the notice does not restrict driving, but backing up without a camera view raises the chance of hitting a person or object behind you.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls, which will also show the newer recall 23V-059. 2. Call your Ram dealer, or FCA US at 1-800-853-1403, and ask about recall Y80 and its replacement 23V-059. 3. Have the software updated — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:DISPLAY FUNCTION
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2021 Ram 1500 pickup trucks. The Trailer Reverse Steering Control Module (TRSCM) may prevent the rearview image from displaying. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview camera that does not display an image reduces the driver's rear view, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the TRSCM software. Interim owner notification letters were mailed on January 19, 2022. Owner notification letters were mailed on February 22, 2022. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is Y80. This recall was superseded by 23V-059.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
