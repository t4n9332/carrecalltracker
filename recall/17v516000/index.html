Chevrolet Equinox, GMC Acadia and Terrain driveshaft can break

NHTSA campaign 17V516000 · Reported 2017-08-22 · General Motors LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Chevrolet Equinox
  • 2018 GMC Acadia
  • 2018 GMC Terrain

What's wrong, in plain English

The right front driveshaft, the bar that sends engine power to the right front wheel, can crack and break apart while you are driving. If it does, the vehicle stops pulling itself forward and coasts to a stop, which is dangerous in traffic. If it breaks while the vehicle is parked on a hill without the parking brake set, the vehicle can roll away on its own. Dealers replace the right front driveshaft assembly at no cost.

How serious is it?

Suddenly losing power in traffic can cause a crash, and a vehicle that rolls away on a slope can hurt someone; GM has not told owners to stop driving, but this is a serious mechanical failure worth fixing right away.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is covered. 2. Call your dealer, or Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC at 1-800-462-8782 (recall 17329), for an appointment. 3. The replacement driveshaft is free; always set the parking brake when parking on a hill.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:DRIVELINE:DRIVESHAFT
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2018 Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain vehicles and 2017-2018 GMC Acadia vehicles. The right front intermediate driveshaft may fracture and separate while driving.
Safety risk
If the front right intermediate drive shaft assembly separates while driving, the vehicle will lose propulsion, increasing the risk of a crash. If the assembly separates while parked on a grade, without the park brake engaged, the vehicle may move unintentionally, increasing the risk of a vehicle roll-away.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace the right front intermediate drive shaft assembly, free of charge. The recall began August 25, 2017. Owners may contact GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is 17329.
