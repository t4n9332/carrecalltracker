Ford Bronco engine wiring can short and cause fire

NHTSA campaign 26V468000 · Reported 2026-07-20 · Ford Motor Company

critical How serious is it? About this label

This one is serious because a fire can start in the engine compartment; the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but get the free repair done as soon as your dealer can schedule it.

What's wrong, in plain English

The bundle of electrical wiring in your Bronco's engine compartment can get damaged. Damaged wiring can short out, and a short circuit creates heat or sparks under the hood, which can start a fire. Ford dealers will wrap protective sheathing around the wiring so it cannot rub through, at no cost to you. Ford is handling this in three groups by model, so your letter may come later than another Bronco owner's letter.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — affected VINs became searchable on July 23, 2026.
2. Contact your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and reference recall 26S55.
3. Have the protective sheathing installed over the wiring — parts and labor are free.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2026 Bronco and Bronco Raptor vehicles. The engine compartment wiring harness may become damaged and short circuit.
Safety risk
A short circuit in the engine compartment can create heat or spark, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will install sheathing over the wiring, free of charge. This is a Phased campaign. Phase 1 will include 2026 Bronco Raptor notified owners on 7/31/2026. Phase 2 will include 2021-2025 Bronco Raptor vehicle will notify owners on or about 8/14/2026. Phase 3 will include 2021-2026 Bronco will notify owners on or about 9/1/2026. Owner notification letters were mailed August 5, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S55. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on July 23, 2026.
