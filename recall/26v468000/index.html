Ford Bronco Engine Wiring Can Short Circuit

NHTSA campaign 26V468000 · Reported Jul 20, 2026 · Ford Motor Company

critical How serious is it? About this label

A short circuit that creates heat or a spark in the engine compartment can increase the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The wiring harness in the engine compartment of your Ford Bronco or Bronco Raptor may become damaged. If the wiring is damaged, it can short-circuit, meaning electricity arcs where it shouldn't. This can create heat or a spark in the engine compartment.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. Dealers will install sheathing over the wiring at no cost.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V468000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2026 Bronco and Bronco Raptor vehicles. The engine compartment wiring harness may become damaged and short circuit.
Safety risk
A short circuit in the engine compartment can create heat or spark, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will install sheathing over the wiring, free of charge. This is a Phased campaign. Phase 1 will include 2026 Bronco Raptor notified owners on 7/31/2026. Phase 2 will include 2021-2025 Bronco Raptor vehicle will notify owners on or about 8/14/2026. Phase 3 will include 2021-2026 Bronco will notify owners on or about 9/1/2026. Owner notification letters were mailed August 5, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S55. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on July 23, 2026.
