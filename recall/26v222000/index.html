Lexus RC, GS and IS fuel pump may quit

NHTSA campaign 26V222000 · Reported 2026-04-08 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Lexus GS
  • 2018 Lexus RC
  • 2019 Lexus GS
  • 2019 Lexus RC
  • 2020 Lexus RC

What's wrong, in plain English

The low-pressure fuel pump that sits inside the fuel tank can stop working. That pump pushes gasoline to the engine, so if it fails the engine can sputter and stall, possibly while you are driving. Toyota has already mailed letters warning owners about the safety risk, and the full repair is still being prepared. Dealers will replace the entire fuel pump assembly at no cost, and a second letter will go out once the remedy is available.

How serious is it?

Serious: an engine that stalls without warning can leave you stranded in traffic and raise the risk of a crash, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your Lexus is included. 2. Contact your Lexus dealer or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331, recall numbers 26LB05 and 26LA05, and ask to be notified when parts arrive. 3. The fuel pump replacement is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2015-2022 Lexus RC, 2016-2020 GS, and 2022 IS vehicles. The low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail.
Safety risk
Fuel pump failure can cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump assembly, free of charge. Interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk were mailed June 5, 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 26LB05 and 26LA05.
