Nissan Z pop-up hood may not activate properly

NHTSA campaign 23V725000 · Reported 2023-10-26 · Nissan North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderate — this does not affect how the car drives or protects you, but it can increase injuries to a pedestrian in a front-end crash; there is no instruction to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The Z has a hood that pops up at the back in a front-end crash with a person on foot, to cushion the impact. On these cars, the fasteners in a clip behind the left side of the front bumper can loosen. If they do, that safety feature may not activate when it should. The car still drives normally and you would probably never notice the loose fasteners, but a pedestrian struck by the car could be hurt worse.

What you should do

1. Confirm your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls under campaign 23V725000 (Nissan recall R23C8).
2. Make an appointment with a Nissan dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
3. The dealer tightens the fasteners at no charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:BUMPERS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Nissan Z vehicles. The fasteners in the left-side front bumper reinforcement clip may loosen, which can prevent the pop-up engine hood from activating.
Safety risk
A pop-up engine hood that does not activate and raise the rear end of the hood in a frontal pedestrian crash, can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will tighten the fasteners, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 7, 2023. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is R23C8.
