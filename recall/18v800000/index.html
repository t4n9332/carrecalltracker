Volvo V60 and V90 Cross Country may lose emergency GPS

NHTSA campaign 18V800000 · Reported 2018-11-13 · Volvo Car USA LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the GPS location is not passed to emergency personnel, it can slow or hinder the emergency response after a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The software in these Volvo vehicles' connectivity module may have an error that keeps the telematics and driver support systems from working correctly. One result is that the car will not send its GPS location to emergency personnel during an emergency. In other words, if help is called, responders may not get the location information the car is supposed to provide. Dealers correct the software at no cost, and owners were notified starting in December 2018.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact a Volvo dealer, or call Volvo customer service at 1-800-458-1552 (recall number R39917). 3. Dealers correct the software free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
Volvo Car USA LLC (Volvo) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Volvo XC90, S90, V60, V60 Cross Country, V90, XC40, XC60, and V90 Cross Country vehicles. The software installed in the Vehicle Connectivity Module (VCM) may have an error causing the Telematics and Driver Support Systems to function improperly. As a result, the GPS will not provide location information to emergency personnel in the event of an emergency.
Safety risk
If the GPS location is not provided to emergency personnel it can inhibit emergency response in the event of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Volvo will notify owners, and dealers will correct the software, free of charge. The recall began December 26, 2018. Owners may contact Volvo customer service at 1-800-458-1552. Volvo's number for this recall is R39917.
