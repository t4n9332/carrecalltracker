Acura MDX tailgate moisture can knock out vehicle lights

NHTSA campaign 25V259000 · Reported 2025-04-17 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Acura MDX
  • 2019 Acura MDX
  • 2020 Acura MDX

What's wrong, in plain English

Water can get into the light assembly and wiring in the tailgate at the back of the vehicle. Once moisture reaches the wiring, lights can stop working — the lights on the tailgate, the position lights, the license plate lights, the taillights, and lights inside the vehicle. Dealers will check the wiring and, as needed, add a fuse harness, replace the tailgate light, and reseal the body, all at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Lights that do not come on make it harder for other drivers to see you, especially at night, which raises the risk of a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your MDX is covered.
2. Call Acura customer service at 1-800-382-2238 or your dealer and ask for recall FLI.
3. Have the free repair done — you still need it even if your vehicle was fixed under recall 19V-256.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2014-2020 Acura MDX vehicles. Moisture may enter the tailgate lid light assembly and wiring harness, which can cause the interior and exterior lights to fail, including the lid lights, position lights, license plate lights and taillights.
Safety risk
Vehicle lights that fail to illuminate can decrease visibility and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the wiring and install a fuse harness, replace the lid light, and repair the body dust sealer, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 30, 2025. Owners may contact Acura's customer service at 1-800-382-2238. Acura's number for this recall is FLI. Vehicles included in this recall that were previously repaired under NHTSA recall number 19V-256 will need to have the new remedy completed.
