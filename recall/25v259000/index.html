Acura MDX Tailgate Lights May Fail From Moisture

NHTSA campaign 25V259000 · Reported Apr 17, 2025 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

Lights that don't work make the vehicle harder to see, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In certain Acura MDX vehicles, moisture can get into the tailgate light assembly and its wiring. This can cause the interior and exterior lights to stop working, including the tailgate lid lights, position lights, license plate lights, and taillights.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact an Acura dealer to have the wiring, lid light, and body sealer inspected and repaired as needed; call Acura customer service at 1-800-382-2238.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V259000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2014-2020 Acura MDX vehicles. Moisture may enter the tailgate lid light assembly and wiring harness, which can cause the interior and exterior lights to fail, including the lid lights, position lights, license plate lights and taillights.
Safety risk
Vehicle lights that fail to illuminate can decrease visibility and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the wiring and install a fuse harness, replace the lid light, and repair the body dust sealer, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 30, 2025. Owners may contact Acura's customer service at 1-800-382-2238. Acura's number for this recall is FLI. Vehicles included in this recall that were previously repaired under NHTSA recall number 19V-256 will need to have the new remedy completed.
