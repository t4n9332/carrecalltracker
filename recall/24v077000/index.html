Kia Telluride engine parts may break and cause fire

NHTSA campaign 24V077000 · Reported 2024-02-07 · Kia America, Inc. · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Kia Telluride

What's wrong, in plain English

Small springs inside the engine of these Tellurides can snap while you are driving. If that happens, the engine can stop delivering power, so the vehicle slows or quits moving in traffic without warning. In a worse case, the broken parts can punch a hole right through the engine block, letting oil escape onto hot engine parts, which can start a fire. The dealer replaces the engine sub-assembly — a major internal engine repair — at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

This is a severe defect: it can cause both a sudden loss of power that risks a crash and an engine fire, so get it repaired right away, though the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Telluride is included. 2. Contact your Kia dealer, or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542 (recall SC296), to schedule the engine repair. 3. Have the engine sub-assembly replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:HARD PARTS INTERNAL/MECHANICAL
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2024 Telluride vehicles. The engine valve springs may break while driving, which can result in a loss of drive power and possibly create a hole in the engine block.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash. A hole in the engine block increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the engine sub-assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 5, 2024. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC296.
