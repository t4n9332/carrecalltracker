Volvo VNL truck instrument panel can fail while driving

NHTSA campaign 26V184000 · Reported 2026-03-25 · Volvo Trucks North America

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious, because the panel can go dark without notice while you are on the road, which raises crash risk; the notice does not say to park the truck, but schedule the fix quickly.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2025-2026 Volvo VNL trucks, the instrument panel can quit while you are driving. If it goes out, you lose the gauges and warning lights, including your speedometer, so you cannot see how fast you are going or whether the truck is reporting a problem. Federal safety rules require these displays to keep working. Volvo Trucks dealers will replace the instrument cluster at no cost to you. Owner letters for this recall went out in May 2026.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls (VINs searchable since April 1, 2026).
2. Contact your Volvo Trucks dealer, or Volvo Trucks at 800-528-6586 (recall RVXX2603).
3. Have the instrument cluster replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2025-2026 VNL (4) vehicles. The instrument panel may fail while driving. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 101, "Controls and Displays."
Safety risk
An instrument panel display that fails to show critical safety information, such as the speedometer or warning lights, increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the instrument panel clusters, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 8, 2026. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2603. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning April 1, 2026.
