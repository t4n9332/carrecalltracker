Toyota 4Runner carries wrong load capacity label

NHTSA campaign 23V680000 · Reported 2023-10-12 · Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Toyota 4runner

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2023 Toyota 4Runners have a sticker listing the wrong maximum load the vehicle can carry. If you go by that sticker when loading people, cargo, or gear, you could end up carrying more weight than the vehicle is built to handle. An overloaded vehicle is harder to control and puts extra strain on the tires. The distributor will mail you a corrected label to replace the wrong one — no dealer visit is required for this one.

How serious is it?

Lower risk than most recalls — the vehicle itself is fine and safe to drive; just avoid heavy loads until you have the corrected label showing the true capacity.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your 4Runner is included. 2. Contact Southeast Toyota Distributors customer service at 1-866-405-4226 (recall number SET23A) if you did not receive your label. 3. Apply the free replacement label when it arrives.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC (SET) is recalling certain 2023 Toyota 4Runner vehicles. The load carrying capacity modification label states the incorrect load carrying capacity, which can result in an overloaded vehicle. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
An overloaded vehicle increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC will mail a new modified label, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 1, 2023. Owners may contact SET's customer service at 1-866-405-4226. SET's number for this recall is SET23A.
