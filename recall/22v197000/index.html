Hyundai Santa Fe and Santa Cruz oil leak fire risk

NHTSA campaign 22V197000 · Reported 2022-03-28 · Hyundai Motor America · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Hyundai Santa Cruz
  • 2022 Hyundai Santa FE

What's wrong, in plain English

A small pipe feeds oil to the turbocharger, the part that helps the engine make more power. On these vehicles that pipe can crack and let oil leak out into the engine bay. Engine and exhaust parts get very hot, and oil dripping onto them can catch fire. Signs to watch for are a burning smell, smoke, or oil spots under the front of the car. The dealer will replace the oil pipe at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious — this is a fire risk; you are not told to park the vehicle, but have it repaired right away and stop driving if you smell burning or see smoke.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered.
2. Call your Hyundai dealer or Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 and reference recall 222.
3. Get the turbocharger oil supply pipe replaced free of charge, as soon as an appointment is available.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:GASOLINE:TURBO/SUPERCHARGER
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2022 Santa Fe and Santa Cruz vehicles. The oil supply pipe to the turbocharger may crack, which could result in an oil leak in the engine compartment.
Safety risk
An oil leak in the presence of an ignition source such as hot engine or exhaust components, can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the turbocharger oil supply pipe, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 26, 2022. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall 222.
