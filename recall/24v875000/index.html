Volkswagen ID.4 backup camera screen may go blank

NHTSA campaign 24V875000 · Reported 2024-11-20 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderate, and it is safe to keep driving as long as you look over your shoulder and use your mirrors when backing up until the repair is done.

What's wrong, in plain English

When you shift into reverse, or turn on the parking camera yourself, the screen may stay blank instead of showing the view behind the vehicle. That means you may not see a child, a pet, or an object behind you, and backing into something becomes more likely. A working backup camera is required by federal safety rules, so these vehicles do not meet them. Dealers will replace the camera control box at no charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your ID.4 is included.
2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer or customer service at 1-800-893-5298 and mention recall 91B3.
3. Have the camera control module replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2023 ID.4 vehicles. The rearview camera image may not display when the vehicle is placed in reverse or when the park function is manually activated. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A blank rearview camera display can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the peripheral camera control module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 26, 2025. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 91B3.
