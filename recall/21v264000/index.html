Subaru Impreza and Crosstrek may stall while driving

NHTSA campaign 21V264000 · Reported 2021-04-15 · Subaru of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Subaru Crosstrek
  • 2018 Subaru Impreza
  • 2019 Subaru Crosstrek
  • 2019 Subaru Impreza

What's wrong, in plain English

On these cars, the engine computer may keep sending power to an ignition coil even after you shut the engine off. That can cause an electrical short in the coils, and a short can make the engine stall. An engine that quits while you are driving — especially in traffic or at speed — makes a crash more likely. Your dealer will update the engine computer software, replace the ignition coils, and replace the front exhaust pipe if it is needed, all free.

How serious is it?

Serious because the engine can stall unexpectedly while you drive, though the notice does not tell owners to park the car.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Impreza or Crosstrek is included. 2. Contact your Subaru dealer, or Subaru at 1-844-373-6614, and mention recall WRE-21. 3. Get the free repair done even if your car was already fixed under the earlier recall 19V743 — those cars need this work too.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Subaru Impreza and 2018-2019 Crosstrek vehicles. The Engine Control Module (ECM) may continue to power the ignition coil after the engine is shut off, causing a short circuit.
Safety risk
A short circuit in the ignition coils may cause a stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Subaru will notify owners, and dealers will update the ECM software, replace the ignition coils and, if necessary, the front exhaust pipe, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning June 1, 2021. Owners can contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRE-21. Note: This recall includes all vehicles previously repaired under Recall 19V743.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
