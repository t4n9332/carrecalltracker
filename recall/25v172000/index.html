Tesla Model S Airbag Defect May Silence Horn

NHTSA campaign 25V172000 · Reported Mar 19, 2025 · Tesla, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

An inoperative horn may prevent a driver from alerting other drivers, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Tesla Model S and Model X vehicles were built with an airbag that has the wrong horn pad installed. Because of this mismatched part, pressing the horn does not make it sound. This means the horn does not work as intended, so a driver is not able to use it to alert other drivers on the road the way the horn is supposed to work.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Tesla service, or call Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752.
3. The inspection and any needed repair are free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V172000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2021-2024 Model S and 2021-2025 Model X vehicles. An air bag with the incorrect horn pad may have been installed, preventing the horn from sounding.
Safety risk
An inoperative horn may prevent the driver from alerting other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla service will inspect and install an air bag with the correct horn pad as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 20, 2025. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-25-20-001.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
