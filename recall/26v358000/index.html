Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager front airbags may not deploy

NHTSA campaign 26V358000 · Reported 2026-06-02 · Braun Corporation · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Chrysler Pacifica
  • 2023 Chrysler Voyager
  • 2024 Chrysler Pacifica
  • 2024 Chrysler Voyager
  • 2025 Chrysler Pacifica

What's wrong, in plain English

Inside the plug that connects the front seat wiring, a small spring can touch electrical contacts it is not supposed to touch. That accidental connection can stop the front airbags from going off in a crash. Nothing will feel wrong while you drive — the problem only shows up at the moment you need the airbag to protect you. These are Pacifica and Voyager vans modified by Braun Corporation. The dealer replaces the spring free of charge.

How serious is it?

Serious — your airbag may not protect you in a crash, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is affected. 2. Call Braun customer service at 1-800-488-0359 or your dealer — owner letters were mailed July 16, 2026. 3. Schedule the free repair as soon as you can.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
Braun Corporation (Braun) is recalling certain 2023-2026 Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager vehicles. The spring inside the front seat harness connector may contact electrical terminals.
Safety risk
The unintended connection may prevent the air bags from deploying, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the spring, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 16, 2026. Owners may contact Braun's customer service at 1-800-488-0359.
