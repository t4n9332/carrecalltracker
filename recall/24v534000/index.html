BMW X3 Interior Cargo Rail May Come Loose

NHTSA campaign 24V534000 · Reported Jul 17, 2024 · BMW of North America, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the cargo rail detaches during a rear-end crash, it increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling certain X3 vehicles, including the sDrive30i, xDrive30i, M40i and X3 M. The attachment that holds the interior cargo rail in place can be damaged if the vehicle is struck from behind, and the cargo rail can then come loose inside the vehicle. Dealers will replace the bolts that fasten the rear cargo rail at no cost. Owners have been sent notification letters about this repair.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your X3 is included.
2. Contact a BMW dealer to schedule the repair, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V534000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2018-2023 X3 sDrive30i, X3 xDrive30i, X3 M40i, and X3 M vehicles. The attachment for the interior cargo rail may become damaged in a rear crash and result in the cargo rail detaching.
Safety risk
Interior cargo rail detachment during a rear crash can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the rear cargo rail attachment bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 2, 2024. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
