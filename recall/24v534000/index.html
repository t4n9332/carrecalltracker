BMW X3 cargo rail may come loose in crash

NHTSA campaign 24V534000 · Reported 2024-07-17 · BMW of North America, LLC · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 BMW X3
  • 2019 BMW X3
  • 2020 BMW X3
  • 2021 BMW X3
  • 2022 BMW X3
  • 2023 BMW X3

What's wrong, in plain English

These X3 models have a rail inside the cargo area, used to secure items in the back. The part that holds that rail in place can be damaged if the vehicle is hit from behind, and the rail can come loose. A loose rail flying around inside the vehicle during a rear-end crash could hurt the people inside. The dealer replaces the bolts that hold the rail.

How serious is it?

Less urgent than most recalls — it only matters in a rear-end crash, where a detached cargo rail could cause injury, and the vehicle is otherwise safe to drive.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your X3 is included. 2. Call your BMW dealer to schedule the repair (BMW customer service: 1-800-525-7417). 3. The dealer replaces the rear cargo rail attachment bolts free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2018-2023 X3 sDrive30i, X3 xDrive30i, X3 M40i, and X3 M vehicles. The attachment for the interior cargo rail may become damaged in a rear crash and result in the cargo rail detaching.
Safety risk
Interior cargo rail detachment during a rear crash can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the rear cargo rail attachment bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 2, 2024. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
