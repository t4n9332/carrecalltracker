Acura ZDX loose suspension bolts can cut power

NHTSA campaign 25V018000 · Reported 2025-01-16 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing drive power in traffic can lead to a crash, so this is a serious defect worth fixing quickly, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Bolts that hold a front suspension bracket to the vehicle's frame can work loose and fall out. If the bracket comes free, it can rub against or damage the thick high-voltage cables or the cooling lines that keep the battery at the right temperature. Damage to either one can shut down the electric drive system, so the vehicle could suddenly stop pulling while you are driving.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your ZDX is affected. 2. Schedule service at an Acura dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (recall KKY). 3. The dealer tightens the bolts and repairs any damaged parts, cables, or coolant lines for free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM: CABLES
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2024 Acura ZDX vehicles. The front passenger stabilizer bar bracket bolts may become loose and detach from the frame, which can damage the high voltage cables or EV battery coolant lines.
Safety risk
Damaged high voltage cables or EV battery coolant lines can cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and tighten the bolts, and repair the stabilizer bar and bracket, high voltage cables, and EV battery coolant lines as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 24, 2025. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is KKY.
