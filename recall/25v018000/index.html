Acura ZDX Bolts May Damage High-Voltage Cables

NHTSA campaign 25V018000 · Reported Jan 16, 2025 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Damaged high voltage cables or coolant lines can cause the vehicle to lose drive power, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2024 Acura ZDX vehicles, the bolts that hold the front passenger stabilizer bar bracket in place may become loose over time. If the bolts become loose enough, the bracket can detach from the vehicle's frame entirely. When this happens, the detached bracket or its bolts can damage the vehicle's high voltage cables or the coolant lines that serve the EV battery.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Honda/Acura dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. The inspection and any needed repairs are free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V018000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM: CABLES
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2024 Acura ZDX vehicles. The front passenger stabilizer bar bracket bolts may become loose and detach from the frame, which can damage the high voltage cables or EV battery coolant lines.
Safety risk
Damaged high voltage cables or EV battery coolant lines can cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and tighten the bolts, and repair the stabilizer bar and bracket, high voltage cables, and EV battery coolant lines as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 24, 2025. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is KKY.
