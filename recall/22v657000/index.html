Volkswagen Atlas trailer hitch bolts may be loose

NHTSA campaign 22V657000 · Reported 2022-08-31 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Volkswagen Atlas
  • 2022 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts holding the trailer hitch onto the vehicle may not have been tightened enough at the factory. Over time a loose bolt can fall out, which puts extra strain on the bolts that are left until they break. If that happens, the hitch and whatever you are towing can come away from the vehicle and into traffic. A dealer will check and tighten the hitch bolts for free on affected 2022 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles.

How serious is it?

This is a serious risk while towing — a trailer coming loose can cause a crash — so the vehicle is fine to drive, but avoid towing until the bolts are tightened.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact your Volkswagen dealer, or VW at 1-800-893-5298, and mention recall 66N8.
3. Get the bolts tightened — it is free and quick — before you tow anything.

Official notice

Component
TRAILER HITCHES
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2022 Atlas and Atlas Cross Sport vehicles. The trailer hitch bolts may have been insufficiently tightened.
Safety risk
Loose bolts may fall out, causing the remaining bolts to break, which could lead to separation of the trailer hitch and trailer, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will tighten the trailer hitch bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed October 28, 2022. Owners may contact Volkswagen's customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 66N8.
