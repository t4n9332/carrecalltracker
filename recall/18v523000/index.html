Dodge and Jeep rear brake calipers may weaken braking

NHTSA campaign 18V523000 · Reported 2018-08-09 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Dodge Grand Caravan
  • 2018 Dodge Journey
  • 2018 Jeep Compass
  • 2019 Dodge Grand Caravan
  • 2019 Jeep Cherokee
  • 2019 Jeep Compass

What's wrong, in plain English

The pistons inside the rear brake calipers may not have enough protective coating. Without it, pockets of gas can form in the rear brake system, and that can make the rear brakes less effective than they should be. You may not notice anything day to day, but the vehicle could take longer to stop, which raises the chance of a crash. Dealers will bleed the brake system to flush the gas pockets out, free of charge. This recall started September 29, 2018.

How serious is it?

Reduced braking is a real crash risk; the notice does not tell you to stop driving, but leave extra stopping distance and get the brakes bled soon.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Contact your Dodge, Jeep or Chrysler dealer, or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall U86), for an appointment. 3. Have the brake system bled — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:DISC:CALIPER
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Dodge Grand Caravan and Jeep Compass, 2018 Dodge Journey, and 2019 Jeep Cherokee vehicles. The rear brake caliper pistons on these vehicles may have an insufficient coating causing gas pockets to form, potentially reducing rear brake performance.
Safety risk
A reduction of braking performance can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will bleed the vehicle's brake system, free of charge. The recall began September 29, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U86.
