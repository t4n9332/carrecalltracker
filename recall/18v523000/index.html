NHTSA campaign 18V523000 · Reported Aug 9, 2018 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Dodge and Jeep vehicles, the pistons inside the rear brake calipers may not have enough protective coating. Without enough coating, gas pockets can form inside the caliper pistons. These gas pockets can reduce how well the rear brakes perform, meaning the rear brakes may not work as effectively as they should.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to schedule the repair.
3. The dealer will bleed the brake system free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V523000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:DISC:CALIPER
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Dodge Grand Caravan and Jeep Compass, 2018 Dodge Journey, and 2019 Jeep Cherokee vehicles. The rear brake caliper pistons on these vehicles may have an insufficient coating causing gas pockets to form, potentially reducing rear brake performance.
Safety risk
A reduction of braking performance can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will bleed the vehicle's brake system, free of charge. The recall began September 29, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U86.
