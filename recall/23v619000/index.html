Volkswagen Jetta may not chime for key left in ignition

NHTSA campaign 23V619000 · Reported Sep 6, 2023 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

low How serious is it? About this label

Without the audible warning, a driver may not realize the key was left in the ignition, which can increase the risk of the vehicle being stolen or of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2019 Jetta vehicles. The instrument cluster may not sound the audible warning that tells the driver the key is still in the ignition when the door is open. Because that warning may not sound, these vehicles do not meet the federal theft protection rule. Dealers will update the instrument cluster free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed November 3, 2023. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 90W7.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included. 2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer to have the instrument cluster updated, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. 3. The update is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V619000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019 Jetta vehicles. The instrument cluster may not provide an audible warning to let the driver know that the key is still in the ignition when the door is open. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 114, "Theft Protection."
Safety risk
Without an audible warning, the driver may be unaware that the key is left in the ignition, which can increase the risk of vehicle theft or a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the instrument cluster, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed November 3, 2023. Owners may contact Volkswagen's customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 90W7.
