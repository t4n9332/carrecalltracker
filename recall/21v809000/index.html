Ford Bronco Sport moonroof glass may detach while driving

NHTSA campaign 21V809000 · Reported 2021-10-15 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford Bronco Sport

What's wrong, in plain English

The glass panel in the moonroof may not have been glued to its frame properly when the vehicle was built. If the bond is weak, the whole piece of glass can come off the roof while you are driving. That leaves a large piece of glass in the road behind you, which can hit another car or a person and cause a crash or injury. A dealer will check the date code on your glass and replace it if needed, for free.

How serious is it?

Serious — the glass can let go without warning and become a hazard for the cars behind you, so get it inspected promptly even though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Bronco Sport is included.
2. Schedule the moonroof inspection with a Ford dealer, or call Ford at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 21S46).
3. The inspection and any glass replacement are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:SUN/MOON ROOF ASSEMBLY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 Bronco Sport vehicles. The moonroof glass may not have been bonded correctly to the moonroof frame, which could result in the glass panel detaching from the vehicle.
Safety risk
A detached glass panel can become a road hazard, and increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the moonroof glass date code and replace the glass, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 18, 2021. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21S46.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
