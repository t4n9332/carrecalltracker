Volkswagen Atlas airbag sensor may be built wrong

NHTSA campaign 20V057000 · Reported 2020-01-31 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Volkswagen Atlas

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2018 Atlas SUVs have a faulty airbag crash sensor. This is the part that tells the airbags when a crash is happening. Because it was made incorrectly, the front airbags could inflate late — or not inflate at all — in a crash that should set them off. That means less protection for the people in the front seats. Volkswagen dealers will replace the crash sensor with a correctly made one.

How serious is it?

Serious — front airbags may be delayed or missing in a crash, though the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle, so it is fine to drive while you get the free repair scheduled.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Atlas is covered. 2. Call a Volkswagen dealer, or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall 69AL), for an appointment. 3. Have the airbag crash sensor replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2018 Atlas vehicles. The vehicles may be equipped with an incorrectly manufactured air bag sensor, which can delay or disable air bag deployment when necessary.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash necessitating frontal air bag deployment, a delayed or disabled airbag deployment can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will replace the air bag crash sensor, free of charge. The recall began February 21, 2020. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 69AL.
