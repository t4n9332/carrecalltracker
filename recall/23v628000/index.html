Nissan Altima and Sentra rearview camera image may fail

NHTSA campaign 23V628000 · Reported 2023-09-08 · Nissan North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

A rearview camera that does not properly display an image reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Nissan is recalling certain Altima and Sentra vehicles because the wiring for the backup camera can be damaged. If that wiring is damaged, the image on the rearview camera screen can come out distorted, or the picture can stop showing up at all. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal government's rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will look at the camera and the wiring and replace whatever is damaged, or protect and reroute the wiring.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included. 2. Contact your Nissan dealer to schedule the inspection and repair, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. 3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2019-2021 Altima and 2020-2021 Sentra vehicles. Damage to the camera harness can cause distortion or loss of the rearview camera display image. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview camera that does not properly display an image can reduce the driver's rear view, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the rearview camera and harness for damage and replace them as necessary. If no damage is found, the dealer will apply protective tape and reroute the rearview camera harness. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed October 19, 2023. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is R23C3.
