Kia Stinger fuel pump can cause sudden power loss

NHTSA campaign 23V634000 · Reported 2023-09-13 · Kia America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious: losing engine power without warning raises the risk of a crash, so book the free repair soon, although Kia has not told owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2018-2021 Kia Stinger cars, a small valve inside the high-pressure fuel pump can stick. When it sticks, fuel pressure builds too high and the engine can lose power. That means the car may suddenly slow down or stop pulling while you are driving, which is dangerous in traffic or at highway speed. Kia mailed owner letters in December 2023.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Stinger is covered.
2. Call Kia at 1-800-333-4542 or your dealer and mention recall SC281.
3. The dealer updates the engine software and replaces the fuel pump if needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2018-2021 Stinger vehicles. The fuel control valve plunger may stick inside the high pressure fuel pump, causing over-pressurization and loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the engine control unit software, inspect, and replace the fuel pump as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 18, 2023. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC281.
