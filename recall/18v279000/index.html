Jeep Compass front control arm may break apart

NHTSA campaign 18V279000 · Reported 2018-05-01 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Jeep Compass

What's wrong, in plain English

The front lower control arms are metal parts that hold each front wheel to the body of the vehicle and let it steer and ride over bumps. On some 2018 Jeep Compass vehicles these arms may have been welded incorrectly, so an arm can come apart. If that happens while you are driving, the wheel can shift out of position and you can lose control of the vehicle. Dealers inspect the arms and replace them if needed, free.

How serious is it?

Losing control with no warning is one of the most dangerous things that can happen on the road, so treat this as urgent and get the inspection done before any highway driving or long trips.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Compass is included in recall U42.
2. Call your Jeep dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and ask for the earliest appointment.
3. The inspection and any control arm replacement are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:CONTROL ARM:LOWER ARM
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018 Jeep Compass vehicles. The front lower control arms may be incorrectly welded, potentially resulting in a control arm separation.
Safety risk
If the lower control arm separates, it can cause a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace the front lower control arms, free of charge. The recall began May 31, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U42.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
