Subaru Solterra wheels can come off while driving

NHTSA campaign 23V064000 · Reported 2023-02-09 · Subaru of America, Inc. · critical severity

⚠ NHTSA advises not driving this vehicle until it is repaired. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Subaru Solterra

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts that hold the wheel hubs on some 2023 Solterras were not tightened correctly at the factory. Over time they can work loose, and a wheel can come off the vehicle while you are driving. If that happens, you would lose control of the car. Subaru is telling owners not to drive these vehicles at all until a dealer checks and tightens the hub bolts, which costs nothing.

How serious is it?

Very serious — do not drive the vehicle at all until it is repaired, because a wheel could come off with no warning.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls right away to see if your Solterra is included.
2. Stop driving it and arrange to have it brought to a dealer rather than driving it there yourself.
3. Contact your Subaru dealer, or Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614 (recall WRE-23), for the free inspection and tightening.

Official notice

Component
WHEELS:HUB
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2023 Solterra vehicles. Improperly tightened hub bolts may loosen and cause the wheels to detach from the vehicle.
Safety risk
Wheel detachment while driving can result in a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to drive their vehicle until the repair has been completed. Dealers will inspect and tighten the hub bolts, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to mailed March 1, 2023. Owners may contact Subaru's customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRE-23.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
