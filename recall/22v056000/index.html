Hyundai Santa Fe brake module can spark engine fire

NHTSA campaign 22V056000 · Reported 2022-02-03 · Hyundai Motor America · critical severity

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Hyundai Santa FE
  • 2018 Hyundai Santa FE Sport
  • 2019 Hyundai Santa FE XL

What's wrong, in plain English

The control box for the anti-lock brakes can fail and cause an electrical short. That short can start a fire under the hood — and it can happen while the car is parked, not just while you are driving. The fix is to replace a fuse for that brake system, and it costs you nothing. Because a parked car can catch fire, Hyundai asks owners to park outside and away from houses, garages and other buildings until the work is done.

How serious is it?

A fire that can start while the vehicle is parked or driving is a real injury risk; the notice does not tell you to stop driving, but parking outside and away from structures is important until the fuse is replaced.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered.
2. Park outside and away from buildings, day and night, until the repair is complete.
3. Book the free fuse replacement with a Hyundai dealer, or call Hyundai at 1-855-371-9460 (recall 218).

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:ANTILOCK/TRACTION CONTROL/ELECTRONIC LIMITED SLIP:CONTROL UNIT/MODULE
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Santa Fe, 2017-2018 Santa Fe Sport, 2019 Santa Fe XL, and 2014-2015 Tucson vehicles. The Anti-Lock Brake System (ABS) module could malfunction and cause an electrical short, which could result in an engine compartment fire.
Safety risk
An engine compartment fire while parked or driving can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. Dealers will replace the ABS multi-fuse, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 31, 2022. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 218.
