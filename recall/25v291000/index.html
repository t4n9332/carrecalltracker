Hyundai Palisade oil pump controller can short and burn

NHTSA campaign 25V291000 · Reported 2025-05-06 · Hyundai Motor America · critical severity what's this?

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Hyundai Palisade

What's wrong, in plain English

A controller for the electric oil pump in your Palisade is not sealed well enough. Moisture can get inside and build up on the circuit board, causing an electrical short that can start a fire. Hyundai says this can happen while you are driving or while the vehicle is parked. Until it is fixed, park outside and away from buildings. A dealer will replace the oil pump controller free of charge. Owner letters were mailed June 11, 2025.

How serious is it?

Serious — the notice does not tell you to stop driving, but a fire can start even while parked, so park outside and away from structures until repaired.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls, and park outside away from your home or garage in the meantime.
2. Contact your Hyundai dealer or Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 and mention recall 278.
3. The dealer replaces the electric oil pump controller free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:FLUID/LUBRICANT:PUMP
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2025 Hyundai Palisade vehicles. An insufficient seal on the controller for the electric oil pump may allow moisture to accumulate in the printed circuit board, which can cause an electrical short and result in a fire.
Safety risk
A fire while parked or driving can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park their vehicles outside and away from structures until the recall remedy is completed. Dealers will replace the electric oil pump controller, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 11, 2025. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 278.
