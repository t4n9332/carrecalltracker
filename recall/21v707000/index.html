Audi E-Tron Brake Booster Corrosion Issue

NHTSA campaign 21V707000 · Reported 2021-09-13 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing ABS, stability control, and brake assist can increase the effort and distance it takes to stop the vehicle, which raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2021 Audi E-Tron Quattro and E-Tron Sportback Quattro vehicles may develop corrosion inside the brake booster. Over time, this corrosion can cause several systems to stop working, including the Anti-Lock Brake System (ABS), Electronic Stability Control (ESC), the speedometer display, and brake assist. This means multiple safety systems tied to braking could be affected at the same time in these vehicles.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact your dealer or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834 to schedule the repair.
3. The brake booster replacement is done free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2021 Audi E-Tron Quattro and E-Tron Sportback Quattro vehicles. Corrosion in the brake booster may cause the Anti-Lock Brake System (ABS), Electronic Stability Control (ESC), speedometer display, and brake assist to stop functioning.
Safety risk
A loss of braking assist, ABS, and ESC will increase the effort and distance required to stop, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the brake booster, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 30, 2021. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 47R7.
