Audi E-Tron brake booster corrosion weakens braking power

NHTSA campaign 21V707000 · Reported 2021-09-13 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious — you can still stop the car, but it takes more effort and more distance, so get it repaired promptly and leave extra following room until it is fixed.

What's wrong, in plain English

Rust can build up inside the brake booster — the part that makes your brake pedal easier to press. If it corrodes, several systems can quit at once: power brake assist, the anti-lock brakes that keep the wheels from locking up, the stability control that helps you hold the road, and the speedometer display. The brakes still work, but the pedal gets much harder to push and the car takes longer to stop. Dealers replace the brake booster.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Call your Audi dealer or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834 (recall 47R7) and schedule the brake booster replacement. 3. Have the work done — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2021 Audi E-Tron Quattro and E-Tron Sportback Quattro vehicles. Corrosion in the brake booster may cause the Anti-Lock Brake System (ABS), Electronic Stability Control (ESC), speedometer display, and brake assist to stop functioning.
Safety risk
A loss of braking assist, ABS, and ESC will increase the effort and distance required to stop, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the brake booster, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 30, 2021. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 47R7.
