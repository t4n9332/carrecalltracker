Toyota Prius dashboard display can go blank

NHTSA campaign 19V876000 · Reported 2019-12-11 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Toyota Prius

What's wrong, in plain English

A short circuit can happen inside the main instrument display behind the steering wheel. When it does, the screen goes blank, so you lose the speedometer, the odometer, and the fuel gauge all at once. You would have no way to see how fast you are going or how much gas you have left. Dealers will check the production date of a memory chip inside the display and, if needed, replace that chip along with the display's circuit board.

How serious is it?

Moderate — driving with no speed or fuel information raises the risk of a crash, but Toyota does not tell owners to stop driving, so it is fine to drive while you arrange the repair.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Prius is covered.
2. Contact your Toyota dealer or Toyota at 1-888-270-9371 (recall 19TA21 / 19TB21) for an appointment.
3. Have the display inspected and repaired at no charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2019 Prius vehicles. An electrical short circuit in the combination meter could occur, causing the speedometer, the odometer, and the fuel gauge to go blank. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 101, "Controls and Displays."
Safety risk
A blank main display will not show important information like vehicle speed or fuel level. Driving with an inoperative display can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the flash memory chip (ROM) production date, and if necessary, replace it along with the combination meter circuit board, free of charge. The recall began February 7, 2020. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 19TA21 and 19TB21.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
