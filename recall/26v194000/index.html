Honda CB1000SP motorcycle fuel hose may come loose

NHTSA campaign 26V194000 · Reported 2026-03-26 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Honda Cb1000sp

What's wrong, in plain English

On these motorcycles, the hose that carries gasoline from the fuel tank to the engine can come disconnected at the tank. If that happens, gas leaks out while you ride. The fuel can spill near the rear tire, which can make you lose control, and gasoline near anything hot or sparking can catch fire. A dealer will replace the fuel hose and secure a nearby wiring harness so the hose stays put.

How serious is it?

This is serious — a fuel leak near the rear tire can cause a crash, and leaking gas near an ignition source can start a fire, so get the repair done right away and do not ride if you smell or see fuel.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your motorcycle is affected (VINs are searchable starting April 4, 2026). 2. Contact your Honda motorcycle dealer or Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870 (recall number KU5) to schedule the repair. 3. Have the fuel hose replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2025 CB1000SP motorcycles. The fuel feed hose may disconnect from the fuel tank, causing a fuel leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak near the rear tire can increase the risk of a crash or injury. In addition, a fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will secure the angle sensor coupler wiring harness and replace the fuel hose, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 20, 2026. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KU5. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning April 4, 2026.
