Honda CB1000SP Fuel Hose May Disconnect and Leak

NHTSA campaign 26V194000 · Reported 2026-03-26 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak near the rear tire increases the risk of a crash or injury, and if the leaking fuel reaches a spark or other ignition source, it can also start a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Honda CB1000SP motorcycles, the hose that carries fuel to the engine may come loose from the fuel tank. This can cause fuel to leak out, including near the rear tire.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your motorcycle is included.
2. Contact a dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870, to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2025 CB1000SP motorcycles. The fuel feed hose may disconnect from the fuel tank, causing a fuel leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak near the rear tire can increase the risk of a crash or injury. In addition, a fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will secure the angle sensor coupler wiring harness and replace the fuel hose, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 20, 2026. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KU5. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning April 4, 2026.
