Nissan Z Pop-Up Hood Sensor Wiring Problem

NHTSA campaign 24V798000 · Reported 2024-10-24 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A pop-up hood that doesn't work as intended in a pedestrian crash raises the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Nissan Z vehicles may have the connectors for the right-side pedestrian detection sensors installed backwards in the front bumper wiring. This can prevent the pop-up engine hood from working the way it's supposed to in a pedestrian crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Nissan dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
3. Dealers will replace the front bumper harness, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:BUMPERS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Nissan Z vehicles. The connectors for the right-side pedestrian detection sensors may be reversed in the front bumper harness, which can prevent the pop-up engine hood from activating as intended.
Safety risk
A pop-up engine hood that does not activate in a pedestrian crash can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front bumper harness, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 11, 2024. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is R24B6.
