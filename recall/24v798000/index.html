Nissan Z Pop-Up Hood May Not Rise for Pedestrians

NHTSA campaign 24V798000 · Reported 2024-10-24 · Nissan North America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Nissan Z
  • 2024 Nissan Z

What's wrong, in plain English

These cars have sensors in the front bumper that detect when the car hits a person. When they go off, the back of the hood pops up to cushion the impact. On some of these cars, two plugs on the right side were connected the wrong way around, so the hood may not pop up when it should. That means a person struck by the car could be hurt worse. The dealer will replace the bumper wiring.

How serious is it?

This fault does not affect how the car drives or stops and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but it puts a pedestrian at greater risk in a collision, so get it fixed.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your Z is part of recall 24V798000.
2. Contact your Nissan dealer or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 (recall number R24B6).
3. Have the dealer replace the front bumper harness, which is done free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:BUMPERS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Nissan Z vehicles. The connectors for the right-side pedestrian detection sensors may be reversed in the front bumper harness, which can prevent the pop-up engine hood from activating as intended.
Safety risk
A pop-up engine hood that does not activate in a pedestrian crash can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front bumper harness, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 11, 2024. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is R24B6.
