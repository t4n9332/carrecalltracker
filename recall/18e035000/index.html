Roush Supercharger Kit Can Cause Mustang Fuel Leak

NHTSA campaign 18E035000 · Reported 2018-05-24 · Roush Performance Products, Inc. · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Ford Mustang

What's wrong, in plain English

Roush sold an aftermarket supercharger kit for the 2018 Ford Mustang, and some of the small hose fittings in those kits were made incorrectly. A wrong fitting can damage the fuel lines that carry gasoline to the engine, and gasoline can then leak out. Leaking fuel raises the chance of a fire. Roush is replacing the faulty fuel rail parts on affected kits for free. This only affects Mustangs that had this particular Roush kit installed.

How serious is it?

This is serious because leaking gasoline can start a fire, and while the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside, you should get it repaired right away and stop driving if you ever smell fuel.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Mustang is covered, and check whether your car has the Roush kit with part number R1318-6006P1-AA. 2. Call Roush customer service at 1-800-597-6874 and ask about recall REMay2018. 3. Have the fuel rail parts replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:GASOLINE:TURBO/SUPERCHARGER
Summary
Roush Performance Products, Inc. (Roush) is recalling certain aftermarket supercharger kits, part number R1318-6006P1-AA, sold for installation on 2018 Ford Mustangs. The hose barb fittings on the supercharger kits may be manufactured incorrectly, possibly causing damage to the fuel lines and resulting in a fuel leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Roush will notify the known owners and replace the defective fuel rail subassemblies, free of charge. The recall began on May 31, 2018. Owners may contact Roush customer service at 1-800-597-6874. Roush's number for this recall is REMay2018.
