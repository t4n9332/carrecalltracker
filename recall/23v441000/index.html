Volvo heavy trucks may have loose steering gear bolts

NHTSA campaign 23V441000 · Reported 2023-06-23 · Volvo Trucks North America · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Volvo VAH
  • 2024 Volvo VN

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts that hold the steering gear in place may not have been tightened properly at the factory. The steering gear is the part that turns your steering wheel motion into the wheels actually turning. If those bolts are loose, you can lose control of the truck while driving. The dealer will tighten the steering gear bolts to the correct specification. The check and the repair cost you nothing. Volvo is mailing letters to owners of the affected trucks.

How serious is it?

Loss of steering control while driving can cause a crash, and the notice does not tell owners to park the truck.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is included. 2. Contact your Volvo Trucks dealer or Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586 (recall RVXX2307). 3. Have the steering gear bolts tightened — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2023-2024 VN, VHD, and VAH trucks. The steering gear fasteners may not be properly tightened.
Safety risk
Loose steering gear fasteners may result in a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will tighten the steering gear fasteners, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 18, 2023. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 1-800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2307.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
