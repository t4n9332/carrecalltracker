Volvo VN, VAH Trucks Steering Fasteners May Loosen

NHTSA campaign 23V441000 · Reported 2023-06-23 · Volvo Trucks North America

high How serious is it? About this label

Loose steering gear fasteners can lead to a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Volvo VN and VAH trucks, the fasteners that hold the steering gear in place may not have been tightened properly at the factory. Because these steering gear fasteners may be loose, they could work themselves looser over time as the truck is used.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Volvo Trucks dealer, or call Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586.
3. The dealer will tighten the steering gear fasteners, free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2023 Volvo VAH
  • 2024 Volvo VN

Official notice

Component
STEERING:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2023-2024 VN, VHD, and VAH trucks. The steering gear fasteners may not be properly tightened.
Safety risk
Loose steering gear fasteners may result in a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will tighten the steering gear fasteners, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 18, 2023. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 1-800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2307.
