Cadillac LYRIQ front sway bar bolts can loosen

NHTSA campaign 25V232000 · Reported 2025-04-10 · General Motors, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Cadillac Lyriq
  • 2024 Cadillac Lyriq
  • 2025 Cadillac Lyriq

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts holding a front anti-sway bar bracket to the frame may not have been tightened properly when the car was built. Over time those bolts can work loose and come out, letting the bar or its bracket break free underneath the car. A loose bar can then hit and damage the high-voltage cables or the battery cooling lines. If that happens, the car can shut down while you are driving and lose power.

How serious is it?

Serious but not a park-it recall: the notice says damaged cables or coolant lines can shut the vehicle down and cause a loss of drive power, raising crash risk, so get it inspected promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your LYRIQ is included.
2. Call your Cadillac dealer, or Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223 (GM recall number N252494190), and book an inspection.
3. The dealer will check and tighten the stabilizer bar bracket bolts free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:STABILIZER BAR
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023-2025 Cadillac LYRIQ vehicles. The left or right front stabilizer bar bracket bolts may have been improperly tightened, which can allow the bolts to loosen and separate from the vehicle frame. A detached stabilizer bar or bracket can damage the vehicle's high voltage cables or EV battery coolant lines.
Safety risk
Damaged cables or coolant lines can result in a vehicle shutdown, causing a loss of drive power and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and tighten the left and/or right stabilizer bar bracket bolts, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 8, 2025. Owners may contact Cadilac customer service at 1-800-333-4223. GM's number for this recall is N252494190.
