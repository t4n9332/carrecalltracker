Cadillac Lyriq Front Stabilizer Bar Bolts May Loosen

NHTSA campaign 25V232000 · Reported 2025-04-10 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Damaged cables or coolant lines can cause the vehicle to shut down and lose drive power while being driven, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Cadillac Lyriq vehicles. The bolts on the bracket that holds the left or right front stabilizer bar may not have been tightened properly. Over time, these bolts can loosen and allow the bracket to separate from the vehicle's frame. If that happens, the loose stabilizer bar or bracket can damage nearby high-voltage cables or the coolant lines for the EV battery.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact your Cadillac dealer, or call Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223, to schedule an inspection.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:STABILIZER BAR
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023-2025 Cadillac LYRIQ vehicles. The left or right front stabilizer bar bracket bolts may have been improperly tightened, which can allow the bolts to loosen and separate from the vehicle frame. A detached stabilizer bar or bracket can damage the vehicle's high voltage cables or EV battery coolant lines.
Safety risk
Damaged cables or coolant lines can result in a vehicle shutdown, causing a loss of drive power and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and tighten the left and/or right stabilizer bar bracket bolts, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 8, 2025. Owners may contact Cadilac customer service at 1-800-333-4223. GM's number for this recall is N252494190.
