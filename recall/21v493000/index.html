Porsche Cayenne Steering Column May Detach

NHTSA campaign 21V493000 · Reported Jun 30, 2021 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A detached steering column can cause the driver to lose control of the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Porsche says the screw connecting the steering column to the steering box on some Cayenne SUVs may fail. If it does, the steering column can become detached from the steering box, affecting the driver's ability to steer the vehicle.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
Contact a Porsche dealer, or call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243, to schedule the repair.
The repair, installing a new screw, is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V493000. Official notice

Component
STEERING:COLUMN
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2021 Cayenne vehicles. The screw connection between the steering column and steering box may fail, which can result in a detached steering column.
Safety risk
A detached steering column can cause a loss of control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install a new screw, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 24, 2021. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AMB4.
