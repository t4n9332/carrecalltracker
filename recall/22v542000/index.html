Volkswagen Golf R and GTI sunroof switch too sensitive

NHTSA campaign 22V542000 · Reported 2022-07-27 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Volkswagen Golf R

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2022 Golf R and Golf GTI models, the touch-sensitive button that opens and closes the sunroof reacts too easily. It can be pressed by accident — a brush of a hand or an object is enough — and the sunroof may then close on its own when nobody meant it to. That fails the federal safety rule for power windows and roofs, and someone could get hurt. Dealers replace the roof switch free of charge.

How serious is it?

The main danger is a body part being caught by a sunroof closing unexpectedly, especially with children in the car; the van— the car is otherwise fine to drive and there is no park-it warning.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your car is included.
2. Contact your Volkswagen dealer, or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall 60F6), to schedule the switch replacement.
3. Until it is fixed, keep hands and heads clear of the sunroof opening — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:SUN/MOON ROOF ASSEMBLY
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2022 Golf R A8 and Golf GTI vehicles. The touch switch for the sunroof may be too sensitive, allowing it to be inadvertently pressed. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 118, "Power-Operated Window Systems."
Safety risk
A sunroof switch that is inadvertently pressed can result in unintended auto-closure of the sunroof, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the roof module switch, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed September 23, 2022. Owners may contact Volkswagen's customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 60F6.
