BMW X6 rear spoiler may detach while driving

NHTSA campaign 20V095000 · Reported 2020-02-19 · BMW of North America, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 BMW X6

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2020 BMW X6 models — the sDrive40i, xDrive40i, and xDrive50i — may have a rear spoiler that can come off the vehicle. The spoiler is the trim piece mounted at the back of the roof or tailgate. If it separates while you are driving, it can fall onto the road behind you, where it becomes an obstacle for other drivers. BMW dealers will look at the spoiler and replace it if needed.

How serious is it?

This is a moderate concern: a spoiler coming loose on the road raises the risk of a crash for you or drivers behind you, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your 2020 X6 is included.
2. Contact your BMW dealer to schedule an inspection, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. Have the dealer inspect the rear spoiler and replace it if necessary — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020 X6 sDrive40i, X6 xDrive40i, and X6 xDrive50i vehicles. The rear spoiler may detach.
Safety risk
If the spoiler detaches while driving, it can become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and replace the rear spoiler, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began April 16, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
