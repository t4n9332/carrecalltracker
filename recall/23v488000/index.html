Tesla Model S and Model X Seat Belt Defect

NHTSA campaign 23V488000 · Reported Jul 14, 2023 · Tesla, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2021-2023 Tesla Model S and Model X vehicles, the front-row seat belts may not be properly connected to their pretensioner anchors, which can cause a seat belt to detach.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact Tesla Service, or call Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752.
3. Tesla will inspect and, if needed, reconnect or replace the seat belts, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V488000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Model S and Model X vehicles. The front-row seat belts may not be connected properly to the pretensioner anchors, which can cause the seat belts to detach.
Safety risk
A detached seat belt may not properly restrain an occupant during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Tesla Service will inspect the front-row seat belts and connect the seat belts to pretensioner anchors, or replace the seat belt assemblies, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 12, 2023. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-23-20-004.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
