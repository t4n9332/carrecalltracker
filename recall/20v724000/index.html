Volkswagen Tiguan front seat belts may tear in crash

NHTSA campaign 20V724000 · Reported 2020-11-20 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Volkswagen Tiguan

What's wrong, in plain English

The webbing — the fabric strap of the front seat belt — can tear if you are in a crash. One or both of the front belts may be affected. Nothing looks or feels wrong from the driver's seat, and the belt still buckles and pulls normally, so you cannot spot this problem yourself. Volkswagen dealers will look at both front seat belts and put in new ones if needed, at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Serious, because a torn belt may not hold you in place in a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so it is fine to keep driving while you schedule the free inspection.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Tiguan is included.
2. Call a Volkswagen dealer or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 and mention recall 69BH.
3. Have the seat belts inspected and replaced if needed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:WEBBING
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2018 Tiguan LWB vehicles. In the event of a crash, one or both of the front seat belt's webbing may tear.
Safety risk
A torn seat belt may not adequately restrain the seat occupant during a crash, increasing their risk of injury.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the front seat belts and replace them as necessary, free of charge. The recall began April 13, 2021. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 69BH.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
