Silverado, Sierra Roof Airbag Inflator May Rupture

NHTSA campaign 25V432000 · Reported 2025-06-26 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

If this happens, compressed gas can escape and send the end cap or pieces of the inflator into the vehicle, increasing the risk of injury or a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra pickup trucks. These trucks have airbags built into the roof rail, above the doors. On the left or right side, the metal end cap on the airbag inflator may come loose and detach, or the inflator itself may crack open, or rupture.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included.
2. Contact a GMC dealer at 1-888-988-7267 or a Chevrolet dealer at 1-800-222-1020 to schedule service.
3. The airbag module replacement is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS
Summary
General Motors (GM) is recalling certain 2018 GMC Sierra 1500, 2019 Sierra 2500, 3500, Chevrolet Silverado 3500, and Chevrolet Silverado 2500 and 2018 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 Crew Cab vehicles. The left or right-side roof-rail air bag (RRAB) inflator end cap may detach from the inflator or the inflator sidewall may rupture.
Safety risk
A detached end cap or inflator rupture can allow compressed gas to escape and project the end cap or fragments of the inflator into the vehicle, increases the risk of injury or crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the left and right side RRAB modules, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 23, 2025. Owners may contact GMC customer service at 1-888-988-7267 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N252513060.
