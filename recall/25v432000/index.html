Chevrolet Silverado, GMC Sierra roof airbag may burst apart

NHTSA campaign 25V432000 · Reported 2025-06-26 · General Motors, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Chevrolet Silverado 1500
  • 2018 GMC Sierra 1500
  • 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 2500
  • 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 3500
  • 2019 GMC Sierra 2500
  • 2019 GMC Sierra 3500

What's wrong, in plain English

The roof-rail airbags run along the ceiling above the doors. The metal canister that fills these airbags with gas can come apart: an end cap can detach, or the side of the canister can split open. If that happens, pressurized gas escapes and can throw the end cap or pieces of metal into the cabin toward the people inside. Dealers replace both the left and right roof airbag units at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Serious, because metal pieces could be thrown inside the vehicle and injure occupants; the notice does not tell you to park it, but get this repaired as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 25V432000 (GM's number N252513060).
2. Call your Chevrolet dealer at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC at 1-888-988-7267 and schedule the airbag replacement right away.
3. Both roof airbag modules are replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS
Summary
General Motors (GM) is recalling certain 2018 GMC Sierra 1500, 2019 Sierra 2500, 3500, Chevrolet Silverado 3500, and Chevrolet Silverado 2500 and 2018 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 Crew Cab vehicles. The left or right-side roof-rail air bag (RRAB) inflator end cap may detach from the inflator or the inflator sidewall may rupture.
Safety risk
A detached end cap or inflator rupture can allow compressed gas to escape and project the end cap or fragments of the inflator into the vehicle, increases the risk of injury or crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the left and right side RRAB modules, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 23, 2025. Owners may contact GMC customer service at 1-888-988-7267 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N252513060.
