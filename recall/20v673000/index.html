Mercedes-Benz front passenger seat slides too far forward

NHTSA campaign 20V673000 · Reported 2020-10-30 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG E53

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2021 E350 and E450, 2019-2021 AMG E53 and 2020-2021 AMG E63 cars left the factory without a small stop that limits how far the front passenger seat can be adjusted. Without it, the seat can be moved to a position that is too close to, or badly placed in front of, the airbag. If the airbag goes off in a crash while the seat is out of range, the passenger can be hurt more than they should be. Dealers will check for the stop and add it if missing.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious — it only matters in a crash with airbag deployment, and the notice does not say to stop driving, so the car is safe to use while you arrange the free repair.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is affected. 2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer, or MBUSA at 1-800-368-6372 (recall 2020110013), to schedule the inspection. 3. Have the seat stop installed if needed — there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC. (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2021 E350 and E450, 2019-2021 AMG E53 and 2020-2021 AMG E63 vehicles. The front passenger seat may be missing the mechanical seat adjustment range limit stop, allowing the seat to move outside of the acceptable range in relation to the air bag.
Safety risk
If the front passenger seat is adjusted beyond the acceptable range in relation to the air bag, there could be an increased risk of injury in the event of a crash with air bag deployment.
Remedy
MBUSA will notify owners, and dealers will inspect for the mechanical seat adjustment range limitation for the front passenger seat and install it if necessary, free of charge. The recall began December 18, 2020. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-368-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2020110013.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
