Mazda CX-50 trailer hitch wiring may come loose

NHTSA campaign 23V135000 · Reported 2023-03-01 · Mazda North American Operations · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Mazda CX-50

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Mazda CX-50s were fitted with a Mazda accessory trailer hitch. The wiring harness that plugs into that hitch can come unplugged and get damaged. If it does, the lights and brakes on a trailer you are towing may stop working, so other drivers cannot see your turn or brake signals and the trailer cannot help slow you down. A dealer repairs the hitch wiring at no charge. Only vehicles with the accessory hitch are affected.

How serious is it?

The CX-50 itself drives normally, but you should not tow anything until the wiring is repaired, because a trailer with no lights or brakes raises the risk of a crash.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your CX-50 is part of recall 5723B.
2. Schedule the free wiring repair with your Mazda dealer, or call Mazda at 1-800-222-5500 option 6.
3. Avoid towing until the repair is complete.

Official notice

Component
TRAILER HITCHES
Summary
Mazda North America Operations (Mazda) is recalling certain 2023 CX-50 vehicles equipped with a Mazda Genuine Accessory trailer hitch. The trailer hitch harness may detach and become damaged, which can result in inoperative trailer lights and/or brakes.
Safety risk
Towing a trailer with inoperative lights or brakes can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will repair the trailer hitch harness, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 11, 2023. Owners may contact Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500 option 6. Mazda's number for this recall is 5723B.
