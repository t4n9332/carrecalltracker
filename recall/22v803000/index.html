Porsche 911 GT3 Seat Belt Anchor Screw Loose

NHTSA campaign 22V803000 · Reported Oct 25, 2022 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

An improperly tightened seat belt anchor can detach during a crash, which increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Porsche 911 GT3 vehicles, the screw that attaches the lower seat belt anchor to the seat frame may not have been tightened properly during assembly. Because the screw is not fully tightened, the anchor may not be as securely attached to the seat frame as it should be. This affects how firmly the seat belt anchor stays fixed to the seat frame on the affected vehicles.

What you should do

1. Check your vehicle's recall status by VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact a Porsche dealer for repair; call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. 3. The screws will be tightened or replaced, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V803000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:ANCHORAGE
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2022 911 GT3 vehicles. The screw that attaches the lower seat belt anchor to the seat frame may not have been tightened properly.
Safety risk
An improperly tightened seat belt anchor can detach during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace and tighten the screws. If the screw threads are damaged, the seat frame will also be replaced. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed December 23, 2022. Owners may contact Porsche's customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ANB6.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
