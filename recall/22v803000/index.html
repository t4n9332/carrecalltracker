Porsche 911 GT3 seat belt anchor screw may loosen

NHTSA campaign 22V803000 · Reported 2022-10-25 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Porsche 911 GT3

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2022 Porsche 911 GT3 cars, the screw that holds the bottom end of a front seat belt to the seat frame may not have been tightened enough at the factory. If that screw is loose, the belt anchor can pull away from the seat during a crash, so the belt would not hold you in place the way it is supposed to. The car otherwise drives normally, and there is no warning light for this.

How serious is it?

Serious in a crash because the seat belt may not protect you, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so normal driving is fine while you get the repair scheduled.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included.
2. Call your Porsche dealer, or Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243 (recall ANB6), and book an appointment.
3. The dealer replaces and properly tightens the screws — and the seat frame if the threads are damaged — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:ANCHORAGE
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2022 911 GT3 vehicles. The screw that attaches the lower seat belt anchor to the seat frame may not have been tightened properly.
Safety risk
An improperly tightened seat belt anchor can detach during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace and tighten the screws. If the screw threads are damaged, the seat frame will also be replaced. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed December 23, 2022. Owners may contact Porsche's customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ANB6.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
