What's wrong, in plain English

Kia is recalling certain Sorento Hybrid and Sorento Plug-in Hybrid vehicles because of a problem with the seat belt pretensioners. In the event of a crash, the front seat belt pretensioner on the driver's side, the passenger's side, or both, may explode instead of working as intended. If a pretensioner explodes, small metal fragments can be thrown into the vehicle's interior, striking the driver or a passenger and causing injury.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Kia dealer, or call Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542.
3. The dealer will secure the seat belt pretensioners with a cap, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V447000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Sorento Hybrid and 2022 Sorento Plug-in Hybrid vehicles. In the event of a crash, the front driver-side and/or passenger-side seat belt pretensioner(s) may explode upon deployment.
Safety risk
An exploding seat belt pretensioner can project metal fragments into the vehicle, striking vehicle occupants and resulting in injury.
Remedy
Dealers will secure the seat belt pretensioners with a cap, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 17, 2022. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC241. This recall expands and replaces NHTSA recall number 22V-127. Vehicles already repaired under the previous recall will need to have the new remedy completed.
