Honda Rebel 300 can leak oil onto hot exhaust

NHTSA campaign 23V261000 · Reported 2023-04-12 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Honda CMX300

What's wrong, in plain English

The cover on the right side of the engine was painted incorrectly at the factory. Because of the bad paint, a small plug pressed into that cover can work its way out and fall off. With the plug gone, engine oil can leak out and land on the hot exhaust pipe or on the rear tire. Oil on a hot pipe can start a fire, and oil on the rear tire can make the bike slide. Dealers replace the cover.

How serious is it?

This one is serious because a leak can cause a fire or make the rear tire lose grip, so get the repair done promptly and stop riding if you see oil leaking from the right side of the engine.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Rebel 300 is covered by recall KP2.
2. Contact your Honda motorcycle dealer, or Honda customer service at 1-800-784-1870, and book the repair.
3. Have the right crankcase cover replaced - it is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:EXHAUST SYSTEM:EMISSION CONTROL:CRANKCASE (PCV)
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023 Rebel 300 (CMX300) motorcycles. The right engine crank house cover was incorrectly painted, allowing the press-fit plug to fall out.
Safety risk
Oil can leak onto the exhaust pipe or rear tire without the plug, increasing the risk of a fire, crash, or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the right crank case cover, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 25, 2023. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-800-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KP2.
