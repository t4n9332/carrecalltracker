Honda Rebel 300 Engine Cover May Leak Oil

NHTSA campaign 23V261000 · Reported Apr 12, 2023 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

critical How serious is it? About this label

Oil leaking onto the exhaust pipe or rear tire increases the risk of a fire, a crash, or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Honda Rebel 300 (CMX300) motorcycles, the right engine crank case cover was painted incorrectly, which can let a press-fit plug fall out of it. Once the plug is gone, oil can leak out and land on the exhaust pipe or the rear tire.

What you should do

1. Check your motorcycle's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Honda dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-800-784-1870.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V261000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:EXHAUST SYSTEM:EMISSION CONTROL:CRANKCASE (PCV)
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023 Rebel 300 (CMX300) motorcycles. The right engine crank house cover was incorrectly painted, allowing the press-fit plug to fall out.
Safety risk
Oil can leak onto the exhaust pipe or rear tire without the plug, increasing the risk of a fire, crash, or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the right crank case cover, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 25, 2023. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-800-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KP2.
