Acura ZDX Rearview Camera Display Failure

NHTSA campaign 26V306000 · Reported May 14, 2026 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

A rearview image that doesn't display correctly reduces what the driver can see behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2024 Acura ZDX and 2024-2025 Honda Prologue vehicles, the rearview camera screen may show a distorted or blank picture instead of a clear view of what's behind the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact an Acura or Honda dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. An interim letter about this safety risk has already been mailed; a full remedy is expected in late 2026.
3. The camera replacement will be free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V306000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
American Honda Motor Company (Honda) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Honda Prologue and 2024 Acura ZDX vehicles. The rearview camera screen may display a distorted or blank image.
Safety risk
A rearview image that does not display correctly reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the rearview camera, free of charge. Interim letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, were mailed July 7, 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the remedy is available, anticipated in late 2026. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's numbers for this recall are DO5 and RO6. The information in your report suggests that Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) may have been aware of this issue more than five business days before filing a report with NHTSA. Please be reminded that under Federal law, this agency is to be notified of all safety defect and/or noncompliance decisions within five business days. 49 CFR 573.6 Significant civil penalties can be assessed for this violation.
