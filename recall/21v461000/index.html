Nissan Sentra bent tie rods can cause steering loss

NHTSA campaign 21V461000 · Reported 2021-06-17 · Nissan North America, Inc. · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Nissan Sentra
  • 2021 Nissan Sentra

What's wrong, in plain English

The tie rods are the metal arms that connect the steering to the front wheels and make them turn. On some Sentras, the left tie rod, the right tie rod, or both may be bent. A bent tie rod can break. If it breaks while you are driving, you lose control of the steering with no warning. A dealer will inspect both tie rods and replace any bad ones at no charge.

How serious is it?

Very serious: the notice does not order you to park the car, but a broken tie rod means sudden loss of steering with no warning, so treat this as urgent and get it inspected before more driving.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Sentra is part of recall 21V461000.
2. Call your Nissan dealer, or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 (Nissan recall number PM985), and ask for the earliest appointment.
3. Have the left and right tie rods inspected and replaced as needed — the repair is free; owner letters were mailed August 5, 2021.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:LINKAGES:TIE ROD ASSEMBLY
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Sentra vehicles. The left and/or right tie rod may be bent.
Safety risk
A bent tie rod can break, causing a loss of steering control and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the left and/or right tie rods as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 5, 2021. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PM985.
