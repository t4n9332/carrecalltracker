Volkswagen ID.4 battery cable can short and cause fire

NHTSA campaign 22V956000 · Reported 2022-12-29 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · critical severity

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Volkswagen ID.4

What's wrong, in plain English

The 12-volt battery charging cable can rub against the steering column shaft as you drive. Over time that rubbing wears through the cable's protective covering, and the bare wire can short out. A short circuit can cut power to the wheels, so the car may suddenly lose drive power. It can also start a fire. Volkswagen says to park the car outside and away from buildings until a dealer has checked the cable.

How serious is it?

Serious — there is a fire risk, so park outside and away from structures until the free repair is finished.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your ID.4 is included.
2. Until it is repaired, park the vehicle outdoors and away from your house, garage, or other structures.
3. Call a Volkswagen dealer or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall 97HA) to schedule the inspection and cable repair or replacement — it is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY:CABLES
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2023 ID4 rear-wheel drive vehicles. The 12-Volt battery charging cable may contact the steering column shaft and cause the cable insulation to wear, possibly resulting in a short circuit.
Safety risk
A short circuit may cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash. A short circuit also increases the risk of a vehicle fire.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the remedy has been completed. Dealers will inspect the 12-Volt battery charging cable and repair or replace it, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed February 27, 2023. Owners may contact Volkswagen's customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 97HA.
