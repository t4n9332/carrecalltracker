What's wrong, in plain English

Daimler Vans USA is recalling one 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter van. The front axle was fitted with brake discs of the wrong size. The discs are the parts the brake pads squeeze to slow the van down, so the wrong size can change how well the brakes work. A dealer will replace the brake discs at no charge to the owner. Owner notification letters for this recall were mailed in December 2021.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm whether your van is included.
2. Contact a dealer, or Daimler Vans USA customer service at 1-877-762-8267 (recall number VS3BRESCHE), to schedule the brake disc replacement.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V854000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:DISC
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling one 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter vehicle. The front axle was equipped with the incorrect-size brake discs.
Safety risk
The incorrect-size brake discs may affect braking performance, extending the distance required to stop the vehicle and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
A dealer will replace the brake discs, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 13, 2021. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3BRESCHE.
