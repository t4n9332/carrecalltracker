Jeep Cherokee crash sensors may fail, affecting airbags

NHTSA campaign 19V449000 · Reported 2019-06-13 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Jeep Cherokee

What's wrong, in plain English

Your vehicle uses small sensors to tell when it has been hit hard enough to need the airbags. On these Cherokees, those sensors can fail, so the system may not recognize certain crashes the way it should. If that happens in a collision, the airbags may not go off when they should, or may not go off correctly, leaving people less protected. Dealers will replace the faulty sensors at no charge.

How serious is it?

Serious, because airbags may not work properly in a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Cherokee is covered. 2. Call your Jeep dealer or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall V63) to book the repair. 3. The sensors will be replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Jeep Cherokee vehicles. The impact and acceleration sensors may fail and reduce the air bag system's ability to properly detect certain crash events.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, the inability to properly detect a crash may result in improper air bag deployment, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the affected sensors, free of charge. The recall began August 8, 2019. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V63.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
