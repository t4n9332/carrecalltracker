Kia Forte and Rio trunk cannot open from inside

NHTSA campaign 21V622000 · Reported 2021-08-09 · Kia Motors America · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Kia Forte
  • 2018 Kia RIO
  • 2019 Kia RIO

What's wrong, in plain English

Every car sold in the U.S. must have a handle inside the trunk so anyone shut in there can get out. On these Kias, the trunk latch can get damaged, and when that happens the inside release will not open the trunk. That matters most if a child climbs into the trunk while playing, or if someone is ever shut inside. The car drives normally otherwise. A dealer will replace the whole trunk latch assembly for free.

How serious is it?

The danger is someone becoming trapped in the trunk rather than anything happening while you drive, so the car is safe to keep driving, but keep children away from the trunk until it is fixed.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Forte or Rio is covered by campaign 21V622000 (Kia's number SC216).
2. Call your Kia dealer, or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542, to schedule the latch replacement.
3. Have the trunk latch assembly replaced at no charge to you.

Official notice

Component
LATCHES/LOCKS/LINKAGES:TRUNK LID:LATCH
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Forte 4-door, Forte Koup 2-door, and 2018-2019 Rio 4-door vehicles. The trunk latch may become damaged, preventing the opening of the trunk from the inside. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 401, "Internal Trunk Release."
Safety risk
A person inside the trunk compartment may become trapped, increasing their risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the trunk latch assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on February 10, 2022. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC216.
