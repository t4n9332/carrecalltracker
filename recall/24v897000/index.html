Jeep Grand Cherokee Side Air Bag Connector May Fail

NHTSA campaign 24V897000 · Reported 2024-11-27 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

An air bag that does not deploy during a crash increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

A connector for the front seat side air bags may not have been properly secured, which could prevent the air bags from deploying. This affects certain Jeep Grand Cherokee and Grand Cherokee L vehicles. Dealers have already secured the connector on all affected vehicles, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle's repair status.
2. If you have questions, contact a Chrysler dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The repair is free, and the notice states all affected vehicles have already been repaired.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Jeep Grand Cherokee and 2021-2024 Jeep Grand Cherokee L vehicles. An improperly secured connector for the front seat side air bags (SAB) may prevent the air bags from deploying. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 214, "Side Impact Protection."
Safety risk
An air bag that does not deploy during a crash increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers secured the connector, free of charge. All affected vehicles have been remedied. Owner notification letters were mailed December 17, 2024. Owners may contact FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's number for this recall is B3B.
