Kia Seltos and Soul Engine Piston Rings Defective

NHTSA campaign 25V099000 · Reported 2025-02-17 · Kia America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

Engine damage from this problem can cause a stall or a fire while driving, increasing the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Kia Seltos and Soul vehicles may have piston oil rings that weren't manufactured correctly. Over time, this can cause damage inside the engine. If the engine is damaged, it may stall unexpectedly while you're driving, or in some cases catch fire. Either of these increases the chance of a crash or of someone being injured.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Kia dealer for an engine inspection and repair; Kia customer service is at 800-333-4542.
3. The engine inspection, any needed replacement, and the software update are free.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:HARD PARTS INTERNAL/MECHANICAL
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Seltos and Soul vehicles. The piston oil rings may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can result in engine damage.
Safety risk
Engine damage can cause a vehicle stall or fire, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the engine as necessary, free of charge. Dealers will also install piston-ring noise sensing system (PNSS) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 4, 2025. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC336.
