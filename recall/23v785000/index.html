Cadillac Lyriq rear seat belt anchor may be weak

NHTSA campaign 23V785000 · Reported 2023-11-22 · General Motors, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Cadillac Lyriq
  • 2024 Cadillac Lyriq

What's wrong, in plain English

The metal bracket that holds the rear seat belts to the seat frame may have been welded poorly at the factory. This affects the left rear and middle rear belts. If the weld gives way in a crash, those belts may not hold the person wearing them, and they could be badly hurt. Cadillac says nobody should sit in the left rear or center rear seat until it is fixed. A dealer replaces the rear seat cushion frame.

How serious is it?

Serious for anyone riding in back: the car may be driven, but the notice says to leave the left rear and center rear seats empty until the repair is done.

What you should do

1. Stop using the left rear and center rear seating positions right away.
2. Confirm your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls, then contact your Cadillac dealer or Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 (recall N232425220).
3. Have the rear seat cushion frame replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER:ANCHORAGE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023-2024 LYRIQ vehicles. The bracket that anchors the left-rear and center-rear seat belts may have been improperly welded to the seat frame.
Safety risk
An improperly welded seat belt bracket may not restrain an occupant as intended, increasing their risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to use the left-rear and center-rear seating positions until the recall repair is completed. Dealers will replace the rear seat cushion frame, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 17, 2024. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N232425220.
