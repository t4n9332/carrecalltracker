Cadillac Lyriq Rear Seat Belt Anchor May Fail

NHTSA campaign 23V785000 · Reported Nov 22, 2023 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A seat belt that doesn't hold a passenger in place as intended increases the risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Cadillac LYRIQ vehicles, the bracket that holds the left-rear and center-rear seat belts to the seat frame may not have been welded correctly. Because of this, that bracket may not hold the seat belts as firmly as it should. A seat belt that isn't anchored securely may not keep a passenger properly restrained. This affects anyone riding in the left-rear or center-rear seating position.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm it's included.
2. Until the repair is done, do not use the left-rear or center-rear seating positions.
3. Contact a Cadillac dealer, or call Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 - the repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V785000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER:ANCHORAGE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023-2024 LYRIQ vehicles. The bracket that anchors the left-rear and center-rear seat belts may have been improperly welded to the seat frame.
Safety risk
An improperly welded seat belt bracket may not restrain an occupant as intended, increasing their risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to use the left-rear and center-rear seating positions until the recall repair is completed. Dealers will replace the rear seat cushion frame, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 17, 2024. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N232425220.
