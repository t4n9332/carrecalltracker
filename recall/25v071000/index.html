Volvo EX90 Headlight Shutters May Close While Driving

NHTSA campaign 25V071000 · Reported 2025-02-07 · Volvo Car USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

A loss of headlight function while driving can reduce visibility and raise the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Volvo is recalling certain EX90 vehicles because of a software error in the low power controller. This error can cause the headlight shutters to close over both the high and low beams while the vehicle is being driven. When the shutters close, the headlights stop putting out light, which can leave the driver with little or no forward lighting on the road.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your EX90 is included.
2. Contact your Volvo dealer, or call Volvo customer service at 1-800-458-1552.
3. The software update is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS:CONCEALMENT DEVICES
Summary
Volvo Car USA, LLC (Volvo) is recalling certain 2025 EX90 vehicles. Due to a software error in the low power controller (LPC), the headlight shutters may close over the high and low beams while driving.
Safety risk
A loss of headlight function while driving can reduce visibility and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The LPC software will be updated manually, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 12, 2025. Owners may contact Volvo's customer service at 1-800-458-1552. Volvo's number for this recall is R10298.
