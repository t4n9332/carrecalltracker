Volvo EX90 headlight covers may close while driving

NHTSA campaign 25V071000 · Reported 2025-02-07 · Volvo Car USA, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Volvo EX90

What's wrong, in plain English

These Volvo EX90s have covers, or shutters, that sit over the headlights. Because of a software error in one of the vehicle's control units, those shutters can close over both the low and high beams while you are driving. If that happens at night or in bad weather, your headlights are blocked and you suddenly cannot see the road ahead, and other drivers have a harder time seeing you. The fix is a software update that a technician loads into the vehicle by hand.

How serious is it?

Losing your headlights at night is serious and increases the risk of a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so avoid night driving if you can until the update is done.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your EX90 is included. 2. Contact your Volvo dealer, or Volvo customer service at 1-800-458-1552 (recall R10298), to schedule the software update. 3. Have the update installed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS:CONCEALMENT DEVICES
Summary
Volvo Car USA, LLC (Volvo) is recalling certain 2025 EX90 vehicles. Due to a software error in the low power controller (LPC), the headlight shutters may close over the high and low beams while driving.
Safety risk
A loss of headlight function while driving can reduce visibility and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The LPC software will be updated manually, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 12, 2025. Owners may contact Volvo's customer service at 1-800-458-1552. Volvo's number for this recall is R10298.
