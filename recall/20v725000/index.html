Honda Pilot: Certain Continental Tires May Fail

NHTSA campaign 20V725000 · Reported Nov 20, 2020 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2021 Honda Pilot vehicles have Continental tires that may have been cured for longer than intended during manufacturing. These over-cured tires can develop a break in the sidewall, causing sudden air loss, or the tire's belt can separate, leading to tread or belt loss. Dealers will inspect all four tires and replace any with the affected serial and mold numbers.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Honda dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. The tire replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V725000. Official notice

Component
TIRES
Summary
American Honda Motor Co. (Honda) is recalling certain 2021 Pilot vehicles equipped with Continental CrossContact LX Sport 245/50R20 102 H tires with DOT serial number A376 D3K9 2920 and mold numbers S-421920 and S-421921. The tires may have been cured for a time beyond the specification limits.
Safety risk
Over-cured tires may develop a break in the sidewall resulting in sudden air loss or a belt edge separation which could lead to a tread/belt loss. Either condition can cause a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Honda will notify owners, and dealers will inspect all four tires and replace the tires that have the affected DOT serial number and mold number, free of charge. The recall began January 11, 2021. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is U8Y.
