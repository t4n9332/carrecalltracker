Audi Q7 and Q8 may suddenly lose steering control

NHTSA campaign 20V149000 · Reported 2020-03-16 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Audi Q7
  • 2019 Audi Q7
  • 2019 Audi Q8

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolt that connects the steering shaft to the steering gear can work itself loose. That shaft is what carries your turning of the steering wheel down to the front wheels, so if the bolt loosens you can lose the ability to steer the vehicle. Audi dealers will inspect that bolt and replace it if necessary, at no cost to you. The recall began in March 2020, so the free repair has been available for a while.

How serious is it?

Very serious — a sudden loss of steering can happen with no warning and cause a crash, so have this checked immediately.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included and the work was not already done.
2. Contact an Audi dealer, or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834, and reference recall 36A1.
3. Have the steering shaft bolt inspected and replaced if needed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019 Audi Q8 and 2017-2019 Audi Q7 vehicles. The bolt connecting the steering shaft to the steering gear may loosen, possibly resulting in a loss of steering control.
Safety risk
A sudden loss of loss of steering control may increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and Audi dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace the steering shaft bolt, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began March 31, 2020. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 36A1.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
