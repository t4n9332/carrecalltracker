Nissan NV200, Chevrolet City Express Fuel Pump Fuse Risk

NHTSA campaign 25V676000 · Reported 2025-10-09 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A blown fuel pump fuse can cause the engine to stall while driving, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Nissan is recalling certain NV200 vans, NV200 taxis, and Chevrolet City Express vans. In these vehicles, a wiring harness for a sensor near the fuel tank may have been routed incorrectly. Because of this, the wires can become damaged and cause a short circuit. That short circuit can blow the fuse that powers the fuel pump, cutting off power to it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact your dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 800-647-7261, to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP:CONTROL/DRIVE MODULE
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2013-2021 NV200 Van, 2014-2017, 2019 NV200 Taxi, and 2015-2018 Chevrolet City Express vehicles. The Fuel Tank Temperature (FTT) sensor harness may have been incorrectly routed, damaging wires and causing a short circuit in the fuel pump fuse.
Safety risk
A blown fuel pump fuse can cause the engine to stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and reroute the FTT sensor harness or replace the fuel pump assembly as necessary, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed January 2, 2026. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 800-647-7261. Nissan's number for this recall is PMA56. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov October 10, 2025.
