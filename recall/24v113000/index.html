Honda Goldwing, CBR fuel pumps can fail and stall

NHTSA campaign 24V113000 · Reported 2024-02-15 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity

What's wrong, in plain English

The fuel pump inside the gas tank has a spinning part that may have been molded wrong. Over time it can warp and jam, so the pump stops sending gas to the engine. If that happens while you're riding, the engine can quit without warning, which is dangerous on a motorcycle in traffic. A Honda dealer will check the fuel pump and put in a new one if needed, at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Serious — the engine can stall with no warning and cause a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop riding, but have the pump checked as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your motorcycle is covered. 2. Contact a Honda dealer or Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870 (recalls KP8, KP9, KQ0, KQ1) to schedule the inspection. 3. Have the fuel pump inspected and replaced if needed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2018-2024 GL1800 (Goldwing), CBR600RR, and 2018-2019 CBR1000RR motorcycles. The fuel pump impellers may have been improperly molded, which can cause them to deform and result in fuel pump failure.
Safety risk
Fuel pump failure can cause an engine stall while driving, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the fuel pump module as necessary, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed June 19, 2024. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's numbers for this recall are KP8, KP9, KQ0, and KQ1.
