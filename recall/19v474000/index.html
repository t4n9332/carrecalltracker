Audi A3 E-Tron Passenger Air Bag May Shut Off

NHTSA campaign 19V474000 · Reported 2019-06-19 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A deactivated passenger air bag will not deploy to help protect against injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

A faulty electrical connector can cause the system that detects whether a passenger is in the seat to wrongly sense a problem. When that happens, it automatically turns off the passenger airbag. With the airbag turned off, it will not deploy to protect a passenger if the vehicle is in a crash. To fix this, dealers will replace the connector and reroute the cable so the system works correctly.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
Contact an Audi dealer, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-253-2834, to schedule the repair.
The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SENSOR:OCCUPANT CLASSIFICATION
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2015-2016 Audi S3 Sedan, 2015-2019 A3 Sedan and A3 Cabriolet, 2016-2018 A3 e-tron, and 2017-2019 RS3 vehicles. An improper electrical contact may cause the Passenger Occupant Detection System (PODS) to falsely detect a malfunction and deactivate the passenger air bag.
Safety risk
A deactivated passenger air bag increases the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will replace the connector and reroute the cable, free of charge. The recall began September 12, 2019. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 69Z4.
