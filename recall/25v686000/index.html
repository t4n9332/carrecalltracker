Ford F-250, F-350 and F-450 rearview camera problem

NHTSA campaign 25V686000 · Reported 2025-10-10 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

A rearview image that does not display properly gives the driver less view behind the truck, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain F-250, F-350 and F-450 Super Duty trucks. The 360-degree view camera system may not display the rearview image properly in certain lighting conditions. Ford's fix is a software update for the module that processes the camera image. The repair is being released in phases by model year, so owners are being told about the safety problem first and will be contacted again once the update is available for their truck.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included.
2. Watch for the letter telling you the update is ready for your model year, then contact a Ford dealer to have the image processing module software updated. Ford customer service can be reached at 1-866-436-7332.
3. The software update is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2022 F-250 SD, F-350 SD, and F-450 SD vehicles. The 360-degree view camera system may not display a rearview image properly in certain lighting conditions.
Safety risk
A rearview camera image that does not display properly can reduce the driver’s view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the image processing module software, free of charge.This will be a phased campaign, with the remedy becoming available in different phases based on model years. Interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk were mailed on October 30, 2025. Additional letters will be mailed once the remedy is available on each phase. Owner notification letters were mailed May 19, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25SA8. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on October 13, 2025.
