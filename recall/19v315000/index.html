Honda Clarity Fuel Cell Water Pump May Fail

NHTSA campaign 19V315000 · Reported 2019-04-18 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

A vehicle that stalls while being driven increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Clarity Fuel Cell vehicles, internal parts may corrode and cause the water pump to fail. A failed water pump can cause the vehicle to stall.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Honda dealer, or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. The water pump replacement is free.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:ELECTRICAL
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Clarity Fuel Cell vehicles. Internal components may corrode and cause the water pump to fail.
Safety risk
A failure of the water pump can cause the vehicle to stall, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Honda will notify owners, and dealers will replace the water pump, free of charge. The recall began June 10, 2019. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is L4N.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
