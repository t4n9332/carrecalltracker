Audi Q6 e-tron rearview camera image may be delayed

NHTSA campaign 25V226000 · Reported 2025-04-09 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Audi says a rearview camera image that is delayed or deactivated reduces the driver's view of what is behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of injury or a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Audi is recalling certain 2025 Q6 and SQ6 vehicles. After the driver shifts into reverse, the picture from the rearview camera may be delayed or may switch off. That means the screen may not show what is behind the vehicle when it is needed. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard. Dealers will update the software in the rearview camera control unit free of charge, and owner letters were mailed May 20, 2025.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact an Audi dealer to have the camera software updated, or call Volkswagen customer service at 800-893-5298 (the recall number is 916C).
3. The software update is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2025 SQ6 and Q6 vehicles. The rearview camera image may be delayed or deactivated after shifting into reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview camera with a delayed or deactivated image reduces the driver's view of what is behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of injury or crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the rearview camera control unit software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 20, 2025. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 916C.
