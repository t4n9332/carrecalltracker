Audi Q6 e-tron backup camera may not display

NHTSA campaign 25V226000 · Reported 2025-04-09 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Audi Q6 E-TRON

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Audi Q6 and SQ6 electric SUVs, the backup camera picture may come up late or not turn on at all after you shift into reverse. Federal rules require the image to show when you back up, and these vehicles do not meet that rule. Without it, you see less of what is behind the SUV, so there is a greater chance of backing into someone or something. A dealer will update the camera's software for free.

How serious is it?

Moderate: the vehicle is fine to drive and the notice sets no restriction, but until the update is done, rely on your mirrors and look behind you before reversing.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Schedule the software update with an Audi dealer, or call customer service at 800-893-5298 and reference recall 916C.
3. Have the update performed — there is no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2025 SQ6 and Q6 vehicles. The rearview camera image may be delayed or deactivated after shifting into reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview camera with a delayed or deactivated image reduces the driver's view of what is behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of injury or crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the rearview camera control unit software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 20, 2025. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 916C.
