Cadillac XT4 and Malibu Wrong Owner's Manual

NHTSA campaign 24V575000 · Reported 2024-08-01 · General Motors, LLC

low How serious is it? About this label

Inaccurate information in the manual could lead to improper use of the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain 2024 Cadillac XT4 and Chevrolet Malibu vehicles that may have the wrong owner's manual in the glove box. Because the manual doesn't match the vehicle, it can contain inaccurate information about how the vehicle's functions work and other safety-related details. Having incorrect information available to the driver could lead to the vehicle being operated the wrong way.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006, Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020, or your dealer, to get the correct manual.
3. The replacement manual is provided free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:OWNERS/SERVICE/OTHER MANUAL
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024 Cadillac XT4 and Chevrolet Malibu vehicles. The vehicles may have the wrong owner's manual in the glove box. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 225, "Child Restraint Anchorage Systems."
Safety risk
The wrong owner's manual may contain inaccurate information about vehicle functions and other safety relevant information, which could lead to improper operation of the vehicle and increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will provide owners with the correct manual, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 26, 2024. Owners may contact Cadillac's customer service at 1-800-458-8006 and Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N242454040.
