Ford F-350 Super Duty labels overstate safe payload capacity

NHTSA campaign 21V087000 · Reported 2021-02-18 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Ford F-350 SD

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2020 Ford F-350 Super Duty trucks with the 6.7L diesel have stickers and paperwork that claim the truck can carry more weight than it really can. The tire and loading label, the safety certification label, and the truck camper loading document all list numbers that are too high. If you load the truck to the printed figure, you can go over its safe weight limit and overload it. Your dealer will replace the labels and document for free.

How serious is it?

An overloaded truck is harder to control and stop, which raises crash risk, so it is fine to keep driving as long as you do not load it to the numbers printed on the labels.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck falls under campaign 21V087000.
2. Contact your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 21S06), to schedule the label replacement.
3. Get the corrected labels and camper loading document installed — they cost you nothing.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020 F-Super Duty F350 vehicles equipped with a 6.7L diesel engine, single rear wheels (SRW), 4X4 Crew Cab, Long Box, and a 12k or 12.4k Gross Vehicle Weight Rating. These vehicles may display overstated payload capacity values on the Tire and Loading Information (TREAD) Label, overstated Accessory Reserve Capacity (ARC) values on the Safety Certification Label, and overstated weight values on the Truck Camper Loading Documentation. If the vehicle is loaded to the payload stated on the TREAD label, it may exceed the Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR) or Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR) and overload the vehicle.
Safety risk
An overloaded vehicle can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will replace the Tire and Loading Information Label, Safety Certification Label, and the Truck Camper Loading Document (if applicable), free of charge. The recall began March 23, 2021. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21S06.
