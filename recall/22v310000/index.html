Toyota 4Runner, Tacoma and Highlander weight labels may fade

NHTSA campaign 22V310000 · Reported May 6, 2022 · Gulf States Toyota, Inc.

low How serious is it? About this label

The notice says a label that cannot be read may allow the vehicle to be overloaded, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Toyota 4Runner, Tacoma, Highlander and Highlander Hybrid vehicles have a label showing how much weight the vehicle can carry. On these vehicles the label may not be permanent and can fade until it can no longer be read. Because of that, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard covering tire and rim selection. Gulf States Toyota is mailing owners a replacement label along with instructions for putting it on.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is covered.
2. Watch for the replacement label and instructions arriving by mail; contact Gulf States Toyota customer service at 1-800-444-1074 (recall number 22R1) if you do not receive them.
3. The replacement label is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V310000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Gulf States Toyota, Inc. (GST) is recalling certain 2022 4Runner, Tacoma, Highlander, and Highlander Hybrid vehicles. The load carrying capacity modification labels may not be permanent and can fade, becoming illegible. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
An illegible label may allow the vehicle to be overloaded, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
GST will notify owners by mail, including a replacement load carrying capacity modification label for their vehicle and detailed replacement instructions, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 30, 2022. Owners may contact GST customer service at 1-800-444-1074. GST's number for this recall is 22R1.
