Infiniti QX50, QX55 Front Door Lock May Not Latch

NHTSA campaign 23V187000 · Reported 2023-03-20 · Nissan North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

An unlocked door that opens during a crash increases the risk of injury to occupants. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Nissan says some INFINITI QX50 and QX55 vehicles may have the wrong door latch installed in the front right passenger door. Because of this, the front right door lock may not lock automatically as it should. If the door is not locked, it could unlatch and open if the vehicle is involved in a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact an INFINITI dealer to schedule the free door lock replacement, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2022 Infiniti QX50
  • 2023 Infiniti QX55

Official notice

Component
LATCHES/LOCKS/LINKAGES:DOORS:LOCK
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2023 INFINITI QX55 and 2022 INFINITI QX50 vehicles. The incorrect door latch may have been installed in the front right-side passenger door lock assembly, preventing the front right-side door lock from automatically locking.
Safety risk
An unlocked door may unlatch and open during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front right-side passenger door lock assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed May 14, 2023. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PC962.
