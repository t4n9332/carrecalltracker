Silverado and Sierra sport bar brake light may fail

NHTSA campaign 22V463000 · Reported Jun 30, 2022 · General Motors, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

General Motors says a high-mounted brake light that does not work can make the truck harder for other drivers to see and increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some of these trucks are fitted with an accessory sport bar. The sport bar contains a brake light mounted up high, and that light may not work. The bar may also block the truck's own high-mounted brake light. Because of this, the trucks may not meet the federal standard covering lamps, reflective devices and related equipment. General Motors is recalling certain 2022 Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra trucks that have this accessory bar installed.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is covered. 2. Contact a dealer to have the sport bar installation inspected, or call GM customer service at 1-888-988-7267 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. 3. The inspection and any corrected wiring are free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V463000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
General Motors (GM) has decided that certain 2022 model year Chevrolet Silverado, and GMC Sierra vehicles equipped with an accessory sport bar. The accessory sport bar contains a high-mounted brake light that may not function. In addition, it may block the vehicle's existing high-mounted brake light. As such, these vehicles may fail to conform to Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "lamps, reflective devices, and associated equipment."
Safety risk
An inoperative high-mounted brake light can reduce visibility to other drivers and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the installation of the accessory bar and provide the correct vehicle wiring, if necessary, free of charge. Interim owner notification letters advising owners of the safety risk were mailed on August 10, 2022. Owner notification letters were mailed on January 27, 2023. Owners may contact GM's customer service on 1-888-988-7267 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020.. GM's recall number for this recall is N222368110.
