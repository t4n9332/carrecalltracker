Silverado and Sierra sport bar blocks brake light

NHTSA campaign 22V463000 · Reported 2022-06-30 · General Motors, LLC · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Chevrolet Silverado 1500
  • 2022 GMC Sierra 1500

What's wrong, in plain English

Some of these trucks were fitted with an accessory sport bar. The brake light mounted on that bar may not work, and the bar can also block the truck's own high center brake light. Either way, drivers behind you may not get a clear signal when you brake, so the truck does not meet the federal lighting standard. A dealer will check how the bar was installed and correct the wiring if needed.

How serious is it?

Moderate: GM does not tell owners to stop driving, so the truck is safe to drive, but a driver behind you may not see you braking, which raises the chance of being rear-ended.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is affected.
2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer (1-800-222-1020) or GMC dealer (1-888-988-7267) and mention recall N222368110.
3. Have the dealer inspect the sport bar and supply the correct wiring — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
General Motors (GM) has decided that certain 2022 model year Chevrolet Silverado, and GMC Sierra vehicles equipped with an accessory sport bar. The accessory sport bar contains a high-mounted brake light that may not function. In addition, it may block the vehicle's existing high-mounted brake light. As such, these vehicles may fail to conform to Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "lamps, reflective devices, and associated equipment."
Safety risk
An inoperative high-mounted brake light can reduce visibility to other drivers and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the installation of the accessory bar and provide the correct vehicle wiring, if necessary, free of charge. Interim owner notification letters advising owners of the safety risk were mailed on August 10, 2022. Owner notification letters were mailed on January 27, 2023. Owners may contact GM's customer service on 1-888-988-7267 or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020.. GM's recall number for this recall is N222368110.
