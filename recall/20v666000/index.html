BMW M4 Fuel Injector Issue May Cause Fuel Leak

NHTSA campaign 20V666000 · Reported Oct 29, 2020 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A damaged fuel line connection can leak fuel, and if there is a spark or other ignition source nearby, that raises the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling certain M2 Coupe and M4 Coupe vehicles because some fuel injectors may have been built without a small damping part that is meant to reduce vibration. Without that part, vibration increases at the point where the fuel line connects to the fuel rail. Over time, this extra vibration can wear down and damage that connection.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a BMW dealer, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. Dealers will inspect the fuel injectors and replace parts if needed, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V666000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020-2021 M2 Coupe and 2020 M4 Coupe vehicles. Fuel injectors may have been installed without a damping component which is used to reduce vibration. If a damping component is not present, vibration will increase at the connection between the fuel line and the fuel rail, which over time, can result in damage at the connection.
Safety risk
A damaged connection between the fuel line and the fuel rail could lead to a fuel leak and, in the presence of an ignition source, increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the fuel injectors and if a damping component is missing, the injectors and the fuel rail will be replaced, free of charge. The recall began December 18, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
