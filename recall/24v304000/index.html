Nissan Sentra Front Driveshaft Can Slip, Causing Power Loss

NHTSA campaign 24V304000 · Reported 2024-04-29 · Nissan North America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Nissan Sentra

What's wrong, in plain English

The front left driveshaft — the bar that sends power from the transmission to the wheel — may not be pushed in all the way, or the clip that holds it may be loose or missing. If it works free, transmission fluid can leak out and the car can stop driving. Dealers will check the driveshaft and, if needed, replace it along with the transmission at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Losing drive power without warning can cause a crash, so get it inspected as soon as you can; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your 2024 Sentra is included.
2. Call your Nissan dealer, or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669, and ask about recall PMA37.
3. Book the inspection and any repair — it is free, and letters went out June 26, 2024.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:DRIVELINE:DRIVESHAFT
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2024 Sentra vehicles. The front left-side driveshaft may not be fully seated in the continuously variable transmission (CVT) assembly, or may have a loose or missing retaining clip, which can cause a transmission fluid leak and loss of drive power.
Safety risk
An unexpected loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the front left driveshaft, and if necessary, replace the driveshaft and CVT assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 26, 2024. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PMA37.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
