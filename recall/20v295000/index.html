Chevrolet Trailblazer label missing tire size and pressure

NHTSA campaign 20V295000 · Reported 2020-05-21 · General Motors LLC · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Chevrolet Trailblazer

What's wrong, in plain English

The information label on certain 2021 Chevrolet Trailblazers built with the optional 17-inch sport terrain tires is incomplete. It does not list the tire size or the correct cold tire pressure for the front and rear tires, which federal rules require it to show. Without that information, an owner could put too much or too little air in the tires. GM is mailing corrected labels that owners stick on themselves, at no cost.

How serious is it?

This is a paperwork problem rather than a broken part, and the vehicle is safe to drive as long as you keep your tires at the pressure GM specifies.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Trailblazer is on the list.
2. Watch for the corrected label in the mail and apply it where the old one is; call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 (recall A202304070) if it does not arrive.
3. Ask a Chevrolet dealer for help with the label or tire pressures if you have questions — there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
TIRES
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2021 Chevrolet Trailblazer vehicles factory-equipped with optional 17-inch sport terrain tires. The certification label on these vehicles does not provide the tire size and cold tire pressure information for front and rear tires, as required. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard Number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
If drivers are uninformed and overinflate or underinflate their tires, this may increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and will mail corrected labels for customers to apply, free of charge. The recall began July 1, 2020. Owners may call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is A202304070.
