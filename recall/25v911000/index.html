Honda CBR650R wiring damage kills lights and horn

NHTSA campaign 25V911000 · Reported 2025-12-23 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Honda Cbr650r

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2024-2025 Honda CBR650R motorcycles, engine vibration can rub and damage the wiring for the turn signal switch. Damaged wiring can short out and blow a fuse. When that fuse goes, you lose your turn signals, your brake light, your speedometer and your horn all at once. Other drivers can no longer see when you are turning or slowing down. The dealer inspects that wiring and repairs it if needed.

How serious is it?

Losing your brake light, signals and horn while riding is a real crash and injury risk, and the notice does not tell riders to stop riding, so get it inspected promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — affected VINs have been searchable since January 8, 2026. 2. Call your Honda dealer or Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870 and mention recall KT7. 3. Have the wiring inspected and repaired at no charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2024-2025 CBR650R motorcycles. Engine vibration may cause the turn signal switch wire harness to become damaged, which can cause an electrical short circuit and blown fuse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
A blown fuse will cause a loss of turn signal, brake light, speedometer, and horn function, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and repair the turn signal switch wire harness as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 9, 2026. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KT7. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on January 8, 2026.
