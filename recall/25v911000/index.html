Honda CBR650R Wiring May Cause Lighting Loss

NHTSA campaign 25V911000 · Reported 2025-12-23 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing your turn signals, brake light, speedometer, and horn all at once increases the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

American Honda is recalling certain 2024-2025 CBR650R motorcycles because engine vibration can damage the turn signal switch wiring. That damage can cause an electrical short and blow a fuse. If the fuse blows, the motorcycle can lose its turn signals, brake light, speedometer, and horn all at once.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Honda dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870.
3. The dealer will inspect and repair the wiring free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2024-2025 CBR650R motorcycles. Engine vibration may cause the turn signal switch wire harness to become damaged, which can cause an electrical short circuit and blown fuse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
A blown fuse will cause a loss of turn signal, brake light, speedometer, and horn function, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and repair the turn signal switch wire harness as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 9, 2026. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KT7. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on January 8, 2026.
