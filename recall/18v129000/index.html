Ram ProMaster City Tire Placard Lists Wrong Capacity

NHTSA campaign 18V129000 · Reported Feb 22, 2018 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

low How serious is it? About this label

If the placard overstates how many people the vehicle can carry, you could overload it, which can cause a tire to fail and increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2018 ProMaster City vehicles have a tire placard that shows the wrong total number of occupants the vehicle is allowed to carry.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Chrysler dealer or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The dealer will install a corrected tire placard, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V129000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018 RAM ProMaster City vehicles. The Tire Placards on these vehicles may indicate the incorrect total number of occupants allowed. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
If the tire placard indicates an incorrect seating capacity, the operator may overload the vehicle, which can cause tire failure, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will install a corrected tire placard, free of charge. The recall began March 15, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U17.
