BMW X3 passenger airbag fabric damaged during shipping

NHTSA campaign 24V772000 · Reported 2024-10-17 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 BMW X3

What's wrong, in plain English

The fabric of the front passenger airbag may have been damaged while it was being shipped between suppliers. A damaged airbag may not fill with air properly in a crash, so someone riding in the front passenger seat could be hurt worse than they otherwise would be. None of these 2025 X3 xDrive30i and X3 M50i vehicles had been sold yet, so BMW did not mail owner notification letters. Dealers replace the airbag free of charge.

How serious is it?

Serious — the vehicle drives normally and the danger only shows up in a crash, but a front passenger is not fully protected until the airbag is replaced.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — no letters were mailed, so look it up yourself.
2. Call a BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, to book the airbag replacement.
3. Have the front passenger airbag replaced at no cost.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:PASSENGER SIDE FRONTAL:CUSHION
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2025 X3 xDrive30i and X3 M50i vehicles. The front passenger air bag fabric may have been damaged during transport between suppliers.
Safety risk
A damaged air bag may not inflate properly, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front passenger air bag, free of charge. None of the affected vehicles have been sold yet and therefore no owner notification letters will be mailed. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
