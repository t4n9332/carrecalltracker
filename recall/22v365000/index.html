Mercedes-Benz vehicles may lose automatic emergency call system

NHTSA campaign 22V365000 · Reported 2022-05-20 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

With the emergency call system disabled, someone in the vehicle could not contact the emergency services call center in an emergency, which could delay emergency responders and increase the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Mercedes-Benz is recalling a wide range of its cars and SUVs, including many AMG models. A software error on the SIM card inside the vehicle's communication module can cause the car to lose its mobile network connection. When that happens, the emergency call system is disabled. That is the system an occupant uses to reach an emergency services call center. The software will be updated either over the air or by a dealer, free of charge.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included. 2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer about the software update, or call MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372; the update may also arrive over the air. 3. The update is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2017-2022 CLA-Class, GLA-Class, GLE-Class, GLS-Class, SLC-Class, A-Class, AMG GT-Class, C-Class, E-Class, S-Class, SL-Class, GLC-Class, CLS-Class, GLB-Class, and G-Class vehicles. Please refer to MBUSA's recall report for specific vehicle model details. An error in the communication module's SIM card software can cause a mobile network connection failure, disabling the emergency call (eCall) system.
Safety risk
A disabled eCall system would prevent a vehicle occupant from contacting the emergency services call center in an emergency, potentially delaying emergency responders and increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
The communication module software will be updated through an Over-the-Air (OTA) update or by a dealer, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 8, 2022. Owner notification letters will be mailed in phases, on July 8, 2022, May 17, 2024, July 29, 2024, and September 27, 2024. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's numbers are for this recall is 2022060005 and 2023110006. (over-the-air software update)
