Tesla Cybertruck accessory light bar can fall off

NHTSA campaign 25V735000 · Reported 2025-10-28 · Tesla, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderately serious: the main danger is to other drivers if the light bar falls into traffic, and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2024 Cybertrucks had an off-road light bar installed at a Tesla service center. The wrong surface primer may have been used to bond the light bar to the windshield, so the attachment can weaken. Over time the light bar can work loose and detach from the truck. If it comes off while you are driving, it drops into the road and becomes a hazard for drivers behind you. Only trucks with this service-installed accessory are affected.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if campaign 25V735000 applies to your Cybertruck.
2. Contact Tesla Service, or Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752 (recall SB-25-90-001), to book an inspection.
3. Tesla will add a mechanical fastener or replace the light bar with a proper attachment, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2024 Cybertruck vehicles equipped with a service-installed off-road light bar accessory. An incorrect surface primer may have been used to attach the lightbar to the windshield, causing the light bar to loosen and detach.
Safety risk
A detached light bar may fall, creating a road hazard for other vehicles and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla Service will inspect the light bar and install an additional mechanical attachment or replace the light bar using tape to adhere the light bar to the windshield as well as an additional mechanical attachment as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 9, 2026. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-25-90-001.
