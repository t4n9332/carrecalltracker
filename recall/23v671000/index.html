Volkswagen Atlas driver side airbag missing a weld

NHTSA campaign 23V671000 · Reported 2023-10-04 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Volkswagen Atlas

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2019 Atlas vehicles may have a driver's side airbag that is missing a weld where the airbag housing is joined together. Without that weld, the airbag could open the wrong way in a crash, or the unit could come loose entirely. Either one means it may not cushion the driver the way it is meant to, and could increase the risk of injury. A dealer will replace the driver's side airbag free of charge.

How serious is it?

An airbag that deploys improperly or detaches in a crash raises the risk of injury, which is serious; the notice does not instruct owners to stop driving, so you can keep using the vehicle while the repair is arranged.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls against recall 23V671000 (Volkswagen's number 69FK). 2. Call your Volkswagen dealer or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 to schedule the replacement. 3. Have the driver's side airbag replaced at no cost.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019 Atlas vehicles. The driver's side impact air bag may be missing a weld at the side air bag housing.
Safety risk
The driver's side impact air bag could deploy improperly or detach in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver's side impact air bag, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 19, 2023. Owners may contact Volkswagen's customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 69FK.
