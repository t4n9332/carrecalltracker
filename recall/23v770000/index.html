BMW steering gear may be loose on several 2024 models

NHTSA campaign 23V770000 · Reported 2023-11-16 · BMW of North America, LLC · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 BMW X3
  • 2024 BMW X4

What's wrong, in plain English

This recall covers certain 2024 BMW models, including the 2 Series Coupe (230i), 330i, X3 sDrive30i, X3 xDrive30i, X3 M40i, X4 xDrive30i and X4 M40i. The steering gear — the part that turns your steering wheel movement into the wheels turning — may not have been tightened properly at the factory, so it can work loose over time. A loose steering gear can make you lose control of the vehicle. Dealers will replace the steering gear at no charge. BMW mailed owner letters on January 11, 2024.

How serious is it?

Losing control of the vehicle is a serious crash risk, so schedule the free repair as soon as you can; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered. 2. Call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 or contact your BMW dealer for an appointment. 3. Have the dealer replace the steering gear — it is free.

Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2024 2 Series Coupe (230i), 330i, X3 sDrive30i, X3 xDrive30i, X3 M40i, and X4 xDrive30i, X4 M40i vehicles. The steering gear may not have been tightened properly, which can allow it to loosen.
Safety risk
A loose steering gear can cause a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the steering gear, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 11, 2024. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
