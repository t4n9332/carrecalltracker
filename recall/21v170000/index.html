Nissan Murano suspension link may break from ball joint

NHTSA campaign 21V170000 · Reported 2021-03-11 · Nissan North America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Nissan Murano

What's wrong, in plain English

A suspension part called the transverse link, on the left side, right side, or both, may have been built incorrectly on these Muranos. The part can pull away from its ball joint, which is the pivot that holds the wheel in position. If that happens while you are driving, you can lose control of the car. A dealer will check the part numbers on these links, replace them if needed, and realign the wheels.

How serious is it?

Serious — a suspension part coming apart can cause you to lose control with little or no warning, so schedule the inspection as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Murano is covered. 2. Contact your Nissan dealer for an inspection, or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669, and mention recall PC797. 3. The inspection, any replacement links, and the alignment are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:LINKAGES
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020 Nissan Murano vehicles. The left and/or right-hand transverse link may not have been manufactured correctly, which could result in it separating from the ball joint.
Safety risk
Ball joint separation can cause a loss of vehicle control, and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the transverse link's lot codes, and if necessary, replace them and perform a vehicle alignment, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 6, 2021. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PC797.
