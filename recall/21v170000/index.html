Nissan Murano Suspension Link May Separate

NHTSA campaign 21V170000 · Reported 2021-03-11 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

If the link separates from the ball joint, the driver can lose control of the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2020 Nissan Murano vehicles, the left and/or right transverse link in the suspension may not have been made correctly. This could allow the link to separate from the ball joint it connects to.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact a Nissan dealer or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 to schedule an inspection. 3. Dealers will inspect the part and replace it and realign the vehicle if needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:LINKAGES
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020 Nissan Murano vehicles. The left and/or right-hand transverse link may not have been manufactured correctly, which could result in it separating from the ball joint.
Safety risk
Ball joint separation can cause a loss of vehicle control, and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the transverse link's lot codes, and if necessary, replace them and perform a vehicle alignment, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 6, 2021. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PC797.
