Acura ZDX stabilizer bar bolts may come loose

NHTSA campaign 25V234000 · Reported 2025-04-10 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Acura ZDX

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts holding the bracket for the front stabilizer bar on the passenger side may not have been tightened enough, so they can work loose and pull away from the frame. If the bar or its bracket falls partly off, it can hit and damage the high-voltage cables or the battery coolant lines underneath. That can shut the vehicle down and leave you without power to move. Dealers will inspect and tighten the bolts and repair any damage for free.

How serious is it?

This is a serious defect because the vehicle can shut down and lose drive power while you are on the road; Honda does not say to stop driving, but have it inspected as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls against recall SLH. 2. Schedule an inspection at an Acura dealer promptly. 3. Call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 if you need help — inspection and repairs are free.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:STABILIZER BAR
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2024 Acura ZDX vehicles. The passenger-side front stabilizer bar bracket bolts may have been improperly tightened, which can allow the bolts to loosen and separate from the vehicle frame. A detached stabilizer bar or bracket can damage the vehicle's high voltage cables or EV battery coolant lines.
Safety risk
Damaged cables or coolant lines can result in a vehicle shutdown, causing a loss of drive power and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and tighten the bolts, and repair the stabilizer bar and bracket, high voltage cables, and EV battery coolant lines as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 30, 2025. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is SLH.
