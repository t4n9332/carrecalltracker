Acura ZDX Stabilizer Bar Bolts May Loosen

NHTSA campaign 25V234000 · Reported 2025-04-10 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing drive power while driving increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Acura ZDX vehicles have passenger-side front stabilizer bar bracket bolts that may not have been tightened properly. If these bolts loosen, they can separate from the vehicle frame, and the stabilizer bar or bracket can detach. A detached part can damage the vehicle's high voltage cables or the battery coolant lines. Damage to those cables or lines can cause the vehicle to shut down and lose drive power while being driven, which increases the chance of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact an Acura dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. The inspection and any needed repairs are free.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:STABILIZER BAR
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2024 Acura ZDX vehicles. The passenger-side front stabilizer bar bracket bolts may have been improperly tightened, which can allow the bolts to loosen and separate from the vehicle frame. A detached stabilizer bar or bracket can damage the vehicle's high voltage cables or EV battery coolant lines.
Safety risk
Damaged cables or coolant lines can result in a vehicle shutdown, causing a loss of drive power and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and tighten the bolts, and repair the stabilizer bar and bracket, high voltage cables, and EV battery coolant lines as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 30, 2025. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is SLH.
