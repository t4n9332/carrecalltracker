What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors, LLC is recalling certain 2024 Chevrolet Blazer, Blazer EV, Equinox, and GMC Terrain vehicles because the door strikers may fracture. If a door striker fractures, the door it is attached to can open unexpectedly while the vehicle is being driven. Dealers will replace all four door strikers, along with the bolts that attach them, on affected vehicles at no cost to owners.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included. 2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer at 1-800-222-1020, or a GMC dealer at 1-800-462-8782. 3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V869000. Official notice

Component

Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024 Chevrolet Blazer, Blazer EV, Equinox, and GMC Terrain vehicles. The door strikers may fracture, resulting in the door opening unexpectedly.
Safety risk
A door opening while the vehicle is being driven can increasing the risk of injury or a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace all four door strikers and attaching bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 24, 2024. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 and GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N232429911.
