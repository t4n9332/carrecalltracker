Volkswagen Golf R fuel line may leak in engine bay

NHTSA campaign 18V864000 · Reported 2018-12-05 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Volkswagen Golf R

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2018 Golf R cars, the quick-connect fitting that joins a fuel hose to the fuel supply line under the hood can come apart. If it does, gasoline can leak out into the engine compartment while you drive. The engine bay gets hot, so leaking fuel there can catch fire. A dealer will check the connection and secure it properly at no cost to you. This recall began January 11, 2019, so the fix has been available for a long time.

How serious is it?

This is serious because a fuel leak near a hot engine raises the risk of a fire; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get it checked as soon as you can and pay attention to any smell of gasoline.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your car is included and whether the repair was already done. 2. Call your Volkswagen dealer and schedule the fix, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall number 20Bi). 3. Have the dealer secure the fuel line connection — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2018 Volkswagen Golf R vehicles. The fuel hose quick connector may detach from the fuel supply line in the engine bay, resulting in a fuel leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the engine compartment can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will secure the fuel line connection, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began January 11, 2019. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 20Bi.
