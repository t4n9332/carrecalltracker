Audi E-Tron GT Front Brake Hose Can Leak

NHTSA campaign 24V465000 · Reported 2024-06-20 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Reduced braking performance can extend the distance needed to stop, which raises the chance of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On these cars, the brake hoses that run to the front wheels can develop tears. A tear can let brake fluid leak out, which can cause that brake circuit to stop working properly. When this happens, the brake pedal may need to be pushed down farther than normal, and the brakes may not slow the car as well as they should, making it take longer to come to a stop.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact an Audi dealer, or call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834, to schedule the repair.
3. The brake hose replacement will be done free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, AIR:SUPPLY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Audi RS E-Tron GT and Audi E-Tron GT vehicles. The front axle brake hoses can develop tears, causing a leak and a brake circuit failure.
Safety risk
A failed brake circuit can cause longer brake pedal travel and reduced braking performance, extending the distance required to stop and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front axle brake hoses, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on August 6, 2024. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 47UP.
