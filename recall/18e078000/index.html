Honda Africa Twin accessory centerstand may fall off

NHTSA campaign 18E078000 · Reported 2018-08-03 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

A detached centerstand can cause a crash or tip the parked bike over; Honda says do not use the affected stand until a dealer repairs it.

What's wrong, in plain English

This recall covers Honda Genuine Accessory Centerstand Kits, part number 08M70-MJP-G50, sold for 2016-2018 Africa Twin motorcycles. A small retaining clip called a circlip can break and let the centerstand come off. If it falls off while you are riding, it becomes a hazard in the road. If it breaks while the bike is parked on the stand, the motorcycle can tip over. Honda says not to use the affected centerstand until it is fixed.

What you should do

1. Check whether you bought this centerstand kit, and look up your motorcycle at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Stop using the centerstand and take the motorcycle to a Honda dealer, or call Honda at 1-866-784-1870 (recall number KJ5). 3. The dealer replaces the circlip and washer free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:MOTORCYCLE:KICKSTAND/CENTER STAND
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain Honda Genuine Accessory Centerstand Kits, part number 08M70-MJP-G50, sold for possible installation on 2016-2018 Africa Twin motorcycles. The circlip can break allowing the centerstand to detach.
Safety risk
If the centerstand detaches while moving, it can become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash. If the centerstand detaches while the motorcycle is parked on the centerstand, the motorcycle can fall over, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Honda will notify all registered owners of 2016-2018 Honda Africa Twins advising them that if they purchased the affected centerstand kit, not to use it and to take the motorcycle to a dealer. Dealers will replace the circlip and washer, free of charge. The recall began October 2, 2018. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KJ5.
