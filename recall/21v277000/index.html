Kia Carnival Fuel Rail May Leak

NHTSA campaign 21V277000 · Reported Apr 21, 2021 · Kia Motors America

critical How serious is it? About this label

A damaged fuel rail tube may leak fuel, increasing the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2022 Kia Carnival vehicles, the plastic fuel rail tube may have been damaged.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Kia dealer or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V277000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) is recalling certain 2022 Carnival vehicles. The plastic fuel rail tube may have been damaged.
Safety risk
A damaged fuel rail tube may leak fuel, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Kia will notify owners, and dealers will inspect, and if necessary, replace the multi-port injection fuel rail, free of charge. The recall began April 30, 2021. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC211.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
