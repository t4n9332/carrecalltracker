Chevrolet Sonic driver's seat back may be weakly welded

NHTSA campaign 18V342000 · Reported 2018-05-27 · General Motors LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Chevrolet Sonic

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2018 Chevrolet Sonic cars, a welded joint in the frame of the driver's seat back was not done properly, which leaves that seat back weaker than it should be. If someone hits your car from behind, the driver's seat back could break instead of holding you in place, and you could be hurt more badly in the crash. A dealer will install a whole new driver's seat back for free.

How serious is it?

This matters mainly in a rear-end crash; the car is otherwise safe to keep driving, but get the seat replaced as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Sonic is covered. 2. Call a Chevrolet dealer, or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 (recall 18178), to book the repair. 3. The dealer replaces the driver's seat back at no charge to you.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2018 Chevrolet Sonic vehicles. A joint in the driver's seat-back frame may not be properly welded, reducing the strength of the seat-back frame.
Safety risk
In the event of a rear-impact crash, the seat-back may fail, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace the driver's seat-back, free of charge. The recall began June 28, 2018. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is 18178.
