NHTSA campaign 18V342000 · Reported May 27, 2018 · General Motors LLC

What's wrong, in plain English

In some of these Chevrolet Sonic vehicles, a joint in the driver's seat-back frame may not have been welded properly. This can leave the seat-back frame weaker than it should be. If the vehicle is hit from behind, the weakened seat-back could fail to hold up the way it's supposed to, which raises the chance that the driver could be hurt in that kind of crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer, or call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020.
3. The driver's seat-back will be replaced free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V342000. Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2018 Chevrolet Sonic vehicles. A joint in the driver's seat-back frame may not be properly welded, reducing the strength of the seat-back frame.
Safety risk
In the event of a rear-impact crash, the seat-back may fail, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace the driver's seat-back, free of charge. The recall began June 28, 2018. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is 18178.
