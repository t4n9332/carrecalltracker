Mazda CX-50 airbag software may deploy airbag wrong

NHTSA campaign 25V413000 · Reported 2025-06-18 · Mazda North American Operations · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Mazda CX-50

What's wrong, in plain English

The computer that controls the airbags was set up with the wrong software settings on certain CX-50 and CX-50 Hybrid vehicles. Because of that, an airbag may not go off the way it should in a crash. A dealer reloads the correct software, which takes care of the problem at no charge. Mazda is not mailing letters for this one because all the affected vehicles are still sitting at dealers rather than with owners.

How serious is it?

An airbag that deploys the wrong way raises the chance of injury in a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm whether your CX-50 is included. 2. Contact your Mazda dealer, or Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500 Option 6 (recall 7625F). 3. Have the airbag control software updated — it is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS: AIR BAG/RESTRAINT CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE
Summary
Mazda North American Operations (Mazda) is recalling certain CX-50 Hybrid and CX-50 vehicles. Incorrectly configured module software may cause the air bag to deploy improperly.
Safety risk
An air bag that deploys improperly increases the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the module software, free of charge. Owner notifications will not be mailed due to all vehicles being under Mazda dealer control. Owners may contact Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500 Option 6. The manufacturer's number for this recall is 7625F.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
