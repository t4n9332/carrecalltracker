Kia K5 side curtain airbags may be installed wrong

NHTSA campaign 23V149000 · Reported 2023-03-07 · Kia America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Kia K5
  • 2022 Kia K5
  • 2023 Kia K5

What's wrong, in plain English

The side curtain airbags, which drop down along the windows to protect your head in a side crash or rollover, may have been put in incorrectly at the factory. Because of that, they may not open the way they are supposed to when a crash happens. You would have no way of knowing there is a problem until it is too late. Kia dealers will inspect the airbags and reinstall them correctly if needed, and the work costs you nothing.

How serious is it?

You would only find out the airbags do not work properly during a crash, so while the vehicle is safe to drive normally, get this checked promptly to protect everyone riding in it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your K5 is included. 2. Schedule the airbag inspection with a Kia dealer, or call Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542 (recall SC263). 3. The inspection and reinstallation are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2021-2023 K5 vehicles. The side curtain air bags may have been installed incorrectly, causing them not to deploy properly in the event of a crash.
Safety risk
Air bags that do not deploy as intended can increase the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and reinstall the side curtain air bags, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 7, 2023. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC263.
