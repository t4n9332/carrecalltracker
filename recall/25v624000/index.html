Genesis GV60 windshield trim can detach onto the road

NHTSA campaign 25V624000 · Reported 2025-09-19 · Hyundai Motor America · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Genesis GV60
  • 2024 Genesis GV60
  • 2025 Genesis GV60

What's wrong, in plain English

The strip of stainless-steel trim along the top edge of the windshield can come loose and fall off the car. The main danger here is not to you but to people behind you, because a piece of metal lying in the road can cause another driver to crash. Dealers will replace the windshield molding at no cost. Owner letters are expected to start going out October 30, 2025, but you can already look your car up.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the car remains safe for you to drive, but a piece of trim coming off at speed becomes a road hazard that could cause a crash for someone else.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — affected VINs became searchable on September 20, 2025.
2. Contact your Genesis dealer, or Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741 (recall 028G), to schedule the work.
3. Have the windshield molding replaced free of charge; you do not have to wait for your letter.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:EXTERIOR TRIM
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2023-2025 Genesis GV60 electric vehicles. The stainless-steel molding at the top of the windshield assembly may detach.
Safety risk
Detached molding can create a road hazard for other vehicles, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the windshield molding, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed beginning October 30, 2025. Owners may contact Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741. Hyundai's number for this recall is 028G. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on September 20, 2025.
