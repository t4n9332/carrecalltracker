What's wrong, in plain English

Hyundai is recalling certain Genesis GV60 electric vehicles. The stainless-steel strip of trim that sits along the top edge of the windshield may come loose and separate from the vehicle. A piece of trim that comes off can land on the road, where it becomes a hazard for other vehicles behind or around the car. Dealers will replace the windshield molding, and owners will be notified by mail.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Genesis dealer to schedule the repair, or call Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741.
3. The replacement molding is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V624000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:EXTERIOR TRIM
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2023-2025 Genesis GV60 electric vehicles. The stainless-steel molding at the top of the windshield assembly may detach.
Safety risk
Detached molding can create a road hazard for other vehicles, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the windshield molding, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed beginning October 30, 2025. Owners may contact Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741. Hyundai's number for this recall is 028G. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on September 20, 2025.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
