Porsche Cayenne Rear Hazard and Parking Lights May Fail

NHTSA campaign 23V527000 · Reported 2023-07-27 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Lights that fail to come on can make the vehicle harder for other drivers to see, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

After a Cayenne is turned off, the rear hazard warning lights and parking lights may fail to come on if they are turned on within 10 seconds after the vehicle's gateway control unit shuts off. Because of this, the vehicle does not meet the federal lighting safety standard for lamps and reflective devices. In this situation, other drivers may not be able to see the hazard or parking lights when they are needed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Porsche dealer, or call Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243, to schedule the repair.
3. The repair (a software update) is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:LIGHTING CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2024 Cayenne, Cayenne S, Cayenne Coupe, Cayenne Turbo GT, and Cayenne S Coupe vehicles. After the vehicle is turned off, the rear hazard warning lights and the parking lights may not illuminate if they are activated within 10 seconds after the gateway control unit shuts off. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Rear hazard warning lights and parking lights that fail to illuminate can reduce the vehicle's visibility to other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the gateway control unit software, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed September 26, 2023. Owners may contact Porsche's customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is APB1.
