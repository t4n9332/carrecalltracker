Ford Bronco Sport, Escape Fuel Injector May Leak

NHTSA campaign 24V187000 · Reported Mar 8, 2024 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak near hot engine parts raises the chance of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In certain Ford Bronco Sport and Escape vehicles with 1.5L engines, a fuel injector inside the engine may develop a crack. If the injector cracks, fuel can leak out of it and collect inside the engine compartment. If that leaking fuel reaches an ignition source, such as a hot engine part or hot exhaust component, it can lead to a fire.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. Dealers will update the engine software and install a drain tube, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V187000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Bronco Sport and 2022 Escape vehicles equipped with 1.5L engines. A fuel injector may crack and leak fuel inside the engine compartment.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source such as hot engine or exhaust components, can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will update the engine control software to include fuel injector leak detection and install a drain tube, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on April 24, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S16. This recall is an expansion of previous recall number 22V-859.
