Dodge Hornet label missing tire and wheel size

NHTSA campaign 24V209000 · Reported 2024-03-19 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) (Stellantis) · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Dodge Hornet

What's wrong, in plain English

The sticker that lists your tire and wheel size is missing that information on some 2024 Dodge Hornet and Alfa Romeo Tonale vehicles. That label is what a tire shop or a mechanic looks at when replacing a wheel or a tire. Without it, someone could install a wheel that is the wrong size for your car. A wheel that does not match can change how the car handles and stops, which could lead to a crash.

How serious is it?

Low day-to-day risk and the notice does not restrict driving, but do not let anyone fit new wheels or tires until the correct label is on the car.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm campaign 24V209000 applies to your vehicle.
2. Contact your Dodge or Alfa Romeo dealer, or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and mention recall number 32B.
3. The dealer applies a corrected label free of charge, which is a quick visit.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2024 Dodge Hornet and Alfa Romeo Tonale vehicles. The manufacturer label is missing the tire rim and tire size information. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
Missing rim and tire size information could lead to an incorrect wheel being installed, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will apply a corrected label, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 4, 2024. Owners may contact FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's number for this recall is 32B.
