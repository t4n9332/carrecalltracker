Mercedes Sprinter Spare Tire Label Shows Wrong Speed Units

NHTSA campaign 20V155000 · Reported 2020-03-18 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

low How serious is it? About this label

Moderate: nothing is mechanically wrong with the van, but misreading the label and speeding on the spare tire could lead to a crash, so it is fine to keep driving while you get the label replaced.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2018-2019 Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans, the label on the spare tire lists the top speed as 55 kilometers per hour instead of 34 miles per hour. A driver who reads that number as 55 mph could drive far faster than the spare tire is built to handle. A dealer will swap in a corrected label that shows the speed in miles per hour. If you are driving on the spare right now, keep your speed at or below 34 mph.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is affected. 2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz or Freightliner Sprinter dealer, or Daimler Vans USA at 1-877-762-8267, and reference recall VS3BEGRESS. 3. Have the corrected label installed at no charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Freightliner Sprinter vehicles. The spare tire label may indicate the maximum speed at 55 kilometers per hour (kph) rather than as 34 miles per hour (mph).
Safety risk
With only the metric speed listed on the spare tire label, the driver could misinterpret the kph as mph and operate the vehicle with the spare tire installed at higher speeds than the tire can withstand, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and Mercedes-Benz or Freightliner Sprinter dealers will replace it with the label with the correct maximum speed information indicated in miles per hour, free of charge. The recall began May 15, 2020. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3BEGRESS.
