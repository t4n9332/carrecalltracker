Toyota Tundra weight label lists the wrong accessory weight

NHTSA campaign 22V854000 · Reported 2022-11-18 · Gulf States Toyota, Inc. · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Toyota Tundra

What's wrong, in plain English

Every truck carries a sticker showing how much weight it can still carry after dealer-added accessories. On some 2023 Toyota Tundras with a sprayed-in bedliner, that sticker understates how much the added accessories actually weigh, so it overstates how much cargo and how many passengers you can still load. Following the number on the sticker could put you over the truck's real limit. Gulf States Toyota is mailing owners a corrected label free, with no dealer visit needed.

How serious is it?

The truck itself is not broken and is safe to drive, but overloading it based on the wrong number would strain the tires and handling, so use conservative loads until the new label arrives.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Tundra falls under recall 22R2.
2. Watch your mail for the free replacement label and apply it over the old one as instructed.
3. If the label never arrives, call Gulf States Toyota at 1-800-444-1074 and ask them to send another at no cost.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Gulf States Toyota, Inc. (GST) is recalling certain 2023 Tundra pickup trucks equipped with non-skid spray-on bed liners. The load carrying capacity modification label may indicate less weight than the actual total weight of accessories installed on the vehicle. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
An incorrect label may lead to unintentionally overloading the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
GST will mail replacement labels to owners, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 10, 2023. Owners may contact GST customer service at 1-800-444-1074. GST's number for this recall is 22R2.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
