Ford Bronco front suspension arm can come apart

NHTSA campaign 25V308000 · Reported 2025-05-09 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity

⚠ NHTSA advises not driving this vehicle until it is repaired. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Ford Bronco

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2024-2025 Broncos, the fasteners holding the lower front suspension arm to the frame may not have been tightened properly. That can let the arm come apart from the frame while you are driving, and if it does you can lose steering and control of the truck. Ford says not to drive these vehicles until they are fixed. A dealer will check the fasteners and replace any that are loose or missing, at no charge.

How serious is it?

Very serious — do not drive the vehicle until it is repaired, because you could lose steering without warning.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Bronco is affected.
2. Stop driving it and arrange transport to the dealer instead of driving it in.
3. Contact your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 25S48), for the free inspection and repair.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:CONTROL ARM:LOWER ARM
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Bronco vehicles. The front lower control arm fasteners may not have been tightened properly, allowing the control arm to detach from the frame.
Safety risk
A detached control arm can cause a loss of vehicle steering and control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until they have been repaired. Dealers will inspect and verify that the lower control arm fasteners are tightened properly. If a fastener is loose or missing, the dealer will replace the fastener. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 3, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S48.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
