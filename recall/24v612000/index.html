MINI Cooper SE High Voltage Battery Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 24V612000 · Reported Aug 15, 2024 · BMW of North America, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

This is serious: owners are told that if a warning message is displayed, they should park the vehicle outside and away from structures until it is repaired, because of the risk of fire.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some MINI Hardtop 2 Door (Cooper SE) vehicles, a fault can occur in the high voltage battery or the high voltage electrical system, which can lead to a short circuit. A short circuit like this raises the risk of a fire.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. If a warning message is displayed, park the vehicle outside and away from structures until the repair is done.
3. Contact a MINI dealer for the free software update; you can also call BMW customer service at 1-866-275-6464.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V612000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY THERMAL:MANAGEMENT:SOFTWARE
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC. (BMW) is recalling certain 2020-2024 MINI Hardtop 2 Door (Cooper SE) vehicles. Faults may occur in the high voltage battery or high voltage system, resulting in a short circuit.
Safety risk
A short circuit increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Owners are advised that if a warning message is displayed in the vehicle, to park the vehicle outside and away from structures until the remedy has been completed. Dealers will provide a software update, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 14, 2024. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-866-ASK-MINI (1-866-275-6464).

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
