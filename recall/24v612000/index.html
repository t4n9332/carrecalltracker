MINI Cooper SE battery fault can cause fire

NHTSA campaign 24V612000 · Reported 2024-08-15 · BMW of North America, LLC · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 MINI Cooper SE
  • 2023 MINI Cooper SE

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2020-2024 MINI Hardtop 2 Door Cooper SE electric cars, a problem can develop in the high-voltage battery or the high-voltage system that powers the car. That problem can cause a short circuit, where electricity takes a wrong path, and a short circuit can start a fire. MINI is providing a software update that helps manage the battery. Until the update is done, watch the dashboard for any warning message.

How serious is it?

This is a fire risk, and if a warning message appears in your car you should park it outside and away from buildings until the free repair is completed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your MINI is covered by campaign 24V612000.
2. If a warning message shows up in the car, park it outside and away from any structure right away.
3. Contact your MINI dealer, or MINI customer service at 1-866-275-6464, to get the software update installed free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY THERMAL:MANAGEMENT:SOFTWARE
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC. (BMW) is recalling certain 2020-2024 MINI Hardtop 2 Door (Cooper SE) vehicles. Faults may occur in the high voltage battery or high voltage system, resulting in a short circuit.
Safety risk
A short circuit increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Owners are advised that if a warning message is displayed in the vehicle, to park the vehicle outside and away from structures until the remedy has been completed. Dealers will provide a software update, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 14, 2024. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-866-ASK-MINI (1-866-275-6464).
