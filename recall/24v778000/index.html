Ford Maverick child seat tether anchors may not hold

NHTSA campaign 24V778000 · Reported 2024-10-18 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Ford Maverick

What's wrong, in plain English

The tether anchors for the second-row child seats on some 2024 Ford Mavericks were not threaded in far enough. A tether is the top strap that keeps a forward-facing child seat from pitching forward in a crash, and it hooks onto one of these anchor points. If an anchor pulls loose because the threads never fully caught, the child seat will not be held the way it should be. Ford dealers repair the anchor points free of charge.

How serious is it?

This raises the risk of injury to a child in a crash rather than causing one, so the vehicle is fine to drive, but get it fixed quickly if you carry a child seat.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Maverick is part of recall 24C33.
2. Schedule the free tether anchor repair with a Ford dealer, or call Ford at 1-866-436-7332.
3. Until the repair is done, ask your dealer or a child seat technician about the safest way to secure the seat in your truck.

Official notice

Component
CHILD SEAT:VEHICLE TETHER ANCHOR
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2024 Maverick vehicles. The second-row child seat tether anchors may not properly secure a child seat, due to incomplete thread engagement. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 225, "Child Restraint Anchorage Systems."
Safety risk
A child seat that is not secured properly increases the risk of injury in the event of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will repair the tether anchorage points, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 4, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24C33.
