Jeep Wagoneer pillar trim can block side curtain airbag

NHTSA campaign 23V545000 · Reported 2023-08-03 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Jeep Grand Wagoneer
  • 2022 Jeep Wagoneer
  • 2023 Jeep Grand Wagoneer
  • 2023 Jeep Wagoneer

What's wrong, in plain English

In these Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer models, the interior trim on the upper part of the post between the front and rear doors may not be pushed fully into place. The side curtain airbag — the long airbag that drops down over the side windows in a crash — comes out from behind that trim. Loose trim can get in its way. If the airbag cannot open the way it should, people inside are less protected. Dealers reseat or replace the trim.

How serious is it?

Serious in a crash, though the vehicle is safe to keep driving day to day; the airbag may not protect occupants fully until the trim is fixed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Jeep dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall 76A), for an appointment.
3. Have the trim inspected and reseated or replaced at no charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:ROOF AND PILLARS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Jeep Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer vehicles. The upper B-pillar interior trim may not be fully seated and could interfere with the side curtain air bag (SABIC) deployment. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 226, "Ejection Mitigation."
Safety risk
A compromised deployment of the SABIC can reduce occupant protection in the event of a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and reseat or replace the trim pieces as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 17, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 76A.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
