Ram diesel trucks: fuel lines may leak and catch fire

NHTSA campaign 19V498000 · Reported 2019-06-27 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 RAM 2500
  • 2019 RAM 3500
  • 2019 RAM 4500
  • 2019 RAM 5500

What's wrong, in plain English

The fuel lines on these diesel trucks may leak fuel into the engine compartment. Diesel pooling or dripping around a hot, running engine is dangerous: if it reaches anything that can ignite it, the truck can catch fire. Dealers will look over the fuel lines and put in new ones if they find a problem. The inspection and any new parts are free of charge.

How serious is it?

A fuel leak near an ignition source can start a fire, so treat this as urgent and get it inspected right away; the notice does not order owners to park the truck.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck falls under campaign 19V498000 (Chrysler's recall V76).
2. Schedule the fuel line inspection with your Ram dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. Let the dealer inspect and replace the fuel lines as needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Ram 2500 and 3500 trucks, and 3500, 4500, and 5500 Cab Chassis vehicles equipped with diesel engines. The fuel lines may leak fuel into the engine compartment.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the fuel lines, replacing them if necessary, free of charge. The recall began July 18, 2019. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V76.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
