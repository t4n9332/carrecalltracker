Toyota Sienna Alloy Wheels May Crack

NHTSA campaign 18V130000 · Reported Feb 22, 2018 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

If a wheel cracks at the spokes, it could separate from the vehicle, causing a loss of vehicle control and increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Sienna vehicles were built with a specific batch of 10-spoke alloy wheels that may not have been properly cooled during manufacturing. These wheels may crack at the spokes if they are hit by an impact while driving.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
Contact a Toyota dealer to have the wheels inspected and replaced if necessary; call Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371 if you have questions.
The inspection and any needed replacement are free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V130000. Official notice

Component
WHEELS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018 Toyota Sienna vehicles built with a specific lot of 10-spoke alloy wheels from one production facility. These wheels may not have been properly cooled during manufacturing. As a result, the wheels may crack in the spokes from an impact while driving.
Safety risk
If the wheels crack at the spokes, the wheel may separate from the hub assembly causing a loss of vehicle control and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Toyota began notifying owners initially by phone on February 26, 2018 and will mail letters to any unnotified owner starting on April 20, 2018. Dealers will inspect the wheels and replace them if necessary depending on their production location, free of charge. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. Toyota's number for this recall is J0J.
