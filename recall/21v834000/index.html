Tesla Model 3 Side Curtain Airbag May Be Twisted

NHTSA campaign 21V834000 · Reported 2021-10-25 · Tesla, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A twisted side curtain airbag may not deploy properly, increasing the risk of injury or of an occupant being ejected from the vehicle during a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Tesla Model 3 vehicles, the left and/or right side curtain airbag may not have been properly secured to the roof rail, which can leave the airbag twisted. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet federal safety standards for side impact protection and for keeping occupants inside the vehicle during a crash. Tesla Service will inspect the airbags and realign them as needed.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
Contact Tesla Service, or call 1-877-798-3752, to have the side curtain airbags inspected.
Any needed realignment is done free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2020-2021, 2023 Model 3 vehicles. The left and/or right side curtain air bag may have been improperly secured to the roof rail, which could result in a twisted air bag. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 214, "Side Impact Protection" and 226, "Ejection Mitigation."
Safety risk
A twisted side curtain air bag may improperly deploy, increasing the risk of injury or occupant ejection during a crash.
Remedy
Tesla Service will inspect and realign the left and right side curtain air bag as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 23, 2021. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-21-20-006.
