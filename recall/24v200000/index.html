What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2022-2024 Kia EV6 vehicles, a part called the Integrated Charging Control Unit can become damaged and stop charging the vehicle's 12-volt battery. When the 12-volt battery isn't being charged properly, the vehicle can lose drive power while it is being driven. Kia is recalling these vehicles to inspect the charging unit and replace it and its fuse if needed, and to update the unit's software.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Kia dealer, or call Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542, to schedule an inspection.
3. Any needed repairs, including the charging unit, fuse, or software update, are free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V200000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2022-2024 EV6 vehicles. The Integrated Charging Control Unit (ICCU) may become damaged and stop charging the 12-Volt battery, which can result in a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the ICCU and its fuse, as necessary. In addition, dealers will update the ICCU software. All repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed April 29, 2024. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC302.
