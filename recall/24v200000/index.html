Kia EV6 charging unit failure can cut drive power

NHTSA campaign 24V200000 · Reported 2024-03-14 · Kia America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Kia EV6
  • 2023 Kia EV6
  • 2024 Kia EV6

What's wrong, in plain English

Your EV6 has a part called the Integrated Charging Control Unit, or ICCU, that keeps the small 12-volt battery charged. On some of these cars that unit can be damaged and stop charging the battery. If the 12-volt battery is no longer being charged, the car can lose drive power. Kia will inspect the unit, replace it and its fuse if needed, and update the unit's software at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

This is serious because a loss of drive power raises the risk of a crash, and while Kia does not tell owners to stop driving, you should get the free repair done as soon as a dealer can take the car.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your EV6 is included in recall 24V200000 (Kia number SC302).
2. Call your Kia dealer for an appointment to have the ICCU inspected, replaced if needed, and its software updated.
3. The repair is free; if you have questions, call Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2022-2024 EV6 vehicles. The Integrated Charging Control Unit (ICCU) may become damaged and stop charging the 12-Volt battery, which can result in a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the ICCU and its fuse, as necessary. In addition, dealers will update the ICCU software. All repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed April 29, 2024. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC302.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
