Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Steering Shaft Screws May Come Loose

NHTSA campaign 20V109000 · Reported 2020-02-25 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 1500
  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500

What's wrong, in plain English

The steering shaft that runs from your steering wheel down to the steering gear is held by a support bearing partway along. On these vans, the screws holding that support may not have been tightened enough at the factory, so they can work loose and fall out. If that happens, the van can become hard to steer. The dealer will fit new screws and tighten them properly. The recall repair started April 13, 2020.

How serious is it?

Difficulty steering can lead to a crash, so while the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, this is a repair worth scheduling right away.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 20V109000 applies to your van.
2. Contact your Sprinter dealer or DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267 (recall number VS3SCHLENK).
3. Have the dealer replace and properly tighten the steering spindle bearing screws — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Freightliner Sprinter vehicles. The screws that secure the intermediate bearing of the steering spindle may not have been properly tightened, potentially causing the screws to loosen and detach.
Safety risk
If the screws detach, the driver may have difficulty steering the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and dealers will replace and properly tighten the steering spindle intermediate bearing screws, free of charge. The recall began April 13, 2020. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3SCHLENK.
