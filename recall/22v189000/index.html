Mercedes-Benz AMG Steering Wheel Hands-Off Detection Fails

NHTSA campaign 22V189000 · Reported Mar 25, 2022 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

If hands-off detection fails, safety warnings and the automatic emergency stop call to first responders may not work, which the notice says increases the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

A software error in the hand detection system may prevent it from noticing when the driver's hands are off the heated steering wheel while using the DISTRONIC distance assist system. When this detection fails, related safety features may not work, including the warning that tells the driver to grab the wheel and the feature that calls first responders during an emergency stop. This increases the risk of a crash or injury.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer or MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372 to schedule the software update.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V189000. Official notice

Component
STEERING: STEERING WHEEL/HANDLE BAR
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2021-2022 C-Class, E-Class, S-Class, SL, EClass Coupe/Convertible, CLS, AMG GT 4-doors Coupe, and EQS vehicles with a heated leather steering wheel. Due to a software error in the hand detection control unit, the hands-off detection might not detect when the driver's hands are away from the steering wheel when using the Active Distance Assist DISTRONIC system.
Safety risk
Deactivated hands-off detection will prevent DISTRONIC system features from functioning, including warnings to grab the steering wheel and calling first responders as part of the Active Emergency Stop Assist, which can increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will update the hand detection control unit software for the DISTRONIC system, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 6, 2022. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2022040010.
