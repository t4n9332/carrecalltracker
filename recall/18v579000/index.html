Toyota Prius Engine Wire Harness Can Wear and Short

NHTSA campaign 18V579000 · Reported Sep 5, 2018 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

critical How serious is it? About this label

An electrical short from this wear can increase the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Toyota is recalling certain Prius vehicles because a section of the engine wire harness that connects to the hybrid Power Control Unit could rub against its cover. Over time, that contact can cause the wire to wear and create an electrical short circuit. Dealers will inspect the wire harness assembly and replace it if any damage is found, and will add a protective sleeve or tape to help prevent this from happening again.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer, or call Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371.
3. The inspection and any needed repair will be done free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V579000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Toyota Prius vehicles. A portion of the engine wire harness connected to the hybrid Power Control Unit (PCU), could contact the cover at this connection and wear, causing an electrical short circuit.
Safety risk
An electrical short can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the engine wire harness assembly and replace it if damage is found. A protective sleeve or protective tape will be included to prevent any future damage. These repairs will be performed free of charge. The recall began October 30, 2018. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. Toyota's number for this recall is J0T.
