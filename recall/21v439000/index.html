Ram 3500, 4500 and 5500 may miss low-oil warning

NHTSA campaign 21V439000 · Reported 2021-06-10 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 RAM 3500
  • 2019 RAM 4500
  • 2019 RAM 5500
  • 2020 RAM 3500
  • 2020 RAM 4500
  • 2020 RAM 5500

What's wrong, in plain English

On these cab chassis trucks with the Cummins 6.7-liter High Output engine, the engine's software may not warn you when the oil level gets too low. Running the engine low on oil can damage it, and that damage can cause an oil leak or even a hole in the engine block. Either one raises the chance of a fire. Dealers will update the engine software at no cost. Owner letters were mailed June 25, 2021.

How serious is it?

Serious — engine damage and an oil leak can lead to a fire, and while the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside, the free update should not be put off.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is covered.
2. Contact your Ram dealer, or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and mention recall Y21.
3. Have the engine software updated — it is free.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:OIL/LUBRICATION
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Ram 3500, 4500, and 5500 cab chassis vehicles equipped with a Cummins 6.7L High Output engine. The engine calibration software may not warn about low oil volume.
Safety risk
Low oil can damage the engine, causing an oil leak or a punctured engine block, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will update the engine calibration software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 25, 2021. Owners may contact FCA US LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US LLC's number for this recall is Y21.
