Ford Expedition: Center Console May Have Sharp Edges

NHTSA campaign 26V368000 · Reported 2026-06-09 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

Contact with the center console's sharp edges increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Ford Expedition SUVs, the chrome plating on the center console may bubble and peel, which can leave sharp edges exposed. Vehicle occupants may come into contact with these sharp edges, increasing the risk of injury. Dealers will inspect the center consoles and replace them as necessary, and this repair will be done at no cost to the owner as part of the recall.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. The repair is free; Ford expects remedy parts to be available in January 2027.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:INTERIOR PANELS:CENTER CONSOLE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2018-2024 Expedition vehicles. The chrome plating on the center console may bubble and peel, resulting in sharp edges.
Safety risk
Vehicle occupants may contact the center console's sharp edges, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the center consoles as necessary, free of charge. Interim letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, were mailed June 16, 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the remedy is available, anticipated in January 2027. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S38. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on June 10, 2026.
