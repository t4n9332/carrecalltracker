Ford Expedition center console chrome can peel into sharp edges

NHTSA campaign 26V368000 · Reported 2026-06-09 · Ford Motor Company

low How serious is it? About this label

The mildest kind of recall — cuts and scrapes rather than a crash or fire risk — and nothing in the notice limits driving the vehicle.

What's wrong, in plain English

The shiny chrome coating on the center console between the front seats can bubble up and flake off. Where it peels, it can leave sharp edges right where an arm, hand or leg rests. Anyone in the vehicle could get cut or scraped by them. Ford has already mailed an early letter warning owners, but the parts for the actual repair are not ready; they are expected around January 2027, when a second letter goes out.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm campaign 26V368000 covers your Expedition.
2. Watch for the second letter once parts arrive, expected January 2027, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 26S38).
3. Have the dealer inspect and, if needed, replace the center console — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:INTERIOR PANELS:CENTER CONSOLE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2018-2024 Expedition vehicles. The chrome plating on the center console may bubble and peel, resulting in sharp edges.
Safety risk
Vehicle occupants may contact the center console's sharp edges, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the center consoles as necessary, free of charge. Interim letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, were mailed June 16, 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the remedy is available, anticipated in January 2027. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S38. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on June 10, 2026.
