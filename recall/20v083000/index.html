BMW C400X Scooter Throttle May Get Stuck

NHTSA campaign 20V083000 · Reported Feb 13, 2020 · BMW of North America, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

A throttle stuck open increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some BMW C400X scooters can let water collect inside the throttle cable. In cold weather, that water can freeze, which can cause the throttle to get stuck in the open position.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your scooter is included in this recall.
2. Contact a BMW dealer, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. The throttle cable replacement is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V083000. Official notice

Component
VEHICLE SPEED CONTROL:CABLES
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2019 C400X scooters. Water may collect in the throttle cable, which can freeze in cold temperatures, resulting in a stuck throttle.
Safety risk
If the throttle sticks open, there would be an increased risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will replace the throttle cable with an improved version, free of charge. The recall began August 19, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
