Ford and GMC based ambulances may show false brake lights

NHTSA campaign 19V342000 · Reported May 3, 2019 · Taylor Made Ambulances

medium How serious is it? About this label

Brake lights that come on when they should not can confuse other drivers, which may raise the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Taylor Made Ambulances is recalling certain ambulances built on Ford E-350, E-450, F-450, F-550 and GMC G3500 chassis that are fitted with Whelen brake lights. Depending on how a particular vehicle is wired, those brake lights can light up even when the driver has not pressed the brake pedal. Taylor Made will notify owners, and Whelen will replace the brake lights.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your ambulance is included.
2. Contact Taylor Made Ambulances customer service at 1-800-468-1310 to arrange for the brake lights to be replaced.
3. The replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V342000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
Taylor Made Ambulances is recalling certain 2017-2018 Ford E-350, F-450, E-450, F-550, and GMC G3500-based ambulances equipped with Whelen brake lights. Depending on the specific wiring of the vehicle, these brake lights may illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed.
Safety risk
If the brake lights incorrectly illuminate, it can confuse other drivers, possibly increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Taylor Made will notify owners and Whelen will replace the brake lights, free of charge. The recall began June 5, 2019. Owners may contact Taylor Made Ambulances customer service at 1-800-468-1310.
