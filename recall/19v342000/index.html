Ford and GMC ambulances: brake lights turn on by themselves

NHTSA campaign 19V342000 · Reported 2019-05-03 · Taylor Made Ambulances · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Ford E-350
  • 2018 Ford E-450
  • 2018 Ford F-450
  • 2018 Ford F-550
  • 2018 GMC G3500

What's wrong, in plain English

The brake lights on these ambulances were wired in a way that can make them light up even when the driver has not touched the brake pedal. Drivers behind may think the ambulance is slowing down when it is not, and may brake hard or swerve for no reason. The lights themselves are not broken; the problem is how they are connected. Whelen will replace the brake lights at no cost to the owner.

How serious is it?

This does not affect how the vehicle drives and it is safe to keep driving, but misleading brake lights can confuse traffic behind you and lead to a crash, so get it fixed soon.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Call Taylor Made Ambulances customer service at 1-800-468-1310 to arrange the brake light replacement. 3. Have the lights replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
Taylor Made Ambulances is recalling certain 2017-2018 Ford E-350, F-450, E-450, F-550, and GMC G3500-based ambulances equipped with Whelen brake lights. Depending on the specific wiring of the vehicle, these brake lights may illuminate when the brake pedal has not been pressed.
Safety risk
If the brake lights incorrectly illuminate, it can confuse other drivers, possibly increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Taylor Made will notify owners and Whelen will replace the brake lights, free of charge. The recall began June 5, 2019. Owners may contact Taylor Made Ambulances customer service at 1-800-468-1310.
