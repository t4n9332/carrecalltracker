Honda Ridgeline Rearview Camera Wiring Can Fail

NHTSA campaign 22V867000 · Reported Nov 23, 2022 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Reduced rear visibility from a non-working camera increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Honda Ridgeline trucks, opening and closing the tailgate can break the wiring harness that connects to the rearview camera. When this wiring breaks, the rearview camera stops working and no longer shows the driver what is behind the truck. Losing this camera view reduces the driver's visibility to the rear, which increases the chance of a crash while driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is included.
2. Contact a Honda dealer, or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V867000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2017-2019 Ridgeline vehicles. Opening and closing the tailgate may break the rearview camera wiring harness, causing the rearview camera to fail.
Safety risk
An inoperative rearview camera can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the rearview camera wiring harness, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 31, 2023. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is 6RW.
