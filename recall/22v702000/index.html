Tesla power windows may not stop on obstructions

NHTSA campaign 22V702000 · Reported 2022-09-19 · Tesla, Inc. · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Tesla Model 3
  • 2019 Tesla Model 3
  • 2020 Tesla Model 3
  • 2020 Tesla Model Y
  • 2021 Tesla Model 3
  • 2021 Tesla Model S
  • 2022 Tesla Model 3

What's wrong, in plain English

Tesla found that the automatic reversal feature on the power windows may not react correctly. Normally, a window that hits your hand or arm while closing should stop and go back down. On these cars it may not respond the right way, so the window can press harder than it should before it reverses. This does not meet the federal safety rule for power windows. Tesla fixes it with a software update sent to the car over the air, so no dealer visit is needed.

How serious is it?

Minor compared with most recalls: a closing window could pinch and injure someone, especially a child, but the car is safe to drive and the fix arrives by itself.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is covered.
2. Keep your car connected to Wi-Fi and accept the over-the-air software update when it appears on the touchscreen — it is free.
3. If the update does not arrive, contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752 and reference SB-22-00-013.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:POWER WINDOW DEVICES AND CONTROLS
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2017-2022 Model 3, 2020-2022 Model Y, and 2021-2022 Model S and Model X vehicles. The window automatic reversal system may not react correctly after detecting an obstruction. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 118, "Power-Operated Window Systems."
Safety risk
A closing window may exert excessive force by pinching a driver or passenger before retracting, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Tesla will perform an over-the-air (OTA) software update of the automatic window reversal system, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 31, 2032. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-22-00-013. (over-the-air software update)
