Tesla power windows may not stop for an obstruction

NHTSA campaign 22V702000 · Reported 2022-09-19 · Tesla, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

A closing window may press on a driver or passenger with too much force before it goes back down, which raises the chance of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Tesla is recalling certain Model 3, Model S, Model X and Model Y vehicles because of the power windows. When a window is closing and something is in the way, the system that is supposed to sense it and stop the window may not respond the way it should. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule for power-operated window systems.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752 if you have questions about the recall.
3. The fix is a free software update to the window reversal system, sent to the car over the air.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:POWER WINDOW DEVICES AND CONTROLS
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2017-2022 Model 3, 2020-2022 Model Y, and 2021-2022 Model S and Model X vehicles. The window automatic reversal system may not react correctly after detecting an obstruction. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 118, "Power-Operated Window Systems."
Safety risk
A closing window may exert excessive force by pinching a driver or passenger before retracting, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Tesla will perform an over-the-air (OTA) software update of the automatic window reversal system, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 31, 2032. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-22-00-013. (over-the-air software update)
