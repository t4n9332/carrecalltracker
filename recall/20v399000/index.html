Kia Sedona loose alternator battery connection can spark

NHTSA campaign 20V399000 · Reported 2020-07-08 · Kia Motors America · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Kia Sedona

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2020 Kia Sedonas, the nut that holds the main positive battery cable onto the alternator may not have been tightened enough at the factory, so it can work loose over time. A loose connection carrying that much electrical current can spark and arc, and that arcing can start a fire in the engine compartment. A Kia dealer will inspect the nut and tighten it properly, free of charge. The repair itself is simple and quick.

How serious is it?

Serious — there is a fire risk, and while the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, you should get the free inspection done quickly and see a dealer immediately if you notice a burning smell or electrical trouble.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Sedona is included in recall 20V399000 (Kia's number SC192).
2. Call your Kia dealer, or Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542, to schedule the alternator terminal inspection.
3. Have the nut inspected and tightened — the work is free, including on used vehicles.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:ALTERNATOR/GENERATOR/REGULATOR
Summary
Kia Motors America (KIA) is recalling certain 2020 Sedona vehicles. The battery positive (B+) terminal nut on the alternator may not have been tightened properly during assembly, possibly causing it to loosen over time.
Safety risk
If the alternator B+ terminal nut loosens, electrical arcing may result, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Kia will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the alternator B+ terminal nut, tightening it as necessary, free of charge. The recall began August 14, 2020. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC192.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
