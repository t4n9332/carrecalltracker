Genesis G90 Seat Belt Pretensioners May Explode

NHTSA campaign 23V210000 · Reported 2023-03-28 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

An exploding seat belt pretensioner can send metal fragments into the vehicle and strike occupants, causing injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Hyundai is recalling certain 2023 Genesis G90 vehicles. In a crash, the seat belt pretensioners in the front and rear seats may explode when they deploy.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
Contact a Hyundai dealer, or call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460.
The repair, which secures the pretensioners with a cap, is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2023 Genesis G90 vehicles. In the event of a crash, the front driver-side, passenger-side, and rear seat belt pretensioners may explode upon deployment.
Safety risk
An exploding seat belt pretensioner can project metal fragments into the vehicle, strike vehicle occupants, and result in injury.
Remedy
Dealers will secure the seat belt pretensioners with a cap, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 17, 2023. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 013G.
