Audi A6 and A7 lose engine power from spills

NHTSA campaign 22V861000 · Reported 2022-11-21 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

What's wrong, in plain English

If someone spills a drink in the back seat of these Audis, the liquid can soak through and reach an electronics box underneath called the gateway control module. Liquid on that module can make it shut down, and when it does the engine suddenly loses power while you are driving — a real problem if you are merging or passing. Dealers will fit a protective cover over the module so spills cannot get to it.

How serious is it?

Serious — the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but a sudden drop in engine power can cause a crash, so get the cover installed promptly and keep drinks out of the back seat until then.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Audi is included.
2. Contact your Audi dealer, or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834, and reference recall 90V2.
3. Have the protective cover installed free of charge; owner letters were expected to be mailed January 20, 2023.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2020-2022 Audi S7, S6 Sedan, A6 Allroad, 2021-2022 RS7, RS6 Avant, 2019-2022 A7, and A6 Sedan vehicles. Liquid spilled in the rear seat may penetrate and cause the gateway control module to shut down.
Safety risk
A gateway control module shutdown will suddenly reduce engine power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install a protective cover, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed January 20, 2023. Owners may contact Audi's customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 90V2.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
