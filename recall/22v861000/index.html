Audi Liquid Spill Can Cut Engine Power

NHTSA campaign 22V861000 · Reported Nov 21, 2022 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A sudden reduction in engine power increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

If liquid is spilled in the back seat, it can leak into a part called the gateway control module and cause it to shut down. When this control module shuts down, it can suddenly reduce how much power the engine is producing while the vehicle is being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact a dealer, or call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. 3. A protective cover will be installed, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V861000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2020-2022 Audi S7, S6 Sedan, A6 Allroad, 2021-2022 RS7, RS6 Avant, 2019-2022 A7, and A6 Sedan vehicles. Liquid spilled in the rear seat may penetrate and cause the gateway control module to shut down.
Safety risk
A gateway control module shutdown will suddenly reduce engine power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will install a protective cover, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed January 20, 2023. Owners may contact Audi's customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 90V2.
