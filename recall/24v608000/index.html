BMW X4, X5 and others: water pump short-circuit fire risk

NHTSA campaign 24V608000 · Reported 2024-08-13 · BMW of North America, LLC · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 BMW X4
  • 2018 BMW X5

What's wrong, in plain English

A wide range of BMW models from 2012 through 2018 are covered. The electrical plug that connects to the water pump, which circulates coolant to keep the engine from overheating, may not be sealed properly. Water can get into that connection and cause an electrical short. An electrical short in that area can start a fire. Dealers will check the pump and its plug, replace them if needed, and add a protective shield, all free.

How serious is it?

This is a fire risk, which is very serious, and while the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle or keep it outside, you should have it inspected as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your BMW is one of the affected models. 2. Contact a BMW dealer to book the free inspection and repair, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417. 3. If you ever smell burning or see smoke, stop, get everyone out, and call for help.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:COOLING SYSTEM:PUMP
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC. (BMW) is recalling certain 2012-2015 X1 sDrive28i, X1 xDrive28i, 2012-2016 Z4 sDrive28i, 528i, 528i xDrive, 328i, 328xi, 2016-2018 X5 xdrive 40e, 2014-2016 228i, 228xi, 428i, 428i xDrive, 328xi Gran Turismo, 2013-2017 X3 sDrive28i, X3 xDrive28i, 2015-2018 X4 xDrive28i, 2015-2016 428xi vehicles. An improperly sealed electrical connector on the water pump may be exposed to water and short circuit.
Safety risk
An electrical short increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the water pump and plug connector as necessary, and install a protective shield, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on March 28, 2025. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
