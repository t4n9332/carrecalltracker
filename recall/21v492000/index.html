Volkswagen Jetta airbags may deploy improperly or unexpectedly

NHTSA campaign 21V492000 · Reported 2021-06-30 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Volkswagen Jetta
  • 2021 Volkswagen Jetta

What's wrong, in plain English

The control unit that decides when the airbags fire was welded into the wrong position at the factory. In a crash, that can make the airbags go off the wrong way, so they may not protect you as designed. The same fault can also set the airbags off when there is no crash at all, which can injure you and increase the risk of a crash. Dealers replace the airbag control unit free of charge.

How serious is it?

This one is serious because the airbags can go off with no warning while you are driving; Volkswagen does not tell owners to park the car, but treat the free repair as urgent.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Jetta is included.
2. Call a Volkswagen dealer or VW customer service at 1-800-893-5298 and reference recall 69CK.
3. Have the dealer replace the airbag control module at no cost.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS: AIR BAG/RESTRAINT CONTROL MODULE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Jetta and Jetta GLI vehicles. The air bag control module was welded in the incorrect position.
Safety risk
Incorrect positioning of the air bag control module can cause the vehicle's air bags to deploy improperly during a crash, increasing the risk of injury. Additionally, the vehicle's air bags can deploy unintentionally, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the air bag control module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on January 28, 2022. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 69CK.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
