Nissan Rogue Throttle Body Gears May Break

NHTSA campaign 26V081000 · Reported Feb 12, 2026 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Nissan says broken throttle body gears can cause a loss of drive power, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

These Rogues are built with the three-cylinder 1.5-liter VC-Turbo engine. The electronic throttle body is the part that controls how much air reaches the engine when you press the accelerator, and inside it are small gears. Nissan says those gears can break. Dealers will reprogram the engine control software and will inspect the electronic throttle body assembly, replacing it if replacement is needed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Nissan dealer to have the engine control software reprogrammed and the throttle body inspected; Nissan customer service is 800-647-7261 (Nissan's recall numbers are R25E2, R25E3 and R25A9).
3. The work is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V081000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:THROTTLEBODY/MANIFOLD
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Rogue vehicles equipped with 3-cylinder 1.5L variable compression turbo (VC-Turbo) engines. The gears in the electronic throttle body assembly may break.
Safety risk
Broken throttle body gears can cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the engine control module (ECM) software and inspect and replace the electronic throttle body assembly as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 8, 2026. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 800-647-7261. Nissan's numbers for this recall are R25E2, R25E3 and R25A9. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning February 27, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
