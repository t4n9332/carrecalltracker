Volkswagen ID.4 Passenger Airbag May Be Folded Wrong

NHTSA campaign 22V858000 · Reported 2022-11-02 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

An air bag that does not protect the occupant as intended in a crash increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Volkswagen is recalling certain ID.4 vehicles because the passenger air bag may have been incorrectly folded. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet federal safety standards that cover occupant protection in a crash. An air bag that is folded incorrectly may not protect the passenger the way it is supposed to during a crash, and this increases the risk of injury to that person.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298, to schedule the repair.
3. The passenger air bag module will be replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2021 ID4 vehicles. The passenger air bag may be incorrectly folded. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An incorrectly folded air bag may not protect the occupant as intended during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Volkswagen will replace the passenger air bag module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 14, 2023. Owners may contact Volkswagen's customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number to this recall is 69EH.
