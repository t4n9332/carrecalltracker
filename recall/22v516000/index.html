Audi Q7 and Q8 fuel pump can fail

NHTSA campaign 22V516000 · Reported 2022-07-20 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Audi Q8
  • 2020 Audi Q7
  • 2020 Audi Q8
  • 2021 Audi Q7
  • 2021 Audi Q8

What's wrong, in plain English

A part inside the fuel pump can break, which can make the pump stop working. The fuel pump is what feeds gasoline to the engine, so if it fails the engine can shut off while you are driving. That can happen without warning, in traffic or at highway speed. This covers certain 2019-2021 Audi Q8 and 2020-2021 Audi Q7 vehicles. Dealers will replace the fuel pump at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Serious, because the engine can stall and raise the risk of a crash; Audi has not told owners to stop driving, but get the repair done promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included.
2. Call your Audi dealer, or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834 (recall 20DR), to book the fuel pump replacement.
3. Have the work done — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019-2021 Audi Q8, and 2020-2021 Audi Q7 vehicles. A component inside the fuel pump may break, causing the fuel pump to fail.
Safety risk
Fuel pump failure can cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 22, 2022. Owners may contact Audi's customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 20DR.
