Toyota Prius C DC-DC Converter May Cause Drive Loss

NHTSA campaign 19V491000 · Reported Jun 26, 2019 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

medium How serious is it? About this label

A loss of drive increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Toyota is recalling certain Prius C vehicles because the DC-DC converter may fail. If it fails, the vehicle's smaller battery stops getting power from the converter, which can cause the car to lose the ability to drive. This means the vehicle could unexpectedly stop moving while you are driving it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer to schedule the repair (Toyota customer service: 1-888-270-9371).
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V491000. Official notice

Component
HYBRID PROPULSION SYSTEM
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Prius C vehicles. The DC-DC converter may fail resulting in the auxiliary battery no longer receiving power from the converter, which may cause a loss of drive.
Safety risk
A loss of drive increases the risk of crash.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners, and dealers will replace the DC-DC converter with a new one, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began August 23, 2019. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371. Toyota's number for this recall is K0K.
