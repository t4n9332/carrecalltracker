Audi SQ7 Fuel Pump May Cause Leak

NHTSA campaign 25V637000 · Reported Sep 24, 2025 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak near a source of ignition increases the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

An Audi SQ7 vehicle has a fuel system problem. The bolts holding the high-pressure fuel pump in place may not be tightened correctly, which can cause a fuel leak.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact Audi or Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298.
3. The inspection and repair are free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V637000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling one Audi SQ7 vehicles. The fasteners securing the high pressure fuel pump may not be tightened properly, resulting in a fuel leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and tighten the screw connections and replace the high pressure fuel pump and fuel lines as necessary, free of charge. The one affected vehicle was repaired. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 23HD. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning September 26, 2025.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
