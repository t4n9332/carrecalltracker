NHTSA campaign 19V593000 · Reported Aug 14, 2019 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A sticking or dragging brake caliper can affect how well the brakes perform, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2019 BMW C 400 X scooters, 2018 to 2020 G 310 GS, and 2017 to 2020 G 310 R motorcycles may have front and rear brake caliper pistons that corrode. This corrosion can cause a piston to stick or drag inside the caliper bore, instead of moving smoothly as intended. When this happens, the affected brake may not engage or release the way it normally would.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact your BMW dealer to schedule the caliper replacement (BMW customer service: 1-800-525-7417).
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V593000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:DISC:CALIPER
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2019 C 400 X, 2018-2020 G 310 GS, and 2017-2020 G 310 R scooters and motorcycles. The front and rear brake caliper pistons may corrode, possibly causing the piston to stick or drag in the caliper bore.
Safety risk
A sticking or dragging brake could affect braking performance, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the front and rear brake calipers, free of charge. The recall began March 13, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
