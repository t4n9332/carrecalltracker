Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator manual clutch can overheat

NHTSA campaign 21V028000 · Reported 2021-01-28 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Jeep Wrangler
  • 2019 Jeep Wrangler
  • 2020 Jeep Gladiator
  • 2020 Jeep Wrangler
  • 2021 Jeep Gladiator
  • 2021 Jeep Wrangler

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator models with a manual transmission can overheat the clutch. The pressure plate, the part that squeezes the clutch together when you let the pedal out, may get hot enough to crack and break apart. Broken pieces can damage nearby parts, drop debris onto the road, or leave the vehicle unable to move under its own power. The overheated clutch parts can also start a fire. Dealers add software that limits engine power when the clutch gets too hot.

How serious is it?

This is serious because an overheated clutch can catch fire or leave you without drive power, and while the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, dealers were told to stop selling these vehicles until they are fixed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Wrangler or Gladiator falls under recall 21V028000.
2. Contact your Jeep dealer to schedule the free software update for the clutch.
3. Until it's done, avoid slipping the clutch for long stretches, and stop and have it checked if you smell burning.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:CLUTCH ASSEMBLY
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2018-2021 Jeep Wrangler and 2020-2021 Jeep Gladiator vehicles equipped with manual transmissions. The clutch pressure plate may overheat and fracture.
Safety risk
Overheated clutch components may increase the risk of a fire. Additionally, damage to other nearby components can result in debris falling onto the road or a loss of drive, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
FCA US LLC will notify owners, and dealers will add software to reduce engine torque capability when clutch assembly temperatures rise to a level that may damage the inner pressure plate, free of charge." FCA has ordered a stop sale for the affected vehicles as of February 25, 2021. Owner notification letters were mailed on March 4, 2021.
