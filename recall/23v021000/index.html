Ford Bronco Sport Brake Module May Leak

NHTSA campaign 23V021000 · Reported Jan 23, 2023 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

A leaking brake module can cause unexpected brake pedal travel or unexpected vehicle movement, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Ford Bronco Sport vehicles have an Anti-lock Brake System module that may have been damaged during manufacturing. Valves inside the module can be damaged, causing the module to leak internally. This leak can affect how the brakes respond, leading to the brake pedal traveling further than expected or the vehicle moving unexpectedly during an Auto Hold event. This means the brakes may not respond the way the driver expects.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact your Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 to schedule the free repair.
3. Dealers will replace the ABS module at no cost.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V021000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:ANTILOCK/TRACTION CONTROL/ELECTRONIC LIMITED SLIP:CONTROL UNIT/MODULE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023 Bronco Sport vehicles. Valves inside the Anti-lock Brake System (ABS) module may have been damaged during manufacturing, causing an internal ABS module leak.
Safety risk
A leak inside the ABS module can cause an unexpected increase in brake pedal travel or unexpected movement during an Auto Hold event, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the ABS Module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 2, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23S01.
