Chevrolet Bolt EV passenger airbag may not open properly

NHTSA campaign 23V567000 · Reported 2023-08-10 · General Motors, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Chevrolet Bolt EV
  • 2023 Chevrolet Bolt EV

What's wrong, in plain English

The dashboard has a line of small holes on its underside that lets the passenger airbag break through and open in a crash. On these vehicles those holes may be missing, so the passenger airbag may not open the way it should. Dealers will replace the entire dashboard at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

This is serious because a passenger airbag that does not open properly leaves the front passenger more likely to be hurt in a crash, though GM has not told owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your Bolt EV is affected. 2. Contact your Chevrolet dealer to book the repair, or call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 and reference recall N232414710. 3. Have the instrument panel replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Chevrolet Bolt EV vehicles. The instrument panel may be missing the perforation on the underside of the panel cover that allows the passenger-side air bag to deploy properly.
Safety risk
An air bag that does not deploy properly in a crash increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the instrument panel, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 21, 2023. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N232414710.
