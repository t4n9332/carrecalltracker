Chevrolet Traverse wheelchair vans can leak transmission fluid

NHTSA campaign 21V817000 · Reported 2021-10-18 · Braun Corporation · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Chevrolet Traverse
  • 2021 Chevrolet Traverse

What's wrong, in plain English

A screw and clip that hold a plastic cover inside the wheel well can rub against the transmission, the part that sends engine power to the wheels. That rubbing can wear through and let transmission fluid leak out. If enough fluid leaks, the vehicle can lose the power to move. Leaking fluid can also catch fire if it reaches something hot. This only affects Traverse vehicles converted by Braun with a power in-floor wheelchair ramp.

How serious is it?

Serious — a leak can leave you without drive power and could start a fire, so get it looked at right away, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Call Braun customer service at 1-800-488-0359 or your dealer to book the repair.
3. The dealer replaces the screw and clip and checks the transmission for damage, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
Braun Corporation (Braun) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Chevrolet Traverse wheelchair accessible vehicles with Power In-floor Ramp conversion. The screw and clip that hold the inner wheel-well plastic shroud in place may chafe against the transmission case, possibly resulting in a transmission fluid leak.
Safety risk
A transmission fluid leak can cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash. In addition, a leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the screw and clip, and inspect for signs of contact with the transmission, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 29, 2021. Owners may contact Braun customer service at 1-800-488-0359.
