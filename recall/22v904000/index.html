Ram pickup tailgate may open while you drive

NHTSA campaign 22V904000 · Reported 2022-12-08 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · medium severity what's this?

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 pickups, the catch that the tailgate latches onto may be out of position. As a result the tailgate may not lock shut properly and can swing open while you are driving. Anything in the bed that is not tied down can then slide or fall out onto the road behind you, where it becomes a hazard for other drivers. The dealer adjusts it free.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious — the truck is safe to drive, but loose cargo falling onto the road can cause a crash, so avoid hauling unsecured items in the bed until it is repaired.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Call your Ram dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall ZB8), for the free tailgate inspection and adjustment.
3. Until then, strap down anything you carry in the bed and check that the tailgate is fully latched before each trip.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:TAILGATE:HINGE AND ATTACHMENTS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2019-2022 Ram 1500, 2500, and 3500 pickup trucks. The tailgate may not latch properly due to misaligned tailgate strikers, which could result in the tailgate opening while driving.
Safety risk
An unintended tailgate opening while driving may result in a loss of unsecured cargo, possibly creating a road hazard and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the tailgate striker alignment to the box latch and adjust, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 22, 2022. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is ZB8.
