Audi rear axle lock nut may break

NHTSA campaign 21V295000 · Reported Apr 26, 2021 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A broken lock nut can throw the rear axle out of alignment, which can cause a loss of control and raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Audi models, the lock nut on a rear axle trailing arm may break because of corrosion. If this happens, the rear axle can become misaligned, affecting how the vehicle handles.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact an Audi dealer to schedule the free repair; you can also call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834.
3. The lock nut, and bolts if needed, will be replaced at no cost.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V295000. Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR:AXLE:NON-POWERED AXLE ASSEMBLY
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2021 Q7, Q8, Q5 Sportback, SQ5 Sportback, RS6 Avant, RS7, RS Q8, 2020-2021 Audi S6 sedan, Q5, A8, S8, A7, A6 Allroad, A5 Cabriolet, A4 sedan, A4 Allroad, SQ5, S7, A6 sedan, S5 coupe, S4 sedan, S5 Cabriolet, A5 coupe, 2019-2021 RS5 coupe, A5 Sportback, S5 Sportback, RS5 Sportback vehicles. The lock nut on the trailing arm of the rear axle may break due to stress corrosion.
Safety risk
A broken lock nut may misalign the rear axle, causing a loss of control and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the lock nuts and, if necessary, the associated bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 4, 2021. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 42L1.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
