Toyota 4Runner accessory grille trim can fall off

NHTSA campaign 17V763000 · Reported 2017-11-29 · Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Toyota 4runner

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 4Runners were fitted with a decorative billet grille overlay — a trim piece added over the front grille — by Southeast Toyota or its dealers. A small nut that holds this trim in place can crack. If it breaks, the grille overlay can come loose and fall off the front of the vehicle, possibly while you are driving. Dealers will install upgraded fasteners so the trim stays put.

How serious is it?

A piece falling into the road can cause a crash for you or the driver behind you, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so it is generally fine to keep driving while you schedule the free repair.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your 4Runner is included.
2. Call your Toyota dealer or Southeast Toyota customer service at 1-866-405-4226 (recall SET17C) and book an appointment.
3. Have the dealer replace the grille fasteners — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:HOOD
Summary
Southeast Toyota Distributors, LLC (SET) is recalling certain 2015-2018 Toyota 4Runner vehicles equipped with an accessory Billet Grille Overlay installed by SET or provided by SET and installed by SET dealers. The U-nut that secures the overlay grille may fracture, allowing the Billet Grille Overlay to detach from the vehicle.
Safety risk
If the Billet Grille Overlay detaches while driving, it can become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
SET will notify owners, and dealers will replace the securing U-nuts with upgraded hardware, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin January 26, 2018. Owners may contact SET customer service at 1-866-405-4226. SET's number for this recall is SET17C.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
