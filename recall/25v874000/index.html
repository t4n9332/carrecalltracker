Kia Sportage Instrument Panel Screen May Fail

NHTSA campaign 25V874000 · Reported Dec 17, 2025 · Kia America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A screen that fails to show the speedometer or warning lights can increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Kia America is recalling certain Sportage and Sportage PHEV vehicles equipped with a 4.2-inch instrument screen and a genuine Kia tow hitch wiring harness accessory. In these vehicles, a software error can cause the instrument panel screen to stop working properly. When that happens, the driver may not be able to see important safety information on the screen, such as the speedometer or warning lights.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Kia dealer at 1-800-333-4542.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V874000. Official notice

Component
TRAILER HITCHES
Summary
Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2023-2025 Sportage Plug-In Hybrid Electric (PHEV) and 2024-2025 Sportage vehicles equipped with a 4.2" screen and a Genuine Kia tow hitch harness accessory. Due to a software error, the instrument panel screen may fail. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 101, "Controls and Displays."
Safety risk
An instrument panel screen that fails to display critical safety information, such as the speedometer or warning lights, can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the tow hitch harness control module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 13, 2026. Owners may contact Kia Customer Service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC359. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning December 31, 2025.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
