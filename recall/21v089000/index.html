BMW X5 right front brake disc may loosen

NHTSA campaign 21V089000 · Reported 2021-02-18 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 BMW X5

What's wrong, in plain English

The right front brake disc on some 2021 X5 sDrive40i and X5 xDrive40i vehicles may have been put together with the wrong fasteners. Over time those fasteners could let the brake disc work loose, which can change how the vehicle handles and steers. Dealers will check the right front disc, replace it if needed, and replace the left side disc too, at no charge. BMW says no owner vehicles are affected, so owner letters are not being sent.

How serious is it?

A loose brake disc is a serious handling and control problem, though BMW reports that no customer-owned vehicles are involved in this recall.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether campaign 21V089000 applies to your X5.
2. If it does, call your BMW dealer or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 to arrange the inspection.
3. Have the brake discs inspected and replaced as needed — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2021 X5 sDrive40i and X5 xDrive40i vehicles. The right front brake disc may have been assembled with incorrect fasteners, which over time, could result in the front brake disc becoming loose.
Safety risk
A loose brake disc could affect vehicle handling and control, and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the right front brake disc and replace it if necessary, along with the unaffected left side brake disc, free of charge. There are no owner vehicles affected; therefore, owner notification is not necessary and, as such, a planned owner notification date is not entered into the recall schedule. Owners may contact BMW's customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
