BMW i4 and i5 battery may overheat, fire risk

NHTSA campaign 24V135000 · Reported 2024-02-23 · BMW of North America, LLC · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 BMW I5

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2024 BMW i4 xDrive40, i4 M50 and i5 eDrive40 cars were built with a large battery pack whose internal parts may not have been welded together well enough. Weak welds can let that section of the battery run too hot, and an overheating battery raises the chance of a fire. The repair is to remove the affected high-voltage battery module and install a new one. Dealers do all of this at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

This is a fire risk, so it should be handled quickly, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving or to park outside.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included in campaign 24V135000.
2. Call your BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, and schedule the battery module replacement.
3. Have the work done; the replacement is free, including parts and labor.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2024 i4 xDrive40, i4 M50 and i5 eDrive40 vehicles. The high-voltage battery module may contain insufficient weld seams, which can result in the battery overheating.
Safety risk
An overheated battery module increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the high-voltage battery module, free of charge. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
