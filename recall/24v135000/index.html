BMW i5 High-Voltage Battery May Overheat

NHTSA campaign 24V135000 · Reported Feb 23, 2024 · BMW of North America, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

An overheated battery module raises the risk that a fire could start in the vehicle. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The high-voltage battery module in these vehicles may not have enough weld seams, or the welds inside it may be incomplete. This is a manufacturing issue with the battery itself. Because of the weak welds, the battery module can overheat. An overheated high-voltage battery raises the risk that a fire could start in the vehicle. The battery module will need to be inspected and replaced to fix this problem.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a BMW dealer or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 to schedule the repair.
3. The battery module replacement is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V135000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2024 i4 xDrive40, i4 M50 and i5 eDrive40 vehicles. The high-voltage battery module may contain insufficient weld seams, which can result in the battery overheating.
Safety risk
An overheated battery module increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the high-voltage battery module, free of charge. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
