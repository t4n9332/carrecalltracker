NHTSA campaign 23V791000 · Reported Nov 22, 2023 · Nissan North America, Inc.

What's wrong, in plain English

Nissan says the left and/or right rear brake caliper assemblies on some Rogue vehicles may be missing an internal bushing. This can allow the O-ring seal to move and brake fluid to leak out. Nissan is recalling the affected vehicles so dealers can replace the left and right rear brake calipers. Two vehicles previously inspected under an earlier recall that did not get a caliper replacement will need the new repair completed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Nissan dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V791000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:DISC:CALIPER
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2021 Rogue vehicles. The left and/or right rear brake caliper assemblies may be missing an internal bushing, allowing the O-ring seal to move and brake fluid to leak.
Safety risk
A loss of brake fluid may reduce braking performance, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the left and right rear brake calipers, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 12, 2024. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's number for this recall is PC933. The two vehicles that were previously inspected under recall 21V-286 and did not receive a brake caliper replacement will need to have the new remedy completed.
