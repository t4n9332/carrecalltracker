Tesla Cybertruck accelerator pedal can stick down

NHTSA campaign 24V276000 · Reported 2024-04-17 · Tesla, Inc. · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Tesla Cybertruck

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2024 Tesla Cybertrucks, the pad on the face of the accelerator pedal can slide off its mounting. If it comes loose, it can slip up and get caught on the interior trim above the pedal, holding the pedal down. The truck can then speed up on its own even after you take your foot off the pedal. Tesla will repair or replace the accelerator pedal assembly at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Very serious — the truck can accelerate without you asking it to, which can cause a crash, though the notice does not tell owners to park the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Cybertruck is affected. 2. Contact Tesla service or Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752 (recall SB-24-33-003) to arrange the repair. 3. Have the accelerator pedal repaired or replaced — there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
VEHICLE SPEED CONTROL:ACCELERATOR PEDAL
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2024 Cybertruck vehicles. The accelerator pedal pad may dislodge and cause the pedal to become trapped by the interior trim.
Safety risk
A trapped accelerator pedal can cause the vehicle to accelerate unintentionally, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla service will replace or repair the accelerator pedal assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 18, 2024. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-24-33-003.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
