Tesla Cybertruck Accelerator Pedal Can Get Trapped

NHTSA campaign 24V276000 · Reported Apr 17, 2024 · Tesla, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A trapped accelerator pedal can cause unintended acceleration, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The pad on the accelerator pedal may come loose and get caught by the interior trim. If that happens, the pedal can become trapped, which can cause the vehicle to accelerate without the driver intending it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Tesla service center, or call Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V276000. Official notice

Component
VEHICLE SPEED CONTROL:ACCELERATOR PEDAL
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2024 Cybertruck vehicles. The accelerator pedal pad may dislodge and cause the pedal to become trapped by the interior trim.
Safety risk
A trapped accelerator pedal can cause the vehicle to accelerate unintentionally, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla service will replace or repair the accelerator pedal assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 18, 2024. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-24-33-003.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
