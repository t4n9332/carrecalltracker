Volvo VNL Trucks: Side Airbag May Not Deploy

NHTSA campaign 23V403000 · Reported 2023-06-04 · Volvo Trucks North America

high How serious is it? About this label

An airbag that fails to deploy in a crash increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Volvo Trucks is recalling certain VNL and VHD trucks that are equipped with RollTek driver seats. On these trucks, a circuit needed for the side airbag is missing. Without this circuit in place, the side airbag will not deploy the way it is supposed to if the truck is involved in a crash. This means the airbag will not provide protection when needed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Volvo Trucks dealer to have a jumper harness connected between the seat and cab harness; you can also call Volvo Trucks at 1-800-528-6586.
3. The repair is free.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2023 Volvo VNL

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Truck) is recalling certain 2022-2024 VNL and VHD vehicles equipped with RollTek driver seats. A missing circuit will prevent the side air bag from deploying during a crash.
Safety risk
An air bag that does not deploy, increases the risk of an injury.
Remedy
Dealers will connect a jumper harness between the seat and main cab harness, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 4, 2023. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 1-800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2305.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
