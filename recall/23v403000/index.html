Volvo VNL and VHD trucks have non-working side airbag

NHTSA campaign 23V403000 · Reported 2023-06-04 · Volvo Trucks North America · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Volvo VNL
  • 2023 Volvo VNL

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2022-2024 Volvo VNL and VHD trucks built with RollTek driver seats are missing an electrical connection. Because that circuit is missing, the side airbag in the driver seat will not go off in a crash. The airbag is simply not connected, so the protection you expect in a side impact is not there. A dealer adds a jumper harness between the seat and the truck's main wiring to complete the circuit.

How serious is it?

The truck drives normally, but the driver's side airbag will not deploy in a crash, which raises the chance of injury, so get it connected as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is covered.
2. Contact a Volvo Trucks dealer, or Volvo customer service at 1-800-528-6586, and reference recall RVXX2305.
3. Have the jumper harness installed — there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Truck) is recalling certain 2022-2024 VNL and VHD vehicles equipped with RollTek driver seats. A missing circuit will prevent the side air bag from deploying during a crash.
Safety risk
An air bag that does not deploy, increases the risk of an injury.
Remedy
Dealers will connect a jumper harness between the seat and main cab harness, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 4, 2023. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 1-800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2305.
