Toyota Prius Software Error May Cause Power Loss

NHTSA campaign 21V734000 · Reported Sep 22, 2021 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing power while driving increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Toyota Prius and Prius Prime vehicles, the computer that controls the engine has software that can mistakenly detect an error, even when nothing is actually wrong. When that happens, the computer can shut down the hybrid system. This causes the car to lose the power that normally drives the wheels, meaning the car can no longer move forward under its own power the way it normally would.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm it's included.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer, or call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331.
3. The software update is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V734000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECU/ECM):SOFTWARE
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2022 Prius and Prius Prime vehicles. The Engine Control Unit (ECU) software may falsely detect an error and shut down the hybrid system, causing a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the ECU software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 18, 2021. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 21TB07/21TA07.
