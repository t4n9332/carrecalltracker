Mercedes GLC Roof Pillar May Not Absorb Crash Impact

NHTSA campaign 25V379000 · Reported Jun 6, 2025 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

If a back-seat passenger's head hits that pillar in a crash, the reduced impact protection can increase the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Mercedes-Benz GLC models built with a panoramic sunroof have a problem with a roof pillar next to the back seat, called the C-pillar. In certain types of crashes, a rear passenger's head can strike that pillar, and the pillar may not soak up enough of the impact. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for protection inside the vehicle. Dealers will change the roof lining and the C-pillar trim free of charge.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is covered.
2. Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer, or call MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372, to schedule the work.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V379000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:ROOF AND PILLARS
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2023-2025 GLC 300, GLC 300 4MATIC, 2024-2025 AMG GLC 43 4MATIC, 2025 GLC 350E 4MATIC, and AMG GLC 63 S E vehicles equipped with a panoramic sunroof. In certain crash scenarios, where the rear occupant's head contacts the C-pillar, the pillar may not absorb an adequate amount of the impact. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 201, "Occupant Protection in Interior Impact."
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, insufficient impact protection can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will modify the roof lining and C-pillar trim, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 9, 2025. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372.
