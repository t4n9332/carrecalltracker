Porsche Taycan battery short circuit can cause fire

NHTSA campaign 24V732000 · Reported 2024-10-01 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc. · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Porsche Taycan
  • 2021 Porsche Taycan
  • 2022 Porsche Taycan
  • 2023 Porsche Taycan
  • 2024 Porsche Taycan

What's wrong, in plain English

The large battery that powers your Taycan can short-circuit inside one of its sections, and a short in a battery this big can start a fire. Porsche is watching data that cars send in online and will contact owners whose battery looks suspect, telling them to charge only to 80 percent until it is replaced. Dealers install new monitoring software as the permanent fix. If your car does not send data, the dealer will test it and replace the battery section if needed.

How serious is it?

Very serious — this is a fire risk; the notice does not tell owners to park outside or stop driving, but follow Porsche's advice to charge only to 80 percent and get your car checked right away.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is covered (owner letters went out August 6, 2025). 2. Call your Porsche dealer or Porsche at 1-800-767-7243 and ask about recalls ARB6 and ARB7. 3. Charge only to 80 percent until the work is done — the software, testing, and any battery replacement are free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2021-2024 Taycan vehicles. The high-voltage battery may experience a short circuit within the battery module.
Safety risk
A short circuit in a high-voltage battery increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will install advanced diagnostic software as the final remedy, anticipated to be available in the first quarter of 2025. Porsche will monitor available online vehicle data and contact owners as necessary, to advise them to only charge the vehicle to 80% capacity until the battery can be replaced. For vehicles in which online data is unavailable, dealers will perform a diagnostic test and replace the battery module assembly, if necessary and advise owners to only charge to 80% capacity. Repairs will be performed free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 6, 2025. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's numbers for this recall are ARB6 and ARB7. This recall expands and replaces previous recall numbers 23V-840, 24V-215, and 24V-217.
