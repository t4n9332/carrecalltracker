Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain Rear Seat Weld Problem

NHTSA campaign 19V184000 · Reported Mar 7, 2019 · General Motors LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

In a crash, the weak welds can increase the risk of injury for whoever is sitting in the rear right seat. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain vehicles may have a problem with the rear right seat. The bracket that holds the head restraint, the padded part at the top of the seat back, may not have enough welds holding it in place. If the vehicle is in a crash, the person sitting in that rear right seat may be more likely to be hurt. Dealers will replace the frame of that seat back free of charge.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is covered.
2. Contact your dealer, or call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782, to book the seat repair.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V184000. Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain vehicles. The rear-right seat may have insufficient welds on the head-restraint bracket.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, the insufficient welds may result in an increased risk of injury for an occupant seated in the rear-right position.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will replace the rear-right seatback frame, free of charge. The recall began April 15, 2019. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N192209550.
