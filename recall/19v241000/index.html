What's wrong, in plain English

Volkswagen is recalling certain 2019 Tiguan LWB vehicles because the tire information label lists the wrong tire dimensions and inflation pressure. Using these incorrect specifications could lead to a tire being improperly inflated or a wrong-sized tire being installed on the vehicle, either of which can increase the risk of a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact your Volkswagen dealer, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298.
3. The corrected label will be installed free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V241000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2019 Tiguan LWB vehicles. The tire information label contains inaccurate tire dimension and inflation pressure specifications. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
The incorrect specifications can result in improper tire inflations or to installing a tire of the wrong size on the vehicle. Either of these situations can increase the risk of a vehicle crash.
Remedy
Volkswagen has notified owners, and dealers will install a corrected tire information label, free of charge. The recall began April 16, 2019. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 01D2.
