Volkswagen Taos fuel line connector can leak gas

NHTSA campaign 21V651000 · Reported 2021-08-20 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Volkswagen Taos

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2022 Volkswagen Taos, the quick-connect fitting that joins the fuel line under the hood can pop off. If that happens, gasoline can leak out into the engine compartment. The engine may stall while you are driving, and leaking fuel near anything hot or sparking can catch fire. Dealers will look at the connection and, if needed, put in a new fuel line and connector at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

This is serious because a leak can both stall the engine and start a fire, and while Volkswagen has not told owners to stop driving, you should get the inspection done as soon as your dealer can take the car.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Taos is included in recall 20DB.
2. Call your Volkswagen dealer to book the inspection, or contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298.
3. Have the inspection and any fuel line and connector replacement done — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2022 Taos vehicles. The underhood fuel supply quick-connector may detach from the fuel supply line causing a fuel leak in the engine compartment.
Safety risk
A fuel leak may cause a stall, increasing the risk of a crash. A fuel leak also increases the risk of fire in the presence of an ignition source.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the connection and replace the fuel supply line and connector as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 31, 2021. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 20DB.
