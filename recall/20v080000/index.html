Ram 1500 4x4 Transfer Case May Cause Power Loss

NHTSA campaign 20V080000 · Reported 2020-02-13 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A sudden loss of power or a loss of 'Park' function increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some four-wheel-drive Ram 1500 pickup trucks have a transfer case gear that may not have been installed correctly. Because of this, the gear problem can cause the truck to become stuck in neutral. If this happens while the truck is being driven, the vehicle could suddenly lose power. In addition, the truck may lose the ability to stay in 'Park,' meaning it might not stay parked as expected.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is included in this recall.
2. Contact a Ram dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The transfer case replacement is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:TRANSFER CASE (4-WHEEL DRIVE)
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Ram 1500 pickup trucks equipped with four-wheel drive. A gear within the transfer case may not have been installed correctly, possibly causing the vehicle to become stuck in neutral. If this occurs while driving, there could be a sudden loss of power. Additionally, a loss of 'PARK' function may occur.
Safety risk
A sudden loss of power or a loss of 'PARK' function may increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the transfer case, free of charge. The recall began March 10, 2020. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is W06.
