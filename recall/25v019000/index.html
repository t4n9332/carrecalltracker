Ford Bronco Sport, Maverick: 12-Volt Battery May Fail

NHTSA campaign 25V019000 · Reported Jan 17, 2025 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

A sudden battery failure can cause a loss of electrical accessories or a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The 12-volt battery in these vehicles can degrade over time and suddenly stop working. If that happens, the vehicle could lose electrical accessories like the hazard lights, or lose the power needed to keep driving. Dealers will inspect the battery and replace it if needed, at no charge. Vehicles already repaired under an earlier recall for this issue will need this new remedy completed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact your dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. 3. The battery inspection, and replacement if needed, is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V019000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Bronco Sport and 2022-2023 Maverick vehicles. The 12-volt battery may experience degradation and suddenly fail.
Safety risk
Battery failure can result in a loss of electrical accessories, including the hazard lights, or cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and if necessary, replace the 12-volt battery, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 2, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S02. Vehicles in this recall were previously repaired under 24V-267 and will need to have the new remedy completed.
