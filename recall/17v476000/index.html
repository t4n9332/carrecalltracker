Infiniti Q50 Software Flaw Can Stall Engine

NHTSA campaign 17V476000 · Reported Jul 27, 2017 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

An engine that stalls while driving raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

These vehicles have a computer that controls the fuel pump, and it may have incorrect software installed. Because of this software error, the system may not correctly track how much fuel remains in the main tank, and it can let the fuel level get too low to keep supplying fuel to the engine. When that happens, the engine can stall while you are driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included. 2. Contact an Infiniti dealer, or call Infiniti customer service at 1-800-662-6200. 3. The software update is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 17V476000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Infiniti Q50 and 2017 Infiniti Q60 vehicles equipped with a 2.0L engine. Incorrect Fuel Pump Control Module (FPCM) software may allow the fuel level in the main tank to be too low to supply fuel to the engine, causing the engine to stall while driving.
Safety risk
An engine stall can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and Infiniti dealers will reprogram the Fuel Pump Control Module (FPCM) with corrected software, free of charge. The recall began August 29, 2017. Owners may contact Infiniti customer service at 1-800-662-6200.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
