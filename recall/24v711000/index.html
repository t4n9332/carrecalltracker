Mini Countryman Front Structure Bolts Loose

NHTSA campaign 24V711000 · Reported Sep 25, 2024 · BMW of North America, LLC

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling certain 228i, 228i xDrive, and MINI Countryman S ALL4 vehicles because the bolts that connect the front body structure to the vehicle's chassis may not have been tightened properly. If these bolts are not tight enough, the connection between the front body structure and the chassis may be looser than it is supposed to be, rather than fully secure as intended for these vehicles' front structure.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included in this recall.
2. Contact a BMW dealer, or call BMW Customer Service at 1-800-525-7417, to have the front structure bolts inspected.
3. Dealers will tighten the bolts if needed, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V711000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2023-2024 228i, 228i xDrive, and 2025 MINI Countryman S ALL4 vehicles. The bolts that connect the front body structure to the vehicle chassis may not have been tightened properly.
Safety risk
A loose front body structure may not adequately protect occupants in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and if necessary, tighten the front structure bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 18, 2024. Owners may contact BMW Customer Service at 1-800-525-7417.
