Hyundai Santa Fe Plug-In Hybrid Fuel Tank May Leak

NHTSA campaign 23V028000 · Reported 2023-01-25 · Hyundai Motor America · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Hyundai Santa FE Plug-In Hybrid

What's wrong, in plain English

The fuel tank on these plug-in hybrids may have been formed incorrectly at the factory. A badly made tank can leak gasoline. If leaking fuel reaches something hot enough to light it, or a spark, it can start a fire. Hyundai has not told owners to park the car outside or to stop driving it. A dealer will inspect the tank and replace it if it is one of the bad ones, at no charge.

How serious is it?

This is a fire risk, which makes it one to handle quickly, though the notice does not tell owners to park outside or stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Santa Fe Plug-In Hybrid falls under campaign 23V028000.
2. Call your Hyundai dealer for the earliest inspection appointment, or Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 (recall number 240).
3. Let the dealer inspect and, if needed, replace the fuel tank — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2022 Santa Fe Plug-In Hybrid vehicles. The fuel tank may have been improperly molded, which can result in a fuel leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the fuel tank, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 20, 2023. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 240.
