Lexus LS front passenger seat sensor may misread occupant

NHTSA campaign 24V275000 · Reported 2024-04-17 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Lexus LS
  • 2019 Lexus LS
  • 2021 Lexus LS

What's wrong, in plain English

On these cars with the Executive Package, parts inside the front passenger seat may rub against each other. That can confuse the sensor built into the seat, the one that figures out whether someone is sitting there and how big they are. The airbag system uses that information to decide how to fire the passenger airbag, so in a crash the airbag may not go off the way it should. A dealer will inspect and repair the seat for free.

How serious is it?

This matters because the passenger airbag may not protect the person in that seat correctly during a crash, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Lexus is included.
2. Call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 or your Lexus dealer and mention recall 24LA03.
3. Have the front passenger seat inspected and repaired — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SENSOR:OCCUPANT CLASSIFICATION:FRONT PASSENGER
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018-2021 Lexus LS500 and 2018-2020 Lexus LS500 Hybrid vehicles equipped with the "Executive Package." The front passenger seat may have been assembled with interference between internal parts that can cause the Occupant Classification System (OCS) sensor to incorrectly detect the occupant. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
Incorrect detection of an occupant may result in improper air bag deployment during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and repair the front passenger seat, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 7, 2024. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 24LA03.
