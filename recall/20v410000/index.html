Toyota Tundra front turn signals may flash too dimly

NHTSA campaign 20V410000 · Reported 2020-07-15 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Toyota Tundra
  • 2019 Toyota Tundra
  • 2020 Toyota Tundra

What's wrong, in plain English

On trucks built with LED headlight assemblies, the wiring for the front turn signals was put together incorrectly. The bulbs still light up, but they do not shine as brightly as they should. That means drivers around you may not notice when you signal a turn or switch on your hazard flashers, especially in daylight. A dealer will change the wiring that feeds the front turn signal bulbs so the lights flash at full brightness again.

How serious is it?

A real but moderate hazard, since other drivers may miss your signals; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your Tundra is included. 2. Contact your Toyota dealer, or Toyota customer service at 1-800-270-9371, and ask about recall 20TB12/20TA12. 3. Have the wire harness modified — it is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TURN SIGNAL
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2018-2020 Toyota Tundra vehicles equipped with LED headlight assemblies. Due to a wiring error, the front turn signals may not flash brightly enough. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
If the turn signals or hazard lamps do not flash brightly enough, other drivers may fail to see that the turn signal or hazard lamp functions have been activated, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners, and dealers will modify the wire harness connected to the front turn signal bulbs free of charge. The recall began August 17, 2020. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-270-9371. Toyota's number for this recall is 20TB12/20TA12.
