Mazda3 wrong valve springs can cause engine stall

NHTSA campaign 17V745000 · Reported 2017-11-21 · Mazda North American Operations · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Mazda MAZDA3

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2018 Mazda3s were built with the wrong exhaust valve springs inside the engine. These small springs help the engine's valves open and close, and the wrong ones can break while you are driving. If one fails, the engine can stall, meaning it suddenly stops running while you are on the road. Mazda dealers will inspect the engine and replace any wrong springs free of charge. Ask for this check even if the car runs fine.

How serious is it?

A sudden stall in traffic can lead to a crash, and while Mazda does not tell owners to park the car, this one is worth an early appointment because the part can fail without warning.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls under recall 17V745000.
2. Call your Mazda dealer and ask for recall 1717K, or contact Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500.
3. Have the engine inspected and any incorrect exhaust valve springs replaced, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:EXHAUST SYSTEM
Summary
Mazda North American Operations (Mazda) is recalling certain 2018 Mazda3 vehicles, equipped with an incorrect exhaust valve spring. The incorrect exhaust valve spring may fail while driving, potentially causing an engine stall.
Safety risk
An engine stall can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Mazda will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the engines and replace any incorrect exhaust valve springs, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin January 20, 2018. Owners may contact Mazda customer service at 1-800-222-5500. Mazda's number for this recall is 1717K.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
