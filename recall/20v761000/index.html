BMW 48-Volt Battery Connection May Cause Fire

NHTSA campaign 20V761000 · Reported 2020-12-09 · BMW of North America, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

Increased electrical resistance at this connection can raise the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain BMW models, the connection between the 48-volt battery and the starter-generator may not have been put together correctly. This can lead to increased electrical resistance at that connection point in the vehicle's electrical system.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a BMW dealer, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. The inspection and any needed repair are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2021 M340i xDrive, M440i xDrive Coupe, 530i, 530i xDrive, 540i, 540i xDrive, 740Li, 740Li xDrive, 840i Gran Coupe, 840i xDrive Gran Coupe, X5 sDrive40i, X5 xDrive40i, X6 sDrive40i, X6 xDrive40i, and X7 xDrive40i vehicles. The connection between the 48-Volt battery and the starter-generator may not have been assembled properly, which can cause increased electrical resistance at the connections.
Safety risk
Increased electrical resistance can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the connection between the 48-Volt battery and the starter-generator assembly, replacing it if necessary, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin April 12, 2021. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
