NHTSA campaign 21V950000 · Reported Dec 9, 2021 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

What's wrong, in plain English

Some of these Jeep Grand Cherokee vehicles have a smart headlight control module. If a software update to that module does not finish properly, one or both of the vehicle's headlights can stop working entirely. Headlights that don't work reduce how well you can see the road ahead and how well other drivers can see your vehicle, which increases the chance of a crash for you or for people driving nearby.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a dealer, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The software update is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V950000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:LIGHTING CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2021 Jeep Grand Cherokee L vehicles equipped with smart lighting modules (SLM). An incomplete SLM software update may cause one or both headlights to become inoperable. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Inoperable headlights can reduce visibility for the driver and oncoming traffic, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the SLM software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 23, 2021. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is Y72.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
