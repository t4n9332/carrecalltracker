Cadillac XT5, XT6, GMC Acadia Camera May Fail

NHTSA campaign 22V709000 · Reported 2022-09-22 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

An inoperative rearview camera reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On vehicles with the optional Surround Vision feature, the coaxial cable connectors for the rearview camera may have been crimped incorrectly. This can cause the rearview camera to stop working or work only some of the time.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
Contact a Cadillac dealer at 1-800-458-8006 or a GMC dealer at 1-800-462-8782 to schedule a repair.
The rearview camera's coaxial cables will be replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Cadillac XT5, XT6, and GMC Acadia vehicles equipped with the optional Surround Vision feature. The rearview camera coaxial cable connectors may have been crimped improperly, causing the rearview camera to fail or function intermittently.
Safety risk
An inoperative rearview camera can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the coaxial cables, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 18, 2023. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N222378380.
