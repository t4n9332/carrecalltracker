Subaru Forester child seat anchor bolts may loosen

NHTSA campaign 25V419000 · Reported 2025-06-19 · Subaru of America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Subaru Forester

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts that hold the child seat anchors to the car body may not have been tightened enough at the factory, so they can work loose. Those anchors are what a child safety seat latches onto. If a bolt is loose, the child seat may not be held firmly during a crash and a child could be hurt. A dealer will inspect the anchor bolts and tighten any that need it, free of charge.

How serious is it?

Serious if you carry a child in a car seat: the vehicle itself is safe to drive, but have the anchors inspected before your next trip with a child seat installed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Forester is covered.
2. Call your Subaru dealer, or Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614, and mention recall WRC-25.
3. Have the anchor bolts inspected and tightened at no cost.

Official notice

Component
CHILD SEAT
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2025 Forester vehicles. The child seat anchor bolts may have been improperly tightened, allowing the bolts to come loose.
Safety risk
A loose child seat anchor bolt increases the risk of injury to a child during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the child seat anchor bolts and, if necessary, tighten the bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 15, 2025. Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRC-25.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
