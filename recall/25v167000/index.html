Mazda CX-50 accessory trailer hitch can fall off

NHTSA campaign 25V167000 · Reported 2025-03-14 · Mazda North American Operations · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Mazda CX-50
  • 2025 Mazda CX-50

What's wrong, in plain English

If your CX-50 was fitted with a Mazda accessory trailer hitch, the bolts holding that hitch to the body may work loose over time. If they loosen enough, the whole hitch assembly can separate from the vehicle, either while you are towing or during ordinary everyday driving. A hitch that drops onto the road becomes an obstacle for you and for the drivers behind you. The repair is straightforward: a dealer tightens the hitch bolts properly. CX-50s without that accessory hitch are not affected.

How serious is it?

A hitch coming off at road speed is a real hazard to other drivers, and while the notice does not tell you to stop driving, you should get the bolts tightened before your next towing trip.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your CX-50 is covered (Mazda's recall number is 7225C).
2. Contact a Mazda dealer and ask for the trailer hitch bolt tightening under this recall.
3. The work is free; schedule it promptly if you tow with the vehicle.

Official notice

Component
TRAILER HITCHES
Summary
Mazda North American Operations (Mazda) is recalling certain 2024-2025 CX-50 vehicles equipped with Mazda Genuine Accessory trailer hitches. The trailer hitch assembly bolts may loosen and cause the assembly to separate from the vehicle body during towing or normal driving conditions.
Safety risk
A detached trailer hitch assembly can create a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will tighten the trailer hitch bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 4, 2025. Mazda's number for this recall is 7225C.
