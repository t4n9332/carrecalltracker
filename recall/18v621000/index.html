Mitsubishi Vehicles May Have Faulty Safety System Software

NHTSA campaign 18V621000 · Reported 2018-09-14 · Mitsubishi Motors North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

If these safety systems fail to work when needed, it can raise the chance of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Mitsubishi Outlander Sport, Outlander PHEV, Eclipse Cross, and Outlander vehicles have a possible software problem in the control unit that manages the brake system. This could stop safety features such as automatic emergency braking, anti-lock brakes, electronic stability control, or the brake auto-hold function from working when they are needed. Dealers will update the software in the hydraulic control unit to fix the problem.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Mitsubishi customer service at 1-888-648-7820.
3. The software update is free.

Official notice

Component
FORWARD COLLISION AVOIDANCE: ADAPTIVE CRUISE CONTROL
Summary
Mitsubishi Motors North America, Inc. (MMNA) is recalling certain 2018 Mitsubishi Outlander Sport vehicles equipped with a Forward Collision Mitigation (FCM) system and 2018 Mitsubishi Outlander PHEV and Eclipse Cross vehicles and 2017-2018 Outlander vehicles equipped with an Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) system and/or an Electric Parking Brake (EPB) system. The software for the Hydraulic Unit Electronic Control Unit (ECU) may be incorrect, possibly preventing the intervention of a safety system such as automatic emergency braking, Anti-lock Braking (ABS), Electronic Stability Control (ESC), or the Brake Auto Hold (BAH) function when any of these systems are in use.
Safety risk
If the safety systems do not function as intended, it can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
MMNA will notify owners, and dealers will update the Hydraulic Unit-ECU software, free of charge. The recall began October 16, 2018. Owners may contact MMNA customer service at 1-888-648-7820. MMNA's number for this recall is SR-18-008.
