Ford Super Duty diesel trucks can stall from transmission part

NHTSA campaign 20V365000 · Reported 2020-06-23 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Ford F-350 SD
  • 2020 Ford F-550 SD

What's wrong, in plain English

A metal plate inside the transmission's torque converter was not heat-treated properly, which makes it brittle. It can crack and break apart. If that happens, the engine may shut off while you are driving, or the brake pedal may suddenly take much more force to push down to stop the truck. Either one can happen without warning and makes the truck much harder to control. The dealer will replace the whole transmission at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

An engine that quits or brakes that suddenly need much more effort raises the risk of a crash, and the notice does not tell owners to park the truck.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is affected. 2. Contact your Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 20S33) to book the repair. 3. Have the transmission replaced — parts and labor are free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:TORQUE CONVERTER
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020 Super-Duty F-250, F-350, and F-550 diesel pickups. The transmission torque converter damper plate was not tempered, resulting in a brittle damper plate that may fracture and cause the engine to stall or require increased brake pedal effort in order to stop the vehicle.
Safety risk
An engine stall or requiring additional braking effort to stop the vehicle can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will replace the transmission assembly, free of charge. This recall began July 6, 2020. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 20S33.
