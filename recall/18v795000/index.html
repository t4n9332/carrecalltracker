Honda Odyssey power sliding doors may not latch shut

NHTSA campaign 18V795000 · Reported 2018-11-09 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious for anyone riding in or near the van; the van can still be driven, but until it is fixed, make sure each sliding door is fully closed and keep passengers, especially children, away from the doors.

What's wrong, in plain English

The power sliding side doors on certain 2018 and 2019 Honda Odyssey minivans have rear latches that can stick instead of catching properly on the door frame. A door that has not fully latched can come open while the van is moving. That could injure someone inside the van or someone standing nearby. Honda dealers will replace the rear latch assemblies on both the left and right sliding doors at no charge to you.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your minivan is affected. 2. Call your Honda dealer or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (recall Z33 or O36) for an appointment. 3. Have both rear sliding door latch assemblies replaced — the parts and labor are free.

  • 2019 Honda Odyssey

Official notice

Component
LATCHES/LOCKS/LINKAGES:DOORS:LATCH
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain model year 2018 and 2019 Honda Odyssey vehicles. The latch assemblies for the power sliding doors may stick and not properly latch to the door strikers.
Safety risk
If the power sliding doors fails to latch, the doors may open while moving, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Honda will notify owners, and dealers will replace the left and right power sliding door rear latch assemblies, free of charge. The recall began January 3, 2019. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's numbers for this recall are Z33 and O36.
