Ford and Lincoln transmission may cause unexpected vehicle movement

NHTSA campaign 25V164000 · Reported 2025-03-14 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Ford Bronco
  • 2025 Ford Explorer
  • 2025 Ford Mustang
  • 2025 Lincoln Aviator

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain 2024-2025 F-150 and Bronco, 2024 Ranger, and 2025 Mustang, Explorer and Aviator vehicles. A part inside the transmission that routes fluid to the gears, called the valve body, may have been machined incorrectly. Reverse gear can fail, or the vehicle can move forward unexpectedly when you have selected reverse or neutral. That kind of surprise movement can cause a crash. Dealers will replace the valve body at no cost.

How serious is it?

This is serious because the vehicle can move in a direction you did not choose with no warning, though Ford does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is part of recall 25S19.
2. Call your Ford or Lincoln dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and schedule the repair.
3. Have the transmission main control valve body replaced; it is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:FLUID/LUBRICANT:VALVES/VALVE BODY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2024-2025 F-150, Bronco, 2024 Ranger, 2025 Mustang, Explorer, and Aviator vehicles. The transmission valve body may have been machined incorrectly, which can cause reverse gear failure, or unexpected forward movement in reverse or neutral gear.
Safety risk
Unexpected vehicle movement increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the transmission main control valve body, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 11, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S19.
