Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager windshield may not be glued properly

NHTSA campaign 24V238000 · Reported 2024-03-28 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) (Stellantis)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Moderate — the van drives normally and you can keep driving it, but in a crash the windshield may not protect you as it should, so schedule the free replacement.

What's wrong, in plain English

The windshield is glued to the body of the van, and that bond is part of the vehicle's safety structure. On these vans the glue may not have been applied correctly, so the windshield could come away from the van in a crash. If it does, people inside are less protected during the crash and can be injured. The van drives normally otherwise. The dealer will install a new windshield free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is included.
2. Call your Chrysler dealer or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403 and reference recall 25B.
3. Book the windshield replacement — it is done at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2024 Pacifica and Voyager vehicles. The windshield may not have been properly bonded to the vehicle, allowing it to detach during a crash. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 212, "Windshield Mounting."
Safety risk
A windshield that detaches from the vehicle during a crash can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the windshield, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 30, 2024. Owners may contact FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's number for this recall is 25B.
