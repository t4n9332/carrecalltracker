Honda Africa Twin DCT Software May Stall Engine

NHTSA campaign 22V662000 · Reported 2022-09-01 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

An engine stall or loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

American Honda is recalling certain CRF1100 Africa Twin motorcycles equipped with a dual-clutch transmission. A software error in the engine's control computer can disconnect internal communication between systems and release the clutch on its own, without the rider doing anything. When that happens, the engine can stall, or the motorcycle can lose the power it needs to keep moving forward under its own power.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your motorcycle is included.
2. Contact Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870 or your dealer to schedule the ECU software update.
3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECU/ECM):SOFTWARE
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2020-2021 CRF1100 Africa Twin motorcycles equipped with a dual-clutch transmission (DCT). A software error in the engine control unit (ECU), may disconnect the controller area network (CAN) communication and release the clutch, which can result in an engine stall or a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
An engine stall or a loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will update the ECU software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 6, 2022. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KNB.
