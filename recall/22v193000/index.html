Ford and Lincoln trucks: trailer brakes may not work

NHTSA campaign 22V193000 · Reported 2022-03-25 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Ford Expedition
  • 2022 Ford F-250 SD
  • 2022 Ford F-450 SD
  • 2022 Ford F-550 SD
  • 2022 Ford Maverick
  • 2022 Lincoln Navigator

What's wrong, in plain English

A software problem in the built-in trailer brake controller means that a trailer hooked up behind your truck or SUV may not brake at all. Trailers with electric or electric-over-hydraulic brakes rely on this system to slow down with you. If it doesn't work, you're stopping the combined weight of the vehicle and the loaded trailer on your truck's brakes alone, so it takes much longer to stop. A dealer reloads the controller's software to fix it.

How serious is it?

Serious if you tow: stopping distances get much longer and that can cause a crash, so the safest choice is to avoid towing with electric trailer brakes until it's fixed, though the notice does not tell you to stop driving the vehicle itself.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included in recall 22V193000 (Ford's number 22S17).
2. Call your Ford or Lincoln dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and book the software update.
3. Have the update done before your next tow; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INTEGRATED TRAILER BRAKE CONTROL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2022 F-150, 2022 Maverick, Expedition, Lincoln Navigator, F-250, F-350, F-450, and F-550 vehicles. Due to a software error, a towed trailer equipped with an electric or electric-over hydraulic brake system may not brake.
Safety risk
Loss of trailer brake functionality could result in extended stopping distance, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the integrated trailer brake control module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 22, 2022. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22S17.
