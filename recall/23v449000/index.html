BMW i4, iX High-Voltage Battery Charging Unit Fault

NHTSA campaign 23V449000 · Reported Jun 28, 2023 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

An improperly connected charging unit can interrupt electrical power while driving and cause the engine to stall, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The high voltage battery's charging unit may not have been manufactured correctly, leaving components inside it improperly connected or assembled.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a BMW dealer at 1-800-525-7417 to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V449000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:HYBRID MANAGEMENT
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2022-2023 iX xDrive50, iX M60, i4 eDrive40, i4 M50, and 2023 i760 xDrive hybrid electric vehicles. The high voltage battery combined charging unit (CCU) may not have been manufactured correctly, resulting in improperly connected and/or assembled components inside the CCU.
Safety risk
An improperly connected or assembled CCU can interrupt electrical power while driving and result in an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the high voltage battery combined charging unit (CCU), free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 23, 2023. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
