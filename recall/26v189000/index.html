Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager Curtain Airbags Lose Air Pressure

NHTSA campaign 26V189000 · Reported 2026-03-26 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Chrysler Pacifica
  • 2022 Chrysler Voyager
  • 2023 Chrysler Pacifica
  • 2023 Chrysler Voyager
  • 2024 Chrysler Pacifica
  • 2024 Chrysler Voyager
  • 2025 Chrysler Pacifica

What's wrong, in plain English

The side curtain airbags in these vans — the long airbags that come down over the side windows — have seams that were not sealed properly. Because of that, they can lose air pressure too quickly instead of staying inflated, so they may not hold you inside the vehicle during a crash or rollover. Dealers will replace the curtain airbags as needed. This recall replaces two earlier ones, so vans already fixed before still need the new repair.

How serious is it?

Serious in a crash, since an airbag that does not hold pressure raises the risk of being thrown from the van; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so keep driving but book the repair.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm campaign 26V189000 covers your van.
2. Call Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403 and ask about recall 06D, 10D, or 11D — do this even if your van was already repaired under recall 25V302 or 25V573.
3. Have the dealer replace the side curtain airbags — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022-2026 Pacifica and Voyager vehicles. The right and left side curtain air bags may have insufficient pressure retention from improperly sealed seams. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 226, "Ejection Mitigation."
Safety risk
Air bags with insufficient pressure retention increase the risk of ejection and injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the side curtain air bags, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 14, 2026. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's numbers for this recall are 06D, 10D, and 11D. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning April 2, 2026. This recall expands and replaces previous NHTSA recall numbers 25V302 and 25V573. Vehicles previously repaired under a prior recall will need to have the new remedy completed.
