Honda Odyssey backup camera can fail from water

NHTSA campaign 26V423000 · Reported 2026-07-01 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Honda Odyssey
  • 2019 Honda Odyssey
  • 2020 Honda Odyssey

What's wrong, in plain English

Water can get inside the backup camera on these minivans. When that happens, the picture may not come up on the dashboard screen after you shift into reverse. You lose the view directly behind the van, so it is easier to back into something, or someone, that the mirrors do not show. Federal rules require that picture to appear, which is why this is a recall. The dealer replaces the camera at no charge to you.

How serious is it?

This raises the chance of backing into a person or object, but the van is otherwise safe to drive — just look over your shoulder and use your mirrors every time you reverse until it is fixed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls (VINs are searchable starting July 9, 2026).
2. Call your Honda dealer, or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 (recall HOX), for an appointment.
3. Have the rearview camera replaced — it is free, even if you paid for a repair before.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2018-2020 Odyssey vehicles. Water may enter into the rearview camera, which can cause the rearview camera image to fail to display when the vehicle is in reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview image that does not display reduces the driver's view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the rearview camera, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 24, 2026. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is HOX. This recall expands previous NHTSA recall number 20V438. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning July 9, 2026.
