Jeep Wagoneer quarter window trim can come off

NHTSA campaign 25V642000 · Reported 2025-09-25 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Jeep Grand Wagoneer
  • 2022 Jeep Wagoneer
  • 2023 Jeep Grand Wagoneer
  • 2023 Jeep Wagoneer
  • 2024 Jeep Grand Wagoneer
  • 2024 Jeep Wagoneer

What's wrong, in plain English

The trim piece at the edge of the driver's and passenger's side windows may not have been fastened down properly at the factory. Over time it can work loose and detach from the vehicle. If it comes off while you're driving, it becomes a loose object in the road that another driver could hit or swerve to avoid. The dealer inspects the area and replaces the quarter window glass assembly if it's needed.

How serious is it?

Moderate: the main danger is to other drivers if the trim falls off in traffic, and the notice does not tell you to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls for recall 25V642000 (FCA's number 88C).
2. Contact your Jeep dealer, or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to schedule the inspection.
3. The inspection and any replacement are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:EXTERIOR TRIM
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Jeep Wagoneer and Jeep Grand Wagoneer vehicles. The quarter trim on the driver and passenger windows may not have been properly secured, which can allow it to detach.
Safety risk
Detached trim pieces can create a road hazard for other vehicles, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the quarter window glass assembly, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning February 19, 2026. Owners may contact FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's number for this recall is 88C. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning October 2, 2025.
