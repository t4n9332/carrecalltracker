Rivian R1T and R1S headlights aimed incorrectly

NHTSA campaign 24V367000 · Reported 2024-05-23 · Rivian Automotive, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Rivian R1S
  • 2023 Rivian R1T
  • 2024 Rivian R1S
  • 2024 Rivian R1T

What's wrong, in plain English

The headlights on these trucks and SUVs may not be pointed where they should be. Headlights have to be aimed within set limits so they light up enough of the road ahead without blinding oncoming drivers. On these vehicles the aim can be off, which means less of the road is lit at night or in bad weather. Rivian will check the aim and adjust the headlights if they are out of position.

How serious is it?

This is a moderate problem — poorly aimed headlights reduce how far ahead you can see at night and raise crash risk, but there is no warning to stop driving the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is covered.
2. Contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4264 (recall FSAM-1445) to arrange the inspection.
3. Get the headlights adjusted — the inspection and adjustment are free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC. (Rivian) is recalling certain 2023-2024 EDV, R1S, and R1T vehicles. The headlights may be improperly aimed. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamp, Reflective Devices and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Improperly aimed headlights can result in insufficient illumination of the road, decreasing the driver's visibility and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will inspect and adjust the headlight alignment as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 28, 2024. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4264. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1445.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
