Chevrolet Equinox EV cruise control may not brake

NHTSA campaign 25V012000 · Reported 2025-01-16 · General Motors, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Chevrolet Equinox EV

What's wrong, in plain English

These all-wheel-drive electric SUVs left the factory with the wrong software in the computer that controls the brakes. Because of that error, the adaptive cruise control, which is supposed to slow the vehicle down automatically when traffic ahead slows, may not apply the brakes when you expect it to. If you are relying on cruise control to slow you down, the vehicle may keep going instead, leaving you to brake yourself.

How serious is it?

You can keep driving, but the vehicle may not brake on its own as expected, so stay ready to brake yourself until the free software update is installed.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered. 2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer for the software update, or call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 (recall N242481530). 3. The dealer reprograms the brake control module free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2025 Chevrolet Equinox EV all-wheel drive electric vehicles. The adaptive cruise control may fail to engage the brakes as expected, due to incorrect brake module software.
Safety risk
A vehicle that does not automatically brake as expected increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the software calibration in the brake system control module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 13, 2025. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N242481530.
