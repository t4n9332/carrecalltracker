What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2025 Chevrolet Equinox EV all-wheel drive electric vehicles, the adaptive cruise control system may not apply the brakes the way it is supposed to when needed. This happens because of incorrect software installed in the module that controls the vehicle's brake system. As a result, the brakes may not engage automatically as the driver expects when the adaptive cruise control feature is being used.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer, or call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020.
3. The software update is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V012000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2025 Chevrolet Equinox EV all-wheel drive electric vehicles. The adaptive cruise control may fail to engage the brakes as expected, due to incorrect brake module software.
Safety risk
A vehicle that does not automatically brake as expected increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the software calibration in the brake system control module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 13, 2025. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N242481530.
