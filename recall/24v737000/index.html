Chevrolet Blazer and Equinox doors may unlatch and open

NHTSA campaign 24V737000 · Reported 2024-10-02 · General Motors, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Chevrolet Blazer
  • 2025 Chevrolet Blazer EV
  • 2025 Chevrolet Equinox EV

What's wrong, in plain English

The metal catches that your doors latch onto — one on each of the four side doors — were not heat-treated correctly at the factory, so they can break. If one breaks, that door can pop open on its own, including while the vehicle is moving. Someone could fall out or be hit by the swinging door. Every affected vehicle is still sitting on a dealer lot, so no owner letters are being sent.

How serious is it?

Serious — a door that opens unexpectedly can injure someone or throw a passenger out — though the notice does not tell anyone to stop driving, and all affected vehicles are still in dealer inventory.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls if you recently bought one of these vehicles. 2. Call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 (GM recall N242477131) or your dealer. 3. The dealer will replace all four door strikers and their bolts free of charge.

Official notice

Component
LATCHES/LOCKS/LINKAGES:DOORS:LATCH
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2025 Chevrolet Blazer, Blazer EV, and Equinox vehicles. The door strikers were not properly heat-treated, which can cause them to break.
Safety risk
A broken door striker can cause the door to open unexpectedly, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace all four side door strikers and attachment bolts, free of charge. All affected vehicles are still in dealership inventory and therefore no owner notification letters are expected to be mailed. Owners may call Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N242477131.
