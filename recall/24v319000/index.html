Rivian R1T and R1S missing dashboard airbag warning label

NHTSA campaign 24V319000 · Reported 2024-05-09 · Rivian Automotive, LLC · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Rivian R1S
  • 2024 Rivian R1T

What's wrong, in plain English

The warning label that belongs on the dashboard may be missing from these trucks and SUVs. That label tells you an airbag can seriously hurt a baby or child riding in the front seat. Without it, a driver may not realize the danger of putting a child up front. Because the label is missing, the vehicle does not meet the federal rule on crash protection. Rivian has mailed owners the warning label information at no charge.

How serious is it?

Nothing is mechanically wrong with the vehicle, but a missing warning could lead someone to seat a child in front and be injured by the airbag; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected. 2. Watch for the label mailing, or contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4264 (recall FSAM-1429) if you did not get it. 3. Apply the label; there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC. (Rivian) is recalling certain 2024 R1T and R1S vehicles. The dashboard air bag warning label may be missing. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
Without the dashboard label, operators may be unaware of the increased risks that an air bag poses to an infant or child in the front seat, increasing the risk of injury to occupants.
Remedy
Rivian mailed owners the warning label information, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 30, 2024. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4264. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1429.
