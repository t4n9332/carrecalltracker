Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Door Wedge Installed Incorrectly

NHTSA campaign 19V716000 · Reported 2019-10-08 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

If a vehicle with an incorrectly installed wedge is in a severe frontal crash, it may take more force to open the doors, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans have a part called the abutting wedge, attached to the door frame to make it easier to open the door after a severe frontal crash, that may have been installed incorrectly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a dealer to have the wedge installation inspected and corrected if needed; call 1-877-762-8267 with questions.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:DOOR
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2019 Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vehicles. In the affected vehicles, the abutting wedge, a component attached to the door jamb to make opening the door easier after a severe frontal impact, may have been installed incorrectly.
Safety risk
If a vehicle that has an incorrectly installed abutting wedge is involved in a severe frontal accident, a greater force may be necessary to open the doors, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the installation of the abutting wedge on the driver and front passenger side, correcting it, as necessary, free of charge. DVUSA issued owners an interim notification on November 22, 2019. The recall began February 10, 2020. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3KEIL.
