Volkswagen Tiguan rear spoiler can come off while driving

NHTSA campaign 22V226000 · Reported 2022-04-06 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Volkswagen Tiguan
  • 2019 Volkswagen Tiguan
  • 2020 Volkswagen Tiguan
  • 2021 Volkswagen Tiguan
  • 2022 Volkswagen Tiguan

What's wrong, in plain English

The add-on spoiler above the rear hatch window was fitted using instructions that were wrong, so it may not be attached firmly enough. Over time it can work loose and separate from the vehicle while you are driving. A piece that size landing on the road is a hazard for the cars behind you and can cause a crash. A dealer will replace the spoiler at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

The vehicle drives normally and is safe to keep using, but the loose spoiler is a danger to other drivers if it comes off, so have it handled promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 (recall 66N5) or your dealer to book the work. 3. Have the spoiler replaced — it is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2022 Tiguan and 2018-2021 Tiguan long wheel base (LWB) vehicles. Due to incorrect installation instructions, the accessory rear hatch spoiler may be insufficiently attached to the vehicle.
Safety risk
An insufficiently attached spoiler may separate from the vehicle, becoming a road hazard and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the spoiler, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 3, 2022. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 66N5.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
