Volkswagen Tiguan Rear Spoiler May Detach

NHTSA campaign 22V226000 · Reported 2022-04-06 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

If the spoiler separates from the vehicle, it becomes a road hazard and increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Volkswagen Tiguan and Tiguan long wheelbase vehicles have an accessory spoiler on the rear hatch that was installed using incorrect instructions. Because of this, the spoiler may not be attached to the vehicle securely enough. A loose spoiler could come off the vehicle while driving and become a hazard on the road for other drivers.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298 or your dealer to schedule replacement of the spoiler.
3. The replacement is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2022 Tiguan and 2018-2021 Tiguan long wheel base (LWB) vehicles. Due to incorrect installation instructions, the accessory rear hatch spoiler may be insufficiently attached to the vehicle.
Safety risk
An insufficiently attached spoiler may separate from the vehicle, becoming a road hazard and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the spoiler, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 3, 2022. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 66N5.
