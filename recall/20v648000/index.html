Volkswagen Jetta fuel rail bolts can loosen and leak

NHTSA campaign 20V648000 · Reported 2020-10-21 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Volkswagen Jetta

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2016-2018 Jettas, the bolts holding the fuel rail, the metal pipe that feeds gasoline to the engine, can work loose over time. If they loosen enough, gasoline can leak out, and a fuel leak raises the risk of a fire. Volkswagen dealers will replace those bolts, and the fuel rail itself if needed, at no cost. If you smell gasoline, stop driving and call Volkswagen or a dealer right away.

How serious is it?

Any recall involving a possible fuel leak and fire is serious, and while Volkswagen does not tell owners to stop driving or park outside, you should book the free repair quickly and never ignore a gasoline smell.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 20V648000 applies to your Jetta.
2. Contact your Volkswagen dealer and ask for recall 24Gi, or call 1-800-893-5298.
3. Have the fuel rail bolts replaced, and the fuel rail too if the dealer finds it necessary, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Jetta vehicles. The fuel rail bolts may become loose over time, allowing fuel to leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Volkswagen will notify owners, and dealers will replace the fuel rail bolts and, as necessary, the fuel rail. The recall began March 31, 2021. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 24Gi.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
