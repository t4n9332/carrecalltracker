Single BMW X5 passenger airbag may not deploy properly

NHTSA campaign 21V520000 · Reported 2021-07-08 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 BMW X5

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW found that one 2019 X5 xDrive 40i was built with an instrument panel — the dashboard structure in front of the passenger seat — that was not made correctly. Because of that flaw, the front passenger airbag might not open the way it is supposed to in a crash, so someone in that seat could be injured more badly. A dealer will replace the entire instrument panel free of charge. Only this one vehicle is affected.

How serious is it?

Serious for the one affected vehicle — the passenger airbag may not protect properly in a crash, though nothing in the notice says the vehicle is unsafe to drive, so schedule the free repair promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your X5 is the vehicle covered by recall 21V520000.
2. If it is, contact your BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, to arrange the instrument panel replacement.
3. Have the panel replaced — parts and labor are free.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling one 2019 X5 xDrive 40i vehicle. The instrument panel may not have been manufactured correctly, which could prevent the passenger side air bag from deploying properly in a crash.
Safety risk
An air bag that does not deploy properly can increase the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
A dealer will replace the instrument panel, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on July 26, 2021. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
