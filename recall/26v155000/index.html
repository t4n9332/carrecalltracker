Ford F-53 and F-59 brake lights may stay lit

NHTSA campaign 26V155000 · Reported Mar 17, 2026 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

Brake lights that stay on can fail to signal to other vehicles when the driver actually intends to brake, which Ford says increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain 2025-2026 F-59 and F-53 commercial stripped chassis vehicles. The brake lights on the outside of the vehicle may stay lit even when the driver is not pressing the brakes. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for lamps, reflective devices and related equipment. Dealers will inspect the brake pedal and repair the brake booster push rod if needed, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 27, 2026.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included — Ford says these VINs became searchable on NHTSA.gov on March 20, 2026.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall number 26C17), to book an appointment.
3. Have the dealer inspect the brake pedal and repair the brake booster push rod as needed — the repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V155000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:BRAKE LIGHTS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2025-2026 F-59 and F-53 commercial stripped chassis vehicles. The exterior brake lights may remain illuminated when the driver is not applying the brakes. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Brake lights that remain illuminated can fail to alert other vehicles of the driver's intention, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the brake pedal and repair the brake booster push rod as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 27, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26C17. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning March 20, 2026.
