Hyundai Santa Fe Sport brake part may cause engine fire

NHTSA campaign 22V810000 · Reported 2022-10-27 · Hyundai Motor America · critical severity

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Hyundai Santa FE Sport

What's wrong, in plain English

The control box for the anti-lock brakes can go bad and cause an electrical short. That short can start a fire in the engine compartment — and it can happen while you are driving or even while the car is sitting parked. Until it is fixed, Hyundai says to park the car outside and away from buildings. A dealer will replace a fuse in the brake system at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

Serious fire risk that can happen even while parked — park outside and away from structures until the repair is done.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected. 2. Park outside and away from your home, garage, and other buildings. 3. Contact your Hyundai dealer or Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 (recall 237) to have the brake fuse replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:ANTILOCK/TRACTION CONTROL/ELECTRONIC LIMITED SLIP:CONTROL UNIT/MODULE
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2018 Santa Fe Sport vehicles. The Anti-Lock Brake System (ABS) module could malfunction and cause an electrical short, which could result in an engine compartment fire while parked or driving.
Safety risk
An engine compartment fire while parked or driving can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. Dealers will replace the ABS multi-fuse, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 16, 2022. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 237. This recall is an expansion of recall 22V-056.
