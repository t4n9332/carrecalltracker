What's wrong, in plain English

These heavy-duty Ford trucks were repaired under an earlier recall, but that fix was not done correctly. Deposits from biodiesel fuel can build up on moving parts inside the high-pressure fuel pump and cause the pump to fail. If that happens, the truck can lose engine power while you are driving. Dealers will update the engine computer software for free. Your truck needs this even though the earlier recall work was already done.

How serious is it?

Losing power while driving can lead to a crash, so this should be handled soon; the notice does not tell owners to park the truck.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — VINs became searchable March 18, 2026.
2. Contact your Ford dealer and ask for recall 26S17 (Ford: 1-866-436-7332).
3. Get the free engine computer software update installed.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020-2022 F-Super Duty F250, F350, F450, F550, 2021-2022 F600, and 2022 F750 vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall number 24V957. Biodiesel deposits may form on the pump drivetrain roller components, which could lead to failure of the high pressure fuel pump.
Safety risk
High pressure fuel pump failure can cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the powertrain control module (PCM) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed mailed April 7, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S17. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on March 18, 2026.
