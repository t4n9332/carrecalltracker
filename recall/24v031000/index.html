Ford Explorer roof pillar trim may come loose

NHTSA campaign 24V031000 · Reported 2024-01-19 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

A piece of trim that falls off the vehicle can become a hazard in the road and increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Explorer vehicles. The clips that hold the trim on the A-pillar — the post on either side of the windshield — may not be fastened properly, which can let the trim piece come off. Dealers will inspect the trim and replace it if needed. The repair is being released in stages depending on the model year, and owner notification letters have been mailed. The work is free.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Ford dealer to schedule the inspection, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:ROOF AND PILLARS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2011-2019 Explorer vehicles. The A-pillar trim retention clips may not be properly engaged, allowing the trim to detach.
Safety risk
A detached trim piece can fall off the vehicle, becoming a road hazard and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the A-pillar trim as necessary, free of charge. This will be a phased campaign, with the remedy becoming available in different phases based on model years. Owner notification letters were mailed between July 18, 2024 and December 16, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S02.
