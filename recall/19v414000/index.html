Ford F-550 backup camera image can appear reversed

NHTSA campaign 19V414000 · Reported 2019-06-05 · Farber Specialty Vehicles

medium How serious is it? About this label

The truck is safe to drive, but until it is fixed, do not trust the backup screen alone: check your mirrors and look behind you before reversing.

What's wrong, in plain English

These 2018 Ford F-550s were built out by Farber with an ASA Voyager monitor that shows the back-up camera picture. The monitor can reset itself to its factory settings, and when it does, the picture comes up flipped, like a mirror image. Left looks like right. If you are backing up and there is something behind you, you could steer the wrong way trying to miss it. Dealers add a small electrical part that stops the settings from resetting.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 19V414000 covers your truck.
2. Call Farber customer service at 1-800-331-3188 or ASA at 1-800-384-4400 to schedule the fix.
3. Have the harness relay capacitor module installed at no charge.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT
Summary
Farber Specialty Vehicles (Farber) is recalling certain 2018 Ford F-550 vehicles equipped with ASA Electronics Voyager monitors used to display the back-up camera image. The affected monitors may revert back to the factory default settings which can cause the camera image to be reversed.
Safety risk
The driver may inadvertently turn the wrong direction to avoid an object behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Farber will notify owners, and dealers will add a harness relay capacitor module, free of charge. The recall began May 9, 2019. Owners may contact Farber customer service at 1-800-331-3188 or ASA customer service at 1-800-384-4400.
