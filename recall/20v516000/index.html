BMW Z4 and Toyota Supra Headlights May Be Misaligned

NHTSA campaign 20V516000 · Reported Aug 27, 2020 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Horizontally adjusted headlamps could increase glare for oncoming drivers or reduce visibility for the vehicle's own driver, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain BMW Z4 and Toyota Supra vehicles, the headlamps can be adjusted horizontally, which does not meet federal lighting requirements.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a BMW dealer (1-800-525-7417) or Toyota dealer (1-888-270-9371) for an inspection.
3. Any needed correction is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V516000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Z4 sDrive30i, Z4 M40i and 2020 Toyota Supra vehicles. The headlamps are able to be adjusted horizontally. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Should the headlamps be adjusted horizontally, there could be increased glare and reduced visibility for oncoming drivers, or reduced visibility for the vehicle operator, thereby increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the headlamp vertical and horizontal adjustment plugs, correcting them as necessary, free of charge. The recall began December 23, 2020. BMW owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 or Toyota owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371.
