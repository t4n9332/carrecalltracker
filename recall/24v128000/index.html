Toyota Camry second-row headrest brackets poorly welded

NHTSA campaign 24V128000 · Reported 2024-02-21 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

medium How serious is it? About this label

The danger only shows up in a crash, and there is no instruction to stop driving, so the vehicle is safe to use, though you may want to seat rear passengers in the left rear seat until it is fixed.

What's wrong, in plain English

The head restraints, meaning the padded headrests, on the middle and right-hand seats in the second row may sit in brackets that were not welded well enough at the factory. A weak bracket may not hold a passenger's head in place during a crash, which can lead to a worse head or neck injury for whoever is sitting there. It also falls short of a federal safety rule for head restraints. Toyota dealers will replace the larger rear seat back section at no charge.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Camry is covered by recall 24TB04 or 24TA04.
2. Schedule the free rear seat back replacement with your Toyota dealer.
3. Call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 if you did not receive the letter mailed in April 2024.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Camry and Camry Hybrid vehicles. The second-row center and right-side seats may have insufficiently welded head restraint brackets. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 202, "Head Restraints."
Safety risk
A head restraint bracket that is insufficiently welded may not properly restrain an occupant during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the 60% rear seat-back frame sub-assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 19, 2024. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 24TB04 and 24TA04.
