Toyota Camry rear head restraint brackets poorly welded

NHTSA campaign 24V128000 · Reported 2024-02-21 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

medium How serious is it? About this label

A head restraint bracket that is not welded properly may not hold an occupant in place during a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Toyota is recalling certain Camry and Camry Hybrid vehicles. The brackets that hold the head restraints on the center and right-hand seats in the second row may not have been welded well enough. Because of that, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for head restraints. Dealers will replace the rear seat-back frame assembly for the larger section of the back seat.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your car is included.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer to book the repair, or call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331.
3. The replacement seat-back frame is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Camry and Camry Hybrid vehicles. The second-row center and right-side seats may have insufficiently welded head restraint brackets. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 202, "Head Restraints."
Safety risk
A head restraint bracket that is insufficiently welded may not properly restrain an occupant during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the 60% rear seat-back frame sub-assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 19, 2024. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 24TB04 and 24TA04.
