Ford Escape and Transit Side Airbags May Fail

NHTSA campaign 20V415000 · Reported Jul 17, 2020 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

If the curtain airbag does not inflate properly in a crash, it increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Escape and Transit vehicles because a part inside the side curtain airbags, called the diffuser, may not have been crimped correctly. In a crash, the diffuser could detach from the inflator, and the curtain airbag may not inflate the way it is supposed to.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V415000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020 Escape and Transit vehicles. The affected vehicles may have an improperly crimped diffuser in the side curtain air bags which may result in the diffuser detaching from the inflator in the event of an air bag deployment.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, if the diffuser detaches from the inflator during air bag deployment, the curtain air bag may not inflate properly, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will replace the side curtain air bag modules, free of charge. An interim letter notifying owners of the safety risk was mailed August 3, 2020. The recall began January 11, 2021. Owners may contact Ford's customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 20S35.
