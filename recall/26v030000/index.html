Volkswagen ID.4 high-voltage battery may overheat

NHTSA campaign 26V030000 · Reported 2026-01-21 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

A high-voltage battery that overheats increases the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Volkswagen is recalling certain ID.4 electric vehicles. The large high-voltage battery that powers the vehicle may overheat. Volkswagen says an overheating battery raises the chance of a fire. Dealers will update the battery's software and, where it is needed, replace the high-voltage battery itself. Owners were sent notification letters, and the work is carried out by Volkswagen dealers at no charge to the owner.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer to arrange the software update, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298.
3. The update and any battery replacement are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2023-2025 ID.4 vehicles. The high-voltage battery may overheat.
Safety risk
A high-voltage battery that overheats increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will update the high-voltage battery software, and replace the high-voltage battery, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 18, 2026. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 93EA. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on January 23, 2026.
