Jeep Grand Cherokee steering shaft can come apart

NHTSA campaign 23V352000 · Reported 2023-05-18 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Jeep Grand Cherokee
  • 2023 Jeep Grand Cherokee

What's wrong, in plain English

The steering column intermediate shaft is the metal link that carries your turning of the steering wheel down toward the front wheels. On some 2022-2023 Jeep Grand Cherokee and 2021-2023 Grand Cherokee L vehicles, this shaft was put together incorrectly and can disconnect at its joint. If that happens, turning the steering wheel no longer steers the vehicle. Dealers will inspect the shaft and, if needed, replace it and the parts that hold it together.

How serious is it?

This is very serious because steering can be lost suddenly and with no warning, which can cause a crash; the notice does not order owners to park the vehicle, but you should get it inspected right away.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your Grand Cherokee is included.
2. Contact your Jeep dealer, or FCA US at 1-800-853-1403 (recall 58A), and ask for the inspection as soon as possible.
3. Have the intermediate shaft inspected and replaced if necessary — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:COLUMN
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Jeep Grand Cherokee and 2021-2023 Jeep Grand Cherokee L vehicles. An incorrectly assembled steering column intermediate shaft may disconnect from the u-joint, and result in a loss of steering control.
Safety risk
A loss of steering control increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and, if necessary, replace the intermediate shaft and assembly parts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 6, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 58A.
