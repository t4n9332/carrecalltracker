Genesis G70, G80, G90 Starter Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 24V107000 · Reported Feb 13, 2024 · Hyundai Motor America

critical How serious is it? About this label

Hyundai is telling owners to park outside and away from structures until the repair is done, because of the fire risk. An engine compartment fire while parked or driving can increase the risk of injury.

What's wrong, in plain English

Water can get into a part of the starter system and cause an electrical short. This short can start a fire in the engine compartment, whether the vehicle is parked or being driven. Hyundai will install a new relay kit in the engine's electrical box to fix the problem.

What you should do

1. Park outside and away from structures until the repair is complete.
2. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
3. Contact a Hyundai dealer, or call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460.
4. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V107000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:STARTER ASSEMBLY:SOLENOID
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2015-2016 Genesis, 2017-2019 Genesis G80, Genesis G90, and 2019 Genesis G70 vehicles. Water may enter the starter solenoid and cause an electrical short, which can result in an engine compartment fire while parked or driving.
Safety risk
An engine compartment fire while parked or driving can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. Dealers will install a remedy relay kit in the engine junction box, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 15, 2024. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's numbers for this recall are 254(H) and 018G(G).
