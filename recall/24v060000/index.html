Silverado, Sierra Tailgate May Unlatch While Driving

NHTSA campaign 24V060000 · Reported Feb 1, 2024 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

An open tailgate while driving can cause cargo to fall onto the road, creating a hazard and increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The tailgate's electronic release switch can short circuit if water gets into it. This can cause the tailgate to unlatch on its own while the truck is parked, and it may then open while driving. If that happens, unsecured cargo could fall out, creating a hazard on the road. Dealers will replace the exterior touchpad switch assemblies to fix the problem, and this repair is free of charge.

What you should do

1. Until repaired, check that the tailgate is closed and latched before driving, as the notice advises.
2. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls, then contact a Chevrolet dealer (1-800-222-1020) or GMC dealer (1-800-462-8782).
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V060000. Official notice

Component
LATCHES/LOCKS/LINKAGES:TAILGATE:LATCH
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2020-2024 Chevrolet Silverado 2500, 3500, GMC Sierra 2500 and 3500 vehicles equipped with power-unlatching tailgates. The tailgate's electronic gate-release switch may short circuit due to water intrusion, which can result in the tailgate inadvertently unlatching while the vehicle is in park.
Safety risk
An unlatched tailgate may open while driving and result in a loss of unsecured cargo, creating a road hazard and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised to check that the tailgate is closed and latched before driving. Dealers will replace the exterior touchpad switch assemblies, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 17, 2024. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N232426400.
