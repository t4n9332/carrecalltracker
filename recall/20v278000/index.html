Modified Grand Caravan fuel gasket may leak

NHTSA campaign 20V278000 · Reported May 15, 2020 · Vantage Mobility International, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak near an ignition source can lead to a fire, which is a serious safety risk. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Vantage-modified Grand Caravan vehicles, the fuel pump gasket may wear out over time and allow fuel to leak. If that fuel leak occurs near a source of ignition, it can increase the risk of a fire.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Vantage customer service at 1-800-488-9082.
3. Parts and reimbursement for the gasket replacement will be provided free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V278000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Vantage Mobility International, LLC (Vantage) is recalling certain Vantage-modified 2016-2019 Dodge Grand Caravan vehicles. The fuel pump gasket may deteriorate, allowing fuel to leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Vantage will notify owners, and will provide parts and reimbursement to owners for repair facilities to replace the fuel tank gasket, free of charge. The recall began May 19, 2020. Owners may contact Vantage customer service at 1-800-488-9082.
