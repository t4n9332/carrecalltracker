Modified Dodge Grand Caravan fuel pump gasket can leak

NHTSA campaign 20V278000 · Reported 2020-05-15 · Vantage Mobility International, LLC · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Dodge Grand Caravan
  • 2019 Dodge Grand Caravan

What's wrong, in plain English

Vantage Mobility is recalling certain Vantage-modified 2016-2019 Dodge Grand Caravan vans. The gasket that seals the fuel pump to the fuel tank can break down over time and allow gasoline to leak out. Leaking fuel near anything hot or sparking can start a fire. Vantage will supply the parts and pay a repair shop to replace the fuel tank gasket, and will reimburse owners who have the work done.

How serious is it?

A fuel leak that can lead to a fire is a serious hazard, so get this repaired as soon as possible and stop driving the van if you smell gasoline or see fuel under it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is covered.
2. Call Vantage Mobility customer service at 1-800-488-9082 to arrange the parts and the repair shop.
3. Have the fuel tank gasket replaced — parts and repair costs are covered by Vantage.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Vantage Mobility International, LLC (Vantage) is recalling certain Vantage-modified 2016-2019 Dodge Grand Caravan vehicles. The fuel pump gasket may deteriorate, allowing fuel to leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Vantage will notify owners, and will provide parts and reimbursement to owners for repair facilities to replace the fuel tank gasket, free of charge. The recall began May 19, 2020. Owners may contact Vantage customer service at 1-800-488-9082.
