Chevrolet and GMC trucks, SUVs may lose brake assist

NHTSA campaign 19V645000 · Reported 2019-09-06 · General Motors LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Chevrolet Silverado 1500
  • 2018 Chevrolet Tahoe
  • 2018 GMC Sierra 1500
  • 2018 GMC Yukon

What's wrong, in plain English

The pump that provides the suction your power brakes rely on can get weaker as the miles add up. As it fades, the brake pedal gets harder to push and the vehicle takes longer to stop. The brakes still work, but you have to press much harder. Dealers will reprogram the brake control computer at no charge. Repairs began October 8, 2019, under GM recall number N192268490.

How serious is it?

This is serious because a longer stopping distance can cause a crash, though the change comes on gradually rather than all at once, so drive with extra following distance and get the free reprogramming done soon.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall N192268490 covers your truck or SUV.
2. Make a dealer appointment for the free brake control module reprogramming.
3. Call Chevrolet at 1-800-630-2438, GMC at 1-800-462-8782, or Cadillac at 1-800-458-8006 with questions.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:POWER ASSIST:VACUUM
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2015-2017 Cadillac Escalade, 2014-2018 Chevrolet Silverado, GMC Sierra, 2015-2018 Chevrolet Suburban, Tahoe, GMC Yukon vehicles. The amount of vacuum created by the vacuum pump may decrease over time.
Safety risk
As the vacuum level drops, the brake assist decreases, increasing braking effort, extending the distance required to stop the vehicle, thereby increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will reprogram the Electronic Brake Control Module, free of charge. The recall began October 8, 2019. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-630-2438, Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N192268490.
