Chevrolet Silverado, GMC Sierra 2500 Spare Tire Can Disable ABS

NHTSA campaign 21V578000 · Reported 2021-07-29 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Without full ABS control of the rear wheels, braking may not work the way it should, which raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Chevrolet Silverado 2500 and GMC Sierra 2500 trucks are built with a 17-inch spare tire along with either 20-inch or 18-inch accessory road tires. If the spare tire is used together with those larger tires, the anti-lock braking system may not be able to control the rear wheels the way it normally would. This mismatch affects how the truck's brakes respond on the road.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is included.
2. Contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782 to have the correct spare tire and label installed.
3. The repair is free.

Affected vehicles (4)

  • 2020 GMC Sierra 2500
  • 2021 Chevrolet Silverado 2500

Official notice

Component
TIRES:TEMPORARY/EMERGENCY SPARE TIRE
Summary
General Motors, LLC. (GM) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Chevrolet Silverado 2500 and GMC Sierra 2500 vehicles equipped with a 17" spare tire (RPO ZHQ or ZXT) and either the 20" accessory road tire (RPO REM) or the 18" accessory road tire (RPO Q2V). Operating the spare tire with other 20" or 18" tires can prevent the Anti-Lock Braking System (ABS) from engaging.
Safety risk
An ABS system that cannot control the rear wheels independently increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will provide the appropriate spare tire and apply a new spare tire information label, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on October 18, 2021. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020; GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N212329780.
