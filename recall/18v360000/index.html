Chevrolet Corvette ZR1 airbags can stop working entirely

NHTSA campaign 18V360000 · Reported 2018-05-31 · General Motors LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Chevrolet Corvette

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2019 Corvette ZR1 cars, braking hard or accelerating hard can push the module that detects crashes into a fault state. Once that happens, it stops watching for crashes and will not fire the airbags if you have one. Given how these cars are driven, the fault can happen during normal spirited driving. Chevrolet dealers will install updated software in the crash-sensing module so hard braking and acceleration no longer trip it.

How serious is it?

Serious — the airbags may not deploy at all in a crash, but the notice does not instruct owners to stop driving, so you can drive while you set up the free software fix.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your ZR1 is affected. 2. Call a Chevrolet dealer, or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 (recall 18195), to schedule service. 3. Have the crash-sensing module reprogrammed — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS: AIR BAG/RESTRAINT CONTROL MODULE
Summary
General Motors LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019 Chevrolet Corvette ZR1 vehicles. Hard braking or acceleration may cause the sensing diagnostic module (SDM) to enter a fault state. As a result, the SDM will not provide crash sensing or deploy the necessary air bags in the event of a crash.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, if the air bags do not deploy as designed, the occupants have an increased risk of injury.
Remedy
GM will notify owners, and dealers will reprogram the SDM with updated software, free of charge. The recall began on June 29, 2018. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is 18195.
