Ram 1500 Spare Tire Size Does Not Match Label

NHTSA campaign 19V448000 · Reported Jun 13, 2019 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Under-inflating or over-inflating the spare tire because of the mismatched label raises the risk of a crash if the spare tire needs to be used. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Ram 1500 trucks may have a spare tire that is a different size than what is listed on the tire information label inside the vehicle. The label also lists the wrong inflation pressure for the spare that was actually included. Because of this mismatch, a driver following the label's instructions could put the wrong amount of air into the spare tire, which increases the risk of a crash if that tire is used.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Chrysler dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, to get the correct-size spare tire.
3. The replacement spare tire is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V448000. Official notice

Component
TIRES:TEMPORARY/EMERGENCY SPARE TIRE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Ram 1500 trucks. The spare tire provided may differ in size and required inflation pressure from the information on the tire placard label. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
The driver, referencing the tire placard label, may incorrectly inflate the spare tire, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler has notified owners, and dealers will replace the incorrect size spare tire with the correct size spare tire, free of charge. The recall began June 27, 2019. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V67.
