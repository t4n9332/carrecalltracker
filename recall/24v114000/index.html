Honda Odyssey and Acura RDX steering can rust, bind

NHTSA campaign 24V114000 · Reported 2024-02-15 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Honda Odyssey
  • 2024 Acura RDX
  • 2024 Honda Odyssey

What's wrong, in plain English

The steering gearbox on these vehicles may not have been tightened properly when it was put together. A gearbox that is not tight lets water get inside, and the water causes rust. Rusted gears can start to bind, which means the steering may feel stiff or may not respond the way it should while you are driving. Honda dealers will replace the entire electric power steering rack on affected vehicles, free of charge to the owner.

How serious is it?

This is serious because losing steering control raises the risk of a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get the free repair scheduled right away and have the vehicle checked if the steering feels stiff or unusual.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included in recall 24V114000 (Honda numbers LHU and FHT).
2. Contact your Honda or Acura dealer to schedule replacement of the electric power steering rack, free of charge.
3. Report any stiff or unusual steering to the dealer; Honda customer service is 1-888-234-2138.

Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Odyssey and 2024 Acura RDX vehicles. The steering gear box assembly may not have been tightened properly, allowing water to enter the assembly and cause corrosion and binding gears.
Safety risk
Corroded and binding steering gears can result in a loss of steering control and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the electric power steering rack, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 4, 2024. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's numbers for this recall are LHU and FHT.
