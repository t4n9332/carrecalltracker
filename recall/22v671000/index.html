Nissan Frontier and Titan Transmission Rollaway Risk

NHTSA campaign 22V671000 · Reported 2022-09-07 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A vehicle that rolls away on its own increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Nissan Frontier and Titan trucks, the part inside the transmission that's supposed to hold the vehicle in place when you shift into park may not engage the way it should. Because of this, even after you think you've put the truck in park, the vehicle may not actually be held still, and it could roll away on its own while nobody is in it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is affected.
2. Until the repair is done, apply the parking brake every time you park.
3. Contact a Nissan dealer at 1-800-867-7669; the repair is free.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2020 Nissan Titan
  • 2022 Nissan Frontier

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020-2023 Titan, 2020-2022 Frontier, and 2023 Z vehicles. The transmission parking pawl may not engage when the vehicle is shifted into park, which can result in a vehicle rollaway.
Safety risk
A vehicle rollaway increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Owners are advised to apply the parking brake every time they park their vehicle. Dealers will perform the applicable repairs below, free of charge:  Model Year 2020-2022 Frontier vehicles: reprogram Transmission Control Module (TCM)  Model Year 2020-2022 Titan vehicles: reprogram TCM and Engine Control Module (ECM)  Model Year 2023 Titan vehicles: reprogram TCM  Model Year 2023 Z vehicles: reprogram TCM. Owner notification letters were mailed October 28, 2022. Owners may contact Nissan's customer service at 1-800-867-7669.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
