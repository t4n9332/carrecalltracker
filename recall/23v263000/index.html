Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator diesel fuel pump may fail

NHTSA campaign 23V263000 · Reported 2023-04-13 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Jeep Gladiator
  • 2021 Jeep Wrangler
  • 2022 Jeep Gladiator
  • 2022 Jeep Wrangler
  • 2023 Jeep Gladiator
  • 2023 Jeep Wrangler

What's wrong, in plain English

These vehicles have a 3.0-liter diesel engine with a high-pressure fuel pump that can break. If the pump fails, the engine can stall, meaning it can quit while you are driving and leave you without power in traffic. Dealers will replace the fuel pump, along with other fuel system parts if they are needed, free of charge. Chrysler mailed owner notification letters on October 17, 2023.

How serious is it?

Serious — a stall in traffic can cause a crash, and while the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, the free repair should be done as soon as you can arrange it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered.
2. Contact your Jeep or Ram dealer, or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and reference recall 01A.
3. Have the fuel pump replaced — there is no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Jeep Wrangler, Gladiator, and 2022-2023 Ram 1500 vehicles equipped with a 3.0L diesel engine. The high pressure fuel pump (HPFP) may fail.
Safety risk
Fuel pump failure can result in an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the HPFP and replace additional fuel system components, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 17, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is 01A.
