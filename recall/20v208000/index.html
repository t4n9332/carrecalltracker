Ram and Jeep Compass windshield wiper arms may loosen

NHTSA campaign 20V208000 · Reported 2020-04-09 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Reduced visibility increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The arms that hold your windshield wipers can work loose. If they do, the wipers may not sweep properly or may stop clearing the glass at all. In rain, snow, or road spray, that leaves you looking through a dirty or wet windshield and makes it much harder to see where you are going. The fix is simple: the dealer tightens the nuts that hold the wiper arms in place, at no cost to you.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls for campaign 20V208000 (Chrysler's number W25).
2. Contact your Ram or Jeep dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, for an appointment.
3. Have the wiper arm nuts tightened — the service is free.

Official notice

Component
VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD WIPER/WASHER:LINKAGES
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Ram 1500, 1500 Classic and Jeep Compass vehicles. The windshield wiper arm or arms may loosen, possibly causing the wipers to not operate properly and reduce the driver's visibility in certain weather conditions.
Safety risk
Reduced visibility increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will tighten the wiper nuts, free of charge. The recall began April 27, 2020. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is W25.
