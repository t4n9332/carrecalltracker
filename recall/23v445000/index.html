What's wrong, in plain English

The fuel pump in these Mercedes-AMG models may shut down on its own while the vehicle is being driven. When the pump shuts down, the engine no longer gets fuel, and the vehicle loses the power that keeps it moving. Mercedes-Benz dealers will replace the fuel pump at no charge to the owner. Owner notification letters were mailed June 14, 2024, and owners can contact Mercedes-Benz customer service to schedule the free repair.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact your Mercedes-Benz dealer or MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372 (recall 2023070012).
3. The fuel pump replacement is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V445000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2021-2023 C-Class, E-Class, S-Class, CLS, SL, GT, GLC, GLE, GLS, and G-Class vehicles. Please refer to MBUSA's recall report for specific vehicle details. The fuel pump may shutdown, which can result in a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 14, 2024. Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2023070012.
