Toyota Tundra, Sequoia Power Steering May Leak

NHTSA campaign 21E103000 · Reported 2021-12-23 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

An oil leak that causes a sudden loss of power steering assist increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain replacement power steering gear assemblies sold as service parts for Toyota Tundra and Sequoia vehicles may have been manufactured incorrectly. This manufacturing problem can result in an oil leak from the power steering gear assembly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Toyota dealer to schedule an inspection; you can also call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331.
3. The inspection and any needed replacement of the power steering gear assembly are free.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:HYDRAULIC POWER ASSIST SYSTEM
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain JTEKT power steering gear assembly service parts for 2007-2021 Tundra and 2008-2022 Sequoia, part numbers 44250-0C160, 44250-0C131, 44250-0C170, and 11250-0C121. The power steering gear assembly may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can result in an oil leak.
Safety risk
An oil leak may cause a sudden loss of power steering assist, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the power steering gear assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on February 17, 2022. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 21TH01.
