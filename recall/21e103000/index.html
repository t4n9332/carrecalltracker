What's wrong, in plain English

This recall covers replacement power steering gear assemblies that were sold and installed on Tundra and Sequoia trucks and SUVs. Some of these parts were built wrong and can leak power steering fluid. If enough fluid leaks out, the steering can suddenly become very heavy and hard to turn while you are driving. Toyota dealers will inspect the part and put in a new steering gear at no charge to you.

How serious is it?

Serious — losing power steering assist can happen suddenly and raises the risk of a crash, so get it inspected promptly even though the notice does not tell you to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck or SUV is affected by recall 21E103000 (Toyota's number 21TH01).
2. Contact your Toyota dealer or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 to book the inspection and replacement, which is free.
3. If your steering ever feels suddenly heavy or hard to turn, stop driving and have the vehicle towed in.

Official notice

Component
STEERING:HYDRAULIC POWER ASSIST SYSTEM
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain JTEKT power steering gear assembly service parts for 2007-2021 Tundra and 2008-2022 Sequoia, part numbers 44250-0C160, 44250-0C131, 44250-0C170, and 11250-0C121. The power steering gear assembly may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can result in an oil leak.
Safety risk
An oil leak may cause a sudden loss of power steering assist, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the power steering gear assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on February 17, 2022. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 21TH01.
