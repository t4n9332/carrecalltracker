Toyota and Lexus SUVs have wrong weight-limit labels

NHTSA campaign 17V831000 · Reported 2017-12-20 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Lexus GX460
  • 2018 Toyota 4runner
  • 2018 Toyota Highlander
  • 2018 Toyota RAV4
  • 2018 Toyota Tundra

What's wrong, in plain English

These trucks and SUVs left the factory with an incorrect sticker showing how much weight the vehicle can safely carry. The label itself does not break anything, but if you trust the wrong number you could put in too many people, too much cargo, or too heavy a load. An overloaded vehicle is harder to steer and stop and puts extra strain on the tires, which raises the chance of a crash. Toyota is mailing owners corrected labels free of charge.

How serious is it?

Low for most owners, and the vehicle is safe to drive, but do not rely on the printed load limit until you have the corrected label.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered.
2. Watch for the corrected label in the mail, or call Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 (recall H0Z; HLF for Lexus) if it has not arrived.
3. Apply the new label, or ask your dealer to — it costs nothing.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Toyota 4Runner and Tundra, 2018 Toyota Highlander, RAV4 and Lexus GX460 and 2017 Toyota Sienna and Tacoma vehicles. These vehicles may have incorrect load carrying capacity modification labels. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
An incorrect load information label can result in the operator overloading the vehicle and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Toyota will notify owners and provide them with corrected labels, free of charge. The recall began on January 23, 2018. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are H0Z for Toyota vehicles, and HLF for Lexus vehicles.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
