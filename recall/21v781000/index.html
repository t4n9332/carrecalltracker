Volvo VNL trucks may shift into neutral unexpectedly

NHTSA campaign 21V781000 · Reported 2021-10-06 · Volvo Trucks North America · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Volvo VNL

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Volvo VNL trucks, the computer software that controls the transmission has a flaw. While you are driving, the transmission can slip into neutral on its own without you doing anything. When that happens the engine stops sending power to the wheels, so you suddenly lose the ability to speed up or pull away — a dangerous situation in traffic, on a hill, or at a crossing. The dealer fixes it by reloading corrected software into the truck's control module.

How serious is it?

This is serious because losing drive power without warning can cause a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get the software update done as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included. 2. Contact your Volvo Trucks dealer, or Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586 (recall RVXX2104), to book the reprogramming. 3. Have the software updated — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2022 VNL vehicles. The electronic control unit software may allow the transmission to shift into neutral unexpectedly.
Safety risk
An unexpected shift to neutral can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the electronic control module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on November 01, 2021. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 1-800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2104.
