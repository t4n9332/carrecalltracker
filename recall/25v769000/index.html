What's wrong, in plain English

GM is recalling certain GMC HUMMER EV SUV vehicles because a wire in the front passenger airbag connection may not have been properly secured. This could keep the airbag from deploying as intended during a crash. Dealers will install an additional wiring assembly to address the problem.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact a GMC dealer to arrange the repair; GMC customer service is available at 1-800-462-8782. 3. Have the dealer install the additional wiring assembly free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V769000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2026 GMC Hummer EV Pickup and SUV vehicles. The front passenger air bag connection may have an improperly crimped wire, which can result in the air bag failing to deploy as intended.
Safety risk
During a crash, an air bag that does not deploy as intended increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will install a jumper harness, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed December 22, 2025. Owners may contact GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N252524530. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning November 6, 2025.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
