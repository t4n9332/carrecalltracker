Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator Clutch Can Overheat

NHTSA campaign 20V124000 · Reported Mar 3, 2020 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

critical How serious is it? About this label

Overheated clutch parts can increase the risk of a fire, and damage to nearby components can cause debris to fall onto the road or cause a loss of drive, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep Wrangler and Gladiator vehicles with manual transmissions because the clutch pressure plate may overheat and fracture. When that happens, the damage can also affect other components located near the clutch.

What you should do

1) Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2) Contact a Chrysler dealer to have the clutch system inspected and repaired; Chrysler customer service: 1-800-853-1403. 3) The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V124000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:CLUTCH ASSEMBLY
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018-2020 Jeep Wrangler and 2020 Gladiator vehicles equipped with manual transmissions. The clutch pressure plate may overheat and fracture.
Safety risk
The overheated clutch components may increase the risk of a fire. Additionally, damage to other nearby components can result in debris falling onto the road or a loss of drive, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will reroute a wire harness and inspect the clutch system, and replace components as needed. The recall began April 30, 2020. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is W12.
