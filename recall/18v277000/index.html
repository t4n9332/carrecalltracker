Ram 4500 and 5500 Brake Hose Corrosion

NHTSA campaign 18V277000 · Reported May 1, 2018 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A brake fluid leak caused by corroded fittings can lead to a loss of braking, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Chrysler is recalling certain 2018 Ram 4500 and 5500 cab chassis trucks because two brake hoses, located between the brake master cylinder and the hydraulic control unit, may have been built without a protective coating on their metal fittings. Without this coating, the fittings can corrode over time. If that happens, brake fluid can leak out of the hoses, which can affect the truck's ability to brake normally.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is affected.
2. Contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 or your dealer to have the brake hoses inspected.
3. If replacement is needed, the repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V277000. Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018 RAM 4500 and 5500 cab chassis trucks. The primary and secondary brake hoses located between the brake master cylinder and the hydraulic control unit may have been manufactured without anti-corrosion plating on the ferrules, which can allow the ferrules to corrode.
Safety risk
If the ferrules corrode, the brake fluid may leak and cause a loss of braking, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the brake hoses and replace them if necessary, free of charge. The recall began June 19, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U41.
