Ram 4500 and 5500 brake hoses may corrode and leak

NHTSA campaign 18V277000 · Reported 2018-05-01 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 RAM 4500
  • 2018 RAM 5500

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2018 Ram 4500 and 5500 cab chassis trucks have brake hoses that were built without the protective anti-rust coating on their metal end fittings. Those hoses run between the brake master cylinder and the brake control unit. Without the coating, the fittings can rust. Rusted fittings can let brake fluid leak out, and losing brake fluid can mean losing your brakes. A dealer will inspect the brake hoses and replace them if they need it.

How serious is it?

Losing braking is very serious and can cause a crash, but the notice does not instruct owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is covered. 2. Contact a Ram dealer or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 about recall U41. 3. Have the brake hoses inspected and replaced if needed — at no charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018 RAM 4500 and 5500 cab chassis trucks. The primary and secondary brake hoses located between the brake master cylinder and the hydraulic control unit may have been manufactured without anti-corrosion plating on the ferrules, which can allow the ferrules to corrode.
Safety risk
If the ferrules corrode, the brake fluid may leak and cause a loss of braking, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the brake hoses and replace them if necessary, free of charge. The recall began June 19, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U41.
