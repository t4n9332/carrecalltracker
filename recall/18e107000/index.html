Aftermarket Highlander Windshields May Cause Engine Stalling

NHTSA campaign 18E107000 · Reported 2018-11-27 · Fujian Wanda Automobile Glass Industry C

high How serious is it? About this label

An engine that stalls while driving increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain aftermarket replacement windshields sold for Highlander vehicles have a wire harness built in. Water can leak into this harness and damage the vehicle's engine control computer. If that computer is damaged, the engine may stall while you are driving. An engine that stalls unexpectedly increases the risk of a crash. This applies to aftermarket windshields, not necessarily the windshield your vehicle came with originally.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact Wanda customer service at 1-864-281-2760 to arrange an inspection. 3. The windshield replacement and any needed engine computer replacement are free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM
Summary
Fujian Wanda Automobile Glass Industry (Wanda) is recalling certain aftermarket Replacement Windshields sold for use in 2014-2018 Toyota Highlander vehicles. The windshields have an attached wire harness that water may leak into, possibly causing damage to the vehicle's Engine Control Module (ECM).
Safety risk
The ECM damage may result in the engine stalling, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Wanda will notify owners, and service centers will replace the windshields, and inspect the ECM for damage, having it replaced, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began January 29, 2019. Owners may contact Wanda customer service at 1-864-281-2760.
