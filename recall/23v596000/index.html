Ford Edge Right Headlight Too Bright

NHTSA campaign 23V596000 · Reported 2023-08-25 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

A headlight that is too bright or aimed incorrectly can create glare for other drivers, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Edge vehicles because the headlight on the front right side may be too bright or may not be aimed the correct way. When a headlight is not aimed the right way or shines too brightly, it does not meet the federal safety standard that covers vehicle lights, reflectors, and related lighting equipment. This is specific to the front right headlight assembly on these Edge vehicles, and does not involve any other part of the vehicle's lighting or safety systems.

What you should do

1. Check your vehicle's VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm it's included in this recall.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, to schedule the headlight inspection.
3. The inspection and any needed headlight replacement will be done free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023 Edge vehicles. The front right headlight may be too bright or aimed incorrectly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
A headlight that is too bright or aimed incorrectly may cause a glare to other road users, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the right headlight assembly as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 9, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23C26.
