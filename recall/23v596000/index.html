Ford Edge right headlight may be too bright or misaimed

NHTSA campaign 23V596000 · Reported 2023-08-25 · Ford Motor Company · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Ford Edge

What's wrong, in plain English

The headlight on the front passenger side may shine too brightly or may be pointed in the wrong direction. The risk is mainly to other people on the road, because the glare can get into the eyes of oncoming drivers or people walking, and a driver who cannot see well may make a mistake. It also means the vehicle does not meet federal rules for headlights. Your dealer will check the light and replace the right headlight assembly if needed.

How serious is it?

This is the least severe kind of recall — it raises crash risk mostly for other drivers through glare — and the notice places no limits on driving your Edge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your 2023 Edge is covered by recall 23V596000.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and reference recall 23C26.
3. Have the right headlight inspected and replaced if needed, at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023 Edge vehicles. The front right headlight may be too bright or aimed incorrectly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
A headlight that is too bright or aimed incorrectly may cause a glare to other road users, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the right headlight assembly as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 9, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23C26.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
