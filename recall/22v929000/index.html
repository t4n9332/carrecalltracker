Chevy Equinox, GMC Terrain passenger airbag may not switch off

NHTSA campaign 22V929000 · Reported 2022-12-15 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious for children, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving; the safest step until the repair is done is to keep children and child seats in the back seat, where they belong anyway.

What's wrong, in plain English

These Equinox and Terrain models have ventilated front passenger seats with a system that senses who is sitting there. It is supposed to shut off the front passenger airbag when a small child or a child car seat is in that spot. On these vehicles it may not shut the airbag off. An airbag that goes off in a crash with a child or infant in the front seat can hurt them badly. Dealers will replace the sensing system for free.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected. 2. Call a Chevrolet dealer at 1-800-222-1020 or a GMC dealer at 1-800-462-8782 and schedule the free repair (GM recall N222388911). 3. Have the dealer replace the passenger presence system at no cost.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SENSOR:OCCUPANT CLASSIFICATION:FRONT PASSENGER
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023 Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain vehicles equipped with ventilated front passenger seats. The Occupant Classification System may not properly disable the front passenger air bag when there is a small child or child restraint detected in the seat.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, a front passenger air bag that deploys with a child or infant in the front passenger seat can increase their risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the passenger presence system, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 24, 2023. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N222388911.
