Chevrolet Silverado, GMC Sierra 2500 tire pressure label wrong

NHTSA campaign 26V530000 · Reported 2026-08-13 · General Motors, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Driving on underinflated tires increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Silverado 2500 and GMC Sierra 2500 trucks built with the enhanced towing option and the 20-inch accessory tire and wheel package. The label that lists tire pressures may show the wrong pressure for the rear tires. Going by it can leave those tires underinflated, and it can also keep the truck's tire pressure monitoring system from showing a low tire pressure warning. Dealers will inspect, update software, add a corrected label overlay, and set the tires to the right pressure as needed, free of charge.

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls; VINs for this recall became searchable on August 13, 2026. 2. Contact a dealer to have the truck inspected and corrected; Chevrolet customer service is 1-800-222-1020 and GMC customer service is 1-800-462-8782. GM's recall number is N262561970. 3. The work is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
TIRES:PRESSURE MONITORING AND REGULATING SYSTEMS
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Chevrolet Silverado 2500 and GMC Sierra 2500 vehicles equipped with the enhanced towing option and 20-inch accessory tire/wheel package. The tire information label may state the incorrect rear tire pressure, resulting in underinflated tires and preventing the tire pressure monitoring system (TPMS) from displaying a low tire pressure warning.
Safety risk
Driving with underinflated tires can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect, update the engine control module (ECM) software, apply a tire label overlay, inflate tires to the correct pressure, and perform a tire pressure monitor system (TPMS) re-learn process as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed September 28, 2026. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N262561970. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VIN) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on August 13, 2026.
