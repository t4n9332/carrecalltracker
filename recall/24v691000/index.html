Hyundai Tucson missing glove box airbag warning label

NHTSA campaign 24V691000 · Reported 2024-09-13 · Hyundai Motor America · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Hyundai Tucson

What's wrong, in plain English

The warning label that belongs on the glove box may be missing from your Tucson. That label tells you not to put an infant or small child in the front passenger seat, because the passenger airbag inflates with enough force to hurt them. Nothing is wrong with the airbag itself — it works normally. The only problem is the missing warning. Hyundai is mailing the label to owners, you stick it on yourself, and it costs nothing.

How serious is it?

Low — the vehicle is safe to drive, but until the label is in place, keep infants and young children out of the front passenger seat.

What you should do

1. Confirm your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Watch your mail for the free warning label from Hyundai and apply it to the glove box. 3. If it does not arrive, call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 (recall 268).

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2025 Tucson vehicles. The glove box air bag warning label may be missing. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
Without the glove box label, operators may be unaware of the increased risks that an air bag poses to an infant or child in the front seat, increasing the risk of injury to occupants.
Remedy
Hyundai will mail owners the warning labels, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 4, 2024. Owners may contact Hyundai Customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 268.
