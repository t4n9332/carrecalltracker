Genesis GV70, GV80, G80, G90 fuel pump failure

NHTSA campaign 24V282000 · Reported 2024-04-19 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious — losing drive power in traffic can cause a crash; Hyundai does not tell owners to park the vehicle, but have it inspected promptly and pay attention to any hesitation or loss of power.

What's wrong, in plain English

The fuel pump is the part that sends gasoline from the tank to the engine. On these vehicles it can fail. If it does, the engine stops getting fuel and the vehicle loses power to drive — it can slow down or stop moving while you are out on the road. Your dealer will inspect the fuel pump and replace the assembly at no charge. This recall is an expansion of an earlier one, so some vehicles are being covered for the first time.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Genesis is included. 2. Schedule the inspection with a Genesis or Hyundai dealer, or call 1-855-371-9460 (recall 022G). 3. Inspection and any pump replacement are free — if your vehicle was checked under the earlier recall 23V-630, it may still need this one.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Genesis GV70, GV80, G80, and G90 vehicles. The fuel pump may fail, which can result in a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the fuel pump assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 7, 2024. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 022G. This recall is an expansion of recall number 23V-630.
