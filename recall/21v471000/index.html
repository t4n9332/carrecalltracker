Nissan Titan and Versa front turn signal bulbs crack

NHTSA campaign 21V471000 · Reported 2021-06-23 · Nissan North America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Nissan Titan
  • 2020 Nissan Titan XD
  • 2020 Nissan Versa
  • 2021 Nissan Versa

What's wrong, in plain English

The bulb in the front right or front left turn signal can crack and stop lighting up. When that happens, drivers coming toward you and people crossing the street cannot see that you are about to turn. You may not notice the problem yourself, since the bulb is at the front of the vehicle. A dealer will look at both front turn signals and put in new bulbs if they need replacing.

How serious is it?

A moderate safety issue — a turn signal nobody can see increases the chance of a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Call your Nissan dealer or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669 (recalls PM986 and PC811) for an appointment.
3. Have the bulbs inspected and replaced at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TURN SIGNAL:BULBS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Titan, Titan XD, and Versa vehicles. The front right and/or left turn signal bulb can crack and fail to illuminate.
Safety risk
Inoperable front turn signals cannot alert on-coming drivers and/or pedestrians that the vehicle will be turning, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the bulbs as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 20, 2021. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's numbers for this recall are PM986 and PC811.
