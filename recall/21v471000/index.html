Nissan Titan and Versa turn signal bulbs may crack

NHTSA campaign 21V471000 · Reported Jun 23, 2021 · Nissan North America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

A turn signal that does not light up cannot warn other drivers or pedestrians that the vehicle is turning, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The front turn signal bulb on one or both sides of these vehicles can crack and stop lighting up. This affects certain Nissan Titan, Titan XD, and Versa vehicles. Nissan is recalling these vehicles so dealers can inspect the turn signal bulbs and replace them if they are cracked or not working.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Nissan dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669, to schedule an inspection.
3. Any needed bulb replacement is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V471000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TURN SIGNAL:BULBS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Titan, Titan XD, and Versa vehicles. The front right and/or left turn signal bulb can crack and fail to illuminate.
Safety risk
Inoperable front turn signals cannot alert on-coming drivers and/or pedestrians that the vehicle will be turning, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the bulbs as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 20, 2021. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's numbers for this recall are PM986 and PC811.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
