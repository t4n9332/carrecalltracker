Polestar 3 water-damaged wiring can kill steering assist

NHTSA campaign 25V453000 · Reported 2025-07-07 · Polestar Automotive USA, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Polestar Polestar 3

What's wrong, in plain English

Water can get into the electrical connectors that join the wiring in the engine bay to the wiring in the front bumper. Over time that water damages the connectors and the wiring harness. Damaged wiring can cause a range of electrical problems in the car, including loss of power steering assist, which makes the steering suddenly much heavier and harder to turn. Polestar will replace the bumper harness and the connector between the bumper and engine bay wiring.

How serious is it?

Serious, because losing power steering assist while driving can lead to a crash, so get it repaired promptly, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included in recall RP1063.
2. Call your Polestar dealer or Polestar customer service at 1-800-806-2504 to book the repair.
3. Have the bumper harness and connector replaced — the work is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING
Summary
Polestar Automotive USA, Inc. (Polestar) is recalling certain 2025 Polestar 3 vehicles. Water may damage the electrical connectors between the engine bay and front bumper cable harnesses.
Safety risk
Damage to harnesses can cause various electrical malfunctions, such as a loss of power steering assist, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Polestar will replace the bumper harness and the inline front bumper/engine bay connector on the engine bay harness, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 31, 2025. Owners may contact Polestar's customer service at 1-800-806-2504. Polestar's number for this recall is RP1063.
