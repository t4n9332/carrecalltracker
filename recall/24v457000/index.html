Tesla Cybertruck Trim Applique May Come Loose

NHTSA campaign 24V457000 · Reported Jun 19, 2024 · Tesla, Inc.

low How serious is it? About this label

A piece that detaches while the vehicle is being driven can become a road hazard for the vehicles behind it, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Tesla Cybertruck vehicles have a trim piece on the trunk bed, called the sail applique, that may not have been glued on properly during assembly. Because the adhesive was not applied the right way, this trim piece can gradually loosen over time. If it loosens enough, the piece can detach and come off the vehicle completely, since it is only held on with adhesive.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Tesla service, or call Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V457000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2024 Cybertruck vehicles. The trunk bed trim sail applique could have been improperly adhered and can loosen.
Safety risk
A loose applique can detach from the vehicle, creating a road hazard for following motorists and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla service will apply adhesion promoter and pressure sensitive tape or replace missing applique as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 18, 2024. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-24-12-006.
