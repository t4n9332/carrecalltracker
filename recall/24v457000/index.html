Tesla Cybertruck bed trim can come loose and fly off

NHTSA campaign 24V457000 · Reported 2024-06-19 · Tesla, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Tesla Cybertruck

What's wrong, in plain English

A decorative trim piece along the edge of the truck bed may not have been glued on properly at the factory. Over time it can work loose and come off while you are driving. A piece of trim lying in the road, or flying off at speed, is dangerous for the drivers behind you and can cause a crash. Tesla will add adhesive and pressure-sensitive tape to hold it down, or replace the piece if it is already missing, at no cost.

How serious is it?

Not dangerous to you inside the truck, but it can cause a crash for drivers behind you, so it is fine to keep driving while you get the trim secured soon.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is affected.
2. Request service in the Tesla app or call 1-877-798-3752, quoting SB-24-12-006.
3. Have the trim re-secured or replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2024 Cybertruck vehicles. The trunk bed trim sail applique could have been improperly adhered and can loosen.
Safety risk
A loose applique can detach from the vehicle, creating a road hazard for following motorists and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla service will apply adhesion promoter and pressure sensitive tape or replace missing applique as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 18, 2024. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-24-12-006.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
