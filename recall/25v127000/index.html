Ford Maverick may miss low battery, losing power

NHTSA campaign 25V127000 · Reported 2025-02-28 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Ford Maverick

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2022 Ford Mavericks repaired incorrectly under an earlier recall have computers that may not notice when the 12-volt battery is running low on charge. Without that warning, the truck can lose electrical items such as the hazard flashers, or it can lose power to the wheels while you are driving. Losing drive power in traffic, with no way to flash your hazards, is dangerous. Dealers will reload the correct control module software free of charge.

How serious is it?

Serious — the truck can lose drive power while you are on the road, which could leave you stranded in traffic, so schedule the free fix promptly even though the notice does not tell you to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Maverick is affected. 2. Call your Ford dealer and ask for recall 25S13, or contact Ford at 1-866-436-7332. 3. Have the powertrain control module software updated at no cost.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:CONTROL MODULE (TCM/PCM/TECM)
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022 Maverick vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall number 24V-590. The body and power train control modules may fail to detect a change in the 12-volt battery state of charge.
Safety risk
An undetected low battery charge can result in a loss of electrical accessories such as hazard lights, or cause a loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the power train control module software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 11, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S13.
