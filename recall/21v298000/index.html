BMW 330e, M3, M340i Seat Belt Bolt Issue

NHTSA campaign 21V298000 · Reported Apr 28, 2021 · BMW of North America, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

An improperly installed seat belt assembly may not restrain an occupant as expected in a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolt that secures the seat belt assembly to the frame was not tightened to the correct specification.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a BMW dealer at 1-800-525-7417 to have the seat belt buckle inspected.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V298000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:BUCKLE ASSEMBLY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2021 330i, 330i xDrive, M340i, M3, and 330e xDrive vehicles. The bolt securing the seat belt assembly to the frame was not tightened to specification.
Safety risk
An improperly installed seat belt assembly may not restrain an occupant as expected, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the front seat belt buckle assembly connection and, if necessary, replace it, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 25, 2021. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
