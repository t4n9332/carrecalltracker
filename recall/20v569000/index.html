Genesis G70 fuel pump defect may stall engine

NHTSA campaign 20V569000 · Reported Sep 21, 2020 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

Not enough fuel reaching the engine can cause it to stall, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Hyundai Motor America is recalling certain 2021 Genesis G70 vehicles with 2.0L GDI engines. A plastic burr left over from manufacturing may block the fuel pump's jet nozzle, which can leave the engine without enough fuel.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Hyundai dealer, or Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 (recall number 005G).
3. Dealers will inspect and repair the fuel pump assembly free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V569000. Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2021 Genesis G70 vehicles equipped with 2.0L GDI engines. The fuel pump's jet nozzle may have a plastic burr remaining from the manufacturing process, possibly causing a blocked nozzle and an insufficient fuel supply to the engine.
Safety risk
Insufficient fuel supply can result in an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Hyundai will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and repair the fuel pump assembly jet nozzle, free of charge. The recall began November 10, 2020. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 005G.
