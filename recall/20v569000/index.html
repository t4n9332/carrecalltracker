Genesis G70 fuel pump defect can stall the engine

NHTSA campaign 20V569000 · Reported 2020-09-21 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

An engine that quits without warning raises the risk of a crash, and although the notice does not tell owners to park the car, you should schedule the free repair promptly rather than waiting.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2021 Genesis G70s with the 2.0-liter engine have a fuel pump with a tiny piece of leftover plastic inside. That bit can block the small nozzle that sends fuel to the engine. Without enough fuel, the engine can stall, meaning it suddenly stops running while you are driving. Hyundai dealers will inspect the fuel pump nozzle and repair it free of charge. Owner letters went out starting November 10, 2020.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your G70 falls under recall 20V569000.
2. Contact your Genesis or Hyundai dealer and ask for recall 005G, or call 1-855-371-9460.
3. Let the dealer inspect and repair the fuel pump jet nozzle at no charge.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2021 Genesis G70 vehicles equipped with 2.0L GDI engines. The fuel pump's jet nozzle may have a plastic burr remaining from the manufacturing process, possibly causing a blocked nozzle and an insufficient fuel supply to the engine.
Safety risk
Insufficient fuel supply can result in an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Hyundai will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and repair the fuel pump assembly jet nozzle, free of charge. The recall began November 10, 2020. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 005G.
