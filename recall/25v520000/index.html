What's wrong, in plain English

Some Volvo VNL trucks have a driver-assist feature that helps guide the truck's steering if it starts to oversteer. This feature may malfunction, and if it does, it can cause the driver to lose control of the truck's steering while the vehicle is being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Volvo will disable the oversteer guidance feature through an over-the-air software update, or a dealer can do it; contact Volvo Trucks customer service at 1-800-528-6586.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V520000. Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2025-2026 Volvo New VN trucks. The dynamic steering and oversteer guidance feature may malfunction and cause a loss of steering control.
Safety risk
A loss of steering control increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The oversteer guidance feature will be disabled through an over-the-air (OTA) update or by a dealer, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 22, 2025. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 1-800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2506.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
