Cadillac XT4 backup camera image may go blank

NHTSA campaign 22V868000 · Reported 2022-11-23 · General Motors, LLC · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Cadillac XT4
  • 2020 Cadillac XT4

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2019-2020 Cadillac XT4 vehicles with the optional Surround Vision feature have a video module that can lose its connection to the rearview camera. When that happens, the backup camera picture does not show up on the screen, so you cannot see what is behind the vehicle when you shift into reverse. Dealers will update the module's software, or replace the module if the update is not enough, at no charge to the owner.

How serious is it?

The vehicle can still be driven and the notice does not say otherwise, but losing the backup camera image reduces what you can see behind you and raises the chance of a crash while backing up.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included in recall 22V868000.
2. Contact your Cadillac dealer, or Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 (GM recall number N222373320), to schedule service.
3. Have the dealer update or replace the video processing module — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Cadillac XT4 vehicles equipped with the optional Surround Vision feature. The video processing module (VPM) may lose communication with the rearview camera, causing a loss of the rearview camera image.
Safety risk
Failure of the rearview camera image to display can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the VPM software or replace the VPM, as necessary, free of charge. An interim owner notification letter informing owners of the safety defect was mailed January 17, 2023. Owner notification letters were mailed February 3, 2023. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N222373320. .
