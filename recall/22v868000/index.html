Cadillac XT4 rearview camera image may go blank

NHTSA campaign 22V868000 · Reported 2022-11-23 · General Motors, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

If the rearview camera image does not display, the driver can see less of what is behind the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain Cadillac XT4 SUVs that have the optional Surround Vision feature. The video processing module may lose its connection with the rearview camera, and the camera image is then lost. Dealers will update the module's software, or replace the module if needed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your XT4 is included. 2. Contact a Cadillac dealer to schedule the repair, or call Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 (GM recall number N222373320). 3. The repair is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Cadillac XT4 vehicles equipped with the optional Surround Vision feature. The video processing module (VPM) may lose communication with the rearview camera, causing a loss of the rearview camera image.
Safety risk
Failure of the rearview camera image to display can reduce the driver's rear visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the VPM software or replace the VPM, as necessary, free of charge. An interim owner notification letter informing owners of the safety defect was mailed January 17, 2023. Owner notification letters were mailed February 3, 2023. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N222373320. .
