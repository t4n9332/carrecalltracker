BMW X3 side curtain airbag may be assembled wrong

NHTSA campaign 24V856000 · Reported 2024-11-12 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious, because the airbag may not protect you correctly in a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so you can drive while you arrange the free inspection.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2025 BMW X3 30 xDrive vehicles may have a right-side head curtain airbag — the airbag that drops down along the side windows to protect your head in a crash — that was put together incorrectly. The part that inflates it may not work as designed, so the airbag could go off late or not open properly in a crash. The dealer will inspect the airbag and replace it if there is a problem. These vehicles had not yet been sold, so no owner letters were mailed.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to check whether your X3 is affected. 2. Contact your BMW dealer or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 to schedule an inspection. 3. Have the airbag inspected and replaced if needed — free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW:CURTAIN
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2025 X3 30 xDrive vehicles. The gas generator in the right-side head curtain air bag may have been assembled incorrectly.
Safety risk
An incorrectly assembled air bag can result in delayed activation or improper deployment of the head curtain air bag, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the right-side head curtain air bag, if necessary, free of charge. The vehicles have not been sold from dealer inventory and therefore no owner letters will be mailed. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
