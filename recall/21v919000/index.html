Jeep Wagoneer center seat back may not lock

NHTSA campaign 21V919000 · Reported 2021-11-23 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

A seat back that is not locked into position can increase the risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2022 Jeep Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer vehicles, the pull strap used to fold the second-row center seat can snag on the seat trim. When it sticks, the seat back may not click and lock into the upright position, even though it looks like it is up. A seat back that is not locked can move in a crash. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for seating systems. Dealers will inspect and replace the strap.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 21V919000 applies to your vehicle.
2. Contact your Jeep dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall number Y86), to make an appointment.
3. Have the dealer inspect and replace the center-seat recliner strap — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Jeep Wagoneer and Grand Wagoneer vehicles. The second-row center-seat recliner pull strap may bind on the seat trim surface, preventing the seat back from locking into the upright position. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 207, "Seating Systems."
Safety risk
A seat back that is not locked into position can increase the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace as necessary, the rear center-seat recliner strap, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 23, 2021. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall is Y86.
