Genesis G90 and GV60 Display Screen May Fail

NHTSA campaign 25V474000 · Reported Jul 17, 2025 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

Not being able to see the speedometer or warning lights while driving increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Genesis G90 and GV60 vehicles have a software error that can cause the instrument panel display to fail. That display normally shows drivers important information, such as the speedometer and warning lights. If it fails, that information is no longer shown while driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if it's included.
2. The display software will be updated automatically over-the-air, or by a dealer; you can contact Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741.
3. The update is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V474000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain Genesis 2023-2025 G90 and GV60 vehicles. Due to a software error, the instrument panel display may fail. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 101, "Controls and Displays."
Safety risk
An instrument panel display that fails to show critical safety information, such as the speedometer or warning lights, increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The instrument panel display software will be updated over-the-air (OTA) or by a dealer, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 22, 2025. Owners may contact Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741. Hyundai's number for this recall is 027G.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
