Hyundai and Genesis front seat belt anchors may detach

NHTSA campaign 26V218000 · Reported 2026-04-06 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

A seat belt that fails in a crash greatly increases injury risk, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving the vehicle.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Hyundai Ioniq 6, Santa Fe, Santa Fe Hybrid and Genesis G90 vehicles, the anchor that holds the driver's or front passenger's seat belt to the car can come loose and detach. If that happens, the seat belt cannot hold the person in place during a crash, so it will not protect them the way it should. Dealers will inspect the anchors and either reinforce or replace them at no charge.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — these VINs became searchable on April 8, 2026. 2. Call your dealer, or Hyundai at 1-855-371-9460 or Genesis at 844-340-9741, referencing recall 298 or 032G. 3. Get the free inspection and repair.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:ANCHORAGE
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2023-2025 Hyundai Ioniq 6, 2023-2026 Genesis G90, 2024-2026 Hyundai Santa Fe, and Hyundai Santa Fe Hybrid vehicles. The driver and passenger seat belt anchors may detach.
Safety risk
A detached seat belt anchor will not adequately restrain the seat occupant, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and reinforce or replace the seat belt anchors, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning May 29, 2026. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 and Genesis customer service at 844-340-9741. Hyundai's numbers for this recall are 298 and 032G. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov April 8, 2026.
