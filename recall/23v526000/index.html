Hyundai SUVs and sedans: oil pump controller can overheat

NHTSA campaign 23V526000 · Reported 2023-07-27 · Hyundai Motor America · critical severity

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Hyundai Elantra
  • 2023 Hyundai Kona
  • 2023 Hyundai Palisade
  • 2023 Hyundai Sonata
  • 2023 Hyundai Tucson
  • 2024 Hyundai Palisade
  • 2024 Hyundai Tucson

What's wrong, in plain English

These cars have a system that shuts the engine off at stoplights to save fuel. The electronic controller for that system's oil pump may have damaged parts inside, and it can overheat. An overheating pump unit can start a fire in the car. Hyundai says park outside and away from buildings until the repair is done. Dealers check the oil pump controller and replace it if needed, at no charge to you.

How serious is it?

Serious fire risk — park the vehicle outdoors and away from buildings until a dealer has inspected and, if needed, replaced the part.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Hyundai is included.
2. Park outside and away from structures until it is repaired.
3. Call your Hyundai dealer or Hyundai at 1-855-371-9460 (recall 246) to book the free inspection and replacement.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:FLUID/LUBRICANT:PUMP
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Palisade Tucson, 2023 Sonata, Elantra, and Kona vehicles. The electronic controller for the Idle Stop & Go oil pump assembly may contain damaged electrical components that can cause the pump controller to overheat.
Safety risk
An electric oil pump assembly that overheats increases the risk of a vehicle fire.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete. Dealers will inspect and replace the electric oil pump controller, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed starting September 7, 2023. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 246.
