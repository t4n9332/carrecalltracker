What's wrong, in plain English

On some Polestar 3 hybrid electric vehicles, changes in the 12-volt electrical system may damage a part called the High to Low Voltage Converter Module. A damaged module can cause the vehicle to suddenly lose drive power.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
Contact a Polestar dealer, or call Polestar customer service at 1-800-806-2504, to have the module replaced.
The replacement is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V940000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM
Summary
Polestar Automotive USA, Inc. (Polestar) is recalling certain 2025 Polestar 3 hybrid electric vehicles. Fluctuations in the 12-volt system may have damaged the High to Low Voltage Converter Module (HLCM).
Safety risk
A damaged HLCM reduces the system's ability to convert energy, causing a sudden loss in drive power and increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the High to Low Voltage Converter Module (HLCM), free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 17, 2025. Owners may contact Polestar customer service at 1-800-806-2504. Polestar's number for this recall is RP1043.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
