Corvette Seat Belts May Lock Out of Reach

NHTSA campaign 24V418000 · Reported 2024-06-06 · General Motors, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

A seat belt that can't be reached can't restrain the occupant, which increases the risk of injury in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2024-2025 Chevrolet Corvette vehicles have a driver seat belt, a passenger seat belt, or both, with a retractor that may lock while the belt is in its stowed position. When this happens, the seat belt becomes inaccessible, meaning the occupant cannot pull it out to buckle up.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Chevrolet dealer, or call customer service at 1-800-222-1020.
3. The seat belt retractor replacement is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:RETRACTOR
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Chevrolet Corvette vehicles. The driver and/or passenger seat belt retractors may lock in a stowed position and become inaccessible to occupants.
Safety risk
An inaccessible seat belt that cannot restrain an occupant, increases the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver and/or passenger seat belt retractors, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 19, 2024. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N242441390.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
