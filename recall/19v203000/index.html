Chrysler 300, Charger and Challenger dash warning lights may fail

NHTSA campaign 19V203000 · Reported 2019-03-14 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

There is no order to stop driving, but missing a warning light means you may not learn about a problem until it becomes dangerous, which raises the risk of a crash.

What's wrong, in plain English

The instrument cluster is the set of gauges and warning lights right in front of the steering wheel. On these cars its software can fail to light up the warnings, so a problem with one of the car's systems might never show on the dash. You could be driving with a fault and have no idea. Federal rules require those warnings to work, so these cars do not meet the standard. A dealer will update the cluster software.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is included.
2. Call a Chrysler or Dodge dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall V32), to schedule the work.
3. Have the dealer update the instrument cluster software — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Dodge Challenger and Charger and Chrysler 300 vehicles. The instrument cluster may not properly illuminate driver warnings. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 101, "Control and Displays."
Safety risk
Failure of the instrument cluster to properly illuminate may prevent the driver from receiving warnings about the vehicle's systems, increasing the risk of crash.
Remedy
Chrysler has notified owners, and dealers will update the instrument cluster software, free of charge. The recall began April 8, 2019. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is V32.
