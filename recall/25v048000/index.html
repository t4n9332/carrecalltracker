Ford Super Duty, Expedition and Corsair backup camera can fail

NHTSA campaign 25V048000 · Reported 2025-01-31 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity

What's wrong, in plain English

The camera that shows what is behind you when you back up may go blank or show a scrambled picture. When that happens you cannot see people, pets or objects behind the vehicle on the screen, which makes backing up riskier. A dealer will check the camera and replace it if needed, at no charge. Ford mailed letters to owners in September 2025, so you may already have one.

How serious is it?

Moderate: the vehicle is otherwise fine to drive, but turn and look over your shoulder and use your mirrors when reversing until the camera is fixed.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is covered (Ford's recall number is 25S05).
2. Contact your Ford or Lincoln dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, to schedule the work.
3. Have the rearview camera replaced. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 Expedition, 2021-2022 Super Duty F-250, F-350, F-450, F-550, F-600, and 2020-2022 Lincoln Corsair vehicles. The rearview camera may display a blank or distorted image.
Safety risk
Rearview camera failure can reduce the driver's view of what is behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the rearview camera as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 18, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S05.
