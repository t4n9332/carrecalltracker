Infiniti QX30 seat belt anchor bolt wrongly installed

NHTSA campaign 18V355000 · Reported 2018-05-30 · Nissan North America, Inc. · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Infiniti QX30

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2018 Infiniti QX30s, the bolt holding the bottom anchor point of the right-hand seat belt may have been installed incorrectly at the factory. That anchor is what keeps the lower end of the belt fastened to the body of the car. In a crash it could pull free, so the belt would not hold the person as it should. Dealers weld in a new retaining plate and fit a new anchor bolt at no cost. Nissan reported all affected vehicles were repaired by July 17, 2018.

How serious is it?

A seat belt anchor that lets go in a crash means much less protection for that occupant, so this needs to be confirmed as fixed even though the vehicle is not unsafe to drive from day to day.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this repair is still listed as open for your QX30.
2. Contact your Infiniti dealer or Infiniti customer service at 1-800-662-6200 to confirm the work was completed on your vehicle.
3. If it has not been done, have the dealer install the new plate and anchor bolt — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:ANCHORAGE
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2018 Infiniti QX30 vehicles. The bolt securing the right-hand side lower seat belt anchorage may have been incorrectly installed during production. This can result in the lower seat belt anchorage detaching in a crash. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with certain requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 210, "Seat Belt Assembly Anchorages", number 209, "Seat Belt Assemblies, and number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
If the lower seat belt anchorage detaches in a crash, it can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Nissan will notify owners, and dealers will weld in a new retaining plate and secure the seat belt anchorage with a new anchor bolt, free of charge. All vehicles were repaired July 17, 2018. Owners may contact Infiniti customer service at 1-800-662-6200.
