Volkswagen ID.4 dashboard screens may go blank or restart

NHTSA campaign 24V344000 · Reported 2024-05-15 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Volkswagen ID.4
  • 2022 Volkswagen ID.4
  • 2023 Volkswagen ID.4

What's wrong, in plain English

A software problem can make the screens fail. The center touchscreen and the screen in front of the driver may not turn on at all, or may go black and restart on their own while you are driving. When that happens you can lose the speedometer, so you cannot see how fast you are going, and you can lose the backup camera picture. A dealer will install corrected software, which fixes both screens at no cost.

How serious is it?

Serious, because losing your speed reading or camera view while driving raises the chance of a crash, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your ID.4 is affected. 2. Call your VW dealer, or VW customer service at 1-800-893-5298, and reference recall 919A. 3. Have the free software update performed.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (VW) is recalling certain 2021-2023 ID.4 vehicles. Due to a software issue, affected vehicles may experience center displays and instrument panel displays that do not boot, or that sporadically reset. This can result in loss of speedometer information or loss of rearview camera image. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 101, "Control and Displays" and 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
Displays that do not show critical information, such as vehicle speed or the rearview camera image, increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the vehicle software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on December 20, 2024. Owners may contact VW customer service at 1-800-893-5298. VW's number for this recall is 919A.
