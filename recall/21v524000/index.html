Hyundai Santa Fe and Sonata fuel leak fire risk

NHTSA campaign 21V524000 · Reported 2021-07-09 · Hyundai Motor America

critical How serious is it? About this label

A fuel leak that can start a fire is very serious; Hyundai has not told owners to stop driving or park outside, but get it inspected right away and stop driving if you smell gasoline.

What's wrong, in plain English

These Santa Fe and Sonata models with the 2.5-liter turbocharged engine can leak gasoline at the joint where the pipe from the high-pressure fuel pump meets the fuel rail on the engine. Leaking fuel in the engine bay raises the risk of a fire. Dealers will inspect that fuel pipe and either tighten the connection or replace the pipe, free of charge. Hyundai mailed owner notification letters on September 3, 2021.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls for campaign 21V524000 (Hyundai's number 207).
2. Contact your Hyundai dealer or Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 to book the inspection.
3. Have the fuel pipe tightened or replaced at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Santa Fe and Sonata vehicles equipped with 2.5L turbocharged engines. Fuel may leak at the pipe connection between the high-pressure fuel pump and fuel rail.
Safety risk
A fuel leak increases the risk of a fire.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and tighten, or replace the fuel pipe as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on September 3, 2021. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 207.
