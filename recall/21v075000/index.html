Ford Bronco Sport rear subframe bolts loose or missing

NHTSA campaign 21V075000 · Reported 2021-02-16 · Ford Motor Company · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford Bronco Sport

What's wrong, in plain English

The rear subframe is the metal cradle underneath the back of the vehicle that holds the rear suspension in place. On some of these SUVs, the bolts that fasten that assembly may be loose or missing entirely. If those bolts are not tight, the rear end may not stay where it should, which can change how the vehicle steers and handles. The back of the vehicle may also hold up poorly if someone hits you from behind.

How serious is it?

This can affect handling and raise crash risk, and it can also mean more injury in a rear-end collision, so schedule the free inspection promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Contact a Ford dealer for the inspection, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 21S04). 3. The dealer tightens or replaces the rear suspension bolts at no cost.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 Bronco Sport vehicles. The rear suspension subframe assembly may have loose or missing bolts.
Safety risk
Loose or missing bolts may affect the vehicle's handling and control, increasing the risk of a crash. It could also reduce the vehicle's performance in a rear impact crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the rear suspension bolts, and secure or replace the bolts, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began March 10, 2021. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21S04.
