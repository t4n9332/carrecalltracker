Dodge Durango Rear Spoiler May Detach

NHTSA campaign 23V115000 · Reported Feb 23, 2023 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

A detached spoiler becomes a hazard on the road and raises the chance of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The rear spoiler on some Durango SUVs may not be lined up correctly with the roof. This misalignment can cause the spoiler to get damaged simply from normal use of the liftgate, opening and closing it over time. If the spoiler becomes damaged, it can eventually come loose and detach from the vehicle while driving, leaving a piece of the vehicle in the road for other drivers to encounter.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected. 2. Contact a Chrysler dealer, or call Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. 3. The inspection and any spoiler replacement are free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V115000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:HATCHBACK/LIFTGATE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Dodge Durango vehicles. The rear spoiler may not be aligned properly with the roof, resulting in damage to the spoiler during liftgate use.
Safety risk
A damaged rear spoiler can detach and become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the rear spoiler, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 9, 2023. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 20A. This recall is an expansion of recall number 21V-842.
