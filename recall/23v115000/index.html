Dodge Durango rear spoiler can come loose

NHTSA campaign 23V115000 · Reported 2023-02-23 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Not an immediate danger to you inside the car and the notice does not tell you to stop driving, but a spoiler that falls off can cause a crash for drivers behind you, so get it looked at soon.

What's wrong, in plain English

The spoiler across the back roof of your Durango may not have been lined up correctly with the roof. Each time the rear hatch is opened and closed, that misalignment can damage the spoiler. A damaged spoiler can eventually break free and fall off while you are driving, landing in the road behind you where another driver could hit it. A dealer will inspect the spoiler and put on a new one if needed.

What you should do

Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
Call your Dodge dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall 20A), to book the inspection.
Have the spoiler inspected and replaced if needed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:HATCHBACK/LIFTGATE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Dodge Durango vehicles. The rear spoiler may not be aligned properly with the roof, resulting in damage to the spoiler during liftgate use.
Safety risk
A damaged rear spoiler can detach and become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the rear spoiler, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 9, 2023. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 20A. This recall is an expansion of recall number 21V-842.
