Genesis GV80, G80, GV70, GV60 seat belt part may explode

NHTSA campaign 23V094000 · Reported Feb 16, 2023 · Hyundai Motor America

high How serious is it? About this label

If one of these parts explodes it can throw metal fragments into the vehicle and strike the people inside, causing injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Hyundai is recalling certain Genesis GV80, G80, GV70 and GV60 vehicles. In a crash, the part that tightens the front seat belt can explode when it goes off. This can affect the driver side belt, the passenger side belt, or both. Dealers will secure these parts with a cap. This recall replaces and expands an earlier recall, so vehicles that were already repaired under that earlier one need the new repair as well.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Genesis dealer, or call Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 (Hyundai's recall number is 012G); the repair is free of charge.
3. GV60 owners were sent an interim letter about the safety risk and will receive a second notice once the final repair is available.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V094000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2020-2023 Genesis GV80, 2021-2023 Genesis G80, 2022-2023 Genesis GV70, and 2023 Genesis GV60 vehicles. In the event of a crash, the front driver-side and/or passenger-side seat belt pretensioners may explode upon deployment.
Safety risk
An exploding seat belt pretensioner can project metal fragments into the vehicle, strike vehicle occupants, and result in injury.
Remedy
Dealers will secure the seat belt pretensioners with a cap, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 14, 2023. For GV60 owners, Interim notification letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, were mailed April 14, 2023. A second notice will be sent once the final remedy is available. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 012G. This recall supersedes and expands recall number 21V-796. Vehicles previously repaired under recall 21V-796 will need to have the new remedy completed.
