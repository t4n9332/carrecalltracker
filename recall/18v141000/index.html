Ford Taurus key can be removed outside of Park

NHTSA campaign 18V141000 · Reported Mar 1, 2018 · Ford Motor Company

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Taurus sedans that use a mechanical key ignition. On these cars, the key may come out of the ignition even when the transmission is not in Park. Federal theft-protection rules require the key to stay in place unless the vehicle is in Park, so these cars do not meet the standard. Dealers will replace the shifter assembly free of charge. The recall began May 11, 2018.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, to have the shifter assembly replaced.
3. The replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 18V141000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:IGNITION
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2017-2018 Ford Taurus vehicles equipped with mechanical key ignition systems. The keys for these vehicles may be able to be removed from the ignition when transmission is not in the PARK position. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 114, "Theft Protection."
Safety risk
If the key is removed from the ignition while the vehicle is not in the PARK position and the parking brake is not applied, the vehicle may roll away, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will replace the shifter assembly, free of charge. The recall began May 11, 2018. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 18C02.
