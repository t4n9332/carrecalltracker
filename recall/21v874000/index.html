Audi A4 and A5 passenger airbag may switch off

NHTSA campaign 21V874000 · Reported 2021-11-10 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. · high severity

What's wrong, in plain English

The front passenger seat has a sensor mat that tells the car whether someone is sitting there, so the passenger airbag knows when to work. On these cars, the cable running between the seat heater and that sensor can develop a bad connection. The car then thinks something is broken and shuts the passenger airbag off, meaning it would not inflate in a crash. If your car was already fixed under the earlier 2019 recall, it still needs this new repair.

How serious is it?

Serious: the car is safe to drive, but a front passenger could be left without airbag protection in a crash, so have it repaired promptly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your car is covered.
2. Contact your Audi dealer or Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834 and reference recall 74E3.
3. Have the cable and seat heating mat or seat cover replaced free of charge, even if the earlier recall 19V-547 was already done.

Official notice

Component
SEATS
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2017-2020 A4 Allroad, A4 Sedan, 2018-2019 RS5 Coupe, 2019 RS5 Sportback, 2018-2020 A5 Cabriolet, A5 Coupe, A5 Sportback, S4 Sedan, S5 Cabriolet, S5 Coupe, and Audi S5 Sportback vehicles. The cable connecting the seat heater to the Passenger Occupant Detection System (PODS) may have a contact fault, causing the software to misdiagnose a malfunction and disable the passenger air bag.
Safety risk
A disabled passenger air bag increases the risk of injury in the event of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the connecting cable and either the heating mat or entire seat cover, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 7, 2022. Owners may contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 74E3. This recall is an expansion of Recall 19V-547 (74D9). Vehicles previously repaired under 19V-547 will need to return for the new remedy.
