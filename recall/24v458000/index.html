What's wrong, in plain English

The label on your Rivian R1S or R1T that shows the vehicle's maximum weight capacity has the wrong number printed on it. Because that number is incorrect, the vehicle doesn't meet a federal safety rule for tire and rim selection, and a vehicle with a wrong capacity number could end up carrying more weight than it's actually built to handle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Rivian will mail you a corrected label; you can also contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4264.
3. The replacement label is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V458000. Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC. (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022-2023 R1S and R1T vehicles. The maximum vehicle capacity weight value on the tire placard label is incorrect. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
A vehicle with an incorrect maximum capacity weight value may be overloaded, which can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian will mail owners a corrected overlay tire placard label, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 8, 2024. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4264. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1491.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
