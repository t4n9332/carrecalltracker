BMW, MINI Hybrid Battery Debris Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 20V601000 · Reported 2020-09-30 · BMW of North America, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

A short-circuit in the battery increases the risk of fire or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain plug-in hybrid BMW and MINI models, along with the BMW i8, may have debris that got into one or more cells of the high-voltage hybrid battery during manufacturing. This debris can cause a short-circuit inside the battery. A short-circuit like this raises the chance of a fire starting in the vehicle, or of someone being injured.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Until the repair is done, do not charge the vehicle, and avoid manual mode, sport mode, or the shift paddles.
3. Contact a BMW dealer at 1-800-525-7417; the battery inspection and any needed replacement are free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020-2021 530e, 530e xDrive, 530e iPerformance and X3 xDrive30e and MINI Cooper Countryman All4 SE, 2020 BMW i8, and 2021 330e, 330e xDrive, 745Le xDrive and X5 xDrive45e vehicles. Debris may have entered one or more of the hybrid battery cells during their production.
Safety risk
The debris could result in a short-circuit, increasing the risk of fire or injury.
Remedy
BMW will inspect and, if necessary, replace the battery module free of charge. The remedy is currently being developed. Until the remedy is completed, drivers are instructed to not charge their vehicles. Drivers are instructed to not drive in manual mode, sport mode, and to not use the shift paddles. Owners were notified of the safety risk October 23, 2020. The recall began November 24, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
