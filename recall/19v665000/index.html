Mercedes Sprinter Hood May Fly Open While Driving

NHTSA campaign 19V665000 · Reported 2019-09-18 · Daimler Vans USA, LLC · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2018 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 1500
  • 2018 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 2500
  • 2018 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 3500
  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 1500
  • 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2018-2019 Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vans, the screw holding the upper hood catch may not have been tightened enough at the factory. Because of that, speed, wind, and rough roads can cause the hood to pop open while you are driving, which blocks your view of the road ahead. A dealer will check that screw connection and tighten it if needed, at no cost to you. This repair has been available since November 2019.

How serious is it?

Serious: a hood flying open at speed can block your view with no warning and lead to a crash, so get this checked quickly even though the notice does not tell you to park the van.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your van is affected. 2. Contact a Sprinter dealer or Daimler Vans USA at 1-877-762-8267 and reference recall VS3HAUBOSS. 3. Have the hood catch screw inspected and tightened — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
LATCHES/LOCKS/LINKAGES:HOOD:LATCH
Summary
Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2018-2019 Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner Sprinter vehicles. The screw connection on the upper hood catch may have been improperly tightened. As a result, factors such as the vehicle's speed, wind resistance and road conditions may cause the hood to open while the vehicle is being driven.
Safety risk
A hood that opens while driving can reduce the driver's visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
DVUSA will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the screw connection, tightening it as necessary, free of charge. The recall began November 1, 2019. Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267. DVUSA's number for this recall is VS3HAUBOSS.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
