Mercedes EQB 300, 350 Front Axle Gear Defect

NHTSA campaign 24V331000 · Reported 2024-05-10 · Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

If the gear fails, the vehicle could roll away on its own, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The welded spur gear inside the front axle electric drivetrain on these vehicles may not have been welded correctly. A weld that was not done properly can leave the gear weaker than it should be. Over time or under stress, that weak weld can cause the gear itself to fail, affecting how the front axle drivetrain holds together and functions in the vehicle.

What you should do

Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
Contact a Mercedes-Benz dealer, or call MBUSA at 1-800-367-6372.
The front axle electric drivetrain will be replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN
Summary
Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2022 EQB 300 and EQB 350 vehicles. The welded spur gear within the front axle electric drivetrain may not have been welded properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 114, "Theft Protection."
Safety risk
An improperly welded spur gear can fail and result in a vehicle rollaway, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the front axle electric drivetrain, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on August 9, 2024. Owners may contact MBUSA at 1-800-367-6372. MBUSA's number for this recall is 2024060007.
