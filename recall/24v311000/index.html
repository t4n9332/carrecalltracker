Cadillac XT5 driver airbag may not deploy

NHTSA campaign 24V311000 · Reported May 2, 2024 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

An air bag that does not deploy as intended increases the risk of injury during a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain 2024 Cadillac XT5 vehicles because the driver's front air bag cushion may tear while the air bag is deploying. If the cushion tears, the air bag may not deploy the way it is designed to. An air bag that fails to deploy properly cannot provide the protection it is meant to give the driver during a crash.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Cadillac dealer, or call Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006.
3. The air bag module replacement is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V311000. Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024 Cadillac XT5 vehicles. The driver's front air bag cushion may tear during deployment, resulting in the air bag not deploying as intended.
Safety risk
An air bag that does not deploy as intended increases the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver's front air bag module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 12, 2024. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N232439241.
