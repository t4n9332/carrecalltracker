Cadillac XT5 driver airbag may tear when deploying

NHTSA campaign 24V311000 · Reported 2024-05-02 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

An airbag that doesn't work as intended increases your risk of injury in a crash, and the problem gives no warning beforehand, so get it replaced promptly.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2024 Cadillac XT5 vehicles, the fabric cushion of the driver's front airbag can tear as it inflates during a crash. If the bag rips, it won't inflate and protect the driver the way it was designed to. You can't tell whether your airbag has this problem by looking at it — it only shows up in a crash. Dealers will replace the whole driver's front airbag module free of charge.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your XT5 is affected. 2. Call your Cadillac dealer or Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 (recall N232439241). 3. Have the driver's airbag module replaced at no cost.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024 Cadillac XT5 vehicles. The driver's front air bag cushion may tear during deployment, resulting in the air bag not deploying as intended.
Safety risk
An air bag that does not deploy as intended increases the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver's front air bag module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 12, 2024. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006. GM's number for this recall is N232439241.
