Audi Q7 Front Passenger Air Bag Problem

NHTSA campaign 21V078000 · Reported 2021-02-17 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

During a crash where the passenger air bag deploys, this can increase the risk of injury to a child in the front passenger seat. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Audi is recalling certain 2020-2021 Q7 vehicles. If a child is sitting in the front passenger seat without a seatbelt on, the air bag control module may cause the second stage of the front passenger air bag to deploy too quickly after the first stage during a crash. This fast, mistimed deployment can happen when the air bags go off to protect the people in the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact an Audi dealer; you can also call Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834.
3. The air bag control module will be updated free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS: AIR BAG/RESTRAINT CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Audi) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Audi Q7 vehicles. The air bag control module may deploy the second stage of the front passenger air bag too quickly after the first stage when the seat is occupied by an unbelted child. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
During a crash necessitating the deployment of the passenger air bag, the second stage may deploy too quickly when a child is in the front passenger seat, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Audi will notify owners, and dealers will update the air bag control module, free of charge. The recall began March 5, 2021. Owners can contact Audi customer service at 1-800-253-2834. Audi's number for this recall is 69BV.
