Subaru Ascent Tires May Lose Air Suddenly

NHTSA campaign 23V258000 · Reported Apr 7, 2023 · Subaru of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

A tire that suddenly loses air pressure while driving raises the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Subaru is recalling certain Ascent vehicles equipped with 20-inch wheels. During the wheel mounting process, the internal bead of a tire may have been damaged. A tire with a damaged bead can suddenly lose air pressure while the vehicle is being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Ascent is included.
2. Contact your Subaru dealer, or call Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614.
3. All four tires will be replaced for free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V258000. Official notice

Component
TIRES
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2023 Ascent vehicles equipped with 20-inch wheels. The internal tire bead may have been damaged during the wheel mounting process.
Safety risk
A damaged tire may experience a sudden loss of air pressure, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace all four tires, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed June 6, 2023. Owners may contact Subaru's customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRH-23.
