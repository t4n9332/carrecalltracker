Subaru Ascent tires may be damaged from factory mounting

NHTSA campaign 23V258000 · Reported 2023-04-07 · Subaru of America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

This is serious because a tire can go flat all at once while you are driving, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so you can keep driving while you get the repair scheduled promptly.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Subaru Ascent SUVs built with 20-inch wheels may have tires that were damaged when the tires were put onto the wheels at the factory. The damage is to the inner edge of the tire, the part that seals against the wheel rim, and you cannot spot it by looking at the tire from outside. A tire damaged this way can lose all its air suddenly instead of slowly going flat.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Call your Subaru dealer, or Subaru customer service at 1-844-373-6614, and mention recall WRH-23. 3. Have the dealer replace all four tires at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
TIRES
Summary
Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2023 Ascent vehicles equipped with 20-inch wheels. The internal tire bead may have been damaged during the wheel mounting process.
Safety risk
A damaged tire may experience a sudden loss of air pressure, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace all four tires, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed June 6, 2023. Owners may contact Subaru's customer service at 1-844-373-6614. Subaru's number for this recall is WRH-23.
