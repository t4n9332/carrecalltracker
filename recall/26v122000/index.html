Ford Explorer, Mustang, Lincoln Nautilus can lose driving power

NHTSA campaign 26V122000 · Reported 2026-03-03 · Ford Motor Company · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Ford Explorer
  • 2025 Ford Mustang
  • 2025 Lincoln Nautilus

What's wrong, in plain English

A valve that routes some exhaust back into the engine can fail. When it does, the vehicle can suddenly lose the power that drives the wheels while you are on the road. That can leave you slowing down or coasting in traffic with other cars around you, with little warning. Dealers will replace the valve at no charge. Notification letters went out and you can look your vehicle up by VIN now.

How serious is it?

Suddenly losing drive power in traffic can lead to a crash; the notice does not say to stop driving, but schedule the free repair as soon as you can.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — VINs became searchable March 4, 2026. 2. Contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 26S10) or your dealer; owner letters were mailed July 31, 2026. 3. Have the EGR valve replaced free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:EXHAUST SYSTEM:EMISSION CONTROL:GAS RECIRCULATION VALVE (EGR VALVE)
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2025 Ranger, Lincoln Nautilus, Mustang, Maverick, Explorer, Escape, Lincoln Corsair, Bronco Sport, and Bronco vehicles. The exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) valve may fail, resulting in an unexpected loss of drive power.
Safety risk
An unexpected loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the EGR valves, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 31, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S10. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on March 4, 2026.
