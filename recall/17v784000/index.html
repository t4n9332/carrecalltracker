Kia Sorento Side Curtain Air Bag Component Risk

NHTSA campaign 17V784000 · Reported 2017-12-08 · Kia Motors America

high How serious is it? About this label

A detached air bag component that becomes a projectile inside the cabin increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Kia is recalling certain 2018 Sorento vehicles because a component in the side curtain air bags may detach during deployment. If it detaches, it may enter the passenger compartment and act as a projectile inside the vehicle cabin.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Sorento is included.
2. Contact a Kia dealer, or call Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542, to schedule the repair.
3. Dealers will replace the driver and passenger side curtain air bags, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW
Summary
Kia Motors America (Kia) is recalling certain 2018 Kia Sorento vehicles. The side curtain air bags on these vehicles have a component that may detach during deployment of the inflatable curtain air bag.
Safety risk
If the component detaches during deployment, it may enter the passenger compartment and be a projectile within the vehicle cabin, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Kia will notify owners, and dealers will replace the driver and passenger side curtain air bags, free of charge. The recall began on December 18, 2017. Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542. Kia's number for this recall is SC159.
