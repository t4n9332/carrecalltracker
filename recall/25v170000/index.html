Tesla Cybertruck light bar can come unglued, fall off

NHTSA campaign 25V170000 · Reported 2025-03-18 · Tesla, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Tesla Cybertruck

What's wrong, in plain English

The light bar on some 2024-2025 Tesla Cybertrucks can come unstuck and detach from the truck. If it does, it can fall off while you are driving and end up in the road, where it becomes a hazard for you and for other drivers. Tesla service will replace the cant rail assembly, the piece the light bar attaches to, at no cost. Owner notification letters were mailed September 19, 2025.

How serious is it?

A part falling into traffic raises crash risk for everyone around you, though the notice does not say to stop driving the truck.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm campaign 25V170000 applies.
2. Schedule service through the Tesla app or call Tesla at 1-877-798-3752 (recall SB-25-10-001).
3. Have the cant rail assembly replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Cybertruck vehicles. The light bar can delaminate and detach from the vehicle.
Safety risk
A detached light bar can become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla service will replace the cant rail assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 19, 2025. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-25-10-001.
