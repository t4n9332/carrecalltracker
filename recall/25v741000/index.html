Jeep Wrangler, Grand Cherokee Battery Fire Risk

NHTSA campaign 25V741000 · Reported 2025-10-30 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

critical How serious is it? About this label

A vehicle fire can increase the risk of injury. Owners are told to park outside and away from structures, and not to charge their vehicles, until the repair is completed.

⚠ Park outside, away from buildings — fire risk. See all such recalls.

What's wrong, in plain English

These Jeep Wrangler 4xe and Grand Cherokee 4xe vehicles have a high voltage battery that may fail internally. This failure can lead to a vehicle fire, whether the vehicle is parked or being driven. A vehicle fire increases the risk of injury.

What you should do

1. Park outside and away from structures, and do not charge the vehicle, until repaired.
2. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
3. Contact a dealer or Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403; the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2020-2025 Jeep Wrangler 4Xe and 2022-2026 Grand Cherokee 4Xe vehicles. The high voltage battery may fail internally and lead to a vehicle fire while parked or driving.
Safety risk
A vehicle fire can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures and not to charge their vehicles until the remedy is completed. Dealers will update the high voltage battery software, and replace the high voltage battery, as necessary, free of charge. Some owner notification letters were mailed beginning December 17, 2025. The remaining owner notification letters will be mailed at a later date. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 68C. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning November 6, 2025. Vehicles that were previously recalled for the same issue under NHTSA Recalls 24V720 and 23V787 will need to have the new remedy performed.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
