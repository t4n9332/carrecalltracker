Lexus NX front headrests can come out too easily

NHTSA campaign 24V482000 · Reported 2024-06-27 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Lexus NX Hybrid

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Lexus NX and RX models, the head restraints — the headrests on the driver's and front passenger's seats — can be pulled out without pressing the release button that is supposed to hold them in place. If a headrest comes out and nobody puts it back, the person in that seat has nothing behind their head, and a hit from behind is much more likely to injure their neck. A dealer replaces both front headrests for free.

How serious is it?

Low for everyday driving and safe to keep driving, as long as both front headrests are in place and pushed firmly down.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included. 2. Contact your Lexus dealer, or Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 (recalls 24LB06 and 24LA06), for an appointment. 3. The dealer replaces the driver and front passenger headrests at no cost.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:FRONT ASSEMBLY:HEAD RESTRAINT
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2024 Lexus RX 350, RX 350h, 2024-2025 NX 250, NX 350, and NX 350h vehicles. The head restraints on the driver and front passenger seats may be inadvertently removed without pressing the lock release button. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) Number 202, "Head Restraints."
Safety risk
A head restraint that is inadvertently removed and not reinstalled can increase the risk of injury during a rear-end crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the driver and front passenger seat head rest assemblies, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed February 14, 2025. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 24LB06 and 24LA06.
