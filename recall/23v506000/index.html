Ford Instrument Panel May Fail To Light Up

NHTSA campaign 23V506000 · Reported 2023-07-21 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

An instrument panel that doesn't light up can't show important safety information, such as the speedometer or warning lights, which may increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Ford Escape, F-450, and F-550 trucks with a digital instrument panel cluster may have a module that was not soldered correctly. This can prevent the instrument panel from lighting up at all. Dealers will replace the module or the instrument panel as needed, at no cost to owners. Ford has already mailed notification letters to owners about this issue.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023 Escape, Super Duty F-250, Super Duty F-350, Super Duty F-450, and Super Duty F-550 vehicles equipped with a digital instrument panel cluster. The module may have been improperly soldered, preventing the instrument panel from illuminating. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 101, "Control and Displays."
Safety risk
An inoperable instrument panel cannot show critical safety information, such as the speedometer or warning lights, which may increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the module or instrument panel as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on November 4, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23C25.
