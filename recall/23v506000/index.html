Ford Escape and Super Duty instrument display may go dark

NHTSA campaign 23V506000 · Reported 2023-07-21 · Ford Motor Company · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2023 Ford Escape
  • 2023 Ford F-450 SD
  • 2023 Ford F-550 SD

What's wrong, in plain English

The digital display behind the steering wheel, the screen that shows your speed and warning lights, may have been soldered badly at the factory. Because of that, the display can fail to light up at all. With a dark screen you cannot see how fast you are going or whether a warning light is trying to tell you something, such as a brake or engine problem. That also breaks a federal rule on controls and displays. Dealers will replace the module or the panel free.

How serious is it?

A blank display leaves you without your speedometer and warning lights, which raises crash risk, though the notice does not tell you to stop driving, so arrange the free repair quickly.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle falls under recall 23C25.
2. Contact a Ford dealer to schedule the free replacement of the instrument panel module or panel.
3. Call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 if you did not get the letter mailed in November 2023.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023 Escape, Super Duty F-250, Super Duty F-350, Super Duty F-450, and Super Duty F-550 vehicles equipped with a digital instrument panel cluster. The module may have been improperly soldered, preventing the instrument panel from illuminating. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 101, "Control and Displays."
Safety risk
An inoperable instrument panel cannot show critical safety information, such as the speedometer or warning lights, which may increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the module or instrument panel as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on November 4, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 23C25.
