Ford F-150 spare tire may have hidden damage

NHTSA campaign 19V903000 · Reported 2019-12-18 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Ford F-150

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2020 Ford F-150 trucks came with a 17-inch spare tire that may have been damaged while it was being mounted onto its wheel. You would not see the damage just by looking at the truck, because the spare sits stored away until you need it. If you put that spare on and drive, it can suddenly lose air or even come off the wheel. Ford dealers will replace the spare tire for free.

How serious is it?

A spare that suddenly goes flat or separates from the wheel could cause a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving the truck.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your F-150 is included. 2. Contact a Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 to get the spare tire replaced free of charge. 3. Avoid using the recalled spare tire until it has been replaced.

Official notice

Component
TIRES:BEAD
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2020 F-150 trucks equipped with 17-inch spare tires. The spare tire could have been damaged when it was installed on the rim.
Safety risk
The damage can cause the tire to rapidly lose air pressure or detach from the wheel, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will replace the spare tire, free of charge. The recall began January 13, 2020. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 19S55.
