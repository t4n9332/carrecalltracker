VW ID.4 Doors May Open Unexpectedly

NHTSA campaign 24V651000 · Reported Sep 4, 2024 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

A door that opens unexpectedly while driving increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On these Volkswagen ID.4 vehicles, water may get into the circuit board inside the door handles. This can cause a door to open unexpectedly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Volkswagen dealer to schedule an inspection, or call Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298.
3. The repair, including a software update, is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V651000. Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:BODY:DOOR:HANDLE:EXTERIOR
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2021-2024 ID.4 vehicles. The door handles may allow water to enter the circuit board assembly, which can cause the doors to open unexpectedly.
Safety risk
A vehicle door that opens unexpectedly while driving increases the risk of injury.
Remedy
Volkswagen dealers will inspect and replace the door handles as necessary, and update the vehicle software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on December 27, 2024. Owners may contact Volkswagen's customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 57J9. This recall expands previous recall numbers 23V-312 and 23V-213. Vehicles previously repaired under 23V-312 and 23V-213 will need to have the new remedy completed.
