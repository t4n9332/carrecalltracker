Ford Bronco Sport and F-150 Parking Lights May Flicker

NHTSA campaign 22V686000 · Reported 2022-09-14 · Ford Motor Company · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Ford Bronco Sport
  • 2023 Ford Bronco Sport

What's wrong, in plain English

When you turn on the headlights, the parking lights may flicker instead of shining steadily. Lights that flicker make it harder for other drivers to see your vehicle and judge where it is, which raises the chance of a crash. Because of this, the vehicle does not meet the federal rule covering lamps and reflectors. The fix is a new LED driver module, and dealers have been installing it since owners were notified in March 2023.

How serious is it?

This reduces how well others can see you, especially at night, but it is a lower-level problem, the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, and the repair is already available.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact your Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall number 22C22) to schedule the work.
3. Have the LED driver module replaced — it is free of charge.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:LIGHTING CONTROL MODULE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Bronco Sport and 2022 F-150 vehicles. The parking lights may flicker when the headlights are activated. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Flickering parking lights can reduce visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the LED Driver module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 29, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22C22.
