Ford Bronco Sport Parking Lights May Flicker

NHTSA campaign 22V686000 · Reported Sep 14, 2022 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

Flickering parking lights can make the vehicle harder for others to see, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Ford Bronco Sport vehicles, the parking lights may flicker when the headlights are turned on.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Ford dealer to have the LED driver module replaced; Ford customer service can be reached at 1-866-436-7332.
3. This repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V686000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:LIGHTING CONTROL MODULE
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Bronco Sport and 2022 F-150 vehicles. The parking lights may flicker when the headlights are activated. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Flickering parking lights can reduce visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the LED Driver module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 29, 2023. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22C22.
