Rivian R1S and R1T backup camera display issue

NHTSA campaign 26V597000 · Reported Sep 16, 2026 · Rivian Automotive, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Rivian R1S and R1T vehicles, a notification can pop up on screen and block part of the rearview camera image when the vehicle is in reverse. This can make it harder for the driver to see what is behind the vehicle while backing up.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. The fix is an over-the-air software update sent automatically to the vehicle, free of charge. 3. If you have questions, contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V597000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022-2027 R1S, 2022-2026 R1T, and 2027 R2 vehicles. The rearview camera image may be obstructed by a notification when in reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
An obstructed rearview camera image can reduce the driver's view behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Rivian has released an over-the-air (OTA) software update, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed November 9, 2026. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1866. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov on November 9, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
