Toyota Sienna Modified by Braun: Steering Shaft May Fracture

NHTSA campaign 19V532000 · Reported Jul 16, 2019 · Braun Corporation

critical How serious is it? About this label

If the steering shaft extension completely fractures, it can cause a loss of steering, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

These are Toyota Sienna vehicles that were modified by Braun Corporation. Extensions added to the steering shaft as part of that modification may have been manufactured incorrectly. Because of this manufacturing problem, the steering shaft extensions could fracture, meaning they could crack or break apart instead of staying intact as they should. This could happen at the extension added to the original steering shaft.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact Braun Corporation customer service at 1-800-488-0359 to schedule repair.
3. The repair (replacing the steering shaft extensions) is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 19V532000. Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
Braun Corporation (Braun) is recalling certain modified 2017-2020 Toyota Sienna vehicles. The steering shaft extensions may have been improperly manufactured, possibly causing the extensions to fracture.
Safety risk
If the steering shaft extension completely fractures, it can cause a loss of steering, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Braun has notified owners, and dealers will replace the steering shaft extensions, free of charge. The recall began July 25, 2019. Owners may contact Braun customer service at 1-800-488-0359.
