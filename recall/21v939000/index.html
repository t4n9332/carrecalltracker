What's wrong, in plain English

Honda is recalling certain Acura RDX and CR-V Hybrid vehicles because a part in the front driveshaft, on the right or left side, may not have been heat-treated correctly during manufacturing. If that part fails, it can cause a sudden loss of drive power to the wheels, or let the vehicle roll away on its own if parked without the parking brake applied. Either situation can increase the risk of a crash or injury.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Honda dealer or call Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138.
3. The driveshaft replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V939000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:DRIVELINE:DRIVESHAFT
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2021 Acura RDX and 2022 CR-V Hybrid vehicles. A component in the right or left front driveshaft may have been improperly heat-treated, which could result in driveshaft failure.
Safety risk
Driveshaft failure can cause a sudden loss of drive power, or cause the vehicle to roll away when parked if the parking brake is not applied. Either condition can increase the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the right or left front driveshaft as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 24, 2022. Owners may contact Honda customer service 1-888-234-2138. Honda's numbers for this recall are BBY, NBW, and OBX.
