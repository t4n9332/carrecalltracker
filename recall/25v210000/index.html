Honda CB300R headlight may flicker or fail

NHTSA campaign 25V210000 · Reported 2025-04-03 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.)

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing headlight illumination while riding makes it harder to see and be seen, raising the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain CB300R motorcycles, the headlight may flicker or shut off completely while you are riding. Losing headlight illumination makes it harder for you and others to see, which raises the risk of a crash or injury. Dealers will replace the headlight assembly on affected motorcycles at no cost to the owner.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your motorcycle is included. 2. Contact a Honda dealer or Honda customer service at 866-784-1870 to schedule the repair. 3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2018-2025 CB300R motorcycles. The headlights may flicker or shut off while driving.
Safety risk
Loss of headlight illumination can reduce visibility, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the headlight assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 27, 2025. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KS0.
