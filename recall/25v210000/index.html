Honda CB300R headlight can flicker or quit

NHTSA campaign 25V210000 · Reported 2025-04-03 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Honda CB300R

What's wrong, in plain English

On these motorcycles the headlight can flicker or shut off completely while you are riding. If it goes dark at night or in bad weather, you lose the light you need to see the road ahead, and drivers around you lose an important clue that a motorcycle is there at all. Honda traced the problem to the headlight assembly itself, and dealers will install a new one at no cost. Owner letters went out in May 2025.

How serious is it?

Serious for night riding — the light can fail without warning, so avoid riding after dark or in poor visibility until the headlight is replaced.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your CB300R is affected.
2. Contact a Honda motorcycle dealer to schedule the repair, or call Honda at 866-784-1870 (recall KS0).
3. Have the headlight assembly replaced — the parts and labor are free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2018-2025 CB300R motorcycles. The headlights may flicker or shut off while driving.
Safety risk
Loss of headlight illumination can reduce visibility, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the headlight assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 27, 2025. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KS0.
