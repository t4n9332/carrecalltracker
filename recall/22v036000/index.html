GM full-size SUVs: rear driveshaft may break

NHTSA campaign 22V036000 · Reported 2022-01-27 · General Motors, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Cadillac Escalade
  • 2021 Cadillac Escalade ESV
  • 2021 Chevrolet Suburban
  • 2021 Chevrolet Tahoe
  • 2021 GMC Yukon
  • 2021 GMC Yukon XL

What's wrong, in plain English

The rear driveshaft is the spinning bar that carries power from the transmission back to the rear wheels. Inside some of these driveshafts, parts were not heat-treated correctly, which leaves them weaker than they should be. A weak driveshaft can break while you are driving, and when it does the vehicle suddenly stops pulling and loses drive power, possibly in traffic or at highway speed. Dealers replace the left and/or right rear driveshaft assembly.

How serious is it?

Serious, because the vehicle can lose power suddenly and without warning while you are driving; the notice does not tell you to park it, but schedule the repair soon.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls for recall 22V036000 (GM's number N212351030).
2. Call Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020, Cadillac at 1-800-458-8006, or GMC at 1-800-462-8782 to book the driveshaft replacement.
3. The parts and labor are free of charge.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:DRIVELINE:DRIVESHAFT
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2021 GMC Yukon, Yukon XL, Cadillac Escalade, Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Suburban, and Chevrolet Tahoe vehicles. The rear wheel driveshaft assemblies may have internal components that were not heat-treated properly, possibly resulting in driveshaft failure.
Safety risk
Driveshaft failure can cause a sudden loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the left and/or right rear driveshaft assemblies, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on March 14, 2022. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020; Cadillac customer service at 1-800-458-8006 or GMC customer service at 1-800-462-8782. GM's number for this recall is N212351030.
