BMW Motorcycle Clutch Cover Bolts May Loosen

NHTSA campaign 22V752000 · Reported Oct 7, 2022 · BMW of North America, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

An oil leak at the clutch cover raises the chance of a crash or of a fire starting on the motorcycle. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling certain S1000RR, M1000RR, S1000XR, and S1000R motorcycles. The bolts that hold the clutch cover in place were not lubricated correctly when the bikes were assembled. Because of this, the bolts can work loose over time. When the clutch cover bolts loosen, the cover itself can become loose as well, and this can allow engine oil to leak out from that part of the motorcycle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your motorcycle is included.
2. Contact a BMW dealer to schedule the free repair; you can also call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. Dealers will replace all 10 clutch cover bolts at no cost to you.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V752000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:CLUTCH ASSEMBLY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2022 S1000RR, M1000RR, and 2022-2023 S1000XR and S1000R motorcycles. The clutch cover bolts were not lubricated properly, which may cause the clutch cover to become loose and leak oil.
Safety risk
An oil leak occurring at the clutch cover increases the risk of a crash or fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace all 10 clutch cover bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 6, 2022. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
