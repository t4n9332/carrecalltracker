BMW S 1000 RR clutch cover bolts can loosen, leak oil

NHTSA campaign 22V752000 · Reported 2022-10-07 · BMW of North America, LLC · critical severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 BMW S 1000 RR

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts holding the clutch cover onto the engine were not greased properly when these motorcycles were built. Without that lubrication, the bolts can work themselves loose and the clutch cover can start leaking engine oil. BMW says an oil leak at the clutch cover raises the risk of a crash or a fire. Dealers will replace all ten clutch cover bolts at no charge to the owner.

How serious is it?

Serious, since an oil leak here can lead to a crash or a fire, so have it fixed before riding much more, although the notice does not tell riders to stop riding.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your motorcycle is included.
2. Schedule the bolt replacement with a BMW Motorrad dealer, or call BMW at 1-800-525-7417.
3. Have all ten clutch cover bolts replaced — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:CLUTCH ASSEMBLY
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2022 S1000RR, M1000RR, and 2022-2023 S1000XR and S1000R motorcycles. The clutch cover bolts were not lubricated properly, which may cause the clutch cover to become loose and leak oil.
Safety risk
An oil leak occurring at the clutch cover increases the risk of a crash or fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace all 10 clutch cover bolts, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed December 6, 2022. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
