Chevy Silverado EV, GMC Sierra EV Seat Belt Bolt

NHTSA campaign 25V015000 · Reported Jan 16, 2025 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

A loose seat belt bolt means the rear center seat belt may not restrain an occupant as intended in a crash, increasing the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some of these electric pickup trucks, the bolt that holds the rear center seat belt to the seat structure may not have been tightened enough during assembly. Because of this, the bolt securing that seat belt to the seat frame could be looser than it is supposed to be, instead of being properly fastened in place. That means the seat belt itself may not stay as firmly attached as it should.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact GM customer service at 1-888-988-7267 or Chevrolet at 1-800-222-1020 to schedule repair.
3. The repair (tightening the seat belt bolt) is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V015000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:REAR/OTHER:ANCHORAGE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2024 Chevrolet Silverado EV and 2024-2025 GMC Sierra EV pickup trucks. The bolt that secures the rear center seat belt assembly to the seat structure may not have been properly tightened.
Safety risk
An improperly secured seat belt assembly may not restrain an occupant as intended, increasing the risk of injury in a crash.
Remedy
GM will tighten the bolt that attaches the center seat belt assembly to the rear seat structure, free of charge. All affected vehicles are still in dealer inventory and, therefore, no owner letters will be mailed. Owners may contact GM customer service at 1-888-988-7267, or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N242489901.
