NHTSA campaign 26V548000 · Reported Aug 25, 2026 · Ford Motor Company

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain Bronco Sport vehicles because the tops of the engine pistons may have been made incorrectly. These piston tops can fail, causing the vehicle to unexpectedly lose power to keep moving. Dealers will inspect the pistons and replace the engine assembly if needed. Ford says additional owner letters will be sent when the repair becomes available, anticipated in December 2026.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Contact a Ford dealer about repair availability; Ford customer service is available at 1-866-436-7332. 3. Have the dealer inspect the pistons and replace the engine assembly if necessary, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V548000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:HARD PARTS INTERNAL/MECHANICAL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2026 Explorer, Bronco, Bronco Sport, Mustang, and Ranger vehicles. The engine piston domes may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can result in piston dome failure and lead to a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
An unexpected loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the engine pistons and replace the engine long block, if necessary, free of charge. Interim letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, were mailed September 3, 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the remedy is available, anticipated in December 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S61. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on August 28, 2026.
