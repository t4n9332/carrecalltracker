Ford Bronco Rearview Camera Image Sticks On Screen

NHTSA campaign 22V825000 · Reported 2022-11-03 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

A distracted driver is at greater risk of being involved in a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some Bronco vehicles with the rearview camera and 8-inch screen, the camera image may keep showing on the display even after the driver has finished backing up and shifted out of reverse. A rearview image that stays on the screen when it isn't needed can distract the driver while driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332; the software update can be done by a dealer or automatically over-the-air.
3. The repair is free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION: SENSING SYSTEM: CAMERA
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Bronco vehicles equipped with rearview camera systems and 8-inch screen displays. The rearview camera image may still be displayed after a backing event has ended. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview image that continues to be displayed after the vehicle is shifted out of reverse may distract the driver, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
The SYNC module software will be updated by a dealer, or through an over-the-air (OTA) update, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on March 25, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 22C27. (over-the-air software update)
