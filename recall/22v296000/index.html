Tesla Model S and Model 3 Screen Can Go Blank

NHTSA campaign 22V296000 · Reported May 3, 2022 · Tesla, Inc.

medium How serious is it? About this label

Losing the rearview camera image or warning lights while driving increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The computer that runs the center touchscreen can overheat while the car is getting ready to fast-charge or while fast-charging. This can cause the computer to slow down or restart. When that happens, the center screen may stop showing the rearview camera image, the gear you have selected, the windshield visibility settings, and warning lights, which can make it harder for the driver to see important information.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Tesla will send an over-the-air software update automatically; you can also contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V296000. Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:DISPLAY FUNCTION
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Model S, Model X, and 2022 Model 3 and Model Y vehicles operating certain firmware releases. The infotainment central processing unit (CPU) may overheat during the preparation or process of fast-charging, causing the CPU to lag or restart.
Safety risk
A lagging or restarting CPU may prevent the center screen from displaying the rearview camera image, gear selection, windshield visibility control settings, and warning lights, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Tesla will perform an over-the-air (OTA) software update that will improve CPU temperature management, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 1, 2022. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-22-00-009. (over-the-air software update)
