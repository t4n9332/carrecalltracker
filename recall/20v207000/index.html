Ram 2500 and 3500 accessory bed step can break

NHTSA campaign 20V207000 · Reported 2020-04-09 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC) · low severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 RAM 2500
  • 2019 RAM 3500
  • 2020 RAM 2500
  • 2020 RAM 3500

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Ram 2500 and 3500 pickups were built with an add-on step under the bed that helps you climb up. If you put weight on it from the side, the step can break without warning. Someone standing on it could fall and get hurt. This does not affect how the truck drives, steers, or stops. Dealers will replace the support brace that holds the step, free of charge.

How serious is it?

The danger here is a fall and injury rather than a crash, and the truck is safe to drive — just avoid stepping on the bed step sideways until it is fixed.

What you should do

1. Verify your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls under campaign 20V207000 (Chrysler's numbers W24 and W44).
2. Call your Ram dealer or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403 to schedule the replacement.
3. Have the bed step support brace replaced at no charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Ram 2500 and Ram 3500 Pick-up trucks equipped with an accessory bed step. In certain side loading conditions, the bed step may unexpectedly fail while in use.
Safety risk
A bed step that unexpectedly fails while in use increases the risk of injury to the user.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will replace the bed step support brace, free of charge. The recall began on July 9, 2020 (RAM 2500) and began on October 6, 2020 (Ram 3500). Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's numbers for this recall are W24 and W44.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
