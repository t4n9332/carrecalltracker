Ford Transit Connect seat belt may not hold child seat

NHTSA campaign 21V592000 · Reported 2021-07-30 · Ford Motor Company · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2021 Ford Transit Connect

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2021-2022 Transit and 2021 Transit Connect vans, the front passenger seat belt has a locking feature that clamps the belt tight so a child seat stays put. That lock can switch off too early, so a child seat strapped into that seat may not be held firmly. A dealer will inspect the seat belt and replace it if needed, free of charge. Ford mailed owner letters on December 30, 2021.

How serious is it?

This matters most to families, since a child seat that is not held tightly can mean worse injuries in a crash; the van is otherwise fine to drive, but avoid putting a child seat in the front passenger seat until the belt is checked.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your van is affected. 2. Contact your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall 21C18), to schedule the inspection. 3. Have the seat belt inspected and replaced if needed — it is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:RETRACTOR
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Transit and 2021 Transit Connect vehicles. The front passenger seat belt automatic locking retractor (ALR) may deactivate early, which can prevent the child restraint system from securing properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An unsecured child restraint system can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the front passenger seat belt assembly, and replace the assembly as necessary, free of charge. Interim owner notification letters were mailed on September 14, 2021. Owner notification letters were mailed on December 30, 2021. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21C18.
