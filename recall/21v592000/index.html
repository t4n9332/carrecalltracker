NHTSA campaign 21V592000 · Reported Jul 30, 2021 · Ford Motor Company

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain Ford Transit and Transit Connect vans, the front passenger seat belt has a feature that locks the belt in place to help hold a child seat securely. This feature may switch off earlier than it should. If that happens, a child seat installed using that seat belt may not be held securely in place. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard for occupant protection.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a Ford dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 (recall number 21C18).
3. Dealers will inspect the seat belt assembly and replace it if needed, free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V592000. Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT:RETRACTOR
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Transit and 2021 Transit Connect vehicles. The front passenger seat belt automatic locking retractor (ALR) may deactivate early, which can prevent the child restraint system from securing properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An unsecured child restraint system can increase the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the front passenger seat belt assembly, and replace the assembly as necessary, free of charge. Interim owner notification letters were mailed on September 14, 2021. Owner notification letters were mailed on December 30, 2021. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 21C18.
