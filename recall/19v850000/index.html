BMW Z4 and Toyota Supra Headlights May Fail

NHTSA campaign 19V850000 · Reported 2019-11-27 · BMW of North America, LLC

medium How serious is it? About this label

Reduced visibility from lost headlights increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The control units that operate the headlights on these vehicles may fail over time. When a control unit fails, it can cause the vehicle to lose the use of one headlight, or in some cases both headlights at once. Losing headlights reduces the driver's visibility, making it harder to see the road while driving.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a BMW dealer at 1-800-525-7417 or a Toyota dealer at 1-888-270-9371.
3. The headlight control units will be replaced free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2020 BMW Z4
  • 2020 Toyota Supra

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:HEADLIGHTS
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020 Z4 M40i, Z4 sDrive30i, 330i and 330i xDrive vehicles as well as 2020 Toyota Supra vehicles. The headlight control units may fail causing a loss of one or both of the headlights.
Safety risk
Headlight failure can reduce the driver's visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace the headlight control units, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin January 17, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 or Toyota customer service at 1-888-270-9371.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
