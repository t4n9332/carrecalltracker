Ford Expedition, Mustang Park Warning Chime Too Short

NHTSA campaign 20V263000 · Reported May 8, 2020 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

If a driver exits the vehicle without it being in park, the vehicle could move on its own, raising the chance of injury or a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In certain Ford Expedition and Mustang vehicles, the warning message and chime that are supposed to alert the driver that the transmission is not in park do not last as long as they were designed to. Because the warning is shorter than intended, a driver may not notice it in time and could exit the vehicle while it is not actually in park.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.
3. Dealers will reprogram the instrument cluster free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V263000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:GEAR POSITION INDICATION (PRNDL)
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2019 Expedition and 2019-2020 Mustang vehicles. The "Transmission Not In Park" warning message and associated chime duration are shorter than intended.
Safety risk
If the driver exits the vehicle without the transmission being shifted into park, unintended vehicle movement may result, increasing the risk of injury or crash.
Remedy
Ford will notify owners, and dealers will reprogram the instrument cluster, free of charge. The recall began May 26, 2020. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 20S21.
