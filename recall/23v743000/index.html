Tesla Model S/X May Have Wrong Airbag Installed

NHTSA campaign 23V743000 · Reported Nov 3, 2023 · Tesla, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

An airbag not designed for the replacement steering yoke or wheel may deploy incorrectly, increasing the risk of injury during a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Tesla Model S and Model X vehicles may have an incorrect airbag installed. This can happen if the steering yoke or round steering wheel was replaced with a different style of yoke or wheel. The airbag that was installed may not be designed to work correctly with that replacement part. Tesla Service will inspect the vehicle and replace the driver airbag as necessary.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Tesla Service, or call Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752, to schedule an inspection.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 23V743000. Official notice

Component
STEERING: STEERING WHEEL/HANDLE BAR
Summary
Tesla, Inc. (Tesla) is recalling certain 2021-2024 Model S and Model X vehicles. An incorrect air bag may have been installed when the steering yoke or round steering wheel was replaced with a different style of yoke or steering wheel.
Safety risk
An air bag that is not designed to function with the replacement steering yoke or wheel may deploy incorrectly, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Tesla Service will inspect and replace the driver air bag as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 2, 2024. Owners may contact Tesla customer service at 1-877-798-3752. Tesla's number for this recall is SB-23-20-005.
