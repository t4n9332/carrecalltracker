Toyota Tacoma Rear Axle Can Separate While Driving

NHTSA campaign 24V152000 · Reported 2024-02-27 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Toyota Tacoma
  • 2023 Toyota Tacoma

What's wrong, in plain English

Debris got in the way when the rear axle was assembled, so the nuts holding it may not be tight enough. Over time the axle shaft can work its way out of the axle housing. If it comes apart while you are driving, the truck can become hard to control and your brakes may not work the way they should. A dealer inspects the rear axle, tightens the nuts, and repairs or replaces any damaged parts for free.

How serious is it?

Serious — losing control or braking power at speed can cause a crash; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but get the inspection done promptly.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your Tacoma is included.
2. Schedule the free inspection and repair with a Toyota dealer, or call Toyota at 1-800-331-4331 (recall 24TB05 or 24TA05).
3. If you notice noise, vibration, or fluid leaking from the rear axle area, stop driving and have the truck towed to the dealer.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AXLE ASSEMBLY
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturer (Toyota) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Tacoma vehicles. The axle shaft sub-assembly may separate from the axle housing due to debris causing improperly secured fasteners.
Safety risk
Axle shaft separation can cause a loss of vehicle stability and brake performance, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the rear axle assembly, tighten the axle retaining nuts as necessary, and repair or replace any damaged components, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 17, 2024. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 24TB05 and 24TA05.
