Toyota Tacoma Rear Axle Shaft Separation Risk

NHTSA campaign 24V152000 · Reported Feb 27, 2024 · Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing

high How serious is it? About this label

Axle shaft separation can cause a loss of vehicle stability and reduced brake performance, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing is recalling certain 2022 and 2023 Tacoma trucks. On these trucks, the rear axle shaft sub-assembly may separate from the axle housing. This can happen because debris gets into the assembly and causes the fasteners that are supposed to hold the axle shaft securely in place to loosen or become improperly secured, which can allow the axle shaft to come apart from the housing.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is affected.
2. Contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331 to have a dealer inspect the rear axle.
3. Any needed repairs are free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V152000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AXLE ASSEMBLY
Summary
Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturer (Toyota) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Tacoma vehicles. The axle shaft sub-assembly may separate from the axle housing due to debris causing improperly secured fasteners.
Safety risk
Axle shaft separation can cause a loss of vehicle stability and brake performance, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the rear axle assembly, tighten the axle retaining nuts as necessary, and repair or replace any damaged components, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 17, 2024. Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 24TB05 and 24TA05.
