INFINITI QX60, Nissan Pathfinder Seat Track Bolt Missing

NHTSA campaign 22V111000 · Reported 2022-02-24 · Nissan North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A missing bolt can allow the seat track to move during a crash, which increases the risk of injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some INFINITI QX60 and Nissan Pathfinder vehicles may have a second-row seat track that is missing a mounting bolt. This bolt is one of the parts required to anchor the seat belt system to the vehicle. Because the bolt may be missing, these vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard covering seat belt anchorages. The second-row seat track itself may not be as securely fastened to the vehicle as it should be as a result of this missing bolt.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included.
2. Contact a Nissan or INFINITI dealer, or call Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669.
3. Dealers will inspect the seat track and install the bolt if needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2022 INFINITI QX60 and Nissan Pathfinder vehicles. The second-row seat track may be missing a mounting bolt. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 210, "Seat Belt Assembly Anchorages."
Safety risk
A missing mounting bolt may allow the seat track to move during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the second-row seat track and install a new mounting bolt as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 13, 2022. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's numbers for this recall are PC867 and PC868.
