Nissan Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60 missing second-row seat bolt

NHTSA campaign 22V111000 · Reported 2022-02-24 · Nissan North America, Inc. · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Infiniti QX60
  • 2022 Nissan Pathfinder

What's wrong, in plain English

Some of these vehicles left the factory with a bolt missing from the track that holds the second-row seat to the floor. With that bolt gone, the seat is not anchored as strongly as it should be, and it could shift or move in a crash. Anyone riding in the second row could be hurt more badly as a result. A dealer will inspect the seat track and install a new mounting bolt if one is missing.

How serious is it?

The danger only shows up in a crash and the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but a rear seat that can move is a real injury risk worth fixing soon.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is part of recall 22V111000 (Nissan PC867/PC868).
2. Contact your Nissan or INFINITI dealer, or Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669, to book the inspection.
3. Have the second-row seat track checked and the bolt installed — the work is free.

Official notice

Component
SEATS:CRITICAL FASTENERS
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2022 INFINITI QX60 and Nissan Pathfinder vehicles. The second-row seat track may be missing a mounting bolt. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 210, "Seat Belt Assembly Anchorages."
Safety risk
A missing mounting bolt may allow the seat track to move during a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the second-row seat track and install a new mounting bolt as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 13, 2022. Owners may contact Nissan customer service at 1-800-867-7669. Nissan's numbers for this recall are PC867 and PC868.
