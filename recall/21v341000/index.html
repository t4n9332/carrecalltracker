Porsche Cayenne Engine Cylinder Cracks Can Stall Car

NHTSA campaign 21V341000 · Reported May 12, 2021 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

A cracked engine that stalls or leaks oil can lead to a crash or a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Some Cayenne GTS vehicles have an engine problem where cracks can develop on the surface inside the engine cylinder. Over time, these cracks may cause damage to the engine, which could make the engine stall while you are driving, or cause the engine to develop an oil leak. Porsche dealers will replace the entire engine completely, free of charge, to fix the issue with these vehicles.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243 to schedule the repair.
3. The engine replacement is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 21V341000. Official notice

Component
ENGINE
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2021 Cayenne Turbo, Cayenne GTS, and Cayenne GTS Coupe vehicles. Cracks may develop on the surface of the engine cylinder bore.
Safety risk
Cracks may cause engine damage and a stall or an oil leak, increasing the risk of a crash or fire.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the engine, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 9, 2021. Owners may contact Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is AMB1.
