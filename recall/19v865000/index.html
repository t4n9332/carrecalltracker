Honda CR-V rear subframe bolts may loosen

NHTSA campaign 19V865000 · Reported 2019-12-05 · Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) · high severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 Honda CR-V
  • 2020 Honda CR-V

What's wrong, in plain English

The bolts holding the rear subframe, the metal structure that supports the rear suspension, were not made correctly. Over time they can work loose, and the subframe can come apart from the car. If that happens, the vehicle becomes hard to control and can suddenly quit moving. Dealers will check the bolts and either retighten them or install new ones at no cost to you.

How serious is it?

This is serious because the vehicle can lose handling or be disabled suddenly, so get it inspected right away even though the notice does not order you to park it.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your CR-V is affected. 2. Contact your Honda dealer or Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138 and reference recall R6M. 3. Have the rear subframe bolts inspected and retorqued or replaced, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:FRAME AND MEMBERS
Summary
Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2019-2020 CR-V vehicles. The rear subframe bolts were improperly manufactured and may loosen, allowing the rear subframe to separate.
Safety risk
A separated rear subframe reduces vehicle handling and can suddenly disable the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Honda will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the rear subframe bolts and retorque them or replace them, as necessary, free of charge. The recall began January 27, 2020. Owners may contact Honda customer service at 1-888-234-2138. Honda's number for this recall is R6M.
