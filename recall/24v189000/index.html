Ford Bronco child safety lock may not hold rear door

NHTSA campaign 24V189000 · Reported 2024-03-08 · Ford Motor Company

medium How serious is it? About this label

The danger is real if you carry children in the back seat, since a door opening while moving can cause serious injury, but the vehicle is otherwise safe to drive.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2023 and 2024 four-door Ford Bronco vehicles have a problem with the child safety lock on the rear door behind the driver. Even with that lock switched on, someone sitting in the back seat can still open the door from inside. That means a child could push the door open while the vehicle is moving. Dealers will check the child safety locks and replace the rear door latch if it is faulty.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Bronco is covered. 2. Until it is fixed, avoid seating a child behind the driver, or keep a close watch on that door. 3. Contact your Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 about recall 24S15 — the inspection and any new latch are free.

Official notice

Component
LATCHES/LOCKS/LINKAGES:DOORS:LOCK
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Bronco 4-door vehicles. The left-side rear door may be opened from the inside of the vehicle when the child safety lock is in the "ON" position.
Safety risk
A malfunctioning child safety lock that allows a rear door to be opened unintentionally can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the child safety locks and replace the rear door latch, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 9, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S15.
