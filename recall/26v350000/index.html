Volvo truck wheel nuts may loosen and wheels detach

NHTSA campaign 26V350000 · Reported 2026-05-28 · Volvo Trucks North America · critical severity

⚠ NHTSA advises not driving this vehicle until it is repaired. See all such recalls.

Affected vehicles

  • 2026 Volvo VNL (4)

What's wrong, in plain English

The nuts holding the wheels on these trucks can work loose, and a wheel can come off entirely. A wheel coming off can make you lose control of the truck, and the loose wheel itself becomes a danger to other people on the road. Volvo says not to drive these trucks until they are repaired. A dealer will check the wheel and hub surfaces, clean off any leftover masking material, and replace worn parts at no cost.

How serious is it?

Very serious — a wheel can come off and cause a crash, so do not drive the truck until the repair is complete.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your truck is included. 2. Do not drive it; contact your Volvo Trucks dealer or Volvo Trucks customer service at 800-528-6586 (recall RVXX2606). 3. Have the dealer inspect the wheels and hubs and replace any worn parts — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
WHEELS:LUGS/NUTS/BOLTS/STUDS
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2026 VAH, 2026-2027 VN, VHD, VNL (4), and 2027 VNR (4) vehicles. The wheel lug nuts may loosen and allow the wheel to detach.
Safety risk
Wheel detachment can cause a loss of vehicle control or create a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Owners are advised not to drive their vehicles until the repair has been performed. Dealers will inspect the wheel and hub mating surfaces, remove any masking material, and replace any worn components if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 15, 2026. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2606.Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on May 29, 2026.
