Ford Maverick tire and load label shows wrong numbers

NHTSA campaign 24V266000 · Reported 2024-04-12 · Ford Motor Company · low severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Ford Maverick

What's wrong, in plain English

The label on your truck that lists how much weight it can carry, what pressure to put in the tires, and what size the spare is may show the wrong numbers. If you go by that label, you could load the truck heavier than it is built to handle or run the wrong tire pressure. Nothing is broken on the vehicle itself, but the information is wrong. Ford is mailing owners a corrected label free of charge to put on.

How serious is it?

Low day-to-day risk and safe to keep driving, though overloading the truck based on the wrong numbers could lead to a crash, so avoid heavy loads until you have the corrected label.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Maverick is included in recall 24C08.
2. Watch your mail for the free replacement label from Ford and apply it over the old one.
3. If it does not arrive, call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332 or ask your dealer.

Official notice

Component
EQUIPMENT:OTHER:LABELS
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2024 Maverick vehicles. The tire and loading information label may state the incorrect vehicle capacity, tire pressure, and spare tire size. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 110, "Tire Selection and Rims."
Safety risk
Incorrect tire and capacity information may result in the vehicle being overloaded, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Ford will mail new labels, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 1, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24C08.
