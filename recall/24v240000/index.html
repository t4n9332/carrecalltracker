Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager lose traction control with cruise on

NHTSA campaign 24V240000 · Reported 2024-03-28 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) (Stellantis) · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Chrysler Pacifica
  • 2024 Chrysler Voyager

What's wrong, in plain English

Traction control is the system that quietly cuts wheel spin when a tire starts to slip — on wet roads, snow, or loose gravel. On these vans, traction control stops working whenever cruise control is switched on. That means the van could slide more easily than you expect in slippery conditions. Until the fix is done, avoid using cruise control when the road is wet, icy, or snowy. The dealer will update the software at no charge.

How serious is it?

Moderate — the van is safe to keep driving, but with cruise control on you lose a system that helps prevent skids, so avoid cruise control in bad weather until it is repaired.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your van is covered.
2. Contact your Chrysler dealer or FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403 and mention recall 22B.
3. Have the traction control software updated — the fix is free.

Official notice

Component
TRACTION CONTROL SYSTEM
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2024 Pacifica and Voyager vehicles. The traction control system (TCS) does not function when cruise control is activated.
Safety risk
An inactive traction control system can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the TCS software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 11, 2024. Owners may contact FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's number for this recall is 22B.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
