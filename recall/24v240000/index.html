Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager lose traction control during cruise control

NHTSA campaign 24V240000 · Reported Mar 28, 2024 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) (Stellantis)

high How serious is it? About this label

Chrysler says a traction control system that is not working can increase the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In these minivans, the traction control system does not work while cruise control is switched on. Traction control is the system that helps keep the wheels gripping the road. Chrysler is recalling certain 2024 Pacifica and Voyager vehicles because of this. Dealers will install updated traction control software at no cost to the owner. Chrysler mailed notification letters to owners in April 2024, and owners with questions can call the company's customer service line.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your minivan is included. 2. Contact a Chrysler dealer to schedule the software update, or call FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403. 3. The software update is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 24V240000. Official notice

Component
TRACTION CONTROL SYSTEM
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2024 Pacifica and Voyager vehicles. The traction control system (TCS) does not function when cruise control is activated.
Safety risk
An inactive traction control system can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the TCS software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 11, 2024. Owners may contact FCA customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's number for this recall is 22B.
