Chevrolet Silverado 1500 airbags may not deploy in crash

NHTSA campaign 22V873000 · Reported 2022-11-23 · General Motors, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Airbags not working in a crash is a serious injury risk, but the affected trucks were never sold to customers and have already been fixed, so no owner needs to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2022 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 trucks, the computer that senses a crash and fires the airbags was still in factory test mode. It was never switched on before the truck left the assembly plant. With it stuck in that mode, the airbags will not go off in a crash, so people inside would not get that protection. These trucks were still sitting on dealer lots, so dealers sent them back to the GM plant and the computer was turned on.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your truck is on the list. 2. If it shows up, contact your Chevrolet dealer or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020 and mention recall N222387870. 3. Any needed work is done at no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS: AIR BAG/RESTRAINT CONTROL MODULE
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2022 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 vehicles. The sensing diagnostic module (SDM) was left in manufacturing mode and not activated at the assembly plant prior to shipment. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 208, "Occupant Crash Protection."
Safety risk
An SDM in manufacturing mode will not deploy the vehicle's air bags in a crash, increasing the risk of occupant injury.
Remedy
Dealers returned the vehicles to the GM assembly plant and the sensing diagnostic modules were activated. The vehicles had not been sold from dealer inventory and therefore no owner letters will be mailed. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N222387870.
