Toyota Supra driver seat belt mount may be weak

NHTSA campaign 19V653000 · Reported 2019-09-12 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2020 Toyota Supra

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2020 Toyota Supra cars, the mount that holds the loop guiding the driver's seat belt over the shoulder may have been welded improperly. In a crash, that mount could be damaged and the seat belt might not hold the driver in place the way it should, which could lead to more serious injury. Toyota dealers will inspect the mount, and if it is defective the vehicle will be replaced free of charge.

How serious is it?

Serious in a crash — the driver's seat belt may not restrain them properly, though the notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Supra is included.
2. Contact your Toyota dealer or the Toyota Customer Experience Center at 1-888-270-9371 (recall K0R).
3. Have the seat belt guide loop mount inspected — any needed remedy is free.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:FRONT
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2020 Toyota Supra vehicles. The driver's seat belt guide loop mount may have been improperly welded.
Safety risk
In the event of a crash, the mount could become damaged and the seat belt may not properly restrain the driver, increasing their risk of injury.
Remedy
Owners have been notified, and Toyota dealers will inspect the seat belt guide loop mount. If the mount is defective, the vehicle will be replaced, free of charge. The recall began October 16, 2019. Owners may contact the Toyota Customer Experience Center at 1-888-270-9371. Toyota's number for this recall is K0R.
