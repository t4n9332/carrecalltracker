BMW and MINI vehicles may lose brake assist power

NHTSA campaign 24V697000 · Reported 2024-09-18 · BMW of North America, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

Very serious, since braking and stability control can fail with little warning; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but treat this as urgent and get it repaired right away.

What's wrong, in plain English

Welds inside a small motor in the brake control unit can break. If that happens, you lose power brake assist and the rear brakes stop working, so the pedal gets hard and the vehicle needs much more distance to stop. The anti-lock brakes and the stability control may also quit, which makes the vehicle harder to keep under control. Dealers will replace the whole brake unit for free.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your BMW or MINI is affected.
2. Call your dealer or BMW Customer Service at 1-800-525-7417 to schedule the repair.
3. Have the dealer replace the integrated brake system; there is no charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRONIC STABILITY CONTROL (ESC):CONTROL MODULE
Summary
BMW of North America (BMW) is recalling certain 2023-2024 X1, 2024-2025 X5, 2025 X6, 2023-2025 X7, 740i, 760i, 2024 XM, 530i, 540i, i7, 750e, X2, 2024-2025 i5, 2024 Rolls Royce Spectre, 2025 MINI Countryman S ALL4, JCW Countryman ALL4, and 2025 MINI Hardtop 2 Door (Cooper, Cooper S) vehicles. Please refer to the recall report for the complete list of models. The welds in the servomotor of the integrated brake control module may break, causing a loss of power brake assist and rear brake function. In addition, the Antilock Brake System (ABS) and Dynamic Stability Control (DSC) system may not function.
Safety risk
A loss of brake power assist or rear brake function can result in extended stopping distance. A loss of ABS or DSC can cause a loss of vehicle handling and control. Either of these scenarios can increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the integrated brake system, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 13, 2024. Owners can contact BMW Customer Service at 1-800-525-7417.
