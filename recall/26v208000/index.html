What's wrong, in plain English

Volvo is recalling certain VNL and VNR trucks because the turn signals may not work properly. A turn signal that doesn't work as it should cannot reliably show other drivers which direction the truck is about to turn. Because of this, the affected trucks do not meet a federal safety standard covering lights, reflective devices, and other equipment used to signal a driver's intentions on the road. The recall covers software used to control the turn signal indicators.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your truck is included.
2. Contact Volvo Trucks customer service at 800-528-6586 or your dealer to schedule the software update.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V208000. Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TURN SIGNAL
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2025-2027 VNL (4) and 2026-2027 VNR (4) vehicles. The vehicle turn signals may not function properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Turn signals that do not function properly may fail to indicate the driver's intention to change vehicle direction, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the turn signal indicator software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 7, 2026. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2604. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning Feb April 8, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
