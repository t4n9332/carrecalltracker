Volvo VNL and VNR trucks have faulty turn signals

NHTSA campaign 26V208000 · Reported 2026-04-01 · Volvo Trucks North America · medium severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Volvo VNL (4)
  • 2026 Volvo VNL (4)

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain Volvo VNL and VNR trucks have turn signals that may not work the way they should. The turn signal is how you tell drivers around you that you are about to turn or change lanes. If it does not come on properly, other drivers get no warning about what you are doing, which can lead to a crash. Because of this, the trucks do not meet the federal safety rule for lamps and signals. A dealer will reprogram the turn signal software.

How serious is it?

Turn signals that fail can lead to a crash, though the notice does not tell owners to park the truck.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls (these VINs became searchable April 8, 2026). 2. Call Volvo Trucks customer service at 800-528-6586 and mention recall RVXX2604 to schedule service. 3. Have the dealer update the turn signal software — it is free.

Official notice

Component
EXTERIOR LIGHTING:TURN SIGNAL
Summary
Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2025-2027 VNL (4) and 2026-2027 VNR (4) vehicles. The vehicle turn signals may not function properly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 108, "Lamps, Reflective Devices, and Associated Equipment."
Safety risk
Turn signals that do not function properly may fail to indicate the driver's intention to change vehicle direction, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the turn signal indicator software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 7, 2026. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2604. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning Feb April 8, 2026.
