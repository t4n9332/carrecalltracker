BMW X6 rear control arms may break apart

NHTSA campaign 18V680000 · Reported 2018-09-28 · BMW of North America, LLC · high severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2019 BMW X6

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2019 BMW X6 models, the bolts holding the rear upper control arms — the parts that keep the back wheels lined up and stable — may not have been hardened correctly when they were made. A weak bolt can let a rear control arm break. If that happens while you are driving, the back of the vehicle can suddenly feel loose and become hard to steer and control. All affected vehicles were still sitting on dealer lots, so no owner letters were mailed.

How serious is it?

A broken suspension part can cause a sudden loss of control, which is very serious, though these vehicles were not yet in customers' hands when the recall was issued.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm whether campaign 18V680000 applies to your X6.
2. Call a BMW dealer, or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417, to schedule the work.
3. The dealer will replace both rear upper control arms, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:REAR
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2019 BMW X6 sDrive35i, X6 xDrive35i, X6 xDrive50i and X6M vehicles. The rear upper control arm pivot bolts may have been improperly hardened, possibly causing the rear upper control arms to break.
Safety risk
If a rear upper control arm breaks, it can affect vehicle handling and control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
All affected vehicles remain on dealer lots, therefore owner letters will not be mailed. BMW dealers will replace both rear upper control arms, free of charge. The recall began September 21, 2018. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
