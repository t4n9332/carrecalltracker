Dodge Challenger and Charger Wrong Wheels Can Damage Tires

NHTSA campaign 19V758000 · Reported 2019-10-24 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A tire that suddenly goes flat at speed can cause a crash, and although the notice does not tell owners to park the car, this one deserves a prompt dealer visit.

What's wrong, in plain English

These cars left the factory with front wheels and brakes that do not fit together properly. Because of the mismatch, the front tire can rub against the steering knuckle, the metal part the wheel is mounted on. That rubbing damages the tire, and a damaged tire can lose air all at once while you are driving. The dealer will put on new front wheels, check the tires and replace any that are damaged, and check the alignment.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm recall 19V758000 applies to your car.
2. Call your Dodge dealer or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall number VB0) to book the work.
3. Have the dealer replace the front wheels, replace damaged tires, and check the alignment — all free of charge.

Official notice

Component
SERVICE BRAKES
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2019 Dodge Challenger and Charger vehicles. An incompatible front wheel and brake package was installed and may allow the front tire to contact the steering knuckle, causing tire damage.
Safety risk
A damaged tire can suddenly lose air pressure and increase the risk of a crash.
Remedy
FCA US will notify owners, and dealers will replace the front wheels and inspect and replace the front tires, as necessary. In addition, dealers will inspect the wheel alignment, adjusting it if necessary. These repairs will be performed free of charge. The recall began December 19, 2019. Owners may contact FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA's number for this recall is VB0.
