Nissan Kicks backup camera screen can go blank

NHTSA campaign 25V324000 · Reported 2025-06-04 · Nissan North America, Inc. · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 Nissan Kicks

What's wrong, in plain English

On certain 2025 Nissan Kicks and Frontier vehicles, the big screen in the middle of the dash can go completely blank when you shift into reverse. That means no backup camera picture, so you cannot see what is directly behind you while backing up. These vehicles do not meet the federal rule for rear visibility. The fix is a free software update for the display at the dealer. Until it is fixed, rely on your mirrors and look over your shoulder when backing up.

How serious is it?

Moderate: the vehicle is not listed as unsafe to drive, but without the rear camera image you can miss a person, child, or object behind you while reversing.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included. 2. Contact your Nissan dealer and reference recall PD152 or PMA48. 3. Have the center display software updated — the update is free.

Official notice

Component
BACK OVER PREVENTION:SOFTWARE
Summary
Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2025 Frontier and Kicks vehicles. The center information display unit may display a blank screen when shifted into reverse. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 111, "Rear Visibility."
Safety risk
A rearview image that does not display can reduce the driver's view of what is behind the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will update the software on the vehicle’s center information display, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 10, 2025. The manufacturer numbers for this recall are PD152 and PMA48.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
