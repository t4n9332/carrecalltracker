BMW M5 And M8 Transmission Wiring May Short

NHTSA campaign 19V883000 · Reported 2019-12-11 · BMW of North America, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing power to the wheels while driving raises the chance of a crash, according to the notice. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

On these BMW M5 and M8 models, the wiring harness that connects to the transmission can become damaged over time. When the wiring is damaged, it can cause an electrical short circuit inside the system. That short circuit can make the transmission shift into neutral by itself, without the driver doing anything, which means the vehicle suddenly loses the power it needs to keep moving forward.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a BMW dealer to have the wiring harness inspected and repaired (BMW customer service: 1-800-525-7417).
3. The repair is free.

Affected vehicles (3)

  • 2019 BMW M5
  • 2020 BMW M5
  • 2020 BMW M8

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2019-2020 M5 and 2020 M8 Gran Coupe, M8 Coupe, and M8 Convertible vehicles. The transmission wiring harness can become damaged, resulting in an electrical short circuit.
Safety risk
An electrical short circuit can cause the transmission to shift to neutral resulting in a loss of propulsion, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
BMW will notify owners, and dealers will replace any damaged transmission harnesses. All harnesses will be rerouted and secured. These repairs will be performed, free of charge. The recall is expected to begin February 3, 2020. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
