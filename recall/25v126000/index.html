Ford EcoSport Front Axle Half Shafts May Disconnect

NHTSA campaign 25V126000 · Reported 2025-02-28 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

A loss of drive power or a rollaway increases the risk of a crash or injury. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

The front axle half shafts that connect to the transmission may disconnect while the vehicle is being driven. If a half shaft disconnects, the vehicle can lose the power that drives the wheels. Losing drive power while on the road increases the chance of a crash. In addition, if a half shaft disconnects and the parking brake has not been applied, the vehicle could roll away on its own, which raises the risk of a crash or an injury.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Ford dealer or Ford customer service at 866-436-7332.
3. Dealers will inspect the half shafts and replace them if needed, free of charge.

Affected vehicles (2)

  • 2022 Ford Ecosport

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:AXLE ASSEMBLY:AXLE SHAFT
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2022 EcoSport vehicles. The front axle half shafts may disconnect from the transmission, which can result in a loss of drive power.
Safety risk
A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash. In addition, a disconnected half shaft can result in a vehicle rollaway if the parking brake is not applied, increasing the risk of a crash or injury.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect and replace the half shafts as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on May 29, 2025. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 25S12.
