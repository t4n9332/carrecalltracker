NHTSA campaign 22V866000 · Reported Nov 23, 2022 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

A shifter that slips out of drive can cause a sudden loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

In some Dodge Charger and Durango vehicles, the shifter assembly was not heat-treated properly. Because of this, the shifter may not fully engage in the 'drive' position. A shifter that is not properly engaged can slip out of drive on its own, causing the vehicle to suddenly lose the power needed to keep moving. This loss of drive power can happen while the vehicle is being driven.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is affected.
2. Contact a dealer, or call FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403.
3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 22V866000. Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:MANUAL TRANSMISSION:COLUMN SHIFT ASSEMBLY
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Dodge Charger and Durango vehicles. The shifter assembly was not properly heat-treated, which can prevent the shifter from properly engaging in the "drive" position.
Safety risk
An improperly engaged shifter assembly may slip out of the "drive" position, resulting in a sudden loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the shifter assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 17, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall ZB3.
