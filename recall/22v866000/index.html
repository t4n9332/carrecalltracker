Dodge Charger, Durango shifter may slip out of drive

NHTSA campaign 22V866000 · Reported 2022-11-23 · Chrysler (FCA US, LLC)

high How serious is it? About this label

Losing drive power suddenly, especially in traffic, can cause a crash with little warning; the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, but this should be repaired as soon as possible.

What's wrong, in plain English

Certain 2022 Dodge Charger and Durango vehicles have a shifter assembly that was not heat-treated correctly, so the metal is not as strong as it should be. Because of that, the shifter may not fully catch in the "drive" position. It can slip out of drive on its own, and the vehicle would suddenly stop pulling while you are on the road. Dealers will replace the shifter assembly free of charge.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if recall 22V866000 applies to your vehicle.
2. Call your Dodge dealer, or FCA US customer service at 1-800-853-1403 (recall number ZB3), for an appointment.
3. Have the dealer replace the shifter assembly — there is no cost to you.

Official notice

Component
POWER TRAIN:MANUAL TRANSMISSION:COLUMN SHIFT ASSEMBLY
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2022 Dodge Charger and Durango vehicles. The shifter assembly was not properly heat-treated, which can prevent the shifter from properly engaging in the "drive" position.
Safety risk
An improperly engaged shifter assembly may slip out of the "drive" position, resulting in a sudden loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the shifter assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 17, 2023. Owners may contact FCA US, LLC customer service at 1-800-853-1403. FCA US, LLC's number for this recall ZB3.
