Porsche Taycan front strut ring can come loose

NHTSA campaign 22V921000 · Reported 2022-12-13 · Porsche Cars North America, Inc.

high How serious is it? About this label

Serious: the notice says a strut failure can cause a loss of handling and control and raise the risk of a crash, so drive carefully and get the inspection done as soon as your dealer can take the car.

What's wrong, in plain English

A ring that holds the top of a front suspension strut in place can work loose. If it does, the strut can come out of position and lose the air that supports the car's weight on that corner. That side of the car can drop, and the car can become hard to steer and control. Your Porsche dealer will look at the strut and put in a new one if it is needed, at no cost to you.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Taycan is included. 2. Call your Porsche dealer, or Porsche customer service at 1-800-767-7243, and ask for recall ANB8. 3. Have the strut inspected and replaced if needed — the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
SUSPENSION:FRONT:MACPHERSON STRUT
Summary
Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (Porsche) is recalling certain 2021 Taycan, Taycan 4S, Taycan Turbo S, Taycan 4 Cross Turismo, and Taycan 4S Cross Turismo vehicles. A retaining ring on top of the suspension strut may come loose, resulting in a dislodged strut and air loss from the strut spring.
Safety risk
An air suspension strut failure may cause a loss of vehicle handling and control, increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect the suspension strut and replace it, if necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed February 10, 2023. Owners may contact Porsche's customer service at 1-800-767-7243. Porsche's number for this recall is ANB8.
