One BMW i4 eDrive40 has loose battery cable connection

NHTSA campaign 25V906000 · Reported 2025-12-23 · BMW of North America, LLC · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2025 BMW I4 Edrive40

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling a single 2025 i4 eDrive40. On that car, the connection where the positive battery cable meets the rear power distribution box may not be tight. A loose connection can cut power and make the car stall while you are driving. It can also get very hot, which can start a fire. A dealer will check that cable connection and tighten or repair it if it is not right.

How serious is it?

This is serious because the car could stall in traffic or the loose connection could overheat and catch fire; the notice does not say to stop driving, but this should be checked right away.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — the affected VIN is searchable starting February 13, 2026. 2. Contact your BMW dealer or BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417 to schedule the check. 3. Have the connection inspected and repaired free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM: CABLES
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling one 2025 I4 EDRIVE40 vehicle. The connection between the positive battery cable and the rear power distributor may become loose.
Safety risk
A loose positive battery cable connection could lead to the vehicle stalling, increasing the risk of a crash. In addition, a loose battery cable can overheat, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
A dealer will check the positive battery cable connection and, if necessary, repair it, free of charge. The owner notification letter is expected to be mailed on February 13, 2026. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417. The Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning February 13, 2026.
