BMW i4 Loose Battery Cable Risks Fire

NHTSA campaign 25V906000 · Reported 2025-12-23 · BMW of North America, LLC

critical How serious is it? About this label

A loose battery cable connection could cause the vehicle to stall, increasing the risk of a crash, and the loose connection can also overheat, increasing the risk of a fire. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

BMW is recalling one 2025 i4 eDrive40 vehicle because the connection between the positive battery cable and the rear power distributor may come loose over time.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a BMW dealer, or call BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.
3. The dealer will check the cable connection and repair it if needed, free of charge.

Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM: CABLES
Summary
BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling one 2025 I4 EDRIVE40 vehicle. The connection between the positive battery cable and the rear power distributor may become loose.
Safety risk
A loose positive battery cable connection could lead to the vehicle stalling, increasing the risk of a crash. In addition, a loose battery cable can overheat, increasing the risk of a fire.
Remedy
A dealer will check the positive battery cable connection and, if necessary, repair it, free of charge. The owner notification letter is expected to be mailed on February 13, 2026. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417. The Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning February 13, 2026.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
