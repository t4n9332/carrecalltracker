Rivian EDV driver's seat belt tensioner cable may be damaged

NHTSA campaign 25V816000 · Reported 2025-11-25 · Rivian Automotive, LLC

high How serious is it? About this label

This affects how well the driver is protected in a crash, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so the van can stay in service while the inspection is arranged.

What's wrong, in plain English

On some 2022-2025 Rivian EDV delivery vans, the cable for the driver's seat belt tensioner can become damaged. The tensioner is the part that pulls the belt snug against you the instant a crash begins. If its cable is damaged, the belt may not hold the driver as firmly as it should in a crash. Rivian has already sent out an over-the-air software update, and dealers will inspect the driver's belt tensioner and replace it if needed.

What you should do

1. Look up your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm the van is included.
2. Make sure the van has received Rivian's over-the-air software update.
3. Contact Rivian service, or customer service at 1-888-748-4261 (recall FSAM-1770), for the free inspection and any needed replacement.

Official notice

Component
SEAT BELTS:PRETENSIONER
Summary
Rivian Automotive, LLC (Rivian) is recalling certain 2022-2025 EDV vehicles. The driver's seat belt pretensioner cable may become damaged.
Safety risk
A damaged seat belt pretensioner cable may not restrain the occupant as intended, increasing the risk of injury during a crash.
Remedy
Rivian has released an over-the-air (OTA) software update. In addition Rivian will inspect and replace the driver's seat belt pretensioner assembly as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed January 16, 2026. Owners may contact Rivian customer service at 1-888-748-4261. Rivian's number for this recall is FSAM-1770.
