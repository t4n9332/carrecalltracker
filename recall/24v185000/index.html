Ford Maverick fuel tank can leak gasoline at seam

NHTSA campaign 24V185000 · Reported 2024-03-08 · Ford Motor Company · critical severity what's this?

Affected vehicles

  • 2024 Ford Maverick

What's wrong, in plain English

Some 2024 Ford Mavericks have a fuel tank that was not sealed properly where its two molded halves are joined. That seam can be left open, allowing gasoline to leak out of the tank. Leaking fuel near anything hot or sparking can start a vehicle fire. Ford tells owners to park and shut the engine off right away if they notice a fuel leak or the check-engine light comes on. Dealers replace the tank free.

How serious is it?

Serious — leaking fuel can cause a fire, so if you smell gasoline, see a puddle under the truck, or the check-engine light turns on, stop driving and shut off the engine immediately; otherwise get the repair done as soon as possible.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Maverick is covered by recall 24V185000 (Ford's number 24S17).
2. Contact your Ford dealer, or Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, and schedule the free fuel tank replacement right away.
3. Until it is fixed, stop and shut the engine off immediately if you smell or see fuel leaking or the check-engine light comes on.

Official notice

Component
FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2024 Maverick vehicles. The fuel tanks may have an open pinch joint between the two molds, which can allow fuel to leak.
Safety risk
A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a vehicle fire.
Remedy
Owners are advised to park and shut off the engine immediately if a fuel leak is detected or the Malfunction Indicator Lamp illuminates. Dealers will replace the fuel tanks, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on July 10, 2024. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 24S17.
