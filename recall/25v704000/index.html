What's wrong, in plain English

General Motors is recalling certain 2025-2026 Cadillac Optiq and 2025-2026 Chevrolet Equinox EV vehicles that are equipped with 21-inch Continental all-season tires. On some of these vehicles, part of the tire's tread, or the entire tread, may separate from the tire while the vehicle is being driven. This is known as tread detachment and can affect one or more of the vehicle's tires.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
2. Contact a Cadillac dealer at 1-800-333-4223 or a Chevrolet dealer at 1-800-222-1020 to schedule an inspection.
3. If your tires need to be replaced, the repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 25V704000. Official notice

Component
TIRES:TREAD/BELT
Summary
General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2025-2026 Cadillac Optic and 2025-2026 Chevrolet Equinox EV vehicles equipped with 21-inch Continental all-season tires. One or more of these tires may experience partial or full tread detachment.
Safety risk
Tire tread detachment increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will inspect all four tires and replace those manufactured during DOT week 4024, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 19, 2025. Owners may contact Cadillac customer service at 1-800-333-4223, or Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N252525030. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning October 16, 2025.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
