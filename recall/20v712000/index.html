Volkswagen Atlas Steering Knuckle May Fail

NHTSA campaign 20V712000 · Reported Nov 17, 2020 · Volkswagen Group of America, Inc.

critical How serious is it? About this label

Volkswagen is telling owners not to drive the vehicle until it has been inspected and repaired. Losing steering control increases the risk of a crash.

What's wrong, in plain English

One or both steering knuckles may have been damaged when the vehicle was built. A damaged steering knuckle can fail, which would cause you to lose the ability to steer the vehicle.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. 2. Do not drive the vehicle; contact a Volkswagen dealer (1-800-893-5298) to arrange the inspection and repair. 3. The repair is free.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 20V712000. Official notice

Component
STEERING
Summary
Volkswagen Group of America, Inc. (Volkswagen) is recalling certain 2021 Atlas and 2020-2021 Atlas Cross Sport vehicles. One or both steering knuckles may have been damaged during production.
Safety risk
A damaged steering knuckle can fail, causing a loss of steering, thereby increasing the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Volkswagen has notified owners, and dealers will inspect and, as necessary, replace the steering knuckle, free of charge. The recall began November 19, 2020. Owners may contact Volkswagen customer service at 1-800-893-5298. Volkswagen's number for this recall is 40Q6. Note: Owners are advised to DO NOT DRIVE the vehicle until the vehicle has been inspected and repaired if necessary.
