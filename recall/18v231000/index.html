Jeep Wrangler rear frame rail rust can loosen seat

NHTSA campaign 18V231000 · Reported 2018-04-12 · Chrysler (FCA US LLC)

medium How serious is it? About this label

Serious in a crash because a rear seat could come loose, but the notice does not tell owners to stop driving, so normal use is fine while you schedule the free inspection.

What's wrong, in plain English

The metal rail that runs under the back of your Jeep can rust. If it rusts badly, the floor under the rear seat gets weaker and the left rear seat mount can work loose. A loose seat is not an obvious problem day to day, but in a crash the back seat could break free from the floor. Dealers check the rail and coat it inside and out with rust protection so the corrosion stops.

What you should do

1. Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your Jeep is included in recall U27.
2. Call your Chrysler/Jeep dealer, or Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403, and book the inspection.
3. Have the rail inspected and rust-coated as needed - the repair is free.

Official notice

Component
STRUCTURE:FRAME AND MEMBERS
Summary
Chrysler (FCA US LLC) is recalling certain 2018 Jeep Wrangler vehicles. The rear under body ladder rail may corrode, possibly reducing the strength of the rear floor pan and causing the left rear seat mount to become loose.
Safety risk
If the floor pan strength is reduced and/or the rear seat mount becomes loose, the rear seat may detach in a crash, increasing the risk of injury.
Remedy
Chrysler will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and, as necessary, coat the interior and exterior of the driver's side rear underbody ladder rail with three-part rust protection. The recall began September 11, 2018. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 1-800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is U27.
