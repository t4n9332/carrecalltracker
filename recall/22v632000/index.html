Hyundai Elantra driver airbag emblem may break loose

NHTSA campaign 22V632000 · Reported 2022-08-18 · Hyundai Motor America · medium severity

Affected vehicles

  • 2022 Hyundai Elantra
  • 2022 Hyundai Elantra N
  • 2023 Hyundai Elantra

What's wrong, in plain English

The emblem on the cover of the driver's front airbag may not have been welded on properly at the factory. If the airbag goes off in a crash, that badge can break away and fly off with it. The airbag itself still does its job, but the loose emblem could strike someone in the car. To fix it, dealers are replacing the entire driver's front airbag rather than just the cover.

How serious is it?

Moderately serious — the problem only matters during a crash, and it is safe to keep driving, but a flying emblem could add to injuries when the airbag deploys.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your Elantra or Elantra N is covered.
2. Contact your Hyundai dealer, or Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460 (recall 234), to schedule the airbag replacement.
3. Bring the car in for the free repair — owner letters went out in October 2022.

Official notice

Component
AIR BAGS:FRONTAL
Summary
Hyundai Motor America (Hyundai) is recalling certain 2022 Elantra N and 2022-2023 Elantra vehicles. The emblem on the cover of the driver's front air bag may not have been welded properly, which can result in the emblem detaching upon deployment.
Safety risk
A detached air bag cover emblem may strike a vehicle occupant, which can increase the risk of injury.
Remedy
Dealers will replace the air bag, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 14, 2022. Owners may contact Hyundai customer service at 1-855-371-9460. Hyundai's number for this recall is 234.

Verify your specific vehicle by VIN at nhtsa.gov. Recall repairs are free of charge by law.
