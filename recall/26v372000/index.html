Ford Mustang, Lincoln Nautilus, Navigator Instrument Cluster Blank

NHTSA campaign 26V372000 · Reported Jun 9, 2026 · Ford Motor Company

high How serious is it? About this label

Without a working instrument cluster, drivers can't see important information such as speed or safety warning lights, which increases the risk of a crash. The notice does not tell owners to stop driving.

What's wrong, in plain English

Ford is recalling certain 2019 Mustang, Nautilus, and Navigator vehicles because an earlier repair for this same problem did not fully fix it. When the vehicle is started, the instrument panel display may fail to work and show a blank screen instead of turning on. This means the gauges and warning lights on the dashboard may not be visible to the driver.

What you should do

1. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to confirm your vehicle is included.
2. Contact your Ford or Lincoln dealer, or call Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332, to schedule the repair.
3. The repair is free of charge.
Where do I find my VIN, and what do I tell the dealer?

Your VIN is the 17-character code on the lower left of the windshield, on your registration and usually on your insurance card. Enter it at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether this recall is still open on your car. When you call the dealer, say you have an open safety recall and give them NHTSA campaign number 26V372000. Official notice

Component
ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL
Summary
Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2019 Ford Mustang, Lincoln Nautilus, and Lincoln Navigator vehicles previously repaired incorrectly under recall number 19V076. At vehicle start-up, the Instrument Panel Cluster Assembly (IPC) may not function, showing a blank display. As a result, these vehicles fail to comply with Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 101, "Controls and Displays."
Safety risk
A blank instrument cluster that fails to display critical safety information, such as vehicle speed or safety system warnings, increases the risk of a crash.
Remedy
Dealers will reprogram the Instrument Panel Cluster Assembly (IPC) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 13, 2026. Owners may contact customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26C27. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov on July 6, 2026.
