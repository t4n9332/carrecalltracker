Kia K900 and Stinger Turbo Oil Leak Risk critical

Reported Mar 1, 2024 · Campaign 24V169000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

On certain Kia K900 and Stinger vehicles with the 3.3-liter turbocharged engine, the oil feed pipe and hose that supply oil to the left turbocharger can wear out over time. This can cause the pipe and hose to leak engine oil. Because this leak happens near hot parts of the engine, it raises the chance of a fire starting in the engine compartment while the car is being driven.

Kia Stinger Fuel Pump Loss of Power high

Reported Sep 13, 2023 · Campaign 23V634000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021

On certain Kia Stinger vehicles, the plunger inside the fuel control valve may get stuck inside the high pressure fuel pump. When this happens, pressure inside the pump can build up too high, a problem the recall calls over-pressurization. This can cause the vehicle to lose drive power, meaning the vehicle's ability to keep moving forward can be affected while it is being driven.

Kia Stinger fuel gauge may read inaccurately medium

Reported Nov 5, 2021 · Campaign 21V862000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On some 2020-2021 Kia Stinger vehicles, a software error in the instrument cluster can cause the fuel gauge to show an inaccurate reading, meaning the gauge may not show how much fuel is actually in the tank. Because of this, the vehicle could unexpectedly run out of fuel and stall while driving. Dealers will update the software at no cost to the owner.

Kia Stinger fuel pump may not supply enough fuel high

Reported Sep 16, 2020 · Campaign 20V560000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Kia is recalling certain 2020-2021 Stinger vehicles. A small piece of leftover plastic from the manufacturing process may be left in the jet nozzle of the fuel pump. That can block the nozzle, so the engine does not get enough fuel. Dealers will inspect the fuel pump and replace the jet nozzle housing assembly if needed, free of charge. The recall began October 29, 2020.

Kia Stinger: Engine Compartment Fire Risk While Driving critical

Reported Aug 27, 2020 · Campaign 20V518000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021

Kia is recalling certain Stinger vehicles equipped with certain engines. While the vehicle is being driven, a fire may start in the engine compartment, in the area where the Anti-lock Brake Hydraulic Electronic Control Unit is located. A fire in the engine compartment can increase the risk of injury or increase the risk of a crash.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Kia Stinger Power Steering May Lock Up high

Reported Jul 22, 2020 · Campaign 20V422000 · Model years: 2020

Kia says a part inside the power steering system on some Stinger vehicles with the 2.0-liter turbo engine may not have been manufactured correctly. Because of this, the steering could lock up or become difficult to turn while you are driving. This problem is inside the electric power steering assembly Kia installed on these vehicles. Kia is recalling the affected vehicles to address this manufacturing issue with the steering system.

Kia Stinger Wiring Harness May Cause Fire critical

Reported Oct 25, 2018 · Campaign 18V754000 · Model years: 2018

Kia Stinger vehicles have a wiring harness at the front of the car that runs through the engine compartment and the passenger compartment. This wiring can rub against a rough burr on the left fender body panel. Over time, that rubbing may wear down the wiring's covering and damage the wires. This kind of damage affects the electrical wiring running through the front of the vehicle.

Kia Stinger recall questions

How many recalls does the Kia Stinger have?

NHTSA lists 7 safety recalls covering the 2018–2023 Kia Stinger. 6 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Kia K900 and Stinger Turbo Oil Leak Risk" (NHTSA campaign 24V169000), reported Mar 1, 2024.

Which Kia Stinger model year has the most recalls?

The 2020 Kia Stinger has the most, with 6 recalls. The 2022 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Kia Stinger?

Yes. 1 Kia Stinger recall carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Kia Stinger: Engine Compartment Fire Risk While Driving (20V518000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Kia Stinger has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Kia dealer, including for second owners.
