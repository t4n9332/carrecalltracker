Kia Forte and Rio Steering Column Bolt Loose high

Reported 2022-05-05 · Campaign 22V304000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

In some Forte and Rio cars, a bolt in the steering column may not be properly secured. If the bolt comes loose, the steering column can separate from the steering rack, which can take away the driver's ability to steer the car.

Kia Rio Brake Master Cylinder Defect high

Reported 2021-12-01 · Campaign 21V936000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Kia is recalling certain Rio vehicles because the brake master cylinder may have been manufactured with sharp edges. Those edges can damage an inner oil seal inside the master cylinder. Once the seal is damaged, the brakes may not perform as well as they should, which can show up as a brake pedal that travels farther than normal or a longer distance needed to bring the vehicle to a stop.

Kia Forte and Rio trunk may not open from inside medium

Reported 2021-08-09 · Campaign 21V622000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On these Kia Forte and Rio cars, the trunk latch can become damaged. When that happens, the trunk cannot be opened from inside the trunk compartment. Federal safety rules require a way to get out of a closed trunk from the inside, and because the latch may fail, these cars do not meet that requirement. A dealer will replace the whole trunk latch assembly with a new one.
