Kia Instrument Panel Display May Fail high

Reported Jan 27, 2026 · Campaign 26V046000 · Model years: 2025

A software error on certain Kia vehicles, including the K4 and Sorento, may cause the instrument panel display to fail.

Kia K4, Sorento Engine Bolts May Loosen high

Reported Aug 25, 2025 · Campaign 25V548000 · Model years: 2025

The bolts that hold the connecting rods inside the engine may not have been tightened correctly when these vehicles were built. Because those bolts aren't secure, the engine's internal parts aren't held together the way they should be. This can cause the engine to suddenly lose the power that keeps the vehicle moving forward, and that loss of power can happen at any time while you're driving.

Kia K4 recall questions

How many recalls does the Kia K4 have?

NHTSA lists 2 safety recalls covering the 2025 Kia K4. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Kia Instrument Panel Display May Fail" (NHTSA campaign 26V046000), reported Jan 27, 2026.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Kia K4?

No. None of the 2 Kia K4 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Kia K4 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Kia dealer, including for second owners.
