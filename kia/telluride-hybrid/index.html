Kia Telluride Driver Seat Belt May Not Extend high

Reported Jun 2, 2026 · Campaign 26V356000 · Model years: 2027

The recall covers certain Telluride and Telluride Hybrid SUVs. The driver's seat belt has a locking mechanism that can jam, which stops the belt from pulling out far enough. Because the belt strap does not extend properly, it may not fit around the driver correctly, so the belt would not hold the driver in place as intended. This means the vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for seat belts.

Kia Telluride Hybrid Power Seat May Trap Someone high

Reported Mar 20, 2026 · Campaign 26V173000 · Model years: 2027

This recall covers certain Telluride Hybrid SUVs with power second-row seats and the Executive Package. These power seats may fail to sense that a person is in the way. If the seat contacts someone while it is moving—during functions like one-touch tilt-and-slide or automatic power fold-and-stow—it may keep moving instead of stopping. This applies to SX Prestige and X-Line SX Prestige trims equipped with that package.

How many recalls does the Kia Telluride Hybrid have?

NHTSA lists 2 safety recalls covering the 2027 Kia Telluride Hybrid. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Kia Telluride Driver Seat Belt May Not Extend" (NHTSA campaign 26V356000), reported Jun 2, 2026.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Kia Telluride Hybrid?

No. None of the 2 Kia Telluride Hybrid recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Kia Telluride Hybrid has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Kia dealer, including for second owners.
