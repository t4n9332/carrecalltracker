Kia EV6, EV9 Battery Fire Risk critical

Reported Jul 2, 2026 · Campaign 26V431000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

The high voltage battery in these vehicles may contain cells with parts that are out of alignment inside them. This can lead to a fire while the vehicle is parked or being driven. Kia will replace the entire battery system to fix the problem.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Kia EV6 Charging Unit May Cause Power Loss high

Reported Nov 18, 2024 · Campaign 24V867000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

Kia America is recalling certain EV6 vehicles because a part called the Integrated Charging Control Unit, which helps charge the car's 12-volt battery, may become damaged and stop working. When this happens, the 12-volt battery stops charging, and the vehicle can lose drive power while it is being driven.

Kia EV6 Charging Unit May Cause Power Loss high

Reported Mar 14, 2024 · Campaign 24V200000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

On certain 2022-2024 Kia EV6 vehicles, a part called the Integrated Charging Control Unit can become damaged and stop charging the vehicle's 12-volt battery. When the 12-volt battery isn't being charged properly, the vehicle can lose drive power while it is being driven. Kia is recalling these vehicles to inspect the charging unit and replace it and its fuse if needed, and to update the unit's software.

Kia EV6 and Niro EV Drive Shaft Failure high

Reported Jan 31, 2024 · Campaign 24V057000 · Model years: 2023

On certain Niro EV and EV6 vehicles, one of the drive shafts may not have been properly hardened during manufacturing. This can cause the shaft to break while the vehicle is being driven, which results in a sudden loss of power to the wheels. Losing power to the wheels while on the road increases the chance of a crash for the driver and others on the road.

Kia EV6 Parking Software Error May Cause Rollaway high

Reported May 11, 2022 · Campaign 22V322000 · Model years: 2022

Kia is recalling certain EV6 vehicles. A software error in the shifter control unit may cause the parking mechanism to disengage on its own, even after the vehicle has been placed in park. This means the vehicle could start rolling on its own without the driver doing anything, since the parking mechanism that's supposed to hold it in place isn't working correctly.

Kia EV6 recall questions

How many recalls does the Kia EV6 have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2022–2024 Kia EV6. 5 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Kia EV6, EV9 Battery Fire Risk" (NHTSA campaign 26V431000), reported Jul 2, 2026.

Which Kia EV6 model year has the most recalls?

The 2022 Kia EV6 has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2024 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 3. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Kia EV6?

Yes. 1 Kia EV6 recall carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Kia EV6, EV9 Battery Fire Risk (26V431000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Kia EV6 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Kia dealer, including for second owners.
