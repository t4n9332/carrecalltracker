Kia Carnival Fuel Pipe Connection May Leak critical

Reported Apr 10, 2026 · Campaign 26V232000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

Kia is recalling certain Carnival vehicles because fuel may leak at the connection between the fuel pipe and the fuel rail. A fuel leak like this increases the risk of a fire.

Kia Carnival and Sportage: Roof Molding May Detach medium

Reported Jan 18, 2024 · Campaign 24V025000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

The molding along the roof of these vehicles may come loose and fall off while driving. A piece of molding that detaches on the road can become a hazard for other drivers. Dealers will inspect the roof molding and either secure it or replace it, at no cost to owners.

Kia Carnival power sliding doors may not auto-reverse medium

Reported Mar 31, 2023 · Campaign 23V236000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Kia is recalling all 2022 Carnival minivans and certain 2023 Carnival minivans. On these vehicles, the auto-reverse feature for the power sliding side doors may not activate in all situations. Dealers will install updated software for the module that controls the power sliding doors, and the work is free. Kia mailed notification letters to owners in late April 2023, and owners who have questions can call the company's customer service line.

Kia Carnival Tow Hitch Wiring Fire Risk critical

Reported Mar 17, 2023 · Campaign 23V179000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Some Kia Carnival vehicles with a tow hitch wiring harness can have water build up on a circuit board inside that harness. This water buildup can cause an electrical short, which can lead to a fire. Kia dealers will check whether your vehicle has this accessory hitch harness, and if so, install a new fuse and a wire extension kit, all at no cost to you.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Kia Carnival Power Sliding Door May Open While Moving high

Reported Nov 19, 2021 · Campaign 21V908000 · Model years: 2022

Kia is recalling certain Carnival vehicles because the remote controller assembly for the passenger-side power sliding door may malfunction. When this happens, the passenger-side sliding door may not latch securely, which means it may not stay closed as it should. Dealers will replace the drum and holder inside the remote controller assembly to correct the problem so the door latches properly.

Kia Carnival Fuel Rail May Leak critical

Reported Apr 21, 2021 · Campaign 21V277000 · Model years: 2022

On certain 2022 Kia Carnival vehicles, the plastic fuel rail tube may have been damaged.
