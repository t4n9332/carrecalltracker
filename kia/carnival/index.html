Kia Carnival recalls

6 recalls covering model years 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Kia Carnival Fuel Line Connection Can Leak Gasoline critical

Reported 2026-04-10 · Campaign 26V232000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

Gasoline can leak where the fuel pipe joins the fuel rail, the part that feeds fuel to the engine. Leaking gasoline in the engine bay can catch fire. Dealers will inspect that connection and either tighten it or replace the fuel pipe if it needs it, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on June 2, 2026, and affected vehicle identification numbers can be looked up on the NHTSA website.

Kia Sportage and Carnival Roof Trim May Fall Off medium

Reported 2024-01-18 · Campaign 24V025000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

On certain 2023-2024 Kia Sportage and 2022-2024 Kia Carnival vehicles, the strip of trim along the roof may work loose and come off. If it detaches while you are driving, it can land in the road and become an obstacle for the cars behind you. A dealer will look at the roof trim and either fasten it properly or replace it. Owner letters went out in March 2024, so the repair is available now.

Kia Carnival power sliding doors may not auto-reverse medium

Reported 2023-03-31 · Campaign 23V236000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

The power sliding side doors are supposed to stop and open back up if they touch something while closing. On these vans that safety feature may not kick in every time. If it doesn't, a door can keep closing on a hand, an arm, or a child getting in or out, and hurt them. The fix is a software update for the computer that runs the doors, done at the dealer.

Kia Carnival tow hitch wiring can short and burn critical

Reported 2023-03-17 · Campaign 23V179000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

If your Carnival has a Kia tow hitch — installed at the factory or added later through a Kia dealer — water can collect on the small circuit board inside the hitch wiring. That can cause an electrical short, and the short can start a fire, either while you are driving or while the van is parked. Kia says to park outside and away from buildings until it is repaired. The dealer will check for a genuine Kia hitch and add a new fuse and wiring kit.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Kia Carnival power sliding door may not latch shut high

Reported 2021-11-19 · Campaign 21V908000 · Model years: 2022

The part that controls the passenger-side power sliding door on some 2022 Carnival minivans can act up, and when it does the door may not latch securely. A door that is not fully latched can swing open while the van is moving, which could hurt someone inside, especially a child or anyone sitting near it. Kia dealers will replace two pieces inside that door control assembly, the drum and holder, at no cost to the owner.

Kia Carnival fuel rail tube can leak gasoline critical

Reported 2021-04-21 · Campaign 21V277000 · Model years: 2022

The plastic tube that carries gasoline to the engine's fuel injectors on certain 2022 Carnival minivans may have been damaged. A damaged tube can let fuel leak out while the engine is running. Leaking gasoline around a hot engine can catch fire. A Kia dealer will inspect the fuel rail and replace it if anything is wrong with it. The recall started in April 2021 and the inspection and any repair cost you nothing.
