Kia Sorento recalls

15 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Kia Instrument Panel Display May Fail high

Reported 2026-01-27 · Campaign 26V046000 · Model years: 2025

A software error on certain Kia vehicles, including the K4 and Sorento, may cause the instrument panel display to fail.

Kia Sorento Blower Motor Wiring Fire Risk critical

Reported 2025-09-25 · Campaign 25V649000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

On these Kia Sorento vehicles, the wiring for the heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) blower motor may not be adequate. This wiring problem can result in a fire. Dealers will replace the wiring harness and the blower motor resistor so this cannot happen.

Kia K4, Sorento Engine Bolts May Loosen high

Reported 2025-08-25 · Campaign 25V548000 · Model years: 2025

The bolts that hold the connecting rods inside the engine may not have been tightened correctly when these vehicles were built. Because those bolts aren't secure, the engine's internal parts aren't held together the way they should be. This can cause the engine to suddenly lose the power that keeps the vehicle moving forward, and that loss of power can happen at any time while you're driving.

Kia Sorento may lose headlights and taillights high

Reported 2025-01-13 · Campaign 25V006000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

This recall affects certain Kia Sorento, Sorento Hybrid, and Sorento Plug-In Hybrid vehicles. A software error in the vehicle's body control computer may cause the low-beam headlights and taillights to stop working.

Kia Sorento Wiring May Cause Steering Loss high

Reported 2024-04-23 · Campaign 24V287000 · Model years: 2024

Some Kia Sorento vehicles may have a wiring harness for the power steering system that was assembled incorrectly. This wiring problem can cause the vehicle to lose power steering assist while it is being driven. Without power steering assist, the vehicle becomes harder to steer, and this increases the risk of a crash.

Kia Sorento Rearview Camera May Lose Image medium

Reported 2023-09-15 · Campaign 23V639000 · Model years: 2019

Due to a manufacturing error, water may get into the rearview camera. When this happens, the camera image may not appear on the vehicle's display screen, so the driver cannot see behind the vehicle using that screen.

Kia Sorento Rearview Camera Clips May Break medium

Reported 2023-08-31 · Campaign 23V610000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Certain Kia Sorento, Sorento Hybrid, and Sorento Plug-in Hybrid SUVs have clips that hold the rearview camera in place. These clips may break over time. If a clip breaks, the camera image may not show up correctly on the vehicle's display screen. This means the picture drivers normally see when backing up might not appear the way it is supposed to.

Kia Sorento Airbag Warning Label May Detach medium

Reported 2023-08-15 · Campaign 23V579000 · Model years: 2023

The airbag warning labels on the driver's and front passenger's sun visors may come loose and fall off. Without the label in place, occupants are not shown the printed warning about the risks of airbag deployment. Because of this, the vehicles no longer meet a federal safety standard covering occupant crash protection.

Kia K5 and Sorento May Lose Power high

Reported 2022-10-11 · Campaign 22V760000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

On certain Kia K5 and Sorento vehicles, a transmission oil pump malfunction can trigger the vehicle's fail-safe, limited-mobility drive mode, but that fail-safe mode may itself be impaired. This can result in a complete loss of drive power.

Kia Sorento and Sportage Tow Hitch Fire Risk critical

Reported 2022-09-19 · Campaign 22V703000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

Certain Kia Sorento, Sorento Hybrid, Sorento Plug-In Hybrid, and Sportage vehicles equipped with a tow hitch wiring harness, either installed at the factory or added later through a Kia dealership, may allow debris and moisture to build up on a circuit board inside the harness module. This buildup can cause an electrical short, which can result in a fire.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Kia Sorento and Telluride may have wrong spare tire medium

Reported 2022-07-19 · Campaign 22V509000 · Model years: 2022

Kia is recalling certain Sorento and Telluride vehicles. These vehicles may have been built with the wrong spare tire. If that spare is put on the vehicle, it can interfere with the anti-lock brakes and the traction control system, so the vehicles do not meet the federal rule for tire and rim selection. Dealers will look at the spare tire and replace it if needed. Owner letters have been mailed, and the replacement is free.

Kia Sorento Turbo Oil Pipe May Crack critical

Reported 2022-03-29 · Campaign 22V202000 · Model years: 2022

In certain 2022 Kia Sorento vehicles, the pipe that supplies oil to the turbocharger may develop a crack. If it cracks, oil can leak out in the engine compartment. An oil leak near hot engine or exhaust parts, which can act as an ignition source, raises the chance that a fire could start.

Kia K5 And Sorento Fuel Line Leak critical

Reported 2021-07-08 · Campaign 21V519000 · Model years: 2021

On certain 2021 Sorento and 2021-2022 K5 vehicles with the 2.5L turbocharged engine, fuel can leak from the connection between the high-pressure fuel pump and the fuel rail.

Kia Sorento steering column may separate high

Reported 2018-09-21 · Campaign 18V656000 · Model years: 2018

Kia Motors America is recalling certain 2018 Kia Sorento vehicles. A crossbar that connects to the power steering column may not have been welded properly, which could allow the steering column to separate.

Kia Sorento Side Curtain Air Bag Component Risk high

Reported 2017-12-08 · Campaign 17V784000 · Model years: 2018

Kia is recalling certain 2018 Sorento vehicles because a component in the side curtain air bags may detach during deployment. If it detaches, it may enter the passenger compartment and act as a projectile inside the vehicle cabin.
