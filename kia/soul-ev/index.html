Kia Soul EV Parking Mechanism May Be Damaged high

Reported 2023-10-26 · Campaign 23V724000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On certain Soul EV vehicles, the mechanism that holds the vehicle in place when the gear shift is set to Park can become damaged. Kia says that if this happens, the vehicle can move on its own even while in Park, if the parking brake isn't also applied and the vehicle is powered on.

Kia Soul EV High-Voltage Battery Fire Risk critical

Reported 2023-03-29 · Campaign 23V218000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On certain Kia Soul EV cars, the high-voltage battery pack could develop an electrical short while you're driving, which raises the chance of a fire. To fix this, dealers will update the battery management software, and replace it if needed. The updated software will turn on the EV warning light and cut back charging and power if it detects an unusual voltage problem, and if that happens, the battery itself will be replaced.

Kia Air Bags May Fail to Deploy in Crash high

Reported 2022-01-25 · Campaign 22V031000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The cover on the air bag control unit may press against a memory chip on the circuit board inside and damage the electrical circuit. This affects certain Kia Forte, Forte Koup, Sedona, Soul, and Soul EV vehicles. If the circuit is damaged, the air bags could be deactivated and not deploy in a crash. Dealers will inspect the control unit and update its software or replace it as needed, free of charge.
