Kia Optima trunk latch may crack, trapping someone inside medium

Reported 2023-08-24 · Campaign 23V594000 · Model years: 2018

Kia is recalling certain Optima and Optima Hybrid cars. The base of the trunk latch can crack. When it cracks, the trunk can no longer be opened from the inside. Federal safety rules require a way to open the trunk from inside, and these cars do not meet that rule. A dealer will replace the trunk latch base at no cost, so the trunk can be opened from the inside again.

Kia Optima Collision Braking Software May Not Engage medium

Reported 2019-07-19 · Campaign 19V539000 · Model years: 2019

Some Kia Optima vehicles with certain turbocharged engines have a software error in the Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist system. Because of this error, the system's automatic braking function may not engage when it detects a stationary vehicle ahead. This means the system may fail to step in and help brake the car in that situation, the way it is designed to.

Kia Optima Hybrid Battery Protection Device Issue high

Reported 2018-03-08 · Campaign 18V161000 · Model years: 2018

Some Optima hybrid vehicles have a voltage protection device meant to guard the high-voltage battery, and it may switch on when it shouldn't. If this happens while you're driving in electric mode, the vehicle can suddenly lose power.
