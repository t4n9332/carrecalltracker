Kia Sportage recalls

11 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Kia Sportage Instrument Panel Screen May Fail high

Reported 2025-12-17 · Campaign 25V874000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Kia America is recalling certain Sportage and Sportage PHEV vehicles equipped with a 4.2-inch instrument screen and a genuine Kia tow hitch wiring harness accessory. In these vehicles, a software error can cause the instrument panel screen to stop working properly. When that happens, the driver may not be able to see important safety information on the screen, such as the speedometer or warning lights.

Sportage Power Steering May Short Circuit high

Reported 2024-06-07 · Campaign 24V422000 · Model years: 2024

On certain Sportage vehicles, the circuit board in the power steering assembly may short circuit. If this happens, the vehicle can lose power steering assist. Losing power steering assist increases the risk of a crash.

Kia Carnival and Sportage: Roof Molding May Detach medium

Reported 2024-01-18 · Campaign 24V025000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

The molding along the roof of these vehicles may come loose and fall off while driving. A piece of molding that detaches on the road can become a hazard for other drivers. Dealers will inspect the roof molding and either secure it or replace it, at no cost to owners.

Kia Seltos, Soul, Sportage: Oil Pump Fire Risk critical

Reported 2023-07-31 · Campaign 23V531000 · Model years: 2023

Kia is recalling certain Seltos, Soul, and Sportage vehicles equipped with an Idle Stop and Go system. The electronic controller for this system's oil pump may have damaged electrical components inside it. This damage can cause the oil pump to overheat, which increases the risk of a vehicle fire.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Sportage Brake Booster Leak May Cut Power Assist high

Reported 2023-06-07 · Campaign 23V410000 · Model years: 2023

On certain Sportage vehicles, the brake booster diaphragm may become misaligned, which can cause an internal vacuum leak. This leak can result in a loss of power brake assist, which can extend the distance needed to stop the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.

Kia SUVs and cars: dashboard display may go blank medium

Reported 2023-04-26 · Campaign 23V298000 · Model years: 2023

These Kia vehicles have a digital instrument cluster with a small screen instead of traditional gauges. When the vehicle starts, this screen may fail to show any information at all. That means the driver cannot see tire pressure readings, warning messages, or warning lights. Kia dealers will update the instrument cluster software at no charge. Owner letters were mailed May 26, 2023.

Kia Sportage Side Curtain Air Bags Twisted high

Reported 2022-11-28 · Campaign 22V878000 · Model years: 2023

In certain 2023 Kia Sportage vehicles, the side curtain air bags may have been installed twisted instead of lying flat. An air bag that is twisted may not open the way it's supposed to in a crash. If it deploys improperly, it may not protect occupants as intended, which can raise the risk of injury.

Kia Sorento and Sportage Tow Hitch Fire Risk critical

Reported 2022-09-19 · Campaign 22V703000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

Certain Kia Sorento, Sorento Hybrid, Sorento Plug-In Hybrid, and Sportage vehicles equipped with a tow hitch wiring harness, either installed at the factory or added later through a Kia dealership, may allow debris and moisture to build up on a circuit board inside the harness module. This buildup can cause an electrical short, which can result in a fire.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Kia Sportage Loose Alternator Nut Risks Stall, Fire critical

Reported 2022-08-29 · Campaign 22V651000 · Model years: 2023

On some Sportage SUVs, the nut that holds the positive battery cable to the alternator — the part that charges the battery while you drive — may loosen. If it loosens enough, it can cause the engine to stall while you are driving, or it can cause an electrical surge in the vehicle's wiring. Both problems trace back to that same loose connection.

Kia Cadenza, Sportage Brake Module Fire Risk critical

Reported 2021-03-04 · Campaign 21V137000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021

Some Kia Cadenza and Sportage vehicles have an electrical circuit in the brake control unit that may short-circuit. This short can cause a fire in the engine compartment.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Kia Sportage Fuel Pipe May Leak, Fire Risk high

Reported 2018-12-19 · Campaign 18V907000 · Model years: 2018

Kia is recalling certain vehicles that previously had an engine replaced under an earlier recall, warranty work, or a related product improvement campaign. During that replacement, the high pressure fuel pipe connected to the fuel pump outlet may have been damaged, misaligned, or improperly tightened, which can cause a fuel leak. Dealers will inspect the fuel pipe and replace it if necessary to fix the problem.
