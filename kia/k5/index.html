Kia K5 recalls

8 recalls covering model years 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Kia K5 Fuel Tank Damage Risk of Fire critical

Reported 2025-11-17 · Campaign 25V794000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Kia says a damaged check valve in some K5 vehicles can let air get into the fuel tank. That extra air can make the tank expand, and the expanded tank can touch hot parts of the exhaust system. Contact with the hot exhaust can melt the fuel tank, so Kia is having dealers check and fix the part before that happens.

Kia K5 rear window trim may loosen and detach medium

Reported 2025-07-28 · Campaign 25V493000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

Kia is recalling certain K5 sedans. The trim pieces along the left and right rear windows may work loose and come off the car. Dealers will inspect the trim on the rear roof pillars and replace it if needed, at no charge. Kia mailed owner notification letters on November 19, 2025. Owners who have questions can contact Kia customer service and refer to the company's number for this recall, SC346.

Kia K5 Parking Lights May Flicker From Software Error low

Reported 2025-05-27 · Campaign 25V351000 · Model years: 2025

A software error in the K5's Power-net Domain Controller (PDC) may cause the parking lights to flicker instead of staying lit properly. Parking lights that don't illuminate the way they should can make the vehicle harder for other drivers to see.

Kia K5 Side Curtain Airbags May Not Deploy Properly high

Reported 2023-03-07 · Campaign 23V149000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

On some Kia K5 cars, the side curtain airbags may not have been installed correctly. These are the airbags that come down over the side windows. Because of how they were put in, they may not open the way they are supposed to in a crash. Dealers will inspect the side curtain airbags and reinstall them if needed, at no charge to the owner.

Kia K5 and Sorento May Lose Power high

Reported 2022-10-11 · Campaign 22V760000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

On certain Kia K5 and Sorento vehicles, a transmission oil pump malfunction can trigger the vehicle's fail-safe, limited-mobility drive mode, but that fail-safe mode may itself be impaired. This can result in a complete loss of drive power.

Kia K5 And Sorento Fuel Line Leak critical

Reported 2021-07-08 · Campaign 21V519000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

On certain 2021 Sorento and 2021-2022 K5 vehicles with the 2.5L turbocharged engine, fuel can leak from the connection between the high-pressure fuel pump and the fuel rail.

Kia K5 Power Steering Effort Can Suddenly Increase high

Reported 2021-06-14 · Campaign 21V447000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

On certain Kia K5 vehicles with a specific engine, the power steering system may not be able to detect a loss of communication within itself. If this happens while driving around a curve with the lane-following feature turned on, the amount of help the steering gives may not adjust the way it should, causing the steering wheel to suddenly feel much harder to turn.

Kia Telluride, K5, Forte Airbag Sensor May Fail high

Reported 2021-03-11 · Campaign 21V164000 · Model years: 2021

Kia is recalling certain Telluride, K5, and Forte vehicles because a sensor in the front passenger seat, called the occupant detection system, may fail to detect when a child restraint seat has been installed there. This detection system is used to help determine whether the front passenger air bag should deploy in a crash. Dealers will replace the detection unit so it can properly sense a child seat in that seating position, at no cost to the owner.
