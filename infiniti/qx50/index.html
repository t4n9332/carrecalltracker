Nissan, Infiniti Engine Bearings May Fail critical

Reported Jun 27, 2025 · Campaign 25V437000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

Certain engines in these vehicles use bearings that may have a manufacturing defect. Over time, this defect can lead to the engine failing. In some cases, a failed bearing can cause a break in the engine block, which allows hot oil inside the engine to leak out while the vehicle is being driven.

Nissan and Infiniti driver airbags may deploy improperly high

Reported Feb 27, 2024 · Campaign 24V154000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti cars and SUVs because of a problem that happened during manufacturing. The inflator in the driver's airbag, which is the part that fills the bag, may absorb moisture. If that happens, the airbag may deploy improperly. Dealers will replace the entire driver's airbag assembly at no cost to the owner. Nissan mailed owner notification letters on April 10, 2024.

Infiniti QX50, QX55 Front Door Lock May Not Latch medium

Reported Mar 20, 2023 · Campaign 23V187000 · Model years: 2022

Nissan says some INFINITI QX50 and QX55 vehicles may have the wrong door latch installed in the front right passenger door. Because of this, the front right door lock may not lock automatically as it should. If the door is not locked, it could unlatch and open if the vehicle is involved in a crash.

Nissan Murano and Infiniti Steering Parts May Crack high

Reported May 27, 2021 · Campaign 21V402000 · Model years: 2021

Some Nissan Murano and Infiniti Q50, QX50, and QX60 vehicles have a front steering knuckle or rear axle housing that may not be strong enough because of a manufacturing heat-treatment problem. If the part is struck hard, such as when a vehicle hits a curb, it could deform. That could cause the driver to lose steering control, or a wheel could separate from the vehicle.

Infiniti QX50 Tire Information Placard May Be Illegible medium

Reported Jan 26, 2021 · Campaign 21V025000 · Model years: 2020

Nissan (Infiniti) is recalling certain 2020 Infiniti QX50 vehicles because the text on the Tire and Loading Information placard, including the tire size and loading capacity, may be illegible. If an owner cannot read this information, the wrong size tire could be installed, or the vehicle could be overloaded, which increases the risk of a crash.

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported Sep 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.

INFINITI QX50 knee airbag cover may detach in crash medium

Reported Sep 6, 2018 · Campaign 18V593000 · Model years: 2019

Nissan is recalling certain 2019 INFINITI QX50 vehicles. The passenger-side knee airbag has a trim cover that is meant to split along a built-in tear seam when the airbag goes off. In a crash that happens in very cold temperatures, that cover may instead separate from the knee airbag unit unexpectedly. INFINITI will notify owners, and retailers will replace the passenger-side knee airbag unit free of charge. The recall began October 23, 2018.

Infiniti QX50 recall questions

How many recalls does the Infiniti QX50 have?

NHTSA lists 7 safety recalls covering the 2019–2024 Infiniti QX50. 3 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Nissan, Infiniti Engine Bearings May Fail" (NHTSA campaign 25V437000), reported Jun 27, 2025.

Which Infiniti QX50 model year has the most recalls?

The 2019 Infiniti QX50 has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2023 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Infiniti QX50?

No. None of the 7 Infiniti QX50 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Infiniti QX50 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Infiniti dealer, including for second owners.
