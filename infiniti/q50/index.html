Infiniti Q50 and Q70 driveshaft can break high

Reported Jun 21, 2024 · Campaign 24V470000 · Model years: 2018

Nissan is recalling certain Infiniti Q50, Q70, and Q70L two-wheel drive vehicles because the driveshaft can develop fatigue and break.

Infiniti Q50, Q60 and QX80 rearview image may not display medium

Reported Aug 2, 2021 · Campaign 21V599000 · Model years: 2021

Nissan is recalling certain Infiniti Q50, Q60, and QX80 vehicles because of a software error. The error can shut off the screen that handles audio and video, so the image from the rear-view camera does not appear. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal rules for rear visibility. A dealer will update the software at no cost to the owner. Nissan mailed notification letters to owners, and the company has a customer service line for questions.

Nissan Murano and Infiniti Steering Parts May Crack high

Reported May 27, 2021 · Campaign 21V402000 · Model years: 2021

Some Nissan Murano and Infiniti Q50, QX50, and QX60 vehicles have a front steering knuckle or rear axle housing that may not be strong enough because of a manufacturing heat-treatment problem. If the part is struck hard, such as when a vehicle hits a curb, it could deform. That could cause the driver to lose steering control, or a wheel could separate from the vehicle.

Infiniti Q50, Q60 Engine May Stall From Fuel Loss high

Reported Apr 4, 2021 · Campaign 21V234000 · Model years: 2021

Infiniti is recalling certain Q50 and Q60 vehicles. These vehicles have a computer that manages engine performance. After the computer detects rapid acceleration, it may incorrectly reduce engine power and cut back the amount of fuel reaching the engine. This drop in fuel supply can leave the engine running low on fuel. When the engine does not get enough fuel, it may stall while the vehicle is being driven.

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported Sep 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.

Infiniti Q50 Software Flaw Can Stall Engine high

Reported Jul 27, 2017 · Campaign 17V476000 · Model years: 2018

These vehicles have a computer that controls the fuel pump, and it may have incorrect software installed. Because of this software error, the system may not correctly track how much fuel remains in the main tank, and it can let the fuel level get too low to keep supplying fuel to the engine. When that happens, the engine can stall while you are driving.

Infiniti Q50 recall questions

How many recalls does the Infiniti Q50 have?

NHTSA lists 6 safety recalls covering the 2018–2021 Infiniti Q50. 4 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Infiniti Q50 and Q70 driveshaft can break" (NHTSA campaign 24V470000), reported Jun 21, 2024.

Which Infiniti Q50 model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Infiniti Q50 has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2019 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Infiniti Q50?

No. None of the 6 Infiniti Q50 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Infiniti Q50 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Infiniti dealer, including for second owners.
