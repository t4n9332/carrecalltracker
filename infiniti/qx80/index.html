Nissan Armada and Infiniti QX80 Incorrect Weight Label low

Reported Jul 15, 2026 · Campaign 26V455000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2027

On certain Nissan Armada and Infiniti QX56/QX80 vehicles equipped with 22-inch wheels, the axle weight rating shown on the vehicle's certification label is incorrect. Because the number is wrong, an owner could load the vehicle beyond what it can safely carry. Nissan will correct the problem by installing new certification labels on the vehicle and mailing replacement labels directly to owners.

Nissan and Infiniti rearview camera may not show image medium

Reported Nov 26, 2025 · Campaign 25V821000 · Model years: 2025

Nissan is recalling certain 2025 Murano, 2025-2026 Armada, and Infiniti QX80 vehicles. A software error in the infotainment system, the main screen in the dashboard, can occur when the vehicle starts up, and then the rearview camera picture does not appear when the driver shifts into reverse. Because of that, these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety rule. A dealer can update the software, or the update can be sent to the vehicle over the air.

Infiniti QX80 Backup Camera May Freeze or Go Blank high

Reported Oct 3, 2024 · Campaign 24V747000 · Model years: 2025

Some 2025 INFINITI QX80 SUVs have a software error in the Around View Monitor system. When the vehicle is shifted into reverse, the rearview camera image can freeze and go blank instead of showing what is behind the vehicle. This means the driver may lose the rearview camera image while backing up, so they cannot rely on it to see what is behind the vehicle.

Nissan Rogue and INFINITI QX80 Rearview Camera May Go Blank medium

Reported Oct 3, 2024 · Campaign 24V748000 · Model years: 2025

A software error can leave the rearview camera screen blank when the vehicle is shifted into reverse. Instead of showing the view behind the vehicle, the display shows nothing. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard that requires that image to appear. The fix is a software update for the screen system, which can either be sent to the vehicle over the air or installed by a dealer.

Infiniti Q50, Q60 and QX80 rearview image may not display medium

Reported Aug 2, 2021 · Campaign 21V599000 · Model years: 2021

Nissan is recalling certain Infiniti Q50, Q60, and QX80 vehicles because of a software error. The error can shut off the screen that handles audio and video, so the image from the rear-view camera does not appear. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal rules for rear visibility. A dealer will update the software at no cost to the owner. Nissan mailed notification letters to owners, and the company has a customer service line for questions.

Nissan Armada and INFINITI QX80 fuel pump may fail high

Reported May 20, 2021 · Campaign 21V373000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan Armada and INFINITI QX80 vehicles built with V8 engines. A part inside the fuel pump, the impeller, can swell and jam inside the pump housing. When that happens the fuel pump stops working, so the engine can no longer be supplied with fuel and can shut off while the vehicle is being driven.

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported Sep 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.

Infiniti QX80 recall questions

How many recalls does the Infiniti QX80 have?

NHTSA lists 7 safety recalls covering the 2018–2027 Infiniti QX80. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Nissan Armada and Infiniti QX80 Incorrect Weight Label" (NHTSA campaign 26V455000), reported Jul 15, 2026.

Which Infiniti QX80 model year has the most recalls?

The 2025 Infiniti QX80 has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2022 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Infiniti QX80?

No. None of the 7 Infiniti QX80 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Infiniti QX80 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Infiniti dealer, including for second owners.
