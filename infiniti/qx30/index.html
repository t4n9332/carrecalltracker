3 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported 2019-09-12 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The backup camera and display settings can be adjusted so the rear view image no longer appears — and the system keeps that setting the next time you shift into reverse. That means the screen can stay blank when you back up and you cannot see what is behind the vehicle. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility rule. A dealer will update the camera settings software for free.

Infiniti QX30 seat belt anchor bolt wrongly installed high

Reported 2018-05-30 · Campaign 18V355000 · Model years: 2018

On some 2018 Infiniti QX30s, the bolt holding the bottom anchor point of the right-hand seat belt may have been installed incorrectly at the factory. That anchor is what keeps the lower end of the belt fastened to the body of the car. In a crash it could pull free, so the belt would not hold the person as it should. Dealers weld in a new retaining plate and fit a new anchor bolt at no cost. Nissan reported all affected vehicles were repaired by July 17, 2018.

Infiniti QX30 driver's airbag can deploy without a crash critical

Reported 2017-11-01 · Campaign 17V691000 · Model years: 2018

The steering wheel parts in these Infiniti QX30s may not be grounded well enough. If static electricity builds up and the coiled wiring inside the steering wheel that feeds the driver's airbag is already broken, the airbag can go off on its own, with no crash and no warning. A sudden deployment in your face while you are driving can injure you and cause you to lose control. Dealers add extra grounding to the steering parts to stop this.
