Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported Sep 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.

Infiniti QX30 Seat Belt Anchorage May Detach high

Reported May 30, 2018 · Campaign 18V355000 · Model years: 2018

On certain 2018 Infiniti QX30 vehicles, the bolt that holds the right-hand lower seat belt anchorage in place may not have been installed correctly during production. Because of this, the seat belt anchorage could detach if the vehicle is in a crash. As a result, these vehicles do not meet federal safety standards for seat belts and seat belt anchorages.

Infiniti QX30 Driver Air Bag May Deploy Unexpectedly high

Reported Nov 1, 2017 · Campaign 17V691000 · Model years: 2018

The steering components in your QX30 may not be grounded well enough. If static electricity builds up and discharges while the air bag's clockspring is broken, the driver's air bag can deploy on its own.

Infiniti QX30 recall questions

How many recalls does the Infiniti QX30 have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2018–2019 Infiniti QX30. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off" (NHTSA campaign 19V654000), reported Sep 12, 2019.

Which Infiniti QX30 model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Infiniti QX30 has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2019 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Infiniti QX30?

No. None of the 3 Infiniti QX30 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Infiniti QX30 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Infiniti dealer, including for second owners.
