Infiniti QX60 recalls

10 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Nissan Murano, Pathfinder, Infiniti QX60: front calipers may break high

Reported 2025-03-19 · Campaign 25V173000 · Model years: 2025

One or both front brake calipers — the parts that squeeze the brake pads against the wheel to slow the car — may not be as strong as they should be, and could break. If one breaks, you can lose braking at the front wheels, which do most of the stopping. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal brake safety standard. Dealers will inspect both front calipers and replace them where needed.

Nissan Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60 seat belt part loose medium

Reported 2024-03-05 · Campaign 24V176000 · Model years: 2024

On certain 2024 Nissan Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60 vehicles, a rivet is missing from the driver's-side front seat belt. That rivet holds down the part of the lap belt that pulls the belt tight in a crash. Without it, that part is not properly fastened to the vehicle, so the lap belt may not hold the person in the seat during a crash. A dealer inspects the belt and replaces the part if needed.

Nissan and Infiniti driver airbag may deploy improperly high

Reported 2024-02-27 · Campaign 24V154000 · Model years: 2024

Because of a manufacturing problem, the driver's airbag in these Nissan and Infiniti vehicles can soak up moisture. A damp airbag may not inflate the way it is supposed to in a crash. That means the airbag in the steering wheel might not protect the driver properly in a collision. There is no warning light or symptom you can check yourself. The dealer replaces the whole driver's airbag at no cost.

Infiniti QX60 headlights aim too low at speed medium

Reported 2023-12-04 · Campaign 23V814000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

These SUVs have headlights that aim themselves automatically as you drive. At certain higher speeds, the system can tilt the beams too far downward. That is the opposite of what you want at speed: the light lands close to the front of the vehicle instead of reaching far down the road, so you see less of what is coming. The dealer fixes it by changing settings in one of the vehicle's control modules.

Nissan Rogue, Pathfinder, Infiniti QX60 driver's seat may loosen high

Reported 2023-04-13 · Campaign 23V268000 · Model years: 2023

The driver's power seat may not be bolted tightly to its frame. Over time the welds holding the frame to a bracket at the back can break loose. A seat that isn't firmly attached may not hold the driver in place in a crash, so a crash that would have caused minor injuries could cause worse ones. A dealer checks the seat and replaces the seat cushion frame if needed, at no charge.

Infiniti QX60 second-row seat may not lock medium

Reported 2023-02-21 · Campaign 23V108000 · Model years: 2023

On some 2023 Infiniti QX60 vehicles, the sliding second-row seat can move back farther than it was designed to go. When that happens, the seat can be hard to move, get stuck, or fail to click into a locked position. A seat that is not locked in place can move during a crash, so it may not hold the person sitting there as it should and they could be hurt. A dealer will take out the second-row seat and put in a new one.

Nissan Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60 missing second-row seat bolt medium

Reported 2022-02-24 · Campaign 22V111000 · Model years: 2022

Some of these vehicles left the factory with a bolt missing from the track that holds the second-row seat to the floor. With that bolt gone, the seat is not anchored as strongly as it should be, and it could shift or move in a crash. Anyone riding in the second row could be hurt more badly as a result. A dealer will inspect the seat track and install a new mounting bolt if one is missing.

Nissan Murano and INFINITI models: weak steering and axle parts high

Reported 2021-05-27 · Campaign 21V402000 · Model years: 2020

Part of the front steering or the rear axle housing on these vehicles may be weaker than it should be, because it wasn't heat-treated correctly at the factory. If you hit something like a curb, that weak part can bend. Bending in the front can make you lose control of the steering. Bending in the back can let a wheel come off the vehicle. Either one can cause a crash.

Nissan and Infiniti vehicles: brake fluid leak may cause fire critical

Reported 2019-11-08 · Campaign 19V807000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan Murano, Maxima and Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60 vehicles. The part that controls the anti-lock brakes can let brake fluid leak onto a circuit board inside it. These vehicles were covered by an earlier recall and were either not repaired or were inspected and judged not to need a repair. This recall also covers more vehicles than the earlier one. Dealers will replace the anti-lock brake part at no cost to owners.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported 2019-09-12 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.
