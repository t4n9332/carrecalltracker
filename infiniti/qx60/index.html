Nissan Murano, Pathfinder, QX60 Brake Caliper May Break high

Reported Mar 19, 2025 · Campaign 25V173000 · Model years: 2025

Nissan is recalling certain Murano, Pathfinder, and INFINITI QX60 vehicles because one or both front brake caliper assemblies may have reduced strength and could break while the vehicle is being driven. If a caliper breaks, the vehicle can lose front brake function on that side, making it harder to slow down and stop the vehicle.

Nissan Pathfinder, Infiniti QX60 seat belt pretensioner issue high

Reported Mar 5, 2024 · Campaign 24V176000 · Model years: 2024

On some 2024 Nissan Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60 vehicles, a part in the left front seat belt that tightens the belt in a crash may not be attached correctly. A rivet holding it in place is missing. Because of this, the seat belt may not hold the occupant properly if the vehicle crashes. Dealers will inspect the seat belt and replace it if needed.

Nissan and Infiniti driver airbags may deploy improperly high

Reported Feb 27, 2024 · Campaign 24V154000 · Model years: 2024

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti cars and SUVs because of a problem that happened during manufacturing. The inflator in the driver's airbag, which is the part that fills the bag, may absorb moisture. If that happens, the airbag may deploy improperly. Dealers will replace the entire driver's airbag assembly at no cost to the owner. Nissan mailed owner notification letters on April 10, 2024.

INFINITI QX60 Headlights May Aim Too Low at Speed medium

Reported Dec 4, 2023 · Campaign 23V814000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

These Infiniti QX60 vehicles have headlights that adjust automatically as you drive. At certain high speeds, the system may aim the headlights downward when it should not. Headlights pointed the wrong way do not light the road as they are meant to, which cuts down on how well the driver can see. A dealer will change the settings in the module that controls the lights to correct the problem.

Nissan Rogue, Pathfinder, Infiniti QX60 Seat Weld Failure high

Reported Apr 13, 2023 · Campaign 23V268000 · Model years: 2023

In some Nissan Rogue and Pathfinder, and Infiniti QX60 vehicles, the driver's power seat may not be fully attached to its frame. Over time, the welds holding the seat frame to a bracket at the back of the seat can come apart. If this happens, the seat is not as sturdy as it should be. In a crash, a seat that isn't properly secured may not hold the driver in place the way it's supposed to.

Infiniti QX60 Second-Row Seat May Not Lock high

Reported Feb 21, 2023 · Campaign 23V108000 · Model years: 2023

On some 2023 Infiniti QX60 vehicles, the second-row adjustable seat may slide back farther than it should. It may also become hard to move, get stuck, or fail to lock into the correct position.

INFINITI QX60, Nissan Pathfinder Seat Track Bolt Missing high

Reported Feb 24, 2022 · Campaign 22V111000 · Model years: 2022

Some INFINITI QX60 and Nissan Pathfinder vehicles may have a second-row seat track that is missing a mounting bolt. This bolt is one of the parts required to anchor the seat belt system to the vehicle. Because the bolt may be missing, these vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard covering seat belt anchorages. The second-row seat track itself may not be as securely fastened to the vehicle as it should be as a result of this missing bolt.

Nissan Murano and Infiniti Steering Parts May Crack high

Reported May 27, 2021 · Campaign 21V402000 · Model years: 2020

Some Nissan Murano and Infiniti Q50, QX50, and QX60 vehicles have a front steering knuckle or rear axle housing that may not be strong enough because of a manufacturing heat-treatment problem. If the part is struck hard, such as when a vehicle hits a curb, it could deform. That could cause the driver to lose steering control, or a wheel could separate from the vehicle.

Nissan and Infiniti vehicles: brake fluid leak may cause fire critical

Reported Nov 8, 2019 · Campaign 19V807000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan Murano, Maxima and Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60 vehicles. The part that controls the anti-lock brakes can let brake fluid leak onto a circuit board inside it. These vehicles were covered by an earlier recall and were either not repaired or were inspected and judged not to need a repair. This recall also covers more vehicles than the earlier one. Dealers will replace the anti-lock brake part at no cost to owners.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported Sep 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.

Infiniti QX60 recall questions

How many recalls does the Infiniti QX60 have?

NHTSA lists 10 safety recalls covering the 2018–2025 Infiniti QX60. 8 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Nissan Murano, Pathfinder, QX60 Brake Caliper May Break" (NHTSA campaign 25V173000), reported Mar 19, 2025.

Which Infiniti QX60 model year has the most recalls?

The 2023 Infiniti QX60 has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2018 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Infiniti QX60?

Yes. 1 Infiniti QX60 recall carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Nissan and Infiniti vehicles: brake fluid leak may cause fire (19V807000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Infiniti QX60 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Infiniti dealer, including for second owners.
