Chevrolet Corvette recalls

10 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Chevrolet Corvette Fuel Leak Fire Risk critical

Reported 2025-08-21 · Campaign 25V536000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Some Chevrolet Corvette vehicles with a left-side radiator and fan combination may allow extra fuel to spill into the fuel-filler pocket area during refueling. That spilled fuel could then leak out and land on a nearby ignition source, such as a hot engine or exhaust part.

GM Cars: Electric Power Steering Assist May Fail high

Reported 2025-03-20 · Campaign 25V175000 · Model years: 2018

General Motors is recalling certain 2016-2018 Cadillac CTS, 2017-2018 Cadillac ATS, Chevrolet Camaro, and Chevrolet Corvette vehicles. The electric power steering assist may fail while the vehicle is being driven. This power steering system normally helps reduce the effort needed to turn the steering wheel, and this recall covers vehicles where that assist can stop working.

Corvette Seat Belts May Lock Out of Reach medium

Reported 2024-06-06 · Campaign 24V418000 · Model years: 2024

Some 2024-2025 Chevrolet Corvette vehicles have a driver seat belt, a passenger seat belt, or both, with a retractor that may lock while the belt is in its stowed position. When this happens, the seat belt becomes inaccessible, meaning the occupant cannot pull it out to buckle up.

Chevrolet Corvette Rear Half-Shaft Missing Ball Bearings high

Reported 2022-02-17 · Campaign 22V091000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Corvette vehicles because the rear half-shaft assemblies may be missing one or more ball bearings. This can cause the vehicle to unexpectedly lose the power that drives the wheels while you are driving.

GM vehicles' airbag warning light works inconsistently medium

Reported 2021-06-10 · Campaign 21V421000 · Model years: 2021

General Motors is recalling certain Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet and GMC vehicles. The module that passes messages between vehicle systems does not correctly handle a loss of communication with the airbag sensing module. As a result, the airbag warning light on the dashboard turns on inconsistently. A dealer can update that module's software, or the update can be sent to the vehicle over the air, free of charge. GM mailed owner notification letters on July 1, 2021.

Chevrolet, Cadillac, Buick Models Lose Brake Assist high

Reported 2020-09-24 · Campaign 20V588000 · Model years: 2020

Contamination at a sensor connection in the electronic brake boost system may interrupt communication between the sensor and the brake booster. This affects certain Chevrolet Corvette and Trailblazer, Cadillac CT4, CT5 and XT4, and Buick Encore GX vehicles. If that happens, the power assist that helps you brake could be lost, meaning you would need much more pedal force to slow or stop the vehicle. Dealers will replace the electronic brake boost module, free of charge.

Chevrolet Corvette Driver Seat Belt May Not Lock high

Reported 2020-09-24 · Campaign 20V581000 · Model years: 2020

General Motors is recalling certain Corvette vehicles because the driver's seat belt retractor may not lock as intended. This means the seat belt might not hold the driver securely in place the way it's designed to during a crash.

Chevrolet Corvette Hood Can Open While Driving high

Reported 2020-08-18 · Campaign 20V489000 · Model years: 2020

Some Corvettes can still be driven even when the front hood is not fully closed and latched, even though the vehicle gives visual and audible warnings. If the hood stays unlatched, it can pop open once the car reaches a certain speed. An open hood blocks the driver's view of the road ahead, which raises the risk of a crash.

Chevrolet Corvette front trunk release button may stop working medium

Reported 2020-08-06 · Campaign 20V461000 · Model years: 2020

General Motors is recalling certain 2020 Chevrolet Corvette vehicles. The release button inside the front trunk compartment may stop working after the vehicle has been shut off for ten minutes. Because of that, these vehicles do not meet the federal rule requiring a working release inside the trunk. Dealers will update the software in the vehicle's body control module so the release switch always works, free of charge. Owners may instead have the fix delivered as an over-the-air software update.

Corvette ZR1 Sensing Module Fault May Block Air Bags high

Reported 2018-05-31 · Campaign 18V360000 · Model years: 2019

GM says hard braking or hard acceleration in some Corvette ZR1 vehicles can cause the sensing diagnostic module to enter a fault state. When that happens, the module will not sense a crash or deploy the air bags if one occurs.
