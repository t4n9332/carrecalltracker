Chevrolet Silverado EV recalls

5 recalls covering model years 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Chevy Silverado EV, GMC Sierra EV Seat Belt Bolt high

Reported 2025-01-16 · Campaign 25V015000 · Model years: 2024

On some of these electric pickup trucks, the bolt that holds the rear center seat belt to the seat structure may not have been tightened enough during assembly. Because of this, the bolt securing that seat belt to the seat frame could be looser than it is supposed to be, instead of being properly fastened in place. That means the seat belt itself may not stay as firmly attached as it should.

Silverado EV Pedestrian Alert Sound May Be Too Quiet high

Reported 2024-12-18 · Campaign 24V937000 · Model years: 2025

On some Chevrolet Silverado EV work trucks, the pedestrian alert sound system may not produce sound loud enough.

GM Electric Vehicles May Lose Drive Power high

Reported 2024-05-09 · Campaign 24V320000 · Model years: 2024

In some 2024 Cadillac Lyriq, Chevrolet Blazer EV, Chevrolet Silverado EV, GMC Hummer EV, and GMC Sierra EV vehicles, the electric motors in the rear drive unit may have wires that aren't insulated well enough. If those wires touch each other, the vehicle can lose the power that drives it.

Chevrolet Silverado EV center seat belt may tear in crash high

Reported 2024-02-08 · Campaign 24V087000 · Model years: 2024

General Motors is recalling certain 2024 Chevrolet Silverado EV vehicles. The wrong bezel, the trim piece the belt passes through, was fitted on the second-row center seat belt, and it may damage or tear that belt in a crash. GM says passengers should not sit in the second-row center seat until the vehicle has been repaired. Dealers will replace the bezel free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 13, 2024. GM's number for this recall is N242430730.

Chevrolet Silverado EV and Hummer EV Seat Belt Bolts Loose high

Reported 2023-11-22 · Campaign 23V786000 · Model years: 2024

In some Chevrolet Silverado EV and GMC Hummer EV vehicles, the bolts that attach the left or right front seat belt buckle may not have been tightened properly. Because of this, that seat belt buckle may not stay attached and hold a passenger securely the way it's designed to. This means the front seat belt on the affected side might not restrain the driver or front passenger as firmly as intended.
