Chevrolet 5500HD recalls

4 recalls covering model years 2019. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Chevrolet Medium-Duty Trucks Brake Sensor Fire Risk critical

Reported 2023-04-13 · Campaign 23V266000 · Model years: 2019

A sensor that measures brake pressure may leak brake fluid into a nearby switch. That leak can cause an electrical short, which can overheat and raise the risk of a fire, whether the truck is being driven or parked.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Chevrolet 5500HD Rear Axle Bolts May Loosen high

Reported 2021-03-19 · Campaign 21V194000 · Model years: 2019

The nuts on the rear axle's U-bolts may not have been tightened properly during assembly. Over time, they can loosen, allowing the axle to shift out of alignment. If this happens, the driveshaft that sends power to the rear wheels can separate, causing a sudden loss of power and a stall while driving.

Chevrolet Medium-Duty Trucks ABS Warning Light Failure high

Reported 2020-05-29 · Campaign 20V319000 · Model years: 2019

If the vehicle's body control module loses contact with the brake control module, the ABS warning light on the dashboard may not turn on even when the anti-lock brake system isn't working correctly. That means a driver might not realize there's a brake problem. Dealers will update the body control module software to fix this, free of charge.

Chevrolet/Isuzu Trucks: Rear Wheel Lug Nuts Loose high

Reported 2019-11-20 · Campaign 19V835000 · Model years: 2019

On some Isuzu and Chevrolet medium-duty trucks, the lug nuts on the rear outer wheels may not have been properly tightened when installed. Over time, loose lug nuts can allow a wheel to loosen and separate from the vehicle. Dealers will inspect the rear outer wheel lug nuts and tighten or replace them as necessary.
