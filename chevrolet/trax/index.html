Buick and Chevrolet Rearview Camera May Show Blank Image medium

Reported Aug 20, 2026 · Campaign 26V539000 · Model years: 2025

General Motors is recalling certain Buick Envista, Buick Encore GX, Chevrolet Trailblazer, and Chevrolet Trax vehicles because the rearview camera screen may display a distorted or blank image instead of showing what's behind the vehicle clearly.

Buick, Chevrolet Instrument Panel May Go Blank high

Reported Nov 6, 2023 · Campaign 23V744000 · Model years: 2024

A software problem can cause the instrument panel display to intermittently go blank. This can happen either right when the vehicle is started or at any point while it is being driven. When the screen goes blank, it stops showing information that is normally displayed there, such as the vehicle's speed and any warning lights that would otherwise be lit. As a result, the driver may not be able to see that information for as long as the screen stays blank.

Chevrolet Trax Recalled for Weak Control Arm Weld high

Reported Sep 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V652000 · Model years: 2018

A joint in one or both front lower control arms may have been welded improperly. If that weld breaks, the control arm could partially come loose from the vehicle.

Chevrolet Trax Front Control Arm Weld May Break high

Reported Apr 18, 2019 · Campaign 19V312000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

General Motors (GM) is recalling certain Chevrolet Trax vehicles because a weld joining one or both front lower control arms may not have been done correctly. If an improperly welded joint like this breaks, the control arm could partially separate from the vehicle instead of remaining securely attached the way it is supposed to. This weld problem affects the left, the right, or both front lower control arms on affected vehicles.

Buick, Chevrolet Vehicles' Air Bags May Not Deploy high

Reported Nov 1, 2018 · Campaign 18V774000 · Model years: 2019

On certain 2019 Buick Encore, Chevrolet Spark, Traverse, and Trax vehicles, the computer module that controls the air bags may have been left switched to a factory 'manufacturing mode' instead of being switched over to normal driving mode before the vehicle was sold. While the module is in that mode, the air bags will not deploy if the vehicle is in a crash.

Chevrolet Trax recall questions

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Trax have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2018–2025 Chevrolet Trax. 4 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Buick and Chevrolet Rearview Camera May Show Blank Image" (NHTSA campaign 26V539000), reported Aug 20, 2026.

Which Chevrolet Trax model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Chevrolet Trax has the most, with 2 recalls. The 2024 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Chevrolet Trax?

No. None of the 5 Chevrolet Trax recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Chevrolet Trax has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Chevrolet dealer, including for second owners.
