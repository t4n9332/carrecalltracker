GM Cars: Electric Power Steering Assist May Fail high

Reported Mar 20, 2025 · Campaign 25V175000 · Model years: 2018

General Motors is recalling certain 2016-2018 Cadillac CTS, 2017-2018 Cadillac ATS, Chevrolet Camaro, and Chevrolet Corvette vehicles. The electric power steering assist may fail while the vehicle is being driven. This power steering system normally helps reduce the effort needed to turn the steering wheel, and this recall covers vehicles where that assist can stop working.

Cadillac and Camaro Transmission May Lock Wheels high

Reported Mar 6, 2025 · Campaign 25V148000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

The 10-speed transmission in these vehicles may become damaged internally, and that damage can cause the wheels to lock up while driving. A wheel locking up while driving increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will install new monitoring software in the transmission's computer to address the problem, free of charge.

Chevrolet Camaro Air Bag Emblem Can Break high

Reported Mar 25, 2021 · Campaign 21V211000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021

On these vehicles, the plastic 'Camaro' emblem on the steering wheel air bag cover may break apart when the driver's front air bag deploys. Pieces of the broken emblem could then be thrown outward from the air bag during deployment. Dealers will replace the entire front driver air bag module on the affected vehicles at no charge to the owner.

Chevrolet Camaro Axle Shaft Retaining Ring Missing high

Reported Mar 18, 2021 · Campaign 21V188000 · Model years: 2021

On certain Chevrolet Camaros, the left or right axle shaft may be missing a retaining ring. Without this ring in place, the axle shaft could come apart while the car is being driven. If that happens, the axle can no longer send power to the wheel it connects to, so the car can suddenly lose drive power while you're on the road.

GM Vehicles Seat Belt May Use Wrong Bolts high

Reported Dec 23, 2020 · Campaign 20V811000 · Model years: 2021

Incorrect bolts may have been used at one or more places where the seat belts attach to these vehicles. If the wrong bolt was used at any of these points, the seat belt may not be properly attached to the vehicle. GM dealers will inspect the fastening locations and replace any bolts that are found to be incorrect, so the seat belts are properly secured to the vehicle as they should be.

GM Trucks and SUVs: Fuel Pump Over-Pressurization Fire Risk high

Reported Nov 21, 2019 · Campaign 19V837000 · Model years: 2020

The fuel pump in these GM trucks and SUVs may be missing a part called a pressure regulator. Without it, the fuel system can build up too much pressure. This extra pressure can crack the fuel pump, allowing fuel to leak out. If that leaked fuel comes near something that can ignite it, it raises the chance of a fire starting in the vehicle.

Chevrolet Camaro recall questions

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Camaro have?

NHTSA lists 6 safety recalls covering the 2018–2022 Chevrolet Camaro. 6 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "GM Cars: Electric Power Steering Assist May Fail" (NHTSA campaign 25V175000), reported Mar 20, 2025.

Which Chevrolet Camaro model year has the most recalls?

The 2021 Chevrolet Camaro has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2019 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Chevrolet Camaro?

No. None of the 6 Chevrolet Camaro recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Chevrolet Camaro has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Chevrolet dealer, including for second owners.
