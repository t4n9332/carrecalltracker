Chevrolet Express Transmission Software May Cause Unintended Movement high

Reported Nov 7, 2024 · Campaign 24V839000 · Model years: 2023

GM is recalling certain Chevrolet Express and GMC Savana vans because of incorrect software in the transmission control module. This software problem can cause harsh shifting, reduced power, the vehicle slowing down on its own, the rear wheels locking up, or the vehicle moving in a direction the driver did not intend.
