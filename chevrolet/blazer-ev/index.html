Chevrolet Blazer EV recalls

6 recalls covering model years 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Chevrolet Blazer EV parking brake wiring may fail high

Reported 2026-01-22 · Campaign 26V031000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Some 2024-2025 Chevrolet Blazer EVs that were fixed under an earlier recall may have been repaired incorrectly. The wiring that runs to the rear parking brake can get damaged or corroded. If that happens, the parking brake may switch on by itself while you are driving, or it may stop working at all when you are parked — meaning the vehicle could roll away on its own.

Chevrolet Blazer EV parking brake wiring can fail high

Reported 2025-06-25 · Campaign 25V433000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

On some 2024-2025 Chevrolet Blazer EVs, the wiring that runs to the rear parking brake can get rubbed through or corroded. Damaged wiring can make the parking brake grab on its own while you are driving, or it can leave the parking brake unable to hold at all, so the vehicle could roll away after you park it. Dealers will check the wiring, reroute it and add protective tape, and replace it if it is already damaged, free of charge.

Chevrolet Blazer and Equinox doors may unlatch and open high

Reported 2024-10-02 · Campaign 24V737000 · Model years: 2025

The metal catches that your doors latch onto — one on each of the four side doors — were not heat-treated correctly at the factory, so they can break. If one breaks, that door can pop open on its own, including while the vehicle is moving. Someone could fall out or be hit by the swinging door. Every affected vehicle is still sitting on a dealer lot, so no owner letters are being sent.

Chevrolet Blazer EV Front Control Arm May Break critical

Reported 2024-06-28 · Campaign 24V487000 · Model years: 2024

The lower control arm is a metal arm that connects the front wheel to the body of the vehicle and keeps it pointed where you steer. On some 2024 Blazer EVs, the arm on the right front was made incorrectly and can crack and break apart. If that happens while you are driving, you can lose control of the vehicle. The dealer will inspect the part and install a new one if it is faulty.

Cadillac Lyriq and Other GM EVs Can Lose Power high

Reported 2024-05-09 · Campaign 24V320000 · Model years: 2024

The wires inside the electric motor that drives the rear wheels may not be properly insulated. If two of them touch, the motor can lose power, and the vehicle can stop pulling while you are driving. Because these are electric vehicles, that means no power to the wheels at all. General Motors is fixing it by replacing the entire rear drive unit, the motor package at the back of the vehicle, free of charge at your dealer.

Chevrolet and GMC door strikers can break unexpectedly high

Reported 2023-12-21 · Campaign 23V869000 · Model years: 2024

On certain 2024 Chevrolet Blazer, Blazer EV, Equinox and GMC Terrain vehicles, the door strikers can crack and break. A striker is the metal loop on the door frame that the door latch grabs to hold the door shut. If one breaks, that door can swing open on its own — including while you are driving down the road. Dealers will replace all four door strikers and their bolts for free.
