Chevy Express and GMC Savana Cutaway Brake Line Risk high

Reported Sep 19, 2024 · Campaign 24V702000 · Model years: 2018

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Express Cutaway and GMC Savana Cutaway vehicles built with optional body mounts. On these vehicles, the brake line may not have enough clearance from the body mounts. Because of this, the brake line can rub against the body mount and become damaged over time, which can cause the brake line to leak brake fluid.

Chevrolet Express Cutaway Van Heater Wiring Risk critical

Reported Apr 6, 2023 · Campaign 23V247000 · Model years: 2018, 2022, 2023

Some of these vehicles are built with wiring for an optional rear heating and air-conditioning blower motor. When that wiring is left unused, its connectors and wires are left exposed instead of being sealed. Left exposed like this, the wiring can corrode over time, and corroded wiring in that circuit can also overheat.
