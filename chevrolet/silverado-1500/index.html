Chevrolet Silverado, GMC Sierra Roof Air Bag Inflator Risk high

Reported May 21, 2026 · Campaign 26V325000 · Model years: 2018

The end cap on the roof rail air bag inflator, on the left or right side, may come loose, or the inflator itself may rupture. This can let compressed gas escape and send the end cap or inflator pieces into the vehicle.

Chevy Silverado and GMC Sierra Roof Air Bag Defect high

Reported Mar 19, 2026 · Campaign 26V166000 · Model years: 2018

Certain Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra crew cab trucks have a roof rail air bag whose inflator may have a problem. The end cap on the left or right side inflator could detach, or the inflator itself could rupture. If that happens, it can release compressed gas and send the end cap or pieces of the inflator into the vehicle.

Chevrolet Silverado, GMC Sierra Transmission Wheel Lockup Risk high

Reported Feb 12, 2026 · Campaign 26V083000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Certain Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra trucks may not have been fully fixed by an earlier recall that addressed this same transmission problem. In these trucks, the transmission control valve can fail. When that happens, the rear wheels may lock up suddenly while the truck is being driven.

Silverado, Sierra Roof Airbag Inflator May Rupture high

Reported Jun 26, 2025 · Campaign 25V432000 · Model years: 2018

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra pickup trucks. These trucks have airbags built into the roof rail, above the doors. On the left or right side, the metal end cap on the airbag inflator may come loose and detach, or the inflator itself may crack open, or rupture.

GM Trucks and SUVs Engine May Fail high

Reported Apr 24, 2025 · Campaign 25V274000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Certain Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC trucks and SUVs with a 6.2L V8 gas engine may have manufacturing defects in the connecting rod and/or crankshaft. These defects can damage the engine and cause it to fail.

Diesel GM trucks, SUVs risk rear wheel lock-up high

Reported Oct 24, 2024 · Campaign 24V797000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

On certain diesel Chevrolet, GMC and Cadillac trucks and SUVs, the transmission control valve may fail. If it fails, the rear wheels can lock up while the vehicle is being driven.

Chevrolet Silverado, GMC Sierra Side Airbag Inflator Defect high

Reported Oct 10, 2024 · Campaign 24V756000 · Model years: 2018

Certain Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra crew cab trucks have a problem with the roof-rail side curtain airbag. The end cap on the left or right side inflator may separate from the inflator, or the inflator's sidewall may split open. If this happens, compressed gas inside can escape suddenly, sending the end cap or other pieces flying into the vehicle.

GM SUVs, Trucks Brake Warning Light May Fail high

Reported Sep 12, 2024 · Campaign 24V674000 · Model years: 2023

Certain Chevrolet, GMC, and Cadillac SUVs and trucks may have a software problem in the brake control module. This software is supposed to turn on a warning light when the brake fluid level gets too low, but it may fail to do so. Because of this, a driver might not realize the brake fluid is low, since the vehicle isn't giving them the warning it's designed to show.

Silverado trailer hitch bracket may let trailer detach high

Reported Aug 21, 2024 · Campaign 24V626000 · Model years: 2024

OEM Systems is recalling certain 2024 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 trucks. The rear bumper mounting bracket that the trailer hitch attaches to may not be strong enough for towing. This could allow a trailer being towed to detach from the vehicle.

GM Vehicles Daytime Running Lights Stay On medium

Reported Dec 8, 2022 · Campaign 22V903000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

In certain Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC vehicles, the daytime running lights may not turn off when the headlights are switched on. This can create glare and reduce visibility for other drivers.

Chevrolet Silverado Airbags May Not Deploy high

Reported Nov 23, 2022 · Campaign 22V873000 · Model years: 2022

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Silverado 1500 trucks because the computer that controls the airbags was left in a factory testing setting and was never switched on before the truck was shipped. Because of this, the airbags may not work the way they're supposed to in a crash. General Motors says these trucks were sent back to the factory and the computer was turned on before any of them were sold to a customer.

Silverado and Sierra sport bar brake light may fail medium

Reported Jun 30, 2022 · Campaign 22V463000 · Model years: 2022

Some of these trucks are fitted with an accessory sport bar. The sport bar contains a brake light mounted up high, and that light may not work. The bar may also block the truck's own high-mounted brake light. Because of this, the trucks may not meet the federal standard covering lamps, reflective devices and related equipment. General Motors is recalling certain 2022 Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra trucks that have this accessory bar installed.

Chevrolet Silverado, GMC Sierra Spare Tire Affects Antilock Brakes medium

Reported Dec 16, 2021 · Campaign 21V985000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Certain Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500 trucks were sold with accessory road wheels and tires plus a 17-inch spare. That spare tire is not compatible with the accessory wheels, and under some conditions it can keep the anti-lock braking system from working properly. Dealers will give owners a new 17-inch spare tire and wheel that matches the accessory wheels, add a new spare tire label, and provide an owner's manual insert explaining which spare to use.

Chevrolet Silverado Driver Air Bag May Not Deploy high

Reported Jul 1, 2021 · Campaign 21V495000 · Model years: 2021

Certain 2021 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 vehicles may have a front driver's side air bag module missing one or more metal tabs. These tabs secure the plastic air bag cover to its housing, so without them the air bag may not deploy properly.

Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra Tire Failure Risk high

Reported Jun 3, 2021 · Campaign 21V423000 · Model years: 2019

Some tires on these trucks were left in the curing process too long during manufacturing. Tires that were cured too long can develop a break in the sidewall, causing sudden air loss, or the edge of the tire's belt can separate, causing the tread to come apart from the tire. Either problem can cause the driver to lose control of the vehicle.

GM Vehicles Recalled for Over-Cured Tire Failure Risk critical

Reported Feb 25, 2021 · Campaign 21V115000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

The tires on these vehicles were left in the curing process for too long during manufacturing. Tires that are cured too long can develop a break in the sidewall, which can cause sudden air loss, or the tire's belt edge can separate, causing the tread or belt to come apart. Either problem can cause the driver to lose control of the vehicle.

GM Vehicles Seat Belt May Use Wrong Bolts high

Reported Dec 23, 2020 · Campaign 20V811000 · Model years: 2021

Incorrect bolts may have been used at one or more places where the seat belts attach to these vehicles. If the wrong bolt was used at any of these points, the seat belt may not be properly attached to the vehicle. GM dealers will inspect the fastening locations and replace any bolts that are found to be incorrect, so the seat belts are properly secured to the vehicle as they should be.

Chevrolet and GMC Trucks and SUVs Seat Belt Bracket Issue high

Reported Dec 17, 2020 · Campaign 20V792000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

In the front-row center seating position, the seat belt bracket may not be properly attached to the seat frame. If this bracket is not secured, the seat belt in that seat may not hold a passenger properly. GM dealers will check both the left and right front-row center seat belt bracket attachments and put them back together correctly if they find a problem.

GM trucks and SUVs recalled for overcured tires high

Reported Nov 5, 2020 · Campaign 20V684000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

This recall covers certain Buick Enclave, Cadillac XT5, GMC Acadia, and Chevrolet Traverse vehicles, along with certain Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500 trucks. These vehicles may have been built with Continental tires that were overcured during manufacturing.

Chevy Silverado and GMC Sierra driveshaft may separate high

Reported Oct 22, 2020 · Campaign 20V650000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra trucks because one of the driveshaft joints may not have been welded properly. If the weld fails, the driveshaft can separate from the vehicle while it's being driven.

GM Trucks And SUVs Lose Brake Power Assist medium

Reported Oct 1, 2020 · Campaign 20V603000 · Model years: 2018

On these Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC trucks and SUVs with a 5.3L or 6.2L V8 engine, the mechanical vacuum pump that helps power the brakes can lose output over time. As the pump weakens, the vehicle gets less power assist for braking, so it takes more pedal effort and a longer distance to stop.

GM SUVs, Trucks: Roof-Rail Air Bag May Underperform high

Reported Jul 30, 2020 · Campaign 20V446000 · Model years: 2020

On affected vehicles, a part called the diffuser, inside the roof-rail air bag inflator, may not have been properly attached to the inflator. This means the diffuser could come apart from the inflator when the air bag deploys.

Chevy Silverado, GMC Sierra Brake Caliper Bolts high

Reported Feb 27, 2020 · Campaign 20V116000 · Model years: 2020

General Motors is recalling certain 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500 trucks. The bolts that attach the front and/or rear brake calipers may not have been heat treated correctly. Because these bolts were not properly treated, they may break when placed under load. If a caliper bolt breaks, the brake caliper could shift out of position or interfere with how the wheel spins.

Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra may lose brake assist high

Reported Jan 30, 2020 · Campaign 20V055000 · Model years: 2019

These trucks received a software update for the module that controls the electronic brake system. That software contains an error, and because of it the truck's electronic brake assist may be switched off. As a result, the vehicles do not meet federal requirements for electronic stability control and for light vehicle brake systems. General Motors is recalling certain 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500 trucks, and dealers will reprogram the module at no charge.

Chevrolet Silverado, GMC Sierra Battery Cable Connection Issue critical

Reported Dec 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V888000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On some Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500 trucks, the positive battery cable ring may have been made with too much glue. This can create a loose or intermittent connection between the cable and the alternator. That loose connection can cause the engine to stall while driving, and in some cases it can generate arcing that produces enough heat to damage nearby parts.

Chevy, Cadillac, GMC vehicles may lose stability control high

Reported Dec 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V889000 · Model years: 2019

This recall affects certain 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 1500, Cadillac CT6, and GMC Sierra 1500 vehicles. A software error in a brake computer may cause the vehicle's stability control and antilock brake systems to shut off. If this happens, the warning lights on the dashboard that are supposed to alert the driver won't turn on, so the driver may not know these safety systems have stopped working.

GM Trucks and SUVs: Fuel Pump Over-Pressurization Fire Risk high

Reported Nov 21, 2019 · Campaign 19V837000 · Model years: 2020

The fuel pump in these GM trucks and SUVs may be missing a part called a pressure regulator. Without it, the fuel system can build up too much pressure. This extra pressure can crack the fuel pump, allowing fuel to leak out. If that leaked fuel comes near something that can ignite it, it raises the chance of a fire starting in the vehicle.

Silverado and Sierra Seat Belt Fire Risk critical

Reported Nov 14, 2019 · Campaign 19V814000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

These trucks have carpet floor covering and front seat belt pretensioners. When a seat belt pretensioner deploys, it releases hot gas. In these vehicles, that hot gas may escape through an opening in the pretensioner's mounting bracket instead of staying contained. If that hot gas reaches the carpet inside the vehicle, it could ignite the carpet material and start a fire inside the cabin.

Chevrolet, GMC trucks and SUVs may brake and pull unexpectedly high

Reported Oct 24, 2019 · Campaign 19V761000 · Model years: 2018

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Suburban, Tahoe and Silverado 1500 and GMC Yukon and Sierra 1500 vehicles built with a 5.3-liter engine, a 3.08-ratio rear axle and four-wheel drive. If a wheel-speed sensor fails while the vehicle is traveling between 41 and 60 mph in four-wheel or automatic mode, the software in the electronic brake control module may turn on the driveline-protection system when it should not.

GM Trucks and SUVs Brake Assist May Weaken high

Reported Sep 6, 2019 · Campaign 19V645000 · Model years: 2018

In some Silverado, Tahoe, Sierra and Yukon trucks and SUVs, the vacuum pump that helps power the brakes may lose vacuum over time. As the vacuum drops, braking takes more effort and the vehicle needs a longer distance to stop.

Chevrolet Silverado and GMC Sierra Passenger Airbag May Fail high

Reported Dec 6, 2018 · Campaign 18V868000 · Model years: 2019

The front passenger airbag module in certain Chevrolet Silverado 1500 and GMC Sierra 1500 crew cab trucks may have been damaged while the truck was being built. Because of that damage, the passenger airbag may not inflate the way it should if the truck is in a crash, so it may not protect the passenger as intended. Dealers will replace the passenger-side airbag module with a new one at no charge to the owner.

Chevrolet Silverado 1500 recall questions

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Silverado 1500 have?

NHTSA lists 31 safety recalls covering the 2018–2024 Chevrolet Silverado 1500. 27 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Chevrolet Silverado, GMC Sierra Roof Air Bag Inflator Risk" (NHTSA campaign 26V325000), reported May 21, 2026.

Which Chevrolet Silverado 1500 model year has the most recalls?

The 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 has the most, with 12 recalls. The 2024 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Chevrolet Silverado 1500?

No. None of the 31 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Chevrolet Silverado 1500 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Chevrolet dealer, including for second owners.
