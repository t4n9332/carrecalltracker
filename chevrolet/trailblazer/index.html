Chevrolet Trailblazer recalls

5 recalls covering model years 2021, 2022, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Buick and Chevrolet Rearview Camera May Show Blank Image medium

Reported 2026-08-20 · Campaign 26V539000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

General Motors is recalling certain Buick Envista, Buick Encore GX, Chevrolet Trailblazer, and Chevrolet Trax vehicles because the rearview camera screen may display a distorted or blank image instead of showing what's behind the vehicle clearly.

Buick Encore GX and Chevrolet Trailblazer emergency jack can break high

Reported 2021-06-10 · Campaign 21V440000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

General Motors is recalling certain Buick Encore GX and Chevrolet Trailblazer vehicles because of a problem with the emergency jack that comes with the vehicle, the tool that lifts the vehicle off the ground. If the jack is not placed the way the owner's manual describes, it can break while it is holding the vehicle up. Dealers will replace the jack with a new one at no cost to the owner.

Chevrolet, Cadillac, Buick Models Lose Brake Assist high

Reported 2020-09-24 · Campaign 20V588000 · Model years: 2021

Contamination at a sensor connection in the electronic brake boost system may interrupt communication between the sensor and the brake booster. This affects certain Chevrolet Corvette and Trailblazer, Cadillac CT4, CT5 and XT4, and Buick Encore GX vehicles. If that happens, the power assist that helps you brake could be lost, meaning you would need much more pedal force to slow or stop the vehicle. Dealers will replace the electronic brake boost module, free of charge.

Buick Encore and Chevy Trailblazer Seat Bolts Missing medium

Reported 2020-09-03 · Campaign 20V537000 · Model years: 2021

On some vehicles, one or both front seats may be missing the bolts that attach the back of the seat to the vehicle. Without these attachment bolts, a seat with someone sitting in it can shift or move out of position if the vehicle is in a crash, which increases the risk that the occupant could be injured.

Chevrolet Trailblazer Missing Tire Pressure Label Info low

Reported 2020-05-21 · Campaign 20V295000 · Model years: 2021

Some Trailblazers with optional 17-inch sport terrain tires have a certification label that's missing the correct tire size and cold tire pressure information for the front and rear tires. Without those correct numbers on the label, you could end up putting the wrong amount of air in your tires.
