Chevrolet Trailblazer recalls

4 recalls covering model years 2021, 2022. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Buick Encore GX and Chevrolet Trailblazer Jack Can Break medium

Reported 2021-06-10 · Campaign 21V440000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

These vehicles came with an emergency jack, the small tool you use to lift the vehicle when you change a flat tire. If the jack is not placed exactly where the owner's manual says it should go, it can crack and break while the vehicle is raised on it. The vehicle can then drop, which could injure anyone underneath or beside it. GM is replacing the jack for free, but parts were not ready when the first letters went out.

GM Brake Assist Can Fail on Corvette, Trailblazer, Cadillacs high

Reported 2020-09-24 · Campaign 20V588000 · Model years: 2021

These vehicles use an electric system that helps boost your braking, so the pedal feels easy to press. Dirt or moisture on a sensor connection can cut off the signal between the sensor and that booster. If that happens, the power assist goes away and the brake pedal gets very hard to push. The car will still stop, but you have to push much harder and it takes longer. GM dealers replace the brake booster module at no cost.

Buick Encore GX and Chevrolet Trailblazer missing front seat bolts high

Reported 2020-09-03 · Campaign 20V537000 · Model years: 2021

The bolts that hold the back of a front seat down to the floor may have been left out at the factory, on one or both front seats. A seat that is not fully bolted down can shift or move during a crash, so whoever is sitting in it is more likely to be hurt. A dealer will check the attachments on both front seats and install any bolts that are missing.

Chevrolet Trailblazer label missing tire size and pressure low

Reported 2020-05-21 · Campaign 20V295000 · Model years: 2021

The information label on certain 2021 Chevrolet Trailblazers built with the optional 17-inch sport terrain tires is incomplete. It does not list the tire size or the correct cold tire pressure for the front and rear tires, which federal rules require it to show. Without that information, an owner could put too much or too little air in the tires. GM is mailing corrected labels that owners stick on themselves, at no cost.
