Chevrolet G4500 ambulance brake lights may come on wrongly medium

Reported Apr 10, 2019 · Campaign 19V285000 · Model years: 2018

North Central Emergency Vehicles is recalling certain 2016 through 2018 ambulances remounted on Ford E-350, Ford E-450 and Chevrolet G4500 chassis that have Whelen brake lights. Depending on how a particular vehicle is wired, the brake lights can come on even when the driver has not pressed the brake pedal. Dealers will replace the brake lights at no cost to the owner.

Ambulance Brake Lights May Turn On By Mistake medium

Reported Mar 26, 2019 · Campaign 19V236000 · Model years: 2018

Depending on how the vehicle is wired, the Whelen brake lights on these ambulances may illuminate even when the brake pedal has not been pressed. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard covering lamps and lighting equipment.
