Chevrolet G4500 ambulance brake lights turn on by themselves low

Reported 2019-04-10 · Campaign 19V285000 · Model years: 2018

These ambulances were built on Ford and Chevrolet van chassis and fitted with Whelen brake lights. Depending on how the vehicle was wired, the brake lights can come on when nobody is pressing the brake pedal. Drivers behind may think the ambulance is slowing or stopping when it is not, and they cannot tell a real stop from a false one. The company will have dealers replace the brake lights at no charge.

Excellance ambulances: brake lights can turn on by themselves medium

Reported 2019-03-26 · Campaign 19V236000 · Model years: 2018

These are ambulances and emergency vehicles built on Ford, Chevrolet, Dodge, Freightliner and International chassis and fitted with Whelen brake lights. Depending on how the vehicle was wired, the brake lights can come on even when the driver is not pressing the brake pedal. That breaks the federal rule for vehicle lamps. Whelen replaces the brake lights free of charge, and owners were told to contact Whelen directly rather than a dealer. The recall began March 21, 2019.
