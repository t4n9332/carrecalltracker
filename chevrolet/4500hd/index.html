Chevrolet Medium-Duty Trucks Brake Sensor Fire Risk critical

Reported Apr 13, 2023 · Campaign 23V266000 · Model years: 2021

A sensor that measures brake pressure may leak brake fluid into a nearby switch. That leak can cause an electrical short, which can overheat and raise the risk of a fire, whether the truck is being driven or parked.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Chevrolet Heavy-Duty Trucks Steering Lock Nuts May Break high

Reported Nov 23, 2022 · Campaign 22V874000 · Model years: 2021

Certain 2021-2022 Chevrolet 4500HD, 5500HD and 6500HD trucks have hex flange lock nuts in the steering and suspension joints that weren't heat-treated correctly during manufacturing. Because of this, these nuts may break. If a nut breaks, the joint it was holding together can lose tension, which can affect how the steering works. Dealers will replace the affected lock nuts as needed to fix this, free of charge.

How many recalls does the Chevrolet 4500HD have?

NHTSA lists 2 safety recalls covering the 2021 Chevrolet 4500HD. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Chevrolet Medium-Duty Trucks Brake Sensor Fire Risk" (NHTSA campaign 23V266000), reported Apr 13, 2023.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Chevrolet 4500HD?

Yes. 1 Chevrolet 4500HD recall carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Chevrolet Medium-Duty Trucks Brake Sensor Fire Risk (23V266000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Chevrolet 4500HD has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Chevrolet dealer, including for second owners.
