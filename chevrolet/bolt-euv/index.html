Chevrolet Bolt EV/EUV: Battery Fire Risk When Fully Charged critical

Reported Oct 31, 2024 · Campaign 24V812000 · Model years: 2022

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Bolt EV and Bolt EUV vehicles that were previously repaired under an earlier recall. The installation of advanced diagnostic software on these vehicles may have failed. Because of this, the high voltage battery could catch fire when it is charged to full or nearly full capacity. A battery fire increases the risk of injury to occupants.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Chevrolet Bolt EV/EUV: Battery Software May Miss Fire Risk critical

Reported Jun 27, 2024 · Campaign 24V481000 · Model years: 2022

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Bolt EV and Bolt EUV vehicles that were previously repaired under an earlier recall for the vehicle's battery. On these vehicles, the advanced diagnostic software meant to watch for defective battery modules may not have been installed correctly. Because of this, the software may fail to detect a defective battery module, which could possibly result in a battery fire.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Chevrolet Bolt EV/EUV Battery Fire Risk critical

Reported Aug 20, 2021 · Campaign 21V650000 · Model years: 2022

The high voltage battery can catch fire when it is charged to full or nearly full. GM has given owners several steps to reduce this risk while they wait for repairs, including limiting how full they charge the battery and parking outside after charging. GM will replace any battery modules that are found to be defective, free of charge.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Chevrolet Bolt EV and EUV Airbag Bolt Loose high

Reported Jul 8, 2021 · Campaign 21V517000 · Model years: 2022

In some Chevrolet Bolt EV and Bolt EUV vehicles, one of the two bolts that hold the driver's front airbag to the steering wheel may be missing, or it may not have been tightened enough.

Chevrolet Bolt EUV recall questions

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Bolt EUV have?

NHTSA lists 4 safety recalls covering the 2022 Chevrolet Bolt EUV. 4 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Chevrolet Bolt EV/EUV: Battery Fire Risk When Fully Charged" (NHTSA campaign 24V812000), reported Oct 31, 2024.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Chevrolet Bolt EUV?

Yes. 3 Chevrolet Bolt EUV recalls carry a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Chevrolet Bolt EV/EUV: Battery Fire Risk When Fully Charged (24V812000); Chevrolet Bolt EV/EUV: Battery Software May Miss Fire Risk (24V481000); Chevrolet Bolt EV/EUV Battery Fire Risk (21V650000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Chevrolet Bolt EUV has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Chevrolet dealer, including for second owners.
