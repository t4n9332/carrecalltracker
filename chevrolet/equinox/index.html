Chevrolet Equinox recalls

16 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Chevy Blazer, Equinox, GMC Terrain Door Strikers May Break high

Reported 2023-12-21 · Campaign 23V869000 · Model years: 2024

General Motors, LLC is recalling certain 2024 Chevrolet Blazer, Blazer EV, Equinox, and GMC Terrain vehicles because the door strikers may fracture. If a door striker fractures, the door it is attached to can open unexpectedly while the vehicle is being driven. Dealers will replace all four door strikers, along with the bolts that attach them, on affected vehicles at no cost to owners.

Chevrolet Equinox, GMC Terrain child seat anchors blocked by coating high

Reported 2023-05-11 · Campaign 23V339000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain vehicles. The four lower child seat anchor bars in the back seat may have too much powder coating on them, and the extra finish could keep a child seat from being attached to the bars. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for child restraint anchorage systems. Dealers will inspect the bars and, if needed, remove and replace the finish, free of charge.

Chevrolet Equinox fuel pump may stall engine high

Reported 2023-01-19 · Campaign 23V013000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Some 2021-2022 Chevrolet Equinox and 2022 GMC Terrain vehicles may have a fuel pump module that does not consistently provide enough fuel to the engine. Because the fuel supply can be inconsistent, the engine may unexpectedly stall out while the vehicle is being driven, rather than running smoothly and continuously as it should.

Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain Airbag Sensor Issue high

Reported 2022-12-15 · Campaign 22V929000 · Model years: 2023

In certain vehicles with ventilated front passenger seats, the system that senses who is sitting in the front passenger seat may not correctly recognize when a small child or a child seat is there. This system is supposed to turn off the front passenger air bag in that situation. If it doesn't detect the child properly, the air bag may stay on instead of turning off.

Chevrolet Equinox, GMC Terrain Loose Driver Seat Bolt high

Reported 2022-09-29 · Campaign 22V724000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Certain Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain vehicles may have a problem with the driver's seat. A bolt that holds the seat frame in place, allowing it to adjust up and down, may be stripped or loose. If this bolt isn't secure, the seat may not properly hold the driver in place during a crash, which increases the risk of injury.

GM Vehicles: Driver's Seat Weld May Fail high

Reported 2022-05-19 · Campaign 22V359000 · Model years: 2022

General Motors is recalling certain 2021 Chevrolet Malibu and 2022 Chevrolet Equinox, Blazer, and Cadillac XT4 vehicles. The driver's seat cushion frame may have a weld that was not done correctly. This faulty weld is located in the power tilt-adjustment mechanism, which is the part that lets the driver's seat tilt up and down. The improper weld affects the frame that supports the driver's seat cushion.

Chevy Equinox, GMC Terrain Tires May Separate high

Reported 2021-08-05 · Campaign 21V612000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

These vehicles come equipped with certain Hankook tires. Some of these tires may not have been manufactured with enough curative agent, a material used in making the tire. Because of this shortage, the tread on the tire can separate from the rest of the tire while the vehicle is being driven, rather than staying bonded to it as it should.

GM Vehicles Seat Belt May Use Wrong Bolts high

Reported 2020-12-23 · Campaign 20V811000 · Model years: 2021

Incorrect bolts may have been used at one or more places where the seat belts attach to these vehicles. If the wrong bolt was used at any of these points, the seat belt may not be properly attached to the vehicle. GM dealers will inspect the fastening locations and replace any bolts that are found to be incorrect, so the seat belts are properly secured to the vehicle as they should be.

GM transmission bolts missing, leak and fire risk critical

Reported 2020-10-29 · Campaign 20V668000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

On certain GM vehicles, the endcap on the start/stop transmission accumulator may be missing bolts. This can let transmission fluid leak out. Over time, the leak can get bad enough that the vehicle loses the ability to move forward. If the leaking fluid comes into contact with something that can ignite it, it could catch fire.

Chevrolet Equinox rear brakes may drag and overheat high

Reported 2019-10-03 · Campaign 19V704000 · Model years: 2020

General Motors is recalling certain 2020 Chevrolet Equinox vehicles. The rear brake calipers may have been built with the wrong lubricant, which can make the rubber seals swell. Swollen seals can leave the rear brakes dragging instead of releasing fully. Dealers will replace the rear brake calipers along with the brake hose gasket, brake fluid and the lubricant used between the wheel and hub. There is no charge to the owner for this work.

Chevrolet Equinox AWD Fuel Tank Seam Leak critical

Reported 2019-09-19 · Campaign 19V667000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Equinox all-wheel drive vehicles. These vehicles have a fuel tank with a seam located near the fuel inlet. On some of these vehicles, that seam may not have been properly sealed when the fuel tank was manufactured. Because the seam was not properly sealed, fuel can leak out of the tank along that seam, rather than staying safely contained inside the tank as it should.

Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain Rear Seat Weld Problem medium

Reported 2019-03-07 · Campaign 19V184000 · Model years: 2019

Certain Chevrolet Equinox and GMC Terrain vehicles may have a problem with the rear right seat. The bracket that holds the head restraint, the padded part at the top of the seat back, may not have enough welds holding it in place. If the vehicle is in a crash, the person sitting in that rear right seat may be more likely to be hurt. Dealers will replace the frame of that seat back free of charge.

Chevrolet, Buick, Cadillac, GMC Rear Brake Issue high

Reported 2018-08-30 · Campaign 18V576000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The rear brake caliper pistons on these vehicles may not have enough of a protective coating, which can allow gas pockets to form inside them. If this happens, it can reduce how well the rear brakes perform. This affects a range of Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC models. To fix the problem, GM will notify owners, and dealers will bleed the vehicle's brake system. This repair is free of charge.

GM Vehicles Fuel Pump May Detach, Damage Line high

Reported 2018-05-31 · Campaign 18V358000 · Model years: 2018

General Motors is recalling a range of vehicles, including the Buick LaCrosse, Cadillac ATS, Chevrolet Colorado, Chevrolet Malibu, Chevrolet Equinox, GMC Acadia, GMC Canyon and GMC Terrain. The high pressure fuel pump, part of the fuel system, may come loose from its mounting flange while the vehicle is being driven. If the pump detaches, it could damage the nearby high pressure fuel line.

Chevrolet Equinox Front Door Glass May Shatter high

Reported 2018-04-19 · Campaign 18V252000 · Model years: 2018

The glass in the driver and front passenger door windows on some vehicles may not have been properly tempered. Properly tempered glass is meant to break into small pieces if it shatters. If this glass isn't tempered correctly and the window breaks, it can break into large pieces instead.

Equinox, Acadia and Terrain: Right Driveshaft May Fracture high

Reported 2017-08-22 · Campaign 17V516000 · Model years: 2018

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Equinox, GMC Terrain, and GMC Acadia vehicles because the right front intermediate driveshaft may fracture and separate while the vehicle is being driven. If this shaft breaks apart while driving, the vehicle will lose the power needed to move forward. The notice also explains that if the shaft separates while the vehicle is parked on a slope without the parking brake engaged, the vehicle could roll away on its own.
