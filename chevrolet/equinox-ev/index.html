GM vehicles may be missing the electronic owner's manual low

Reported Feb 26, 2026 · Campaign 26V114000 · Model years: 2025

General Motors is recalling a wide range of Cadillac, Chevrolet, GMC and Buick vehicles, including the Escalade IQ and IQL, OPTIQ, Colorado, Equinox EV, Silverado 3500, Canyon and Sierra 3500. During production, the radio may not have been set to the correct status to download the electronic owner's manual, so the manual is not there for the owner to use. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard covering occupant protection.

Chevrolet Equinox EV Pedestrian Alert Sound Issue medium

Reported Dec 18, 2025 · Campaign 25V878000 · Model years: 2025

These electric Equinox vehicles are supposed to make a sound that gets noticeably louder when the vehicle starts moving from a stop, so pedestrians can tell it's no longer parked. In some vehicles, the sound doesn't change enough in volume between being stopped and moving at low speed, so it doesn't meet the federal safety standard for the noise electric and hybrid vehicles must make.

Cadillac Optiq, Equinox EV Tire Tread Detachment high

Reported Oct 16, 2025 · Campaign 25V704000 · Model years: 2025

General Motors is recalling certain 2025-2026 Cadillac Optiq and 2025-2026 Chevrolet Equinox EV vehicles that are equipped with 21-inch Continental all-season tires. On some of these vehicles, part of the tire's tread, or the entire tread, may separate from the tire while the vehicle is being driven. This is known as tread detachment and can affect one or more of the vehicle's tires.

Equinox EV Pedestrian Alert Sound Too Quiet medium

Reported Sep 25, 2025 · Campaign 25V639000 · Model years: 2024

General Motors is recalling certain Chevrolet Equinox EV vehicles because the pedestrian alert sound system may not produce enough of a change in volume between when the vehicle is stopped and when it is moving at low speeds. This sound is meant to let people outside the vehicle know it is there. Because the volume does not change enough, the vehicle does not meet the federal minimum sound requirements for hybrid and electric vehicles.

Chevrolet Equinox EV Cruise Control May Not Brake medium

Reported Jan 16, 2025 · Campaign 25V012000 · Model years: 2025

On certain 2025 Chevrolet Equinox EV all-wheel drive electric vehicles, the adaptive cruise control system may not apply the brakes the way it is supposed to when needed. This happens because of incorrect software installed in the module that controls the vehicle's brake system. As a result, the brakes may not engage automatically as the driver expects when the adaptive cruise control feature is being used.

Chevy Equinox EV Pedestrian Warning Sound Too Quiet medium

Reported Dec 12, 2024 · Campaign 24V925000 · Model years: 2025

On some 2025 Chevrolet Equinox EV vehicles, the software that controls the pedestrian alert sound system may be incorrect, and the vehicle may not produce the alert sound at a loud enough volume. This system is meant to make noise so that people walking nearby can hear the vehicle approaching. Dealers will update the body control module software to fix the issue, free of charge.

Chevrolet Blazer, Equinox EV Door Striker Failure high

Reported Oct 2, 2024 · Campaign 24V737000 · Model years: 2025

The door strikers on your vehicle were not properly heat-treated, which can cause them to break. If a door striker breaks, the door it's attached to can open unexpectedly, which increases the risk of injury to anyone inside. All affected vehicles are still at dealerships, so no owner notification letters are being mailed. Dealers will replace all four side door strikers and their attachment bolts, free of charge.

Chevrolet Equinox EV recall questions

How many recalls does the Chevrolet Equinox EV have?

NHTSA lists 7 safety recalls covering the 2024–2025 Chevrolet Equinox EV. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "GM vehicles may be missing the electronic owner's manual" (NHTSA campaign 26V114000), reported Feb 26, 2026.

Which Chevrolet Equinox EV model year has the most recalls?

The 2025 Chevrolet Equinox EV has the most, with 6 recalls. The 2024 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Chevrolet Equinox EV?

No. None of the 7 Chevrolet Equinox EV recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Chevrolet Equinox EV has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Chevrolet dealer, including for second owners.
