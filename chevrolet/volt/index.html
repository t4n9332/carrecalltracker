2 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Cadillac, Chevrolet, GMC rear seat belts may not lock medium

Reported 2018-09-26 · Campaign 18V673000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

When you pull a rear seat belt all the way out, it is supposed to switch into a locked mode that holds a child seat tightly against the seat. On some second-row and third-row belts, that locking feature may not work. Without it, a child seat cannot be strapped down firmly with the belt and could move around in a crash, so the child is less protected. Dealers check the rear belt retractors and replace any that do not lock as they should.

GM cars, SUVs may have weak rear brakes medium

Reported 2018-08-30 · Campaign 18V576000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The pistons inside the rear brake calipers, the parts that squeeze the brake pads against the wheels, were not coated properly. That can let gas pockets form in the brake system at the rear wheels. Gas squashes when you press the pedal instead of pushing firmly on the pads, so the rear brakes do less of the work and the vehicle can take longer to stop than you expect. The dealer bleeds the brake system to clear the gas out.
