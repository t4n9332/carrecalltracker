Subaru Ascent and Forester have wrong weight rating label low

Reported Jul 7, 2026 · Campaign 26V436000 · Model years: 2025

Subaru is recalling certain Crosstrek Hybrid, Forester, Forester Hybrid and Ascent vehicles. The certification label on these vehicles shows an incorrect Gross Axle Weight Rating, the maximum weight an axle is rated to carry. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard covering tire and rim selection. Subaru will mail owners a new label free of charge, and owners who prefer can have a dealer install the new label instead, also free.

Subaru Forester Child Seat Anchor Bolts May Loosen medium

Reported Jun 19, 2025 · Campaign 25V419000 · Model years: 2025

Some Subaru Forester vehicles have bolts that anchor a child seat in place, and these bolts may not have been tightened properly at the factory when the vehicle was built. Because of this, the bolts can come loose over time. A loose anchor bolt means the hardware used to attach a child seat to the vehicle may not hold it as firmly in place as it should.

Subaru Forester Alloy Wheels May Be Improperly Attached high

Reported Jan 23, 2025 · Campaign 25V033000 · Model years: 2025

Some 2025 Subaru Forester vehicles with 18-inch aluminum alloy wheels may have wheels made with an incorrectly specified lug seat surface. This can result in a wheel not being properly attached to the vehicle. An improperly attached wheel can cause the vehicle to lose stability while being driven. This loss of stability can make the vehicle harder to control on the road.

Subaru Outback, Forester switch failure disables reverse lights, camera medium

Reported Nov 9, 2023 · Campaign 23V755000 · Model years: 2022

Subaru is recalling certain Crosstrek, Forester, Legacy and Outback vehicles. A weld that was not strong enough can let water get into the inhibitor switch, the switch that tells the car which gear the transmission is in, and the water can make that switch fail. If it does, the reverse lights may not come on and the rearview camera image may not appear. Dealers will replace the switch free of charge.

Subaru Front Driveshafts May Crack and Break high

Reported Nov 9, 2023 · Campaign 23V754000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

On some Subaru Crosstrek, Forester, Impreza, and WRX vehicles, the outer race on the front driveshaft assemblies may develop cracks and break. This can cause a loss of drive power while driving, or cause the vehicle to roll away if it's parked without the parking brake set.

Subaru Forester Coolant Leak Fire Risk high

Reported Oct 26, 2023 · Campaign 23V723000 · Model years: 2024

Subaru is recalling certain 2024 Forester vehicles because the engine water pipe may have loose attachment bolts, allowing the pipe to lose its seal and leak coolant. Leaking coolant can reach the hot exhaust pipe. Subaru's distributor processing facilities will tighten the water pipe attachment bolts to fix the issue.

Subaru, Toyota 86 Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported Jul 29, 2021 · Campaign 21V587000 · Model years: 2018

The low-pressure fuel pump, located inside the fuel tank, may fail on these vehicles. If the pump fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven. To fix the issue, dealers will replace the low-pressure fuel pump. This repair is free of charge, and it applies to a number of affected Subaru models as well as the Toyota 86.

Subaru Crosstrek and Forester Loose Suspension Bolts high

Reported Apr 15, 2021 · Campaign 21V263000 · Model years: 2019

On some Subaru Crosstrek and Forester vehicles, the bolts holding the rear stabilizer bracket may loosen and come out. If the bracket isn't held in place, it could shift out of position. A shifted bracket increases the risk of a crash.

Subaru Ascent, Crosstrek, Forester Power Loss Risk high

Reported Dec 2, 2019 · Campaign 19V856000 · Model years: 2019

The aluminum Positive Crankcase Ventilation valve in your engine can separate. If it does, the pieces that break off can travel into the engine, causing your vehicle to lose power while you're driving. Subaru will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and replace the valve, free of charge. If the valve has separated and its pieces can't be found, the engine's short block will be replaced instead, free of charge.

Subaru Forester Passenger Airbag May Deactivate high

Reported Oct 3, 2019 · Campaign 19V701000 · Model years: 2018

On certain 2015-2018 Forester vehicles equipped with heated seats, an electrical connection for the front passenger seat's occupant detection system may loosen. If this happens, the front passenger airbag may turn off even though the seat is occupied.

Subaru Crosstrek, Forester may lose power steering assist medium

Reported Feb 6, 2019 · Campaign 19V065000 · Model years: 2019

On some 2019 Subaru Forester and Crosstrek vehicles, a connector inside the electric power steering unit may short circuit. If this happens, the vehicle loses power steering assistance, which means turning the steering wheel takes noticeably more effort. Subaru has notified dealers, who will inspect the steering gearbox and replace it if necessary, free of charge.

Subaru Forester Label Lists Wrong Tire Size medium

Reported Jul 10, 2018 · Campaign 18V455000 · Model years: 2018

On some Foresters, the label that lists wheel size, tire size, and air pressure information may show the wrong numbers. If owners rely on this label when choosing tires or setting tire pressure, they could end up putting the wrong size tires on the vehicle or filling the tires to the wrong pressure, since the printed information does not match what the vehicle actually needs.

Subaru Forester recall questions

How many recalls does the Subaru Forester have?

NHTSA lists 12 safety recalls covering the 2018–2025 Subaru Forester. 7 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Subaru Ascent and Forester have wrong weight rating label" (NHTSA campaign 26V436000), reported Jul 7, 2026.

Which Subaru Forester model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Subaru Forester has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2022 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Subaru Forester?

No. None of the 12 Subaru Forester recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Subaru Forester has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Subaru dealer, including for second owners.
