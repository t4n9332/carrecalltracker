Subaru BRZ and Toyota GR86 rear turn signals fail medium

Reported Aug 31, 2023 · Campaign 23V609000 · Model years: 2022

Subaru is recalling certain BRZ vehicles and Toyota GR86 vehicles because the rear turn signals may stop working from time to time. The problem may come and go rather than staying constant. Dealers will replace both rear combination lamp assemblies, the units that hold the rear lights, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed January 8, 2024. Subaru owners and Toyota owners each have their own customer service line for this recall.

Subaru, Toyota 86 Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported Jul 29, 2021 · Campaign 21V587000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The low-pressure fuel pump, located inside the fuel tank, may fail on these vehicles. If the pump fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven. To fix the issue, dealers will replace the low-pressure fuel pump. This repair is free of charge, and it applies to a number of affected Subaru models as well as the Toyota 86.

Subaru Legacy, Outback, BRZ backup camera screen may stay blank medium

Reported Dec 31, 2018 · Campaign 18V935000 · Model years: 2018

Subaru is recalling certain Legacy, Outback, and BRZ vehicles. A software problem can keep the audio display system from starting up, and when that happens the camera display stays blank. If that occurs, the rear camera image will not appear while you are backing up. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rearview mirror safety standard. Dealers will reprogram the audio system display software at no charge. Subaru has notified owners, and the recall began February 5, 2019.

Subaru BRZ recall questions

How many recalls does the Subaru BRZ have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2018–2022 Subaru BRZ. 1 of them is rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Subaru BRZ and Toyota GR86 rear turn signals fail" (NHTSA campaign 23V609000), reported Aug 31, 2023.

Which Subaru BRZ model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Subaru BRZ has the most, with 2 recalls. The 2019 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Subaru BRZ?

No. None of the 3 Subaru BRZ recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Subaru BRZ has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Subaru dealer, including for second owners.
