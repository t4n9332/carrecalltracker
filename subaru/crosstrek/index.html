Subaru Outback, Forester switch failure disables reverse lights, camera medium

Reported Nov 9, 2023 · Campaign 23V755000 · Model years: 2021

Subaru is recalling certain Crosstrek, Forester, Legacy and Outback vehicles. A weld that was not strong enough can let water get into the inhibitor switch, the switch that tells the car which gear the transmission is in, and the water can make that switch fail. If it does, the reverse lights may not come on and the rearview camera image may not appear. Dealers will replace the switch free of charge.

Subaru Front Driveshafts May Crack and Break high

Reported Nov 9, 2023 · Campaign 23V754000 · Model years: 2024

On some Subaru Crosstrek, Forester, Impreza, and WRX vehicles, the outer race on the front driveshaft assemblies may develop cracks and break. This can cause a loss of drive power while driving, or cause the vehicle to roll away if it's parked without the parking brake set.

Subaru Crosstrek, Impreza Wiring May Cause Power Loss high

Reported Aug 4, 2023 · Campaign 23V551000 · Model years: 2024

Subaru is recalling certain Crosstrek and Impreza vehicles. On these vehicles, there is not enough clearance between the instrument panel wiring harness and the steering beam bracket. Because of this tight clearance, the wiring harness may come into contact with the bracket and rub against it. This rubbing can wear through the protective insulation around the wires, and once the insulation is worn through, it may lead to a short circuit.

Subaru Crosstrek and Forester Loose Suspension Bolts high

Reported Apr 15, 2021 · Campaign 21V263000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On some Subaru Crosstrek and Forester vehicles, the bolts holding the rear stabilizer bracket may loosen and come out. If the bracket isn't held in place, it could shift out of position. A shifted bracket increases the risk of a crash.

Subaru Impreza and Crosstrek Risk Ignition Coil Short Circuit high

Reported Apr 15, 2021 · Campaign 21V264000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Certain Subaru Impreza and Crosstrek vehicles have an engine control module that may keep sending power to the ignition coil even after the engine is turned off. This can cause a short circuit in the ignition coils, which may make the engine stall. Dealers will update the engine control module software, replace the ignition coils, and, if needed, replace the front exhaust pipe.

Subaru Crosstrek Rear Seat Belts May Not Lock high

Reported Mar 31, 2020 · Campaign 20V189000 · Model years: 2019

Subaru says the rear seat belts in some Crosstrek vehicles have a dual-mode locking mechanism that senses the belt webbing. That locking mechanism may not lock as intended.

Subaru Ascent, Crosstrek, Forester Power Loss Risk high

Reported Dec 2, 2019 · Campaign 19V856000 · Model years: 2019

The aluminum Positive Crankcase Ventilation valve in your engine can separate. If it does, the pieces that break off can travel into the engine, causing your vehicle to lose power while you're driving. Subaru will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and replace the valve, free of charge. If the valve has separated and its pieces can't be found, the engine's short block will be replaced instead, free of charge.

Subaru Crosstrek, Impreza Risk of Engine Stall high

Reported Oct 18, 2019 · Campaign 19V743000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Subaru says a computer that controls the engine in some Impreza and Crosstrek vehicles may keep sending power to the ignition coil even after the engine has been turned off. Leaving that power on can lead to a short circuit and a blown fuse. If a short circuit happens while the vehicle is being driven, the vehicle may suddenly stall.

Subaru Impreza and Crosstrek Engine Valve May Fail high

Reported Oct 17, 2019 · Campaign 19V744000 · Model years: 2018

Some Impreza and Crosstrek vehicles have an engine part that may come apart. If pieces separate, they can get into the engine, and this may cause the engine to suddenly lose power while you are driving.

Subaru Crosstrek Rear Window Glass May Shatter medium

Reported May 30, 2019 · Campaign 19V404000 · Model years: 2019

On certain 2019 Subaru Crosstrek vehicles, the window glass in the driver's side rear door may not have been properly tempered during manufacturing. This means the vehicle does not meet the federal safety standard covering glazing materials, the glass used in vehicle windows. If this glass isn't properly tempered, it could shatter into large pieces, which increases the risk of injury to anyone in the vehicle.

Subaru Crosstrek, Forester may lose power steering assist medium

Reported Feb 6, 2019 · Campaign 19V065000 · Model years: 2019

On some 2019 Subaru Forester and Crosstrek vehicles, a connector inside the electric power steering unit may short circuit. If this happens, the vehicle loses power steering assistance, which means turning the steering wheel takes noticeably more effort. Subaru has notified dealers, who will inspect the steering gearbox and replace it if necessary, free of charge.

Subaru Crosstrek Floor Mat Can Block Pedals high

Reported Nov 7, 2017 · Campaign 17V701000 · Model years: 2018

Subaru is recalling certain Crosstrek vehicles that may have an incorrect bracket holding the floor mat in place. Because the bracket is wrong, the floor mat can move out of position. If the mat shifts, it can get in the way of the accelerator or brake pedal and interfere with how they work.
