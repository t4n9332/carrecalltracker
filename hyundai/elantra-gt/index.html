Hyundai Elantra GT Sunroof May Not Detect Obstacles medium

Reported Dec 14, 2018 · Campaign 18V891000 · Model years: 2018

Some Hyundai Elantra GT vehicles have a panoramic sunroof motor that may not be programmed correctly. Because of this, the sunroof may not detect an obstacle in its path while it is closing. If the sunroof cannot sense an obstacle, it may not stop or reverse the way it is supposed to, and it could keep closing instead of stopping on the object.
