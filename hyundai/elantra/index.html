Hyundai Elantra recalls

13 recalls covering model years 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Hyundai Rearview Camera May Fail To Display medium

Reported 2024-11-21 · Campaign 24V879000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Certain Hyundai vehicles have a damaged printed circuit board in the rearview camera. Because of this, the rearview camera image may fail to display. This means the vehicle does not meet a federal safety standard for rear visibility.

Hyundai Elantra, Kona and Venue may lose drive power high

Reported 2024-05-01 · Campaign 24V308000 · Model years: 2024

Hyundai is recalling certain Elantra, Venue, and Kona vehicles because of a problem with a part of the exhaust system called the exhaust gas recirculation valve. An electrical short circuit can happen inside this valve assembly, and when that happens the vehicle can lose drive power. A dealer will replace the exhaust gas recirculation valve assembly at no cost to the owner. Hyundai mailed letters to owners about this recall, and the company has a customer service line for questions.

Hyundai Sonata, Tucson, Palisade Oil Pump Fire Risk critical

Reported 2023-07-27 · Campaign 23V526000 · Model years: 2023

Some Hyundai vehicles have an electronic controller for the engine stop-start oil pump that may contain damaged electrical components. This damage can cause the pump controller to overheat, which increases the risk of a vehicle fire.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Elantra airbag cover emblem may come loose medium

Reported 2022-08-18 · Campaign 22V632000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

On these Hyundai Elantra and Elantra N cars, the emblem on the cover of the driver's front airbag may not have been welded on properly. Because of that weld, the emblem can separate from the cover when the airbag goes off. The remedy is a full replacement of the airbag rather than a repair to the cover, and a dealer will do this work at no cost. Hyundai has already mailed letters to owners about the recall.

Hyundai Accent and Elantra Seat Belt Pretensioner Risk high

Reported 2022-05-19 · Campaign 22V354000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

In certain Accent and Elantra vehicles, the seat belt pretensioners in the front seats may explode when they deploy during a crash. If this happens, small metal fragments from the pretensioner could fly into the vehicle and strike the people inside, causing injury. Dealers will fix the problem by securing the seat belt pretensioners with a cap, free of charge to the owner.

Hyundai Accent, Elantra Seat Belt Pretensioners May Explode high

Reported 2022-04-01 · Campaign 22V218000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

In a crash, the seat belt pretensioners in the front seats of your vehicle may explode instead of working the way they're supposed to. If that happens, metal pieces from the pretensioner can be thrown into the vehicle and strike the driver or front passenger, causing injury. This applies to both the driver's side and passenger's side front seat belts. Dealers will replace the pretensioners for free once parts are available.

Hyundai Elantra seat belt pretensioner may explode high

Reported 2022-03-03 · Campaign 22V123000 · Model years: 2022

Certain Elantra and Elantra HEV vehicles have a front passenger-side seat belt pretensioner that may explode when it deploys during a crash. If that happens, small metal fragments can be thrown into the vehicle and strike the people riding inside, causing injury. Owner notification letters have already been mailed to affected owners. Dealers will replace the seat belt pretensioners in affected vehicles at no cost to the owner.

Hyundai Windshields May Detach During a Crash high

Reported 2021-12-28 · Campaign 21V00M000 · Model years: 2021

Some Hyundai Elantra, Santa Fe, and Sonata vehicles may have a windshield that was not properly bonded to the vehicle during manufacturing. If the vehicle is in a crash, the windshield could detach. A windshield that comes loose during a crash increases the risk of injury.

Genesis and Hyundai Models Seat Belt Pretensioner Risk high

Reported 2021-10-13 · Campaign 21V796000 · Model years: 2021

On certain Genesis GV70, Genesis GV80, Hyundai Elantra, Elantra HEV, and Hyundai Venue vehicles, the front seat belt pretensioners on the driver's side, the passenger's side, or both may explode when they go off in a crash. If this happens, small metal pieces from the exploding pretensioner can be thrown into the vehicle's cabin and can strike the people riding inside, causing injury.

Hyundai Elantra, Kona, Veloster Engine Damage Risk high

Reported 2021-04-28 · Campaign 21V301000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Hyundai is recalling certain Elantra, Kona and Veloster vehicles equipped with 2.0L engines. A part inside the engine, called the piston oil rings, may not have been properly heat-treated during manufacturing. Because of this, the engine could suffer damage over time.

Hyundai Elantra Front Seat Recliners Improperly Welded medium

Reported 2021-04-28 · Campaign 21V302000 · Model years: 2021

Hyundai is recalling certain 2021 Elantra vehicles. The welds on the driver and front passenger seat back recliners may not have been done correctly.

Hyundai Elantra, Ioniq Hybrid rear wheel may detach high

Reported 2019-10-09 · Campaign 19V720000 · Model years: 2020

On some 2019 Ioniq Hybrid and 2020 Elantra vehicles, the lug nuts on the right rear wheel may not have been tightened enough. This could allow the wheel to come loose and separate from the vehicle. Dealers will inspect the lug nuts and tighten them if necessary, free of charge.

Hyundai Elantra Loose Front Ball Joint Fasteners high

Reported 2019-10-09 · Campaign 19V721000 · Model years: 2020

Hyundai is recalling certain Elantra vehicles because the fasteners holding the front lower ball joint in place may not have been tightened enough. If the fasteners are too loose, the ball joint could come loose from the lower control arm.
