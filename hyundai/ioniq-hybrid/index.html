Hyundai Ioniq Hybrid Battery Relay May Overheat critical

Reported Jun 28, 2022 · Campaign 22V459000 · Model years: 2018

Your Ioniq Hybrid has a power relay assembly located underneath the rear seat, which is part of the hybrid system. This relay may overheat. This recall covers the same relay problem as an earlier recall, but it now replaces that older recall. If your vehicle was already repaired under the earlier recall, it still needs the new repair described here.

Hyundai Elantra, Ioniq Hybrid rear wheel may detach high

Reported Oct 9, 2019 · Campaign 19V720000 · Model years: 2019

On some 2019 Ioniq Hybrid and 2020 Elantra vehicles, the lug nuts on the right rear wheel may not have been tightened enough. This could allow the wheel to come loose and separate from the vehicle. Dealers will inspect the lug nuts and tighten them if necessary, free of charge.

Hyundai Ioniq Hybrid Relay May Overheat Near Seat critical

Reported Oct 5, 2018 · Campaign 18V704000 · Model years: 2018

Hyundai is recalling certain Ioniq Hybrid and Ioniq Plug-In Hybrid vehicles because the main relay inside the Power Relay Assembly may not connect properly, creating higher electrical resistance at the connection. Hyundai will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the Power Relay Assembly for damage, replacing just the main relay if none is found, or replacing the entire assembly if damage is found, at no cost to the owner.

Hyundai Ioniq Hybrid recall questions

How many recalls does the Hyundai Ioniq Hybrid have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2018–2019 Hyundai Ioniq Hybrid. 3 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Hyundai Ioniq Hybrid Battery Relay May Overheat" (NHTSA campaign 22V459000), reported Jun 28, 2022.

Which Hyundai Ioniq Hybrid model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Hyundai Ioniq Hybrid has the most, with 2 recalls. The 2019 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Hyundai Ioniq Hybrid?

No. None of the 3 Hyundai Ioniq Hybrid recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Hyundai Ioniq Hybrid has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Hyundai dealer, including for second owners.
