Hyundai Ioniq 9 Powered Rear Seats May Trap People high

Reported Sep 11, 2026 · Campaign 26V588000 · Model years: 2026

Certain Hyundai Ioniq 9 vehicles with Limited, Calligraphy, or Calligraphy Design trim have second-row powered seats that may fail to detect a person. The seats may keep moving after touching someone during powered adjustments, including automatic folding and stowing. Someone could become trapped by a moving rear seat. The notice specifically identifies children as being at risk of getting trapped and injured.

Hyundai Ioniq 5 Rear Suspension Fasteners May Detach high

Reported May 19, 2026 · Campaign 26V314000 · Model years: 2026

The fasteners holding the rear suspension on some vehicles may loosen and detach. If this happens, it can result in a loss of vehicle control.

Hyundai Ioniq 5/9: Loose Battery Bolt Fire Risk critical

Reported Feb 6, 2026 · Campaign 26V068000 · Model years: 2026

A bolt connecting parts inside the vehicle's high voltage battery system may not be tightened properly. This can lead to an electrical short inside the battery system.
