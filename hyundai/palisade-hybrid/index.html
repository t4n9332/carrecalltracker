Hyundai Palisade and Palisade Hybrid Seat Belt Display May Mislead high

Reported Mar 20, 2026 · Campaign 26V169000 · Model years: 2026

Certain Hyundai Palisade and Palisade Hybrid vehicles with Limited or Calligraphy trim may have damaged wiring inside the third row seat belt buckle on the driver's side. This can make the dashboard continuously show that seat's belt as fastened. The display may then fail to alert occupants when that seat belt is unfastened. Dealers will add a wiring extension and replace the seat belt assembly if needed.

Hyundai Palisade and Palisade Hybrid Power Seats May Trap People high

Reported Mar 17, 2026 · Campaign 26V160000 · Model years: 2026

Certain Hyundai Palisade and Palisade Hybrid vehicles with Limited or Calligraphy trim have powered seats in the second and third rows that may fail to detect a person. A seat may keep moving after touching someone during powered functions, including tilting and sliding or folding and stowing. A person, especially a child, could become trapped by a rear powered seat.

Hyundai Tucson Hybrid Instrument Display May Fail medium

Reported Jan 27, 2026 · Campaign 26V047000 · Model years: 2026

On certain Tucson Hybrid, Tucson Plug-In Hybrid, and several other Hyundai models, a software error can cause the instrument panel display to fail. Because this display is required to function properly under the federal safety standard for vehicle controls and displays, a failure means the vehicle no longer meets that requirement.

Hyundai Palisade Hybrid recall questions

How many recalls does the Hyundai Palisade Hybrid have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2026 Hyundai Palisade Hybrid. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Hyundai Palisade and Palisade Hybrid Seat Belt Display May Mislead" (NHTSA campaign 26V169000), reported Mar 20, 2026.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Hyundai Palisade Hybrid?

No. None of the 3 Hyundai Palisade Hybrid recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Hyundai Palisade Hybrid has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Hyundai dealer, including for second owners.
