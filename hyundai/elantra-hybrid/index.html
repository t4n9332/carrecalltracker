Hyundai Elantra Hybrid power unit can overheat, fire risk critical

Reported 2026-05-15 · Campaign 26V308000 · Model years: 2025

The hybrid power control unit, the box that manages electricity between the battery and the motor, can overheat on some 2024 through 2026 Hyundai Elantra Hybrids. Overheating raises the risk of a fire. The fix is a free software update at the dealer that keeps the unit from getting too hot. Hyundai mailed letters to owners on July 3, 2026, and affected vehicle numbers have been searchable on the government recall site since May 16, 2026.

Hyundai Elantra and Santa Fe: rearview camera may not display medium

Reported 2024-11-21 · Campaign 24V879000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

A circuit board inside the backup camera on these Hyundai vehicles can be damaged, and when that happens the camera picture may not appear on the screen as you back up. You could be looking at a blank display instead of the area behind the car. Federal rules require a working rear view, so these vehicles fall short of the standard. The fix is a brand-new rearview camera, installed by the dealer at no cost to you.
