Hyundai Nexo Fuel Tank Fire Risk critical

Reported Oct 11, 2024 · Campaign 24V761000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

A safety part on the hydrogen fuel tank that releases pressure may break. If it breaks, hydrogen gas can leak out, which can start a fire while the vehicle is parked. Hyundai will replace this part to fix the problem.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Nexo Hydrogen Leak Detection May Fail critical

Reported Apr 14, 2022 · Campaign 22V258000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

The sensors that are supposed to detect hydrogen gas leaks in the fuel system may fail to do their job. If a leak isn't detected, it can increase the risk of a fire, whether the car is parked or being driven.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Nexo Hydrogen Fuel Leak Risk critical

Reported Apr 7, 2022 · Campaign 22V228000 · Model years: 2019

Some Hyundai Nexo vehicles have a hydrogen fuel inlet receptacle filter that could detach after hydrogen refueling. If the filter detaches, it can cause a leak in the hydrogen fuel system.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Kona Electric and Nexo Braking System Issue high

Reported Dec 1, 2020 · Campaign 20V748000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Hyundai is recalling certain Kona Electric and Nexo vehicles because the brake system may pick up an abnormal signal from a sensor. When that happens, it can significantly cut down how much braking power the vehicle has. That means it could take longer to stop than expected, which matters most in situations where you need to brake quickly. The problem is related to the software that runs this electronic braking system.

Hyundai Nexo and Sonata remote parking may fail high

Reported Apr 9, 2020 · Campaign 20V213000 · Model years: 2020

The Remote Smart Parking Assist feature is supposed to stop the vehicle if it detects a malfunction in the system. That software may fail to prevent the vehicle from moving when such a malfunction is detected.

Hyundai Nexo recall questions

How many recalls does the Hyundai Nexo have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2019–2023 Hyundai Nexo. 5 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Hyundai Nexo Fuel Tank Fire Risk" (NHTSA campaign 24V761000), reported Oct 11, 2024.

Which Hyundai Nexo model year has the most recalls?

The 2019 Hyundai Nexo has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2022 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Hyundai Nexo?

Yes. 3 Hyundai Nexo recalls carry a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Hyundai Nexo Fuel Tank Fire Risk (24V761000); Hyundai Nexo Hydrogen Leak Detection May Fail (22V258000); Hyundai Nexo Hydrogen Fuel Leak Risk (22V228000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Hyundai Nexo has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Hyundai dealer, including for second owners.
