Hyundai Accent recalls

6 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Hyundai Accent and Elantra seat belt part can explode high

Reported 2022-05-19 · Campaign 22V354000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

These cars have devices in the front seat belts that pull the belt tight in a crash. In these vehicles, that device can explode when it goes off instead of working normally. If that happens, metal pieces can fly into the cabin and strike the driver or front passenger, causing injury. The dealer secures the part with a cap, free of charge. Even if your car was repaired under an earlier recall, it still needs this new fix.

Hyundai Accent steering column may detach while driving critical

Reported 2022-05-06 · Campaign 22V311000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

A bolt in the steering column may not have been tightened properly at the factory. The steering column is the shaft that connects your steering wheel to the parts that turn the front wheels. If that bolt is loose, the column can separate from the steering rack, and turning the wheel would no longer turn the car. Dealers will check the bolt and tighten it if needed, free of charge.

Hyundai Elantra and Accent: seat belt part can explode high

Reported 2022-04-01 · Campaign 22V218000 · Model years: 2020

These cars have a device in the front seat belts that pulls the belt tight during a crash. On the recalled cars, that device can blow apart instead of working normally, sending metal pieces flying inside the car and injuring the driver or front passenger. Hyundai mailed a warning letter on May 31, 2022, before replacement parts were ready. Important: this recall has been replaced by recall 22V-354, and cars already repaired under this one need the newer repair too.

Hyundai Accent seat belt tighteners can explode in crash high

Reported 2022-02-09 · Campaign 22V069000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

In these 2020-2021 Accents, the front seat belts have a device that yanks the belt tight the instant a crash starts. On the recalled cars, that device can blow apart instead of working normally. If that happens in a crash, metal pieces can be thrown into the cabin and hit the driver or front passenger, causing injuries on top of the crash itself. A dealer replaces both front seat belt tighteners at no cost. Hyundai later issued a newer repair, so even already-fixed cars need the new one.

Hyundai Accent brake part may weaken stopping power high

Reported 2021-12-02 · Campaign 21V937000 · Model years: 2021

The brake master cylinder is the part that pushes brake fluid to the brakes when you press the pedal. On these cars it may have been made with sharp edges inside that can damage the rubber seal. A damaged seal lets pressure escape, so the brake pedal may travel farther toward the floor than normal and the car may need a longer distance to come to a stop than it should.

Hyundai Sonata and Accent trunks may not open from inside medium

Reported 2021-08-06 · Campaign 21V619000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

The latch on the trunk lid can become damaged. If that happens, the release handle inside the trunk will not open it, so anyone who ends up shut in the trunk — most often a child playing — cannot get out on their own. Federal rules require trunks to open from the inside, and these cars no longer meet that rule. Nothing about the way the car drives is affected. A dealer will replace the latch base at no cost.
