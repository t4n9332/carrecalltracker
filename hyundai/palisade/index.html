Hyundai Palisade Third-Row Airbag May Deploy Wrong high

Reported Jan 23, 2026 · Campaign 26V034000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

Some Hyundai Palisade SUVs have side curtain airbags for the third-row seats that may not deploy correctly in a crash. These airbags are meant to help protect third-row passengers, but in some vehicles they may not work the way they're supposed to. Hyundai has already begun mailing letters to owners about this safety risk. Dealers will install a protective film over the rear window and remove certain padding where needed, at no cost to you.

Hyundai Palisade seat belt buckles may not latch high

Reported Sep 12, 2025 · Campaign 25V607000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

Hyundai is recalling certain Palisade SUVs because seat belt buckles may fail to latch. The problem affects the buckles for the driver's seat, the front passenger seat and the two second-row window seats. Dealers will replace the buckle assemblies. The replacement parts are being released in stages, so owners are being notified about the problem first and will receive a second letter when the repair is available for their vehicle.

Hyundai Palisade Oil Pump Fire Risk critical

Reported May 6, 2025 · Campaign 25V291000 · Model years: 2025

Some Hyundai Palisade vehicles have an electric oil pump controller with an insufficient seal. This can let moisture build up on the circuit board inside, which can cause an electrical short and result in a fire.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Palisade Engine Valve Springs May Break critical

Reported Feb 13, 2024 · Campaign 24V106000 · Model years: 2024

Some Hyundai Palisade vehicles have engine valve springs that may break while the vehicle is being driven. A broken valve spring can cause the engine to lose power. It is also possible for a broken spring to create a hole in the engine block.

Hyundai Palisade Passenger Air Bag May Deactivate high

Reported Aug 25, 2023 · Campaign 23V599000 · Model years: 2024

Hyundai is recalling certain Palisade vehicles that have dark premium suede seats. These vehicles have a system that senses whether someone is sitting in the front passenger seat, and this system may fail to correctly detect a passenger. When that happens, the system can turn off the front passenger air bag even though someone is sitting there, which does not meet federal air bag safety requirements.

Hyundai Sonata, Tucson, Palisade Oil Pump Fire Risk critical

Reported Jul 27, 2023 · Campaign 23V526000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Some Hyundai vehicles have an electronic controller for the engine stop-start oil pump that may contain damaged electrical components. This damage can cause the pump controller to overheat, which increases the risk of a vehicle fire.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Palisade Brake Booster May Leak Vacuum high

Reported Jun 8, 2023 · Campaign 23V415000 · Model years: 2023

On some 2023 Palisade vehicles, a part inside the brake booster can shift out of place and create an internal vacuum leak. This can reduce how much power assist you get when braking.

Hyundai Palisade Windshield Wipers May Fail In Snow high

Reported Mar 17, 2023 · Campaign 23V180000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

In certain Hyundai Palisade vehicles, snow or ice buildup on the windshield can cause the windshield wiper motor to run on and off or stop working altogether, so the wipers stop clearing the windshield.

Hyundai Palisade Tow Hitch Wiring Fire Risk critical

Reported Aug 18, 2022 · Campaign 22V633000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

On vehicles with a factory or dealer-installed tow hitch wiring harness, dirt and moisture can build up on the circuit board inside it. This buildup can cause an electrical short, which can lead to a fire while the vehicle is parked or being driven.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Palisade Windshield Wiper Motor Failure high

Reported Aug 15, 2022 · Campaign 22V627000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On some Hyundai Palisade vehicles, the windshield wiper motor may stop working, causing the wipers to fail.

Hyundai Palisade Instrument Cluster Display May Disable medium

Reported May 18, 2022 · Campaign 22V353000 · Model years: 2022

On certain 2022 Palisades, the instrument cluster may have been built with too much adhesive, which can disable the display screen. When that happens, the driver can't see the meters, gauges, and warning lights.

Hyundai Palisade Brake Fluid May Reduce Braking Power high

Reported Oct 27, 2021 · Campaign 21V840000 · Model years: 2021

Some Palisade SUVs may have brake fluid that has become contaminated. This can cause seals inside the brake master cylinder to swell, and swollen seals reduce how well the brakes perform. When braking performance is reduced, it can take a longer distance to bring the vehicle to a stop. Needing more distance to stop increases the chance of a crash happening before the vehicle can stop in time.

Hyundai Palisade Side Airbags May Not Inflate high

Reported Nov 7, 2019 · Campaign 19V802000 · Model years: 2020

Hyundai is recalling certain Palisade vehicles because the bolts used to mount the side curtain airbags may damage those airbags when they deploy. If the airbags are damaged this way, they may not inflate the way they are supposed to during a crash, which means they may not protect occupants as intended.
