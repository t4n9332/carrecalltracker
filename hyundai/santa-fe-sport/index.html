Hyundai Santa Fe Sport ABS Module Fire Risk critical

Reported Oct 27, 2022 · Campaign 22V810000 · Model years: 2018

Hyundai is recalling certain Santa Fe Sport vehicles because the anti-lock brake system module could malfunction and cause an electrical short, which could result in an engine compartment fire while the vehicle is parked or being driven.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Santa Fe: ABS Module Engine Fire Risk critical

Reported Feb 3, 2022 · Campaign 22V056000 · Model years: 2018

Hyundai is recalling certain Santa Fe, Santa Fe Sport, and Santa Fe XL vehicles. The Anti-Lock Brake System module in these vehicles could malfunction and cause an electrical short. This electrical short could result in a fire in the engine compartment, whether the vehicle is parked or being driven, which can increase the risk of injury.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Santa Fe Sport Side Airbag Defect high

Reported Dec 13, 2017 · Campaign 17V801000 · Model years: 2018

Certain Hyundai Santa Fe Sport vehicles have side curtain airbags with a part that may come loose when the airbag deploys in a crash. If this part detaches, the curtain airbag may not inflate the way it's supposed to. It's also possible for the loose part to end up inside the vehicle where passengers are sitting.

Hyundai Santa Fe Sport Front Coil Spring Fracture critical

Reported Sep 20, 2017 · Campaign 17V577000 · Model years: 2018

The front coil springs on these vehicles may not have been made correctly. Because of this, a spring can fracture, or break, while the vehicle is being driven under certain loads. A broken spring can puncture the tire, which can cause the driver to lose control of the vehicle and increase the risk of a crash.

Hyundai Santa FE Sport recall questions

How many recalls does the Hyundai Santa FE Sport have?

NHTSA lists 4 safety recalls covering the 2018 Hyundai Santa FE Sport. 4 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Hyundai Santa Fe Sport ABS Module Fire Risk" (NHTSA campaign 22V810000), reported Oct 27, 2022.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Hyundai Santa FE Sport?

Yes. 2 Hyundai Santa FE Sport recalls carry a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Hyundai Santa Fe Sport ABS Module Fire Risk (22V810000); Hyundai Santa Fe: ABS Module Engine Fire Risk (22V056000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Hyundai Santa FE Sport has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Hyundai dealer, including for second owners.
