Hyundai Ioniq Electric May Accelerate Unexpectedly high

Reported Dec 7, 2021 · Campaign 21V944000 · Model years: 2019

Hyundai is recalling certain Ioniq Electric vehicles because an unstable electrical ground can cause the car to enter a fail-safe mode with less power and slower acceleration. In certain conditions while the vehicle is in this fail-safe mode, it may speed up on its own even after the driver has let go of the gas pedal.

Hyundai Ioniq, Kona Electric: Battery Short-Circuit Fire Risk critical

Reported Mar 1, 2021 · Campaign 21V127000 · Model years: 2020

Hyundai is recalling certain Ioniq Electric and Kona Electric vehicles. The lithium-ion battery installed in these vehicles may develop an electrical short. An electrical short in the battery increases the risk of a fire.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Ioniq Electric recall questions

How many recalls does the Hyundai Ioniq Electric have?

NHTSA lists 2 safety recalls covering the 2019–2020 Hyundai Ioniq Electric. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Hyundai Ioniq Electric May Accelerate Unexpectedly" (NHTSA campaign 21V944000), reported Dec 7, 2021.

Which Hyundai Ioniq Electric model year has the most recalls?

The 2019 Hyundai Ioniq Electric has the most, with 1 recall. The 2019 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Hyundai Ioniq Electric?

Yes. 1 Hyundai Ioniq Electric recall carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Hyundai Ioniq, Kona Electric: Battery Short-Circuit Fire Risk (21V127000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Hyundai Ioniq Electric has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Hyundai dealer, including for second owners.
