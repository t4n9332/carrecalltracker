Hyundai Elantra N recalls

5 recalls covering model years 2022, 2023, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Hyundai Elantra N Airbag Deployment May Be Delayed high

Reported 2026-04-21 · Campaign 26V254000 · Model years: 2025

On certain Hyundai Elantra N, Tucson, Tucson Hybrid, Tucson Plug-In Hybrid, and Elantra vehicles, the side-impact sensors in the B-pillar may have been put together incorrectly, which can delay the airbags from deploying.

Hyundai Rearview Camera May Fail To Display medium

Reported 2024-11-21 · Campaign 24V879000 · Model years: 2022

Certain Hyundai vehicles have a damaged printed circuit board in the rearview camera. Because of this, the rearview camera image may fail to display. This means the vehicle does not meet a federal safety standard for rear visibility.

Hyundai, Genesis Fuel Pump May Cause Power Loss high

Reported 2024-07-12 · Campaign 24V528000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Certain Hyundai and Genesis vehicles, including the Veloster N, Genesis G70, Elantra N, and Kona N, may have a fuel pump that fails. If the fuel pump fails, the vehicle can lose engine power while being driven. Some owners were sent an early letter warning them about this safety risk before the full notice with the repair information went out.

Hyundai Vehicles May Suddenly Lose Drive Power high

Reported 2022-10-06 · Campaign 22V746000 · Model years: 2022

These vehicles have a backup driving mode meant to keep the car moving if the transmission's oil pump fails. That backup mode may not work properly, and this can lead to a complete loss of drive power. Losing drive power while driving increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will inspect the transmission and replace it if needed, and will also update the transmission's computer software, all free of charge.

Hyundai Elantra airbag cover emblem may come loose medium

Reported 2022-08-18 · Campaign 22V632000 · Model years: 2022

On these Hyundai Elantra and Elantra N cars, the emblem on the cover of the driver's front airbag may not have been welded on properly. Because of that weld, the emblem can separate from the cover when the airbag goes off. The remedy is a full replacement of the airbag rather than a repair to the cover, and a dealer will do this work at no cost. Hyundai has already mailed letters to owners about the recall.
