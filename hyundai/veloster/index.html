Hyundai Elantra, Kona, Veloster Engine Damage Risk high

Reported Apr 28, 2021 · Campaign 21V301000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Hyundai is recalling certain Elantra, Kona and Veloster vehicles equipped with 2.0L engines. A part inside the engine, called the piston oil rings, may not have been properly heat-treated during manufacturing. Because of this, the engine could suffer damage over time.

Hyundai Veloster Driver's Door Latch May Fail high

Reported Apr 17, 2019 · Campaign 19V311000 · Model years: 2019

In certain side-impact crashes, the driver's door latch housing and its locking cable can become damaged. When that happens, the damage can let the driver's side door come open during the crash.

Hyundai Veloster recall questions

How many recalls does the Hyundai Veloster have?

NHTSA lists 2 safety recalls covering the 2019–2021 Hyundai Veloster. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Hyundai Elantra, Kona, Veloster Engine Damage Risk" (NHTSA campaign 21V301000), reported Apr 28, 2021.

Which Hyundai Veloster model year has the most recalls?

The 2019 Hyundai Veloster has the most, with 2 recalls. The 2020 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Hyundai Veloster?

No. None of the 2 Hyundai Veloster recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Hyundai Veloster has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Hyundai dealer, including for second owners.
