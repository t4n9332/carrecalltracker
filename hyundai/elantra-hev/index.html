Hyundai Elantra Hybrid Can Accelerate Unintentionally high

Reported 2023-08-18 · Campaign 23V589000 · Model years: 2023

A software error in the motor control unit of some Elantra HEV hybrids may cause the vehicle to accelerate on its own after the driver releases the brake pedal. This unintended acceleration is not something the driver did, and it increases the risk of a crash.
