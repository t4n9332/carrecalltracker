Hyundai SUVs: Automatic Braking May Trigger Unexpectedly high

Reported May 19, 2026 · Campaign 26V316000 · Model years: 2025

Hyundai is recalling certain 2025-2026 Santa Cruz, Tucson, Tucson Hybrid, and Tucson Plug-In Hybrid vehicles. Software in the front cameras may cause the forward collision avoidance system to turn on too early. When this happens, the system unexpectedly applies the brakes on its own, even when it isn't needed. This is a software problem in the camera that watches for collisions.

Hyundai Santa Fe, Tucson Air Bags May Not Deploy high

Reported Nov 21, 2025 · Campaign 25V809000 · Model years: 2025

Hyundai is recalling certain 2025 Tucson, and 2025-2026 Santa Cruz, Santa Fe, and Santa Fe Hybrid vehicles because the side curtain air bags may not have been installed correctly. This can cause the air bags not to deploy the way they are supposed to in a crash. Dealers will reinstall the side curtain air bags correctly, at no cost to owners.

Hyundai Tucson and Santa Cruz Can Roll Without Brake high

Reported Nov 20, 2024 · Campaign 24V877000 · Model years: 2025

On certain new Tucson and Santa Cruz models, a wiring assembly near the console may be routed incorrectly. This can let the gear shifter be moved out of Park without the brake pedal being pressed. Dealers will reroute the wiring correctly. In the meantime, Hyundai is telling owners to use the parking brake whenever they park.

Hyundai Tucson, Santa Cruz may lose power steering assist medium

Reported Jun 5, 2024 · Campaign 24V412000 · Model years: 2024

On some 2024 Hyundai Tucson and Santa Cruz vehicles, a circuit board inside the power steering system can short circuit. If this happens, the vehicle loses power steering assist, which means the steering wheel becomes much harder to turn. Dealers will replace the power steering electric power pack at no cost to the owner.

Hyundai Santa Fe and Santa Cruz Tow Hitch Fire Risk critical

Reported Mar 17, 2023 · Campaign 23V181000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Certain Hyundai Santa Fe, Santa Fe Hybrid, Santa Fe Plug-in Hybrid, and Santa Cruz vehicles equipped with a tow hitch wiring harness, either installed at the factory or added later through a Hyundai dealership, may allow water to build up on a circuit board inside the harness module. This buildup can cause an electrical short, which can result in a fire.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Santa Cruz and Tucson Roof Molding Detachment medium

Reported Jan 31, 2023 · Campaign 23V038000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

On certain Tucson and Santa Cruz vehicles, the trim moldings running along the roofline may not be properly secured. While the vehicle is being driven, a molding can come loose and detach from the roof. If a molding falls off, it can land on the roadway, becoming a hazard for other drivers and increasing the chance of a crash nearby.

Hyundai Vehicles May Suddenly Lose Drive Power high

Reported Oct 6, 2022 · Campaign 22V746000 · Model years: 2022

These vehicles have a backup driving mode meant to keep the car moving if the transmission's oil pump fails. That backup mode may not work properly, and this can lead to a complete loss of drive power. Losing drive power while driving increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will inspect the transmission and replace it if needed, and will also update the transmission's computer software, all free of charge.

Hyundai Santa Fe, Santa Cruz Turbo Oil Leak Risk high

Reported Mar 28, 2022 · Campaign 22V197000 · Model years: 2022

The pipe that supplies oil to the turbocharger may develop a crack over time. If the pipe cracks, oil can leak out into the engine compartment. Because the engine compartment contains hot parts such as the engine itself and the exhaust system, a leaking oil in that area near those hot components can increase the chance of a fire starting.

Hyundai Santa Cruz recall questions

How many recalls does the Hyundai Santa Cruz have?

NHTSA lists 8 safety recalls covering the 2022–2025 Hyundai Santa Cruz. 6 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Hyundai SUVs: Automatic Braking May Trigger Unexpectedly" (NHTSA campaign 26V316000), reported May 19, 2026.

Which Hyundai Santa Cruz model year has the most recalls?

The 2022 Hyundai Santa Cruz has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2024 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Hyundai Santa Cruz?

Yes. 1 Hyundai Santa Cruz recall carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Hyundai Santa Fe and Santa Cruz Tow Hitch Fire Risk (23V181000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Hyundai Santa Cruz has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Hyundai dealer, including for second owners.
