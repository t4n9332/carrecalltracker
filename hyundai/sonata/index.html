Hyundai Sonata recalls

10 recalls covering model years 2018, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Hyundai Sonata Fuel Tank Damage Risk of Fire critical

Reported 2025-11-18 · Campaign 25V796000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

Hyundai says a damaged check valve in some Sonata vehicles can let air get into the fuel tank. That extra air can make the tank expand, and the expanded tank can touch hot parts of the exhaust system. Contact with the hot exhaust can melt the fuel tank, so Hyundai is having dealers check the check valve, fuel tank, and engine control software.

Sonata Brake Lights May Flash Incorrectly high

Reported 2024-08-07 · Campaign 24V583000 · Model years: 2024

On certain Sonata vehicles, a software issue may cause the brake lights to flash and not illuminate properly. Brake lights that don't work correctly can confuse other drivers on the road, which increases the risk of a crash.

Hyundai Sonata, Tucson, Palisade Oil Pump Fire Risk critical

Reported 2023-07-27 · Campaign 23V526000 · Model years: 2023

Some Hyundai vehicles have an electronic controller for the engine stop-start oil pump that may contain damaged electrical components. This damage can cause the pump controller to overheat, which increases the risk of a vehicle fire.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Vehicles May Suddenly Lose Drive Power high

Reported 2022-10-06 · Campaign 22V746000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

These vehicles have a backup driving mode meant to keep the car moving if the transmission's oil pump fails. That backup mode may not work properly, and this can lead to a complete loss of drive power. Losing drive power while driving increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will inspect the transmission and replace it if needed, and will also update the transmission's computer software, all free of charge.

Hyundai Sonata and Genesis G70 trunk latch can fail medium

Reported 2022-03-28 · Campaign 22V196000 · Model years: 2020

The trunk latch on these cars can become damaged. When that happens, the trunk cannot be opened from the inside. Federal safety rules require a trunk release that works from within the trunk compartment, and these vehicles do not meet that requirement. Hyundai is recalling certain 2020 Sonata and Sonata Hybrid cars and 2019 Genesis G70 cars. Dealers will inspect the trunk latch and replace the latch base if it is needed, at no cost to the owner.

Hyundai Windshields May Detach During a Crash high

Reported 2021-12-28 · Campaign 21V00M000 · Model years: 2021

Some Hyundai Elantra, Santa Fe, and Sonata vehicles may have a windshield that was not properly bonded to the vehicle during manufacturing. If the vehicle is in a crash, the windshield could detach. A windshield that comes loose during a crash increases the risk of injury.

Hyundai Sonata and Accent trunk may not open from inside medium

Reported 2021-08-06 · Campaign 21V619000 · Model years: 2018

Hyundai is recalling certain Sonata, Sonata Hybrid, Accent and Azera vehicles. The latch on the trunk lid may become damaged, and when that happens the trunk cannot be opened from inside the trunk compartment. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard that requires a way to release the trunk from the inside. Dealers will replace the base of the trunk latch.

Hyundai Santa Fe and Sonata Fuel Leak Risk critical

Reported 2021-07-09 · Campaign 21V524000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Hyundai is recalling certain Santa Fe and Sonata vehicles equipped with 2.5-liter turbocharged engines. The notice says fuel may leak at the pipe connection between the high-pressure fuel pump and the fuel rail. A fuel leak like this increases the risk of a fire.

Hyundai Nexo and Sonata remote parking may fail high

Reported 2020-04-09 · Campaign 20V213000 · Model years: 2020

The Remote Smart Parking Assist feature is supposed to stop the vehicle if it detects a malfunction in the system. That software may fail to prevent the vehicle from moving when such a malfunction is detected.

Hyundai Sonata Tire Pressure Label Shows Wrong Size medium

Reported 2020-02-28 · Campaign 20V122000 · Model years: 2020

On these Hyundai Sonata vehicles, the tire pressure label inside the driver's door and the information in the owner's manual list an incorrect tire size. An owner relying on this information when choosing or installing tires could end up picking the wrong size, which the notice says could affect how the vehicle handles.
