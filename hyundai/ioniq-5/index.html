Hyundai Ioniq 5 recalls

14 recalls covering model years 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Hyundai Ioniq 5 Battery Cells Risk Fire critical

Reported 2026-07-02 · Campaign 26V432000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Certain Hyundai Ioniq 5 vehicles have high voltage battery cells that may contain misaligned electrodes inside them. This kind of misalignment inside the battery can lead to a fire, and this can happen whether the vehicle is parked or being driven at the time.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Ioniq 5 Rear Suspension Fasteners May Detach high

Reported 2026-05-19 · Campaign 26V314000 · Model years: 2025

The fasteners holding the rear suspension on some vehicles may loosen and detach. If this happens, it can result in a loss of vehicle control.

Hyundai Ioniq 5/9: Loose Battery Bolt Fire Risk critical

Reported 2026-02-06 · Campaign 26V068000 · Model years: 2025

A bolt connecting parts inside the vehicle's high voltage battery system may not be tightened properly. This can lead to an electrical short inside the battery system.

Hyundai Ioniq 5 Seat Belt Retractors Not Secured high

Reported 2025-11-18 · Campaign 25V797000 · Model years: 2025

On some Hyundai Ioniq 5 vehicles, the seat belt retractors for the driver and front passenger seats may not be securely fastened. A retractor that isn't properly secured may not work the way it's supposed to if the vehicle is in a crash.

Hyundai Ioniq 5 Recalled for Loose Suspension Bolts high

Reported 2025-09-12 · Campaign 25V605000 · Model years: 2025

Certain bolts that adjust the alignment of the rear suspension on some vehicles may not have been tightened properly.

Hyundai Ioniq 5 Battery Wiring May Cause Fire critical

Reported 2025-07-24 · Campaign 25V482000 · Model years: 2025

Hyundai is recalling certain Ioniq 5 vehicles because the high voltage battery system may contain a bus bar that was not tightened properly, which can cause a short-circuit.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Ioniq 5 Headlight Aim Label Wrong medium

Reported 2025-06-03 · Campaign 25V373000 · Model years: 2025

On certain 2025 Hyundai IONIQ 5 vehicles, the right-side headlight may have an incorrect aim label attached, which can result in the headlight being aimed incorrectly.

Hyundai Ioniq 5 N Brake and Acceleration Software Flaw high

Reported 2025-04-11 · Campaign 25V235000 · Model years: 2025

Hyundai is recalling certain Ioniq 5 N vehicles that have the left-foot braking and N e-shift features. Software in the electronic brake system and vehicle control unit may cause the brakes to work less effectively whenever left-foot braking is turned on. On top of that, if the N e-shift feature is active, the car may keep accelerating on its own even after the driver takes their foot off the gas pedal, instead of slowing down as expected.

Hyundai Ioniq 5 N: Left-Foot Braking May Weaken high

Reported 2025-02-06 · Campaign 25V065000 · Model years: 2025

On vehicles with the left-foot braking feature, software in the electronic brake and vehicle control systems may cause weaker braking when that feature is used. This can mean the vehicle needs more distance to stop.

Hyundai Ioniq 5 Rear Air Bag May Not Deploy high

Reported 2025-02-06 · Campaign 25V064000 · Model years: 2025

A wiring problem in the rear floor of the vehicle may create a break in the circuit. This can cause the rear side air bag to fail to deploy the way it is supposed to in a crash.

Hyundai Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6, Genesis GV60 Charging Unit Failure high

Reported 2024-11-18 · Campaign 24V868000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

Certain Hyundai Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6, and Genesis GV60 vehicles have an Integrated Charging Control Unit that may become damaged. When this happens, the unit can stop charging the 12-volt battery. If the 12-volt battery is not being charged, the vehicle can lose drive power.

Hyundai and Genesis EV Charging Unit Can Fail high

Reported 2024-03-15 · Campaign 24V204000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

On these Hyundai and Genesis electric vehicles, the Integrated Charging Control Unit may become damaged and stop charging the 12-volt battery. This can result in a loss of drive power.

Hyundai IONIQ 5, IONIQ 6, Genesis GV60 Driveshaft May Break high

Reported 2024-02-01 · Campaign 24V065000 · Model years: 2023

Certain 2023 Hyundai IONIQ 5, IONIQ 6, and Genesis GV60 vehicles have a rear inner driveshaft that may not have been heat-treated correctly during manufacturing. Because of this, the driveshaft can break when it is put under load, such as while accelerating. If the driveshaft breaks, the vehicle loses the power that turns the wheels, meaning it can no longer drive forward under its own power.

Hyundai Ioniq 5 Parking Software May Cause Rollaway high

Reported 2022-05-11 · Campaign 22V324000 · Model years: 2022

Hyundai is recalling certain Ioniq 5 vehicles. A software error in the shifter control unit may cause the parking mechanism to disengage on its own, even after the vehicle has been placed in park. This means the vehicle could start rolling on its own without the driver doing anything, since the parking mechanism that's supposed to hold it in place isn't working correctly.
