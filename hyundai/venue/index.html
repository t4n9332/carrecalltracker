Hyundai Elantra, Kona and Venue may lose drive power high

Reported May 1, 2024 · Campaign 24V308000 · Model years: 2024

Hyundai is recalling certain Elantra, Venue, and Kona vehicles because of a problem with a part of the exhaust system called the exhaust gas recirculation valve. An electrical short circuit can happen inside this valve assembly, and when that happens the vehicle can lose drive power. A dealer will replace the exhaust gas recirculation valve assembly at no cost to the owner. Hyundai mailed letters to owners about this recall, and the company has a customer service line for questions.

Hyundai Venue Seat Belt Pretensioners May Explode high

Reported Jun 27, 2022 · Campaign 22V458000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

On certain Venue vehicles, the front seat belt pretensioners, on the driver's and/or passenger's side, may explode when they deploy in a crash.

Genesis and Hyundai Models Seat Belt Pretensioner Risk high

Reported Oct 13, 2021 · Campaign 21V796000 · Model years: 2021

On certain Genesis GV70, Genesis GV80, Hyundai Elantra, Elantra HEV, and Hyundai Venue vehicles, the front seat belt pretensioners on the driver's side, the passenger's side, or both may explode when they go off in a crash. If this happens, small metal pieces from the exploding pretensioner can be thrown into the vehicle's cabin and can strike the people riding inside, causing injury.
