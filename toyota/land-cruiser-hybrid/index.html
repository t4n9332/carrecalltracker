Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrid Dashboard Warnings May Fail high

Reported May 27, 2026 · Campaign 26V341000 · Model years: 2024

The instrument panel display in these vehicles, which normally shows warning lights and gauges, may fail to show certain warnings or indicators to the driver. This means information such as engine coolant temperature or the vehicle's electrical charge level might not appear on the dashboard when it should, so a driver may not see an important warning while driving.

Toyota and Lexus vehicles have incorrect weight capacity labels low

Reported Mar 24, 2026 · Campaign 26V179000 · Model years: 2024

Toyota is recalling certain Toyota and Lexus trucks, SUVs and hybrid models. The load carrying capacity modification label, which lists how much added weight the vehicle can carry, may show inaccurate values. Because of that, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard covering tire selection and rims. Dealers will replace the label free of charge, and Toyota mailed owner letters on May 26, 2026.

Toyota, Lexus and Subaru rearview camera may freeze medium

Reported Oct 30, 2025 · Campaign 25V744000 · Model years: 2024

Toyota is recalling certain 2022 through 2026 Toyota and Lexus vehicles and the Subaru Solterra that have the Panoramic View Monitor camera system. Models include RAV4, Highlander, Prius, Sienna Hybrid, Camry Hybrid, Mirai, bZ4X and the Lexus NX and TX Hybrid. A software error can cause the backup camera picture to freeze or go blank when the car is in reverse, so these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard.

Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrid recall questions

How many recalls does the Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrid have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2024 Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrid. 1 of them is rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrid Dashboard Warnings May Fail" (NHTSA campaign 26V341000), reported May 27, 2026.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrid?

No. None of the 3 Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrid recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrid has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Toyota dealer, including for second owners.
