Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrid recalls

3 recalls covering model years 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrid cluster may hide warning lights medium

Reported 2026-05-27 · Campaign 26V341000 · Model years: 2024

On certain Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrids and some related Toyota and Lexus models, the display screen in front of the driver may fail to show some warning lights and gauges. That means you might not be told about problems such as engine coolant getting too hot or trouble with the electrical charge, so you could keep driving with a fault you know nothing about. Toyota fixes this with a free software update sent to the car over the air.

Toyota and Lexus vehicles carry wrong weight capacity label low

Reported 2026-03-24 · Campaign 26V179000 · Model years: 2024

On many recent Toyota and Lexus models, the sticker that tells you how much added weight the vehicle can carry shows incorrect numbers. That label is what owners use to judge how many passengers plus how much luggage, gear or cargo is safe to load. Because the figures are wrong, you could load the vehicle heavier than it is built to handle without realizing it. The dealer replaces the label free.

Toyota, Lexus and Subaru Solterra rearview cameras may freeze medium

Reported 2025-10-30 · Campaign 25V744000 · Model years: 2024

These vehicles have a Panoramic View Monitor system that shows what is behind and around you. A software error can make the backup camera image freeze or go completely blank when you shift into reverse. That means you may not see a child, a pet or an object behind the vehicle. Federal rules require a working rear view. A dealer will update the parking assist software at no cost; it is a software fix, not a part replacement.
