Toyota Camry recalls

14 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Toyota Camry and Lexus fuel pumps may stall engine high

Reported 2025-01-22 · Campaign 25V028000 · Model years: 2018

The low-pressure fuel pump sits inside the gas tank and sends gasoline to the engine. On certain 2018 Camry, 2019 Lexus NX300 and 2019 RX350L vehicles, that pump can fail. When it does, the engine can stall, which may happen while you are moving — in an intersection, merging, or on the highway. Toyota and Lexus dealers will replace the whole fuel pump assembly for free. Owner letters for this recall were mailed in March 2025.

Toyota Camry second-row headrest brackets poorly welded medium

Reported 2024-02-21 · Campaign 24V128000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

The head restraints, meaning the padded headrests, on the middle and right-hand seats in the second row may sit in brackets that were not welded well enough at the factory. A weak bracket may not hold a passenger's head in place during a crash, which can lead to a worse head or neck injury for whoever is sitting there. It also falls short of a federal safety rule for head restraints. Toyota dealers will replace the larger rear seat back section at no charge.

Toyota and Lexus front passenger airbag may not inflate high

Reported 2023-12-20 · Campaign 23V865000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

These Toyota and Lexus vehicles have a sensor in the front passenger seat that tells the airbag system whether someone is sitting there. A short circuit can develop inside that sensor. If it does, the front passenger airbag may not inflate during a crash, so whoever is riding in that seat could be hurt worse than they otherwise would be. The dealer will inspect the sensor and replace it if needed, free of charge. Owner letters are being mailed in stages.

Toyota Camry lug nuts may loosen and wheel detach critical

Reported 2023-06-15 · Campaign 23V432000 · Model years: 2023

The nuts that hold the wheels on these Camry and Camry Hybrid cars may not be tight enough. A loose wheel can work its way off while you are driving, which can make you lose control of the car. Toyota says not to drive these vehicles at all until they are fixed. A dealer will check and tighten every lug nut, and replace any parts that were damaged, at no cost to you.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired.

Toyota Camry pump failure can cut brake power assist high

Reported 2021-11-17 · Campaign 21V890000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

These Camrys use a pump that makes the brake pedal easier to push. A small part inside the pump can break, and the pump can then stop working. When that happens you suddenly lose the power help behind the pedal, so braking takes much more effort from your foot and the car needs more room to stop. That can lead to a crash. A dealer will repair or replace the pump for free. Owner letters were mailed in May 2022.

Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps can fail, stalling engine high

Reported 2020-11-04 · Campaign 20V682000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

The fuel pump sits inside the gas tank and pushes gasoline to the engine. On these vehicles that pump can fail. If it does, the engine can stall — shut off — while you are driving, including in traffic or at highway speed. The recall covers a long list of Toyota and Lexus cars, trucks, vans and SUVs. Your dealer will replace the fuel pump assembly with an improved version, free of charge.

Toyota Camry and RAV4 engine cracks can leak coolant critical

Reported 2020-02-06 · Campaign 20V064000 · Model years: 2020

The engine block on these 2.5-liter four-cylinder engines can have tiny holes left over from casting, and cracks can form there. Coolant — the liquid that keeps the engine from overheating — can then leak, either inside the engine or onto the ground. Low coolant can let the engine overheat, and a gas model can stall while you are driving. The engine can also be damaged badly enough to leak oil, which can start a fire. Dealers inspect and replace the engine if needed.

Toyota and Lexus Vehicles: Fuel Pump Can Stall Engine high

Reported 2020-01-13 · Campaign 20V012000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The fuel pump sits inside the gas tank and pushes gas to the engine. On these Toyota and Lexus vehicles, that pump can fail. If it fails, the engine can stall, meaning it shuts off while you are driving. A stall in traffic leaves you without power and can lead to a crash. This recall started in January 2020 and Toyota expanded it twice in March 2020, adding some model years and removing others, so the covered list changed. A dealer replaces the pump at no charge.

Toyota Camry passenger airbag sensor set up wrong high

Reported 2019-08-01 · Campaign 19V567000 · Model years: 2019

The system that senses whether someone is sitting in the front passenger seat, and how big they are, may have been set up wrong at the factory. The car uses that reading to decide how the passenger airbag and the knee airbag should fire. Because the setting is off, those airbags may not go off the way they should in a crash, so a front passenger could be hurt worse. Toyota dealers will reset the sensor for free.

Toyota models carry incorrect weight capacity label with floor mats low

Reported 2019-06-28 · Campaign 19V503000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

These Toyotas came with factory-installed floor mats, and the sticker that lists how much weight the vehicle can safely carry with those mats in place may show the wrong numbers. If you go by that sticker when loading people and cargo, you could put more weight in the vehicle than it is built to handle. An overloaded vehicle is harder to control. The fix is a corrected sticker, provided free, that you place over the wrong one.

Toyota load capacity label may become unreadable low

Reported 2019-03-27 · Campaign 19V244000 · Model years: 2019

Every vehicle has a sticker, usually in the driver's door opening, that tells you how much weight it can safely carry, including passengers and cargo. On these vehicles the printing on that sticker can fade until you cannot read it. If you cannot see those numbers, you may load the vehicle heavier than it was built to handle, which can affect how it steers, brakes, and wears its tires. The dealer will give you a corrected replacement label.

Toyota Camry and Highlander May Lose Brake Assist critical

Reported 2018-04-03 · Campaign 18V211000 · Model years: 2018

A part inside the pump that boosts your braking was machined incorrectly at the factory. That pump is what makes the brake pedal easy to press. If it fails, the power assist can disappear suddenly while you are driving. The brakes themselves still work, but the pedal becomes much harder to push and stopping takes far more effort and distance. The failure can happen without warning. Dealers replace the brake booster vacuum pump at no cost to you.

Toyota Camry engines may stall from oversized pistons high

Reported 2018-03-28 · Campaign 18V200000 · Model years: 2018

Some 2018 Toyota Camry cars built with the 2.5-liter four-cylinder engine left the factory with pistons that are slightly too big. Pistons are the parts that move up and down inside the engine to make power. When they are the wrong size, the engine can shut off on its own while you are driving. Your dealer will check the date codes stamped on the pistons, and if yours came from the bad batch, the whole engine gets replaced at no cost.

Toyota Camry V6 fuel line may leak gasoline critical

Reported 2018-02-14 · Campaign 18V108000 · Model years: 2018

On some V6 Camrys, the metal pipes that carry gasoline to the engine may not have been joined tightly to the fuel hoses under the hood. A loose connection can let gasoline leak out. Gasoline that leaks near anything hot or sparking can catch fire. The dealer will check that the fuel pipes are connected correctly and fix any that are not, at no cost to you.
