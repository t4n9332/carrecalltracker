Toyota Camry recalls

14 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Toyota Camry Low-Pressure Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported 2025-01-22 · Campaign 25V028000 · Model years: 2018

On certain Camry vehicles, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If the pump fails, the engine can stall.

Toyota Camry rear head restraint brackets poorly welded medium

Reported 2024-02-21 · Campaign 24V128000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Toyota is recalling certain Camry and Camry Hybrid vehicles. The brackets that hold the head restraints on the center and right-hand seats in the second row may not have been welded well enough. Because of that, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for head restraints. Dealers will replace the rear seat-back frame assembly for the larger section of the back seat.

Toyota, Lexus airbag sensor may block deployment high

Reported 2023-12-20 · Campaign 23V865000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

On certain Toyota and Lexus models, a short circuit can develop in the sensor that detects who is sitting in the front passenger seat, called the occupant classification sensor. If this happens, it can prevent the front passenger airbag from deploying in a crash.

Toyota Camry Loose Lug Nuts Wheel Risk critical

Reported 2023-06-15 · Campaign 23V432000 · Model years: 2023

The nuts that hold the wheels onto some Camry and Camry Hybrid vehicles may not be tight enough. If a lug nut works loose, the wheel it is holding can separate from the vehicle while you are driving. Toyota dealers will check the lug nuts on all affected vehicles, tighten any that need it, and replace any parts that were damaged in the process, all free of charge to the owner.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired.

Toyota Camry Brake Vacuum Pump May Fail high

Reported 2021-11-17 · Campaign 21V890000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Some Camry vehicles have a part inside the brake vacuum pump that may break. If it breaks, the pump can fail, which may cause a sudden loss of power brake assist.

Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps may fail, stalling engine high

Reported 2020-11-04 · Campaign 20V682000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

Toyota is recalling a large number of Toyota and Lexus vehicles from the 2017 through 2020 model years, including Camry, Corolla, RAV4, Highlander, Tacoma, Tundra, Sienna and many Lexus models. The low-pressure fuel pump, which sits inside the fuel tank and sends gasoline to the engine, may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while you are driving. This recall expands an earlier recall covering the same fuel pump problem.

Toyota Camry and RAV4 engines may leak coolant critical

Reported 2020-02-06 · Campaign 20V064000 · Model years: 2020

Tiny flaws in the engine casting may develop into cracks over time. These cracks can let engine coolant leak, either inside the engine or outside of it. A coolant leak can cause the engine to overheat, and in gasoline-only vehicles this can make the engine stall while driving. The engine may also suffer mechanical damage, which could lead to an oil leak.

Toyota, Lexus fuel pump may fail, stall engine high

Reported 2020-01-13 · Campaign 20V012000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On many Toyota and Lexus models, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If the pump fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven, which affects the driver's ability to keep control of the vehicle at that moment.

Toyota Camry Sensor Flaw May Stop Airbag Deployment high

Reported 2019-08-01 · Campaign 19V567000 · Model years: 2019

In these vehicles, the system that senses how heavy the front passenger is may not have been set up correctly. This system helps decide whether the front passenger air bag and knee air bag should inflate in a crash. Because it may be miscalibrated, those air bags might not inflate the way they are supposed to during a crash. Toyota will recalibrate this system so it works correctly, at no cost to the owner.

Toyota Floor Mat Label May Be Wrong low

Reported 2019-06-28 · Campaign 19V503000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Certain Toyota vehicles with factory-installed floor mats have a load-carrying capacity label that may show incorrect information, so these vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard for tire and rim selection. An incorrect label could lead someone to unintentionally overload the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash. The distributor will provide a corrected label to place over the wrong one, free of charge.

Toyota load capacity label may become unreadable low

Reported 2019-03-27 · Campaign 19V244000 · Model years: 2019

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain 2019 Toyota vehicles because of a problem with the load capacity label. The printing on the label, which shows how much weight the vehicle can carry, may become impossible to read. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule covering tire selection and rims. Dealers will give owners a corrected load carrying capacity label to replace the one that cannot be read.

Toyota Camry, Highlander Brake Assist May Fail high

Reported 2018-04-03 · Campaign 18V211000 · Model years: 2018

Toyota is recalling certain Camry and Highlander vehicles because of a manufacturing problem with the brake booster vacuum pump assembly. An oil passage inside the pump's rotor may not have been machined correctly, and this could lead to a sudden loss of brake assist while driving. Losing brake assist suddenly can make it harder to stop the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash.

Toyota Camry Engine Pistons May Cause Stalling high

Reported 2018-03-28 · Campaign 18V200000 · Model years: 2018

Some 2018 Toyota Camry cars with a 2.5-liter four-cylinder engine may have pistons inside the engine that are bigger than they're supposed to be. This can cause the engine to stall unexpectedly while driving.

Toyota Camry V6 Fuel Line Leak Risk critical

Reported 2018-02-14 · Campaign 18V108000 · Model years: 2018

Certain Toyota Camry vehicles with V6 engines may have fuel delivery pipes that aren't properly connected to the fuel hoses in the engine compartment. A loose connection like this can allow fuel to leak from the fuel system.
