Gulf States Toyota vehicles have wrong weight capacity labels low

Reported 2024-07-23 · Campaign 24V548000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

These Toyotas were sold with accessories added through Gulf States Toyota. When accessories are added, a sticker is supposed to be placed on the vehicle showing how much less weight it can now carry in people and cargo. On these vehicles that sticker shows the wrong numbers. If you go by it, you could load the vehicle heavier than it is rated for. Gulf States Toyota is mailing corrected labels to owners at no charge; nothing on the vehicle needs repair.

Subaru BRZ and Toyota GR86 rear turn signals fail medium

Reported 2023-08-31 · Campaign 23V609000 · Model years: 2022

The turn signals at the back of these cars can stop working from time to time. The problem comes and goes, so the lights may work one moment and not the next. Drivers behind you would get no warning that you are about to turn or change lanes, which can lead to a crash. The fix is to replace both rear light assemblies — the complete taillight units — and the dealer does this free of charge.
