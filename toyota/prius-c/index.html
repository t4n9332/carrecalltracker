Toyota Prius C may lose drive power suddenly high

Reported 2019-06-26 · Campaign 19V491000 · Model years: 2018

Your Prius C has a part that takes power from the big hybrid battery and uses it to charge the small 12-volt battery that runs the car's electronics. On some of these cars that part can fail. When it does, the small battery stops getting charged, and the car can lose the ability to drive, possibly while you are out on the road. Toyota is replacing the part free of charge.

Toyota Prius C missing passenger airbag warning label low

Reported 2018-03-15 · Campaign 18V176000 · Model years: 2018

A sticker is missing. On these Toyota Prius C cars sold in Puerto Rico, the factory did not attach the label that warns about the front passenger airbag and tells you where children should ride. Nothing on the car is broken — but without that label, someone may put a child in the front seat where the airbag could hurt them in a crash. Toyota will mail replacement stick-on labels to owners at no cost.
