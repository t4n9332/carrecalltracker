Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps can fail, stalling engine high

Reported 2020-11-04 · Campaign 20V682000 · Model years: 2018

The fuel pump sits inside the gas tank and pushes gasoline to the engine. On these vehicles that pump can fail. If it does, the engine can stall — shut off — while you are driving, including in traffic or at highway speed. The recall covers a long list of Toyota and Lexus cars, trucks, vans and SUVs. Your dealer will replace the fuel pump assembly with an improved version, free of charge.

Land Cruiser and LX 570 passenger airbags may deactivate high

Reported 2018-12-13 · Campaign 18V887000 · Model years: 2018

There is a wire under the front passenger seat that tells the car whether the seat belt is buckled and pulled tight. Over years of use that wire can break. If it does, the car shuts off the front passenger's front airbag, knee airbag, and the side airbag built into that seat. Those airbags would then not inflate in a crash, which matters most for whoever is riding in the front passenger seat. A dealer will fix or replace the seat belt assembly.
