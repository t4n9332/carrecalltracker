Toyota RAV4 Plug-In Hybrid May Lose Power Driving high

Reported Oct 7, 2026 · Campaign 26V659000 · Model years: 2026

A computer that helps run the plug-in hybrid system may run into an error. If this happens while driving at higher speeds, the hybrid system may reduce power or shut down. This can cause the vehicle to suddenly lose drive power while it is moving.
