Toyota, Lexus airbag sensor may block deployment high

Reported Dec 20, 2023 · Campaign 23V865000 · Model years: 2021

On certain Toyota and Lexus models, a short circuit can develop in the sensor that detects who is sitting in the front passenger seat, called the occupant classification sensor. If this happens, it can prevent the front passenger airbag from deploying in a crash.

Toyota Corolla and Avalon Airbags May Not Deploy high

Reported Jan 17, 2020 · Campaign 20V024000 · Model years: 2018

Certain Toyota Corolla, Avalon, and Avalon Hybrid vehicles have an airbag control unit that may malfunction during certain crashes. This could stop the airbags and seat belt pretensioners from deploying the way they are supposed to. Dealers will inspect the airbag control unit and install a noise filter between it and its wire harness, as necessary.

Toyota load capacity label may become unreadable low

Reported Mar 27, 2019 · Campaign 19V244000 · Model years: 2019

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain 2019 Toyota vehicles because of a problem with the load capacity label. The printing on the label, which shows how much weight the vehicle can carry, may become impossible to read. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule covering tire selection and rims. Dealers will give owners a corrected load carrying capacity label to replace the one that cannot be read.

Toyota Camry, Avalon Hybrid Fuel Pipe May Leak critical

Reported Feb 14, 2019 · Campaign 19V097000 · Model years: 2019

On some of these vehicles, the fuel filler pipe may not have been properly reconnected to the fuel tank during an inspection that takes place after the vehicle is assembled. Because of this, the filler pipe may not be securely attached, which can allow fuel to leak out of the tank. If that leaked fuel comes into contact with a source of ignition, it can start a fire.

Toyota Avalon, Sequoia, Tundra: Airbags May Not Deploy high

Reported Oct 4, 2018 · Campaign 18V685000 · Model years: 2019

On some Avalon, Avalon Hybrid, Sequoia, and Tundra vehicles, the air bag control computer may mistakenly detect a fault during the vehicle's start-up self-check. If that happens, the air bags may not deploy as intended in a crash.

Toyota Avalon Hybrid recall questions

How many recalls does the Toyota Avalon Hybrid have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2018–2021 Toyota Avalon Hybrid. 4 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Toyota, Lexus airbag sensor may block deployment" (NHTSA campaign 23V865000), reported Dec 20, 2023.

Which Toyota Avalon Hybrid model year has the most recalls?

The 2019 Toyota Avalon Hybrid has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2018 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Toyota Avalon Hybrid?

No. None of the 5 Toyota Avalon Hybrid recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Toyota Avalon Hybrid has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Toyota dealer, including for second owners.
