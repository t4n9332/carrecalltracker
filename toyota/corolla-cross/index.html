Toyota Corolla Cross recalls

4 recalls covering model years 2022, 2023. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Toyota and Lexus vehicles may have disabled driver's airbag high

Reported 2025-01-29 · Campaign 25V040000 · Model years: 2023

Certain Toyota Corolla, Corolla Cross, Corolla Cross Hybrid, Highlander, Highlander Hybrid and Tacoma vehicles, plus Lexus RX350, NX250 and NX350 models, may have been repaired incorrectly under an earlier recall. Inside the steering column is a coiled ribbon of wiring that carries power to the airbag in the steering wheel. A weld in that connection may not have been done properly, so the connection can come apart and shut the driver's airbag off. The airbag then will not inflate in a crash.

Toyota Corolla Cross: passenger airbag may not open properly high

Reported 2023-12-20 · Campaign 23V864000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

The plastic dashboard on the passenger side is supposed to have a built-in weak seam so the passenger airbag can burst through it in a crash. On these vehicles the dashboard may have been made without that seam, so the passenger airbag may not open the way it should. These cars were already looked at once under an earlier recall, but they must go back to the dealer for another inspection and, if needed, a new dashboard.

Toyota and Lexus steering wiring can disable driver's airbag high

Reported 2023-07-12 · Campaign 23V480000 · Model years: 2023

Inside the steering column is a coiled ribbon of wiring that keeps the driver's airbag connected as the steering wheel turns. On these vehicles, one electrical connection in that part may not have been welded well enough. If it comes apart, the driver's airbag is switched off and will not inflate in a crash. This recall also means these vehicles do not meet the federal crash-protection standard for occupant safety. Dealers will inspect this part and replace it at no cost if needed.

Toyota Corolla Cross passenger airbag may not open properly high

Reported 2023-05-30 · Campaign 23V384000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

The dashboard panel in front of the passenger seat may have been built without a required weakened seam. That seam is what lets the passenger airbag break through the panel and open correctly in a crash. Without it, the airbag may not inflate the way it should, so a passenger could be hurt more badly in a collision. The vehicle does not meet the federal crash protection standard. Dealers will inspect the panel and replace it if needed.
