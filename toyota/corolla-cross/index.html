Toyota And Lexus Steering Defect Disables Airbag high

Reported Jan 29, 2025 · Campaign 25V040000 · Model years: 2023

These Toyota and Lexus vehicles may have been incorrectly repaired under an earlier recall for the same part. Inside the steering column there is an electrical connection that may not be welded well enough, and if it comes apart, the driver's airbag can turn off. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard for protecting occupants in a crash.

Toyota Corolla Cross Passenger Airbag May Not Deploy high

Reported Dec 20, 2023 · Campaign 23V864000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

On certain Corolla Cross vehicles previously inspected under an earlier recall, the instrument panel may have been built without a required cut that lets the front-passenger airbag deploy properly. Dealers will inspect the instrument panel and replace it if needed. Vehicles already inspected under the earlier recall must return to the dealer for this additional check.

Toyota, Lexus: Steering Cable May Disable Air Bag high

Reported Jul 12, 2023 · Campaign 23V480000 · Model years: 2023

On certain Toyota Corolla, Corolla Cross, Highlander, Tacoma, and related Lexus vehicles, an electrical connection inside the steering column's spiral cable assembly may not be welded well enough. If this connection separates, it can deactivate the driver's air bag. A deactivated air bag will not deploy the way it's supposed to. Dealers will inspect the spiral cable assembly and replace it if necessary, free of charge.

Toyota Corolla Cross Passenger Air Bag May Fail high

Reported May 30, 2023 · Campaign 23V384000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Some Corolla Cross vehicles were built with an instrument panel that is missing a required perforation. That perforation is needed so the front-passenger air bag can break through the panel and deploy properly. Without it, the air bag may not deploy the way it's supposed to if the vehicle is in a crash, which is a safety feature meant to protect the front passenger.

Toyota Corolla Cross recall questions

How many recalls does the Toyota Corolla Cross have?

NHTSA lists 4 safety recalls covering the 2022–2023 Toyota Corolla Cross. 4 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Toyota And Lexus Steering Defect Disables Airbag" (NHTSA campaign 25V040000), reported Jan 29, 2025.

Which Toyota Corolla Cross model year has the most recalls?

The 2023 Toyota Corolla Cross has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2022 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Toyota Corolla Cross?

No. None of the 4 Toyota Corolla Cross recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Toyota Corolla Cross has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Toyota dealer, including for second owners.
