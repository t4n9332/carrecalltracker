Toyota 4runner recalls

12 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Toyota Instrument Panel May Fail To Show Warnings high

Reported 2025-09-11 · Campaign 25V595000 · Model years: 2025

A software error in the instrument panel can occur when the vehicle starts up. Because of this, the panel may fail to show the vehicle's speed, brake system warning, and tire pressure warning lights. This is happening on a range of Toyota models covered by this recall. The recall is being carried out in phases, with owner notification letters sent out in stages, and additional letters expected in the future.

Toyota accessory weight label may show wrong numbers low

Reported 2024-07-23 · Campaign 24V548000 · Model years: 2024

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain Toyota vehicles, mostly from the 2023 through 2025 model years, that were fitted with Gulf States Toyota accessories. Models include 4Runner, Corolla, Highlander, Prius, Tacoma, Tundra, Grand Highlander, Sequoia Hybrid, bZ4X, Venza Hybrid and others. The sticker that lists how much weight the added accessories take up may show wrong numbers, so these vehicles do not meet the federal standard for tire and rim selection.

Toyota 4Runner Label Lists Wrong Load Capacity medium

Reported 2023-10-12 · Campaign 23V680000 · Model years: 2023

Some 2023 Toyota 4Runner vehicles have a label showing the wrong maximum load capacity for the vehicle. If an owner loads the vehicle based on this incorrect number, the vehicle could end up carrying more weight than it is actually rated for, resulting in an overloaded vehicle. Southeast Toyota Distributors will mail out a corrected label free of charge to fix the mistake.

Toyota 4Runner Load Capacity Labels Are Incorrect low

Reported 2022-07-06 · Campaign 22V480000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Some 4Runners fitted with 20-inch Black Gunner Wheels were built with the wrong load carrying capacity modification labels. These labels tell the owner how much weight the vehicle can carry. Because the printed information is wrong, the vehicles do not meet the federal standard covering tire and rim selection. An owner going by the label could end up putting more weight in the vehicle than it is meant to carry. Replacement labels will be mailed to owners.

Toyota 4Runner, Tacoma and Highlander weight labels may fade low

Reported 2022-05-06 · Campaign 22V310000 · Model years: 2022

Some Toyota 4Runner, Tacoma, Highlander and Highlander Hybrid vehicles have a label showing how much weight the vehicle can carry. On these vehicles the label may not be permanent and can fade until it can no longer be read. Because of that, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard covering tire and rim selection. Gulf States Toyota is mailing owners a replacement label along with instructions for putting it on.

Toyota 4Runner High Beam Headlight Failure medium

Reported 2021-12-13 · Campaign 21V963000 · Model years: 2022

On some Toyota 4Runner vehicles, installing accessory fog lights can cause the driver's side high beam headlight to stop working. This does not meet federal lighting safety requirements. Without a working high beam on the driver's side, the driver has less light to see the road ahead.

Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps may fail, stalling engine high

Reported 2020-11-04 · Campaign 20V682000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Toyota is recalling a large number of Toyota and Lexus vehicles from the 2017 through 2020 model years, including Camry, Corolla, RAV4, Highlander, Tacoma, Tundra, Sienna and many Lexus models. The low-pressure fuel pump, which sits inside the fuel tank and sends gasoline to the engine, may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while you are driving. This recall expands an earlier recall covering the same fuel pump problem.

Toyota 4Runner Loose Steering and Suspension Bolts high

Reported 2019-09-20 · Campaign 19V670000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On certain 4Runners, some steering and suspension parts may not have been tightened properly when they were installed. If a bolt is loose, a part could shift or even come off the vehicle. That could make the truck harder to control or send a piece into the road as a hazard for other drivers. Dealers will check these fasteners and tighten them back up as needed.

Toyota load capacity label may become unreadable low

Reported 2019-03-27 · Campaign 19V244000 · Model years: 2019

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain 2019 Toyota vehicles because of a problem with the load capacity label. The printing on the label, which shows how much weight the vehicle can carry, may become impossible to read. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule covering tire selection and rims. Dealers will give owners a corrected load carrying capacity label to replace the one that cannot be read.

Toyota 4Runner Label May Show Wrong Weight Rating low

Reported 2018-10-25 · Campaign 18V753000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Southeast Toyota Distributors modified certain 4Runner vehicles to add a TRD wheel package. On these vehicles, the certification label may show the wrong gross vehicle weight rating, the maximum weight the vehicle is designed to carry. Because the label is wrong, the vehicle may not meet federal rules for tire and rim selection. Southeast Toyota will mail owners a corrected label to fix the mistake.

Toyota and Lexus vehicles carry incorrect load capacity labels low

Reported 2017-12-20 · Campaign 17V831000 · Model years: 2018

Toyota is recalling certain Toyota 4Runner, Tundra, Highlander, RAV4, Sienna and Tacoma vehicles, along with the Lexus GX460. These vehicles may have an incorrect label showing how much weight the vehicle is able to carry. Because of this, they do not meet the federal safety rule covering tire selection and rims. Toyota will contact owners and send them corrected labels at no cost.

Toyota 4Runner Grille Overlay May Detach medium

Reported 2017-11-29 · Campaign 17V763000 · Model years: 2018

Some 2015-2018 Toyota 4Runner vehicles have an accessory Billet Grille Overlay installed by Southeast Toyota Distributors or its dealers. The U-nut that secures this overlay grille in place may fracture, which can let the grille overlay come loose and detach from the front of the vehicle.
