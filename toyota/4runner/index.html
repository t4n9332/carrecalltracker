Toyota 4runner recalls

12 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Toyota and Lexus dashboard may not show speed or warnings medium

Reported 2025-09-11 · Campaign 25V595000 · Model years: 2025

When these Toyota and Lexus vehicles start up, a software error can leave the instrument panel, the display behind the steering wheel, missing important information. Your speed may not appear, and the warning lights for the brake system and for low tire pressure may not light up. That means you would not know how fast you are going, or that the brakes or tires need attention. Most vehicles get a free software update sent to the car wirelessly.

Toyota accessory weight label may show wrong numbers low

Reported 2024-07-23 · Campaign 24V548000 · Model years: 2024

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain Toyota vehicles, mostly from the 2023 through 2025 model years, that were fitted with Gulf States Toyota accessories. Models include 4Runner, Corolla, Highlander, Prius, Tacoma, Tundra, Grand Highlander, Sequoia Hybrid, bZ4X, Venza Hybrid and others. The sticker that lists how much weight the added accessories take up may show wrong numbers, so these vehicles do not meet the federal standard for tire and rim selection.

Toyota 4Runner carries wrong load capacity label low

Reported 2023-10-12 · Campaign 23V680000 · Model years: 2023

Certain 2023 Toyota 4Runners have a sticker listing the wrong maximum load the vehicle can carry. If you go by that sticker when loading people, cargo, or gear, you could end up carrying more weight than the vehicle is built to handle. An overloaded vehicle is harder to control and puts extra strain on the tires. The distributor will mail you a corrected label to replace the wrong one — no dealer visit is required for this one.

Toyota 4Runner has wrong weight limit sticker low

Reported 2022-07-06 · Campaign 22V480000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Some 4Runners fitted with 20-inch Black Gunner Wheels left the distributor with the wrong label showing how much weight the vehicle can safely carry. That sticker tells you the maximum combined weight of people and cargo you should put in the truck. Because the number printed on it is incorrect, you could load up the 4Runner past what it is actually rated to handle without realizing it. The distributor is mailing corrected labels to owners at no cost.

Toyota 4Runner, Tacoma, Highlander weight labels can fade low

Reported 2022-05-06 · Campaign 22V310000 · Model years: 2022

The sticker that tells you how much weight your vehicle can safely carry was printed with ink that can fade until you can no longer read it. Without a readable label, you could load the truck or SUV with more people and cargo than it is built to handle. Gulf States Toyota is mailing owners a free replacement label with instructions to put it on yourself. Letters went out June 30, 2022.

Toyota 4Runner driver side high beam may not work medium

Reported 2021-12-13 · Campaign 21V963000 · Model years: 2022

On these 4Runners, accessory fog lights were installed, and the wiring can leave the driver's side high beam unable to come on. Federal rules require both high beams to work. With only one working, you get less light on dark roads and it is harder to see far ahead or spot hazards early. A dealer will install an updated wiring harness so the high beam works again, at no charge to you.

Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps may fail, stalling engine high

Reported 2020-11-04 · Campaign 20V682000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Toyota is recalling a large number of Toyota and Lexus vehicles from the 2017 through 2020 model years, including Camry, Corolla, RAV4, Highlander, Tacoma, Tundra, Sienna and many Lexus models. The low-pressure fuel pump, which sits inside the fuel tank and sends gasoline to the engine, may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while you are driving. This recall expands an earlier recall covering the same fuel pump problem.

Toyota 4Runner steering and suspension bolts may be loose high

Reported 2019-09-20 · Campaign 19V670000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On these 4Runners, some of the nuts and bolts that hold steering and suspension parts together may not have been tightened enough when the vehicles were being prepared. Loose fasteners can let those parts shift or work their way free over time. If that happens while you are driving, you could lose control of the vehicle, or an add-on part could fall off and become a hazard for the cars behind you. A dealer checks and retightens them.

Toyota load capacity label may become unreadable low

Reported 2019-03-27 · Campaign 19V244000 · Model years: 2019

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain 2019 Toyota vehicles because of a problem with the load capacity label. The printing on the label, which shows how much weight the vehicle can carry, may become impossible to read. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule covering tire selection and rims. Dealers will give owners a corrected load carrying capacity label to replace the one that cannot be read.

Toyota 4Runner TRD package has wrong weight-rating label low

Reported 2018-10-25 · Campaign 18V753000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Southeast Toyota added a TRD wheel package to these 4Runners. The sticker on the door frame that lists how much total weight the vehicle can safely carry may show the wrong number. If you load the truck based on that sticker, you could end up carrying more weight than the vehicle and its tires are built to handle. The fix is simple: the distributor mails you a corrected sticker to put on in place of the wrong one.

Toyota and Lexus SUVs have wrong weight-limit labels low

Reported 2017-12-20 · Campaign 17V831000 · Model years: 2018

These trucks and SUVs left the factory with an incorrect sticker showing how much weight the vehicle can safely carry. The label itself does not break anything, but if you trust the wrong number you could put in too many people, too much cargo, or too heavy a load. An overloaded vehicle is harder to steer and stop and puts extra strain on the tires, which raises the chance of a crash. Toyota is mailing owners corrected labels free of charge.

Toyota 4Runner accessory grille trim can fall off medium

Reported 2017-11-29 · Campaign 17V763000 · Model years: 2018

Some 4Runners were fitted with a decorative billet grille overlay — a trim piece added over the front grille — by Southeast Toyota or its dealers. A small nut that holds this trim in place can crack. If it breaks, the grille overlay can come loose and fall off the front of the vehicle, possibly while you are driving. Dealers will install upgraded fasteners so the trim stays put.
