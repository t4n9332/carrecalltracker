Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps may fail, stalling engine high

Reported Nov 4, 2020 · Campaign 20V682000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

Toyota is recalling a large number of Toyota and Lexus vehicles from the 2017 through 2020 model years, including Camry, Corolla, RAV4, Highlander, Tacoma, Tundra, Sienna and many Lexus models. The low-pressure fuel pump, which sits inside the fuel tank and sends gasoline to the engine, may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while you are driving. This recall expands an earlier recall covering the same fuel pump problem.

Toyota, Lexus fuel pump may fail, stall engine high

Reported Jan 13, 2020 · Campaign 20V012000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On many Toyota and Lexus models, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If the pump fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven, which affects the driver's ability to keep control of the vehicle at that moment.

Toyota Sienna Wheelchair Van Brake Hose Problem high

Reported Jul 25, 2019 · Campaign 19V553000 · Model years: 2018

Certain wheelchair-accessible vans built on the Toyota Sienna and sold as AMS Genesis models may have rubber brake hoses at the rear wheels that are too short. Because the hoses are too short, they can rub against a part of the rear axle called the trailing arm. Over time, this rubbing can wear against the material of the brake hose itself.

Toyota Sienna Modified by Braun: Steering Shaft May Fracture critical

Reported Jul 16, 2019 · Campaign 19V532000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

These are Toyota Sienna vehicles that were modified by Braun Corporation. Extensions added to the steering shaft as part of that modification may have been manufactured incorrectly. Because of this manufacturing problem, the steering shaft extensions could fracture, meaning they could crack or break apart instead of staying intact as they should. This could happen at the extension added to the original steering shaft.

Toyota Floor Mat Label May Be Wrong low

Reported Jun 28, 2019 · Campaign 19V503000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Certain Toyota vehicles with factory-installed floor mats have a load-carrying capacity label that may show incorrect information, so these vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard for tire and rim selection. An incorrect label could lead someone to unintentionally overload the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash. The distributor will provide a corrected label to place over the wrong one, free of charge.

Toyota Sienna Mobility Vans May Lose Brake Power high

Reported Jun 4, 2019 · Campaign 19V410000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Braun Corporation is recalling certain Toyota Sienna mobility van conversions. During the conversion, the brake lines may have had the wrong tube nut fittings installed. These incorrect fittings can allow pressure in the braking system to drop. When brake system pressure drops, it can reduce the braking power available at the rear brakes, making it harder to slow the vehicle down.

Toyota Sienna Door Weld May Affect Air Bags critical

Reported Apr 23, 2019 · Campaign 19V318000 · Model years: 2019

Some Toyota Sienna vehicles may have incorrect welds inside the passenger front door. These welds can affect the readings from the air bag sensor for the front passenger seat. Because of this, the passenger side front air bag and the curtain shield air bag could deploy when they should not, or fail to deploy when they are needed. Either problem increases the risk of a crash or injury.

Toyota load capacity label may become unreadable low

Reported Mar 27, 2019 · Campaign 19V244000 · Model years: 2019

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain 2019 Toyota vehicles because of a problem with the load capacity label. The printing on the label, which shows how much weight the vehicle can carry, may become impossible to read. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule covering tire selection and rims. Dealers will give owners a corrected load carrying capacity label to replace the one that cannot be read.

Toyota Sienna Alloy Wheels May Crack high

Reported Feb 22, 2018 · Campaign 18V130000 · Model years: 2018

Certain Sienna vehicles were built with a specific batch of 10-spoke alloy wheels that may not have been properly cooled during manufacturing. These wheels may crack at the spokes if they are hit by an impact while driving.

Toyota Sienna recall questions

How many recalls does the Toyota Sienna have?

NHTSA lists 9 safety recalls covering the 2018–2020 Toyota Sienna. 7 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps may fail, stalling engine" (NHTSA campaign 20V682000), reported Nov 4, 2020.

Which Toyota Sienna model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Toyota Sienna has the most, with 7 recalls. The 2020 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Toyota Sienna?

No. None of the 9 Toyota Sienna recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Toyota Sienna has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Toyota dealer, including for second owners.
