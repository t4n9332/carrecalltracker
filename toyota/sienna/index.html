Toyota Sienna recalls

9 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps can fail, stalling engine high

Reported 2020-11-04 · Campaign 20V682000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

The fuel pump sits inside the gas tank and pushes gasoline to the engine. On these vehicles that pump can fail. If it does, the engine can stall — shut off — while you are driving, including in traffic or at highway speed. The recall covers a long list of Toyota and Lexus cars, trucks, vans and SUVs. Your dealer will replace the fuel pump assembly with an improved version, free of charge.

Toyota and Lexus Vehicles: Fuel Pump Can Stall Engine high

Reported 2020-01-13 · Campaign 20V012000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The fuel pump sits inside the gas tank and pushes gas to the engine. On these Toyota and Lexus vehicles, that pump can fail. If it fails, the engine can stall, meaning it shuts off while you are driving. A stall in traffic leaves you without power and can lead to a crash. This recall started in January 2020 and Toyota expanded it twice in March 2020, adding some model years and removing others, so the covered list changed. A dealer replaces the pump at no charge.

Toyota Sienna wheelchair vans: rear brake hoses may rub high

Reported 2019-07-25 · Campaign 19V553000 · Model years: 2018

These are Sienna vans that were converted for wheelchair use by AMS/Vantage. The rubber brake hoses going to the rear wheels may be too short, so they rub against a metal arm in the rear suspension every time the van moves. Over time the rubbing can wear through a hose and cause it to fail. If that happens you lose part of your braking, it takes longer to stop, and the van can be harder to control.

Modified Toyota Sienna steering extensions may crack and break high

Reported 2019-07-16 · Campaign 19V532000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

These are Toyota Sienna vans that were modified by the Braun Corporation. The steering shaft extension — a piece that connects your steering wheel to the rest of the steering system — may have been built incorrectly and can crack. If it cracks all the way through, the steering wheel can stop turning the wheels. Braun has already contacted owners, and dealers will put in new steering shaft extensions at no cost to you.

Toyota models carry incorrect weight capacity label with floor mats low

Reported 2019-06-28 · Campaign 19V503000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

These Toyotas came with factory-installed floor mats, and the sticker that lists how much weight the vehicle can safely carry with those mats in place may show the wrong numbers. If you go by that sticker when loading people and cargo, you could put more weight in the vehicle than it is built to handle. An overloaded vehicle is harder to control. The fix is a corrected sticker, provided free, that you place over the wrong one.

Toyota Sienna wheelchair vans may lose rear braking power high

Reported 2019-06-04 · Campaign 19V410000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

These are 2014-2019 Toyota Sienna vans that Braun converted for wheelchair access. During the conversion, the wrong fittings may have been put on the brake lines. The wrong fittings can let pressure escape from the brake system, so the rear brakes may not grip as hard as they should. That means the van can take longer to stop. A dealer adds a small adaptor to the brake line to fix it.

Toyota Sienna passenger airbags may deploy at wrong time high

Reported 2019-04-23 · Campaign 19V318000 · Model years: 2019

On certain 2019 Toyota Siennas, the front passenger door may have been welded incorrectly inside. That door holds a sensor that tells the airbag system when a crash is happening. With bad welds, the sensor can send wrong readings, so the front passenger airbag and the curtain airbag above the side windows may go off when they should not, or fail to go off when they are needed. Dealers replace the whole passenger front door assembly free.

Toyota load capacity label may become unreadable low

Reported 2019-03-27 · Campaign 19V244000 · Model years: 2019

Every vehicle has a sticker, usually in the driver's door opening, that tells you how much weight it can safely carry, including passengers and cargo. On these vehicles the printing on that sticker can fade until you cannot read it. If you cannot see those numbers, you may load the vehicle heavier than it was built to handle, which can affect how it steers, brakes, and wears its tires. The dealer will give you a corrected replacement label.

Toyota Sienna alloy wheels can crack and come off high

Reported 2018-02-22 · Campaign 18V130000 · Model years: 2018

Some 2018 Sienna vans were built with 10-spoke alloy wheels from one factory that were not cooled properly as they were made. That leaves the metal weak, so hitting a pothole or curb can crack the spokes. A cracked wheel can break away from the hub while you are driving, and you would likely lose control of the van. Toyota dealers will inspect the wheels and replace them if the production location calls for it, at no charge.
