Toyota Grand Highlander recalls

5 recalls covering model years 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Toyota and Lexus vehicles have incorrect weight capacity labels low

Reported Mar 24, 2026 · Campaign 26V179000 · Model years: 2025

Toyota is recalling certain Toyota and Lexus trucks, SUVs and hybrid models. The load carrying capacity modification label, which lists how much added weight the vehicle can carry, may show inaccurate values. Because of that, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard covering tire selection and rims. Dealers will replace the label free of charge, and Toyota mailed owner letters on May 26, 2026.

Toyota, Lexus and Subaru rearview camera may freeze medium

Reported Oct 30, 2025 · Campaign 25V744000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Toyota is recalling certain 2022 through 2026 Toyota and Lexus vehicles and the Subaru Solterra that have the Panoramic View Monitor camera system. Models include RAV4, Highlander, Prius, Sienna Hybrid, Camry Hybrid, Mirai, bZ4X and the Lexus NX and TX Hybrid. A software error can cause the backup camera picture to freeze or go blank when the car is in reverse, so these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard.

Toyota Instrument Panel May Fail To Show Warnings high

Reported Sep 11, 2025 · Campaign 25V595000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

A software error in the instrument panel can occur when the vehicle starts up. Because of this, the panel may fail to show the vehicle's speed, brake system warning, and tire pressure warning lights. This is happening on a range of Toyota models covered by this recall. The recall is being carried out in phases, with owner notification letters sent out in stages, and additional letters expected in the future.

Toyota accessory weight label may show wrong numbers low

Reported Jul 23, 2024 · Campaign 24V548000 · Model years: 2024

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain Toyota vehicles, mostly from the 2023 through 2025 model years, that were fitted with Gulf States Toyota accessories. Models include 4Runner, Corolla, Highlander, Prius, Tacoma, Tundra, Grand Highlander, Sequoia Hybrid, bZ4X, Venza Hybrid and others. The sticker that lists how much weight the added accessories take up may show wrong numbers, so these vehicles do not meet the federal standard for tire and rim selection.

Toyota and Lexus SUV curtain airbags may deploy wrong high

Reported Jun 20, 2024 · Campaign 24V461000 · Model years: 2024

Certain Toyota Grand Highlander and Lexus TX vehicles have driver and passenger side curtain air bags that may not unroll the way they are supposed to. Instead of deploying properly to protect occupants, the air bag could partially deploy outside of an open window. This means that in a crash, the air bag may not be positioned correctly to help protect the people inside the vehicle.

Toyota Grand Highlander recall questions

How many recalls does the Toyota Grand Highlander have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2024–2025 Toyota Grand Highlander. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Toyota and Lexus vehicles have incorrect weight capacity labels" (NHTSA campaign 26V179000), reported Mar 24, 2026.

Which Toyota Grand Highlander model year has the most recalls?

The 2024 Toyota Grand Highlander has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2025 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 3. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Toyota Grand Highlander?

No. None of the 5 Toyota Grand Highlander recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Toyota Grand Highlander has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Toyota dealer, including for second owners.
