Wheelchair Restraints in Various Vehicles May Not Lock high

Reported Dec 17, 2025 · Campaign 25V876000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Certain vehicles equipped with QRT-Deluxe or QRT-Max wheelchair restraints may have retractors that fail to lock. If the retractor does not lock, a wheelchair inside the vehicle may not be properly secured.

Honda, Chrysler and Toyota Vans Wheelchair Restraint Issue medium

Reported Dec 17, 2025 · Campaign 25V877000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

Some vehicles equipped with certain wheelchair restraint systems have retractors that may not lock properly. This means a wheelchair might not be securely held in place while the vehicle is being driven. Dealers will inspect the retractors and replace them if needed, free of charge.

Toyota, Lexus and Subaru rearview camera may freeze medium

Reported Oct 30, 2025 · Campaign 25V744000 · Model years: 2025

Toyota is recalling certain 2022 through 2026 Toyota and Lexus vehicles and the Subaru Solterra that have the Panoramic View Monitor camera system. Models include RAV4, Highlander, Prius, Sienna Hybrid, Camry Hybrid, Mirai, bZ4X and the Lexus NX and TX Hybrid. A software error can cause the backup camera picture to freeze or go blank when the car is in reverse, so these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard.

Toyota Sienna Hybrid Second-Row Seat Rails Flawed high

Reported Oct 7, 2025 · Campaign 25V668000 · Model years: 2025

Toyota is recalling certain Sienna Hybrid vehicles because the rails that hold the second-row seats in place may have been welded incorrectly. A seat rail that is not welded properly may not hold the seat securely in a crash. If the seat cannot properly restrain the person sitting in it during a crash, that person has a higher chance of being injured.

Toyota Sienna Third-Row Seat Bolts Loose high

Reported Feb 13, 2025 · Campaign 25V086000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

On certain Toyota Sienna Hybrid vehicles, the bolts holding the third-row seatback assemblies in place may not have been tightened properly. Because of this, these seats do not meet federal safety standards covering head restraints, seating systems, and child restraint anchors.

Toyota, Lexus airbag sensor may block deployment high

Reported Dec 20, 2023 · Campaign 23V865000 · Model years: 2021

On certain Toyota and Lexus models, a short circuit can develop in the sensor that detects who is sitting in the front passenger seat, called the occupant classification sensor. If this happens, it can prevent the front passenger airbag from deploying in a crash.

Toyota and Lexus stability control may stay switched off high

Reported Apr 13, 2022 · Campaign 22V239000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Toyota is recalling certain Toyota and Lexus vehicles because of a problem with the software that controls the skid control computer. If the driver turns off the vehicle stability control system, the software may fail to turn it back on the next time the vehicle is started. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule for electronic stability control systems. Dealers will update the software so the system switches back on as it should.

Toyota Sienna Rear Seat Belt Webbing Guide Defect medium

Reported Nov 17, 2021 · Campaign 21V889000 · Model years: 2022

Some eight-passenger Sienna vehicles may have an incorrect webbing guide installed on the outboard second-row seat belts. If the seat belt pretensioner activates, this incorrect guide may damage the seat belt webbing and cause it to tear.

Toyota Sienna Hybrid recall questions

How many recalls does the Toyota Sienna Hybrid have?

NHTSA lists 8 safety recalls covering the 2021–2025 Toyota Sienna Hybrid. 5 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Wheelchair Restraints in Various Vehicles May Not Lock" (NHTSA campaign 25V876000), reported Dec 17, 2025.

Which Toyota Sienna Hybrid model year has the most recalls?

The 2025 Toyota Sienna Hybrid has the most, with 5 recalls. The 2023 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Toyota Sienna Hybrid?

No. None of the 8 Toyota Sienna Hybrid recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Toyota Sienna Hybrid has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Toyota dealer, including for second owners.
