Toyota Highlander second-row seat back may not lock high

Reported Mar 5, 2026 · Campaign 26V128000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

This recall covers certain Toyota Highlander and Highlander Hybrid vehicles. The second-row seat backs may fail to lock into position when the seat back is being adjusted.

Toyota Highlander Third-Row Seat Belt Anchor Defect medium

Reported Dec 17, 2025 · Campaign 25V872000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Some Highlander and Highlander Hybrid vehicles may have the third-row center seat belt's roof anchor installed at an incorrect angle. This can prevent the seat belt from properly restraining the occupant.

Toyota, Lexus and Subaru rearview camera may freeze medium

Reported Oct 30, 2025 · Campaign 25V744000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

Toyota is recalling certain 2022 through 2026 Toyota and Lexus vehicles and the Subaru Solterra that have the Panoramic View Monitor camera system. Models include RAV4, Highlander, Prius, Sienna Hybrid, Camry Hybrid, Mirai, bZ4X and the Lexus NX and TX Hybrid. A software error can cause the backup camera picture to freeze or go blank when the car is in reverse, so these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard.

Toyota Instrument Panel May Fail To Show Warnings high

Reported Sep 11, 2025 · Campaign 25V595000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

A software error in the instrument panel can occur when the vehicle starts up. Because of this, the panel may fail to show the vehicle's speed, brake system warning, and tire pressure warning lights. This is happening on a range of Toyota models covered by this recall. The recall is being carried out in phases, with owner notification letters sent out in stages, and additional letters expected in the future.

Toyota And Lexus Steering Defect Disables Airbag high

Reported Jan 29, 2025 · Campaign 25V040000 · Model years: 2023

These Toyota and Lexus vehicles may have been incorrectly repaired under an earlier recall for the same part. Inside the steering column there is an electrical connection that may not be welded well enough, and if it comes apart, the driver's airbag can turn off. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard for protecting occupants in a crash.

Toyota accessory weight label may show wrong numbers low

Reported Jul 23, 2024 · Campaign 24V548000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain Toyota vehicles, mostly from the 2023 through 2025 model years, that were fitted with Gulf States Toyota accessories. Models include 4Runner, Corolla, Highlander, Prius, Tacoma, Tundra, Grand Highlander, Sequoia Hybrid, bZ4X, Venza Hybrid and others. The sticker that lists how much weight the added accessories take up may show wrong numbers, so these vehicles do not meet the federal standard for tire and rim selection.

Toyota Highlander Accessory Tires Rated Too Low high

Reported Jun 18, 2024 · Campaign 24V452000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Southeast Toyota Distributors says some Highlander vehicles came with 20-inch accessory tires that aren't rated to carry as much weight as these vehicles need. Because the tires' weight rating is too low for the vehicle, they could end up carrying more weight than they were built to handle. This means the vehicles don't meet a federal safety standard for tire selection.

Toyota Highlander Blackout Package Tires Underrated high

Reported Jun 6, 2024 · Campaign 24V419000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

Certain Highlander L and LE models built with the Blackout Package's 20-inch black alloy wheels were fitted with tires that do not have a high enough load rating for the vehicle. This means those tires are not rated to safely carry the weight they may need to carry on these vehicles.

Toyota, Lexus airbag sensor may block deployment high

Reported Dec 20, 2023 · Campaign 23V865000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On certain Toyota and Lexus models, a short circuit can develop in the sensor that detects who is sitting in the front passenger seat, called the occupant classification sensor. If this happens, it can prevent the front passenger airbag from deploying in a crash.

Toyota Highlander Front Bumper Cover May Detach medium

Reported Oct 26, 2023 · Campaign 23V720000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

Toyota is recalling certain Highlander and Highlander Hybrid SUVs. During normal driving, a minor impact to the front lower bumper cover may cause the cover to come loose or fall off the vehicle. This means something as small as a light bump could leave the front bumper cover hanging or detached from the car while it's on the road.

Toyota, Lexus: Steering Cable May Disable Air Bag high

Reported Jul 12, 2023 · Campaign 23V480000 · Model years: 2023

On certain Toyota Corolla, Corolla Cross, Highlander, Tacoma, and related Lexus vehicles, an electrical connection inside the steering column's spiral cable assembly may not be welded well enough. If this connection separates, it can deactivate the driver's air bag. A deactivated air bag will not deploy the way it's supposed to. Dealers will inspect the spiral cable assembly and replace it if necessary, free of charge.

Toyota 4Runner, Tacoma and Highlander weight labels may fade low

Reported May 6, 2022 · Campaign 22V310000 · Model years: 2022

Some Toyota 4Runner, Tacoma, Highlander and Highlander Hybrid vehicles have a label showing how much weight the vehicle can carry. On these vehicles the label may not be permanent and can fade until it can no longer be read. Because of that, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard covering tire and rim selection. Gulf States Toyota is mailing owners a replacement label along with instructions for putting it on.

Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps may fail, stalling engine high

Reported Nov 4, 2020 · Campaign 20V682000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Toyota is recalling a large number of Toyota and Lexus vehicles from the 2017 through 2020 model years, including Camry, Corolla, RAV4, Highlander, Tacoma, Tundra, Sienna and many Lexus models. The low-pressure fuel pump, which sits inside the fuel tank and sends gasoline to the engine, may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while you are driving. This recall expands an earlier recall covering the same fuel pump problem.

Highlander seat trim can block side airbag high

Reported Oct 14, 2020 · Campaign 20V633000 · Model years: 2020

Some Highlander and Highlander Hybrid vehicles have incorrect trim covers on one or both front seats. These covers can prevent the seat-mounted side airbag from deploying properly in a crash, which increases the risk of injury to the occupant.

Toyota Highlander Fuel Programming Error May Stall Engine medium

Reported Mar 19, 2020 · Campaign 20V162000 · Model years: 2020

On some 2020 Highlander vehicles with the 3.5L V6 engine, a programming error in the engine control computer can cause fuel to not be correctly supplied to the engine while the stop-and-restart feature is in use.

Toyota, Lexus fuel pump may fail, stall engine high

Reported Jan 13, 2020 · Campaign 20V012000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On many Toyota and Lexus models, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If the pump fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven, which affects the driver's ability to keep control of the vehicle at that moment.

Toyota load capacity label may become unreadable low

Reported Mar 27, 2019 · Campaign 19V244000 · Model years: 2019

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain 2019 Toyota vehicles because of a problem with the load capacity label. The printing on the label, which shows how much weight the vehicle can carry, may become impossible to read. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule covering tire selection and rims. Dealers will give owners a corrected load carrying capacity label to replace the one that cannot be read.

Aftermarket Highlander Windshields May Cause Engine Stalling high

Reported Nov 27, 2018 · Campaign 18E107000 · Model years: 2018

Certain aftermarket replacement windshields sold for Highlander vehicles have a wire harness built in. Water can leak into this harness and damage the vehicle's engine control computer. If that computer is damaged, the engine may stall while you are driving. An engine that stalls unexpectedly increases the risk of a crash. This applies to aftermarket windshields, not necessarily the windshield your vehicle came with originally.

Toyota Camry, Highlander Brake Assist May Fail high

Reported Apr 3, 2018 · Campaign 18V211000 · Model years: 2018

Toyota is recalling certain Camry and Highlander vehicles because of a manufacturing problem with the brake booster vacuum pump assembly. An oil passage inside the pump's rotor may not have been machined correctly, and this could lead to a sudden loss of brake assist while driving. Losing brake assist suddenly can make it harder to stop the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash.

Toyota and Lexus vehicles carry incorrect load capacity labels low

Reported Dec 20, 2017 · Campaign 17V831000 · Model years: 2018

Toyota is recalling certain Toyota 4Runner, Tundra, Highlander, RAV4, Sienna and Tacoma vehicles, along with the Lexus GX460. These vehicles may have an incorrect label showing how much weight the vehicle is able to carry. Because of this, they do not meet the federal safety rule covering tire selection and rims. Toyota will contact owners and send them corrected labels at no cost.
