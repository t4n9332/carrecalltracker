Toyota Tundra, Sequoia Power Steering May Leak high

Reported Dec 23, 2021 · Campaign 21E103000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

Certain replacement power steering gear assemblies sold as service parts for Toyota Tundra and Sequoia vehicles may have been manufactured incorrectly. This manufacturing problem can result in an oil leak from the power steering gear assembly.

Toyota Tundra, Sequoia Power Steering Gear May Leak high

Reported Nov 23, 2021 · Campaign 21V920000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022

The power steering gear in these Toyota Tundra and Sequoia vehicles may have been manufactured incorrectly. This manufacturing problem can cause the steering gear to develop an oil leak. If enough oil leaks out, the power assist that normally makes the steering wheel easier to turn can suddenly stop working, which makes the vehicle much harder to steer while you are driving it.

Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps may fail, stalling engine high

Reported Nov 4, 2020 · Campaign 20V682000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Toyota is recalling a large number of Toyota and Lexus vehicles from the 2017 through 2020 model years, including Camry, Corolla, RAV4, Highlander, Tacoma, Tundra, Sienna and many Lexus models. The low-pressure fuel pump, which sits inside the fuel tank and sends gasoline to the engine, may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while you are driving. This recall expands an earlier recall covering the same fuel pump problem.

Toyota, Lexus fuel pump may fail, stall engine high

Reported Jan 13, 2020 · Campaign 20V012000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On many Toyota and Lexus models, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If the pump fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven, which affects the driver's ability to keep control of the vehicle at that moment.

Toyota load capacity label may become unreadable low

Reported Mar 27, 2019 · Campaign 19V244000 · Model years: 2019

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain 2019 Toyota vehicles because of a problem with the load capacity label. The printing on the label, which shows how much weight the vehicle can carry, may become impossible to read. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule covering tire selection and rims. Dealers will give owners a corrected load carrying capacity label to replace the one that cannot be read.

Toyota Avalon, Sequoia, Tundra: Airbags May Not Deploy high

Reported Oct 4, 2018 · Campaign 18V685000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On some Avalon, Avalon Hybrid, Sequoia, and Tundra vehicles, the air bag control computer may mistakenly detect a fault during the vehicle's start-up self-check. If that happens, the air bags may not deploy as intended in a crash.

Toyota Tundra, Sequoia Stability Control May Deactivate medium

Reported Feb 20, 2018 · Campaign 18V122000 · Model years: 2018

Toyota says electrical interference in a power supply circuit inside your vehicle can cause the electronic stability control system to be deactivated. This means the system could turn off without warning. As a result, the vehicle does not meet the federal safety standard that applies to electronic stability control systems.
