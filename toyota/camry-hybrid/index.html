Toyota Camry Hybrid recalls

12 recalls covering model years 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Toyota Camry Hybrid dash display failure disables hazard lights high

Reported 2026-08-06 · Campaign 26V511000 · Model years: 2025

On certain 2025-2026 Toyota Camry Hybrids, the instrument cluster — the display screen behind the steering wheel — can fail as you start the car. When it fails, the hazard lights, turn signals, seat belt warning, and smart key reminder all stop working. That means other drivers cannot see when you are signaling a turn or warning them of trouble. The vehicles do not meet several federal safety rules. Dealers will update the display software for free.

Toyota Camry Hybrid inverter bolt may cause power loss critical

Reported 2025-12-16 · Campaign 25V869000 · Model years: 2025

A bolt inside the inverter — the box that sends power from the hybrid battery to the electric motor — may not have been tightened enough on some 2025-2026 Camry Hybrids. With a loose bolt, the connection may not be solid. That can make the car suddenly lose the power that drives the wheels, and it can also cause an electrical short that starts a fire while the car is on. Toyota dealers will replace the whole inverter at no cost.

Toyota, Lexus and Subaru Solterra rearview cameras may freeze medium

Reported 2025-10-30 · Campaign 25V744000 · Model years: 2025

These vehicles have a Panoramic View Monitor system that shows what is behind and around you. A software error can make the backup camera image freeze or go completely blank when you shift into reverse. That means you may not see a child, a pet or an object behind the vehicle. Federal rules require a working rear view. A dealer will update the parking assist software at no cost; it is a software fix, not a part replacement.

Toyota and Lexus dashboard may not show speed or warnings medium

Reported 2025-09-11 · Campaign 25V595000 · Model years: 2025

When these Toyota and Lexus vehicles start up, a software error can leave the instrument panel, the display behind the steering wheel, missing important information. Your speed may not appear, and the warning lights for the brake system and for low tire pressure may not light up. That means you would not know how fast you are going, or that the brakes or tires need attention. Most vehicles get a free software update sent to the car wirelessly.

Toyota Camry Hybrid rear center seat belt may be damaged medium

Reported 2025-02-06 · Campaign 25V059000 · Model years: 2025

The seat belt in the middle of the second-row seat may have damaged webbing — the woven strap that holds you in place. Toyota says the belt does not meet the federal safety standard for seat belt assemblies. If that strap is damaged, it may not hold a passenger properly. A dealer will look at the belt and install a whole new seat belt assembly if it is damaged, at no cost. Toyota mailed owner letters on March 20, 2025.

Gulf States Toyota vehicles have wrong weight capacity labels low

Reported 2024-07-23 · Campaign 24V548000 · Model years: 2025

These Toyotas were sold with accessories added through Gulf States Toyota. When accessories are added, a sticker is supposed to be placed on the vehicle showing how much less weight it can now carry in people and cargo. On these vehicles that sticker shows the wrong numbers. If you go by it, you could load the vehicle heavier than it is rated for. Gulf States Toyota is mailing corrected labels to owners at no charge; nothing on the vehicle needs repair.

Toyota Camry second-row headrest brackets poorly welded medium

Reported 2024-02-21 · Campaign 24V128000 · Model years: 2023

The head restraints, meaning the padded headrests, on the middle and right-hand seats in the second row may sit in brackets that were not welded well enough at the factory. A weak bracket may not hold a passenger's head in place during a crash, which can lead to a worse head or neck injury for whoever is sitting there. It also falls short of a federal safety rule for head restraints. Toyota dealers will replace the larger rear seat back section at no charge.

Toyota and Lexus front passenger airbag may not inflate high

Reported 2023-12-20 · Campaign 23V865000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

These Toyota and Lexus vehicles have a sensor in the front passenger seat that tells the airbag system whether someone is sitting there. A short circuit can develop inside that sensor. If it does, the front passenger airbag may not inflate during a crash, so whoever is riding in that seat could be hurt worse than they otherwise would be. The dealer will inspect the sensor and replace it if needed, free of charge. Owner letters are being mailed in stages.

Toyota Camry lug nuts may loosen and wheel detach critical

Reported 2023-06-15 · Campaign 23V432000 · Model years: 2023

The nuts that hold the wheels on these Camry and Camry Hybrid cars may not be tight enough. A loose wheel can work its way off while you are driving, which can make you lose control of the car. Toyota says not to drive these vehicles at all until they are fixed. A dealer will check and tighten every lug nut, and replace any parts that were damaged, at no cost to you.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired.

Toyota Camry and RAV4 engine cracks can leak coolant critical

Reported 2020-02-06 · Campaign 20V064000 · Model years: 2020

The engine block on these 2.5-liter four-cylinder engines can have tiny holes left over from casting, and cracks can form there. Coolant — the liquid that keeps the engine from overheating — can then leak, either inside the engine or onto the ground. Low coolant can let the engine overheat, and a gas model can stall while you are driving. The engine can also be damaged badly enough to leak oil, which can start a fire. Dealers inspect and replace the engine if needed.

Toyota load capacity label may become unreadable low

Reported 2019-03-27 · Campaign 19V244000 · Model years: 2019

Every vehicle has a sticker, usually in the driver's door opening, that tells you how much weight it can safely carry, including passengers and cargo. On these vehicles the printing on that sticker can fade until you cannot read it. If you cannot see those numbers, you may load the vehicle heavier than it was built to handle, which can affect how it steers, brakes, and wears its tires. The dealer will give you a corrected replacement label.

Toyota Camry and Avalon Hybrid may leak fuel critical

Reported 2019-02-14 · Campaign 19V097000 · Model years: 2019

The fuel filler pipe is the tube that carries gasoline from the cap where you fill up down to the tank. On the affected hybrids, that pipe may not have been reconnected properly after an inspection at the factory. A loose pipe can let fuel leak out, and leaking gasoline near anything hot or sparking can start a fire. A dealer will inspect the pipe and replace it if needed, free of charge.
