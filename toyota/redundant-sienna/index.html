Toyota and Lexus hybrids may lose stability control medium

Reported 2022-04-13 · Campaign 22V239000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Your vehicle has a system that helps keep it from sliding or spinning out in a turn or on a slick road. You can switch that system off with a button. On these vehicles, a software error means the system may stay switched off the next time you start the car, instead of turning itself back on the way it is supposed to. A dealer will reload the correct software so it comes back on by itself.

Toyota Sienna second-row seat belts may tear high

Reported 2021-11-17 · Campaign 21V889000 · Model years: 2022

Your 2022 Sienna was built with seating for eight, and the second-row seat belts on the outside seats may have the wrong webbing guide — a small part the belt strap slides through. In a crash, the belt tightens fast, and the wrong guide can damage the strap and tear it. A torn belt cannot hold you the way it is supposed to. Dealers will replace both outboard second-row seat belt assemblies at no cost.
