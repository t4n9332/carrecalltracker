4 recalls covering model years 2020, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Toyota Corolla Steering Shaft May Crack And Detach high

Reported 2024-11-21 · Campaign 24V878000 · Model years: 2024

Some Toyota Corolla and Corolla Hybrid vehicles may have a steering intermediate shaft that develops cracks during the manufacturing process. Over time, the shaft may fracture and possibly detach from the steering column, so it no longer connects properly. Dealers will replace the intermediate steering shaft to fix this, at no cost to owners. Toyota has already mailed notification letters to owners about this issue.

Toyota Corolla Hybrid Brake Assist Software Error high

Reported 2024-09-20 · Campaign 24V708000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

A software error in the computer that controls your vehicle's skid control and braking system can cause a loss of power brake assist when you turn a corner. Power brake assist is the system that reduces the effort needed to press the brake pedal.

Toyota C-HR, Corolla Rear Seat Belts May Not Lock high

Reported 2019-12-11 · Campaign 19V877000 · Model years: 2020

On some C-HR, Corolla, and Corolla Hybrid vehicles, the rear seat belts use a locking mechanism that responds to how the belt webbing moves. In certain situations, this locking mechanism may not lock the belt as it's supposed to. If the vehicle is in a crash that involves more than one impact, the seat belt may not hold the occupant in place properly.

Toyota Prius, RAV4 Hybrid Brake Assist May Fail high

Reported 2019-07-24 · Campaign 19V544000 · Model years: 2020

The brake booster pump on these vehicles may fail. When it does, the Vehicle Stability Control system deactivates and the vehicle loses its power assist for braking. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard covering electronic stability control systems.
