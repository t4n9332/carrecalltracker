Toyota, Lexus and Subaru rearview camera may freeze medium

Reported Oct 30, 2025 · Campaign 25V744000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Toyota is recalling certain 2022 through 2026 Toyota and Lexus vehicles and the Subaru Solterra that have the Panoramic View Monitor camera system. Models include RAV4, Highlander, Prius, Sienna Hybrid, Camry Hybrid, Mirai, bZ4X and the Lexus NX and TX Hybrid. A software error can cause the backup camera picture to freeze or go blank when the car is in reverse, so these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard.

Toyota Instrument Panel May Fail To Show Warnings high

Reported Sep 11, 2025 · Campaign 25V595000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

A software error in the instrument panel can occur when the vehicle starts up. Because of this, the panel may fail to show the vehicle's speed, brake system warning, and tire pressure warning lights. This is happening on a range of Toyota models covered by this recall. The recall is being carried out in phases, with owner notification letters sent out in stages, and additional letters expected in the future.

Toyota accessory weight label may show wrong numbers low

Reported Jul 23, 2024 · Campaign 24V548000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain Toyota vehicles, mostly from the 2023 through 2025 model years, that were fitted with Gulf States Toyota accessories. Models include 4Runner, Corolla, Highlander, Prius, Tacoma, Tundra, Grand Highlander, Sequoia Hybrid, bZ4X, Venza Hybrid and others. The sticker that lists how much weight the added accessories take up may show wrong numbers, so these vehicles do not meet the federal standard for tire and rim selection.

Toyota and Lexus stability control may stay switched off high

Reported Apr 13, 2022 · Campaign 22V239000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Toyota is recalling certain Toyota and Lexus vehicles because of a problem with the software that controls the skid control computer. If the driver turns off the vehicle stability control system, the software may fail to turn it back on the next time the vehicle is started. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule for electronic stability control systems. Dealers will update the software so the system switches back on as it should.

Toyota Venza Hybrid Rear Turn Signals May Fail high

Reported Jan 26, 2022 · Campaign 22V033000 · Model years: 2021

On some Toyota Venza Hybrid vehicles, water may get into the rear turn signal LED lights. If water gets in, condensation can form on the circuit board inside the light, which may cause a short circuit. This issue involves the rear turn signal lights at the back of the vehicle. Because of this, these vehicles may not meet a federal safety standard covering vehicle lamps and lighting equipment.

Toyota Venza Hybrid recall questions

How many recalls does the Toyota Venza Hybrid have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2021–2024 Toyota Venza Hybrid. 3 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Toyota, Lexus and Subaru rearview camera may freeze" (NHTSA campaign 25V744000), reported Oct 30, 2025.

Which Toyota Venza Hybrid model year has the most recalls?

The 2023 Toyota Venza Hybrid has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2022 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Toyota Venza Hybrid?

No. None of the 5 Toyota Venza Hybrid recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Toyota Venza Hybrid has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Toyota dealer, including for second owners.
