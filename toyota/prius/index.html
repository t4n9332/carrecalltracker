Toyota Prius recalls

Toyota Prius Rear Door May Open While Driving high

Reported Jan 28, 2026 · Campaign 26V049000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

Toyota is recalling certain Prius, Prius Prime, and Prius Plug-in Hybrid vehicles because water may get into the rear door switch and cause a short circuit. This can cause an unlocked rear door to open unexpectedly while the vehicle is being driven.

Toyota, Lexus and Subaru rearview camera may freeze medium

Reported Oct 30, 2025 · Campaign 25V744000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

Toyota is recalling certain 2022 through 2026 Toyota and Lexus vehicles and the Subaru Solterra that have the Panoramic View Monitor camera system. Models include RAV4, Highlander, Prius, Sienna Hybrid, Camry Hybrid, Mirai, bZ4X and the Lexus NX and TX Hybrid. A software error can cause the backup camera picture to freeze or go blank when the car is in reverse, so these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard.

Toyota accessory weight label may show wrong numbers low

Reported Jul 23, 2024 · Campaign 24V548000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain Toyota vehicles, mostly from the 2023 through 2025 model years, that were fitted with Gulf States Toyota accessories. Models include 4Runner, Corolla, Highlander, Prius, Tacoma, Tundra, Grand Highlander, Sequoia Hybrid, bZ4X, Venza Hybrid and others. The sticker that lists how much weight the added accessories take up may show wrong numbers, so these vehicles do not meet the federal standard for tire and rim selection.

Toyota Prius Rear Doors May Open Unexpectedly high

Reported Apr 17, 2024 · Campaign 24V274000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Certain Toyota Prius and Prius Prime vehicles may let water get into the rear door switch, causing a short circuit. This can make an unlocked rear door open unexpectedly. Dealers will replace both the right and left rear door opener switches.

Toyota Prius Software Error May Cause Power Loss high

Reported Sep 22, 2021 · Campaign 21V734000 · Model years: 2022

In some Toyota Prius and Prius Prime vehicles, the computer that controls the engine has software that can mistakenly detect an error, even when nothing is actually wrong. When that happens, the computer can shut down the hybrid system. This causes the car to lose the power that normally drives the wheels, meaning the car can no longer move forward under its own power the way it normally would.

Toyota Prius Instrument Display May Go Blank medium

Reported Dec 11, 2019 · Campaign 19V876000 · Model years: 2019

Certain 2019 Toyota Prius vehicles may develop an electrical short in the combination meter, the panel behind the steering wheel that displays your speed, mileage, and fuel level. If a short occurs, the speedometer, odometer, and fuel gauge can all go blank at the same time, leaving the driver without that information while driving. Without a working display, the driver has no way to see current speed, mileage, or how much fuel is left in the tank.

Toyota Prius, RAV4 Hybrid Brake Assist May Fail high

Reported Jul 24, 2019 · Campaign 19V544000 · Model years: 2019

The brake booster pump on these vehicles may fail. When it does, the Vehicle Stability Control system deactivates and the vehicle loses its power assist for braking. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard covering electronic stability control systems.

Toyota load capacity label may become unreadable low

Reported Mar 27, 2019 · Campaign 19V244000 · Model years: 2019

Gulf States Toyota is recalling certain 2019 Toyota vehicles because of a problem with the load capacity label. The printing on the label, which shows how much weight the vehicle can carry, may become impossible to read. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rule covering tire selection and rims. Dealers will give owners a corrected load carrying capacity label to replace the one that cannot be read.

Toyota Prius Engine Wire Harness Can Wear and Short critical

Reported Sep 5, 2018 · Campaign 18V579000 · Model years: 2018

Toyota is recalling certain Prius vehicles because a section of the engine wire harness that connects to the hybrid Power Control Unit could rub against its cover. Over time, that contact can cause the wire to wear and create an electrical short circuit. Dealers will inspect the wire harness assembly and replace it if any damage is found, and will add a protective sleeve or tape to help prevent this from happening again.

Toyota Prius recall questions

How many recalls does the Toyota Prius have?

NHTSA lists 9 safety recalls covering the 2018–2025 Toyota Prius. 5 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Toyota Prius Rear Door May Open While Driving" (NHTSA campaign 26V049000), reported Jan 28, 2026.

Which Toyota Prius model year has the most recalls?

The 2023 Toyota Prius has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2018 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Toyota Prius?

No. None of the 9 Toyota Prius recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Toyota Prius has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Toyota dealer, including for second owners.
