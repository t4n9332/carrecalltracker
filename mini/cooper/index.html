MINI Cooper recalls

4 recalls covering model years 2019, 2020. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

BMW X1, X2 and MINI airbags may not deploy high

Reported 2020-05-20 · Campaign 20V283000 · Model years: 2020

A sensor inside the airbag control unit tells the vehicle when it has rolled over. On these vehicles that sensor may not work correctly. If the car rolls over in a crash, the head airbags, the seat belt tighteners and other safety equipment may not turn on, leaving people inside less protected in exactly the kind of crash where that protection matters most. Dealers replace the airbag control unit free of charge.

MINI Cooper hardtops may be missing rear crash padding medium

Reported 2019-08-28 · Campaign 19V634000 · Model years: 2020

These MINI Hardtops may have left the factory missing a crash pad behind the rear side trim panels. That pad is a block of padding built into the body that soaks up some of the force if another vehicle hits the side of your car. Without it, anyone sitting in the back seat is more likely to be hurt in a side-impact crash. The car drives normally and nothing will feel wrong. MINI dealers will install the missing pads for free.

BMW and MINI steering tie rod may break critical

Reported 2019-08-14 · Campaign 19V601000 · Model years: 2019

The tie rod is the bar that connects your steering to a front wheel. On these vehicles it may have been put together incorrectly at the factory. That mistake wears out the joint at the end of the rod faster than normal, and over time the rod can break. If it snaps while you are driving, that wheel stops following the steering wheel and you can lose control of the car. Dealers will inspect and replace the tie rods and ball joints as needed.

BMW and MINI crankshaft sensor fault can stall engine high

Reported 2018-07-12 · Campaign 18V465000 · Model years: 2019

A sensor that tracks the position of the engine's crankshaft was built with the wrong software inside it. Because of that, it may not read the engine correctly, and the engine can suddenly stall. A stall can happen while you are driving, leaving you without power in traffic. Dealers will replace the crankshaft sensor for free. BMW first announced this for one model and later expanded it to thousands more BMW and MINI vehicles.
