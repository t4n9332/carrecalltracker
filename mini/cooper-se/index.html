MINI Cooper SE High Voltage Battery Fire Risk critical

Reported 2024-08-15 · Campaign 24V612000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

On some MINI Hardtop 2 Door (Cooper SE) vehicles, a fault can occur in the high voltage battery or the high voltage electrical system, which can lead to a short circuit. A short circuit like this raises the risk of a fire.

MINI Cooper SE Battery Water Leak Fire Risk critical

Reported 2024-04-29 · Campaign 24V302000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

The seal on the high-voltage battery housing may not have been done properly, which can let water get into the battery. Water damage to the battery can cause a fire or make the vehicle stall. Dealers will inspect and leak-test the battery, and repair it as needed, free of charge.
