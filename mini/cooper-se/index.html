MINI Cooper SE High Voltage Battery Fire Risk critical

Reported Aug 15, 2024 · Campaign 24V612000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

On some MINI Hardtop 2 Door (Cooper SE) vehicles, a fault can occur in the high voltage battery or the high voltage electrical system, which can lead to a short circuit. A short circuit like this raises the risk of a fire.

MINI Cooper SE Battery Water Leak Fire Risk critical

Reported Apr 29, 2024 · Campaign 24V302000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

The seal on the high-voltage battery housing may not have been done properly, which can let water get into the battery. Water damage to the battery can cause a fire or make the vehicle stall. Dealers will inspect and leak-test the battery, and repair it as needed, free of charge.

MINI Cooper SE recall questions

How many recalls does the MINI Cooper SE have?

NHTSA lists 2 safety recalls covering the 2022–2023 MINI Cooper SE. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "MINI Cooper SE High Voltage Battery Fire Risk" (NHTSA campaign 24V612000), reported Aug 15, 2024.

Which MINI Cooper SE model year has the most recalls?

The 2022 MINI Cooper SE has the most, with 2 recalls. The 2022 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the MINI Cooper SE?

No. None of the 2 MINI Cooper SE recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my MINI Cooper SE has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised MINI dealer, including for second owners.
