Genesis GV60 recalls

9 recalls covering model years 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Genesis GV60 windshield trim can detach onto the road medium

Reported 2025-09-19 · Campaign 25V624000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

The strip of stainless-steel trim along the top edge of the windshield can come loose and fall off the car. The main danger here is not to you but to people behind you, because a piece of metal lying in the road can cause another driver to crash. Dealers will replace the windshield molding at no cost. Owner letters are expected to start going out October 30, 2025, but you can already look your car up.

Genesis G90 and GV60 dashboard display may go blank medium

Reported 2025-07-17 · Campaign 25V474000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

A software problem in these Genesis G90 and GV60 models can make the main dashboard display stop working. That is the screen that shows your speed and your warning lights. If it goes blank, you cannot see how fast you are going or whether the car is trying to warn you about something. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for controls and displays.

Hyundai and Genesis EVs can lose power from charging unit high

Reported 2024-11-18 · Campaign 24V868000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

A unit that keeps the small 12-volt battery topped up can be damaged and stop working. When that battery runs down, the car can lose drive power and stop moving, which could leave you stranded or cause a crash. Dealers will check the unit and its fuse, replace them if needed, and update the software, all free. If your vehicle was already fixed under the earlier 2024 recall, it still needs this new repair.

Genesis GV60 and GV80 backup camera may shut off medium

Reported 2024-08-07 · Campaign 24V584000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Hyundai is recalling certain 2024-2025 Genesis GV60 and 2025 GV80 vehicles. When you shift into reverse, the backup camera picture may shut off instead of showing on the screen. Without that picture, you cannot see what is directly behind the vehicle while you are backing up. Dealers will update the camera software at no cost, and owner notification letters were mailed September 11, 2024.

Hyundai IONIQ and Genesis EVs may lose drive power high

Reported 2024-03-15 · Campaign 24V204000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Your vehicle has a part called the Integrated Charging Control Unit, which keeps the small 12-volt battery charged while you drive. Hyundai found this unit can be damaged and stop charging that battery. If that happens, the car can lose power to the wheels while you are driving. A dealer will check the unit and its fuse, replace them if needed, and update the software. Hyundai later issued a newer fix, so even cars already repaired need to go back.

Hyundai IONIQ 5, IONIQ 6, Genesis GV60: driveshaft can break high

Reported 2024-02-01 · Campaign 24V065000 · Model years: 2023

The shaft that sends power to the rear wheels of these electric vehicles may not have been heat-treated correctly when it was made. A shaft made this way can break while the car is under load, and the car then loses drive power. Losing power in traffic, on a hill, or while merging onto a highway can lead to a crash. Hyundai mailed owner letters on March 29, 2024. A dealer will replace the rear inner driveshaft at no cost.

Genesis GV60 front passenger seat belt may not fasten high

Reported 2023-08-04 · Campaign 23V557000 · Model years: 2023

On some 2023 GV60s, the front passenger seat belt has a faulty locking part inside the belt reel. Because of it, the belt may not pull out far enough to reach the buckle, or it may not latch at all. If the belt does not work properly, it cannot hold the passenger in place during a crash. Dealers will inspect the front passenger belt and replace it at no cost if it is affected.

Genesis GV60 rear driveshafts may break, losing power high

Reported 2023-04-26 · Campaign 23V300000 · Model years: 2023

On these all-wheel-drive electric SUVs, the rear driveshafts — the spinning shafts that send power from the motor to the rear wheels — can crack and break apart. If one fails, the vehicle can lose the power that drives it forward, which could leave you slowing down or stopped without warning, including in traffic. A dealer will replace both the left and right rear driveshafts with new parts.

Genesis seat belt part may explode during a crash high

Reported 2023-02-16 · Campaign 23V094000 · Model years: 2023

Front seat belts have a small device that tightens the belt the instant a crash happens. In these vehicles that device can burst apart when it goes off, throwing metal pieces into the cabin that could hit and injure the driver or front passenger. The dealer will fit a cap over the part to hold it together, free of charge. GV60 owners got a warning letter first and will get a second letter when their repair is ready. Cars fixed under the earlier 2021 recall need this repair too.
