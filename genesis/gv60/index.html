Genesis GV60 recalls

9 recalls covering model years 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Genesis GV60 windshield trim may come off vehicle high

Reported 2025-09-19 · Campaign 25V624000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

Hyundai is recalling certain Genesis GV60 electric vehicles. The stainless-steel strip of trim that sits along the top edge of the windshield may come loose and separate from the vehicle. A piece of trim that comes off can land on the road, where it becomes a hazard for other vehicles behind or around the car. Dealers will replace the windshield molding, and owners will be notified by mail.

Genesis G90 and GV60 Display Screen May Fail high

Reported 2025-07-17 · Campaign 25V474000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

Certain Genesis G90 and GV60 vehicles have a software error that can cause the instrument panel display to fail. That display normally shows drivers important information, such as the speedometer and warning lights. If it fails, that information is no longer shown while driving.

Hyundai Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6, Genesis GV60 Charging Unit Failure high

Reported 2024-11-18 · Campaign 24V868000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

Certain Hyundai Ioniq 5, Ioniq 6, and Genesis GV60 vehicles have an Integrated Charging Control Unit that may become damaged. When this happens, the unit can stop charging the 12-volt battery. If the 12-volt battery is not being charged, the vehicle can lose drive power.

Genesis GV60, GV80 Backup Camera May Go Dark high

Reported 2024-08-07 · Campaign 24V584000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

In certain Genesis GV60 and GV80 vehicles, the rearview camera image may shut off while the vehicle is being driven in reverse. This means that the picture the camera normally shows on the display, which helps the driver see what is behind the vehicle, may disappear while the driver is backing up, leaving the driver without that view of what is behind the vehicle.

Hyundai and Genesis EV Charging Unit Can Fail high

Reported 2024-03-15 · Campaign 24V204000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

On these Hyundai and Genesis electric vehicles, the Integrated Charging Control Unit may become damaged and stop charging the 12-volt battery. This can result in a loss of drive power.

Hyundai IONIQ 5, IONIQ 6, Genesis GV60 Driveshaft May Break high

Reported 2024-02-01 · Campaign 24V065000 · Model years: 2023

Certain 2023 Hyundai IONIQ 5, IONIQ 6, and Genesis GV60 vehicles have a rear inner driveshaft that may not have been heat-treated correctly during manufacturing. Because of this, the driveshaft can break when it is put under load, such as while accelerating. If the driveshaft breaks, the vehicle loses the power that turns the wheels, meaning it can no longer drive forward under its own power.

Genesis GV60 Passenger Seat Belt May Not Fasten high

Reported 2023-08-04 · Campaign 23V557000 · Model years: 2023

Certain 2023 Genesis GV60 vehicles have a problem with the automatic locking retractor for the front passenger-side seat belt. This retractor may prohibit the seat belt from fastening properly and may also prevent it from extending properly. Dealers will inspect the front passenger-side seat belt and replace it as necessary to correct the problem.

Genesis GV60 Rear Driveshafts May Fracture and Fail high

Reported 2023-04-26 · Campaign 23V300000 · Model years: 2023

Some all-wheel drive Genesis GV60 vehicles may have rear driveshafts that can fracture. If a driveshaft fractures, the vehicle can lose drive power.

Genesis GV80, G80, GV70, GV60 seat belt part may explode high

Reported 2023-02-16 · Campaign 23V094000 · Model years: 2023

Hyundai is recalling certain Genesis GV80, G80, GV70 and GV60 vehicles. In a crash, the part that tightens the front seat belt can explode when it goes off. This can affect the driver side belt, the passenger side belt, or both. Dealers will secure these parts with a cap. This recall replaces and expands an earlier recall, so vehicles that were already repaired under that earlier one need the new repair as well.
