Genesis Fuel Pipe Leak May Increase Fire Risk critical

Reported Apr 10, 2026 · Campaign 26V229000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

Fuel may leak from the connection point where the fuel pipe meets the fuel rail in these Genesis vehicles. A fuel leak in this location increases the risk of a fire. Genesis dealers will fix the problem by inspecting the connection and tightening it as needed, or by replacing the fuel pipe entirely if that turns out to be necessary for the vehicle.

Genesis G80 and GV80 Instrument Display May Fail high

Reported Jan 16, 2026 · Campaign 26V019000 · Model years: 2025

A software error can cause the instrument panel display in front of the driver to fail. This display normally shows important information such as the speedometer and fuel gauge. If it stops working because of this software error, the driver may lose access to that information while driving.

Genesis Instrument Panel Display May Fail medium

Reported Feb 20, 2025 · Campaign 25V105000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

In certain Genesis G80, GV70, and GV80 vehicles, a software error can cause the instrument panel display to fail. This is the screen that normally shows things like the speedometer and warning lights, so a driver may not be able to see this information if it stops working. Dealers will inspect the instrument panel and update its software as needed to fix the problem, free of charge.

Genesis Fuel Pump Failure Can Cut Engine Power high

Reported Apr 19, 2024 · Campaign 24V282000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

The fuel pump that supplies fuel to the engine may fail. If it fails, the vehicle can lose the power that keeps it moving, even while you are driving. Losing drive power like this means the engine may stop providing the force needed to keep the vehicle moving forward on the road, which can happen at any time while driving these vehicles.

Genesis Turbo Oil Pipe May Leak, Risking Fire critical

Reported Mar 8, 2024 · Campaign 24V191000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

Hyundai is recalling certain Genesis G70, G80, and G90 vehicles with a 3.3-liter turbocharged V6 engine. The left turbocharger oil feed pipe may deteriorate and leak oil. An oil leak near hot engine parts can increase the risk of a fire. Dealers will replace the left turbocharger oil feed pipe. This recall replaces an earlier recall, so vehicles already repaired under that recall will need the new repair completed.

Genesis G70, G80, G90 Starter Fire Risk critical

Reported Feb 13, 2024 · Campaign 24V107000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Water can get into a part of the starter system and cause an electrical short. This short can start a fire in the engine compartment, whether the vehicle is parked or being driven. Hyundai will install a new relay kit in the engine's electrical box to fix the problem.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Genesis Fuel Pump Failure May Cut Power high

Reported Sep 11, 2023 · Campaign 23V630000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

On certain Genesis G80, GV70, and GV80 vehicles, the fuel pump may fail. If it does, the vehicle can lose drive power while being driven.

Genesis GV80, G80, GV70, GV60 seat belt part may explode high

Reported Feb 16, 2023 · Campaign 23V094000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

Hyundai is recalling certain Genesis GV80, G80, GV70 and GV60 vehicles. In a crash, the part that tightens the front seat belt can explode when it goes off. This can affect the driver side belt, the passenger side belt, or both. Dealers will secure these parts with a cap. This recall replaces and expands an earlier recall, so vehicles that were already repaired under that earlier one need the new repair as well.

Genesis G80, GV80 Fuel Tube May Leak critical

Reported Mar 24, 2021 · Campaign 21V208000 · Model years: 2021

Some Genesis G80 and GV80 vehicles equipped with certain turbo engines have a fuel tube that connects the high pressure fuel pump to the fuel rail. This tube may not seal properly. Because the seal may fail, fuel can leak out from where the tube connects to the fuel system.

Genesis G80 Brake Module May Catch Fire critical

Reported Mar 10, 2021 · Campaign 21V160000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

The anti-lock brake module can malfunction and short-circuit. If that happens, it can start a fire in the engine area, whether the car is parked or being driven.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Genesis G80/G90 Turbo Oil Leak Fire Risk critical

Reported Jul 19, 2019 · Campaign 19V538000 · Model years: 2018

Hyundai is recalling certain Genesis G80 and G90 vehicles equipped with 3.3-liter turbocharged engines. These vehicles have an oil supply pipe that feeds the turbocharger. On affected vehicles, the seal on this oil supply pipe may be loose rather than properly sealed. Because the seal is loose, oil from this pipe can leak out and land on the engine's exhaust manifold, rather than staying contained within the turbocharger's oil supply system.

Genesis G80 Airbag Sensor May Misread Passenger Seat high

Reported Jul 27, 2018 · Campaign 18V496000 · Model years: 2018

The passenger airbag system may wrongly detect that an adult is sitting in the front passenger seat, even when no one is there. Because of this misreading, if a child or infant is actually riding in that seat during a crash, the airbag may inflate instead of staying switched off, which is what should happen when a small child is in that seat. The system is not correctly recognizing who, or what, is actually in the seat.

Genesis G80, G90 Windshield May Detach high

Reported May 10, 2018 · Campaign 18V305000 · Model years: 2018

The windshield and rear window on these vehicles may have been installed with the wrong type of primer. This can weaken the bond that holds the glass to the vehicle frame. Because the incorrect primer was used, the strength of that bond may decrease over time, meaning the glass is not held in place as securely as it should be as the vehicle continues to be driven and used.

Genesis G80 recall questions

How many recalls does the Genesis G80 have?

NHTSA lists 13 safety recalls covering the 2018–2025 Genesis G80. 12 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Genesis Fuel Pipe Leak May Increase Fire Risk" (NHTSA campaign 26V229000), reported Apr 10, 2026.

Which Genesis G80 model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Genesis G80 has the most, with 6 recalls. The 2020 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Genesis G80?

Yes. 2 Genesis G80 recalls carry a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Genesis G70, G80, G90 Starter Fire Risk (24V107000); Genesis G80 Brake Module May Catch Fire (21V160000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Genesis G80 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Genesis dealer, including for second owners.
