Hyundai, Genesis Fuel Pump May Cause Power Loss high

Reported Jul 12, 2024 · Campaign 24V528000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

Certain Hyundai and Genesis vehicles, including the Veloster N, Genesis G70, Elantra N, and Kona N, may have a fuel pump that fails. If the fuel pump fails, the vehicle can lose engine power while being driven. Some owners were sent an early letter warning them about this safety risk before the full notice with the repair information went out.

Genesis Turbo Oil Pipe May Leak, Risking Fire critical

Reported Mar 8, 2024 · Campaign 24V191000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

Hyundai is recalling certain Genesis G70, G80, and G90 vehicles with a 3.3-liter turbocharged V6 engine. The left turbocharger oil feed pipe may deteriorate and leak oil. An oil leak near hot engine parts can increase the risk of a fire. Dealers will replace the left turbocharger oil feed pipe. This recall replaces an earlier recall, so vehicles already repaired under that recall will need the new repair completed.

Genesis G70, G80, G90 Starter Fire Risk critical

Reported Feb 13, 2024 · Campaign 24V107000 · Model years: 2019

Water can get into a part of the starter system and cause an electrical short. This short can start a fire in the engine compartment, whether the vehicle is parked or being driven. Hyundai will install a new relay kit in the engine's electrical box to fix the problem.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Hyundai Sonata and Genesis G70 trunk latch can fail medium

Reported Mar 28, 2022 · Campaign 22V196000 · Model years: 2019

The trunk latch on these cars can become damaged. When that happens, the trunk cannot be opened from the inside. Federal safety rules require a trunk release that works from within the trunk compartment, and these vehicles do not meet that requirement. Hyundai is recalling certain 2020 Sonata and Sonata Hybrid cars and 2019 Genesis G70 cars. Dealers will inspect the trunk latch and replace the latch base if it is needed, at no cost to the owner.

Genesis G70 Brake Module May Catch Fire critical

Reported Mar 10, 2021 · Campaign 21V161000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

The anti-lock brake module can malfunction and short-circuit. If that happens, it can start a fire in the engine area, whether the car is parked or being driven.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Genesis G70 fuel pump defect may stall engine high

Reported Sep 21, 2020 · Campaign 20V569000 · Model years: 2021

Hyundai Motor America is recalling certain 2021 Genesis G70 vehicles with 2.0L GDI engines. A plastic burr left over from manufacturing may block the fuel pump's jet nozzle, which can leave the engine without enough fuel.

Genesis G70 recall questions

How many recalls does the Genesis G70 have?

NHTSA lists 6 safety recalls covering the 2019–2023 Genesis G70. 5 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Hyundai, Genesis Fuel Pump May Cause Power Loss" (NHTSA campaign 24V528000), reported Jul 12, 2024.

Which Genesis G70 model year has the most recalls?

The 2019 Genesis G70 has the most, with 5 recalls. The 2023 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Genesis G70?

Yes. 2 Genesis G70 recalls carry a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Genesis G70, G80, G90 Starter Fire Risk (24V107000); Genesis G70 Brake Module May Catch Fire (21V161000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Genesis G70 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Genesis dealer, including for second owners.
