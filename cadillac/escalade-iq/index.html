Cadillac Escalade IQ, Hummer EV Battery Fire Risk critical

Reported Jul 30, 2026 · Campaign 26V494000 · Model years: 2026

Certain Cadillac Escalade IQ, Escalade IQL and GMC Hummer EV Pickup vehicles may have a problem with the high voltage battery. An internal component inside the battery may not be properly secured in place. If this component becomes loose, it can move around inside the battery and cause damage to it. Damage to the high voltage battery in this way increases the risk of a fire.

GM vehicles may be missing the electronic owner's manual low

Reported Feb 26, 2026 · Campaign 26V114000 · Model years: 2026

General Motors is recalling a wide range of Cadillac, Chevrolet, GMC and Buick vehicles, including the Escalade IQ and IQL, OPTIQ, Colorado, Equinox EV, Silverado 3500, Canyon and Sierra 3500. During production, the radio may not have been set to the correct status to download the electronic owner's manual, so the manual is not there for the owner to use. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard covering occupant protection.
