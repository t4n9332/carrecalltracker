Cadillac CT4 recalls

5 recalls covering model years 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Cadillac and Camaro Transmission May Lock Wheels high

Reported Mar 6, 2025 · Campaign 25V148000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

The 10-speed transmission in these vehicles may become damaged internally, and that damage can cause the wheels to lock up while driving. A wheel locking up while driving increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will install new monitoring software in the transmission's computer to address the problem, free of charge.

GM Vehicles Daytime Running Lights Stay On medium

Reported Dec 8, 2022 · Campaign 22V903000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

In certain Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC vehicles, the daytime running lights may not turn off when the headlights are switched on. This can create glare and reduce visibility for other drivers.

Cadillac CT4 and CT5 side airbags may deploy incorrectly high

Reported Aug 5, 2021 · Campaign 21V611000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

The side-curtain air bags mounted along the roof rail, on both the left and right sides, may not have been installed correctly. If the vehicle is in a crash, this could prevent an air bag from deploying the way it's supposed to. An air bag that doesn't deploy correctly may not protect the driver and passengers as intended during a crash.

GM vehicles' airbag warning light works inconsistently medium

Reported Jun 10, 2021 · Campaign 21V421000 · Model years: 2021

General Motors is recalling certain Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet and GMC vehicles. The module that passes messages between vehicle systems does not correctly handle a loss of communication with the airbag sensing module. As a result, the airbag warning light on the dashboard turns on inconsistently. A dealer can update that module's software, or the update can be sent to the vehicle over the air, free of charge. GM mailed owner notification letters on July 1, 2021.

Chevrolet, Cadillac, Buick Models Lose Brake Assist high

Reported Sep 24, 2020 · Campaign 20V588000 · Model years: 2020

Contamination at a sensor connection in the electronic brake boost system may interrupt communication between the sensor and the brake booster. This affects certain Chevrolet Corvette and Trailblazer, Cadillac CT4, CT5 and XT4, and Buick Encore GX vehicles. If that happens, the power assist that helps you brake could be lost, meaning you would need much more pedal force to slow or stop the vehicle. Dealers will replace the electronic brake boost module, free of charge.

Cadillac CT4 recall questions

How many recalls does the Cadillac CT4 have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2020–2023 Cadillac CT4. 3 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Cadillac and Camaro Transmission May Lock Wheels" (NHTSA campaign 25V148000), reported Mar 6, 2025.

Which Cadillac CT4 model year has the most recalls?

The 2020 Cadillac CT4 has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2023 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Cadillac CT4?

No. None of the 5 Cadillac CT4 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Cadillac CT4 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Cadillac dealer, including for second owners.
