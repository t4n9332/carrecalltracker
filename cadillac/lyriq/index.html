Cadillac Lyriq recalls

11 recalls covering model years 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Cadillac Lyriq Rearview Camera Screen May Go Gray high

Reported Feb 12, 2026 · Campaign 26V082000 · Model years: 2024

On some Cadillac Lyriq vehicles, the rearview camera screen may turn gray and stop showing any picture from the camera. Without that camera image, the driver loses the extra view behind the vehicle that the system is supposed to provide.

Cadillac Lyriq Battery Bolts May Be Loose critical

Reported Jul 24, 2025 · Campaign 25V483000 · Model years: 2025

Some Cadillac Lyriq and Vistiq vehicles may have loose or missing bolts holding the high-voltage battery to the floor of the vehicle.

Cadillac Lyriq Driver Display Can Go Blank high

Reported May 29, 2025 · Campaign 25V356000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

The driver's video display may suddenly go blank while you're driving. That screen shows things like the speedometer, warning lights, and the rearview camera image, so losing it means losing that information while on the road. Dealers will update the display's software, or it can be updated automatically over the air, free of charge.

Cadillac Lyriq Front Stabilizer Bar Bolts May Loosen high

Reported Apr 10, 2025 · Campaign 25V232000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

General Motors is recalling certain Cadillac Lyriq vehicles. The bolts on the bracket that holds the left or right front stabilizer bar may not have been tightened properly. Over time, these bolts can loosen and allow the bracket to separate from the vehicle's frame. If that happens, the loose stabilizer bar or bracket can damage nearby high-voltage cables or the coolant lines for the EV battery.

Cadillac Lyriq Owner's Manual Misdescribes Head Restraints medium

Reported Mar 27, 2025 · Campaign 25V193000 · Model years: 2025

General Motors is recalling certain 2025 Cadillac LYRIQ vehicles because the owner's manual inaccurately describes the rear outboard head restraints. If an owner relies on this incorrect description, the head restraints may end up improperly adjusted, which increases the risk of injury in a crash.

Cadillac Lyriq Brakes May Release Pressure Unexpectedly high

Reported Aug 8, 2024 · Campaign 24V589000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Some Cadillac Lyriq all-wheel-drive vehicles have an anti-lock brake system that can turn on by itself and let out brake pressure in the vehicle's brake system.

GM Electric Vehicles May Lose Drive Power high

Reported May 9, 2024 · Campaign 24V320000 · Model years: 2024

In some 2024 Cadillac Lyriq, Chevrolet Blazer EV, Chevrolet Silverado EV, GMC Hummer EV, and GMC Sierra EV vehicles, the electric motors in the rear drive unit may have wires that aren't insulated well enough. If those wires touch each other, the vehicle can lose the power that drives it.

Cadillac Lyriq Rear Seat Belt Anchor May Fail high

Reported Nov 22, 2023 · Campaign 23V785000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

In some Cadillac LYRIQ vehicles, the bracket that holds the left-rear and center-rear seat belts to the seat frame may not have been welded correctly. Because of this, that bracket may not hold the seat belts as firmly as it should. A seat belt that isn't anchored securely may not keep a passenger properly restrained. This affects anyone riding in the left-rear or center-rear seating position.

Cadillac Lyriq Recalled for Missing Pedestrian Warning Sounds medium

Reported Oct 12, 2023 · Campaign 23V682000 · Model years: 2023

The body control module on some vehicles may have been updated with incorrectly calibrated software, causing it to fail to make the sounds meant to warn pedestrians that the vehicle is approaching.

Cadillac Lyriq Battery Pack Loss Of Power high

Reported May 25, 2023 · Campaign 23V367000 · Model years: 2023

Certain Cadillac Lyriq vehicles may have connections inside the high-voltage battery pack that are out of position or were not welded correctly. This problem can cause the vehicle to suddenly lose drive power while you are driving, meaning the car may stop being able to move forward or accelerate as expected. Losing power unexpectedly on the road can make it difficult to keep the vehicle moving safely, especially in traffic or at higher speeds.

Cadillac Lyriq Instrument Panel May Go Blank medium

Reported Sep 22, 2022 · Campaign 22V710000 · Model years: 2023

General Motors says a software error in the driver video display control module on some Cadillac Lyriq vehicles may cause the instrument panel to go blank. When this happens, the panel would not show information like your speed and warning lights. GM is recalling the affected vehicles to fix this software error in the display control module.

Cadillac Lyriq recall questions

How many recalls does the Cadillac Lyriq have?

NHTSA lists 11 safety recalls covering the 2023–2025 Cadillac Lyriq. 8 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Cadillac Lyriq Rearview Camera Screen May Go Gray" (NHTSA campaign 26V082000), reported Feb 12, 2026.

Which Cadillac Lyriq model year has the most recalls?

The 2023 Cadillac Lyriq has the most, with 7 recalls. The 2025 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 3. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Cadillac Lyriq?

No. None of the 11 Cadillac Lyriq recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Cadillac Lyriq has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Cadillac dealer, including for second owners.
