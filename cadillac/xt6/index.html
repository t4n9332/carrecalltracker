Cadillac XT6 recalls

12 recalls covering model years 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Cadillac XT5 and XT6 dashboard reminder lights may not work medium

Reported 2024-06-20 · Campaign 24V459000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

General Motors is recalling certain Cadillac XT5 and XT6 vehicles. On these cars, the instrument panel may not light up when the hazard warning lights, the high beams, or the turn signals are switched on, so the usual reminder on the dash is missing. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet two federal safety rules, one covering controls and displays and one covering lamps and reflectors. Dealers will update the instrument panel software.

Cadillac XT5, XT6 and GMC Acadia windshield problem high

Reported 2023-10-12 · Campaign 23V681000 · Model years: 2024

This recall affects certain GMC Acadia and Cadillac XT5 and XT6 vehicles. Some windshields may have been installed without the correct sealant needed to bond the glass to the vehicle's body. Without that seal, the windshield may not be properly attached, and it could come loose from the vehicle.

Cadillac, Chevy, GMC SUVs Transmission Half-Shaft Failure high

Reported 2023-03-16 · Campaign 23V172000 · Model years: 2023

Certain Cadillac XT5, XT6, Chevrolet Blazer, Traverse and GMC Acadia SUVs may have been built with an incorrect transmission part called a sun gear. This can cause the half-shaft on the driver's side to come loose from the transmission.

Cadillac and GMC label with unreadable tire size information medium

Reported 2022-09-22 · Campaign 22V708000 · Model years: 2023

General Motors is recalling certain 2023 Cadillac XT5 and XT6 and GMC Acadia vehicles. The certification label on the driver's side B-pillar, the post between the front and rear doors, may have tire size information on it that cannot be read. Because of that, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule that covers how tires and rims are chosen for a vehicle. Dealers will put on a new certification label.

Cadillac XT5, XT6, GMC Acadia Camera May Fail high

Reported 2022-09-22 · Campaign 22V709000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On vehicles with the optional Surround Vision feature, the coaxial cable connectors for the rearview camera may have been crimped incorrectly. This can cause the rearview camera to stop working or work only some of the time.

Cadillac XT5/XT6 and GMC Acadia Fuel Tank Valve Leak critical

Reported 2022-06-23 · Campaign 22V446000 · Model years: 2022

General Motors is recalling certain Cadillac XT5, XT6, and GMC Acadia All-Wheel Drive vehicles. A valve inside the fuel tank, meant to seal the tank shut if the vehicle rolls over, may not seal properly. If that valve fails to seal during a rollover crash, fuel can leak out of the tank. A fuel leak like this raises the chance of a fire starting after the crash.

Cadillac XT5, XT6, GMC Acadia Rear Suspension Link May Separate critical

Reported 2022-06-16 · Campaign 22V427000 · Model years: 2022

On some of these vehicles, a part called the toe link connects to the left-rear suspension and helps keep the rear wheel aligned. That toe link may not have been tightened properly when the vehicle was built. If it stays loose, the toe link could become loose or come apart from the suspension instead of staying securely attached the way it should be.

Cadillac XT6 brake fluid cap missing warning label medium

Reported 2022-06-02 · Campaign 22V392000 · Model years: 2022

On certain 2022 Cadillac XT6 vehicles, the cap on the brake fluid reservoir may be missing its warning label. Because the label is missing, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for light vehicle brake systems. Dealers will look at the brake fluid reservoir cap and replace it if needed. General Motors mailed notification letters to owners at the end of June 2022.

Cadillac and GMC Fuel Lines May Leak high

Reported 2021-06-03 · Campaign 21V422000 · Model years: 2021

On these vehicles, the fuel supply lines may not have been properly attached, and they may not be fully seated where they connect. Because the lines are not secured the way they should be, they can gradually work themselves loose or come apart entirely. When a fuel line separates like this, gasoline is able to leak out of the fuel system. A leak of this kind raises the chance that a fire could start.

GM transmission bolts missing, leak and fire risk critical

Reported 2020-10-29 · Campaign 20V668000 · Model years: 2020

On certain GM vehicles, the endcap on the start/stop transmission accumulator may be missing bolts. This can let transmission fluid leak out. Over time, the leak can get bad enough that the vehicle loses the ability to move forward. If the leaking fluid comes into contact with something that can ignite it, it could catch fire.

GM SUVs' Fuel Pump Blockage Can Stall Engine high

Reported 2020-10-15 · Campaign 20V639000 · Model years: 2020

In some of these vehicles, a leftover piece of plastic from manufacturing may be stuck in the fuel pump's nozzle. This can block the nozzle and keep enough fuel from reaching the engine. When that happens, the engine may not get the fuel it needs to keep running. GM will have dealers replace the fuel pump module to fix the problem, at no cost to owners.

GM SUVs, Trucks: Roof-Rail Air Bag May Underperform high

Reported 2020-07-30 · Campaign 20V446000 · Model years: 2020

On affected vehicles, a part called the diffuser, inside the roof-rail air bag inflator, may not have been properly attached to the inflator. This means the diffuser could come apart from the inflator when the air bag deploys.
