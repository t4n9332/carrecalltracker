Cadillac and Camaro Transmission May Lock Wheels high

Reported Mar 6, 2025 · Campaign 25V148000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

The 10-speed transmission in these vehicles may become damaged internally, and that damage can cause the wheels to lock up while driving. A wheel locking up while driving increases the risk of a crash. Dealers will install new monitoring software in the transmission's computer to address the problem, free of charge.

Chevy, Cadillac, GMC vehicles may lose stability control high

Reported Dec 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V889000 · Model years: 2019

This recall affects certain 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 1500, Cadillac CT6, and GMC Sierra 1500 vehicles. A software error in a brake computer may cause the vehicle's stability control and antilock brake systems to shut off. If this happens, the warning lights on the dashboard that are supposed to alert the driver won't turn on, so the driver may not know these safety systems have stopped working.

Cadillac CT6 Tire Sensors Use Wrong Frequency medium

Reported Oct 15, 2019 · Campaign 19V731000 · Model years: 2020

On vehicles fitted with certain dealer-installed 20-inch wheels, the tire pressure monitoring sensors use the wrong wireless frequency. Because of this, the system cannot turn on the low tire pressure warning light when tire pressure drops below the recommended level.

Cadillac CT6 Turn Signals May Not Cancel Automatically medium

Reported Feb 21, 2019 · Campaign 19V117000 · Model years: 2019

On some Cadillac CT6 vehicles, turning the steering wheel will not automatically cancel the turn signal after a turn. The driver may not notice that the signal is still flashing and may not turn it off manually.

Cadillac, Chevrolet Rear Seat Belt May Not Lock medium

Reported Sep 26, 2018 · Campaign 18V673000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

General Motors is recalling certain Cadillac CT6, Escalade ESV, Chevrolet Suburban, Volt, and GMC Yukon XL vehicles. On some of these vehicles, the retractor for a second-row or third-row seat belt may not automatically lock when the belt is pulled all the way out. Because this locking feature is what holds a child seat in place, a child seat installed using that belt may not be properly secured.

Cadillac CT6 Child Seat Anchor Bars May Be Blocked medium

Reported Jun 28, 2018 · Campaign 18V437000 · Model years: 2018

Some 2016-2018 Cadillac CT6 vehicles were built with extra adhesive on the rear inboard child seat anchor bars. This buildup can get in the way when someone tries to attach a child safety seat to those bars, making correct installation difficult. If the child seat cannot be attached the right way to the anchor bar, it may not hold the seat securely.
