Cadillac XT4 recalls

8 recalls covering model years 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Cadillac XT4 dashboard cover may block passenger airbag high

Reported Oct 24, 2024 · Campaign 24V795000 · Model years: 2025

The cover on the instrument panel may be missing a perforation on its underside that is needed for the passenger-side airbag to deploy properly.

Cadillac XT4 and Malibu Wrong Owner's Manual low

Reported Aug 1, 2024 · Campaign 24V575000 · Model years: 2024

General Motors is recalling certain 2024 Cadillac XT4 and Chevrolet Malibu vehicles that may have the wrong owner's manual in the glove box. Because the manual doesn't match the vehicle, it can contain inaccurate information about how the vehicle's functions work and other safety-related details. Having incorrect information available to the driver could lead to the vehicle being operated the wrong way.

Cadillac XT4 rearview camera image may go blank medium

Reported Nov 23, 2022 · Campaign 22V868000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

General Motors is recalling certain Cadillac XT4 SUVs that have the optional Surround Vision feature. The video processing module may lose its connection with the rearview camera, and the camera image is then lost. Dealers will update the module's software, or replace the module if needed.

GM Vehicles: Driver's Seat Weld May Fail high

Reported May 19, 2022 · Campaign 22V359000 · Model years: 2022

General Motors is recalling certain 2021 Chevrolet Malibu and 2022 Chevrolet Equinox, Blazer, and Cadillac XT4 vehicles. The driver's seat cushion frame may have a weld that was not done correctly. This faulty weld is located in the power tilt-adjustment mechanism, which is the part that lets the driver's seat tilt up and down. The improper weld affects the frame that supports the driver's seat cushion.

Chevrolet Malibu and Cadillac XT4 Loose Seat Belt high

Reported Aug 20, 2021 · Campaign 21V649000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

On these Chevrolet Malibu and Cadillac XT4 vehicles, the rear seat belt retractors may be improperly secured with loose or missing fasteners.

GM Vehicles Recalled for Over-Cured Tire Failure Risk critical

Reported Feb 25, 2021 · Campaign 21V115000 · Model years: 2020

The tires on these vehicles were left in the curing process for too long during manufacturing. Tires that are cured too long can develop a break in the sidewall, which can cause sudden air loss, or the tire's belt edge can separate, causing the tread or belt to come apart. Either problem can cause the driver to lose control of the vehicle.

GM transmission bolts missing, leak and fire risk critical

Reported Oct 29, 2020 · Campaign 20V668000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On certain GM vehicles, the endcap on the start/stop transmission accumulator may be missing bolts. This can let transmission fluid leak out. Over time, the leak can get bad enough that the vehicle loses the ability to move forward. If the leaking fluid comes into contact with something that can ignite it, it could catch fire.

Chevrolet, Cadillac, Buick Models Lose Brake Assist high

Reported Sep 24, 2020 · Campaign 20V588000 · Model years: 2020

Contamination at a sensor connection in the electronic brake boost system may interrupt communication between the sensor and the brake booster. This affects certain Chevrolet Corvette and Trailblazer, Cadillac CT4, CT5 and XT4, and Buick Encore GX vehicles. If that happens, the power assist that helps you brake could be lost, meaning you would need much more pedal force to slow or stop the vehicle. Dealers will replace the electronic brake boost module, free of charge.

Cadillac XT4 recall questions

How many recalls does the Cadillac XT4 have?

NHTSA lists 8 safety recalls covering the 2019–2025 Cadillac XT4. 6 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Cadillac XT4 dashboard cover may block passenger airbag" (NHTSA campaign 24V795000), reported Oct 24, 2024.

Which Cadillac XT4 model year has the most recalls?

The 2020 Cadillac XT4 has the most, with 5 recalls. The 2021 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Cadillac XT4?

No. None of the 8 Cadillac XT4 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Cadillac XT4 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Cadillac dealer, including for second owners.
