Rivian EDV driver's seat belt tensioner cable may be damaged high

Reported 2025-11-25 · Campaign 25V816000 · Model years: 2022

On some 2022-2025 Rivian EDV delivery vans, the cable for the driver's seat belt tensioner can become damaged. The tensioner is the part that pulls the belt snug against you the instant a crash begins. If its cable is damaged, the belt may not hold the driver as firmly as it should in a crash. Rivian has already sent out an over-the-air software update, and dealers will inspect the driver's belt tensioner and replace it if needed.

Rivian trucks and SUVs may have loose steering bolt critical

Reported 2022-10-06 · Campaign 22V744000 · Model years: 2022

A bolt that joins the front suspension arm to the part the wheel steers on may not have been tightened enough when the vehicle was built. If that bolt works its way loose and comes apart, the connection holding the front wheel in place can separate. You could suddenly lose the ability to steer the vehicle. Rivian dealers and service technicians will inspect the bolt and tighten it if needed, and the check costs you nothing.
