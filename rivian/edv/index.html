Rivian EDV Seat Belt Pretensioner Cable May Fail high

Reported Nov 25, 2025 · Campaign 25V816000 · Model years: 2022

On some Rivian EDV vehicles, the cable that is part of the driver's seat belt pretensioner may become damaged. This pretensioner cable is a component of the seat belt system on the driver's side of the vehicle. If the cable becomes damaged, it may not function as intended, affecting how the seat belt pretensioner operates on the driver's seat belt in these vehicles.

Rivian R1T, R1S, EDV Loose Steering Fastener high

Reported Oct 6, 2022 · Campaign 22V744000 · Model years: 2022

The fastener that connects the front upper control arm to the steering knuckle may not have been tightened properly when the vehicle was built. If this fastener is loose, the connection between the control arm and the steering knuckle could come apart while the vehicle is being driven.

Rivian EDV recall questions

How many recalls does the Rivian EDV have?

NHTSA lists 2 safety recalls covering the 2022 Rivian EDV. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Rivian EDV Seat Belt Pretensioner Cable May Fail" (NHTSA campaign 25V816000), reported Nov 25, 2025.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Rivian EDV?

No. None of the 2 Rivian EDV recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Rivian EDV has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Rivian dealer, including for second owners.
