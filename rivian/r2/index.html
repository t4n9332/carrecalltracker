Rivian R1S and R1T backup camera display issue medium

Reported Sep 16, 2026 · Campaign 26V597000 · Model years: 2027

In some Rivian R1S and R1T vehicles, a notification can pop up on screen and block part of the rearview camera image when the vehicle is in reverse. This can make it harder for the driver to see what is behind the vehicle while backing up.
