Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps may fail, stalling engine high

Reported Nov 4, 2020 · Campaign 20V682000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Toyota is recalling a large number of Toyota and Lexus vehicles from the 2017 through 2020 model years, including Camry, Corolla, RAV4, Highlander, Tacoma, Tundra, Sienna and many Lexus models. The low-pressure fuel pump, which sits inside the fuel tank and sends gasoline to the engine, may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while you are driving. This recall expands an earlier recall covering the same fuel pump problem.

Toyota Land Cruiser and Lexus LX570 Airbag Deactivation high

Reported Dec 13, 2018 · Campaign 18V887000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On some Toyota Land Cruiser and Lexus LX 570 vehicles, a wire harness connected to the front passenger seat belt's tension sensor can break over time. If this wire breaks, it can turn off the front passenger air bag, the knee air bag, and the side air bag built into the passenger seat. These air bags would then not be available to help protect the passenger.

Lexus LX570 recall questions

How many recalls does the Lexus LX570 have?

NHTSA lists 2 safety recalls covering the 2018–2019 Lexus LX570. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps may fail, stalling engine" (NHTSA campaign 20V682000), reported Nov 4, 2020.

Which Lexus LX570 model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Lexus LX570 has the most, with 2 recalls. The 2018 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Lexus LX570?

No. None of the 2 Lexus LX570 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Lexus LX570 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Lexus dealer, including for second owners.
