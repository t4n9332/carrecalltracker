Lexus NX Rearview Camera May Not Display medium

Reported Mar 18, 2026 · Campaign 26V162000 · Model years: 2022

On certain Lexus NX, RX, and TX vehicles, the rearview camera image may fail to show up on screen when the car is shifted into reverse. This means the vehicle does not meet the federal requirement for rear visibility.

Toyota, Lexus and Subaru rearview camera may freeze medium

Reported Oct 30, 2025 · Campaign 25V744000 · Model years: 2022

Toyota is recalling certain 2022 through 2026 Toyota and Lexus vehicles and the Subaru Solterra that have the Panoramic View Monitor camera system. Models include RAV4, Highlander, Prius, Sienna Hybrid, Camry Hybrid, Mirai, bZ4X and the Lexus NX and TX Hybrid. A software error can cause the backup camera picture to freeze or go blank when the car is in reverse, so these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard.

Lexus NX recall questions

How many recalls does the Lexus NX have?

NHTSA lists 2 safety recalls covering the 2022 Lexus NX. 0 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Lexus NX Rearview Camera May Not Display" (NHTSA campaign 26V162000), reported Mar 18, 2026.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Lexus NX?

No. None of the 2 Lexus NX recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Lexus NX has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Lexus dealer, including for second owners.
