Lexus NX, RX and TX backup camera may not display medium

Reported 2026-03-18 · Campaign 26V162000 · Model years: 2022

On certain 2022-2025 Lexus NX350 and NX250, 2023-2026 RX350, and 2024-2026 TX350 vehicles, the backup camera picture may not come up on the screen when you shift into reverse. Without that picture you cannot see what is directly behind the vehicle, which federal rules require. Dealers will update the camera software, or replace the camera if the update does not fix it, free of charge. Owner letters were mailed May 15, 2026.

Toyota, Lexus and Subaru Solterra rearview cameras may freeze medium

Reported 2025-10-30 · Campaign 25V744000 · Model years: 2022

These vehicles have a Panoramic View Monitor system that shows what is behind and around you. A software error can make the backup camera image freeze or go completely blank when you shift into reverse. That means you may not see a child, a pet or an object behind the vehicle. Federal rules require a working rear view. A dealer will update the parking assist software at no cost; it is a software fix, not a part replacement.
