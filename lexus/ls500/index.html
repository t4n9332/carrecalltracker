Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps may fail, stalling engine high

Reported Nov 4, 2020 · Campaign 20V682000 · Model years: 2018

Toyota is recalling a large number of Toyota and Lexus vehicles from the 2017 through 2020 model years, including Camry, Corolla, RAV4, Highlander, Tacoma, Tundra, Sienna and many Lexus models. The low-pressure fuel pump, which sits inside the fuel tank and sends gasoline to the engine, may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while you are driving. This recall expands an earlier recall covering the same fuel pump problem.

Toyota, Lexus fuel pump may fail, stall engine high

Reported Jan 13, 2020 · Campaign 20V012000 · Model years: 2018

On many Toyota and Lexus models, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If the pump fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven, which affects the driver's ability to keep control of the vehicle at that moment.

Lexus LS500 recall questions

How many recalls does the Lexus LS500 have?

NHTSA lists 2 safety recalls covering the 2018 Lexus LS500. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Toyota and Lexus fuel pumps may fail, stalling engine" (NHTSA campaign 20V682000), reported Nov 4, 2020.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Lexus LS500?

No. None of the 2 Lexus LS500 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Lexus LS500 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Lexus dealer, including for second owners.
