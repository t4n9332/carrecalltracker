Lexus LS Passenger Airbag Sensor May Misread Occupant high

Reported Apr 17, 2024 · Campaign 24V275000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2021

In certain Lexus LS500 and LS500 Hybrid vehicles with the Executive Package, the front passenger seat may have been built with parts that interfere with each other, which can cause the seat's occupant sensor to incorrectly read whether someone is sitting there.

Lexus LS Run-Flat Tires Can Lose Tread high

Reported Feb 27, 2019 · Campaign 19V134000 · Model years: 2018

These vehicles have run-flat tires with a reinforcement layer built into the sidewall. During the process of mounting the tire onto the wheel, that layer may have cracked. A cracked layer can cause the tire's outer tread to separate from the rest of the tire while driving, which can affect how the vehicle handles and stays stable on the road.
