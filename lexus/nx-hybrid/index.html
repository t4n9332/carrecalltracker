Lexus NX Hybrid recalls

3 recalls covering model years 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Toyota, Lexus and Subaru rearview camera may freeze medium

Reported 2025-10-30 · Campaign 25V744000 · Model years: 2024

Toyota is recalling certain 2022 through 2026 Toyota and Lexus vehicles and the Subaru Solterra that have the Panoramic View Monitor camera system. Models include RAV4, Highlander, Prius, Sienna Hybrid, Camry Hybrid, Mirai, bZ4X and the Lexus NX and TX Hybrid. A software error can cause the backup camera picture to freeze or go blank when the car is in reverse, so these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard.

Lexus NX front headrests can come out too easily low

Reported 2024-06-27 · Campaign 24V482000 · Model years: 2024

On some Lexus NX and RX models, the head restraints — the headrests on the driver's and front passenger's seats — can be pulled out without pressing the release button that is supposed to hold them in place. If a headrest comes out and nobody puts it back, the person in that seat has nothing behind their head, and a hit from behind is much more likely to injure their neck. A dealer replaces both front headrests for free.

Toyota and Lexus steering wiring can disable driver's airbag high

Reported 2023-07-12 · Campaign 23V480000 · Model years: 2024

Inside the steering column is a coiled ribbon of wiring that keeps the driver's airbag connected as the steering wheel turns. On these vehicles, one electrical connection in that part may not have been welded well enough. If it comes apart, the driver's airbag is switched off and will not inflate in a crash. This recall also means these vehicles do not meet the federal crash-protection standard for occupant safety. Dealers will inspect this part and replace it at no cost if needed.
