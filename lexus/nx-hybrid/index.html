Toyota, Lexus and Subaru rearview camera may freeze medium

Reported Oct 30, 2025 · Campaign 25V744000 · Model years: 2024

Toyota is recalling certain 2022 through 2026 Toyota and Lexus vehicles and the Subaru Solterra that have the Panoramic View Monitor camera system. Models include RAV4, Highlander, Prius, Sienna Hybrid, Camry Hybrid, Mirai, bZ4X and the Lexus NX and TX Hybrid. A software error can cause the backup camera picture to freeze or go blank when the car is in reverse, so these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard.

Lexus NX Head Restraints May Detach Unexpectedly high

Reported Jun 27, 2024 · Campaign 24V482000 · Model years: 2024

The head restraints on the driver and front passenger seats in these vehicles may come off without the lock release button being pressed. If a head restraint is removed this way and not put back in place, it may not be there to help protect the driver or passenger during a rear-end crash.

Toyota, Lexus: Steering Cable May Disable Air Bag high

Reported Jul 12, 2023 · Campaign 23V480000 · Model years: 2024

On certain Toyota Corolla, Corolla Cross, Highlander, Tacoma, and related Lexus vehicles, an electrical connection inside the steering column's spiral cable assembly may not be welded well enough. If this connection separates, it can deactivate the driver's air bag. A deactivated air bag will not deploy the way it's supposed to. Dealers will inspect the spiral cable assembly and replace it if necessary, free of charge.

Lexus NX Hybrid recall questions

How many recalls does the Lexus NX Hybrid have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2024 Lexus NX Hybrid. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Toyota, Lexus and Subaru rearview camera may freeze" (NHTSA campaign 25V744000), reported Oct 30, 2025.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Lexus NX Hybrid?

No. None of the 3 Lexus NX Hybrid recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Lexus NX Hybrid has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Lexus dealer, including for second owners.
