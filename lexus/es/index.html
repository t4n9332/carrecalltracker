Toyota, Lexus airbag sensor may block deployment high

Reported Dec 20, 2023 · Campaign 23V865000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On certain Toyota and Lexus models, a short circuit can develop in the sensor that detects who is sitting in the front passenger seat, called the occupant classification sensor. If this happens, it can prevent the front passenger airbag from deploying in a crash.

Lexus ES recall questions

How many recalls does the Lexus ES have?

NHTSA lists 1 safety recall covering the 2020–2021 Lexus ES. 1 of them is rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Toyota, Lexus airbag sensor may block deployment" (NHTSA campaign 23V865000), reported Dec 20, 2023.

Which Lexus ES model year has the most recalls?

The 2020 Lexus ES has the most, with 1 recall. The 2020 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Lexus ES?

No. None of the 1 Lexus ES recall on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Lexus ES has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Lexus dealer, including for second owners.
