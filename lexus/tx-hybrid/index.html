Toyota, Lexus and Subaru Solterra rearview cameras may freeze medium

Reported 2025-10-30 · Campaign 25V744000 · Model years: 2024

These vehicles have a Panoramic View Monitor system that shows what is behind and around you. A software error can make the backup camera image freeze or go completely blank when you shift into reverse. That means you may not see a child, a pet or an object behind the vehicle. Federal rules require a working rear view. A dealer will update the parking assist software at no cost; it is a software fix, not a part replacement.

Toyota Grand Highlander and Lexus TX curtain airbags deploy wrong medium

Reported 2024-06-20 · Campaign 24V461000 · Model years: 2024

The side curtain airbags drop down from above the side windows in a crash and are meant to keep people from being thrown out of the vehicle. On these vehicles the airbag may not unroll the way it should, and if a window is open it can partly deploy outside the vehicle instead of covering the window opening. When that happens it does not protect the people sitting beside it as well as it is supposed to.
