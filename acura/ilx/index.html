Acura ILX Brake Fluid Contamination Weakens Braking high

Reported Dec 11, 2025 · Campaign 25V859000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

In these Acura ILX vehicles, the brake fluid can become contaminated. Over time, that contamination can cause a seal inside the brake master cylinder to deform, or change shape. Once the seal deforms, brake fluid can leak out from inside the system. When fluid leaks this way, it can reduce how well the brakes work, meaning the brakes may not perform as they should when needed.

Honda and Acura Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported Dec 18, 2023 · Campaign 23V858000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

In a wide range of Honda and Acura vehicles, the fuel pump located inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails while you are driving, the engine can suddenly stall.

Honda, Acura fuel pump may fail, stall engine high

Reported Mar 25, 2021 · Campaign 21V215000 · Model years: 2019

On many Honda and Acura models, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven.

Acura ILX Driveshaft Can Separate From Half Shaft high

Reported Jun 6, 2019 · Campaign 19V424000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Some Acura ILX vehicles have too much grease in a part called the half shaft. The excess grease can prevent a set-ring from fully engaging, which can allow the driveshaft to come apart from the half shaft.

Acura ILX Fuel Gauge May Read Incorrectly medium

Reported Jan 24, 2019 · Campaign 19V052000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Some Acura ILX vehicles may have a fuel tank that is deformed, which raises the height of the fuel pump inside the tank. This can cause the fuel level float to stick, so the fuel gauge may show more fuel in the tank than there actually is. Because of this, the gauge reading may not match how much gas is really left in the tank.

Acura ILX recall questions

How many recalls does the Acura ILX have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2018–2020 Acura ILX. 4 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Acura ILX Brake Fluid Contamination Weakens Braking" (NHTSA campaign 25V859000), reported Dec 11, 2025.

Which Acura ILX model year has the most recalls?

The 2019 Acura ILX has the most, with 5 recalls. The 2020 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Acura ILX?

No. None of the 5 Acura ILX recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Acura ILX has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Acura dealer, including for second owners.
