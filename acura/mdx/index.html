Honda, Acura Rear Subframe May Corrode And Fail high

Reported Jun 4, 2026 · Campaign 26V365000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

On these vehicles, the rear subframe can corrode at the points where the suspension is mounted. If this happens, rear suspension parts, such as the rear control arm, can fail. This affects vehicles sold in a number of states. Dealers will inspect the rear subframe and install a reinforcement kit, and repair or replace subframe components as needed, free of charge. A final remedy is expected later, and additional letters will follow once it's ready.

Honda and Acura front passenger airbags may deploy unintentionally high

Reported May 21, 2026 · Campaign 26V332000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

Honda is recalling certain Honda and Acura vehicles, including the Accord, Civic, CR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Ridgeline, HR-V, Fit, Insight, Passport and the Acura TLX, RDX and MDX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short circuit. If that happens, the airbags can go off when they should not during a crash. Dealers will replace the seat weight sensors at no cost. This recall expands an earlier recall.

Acura, Honda Brake Pedal Pin Can Shift high

Reported Jun 12, 2025 · Campaign 25V391000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

The brake pedal in these vehicles is held in place by a pin that was not secured correctly. Because of this, the pedal can shift out of its normal position while the vehicle is being driven. When the pedal moves out of place, the driver may not be able to press the brakes the way they normally would, which can affect the vehicle's ability to slow down or stop.

Acura MDX Tailgate Lights May Fail From Moisture high

Reported Apr 17, 2025 · Campaign 25V259000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

In certain Acura MDX vehicles, moisture can get into the tailgate light assembly and its wiring. This can cause the interior and exterior lights to stop working, including the tailgate lid lights, position lights, license plate lights, and taillights.

Acura MDX Touchscreen May Go Blank, Hiding Camera medium

Reported Jan 23, 2025 · Campaign 25V032000 · Model years: 2025

Some 2025 Acura MDX vehicles have a touchscreen in the center console that may go blank. When this happens, the rearview camera image does not display as it should. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet federal safety rules that require a working rear visibility display. Without the camera image, the driver may not be able to see what is behind the vehicle when backing up.

Honda and Acura passenger seat sensor may crack high

Reported Feb 1, 2024 · Campaign 24V064000 · Model years: 2020, 2022

Honda is recalling certain 2020 through 2022 Honda and Acura vehicles, including Accord, Civic, CR-V, HR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Passport, Ridgeline, Fit, Insight and the Acura MDX, RDX and TLX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short out. When that happens, the system may not switch off the front passenger airbag when it is supposed to.

Honda and Acura Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported Dec 18, 2023 · Campaign 23V858000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

In a wide range of Honda and Acura vehicles, the fuel pump located inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails while you are driving, the engine can suddenly stall.

Acura, Honda Engine Bearing May Wear and Seize critical

Reported Nov 13, 2023 · Campaign 23V751000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

These vehicles have an engine part called a connecting rod bearing, and a manufacturing error affects it. Because of this defect, the bearing can wear down over time and eventually seize up inside the engine. When that happens, the engine can become damaged. A damaged engine may not run the way it should, and it could even stop working suddenly while the vehicle is being driven.

Honda, Acura Models: Brake Master Cylinder May Separate high

Reported Jun 29, 2023 · Campaign 23V458000 · Model years: 2020

A fastener that connects the brake booster to the brake master cylinder may not have been put together correctly when the vehicle was built. Over time, this can cause the master cylinder to come apart from the booster assembly.

Honda, Acura fuel pump may fail, stall engine high

Reported Mar 25, 2021 · Campaign 21V215000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On many Honda and Acura models, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven.

Honda and Acura Models: Timing Belt May Fail high

Reported Apr 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V298000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On certain Acura MDX, TLX, and Honda Odyssey, Pilot, and Ridgeline vehicles, the teeth on the timing belt may separate from the belt. If the timing belt teeth separate, the engine may stall while driving. Honda will notify owners, and dealers will inspect the vehicle and replace the timing belt if it's affected. If a new timing belt is installed, the engine will also be inspected and any damaged parts will be replaced, all free of charge.

Acura MDX Taillights May Fail From Moisture medium

Reported Mar 28, 2019 · Campaign 19V256000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Some Acura MDX and MDX Sport Hybrid vehicles may let moisture into the tailgate lid lights. This can cause the tailgate lid lights and the taillights to stop working. Losing the taillights reduces how visible the vehicle is to others, which increases the risk of a crash.

Acura MDX, TLX Fuel Pump May Cause Engine Stall high

Reported Jan 29, 2019 · Campaign 19V060000 · Model years: 2018

Certain Acura MDX and TLX vehicles have a problem with the fuel pump. Tiny particles in the fuel can stick to parts inside the pump over time, causing it to lose performance. When the fuel pump isn't working the way it should, it can make the engine stall while the vehicle is being driven. This kind of engine stall increases the risk of a crash, since it can happen while you are out on the road driving normally.

Acura and Honda Models Have Reduced Brake Performance high

Reported Nov 1, 2018 · Campaign 18V777000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On certain Acura MDX, Acura MDX Sport Hybrid, Honda HR-V, and Honda Odyssey vehicles, a coating inside the rear brake calipers may not have been applied correctly during manufacturing. This can allow small gas pockets to form inside the caliper pistons. Over time, these gas pockets can reduce how well the brakes are able to slow or stop the vehicle, lowering overall braking performance.

Honda and Acura Power Seats May Detach high

Reported Apr 5, 2018 · Campaign 18V221000 · Model years: 2018

On these vehicles, the powered front seats may have been built with rivets that were not manufactured correctly. This affects the driver and passenger seats in the Honda vehicles, and the driver seat in the Acura vehicles. In a crash, these rivets can break, which can cause the seat to come loose from the floor of the vehicle instead of staying secured in place.

Acura MDX recall questions

How many recalls does the Acura MDX have?

NHTSA lists 15 safety recalls covering the 2018–2025 Acura MDX. 13 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Honda, Acura Rear Subframe May Corrode And Fail" (NHTSA campaign 26V365000), reported Jun 4, 2026.

Which Acura MDX model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Acura MDX has the most, with 10 recalls. The 2022 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Acura MDX?

No. None of the 15 Acura MDX recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Acura MDX has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Acura dealer, including for second owners.
