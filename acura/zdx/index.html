Acura ZDX Rearview Camera Display Failure high

Reported May 14, 2026 · Campaign 26V306000 · Model years: 2024

On some 2024 Acura ZDX and 2024-2025 Honda Prologue vehicles, the rearview camera screen may show a distorted or blank picture instead of a clear view of what's behind the vehicle.

Acura ZDX Instrument Panel and Camera Software Failure high

Reported Feb 26, 2026 · Campaign 26V112000 · Model years: 2024

A software problem in some Acura ZDX vehicles can cause the instrument panel display to stop working, so drivers may not see the speedometer or warning lights. A separate software issue can make the rearview camera show a blank screen instead of the view behind the vehicle when it's in reverse. Both issues can leave drivers without information they need to drive or back up safely.

Acura ZDX Stabilizer Bar Bolts May Loosen high

Reported Apr 10, 2025 · Campaign 25V234000 · Model years: 2024

Some Acura ZDX vehicles have passenger-side front stabilizer bar bracket bolts that may not have been tightened properly. If these bolts loosen, they can separate from the vehicle frame, and the stabilizer bar or bracket can detach. A detached part can damage the vehicle's high voltage cables or the battery coolant lines. Damage to those cables or lines can cause the vehicle to shut down and lose drive power while being driven, which increases the chance of a crash.

Acura ZDX Bolts May Damage High-Voltage Cables medium

Reported Jan 16, 2025 · Campaign 25V018000 · Model years: 2024

On certain 2024 Acura ZDX vehicles, the bolts that hold the front passenger stabilizer bar bracket in place may become loose over time. If the bolts become loose enough, the bracket can detach from the vehicle's frame entirely. When this happens, the detached bracket or its bolts can damage the vehicle's high voltage cables or the coolant lines that serve the EV battery.

Acura ZDX Anti-Lock Brakes May Release Pressure high

Reported Aug 15, 2024 · Campaign 24V614000 · Model years: 2024

In certain all-wheel-drive ZDX vehicles, the anti-lock brake system may activate on its own and release pressure in the vehicle's regular brake system. This can happen unexpectedly while the vehicle is being driven, reducing the vehicle's ability to brake normally.

Acura ZDX recall questions

How many recalls does the Acura ZDX have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2024 Acura ZDX. 4 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Acura ZDX Rearview Camera Display Failure" (NHTSA campaign 26V306000), reported May 14, 2026.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Acura ZDX?

No. None of the 5 Acura ZDX recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Acura ZDX has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Acura dealer, including for second owners.
