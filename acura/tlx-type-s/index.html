Acura MDX, TLX, Honda Pilot Engine May Stall high

Reported Jan 23, 2025 · Campaign 25V031000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

Certain Acura MDX Type-S, Acura TLX Type-S, and Honda Pilot vehicles have a software error in the fuel injection electronic control unit, sometimes called the FI-ECU. Because of this software error, the engine may stall or lose power while the vehicle is being driven.
