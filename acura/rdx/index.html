Honda and Acura front passenger airbags may deploy unintentionally high

Reported May 21, 2026 · Campaign 26V332000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Honda is recalling certain Honda and Acura vehicles, including the Accord, Civic, CR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Ridgeline, HR-V, Fit, Insight, Passport and the Acura TLX, RDX and MDX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short circuit. If that happens, the airbags can go off when they should not during a crash. Dealers will replace the seat weight sensors at no cost. This recall expands an earlier recall.

Acura RDX Power Steering Software May Fail high

Reported Sep 5, 2025 · Campaign 25V582000 · Model years: 2025

Honda is recalling certain Acura RDX vehicles because the software that controls the electric power steering assist may not be calibrated properly. If this happens, the steering system can enter a failsafe mode, resulting in a loss of power steering assist while driving. Dealers will reprogram the electric power steering software so it is calibrated correctly, and this repair will be done free of charge.

Acura RDX and Odyssey Steering Corrosion Risk high

Reported Feb 15, 2024 · Campaign 24V114000 · Model years: 2024

On some Acura RDX and Honda Odyssey vehicles, the steering gear box may not have been tightened properly. This can let water get inside, causing corrosion and the gears to bind. Corroded, binding gears can make the steering harder to control.

Honda and Acura passenger seat sensor may crack high

Reported Feb 1, 2024 · Campaign 24V064000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Honda is recalling certain 2020 through 2022 Honda and Acura vehicles, including Accord, Civic, CR-V, HR-V, Odyssey, Pilot, Passport, Ridgeline, Fit, Insight and the Acura MDX, RDX and TLX. The sensor that measures the weight of the person in the front passenger seat can crack and short out. When that happens, the system may not switch off the front passenger airbag when it is supposed to.

Honda and Acura Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported Dec 18, 2023 · Campaign 23V858000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

In a wide range of Honda and Acura vehicles, the fuel pump located inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails while you are driving, the engine can suddenly stall.

Acura Integra, RDX and Accord Brake Fluid Leak high

Reported Jun 15, 2023 · Campaign 23V430000 · Model years: 2023

On some Acura Integra, Acura RDX, and Honda Accord vehicles, a valve inside the brake system may leak brake fluid. This can cause the vehicle to move on its own when the brake hold feature is on, or make the brake pedal feel like it needs to be pushed further down than normal.

Honda, Acura Seat Belt Buckles May Not Latch high

Reported Mar 9, 2023 · Campaign 23V158000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Certain Honda and Acura vehicles, including the CR-V, Accord, Accord Hybrid, Odyssey, Insight, and RDX, may have a manufacturing problem with the front seat belts. The buckle's channel may interfere with the release button, which can prevent the seat belt buckle from latching properly. This means the seat belt may not click into place and hold securely the way it is supposed to for the driver or front passenger seat.

Acura RDX, Honda CR-V Hybrid Driveshaft May Fail high

Reported Dec 2, 2021 · Campaign 21V939000 · Model years: 2021

Honda is recalling certain Acura RDX and CR-V Hybrid vehicles because a part in the front driveshaft, on the right or left side, may not have been heat-treated correctly during manufacturing. If that part fails, it can cause a sudden loss of drive power to the wheels, or let the vehicle roll away on its own if parked without the parking brake applied. Either situation can increase the risk of a crash or injury.

Honda, Acura fuel pump may fail, stall engine high

Reported Mar 25, 2021 · Campaign 21V215000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On many Honda and Acura models, the low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail. If it fails, the engine can stall while the vehicle is being driven.

Honda and Acura Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported May 28, 2020 · Campaign 20V314000 · Model years: 2019

Certain Honda and Acura vehicles have a low-pressure fuel pump, inside the fuel tank, that may fail. If the pump fails while you are driving, the engine can stall, which raises the risk of a crash. Honda will replace the fuel pump assembly on affected vehicles, free of charge, as part of this recall.
