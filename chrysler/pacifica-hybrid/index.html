Chrysler Pacifica Hybrid fire risk from electrical connection critical

Reported 2020-06-09 · Campaign 20V334000 · Model years: 2020

A large electrical connection behind the driver's seat in these plug-in hybrid minivans can develop a bad contact. A bad contact makes heat, and that heat can start a fire, with the van running or parked and switched off. Until the final fix is ready, Chrysler says not to park these vans inside a garage or other building, or close to other vehicles, and to keep drinks, wet umbrellas and anything else that could leak out of the back seat area.
