Pacifica Hybrid 12-Volt Isolator May Overheat critical

Reported Jun 9, 2020 · Campaign 20V334000 · Model years: 2020

FCA is recalling certain Pacifica Plug-in Hybrid vehicles because the 12-volt isolator post located behind the driver's seat may develop high resistance at that connection. The final repair for this problem is still being developed. Until it is ready, dealers will inspect this connection, and if the post is found to be compromised, the isolator post and its cables will be replaced.
