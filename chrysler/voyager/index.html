Chrysler Voyager recalls

13 recalls covering model years 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Chrysler Pacifica, Voyager Seat Wiring May Disable Airbags high

Reported Jun 2, 2026 · Campaign 26V358000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Some Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager vehicles have a wiring connector for the front seat that contains a spring. That spring may shift and touch electrical contacts inside the connector that it should not touch. This unintended contact can affect the front airbags and may keep them from working the way they are supposed to in a crash.

Chrysler Pacifica, Voyager Side Curtain Air Bags May Leak high

Reported Mar 26, 2026 · Campaign 26V189000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

The right and left side curtain air bags may not hold enough pressure because their seams were not sealed properly. This affects certain Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager vehicles. This recall replaces two earlier recalls; vehicles already fixed under those recalls will still need this new repair. Dealers will replace the side curtain air bags as necessary, free of charge.

Wheelchair Restraints in Various Vehicles May Not Lock high

Reported Dec 17, 2025 · Campaign 25V876000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

Certain vehicles equipped with QRT-Deluxe or QRT-Max wheelchair restraints may have retractors that fail to lock. If the retractor does not lock, a wheelchair inside the vehicle may not be properly secured.

Chrysler Pacifica/Voyager Rearview Camera May Not Display medium

Reported Jun 12, 2025 · Campaign 25V388000 · Model years: 2023

On certain Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager vehicles, a manufacturing error can keep the rearview camera image from showing up on the screen. Because of this, these vehicles don't meet federal safety standards for rear visibility. Without a working camera image, the driver has a reduced view of what is behind the vehicle, which increases the risk of a crash.

Chrysler Pacifica, Voyager: Side Air Bag Seal Issue high

Reported May 8, 2025 · Campaign 25V302000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

On certain Pacifica and Voyager minivans, the side curtain air bags may not hold pressure properly because of seams that weren't sealed correctly. These air bags are designed to stay inflated and help protect occupants during a crash, but this sealing problem means they may lose pressure and not work as intended. Dealers will replace the affected side curtain air bags to fix the issue.

Chrysler Pacifica, Voyager Seats May Sit Near Airbag high

Reported Nov 25, 2024 · Campaign 24V888000 · Model years: 2024

On certain 2024 Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager vehicles, a seat limiter that is supposed to stop the seat from sliding too far forward may not have been installed. Without this limiter, the seat can move further forward than it should. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet federal rules for protecting occupants in a crash. Dealers will check the seat and install a limiter if needed.

Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager side curtain airbags may not open high

Reported Oct 24, 2024 · Campaign 24V793000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2023

Chrysler is recalling certain Chrysler Pacifica and Chrysler Voyager vehicles. A connector for the side curtain airbags, the airbags along the side windows, may not be properly secured, and that can keep those airbags from opening. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rules on side impact protection and on keeping people from being thrown out of the vehicle. Chrysler says dealers will secure the connector.

Chrysler, Jeep, Ram Rearview Camera May Not Display high

Reported Jun 13, 2024 · Campaign 24V436000 · Model years: 2022

A software issue in the radio can prevent the rearview camera image from displaying on the screen. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the requirements of the federal safety standard for rear visibility, FMVSS 111. To fix the problem, dealers will update the radio software, or the software update will be delivered over-the-air. This repair is free of charge for owners of the affected vehicles.

Chrysler Pacifica, Voyager Windshield Detachment Risk high

Reported Mar 28, 2024 · Campaign 24V238000 · Model years: 2024

Chrysler is recalling certain Pacifica and Voyager minivans because the windshield may not have been properly attached to the vehicle. This means the windshield could come loose from the vehicle during a crash.

Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager lose traction control during cruise control high

Reported Mar 28, 2024 · Campaign 24V240000 · Model years: 2024

In these minivans, the traction control system does not work while cruise control is switched on. Traction control is the system that helps keep the wheels gripping the road. Chrysler is recalling certain 2024 Pacifica and Voyager vehicles because of this. Dealers will install updated traction control software at no cost to the owner. Chrysler mailed notification letters to owners in April 2024, and owners with questions can call the company's customer service line.

Jeep, Ram, Chrysler Air Bag May Not Deploy high

Reported Mar 14, 2024 · Campaign 24V199000 · Model years: 2023

Certain Jeep, Ram, and Chrysler vehicles may have a steering column control module that wasn't welded correctly. Because of this welding problem, the driver's air bag may not deploy during a crash the way it is supposed to. This affects a range of trucks and SUVs, including the Wrangler, Grand Cherokee, Gladiator, Wagoneer, Grand Wagoneer, Pacifica, and Voyager, meaning the driver may not get the protection the air bag is meant to provide.

Chrysler Pacifica, Voyager wiper arms may loosen medium

Reported Aug 11, 2022 · Campaign 22V619000 · Model years: 2022

The nuts holding the windshield wiper arms on these vehicles may not be tightened properly, which can cause the wipers to stop working correctly.

Chrysler Pacifica and Voyager second-row seat may not latch medium

Reported Mar 24, 2022 · Campaign 22V181000 · Model years: 2021

Chrysler is recalling certain 2021-2022 Pacifica and 2021 Voyager vehicles. The latch that holds a second-row seat to the floor may stick in the open position, so the seat does not lock down to the floor the way it should. Because of that, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for child seat anchor systems. Dealers will look at the seat and, if needed, replace the frame in the second-row seat cushion.

Chrysler Voyager recall questions

How many recalls does the Chrysler Voyager have?

NHTSA lists 13 safety recalls covering the 2020–2024 Chrysler Voyager. 10 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Chrysler Pacifica, Voyager Seat Wiring May Disable Airbags" (NHTSA campaign 26V358000), reported Jun 2, 2026.

Which Chrysler Voyager model year has the most recalls?

The 2023 Chrysler Voyager has the most, with 7 recalls. The 2020 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Chrysler Voyager?

No. None of the 13 Chrysler Voyager recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Chrysler Voyager has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Chrysler dealer, including for second owners.
