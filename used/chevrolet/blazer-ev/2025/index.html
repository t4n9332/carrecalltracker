Buying a used 2025 Chevrolet Blazer EV? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 19 owner complaints on file

Three high-severity recalls, no critical ones: doors that may unlatch and open on the Blazer and Equinox, and two separate parking brake wiring recalls. None carried stop-driving or park-outside instructions. Nineteen unverified owner complaints were filed with NHTSA, citing one crash and three injuries. Forward collision avoidance leads with 11 reports, well ahead of service brakes, electrical, and back-over prevention — a driver-assist pattern that the recall list itself does not address.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both parking brake wiring recalls and the door latch recall were performed
  3. Open, close, and latch each door, listening for anything loose or inconsistent
  4. Drive it long enough to see whether forward-collision braking activates unprompted
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
