Buying a used 2024 Chevrolet Colorado? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 121 owner complaints on file

Recalls here shift toward a fuel tank ring that may leak, plus trucks built with the wrong tires and faulty pressure warnings, and loose seat belt bolts. Complaint volume stayed high at 121 unverified NHTSA reports — electrical 35, service brakes 28, forward collision avoidance 22, engine 21 — a spread similar to 2023 but less concentrated on collision avoidance. Reported outcomes include 6 crashes, 5 injuries and 1 fire.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel tank ring recall repair was completed
  3. Check the tires match the door placard and TPMS reads correctly
  4. Ask about warning lights, brake feel, or electrical faults
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
