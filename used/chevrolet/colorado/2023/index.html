Buying a used 2023 Chevrolet Colorado? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 135 owner complaints on file

This 2023 Colorado has three recalls: front wheel bolt damage, a camera issue that may trigger sudden braking, and loose seat belt bolts. NHTSA has 134 owner complaints on file (2 crashes, 0 fires, 6 injuries reported), with forward collision avoidance the top category at 45, followed by electrical system (41), service brakes (25), and engine (21). These are unverified owner reports. The complaint volume in collision-avoidance and electrical systems is worth asking the seller about directly.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the front wheel bolt recall was completed
  3. Confirm the forward-collision camera recall repair was done
  4. Ask about any forward collision avoidance or electrical system issues experienced
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
