Buying a used 2020 Chevrolet Equinox? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 306 owner complaints on file

This 2020 Equinox has two critical recalls - a fuel tank seam leak on AWD models and a GM transmission-bolt leak/fire risk - plus high-severity issues covering rear brakes that may drag and overheat, and child seat anchors blocked by coating. NHTSA logged 306 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 20 crashes, 7 fires, and 12 injuries. Service brakes (109) and engine (87) are the most-cited areas, followed by fuel/propulsion system (50) and unknown/other (43).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. If AWD, confirm the fuel tank seam leak recall was repaired
  3. Verify the rear brake drag/overheat recall was addressed
  4. Ask if the child seat anchor coating issue was fixed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
