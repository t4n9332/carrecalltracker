Buying a used 2019 Chevrolet Equinox? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 301 owner complaints on file

This 2019 Equinox has two critical recalls - a fuel tank seam leak on AWD models and a GM transmission-bolt leak/fire risk - plus a high-severity rear brake issue and a medium rear seat weld problem. NHTSA recorded 301 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 22 crashes, 7 fires, and 9 injuries. Engine (100) and service brakes (80) are the most-cited complaint areas, followed by electrical system (43) and fuel/propulsion system (40).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. If AWD, confirm the fuel tank seam leak recall was repaired
  3. Verify the transmission bolt recall was completed
  4. Ask about engine and brake repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
