Buying a used 2022 Chevrolet Equinox? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 166 owner complaints on file

Five recalls: a critical one for tires that can lose tread, a high-rated fuel pump problem that can stall the engine, and three medium seating items — a badly welded driver seat frame, a loose driver seat bolt, and blocked child seat anchors. None require stop-driving or parking outside. NHTSA shows 166 unverified complaints (13 crashes, 3 fires, 7 injuries) heavily weighted toward fuel and propulsion categories, which is consistent with the fuel pump recall.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the tire recall remedy and inspect current tires for tread condition
  3. Check the VIN for the fuel pump recall and ask about any stalling history
  4. Push and rock the driver seat for play, and confirm the seat recalls were done
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
