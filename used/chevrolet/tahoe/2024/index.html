Buying a used 2024 Chevrolet Tahoe? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 65 owner complaints on file

Three recalls, all shared with 2023: diesel fuel leaks, a missing brake fluid warning, and possible 6.2L V8 engine failure. Two of the three depend on the engine fitted, so the VIN decides what actually applies. The 65 unverified owner complaints are a small sample for a recent model year and lean toward engine (22), electrical (13) and service brakes (12), with no fires and one crash reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Decode the VIN to see whether the diesel and 6.2L V8 recalls apply at all
  3. Confirm any applicable recall work is already performed, since the truck is new enough to still have open items
  4. Ask whether the brake fluid warning software update was installed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
