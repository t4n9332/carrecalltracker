Buying a used 2019 Chevrolet Tahoe? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 105 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

This year is unusual: the critical recall for Tahoe and Yukon wheels that can lock up carries a do-not-drive warning, meaning owners were told to stop driving until the remedy was performed. A second recall covers trucks and SUVs that may suddenly pull to one side. NHTSA lists 105 owner complaints, all unverified reports, led by power train (38), engine (29) and fuel/propulsion (25), with 8 crashes and 6 injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the wheel lock-up recall was completed before driving it at all
  3. Get that remedy in writing by VIN, not just the seller's word
  4. Ask for transmission, engine and fuel system repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
