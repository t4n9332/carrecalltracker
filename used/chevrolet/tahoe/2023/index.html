Buying a used 2023 Chevrolet Tahoe? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 214 owner complaints on file

Engine complaints dominate this year more than any other Tahoe listed — 115 of 214 unverified NHTSA filings — alongside a recall for 6.2L V8 engines that may fail. Also open on the list: rear suspension bolts that can break, a missing brake fluid level warning, a diesel fuel leak campaign, and the daytime running lamp item. No fires and only one injury were reported across those complaints.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for engine service history, and whether the 6.2L V8 recall applies to this VIN
  3. Have the rear suspension bolts inspected, or confirm that recall was completed
  4. Check the brake fluid warning recall status and the fluid level yourself
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
