Buying a used 2024 Chevrolet Silverado 2500? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 139 owner complaints on file

The critical recall on this year is loss of steering, which is more serious than the routine remainder: two tailgate unlatch recalls and an incorrect tire pressure label. As a recent model year, it has the highest complaint volume here — 139 unverified NHTSA reports, with electrical system, service brakes and powertrain all heavily represented. Three crashes, one fire and three injuries were alleged. No recall carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering loss recall was performed and test for any heaviness, wandering or noise at the wheel.
  3. Check the door jamb tire pressure label against the owner's manual, since the label may be wrong.
  4. Ask about brake feel and any warning lights, given service brakes and electrical lead the complaints.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
