Buying a used 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 2500? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 74 owner complaints on file

Two critical recalls cover the diesel block heater cord, which can short and burn. The roof airbag record is the standout: four separate recalls for inflators that can burst or rupture, including one expansion, so coverage depends on the exact VIN. A dual fuel tank fault can stall the engine. Owners filed 74 unverified complaints with NHTSA, including six alleging fire, clustered in electrical system and engine. No recall here carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN on NHTSA and confirm all four roof airbag recalls, including the expansion, show as completed.
  3. If it's a diesel, ask whether the block heater cord recall was performed and inspect the cord for melting or scorch marks.
  4. Ask if the truck has dual fuel tanks and whether it has ever stalled or lost power while driving.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
