Buying a used 2019 Chevrolet 6500HD? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Two recalls apply: a critical brake fluid leak that can cause a fire — the one recall here that carried a park-outside instruction — and a medium-severity issue where the ABS warning light may be hidden from the driver. Neither required stop-driving. Only one unverified owner complaint was filed with NHTSA, in the engine category, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. The thin complaint record means recall completion status carries most of the weight.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake fluid leak fire recall was completed by VIN
  3. Check that the ABS warning light works at key-on and is visible
  4. Inspect brake lines and the master cylinder area for leaks or residue
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
