Buying a used 2020 Chevrolet Camaro? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 31 owner complaints on file

This 2020 Camaro carries three recalls: a fuel pump over-pressurization fire risk, an air bag emblem that can break, and a transmission issue that may lock the wheels. NHTSA has 31 owner complaints on file (7 crashes, 0 fires, 4 injuries reported), with power train the top category at 10, followed by electrical system (6), unknown/other (5), and engine (4). These are unverified owner reports. Confirming recall completion, especially the fuel pump and transmission recalls, is worth doing.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump over-pressurization recall was completed
  3. Ask whether the transmission wheel-lock recall was addressed
  4. Ask about any power train complaints or repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
