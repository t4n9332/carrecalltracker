Buying a used 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 3500? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 28 owner complaints on file

This 2020 Silverado 3500 has a critical seat belt fire risk recall, plus high-severity recalls covering hoods that may open while driving, a roof-rail airbag that may underperform, a seat belt bracket issue, a tailgate that may unlatch while driving, a diesel rear wheel lock-up risk, and a transmission wheel lockup issue. NHTSA logged 28 owner complaints (unverified reports): 1 crash, 1 fire, and no reported injuries. Power train complaints lead at 9, followed by service brakes at 8, engine at 6, and electrical system at 5.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seat belt fire risk recall was completed
  3. Ask about hood latch and tailgate latch inspection history
  4. Check diesel rear wheel lock-up and transmission lockup recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
