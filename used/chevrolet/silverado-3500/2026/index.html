Buying a used 2026 Chevrolet Silverado 3500? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

A young model year with a thin record so far. One high-severity recall covers Silverado 3500 and Sierra 3500 trucks that can stall; the other two are paperwork items — trucks shipped without the required owner's manual, and GM vehicles missing the electronic owner's manual download. No stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Only 2 owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA, both unverified, touching vehicle speed control, engine, suspension and structure — too few to show a pattern.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm by VIN whether the stall recall remedy has been performed
  3. Ask whether the truck has ever stalled or lost power while driving
  4. Verify the printed owner's manual is present and the digital download works
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
