Buying a used 2018 Chevrolet Silverado 3500? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 22 owner complaints on file

Three critical recalls sit on this year: a fuel tank leak risk specific to the 3500, and two covering the diesel block heater cord that can short and burn. Also listed are dual fuel tanks that can stall the engine, a crew cab roof airbag that can burst, and faulty front seat belts. The complaint file is thin — 22 unverified NHTSA reports with no crashes, fires or injuries, led by engine and electrical.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the fuel tank leak recall was completed and look and smell for fuel seepage under the bed.
  3. If diesel, confirm the block heater recalls were done and inspect the cord end for damage.
  4. On a crew cab, verify the roof airbag recall is closed and have the front seat belts checked for the belt defect.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
