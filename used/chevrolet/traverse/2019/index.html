Buying a used 2019 Chevrolet Traverse? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 404 owner complaints on file

This model year has a critical recall for missing transmission bolts that can cause a leak and fire risk, along with recalls for air bags that may not deploy and side curtain airbags that may be loose. NHTSA has logged 404 owner complaints, including 14 crashes, 2 fires, and 16 injuries; these are unverified consumer reports. Complaint categories are led by power train (166), electrical system (111), an unknown/other category (95), and engine (62).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the transmission bolt recall has been completed
  3. Ask whether the air bag deployment and side curtain airbag recalls were addressed
  4. Ask about power train or electrical system repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
