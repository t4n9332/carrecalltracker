Buying a used 2020 Chevrolet Traverse? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 225 owner complaints on file

This model year carries 8 recalls, including three critical issues — missing transmission bolts, over-cured tires, and a transmission leak risk for wheelchair van conversions — plus recalls for a cracked frame rail, a roof-rail air bag that may underperform, a fuel pump blockage that can stall the engine, and side air bag wiring. NHTSA has logged 225 owner complaints, including 11 crashes, 1 fire, and 7 injuries; these are unverified consumer reports. Top categories: engine (70), unknown/other (51), power train (39), electrical (28).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the transmission bolt and over-cured tire recalls were completed
  3. Ask whether the fuel pump blockage recall was addressed
  4. Ask about frame rail or air bag wiring repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
