Buying a used 2020 Chevrolet Traverse? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 225 owner complaints on file

The most-recalled Traverse year here, with eight campaigns: a fuel pump that can stall the engine, transmission fluid leaks, a possible cracked frame, two separate Continental tire defect recalls, and three airbag items covering roof airbag deployment and strength. One transmission recall applies only to wheelchair-accessible conversions. Complaints drop to 225 unverified filings, led by engine (70), with 11 crashes and 7 injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check whether the original Continental tires are still fitted, and whether either tire recall was addressed
  3. Ask for documentation on the frame inspection recall
  4. Confirm the fuel pump recall was completed and ask about any stalling episodes
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
