Buying a used 2023 Chevrolet Traverse? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 88 owner complaints on file

This 2023 Traverse has two related recalls covering a half-shaft that can separate, also affecting the Blazer, Cadillac and GMC SUVs sharing the platform. NHTSA has logged 88 owner complaints for this model year, including 5 crashes and 8 injuries; these are unverified driver reports, not confirmed defects. Powertrain complaints lead at 29, followed by electrical system at 19, engine at 17, and service brakes at 12.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if the half-shaft recall repair was completed
  3. Confirm which half-shaft recall campaign applies and if parts were replaced
  4. Ask about any drivetrain noises or powertrain repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
