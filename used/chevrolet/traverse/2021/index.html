Buying a used 2021 Chevrolet Traverse? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 89 owner complaints on file

This 2021 Traverse has three recalls: a critical transmission leak risk on wheelchair-van conversions, incorrect airbag software also affecting the Buick Enclave, and a side air bag wiring issue shared with the Enclave. NHTSA has logged 89 owner complaints for this model year, including 4 crashes and 6 injuries; these are unverified driver reports, not confirmed defects. Electrical system complaints lead at 22, followed by unknown/other at 19, powertrain at 15, and engine at 11.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if the wheelchair-van transmission leak recall was completed
  3. Confirm the airbag software recall was performed
  4. Ask about the side air bag wiring recall and any electrical repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
