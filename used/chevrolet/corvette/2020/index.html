Buying a used 2020 Chevrolet Corvette? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 34 owner complaints on file

This year has four recalls, three rated high: the car can be driven with the front hood unlatched, brake assist can fail, and the driver's seat belt may not lock in a crash. A front trunk release button recall is minor. Of 34 unverified owner complaints to NHTSA, structure led with 10 — consistent with the hood and trunk issues — followed by power train. One crash and one injury were reported, no fires.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the hood latch recall fix and check for hood or front-end damage
  3. Confirm the brake assist and driver seat belt recalls were completed
  4. Test the front trunk release button before buying
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
