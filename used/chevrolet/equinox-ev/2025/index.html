Buying a used 2025 Chevrolet Equinox EV? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 163 owner complaints on file

This 2025 Equinox EV carries six recalls, most rated high or medium: a door striker failure, a tire tread detachment issue (shared with the Cadillac Optiq), two separate pedestrian warning/alert sound problems, a cruise control braking issue, and a low-severity missing electronic owner's manual. NHTSA has 163 owner complaints (unverified reports) with 4 crashes and 2 injuries, no fires. Visibility/wiper (80) is by far the most-cited area, followed by forward collision avoidance (41), unknown/other (26), and service brakes (20).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the door striker and tire tread detachment recalls were repaired
  3. Ask about pedestrian warning/alert sound and cruise control software updates
  4. Ask about any wiper/visibility system complaints or repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
