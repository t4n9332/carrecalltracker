Buying a used 2025 Chevrolet Equinox EV? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 163 owner complaints on file

Six recalls is a long list for one model year: doors that may unlatch and open, tire tread that can peel off, two pedestrian alert sound items, a cruise control that may not brake, and a missing electronic owner's manual download. Owners filed 163 unverified complaints with NHTSA, with visibility/wiper dominating at 80 and forward collision avoidance at 41; four crashes and two injuries were reported, no fires. The door latch and tire tread items are the two rated high.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the door latch recall repair and open/close every door
  3. Check whether the recalled tires were replaced, and inspect tread
  4. Ask about wiper and visibility problems, and test cruise control braking
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
