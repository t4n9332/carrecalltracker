Buying a used 2018 Chevrolet Trax? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 101 owner complaints on file

This 2018 Trax was recalled for a front control arm weld that may break, covered by two separate recall notices. NHTSA has logged 101 owner complaints for this model year, including 2 crashes, 2 fires, and 2 injuries; these are unverified driver reports, not confirmed defects. Suspension complaints lead at 34, followed by engine at 29, electrical system at 18, and unknown/other at 17.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if the front control arm weld recall was completed
  3. Have a mechanic inspect the front control arms and suspension
  4. Ask about any fire-related incidents or engine warning lights
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
