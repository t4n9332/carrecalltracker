Buying a used 2023 Chevrolet Silverado 1500? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 372 owner complaints on file

A shorter list than earlier years: trucks that may not display a low brake fluid warning, 6.2L V8 engines that may fail, and daytime running lights that stay on. None carry stop-driving or park-outside instructions. NHTSA has 372 owner complaints — unverified reports — including 7 crashes, 6 fires and 2 injuries, with engine the largest area at 144 and powertrain at 104. Engine concerns appear in both the recall and complaint records.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check which engine it has and whether the 6.2L V8 recall remedy is documented.
  3. Check the brake fluid level yourself, since the low-fluid warning may not display.
  4. Ask whether the daytime running light recall has been performed.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
