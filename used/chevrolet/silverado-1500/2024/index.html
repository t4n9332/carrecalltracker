Buying a used 2024 Chevrolet Silverado 1500? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 310 owner complaints on file

This 2024 Silverado 1500 carries two recalls: a trailer hitch bracket that may let a trailer detach, and a GM trucks/SUVs engine failure recall. NHTSA has logged 306 owner complaints (unverified reports) on this model year, including 8 crashes and 3 injuries, with no reported fires. Engine complaints are the largest category at 146, followed by power train at 93, electrical system at 41, and 20 listed as unknown or other, out of 306 total complaints.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if the trailer hitch bracket recall was completed
  3. Confirm the engine failure recall repair status
  4. Ask about engine performance history (oil consumption, warning lights)
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
