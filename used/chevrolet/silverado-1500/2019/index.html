Buying a used 2019 Chevrolet Silverado 1500? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 1,076 owner complaints on file

This model year carries ten recalls, including three critical ones: a seat belt fire risk, a battery cable connection issue, and over-cured tires with a failure risk. Other recalls address a passenger air bag that may fail to deploy, loss of stability control, loss of brake assist, driveshaft separation, and seat belt bracket issues. NHTSA has logged 1,076 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 27 crashes, 8 fires, and 15 injuries cited; engine and power train are the most-mentioned categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the three critical recalls (seat belt, battery cable, tires) have been completed
  3. Check tire condition and DOT date codes for the over-cured tire recall
  4. Ask about engine, power train, or service brake issues reported
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
