Buying a used 2018 Chevrolet Silverado 1500? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 515 owner complaints on file

The roof-rail airbag appears in four separate recalls — bursting, rupturing, and an expanded population — alongside two brake power-assist recalls and one for trucks that may suddenly pull to one side. None carry stop-driving or park-outside instructions. NHTSA shows 515 owner complaints, unverified, with 10 crashes, 4 fires and 11 injuries; powertrain leads at 169, then electrical and engine at 101 each and service brakes at 100. Braking recurs in both records.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN against all four roof airbag recalls — a later one expanded coverage, so an earlier repair may not settle it.
  3. On a straight road, note whether the truck pulls to one side with a light grip.
  4. Ask whether both brake power-assist recalls were completed and note pedal effort from a cold start.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
