Buying a used 2018 Chevrolet Silverado 1500? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 515 owner complaints on file

This model year carries seven recalls, rated high or medium, covering brake assist that may weaken or lose power, unexpected braking or pulling, and several separate recalls addressing side and roof air bag inflator defects. NHTSA has logged 515 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 10 crashes, 4 fires, and 11 injuries cited. Power train, electrical system, engine, and service brakes are each cited in over 100 complaints.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all air bag inflator recalls (side and roof) have been completed
  3. Ask about any brake assist or unexpected braking/pulling repairs
  4. Ask about power train, engine, or service brake issues reported
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
