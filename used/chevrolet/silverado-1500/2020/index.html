Buying a used 2020 Chevrolet Silverado 1500? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 726 owner complaints on file

Four recalls are flagged critical: a fuel pump leak with fire risk, a battery cable that can stall the truck, and two covering rear wheels that can suddenly lock, one of them diesel-specific. High-severity items include brake caliper bolts that can break, a driveshaft that may separate, seat belt fire risk, and two Continental tire recalls. NHTSA has 726 unverified owner complaints on file, with 13 crashes, 9 fires and 12 injuries; engine and powertrain dominate.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump and battery cable recalls are closed, and whether this is the diesel covered by the rear-wheel lock recall.
  3. Ask for documentation of brake caliper bolt and driveshaft inspection or repair.
  4. Check the tire brand and whether either Continental tire recall applied to this VIN.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
