Buying a used 2021 Chevrolet Suburban? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 192 owner complaints on file

This year carries an unusually long list — ten recalls. The critical item covers diesel models whose rear wheels can lock. Five separate seat belt recalls follow (front seat bolts, center bracket, wrong bolts, third-row belts), along with loss of power steering assist, a fuel pump that can stall the engine, a rear driveshaft that may break, and 6.2L V8 engine failure. Of 192 unverified NHTSA complaints — 6 crashes, 1 fire, 5 injuries — engine and power train dominate.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN against all ten recalls; the seat belt fixes are separate jobs
  3. If it's the diesel, confirm the rear wheel lock recall was completed
  4. Ask for 6.2L V8 and fuel pump service records
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
