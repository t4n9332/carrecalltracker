Buying a used 2022 Chevrolet Suburban? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 119 owner complaints on file

This 2022 Suburban has recalls for a third-row seat belt buckle rivet flaw, an engine that may fail, rear wheel lockup, and daytime running lights that stay on. NHTSA has logged 119 owner complaints for this model year (unverified reports), including 4 crashes, 1 fire, and 4 injuries. Engine is the most-cited category at 60, followed by power train at 30, electrical system at 22, and fuel/propulsion system at 12.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm seat belt buckle rivet recall completion
  3. Ask about engine failure and rear wheel lockup recall repairs
  4. Check engine and power train repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
