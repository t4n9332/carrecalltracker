Buying a used 2022 Chevrolet Bolt EV? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 59 owner complaints on file · 3 park-outside

The 2022 carries the same five critical Bolt items, battery fire risk when fully charged, two battery-software installation campaigns, all park-outside, and two seat belt fire hazards, plus two airbag recalls: a possibly missing driver airbag fastener and a passenger airbag that may not open properly. Complaints number 59 with NHTSA, all unverified owner reports, led by electrical system (29) and fuel/propulsion (11). Three crashes are recorded, with no fires and no injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both airbag recalls, driver fastener and passenger deployment, were performed
  3. Request dealer records showing the battery software install succeeded
  4. Ask whether charging was restricted and whether the car was parked outside
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
