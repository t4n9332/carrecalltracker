Buying a used 2019 Chevrolet Bolt EV? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 175 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

This model year has six recalls, including three critical battery and seat belt pretensioner fire risk campaigns tied to charging, plus a rear brake issue, a rear door that may open while driving, and a post-crash seat belt fire risk. NHTSA has logged 175 owner complaints, including 2 crashes, 10 fires, and 5 injuries reported, though these are unverified accounts. Electrical system leads the complaint categories at 85, followed by steering (45), fuel/propulsion system (44), and unknown or other (20).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both battery fire risk recalls and any battery replacement have been completed
  3. Verify the seat belt pretensioner and rear door latch recalls have been repaired
  4. Ask about electrical system and fuel/propulsion system complaint history and repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
