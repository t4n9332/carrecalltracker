Buying a used 2021 Chevrolet Bolt EV? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 67 owner complaints on file · 3 park-outside

This model year has five recalls, four of them critical: three battery fire risk campaigns tied to charging and a seat belt pretensioner fire risk, plus a post-crash seat belt fire risk. NHTSA has logged 67 owner complaints, including 4 crashes and 1 injury reported, though these are unverified accounts. Electrical system leads the complaint categories at 33, followed by fuel/propulsion system (15), unknown or other (12), and power train (10).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the battery fire risk recalls and any battery replacement have been completed
  3. Verify the seat belt pretensioner recall repair status
  4. Ask about electrical and fuel/propulsion system complaint history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
