Buying a used 2018 Chevrolet Express Cutaway Van? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Only two recalls are listed, but both are rated critical: a wiring fire risk and a brake line that can leak. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The complaint record is essentially empty — a single unverified NHTSA report, in service brakes — which reflects the small number of these cutaway chassis on the road rather than a proven track record. On a work vehicle, upfit condition and prior use matter alongside the recall status.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN with a dealer to confirm both the wiring and brake line recalls were completed
  3. Have the brake lines and pedal feel inspected, including under the upfitted body
  4. Ask about the upfitter, prior commercial use and mileage history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
