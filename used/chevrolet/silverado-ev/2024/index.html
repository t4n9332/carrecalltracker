Buying a used 2024 Chevrolet Silverado EV? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 25 owner complaints on file

This 2024 Silverado EV has four high-severity recalls: two involve loose seat belt bolts, one addresses a center seat belt that may tear in a crash, and one covers a condition that may cause the vehicle to lose drive power. NHTSA has logged 25 owner complaints for this model year (unverified reports), including 1 crash and 1 injury. Electrical system complaints lead at 11, followed by structure at 7, unknown/other at 5, and visibility/wiper at 3.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both seat belt bolt recalls were completed
  3. Verify center seat belt tear recall repair
  4. Ask about the lose-drive-power recall fix status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
