Buying a used 2024 Chevrolet Silverado EV? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 25 owner complaints on file

Seat belts dominate this record: three separate recalls for loose front, rear and middle-rear seat belt bolts, plus one for a middle rear seat belt that can tear. A fourth high-severity recall covers GM EVs that can lose power. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. NHTSA shows 25 owner complaints, unverified, including 1 crash and 1 injury, concentrated in electrical system and structure, with a few visibility/wiper reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Have all four seat belt recalls verified by VIN, including bolt torque
  3. Inspect and tug-test the middle rear belt webbing for tearing
  4. Ask whether the truck has ever lost propulsion power, and if that recall was done
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
