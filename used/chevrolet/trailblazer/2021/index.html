Buying a used 2021 Chevrolet Trailblazer? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 192 owner complaints on file

This model year has recalls for a loss of brake assist, an emergency jack that can break, missing seat bolts, and missing tire pressure label information. NHTSA has logged 192 owner complaints, including 7 crashes, 3 fires, and 5 injuries; these are unverified consumer reports. Complaint categories are led by an unknown/other category (53), electrical system (41), power train (40), and engine (38).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake assist recall has been completed
  3. Ask whether the emergency jack and seat bolt recalls were addressed
  4. Ask about electrical system or power train repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chevrolet dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
