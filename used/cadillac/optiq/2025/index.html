Buying a used 2025 Cadillac Optiq? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 12 owner complaints on file

Two recalls apply to the 2025 Optiq: a high-severity one covering tire tread that can peel off, shared with the Chevrolet Equinox EV, and a minor administrative one for GM vehicles missing the electronic owner's manual download. Neither instructs owners to stop driving or park outside. Twelve unverified owner complaints are on file with NHTSA — one crash, no fires or injuries — spread fairly evenly across forward collision avoidance, the electrical system, unspecified issues and the service brakes.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check whether the recalled tires were replaced, and note the current tire brand and date codes
  3. Confirm the owner's manual recall notice was addressed at a dealer
  4. Test the brakes and driver-assist warnings for false or missing alerts
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
