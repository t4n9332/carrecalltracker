Buying a used 2022 Cadillac CT4? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file

This 2022 CT4 has two open recalls: one addressing side airbags that may deploy incorrectly, and one for daytime running lights that may stay on. NHTSA has logged 6 owner complaints for this model year, including 1 crash and 1 injury reported; these are unverified owner reports. Steering and service brakes each account for 4 of the complaint mentions, with electrical system and air bags also cited. Recall completion status and any lingering steering or brake concerns are worth confirming before purchase.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the side airbag deployment recall has been completed
  3. Ask whether the daytime running light recall has been addressed
  4. Have a mechanic inspect the steering and brake systems
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
