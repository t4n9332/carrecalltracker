Buying a used 2022 Cadillac CT4? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file

The recall list is shorter for 2022: side curtain airbags possibly misinstalled, rated high, plus a medium item for daytime running lights staying on. The transmission wheel-lock recall is not listed for this year. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Six unverified owner complaints are on file with NHTSA, but they include one crash and one injury, and concentrate in steering and service brakes, with a few electrical and airbag reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check the VIN for the side curtain airbag recall and ask for the repair record.
  3. On the test drive, note steering pull, play or unusual brake behavior.
  4. Ask whether the car has been in a collision or had airbag work done.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
