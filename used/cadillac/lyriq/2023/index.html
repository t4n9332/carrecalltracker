Buying a used 2023 Cadillac Lyriq? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 24 owner complaints on file

This 2023 Lyriq has seven recalls, five rated high severity: battery pack power loss, a rear seat belt anchor that may fail, brakes that may release pressure unexpectedly, front stabilizer bar bolts that may loosen, and a driver display that can go blank, plus two medium recalls for instrument panel blackout and missing pedestrian warning sounds. Owners filed 24 unverified NHTSA complaints (2 crashes, 1 injury), with exterior lighting the largest category at 10, followed by electrical system, forward collision avoidance, and service brakes complaints.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm battery pack and brake-pressure recalls have been completed
  3. Ask about exterior lighting and instrument/driver display issues
  4. Verify rear seat belt anchor recall repair was done
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
