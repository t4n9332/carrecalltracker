Buying a used 2023 Cadillac Lyriq? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 24 owner complaints on file

Seven recalls apply to the 2023 Lyriq, clustered in two areas: braking and electronics. A critical one covers loss of brake pressure from the ABS; others address a blank instrument panel (twice), battery connections that can cut drive power, a weak rear seat belt anchor, loose front sway bar bolts and missing pedestrian warning sounds. None carry stop-driving instructions. The 24 unverified NHTSA complaints lean toward exterior lighting and electrical, with two crashes and one injury reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Pull the VIN recall status and confirm the ABS brake pressure remedy is completed
  3. Drive it long enough to see whether the instrument or driver display blanks out
  4. Ask about exterior lighting problems and whether the pedestrian sound recall was done
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
