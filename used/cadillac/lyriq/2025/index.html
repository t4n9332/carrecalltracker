Buying a used 2025 Cadillac Lyriq? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 18 owner complaints on file

The LYRIQ's 2025 record is dominated by one critical recall: battery bolts that may be loose, shared with the VISTIQ. A second recall covers front sway bar bolts that can loosen; a third is paperwork only, correcting the owner's manual description of the rear head restraints. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Eighteen unverified NHTSA owner complaints cluster in the electrical system and forward collision avoidance, and include four crashes, one fire and five injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN and confirm the battery bolt recall repair is closed at a dealer
  3. Ask whether the front sway bar bolts were inspected and retorqued
  4. Test the electrical system and forward collision alerts on an extended drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
