Buying a used 2024 Cadillac Lyriq? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 113 owner complaints on file

Six recalls repeat much of the prior year: a critical loss of brake pressure from the ABS, a weak rear seat belt anchor, a power-loss fix shared with other GM EVs, loose front sway bar bolts, plus display and backup camera blanking. The complaint record is the notable part — 113 unverified NHTSA reports citing electrical, forward collision avoidance, service brakes and back-over prevention, and including 17 crashes, two fires and four injuries, a higher crash count than others listed here.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the ABS brake pressure and GM EV power-loss recalls are both completed
  3. Test the backup camera and driver display repeatedly for gray screens or blanking
  4. Ask whether forward collision or braking systems have activated unexpectedly
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
