Buying a used 2024 Cadillac Lyriq? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 113 owner complaints on file

The 2024 Lyriq has six recalls, all rated high severity: a rear seat belt anchor that may fail, an EV drive-power loss issue, brakes that may release pressure unexpectedly, front stabilizer bar bolts that may loosen, a driver display that can go blank, and a rearview camera screen that may turn gray. Owners filed 113 unverified NHTSA complaints (17 crashes, 2 fires, 4 injuries), with electrical system and forward collision avoidance each cited 37 times, followed by service brakes at 26 and back-over prevention at 19.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm EV drive-power loss recall was completed
  3. Ask about rearview camera and driver display screen issues
  4. Verify brake-pressure and seat belt anchor recalls were done
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
