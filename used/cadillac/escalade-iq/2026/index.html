Buying a used 2026 Cadillac Escalade IQ? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

Same two recalls as the extended-length version: a critical one for battery fire risk shared with the GMC Hummer EV, carrying no stop-driving or park-outside instruction, plus a low-severity fix for a missing electronic owner's manual download. Only two unverified owner complaints have reached NHTSA, both filed under visibility/wiper, with no crashes, fires or injuries. That is a very small sample for a new model, so the recall record is the more useful signal.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get dealer documentation that the battery fire risk recall work is finished
  3. Test wipers and washers through all speeds, given the two visibility complaints
  4. Check that the electronic owner's manual is present in the vehicle software
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
