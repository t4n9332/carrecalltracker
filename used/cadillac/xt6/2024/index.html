Buying a used 2024 Cadillac XT6? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 15 owner complaints on file

Recalls are lighter this year: a high-severity windshield bonding problem shared with the Acadia and XT5, plus a minor recall for dashboard lights that may not illuminate. Neither required stop-driving or parking outside. The 15 unverified NHTSA owner complaints report no crashes, fires, or injuries, but they lean toward areas the recalls don't cover — power train at seven and electrical at six, with forward collision avoidance and service brakes also appearing. Recall scope and complaint pattern diverge here.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the windshield bonding recall was inspected or the glass rebonded
  3. Confirm all dashboard warning lights illuminate at key-on, then go out
  4. Test drive for shift quality and any driver-assist or braking alerts
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
