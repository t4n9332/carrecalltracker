Buying a used 2023 Cadillac XT6? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 13 owner complaints on file

The standout is a high-severity recall covering GM SUVs that may lose power from a faulty transmission gear; the second recall is minor, an unreadable tire information label. Neither called for stop-driving or parking outside. Among 13 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA, one mentions a crash and none report fires or injuries. Power train is the leading complaint area at four reports, which lines up with the transmission recall, followed by visibility/wiper and back-over prevention.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the transmission gear recall was completed and ask if power loss ever occurred
  3. Test drive long enough to feel shifts under load and from a stop
  4. Check that the tire and loading label on the door jamb is legible
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
