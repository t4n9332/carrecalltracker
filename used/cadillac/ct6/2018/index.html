Buying a used 2018 Cadillac CT6? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 12 owner complaints on file

Both recalls here are medium severity and neither requires you to stop driving or park outside: excess glue blocking the child seat anchors, and rear seat belts that may fail to lock. Nothing engine-, brake-, or steering-related was recalled this year. The 12 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA report no crashes, fires, or injuries, but lean toward power train and engine, with some steering and fuel/propulsion reports. Both recalls are restraint-related, which matters most if children ride in back.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Try the rear LATCH anchors with an actual child seat — glue can block them.
  3. Pull each rear belt fully out to confirm it enters locking mode.
  4. Ask for engine and transmission service records, the two largest complaint areas.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
