Buying a used 2020 Cadillac CT6? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 11 owner complaints on file

Two recalls: a transmission that may lock the wheels, and accessory wheels fitted with the wrong tire pressure sensors. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, and the 2019 car's anti-lock brake and seat belt recalls do not apply. The 11 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA include two reported crashes and no fires or injuries, concentrated in power train, then service brakes, forward collision avoidance, and wheels. Power train appears in both the recall and complaint record.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the transmission recall was performed, and drive it both cold and warmed up.
  3. If it wears accessory wheels, verify the tire pressure sensors are correct and reading accurately.
  4. Ask whether any brake or forward-collision-avoidance faults have been diagnosed.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
