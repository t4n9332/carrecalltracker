Buying a used 2019 Cadillac CT6? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 16 owner complaints on file

This is the heaviest recall year for the CT6: anti-lock brakes that can be lost quietly, a transmission that may lock the wheels, rear seat belts that may not lock, and turn signals that do not shut off. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Of 16 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA, 13 concern the power train — the sharpest concentration across these CT6 years — with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get VIN confirmation that both the anti-lock brake and transmission recalls were completed.
  3. Test drive for shift quality and watch that the ABS and brake warning lights behave normally.
  4. Check that the turn signals self-cancel and that the rear belts lock when fully extended.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
