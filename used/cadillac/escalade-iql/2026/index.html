Buying a used 2026 Cadillac Escalade IQL? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file

This model year carries a critical recall for battery fire risk, along with a lower-severity issue involving a missing electronic owner's manual. Owners filed 3 complaints with NHTSA, unverified reports, touching electrical system (2), and single mentions each for unknown or other, engine, and lane departure categories. One crash was reported among these complaints, with no fires or injuries noted.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the battery fire risk recall has been completed
  3. Verify the electronic owner's manual is accessible
  4. Ask about any electrical system faults or repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
