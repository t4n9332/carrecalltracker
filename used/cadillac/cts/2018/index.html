Buying a used 2018 Cadillac CTS? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 8 owner complaints on file

Both recalls for this year involve steering: one for possible loss of power steering on the CTS and other GM cars, another for loss of electric power steering assist. Both are high severity, though neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Owner complaints are few — eight unverified reports to NHTSA, one crash, no fires or injuries — split mainly between power train and electrical system. One failure mode dominates the recall record here rather than a spread of unrelated problems.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify by VIN that both power steering recalls were completed.
  3. Drive at parking-lot and highway speeds, watching for sudden changes in steering effort or a steering warning.
  4. Ask about electrical faults and transmission service, the top complaint areas.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
