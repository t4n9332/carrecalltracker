Buying a used 2019 Cadillac XTS? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

Two recalls: a high-severity issue with front seat belt buckles missing a rivet, and a medium recall covering GM cars and SUVs with weak rear brakes. Neither carried a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Complaint volume is low — five unverified reports filed with NHTSA, one citing a crash, none citing fires or injuries — spread thinly across power train, engine, air bags, and seats with one report each. The safety-restraint recall is the item to document.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get written confirmation the front seat belt buckle recall was completed
  3. Ask when rear brakes were last serviced and whether the brake recall was performed
  4. Latch and tug both front seat belts and check for any air bag warning light
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
