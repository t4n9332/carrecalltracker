Buying a used 2022 Cadillac CT5? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 11 owner complaints on file

This 2022 CT5 has two open recalls: side airbags that may deploy incorrectly, and daytime running lights that may stay on. NHTSA has logged 11 owner complaints for this model year, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported; these are unverified owner accounts. Structure accounts for 3 of the complaints, while tires, electrical system, and air bags each account for 2. Confirming whether both recalls have been completed and asking about any tire or structural issues noted by owners is worth doing.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the side airbag recall has been completed
  3. Ask whether the DRL recall has been addressed
  4. Check tires and look for any structural repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
