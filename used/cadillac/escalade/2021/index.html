Buying a used 2021 Cadillac Escalade? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 87 owner complaints on file

This is the heaviest recall year of the group — nine campaigns, including two critical ones that apply only to diesel models: rear wheels that can lock and a fuel leak. Gas versions face the 6.2L V8 failure recall, a fuel pump that can stall the engine, a rear driveshaft that may break, and two separate third-row seat belt fixes. None carry stop-driving instructions. Of 87 unverified NHTSA complaints, 48 cite the engine; three crashes, two fires and two injuries were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Identify whether it is the diesel or 6.2L V8, then match it to the right open recalls
  3. Have both third-row seat belt recalls verified as completed, and inspect the belts
  4. Ask about any stalling, driveshaft noise, or airbag warning light behavior
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
