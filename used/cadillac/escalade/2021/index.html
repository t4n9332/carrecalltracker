Buying a used 2021 Cadillac Escalade? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 87 owner complaints on file

This model year carries nine recalls, including a wrong fuel tank installation, third-row seat belts that may break, a fuel pump that may stall the engine, driveshaft failure, seat belt buckle rivet flaws, daytime running lights that may not turn off, rear wheel lock-up, possible engine failure, and an inconsistent airbag warning light. Owners filed 87 complaints with NHTSA, unverified reports, led by engine issues (48), with power train, engine cooling, and electrical system each at 10. These complaints include 3 crashes, 2 fires, and 2 injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel tank and fuel pump recalls have been completed
  3. Verify the third-row seat belt and buckle rivet recalls were done
  4. Ask about driveshaft, engine, or rear wheel lock-up repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
