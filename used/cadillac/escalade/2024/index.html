Buying a used 2024 Cadillac Escalade? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 46 owner complaints on file

Only two recalls are listed for this year, both high severity: instrument clusters that may not display a brake fluid warning, and 6.2L V8 engines that may fail. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Engine reports dominate the unverified complaint file — 33 of 46 filed with NHTSA — followed by power train, electrical and fuel system, echoing the engine recall. No crashes or fires appear in those reports; one injury is listed.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the 6.2L V8 recall inspection was completed and what the result was
  3. Verify the brake fluid warning software update is on the vehicle's recall record
  4. Request records of any engine, power train or fuel system repairs under warranty
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
