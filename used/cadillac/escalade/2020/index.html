Buying a used 2020 Cadillac Escalade? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 14 owner complaints on file

Two recalls stand out for this year: a critical one for a fuel pump leak that creates fire risk, and a high-severity campaign over potentially defective Continental tires — meaning the original tires may or may not still be on the vehicle. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Fourteen unverified complaints reached NHTSA, led by engine and power train, plus a few on forward collision avoidance; two crashes and one fire were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump recall repair was done and check for fuel odor or leaks
  3. Inspect tire brand and DOT dates to see whether the recalled Continental tires remain
  4. Ask whether forward collision alerts have ever triggered falsely or failed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
