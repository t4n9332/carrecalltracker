Buying a used 2022 Cadillac Escalade? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 82 owner complaints on file

Four recalls: a critical one for rear wheels that may lock, high-severity fixes for a third-row seat belt flaw and for 6.2L V8 engines that may fail, and a medium item for daytime running lights that stay on. None carry stop-driving or park-outside instructions. What stands out is the complaint mix — 64 of 82 unverified NHTSA reports name the engine, with power train next. Four crashes, one fire and three injuries appear in those reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the 6.2L V8 recall inspection passed or an engine was replaced
  3. Verify the rear wheel lock and third-row seat belt recalls are closed out
  4. Listen for engine ticking or knocking on a cold start during the test drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
