Buying a used 2020 Cadillac XT5? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 22 owner complaints on file

Five recalls apply to the 2020 XT5. The critical one involves a fuel pump that can stall the engine, affecting several GM SUVs. Two separate recalls cover potentially faulty Continental tires, and medium-severity ones address weak roof airbags and a rearview camera that fails. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. The 22 unverified owner complaints to NHTSA report no crashes or fires, and concentrate in the power train and steering, with engine issues also noted.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump recall repair was done and ask about any stalling history
  3. Check the tires against both Continental recalls, or confirm replacement
  4. Drive for steering and drivetrain symptoms, and test the rearview camera
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
