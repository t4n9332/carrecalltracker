Buying a used 2024 Cadillac XT5? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 10 owner complaints on file

The 2024 XT5 carries two high-severity recalls — a windshield bonding problem shared with the GMC Acadia and XT6, and a driver airbag that may tear when deploying — plus a low-severity one for dashboard lights that may not illuminate. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Only 10 unverified owner complaints have reached NHTSA, though they include one crash and one injury; power train is the leading area, with engine and air bag reports behind it.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the windshield was re-bonded or replaced under the recall
  3. Confirm the driver airbag recall was completed and no airbag light is showing
  4. Check dashboard illumination at startup and drive for any power train hesitation
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
