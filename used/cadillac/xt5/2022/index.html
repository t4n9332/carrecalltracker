Buying a used 2022 Cadillac XT5? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 10 owner complaints on file

Three recalls cover the 2022 XT5. The critical one is a fuel tank valve that leaks, shared with the XT6 and GMC Acadia; the other two are high severity — a rear suspension bolt issue and a faulty transmission gear that can cause a loss of power. None tells owners to stop driving or park outside. Ten unverified NHTSA complaints are on record, including two crashes and one injury, spread across power train, air bags and back-over prevention.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel tank valve recall repair is closed for this VIN
  3. Ask whether the rear suspension bolt and transmission gear recalls were performed
  4. Drive at varied speeds watching for power loss, and check airbag and camera warnings
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
