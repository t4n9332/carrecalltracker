Buying a used 2021 Cadillac Escalade ESV? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 88 owner complaints on file

This year carries the longest recall list of any Escalade ESV here — ten, including a critical rear-wheel lock-up issue on diesels, third-row seat belts that may break (two separate recalls), loss of power steering assist, a rear driveshaft that may break, a fuel pump that can stall the engine, wrong seat belt bolts, and 6.2L V8 engines that may fail. Of 88 unverified NHTSA complaints, 58 involve the engine; one fire, three crashes, and three injuries were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Pull a full VIN recall report — ten recalls apply, including two separate third-row seat belt recalls.
  3. Confirm which engine it has; the 6.2L V8 failure recall and the heavy engine complaints matter most there.
  4. If it is the diesel, verify the rear wheel lock-up recall repair was done.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
