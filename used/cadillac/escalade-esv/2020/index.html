Buying a used 2020 Cadillac Escalade ESV? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 21 owner complaints on file

This 2020 Escalade ESV has two recalls: an over-cured tire failure risk, and a fuel pump over-pressurization issue tied to fire risk. NHTSA has logged 21 owner complaints for this model year, including 3 crashes and 3 injuries reported; these are unverified owner accounts. Engine accounts for 14 of the 21 complaints, with air bags and wheels each cited 4 times and fuel/propulsion system cited 3 times. Checking tire manufacture dates and fuel pump recall completion, along with engine history, is worth doing.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check tire manufacture dates against the over-cured tire recall
  3. Confirm the fuel pump recall has been completed
  4. Have the engine inspected and ask about repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
