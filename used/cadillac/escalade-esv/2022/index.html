Buying a used 2022 Cadillac Escalade ESV? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 55 owner complaints on file

This model year has four recalls: a third-row seat belt buckle rivet flaw, a possible engine failure, rear wheel lockup, and daytime running lights that may stay on. Owners filed 55 complaints with NHTSA, which are unverified reports; engine problems were cited most often (42), followed by power train (12), fuel/propulsion system (8), and electrical system (7). Among these complaints, 1 crash and 2 injuries were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the third-row seat belt buckle rivet recall has been completed
  3. Ask about any engine performance or failure issues and repair history
  4. Confirm the rear wheel lockup recall was addressed by a dealer
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
