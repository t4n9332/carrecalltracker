Buying a used 2024 Cadillac Escalade ESV? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 22 owner complaints on file

Two open GM recalls apply here: a missing brake fluid level warning in the instrument cluster, and a 6.2L V8 that may fail. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Of 22 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA, 14 name the engine, with power train, engine cooling and service brakes behind it — an echo of the recall subjects rather than a separate issue. One crash, no fires or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof the 6.2L V8 recall inspection or engine replacement was completed by a dealer
  3. Confirm the brake fluid warning recall software fix was applied; test that cluster warnings display
  4. Request service records for any engine or power train repairs already performed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Cadillac dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
