Buying a used 2018 Jeep Cherokee? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 683 owner complaints on file

Seven recalls, several of them serious: a fuel tube that may leak into the engine, an oil pump failure that can stall the engine, cruise control that may stick on, a front axle shaft that can break, an engine computer stalling defect, and a power transfer unit failure with rollaway risk. Owners filed 683 unverified complaints with NHTSA, including 11 crashes, five fires and seven injuries, concentrated in the power train, electrical system and engine.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get written proof each recall was performed, especially the fuel tube, oil pump and power transfer unit.
  3. Ask about stalling history and any transmission or axle repairs; look and smell for fuel or oil leaks.
  4. Test cruise control engaging and cancelling, and confirm the vehicle holds in Park on a slope.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
