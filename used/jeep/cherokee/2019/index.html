Buying a used 2019 Jeep Cherokee? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 1,633 owner complaints on file

This model year has ten recalls, all rated high severity, covering a 2.0L engine component failure risk, a transmission weld risk, stalling, a rear brake problem, transmission calibration causing stalling, instrument cluster failure, loose passenger seat mounting bolts, airbag crash sensor failure, a steering rack gear that may break, and drivetrain failure that can cause rollaway. NHTSA logged 1,633 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 40 crashes, 19 fires, and 37 injuries. Power train leads at 796, followed by engine at 456, electrical system at 339, and unknown/other at 255.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm which of the ten recalls have been completed and request documentation
  3. Test the steering, transmission, and brakes for any unusual behavior during a test drive
  4. Ask specifically about airbag, seat mounting, and instrument cluster repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
