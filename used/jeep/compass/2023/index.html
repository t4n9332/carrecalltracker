Buying a used 2023 Jeep Compass? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 45 owner complaints on file

Two recalls apply to this model year: a dashboard display that may be too dim and a rearview camera that may not display. NHTSA has logged 45 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year, including 2 crashes and 1 injury, with no fires reported. Electrical system (13) is the top complaint category, followed by forward collision avoidance (7), engine (7), and steering (6).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the dashboard display recall was completed
  3. Test the rearview camera function
  4. Check the forward collision avoidance system and engine operation
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
