Buying a used 2018 Jeep Compass? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 937 owner complaints on file

This model year has five high-severity recalls: an oil pump that may fail, a passenger airbag with loose mounting nuts, a front control arm welding defect, a stalling issue, and a rear brake problem, plus a medium-severity instrument panel dimming defect. NHTSA logged 937 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 28 crashes, 5 fires, and 18 injuries. Engine complaints lead at 470, followed by electrical system at 375, unknown/other at 193, and power train at 151.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all five high-severity recalls have been completed, especially the control arm and airbag mounting fixes
  3. Ask about any engine or electrical system repairs, the top two complaint categories
  4. Check for any stalling or brake-related repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
