Buying a used 2025 Jeep Wagoneer S? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 51 owner complaints on file

Six recalls apply, four rated serious: the rollaway risk from the electrics, trailer lights and brakes that may fail, a display failure, and a wrong dashboard support beam — a structural build error. Headlight aiming and a liftgate hinge cover round out the list. NHTSA holds 51 unverified owner reports for this year with no crashes, fires, or injuries; electrical system leads by a wide margin, followed by service brakes, forward collision avoidance, and power train.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the dashboard support beam recall was inspected or repaired
  3. Verify the rollaway, display, and trailer wiring recalls by VIN
  4. Test brake feel and check for stored electrical or brake fault codes
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
