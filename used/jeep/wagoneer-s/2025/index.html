Buying a used 2025 Jeep Wagoneer S? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 52 owner complaints on file

This 2025 Wagoneer S carries six recalls, four high-severity: risk of the vehicle rolling away, an incorrect cross beam, a trailer tow module failure, and a display that may fail; two medium-severity, covering headlights that may not aim correctly and a liftgate hinge cover that may detach. NHTSA has logged 51 unverified owner complaints for this model year, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. Electrical system complaints lead at 36, followed by service brakes at 14, forward collision avoidance at 9, and power train at 8.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rollaway-risk recall was completed
  3. Ask about the cross beam and trailer tow module recalls
  4. Check whether the display failure recall was fixed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
