Buying a used 2024 Jeep Wagoneer? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 51 owner complaints on file

This 2024 Wagoneer carries eight recalls, three high-severity: two separate rearview camera failures and an air bag that may not deploy. The other five, medium-severity, cover a third-row seat belt that won't fasten, loose door and window trim, a tire pressure warning system that may miss low tires, and roof rack crossbars that may come loose. NHTSA logged 50 unverified owner complaints, including 2 crashes, no fires, and 2 injuries. Engine complaints lead at 28, electrical system at 23, unknown or other at 14, and power train at 11.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm which of the eight recalls have been completed
  3. Ask about the rearview camera and air bag recalls specifically
  4. Check service records for engine repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
