Buying a used 2024 Jeep Wagoneer? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 50 owner complaints on file

Seven recalls, one serious — a driver airbag that may not deploy. The others repeat familiar Wagoneer items: two backup camera failures, third-row seat belts that may not fasten, door and quarter window trim that can detach while driving, and a tire warning light that may fail. NHTSA holds 50 unverified owner reports for this year, with two crashes and two injuries; engine is the top complaint area, followed by electrical system and power train.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check VIN status on the driver airbag non-deployment recall
  3. Inflate or deflate a tire to confirm the tire warning light responds
  4. Inspect door and quarter window trim for loose or replaced panels
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
