Buying a used 2022 Jeep Wagoneer? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 201 owner complaints on file

This 2022 Wagoneer carries nine recalls, four high-severity, including air bags that may disable, pillar trim that can block the side curtain airbag, and two separate rearview camera display failures. The other five, medium-severity, cover seat, seat belt, and trim issues, including door and window trim that can detach. NHTSA has logged 201 unverified owner complaints for this model year, including 12 crash mentions, 3 fires, and 5 injuries. Electrical system complaints lead at 116, followed by engine at 52, service brakes at 38, and unknown or other at 31.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm which of the nine recalls have been completed
  3. Ask about the rearview camera display recalls specifically
  4. Check service records for electrical system repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
