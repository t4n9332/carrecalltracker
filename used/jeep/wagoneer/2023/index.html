Buying a used 2023 Jeep Wagoneer? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 106 owner complaints on file

Eight recalls apply, two serious: pillar trim that can block a side curtain airbag, and a driver airbag that may not deploy. The others cover backup camera blackouts, third-row seat belts that may not fasten, a gear display that can vanish, and door and quarter window trim that can come off while driving. Owners filed 106 unverified reports with NHTSA, including five crashes and two injuries; engine leads the complaint areas, just ahead of electrical system and power train.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the driver airbag and pillar trim recalls are completed by VIN
  3. Confirm the gear position display stays visible while shifting
  4. Ask for engine service history given engine is the top complaint area
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
