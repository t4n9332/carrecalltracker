Buying a used 2021 Jeep Gladiator? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 290 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

2021 adds the critical power-steering wiring fire recall — the only item here instructing owners to park outside until repaired — to two critical manual-clutch overheat recalls. High-severity items cover the diesel fuel pump (two separate recalls) and unexpected engine stalling, with the dash cluster and a blanking backup camera rated medium. Of 290 unverified NHTSA complaints, steering leads at 94, followed by power train (70) and engine (53); seven crashes, three fires and seven injuries are reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the power-steering wiring recall is closed — it carries a park-outside warning.
  3. If it is the diesel, verify both fuel pump recalls and ask about any stalling history.
  4. Ask whether it is a manual and whether the clutch recall work was performed.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
