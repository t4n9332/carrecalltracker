Buying a used 2022 Jeep Grand Wagoneer? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 59 owner complaints on file

This model year carries nine recalls, including high-severity issues with air bags that may disable, pillar trim that can block the side curtain air bag, and rearview cameras that may fail or not display. Medium-severity recalls cover the second-row seat lock, third-row seat belt buckle, door trim, and window trim piece. NHTSA has logged 59 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 2 crashes, 1 fire, and 6 injuries. Electrical system complaints lead at 21, followed by steering (12), service brakes (9), and engine (9).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Test the rearview camera and confirm the air bag disable recall was completed
  3. Check pillar trim installation and third-row seat belt buckle function
  4. Inspect door trim and window trim pieces, and test the second-row seat lock
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
