Buying a used 2022 Jeep Grand Wagoneer? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 59 owner complaints on file

Nine recalls, mostly early-production execution problems: three separate backup camera display recalls, pillar trim that can block a side curtain airbag, airbags that may shut off, a center seat back that may not lock, third-row belts that may not fasten, and door and quarter window trim that can come off while driving. Owners filed 59 unverified NHTSA complaints — electrical 21, steering 12, brakes and engine nine each — including two crashes, one fire and six injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN for all nine recalls; the three backup camera recalls were separate repairs
  3. Latch and tug every third-row seat belt and confirm the center seat back locks
  4. Inspect door and quarter window trim for loose clips, and check the reverse camera image
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
