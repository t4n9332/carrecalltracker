Buying a used 2024 Jeep Grand Wagoneer? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 33 owner complaints on file

The recall list is shorter than 2022–23 but familiar: a driver airbag that may not deploy is the most serious, alongside two backup camera display recalls, third-row seat belts that may not fasten, door and quarter window trim that can detach while driving, and a tire warning light that may fail. Of 33 unverified NHTSA complaints, 20 involve the electrical system and 13 the engine; one crash is reported, with no fires or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get VIN recall status on the driver airbag non-deployment recall first
  3. Cycle into reverse repeatedly to confirm the camera displays consistently
  4. Check third-row belt latches, trim security, and that the tire warning light works at startup
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
