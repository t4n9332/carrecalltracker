Buying a used 2023 Jeep Grand Wagoneer? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 45 owner complaints on file

Seven recalls carry over the Wagoneer trim and camera issues, and two involve airbags: pillar trim that can block the side curtain airbag, and a driver airbag that may not deploy. Backup camera display, third-row seat belt latching, and detaching door and quarter window trim repeat from 2022. NHTSA lists 45 unverified owner complaints, led by electrical (15) and engine (13), with 10 on forward collision avoidance; two crashes and one injury reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm by VIN that both airbag recalls — pillar trim and driver airbag deployment — are closed
  3. Shift into reverse several times to verify the backup camera image appears every time
  4. Buckle all third-row belts and check door and quarter window trim attachment
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
