Buying a used 2023 Jeep Grand Cherokee L? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 91 owner complaints on file

Recalls for this vehicle include a side air bag connector that may fail, a blower motor that may fail and disable the defroster, head restraints that may not lock, two separate rear coil spring detachment recalls, and side air bags that may deploy late. NHTSA has logged 91 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 3 crashes, 2 fires, and 0 injuries. The most common complaint categories are suspension (36), electrical system (23), forward collision avoidance (12), and unknown/other (8).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the side air bag connector and deploy-late recalls were addressed
  3. Ask about the blower motor/defroster recall and both coil spring recalls
  4. Test the head restraint locking mechanism
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
