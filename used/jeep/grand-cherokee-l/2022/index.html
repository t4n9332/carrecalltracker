Buying a used 2022 Jeep Grand Cherokee L? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 218 owner complaints on file

The 2022 Grand Cherokee L carries the same four recalls as 2021: two critical ones for rear coil springs that can fall off, a high-severity side airbag non-deployment recall, and a backup camera screen that may go blank. What differs is the complaint record — 218 unverified NHTSA reports, nearly triple the prior year, with 75 in electrical system and 61 in forward collision avoidance. Eleven crashes and nine injuries are reported, no fires. Driver-assist and electrical behavior are the recurring owner themes.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether both rear spring recalls were completed and inspect the rear suspension.
  3. Drive-test automatic emergency braking for false or phantom activations.
  4. Confirm the side airbag recall repair and watch for electrical or screen glitches.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
