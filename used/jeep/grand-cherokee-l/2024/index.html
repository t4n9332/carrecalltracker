Buying a used 2024 Jeep Grand Cherokee L? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 41 owner complaints on file

The one critical recall is loose radiator bolts, which the notice ties to possible airbag and fuel problems; two high-severity recalls again cover side airbags not deploying or deploying too late. Most of the rest are display and instrument issues: speedometer accuracy, backup camera, gear display, and a tire warning light. Electrical leads the 41 unverified NHTSA complaints at 18, ahead of forward collision avoidance, engine, and brakes. Those 41 reports include four crashes and five injuries. No stop-driving or park-outside warnings.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof the radiator bolt recall repair was performed
  3. Confirm both side airbag recalls are closed for this VIN
  4. Drive it and watch the speedometer, gear display, backup camera and tire light
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
