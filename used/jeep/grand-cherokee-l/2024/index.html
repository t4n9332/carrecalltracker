Buying a used 2024 Jeep Grand Cherokee L? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 41 owner complaints on file

This vehicle has a critical recall for radiator crossmember bolts that may be loose, plus recalls for a speedometer software error, a side air bag connector that may fail, a rearview camera image that may not show, head restraints that may not lock, side air bags that may deploy late, a gear display that may not appear, and a tire pressure warning system that may miss low tires. NHTSA logged 41 owner complaints (unverified), including 4 crashes, 0 fires, and 5 injuries; electrical system (18) is the top complaint category.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the radiator crossmember bolt recall was completed
  3. Ask about the air bag connector, camera, and gear display recalls
  4. Test the tire pressure warning system and speedometer
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
