Buying a used 2025 Jeep Grand Cherokee L? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 32 owner complaints on file

This year has the lightest recall record of the Grand Cherokee L run listed here: one high-severity recall for side airbags that may deploy too late, plus a low-severity tire warning light failure. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Owner reports are also fewer at 32, though half fall under unknown or other (16), with electrical (12), power train (7), and forward collision avoidance (7) behind it. Those unverified NHTSA reports include five crashes and one injury — a high crash share for the volume.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check VIN status on the side airbag timing recall — a newer car may still be unrepaired
  3. Confirm the tire pressure warning light illuminates and clears at startup
  4. Test drive for drivetrain hesitation and any driver-assist or collision-warning faults
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
