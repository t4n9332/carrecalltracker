Buying a used 2023 Jeep Grand Cherokee? Check these 21 recalls first

21 NHTSA recalls · 384 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year carries a large recall list — ten in total, including a critical battery fire risk recall that advises parking outside, plus high-severity recalls covering the steering shaft, rear coil springs, ball joints, air bag deployment, rearview camera, side air bag connector, blower motor, and head restraints. NHTSA logged 384 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 14 crashes, 3 fires, and 17 injuries. Electrical system complaints lead at 177, followed by engine (83), unknown/other (63), and suspension (60).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the battery fire recall was completed and ask whether the vehicle is parked outdoors as advised
  3. Ask if the steering shaft, ball joint, and rear coil spring recalls have been inspected or repaired
  4. Test the rearview camera and blower motor/defroster, and confirm the air bag recall was completed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
