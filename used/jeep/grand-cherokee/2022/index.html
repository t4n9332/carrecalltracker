Buying a used 2022 Jeep Grand Cherokee? Check these 13 recalls first

13 NHTSA recalls · 368 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Four critical recalls: a front wheel that can fall outward, rear springs that can fall off (covered by two separate recalls), and a 4xe plug-in hybrid battery fire risk — the only item here that tells owners to park outside. High-severity recalls cover side airbags not deploying or deploying late, plus loss of driving power on plug-in hybrids. Electrical is far and away the top complaint area, 167 of 368 unverified NHTSA reports. Those reports include 13 crashes and seven injuries, with no fires recorded.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Determine whether it is a 4xe plug-in hybrid, and if so confirm the battery fire recall repair
  3. Verify the front wheel and both rear spring recalls are closed by VIN
  4. Test the backup camera, defroster and side airbag warning light before buying
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
