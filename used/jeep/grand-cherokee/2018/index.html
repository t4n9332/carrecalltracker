Buying a used 2018 Jeep Grand Cherokee? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 853 owner complaints on file

This year carries the heaviest record here: ten recalls and 853 unverified NHTSA complaints including 42 crashes, 10 fires, and 33 injuries. Critical items are a Trackhawk fuel line that can separate and leak, a diesel EGR cooler that can crack and cause fire, and vehicles that can roll out of Park — with a second Park-related recall behind it. Others cover cruise control sticking on, engine computer stalling, diesel fuel pump failure and stalling, and floor mats catching the gas pedal. Electrical dominates complaints at 339.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Identify the exact engine — Trackhawk fuel line and the diesel EGR, pump and stall recalls apply only to specific versions
  3. Get VIN recall status for both roll-away/Park recalls and the cruise control and engine computer recalls
  4. Confirm the correct factory floor mats are installed and secured, not aftermarket or stacked
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
