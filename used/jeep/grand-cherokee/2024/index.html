Buying a used 2024 Jeep Grand Cherokee? Check these 16 recalls first

16 NHTSA recalls · 241 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Critical recalls cover a steering knuckle that can break and let a wheel come off, loose radiator bolts tied to airbag and fuel problems, and a 4xe battery fire risk with a park-outside warning. Five high-severity recalls follow, four of them airbag- or restraint-related: missing bumper foam affecting deployment, child seat anchor welds, side airbags not deploying or deploying late, plus plug-in hybrid power loss. Electrical (109) and engine (103) nearly tie among 241 unverified NHTSA complaints, which include 10 crashes and no fires.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering knuckle recall repair in writing and inspect for uneven tire wear or steering noise
  3. Ask whether it is a 4xe, and if so whether the battery fire recall was completed
  4. Verify the radiator bolt, bumper foam and side airbag recalls are closed, and test the defroster and high beams
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
