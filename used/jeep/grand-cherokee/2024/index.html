Buying a used 2024 Jeep Grand Cherokee? Check these 16 recalls first

16 NHTSA recalls · 241 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year has two critical recalls — loose radiator crossmember bolts and a battery fire risk that advises parking outside — along with eight high-severity recalls spanning the steering knuckle, child seat tether welds, speedometer display, side air bag connector, rearview camera, head restraints, side air bag timing, and gear display. NHTSA has logged 241 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 10 crashes and 2 injuries, with no fires reported. Electrical system (109) and engine (103) lead the complaint categories, followed by fuel/propulsion system (42) and unknown/other (30).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the radiator crossmember bolt and battery fire recalls were completed, and ask about outdoor parking
  3. Check whether the steering knuckle and child-seat tether weld recalls were completed
  4. Test the rearview camera, speedometer display, and gear display for the reported issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
