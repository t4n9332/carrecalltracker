Buying a used 2019 Jeep Grand Cherokee? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 365 owner complaints on file

This vehicle has critical recalls for a diesel EGR cooler that may crack and a rollaway risk shared with the Dodge Durango, plus recalls for power loss, diesel fuel pump failure, diesel engine stalling, a brake module fault, and a backup camera image that may stay on screen. NHTSA has logged 365 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 18 crashes, 4 fires, and 10 injuries. Service brakes (116) is the top complaint category, followed by electrical system (82), unknown/other (76), and hydraulic (64).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the diesel EGR cooler and rollaway recalls were completed
  3. Ask about the brake module fault and diesel fuel pump/stall recalls
  4. Have the brakes and parking mechanism inspected
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
