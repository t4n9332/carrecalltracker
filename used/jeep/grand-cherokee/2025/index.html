Buying a used 2025 Jeep Grand Cherokee? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 73 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year has one critical recall — a battery fire risk that advises parking outside — plus a high-severity recall for side air bags that may deploy late, and two medium-severity recalls covering PHEV drive power loss and a tire pressure warning system that may miss low tires. NHTSA has logged 72 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 8 crashes and 8 injuries, with no fires reported. Electrical system complaints lead at 29, followed by engine (20), unknown/other (13), and forward collision avoidance (11).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the battery fire recall was completed and ask about outdoor parking
  3. Ask whether the tire pressure monitoring system has been checked for accuracy
  4. If it's a PHEV, ask whether the drive-power-loss recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
