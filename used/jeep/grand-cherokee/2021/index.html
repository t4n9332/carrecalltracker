Buying a used 2021 Jeep Grand Cherokee? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 403 owner complaints on file

Three critical recalls concern parts coming apart: a steering shaft, rear coil springs, and a front wheel that can fall outward. Steering is also the second-largest owner-complaint area, 102 of 403 unverified NHTSA reports, behind electrical at 114. High-severity recalls cover airbags shutting off, a missing child seat anchor weld, and loss of engine power. Four medium recalls involve lighting and the backup camera. Those complaints include 20 crashes, eight fires, and 13 injuries; no recall requires stop-driving or parking outside.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get written confirmation the steering shaft, coil spring and front wheel recalls were repaired
  3. If carrying children, verify the child seat anchor weld recall status and test the anchors
  4. Check high beams, tail light markers, headlights and the backup camera all work
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
