Buying a used 2024 Jeep Grand Cherokee 4XE? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 23 owner complaints on file

Both critical recalls on the 2024 Grand Cherokee 4xe involve fire: high-voltage batteries that can catch fire, shared with the Wrangler 4xe, and an engine that may fail or catch fire. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. A tire pressure warning light that may fail is the only minor item. The 23 unverified NHTSA complaints split mainly between electrical system (12) and engine (11) — the same systems the recalls cover — with one crash, two fires and one injury.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both fire-related recalls, battery and engine, are closed for this VIN.
  3. Ask whether the hybrid battery pack or engine has been repaired or replaced.
  4. Verify the tire pressure warning light works and have stored fault codes scanned.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
