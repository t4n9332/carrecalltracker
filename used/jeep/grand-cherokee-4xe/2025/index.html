Buying a used 2025 Jeep Grand Cherokee 4XE? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

The 2025 4xe drops the battery fire recall that applies to 2024, leaving one critical action — an engine that may fail or catch fire — plus a low-rated recall for a tire pressure warning light that may fail. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. With just two unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA, one of which mentions a fire, there is almost no field record to read for this year; the recall itself is the main documented issue.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check VIN recall status for the engine fire recall and whether repair was done.
  3. Ask about engine warning lights, stalling, or any burning smell.
  4. Confirm the tire pressure warning light illuminates and clears at start-up.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
