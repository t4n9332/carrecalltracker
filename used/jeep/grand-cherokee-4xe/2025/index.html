Buying a used 2025 Jeep Grand Cherokee 4XE? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

This model year carries two recalls: a critical engine failure risk from debris and a tire pressure warning system that may miss low tires. NHTSA logged 2 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year, including 1 fire, with no crashes or injuries reported. Complaint categories are spread across engine, electrical system, vehicle speed control, and service brakes, each with 1 complaint on file.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine debris-related recall was completed
  3. Test the tire pressure warning system accuracy
  4. Ask the seller about the reported fire-related complaint
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
