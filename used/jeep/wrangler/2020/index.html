Buying a used 2020 Jeep Wrangler? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 609 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Ten recalls, seven critical, clustering around fire and fuel: a leaking fuel line, a frame stud that can puncture the fuel tank, three clutch overheating recalls, and a 4xe battery fire risk that carries a park-outside warning. A front control arm may also separate from the axle. Of 609 unverified owner reports to NHTSA, 16 involve crashes and 12 fires; steering and electrical system lead, with engine behind them. The fuel and fire items stand out most.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. If it is a 4xe, confirm the battery fire recall repair and park-outside status
  3. Verify the fuel line and frame stud fuel tank recalls by VIN
  4. Inspect front control arm mounts and check for fuel odor underneath
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
