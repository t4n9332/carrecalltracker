Buying a used 2020 Jeep Wrangler? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 609 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This 2020 Wrangler has five critical recalls: two clutch overheating/fracture campaigns, a fuel line crack, a frame stud that may puncture the fuel tank, and a battery fire risk (shared with the Grand Cherokee) with a park-outside advisory. High-severity recalls cover a control arm that may detach, a rearview camera image that may not show, a diesel fuel pump that can stall the engine, and camera/brake light failure. NHTSA logged 609 owner complaints for this model year, including 16 crashes, 12 fires, and 7 injuries; steering and electrical system are the most-cited areas. These are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm battery fire recall (park-outside advisory) was completed
  3. Verify fuel line/frame stud fuel tank recalls were repaired to rule out leaks
  4. Ask about clutch overheating recall status and any steering complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
