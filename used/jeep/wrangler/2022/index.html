Buying a used 2022 Jeep Wrangler? Check these 15 recalls first

15 NHTSA recalls · 383 owner complaints on file · 3 park-outside

Recall exposure mirrors 2021 but the mix shifts: the frame stud fuel-tank puncture and manual clutch fire recalls carry over, the power steering wiring and 4xe battery fire recalls still call for parking outside, and two recalls address a plug-in hybrid battery fuse that can overheat. 4xe models are also covered for shutting off while driving. NHTSA logged 383 owner complaints, unverified, with electrical (162) the largest area, alongside 11 fire and 13 crash reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify by VIN that both 4xe battery fire recalls and the power steering wiring recall are closed
  3. On a 4xe, ask whether the battery fuse overheat recalls were done and whether the vehicle has ever shut off while driving
  4. Confirm the frame stud recall repair, and on a diesel ask about the fuel pump recall
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
