Buying a used 2021 Jeep Wrangler? Check these 15 recalls first

15 NHTSA recalls · 913 owner complaints on file · 3 park-outside

This 2021 Wrangler has five critical recalls: clutch overheating/fracture, a frame stud that may puncture the fuel tank, a hybrid battery fire risk, a battery fire risk shared with the Grand Cherokee, and a steering pump fire risk - the last three carry park-outside advisories. High-severity items include a rearview camera that may not show, a diesel fuel pump that can stall the engine, a seat belt pretensioner that may fail, and a 4xe engine that can shut down. NHTSA logged 913 complaints for this model year, including 20 crashes, 27 fires, and 14 injuries; electrical system and steering lead. Unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm hybrid battery, battery, and steering pump fire-risk recalls (park-outside advisories) were completed
  3. If 4xe/hybrid, verify engine shutdown and battery-related recall repairs
  4. Ask about clutch overheating recall status and any electrical system complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
