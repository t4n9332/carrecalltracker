Buying a used 2019 Jeep Wrangler? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 737 owner complaints on file

This 2019 Wrangler has recalls spanning three critical items - steering wheel detachment risk and two clutch overheating/fracture campaigns - plus high-severity issues covering a front track bar bracket weld defect, front seat belts that may not lock, a clutch overheating issue, and camera/brake light failure, with medium recalls for a backup camera image that stays on screen and instrument panel cluster failure. NHTSA has logged 737 owner complaints for this model year, including 9 crashes, 10 fires, and 8 injuries; more than half concern steering, with electrical system, suspension, and powertrain also common. These are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering wheel detachment and clutch overheat recalls were repaired
  3. Test seat belt locking and inspect front track bar bracket weld
  4. Check camera/brake light function and instrument cluster operation
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
