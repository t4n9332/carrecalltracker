Buying a used 2018 Jeep Wrangler? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 1,815 owner complaints on file

This 2018 Wrangler has ten recalls, including two critical campaigns for clutch overheating and fracture, plus high-severity issues covering frame rail corrosion, an incorrect park lock rod, cruise control that may not cancel, a steering shaft weld defect, unexpected stalling, a track bar bracket weld defect, a rooftop tent cover, and a seat belt buckle. NHTSA has logged 1,815 owner complaints for this model year, including 22 crashes, 25 fires, and 13 injuries; more than half concern steering, with suspension, electrical, and unspecified other categories also frequent. These are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm clutch overheating/fracture recall repair status
  3. Ask about steering component history and inspect for looseness or noise
  4. Verify rear frame rail corrosion and track bar bracket weld recalls were addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
