Buying a used 2022 Jeep Renegade? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 26 owner complaints on file

The same two recalls as 2021 carry over, with nothing added: a front airbag that may not deploy in a crash, and an incorrect reverse light assembly. The complaint record is the lightest of these Renegade years — 26 unverified NHTSA filings, led by electrical system (12) and engine (11), with power train at six and forward collision avoidance at five. One crash is reported, with no fires and no injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the front airbag recall repair was completed by VIN
  3. Verify the reverse lights illuminate correctly and the assembly was replaced if affected
  4. Note any electrical glitches, warning lights or engine roughness during a test drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
