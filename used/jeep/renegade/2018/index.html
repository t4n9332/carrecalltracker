Buying a used 2018 Jeep Renegade? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 442 owner complaints on file

This model year has two high-severity recalls: one for vehicles that may stall while driving, and one for a fuel pump that may lose pressure. NHTSA has logged 442 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 16 crashes, 8 fires, and 13 injuries. Engine complaints lead at 189, followed by electrical system (131), unknown/other (94), and fuel/propulsion system (90).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the stall and fuel pump recalls were completed
  3. Ask about any history of stalling or loss of power while driving
  4. Have the fuel system and engine inspected before purchase
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Jeep dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
