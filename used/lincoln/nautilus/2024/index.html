Buying a used 2024 Lincoln Nautilus? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 21 owner complaints on file

The 2024 Nautilus has the longest recall list of recent years — seven, led by a critical engine block heater that can overheat and a high-severity fault where the screens go blank while driving. Four separate campaigns address power windows that may not reverse or stop when obstructed, plus a rearview camera recall. None carried stop-driving or park-outside advice. NHTSA shows 21 unverified complaints with 2 crashes and 1 injury, weighted to electrical, visibility and service brakes.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask which of the four power window recalls were completed
  3. Run the screens and camera on a long test drive to check for blanking
  4. Confirm the block heater recall repair if the car has one fitted
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
