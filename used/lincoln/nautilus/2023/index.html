Buying a used 2023 Lincoln Nautilus? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 7 owner complaints on file

Three recalls apply to this model year: two high-severity recalls covering weak child seat anchor welds and rear shock absorbers that may cause damage, plus a lower-severity rearview camera freeze issue. NHTSA logged 7 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 1 crash and no reported fires or injuries, split among electrical system (3), an unspecified category (3), seat belts (1), and service brakes (1).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the child seat anchor weld recall repair, especially if installing a car seat
  3. Ask about the rear shock absorber recall completion
  4. Test the rearview camera for freezing
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
