Buying a used 2019 Lincoln Nautilus? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 101 owner complaints on file

This model year has eight recall campaigns, including a critical transmission bolt fire risk, plus high-severity recalls covering a driver airbag cover, seat wiring tied to airbag function, a blank instrument cluster, and a lane-assist alert failure. NHTSA received 101 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 2 crashes and no reported fires or injuries; electrical system (29) and power train (28) are the most common complaint categories, followed by engine (15).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the transmission bolt fire-risk recall was completed
  3. Test the instrument cluster and rearview camera for blank or freezing displays
  4. Verify the seat wiring/airbag and driver airbag cover recalls were repaired
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
