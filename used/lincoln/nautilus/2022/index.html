Buying a used 2022 Lincoln Nautilus? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 13 owner complaints on file

The 2022 Nautilus repeats 2021's two recalls — a high-severity EcoBoost engine failure that can cause power loss, and a rearview camera that may freeze or blank — with no stop-driving or park-outside instructions. Its complaint file is the lightest of the recent model years: 13 unverified NHTSA reports with 1 crash and no fires or injuries, led by structure (6), unknown/other (3) and power train (3), plus a single service brake report.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check recall status for the EcoBoost engine campaign before purchase
  3. Test the rearview camera for freezing or blank images
  4. Inspect body and structural panels for prior repair, given the structure complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
