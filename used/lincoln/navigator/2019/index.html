Buying a used 2019 Lincoln Navigator? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 78 owner complaints on file

Recalls for this model year cover rear suspension fasteners, seat belts that may lock (listed under several overlapping titles), a blank instrument cluster, and a transmission park system issue, plus two low-severity paperwork recalls for missing manual instructions and a missing airbag warning label. NHTSA has logged 78 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 3 crashes, 1 fire, and 9 injuries; the largest complaint categories are power train (22) and seat belts (20).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rear suspension fastener recall was completed
  3. Test seat belt buckles for locking and confirm recall status
  4. Check that the instrument cluster displays normally, not blank
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
