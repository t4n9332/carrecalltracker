Buying a used 2019 Lincoln Navigator? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 78 owner complaints on file

The 2019 Navigator repeats the roll-away and seat belt lock-up recalls, adds loose rear suspension bolts, two campaigns for blank gauge or dashboard displays, a backup camera, and two low-severity label and manual fixes. None advised stopping driving or parking outside. It carries the heaviest complaint file here: 78 unverified NHTSA reports with 3 crashes, 1 fire and 9 injuries, led by power train (22) and seat belts (20) — the latter mirroring the belt recalls.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether all three seat belt recalls were performed
  3. Watch the gauge cluster and dashboard display for blanking during the test drive
  4. Have the rear suspension bolts checked for correct torque
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
