Buying a used 2024 Lincoln Navigator? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 16 owner complaints on file

Two critical recalls stand out for 2024, both covering engines that can leak oil, one described as a sudden leak. Leaking front brake lines and the trailer brake and light campaign are also open items, alongside two rearview camera recalls and a missing airbag warning label. NHTSA lists 16 unverified owner complaints, including four crashes and one injury, with suspension, powertrain, electrical and wheel issues the most-reported areas — a different mix from the recalls themselves.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both engine oil leak recalls were completed, and inspect under the engine and on the driveway for fresh oil
  3. Verify the front brake line recall repair and look at the lines during a lift inspection
  4. Test the rearview camera and, on the drive, listen and feel for suspension or drivetrain noise given those complaint areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
