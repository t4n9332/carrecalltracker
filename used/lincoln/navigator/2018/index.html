Buying a used 2018 Lincoln Navigator? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 52 owner complaints on file

Ten recalls cover the 2018 Navigator, led by a critical fuel pump that can leak and high-severity items: 10-speed transmissions that may not hold Park, vehicles that may roll away, a brake fluid leak, a second-row seat that can shift, and seat belts that can lock. None carried stop-driving or park-outside advice. Of 52 unverified NHTSA complaints — 3 fires, 1 injury, no crashes — power train (20) and engine (12) dominate.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump leak and brake fluid leak recalls were completed
  3. Test that the transmission holds Park on a slope
  4. Check second-row seat latching and all seat belt retractors
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
