Buying a used 2023 Lincoln Navigator? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file

Recalls for this model year include an axle pinion fracture, brake line damage, and a trailer brake-and-light failure, alongside medium recalls for a freezing rearview camera image, camera/ADAS loss, and wiper arms that may break; two low-severity recalls cover missing manual instructions and a missing airbag warning label. NHTSA has logged 6 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, with 0 crashes, fires, or injuries; the largest complaint category is electrical system (4).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the axle pinion fracture recall was completed
  3. Inspect brake lines for damage per the recall
  4. Test the rearview camera and ADAS features for proper function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
