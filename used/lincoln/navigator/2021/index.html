Buying a used 2021 Lincoln Navigator? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 32 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

The 2021 Navigator's critical recall is an engine compartment fire risk that carries park-outside advice; no recall tells owners to stop driving. High-severity campaigns cover possibly faulty side airbag sensors, a fuel pump failure that can stall the engine, front seat belts that can lock, and roll-away risk. Wiper arms, two camera recalls and a moonroof wind deflector fill out the list. NHTSA holds 32 unverified complaints, 2 crashes, no fires, led by engine and fuel items.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine compartment fire-risk recall repair before storing it in a garage
  3. Verify the fuel pump and side airbag sensor recalls on this VIN
  4. Check for stalling history, airbag warning lights, and camera function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
