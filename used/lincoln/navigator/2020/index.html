Buying a used 2020 Lincoln Navigator? Check these 13 recalls first

13 NHTSA recalls · 31 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year has a critical battery fire risk recall that advises parking outside until repaired, along with recalls for seat belts that may lock and a transmission park system issue. Medium-severity recalls cover pre-collision assist not enabling, a headrest weld, wiper arms, a rearview camera, and collision assist not working. NHTSA has logged 31 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 2 crashes, 2 fires, and 0 injuries; the largest complaint category is power train (17).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the battery fire recall was completed; ask if it was parked outside per the advisory
  3. Test the seat belt locking mechanism and transmission park function
  4. Verify pre-collision and collision assist systems are enabled and working
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
