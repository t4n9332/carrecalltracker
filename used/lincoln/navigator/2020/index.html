Buying a used 2020 Lincoln Navigator? Check these 13 recalls first

13 NHTSA recalls · 31 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

One 2020 Navigator recall — a fuse box fire risk rated critical — advises parking outside until repaired, though not to stop driving. The rest cover seat belts that can lock, roll-away risk, a crash-warning system not enabled, automatic braking not working, a rear headrest bracket weld, wiper arms and the backup camera. NHTSA lists 31 unverified owner complaints with 2 crashes and 2 fires reported, and power train (17) is the clear leader.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get proof the fuse box fire-risk recall was repaired
  3. Confirm the crash-warning and automatic braking software updates were applied
  4. Test seat belt retractors, wipers and the backup camera
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
