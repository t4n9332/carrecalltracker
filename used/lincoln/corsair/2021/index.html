Buying a used 2021 Lincoln Corsair? Check these 22 recalls first

22 NHTSA recalls · 34 owner complaints on file

This model year carries 10 recalls, 5 of them critical, covering engine fire risk, a hybrid battery weld defect, and a block heater fire risk; other recalls address rear drive axle seizure, engine stall risk, unexpected shifts to neutral, and a rearview camera. None carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. NHTSA logged 34 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 2 reporting injuries and no crashes or fires. An unknown/other category led complaints at 13, followed by back-over prevention at 9, electrical system at 6, and power train at 5.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine fire risk and block heater recalls have been completed
  3. Ask about any unexpected shifts to neutral or stalling episodes
  4. Test the rearview camera and check the rear drive axle for unusual noise
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
