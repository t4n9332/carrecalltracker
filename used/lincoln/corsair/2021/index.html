Buying a used 2021 Lincoln Corsair? Check these 22 recalls first

22 NHTSA recalls · 34 owner complaints on file

This year's list is dominated by fire and power-loss issues. Five critical recalls apply, including engine fire risk, hybrid engine fires, a hybrid battery short circuit, block heater fires, and one covering a prior repair that left the fire risk unresolved. High-severity items add rear axle lubricant loss, stalling, and three separate recalls for loss of drive power. The 34 unverified NHTSA complaints skew to unspecified, back-over prevention, electrical, and power train, and include two reported injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check whether the earlier fire-risk repair was redone under the follow-up recall, not just the original
  3. Ask about any stalling or sudden loss of drive power, and confirm all drive-power recalls are closed
  4. Have the rear axle inspected for lubricant loss and review hybrid battery service records
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
