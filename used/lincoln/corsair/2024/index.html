Buying a used 2024 Lincoln Corsair? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 20 owner complaints on file

The recall list is shorter than earlier Corsair years: one critical campaign for a hybrid battery short circuit, plus a rearview camera that can freeze or blank, water intrusion in the rear lights that disables brake lights, and a missing dashboard airbag warning label. None carried stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Of 20 unverified NHTSA owner complaints, electrical system is the clear leader, followed by air bags, forward collision avoidance and exterior lighting; 2 crashes and 1 injury were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Have someone confirm both brake lights work and inspect rear lamps for moisture
  3. Check VIN status on the hybrid battery short-circuit recall
  4. Test the rearview camera and forward collision warning system on a drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
