Buying a used 2020 Lincoln Corsair? Check these 16 recalls first

16 NHTSA recalls · 60 owner complaints on file

This model year carries 10 recalls, including critical fire-risk recalls for transmission bolts and a block heater, plus several rearview and 360-degree camera display recalls and a rear coil spring recall; none carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. NHTSA logged 60 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 2 crashes, 1 fire, and 1 injury. Back-over prevention led complaints at 17, followed by electrical system at 12, an unknown/other category at 11, and exterior lighting at 6.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the camera-display recalls and rear coil spring recall are completed
  3. Test the rearview camera for cut-outs, blank screen, or freezing
  4. Ask about the reported fire incident and any repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
