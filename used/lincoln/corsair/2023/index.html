Buying a used 2023 Lincoln Corsair? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 14 owner complaints on file

This model year carries 8 recalls, 5 of them critical, covering engine fire risk, leaking fuel injectors, a battery defect fire risk, and a block heater that may overheat; remaining recalls address a rearview camera image freeze, a short seat belt chime, and a missing airbag warning label. None carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. NHTSA logged 14 owner complaints (unverified reports), with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. Electrical system led complaints at 7, followed by an unknown/other category at 6.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine fire, fuel injector, and battery defect recalls have been completed
  3. Ask about any electrical system issues or warning lights
  4. Check the rearview camera for freezing and confirm the airbag warning label is present
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
