Buying a used 2023 Lincoln Corsair? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 15 owner complaints on file

The 2023 Lincoln Corsair has 8 recalls in this group. Engine fire risk, leaking fuel injectors, a battery defect posing fire risk and block-heater overheating dominate the listed concerns. The remaining items involve a frozen rearview camera image, a short seat belt warning chime and a missing airbag warning label. Its 14 NHTSA owner complaints are unverified reports, including 7 electrical-system and 6 unknown-or-other reports. Reported outcomes are 0 crashes, 0 fires and 0 injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Request VIN-specific engine and fuel-injector recall completion records.
  3. Verify applicable battery and block-heater recall repairs.
  4. Check for frozen rearview images and ask about electrical faults.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
