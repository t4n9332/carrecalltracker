Buying a used 2023 Lincoln Corsair? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 14 owner complaints on file

Five critical recalls apply: hybrid engine fire risk, hybrid battery short circuit, fuel injectors that may leak gasoline, engine block heaters that can overheat, and a campaign covering a prior repair that left fire risk in place. Lesser items include a rearview camera that can freeze or blank, a missing dashboard airbag label and a short seat belt chime. None carried stop-driving or park-outside warnings. All 14 unverified owner complaints involved no crashes, fires or injuries; electrical system led.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the fuel injector and block heater recalls were completed by VIN
  3. Ask whether the hybrid battery and re-repair fire campaigns were performed
  4. Look for the dashboard airbag warning label and test the seat belt chime
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
