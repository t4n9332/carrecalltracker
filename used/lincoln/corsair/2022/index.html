Buying a used 2022 Lincoln Corsair? Check these 19 recalls first

19 NHTSA recalls · 34 owner complaints on file

This model year's record is dominated by hybrid powertrain issues. Six critical recalls cover engine fire risk, hybrid battery short circuits and sudden loss of drive power, including one campaign issued because an earlier repair left the fire risk unresolved. Engine block heater fires and two backup camera recalls also apply. None carried stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Among 34 unverified NHTSA owner complaints, electrical system and back over prevention lead, with 2 crashes and 4 injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN for open status on every hybrid fire, battery and power-loss recall
  3. Confirm the earlier fire-risk repair was redone under the follow-up campaign
  4. Cycle the backup camera several times to check for a vanishing image
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
