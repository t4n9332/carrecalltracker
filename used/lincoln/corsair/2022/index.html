Buying a used 2022 Lincoln Corsair? Check these 19 recalls first

19 NHTSA recalls · 34 owner complaints on file

This model year carries 10 recalls, 5 of them critical, covering engine fire risk and a hybrid battery fire risk, plus a block heater fire risk; other recalls address unexpected shifts to neutral (listed twice), a hybrid battery that may fail, and a rearview camera display problem. None carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. NHTSA logged 34 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 2 crashes and 4 injuries, no fires. Electrical system and back-over prevention tied as the leading complaint areas at 8 each, followed by an unknown/other category at 5.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine fire risk and hybrid battery recalls have been completed
  3. Ask about any unexpected shifts to neutral
  4. Test the rearview camera display for problems
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
