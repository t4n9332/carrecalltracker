Buying a used 2019 Lincoln Continental? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 31 owner complaints on file

The 2019 shares the 2018 door latch recall and camera fogging recall, and adds a third for a rearview camera image that can go blank. Complaint volume is notably higher than adjacent years at 31 unverified reports to NHTSA, with latches/locks/linkages the top category by a wide margin and structure second, then electrical and back-over prevention. One crash is reported in those complaints, no fires and no injuries. The complaint pattern closely mirrors the recall subjects.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check the VIN for the door latch recall and both camera recalls
  3. Cycle every door, including from inside and outside, watching for latch or ajar faults
  4. Test the backup camera for both fogging and a blank or dropped image
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
