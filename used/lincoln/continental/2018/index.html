Buying a used 2018 Lincoln Continental? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 19 owner complaints on file

The 2018 Continental has a short recall list: a high-severity one for doors that may not latch shut properly, and a medium one for rearview cameras that fog. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 19 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA line up with those areas, with latches/locks/linkages the single largest category, followed by electrical, back-over prevention, and unspecified issues. One crash is reported among those complaints, with no fires and no injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the door latch recall was completed and open and close all four doors
  3. Inspect the rearview camera on a cold or damp morning for fogging
  4. Ask the seller whether any door has ever failed to stay latched or shown an ajar warning
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
