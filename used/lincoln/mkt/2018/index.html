Buying a used 2018 Lincoln MKT? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 7 owner complaints on file

This model year's two recalls cover a rear toe link that may fracture and a B-pillar door trim panel that can detach; neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. NHTSA logged 7 owner complaints for this model year (unverified reports), with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. Complaint areas are spread thinly across structure, an unknown/other category, fuel/propulsion system, and back-over prevention, each with only a few reports on file.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rear toe link recall has been completed
  3. Inspect the B-pillar door trim for looseness or detachment
  4. Ask about any structural repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
