Buying a used 2018 Lincoln MKT? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 7 owner complaints on file

A short list for this year. The critical item is a rear suspension link that can break, a structural failure worth confirming as repaired. The other recall is door pillar trim that can detach while driving. Neither carried a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Owner complaint volume is very low at 7 unverified NHTSA reports with no crashes, fires or injuries, spread across structure, unknown or other, fuel/propulsion and back over prevention, so there is little complaint data to draw on.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rear suspension link recall was completed and inspect the rear suspension
  3. Check that door pillar trim pieces are secure on all doors
  4. Listen for clunks or rear-end noise over bumps during the test drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
