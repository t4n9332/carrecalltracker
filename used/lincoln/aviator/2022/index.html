Buying a used 2022 Lincoln Aviator? Check these 21 recalls first

21 NHTSA recalls · 44 owner complaints on file

This model year has two critical recalls, for a fuel filler tube issue and battery fire risk, plus high-severity recalls covering engine rail defects, engine valve failure, shifting second-row seats, a fuel pump that may fail and stall the engine, and loosening seat belt anchor bolts. NHTSA logged 44 owner complaints (unverified), including 4 crashes, 1 fire, and 3 injuries, with back-over prevention (8), structure (8), and electrical system (8) as top categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel filler tube and battery fire recalls are completed
  3. Verify the second-row seat and seat belt anchor bolt recalls were done
  4. Ask the seller about backup camera or rearview visibility issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
