Buying a used 2025 Lincoln Aviator? Check these 15 recalls first

15 NHTSA recalls · 14 owner complaints on file

This is the busiest Aviator year listed, led by a critical transmission recall for unexpected vehicle movement and a related loose transmission bolt recall. Loose fasteners are a running theme: trailer hitch bolts, seat belt anchor bolts, and a subframe bolt. Medium items include a backup camera that can shut off, shifting second-row seats, rear windows that may pinch, detaching rear trim, and windshields with air bubbles. The 14 unverified NHTSA complaints center on service brakes, forward collision avoidance, electrical, and seats.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the critical transmission and transmission bolt recalls are closed by VIN
  3. Ask for documentation on the subframe, hitch, and seat belt anchor bolt inspections
  4. Test the backup camera, rear window operation, and look across the windshield for bubbles
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
