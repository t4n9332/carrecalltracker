Buying a used 2023 Lincoln Aviator? Check these 16 recalls first

16 NHTSA recalls · 24 owner complaints on file

This model year has no critical recalls but several high-severity ones, including a transmission park issue, shifting second-row seats, a fuel pump that may fail and stall the engine, and loosening seat belt anchor bolts, plus multiple medium-severity camera recalls (360-degree display, rearview, back-up image flicker). NHTSA logged 24 owner complaints (unverified), including 1 crash, with electrical system (7) and back-over prevention (5) as the top categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the park-position and second-row seat recalls are completed
  3. Test all camera functions, including rearview and 360-degree display, for issues
  4. Ask about any fuel pump failure or stalling incidents
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
