Buying a used 2024 Lincoln Corsair Phev? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

The 2024 plug-in hybrid carries the same two critical battery recalls as 2023: one for battery failure and one for battery fire risk, neither flagged with a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Complaint volume is low at 4 unverified reports to NHTSA, but those few touch engine, fuel/propulsion, power train, and electrical, and include one crash and one injury. With a sample that small, the complaint mix says little on its own; the battery recalls are the substantive item.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify both critical battery recalls are closed by VIN before purchase
  3. Ask for hybrid system and battery service history, including any warranty claims
  4. Drive it under electric and gas power and watch for propulsion or power train warnings
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lincoln dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
