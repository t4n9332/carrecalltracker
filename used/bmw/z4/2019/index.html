Buying a used 2019 BMW Z4? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

The standout is a starter relay fire risk carrying a park-outside advisory until it's repaired — the only such flag in this group. Also listed: an engine balance shaft that can come loose, a steering tie rod that can break, and loss of power brake assist, both shared with the Toyota Supra. Just five unverified owner complaints are on file, touching engine, electrical, steering and air bags, including one crash.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for documentation that the starter relay fire recall was completed.
  3. Confirm the tie rod, brake assist and balance shaft recalls were performed.
  4. Check headlight aim and backup camera operation before buying.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
