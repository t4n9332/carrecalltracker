Buying a used 2022 BMW Z4? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

The recall list is short but fire-weighted: a starter relay fire risk carrying a park-outside advisory, and a starter that can overheat and catch fire. Both are rated critical and both are worth confirming as repaired rather than assumed. The third recall is milder — windows on some 2, 8 Series and Z4 models can close without the key present. One owner complaint has been filed with NHTSA, touching electrical system and visibility; a single unverified report is not a pattern.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify on the VIN that both starter recalls, relay and overheating, have been completed.
  3. Ask whether the car was garaged or parked outside while the relay recall was open.
  4. Test each window, including one-touch close with the key out of the car.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
