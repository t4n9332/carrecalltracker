Buying a used 2024 BMW IX? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 32 owner complaints on file

The 2024 iX has a shorter list than earlier years — four recalls, with no do-not-drive campaign. One critical recall covers battery modules that can fail; high-severity ones address battery wiring that can cause stalling, cruise control that can turn itself back on, and motor software that can cut power. NHTSA shows 32 owner complaints with 13 crashes, 2 fires and 10 injuries, the highest injury count here, concentrated in electrical, forward collision avoidance and service brakes. These are unverified.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the battery module and battery wiring recalls were completed on this VIN
  3. Test cruise control for self-reactivation and the drivetrain for power interruptions
  4. Ask about brake behavior, unexpected collision warnings, and any accident or fire history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
