Buying a used 2023 BMW IX? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 38 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive · 1 park-outside

Ten recalls, including a critical battery fire risk carrying do-not-drive and park-outside warnings, plus critical campaigns for sudden drive power loss and failing battery modules. High-severity items add a driver airbag that may not deploy, an M60 drive gear weld that could break and allow rollaway, stalling from battery wiring, and self-reactivating cruise control. NHTSA lists 38 owner complaints with 12 crashes and 6 injuries, led by air bags and forward collision avoidance. Complaints are unverified reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the do-not-drive battery fire and battery module recalls were remedied
  3. On an M60, verify the drive gear weld recall was performed and test park hold on an incline
  4. Ask about airbag warning lights, prior airbag deployment, and any collision system faults
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
