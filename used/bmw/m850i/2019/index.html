Buying a used 2019 BMW M850I? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

For the 2019 M850i, the item that stands out is a critical high pressure fuel pump recall: the pump can leak and cause a fire, though owners are not told to stop driving or park outside. The other two campaigns are routine backup camera issues — an image that may fail and a picture that can vanish. NHTSA shows a single unverified owner complaint, with no crashes, fires or injuries, filed under engine and engine cooling.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get VIN proof the fuel pump recall repair was completed
  3. Check for fuel smell or residue around the engine bay
  4. Ask for cooling system and engine service records
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
