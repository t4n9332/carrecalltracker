Buying a used 2024 BMW X1? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 30 owner complaints on file

Every open recall on this year traces to one theme: loss of brake power assist, including one rated critical, plus an integrated-brake inspection campaign. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The unverified NHTSA complaint file leans the same way — 30 reports (3 crashes, 1 injury) led by service brakes with 19, stability control with 10 and hydraulic with 9. The overlap between what was recalled and what owners report is the notable part.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN for all three brake recalls and confirm each repair is closed
  3. Ask if the pedal ever went hard or a brake/assist warning appeared
  4. On the test drive, watch for ABS or stability-control lights
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
