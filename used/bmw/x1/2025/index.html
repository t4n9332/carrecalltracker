Buying a used 2025 BMW X1? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 14 owner complaints on file

Three high-severity recalls, and only one is the brake power-assist issue shared across recent BMWs. The other two are crash-protection items: X1 door pillars that may be under-strength, and front seat belts that may not restrain as intended. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Unverified owner reports are few — 14 total (2 crashes, 1 fire) — led by electrical system with 7, then service brakes, engine and power train, which is thin for a new model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the door-pillar recall remedy was performed, and ask what it involved
  3. Verify the seat belt recall is closed; latch and tug both front belts
  4. Ask about electrical faults or warning lights, the top complaint area
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
