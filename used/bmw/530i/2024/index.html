Buying a used 2024 BMW 530I? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 13 owner complaints on file

Brakes dominate this car's record: four of six recalls involve the braking system, including a critical loss of brake assist power and a module failure that can weaken brakes and stability control. A separate critical recall covers a starter that can overheat and catch fire, and a hands-on steering sensor may not work. Complaints echo it — 13 unverified NHTSA reports, 10 naming service brakes, six hydraulic, six stability control, three crashes, no fires.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run a VIN recall check covering all four brake recalls and the starter recall
  3. Test brake pedal feel from cold and watch for brake or stability control lights
  4. Confirm the hands-on steering sensor and driver assist work without faults
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
