Buying a used 2020 BMW R 1250 RT? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file

2020 adds a rear drive pin that may loosen on top of the two high-severity recalls shared across R 1250 models: leaking front brake calipers and a gearbox shaft that can break. Two low-severity recalls concern brake fluid instruction labeling. Six unverified owner complaints went to NHTSA — five under service brakes, one power train — with no crashes, fires or injuries reported. Brakes and the final drive are the areas to focus on.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the rear drive pin recall was completed and check the final drive for play or noise
  3. Get proof the front brake caliper recall was performed; inspect for fluid leaks
  4. Confirm the gearbox shaft recall shows as completed on this VIN
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
