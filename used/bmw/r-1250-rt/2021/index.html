Buying a used 2021 BMW R 1250 RT? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

For 2021 the list shortens to one high-severity recall, the R 1250 gearbox shaft that can break, and a low-severity recall for a tire pressure label that falls off. The brake caliper recall from earlier years is not listed here. Four unverified owner complaints reached NHTSA, spread across forward collision avoidance, electrical system, wheels and service brakes rather than concentrated in one area, with no crashes, fires or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the gearbox shaft recall was completed and request the repair order
  3. If equipped, test the forward collision warning system for false or missing alerts
  4. Check the tire pressure label is in place and inspect wheels for damage or repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
