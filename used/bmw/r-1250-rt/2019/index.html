Buying a used 2019 BMW R 1250 RT? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

The same two high-severity recalls as other 2019 R 1250 bikes apply: front brake calipers that can leak fluid, and a gearbox shaft that can break. Two low-severity recalls concern missing brake fluid instructions on the cap and on accessory reservoir covers. Nine owner complaints reached NHTSA, eight under service brakes. These are unverified reports, but the brakes theme lines up with the caliper recall. No crashes or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for repair orders covering the brake caliper recall on this VIN
  3. Inspect front calipers for fluid residue and check lever travel when hot
  4. Confirm the gearbox shaft recall was completed before purchase
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
