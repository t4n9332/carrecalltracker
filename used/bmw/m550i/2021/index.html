Buying a used 2021 BMW M550I? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

One critical item defines the 2021 M550i: a driveshaft connection that can fail or let the vehicle roll away. The other two campaigns are the familiar low-to-medium backup camera issues — an image that may fail, and a partly blocked view shared with X3, X4 and 5 Series cars. Nothing is flagged stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA shows a single unverified owner complaint, with no crashes, fires or injuries, filed under power train.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get VIN proof the driveshaft connection recall was performed
  3. Ask whether the car has ever moved or rolled while parked
  4. Listen for driveline clunk or vibration during the test drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
