Buying a used 2020 BMW M550I? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

The 2020 M550i's recall record is unusual for being almost entirely about the backup camera: three separate campaigns covering an image that may fail, a picture that can vanish, and a partly blocked view on X3, X4 and 5 Series cars. All are medium or low severity, with no stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Two unverified NHTSA complaints, with no crashes, fires or injuries, mention power train, fuel system and gasoline rather than the camera.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all three backup camera recalls were closed out by VIN
  3. Cycle into reverse several times to check the camera image holds
  4. Ask about any fuel system or drivetrain repairs in service history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
