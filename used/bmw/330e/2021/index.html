Buying a used 2021 BMW 330E? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

Two of the 2021 330e's five recalls concern the plug-in hybrid battery catching fire, one critical and one for a short circuit; neither is flagged park-outside. Also listed: loose seat belt bolts on 3 Series and M3, a passenger belt retractor fault, and a backup camera that may fail. Four unverified owner complaints are on file, one mentioning an injury, mostly engine and power train, with no crashes or fires reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both hybrid battery recalls are closed and ask if the battery was replaced
  3. Have the seat belt bolts and passenger belt retractor inspected
  4. Check charging behavior, electric range and the backup camera image
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
