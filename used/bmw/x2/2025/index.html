Buying a used 2025 BMW X2? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Three high-severity recalls: brake power-assist loss, an integrated-brake failure requiring inspection, and front seat belts that may not restrain properly. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, but two of the three are brake-related, so completion status matters more than usual here. Owner reporting is essentially nonexistent — a single unverified NHTSA complaint filed under service brakes, with no crashes, fires or injuries — far too little to read a pattern into.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the integrated-brake inspection was actually done, not just scheduled
  3. Verify the brake-assist and seat belt recalls are closed by VIN
  4. Inspect front seat belt latching and retraction before buying
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
