Buying a used 2024 BMW X2? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

Both open recalls cover the same failure — loss of brake power assist, one of them rated critical — and neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Owner reporting is very thin: just 2 unverified NHTSA complaints, with no crashes, fires or injuries, citing service brakes, forward collision avoidance, hydraulic and stability control. With so few reports, the complaint record says little in either direction, so recall completion status is the more useful signal on this one.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN and confirm both brake-assist recalls are marked complete
  3. Ask the seller whether brake assist ever cut out or felt heavy
  4. Check that forward collision and stability systems are fault-free
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
