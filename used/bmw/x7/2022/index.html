Buying a used 2022 BMW X7? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 20 owner complaints on file

This is the lightest recall year in the X7 range covered here: just two medium-severity campaigns, both familiar to the model — a sunroof that can close without the key present, and third-row seat backs that may not lock. No fire, brake or steering recalls are listed. Complaint volume is also the lowest, 20 unverified reports to NHTSA, spread across unspecified issues, electrical system, steering and back-over prevention, with one fire reported and no injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check the sunroof cannot close with the key away from the vehicle.
  3. Fold and latch the third-row seat backs to confirm they lock.
  4. Test steering feel and the backup camera on the drive, given complaint areas.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
