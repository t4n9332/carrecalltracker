Buying a used 2021 BMW X7? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 46 owner complaints on file

This 2021 X7 carries a critical recall for a 48-volt battery connection that may cause fire, plus high-severity recalls for a loose steering column and an ABS system that may lose function. Additional medium recalls cover tire labels, a backup camera issue, second-row head restraints, a sunroof closing unexpectedly, and loose third-row seat backrest springs. Owner complaints total 46, with 2 fires, 1 injury, and 0 crashes reported; engine and power train tie for the top category at 11 each, followed by electrical system at 8 and seats at 5.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm 48-volt battery connection fire recall has been completed
  3. Ask about steering column looseness recall completion
  4. Ask about third-row seat backrest spring recall and any seat issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
