Buying a used 2019 BMW X7? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 47 owner complaints on file

The first X7 model year carries three fire-related recalls — a high-pressure fuel pump leak and two covering starter motor overheating — plus high-severity fixes for misaligned head airbags, under-tightened left-side wheel bolts, and a brake unit failure. Several lower-severity items involve seats, the sunroof, and the backup camera. None of the 47 unverified NHTSA owner complaints report fires; they cluster in electrical system (22) and engine (11), echoing the electrical theme in the recalls.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof the fuel pump and both starter motor fire recalls were completed.
  3. Confirm the left-side wheel bolt and head airbag recalls were done by a dealer.
  4. Test the backup camera, sunroof closing, and third-row seat back locks.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
