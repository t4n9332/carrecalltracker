Buying a used 2019 BMW X7? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 47 owner complaints on file

This 2019 X7 carries several critical recalls, including two separate starter overheating campaigns tied to engine fire risk and a fuel pump that may crack and leak fuel, alongside high-severity recalls for loose lounge seat bolts, misaligned head air bags, loose left-side wheel bolts, a vanishing backup camera image, and an ABS system that may lose function. Owner complaints total 47, with 1 crash and 0 fires or injuries reported; electrical system issues are the largest category at 22 of 47 complaints, followed by engine at 11.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm starter motor overheating and fuel pump recalls have been completed
  3. Ask about status of left side wheel bolt and head air bag alignment recalls
  4. Ask seller about any electrical system problems experienced
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
