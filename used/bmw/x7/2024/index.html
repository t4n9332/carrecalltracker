Buying a used 2024 BMW X7? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 52 owner complaints on file

The brake campaigns continue — loss of power assist, integrated brakes needing inspection, and a brake module fault affecting stability control — joined by three airbag-related recalls: a passenger knee airbag fault, knee airbags that may not deploy, and a dashboard that can break during airbag deployment. Complaints are heavily brake-weighted: of 52 unverified reports, 40 cite service brakes, 21 stability control and 13 hydraulic, with two crashes and no fires or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the four brake recalls, including the inspection-based one, are closed.
  3. Confirm both knee airbag recalls and the dashboard recall were performed.
  4. Watch for brake or stability control warning lights during a longer test drive.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
