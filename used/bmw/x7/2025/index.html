Buying a used 2025 BMW X7? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 28 owner complaints on file

Four recalls, all safety-system related: three cover the brakes — loss of power assist twice over and integrated brakes that may fail and need inspection — and one covers front seat belt sensing that can be misread, which affects airbag behavior. The unverified complaint file is small for a recent year at 28, but follows the same pattern: service brakes 12, hydraulic 6, stability control 6, with two crashes and one injury reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get dealer confirmation that all three brake recalls were completed.
  3. Ask whether the front seat belt sensing recall was performed.
  4. Check for brake, ABS or airbag warning lights at startup.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
