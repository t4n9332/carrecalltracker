Buying a used 2020 BMW X7? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 52 owner complaints on file

This 2020 X7 has two critical recalls for starter motor overheating that can lead to engine fire, plus high-severity recalls covering a steering rack that can break, a starter bolt that may cause stalling, a front suspension weld that may fail, a vanishing backup camera image, and an ABS system that may lose function. Owner complaints total 52, including 2 crashes, 3 fires, and 3 injuries reported; power train complaints lead at 14 of 52, followed closely by engine at 13 and electrical system at 11.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both starter overheating and starter bolt stall recalls are completed
  3. Ask about steering rack and front suspension weld recall completion
  4. Ask about power train or engine issues given top complaint categories
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
