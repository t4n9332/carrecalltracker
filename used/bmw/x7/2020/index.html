Buying a used 2020 BMW X7? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 52 owner complaints on file

A steering gear whose teeth can break heads the list, alongside two starter motor fire recalls carried over from 2019, a front suspension weld that may crack, the brake unit failure, and a loose bolt that can stall the engine. Owner reports to NHTSA — 52, unverified — skew toward power train (14) and engine (13), and include three fires, two crashes and three injuries, the most of any X7 year here.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get VIN recall status on the steering gear and front suspension weld repairs.
  3. Verify both starter motor fire recalls and the brake unit recall were performed.
  4. Drive it long enough to check for stalling or power train roughness.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
