Buying a used 2023 BMW X7? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 44 owner complaints on file

Brakes dominate this year: three separate campaigns for loss of power brake assist or integrated brake failure, plus a brake module fault that can also weaken stability control. Two other critical recalls involve a windshield leak that may cause a fire and loose exhaust bolts on X7 and XM. The unverified complaint record mirrors that — of 44 reports, 26 cite service brakes and 13 electronic stability control, with two crashes and no fires or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all four brake-related recalls were completed on this VIN.
  3. Ask for proof the windshield leak and exhaust bolt recalls were fixed.
  4. Note brake pedal feel and any stability control warning lights on the test drive.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
