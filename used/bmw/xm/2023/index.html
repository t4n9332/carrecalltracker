Buying a used 2023 BMW XM? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

The XM's launch year shares most of its recalls with BMW's other large SUVs: loose exhaust bolts flagged as critical, three separate brake campaigns covering power assist loss, integrated brake failure and a module fault tied to stability control, plus two knee airbag issues. Only two unverified owner complaints are on file with NHTSA — too small a sample to read much into — citing service brakes, power train and stability control, with no crashes, fires or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the exhaust bolt recall was completed.
  3. Verify all three brake recalls, including the inspection-based one, are closed.
  4. Ask for proof both knee airbag recalls were performed.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
