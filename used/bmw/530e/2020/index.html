Buying a used 2020 BMW 530E? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

The 2020 530e's critical item is the BMW/MINI plug-in hybrid battery recall for fire risk — not flagged park-outside or stop-driving — alongside two separate backup camera recalls for an image that may fail or vanish. Owner feedback is thin: two unverified NHTSA complaints, one electrical system and one exterior lighting, with no crashes, fires or injuries. With so few reports filed, the recall record is the more useful signal on this car.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the hybrid battery recall remedy was completed, by VIN
  3. Test the backup camera repeatedly, including from a cold start
  4. Check all exterior lights and dashboard warning lamps
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
