Buying a used 2019 BMW 530E? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 12 owner complaints on file

The 2019 530e's recall record is short — a critical one for the portable charging cord, which poses shock and fire risk, plus a low-severity backup camera fault. The complaint record stands out more: 12 unverified owner reports to NHTSA, including three crashes, one fire and three injuries, concentrated in electrical system, power train, vehicle speed control and air bags. Those are unverified filings, not confirmed defects, but the spread is broader than the recall list suggests.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the original portable charging cord was replaced under recall
  3. Inspect the cord and charge port for heat damage or discoloration
  4. Review the vehicle history for prior crash or airbag repair
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
