Buying a used 2021 BMW R 1250 GS? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

The recall record thins out for 2021: one high-severity item, the R 1250 gearbox shaft that can break, and one low-severity recall for a tire pressure label that falls off. The leaking front brake caliper recall listed on earlier R 1250 years does not appear for this year. A single unverified owner complaint was filed with NHTSA, categorized only as unknown or other, with no crashes, fires or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the gearbox shaft recall was completed and get the repair order
  3. Listen and feel for gearbox or shifting irregularities on a test ride
  4. Check whether the tire pressure label is present or was replaced under the recall
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
