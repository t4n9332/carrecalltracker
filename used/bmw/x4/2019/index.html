Buying a used 2019 BMW X4? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 10 owner complaints on file

This model year has seven recalls, five of them high severity, covering rear brake caliper coating, passenger airbag deployment, passenger airbag shutoff, an airbag panel piece risk, and a backup camera image that can vanish, plus two medium recalls for a front seatback weld and partial/blank camera display. Owners have filed 10 complaints with NHTSA, including 1 fire (unverified report), spread across electrical system, power train, engine, and service brakes at 2 to 3 each.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the passenger airbag deployment and shutoff recalls were completed
  3. Ask whether the backup camera image recall has been performed
  4. Ask about the rear brake caliper coating and seatback weld recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
