Buying a used 2019 BMW X4? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 10 owner complaints on file

The 2019 X4's recalls cluster around crash protection and braking: rear brakes that may lose stopping power, two separate campaigns for a passenger airbag that may not deploy, front seatbacks that can fold unexpectedly, and a dashboard panel that may break in a crash. Three more cover backup camera image loss. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. NHTSA has only 10 owner complaints — unverified reports — with no crashes and one fire, spread across electrical, power train, engine and service brakes.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both passenger airbag recalls were performed, not just one, and that the airbag light works at startup.
  3. Push and shake both front seatbacks for play, and check rear brake feel and pad/rotor condition.
  4. Cycle into reverse several times to see whether the backup camera image appears reliably.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
