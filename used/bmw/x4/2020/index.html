Buying a used 2020 BMW X4? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 15 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive · 1 park-outside

The 2020 X4 stands out for severity: one recall for front suspension parts that may break carries a stop-driving warning, and a starter relay fire-risk recall carries a park-outside warning. M40i models have a separate loss-of-steering-control recall, and there are further campaigns for a tie rod that can break and loss of power brake assist. Lower-priority items cover the dashboard panel, a seat belt sensor and backup cameras. NHTSA lists 15 unverified owner complaints, 1 crash, no fires; electrical, suspension and air bags lead.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify in writing that the stop-driving front suspension recall and the starter relay fire recall are both completed.
  3. Ask whether it is an M40i, and if so whether the steering-control recall was done.
  4. Have a shop inspect tie rods and suspension joints, and test brake pedal effort with the engine running.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
