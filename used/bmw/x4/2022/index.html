Buying a used 2022 BMW X4? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

The 2022 X4 has a short recall list weighted toward fire and rollaway risk: a starter relay fire risk with a park-outside warning, a separate starter that can overheat and catch fire, and a transmission fault that can cause the vehicle to roll away. Two minor campaigns cover an incorrect tire label and misplaced rear license plate lights. Owner feedback is thin — NHTSA lists 3 unverified complaints, no crashes or fires, in service brakes, structure and lane departure.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for dealer records on both starter recalls and the transmission rollaway fix.
  3. Test that the transmission reliably holds in Park on an incline and that the parking brake engages.
  4. Compare tire sizes and pressures on the car to the door label, which was recalled as incorrect.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
