Buying a used 2022 BMW X4? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year has five recalls, two critical: a starter relay fire risk marked park-outside and an engine starter fire risk, plus a high-severity transmission leak that may cause rollaway, and lower-severity recalls for a tire label error and an unlit rear license plate. Owners have filed 3 complaints with NHTSA, with none reporting crashes, fires, or injuries, touching service brakes, structure, and lane departure.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the starter relay and engine starter fire recalls were completed
  3. Ask about the park-outside advisory status for the starter relay recall
  4. Ask whether the transmission leak/rollaway recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
