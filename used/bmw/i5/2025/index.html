Buying a used 2025 BMW I5? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 8 owner complaints on file

This model year has three high-severity recalls, all related to the brake system: power assist loss, a weld that may fail, and integrated brake system malfunction. No critical or medium recalls are listed. NHTSA has 8 unverified owner complaints on file, including 2 crash-related and 3 noting injuries; no fires are noted. Complaint areas include electrical system at 4, forward collision avoidance and service brakes at 3 each, and unknown or other at 2.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake power assist recall was completed
  3. Confirm the brake weld and integrated brake system recalls were completed
  4. Ask about the service brake and injury-related complaints on file
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
