Buying a used 2018 BMW X6? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

Chassis hardware is the story for this year. A critical recall covers a front suspension arm that can break, with two related campaigns for loose front suspension bolts and wheel bolts that may not have been tightened properly. A fourth addresses child seat anchor bars that can become damaged. Owner complaint volume is very low — 4 unverified NHTSA reports, one of them a fire — which reflects small sales numbers as much as vehicle condition.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for records showing the front suspension arm and bolt recalls were completed, and have the front suspension inspected before purchase.
  3. Have wheel bolt torque checked, and look for uneven tire wear or vibration at highway speed.
  4. If you use child seats, inspect the LATCH anchor bars and confirm that recall remedy was applied.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
