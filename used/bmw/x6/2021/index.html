Buying a used 2021 BMW X6? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 7 owner complaints on file

This model year has a critical recall for a 48-volt battery connection that may cause fire, along with high-severity recalls covering tires that may lose pressure quickly, a loose steering column, and an ABS-related brake system issue. Additional recalls address a partial or blank backup camera image, a sunroof that may close unexpectedly, and headlights that may glow blue. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA total 7, including 1 crash and no fires or injuries reported, unverified; service brakes, electrical system, and forward collision avoidance each account for 2 complaints.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the 48-volt battery connection recall was completed
  3. Check tire pressure retention and steering column tightness
  4. Test backup camera and ABS brake function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
