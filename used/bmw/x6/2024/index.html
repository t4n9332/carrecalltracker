Buying a used 2024 BMW X6? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

Every recall for this year is brakes or airbags: three campaigns for loss of brake power assist, integrated brakes that may fail and need inspection, and a brake module fault that also degrades stability control, plus two covering a passenger knee airbag that may not deploy. The 9 unverified owner complaints follow the same lines, led by service brakes, stability control and hydraulic reports. No crashes, fires or injuries appear in those complaints.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get dealer confirmation that all brake recalls and the integrated brake inspection are closed for this VIN.
  3. Watch for brake, ABS or stability control warnings at startup and during braking on the test drive.
  4. Ask whether both knee airbag recalls were performed and check that the airbag warning light behaves normally.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
