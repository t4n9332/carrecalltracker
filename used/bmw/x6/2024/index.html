Buying a used 2024 BMW X6? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

This model year has multiple high-severity recalls related to the brake system, including separate actions for loss of brake power assist and a possible integrated brake system malfunction, along with recalls addressing knee airbag deployment. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA total 9, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported, unverified. Complaint areas are led by service brakes at 11, followed by electronic stability control at 6, hydraulic system at 5, and forward collision avoidance at 2.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify brake power assist and integrated brake system recalls are done
  3. Confirm knee airbag deployment recall completion
  4. Test ABS/ESC warning lights and brake pedal feel
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
