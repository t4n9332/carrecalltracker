Buying a used 2025 BMW X6? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

A short list, all safety-system related: one critical and two related recalls for loss of brake power assist and integrated brakes that may fail and need inspection, plus a recall for front seat belt sensing that can misread occupancy and affect airbag deployment. Just 1 unverified NHTSA complaint is on file, touching speed control, back-over prevention and forward collision avoidance — normal for a current model year, and too little history to draw conclusions from.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask the dealer to confirm the brake assist recalls and required brake inspection are complete for this VIN.
  3. Check that the front seat belt and airbag warning lights respond correctly with occupants in both front seats.
  4. Test brake feel, the driver assistance systems and the reversing camera during an extended test drive.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
