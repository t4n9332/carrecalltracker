Buying a used 2025 BMW X6? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This model year's recalls focus on the brake system, with separate actions for loss of brake power assist, a brake system weld that may fail, and a possible integrated brake system malfunction, plus a seat belt sensor defect recall. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA are minimal, totaling 1, which involved a crash, unverified; the single complaint touches vehicle speed control, back-over prevention, and forward collision avoidance categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm brake power assist and brake weld recalls are completed
  3. Ask about the seat belt sensor defect recall status
  4. Test forward collision and back-over prevention systems
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
