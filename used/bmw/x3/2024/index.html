Buying a used 2024 BMW X3? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 37 owner complaints on file

This model year has three high-severity recalls covering the steering gear, rear door locks, and steering spindle; none were flagged as stop-driving or park-outside. Owners have filed 37 complaints with NHTSA, including 4 crashes and 2 injuries (unverified reports). Electrical system complaints were most common at 10, followed by back-over prevention at 6, unknown/other at 5, and power train at 4.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering gear and steering spindle recalls have been completed
  3. Ask whether the rear door lock recall repair was performed
  4. Ask about any electrical system issues the seller has experienced
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
