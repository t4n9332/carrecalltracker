Buying a used 2019 BMW X3? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 168 owner complaints on file

This model year has 10 recalls, six high-severity (lane keep assist detection, rear brake caliper coating, passenger airbag non-deployment, backup camera image loss, airbag panel piece risk, and passenger airbag shutoff) and four medium (seatback weld, backup camera partial image, cargo rail loose, cargo rail detach in crash). NHTSA has logged 168 owner complaints (unverified reports) with 5 crashes, 1 fire, and 3 injuries; electrical system (46) and unknown/other (43) lead, followed by engine (32) and structure (31).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm VIN shows completion of all 10 recalls, especially the airbag and brake caliper items
  3. Ask about any electrical system fault history
  4. Check backup camera function and cargo rail attachment
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
