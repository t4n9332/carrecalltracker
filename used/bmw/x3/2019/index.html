Buying a used 2019 BMW X3? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 168 owner complaints on file

Much the same recall picture as 2018, including two separate passenger-airbag non-deployment campaigns, rear brakes that may lose stopping power, and seatbacks that can fold forward. The remainder are medium and low items: dashboard panel, cargo rails, backup camera, lane-keeping. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Unverified owner reports are high at 168 (5 crashes, 1 fire, 3 injuries), concentrated in electrical system with 46, engine with 32 and structure with 31.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check both passenger-airbag campaigns separately by VIN, not just one
  3. Confirm the rear brake and seatback recalls are complete
  4. Ask about electrical faults and engine repairs, which dominate owner reports
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
