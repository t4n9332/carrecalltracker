Buying a used 2021 BMW X3? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 109 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Fire and fuel dominate here. Four critical recalls cover plug-in hybrid battery fires, a fuel filler pipe that can leak, and two separate starter overheating campaigns, one of which carries a park-outside advisory until repaired. Lower-severity items involve seat-frame welds, backup camera and rear cargo rails. Unverified owner reports total 109 (5 crashes, 2 fires, 2 injuries), concentrated in electrical system, engine and structure rather than in fuel or battery areas.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the park-outside starter recall is repaired before parking it in a garage
  3. Confirm the fuel filler pipe recall is closed; smell and look for fuel seepage at the filler neck
  4. If it's the plug-in hybrid, check both battery fire recalls by VIN
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
