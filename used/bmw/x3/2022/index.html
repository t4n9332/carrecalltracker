Buying a used 2022 BMW X3? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 74 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Three critical recalls are all fuel or fire related: a fuel rail that can crack, plus two starter overheating campaigns, one carrying a park-outside advisory. Two high-severity items stand out beyond that — a transmission fault that can cause rollaway, and rear doors that can unlock and open unexpectedly. Unverified owner reports number 74 but include 4 fires and 6 injuries, the highest injury count here, spread across structure, electrical and forward collision avoidance.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel rail and park-outside starter recalls are both repaired
  3. Verify the rear door unlocking recall remedy; test both rear doors latched and moving
  4. Ask whether the vehicle ever rolled after being shifted into Park
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
