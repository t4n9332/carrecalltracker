Buying a used 2018 BMW X3? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 74 owner complaints on file

Ten recalls, but the weight sits in three: rear brakes that may lose stopping power, front seatbacks that can fold unexpectedly, and a passenger airbag that may not deploy. The rest are lower-severity — instrument display, rear spoiler, lane-keeping, dashboard panel, cargo rail, turn-signal color, backup camera. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Unverified owner reports total 74 (4 crashes, 2 fires, 2 injuries) and cluster in electrical system and engine, areas the recalls don't address.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seatback and passenger-airbag recalls are closed; push on both seatbacks
  3. Verify the rear brake recall was done and check rear pad and rotor condition
  4. Ask for service records on electrical and engine work, the top complaint areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
