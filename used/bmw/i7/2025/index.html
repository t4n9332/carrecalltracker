Buying a used 2025 BMW I7? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

The 2025 i7 shows a short recall list: two high-severity brake campaigns, one for loss of power assist and one for integrated brakes that may fail and need inspection. No critical or do-not-drive recalls are listed for this year. NHTSA has a single owner complaint on file, which reported a crash and an injury and touched air bags, service brakes and forward collision avoidance. One unverified complaint is not a pattern, but the brake recalls are worth confirming.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify both brake recalls, including the inspection, were completed by a dealer
  3. Check that no air bag or brake warning lights are illuminated at startup
  4. Ask whether the car has any accident history or prior brake system repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
