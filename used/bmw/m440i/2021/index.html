Buying a used 2021 BMW M440I? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

Two safety-critical items lead the 2021 M440i's record: a 48-volt battery connection that can overheat and cause a fire, and a convertible-specific side airbag that may not deploy. Three lesser campaigns cover a backup camera image that may fail, the passenger seat belt retractor shared with MINI and Supra, and a seat belt warning chime on M340i and M440i cars. Of four unverified NHTSA complaints, one cites a crash, one a fire and two injuries, touching seat belts, seats, forward collision avoidance and engine.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the 48-volt battery connection recall repair is on the VIN record
  3. On a convertible, verify the side airbag recall was performed
  4. Test the backup camera and seat belt warning chime on the drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
