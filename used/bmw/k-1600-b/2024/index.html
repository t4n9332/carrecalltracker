Buying a used 2024 BMW K 1600 B? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This year carries two critical recalls: a front suspension strut that may fail while riding, and the fire-risk campaign covering K 1600 touring motorcycles. Neither instructs owners to stop riding or park outside, but both sit at the top severity tier, and the front strut item is specific to this newer build rather than carried over. Complaint volume is one unverified NHTSA report — no crashes, fires or injuries — under steering and an unclassified area.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the front suspension strut recall repair is done, since it can fail while riding
  3. Get written VIN confirmation that the fire recall was also completed
  4. Ask if the bike is still under factory warranty and whether it transfers
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
