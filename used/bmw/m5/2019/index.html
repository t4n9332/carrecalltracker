Buying a used 2019 BMW M5? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 8 owner complaints on file

The 2019 M5 is the busiest year in this group on both counts. A high pressure fuel pump that can leak and cause fire, and a wiring fault on the M5 and M8 that can cut power, sit above a minor backup camera recall. Eight unverified NHTSA complaints — the most here, though still with no crashes, fires or injuries — concentrate on the engine and engine cooling, with power train and fuel/propulsion mentions alongside.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get VIN confirmation the high pressure fuel pump recall was done
  3. Verify the M5/M8 wiring recall repair was completed
  4. Ask for engine and cooling system repair records
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
