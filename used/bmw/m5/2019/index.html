Buying a used 2019 BMW M5? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 8 owner complaints on file

This 2019 M5 has a critical recall for a fuel pump that may crack and leak fuel, plus two high-severity recalls: a backup camera image that can vanish and transmission wiring that may short. NHTSA lists 8 owner complaints for this model year, with 4 tied to the engine, 2 to engine and engine cooling, 2 to power train, and 1 to the fuel/propulsion system; no crashes, fires, or injuries were reported. These are unverified owner-submitted reports. None of the recalls are marked stop-driving or park-outside.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump crack/leak recall has been repaired
  3. Verify the transmission wiring short recall was completed
  4. Ask about the vanishing backup camera image recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
