Buying a used 2018 BMW X5? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 71 owner complaints on file

This model year has five recalls: a critical charging cable fire risk (530e/X5), plus high-severity recalls for front suspension bolts loosening, front lower control arm failure, a high-pressure fuel pump that may fail, and a water pump connector that may short. Owners have filed 71 complaints with NHTSA, including 2 crashes, 1 fire, and 3 injuries (unverified reports), led by electrical system at 14, with engine and fuel/propulsion system each near that level at 11.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether this is a plug-in hybrid subject to the charging cable fire recall
  3. Confirm the suspension bolt and control arm recalls were completed
  4. Ask about the fuel pump and water pump connector recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
