Buying a used 2018 BMW X5? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 71 owner complaints on file

The 2018 X5 has five recalls, three of them serious: a front suspension arm that may break, a water pump that can short-circuit and cause a fire, and — on plug-in models — a portable charging cord with shock and fire risk. Also recalled are loose front suspension bolts and, on diesels, a high-pressure fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine. NHTSA lists 71 owner complaints — unverified — with 1 fire and 3 injuries, led by electrical (14), engine (11) and fuel/propulsion (11).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Establish whether it is a diesel or plug-in hybrid, since two recalls apply only to those versions.
  3. Have the front suspension arms and mounting bolts inspected before purchase.
  4. Ask whether the water pump recall was done and whether any charging cord was replaced.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
