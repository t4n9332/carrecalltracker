Buying a used 2020 BMW X5? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 110 owner complaints on file

This model year has ten recalls, two critical for starter-overheating fire risk, plus six high-severity recalls covering steering rack breakage, a loose steering column bearing plate, a starter bolt that may cause stalling, front suspension weld failure, and ABS brake system loss, along with medium recalls for camera display and unexpected sunroof closing. Owners have filed 110 complaints with NHTSA, including 3 crashes, 6 fires, and 5 injuries (unverified reports), led by electrical system at 39 and engine at 33.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the starter overheating recall was completed
  3. Ask whether the steering rack and front suspension weld recalls were addressed
  4. Ask about the ABS brake system and sunroof recall repair status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
