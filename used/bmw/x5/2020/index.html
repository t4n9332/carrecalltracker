Buying a used 2020 BMW X5? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 110 owner complaints on file

The 2020 X5 carries ten recalls, including steering gear teeth that may break and two starter motor fire campaigns. Others cover a loose steering column plate, a front suspension weld that may break, brake unit failure, a loose bolt that can stall the engine, a sunroof that closes without the key, and backup camera faults. NHTSA lists 110 owner complaints — unverified reports — noting 3 crashes, 6 fires and 5 injuries, with electrical (39) and engine (33) far ahead.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for documentation that the steering gear and both starter fire recalls were completed.
  3. Have the front suspension welds and steering column inspected, and note any steering notchiness or play.
  4. Look for engine or electrical fault history in service records, given six fires among owner reports.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
