Buying a used 2025 BMW X5? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 61 owner complaints on file

The 2025 record repeats the brake theme: three recalls covering loss of brake power assist and integrated brakes that may fail and need inspection, plus one for front seat belt sensing that can misread occupancy and affect airbag deployment. A low-priority recall corrects a wrong tire size on the information sticker. Of 61 unverified owner complaints so far, service brakes, stability control and hydraulic systems lead — on a young car with a short reporting history.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm with a dealer that the brake assist recalls and the required integrated brake inspection are both closed for this VIN.
  3. Ask the seller whether brake, ABS or stability control warning lights have ever appeared, and watch the cluster at startup.
  4. Check that front seat belt warnings and airbag status lights behave normally with an occupant in each front seat.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
