Buying a used 2021 BMW X5? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 105 owner complaints on file

This model year carries three critical recalls tied to fire risk: a plug-in hybrid battery, hybrid battery debris, and a 48-volt battery connection. High-severity recalls cover tires that may lose pressure quickly, a front suspension weld, a loose steering column, an ABS-related brake system issue, and a front brake disc that may come loose. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA total 105, including 9 crashes, 3 fires, and 7 injuries reported, unverified; electrical system, power train, and engine are the most-cited areas at 36, 26, and 24.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all three battery fire recalls have been completed
  3. Check tire pressure retention for fast leaks
  4. Inspect the front brake disc and steering column mounting
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
