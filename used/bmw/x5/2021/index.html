Buying a used 2021 BMW X5? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 105 owner complaints on file

The 2021 X5 carries an unusually broad recall list for one model year — three separate fire-related campaigns (plug-in hybrid battery, hybrid battery short, 48-volt battery connection), plus front suspension weld, steering column bolt, brake unit and right front brake disc repairs. Pirelli tires that can lose air suddenly were also recalled. Owners filed 105 unverified NHTSA complaints, concentrated in electrical system, power train and engine, and including 3 fire reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN with a BMW dealer to confirm the battery fire recalls are closed — especially on a plug-in hybrid.
  3. Ask whether the suspension weld, steering column bolt and brake unit recalls were performed; listen for clunks and steering play on the test drive.
  4. Confirm the recalled Pirelli tires were replaced or inspected, and verify the backup camera and rear head restraints work as intended.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
