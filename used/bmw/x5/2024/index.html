Buying a used 2024 BMW X5? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 145 owner complaints on file

Brakes dominate this year. Five recalls address loss of brake power assist, integrated brake failure requiring inspection, and a brake module fault that also weakens stability control; two more cover a passenger knee airbag that may not deploy, plus a dashboard that can break during airbag deployment. The 145 unverified owner complaints track the same pattern — 90 cite service brakes, 56 stability control, 47 hydraulic. A minor recall corrects a wrong tire size sticker.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get a dealer VIN report showing every brake recall and inspection was completed, not just scheduled.
  3. On the test drive, note any hard pedal, warning lights, or brake and stability control faults at startup.
  4. Ask about the knee airbag and dashboard recalls, and compare the door-jamb tire sticker to the tires actually fitted.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any BMW dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
