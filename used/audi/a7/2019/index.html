Buying a used 2019 Audi A7? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 43 owner complaints on file

Six recalls apply, none rated critical: a passenger airbag that may not deploy correctly, engine power loss from fluid spills, then lower-severity fuel gauge, brake fluid cap labeling, rearview camera and lane departure warning items. Owner complaints are notably more numerous than on the 2018 or 2020 A7 — 43 filed with NHTSA, led by electrical (24) and engine (9), with one injury and no fires or crashes reported. Those complaints are unverified, but electrical issues are the recurring theme across these model years.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask what electrical faults the owner has had — 24 of 43 complaints cite them.
  3. Verify the rearview camera and lane departure warning both work on a test drive.
  4. Confirm the airbag and engine-power-loss recalls are closed by VIN.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
