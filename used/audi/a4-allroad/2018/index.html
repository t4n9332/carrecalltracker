Buying a used 2018 Audi A4 Allroad? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

Two of the three recalls on this year concern the passenger airbag — one where the airbag may shut off, another for a loose airbag screw. The third, sharp edges on door speaker trim, is minor. Complaint volume with NHTSA is low at nine, though three involved crashes and one an injury, and air bags and electrical lead the list. Those are unverified owner reports, not confirmed defects. None of the recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN with a dealer to confirm both passenger airbag recalls were completed.
  3. Watch the airbag and passenger-occupancy lights through a few starts with someone in the seat.
  4. Inspect the door speaker trim for sharp edges and ask if that recall was performed.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
