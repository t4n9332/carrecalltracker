Buying a used 2021 Audi S4? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

This 2021 S4 has four recalls: two high-severity (a rear axle lock nut that may break, and rear seat belts that may not properly secure child seats) and two medium-severity (a rear axle alignment that may not have been checked, and a backup camera screen that may go blank in reverse). None require stopping driving or parking outside. NHTSA shows only 2 owner complaints for this model year, split between electrical system and an unspecified category, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. These are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm rear axle lock nut and alignment recalls were completed
  3. Test the rear child-seat belts and backup camera function in reverse
  4. Ask for records showing both high-severity recalls were addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
