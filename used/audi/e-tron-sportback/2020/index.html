Buying a used 2020 Audi E-TRON Sportback? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

Short recall list, but the critical item is significant: the brake pedal can detach from the brake booster. The only other recall covers a rearview camera image that may not display. Owner complaints number four with NHTSA, spanning electrical, structure and fuel/propulsion areas, with no crashes, fires or injuries reported. That is too few unverified reports to establish a pattern, so documented completion of the brake recall is the main thing to nail down.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get written, VIN-matched proof the brake pedal/booster recall was completed.
  3. Press the brake pedal firmly on the test drive and note any unusual play or noise.
  4. Confirm the rearview camera image appears reliably every time in reverse.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
