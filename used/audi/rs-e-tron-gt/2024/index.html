Buying a used 2024 Audi RS E-TRON GT? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Severity is the story here: three critical recalls — a portable charging cable that can overheat and two separate actions for an e-tron GT battery that can short circuit and burn — plus front brake hoses that can tear and leak, and the rearview camera recall. None is listed as stop-driving or park-outside. Owner reporting is almost nonexistent: one unverified NHTSA complaint, involving a crash, citing back-over prevention and forward collision avoidance.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get dealer documentation that both battery short-circuit recalls were completed on this VIN
  3. Ask whether the supplied portable charging cable was replaced or repaired under recall
  4. Have the front brake hoses inspected for tears or leaks and confirm that recall was done
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
