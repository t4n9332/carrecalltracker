Buying a used 2020 Audi SQ5? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 7 owner complaints on file

A shorter list than surrounding years: high-severity recalls for a rear suspension nut that can break and for loss of engine power, plus medium recalls covering front seat backrests that may be weak and rear axle alignment that causes uneven tire wear. None carry stop-driving or park-outside instructions. Seven unverified NHTSA owner complaints are on file with no crashes, fires or injuries; the most cited areas were unspecified/other, forward collision avoidance, speed control and back-over prevention — largely driver-assistance territory.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rear suspension nut and engine power-loss recalls were performed
  3. Test the driver-assist features — forward collision warning, cruise control, backup camera — on a real drive
  4. Push and inspect the front seat backrests, and check rear tires for uneven wear
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
