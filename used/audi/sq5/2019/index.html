Buying a used 2019 Audi SQ5? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 19 owner complaints on file

The 2019 SQ5 carries a critical recall for possible brake master cylinder failure, plus high-severity recalls for a passenger airbag that may be damaged and for loss of engine power; trim detachment items are lower severity. Nineteen unverified NHTSA owner complaints are logged, including one crash and no injuries, spread across engine, service brakes, electrical system and exterior lighting. The brake complaints and the master cylinder recall point at the same area, so service history there deserves attention.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the brake master cylinder and passenger airbag recalls were completed by VIN
  3. Drive it and note brake pedal feel, warning lights, and any hesitation or power loss
  4. Check all exterior lights and the wheel arch and rear wheel trim attachment
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
