Buying a used 2021 Audi S8? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

The 2021 S8's standout recall is an A8/S8 engine computer issue that can stall the car; also high-severity are a rear suspension nut that can break and a rear belt that may not secure a child seat. Medium items are heavily display-related: three separate camera recalls, dashboard display loss, and axle alignment causing uneven tire wear. No stop-driving or park-outside warning is attached. Only one unverified NHTSA owner complaint exists, touching power train, electrical and engine, with no crash, fire or injury.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the engine control software recall was completed and whether any stalling occurred
  3. Start the car and cycle reverse to confirm the camera and dashboard displays come up every time
  4. Test child seat installation on the rear belt if relevant, and inspect rear tire wear
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
