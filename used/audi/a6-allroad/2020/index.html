Buying a used 2020 Audi A6 Allroad? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 8 owner complaints on file

Two high-severity recalls define this year: a passenger airbag that may not deploy correctly, and a condition where spills can cause the engine to lose power. Rear axle alignment (uneven tire wear) and a rearview camera that may fail to show an image are the medium items. Just eight unverified owner complaints are on file — no crashes or fires, one injury — and six of those fall under electrical system. No stop-driving or park-outside warning is attached.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the engine has ever lost power or stalled, and look for spill damage around the console.
  3. Confirm the passenger airbag recall repair through a dealer VIN lookup.
  4. Check that the rearview camera image appears every time on startup.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
