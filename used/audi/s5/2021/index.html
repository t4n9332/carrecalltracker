Buying a used 2021 Audi S5? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Five recalls touch this year, the most on this list, covering a rear suspension nut that can break, a rear belt that may not hold a child seat, rear light wiring that can chafe, rear axle alignment causing uneven tire wear, and a backup camera that can go blank. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Just one unverified owner complaint is on file, in the suspension area, with no crashes, fires or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for a completed-recall printout covering all five campaigns for this VIN
  3. Check rear light operation and inspect the rear wiring for chafing or repairs
  4. Test the backup camera and the rear belt child-seat lock before committing
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
