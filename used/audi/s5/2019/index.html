Buying a used 2019 Audi S5? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file

This year brings a suspension-heavy recall list: front shock components that can crack, shared with the Q7 and Q8, a rear suspension nut that can break, plus the passenger airbag shut-off and a rear axle alignment fault causing uneven tire wear. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Only six unverified owner complaints are on file, three for the engine and one each for electrical, fuel or propulsion and wheels, with no crashes, fires or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether both the front shock and rear suspension nut recalls were performed
  3. Listen for knocks or clunks over bumps and inspect the front struts for damage
  4. Check all four tires for uneven wear tied to the rear axle alignment recall
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
