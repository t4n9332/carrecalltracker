Buying a used 2018 Audi S5? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 26 owner complaints on file

This 2018 S5 has two high-severity recalls concerning the passenger airbag: one where it may be disabled, and a separate one involving a faulty cable that can switch it off. Neither recall requires stopping driving or parking outside. NHTSA lists 26 owner complaints for this model year, including 2 crash reports and 1 injury reported, with no fires; engine issues make up the largest category (9), followed by unspecified concerns, airbags, and visibility/wipers. These complaints are unverified owner-filed reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both passenger airbag recalls were completed, including the cable fix
  3. Ask about engine-related repair history for this specific car
  4. Check airbag warning lights and wiper/visibility system function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
