Buying a used 2024 Audi Q3? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 18 owner complaints on file

Two recalls so far on this recent model year: a front passenger airbag that can switch itself off, and a backup camera blackout shared with the VW Tiguan. Neither is a stop-driving item. With 18 unverified NHTSA complaints on a young car, the mix is worth noting — electrical leads, but service brakes is second, an area no recall covers, and the reports include two crashes and two injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Seat a passenger up front and confirm the passenger airbag indicator stays on.
  3. Cycle into reverse several times to see whether the backup camera blacks out.
  4. Ask about brake complaints or dealer brake work — brakes are a top unverified complaint area.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
