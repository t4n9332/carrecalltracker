Buying a used 2021 Audi Q3? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 15 owner complaints on file

Only two recalls: a rear seat belt that may not hold a child seat securely, and fuel that can spill when the gas cap is opened. Neither carried a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Complaint volume is low at 15 unverified NHTSA reports, but they include two crashes and two injuries, and the largest single area is the fuel/propulsion system — the same area the gas cap recall touches. Electrical and engine reports follow.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Open the fuel filler yourself and check for spillage or a strong fuel smell.
  3. Test-fit a child seat with the rear belt and confirm the belt recall was performed.
  4. Ask the seller about any fuel system repairs or check-engine visits.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
