Buying a used 2019 Audi Q3? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 10 owner complaints on file

The headline item is a critical recall for a loose retaining clip that could let the steering lock up, paired with a passenger airbag that may not open correctly. A delayed tire pressure warning is the minor one. Against that, the complaint record is light: 10 unverified NHTSA reports with no crashes, fires or injuries, spread thinly across electrical, engine, fuel system and structure. No recall here carried a stop-driving or park-outside warning.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering retaining clip recall was completed at a dealer, with paperwork.
  3. Ask for documentation on the passenger airbag recall repair.
  4. Watch that the airbag status light and tire pressure warning behave normally.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
