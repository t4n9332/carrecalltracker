Buying a used 2020 Audi E-TRON? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

The 2020 carries the same two critical battery-and-charging recalls as other e-tron years — modules that can overheat and catch fire, and a portable charging cable that can overheat — plus a brake booster part that can come apart and a rearview camera that may not display. What stands out is the complaint record: just one unverified NHTSA complaint, touching electrical and structure, with no reported crashes, fires or injuries. That is a very small sample rather than a clean bill of health.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get recall completion records for the battery module and charging cable remedies.
  3. Ask whether the brake booster recall repair was performed; note any long or inconsistent pedal.
  4. Shift into reverse several times and confirm the backup camera comes up every time.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
