Buying a used 2025 Audi Q8? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file

Only two recalls so far: seat belt buckles missing a rivet, shared with the 2025 Q7, and the rearview camera that may fail to display. Neither is flagged stop-driving or park-outside. The complaint file is nearly empty — three unverified NHTSA reports, one involving a crash, touching engine, air bags and forward collision avoidance. That thin record reflects how new the vehicle is rather than a proven track record, so the picture may change.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check the VIN for the seat belt buckle recall and get proof of the fix
  3. Cycle the rearview camera several times to confirm the image always displays
  4. Ask whether any airbag, engine or driver-assistance warnings have appeared since delivery
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
