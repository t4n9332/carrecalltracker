Buying a used 2021 Audi Q8? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 30 owner complaints on file

Seven recalls for one model year, though none carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning: a rear suspension nut that can break, a rear belt that may not hold a child seat, the fuel pump, rear axle alignment causing uneven tire wear, and three separate display and camera items. The 30 unverified NHTSA complaints skew electrical, with engine, power train and fuel system next; no crashes, fires or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for recall completion records covering the rear suspension nut and the rear seat belt
  3. Inspect rear tires for uneven or inner-edge wear and ask if the rear axle alignment recall was done
  4. Confirm the dashboard display and backup camera power up normally, with no blank screens
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
