Buying a used 2021 Audi A5 Cabriolet? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 7 owner complaints on file

A short record for this year. One high-severity recall — a rear belt that may not hold a child seat — plus two medium ones: rear axle alignment that can cause uneven tire wear, and a backup camera that may display a blank screen. Only seven unverified owner complaints are on file with NHTSA, with no crashes, fires or injuries reported, spread thinly across electrical, powertrain and air bag categories. No stop-driving or park-outside warnings apply.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the child-seat belt recall was completed if the rear seat will carry a car seat.
  3. Check rear tires for uneven or inner-edge wear from the alignment issue.
  4. Cycle the backup camera several times to see whether the display ever comes up blank.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
