Buying a used 2021 Audi A4? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 11 owner complaints on file

Four recalls, two rated high: a rear suspension nut that can break and a rear belt that may not hold a child seat. Two medium items — rear axle alignment causing uneven tire wear and a backup camera that can show a blank black screen — round it out. Owner complaints are few (11, unverified) but three mention crashes and one an injury, with electrical, powertrain and lane departure the recurring areas. No stop-driving or park-outside advisories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. If a child seat will ride back there, confirm the rear belt recall repair and test the belt lock.
  3. Cycle the backup camera through several restarts to check for a blank screen.
  4. Ask for recall completion records on the rear suspension nut and the axle alignment.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
