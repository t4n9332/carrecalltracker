Buying a used 2020 Audi A4? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 127 owner complaints on file

Complaint volume stands out: 127 unverified owner reports to NHTSA, far more than the neighboring Audi model years here, and heavily concentrated in electrical system (91) and back-over prevention (43) rather than mechanical failures — one crash, no fires or injuries. The recalls are a rear suspension nut that can break, a passenger airbag that may switch off, and rear axle alignment causing uneven tire wear. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get written confirmation the rear suspension nut recall was completed on this VIN.
  3. Inspect rear tires for uneven inner-edge wear and ask if the axle alignment recall was done.
  4. Exercise the backup camera and all electronics from a cold start, given the electrical complaint pattern.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
