Buying a used 2020 Audi S6? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

The 2020 S6 carries the A6/A7 recall set: three high-severity actions — a passenger airbag that may not deploy properly, a rear suspension nut that can break, and engine power loss caused by spills — plus medium items covering rear axle alignment that causes uneven tire wear, a fuel gauge that can read wrong, and a rearview camera image that may fail to display. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Only two unverified NHTSA owner complaints are on file, with no crashes, fires or injuries; electrical was the most cited area.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get a VIN recall check confirming the airbag and rear suspension nut remedies were performed
  3. Inspect rear tire wear for the uneven pattern the axle alignment recall describes
  4. Test the backup camera and fuel gauge through a full start-up and drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
