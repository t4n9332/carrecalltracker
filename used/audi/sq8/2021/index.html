Buying a used 2021 Audi SQ8? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file

The 2021 SQ8 has the longest list here — six recalls, headed by a critical one for engine cylinders that may crack, shared with SQ7 and RS models. Others cover a rear belt that may not hold a child seat, rear axle alignment causing uneven tire wear, and three separate display faults: blank backup camera, failing rearview camera image, and loss of the dashboard display. Three unverified NHTSA complaints, two in power train, no crashes or fires.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get dealer confirmation the engine cylinder recall was performed
  3. Inspect tires for uneven inner/outer wear and ask about rear axle alignment
  4. Check the dashboard display and both camera views, and test the rear belt lock
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
