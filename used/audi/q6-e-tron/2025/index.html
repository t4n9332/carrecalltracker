Buying a used 2025 Audi Q6 E-TRON? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 31 owner complaints on file

A first-model-year EV with a short recall list and nothing rated critical: rear seat belts that may not lock child seats, plus two separate rearview camera defects, one a blurry image and one a camera that may not display at all. The complaint side is busier, with 31 unverified NHTSA reports, roughly two-thirds of them electrical, then forward collision avoidance, power train and fuel/propulsion. One crash and two injuries are recorded. The pattern leans toward electronics rather than mechanical failure.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both rearview camera recalls were performed, then check image clarity yourself
  3. Test the rear belt locking mode with a child seat if you use one
  4. Ask what software updates the car has received given the electrical complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
