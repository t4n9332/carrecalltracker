Buying a used 2019 Audi Q5? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 50 owner complaints on file

The 2019 drops the caliper recall that hit the 2018 but keeps the critical brake master cylinder failure, along with a possibly damaged passenger airbag and engine power loss; two trim recalls are minor. Complaints total 50 unverified NHTSA reports, a much smaller count than 2018, though they include five crashes and six injuries. Electrical leads, with engine, service brakes and steering close behind — brakes and engine overlapping the recall topics.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for the master cylinder recall record and check for a soft or sinking brake pedal.
  3. Verify the passenger airbag recall inspection or replacement was completed.
  4. Ask whether the car has lost power or gone into limp mode, and have fault codes scanned.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
