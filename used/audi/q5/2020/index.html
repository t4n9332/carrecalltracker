Buying a used 2020 Audi Q5? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 25 owner complaints on file

This model year carries six recalls: a portable charging cable fire risk, a rear axle lock nut that may break, water damage that can cut power (Q5/SQ5), rear wheel arch trim that can detach, a front seat frame weld defect, and a rear axle alignment check. NHTSA has logged 25 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 3 crashes and 1 fire, with electrical system (8) the most cited category, followed by unknown/other (5), engine (4), and steering (3).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the charging cable and rear axle lock nut recalls were completed
  3. Ask about any water intrusion or electrical repair history
  4. Verify the seat frame weld and rear axle alignment recalls were addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
