Buying a used 2021 Audi Q5? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 71 owner complaints on file

The busiest Q5 year listed here, with eight recalls and three rated critical: engine bearings that can fail, an overheating portable charging cable, and a hybrid battery that can overheat and catch fire. Also engine power loss, a rear suspension nut, a rear belt that may not hold a child seat, axle alignment and a blank backup camera. NHTSA logged 71 unverified owner complaints, weighted to electrical (26), engine (17) and power train (12), with two crashes and two injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm by VIN that the engine bearing and hybrid battery recalls are closed
  3. On plug-in hybrid versions, ask what battery remedy was applied and when
  4. Test the backup camera and check the rear belt locks a child seat properly
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
