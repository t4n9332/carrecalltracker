Buying a used 2021 Audi Q5? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 71 owner complaints on file

This model year has eight recalls, three rated critical: engine bearings that may fail, a portable charging cable fire risk, and a high-voltage battery that may overheat. Other recalls address a rear axle lock nut, rear child-seat belts, water damage cutting power, axle alignment, and a backup camera that may go blank. NHTSA has logged 71 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 2 crashes and 2 injuries; electrical system (26) and engine (17) are most cited, followed by power train (12) and forward collision avoidance (10).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine bearing, charging cable, and high-voltage battery recalls are completed
  3. Ask about any electrical system repairs, given it's the top complaint category
  4. Verify rear axle lock nut/alignment and child-seat belt recalls were addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
