Buying a used 2020 Audi A5? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 60 owner complaints on file

Sixty unverified owner complaints is high for this group, and they cluster tightly: electrical system (41) and back-over prevention (15), with one crash and no fires or injuries reported. There are four recalls — a rear suspension nut that can break and a passenger airbag that may switch off, both high severity, plus rear light wiring that can chafe and rear axle alignment that wears tires unevenly. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Test infotainment, driver assists and the rear camera thoroughly, given the electrical complaint cluster.
  3. Check all rear lights and ask whether the wiring chafe recall was performed.
  4. Get dealer confirmation the rear suspension nut recall is closed.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
