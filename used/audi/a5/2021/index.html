Buying a used 2021 Audi A5? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 7 owner complaints on file

Five recalls on record, more than any other 2021 Audi here, but the complaint file is thin: seven unverified owner reports to NHTSA with no crashes, fires or injuries. The two high-severity items are a rear suspension nut that can break and a rear belt that may not hold a child seat. The medium ones cover rear light wiring chafing, rear axle alignment causing uneven tire wear, and a backup camera that can go blank. No stop-driving warnings.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask the dealer to check all five recalls by VIN and say which remain open.
  3. Verify the backup camera and rear lights work before committing.
  4. Confirm the child-seat belt recall repair if kids will ride in back.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
