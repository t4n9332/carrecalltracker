Buying a used 2021 Audi A5? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 7 owner complaints on file

This 2021 A5 has recalls for rear wiring that lacks scuff protection, a rear axle lock nut that may break, rear seat belts that may not properly secure child seats, rear axle alignment that may not have been checked, and a backup camera screen that may go blank in reverse. NHTSA has 7 owner complaints on file (unverified reports), with none mentioning a crash, fire, or injury. Unknown/other and electrical system each account for 2 complaints, with power train and air bags at 1 each.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rear wiring and axle recalls were completed
  3. Test the backup camera and rear child-seat belt anchors
  4. Ask about any electrical system issues reported
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
