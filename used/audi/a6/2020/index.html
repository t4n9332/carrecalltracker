Buying a used 2020 Audi A6? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 25 owner complaints on file

Nine recalls apply, including the starter generator fire risk, two separate passenger-airbag recalls, a rear suspension nut that can break, and engine power loss from spilled fluids. The rest are lower-severity items: rear axle alignment causing uneven tire wear, fuel gauge accuracy, brake fluid cap labeling and rearview camera display. Owner complaints drop to 25 with no crashes, fires or injuries reported; electrical leads at 17. Those reports are unverified.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the starter generator and both passenger-airbag recalls are closed by VIN.
  3. Inspect rear tires for uneven wear tied to the rear axle alignment recall.
  4. Confirm the rear suspension nut recall was done and listen for rear-end noise.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
