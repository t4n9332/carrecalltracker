Buying a used 2018 Audi A6? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

Only two recalls apply here, but both are serious: fuel rails that can leak gasoline, and a passenger airbag that may not deploy. Complaint volume is tiny — two reports filed with NHTSA, touching electrical, fuel and airbag areas, including one fire. These are unverified owner reports and far too few to show a pattern, so the recall record carries more weight than the complaint record on this model year. Confirm both repairs were completed by VIN.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for VIN-specific proof the fuel rail leak recall was completed.
  3. Confirm the passenger airbag recall was performed and the airbag light cycles normally.
  4. Check the engine bay and underbody for fuel odor or staining after a warm-up.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
