Buying a used 2021 Audi RS Q8? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

Seven recalls, headed by a critical one for engine cylinders that may crack on SQ7, SQ8 and RS engines. Below that: a rear suspension nut that can break, a rear belt that may not secure a child seat, rear axle alignment causing uneven tire wear, and three overlapping display and backup camera actions. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Only two unverified NHTSA complaints exist, both electrical with one steering mention — too few to show a pattern.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for records on the cracked-cylinder recall and any engine work, plus a compression or leak-down check
  3. Confirm the rear suspension nut and rear seat belt recalls were completed
  4. Inspect rear tire wear and verify the dashboard and backup camera displays function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
