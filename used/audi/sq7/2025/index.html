Buying a used 2025 Audi SQ7? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

The 2025 SQ7 has a critical recall of its own: fuel pump bolts that may leak fuel, creating a fire risk. It also shares Audi's recurring rearview camera display failure. Neither recall is flagged stop-driving or park-outside. Two unverified owner complaints are on file with NHTSA, one citing an injury, spread across steering, lane departure and electrical system — too few reports to show a pattern this early in the model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump bolt recall remedy is done, by VIN at a dealer
  3. Smell and look for fuel odor or damp spots around the engine bay
  4. Test the rearview camera, steering feel and lane departure on the drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
