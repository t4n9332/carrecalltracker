Buying a used 2021 Audi SQ7? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file

The headline item is a critical recall covering SQ7, SQ8 and RS engines whose cylinders may crack — an engine-internal issue, though no stop-driving or park-outside instruction is attached. Also listed: a high-severity rear belt that may not hold a child seat, and medium recalls for axle alignment tire wear, dashboard display loss and two rearview camera faults. Six unverified NHTSA owner complaints, none involving crashes, fires or injuries, cite electrical system, engine, forward collision avoidance and back-over prevention.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the engine cylinder recall was inspected or remedied, and request the documentation
  3. Get a pre-purchase engine inspection including compression or leak-down and coolant/oil condition
  4. Confirm the dashboard and rearview camera displays work consistently, and test the rear child-seat belt
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
