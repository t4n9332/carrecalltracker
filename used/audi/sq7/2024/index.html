Buying a used 2024 Audi SQ7? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

The 2024 SQ7's recall list is short: a driver seat airbag that may be loose, shared with Q7 and Q8, and a rearview camera that may fail to display an image, a defect Audi has repeated across several model years. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. NHTSA shows a single owner complaint, an unverified report in the electrical system category, with no crashes, fires or injuries attached to it.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. VIN-check that the driver seat airbag recall repair was completed
  3. Push and pull the driver seat to feel for any looseness
  4. Restart the car a few times and confirm the backup camera image appears
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
