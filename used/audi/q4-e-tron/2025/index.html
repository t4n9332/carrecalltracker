Buying a used 2025 Audi Q4 E-TRON? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

The 2025 Audi Q4 E-Tron's listed recall record centers on battery fire risk and potential loss of drive power. Documentation should establish the status of both issues for the individual vehicle. NHTSA has 4 owner complaints, which are unverified reports, with 2 listing service brakes, 2 electrical systems, 1 forward collision avoidance, and 1 tires. These category counts can overlap within complaints. The supplied complaint record lists 0 crashes, 0 fires, and 0 injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify VIN-specific battery fire and drive-power recall completion.
  3. Ask about brake problems, electrical warnings, or interrupted propulsion.
  4. Review collision-avoidance fault history and inspect tire condition.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
