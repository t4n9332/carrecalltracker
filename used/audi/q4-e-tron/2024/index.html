Buying a used 2024 Audi Q4 E-TRON? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 22 owner complaints on file

Three recalls apply to this model year: a critical issue with overheating portable charging cables, a high-severity risk of losing drive power, and a medium-severity headlight software issue. NHTSA has 22 owner complaints on file (unverified reports), including 2 crashes and 2 injuries, with the top categories being electrical system (15), service brakes (4), power train (2), and steering (2).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the charging cable recall was completed and inspect charging equipment condition
  3. Ask if the vehicle has experienced any loss of drive power
  4. Confirm the headlight software update was applied
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
