Buying a used 2024 Audi Q7? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

A short record so far: three recalls, two of them airbag related, covering a driver seat airbag that may be loose and a front passenger airbag that may switch itself off, plus a rearview camera that may fail to display. Only two unverified NHTSA complaints have been filed, with no crashes, fires or injuries, and they fall under unknown or other and lane departure. That thin file reflects how recently this model year reached buyers as much as anything else.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both airbag recalls were completed before you take delivery
  3. Have a dealer verify the driver seat airbag fitment and passenger airbag status light
  4. Check that the rearview camera displays reliably across several separate starts
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
