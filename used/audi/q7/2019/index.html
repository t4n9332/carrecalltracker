Buying a used 2019 Audi Q7? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 61 owner complaints on file

This model year has four recalls: a steering shaft bolt that may loosen, rated critical; shock absorbers that may crack (shared with the Q8 and S5); side curtain airbags that may not inflate; and C-pillar padding that doesn't sufficiently absorb impact. NHTSA has logged 61 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 5 crashes and no reported fires or injuries; steering and suspension (10 each) are the top categories, followed by electrical system (9) and fuel/propulsion system (8).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering shaft bolt and shock absorber recalls were completed
  3. Ask whether the side curtain airbag recall remedy was performed
  4. Ask about any steering or suspension repairs, the top complaint categories
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
