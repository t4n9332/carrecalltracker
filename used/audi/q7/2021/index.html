Buying a used 2021 Audi Q7? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 72 owner complaints on file

Nine recalls, the most of any vehicle here, though none is rated critical. Four are high severity: door sensors that can delay airbag deployment, a rear suspension nut that can break, a rear belt that may not hold a child seat, and a fuel pump that can fail. The rest are camera, dashboard display and axle alignment issues. That matches the complaints, where 43 of 72 unverified NHTSA reports are electrical, then engine at 18. Five injuries are recorded.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Pull a full VIN recall report, since nine separate campaigns need confirming
  3. From a cold start, test the dashboard display, backup camera and infotainment repeatedly
  4. Ask about fuel pump work and inspect rear tire wear for alignment problems
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
