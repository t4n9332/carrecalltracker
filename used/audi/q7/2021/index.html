Buying a used 2021 Audi Q7? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 72 owner complaints on file

This model year has nine recalls, five rated high: a door sensor connector that may detach, a front passenger air bag problem, a rear axle lock nut, rear belts that may not properly secure child seats, and a fuel pump that may fail. Four more, rated medium, cover axle alignment, a backup camera, an instrument panel display, and a rearview camera. NHTSA has logged 72 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 3 crashes and 5 injuries; electrical system (43) is the top category, followed by engine (18) and unknown/other (11).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the door sensor connector and fuel pump recalls were completed
  3. Ask about front passenger air bag and rear axle lock nut recall status
  4. Ask about any electrical system repairs, by far the top complaint category
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
