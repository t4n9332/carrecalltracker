Buying a used 2018 Audi Q7? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 142 owner complaints on file

This model year has three recalls, two rated critical: fuel rails that may leak (shared with the A6 and A7), and a steering shaft bolt that may loosen (shared with the Q8). A third recall covers C-pillar padding that doesn't sufficiently absorb impact. NHTSA has logged 142 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 3 crashes and 3 injuries; engine (38) is the top category, followed by exterior lighting (23), unknown/other (19), and suspension (16).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel rail leak and steering shaft bolt recalls were completed
  3. Ask about engine-related repairs or complaints, the top complaint category
  4. Ask whether the C-pillar padding recall remedy was performed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
