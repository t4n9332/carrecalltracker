Buying a used 2019 Audi RS5? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file

Four recalls stack up this year: a rear suspension nut that can break, the passenger airbag shut-off issue, the RS5 Coupe wrong battery terminal cover, and a rear axle alignment fault that causes uneven tire wear. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. NHTSA holds three unverified owner complaints, two electrical, one structural and one unclassified, with no crashes, fires or injuries. The suspension and alignment items are the ones that leave visible physical evidence.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the rear suspension nut recall was performed and get the repair order
  3. Inspect rear tire inner shoulders for uneven wear from the axle alignment fault
  4. Verify the correct battery terminal cover and completed passenger airbag recall
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Audi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
