Buying a used 2019 Infiniti QX60? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 37 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year has a critical recall for a brake fluid leak that may cause a fire, with a park-outside advisory until repaired, plus a medium backup camera recall. 37 owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA (unverified reports), including 2 crashes and 2 injuries, with no fires reported among them. Power train complaints are most common at 10, followed by engine at 9, electrical system at 6, and back-over prevention at 5. Confirming the brake fluid recall was completed before relying on garage parking is worth doing.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm brake fluid leak/fire recall repair completed
  3. Ask whether the park-outside advisory was followed before repair
  4. Ask about power train and engine issue history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Infiniti dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
