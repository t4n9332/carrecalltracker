Buying a used 2022 Infiniti QX60? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 22 owner complaints on file

No critical recalls here — the two on record are medium severity: a missing second-row seat bolt shared with the Nissan Pathfinder, and headlights that aim too low at speed. Neither carried a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Owner complaints to NHTSA total 22, all unverified reports, spread across unknown/other (6), electrical system (5), engine (3) and power train (2). No crashes, fires or injuries appear among the complaints filed for this model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof the second-row seat bolt recall was inspected or corrected.
  3. Verify the headlight aim recall remedy was applied, and drive the car after dark.
  4. Check electronics thoroughly — screens, cameras, sensors — since electrical leads the complaints.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Infiniti dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
