Buying a used 2022 Infiniti QX60? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 22 owner complaints on file

This model year has a high-severity recall for a missing seat track bolt, plus a medium recall for headlights that may aim too low at speed. 22 owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA (unverified reports), with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. Complaints are spread across unknown/other at 6, electrical system at 5, engine at 3, and power train at 2, without one dominant category. Confirming the seat track bolt recall was completed and checking headlight aim are worth doing.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm seat track bolt recall repair completed
  3. Check headlight aim and adjustment
  4. Ask about electrical system issue history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Infiniti dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
