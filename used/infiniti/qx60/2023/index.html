Buying a used 2023 Infiniti QX60? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 27 owner complaints on file

Three recalls, none critical. The high-severity one covers a driver's seat that may loosen, shared with the Nissan Rogue and Pathfinder; two medium ones address a second-row seat that may not lock and headlights that aim too low at speed. Seat-related issues therefore dominate the record. Of 27 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA, unknown/other leads at 8, then electrical system (7), engine (5) and fuel/propulsion (4). One fire and four injuries are reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Grab the seat back and base and check for movement; confirm the driver's seat recall was done.
  3. Latch and tug the second-row seat to verify it locks, and ask about that recall's status.
  4. Ask whether the headlight aim recall was performed, then check the beam pattern at night.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Infiniti dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
