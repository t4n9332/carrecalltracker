Buying a used 2024 Infiniti QX60? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 19 owner complaints on file

The notable recall is high severity: a Nissan and Infiniti driver airbag that may deploy improperly. Two medium recalls follow — headlights aiming too low at speed, and a loose seat belt part shared with the Nissan Pathfinder. None carried a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Nineteen owner complaints were filed with NHTSA, all unverified, led by electrical system (8), then unknown/other (4), forward collision avoidance (4) and engine (3). One crash and one injury are reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the driver airbag recall remedy was completed and no airbag warning light is lit.
  3. Tug and inspect the seat belt anchors, and ask if the seat belt recall repair was done.
  4. Exercise the electronics and driver aids, noting any false forward collision warnings.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Infiniti dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
