Buying a used 2019 Infiniti Q70? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

Two recalls cover this year: a high-severity driveshaft that can break, also affecting the Q50, and a lower-severity backup camera image that can stay off. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Only two owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA — unverified reports with no crashes, fires or injuries — mentioning power train and engine twice each plus vehicle speed control. That volume is too small to characterize the model year, so the driveshaft recall is the item to verify.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the driveshaft recall repair is recorded as completed for this VIN
  3. Feel for vibration or clunking from the driveline during acceleration and coasting
  4. Check the backup camera and that throttle response is smooth and consistent
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Infiniti dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
