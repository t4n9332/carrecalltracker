Buying a used 2021 Infiniti QX80? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 7 owner complaints on file

Three recalls, none carrying stop-driving or park-outside instructions. The fuel pump defect is the significant one, since a failing pump can leave the vehicle stalling or unable to restart. The backup camera shutting off and the incorrect weight-rating label are lower-stakes fixes. Only seven owner complaints were filed with NHTSA — these are unverified reports — with no crashes, fires or injuries, spread thinly across fuel/propulsion, vehicle speed control, engine and power train. A light complaint record for the model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN with a dealer to confirm the fuel pump recall remedy was actually completed, not just mailed.
  3. Ask whether the vehicle has ever stalled or failed to restart, and start it both cold and warm.
  4. Shift to reverse and confirm the camera image stays up; check the door-jamb weight rating label was corrected.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Infiniti dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
