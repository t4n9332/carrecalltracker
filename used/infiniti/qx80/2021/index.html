Buying a used 2021 Infiniti QX80? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 7 owner complaints on file

This model year has a high-severity recall for a fuel pump that may fail, a medium-severity recall where the rearview camera image may not display, and a low-severity incorrect weight label. NHTSA lists 7 owner complaints (unverified reports), with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. Complaint areas are split evenly: fuel/propulsion system, vehicle speed control, and engine each have 2, with power train at 1. Two of the three recalls involve fuel delivery and rearview camera display.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump recall repair was completed
  3. Test that the rearview camera reliably displays an image when in reverse
  4. Ask about any fuel system or engine issues reported during ownership
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Infiniti dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
