Buying a used 2025 Infiniti QX80? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 8 owner complaints on file

The recall record for this year is dominated by one system: three separate backup-camera recalls, each about the image going blank or failing to display, plus a low-severity incorrect weight-rating label. None carry stop-driving or park-outside instructions. Eight owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA — unverified reports — including one crash, a low volume typical of a recent model year. Those complaints fall under unspecified issues, electrical system, wheels and forward collision avoidance.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask the dealer which of the three camera recalls apply to this VIN and whether each remedy is done.
  3. Cycle into reverse on several separate starts during the test drive to see if the screen ever goes blank.
  4. Ask about electrical or driver-assist warning lights, any collision-avoidance faults, and whether the vehicle has been in a crash.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Infiniti dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
