Buying a used 2020 Infiniti QX80? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file

The recall to focus on is high severity: fuel pumps that can fail on the Nissan Armada and Infiniti QX80, which carried no stop-driving or park-outside instruction. A low-severity recall for a wrong weight rating label also applies. The complaint record is thin — just 6 unverified reports filed with NHTSA, split across unknown/other (2), forward collision avoidance (2), service brakes (1) and visibility/wiper (1), with no crashes, fires or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for documentation that the fuel pump recall repair was completed on this VIN.
  3. Note any hesitation, stalling or hard starting during a longer test drive.
  4. Confirm the weight rating label is the corrected one and check brake feel and wiper operation.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Infiniti dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
