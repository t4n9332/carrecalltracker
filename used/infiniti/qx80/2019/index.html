Buying a used 2019 Infiniti QX80? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 20 owner complaints on file

This year carries the same two recalls as the 2018: a medium one for a backup camera image that can stay off, and a low-severity wrong weight rating label shared with the Nissan Armada. Neither involved a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA rise to 20, all unverified reports, led by electrical system (8) and unknown/other (5), with fuel/propulsion (2) and visibility/wiper (2) behind. No crashes, fires or injuries are reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the backup camera recall was completed and the image appears without delay.
  3. Confirm the weight rating label matches the vehicle before relying on it for towing.
  4. Test electrical items and the wipers and washers, the two areas owners mention most.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Infiniti dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
