Buying a used 2019 Infiniti QX50? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 139 owner complaints on file

This model year has a critical recall for engine bearings that may fail, plus medium recalls for a knee airbag cover that may detach in a crash and a backup camera image that can stay off. 139 owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA (unverified reports), including 3 crashes, 1 fire, and 3 injuries. Engine-related complaints dominate the total, with 53 filed under engine and 31 under engine and engine cooling. Confirming the engine bearing recall was completed and reviewing engine service history are worth doing given this pattern.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm engine bearing recall repair completed
  3. Request engine service and maintenance records
  4. Confirm knee airbag cover recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Infiniti dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
