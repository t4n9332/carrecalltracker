Buying a used 2021 Infiniti Q60? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file

Two recalls apply to this year: a high-severity one for an engine that may stall during acceleration, shared with the Q50, and a lower-severity backup camera screen that shuts off. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA are unusually few — three, all unverified, with no crashes, fires or injuries reported — touching engine (2), structure, power train and forward collision avoidance. That is too small a sample to read as a reliability signal either way.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get written confirmation the stalling recall repair was completed
  3. Test acceleration from a stop and from low speed for any stumble or shutdown
  4. Verify the backup camera and forward collision warning both function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Infiniti dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
