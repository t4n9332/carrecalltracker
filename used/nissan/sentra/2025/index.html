Buying a used 2025 Nissan Sentra? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 30 owner complaints on file

This year's list is assembly-related: three high-severity recalls cover a driver's seat that may not be properly secured, doors that can open while driving, and a driveshaft that may slip and create a rollaway risk. A medium recall covers windshield air bubbles that can block vision. With only 30 complaints on file, the record is thin and scattered — unknown/other (6), power train, electrical system and tires (4 each) — but 6 of those unverified reports mention crashes and 3 mention injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Rock the driver's seat fore-aft and side-to-side to check it is firmly secured to the floor.
  3. Confirm the door recall was performed and check that each door latches firmly and stays shut.
  4. Ask about the driveshaft recall, test that the car holds in Park on a slope, and inspect the windshield for bubbles.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
