Buying a used 2025 Nissan Sentra? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 30 owner complaints on file

This model year has three high-severity recalls: a driver's seat frame that may be loose, doors that may open while driving, and a driveshaft issue that may cause power loss, along with a medium recall for windshield air bubbles. NHTSA has logged 30 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 6 crashes and 3 injuries, with no fires reported. Complaint categories include an "unknown or other" group (6), plus powertrain, electrical system, and tires, each with 4 reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the driver's seat frame recall repair was completed
  3. Test that all doors latch securely, including while driving
  4. Confirm the driveshaft recall repair was completed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
