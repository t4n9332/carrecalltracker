Buying a used 2019 Nissan Sentra? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 221 owner complaints on file

This model year has two medium-severity recalls: a backup camera that can stay off, and a brake light switch that may fail. NHTSA has logged 221 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 16 crashes, 1 fire, and 18 injuries. Electrical system complaints lead at 64, followed by powertrain (54), an "unknown or other" category (45), and service brakes (38).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the backup camera recall repair was performed
  3. Test the brake lights and brake light switch for proper function
  4. Ask about any electrical system repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
