Buying a used 2018 Nissan Sentra? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 185 owner complaints on file

Recall exposure is light: just two, both medium severity — a backup camera image that can stay off and brake lights that may fail to light. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. The complaint file is the bigger story: 185 unverified owner reports led by power train (46), service brakes (40), and electrical system (32), with 17 crashes, 3 fires and 11 injuries logged. Power train and braking behavior are the themes those categories point toward.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Have someone confirm both brake lights illuminate, and ask if the brake light recall was performed
  3. Test drive for transmission hesitation, shudder or slipping given the power train complaint volume
  4. Ask for brake service history and check the backup camera powers up every time
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
