Buying a used 2018 Nissan Sentra? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 185 owner complaints on file

This model year has two medium-severity recalls: one where the backup camera image can stay off, and one for a brake light switch that may fail. NHTSA has logged 185 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 17 crashes, 3 fires, and 11 injuries. The largest complaint categories are powertrain (46) and service brakes (40), followed by an "unknown or other" group (38) and electrical system reports (32).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the backup camera recall repair was performed
  3. Test the brake lights and brake light switch for proper function
  4. Ask about any powertrain or brake-related repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
