Buying a used 2021 Nissan Sentra? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 131 owner complaints on file

The same two critical tie-rod recalls carry over, covering rods that can bend and break and cause loss of steering; steering is also the top complaint area at 40 reports. A third recall addresses Altima/Sentra backup camera wiring. Fewer complaints than 2020 — 131 — but a higher share involve harm: 14 crashes, 8 injuries and 1 fire among unverified owner reports. Forward collision avoidance and electrical system tie at 38 each, with service brakes at 23.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN for open tie-rod recalls and ask whether the steering has ever felt loose or wandering.
  3. Check the backup camera in reverse for blanking or flicker, which the wiring recall addresses.
  4. Brake firmly from speed on the test drive and check for any stored or active warning lights.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
