Buying a used 2025 Nissan Murano? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 21 owner complaints on file

This is a current model year, so the record is short. The recall that matters most covers front brake calipers that may break, shared with Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60; a second, lower-severity recall covers a backup camera that may not show an image. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. NHTSA shows 21 unverified owner complaints, 2 crashes and 3 injuries, spread across electrical system (7), forward collision avoidance, power train and engine (4 each).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm by VIN that the front brake caliper recall repair has been performed.
  3. Shift to reverse several times and confirm the backup camera displays an image every time.
  4. Exercise the electrical features — infotainment, windows, warning lights — since that is the top complaint area.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
