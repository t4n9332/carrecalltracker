Buying a used 2020 Nissan Murano? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 45 owner complaints on file

This 2020 Murano has two high-severity recalls: a suspension link that may separate, and steering parts shared with Infiniti models that may crack. NHTSA has logged 45 owner complaints for this model year, including 1 crash and 2 injuries reported, with 0 fires; forward collision avoidance accounts for 14 of those complaints, followed by 10 in the unknown/other category and 7 for suspension. These are unverified owner-submitted reports, not confirmed defects.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Have the suspension link recall repair verified
  3. Confirm the steering parts recall status with the dealer
  4. Ask about any suspension-related noises or handling issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
