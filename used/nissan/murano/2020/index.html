Buying a used 2020 Nissan Murano? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 45 owner complaints on file

Both open recalls on the 2020 Murano involve chassis hardware: a suspension link that may break at the ball joint, and steering and axle parts that may be weaker than specified. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Complaint volume with NHTSA is modest at 45, with 1 crash and 2 injuries reported. The largest cluster of these unverified owner reports concerns forward collision avoidance (14), followed by suspension (7) and power train (4).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for dealer proof both recalls — suspension link and steering/axle parts — were completed by VIN.
  3. Have a shop inspect the front suspension ball joints and links for play, damage, or torn boots.
  4. On the test drive, check the forward collision warning and automatic braking, including any false alerts.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
