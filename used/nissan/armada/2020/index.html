Buying a used 2020 Nissan Armada? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 10 owner complaints on file

A short recall list for the 2020 Armada: a high-severity fuel pump that can fail, shared with the Infiniti QX80, and a low-severity recall for an incorrect weight-rating label. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Owner complaints are few — 10 filed with NHTSA, all unverified — though they include one crash, one fire and one injury. Electrical system (5) and forward collision avoidance (3) lead the areas cited, but at this volume there is little owner-reported pattern to read either way.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump recall was performed, and ask whether the vehicle has ever stalled or failed to start.
  3. Look for the corrected weight-rating label on the driver's door jamb if you plan to tow or haul.
  4. Ask about electrical faults and whether the forward collision system has ever triggered on its own.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
