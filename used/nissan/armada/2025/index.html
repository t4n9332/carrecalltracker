Buying a used 2025 Nissan Armada? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 21 owner complaints on file

Both recalls on the 2025 Armada are lower severity: a backup camera that may not show an image, and an incorrect weight-rating label shared with the Infiniti QX80. Neither is flagged stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA has 21 owner complaints on file, unverified reports tied to 2 crashes and 1 injury, with no fires — a modest count for a vehicle this new. Electrical system leads at 7, followed by unspecified issues (6), vehicle speed control (3) and seats (2).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both recalls were addressed before delivery, and test the backup camera repeatedly in reverse.
  3. Verify the corrected weight-rating label is on the door jamb if towing or payload capacity matters to you.
  4. Ask about electrical faults, seat problems and any cruise or speed-control irregularities owners have reported.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
