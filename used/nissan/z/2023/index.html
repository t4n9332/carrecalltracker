Buying a used 2023 Nissan Z? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

Both open recalls concern one system: the pop-up hood meant to rise and cushion a pedestrian in an impact, which may not activate properly. Both are rated medium, and neither carried a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The complaint file is very thin — 2 owner reports to NHTSA, unverified, with no crashes, fires or injuries, spread across fuel/propulsion, electrical system and structure. With so few reports, the recall record is the more informative signal for this year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get a VIN recall check confirming both pop-up hood remedies were completed
  3. Ask whether the hood has ever been replaced or repaired after a front impact
  4. Verify no restraint or safety-system warning lights stay lit after startup
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
