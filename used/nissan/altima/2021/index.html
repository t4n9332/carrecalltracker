Buying a used 2021 Nissan Altima? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 70 owner complaints on file

Only two recalls apply to the 2021 Altima: the critical tie rod that can come apart and cause steering loss, and backup camera wiring that can fail. That is a much shorter list than the 2019 and 2020 cars, and neither recall is flagged stop-driving or park-outside. Among 70 owner complaints filed with NHTSA — unverified reports covering 7 crashes, 1 fire and 4 injuries — the leading areas are forward collision avoidance (22), service brakes (21) and electrical system (21).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the tie rod recall was performed and check the steering for looseness, wander or noise while driving.
  3. Test the backup camera repeatedly in reverse to rule out the intermittent wiring failure covered by the recall.
  4. Ask whether the forward collision system has ever warned or braked unexpectedly, and how the brakes have behaved.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
