Buying a used 2018 Nissan Altima? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 225 owner complaints on file

Two recalls apply to the 2018 Altima: a high-severity hood latch that may rust and stick, and a backup camera image that can stay off. Neither is flagged stop-driving or park-outside. The more notable figure is volume — 225 owner complaints filed with NHTSA, which are unverified reports, linked to 11 crashes and 6 injuries, with no fires. After unspecified issues, service brakes (49), electrical system (37) and air bags (36) are the most-cited areas on this model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the hood latch recall was completed, then open and close the hood yourself to check it latches cleanly.
  3. Put the car in reverse and verify the backup camera image comes on every time, not intermittently.
  4. Ask about brake work and check the air bag warning light cycles off at startup, given those complaint areas.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
