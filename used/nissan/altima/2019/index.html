Buying a used 2019 Nissan Altima? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 231 owner complaints on file

This model year carries a long list: seven recalls, three of them critical — a fuel line clip that may leak fuel, tires that may fail suddenly, and turbo engines that may fail or burn. Two high-severity items follow: brake lights that stay on with rollaway risk, and a fuel pump ring that may leak gas. None are flagged stop-driving. Of 231 unverified owner complaints to NHTSA, 22 involve crashes, 5 fires and 11 injuries, led by electrical system, engine and forward collision avoidance.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN for all open recalls, especially the fuel line clip and fuel pump ring, and check for any fuel odor.
  3. Ask whether the car has the turbo engine and request engine service and oil-change records for it.
  4. Verify the recalled tires were replaced, and confirm the brake lights go out when the pedal is released.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
