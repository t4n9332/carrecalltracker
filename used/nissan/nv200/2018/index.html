Buying a used 2018 Nissan NV200? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 14 owner complaints on file

The 2018 NV200 carries a stall recall shared with the Chevrolet City Express, plus a lower-severity recall for a backup camera image that can stay off. Neither has a stop-driving or park-outside warning. This is a low-volume commercial van, and the complaint record reflects that: 14 reports to NHTSA, with 1 crash and 1 injury. Six are filed under unknown or other, with electrical system and air bags at 2 each. These reports are unverified.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for the dealer record showing the stall recall repair was performed.
  3. Confirm the backup camera shows an image reliably, not intermittently.
  4. Ask whether the van stalled or failed to start during its working life, and who serviced it.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
