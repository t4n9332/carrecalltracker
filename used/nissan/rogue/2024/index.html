Buying a used 2024 Nissan Rogue? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 201 owner complaints on file

This model year carries two critical recalls — one for engine bearings that may fail (a Nissan/Infiniti-wide issue) and one specific to Rogue turbo engines that may fail or catch fire — plus a high-severity recall for throttle body gears that may break and a medium recall for a rearview camera that can go blank. NHTSA has logged 199 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 6 crashes, 2 fires, and 6 injuries. The most common complaint categories are an "unknown or other" group (63) and engine (62), followed by powertrain (43) and visibility/wiper (25).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine bearing recall has been completed on this VIN
  3. Confirm the turbo engine fire-risk recall has been completed
  4. Test the throttle body response and rearview camera for blanking
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
