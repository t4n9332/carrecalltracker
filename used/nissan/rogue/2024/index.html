Buying a used 2024 Nissan Rogue? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 198 owner complaints on file

Recalls here center on the turbocharged engine: two critical ones for turbo engines that may fail or burn and for the VC-Turbo seizing, plus a high-severity recall for turbo engines losing driving power. A backup camera recall rounds out the list. Owner complaints total 198 — 61 on the engine and 42 on the power train, with 25 in visibility/wipers — alongside 6 crashes, 2 fires and 6 injuries. These complaints are unverified reports, not confirmed defects.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether all three engine recalls have been performed and whether any loss-of-power event occurred
  3. Drive under load and at highway merge speeds to check for hesitation or power loss
  4. Confirm the backup camera displays reliably and check wiper and visibility function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
