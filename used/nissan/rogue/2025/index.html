Buying a used 2025 Nissan Rogue? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 179 owner complaints on file

As the newest year listed, the record is still thin. The VC-Turbo engine seize recall is critical, a separate recall covers turbo engines losing driving power, and a backup camera that can go blank is the routine item. Of 179 unverified owner complaints, the visibility categories stand out — 48 under visibility/wiper and 30 more under visibility — a different mix from the engine-heavy 2023 and 2024 filings. Crashes (5), fires (1) and injuries (6) are low.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the VC-Turbo seize and power-loss recalls are closed on this specific VIN
  3. Test wipers, defrost and all-around visibility, the largest owner complaint grouping here
  4. Cycle into reverse several times to confirm the backup camera never goes blank
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
