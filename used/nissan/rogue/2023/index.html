Buying a used 2023 Nissan Rogue? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 707 owner complaints on file

Engine trouble is the headline. Two critical recalls cover turbo engines that may fail or burn and the VC-Turbo engine seizing, and 274 of 707 unverified owner complaints — the largest count among these years — concern the engine, plus 122 on engine cooling and 96 on fuel system. Separately, four high-severity recalls address restraints and seating: a driver's seat that may loosen, rear seat belt anchor bolts that can loosen, front seat belts that may not restrain occupants, and a driver airbag that may deploy improperly.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the VC-Turbo engine has been repaired or replaced, and get all engine service records
  3. Check the driver's seat for play in its mounting and confirm the seat recall repair was done
  4. Verify the airbag and both front and rear seat belt recalls are closed before taking delivery
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
