Buying a used 2023 Nissan Rogue? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 709 owner complaints on file

This 2023 Rogue has six recalls, including two critical issues: engine bearings that may fail and turbo engines that may fail or catch fire. High-severity recalls cover seat weld failures, loose rear seat belt anchor bolts, driver airbags that may deploy improperly, and seat belt pretensioners that may not restrain occupants. NHTSA has logged 708 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 22 crashes and 3 fires, with engine (275) as the most-cited category.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine bearing and turbo engine recalls have been completed
  3. Check the status of the seat weld and seat belt anchor bolt recalls
  4. Ask about any engine-related service or repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
