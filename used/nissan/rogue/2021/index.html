Buying a used 2021 Nissan Rogue? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 286 owner complaints on file

This 2021 Rogue has ten recalls, including two critical issues: a fuel hose that may leak or disconnect, and engine bearings that may fail. High-severity recalls cover wrong wheel nuts, brake caliper fluid leaks, and an overheating fuel pump. Medium recalls address child seat anchors, rearview camera/display failures, and rear seat belts not retracting. NHTSA has logged 286 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 21 crashes and 3 fires, with electrical system (60), engine (51), and fuel/propulsion (33) among top categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel hose, engine bearing, and brake caliper recalls have been completed
  3. Verify the wheel nuts are the correct type
  4. Test the rearview camera/display and rear seat belt retraction
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
