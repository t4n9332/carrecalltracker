Buying a used 2022 Nissan Rogue? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 258 owner complaints on file

This 2022 Rogue has six recalls, including two critical issues: a fuel tank wall that may be too thin and engine bearings that may fail, plus a high-severity fuel tank puncture fire risk. Medium recalls cover rearview camera and display failures and rear seat belts not retracting. NHTSA has recorded 258 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 5 crashes and 1 fire, with engine (137) as the most-cited category, followed by fuel/propulsion system (44).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel tank and engine bearing recalls have been completed
  3. Ask about any engine-related service or repair history
  4. Test the rearview camera/display and rear seat belt retraction
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
