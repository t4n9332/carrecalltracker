Buying a used 2022 Nissan Rogue? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 258 owner complaints on file

Two critical recalls dominate: a gas tank that is too thin with fire risk, and turbo engines that may fail or burn. A related high-severity recall covers a fuel tank that may be punctured and leak gas. Backup camera and rear seat belt retraction recalls are the routine items. Of 258 unverified owner complaints, 137 concern the engine, 44 the fuel system and 42 engine cooling. Crash, fire and injury counts are comparatively low at 5, 1 and 3.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm in writing that both fuel tank recalls and the turbo engine recall were completed
  3. Inspect underneath for fuel smell, staining or tank damage before purchase
  4. Ask for engine and cooling system service records, the heaviest complaint areas for this year
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
