Buying a used 2020 Nissan Leaf? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 164 owner complaints on file

This 2020 Leaf carries six recalls, including a critical battery-overheating issue tied to fast charging, a rear window detachment recall shared with the Altima and Maxima, and a risk of unintended acceleration. Three more recalls address backup and rearview camera display problems and incorrect defroster instructions in the owner's manual. NHTSA has logged 164 owner complaints for this model year, including 1 crash and 0 reported fires or injuries; electrical system issues account for 120 of those complaints, with fuel/propulsion system next at 34. These are unverified owner-submitted reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the battery overheating recall (fast-charging) has been completed
  3. Confirm the rear window detachment recall repair status
  4. Test the backup/rearview camera display for glitches
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
