Buying a used 2018 Nissan Rogue Sport? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 94 owner complaints on file

The headline recall is unusual: a folding key that can shut the vehicle off. A second, lower-severity recall covers a backup camera image that can stay off. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. What stands out more is the complaint record — 94 unverified reports to NHTSA, but with 8 crashes and 14 injuries attached, the highest injury count among these Nissans. Reports cluster in electrical system (19), service brakes (18) and engine (17).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the folding key recall was performed and ask what the remedy involved.
  3. Test the backup camera in reverse repeatedly, not just once.
  4. Pull a full accident history and inspect for prior collision repair, given the crash and injury counts.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
