Buying a used 2019 Nissan Rogue Sport? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 100 owner complaints on file

Same two recalls as 2018: a folding key that can shut the vehicle off, and a backup camera image that can stay off. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. The complaint pattern differs, though — of 100 unverified reports to NHTSA, the largest group by far is forward collision avoidance (29), ahead of electrical system (22) and engine (20). Totals include 2 crashes, 1 fire and 1 injury.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get recall completion records for both the folding key and the backup camera.
  3. On the test drive, note any unprompted braking or warnings from the collision-avoidance system.
  4. Ask whether the collision-avoidance system has ever been serviced, recalibrated, or switched off.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
