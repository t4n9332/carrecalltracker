Buying a used 2021 Nissan Rogue Sport? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 35 owner complaints on file

Three recalls cover this year: a folding key that can shut the vehicle off, liftgate hinges that can separate in a crash, and seat belts that may not properly hold child seats. Complaint volume is low at 35 NHTSA filings, with 7 crashes, 1 fire and 5 injuries logged. The largest cluster of these unverified owner reports is forward collision avoidance (11), ahead of electrical, engine and brakes. No recall here carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get dealer proof all three recalls were completed, especially the child-seat belt remedy if you will install a car seat
  3. On a test drive, note any false or missing automatic emergency braking alerts — the top complaint area
  4. Fold and use the key repeatedly and confirm the ignition never cuts out while running
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
