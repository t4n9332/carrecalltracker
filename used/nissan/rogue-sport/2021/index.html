Buying a used 2021 Nissan Rogue Sport? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 35 owner complaints on file

This 2021 Rogue Sport has three recalls, including two high-severity issues: liftgate hinges that may separate and a folding key that can shut off the vehicle. A medium recall covers a seat belt that may release early. NHTSA has logged 35 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 7 crashes and 5 reported injuries. Forward collision avoidance (11) is the most-cited complaint category.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the liftgate hinge and folding key recalls have been completed
  3. Test the seat belt latch and release function
  4. Ask about the forward collision avoidance system's performance
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
