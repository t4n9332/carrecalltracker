Buying a used 2025 Nissan Kicks? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 37 owner complaints on file

For a current model year, four recalls is a lot, though they skew toward screens and labels rather than mechanical failure: a blank driver display, a missing shift-to-park warning that allows roll-away, a blank backup camera, and a sticker listing wrong weight and wheel information. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Thirty-seven unverified owner complaints have been filed, including two crashes, one fire, and one injury, spread across visibility/wiper, electrical, and steering.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all four recalls, including the shift-to-park warning fix, show as completed for this VIN.
  3. Watch the driver display and backup camera on several startups and reverses to see if either goes blank.
  4. Compare the vehicle's weight and wheel sticker against the corrected information from the dealer.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
