Buying a used 2019 Nissan Titan? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 47 owner complaints on file

Carryover of the two critical items: alternator wiring that can short and stall the engine, and tires that may fail suddenly. The medium recalls are a backup camera image that can stay off and LED headlights with incorrect aiming marks. Complaint volume is roughly half the 2018 count at 47, again led by engine (17) and electrical system (11), with power train at 9. Two of these unverified owner reports mention fires and three mention crashes — notable given the wiring-short recall.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the alternator wiring and tire recalls are closed on this VIN.
  3. Ask about any stalling, burning smell or electrical faults, and inspect engine bay wiring.
  4. Check the backup camera image in reverse and the LED headlight aim after dark.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
