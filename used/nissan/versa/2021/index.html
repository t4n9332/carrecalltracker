Buying a used 2021 Nissan Versa? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 31 owner complaints on file

Three recalls apply, and two of them cover the same problem from different angles: loss of power steering assist, one rated high and one medium. The third is the cracking front turn signal bulb issue. None carried a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. NHTSA shows 31 owner complaints — unverified — with 5 crashes and 3 injuries reported and no fires. Complaints concentrate in power train, electrical system, air bags and engine, so steering feel and electrical faults deserve a close look.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm by VIN that both power steering assist recalls were performed
  3. On the test drive, watch for steering that suddenly turns heavy, especially at low speed
  4. Check that all dash warning lights clear at startup and the turn signal lenses are intact
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
