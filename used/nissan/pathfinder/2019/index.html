Buying a used 2019 Nissan Pathfinder? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 75 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This 2019 Pathfinder has two recalls: a critical brake fluid leak that can lead to fire, which advises parking outside until repaired, and a lower-severity issue where the backup camera image can stay off. NHTSA has logged 75 owner complaints for this model year, including 4 crashes, 1 fire, and 2 injuries reported; the unknown/other category accounts for 20 of those complaints, followed by 15 for engine, 13 for service brakes, and 10 for forward collision avoidance. These are unverified owner-submitted reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake fluid leak recall has been completed
  3. Ask whether the vehicle has been parked outside per the recall advisory pending repair
  4. Test that the backup camera displays an image reliably
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
