Buying a used 2023 Nissan Ariya? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 41 owner complaints on file

This model year carries four recalls, including a steering wheel bolt that may loosen or detach and two separate software issues that can cut drive power unexpectedly, plus a related report of the motor losing drive power. NHTSA logged 40 owner complaints (unverified reports), with one crash and no fires or injuries. Electrical system issues account for 25 of those complaints, followed by powertrain (9), engine (9), and vehicle speed control (5). Drive-power and electrical concerns are the recurring theme across both the recalls and complaint categories for this year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering wheel bolt recall has been inspected or repaired
  3. Ask if the software updates addressing drive power loss have been applied
  4. Ask the seller about any experience with electrical system warnings or power interruptions
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Nissan dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
