Buying a used 2019 Dodge Charger? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 120 owner complaints on file

The critical item is a curtain-airbag recall for bags that may rupture; a wheel recall covering wrong wheels that can damage tires also applies. Lesser recalls cover dash warning lights that may fail, a Pursuit-only backup camera, and a missing sun visor airbag label. The complaint record is airbag-heavy: 49 of 120 NHTSA filings name air bags, with electrical and exterior lighting behind, plus 7 crashes, 3 fires and 11 injuries reported — all unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the curtain airbag recall repair by VIN before purchase
  3. Check the wheels against factory specification and inspect tires for damage
  4. Confirm no airbag warning light is lit and test all exterior lights
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
