Buying a used 2018 Dodge Charger? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 122 owner complaints on file

This model year carries six recall campaigns, including a side airbag rupture issue, a stalling condition, a cruise-control-cancel defect, and a backup camera that may not display in Stealth mode. NHTSA has logged 122 owner complaints (unverified reports) tied to this model year, including 5 crashes, 1 fire, and 6 injuries. Electrical system complaints lead at 45, followed by unknown/other at 33, structure at 19, and engine at 11.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all six recalls, including the airbag and driveshaft campaigns, are completed via VIN lookup
  3. Test backup camera display, especially in Stealth mode
  4. Ask about any stalling episodes or cruise-control-cancel issues in the vehicle's history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
