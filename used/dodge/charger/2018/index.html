Buying a used 2018 Dodge Charger? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 122 owner complaints on file

This year carries the most serious item in the group: a critical recall for curtain airbags that may rupture. Alongside it sit a Park-engagement recall, cruise control that can stick on, an engine computer that may cause stalling, and a driveshaft recall limited to police Pursuit cars. None are flagged stop-driving. NHTSA holds 122 owner complaints, led by electrical system, unclassified reports and structure, with 5 crashes, 1 fire and 6 injuries. Those are unverified.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the curtain airbag rupture recall was completed before taking delivery
  3. Ask whether it is a former police Pursuit car and check the front driveshaft recall
  4. Verify it holds in Park and that cruise control engages and cancels normally
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
