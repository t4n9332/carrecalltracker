Buying a used 2023 Dodge Charger? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 26 owner complaints on file

Two recalls apply: a high-severity one for a parking brake that may not hold, and one for a left rear seat back that may not lock. Owner complaints number 26, all unverified reports filed with NHTSA, led by electrical system at 10 and powertrain at nine, with five under service brakes, which overlaps the parking brake recall area. Three crashes were reported, with no fires or injuries. Recall exposure is limited relative to older Chargers.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Set the parking brake on a slope and confirm the car holds
  3. Fold and latch the left rear seat back to verify it locks
  4. Ask about electrical or transmission repairs in the service history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
