Buying a used 2018 Dodge Journey? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 232 owner complaints on file

Six recalls span this year: cruise control that may stick on, an engine computer fault linked to stalling, rear brake calipers that may weaken braking, two separate backup camera image problems, and a side reflector that is too dim. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. NHTSA holds 232 unverified owner complaints with 7 crashes, 2 fires and 7 injuries; electrical system leads at 72, with exterior lighting at 26 echoing the reflector recall.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Test cruise control engagement and cancellation, and confirm that recall was repaired
  3. Check the backup camera for slow or missing images, covered by two separate recalls
  4. Ask about stalling history and confirm the engine computer recall was performed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
