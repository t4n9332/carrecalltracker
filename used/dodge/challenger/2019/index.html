Buying a used 2019 Dodge Challenger? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 58 owner complaints on file

The headline recall is a wheel issue: incorrect wheels fitted to Challengers and Chargers can damage tires. Two lesser items cover dashboard warning lights that may fail and a backup-camera image that stays on screen. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. NHTSA logged 58 owner complaints for this year, led by electrical system, unclassified reports, wheels and steering, with 5 crashes, 3 fires and 4 injuries reported. Complaints are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the wheels match factory specification and that the wheel recall was performed
  3. Watch the instrument cluster at startup to confirm warning lights illuminate and clear
  4. Shift out of reverse and confirm the backup camera image clears from the screen
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
