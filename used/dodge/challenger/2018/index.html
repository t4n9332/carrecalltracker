Buying a used 2018 Dodge Challenger? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 75 owner complaints on file

This 2018 Challenger has four high-severity recalls: an incorrect park lock rod, cruise control that may not cancel, cruise control that may not shut off, and a stalling issue. NHTSA has logged 75 owner complaints for this model year, including 9 crashes, 2 fires, and 8 injuries reported; these are unverified owner reports. The largest complaint categories are electrical system (28) and unknown or other (28), followed by power train (8) and wheels (6).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm park lock rod, stall, and cruise-control recalls are completed
  3. Test cruise control cancel/shut-off function
  4. Ask about prior electrical system repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
