Buying a used 2018 Dodge Grand Caravan? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 269 owner complaints on file

Four recall campaigns touch this year: a fuel pump gasket that can leak on modified vans, an engine computer fault linked to stalling, rear brake calipers that may weaken braking, and wheelchair restraints on Rollx conversion vans. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. NHTSA logged 269 unverified owner complaints, including 8 crashes and 13 injuries. Electrical system dominates the complaint mix at 92, ahead of structure at 59 and engine at 34.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN through NHTSA to confirm the engine computer and brake caliper recalls were completed
  3. Ask whether the van is a Rollx or other wheelchair conversion, and if the restraint recall applies
  4. Have the fuel pump area inspected for leaks or gasket work, especially on a modified van
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
