Buying a used 2019 Dodge Grand Caravan? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 42 owner complaints on file

The recall list is shorter than 2018's but adds one specific to this year: rear seat anchors that may fail in a crash. Also open are the modified-van fuel pump gasket leak, rear brake calipers that may weaken braking, and Rollx wheelchair restraints. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Owners filed 42 unverified complaints with no crashes or fires and 4 injuries, led by power train at 13 and seats at 8.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rear seat anchor recall repair was performed, and inspect the third-row and middle seat mounts
  3. Verify the fuel pump gasket recall status if the van was modified or converted
  4. Ask about any transmission or drivability issues, given power train leads the complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
