Buying a used 2020 Dodge Durango? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 54 owner complaints on file

Recall exposure is light for this year: a side curtain airbag bolt that may be loose or missing, and a backup camera image that stays on screen. Owner complaints number 54, unverified reports filed with NHTSA, with exterior lighting the clear leader at 21, then electrical at nine and engine at seven. No crashes were reported, though three fires and five injuries were. Lighting is the recurring owner-reported theme here rather than a recall subject.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if the side curtain airbag bolt recall was inspected and corrected
  3. Check headlights, taillights and turn signals through a full cycle
  4. Confirm the backup camera image clears after leaving reverse
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
