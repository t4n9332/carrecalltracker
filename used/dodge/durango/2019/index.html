Buying a used 2019 Dodge Durango? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 141 owner complaints on file

Two critical recalls apply, one for sudden loss of power and one for vehicles that can roll out of Park, plus a brake module failure recall and a minor one for a backup camera image that stays on screen. Unverified owner reports to NHTSA total 141, led by exterior lighting at 45 and electrical system at 37, with 26 under service brakes and 24 under engine, areas overlapping the recalls. Five crashes, two fires and eight injuries were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the loss-of-power and roll-out-of-Park recalls were repaired by VIN
  3. Brake-test and ask whether the brake module was ever replaced
  4. Shift into reverse and out; confirm the camera image clears
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
