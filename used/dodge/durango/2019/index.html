Buying a used 2019 Dodge Durango? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 141 owner complaints on file

Four recalls apply to this model year, including a critical rollaway risk, a condition where the vehicle may lose power, a brake module fault, and a backup camera image that may stay on the screen. NHTSA has recorded 141 owner complaints (unverified) for this model year, with 5 crashes, 2 fires, and 8 injuries reported. Exterior lighting leads with 45 complaints, followed by electrical system at 37, service brakes at 26, and engine at 24.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rollaway, power-loss, brake module, and camera recalls are completed via VIN lookup
  3. Test brake function and backup camera display behavior
  4. Ask about any exterior lighting or engine-related issues from the seller
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
