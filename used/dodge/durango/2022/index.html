Buying a used 2022 Dodge Durango? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 56 owner complaints on file

Seven recalls carry into this year, led by a critical steering bind or failure and a shifter that may slip out of Drive. The rest are medium: two stability control items, two rear spoiler recalls, and a backup camera that may show nothing. Owner complaints drop to 56, unverified reports to NHTSA, still led by exterior lighting at 25, then electrical, engine and powertrain at around ten each. Two crashes, one fire and one injury were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering and shifter recalls were completed at a dealer
  3. Check that the stability control warning light illuminates at startup
  4. Inspect rear spoiler mounting and confirm the backup camera works
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
