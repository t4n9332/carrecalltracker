Buying a used 2018 Dodge Durango? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 231 owner complaints on file

This year carries five recalls, several serious: vehicles that can roll out of Park, a separate recall for not staying in Park, cruise control that may stick on, an engine computer that may cause stalling, and brake module failure. Owner complaints are high at 231, all unverified reports to NHTSA, dominated by electrical system at 87 and exterior lighting at 56, with 26 engine entries. Nine crashes and five injuries were reported, and no fires.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify all five recalls were closed out by VIN at a dealer
  3. Test that the vehicle holds in Park on an incline
  4. Check every exterior light and dash warning given lighting complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
