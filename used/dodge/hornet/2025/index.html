Buying a used 2025 Dodge Hornet? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

This model year has high-severity recalls for a brake pedal that may collapse while driving and a rearview camera display failure, along with a medium recall for seat belts that may not retract. NHTSA has logged only 2 owner complaints so far, an unverified count, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported; one complaint each falls under fuel/propulsion system and electrical system.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if the brake pedal collapse recall was completed
  3. Confirm the rearview camera and seat belt recalls were addressed
  4. Ask about any fuel/propulsion or electrical issues, the only complaint areas reported so far
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
