Buying a used 2024 Dodge Charger EV? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 23 owner complaints on file

This 2024 Charger EV has three recalls: two high-severity issues covering unintended roll-away and display failure, plus a medium-severity issue where the pedestrian warning sound may not activate. NHTSA has logged 23 owner complaints for this model year, including 1 fire and no crashes or injuries reported; these are unverified owner reports. The largest complaint category is electrical system (16), with service brakes (4), vehicle speed control (4), and unknown or other (4) also cited.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify roll-away and display-failure recalls are completed
  3. Test pedestrian warning sound activation
  4. Ask about electrical system repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
