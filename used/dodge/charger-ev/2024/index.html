Buying a used 2024 Dodge Charger EV? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 23 owner complaints on file

As an early electric Charger, the recalls reflect that: vehicles that may roll away, an instrument display that can fail, and a missing pedestrian warning sound required on quiet EVs. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Owners filed 23 NHTSA complaints, heavily weighted toward electrical system, with service brakes and vehicle speed control also appearing, plus one fire report. These are unverified owner reports; display and software items dominate the record.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm by VIN that the rollaway and display failure recalls have been remedied
  3. Park on a slope in Park and verify the vehicle holds without rolling
  4. Check the driver display boots fully and the pedestrian warning sound works at low speed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
