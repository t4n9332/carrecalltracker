Buying a used 2025 Dodge Charger EV? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 37 owner complaints on file

This 2025 Charger EV has three recalls: two high-severity issues covering unintended roll-away and display failure, plus a medium-severity issue where the pedestrian warning sound may not activate. NHTSA has logged 36 owner complaints for this model year, including 2 crashes and 2 injuries reported, with no fires; these are unverified owner reports. The largest complaint category is electrical system (25), followed by fuel/propulsion system (14), power train (10), and service brakes (8).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify roll-away and display-failure recalls are completed
  3. Test pedestrian warning sound activation
  4. Ask about fuel/propulsion and power train repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
