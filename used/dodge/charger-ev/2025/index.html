Buying a used 2025 Dodge Charger EV? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 36 owner complaints on file

The recall list matches the 2024 car — rollaway risk, a display that can fail, and a pedestrian warning sound that may not sound — so establish whether this VIN was built before or after the fixes. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Complaints run higher than 2024 at 36 NHTSA filings, led by electrical system, fuel/propulsion, power train and service brakes, with 2 crashes and 2 injuries reported. All complaints are unverified.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for the software update history and confirm recall completion by VIN
  3. Verify the exterior pedestrian warning sound is audible at low speed
  4. Ask about any loss of drive power, charging faults, or brake warnings
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Dodge dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
