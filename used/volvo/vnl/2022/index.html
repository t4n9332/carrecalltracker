Buying a used 2022 Volvo VNL? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

This model year's recalls include a side airbag that may not deploy, a possibility of shifting into neutral unexpectedly, loose flywheel bolts, Alcoa wheels that may bend or break, and air tank bracket fasteners that may break. Owner complaints (unverified reports) filed with NHTSA total 2, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported, and engine/engine cooling cited most (2 mentions) followed by brakes, air, and structure (1 each). Hubcap separation and windshield wiper motor failure round out the medium-severity recalls.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm side airbag deployment recall was completed
  3. Ask about wheel (Alcoa) and flywheel bolt recall repair history
  4. Confirm air tank bracket fastener recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volvo dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
