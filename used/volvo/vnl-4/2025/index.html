Buying a used 2025 Volvo VNL (4)? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

Ten recalls land on this model year, weighted toward steering and braking: a critical campaign for sudden loss of steering control, plus high-rated items for delayed brake response, engine-stalling gear bolts, roll-away despite the parking brake, instrument panel failure while driving, and loss of anti-lock brakes and stability control. None carry a stop-driving instruction. Only two unverified owner complaints are filed, but between them they allege one crash, one fire, and two injuries, with steering the top area.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Pull a full VIN recall report and confirm the steering, brake, and ABS/stability campaigns are all closed.
  3. Test the parking brake holding on a grade and check for any roll-away or delayed brake release.
  4. Watch the instrument panel through a cold start and a long drive for blackouts or dropped warning lamps.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volvo dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
