Buying a used 2026 Volvo VNL (4)? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

This model year carries a critical recall for wheel lug nuts that may loosen, marked stop-driving, alongside two separate steering-related recalls (steering assist malfunction and a steering arm that may loosen). Other recalls cover brakes releasing slowly, a rear brake modulator short-circuit risk, cargo bar security, automatic high beams, and a door that may not open from inside. Owner complaints (unverified reports) filed with NHTSA total 4, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported, spread across powertrain, brakes, back-over prevention, and structure categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the wheel lug nut recall (stop-driving) has been completed
  3. Ask about repair status for both steering recalls (assist malfunction, steering arm)
  4. Confirm door-latch and cargo bar recall fixes were done
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volvo dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
