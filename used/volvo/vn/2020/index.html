Buying a used 2020 Volvo VN? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

A short record for this year: one high-severity recall for VN and VAH trucks losing electronic safety systems, and one medium recall for a passenger bench seat that may loosen. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. A single owner complaint is on file with NHTSA — an unverified report — touching service brakes, hydraulics, and the electrical system, with no crashes, fires, or injuries recorded. The recall list is notably lighter than later VN years.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the electronic safety systems recall was completed and that driver-assist warnings are clear at startup
  3. Push and pull on the passenger bench seat to check for movement in its mounts
  4. Ask about any brake, hydraulic, or electrical repairs and request service records
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volvo dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
