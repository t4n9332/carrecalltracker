Buying a used 2024 Volvo VN? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This 2024 Volvo VN carries seven recalls, six rated high severity, covering steering fasteners that may loosen, a passenger seat that may not be secured, accelerator sensor disconnection, unintended acceleration risk, an electronic control unit that may misread signals, and an idler gear that can detach; one medium recall covers an incorrect weight label. None require stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA lists 1 owner complaint, unverified, involving 1 crash and 1 injury, filed under an unspecified category.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the steering fastener, idler gear, and accelerator sensor recalls have been completed
  3. Confirm the passenger seat mounting was inspected or repaired under recall
  4. Verify the truck's weight/GVWR label matches actual specification
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volvo dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
