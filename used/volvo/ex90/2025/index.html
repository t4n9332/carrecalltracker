Buying a used 2025 Volvo EX90? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 69 owner complaints on file

This model year has three recalls: loose second-row seat bolts, a power tailgate that may suddenly drop, and headlight shutters that may close while driving. NHTSA lists 69 owner complaints, including 2 crashes and 1 injury reported, with no fires. Electrical system complaints (33) make up the largest category, followed by unknown/other (16), fuel/propulsion system (13), and power train (9). These figures are unverified owner reports. Verifying all three recalls were completed is worth doing before purchase.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm second-row seat bolt recall was completed
  3. Confirm power tailgate recall repair status
  4. Ask about any headlight shutter malfunction while driving
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volvo dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
