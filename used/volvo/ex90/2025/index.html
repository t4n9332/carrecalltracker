Buying a used 2025 Volvo EX90? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 69 owner complaints on file

The EX90's first model year carries three recalls: headlight covers that can close while driving, a power tailgate that can drop suddenly, and second-row seat bolts that may be loose. None carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. More notable for a first-year vehicle is the complaint volume — 69 unverified owner reports to NHTSA, 33 of them electrical system, plus fuel/propulsion and power train entries, with two crashes and one injury alleged.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for dealer proof that all three recalls are closed on this VIN: headlight covers, power tailgate, second-row seat bolts.
  3. Cycle the power tailgate repeatedly and confirm it holds at partial open without dropping.
  4. On the test drive, work through infotainment, driver display, and charging for the electrical faults owners report most.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volvo dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
