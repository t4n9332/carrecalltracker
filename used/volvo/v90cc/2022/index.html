Buying a used 2022 Volvo V90CC? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

This year's recall record is narrow and repetitive: two separate medium-rated campaigns, both concerning the backup camera failing to display when reversing. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, and nothing critical or high-rated is listed. Owner complaints total two, unverified, filed under electrical system, back over prevention, and forward collision avoidance, with no crashes, fires, or injuries. Because the two recalls are distinct campaigns, one being completed does not mean the other was.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask the dealer to confirm both backup camera campaigns are closed by VIN — they are separate recalls.
  3. Shift into reverse several times, cold and after restarts, and watch for a blank or delayed image.
  4. Ask whether the camera display has ever dropped out and whether any electrical work was done for it.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volvo dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
