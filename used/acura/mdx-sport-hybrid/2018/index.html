Buying a used 2018 Acura MDX Sport Hybrid? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This hybrid MDX year has a short record: one recall for rear brakes that may stop poorly and one for water intrusion in the tailgate lights that can knock out the taillights. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Only a single unverified owner complaint has been filed with NHTSA, in the visibility/wiper category, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. The thin complaint history means there is little owner data to weigh against the recalls.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Have the rear brakes inspected and confirm the rear brake recall was completed.
  3. Check both tailgate lamps for condensation or moisture inside the housings.
  4. Confirm all exterior lights, including taillights and brake lights, work.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
