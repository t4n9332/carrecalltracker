Buying a used 2019 Acura ILX? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 36 owner complaints on file

The 2019 ILX carries the same driveshaft-separation, brake-leak and fuel-gauge recalls as the 2018, plus two separate fuel pump recalls for stalling. What shifts is the complaint mix: among 36 unverified NHTSA reports, service brakes and hydraulic items rank as high as fuel system entries, alongside two crashes and one injury. None of the recalls carry a stop-driving or park-outside warning, but the braking theme repeats across both recall and complaint records.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get VIN-specific confirmation that both fuel pump recalls were completed.
  3. Have the brake master cylinder and hydraulic lines inspected for internal leakage.
  4. Confirm the driveshaft recall repair and listen for driveline noise while driving.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
