Buying a used 2020 Acura ILX? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 10 owner complaints on file

The final ILX year is the quietest of the three: only two recalls, both shared across Honda and Acura — the fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine, and an internal leak that can weaken the brakes. Neither requires stop-driving or outdoor parking. Just 10 unverified owner complaints were filed with NHTSA, and they point elsewhere — wipers and visibility, steering, and driver-assist features like lane departure and forward collision avoidance.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify by VIN that the fuel pump and brake recalls are closed.
  3. Test the wipers and washers and check for any visibility issues.
  4. Drive with lane departure and collision warning active to confirm normal behavior.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
