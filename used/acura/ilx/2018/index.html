Buying a used 2018 Acura ILX? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 23 owner complaints on file

Four recalls dominate this year: a driveshaft that can separate, a fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine, an internal brake leak that weakens braking, and a fuel gauge that can read wrong. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. The 23 unverified NHTSA owner complaints line up with that: fuel and propulsion issues lead, with a handful of brake reports. Two crashes, no fires or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for recall completion proof on the driveshaft, fuel pump, and brake repairs.
  3. Test-drive for stalling or hesitation; confirm the fuel pump recall by VIN.
  4. Check the fuel gauge reads accurately and the brake pedal holds firm.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
