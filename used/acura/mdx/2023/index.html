Buying a used 2023 Acura MDX? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 39 owner complaints on file

Two high-severity recalls apply: a brake pedal that can shift out of position and a seat sensor that may cause unintended airbag deployment. Neither is flagged stop-driving or park-outside. Complaint volume is modest at 39 unverified NHTSA reports, with 2 crashes, no fires and 2 injuries, and it spreads fairly evenly across power train (10), service brakes (8), electrical (8) and forward collision avoidance (7) rather than clustering in one system. The brake pedal recall is the item to confirm first.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake pedal recall was performed on this specific VIN
  3. Press the brake pedal hard from a stop and feel for shift or looseness
  4. Ask about brake or forward-collision warning messages and related dealer visits
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
