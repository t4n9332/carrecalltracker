Buying a used 2025 Acura MDX? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 101 owner complaints on file

Three recalls apply here: the two high-severity items shared with recent years — brake pedal shifting out of position and a seat sensor that may deploy the airbag — plus a medium-severity one for a touchscreen that can go blank and hide the backup camera. None require stop-driving or parking outside. NHTSA logs 101 unverified complaints for a recent model year, with 2 crashes and 5 injuries, and engine (61) plus electrical system (46) dominate the reports by a wide margin.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all three recalls, including the touchscreen/backup camera fix, are closed on the VIN
  3. Start the car cold and check the backup camera appears when shifting into reverse
  4. Ask about engine running complaints and any engine or fuel-system repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
