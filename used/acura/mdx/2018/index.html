Buying a used 2018 Acura MDX? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 165 owner complaints on file

This is the busiest of the MDX years listed, with ten recalls spanning a critical engine bearing failure that can seize the engine, loosening seats, poor rear braking, two fuel pump stalling recalls, a timing belt that can lose teeth, a seat sensor tied to unintended airbag deployment, rear frame rust-through, and tailgate light moisture. The 165 unverified NHTSA complaints track the same areas — engine, fuel and propulsion, electrical, power train — and include four fire reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN for open recalls — the engine bearing and timing belt especially.
  3. Inspect the rear frame underside for rust or perforation; ask about winter use.
  4. Check seat mounting for movement and confirm no airbag light on startup.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
