Buying a used 2018 Acura MDX? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 165 owner complaints on file

This model year carries ten recalls, including a critical notice for engine bearings that may wear and seize, plus separate recalls covering power seats that may detach, reduced brake performance, fuel pump failure or stall, timing belt failure, front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally, rear subframe corrosion, and moisture-related taillight/tailgate light failures. Owners have filed 165 unverified complaints with NHTSA, including 3 crashes and 4 fires; engine (35), fuel/propulsion system (28), and electrical system (27) are the leading categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for service records confirming the engine bearing recall repair, given the critical severity
  3. Verify the timing belt, fuel pump, and rear subframe recalls have all been completed
  4. Check for airbag recall completion and any history of engine or electrical repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
