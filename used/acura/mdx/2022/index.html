Buying a used 2022 Acura MDX? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 148 owner complaints on file

Both high-severity recalls on this year trace to the passenger seat sensor — one for cracking that affects airbag function, the other for unintended airbag deployment. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning, so both are ordinary dealer fixes. Owners filed 148 unverified complaints with NHTSA, including 6 crashes, 2 fires and 4 injuries. The standout is complaint concentration: power train at 62, far ahead of electrical (20) and steering (19), which is worth probing on a test drive.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN and confirm both seat-sensor/airbag recall remedies are closed
  3. Watch the passenger airbag indicator with weight in the front seat
  4. Test drive for shifting or drivetrain roughness and request transmission service records
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
