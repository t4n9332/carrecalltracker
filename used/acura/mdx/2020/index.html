Buying a used 2020 Acura MDX? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 220 owner complaints on file

Two recalls on this year are rated critical: a brake failure risk shared with the Honda Civic and Pilot, and an engine bearing fault that can seize the engine. Alongside them sit two fuel pump stalling recalls, two seat sensor recalls tied to airbag deployment, rear frame rust-through, and tailgate light moisture. Engine tops the 220 unverified NHTSA complaints at 82, ahead of electrical, uncategorized reports, and forward collision avoidance. Three crashes and two injuries were reported; no fires.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both critical recalls — brakes and engine bearing — are closed for this VIN.
  3. Test brake pedal firmness and stopping feel from low and highway speeds.
  4. Check that forward collision avoidance works without false or missing warnings.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
