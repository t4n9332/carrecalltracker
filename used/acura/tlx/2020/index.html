Buying a used 2020 Acura TLX? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 62 owner complaints on file

This model year has a critical engine bearing wear/seize recall, two fuel pump recalls, a passenger seat sensor crack recall, and a front passenger airbag recall for unintended deployment. NHTSA has 62 owner complaints on file (unverified reports), including 4 crashes, no fires, and 1 injury. Complaint areas are led by engine, followed by fuel system, gasoline, and electrical system. Reviewing recall paperwork for these specific repairs is advisable before further evaluation.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm engine bearing recall repair was completed
  3. Ask about status of both fuel pump recalls
  4. Verify passenger seat sensor recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
