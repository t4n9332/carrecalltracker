Buying a used 2022 Acura TLX? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 26 owner complaints on file

This model year has a critical recall for a fuel tank weld that may leak fuel, plus recalls covering tire damage and a brake pedal pin that can shift. NHTSA shows 26 owner complaints (unverified reports), with no reported crashes, fires, or injuries. Complaint topics lead with power train, followed by steering, suspension, and electrical system. Confirming whether the fuel tank and tire recalls have been completed is a reasonable step before further evaluation.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify fuel tank weld recall was completed
  3. Ask whether tires were inspected or replaced under the tire recall
  4. Confirm brake pedal pin recall repair status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
