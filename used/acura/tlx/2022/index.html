Buying a used 2022 Acura TLX? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 26 owner complaints on file

A shorter list for 2022: a critical fuel tank weld that can leak gasoline, a brake pedal that can shift out of position, and tires that may have been cut during factory handling. Complaint activity is light — 26 unverified reports to NHTSA with no crashes, fires or injuries — led by power train at 10, then steering, suspension and electrical. The fuel tank and brake pedal recalls carry over from the 2021 model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the fuel tank weld recall was completed before purchase.
  3. Inspect the tires for sidewall cuts and ask whether the original tires were replaced or inspected.
  4. Check brake pedal feel and position, and confirm the brake pedal recall repair was done.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
