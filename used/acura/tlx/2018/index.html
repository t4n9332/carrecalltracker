Buying a used 2018 Acura TLX? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 93 owner complaints on file

Two safety themes dominate: a critical recall for a faulty engine bearing that can seize the engine, and repeated fuel pump recalls that can stall the car, including one specific to the MDX and TLX. A seat sensor recall covers unintended airbag deployment. Owners filed 93 unverified NHTSA complaints (3 crashes, 1 fire, 2 injuries), led by engine at 30, then electrical and fuel. No recall here carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get proof the engine bearing recall was completed, plus any engine repair or replacement records.
  3. Confirm both fuel pump recalls were performed — ask if the car has ever stalled while driving.
  4. Ask whether the seat occupant sensor was replaced and if any airbag warning light has appeared.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
