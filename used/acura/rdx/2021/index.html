Buying a used 2021 Acura RDX? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 111 owner complaints on file

Three high-severity recalls apply, and one is specific to this year: driveshafts that may break, shared with the Honda CR-V Hybrid. The other two are the familiar seat sensor items — cracking and unintended airbag deployment. No stop-driving or park-outside instruction attaches to any of them. NHTSA lists 111 unverified complaints with 3 crashes, no fires and 1 injury, spread across unknown/other (30), engine (20), service brakes (16) and power train (13).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the driveshaft recall inspection or replacement was performed on this VIN
  3. Listen for clunks or vibration pulling away from a stop and under acceleration
  4. Check brake pedal feel and remaining pad life, and ask about past brake work
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
