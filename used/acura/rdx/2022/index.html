Buying a used 2022 Acura RDX? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 31 owner complaints on file

The recall picture narrows to two high-severity seat sensor items: one for a sensor that may crack and compromise the airbag, one for unintended airbag deployment. Neither is flagged stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA shows just 31 unverified complaints, but 4 of them involve crashes and 2 involve injuries, so the crash share is notable against the small total. Reported areas are led by unknown/other (11) and visibility/wiper (9), with service brakes (4) further down.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check the VIN to confirm both seat sensor recalls have been completed
  3. Sit in the passenger seat and watch the airbag on/off indicator respond
  4. Test wipers, washers and windshield/camera clarity during the inspection
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
