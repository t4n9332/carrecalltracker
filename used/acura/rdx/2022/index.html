Buying a used 2022 Acura RDX? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 31 owner complaints on file

This model year has two recalls: a passenger seat sensor that may crack and front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally, neither marked stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA has logged 31 owner complaints, including 4 crash reports and 2 injury reports, with no fires; these are unverified accounts. Unknown/other leads complaint areas at 11, followed by visibility/wiper (9), service brakes (4), and visibility (3).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the passenger seat sensor recall has been completed
  3. Confirm the front passenger airbag recall has been addressed
  4. Check wiper/visibility function and brakes before purchase
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
