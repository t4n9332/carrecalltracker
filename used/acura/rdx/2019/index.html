Buying a used 2019 Acura RDX? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 436 owner complaints on file

This year carries five high-severity recalls, three of them separate fuel pump actions that can cause the engine to stall, plus front seat belts that may not latch and a seat sensor that may deploy the airbag. None are flagged stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA holds 436 unverified complaints — high for this group — with 3 crashes, no fires and 5 injuries. Beyond the large unknown/other bucket (141), visibility/wiper (96) and engine (89) are the recurring themes.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get written confirmation that every fuel pump recall was completed for this VIN
  3. Latch and tug both front seat belts repeatedly and ask about the seat belt recall
  4. Test wipers, washers, defroster and all-around visibility; ask about any stalling history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Acura dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
