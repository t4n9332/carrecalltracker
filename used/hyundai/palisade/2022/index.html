Buying a used 2022 Hyundai Palisade? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 302 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year has five recalls: a critical tow-hitch wiring fire risk requiring outside parking, wipers that may fail in snow, seat belt buckles that may not latch, a third-row airbag that may deploy incorrectly, and an instrument cluster display that may disable. NHTSA has logged 302 owner complaints for this model year (unverified reports), including 5 crashes, 2 fires, and 7 injuries. The largest complaint category is seat belts (70), followed by engine (46), unknown/other (46), and visibility/wiper (33).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the tow-hitch fire recall was completed and confirm outside-parking status
  3. Test wiper operation in snow and check instrument cluster display function
  4. Check seat belt buckle latching and third-row airbag recall completion
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
