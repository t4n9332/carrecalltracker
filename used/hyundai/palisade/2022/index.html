Buying a used 2022 Hyundai Palisade? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 302 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

The 2022 Palisade carries five recalls, the most serious a tow hitch wiring fire risk that came with a park-outside instruction until repaired. Seat belt buckles that may not latch is the other high-severity item, and it lines up with the largest complaint category — 70 of 302 unverified NHTSA owner reports cite seat belts. Wiper failure in snow shows up both as a recall and in 33 visibility reports. Blank dashboard and third-row curtain airbag recalls round out the list.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof the tow hitch wiring recall was completed, and whether a hitch was ever fitted.
  3. Latch and unlatch every seat belt buckle, including the third row.
  4. Run the wipers through all speeds and confirm the dash display powers up without blanking.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
