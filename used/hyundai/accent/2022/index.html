Buying a used 2022 Hyundai Accent? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 16 owner complaints on file

Only two recalls apply, but one is critical: a steering column that may detach while driving, alongside the Accent/Elantra seat belt pretensioner explosion campaign. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Of 16 unverified NHTSA owner complaints — again a small sample — electrical system leads with six, while seat belts (4) and steering (3) also appear, echoing both recall subjects. Two crashes, one fire and two injuries are reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the steering column recall was completed before purchase
  3. Check for steering looseness, noise, or off-center wheel while driving
  4. Test all electrical functions and inspect both front seat belts
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
