Buying a used 2020 Hyundai Sonata? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 178 owner complaints on file

No critical-rated recalls here, but the top item is a fuel tank that can melt and leak. The others are narrower: remote parking that may move the car unexpectedly, a trunk release that may fail, and a door label listing the wrong tire size. Fuel and propulsion is also the largest complaint area (45 of 178 unverified NHTSA reports), followed by electrical (40) and engine (31), with 5 crashes, 1 fire and 4 injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel tank recall was completed, and check for fuel odor around the rear.
  3. Test the remote parking function and the trunk release before agreeing to buy.
  4. Compare the tire size on the door label against the tires actually fitted.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
