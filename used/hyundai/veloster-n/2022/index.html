Buying a used 2022 Hyundai Veloster N? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 36 owner complaints on file

Both recalls on this year point at the car stopping rather than crashing: a sudden loss of all drive power, and a fuel pump failure specific to Hyundai and Genesis performance models. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 36 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA report no crashes, fires or injuries, and they cluster oddly against the recalls — lane departure (28) and steering (17) lead by far, with engine and power train at four each.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get dealer paperwork showing the drive-power-loss and fuel pump recalls were both completed
  3. On the test drive, watch for false or missing lane departure warnings, the top complaint area
  4. Ask whether the car has ever stalled, lost power or been hard to restart when warm
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
