Buying a used 2021 Hyundai Veloster N? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 18 owner complaints on file

A small complaint file — 18 unverified NHTSA reports, including 1 crash and 1 fire — so there is less pattern to read here than on higher-volume models. The top categories are lane departure (9) and steering (6). Two recalls apply: a fuel pump that can fail on Hyundai and Genesis performance models, and the campaign covering vehicles that may suddenly lose all drive power. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the performance-model fuel pump recall was performed, and ask about any stalling or no-start episodes.
  3. Verify the loss-of-drive-power recall status by VIN at a Hyundai dealer.
  4. On the test drive, check steering feel and whether lane-keeping assist behaves predictably or nags falsely.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
