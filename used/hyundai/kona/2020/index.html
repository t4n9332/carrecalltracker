Buying a used 2020 Hyundai Kona? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 158 owner complaints on file

One critical recall dominates this year: engines on the Elantra, Kona and Veloster can fail and burn. The other is administrative — a sticker listing the wrong axle weight limit. The unverified complaint file points the same direction as the recall: of 158 reports to NHTSA, 105 are filed under engine and 20 under power train, with 8 crashes, 3 fires and 12 injuries recorded. Engine service history is the central question on this one.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the engine recall remedy or any engine replacement was performed
  3. Request full oil change and service records for the engine
  4. Listen for knocking and check for oil consumption on a cold start
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
