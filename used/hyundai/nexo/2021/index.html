Buying a used 2021 Hyundai Nexo? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

The recall list narrows to two, both critical and both hydrogen-related: leak sensors that may fail to detect a hydrogen leak, and a tank component that can break and release gas. Each carries a park-outside warning, neither a stop-driving order. Owner complaints are close to nonexistent — 2 unverified reports to NHTSA, no crashes, fires or injuries — which is far too small a sample to draw conclusions from in either direction.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both hydrogen recalls were completed, with dealer documentation
  3. Ask which nearby dealer can service a Nexo and refuel it
  4. Have the hydrogen leak sensors and tank hardware inspected before purchase
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
