Buying a used 2019 Hyundai Kona Electric? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 54 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

This model year carries two critical battery fire-risk recalls, both instructing owners to park outside, plus a high-severity braking system recall. NHTSA has logged 54 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 2 crashes, 2 fires, and 1 injury; electrical system complaints dominate at 42, with fuel/propulsion at 10, and engine and powertrain at 5 each.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both battery fire-risk recalls have been completed
  3. Ask if the vehicle was ever parked outside per the recall advisory
  4. Verify the braking system recall repair was performed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
