Buying a used 2024 Hyundai Tucson? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 95 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

Four recalls, spanning two critical park-outside items — the overheating oil pump controller and the tow hitch wiring water intrusion — plus a loss of power steering and a tire label that wrongly lists a spare. The 95 unverified NHTSA complaints skew toward driver assistance and braking: forward collision avoidance 23, electrical 18, service brakes 15, with 12 crashes and 6 injuries reported and no fires. No stop-driving instructions apply.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the oil pump controller and tow hitch wiring recalls are both completed.
  3. Ask whether steering has ever gone heavy or lost assist, and confirm the power steering recall repair.
  4. Open the cargo floor to see whether a spare is actually present, since the tire label may be wrong.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
