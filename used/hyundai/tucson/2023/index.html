Buying a used 2023 Hyundai Tucson? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 132 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

Both recalls on this year are rated critical and both come with park-outside guidance: an oil pump controller that can overheat, and tow hitch wiring that lets water in. Neither carries a stop-driving instruction. Owners filed 132 unverified NHTSA complaints — 12 crashes, 1 fire, 5 injuries — with forward collision avoidance (28) and electrical system (26) near the top, alongside engine and a large unspecified category.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get dealer documentation that both critical recalls are closed, not just acknowledged.
  3. Inspect the tow hitch wiring and surrounding area for moisture, corrosion or burn marks.
  4. Ask whether the collision-avoidance system has given false alerts or unexpected interventions.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
