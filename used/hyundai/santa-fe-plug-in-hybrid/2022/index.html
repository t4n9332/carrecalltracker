Buying a used 2022 Hyundai Santa FE Plug-In Hybrid? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 27 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This plug-in hybrid model year has two critical recalls, a fuel tank leak risk and a tow hitch fire risk that advises parking outside, along with a high-severity seat belt pretensioner explosion recall and medium recalls for instrument display inversion and rearview camera failure. NHTSA lists 27 owner complaints, described as unverified reports, with 2 crashes and no reported fires or injuries. Engine and electrical system complaints each total 7, and fuel/propulsion system issues account for 6.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm fuel tank leak recall repair status
  3. Ask if tow hitch fire recall was completed and outdoor parking followed
  4. Verify seat belt pretensioner recall completion
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
