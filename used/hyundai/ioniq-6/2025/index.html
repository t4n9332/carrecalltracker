Buying a used 2025 Hyundai Ioniq 6? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 34 owner complaints on file

The shortest IONIQ 6 recall list here: a charging unit fault that can cause power loss, front seat belt anchors that may detach, and a charging port door that can come off. No stop-driving or park-outside advisories apply. Owner reporting is light — 34 unverified complaints to NHTSA, 2 crashes, no fires or injuries — and 20 fall under electrical system, the same area as the power-loss recall.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the charging unit recall repair was completed
  3. Inspect the front seat belt anchors and ask about that recall
  4. Open and close the charge port door and verify its recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
