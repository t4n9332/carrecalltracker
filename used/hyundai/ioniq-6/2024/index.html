Buying a used 2024 Hyundai Ioniq 6? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 38 owner complaints on file

Five recalls, four high-severity, centered on losing power or restraint integrity: two drive-power defects, loose bolts that can also cut power, a charging unit fault, and front seat belt anchors that may detach, plus the carryover charging port door. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Owners filed 38 unverified NHTSA complaints (2 crashes, no fires or injuries), mostly electrical system, with a small cluster under vehicle speed control.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. VIN-check the loose-bolt recall and both drive-power recalls
  3. Ask whether the front seat belt anchor recall was performed
  4. Test speed control behavior and the charging port door on the drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
