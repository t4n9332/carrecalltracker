Buying a used 2023 Hyundai Ioniq 6? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 56 owner complaints on file

Recalls for this model year cover a driveshaft that may break, a charging unit that can fail, seat belt anchors that may detach, and a charging port door that may detach. 56 owner complaints are on file, led by electrical system (33), then unknown/other (9), power train (8), and fuel/propulsion system (6). No crashes or fires were reported among these unverified complaints; one injury report appears.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the driveshaft and charging unit recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the seat belt anchor and charging port door recalls were completed
  4. Ask about electrical system issues history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
