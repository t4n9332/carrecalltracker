Buying a used 2022 Hyundai Elantra N? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 17 owner complaints on file

Four recalls apply to this model year: vehicles that may suddenly lose drive power, a fuel pump that may cause power loss, an airbag cover emblem that may come loose, and a rearview camera that may fail to display. NHTSA has logged 17 owner complaints (unverified reports), with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. Top complaint categories are electrical system (6), power train (4), engine (3), and fuel/propulsion system (2).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the drive-power-loss recall was completed
  3. Ask if the fuel pump power-loss recall was performed
  4. Verify the rearview camera display recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
