Buying a used 2022 Hyundai Elantra N? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 17 owner complaints on file

Recalls on this performance variant skew toward drivability: a campaign for Hyundai vehicles that may suddenly lose all drive power, and a fuel pump that can fail on Hyundai and Genesis performance models. Two lesser items cover a driver airbag emblem that may break loose and a rearview camera that may not display. Of 17 unverified owner complaints, electrical system and power train lead; no crashes, fires or injuries are reported among them.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump and loss-of-drive-power recall repairs by VIN
  3. Drive at length watching for hesitation, stalling, or power loss
  4. Check the rearview camera display and the airbag emblem on the steering wheel
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
