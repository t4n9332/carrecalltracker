Buying a used 2022 Hyundai Santa Cruz? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 183 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

The 2022 Santa Cruz carries two fire-related recalls — an engine oil leak and tow hitch wiring, the latter with a park-outside instruction. A third recall covers sudden loss of all drive power, which matches the complaint record: 90 of 183 unverified NHTSA owner reports fall under power train, with another 50 on the engine. Roof trim that can fly off is the minor item. Those reports include five crashes, one fire and five injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for documentation that both fire recalls, oil leak and tow hitch, were repaired.
  3. Inspect under the engine for oil leaks or fresh seepage.
  4. Test drive for hesitation or power loss when accelerating from a stop.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
