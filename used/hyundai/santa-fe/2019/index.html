Buying a used 2019 Hyundai Santa FE? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 197 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Two recalls: the tow hitch fire risk that Hyundai asked owners to park outside for, and side airbags that may be damaged. The complaint side is heavier than the recall count suggests — 197 unverified owner reports to NHTSA, including 16 crashes, 12 fires and 10 injuries, with engine (70) the largest category ahead of powertrain (34) and electrical (26). Fire-related reports at that level make service records and any engine work worth reviewing in detail.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check whether a tow hitch is fitted and whether that fire recall was performed.
  3. Ask for the side airbag recall status on this VIN before any test drive.
  4. Request full engine and powertrain repair records, including warranty and goodwill work.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
