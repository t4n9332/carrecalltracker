Buying a used 2024 Hyundai Santa FE? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 150 owner complaints on file

This model year has five high-severity recalls covering rollaway risk in park, wiring damage that may affect airbags, a starter cover electrical-short risk, a rearview camera that may not display, and seat belt anchors that may detach, plus two medium recalls for a blocked rearview camera image and a sunshade switch closing unexpectedly. NHTSA has recorded 145 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 11 crashes, 1 fire, and 9 injuries. Powertrain leads with 41 complaints, followed by an unspecified category (30), electrical system (28), and forward collision avoidance (15).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm rollaway-in-park and starter cover recalls were completed
  3. Ask about wiring/airbag and seat belt anchor recall repair status
  4. Test rearview camera display for blockage or failure
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
