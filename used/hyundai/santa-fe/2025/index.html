Buying a used 2025 Hyundai Santa FE? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 131 owner complaints on file

Most of this year's recalls point to assembly problems rather than wear: engine bolts that may not have been tightened properly, side curtain airbags possibly installed incorrectly, and front seat belt anchors that can detach. A starter cover fire risk in a crash and a backup camera that may not display round out the list. NHTSA shows 131 unverified owner complaints, no fires, 5 crashes and 4 injuries, led by electrical (28), powertrain (20) and forward collision avoidance (20).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for documentation that the engine bolt recall repair was actually performed.
  3. Confirm the side curtain airbag and seat belt anchor recalls were completed.
  4. Test the backup camera on multiple starts and note any forward collision warnings while driving.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
