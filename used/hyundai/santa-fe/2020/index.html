Buying a used 2020 Hyundai Santa FE? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 209 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

The recall list is short — the tow hitch fire risk, which carries a park-outside advisory until repaired, and a windshield that may not be properly bonded. Owner complaints run heavier: 209 unverified reports to NHTSA, with 13 crashes, 9 fires and 15 injuries logged. Engine leads at 76 reports, followed by powertrain (26) and electrical (25). A short recall list here does not describe the whole picture, and the fire reports are unverified but numerous.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the tow hitch fire recall was completed, especially if a hitch is installed.
  3. Inspect the windshield for leaks, wind noise, or signs of prior replacement.
  4. Ask for engine service history given the engine and fire-related complaint volume.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
