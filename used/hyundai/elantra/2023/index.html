Buying a used 2023 Hyundai Elantra? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 98 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

A critical oil pump fire risk recall applies to this vehicle, with a park-outside advisory, along with a separate recall for a loose airbag cover emblem. 98 owner complaints are on file, split evenly between unknown/other and electrical system at 23 each, with forward collision avoidance (20) and engine (18) also frequent. Complaints include 8 crash reports and 6 injury reports; these are unverified owner accounts.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the oil pump fire recall was completed and ask about the park-outside advisory status
  3. Confirm the airbag emblem recall was addressed
  4. Ask about any engine or electrical warning light history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
