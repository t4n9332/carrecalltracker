Buying a used 2020 Hyundai Elantra? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 250 owner complaints on file

This year stands apart from the others listed. Two critical recalls apply — a rear wheel that may come off, and engines that can fail and burn — plus possibly loose front suspension bolts. None is flagged stop-driving or park-outside. The complaint record is also the heaviest here: 250 unverified NHTSA reports including 21 crashes, six fires and 16 injuries, with engine (105) and power train (47) far ahead of every other area.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine and rear wheel recall repairs are closed against the VIN
  3. Ask whether the engine was ever replaced and get documentation if so
  4. Have the front suspension bolts and rear wheel hardware inspected before buying
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
