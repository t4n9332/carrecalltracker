Buying a used 2021 Hyundai Elantra? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 130 owner complaints on file

Recalls for this model year include seat belt pretensioners that may explode, a windshield that may detach during a crash, and front seat recliners that were improperly welded, plus a rearview camera display recall. 130 owner complaints are on file, spread fairly evenly across forward collision avoidance (32), electrical system (30), unknown/other (29), and seat belts (27). Complaints include 8 crashes, 1 fire, and 3 injuries; these are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seat belt pretensioner recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the windshield recall was completed
  4. Test the rearview camera and forward collision system
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
