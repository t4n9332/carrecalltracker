Buying a used 2021 Hyundai Santa FE Hybrid? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 20 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Three recalls cover the 2021 Santa Fe Hybrid: tow hitch fire risk, which carries a park-outside instruction; a seat belt part that can explode; and a rearview camera that may not display. The owner complaint record is light — 20 unverified NHTSA reports with no crashes, fires or injuries recorded — spread thinly across engine, power train and fuel/propulsion. With so few reports filed, the recall list is the more informative part of this year's record.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the tow hitch and seat belt recalls show as completed for this VIN.
  3. Shift into reverse several times and confirm the camera image appears every time.
  4. Ask whether a trailer hitch was added, and who installed it.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
