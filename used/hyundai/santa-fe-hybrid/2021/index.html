Buying a used 2021 Hyundai Santa FE Hybrid? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 20 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year has three recalls: a critical tow-hitch fire risk (shared with Santa Cruz) requiring outside parking, a seat belt pretensioner explosion issue, and a rearview camera that may fail to display. NHTSA has logged 20 owner complaints for this model year (unverified reports), with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. The largest complaint category is engine (8), followed by power train (7), unknown/other (5), and fuel/propulsion system (4).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the tow-hitch fire recall was completed and outside-parking status
  3. Ask about seat belt pretensioner recall completion
  4. Test rearview camera display function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
