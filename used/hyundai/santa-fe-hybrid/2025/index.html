Buying a used 2025 Hyundai Santa FE Hybrid? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 88 owner complaints on file

The 2025 Santa Fe Hybrid has three recalls, none with a stop-driving or park-outside instruction: side curtain airbags possibly installed wrong, front seat belt anchors that may detach, and a backup camera image that may not appear. Two of the three are restraint-related. Owner reports total 88 for a recent model year, led by engine (29), electrical (27) and fuel/propulsion (22), with 20 on vehicle speed control; they include four crashes and seven injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the curtain airbag and seat belt anchor recalls were performed on this VIN.
  3. Tug firmly on the front seat belt anchors and check for movement.
  4. Test the backup camera repeatedly and note any surging or hesitation while driving.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Hyundai dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
