Buying a used 2025 Polestar Polestar 3? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 78 owner complaints on file

This 2025 Polestar 3 carries six recalls, including three high-severity items: sudden loss of drive power, wiring damage that may cause malfunctions, and a panoramic sunroof detachment risk, plus medium recalls for a detachable front bumper and loose rear seat backrest brackets, and a low-severity rearview camera issue. NHTSA logged 78 owner complaints for this model year, described here as unverified reports, including 4 crashes and 1 injury. Of those, 49 fall under electrical system, 10 each under forward collision avoidance and fuel/propulsion system, and 8 under back-over prevention.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the drive-power-loss recall has been performed
  3. Ask about the wiring damage recall and sunroof detachment recall status
  4. Inspect the panoramic sunroof and front bumper attachment
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Polestar dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
