Buying a used 2022 Polestar Polestar 2? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 305 owner complaints on file

One high-severity recall covers sudden loss of power while driving; the other three all concern the rearview camera not showing an image. Neither type carried a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. This year has the heaviest complaint file of the range — 305 unverified NHTSA reports, with 1 crash and no fires or injuries. Back-over prevention dominates at 209 reports, followed by electrical system (46), uncategorized (40) and visibility/wiper (20), echoing the camera and electronics recalls.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Test the backup camera repeatedly, including on a cold start, for a blank or frozen screen
  3. Confirm the sudden-power-loss recall is recorded as completed for this VIN
  4. Ask the seller whether the car has ever shut down unexpectedly or thrown electrical faults
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Polestar dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
