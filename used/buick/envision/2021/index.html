Buying a used 2021 Buick Envision? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 23 owner complaints on file

This model year carries two medium-severity recalls: one involving inconsistent airbag warning light function and one where the daytime running lights may stay on continuously; neither is flagged as stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA has logged 23 owner complaints for this vehicle, including 2 crash reports and no fire or injury reports. The most-cited complaint areas are fuel/propulsion system (6), power train (5), and electrical system (5). Owner complaints are unverified reports submitted directly to NHTSA.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the airbag warning light recall was completed
  3. Check daytime running light behavior for staying on unexpectedly
  4. Ask about any fuel/propulsion or electrical system repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Buick dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
