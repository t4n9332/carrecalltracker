Buying a used 2019 Buick Regal? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 19 owner complaints on file

Three medium-severity recalls apply: weak rear brakes, front seat frames with possibly bad welds, and possible loss of backup power brake assist. None carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The 19 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA report no crashes, fires or injuries — a different picture from the 2018 car. They lean toward electrical system and power train, with smaller numbers under engine and service brakes.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check recall status by VIN, including the front seat frame weld inspection.
  3. Rock and adjust both front seats to feel for looseness in the frame.
  4. Ask about any electrical or transmission faults, warning lights, or shift issues.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Buick dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
