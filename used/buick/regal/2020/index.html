Buying a used 2020 Buick Regal? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

The final year has the shortest recall list here: two medium-severity items, front seat frames with possibly bad welds and possible loss of backup power brake assist. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Only two unverified owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA — one electrical system, one tires — with no crashes, fires or injuries reported. That count is too small to read as a reliability signal either way, given how few of these were sold.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN for recall status on both open items.
  3. Inspect the front seats for movement or play in the frame.
  4. Confirm brake pedal effort stays normal and ask about any assist warnings.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Buick dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
