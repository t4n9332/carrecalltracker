Buying a used 2019 Buick Lacrosse? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file

The 2019 car carries two recalls: a critical transmission fluid leak and a medium-severity rear brake weakness. Unlike the 2018, no fuel pump fire recall is listed for this year. Neither recall carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Only six unverified owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA, with no crashes, fires or injuries reported; they fall mainly under unknown/other and engine, with single entries for fuel/propulsion and electrical.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the transmission fluid leak recall was performed, by VIN.
  3. Inspect the driveway or garage floor where the car is parked for fluid spots.
  4. Ask whether rear brake service or the rear brake recall work has been done.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Buick dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
