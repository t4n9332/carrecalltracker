Buying a used 2021 Buick Enclave? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 25 owner complaints on file

Much shorter list than the 2020: two recalls, both about airbags — one covering airbags that may not deploy properly and one specific to roof airbags — shared with the Chevrolet Traverse. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside advisory, but both matter in a crash, so completion status is worth confirming. Owners have filed 25 complaints with NHTSA with no crashes, fires or injuries reported; electrical system and service brakes lead at six each, then seat belts. Complaints are unverified reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify both airbag recalls are closed on this VIN and watch the airbag light at startup.
  3. Test the brakes on the drive; service brakes tie for the most-reported complaint area.
  4. Check that each seat belt latches and retracts, given the seat belt complaints on file.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Buick dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
