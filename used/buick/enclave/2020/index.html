Buying a used 2020 Buick Enclave? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 56 owner complaints on file

This 2020 Enclave has six recalls, two critical: missing transmission bolts that can cause a leak and fire risk, and over-cured tires with a failure risk (also flagged in a separate overcured-tire recall for GM trucks and SUVs). Other recalls cover a frame rail crack, roof-rail air bag underperformance, and a fuel pump blockage that can stall the engine. NHTSA has 56 owner complaints for this model year (unverified reports), including 2 crashes and 2 injuries, with top categories being engine (15), unknown/other (12), electrical system (9), and power train (8).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the transmission bolt recall was completed (leak/fire risk)
  3. Check tire DOT date codes for the over-cured tire recall
  4. Ask about engine, electrical, or power train issues given complaint history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Buick dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
