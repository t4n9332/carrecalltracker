Buying a used 2018 Honda Crf250l? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This model year carries two high-severity recalls: a wiring harness installation problem and a motorcycle transmission part that may come loose. NHTSA has recorded 1 owner complaint for this year, an unverified report, filed under the electrical system category, with no crashes, fires, or injuries noted in that complaint. Confirming recall completion is the main record-based check available for this specific model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the wiring harness recall was completed
  3. Confirm the transmission part recall was completed and inspect for looseness
  4. Ask about any electrical system issues, the category of the one complaint on file
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
