Buying a used 2019 Honda Insight? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 97 owner complaints on file

This year carries a long recall list: airbags that may not deploy in a crash, three separate fuel pump recalls for stalling, front seat belts that may not latch, and a seat sensor that may cause unintended airbag deployment, plus medium-severity backup camera and safety-system software recalls. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Among 97 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA, forward collision avoidance (17) and fuel system and gasoline (15 each) lead; 12 crashes, six injuries, one fire.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify all three fuel pump recalls and the airbag non-deployment recall are closed on this VIN.
  3. Cold-start the car and watch for stalling or hesitation before and during the test drive.
  4. Check the backup camera display and driver-assist warning lights through several restarts.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
