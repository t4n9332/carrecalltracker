Buying a used 2019 Honda Insight? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 97 owner complaints on file

This model year has eight recalls, most rated high severity, covering an airbag deployment failure, multiple fuel pump recalls that could cause stalling, software that may disable key systems, seat belt buckles that may not latch, front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally, and a lower-severity backup camera display recall; none are stop-drive or park-outside. NHTSA has logged 97 owner complaints, unverified reports, including 12 crashes, 1 fire, and 6 injuries. Top complaint areas are forward collision avoidance (17), fuel system (15), gasoline (15), and unknown/other (14).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all fuel pump recalls were completed
  3. Ask about any stalling incidents or fuel system repairs
  4. Test the backup camera display and check seat belt buckle latching
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
