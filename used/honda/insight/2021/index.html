Buying a used 2021 Honda Insight? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 24 owner complaints on file

A shorter list than earlier Insight years: three high-severity recalls, covering hybrids that may lose power while driving and two seat sensor issues, one where the passenger seat sensor may crack and one that may cause unintended airbag deployment. Neither triggers a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA are low at 24, and unverified, led by forward collision avoidance (8), service brakes (4) and air bags (3). One crash and one injury were reported; no fires.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check the VIN for the hybrid power-loss and both seat sensor recalls.
  3. Look for an illuminated airbag light and ask whether the passenger seat sensor was replaced.
  4. Note any unexpected collision-avoidance braking or alerts on a mixed-speed test drive.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
