Buying a used 2022 Honda Insight? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 32 owner complaints on file

This model year has three recalls, all rated high severity: a center seat belt that may not lock, a fuel pump that may fail, and front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally; none are stop-drive or park-outside. NHTSA has logged 32 owner complaints, unverified reports, including 2 injuries and no reported crashes or fires. Top complaint areas are forward collision avoidance (14), electrical system (10), unknown/other (7), and service brakes (5).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump and center seat belt recalls were completed
  3. Test the center rear seat belt lock function
  4. Ask about any fuel system related repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
