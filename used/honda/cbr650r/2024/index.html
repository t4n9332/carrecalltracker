Buying a used 2024 Honda Cbr650r? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Two high-severity recalls apply to this year: a gear shift arm that can fall off, also covering the CB650R, and wiring damage that kills the lights and horn. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning, though both affect control and visibility to other drivers. Just one unverified NHTSA complaint is on file, logged under an unclassified category, with no crashes, fires or injuries — a count too small to read as a durability signal.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the shift arm and wiring recalls were completed by VIN
  3. Check the shift lever and its fastener for looseness before riding
  4. Test headlight, taillight, turn signals and horn, and inspect the harness for chafing
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
