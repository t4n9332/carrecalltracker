Buying a used 2019 Honda Accord? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 684 owner complaints on file

This year's record is dominated by high-severity recalls: three separate fuel-pump campaigns that can stall the engine, front seat belts that may not latch, and a seat sensor linked to unintended airbag deployment. A medium software recall can disable safety systems. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Owners filed 684 unverified NHTSA complaints — 24 crashes, two fires, 27 injuries — clustered in engine, forward collision avoidance, fuel system and electrical categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof all fuel-pump recalls were completed, by VIN
  3. Confirm the front seat belt latch and seat sensor recalls were done
  4. Test drive for stalling and for false forward-collision alerts
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
