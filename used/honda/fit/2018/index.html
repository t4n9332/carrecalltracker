Buying a used 2018 Honda FIT? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 116 owner complaints on file

This model year has two high-severity recalls, a fuel pump that may fail and front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally, along with two medium recalls addressing a rearview camera that may not display or appear. NHTSA lists 116 owner complaints (4 crashes, 0 fires, 9 injuries) for this year, an unverified record, led by electrical system (34), fuel/propulsion system (30), engine (22), and unknown/other (17).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump and front passenger airbag recalls were completed
  3. Test the rearview camera display for the related recall issue
  4. Ask about electrical system or engine issues reported by prior owners
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
