Buying a used 2019 Honda FIT? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 136 owner complaints on file

The 2019 Fit is covered by three separately listed fuel pump recalls, all describing failure that can stall the engine, plus the seat sensor airbag recall and both backup camera recalls — six recalls to verify, the most of any vehicle here. None carry a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Of 136 unverified owner complaints, electrical system leads at 49 and fuel/propulsion at 40; reported totals include 9 crashes, 1 fire and 3 injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN and confirm all three fuel pump recalls individually
  3. Check the backup camera for a blank or dropping image in reverse
  4. Ask the seller about any stalling at speed or no-start episodes
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
