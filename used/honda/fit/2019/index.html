Buying a used 2019 Honda FIT? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 136 owner complaints on file

This model year carries multiple recall campaigns for a fuel pump that may fail, listed three separate times in NHTSA records, plus a front passenger airbag that may deploy unintentionally and a rearview camera that may not display. NHTSA has logged 136 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 9 crashes, 1 fire, and 3 injuries. The largest complaint categories are electrical system (49) and fuel/propulsion system (40), followed by unknown/other (25) and engine (16), which line up with the recall topics listed above.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump recall (listed multiple times) has been completed
  3. Ask if the passenger airbag recall repair was done
  4. Verify the rearview camera display recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
