Buying a used 2020 Honda CR-V? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 354 owner complaints on file

Seven recalls apply, none flagged critical: rear subframe bolts that may loosen, front seat belts that may not latch, a fuel pump that can stall the engine, two passenger seat-sensor airbag defects, a fuel gauge that can read wrong and lead to stalls, and a mislabeled door placard. Owners filed 354 unverified complaints with NHTSA — 21 crashes, 17 injuries, 2 fires — led by forward-collision avoidance, air bags, electrical and steering. Air bag complaints sit alongside those seat-sensor recalls.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the passenger airbag-off light responds correctly with someone seated.
  3. Verify the subframe bolt and fuel pump recalls were completed.
  4. Compare the door placard tire and weight info against the tires fitted.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
