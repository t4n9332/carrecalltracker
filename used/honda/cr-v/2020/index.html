Buying a used 2020 Honda CR-V? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 354 owner complaints on file

This model year carries seven recalls, including five rated high severity: rear subframe bolts that may loosen, seat belt buckles that may not latch, a fuel pump that may fail, a passenger seat sensor that may crack, and front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally. Two medium recalls cover an inaccurate fuel gauge and a door label with wrong weight/tire data. NHTSA has logged 354 owner complaints (21 crashes, 2 fires, 17 injuries), an unverified record, with forward collision avoidance, air bags, electrical system, and steering cited most often.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the rear subframe bolt and seat belt buckle recalls have been completed
  3. Confirm the fuel pump and passenger seat sensor recalls were performed
  4. Test the forward collision system and fuel gauge reading on a test drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
