Buying a used 2018 Honda CR-V? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 2,137 owner complaints on file

This 2018 CR-V has five recalls: unintended power steering input, a fuel pump that may fail and stall the engine (listed as two separate fuel pump recalls), seat belt buckles that may not latch, and front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally. None require stopping use or parking outside per NHTSA. NHTSA has logged 2,137 owner complaints for this model year, including 46 crashes, 2 fires, and 47 injuries; engine (527) is the largest category, followed by forward collision avoidance (392), fuel/propulsion system (369), and steering (323). These are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both fuel pump recalls and the power steering recall were completed
  3. Test the forward collision/automatic braking system and steering response
  4. Ask about seat belt buckle latch function on all seats
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
