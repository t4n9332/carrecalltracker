Buying a used 2019 Honda CR-V? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 1,095 owner complaints on file

This 2019 CR-V carries eight recalls, including a critical fuel tank weld defect that can leak and cause fire, a steering wheel defect that can disable the airbag, loosening rear subframe bolts, two separate fuel pump failure recalls, seat belt buckles that may not latch, and unintended front passenger airbag deployment. NHTSA has logged 1,095 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 30 crashes, 2 fires, and 35 injuries. Leading complaint categories are forward collision avoidance (298), steering (194), electrical system (159), and fuel/propulsion system (139).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel tank weld and both fuel pump recalls were completed
  3. Ask whether the steering wheel airbag-disable recall was repaired
  4. Check rear subframe bolt recall status and any forward collision avoidance or electrical complaints on this VIN
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
