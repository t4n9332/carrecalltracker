Buying a used 2024 Honda CR-V? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 388 owner complaints on file

The 2024 carries the same steering gearbox recall as the 2023, plus a low-severity recall for tires missing the required date code marking. Complaint volume is 388, below 2023, but unverified owner reports include more crashes — 27 — along with 14 injuries and no fires. Steering still leads at 124. This year also shows visibility/wiper complaints at 48, an area absent from the 2022 and 2023 lists, with forward collision avoidance also at 48.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering gearbox recall is closed for this specific VIN
  3. Check tire sidewalls for date codes and ask if tires were replaced
  4. Test wipers, washers and camera views in rain or low light
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
