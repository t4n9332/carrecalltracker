Buying a used 2023 Honda CMX300? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This 2023 Rebel 300 carries a critical-severity recall for oil leaking onto hot exhaust, plus two separate recalls covering a handlebar screw that can fall out or detach. The oil recall is the standout given the fire exposure it describes, though it carries no stop-riding or park-outside instruction. The complaint record is essentially blank — one unverified NHTSA report under power train — which is typical of a low-volume motorcycle model year rather than a signal of anything.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get written confirmation the oil-leak recall repair was performed by a Honda dealer.
  3. Inspect the engine, exhaust and underside for oil residue or burn marks.
  4. Check that the handlebar clamp screws are all present and properly torqued.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
