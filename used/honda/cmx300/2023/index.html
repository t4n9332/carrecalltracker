Buying a used 2023 Honda CMX300? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This model year carries a critical-severity recall for an engine cover that may leak oil, along with a separate recall for a handlebar lock screw that may loosen — that recall was issued more than once. NHTSA has only 1 owner complaint on file for this model year, an unverified report, filed under the power train category, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported in connection with it. Neither recall is flagged as stop-drive or park-outside.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine cover recall was completed and check for oil leaks
  3. Verify the handlebar lock screw recall repair and check screw tightness
  4. Ask about any power train-related complaints or repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
