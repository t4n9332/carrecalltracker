Buying a used 2021 Honda Odyssey? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 140 owner complaints on file

This model year has 8 recalls, topped by one critical issue: a possible fuel tank leak on Braun-modified (wheelchair-conversion) vans. High-severity recalls cover the fuel pump, a passenger seat sensor that may crack, and airbags (side and front passenger) that may deploy unexpectedly; medium recalls involve side mirror glass, the rearview camera, and wheelchair restraints on van conversions. NHTSA has recorded 140 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year, including 2 crashes, 1 fire, and 4 injuries, with electrical system (53) the most-cited category.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the fuel pump and airbag recalls have been completed
  3. If wheelchair-conversion equipped, verify the fuel tank and wheelchair restraint recalls were addressed
  4. Check side mirror glass and rearview camera for recall-related repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
