Buying a used 2020 Honda Odyssey? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 460 owner complaints on file

This model year carries 10 recalls, including two critical ones covering third-row power outlet wiring that can short and a fuel tank leak risk on Braun-modified (wheelchair-conversion) vans. Additional high-severity recalls address sliding door latches, the rearview camera display, seat belt buckles, the fuel pump, a passenger seat sensor, and airbags that may deploy unexpectedly. NHTSA has logged 460 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year, including 4 crashes, 1 fire, and 20 injuries, with forward collision avoidance (176) and electrical system (160) as the most-cited categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the sliding door latch and rearview camera recalls were completed
  3. Ask whether the seat belt buckle and airbag recalls have been performed
  4. If it's a wheelchair-conversion (Braun-modified) van, verify the fuel tank recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
