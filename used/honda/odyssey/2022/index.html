Buying a used 2022 Honda Odyssey? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 131 owner complaints on file

This is the busiest Odyssey year listed: nine recalls, five rated high. Three touch airbags — a passenger seat sensor that can crack, a seat sensor linked to unintended deployment, and side airbags that may deploy without a crash. A fuel pump that can stall the engine is also covered. More routine: mirror glass, rearview camera, pinhole tires, second-row seat locking. Of 131 unverified owner complaints to NHTSA, electrical system and forward collision avoidance lead, with 21 injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all airbag and seat-sensor recalls closed by VIN
  3. Ask whether the fuel pump recall repair was performed
  4. Test rearview camera, mirrors, and second-row seat latches
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
