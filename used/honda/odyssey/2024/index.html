Buying a used 2024 Honda Odyssey? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 102 owner complaints on file

This model year has 3 recalls, with two high-severity items covering possible steering corrosion and front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally, plus a medium recall for wheelchair restraints on van conversions. NHTSA has logged 102 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year, including 1 crash and 5 injuries with no reported fires, with electrical system (45) and engine (37) as the most-cited categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the steering corrosion and airbag recalls have been completed
  3. If wheelchair-conversion equipped, verify the wheelchair restraint recall was addressed
  4. Ask the seller about any electrical or engine issues reported to the dealer
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
