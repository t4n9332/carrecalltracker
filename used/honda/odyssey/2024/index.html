Buying a used 2024 Honda Odyssey? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 100 owner complaints on file

A shorter list than the earlier years: three recalls, all high severity — steering that can rust and bind, wheelchair-van restraints that may not lock, and a seat sensor that can cause unintended airbag deployment. The fuel pump recall that hit 2022 and 2023 is not listed here. NHTSA has 100 unverified owner complaints, weighted toward electrical system and engine, with one crash and five injuries reported and no fires.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm steering and seat-sensor recalls performed by VIN
  3. Ask about any engine or stalling issues and repair records
  4. Exercise all electrical features, including screen and driver aids
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
