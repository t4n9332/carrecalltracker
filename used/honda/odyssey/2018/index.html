Buying a used 2018 Honda Odyssey? Check these 16 recalls first

16 NHTSA recalls · 982 owner complaints on file

An unusually deep recall list. Three are critical: third-row outlet wiring that can overheat, engines that can seize from a faulty bearing, and a fuel leak and fire risk on Braun-modified vans. High-severity items cover sliding doors opening while moving or not latching, rollaway after parking, rear brakes, seat belts, fuel pump, and side airbags deploying without a crash. Of 982 unverified NHTSA complaints, electrical (256), collision avoidance (198), engine (186) and power train (164) dominate.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get documentation that the engine bearing and third-row outlet wiring recalls were completed.
  3. Cycle both power sliding doors repeatedly and confirm they latch and stay shut.
  4. Park on an incline to confirm it holds, and ask for engine and electrical repair records.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
