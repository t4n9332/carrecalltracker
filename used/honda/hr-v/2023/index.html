Buying a used 2023 Honda HR-V? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 686 owner complaints on file

This model year has three recalls: a seat belt pretensioner missing a rivet, a fuel pump that may fail, and a steering gearbox that can cause difficulty steering; none are listed as stop-drive or park-outside. NHTSA has logged 685 owner complaints for this vehicle, which are unverified reports, including 7 crashes and 4 injuries, no fires. The complaint record shows visibility/wiper issues (319) and unknown/other (214) as the largest categories, with visibility (78) and steering (77) also listed.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seat belt pretensioner and fuel pump recalls were completed
  3. Check for wiper or visibility-related complaints or repairs in service history
  4. Test steering for stiffness or difficulty turning
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
