Buying a used 2023 Honda HR-V? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 685 owner complaints on file

Three high-severity recalls apply: front seat belts that may be missing a rivet, a fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine, and a steering gearbox that can make steering difficult. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. What stands out in the 685 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA is visibility and wipers, with 319 reports plus 78 more under visibility. Steering drew 77. Seven crashes and four injuries were reported; no fires.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN on NHTSA and confirm the seat belt rivet, fuel pump and steering gearbox recalls were completed.
  3. Test wipers at every speed and check the windshield for glare, streaking or fogging in daylight and at night.
  4. On the test drive, note any heavy, notchy or uneven steering effort, especially at parking-lot speeds.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
