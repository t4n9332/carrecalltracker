Buying a used 2019 Honda HR-V? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 234 owner complaints on file

The 2019 HR-V has the widest recall spread of these years — seven campaigns. Five are high severity: rear brakes that may stop poorly, three separate fuel pump stall recalls, and a seat sensor that may cause unintended airbag deployment. Two medium recalls cover a backup camera image that may fail or not appear. None were issued as stop-driving or park-outside. Owners filed 234 unverified NHTSA complaints, with 18 crashes, 1 fire and 4 injuries, mostly electrical and fuel system.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Pull the full VIN recall report and confirm each of the seven campaigns is closed, especially all three fuel pump ones
  3. Turn on the backup camera repeatedly during the test drive to see whether the image appears every time
  4. Check the airbag warning light at startup and ask about any seat sensor work
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
