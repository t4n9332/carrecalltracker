Buying a used 2022 Honda HR-V? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 86 owner complaints on file

The 2022 HR-V carries three recalls: one high severity for a passenger seat sensor that may crack with airbag risk, and two medium ones for a backup camera image that may fail or not appear. No stop-driving or park-outside warnings were issued. Owners filed 86 unverified complaints with NHTSA, including 10 crashes, 1 fire and 8 injuries. Complaint areas lean electrical at 26, then power train and engine at 18 each, with 14 uncategorized.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seat sensor recall was completed by VIN and check the passenger airbag indicator
  3. Cycle the backup camera several times to confirm the image loads reliably
  4. Ask about transmission or engine symptoms, the second-largest reported areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
