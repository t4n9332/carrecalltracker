Buying a used 2018 Honda HR-V? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 156 owner complaints on file

The 2018 HR-V carries three high-severity recalls: rear brakes that may stop poorly, and two separate campaigns for fuel pumps that can fail and stall the engine. None were issued with stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Owners filed 156 unverified complaints with NHTSA, including 11 crashes and 5 injuries and no fires. The largest group by far is electrical system at 47, followed by fuel system and gasoline entries at 27 each and 22 uncategorized.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask the dealer to confirm by VIN that both fuel pump recalls and the rear brake recall were completed
  3. Test drive long enough to check for stalling or hesitation at low speed
  4. Have the rear brakes measured and the electrical system scanned for stored fault codes
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
