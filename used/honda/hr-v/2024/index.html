Buying a used 2024 Honda HR-V? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 108 owner complaints on file

Four high-severity recalls apply, including two separate ones for a driver's seat that may be loose, plus the missing seat belt rivet and the steering gearbox that can make steering difficult. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Of 108 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA, steering leads with 51, followed by service brakes and forward collision avoidance at 12 each. Eight crashes, eight injuries and one fire were reported in that small complaint pool.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both driver's-seat recalls were performed, then push and rock the seat to feel for play.
  3. Ask whether the steering gearbox remedy was done, and drive for stiff or inconsistent steering.
  4. Test braking and the forward collision system for false alerts or unexpected automatic braking.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
