Buying a used 2022 Honda Crf1100? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file

The 2022 Africa Twin carries the same two recalls as the 2021 — a stall condition tied to Wheelie Control, and a horn and high beam that may fail — with no stop-driving or park-outside advisory. NHTSA shows just 6 unverified owner complaints, one involving a crash, none reporting fire or injury. Those reports spread thinly across fuel/propulsion, electrical system, service brakes and unclassified areas rather than pointing at any single recurring problem.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both recalls are closed by running the VIN
  3. Check horn, high beam and all lighting from a cold start
  4. Test front and rear brake feel and lever travel
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
