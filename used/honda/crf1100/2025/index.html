Buying a used 2025 Honda Crf1100? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

Three high-severity recalls apply to this model year: a software glitch that may stall the engine, an oil leak risk affecting the CMX1100, CRF1100, and XL750, and a defect that can cause loss of the horn and high-beam switch. NHTSA has recorded 4 owner complaints for this year (0 crashes, 0 fires, 0 injuries), an unverified record, with service brakes (3) and electrical system (1) cited.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine-stall software recall was completed
  3. Ask whether the oil leak recall repair was performed
  4. Test the horn and high-beam switch, and check for oil leaks
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
