Buying a used 2025 Honda Crf1100? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

The 2025 adds a high-severity recall the earlier Africa Twins do not have: engine oil may leak onto the rear tire. The Wheelie Control stall recall and the horn and high beam recall both carry over, and none of the three includes a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Only 4 unverified owner complaints are on file, with no crashes, fires or injuries — expected for a recent model year — and three of those four fall under service brakes.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Inspect the rear tire, wheel and swingarm for oil residue
  3. Confirm the oil leak and Wheelie Control recalls are closed by VIN
  4. Check brake pad thickness, fluid condition and stopping feel
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
