Buying a used 2018 Honda GL1800? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 34 owner complaints on file

The 2018 Gold Wing carries two recalls that span the early GL1800 run: a critical engine bolt that may break and lock the rear wheel, and a high-severity fuel pump that can fail and stall the bike. Neither was issued with a stop-driving or park-outside warning. This year also shows the heaviest owner feedback of the group — 34 unverified NHTSA complaints, including 5 crashes and 7 injuries, concentrated in vehicle speed control, electrical, steering and engine.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get proof both recalls — engine bolt and fuel pump — were completed by VIN
  3. Ride-test throttle response and steering feel, the top unverified complaint areas
  4. Ask whether the bike has ever stalled or lost power while moving
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
