Buying a used 2020 Honda GL1800? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

For 2020 the Gold Wing carries three recalls: the critical engine bolt that may break and lock the rear wheel, the fuel pump stall recall, and a newer medium-severity software error that can also stall the engine. None were issued as stop-driving or park-outside. The complaint side is very quiet — a single unverified NHTSA report, touching power train, service brakes and engine, with no crashes, fires or injuries recorded for the year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the engine control software update from the stall recall was applied
  3. Verify the engine bolt and fuel pump recalls closed by VIN
  4. Check brake feel and pad life, one of the few areas an owner reported
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
