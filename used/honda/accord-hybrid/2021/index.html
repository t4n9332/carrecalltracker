Buying a used 2021 Honda Accord Hybrid? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 48 owner complaints on file

A shorter recall list than surrounding years, with no fuel pump or power-loss campaign. The three recalls involve a center seat belt that may not lock child seats, a passenger seat sensor that may crack, and a seat sensor that may cause unintended airbag deployment — all occupant-safety items, none requiring parking outside. Owner complaints total 48 unverified NHTSA reports, led by forward collision avoidance and engine, with two crashes and no injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. If installing a child seat, test that the center belt locks properly
  3. Verify both seat sensor recalls were completed and the airbag light is off
  4. Ask about unexpected collision-warning or braking behavior, the top complaint area
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
