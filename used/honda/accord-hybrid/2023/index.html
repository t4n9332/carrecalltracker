Buying a used 2023 Honda Accord Hybrid? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 139 owner complaints on file

The busiest recall year among these hybrids: six campaigns, including a critical fuel pump that can leak, a transmission that may lose power, two separate recalls for a driver's seat that may be loose, a drive-power loss recall, and a tire kit cap that can fly off. Owner complaints reach 139 unverified NHTSA reports concentrated in electrical (49) and service brakes (45), with four crashes and four injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump leak recall was completed and check for fuel odor around the car
  3. Rock the driver's seat firmly to check for looseness or play in the mounting
  4. Ask about power loss or hesitation while driving, and about any brake or electrical repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
