Buying a used 2023 Honda Accord Hybrid? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 140 owner complaints on file

This model year carries six recalls, including a critical fuel pump leak issue and high-severity campaigns for possible transmission failure, a loose driver's seat frame, loss of drive power, and a tire repair kit bottle hazard. NHTSA has logged 140 owner complaints, including 5 crashes, 4 injuries, and no fires. Electrical system (49) and service brakes (45) are the top complaint categories, followed by engine (30) and unspecified issues (24). These are unverified owner-submitted reports, not confirmed defect findings.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump leak recall has been completed
  3. Check the driver's seat frame for looseness and confirm that recall was addressed
  4. Ask about brake system condition given the number of service brake complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
