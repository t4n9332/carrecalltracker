Buying a used 2023 Honda Passport? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 38 owner complaints on file

This model year carries five recalls, including a critical fuel filler pipe leak risk and high-severity issues covering the brake master cylinder, steering gearbox, and rear subframe corrosion, plus a rearview camera recall. NHTSA logged 38 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 3 crashes and 7 injuries, with no fires. Forward collision avoidance (12) and service brakes (11) were the most-cited complaint categories, followed by unknown/other (8) and engine (5).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel filler pipe recall has been completed
  3. Ask whether the brake master cylinder and steering gearbox recalls were addressed
  4. Have the rear subframe checked for corrosion
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
