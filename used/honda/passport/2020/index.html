Buying a used 2020 Honda Passport? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 100 owner complaints on file

This model year has 10 recalls, none critical: five high-severity items cover tires that may lose air suddenly, the fuel pump, a cracking passenger seat sensor, front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally, and rear subframe corrosion. Medium recalls involve instrument panel glitches, rearview camera problems, and side mirror glass; a low-severity recall covers a weight rating label that may wipe off. NHTSA has recorded 100 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year, including 1 crash and no reported fires or injuries, with forward collision avoidance (34) the most-cited category.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the fuel pump and rear subframe corrosion recalls were completed
  3. Check tire pressure history and confirm the airbag/seat sensor recalls were addressed
  4. Verify the rearview camera and side mirror glass recalls were performed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
