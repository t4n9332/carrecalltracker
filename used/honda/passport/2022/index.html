Buying a used 2022 Honda Passport? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 61 owner complaints on file

The shortest Passport list here: four recalls. One is critical — the brake failure risk recall — plus rear frame rust-through, side mirror glass that can fall off, and a rearview camera that may fail. The fuel pump and seat-sensor airbag recalls that cover 2020 and 2021 are not listed for this year. NHTSA shows 61 unverified owner complaints, spread fairly evenly across electrical system, forward collision avoidance and engine, with one crash and two injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake recall was performed at a dealer
  3. Inspect rear frame for rust and check mirror glass
  4. Verify the rearview camera displays reliably every time
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
