Buying a used 2019 Honda Passport? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 275 owner complaints on file

Ten recalls, including a critical one for tires that may fail suddenly and a high-severity report of missing frame welds. Also covered: two fuel pump recalls, a hood that can fly open, a seat sensor tied to unintended airbag deployment, and rear frame rust-through. Three separate recalls address blank dashboards and backup cameras. NHTSA lists 275 unverified owner complaints — electrical system, forward collision avoidance and engine lead — with seven crashes reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm tire and frame-weld recall inspections were done
  3. Inspect rear frame for rust, especially in salt-belt cars
  4. Check hood latch, dashboard display and backup camera
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
