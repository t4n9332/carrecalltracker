Buying a used 2021 Honda Passport? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 140 owner complaints on file

This model year has 7 recalls, none critical: five high-severity items cover a brake master cylinder that may separate, the fuel pump, a passenger seat sensor that may crack, front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally, and rear subframe corrosion. Two medium recalls involve side mirror glass and the rearview camera display. NHTSA has logged 140 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year, including 10 crashes, no reported fires, and 3 injuries, with forward collision avoidance (51) the most-cited category.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the brake master cylinder recall has been completed
  3. Confirm the fuel pump, seat sensor, and airbag recalls were addressed
  4. Check for rear subframe corrosion and rearview camera recall repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
