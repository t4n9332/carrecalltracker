Buying a used 2021 Honda Passport? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 140 owner complaints on file

Seven recalls, headlined by a critical brake failure risk recall. High-severity items: fuel pump stalling, a passenger seat sensor that can crack, a seat sensor tied to unintended airbag deployment, and rear frame rust-through. Side mirror glass and rearview camera are the minor ones. NHTSA lists 140 unverified owner complaints with ten crashes reported — the highest crash count among these Passports — led by forward collision avoidance, engine, electrical system and power train.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the brake recall repair was completed by VIN
  3. Test braking feel and pedal travel on the drive
  4. Ask about false automatic-braking or collision alerts
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
