Buying a used 2020 Honda Ridgeline? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 168 owner complaints on file

Nine recalls apply to this model year, covering a brake master cylinder that may separate, fuel pump failure, a passenger seat sensor that may crack, a rearview camera that may not display, front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally, rear subframe corrosion, a tonneau cover panel that may separate, hood latch, and side mirror glass that may fall off. NHTSA has recorded 168 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 1 crash and 1 injury reported. Top complaint categories are electrical system (48), unknown or other (41), engine (37), and forward collision avoidance (14).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the rear subframe corrosion recall has been inspected or repaired
  3. Confirm the rearview camera and passenger seat sensor recalls were addressed
  4. Check electrical system complaint history with a pre-purchase inspection
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
