Buying a used 2019 Honda Ridgeline? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 233 owner complaints on file

The 2019 is the heaviest Ridgeline year here, with two critical recalls: a fuel pump that can crack and leak gas, and engines that can seize from a faulty bearing. Also listed are airbags that may not deploy in a crash, a timing belt that can lose teeth, two more fuel pump actions, a hood that can fly open and rear frame rust. Fittingly, fuel system and gasoline top the 233 unverified complaints at 39 each, with 2 fires reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get written confirmation the engine bearing and fuel pump recalls were completed.
  3. Ask about the timing belt recall and any history of rough running or stalling.
  4. Confirm no airbag warning light, and inspect the rear frame and hood latch.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
