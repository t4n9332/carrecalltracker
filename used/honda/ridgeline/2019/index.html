Buying a used 2019 Honda Ridgeline? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 233 owner complaints on file

This model year carries ten recalls, including a critical engine bearing wear/seize issue and several fuel pump recalls (crack/fire risk, stall risk), plus airbag-related recalls (deployment failure, trim clip defect, unintended front passenger airbag deployment), a timing belt recall, hood latch, and tonneau cover panel recalls. NHTSA has logged 233 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 8 crashes, 2 fires, and 9 injuries. Complaint areas cluster in fuel system (39), gasoline (39), power train (36), and engine (35).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine bearing recall has been completed
  3. Ask for records showing the fuel pump recalls were repaired
  4. Verify airbag-related recalls (deployment failure, trim clip, front passenger airbag) are closed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
