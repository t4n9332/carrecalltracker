Buying a used 2023 Honda Ridgeline? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 126 owner complaints on file

Five recalls apply, covering a brake master cylinder that may separate, a steering gearbox that can loosen, a rearview camera that may not display, front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally, and rear subframe corrosion. NHTSA has recorded 126 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 2 crashes and 2 fires reported, and no injuries listed. Top complaint areas are exterior lighting (39), engine (20), unknown or other (19), and electrical system (17).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the steering gearbox recall was completed
  3. Confirm the rear subframe corrosion recall inspection
  4. Ask about exterior lighting complaint patterns and any fixes
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
