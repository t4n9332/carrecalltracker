Buying a used 2020 Honda Pilot? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 485 owner complaints on file

The 2020 carries one critical recall — tires that can fail suddenly — alongside missing frame welds, a stalling fuel pump, two passenger seat sensor airbag recalls and rear frame rust. What stands out is the screen cluster: four separate medium recalls cover a blank dashboard, backup camera failure and side mirror glass. That lines up with the 485 unverified owner complaints, where electrical system was the largest area at 210. No stop-driving or park-outside warnings.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Test the backup camera and dashboard display repeatedly during the test drive.
  3. Confirm the tire recall and frame weld inspection were performed on this VIN.
  4. Check the passenger seat airbag warning light and inspect the rear frame for rust.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
