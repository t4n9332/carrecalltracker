Buying a used 2022 Honda Pilot? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 152 owner complaints on file

The 2022 has the shortest recall list of these Pilot years: a critical brake failure risk shared with Civic and Acura MDX, two passenger seat sensor recalls tied to airbag risk, rear frame rust, and a rearview camera failure. Complaint volume is also lower, at 152 unverified reports with no fires and 7 crashes alleged. Notably, forward collision avoidance is the top complaint area at 41, ahead of electrical and engine. No stop-driving warnings.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the brake failure recall was performed on this specific VIN.
  3. Watch for airbag or seat sensor warning lights with a passenger seated.
  4. Ask the seller about false collision warnings or unexpected automatic braking.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
