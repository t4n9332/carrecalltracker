Buying a used 2025 Honda Pilot? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 197 owner complaints on file

For 2025 the steering lock-up recall is not among those listed; what remains is a critical fuel pipe that can leak gas, a software glitch that can stall the engine, a brake pedal that can shift out of position, and a third-row seat belt that can get trapped behind a side panel. Unverified complaints total 197 — more than 2023 or 2024 — led by forward collision avoidance, electrical, steering and service brakes, with 11 crashes alleged.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pipe recall was completed and check for fuel odor underhood.
  3. Pull out each third-row belt fully to confirm it isn't caught behind the side panel.
  4. Ask about braking feel, false collision warnings, and any stall or software updates.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
