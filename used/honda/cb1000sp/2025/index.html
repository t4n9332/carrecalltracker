Buying a used 2025 Honda Cb1000sp? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file

This motorcycle carries a critical recall for a fuel hose that may come loose, plus high-severity recalls for a shift lever bolt that can fall out and an engine that may seize or leak oil. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings, but all three affect basic riding safety. Only three unverified NHTSA complaints are on file — no crashes or fires, one injury — touching engine, power train and suspension, too few to show a pattern.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all three recalls were completed and inspect the fuel hose connection
  3. Check the shift lever bolt is present and torqued
  4. Look for oil leaks or seepage and ask about oil consumption and service history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
