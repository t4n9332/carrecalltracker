Buying a used 2024 Honda Civic Hatchback? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 131 owner complaints on file

This model year has two recalls: a high-severity loose driver's seat frame issue and a medium-severity steering gearbox recall that can cause difficulty steering. NHTSA has logged 131 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 14 crashes, 1 fire, and 12 injuries. Steering is the most-cited complaint area (67), followed by unknown/other (30), forward collision avoidance (17), and lane departure (10). Neither recall is flagged as stop-drive or park-outside.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the loose driver's seat frame recall has been completed
  3. Ask whether the steering gearbox recall was addressed
  4. Ask about any steering or forward-collision-avoidance issues on this specific car
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
