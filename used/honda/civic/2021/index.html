Buying a used 2021 Honda Civic? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 143 owner complaints on file

This model year has recalls for a brake master cylinder that may separate, a fuel pump that may fail, a passenger seat sensor that may crack, and front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally. NHTSA has 143 owner complaints on file (unverified reports): 5 crashes, 2 fires, 2 injuries. Steering is the top complaint area (63), followed by forward collision avoidance (33), service brakes (18), and electrical system (17). Neither recall is flagged as stop-drive or park-outside.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake master cylinder recall repair status
  3. Verify the fuel pump and airbag recalls were completed
  4. Ask about any steering or forward-collision-avoidance complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
