Buying a used 2019 Honda Civic? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 348 owner complaints on file

For 2019 the recall picture narrows to two issues: three separate fuel-pump recalls covering pumps that can fail and stall the engine, and a passenger seat sensor that can trigger unintended airbag deployment. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. NHTSA holds 348 unverified owner complaints, with steering the single largest category at 80 and fuel-related entries filling the next three slots. Reports include 23 crashes and 14 injuries, but no fires.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all three fuel-pump recalls show as completed for this VIN.
  3. Ask whether the car has ever stalled or hesitated when starting or accelerating.
  4. Check the passenger seat-sensor recall status and that the airbag indicator works.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
