Buying a used 2018 Honda Civic? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 598 owner complaints on file

The 2018 Civic carries two recall themes worth tracing on this VIN: a power-steering defect that can make the system turn the wrong way, and Honda's fuel-pump recalls covering stalling. A seat-sensor recall addresses unintended airbag deployment. Two low-severity recalls are paperwork only — a garbled certification label and missing child-seat info in the Hatchback owner's guide. Owners filed 598 unverified NHTSA complaints, steering the largest group at 224, with fuel system close behind. Reports include 26 crashes and 4 fires.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for a VIN recall check confirming the power-steering and fuel-pump remedies were completed.
  3. Test steering at low speed and at highway speed for pulling or unexpected assist.
  4. Confirm the seat-sensor recall repair and that the airbag warning light cycles normally.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
