Buying a used 2020 Honda Civic? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 224 owner complaints on file

Recalls for this model year include the fuel pump (issued multiple times), a brake master cylinder that may separate, a passenger seat sensor that may crack, and front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally. NHTSA logged 224 owner complaints (unverified reports): 15 crashes, 1 fire, 12 injuries. Electrical system and unknown/other each account for 36 complaints, followed by power train (31) and air bags (29). Neither recall is flagged as stop-drive or park-outside.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake master cylinder recall was completed
  3. Verify the passenger seat sensor and airbag recalls were addressed
  4. Ask about any electrical system or air bag complaints on this car
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
