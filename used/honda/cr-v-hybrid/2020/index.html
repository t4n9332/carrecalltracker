Buying a used 2020 Honda CR-V Hybrid? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 76 owner complaints on file

This 2020 CR-V Hybrid carries five recalls: a power loss risk, a battery cable fire risk, a fuel pump that may fail, a passenger seat sensor that may crack, and front passenger airbags that may deploy unintentionally. None require stopping use or parking outside per NHTSA records. Owners have filed 76 complaints with NHTSA, including 4 crashes and 3 injuries; the largest complaint categories are engine (19), service brakes (14), electrical system (12), and forward collision avoidance (12). These are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof the battery cable and fuel pump recalls were completed
  3. Test brakes and the forward collision/automatic braking system on a drive
  4. Check for engine warning lights or hybrid system fault codes
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
