Buying a used 2025 Honda CR-V Hybrid? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 27 owner complaints on file

Two critical fuel-leak recalls define this year — a fuel pump that can leak and a fuel hose that can leak gasoline — alongside a steering gearbox that may make steering difficult and a tire kit cap that can fly off. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Complaint totals are small at 27 unverified reports, in line with a recent model year, but they include 5 crashes, 4 injuries and 1 fire. Steering, service brakes and fuel/propulsion lead the areas.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both fuel-leak recalls — pump and hose — are closed for this VIN.
  3. Check for gasoline odor at startup and again after driving.
  4. Test steering effort and brake pedal feel during the test drive.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Honda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
