Buying a used 2018 Lexus ES350? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

Two recalls apply here, both covering the same issue: a low-pressure fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine. There is no stop-driving or park-outside advisory attached. The NHTSA complaint file is very small — 5 reports total, including 1 crash and 1 injury — spread across vehicle speed control, structure, and service brakes. Complaints are unverified owner reports, and low counts reflect voluntary filing rather than a measured failure rate.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN to confirm the fuel pump replacement was actually performed, not just mailed out.
  3. Ask the seller whether the car has ever stalled, hesitated, or thrown a check engine light.
  4. Test drive long enough to include a cold start, idle, and highway acceleration.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lexus dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
