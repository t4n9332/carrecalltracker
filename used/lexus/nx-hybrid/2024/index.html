Buying a used 2024 Lexus NX Hybrid? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

This model year has three recall entries: a steering cable that may disable the air bag, head restraints that may detach unexpectedly, and a rearview camera that may freeze. None are listed as stop-driving or park-outside. Owners have filed 2 complaints with NHTSA, described here as unverified reports, with no crashes, fires, or injuries noted; one concerns vehicle speed control and one concerns seats. Recall activity here spans airbag, seat, and camera systems.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all three recalls (steering cable, head restraints, rearview camera) have been completed
  3. Check that head restraints are securely attached
  4. Test the rearview camera display for freezing
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lexus dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
