Buying a used 2024 Lexus NX Hybrid? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

Three open recalls for this model year, none rated stop-driving or park-outside. The most serious involves steering wiring that can disable the driver's airbag; a second covers rearview cameras that may freeze, and a third, front headrests that come out too easily. NHTSA has logged just two owner complaints — unverified reports — one on vehicle speed control and one on seats, with no crashes, fires, or injuries. The record is thin, typical of a recent model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof the steering wiring/airbag recall repair was completed at a Lexus dealer
  3. Start the car and shift to reverse several times to confirm the rearview camera image appears and does not freeze
  4. Tug each front headrest to see whether it releases too easily and confirm the headrest recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lexus dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
