Buying a used 2019 Lexus RX350L? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 34 owner complaints on file

Recalls mirror the 2018 car — two filings for a fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine, neither stop-driving nor park-outside. The complaint record is heavier: 34 unverified owner reports to NHTSA, including ten crashes and two injuries. Forward collision avoidance and fuel/propulsion lead with eleven reports each, followed by service brakes at nine and engine at seven. The driver-assist and stalling clusters are what set this year apart from 2018.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump recall repair is documented on the VIN
  3. Ask whether the forward collision warning or automatic braking has ever activated unexpectedly or thrown a fault
  4. Drive long enough to check for stalling and to test the brakes repeatedly from speed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lexus dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
