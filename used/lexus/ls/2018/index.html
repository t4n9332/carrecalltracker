Buying a used 2018 Lexus LS? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 10 owner complaints on file

This model year has two recall entries: one for run-flat tires that can lose tread, and one for a passenger airbag sensor that may misread the occupant. Neither recall is listed as stop-driving or park-outside. Owners have filed 10 complaints with NHTSA, described here as unverified reports, with no crashes, fires, or injuries noted. The most cited complaint areas are unknown or other (3 reports), wheels (3 reports), electrical system (3 reports), and seats (1 report).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify both recalls (run-flat tires, airbag sensor) have been completed
  3. Inspect tire tread condition and run-flat tire age
  4. Ask about any electrical system issues reported or repaired
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lexus dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
