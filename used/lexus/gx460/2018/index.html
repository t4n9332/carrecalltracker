Buying a used 2018 Lexus GX460? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

This model year has a recall for a fuel pump that may fail and cause engine stalling, plus a low-severity recall for incorrect load capacity labels. NHTSA logged 9 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year, including 2 crashes and 3 injuries, with no reported fires; suspension and air bag items were the most-cited categories, followed by back-over prevention and electrical system reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the fuel pump recall repair has been completed
  3. Verify the load capacity label reflects the correct rating
  4. Have the suspension inspected given it's the most-cited complaint area
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lexus dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
