Buying a used 2019 Lexus LX570? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This year carries the same two recalls as the 2018 LX 570: passenger airbags that may deactivate, and the Toyota/Lexus fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine. Neither has a stop-driving or park-outside advisory. NHTSA shows just 1 owner complaint, filed under unknown/other, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. That volume is too low to signal anything either way; complaints are voluntary, unverified reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN through a recall lookup to confirm both the airbag and fuel pump remedies were performed.
  3. Verify the passenger airbag indicator behaves correctly when the seat is occupied and empty.
  4. Ask about any stalling or no-start incidents and request dealer service history.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lexus dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
