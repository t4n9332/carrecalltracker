Buying a used 2018 Lexus LX570? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

This model year has two recall entries: one addressing airbag deactivation and one covering a fuel pump that may fail and stall the engine. Neither is listed as stop-driving or park-outside. Owners have filed 4 complaints with NHTSA, described here as unverified reports, with no crashes, fires, or injuries noted. The most cited complaint areas are air bags (2 reports), unknown or other (1 report), service brakes (1 report), and seat belts (1 report).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the airbag deactivation recall has been addressed
  3. Confirm the fuel pump recall has been completed
  4. Ask about any airbag warning lights or prior airbag-related repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Lexus dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
