Buying a used 2022 RAM 5500? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 26 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

This 2022 Ram 5500 has four recalls: two fire-risk campaigns covering diesel-truck relay and heater-relay components, an engine-computer short-circuit recall, and a driver's air bag non-deployment recall. NHTSA has logged 26 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, with 1 crash and no fires or injuries reported. The top complaint categories are power train (21), steering (3), wheels (1), and electrical system (1), with power train accounting for most of the complaints filed.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the relay/heater-relay fire recalls were completed
  3. Verify the engine computer short-circuit recall was performed
  4. Ask about any power train issues, the top complaint category
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
