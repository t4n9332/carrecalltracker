Buying a used 2018 RAM 5500? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 37 owner complaints on file

Brakes dominate this year. Recalls cover hoses that can corrode and leak plus anti-lock brake and stability control failure, and 24 of the 37 owner complaints filed with NHTSA name service brakes, with 12 more citing hydraulics — all unverified reports. Other campaigns: unexpected shift out of Park, cruise control that can stick on, breakable wheel studs, faulty Kidde fire extinguishers, and two brake-light recalls tied to upfitted fire and utility bodies. One crash reported, no fires or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Have rear brake hoses and hydraulic lines inspected for corrosion and leaks
  3. Ask whether it carries an upfitted body covered by the brake-light recalls
  4. Confirm it holds in Park on a slope and check wheel stud and lug service history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
