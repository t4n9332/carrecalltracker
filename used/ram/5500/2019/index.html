Buying a used 2019 RAM 5500? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 12 owner complaints on file

Five critical recalls, with fuel and fire running through them: diesel fuel lines that may leak and catch fire, engine damage with fire risk, a low-oil warning that may be missed, and stalling without warning. Rear brake hoses that can burst plus three further hose or line wear campaigns cover the brakes. Of 12 owner complaints filed with NHTSA — unverified reports — most cite fuel system, diesel, or propulsion, and among them one fire and two injuries are reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Inspect diesel fuel lines for leaks or fuel odor and confirm that recall was done
  3. Get repair orders for the engine damage and low-oil warning recalls
  4. Ask about any stalling and have all rear brake hoses and lines checked for wear
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
