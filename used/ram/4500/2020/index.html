Buying a used 2020 RAM 4500? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

This 2020 Ram 4500 carries three critical recalls: engine damage that can cause fire, a low oil warning software problem, and diesel stalling without warning, plus multiple high-severity brake line and hose wear recalls, a wheel stud recall, and a medium-severity wiper arm recall. None require stopping the vehicle or parking outside. NHTSA has logged only 4 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, spread across visibility (2), unknown/other (1), fuel system (1), and diesel (1), with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm engine damage, oil warning, and diesel stall recalls are completed
  3. Inspect rear brake lines/hoses for wear or leaks
  4. Verify wheel stud recall repair
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
