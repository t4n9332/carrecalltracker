Buying a used 2022 RAM 4500? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

This 2022 Ram 4500 has two critical recalls for diesel-related relay fire risk (a heater relay and a separate relay), both of which advise parking outside due to fire risk, plus high-severity recalls for an engine computer that may short-circuit and a driver's airbag that may not deploy. NHTSA has logged 3 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, all categorized under power train, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether both diesel relay fire-risk recalls are completed and confirm outdoor parking is no longer advised
  3. Verify engine computer short-circuit recall repair
  4. Confirm driver's airbag recall repair status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
