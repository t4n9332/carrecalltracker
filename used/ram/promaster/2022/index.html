Buying a used 2022 RAM Promaster? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 63 owner complaints on file

Eight recalls, four of them about the rearview camera alone: wrong camera type shipped, blank display, and image that may not appear. The critical item remains radiator fan wiring that can overheat and catch fire, alongside vans missing a side door beam and a driver seat belt warning that may not work. Of 63 unverified owner complaints, steering leads with 18 and forward collision avoidance follows with 15, areas no recall here addresses. One fire and one crash are cited.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Drive it at highway speed and note any steering wander, pull or false collision warnings
  3. Confirm all camera-related recalls were completed and the correct camera is installed
  4. Verify by VIN the side door beam and fan wiring recall repairs, and test the seat belt chime
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
