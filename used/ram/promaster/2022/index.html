Buying a used 2022 RAM Promaster? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 63 owner complaints on file

This 2022 ProMaster carries eight recalls, including two separate campaigns for a rearview camera that may not display, plus a missing side door safety beam, a radiator fan circuit that may overheat, and a wheelchair restraint lock issue. Medium-severity recalls cover a wrong camera type, a seat belt warning that may not work, and a left taillight/turn signal failure. NHTSA logged 63 owner complaints (unverified reports), with steering (18) and forward collision avoidance (15) the most-cited areas, plus 1 crash and 1 fire reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both rearview-camera recalls (display failure and wrong camera type) have been completed
  3. Ask about the radiator fan circuit recall and any overheating history
  4. Verify the side door safety beam and taillight/turn signal recalls were addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
