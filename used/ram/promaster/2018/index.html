Buying a used 2018 RAM Promaster? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 37 owner complaints on file

This is the heaviest recall year in the range: ten actions, including two critical fire risks, an engine cooling fan that can overheat and burn and radiator fan wiring that can overheat and catch fire. Others cover a cracking door-mounted side airbag sensor, engine computer stalling, shifter cables separating from the transmission, foldaway seats that may unlatch, and a reversed backup camera image. Of 37 unverified owner complaints, four mention crashes; electrical and engine lead, with powertrain close behind.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify by VIN that both radiator/cooling fan fire recalls were completed, not just scheduled
  3. Shift through every gear and confirm the shifter cable recall repair was done
  4. Fold and latch the rear seats, and check for stalling history and door airbag sensor faults
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
