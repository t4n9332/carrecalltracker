Buying a used 2019 RAM Promaster? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 58 owner complaints on file

This 2019 Ram ProMaster has nine recalls, including high-severity campaigns for a shifter cable that may cause unintended movement, seat belts that may not secure occupants, a park gear that may not engage, a radiator fan circuit that may overheat, and wheelchair restraints that may not lock, plus medium-severity seat lock, seat belt, and backup camera recalls. NHTSA has logged 58 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 2 crashes; top categories are power train (24), engine (10), unknown or other (8), and engine and engine cooling (6).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the park gear recall was completed
  3. Confirm the shifter cable recall was repaired
  4. Ask about power train/engine issues, the top complaint categories
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
