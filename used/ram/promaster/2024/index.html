Buying a used 2024 RAM Promaster? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 12 owner complaints on file

Five recalls. The critical one is the long-running radiator fan wiring that can overheat and catch fire. Two are build-specific: vans missing a side door beam, and a wheelchair restraint issue limited to Rollx conversions. Electric ProMaster vans have a separate recall for losing drive power, so which powertrain this van has matters. Only 12 owner complaints are on file, spread thinly across forward collision avoidance, electrical and engine, with one citing a fire. These reports are unverified.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm whether this is a gas or electric van, and if electric, that the drive power recall was done
  3. Have the VIN checked for the fan wiring and side door beam recalls
  4. Test the backup camera display and any forward collision warning behavior on a drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
