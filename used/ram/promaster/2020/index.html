Buying a used 2020 RAM Promaster? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 42 owner complaints on file

This 2020 Ram ProMaster has five recalls: a side air bag that may not inflate, a park gear that may not engage, a radiator fan circuit that may overheat, wheelchair restraints that may not lock, and a backup camera that may fail. NHTSA has logged 42 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 3 crashes and 2 injuries; top categories are back over prevention (11), power train (10), unknown or other (9), and electrical system (8).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the park gear recall was repaired
  3. Confirm the side air bag recall was addressed
  4. Ask about backup camera function given the back-over-prevention complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
