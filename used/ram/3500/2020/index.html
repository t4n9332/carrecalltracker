Buying a used 2020 RAM 3500? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 116 owner complaints on file

Recalls here concentrate on fire risk: two separate transmission fluid leak campaigns, diesel engine damage, a missed low-oil warning, and stalling without warning. Wheel studs, wiper arms, a stability control warning light, a tailgate that may open while driving and an accessory bed step round it out. No stop-driving or park-outside orders were issued. NHTSA received 116 unverified owner complaints — seven fires, six crashes, four injuries — mostly fuel, propulsion and engine.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both transmission fluid leak recalls were closed, not just one
  3. Look under the truck for fluid residue or heat marks near the transmission and exhaust
  4. Test the tailgate latch and check whether the accessory bed step recall applies
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
