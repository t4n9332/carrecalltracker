Buying a used 2019 RAM 3500? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 134 owner complaints on file

This model year carries five critical recalls: a diesel fuel leak fire risk, a transmission fire risk, an engine damage fire risk issued jointly for 3500, 4500, and 5500 trucks, a low oil warning software problem, and a diesel stall-without-warning issue, plus wheel stud and side airbag inflator recalls. NHTSA has 134 owner complaints on file (unverified reports) for this year, including 10 crashes, 5 fires, and 8 injuries, with fuel system, diesel, fuel/propulsion, and engine named most often.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel leak, transmission fire, and engine damage fire recalls were completed
  3. Ask whether the low oil warning software update was applied
  4. Check the wheel stud and side airbag inflator recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
