Buying a used 2022 RAM 3500? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 189 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

Two park-outside fire recalls carry over — the diesel heater relay and engine fire risk — alongside a transmission fluid leak, loss of engine power, a driver airbag that may not deploy, and several display-related campaigns for backup camera, tailgate and stability control light. What stands out is complaint volume: 189 unverified NHTSA reports, 114 of them power train, far above neighboring years. Those reports include four crashes, five fires and seven injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask specifically about transmission or driveline behavior, given the heavy power train complaint volume
  3. Confirm the heater relay and engine fire recalls were completed
  4. Check that the backup camera image appears reliably every time reverse is selected
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
