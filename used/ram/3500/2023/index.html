Buying a used 2023 RAM 3500? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 17 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year has one critical recall, a diesel heater relay fire risk carrying a park-outside advisory, plus high-severity recalls for an airbag that may not deploy and a transmission leak that can cut power. Medium recalls cover a rearview camera that may not show an image and light controls that may swap turn signals and high beams. NHTSA has 17 owner complaints on file (unverified reports) for this year, with electrical system, engine, exterior lighting, and seat belts each named a few times.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the diesel heater relay fire-risk recall has been completed
  3. Ask whether the transmission leak recall repair was done
  4. Test the rearview camera and turn signal/high beam controls
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
