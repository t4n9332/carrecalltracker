Buying a used 2021 RAM 3500? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 62 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

This model year has four critical recalls, including two diesel relay fire risks for which the notice advises parking outside, plus a separate diesel fire risk recall and a transmission fluid leak recall. High-severity recalls cover breakable wheel studs, a side airbag curtain defect, and a driver's airbag that may not deploy. NHTSA has 62 owner complaints on file (unverified reports) for this year, including 0 crashes, 4 fires, and 0 injuries, with electrical system, powertrain, engine, and an unknown/other category named most often.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both diesel relay fire-risk recalls, including the heater relay, have been completed
  3. Ask whether the vehicle was ever subject to the park-outside advisory under those recalls
  4. Verify the wheel stud and airbag recalls were addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
