Buying a used 2021 RAM 3500? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 62 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

Fire-related recalls dominate, and two of them — a diesel heater relay and an engine fire risk — came with instructions to park the truck outside until repaired. A transmission fluid leak, breakable wheel studs, a driver airbag that may not deploy and side curtain airbags that can deploy improperly follow. Complaint volume is comparatively low at 62 unverified NHTSA reports, with no crashes or injuries but four fires, clustered in electrical and power train areas.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the heater grid relay and engine fire recalls are repaired, since both required parking outside
  3. Ask whether either airbag recall (driver deployment, curtain deployment) has been performed
  4. Ask about any electrical or power train issues, the top owner-reported areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
