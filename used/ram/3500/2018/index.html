Buying a used 2018 RAM 3500? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 798 owner complaints on file

The critical items mirror the 2500: a steering link that can come apart, an added break risk on trucks with an AEV lift kit, and side airbags that can burst apart. Unique to the 3500 is a recall for wheel studs that may break; anti-lock brakes and stability control can also fail. Owner complaints total 798 unverified reports and are overwhelmingly about service brakes — 660 mentions — with hydraulic and electrical next, and 15 crashes.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask about brake service history and test hard stops — service brakes dominate this year's complaints.
  3. Confirm the wheel stud recall was performed and have the studs and lug torque inspected.
  4. Verify the steering link recall is closed and ask whether an AEV lift kit was ever fitted.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
