Buying a used 2020 RAM Promaster City? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Two recalls cover this year, and they sit at opposite ends. One is critical: the van can lose braking with no warning beforehand. The other is minor, a missing warning light for turn signal failure. NHTSA lists only one owner complaint for the 2020 ProMaster City, filed under structure, with no crashes, fires or injuries. Complaints are unverified owner reports, and a tally of one reflects a low-volume model more than proven reliability.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Have a Ram dealer run the VIN and confirm the brake-loss recall repair was completed
  3. Test all four turn signals and check the dash warns when one fails
  4. Ask the seller about any brake pedal, warning-light or stopping-distance issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
