Buying a used 2021 RAM Promaster City? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

This 2021 Ram ProMaster City carries three recalls: a critical campaign for vans that may lose braking power, a recall for turn signals that may not warn properly, and a recall for seat belts that may not securely hold child seats. NHTSA has logged 5 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported; the top categories are engine (3), exterior lighting (1), seat belts (1), and equipment (1).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the braking-power-loss recall was repaired
  3. Verify the turn signal recall was completed
  4. Ask about the seat belt/child seat recall and the engine complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
