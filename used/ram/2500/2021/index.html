Buying a used 2021 RAM 2500? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 107 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

This model year has four critical recalls, including two diesel relay fire risks for which the recall notice advises parking outside rather than in a garage, plus a separate diesel fire risk recall and a transmission fluid leak recall. High-severity recalls cover a side airbag curtain defect and a driver's airbag that may not deploy. NHTSA has 107 owner complaints on file (unverified reports) for this year, including 6 crashes, 8 fires, and 2 injuries, with electrical system, engine, powertrain, and structure named most often.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both diesel relay fire-risk recalls, including the heater relay, have been completed
  3. Ask whether the vehicle was ever subject to the park-outside advisory under those recalls
  4. Verify the airbag curtain and driver airbag recalls were addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
