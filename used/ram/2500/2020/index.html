Buying a used 2020 RAM 2500? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 162 owner complaints on file

This model year has three critical recalls: a transmission fire risk, a diesel stall-without-warning issue, and a transmission fluid leak, along with a side airbag inflator rupture recall. Medium recalls include an accessory bed step, a backup camera image that stays on screen, loose driver mirror glass, wiper arms, a stability warning light, and tailgates that may open while driving. NHTSA lists 162 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year, including 5 crashes, 4 fires, and 4 injuries, with fuel/propulsion, engine, fuel system, and diesel categories named most often.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the transmission fire and fluid leak recalls have been completed
  3. Ask about the diesel stall recall repair status
  4. Check the tailgate latch and mirror glass for the medium recalls
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
