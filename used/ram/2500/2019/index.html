Buying a used 2019 RAM 2500? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 193 owner complaints on file

Fire and stalling drive the 2019 list: diesel fuel lines that may leak and ignite, transmission fluid that can leak and burn, and heavy-duty diesels that can stall without warning — all critical, though none required parking outside. Brake calipers that may overheat and several airbag inflator recalls follow. Complaint volume is low at 193 unverified reports, but nine of them describe fires, and fuel/propulsion and engine are the leading areas cited.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Look underneath for fuel or transmission fluid leaks and any scorching near hot exhaust components.
  3. Confirm the diesel fuel line and stalling recalls were completed; ask about any unexplained shutdowns.
  4. Check that the tailgate latches securely and the wiper arms are tight — both are recall items.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
