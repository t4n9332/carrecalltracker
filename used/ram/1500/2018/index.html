Buying a used 2018 RAM 1500? Check these 15 recalls first

15 NHTSA recalls · 660 owner complaints on file

This model year has 10 recalls, one critical for a diesel EGR cooler fire risk. High-severity recalls include a shift interlock that may allow rollaway, a wrong park lock rod shared across Chrysler Group models, cruise control that may not cancel, a backup camera that may fail, a rear driveshaft that may crack or fracture, a tailgate that may open while driving, and a diesel stall risk. NHTSA has 660 unverified owner complaints (25 crashes, 8 fires, 25 injuries), led by electrical system (159) and engine (110).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. If diesel-equipped, confirm the EGR cooler fire-risk recall is completed
  3. Test the shift interlock, park lock, and tailgate latch
  4. Check the backup camera and cruise control cancel function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
