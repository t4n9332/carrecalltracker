Buying a used 2018 RAM 1500? Check these 15 recalls first

15 NHTSA recalls · 660 owner complaints on file

This year leans hard on powertrain and fire risk: the EcoDiesel EGR cooler can crack and catch fire, and side airbag inflators can burst apart — both critical, neither with a stop-driving order. Several high-severity recalls cover trucks shifting out of Park, cruise control sticking on, engine-computer stalling, a rear axle lacking oil and a driveshaft that can crack. Owners filed 660 unverified NHTSA complaints, including 8 fires and 25 crashes, concentrated in electrical, engine and steering.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. If it's the 3.0L EcoDiesel, ask for the repair order proving the EGR cooler recall was performed.
  3. Confirm both 'may not stay in Park' recalls were completed, and test that it holds on an incline.
  4. Have the rear axle checked for oil level and leaks, and the rear driveshaft inspected for cracks.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
