Buying a used 2021 RAM 1500? Check these 15 recalls first

15 NHTSA recalls · 677 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

One recall here carries a do-not-drive instruction: 2021 Ram 1500 Classic trucks missing a brake pedal retaining clip. The rest are high or medium severity — driver airbag connection and deployment faults, second-row belt anchors, stability control that can switch off, an eTorque software glitch that can stall the engine, a diesel fuel pump failure, and two backup camera recalls. Among 677 unverified owner complaints, engine leads with 276 mentions ahead of electrical; 27 crashes were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. If it's a 1500 Classic, verify the do-not-drive brake pedal clip recall was done before test driving.
  3. Ask whether the eTorque stall and stability-control recalls were completed, and note any stalling on the drive.
  4. Check the backup camera image appears every time and the airbag warning light cycles correctly.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
