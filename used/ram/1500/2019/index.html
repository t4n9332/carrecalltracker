Buying a used 2019 RAM 1500? Check these 30 recalls first

30 NHTSA recalls · 1,472 owner complaints on file

This model year has 10 recalls, five critical: brake pedal separation, auxiliary battery wiring that may overheat and catch fire, a steering wheel detachment risk (shared with the Jeep Wrangler), a bed step kit fuel leak, and a diesel EGR cooler fire risk. High-severity recalls include cruise control that may not cancel, a windshield defroster that doesn't work, and a loose fastener that can cut power steering. NHTSA has 1,472 unverified owner complaints (75 crashes, 11 fires, 63 injuries), led by electrical system (379) and steering (336).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake pedal and steering wheel detachment recalls are completed
  3. If a bed step kit is installed, check for fuel leaks
  4. Test power steering assist and windshield defroster function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
