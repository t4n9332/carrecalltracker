Buying a used 2019 RAM 1500? Check these 30 recalls first

30 NHTSA recalls · 1,472 owner complaints on file

2019 carries the heaviest list of these Ram 1500 years, with five critical items: a brake pedal that can separate from its assembly, a steering wheel that may detach, an auxiliary battery connection that can overheat, a fuel leak tied to a Mopar rear step kit, and the EcoDiesel EGR cooler fire risk. Complaint volume is also highest here — 1,472 unverified owner reports covering 75 crashes and 63 injuries — clustered in electrical and steering.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get a VIN recall check confirming both the steering wheel and brake pedal recalls were completed.
  3. Ask whether a Mopar rear step kit was ever installed, and check for fuel smell or leaks.
  4. Test the defroster and defogger, and watch for power-steering faults on a cold start and test drive.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any RAM dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
