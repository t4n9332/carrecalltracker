Buying a used 2019 Mazda CX-9? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 53 owner complaints on file

Two high-severity recalls apply: tires that may lose their tread, and the fuel pump failure that can stall the engine. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. The tire recall is worth tracing carefully, since tires get replaced over a used vehicle's life and the original set may be long gone. Owner activity is lighter than the 2018's, 53 unverified NHTSA complaints, led by engine at 23 and electrical system at 7, with one crash and no fires or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask what tires are on the car now and whether the recalled tires were ever replaced under the recall
  3. Confirm the fuel pump recall was completed by running the VIN at a Mazda dealer
  4. Inspect all four tires for tread condition, matching brand and date codes
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mazda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
