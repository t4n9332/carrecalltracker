Buying a used 2019 Mazda CX-9? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 53 owner complaints on file

This 2019 CX-9 has two recalls: tires that may lose tread while driving, and a fuel pump that may fail and stall the engine. Neither notice requires stopping driving or parking outside. NHTSA has logged 53 owner complaints for this model year, including 1 crash and no reported fires or injuries; these are unverified owner reports. Engine (23) is the most cited category, followed by unknown or other (14), electrical system (7), and power train (5).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the tire tread recall has been completed and inspect tire condition
  3. Confirm the fuel pump recall has been completed
  4. Ask about engine performance history and any stalling incidents
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mazda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
