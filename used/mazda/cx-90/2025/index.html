Buying a used 2025 Mazda CX-90? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 29 owner complaints on file

This 2025 CX-90 has two recalls: an electrical unit software issue and a fuel gauge that may read incorrectly. Neither notice requires stopping driving or parking outside. NHTSA has logged 28 owner complaints for this model year, including 1 crash and no reported fires or injuries; these are unverified owner reports. Service brakes (7), engine (6), unknown or other (6), and forward collision avoidance (5) are the most cited complaint categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the electrical unit software recall has been completed
  3. Confirm the fuel gauge recall has been completed
  4. Ask about any brake or forward collision system issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mazda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
