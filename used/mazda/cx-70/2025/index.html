Buying a used 2025 Mazda CX-70? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 50 owner complaints on file

This 2025 CX-70 has four recalls: control module software and hybrid inverter software that may each cut power, an electrical unit software issue, and a fuel gauge that may read incorrectly. None of the notices require stopping driving or parking outside. NHTSA has logged 50 owner complaints for this model year, including 2 crashes, no fires, and 4 injuries reported; these are unverified owner reports. Lane departure (19) is the most cited category, followed by forward collision avoidance (9), power train (7), and engine (5).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the control module and hybrid inverter software recalls have been completed
  3. Confirm the electrical unit software and fuel gauge recalls have been completed
  4. Ask about any lane departure or forward collision system issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mazda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
