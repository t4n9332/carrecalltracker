Buying a used 2024 Mazda CX-30? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 31 owner complaints on file

This 2024 CX-30 has two recalls on file: a camera fault that can disable safety systems, and an air bag issue where air bags may not deploy. Neither notice requires stopping driving or parking outside. NHTSA has logged 31 owner complaints for this model year, including 2 crashes, 1 fire, and 3 injuries reported; these are unverified owner reports. Forward collision avoidance (9), air bags (7), service brakes (6), and electrical system (6) are the most cited complaint categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the camera-fault safety-system recall has been completed
  3. Confirm the air bag deployment recall has been completed
  4. Ask about any forward collision avoidance or brake system issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mazda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
