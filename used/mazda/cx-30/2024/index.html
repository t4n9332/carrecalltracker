Buying a used 2024 Mazda CX-30? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 31 owner complaints on file

Two recalls, both safety-system related: airbags that can switch themselves off, and a camera fault that disables automatic emergency braking. Neither is rated critical. The complaint side echoes them — of 31 unverified owner reports to NHTSA, forward collision avoidance and air bags are the two largest categories, followed by service brakes and electrical. Those filings include two crashes, one fire and three injuries, and are unconfirmed reports rather than confirmed defects.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the airbag recall was completed and that the passenger airbag status light behaves normally with someone seated
  3. Ask whether the camera software update for automatic emergency braking was applied
  4. Watch for driver-assist or airbag warning lights during a longer test drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mazda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
