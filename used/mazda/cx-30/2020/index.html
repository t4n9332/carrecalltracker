Buying a used 2020 Mazda CX-30? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 33 owner complaints on file

The launch year has three recalls: front brake components that can loosen, a fuel leak affecting all-wheel-drive versions, and a power liftgate that can lower on its own. Two of the three are rated high. NHTSA has 33 unverified owner complaints, one involving a crash and none reporting fire or injury; service brakes, electrical and fuel or propulsion are the most common categories, which overlaps with the brake and fuel recalls.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if the front brake recall was done and have the front brakes inspected for loose hardware
  3. If it is all-wheel drive, confirm the fuel leak recall repair and check for fuel odor
  4. Open and close the power liftgate several times and confirm it holds position
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mazda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
