Buying a used 2018 Mazda MX-5? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file

This model year has three recalls: a transmission that may downshift unexpectedly, a fuel pump that may fail and stall the engine, and air bags that may deploy with too much force. NHTSA has logged only 3 owner complaints (unverified reports), with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. The complaints are spread across power train, electrical system, vehicle speed control, and engine, one each, without any single category standing out.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if the transmission downshift recall was completed
  3. Verify fuel pump recall service history
  4. Check airbag recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mazda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
