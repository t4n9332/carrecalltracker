Buying a used 2018 Mazda MX-5? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file

Three recalls, none flagged stop-driving or park-outside: a transmission condition where the car can downshift and slow suddenly, a fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine, and a medium-severity airbag recall for deployment that may be too forceful. The complaint side is thin — just 3 unverified owner reports filed with NHTSA, spread one each across power train, electrical, vehicle speed control and engine, with no crashes, fires or injuries listed.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the fuel pump recall was performed, not just mailed
  3. Ask whether the sudden-downshift recall fix was applied
  4. Confirm the airbag deployment-force recall is closed by VIN
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mazda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
