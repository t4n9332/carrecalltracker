Buying a used 2024 Mazda MAZDA3? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 12 owner complaints on file

Three recalls on a recent model year, one high severity: airbags on the Mazda3 and CX-30 that may shut themselves off. Two medium items are camera related, camera views that may not display, and a camera fault that disables automatic braking. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Owner data is thin at this age, 12 unverified NHTSA complaints, led by electrical system at 5, with 2 crashes and 1 injury reported. That small a sample says little either way.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the airbag shutoff recall was completed and watch the airbag status indicators at startup
  3. Check that every camera view displays correctly, front, rear and any surround view
  4. Ask whether any warning has appeared saying the automatic braking system was unavailable
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mazda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
