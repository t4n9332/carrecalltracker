Buying a used 2024 Mazda MAZDA3? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 12 owner complaints on file

This model year has three recalls: a camera fault that may disable safety systems, air bags that may not deploy, and a backup camera display that may go blank. NHTSA has logged 12 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 2 crashes and 1 injury, no fires. Electrical system complaints lead at 5, followed by unknown/other at 4, with forward collision avoidance and structure complaints at 2 each — the electrical and camera-related complaints correspond to the camera and display recalls.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the air bag non-deployment recall was completed
  3. Check that the camera-based safety systems and backup camera work
  4. Ask about any electrical system issues reported
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mazda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
