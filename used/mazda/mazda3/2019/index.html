Buying a used 2019 Mazda MAZDA3? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 74 owner complaints on file

This model year carries six recalls: lug nuts that may loosen and let a wheel detach, engines that may stall unexpectedly, automatic braking that may activate on its own, incorrect airbag/seat belt warning lights, front head restraints that may not lock, and a rearview mirror that may detach. NHTSA has 74 owner complaints on file (unverified reports), with 3 crashes and 1 injury, no fires. Service brakes and unknown/other categories each show 15 complaints, followed by engine and power train at 11 each, aligning with the stall and braking recalls.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the lug nut/wheel recall was performed
  3. Test for stalling and unexpected automatic braking
  4. Check head restraints lock and rearview mirror is secure
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mazda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
