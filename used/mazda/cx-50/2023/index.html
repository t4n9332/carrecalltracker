Buying a used 2023 Mazda CX-50? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 63 owner complaints on file

This 2023 CX-50 has two recalls: a brake control unit that may fail, and a trailer hitch wiring issue that may cause the wiring to detach. Neither notice requires stopping driving or parking outside. NHTSA has logged 63 owner complaints for this model year, including 9 crashes, no fires, and 3 injuries reported; these are unverified owner reports. Service brakes (16), forward collision avoidance (14), electrical system (9), and unknown or other (8) are the most cited complaint categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake control unit recall has been completed
  3. Ask whether a trailer hitch was installed and if the wiring recall was addressed
  4. Ask about any brake system repairs or complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mazda dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
