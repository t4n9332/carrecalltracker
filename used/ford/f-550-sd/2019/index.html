Buying a used 2019 Ford F-550 SD? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

The 2019 F-550 Super Duty has two critical fire-risk recalls — one involving a heater cable, one involving seat belts — plus medium recalls covering rearview camera display problems. NHTSA has 4 owner complaints filed for this model year, two of which reported fires; these are unverified reports. Complaint mentions include engine (2), with fuel/propulsion system and tires each mentioned once. Given the fire-related recalls and complaint history, confirming recall completion is a specific, concrete step.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify both the heater cable and seat belt fire-risk recalls are completed
  3. Ask directly whether the truck has ever experienced a fire or burning smell
  4. Check rearview camera display for distortion or dropout
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
