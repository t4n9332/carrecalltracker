Buying a used 2020 Ford F-550 SD? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

The 2020 F-550 has several high-severity recalls: risk of engine stall, a windshield that may detach in a crash, transmission debris that can block Park, and a fuel pump that may fail again after a prior repair. NHTSA has 4 owner complaints for this model year, including one that reported a crash; these are unverified reports. Fuel/propulsion system accounts for 3 of the complaints, with power train, fuel system, and diesel categories mentioned as well.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump recall, including any prior repair, has been completed
  3. Verify the transmission reliably holds Park and the windshield is properly seated
  4. Ask the owner about the fuel-system complaint and the reported crash
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
