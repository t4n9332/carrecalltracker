Buying a used 2023 Ford F-550 SD? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

Nine recalls, including a stop-driving notice for front wheels that can detach and a high-severity one for a driver airbag that may be disconnected. Others cover fuel pump failure that can stall the engine, trailer brakes and lights, an instrument display that can go dark, power windows that may not auto-reverse, rearview cameras that freeze or blank, and breakable wiper arms. Just one owner complaint is filed with NHTSA — an unverified report about exterior lighting.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the front wheel and driver airbag recalls were both performed
  3. Watch for an airbag warning light and a fully lit instrument cluster at startup
  4. Test the power windows for auto-reverse and the rearview camera for freezing
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
