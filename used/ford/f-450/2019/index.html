Buying a used 2019 Ford F-450? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 19 owner complaints on file

Four recalls, led by a block heater cord that can catch fire and front seat backs Ford flagged as potentially weak; neither was issued with a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Two smaller ones cover a reversed backup camera image on LGE Coachworks-converted trucks and rear lights that shine too brightly. NHTSA has 19 owner complaints on file — unverified reports — concentrated in steering (8) and suspension (5), with one injury and no crashes or fires reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get repair orders showing the block heater cord recall was completed
  3. Confirm the seat back remedy was performed and ask if it is an LGE Coachworks conversion
  4. Test-drive for steering and suspension symptoms, the two largest complaint areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
