Buying a used 2023 Ford Maverick? Check these 21 recalls first

21 NHTSA recalls · 264 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

This model year has ten open recalls, including four critical ones: two separate engine fire risk campaigns, a block heater fire risk, and a front suspension detachment recall that carries a stop-driving order. High-severity recalls cover tires that may cause handling loss, three separate battery detection issues, and two recalls for shifting into neutral unexpectedly. NHTSA logged 264 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year: 9 crashes, 1 fire, 5 injuries, led by power train at 94, electrical system at 78, and engine at 70.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the front suspension detachment recall (stop-driving order) was completed
  3. Verify both engine fire risk recalls and the block heater recall were performed
  4. Confirm the battery detection and shift-to-neutral recalls were addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
