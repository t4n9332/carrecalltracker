Buying a used 2019 Ford Edge? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 762 owner complaints on file

The standout recall is a critical Ford/Lincoln transmission leak that creates a fire risk; a driver seat belt that may pull free in a crash and a rearview camera blanking issue round out the list. NHTSA holds 762 owner complaints — unverified reports — with seven crashes, one fire and four injuries. Unlike the 2018 car, these complaints center on power train (455) rather than engine (158), a shift worth noting alongside the transmission recall.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Look underneath for transmission fluid leaks or stains
  3. Tug the driver seat belt hard and check its anchor point
  4. Ask about shifting, shudder or hesitation and any transmission repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
