Buying a used 2019 Ford Edge? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 762 owner complaints on file

This model year has three recalls: a critical issue involving transmission bolts that may leak and pose a fire risk, a high-severity seat belt anchor detachment concern, and a medium rearview camera display issue. NHTSA has logged 762 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this vehicle, including 7 crashes, 1 fire, and 4 injuries. Powertrain complaints dominate at 455, more than half the total, followed by 158 in ENGINE, 74 in UNKNOWN OR OTHER, and 34 in ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the transmission bolt fire-risk recall was completed
  3. Ask about seat belt anchor recall repair status
  4. Check for a rearview camera software update related to the recall
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
