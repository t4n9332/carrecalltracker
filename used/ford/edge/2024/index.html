Buying a used 2024 Ford Edge? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 42 owner complaints on file

The 2024 Edge has the lightest recall record of these years: two medium-severity rearview camera recalls covering images that may freeze, blank, or fail to appear. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. NHTSA holds 42 unverified owner complaints, with no single dominant area — electrical system (7), back over prevention (6) and power train (5) lead, alongside 8 filed as unknown or other. Those reports allege 5 crashes and 5 injuries, and remain unverified.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Test the backup camera through several reverse cycles for blanking or a failure to display
  3. Ask whether both camera recalls were completed, or check the VIN on NHTSA's recall lookup
  4. Ask about any electrical glitches, the largest named complaint area for this year
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
