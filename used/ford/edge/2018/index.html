Buying a used 2018 Ford Edge? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 810 owner complaints on file

This year carries the longest recall list in this group — seven, including a critical Ford/Lincoln power cable arcing fire risk, torque converter weld failure, front and rear brake hose problems shared with the Lincoln MKX, a roll-away-from-Park condition, doors that may open in a crash, and a backup camera fault. NHTSA has 810 owner complaints, unverified, with 17 crashes, three fires and 24 injuries, concentrated on engine (437), engine and engine cooling (173) and power train (81).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all seven recalls, especially brake hoses and power cables, were completed
  3. Inspect front and rear brake hoses for bulging, cracking or seepage
  4. Park on an incline and check the vehicle holds in Park
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
