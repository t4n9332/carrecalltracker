Buying a used 2020 Ford Transit? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 83 owner complaints on file

Seating and restraints dominate: separate recalls cover seat belts in converted or upfitted vans, wheelchair restraints in Rollx conversions, and side curtain airbags that may not inflate. A critical recall addresses a generator fuel hose that can leak gasoline, and AWD vans have rear driveshaft bolts that can loosen; the wrong-part parking brake and two blank-backup-camera recalls also apply. Of 83 unverified NHTSA complaints, seats were the top area at 27, then power train; three fires were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the van was converted or upfitted, since the belt and wheelchair recalls target those builds.
  3. On AWD, confirm the rear driveshaft bolt repair; test the parking brake on any Transit.
  4. Cycle the backup camera repeatedly, and ask if a factory onboard generator is fitted.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
