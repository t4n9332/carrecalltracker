Buying a used 2018 Ford Transit? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 57 owner complaints on file

This model year carries ten recalls, none rated critical, with a recurring theme around seats and safety restraints: several separate recalls address foldaway or seat-back locks that may unlock or fail to lock, plus a hybrid driveshaft that may detach, a glass roof hatch that may shatter, and a parking brake cable that may fail. Owners have filed 57 unverified complaints with NHTSA, including 1 crash, 1 fire, and 12 injuries; the most-cited areas are engine (14), power train (12), unknown/other (11), and electrical system (8).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if the foldaway/seat-back seat lock recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the hybrid driveshaft recall (if equipped) was performed
  4. Check parking brake cable and glass roof hatch recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
