Buying a used 2018 Ford Ecosport? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 726 owner complaints on file

This model year has a high-severity recall for an oil pump belt that may fail, and a medium-severity notice about an incorrect brake fluid label. NHTSA has logged 726 owner complaints for this vehicle (unverified reports), including 18 crashes, 2 fires, and 10 injuries; the leading categories are engine (355) and engine and engine cooling (311), followed by power train (67) and unknown or other (61).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Oil pump belt service/replacement history
  3. Engine and engine-cooling system inspection
  4. Brake fluid label accuracy vs. actual fluid used
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
