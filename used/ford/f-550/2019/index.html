Buying a used 2019 Ford F-550? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 14 owner complaints on file

This 2019 F-550 has four recalls: a critical one for an engine block heater cable that may corrode, a high-severity one for front seat recliners that may fail, and two medium ones covering first responder brake lights and a backup camera image that may reverse; none are marked stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA logged 14 owner complaints for this model year (unverified reports), including 1 crash and 3 fires reported, with 0 injuries. Top complaint categories are engine (5), structure (4), electrical system (3), and steering (2).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine block heater cable and front seat recliner recalls were completed
  3. Ask about the reported fires and crash tied to this model year and any related repairs
  4. Have the engine and electrical system inspected given complaint history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
