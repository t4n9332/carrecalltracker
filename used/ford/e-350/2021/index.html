Buying a used 2021 Ford E-350? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 22 owner complaints on file

Recalls for this year include possible rear axle/driveshaft failure, wiring harness damage from rubbing, sudden power steering fluid loss, and an engine cover that may overheat. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA total 22, including 1 crash report and no reported fires or injuries (unverified reports). Engine (11) is the most-cited complaint category, followed by steering (7) and service brakes (7), with hydraulic system also mentioned (5).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm power steering fluid loss and wiring harness rubbing recalls have been repaired
  3. Inspect rear axle/driveshaft and engine cover for recall-related wear or heat damage
  4. Ask about steering and brake system service history given complaint frequency
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
