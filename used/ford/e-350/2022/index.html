Buying a used 2022 Ford E-350? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 14 owner complaints on file

Three recalls apply to the 2022 E-350: a critical one for fuel tanks that may rust, plus the same two power steering loss recalls carried over from the prior year. There are 14 unverified owner complaints on file with NHTSA, with steering (7) the largest category and brakes (4) next; one injury reported, no crashes or fires. The fuel tank recall is worth particular attention on vans that lived in road-salt regions.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the fuel tank rust recall was inspected or the tank replaced
  3. Confirm both power steering loss recalls are closed on this VIN
  4. Drive it and note any steering effort changes — the top complaint area
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
