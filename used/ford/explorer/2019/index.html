Buying a used 2019 Ford Explorer? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 294 owner complaints on file

This model year has a critical block heater fire risk recall, along with high-severity recalls for front seat recliners that may fail, roof rail covers that can come loose or detach, and a rear suspension toe link that may fracture. A medium recall covers roof pillar trim that may come loose. NHTSA has logged 294 unverified owner complaints, including 2 crash reports and 6 injury reports with no fires reported, and structure (148) issues cited most, followed by unknown-category (45), power train (35), and engine (19).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the block heater fire-risk recall has been repaired
  3. Verify the roof rail cover recall (loose/detach) was completed
  4. Confirm the rear suspension toe link recall repair status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
