Buying a used 2018 Ford Explorer? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 727 owner complaints on file

The 2018 Explorer carries three critical recalls — a fuel pressure sensor that may leak gasoline, engine block heaters that can catch fire, and a rear suspension toe link that may break — plus seat backs that may be dangerously weak, roof rail covers listed under two separate campaigns, and windshield pillar trim that can fall off. Complaint volume stands out: 727 unverified NHTSA reports, 287 filed under structure, with 7 fires and 15 crashes alleged by owners.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN to confirm the toe link and fuel pressure sensor recalls were completed.
  3. Ask whether the block heater was ever used and inspect its cord and connector.
  4. Check that both roof rail covers and the windshield pillar trim are firmly attached.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
