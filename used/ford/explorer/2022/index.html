Buying a used 2022 Ford Explorer? Check these 24 recalls first

24 NHTSA recalls · 263 owner complaints on file

The 2022 Explorer's recalls cluster around two themes. Fuel: a filler tube that may detach and a fuel line that may leak gasoline. Rollaway: separate campaigns for not staying in Park and for a bolt that can disconnect the driveshaft. Weak engine rails, plug-in hybrid battery failure, Police Interceptor engine fire risk, and block heater fires fill out the list. Owners filed 263 unverified NHTSA complaints, alleging 17 crashes and 23 injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Smell for fuel odor around the filler neck and underbody and look for staining.
  3. Confirm all Park/rollaway and driveshaft recalls show completed on this VIN.
  4. Ask whether the engine rail recall and, if hybrid, the battery recall were performed.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
