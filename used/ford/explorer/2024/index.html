Buying a used 2024 Ford Explorer? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 20 owner complaints on file

The 2024 Explorer's recall list leans toward fasteners and software: seat belt anchor and trailer hitch bolts that can loosen, second-row seats covered by two separate recalls for shifting, and a defect where the vehicle may not hold in Park. Two fire-related recalls — a hybrid battery cable that can rub and spark, and a block heater that can short — carry no stop-driving or park-outside instruction. NHTSA logged 20 unverified owner complaints, including one crash, one fire and three injuries, clustered in driver assistance, visibility and suspension.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN for open recalls, especially the Park-engagement and seat belt anchor bolt repairs
  3. Confirm whether it is the hybrid, and if so that the battery cable recall was completed
  4. Shift the second row and check the backup camera image for blanking or a flipped view
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
