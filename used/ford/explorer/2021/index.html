Buying a used 2021 Ford Explorer? Check these 26 recalls first

26 NHTSA recalls · 419 owner complaints on file

The 2021 Explorer's recalls lean toward rollaway and driveline risk: three separate campaigns involve a driveshaft that may disconnect or the vehicle not staying in Park, alongside wrong rear suspension parts, side airbags that may fail, EcoBoost engines that can break and lose power, plug-in hybrid battery failure, Police Interceptor engine fire risk, and block heater fires. Owners filed 419 unverified NHTSA complaints, led by power train with 118, alleging 5 fires and 16 injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify every Park/rollaway and driveshaft bolt recall shows closed on this VIN.
  3. Ask the seller whether the vehicle has ever rolled or moved while shifted into Park.
  4. Request engine service records; EcoBoost failure is a listed recall concern for this year.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
