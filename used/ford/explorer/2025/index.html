Buying a used 2025 Ford Explorer? Check these 20 recalls first

20 NHTSA recalls · 96 owner complaints on file

Recalls for the 2025 Explorer concentrate on the drivetrain: leaking fuel injectors, connecting rods and engines that can fail, a transmission linked to unexpected vehicle movement, and a computer glitch tied to stalling and rollaway. Loose fasteners appear again at the transmission, front brake calipers, trailer hitch and seat belt anchors. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Owners filed 96 unverified NHTSA complaints — seven crashes, four injuries, no fires — weighted toward electrical, brake and powertrain areas.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get a VIN recall printout covering the injector, transmission and stall/rollaway campaigns
  3. Ask whether the engine or connecting rod recall required an inspection or a replacement
  4. Test drive for stalling, brake feel and any warning lights before committing
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
