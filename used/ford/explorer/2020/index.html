Buying a used 2020 Ford Explorer? Check these 33 recalls first

33 NHTSA recalls · 1,208 owner complaints on file

This model year carries several critical fire-risk recalls, covering a wiring harness, a leaking fuel line, a driveshaft that can crack, a fuel tank valve weld, a battery, and a block heater. High-severity recalls address front seat recliners, loose airbag bolts, and an unsecured rear seat belt buckle. NHTSA has logged 1,208 unverified owner complaints, including 10 fire reports, 35 crash reports, and 38 injury reports, with power train (481) and unknown-category (204) issues most cited, followed by electrical system (182) and engine (113).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask which of the fire-risk recalls (wiring harness, fuel line, driveshaft, fuel tank, battery, block heater) have been completed
  3. Verify the front seat recliner recall repair status
  4. Confirm the rear seat belt buckle recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
