Buying a used 2023 Ford Expedition? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 64 owner complaints on file

The 2023 Expedition's recall list is long but splits into two tiers. Safety-relevant items include a front axle gear that may break, front brake lines that can leak, and trailer brakes and lights that may fail. The rest are minor: a frozen or blank rearview camera, wiper arms that can break, a missing dashboard airbag label, missing manual instructions, and console chrome that can peel into sharp edges. Owners filed 64 unverified NHTSA complaints, led by electrical system and power train.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get a dealer VIN printout showing the front axle gear and brake line recalls completed.
  3. Cycle the rearview camera repeatedly on a test drive to see if it freezes or blanks.
  4. If you tow, have the seller demonstrate trailer brakes and lights with a trailer connected.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
