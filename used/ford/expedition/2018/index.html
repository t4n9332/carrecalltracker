Buying a used 2018 Ford Expedition? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 374 owner complaints on file

One critical recall covers a fuel pump that can leak; the rest concentrate on two themes — second-row seats that may not be secured or can shift, and park/rollaway problems tied to the 10-speed transmission — plus front seat belts that can lock up and a brake fluid leak. Among 374 unverified owner complaints, power train (146) and engine (98) dominate, with 25 injuries and eight crashes alleged.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Shift into Park on an incline and confirm the vehicle actually holds without rolling.
  3. Pull and latch each second-row seat to check it locks firmly and doesn't shift.
  4. Ask for 10-speed transmission service history and check brake fluid level and lines for leaks.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
