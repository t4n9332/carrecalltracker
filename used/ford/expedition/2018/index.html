Buying a used 2018 Ford Expedition? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 374 owner complaints on file

This model year has ten recalls, including a critical fuel pump weld fracture issue, plus high-severity recalls for a power window problem, vehicles that may roll away in park, three separate seat belt locking issues, a brake fluid leak, and a transmission park system that may fail. NHTSA logged 374 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 8 crash reports, 2 fire reports, and 25 injury reports. POWER TRAIN leads at 146, followed by engine, electrical system, and unspecified issues.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump weld recall has been completed
  3. Test that the transmission holds the vehicle in Park
  4. Check all seat belts for proper locking function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
