Buying a used 2019 Ford Expedition? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 492 owner complaints on file

This model year has ten recalls, the highest-severity being high-rated: front seat recliners that may fail, rear suspension fastener issues, a park warning chime that is too short, three separate seat belt locking issues, and a transmission park system that may fail, plus lower-severity chime, console, and manual issues. NHTSA logged 492 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 8 crash reports, 3 fire reports, and 37 injury reports. POWER TRAIN leads at 216, followed by engine, seat belts, and unspecified issues.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Test that the transmission park system holds the vehicle in Park
  3. Check all seat belts for proper locking function
  4. Inspect rear suspension fasteners and front seat recliner mechanisms
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
