Buying a used 2019 Ford Expedition? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 492 owner complaints on file

No critical recalls here, but several high-severity ones: seat backs described as dangerously weak, loose rear suspension bolts, front seat belts that can lock up, and a rollaway risk, alongside repeat campaigns for a PARK warning chime that sounds too briefly — including one after an earlier repair. Complaints total 492 unverified reports, the highest of these Expedition years, led by power train (216), engine (107) and seat belts (95).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Cycle each front seat belt fully in and out to see whether it binds or locks up.
  3. Ask for proof the rear suspension bolt and seat back recalls were completed.
  4. Open the driver's door with the vehicle out of Park and listen for the warning chime.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
