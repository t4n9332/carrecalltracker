Buying a used 2021 Ford Expedition? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 232 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

A critical engine compartment fire risk carries a park-outside advisory until repair. Beyond that, the year skews toward sensors and hardware: possibly faulty side airbag sensors, front seat belts that can lock, a rollaway risk, two wiper arm recalls, two backup camera recalls and a moonroof wind deflector that can fly off. Of 232 unverified owner complaints, power train (110) and engine (55) lead, with four fires alleged.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the engine compartment fire recall was completed — it came with a park-outside advisory.
  3. Run the wipers at full speed and check the arms are tight, and confirm the backup camera displays reliably.
  4. Inspect the moonroof wind deflector is present and secure, and ask about the side airbag sensor recall.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
