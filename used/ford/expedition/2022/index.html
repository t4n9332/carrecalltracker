Buying a used 2022 Ford Expedition? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 112 owner complaints on file

Towing is the theme: three recalls involve trailer brakes or trailer lights not working, and they sit alongside front brake lines that can leak and a fuel pump failure that can stall the engine. Lower-severity items cover rearview cameras, wiper arms and a moonroof wind deflector. At 112, complaints are the lowest of these Expedition years — unverified reports led by electrical system, other, forward collision avoidance and power train, none alleging fire.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. If you plan to tow, test the integrated trailer brake controller and trailer lights with an actual trailer.
  3. Have the front brake lines inspected for leaks and ask whether the fuel pump recall was performed.
  4. Check the backup camera and wipers, and ask whether the collision warning system false-alarms.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
