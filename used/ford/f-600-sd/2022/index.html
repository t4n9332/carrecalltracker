Buying a used 2022 Ford F-600 SD? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

The stop-driving recall carries over: front wheels can detach on Super Duty trucks, and until that repair is verified the truck is not meant to be driven. Two high-severity diesel fuel pump recalls follow, plus lower-severity items — backup camera failure, wiper arms that can break, and a missing tire information label. Owner reporting is almost nonexistent: one unverified NHTSA complaint logged under engine, with a fire reported and no crashes or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the front wheel detachment recall is closed before driving it.
  3. Confirm both diesel fuel pump recalls were performed.
  4. Test the backup camera, inspect the wiper arms, and look for the tire information label.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
