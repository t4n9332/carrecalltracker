Buying a used 2020 Ford Explorer Police Intercept? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This model year, sold as a police-fleet vehicle, has two high-severity recalls: one for washer fluid that may be contaminated with brake fluid, and one for a rear axle bolt issue. Owners should check whether either repair has been completed, since both relate to brake system components. NHTSA has logged only 1 unverified owner complaint for this model year, filed under the power train category, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported alongside it.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the washer fluid/brake fluid contamination recall was fixed
  3. Verify the rear axle bolt recall repair was completed
  4. Ask about any power train problems, the only reported complaint category
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
