Buying a used 2020 Ford Mustang? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 119 owner complaints on file

This model year has eight open recalls: four high-severity campaigns covering a park warning chime that sounds too briefly, a camera misalignment that can affect safety features, a brake pedal bracket that may fracture, and a brake fluid warning light that may fail to illuminate. Four medium recalls address rearview camera cutouts, blank images, and a camera/driver-assist shutoff issue. NHTSA logged 119 owner complaints (unverified reports): 13 crashes, 5 fires, 8 injuries, with engine and electrical system complaints at 25 each and power train at 24.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the brake pedal bracket and brake fluid warning light recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the camera misalignment recall (affects safety features) was performed
  4. Test the rearview camera for cutouts or blank images
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
