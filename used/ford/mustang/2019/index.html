Buying a used 2019 Ford Mustang? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 148 owner complaints on file

All six recalls on this year are rated medium and cluster around screens and software: blank gauge and dashboard displays, backup and rearview camera failures, a software glitch that disables the camera and safety features, and a park warning chime that is too short. Nothing critical is listed and none carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. NHTSA shows 148 owner complaints, which are unverified, led by engine at 50, with three crashes, one fire and one injury.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the infotainment and camera software updates were applied by a dealer
  3. Watch the gauge cluster and dash display through a full cold start
  4. Ask for engine repair records, since engine leads the complaint areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
