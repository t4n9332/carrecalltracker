Buying a used 2018 Ford Mustang? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 191 owner complaints on file

This model year has four open recalls: a critical fuel leak risk tied to Roush supercharger units, a high-severity recall for an instrument cluster that may not show gear position, another high-severity recall for vehicles that may roll away in park, and a medium recall for a distorted rearview camera image. NHTSA logged 191 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year, including 8 crashes, 1 fire, and 5 injuries, led by engine complaints at 56, power train at 45, and electrical system at 30.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if it has the Roush supercharger and whether the fuel leak recall was done
  3. Confirm the gear-position cluster and park roll-away recalls were completed
  4. Test the rearview camera for a distorted image
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
