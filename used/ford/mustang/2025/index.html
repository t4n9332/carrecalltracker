Buying a used 2025 Ford Mustang? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 17 owner complaints on file

This 2025 Mustang has six recalls shared across several Ford/Lincoln models, covering a transmission defect, headlights that may fail, an EGR valve failure, wiring that can cause power loss, exterior lights that may stop working, and wipers that may fail in cold weather. NHTSA has logged 16 owner complaints (unverified reports) so far, with 1 crash and no reported fires or injuries; exterior lighting and visibility-related categories make up a notable share of those complaints, alongside engine-related reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask about headlight failure recall completion
  3. Confirm EGR valve and transmission recall repairs
  4. Check exterior light/wiper function in cold weather
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
