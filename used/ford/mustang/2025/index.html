Buying a used 2025 Ford Mustang? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 16 owner complaints on file

The 2025 Mustang carries a shorter list than the prior year, but one critical recall involves a transmission fault that can cause unexpected vehicle movement, and others cover a loss of driving power and engine wiring that can crack. Lighting is a recurring theme — headlights plus tail and marker lights — along with wipers that can stick on high in cold weather. Only 16 unverified NHTSA complaints are on file, led by exterior lighting and visibility.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the transmission unexpected-movement recall was completed before purchase.
  3. Test headlights, tail and marker lights, and wipers at all speeds on a cold start.
  4. Ask whether the car ever lost power while driving, and check for engine wiring repairs.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
