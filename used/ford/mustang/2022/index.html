Buying a used 2022 Ford Mustang? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 49 owner complaints on file

Three critical recalls, two of them covering a steering wheel that may turn on its own, plus a roll-away-from-Park campaign listed under a Ford and Lincoln SUV recall. High-rated items repeat from 2021: knee airbag deployment, two low brake fluid warning light recalls, and a fuel pump that can stall the engine. NHTSA has just 49 owner complaints — unverified reports — with fuel system and gasoline at the top and steering close behind, mirroring the recall list.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get repair documentation for both steering wheel recalls specifically
  3. Ask whether the car has ever moved or rolled after being shifted to Park
  4. Confirm the fuel pump recall is closed and test the brake fluid warning lamp
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
