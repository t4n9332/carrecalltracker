Buying a used 2019 Ford Econoline? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This model year has two medium-severity recalls, both concerning the backup camera image: one where the display may show a reversed picture, and another where it may appear distorted. NHTSA logged just 1 owner complaint for this vehicle (unverified report), with no reported crashes, fires, or injuries, filed under the electrical system category. The complaint record for this model year is otherwise limited on file with NHTSA.
