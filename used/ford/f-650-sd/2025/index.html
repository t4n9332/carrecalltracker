Buying a used 2025 Ford F-650 SD? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

Three recalls on a very new truck, and the severity is what stands out: a steering loss condition covering F-650 and F-750 trucks, a roll-away risk in Park shared with the E-350, and a liftgate platform that can fall if its pins break. None were issued with stop-driving or park-outside instructions. Owners have filed four NHTSA complaints, all in service brakes, with no crashes, fires or injuries; those are unverified reports and a small sample for this model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the steering and Park roll-away recall remedies by VIN before any test drive
  3. If the truck has a liftgate, inspect the platform pins and ask for the recall repair record
  4. Ask the seller about brake complaints or brake work, since all four owner reports are brake-related
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
