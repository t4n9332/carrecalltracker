Buying a used 2022 Ford Bronco Sport? Check these 15 recalls first

15 NHTSA recalls · 356 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

Ten recalls including several critical ones: engine oil leaks, three separate fuel injector leak campaigns, a fuel leak fire risk, block heater fire risk, and a suspension recall carrying a stop-driving advisory. High-severity items add stalling, poor braking performance and a fuel pump fault that can stall the engine. Owners filed 356 unverified complaints with 13 crashes, 12 fires and 17 injuries, led by engine, electrical and power train.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the suspension stop-driving recall was repaired before any test drive
  3. Confirm each fuel injector campaign applying to this VIN was closed out
  4. Ask about stalling episodes and test braking response on a road test
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
