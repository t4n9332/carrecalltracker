Buying a used 2021 Ford Bronco Sport? Check these 16 recalls first

16 NHTSA recalls · 686 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

This model year carries several recalls, including critical ones for engine oil leaks, fuel injector leaks across three campaigns, fuel injector fire risk, block heater fire risk, and a front suspension detachment risk requiring owners to stop driving until repaired. High-severity recalls cover rear drive axle seizure, incorrect front control arms, loose rear suspension bolts, and engine stall risk. NHTSA logged 686 owner complaints, an unverified count, including 18 crashes, 16 fires, and 12 injuries; electrical system leads at 207, followed by powertrain (180), engine (145), and service brakes (128).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask which fuel injector and engine oil leak recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the front suspension detachment recall was repaired, since it instructed owners to stop driving
  4. Check for rear drive axle, control arm, and suspension bolt recall completion
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
