Buying a used 2023 Ford Bronco Sport? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 154 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

Fuel-related recalls dominate: three fuel injector leak campaigns plus a fuel leak fire risk, alongside block heater fire risk and a suspension recall NHTSA flags stop-driving until repaired. Other items cover an anti-lock brake module leak, incorrectly built tires, loss of drive power and sudden battery failure. Of 154 unverified owner complaints, 9 involved crashes, 5 fires and 9 injuries; fuel system and gasoline categories each show 30 reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the stop-driving suspension recall and the ABS module recall were completed
  3. Check whether the original tires are still fitted and covered by the tire recall
  4. Ask about sudden battery failure or loss of drive power, and check battery age
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
