Buying a used 2024 Ford Bronco Sport? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 107 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

The list shortens but keeps the serious items: a fuel leak fire risk, block heater fire risk, and a suspension recall carrying a stop-driving advisory. Two separate campaigns address loss of drive power, plus software that may miss a low battery charge, wipers that can quit, and a rearview camera that may go blank. Owners filed 107 unverified complaints with 5 crashes, 7 fires and 1 injury, led by electrical and engine.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the suspension stop-driving recall was repaired and ask for the dealer paperwork
  3. Ask whether both loss-of-drive-power recalls and the battery software update were applied
  4. Test the wipers and rearview camera, and check for any fuel odor
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
