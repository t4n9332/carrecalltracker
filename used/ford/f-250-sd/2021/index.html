Buying a used 2021 Ford F-250 SD? Check these 13 recalls first

13 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

The 2021 F-250's recalls center on diesel fuel delivery and steering: a fuel filter cap that can leak, standard and high-pressure fuel pumps that may fail, and two steering campaigns, one involving a stabilizer kit that can lock the steering. A driveshaft that may break and a windshield that may detach are also covered. NHTSA shows only four unverified owner complaints — power train, structure, steering and engine cooling, one each — with no crashes, fires or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. If it is a diesel, confirm both fuel pump recalls and check the fuel filter cap for seepage
  3. Ask about the steering recalls and whether any aftermarket stabilizer kit is fitted
  4. Inspect the windshield bonding and edges, and test the wiper arms and backup camera
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
