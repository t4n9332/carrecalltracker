Buying a used 2022 Ford F-250 SD? Check these 17 recalls first

17 NHTSA recalls · 121 owner complaints on file

Towing hardware stands out on the 2022 F-250: three separate recalls address trailer brakes, or trailer brakes and lights, that may fail. Airbags appear twice — faulty side airbag sensors and a driver airbag cover that may break apart — alongside diesel fuel pump failures and a driveshaft that may break. No recall here is rated critical. Complaint volume is high for this group: 121 unverified NHTSA reports citing two crashes, four fires and four injuries, led by powertrain, electrical, engine and fuel.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Hook up a trailer if possible and verify the integrated brake controller and lights work
  3. Confirm the side airbag sensor and driver airbag cover recalls were completed
  4. For diesels, ask about fuel pump history and any stalling or fuel smell complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
