Buying a used 2022 Ford F-250 SD? Check these 17 recalls first

17 NHTSA recalls · 121 owner complaints on file

This model year's recalls top out at high severity, with no critical recalls listed, and cover a driver airbag cover that may break apart, side air bags that may fail, driveshaft damage from loose insulators, three separate trailer brake control/light failure recalls, and two separate fuel pump failure recalls; wiper arm and fuel pump warning issues are rated medium. NHTSA lists 121 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 2 crashes, 4 fires, and 4 injuries described; top categories are power train (21), electrical system (19), engine (17), and fuel system (16).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the trailer brake control and light recalls were completed, especially if the vehicle tows
  3. Verify both fuel pump failure recalls were addressed
  4. Ask about repair records for the driver airbag cover and driveshaft insulator recalls
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
