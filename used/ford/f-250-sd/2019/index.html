Buying a used 2019 Ford F-250 SD? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 8 owner complaints on file

Steering dominates the 2019 F-250's record. A recalled steering stabilizer kit can lock the steering, and five of eight unverified NHTSA complaints name steering, with four naming suspension; none reports crashes, fires or injuries. Other recalls cover a seat belt that can spark, a block heater cord fire risk, a driveshaft that may break, rolling after shifting into Park, and tires that may fail. Camera, tailgate and lighting items make up the medium-severity group.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether a steering stabilizer kit was ever installed and if the recall was addressed
  3. Drive at highway speed listening for driveshaft vibration or steering wander
  4. Confirm the tailgate latches securely and the backup camera works without fogging
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
