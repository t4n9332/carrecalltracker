Buying a used 2024 Ford Bronco? Check these 16 recalls first

16 NHTSA recalls · 74 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

The 2024 Bronco's recall list leans heavily on the front suspension: a part that can detach, a control arm that can come apart (stop-driving), and a bolt that may fail. Fuel pump bolts that may leak gas, transmission-related unexpected movement, block heater fires and engine wiring shorts are also critical. Only 74 unverified NHTSA complaints are on file — 4 crashes, 3 fires, 1 injury — though this year is newer with fewer miles behind it.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all three front suspension recalls are done, including the stop-driving control arm
  3. Ask about the fuel pump bolt repair and check for any fuel smell
  4. Verify the front seat bolt and rear glass panel recalls were completed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
