Buying a used 2024 Ford Bronco? Check these 16 recalls first

16 NHTSA recalls · 74 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

Recalls for this year include a stop-driving front control arm detachment risk, a front suspension ball joint that may detach, fuel pump bolts that may leak, engine wiring that can short circuit, and a transmission-to-transfer case joint that may wear, among others. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA total 74, including 4 crash reports, 3 fire reports, and 1 injury report (unverified reports). Unknown/other (21) and electrical system (18) are the top complaint categories, with structure (13) and service brakes (9) also cited.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the stop-driving front control arm/ball joint detachment recalls have been completed
  3. Ask about the fuel pump bolt leak recall and check for any fuel leak signs
  4. Inspect the transmission-to-transfer case joint and structure given the complaint areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
