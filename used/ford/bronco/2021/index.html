Buying a used 2021 Ford Bronco? Check these 20 recalls first

20 NHTSA recalls · 225 owner complaints on file

The 2021 Bronco carries ten recalls, three flagged critical: engine block heater fires, engine wiring that can short and start a fire, and a defect allowing power loss or rollaway. Multiple airbag-related recalls apply, plus EcoBoost engine failure and fuel pump stalling. None carry a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Owners filed 225 unverified NHTSA complaints — 6 crashes, 3 fires, 7 injuries — concentrated in engine, electrical and suspension. Hardtop roof panel separation is a Bronco-specific item here.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN for open recalls, especially the engine wiring and block heater fire repairs
  3. Ask whether the hardtop roof panel and side crash sensor recalls were completed
  4. Ask for service records on the EcoBoost engine and fuel pump, the top complaint areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
