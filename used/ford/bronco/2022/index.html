Buying a used 2022 Ford Bronco? Check these 23 recalls first

23 NHTSA recalls · 374 owner complaints on file

Recalls for this year include a steering gear that can lock up, engine wiring that can short circuit, a windshield that may detach in a crash, a park-position defect ("may not stay in park"), corroding rear shock absorbers, and a fuel pump that may stall the engine, among others. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA total 374, including 6 crash reports, 2 fire reports, and 4 injury reports (unverified reports). Visibility/wiper (65) is the most-cited complaint category, followed by engine (51) and service brakes (42).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm steering gear lock-up and 'may not stay in park' recalls have been repaired
  3. Test wipers/visibility system and service brakes given these are top complaint areas
  4. Inspect rear shock absorbers and windshield mounting for recall-related wear
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
