Buying a used 2023 Ford Bronco? Check these 18 recalls first

18 NHTSA recalls · 221 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

This is the only vehicle here with a stop-driving recall: Bronco and Ranger wheels that may have loose lug nuts. Five other critical recalls apply, covering steering lock-up, rollaway from Park, block heater fires and engine wiring shorts, plus a second rollaway-when-parked recall. Visibility/wiper leads owner complaints by a wide margin — 89 of 221 unverified NHTSA filings — followed by electrical and powertrain. Rear glass panels falling off is a separate, lower-severity recall.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the lug nut recall is closed before driving — it carries a stop-driving warning
  3. Ask about visibility or wiper problems, by far the largest complaint area
  4. Check that both rollaway recalls and the rear glass panel repair are complete
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
