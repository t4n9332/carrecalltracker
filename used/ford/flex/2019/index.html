Buying a used 2019 Ford Flex? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 244 owner complaints on file

This model year has four recalls: a critical recall for a power cable fire risk, a high-severity recall for a rearview camera that may show a blank image, a high-severity recall for door trim detachment risk, and an additional medium-severity rearview camera blank-image recall. NHTSA has logged 244 owner complaints (unverified) for this model year, including 4 crashes and 3 injuries, with no reported fires; the top complaint areas are back-over prevention (136), structure (55), unknown or other (49), and electrical system (22).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the power cable fire-risk recall has been completed
  3. Test the rearview camera for a blank-image issue
  4. Inspect door trim for detachment or looseness
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
