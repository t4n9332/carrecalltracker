Buying a used 2019 Ford Flex? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 244 owner complaints on file

Four recalls, led by a critical one for power cables that may arc and create a fire risk; the rest are two rearview camera recalls and door pillar trim that can fall off while driving. Complaint volume is high for this year at 244 NHTSA reports, though with four crashes, no fires and three injuries. Back over prevention dominates at 136, tracking the camera recalls, followed by structure (55), other (49) and electrical (22). All complaints are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get proof by VIN that the power cable arcing recall repair was completed
  3. Restart the vehicle several times and confirm the backup camera image holds
  4. Inspect the door pillar trim attachment and check for electrical warning lights
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
