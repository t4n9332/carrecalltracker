Buying a used 2020 Ford F-150? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 37 owner complaints on file

Critical recalls for this model year cover a battery cable fastener that may loosen and a starter cable nut fire risk, alongside high-severity recalls for failing front seat recliners, a possibly damaged spare tire, daytime running lights that stay on, and a shift cable defect; wiper arm and rearview camera issues are rated medium. NHTSA lists 37 owner complaints (unverified reports), with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported; complaints are concentrated in power train (30), followed by engine (4) and electrical system (4).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the starter cable nut fire recall and battery cable fastener recall were completed
  3. Verify the shift cable defect and seat recliner recalls were addressed
  4. Ask about repair records for the wiper arm and rearview camera recalls
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
