Buying a used 2020 Ford F-150? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 37 owner complaints on file

Two electrical fire and stall risks lead the 2020 F-150 list: a battery cable bolt that can loosen and a starter cable nut that can spark and burn. Also covered are weak seat backs, a spare tire with possible hidden damage, and a Park-engagement defect shared with Ranger and Expedition. Several medium recalls address cameras, wiper arms and daytime running lights. Of 37 unverified NHTSA complaints — none citing crashes, fires or injuries — 30 involve the power train.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Inspect the battery and starter cable connections, and confirm both recalls were completed
  3. Lower and examine the spare tire, which was recalled for possible hidden damage
  4. Park on a slope to confirm the truck stays in Park, and test the wipers and camera
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
