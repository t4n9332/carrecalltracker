Buying a used 2018 Ford F-150? Check these 19 recalls first

19 NHTSA recalls · 63 owner complaints on file

Critical recalls for this model year include a fuel pump weld that may fracture and two separate heater-cable fire-risk issues, alongside high-severity recalls for missing Raptor roof rivets, an engine stall from poor lubrication, a shifter that may select the wrong gear, a vehicle that may roll away in park, a seat belt pretensioner fire risk, failing front seat recliners, and daytime running lights that stay on. NHTSA lists 63 owner complaints (unverified reports), with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported; complaints are concentrated in power train (48) and engine (10).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump weld and heater cable fire recalls were completed
  3. Verify the roll-away-in-park and seat belt pretensioner recalls were addressed
  4. Ask about repair records for the seat recliner and daytime running light recalls
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
