Buying a used 2021 Ford F-350 SD? Check these 15 recalls first

15 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

This model year carries an unusually dense recall list centered on steering, driveline and fuel. One front-wheel-detachment recall carries a stop-driving warning until repaired, and separate campaigns cover steering stabilizer lock-up, loss of steering control, a rear axle that may bend with driveshaft separation, a fifth-wheel hitch bracket, and multiple diesel fuel pump and filter cap issues. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA are few by comparison — four, unverified, with no crashes, fires or injuries — and cluster on fuel and diesel systems.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN to confirm the front-wheel detachment (stop-driving) recall was completed
  3. Ask for receipts on the diesel high-pressure fuel pump and fuel filter cap repairs
  4. Test drive for steering wander or clunks; inspect driveshaft, rear axle and any fifth-wheel hitch
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
