Buying a used 2019 Ford Fusion? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 374 owner complaints on file

Two critical recalls apply to this model year: a plug-in hybrid battery module fire risk and a block heater fire risk shared across Ford and Lincoln models. A medium-severity recall covers rearview cameras that may show a blank image. NHTSA has logged 374 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year, including 5 crashes, 2 fires, and 7 injuries, with engine issues the largest category at 159, followed by electrical system at 81, power train at 73, and engine cooling at 54.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if this is the plug-in hybrid version and whether its battery module recall was completed
  3. Confirm the block heater fire recall was addressed if applicable
  4. Test the rearview camera for image dropouts
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
