Buying a used 2019 Ford Fusion? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 374 owner complaints on file

Two of the three recalls are fire-related: a battery control module issue specific to the plug-in hybrid, and an engine block heater campaign shared across many Ford and Lincoln models. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The third is a rearview camera that can go blank. NHTSA lists 374 owner complaints — unverified reports — weighted heavily toward engine and engine cooling, then electrical and powertrain, with five crashes, two fires and seven injuries attached.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm whether it is the plug-in hybrid, and if so that the battery control module recall was performed
  3. Run the VIN for open recalls, including the engine block heater fix
  4. Cycle the rearview camera several times and ask for engine and cooling repair records
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
