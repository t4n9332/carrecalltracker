Buying a used 2020 Ford Escape? Check these 23 recalls first

23 NHTSA recalls · 1,523 owner complaints on file

This model year has ten recalls, nearly all rated critical, covering transmission bolt leaks, engine oil leaks, multiple engine fire risk campaigns, two separate fuel injector leak recalls, a battery defect fire risk, and a block heater fire risk. NHTSA logged 1,523 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 20 crash reports, 4 fire reports, and 28 injury reports. The top complaint category is STRUCTURE at 605, followed by unspecified issues, powertrain, and electrical system.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask the seller which of the ten recalls have been completed and get documentation
  3. Have the engine and fuel injectors inspected for leaks
  4. Check the battery and block heater components for fire-risk recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
