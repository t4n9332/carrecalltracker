Buying a used 2019 Ford Escape? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 583 owner complaints on file

This model year carries three recalls: a critical block heater fire risk, a tire failure recall shared with the F-150, and a shifter cable defect. NHTSA has logged 583 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 24 crash reports, 3 fire reports, and 28 injury reports. ENGINE is the leading complaint category at 307, followed by powertrain at 124, engine cooling at 91, and unspecified issues at 42.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the block heater fire recall was completed
  3. Check tire condition and recall/replacement status
  4. Ask about engine-related repair history given the complaint volume
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
