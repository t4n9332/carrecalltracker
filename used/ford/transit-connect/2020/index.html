Buying a used 2020 Ford Transit Connect? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 32 owner complaints on file

The 2020 Transit Connect repeats the 2019 pattern — a transmission leak fire risk, two engine-overheat recalls tied to the engine computer and software, airbags that may not inflate, and two rollaway recalls covering the shifter bushing and rolling from Park. New here is a windshield that may detach in a crash. Of 32 unverified NHTSA complaints, power train (10) and service brakes (6) lead, and two fires and two crashes are among them.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the windshield recall was addressed, and ask whether the glass was ever replaced elsewhere.
  3. Get proof the airbag and both engine-overheat recalls were completed.
  4. Check for transmission fluid leaks and confirm the shifter and roll-from-Park recalls are closed.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
