Buying a used 2020 Ford Transit Connect? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 32 owner complaints on file

This model year has eight open recalls, three rated critical and centered on fire risk: transmission bolts that may leak, software that delays engine cooling response, and separate engine overheating reports. Other recalls cover a missing airbag booster propellant, a shifter cable bushing failure, windshield adhesion, and a shifter cable issue shared with other Ford models; a rearview camera blank-image issue is rated medium. Owners have filed 32 unverified complaints with NHTSA, including 2 crashes and 2 fires, concentrated in power train (10) and service brakes (6).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm transmission bolt and engine-cooling/overheating recalls were completed
  3. Check airbag inflator booster propellant recall status
  4. Verify shifter cable bushing and windshield adhesion recalls were done
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
