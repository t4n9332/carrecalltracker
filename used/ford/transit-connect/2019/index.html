Buying a used 2019 Ford Transit Connect? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 51 owner complaints on file

The 2019 Transit Connect adds notable items over 2018: a transmission leak that creates fire risk, two recalls covering an engine computer and software that can let the engine overheat, and airbags that may not inflate. It keeps the rollaway pair — shifter bushing failure and rolling from Park — plus the panoramic roof panel recall. Owners filed 51 unverified NHTSA complaints, weighted toward power train (24) and service brakes (19), with 2 crashes and 1 injury.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for documentation that the airbag recall and both engine-overheat fixes were performed.
  3. Look underneath for transmission fluid leaks and watch the temperature gauge on the test drive.
  4. Confirm the shifter bushing and roll-from-Park recalls are closed; test that it holds in Park on a hill.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
