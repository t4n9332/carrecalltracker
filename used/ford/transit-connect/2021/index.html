Buying a used 2021 Ford Transit Connect? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 8 owner complaints on file

The 2021 Transit Connect carries the shortest list of the four van years: no critical recalls, but the rollaway pair persists — shifter bushing failure and vans that may roll from Park — plus a seat belt that may not properly hold a child seat and a rearview camera that can go blank. Only 8 unverified NHTSA complaints are on file, with no crashes, fires or injuries reported; power train and service brakes account for most of them.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the shifter bushing and roll-from-Park recalls were completed.
  3. If installing a child seat, verify the seat belt recall repair and test the belt's locking function.
  4. Watch the backup camera for blank or frozen images during the test drive.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
