Buying a used 2022 Ford F-750? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

The recall list here is narrower than 2021's and points in one direction: two separate diesel fuel pump recalls, one of them specific to the high-pressure pump. Both are rated high rather than critical, and neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. NHTSA shows a single owner complaint, logged under fuel system and diesel, with no crashes, fires or injuries — an unverified report, but it matches the recall theme. Fuel delivery history is the area worth documenting on any truck you consider.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check the VIN for both fuel pump recalls — they are separate repairs
  3. Ask for receipts covering any fuel pump replacement or hard-start repair
  4. Watch for hard starting, stumbling or power loss during a loaded test drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
