Buying a used 2021 Ford F-450 SD? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 17 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

Steering and fuel dominate this year: a stop-driving recall for front wheels that can detach, another for loss of steering control, and a diesel fuel filter cap that can leak, alongside three separate fuel pump campaigns tied to pump failure and engine stalling. Lesser items include a windshield that may detach, wiper arms that can break, and a backup camera. Seventeen unverified complaints to NHTSA include one fire, no crashes or injuries, spread across electrical, brakes, power train and structure.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the front-wheel (stop-driving) and steering-control recalls are closed on this VIN
  3. Look for diesel odor or seepage at the fuel filter cap and ask about any stalling history
  4. Inspect the windshield bonding and seal, and test the wipers and backup camera
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
