Buying a used 2025 Ford F-450 SD? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

This model year has four recalls, one critical: a brake-loss defect on the F-450 Super Duty rated a stop-driving repair by NHTSA. High-severity recalls cover a shifter that may detach from the column, an instrument cluster that can fail at startup, and trailer brake and light failure. NHTSA logged 2 owner complaints, unverified reports, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported; wheels and power train were each cited once.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake-loss stop-driving recall was completed before purchase
  3. Ask about shifter and instrument cluster recall repairs
  4. Test trailer brake and light function before towing
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
