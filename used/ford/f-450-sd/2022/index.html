Buying a used 2022 Ford F-450 SD? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 19 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

This model year has ten recalls, one critical: a wheel detachment risk rated a stop-driving repair by NHTSA. High-severity recalls also cover trailer brake control failures (two separate recalls), fuel pump failures (two separate recalls), and trailer brake and light failure. Medium recalls address a wiper arm and rearview camera issues. NHTSA logged 19 owner complaints, unverified reports, including 3 fires and no crashes or injuries; electrical system was the most-cited area with 8 mentions, followed by service brakes at 5 and engine at 4.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the wheel-detachment stop-driving recall was completed
  3. Test trailer brake function given two separate trailer brake recalls
  4. Ask about fuel pump recall repairs, recalled twice
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
