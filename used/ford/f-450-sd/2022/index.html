Buying a used 2022 Ford F-450 SD? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 19 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

Towing hardware is the theme — three separate recalls cover trailer brakes, or trailer brakes and lights, that may fail — alongside a stop-driving recall for front wheels that can detach and three fuel pump campaigns linked to stalling. Wiper arms and two backup camera recalls round out the list. Nineteen unverified complaints filed with NHTSA include three fires but no crashes or injuries, concentrated in electrical system, service brakes and engine. Brake and electrical items appear on both the recall and complaint sides.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the front-wheel detachment (stop-driving) repair is documented on the VIN
  3. Test the integrated trailer brake controller and trailer lights with an actual trailer
  4. Ask about stalling or hard starting, and whether fuel pump recall work was done
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
