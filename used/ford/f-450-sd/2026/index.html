Buying a used 2026 Ford F-450 SD? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Very little is on file yet, which reflects how new these trucks are rather than a judgment on them. Two recalls apply: trailer brakes and lights that may fail, and an instrument cluster that may fail at startup. A single unverified owner complaint has been filed with NHTSA, in the steering category, with no crashes, fires or injuries. Expect the record to grow as more of these reach owners and accumulate miles, so today's short list may not stay short.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask the dealer to confirm both recalls have remedies available and were performed
  3. Test the trailer brake controller and trailer lighting with a trailer attached
  4. Cold start it more than once and confirm all gauges and warning lights display
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
