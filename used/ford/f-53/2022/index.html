Buying a used 2022 Ford F-53? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file

This motorhome chassis has only two recalls, but both hit fundamentals: a steering wheel that can detach and rear brake bolts that can loosen. Ford issued neither with a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, though both are the kind of work you want documented on paper before money changes hands. Only three owner complaints are on file with NHTSA — unverified reports — two of them about service brakes, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the chassis VIN for open recalls, specifically the steering wheel and rear brake bolt remedies
  3. Have the rear brakes inspected for bolt torque and signs of loosening
  4. Ask for chassis service history separate from any coach or house work
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
