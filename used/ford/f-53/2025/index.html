Buying a used 2025 Ford F-53? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

The 2025 F-53 chassis has two high-severity recalls — one for brake lights that may malfunction and one for a missing front wheel hub nut — plus a medium recall for brake lights that may stay lit continuously. NHTSA has only 1 owner complaint on file for this model year, an unverified report touching suspension, wheels, and service brakes, with no reported crashes, fires, or injuries. Given how new this model year is, recall completion status is the most concrete thing to check.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the front wheel hub nut recall was completed and torque verified
  3. Test brake light operation for both malfunction and stuck-on conditions
  4. Ask about any wheel or suspension issues the owner has noted
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
