Buying a used 2025 Ford F-53? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Three recalls on this chassis year: a critical one for a front hub missing its spindle nut, plus two covering brake lights — one for lights that may misbehave, another for lights that stay on. None carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, though the hub item is wheel-end work worth confirming was done. With a single NHTSA owner complaint — an unverified report touching suspension, wheels, and service brakes — there is almost no field history for this year yet.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify in writing that the front hub spindle nut recall was inspected and corrected
  3. Watch the brake lights at rest and under braking for both recall symptoms
  4. Ask whether the chassis has been driven loaded, given how little history exists
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
