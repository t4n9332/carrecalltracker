Buying a used 2020 Ford F-59? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

This is a stripped chassis, so its record reflects the frame underneath a motorhome or delivery body rather than a finished vehicle. Two recalls stand out and both are chassis-critical: a steering shaft bolt that may be loose, and a rear axle that can fail on F-53 and F-59 chassis. The five unverified owner complaints are few but pointed — all five cite suspension, two the power train — with no crashes, fires or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof the steering shaft bolt recall was completed.
  3. Verify the F-53/F-59 rear axle recall against the chassis VIN.
  4. Inspect the suspension and ask about ride or handling complaints.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
