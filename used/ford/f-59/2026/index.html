Buying a used 2026 Ford F-59? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

As a current-model chassis, this one has a short history: two recalls and a single unverified owner complaint, so there is little pattern to read. The critical item is a front hub that may be missing its spindle nut — a wheel-end assembly issue — alongside a brake-light circuit that can stay on. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, but the hub recall is the one to confirm was performed.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the front hub spindle nut recall was completed before delivery.
  3. Watch whether the brake lights stay on with the pedal released.
  4. Ask what dealer or upfitter work has been done since the chassis was built.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
