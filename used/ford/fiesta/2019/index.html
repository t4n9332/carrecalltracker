Buying a used 2019 Ford Fiesta? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 100 owner complaints on file

Three recalls apply: brake calipers that can drag and overheat, plus two separate rearview camera recalls covering a blanking screen and image failure. The complaint record is heavier than that list suggests — 100 NHTSA reports including 13 crashes, one fire and nine injuries. Top areas are back over prevention (30), power train (21), other (15) and service brakes (11), so the power train reports are not matched by any recall listed here. These are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check the backup camera image at start-up and again after driving for blanking
  3. Have the front brakes inspected for dragging calipers, uneven pad wear or heat damage
  4. Ask about transmission behavior and any repairs, given 21 power train complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
