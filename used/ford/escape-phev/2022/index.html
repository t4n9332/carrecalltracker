Buying a used 2022 Ford Escape Phev? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 78 owner complaints on file

Both recalls on this plug-in hybrid are critical and both concern the high-voltage battery: one for potential battery failure and one for battery fire risk. Neither directs owners to stop driving or park outside. NHTSA holds 78 unverified owner complaints, heavily weighted toward engine (36) and electrical system (23) — the same systems the recalls touch — with 11 in power train. Those reports allege 1 crash and 1 injury, and no fires. Battery recall status is the central question on this year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get written proof both high-voltage battery recalls were completed, by VIN, before purchase
  3. Ask whether the battery pack was ever replaced or serviced, and for the shop paperwork
  4. Test EV-mode driving and charging for warning lights or power loss, given the engine and electrical complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Ford dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
