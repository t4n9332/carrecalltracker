Buying a used 2024 Subaru Crosstrek? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 115 owner complaints on file

Two recalls so far on the 2024 Crosstrek, and both affect whether the car keeps moving: wiring that can cut engine power, and a front driveshaft that can break, shared with Forester, Impreza and WRX. Its 115 unverified NHTSA complaints skew toward unspecified problems (31), followed by exterior lighting (13), visibility/wiper (12) and forward collision avoidance (11). Eight crashes, three injuries and two fires appear in those reports, a notable count for a model year this recent.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both the wiring and front driveshaft recall repairs are complete on this VIN
  3. Check all exterior lights and ask about any headlight or taillight failures or replacements
  4. Test the forward collision avoidance system for false alerts or dashboard warnings during the drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Subaru dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
