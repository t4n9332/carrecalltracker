Buying a used 2023 Subaru Solterra? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 25 owner complaints on file · 2 do-not-drive

This 2023 Solterra carries two critical recalls tied to wheel bolts loosening and wheels potentially coming loose, both flagged as stop-driving issues, plus a high-severity defroster failure recall and a medium-severity rearview camera freezing issue. NHTSA has 25 owner complaints on file (unverified reports), including 4 crashes and no reported injuries or fires; the leading complaint categories are forward collision avoidance (9), electrical system (8), and vehicle speed control (6).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify both wheel bolt/wheel-loosening recalls were completed before driving
  3. Confirm the defroster and rearview camera recalls were addressed
  4. Ask about any forward collision avoidance or speed control system malfunctions
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Subaru dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
