Buying a used 2022 Subaru Ascent? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 55 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

The 2022 Ascent carries the same two recalls as 2021: the critical heater ground bolt, with its park-outside advisory until repaired, and an incorrect weight-rating label. Complaint volume is lower at 55 unverified NHTSA reports, but those include 6 crashes, 6 injuries and 2 fires, a higher share of serious outcomes than the higher-volume earlier years. Power train (14) and service brakes (11) lead the complaint areas, with engine (9) and visibility/wiper (7) behind.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the heater ground bolt recall is closed and look for any heater or burning-smell history in service records
  3. Have the brakes inspected and note any pedal or ABS oddities on the test drive, given service brakes is a top complaint area
  4. Ask about transmission or drivetrain repairs, the largest reported complaint area for this year
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Subaru dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
