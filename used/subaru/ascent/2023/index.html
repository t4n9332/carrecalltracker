Buying a used 2023 Subaru Ascent? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 44 owner complaints on file

This model year has four recalls: a front control arm ball joint issue, driveshaft bolts that may loosen and disconnect, tires that may lose air suddenly, and a low-severity incorrect weight rating label. NHTSA has 44 owner complaints for this year, including 4 crashes, 1 fire, and 4 injuries; service brakes (10) is the largest category, followed by unknown/other (8), forward collision avoidance (7), and visibility/wiper (6). These complaint counts are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the front control arm ball joint and driveshaft bolt recalls were completed
  3. Check tire pressure and ask about the tire air-loss recall
  4. Ask about brake service history given the service brakes complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Subaru dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
