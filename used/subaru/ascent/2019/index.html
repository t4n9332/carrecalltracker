Buying a used 2019 Subaru Ascent? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 441 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Launch year of the Ascent, and the record is heavy. A critical recall for a heater ground bolt that may overheat and melt carries a park-outside instruction. Others cover missing spot welds near the B-pillar, driveshaft bolts that can loosen and separate, a transmission fault that can cost drive power, software that can break the chain, an engine valve that can break apart, and two fuel pump campaigns tied to stalling. NHTSA logged 441 complaints — 6 fires, 10 crashes, 8 injuries — led by electrical system and power train. Unverified.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the critical heater ground bolt recall was completed, since that one carries a park-outside warning
  3. Verify both fuel pump recalls and the transmission software campaign are closed, and ask about any stalling or loss of power
  4. Ask for service records covering the driveshaft bolt, engine valve, and B-pillar spot weld inspections
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Subaru dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
