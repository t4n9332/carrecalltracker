Buying a used 2020 Subaru Ascent? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 322 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year has a critical cabin heater fire-risk recall with a park-outside-until-repaired advisory, along with recalls for a fuel pump that may fail, a drive chain risk, a front tie rod that may separate, and an incorrect weight rating label. NHTSA has 322 owner complaints for this year, including 6 crashes, 1 fire, and 5 injuries; electrical system (101) is the largest category, followed by power train (58), unknown/other (56), and visibility/wiper (49). These complaint figures are owner-reported and unverified.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the cabin heater fire recall was completed and the park-outside advisory addressed
  3. Ask about front tie rod and drive chain recall repairs
  4. Verify the fuel pump recall was performed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Subaru dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
