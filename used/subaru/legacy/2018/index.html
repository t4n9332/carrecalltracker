Buying a used 2018 Subaru Legacy? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 117 owner complaints on file

One high-severity recall — a fuel pump that can quit while driving — plus two medium ones: a fuel gauge that reads wrong and a backup camera that goes blank. The complaint record leans the same way. Of 117 unverified owner reports to NHTSA, electrical system is the largest area at 40, then wiper/visibility at 25 and engine at 10. Seven crashes, one fire and one injury appear among those reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pump recall was completed by VIN
  3. Restart the car several times and watch whether the backup camera blanks
  4. Compare the fuel gauge reading against an actual fill-up
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Subaru dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
