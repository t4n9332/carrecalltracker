Buying a used 2018 Subaru Forester? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 493 owner complaints on file

The standout on the 2018 Forester is a recall for a front passenger airbag that may switch itself off, and the complaint record echoes it: air bags is the second-largest area, at 96 of 493 unverified NHTSA complaints. Visibility/wiper leads at 135, with electrical system (70) behind. A fuel pump recall shared with the Toyota 86 and a low-severity incorrect tire information sticker round out the list. Reported outcomes include 26 crashes, 9 injuries and one fire.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Sit an adult in the front passenger seat and confirm the passenger airbag status light reads ON
  3. Verify the airbag and fuel pump recall repairs are both recorded against the VIN
  4. Check the tire placard against the tires actually fitted, since the sticker may be wrong
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Subaru dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
