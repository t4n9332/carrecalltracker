Buying a used 2025 Subaru Forester? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 93 owner complaints on file

Three recalls, two of them assembly-quality issues: alloy wheels that may be improperly attached and child seat anchor bolts that can loosen. The third, incorrect weight-rating labels, is paperwork rather than hardware. For such a new model year the complaint count stands out — 93 unverified owner reports to NHTSA, including 11 crashes, two fires and six injuries. Fuel/propulsion, wiper/visibility and forward collision avoidance are the most-cited areas.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the wheel-attachment recall was done and have lug torque verified
  3. Check the child seat anchor bolt recall by VIN if you'll mount car seats
  4. Verify the corrected weight-rating label is on the door jamb
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Subaru dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
