Buying a used 2019 Subaru Forester? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 833 owner complaints on file

Three recalls apply to this model year: a high-severity power loss risk, high-severity loose suspension bolts, and a medium-severity power steering assist loss; none are marked stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA has logged 833 owner complaints for this year (unverified reports), including 37 crashes, 11 fires, and 21 injuries. Visibility/wiper complaints lead at 370, followed by unknown or other at 144, electrical system at 134, and engine at 106.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if power loss and suspension bolt recalls were completed
  3. Test power steering for any loss of assist
  4. Check wiper/visibility system and engine for reported issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Subaru dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
