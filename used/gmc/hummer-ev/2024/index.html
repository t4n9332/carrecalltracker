Buying a used 2024 GMC Hummer EV? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Two high-severity recalls define this year: loose front seat belt bolts shared with the Silverado EV, and a defect that can cause a loss of power, covering several GM EVs including the Cadillac Lyriq. Neither is flagged stop-driving or park-outside. The complaint record is nearly empty, with a single unverified owner report to NHTSA under forward collision avoidance and no crashes, fires, or injuries, reflecting low volume rather than a proven clean record.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get written confirmation the front seat belt bolt recall was completed
  3. Confirm the loss-of-power recall remedy, including any software update, was applied
  4. Ask whether the vehicle has ever lost power or shut down while driving
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
