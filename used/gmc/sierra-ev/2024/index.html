Buying a used 2024 GMC Sierra EV? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file

Three recalls, all electrical or safety-hardware in nature: a power-loss defect shared with the Cadillac Lyriq and other GM EVs, front turn signals that are hard to see, and a loose rear seat belt bolt on Silverado EV and Sierra EV. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Only six unverified owner complaints are on file with NHTSA — no crashes or injuries, one fire — touching latches/locks, service brakes, power train and electrical.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the GM EV power-loss recall repair is complete and ask about any loss-of-power episodes
  3. Confirm the rear seat belt bolt was inspected or retorqued under recall
  4. Check the front turn signals and whether the visibility recall remedy was applied
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
