Buying a used 2023 GMC Canyon? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 60 owner complaints on file

Three recalls shared with the Chevrolet Colorado: trucks that brake on their own, front wheel bolts that may break, and loose seat belt bolts. None carry stop-driving or park-outside instructions. The 60 unverified owner complaints show no crashes, 1 fire, and 1 injury. The complaint mix echoes the braking recall — forward collision avoidance is second-largest at 18 reports, behind electrical system at 20, with service brakes at 9.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the truck has ever braked unexpectedly and if that recall is closed
  3. Verify the front wheel bolt and seat belt bolt recalls were completed
  4. Have wheel bolt condition and torque checked, especially if wheels were removed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
