Buying a used 2025 GMC Canyon? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 8 owner complaints on file

A lighter record than the prior year. Two recalls apply: a passenger airbag that may inflate too forcefully, and a low-severity item for vehicles missing the electronic owner's manual download. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Only 8 unverified owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA, spread thinly across service brakes, electrical system, forward collision avoidance, and other, with one injury reported. The small count partly reflects how new the model year is.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the passenger airbag inflator recall has been performed
  3. Check whether the electronic owner's manual is present and downloadable
  4. Test the forward collision avoidance system and brake feel on a road test
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
