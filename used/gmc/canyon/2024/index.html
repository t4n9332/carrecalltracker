Buying a used 2024 GMC Canyon? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 205 owner complaints on file

This year has a critical fuel tank leak recall, a high-severity recall for a passenger airbag that may deploy improperly, and a medium-severity flickering headlight recall. NHTSA recorded 205 owner complaints, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. The bulk of those complaints — 159 of 205 — fall under exterior lighting, ahead of electrical system at 26, engine at 14, and service brakes at 12 (all unverified owner reports).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel tank leak recall was completed
  3. Ask if the passenger airbag recall remedy was applied
  4. Check that headlights function properly (flickering headlight recall)
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
