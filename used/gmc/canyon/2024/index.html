Buying a used 2024 GMC Canyon? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 205 owner complaints on file

Three recalls cover a fuel tank ring that may leak, headlights that can flicker while driving, and a passenger airbag that may inflate too forcefully. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. What stands out is how closely the complaint record tracks one recall: of 205 unverified owner reports filed with NHTSA, 159 fall under exterior lighting, with electrical system a distant second at 26. No crashes, fires, or injuries are reported among them.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel tank ring leak recall was completed on this VIN
  3. Run the headlights at night and ask if flickering was ever reported or repaired
  4. Verify the passenger airbag inflator recall remedy is done
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
