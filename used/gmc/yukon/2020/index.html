Buying a used 2020 GMC Yukon? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 36 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

Two critical recalls apply: a fuel pump leak with fire risk, and wheels that can lock up, the latter carrying a do-not-drive advisory until repaired. Two more cover pulling to one side and potentially defective Continental tires. Despite that list, owner complaint volume is the lowest of these Yukon years at 36 unverified NHTSA reports, with no crashes, fires, or injuries noted; powertrain and engine lead the categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the wheel lock-up stop-driving recall and the fuel pump recall are both closed
  3. Check whether the original Continental tires are still fitted or were replaced
  4. Look for fuel smell or staining and ask about any fuel system repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
