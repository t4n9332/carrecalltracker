Buying a used 2024 GMC Yukon? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 172 owner complaints on file

Three recalls shape this year, and which ones apply depends on the drivetrain: a critical fuel-leak repair covering the diesel SUVs, a missing brake fluid warning in trucks and SUVs, and a recall for 6.2L V8 engines that may fail. None carry a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Of 172 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA, 123 fall under ENGINE, with electrical and power train far behind; five injuries, no crashes or fires reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm which engine the truck has — diesel, 6.2L V8, or neither — then match it against the recalls
  3. Ask for dealer paperwork showing the fuel-leak and 6.2L V8 recall work was completed
  4. Verify the brake fluid low-level warning actually illuminates, and ask about any engine repairs or replacement
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
