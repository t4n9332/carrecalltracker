Buying a used 2022 GMC Yukon? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 179 owner complaints on file

This model year carries five recalls, including a critical one for an incorrectly sized fuel tank and high-severity campaigns covering a third-row seat belt buckle rivet flaw, possible engine failure, and rear wheel lockup; a lower-severity daytime running light issue is also listed. Owners have filed 179 complaints with NHTSA, including 1 crash and no reported fires or injuries. Engine-related complaints (107) are the largest category, followed by electrical system (31) and power train (29) reports. These are unverified owner accounts, not confirmed defects.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the fuel tank recall repair has been completed and documented
  3. Confirm the third-row seat belt buckle rivet recall was addressed
  4. Have the engine inspected given the volume of engine-related complaints and the engine failure recall
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
