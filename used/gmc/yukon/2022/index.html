Buying a used 2022 GMC Yukon? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 179 owner complaints on file

Five recalls, two of them critical: diesel models may leak fuel, and rear wheels may lock. The rest cover third-row seat belts that may break, possible 6.2L V8 engine failure, and daytime running lights that stay on. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside advisory. Of 179 unverified owner complaints to NHTSA, 107 concern the engine, with electrical (31) and powertrain (29) next; one crash and no injuries are noted.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine — diesel and 6.2L V8 each carry a different recall
  3. Verify the rear wheel lock and third-row seat belt repairs on this VIN
  4. Ask about engine warning lights or electrical faults, the top two complaint areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
