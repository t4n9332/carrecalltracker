Buying a used 2018 GMC Terrain? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 219 owner complaints on file

This model year has five recalls, including a critical issue with missing transmission bolts that can cause leaks and fire risk, plus high-severity recalls for a driveshaft that may fracture, an airbag sensor that may stop working, a fuel pump that may detach, and a rear brake issue. NHTSA has logged 219 owner complaints, including 14 crashes, 1 fire, and 12 injuries reported. Service brakes (81) and engine (53) are the most-cited categories, followed by power train (41) and electrical system (35).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the transmission bolt recall was completed and inspect for leaks
  3. Verify the driveshaft, airbag sensor, and fuel pump recalls were performed
  4. Have brakes inspected given the high number of brake-related complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
