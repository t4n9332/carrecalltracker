Buying a used 2020 GMC Terrain? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 195 owner complaints on file

This model year has a critical recall for missing transmission bolts that can cause leaks and fire risk, plus a high-severity recall for child seat anchors that may be blocked by a coating. NHTSA has logged 195 owner complaints, including 7 crashes, 2 fires, and 5 injuries reported. Service brakes (70) and engine (57) are the most-cited categories, followed by an unknown/other category (34) and power train (27). These are unverified owner reports, not confirmed investigation findings.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the transmission bolt recall was completed and inspect for leaks
  3. Check child seat anchors for coating blocking access per the recall
  4. Have brakes inspected given the high number of brake-related complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
