Buying a used 2021 GMC Terrain? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 35 owner complaints on file

Two high-severity recalls apply to this year: tires that may separate, and child seat anchors that may be blocked by a coating. NHTSA has logged 35 owner complaints, including 4 crashes and 4 injuries reported, with no fires. Engine (9), service brakes (8), and power train (8) are the most-cited complaint categories, followed by electrical system (7). These complaint counts are unverified owner submissions rather than confirmed defect findings.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Inspect tires for separation issues covered by the recall
  3. Check child seat anchors for coating blocking access
  4. Have brakes and engine inspected given complaint areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
