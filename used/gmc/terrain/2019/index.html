Buying a used 2019 GMC Terrain? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 182 owner complaints on file

Three recalls, headed by the critical GM transmission fluid leak, plus weak rear brakes and rear seat welds that may fail on Equinox and Terrain; none carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. The complaint record is the bigger story: 182 unverified owner reports to NHTSA, with 7 crashes, 3 fires and 12 injuries. Service brakes (69) and engine (67) dominate, with power train (50) and electrical system (38) close behind.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the transmission fluid leak recall was performed and look for leaks underneath
  3. Ask whether the rear seat weld inspection was done, and check the rear seat for movement
  4. Get an independent brake and engine inspection given the complaint concentration there
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
