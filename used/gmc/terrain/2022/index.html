Buying a used 2022 GMC Terrain? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 87 owner complaints on file

Both recalls are medium severity and hardware fixes: a loose driver seat bolt and blocked child seat anchors, neither with a stop-driving or park-outside warning. What distinguishes this year is where complaints land. Of 87 unverified owner reports to NHTSA — none involving a crash, fire or injury — fuel and propulsion dominate: fuel/propulsion system (32), fuel system (15) and gasoline (14), plus an unknown-or-other group at 14.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask about any fuel system or propulsion issues and request related service records
  3. Confirm the driver seat bolt recall was done, and check the seat for looseness
  4. Verify the child seat anchor recall remedy and test LATCH access
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
