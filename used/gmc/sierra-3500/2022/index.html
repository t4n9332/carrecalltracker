Buying a used 2022 GMC Sierra 3500? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 31 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Three recalls apply to this year: a critical brake module fire risk advising owners to park outside, a tailgate that may unlatch while driving, and a rear wheel lock-up risk on diesel trucks. NHTSA has logged 31 owner complaints, including 3 crashes and 5 injuries reported, with no fires. Electrical system (7) and power train (6) are the most-cited complaint categories, followed by tires (5). These figures come from unverified owner submissions, not confirmed investigation findings.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake module fire recall was completed
  3. Test the tailgate latch and confirm the unlatch recall was performed
  4. If diesel, ask whether the rear wheel lock-up recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
