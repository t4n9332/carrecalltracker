Buying a used 2021 GMC Sierra 3500? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 20 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Two critical recalls define this year: a brake module fire risk that came with a park-outside advisory until repaired, and diesels whose rear wheels can lock. Seat belts appear twice, as an unsecured center bracket and as wrong bolts, alongside a tailgate that can unlatch by itself. Complaint activity is modest at 20 unverified NHTSA reports with no crashes, fires or injuries recorded; power train, brakes and electrical are the main areas.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get written confirmation the brake module fire recall was repaired before garaging the truck
  3. Verify the diesel rear-wheel-lock recall on the VIN
  4. Ask about both seat belt recalls and inspect the center belt bracket and anchor bolts
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
