Buying a used 2018 GMC Sierra 3500? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 14 owner complaints on file

Three critical recalls stand out: a fuel tank leak risk specific to 3500 models, and two actions on the diesel block heater cord that can short and burn. Dual fuel tanks can also stall the engine, a crew cab roof airbag can burst, and front seat belts were found faulty. Complaint volume is low at 14 unverified NHTSA reports, with no crashes, fires or injuries logged; brakes lead with six, then steering and electrical.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof the 3500 fuel tank leak recall was completed and look and smell for fuel seepage underneath
  3. Confirm the block heater cord recalls were done and inspect the cord end for damage
  4. On a crew cab, verify the roof airbag recall and ask about the front seat belt recall
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
