Buying a used 2026 GMC Sierra 3500? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

This truck is new enough that its record is thin. One high-severity recall covers Silverado 3500 and Sierra 3500 trucks that can stall; a second, low-severity one is administrative — vehicles missing the electronic owner's manual download. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Only four unverified owner complaints are on file with NHTSA, with no crashes, fires or injuries, spread across wheels, power train, service brakes and hydraulic.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the stalling recall repair has been done, and whether the truck has ever stalled
  3. Confirm the electronic owner's manual download is available in the vehicle
  4. Inspect wheels and tires and check brake feel, the few areas owners have reported
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
