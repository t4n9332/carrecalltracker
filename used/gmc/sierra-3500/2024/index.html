Buying a used 2024 GMC Sierra 3500? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 44 owner complaints on file

The standout is a critical recall covering Silverado and Sierra HD trucks that can lose steering; neither it nor the two tailgate recalls — both for a tailgate that can unlatch on its own — carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. NHTSA has 44 owner complaints on file, which are unverified reports: no crashes, one fire and one injury, clustered in power train (12), electrical system (12), service brakes (9) and engine (6).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get the VIN checked for the HD steering-loss recall and confirm the repair was completed
  3. Confirm both tailgate unlatch recalls were performed, and test the tailgate latch yourself
  4. Test-drive for power train, electrical and brake behavior, the top complaint areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
