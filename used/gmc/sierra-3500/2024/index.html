Buying a used 2024 GMC Sierra 3500? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 44 owner complaints on file

This model year has a critical recall for a steering gear that may fracture, along with two separate tailgate recalls covering unlatching while driving. NHTSA has logged 44 owner complaints, with 1 fire and 1 injury reported and no crashes. Power train and electrical system are tied as the top complaint categories at 12 each, followed by service brakes (9) and engine (6). These complaint counts are unverified owner reports, not confirmed defect findings.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering gear recall was completed
  3. Check that both tailgate recalls were performed and test the latch
  4. Ask about the cause of the reported fire and any repair records
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
