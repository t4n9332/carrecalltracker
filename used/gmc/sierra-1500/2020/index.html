Buying a used 2020 GMC Sierra 1500? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 461 owner complaints on file

This year carries three critical recalls — seat belt fire risk, a battery cable connection issue, and over-cured tire failure risk — along with several high-severity recalls covering a fuel pump over-pressurization fire risk, brake caliper bolts, roof-rail airbag underperformance, driveshaft separation, seat belt brackets, and rear wheel lock-up on diesel trucks. NHTSA logged 461 owner complaints, including 12 crashes, 5 fires, and 13 injuries (unverified). Engine complaints lead at 229, followed by power train at 95, electrical system at 78, and service brakes at 53.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seat belt fire risk and battery cable recalls were completed
  3. Ask if the over-cured tire recall replacement was done
  4. Ask about fuel pump fire risk recall completion
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
