Buying a used 2022 GMC Sierra 1500? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 579 owner complaints on file

Recalls for this year include rear wheel lock-up specific to diesel trucks, an engine that may fail, and transmission-related wheel lockup risk (all high severity), plus medium-severity recalls for a sport bar brake light that may fail, daytime running lights that stay on, and a chrome grille deflector that may detach. NHTSA logged 579 owner complaints, including 10 crashes, 5 fires, and 3 injuries (unverified). Engine complaints lead at 234, followed by power train at 107, structure at 99, and electrical system at 81.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if the engine failure recall remedy was applied
  3. Confirm the transmission wheel lockup recall was addressed
  4. Ask about the rear wheel lock-up (diesel) recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
