Buying a used 2019 GMC Sierra 1500? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 637 owner complaints on file

This year carries a heavy recall load, including three critical campaigns — seat belt fire risk, a battery cable connection issue, and over-cured tire failure risk — plus multiple high-severity recalls covering airbag failure, stability control loss, brake assist loss, driveshaft separation, seat belt brackets, and tire failure, along with a medium-severity spare tire/ABS issue. NHTSA logged 637 owner complaints, including 12 crashes, 3 fires, and 11 injuries (unverified). Engine complaints lead at 299, followed by power train at 136, service brakes at 115, and electrical system at 94.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seat belt fire risk recall was completed
  3. Ask if the battery cable connection recall was addressed
  4. Confirm the over-cured tire recall replacement was done
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
