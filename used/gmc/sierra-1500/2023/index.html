Buying a used 2023 GMC Sierra 1500? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 468 owner complaints on file

This model year carries three recalls: a brake warning light that may fail to alert, a possible engine failure, and daytime running lights that can stay on continuously. None are listed as stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA has logged 466 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this vehicle, including 5 crashes, 2 fires, and 2 injuries. Engine-related complaints lead at 203, followed by power train (91), structure (78), and electrical system (61).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake warning light recall has been completed by a dealer
  3. Ask about the engine failure recall status and any related repair records
  4. Check daytime running light behavior and whether that recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
