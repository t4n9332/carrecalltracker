Buying a used 2020 GMC Yukon XL? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 46 owner complaints on file

Two recalls, both worth tracing on a specific truck: a critical fuel pump leak with fire risk across GM trucks and SUVs, and a high-severity recall for defective Continental tires. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. NHTSA lists 46 unverified owner complaints — 2 crashes, 1 fire, 1 injury — and they tilt heavily toward the engine (24) and power train (12), with service brakes and an unknown-or-other category at 3 each.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN to confirm the fuel pump recall repair and check for fuel odor or leaks
  3. Identify the tire brand and whether the Continental tires were replaced under recall
  4. Request engine and drivetrain service records given the complaint concentration there
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
