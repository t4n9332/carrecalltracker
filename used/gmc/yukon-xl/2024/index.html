Buying a used 2024 GMC Yukon XL? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 69 owner complaints on file

The shortest recall list of these Yukon XL years: a missing brake fluid level warning and possible 6.2L V8 engine failure, both high severity but neither with a stop-driving or park-outside advisory. Owner complaint volume is lower too, at 69 unverified reports to NHTSA with no crashes, fires, or injuries noted. Engine accounts for 52 of those reports, well ahead of electrical and powertrain, so engine behavior is the theme worth probing.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check the VIN for open status on the brake fluid warning and 6.2L V8 engine recalls
  3. Ask whether the engine has ever been repaired, replaced, or diagnosed under warranty
  4. Confirm remaining powertrain warranty coverage given the engine failure recall
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
