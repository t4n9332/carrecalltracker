Buying a used 2021 GMC Yukon XL? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 219 owner complaints on file

This model year has ten open recall campaigns, covering seat and seat-belt bolts and brackets, third-row seat belt buckle rivets, a power steering software issue, a fuel pump that may stall the engine, a driveshaft that may fail, and daytime running lights that may not turn off. NHTSA has logged 219 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 2 crashes, 1 fire, and 5 injuries. Engine complaints dominate at 134, followed by power train (47), fuel/propulsion system (27), and engine cooling (21).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm which of the ten recalls have been completed, especially the seat belt/seat bolt ones
  3. Ask about the power steering software update status
  4. Ask about engine performance and any repair history, given the high engine complaint count
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
