Buying a used 2021 GMC Yukon XL? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 219 owner complaints on file

This model year carries an unusually long recall list, including four separate seat belt actions — loose front seat bolts, an unsecured center belt bracket, wrong seat belt bolts, and third-row belts that may break. Others cover loss of power steering assist, a fuel pump that can stall the engine, a rear driveshaft that may break, 6.2L V8 engine failure, and a critical diesel rear-wheel lock-up issue. Engine dominates the 219 unverified NHTSA owner complaints, with 134 filed there.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN through NHTSA to confirm all four seat belt recalls were completed
  3. Confirm which engine it has — the 6.2L V8 and the diesel each have their own recall
  4. Ask for engine and fuel pump repair records, the largest complaint areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
