Buying a used 2021 GMC Acadia? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 149 owner complaints on file

Only two recalls apply: a critical fuel leak risk shared with the Cadillac XT5 and XT6, and a rearview camera that can fail. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. NHTSA lists 149 unverified owner complaints, including 7 crashes and 3 injuries but no fires. Electrical system leads at 38 reports, then engine at 29 and fuel/propulsion system at 24 — that last group overlaps with the subject of the fuel recall.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel leak recall repair is documented for this VIN
  3. Test the rearview camera several times, including after the vehicle has sat
  4. Ask about fuel smells, fuel gauge behavior, or hard starting
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
