Buying a used 2020 GMC Acadia? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 100 owner complaints on file

This model year carries six recalls, including two critical items (missing transmission bolts with fire risk, and over-cured tires), plus high-severity recalls for a roof-rail air bag, a fuel pump blockage that can stall the engine, overcured tires again, and a backup camera issue. NHTSA has 100 owner complaints on file, unverified reports, with 4 crashes and 2 injuries. Electrical system leads with 27 complaints, followed by engine at 26, power train at 21, and steering at 18.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all six recalls, including transmission bolts, tires, airbag, fuel pump, and camera, were completed
  3. Ask about steering system repair or complaint history
  4. Check current tire condition and production date codes
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
