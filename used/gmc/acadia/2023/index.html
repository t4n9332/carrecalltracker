Buying a used 2023 GMC Acadia? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 42 owner complaints on file

No critical recalls on this year, but three high-severity ones: an axle shaft that can separate, a faulty transmission gear that can cause power loss, and a windshield bonding problem, plus a low-severity unreadable tire label. None carry stop-driving or park-outside instructions. NHTSA holds 42 unverified owner complaints, including 3 crashes, 1 fire, and 4 injuries, split mainly between engine and power train at 12 reports each.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the windshield has been replaced or re-bonded under the recall
  3. Listen and feel for driveline clunks, shudder, or power loss on a test drive
  4. Check the tire and load label is legible and matches the fitted tires
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
