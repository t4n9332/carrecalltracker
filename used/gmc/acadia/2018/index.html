Buying a used 2018 GMC Acadia? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 393 owner complaints on file

Two recalls: a critical fuel pump leak with fire risk and a driveshaft that can break. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. What stands out is volume — 393 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA, including 8 crashes, 1 fire, and 12 injuries. Power train dominates at 163 reports, with electrical system at 108 and engine at 33. That powertrain concentration is the defining pattern of this model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify both the fuel pump and driveshaft recalls were completed on this VIN
  3. Drive long enough to feel shift quality, hesitation, or driveline vibration
  4. Ask for service records covering transmission and electrical repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
