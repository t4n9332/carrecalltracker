Buying a used 2018 GMC Sierra 2500? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 35 owner complaints on file

Five recalls on a much lower-volume truck. Two critical ones both involve the diesel block heater cord shorting and catching fire, neither flagged park-outside. High-severity items cover dual fuel tanks that can stall the engine and a crew cab roof airbag that can burst; faulty front seat belts on HD trucks is medium. Only 35 unverified complaints were filed with NHTSA, led by engine at 8 and electrical at 7, including 2 fire reports and no crashes or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. If diesel, confirm both block heater cord recalls were completed and inspect the cord
  3. Ask whether the dual fuel tank system has ever caused stalling
  4. Verify the roof airbag and front seat belt recalls against this VIN
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
