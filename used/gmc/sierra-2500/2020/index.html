Buying a used 2020 GMC Sierra 2500? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 74 owner complaints on file

This model year has nine recalls, including a critical seat belt fire risk, two separate hood-may-open-while-driving recalls, a roof-rail air bag underperformance issue, a seat belt bracket problem, a spare tire that can disable ABS, a tailgate that may unlatch while driving, and two rear-wheel lockup issues tied to diesel trucks and transmissions. None require stopping driving or parking outside. NHTSA has 74 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 1 crash, 3 fires, and 1 injury; power train complaints lead at 22, followed by unknown/other (16), electrical system (13), and service brakes (12).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seat belt fire-risk recall and hood-latch recalls were completed
  3. Ask whether the rear-wheel lockup recall (diesel/transmission related) was addressed
  4. Check spare tire and ABS status given that recall, plus tailgate latch operation
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any GMC dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
