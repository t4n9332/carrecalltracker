Buying a used 2021 Volkswagen ID.4? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 332 owner complaints on file

The launch year carries ten recalls, including a steering gear that can cause loss of steering, battery wiring that may cause a stall, loss of drive power, doors that may open on their own, blank or restarting dashboard screens, a missing gear-position display with rollaway risk, and two child-seat belt issues. Complaint volume is high: 332 unverified NHTSA reports, 177 of them electrical, with 27 crashes, 22 injuries and one fire recorded on this model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for a VIN recall report showing the steering gear, battery wiring and drive-power recalls were completed.
  3. Cycle the screens and gear-position display repeatedly, and confirm all doors latch and stay shut.
  4. If carrying kids, test the rear belts locking a child seat, and note any braking or collision-warning oddities.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
