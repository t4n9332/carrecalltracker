Buying a used 2022 Volkswagen ID.4? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 135 owner complaints on file

This model year's recalls center on software issues: a display bug, a gear-position display problem, and a rearview camera that may go blank, freeze, or be delayed, covered by several separate recall notices, along with doors that may open while driving. NHTSA has logged 135 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 15 crash reports, 1 fire report, and 10 injury reports. Electrical system is the most-cited category at 55, followed by an unspecified 'unknown or other' category at 31 and forward collision avoidance at 26.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rearview camera and display software recalls were completed
  3. Test the rearview camera and gear-position display for correct operation
  4. Ask about the door latch recall given reports of doors opening while driving
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
