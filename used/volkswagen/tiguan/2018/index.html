Buying a used 2018 Volkswagen Tiguan? Check these 13 recalls first

13 NHTSA recalls · 282 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

This 2018 Tiguan has ten open recalls, the most notable being a critical loose ball joint nut recall marked stop-driving until repaired, along with a critical sunroof light fire-risk recall. Other recalls cover airbags, engine support bolts, a rear shock absorber weld, seat belt webbing, roof repair, and rear spoiler detachment. NHTSA has received 282 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, including 5 crashes, 3 fires, and 11 injuries. Engine leads complaint categories at 80, followed by unknown/other (50), power train (49), and electrical system (43).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the stop-driving ball joint nut recall has been completed
  3. Confirm the sunroof fire-risk recall repair status
  4. Ask for service records covering the airbag and seat belt recalls
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
