Buying a used 2019 Volkswagen Beetle? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 14 owner complaints on file

This 2019 Beetle carries four recalls: a critical driver airbag inflator that may explode, a brake master cylinder that may leak, an instrument cluster that may not warn of brake trouble, and a key that can be removed without shifting to Park. NHTSA has logged 14 unverified owner complaints for this model year, including 1 crash and no reported fires or injuries. The most cited complaint areas are electrical system (4), unknown or other (2), service brakes (2), and engine (2).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the driver airbag inflator recall has been completed
  3. Ask whether the brake master cylinder leak recall was performed
  4. Verify the key-removable-not-in-Park and instrument-cluster brake-warning recalls are closed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
