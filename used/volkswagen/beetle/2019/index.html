Buying a used 2019 Volkswagen Beetle? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 14 owner complaints on file

Four recalls on the last Beetle model year, one critical: a driver's airbag that can explode and throw metal fragments. The rest are a key that can come out when the car is not in Park, a leaking Beck/Arnley aftermarket brake part, and a fault where the car may not display a brake failure warning. Only 14 unverified NHTSA complaints were filed, spread thinly across electrical, brakes and engine, with 1 crash and no fires or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the driver's airbag inflator replacement was done on this VIN
  3. Ask whether the brake warning display recall software update was applied
  4. Check whether any aftermarket Beck/Arnley brake parts were fitted and inspect for leaks
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
