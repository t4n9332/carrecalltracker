Buying a used 2025 Volkswagen Taos? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 10 owner complaints on file

Two open recalls on this model year: a fuel sensor that can come loose in a crash, rated critical, and a blank instrument panel issue shared with the Jetta. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. With 10 complaints filed with NHTSA — unverified owner reports — the record is thin for a recent model year: one crash, no fires or injuries. Electrical system leads those reports, followed by engine, brakes, and forward collision avoidance.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN and confirm both the fuel sensor and instrument panel recalls are closed.
  3. Watch the instrument cluster through a full startup and drive — speed, fuel, warning lights.
  4. Test the forward collision warning and the brakes; both show up in owner reports.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
