Buying a used 2018 Volkswagen Passat? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 45 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

Braking dominates this year. One recall for brake calipers that can reduce braking power carries a stop-driving warning; a second covers caliper bolts that may be loose, shared with the Atlas. Lesser items are headlight aim that isn't locked and internal-use cars sold without safety standard confirmation. NHTSA lists 45 unverified owner complaints, service brakes the largest area at 12, followed by engine and electrical, with 5 crashes and 2 injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the stop-driving brake caliper recall was completed on this VIN.
  3. Ask whether the caliper bolt recall was done, and test pedal feel and stopping.
  4. Ask whether the car was a Volkswagen internal or company vehicle.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
