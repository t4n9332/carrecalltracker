Buying a used 2019 Volkswagen Jetta? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 547 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

The heaviest year in this group. One recall for front wheel bearings that may break carries a stop-driving warning. Others cover fuel leak risk, rear coil springs that can break across two separate recalls, a possibly wrong driver airbag, ignition switch failure, and cars bought back over compliance. NHTSA holds 547 unverified owner complaints, led by power train at 162, electrical 153 and engine 147, with 19 crashes, 3 fires and 36 injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the stop-driving front wheel bearing recall was completed on this VIN.
  3. Ask about past transmission and engine repairs, the two largest complaint areas.
  4. Confirm the driver airbag recall and both rear coil spring recalls.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
