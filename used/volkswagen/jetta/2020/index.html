Buying a used 2020 Volkswagen Jetta? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 52 owner complaints on file

Two critical recalls stand out: airbags that may deploy improperly or unexpectedly, and a fuel leak risk shared with the Golf and Audi A3. Front brake caliper bolts that may loosen and a failure-prone ignition switch follow, plus weaker front seat backrests and delayed tire pressure warnings. The complaint record is modest for the year, with 52 unverified owner reports led by electrical, engine and service brakes, and one crash and two injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the airbag deployment and fuel leak recalls were both completed.
  3. Ask whether the front brake caliper bolt recall was performed.
  4. Test the ignition switch and note any stalling or electrical dropouts.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
