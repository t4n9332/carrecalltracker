Buying a used 2018 Volkswagen Jetta? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 31 owner complaints on file

A light recall year by Jetta standards. The one critical item is fuel rail bolts that can loosen and leak; the others cover a key that can be removed when the car isn't in Park, and an aftermarket Beck/Arnley brake part that leaks, which matters only if that part was ever fitted. NHTSA lists 31 unverified owner complaints, spread across fuel/propulsion, air bags and power train, with one crash and no fires or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for service records showing the fuel rail bolt recall was done.
  3. Smell and look for fuel seepage around the engine's fuel rail.
  4. Ask whether any aftermarket Beck/Arnley brake parts were installed.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
