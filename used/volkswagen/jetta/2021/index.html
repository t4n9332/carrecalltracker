Buying a used 2021 Volkswagen Jetta? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 67 owner complaints on file

This model year's recalls involve an incorrectly installed airbag module, missing front seat bolts, and a rearview camera that may fail to display, freeze, or fail. NHTSA has logged 67 owner complaints for this year (unverified reports), including 3 crash reports and 1 injury report, with no fires reported. Service brakes are the most-cited category at 25, followed by electrical system at 13, an unspecified 'unknown or other' category at 11, and fuel/propulsion system at 9.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the airbag module and seat bolt recalls were completed
  3. Test the rearview camera for reliable display
  4. Ask about any service brake issues, the top complaint category for this year
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
