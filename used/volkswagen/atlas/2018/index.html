Buying a used 2018 Volkswagen Atlas? Check these 16 recalls first

16 NHTSA recalls · 461 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

The Atlas's first model year has the longest and most serious record here: two fuel tank leak recalls, airbags that may deploy on their own, and a brake caliper recall carrying a do-not-drive instruction. Rear coil springs that can break, tires that may fail at the sidewall, and a miswired airbag sensor add to it. Owner complaints are high — 461 unverified reports with 8 crashes and 18 injuries — spread across electrical (110), air bags (100), engine (97) and brakes (75).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN for the do-not-drive brake caliper recall and both fuel tank recalls.
  3. Ask whether the airbag sensor and passenger airbag recalls were done; check the airbag light.
  4. Inspect rear coil springs and tire sidewalls for cracks or damage.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
