Buying a used 2022 Volkswagen Atlas? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 181 owner complaints on file

Seven recalls, three critical: fuel injectors missing filters, engine bearings that can fail (shared with the Audi Q5), and rear suspension bolts that may loosen and fall out. Airbag campaigns cover a side airbag deploying too late and a passenger airbag switching off wrongly; the backup camera can also go blank. Owners filed 181 unverified NHTSA complaints, led by engine (50), service brakes (47), electrical (44) and air bags (30), with 3 crashes, 2 fires and 2 injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine bearing recall inspection or repair was performed on this VIN
  3. Ask whether rear suspension and trailer hitch bolts were checked, especially if it towed
  4. Check the passenger airbag status light and that the backup camera image appears
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
