Buying a used 2019 Volkswagen Atlas? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 473 owner complaints on file

This model year has a critical recall for a fuel tank defect that risks leaks and stalling, plus several airbag-related recalls: a wrong-side airbag installation, side airbag deployment delay, a passenger airbag that can deactivate, and a side airbag that may detach in a crash. Additional recalls cover a rear coil spring fracture risk and tire sidewall defects. NHTSA has logged 473 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 7 crashes and 10 injuries; air bags account for 141 of them, the largest category, followed by service brakes (76).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel tank defect recall and multiple airbag recalls are completed
  3. Ask about coil spring fracture and tire sidewall defect repairs
  4. Test forward collision avoidance and brake system function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
