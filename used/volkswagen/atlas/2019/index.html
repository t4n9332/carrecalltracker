Buying a used 2019 Volkswagen Atlas? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 473 owner complaints on file

Airbags dominate this year: four separate recalls cover wrong side airbags, a missing weld in the driver airbag, late side deployment, and a passenger airbag that may switch off. The single critical item is a fuel tank that may leak gasoline. Three low-severity recalls — missing key chime, headlight aim, a missing label — are routine. Air bags also top the 473 unverified owner complaints with 141 reports, ahead of brakes (76), electrical (73) and forward collision avoidance (72); 7 crashes, 10 injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all four airbag recalls are closed on this VIN and no airbag light stays on.
  3. Ask for proof the fuel tank recall was completed; check for any fuel odor.
  4. Inspect rear coil springs and tire sidewalls; ask about forward collision false alerts.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
