Buying a used 2025 Volkswagen Atlas? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 46 owner complaints on file

Five recalls on this newest year, two critical: a transmission that may quit without warning and an engine cover that can melt and cause fire. The others are loose bolts shared with the ID.4 and two rearview camera image recalls. Owners filed 46 unverified NHTSA complaints, the fewest here, though that partly reflects how recently these cars reached buyers; top areas are electrical system (16), power train (12) and engine (7), with 1 crash and no fires or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the transmission recall remedy has been applied and if power loss ever occurred
  3. Confirm the engine cover recall is closed and look for heat damage under the hood
  4. Test the backup camera and ask about any warning lights or electrical glitches
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
