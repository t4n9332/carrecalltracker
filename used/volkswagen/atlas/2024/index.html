Buying a used 2024 Volkswagen Atlas? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 168 owner complaints on file

Eight recalls with three critical ones: engine bearings that may fail with fire risk, brakes that can lose function from a bad part, and an engine cover that can melt and cause fire. Three separate backup camera recalls cover blank, distorted and missing images. Owners filed 168 unverified NHTSA complaints, led by electrical system (43), service brakes (35) and engine (27), with 6 crashes, 2 fires and 10 injuries reported — fire and brake themes that echo the recalls.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both fire-related recalls, engine bearings and engine cover, are closed on this VIN
  3. Verify the brake component recall repair and test pedal feel and stopping from speed
  4. Shift into reverse and check the rearview camera image appears and is undistorted
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
