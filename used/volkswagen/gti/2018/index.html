Buying a used 2018 Volkswagen GTI? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 16 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

Three recalls: brake calipers that can reduce braking power, which carried a do-not-drive warning until fixed; a fuel leak risk; and a key that can be removed with the car not in Park. Notably, service brakes is also the largest owner complaint area — 6 of 16 unverified reports filed with NHTSA for this year, which logged two crashes and two injuries overall. Brake condition and proof of recall completion are the natural focus here.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Require written confirmation the brake caliper recall was completed before driving it far.
  3. Test braking hard from speed and note pedal feel, pull or vibration; ask when pads and fluid were last done.
  4. Ask for the fuel leak recall record and try removing the key with the shifter out of Park.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
