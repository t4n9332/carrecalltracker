Buying a used 2018 Volkswagen Golf R? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

No critical recalls, but a high-severity fuel line that may leak in the engine bay is the one that stands out. Two of the four campaigns are paperwork-related: certain cars, including internal-use vehicles, were sold without safety verification or confirmation they met safety standards. Rear head restraints may also loosen. Only 9 unverified NHTSA complaints were filed, mostly power train (3) and service brakes (3), with no crashes, fires or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof the engine bay fuel line recall was inspected or repaired
  3. Check whether this car falls under the sold-without-safety-verification recalls
  4. Tug the rear head restraints to confirm they lock firmly in place
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
