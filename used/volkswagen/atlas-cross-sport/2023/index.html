Buying a used 2023 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 18 owner complaints on file

Six recalls, two of them critical: engine bearings that can fail and rear suspension bolts that may loosen and fall out. The rest are high-severity but narrower — brake lines that may leak fluid, a side airbag that may deploy late, a passenger airbag that may switch off wrongly, and loose bolts shared with the ID.4. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Complaint volume is low: 18 unverified owner reports with one crash, concentrated in engine (6) and air bags (5).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for recall records on the engine bearing repair and any related engine work.
  3. Have the brake lines inspected for leaks and check fluid level and condition.
  4. Confirm the rear suspension and ID.4-shared loose-bolt inspections were done.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
