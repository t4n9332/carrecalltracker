Buying a used 2022 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 70 owner complaints on file

The critical items here are mechanical rather than steering-related: fuel injectors missing filters that can leak fuel, engine bearings that can fail, and rear suspension bolts that may loosen and fall out. Two airbag recalls — late side deployment and a passenger airbag switching off — plus loose trailer hitch bolts follow; a blank backup camera is the minor one. Complaints are lighter: 70 unverified reports, one crash and one fire, led by brakes (23), electrical (19) and engine (18).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine bearing and fuel injector recalls were performed; listen for engine noise.
  3. Have rear suspension bolts and any trailer hitch bolts checked for torque.
  4. Test the backup camera and the brakes — brakes top the unverified complaints.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
