Buying a used 2025 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 20 owner complaints on file

A short list for a recent model year: two critical recalls — a transmission that may quit without warning and an engine cover that can melt and cause fire — plus loose bolts shared with the ID.4 and two rearview camera image recalls. No recall carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. With only 20 unverified owner complaints and no reported crashes, fires or injuries, the record is thin so far; electrical (11) and power train (6) are the areas named most.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the transmission recall remedy was applied; drive long enough to feel shifts.
  3. Confirm the engine cover recall was completed and inspect the cover for heat damage.
  4. Check the rearview camera on multiple starts and ask about the loose-bolt inspection.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
