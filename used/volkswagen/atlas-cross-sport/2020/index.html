Buying a used 2020 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 106 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

This 2020 Atlas Cross Sport has ten recalls, including one stop-driving recall for a steering knuckle that may fail, plus a second knuckle-fracture recall and a Continental tire failure risk. Several recalls cover airbags, including deployment delay and a passenger airbag that can deactivate. Lower-severity recalls address a seat frame weld, headlight aim, and tire pressure monitor software. NHTSA has 106 owner complaints on file (unverified); top categories are electrical system at 33, air bags at 23, an unknown/other bucket also at 23, and service brakes at 18.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm steering knuckle recalls completed
  3. Confirm all airbag recalls completed
  4. Ask about tire condition/replacement
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
