Buying a used 2020 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 106 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

Ten recalls, and the one that stands out is a critical steering component that may break — it carries a do-not-drive instruction until repaired. A second critical item involves a water leak that can set off an airbag. Several more concern airbags: late side deployment and a passenger airbag that may switch off wrongly. Routine by comparison: headlight aim and a missing weight label. Of 106 unverified owner complaints, electrical (33) and air bags (23) lead, with 3 crashes and 1 fire.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN and confirm the do-not-drive steering recall repair is on record.
  3. Ask for proof of the airbag recall work, including the water leak and passenger airbag shutoff.
  4. Inspect tires for tread separation and check front steering for play.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
