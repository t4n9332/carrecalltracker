Buying a used 2021 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 165 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

This 2021 Atlas Cross Sport has ten recalls, including three critical-severity items: a stop-driving steering knuckle failure recall, a relay defect fire risk, and a fuel injector fire risk. Other high-severity recalls cover a second steering knuckle fracture, a tire failure risk, wheels that may separate, loosening front wheel bolts, and airbag deployment delay and shutoff problems. NHTSA has 165 owner complaints on file (unverified): 9 crashes, 1 fire, 7 injuries. Top categories are electrical system at 54, air bags at 37, service brakes at 36, and engine at 33.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the three critical fire-risk/steering recalls completed
  3. Confirm stop-driving steering knuckle recall completed
  4. Ask about wheel bolt inspection history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Volkswagen dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
