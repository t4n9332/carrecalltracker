Buying a used 2024 Tesla Model 3? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 216 owner complaints on file

This model year has 5 recalls: a side airbag missing a deflector part, a hood latch that may not detect an unlatched hood, possible loss of the backup camera, a tire pressure warning light fault, and vehicles reported missing required safety features. NHTSA logged 216 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 25 crashes, 1 fire, and 19 injuries. Top complaint categories are unknown or other (70), forward collision avoidance (48), electrical system (44), and steering (36).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the missing-safety-features and side airbag deflector recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the backup camera and tire pressure warning light both work
  4. Check the hood latch recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Tesla dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
