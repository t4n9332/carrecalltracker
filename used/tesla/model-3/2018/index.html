Buying a used 2018 Tesla Model 3? Check these 19 recalls first

19 NHTSA recalls · 1,015 owner complaints on file

This 2018 Model 3 has ten recalls, nine rated high severity, covering seat belt fasteners and reassembly, rearview camera cable damage and display delay, a boombox feature that can mask pedestrian warning sounds, Autosteer and Full Self-Driving Beta behavior at intersections, front suspension fasteners that may loosen, and headlights rated too bright, plus one medium-severity automatic braking bug. NHTSA has 1015 owner complaints on file (unverified reports), including 102 crashes, 44 injuries, and 4 fires; top categories are forward collision avoidance (202), suspension (182), and electrical system (168).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm which of the nine high-severity recalls (seat belts, suspension fasteners, camera, Autosteer/FSD) were completed
  3. Ask about suspension repairs given the high suspension complaint volume
  4. Test rearview camera display and Autosteer/FSD Beta behavior if equipped
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Tesla dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
