Buying a used 2023 Tesla Model 3? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 424 owner complaints on file

This model year has 10 recalls: a possibly twisted side curtain airbag, taillight failure, a battery disconnect that may fail, a hood latch that may not detect an unlatched hood, headlights described as too bright, undersized warning lights, a seat belt warning chime that may not activate, a tire pressure warning light fault, plus Full Self-Driving Beta and Autosteer misuse recalls. NHTSA logged 423 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 72 crashes, 1 fire, and 49 injuries. Top complaint categories are forward collision avoidance (177), steering (87), vehicle speed control (68), and service brakes (57).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the battery disconnect, taillight, and hood latch recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the tire pressure warning light and seat belt chime function correctly
  4. Check the side curtain airbag recall status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Tesla dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
