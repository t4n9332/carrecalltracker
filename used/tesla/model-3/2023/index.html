Buying a used 2023 Tesla Model 3? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 422 owner complaints on file

The 2023 Model 3 carries a long recall list with driver assistance at its center: separate actions cover Full Self-Driving Beta breaking traffic laws and Autosteer controls that may not prevent driver misuse. Other high-severity items are a twisted side curtain airbag, a post-crash battery cutoff that may fail, and a hood latch warning that fails. Its 422 NHTSA complaints are unverified owner reports and cluster in forward collision avoidance (177) and steering (86) — the same systems. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get VIN-level proof the side curtain airbag, battery cutoff and hood latch recalls were completed.
  3. Ask which driver-assistance software recalls (FSD Beta, Autosteer) have been applied to this car.
  4. On the test drive check taillights, backup camera lag, headlight glare and the tire pressure warning light.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Tesla dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
