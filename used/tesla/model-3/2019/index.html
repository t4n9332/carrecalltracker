Buying a used 2019 Tesla Model 3? Check these 22 recalls first

22 NHTSA recalls · 611 owner complaints on file

This model year carries 10 recalls, several tied to physical fasteners: seat belt fastener issues, loose brake caliper bolts, front and general suspension fastener problems, plus a possibly damaged rearview camera cable and reassembled rear seat belts. Software-related recalls cover the Boombox pedestrian-sound feature, camera display delay, and unsafe Full Self-Driving Beta/Autosteer behavior. NHTSA logged 611 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 58 crashes, 5 fires, and 27 injuries. Top complaint categories are forward collision avoidance (168), electrical system (91), air bags (83), and suspension (82).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the seat belt fastener and brake caliper/suspension fastener recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the rearview camera displays without delay and the cable shows no damage
  4. Check suspension components for looseness given the fastener-related recalls
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Tesla dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
