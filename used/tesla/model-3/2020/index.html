Buying a used 2020 Tesla Model 3? Check these 21 recalls first

21 NHTSA recalls · 450 owner complaints on file

Alongside the carryover seat belt, suspension bolt, caliper bolt, unexpected braking and FSD Beta campaigns, this year adds a high-severity recall for a side curtain airbag that may be twisted. NHTSA shows 450 unverified complaints with 54 crashes, 2 fires and 33 injuries. Forward collision avoidance is by far the biggest reported area, followed by vehicle speed control and service brakes — braking and driver-assist behavior recur across both the recalls and the reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the side curtain airbag recall shows as completed for this VIN.
  3. Confirm the caliper bolt and front suspension bolt repairs were done, and have the fasteners inspected.
  4. Ask about sudden unprompted braking and brake feel, given speed control and service brakes rank in the top reported areas.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Tesla dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
