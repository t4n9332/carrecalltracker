Buying a used 2020 Tesla Model 3? Check these 21 recalls first

21 NHTSA recalls · 450 owner complaints on file

This model year has 10 recalls, including seat belt fastener issues, loose brake caliper bolts, a possibly twisted side curtain airbag, suspension fastener problems, and a damaged rearview camera cable. Software recalls address the Boombox pedestrian-sound feature, camera display delay, reassembled rear seat belts, and unsafe Full Self-Driving Beta/Autosteer behavior. NHTSA logged 450 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 54 crashes, 2 fires, and 33 injuries. Top complaint categories are forward collision avoidance (156), unknown or other (81), vehicle speed control (69), and service brakes (48).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the side curtain airbag and brake caliper/suspension fastener recalls were completed
  3. Confirm seat belt fasteners and rear seat belt assembly were inspected under recall
  4. Test the rearview camera for display delay or cable damage
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Tesla dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
