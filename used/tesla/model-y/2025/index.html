Buying a used 2025 Tesla Model Y? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 98 owner complaints on file

The 2025 Model Y has a light recall record so far: a tire pressure warning light fault, possible loss of the backup camera, and a missing weight certification label. Nothing rated high or critical, and no stop-driving or park-outside advisories. Complaints number 98 — unverified owner reports — with 30 crashes, 3 fires and 13 injuries described, concentrated in forward collision avoidance (45) and lane departure (19). Being a recent model year, the record is still short and may grow.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the backup camera and tire pressure warning light both function normally.
  3. Look for the weight certification label in the driver's door jamb.
  4. Test automatic emergency braking and lane-keeping behavior on a highway stretch.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Tesla dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
