Buying a used 2025 Tesla Model Y? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 98 owner complaints on file

This model year has 3 recalls: possible loss of the backup camera, a tire pressure warning light fault, and a missing vehicle certification label. NHTSA logged 98 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 30 crashes, 3 fires, and 13 injuries. Top complaint categories are forward collision avoidance (45), unknown or other (23), lane departure (19), and electrical system (17).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the backup camera and tire pressure warning light recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the vehicle certification label is present
  4. Ask the seller about any forward collision or lane departure warning issues experienced
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Tesla dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
