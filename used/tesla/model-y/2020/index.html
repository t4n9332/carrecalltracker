Buying a used 2020 Tesla Model Y? Check these 24 recalls first

24 NHTSA recalls · 276 owner complaints on file

The 2020 Model Y's record centers on fasteners and suspension. One critical recall covers loose bolts that can let a suspension arm detach, alongside separate actions for suspension parts that can break apart, front suspension bolts and brake caliper bolts loosening, and two seat belt attachment recalls. Also listed: unprompted braking, the FSD Beta traffic-law recall and a hood latch warning failure. Its 276 unverified complaints (47 crashes, 25 injuries reported) lead with forward collision avoidance, speed control and service brakes.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for paperwork showing the suspension link, suspension bolt and caliper bolt recalls were performed.
  3. Have a shop confirm front and rear seat belt anchor fasteners are secure.
  4. Listen for clunks over bumps, and test trailer brake lights if a tow package is fitted.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Tesla dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
