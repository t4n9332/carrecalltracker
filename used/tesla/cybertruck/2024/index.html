Buying a used 2024 Tesla Cybertruck? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 170 owner complaints on file

This 2024 Cybertruck has been subject to ten recalls, including four high-severity issues covering a trapped accelerator pedal, a windshield wiper motor that may fail, a drive inverter fault that cuts power, and a wheel stud that may separate, plus several medium and low-severity items like tire pressure warnings and a detachable light bar. NHTSA has 170 owner complaints on file (unverified reports), including 22 crashes, 19 injuries, and 2 fires; the leading categories are electrical system (53) and structure (36).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm high-severity recalls (accelerator pedal, wiper motor, drive inverter, wheel stud) are completed
  3. Ask about any structural body panel or electrical system issues
  4. Verify tire pressure warning light and light bar attachment are functioning correctly
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Tesla dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
