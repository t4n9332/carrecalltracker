Buying a used 2021 Chrysler Pacifica? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 368 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This 2021 Chrysler Pacifica has 7 open recalls, including a critical hybrid battery pack fire risk recall advising owners to park outside, a wiring fault that can stall the engine, a rearview camera display issue, side curtain air bag and second-row seat latch defects, and two separate wheelchair restraint recalls; none require stop-driving. NHTSA has logged 368 owner complaints for this model year, unverified reports, with 11 crash, 1 fire, and 11 injury reports. Complaint areas are led by electrical system (149), engine (140), powertrain (119), and fuel/propulsion system (75).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. For hybrid models, confirm the battery pack fire recall repair is completed
  3. Verify the second-row seat latch and side curtain air bag recalls were addressed
  4. Ask whether the vehicle has been parked outside per the recall advisory
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
