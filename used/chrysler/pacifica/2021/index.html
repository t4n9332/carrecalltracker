Buying a used 2021 Chrysler Pacifica? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 368 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This year carries the critical plug-in hybrid battery fire recall, with NHTSA advising owners park outside until repaired. Alongside it: hybrid shutdown while driving, side curtain airbags that may not deploy, and second-row seats that may not lock — worth attention on a family van. Complaint volume is the highest of the Pacifica years here, 368 unverified reports, concentrated in electrical system (149), engine (140), power train (119) and fuel/propulsion (75), with 11 crashes, one fire and 11 injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Latch and tug each second-row seat, and check the seat-lock recall status by VIN
  3. On a Hybrid, verify both the battery fire recall and the shutdown-while-driving recall are closed
  4. Ask about stalling, warning lights or charging faults, given the heavy engine and fuel/propulsion complaint volume
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
