Buying a used 2025 Chrysler Pacifica? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 27 owner complaints on file

Nearly every open recall here involves airbags — three covering curtain airbags that leak or lose air pressure, plus front airbags that may not deploy. Two others matter on the road: a possible loss of electronic stability control, and a wrong radio part that can blank the backup camera. As a current model year the complaint record is small and early — 27 unverified reports, topped by air bags (8), vehicle speed control (5), forward collision avoidance (5) and engine (5), with three crashes and no injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check the VIN for the curtain and front airbag recalls and whether repairs were completed
  3. Confirm the backup camera displays every time, and ask whether the radio has been replaced
  4. Ask whether any stability control or ESC warning lights have appeared
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
