Buying a used 2018 Chrysler Pacifica? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 866 owner complaints on file

This 2018 Chrysler Pacifica has 10 open recalls covering areas including seat belts, fire extinguishers, cruise control cancellation, park release, transmission welds, axle shafts, engine stalling, control arms, and power steering/stalling; none are listed as requiring stop-driving or park-outside precautions. NHTSA has logged 866 owner complaints for this model year, unverified reports, with 27 crash reports, 14 fire reports, and 15 injury reports. Complaint areas are led by engine (372), electrical system (286), steering (173), and powertrain (154).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify all 10 recall repairs are marked completed via VIN lookup
  3. Ask seller about any stalling, power steering, or engine warning light history
  4. Have a mechanic inspect the axle shaft, control arm, and transmission areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
