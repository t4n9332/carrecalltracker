Buying a used 2018 Chrysler Pacifica? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 866 owner complaints on file

This year carries an unusually long recall list — ten entries, most high severity — covering a second-row seat belt that can come unlatched, a transmission that may stop working, an axle shaft that may come apart, a right front control arm that can separate, and two separate stalling causes. Complaint volume is high as well: 866 unverified NHTSA reports led by engine, electrical and steering, including 27 crashes, 14 fires and 15 injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Pull a full VIN recall report and confirm each of the ten repairs was completed.
  3. Test every second-row seat belt latch and check the battery connection is tight.
  4. Ask directly whether the van has ever stalled while driving, and have the transmission and front control arm inspected.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
