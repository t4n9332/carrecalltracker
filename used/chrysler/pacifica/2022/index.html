Buying a used 2022 Chrysler Pacifica? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 208 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This 2022 Chrysler Pacifica has 10 open recalls, including a critical hybrid battery pack fire risk recall advising owners to park outside, an engine-stalling wiring fault, a rearview camera issue, two separate side air bag/curtain recalls, a second-row seat latch defect, loose wiper arms, and two wheelchair restraint recalls; none require stop-driving. NHTSA has logged 208 owner complaints for this model year, unverified reports, with 6 crash, 2 fire, and 13 injury reports. Complaint areas are led by powertrain (71), engine (68), electrical system (57), and fuel/propulsion system (26).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. For hybrid models, confirm the battery pack fire recall repair is completed
  3. Verify both side air bag/curtain air bag recalls show completed repairs
  4. Ask about wiper arm looseness and second-row seat latch function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
