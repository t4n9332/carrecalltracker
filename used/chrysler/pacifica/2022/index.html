Buying a used 2022 Chrysler Pacifica? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 208 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Airbags are the recurring theme: three separate recalls cover side curtain airbags that may not deploy, may leak, or lose air pressure. The critical plug-in hybrid battery fire recall carries a park-outside advisory, and hybrid shutdown and unlocking second-row seats are also open items. Smaller ones cover loosening wiper arm nuts and a blank backup camera. Of 208 unverified owner complaints, power train (71), engine (68) and electrical system (57) lead; six crashes, two fires and 13 injuries were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm by VIN which of the three curtain airbag recalls apply and whether each was completed
  3. Watch for an airbag warning light at startup and ask what airbag work has been done
  4. Test the wipers and the backup camera, and confirm the wiper arm nut recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
