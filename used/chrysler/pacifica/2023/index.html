Buying a used 2023 Chrysler Pacifica? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 152 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Two critical recalls hit plug-in hybrid models: a fuel line that may leak gasoline, and a battery part that can cause fire, with a park-outside advisory on the latter. Airbag recalls stack up too — driver airbag non-deployment plus three separate side curtain airbag defects. Complaint volume drops to 152 unverified reports, but air bags (34) now appear among the top areas alongside engine (47), power train (41) and electrical system (41); three crashes and one injury reported, no fires.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. On a Hybrid, confirm the fuel line leak recall is complete and check for any fuel smell around the van
  3. Verify the driver airbag and all curtain airbag recalls are closed by VIN
  4. Ask whether the van has ever lost power or shut off while driving
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
