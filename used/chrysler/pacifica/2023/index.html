Buying a used 2023 Chrysler Pacifica? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 152 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This 2023 Chrysler Pacifica has 10 open recalls, including two critical hybrid items — a fuel line leak risk and a battery fire risk advising owners to park outside — plus a stalling wiring fault, an air bag deployment issue, a rearview camera problem, side air bag recalls, a wheelchair restraint defect, and seat wiring that may disable air bags; none require stop-driving. NHTSA logged 152 owner complaints, unverified reports, with 3 crashes, 0 fires, 1 injury. Top complaint areas: engine (47), powertrain (41), electrical system (41), air bags (34).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. For hybrid models, confirm both the fuel line leak and battery fire recalls are completed
  3. Verify the air bag deployment and side air bag recalls show completed repairs
  4. Ask about any stalling, seat wiring, or rearview camera issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
