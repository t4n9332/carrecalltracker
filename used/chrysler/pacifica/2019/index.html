Buying a used 2019 Chrysler Pacifica? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 354 owner complaints on file

Three separate high-severity recalls on this year point the same direction: stalling — from the engine computer, from a loose battery connection, and on the Hybrid, a shutdown while driving. Three more cover backup camera images that fail to appear or stay on screen. Of 354 unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA, electrical system (116), engine (94) and power train (86) dominate, echoing the recall themes; 11 crashes, three fires and three injuries were reported. A Rollx wheelchair-van restraint recall applies only to converted vans.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN on NHTSA to confirm the engine-computer and battery-connection stalling recalls were completed
  3. Ask whether the van has ever stalled or shut off in traffic, and for the repair records
  4. Shift into and out of reverse several times to confirm the backup camera behaves correctly
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
