Buying a used 2024 Chrysler Pacifica? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 42 owner complaints on file

The recall list for this year is airbag-heavy: driver airbag and front airbags that may not deploy, plus curtain airbags that may leak or lose pressure. Others are assembly issues — a missing seat stop part, a windshield that may not be glued properly, and loss of traction control when cruise control is on. Being recent, the record is thin: 42 unverified owner complaints, led by electrical system (13), power train (11) and air bags (10), with two crashes and two injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the windshield has been replaced or inspected under the bonding recall
  3. Confirm the missing seat stop part recall was performed and that the seats slide and stop normally
  4. Use cruise control on the test drive and watch for traction control warnings
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
