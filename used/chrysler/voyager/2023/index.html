Buying a used 2023 Chrysler Voyager? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Five of the seven recalls on this year involve airbags: driver airbag and front airbags that may not deploy, plus three covering curtain airbags that may not deploy, may leak, or lose air pressure. The rest are a backup camera that may go blank, and a Rollx recall that applies only to wheelchair-converted vans. The complaint record is essentially empty — a single unverified owner report, touching air bags and forward collision avoidance, with no crashes, fires or injuries — too little to show a pattern.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Pull the VIN recall report and confirm each of the five airbag recalls has been completed
  3. Watch for an airbag warning light at startup and during the test drive
  4. Shift into reverse several times to confirm the backup camera image appears
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
