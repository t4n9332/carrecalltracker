Buying a used 2022 Chrysler Voyager? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

Two of the five recalls here cover the same component from different angles: side curtain airbags that may leak and curtain airbags that lose air pressure. The rest are lower severity — wiper arm nuts that may loosen and a backup camera that may show nothing — and a Rollx recall that applies only to wheelchair-converted vans. Only five unverified owner complaints are on file (one crash, one injury), spread thinly across electrical system, vehicle speed control, latches and forward collision avoidance, so there's little pattern to read.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify both curtain airbag recalls were completed and that no airbag light stays on
  3. Check that the backup camera image appears each time you shift into reverse
  4. Confirm the wiper arm nut recall was done and that the wipers don't slip in use
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
