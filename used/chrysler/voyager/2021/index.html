Buying a used 2021 Chrysler Voyager? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 16 owner complaints on file

This 2021 Chrysler Voyager has 2 open recalls: a side curtain air bag deployment issue and a second-row seat latch defect; neither requires stop-driving or park-outside precautions. NHTSA logged 16 owner complaints for this model year, unverified reports, with no crash, fire, or injury reports cited. Top complaint areas: an unspecified category (5), steering (4), electrical system (4), and powertrain (2).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the side curtain air bag recall repair was completed
  3. Test second-row seat latch function
  4. Ask about any steering or electrical system issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
