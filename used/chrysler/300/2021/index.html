Buying a used 2021 Chrysler 300? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 20 owner complaints on file

Same pairing as 2020: the critical curtain airbag rupture recall and a moderate windshield bonding recall. The complaint record is quiet — 20 unverified NHTSA reports with no crashes, fires or injuries listed — led by air bags, then engine and seat belts. With numbers this low, a documented recall repair and the individual car's service history matter more than the aggregate figures.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the curtain airbag recall repair is recorded against this VIN.
  3. Ask whether the windshield was checked or re-bonded under the recall.
  4. Inspect seat belt operation and ask about any engine warning lights.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
