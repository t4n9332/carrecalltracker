Buying a used 2022 Chrysler 300? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

This 2022 Chrysler 300 has two open recalls, both related to TPMS/tire sensor failures shared across Chrysler 300, Challenger, and Charger models, neither requiring stop-driving or park-outside precautions. NHTSA logged 5 owner complaints for this model year, unverified reports, including 2 crashes, 0 fires, and 2 injuries, with air bags cited twice and single mentions each for powertrain, electrical system, and an unspecified category.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the TPMS/tire sensor recall repairs were completed using the VIN
  3. Ask about any air bag warning lights or repair history
  4. Test tire pressure monitoring system function during inspection
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Chrysler dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
