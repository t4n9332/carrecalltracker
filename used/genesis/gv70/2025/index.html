Buying a used 2025 Genesis GV70? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 38 owner complaints on file

The 2025 GV70 lists two recalls: the critical multi-model fuel line leak and a medium-severity driver display failure. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. What's notable is the complaint mix — 38 unverified NHTSA reports on a recent model year, including 6 crashes and 3 injuries, led by forward collision avoidance (12) and electrical (11), with lane departure (5) also appearing. Those driver-assist areas aren't covered by either recall.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel line recall was completed before purchase
  3. Test forward collision and lane-keeping systems on a longer drive for false or missed alerts
  4. Check the driver display for blanking or flicker, and ask if the display recall was performed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
