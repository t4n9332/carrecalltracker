Buying a used 2024 Genesis GV70? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 17 owner complaints on file

The 2024 GV70 has a much shorter list: the critical fuel line leak recall that spans several Genesis models, and a high-severity recall for the vehicle slipping out of Park and rolling away. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning, but the rollaway issue is a parking-safety concern worth confirming as repaired. Unverified NHTSA complaints number 17, with no crashes or fires and one injury reported, spread across unknown/other (7), power train (6), and electrical (3).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the roll-away/Park recall remedy was performed, by repair order
  3. Verify the fuel line recall was completed and check for fuel smell underhood
  4. Test that the vehicle holds firmly in Park on an incline before releasing the brake
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
