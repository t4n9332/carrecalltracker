Buying a used 2023 Genesis GV70? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 62 owner complaints on file

This model year has three recalls: a critical fuel pipe leak recall tied to fire risk, a seat belt component that may explode, and a fuel pump failure recall that can cut engine power. NHTSA has logged 59 owner complaints for this year, including 5 crashes and 7 injuries, with power train (16), engine (14), fuel/propulsion system (13), and electrical system (9) as the top categories. Owner complaints are unverified consumer reports, not confirmed defects.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pipe leak and fuel pump recalls were completed
  3. Ask about any engine or power train complaint history
  4. Verify the seat belt recall remedy was applied
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
