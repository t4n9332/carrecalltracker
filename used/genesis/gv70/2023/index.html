Buying a used 2023 Genesis GV70? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 59 owner complaints on file

The 2023 GV70 carries three recalls: a critical fuel line leak, a fuel pump failure, and a seat belt part that may explode during a crash — none with stop-driving or park-outside warnings. That's fewer than the 2022, but the same fuel and restraint themes. Owners filed 59 unverified NHTSA complaints, including 5 crashes and 7 injuries — the highest injury count among these vehicles — with power train (16), engine (14), and fuel/propulsion (13) leading.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the fuel line and fuel pump recall repairs are documented in the service history
  3. Confirm the seat belt recall was completed and belts retract and latch normally
  4. Ask the seller about stalling, hard starts, or drivetrain hesitation, given the complaint pattern
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
