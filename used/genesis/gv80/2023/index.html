Buying a used 2023 Genesis GV80? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 37 owner complaints on file

Four recalls apply to this model year: a critical fuel pipe leak with fire risk, a seat belt part recall, a fuel pump issue that can cut engine power, and an instrument panel display recall. NHTSA shows 37 owner complaints, unverified reports, with no crashes, fires, or injuries recorded. Electrical system leads with 10 complaints, followed by power train at 6, and fuel/propulsion system and an unknown/other category at 5 each, indicating electrical and drivetrain concerns are the most reported areas.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pipe leak recall has been completed
  3. Ask about instrument panel display problems or repair history
  4. Verify the seat belt component recall was performed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
