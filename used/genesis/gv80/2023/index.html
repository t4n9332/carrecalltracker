Buying a used 2023 Genesis GV80? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 37 owner complaints on file

The 2023 GV80 repeats the fuel-system pattern: a critical fuel line leak recall and a fuel pump failure recall, plus a seat belt part that may explode in a crash and a medium recall for a driver display that can fail. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Of the 37 unverified NHTSA complaints on file, none report a crash, fire, or injury; the top areas are electrical (10), power train (6), and fuel/propulsion (5).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get proof the fuel line and fuel pump recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the seat belt recall repair and check belt operation in each seat
  4. Watch the driver display through a full start-up and drive cycle for dropouts
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
