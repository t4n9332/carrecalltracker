Buying a used 2025 Genesis GV80? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 18 owner complaints on file

Three recalls here: the same critical fuel-line leak shared across Genesis models, a high-severity dashboard display that can go blank, and a backup camera that may shut off. None carry stop-driving or park-outside instructions. The 18 unverified owner complaints include 2 crashes and 4 injuries. Notably, the complaint mix differs from the 2024 car — alongside electrical system, owners report lane departure, forward collision avoidance, and vehicle speed control issues.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel-line, dashboard display, and backup camera recalls are all closed
  3. Test the dashboard display on a cold start and the camera in reverse
  4. Ask if lane departure, collision warning, or speed control ever behaved unexpectedly
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
