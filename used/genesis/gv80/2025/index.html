Buying a used 2025 Genesis GV80? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 18 owner complaints on file

Three recalls cover this model year: a critical fuel pipe leak with fire risk, a backup camera that may go dark, and an instrument display recall. NHTSA lists 18 owner complaints, unverified reports, with 2 crashes and 4 injuries noted. Electrical system leads with 5 complaints, followed by lane departure and forward collision avoidance systems at 4 each, and vehicle speed control at 3, pointing to driver-assistance features as a notable complaint area alongside the recalled camera and display issues.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel pipe leak recall has been completed
  3. Test the backup camera for reliable operation
  4. Test lane departure, forward collision, and speed control systems
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
