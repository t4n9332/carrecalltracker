Buying a used 2024 Genesis GV80? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 17 owner complaints on file

Two recalls apply: a critical fuel-line leak covering several Genesis models, and a medium-severity driver display that can fail. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. NHTSA shows 17 owner complaints — unverified reports filed by owners — with no crashes or fires and one injury. They cluster in engine and electrical system, five each, with three on service brakes. Complaint volume is low for this model year; the fuel-line item is the one to confirm as repaired.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel-line recall repair is documented against this VIN
  3. Ask whether the driver display has ever gone blank or been replaced
  4. Ask about fuel smell, engine warning lights, or brake concerns during ownership
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
