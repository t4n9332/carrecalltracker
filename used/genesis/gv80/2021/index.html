Buying a used 2021 Genesis GV80? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 59 owner complaints on file

This model year carries five recalls: two separate fuel tube/fuel pipe leak recalls tied to fire risk, a seat belt pretensioner recall, a seat belt component that may explode, and a fuel pump failure recall that may cut power. NHTSA has logged 59 owner complaints for this year, including 5 crashes, 1 fire, and 3 injuries, with electrical system (14), exterior lighting (12), fuel/propulsion system (11), and forward collision avoidance (10) as the top categories. Owner complaints are unverified consumer reports, not confirmed defects.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify both fuel tube/fuel pipe leak recalls were completed
  3. Ask about any exterior lighting issues or repairs
  4. Confirm the seat belt pretensioner and fuel pump recalls were done
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
