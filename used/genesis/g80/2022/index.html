Buying a used 2022 Genesis G80? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 7 owner complaints on file

Fuel delivery dominates this year's record: a critical fuel-line leak recall plus two separate fuel-pump failure campaigns, alongside a seat-belt pretensioner part that could rupture in a crash. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Only seven owner complaints were filed with NHTSA — unverified reports, with no crashes, fires or injuries — but they cluster in the same fuel system and electrical areas the recalls cover, so paperwork proving each remedy matters more than the low complaint count.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for a VIN-specific recall printout showing the fuel line and both fuel pump remedies completed.
  3. Confirm the seat belt pretensioner recall was actually performed, not just scheduled.
  4. Note any fuel smell, hard starting or stalling on both a cold and a warm test drive.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
