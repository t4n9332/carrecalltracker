Buying a used 2023 Genesis G80? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

This year repeats the critical fuel-line leak recall and the fuel-pump failure campaign, adds a seat-belt component that may rupture in a crash, and a lower-severity fix for a driver display that can fail. Nine owner complaints were filed with NHTSA — unverified reports — including one crash and one injury, and six of them fall under fuel system or gasoline headings. No recall here carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction; all are dealer-repaired items worth confirming individually.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the fuel line and fuel pump recall work is closed on this specific VIN.
  3. Start the car and watch the driver display boot fully, with no blank or frozen gauges.
  4. Ask whether the seat belt recall parts were replaced or only inspected.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
