Buying a used 2020 Genesis G80? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 0 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year has two critical recalls: a brake module that may catch fire, which carries a park-outside advisory, and a turbo oil pipe that may leak and pose a fire risk. Neither recall is marked stop-driving. There are no owner complaints on record with NHTSA for this model year; that reflects an absence of filed reports rather than a confirmed clean history.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake module fire recall has been completed
  3. Confirm the turbo oil pipe leak recall has been completed
  4. Ask if the seller was told to park outside due to the brake fire risk
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
