Buying a used 2020 Genesis G80? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 0 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Two critical fire recalls apply: an anti-lock brake module that can catch fire, which carries park-outside guidance until it's repaired, and a turbocharger oil leak affecting the G70/G80/G90 turbo engines. Nothing else is listed for this year. NHTSA shows no owner complaints on record — that is an absence of filings rather than evidence the car is trouble-free, so completed recall work and the car's own service history are what you can actually verify here.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask the dealer to confirm the ABS module recall repair by VIN.
  3. Check the engine bay for oil residue or burning-oil smell around the turbo.
  4. Request full service records, since there's no complaint data to lean on.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
