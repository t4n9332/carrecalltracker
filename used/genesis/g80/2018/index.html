Buying a used 2018 Genesis G80? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 95 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

This is the heaviest record in the group: four critical fire-related recalls, including two turbo oil leak campaigns, an anti-lock brake module, and water intrusion in the starter — the latter two carry park-outside guidance. Two more stand out: a windshield that may come off and a passenger airbag sensor that may misread an empty seat. Owners filed 95 unverified NHTSA complaints, led by electrical and steering, with eight crashes and five injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Pull a VIN recall report; several fire recalls here, two with park-outside advisories.
  3. Inspect the windshield for aftermarket replacement or signs of re-bonding.
  4. Have someone sit in the front passenger seat and confirm the airbag status light behaves.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
