Buying a used 2024 Genesis G80? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

The record here is thin: one critical recall for a fuel line that may leak and one medium-severity campaign for a driver display that can fail, neither flagged for stop-driving or parking outside. A single owner complaint has been filed with NHTSA, an unverified report concerning lane departure. With so little data, the age of the vehicle matters — absence of complaints is not proof of reliability, so confirm the two recalls were actually completed.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get the fuel line recall status in writing from a Genesis dealer.
  3. Cycle the ignition twice and check the digital driver display for blanking.
  4. Test lane keeping assist on a marked road and note any false alerts or steering tugs.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
