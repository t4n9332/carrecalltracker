Buying a used 2019 Genesis G80? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 27 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

This model year has three recalls, all rated critical and centered on fire risk: a brake module that may catch fire, a starter fire risk, and a turbo oil pipe that may leak. Two of these recalls advise parking outside. Owners have filed 27 unverified complaints with NHTSA, including 2 crashes and 2 injuries; top areas are electrical system and unknown/other, tied at 8 each, followed by engine and service brakes, tied at 5 each.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm brake module and starter fire recalls were completed
  3. Check turbo oil pipe leak recall repair status
  4. Ask whether the vehicle has been parked outside per recall guidance
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
