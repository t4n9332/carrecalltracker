Buying a used 2025 Genesis G80? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file

Two open items on this newest model year: the critical fuel-line leak recall that spans the Genesis lineup, and a higher-severity campaign for a dashboard display that can go blank. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Three owner complaints have reached NHTSA, unverified reports with no crashes, fires or injuries, spread across speed control, lane departure, forward collision avoidance and electrical categories — a pattern leaning toward screens and driver-assist behavior rather than mechanical failure.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fuel line recall remedy was performed, not just scheduled.
  3. Watch the dashboard display through a full startup and a restart for blank screens.
  4. Drive with cruise control and collision warning active to check for false or abrupt interventions.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
