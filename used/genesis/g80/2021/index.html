Buying a used 2021 Genesis G80? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

This model year carries two critical fuel-related recalls — one for a fuel tube that may leak and a separate one for a fuel pipe leak raising fire risk — plus two high-severity recalls for a seat belt part that may explode and a fuel pump that may fail and cut power. None are marked stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA has 9 owner complaints on file (0 crashes, 0 fires, 0 injuries), with service brakes and tires each cited 3 times and electrical system twice; these are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both fuel tube/pipe leak recalls are completed
  3. Ask about the seat belt part recall status
  4. Ask about the fuel pump failure recall and any brake or tire issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
