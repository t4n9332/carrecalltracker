Buying a used 2021 Genesis G80? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

The 2021 record differs from earlier G80s: the ABS fire recall isn't listed here, but two critical fuel line campaigns are, covering the G80 alongside the G90, GV70 and GV80, where the line may leak gasoline. A fuel pump recall and a seat belt component that may explode during a crash also apply. Just nine unverified NHTSA complaints are on record, none involving crashes, fires or injuries, with service brakes and tires most cited.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof both fuel line recalls were repaired, and check for any fuel smell.
  3. Confirm the seat belt component recall was completed on all affected belts.
  4. Have the fuel pump recall checked by VIN and note any hard starting.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
