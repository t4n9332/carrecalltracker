Buying a used 2018 Genesis G90? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 16 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year carries several critical recalls: a turbo oil leak fire risk, a starter that may catch fire (with a park-outside advisory), and a separate turbo oil pipe leak fire risk recall, plus a high-severity recall for a windshield that may detach. NHTSA has 16 owner complaints on file, including 1 crash, with electrical system cited 5 times, engine 4 times, and unknown/other and powertrain each cited twice; these are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the starter fire recall has been completed
  3. Confirm both turbo oil leak recalls are completed
  4. Ask about the windshield detachment recall and any electrical issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
