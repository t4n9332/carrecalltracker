Buying a used 2023 Genesis G90? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

This is the most recall-heavy G90 year listed: a critical fuel-line leak, a fuel-pump failure campaign, two separate seat-belt issues — pretensioners that may burst and front anchors that can detach — plus driver-assist radar causing sudden unexpected braking and a dashboard display that can go blank. Five owner complaints reached NHTSA, unverified reports including one crash, and two cite seat belts. None of the recalls carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction, but the volume means several remedies to confirm.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get a VIN report listing all six recalls and which ones have been completed.
  3. Tug-test each front seat belt and ask if the anchor and pretensioner recalls were done.
  4. Drive with adaptive cruise and automatic braking on, noting any unprompted hard braking.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
