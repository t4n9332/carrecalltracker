Buying a used 2025 Genesis G90? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

The same four campaigns apply to this year: the critical fuel-line leak, radar-triggered sudden unexpected braking, front seat-belt anchors that may detach, and a dashboard display that can go blank — none with a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Only two owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA, unverified reports with no crashes, fires or injuries, touching electrical, wheels and an unclassified area. On a car this new, recall completion status is more informative than the complaint tally.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN for open recalls and confirm the fuel line remedy is closed.
  3. Verify the seat belt anchor inspection or replacement was performed.
  4. Check the wheels and tires for damage and confirm the display powers up normally.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
