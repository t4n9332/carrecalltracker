Buying a used 2024 Genesis G90? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file

Four recalls carry over here: the critical fuel-line leak, driver-assist radar that can trigger sudden unexpected braking, front seat-belt anchors that may detach, and a dashboard display that can go blank. None requires stop-driving or parking outside. Three owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA, unverified reports with no crashes, fires or injuries, one of which falls under service brakes — worth noting alongside the braking recall, though a single report establishes nothing on its own.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof the fuel line and seat belt anchor recalls were completed.
  3. Check whether the radar braking recall update has been installed on this VIN.
  4. Drive in traffic with driver assist engaged and note any sudden braking events.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
