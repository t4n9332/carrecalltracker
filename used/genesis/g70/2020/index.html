Buying a used 2020 Genesis G70? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 13 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Two critical fire recalls carry over: an anti-lock brake module that can catch fire, with park-outside guidance until it's fixed, and a turbocharger oil leak. A fuel pump recall on performance models applies as well. The complaint record is thin — 13 unverified NHTSA reports, including one fire and no crashes, spread across fuel system, electrical, tires and unclassified issues. Thirteen filings is a small sample, so read it as limited evidence rather than a clean record.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm by VIN that the ABS module recall repair was completed.
  3. Inspect the turbo area for oil leaks and ask for oil-change records.
  4. Ask whether the fuel pump recall applies to this car and was performed.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
