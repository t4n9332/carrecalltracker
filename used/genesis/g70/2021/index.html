Buying a used 2021 Genesis G70? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year has four recalls, two rated critical for fire risk: a brake module that may catch fire, which advises parking outside, and a turbo oil pipe that may leak. Two high-rated recalls address fuel pump defects, one describing a stalling engine and another a power-loss risk. Owners have filed 6 unverified complaints with NHTSA, including 1 crash and 1 fire; top areas are engine (4), unknown/other (3), fuel/propulsion system (2), and electrical system (1).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm brake module fire recall was completed
  3. Check turbo oil pipe leak recall status
  4. Ask if either fuel pump recall (stall or power loss) was performed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
