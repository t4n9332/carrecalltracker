Buying a used 2021 Genesis G70? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This year carries the same two critical fire recalls — an anti-lock brake module that can ignite, which comes with park-outside guidance, and a turbocharger oil leak — plus two fuel pump campaigns, one specific to a G70 defect that can stall the engine. Stalling is the practical thing to probe on a test drive. Only six unverified NHTSA complaints are on record, most tagged to the engine, including one crash and one fire; that's too few to draw much from.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both fuel pump recalls are closed and ask whether the car has ever stalled.
  3. Verify the ABS module recall repair before parking it indoors.
  4. Check for turbo oil leaks and ask for maintenance records.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
