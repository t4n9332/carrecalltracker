Buying a used 2025 Genesis GV60? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

The 2025 GV60's record is the lightest of the GV60 years listed: one high-severity recall for power loss from the charging unit, plus three medium ones — backup camera shutoff, blank dashboard display, and windshield trim that can detach onto the road. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Only 5 unverified NHTSA complaints are on file, one citing a crash, spread thinly across electrical, back-over prevention, and service brakes. Low complaint counts partly reflect the model's short time on the road.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the charging-unit power-loss recall was completed before delivery
  3. Cycle the backup camera and driver display several times during the test drive
  4. Ask if the windshield trim recall repair was done and inspect the trim edges
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
