Buying a used 2023 Genesis GV60? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 58 owner complaints on file

The 2023 GV60 carries an unusually dense recall list for an early-production EV: two separate driveshaft-fracture recalls, a drive-power loss recall, and a charging-unit failure that can cut power, plus two seat belt actions and a blank dashboard display. None carry stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Owners filed 58 unverified NHTSA complaints, 2 involving crashes and 1 an injury, concentrated in electrical (33) and power train (13) — the same areas the recalls address.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get repair receipts proving both driveshaft recalls and the charging-unit remedy were completed
  3. Confirm the front passenger belt latches and the pretensioner recall was performed
  4. On the test drive, watch for a blank instrument display and inspect windshield trim attachment
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
