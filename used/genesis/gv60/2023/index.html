Buying a used 2023 Genesis GV60? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 58 owner complaints on file

This model year has eight recalls, covering a seat belt component that may explode, rear driveshafts that may fracture, a passenger seat belt that may not fasten, a separate driveshaft break recall, EV charging unit failure (named in two separate recall notices), a display screen failure, and windshield trim that may come off. NHTSA has logged 58 owner complaints for this year, including 2 crashes and 1 injury, concentrated in electrical system (33), power train (13), an unspecified category (10), and fuel/propulsion system (6). Owner complaints are unverified consumer reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the seat belt and driveshaft recalls were completed
  3. Test the EV charging unit for normal operation
  4. Ask about any electrical system issues, the largest complaint category
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
