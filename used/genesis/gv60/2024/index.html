Buying a used 2024 Genesis GV60? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 14 owner complaints on file

The 2024 GV60 sheds the driveshaft recalls of the prior year but keeps the two high-severity electrical ones: loss of drive power and power loss from the charging unit. Three medium recalls cover a backup camera that can shut off, a dashboard display that can go blank, and windshield trim that can detach. No stop-driving or park-outside warnings. Unverified NHTSA complaints total 14, one citing a crash, led by electrical (8) and service brakes (3).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the drive-power and charging-unit recalls were closed out by a Genesis dealer
  3. Test the backup camera repeatedly in one session to see if it drops out
  4. Ask whether the windshield trim and dashboard display recalls have been performed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Genesis dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
