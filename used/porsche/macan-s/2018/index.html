Buying a used 2018 Porsche Macan S? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Five recalls apply, including three high-severity issues: a control unit that may fail, a front passenger seat sensor that may fail, and rear seat belt anchors that may not be tight, plus lower-severity recalls for a ski bag strap and missing headlight adjustment screw caps. NHTSA lists 1 owner complaint (unverified report) for this model year, involving the engine.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the control unit and passenger seat sensor recalls are completed
  3. Check rear seat belt anchor tightness
  4. Ask about any engine warning light history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
