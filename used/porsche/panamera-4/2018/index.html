Buying a used 2018 Porsche Panamera 4? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

This trim's recall list differs slightly from other 2018 Panameras: alongside the critical coolant pump fire recall, its high-severity item is loose rear seat belt anchors, shared with the Macan, rather than anti-roll bar links. Two medium recalls cover a missing worn brake pad warning and control unit soldering. NHTSA logged nine unverified owner complaints for the model year — engine, power train, electrical and brakes — including one crash and one injury. No stop-driving or park-outside orders were attached.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the coolant pump recall was performed, by VIN
  3. Tug-test rear seat belt anchors and ask if that recall was done
  4. Check brake pad life directly, since there is no wear warning
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
