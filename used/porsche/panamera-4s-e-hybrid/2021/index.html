Buying a used 2021 Porsche Panamera 4S E-Hybrid? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

This model year has two recalls: a critical issue where the portable charging cable and outlet can overheat, and a front suspension arm defect affecting Panamera and Taycan models. NHTSA has logged only 2 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, with 0 crashes, 0 fires, and 0 injuries reported. One complaint falls under power train and one under an unspecified category.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask about charging cable/outlet overheat recall completion
  3. Confirm front suspension arm recall inspection or repair
  4. Test charging cable and outlet for damage or heat
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
