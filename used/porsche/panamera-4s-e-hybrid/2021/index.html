Buying a used 2021 Porsche Panamera 4S E-Hybrid? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

Two recalls on this model year carry critical severity: a front suspension arm that can break, shared with the Taycan, and a portable charging cable that can overheat — the latter matters directly here because the 4S E-Hybrid plugs in. Neither came with a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. NHTSA lists only two owner complaints for 2021, with no crashes, fires or injuries reported; those filings are unverified and fall under power train and an unspecified area.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for VIN-specific proof the front suspension arm recall was completed
  3. Confirm the supplied portable charging cable was replaced under recall
  4. Test-drive for any drivetrain hesitation, given the one power train complaint
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
