Buying a used 2018 Porsche Panamera Turbo S E-Hybrid? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

Only two recalls are listed for this trim, fewer than other 2018 Panameras: a high-severity one for rear anti-roll bar links that can detach, and a medium one for control unit soldering that may affect driving. The coolant pump fire and brake pad warning recalls are not listed here. Neither recall carried stop-driving or park-outside instructions. The 2018 model year drew nine unverified NHTSA owner complaints overall, mostly engine, power train and electrical, with one crash and one injury.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rear anti-roll bar link recall was completed by VIN
  3. Ask whether the control unit soldering remedy or software update was performed
  4. Have the hybrid system and battery health checked by a Porsche technician
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
