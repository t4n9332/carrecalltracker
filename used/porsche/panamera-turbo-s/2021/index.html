Buying a used 2021 Porsche Panamera Turbo S? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

This 2021 Panamera Turbo S has three open recalls: a critical one for a coolant pump that may spark an electrical fire, a front suspension arm defect, and a seat belt issue that may not properly lock child car seats. NHTSA has logged 2 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported; one complaint was filed under power train and one under an unspecified category.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the coolant pump fire-risk recall has been completed
  3. Confirm the front suspension arm recall repair status
  4. If installing a child seat, verify the seat belt recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
