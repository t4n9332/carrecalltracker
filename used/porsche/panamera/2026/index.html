Buying a used 2026 Porsche Panamera? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This 2026 Panamera has two open recalls: a critical one for fuel pump bolts that may leak fuel, and a high-severity recall for a suspension strut that may loosen. NHTSA has logged 1 owner complaint (an unverified report) for this model year, involving 1 injury and no crashes or fires; it was filed under the air bags category.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the fuel pump bolt recall (fuel leak risk) has been completed
  3. Confirm the suspension strut recall repair status
  4. Ask about the air bag system given the injury-related complaint on file
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
