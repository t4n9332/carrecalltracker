Buying a used 2018 Porsche Panamera? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 12 owner complaints on file

The 2018 Panamera's most serious open item is a coolant pump that can short out and start a fire; no stop-driving or park-outside warning was attached. Hybrid cars add a corroding front brake line recall, and there's a campaign for brief loss of power steering assist, two separate worn-brake-pad warning recalls and a control unit soldering fix. NHTSA logged 12 owner complaints — unverified reports — clustered in power train, engine and electrical, with one crash and one injury and no fires.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof the coolant pump recall was completed by a dealer
  3. If it's the hybrid, confirm the front brake line corrosion recall was done
  4. Test drive for steering assist dropouts and check brake pad wear manually
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
