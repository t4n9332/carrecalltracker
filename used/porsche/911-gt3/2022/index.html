Buying a used 2022 Porsche 911 GT3? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 10 owner complaints on file

Three recalls apply to the 2022 GT3, none flagged stop-driving or park-outside. The most serious is a seat belt anchor screw that may loosen; a second covers seat-mounted airbags in bucket-seat cars, and a third is a Touring-only issue where the brake lights flash together. Ten owner complaints are on file with NHTSA — unverified reports — and six concern the fuel/propulsion system, the one concentration worth probing. No crashes, fires or injuries were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seat belt anchor screw recall was performed and torque-checked at a dealer.
  3. Ask about fuel or propulsion symptoms — fueling issues, stalling, warning lights.
  4. If it is a Touring, verify the brake light recall fix was applied.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
