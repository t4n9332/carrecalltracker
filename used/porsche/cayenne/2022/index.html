Buying a used 2022 Porsche Cayenne? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 3 owner complaints on file

This is the lightest record among recent Cayenne years listed here: only two medium recalls, for rear wheels that may be out of alignment and a backup camera that can go blank, the latter shared with Panamera and Taycan. NHTSA lists just 3 unverified owner complaints — one each in suspension, visibility, electrical system and back-over prevention — with no crashes, fires or injuries reported. Routine verification rather than anything that sets this year apart.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rear alignment recall was performed and check rear tires for uneven wear.
  3. Cycle the backup camera several times during a test drive to see if it blanks.
  4. Ask the seller for a dealer printout of open recalls by VIN.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
