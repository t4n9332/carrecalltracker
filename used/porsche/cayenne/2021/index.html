Buying a used 2021 Porsche Cayenne? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 21 owner complaints on file

Five recalls cover this model year, including three high-severity issues: a rear axle lock nut that may break, a passenger airbag that may not deploy, and a steering column that may detach, plus lower-severity rearview camera and rear axle alignment recalls. NHTSA lists 21 owner complaints (unverified reports), with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported. Electrical system complaints lead at 6, followed by unspecified issues at 5, airbags at 4, and engine at 3.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rear axle lock nut and steering column recalls are completed
  3. Confirm the passenger airbag recall repair status
  4. Ask whether the rear axle alignment recall was completed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
