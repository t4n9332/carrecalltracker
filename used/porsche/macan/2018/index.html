Buying a used 2018 Porsche Macan? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 34 owner complaints on file

Alongside the airbag and belt items shared with the Macan S, this listing adds a critical recall for a fuel pump that may crack and leak. The rest: a passenger airbag that may not deploy, loose rear seat belt anchors, a control unit failure that can disable alerts, and a ski bag strap. Owners filed 34 unverified complaints with NHTSA — one fire, no crashes or injuries — led by air bags, steering and fuel/propulsion system.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get VIN-level confirmation the fuel pump recall was performed, and ask about any fuel smell.
  3. Check that the airbag warning light cycles normally and the rear belt anchors sit tight.
  4. Ask the seller about steering issues, a frequent unverified complaint area for this year.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
