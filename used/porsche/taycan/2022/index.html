Buying a used 2022 Porsche Taycan? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 79 owner complaints on file

This model year has 10 recalls, six critical and centered on the high-voltage battery: fire risk, short-circuit risk, and a charging cable/outlet overheating issue repeated across several campaigns. High-severity recalls also cover a screen and camera that can go black, a brake-wear warning showing the wrong color, leaking front brake hoses, and a front passenger airbag that may switch off. NHTSA has 79 unverified owner complaints on file (2 crashes, 2 fires, 1 injury), with electrical system (36) and power train (18) the most common categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm completion of the battery fire/short-circuit and charging cable recalls
  3. Check the front passenger airbag on/off indicator
  4. Inspect front brake hoses and test the brake-wear warning display
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
