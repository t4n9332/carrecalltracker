Buying a used 2024 Porsche Taycan? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

The 2024 Taycan's record is dominated by battery-related fire risk: two separate critical recalls cover a battery short circuit, plus one for a portable charging cable that can overheat. None carried a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. A high-severity recall addresses front brake hoses that can crack and leak; a blank backup camera and a parking-light/turn-signal software fault are lesser items. Only nine NHTSA complaints are on file — unverified owner reports, mostly electrical — with no crashes, fires or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get a VIN recall printout showing both battery short-circuit recalls closed at a Porsche dealer.
  3. Ask whether the portable charging cable was replaced under recall, and that the cable comes with the car.
  4. Inspect front brake hoses for cracking or weeping; test the backup camera and turn signals with parking lights on.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
