Buying a used 2023 Porsche Taycan? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 20 owner complaints on file

This model year has 10 recalls, seven critical, including a battery seal issue, a battery fire risk, a charging cable/outlet overheating issue, and repeated high-voltage battery short-circuit recalls. High-severity recalls also cover leaking front brake hoses and a front passenger airbag that may switch off, plus a medium recall for a passenger seat belt warning that doesn't work. NHTSA has 19 unverified owner complaints on file (3 crashes, 0 fires, 3 injuries), with electrical system (9) and service brakes (7) the leading categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the battery seal, short-circuit, fire, and charging cable recalls are completed
  3. Check the front passenger airbag switch and passenger seat belt warning light
  4. Inspect front brake hoses for leaks
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Porsche dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
