Buying a used 2018 Mitsubishi Outlander Phev? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 15 owner complaints on file

This model year carries five recalls, including two software fixes for forward collision braking and safety systems, plus a liftgate gas spring that may fail, liftgate supports that may corrode, and a rear seat belt buckle that may not latch. NHTSA has 15 owner complaints for this model year (unverified reports), with 1 crash and 1 fire reported. Power train (5), vehicle speed control (4), electrical system (4), and service brakes (4) are the top complaint areas.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all five recalls, including liftgate and seat belt fixes, were completed
  3. Ask about the reported fire incident and its cause
  4. Check power train condition and any speed control complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mitsubishi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
