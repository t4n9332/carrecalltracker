Buying a used 2023 Mitsubishi Outlander Phev? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Recalls on the 2023 Outlander PHEV are limited to the backup camera: one where the image may not appear, another where it can freeze. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning, and no restraint, brake or strut campaigns appear for this year. NHTSA lists just one unverified owner complaint, in the engine category, with no crashes, fires or injuries reported. It is a short record, partly reflecting how new the vehicle is.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Shift to reverse several times and confirm the image appears and stays live
  3. Ask whether the camera software recall updates were applied
  4. Request service history for any engine or drivability visits
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mitsubishi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
