Buying a used 2022 Mitsubishi Outlander Phev? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

The 2022 Outlander PHEV's recall record is narrow: two campaigns, both about liftgate struts that can rust and let the hatch drop suddenly. Neither is a stop-driving or park-outside situation. The complaint record is nearly empty for this model year, with a single unverified owner report to NHTSA, filed under back over prevention, and no crashes, fires or injuries. That is a light file compared with earlier Outlander years.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check both liftgate strut recalls by VIN, since they are separate campaigns
  3. Raise the hatch fully and look for rust or weeping at the struts
  4. Test the backup camera and rear sensors before buying
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mitsubishi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
