Buying a used 2022 Mitsubishi Outlander Phev? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This model year carries two liftgate-related recalls: a high-severity gas spring that may fail, and support struts that may corrode over time and fail. NHTSA has only 1 owner complaint on file for this model year (an unverified report), with no crashes, fires, or injuries noted. That single complaint was filed under the back-over prevention category (rear camera or sensor systems), which is separate from the liftgate recalls above.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the liftgate gas spring recall was completed
  3. Ask if the liftgate supports have been inspected for corrosion
  4. Test the back-over/rear camera system during inspection
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mitsubishi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
