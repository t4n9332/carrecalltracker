Buying a used 2022 Mitsubishi Outlander? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 213 owner complaints on file

Recalls for this model year cover seat belts that may not secure child seats, a fuel pump that may fail, and a backup camera image that can fail, plus medium-severity recalls for a weak center pillar, seat belts that may not retract fully, and rearview camera image problems. NHTSA has logged 213 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 14 crash reports and 10 injury reports, with structure, unknown or other, forward collision avoidance, and electrical system listed as the most-cited categories among them.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seat belt, fuel pump, and backup camera recalls were completed
  3. Inspect the center pillar area and rearview camera display for issues
  4. Ask about structure-related complaints or repairs given the number filed for this model year
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mitsubishi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
