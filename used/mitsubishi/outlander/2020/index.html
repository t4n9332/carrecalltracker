Buying a used 2020 Mitsubishi Outlander? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 31 owner complaints on file

The 2020 Outlander carries three recalls: a rear seat belt assembled incorrectly, and two covering liftgate struts that can corrode and drop the hatch. Neither category is stop-driving or park-outside. Complaints are more telling than the recall count: 31 unverified NHTSA reports, led by electrical system (11), unknown or other (8), forward collision avoidance (6) and air bags (4), with five crashes and seven injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the forward collision system has ever braked or warned falsely
  3. Check for stored fault codes and any recurring electrical repairs
  4. Verify the rear seat belt recall repair was actually performed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mitsubishi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
