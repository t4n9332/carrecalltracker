Buying a used 2019 Mitsubishi Outlander? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 29 owner complaints on file

Seat belts headline the 2019 Outlander: a rear belt assembled incorrectly, plus a second-row buckle that may stop latching. Two liftgate strut corrosion recalls and a low-priority wrong rear wheel size label round it out; none are stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA holds 29 unverified owner complaints, led by service brakes (9), air bags (7) and electrical system (7), including five crashes, one fire and five injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Inspect and test all rear belts, especially where a child seat would go
  3. Ask when brake pads, rotors or hydraulics were last serviced
  4. Confirm the seat belt and strut recalls show as completed by VIN
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mitsubishi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
