Buying a used 2018 Mitsubishi Outlander Sport? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 21 owner complaints on file

The 2018 Outlander Sport has two recalls, both tied to driver-assist braking: loss of braking safety system help, and braking that continues too long after pedestrian detection. Neither is stop-driving or park-outside. Owners filed 21 unverified complaints with NHTSA, led by electrical system (7), air bags (6) and power train (5); four crashes and three injuries appear in that record, which is worth weighing against the short recall list.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both braking-assist recalls were completed by VIN
  3. Watch for an air bag warning light at startup and while driving
  4. Ask about transmission behavior, hesitation or shudder in traffic
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mitsubishi dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
