Buying a used 2024 Toyota Corolla? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 63 owner complaints on file

The critical steering shaft recall, where the shaft can crack and break apart, is the main item, alongside a low-severity Gulf States Toyota incorrect weight capacity label. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Among 63 unverified NHTSA complaints, forward collision avoidance (13), steering (11) and service brakes (10) lead, and owners reported 11 crashes and 12 injuries — a high injury count relative to the filing total. Driver-assist behavior comes up repeatedly in those reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering shaft recall repair by VIN before purchase
  3. Ask whether the pre-collision system has ever braked or warned unexpectedly
  4. Check the door jamb weight capacity label if the car came through Gulf States Toyota
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
