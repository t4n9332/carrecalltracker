Buying a used 2019 Toyota Corolla? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 202 owner complaints on file

Five recalls, three high severity: a hatchback transmission part that can cause stalling, the fuel pump stall recall, and airbags that may not deploy. Two low-severity load capacity label recalls round it out, and none carries a stop-driving instruction. Among 202 unverified NHTSA complaints, power train (54) and engine (39) dominate, with air bags at 31; owners reported 23 crashes, 1 fire and 15 injuries. Stalling is the recurring thread in both recalls and complaints.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify by VIN whether the transmission and fuel pump recalls were completed, especially on a hatchback
  3. Ask about stalling, rough shifting or transmission repairs, and request service records
  4. Confirm the airbag recall repair and that the airbag light cycles normally
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
