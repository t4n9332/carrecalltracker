Buying a used 2023 Toyota Corolla? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 73 owner complaints on file

This model year has three high-severity recalls, all related to steering: a steering cable that may disable the air bag, a steering shaft that may crack and detach, and a separate steering defect that disables the airbag. NHTSA has 73 owner complaints on file, including 8 crashes, 1 fire, and 4 injuries reported; these are unverified owner reports. Top complaint areas are unknown/other (18) and steering (16), followed by electrical system and engine, each at 13.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all three steering-related recalls were completed
  3. Ask about any steering complaints or repairs
  4. Ask about electrical system issues reported by a prior owner
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
