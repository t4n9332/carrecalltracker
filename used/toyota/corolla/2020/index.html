Buying a used 2020 Toyota Corolla? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 306 owner complaints on file

This model year has three high-severity recalls: rear seat belts that may not lock, fuel pumps that may fail and stall the engine, and an airbag sensor that may block deployment. NHTSA has 306 owner complaints on file, including 22 crashes, 1 fire, and 23 injuries reported; these are unverified owner reports. Top complaint areas are engine (108) and unknown/other (79), followed by power train (37) and electrical system (31).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seat belt, fuel pump, and airbag sensor recalls were completed
  3. Ask about any engine-related repairs or complaints
  4. Ask about power train or stalling issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
