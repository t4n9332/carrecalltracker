Buying a used 2023 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

This Corolla Cross Hybrid has five recalls: two related steering defects that may disable airbag deployment, a brake assist software error, a backup warning sound that may be too quiet, and a low-severity accessory weight label error. None are marked stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA logged just 2 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported, filed under service brakes, forward collision avoidance, and visibility.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both steering/airbag recalls have been completed
  3. Test brake assist function during a test drive
  4. Check backup warning sound and rearview camera operation
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
