Buying a used 2023 Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

Five recalls, weighted toward airbags and brakes: two separate actions cover wiring and related faults that can leave the driver's airbag disabled, and another covers a possible loss of brake power assist. The remaining two are minor — a Gulf States weight-capacity label error and a backup warning sound that is too quiet. Only two owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA for this model year, and none report a crash, fire or injury; that volume is too small to read much into.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both driver's airbag recalls were completed, not just one
  3. Check that the airbag warning light goes out after startup
  4. Ask whether the brake power assist recall repair is on file
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
