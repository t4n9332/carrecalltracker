Buying a used 2023 Toyota Prius Prime? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

Two of the four recalls on the 2023 Prius Prime are separate high-severity campaigns covering rear doors that can open while driving; the others are a rearview camera freeze and an incorrect weight capacity label on Gulf States cars. None require stopping driving or parking outside. Only five unverified owner complaints are on file with NHTSA, with no crashes, fires, or injuries, though one falls under latches/locks/linkages, the system the door recalls involve.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both rear door recalls were completed, as they are two distinct campaigns.
  3. Open and close both rear doors repeatedly and check the child locks and door-ajar warning.
  4. Test the backup camera through several reverse cycles for freezing.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
