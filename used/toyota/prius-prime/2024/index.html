Buying a used 2024 Toyota Prius Prime? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 11 owner complaints on file

The 2024 Prius Prime carries the same recall set as the prior year: two separate high-severity campaigns for rear doors that can open while driving, plus a rearview camera freeze and a Gulf States weight capacity label error. None require stopping driving or parking outside. NHTSA lists 11 unverified owner complaints including one crash and one injury; electrical is the most cited area, and two reports fall under latches/locks/linkages, the same system the door recalls address.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof that both rear door recalls were performed, not just one.
  3. Cycle both rear doors and confirm the door-ajar warning works before driving off.
  4. Ask about any electrical faults, and inspect tire condition and age.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
