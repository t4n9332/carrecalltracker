Buying a used 2022 Toyota Highlander Hybrid? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file

This model year carries four recalls: two high-severity items covering stability control that may stay switched off and a second-row seat back that may not lock in place, a medium-severity issue where the front bumper cover may detach, and a low-severity issue where weight labels may fade. NHTSA has logged 6 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 0 crashes, 0 fires, and 0 injuries. The most-cited complaint areas are electrical system (2), engine (2), service brakes (2), and steering (1).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the stability control and second-row seat back recalls have been completed
  3. Ask if the bumper cover detachment recall was addressed
  4. Ask about electrical, engine, or brake issues, the top complaint areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
