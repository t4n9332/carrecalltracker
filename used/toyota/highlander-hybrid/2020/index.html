Buying a used 2020 Toyota Highlander Hybrid? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

This model year carries four recalls, three of them high severity: seat trim that can block the side airbag, stability control that may stay switched off, and an airbag sensor that may block deployment, plus a medium-severity issue where the front bumper cover may detach. NHTSA has logged 9 owner complaints (unverified reports), with 0 crashes, 0 fires, and 0 injuries. The most-cited complaint areas are air bags (4), service brakes (2), structure (1), and hydraulic (1).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seat-trim side airbag recall has been completed
  3. Confirm the stability control and airbag sensor recalls have been addressed
  4. Ask about airbag or brake issues, the top complaint areas
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
