Buying a used 2019 Toyota Camry? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 394 owner complaints on file

No recall on this year is flagged critical, but the pattern is fuel-pump-centered: three separate actions cover pumps that can fail and stall the engine, one of which can also cut brake power assist. A passenger airbag sensor configured incorrectly is the other significant item; two remaining recalls concern load-capacity labels. Unverified owner complaints to NHTSA concentrate in power train, engine and fuel/propulsion areas, with crashes and injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask which of the three fuel pump recalls were performed, and when
  3. Verify the passenger airbag sensor remedy appears on the VIN record
  4. Watch the passenger airbag status light with someone seated there
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
