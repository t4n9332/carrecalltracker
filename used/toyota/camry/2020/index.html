Buying a used 2020 Toyota Camry? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 271 owner complaints on file

This Camry has three recalls: a critical coolant leak affecting engines shared with the RAV4, a fuel pump that may fail and stall the engine, and an airbag sensor that may block deployment. None are marked stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA logged 271 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, with 14 crashes, 2 fires, and 22 injuries reported; top areas are electrical system (66), engine (60), air bags (48), and unknown/other (44).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the coolant leak, fuel pump, and airbag sensor recalls are completed
  3. Ask about coolant levels or overheating history
  4. Check electrical system function, including lights and accessories
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
