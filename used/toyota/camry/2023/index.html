Buying a used 2023 Toyota Camry? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 64 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

This Camry has a critical lug nut recall marked stop-driving, meaning affected vehicles should not be driven until repaired, plus a medium-severity recall for poorly welded rear head restraint brackets. Neither issue is marked park-outside. NHTSA logged 64 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this model year, with 9 crashes, 2 fires, and 7 injuries reported; top areas are unknown/other (18), electrical system (9), fuel/propulsion system (7), and power train (7).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the lug nut recall has been completed before driving
  3. Inspect rear head restraint brackets for recall repair
  4. Ask about any electrical or fuel system issues reported
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
