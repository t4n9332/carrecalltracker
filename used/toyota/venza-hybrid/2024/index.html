Buying a used 2024 Toyota Venza Hybrid? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 25 owner complaints on file

The same three recalls as the 2023 car: a dashboard that may not show speed or warnings, a rearview camera that can freeze, and a wrong weight capacity label on Gulf States Toyota vehicles. None is rated critical. Owner complaints are few — 25 unverified NHTSA reports — but include 7 crashes and 4 injuries, with service brakes (5), electrical (5) and forward collision avoidance (4) the most frequently cited areas.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask about brake feel, warning lights or any brake service, given the service brakes complaints on file.
  3. Check for unexpected forward collision alerts or braking during the test drive.
  4. Confirm the instrument display and rearview camera behave normally, and verify both recalls were performed by VIN.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
