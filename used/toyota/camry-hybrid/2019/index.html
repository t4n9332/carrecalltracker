Buying a used 2019 Toyota Camry Hybrid? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 11 owner complaints on file

The 2019 Camry Hybrid carries one critical recall — a fuel leak affecting Camry and Avalon hybrids — alongside a minor load-capacity label recall. The fuel leak recall did not carry a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Only 11 unverified NHTSA complaints are on file, but they report 3 crashes and 4 injuries, a high ratio for that volume; cited areas are air bags, visibility/wipers, seat belts, and fuel/propulsion. No fires were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get dealer documentation that the fuel leak recall repair was completed
  3. Check for any fuel odor in the cabin or near the tank
  4. Ask whether the airbag or seat belt systems have ever been serviced or replaced
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
