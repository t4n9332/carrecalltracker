Buying a used 2023 Toyota Camry Hybrid? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

The 2023 Camry Hybrid has one critical recall with a stop-driving warning: lug nuts that may loosen and allow a wheel to detach. The second recall is medium severity — poorly welded second-row headrest brackets. Because the car is recent, the complaint record is nearly empty: a single unverified NHTSA complaint, filed under fuel system, with no crashes, fires, or injuries. That thin record means recall completion status matters more here than complaint patterns.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get written confirmation the lug nut recall was completed before taking delivery
  3. Inspect the second-row headrests and ask if the bracket recall was performed
  4. Run the VIN yourself rather than relying on the seller's word
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
