Buying a used 2020 Toyota Camry Hybrid? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

Two recalls apply to the 2020 Camry Hybrid: a critical one for engine cracks that can leak coolant, shared with the RAV4, and a high-severity front passenger airbag that may not inflate. Neither carried a stop-driving or park-outside warning. The unverified NHTSA complaint volume is low at 9, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported — but 8 of the 9 concern air bags, which lines up with the airbag recall rather than the engine issue.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine coolant-leak recall inspection or repair was done
  3. Check coolant level and look for residue or leak traces around the engine
  4. Verify the passenger airbag occupant-detection light works when someone sits up front
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
