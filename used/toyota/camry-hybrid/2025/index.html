Buying a used 2025 Toyota Camry Hybrid? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 157 owner complaints on file

Six recalls, most of them electronic. The critical item is an inverter bolt that can cause a loss of power; a high-severity one covers a dash display failure that also disables the hazard lights, and two more address dashboards not showing speed or warnings and rearview cameras that may freeze. A rear center seat belt and a Gulf States label error round it out. None carry stop-driving or park-outside advisories. The unverified NHTSA complaints cluster in structure, electrical and visibility.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for dealer proof the inverter bolt recall was completed
  3. At startup, confirm the dash shows speed and warnings and that the hazard lights work
  4. Cycle the rearview camera several times to see if the image freezes
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
