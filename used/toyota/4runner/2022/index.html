Buying a used 2022 Toyota 4runner? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 39 owner complaints on file

The 2022 4Runner's recall record is light. The only mechanical item is a driver-side high beam that may not work; the other two are weight-label issues — labels that can fade and an incorrect weight limit sticker. Nothing is rated high or critical and none carry stop-driving or park-outside advisories. NHTSA holds 39 unverified owner complaints (5 crashes, 1 fire, 7 injuries described), spread across suspension, air bags and forward collision avoidance with no clear concentration.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Switch on the high beams and confirm the driver-side lamp lights.
  3. Compare the door-jamb weight limit label against the correct rating for this trim.
  4. Inspect the suspension for noise or play and confirm no airbag warning light is lit.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
