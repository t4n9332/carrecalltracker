Buying a used 2019 Toyota 4runner? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 64 owner complaints on file

The 2019 4Runner repeats the two significant 2018 recalls — possibly loose steering and suspension bolts, and the fuel pump that can fail and stall the engine — but drops the grille trim action. The other two are labeling only: a TRD weight-rating sticker and a load capacity label that can become unreadable. Its 64 unverified NHTSA complaints (5 crashes, 1 fire, 3 injuries described) spread thinly across steering, electrical and fuel/propulsion, with no one area dominating.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the fuel pump recall repair is documented; ask whether the engine has ever stalled.
  3. Ask for the repair order covering the steering and suspension bolt recall.
  4. Test drive for steering vagueness or noise and check the load capacity label is legible.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
