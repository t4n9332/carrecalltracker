Buying a used 2019 Toyota Prius? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 30 owner complaints on file

The 2019 Prius has one high-severity recall, hybrids that can lose braking assist, along with a dashboard display that can go blank and a load capacity label that may become unreadable. None carry a stop-driving or park-outside warning. The complaint record echoes the brake recall: of 30 unverified NHTSA reports, 11 concern service brakes, and seven crashes and two injuries are logged across the total. Engine and electrical reports follow well behind.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the braking assist recall repair appears in this VIN's service history.
  3. On the test drive, note pedal feel and any brake warning lights or messages.
  4. Check the dash display stays lit through startup and driving, and that the load capacity label is legible.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
