Buying a used 2019 Toyota Prius? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 30 owner complaints on file

This model year has a high-severity recall for brake assist that may fail, also covering RAV4 Hybrid models, plus a medium-severity issue where the instrument display may go blank, and a low-severity load capacity label defect. NHTSA has 30 owner complaints on file, unverified reports, including 7 crashes and 2 injuries. The most common complaint category is service brakes (11), followed by unknown or other (8), engine (6), and electrical system (4).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake assist recall has been completed
  3. Test brake performance and pedal feel during a test drive
  4. Ask about the instrument display going blank at any point
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
