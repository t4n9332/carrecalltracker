Buying a used 2025 Toyota Prius? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 16 owner complaints on file

This model year has one high-severity recall for a rear door that may open while driving, plus a medium-severity rearview camera freeze issue shared with some Lexus and Subaru models. NHTSA lists 16 owner complaints, unverified reports, including 1 crash and 1 injury. The leading complaint category is electrical system (6), followed by unknown or other (3), forward collision avoidance (3), and power train (2). Neither recall is flagged as stop-driving or park-outside.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the rear door recall has been completed
  3. Test that the rear doors latch securely closed
  4. Confirm the rearview camera and forward collision system work properly
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
