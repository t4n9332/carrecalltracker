Buying a used 2019 Toyota Avalon Hybrid? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

The 2019 Avalon Hybrid has one critical recall — a fuel leak affecting Camry and Avalon Hybrid models — plus a high-severity recall for airbags that may not deploy, shared with Tundra and Sequoia, and a load capacity label that can become unreadable. Neither serious recall carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. The complaint record is nearly empty: a single unverified NHTSA report, concerning the fuel system, with no crashes, fires or injuries described.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the Camry/Avalon Hybrid fuel leak recall was completed for this specific VIN.
  3. Ask whether the airbag non-deployment recall repair was performed, and check the airbag light.
  4. Smell for fuel odor around the tank area and after refueling.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
