Buying a used 2023 Toyota Corolla Cross? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 174 owner complaints on file

This model year has four high-severity recalls: two concerning a passenger air bag that may fail to deploy, and two concerning a steering cable/defect that may disable the air bag. NHTSA has 174 owner complaints on file, including 6 crashes, no reported fires, and 5 injuries; these are unverified owner reports. Top complaint areas are engine (102) and electrical system (68), followed by steering (44) and visibility/wiper (25).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all four airbag and steering-related recalls were completed
  3. Ask about any steering complaints or repairs
  4. Ask about electrical system or wiper/visibility issues
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
