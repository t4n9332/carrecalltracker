Buying a used 2022 Toyota Tacoma? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 57 owner complaints on file

This model year has recalls for child seat anchors that may fail, a rear axle shaft separation risk, and a low-severity fading weight label. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA total 57, including 11 crashes and 10 injuries reported, with no fires noted; power train (10) is the top complaint category, followed by unknown/other and air bags (8 each) and structure (7). These figures reflect unverified owner-submitted reports to NHTSA.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the child seat anchor recall is completed
  3. Confirm the rear axle shaft recall is completed and inspect the axle
  4. Ask about any airbag-related repairs or complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
