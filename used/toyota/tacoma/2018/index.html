Buying a used 2018 Toyota Tacoma? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 203 owner complaints on file

Three recalls, all high severity: a brake part that may weaken stopping power, and the Toyota/Lexus fuel pump failure that can stall the engine, covered by two separate campaigns. The unverified complaint record lines up with them — of 203 owner reports to NHTSA, service brakes (46) and fuel/propulsion (41) are the two largest categories, followed by power train. Seven crashes and 2 injuries were reported, no fires. No recall carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the brake recall is closed, then test stopping power and note any long or soft pedal travel.
  3. Ask whether the fuel pump recall was done and whether the truck has ever stalled or failed to start.
  4. Request service records for brake and fuel system work and run the VIN for any still-open recall.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
