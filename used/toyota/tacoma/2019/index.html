Buying a used 2019 Toyota Tacoma? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 218 owner complaints on file

Recalls for this model year include a brake master cylinder seal problem, a fuel pump failure risk linked to stalling (covered by two notices), and a low-severity label that may become unreadable. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA total 218, including 11 crashes, 1 fire, and 6 injuries reported; service brakes (56) and fuel/propulsion system (55) lead the categories, followed by power train (40) and engine (28). These are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake master cylinder seal recall is completed
  3. Confirm the fuel pump recall is completed and ask about any stalling history
  4. Check that the load capacity label is legible
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
