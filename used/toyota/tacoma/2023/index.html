Buying a used 2023 Toyota Tacoma? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 54 owner complaints on file

Recalls for this model year include a child seat anchor failure risk, a rear axle shaft separation risk, and a steering cable defect that can disable the air bag, the steering issue appearing in two related notices. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA total 54, with 5 crashes, 1 fire, and 5 injuries reported; electrical system (12) and unknown/other (10) lead the categories, followed by steering (9) and air bags (8), based on unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering cable/air bag recall is completed
  3. Confirm the rear axle shaft and child seat anchor recalls are completed
  4. Ask about any electrical or steering issues experienced
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
