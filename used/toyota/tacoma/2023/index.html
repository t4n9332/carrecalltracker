Buying a used 2023 Toyota Tacoma? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 54 owner complaints on file

This year carries four high-severity recalls, two of them airbag-related: steering wiring that can disable the driver's airbag, and a separate recall for vehicles that may have a disabled driver's airbag. It also repeats the 2022 rear axle separation and child seat anchor weld recalls. NHTSA shows 54 owner complaints — unverified reports — led by electrical system, steering and air bags, with 5 crashes, 1 fire and 5 injuries. The airbag cluster stands out.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check both driver's airbag recalls are closed and the airbag light behaves normally at startup
  3. Confirm the rear axle separation recall repair was performed
  4. Ask whether the child seat anchor weld recall was completed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
