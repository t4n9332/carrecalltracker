Buying a used 2024 Toyota Tacoma? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 149 owner complaints on file

This model year carries recalls for a rear brake hose leak risk, an instrument panel that may fail to show warning lights, a shock absorber reservoir that can detach, and two separate label errors overstating weight capacity. Owner complaints filed with NHTSA total 146, with 5 crashes and 32 injuries reported, and 1 fire; power train (52) and service brakes (34) are the leading categories, followed by unknown/other (21) and electrical system (16), per unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rear brake hose recall is completed and inspect the hoses
  3. Confirm the instrument panel and shock absorber reservoir recalls are completed
  4. Ask about any power train or brake issues experienced
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
