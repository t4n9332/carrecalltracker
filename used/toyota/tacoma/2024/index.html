Buying a used 2024 Toyota Tacoma? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 155 owner complaints on file

The 2024 Toyota Tacoma has 5 recalls covering rear brake hose leaks, missing instrument panel warnings, a detachable shock absorber reservoir, and two weight label issues. The label corrections concern loading information; the other campaigns address braking, warning displays, and suspension hardware. NHTSA lists 155 owner complaints, which are unverified reports, including 55 power train, 34 brake, 25 unknown or other, and 16 electrical entries. Reported outcomes include 6 crashes, 1 fire, and 33 injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify rear brake hose recall completion and ask about fluid leaks.
  3. Confirm instrument panel and shock reservoir recall repairs; inspect corrected weight labels.
  4. Ask about power train symptoms and electrical faults, with repair records.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
