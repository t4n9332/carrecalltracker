Buying a used 2020 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 13 owner complaints on file

Five recalls, weighted toward restraints: a critical engine crack that can leak coolant, high-severity front suspension arms that may crack, a driver air bag that may underperform in a crash, a front passenger air bag that may not inflate, and a medium recall for sudden loss of power steering assist. Owner complaints are comparatively light — 13 unverified reports to NHTSA with no crashes, fires or injuries — but air bags lead them with four, echoing the two air bag recalls.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check the VIN for all four open recalls, especially the two air bag repairs
  3. Watch that the air bag and passenger-occupancy lights behave normally at startup
  4. Ask whether steering has ever gone heavy suddenly, and inspect the front control arms
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
