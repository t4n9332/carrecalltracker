Buying a used 2025 Toyota RAV4 Hybrid? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

Two recalls apply to this model year: a rearview camera freeze issue and an incorrect weight capacity label recall. NHTSA lists 5 unverified owner complaints, including 1 crash and 1 injury. Visibility is the most cited category (3), with structure, air bags, and power train each appearing once.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both the camera freeze and weight label recalls are completed
  3. Ask if the vehicle has been in any reported crash
  4. Test the rearview camera and check for structural or air bag warning lights
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
