Buying a used 2021 Toyota Sienna Hybrid? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 42 owner complaints on file

Four recalls: a front passenger airbag that may not inflate, loose third-row seatback bolts, a stability-control fault on hybrids, and one that applies only to wheelchair-conversion vans. The NHTSA file holds 42 unverified owner reports, 13 of them about air bags, then brakes, structure and seats, with one fire and one injury noted. Airbags appear in both the recall list and the complaint file, which is the clearest pattern for this year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the front passenger airbag recall repair and watch the occupant light cycle at startup.
  3. Fold down the third row and confirm the seatback bolt recall was performed.
  4. Test the brakes at varied speeds; brakes are the second-largest unverified complaint group.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
