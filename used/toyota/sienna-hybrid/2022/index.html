Buying a used 2022 Toyota Sienna Hybrid? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 15 owner complaints on file

Four recalls, three of them about how occupants are held or the van stays stable: second-row seat belts that can tear, third-row seatback bolts that may be loose, and a hybrid stability-control fault. The fourth covers wheelchair-conversion restraints only. Fifteen NHTSA complaints — unverified owner reports — spread across brakes, electrical, lane departure and structure, including one crash and one injury. No single area dominates, though seating hardware appears in both the recalls and the structure reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Pull out both second-row belts and inspect the webbing for tearing; confirm that recall was done.
  3. Check third-row seatback bolt recall completion before carrying passengers back there.
  4. Ask about brake service history; brakes lead the unverified complaint areas for this year.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
