Buying a used 2025 Toyota Sienna Hybrid? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 266 owner complaints on file

Seats dominate this model year's record: two recalls cover badly welded second-row seat rails and possibly loose third-row seatback bolts, and 212 of 266 unverified NHTSA owner complaints fall under SEATS. A separate recall covers rearview cameras that may freeze on Toyota, Lexus and Subaru Solterra models. Two more recalls apply only to wheelchair-conversion vans, where restraints may not lock a wheelchair. Reported totals include 2 crashes, 7 injuries, no fires; none of the recalls call for stopping driving or parking outside.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN for the second-row seat rail weld and third-row seatback bolt recalls; ask if both repairs are done.
  3. Slide, fold and latch every second- and third-row seat, feeling for play, rocking or rattles.
  4. Start the van and confirm the rearview camera image appears promptly and does not freeze.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
