Buying a used 2025 Toyota Sienna Hybrid? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 266 owner complaints on file

This 2025 Sienna Hybrid has five recalls: loose third-row seat bolts, flawed second-row seat rails, wheelchair restraints that may not lock, a rearview camera that may freeze, and a related wheelchair restraint issue. NHTSA has logged 266 owner complaints for this model year (unverified reports), with 2 crashes and 7 injuries and no fires reported. Seats account for 212 of those 266 complaints, far outnumbering the next categories of an unknown/other group at 29, structure at 15, and visibility at 12.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the second-row seat rail and third-row seat bolt recalls were completed
  3. Inspect all rows of seats and seat rails given the high complaint volume in that area
  4. If it's a wheelchair-accessible van, confirm the restraint lock recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
