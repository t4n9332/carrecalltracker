Buying a used 2020 Toyota Corolla Hybrid? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

A very short record: one recall for hybrids that can lose braking assist and one for rear seat belts that may not lock, shared with the C-HR. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside advisory. Just a single owner complaint has been filed with NHTSA for this model year, in the electrical system category, with no crashes, fires or injuries reported — though a complaint count that small reflects reporting volume as much as reliability.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the hybrid braking assist recall was completed
  3. Pull and lock each rear seat belt to check the retractors
  4. Ask whether the seat belt recall parts were replaced or only inspected
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
