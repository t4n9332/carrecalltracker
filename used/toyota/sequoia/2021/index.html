Buying a used 2021 Toyota Sequoia? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

This Sequoia model year has two recalls, both related to power steering leaks. Neither is listed as stop-drive or park-outside. NHTSA has 4 owner complaints on file, including 1 crash and 1 injury report; these are unverified owner accounts. Complaint categories include power train, fuel/propulsion system, structure, and suspension, each with 1 report. Buyers should confirm whether the power steering recalls have been addressed on this specific vehicle before purchase.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both power steering leak recalls were completed
  3. Ask about any past crash or steering-related repairs
  4. Inspect power steering system for leaks during test drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
