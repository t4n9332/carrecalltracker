Buying a used 2021 Toyota Sequoia? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

Two recalls, both steering-related: a part that can leak fluid, and a separate campaign for possible loss of power steering assist. Those are the systems to document. The NHTSA complaint file is small — four unverified owner reports spread thinly across power train, fuel, structure and suspension, one describing a crash and one an injury. With so few reports no pattern is visible, so the recalls are the more solid signal on this year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get VIN-level confirmation that both steering recalls were completed.
  3. Look under the front end for power steering fluid residue or recent seal work.
  4. On the test drive, note steering effort, wander, or any warning lights.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
