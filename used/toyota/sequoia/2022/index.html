Buying a used 2022 Toyota Sequoia? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This year's record is short: two recalls, both about steering — a component that can leak fluid, and a possible loss of power steering assist. Just one NHTSA complaint has been filed, an unverified owner report, and it also concerns steering; no crashes, fires or injuries are attached. One report is too little to call a trend, but it falls in the same system the recalls cover, making steering the area to examine most closely.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm on the VIN that both steering recalls have been performed.
  3. Check the steering gear area for leaks or fluid staining before buying.
  4. Drive at parking-lot and highway speeds, noting any change in steering effort.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
