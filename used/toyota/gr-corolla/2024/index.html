Buying a used 2024 Toyota GR Corolla? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

The same two recalls as the prior year apply: a medium-severity instrument panel defect where speed and warnings may not display, and a low-severity Gulf States Toyota weight capacity label correction. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. The NHTSA complaint record is very thin — 5 unverified filings with no crashes, fires or injuries — citing power train (3), engine (2) and vehicle speed control. Too few reports to read any pattern from.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the dashboard display recall was completed and the cluster works at startup
  3. Ask whether the car was tracked, modified or launched hard, and review drivetrain service records
  4. Confirm the weight capacity label on the door jamb is correct
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
