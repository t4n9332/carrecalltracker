Buying a used 2024 Toyota Land Cruiser Hybrid? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 38 owner complaints on file

The 2024 Land Cruiser Hybrid's three recalls are all mid- or low-severity: a rearview camera that can freeze, an instrument cluster that may hide warning lights, and an incorrect weight capacity label. Nothing is rated high, and none require stopping driving or parking outside. More notable is the complaint pattern: 38 unverified NHTSA reports with no crashes, fires, or injuries, but 15 of them about service brakes and eight about power train. Brake behavior deserves close attention on a test drive.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Brake hard and from low speed on the test drive, noting pedal feel, noise, or hesitation.
  3. Confirm the cluster warning lights illuminate and clear at startup and the camera image appears promptly.
  4. Check the weight capacity label was corrected, especially if you plan to tow or load heavily.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
