Buying a used 2018 Toyota Avalon? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 24 owner complaints on file

The 2018 Avalon carries just two recalls, both high severity: the Toyota/Lexus fuel pump that can stall the engine, and a Corolla/Avalon action for airbags that may not deploy in a crash. Neither has a stop-driving or park-outside advisory. NHTSA holds 24 unverified owner complaints (7 crashes, 4 injuries described) — a small pool, but air bags is the largest labeled category at 6, overlapping the airbag recall, followed by steering and fuel/propulsion.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the fuel pump recall repair is documented and ask about any stalling or no-start history.
  3. Confirm the airbag recall was completed and that no airbag warning light stays on.
  4. Ask whether the car has been in a crash where airbags did or did not deploy.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
