Buying a used 2021 Toyota Avalon? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 21 owner complaints on file

This 2021 Avalon has two high-severity recalls, both related to airbags: one for a driver airbag that may underperform in a crash, and another for a sensor that may block airbag deployment. NHTSA has 21 owner complaints filed for this model year, including 1 crash report and no reported fires or injuries. More than half of those complaints (12 of 21) fall under air bags, with smaller numbers for engine (4), vehicle speed control (3), and forward collision avoidance (2). These are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether both airbag-related recalls have been completed
  3. Confirm any airbag warning lights or deployment issues have been resolved
  4. Ask about any engine or speed control complaints during ownership
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
