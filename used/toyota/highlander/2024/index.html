Buying a used 2024 Toyota Highlander? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 42 owner complaints on file

This model year has seven recalls: underrated tires on blackout package and accessory wheels, an instrument panel that may fail to show warnings, a second-row seat back that may not lock, a rearview camera that may freeze, a third-row seat belt anchor defect, and a weight label with incorrect numbers. NHTSA has recorded 42 owner complaints, including 4 crashes and 5 injuries; unknown/other (13) is the most-cited category, followed by service brakes (8), fuel/propulsion system (7), and electrical system (6). These complaints are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if blackout package or accessory tires were replaced for correct rating
  3. Test the second-row seat back lock and third-row seat belt anchor
  4. Verify the instrument panel warnings and rearview camera work correctly
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
