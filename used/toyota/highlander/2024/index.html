Buying a used 2024 Toyota Highlander? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 42 owner complaints on file

Seven recalls cover the 2024 Highlander, four rated high: two for tires with insufficient load ratings, one of them Blackout Package specific, plus a misaligned third-row center belt anchor and second-row seat backs that may not lock. The remainder are a dash display fault, a rearview camera freeze, and a mislabeled weight capacity on Gulf States cars. None require stopping driving. NHTSA has 42 unverified owner complaints, including 4 crashes and 5 injuries, clustering in brakes, fuel/propulsion, and electrical.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the tire load-rating recall was done and installed tires match the door-jamb placard.
  3. Test the third-row center belt and latch each second-row seat back until it locks.
  4. Ask about brake or drivetrain issues, and check the camera and dash display at startup.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
