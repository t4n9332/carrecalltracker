Buying a used 2022 Toyota Highlander? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 242 owner complaints on file

Five recalls apply to this model year, three tied to tires: blackout package tires and accessory tires rated below requirements, plus a second-row seat back that may not lock, a front bumper cover that may detach, and a weight label that may fade. NHTSA has recorded 242 owner complaints, including 16 crashes and 1 fire; power train (51) and structure (42) are the most-cited categories, followed by service brakes (37) and unknown/other (35). These complaints are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask if underrated tires (blackout package or accessory) were replaced
  3. Test that the second-row seat back locks in place
  4. Check the weight/capacity label is legible
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
