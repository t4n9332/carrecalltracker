Buying a used 2024 Toyota Tundra? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 334 owner complaints on file

This model year has ten recalls, including high-severity issues covering unexpected movement in neutral, tires that may lose air, and engine stalling from bearing failure (listed twice, at both high and medium severity). Medium-severity items include a reverse light that can fail from moisture and two separate rearview camera display problems. Three low-severity recalls involve label errors overstating weight or load capacity. NHTSA has logged 333 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 2 crashes and 3 fires, no injuries; top categories are engine (147), power train (83), fuel/propulsion system (46), and unknown or other (36).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the neutral-rollaway and tire air-loss recalls were completed
  3. Verify the engine bearing-failure stall recall was addressed
  4. Test rearview camera and reverse light function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
