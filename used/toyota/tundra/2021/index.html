Buying a used 2021 Toyota Tundra? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 24 owner complaints on file

This model year carries three recalls: a critical-severity headlight wiring issue that can overheat, and power steering leaks, listed as two separate high-severity recalls covering the steering gear and steering system. NHTSA has logged 24 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 1 crash, 1 fire, and 1 injury; the top complaint categories are unknown or other (6), electrical system (4), seats (4), and visibility/wiper (3).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the headlight wiring recall was completed
  3. Verify both power steering leak recalls were repaired
  4. Ask about seat and visibility/wiper complaint history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
