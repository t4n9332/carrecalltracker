Buying a used 2019 Toyota Tundra? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 72 owner complaints on file

This model year carries ten recalls: a critical headlight wiring issue that can overheat, high-severity airbags that may not deploy, fuel pumps that may fail and stall the engine (two separate recalls), and power steering leaks (listed twice), plus a medium-severity dim turn signal issue and three low-severity load-label errors. NHTSA has logged 72 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 4 crashes, 2 fires, and 7 injuries; top categories are fuel/propulsion system (15), unknown or other (15), electrical system (13), and forward collision avoidance (11).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm headlight wiring and airbag recalls were completed
  3. Verify both fuel pump recall repairs were done
  4. Ask about forward collision avoidance system function history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
