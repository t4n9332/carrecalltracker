Buying a used 2019 Toyota C-HR? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 107 owner complaints on file

Two safety recalls dominate the 2019 C-HR: rear wheel bolts that may loosen, rated high, and rear seat belts that may not lock, rated medium; a load-capacity label recall is minor. None carried a stop-driving or park-outside warning. The complaint record is heavier than the recall list suggests — 107 unverified NHTSA complaints with 3 crashes and 3 injuries — and 58 of those fall under power train, an area no recall here addresses.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the rear wheel bolt recall was completed and have the rear wheels inspected
  3. Test the rear seat belts for locking, especially for a child seat
  4. Drive at varied speeds watching for CVT/drivetrain hesitation or noise
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
