Buying a used 2019 Toyota Sienna? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 45 owner complaints on file

This 2019 Sienna has seven recalls, including a critical door weld defect that may affect air bag deployment, a critical steering shaft fracture risk on Braun-modified mobility vans, mobility van brake power loss, and two separate fuel pump failure recalls, plus low-severity label issues. NHTSA has logged 45 owner complaints for this model year (unverified reports), including 2 crashes and 4 injuries with no fires reported. An unknown/other category leads complaints at 8, with power train and fuel/propulsion system each at 7 and structure at 6.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the door weld/air bag recall was completed
  3. Confirm both fuel pump recalls were completed
  4. If it's a mobility van, verify the brake power loss recall was addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
