Buying a used 2025 Toyota RAV4? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 85 owner complaints on file

As a nearly new vehicle, the record is short. The most serious item is a high-severity recall for front seat brackets that may be poorly welded; alongside it are the familiar instrument-panel display and rearview-camera freeze recalls. None carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Owners have filed 85 unverified complaints with NHTSA, thinly spread across electrical, engine and wiper/visibility, with seven mentioning a crash and seven an injury.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the front seat bracket recall was inspected or repaired on this VIN
  3. Push and pull on both front seats to check for looseness or play in the frame
  4. Verify the instrument-panel and backup-camera software remedies were performed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
