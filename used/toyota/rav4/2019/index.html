Buying a used 2019 Toyota RAV4? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 876 owner complaints on file

This is the busiest year of the group: seven recalls, headed by a critical engine crack that can leak coolant, plus front suspension arms that may crack, fuel pumps that can fail and stall the engine, backup cameras that may not turn on, and sudden loss of power steering assist; two are label corrections. The complaint record matches — 876 unverified owner reports, concentrated in fuel/propulsion (255) and engine (204), with 25 crashes and 21 injuries cited.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN for all seven recalls, especially the engine coolant leak and the fuel pump
  3. Check coolant level and look for residue or staining around the engine, and ask about overheating history
  4. On the test drive, confirm steering effort stays constant and the backup camera comes on every time
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
