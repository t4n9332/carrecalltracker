Buying a used 2019 Toyota RAV4? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 876 owner complaints on file

This model year has seven recalls, including a critical engine coolant leak issue, a front suspension arm crack recall, electric power steering water damage, and a fuel pump failure recall, plus a back-up camera issue and two label recalls. NHTSA records 876 unverified owner complaints, with 25 crashes, 2 fires, and 21 injuries. Fuel/propulsion (255) and engine (204) lead the categories, followed by unknown/other (169) and electrical system (102).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the coolant leak, suspension arm, and fuel pump recalls were completed
  3. Ask about electric power steering repair history, especially after water exposure
  4. Test the back-up camera and check for stalling or fuel system warning lights
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
