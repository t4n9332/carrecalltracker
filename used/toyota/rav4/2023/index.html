Buying a used 2023 Toyota RAV4? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 153 owner complaints on file

Three medium-severity recalls, none carrying stop-driving or park-outside warnings: faulty valve stems on accessory wheels, an instrument panel that may not display speed or warnings, and a rearview camera that can freeze. The valve-stem item only applies if the car wears Toyota accessory wheels. Owners have filed 153 unverified complaints with NHTSA, spread across electrical and 'unknown or other' with a noticeable steering cluster of 26; 18 of the reports mention a crash and 14 an injury.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check whether the wheels are factory or Toyota accessory wheels, and whether the valve-stem recall applies
  3. Verify the speedometer and warning lights display normally from the moment you start the car
  4. Note any pull, wander, or uneven steering assist during the test drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Toyota dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
