Buying a used 2023 Rivian R1S? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 74 owner complaints on file

Nine recalls, none critical and none requiring stop-driving or parking outside, but several are occupant-protection related: loosely fastened side curtain airbags, trim clips affecting curtain airbags, and loose front seat belt anchors and bolts. A rear suspension link that may come apart and disabled windshield defroster controls are also open, alongside minor headlight aim, reverse light, and weight-label items. This year drew 74 NHTSA complaints — 10 crashes, 3 injuries, no fires — concentrated in airbags, electrical system, and seats. Complaints are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Pull the VIN recall status for the two curtain airbag campaigns and the seat belt anchor and bolt campaigns
  3. Test the windshield defroster controls and confirm the defroster software recall was applied
  4. Ask about any airbag, seat, or electrical faults the seller has experienced, and check for stored warning lights
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Rivian dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
