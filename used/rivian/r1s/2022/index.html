Buying a used 2022 Rivian R1S? Check these 12 recalls first

12 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

This 2022 R1S carries ten high-severity recalls, spanning seat belt anchors (front and second-row), a loose steering fastener, side curtain airbags improperly secured, airbag trim clips, a passenger seat belt sensor that may disable the airbag, a defroster that may stop working, an accelerator pedal glitch, and a rear toe link that may separate. NHTSA logged only 4 owner complaints (unverified reports) — 3 involving seats and 2 involving air bags — with 1 crash and 2 injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify all seat belt anchor and second-row seat belt recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the side curtain airbag and airbag trim clip recalls were addressed
  4. Ask about the rear toe link and steering fastener recall repairs
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Rivian dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
