Buying a used 2025 Rivian R1S? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 54 owner complaints on file

This model year carries 10 recalls, six high severity: a front seat belt anchor risk, loss of drive power, a highway assist system that may miss vehicles, a rear toe link that may separate, loose second-row seat belts, and front turn signals that may fail. Medium recalls involve lighting and cruise control markings. NHTSA logged 53 owner complaints, including 5 crashes, 1 fire, and 4 injuries; unknown/other led at 14, followed by forward collision avoidance and exterior lighting at 9 each, and electrical system at 8. These are unverified reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm seat belt anchor, toe link, and turn signal recalls were completed
  3. Ask about any drive power loss or highway assist incidents
  4. Test exterior lights, headlights in cold, and cruise control markings
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Rivian dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
