Buying a used 2025 Rivian R1S? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 53 owner complaints on file

Recalls on this year lean heavily toward lighting and electronics: low beams that fail in cold, exterior lights that may not turn on, and two separate front turn signal failure campaigns, plus loss of drive power and driver-assist that may miss a vehicle ahead. Loose seat belt anchors and bolts and a rear suspension link that may separate carry over. None require stop-driving. Fifty-three unverified NHTSA complaints include five crashes, one fire, and four injuries, led by forward collision avoidance, exterior lighting, and electrical system.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Cycle every exterior light — low beams, turn signals, running lights — and ask whether the lighting recalls were performed
  3. Confirm the drive-power-loss and driver-assist recall software updates are installed on this VIN
  4. Ask the seller directly about any sudden power loss, warning messages, or driver-assist braking events
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Rivian dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
