Buying a used 2025 Rivian R1T? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 10 owner complaints on file

Seven recalls on this year: loose front seat belt anchors and loose seat belt bolts, a rear suspension link that may come apart, loss of drive power, driver-assist that may fail to detect a vehicle ahead, and two separate front turn signal failure campaigns. None require stop-driving or parking outside. Only ten NHTSA complaints so far, with no reported crashes, fires, or injuries — suspension leads, then electrical and power train. Complaint counts on a recent model year are still accumulating, and these reports are unverified.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the drive-power-loss and driver-assist recall updates are installed, and ask if the vehicle has ever lost propulsion
  3. Operate both front turn signals and ask whether either turn signal recall was performed
  4. Verify the rear suspension link and seat belt bolt recalls are closed on the VIN
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Rivian dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
