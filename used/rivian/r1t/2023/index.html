Buying a used 2023 Rivian R1T? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 38 owner complaints on file

This model year carries seven recalls covering a defroster that may stop working, damaged airbag trim clips, a front seat-belt anchor risk, a rear toe link that may separate, loose second-row seat belts, misaimed headlights, and an incorrect weight label. Owners have filed 38 complaints with NHTSA, including 3 crashes and 1 fire; air bags account for 14 of those complaints, the largest single category, followed by electrical system (6), unknown/other (5), and service brakes (5). These are owner-reported and unverified.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the airbag trim clip and seat-belt anchor recalls have been completed
  3. Test the defroster and headlight aim on a test drive
  4. Review service records for the rear toe link and second-row seat belt recalls
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Rivian dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
