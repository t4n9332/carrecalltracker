Buying a used 2022 Rivian R1T? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 66 owner complaints on file

This 2022 R1T carries ten high-severity recalls, including a front passenger airbag that may not switch off, seat belt anchor issues (front and second-row), a loose steering fastener, a passenger seat belt sensor that may disable the airbag, a defroster that may stop working, an accelerator pedal glitch, airbag trim clips, and a rear toe link that may separate. NHTSA logged 66 owner complaints (unverified reports), with suspension (16) the top category, and 7 crashes and 8 injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the front passenger airbag and airbag sensor recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the seat belt anchor and steering fastener recall repairs were performed
  4. Ask about the rear toe link and accelerator pedal glitch recalls
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Rivian dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
