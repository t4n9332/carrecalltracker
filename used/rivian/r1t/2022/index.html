Buying a used 2022 Rivian R1T? Check these 11 recalls first

11 NHTSA recalls · 66 owner complaints on file

First-year R1T with ten recalls, including a critical loose steering bolt and two passenger airbag issues — one where the airbag may not switch off for a child seat, one where it may not deploy. Others cover seat belt anchors and bolts, curtain airbag trim clips, defroster controls, accelerator pedal release, and a rear suspension link that may come apart. None require stop-driving. Sixty-six NHTSA complaints — 7 crashes, 8 injuries, no fires — center on suspension, forward collision avoidance, and steering; these are unverified.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the critical steering bolt recall is closed on the VIN and ask about any steering noise or play on the test drive
  3. Confirm both passenger airbag recalls were performed, and watch the passenger occupancy indicator with weight in the seat
  4. Have the rear suspension links inspected and confirm the suspension link recall repair was completed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Rivian dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
