Buying a used 2022 Rivian EDV? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 7 owner complaints on file

This commercial van carries two open safety campaigns: a critical loose steering bolt affecting Rivian trucks and SUVs, and a damaged driver's seat belt tensioner cable specific to the EDV. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Complaint volume is very low — seven filings with NHTSA, none reporting a crash, fire, or injury — but five of the seven concern seat belts, echoing the tensioner recall. Those owner complaints are unverified reports, not confirmed defects.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN and confirm the critical steering bolt recall was actually performed, not just mailed
  3. Ask for paperwork showing the driver's seat belt tensioner cable was inspected or replaced
  4. Latch, tug, and fully retract both front belts during the walkaround and watch for belt warning lights
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Rivian dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
