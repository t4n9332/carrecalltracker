Buying a used 2025 MINI Countryman S ALL4? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Four open recalls, three of them serious: loss of brake assist power, front seat belts that may not restrain, and integrated brakes that may fail and need inspection, plus a lower-severity campaign for loose front body bolts shared with the BMW 228i. None is flagged stop-driving or park-outside. Only one unverified owner complaint has been filed with NHTSA, citing service brakes, hydraulic and stability control — very thin data for a current model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask the dealer for VIN status on all four campaigns, brakes and seat belts first
  3. Test both front seat belt retractors for proper locking and retraction
  4. Confirm the front body bolt inspection was performed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any MINI dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
