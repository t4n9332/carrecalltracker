Buying a used 2020 MINI Cooper S? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

Two open recall campaigns touch crash safety on this model year: a BMW/MINI defect where the airbags may not deploy, and a separate campaign for hardtops missing rear crash padding. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Owner complaints to NHTSA are few — five unverified reports, including one crash and one injury — spread thinly across engine, vehicle speed control, service brakes and steering, so no single pattern dominates the complaint record.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN to confirm the airbag recall repair was completed
  3. Ask whether the rear crash padding campaign was performed on this hardtop
  4. On the test drive, note any engine, brake or steering irregularity
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any MINI dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
