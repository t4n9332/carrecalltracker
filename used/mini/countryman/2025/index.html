Buying a used 2025 MINI Countryman? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 17 owner complaints on file

Two open recalls: a brake system that can lose power assist, and a rear armrest that can come loose in a crash. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Complaint volume is what stands out for so new a vehicle — 17 unverified reports to NHTSA, including two crashes and two injuries — led by electrical system with seven and service brakes with six, plus four forward collision avoidance reports. Electronics and brakes are the areas to probe.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake power assist recall was completed for this VIN
  3. Ask about electrical, screen or driver-assist faults and any dealer visits for them
  4. Check the rear armrest recall status and that the armrest is secure
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any MINI dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
