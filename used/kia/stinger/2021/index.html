Buying a used 2021 Kia Stinger? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 17 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This model year carries a critical engine-fire recall advising owners to park outside until repaired, along with a turbo oil-leak recall shared with the K900. Two fuel-pump recalls address inadequate fuel supply and loss of power, plus a minor fuel-gauge accuracy recall. NHTSA logged 17 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year, including 1 crash and no reported fires or injuries; service brakes and general/unspecified issues were the most-cited categories, followed by engine and fuel system reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine-fire recall repair has been completed (advisory to park outside until fixed)
  3. Ask about fuel pump recall repairs (inadequate supply / power loss)
  4. Have the brake system inspected given it's a commonly cited complaint area
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
