Buying a used 2019 Kia Stinger? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 44 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Three recalls apply: two critical fire risks — the brake control unit, which carries park-outside advice, and a turbo oil leak — plus a high-severity fuel pump flaw that can cause sudden power loss. Unverified NHTSA complaints total 44, with 3 fire reports, 1 crash, and no injuries. Engine and service brakes are nearly tied at the top of the complaint areas, with electrical system and fuel/propulsion behind them. It's a shorter list than 2018's.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake control unit, turbo oil leak, and fuel pump recalls are all closed by VIN
  3. Check for turbo oil seepage and any smell of burning oil after a drive
  4. Test the brakes for pedal feel and warning lights — brakes are a leading complaint area
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
