Buying a used 2018 Kia Stinger? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 67 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Four recalls apply to this model year: a wiring harness fire recall, an engine compartment fire risk recall with a park-outside advisory, a turbo oil leak recall, and a fuel pump loss-of-power recall. 67 owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA (unverified reports), including 5 fires, 2 crashes, and 2 injuries; engine complaints make up 22 of the 67, followed by electrical system at 11 and fuel/propulsion system at 10.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the wiring harness fire recall and engine compartment fire recall were both completed
  3. Ask about the turbo oil leak recall and any oil consumption issues
  4. Ask about the fuel pump recall completion and brake system service history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
