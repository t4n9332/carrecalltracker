Buying a used 2018 Kia Optima? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 232 owner complaints on file

This year has two recalls: a high-severity recall addressing a battery protection device issue on the Optima Hybrid, and a medium-severity recall for a trunk latch that may crack and could trap someone inside. NHTSA has 232 owner complaints on file (unverified reports), including 10 crashes, 10 fires, and 9 injuries. Engine is the most-cited complaint category with 131 mentions, followed by unknown/other at 37, electrical system at 33, and power train at 18.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the battery protection device recall applies to this specific vehicle and was completed
  3. Confirm the trunk latch recall was addressed
  4. Request service records on any engine-related repairs or complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
