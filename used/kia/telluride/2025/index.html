Buying a used 2025 Kia Telluride? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 111 owner complaints on file

The recall list here is shorter than earlier Telluride years: a front seat back that may fail in a crash, a wrong spare tire, and detaching door belt molding trim. None carry stop-driving or park-outside advisories. Owners have filed 111 unverified NHTSA complaints so far (3 crashes, 2 fires, 5 injuries), with engine the largest category at 40 — a different pattern from the electrical-heavy complaint mix of 2022–2024 cars. Two fire reports appear in that count though no fire-related recall is listed for 2025.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the front seat back recall was remedied and check both front seats for play or looseness.
  3. Open the spare tire well and verify the spare matches the correct specification.
  4. Ask about any engine running, stalling, or noise issues and request service records.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
