Buying a used 2020 Kia Telluride? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 756 owner complaints on file · 3 park-outside

The launch year carries three critical fire recalls — tow hitch wiring and two separate power seat motor risks — all with park-outside advice, plus a high-severity rollaway-in-park recall and medium recalls covering child seat belt securing, trailer brake lights with smart cruise, and high beam headlights. Unverified NHTSA complaints are very high at 756, with 9 fires, 8 crashes, and 11 injuries. Exterior lighting leads at 183, then engine and visibility/wiper — a heavy lighting and glass theme.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get VIN proof all three fire recalls and the rollaway recall were completed
  3. Test both front power seats for motor noise or heat, and check the high beams
  4. Ask about headlight, taillight, and windshield/wiper issues — those areas draw the most owner complaints
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
