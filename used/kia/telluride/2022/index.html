Buying a used 2022 Kia Telluride? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 321 owner complaints on file · 3 park-outside

This model year carries three separate fire-risk recalls — tow hitch wiring, a stuck front seat knob, and an overheating power seat motor — each with a park-outside advisory but no stop-driving order. A rollaway-in-park recall and two blank-dashboard-screen campaigns also apply, plus a wrong-spare-tire notice. Owners filed 321 unverified NHTSA complaints (10 crashes, 4 fires, 9 injuries), clustering in visibility/wiper and electrical system categories, which tracks with the display and wiring recalls.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN and confirm all three fire-risk recalls (tow hitch wiring, seat knob, power seat motor) are closed.
  3. Cold-start the car and watch that the dashboard screen comes up, then test wipers and lights.
  4. Confirm the rollaway-in-park recall was done, and that the car holds on an incline in Park.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
