Buying a used 2024 Kia Telluride? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 215 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

This model year includes a critical recall for engine valve springs that may break, plus two fire-risk recalls — seat knob overheating and power seat motor — each advising owners to park outside until repaired. A driveshaft recall addresses a rollaway risk, and a separate recall covers door trim molding that can peel off. NHTSA logged 215 owner complaints (unverified reports) for this year, including 6 crashes, 2 fires, and 4 injuries; general/unspecified issues, electrical system, visibility/wiper, and forward collision avoidance items were the most-cited categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the engine valve spring recall and both fire-risk seat recalls were repaired
  3. Ask about the driveshaft rollaway recall completion
  4. Have the forward collision avoidance system tested given it's a cited complaint area
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
