Buying a used 2024 Kia Telluride? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 215 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

Three critical fire recalls apply here: engine parts that may break and cause a fire, plus the two seat-motor fire campaigns that carry park-outside advisories. A rollaway-in-park recall and a low-severity door belt molding trim recall round out the list. NHTSA shows 215 unverified owner complaints (6 crashes, 2 fires, 4 injuries); electrical system leads the named areas at 47, followed by visibility/wiper and forward collision avoidance at 28.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm by VIN that the engine fire recall and both seat-motor fire recalls are completed.
  3. Ask whether the driver-assist/forward collision warning has ever falsely activated or been serviced.
  4. Verify the rollaway-in-park remedy, and inspect the door belt molding for detachment.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
