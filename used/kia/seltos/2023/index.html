Buying a used 2023 Kia Seltos? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 46 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Both open recalls on this year are critical and point at the same area: an oil pump that can overheat, which Kia paired with advice to park outside until repaired, and a separate stall-or-fire engine defect. NHTSA logs 46 owner complaints, which are unverified reports, with engine and electrical system the largest groups, plus four crashes and three injuries; no fires were reported. A handful concern forward collision avoidance. Recall completion on the specific VIN is the main thing to establish.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get VIN-specific proof both critical recalls (oil pump, stall/fire) were completed
  3. Ask whether it was parked outside and if it has ever stalled or thrown warning lights
  4. Test forward collision warning and all electrical accessories during the drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
