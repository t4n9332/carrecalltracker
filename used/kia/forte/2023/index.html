Buying a used 2023 Kia Forte? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 61 owner complaints on file · 2 do-not-drive

This 2023 Forte has two critical recalls for a front control arm weld that may fail, both carrying a stop-driving advisory, plus a high-severity recall for a front steering knuckle that may crack. Owners filed 61 complaints with NHTSA, unverified reports, including 11 crashes, 4 fires, and 5 injuries. Steering issues account for 14 of the 61 complaints, electrical system for 10, and engine and forward collision avoidance each cited 9 times.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the front control arm weld recalls (stop-driving advisory) have been completed
  3. Verify the front steering knuckle recall repair
  4. Ask about any steering-related repairs or complaints history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
