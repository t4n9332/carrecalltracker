Buying a used 2018 Kia Forte? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 136 owner complaints on file

Engine issues stand out here. One recall covers an oil pump problem that can damage the engine, and engine is by far the largest owner-complaint area, 57 of 136 unverified reports filed with NHTSA. A second recall addresses airbags that may not deploy, matching 16 air bag complaints, and a third covers a trunk that cannot be opened from inside. The complaint file also lists 13 crashes, three fires and nine injuries, all unverified.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for oil change records and proof the oil pump recall repair was done
  3. Verify the airbag recall remedy and that the airbag warning light cycles normally
  4. Test the trunk's interior emergency release handle
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
