Buying a used 2021 Kia Sportage? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 50 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

Two recalls apply to this model year, both fire-related with a park-outside advisory: one for the brake module, one for the tow hitch. 50 owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA (unverified reports), including 7 fires, 3 crashes, and 4 injuries; engine complaints total 21 of the 50, the largest category, with unknown/other at 14, and engine cooling and electrical system each at 6.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake module fire recall was completed
  3. Confirm the tow hitch fire recall was completed
  4. Ask about engine cooling system service history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
