Buying a used 2018 Kia Sportage? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 163 owner complaints on file · 2 park-outside

This model year has three recalls tied to fire risk: a brake module recall and a tow hitch recall, both with a park-outside advisory, plus a fuel pipe leak recall. 163 owner complaints have been filed with NHTSA (unverified reports), including 12 fires, 7 crashes, and 5 injuries; engine complaints account for 90 of the 163, the largest single category, followed by electrical system at 29 and unknown/other at 19.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the brake module fire recall was completed and the park-outside advisory cleared
  3. Confirm the tow hitch fire recall was completed
  4. Ask about the fuel pipe leak recall and any engine-related repair records
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
