Buying a used 2024 Kia Sportage? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 68 owner complaints on file

The recall picture is shorter than 2023: one high-severity recall for a sudden loss of power steering assist, plus medium recalls for roof trim that may fall off and an instrument screen that can go blank. None carry stop-driving or park-outside advice. Unverified NHTSA complaints number 68 with 7 crashes, 9 injuries, and no fires. Beyond unspecified issues, electrical system leads, followed by forward collision avoidance and engine — driver-assist behavior is worth a close test drive.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the power steering recall remedy was performed and test for heavy or inconsistent steering effort
  3. Check that the instrument cluster screen wakes reliably and stays on
  4. Ask whether the forward collision system has ever braked unexpectedly or thrown warnings
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
