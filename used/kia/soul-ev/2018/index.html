Buying a used 2018 Kia Soul EV? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 221 owner complaints on file

The 2018 Soul EV has a critical recall for high-voltage battery fire risk, plus two high-severity recalls covering possible air bag non-deployment and a parking mechanism that may be damaged. NHTSA logged 221 owner complaints, including 15 crash, 14 fire, and 12 injury reports. Top complaint categories, as recorded by NHTSA, are engine (141), power train (32), unknown/other (30), and electrical system (21). These are unverified owner-submitted reports, not confirmed defects.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the high-voltage battery fire risk recall was completed
  3. Confirm the air bag deployment recall was completed
  4. Test the parking mechanism/park-lock function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
