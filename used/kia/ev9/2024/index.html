Buying a used 2024 Kia EV9? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 207 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This 2024 EV9 has a critical battery fire recall, a high-severity differential gear bolt recall shared with the Niro EV, and a high-severity front passenger seat belt defect recall. Further medium-severity recalls cover ceiling lining crash protection, parking assist software, instrument screen blackouts, and rear seat mounting bolts. Owners filed 207 complaints with NHTSA, unverified reports, including 1 crash, 2 fires, and 4 injuries. Electrical system issues account for 102 of the 207 complaints, with 39 unknown or other, 24 forward collision avoidance, and 21 suspension.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm battery fire recall remedy status
  3. Verify the differential gear bolt and seat belt defect recalls are completed
  4. Ask about the remaining medium-severity recalls: parking assist software, instrument screen, seat mounting bolts
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
