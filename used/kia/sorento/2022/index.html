Buying a used 2022 Kia Sorento? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 187 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

The 2022 Sorento has six recalls: three critical (a turbo oil pipe that may crack, a tow hitch fire risk with a park-outside advisory, and blower motor wiring fire risk), one high-severity recall for possible power loss, and two medium-severity recalls for an incorrect spare tire and rearview camera clips that may break. NHTSA logged 187 owner complaints, including 4 crash, 1 fire, and 4 injury reports. Top categories are power train (63), engine (52), unknown/other (37), and electrical system (30); these are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm turbo oil pipe, tow hitch, and blower motor wiring recalls were completed
  3. Verify the spare tire included matches the correct specification
  4. Inspect rearview camera mounting clips for damage
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
