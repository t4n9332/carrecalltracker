Buying a used 2021 Kia Sorento? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 12 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

The 2021 Sorento has four recalls: a critical fuel line leak recall, a critical tow hitch fire risk recall advising owners to park outside, a critical blower motor wiring fire risk recall, and a high-severity recall for possible loss of power while driving. NHTSA has 12 owner complaints on file for this model year, with no reported crashes, fires, or injuries. Top complaint categories are power train (10), engine (4), fuel/propulsion system (3), and electrical system (2). These are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm fuel line leak recall was completed
  3. Confirm tow hitch fire recall and blower motor wiring recall completion
  4. Ask about any sudden power-loss incidents or power train warning lights
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
