Buying a used 2018 Kia Sorento? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 252 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

The 2018 Sorento has three recalls: a critical tow hitch fire risk recall advising owners to park outside, a side curtain air bag component recall, and a steering column separation recall. NHTSA logged 252 owner complaints for this model year, including 12 crash reports, 11 fire reports, and 11 injury reports. The largest complaint category is engine at 104, followed by exterior lighting at 41, unknown/other at 37, and electrical system at 29. Complaints are unverified owner reports, not confirmed defects.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm tow hitch fire recall completed and whether park-outside advisory still applies
  3. Confirm side curtain air bag component recall was completed
  4. Confirm steering column recall was completed and ask about steering feel/noise
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
