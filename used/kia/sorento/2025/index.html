Buying a used 2025 Kia Sorento? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 73 owner complaints on file

The 2025 Sorento has three high-severity recalls: possible loss of headlights and taillights, engine bolts that may loosen, and instrument panel display failure. NHTSA logged 73 owner complaints for this model year, including 5 crash and 3 injury reports, with no fires reported. Top complaint categories are electrical system (21), unknown/other (20), engine (19), and power train (19). These figures are unverified owner-submitted reports, not confirmed defects.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the headlight/taillight recall was completed
  3. Confirm the engine bolt recall was completed
  4. Test the instrument panel display for glitches or failure
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
