Buying a used 2019 Kia Sorento? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 426 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Only two recalls are listed, tow hitch wiring fire risk with outdoor parking advised and a backup camera that can go blank from water intrusion, but the complaint record is the largest of this group: 426 unverified owner reports including 19 fires, 16 injuries and 10 crashes. Engine dominates by a wide margin, followed by electrical system and structure. The gap between a short recall list and heavy engine complaint volume defines this model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for full engine service history, including any warranty engine work or replacement
  3. Confirm the tow hitch wiring repair and inspect for added trailer wiring
  4. Test the backup camera and look for water intrusion around the tailgate
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
