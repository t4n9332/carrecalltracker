Buying a used 2023 Kia Sorento? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 119 owner complaints on file

The 2023 Sorento has one critical recall, for blower motor wiring fire risk, plus two medium-severity recalls covering an airbag warning label that may detach and rearview camera clips that may break. NHTSA logged 119 owner complaints, including 3 crash, 2 fire, and 3 injury reports. Top complaint categories are electrical system (37), unknown/other (35), engine (30), and power train (20). These figures are unverified owner-submitted reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm blower motor wiring fire risk recall was completed
  3. Check whether the airbag warning label is present and attached
  4. Inspect rearview camera clips for looseness or breakage
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
