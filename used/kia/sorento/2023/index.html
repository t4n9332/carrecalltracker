Buying a used 2023 Kia Sorento? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 119 owner complaints on file

Three recalls, led by a critical one: heater fan wiring that can catch fire. Neither that nor the backup-camera image failure or the peeling airbag warning labels on the visors carries a stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Owners filed 119 unverified complaints with NHTSA, including 3 crashes, 2 fires and 3 injuries; electrical system (37) leads, followed by unclassified reports (35), engine (30) and power train (20). The electrical pattern echoes the recall themes.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the heater fan wiring recall repair is completed on this VIN.
  3. Test the backup camera over several cold and warm starts for a blank image.
  4. Check that both sun visors still have their airbag warning labels attached.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
