Buying a used 2021 Kia K5? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 164 owner complaints on file

This year has six recalls: two critical involving a fuel line leak (shared with Sorento) and fuel tank damage that raises fire risk, plus four high-severity recalls for an airbag sensor that may fail, power steering effort that can suddenly increase, possible loss of power while driving, and side curtain airbags that may not deploy properly. NHTSA has logged 164 owner complaints (unverified reports) with 8 crashes, 2 fires, and 7 injuries; electrical system and fuel/propulsion system are the most-cited complaint categories, followed by unknown/other and engine.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the fuel line leak and fuel tank fire-risk recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the airbag sensor and side curtain airbag recalls were addressed
  4. Test steering feel and watch for sudden power loss during a test drive
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
