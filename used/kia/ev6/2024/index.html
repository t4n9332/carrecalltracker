Buying a used 2024 Kia EV6? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 74 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

The shortest EV6 recall list: the battery-cell fire recall with its park-outside advisory, plus the two charging unit recalls for loss of drive power that have followed the model since 2022. Neither the roll-away nor the drive shaft recall applies to this year. Of 74 unverified owner complaints, electrical accounts for 52, with power train (16) and fuel/propulsion (15) next. Relative to that volume, 6 crashes, 1 fire, and 3 injuries were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the charging unit recalls were performed and ask about any charging or drive-power failures
  3. Verify the battery-cell fire recall remedy on this VIN before purchase
  4. Ask about any electrical faults or warning lights, by far the largest complaint area here
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
