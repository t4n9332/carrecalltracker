Buying a used 2023 Kia Sorento Phev? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 8 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

Only two recalls apply: tow hitch wiring that can catch fire, where Kia advised parking outside until repaired, and a backup camera that may not display an image. The complaint record is small, just eight unverified owner reports with no crashes, fires or injuries, touching electrical system, engine and fuel/propulsion. With so few reports there is little pattern to read either way, so the practical work is confirming both remedies and inspecting the plug-in hardware yourself.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the tow hitch wiring recall was completed, especially if a hitch is fitted
  3. Check the backup camera displays reliably across several restarts
  4. Ask for charging history and have the plug-in system and battery checked
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
