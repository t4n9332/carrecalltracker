Buying a used 2025 Kia K4? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 45 owner complaints on file

A first-year model with two recalls: engines that may lose drive power, and an instrument display that can go blank while driving. Neither has a stop-driving or park-outside warning. For a new nameplate, 45 unverified complaints have already been filed with NHTSA, led by electrical (12), with seven citing lane departure systems and seven the engine. Those filings report 10 crashes and eight injuries but no fires. Driver-assistance behavior is worth judging yourself.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the drive power and instrument cluster recall remedies were applied
  3. Watch the instrument display through a full drive cycle for blanking
  4. Test the lane departure and lane keeping systems on a highway stretch
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
