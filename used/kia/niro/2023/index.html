Buying a used 2023 Kia Niro? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 44 owner complaints on file

This year has two recalls: a high-severity recall for seat wiring that may disable air bags, and a medium-severity recall covering dashboard displays that may go blank. NHTSA has 44 owner complaints on file (unverified reports), including 8 crashes and 6 injuries, no fires. Unknown/other is the most-cited complaint category with 15 mentions, followed by electrical system at 14, then forward collision avoidance at 7 and power train at 6.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the seat wiring/airbag recall was completed
  3. Confirm the dashboard display recall was addressed
  4. Test the dashboard display and forward collision system for proper operation
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
