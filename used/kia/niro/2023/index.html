Buying a used 2023 Kia Niro? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 44 owner complaints on file

The 2023 Niro has two open recalls: wiring damage that may affect airbags and seat belts, rated high, and a dashboard display that can go blank. Neither carries a stop-driving or park-outside warning. Its 44 NHTSA owner complaints — unverified reports — concentrate in the electrical system (14) and forward collision avoidance (7), with powertrain reports as well. Eight crashes and six injuries appear across that complaint file.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the airbag/seat belt wiring recall and display recall were both done
  3. Start the car and watch the instrument display for blanking or delayed boot
  4. Ask whether forward collision warning has ever activated falsely or faulted
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
