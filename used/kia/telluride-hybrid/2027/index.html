Buying a used 2027 Kia Telluride Hybrid? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 8 owner complaints on file

This 2027 Telluride Hybrid has two high-severity recalls: a power seat that may trap someone, and a driver seat belt that may not extend fully. NHTSA has 8 owner complaints on file for this model year, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported; these are unverified owner accounts. Complaints span several categories, with electrical system (3) and engine (2) most cited, followed by wheels (1) and power train (1).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm whether the power seat entrapment recall repair was completed
  3. Ask if the driver seat belt extension recall was addressed
  4. Ask about any electrical system or engine issues, given the complaints in those categories
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
