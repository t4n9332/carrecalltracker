Buying a used 2022 Kia Carnival? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 133 owner complaints on file · 1 park-outside

This 2022 Carnival has three critical recalls: a fuel rail that may leak, tow hitch wiring with a fire risk (park-outside advisory), and a fuel pipe connection that may leak. A high-severity recall covers a power sliding door that may open while moving, plus medium recalls for doors that may not auto-reverse and roof molding (shared with the Sportage) that may detach. NHTSA logged 133 owner complaints for this model year, including 4 crashes and 34 injuries with no fires; unspecified-other (34) and structure (32) lead, followed by electrical system (22) and engine (16). Unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm tow hitch wiring fire-risk recall (park-outside advisory) was completed if towing package installed
  3. Verify fuel rail and fuel pipe connection recalls were repaired
  4. Test power sliding doors for auto-reverse function and unintended opening while moving
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
