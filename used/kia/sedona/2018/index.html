Buying a used 2018 Kia Sedona? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 48 owner complaints on file

This model year carries a high-severity recall for air bags that may fail to deploy in a crash, plus two medium-severity recalls covering the power sliding door's auto-reverse function and seat sensor wiring that may break. None are listed as stop-driving or park-outside. NHTSA has logged 48 owner complaints, including 4 fire reports, 1 crash, and 1 injury; the top categories are engine (21), electrical system (11), air bags (6), and structure (3). These are unverified owner reports.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the air bag recall repair was completed
  3. Test the power sliding door's auto-reverse function
  4. Ask about any engine or electrical issues reported by the seller
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Kia dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
