Buying a used 2018 Mercedes-Benz Metris? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 28 owner complaints on file

Ten recalls, including two critical fire-related items: an uncovered backup battery that can short, and fuel hoses that can leak. High-severity entries cover driveshaft bolts that can loosen and fall out, sudden power steering loss, a loose brake fluid tank, and two campaigns for vans that skipped final safety inspection. Of 28 unverified NHTSA complaints — none reporting crashes, fires or injuries — half concern tires, with wheels and steering also appearing.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for a dealer printout showing all ten recalls closed, especially both fire items
  3. Inspect tires and wheels, and check the door tire load rating label
  4. Test power steering assist and have driveshaft bolts inspected
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
