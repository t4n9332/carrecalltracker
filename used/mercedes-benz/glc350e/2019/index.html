Buying a used 2019 Mercedes-Benz Glc350e? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 63 owner complaints on file

Two separate high-severity recalls cover a roof panel that can detach while driving, alongside seat belt buckles that may falsely read as unfastened, a passenger airbag light that can mislead, and a mislabeled airbag warning in the wrong language. None carries stop-driving or park-outside guidance. The unverified complaint record is the largest here: 63 filed with NHTSA, including 4 crashes, 4 fires and 4 injuries, concentrated in electrical system, engine and structure.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Inspect the panoramic roof panel and confirm both roof recalls were completed
  3. Test seat belt buckle warnings and the passenger airbag indicator
  4. Ask about the hybrid electrical system and engine repair history
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
