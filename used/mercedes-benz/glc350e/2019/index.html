Buying a used 2019 Mercedes-Benz Glc350e? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 63 owner complaints on file

This GLC350E has five recalls, ranked high for passenger air bag status displaying incorrectly, seat belts sometimes registering as unbuckled, and a sunroof panel that may detach; a separate recall covers the front roof panel, and a low-severity one covers a mislabeled airbag warning. NHTSA has logged 63 owner complaints on this model year, including 4 crash reports, 4 fire reports, and 4 injury reports (unverified owner-submitted data). The most common complaint categories are electrical system (17) and unknown/other (16), followed by engine (14) and structure (7).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the passenger air bag status, seat belt buckle sensor, and sunroof/roof panel recalls have been completed
  3. Have the electrical system inspected given it is the top complaint category
  4. Request service records covering any fire, crash, or injury incident on this specific vehicle
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
