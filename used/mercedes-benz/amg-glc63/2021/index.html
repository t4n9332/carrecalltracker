Buying a used 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG GLC63? Check these 6 recalls first

6 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

This model year carries six recalls, including two high-severity ones: a seat switch that may prevent airbags from working properly, and an AMG fuel pump that may shut down and cause power loss while driving. Other recalls cover a possible wrong-location emergency call, rearview camera software failure, a headlight connection that may not be watertight, and loss of the automatic emergency call system. NHTSA lists 2 owner complaints (unverified reports), split across suspension, powertrain, engine, and electrical system, with no reported crashes, fires, or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seat switch/airbag recall has been completed
  3. Ask whether the AMG fuel pump recall was performed
  4. Check headlight connector sealing and rearview camera software update status
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
