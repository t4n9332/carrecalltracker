Buying a used 2018 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 2500? Check these 9 recalls first

9 NHTSA recalls · 14 owner complaints on file

This Sprinter 2500 has nine recalls, none rated critical. High-severity items cover a right-side door that won't open, rear side windows with the wrong glass, airbags that may be inactive, a skipped final inspection step, and power steering hoses that may leak. Medium and low items involve the hood possibly opening while driving and incorrect or missing spare tire speed labels. NHTSA has logged 14 owner complaints (unverified), with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported; vehicle speed control (4) is the leading category, followed by steering (3) and power train (3).

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the right-side door and inactive airbag recalls have been completed
  3. Test power steering operation and check for hose leaks given that recall
  4. Verify the final inspection recall was completed and documented
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
