Buying a used 2025 Mercedes-Benz E 350? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

Same pair of recalls as the 4MATIC version: a critical rear axle bracket that may break loose, which carries a stop-driving instruction until repaired, and a high-severity dashboard display that can go blank while driving. The complaint record is very small — two unverified reports to NHTSA touching electrical system, visibility/wipers and engine, with no crashes, fires or injuries. With this model year barely in the field, recall completion status is more informative than complaint counts.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the rear axle bracket recall repair is documented for this VIN before any test drive.
  3. Watch the instrument cluster through a full drive for blank screens or restarts.
  4. Test the wipers and washers on all settings, given the visibility/wiper complaint on file.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
