Buying a used 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter (VS30)? Check these 14 recalls first

14 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file · 1 do-not-drive

This Sprinter has ten recalls, two critical: a steering shaft that can disconnect (the only one of these ten flagged stop-driving) and blower wiring that may overheat. Other recalls involve a brake hose that can wear through, incorrect owner's manual guidance on auto-parking, missing chassis stabilization, incorrect rear leaf spring shims, a fuel pump that may leak, steering hoses that may leak fluid, a stability control software issue on camper builds, and rear fog lights that may not work. NHTSA has logged 4 owner complaints (unverified), including 1 crash and 1 fire report.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering shaft disconnection recall (marked stop-driving) has been completed
  3. Verify the blower wiring overheating recall and brake hose wear recall have been addressed
  4. Ask for details on the reported crash and fire incident, if known to the seller
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
