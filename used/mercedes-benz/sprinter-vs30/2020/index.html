Buying a used 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter (VS30)? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 4 owner complaints on file

A shorter list than the 2019 van. One critical recall covers fan wiring that poses a fire risk, with no stop-driving or park-outside instruction. Two high-severity recalls address swivel seat airbag wiring and front brake discs of the wrong specification. Four unverified owner complaints are on file with NHTSA — spread across structure, equipment, power train and vehicle speed control — with no crashes, fires or injuries reported, a light record for a commercial van.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the fan wiring fire risk recall was completed
  3. Verify the correct front brake discs were installed under recall
  4. Ask whether swivel seats are fitted and the airbag wiring was checked
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
