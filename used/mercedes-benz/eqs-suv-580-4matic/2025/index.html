Buying a used 2025 Mercedes-Benz EQS SUV 580 4MATIC? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

The recall record here is short. One critical campaign covers Mercedes EQ electric models that may suddenly lose driving power; it carries no stop-driving or park-outside instruction. A separate medium-severity recall addresses the wrong pedestrian backup warning sound, a compliance issue rather than a driving hazard. Only one unverified owner complaint has been filed with NHTSA, under power train, with no crash, fire or injury reported — a thin record typical of a recent model year.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the sudden power loss recall remedy was completed
  3. Ask whether the backup warning sound recall was addressed
  4. Ask if the vehicle ever lost drive power or entered reduced power mode
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
