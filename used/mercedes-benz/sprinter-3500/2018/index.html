Buying a used 2018 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 3500? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

This Sprinter 3500 has ten recalls, with the highest-severity items covering a right-side door that won't open, rear side windows with the wrong glass, airbags that may be inactive, a skipped final inspection step, power steering hoses that may leak, and stability control loss on camper builds. Medium and low items involve the hood possibly opening while driving and incorrect or missing spare tire speed labels. NHTSA has logged 9 owner complaints (unverified), with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported; service brakes (3) is the leading category.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the right-side door, inactive airbag, and power steering hose recalls have been completed
  3. If this is a camper conversion, verify the stability control recall was addressed
  4. Have the service brakes inspected given that is the top complaint category
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
