Buying a used 2018 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 3500? Check these 10 recalls first

10 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

Ten recall entries cover the familiar 2018 Sprinter set — power steering loss, hood latch, airbags possibly never switched on, wrong rear window glass, skipped final inspection and right-side doors that may not open — plus one specific to this chassis: 3500 campers can lose stability control. Two are spare-tire label items. Nine unverified owner complaints filed with NHTSA lead with service brakes and vehicle speed control; no crashes, fires or injuries were reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. If it is a camper conversion, confirm the stability control recall was performed.
  3. Have the brakes inspected — service brakes lead the complaint list.
  4. Check the power steering, hood latch and airbag recalls by VIN.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
