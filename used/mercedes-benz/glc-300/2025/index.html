Buying a used 2025 Mercedes-Benz GLC 300? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 16 owner complaints on file

Same three recalls as the 4MATIC version: a critical steering bolt that may loosen, a high-severity dashboard display that can go blank while driving, and a medium roof pillar padding issue, none with stop-driving or park-outside guidance. The unverified complaint count is higher here — 16 filed with NHTSA, three citing a crash and one an injury. Electrical system dominates at 12 reports, with engine and forward collision avoidance also appearing.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering bolt recall was completed by a dealer
  3. Ask about blank or glitching dashboard display episodes
  4. Ask whether any driver assistance or engine faults have been diagnosed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
