Buying a used 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG GLC43? Check these 7 recalls first

7 NHTSA recalls · 6 owner complaints on file

Seven recalls, two of them high severity: a seat switch that can disable airbags in a crash, and a fuel pump that can fail and cause a loss of power. Headlights appear twice — failure from water intrusion, and an aim set too high, which can blind oncoming drivers — and exterior lighting also shows up among the six unverified NHTSA complaints, which are led by engine reports. Backup camera and emergency call recalls round out the list. No crashes, fires or injuries recorded.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Inspect both headlight housings for moisture or fogging and confirm the aim recall was completed.
  3. Verify the seat switch/airbag and fuel pump recalls by VIN before purchase.
  4. Ask about any stalling or sudden power loss and request engine service records.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
