Buying a used 2019 Mercedes-Benz AMG GLA45? Check these 2 recalls first

2 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This model year has two medium-severity recalls, both tied to the emergency call system: one where it may send an incorrect location, and one where it may stop working altogether. NHTSA shows 1 owner complaint (an unverified report) for this vehicle, filed under the power train category, with no crashes, fires, or injuries recorded.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm both emergency call system recalls were completed
  3. Verify the emergency call system currently reports location correctly
  4. Ask the seller about any power train issues, including the reported complaint
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
