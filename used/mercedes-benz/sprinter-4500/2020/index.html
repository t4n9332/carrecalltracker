Buying a used 2020 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500? Check these 22 recalls first

22 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Ten recalls, matching the 2020 Sprinter set: loose fuel line clamps, leaking brake hoses and rear brake calipers, two power steering loss campaigns, two rollaway-after-Park entries, swivel seat wiring that can disable a side airbag, missing frame reinforcement fasteners and a driveshaft mount that can loosen. The complaint record is nearly empty — a single unverified owner report to NHTSA, filed under structure and equipment, with no crashes, fires or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Check that Park and the parking brake hold on an incline; confirm both rollaway recalls.
  3. Inspect for brake and power steering fluid leaks at hoses and calipers.
  4. Ask whether the frame fastener and driveshaft mount recalls were performed.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
