Buying a used 2019 Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 4500? Check these 38 recalls first

38 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file

This model year has 10 recalls, including three critical ones: an EGR pipe fire risk, an exhaust flange fire risk, and fuel line clamps that may loosen. Other high-severity recalls cover incorrect rear side window glass, airbags that may stay inactive, a skipped final inspection, a front seat weld that can break, possible diesel fuel leaks, loose steering bearing screws, and a missing steering column cover. NHTSA lists 9 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 1 crash and 1 fire; electrical system and forward collision avoidance issues are each cited 3 times.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask whether the EGR pipe and exhaust flange fire-risk recalls have been completed
  3. Confirm the fuel line clamp and steering bearing screw recalls were repaired
  4. Verify the rear side window glass and front seat weld recalls were addressed
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
