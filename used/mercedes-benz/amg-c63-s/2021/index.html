Buying a used 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG C63 S? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

Four recalls, no stop-driving or park-outside flags. The two high-severity ones are a seat switch that may disable airbags in a crash and a fuel pump that can fail and cause power loss. The medium pair covers rear headrests mounted incorrectly on Cabriolet models and a backup camera image that may not appear. NHTSA shows only 1 unverified owner complaint for this year, under seat belts — too few reports to read a pattern from.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Verify the seat switch and fuel pump recalls by VIN
  3. Check the backup camera image appears every time in reverse
  4. On a Cabriolet, confirm rear headrests sit correctly in their mounts
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
