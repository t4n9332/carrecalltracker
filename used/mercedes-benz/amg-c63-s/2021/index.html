Buying a used 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG C63 S? Check these 4 recalls first

4 NHTSA recalls · 1 owner complaints on file

This model year has four recalls: a seat switch that may stop air bags from working properly, a fuel pump that may shut down and cause power loss, convertible rear headrests that may be mounted incorrectly, and a rearview camera that may not display. NHTSA has only 1 owner complaint on file (unverified report), in the seat belts category, with no crashes, fires, or injuries reported.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the seat switch/airbag recall was completed
  3. Ask about fuel pump recall service history
  4. Check rear headrests and rearview camera function
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
