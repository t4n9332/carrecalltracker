Buying a used 2022 Mercedes-Benz EQB 350 4MATIC? Check these 5 recalls first

5 NHTSA recalls · 9 owner complaints on file · 4 park-outside

This model year has four critical recalls tied to the high-voltage battery, covering fire risk and possible loss of drive power; none are marked stop-driving, but all advise parking outside and away from structures. A separate high-severity recall addresses a front axle gear defect. NHTSA shows 9 owner complaints (unverified reports), with no reported crashes, fires, or injuries; electrical system appears in 8 of the 9, with fuel/propulsion system (2), an unspecified category (2), and engine (1) also mentioned.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm all four battery-related recalls have been completed
  3. Ask about the park-outside advisory tied to the battery recalls
  4. Have the front axle gear and battery system inspected
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
