Buying a used 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG G63? Check these 8 recalls first

8 NHTSA recalls · 2 owner complaints on file

For a 2021 G63 the recall list is the story: six high-severity campaigns, including a seat switch that can disable airbags, loose side crash sensor connectors, rear doors that may not latch or may open while driving, wiring that can disable brakes and stability control, and a fuel pump that can fail and cut power. Two medium recalls cover child-seat belt anchoring and the emergency call system. Owner complaints are few — two unverified NHTSA reports, no crashes, fires or injuries.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Run the VIN and confirm the brake/stability-control wiring and side crash sensor connector recalls were completed.
  3. Open and close both rear doors repeatedly and ask for the door latch recall repair records.
  4. Ask whether the seat switch/airbag and fuel pump recalls have been performed, and when.
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
