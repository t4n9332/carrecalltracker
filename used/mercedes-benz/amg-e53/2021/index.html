Buying a used 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG E53? Check these 15 recalls first

15 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

This model year has ten recalls, with three critical ones tied to wiring/cable fire risk, four high-severity recalls covering a front passenger seat missing an adjustment stop, airbag-related seat switches, side-impact airbag sensor loosening, and a steering wheel hands-off detection failure, and three medium recalls including a passenger seat positioned too close to the airbag, emergency call location errors, and a rearview camera software issue. NHTSA lists 5 owner complaints (unverified reports), with no crashes or fires and 0 injuries, most frequently touching forward collision avoidance and unknown/other categories.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Ask for proof the wiring/cable fire-risk recalls were completed
  3. Confirm the passenger seat position and seat-switch airbag recalls were addressed
  4. Test steering wheel hands-off detection and the rearview camera
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
