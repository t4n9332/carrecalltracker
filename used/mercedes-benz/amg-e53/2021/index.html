Buying a used 2021 Mercedes-Benz AMG E53? Check these 15 recalls first

15 NHTSA recalls · 5 owner complaints on file

Ten recalls, including the same three critical wiring and fire items: an AMG harness that may short, an E53/CLS53 cable that can overheat and catch fire, and transmission wiring that can short and burn. High-severity entries add a seat switch that may disable airbags, loose side crash sensor connectors, and a fuel pump that can quit and cut power. Medium items center on front passenger seat position versus the airbag, eCall accuracy and the backup camera. Five unverified complaints.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Get a VIN recall report showing all three fire recalls done
  3. Confirm the fuel pump and side crash sensor repairs were performed
  4. Check the front passenger seat travel and its distance to the airbag
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
