Buying a used 2025 Mercedes-Benz GLC 300 4MATIC? Check these 3 recalls first

3 NHTSA recalls · 10 owner complaints on file

This model year has two high-severity recalls: a steering coupling bolt that may loosen, and an instrument panel display that may go blank, plus a medium-severity recall for a roof pillar that may not absorb crash impact as designed. NHTSA lists 10 owner complaints (unverified reports), including 2 injuries and no reported crashes or fires; electrical system accounts for 6, with structure (2), forward collision avoidance (2), and powertrain (1) also mentioned.

Before you buy

  1. Get the 17-character VIN from the seller and run it at nhtsa.gov/recalls. It shows which recalls are still unrepaired on that exact car.
  2. Confirm the steering coupling bolt recall has been completed
  3. Ask if the instrument panel display recall was performed
  4. Have the electrical system and forward collision system tested
  5. Open recalls are repaired free at any Mercedes-Benz dealer, whoever owns the car — an open recall is a to-do item, not a bill.
