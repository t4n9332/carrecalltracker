Buick and Chevrolet Rearview Camera May Show Blank Image medium

Reported Aug 20, 2026 · Campaign 26V539000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

General Motors is recalling certain Buick Envista, Buick Encore GX, Chevrolet Trailblazer, and Chevrolet Trax vehicles because the rearview camera screen may display a distorted or blank image instead of showing what's behind the vehicle clearly.

Buick, Chevrolet Instrument Panel May Go Blank high

Reported Nov 6, 2023 · Campaign 23V744000 · Model years: 2024

A software problem can cause the instrument panel display to intermittently go blank. This can happen either right when the vehicle is started or at any point while it is being driven. When the screen goes blank, it stops showing information that is normally displayed there, such as the vehicle's speed and any warning lights that would otherwise be lit. As a result, the driver may not be able to see that information for as long as the screen stays blank.

Buick Encore GX and Chevrolet Trailblazer emergency jack can break high

Reported Jun 10, 2021 · Campaign 21V440000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

General Motors is recalling certain Buick Encore GX and Chevrolet Trailblazer vehicles because of a problem with the emergency jack that comes with the vehicle, the tool that lifts the vehicle off the ground. If the jack is not placed the way the owner's manual describes, it can break while it is holding the vehicle up. Dealers will replace the jack with a new one at no cost to the owner.

Chevrolet, Cadillac, Buick Models Lose Brake Assist high

Reported Sep 24, 2020 · Campaign 20V588000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Contamination at a sensor connection in the electronic brake boost system may interrupt communication between the sensor and the brake booster. This affects certain Chevrolet Corvette and Trailblazer, Cadillac CT4, CT5 and XT4, and Buick Encore GX vehicles. If that happens, the power assist that helps you brake could be lost, meaning you would need much more pedal force to slow or stop the vehicle. Dealers will replace the electronic brake boost module, free of charge.

Buick Encore GX recall questions

How many recalls does the Buick Encore GX have?

NHTSA lists 4 safety recalls covering the 2020–2025 Buick Encore GX. 3 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Buick and Chevrolet Rearview Camera May Show Blank Image" (NHTSA campaign 26V539000), reported Aug 20, 2026.

Which Buick Encore GX model year has the most recalls?

The 2020 Buick Encore GX has the most, with 2 recalls. The 2022 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Buick Encore GX?

No. None of the 4 Buick Encore GX recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Buick Encore GX has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Buick dealer, including for second owners.
