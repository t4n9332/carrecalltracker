Buick Regal Power Brake Assist Failure Risk high

Reported 2022-06-30 · Campaign 22V465000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

Certain Buick Regal vehicles may lose power brake assist if there is a vacuum-power brake assist failure. A software error in the brake control module can cause this loss of assist. Without power assist, it takes more distance to bring the vehicle to a stop, which increases the risk of a crash. Because of this issue, these vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard for brake systems.

Buick Regal Front Seat Frame Weld Defect high

Reported 2019-10-24 · Campaign 19V762000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Certain Buick Regal vehicles have a front seat frame with a lower crossbar that may not have been welded correctly, on either the driver's or passenger's seat. Seats with this kind of weld problem may not hold together as well as they should, which can affect how much protection the seat provides in a crash.

Chevrolet, Buick, Cadillac, GMC Rear Brake Issue high

Reported 2018-08-30 · Campaign 18V576000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The rear brake caliper pistons on these vehicles may not have enough of a protective coating, which can allow gas pockets to form inside them. If this happens, it can reduce how well the rear brakes perform. This affects a range of Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC models. To fix the problem, GM will notify owners, and dealers will bleed the vehicle's brake system. This repair is free of charge.

Buick Regal Rear Seat Belts May Be Misrouted high

Reported 2018-04-12 · Campaign 18V230000 · Model years: 2018

On some Buick Regal vehicles, the rear outboard seat belts may have been routed incorrectly around a bracket during manufacturing instead of the proper path. Because of this, the seat belt may not hold a passenger securely in place the way it should.
