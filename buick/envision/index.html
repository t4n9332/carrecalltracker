Buick Envision Missing Tire Information Label low

Reported Sep 25, 2024 · Campaign 24V710000 · Model years: 2024

General Motors is recalling certain Buick Envision vehicles because they are missing a label that lists the correct tire size, correct tire pressure, and vehicle loading information. Because this label is missing, that tire and loading information is not available to the owner where it is supposed to be. As a result, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard that covers tire and rim selection information for vehicles.

GM Vehicles Daytime Running Lights Stay On medium

Reported Dec 8, 2022 · Campaign 22V903000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

In certain Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet, and GMC vehicles, the daytime running lights may not turn off when the headlights are switched on. This can create glare and reduce visibility for other drivers.

GM vehicles' airbag warning light works inconsistently medium

Reported Jun 10, 2021 · Campaign 21V421000 · Model years: 2021

General Motors is recalling certain Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet and GMC vehicles. The module that passes messages between vehicle systems does not correctly handle a loss of communication with the airbag sensing module. As a result, the airbag warning light on the dashboard turns on inconsistently. A dealer can update that module's software, or the update can be sent to the vehicle over the air, free of charge. GM mailed owner notification letters on July 1, 2021.

Buick Envision recall questions

How many recalls does the Buick Envision have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2021–2024 Buick Envision. 0 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Buick Envision Missing Tire Information Label" (NHTSA campaign 24V710000), reported Sep 25, 2024.

Which Buick Envision model year has the most recalls?

The 2021 Buick Envision has the most, with 2 recalls. The 2022 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Buick Envision?

No. None of the 3 Buick Envision recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Buick Envision has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Buick dealer, including for second owners.
