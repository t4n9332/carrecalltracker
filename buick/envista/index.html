Buick and Chevrolet Rearview Camera May Show Blank Image medium

Reported 2026-08-20 · Campaign 26V539000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

General Motors is recalling certain Buick Envista, Buick Encore GX, Chevrolet Trailblazer, and Chevrolet Trax vehicles because the rearview camera screen may display a distorted or blank image instead of showing what's behind the vehicle clearly.

Buick, Chevrolet Instrument Panel May Go Blank high

Reported 2023-11-06 · Campaign 23V744000 · Model years: 2024

A software problem can cause the instrument panel display to intermittently go blank. This can happen either right when the vehicle is started or at any point while it is being driven. When the screen goes blank, it stops showing information that is normally displayed there, such as the vehicle's speed and any warning lights that would otherwise be lit. As a result, the driver may not be able to see that information for as long as the screen stays blank.
