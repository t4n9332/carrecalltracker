Ford and Lincoln rearview cameras may show blank image medium

Reported 2025-10-13 · Campaign 25V695000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Ford is recalling a range of Ford and Lincoln vehicles, including the Fiesta, Flex, Explorer, Escape, C-Max, Fusion, Taurus, Mustang and the Lincoln MKT and MKZ. On these vehicles, the camera that shows what is behind you when you shift into reverse may show a picture that is distorted, that cuts in and out, or that is blank. Ford says dealers will check the camera and replace it if needed.

Ford Taurus Door Trim May Detach While Driving medium

Reported 2025-09-12 · Campaign 25V611000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On certain Ford Taurus vehicles, the trim panel next to the front doors, known as the B-pillar trim, may come loose and detach while the vehicle is being driven. This can happen on the driver's side or the front passenger's side. A piece of trim detaching while driving can end up on the roadway.

Ford Flex, Taurus, Lincoln MKT: Rear Toe Link May Fracture high

Reported 2020-02-10 · Campaign 20V072000 · Model years: 2018

Ford is recalling certain Flex and Taurus vehicles, along with Lincoln MKT vehicles, that are equipped with the Police Interceptor or SHO Performance Pack. On these vehicles, a rear suspension part called a toe link may crack or break apart because of stress placed on the rear suspension. If this happens, the toe link could fail while the vehicle is being driven on the road.

Ford Taurus key can be removed outside of Park high

Reported 2018-03-01 · Campaign 18V141000 · Model years: 2018

Ford is recalling certain Taurus sedans that use a mechanical key ignition. On these cars, the key may come out of the ignition even when the transmission is not in Park. Federal theft-protection rules require the key to stay in place unless the vehicle is in Park, so these cars do not meet the standard. Dealers will replace the shifter assembly free of charge. The recall began May 11, 2018.
