Ford Super Duty Fuel Pump May Fail Again high

Reported 2026-03-17 · Campaign 26V158000 · Model years: 2022

Ford is recalling certain F-250 SD, F-350 SD, F-450 SD, F-550 SD, F-600 SD, and F-750 Super Duty trucks that were previously repaired incorrectly under recall 24V957. Biodiesel deposits may build up on the fuel pump's drivetrain roller components, which could cause the high-pressure fuel pump to fail. A fuel pump failure can cause a loss of drive power. Dealers will update the powertrain control module software, at no cost to the owner.

Ford Super Duty Trucks Fuel Pump Failure Risk medium

Reported 2024-12-20 · Campaign 24V957000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

In certain diesel trucks, deposits can form on parts of the fuel pump's drivetrain rollers over time. This buildup can cause the high pressure fuel pump to stop working properly. Dealers will fix the issue by updating the powertrain control module and transmission control module software, at no cost to you.

Ford Super Duty Fuel Filter Cap Can Leak critical

Reported 2022-01-14 · Campaign 22V013000 · Model years: 2021

On these Ford Super Duty and Medium Duty trucks with 6.7-liter diesel engines, a small void in the underhood secondary fuel filter cap may develop into a pinhole over time. This can allow fuel to leak from the top of the secondary fuel filter.

Ford F-650/F-750 Transmission Line May Leak, Cause Fire critical

Reported 2020-11-10 · Campaign 20V691000 · Model years: 2021

Ford is recalling certain F-650 and F-750 trucks equipped with gas engines. A transmission oil cooler line may rub against the air conditioning compressor pulley, and this contact can cause the line to leak transmission fluid. If the leaking fluid comes into contact with hot engine parts, it can create a risk of fire in the engine compartment.
