Ford Focus recalls

7 recalls covering model years 2018. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Ford Focus/Fusion Clutch Failure May Cause Fire critical

Reported 2026-06-09 · Campaign 26V376000 · Model years: 2018

Ford is recalling certain Focus and Fusion vehicles because two earlier repairs for this problem did not fully fix it. The clutch inside the transmission may crack or break, which can damage the transmission and cause a fluid leak. If that leaked fluid reaches a hot part of the engine or exhaust, it could ignite. Ford has sent owners an interim letter about this fire risk while it finishes developing the full repair for this problem.

Ford Focus Fuel System Valve May Stall Engine high

Reported 2026-06-09 · Campaign 26V369000 · Model years: 2018

Ford is recalling certain Focus vehicles because an earlier repair for this same defect did not fully correct it. A valve in the fuel system, called the canister purge valve, may malfunction and cause the engine to stall unexpectedly while you are driving. This is the same underlying issue that a previous recall was supposed to fix, but that repair did not resolve the problem completely, so affected vehicles still have the risk of an unexpected engine stall while on the road.

Ford Escape and Focus Block Heater Fire Risk critical

Reported 2026-01-15 · Campaign 26V011000 · Model years: 2018

The engine block heater in some Ford Escape and Focus vehicles can crack over time and leak coolant. If coolant leaks while the heater is plugged in, it can cause an electrical short circuit inside the heater. This short circuit raises the chance that a fire could start in the vehicle.

Ford Explorer and Focus Block Heater Fire Risk critical

Reported 2026-01-15 · Campaign 26V012000 · Model years: 2018

The engine block heater in some Ford Explorer and Focus vehicles can crack over time and leak coolant. If coolant leaks while the heater is plugged in, it can cause an electrical short circuit inside the heater. This short circuit raises the chance that a fire could start in the vehicle.

Ford Focus, EcoSport Oil Pump Belt May Fail high

Reported 2023-12-22 · Campaign 23V905000 · Model years: 2018

The belt that drives the oil pump, or the part that keeps it tight, may fail. This can cause the engine to lose oil pressure.

Ford Focus Clutch May Fracture, Risk Of Fire critical

Reported 2018-11-28 · Campaign 18V845000 · Model years: 2018

This recall covers certain 2016 to 2018 Ford Focus vehicles with a 1.0-liter engine and 6-speed manual transmission. The clutch may crack. A cracked clutch can damage the transmission and cause a transmission fluid leak. If that leak happens near hot engine or exhaust parts, which can act as an ignition source, it raises the chance of a fire starting in the vehicle.

Ford Focus Fuel System May Cause Stalling critical

Reported 2018-10-22 · Campaign 18V735000 · Model years: 2018

Ford says the Canister Purge Valve on some Focus vehicles with a 2.0L GDI or GTDI engine may malfunction, causing too much vacuum to build up in the fuel vapor system. This may cause the engine to stall while driving, without warning, and the vehicle may not be able to restart. Until the vehicle is serviced, Ford advises owners to keep the fuel tank at least half full.
