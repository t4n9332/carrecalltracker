Ford Expedition recalls

45 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Ford Expedition, Explorer and Lincoln SUVs May Roll Away high

Reported 2026-06-24 · Campaign 26V402000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021

The part inside the automatic transmission that locks the wheels when you shift into Park can catch while the vehicle is still moving. That can damage the parking system, so the vehicle may not stay put after you park it and could roll on its own. Ford will update the transmission computer software and inspect and replace any damaged parts. The fix is not ready yet; Ford expects it around April 2027 and will mail a second letter then.

Ford Expedition center console chrome can peel into sharp edges low

Reported 2026-06-09 · Campaign 26V368000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

The shiny chrome coating on the center console between the front seats can bubble up and flake off. Where it peels, it can leave sharp edges right where an arm, hand or leg rests. Anyone in the vehicle could get cut or scraped by them. Ford has already mailed an early letter warning owners, but the parts for the actual repair are not ready; they are expected around January 2027, when a second letter goes out.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator front seat belts can lock high

Reported 2026-05-27 · Campaign 26V344000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

The seat belt for the driver's seat, the front passenger seat, or both can lock up on its own. When that happens, the belt will not pull out and will not pull back in, so it cannot be worn properly across the person in the seat. A belt stuck like that may not hold someone in place in a crash. Dealers will inspect the belt retractors and replace any bad ones, free of charge.

Ford Super Duty and Lincoln Navigator wiper arms can break medium

Reported 2026-03-31 · Campaign 26V204000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

The metal arms that hold the windshield wipers can break. If an arm breaks, that wiper stops sweeping the glass. In heavy rain or snow you could suddenly be left with little or no clear view through the windshield while you are driving. A dealer will inspect the wiper arms and replace them if needed, free of charge. Ford mailed owner letters in August 2026, and you can look up your VIN online now.

Ford F-Series, Expedition: Trailer Brakes and Lights May Fail high

Reported 2026-02-20 · Campaign 26V104000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

These Ford and Lincoln trucks and SUVs have a built-in trailer brake controller. On the affected vehicles, that controller can stop communicating with the rest of the vehicle while you are towing. When that happens, the trailer's brake lights and turn signals can go dark, and the trailer's brakes may stop working. That makes the trailer harder to control and makes it harder for drivers behind you to see when you are braking or turning. A free software update fixes it.

Ford Expedition PARK warning too short after earlier repair medium

Reported 2025-12-18 · Campaign 25V886000 · Model years: 2019

Some 2019 Ford Expeditions that were already repaired under an earlier recall did not get the fix done right. The warning message and chime that tell you the transmission is not in PARK last a shorter time than they should. That means you may step out of the vehicle without realizing it was never shifted into PARK, and the Expedition could roll on its own. A dealer will reload the correct gauge cluster software for free.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator can lose brake assist critical

Reported 2025-12-01 · Campaign 25V823000 · Model years: 2025

Your 2025 Navigator or Expedition uses an electronic unit to create the power assist that makes the brake pedal easy to press. It can quit while you drive, or while a driver-assistance feature is controlling the vehicle. Then the pedal gets hard, stopping takes much more distance, and the assist features may not brake as you expect. The failure comes without warning. Some 2025 F-150, Bronco and Ranger vehicles are also recalled. Dealers replace the booster free.

Ford Expedition, F-Series moonroof wind deflector can fly off medium

Reported 2025-10-24 · Campaign 25V729000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

The clips that hold the wind deflector to the moonroof frame may not be attached well enough. The deflector is the small screen at the front edge of the moonroof opening that cuts down wind noise. If the clips let go, the deflector can come off the vehicle and land on the road, where it becomes a hazard for you and for drivers behind you. Dealers will inspect it and repair or reattach it as needed, free of charge.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator fuse box fire risk critical

Reported 2025-09-19 · Campaign 25V628000 · Model years: 2020

An electrical short inside the fuse box that connects to the battery can start an engine fire. It can happen while you are driving or while the SUV is parked. Ford says to park outside, away from buildings and other vehicles, until the work is done. The dealer will inspect that fuse box and replace it if needed, remove a ground wire, and on some vehicles fit an extra electrical box with a jumper wire. Everything is free.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Ford and Lincoln SUVs, trucks can lose brake assist high

Reported 2025-07-25 · Campaign 25V488000 · Model years: 2025

The part that helps your brakes work with light pedal pressure can stop working while you are driving, or while you are using driver-assist features that brake for you. If that happens, the brake pedal gets much harder to push and the vehicle takes longer to stop. The car may also fail to slow down on its own when a driver-assist feature expects it to. A free software update fixes it, either sent to the vehicle or done at a dealer.

Ford and Lincoln fuel pump failure can stall the engine high

Reported 2025-07-07 · Campaign 25V455000 · Model years: 2022

On these Ford and Lincoln trucks, SUVs and Mustangs, the low-pressure fuel pump, which is the part that moves gasoline out of the tank, can fail. If it does, the engine can stall and shut off while you are driving. The repair depends on what you own: on Super Duty pickups the dealer replaces the fuel pump itself, and on all the other vehicles the dealer reprograms the engine computer. Either repair is free. Owner letters were mailed July 27, 2026.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator Passenger Airbag Too Forceful high

Reported 2025-06-27 · Campaign 25V439000 · Model years: 2025

The airbag in front of the passenger seat may have been built incorrectly at the factory. Instead of cushioning the person in a crash, it can inflate with too much force. That extra force can injure whoever is sitting in that seat during a crash. Dealers will remove the passenger side airbag and install a new one at no charge. Owner letters were mailed on August 8, 2025.

Ford and Lincoln rearview camera image may go blank medium

Reported 2025-06-27 · Campaign 25V442000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

A software error affects the backup camera. The screen may show a blank image when you shift into reverse, so you cannot see what is behind the vehicle. Or the camera picture may stay on the screen after you have shifted out of reverse, which can distract you while driving forward. Either situation raises the risk of a crash. A dealer will update the rearview camera software free of charge.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator automatic braking not working medium

Reported 2025-05-23 · Campaign 25V346000 · Model years: 2020

These vehicles came in for an earlier recall, but that repair was done incorrectly. Because of a software mistake, the crash-avoidance features are not working: your vehicle will not warn you about a vehicle stopped ahead, and it will not brake on its own to help avoid or soften a collision. Your regular brakes and the rest of the vehicle still work normally, but that automatic safety net is missing. The dealer will reload the correct software.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator seat belts can lock up medium

Reported 2025-05-23 · Campaign 25V197000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

The seat belt for the driver, the front passenger, or both can lock up on its own. When that happens the belt will not pull out or pull back in, so you may not be able to buckle up properly or sit comfortably. A belt stuck like that cannot hold a person the way it is supposed to in a crash. Dealers will replace the seat belt retractor free of charge. Some vehicles already checked under an earlier recall still need this new repair.

Ford and Lincoln rearview cameras may freeze or blank medium

Reported 2025-05-09 · Campaign 25V315000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

A software error in the module that runs the screen can make the backup camera image lag, freeze, or not appear at all when you put the vehicle in reverse. You could back into something or someone you cannot see. The fix is a software update to that module, done free at a dealer or, on some vehicles, sent to the vehicle over the air. Ford is doing this in phases, so timing depends on your model and year.

Ford Expedition and Navigator front brake lines can leak high

Reported 2025-05-09 · Campaign 25V314000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

The brake lines at the front of the vehicle can rub against the air cleaner outlet pipe, part of the engine's air intake. Over time the rubbing can wear through the line and let brake fluid leak out. With fluid leaking, the brakes do not work as well as they should, and weak brakes make a crash more likely. Dealers will inspect the front brake line and replace the line or the air intake pipe if needed, free of charge.

Ford F-150, Expedition and Navigator brake fluid leak high

Reported 2025-04-11 · Campaign 25V236000 · Model years: 2018

The part that pushes brake fluid to your wheels when you press the pedal can leak. Fluid escapes from the front-wheel circuit into the power-assist unit behind it. With less fluid doing its job, the brakes do not work as well and your vehicle needs a longer distance to stop. Ford dealers will replace that part, and the power-assist unit as well if it is already leaking. The work is free, and letters went to owners in September 2025.

Ford F-150, Expedition, Lincoln Navigator engines may leak oil critical

Reported 2025-03-28 · Campaign 25V198000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

These Ford F-150 and Expedition and Lincoln Navigator vehicles with the 3.5-liter GTDI engine may have a small plug in the engine that was not lined up correctly at the factory. If that plug shifts, engine oil can pour out fast. Losing oil quickly can make the engine shut off while you are driving, so you lose power in traffic. Oil spraying onto hot engine or exhaust parts can also start a fire.

Ford trucks and SUVs may not brake towed trailers high

Reported 2025-02-28 · Campaign 25V132000 · Model years: 2022

Certain Ford F-150, Maverick, Expedition, F-250, F-350, F-450 and F-550 trucks and Lincoln Navigator SUVs were fixed incorrectly under an earlier recall, so the problem remains. Because of a software error, the built-in trailer brake controller may fail to apply the brakes on a trailer that uses electric or electric-over-hydraulic brakes. The trailer then pushes the vehicle instead of helping it slow down, so stopping takes much longer than normal. That extra stopping distance makes a crash more likely.

Ford Super Duty, Expedition and Corsair backup camera can fail medium

Reported 2025-01-31 · Campaign 25V048000 · Model years: 2021

The camera that shows what is behind you when you back up may go blank or show a scrambled picture. When that happens you cannot see people, pets or objects behind the vehicle on the screen, which makes backing up riskier. A dealer will check the camera and replace it if needed, at no charge. Ford mailed letters to owners in September 2025, so you may already have one.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator: sudden engine oil leak critical

Reported 2024-12-20 · Campaign 24V851000 · Model years: 2024

A small plug pressed into the engine may be out of line. If it slips, engine oil can pour out quickly. Losing oil that fast can make the engine shut down while you are driving, and oil that lands on hot engine or exhaust parts can start a fire. The dealer will check whether the plug is lined up correctly and put in a new one if it is not, at no charge to you.

Ford and Lincoln vehicles missing dashboard airbag warning label low

Reported 2024-11-08 · Campaign 24V852000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

The airbag warning label that belongs on the dashboard may be missing from your vehicle. That label explains how people should be seated and buckled in — for example, where children should ride — so the airbags and seat belts protect everyone properly in a crash. Without it, someone riding in the vehicle could sit in a way that increases their risk of injury. Ford will mail the warning labels to owners free of charge.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator seat belts can lock up high

Reported 2024-02-09 · Campaign 24V099000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

The seat belt for the driver or the front passenger has a device that tightens the belt in a crash. In these vehicles it can go off on its own for no reason. When that happens the belt locks up — it will not pull out and it will not pull back in. A locked belt may not hold you properly in a crash. Dealers will replace the seat belt retractor if needed and add a missing air conditioning drain tube elbow, free of charge.

Ford Expedition, Super Duty: Manual Missing Head Restraint Instructions low

Reported 2023-06-09 · Campaign 23V420000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

The owner's manual for these Ford and Lincoln vehicles is missing instructions. It does not explain how to adjust or remove certain head restraints — the padded rests at the top of the seats. Federal rules require those instructions to be included. Without them, an owner may leave a head restraint in the wrong position, which could mean less protection for someone's head and neck in a crash. Ford is mailing a free printed addendum that covers the center seating positions. Nothing on the vehicle itself needs repair.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator front axle gear may break high

Reported 2023-02-24 · Campaign 23V128000 · Model years: 2023

A gear inside the front axle may have skipped a hardening step at the factory, which leaves it weaker than it should be. That gear can snap. If it does, you lose four-wheel drive, and the front wheels may lock up, which could make you lose control. A dealer will replace the front axle gear set, or the whole axle if the part has already broken. The same recall also covers certain 2023 F-150 trucks.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator engine compartment fire risk critical

Reported 2022-05-17 · Campaign 22V346000 · Model years: 2021

A fire can start in the engine compartment of these SUVs. It can happen while you are driving, or while the vehicle is parked with the ignition off. Ford says to park outside, away from buildings and other vehicles, until it is repaired. At the dealer, a fuse box connected to the battery will be checked and replaced if needed, and a ground wire removed. Some vehicles also get an added electrical box. The parts are not ready for every vehicle yet.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Ford trucks and SUVs: windshield wiper arms can break medium

Reported 2022-04-14 · Campaign 22V250000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

The metal arms that hold your windshield wipers can break. If an arm snaps, that wiper stops moving and part of the glass no longer gets cleared. In rain, snow or road spray you could suddenly lose much of your view through the windshield. Ford dealers will replace both front wiper arms at no cost. Owners were warned by letter in May 2022, and letters went out again in October 2022 once repair parts were available.

Ford and Lincoln trucks: trailer brakes may not work high

Reported 2022-03-25 · Campaign 22V193000 · Model years: 2022

A software problem in the built-in trailer brake controller means that a trailer hooked up behind your truck or SUV may not brake at all. Trailers with electric or electric-over-hydraulic brakes rely on this system to slow down with you. If it doesn't work, you're stopping the combined weight of the vehicle and the loaded trailer on your truck's brakes alone, so it takes much longer to stop. A dealer reloads the controller's software to fix it.

Ford and Lincoln trucks and SUVs may have faulty side airbag sensors high

Reported 2021-10-15 · Campaign 21V805000 · Model years: 2021

The side impact sensors inside the front doors may have been damaged when the supplier assembled them. These sensors are what tell the side airbags a crash is happening. If a sensor is damaged, the side airbags may not go off properly in a side crash, so you would not get the protection they are designed to provide. A dealer will replace the sensors in both front doors, driver side and passenger side, at no cost.

Ford and Lincoln backup camera image may go blank medium

Reported 2020-09-23 · Campaign 20V575000 · Model years: 2020

A poor electrical connection to the backup camera can make the screen go blank or show a fuzzy, distorted picture when you shift into reverse. It may happen on and off rather than all the time. If you cannot see what is behind you, it is easier to back into a person or an object. Federal rules require a working rear camera on these vehicles. Dealers will replace the camera at no charge.

Ford Expedition may mistake a child for an adult high

Reported 2020-09-23 · Campaign 20V574000 · Model years: 2020

A sensor built into the front passenger seat belt measures how the belt is pulled to judge whether a child or an adult is sitting there. On these Expeditions, it can read a child as an adult. Normally the system switches the passenger airbag off for a small occupant; with the wrong reading, the airbag can go off in a crash instead of staying off, which can injure a child in that seat. Dealers will replace the seat belt buckle free.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator rear headrest bracket weld medium

Reported 2020-06-23 · Campaign 20V366000 · Model years: 2020

The bracket that holds the headrest on the driver's side second-row seat may not have been welded to the seat frame well enough. A weak weld means the headrest could be weaker than it should be. In a crash, it may not hold a passenger's head back the way it is supposed to, so someone sitting in that seat could get hurt more than they otherwise would. The seat does not meet the federal safety rule for head restraints. A dealer will look at the seat and replace it if needed.

Ford Mustang and Expedition park warning too short medium

Reported 2020-05-08 · Campaign 20V263000 · Model years: 2019

Ford is recalling certain 2019 Expedition and 2019-2020 Mustang vehicles. When the transmission is not in park, the car is supposed to show a "Transmission Not In Park" message and sound a chime to warn you. On these vehicles that message and chime do not last as long as they should, so a driver may not notice them. If you get out without shifting into park, the vehicle can roll on its own. Dealers will reprogram the instrument cluster for free.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator crash-warning system not enabled medium

Reported 2020-05-08 · Campaign 20V262000 · Model years: 2020

These SUVs came with the camera-only Pre-Collision Assist system, which is meant to warn you and help brake if you are about to hit something ahead. Some computer modules were coded incorrectly at the factory, so the feature was never switched on, and the menu you would use to turn it on is not there. A dealer reloads the software in several modules and resets the tire pressure monitor. Vehicles with the radar-plus-camera version are not affected.

Ford Expedition passenger seat belt sensor may misread occupant high

Reported 2020-04-16 · Campaign 20V217000 · Model years: 2020

On certain 2020 Ford Expeditions, a sensor built into the front passenger seat belt buckle can fail. That sensor helps the vehicle figure out who is sitting in the seat, and a bad reading can put the person in the wrong category. If a crash happens, the car's safety systems may not protect that passenger the way they are supposed to, raising the chance of injury. A dealer will replace the whole passenger buckle.

Ford F-150, Ranger, Expedition may not be in park high

Reported 2020-04-03 · Campaign 20V197000 · Model years: 2020

A small clip that holds the shift cable in place may not be pushed all the way in. If it works loose, the transmission can be in a different gear than the one shown on the shifter. The biggest danger is thinking you left it in park when you did not — the vehicle can roll away after you get out. Always set the parking brake until a dealer checks and reseats the clip.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator rear suspension bolts loose high

Reported 2019-10-28 · Campaign 19V773000 · Model years: 2019

Each rear wheel is held in line by a small arm called a toe link. On these SUVs the bolts attaching that arm to the frame may not have been tightened enough, so the arm can come loose or separate. If it does, the back of the vehicle can suddenly wander or feel unstable while you are driving. Dealers will tighten the bolts, reset the rear alignment, and replace unevenly worn tires.

Ford and Lincoln seat backs may be dangerously weak high

Reported 2019-08-28 · Campaign 19V633000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

The mechanism that lets a seat back tilt is supposed to have three metal catches holding it in place, and on these vehicles one catch may be missing. With a catch missing, the seat back is weaker than it should be and may feel loose. In a crash, a seat back like this may not hold the person in the seat properly, which can make injuries worse. Dealers will inspect the seats and replace them as needed, free of charge.

Ford Expedition and Lincoln Navigator Second-Row Seat May Shift high

Reported 2018-11-14 · Campaign 18V805000 · Model years: 2018

The middle seat in the second row may be missing one or both of the reinforcement brackets that belong in its seat track. Without them, the seat could shift or move during a crash instead of staying put. A seat that moves cannot hold the person in it properly, so that passenger could be hurt more badly. Ford will check the seat and, if brackets are missing, replace the center seat frame. A second letter follows once the remedy is ready.

Ford F-150, Expedition and Navigator Fuel Pump Can Leak critical

Reported 2018-06-12 · Campaign 18V392000 · Model years: 2018

These trucks and SUVs use a 3.5-liter turbocharged V6 engine with a high-pressure fuel pump. Welds holding that pump assembly together can crack. If a weld breaks, fuel or oil can leak out of the pump. Leaking fuel near a hot surface or a spark can catch fire. This is a parts problem, not something regular maintenance fixes, so the dealer replaces the high-pressure fuel pump and the related parts that go with it, free of charge.

Ford F-150 and Expedition may roll after shifting to Park high

Reported 2018-04-04 · Campaign 18V214000 · Model years: 2018

A clip on the gear shift cable may not be seated correctly. If that clip comes loose, the gear shown on the shifter may not be the gear the transmission is actually in. You could put it in Park, step out, and have the vehicle roll away, or find it moving in a gear you did not select. A dealer will inspect the shift cable locking clip and seat it properly if needed.

Ford and Lincoln 10-speed transmissions may not hold Park high

Reported 2018-04-04 · Campaign 18V213000 · Model years: 2018

These vehicles have a 10-speed automatic transmission, and a small metal pin inside it may have been left out at the factory. Without that pin, the transmission may not actually hold the vehicle in Park, even though you moved the shifter to Park and the dashboard shows Park. If you get out without setting the parking brake, the vehicle can roll away on its own. A dealer will look inside the transmission and add the missing pin.

Ford Expedition, Lincoln Navigator rear windows may not auto-reverse medium

Reported 2018-01-16 · Campaign 18V058000 · Model years: 2018

The controls for the second row power windows can stop working for a moment. Normally, if a window is closing and hits something like a finger or a hand, it is supposed to stop and go back down on its own. When the control quits, the window may not reverse, so it can keep pressing on whatever is caught. Dealers will check the second row window control modules and replace them if the build date calls for it, free of charge.

Ford Expedition second-row seats may not be secured properly high

Reported 2017-12-01 · Campaign 17V775000 · Model years: 2018

On some 2018 Expeditions, the second-row window seats can slide forward farther than they should, and the bolt holding the seat back to the seat base may not have been installed correctly. Either problem means the seat or the person in it may not be in the right position in a crash, so the seat belt and headrest cannot do their job. Dealers check the seat travel and the bolts, and replace or fix parts as needed.
