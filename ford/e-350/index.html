Ford E-350 recalls

Ford E-350 and E-450 engine cover may get hot medium

Reported Nov 17, 2025 · Campaign 25V793000 · Model years: 2021

Ford is recalling certain 2021 E-350 and E-450 vehicles that received an incomplete software update under an earlier recall, 20V795. On these vehicles, the engine cover may become very hot. Dealers will update the engine software that manages heat during high idle, free of charge. Ford mailed owner notification letters on January 7, 2026, and says the vehicle identification numbers in this recall can be looked up on NHTSA.gov beginning December 22, 2025.

Ford E-350 and E-450 Power Steering Fluid Loss high

Reported Feb 9, 2024 · Campaign 24V097000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Ford is recalling certain E-350 and E-450 vans with dual rear wheels because a connection between the power steering line and the brake hydroboost unit may not be secure enough. This can lead to a sudden loss of power steering fluid. Losing this fluid can cause you to lose both power steering assist and power brake assist at the same time, which increases the risk of a crash.

Ford E-350 And F-650 Park Pawl Issue high

Reported Sep 8, 2023 · Campaign 23V625000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

On certain 2024-2025 Ford E-350, F-650, and F-750 vehicles, a casting defect in the transmission may keep the park pawl from fully engaging the park gear.

Ford E-350, E-450, F-59 Fuel Tank Leak Risk critical

Reported Dec 14, 2022 · Campaign 22V925000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Certain Ford E-350, E-450 and F-59 vehicles have a fuel tank with paint along the weld seam that can peel away. Once the paint peels, the exposed metal underneath can corrode, and that corrosion can lead to a fuel leak.

Ford Trucks and Vans Missing Tire Information on Label low

Reported May 27, 2022 · Campaign 22V384000 · Model years: 2023

Ford is recalling certain F-Series Super Duty trucks and E-Series vans, plus some other Ford vehicles that were given a reprinted label. The Safety Certification Label on these vehicles is missing the tire size, rim type and tire pressure figures for the front and rear axles. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rules covering tire selection and rims and covering wheels and rims. Dealers will replace the label at no cost to the owner.

Ford E-350 and E-450 may suddenly lose power steering fluid high

Reported Feb 16, 2022 · Campaign 22V089000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Ford is recalling certain E-350 and E-450 vehicles that have dual rear wheels. The connection between the power steering pressure line and the brake Hydroboost unit may not be made well enough, and power steering fluid can be lost suddenly as a result. Dealers will check that connection and replace the power steering lines if needed, at no cost to the owner. Ford has mailed letters to owners about this recall.

Ford E-350 and E-450 Engine Cover May Overheat medium

Reported Dec 17, 2020 · Campaign 20V795000 · Model years: 2021

Some E-350 and E-450 vehicles may have insulation that is not positioned correctly on the underside of the engine cover, allowing the cover to become very hot. Direct, continuous contact with certain areas of the cover in the passenger compartment may cause injury or burns to the skin of the driver or front passenger.

Ford E-350, E-450 Wiring Harness Rubbing Damage high

Reported Mar 23, 2020 · Campaign 20V175000 · Model years: 2021

On some E-350 and E-450 vans, a wire harness near the rear of the vehicle can rub against the vehicle's frame. Over time, this can wear through wiring that supports the fuel pump and the anti-lock braking system. Damage to the fuel pump wiring could cause the engine to stall unexpectedly. Damage to the ABS wiring can reduce anti-lock brake performance and cause a loss of roll stability control.

Ford Trucks May Have Rear Axle, Driveshaft Failure high

Reported Feb 21, 2020 · Campaign 20V099000 · Model years: 2021

Some Ford F-550, E-350, and E-450 trucks may have rear axle differential fluid that is below the proper level. This can lead to the rear axle assembly failing and the driveshaft separating from the vehicle, which can cause a loss of drive. Dealers will inspect the differential fluid level and adjust it, or replace the rear axle if it is needed. This repair will not cost owners anything, and Ford has already notified owners about this issue.

Ford E-350/E-450 Shuttle Bus Backup Camera May Reverse high

Reported Jun 18, 2019 · Campaign 19V466000 · Model years: 2018

SVO Group is recalling certain Ford E-350 and E-450 shuttle buses equipped with ASA Electronics Voyager monitors that display the back-up camera image. These monitors may revert back to their factory default settings, and when that happens, the image shown on the screen may appear reversed instead of showing accurately what is behind the vehicle.

Ford and GMC based ambulances may show false brake lights medium

Reported May 3, 2019 · Campaign 19V342000 · Model years: 2018

Taylor Made Ambulances is recalling certain ambulances built on Ford E-350, E-450, F-450, F-550 and GMC G3500 chassis that are fitted with Whelen brake lights. Depending on how a particular vehicle is wired, those brake lights can light up even when the driver has not pressed the brake pedal. Taylor Made will notify owners, and Whelen will replace the brake lights.

Ambulance Brake Lights May Turn On By Mistake medium

Reported Mar 26, 2019 · Campaign 19V236000 · Model years: 2018

Depending on how the vehicle is wired, the Whelen brake lights on these ambulances may illuminate even when the brake pedal has not been pressed. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard covering lamps and lighting equipment.

Ford Transit and E-350 Seat Back May Unlock high

Reported Mar 18, 2019 · Campaign 19V205000 · Model years: 2018

These Ford Transit and E-350 vans were modified with foldaway seats made by Freedman. When the seat back cushion is folded up into its upright position, it may not stay locked in place if the seat comes under load, such as during a crash or a sudden stop. Because the seat back could come unlocked under this kind of load, the vans do not meet a federal safety standard for seating systems.

Ford E-350 recall questions

How many recalls does the Ford E-350 have?

NHTSA lists 13 safety recalls covering the 2018–2025 Ford E-350. 8 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Ford E-350 and E-450 engine cover may get hot" (NHTSA campaign 25V793000), reported Nov 17, 2025.

Which Ford E-350 model year has the most recalls?

The 2021 Ford E-350 has the most, with 6 recalls. The 2024 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Ford E-350?

No. None of the 13 Ford E-350 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Ford E-350 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Ford dealer, including for second owners.
