Ford Maverick recalls

34 recalls covering model years 2022, 2023, 2024. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Ford Bronco Sport and Maverick Suspension Can Come Apart critical

Reported 2026-05-27 · Campaign 26V340000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

A part called the lower control arm connects the front wheel to the vehicle and helps hold it in place. On these vehicles, the ball joint at the end of that arm may have been installed or repaired incorrectly at the factory, so the control arm can come apart from the front wheel. If that happens while you are driving, you can lose control of the vehicle. Ford says do not drive these vehicles until they have been fixed.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired.

Ford F-Series, Expedition: Trailer Brakes and Lights May Fail high

Reported 2026-02-20 · Campaign 26V104000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

These Ford and Lincoln trucks and SUVs have a built-in trailer brake controller. On the affected vehicles, that controller can stop communicating with the rest of the vehicle while you are towing. When that happens, the trailer's brake lights and turn signals can go dark, and the trailer's brakes may stop working. That makes the trailer harder to control and makes it harder for drivers behind you to see when you are braking or turning. A free software update fixes it.

Ford and Lincoln engine block heaters can catch fire critical

Reported 2025-10-10 · Campaign 25V685000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

An engine block heater is the plug-in warmer some vehicles have to help the engine start in very cold weather. On these Fords and Lincolns the heater can crack and leak coolant, and the leak can cause an electrical short when the heater is plugged in. That short can start a fire. Ford is telling owners not to plug in the block heater at all until the vehicle is fixed. Dealers will replace it, or seal it off and remove the cord, free.

Ford and Lincoln rearview camera image may go blank medium

Reported 2025-06-27 · Campaign 25V442000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

A software error affects the backup camera. The screen may show a blank image when you shift into reverse, so you cannot see what is behind the vehicle. Or the camera picture may stay on the screen after you have shifted out of reverse, which can distract you while driving forward. Either situation raises the risk of a crash. A dealer will update the rearview camera software free of charge.

Ford Escape and Maverick hybrids: bad repair leaves fire risk critical

Reported 2025-05-23 · Campaign 25V345000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Some Escape, Maverick and Corsair hybrids and plug-in hybrids with the 2.5-liter engine were fixed incorrectly during an earlier recall, so the original problem is still there. If the engine breaks down, engine oil and fuel vapor can spray into the engine bay and land on hot engine or exhaust parts. That can start a fire under the hood. Ford will reprogram the engine's computer at no cost to reduce the chance of this happening.

Ford Bronco Sport and Maverick may lose drive power high

Reported 2025-03-14 · Campaign 25V158000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

These vehicles were brought in for an earlier recall, but the fix was not done correctly. The computers that manage the electrical system and the engine may not notice when the 12-volt battery is running low. Without that warning, things like your hazard lights can stop working, or the vehicle can lose power while you are driving. Dealers will reload the correct software into both computers free of charge.

Ford Maverick Hybrid dashboard gauges may not light up medium

Reported 2025-02-28 · Campaign 25V123000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

These Mavericks were repaired under an earlier recall, but the fix was done wrong. The result is that the dashboard display may not light up properly. That means your speedometer, gear indicator, and warning lights — the ones that tell you about brakes, airbags, or headlights — might not show. Without them, you would not know how fast you are going or that something is wrong with the car. The dealer will reload the dashboard software.

Ford trucks and SUVs may not brake towed trailers high

Reported 2025-02-28 · Campaign 25V132000 · Model years: 2022

Certain Ford F-150, Maverick, Expedition, F-250, F-350, F-450 and F-550 trucks and Lincoln Navigator SUVs were fixed incorrectly under an earlier recall, so the problem remains. Because of a software error, the built-in trailer brake controller may fail to apply the brakes on a trailer that uses electric or electric-over-hydraulic brakes. The trailer then pushes the vehicle instead of helping it slow down, so stopping takes much longer than normal. That extra stopping distance makes a crash more likely.

Ford Maverick, Escape, Lincoln Corsair can lose drive power high

Reported 2025-02-28 · Campaign 25V133000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

Certain Ford Maverick, Escape and Lincoln Corsair hybrids were repaired incorrectly under an earlier recall, so the original problem is still there. Software in the hybrid powertrain computer can shift the vehicle into neutral on its own. When that happens, the engine still runs but the wheels get no power, so the vehicle coasts and slows down even though you are pressing the gas pedal. Losing power like that, especially in traffic or while merging, raises the chance of a crash.

Ford Maverick child seat tether anchors may not hold medium

Reported 2024-10-18 · Campaign 24V778000 · Model years: 2024

The tether anchors for the second-row child seats on some 2024 Ford Mavericks were not threaded in far enough. A tether is the top strap that keeps a forward-facing child seat from pitching forward in a crash, and it hooks onto one of these anchor points. If an anchor pulls loose because the threads never fully caught, the child seat will not be held the way it should be. Ford dealers repair the anchor points free of charge.

When you shift into reverse, the picture from the backup camera can lock up on the radio screen instead of showing what's happening live behind the truck. A Ford dealer updates the radio software for free; owner letters went out in March 2025.
