Bronco Sport, Maverick Front Suspension Detachment Risk critical

Reported May 27, 2026 · Campaign 26V340000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

Some Ford Bronco Sport and Maverick vehicles may have front lower control arm ball joints that were installed or repaired incorrectly at the assembly plant. This can allow the control arm to disconnect from the front wheel. A detached control arm can cause a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired.

Ford and Lincoln Trailer Brake and Light Failure high

Reported Feb 20, 2026 · Campaign 26V104000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

When towing a trailer, the integrated trailer module in certain Ford and Lincoln vehicles may lose communication with the vehicle. This can cause a loss of trailer brake and turn signal lights, or a loss of trailer brake function.

Ford and Lincoln Block Heater Fire Risk critical

Reported Oct 10, 2025 · Campaign 25V685000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

In certain Ford and Lincoln vehicles, the engine block heater can crack and develop a coolant leak. If the block heater is plugged in when this happens, it can short circuit.

Ford and Lincoln rearview cameras may show blank image medium

Reported Jun 27, 2025 · Campaign 25V442000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

Ford is recalling certain Ford and Lincoln vehicles, including the Explorer, Escape, F-150, Expedition, Ranger, Mustang, Bronco Sport, Maverick, Transit and the Lincoln Aviator, Corsair, Navigator and Nautilus. A software problem can make the rearview camera show a blank picture when the vehicle is backing up. The picture can also stay on the screen after the driver has finished backing up. Dealers will update the rearview camera software at no cost. Letters were mailed to owners in September 2025.

Ford Escape, Maverick, Lincoln Corsair Engine Fire Risk critical

Reported May 23, 2025 · Campaign 25V345000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Certain Ford Escape, Lincoln Corsair, and Ford Maverick hybrid and plug-in hybrid vehicles were repaired incorrectly under an earlier recall. If the engine fails, engine oil and fuel vapor may be released into the engine compartment and build up near hot engine or exhaust parts, which are possible sources of ignition. This may result in a fire in the engine compartment.

Ford Bronco Sport, Maverick Battery Detection Fault high

Reported Mar 14, 2025 · Campaign 25V158000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

This recall covers Ford Bronco Sport and Maverick vehicles that were already repaired once for a related problem, but the fix did not fully work. The control modules that manage the body and transmission may fail to notice when the 12-volt battery is running low. If that happens, the vehicle could lose electrical features like hazard lights, or lose the power needed to keep driving.

Ford Maverick Battery Detection Software Needs Fixing medium

Reported Feb 28, 2025 · Campaign 25V127000 · Model years: 2022

Ford is recalling certain 2022 Maverick trucks that were previously repaired under an earlier recall, but the fix didn't correct the problem. The vehicle's body and powertrain control modules may fail to notice when the 12-volt battery's charge level changes. This means the vehicle may not detect a low battery the way it's supposed to.

Ford Escape, Maverick, Lincoln Corsair May Shift to Neutral high

Reported Feb 28, 2025 · Campaign 25V133000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

Ford is recalling certain Maverick, Escape, and Lincoln Corsair vehicles that were fixed incorrectly under an earlier recall for this same issue. A software problem in the hybrid transmission control module may cause the vehicle to shift into neutral unexpectedly, which means the vehicle can suddenly lose the power that drives the wheels.

Ford And Lincoln Trailer Brakes May Fail high

Reported Feb 28, 2025 · Campaign 25V132000 · Model years: 2022

These Ford and Lincoln vehicles were already repaired once under an earlier recall for the same problem, but a software error in that repair means a towed trailer equipped with electric or electric-over-hydraulic brakes may not brake as it should. If the trailer's brakes don't work properly, it can take longer to bring the vehicle and trailer to a stop, which raises the chance of a crash.

Ford Maverick Brake Lights Turn On Unexpectedly medium

Reported Feb 28, 2025 · Campaign 25V124000 · Model years: 2022

This recall covers Ford Maverick trucks that were fixed once already under an earlier recall, but the repair did not fully solve the problem. In certain situations, the computer module that controls the antilock brakes can turn on the brake lights even though the driver has not touched the brake pedal. Because of this, the vehicle does not meet a federal safety standard for lights on the outside of the car.

When this happens, the tail lights are not illuminated the way they are meant to be, which reduces how visible the vehicle is to other drivers on the road.

Reported Feb 14, 2025 · Campaign 25V094000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

On certain 2022-2024 Ford Maverick vehicles that were already repaired once under an earlier recall, the fix did not work correctly. On these vehicles, that faster flash rate does not kick in, so the driver has no way to know a turn signal has stopped working.

The 12-volt battery in these vehicles can degrade over time and suddenly stop working. If that happens, the vehicle could lose electrical accessories like the hazard lights, or lose the power needed to keep driving. Dealers will inspect the battery and replace it if needed, at no charge. Vehicles already repaired under an earlier recall for this issue will need this new remedy completed.

As a result, a child seat attached to one of these anchors might not be held as securely in place as it should be.

Certain hybrid and plug-in hybrid Escape, Maverick, and Corsair vehicles can suffer an engine failure that releases engine oil and fuel vapor into the engine compartment. Ford Maverick recall questions

How many recalls does the Ford Maverick have?

NHTSA lists 34 safety recalls covering the 2022–2024 Ford Maverick. 18 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Bronco Sport, Maverick Front Suspension Detachment Risk" (NHTSA campaign 26V340000), reported May 27, 2026.

Which Ford Maverick model year has the most recalls?

The 2022 Ford Maverick has the most, with 28 recalls. The 2024 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 15. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Ford Maverick?

Yes. 1 Ford Maverick recall carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Bronco Sport, Maverick Front Suspension Detachment Risk (26V340000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Ford Maverick has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Ford dealer, including for second owners.
