Ford Ranger recalls

14 recalls covering model years 2019, 2020. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Ford and Lincoln Block Heater Fire Risk critical

Reported 2025-10-10 · Campaign 25V685000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

In certain Ford and Lincoln vehicles, the engine block heater can crack and develop a coolant leak. If the block heater is plugged in when this happens, it can short circuit.

Ford Rearview Camera May Show Distorted Image medium

Reported 2025-09-04 · Campaign 25V572000 · Model years: 2019

Some Ford pickup trucks, SUVs, vans, and the Lincoln MKC may have a problem with the rearview camera. When the vehicle is shifted into reverse, the camera image on the screen may appear distorted, show an upside-down picture, or go completely blank instead of a normal picture. Because of this, the driver may not have a clear view of what is behind the vehicle while backing up.

Ford and Lincoln rearview cameras may show blank image medium

Reported 2025-06-27 · Campaign 25V442000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Ford is recalling certain Ford and Lincoln vehicles, including the Explorer, Escape, F-150, Expedition, Ranger, Mustang, Bronco Sport, Maverick, Transit and the Lincoln Aviator, Corsair, Navigator and Nautilus. A software problem can make the rearview camera show a blank picture when the vehicle is backing up. The picture can also stay on the screen after the driver has finished backing up. Dealers will update the rearview camera software at no cost. Letters were mailed to owners in September 2025.

Ford Bronco, Ranger Tire Label Shows Wrong Capacity medium

Reported 2023-05-26 · Campaign 23V379000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

On some Bronco and Ranger vehicles, the tire and loading information label lists the wrong load-carrying capacity. Because the number is incorrect, an owner following the label could end up putting too much weight in the vehicle. Ford will mail a corrected label for owners to put over the old one, or a dealer can install it, free of charge.

Ford Ranger Front Passenger Seat Belt Misrouted high

Reported 2021-10-15 · Campaign 21V811000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

The front passenger seat belt in certain trucks may not have been put together correctly, which can cause the belt webbing to be routed the wrong way. Seat belts have to be assembled in a very specific way to work as intended. Because this one may be misrouted, it may not function the way it is supposed to for the passenger sitting there.

Ford Ranger Rear Seats May Block Child Seats high

Reported 2021-09-30 · Campaign 21V765000 · Model years: 2020

On certain 2020-2021 Ford Ranger Super Cab trucks with rear seats that have fixed head restraints, those head restraints may get in the way when installing certain child seats. In addition, a child seat may not attach properly to the lower anchors in the back seat. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard for child seat anchor systems.

Ford and Lincoln rearview camera image may cut out medium

Reported 2020-09-23 · Campaign 20V575000 · Model years: 2020

Ford is recalling a wide range of vehicles, including the F-150 and Super Duty pickups, Explorer, Mustang, Transit, Expedition, Escape, Ranger and Edge, plus the Lincoln Nautilus and Corsair. A poor electrical connection can cause the rearview camera to show a blank or distorted image from time to time. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard. Dealers will replace the rearview camera free of charge, and owners have been notified by mail.

Ford Expedition, F-150, Ranger Shift Cable Defect high

Reported 2020-04-03 · Campaign 20V197000 · Model years: 2020

A lock clip on the transmission shift cable in your vehicle may not be fully seated. If that happens, the transmission could actually be in a different gear than what's shown on the gear shift indicator. If the parking brake isn't applied while this is happening, the vehicle could move on its own without you intending it to. Ford will notify owners, and dealers will inspect and correct the shift cable locking clip installation as necessary, free of charge.

Ford Ranger HVAC Blower Motor Fire Risk critical

Reported 2020-03-09 · Campaign 20V137000 · Model years: 2019

Ford is recalling certain 2019 Ranger trucks that previously had their HVAC blower motor replaced under an earlier recall. The replacement blower motor may have been installed with too little clearance between an electrical terminal and the metal base plate underneath it. This narrow clearance can allow a resistive electrical short to develop between those two parts inside the replacement motor.

Ford Ranger Tail Light Connector May Fail medium

Reported 2019-11-21 · Campaign 19V839000 · Model years: 2019

On certain Ford Ranger trucks, the electrical connector for the rear lights may be misaligned or not seated properly. This can cause the tail lights to work only sometimes, or not at all, including the brake, turn signal, rear position, and reverse lights.

Ford Ranger Heater Blower Motor May Short high

Reported 2019-10-10 · Campaign 19V726000 · Model years: 2019

Certain Ranger trucks have a heating and air conditioning blower motor — the part that powers your heater and AC fan — that can develop an electrical short.

Ford Ranger Front Passenger Seat Belt Misassembled high

Reported 2019-07-10 · Campaign 19V518000 · Model years: 2019

On some Ford Ranger trucks with a SuperCab body, the front passenger seat belt may not have been assembled correctly at the factory. A seat belt that isn't put together properly may not hold the passenger securely in place. That matters most in a crash, when the seat belt needs to work correctly to keep the occupant restrained and reduce the chance of injury.

Ford Ranger Shifter Cable Bolts May Loosen high

Reported 2019-05-13 · Campaign 19V366000 · Model years: 2019

On some 2019 Ford Ranger trucks, the bolts that hold the shifter cable bracket onto the transmission can loosen over time. If that happens, the transmission may end up in a different gear than what the gear shift lever is showing you inside the truck. The actual gear the truck is in and the gear position shown on the lever may no longer match each other.

Ford Ranger can shift out of Park unexpectedly high

Reported 2019-02-01 · Campaign 19V071000 · Model years: 2019

Ford is recalling certain Ranger pickups with 10-speed automatic transmissions. On some of these trucks, the shift lever can be moved out of Park without the key in the ignition and without the brake pedal pressed. Federal theft-protection rules require that the vehicle not shift out of Park that way, so these vehicles do not meet the standard. Dealers will inspect the floor shifter interlock parts and repair them if needed, free of charge. The recall began February 22, 2019.
