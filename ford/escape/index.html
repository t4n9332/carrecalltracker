Ford and Lincoln Rearview Camera Image May Invert medium

Reported Mar 3, 2026 · Campaign 26V123000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

The image on the center display may flip or turn upside down, showing an incorrect rearview image when the vehicle is put into reverse. Because the rearview image is not displayed correctly, these vehicles do not meet federal safety standards for rear visibility. To fix the problem, Ford dealers will update the software in the accessory protocol interface module at no charge to the owner.

Ford, Lincoln Wiper Motor May Fail To Work medium

Reported Mar 3, 2026 · Campaign 26V117000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Ford is recalling certain Explorer, Escape, Lincoln Aviator, and Lincoln Corsair vehicles because the windshield wiper motor may fail. If the motor fails, the windshield wipers may stop working properly, which can make it harder for the driver to see the road. Dealers will inspect the front wiper motors and replace them if necessary, at no cost to the owner. A final repair is expected to be available in October 2026.

Ford Escape and Focus Block Heater Fire Risk critical

Reported Jan 15, 2026 · Campaign 26V011000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The engine block heater in some Ford Escape and Focus vehicles can crack over time and leak coolant. If coolant leaks while the heater is plugged in, it can cause an electrical short circuit inside the heater. This short circuit raises the chance that a fire could start in the vehicle.

Ford Escape liftgate hinge covers may come off low

Reported Dec 1, 2025 · Campaign 25V829000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Ford is recalling certain Escape vehicles because the covers over the liftgate hinges may not be attached properly. The liftgate is the rear door that lifts up. A cover that is not secured can come off the vehicle. Dealers will inspect the covers and reinstall them, or replace any that are missing, at no cost to the owner. Ford mailed notification letters to owners, and the company has a customer service line for owners who have questions about this recall.

Ford Escape Rear Brake Pads Made Incorrectly high

Reported Oct 10, 2025 · Campaign 25V693000 · Model years: 2020

Certain Escape SUVs have rear brake pads that were not manufactured correctly. If the brake booster ever malfunctions, these incorrectly made pads can reduce how well your brakes work, meaning the brakes may not perform as they should when you need them most.

Ford and Lincoln Block Heater Fire Risk critical

Reported Oct 10, 2025 · Campaign 25V685000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

In certain Ford and Lincoln vehicles, the engine block heater can crack and develop a coolant leak. If the block heater is plugged in when this happens, it can short circuit.

Ford Bronco Sport, Escape: Fuel Injector Fire Risk critical

Reported Jul 11, 2025 · Campaign 25V467000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

On certain Ford Bronco Sport and Escape vehicles with 1.5-liter engines, a fuel injector may crack and leak fuel inside the engine compartment. If this fuel leak occurs near a hot engine or exhaust part, it could ignite. Ford will update the engine control software as an interim fix, and will later replace the high-pressure fuel rail assembly and fuel injectors to fully correct the problem.

Ford and Lincoln rearview cameras may show blank image medium

Reported Jun 27, 2025 · Campaign 25V442000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Ford is recalling certain Ford and Lincoln vehicles, including the Explorer, Escape, F-150, Expedition, Ranger, Mustang, Bronco Sport, Maverick, Transit and the Lincoln Aviator, Corsair, Navigator and Nautilus. A software problem can make the rearview camera show a blank picture when the vehicle is backing up. The picture can also stay on the screen after the driver has finished backing up. Dealers will update the rearview camera software at no cost. Letters were mailed to owners in September 2025.

Ford Escape Engine Defect May Cause Oil Leak high

Reported Jun 3, 2025 · Campaign 25V372000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

The engine's cylinder head may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can cause small plugs inside it to fail. When a plug fails, engine oil can leak out. Losing oil can cause the engine to lose power while driving. If the leaking oil reaches a hot part of the engine or the exhaust system, it can also start a fire.

Ford Escape, Maverick, Lincoln Corsair Engine Fire Risk critical

Reported May 23, 2025 · Campaign 25V345000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023

Certain Ford Escape, Lincoln Corsair, and Ford Maverick hybrid and plug-in hybrid vehicles were repaired incorrectly under an earlier recall. If the engine fails, engine oil and fuel vapor may be released into the engine compartment and build up near hot engine or exhaust parts, which are possible sources of ignition. This may result in a fire in the engine compartment.

Ford Escape and Lincoln block heater may overheat critical

Reported May 23, 2025 · Campaign 25V343000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Ford is recalling certain Lincoln Nautilus, Escape, Lincoln Corsair, Maverick, and Bronco Sport vehicles equipped with a 1.5L or 2.0L engine and an engine block heater. The block heater may overheat while the vehicle is parked with it plugged in.

Ford and Lincoln rearview camera image may freeze medium

Reported May 9, 2025 · Campaign 25V315000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Ford is recalling certain 2021 through 2025 Ford and Lincoln vehicles, including Bronco, Edge, Escape, Expedition, Mustang, Ranger, Transit, F-150, Super Duty pickups, Mach-E and the Lincoln Nautilus, Navigator and Corsair. A software error can make the backup camera picture come up late, freeze, or not appear at all when the vehicle is in reverse. Ford is handling this recall in phases, so the fix becomes available at different times depending on the model and model year.

Ford Escape, Bronco Sport Fuel Injector Leak Redo critical

Reported Mar 14, 2025 · Campaign 25V165000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

This recall covers certain Ford Escape and Bronco Sport vehicles that were already repaired once for a related fuel system problem, but the repair did not fully fix it. A fuel injector in these vehicles may crack and leak fuel inside the engine compartment.

Ford Escape, Maverick, Lincoln Corsair May Shift to Neutral high

Reported Feb 28, 2025 · Campaign 25V133000 · Model years: 2022

Ford is recalling certain Maverick, Escape, and Lincoln Corsair vehicles that were fixed incorrectly under an earlier recall for this same issue. A software problem in the hybrid transmission control module may cause the vehicle to shift into neutral unexpectedly, which means the vehicle can suddenly lose the power that drives the wheels.

Ford Escape, Lincoln Corsair Battery Defect Risks Fire critical

Reported Dec 20, 2024 · Campaign 24V954000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Certain Ford Escape and Lincoln Corsair vehicles have a manufacturing defect in one or more of the high voltage battery cells. This defect may cause an internal short circuit and battery failure. A failed battery can cause the vehicle to lose drive power, and it can also increase the risk of a fire.

Ford and Lincoln vehicles missing dashboard airbag warning label low

Reported Nov 8, 2024 · Campaign 24V852000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Ford is recalling certain Ford and Lincoln vehicles, including the Explorer, Escape, F-150, Expedition and the Lincoln Aviator, Corsair and Navigator. The airbag warning label that belongs on the dashboard may be missing. That label shows how seat belts and other restraints should be used. Because the label is missing, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for occupant crash protection. Ford will mail owners the warning labels at no cost. Letters were mailed to owners in December 2024.

Ford Escape Passenger Air Bag Cover May Break high

Reported Nov 8, 2024 · Campaign 24V850000 · Model years: 2024

Ford is recalling certain 2024-2025 Escape vehicles because the passenger side instrument panel cover may have been manufactured incorrectly. This can prevent the air bag underneath from deploying as intended. During deployment, the air bag cover may break apart and send fragments into the vehicle, or the air bag itself may deploy improperly. As a result, these vehicles do not meet federal occupant crash protection standards.

Ford, Lincoln Fuel Injectors May Leak Fuel critical

Reported Aug 23, 2024 · Campaign 24V634000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Ford is recalling these vehicles because the fuel injectors may have been assembled without O-ring support discs. Without these discs in place, the fuel injectors can develop a fuel leak. A fuel leak that happens near something that could ignite it raises the chance of a fire starting in the vehicle. This affects certain Lincoln Corsair, Escape, Mustang, and Explorer vehicles built with this fuel injector assembly problem.

Ford Escape Adaptive Cruise Control Radar Misaligned high

Reported Jun 14, 2024 · Campaign 24V440000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

On certain Ford Escape two-wheel drive vehicles with adaptive cruise control, the radar module used by the system was not lined up correctly when the vehicle was built. Because of this, the cruise control may mistake something like an overhead object for something actually in the vehicle's path, causing the vehicle to unexpectedly slow down or stop. Other features that rely on this radar, like collision warning and automatic braking, may also not work as well as they should.

This recall covers certain Ford Maverick, Ford Escape, and Lincoln Corsair vehicles. A software problem in the system that manages the hybrid powertrain may cause the vehicle to shift into neutral unexpectedly, resulting in a loss of drive power.

Ford Escape recall questions

How many recalls does the Ford Escape have?

NHTSA lists 41 safety recalls covering the 2018–2024 Ford Escape. 33 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Ford and Lincoln Rearview Camera Image May Invert" (NHTSA campaign 26V123000), reported Mar 3, 2026.

Which Ford Escape model year has the most recalls?

The 2020 Ford Escape has the most, with 23 recalls. The 2019 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 3. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Ford Escape?

No. None of the 41 Ford Escape recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Ford Escape has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Ford dealer, including for second owners.
