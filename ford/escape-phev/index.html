Ford Escape and Lincoln Corsair Hybrid Battery Defect critical

Reported Feb 17, 2026 · Campaign 26V091000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

On these Ford Escape and Lincoln Corsair plug-in hybrid vehicles, a manufacturing defect in one or more high voltage battery cells may cause an internal short circuit and battery failure.

Ford Escape and Lincoln Corsair plug-in hybrid battery may fail critical

Reported Nov 14, 2025 · Campaign 25V789000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Ford is recalling certain Ford Escape and Lincoln Corsair plug-in hybrid vehicles. One or more of the cells inside the high voltage battery may have been built with a defect. That defect can cause a short circuit inside the battery, and the battery can then fail. Dealers will update two control units, the ones that manage the battery and the hybrid system. Vehicles fixed under an earlier recall still need this new repair done.
