Ford Fusion Energi high-voltage fuses lack proper shielding medium

Reported 2019-02-15 · Campaign 19V099000 · Model years: 2019

These Ford Fusion Energi plug-in hybrids do not have enough shielding around some high-voltage parts — specifically the fuses in the electrical center. Federal rules require that people cannot touch those live parts during normal use, and these cars fall short. Anyone reaching into that area could get a serious electric shock. The dealer will install a cover over the high-voltage fuses, free of charge, which brings the car back in line with the rules.
