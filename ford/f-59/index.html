Ford F-53 and F-59 Missing Front Wheel Hub Nut high

Reported Apr 14, 2026 · Campaign 26V235000 · Model years: 2026

On certain Ford F-53 and F-59 vehicles, the front axle hub assembly on the wheels may be missing a nut and a small pin that are supposed to be installed there to hold everything together properly. If this hardware is missing, the brakes on that wheel could be damaged, or the wheel could become loose and separate from the vehicle while it is being driven.

Ford F-53 and F-59 brake lights may stay lit medium

Reported Mar 17, 2026 · Campaign 26V155000 · Model years: 2026

Ford is recalling certain 2025-2026 F-59 and F-53 commercial stripped chassis vehicles. The brake lights on the outside of the vehicle may stay lit even when the driver is not pressing the brakes. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for lamps, reflective devices and related equipment. Dealers will inspect the brake pedal and repair the brake booster push rod if needed, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 27, 2026.

Ford F-53 and F-59 Rear Brake Caliper Bolts Loose high

Reported Mar 10, 2023 · Campaign 23V164000 · Model years: 2022

The bolts that hold the rear brake calipers in place may not have been tightened correctly. The caliper is the part that squeezes the brake pads to slow the vehicle. If those bolts are not tight, the caliper can work loose and may damage the wheel or the brake lines running to it. Dealers will check the rear caliper bolts, tighten them if needed, and replace any bolts that are damaged.

Ford E-350, E-450, F-59 Fuel Tank Leak Risk critical

Reported Dec 14, 2022 · Campaign 22V925000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Certain Ford E-350, E-450 and F-59 vehicles have a fuel tank with paint along the weld seam that can peel away. Once the paint peels, the exposed metal underneath can corrode, and that corrosion can lead to a fuel leak.

Ford F-53 F-59 Steering Wheel Bolt May Loosen high

Reported Jul 22, 2022 · Campaign 22V528000 · Model years: 2022

On certain F-53 and F-59 stripped chassis vehicles, the bolt attaching the steering wheel may loosen, which can cause the steering wheel to become loose or detach from the steering column.

Ford F-59 Recalled for Steering Bolt Issue high

Reported Mar 11, 2022 · Campaign 22V145000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Ford is recalling certain F-59 vehicles because the bolt that secures the intermediate steering shaft to the steering gear box may not be properly tightened. If this bolt is not secure, the intermediate steering shaft could separate from the steering gear box. This separation can affect the driver's ability to steer the vehicle. Dealers will inspect the bolt and replace it if necessary, free of charge.

Ford F-53, F-59 Rear Axle Lubricant Problem high

Reported Mar 24, 2021 · Campaign 21V202000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

These vehicles may not have enough lubricant in the rear axle. This can damage the rear wheel bearings and cause them to seize, which may lead to failure of the rear axle or driveshaft. If that happens, the vehicle could lose power, the parking brake may not work, and the transmission may not shift into 'Park.' Dealers will inspect the rear wheel bearings and replace them if needed, at no charge.

Ford F-59 recall questions

How many recalls does the Ford F-59 have?

NHTSA lists 7 safety recalls covering the 2020–2026 Ford F-59. 6 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Ford F-53 and F-59 Missing Front Wheel Hub Nut" (NHTSA campaign 26V235000), reported Apr 14, 2026.

Which Ford F-59 model year has the most recalls?

The 2022 Ford F-59 has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2020 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Ford F-59?

No. None of the 7 Ford F-59 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Ford F-59 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Ford dealer, including for second owners.
