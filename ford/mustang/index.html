Ford Mustang recalls

37 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Ford Mustang engine wiring can crack, cutting power and lights high

Reported 2026-08-25 · Campaign 26V547000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Ground wires in the engine bay of some 2024-2026 Mustangs can crack where they connect. These connections complete the electrical circuits for several systems, so a break can shut down the engine's power while you drive, or stop the headlights, windshield washer, air conditioning, or engine cooling fan from working. Ford has told owners about the risk, but the parts to fix it are not ready yet; repair letters are expected around March 2027, and the work is free.

Ford Mustang wipers may stick on high in cold medium

Reported 2026-06-30 · Campaign 26V418000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

In cold weather, the windshield wipers on these Mustangs may run only on their fastest setting, and the washer that sprays fluid on the glass may not work right. That means you can't slow the wipers down for light rain or snow, and you may not be able to clear away salt, slush or grime. The problem traces back to the wiper motor. A dealer will check it and replace it if needed.

Ford and Lincoln dashboard display can go blank medium

Reported 2026-06-09 · Campaign 26V372000 · Model years: 2019

When you start the vehicle, the main display behind the steering wheel — the one showing your speed and warning lights — may come up completely blank. These vehicles had an earlier recall repair for this, but it was not done correctly, so the problem can still happen. With a blank display you cannot see how fast you are going or whether any safety warning lights have come on. A dealer will reload the correct software into that display unit at no cost.

Ford Explorer, Mustang, Lincoln Nautilus can lose driving power high

Reported 2026-03-03 · Campaign 26V122000 · Model years: 2025

A valve that routes some exhaust back into the engine can fail. When it does, the vehicle can suddenly lose the power that drives the wheels while you are on the road. That can leave you slowing down or coasting in traffic with other cars around you, with little warning. Dealers will replace the valve at no charge. Notification letters went out and you can look your vehicle up by VIN now.

Ford and Lincoln rearview camera image may fail medium

Reported 2025-10-13 · Campaign 25V695000 · Model years: 2020

The backup camera on these vehicles may show a picture that is warped, flickers in and out, or goes completely blank when you shift into reverse. Without a clear view behind you, it's easier to miss a child, a pet, or an object while backing up. Dealers will inspect the camera and replace it if needed, free of charge. Ford is doing this in phases by model and year, so the parts may not be ready for your vehicle yet.

Ford Mustang low brake fluid warning light may not come on high

Reported 2025-10-10 · Campaign 25V689000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Some 2021-2023 Mustangs that were repaired under an earlier recall were not fixed correctly. On these cars, the sensor that watches your brake fluid level may fail to turn on the dashboard warning light when the fluid gets low. That means you could keep driving with low brake fluid and never be told, and low fluid makes the brakes work less well. Ford says these cars do not meet the federal brake system rule. A dealer will reload the correct software.

Ford and Lincoln vehicles: backup camera image may fail medium

Reported 2025-09-04 · Campaign 25V572000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

When you put the vehicle in reverse, the backup camera may show a picture that is upside down, distorted, or completely blank. That leaves you with less of a view behind you, so it is easier to miss a person or an object while backing up. Dealers will inspect the camera and replace it if needed at no cost. Ford is mailing owner letters in phases, starting January 2026 with more going out in June 2026.

Ford Mustang tail and marker lights may stop working medium

Reported 2025-08-22 · Campaign 25V546000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Water can get inside the small computer that runs your Mustang's outside lights. When that happens, the computer loses touch with some of the lights — the license plate light, the side marker lights, and the tail lights — and they may stop coming on. Other drivers behind you may not see your car at night or in bad weather. A dealer will seal the body seams and replace the computer if it has corroded.

Ford Mustang instrument display may go dark while driving medium

Reported 2025-08-08 · Campaign 25V516000 · Model years: 2024

One 2024 Mustang was fixed incorrectly during an earlier recall repair. Because of that, the display behind the steering wheel may not light up when you start the car or while you are driving. That screen shows your speed, the gear you are in, and warning lights. Without it, you cannot see how fast you are going or whether the car is trying to warn you about a problem.

Ford Mustang and Lincoln Nautilus headlights can fail high

Reported 2025-08-08 · Campaign 25V519000 · Model years: 2025

The small driver modules that run the LED headlights can contain a burnt diode. When that happens, the affected headlight can lose its low beam, high beam, daytime running light, front position light and front turn signal. On the Nautilus, the tail light, rear turn signal and rear position light on that side can go out as well. That means the vehicles do not meet the federal rule for lamps. Dealers inspect and replace the modules free.

Ford and Lincoln fuel pump failure can stall the engine high

Reported 2025-07-07 · Campaign 25V455000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

On these Ford and Lincoln trucks, SUVs and Mustangs, the low-pressure fuel pump, which is the part that moves gasoline out of the tank, can fail. If it does, the engine can stall and shut off while you are driving. The repair depends on what you own: on Super Duty pickups the dealer replaces the fuel pump itself, and on all the other vehicles the dealer reprograms the engine computer. Either repair is free. Owner letters were mailed July 27, 2026.

Ford and Lincoln rearview camera image may go blank medium

Reported 2025-06-27 · Campaign 25V442000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

A software error affects the backup camera. The screen may show a blank image when you shift into reverse, so you cannot see what is behind the vehicle. Or the camera picture may stay on the screen after you have shifted out of reverse, which can distract you while driving forward. Either situation raises the risk of a crash. A dealer will update the rearview camera software free of charge.

Ford and Lincoln rearview cameras may freeze or blank medium

Reported 2025-05-09 · Campaign 25V315000 · Model years: 2024

A software error in the module that runs the screen can make the backup camera image lag, freeze, or not appear at all when you put the vehicle in reverse. You could back into something or someone you cannot see. The fix is a software update to that module, done free at a dealer or, on some vehicles, sent to the vehicle over the air. Ford is doing this in phases, so timing depends on your model and year.

Ford and Lincoln transmission may cause unexpected vehicle movement critical

Reported 2025-03-14 · Campaign 25V164000 · Model years: 2025

Ford is recalling certain 2024-2025 F-150 and Bronco, 2024 Ranger, and 2025 Mustang, Explorer and Aviator vehicles. A part inside the transmission that routes fluid to the gears, called the valve body, may have been machined incorrectly. Reverse gear can fail, or the vehicle can move forward unexpectedly when you have selected reverse or neutral. That kind of surprise movement can cause a crash. Dealers will replace the valve body at no cost.

Ford Mustang steering wheel may turn by itself critical

Reported 2025-02-14 · Campaign 25V096000 · Model years: 2022

These Mustangs were already in the shop for an earlier steering recall, and that repair was not done correctly. A backup sensor that reads how hard you are turning the wheel may not be set right. Because of that, the steering wheel can move side to side on its own, even when you are holding it steady and not trying to turn. The dealer will load corrected power steering software at no cost to you.

Ford Mustang instrument cluster may go dark while driving medium

Reported 2024-10-25 · Campaign 24V802000 · Model years: 2024

On some 2024 Ford Mustangs, the instrument panel behind the steering wheel may stay dark when you start the car, or go blank while you are driving. When that happens you cannot see how fast you are going, and you cannot see the warning lights that tell you something is wrong with the vehicle. The cause is in the cluster's software, and the fix is new software loaded by the dealer.

Ford and Lincoln fuel injectors may leak gasoline critical

Reported 2024-08-23 · Campaign 24V634000 · Model years: 2024

The fuel injectors spray gasoline into the engine. On these vehicles, some injectors were put together at the factory without a small support disc that helps the rubber seal hold tight. Without that disc, gasoline can leak out. If leaking fuel reaches something hot, such as the engine or exhaust, it can catch fire. Only certain vehicles built with the faulty assembly are affected. A dealer will install new seals and the missing support discs at no cost.

Ford Mustang rear suspension knuckle may fail while driving high

Reported 2024-08-09 · Campaign 24V593000 · Model years: 2024

On certain 2024 Mustangs, a rear suspension part called the knuckle — the piece that holds the rear wheel in place — may have a flaw from the way it was cast. A flawed knuckle can crack and fail. If that happens while you are driving, the car can suddenly handle badly and you can lose steering control, which can cause a crash. A dealer will inspect the rear knuckles and replace any bad ones.

Ford Mustang Brake Warning Light Hides Low Brake Fluid high

Reported 2024-06-28 · Campaign 24V496000 · Model years: 2024

After a dealer updated the computer that runs your Mustang's electrical features, the brake fluid sensor may have been left set up wrong. The result is a brake fluid warning light that stays lit all the time. Because the light is always on, you would not notice if your brake fluid actually ran low. Low brake fluid can make the brakes stop working. The dealer will reload the correct software so the light works normally again.

Ford Mustang Steering Wheel May Turn On Its Own critical

Reported 2024-06-28 · Campaign 24V493000 · Model years: 2022

Your Mustang has a backup sensor that tells the power steering system how hard you are turning the wheel. On these cars it may have been set up wrong, and when that happens the steering wheel can move side to side by itself even though you are holding it steady. That makes the car hard to control. The repair is a software update to the power steering computer, done at the dealer.

Ford Mustang clutch line may melt, leak fluid high

Reported 2024-06-03 · Campaign 24V394000 · Model years: 2024

On certain 2024 Mustangs, the clutch pressure line may not be held in place properly and can touch hot exhaust parts. The heat can melt the line, which leads to a brake fluid leak. If that happens, the car can lose drive power while you are driving, and leaking brake fluid can catch fire if it meets a source of ignition. Ford dealers will inspect the clutch pressure line and repair it if needed, at no charge.

Ford Mustang fuel line can leak gas and catch fire critical

Reported 2024-03-22 · Campaign 24V220000 · Model years: 2024

Some 2024 Ford Mustangs with the 2.3-liter engine have a low-pressure fuel line that may be damaged or cut. That can let gasoline leak out. If the leaking fuel reaches something hot, like the engine or exhaust, it can catch fire. Losing fuel can also make the engine stall while you are driving. The dealer replaces the low-pressure fuel supply line at no cost to you. Ford mailed owner letters in April 2024.

Ford Mustang brake pedal may be loose and fail critical

Reported 2023-11-28 · Campaign 23V800000 · Model years: 2024

The brake pedal assembly in these cars may have been built wrong, which can leave the brake pedal feeling loose. A loose brake pedal can mean the brakes stop working when you press it. Ford is telling owners not to drive these cars until they are repaired. A dealer will replace the brake pedal and its mounting bracket with correctly built parts, and you will not be charged anything for the work.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired.

Ford Mustang low brake fluid warning light may not work high

Reported 2023-10-27 · Campaign 23V727000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

Your Ford Mustang is supposed to light up a warning on the dash when the brake fluid gets low. On these cars that warning light may never come on, even when the fluid really is low. So you could keep driving for a long time without knowing there is a problem. Low brake fluid means the brakes do not work as well as they should, and the car may take longer to stop.

Ford and Lincoln SUVs may roll away from Park critical

Reported 2023-02-10 · Campaign 23V070000 · Model years: 2022

A bolt inside the automatic transmission may be loose. If it is, the transmission may not actually go into Park, even though the gear shifter shows "PARK." That means the vehicle is not locked in place and can roll away on its own after you get out. Dealers will inspect the transmission and replace it if necessary, at no cost to you. Ford mailed owner letters on April 25, 2023.

Ford Mustang Software Glitch Disables Backup Camera, Safety Features medium

Reported 2022-05-27 · Campaign 22V382000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

The main engine computer can send a false error signal to other parts of the car. That signal can shut off your backup camera and your reverse light at the same time, so you cannot see well behind you when backing up. It can also switch off traction control, forward collision warning, blind spot warning, and cross traffic alert. A dealer reloads the computer's software free of charge and the features work normally again.

Ford Mustang front camera aimed wrong, disables safety features medium

Reported 2022-05-13 · Campaign 22V334000 · Model years: 2022

The camera that looks out the front of the windshield was not aimed correctly at the factory, so it does not see the road the way it is supposed to. Because several driver-assistance features rely on that camera, they may work poorly or shut off entirely. That includes the automatic emergency braking system, adaptive cruise control, lane keeping, the driver alert warning, and automatic high beams. A dealer will simply realign the camera.

Ford Mustang passenger knee airbag may deploy improperly high

Reported 2022-02-16 · Campaign 22V083000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

One of the welds on the knee airbag for the front passenger was not done well enough. That airbag sits low in the dash and is meant to cushion the passenger's legs in a crash. Because of the weak weld, it may not open the way it should. If it opens the wrong way during a crash, it can cause injury instead of preventing it. Dealers will replace the front passenger knee airbag at no cost to you.

Ford Mustang: side curtain airbags may inflate improperly high

Reported 2021-12-16 · Campaign 21V980000 · Model years: 2021

Certain 2021 Ford Mustang vehicles have side curtain airbags — the airbags that drop down along the side windows — that were built with the wrong amount of compressed gas inside the inflator. With the wrong gas charge, the airbag may not fill up properly in a crash. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety rules for side impact protection and for keeping occupants from being thrown out of the vehicle. Dealers will replace the side curtain airbags.

Ford and Lincoln backup camera image may go blank medium

Reported 2020-09-23 · Campaign 20V575000 · Model years: 2020

A poor electrical connection to the backup camera can make the screen go blank or show a fuzzy, distorted picture when you shift into reverse. It may happen on and off rather than all the time. If you cannot see what is behind you, it is easier to back into a person or an object. Federal rules require a working rear camera on these vehicles. Dealers will replace the camera at no charge.

Ford Mustang brake pedal bracket can break during hard stops high

Reported 2020-09-23 · Campaign 20V573000 · Model years: 2020

On these Mustangs with automatic transmissions, the metal bracket that holds the brake pedal in place can crack when you brake hard in a sudden stop. If the bracket breaks, the pedal may not work the way it should and you could lose some or all of your ability to slow the car — most likely at the exact moment you need the brakes most. Ford dealers will replace the brake pedal bracket assembly free of charge.

Ford Mustang forward camera misaligned, driver aids may fail medium

Reported 2020-05-27 · Campaign 20V306000 · Model years: 2020

Certain 2020 Ford Mustangs have a forward-looking camera that may be aimed incorrectly. That camera feeds the driver-assistance features — automatic emergency braking (Pre-Collision Assist), adaptive cruise control, lane keeping, the driver alert system and automatic high beams — so any of them may not work as intended. The real danger is expecting the car to brake or steer for you when it cannot. A dealer will re-aim the camera for free.

Ford Mustang and Expedition park warning too short medium

Reported 2020-05-08 · Campaign 20V263000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Ford is recalling certain 2019 Expedition and 2019-2020 Mustang vehicles. When the transmission is not in park, the car is supposed to show a "Transmission Not In Park" message and sound a chime to warn you. On these vehicles that message and chime do not last as long as they should, so a driver may not notice them. If you get out without shifting into park, the vehicle can roll on its own. Dealers will reprogram the instrument cluster for free.

Ford and Lincoln gauge display may go blank medium

Reported 2019-02-11 · Campaign 19V076000 · Model years: 2019

When you start the vehicle, the instrument display behind the steering wheel may come up completely blank instead of showing your gauges, which means it does not meet the federal rule for controls and displays. With a blank screen you cannot see how fast you are going, how much fuel is left, the engine temperature, or any warning lights from the safety systems. Driving without that information makes it easier to speed or to miss a problem the vehicle is trying to tell you about.

Roush Supercharger Kit Can Cause Mustang Fuel Leak critical

Reported 2018-05-24 · Campaign 18E035000 · Model years: 2018

Roush sold an aftermarket supercharger kit for the 2018 Ford Mustang, and some of the small hose fittings in those kits were made incorrectly. A wrong fitting can damage the fuel lines that carry gasoline to the engine, and gasoline can then leak out. Leaking fuel raises the chance of a fire. Roush is replacing the faulty fuel rail parts on affected kits for free. This only affects Mustangs that had this particular Roush kit installed.

Ford and Lincoln 10-speed transmissions may not hold Park high

Reported 2018-04-04 · Campaign 18V213000 · Model years: 2018

These vehicles have a 10-speed automatic transmission, and a small metal pin inside it may have been left out at the factory. Without that pin, the transmission may not actually hold the vehicle in Park, even though you moved the shifter to Park and the dashboard shows Park. If you get out without setting the parking brake, the vehicle can roll away on its own. A dealer will look inside the transmission and add the missing pin.

Ford Mustang gear display may not show PARK medium

Reported 2017-12-18 · Campaign 17V814000 · Model years: 2018

On certain 2018 Mustangs with an automatic transmission and the large 12.4-inch digital gauge screen, the display may not show which gear the car is in if you shut the ignition off while it is not in PARK. You may also not hear the usual chime warning you that the key is still in the ignition when you open the driver's door. So nothing tells you the car was left out of PARK.
