Bronco Sport, Maverick Front Suspension Detachment Risk critical

Reported May 27, 2026 · Campaign 26V340000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Some Ford Bronco Sport and Maverick vehicles may have front lower control arm ball joints that were installed or repaired incorrectly at the assembly plant. This can allow the control arm to disconnect from the front wheel. A detached control arm can cause a loss of vehicle control, increasing the risk of a crash.

⚠ NHTSA advises: do not drive this vehicle until repaired.

Ford and Lincoln Block Heater Fire Risk critical

Reported Oct 10, 2025 · Campaign 25V685000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

In certain Ford and Lincoln vehicles, the engine block heater can crack and develop a coolant leak. If the block heater is plugged in when this happens, it can short circuit.

Ford Bronco Sport, Escape: Fuel Injector Fire Risk critical

Reported Jul 11, 2025 · Campaign 25V467000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

On certain Ford Bronco Sport and Escape vehicles with 1.5-liter engines, a fuel injector may crack and leak fuel inside the engine compartment. If this fuel leak occurs near a hot engine or exhaust part, it could ignite. Ford will update the engine control software as an interim fix, and will later replace the high-pressure fuel rail assembly and fuel injectors to fully correct the problem.

Ford and Lincoln rearview cameras may show blank image medium

Reported Jun 27, 2025 · Campaign 25V442000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Ford is recalling certain Ford and Lincoln vehicles, including the Explorer, Escape, F-150, Expedition, Ranger, Mustang, Bronco Sport, Maverick, Transit and the Lincoln Aviator, Corsair, Navigator and Nautilus. A software problem can make the rearview camera show a blank picture when the vehicle is backing up. The picture can also stay on the screen after the driver has finished backing up. Dealers will update the rearview camera software at no cost. Letters were mailed to owners in September 2025.

Ford Escape, Bronco Sport Fuel Injector Leak Redo critical

Reported Mar 14, 2025 · Campaign 25V165000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

This recall covers certain Ford Escape and Bronco Sport vehicles that were already repaired once for a related fuel system problem, but the repair did not fully fix it. A fuel injector in these vehicles may crack and leak fuel inside the engine compartment.

Ford Bronco Sport, Maverick Battery Detection Fault high

Reported Mar 14, 2025 · Campaign 25V158000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

This recall covers Ford Bronco Sport and Maverick vehicles that were already repaired once for a related problem, but the fix did not fully work. The control modules that manage the body and transmission may fail to notice when the 12-volt battery is running low. If that happens, the vehicle could lose electrical features like hazard lights, or lose the power needed to keep driving.

Ford Bronco Sport, Maverick: 12-Volt Battery May Fail high

Reported Jan 17, 2025 · Campaign 25V019000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

The 12-volt battery in these vehicles can degrade over time and suddenly stop working. If that happens, the vehicle could lose electrical accessories like the hazard lights, or lose the power needed to keep driving. Dealers will inspect the battery and replace it if needed, at no charge. Vehicles already repaired under an earlier recall for this issue will need this new remedy completed.

Ford Bronco Sport May Miss Low Battery Charge high

Reported Sep 13, 2024 · Campaign 24V683000 · Model years: 2024

Certain 2024 Ford Bronco Sport vehicles have body and powertrain control modules that may fail to detect a change in the 12-volt battery's state of charge. If the modules do not notice that the battery charge has dropped, the vehicle may lose electrical accessories, such as the hazard lights, or it may lose the power needed to keep driving.

Ford Maverick and Bronco Sport windshield wipers may stop working medium

Reported Aug 9, 2024 · Campaign 24V594000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Ford is recalling certain Maverick, Bronco Sport, and Mustang Mach-E vehicles because the motor that drives the front windshield wipers can quit working. If that motor fails, the wipers stop moving. Dealers will look at the wiper motor and put in a new one if it needs replacing, at no cost to the owner. Ford has mailed letters to affected owners to let them know about the problem.

Ford Bronco Sport, Maverick Battery Detection Problem high

Reported Apr 12, 2024 · Campaign 24V267000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

On these Ford vehicles, the body and powertrain control modules may fail to notice when the 12-volt battery's charge gets low. If a low battery charge isn't detected, the vehicle could lose electrical accessories such as the hazard lights, or it could lose the power needed to keep driving.

Ford Maverick Bronco Sport Tires May Cause Handling Loss high

Reported Mar 22, 2024 · Campaign 24V223000 · Model years: 2023

Some Maverick and Bronco Sport vehicles have tires that may have been manufactured incorrectly. This can lead to a loss of vehicle handling and control.

Ford Bronco Sport, Escape Fuel Injector May Leak high

Reported Mar 8, 2024 · Campaign 24V187000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

In certain Ford Bronco Sport and Escape vehicles with 1.5L engines, a fuel injector inside the engine may develop a crack. If the injector cracks, fuel can leak out of it and collect inside the engine compartment. If that leaking fuel reaches an ignition source, such as a hot engine part or hot exhaust component, it can lead to a fire.

Ford Bronco Sport Brake Module May Leak high

Reported Jan 23, 2023 · Campaign 23V021000 · Model years: 2023

Some Ford Bronco Sport vehicles have an Anti-lock Brake System module that may have been damaged during manufacturing. Valves inside the module can be damaged, causing the module to leak internally. This leak can affect how the brakes respond, leading to the brake pedal traveling further than expected or the vehicle moving unexpectedly during an Auto Hold event. This means the brakes may not respond the way the driver expects.

Ford Escape and Bronco Sport Fuel Injector Leak critical

Reported Nov 18, 2022 · Campaign 22V859000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023

On these Ford Escape and Bronco Sport vehicles with 1.5-liter engines, a fuel injector may crack and leak fuel inside the engine compartment.

Ford Bronco Sport Fuel Pump Module Stall Risk high

Reported Oct 20, 2022 · Campaign 22V787000 · Model years: 2022

On these vehicles, the bracket that holds the fuel pump control module to the fuel tank may not be properly secured. If it isn't, the module can fail, which can cause the engine to stall while driving. A stall increases the risk of a crash.

Ford Bronco Sport Parking Lights May Flicker high

Reported Sep 14, 2022 · Campaign 22V686000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

On certain Ford Bronco Sport vehicles, the parking lights may flicker when the headlights are turned on.

Ford Escape, Bronco Sport Engine Oil May Leak critical

Reported Mar 25, 2022 · Campaign 22V191000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

On vehicles with the 1.5L engine, the engine oil separator housing may crack. Once cracked, it can develop an oil leak. If that leaking oil reaches a hot engine or exhaust component that could ignite it, it raises the chance of a fire.

Ford Bronco Sport, Escape Rear Brake Problem medium

Reported Nov 23, 2021 · Campaign 21V922000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

The rear brake linings on these vehicles may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can affect how well the brakes perform. In certain situations, you may need to press the brake pedal harder than usual, and the distance needed to stop the vehicle may be longer than expected. To fix this, dealers will replace the front brake pads at no charge to the owner.

Ford Bronco Sport Moonroof Glass May Detach high

Reported Oct 15, 2021 · Campaign 21V809000 · Model years: 2021

The moonroof glass on affected vehicles may not have been bonded correctly to the moonroof frame during manufacturing. Because of this, the glass panel could come loose and detach from the vehicle.

Ford Escape, Bronco Sport, Corsair Engine Stall Risk high

Reported Aug 10, 2021 · Campaign 21V625000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Ford is recalling certain Escape, Bronco Sport, and Lincoln Corsair vehicles because the fuel delivery module inside the fuel tank may leak. This can cause low fuel pressure, which could cause the engine to stall. An engine stall increases the risk of a crash.

Ford Bronco Sport Rear Suspension Bolts May Be Loose high

Reported Feb 16, 2021 · Campaign 21V075000 · Model years: 2021

Certain 2021 Ford Bronco Sport vehicles may have loose or missing bolts in the rear suspension subframe assembly.

Ford Bronco Sport Has Wrong Front Control Arms high

Reported Feb 16, 2021 · Campaign 21V074000 · Model years: 2021

Certain 2021 Ford Bronco Sport vehicles may have been built with the incorrect front lower control arms installed on the vehicle.

Ford, Lincoln Crossovers Rear Drive Axle Seizure high

Reported Jan 15, 2021 · Campaign 21V011000 · Model years: 2021

Certain Ford Edge, Escape and Bronco Sport, along with Lincoln Corsair and Nautilus vehicles, may have an inadequate amount of lubricant in the rear drive unit. Without enough lubricant, the rear drive axle can seize while the vehicle is being driven, affecting how the vehicle moves.

Ford Bronco Sport recall questions

How many recalls does the Ford Bronco Sport have?

NHTSA lists 23 safety recalls covering the 2021–2024 Ford Bronco Sport. 20 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Bronco Sport, Maverick Front Suspension Detachment Risk" (NHTSA campaign 26V340000), reported May 27, 2026.

Which Ford Bronco Sport model year has the most recalls?

The 2021 Ford Bronco Sport has the most, with 16 recalls. The 2024 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 8. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Ford Bronco Sport?

Yes. 1 Ford Bronco Sport recall carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA: Bronco Sport, Maverick Front Suspension Detachment Risk (26V340000). These apply only to the affected vehicles, so check your VIN.

How do I check whether my Ford Bronco Sport has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Ford dealer, including for second owners.
