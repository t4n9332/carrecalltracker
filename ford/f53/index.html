Ford F-53 chassis rear stabilizer brackets may break off high

Reported Sep 30, 2022 · Campaign 22V731000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Ford is recalling certain 2020-2021 F-53 recreational stripped chassis vehicles. On these vehicles, the brackets that hold the rear stabilizer bar in place can break and come away from the vehicle. Dealers will check how tight the bolts are on both rear stabilizer bar attachment brackets and will replace the welded nuts and bolts if needed, at no cost to the owner. Ford mailed notification letters to owners in March 2023.

Ford F53 Front Wheel Studs May Not Be Treated Correctly high

Reported Jul 16, 2018 · Campaign 18V470000 · Model years: 2018

On affected chassis with 22.5 inch front wheels, the wheel hub assembly may have studs that were not properly heat treated.

Ford F53 recall questions

How many recalls does the Ford F53 have?

NHTSA lists 2 safety recalls covering the 2018–2021 Ford F53. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Ford F-53 chassis rear stabilizer brackets may break off" (NHTSA campaign 22V731000), reported Sep 30, 2022.

Which Ford F53 model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Ford F53 has the most, with 1 recall. The 2018 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Ford F53?

No. None of the 2 Ford F53 recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Ford F53 has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Ford dealer, including for second owners.
