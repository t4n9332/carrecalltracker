Ford and Lincoln vehicles: backup camera image may fail medium

Reported 2025-09-04 · Campaign 25V572000 · Model years: 2019

When you put the vehicle in reverse, the backup camera may show a picture that is upside down, distorted, or completely blank. That leaves you with less of a view behind you, so it is easier to miss a person or an object while backing up. Dealers will inspect the camera and replace it if needed at no cost. Ford is mailing owner letters in phases, starting January 2026 with more going out in June 2026.

Ford Econoline and Ram ProMaster backup camera image reversed medium

Reported 2019-06-11 · Campaign 19V428000 · Model years: 2019

These work vans and trucks have an add-on backup camera screen that can reset itself to factory settings. When that happens, the picture flips left to right, like a mirror image. Something that appears on the right side of the screen is really on your left. Backing up, you could steer the wrong way to avoid a person or object behind you. The company that installed the screens will notify owners and provide a free fix.
