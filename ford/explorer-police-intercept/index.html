Ford Explorer Police Interceptor Rear Axle Bolt Issue high

Reported Apr 14, 2022 · Campaign 22V255000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On certain Ford Explorer Police Interceptor and related Explorer models, a bolt that mounts the rear axle can fracture, or break. If this bolt breaks, it can cause the driveshaft to disconnect. When the driveshaft disconnects, the vehicle can roll away on its own if it is left in 'Park' without the parking brake applied, since the disconnected driveshaft can no longer keep the vehicle from moving.

Explorer, Aviator washer fluid contaminated with brake fluid high

Reported Jan 15, 2021 · Campaign 21V014000 · Model years: 2020

On some Explorer Police Interceptor Utility and Aviator vehicles, the windshield washer fluid was contaminated with brake fluid. Washer fluid contaminated this way can reduce the driver's ability to clear the windshield, which increases the risk of a crash.
