Nissan Rogue Folding Key May Shut Off Vehicle high

Reported Feb 16, 2023 · Campaign 23V093000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022

These Nissan Rogue and Rogue Sport vehicles use a jackknife style ignition key. While the vehicle is being driven, this jackknife key may collapse into a folded position. If the driver then makes contact with the key while it is in that collapsed position, it could accidentally shut off the vehicle while driving. If the vehicle shuts off while it is being driven, the airbags may not deploy the way they are supposed to in a crash.

Nissan Rogue Sport Seat Belt May Release Early medium

Reported Jul 21, 2021 · Campaign 21V555000 · Model years: 2021

The front passenger seat belt's automatic locking feature, which is used to hold a child seat tightly in place, may switch off earlier than it should. When that happens, the seat belt doesn't hold a child seat as securely as it's supposed to.

Nissan Rogue Sport Liftgate Hinges May Separate high

Reported Jul 9, 2021 · Campaign 21V525000 · Model years: 2021

Nissan is recalling certain Rogue Sport vehicles because the hinges that hold the liftgate in place can separate from the vehicle if the vehicle is involved in a rear-end crash. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet federal safety standards for door locks and door retention. This means the liftgate may not stay properly attached and provide the protection it's designed for during that kind of crash.

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported Sep 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.

Nissan Rogue Sport recall questions

How many recalls does the Nissan Rogue Sport have?

NHTSA lists 4 safety recalls covering the 2018–2022 Nissan Rogue Sport. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Nissan Rogue Folding Key May Shut Off Vehicle" (NHTSA campaign 23V093000), reported Feb 16, 2023.

Which Nissan Rogue Sport model year has the most recalls?

The 2021 Nissan Rogue Sport has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2020 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Nissan Rogue Sport?

No. None of the 4 Nissan Rogue Sport recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Nissan Rogue Sport has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Nissan dealer, including for second owners.
