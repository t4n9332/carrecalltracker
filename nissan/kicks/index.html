Nissan Kicks Software Error Can Blank Instrument Screen medium

Reported May 21, 2026 · Campaign 26V331000 · Model years: 2025

A software error in the vehicle's combination meter, the display behind the steering wheel, may cause the screen to go partly or fully blank. When that happens, the screen can't show important information such as your speed or warning lights while you're driving.

Nissan Kicks and Frontier backup camera screen may blank medium

Reported Jun 4, 2025 · Campaign 25V324000 · Model years: 2025

Nissan is recalling certain Frontier and Kicks vehicles because the center display screen may go blank when the driver shifts into reverse. That screen is where the rear camera image normally appears, so the driver may see nothing at all while backing up. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule for rear visibility. Dealers will update the software that runs the center display screen.

Nissan Kicks Certification Label Lists Wrong Weight Ratings low

Reported Apr 18, 2025 · Campaign 25V188000 · Model years: 2025

On certain 2025 Kicks, the certification label may show incorrect weight rating numbers, which could lead to the vehicle being loaded beyond what it can safely carry. Some SR trim vehicles with the optional 19-inch wheels may also have a label listing the wrong wheel size. This means the vehicles don't meet the required certification and tire standards.

Nissan Kicks Park Warning May Not Sound high

Reported Dec 23, 2024 · Campaign 24V969000 · Model years: 2025

Nissan is recalling certain 2025 Kicks vehicles because a warning that's supposed to alert the driver if the car is turned off while still in gear, instead of "Park," may not go off as it should. Without that warning, a driver could turn off and leave the vehicle without realizing it isn't in Park, and the vehicle could then move on its own.

Nissan and Infiniti driver airbags may deploy improperly high

Reported Feb 27, 2024 · Campaign 24V154000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti cars and SUVs because of a problem that happened during manufacturing. The inflator in the driver's airbag, which is the part that fills the bag, may absorb moisture. If that happens, the airbag may deploy improperly. Dealers will replace the entire driver's airbag assembly at no cost to the owner. Nissan mailed owner notification letters on April 10, 2024.

Nissan Kicks and Versa may lose power steering high

Reported Jul 18, 2023 · Campaign 23V496000 · Model years: 2021

Some 2021 Nissan Versa and Kicks vehicles have a wiring issue in the electric power steering system. An occasional bad connection in the sensor that measures steering effort can turn off the power steering assist. This recall also covers vehicles that were already checked under an earlier recall but never got their steering column replaced; those vehicles will need to be inspected again.

Nissan Kicks And Versa Power Steering Loss high

Reported Sep 15, 2022 · Campaign 22V693000 · Model years: 2021

On some Nissan Versa and Kicks vehicles, the electric power steering system has a torque sensor that can develop an intermittent electrical connection. When this happens, the power steering assist can turn off. Without power steering assist, it takes more effort to turn the steering wheel, especially at low speeds.

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported Sep 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.

Nissan Kicks recall questions

How many recalls does the Nissan Kicks have?

NHTSA lists 8 safety recalls covering the 2018–2025 Nissan Kicks. 4 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Nissan Kicks Software Error Can Blank Instrument Screen" (NHTSA campaign 26V331000), reported May 21, 2026.

Which Nissan Kicks model year has the most recalls?

The 2025 Nissan Kicks has the most, with 4 recalls. The 2018 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Nissan Kicks?

No. None of the 8 Nissan Kicks recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Nissan Kicks has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Nissan dealer, including for second owners.
