Nissan Kicks recalls

8 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Nissan Kicks driver display screen can go blank high

Reported 2026-05-21 · Campaign 26V331000 · Model years: 2025

A software error in the instrument display of certain 2025 and 2026 Kicks vehicles can make the screen in front of the driver go partly or completely blank. When that happens you cannot see your speed or any warning lights, so you would not know how fast you are going or that something needs attention. Because of this the display does not meet the federal rule for controls and displays. Dealers will reprogram the software.

Nissan Kicks backup camera screen can go blank medium

Reported 2025-06-04 · Campaign 25V324000 · Model years: 2025

On certain 2025 Nissan Kicks and Frontier vehicles, the big screen in the middle of the dash can go completely blank when you shift into reverse. That means no backup camera picture, so you cannot see what is directly behind you while backing up. These vehicles do not meet the federal rule for rear visibility. The fix is a free software update for the display at the dealer. Until it is fixed, rely on your mirrors and look over your shoulder when backing up.

Nissan Kicks sticker lists wrong weight and wheel info low

Reported 2025-04-18 · Campaign 25V188000 · Model years: 2025

The certification sticker on the car shows the wrong maximum weight the vehicle and its axles are allowed to carry. Going by those numbers, you could load the car more heavily than it is built to handle. On some SR models with the optional 19-inch wheel package, the same label also lists the wrong wheel size, which can lead to putting the wrong air pressure in the tires. A dealer puts on corrected labels.

Nissan Kicks may roll away without shift-to-park warning medium

Reported 2024-12-23 · Campaign 24V969000 · Model years: 2025

On some 2025 Nissan Kicks, the reminder that is supposed to tell you the car is not in Park may not come on. If you shut the engine off while the car is still in Drive or another gear, you may get no warning at all. You could walk away thinking the car is parked, and it can roll on its own and hit something or someone. The dealer reprograms the body control computer so the warning works again.

Nissan and Infiniti driver airbag may deploy improperly high

Reported 2024-02-27 · Campaign 24V154000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Because of a manufacturing problem, the driver's airbag in these Nissan and Infiniti vehicles can soak up moisture. A damp airbag may not inflate the way it is supposed to in a crash. That means the airbag in the steering wheel might not protect the driver properly in a collision. There is no warning light or symptom you can check yourself. The dealer replaces the whole driver's airbag at no cost.

Nissan Versa and Kicks can lose power steering assist medium

Reported 2023-07-18 · Campaign 23V496000 · Model years: 2021

Some 2021 Nissan Versa and Kicks vehicles have a faulty electrical connection in the sensor that tells the power steering system how hard you are turning the wheel. The connection can cut in and out, which shuts off the power steering help. The car can still be steered, but the wheel becomes much harder to turn, especially at low speeds such as parking or pulling out. Dealers will check a code on the steering column and replace the column if needed.

Nissan Versa and Kicks may lose power steering assist high

Reported 2022-09-15 · Campaign 22V693000 · Model years: 2021

On certain 2021 Nissan Versa and Kicks vehicles, an electrical connection inside the power steering sensor can cut out now and then. That sensor tells the car how hard you are turning the wheel, and if the connection drops, the power assist shuts off. The steering still works, but the wheel suddenly becomes much harder to turn, especially at low speeds like parking or pulling out of a driveway.

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported 2019-09-12 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The backup camera and display settings can be adjusted so the rear view image no longer appears — and the system keeps that setting the next time you shift into reverse. That means the screen can stay blank when you back up and you cannot see what is behind the vehicle. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility rule. A dealer will update the camera settings software for free.
