Nissan Altima recalls

14 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Nissan Altima Passenger Air Bag May Not Deploy high

Reported 2026-08-13 · Campaign 26V526000 · Model years: 2025

Certain 2025 Nissan Altima vehicles may have had the front passenger air bag and the front passenger knee air bag installed with incorrect mounting fasteners. Because of this, these air bags may not deploy as intended if the vehicle is in a crash. This affects the front passenger seating position in affected vehicles, where both the main air bag and the knee air bag could be affected.

Nissan Altima and Sentra Doors May Open high

Reported 2026-01-21 · Campaign 26V023000 · Model years: 2025

Some Nissan Altima and Sentra vehicles have door strikers, the part that holds a door latched shut, that may have been welded incorrectly and could break. If a striker breaks, the door it's attached to could open on its own while the vehicle is being driven.

Nissan, Infiniti Engine Bearings May Fail critical

Reported 2025-06-27 · Campaign 25V437000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Certain engines in these vehicles use bearings that may have a manufacturing defect. Over time, this defect can lead to the engine failing. In some cases, a failed bearing can cause a break in the engine block, which allows hot oil inside the engine to leak out while the vehicle is being driven.

Nissan Altima Steering Rack Bolts May Loosen high

Reported 2023-12-21 · Campaign 23V882000 · Model years: 2024

On certain Altima vehicles, the bolts connecting the electronic power steering unit to the steering rack may loosen. This can cause a loss of steering control or the steering to lock up.

Nissan Altima and Sentra rearview camera image may fail medium

Reported 2023-09-08 · Campaign 23V628000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Nissan is recalling certain Altima and Sentra vehicles because the wiring for the backup camera can be damaged. If that wiring is damaged, the image on the rearview camera screen can come out distorted, or the picture can stop showing up at all. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal government's rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will look at the camera and the wiring and replace whatever is damaged, or protect and reroute the wiring.

Nissan Altima and Titan Tires May Fail high

Reported 2021-03-11 · Campaign 21V169000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Certain Nissan Altima and Titan vehicles were equipped with Continental tires that may have been cured for too long during manufacturing. An over-cured tire can develop a break in its sidewall, leading to sudden air loss or the belt separating from the tire.

Nissan Altima Steering Tie Rod May Detach high

Reported 2021-03-04 · Campaign 21V138000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

A bolt that holds the tie rod ball joint in place on the steering was not tightened correctly when the vehicle was built. This can allow the tie rod ball joint to come loose and detach from the steering knuckle. If that happens, the driver can lose the ability to steer the vehicle, which can lead to a loss of control.

Nissan Altima Tires May Be Over Cured critical

Reported 2020-11-05 · Campaign 20V685000 · Model years: 2020

Nissan is recalling certain 2020 Altima S grade vehicles equipped with specific Continental ProContact TX tires, size 215/60 R16 H, with TIN date code 3320 and Mold ID S-419149. These tires may have been over cured during manufacturing. Dealers will inspect a vehicle's tires to see if they are affected by this issue, and will replace any that are, free of charge.

Nissan Altima, Leaf, Maxima Rear Window May Detach high

Reported 2020-09-22 · Campaign 20V570000 · Model years: 2020

Nissan is recalling certain Altima, Maxima, and Leaf vehicles because the rear window glass may not stay properly attached to the vehicle. This means the back window could come loose from the body of the car instead of remaining securely fastened in place as it should be. If the glass separates from the vehicle, it no longer serves as a secure, attached part of the car's structure at the rear.

Nissan Altima Hood Could Pop Open While Driving high

Reported 2020-05-28 · Campaign 20V315000 · Model years: 2018

Your Altima has two hood latches: a primary one and a backup. If the primary latch is accidentally released, the backup latch underneath can corrode over time. Corrosion can make the backup latch bind up and stay unlatched even though the hood appears closed. If the primary latch then gets released again, the backup latch may not catch it, and the hood could suddenly fly open while you are driving.

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported 2019-09-12 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.

Nissan Altima Fuel Pump Lock Ring May Disengage critical

Reported 2019-04-18 · Campaign 19V316000 · Model years: 2019

On certain 2019 front-wheel-drive Altimas with the 2.5 liter engine, the fuel pump lock ring may not be fully engaged, which can allow fuel to leak. This means the vehicle does not meet the federal fuel system safety standard.

Nissan Altima Brake Light Switch Misalignment Issue high

Reported 2019-03-25 · Campaign 19V235000 · Model years: 2019

The bracket holding the brake light switch may be installed in the wrong spot, causing the brake switch to stay turned on. This can make the brake lights stay lit even when the driver isn't pressing the brake pedal, which may confuse other drivers. It can also let the transmission shift lever move out of Park without the brake pedal being pressed, allowing the vehicle to roll away unexpectedly.

Nissan Altima: Fuel Tube Clip May Be Unlocked critical

Reported 2018-12-20 · Campaign 18V922000 · Model years: 2019

On some 2019 Nissan Altima vehicles, the clip connecting the low pressure fuel tube to the high pressure fuel pump may not have locked into position. If the clip stays unlocked, the fuel tube may shift and leak fuel. If the tube fully disconnects, the engine can stall. Dealers will route the fuel hose into the holding clip and lock it into place as necessary.
