Nissan Altima, Leaf, Maxima Rear Window May Detach high

Reported 2020-09-22 · Campaign 20V570000 · Model years: 2020

Nissan is recalling certain Altima, Maxima, and Leaf vehicles because the rear window glass may not stay properly attached to the vehicle. This means the back window could come loose from the body of the car instead of remaining securely fastened in place as it should be. If the glass separates from the vehicle, it no longer serves as a secure, attached part of the car's structure at the rear.

Nissan and Infiniti vehicles: brake fluid leak may cause fire critical

Reported 2019-11-08 · Campaign 19V807000 · Model years: 2018

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan Murano, Maxima and Pathfinder and Infiniti QX60 vehicles. The part that controls the anti-lock brakes can let brake fluid leak onto a circuit board inside it. These vehicles were covered by an earlier recall and were either not repaired or were inspected and judged not to need a repair. This recall also covers more vehicles than the earlier one. Dealers will replace the anti-lock brake part at no cost to owners.

⚠ NHTSA advises: park outside and away from structures (fire risk).

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported 2019-09-12 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.
