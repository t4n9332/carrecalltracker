Nissan Rogue recalls

22 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Nissan Rogue Turbo Engines May Fail Or Catch Fire critical

Reported 2026-02-12 · Campaign 26V080000 · Model years: 2023, 2024, 2025

In these Nissan Rogue vehicles with the 1.5L turbo engine, high engine temperatures can break down the engine oil. When the oil breaks down, the engine bearings can seize up and become damaged, which can lead to the engine failing. If a bearing fails badly enough, it can also cause a breach in the engine block, allowing hot oil to leak out of the engine.

Nissan Rogue Throttle Body Gears May Break high

Reported 2026-02-12 · Campaign 26V081000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

These Rogues are built with the three-cylinder 1.5-liter VC-Turbo engine. The electronic throttle body is the part that controls how much air reaches the engine when you press the accelerator, and inside it are small gears. Nissan says those gears can break. Dealers will reprogram the engine control software and will inspect the electronic throttle body assembly, replacing it if replacement is needed.

Nissan, Infiniti Engine Bearings May Fail critical

Reported 2025-06-27 · Campaign 25V437000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Certain engines in these vehicles use bearings that may have a manufacturing defect. Over time, this defect can lead to the engine failing. In some cases, a failed bearing can cause a break in the engine block, which allows hot oil inside the engine to leak out while the vehicle is being driven.

Nissan Rogue and INFINITI QX80 Rearview Camera May Go Blank medium

Reported 2024-10-03 · Campaign 24V748000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

A software error can leave the rearview camera screen blank when the vehicle is shifted into reverse. Instead of showing the view behind the vehicle, the display shows nothing. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard that requires that image to appear. The fix is a software update for the screen system, which can either be sent to the vehicle over the air or installed by a dealer.

Nissan Rogue Seat Belt Pretensioners May Not Restrain Occupants high

Reported 2024-02-28 · Campaign 24V156000 · Model years: 2023

On certain Nissan Rogue vehicles, the seat belt pretensioners for the driver and front passenger seats may have been manufactured incorrectly. Because of this, the seat belts may not properly restrain occupants in a crash, and the vehicles do not meet federal seat belt safety requirements. An occupant who is not properly held in place by their seat belt faces a greater risk of injury if a crash happens.

Nissan and Infiniti driver airbags may deploy improperly high

Reported 2024-02-27 · Campaign 24V154000 · Model years: 2023

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti cars and SUVs because of a problem that happened during manufacturing. The inflator in the driver's airbag, which is the part that fills the bag, may absorb moisture. If that happens, the airbag may deploy improperly. Dealers will replace the entire driver's airbag assembly at no cost to the owner. Nissan mailed owner notification letters on April 10, 2024.

Nissan Rogue Rear Brake Calipers May Leak high

Reported 2023-11-22 · Campaign 23V791000 · Model years: 2021

Nissan says the left and/or right rear brake caliper assemblies on some Rogue vehicles may be missing an internal bushing. This can allow the O-ring seal to move and brake fluid to leak out. Nissan is recalling the affected vehicles so dealers can replace the left and right rear brake calipers. Two vehicles previously inspected under an earlier recall that did not get a caliper replacement will need the new repair completed.

Nissan Rogue Rear Seat Belt Anchor Bolts Loose high

Reported 2023-05-25 · Campaign 23V374000 · Model years: 2023

Nissan is recalling certain 2023 Nissan Rogue vehicles because the fastener attaching a rear seat belt to its anchor point may not have been tightened properly at the factory. Over time, this loose fastener can loosen further. If a crash happens, a loose rear seat belt anchor may not properly hold a passenger in place, raising the risk of injury to that occupant.

Nissan Rogue, Pathfinder, Infiniti QX60 Seat Weld Failure high

Reported 2023-04-13 · Campaign 23V268000 · Model years: 2023

In some Nissan Rogue and Pathfinder, and Infiniti QX60 vehicles, the driver's power seat may not be fully attached to its frame. Over time, the welds holding the seat frame to a bracket at the back of the seat can come apart. If this happens, the seat is not as sturdy as it should be. In a crash, a seat that isn't properly secured may not hold the driver in place the way it's supposed to.

Nissan Rogue Folding Key May Shut Off Vehicle high

Reported 2023-02-16 · Campaign 23V093000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

These Nissan Rogue and Rogue Sport vehicles use a jackknife style ignition key. While the vehicle is being driven, this jackknife key may collapse into a folded position. If the driver then makes contact with the key while it is in that collapsed position, it could accidentally shut off the vehicle while driving. If the vehicle shuts off while it is being driven, the airbags may not deploy the way they are supposed to in a crash.

Nissan Frontier and Rogue Rearview Display Failure medium

Reported 2022-10-13 · Campaign 22V772000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

On some Nissan Frontier and Rogue vehicles, the in-vehicle infotainment system may reboot over and over again on its own, especially right after the vehicle is started. When the system keeps restarting like this, the rearview camera display may stop working properly. Without a working rearview display, the driver has less visibility of what is behind the vehicle while backing up, which the vehicle needs in order to meet a federal rear-visibility safety requirement.

Nissan Rogue Rear Seat Belts May Not Retract medium

Reported 2022-09-01 · Campaign 22V666000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

Nissan is recalling certain Rogue vehicles because the seat belts for the left and right rear seats may not retract properly. When a seat belt doesn't retract the way it's supposed to, it can be harder for a passenger to use correctly. Because of this, the affected vehicles do not meet federal safety standards covering seat belts and how well they protect occupants in a vehicle.

Nissan Rogue Fuel Tank Puncture Risks Fire high

Reported 2022-08-31 · Campaign 22V660000 · Model years: 2022

On some of these Rogues, the fuel tank may have been punctured while vehicle modifications were being performed. A hole in the fuel tank could leak fuel, which raises the risk of a fire.

Nissan Rogue and Pathfinder Rearview Camera Failure medium

Reported 2022-07-21 · Campaign 22V527000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

If the Sirius XM subscription status is set to 'Not Subscribed' on your Nissan Rogue, Frontier, or Pathfinder, it can cause the infotainment system to reboot. When that happens, the rearview camera stops working.

Nissan Rogue Fuel Tank Wall May Be Too Thin critical

Reported 2022-04-14 · Campaign 22V259000 · Model years: 2022

Some 2022 Nissan Rogue vehicles have a manufacturing problem where an area on the bottom wall of the fuel tank is thinner than it should be. Road debris could puncture that thin spot in the tank wall and cause a gas leak. The fuel tank may also not perform the way it's supposed to during a crash.

Nissan Rogue Fuel Pump May Overheat and Fail high

Reported 2021-12-09 · Campaign 21V957000 · Model years: 2021

Some Nissan Rogue vehicles have a fuel pump that wears unevenly on the inside. This abnormal wear can cause the pump to overheat and eventually fail while the vehicle is being driven. A fuel pump failure can cause the engine to stall, and a stalled engine while driving increases the chance of a crash for you and for others on the road.

Nissan Rogue Hybrid Wiring Damage Can Blow Fuse high

Reported 2021-10-26 · Campaign 21V839000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On certain Rogue Hybrid vehicles, a bracket for the engine's Electronic Control Module can come into contact with the engine wiring harness nearby. Nissan says this contact can damage the harness and cause a fuse to blow.

Nissan Rogue Child Seat Anchor May Fail medium

Reported 2021-06-24 · Campaign 21V474000 · Model years: 2021

Some Nissan Rogue vehicles have a problem with the second-row seats. The metal wires used to anchor a child seat to the seatback may not have been welded on correctly. This means a child seat attached to these anchors may not be held securely in place.

Nissan Rogue Rear Brake Calipers May Leak Fluid high

Reported 2021-04-22 · Campaign 21V286000 · Model years: 2021

Certain 2021 Rogues may have rear brake caliper assemblies missing an internal bushing. Without it, an O-ring seal inside the caliper can shift out of place and let brake fluid leak out.

Nissan Rogue wrong wheel nuts may cause wheel separation high

Reported 2021-03-17 · Campaign 21V186000 · Model years: 2021

Some Nissan Rogue vehicles may have had incorrect wheel nuts installed. These wrong nuts may loosen over time, and loosening nuts can cause the wheel to vibrate. That vibration can break the wheel studs, and if the studs break, the entire wheel assembly can separate from the vehicle while driving. A wheel separating from the vehicle increases the risk of a crash.

Nissan Rogue Fuel Hose May Leak or Disconnect critical

Reported 2021-02-11 · Campaign 21V068000 · Model years: 2021

On certain 2021 Nissan Rogue vehicles, a fuel hose may not be held in the correct position, and a lock ring on the fuel tank may not be fully seated. Because the hose isn't properly secured, fuel could leak out, or the hose could come apart completely. If the hose comes apart, the engine could stall while the vehicle is being driven.

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported 2019-09-12 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.
