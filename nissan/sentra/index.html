Nissan Sentra recalls

14 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Nissan Sentra driveshaft may slip, causing rollaway risk high

Reported 2026-06-26 · Campaign 26V410000 · Model years: 2025

The left front driveshaft, the rod that sends engine power to the front wheel, may not be pushed all the way into the transmission. It can work its way loose, leak transmission fluid, and leave the car without power to the wheels. Because the shaft is not fully locked in, the car may also roll away on its own when parked if you have not set the parking brake. Dealers will inspect and replace parts if needed.

Nissan Altima and Sentra: doors may open while driving high

Reported 2026-01-21 · Campaign 26V023000 · Model years: 2025

Each door latches onto a metal catch bolted to the body. On these vehicles those catches may have been welded badly and can break. If one breaks, that door can swing open while the car is moving. Someone leaning against the door could fall out, and a door flying open can cause a crash. The dealer will replace the door catches, called strikers, free of charge.

Nissan Sentra windshields may have vision-blocking air bubbles medium

Reported 2025-11-21 · Campaign 25V807000 · Model years: 2025

Some 2025 Sentra windshields left the factory with air bubbles trapped in the glass. You may see them as small blisters or distortions in your line of sight. Because of this, the glass does not meet the federal safety rule for car windows. Bubbles in the wrong spot can hide another car, a cyclist, or a person crossing the road. A Nissan dealer will check your windshield and replace it if needed, free.

Nissan Sentra driver's seat may not be secured high

Reported 2025-03-11 · Campaign 25V153000 · Model years: 2025

On certain 2025 Sentra cars, the driver's seat frame may not be fully fastened to the outer side of the seat track. That means the seat is not held down the way it should be. In a crash, a loose seat may not hold the driver in place properly, and the seat belt anchor points may not do their job. These cars do not meet the federal safety rules for seats and seat belt anchorages. A dealer will inspect the seat and replace the lower seat frame if needed.

Nissan Sentra windshield bubbles hide the VIN low

Reported 2024-07-01 · Campaign 24V498000 · Model years: 2024

The windshield may have air bubbles in the glass at the lower driver's side corner. That is the spot where your car's VIN, the number that identifies your exact vehicle, shows through. The bubbles can make the number hard to read, which breaks a federal glass standard. If you cannot read your VIN, you may not be able to tell whether your car is covered by a future recall and could miss a free repair. Dealers will check the windshield and replace it if needed.

Nissan Sentra Front Driveshaft Can Slip, Causing Power Loss high

Reported 2024-04-29 · Campaign 24V304000 · Model years: 2024

The front left driveshaft — the bar that sends power from the transmission to the wheel — may not be pushed in all the way, or the clip that holds it may be loose or missing. If it works free, transmission fluid can leak out and the car can stop driving. Dealers will check the driveshaft and, if needed, replace it along with the transmission at no cost to you.

Nissan and Infiniti driver airbag may deploy improperly high

Reported 2024-02-27 · Campaign 24V154000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Because of a manufacturing problem, the driver's airbag in these Nissan and Infiniti vehicles can soak up moisture. A damp airbag may not inflate the way it is supposed to in a crash. That means the airbag in the steering wheel might not protect the driver properly in a collision. There is no warning light or symptom you can check yourself. The dealer replaces the whole driver's airbag at no cost.

Nissan Altima and Sentra backup camera wiring can fail medium

Reported 2023-09-08 · Campaign 23V628000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

The wiring that carries the picture from the backup camera can get damaged. When that happens, the image on the screen may look distorted or may not appear at all when you shift into reverse, so these Altima and Sentra vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility requirement. Dealers will inspect the camera and its wiring and replace parts if they find damage. If nothing is damaged, they will add protective tape and reroute the wiring so it stops rubbing.

Nissan Sentra steering tie rods can bend and break critical

Reported 2023-08-15 · Campaign 23V581000 · Model years: 2020, 2021, 2022

A tie rod is the part that connects the steering to a front wheel. On these cars the left tie rod, the right one, or both can bend. A bent tie rod can eventually break, and if that happens you lose the ability to steer. Warning signs are a steering wheel that sits off-center when you are driving straight, or a vibration you can feel. Dealers will inspect and replace bent or broken rods, then later replace both with redesigned parts.

Nissan Sentra water leak can corrode electrical parts high

Reported 2023-06-12 · Campaign 23V421000 · Model years: 2022

On some 2022 Sentra cars, the seal near the base of the windshield on the driver's side may be missing or installed wrong. Water can get inside the car and rust the electrical wiring. That damage can knock out important systems — the windshield wipers, outside lights, or the airbags may stop working, and you might be able to shift out of Park without pressing the brake. Damaged wiring can also short out and start a fire.

Nissan Sentra right headlight may be aimed wrong medium

Reported 2022-04-13 · Campaign 22V244000 · Model years: 2020

Some 2020 Nissan Sentra cars with LED headlights may have the right-hand headlight pointed in the wrong direction. A headlight aimed incorrectly doesn't light up the road the way it should, so the driver may not see as much at night. The dealer will replace the right-hand headlight free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 10, 2022. Nissan's number for this recall is R20B4.

Nissan Sentra bent tie rods can cause steering loss critical

Reported 2021-06-17 · Campaign 21V461000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

The tie rods are the metal arms that connect the steering to the front wheels and make them turn. On some Sentras, the left tie rod, the right tie rod, or both may be bent. A bent tie rod can break. If it breaks while you are driving, you lose control of the steering with no warning. A dealer will inspect both tie rods and replace any bad ones at no charge.

Nissan Sentra Brake Lights May Fail to Light medium

Reported 2021-03-03 · Campaign 21V135000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The switch that turns on your brake lights when you press the brake pedal can get contaminated inside. When that happens the electrical connection does not close, and your brake lights stay dark even though you are actually slowing down. Drivers behind you get no warning that you are braking. The problem can come and go, and you would not notice it from the driver's seat unless someone tells you. The dealer replaces the switch and adds a protective seal to keep dirt out.

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported 2019-09-12 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.
