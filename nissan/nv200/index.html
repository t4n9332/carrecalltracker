Nissan NV200 and Chevy City Express can stall high

Reported 2025-10-09 · Campaign 25V676000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

A wiring bundle for the sensor that reads fuel tank temperature may have been routed the wrong way at the factory. Run wrong, those wires can rub and get damaged, causing a short that blows the fuse for the fuel pump. With that fuse blown, the pump stops feeding fuel and the engine can quit while you are driving — including in traffic or at an intersection. A dealer will inspect the wiring and reroute it, or replace the fuel pump assembly if needed.

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported 2019-09-12 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The backup camera and display settings can be adjusted so the rear view image no longer appears — and the system keeps that setting the next time you shift into reverse. That means the screen can stay blank when you back up and you cannot see what is behind the vehicle. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility rule. A dealer will update the camera settings software for free.
