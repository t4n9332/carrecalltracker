Nissan Frontier recalls

8 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2022. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Nissan Frontier and Rogue backup camera may go blank medium

Reported 2022-10-13 · Campaign 22V772000 · Model years: 2022

When you restart your Nissan, the screen in the dash can get stuck restarting over and over. While that is happening, the backup camera picture does not show up, so you cannot see what is behind you when you shift into reverse. Federal rules require a working rear camera view, so these trucks and SUVs do not meet that standard. The fix is new software for the screen, done at a dealer or sent to the vehicle wirelessly.

Nissan Titan and Frontier may roll away in Park high

Reported 2022-09-07 · Campaign 22V671000 · Model years: 2022

In certain 2020 through 2023 Nissan Titan, 2020 through 2022 Frontier, and 2023 Z vehicles, the part inside the transmission that locks the vehicle in place when you shift into Park may not catch. If it does not, the vehicle can roll away on its own after you think it is parked. Nissan tells owners to set the parking brake every single time they park until the repair is made. Dealers will update the vehicle's computer software.

Nissan Rogue, Frontier, Pathfinder backup camera can quit medium

Reported 2022-07-21 · Campaign 22V527000 · Model years: 2022

Certain 2021-2022 Nissan Rogue and 2022 Frontier, Pathfinder and Titan rental vehicles have a problem with the dashboard infotainment system. If the SiriusXM satellite radio subscription is set to "Not Subscribed," the unit can restart on its own, and while that happens the backup camera stops working. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the federal rear-visibility rule. Without a camera picture you can see less behind you when reversing, raising the risk of a crash.

Nissan Frontier and Titan may roll away in park high

Reported 2022-06-24 · Campaign 22V457000 · Model years: 2022

A small part inside the automatic transmission, called the parking pawl, is what holds the truck still when you put it in Park. On these trucks it may not catch. If it does not, the truck can roll away on its own after you shift into Park and walk away, even though the shifter says P. Nissan says to use the parking brake every single time you park until the repair is done.

Nissan Frontier left-rear seat belt bolts may loosen high

Reported 2021-12-09 · Campaign 21V958000 · Model years: 2022

The bolts that hold the left-rear seat belt bracket to the body may not have been tightened enough at the factory. That bracket is the anchor point the belt pulls against. If it is loose, the bracket can break under the heavy load of a crash, and the belt would no longer hold the passenger in place. Nissan dealers will replace those bolts and fix any damaged threads in the seat frame at no cost.

Nissan Frontier missing tow hitch risks fuel tank leak critical

Reported 2021-11-22 · Campaign 21V917000 · Model years: 2022

Some 2022 Frontier Crew Cab Long Bed trucks left the factory without the tow hitch that was supposed to be installed. That hitch also helps protect the fuel tank. Without it, if another vehicle hits the truck from behind, a piece of the rear suspension can push into the fuel tank and tear it open. Leaking gasoline at a crash scene can catch fire. The truck does not meet the federal fuel system safety rule.

Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported 2019-09-12 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.

Nissan Frontier and Versa key can shut engine off high

Reported 2018-08-22 · Campaign 18V551000 · Model years: 2018

These vehicles use a regular metal key. Over time, the weight of a heavy key ring, a rough road, or a hard bump can push the key out of the run position and back to accessory, which shuts the engine off while you are driving. A stall in moving traffic raises the risk of a crash. Dealers will check the ignition switch and replace it if it turns out to be one of the affected parts, free of charge.
