Nissan 370Z driver-side curtain airbag installed incorrectly medium

Reported 2018-03-06 · Campaign 18V153000 · Model years: 2018

The curtain airbag on the driver's side — the one that drops down from above the window in a side crash — may have been put in wrong at the factory. Because of that, it might not open the way it should if you are hit from the side. Nissan says these cars do not meet the federal rules for side-impact protection and for keeping people from being thrown out of the vehicle. A dealer will inspect the airbag and correct the installation.
