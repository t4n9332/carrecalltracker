Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off medium

Reported Sep 12, 2019 · Campaign 19V654000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Nissan is recalling certain Nissan and Infiniti vehicles, including the Altima, Rogue, Sentra, Murano, Pathfinder, Titan, Frontier, Leaf, Versa and several Infiniti Q and QX models. The back-up camera and display settings can be changed so that the rear view picture no longer appears, and the vehicle keeps that setting the next time it is put in reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility safety standard. Dealers will update the camera settings software at no cost.

Nissan Frontier, Versa Key Ignition May Shut Off Engine high

Reported Aug 22, 2018 · Campaign 18V551000 · Model years: 2018

Nissan is recalling certain Frontier, Versa, and Versa Note vehicles with a mechanical key ignition system. Over time, the weight hanging on the key ring, combined with road conditions or another jarring event, may cause the ignition switch to move out of the run position and into the accessory position. When that happens, the engine turns off while the vehicle is being driven, since the ignition switch is no longer in the position needed to keep the engine running.

Nissan Versa Note Side Airbag Inflator Part May Detach high

Reported Dec 20, 2017 · Campaign 17V837000 · Model years: 2018

On some Nissan Versa Note vehicles, a component of the side curtain airbag's inflator may detach while the airbag is deploying. Instead of staying inside the airbag system, this piece could end up inside the passenger area of the vehicle.

Nissan Versa Note recall questions

How many recalls does the Nissan Versa Note have?

NHTSA lists 3 safety recalls covering the 2018–2019 Nissan Versa Note. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Nissan and Infiniti backup camera image can stay off" (NHTSA campaign 19V654000), reported Sep 12, 2019.

Which Nissan Versa Note model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 Nissan Versa Note has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2019 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Nissan Versa Note?

No. None of the 3 Nissan Versa Note recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Nissan Versa Note has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Nissan dealer, including for second owners.
