Nissan Z Pop-Up Hood Sensor Wiring Problem high

Reported Oct 24, 2024 · Campaign 24V798000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

Certain Nissan Z vehicles may have the connectors for the right-side pedestrian detection sensors installed backwards in the front bumper wiring. This can prevent the pop-up engine hood from working the way it's supposed to in a pedestrian crash.

Nissan Z Pop-Up Hood Fasteners May Loosen high

Reported Oct 26, 2023 · Campaign 23V725000 · Model years: 2023, 2024

On certain Nissan Z cars, fasteners that hold a clip on the left side of the front bumper reinforcement can loosen over time. This clip is part of what allows the pop-up engine hood to activate, so if the fasteners come loose, the hood may fail to pop up and raise its rear edge the way it's designed to.

Nissan Z recall questions

How many recalls does the Nissan Z have?

NHTSA lists 2 safety recalls covering the 2023–2024 Nissan Z. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Nissan Z Pop-Up Hood Sensor Wiring Problem" (NHTSA campaign 24V798000), reported Oct 24, 2024.

Which Nissan Z model year has the most recalls?

The 2023 Nissan Z has the most, with 2 recalls. The 2023 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 2. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the Nissan Z?

No. None of the 2 Nissan Z recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my Nissan Z has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised Nissan dealer, including for second owners.
