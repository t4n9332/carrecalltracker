Mitsubishi Outlander recalls

15 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Outlander Rearview Camera May Not Display medium

Reported 2026-08-25 · Campaign 26V549000 · Model years: 2025

Mitsubishi is recalling certain Outlander and Outlander PHEV vehicles because of a software problem in the vehicle's infotainment system. Due to improper programming, the system may fail to show the rearview camera image on the screen when the vehicle is put into reverse. This means the driver may not be able to see the picture from the rearview camera while backing up, which does not meet federal rear visibility requirements.

Mitsubishi Outlander Liftgate Gas Spring May Fail high

Reported 2026-04-20 · Campaign 26V252000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

The gas spring cylinders that hold up the rear liftgate may corrode over time and lose pressure. This can cause a cylinder to rupture or cause the liftgate to fall shut unexpectedly.

Mitsubishi Outlander liftgate supports may corrode and fail medium

Reported 2025-08-07 · Campaign 25V507000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

Mitsubishi is recalling certain Outlander and Outlander PHEV vehicles that were sold in, or are now registered in, a list of states that includes Alaska, New York, Illinois, Michigan, Pennsylvania and the District of Columbia. The gas-filled cylinders that hold the rear liftgate open can corrode and lose pressure. A cylinder in that condition can burst, or the liftgate it is supposed to hold up can fall unexpectedly. Mitsubishi will replace the left and right liftgate gas springs.

Mitsubishi Outlander rearview camera image may freeze or vanish medium

Reported 2025-06-02 · Campaign 25V369000 · Model years: 2022, 2023, 2024

Mitsubishi is recalling certain Outlander and Outlander PHEV vehicles. Because of a software error in the vehicle's infotainment system, the picture from the rearview camera may freeze, or it may not appear on the display at all. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for rear visibility. This recall replaces and expands an earlier recall, and vehicles that were already repaired under that earlier recall need the new repair as well.

Mitsubishi Outlander Backup Camera Image Can Fail high

Reported 2023-06-06 · Campaign 23V345000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

A software error in the vehicle's infotainment system can prevent the backup camera's rearview image from appearing on the display. When this happens, the driver backing up does not get the rearview camera picture that the display is meant to show, even though the camera and display are otherwise working.

Mitsubishi Outlander rearview camera may not show image medium

Reported 2022-07-08 · Campaign 22V470000 · Model years: 2022

Certain 2022 Mitsubishi Outlanders have a software error that affects the rearview camera. Because of the error, the screen may not show the rearview image at all. That leaves the driver without the camera view of the area behind the vehicle. As a result, these vehicles do not meet the federal rear visibility standard. Dealers will update the rearview camera software free of charge, and owner notification letters were mailed on August 1, 2022.

Mitsubishi Outlander Seat Belts May Not Retract Fully medium

Reported 2022-05-13 · Campaign 22V335000 · Model years: 2022

In some Outlander SUVs, the automatic locking feature in the right front seat belt and in both second-row seat belts may stop working before the belt is completely pulled back in. This feature is what normally holds a child car seat tightly in place. If it shuts off too early, a child restraint system buckled into one of these seat belts may end up loose instead of held securely, so it might not fully do its job of keeping the child seat in place.

Mitsubishi Outlander Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported 2022-01-24 · Campaign 22V027000 · Model years: 2022

Mitsubishi is recalling certain 2022 Outlander vehicles because the wires inside the fuel pump may be fused together incorrectly, which can cause the fuel pump to fail.

Mitsubishi Outlander Center Pillar May Be Weak medium

Reported 2021-10-18 · Campaign 21V821000 · Model years: 2022

On one 2022 Outlander, a software error in a welding robot may have caused the vehicle's center pillar to be welded incorrectly. Because of this, the vehicle does not meet federal safety standards for side-impact protection, seat belt anchoring, and roof strength. If the vehicle is in a crash, this weaker body structure could increase the risk of injury.

Mitsubishi Outlander seat belts may not secure child seats high

Reported 2021-08-02 · Campaign 21V596000 · Model years: 2022

Mitsubishi is recalling certain 2022 Outlander vehicles because the front and second-row passenger seat belt automatic locking retractors may deactivate too early. When this happens, the retractor may not hold a child restraint system securely in place the way it is designed to. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet federal requirements covering occupant crash protection and child seat security.

Mitsubishi Outlander rear seat belt buckle may not latch medium

Reported 2020-07-13 · Campaign 20V403000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Mitsubishi is recalling certain Outlander and Outlander PHEV vehicles. When the second-row seat backs are folded down, the center seat belt buckle can press against the buckle on the right side. That contact can knock the cover off the right-side buckle, leaving it unable to latch and unable to hold a passenger in place. Dealers will modify the center buckle cover, add a new warning label to the seat support, and supply a new label insert for the owner's manual, free of charge.

Mitsubishi Outlander Rear Seat Belt Assembly Error high

Reported 2020-05-18 · Campaign 20V280000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Mitsubishi is recalling certain Outlander and Outlander PHEV vehicles because the seat belt for the right-side second-row seat may have been assembled incorrectly. Because of this, the seat belt may not properly hold and restrain the person sitting in that seat the way it is designed to.

Mitsubishi SUVs: Forward Collision Braking Software Issue high

Reported 2018-09-14 · Campaign 18V620000 · Model years: 2018

Certain 2018 Outlander PHEV, Outlander Sport, 2018-2019 Eclipse Cross, and 2017-2018 Outlander vehicles have a Forward Collision Mitigation system that detects pedestrians. Because of incorrect software, the system's control unit may apply the brakes for longer than needed, even after a pedestrian is no longer detected. This means the vehicle could keep braking on its own after the system should have released the brakes, since the software isn't correctly recognizing when to stop applying them.

Mitsubishi Vehicles May Have Faulty Safety System Software high

Reported 2018-09-14 · Campaign 18V621000 · Model years: 2018

Certain Mitsubishi Outlander Sport, Outlander PHEV, Eclipse Cross, and Outlander vehicles have a possible software problem in the control unit that manages the brake system. This could stop safety features such as automatic emergency braking, anti-lock brakes, electronic stability control, or the brake auto-hold function from working when they are needed. Dealers will update the software in the hydraulic control unit to fix the problem.

Mitsubishi Outlander label shows wrong rear rim size medium

Reported 2018-09-05 · Campaign 18V591000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Mitsubishi is recalling certain Outlander vehicles that came with 18-inch alloy wheels. The certification label on these vehicles lists the wrong rim size for the rear wheels. Because of that error, the label does not meet the federal rule covering tire selection and rims. Mitsubishi will send owners a corrected label at no charge, and owners who prefer can have a dealer put the new label on for them instead, also at no charge.
