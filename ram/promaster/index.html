RAM Promaster recalls

25 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Wheelchair Restraints in Various Vehicles May Not Lock high

Reported Dec 17, 2025 · Campaign 25V876000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

Certain vehicles equipped with QRT-Deluxe or QRT-Max wheelchair restraints may have retractors that fail to lock. If the retractor does not lock, a wheelchair inside the vehicle may not be properly secured.

Ram ProMaster Radiator Fan Circuit May Overheat high

Reported Oct 23, 2025 · Campaign 25V720000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025

The electrical circuit that powers the radiator cooling fan in these Ram ProMaster vans may overheat. An electrical circuit that gets too hot increases the chance of a fire starting in the vehicle. Because of this safety risk, some owners have already been sent an early notification letter ahead of the full recall notice, telling them about the danger before the repair is ready. Chrysler will fix the affected fan system as part of this recall.

Ram ProMaster Electric Vehicles Can Lose Drive Power high

Reported Oct 6, 2025 · Campaign 25V665000 · Model years: 2024, 2025

Some Ram ProMaster battery electric vehicles have software in the electric drive module that may not manage torque correctly. This can cause the vehicle to lose drive power.

Ram ProMaster, Dodge Journey backup camera may fail medium

Reported Aug 26, 2025 · Campaign 25V552000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Cracks can develop in a microprocessor used by the rearview camera on these vehicles. When that happens, the camera's image may not display properly on the screen, so these vehicles do not meet the federal safety standard for rear visibility. Dealers will replace the rearview camera at no charge, though the replacement parts are not yet available. Interim letters warning owners of the issue were mailed starting September 30, 2025, and a second letter announcing the final remedy is expected around July 2026.

Ram ProMaster Rearview Camera May Not Display high

Reported May 21, 2025 · Campaign 25V330000 · Model years: 2022, 2024, 2025

Some Ram ProMaster vans have a software error in the security gateway module that can cause the rearview camera image to not display at all. This means the driver may not be able to see the view behind the vehicle when backing up. Dealers will update the security gateway module software to fix this, at no cost to owners. Chrysler has already begun mailing notification letters to owners about this issue.

Chrysler, Jeep, Ram Rearview Camera May Not Display high

Reported Jun 13, 2024 · Campaign 24V436000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

A software issue in the radio can prevent the rearview camera image from displaying on the screen. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet the requirements of the federal safety standard for rear visibility, FMVSS 111. To fix the problem, dealers will update the radio software, or the software update will be delivered over-the-air. This repair is free of charge for owners of the affected vehicles.

Ram ProMaster Missing Side Door Safety Beam high

Reported Jun 6, 2024 · Campaign 24V416000 · Model years: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024

Some ProMaster vans are missing a beam inside the side door meant to help protect occupants in a side-impact crash. These vans also have a tire placard listing the wrong seating capacity, which could lead to the vehicle carrying more people than it should.

Ram ProMaster Electronic Stability Control Malfunction medium

Reported Apr 25, 2024 · Campaign 24V290000 · Model years: 2021

An electrical ground connection in these ProMaster vans may not be secure. If the connection is loose, the electronic stability control system may stop working.

Ram ProMaster left taillight and turn signal may fail medium

Reported Mar 14, 2024 · Campaign 24V197000 · Model years: 2021, 2022

In these Ram ProMaster vans, a wiring connection may be poor, which can stop the left taillight and the left turn signal from working. When that happens, the left rear light does not come on and the left turn signal does not flash, so other drivers get no signal that the van is turning and have a harder time seeing it. Because of this, the vans do not meet the federal rule covering lamps, reflective devices and related equipment.

Ram ProMaster driver seat belt warning may not work medium

Reported Feb 22, 2024 · Campaign 24V129000 · Model years: 2022, 2023

Chrysler is recalling certain Ram ProMaster vans. The warning light and chime that remind the driver to buckle up may not turn on the way they are supposed to. Because of this, these vans do not meet the federal safety rule covering occupant crash protection. Dealers will look at the driver's seat belt buckle and replace it if needed.

Ram ProMaster Park Gear May Not Engage high

Reported Apr 27, 2023 · Campaign 23V301000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

On certain ProMaster vans with a 62TE transmission, debris inside the transmission may keep the park pawl from fully engaging the park gear.

Ram ProMaster Rearview Camera Is Wrong Type medium

Reported Dec 22, 2022 · Campaign 22V947000 · Model years: 2022

Some Ram ProMaster Chassis Cab and incomplete vehicles were built with an analog rearview camera, which is the wrong type for this vehicle and cannot be properly installed. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet federal safety rules that require a working rearview camera. Without the correct camera, the driver may not get a proper rearview image when backing up, which can reduce how well they can see behind the vehicle.

Ram ProMaster seat belts may not hold child seats medium

Reported Sep 9, 2021 · Campaign 21V699000 · Model years: 2021

On some 2021 Ram ProMaster and ProMaster City vehicles, the seat belt's automatic locking retractor may stop working too soon. This can keep a child restraint system from being held securely in place. Dealers will inspect the seat belt retractors and replace them if needed, free of charge.

Ram ProMaster Paratransit Shifter Cable Separation high

Reported Oct 15, 2020 · Campaign 20V582000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On some Ram ProMaster Paratransit vehicles with a 3.6L engine, the transmission shifter cable can separate and disconnect from the transmission. When this happens, the vehicle may not shift the way the driver intends, and the gear shown on the shift lever may not match the gear the transmission is actually in. Because of this mismatch, the driver may not be aware of what gear the vehicle is actually in.

Ram 1500 and ProMaster Side Airbag May Not Inflate high

Reported Jun 25, 2020 · Campaign 20V374000 · Model years: 2020

A part inside the side curtain airbag, called the diffuser, may not have been crimped properly. This could cause the diffuser to detach from the inflator if the airbag deploys in a crash. If the diffuser detaches during deployment, the airbag may not inflate the way it should. This increases the risk of injury in a crash. Dealers will replace the side curtain airbag assemblies, free of charge.

Ford Transit and Ram Promaster van seat belts may not secure high

Reported May 5, 2020 · Campaign 20V250000 · Model years: 2019

National Van Builders is recalling certain vans built on Ford Transit and Ram Promaster models that were fitted with Freedman foldaway seats. The seat belt retractor, the part that lets the belt pull out and reel back in, may not have been set up correctly. Because of that, the seat belt may not properly secure the person wearing it, and the belts do not meet the federal safety standard for seat belt assemblies. Dealers will replace the retractors free of charge.

Ford Transit, Ram ProMaster Vans Have Seat Belt Issue medium

Reported Apr 20, 2020 · Campaign 20V222000 · Model years: 2019

TransitWorks says some vans built on Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster chassis, equipped with certain foldaway seats, may have seat belt retractors that were not set correctly. Because of this, the seat belt in an affected seat may not properly hold the occupant in place.

Ram ProMaster Shifter Cable May Cause Unintended Movement high

Reported Jan 23, 2020 · Campaign 20V036000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

In some Ram ProMaster vans with 3.6L engines, the cable that connects the transmission shifter to the transmission can separate and come loose. When that happens, the vehicle may not shift into the gear the driver is trying to select, and the gear shown on the shift lever may not match the gear the transmission is actually in. This means the driver could think the vehicle is in one gear when it's actually in a different one.

Ram ProMaster Cooling Fan May Seize And Overheat high

Reported Nov 15, 2019 · Campaign 19V818000 · Model years: 2018

Certain 2015 through 2018 Ram ProMaster vans built with a 3.0-liter diesel engine or a 3.6-liter gasoline engine, and equipped with air conditioning, may have a problem with the engine cooling fan. The fan can seize up while the engine is running. When the fan seizes in this way, the motor that powers the fan can begin to overheat as a result.

Backup camera monitors may show reversed image medium

Reported Jun 11, 2019 · Campaign 19V428000 · Model years: 2018

Certain vehicles equipped with these aftermarket monitors for the back-up camera may reset to factory default settings, which can cause the camera image to display reversed left-to-right.

Modified Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster seats may not lock medium

Reported Apr 29, 2019 · Campaign 19V333000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

New England Wheels is recalling certain Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster 2500 and 3500 vans that were modified with Freedman Go-ES Foldaway seats. When the back seat cushion is turned up from its stowed position into the upright position, it may not stay locked in place when weight pushes on it, such as in a crash or a sudden stop. Owners have been notified and told to contact Freedman, which will repair the foldaway seats at no charge.

Ford Transit and Ram Promaster Seat Back Unlock Risk high

Reported Apr 18, 2019 · Campaign 19V313000 · Model years: 2018

Mobility Transportation Services is recalling certain Ford Transit, Ram Promaster, and Ford Econoline vans modified with Freedman GO-ES Foldaway Seats. When the back seat cushion is rotated up from its stowed position into the upright position, it may not stay locked into place if the seat comes under load, such as during a crash or a sudden stop. This applies to the specific foldaway seat parts installed in these modified vans.

Ford Transit and Ram ProMaster Foldaway Seat Lock Failure medium

Reported Mar 1, 2019 · Campaign 19V332000 · Model years: 2018

On certain Ram ProMaster and Ford Transit vehicles fitted with Freedman GO-ES foldaway seats, the seat back cushion may not lock securely in place when it is raised from its folded position to the upright position. If the vehicle is in a crash or stops suddenly, the seat back may not stay locked under that load. A seat back that moves increases the risk of injury to the person sitting in it.

Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram Vehicles May Stall high

Reported Aug 9, 2018 · Campaign 18V524000 · Model years: 2018

A voltage regulator chip on the circuit board inside the powertrain control module may fail. If this chip fails, the vehicle may stall while it is being driven, or it may fail to start at all. To correct this problem, Chrysler dealers will replace the entire powertrain control module with a new one so the vehicle does not experience this stalling or no-start condition again.

Ram ProMaster Airbag Sensor May Fail high

Reported Nov 14, 2017 · Campaign 17V712000 · Model years: 2018

Some Ram ProMaster vehicles were built using an incorrect rivet to attach the front door impact sensor. Because of this, the sensor housing may crack. If the sensor is damaged, it may not work correctly during certain side-impact crashes, which could affect whether the side airbags deploy as intended.

RAM Promaster recall questions

How many recalls does the RAM Promaster have?

NHTSA lists 25 safety recalls covering the 2018–2025 RAM Promaster. 15 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Wheelchair Restraints in Various Vehicles May Not Lock" (NHTSA campaign 25V876000), reported Dec 17, 2025.

Which RAM Promaster model year has the most recalls?

The 2018 RAM Promaster has the most, with 10 recalls. The 2025 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 4. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the RAM Promaster?

No. None of the 25 RAM Promaster recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my RAM Promaster has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised RAM dealer, including for second owners.
