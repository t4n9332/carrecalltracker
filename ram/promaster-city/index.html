Ram ProMaster City Turn Signals May Not Warn high

Reported Sep 21, 2021 · Campaign 21V728000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Some Ram ProMaster City vans may have turn signal lights that fail without changing their flashing rate to alert the driver. This means the driver may not know that a turn signal has stopped working. Dealers will update the Body Controller Module software to fix this, at no cost to owners. Chrysler has already mailed notification letters to owners about this issue.

Ram ProMaster seat belts may not hold child seats medium

Reported Sep 9, 2021 · Campaign 21V699000 · Model years: 2021

On some 2021 Ram ProMaster and ProMaster City vehicles, the seat belt's automatic locking retractor may stop working too soon. This can keep a child restraint system from being held securely in place. Dealers will inspect the seat belt retractors and replace them if needed, free of charge.

Ram ProMaster City vans may lose braking power critical

Reported Mar 9, 2021 · Campaign 21V147000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

The module that controls the anti-lock brake system may have internal damage. This damage could cause a loss of normal braking performance or a loss of automatic braking features.

RAM ProMaster City Steering Rack May Crack medium

Reported Mar 6, 2018 · Campaign 18V149000 · Model years: 2018

Some RAM ProMaster City vans have a manufacturing defect in the rack and pinion, a part of the hydraulic steering system. A casting flaw in the gears can let a crack form, and that crack can let the power-steering fluid leak out. If enough fluid is lost, steering the vehicle can suddenly require much more effort than normal to turn the wheel.

Ram ProMaster City Tire Placard Lists Wrong Capacity low

Reported Feb 22, 2018 · Campaign 18V129000 · Model years: 2018

Some 2018 ProMaster City vehicles have a tire placard that shows the wrong total number of occupants the vehicle is allowed to carry.

RAM Promaster City recall questions

How many recalls does the RAM Promaster City have?

NHTSA lists 5 safety recalls covering the 2018–2021 RAM Promaster City. 2 of them are rated high or critical on this site. The most recent is "Ram ProMaster City Turn Signals May Not Warn" (NHTSA campaign 21V728000), reported Sep 21, 2021.

Which RAM Promaster City model year has the most recalls?

The 2021 RAM Promaster City has the most, with 3 recalls. The 2019 model year has the fewest of the years we cover, with 1. Newer model years have had less time to collect recalls, so a low count on a recent year is not a guarantee.

Is there a do-not-drive or park-outside recall on the RAM Promaster City?

No. None of the 5 RAM Promaster City recalls on file carries a do-not-drive or park-outside advisory from NHTSA.

How do I check whether my RAM Promaster City has an open recall?

Enter your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls. A recall often applies to only some vehicles of a model year, and the VIN lookup shows the ones still unrepaired on your car. Recall repairs are free at any franchised RAM dealer, including for second owners.
