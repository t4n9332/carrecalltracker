RAM 1500 recalls

50 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Ram 1500 second-row seat belt anchors improperly installed high

Reported 2026-07-30 · Campaign 26V495000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

The anchors that hold the seat belt buckles for the center and left second-row seats may not have been attached correctly at the factory. If an anchor is not properly secured, the seat belt may not hold a passenger in place during a crash. Because of this, the trucks do not meet the federal standard for seat belt anchorages. Dealers will inspect the anchors and fasten them to the body of the truck as needed, free of charge.

Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 side airbag inflators can burst high

Reported 2025-12-01 · Campaign 25V824000 · Model years: 2019

These trucks have curtain airbags that drop down alongside the side windows in a crash. Because of a manufacturing defect, the metal canister that fills one or both of those bags can burst instead of inflating them properly. If that happens, sharp pieces of metal can fly out and strike people inside the truck. The problem only shows up in a crash, which is exactly when the airbag is supposed to protect you. Dealers replace the affected airbags free.

Ram 1500 through 5500 driver airbag may not deploy high

Reported 2025-11-06 · Campaign 25V765000 · Model years: 2021

A control part inside the steering column may keep the driver's airbag from working the way it should. If you are in a crash, the airbag in the steering wheel may not inflate, leaving you without that protection. These trucks do not meet the federal rule for crash protection because of it. Nothing about the way the truck drives will feel different, so you cannot tell on your own whether yours is affected.

Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 side airbags can burst apart critical

Reported 2025-09-11 · Campaign 25V592000 · Model years: 2018

The curtain airbags that drop down over the side windows have a part inside that inflates them with gas. Because of a manufacturing mistake, that part can break apart when the airbag goes off. Instead of just cushioning you, it can send sharp metal pieces flying into the cab and hurt the people inside. Dealers will replace one or both side curtain airbags as needed, at no cost. Owner letters were mailed in September 2025.

Reported 2025-05-08 · Campaign 25V298000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Chrysler is recalling certain 2019-2024 Ram 1500 pickups. The driver's side airbag may not have been connected properly, so it might not inflate the way it is supposed to in a crash. Dealers fixed this by securing the airbag connectors at no charge, and Chrysler reports that all affected trucks have already been repaired. Owner notification letters were mailed May 27, 2025. It is still worth checking your own truck to confirm the work was done.

Reported 2024-09-05 · Campaign 24V653000 · Model years: 2019, 2021

Electronic stability control is the system that quietly applies individual brakes to keep the truck from sliding or spinning in a turn or on a slick road. A software problem in the anti-lock brake computer can switch that system off. Normal braking still works, but the extra help in a skid may not be there when you need it. These trucks do not meet the federal rule for stability control. Dealers reprogram the computer free.

Ram 1500 and Jeep Grand Cherokee diesels can stall high

Reported 2023-06-08 · Campaign 23V411000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

These Ram 1500 and Jeep Grand Cherokee trucks with the 3.0-liter diesel engine have a toothed ring on the crankshaft that can come apart in layers. The engine computer reads that ring to time the fuel injectors and the camshaft, so when it breaks down the engine loses its timing and can stall. Dealers will reprogram the engine computer so the vehicle keeps running, free of charge. This recall is an expansion of an earlier one, 20V-475.

Reported 2023-04-13 · Campaign 23V265000 · Model years: 2021

Certain 2021 Ram 1500 trucks with the 5.7L eTorque engine have a software problem in the computer that controls the engine and transmission. Owner notification letters were mailed April 27, 2023. Chrysler's number for this recall is 37A.
