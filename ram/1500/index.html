RAM 1500 recalls

50 recalls covering model years 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021. To check whether your vehicle is affected, look up your 17-character VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — a recall may apply only to some vehicles of a given year.

Ram 1500 Rear Seat Belt Anchor Problem high

Reported 2026-07-30 · Campaign 26V495000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Certain Ram 1500 trucks may have a problem with the seat belt buckles in the second row. The anchors that attach the center and left-side seat belt buckles to the vehicle may not have been installed correctly. Because of this, these seat belts may not properly hold occupants in place during a crash, which increases the risk of injury. These vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard for seat belt anchorages.

Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 side airbag inflators may rupture high

Reported 2025-12-01 · Campaign 25V824000 · Model years: 2019

Chrysler is recalling certain Ram 1500 Classic, Ram 2500, and Ram 3500 vehicles because of a manufacturing defect in the side curtain airbags. The part that fills the airbag with gas, on the right side, the left side, or both, may break apart. A dealer will replace one or both of the side curtain airbags as needed, at no cost to the owner. Chrysler mailed notification letters to owners, and the company has a customer service line for owners with questions.

Ram Trucks: Driver's Air Bag May Not Deploy high

Reported 2025-11-06 · Campaign 25V765000 · Model years: 2021

Certain Ram 1500, 2500, 3500, 4500, and 5500 trucks have a problem with the steering column control module that can stop the driver's side air bag from deploying properly in a crash. The air bag is meant to help protect the driver, but this issue means it may not inflate the way it's supposed to when it's needed, which could affect how well the driver is protected during a collision.

RAM 1500, 2500, 3500 Side Airbag Inflators May Rupture high

Reported 2025-09-11 · Campaign 25V592000 · Model years: 2018

The side curtain airbags on the left and right sides of these trucks have inflators that were manufactured with a defect. Because of this defect, the inflator can rupture when the airbag deploys. If that happens, small sharp pieces of metal can fly out with enough force to strike people inside the vehicle, which can cause injury to the driver or passengers nearby.

Ram 1500 Driver Air Bag May Not Deploy high

Reported 2025-05-08 · Campaign 25V298000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

On certain 2019-2024 Ram 1500 trucks, the air bag on the driver's side may not be properly connected. Because of this loose connection, if the vehicle is in a crash, the air bag might not inflate the way it is supposed to.

Ram Trucks Side Airbag Inflator Can Rupture high

Reported 2025-01-16 · Campaign 25V010000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On these Ram trucks, the right and left side curtain air bag inflators may rupture due to a manufacturing defect.

Ram 1500 Tow Mirror Glass Can Fall Off medium

Reported 2024-10-10 · Campaign 24V754000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

Chrysler (FCA US) is recalling certain Ram 1500 Classic pickup trucks equipped with heated trailer tow rearview mirrors. The glass in the driver's side mirror can come loose and detach from the mirror housing. Because the mirror glass may fall off, the driver may not be able to see clearly out of that mirror while driving.

Ram 1500 Software Can Shut Off Stability Control high

Reported 2024-09-05 · Campaign 24V653000 · Model years: 2019, 2021

Certain Ram 1500 trucks have a software problem in the computer that runs the anti-lock brakes. Because of the fault, that computer can shut off the truck's electronic stability control system. With stability control switched off, the truck no longer meets the federal safety standard that covers these systems. The fix is a software update for the brake control computer, done by a dealer at no charge to the owner.

Jeep and RAM Diesel Engines May Stall high

Reported 2023-06-08 · Campaign 23V411000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On some Jeep Grand Cherokee and RAM 1500 trucks with 3.0L diesel engines, a sensor on the crankshaft may come apart. This can cause the engine to lose track of fuel injector and camshaft timing, which may make the engine stall.

Ram 1500 Software Glitch May Stall Engine medium

Reported 2023-04-13 · Campaign 23V265000 · Model years: 2021

Some 2021 Ram 1500 trucks with the 5.7-liter eTorque engine have a computer chip, called the powertrain control module, that controls the engine. A software problem in this chip can cause the engine to get the wrong mixture of fuel and air. This incorrect fuel mixture can cause the engine to stall while you are driving.

Ram 1500 Rearview Camera May Not Display medium

Reported 2023-02-09 · Campaign 23V059000 · Model years: 2021

Chrysler is recalling certain Ram 1500 and Ram 2500 trucks because a part called the trailer reverse steering control module may stop the rearview camera image from appearing on the screen when the truck is shifted into reverse. Because the rearview image does not always show up, these vehicles do not meet a federal safety rule that requires vehicles to give the driver a view of what is behind them while backing up.

Ram pickup tailgates may open while driving medium

Reported 2022-12-08 · Campaign 22V904000 · Model years: 2019, 2020, 2021

Chrysler is recalling certain Ram 1500, 2500 and 3500 pickup trucks. The strikers that the tailgate latches onto may be out of alignment, so the tailgate may not latch properly. A tailgate that is not latched can open while the truck is being driven. Dealers will inspect how the tailgate striker lines up with the latch on the truck bed and adjust it if needed, free of charge. Chrysler mailed owner letters on December 22, 2022.

Jeep and Ram Diesel Fuel Pump Can Stall Engine high

Reported 2022-10-13 · Campaign 22V767000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

On these Jeep and Ram vehicles with 3.0-liter diesel engines, the high pressure fuel pump may fail. If this happens, the engine can stall.

Jeep, Ram Rearview Camera Image May Not Show high

Reported 2022-08-25 · Campaign 22V638000 · Model years: 2020, 2021

A software error in the radio may prevent the rearview camera image from displaying when a driver shifts into reverse. Without that image showing, the driver has less visibility of what is behind the vehicle. Because of this, these vehicles do not meet a federal safety standard for rear visibility.

Jeep and RAM Diesel Fuel Pump May Fail high

Reported 2022-06-09 · Campaign 22V406000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

On some Jeep Grand Cherokee and RAM 1500 vehicles with 3.0L diesel engines, the high-pressure fuel pump can fail and send debris into the fuel system. This can starve the engine of fuel.

Ram Pickup Side Airbag Curtain Defect high

Reported 2021-11-24 · Campaign 21V925000 · Model years: 2021

The side air bag inflatable curtains in these trucks may have been built with the wrong amount of compressed gas inside them. Because of this, the air bag curtain may not deploy the way it is designed to if it is needed during a crash. Dealers will replace the side air bag curtain assemblies on the affected trucks, at no charge to the owner.

Ram 1500 Rearview Camera Image May Not Display medium

Reported 2021-11-23 · Campaign 21V918000 · Model years: 2021

Certain 2021 Ram 1500 pickup trucks have a problem with a part called the Trailer Reverse Steering Control Module. This part may prevent the rearview camera image from showing up on the display when the truck is in reverse. Because of this, these trucks don't meet a federal safety standard covering rear visibility. Dealers will update the software in this module to fix the problem, free of charge.

Ram 1500 Wrong-Size Spare Tire Installed low

Reported 2021-09-09 · Campaign 21V698000 · Model years: 2021

Ram is recalling certain 1500 trucks that may have been built with a spare tire that is the wrong size. The tire size and correct inflation pressure listed on the vehicle's tire information label do not match the spare tire that was actually installed. Because of this mismatch, a driver following the label could put the wrong amount of air into the spare tire.

Ram 1500 side airbag inflator may rupture high

Reported 2021-08-12 · Campaign 21V632000 · Model years: 2018, 2019, 2020

This recall affects certain Ram 1500, 2500, and 3500 trucks. The inflator for the side airbag curtain, the part that fills the airbag with gas, may have its end cap detach, or the inflator itself may split open. If that happens, gas can escape suddenly and push the end cap or other pieces into the vehicle.

Ram 1500 Rearview Camera Image May Not Show low

Reported 2021-06-10 · Campaign 21V438000 · Model years: 2021

Some Ram 1500 vehicles have a module that controls a trailer reverse steering and camera system. Because of an issue with this module, the rearview camera image may fail to display on the vehicle's screen. Without that image, the driver's view of what is behind the vehicle is reduced, and a reduced rear view increases the risk of a crash or an injury to someone behind the vehicle.

Ram 1500 Brake Pedal Separation Risk critical

Reported 2021-02-01 · Campaign 21V037000 · Model years: 2021

Some Ram 1500 Classic SLT trucks were built without the retaining clip that holds the brake pedal push rod in place. Without this clip, the brake pedal can separate from the brake system.

Ram 1500, 2500 Driver Mirror Glass May Detach medium

Reported 2020-10-08 · Campaign 20V622000 · Model years: 2020

Some Ram 1500 and 2500 trucks have a driver-side mirror where the glass can come loose from its backing plate, so it no longer gives a clear reflection.

Jeep, Ram Front Seat Belts May Not Lock high

Reported 2020-09-24 · Campaign 20V580000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

In the event of a crash, the front seat belt retractors on these vehicles may not properly slow an occupant's forward movement after the belt locks. This means the seat belt may not restrain the occupant as effectively as intended during a crash.

Jeep, Ram and Dodge backup camera image stays on screen medium

Reported 2020-09-10 · Campaign 20V191000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Chrysler is recalling certain Jeep, Ram, Dodge and Chrysler vehicles with 8.4-inch or 12-inch radio screens. A software error can leave the backup camera picture on the screen after the driver has shifted out of reverse. Because of this, the vehicles do not meet the federal safety rule covering rear visibility. Dealers will update the radio display software. Owners can also choose to have the software updated remotely, over the air, instead of visiting a dealer.

Ram 1500 Accelerator Pedal Floormat Interference medium

Reported 2020-08-27 · Campaign 20V513000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Chrysler is recalling certain 2019 and 2020 Ram 1500 trucks equipped with adjustable pedals. On these trucks, the floormats may prevent the accelerator pedal from returning to its normal resting, or idle, position when the pedal is in certain positions.

Ram 1500 Diesel Trucks Risk Engine Stall high

Reported 2020-08-13 · Campaign 20V475000 · Model years: 2018

Certain 2014-2018 Ram 1500 trucks with the 3.0L diesel engine have a sensor wheel on the engine's crankshaft that can delaminate, or come apart. This sensor helps the engine keep the fuel injectors and camshaft timing working together. If the wheel breaks down, the engine can lose that timing information, and the truck's engine may stall while you are driving it.

Ram 1500 and ProMaster Side Airbag May Not Inflate high

Reported 2020-06-25 · Campaign 20V374000 · Model years: 2020

A part inside the side curtain airbag, called the diffuser, may not have been crimped properly. This could cause the diffuser to detach from the inflator if the airbag deploys in a crash. If the diffuser detaches during deployment, the airbag may not inflate the way it should. This increases the risk of injury in a crash. Dealers will replace the side curtain airbag assemblies, free of charge.

Ram 1500 and Jeep Compass windshield wipers may loosen high

Reported 2020-04-09 · Campaign 20V208000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

Chrysler is recalling certain Ram 1500, Ram 1500 Classic and Jeep Compass vehicles. The arm or arms that hold the windshield wipers may come loose. If that happens, the wipers may not work properly, and the driver may not be able to see as well in some weather. Dealers will tighten the nuts that hold the wiper arms in place, free of charge.

Ram 1500 4x4 Transfer Case May Cause Power Loss high

Reported 2020-02-13 · Campaign 20V080000 · Model years: 2019

Some four-wheel-drive Ram 1500 pickup trucks have a transfer case gear that may not have been installed correctly. Because of this, the gear problem can cause the truck to become stuck in neutral. If this happens while the truck is being driven, the vehicle could suddenly lose power. In addition, the truck may lose the ability to stay in 'Park,' meaning it might not stay parked as expected.

Ram 1500 Tire Pressure Warning May Not Work medium

Reported 2019-12-12 · Campaign 19V887000 · Model years: 2019

On some 2019 Ram 1500 trucks, the wrong tire pressure sensor may have been installed during manufacturing. Because of this, the dashboard warning light may not turn on when a tire's air pressure gets low, so the driver would have no way of knowing there is a problem with the tire. Chrysler dealers will replace the sensor to fix the problem, free of charge.

RAM 1500 Steering Gear May Lose Power Assist high

Reported 2019-11-14 · Campaign 19V812000 · Model years: 2019

Some Ram 1500 trucks were built with a power steering part that got contaminated during manufacturing. This part can short circuit, which may cause the power steering assist to cut in and out unexpectedly. When that happens, steering can suddenly feel harder to control, especially at lower speeds or during tight maneuvers like parking. This inconsistent steering effort can make the truck harder to handle safely.

Ram 1500 Diesel EGR Cooler Fire Risk critical

Reported 2019-10-24 · Campaign 19V757000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Certain Ram 1500 trucks with diesel engines have an engine cooling part called the EGR cooler that can develop cracks. If it cracks, hot coolant that has turned to vapor can leak into the engine's air intake system. Once inside, this vapor can mix with air and combust inside the intake manifold.

Ram 1500 Bed Step Kit May Cause Fuel Leak critical

Reported 2019-08-27 · Campaign 19E059000 · Model years: 2019

This recall covers a rear step kit that was installed on some Ram 1500 trucks. In a rear-impact crash, the step kit may cause the truck's rear control arms to come into contact with the fuel tank. That contact between the control arms and the fuel tank is the safety problem covered by this recall, and it applies specifically to trucks with this step kit installed.

Ram 1500 Instrument Cluster May Fail to Show Warnings medium

Reported 2019-07-26 · Campaign 19V556000 · Model years: 2019

Chrysler is recalling certain Ram 1500 trucks because the instrument cluster may not properly light up warning indicators for the driver. This means important warning lights about the vehicle's systems might not display as they should, so the driver may not be alerted to a problem.

Ram 1500 Spare Tire Size Does Not Match Label medium

Reported 2019-06-13 · Campaign 19V448000 · Model years: 2019

Some Ram 1500 trucks may have a spare tire that is a different size than what is listed on the tire information label inside the vehicle. The label also lists the wrong inflation pressure for the spare that was actually included. Because of this mismatch, a driver following the label's instructions could put the wrong amount of air into the spare tire, which increases the risk of a crash if that tire is used.

Ram 1500 Airbag Control Module Memory May Corrupt high

Reported 2019-05-31 · Campaign 19V407000 · Model years: 2019, 2020

In some Ram 1500 trucks, the memory in the computer that controls the air bags and seat belt pretensioners can become corrupted. When this happens, it can disable the vehicle's air bags and seat belt pretensioners so they would not work as intended.

Ram Pickup Tailgate May Open While Driving high

Reported 2019-05-09 · Campaign 19V347000 · Model years: 2018

On certain Ram 1500, 2500, and 3500 pickup trucks with a power locking tailgate, a part called the tailgate actuator limiter tab may fracture. If it does, the tailgate can unlatch and open while the truck is being driven.

Ram 1500 Rear Driveshaft May Crack or Fracture high

Reported 2019-04-25 · Campaign 19V324000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

The weld connecting the rear driveshaft's tube and yoke may not be fully fused. This could cause the weld to crack or the driveshaft to fracture. If the driveshaft fractures, it could detach from the truck and become a hazard on the road, or the truck could lose drive power. Either of these can increase the risk of a crash. Dealers will replace the rear driveshaft, free of charge.

Jeep Wrangler, Ram 1500 Steering Wheel Detachment Risk critical

Reported 2019-03-14 · Campaign 19V201000 · Model years: 2019

Some Jeep Wrangler and Ram 1500 vehicles have a part in the steering column, called the stub shaft, that was not machined correctly during the manufacturing process. Because of this, the part may break. If it breaks, the steering wheel could come loose and separate completely from the steering column, leaving the driver without the ability to steer the vehicle at all.

Ram 1500 Auxiliary Battery Wiring May Overheat And Catch Fire critical

Reported 2019-02-28 · Campaign 19V142000 · Model years: 2019

Certain Ram 1500 trucks with a 48-volt auxiliary battery system behind the rear seat may have had an incorrect assembly repair. This can cause the wire terminal connection on the auxiliary battery to overheat. An overheating battery connection can start a fire inside the cabin, and this can happen even when the vehicle's ignition is off.

Ram 1500 Brake Pedal May Separate While Driving critical

Reported 2019-02-05 · Campaign 19V067000 · Model years: 2019

On certain 2019 Ram 1500 trucks equipped with adjustable pedals, the brake pedal may travel farther than it's supposed to and separate from the rest of the pedal assembly while the vehicle is being driven. If the brake pedal comes apart from the assembly like this, the driver may not be able to use the brakes to slow down or stop the truck when needed.

Ram 1500 Windshield Defroster Doesn't Work high

Reported 2019-01-24 · Campaign 19V051000 · Model years: 2019

Chrysler is recalling certain 2019 Ram 1500 trucks equipped with a 12-inch touchscreen and a base-level heating and air conditioning system. In this combination, the windshield defrosting and defogging system doesn't work.

Ram 1500 Loose Fastener May Cut Power Steering high

Reported 2019-01-24 · Campaign 19V020000 · Model years: 2019

Chrysler is recalling certain 2019 Ram 1500 trucks because a battery ground fastener on the driver-side front fender may not have been properly secured. This can lead to a loss of power steering assist.

RAM 1500 Passenger Seat Position Sensor May Loosen high

Reported 2018-11-20 · Campaign 18V822000 · Model years: 2019

On some RAM 1500 trucks, the sensor that tracks the passenger seat's position can come loose and shift out of place. When that happens, the system can no longer tell whether the seat is all the way forward, which affects how the front airbag adjusts when it deploys. Dealers will check the sensor bracket on the passenger seat track and replace it if needed.

RAM 1500 Rear Axle Oil Level Problem high

Reported 2018-08-23 · Campaign 18V556000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Some RAM 1500 trucks may have a rear differential that was not filled with enough oil, which can cause the rear axle assembly to fail. If the axle fails, the truck could lose the ability to drive, or the rear wheels could lock up unexpectedly while driving. Either situation makes the truck harder to control and increases the chance of a crash.

Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep and Ram Vehicles May Stall high

Reported 2018-08-09 · Campaign 18V524000 · Model years: 2018

A voltage regulator chip on the circuit board inside the powertrain control module may fail. If this chip fails, the vehicle may stall while it is being driven, or it may fail to start at all. To correct this problem, Chrysler dealers will replace the entire powertrain control module with a new one so the vehicle does not experience this stalling or no-start condition again.

Dodge Journey, Ram Trucks Backup Camera May Fail high

Reported 2018-06-14 · Campaign 18V398000 · Model years: 2018

Some Dodge Journey and Ram 1500, 2500, and 3500 vehicles may have a backup camera that loses its picture while the driver is backing up. This means the camera image can disappear from the screen right when the driver is relying on it to see what's behind the vehicle.

Dodge, Jeep, Ram and Chrysler cruise control may not cancel high

Reported 2018-05-17 · Campaign 18V332000 · Model years: 2018, 2019

Chrysler is recalling certain Dodge, Jeep, Ram and Chrysler vehicles from several model years because the cruise control may not shut off. If a wire inside the vehicle shorts out while cruise control is in use, the driver may not be able to cancel it by pressing the brake pedal or by switching the system off. The vehicle could keep going at its set speed or even speed up. Dealers will inspect and reprogram the engine or powertrain computer.

Multiple Chrysler Group Models Have Wrong Park Lock Rod high

Reported 2018-05-01 · Campaign 18V280000 · Model years: 2018

On certain 2018 Dodge Charger, Durango and Challenger, Jeep Grand Cherokee and Wrangler, Chrysler 300, and Ram 1500 vehicles, the wrong part may have been installed in the transmission that locks it into park. Because of this, the transmission may fail to shift into 'park' and hold the vehicle in place, meaning the vehicle could move on its own when the driver thinks it is safely parked.

RAM Trucks' Shift Interlock May Allow Rollaway high

Reported 2018-02-08 · Campaign 18V100000 · Model years: 2018

In some RAM pickups and cab chassis trucks with a column-mounted gear shifter, holding the brake pedal down for a long time while the truck is running and in Park can cause a small part called the brake-transmission shift interlock pin to get stuck open. When that happens, the truck can be shifted out of Park into another gear without anyone pressing the brake pedal or having the key in the ignition.
